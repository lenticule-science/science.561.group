## Where do the things in a physical theory come from?

A physical model often begins with a list of things: particles, systems, states, events, observers. It then specifies how those things interact. That is an extraordinarily productive way to calculate. It also leaves a question open: what makes a system distinguishable in the first place, and what makes its identity continue through an interaction?

Lenticule Science's **Relationality** program places that question at the beginning. Its philosophical commitment is that distinguishable things are formations of relating. A boundary, an observation, or a persistent identity must be accounted for through the relations that make it possible. Our [account of identity through change](/the-same-thing-otherwise-identity-as-continuing-relation/) develops that argument. Obsmetrologia is the accompanying mathematical research program: it attempts to construct lawful distinctions, claim positions, evidence, transformations and their consequences in an explicit order.

The Group locates this program in Lakota kin-relational thought. Its mathematical and physical constructions are specific, revisable articulations of that grounding. Experiments test the proposed scientific bridges; they do not adjudicate the legitimacy of the living tradition.

This changes the questions we ask of physics. Can causal sequencing be constructed before a background geometry is supplied? What exactly can an observation preserve about the formation it observes? When do many local accounts fit together, and when does insisting on a single global account destroy something the local accounts actually establish?

There is enough existing work to make those questions concrete. This guide connects three worked investigations rather than asking the reader to accept a finished theory of nature.

## The construction matters as much as the vocabulary

Obsmetrologia does not begin by declaring a universe of independently finished objects and putting a graph on top. Its formal starting point admits a **Mark** and an ordered two-position **Utterance**. **Articulating** supplies recursive continuation. Later constructions must retain the evidence of how their carriers were obtained.

An utterance's two positions can each be articulated or not. Those two binary distinctions produce four corners. The later relation to four-valued evidence is a mathematical transport from that carrier, rather than the introduction of Belnap logic as the originating ontology. Familiar logic is useful for explaining a recovered structure; it cannot silently replace the construction that is supposed to generate it.

The four named acts answer different questions:

| Act | What is being formed or determined? |
| --- | --- |
| Articulating | An oriented distinction and its witnessed continuation. |
| Predicating | A directed claim position, on which subject, predicate and object can be read. |
| Positing | Standing or value admitted at a claim position. |
| Settling | The consequences completed under an admitted closure rule. |

The program names their composite **Attending**. A bounded settling operation can be idempotent on its admitted carrier while further Attending remains possible with changed material. Treating the entire changing-carrier construction as one fixed endomap would hide the very emergence the program is investigating.

There are also distinct routes through the current mathematics. The kinematic curriculum obtains **prefix causal order directly from Articulating**. It does not prove that this prefix order is the same construction as the order studied through Positing and Settling. Keeping the routes visible lets us ask for a theorem connecting them instead of obtaining the connection by drawing an arrow in an overview.

This is one place where our research can advance by realizing what already exists. A successful transport must say which source witnesses it retains, which distinctions it forgets, and what a downstream reader may infer. That is both a mathematical problem and a specification for the technologies that carry observations and proofs.

## Causality before coordinates

The native kinematic route begins with the continuation of an articulation. A previous articulation lies beneath a continued one. That relation gives a strict order. From ordered pairs of distinct, causally related events, the construction forms **causal intervals**. These intervals can themselves be ordered: one interval precedes another when the first finishes no later than the second begins.

The result is a derived causal carrier whose events are intervals. Nothing in that construction needs a metre, a second, an external clock, a probability distribution or a choice of spacetime coordinates. Those quantities remain available as later scientific questions.

The [worked causal article](/causal-intervals-and-projection/) shows why interval sequencing must be distinguished from interval nesting, proves a finite transport result, and gives a four-event counterexample to a tempting stronger inference. It also explains how causal-set dimension estimators enter as a comparison with additional sampling assumptions.

What is gained is a precise object on which more questions can be asked. Which interval transformations preserve independent activity? Which quotient preserves a proof's dependencies? Which extra observations would justify treating an order as a model of a physical spacetime? An answer to one of these can improve computation before the larger physical interpretation is settled.

## Information is relative to a distinction that can be made

An observation is a transformation from possible source formations to recorded readings. Different sources can produce the same reading. The interesting question is what remains distinguishable after that transformation, and whether the forgotten distinctions matter to the next inquiry.

The [boundary example](/information-boundaries-and-physical-records/) starts with two bits and a parity readout. Four possible interiors produce only two boundary readings. A boundary tells us something definite about the interior while leaving a precise ambiguity. Adding another observation removes that ambiguity.

This provides a small, complete test of a larger research question: when is a boundary sufficient to determine what happens inside, either exactly or up to an explicitly chosen equivalence? The existing kinetic program calls for this finite boundary-sufficiency inquiry. Its value is that a claim of reconstruction becomes a checkable property of an observation map.

The same example shows how several uses of “information” can be connected without being confused. Counting compatible interiors measures ambiguity. A distribution over those interiors gives Shannon conditional entropy. A physical memory, reservoir and transformation protocol make a thermodynamic question possible. The transitions require structure and evidence. Explaining them makes the program more powerful, because it tells us what a proposed experiment or implementation must actually supply.

## Quantum theory tests the strength of the construction

The quantum question is especially demanding. A relational approach makes it natural to examine measurement contexts and the conditions under which their accounts can be combined. It does not, by itself, select the probabilities observed in quantum experiments.

Our [quantum example](/quantum-contexts-and-probability/) gives an unusually clear calibration. One parameter controls a family of correlation tables. Every table obeys no-signalling. One part has a global classical model; another is realizable in quantum theory but violates a Bell inequality; the remainder exceeds the quantum bound. Throughout most of this family, every local outcome is possible. A method that checks only possible versus impossible outcomes therefore misses an entire range of quantum contextuality.

The native mathematics already represents measurement contexts, allowed local outcomes, finite weighted distributions and the projection from weights to support. It proves no-signalling for a weighted PR model and exhibits support-level obstructions. The research frontier is to carry those results through probability compatibility and physical realization while preserving their different claims. Our worked family exposes a concrete next requirement: a weighted model can have fully compatible supports while no global probability model reproduces its context distributions.

A native obstruction must therefore be identified at the correct level: an assignment of outcomes, a probability distribution over assignments, an amplitude-bearing representation, or a physically realized measurement process. Calling them all “failure to glue” would lose the distinction that the example exposes.

The existing kinetic research program already organizes the quantum bridge as progressively harder questions: compositional processes, contextuality, interference, quantum information, the Bell and Tsirelson bounds, and the Born probability rule. The worked example supplies a discriminating target for that program. It is prior quantum mathematics used to assess a proposed transport, not evidence that the transport has already been derived.

## Where a relational starting point could go further

The strongest prospect is an explicit account of **how a representation becomes lawful and what it preserves**. This may connect problems that are often studied with different tools:

- **Individuation:** determine which relations make a formation distinguishable and which changes preserve its warranted continuity.
- **Observation:** construct the map from source to reading, with its lost distinctions retained as a question rather than erased from the record.
- **Local composition:** determine which local accounts admit a shared completion, which conflict, and which require a different carrier.
- **Causal computation:** use preserved dependency structure to restrict recomputation and justify concurrent execution.
- **Physical comparison:** identify exactly which generated quantities can be calibrated against measured probabilities, geometry, energy or time.

These prospects are reasons to investigate. A specific advance must produce something sharper: a preservation theorem, a counterexample, an implementation with a fair baseline, or a prediction that distinguishes competing physical models. A reformulation that merely reproduces an established result can still be useful when it creates a reliable bridge, but that usefulness should be stated as such.

The technology has a direct role here. The SignCell, SignChain, SignGraph and SignCraft work is intended to retain source claims, evidence and transformations across computation. Metrologic carries executable mathematics. Holofoam supplies a setting for inspecting generated worlds and their dynamics. Those consumers should carry the actual invariant a research result earns, rather than inherit a physical interpretation from a name such as “causal,” “quantum” or “holographic.”
