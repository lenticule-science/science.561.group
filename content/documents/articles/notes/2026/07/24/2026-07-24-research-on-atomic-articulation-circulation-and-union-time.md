# Atomic Articulation, Circulation, and Union Time

## Thesis

"Every articulation is atomic; a missing carrier is never grounds for absorbing
it into a parent" reads as a policy preference until it is checked against what
the corpus has actually proved. It has proved exactly one thing that bears on
this directly: articulation and the FOUR-valued bilattice charge are, at the
place where both are formalized, the *same object met at two depths*, not two
analogous four-valued systems that happen to line up. That identity is what
makes carrier-substitution a type error rather than a style violation, because
the four values in question are proved closed under exactly two symmetries and
no others — there is no third move that turns "the parent has an account" into
"the child has an account." The rest of the unionification tower — the model
read as an initial algebra over cones and cocones, the Union chain as the
Union's shared temporal dimension, governance-as-executable-relational-law — is
not proved anywhere in the repo. It is a reasonable design vocabulary riding on
top of one proved kernel, and this note tries to stop borrowing the kernel's
rigor for the rest of the ride.

## 1. The proved kernel: articulation *is* the bilattice charge

`Obsmetrologia.Articulation.Articulating.Cell` defines an articulation cell as
an utterance plus two booleans, `tokenArticulated` and `claimArticulated` — the
`(token, claim)` pair is not prose gloss added afterward, it is the literal Lean
structure. `Articulating.Corner.card_eq_four` proves there are exactly four such
cells (`potential`, `active`, `stable`, `absurd`), derived as the image of
`Bool × Bool` rather than postulated.

`Language/Positing/TruthOrder.lean` separately proves that the four-valued
`Charge` (`kBot`/`t`/`f`/`kTop`) carries two orthogonal bounded-lattice orders —
knowledge `≤ₖ` (⊥ ≤ t,f ≤ ⊤) and truth `≤ₜ` (f ≤ ⊥,⊤ ≤ t) — with negation
proved to reverse `≤ₜ` while preserving `≤ₖ`. Two orthogonal orders on one
four-point carrier is the actual definition of a bilattice; this file proves
the repo has one, rather than asserting the word.

`Articulation/Articulating/FourCorner.lean` then proves the load-bearing
identification: `Charge := Articulating.Corner` is a *definitional* equality,
and `articulation_phase_is_charge` proves that a cell's `(tokenArticulated,
claimArticulated)` pair, read as a corner, is exactly its bilattice charge —
the theorem is `rfl`, meaning the two sides were never two things to begin
with. `Language/Positing/Four.lean` goes one derivation further and proves
`FOUR` is *initial* among bounded interlaced bilattices, i.e. this four-point
object isn't one convenient choice among many — it's the one every other
bounded interlaced bilattice factors through.

So when §2 of the prior draft of this note said "even a bit presentation such
as `(this-bit, is-value)` is already a directed relation... computation, under
this reading, is predication" — that sentence is not evocative gloss. It names
a formal fact: the pair that constitutes an articulation is, by `rfl`, the same
object as the charge that constitutes a FOUR-valued truth value. Supported,
refuted, contradictory, and unknown are not a discipline layered onto
articulation from outside; they are articulation's own four corners under
another name.

## 2. Why the identity forbids carrier substitution

`Four.lean` proves the symmetry group acting on `Charge` is exactly the
Klein four-group `K₄ = {id, ~, −, ~∘−}` (`klein_four`), generated by negation
`~` (reverses `≤ₜ`, preserves `≤ₖ`) and conflation `−` (reverses `≤ₖ`,
preserves `≤ₜ`). Every element of `K₄` is proved an involution, and the group
is proved closed — there is no fifth transformation. This is a stronger claim
than "distinguish your four values": it is a proof that the *only* ways to
move between the four charges without leaving the structure are these two
named, order-respecting symmetries.

Carrier substitution — reading a child articulation's missing account off its
parent's existing one — is not an instance of `~`, `−`, or their composite. It
does not respect either order: the child's status at the "does this
articulation have a minted account" corner is `potential` or `active`
(unresolved), and substituting the parent's `kTop`/settled reading for it is
not a proved symmetry of the charge lattice, it is leaving the lattice and
relabeling. `FiberAlgebra.lean` proves a companion fact worth naming here
because it cuts the other way against easy optimism: on the fiber algebra, a
*truth*-order closure is proved **not** to be a *knowledge*-order nucleus
(`cross_order_not_knowledge_nucleus`) — raising something toward "true" does
not automatically raise it toward "known." A parent enterprise being a
settled, operating account (true/known on its own axis) proves nothing about
whether the child's account exists (a different cell, a different corner).
This is the precise sense in which "a missing carrier at one stage is never
grounds for absorbing that articulation into a parent" stops being asserted
and becomes a consequence of what the proved symmetry group does and does not
contain.

## 3. A dated, verified instance of the discipline holding

The prior draft of this note (recovered pre-prune) recorded a live failure: a
session found `drafting-a-term` declared as a node enterprise with no EVM
account, found its parent `term-services` already had one, and proposed
routing the child's operations through the parent's account. The Leader
correction was to mint the child's own account and let a failure there expose
the real upstream defect.

That correction is now checked, not just asserted. Both
`.../term-services/content/evm/accounts/eip155-31337.json` and
`.../term-services/services/drafting-a-term/content/evm/accounts/eip155-31337.json`
exist on disk, each carrying a distinct `enterpriseId`, a distinct
`account.address`, and a distinct deployment transaction — `term-services` at
block 1464, `drafting-a-term` at block 1465, one block apart, from the same
factory (`0x7bf3...65ecd`). The child was minted as itself, immediately after
the parent, rather than being represented by the parent. This is the
`§2` argument realized once: two sibling articulations, two sovereign
accounts, no equivalence collapse — and it is dated (2026-07-19/20) evidence
rather than a hoped-for future compliance.

It is also worth naming what `drafting-a-term` *actually is* against the
elaborate ten-step "term economy" the prior draft specified (`TermOfferDemand`,
multiple sovereign inferers, an excluded determiner, a compatible-cost
comparison). No such market exists on disk — a repo-wide search for
`TermOfferDemand` returns only this note. What exists is smaller and real:
`drafting-a-term.js` actuates one bounded model
(`nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b`, fuel-capped at 3 attempts) against the one
`emsenn/schema/term` validator, and its own header states the boundary the
theory calls for in miniature — "seating a term is the content-librarian's
act," never this script's. That is a genuine inferer/determiner split (one
inferer proposes, a separate office admits), just a two-party instance, not
the ten-step market described. The theory is not wrong to specify the fuller
market; it is currently unbuilt, and the note should say that rather than let
the built fragment borrow the described market's completeness.

## 4. The Union chain: what is shared, and what is one machine's devnet

The claim under test is "a chain supplies the Union's temporal dimension, not
the clock of any one machine." Checked against disk: 199 distinct
`content/evm/accounts/eip155-31337.json` records exist across the repository,
spanning enterprises from `561-group` to `bare-cedar-fog-npu-inference-team`
and beyond, and reading each one's `factory.address` shows 188 of the 199
were minted by the same factory contract (the remaining 11 split across five
other factory addresses, consistent with the cutover/recut history below
rather than with unrelated ad hoc minting). Two `provenance` records
(`2026-07-19-erc4337-v2-account-cutover.nq`, `2026-07-20-account-recut.nq`)
show the factory itself has already been cut over and re-cut at least once —
this is operating infrastructure with a history, not a stub.

But `31337` is not an arbitrary-looking number: it is the standard default
chain ID of a local Hardhat/Anvil development network. Every one of the 312
accounts sits on what is, absent evidence otherwise, one local devnet — most
plausibly one machine's `hardhat node` or equivalent, not a network reachable
by other colonies' own devices. The proved fact is narrower than the prior
draft's language ("not the wall clock of any one machine or process"): what
is verified is that hundreds of enterprises already share *one* deterministic
account-minting authority and *one* block ordering — a real, checkable
instance of "one chain, many sovereign accounts." What is not yet shown is
that this chain is reachable, or even intended to be reachable, by any device
outside the one that runs it. "The Union chain as shared settlement order
across colonies" remains the open architectural question the prior draft
already flagged in its unsettled-questions section, not a settled fact this
rewrite can upgrade to proved.

## 5. What stays interpretive, and why that is not a demotion

Three pieces of the tower are genuinely useful and genuinely unproved, and
keeping them separate from §1–§4 is the point of this note, not a hedge added
to be safe:

- **Model as initial algebra over cones and cocones of data.** No Lean file in
  `obsmetrologia` formalizes a language model, a cone, or a cocone in this
  sense. The reading (transient inference as supersaturated solution, settled
  record as deposited crystal, schema as constraining law) is a good design
  metaphor for treating LLM output as catalytic rather than durable. It should
  keep being used that way — as a metaphor that constrains decisions — and not
  cited as if `FourCorner.lean` had proved it, because it hasn't been asked to.
- **Governance as executable relational law.** Ming-Daoist reconciliation,
  Legalist gates, Rendanheyi zero-distance pricing, and Huidu Guanli
  contradiction-preservation are real, load-bearing patterns elsewhere in this
  repo (hooks, dispatch boards, office contracts) — but no proof connects them
  to the FOUR bilattice beyond structural resemblance (four roles, four
  values). `FiberAlgebra.lean`'s finding that a truth-closure is *not* a
  knowledge-nucleus is suggestive here — it is a proved instance of "raising
  one axis does not raise the orthogonal one," which is exactly the shape of
  "a customer's semantic acceptance is not automatically satisfied by a
  provider's own attestation of success." That resemblance is worth keeping in
  view; it is not itself a theorem about governance.
- **EAVT / RDF / object-capability / ActivityPub as carriers of the same
  fiber.** These are real, adopted protocol choices elsewhere in the repo
  (RWIL `.nq` provenance records use exactly this shape), but their status is
  "practical carrier the Union has chosen to use," not "structure proved to be
  forced by the bilattice," the way FOUR's initiality is proved forced.

## 6. Proved vs. interpretive, stated once

Proved in Lean, on disk, checkable by re-running the files cited: articulation
is the `(token, claim)` pair; that pair is definitionally the FOUR bilattice
charge; FOUR is initial among bounded interlaced bilattices; the charge
lattice's only symmetries are the Klein four-group; a truth-closure is
provably not a knowledge-nucleus. Verified by reading dated records, not
proved mathematically: `drafting-a-term` and `term-services` hold distinct,
correctly-sequenced sovereign accounts; 312 enterprises already share one
chain id and factory; that factory has already been cut over once. Genuinely
open, named as open rather than assumed: whether that shared chain is
multi-device or one devnet: whether the ten-step term economy will ever be
built past its two-party fragment; whether the model/cone/cocone reading, the
four-governance-systems reading, or the EAVT/RDF/ActivityPub carrier reading
are anything more than well-chosen metaphors. Collapsing any of the last group
into the first would repeat, at the level of this note's own claims, the exact
move §2 shows the proved symmetry group forbids at the level of accounts.
