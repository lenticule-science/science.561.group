Alexandre Marius Jacob (1879–1954) was a French anarchist illegalist born in Marseille who organized theft as a disciplined practice and a form of [expropriation](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/expropriation.md). He went to sea as a cabin boy as a child, then apprenticed as a typographer. From about 1900 to 1903 he led a burglary crew that called itself "Les Travailleurs de la Nuit" (the Night Workers), credited with well over a hundred burglaries across France (the commonly cited count of about 150 is unverified here).

## Core ideas

The Night Workers operated according to fixed rules: targets were chosen by category — those Jacob called parasites (the clergy, magistrates, officers, the idle rich) — and never workers. He exempted doctors, artists, and writers as useful. A fixed share of proceeds went to anarchist propaganda. The crew kept accounts and employed careful methods: the umbrella opened under a hole bored in a ceiling to catch debris, and Jacob sometimes left notes for his victims.

Jacob's written statement "Pourquoi j'ai cambriolé" ("Why I Burgled"), read at his trial at Amiens in March 1905, articulated [illegalism](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/illegalism.md) as systematic principle: the right to live is not begged for but taken. [Property is theft](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/pierre-joseph-proudhon.md) in Proudhon's sense, and he chose burglary over wage labor and over harming persons. These choices were not accidents but deliberate articulation of targets, methods, and the allocation of proceeds — the practice held principles visible in its structure.

## Life and imprisonment

Arrested in 1903 at Abbeville after a policeman was killed in the arrest of the crew, Jacob was sentenced to hard labour for life in French Guiana. He attempted escape repeatedly before returning to France in 1925 and being freed in 1928. Later he lived as a travelling haberdashery merchant in the Indre, supported Spanish anarchist exiles after 1939, and died by suicide in 1954 at Reuilly (Indre), leaving a letter.

Jacob is widely said to be a model for Maurice Leblanc's literary figure Arsène Lupin — the gentleman burglar operating according to a code of honour, refusing to harm persons even as he systematically expropriates wealth.

## Where his work figures in this library

Jacob's practice embodies the categorical target policy and the fixed share to the cause as design constraints for anarchist finance. The Night Workers' organization — clear rules, account-keeping, allocation structures, exemptions for workers — supplies the library with a case study in how [direct action](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/direct-action.md) operationalizes [expropriation](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/expropriation.md) at scale. His articulation of targeting rules connects [illegalism](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/illegalism.md) not as individual act but as organized practice to the work of [Clément Duval](./clement-duval.md). His insistence on never targeting workers and on fixed allocation to the cause grounds the later practice of [Lucio Urtubia](./lucio-urtubia.md). The milieu around [Albert Libertad](./albert-libertad.md)'s *l'anarchie* carried the debate his practice provoked, and [Max Stirner](./max-stirner.md)'s reading of property as power is the philosophical ground both drew on.
