John Carroll's Nurnberg Funnel research showed that users skip manuals and learn by doing; documentation should support exploration and error recovery rather than march through features.

## Core ideas

- **Minimalist documentation**: cut conceptual overhead; let users start tasks immediately
- **Active user paradox**: users want to act, not read; design for it
- **Scenario-based design**: design from user activity narratives, not feature lists

## Key works

- *The Nurnberg Funnel* (1990)
- *Minimalism Beyond the Nurnberg Funnel* (1998)
- *Making Use: Scenario-Based Design of Human-Computer Interactions* (2000)
