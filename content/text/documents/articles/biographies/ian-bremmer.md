Ian Bremmer (1969-) is an American political scientist and founder of the political-risk consultancy Eurasia Group (1998) and GZERO Media. His work tracks the shift from a unipolar, US-led post-Cold-War order toward a fragmented order in which international problems go chronically under-addressed because no state or coalition is both able and willing to bear the cost of leading a global response.

## Core ideas

- **[G-Zero world](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/g-zero-world.md).** *Every Nation for Itself: Winners and Losers in a G-Zero World* (2012) names the successor condition to G7- or G2-style global leadership: a "G-Zero," in which capacity and political will to address transnational problems — pandemics, climate change, financial instability, conflict — are held by no single actor or durable coalition, leaving such problems to fester or be managed only piecemeal.
- **The J Curve.** *The J Curve: A New Way to Understand Why Nations Rise and Fall* (2006) models a country's stability against its openness, arguing that stability often falls before it rises as a closed state opens up, producing a J-shaped rather than linear relationship between openness and stability.
- **Globalism's retreat.** *Us vs. Them: The Failure of Globalism* (2018) and *The Power of Crisis* (2022) extend the framework to populist backlash against globalization and to the argument that shared crises (pandemic, climate, disruptive technology) could force renewed coordination precisely because the G-Zero condition makes unilateral responses inadequate.
- **Annual risk forecasting.** Eurasia Group's "Top Risks" reports apply the framework year over year to specific, named, near-term geopolitical risks.

## What the 561 Group reads them for

Bremmer's G-Zero framing supplies a structural account of why choke-point governance — the pattern the [2025-12-25 oil-sanctions log](../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/logs/personal-logs/2025/12/25/2025-12-25-emsenn-personal-log-about-latest-oil-sanctions.md) reads directly out of the Russia sanctions regime — persists rather than resolving into a coordinated global response: no single actor has the standing will and capacity to replace ad hoc, self-interested control of insurance pools, benchmarks, and clearing systems with a coordinated alternative. The house reads Bremmer's "Top Risks" forecasting practice with the same [superforecasting](../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-web-engineering-services-section/services/web-platform-services-section/services/web3-platform-services-section/web3-gambling-platform-services-section/services/kalshi-services-section/services/forecasting-service/content/text/glossary/terms/superforecasting.md) standard applied to [Peter Zeihan](./peter-zeihan.md) and [George Friedman](./george-friedman.md): a plausible structural frame is not a scored forecast, and Bremmer's G-Zero and [Ray Dalio](./ray-dalio.md)'s "changing world order" are read as complementary namings of the same multipolar disorder from political-science and economic-cycle vantage points respectively.
