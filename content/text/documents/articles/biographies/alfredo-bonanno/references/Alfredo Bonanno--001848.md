> Alfredo Maria Bonanno (4 March 1937 – 6 December 2023) was an [[Italy|Italian]] [[anarchy|anarchist]], recognized as a prominent theorist and proponent of [[contemporary insurrectionary anarchism]]. A long-time anarchist, he was imprisoned multiple times. Bonanno was an editor of [[Anarchismo Editions]], among many other publications, only some of which have been translated into English. He was involved in the anarchist movement for over four decades.
