*** January 1st
**** Congress of Racial Equality founed by James Farmer, Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A., 1942

     On this date in 1942, in [[file:chicago_illinois_u_s_a.org][Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A.]], [[file:james_farmer.org][James Farmer]] founded the [[file:congress_of_racial_equality.org][Congress of Racial Equality]]. The group's membership largely came from the [[file:pacifist.org][pacifist]] group [[file:fellowship_of_reconciliation.org][Fellowship of Reconciliation]]. They were inspired by [[file:mahatma_gandhi.org][Mahatma Gandhi]]'s strategy of [[file:non_violence.org][non-violence]] and sought to apply it in abolishing [[file:segregation.org][segregation]]. Notably, the group was 28 men and 22 women, with nearly 2/3rds of its founding membership being white.
     
**** Oscar Grant III, shot by police, Oakland, California, U.S.A., 2009

     [[file:oscar_grant_iii.org][Oscar Grant III]] was shot by [[file:police.org][police]] in [[file:oakland_california_u_s_a.org][Oakland, California, U.S.A.]], by officers affiliated with the city's public transport, while unarmed and held in a prone position. His shooting was captured on video by bystanders.
