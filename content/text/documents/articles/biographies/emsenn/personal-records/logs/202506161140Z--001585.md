I'm back in [[Obsidian]], back in a vault that I last used most actively around [[2023, Winter]]. It's [[2025-06-16|June 16th, 2025]], and pretty much every [[day]] feels like it's adding more [[evidence]] to my [[hypothesis]] that we are living in [[le denouement de l'humanite]]: a [[time]] when, due to [[global resource constraint]], various [[thread|threads]] of [[human existence]] are [[weave|weaving]] themselves into [[knot|knots]].

The [[Obsidian vault]] that I'm currently in is tied to [[my personal website]], so I'm [[write|writing]] this [[log entry]] with half-a-mind toward it being published, but more toward just, getting back toward putting information into the Vault, with the idea that doing so will facilitate producing publishable content.

On that idea, I've recently been struggling with what exactly I want "publishable content" to be. It feels grandiose, but given the state of things, both at-large and within my personal/social life, it's hard to know what would be a useful thing to produce.

Pragmatically, [[my Substack]] has continued to gain subscribers, due to [[Peter Gelderloos|Peter]] recommending it, so it would make sense to post things that are aligned with that audience's interests, but further their education on matters I think are important.

Which, that paragraph I've just written, indicates a broader issue with solving the problem, at least as I see it: Every time I go to look at it, I make a few little statements that are all accurate enough, but don't really give me a complete map of how to properly handle... what might be less "writing" as a task, and more "intelligence production."

Which is what brought me back to Obsidian - it's the best-fit software for what I'm looking to do, and this vault represents the largest collection of notes I have, though doesn't include my most recent notes.

And another aspect is, due to things in my personal life that are beyond the scope of this log entry, I'm feeling pressure to establish myself as a more full and autonomous person in "normal society," especially with regards to having an income and capital reserve. Since I don't currently have any identification, or real means to get one, there's some difficulty in this, but it's currently at the point where I don't have the means to go out and buy myself a pair of shoes if I need them, and in fact still have some credit card debt from Pine Ridge.

The best way I can see to handle that is to use what I've come to call [[social paraform|social paraforms]], which are [[synthetic people]] created using a combination of [[AI agent|AI agents]] and my own effort. Each paraform handles a certain kind of information through certain framings, with a [[fictional identity]] to justify it.

It's an idea I explored too early for myself, a few months ago: my life wasn't stable enough to really support the effort it took to run such paraforms, so they fizzled. But, in the brief time I was operating them, each gained a small organic following, and with greater stability and effort, I'm sure I could do a better job, more consistently.

I want to note that in this sense, I intend to treat aspects of my own identity as paraforms - [[zeds/old-notes/Delta Register/emsenn]] and [[Morgan Sennhauser]] will both live as different people, online, even though there is no obfuscation they are the same embodied person.

The idea of paraform - the actual root idea - is supported by a [[relational calculus]] that I've developed, in which [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]], as [[act|acts]], [[induce]] the conditions of existence. It gets complicated from there.
