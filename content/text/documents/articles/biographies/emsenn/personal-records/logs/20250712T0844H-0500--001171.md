I'm still [[experiencing]] [[anxiety]] and other [[feeling|feelings]] around what and how to write. Yesterday, I [[Journal, 2025-07-11|journaled]] about how my desire for a [[personal Website]] might be outdated, and that the [[platformized Web]] is the actual [[space]] the [[Web]] takes place on.

But then that got me thinking about what comes *after* the platformized Web, and so on. I've also just been anxious about how much [[my disability]] is bothering me recently - lots of [[nausea]] and [[pain]]. That, combined with a general inability for folk to accept that I'm [[writing]] from a place of having done radical praxis, usually far more radical than my readers will have done, stacked against, I have to write for those people for the money I need to actually make any sort of decision that isn't reliant on convincing folk.

And maybe that right there is the contrast that keeps me circling: I am habituated to using my labor to convince people to support me, not gett the support I need myself. But writing to convince people about what's good and true in the universe, at least as far as I see it, and writing to get people with money to give me money, are just… different tasks.

And I need to stop muddying the two - which means identifying how/why exactly I end up falling into this pattern of, my writing has to save the world. It's a belief I clearly hold, that I need to do my part to save the world, and it's clearly a way I sabotage myself against saving the world around me.

But, it's also a way I avoid dealing with the real hardships that, doing what is immediately best for the world around me isn't what I'm best at. Right now, [[my knees]] are *burning* any time I try and move them, I've got a bad headache, and, as I've just explained, I'm grumpy, in a way that's legitimized by tying into real problems about [[my survival]].
