# emsenn's anniversary calendar {#emsenns-anniversary-calendar-1}

## January

### January 1st

1.  Congress of Racial Equality founed by James Farmer, Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A., 1942

    On this date in 1942, in [Chicago, Illinois, U.S.A.](chicago_illinois_u_s_a.org), [James Farmer](james_farmer.org) founded the [Congress of Racial Equality](congress_of_racial_equality.org). The group's membership largely came from the [pacifist](pacifist.org) group [Fellowship of Reconciliation](fellowship_of_reconciliation.org). They were inspired by [Mahatma Gandhi](mahatma_gandhi.org)'s strategy of [non-violence](non_violence.org) and sought to apply it in abolishing [segregation](segregation.org). Notably, the group was 28 men and 22 women, with nearly 2/3rds of its founding membership being white.

2.  Oscar Grant III, shot by police, Oakland, California, U.S.A., 2009

    [Oscar Grant III](oscar_grant_iii.org) was shot by [police](police.org) in [Oakland, California, U.S.A.](oakland_california_u_s_a.org), by officers affiliated with the city's public transport, while unarmed and held in a prone position. His shooting was captured on video by bystanders.
