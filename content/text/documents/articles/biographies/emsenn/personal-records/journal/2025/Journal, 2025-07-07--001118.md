0810h-0500

I've taken a complete break from [[writing]] over the past several days, [[playing]] [[Red Dead Redemption 2]] as a way of taking a [[vacation]] from "work," which, being the self-defined thing it is, is pretty totalizing of my life, while also never seeming to get my full attention.

I've been thinking about how much seems to reinforce the notion that my best course of action is to produce fiction and TTRPG materials, for reasons too many and twisted to try and write during this short break from [[gaming]].

One thing I wanted to note is that I was thinking about how my recent binge of disability has kept me from being as physically active with [[Burdock]] as I want, and that doing the math, I'm disabled about 25-30% of my days, if not more, which is more than I would have answered if someone had asked me; I'd have said 5-10%. I mentioned this to [[Es]], who pointed out that this is far fewer days than when we first got together, which is true.

Reflecting, I think maybe I underestimated how the dynamism of my disability would preclude projects that would be hurt by stretches of abandonment - which is fairly uniquely untrue of writing, especially with computers, as a stash of paused writing takes up literally no physical space, just space on my harddrive.

So the question becomes more specific: not what should I be done, which is writing, but how should I be doing it.

And there, fiction - and especially TTRPG - allow for the broadest range of expression, so that emerges as an answer.

So my next steps seem to be organizing my existing archive, so I have that as a basis for writing more from, especially with an eye toward publishing a website that'll be a good host for this diversity of posting. Emacs and Org-mode is probably a lot better in some ways - and I have the code for publishing an Org-roam collection as a website, but I already have Obsidian set up, with more recent of writing. And, I imagine that on any sort of longer timeframe, I will want to write my own software for encoding information (probably under the pretense of a MUD engine), so will probably move from Obsidian to that.
