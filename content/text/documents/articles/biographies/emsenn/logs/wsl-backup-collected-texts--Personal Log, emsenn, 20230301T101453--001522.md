##### On Public Death
Connected the dots as I was laying awake last night, and I've been watching national public discourse about dead friends, comrades, and accomplices, for more than two decades.

I was 13 the first time a reporter found my phone number and used it to ask me if I wanted to talk about my friend who'd recently killed themself. I didn't, it didn't matter; I had to listen to folk tell me about my friend everywhere I went, and I don't think I've made it a year since without a similar thing happening.
##### Today's Plans
Prepare [[Groundhog wagons|our wagons]] for the [[Migration to Pine Ridge]], which means getting them set up to support us living out of them (and what they carry) while we pack up the rest of the house. We're trying to view it a bit like a [[military simulation]], where the [[simulation scenario]] is we are deployed to help someone pack up and clean their home. Which is true, it's just we're the someone, and we're also going to be using the home we're packing up as our [[basecamp]].

I was talking about it with [[Es]] and [[Terran]] last night and this is, I think, one of the strengths of our [[anarcho-nihilism]]: our ability to reshape the meaning of the same [[space]], [[time]], and [[act|acts]], through changing what we use to give them meaning. In one [[militant perspective]], our current act is a [[rout]] from our [[incursion]] into this [[settler-colony]], but from another militant perspective, it's a [[training simulation]].
