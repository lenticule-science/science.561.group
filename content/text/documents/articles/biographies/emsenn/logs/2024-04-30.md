## Journal

### 20240430T101254

As I'm watching the [[Palestinian Divestment Protests]], where folk are setting up encampments despite hostile police action, to struggle so much to have an organization they choose to give money to change where that money goes, to change the [[genocide of Palestinians]], I can't help but reflect on the fact that here on [[Pine Ridge]], there's so many families who are clear: they would welcome anyone willing to come stay if that person wanted to help [[Indigenous sovereignty]]. Almost all of these protesting students are [[settler|settlers]] here on [[Turtle Island]], yet their view of the world means they don't even see a way of doing things that could mean a lot less [[fighting]], and a lot more [[direct action]].
