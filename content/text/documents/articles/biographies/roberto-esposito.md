Roberto Esposito (b. 1950) is an Italian philosopher whose work reconstructs the concept of community and biopolitics through what he calls the immunization paradigm. Working in dialogue with Michel Foucault's analytics of biopower and Giorgio Agamben's account of sovereign exception, Esposito develops a distinct genealogy in which community (*communitas*) and its immunizing defense (*immunitas*) are two poles of the same etymological and conceptual root — the Latin *munus*, the gift or obligation that community's members owe one another and that immunity exempts its bearer from.

## Core ideas

- **The immunization paradigm.** Political modernity is organized around protecting individual and collective life from the very relational exposure — the *munus* — that constitutes community. Immunization is the paradoxical means by which life is protected by partially negating the community that sustains it.
- **Communitas as shared obligation, not shared property.** Against readings of community as a bounded collective possessing something in common, Esposito argues *communitas* names what its members lack and owe one another — an originary debt or exposure, not a common substance.
- **Bíos and the affirmative turn.** *Bíos: Biopolitics and Philosophy* (2004) distinguishes a "negative" or thanatopolitical biopolitics, exemplified by Nazism, from the possibility of an affirmative biopolitics that would protect life without immunizing it into deadened self-enclosure.
- **The impersonal and the third person.** *Terza persona* (2007) develops a philosophy of the impersonal — a mode of life and thought that exceeds the juridical category of the person — as a further route toward an affirmative biopolitics.
- **The dispositif of the two (Due).** *Due: La macchina della teologia politica e il posto del pensiero* (2013) extends the immunization analysis into the paired, binary structures (such as the two natures of Christ) that Esposito reads as underwriting Western political theology.

## Key works

- *Communitas: The Origin and Destiny of Community* (1998)
- *Immunitas: The Protection and Negation of Life* (2002)
- *Bíos: Biopolitics and Philosophy* (2004)
- *Terza persona* (2007)
- *Due: La macchina della teologia politica e il posto del pensiero* (2013)
