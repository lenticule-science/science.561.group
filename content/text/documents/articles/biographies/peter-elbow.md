Peter Elbow's work centers the writer's voice and the productive separation of generating and editing. Writing Without Teachers established freewriting and the believing/doubting game as core composition methods.

## Core ideas

- **Freewriting**: uncensored continuous writing as a tool for finding what one means
- **Believing and doubting game**: alternating credulity and skepticism as a critical method
- **Voice in writing**: treating voice as something teachable, not innate

## Key works

- *Writing Without Teachers* (1973)
- *Writing With Power* (1981)
- *Vernacular Eloquence* (2012)
