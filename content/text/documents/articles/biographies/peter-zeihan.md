Peter Zeihan (1973-) is an American geopolitical strategist, formerly of Stratfor, now running his own consultancy and YouTube-based commentary practice ("Zeihan on Geopolitics"). His work argues that the globalized world built after 1945 was never a natural or permanent state of affairs: it was underwritten by the US Navy's guarantee of open sea lanes, extended to allies as a Cold War bribe in exchange for standing with Washington against the Soviet Union. As American strategic interest in that guarantee fades — accelerated by the US shale revolution, which sharply reduced US dependence on Middle Eastern oil — Zeihan argues the guarantee itself becomes optional, and with it the low-cost, low-risk global shipping that made long, thin, just-in-time supply chains possible.

## Core ideas

- **The end of the American security guarantee.** *The Accidental Superpower* (2014) argues the US maintains open global shipping lanes less out of altruism than Cold War bargaining, and that this guarantee is now a discretionary expense the US has less reason to pay for.
- **Demographic collapse as the deeper driver.** *The Absent Superpower* (2016) and *The End of the World Is Just the Beginning* (2022) argue that aging, shrinking workforces — most severely in China, but also in much of East Asia, Europe, and Russia — make sustained industrial-scale production and consumption growth structurally impossible in most of the world within Zeihan's forecast horizon, regardless of what happens to trade policy.
- **[Demographic deglobalization](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/demographic-deglobalization.md).** The consequence Zeihan draws from the two prior points: globalization reverses into regional blocs organized around whichever powers retain the demographic and geographic capacity to secure their own supply chains, with the United States — insulated by its two ocean buffers, the Mississippi river system, energy independence, and comparatively favorable demographics — positioned as the least damaged.
- **[Disunited Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disunited_Nations)** (2020) extends the framework country-by-country, forecasting which states weather deglobalization and which do not.

## What the 561 Group reads them for

Zeihan supplies the geopolitical-forecasting register's most confident and most cited deglobalization thesis, and the house reads it two ways at once. First, as data: his account of maritime insurance, shipping benchmarks, and clearing systems as choke points that govern who bears the cost of energy volatility overlaps closely with the owner's own reading of the 2025 oil sanctions round ([2025-12-25 personal log](../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/logs/personal-logs/2025/12/25/2025-12-25-emsenn-personal-log-about-latest-oil-sanctions.md)), which reaches a similar conclusion — that sanctions and export controls function as durable reallocations of market authority to financial and logistical intermediaries — independently of Zeihan's demographic framing.

Second, as a specimen the house's own [superforecasting](../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-web-engineering-services-section/services/web-platform-services-section/services/web3-platform-services-section/web3-gambling-platform-services-section/services/kalshi-services-section/services/forecasting-service/content/text/glossary/terms/superforecasting.md) standard is built to discipline. Zeihan's forecasts are specific, near-term, and rarely hedged with calibrated probabilities — the "hedgehog" style [Philip Tetlock](./philip-tetlock.md)'s research found to underperform structured, updated, probabilistic judgment. The house's forecasting section runs on Brier-scored, continuously updated probability estimates ([forecasting-services-section README](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/forecasting-services-section/README.md)) precisely because pundit-style geopolitical forecasting of Zeihan's kind has a documented track record of overconfidence. Zeihan's deglobalization thesis is read as a hypothesis to score, not a forecast to inherit — and it sits in direct tension with [Parag Khanna](./parag-khanna.md)'s connectivity-driven optimism, which the house's own energy-return-on-investment position (below) tends to side against.
