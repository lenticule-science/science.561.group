Clément Duval (1850–1935) was a French anarchist and metalworker whose trial statement set out the doctrine of individual recovery. Wounded in the Franco-Prussian War, he was a member of the Paris anarchist group "La Panthère des Batignolles." In October 1886 he burgled the Paris home of the painter Madeleine Lemaire, during which the house caught fire; he stabbed a policeman while resisting arrest. Tried in January 1887, he was sentenced to death, commuted to hard labour for life in the penal colony of French Guiana (the Îles du Salut).

## Core ideas

Duval's statement at trial and his letter to the anarchist paper *Le Révolté* set out the doctrine of "reprise individuelle" ([individual recovery](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/illegalism.md)): theft exists only through the exploitation of man by man (the collective form of the same claim is [expropriation](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/expropriation.md)). When society refuses a person the right to exist, that person takes it. His core claim was that his burglary was not theft but the recovery of a portion of stolen collective wealth, and that the fire was an accident.

The argument turns on the distinction between [property](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/property.md) as a social arrangement and property as a thing. The target is the [property](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/property.md) relation itself, not the person; the act is "recovery" of collective product appropriated through institutional arrangement.

## Key works

- Letter to *Le Révolté* (1886, recounting the burglary and its doctrine)
- *Memorie autobiografiche* (1929, Italian); later French edition *Moi, Clément Duval, bagnard et anarchiste*

## Where his work figures in this library

Duval articulates the earliest full statement of [illegalism](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/illegalism.md) as doctrine, the argument that the target is the [property](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/property.md) relation, not a person, and that the act is "recovery" of collective product. His analysis connects [property is theft](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/pierre-joseph-proudhon.md) — Proudhon's foundational claim — with [direct action](../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/direct-action.md) as the practical recovery of that theft. His doctrine shaped the subsequent development of anarchist illegalism and the categorical ethics that would guide figures like [Alexandre Marius Jacob](./alexandre-marius-jacob.md).
