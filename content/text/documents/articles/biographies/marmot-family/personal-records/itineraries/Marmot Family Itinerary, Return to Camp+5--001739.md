## Schedule

### Day 1

On the first day back at camp, our main work will be to do some basic unpacking.

### Day 2

On the second day back at camp, our main work will be to clear the brush at the site we picked. We'll be burning it in our big rocket stove, and pulling out the coals to make biochar with, so we'll need the following equipment on-hand:

- [[saw|saws]]
- [[big rocket stove]]
- [[coal crucible]]
- [[flat-end shovel]]
  - for removing coals from stove
- [[large closed-bottom flip-top tote]], filled with [[water]]
  - for [[quenching]] the coal crucible

### Day 3

Day 3 will be much like day 2, but we might also get to [[mowing]] and [[raking]]:

- [[grass saw]]
- [[thatching rake]] (or [[garden rake]])
- [[large closed-bottom flip-top tote|large closed-bottom flip-top totes]]
  - for [[containing]] the [[debris]] from mowing and raking

### Day 4

Day 4 we'll begin to clear the actual area where the tent will go.

### Day 5

Day 5 we'll set up the tent and a basic kitchen area.

- [[GAZ Barracks]]
- [[propane stove]]
- [[cast iron Dutch oven]]

Then moving forward we'll actually have the start of a home base.
