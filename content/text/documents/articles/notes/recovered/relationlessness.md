## Structure

Relationlessness is the predication cell

$$\phi^* = (X, p^*, R)$$

in the predicate category $\mathbf{C}$, where:

- $X$ is the subject-coordinate — the would-be configuration name *relationlessness* itself;
- $p^* \in \Pi$ is the predicate *has-no-relation*;
- $R$ is the object-coordinate — the relating-locus the cell's predicate operates on.

By [`relationlessness-relates`](../../prose/learning/claims/relationlessness-relates.md), articulating *relationlessness* is the assertion of $\phi^*$; its value $v(p^*)(X, R) >_k \bot$ in $A_{p^*}$.

## Role

Relationlessness is the *originating cell* of the predicate category. Its directed subject-object asymmetry — $\phi^* = (X, p^*, R) \neq (R, p^*, X)$ as cells — is the lopsidedness every subsequent cell inherits through the predicate-category morphisms (specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection). The category is the closure of $\phi^*$ under these morphisms.

The cell's existence forces the Leibniz answer: *nothing*, taken as the configurational absence, is itself a relation. There is no separate "absence-of-relation" slot in the category to occupy; the slot, in articulation, IS the cell.

## Distinction from void

Relationlessness is a cell. Void is a value. The originating cell relationlessness exists and has value above the bilattice bottom (by the claim); the value any cell takes at the knowledge-order bottom is void.

| | What it names | Stratum |
|---|---|---|
| relationlessness | the originating predication cell $\phi^*$ | cell-level (object of $\mathbf{C}$ via its triple) |
| void | the no-evidence value $\bot \in A_p$ | value-level (element of the fiber algebra) |

The two are duals: cell-existence (relationlessness relates, by the originating theorem) vs. cell-internal evidence-content (void is the admissible $\leq_k$-bottom). See [`void`](void.md).

## Position

Articulated nothing IS the originating cell of the predicate category. The Leibniz question — *why is there something rather than nothing?* — has no remainder once the would-be configurational absence is recognized as the directed asymmetric triple it is in articulation.
