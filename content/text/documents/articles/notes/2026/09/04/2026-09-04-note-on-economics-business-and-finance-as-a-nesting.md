## 5. What this asks of engineering

- The economic-systems layer reads resources and commodities across every surface (books, chain,
  traffic, compute) with the same reconciliation discipline the books now have: two witnesses or
  it is not settled, unknown is never zero.
- The business-systems layer is where circulation is measured: funnels, exchanges, the internal
  market, the games' economies, priced per crossing (wit) and settled per matched pair (tally).
- The financial-systems layer is the booking: ledgers, assertions, valuation, trading venues, and
  the mandate envelopes that let capital act (the meet-then-beat skill's authority-and-evidence
  loop).

The ordering of the day's work follows: books true, then books valued, then the capital systems
that act on them.
