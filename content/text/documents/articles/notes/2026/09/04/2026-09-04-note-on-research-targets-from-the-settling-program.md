This carrier records the research targets developed across 2026-09-03/04,
so they persist as a document rather than only in conversation. It follows the
[regrounding program](../../08/21/2026-08-21-the-regrounding-program.md)'s
anti-cosplay discipline stated in that note's section 0: each target adds a
falsifiable structural claim, states plainly that it is a framing
contribution to a live professional field rather than a claimed
resolution, and engages that field's literature by name. Every standalone
claim below is the session's synthesis, **labeled inference**; every
accepted result is cited; every house object is a path on disk; every
target names a falsifier and the theorem or instrument it forces.

## A. One target per regime science

The four targets below correspond to the four regime sciences named in
[Four Regimes of Natural Existence](../../../../../../../../services/natural-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/four-regimes-of-natural-existence.md)
(identified 2026-09-03 and corrected 2026-09-04): kinetic, quantum,
causal, and thermodynamic existence, each read from a distinct closure
regime over the ordinal `κ`.

### A1. Information theory — kinetic rung

**Accepted results.** Chaitin's Ω is the halting probability of a
universal prefix machine: a real number that is the limit of a
computable, increasing sequence of rationals, yet has no computable
modulus of convergence — equivalently, no algorithm can say in advance
how many bits of a program's runtime must be checked before a given bit
of Ω is settled (Chaitin 1975 [@chaitin1975]; Solovay's unpublished 1975
notes on this non-computability, as reported in Calude 2002
[@calude2002]; Downey and Hirschfeldt 2010 [@downeyhirschfeldt2010] give
the modern algorithmic-randomness treatment).

**House object.** `Dynamics/Flow/NSCFiring.lean`'s header defines a
non-Scott-continuous ("non-Scott", NSC) firing as one whose finality
certificate is a ∀-antecedent over arbitrarily longer programs — the
same shape as certifying a bit of Ω.

**Standalone claim (inference).** A settling's regime is bounded by the
Turing degree of its modulus of convergence: a settling whose finality
condition has a computable modulus sits at or below the causal (Scott)
regime, and a settling whose finality condition provably has no
computable modulus is a candidate inhabitant of the aperiodic regime —
which the ordinal route through `Closure/RegimeCofinality.lean`'s
`Ord.regimeOfOrdHonest_ne_aperiodic` proves is structurally unreachable
from any real `Ord` evidence. Redefining aperiodicity by
non-computable-modulus recognisability, rather than by countable
cofinality `ω₁`, would give the aperiodic regime its first non-stipulated
inhabitant in core Lean.

**Proof sketch / decisive check.** Exhibit a settling closure whose
finality predicate is Turing-equivalent to "bit n of Ω is 1," show its
modulus of convergence is not computable (by the standard Ω
non-computability argument), and check whether `RegimeCofinality`'s honest
classifier can be extended to recognize it without appeal to `ω₁`
directly.

**Falsifier.** A computable modulus of convergence for a settling the
corpus already classifies aperiodic (per the 2026-09-04 regime census
below) would refute the claim that non-computable-modulus recognisability
tracks the aperiodic regime.

**Forced instrument.** A `RegimeCofinality`-adjacent classifier keyed to
modulus-of-convergence computability rather than `ω₁`-cofinality, tested
first against the corpus's own `KineticTheory/Plasma/TopologicalWaveSheaf.lean`
`canonicalEdgeBand` datum — the sole existing `countableCofinality = false`
witness (regime census: 3 concrete `ClosureRegimeData` datums total, 0 of
them with `countableCofinality = false`).

### A2. Quantum theory — transfinite rung

**Accepted results.** Information causality — the amount of information
Bob can extract about Alice's data from m received classical bits plus
prior correlations cannot exceed m — recovers Tsirelson's bound
(Tsirelson 1980 [@tsirelson1980]) as a consequence, and is violated by
the Popescu-Rohrlich (PR) box (Popescu and Rohrlich 1994
[@popescurohrlich1994]) (Pawłowski, Paterek, Kaszlikowski, Scarani,
Winter, and Żukowski 2009 [@pawlowski2009]); van Dam showed PR-box
correlations would collapse communication complexity to a single bit
(van Dam 2005/2013 [@vandam2013]).

**House object.** `QuantumContextuality/CohomologicalContextuality.lean`
already proves, axiom-free, that the PR box has no global section
(`prBox_hasGlobalSection_iff`, line 290, discharged via
`hasGlobalSection_iff_coboundary` at line 326) for the finite CHSH-type
scenario.

**Standalone claim (inference).** Information causality is "acknowledgement
cannot exceed witness" stated for physics — the WIT/ACK reading developed
in the [2026-09-04 note on acknowledgement beside wit](../../../../../../../../services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/04/2026-09-04-note-on-acknowledgement-beside-wit.md):
m transmitted (witnessed) bits bound Bob's acknowledged information
gain, and the PR box is exactly the case where acknowledgement outstrips
witness — infinite uptake from one finite witness. Whether information
causality exactly singles out the quantum correlation set (as opposed to
merely excluding the PR box) is explicitly open in the outside literature
(the Navascués et al. "almost-quantum" set is a known near-miss the
house has not yet checked against); this caveat is carried forward, not
resolved here.

**Proof sketch / decisive check.** Recast `prBox_hasGlobalSection_iff`'s
non-existence result as an instance of an information-causality bound
for the CHSH scenario already formalized in the same file, and check
whether the Tsirelson-bound-respecting quantum correlations that do have
a coboundary witness are exactly those satisfying the recast bound.

**Falsifier.** A correlation the house's cohomological machinery
certifies as having a global section (no obstruction) that nonetheless
violates the recast information-causality bound.

**Forced instrument.** A Lean lemma in
`QuantumContextuality/CohomologicalContextuality.lean` stating the CHSH
information-causality inequality as a corollary of the existing
no-global-section proof, with the almost-quantum-set caveat recorded as
an open item rather than silently dropped.

### A3. Causal theory — Scott rung

**Accepted results.** The causal order of a spacetime (which events can
send light signals to which) determines its conformal geometry up to a
local rescaling (Malament 1977 [@malament1977]); Sorkin's causal-set
program adds discrete number to that order to recover full geometry,
under the slogan "order + number = geometry" (Sorkin 2003
[@sorkin2003]); Rideout and Sorkin gave a classical sequential growth
(CSG) dynamics for causal sets satisfying Bell causality (the
probability of an added event does not depend on anything to its future)
and discrete general covariance (Rideout and Sorkin 2000
[@rideoutsorkin2000]).

**House object.** `Cohomology/SettlingCohomologyH1.lean`'s `H⁰` is
documented as "a count of designated cells"; `CausalSets/Causal/RegimeSection.lean`
carries a past-count time function over a finite causal set.

**Standalone claim (inference).** A covariant CSG growth dynamics is a
confluent (Church-Rosser) closure, and the conformal factor Malament's
theorem cannot recover from causal order alone is exactly the `H⁰`
designated-cell count `RegimeSection` already computes from the growth
process's past structure.

**Proof sketch / decisive check.** Model a finite CSG run as a rewriting
system on causal sets (each growth step one rewrite), state Bell
causality as a local commutation condition between independent growth
steps, and check whether that local commutation is sufficient for global
confluence by the standard diamond-lemma argument.

**Falsifier.** A covariant CSG dynamics satisfying Bell causality and
discrete general covariance whose deterministic skeleton (fixing the
labeling randomness) fails confluence — two orderings of the same
independent growth steps reaching different causal sets.

**Forced instrument.** A confluence theorem for finite causal-set growth
closures in `CausalSets/`, proved from Bell causality as a diamond-lemma
hypothesis, with `RegimeSection`'s past-count function shown to be
well-defined independent of growth order exactly when confluence holds.

### A4. Thermodynamics — strict rung

**Accepted results.** The Jarzynski equality relates nonequilibrium work
to equilibrium free-energy differences (Jarzynski 1997
[@jarzynski1997]); Crooks's fluctuation theorem sharpens it to a
detailed work-distribution symmetry (Crooks 1999 [@crooks1999]); Sagawa
and Ueda extended the second law to feedback-controlled systems, `W ≥ ΔF
− kT·I` where `I` is mutual information gained by measurement (Sagawa
and Ueda 2010 [@sagawaueda2010]); Toyabe et al. demonstrated this
information-to-energy conversion experimentally (Toyabe et al. 2010
[@toyabe2010]).

**House object.** `Dynamics/Flow/FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean`'s
`budget_splits` (line 1121: `Φ₀ = commits + mints + 2·jumps`, both spends
bounded by seed energy) and `Thermodynamics/BoltzmannEntropy.lean`'s
`htheorem` (line 125: a smoothing exchange never increases the
multiset-invariant `H`); the historical engineering note's XCC-007
([former MetroPlat low-level design](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/15/2026-07-15-note-on-low-level-design.md),
section 8.1) proposed requiring any exchange between the system's two
currencies to name a log rate as a 1-cochain, so holonomy around a
currency circuit reads as arbitrage rather than hidden error.

**Standalone claim (inference).** The WIT-ACK exchange rate XCC-007
requires has a physical instance: `log rate = β·I`, the same mutual
information term Sagawa-Ueda's bound charges against free energy, with
`β` the network's inverse temperature.

**Proof sketch / decisive check.** Build a finite detailed-balanced
reaction network (using `ReactionNetworks/DetailedBalance.lean`'s
`Reversible`/`DetailedBalance`/`detailedBalance_iff_netFlux_zero`, which
already proves detailed balance is a fixed point of the Euler flow via
`detailedBalance_euler_fixed`), compute its variational free energy
using `Calculus/Probability.lean`, and check whether `Φ₀`'s
`budget_splits` decomposition coarse-grains that free energy under the
proposed exchange rate.

**Falsifier.** The ledger's `Φ₀` failing to coarse-grain a finite
model's variational free energy under any choice of `β` — the same check
target C3 below runs from the neuroscience side.

**Forced instrument.** A finite-network Crooks theorem stated over
`ReactionNetworks/DetailedBalance.lean`'s `Reversible` pairs, connecting
`netFlux` to a fluctuation symmetry and citing `htheorem`'s
non-increase result as the corresponding H-theorem bound.

## C. The three cross-field targets

**C1 — money forgets exactly the cycle space of the obligation graph.**
"Money is memory": a monetary system can substitute for the social
memory required to track and enforce individual promises (Kocherlakota
1998 [@kocherlakota1998]); the historical record of tally-based credit
clearing supports the same reading (Graeber 2011 [@graeber2011]). House:
`Cohomology/CoordinationCohomology.lean`'s `H1rank` (line 291: `(cells +
components) - entities`) and `LandauerEntropy.lean`'s `landauerBits`
(line 44: defined as `N.H1rank`) — the
[2026-08-21 note on money as witness forgetting](../../08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-money-as-witness-forgetting.md)
already develops this line. Standalone claim (inference): the number of
independent obligation-cycles a netting system can clear without
individual tracking is bounded by `H1rank` — the kernel of the
obligation graph's boundary map — via a one-line argument: a cycle in
the obligation graph nets to zero exactly when its boundary vanishes,
and `H1rank` counts the graph's independent cycles by the standard
Euler-characteristic identity `cells - entities + components`.
Falsifier: a real netting system whose gross-to-net reduction ratio
exceeds the cycle rank `H1rank` computed from its own obligation graph.

**C2 — impossibility results as non-Scott firings of the acknowledgement
closure.** Common-knowledge and coordinated-attack results show
simultaneous common knowledge cannot be attained over an unreliable
channel (Halpern and Moses 1990 [@halpernmoses1990]; Dwork and Moses
1990 [@dworkmoses1990]); consensus is impossible with even one faulty
process in an asynchronous system (Fischer, Lynch, and Paterson 1985
[@fischer1985]); Chandra and Toueg's unreliable failure detectors
(Chandra and Toueg 1996 [@chandratoueg1996]) and Chandra, Hadzilacos,
and Toueg's weakest failure detector Ω for consensus (Chandra,
Hadzilacos, and Toueg 1996 [@chandrahadzilacostoueg1996]) characterize
exactly how much extra information restores solvability. Standalone
claim (inference): these are non-Scott firings of an acknowledgement
closure in the sense of `Dynamics/Flow/NSCFiring.lean`'s header
definition (a finality certificate that is a ∀-antecedent over
arbitrarily longer message chains), and Ω is the least restriction that
makes the acknowledgement closure Scott-continuous again. Decisive
check: state and decide the finite (bounded-process, bounded-round)
case, where the closure is automatically Scott-continuous, as an
instrument distinguishing the finite-decidable case from the
general-undecidable one. Falsifier: a failure detector strictly weaker
than Ω (in the Chandra-Hadzilacos-Toueg partial order) that still
restores Scott-continuity to the acknowledgement closure.

**C3 — precision as ACK clocking.** The free-energy principle treats
perception and action as minimizing variational free energy under a
generative model (Friston 2010 [@friston2010]); precision-weighting of
prediction errors implements attention (Feldman and Friston 2010
[@feldmanfriston2010]); predictive coding in visual cortex is the
concrete neural mechanism (Rao and Ballard 1999 [@raoballard1999]);
additive-increase/multiplicative-decrease (AIMD) congestion control is
the network-protocol analogue of adaptively clocking acknowledgement
rate (Chiu and Jain 1989 [@chiujain1989]). Standalone claim (inference):
precision-weighting is ACK clocking — the rate at which a generative
model's predictions are allowed to update in response to
acknowledged prediction error, structurally the same throttle AIMD
applies to a network sender. Decisive check: compute variational free
energy on a small finite generative model (a handful of hidden states
and observations) and compare its trajectory against the ledger's `Φ₀`
(`Dynamics/Flow/FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean`'s `budget_splits`) across a
settling run on the same model — the same instrument target A4's
falsifier names, run here from the neuroscience side. Falsifier: a
finite generative model where `Φ₀` and the computed variational free
energy diverge in trend across settling.
