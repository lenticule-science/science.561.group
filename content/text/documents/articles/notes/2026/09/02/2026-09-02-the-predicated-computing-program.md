## 3. The thermodynamic thesis (the moat)

The incumbent class performs energy descent on a learned, dense,
unauditable landscape and pays a full dense forward pass for every
token, with no account of what it destroys. This machine's inference is
local (profiles/pores — Theorem 3.5 of the taproot), monotone (nothing
is recomputed; knowledge only accumulates), and self-accounting (every
settlement's Landauer bill is its H¹ discharge; every sample logs its
excluded mass as wit-records). Energy efficiency is not a feature of
the class; it is the physics of the class. In a world where inference
energy is the binding constraint, the class competes on accounted
joules — and it is the only entrant that can hand its customer the
ledger.
