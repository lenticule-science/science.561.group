## The program

Lenticule Science is an agentic digital research lab intended to meet and
beat the software classes it touches. The knowledge substrate treats
`[file: [key: value]]` as `[subject: [predicate: object]]`; Mark, Utterance,
and the mathematics compose into more complex artifacts, and the published
form of that flow — real OWL on the web — is a corporate asset. Study the
theory, especially proof-path addressing, before choosing an architecture.
Agent sessions must not invent biographical claims about the human in either
direction.

Nearest usable outcome: an agent (or web visitor) traverses the science
corpus as a typed, stratified, provenance-carrying graph — one real note
admitted, settled, served, its terms dereferenceable on the web, the run's
receipts priced.

## Doctrine

D1. The admission codec is constitutional and two-legged: forward,
carriers to utterances/rows; backward, the settled substrate computes
demands on authorship (frontier-derived). The backward leg's seam ships in
v1 even if its first implementation is simple.
D2. Two-orders thesis: the substrate keeps the knowledge order (grow-only,
witnessed, refutations retained) distinct from the truth order. Deletion
never; every served answer reconstructable with full evidential history.
This is the program's competitive claim: belief-trackers must delete;
knowledge-trackers may only grow, and are auditable in a way no
belief-tracker can be.
D3. Strata are read-time projections with promotion receipts; σ and Δ run
as separate passes; rows stay level-0 append-only.
D4. The autonomic loop is governed metabolically: discharge an obstruction
when expected anti-Landauer yield exceeds discovery-plus-repair cost.
Settling passes emit their register ledger and H⁰/H¹ counts as receipts;
the index balance is a run-integrity check.
D5. Papers are procured external evidence — the one genuinely independent
verification channel — with triple-bound citations (carrier, world,
evidence) behind a runnable gate; the vocabulary server is the public
cone-leg (dereferenceable term IRIs; ttl/JSON-LD generated from settled
glossary, never hand-declared).
D6. No LLM-extracted claims enter the substrate; rule-based admission
only; unauthorized frontmatter keys become typed refusal rows; consent
status is a first-class admitted field.
