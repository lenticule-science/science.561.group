## The settled reading

1. **The mathematics is the product.** Obsmetrologia's functions (e.g.
   `CoverNerve.H0rank`/`H1rank`, `Landauer.landauerBits`, the KCBS/CHSH
   values, the allocation solver) are the assets. Lean is the persuasion
   device that got agents to take the math seriously — never a requirement,
   never a badge, never special.
2. **A die is the function, implementation-agnostic.** A hand-rolled JS
   implementation is a perfectly lawful die. The sin is not JS; the sin is
   functions not seated as hireable, priced, single-capability enterprises
   on the market (agent card, contract, x402), so agents cite the math
   ("Certified by Lean!") instead of invoking it. Fab dies and the badge
   behavior dies with them, because invoking becomes cheaper than citing.
3. **"Obsmetric Lean proof" is one service line** — a verification
   instrument an enterprise can hire or sell about a die, like a test lab.
   It is priced and optional, never a gate on consumers. Nothing downstream
   ever "requires Lean."
4. **Badge prose is banned.** "Lean-certified"/"formally verified" claims in
   prose or docstrings without a checked linkage were already ruled TROPHY
   CRUFT by the 2026-07-11 operator correction
   (`.../2026/07/11/2026-07-11-research-on-metroplat-architecture-model.md`).
   Purge on sight; never add.

## What a cast die IS (2026-07-24 follow-on session)

"One script edited" is exactly wrong. Each die is cast whole, all parts at
once — literally each one:

- its own GitHub repository
- licensed
- documented
- released and packaged
- x402 endpoints — on Hive, Base, other chains
- and the Union consumes those same endpoints for its own computation

A function reachable only inside another service (e.g. an allocator buried
in Aagaard) is NOT a die — it is unreleased inventory. The absence of this
network of fully-cast dies is why judgment runs on vibes: there is nothing
to hire, so agents improvise. Casting order stands: cast the
single-capability die, mint its enterprise, form it as a node. The standing
gap: almost none of the Union's code is written to this standard, and the
math cannot be sold until it is. Precedent for the market face:
paid-ephemeris-gateway (live paid x402 Base/USDC offer, requalified 6-hourly
via GitHub Actions).
