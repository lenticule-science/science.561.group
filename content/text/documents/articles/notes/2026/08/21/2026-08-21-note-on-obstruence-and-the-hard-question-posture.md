## 1. The ob- convergence

obstruence, obsmetrologia — back at speech again.
The convergence is technical, not punning. Obstruents are the phonetic
class of speech sounds produced by obstructing the airstream (stops,
fricatives, affricates) — the consonantal skeleton carrying most of
speech's contrastive load. The Latin root runs three ways through the
program: ob-servare (to watch toward: observation), ob-struere (to build
against: obstruction, the H¹ classes), obstruent (the sound made by
building against the breath).

The deeper alignment: the Witnessed Field theory locates linguistic
identity in obstruction classes (community variation that cannot be
flattened), and phonetics locates phonemic content in obstruents — in the
controlled buildings-against that differentiate meanings. Speech is wind
through a hollow with deliberate obstruction, which is the mechanism of a
flute; the tongue fingers the holes. The grove's harvest image (feelings
played through the hollow of finished structure) and the articulatory
mechanics of consonants are one diagram. [Alignment graded: the phonetic
facts are well-established; the identification of the two senses of
obstruction is a framing observation, Research-grade, offered because it
keeps proving generative rather than because it is yet load-bearing.]

## 2. The hard-question posture

Generalization, stated with its discipline attached: most famously-hard
problems are chronic gluing failures that their fields flatten by one of
three classical moves — delete one section (eliminativism), refuse the
site (dualism), or force the gluing (reductive identification).
Obsmetrologia's first move differs: measure the obstruction and TYPE it.

The typology:

1. **Genuine H¹** — an irreducible structural obstruction. Not solved but
   inhabited knowingly; it becomes load-bearing structure (as dialect
   variation did in the Witnessed Field; as the Hubble tension may prove
   to be for cosmological measurement).
2. **Witness error** — systematics: one section's evidence chain is
   contaminated. Belief fusion with provenance localizes where.
3. **Gauge artifact** — chart confusion: the rival accounts were
   coordinates on one invariant, and the problem dissolves under the
   transition map (as competence/performance did).

Candidate applications, each strictly a FRAMING contribution to a live
professional field and never a claimed resolution: the Hard Problem of
consciousness (first- and third-person report as sections over the same
events, introspection admitted as a witness modality with provenance;
whether the explanatory gap is type 1, 2, or 3 has never before been a
well-posed question); the quantum measurement problem (interpretations as
gauge choices over one observation field — the interpretation wars
testable for type-3-ness); free will (plausibly type 3 across description
levels, showable rather than assertable). 

Discipline (inherits the Regrounding program's anti-cosplay rules):
claiming to solve a hard problem is how frameworks die of embarrassment;
the offer is to make the disagreement's structure measurable. Any
application that adds no falsifiable structure or measurement protocol is
decoration and gets demoted. Each application engages its field's native
literature; the typology is proposed to the field, not imposed on it.
