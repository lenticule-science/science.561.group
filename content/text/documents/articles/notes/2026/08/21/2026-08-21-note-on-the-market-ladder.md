clearing, open interest. Five rungs, each with its referee.

**Rung 1 — charge-thick pricing (near-mainstream).** Price is the
market's H0; the bilattice adds the second dimension: (pro, con) with
independent witness mass. Identical prices with different charge
structures are different objects. Known orphan anomalies unify here:
analyst-dispersion return prediction (Diether et al. 2002), divergence
of opinion (Miller 1977). Instrument: a charge surface over the book
(options skew, depth imbalance, holding-time-weighted volume as con-side
mass); volume-profile TA re-founded as provenance-weighted
witness-density. Referee: does charge structure at fixed price predict
vol regime and drift beyond existing dispersion measures?

**Rung 2 — the strategy foam and settlement kinetics.** Strategies as

PEAD as slow settling — drift duration predicted by pre-event charge

unpaired_disclination_survives_descent, some market holonomy is
UNDISCHARGEABLE. Prediction: anomalies split topologically —
dischargeable (post-publication decay, McLean-Pontiff) versus market
disclinations (closed-end fund discounts, capital-control ADR spreads:
persistent for decades, never fully closed). The durable edges are
topological; classify before allocating. Referee: obstruction-type
classification against the anomaly-persistence record.

**Rung 4 — the overnight dream and the Landauer floor.** The documented
