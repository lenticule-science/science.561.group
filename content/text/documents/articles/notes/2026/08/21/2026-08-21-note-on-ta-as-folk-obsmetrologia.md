# TA as Folk Obsmetrologia

The standard applied to TCM applies to technical analysis: a practice
tradition surviving long selection (Dow a century; candlestick notation
since Honma's eighteenth-century rice markets) is pre-instrumental, not
pre-scientific. Chartists are the longest-running observers of the
market's witness record, naming configurations without a mathematics.
The re-founding dictionary, each entry with mechanism and referee:

1. **Candlesticks = folk charge-notation.** Body: net charge movement;
   wicks: witnessed refutations (probes that failed to settle); doji:
   paraconsistent standoff; engulfing: reversal with greater witness
   mass. A folk RMN in ink.
2. **Support/resistance = settled-charge density; role-reversal =
   charge conjugation** (trapped witnesses flip sign at breakeven).
   Referee: reversal strength scales with provenance-weighted volume at
   the level (holding-time weighting), not chart geometry.
3. **Volume lore = the witnessing axiom.** Unwitnessed moves are
   bottom-information-order and settle back; ties to the
   overnight-as-dream claim (thin-volume regimes behave like
   provenance-relaxed fusion).
4. **Head-and-shoulders = charge-exhaustion sequence** — the lore's own
   right-shoulder volume fade is the witness-mass divergence. Referee:
   pattern success conditions on exhaustion measures, not shape.
   Field's-own-turn citation: Lo-Mamaysky-Wang 2000.
5. **Divergence trading = charge-thickness trading with crude proxies**
   (oscillators as poor man's bilattice). Upgrade the proxy, keep the
   insight.
6. **Momentum = settlement velocity.** TA's founding claim (trends
   persist) is academia's most robust anomaly (Jegadeesh-Titman);
   priority belongs to the practitioners.
7. **Multi-timeframe analysis = sheaf over chart resolutions**;
   alignment = cross-scale gluing; NEW INSTRUMENT: persistent-homology
   TA — a level/cycle is real iff it persists across the timeframe
   filtration (TDA-finance cousin: Gidea-Katz). Buildable in
   pymetrologic.
8. **Elliott's phi-ratios = candidate folk detection of the golden
   gap** (lambda_5 = 2 - phi, the dervish's irrational floor). Referee,
   clean and binary: do the pymetrologic settling/foam simulations
   spontaneously produce phi-retracement statistics? Whirl = Elliott
   witnessed something; no whirl = demote with respect.

Meta-claim: transmission through generations of practitioners is itself
a settling process; patterns that survive it are thick by survivorship
and earn the disclination benefit-of-the-doubt — investigation before
dismissal. All Research-grade; referees named.
