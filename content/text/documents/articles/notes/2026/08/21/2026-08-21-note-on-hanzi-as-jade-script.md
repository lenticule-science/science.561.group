# Hanzi as Jade Script

The claim: writing systems are gauge choices on the witnessed field, and
the great typological divide — alphabet versus character — is a choice of
WHICH stratum to coordinate. Alphabets fix the phoneme gauge: thin
coordinates on the sound layer, cheap to learn, fast to write, mute about
meaning. Hanzi fix the meme gauge: each character coordinates a
charge-cell directly, and — the decisive property — carries its witnesses
visibly. Radicals are witness marks embedded in the glyph (the water in
河, the heart in 恨); phonetic components are fossilized sound-witnesses;
etymology is tabasheer — the crystallized evidence of the settlement that
produced the form, readable in the hollow of the character.

By the thickness doctrine, hanzi are the jade-stage script: maximally
settled, un-approximable by thin systems (every romanization is a lossy
projection — pinyin is the script's provenance-forgetting embedding,
useful exactly the way embeddings are), and durable for the same reason
jade is: three millennia of continuous legibility because the meaning
gauge outlives sound change that bankrupts phoneme gauges. The native
tradition already holds the typology: the 六書 liùshū (six
character-formation principles) is a witnessed taxonomy of HOW characters
carry their charges — pictographic, indicative, compound-indicative,
phono-semantic, and the two transfer categories — i.e., a classification
of witness-structures, made by the tradition itself.

Pedagogy implication (for Alotl's hanzi work): teach characters as
witnessed settlements, not stroke lists — every character introduced with
its radicals-as-witnesses and its etymology-as-tabasheer, so the learner
acquires the provenance and not just the glyph. This makes a testable
prediction, runnable in the Alotl practice records: provenance-first
character teaching should beat rote-form teaching on retention at
FSRS-measured intervals, because thick items with independent fibers
survive partial forgetting the way graded culm walls survive stress. This
is an open question rather than a settled finding — if provenance-first
teaching does not outperform rote teaching on retention, the framing would
lose its pedagogical claim and keep only its typological one.
