# Immunity as Belief Board

lymphocytes — a paraconsistent, provenance-carrying epistemic organ whose
subject is molecular identity. The dictionary:

- **Self/non-self as settled charge**, established during thymic
  education: the negative-selection process is literal settlement — a
  charge configuration over the antigen space reaching its fixed point
  before deployment.
- **Antibodies as witnessed refutations**: an antibody is a charged
  claim (this shape: refused) carrying its provenance (the clone lineage

  somatic hypermutation is the abductive spiral run in germinal
  centers, variation speculating and antigen witnessing).
- **Tolerance as residual-uncertainty classification**: commensal and
  food antigens carry nonzero raw conflict yet are correctly filed as
  residual uncertainty rather than named conflicts — the exact

  false conflict-naming** — the classifier promoting residual
  uncertainty to named refutation against the self's own settled
  sections. **Allergy is an overcharged witness**: evidence weighting
  gone disproportionate to the claim.
- **Immune memory as jade**: long-lived plasma cells are settled charges
  maintained for decades — vaccination is deliberate settlement via
  curated fixture evidence.
