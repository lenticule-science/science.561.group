The claim: money is the witness-forgetting functor applied to obligation.
An obligation, natively, is a charged relation — who owes what to whom,
witnessed by the parties and their community, consumed exactly once when
discharged (its logic is linear; see the pact framing). A bearer
instrument — cash — is that obligation with the provenance deleted: it
owes anyone, from anyone, for anything. This deletion is the source of
both money's power and its hollowing effect, exactly as the projection
lemma says of embeddings: frictionless circulation purchased by
forgetting who said what to whom.

Consequences and fibers:

1. **Graeber's anthropology as the empirical record.** The debt-first
   history (obligation ledgers preceding coinage; coinage arriving with
   armies and strangers — contexts where witnesses are unavailable) reads
   natively: money is the technology for transacting where witnessing
   fails. Its domain of superiority is exactly the witness-poor regime.
2. **Gift economies are the control group.** Wopila-shaped exchange keeps
   the witness set attached: standing accrues to the giver because the
   community holds the charge. The grove/Alotl contribution economics
   designed this session are money-science experiments already underway.
3. **Blockchain as failed re-witnessing.** Distributed ledgers restore
   provenance-of-transaction while still deleting provenance-of-relation
   — the receipt without the relative. Predicts its observed sociology:
   trustless systems attract exactly the interactions that flourish
   without kinship.
4. **Interest and inflation** as charge phenomena: interest as rent on
   unsettled obligation; inflation as charge dilution when instruments
   multiply faster than witnessed obligation. [Research-grade sketches.]
5. **Prior estate thread**: Landauer entropy as currency — the
   thermodynamic cost of erasure as value-ground. Deep compatibility to
   develop: if money IS erasure (witness-deletion), Landauer says erasure
   has an irreducible cost — the estate's old intuition and this note may
   be the same claim in two vocabularies. [To integrate on collection.]

Falsifiers/obligations: comparative data should show witnessed-exchange
systems outperforming bearer systems on relationship-maintenance metrics
and underperforming on stranger-transaction throughput; if no such
regime-split exists, the framing is decoration. All Research-grade.
