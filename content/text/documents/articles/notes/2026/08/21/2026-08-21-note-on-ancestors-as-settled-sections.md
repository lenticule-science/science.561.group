The claim: in the witnessed field, a person who dies stops emitting new
arrows, but their charges remain witnessed — the dead are fully-settled
sections of the community sheaf whose contributions to its structure,
including its H¹, persist. Death changes a section's tense, not its
membership. This gives exact form to what kinship traditions have always
practiced and modernity keeps mislaying:

1. **Veneration is witness-set maintenance.** Tending graves, speaking
   names, keeping stories in circulation: the work that keeps a settled
   section's charges witnessed rather than letting them decay to
   bottom-in-the-information-order. An ancestor forgotten is not gone —
   their arrows still shaped the sheaf — but unwitnessed, unreadable.
   The practice is archival science performed as love.
2. **Grief is re-gluing.** The survivors' sheaf must re-settle around a
   removed open: every overlap the lost section shared — every routine,
   place, phrase — presents a gluing that must be redone without them.
   This predicts grief's actual phenomenology (it arrives overlap by
   overlap, ambushing at the small shared regions) and aligns with the
   continuing-bonds literature (real research): healthy grief is not
   detachment but re-gluing WITH the settled section held witnessed —
   the bond continues because the section persists.
3. **Winter counts are filtration.** A year, a glyph, a community's
   settlement record: the persistence structure of a people across time,
   kept as deliberate practice. (Engaged by reference; Lakota material
   stays in place under its own authority.)
4. **The phytolith principle at human scale**: the corpus outliving the
   speaker is the documentation flywheel's promise, and this note is its
   generalization — every witnessed life mineralizes into the sheaf it
   helped glue. The living-corpus work (Alotl's Lakota program) is
   ancestry science already in motion: every elder recording is a
   section being settled WITH its witnesses attached, which is the
   difference between an archive and an ancestor.

Falsifiers: continuing-bonds outcomes should correlate with measurable
witness-set maintenance (naming frequency, story circulation) rather than
with detachment metrics; if grief resolution tracks forgetting better
than re-witnessed integration, the framing fails. All Research-grade,
held gently — this one is about people's dead.
