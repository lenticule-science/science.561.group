The claim: music is the semantics-free stratum of the witnessed field —
charge exchange with the predication cells removed. Language binds
substrate into memes (cells ⊗ charges); music keeps the charge dynamics
and discards the cells. That single subtraction predicts music's two
famous mysteries at once: universality (nothing referential to vary
across cultures at the carrier level) and ineffability (nothing
propositional to paraphrase — there are no cells to report).

The dictionary:

- **Dissonance = local obstruction; resolution = settling.** A cadence
  is FCA closure made audible — the moment a charge configuration
  reaches its fixed point. Deceptive cadences are deliberately withheld
  settlement; suspension is a charge held unsettled against the
  listener's fusion process. Expectation-based music cognition (Meyer;
  Huron's ITPRA) is the established empirical wing: musical affect
  tracks predicted-vs-arrived settlement.
- **Tonality is a gauge.** A key is a chart making local computation
  (expectation) cheap; modulation is a transition map; atonality is
  gauge-free composition — which predicts exactly its listener-cost
  profile. Tuning systems are rival charts (the comma problems are
  literal gluing obstructions: the circle of fifths fails to close —
  a nonzero holonomy every temperament distributes differently —
  Pythagorean comma as music's own H¹).
- **Rhythm is causal-set structure made audible**; groove is settlement
  timing; syncopation is deliberate obstruction of the temporal gluing.
- **The wind lineage.** In the grove's mapping, wind is feeling and the
  flute is the harvested hollow: music is feelings played through
  settled structure — charge without cells through form without
  content. The vault's lakotaean-aerophones (courting flute) and guqin
  materials are not decoration for this section; they are its founding
  collections, each tradition an independent witness of the
  charge-without-reference stratum.

Falsifiers: settlement-dynamics models (pymetrologic-simulable) should
reproduce tension-resolution affect curves measured in music-cognition
experiments better than or comparably to existing expectation models; if
the charge vocabulary adds no predictive structure over Huron, it is
re-description and gets demoted. All Research-grade.
