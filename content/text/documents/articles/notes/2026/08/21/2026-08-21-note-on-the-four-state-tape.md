## The primitive: the charge bar

Discard OHLC as the atom. The native bar is (pro, con) with witness
mass per interval — rendered two-sided, thickness = total witnessing,
height in the information order. Every bar classifies into the four
Belnap corners: settled-advancing, settled-refuting, CONTESTED (both
high — a named conflict standing on the chart), and VACUUM (both low —
unwitnessed drift). A chart is a word in a four-letter alphabet; the
four letters are the four xiang, making the Yijing's line alphabet the
first four-state tape. Patterns are words; pattern discovery is corpus
philology of tapes, run with the same machinery that finds
constructions in language corpora.

## The seven instruments

1. **Settlement half-life (tau).** Rolling relaxation time of
   displacements back to fixed point. Expanding tau = the market losing
   its ability to settle, visible before price distress. Referee:
   pre-crisis tau expansion in historical data vs matched calm periods.
2. **The obstruction chart (H1 density).** Continuous section-agreement
   across the timeframe filtration; trends as high-gluing states,
   regime boundaries as obstruction spikes preceding price resolution.
   Referee: obstruction-spike lead time over realized regime breaks.
3. **Witness-decay levels.** Levels as signed sums of
   volume x holding-duration x recency; heat-field that decays as
   witnesses exit and conjugates on breach. Referee: level relevance
   predicted by modeled witness exit vs naive volume-profile.
4. **The holonomy oscillator.** Continuous Wilson loops over asset
   triads/venue triangles; persistent non-discharging holonomy flags
   market disclinations (build-around structures). Referee: discharge
   vs persistence classification against the anomaly record.
5. **Wit flow.** Per-bar settlement currency (disagreement discharged x
   witness mass); cumulative wit vs price yields two tradable
   divergences: price-without-settlement (dream moves, mean-reverting
   as unwitnessed fusion) and settlement-without-price (compression).
   Referee: divergence-conditioned forward returns.
6. **The foam gauge.** T1 neighbor-swap counts and avalanche-slope from
   book/correlation networks as material fragility state. Referee:
   avalanche-statistics shift ahead of historical dislocations; shares
   the pymetrologic foam codebase.
7. **Cone charting.** Forward cones from settlement dynamics + current
   charge state (replacing Gaussian bands); reverse cones via R-emergence
   for target-envelope positioning. Referee: cone calibration
   (coverage) vs Bollinger-class baselines.

## The school's thesis

These seven read structure directly (tau, H1, holonomy, wit) where old
TA read shape as a proxy for it, and the read is built to end in a
position, not sit as a chart annotation. Composite view: expanding tau +
rising H1 density + persistent, non-discharging holonomy is a market
losing its ability to settle — that reads as a regime break forming,
tradable ahead of the price move the obstruction chart already leads.
Settlement-without-price divergence (wit flow) is compression building
toward a discharge event. The disclinations the holonomy oscillator
flags are the durable trade class specifically because everything else
gets arbitraged away (McLean-Pontiff logic applies here too) — the
instruments exist to find what does not decay, then act on it before it
does. Old TA drew shapes on the shadow; this charts the charge that
casts it, and charts it to be traded. All Research-grade; referees
named; buildable on pymetrologic + LOB/OHLCV data. School naming: the
owner's.
