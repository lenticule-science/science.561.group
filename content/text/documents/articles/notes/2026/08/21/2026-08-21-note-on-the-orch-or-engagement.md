# The Orch-OR Engagement

Purpose: open a falsifiable engagement with Penrose-Hameroff Orchestrated
Objective Reduction (Orch-OR), not adjudicate it. Parts 1-3 report the
theory and its record as honestly as the search results support, each
claim graded. Part 4 states the estate's own angle as a **framing
contribution** — a testable prediction Orch-OR's settlement-reading would
make, addressed to neural dynamics, offered for its own falsifiability
and explicitly **not** offered as evidence Orch-OR is correct. Part 5
grades what a real research program on either front would need.

Grading key used throughout: **[established]** — settled physics/math or
directly reported data; **[theoretical]** — a model's prediction, not yet
directly confirmed; **[experimental, contested]** — data exists and is
disputed in the literature; **[framing]** — an interpretive move, true or
false only relative to further work; **[speculative]** — stated as such
by its own proponents.

## 1. The theory's actual claims

Orch-OR has two separable halves, and most of the confusion in both
popular coverage and internal estate chatter comes from not keeping them
apart.

**Penrose's half: Objective Reduction (OR).** This is a proposed physical
mechanism for wavefunction collapse, independent of any brain claim.
Penrose argues that a quantum superposition of two mass distributions
carries an intrinsic gravitational self-energy difference E_G — because
general relativity and quantum linearity are in "profound conflict" over
what spacetime geometry a superposed mass sources — and that this
ill-definedness forces spontaneous, *objective* collapse (not
observer-triggered) after a characteristic time

  τ ≈ ℏ / E_G,  with E_G = G m² / a

(the Diósi-Penrose form; G the gravitational constant, m the superposed
mass, a the spatial separation) [established as a stated proposal;
Penrose, *Shadows of the Mind*, 1994; formalized further with Diósi's
independent 1980s work]. Larger, more separated superpositions collapse
faster; a proton-scale superposition takes cosmological time, a
paperclip-scale one collapses in roughly a Planck time's remove from
instantly. OR is a candidate solution to the measurement problem, tested
today by tabletop gravitational-decoherence experiments unrelated to
biology [established as an active experimental program; e.g. optomechanical
tests of mass-dependent decoherence].

**Hameroff's half: orchestration.** Hameroff, an anesthesiologist,
proposed that microtubules — the cytoskeletal lattices built from
tubulin dimers, present in every neuron — are structured to sustain
quantum superpositions of tubulin conformational states long enough for
OR to trigger at a rate matched to neural processing (proposed ~25 ms,
i.e. roughly 40 Hz gamma-band), and that this orchestrated, biologically
timed OR event *is* a discrete moment of conscious experience. The
"orchestration" claim requires three things to hold simultaneously:
tubulins must support long-lived superposition, microtubules must
shield/organize that superposition (via ordered water, counter-ion Debye
layers, or the lattice's proposed excitonic/vibrational structure), and
the resulting OR timing must land in a physiologically relevant range
[speculative as originally proposed; the specific mechanism has been
revised repeatedly by its own authors, see Part 3].

These are logically separable: OR could be right about physics while
orchestration is wrong about biology, or vice versa. Most of the
criticism record below targets orchestration, not OR itself.

## 2. The criticism record

**Tegmark's decoherence objection (2000).** Max Tegmark modeled
superposed tubulin states and calculated decoherence timescales of
roughly 10⁻¹³ to 10⁻²⁰ seconds — many orders of magnitude too fast for
any 25 ms orchestration to complete, let alone repeat at 40 Hz. This
became the standard citation for dismissing Orch-OR outright
[established as published, widely cited].

**Hagan, Hameroff & Tuszynski's response (2002).** The authors argued
Tegmark's model targeted the wrong physical picture — a soliton
delocalized across the full microtubule, rather than Penrose-Hameroff's
actual proposal of separation at the scale of atomic nuclei within
individual tubulins — and that Tegmark's formula used the wrong
dielectric constant for the intracellular environment. Recalculating
with proposed shielding mechanisms (ordered water, the Debye counter-ion
layer), they obtained decoherence times of roughly 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁴ seconds
[published rebuttal, established as such]. This closes several orders of
magnitude of the gap but still falls short of 25 ms by roughly a factor
of 100-1000 — a real narrowing, not a resolution, and the estate should
not round this up.

**Reimers, McKemmish, McKenzie, Mark & Hush (2009), *Physical Review E*
80, 021912.** A harder, chemistry-grounded objection: using known tubulin
structural and dynamical data, they argued the specific mechanism Orch-OR
requires (coherent superposition of tubulin conformational states acting
as qubits) is not physically realizable in real tubulin — not just too
fast-decohering but built on an incorrect biochemical picture of what
tubulin conformational change even is — and that no modification
consistent with known tubulin biology could repair it [established as
published; this is the most technically serious standing objection].
Electronic-state decoherence estimates in tubulin dimers land in the
10 fs-30 ps range, some 12+ orders of magnitude short of 25 ms.

**Hameroff & Penrose's reply (2014), "Consciousness in the Universe: A
Review of the 'Orch OR' Theory," *Physics of Life Reviews* 11, 39-78,**
with a companion volume of invited peer commentaries and a reply to
critics in the same issue. This is the current canonical statement of
the revised theory: orchestration now leans on the tryptophan/aromatic
ring network's coherent excitonic and vibrational structure (rather than
the earlier tubulin-conformational-qubit picture McKemmish et al.
targeted), proposed ordered-water and Debye-layer shielding, and reported
anesthetic-microtubule binding as independent support. The exchange is
genuinely unresolved in the literature: proponents hold that the 2009
objection targeted an outdated version of the model; critics hold that
the revision still has not closed the decoherence gap with a mechanism
anyone outside the Orch-OR camp has independently verified [experimental,
contested].

Honest summary of where this stands in 2026: OR-the-physics is a live,
respectable minority position tested by ongoing gravity-decoherence
experiments unrelated to biology. Orchestration-the-biology remains the
most criticized part of the theory, has been revised at least twice under
pressure from specific, technically serious objections, and has narrowed
but not closed its central timescale gap. The recent empirical work in
Part 3 bears on orchestration, not on OR itself.

## 3. Recent empirical hooks (verified, graded)

Search-verified as of 2026-08-21. Each entry: citation, what was actually
shown, and the grade.

**3.1 UV superradiance in tryptophan networks — Babcock, Kurian,
Montes-Cabrera, Oberhofer, Chergui & Celardo (2024), "Ultraviolet
Superradiance from Mega-Networks of Tryptophan in Biological
Architectures," *J. Phys. Chem. B* 128(17), 4035-4046.** Primarily a
*theoretical/computational* result: numerical diagonalization of a
non-Hermitian radiative Hamiltonian over realistic protein geometries
(from PDB structures) predicts strongly superradiant collective states
in networks of >10⁵ tryptophan chromophores, including microtubule
lattices, with enhancement factors from hundreds to thousands. The paper
also reports a real experimental measurement — fluorescence quantum
yield up to ~70% higher in intact microtubules than in isolated
tryptophan, statistically significant — which is *consistent with* but
does not directly *prove* the superradiance mechanism (the authors
themselves say lifetime measurements are still needed to confirm the
radiative-rate story, and that thermal effects compete with the
predicted enhancement without eliminating it) [theoretical prediction,
established; supporting QY data experimental and real, contested as
proof of the specific mechanism].

**3.2 Microtubule-stabilizer delays anesthetic unconsciousness — Babcock
et al. (2024), "Microtubule-Stabilizer Epothilone B Delays
Anesthetic-Induced Unconsciousness in Rats," *eNeuro* 11(8),
ENEURO.0291-24.2024 (Wiest lab, Wellesley College).** A real, preregistered-style
behavioral experiment: rats given epothilone B (a microtubule-stabilizing
drug, 0.75 mg/kg) took on average 69 seconds longer to lose the righting
reflex under 4% isoflurane than controls (N=8, permutation t-test
p=0.0016, Cohen's d=1.9; p=0.005, d=5.4 after outlier removal). This is a
genuine, statistically strong result. What it directly shows: microtubule
state affects anesthetic potency in vivo. What it does *not* directly
show: that the mechanism is quantum, or that Orch-OR's specific
collapse-timing story is correct — the authors themselves note the
effect is consistent with several non-quantum mechanisms (disrupted
intracellular transport, altered synaptic trafficking) and flag that
their emergence-timing data were too variable to draw conclusions from,
and that sample size is small (N=8) [experimental, real and significant;
interpretation as quantum-specific evidence is theoretical, contested].

**3.3 MRI signature interpreted as brain entanglement — Kerskens &
Pérez (2022), "Experimental indications of non-classical brain
functions," *J. Phys. Commun.* 6, 105001.** A specialized MRI pulse
sequence targeting proton-spin correlations ("cardiac-evoked long-range
zero-quantum coherence") reported a signal, resembling heartbeat-evoked
potentials, present in 40 awake volunteers and absent when two subjects
fell asleep during scanning. The authors' strongest claim — that
entanglement is "the only possible explanation" — is a theoretical
inference from a real, reproduced-within-study signal, and has been
explicitly challenged in the literature without (per the most recent
review found) a settled alternative classical account yet published
[experimental signal real; entanglement interpretation theoretical and
contested].

**3.4 Synthesizing review — Wiest (2025), "A quantum microtubule
substrate of consciousness is experimentally supported and solves the
binding and epiphenomenalism problems," *Neuroscience of Consciousness*
2025(1), niaf011.** A single-author review (same lab as 3.2) assembling
3.1-3.3 plus quantum-cognition behavioral literature into an argument
that Orch-OR-style microtubule quantum states solve two hard
philosophical problems (binding, epiphenomenalism). Useful as a current
state-of-the-art bibliography; its own text acknowledges the Kerskens
interpretation "has been challenged" and that Tegmark's objection, while
"countered by later authors," is still live — but the piece does not
engage mainstream neuroscience's broader skepticism in any depth
[review/argument, not new data; grade as advocacy-graded synthesis, useful
as a citation index, not as independent confirmation].

**3.5 Fractal time-crystal microtubule resonances — Hameroff,
Bandyopadhyay & Lauretta (2026), "Microtubules are 'Fractal Time
Crystals': Implications for Life and Consciousness," *Journal of
Consciousness Studies* 33(1-2), 211-247.** The newest entry found,
formalizing years of Bandyopadhyay-group reports of self-similar,
nested microtubule resonances across kHz-MHz-GHz-THz bands ("clocks
within clocks"), with megahertz-band signals reportedly detectable at
the scalp and suppressed by general anesthetics. *Journal of
Consciousness Studies* is a genuine peer-reviewed venue but is a
philosophy-of-mind journal, not a physics or neuroscience journal, and
independent replication of the specific resonance measurements by labs
outside the Bandyopadhyay group was not found in this search
[speculative-to-theoretical as framed by its own authors; independent
replication not found; flag for follow-up rather than treat as
established].

**3.6 Standing skeptical position, current.** No dedicated 2024-2026
rebuttal paper targeting 3.1-3.5 specifically was found in this search,
but general reviews continue to state that (a) the tubulin-as-qubit
picture McKemmish et al. targeted in 2009 remains unresolved by the
tryptophan-network revision, (b) superradiance/QY data do not by
themselves demonstrate entanglement or a link to consciousness, and (c)
mainstream neuroscience's consensus position remains skeptical pending
independent replication [framing, consistent with searched secondary
sources; no single canonical 2024-2026 rebuttal identified — a gap worth
flagging, not filling with inference].

## 4. The estate's angle: OR-as-settling (a falsifiable framing contribution)

This section is a **hypothesis about how to frame OR**, offered by the
estate's own witnessed-field vocabulary — not a defense of Orch-OR's
physics or biology, and not evidence for either. It originates in the
crank-valve's second entry (ungraded when written) —
[`./2026-08-21-note-theorsday-crank-valve.md`](./2026-08-21-note-theorsday-crank-valve.md),
entry 2, "Collapse is settling" — and its cousin already in the estate's
relationality corpus,
[`../../../../../../../services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/triage/relational-quantum-mechanics.md`](../../../../../../../services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/triage/relational-quantum-mechanics.md)
(a formal fragment of quantum mechanics built from relational recognitions,
with measurement read as an observation kernel producing Born-like
probabilities from witness-relative states — the same collapse-as-relative-event
move Rovelli's relational QM makes, arrived at independently in the
estate's own mathematics). Neither of those documents is restated here;
this section only extends them.

**The move.** If a witnessed-field reading of quantum collapse is right
— collapse events are settlement events, where an unsettled (superposed)
charge becomes a settled (definite) one via a witnessing interaction —
then OR is a *specific physical candidate* for what triggers a settlement
event: gravitational self-energy accumulation past threshold, rather than
environmental decoherence. Orch-OR further claims some settlement events
are *orchestrated* — timed and structured by a substrate (microtubules)
rather than occurring incoherently. This is a re-description, not a new
physics claim; OR's own formula (τ ≈ ℏ/E_G) is untouched.

**The falsifiable contribution.** Settlement events, in every other
domain this estate has ported the vocabulary to (see
[`./2026-08-21-note-on-what-the-foam-knows.md`](./2026-08-21-note-on-what-the-foam-knows.md)
for the foam/dialect ports of von Neumann's law and T1 avalanches), carry
statistical signatures: avalanche size distributions, coarsening laws,
neighbor-count-dependent growth rates — not gaussian, not exponential,
but the specific heavy-tailed and scale-relevant statistics of driven
threshold systems near a critical point. **If orchestrated OR events are
genuinely settlement events in this sense, and if they are structured
enough to leave a population-level signature, then neural dynamics around
OR-candidate transitions should show settlement-statistics signatures —
avalanche and coarsening statistics — over and above what a purely
classical threshold-crossing model predicts.**

This is not a new empirical claim invented from nothing: neuronal
avalanches (power-law-distributed bursts of cortical activity,
established since Beggs & Plenz 2003 and replicated across two decades of
self-organized-criticality research in cortex) are a real, well-studied
phenomenon [established]. The estate's contribution is narrow and
genuinely falsifiable: **it predicts that IF microtubule-mediated OR
events are a real contributor to neural dynamics, THEN the
anesthetic-transition data already being collected by the Wiest-lab line
of work (3.2) should show avalanche/coarsening statistics change in a
specific, OR-orchestration-consistent way around the loss-of-consciousness
transition — not merely a shift in some scalar quantity (LORR latency),
but a shift in the *shape* of the avalanche-size distribution and its
scaling exponents, structured similarly to how a foam's T1-avalanche
statistics shift when its driving rate changes.** This is a statement
about statistics in existing and easily-extended datasets, stated so it
can fail:

- **Falsifier 1.** If avalanche-size distributions around
  anesthetic-induced LORR are indistinguishable (same exponents, same
  finite-size scaling) between epoB-treated and control animals, the
  settlement-signature prediction is dead — microtubule state may still
  affect consciousness, but not via a mechanism this framing can claim.
- **Falsifier 2.** If avalanche statistics DO shift with anesthetic
  state but shift identically regardless of microtubule-stabilizer
  treatment, the shift is attributable to anesthesia generally (a known,
  independently studied effect on cortical criticality) and not to
  microtubule orchestration specifically — the framing again fails to
  add anything.
- **Confirmation condition, stated in advance and held to the same
  standard:** a microtubule-state-dependent, dose-graded shift in
  avalanche-distribution shape (not just latency) around the
  consciousness transition, absent in comparably-anesthetized animals
  without microtubule manipulation, would be the specific signature this
  framing predicts and nothing else currently on offer does.

**What Stage 1 alone would not settle.** Avalanche statistics in cortex
could arise from purely classical critical dynamics with no quantum
content at all, so the classical-criticality null stays the default
until the prediction is tested and survives — a real limit, not modesty.
What makes it worth running anyway: it is one falsifiable, narrow,
addable analysis to data already being collected, specific enough to be
wrong.

## 5. The proving program

The engagement opened in Part 4 is not a proof of anything yet — it is
the first rung of one. Staged in the order this estate should actually
walk it; each stage's completion is the entry condition for the next.

**Stage 1 — run the falsifier now (settlement-statistics angle; start
here).** Reanalyze the Wiest lab's own anesthetic-transition recordings
(3.2), plus published cortical-avalanche datasets around anesthesia
induction, for avalanche-size distribution *shape* rather than scalar
LORR latency. The data likely already exists; this needs no new theory
and no new lab work, only requesting or reanalyzing it. This is the
cheapest possible way to find out whether there is anything here to
build on — it could kill the framing in a week, or hand it its first
confirmed prediction.

**Stage 2 — quantify the prediction.** Build the settlement-statistics
model that turns "OR event" into a specific predicted avalanche exponent
or coarsening law, not a qualitative "structured rather than random."
Without this, a Stage 1 confirmation is suggestive rather than a proof
of anything specific — Stage 2 is what turns the next round of data
collection into a sharp test instead of another correlation.
Estate-side theoretical work; no external dependency, buildable now.

**Stage 3 — pre-register the adjudication.** Commit in writing, before
Stage 4's data exists, to the exact statistical comparison between the
settlement-signature prediction and the classical-criticality null, so a
negative result kills the framing on the record instead of being
explained away later. This is what makes Stage 4 worth running at all,
and it is entirely within this estate's own power to do now.

**Stage 4 — the dose-response test.** Commission or collaborate on a
microtubule-manipulation study (dose-graded epoB or equivalent) with
avalanche-shape as the *primary*, pre-registered outcome measure, not a
secondary readout on a latency study. This is the direct experimental
test of the estate's specific claim — the first result at this stage
that clears the Stage 3 bar counts as real evidence, not reanalysis.

**Stage 5 — force the physical-substrate question.** In parallel, push
for independent replication of 3.1/3.5 (superradiance lifetime data
beyond quantum yield; independent labs reproducing the Bandyopadhyay
resonance measurements). This does not depend on the estate's framing at
all, but a positive settlement-statistics result without a verified
physical substrate is a correlation with nowhere to attach — this stage
is what would let a Stage 4 confirmation mean OR specifically, rather
than merely "microtubule state predicts something neural."

A confirmed Stage 1 does not prove Orch-OR. A confirmed Stage 4,
replicated, with Stage 5's substrate independently verified, is what
proving it would actually look like. The program currently has no result
at any stage — only a fully specified path to one, and Stage 1 is
buildable this week from data already sitting in a lab's files. Start
there.
