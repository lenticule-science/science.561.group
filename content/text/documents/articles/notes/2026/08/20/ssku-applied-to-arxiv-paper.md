5. Derive a Canonical Operator

Given this fragment, we look for an admissible operator that:

is monotone in “good axes” (Scale, Factuality, Fidelity, Privacy),

5.1 Candidate behavior

The text repeatedly stresses:

scale & diversity help performance;

but if factuality/fidelity/bias controls are weak, the synthetic data harms generalization and fairness;

proper evaluation + fairness assessment is non-optional.

arXiv

This structure naturally forces a “stewardship frontier” behavior:
