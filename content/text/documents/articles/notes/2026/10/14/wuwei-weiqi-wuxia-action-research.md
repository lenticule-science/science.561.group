# The science upstream of five Wednesday tactics

## Question, status and receiving

**Demand:** can non-coercive activity, strategic option selection, timing/affordance alignment, Go gameplay and martial-hero narratives become **distinct, scientifically honest, instrumentable Methods of Action** rather than decorative weekday names?

**Status:** this is a dated, source-linked *research synthesis* and a set of testable proposals. It does not establish an Obsmetrologia theorem, a causal effectiveness result, a formally validated decision procedure, a launched game, a public narrative, or cultural authenticity. Interpretation of historical texts and performance of algorithms require **different standards of warrant**. The corporate receiver is the 561 Group's authorized Wednesday Strategy of Action skill family; further implementation, if required, belongs to Red Cup's reusable code, Coesite's reusable operation definitions and the corresponding Group or Causeway instance. Cold Red Gate being unavailable limits live receiving, not primary-text inquiry or GitHub source work.

## Literature and distinctions

### 1. Philosophical action

The *Laozi*'s wúwéi (無為/无为) is discussed with dao, de, ziran and a critique of aggressive/desire-driven action, but not as a universal minimization routine. The Stanford Encyclopedia's [Laozi](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2017/entries/laozi/) and [Neo-Daoism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/neo-daoism/) entries document nonaction, naturalness, Wang Bi's commentary and contested readings. The [IEP account](https://iep.utm.edu/daoismdaoist-philosophy/) discusses Cook Ding navigating joints in the *Zhuangzi*, where the point extends beyond skill acquisition. Do not retroactively claim Daoist writers invented control theory, agile software, intervention minimization or Go tactics. Wu in 無為 and wu in 武俠 are different written words and tones; wei in 為 and wei in 圍棋 are different graphs despite the English play on sound.

The useful *engineering hypothesis* is narrower: when lawful dynamics already move toward the receiver's goal, supporting affordances or removing interference can outperform coercive intervention on net outcome and recipient autonomy. Counterhypotheses include irreversible risks, coercive circumstances, inaction biases and unstable dynamics that make delay costly. An apparent absence of force may conceal externalized costs on others.

### 2. Choice as action under a description

[Stanford Encyclopedia: Practical Reason and Structure of Actions](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/practical-reason-action/) distinguishes figuring out what to do from merely predicting consequences; [Stanford Encyclopedia: Intention](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/intention/) surveys action under descriptions and practical knowledge. This connects the Group's Method of Actions to goals, intention, rights, causation and evidence rather than a universal reward score. A chosen act can be intended under one description and harmful under another; the receiver and authorized office must check both.

A useful *decision-model proposal*, not a settled law: for admissible action a in an authorized set A(S), uncertain state θ and receiver-specific goal g, evaluate **E[U(a,θ,g) | E] − c(a)**, then compare against abstain, defer, information-gathering and reversible probes. E is actual predecision evidence. List all utility components, assumptions, delay cost, distribution shift, rights and non-compensable constraints. When preferences, probabilities or costs are unavailable, do **not** manufacture cardinal utilities, exact regrets or net-positive claims. Expected value of additional information is conditional on this model, its information delay and sampling costs.

### 3. Timing, enabling conditions and counterfactuals

A dynamics analogy may distinguish **do now**, **wait with observation**, **remove a constraint**, **change a future affordance** and **stop a harmful intervention**. The operational benchmark is not simply least input; it is observed, rights-respecting recipient value across a named horizon under comparable starting states. An action that saves an operator ten minutes while increasing customer effort is not an efficiency win. A natural event that occurs regardless of our action must not be counted as caused by us: measure marginal effect, including waiting/coordination/opportunity cost and possible self-selection. Compare active intervention against justified controls, interrupted time series or causal designs when randomization is unacceptable. No unsupported claim of equivalence with variational least action, thermodynamics, free-energy minimization or control-optimality is permitted.

A simple proposed hypothesis class is: conditional on state s and legitimate goal g, changing an affordance at time t may produce a better **verified goal minus total externalities** outcome than forcing a downstream actor. This is a falsifiable comparison **for a specified system**; no universal dominance claim follows.

### 4. Go as exact strategy laboratory

Go / weiqi / baduk provides discrete game states, finite legal move spaces at each position, scored outcomes, adversarial response and replayable counterexamples. It is more tightly testable than everyday metaphors of 'territory' or 'influence'. **Legality depends on ruleset and history**: occupied intersections, captures/liberties, suicide convention, ko/superko, passes, handicap, komi, dead-stone resolution and territory/area scoring. [American Go Association rules](https://www.usgo.org/content.aspx?club_id=454497&module_id=563542&page_id=22) explain their area/territory convention; the [SGF FF[4] standard](https://www.red-bean.com/sgf/sgf4.html) and [Go-specific rules](https://www.red-bean.com/sgf/go.html) specify a portable representation, not a rules authority for every competition. **SGF syntax validity is not legal-play validity**: the FF[4] property rules explicitly allow storing move records that game rules might reject. Game event verification must separately enforce the selected rules.

[Silver et al., Nature 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16961) and [Silver et al., Nature 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24270) establish empirical success of policy/value search and self-play systems under specified evaluations; they do not establish that a Daoist heuristic improves Go or other decisions. Concepts needing position-based operationalization include: local versus whole-board value; group life and death; liberties and connection; territory versus prospective influence; sente/gote (initiative and response obligation); tenuki (choosing to play elsewhere); sacrifice; ko threats; reading depth and endgame. Candidate heuristics must be judged against legal responses and stronger references, not elegant names.

**Counterexample design for non-forcing Go:** use (i) a live, stable position where a calm global move outperforms a locally forcing exchange; (ii) a threatened, tactically unsettled group where a tenuki or defer loses outright. Construct and verify both positions with an actual rule engine and signed-off expert/strong reference analysis. Until that verification occurs, **do not present an invented board diagram as an established puzzle solution**.

### 5. Learning, motivation and economic safety

[Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski 2006](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) investigate perceived autonomy, competence and relatedness in game enjoyment. [Tyack and Mekler 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12639) criticize shallow/incomplete application of self-determination theory in games research. A design that merely increases time-on-device or ad impressions has **not** therefore established better learning, user wellbeing or sustained enjoyment. [Cepeda et al. 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/) report distributed-practice advantages for verbal recall; direct transfer to Go skill is a hypothesis needing Go-specific pre/post and delayed assessments.

Evaluate onboarding comprehension, rules violations, solved held-out puzzles, calibration of move judgments, delayed retention/skill, motivation, agency, accessibility, unfairness, churn after opt-outs and fraud. Compare cohorts with predeclared eligibility, appropriate randomization/consent or quasi-experimental methods, and uncertainty intervals. Reward advertisements must be optional and their absence cannot invalidate core Go. Satoshi offers require *separately* funded risk bounds, identity protections, server-authoritative proof, eligible jurisdictions and actual payment receipt. Puzzles, prizes, ads and accounts are distinct lawful events; a screen animation, client claim or specification is not payout.

### 6. Wuxia's ethics and the agency of fiction

[Yilin Wang 2021](https://sfwa.org/2021/12/28/what-speculative-fiction-writers-can-learn-from-the-origins-and-evolution-of-the-wuxia-genre/) documents xia (俠), jianghu (江湖), resistance to unjust power, moral ambiguity, gendered and classed figures, historical adaptations and transnational influences. A wuxia method should therefore model **moral agency and social relationships**, not collapse the genre to choreographed combat. Distinguish wuxia 武俠 from the related cultivation-inflected xianxia 仙俠; xuanhuan is another historical/marketing category with contested boundaries. The Leader's 'Wuxday' is a house tactic name, not a Chinese genre. 'Wuxian' lacks established characters and referent in the request; preserve ambiguity rather than guessing its meaning.

Research proposal: in an interactive narrative with declared causal rules, a meaningful character act changes both (a) state and (b) the obligations, harms or options faced by others. Compare a coherent consequence-bearing scenario with cosmetically different but forced-outcome presentation on player recall of moral choices, perceived agency, retention of causal knowledge and accessibility. Genre consultation with Sinophone readers/translators and informed creators is an empirical/cultural receiving step, not optional copy-editing. Do not copy protected franchises or portray violence as inherently righteous.

## Five separate study protocols

| ID | Tactical hypothesis | Unit of comparison | Baseline and disconfirming case | Distinct outcome and receiver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H-WU | Correctly chosen non-coercive or constraint-removal interventions can improve net accepted outcome in eligible dynamics. | Same office obligation under comparable resource/environment conditions | Immediate direct intervention; case where waiting violates urgent duty | Goal attainment, harm, externalized effort, recipient acceptance |
| H-WEI | Explicit option sets, outside options and preregistered evaluation improve *decisions*, not just explanations. | Repeated decisions with decision-time information | Incumbent practice; perfect retrospective story that fails out-of-sample | Proper-score calibration when probabilistic, avoidable regret/cost, reversible success |
| H-WUWEI | Time- and affordance-matched actions improve downstream ability without shifting unpriced burdens. | Dependency chain and receiving actor | Fixed-timing push; case where event never arrives or cost of delay dominates | Marginal receiver work, lag-to-receipt, total cost, autonomy |
| H-QI | Rule-correct Go practice with meaningful feedback improves verified play and learning. | Held-out legal board situations, users and sessions | Static puzzles/rules tutorial and strong-player reference; tempting illegal or losing tenuki | Rule validity, delayed skill, accessibility, privacy, verified earning (only if separately authorized) |
| H-XIA | Consequence-bearing martial-hero narratives enable understandable and morally meaningful agency. | Replayed narrative encounter, user comprehension and outcome | Linear scripted story; exploitative or culturally careless variant | Causal consistency, player choice comprehension, independent cultural/user receiving |

These studies are independent: the non-forcing method may be tested without Go; Go learning may succeed without Daoism; a heroic narrative may work without cryptocurrency. **Compositional hypotheses** require verified interface compatibility and preserve each study's own counterevidence. A character's fictional victory is not evidence for superior real-world strategic judgment.

## Proposed tactic assay common to all five

Start from a **real existing subject/Work Arena obligation**, not a research-created ticket. Each tactic reads current state S, declared goal G, evidence E, actor/office authority A, relevant model and material uncertainty; observes/proposes choices; then either returns a qualified observation or requests a separate **guarded authorized action**. If an action is performed, record the actual action and receiving border. Do not let an observation silently mutate source, rights, money, or game state.

For any declared success: publish the exact baseline, time window, observable, provenance, relevant uncertainty and *disconfirming witness*. A model is explanatory only in its tested domain; research note → corporate procedure → source code → live service → external acceptance are different states. Keep failures as evidence (FOUR positive/negative testimony if supported) and feed improvements into the same source owner. The Group's Wednesday specification provides the applied procedure; Science does not impersonate an Engineering validator or grant operating authority.
