# MAPE-K as staged closure with residue and open inputs

The Founder proposes reading Monitor–Analyze–Plan–Execute over Knowledge as a finite-first spiral in which each stage tries to close the preceding stage's residue, while new knowledge and material enter from outside. This is a concrete research and orchestration direction. A sound realization must preserve the four distinct work kinds, construct typed stage transitions, and prove closure claims on named carriers. The proposed correspondences among several quartets remain hypotheses until those constructions exist.

This note preserves the original proposal and its explicit correction before giving an analytical construction. It changes no existing theorem, runbook, office authority, or runtime behavior. Its intended use is to guide a reviewable scientific specification and then an exercised implementation in the existing agent orchestration engine, FeltOS, and MetroPlat.

## 2 What the correction establishes

The corrected reading is **Imagination is an inquiry**. The original proposal associates Monitoring with Process, Imagination with Inquiry, Possibility with Derivation, and Necessity with Procedure. The Founder also makes explicit that each of the four work kinds can be a workflow and can have a runbook. The existence of a runbook therefore does not classify work as a process or procedure.

These are distinct propositions:

1. The proposed MAPE-K interpretation associates four functions with four work kinds.
2. Work kinds differ in how meaning and execution are settled.
3. Settling trajectories may be classified by a closure ordinal under stated assumptions.
4. Reality/Imagination/Possibility/Necessity, Arena/Strategy/Move/Action, absurd/potential/active/stable, and SignCell/SignChain/SignGraph/SignCraft may have useful relationships.

No equality, isomorphism, equivalence of categories, or one-to-one correspondence among these lists follows from each having four entries. The proposed associations should be kept as named hypotheses, with the direction of each proposed map and the structures it preserves stated explicitly. In particular, this note does not assign one closure ordinal to each MAPE-K stage.

A runbook for inquiry can specify the question, admissible observations, exploration methods, resources, limits, evidence retention, and reporting of a changed question. It need not predict the answer or every step. A derivation runbook fixes the inference method and result family; a procedure runbook fixes conditions and routing; a process runbook fixes the transformation. An outer workflow may compose all four.

## 3 Existing sources and their limits

### The work kind quartet

The recovered Work Science entries define two proposed closures on an algebra of work, meaning closure σ and execution closure Δ [W]. Their fixedness conditions are:

- Process: σ(x) = x and Δ(x) = x
- Procedure: σ(x) = x and Δ(x) ≠ x
- Derivation: σ(x) ≠ x and Δ(x) = x
- Inquiry: σ(x) ≠ x and Δ(x) ≠ x

These four predicates partition any already-defined carrier on which the two maps are defined. This partition alone does not construct that carrier, make either map a closure operator, prove commutation, or guarantee that each part is nonempty.

The process entry expressly leaves the proof that joint closure implies deterministic skill work open, pending construction of the algebra of skill work. Its implementation conventions, such as which skills carry scripts, models, or fuel caps, are house conventions rather than consequences of order theory. Operational resource and permission limits remain applicable to every work kind.

The nuclear quartet source [N] assumes commuting nuclei on a Heyting algebra. Under those assumptions the joint map σ ∘ Δ is an idempotent projection onto their common fixed points. A nucleus additionally preserves finite meets; an arbitrary closure operator need not. MAPE-K stages have not been shown to be those nuclei. Commutation must be proved, not borrowed from the notation.

Two narrower upstream obligations remain. The condition H ≠ H* does not imply all quartet inclusions are strict: take one nucleus to be the identity and the other nonidentity. Fixed-point algebras also need their induced operations specified; their inclusion need not preserve ambient joins or bottom. For a genuine meet-preserving nucleus, implication between fixed elements is fixed and agrees with ambient implication. The source’s claim that every arrow is a Heyting homomorphism still requires precise conventions and proof, including the projection’s preservation laws. These are obligations in the supporting mathematics, distinct from the Founder’s practical insight that each work kind needs an appropriate workflow approach.

### Closure ordinal regimes

The recovered four-regime source [R] partitions an ordinal κ into κ < ω, κ = ω, ω < κ < ω₁, and κ ≥ ω₁. It starts with eventually-zero external increments. This hypothesis matters: continuously entering observations or material do not satisfy it automatically.

An already idempotent closure C on a fixed carrier satisfies C(C(x)) = C(x), so its iteration stabilizes after the first application. Any nontrivial settling ordinal must identify the non-idempotent inference step, incremental evolution, or other construction being iterated, separately from its saturated closure.

The ordinal partition is exhaustive and disjoint once a closure ordinal is defined. Several stronger equivalences asserted by the source require repair or additional hypotheses:

- Finite stabilization does not imply a finite carrier. The identity map stabilizes immediately on an infinite carrier.
- Scott continuity gives stabilization by ω for the usual increasing iteration under suitable completeness assumptions; an infinite carrier does not force the realized closure ordinal to equal ω.
- Reaching a fixed point at ω on one trajectory does not prove that a map preserves all directed joins.
- Failure of Scott continuity does not by itself force every trajectory to last beyond ω.
- A finite state space does not force an arbitrary program or recurrent workflow to terminate. The finite-chain argument additionally needs an increasing trajectory or another well-founded progress argument.

The task and recurrent-practice entries are explicitly marked `status: sketch` [T, P]. Their distinction between an operator's worst-case behavior and a realized execution is useful, but requires an exact domain of allowed increment sequences before either quantity is well-defined. A stream with fresh inputs forever must not be described as the iteration of one fixed operator with no inputs.

The recurrent-practice entry separately distinguishes nonperiodic substitution sequences from the source's closure-ordinal use of “aperiodic.” That distinction should be preserved. “Aperiodic” must name its definition and carrier; it is not a synonym for “interesting,” “indefinite,” or “an inquiry.” Likewise κ ≥ ω₁ means no stabilization at a countable stage on that specified trajectory, not a proof that no stabilization ever occurs.

### Existing MAPE-K research

The July 11 MAPE-K note [M], especially section 4, already requires semantics for observation, analysis, planning, execution, and knowledge; distinguishes operational, observed, belief, and normative states; and emphasizes that local correctness does not imply correctness of the coupled loop and its environment. It also identifies stage-boundary contracts, model versioning, partial observability, authority, and independent execution admissibility as necessary concerns.

The new proposal extends this existing problem with an explicit account of stage-local settling, residue, external inputs, and improvement. It does not replace the existing semantics with a diagram.

## 4 A typed construction to investigate

The following is a proposed signature discipline. It is not a claim that these functions or proofs already exist.

### Separate three operations

For each stage i and fixed context version k, define:

1. A **local inference step** Fᵢ,ₖ : Hᵢ,ₖ → Hᵢ,ₖ on a specified ordered carrier of stage information. This is an endomap. Establish its properties individually.
2. A **local saturation** Cᵢ,ₖ : Hᵢ,ₖ → Hᵢ,ₖ, if justified, obtained by iterating the step to its least fixed point above the initial state. C is the closure operator; F need not be idempotent.
3. A **cross-stage translation** τᵢ,ₖ : Outputᵢ,ₖ × Residueᵢ,ₖ × Inputᵢ₊₁,ₖ → Hᵢ₊₁,ₖ. Its domain and codomain differ. It is not itself a closure operator merely because it helps settle the next stage.

Context k must identify the admitted knowledge, rule and model versions, relevant permissions, objectives, environmental assumptions, and evidence scope. If these parameters change, one has a new operator or carrier and must state how the earlier state is transported.

The local carrier may be a powerset of typed evidence, a product of ordered information domains, or another justified algebra. It cannot be chosen solely because it makes the desired theorem easy. If closures are called nuclei, construct the required Heyting or frame structure and prove finite-meet preservation as well as closure laws.

### Preserve stage domains and result types

A candidate interface makes the intended transformations explicit:

- Monitor: admitted sensor observations and previous residue → evidenced arena description, with observation provenance and unresolved observation gaps
- Analyze: arena description, question, and admitted knowledge → candidate strategies or explanations, with supporting and conflicting evidence and unresolved questions
- Plan: strategy candidates, arena constraints, and action semantics → candidate moves or plans, with preconditions and remaining feasibility obligations
- Execute: an authorized move plus current precondition evidence → an execution outcome and receiving evidence, or a typed refusal, failure, or partial outcome

These signatures express the Founder's proposed relationships. They are construction targets, not proofs that Monitoring is universally a Process or Analysis universally an Inquiry. A concrete stage may need smaller work-kind components. If an inquiry discovers a new output type, its external result can use a dependent package Σ(T : AdmittedResultType), T with provenance, or a typed new-question result. This leaves the payload open without dispensing with interface types. The set of admissible result types and any universe boundary must itself be defined.

An execution result must distinguish a command being issued, an effect being observed, and a receiver accepting the outcome. Knowledge may grow when an attempted effect fails. That growth must not turn the failed effect into success.

### Build from the existing Sign family

Recover the existing definitions and category structures for SignCell, SignChain, SignGraph, and SignCraft before choosing representations. For every construction, name its input object, output object, subobjects, maps, identities, composition, and any claimed preservation laws. Prove recursive constructions terminate or are appropriately guarded; merely using recursive JSON or matching names does not establish a category.

The Founder's proposed cells:data, chains:code, graphs:network, craft:system relationship supplies the intended direction. To use it as more than a design interpretation, construct the relevant functors and prove what they preserve. Claim equivalence only if suitable inverse or quasi-inverse data and coherence proofs are provided. This note does not prescribe a replacement Sign implementation.

## 5 What residue means

Define residue relative to a stage, context, goal, and adequacy criterion. It should retain the exact unmet obligations, uncertainty, conflicts, or unavailable observations needed by a downstream stage or a later cycle.

On a powerset carrier, a possible mathematical closure gap is C(x) ∖ x. It is empty exactly when x is fixed by C. On a general ordered carrier, subtraction may not exist; retain the pair (x, C(x)) or use a separately constructed gap object and prove that its empty or zero case is equivalent to fixedness. Residuation is an additional algebraic structure, not an interchangeable word for residue.

A local closure gap and a goal residue are different. C(x) = x says that the selected rules add no information within the chosen context. It does not say that the goal is true, evidence is complete, contradictions are absent, the environment is safe, or a recipient is satisfied. A useful residue type may therefore include both mathematical closure obligations and domain-specific unmet goals, with the distinction retained.

Calling residue “entropy” additionally requires a defined measure, its units or normalization, and the relation between its value and admissible action. No Shannon, thermodynamic, or logical entropy identity is asserted here. New information can expose more obligations; the count of residual items need not decrease even when inquiry is productive.

## 6 A valid finite first result

The following elementary result is available independently of the proposed semantic correspondences.

Let H be a finite-height poset, and let C₁,…,Cₘ : H → H be monotone, extensive endomaps. Let T = Cₘ ∘ ⋯ ∘ C₁. For any x, the sequence x₀ = x and xₙ₊₁ = T(xₙ) is increasing and stabilizes after finitely many strict increases. Its stable value is the least common fixed point of the Cᵢ above x. If this construction is defined for every x, the saturation x ↦ x∞ is monotone, extensive, and idempotent.

**Proof.** Each Cᵢ is monotone and extensive, so T is monotone and extensive. Finite height forbids an infinite strictly increasing chain. At a fixed point y of T, the chain y ≤ C₁(y) ≤ C₂(C₁(y)) ≤ ⋯ ≤ T(y) = y consists entirely of equalities, so each Cᵢ(y) = y. Conversely any common fixed point is fixed by T. If z ≥ x is a common fixed point, induction gives Tⁿ(x) ≤ z. Thus x∞ is the least such point. Extensivity follows from the increasing chain; monotonicity follows by induction on iterates; idempotence follows because x∞ is already fixed. □

A full sweep always applies every map in this statement. An asynchronous or selective implementation needs an appropriate fairness argument; skipping a stage indefinitely is not covered. A quantitative termination bound needs the height or another ranking measure on the relevant reachable carrier, not merely the word “finite.”

One sweep need not be idempotent even when every Cᵢ is already a closure operator. On the powerset of {a,b,c}, let C₁ add b when a is present and C₂ add c when b is present. Both are monotone, extensive, and idempotent. With T = C₁ ∘ C₂, T({a}) = {a,b} and T²({a}) = {a,b,c}. This verifies the need to distinguish a composed step from its saturation. No MAPE-K or work-kind labels are assigned to this example.

For an ω-complete ordered carrier and an ω-continuous, extensive monotone T, the increasing chain has a supremum xω and continuity gives T(xω) = supₙ T(xₙ) = supₙ xₙ₊₁ = xω. This supplies a conditional bound by ω. Establishing equality with ω requires proving that no finite stage is fixed. Transfinite claims require explicit limit-stage rules, a suitable carrier and joins, and a proof of the claimed stabilization bound.

This supplies one candidate finite-first construction: prefer justified finite settling where it fits. The Founder’s proposed finite-first stage-order correspondence is a separate hypothesis and is not proved by this result. Neither claim requires pretending that every inquiry, world transition, or continuous input stream has finite closure.

## 7 Open inputs make a spiral rather than one closed iteration

A fixed-context saturation is Cₖ : Hₖ → Hₖ. After an external input uₖ and a lawful change of context, define a separate admission map Jₖ : Hₖ × Uₖ → Hₖ₊₁ and write xₖ₊₁ = Cₖ₊₁(Jₖ(xₖ,uₖ)), with Cₖ₊₁ : Hₖ₊₁ → Hₖ₊₁. J carries ingestion, provenance, deduplication, and any migration obligations. If monotone information growth across contexts is claimed, exhibit order embeddings or another comparison relation and prove compatibility. Model or policy revision may invalidate old conclusions, so monotonicity should usually concern retained provenance rather than unqualified truth of every earlier conclusion.

Material inputs and executions belong to an environmental transition relation, for example Env × AuthorizedAction × Disturbance → Outcomes(Env). They need not be extensive or monotone in the information order. A monotone log of observed transitions is different from a monotone world. The relation between log, observation, belief, and environment needs a soundness or calibration argument appropriate to the domain.

The original eventual-zero-increments convergence theorem cannot establish whole-loop quiescence when fresh inputs continue indefinitely. Meaningful receiving conditions can instead be local: saturation for a named snapshot, discharge of a particular goal, a witnessed execution result, or stability under a stated class of future inputs. Each claim must say which boundary it uses. Finishing one campaign does not imply an indefinite endeavor has ended.

### 7a.1 The resource-conservation problem and the actual output

An unbounded source family may include source-code changes, issue comments, RSS/Atom, public documents, activity streams, authorized private messages, agent Work Arena moves, environment/host telemetry, scientific results, customer demand and physical sensor samples. Each differs in sampling process, trust domain, age, units, causal interpretation, action authority and right of retention. We must never silently type every arrival as a work instruction, truth, or WIT.

For channel i, let arrivals a_i(t) be measured admitted units in a window and service s_i(t) the units actually processed to the next *specified* stage. With nonnegative backlog q_i, the elementary conservation law is q_i(t+1) = max(0, q_i(t) + a_i(t) - s_i(t)). A claimed uniformly bounded queue under sustained weighted arrival load exceeding available service contradicts this law. Bursts force an explicit policy: increase resources within granted budgets, defer a derived view, combine redundant *presentations*, reduce sampling, or admit incomplete coverage. Source evidence that must lawfully persist does not become dispensable merely because its downstream semantic analysis is deferred. Conversely, provider rights or retention limits can require deletion or non-admission at the proper custody boundary; "append-only" cannot override those obligations.

Success is measured as a **legitimate consequence at an independently receiving boundary**: a new scientific possibility learned, a timely owner disposition, an independently accepted useful service, an environment alteration observed, or an informative refusal. Success is not the count of raw events, categorized texts, LLM summaries, published notes, token consumption, or merely posted Work. Source capture, source interpretation, agent uptake, authorized action, and recipient ACK/assay have distinct identities and possible failure.

### 7a.3 Five distinct identities that naive deduplication loses

A sufficient candidate **admitted arrival** (not yet a Science theorem or universal wire decision) has at least: a source namespace and stable occurrence identity; publisher/source actor; schema/type and content/media type; source event time when reported; separately observed arrival/ingestion time; original bytes or a permitted immutable content pointer; source/payload version; causal predecessors where actually witnessed; principal, scope, privacy and permitted use; an explicit acquisition cursor/coverage condition; and source-channel error or refusal evidence. Preserve clock uncertainty; an absent timestamp remains unknown rather than being manufactured.

Keep these equivalence questions distinct:

1. **Replayed transport occurrence:** repeated source + original occurrence id, even if delivered over two transports. Dedup replay *effects*, preserving the receipt of an attempted retransmission where useful.
2. **Byte-identical content:** two payloads share canonical content identity; this does **not** entail independent sources, identical rights, or that both are the same event.
3. **Shared causal origin:** two reports are copies of one press release, post or model output. Their apparent agreement cannot be counted as independent corroboration.
4. **Equivalent proposition within a specified context:** two distinct reports may have the same claim subject/predicate/object after explicit and qualified entity/time/unit mappings; they still have separate source and evaluation evidence.
5. **Independent evidence:** different production paths and methods support or oppose a claim, with source dependency and reliability qualifications. Two highly correlated sensors, bots, mirrored articles or subsidiaries are not automatically independent because there are two URLs.

The existing Communicating Newswire source key correctly separates feed id and external item id; retain this mechanism for its domain. A Group-wide correlation graph needs real witnessed copy/derivation relations and cannot infer independence from unique hashes. Event identities, evidence provenance, FOUR WIT distinction-steps, counterparty ACK and matched economic tally remain different sorts.

### 7a.4 Monitor at the edge, not by asking an LLM to read the world

A **source-owned sensing operator** exposes its genuine kind and handedness h(d) = (receive, send, actuate, observe), including time/byte/energy/financial expense, permissions, errors and bounded sample window. For each source and carrier type, choose the cheapest legitimate sensor which can preserve the distinction the receiver needs: RSS/Atom item identity, Git revision and exact changed paths, a signed or authenticated ActivityPub delivery, a physiological reading with units and calibration, or a scoped human-session speech act. Cross-channel mapping is a qualified downstream relation, not a reason to invent one opaque "text" type.

A useful local cascade is: admission/replay → typed extraction → deterministic change recognition → source-origin clustering → for/against evidence admission → cheap domain-specific screening → selective expensive analysis. Two source observations can be joined monotonically while decisions depending on "exactly true", "no other participant", "latest", "exclusive writer" or irreversible action require a competent owner and ordered epoch. This is the CALM boundary, not an argument for either universal consensus or universal lock freedom.

**Event time is not processing time.** Provider event time, sensor sampling time, inbox receiving time and host processing time require different fields; their clocks can disagree and events can arrive out of order. Per-source cursors and watermarks must state what they cover and what lateness they tolerate. A watermark is an estimate of completeness for a window, not proof that all possible sources or all conversations were observed. A late correction contributes new evidence and recomputes affected derived views; it does not rewrite an earlier source act as if it never happened. Apache Beam's event-time/watermark/early/late trigger model is a qualifying outside comparison, not automatic permission to replace the Group's native event substrate.

### 7a.5 Asymmetric swarm composition and controllable temporary hierarchy

Robot-swarm research supplies mechanisms, not a proof that a thousand heterogeneous LLMs become a colony. Classical stigmergy coordinates via state left in a common environment; a generic shared database is not automatically stigmergic unless local observers change action in response to its trace gradients. Swarm members here have unequal observation, transmission, effect and budget surfaces. Their abilities and rights are read from current source-owned office and skill records, not from a model's self-description.

Use **bounded locality**: each agent receives the portion of the causal/evidential field relevant to its actual office, work, target and legal visibility, plus named frontier pointers for requesting further lawful evidence. Low-cost agents or deterministic systems should admit common/known source changes without invoking language models. Stigmergic depositions remain source-qualified, not silent votes. A shared field can contain nonmatching interpretations of one local fact without one controller flattening them.

Use **temporary scoped coordination** for cross-source identity adjudication, an authoritative financial or safety decision, an external principal's permission, conflicting actor rights, destructive state changes or coordination under congestion. The owner-specific coordination form may change when communication load, membership and authority change. Self-organizing Nervous Systems (Zhu et al. 2024) are relevant evidence for reconfigurable local hierarchies in heterogeneous robot swarms, while Soma et al. 2023 demonstrate that communication and motion congestion can defeat presumed swarm scalability. These are engineering comparators; they do not appoint a new 561 Group chain of command or prove equivalence of robot embodiment and agent offices.

The true cost objective is a **vector** with named units: ingested bytes and transport retries, storage bytes·time, decode CPU time, inference tokens and dollars, network bandwidth, human interruptions, wall-clock decision latency, energy where metered, and external outcomes received. A simple O(N + |L|) substrate claim is conditional on bounded reads/writes, indexed locality and a specified work unit; arbitrary graph scans, nested joins, model calls and replays invalidate it. Measure scaling under actual offered load instead of projecting an asymptotic slogan onto all components.

### 7a.6 Signal is task-relative evidence, not a property of a message

One event can be noise to a newsroom, vital to an expiring grant, irrelevant to engineering, and a high-value input to a scientific counterexample. Define signal relative to (source-qualified claim or change, legitimate receiver, operational question, time horizon, admitted action alternatives, cost/rights). Do not manufacture a single scalar "truthiness" or global priority from the number of mentions.

Keep at least six distinct observations of frontier value: new source evidence; contradiction or missing evidence on an important condition; a deadline approaching while a uniquely qualified actor has not disposed of it; a dependency whose resolution unlocks several real recipients; novel possibility worth an information-gathering experiment; and independently received benefit or failure that changes later sensing. Ranking can be lexicographic or Pareto under operational policy, preserving source and refusal reasons. Monetary expected value and probabilistic expected information gain require a calibrated probability model and a payer/beneficiary, not raw FOUR counts.

The **attention allocation** problem at cycle t is choose a feasible set S from a source-derived candidate frontier F under explicit capacities and rights. Compare measured, calibrated marginal consequence ΔU(x | K_t, S) against the *additional* cost of reading, investigating and acting on x. Maintain reserved capacity for rare high-consequence negative evidence and exploration of currently undercovered source families. Prefer independently received completions per scarce attention expenditure to “loudest source” or “most repeated item”. The finite-budget ATT allocation in ObsmetrologiaEconomics supports abstract cost/benefit feasibility but does not prove that arbitrary API tokens, human minutes, physical joules and WIT covers have equal denomination. Keep those measured resources separate until a legitimate bridge is derived.

**Prevent feedback collapse.** If the monitor samples only channels that already ranked highly, its future evidence confirms its earlier policy and blinds the Group to new publics/risks. Source policy therefore needs explicit exploration and diversity constraints; bounded sentinel sampling of low-attention/quiet sources; and recorded why a source was not sampled. A server failure is a visibility obstruction, not evidence that the watched condition is false. Noise, manipulation and prompt-injection content can be studied as data but cannot issue office instructions.

### 7a.7 Stage signatures: residual knowledge must cross without granting power

At a fixed provider/office/context version k, distinguish the following proposed *typed* transformations (their names here specify contracts, not existing exported symbols):

- **Monitor / Process:** (authorized source handles, source-specific cursors, allowed byte/time budget, prior monitor residue) → (bounded source-qualified arrivals, source error/coverage evidence, new observable events, cursor candidates, unobserved/late-input residue). Acquisition and evidence admission use the original owners; cursor commit requires the producer's actual qualified semantics.
- **Analyze / Inquiry:** (admitted observations and their for/against relations, problem/receiver, qualified historical knowledge, bounded inquiry budget) → (competing evidence-backed hypotheses or revised inquiry question, source-dependency/conflict explanation, epistemic unknowns, used resources). An LLM may propose hypotheses but cannot supply missing provenance or independent recognition. Analysis does not authorize an effect.
- **Plan / Derivation:** (hypotheses, goals/requirements, scoped authority and feasibility facts, available office capacities, deadline and measurement conventions) → (candidate moves, estimated costs/benefits and uncertainties, discriminating next observations, preconditions and unresolved plan residue). Science qualifies what is possible; Group/customer/Board evidence qualifies which possibilities are necessary; Engineering settles specification and design. A proposal of a move is not admission to execute.
- **Execute / Procedure:** (one permitted move, actual authenticated incumbent and object capability, current epoch, effect preconditions and limits) → (effect attempted, authorized refusal, or observed outcome; independent receiving pending/recognized/contested; exact executor and causal traces). Environment transitions are not presumed monotone. Producer-signed or self-reported success is not counterparty ACK.
- **Knowledge / recursively improving loop:** (all retained source/effect evidence and original independent receiving, model revision and operational policy) → (updated source reliability observations, lawful monitoring parameters, measurement corrections, revised scientific questions and actual accepted capabilities). A failed action can grow Knowledge while leaving the goal unmet. No source can rewrite its own past false positive into an ACK by doing more summarization.

K is **typed**, at least distinguishing models, goals, history, beliefs/evidence, action semantics and epistemic confidence/coverage as in the July 11 study. Operational state, belief state, normative state and actual world state are different carriers. New inputs u_k transport between contexts; fixed-point claims apply only within their specified snapshot/operator. A source-corrected requirement or changed authorization can re-open a previously derived move even when old evidence remains retained.

### 7a.8 A falsifiable native receiving experiment, not a dashboard exercise

Begin with five independent input regimes and actual source owners, not one synthetic giant text stream:

1. Source-code/Work change feed: existing authorized GitHub issue/PR/commit receipts with exact repo, revision, principal, Work identity and branch/dependency facts.
2. Public publishing syndication: original AOE Communicating RSS/Atom Newswire on at least two independently supplied feeds plus a repeated syndicated copy, retaining item identity and cursor/late-event behavior.
3. Authenticated agent activity: existing MetroPlat/FeltOS Work Arena and selected, source-qualified ActivityPub/CloudEvents submissions from genuine different actors; one body's own ACK must refuse.
4. Session/Leader input: **only** an actually authorized provider feed with source cursor and scoped privacy release; if that provider lacks an exhaustive cross-session API, coverage remains partial/unknown. The already existing Leader Movement Team assessor stays the speech-act/privacy boundary; no private full conversation dump into public Science.
5. A controlled physical or synthetic sensor family (or an independently observable production host metric) with explicit units, timestamps, calibration and a deliberately biased/failing sensor. Synthetic data must never be represented as a live physical event.

Run recorded adversarial replay cases before claiming useful scale: (a) 100× duplicate/repost burst, which must not create 100 independently verified WITs or 100 Work threads; (b) one credible late contrary observation after apparent agreement; (c) silent source and stale cursor, returning unknown coverage rather than false; (d) rare urgent source amid popular repetitive chatter, with reserved timely budget; (e) malicious payload instructing an agent to bypass governance; (f) private/personal data mapped to a corporate project with no release, which must refuse; (g) processor crash between receipt and cursor commit; (h) over-capacity producer and bounded consumer; (i) precondition or authorization revoked after plan creation; (j) a completed agent report lacking independent customer receiving. Also test different channel units, contradictory clocks, mirrored-but-not-independent sources and a dynamically changed actor roster.

Compare against **three actual baselines** on the same fixed source traces: FIFO by arrival time; single-model batch summarization within the same financial/latency limits; and the existing native Work/Newswire producers without the new selective frontier. The proposed hybrid must measure p50/p95/p99 observation-to-qualified-disposition and observation-to-received-effect latencies, source identity and temporal reconstruction correctness, recall of predeclared consequential events, false escalation per real timely disposition, missed-coverage fraction with its denominator, actual independent ACK rate, cost vector and under-load recovery. A single aggregate accuracy score can hide failure on scarce critical negatives; report per-class results with fixed ground truth, controls and legitimate reviewers. Distinguish confirmed accepted action from a staged Work proposal and source-only tests from installed receiver use.

**Falsifiers**: the new frontier misses a protected rare event that FIFO finds; correlation folding hides independent counterevidence; a private source crosses principal boundaries; lateness rewrites history; a source outage is labeled false; a listener infers actor powers from a received message; the hybrid costs more and acts no more usefully than the native baseline under matched capacity; or a planned action is called received despite an absent counterpart. Any such case reopens the original design, not a certificate-generation task.

## 8 Proof and implementation obligations

Before a runtime claims to implement the proposed theory, require the following evidence:

1. **Carrier construction.** Define each stage's domain, codomain, order, joins or meets used, admissible result families, residue type, and context index using the actual Sign-family primitives.
2. **Local laws.** Prove monotonicity and extensivity for each inference step where claimed; construct saturation and prove its idempotence. For nuclei, prove finite-meet preservation. For commuting projections, prove commutation.
3. **Semantic linkage.** Explain and justify how σ and Δ represent meaning and execution settlement for the constructed work objects. An enumerated field called `inquiry` is not this proof.
4. **Cross-stage correctness.** Establish preconditions and postconditions for each translation and show that settled output and residue are consumed without losing provenance, uncertainty, authority, or unresolved obligations.
5. **Composition.** Prove the selected whole-cycle result, or give counterexamples and a weaker valid contract. Local closure laws alone do not establish safe control of the coupled world.
6. **Open-input handling.** Specify observation admission, changing rule and model versions, material transitions, transport between contexts, and reopening conditions. Distinguish snapshot closure from all-future invariance.
7. **Work-kind behavior.** Exercise an actual Process, Inquiry, Derivation, and Procedure with runbooks. Verify that inquiry can transform its question, derivation produces its typed judgment, procedure routes on evidenced conditions, and process reproduces its specified transformation. Preserve budget exhaustion and blocked effects as typed outcomes. An empty or failed model transport is not by itself an inquiry judgment that no answer exists; that conclusion needs its own evidenced result.
8. **Authority and receiving.** Ensure the executor independently checks permitted effects and current preconditions. Verify actual recipient access and acceptance where required. Scientific possibility and mathematical necessity do not grant authority to act.
9. **Realization chain.** Link the reviewed specification to generated or implemented source, tests and proof artifacts, then to the actual consumer in the agent orchestration engine, FeltOS, and MetroPlat. Verify the versioned consumer, not only a fixture or descriptor.
10. **Improvement.** Retain failed obligations and observed mismatch as evidence for revising the theory or implementation. A revised model must record which guarantees survive and which require renewed proof.

The first useful implementation should be bounded enough to demonstrate these distinctions, while using existing owning services and data types. It should include the noncommuting-closure counterexample, a changing-input example that reopens residue, and a blocked-execution example where knowledge grows but the requested effect remains unmet. These are receiving requirements, not a claim that tests have already passed.
