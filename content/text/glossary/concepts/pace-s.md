**PACE-S** names the general loop of Pause, Attune, Care, and Engage over Somatic knowledge. The four verbs Pause/Attune/Care/Engage form [PACE Yourself](../../../../../public-services-section/services/public-health-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pace-yourself.md), the house pacing brand for infection-associated chronic illness. The next day, the fifth term linked the loop to MAPE-K (Monitor, Analyze, Plan, Execute, Knowledge -- see the [MAPE-K research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/11/2026-07-11-research-on-mape-k.md)): PACE-S / Somatic.

## Correspondence to MAPE-K

| PACE-S | MAPE-K | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pause | Monitor | Stop and take a reading before continuing; the pause is itself the act of observation. |
| Attune | Analyze | Read what the observation means against one's own baseline, not a population threshold. |
| Care | Plan | Choose the response that repairs relation rather than extracts. |
| Engage | Execute | Re-enter action on the terms the prior three stages established. |
| Somatic | Knowledge | The body's own record -- felt sense, biometric trace, history -- as the persistent substrate the loop reads and writes. |

## Realized and unrealized

[PACE Yourself](../../../../../public-services-section/services/public-health-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-pace-yourself.md) is PACE-S's first realized instance, documented with the four verbs (Pause, Attune, Care, Engage). Somatic was named the following day and is not part of that four-verb record. Whether and how to add it remains unresolved and belongs to public-health-services-section, not to this concept record.

A second, distinct application is under live discussion as of 2026-09-11: using PACE-S as the self-regulation loop for an office-holder (initially the `executive-officer-to-the-leader` office) -- Pausing on a Leader affect signal, Attuning to what it actually indicates, Care grounding the response in genuine professional perspective, Engaging from that place. This application has no body to supply Somatic, so what "Somatic" becomes for a non-embodied office is an open design question, not yet answered by the Founder. No duty or skill record exists for this application yet; this paragraph is the only persisted trace of it pending that design work.
