A **requirement** is a statement that some relationship among phenomena in the world shall hold, that a principal needs it, and that an observation could show whether it holds. The word names the first stage of the chain requirement, specification, architecture, design, fabrication, operation, service.

## What science holds
1. **A requirement concerns the world, a specification the machine.** Zave and Jackson separate a desired relationship among environmental phenomena (the requirement) from the machine behavior sufficient to help establish it (the specification). The topic [requirements to specification](../../../../services/engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/requirements-to-specification.md) also separates a goal, an expectation and a decision from both, and states that provenance is not truth.
2. **A requirement is testable.** It is one statement with a named failure and an acceptance or refusal an independent observer could record (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148; [software requirements specification](../../../../services/engineering-science-services-section/services/systems-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/software-requirements-specification.md)). A statement whose failure cannot be named is a wish.
3. **A requirement belongs to a principal and keeps their words.** The requester's source wording is preserved apart from any refinement into stakeholder, system and software requirements.
4. **A requirement is data derived from claims about tokens.** It belongs to the actual accountable section, not content publication. An *authored condition* names a subject, criterion and desired reading; the *measurement* that can settle it is a separately sourced event. A shell `command` is one possible instrument for a machine-measurable condition, not a necessary property of all stakeholder or service requirements. A missing command means unmeasured until independently supported evidence arrives, **not** satisfied. The Group's `requirements-derive-from-claims-about-tokens` rule constrains provenance and placement; it does not itself say every condition is a command.
5. **A figure the principal states is the acceptance line.** A budget of memory, time, disk or cost is recorded as the requirement and is the line every change is judged against (rule `a-leader-resource-budget-is-the-acceptance-line`).
6. **A failing implementation is not a withdrawn requirement.** The requirement persists until it is met or the principal retires it; its status is measured, not reported.
7. **Attestation is a separate event.** A requirement is distributed, attested or closed by events recorded apart from it, and a body does not attest its own work.

## From qualified possibility to market necessity

The original [Lenticule Science research question](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/research/group-demand/science-to-market-projection-20261018.md) proposes a **partial, source-preserving projection**, not a theorem equating science and markets. Science refines original notes into qualified propositions, exclusions, counterexamples and precise glossary senses. A versioned Science statement establishes a *warranted possibility under named assumptions*; it does not establish that any buyer wants it. Independently, the corporate Group qualifies the buyer or beneficiary's actual task, alternatives, demand, standards, price/cost and rights in a dated receiving context. That market observation establishes a *conditional necessity for that context*, not a general truth about the scientific theory.

Only with both sources and a competent admitting decision can the Group propose an engineering-facing requirement. Preserve the originating utterance, scientific claim/proof status and counterexamples, market observation and its opposite evidence, rights and jurisdiction, omitted interpretations, and the named assayer/receiver. Absent warranted science, an actual relevant need, lawful authority or an identifiable beneficiary, the correct result is an **unknown or refusal**, not a newly minted feature. A second receiving context may demand a different service from the same scientific possibility without editing the Science result. A proposed public SaaS must be tested as **running, rights-respecting, economically viable and independently customer-received**, not accepted merely as published source code.

The related relational senses [Possible](../../../../services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/possible.md) and [Necessary](../../../../services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/necessary.md) refer to contextual inquiry; neither alone confers engineering feasibility or commercial demand. Formal caution matters: [Obsmetrologia's same-datum gluing counterexample](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Closure/ProjectionHiddenObstruction.lean) proves that one rich source lacks a global Record-valued section although its FOUR-charge image glues. This **does not prove** the market transformation, but prevents claiming that a coarse, positive reading always reflects settlement of a richer underlying obligation. Its exact hypotheses must not be generalized by metaphor.

## Acceptance cells are not evidence

In the existing [Coesite-owned requirement shape](https://github.com/coesite-operations/operations-services-section/blob/main/services/requirement-management-operation-services-section/schemas/requirement.schema.json), `met-when` comprises addressed `cell`, desired `target`, optional explanatory `says`, and optional real `command`/exit expectations. These are **conditions to assess**, not measured truth, execution authorizations or independently received Work. [Organizing's original requirement reader](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/agent-organization-engine/blob/main/lib/organizing/src/requirements.mjs) instead derives operative `REQUIRES` from attributed SignCell telling and observed `SATISFIES` separately; its FOUR readings distinguish unknown, violation, contest and satisfaction. An authored JSON value `"target":"held"` states a *desired outcome*, not a held observation. Testing whether that JSON file exists can establish source custody, never the service effect it describes. The earlier claim in this term that **“each condition is a command a machine runs”** was overbroad and is superseded, not preserved as a normative rule.

## In the 561 Group
The Group's operational account, with the improvement skill, is the term [requirement](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/requirement.md) in 561-group-services-section. Requirements are the input of [meet-then-beat](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meet-then-beat.md), which measures a capability against the real-world alternatives, and of [using-and-improving](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/using-and-improving.md), which meets them by improving the Group's own software.
