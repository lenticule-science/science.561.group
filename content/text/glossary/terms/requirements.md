A **requirement** is a statement that some relationship among phenomena in the world shall hold, that a principal needs it, and that an observation could show whether it holds. The word names the first stage of the chain requirement, specification, architecture, design, fabrication, operation, service.

## What science holds
1. **A requirement concerns the world, a specification the machine.** Zave and Jackson separate a desired relationship among environmental phenomena (the requirement) from the machine behavior sufficient to help establish it (the specification). The topic [requirements to specification](../../../../services/engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/requirements-to-specification.md) also separates a goal, an expectation and a decision from both, and states that provenance is not truth.
2. **A requirement is testable.** It is one statement with a named failure and an acceptance or refusal an independent observer could record (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148; [software requirements specification](../../../../services/engineering-science-services-section/services/systems-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/software-requirements-specification.md)). A statement whose failure cannot be named is a wish.
3. **A requirement belongs to a principal and keeps their words.** The requester's source wording is preserved apart from any refinement into stakeholder, system and software requirements.
4. **A requirement is data derived from claims about tokens.** It is held as data at the section it concerns, never as content, and each condition is a command a machine runs (rule `requirements-derive-from-claims-about-tokens`).
5. **A figure the principal states is the acceptance line.** A budget of memory, time, disk or cost is recorded as the requirement and is the line every change is judged against (rule `a-leader-resource-budget-is-the-acceptance-line`).
6. **A failing implementation is not a withdrawn requirement.** The requirement persists until it is met or the principal retires it; its status is measured, not reported.
7. **Attestation is a separate event.** A requirement is distributed, attested or closed by events recorded apart from it, and a body does not attest its own work.

## In the 561 Group
The Group's operational account, with the improvement skill, is the term [requirement](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/requirement.md) in 561-group-services-section. Requirements are the input of [meet-then-beat](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meet-then-beat.md), which measures a capability against the real-world alternatives, and of [using-and-improving](../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/using-and-improving.md), which meets them by improving the Group's own software.
