**L-Space** is an enterprise of the 561 Group, a market in the sense of the Group's organizing agency, owned by the entity `561-group-llc` and hosted on `bare-cedar-fog`. Its faces are the readers and researchers of Lenticule Science's research corpus. It publishes that corpus as a public knowledge garden at `l-space.561.group`, and the corpus is walked as the VaultMaze world. Its office is `l-space-lead`; the work it assesses is `assessing-l-space-demand` by the skill `assess-taken-demand`.

See `561-group-semioverse-l-space` for L-Space as the 561 Group holds it in the Semioverse.
