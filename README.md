# Lenticule Science

The **lab** of the 561 Group. Organized as a swarm.

Lenticule Science is the 561 Group's research unit — where the firm's
foundational Obsmetrologia mathematics and its independently contractable
semantic and metrological services are developed. It sits under the 561 Group
polity alongside the RSC Quiescent product company.

## Standard books this unit keeps

- `research-agenda.md` — the standing questions the lab is pursuing.
- `experiment-log/` — what was tried, what was found (append-only).
- `milestones.md` — the lab's current milestone per line of work.

## Org

- **Over:** 561 Group (polity, tióšpaye).
- **Sibling:** RSC Quiescent (product company).
- **Organized as:** swarm.
- **Owns:** Obsmetrological Services Section
  (`services/obsmetrological-services-section/`) — a Support Node
  Metro-Enterprise that itself owns Obsmetrologia and maintains OML, its
  Primary Support Node Micro-Enterprise.
