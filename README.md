# Lenticule Science publication source

**Proposed address:** `https://science.561.group/`. Source exists; publication and
provider deployment are **not yet established**. The authoritative property
status is in Causeway Public Web's `sites/registry.json`.

- [`site.json`](site.json) — this organization's TUFTE site parameters.
- [`content/intro.md`](content/intro.md) — unreleased editorial candidate,
  retained as `status: potential` until received by the owning editor.

**What belongs here:** Explain a research question through its assumptions, observations, formal claims, counterexamples and remaining uncertainty. Treat a model, a proof, a design analogy and a measured result as different things. Link to relevant admitted Science evidence; do not turn an accessible L-Space room into a public publication by inference.

**Publication authority:** Science's own publication and reuse authority. Subject decisions do
not transfer to the renderer, the estate registry or the machine operator.
No draft, readable repository page or green test becomes a public article by
itself.

**Shared build/release and social distribution procedure:**
[Causeway Public Web's single web-estate runbook](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-web-services-section/blob/main/sites/README.md).
Do not copy that procedure into this directory. Use the admitted Red Cup TUFTE
publisher, one authorized release route and independently observed public
receiving before claiming this address is live.

Publication orders and original source receipts are held in `data/records/`;
the October 20 portal candidate materialization is in `data/logs/`. Historical
receipts retain their original revision-relative paths and hashes. These
artifacts record source and candidate history, not current deployment.
