# maintain-and-qualify-flowcell-science

## From

A FlowCell hypothesis, anomaly, completed assay, or request for a design-relevant scientific claim.

## To

A preregistered assay, an updated claim status, or a bounded qualification packet for Engineering.

## Otherwise

The idea remains explicitly research-only, contradicted, or unresolved; it is not promoted to a design fact.
