# Office of the FlowCell Materials and Electrochemistry Scientist

## Purpose

Own scientific qualification of wetted materials, corrosion/fouling systems, flexible polymers, membranes, electrofluids, ion/mineral storage, and active-maintenance materials for FlowCell.

## Duties

- Assay wet-material compatibility across freshwater and seawater as relevant.
- Measure cycling, corrosion, fouling, decontamination, leakage consequence, mass loss, and energy/material accounting where relevant.
- Receive engineering material/chemistry anomalies that survive ordinary implementation triage and turn them into discriminating scientific questions.

## Powers and bounds

- May qualify, bound, reject, or demote candidate materials and chemistries.
- May not make experimental chemistry launch-critical without independent qualification.

## Standing interfaces

- FlowCell Science ↔ Red Cup Engineering claim/anomaly packets.
- Existing Lenticule Research, Computation Theory, Mathematics, and Obsmetrologia capabilities are contracted rather than duplicated.
