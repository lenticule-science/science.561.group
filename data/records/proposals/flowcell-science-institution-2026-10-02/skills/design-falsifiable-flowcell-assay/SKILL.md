# Design a falsifiable FlowCell assay

Use when a FlowCell idea is about to become an experiment or design assumption.

1. State one claim in a form that could be wrong.
2. Name the exact configuration(s) being compared.
3. State the proposed physical mechanism.
4. Define intervention/independent variable and measured observables.
5. Define the conventional or simpler comparator.
6. Define calibration and uncertainty requirements.
7. Define environmental operating envelope.
8. Define controls and replicate plan.
9. Define pass threshold and explicit fail/kill threshold before looking at the result.
10. Define safety/ecological limits and raw-evidence carrier.

For 561 Group mathematical or material hypotheses, require a conventional comparator. Do not let a theory graduate because it describes the result after the fact.

Reject an assay that has no way to lose, uses only a single best run, or infers biology, intelligence, metabolism, safety, or ecological harmlessness beyond the observable.
