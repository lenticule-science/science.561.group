# Return a FlowCell field anomaly to Science

Accept an anomaly only after ordinary engineering causes are checked to the extent practical.

Carry:
- exact configuration and parent revision;
- expected model or qualified claim;
- observed behavior and mismatch size;
- raw traces/event timing;
- calibration state;
- reproduction count;
- implementation causes checked;
- environmental state;
- safety/ecological consequence;
- one discriminating scientific question.

Do not return “it behaved weirdly.” Preserve contradictory evidence and enough context for Science to design a decisive assay without redoing Engineering triage.
