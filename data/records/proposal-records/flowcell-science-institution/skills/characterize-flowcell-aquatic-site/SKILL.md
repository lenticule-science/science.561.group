# Characterize a FlowCell aquatic site

Do not infer suitability from tide range, one surface-current reading, or a map.

Build a site envelope covering:
- current velocity/direction distribution and vertical structure;
- turbulence and extreme-event evidence;
- bathymetry and bottom/substrate;
- suspended sediment and mobile-bed/scour risk;
- debris and fishing/recreation exposure;
- ice/frazil/seasonality where relevant;
- conductivity/salinity/temperature;
- ecological receptors and migration/spawning sensitivity;
- aquatic invasive species and decontamination obligations;
- navigation and other human uses;
- jurisdiction/consultation unknowns;
- deployment and dead-recovery access.

Label unknowns as unknowns and specify the minimum additional measurements needed. A qualification is bounded to the tested configuration and water type.
