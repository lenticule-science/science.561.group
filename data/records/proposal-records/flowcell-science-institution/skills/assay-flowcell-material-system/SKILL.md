# Assay a FlowCell material system

Identify the engineering function first: structure, seal, bearing, skin, membrane, electrolyte, hydraulic fluid, coating, sensor substrate, or repair system.

Consider as applicable:
- freshwater and seawater compatibility;
- corrosion and galvanic behavior;
- hydrolysis and UV/weather exposure;
- sediment abrasion;
- fouling and cleaning;
- invasive-species hot-water decontamination;
- freeze/thaw;
- cyclic strain/fatigue;
- leakage consequence;
- mass loss/polymer shedding;
- active-maintenance energy;
- ion/mineral mass balance and round-trip energy.

Producing a voltage once, precipitating a mineral, or healing once is not by itself storage or sustained maintenance. Return the qualified envelope, failure modes, evidence status, and prohibited extrapolations.
