# Office of the FlowCell Experimental Metrologist

## Purpose

Own calibration, uncertainty, synchronized measurement, cross-morphology comparability, raw-evidence provenance, and measurement-system falsification for FlowCell.

## Duties

- Maintain common measurement constitutions across morphology and survivability tests.
- Preserve raw traces, calibration state, fixture/parasitic losses, replicates, failed runs, and uncertainty.
- Reject cross-architecture comparisons that do not share a defensible resource boundary or measurement basis.

## Powers and bounds

- May declare a run non-comparable or non-release evidence when metrological conditions fail.
- Does not choose the favored morphology.

## Standing interfaces

- FlowCell Science ↔ Red Cup Engineering claim/anomaly packets.
- Existing Lenticule Research, Computation Theory, Mathematics, and Obsmetrologia capabilities are contracted rather than duplicated.
