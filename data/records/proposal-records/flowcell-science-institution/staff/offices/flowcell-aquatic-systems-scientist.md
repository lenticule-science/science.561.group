# Office of the FlowCell Aquatic Systems Scientist

## Purpose

Own the measured aquatic operating envelope for FlowCell: currents, sediment, scour, ice, fish/wildlife exposure, invasive-species constraints, navigation, and site ecology.

## Duties

- Characterize candidate sites as coupled physical/ecological systems rather than velocity numbers.
- Define sediment, debris, fouling, ice, fish-exposure, decontamination, and environmental monitoring needs for experiments.
- State unknowns as unknowns and specify the minimum observations needed to qualify or reject a site.

## Powers and bounds

- May bound or reject a site qualification on ecological or measurement grounds.
- May not infer ecological harmlessness from a null observation.

## Standing interfaces

- FlowCell Science ↔ Red Cup Engineering claim/anomaly packets.
- Existing Lenticule Research, Computation Theory, Mathematics, and Obsmetrologia capabilities are contracted rather than duplicated.
