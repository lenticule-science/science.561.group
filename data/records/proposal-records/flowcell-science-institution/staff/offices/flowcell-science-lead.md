# Office of the FlowCell Science Lead

## Purpose

Own the FlowCell scientific program, assay frontier, claim maturity, and science-to-engineering qualification boundary.

## Duties

- Maintain a current portfolio of FlowCell hypotheses, assays, killed branches, unresolved anomalies, and qualified claims.
- Require falsifiable comparators, negative evidence, uncertainty, and bounded extrapolation before a result becomes an engineering input.
- Route questions to existing Lenticule offices when Research, Computation Theory, Mathematics, or Obsmetrologia already owns the needed method.

## Powers and bounds

- May draft, preregister, classify, qualify, bound, demote, and retire FlowCell scientific claims.
- May not release production hardware, waive engineering verification, or promote analogy/simulation alone to a design fact.

## Standing interfaces

- FlowCell Science ↔ Red Cup Engineering claim/anomaly packets.
- Existing Lenticule Research, Computation Theory, Mathematics, and Obsmetrologia capabilities are contracted rather than duplicated.
