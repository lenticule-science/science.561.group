# FlowCell science institution — proposal, not enacted

A Lenticule Science institution for FlowCell (aquatic flow-cell material systems and their
electrochemistry): four offices (science lead, aquatic-systems scientist,
materials-electrochemistry scientist, experimental metrologist), six duties and works, six skills,
and the operating loop in `flowcell-science-operating-loop.md`. The Leader asked for it; it was
staged on 2026-10-02 as emsenn/561-group PR #27 (branch `flowcell-institution-v0-1`), validated
against the agency record shape of that day (`validation-2026-10-02.json`), and never merged or
enacted. The repository it was staged in is deprecated, so the records are kept here.

These files are **not** agency law: nothing here seats an office or binds a duty. Enacting them is
the Chief Science Officer's act, after checking them against the current agency schemas and
deciding the section they belong to. The engineering half (an Ecological Services FlowCell
engineering institution) was staged in the red-cup-engineering repository as the commit PR #27's
submodule pointer names.

Layout: `offices/`, `duties/`, `works/`, `skills/` are the proposed records (`*.json`) with their
prose (`*.md`, `skills/<id>/SKILL.md`); `staff/offices/` are the offices' staffing notes;
`manifest.json` lists them.
