**Olfactory Metrology** — demand: https://github.com/561-group/561-group-opportunity-services-section/issues/23.
Scientific question: can a multi-reference cross-site calibration predict blinded measurements at held-out sites better than a single-reference baseline? Predeclare response type, censoring, domain, site/reference/day blocks and evaluation window. The primary observables are held-out interval coverage and proper predictive score by reference. Compare within-site repeatability with between-site transfer error; avoid interpreting narrow within-site error as inter-lab comparability. Falsifier: held-out coverage violates the declared target or the added multi-reference terms do not improve predictive performance within uncertainty. Red Cup receiving: a blinded crossed-design fixture, graph connectivity checks, synthetic drift and label-permutation negatives, leakage-free leave-one-site-out folds. Science acceptance still requires a rights-cleared real dataset and independent reanalysis; not accredited proficiency testing.
