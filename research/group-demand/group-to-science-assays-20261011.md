## Physarum receiving boundary

The experiment must discriminate geometry, persistence, morphology, extracellular cues and internal memory rather than collapsing them into one explanation. Engineering supplies mirrored, dimension-certified, parametric maze apparatus and records print revision and environmental conditions. Science reconstructs the published event counts, preregisters randomized trials and mirrors, includes indecision as a reported outcome, and tests corrected geometry against competing mechanisms. A corrected geometric replicate that removes an effect falsifies the **geometry-invariant hypothesis for that assay**, not generalized learning capability. A printed device is not an observed biological result.

## Common acceptance contract

For every demand, keep a single canonical research record specifying: provenance and custodian; operational construct and units; hypotheses/baselines; rights and sampling frame; uncertainty, falsifier and negative controls; a versioned Engineering fixture or Science protocol; *independent* receiving; and the next observable. Dispositions must distinguish **implemented**, **synthetically verified**, **empirically supported**, **contradicted**, **not identifiable**, and **unassayed**. Do not infer a successful public service, accredited status, revenue, real institutional autonomy, or governed process from a commit or an illustrative fixture.

This register supersedes its own earlier duplicate protocol summaries and the separate 2026-10-11 one-paragraph Olfactory Science Intake. Changes to the detailed assays belong in their linked canonical records, not another synopsis here.
