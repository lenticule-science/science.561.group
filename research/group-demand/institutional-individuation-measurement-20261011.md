Given finite Markov transition kernel P and a predefined state partition π, define q_x(B)=Σ_{z:π(z)=B}P(x,z). Strong lumpability requires q_x(B)=q_y(B) for all source states x,y sharing a block and each destination block B. Measure ε = max_same_block(x,y) 1/2 Σ_B |q_x(B)-q_y(B)|. The one-block and singleton partitions are non-discriminating controls.

For field testing, predeclare the state taxonomy, candidate partitions, observation unit, temporal splits, missingness, numerical tolerance and uncertainty method. Estimate transitions from provenance-retaining event observations; evaluate held-out predictive loss and calibration against unconstrained and simpler baselines. Check stationarity and state coverage. A partition failing held-out loss or sensitivity checks falsifies its scoped predictive-closure claim, not an institution's legitimacy.

Synthetic four-case algebra requires no new science. Field measurement remains unassayed. The Board retains institutional decisions.
