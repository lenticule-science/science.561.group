## Independent scientific and interoperability baselines

The Group's research does not establish every mathematical property by
citational resemblance. The following are **external comparators and
constraints**, with specifically limited scope, useful for falsifying or
refining the proposed projection:

- [Foster, Greenwald, Moore, Pierce and Schmitt, *Combinators for Bidirectional Tree Transformations* (ACM TOPLAS, 2007)](https://natefoster.org/publications/) established a lens-based family of bidirectional **data-view update** techniques. That research motivates checking whether local projected edits preserve the underlying source, and when view/reconciliation laws fail. But a provenance link from a software requirement back to a science claim is **not an inverse lens**, and an authorized market revision need not be reversible as a transformation of science. Derive any analogous laws rather than borrowing `GetPut`/`PutGet` as a slogan.
- [Abramsky and Brandenburger, *The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality* (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264) rigorously relates compatible measurement-context data and obstruction to global sections in its own mathematical setting. The relevant analogy is testing **overlap restrictions and non-gluing** of locally valid claims, never assuming a public office, a customer, or FOAM data is a quantum measurement scenario. A Group-specific sheaf requires declared base, contexts, restrictions and independently exhibited obstruction.
- [W3C PROV-O Recommendation (2013)](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) provides representations for entities, activities, agents, derivation, revisions and *qualified relations* needed to retain who transformed evidence, by which activity and when. This is an interoperability option for **transformation provenance**, not a source of institutional authority or evidence that a scientific proposition is true.
- [W3C SKOS Recommendation (2009)](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/) provides concept schemes, labels, notes and related concepts appropriate to a **glossary projection**. SKOS makes terms addressable and interoperable; it does not prove a theorem, define a legal office or decide a market requirement. The Group already has a generated SKOS projection; improve its Engineering generator rather than hand-edit its output.
- [W3C SHACL Recommendation (2017)](https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/) checks RDF **data graphs against declared shapes**, making explicit target selection, graph population and validation scope essential. The Group's Science-owned SHACL shapes can reject invalid populated incumbencies or receipt chains; a conformant *empty* target graph says nothing about actual corporate instances.
- [ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html) governs systems/software requirements engineering including services (reviewed and confirmed in 2024). It is an external requirements discipline, not an assertion that every philosophical possibility is commercially necessary or that the Group's specific five-stage admission process is the standard's mandated waterfall.

These references suggest competing methods—source-bound view-update discipline,
provenance qualification, glossary projection, graph integrity and external
requirement elicitation—whose relevant behavior can be tested *against*
the originating Leader request. Neither their combination nor a mathematical
identification of them with Obsmetrologia is currently proved.

## Operational constructs proposed for scientific assay

Let the following be *provisional typed carriers*, not freshly introduced foundational axioms:

- `U`: attributed original utterance/span and immutable source revision; authorization and speaker are separately assessed.
- `K`: a versioned body of source-qualified scientific propositions, theorem statements, declared premises, negative examples, empirical assays, open questions and uncertainty; distinct from the utterance that motivated study.
- `G(K)`: the owning Science glossary and semantic relations **derived from** `K`, recording sense, domain, codomain where applicable, intension, exclusions, revision and evidence. A label without those dependencies is not an admitted semantic macro.
- `M(b,j,t)`: a dated, independently sourced observation/claim about beneficiary `b`, jurisdiction/rights `j`, time `t`, alternatives, platform standards, economic contribution, constraints, and willingness/ability to receive or pay. Hypotheses about future demand remain explicitly hypotheses; demand is not inferred from the model's own proposal.
- `A`: distinct admissible authorities, including office/incumbency, science reviewer, contracting institution, software owner and receiving customer. An actor's view is not an appointment.
- `R`: uniquely traced candidate market/stakeholder/system requirements with original words, scientific justification, countercases, and separately receivable acceptance/refusal conditions.

The testable candidate is a **partial, guarded, provenance-preserving projection**
`P_(b,j,t) : (U, K, G(K), M(b,j,t), A) ⇀ (R, refusals, open research/market questions)`.
It is *partial* because no warranted science, no observed need, unresolved rights, or an unidentified receiver must be allowed to yield **no admitted requirement** rather than a plausible invented implementation. A changing market frame may alter `R` while leaving the scientific propositions in `K` unchanged; a scientific counterexample may invalidate particular descendant requirements without rewriting a customer's historic demand.

Projection is **not equivalence**. A downstream requirement or software artifact cannot generally reconstruct all of `K` or `U` by inference. Instead, every admitted descendant should retain **addressable source and transformation provenance**, including explicit information omitted by a local view. Proposed invariant `I_prov`: for each admitted requirement `r`, an authorized reader can recover its originating demand span, scientific proposition(s) and status, actual market/standards observations, applied constraints, responsible interpretation, and the scope of information lost. This is a *candidate experimentally decidable invariant for a specific carrier*, not a demonstrated theorem for the Semioverse.

**Provisional necessity distinction:** science may permit many realizations `Poss(K)`; market evidence and rights select or reject candidates `Need_(b,j,t)`. Requirements are not mathematically equal to either alone. The nature of their pairing—intersection, fiber product, guarded relation, or another structure—**remains a research choice** pending source-derived types, laws, countermodels and empirical need. Do not import conventional mathematics as the foundations of Obsmetrologia merely to name this pairing. Prove any proposed correspondence in the relevant downstream application theory without strengthening the pure kernel's primitives by assertion.

## Distinct projected views and refusers

A corporate ontology must keep these types and links separate: **Actor** (identifiable principal), **Character** (continuing enacted identity, not necessarily a distinct account), **Persona/Mask** (presentation or social projection), **Office** (institutional role), **Incumbency** (evidenced holding of that office), **Duty** (standing accountability), **Skill/Procedure** (qualified capability/method), **Work/Task** (particular authorized activity), **Obligation** (owed-to/owed-by relation and grounds), and **Receipt** (separately attributed evidence/receiving). Do not turn one into another by matching a string or folder. A personality prompt, rendered room, documented skill, a Github issue or work screenshot cannot bestow office, power or customer acceptance.

Counterevidence is retained at the correct typed claim/context with its own time, provenance and opposing evidence. Missing, disallowed, contradicted and merely alternative interpretations must not collapse into `false`, `true`, or unexamined agreement. Where a boundary identifies conflicting local policies, *refusal to glue* may be the correct operation. An agent/user may examine a projected 'shadow' without seeing protected originals; reversibility means an **authorized trace/review path**, not unauthorized recovery of protected source text.

## Discriminating research program

**Case A: shared possibility, different markets.** Select one genuinely Science-qualified capability with at least two independently evidenced receiving markets (e.g. internal source-bound agency work and externally sold SaaS). Freeze scientific propositions and term identities; vary the actual receiver, jurisdiction, external alternatives, contract and recurring cost. Predict two distinguishable requirement sets where evidence supports different constraints, with links to the same sources. The null/bad outcome is two different market projections that both merely copy a keyword or invented feature. No simulated buyer counts as market demand.

**Case B: counterevidence and protected views.** Offer two provenance-attributed pieces of conflicting evidence about the *same typed proposition under the same valid scope*, plus alternative parses and a protected claim outside one reader's rights. Test whether the system retains contention/unknown, keeps parsed alternatives distinct, and refuses to infer the protected evidence or grant rights from rendered geometry. Compare with a source stripped of all provenance as a deliberately bad control.

**Case C: upstream revision and partial invalidation.** Revise a qualified Science hypothesis by a new falsifier while keeping the original Leader demand and market observations immutable. Check whether only affected descendants become unqualified/need redetermination, and whether already received unrelated capabilities remain accessible. An implementation that silently rewrites the original demand fails.

**Case D: typed institution and field receiving.** Read a real Group Character→Incumbency→Office→Duty→Procedure→Work→Obligation/Receipt chain using the canonical existing readers and Science ontology. Check a valid admitted instance, another legitimate office, and missing/wrong authority; in the refusal case no code path may fabricate a grant. Separately require a public service receiver to show an *installed running service*, actual customer task completion and observed economics—not only a Git SHA, software module, schema pass or open-source release.

**Measures:** fraction of material utterance claims retaining attributed source spans; non-coercive recovery of every asserted scientific/market constraint; rate of false admissions and rightful refusals; ability to reconstruct who selected a requirement and with what evidence; revision stability of unrelated descendants; independently measured user/task and operating effects. Predeclare the inventory, sample, expected permissions, observation times and thresholds before interpreting outcomes. A pretty interface is not a measurement of these quantities.

## Actual handoff and stop rule

1. **Science** conducts investigation and formal derivation in the *existing* disciplinary owners; refines source-qualified notes into precise research results and owner-held glossary terms. Obsmetrologia accepts only theorems/proof obligations its own kernel or experiment supports. This protocol is currently **research-only; its proposed projection law is unproved**.
2. **561 Group** contributes its own exact demand and independently evidenced *market necessity*, standards applicability, rights and total operating/servicing economics. This selects candidate requirements; market observations do not edit scientific truths.
3. **Red Cup Engineering** receives the provenance-qualified requirements, makes separate behavioral specification, architecture description, detailed design and qualified deterministic fabrication, drawing its reusable machinery from SignCell → SignChain → SignGraph → SignCraft → AOE → FeltOS/MetroPlat where actually applicable. Never implement an unqualified mathematical conjecture as a purported universal law.
4. **Coesite Operations** supplies reusable declarative duties, procedures, resource allocation, deployment/continuity and receiving methods *for* that technology. It neither implements a second code engine nor appoints Group officers.
5. **561 Group** instantiates the real offices/characters/incumbencies and receives the selected installed operation under its specific grants. **Causeway Public Services** then runs and receives a service for actual beneficiaries/markets—including SaaS uptime, data practices, support, customer tasks, contractual obligations and settlement—rather than an open-source publication masquerading as a service.

The flows of demand, falsifiers, cost and operational faults also go **upstream**. This is not a once-only waterfall. A stage may be reused only with its exact valid scope, and any missing admission must remain visible. Current evidence establishes the *relevant source writings and open research program*, **not** a qualified formal projection, complete aligned agency graph, deterministic end-to-end code generation, deployed FeltOS/MetroPlat instance, or paying SaaS receiver.

**First Engineering-facing research request:** produce one corpus-backed typed transformation fixture that retains the original utterance, one source-qualified scientific term/proposition, two independent market contexts, a justified conditional requirement, and a no-admission/refusal. Bind it to the existing Engineering reader and both Corporate and Public receivers; first identify the current actual consumers and stage admissions. Do not write another standalone prototype.
