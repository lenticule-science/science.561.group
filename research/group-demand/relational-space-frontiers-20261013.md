## 1. Foundational constraint: relationality precedes its mathematical shadows

The Leader's research position is stronger than saying that Obsmetrologia is a mathematical foundation **for** fictional universes. **All mathematics is understood as a degenerate shadow of the relationality research program as formalized through Obsmetrologia; possible universes already lie within its relational space.** The artificial-world, agent-speciation and computational-metabolism programs are already aspects of the rendanheyi board ecology, not candidate new departments to be independently recreated here.

A precise research obligation is to exhibit, rather than merely name, the relevant **projections or interpretations**: specify a relational construction R, the conditions by which a mathematical theory or world-model T is obtained from it, which relations are forgotten or collapsed by the interpretation, and an independently checkable preservation or failure theorem. A claimed embedding of world W in R requires definitions, witness, and a proof or counterexample; a demonstration simulated in software is not a proof of the general claim. The notation R, W, and T here is schematic, **not** a new formally established ontology and **not** a claim that the full theory has already been proved.

Research delivery uses existing Obsmetrologia, its formalization and the relationality science section. A specialized simulation may serve as an instrument, not a rival cosmology or canonical mathematical language. Positive experimental results must not be retroactively taken as proof of relationality.

## 2. Counterfactual industrial archaeology and the Museum of Uninvented Machinery (idea 5)

**Scientific owner:** History and Archaeology Science (counterfactual historiography), partnered with Physical/Engineering Science for device physics and Red Cup Engineering for measured fabrication.  
**Intensity:** deliberately low, durable trickle; one sourced candidate mechanism at a time, not an unconstrained generated catalog.

**Question.** Which feasible mechanisms and fabrication traditions could have followed from historically available materials, techniques and mathematical knowledge, but did not become dominant? Can the resulting object be specified, constructed, and functionally verified, while keeping the history explicitly counterfactual?

**Minimum research record for one candidate**
1. A concrete historical fork: date range, geography, known techniques/materials, primary or credible secondary sources, and what we do **not** know.
2. An explicit counterfactual claim: a mechanism that a documented toolchain plausibly could have manufactured under specified skills and tolerances. Treat social adoption and diffusion as uncertain, not inevitable.
3. Engineering model: mechanism diagram, power/force/flow/information budget, dimensioned or parametric design, fabrication process, and expected failure modes. Separate historical feasibility from modern reproduction.
4. Competing baselines: simplest contemporary mechanism with the same task, actual historical reference, and an intentionally nonfunctional negative control where sensible.
5. Independent test: measure a claimed function, tolerance, wear, power or error against preregistered criteria; record revision and material lots. A pretty print is not functioning machinery.
6. If reproducible, publish an exhibit combining labeled speculative history with **real** measured engineering; if not, publish the failure without an invented historical fact.

**First candidate family:** mechanical/fluidic logic and low-electricity information mechanisms. Compare a simple fluidic switching or mechanical memory element to a switching baseline under identical time and energy/pressure measurements; only escalate to fabrication if the documentable historical fork and construction constraints survive desk review. Avoid claiming the proposed device actually existed historically.

**Trickle allocation:** a short monthly source-and-feasibility sweep; advance **at most one** candidate to physical engineering receiving in a quarter when the evidence justifies it. This is a research attention budget, not a recurring job that has been scheduled.

**Falsifiers:** absent manufacturing plausibility at the claimed historical point; mechanism fails conservation/dimension/force checks; fabrication fails a predeclared function without an identified revision path; every supposed novelty is a known reproduced artifact under a different name.

## 3. Navigable mathematics / proof park (idea 6)

**Scientific owner:** Information → Mathematics / Obsmetrologia; the playable interface is a downstream Red Cup engineering and potentially Causeway cultural product.  
**Experience:** *Occupy White Walls* meets *RollerCoaster Tycoon*—discoverable galleries, rooms, pathways and constructed rides, but the territory and transitions are derived from actual mathematical states.

**Hypothesis.** A spatial, manipulable presentation of proof states and legitimate transformations can help people or agents discover, understand or transfer proofs better than a conventional tactic-list interface on specified tasks. A beautiful map alone does not validate this hypothesis.

**Verifiable world rules:**
- A location denotes a real theorem goal, context, definitional environment and proof-system version. The displayed geometry is a rendering of relations, not the theorem's source of truth.
- A door/path is an admissible tactic or term transformation. A ride is a reusable composition of transformations with explicit preconditions and postconditions.
- The only action that marks a mathematical goal as proved is acceptance by the authoritative Lean kernel or a separately qualified checker for a precisely declared language. Neither visual proximity, an LLM's judgment, nor a rendering counts as proof.
- Failed transitions and unsatisfied obligations remain inspectable; contradictory claims are not silently eliminated to prettify a world.
- Obsmetrologia concepts must be introduced as actual imported definitions/theorems, **not** substituted with a toy "relation engine" that cannot check anything upstream.

**First scientific comparison:** one frozen family of real, modest Obsmetrologia goals; participants or agents use (A) plain proof assistant, (B) a spatial rendering with no constructive affordance, (C) the same rendering with freely composable theorem-checked rides. Randomize task and order, log accepted proofs, cost, time, transfer to held-out goals, and failure rates. Compare performance by both task and participant, not only total number of clicks. A synthetic walkthrough is a UI check, not a scientific result.

**Relevant prior art to evaluate, not duplicate:** Lean 4 Natural Number Game / lean4game (https://github.com/leanprover-community/NNG4). Our research distinction is general interactive proof *construction and navigation*, with relational provenance, not merely adding more educational game levels.

**Go / no-go:** advance a prototype only if it can consume authoritative proof states and produce independently checker-accepted proofs. Reject any interface that claims success from animations alone.

## 4. Energy-bearing organic memory: charging writes, discharge consumes (idea 7)

**Scientific owner:** Physical Science (electrochemistry, energy storage, and measurement) together with Biological Science (organic/biogenic material provenance where relevant). Connect to the existing Physarum study **without** treating maze behavior as electrochemical memory evidence: https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/27.

**Central hypothesis (strong form):** a single physical, organic or bio-derived substrate can store retrievable **task-specific information** in the same state variables that store recoverable energy, so (i) charging participates causally in writing memory and (ii) extracting useful work causes a predictable, measurable decrease in the memory that was written. This is **not** satisfied merely by reading a battery's state of charge and calling it a memory.

**Distinguish at least five mechanisms:** (A) generic charge-state meter (trivial baseline), (B) electrochemical hysteresis and path dependence, (C) distinct charge-conditioned memory states at comparable energy, (D) passive time decay/leakage, and (E) read-induced disturbance. Coupling is a measurable hypothesis, not presumed biology or proof that computation violates thermodynamics.

**Candidate substrate families for literature screening:** organic ionic/electrochemical conductors; redox-active conductive polymers; hydrogels and biopolymer composites; fungus- or cellulose-derived conductive supports where validated. Compare with nonorganic electrochemical controls and a separate battery-plus-memory system. Avoid acquiring biological cultures, uncontrolled electrodes, unknown electrolytes or charging live plants without an accepted apparatus and safety protocol.

**Preregister a blinded charge/write/discharge study before constructing a bespoke device:**
1. Select an independently measurable energy observable E (joules recovered under a frozen discharge regime, not voltage alone), an information-bearing input label X, a readout Y, and state-of-health controls. Randomly assign X across devices/cycles.
2. The design **must** include distinct programmed X labels at *matched E*; otherwise the experiment can demonstrate only battery telemetry.
3. Measure classification accuracy or conditional mutual information I(X;Y | energy band) on held-out randomized trials with blinding; calibrate sensor uncertainty and autocorrelation. If available observations do not support reliable conditional estimation, report discrimination/error bounds rather than unstable information estimates.
4. Randomize discharge fraction D and elapsed time T separately. Compare memory retained after useful energy extraction against matched-energy passive wait, sham read, and equivalent read frequency; distinguish consumption from time/leakage/read wear.
5. Include negative controls for decoder leakage (label shuffling, device identity, measurement sequence, temperature, electrolyte and batch); repeat across cycles, lots and matched controls.
6. Report joules delivered, recoverable energy, write energy, retention at time T, successful bits per device (with confidence intervals), cycles to degradation, and read energy/perturbation. Report **energy cost per independently recovered bit**, not marketing "bits per watt" without a time basis.
7. Predetermine thresholds using instrument resolution/power analysis. Inconclusive means inconclusive; observational hysteresis alone is not evidence of charge-dependent *stored data*.

**Mechanistic falsifiers:** no label discrimination after matching energy; equivalent fading under passive waiting and energy extraction; discriminability entirely predicted by voltage/temperature; effects vanish on independent device lots; all apparent capacity comes from external electronics.

**Literature starting points (not evidence for this stronger integrated energy/memory result):**
- Tanim et al. 2023 review of natural organic memristors and synapses: https://doi.org/10.3390/mi14020235
- Organic electrochemical neurons/synapses, arXiv 2403.00005: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00005
- Organic electrochemical transistor work on sensing/memory/processing: https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.04361
None of these sources by itself proves that discharging usable stored energy irreversibly consumes independently encoded memory. That conjunction is **our experimental question**.

**Receiving gate:** Lenticule Science accepts controls, safety and physical metrology; Red Cup accepts only a bounded apparatus design; Coesite accepts calibrated actual instruments and procedures; no client or public service claim without replicated hardware results.

## 5. Archaeologists of tomorrow / least-cost irreversible evidence (idea 8)

**Scientific owner:** Information → Library Science with History and Archaeology; quantitative decision methods from statistical/information science. This includes noninvasive environmental observations and preservation of disappearing digital records.

**Decision problem.** At time t, choose which observations to preserve now because acquisition may become impossible later. Maximize **expected marginal decision value** subject to observation, custody, legal/consent, privacy and long-term storage costs. "Archive everything" is not a decision rule.

Define for candidate observation a and plausible later decision problem d:
- Value-of-information is the expected reduction in future decision loss from admissible evidence a versus the available evidence set, computed under an explicit probability/utility model.
- Net value subtracts acquisition, consent/licensing, verification, preservation, migration and attention costs. Include an irreversibility multiplier or probability that evidence will disappear before another opportunity; never double-count the same risk and loss.
- Prefer incremental information conditional on what is already held to prevent redundant captures; note correlated observations and unknown future questions.
- Store source, observation instrument, collection time, jurisdiction, consent/rights, hash or custody evidence, uncertainty, expected disappearance and retention/review criteria using **existing conformant** evidence resources. A new prose note or a heap of ad hoc JSON is not an archival format.

**First comparison:** take a limited set of rights-cleared public webpages or voluntary environmental observation opportunities. Compare equal-budget strategies: random capture, most-recent-first, popularity-first, and estimated value-of-information/irreversibility policy. Evaluate later query/task usefulness, provenance completeness, unique facts preserved, bytes spent and retention costs on held-out periods. Put strong penalties on inaccessible or unconsented evidence; do not infer future value from what the model itself invents.

**Falsifiers:** VoI policy does no better than simple deduplication/random preservation; cost model excludes verification/migration; private or restricted material is collected without lawful authority; apparent uniqueness is a failure to look for duplicates.

**Literature anchor:** Luhede et al., *The value of information in water quality monitoring and management*, *Ecological Economics* 219 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2024.108128. Their empirical decision context is environmental management; applying this to future archaeology is a hypothesis, not a validated generalization.

## 6. Counterfactual prevention economics for digital and agent infrastructure (idea 9)

**Scientific owner:** Economics and Business Science with causal-inference methods; downstream users are Coesite Operations, Red Cup Engineering and Causeway Services. Institutional proof standards belong to the existing rendanheyi ecology, not a separate "prevented events DAO."

**Question.** When an intervention prevents an incident, outage, abuse, fraudulent agent action, or avoidable compute expenditure, can independently verifiable causal value be distinguished from an unsupported claim about an event that did not occur?

Specify, *before observing post-intervention data*:
- **Unit:** e.g. eligible deploy, agent action, service-time cell, customer task, or agent workflow—pick one; avoid switching between requests and incidents.
- **Treatment:** a concrete preventer (recovery automation, validation gate, rate policy, automated incident repair); actual exposure recorded at versioned time.
- **Outcome:** user-visible outage minutes, verified remediation labor, corrupted actions, actual failed tasks, independently priced compute or loss cost. Separate **harm prevented** from incidental workload shifted to users or other services.
- **Counterfactual:** randomized or staggered rollout where ethically and operationally acceptable; otherwise interrupted time-series, synthetic control, matched comparisons with explicit unmeasured-confounding sensitivity.
- **Claim:** a confidence/credible interval for incremental avoided loss net of intervention operating cost, expressed as a conditional estimate under declared assumptions—not an invoice for a hypothetical incident.
- **Adverse effects:** false positives, new failures created by the preventer, latency, user inconvenience and noncompliance are counted against net value.

**First defensible experiment:** choose a low-risk, already-authorized engineering validation gate and a staged rollout across comparable, **noncritical** eligible workflows. Measure independently witnessed failures and time-to-repair before/after; log assignment, eligibility and bypasses. Do not disable protection in high-consequence production merely to obtain a control group. If randomization is unsuitable, state observational inference limits and do not fabricate an estimate.

**Negative controls:** intervention applied after the outcome; unrelated error class; affected service not actually exposed; changed telemetry definitions; escalation that shifts failures out of the measured channel. Payment or revenue model comes *after* independent causal acceptance and clearly allocated risk.

**Falsifiers:** intervention does not reduce independently observed harm; net costs exceed plausible avoided loss; results reverse under modest confounder sensitivity; claimed benefits depend entirely on simulator output rather than independent observations.

## 7. Machine-native sports, performance and agent-to-agent media (idea 10)

**Scientific owner:** Information/Intelligence Science, with measurement and media reception from the appropriate social-science/public media owners. Tie to existing 561 AI-for-AI audiovisual and microdrama work; **do not** create a second production platform.

**Question.** Can agent audiences develop stable, reproducible preferences for performances whose aesthetic/technical structure is not designed chiefly for human perception? Is a competition genuinely machine-native, or only a conventional leaderboard translated into tokens?

**First event candidate: Minimum Witness.** Competitors receive the *same* finite mathematical challenge, versioned proof environment and compute/attention budget. They may submit machine-interpretable proof constructions and explanatory performances in existing supported open formats. Independent proof checkers accept or reject mathematical correctness. Conditional on correctness, evaluate resource budgets, construction diversity, generalization to held-out tasks, and machine-audience pairwise preference using blinded, randomized presentations. There must be no reward for unverified theorem claims or merely longer reasoning traces.

**Research controls:** compare human-designed and agent-designed event rules; hold evaluator models separate from performers; blind origin/identity; audit prompt/data contamination; rerun events with changed audiences and frozen inputs; measure preference consistency and the effect of audience training. Stable model preferences are not evidence of consciousness or a new universal aesthetic.

**Output:** one replayable event with independently checkable scores, source-linked artifacts, and a versioned audience-study protocol consumed by existing media operations. A performance is not a proof and a proof is not an aesthetic judgment.
