# Manoomin: native-host evidence and decision gates (2026-10-11)

Provenance: [Group demand #67](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-services-section/issues/67); [Lenticule science receiver #92](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/92). The Leader ordered prevention work. The public jiaobai report is an unverified signal, not an outbreak or legal finding.

## Species-specific evidence

- **Zizania latifolia:** Established association with *Ustilago esculenta*; this plant/fungus pair was documented in a 1991 California planting. CDFA's [2017 fungus review](https://blogs.cdfa.ca.gov/Section3162/?p=4271) also reports eradicated Louisiana occurrences in 1999–2000 and a 2017 Riverside County laboratory identification in the introduced plant. None is an infection finding in native manoomin.
- **Z. palustris:** No confirmed host observation or powered negative survey established in this source review. Susceptibility remains unknown.
- **Z. aquatica:** The [CDFA 2017 host list](https://blogs.cdfa.ca.gov/Section3162/?p=4271) names this taxon, while [Sinha et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.5897/AJMR2016.8207) calls *Z. latifolia* the only known host. The evidentiary basis for CDFA's entry must be clarified before presenting native infection as demonstrated.
- **Z. texana:** No confirmed host observation or powered negative survey established in this review. Susceptibility remains unknown.
- **Distinct pathway:** [CDFA's plant review](https://blogs.cdfa.ca.gov/Section3162/?p=4338) treats the introduced plant's invasive effects separately from possible fungal host transfer.

**Discriminator:** a traceable, independently verified native-host record with reliable species identification would change the host-evidence assessment. An unsupported risk inference would not. Unknown is neither evidence of absence nor evidence of infection.

## Rules and safe response

[APHIS](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/how-to-import) requires commodity- and origin-specific import checks. Obtain a dated official determination separately for live plants/rhizomes, fresh cut stems, processed food and seeds, including origin, treatment, destination and relevant state rules. A 2017 prohibition statement does not establish the legal status of every 2026 retail form.

[DATCP's WTCAC manoomin survey](https://datcp.wi.gov/Pages/Programs_Services/PlantIndustryLab.aspx) was not described as an *U. esculenta* absence survey. Ask competent specialists for the source of CDFA's *Z. aquatica* listing and an authorized reporting route. Do not acquire, culture, ship, experimentally expose, or manipulate suspect material. Do not accuse sellers or report native infection without verification.
