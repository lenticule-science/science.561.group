## Science-needed distinction

Routine inventory, custody records, profile backup, machine maintenance,
costing and proven-object reprints **do not need additional scientific
research** before ordinary authorized Engineering/Operations work. They
still require real asset/production receiving.

A *confirmatory Physarum research maze* **does need Science** because
printed geometry is the experimental treatment and the causal interpretation
is contested. This contract is not authority to deploy/publish a service or
fabricate research specimens.

## What Engineering can use now

- The Group has documented ownership/custody questions for its Centauri
  Carbon and K2 Plus; treat them as *potential* apparatus machines, not as
  qualified calibrated laboratory instruments.
- Science #27 specifies geometric continuation (H_G), directional
  persistence (H_D), embodied morphology (H_M), externalized trail cues
  (H_E) and retained turn-dependent state (H_S) as distinct hypotheses.
- The available publication-transcription assay records 1274 total trials,
  316 undecided and 958 decisive across four conditions. The 7 mm aggregate
  has two decisive observations not reconciled with forced-turn/lateral
  subtotals, and printed percentages/p-values require source review.
  These are **source-integrity findings**, not replicated biology.
- The author-reported nominal corridor/arm dimensions can inform
  *exploratory parametric CAD* and *measurement coupon design* only.
  No production tolerances or surface/contact compatibility have been
  qualified through physical measurements.

## Acceptance obligations before a confirmatory instrument freeze

1. Science verifies the primary table images and available original
   trial-level records, or explicitly pre-registers an uncertainty-aware
   replication that does not rely on unreconciled source data.
2. Science fixes its outcome codebook (alternate, repeat, undecided/both-arm),
   unit-of-analysis, source/geometry confounds, negative controls, orientation
   blocking and a multiplicity/power method with explicit experimental
   batch structure.
3. Science freezes a versioned geometry family, critical dimensions and
   measurement uncertainty/tolerance targets. Asymmetric source geometry
   must not be silently substituted by a corrected model.
4. Engineering makes version-controlled CAD/source exports, coupons and
   calibration logs. For each physical maze unit record printer identity,
   material lot, profile, fabrication date, actual critical measurements,
   cleaning/contact constraints and a unique unit ID.
5. Independent Science measures/assesses geometry and validates study
   compatibility; a separate Engineering recipient checks that the actual
   production source and measured parts match Science's accepted
   instrument requirements.

The source-to-instrument assay is a **documentary precheck** and cannot
authenticate its own evidence, independently receive a release, or approve
a physical experiment. Its deliberately synthetic tests do not represent
lab measurements.

## Permitted next work

Engineering may draw exploratory alternatives or characterize dimensional
repeatability using nonconfirmatory coupons. The firm may also inventory and
operate known machines for unrelated routine tasks. Do not claim a
confirmed Physarum maze, a replicated biological result, causal memory
evidence, or a preregistered instrument until the gates above are received.

**Research falsifiers:** if alternation disappears after correcting corner
geometry, H_G gains support relative to H_S; if slime homogenization removes
a bias without changing substrate physical properties, H_E gains support;
if front-state kinematics fully account for a turn effect under balanced
geometries, strong abstract-turn-memory readings become unnecessary.
Effects persisting under controlled geometry, substrate and morphology would
motivate—not alone prove—further H_S-specific experiments.

The primary biology must be assayed by independently collected data and
appropriately qualified methods, not by a JavaScript test suite.
