## Artifact hygiene rules

1. **One canonical home per program.** New science artifacts belong under a named program directory, not Library root.
2. **CURRENT beats version-spray.** Iterative state should update one `CURRENT.md` (or an explicitly version-controlled canonical carrier) rather than generate `CURRENT-v2`, `CURRENT-v3`, etc. as independent remembered objects.
3. **Session exports are receipts, not knowledge integration.** A generated/downloaded file at root is considered unintegrated until placed under a program and referenced by this register or the program's CURRENT file.
4. **Duplicate detection before creation.** Before generating a durable research artifact, search/list the program directory and reuse or update the canonical carrier when possible.
5. **Canonical-ingestion state must be explicit.** Distinguish Library-durable, Drive-durable, Git/monorepo-durable, and Semioverse/L-Space-ingested. Never collapse these into the word “saved.”
6. **No destructive cleanup without identity.** Root duplicates may be removed only after content identity is established or after their canonical successor is explicitly designated.
7. **Recover stranded sequences.** When a session leaves a coherent series at root, move it intact into its program rather than creating a fresh summary that leaves the originals orphaned.
8. **Science programs need a lineage surface.** A future CSO cycle should be able to enter the program directory, read one entrypoint, and know what exists, what supersedes what, what remains open, and where canonical ingestion stands.
