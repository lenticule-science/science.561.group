## Scientific claim

A set of passing stage observations does not establish a completed service effect unless they belong to the **same independently evidenced effect**. A common umbrella work reference, timestamp, or software deployment version is insufficient to tie separate transactions together. A multi-provider trace requires an authenticated effect manifest tying exact component revisions and evidence to an actual scope/order/release, and independently testable receipts.

### Falsifier and scope

Falsifier: an independently adjudicated cross-provider effect demonstrates that the binding procedure rejects an authentic complete transaction because an otherwise adequate independently authenticated chain does not admit a single effect manifest. Repair the manifest-relation semantics and measure false-rejection cost; do not weaken the ban on mixing unrelated service effects.

This packet implements **conditional structural assays**. `verifyManifest` and `verifyEvidence` are supplied callbacks. If a caller supplies tautologically true callbacks, those do not establish independent provider authentication. Consequently neither `conditionallyCompleteEffects` nor `liveIntake=verified` is public delivery, customer assent, transfer of rights, final editorial correction, or payout settlement. The older remedy assay's `verified` vocabulary is retained for compatibility; it must not be interpreted as a provider credential.

### Unanswered scientific questions

1. How should an authenticated manifest bind an order/sale/submission to components shared across many customers and to distinct provider identities and versions?
2. What protocol-native receipt and independent-public-witness combination achieves acceptable measured false-positive and false-negative rates for real transactions?
3. How should temporal validity, correction/supersession, provenance rights, accessible task success, and dissent be modeled without allowing a source owner to self-certify receiving?
4. What is the expected value of acquiring another independent witness given cost, latency, consequential risk, and the option of no further action?
