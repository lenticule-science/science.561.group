# MP-SCI: Causeway manoomin evidence correction (2026-10-11)

Demand: [Causeway Public Services #33](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-services-section/issues/33); [Lenticule #92](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/92); [Group canonical plan](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-services-section/blob/main/content/text/documents/articles/plans/manoomin-protection.md). Public-source desk review only. No client data, field work, external contact, product identification or official determination.

## Findings with source limitations

1. The [1991 Plant Disease report](https://www.apsnet.org/publications/plantdisease/backissues/Documents/1991Abstracts/PD_75_1075D.htm) documents `Ustilago esculenta` in introduced `Zizania latifolia` near Modesto, California. It does not document an infected native manoomin bed.
2. The [California Department of Food and Agriculture 2017 pest rating](https://blogs.cdfa.ca.gov/Section3162/?p=4271) reports an additional confirmed 2017 detection in introduced `Z. latifolia` from Riverside County and mentions a 1999 Louisiana occurrence. It lists native `Z. aquatica` among fungus hosts, but the underlying voucher/primary record for that host listing was not verified in this desk review. **This is actionable new evidence**: do not say 1991 was the only recorded US introduced-host event; do not infer native field infection from an agency host list.
3. For `Z. palustris` and `Z. texana`, the cited primary introduced-host literature and the examined CDFA excerpt do not independently establish native infection or its absence. For `Z. aquatica`, separate the agency-listed host claim from a confirmed specimen and from current local transmission. Distinct species need distinct evidence and uncertainty.
4. [Wisconsin DATCP's 2025 WTCAC manoomin survey description](https://datcp.wi.gov/Pages/Programs_Services/PlantIndustryLab.aspx) lists bacterial blight/streak and brown-spot work, not an `U. esculenta` survey. It demonstrates an existing tribal-directed diagnostic relationship, not a pathogen detection or a valid negative.
5. The CDFA's 2017 statement that the host was prohibited under then-7 CFR 319.37-2 is **not a 2026 import ruling**. [2018 federal restructuring](https://regulations.justia.com/regulations/fedreg/2018/03/19/2018-05424.html) changed the plant-for-planting provisions. Current [7 CFR 319.37-1(c)](https://ecfr.io/Title-7/Section-319.37-1) differentiates consumption/processing imports from planting imports; [APHIS ACIR](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/how-to-import) directs case-specific import requirements. Resolve plant identity, country, material form, treatment and destination with competent authorities. A retailer is neither legal nor illegal on this record.
6. The [2026 Wisconsin Wild Rice Stewardship Council announcement](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/WIGOV/bulletins/417ec2b) documents a potential existing public coordination route, not authorization to act for its members.

## Falsifiable decision questions

- **Species-specific host claim:** can a qualified specialist provide dated, authenticated evidence for `U. esculenta` and each native species individually? A host name in a secondary agency list is not itself independent proof of a recent native field infection. An authentic disconfirming host analysis would change the risk assessment; silence or missing search hits would not.
- **Commodity-specific pathway:** do the applicable authority determinations materially differ among living propagative material, fresh food stems and processed food? Do not assume risk or restrictions transfer across forms. Observe only public documentation until competent routing instructs otherwise.
- **Useful liaison:** does one verified, respectful route to tribal stewards and plant-health authorities reduce duplication and provide actionable guidance? Measure verified receipt and case quality, not contacts attempted or invented ecological benefit.

For Coesite scientific operations: register source URI, publisher, event date, retrieval date, source class (public allegation / primary science / state synthesis / formal ruling / independent field observation), exact plant species, material form, jurisdiction, response state, uncertainty and next review. Missing evidence is `unknown`, not zero or exoneration; reposts are not independent corroboration. Keep sensitive places, private correspondence and traditional knowledge out of public source.

**No further research needed for safe preparatory steps:** preserve source links, use published pest-reporting channels, request current rules, coordinate with existing tribal-directed work. **Further specialist evidence required:** biological native-host susceptibility, exact commodity compliance, independent product identification and measured exposure. No field observation, regulatory reply, external correspondence or prevention outcome is claimed. Trigger review on an authority reply, qualified specimen, new verified exposure, or source correction.
