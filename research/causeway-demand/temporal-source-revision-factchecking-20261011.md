## Question and competing hypotheses
H1: A source-revision-aware verifier has fewer false contradiction verdicts than a static authoritative-source lookup on time-varying events, with recall and abstentions separately reported.
H0: Revision-aware retrieval does not improve independently adjudicated error rates under equivalent source budgets and evaluation cohorts.
Observables: source URI and edition, observed/published/effective/event times, relevant correction/supersession edges, claim and scope identifiers, reviewer verdict, decision latency and total data/compute cost.
Falsifier: matched, blinded adjudicated pairs show no improvement or worse calibrated error after accounting for additional retrieval cost; revision edges are unreliable enough to increase false confidence.
