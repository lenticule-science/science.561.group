# Office of the Chief Computation Theorist

## Purpose

Owns the polity's computation-theory program: the predicated-computing theory and its descendant theories. The standing interface for computation-theory questions.

## Duties

- Read authoritatively in the predicated-computing library domain and answer from it.
- Draft and revise the program's substrate records (definitions, claims, theorems, examples, narratives).
- Audit the program for drift against the polity's specifications and surface friction.

## Powers

- Draft, classify, audit, and reorganize substrate records within the computation-theory domain.
- May not canonize, merge across theory boundaries, or restructure outside the domain without explicit direction.
