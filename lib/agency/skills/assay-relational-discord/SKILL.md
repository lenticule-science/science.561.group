# Assay relational discord

**Tunesday** follows Mathy Monday. Science does not adjust its laws to fit an implementation: it determines which relationships actually must agree and supplies falsifiable, receiver-specific specifications for engineering. Other days may invoke this skill whenever a discord appears. This is a reusable Science procedure, not a new office, evidence repository, scheduler, or score.

## Entry and authority

Receive an existing work-board allocation or Science duty, including exact subject, owner, receiver, affected frontier, baseline version, and evidence. Locate the owning Obsmetrologia declaration and Lean check when relevant; otherwise locate the actual domain standard, contract or explicit institutional requirement. Recover prior work, experiments, established results, accepted tolerances and contradictory testimony. Do not create another note or work item if the original already exists.

If no applicable equality, bound, refinement, consent boundary or receiver obligation is established, the discrepancy is a difference **not yet shown to be discord**. Do not assume that two independently governed enterprises ought to be identical.

## Scientific procedure

1. **Specify comparison before observing its outcome.** State the reference law, its version and authority; carrier types; direction and unit of each map; relevant gauge/coordinate choices; allowed variation; receiver's judgement; and how a counterexample would be witnessed. A path-square or cycle is tested for commutation only if the specification demands that relation. Never turn a metaphor about harmonics, holonomy or curvature into a theorem.
2. **Recover the evidence as admitted SignCells, SCA/SEN assertions and SEAVT histories where available.** Keep original source, observer, time, polarity and identity. Distinguish an unobserved claim from one supported by contrary evidence. Preserve the four knowledge readings: neither testimony, testimony for, testimony against, and both. Knowledge joins are commutative, associative and idempotent; duplicate reports alone never increase confidence or impact. Source content may be a lead rather than an admitted claim.
3. **Measure the failure that the law permits measuring.** For typed arrows f, g, h, k with a required square, compare g∘f with k∘h using kernel-checked equality or explicit golden vectors as applicable. For approximate measurements, record units, uncertainty and declared tolerance, not a unitless harmony percentage. For circuits, calculate residue only under a stated composition law and boundary class. A nonzero value is not automatically a violation when the boundary permits it.
4. **Classify locality.** Separate component defects, incompatible seams, temporal drift and genuinely composed or cyclic obstruction. Distinguish correlated downstream symptoms from independently evidenced common cause. Supply a causal claim only with independent tests or a derivation; otherwise preserve competing hypotheses.
5. **Produce a bounded, falsifiable handoff.** Identify a witnessable failure, minimal reproduction, expected behavior, forbidden regressions, candidate golden vectors, and the Engineering receiving office. Specify whether the remedy requires theory work, a change to an implementation, or a policy/receiver decision. Preserve rejected conjectures and unresolved premises without promoting them to proved facts.
6. **Re-assay after intervention.** Run the same comparison against changed evidence. A source patch, green build, or local assay does not establish deployed or independently received improvement. Reopen contested or violated obligations.

## Boundary to Engineering and Operations

Science owns admissible definitions, proof obligations, calibration, and counterexamples; Engineering owns mechanisms and compatibility repairs; Operations owns rollout, live observation and independent receiver acceptance. A request can be sent upstream again if the comparison exposes an invalid premise. Do not merge distinct canonical knowledge stores or fabricate consensus.

An obligation can be triaged urgently when safety or active service loss is evidenced. Otherwise prefer partial ordering based on justified urgency, receiver harm, propagation and verified recoverable cost; incomparable claims stay incomparable. Never add several downstream symptoms as separate benefits of one upstream repair.

## Closure

Science receiving requires the responsible Science office to re-run and accept the declared assayer, or to specify the remaining proof and measurement gap. An Engineering handoff is a qualified specification, not Science's acceptance of an Engineering result. Attach evidence to the existing typed work and SignGraph/SignChain surfaces; do not create a new Tunesday-only register.

Related existing capabilities: Obsmetrologia and Lean, typed-circuit-residue-assay, hierarchical-circuit-equivalence-assay, researching in the Science section, and Union Officer Academy evidence-settlement/professional-formation procedures.

## Literal musical and audiovisual metrology

Tunesday also means music, not merely a metaphor. For a real soundtrack, generative score or microdrama, specify the listening/receiving context before deciding what counts as discord: reference tuning/temperament and pitch, tempo and rhythmic grid, phase and timing, loudness and true peak, spectral balance, dialogue intelligibility, audiovisual synchronization and uncertainty in the measurements. Compare original authored intent with rendered and heard outcomes, including an independent listener or creative authority where perception matters. A deliberate dissonance, asymmetric beat, unusual timbre or psychedelic audiovisual relation can satisfy the artistic contract. Acoustic measurements constrain a claim; they do not substitute for culturally situated creative judgement or assert a universal aesthetic optimum.
