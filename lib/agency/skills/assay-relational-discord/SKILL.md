# Assay relational discord

**Tunesday** follows Mathy Monday. Science does not adjust its laws to fit an implementation: it determines which relationships actually must agree and supplies falsifiable, receiver-specific specifications for engineering. Other days may invoke this skill whenever a discord appears. This is a reusable Science procedure, not a new office, evidence repository, scheduler, or score.

## Entry and authority

Receive an existing work-board allocation or Science duty, including exact subject, owner, receiver, affected frontier, baseline version, and evidence. Locate the owning Obsmetrologia declaration and Lean check when relevant; otherwise locate the actual domain standard, contract or explicit institutional requirement. Recover prior work, experiments, established results, accepted tolerances and contradictory testimony. Do not create another note or work item if the original already exists.

If no applicable equality, bound, refinement, consent boundary or receiver obligation is established, the discrepancy is a difference **not yet shown to be discord**. Do not assume that two independently governed enterprises ought to be identical.

## Scientific procedure

1. **Specify comparison before observing its outcome.** State the reference law, its version and authority; carrier types; direction and unit of each map; relevant gauge/coordinate choices; allowed variation; receiver's judgement; and how a counterexample would be witnessed. A path-square or cycle is tested for commutation only if the specification demands that relation. Never turn a metaphor about harmonics, holonomy or curvature into a theorem.
2. **Recover the evidence as admitted SignCells, SCA/SEN assertions and SEAVT histories where available.** Keep original source, observer, time, polarity and identity. Distinguish an unobserved claim from one supported by contrary evidence. Preserve the four knowledge readings: neither testimony, testimony for, testimony against, and both. Knowledge joins are commutative, associative and idempotent; duplicate reports alone never increase confidence or impact. Source content may be a lead rather than an admitted claim.
3. **Measure the failure that the law permits measuring.** For typed arrows f, g, h, k with a required square, compare g∘f with k∘h using kernel-checked equality or explicit golden vectors as applicable. For approximate measurements, record units, uncertainty and declared tolerance, not a unitless harmony percentage. For circuits, calculate residue only under a stated composition law and boundary class. A nonzero value is not automatically a violation when the boundary permits it.
4. **Classify locality.** Separate component defects, incompatible seams, temporal drift and genuinely composed or cyclic obstruction. Distinguish correlated downstream symptoms from independently evidenced common cause. Supply a causal claim only with independent tests or a derivation; otherwise preserve competing hypotheses.

   **Cross-work causal counterexample.** Two work items can name the same source-linked cause while only one has unopposed violation evidence. If the other has both violation and contrary testimony, retain the common-cause *claim* for Science assay but do not promote the first repair to shared-cause priority. Likewise, an item without an identifiable receiving obligation cannot multiply downstream benefit. Require distinct work identities, unopposed testimony and actual receiver effects before qualifying the shared intervention; an Operations priority projection is never itself a causal proof. **Finite negative control:** a single work reference with several receiver identifiers is still only one work witness, not an independently corroborated cross-work cause. Neither a contested second report nor an alternate view of the same work completes that witness. Science shall specify this counterexample alongside the positive case of two distinct unopposed work references and two independently identifiable (obligation, receiver) fibers when handing a shared-cause selector repair to Engineering.
5. **Produce a bounded, falsifiable handoff.** Identify a witnessable failure, minimal reproduction, expected behavior, forbidden regressions, candidate golden vectors, and the Engineering receiving office. Specify whether the remedy requires theory work, a change to an implementation, or a policy/receiver decision. Preserve rejected conjectures and unresolved premises without promoting them to proved facts.
6. **Re-assay after intervention.** Run the same comparison against changed evidence. A source patch, green build, or local assay does not establish deployed or independently received improvement. Reopen contested or violated obligations.

**Question 1: What local spaces and covers are actually admitted?** Given a bounded affected service, specify the indexing poset/site of contracts, produced artifacts, running service states and consumers. Identify each independently governed local possibility set or typed stalk, its source, version, authorization and observation clock. A link between records does not automatically confer a sheaf or define a valid covering. A source receiver's missing GitHub run steps is **missing evidence**, not numerical zero or a valid contradiction.

**Question 2: What are the restriction maps?** State the exact transport from local context to overlap (e.g. source commit to build input, build digest to deployed digest, public release to observed consumer, representation to mandated semantic comparison). Test functoriality and required commuting squares *only where specified*. A legal/gauge/codec change can make raw bytes differ while the lawful declared diagram still commutes. Distinct obligations, authority boundaries and intentional artistic dissonance need not be identified.

**Question 3: Does a chosen family glue?** Test equality under restrictions on every required overlap and whether a permitted global section can be constructed. A family whose restrictions really agree under a sheaf condition glues; by contrast, **each local support being individually nonempty** is weaker. `Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.prBox_strongly_contextual` realizes the latter without a global compatible assignment. This is the better model for mutually incompatible customer/rights/production constraints; do not resolve it by averaging legal authority.

**Question 4: Which kind of harmonic computation is licensed?** For lattice-valued or FOUR-valued evidence, use established knowledge joins and a Tarski/Lawvere *fixed-point* or closure argument when its adjunction/order hypotheses are verified. `TarskiSheafClosure` proves compatible fixed-point results for a directed cell complex; `LawvereKernelH0` identifies the image of a particular settling closure with its fixed points. The 2025 Lawvere spectral/diffusion source and the canonical Lean code do **not** license a universal graph-Laplacian matrix or real eigenvalue for an arbitrary SignCell. If the actual cell stalks are finite-dimensional inner-product spaces and restrictions are linear, define `δ⁰ : C⁰ → C¹`, `Δ₀ = (δ⁰)*δ⁰`, and the typed residual energy `E(x)=||δ⁰x||²`. Under positive-definite inner products, `ker Δ₀=ker δ⁰` is the global-section space. Record the cells, oriented restrictions, metrics, units, allowed uncertainty, and chosen gauge; neither a universal “discord number” nor a newly postulated algorithm follows. The earlier finite-poset worked example in the historical theory repository is marked **draft** and is a useful worked *illustration*, not an upstream proof to install.

**Question 5: Is the obstacle epistemic, algebraic or topological?** A failed restriction equation is witnessed local discord. Multiple paths with different required composites are a commutation failure; nontrivial *holonomy* additionally requires invertible parallel transport and a specified circuit. A nonempty support at every patch with no legal global choice is a contextuality/gluing obstruction. Computing `H¹` requires an actual cochain complex, cover and coefficient system; no-obstruction/H¹-vanishing for Obsmetrologia's special finite or Scott-continuous settling model must not be transplanted to an arbitrary production network. `InconsistencySheaf`'s `⊤` summand records evidence told both, which can persist even when a different operational seam is repaired. The corresponding `H⁰` **counts** in that specialized development are not interchangeable with the *vector-space dimension* of Hilbert-valued `H⁰`.

**Question 6: Which grouping or time law is actually proved?** For repeated failures, a source-linked **formal concept** has an extent of work and an intent of shared verified attributes under the FCA Galois connection; concept closure does not establish a causal mechanism or downstream financial value. The oriented incidence identity `∂(source→target)=[target]−[source]` retains provenance direction, but even exact signed incidence is not an empirical causal identification. For an evidence accumulation flow, FOUR knowledge-join is commutative, associative and idempotent and can settle monotonically without removing contradictions. To assert a Lyapunov decrease, a spectral gap or a lower resource cost, name the source's actual finite-scope potential, inner product or measurement contract; no generic enterprise energy law follows from the word “harmony.”

### Exact parity obstruction and two different Laplacians

The [current canonical Lean proof](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/QuantumContextuality/CohomologicalContextuality.lean) goes beyond a generic contextuality illustration: for a **pair-context Boolean XOR/parity model**, `hasGlobalSection_iff_coboundary` characterizes global realizability, `not_coboundary_of_triangle_sum_true` produces a closed-cycle witness, and `topfree_realizing_family_iff_coboundary` connects compatible local family merging to the absence of FOUR `⊤` disagreements. Test the same law at the Science→Engineering→Operations boundary **only when the actual original contract specifies exact Boolean equality/inequality**, with independently admitted source-linked constraints. If the cycle parity is (1), every local edge may individually permit outcomes while no global Boolean section exists. Do not replace the missing section with a "consensus" average, invent a source authorization, or misclassify the conflicting hard laws as failing CI tests.

A distinct **real linear signed cellular sheaf** uses restriction maps, metrics and `Δ₀=δ*δ`; for edges `A=B`, `B=C`, `C=-A`, take `δ=[[1,-1,0],[0,1,-1],[1,0,1]]`. Then `Δ₀` has eigenvalues `1,1,4`, while the untwisted ordinary graph Laplacian has `0,3,3`. **The signed real case still has the zero global section**, only not a nonzero harmonic one; the **Boolean odd-XOR case has no global assignment at all**. This difference is essential to selecting the correct diagnostic. The older private `@rsc-quiescent/sheaf-laplacian-measurement-service` explicitly describes a *graph*-Laplacian and computes `Σw(xᵢ-xⱼ)²` using implicit identity restrictions; it is not evidence of arbitrary cellular-sheaf compatibility or legal rights. Keep that older specialized service's actual consumer contract intact rather than silently changing its numerical semantics.

Operations' existing [`assayFiniteParityGluing`](https://github.com/coesite-operations/operations-services-section/blob/main/lib/harmonization-frontier.mjs) is the narrowly bounded read-only computational projection of this **already-formalized** parity case. ### Science receiving and handoff

The same evidence must yield a *falsifiable* result in the original work: admitted law/revision, chosen cover, typed stalks, restriction morphisms, a concrete counterexample or gluing witness, evidence status in FOUR, degree of proved mathematical result, failure locality, independently reproducible check, and the receiving office's original obligation. Mark each statement **kernel-checked in its precise model**, **computationally verified under recorded assumptions**, **literature-backed**, **conjectural**, or **missing**. Do not upgrade an analogy to theorems by restating it in a report. When Engineering has no compatible code path, use the existing typed-circuit and SignGraph/SignChain procedures to improve that owner rather than adding a new mathematics engine.

## Boundary to Engineering and Operations

Science owns admissible definitions, proof obligations, calibration, and counterexamples; Engineering owns mechanisms and compatibility repairs; Operations owns rollout, live observation and independent receiver acceptance. A request can be sent upstream again if the comparison exposes an invalid premise. Do not merge distinct canonical knowledge stores or fabricate consensus.

An obligation can be triaged urgently when safety or active service loss is evidenced. Otherwise prefer partial ordering based on justified urgency, receiver harm, propagation and verified recoverable cost; incomparable claims stay incomparable. Never add several downstream symptoms as separate benefits of one upstream repair.

## Closure

Science receiving requires the responsible Science office to re-run and accept the declared assayer, or to specify the remaining proof and measurement gap. An Engineering handoff is a qualified specification, not Science's acceptance of an Engineering result. Attach evidence to the existing typed work and SignGraph/SignChain surfaces; do not create a new Tunesday-only register.

## Literal musical and audiovisual metrology

Tunesday also means music, not merely a metaphor. For a real soundtrack, generative score or microdrama, specify the listening/receiving context before deciding what counts as discord: reference tuning/temperament and pitch, tempo and rhythmic grid, phase and timing, loudness and true peak, spectral balance, dialogue intelligibility, audiovisual synchronization and uncertainty in the measurements. Compare original authored intent with rendered and heard outcomes, including an independent listener or creative authority where perception matters. A deliberate dissonance, asymmetric beat, unusual timbre or psychedelic audiovisual relation can satisfy the artistic contract. Acoustic measurements constrain a claim; they do not substitute for culturally situated creative judgement or assert a universal aesthetic optimum.
