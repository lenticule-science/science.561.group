  • Kolektiva Post, [2023-10-14 Sat 21:14]

    Today in Durham and nearby, there were long rains, which along with the season and temperature means there should be an abundance of puffballs to forage tomorrow morning through afternoon!

    There's Lycoperdon marginatum, which we call the "flaking puffball," and already have noticed more than a half dozen fruit clumps coming up around zone 1 of the land we steward.

    There's also Lycoperdon perlatum, which we call the "speckled puffball." It crops up throughout our Zone 5.

    So the plan right now is to go out and get L. marginatum in the morning while we do our morning garden walkabouts, fry some up with some of our onions, nettle & then add in some local cornmeal, water, random herbs to taste, and bam, breakfast.

    Then we'll head back out get some of the L. perlatum, which we'll dehydrate. I've got this notion that amending wheat flour or similar with it might increase its shelf life, since its antimicrobial to many of the things which rot flour. That's the mushroom I've attached a photo of; we should have some good photos of L. marginatum by tomorrow!
