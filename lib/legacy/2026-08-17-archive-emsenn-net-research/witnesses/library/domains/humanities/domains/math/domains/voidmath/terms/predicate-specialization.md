A **predicate specialization** is a morphism in the [predicate category](predicate-category.md), denoted $f : q \to p$, given by a sub-support inclusion

$$\iota_f : \mathrm{dom}(q) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{dom}(p)$$

where $\mathrm{dom}(p), \mathrm{dom}(q) \subseteq E \times E$ are structurally designated sub-supports attached to $p$ and $q$ within the [entity set](entity-set.md) $E$.

The induced fiber-algebra map $f^* : A_p \to A_q$ is restriction along $\iota_f$, extending by $\bot$ outside the smaller domain:

$$f^*(v)(s, o) = \begin{cases} v(s, o) & \text{if } (s, o) \in \mathrm{dom}(q) \\ \bot & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

A specialization encodes that $q$ is a *sub-predicate* of $p$: $q$'s cells are a structurally designated subset of $p$'s cells, with $q$'s values inherited from $p$'s on that subset and extended by $\bot$ outside. Specialization is one of the four canonical generator classes of the [predicate category](predicate-category.md).

## What specialization encodes

Specialization captures the inheritance-by-restriction relationship between predicates whose domains differ:

- **Domain narrowing**: a predicate *parent-of-a-minor* specializes *parent-of*: its cells are exactly the parent-of cells where the object is a minor.
- **Sortedness**: when the entity set is sorted, predicates may apply only on certain sort combinations; specialization is the morphism from the sorted predicate to the broader one.
- **Subkind relations**: in any taxonomic structure, a more specific predicate is a specialization of a more general one.

The specialization morphism is *order-preserving on cells* but only *partially defined on values* — values extend by $\bot$ outside the smaller domain, which is the formal expression of "the more specific predicate doesn't apply here, so we have no information about it."

## Specialization as the fibration's substitution

In the [predication fibration](predication-fibration.md), specialization is the prototypical case of substitution: the reindexing functor $f^* : \mathcal{E}_p \to \mathcal{E}_q$ along a specialization morphism is restriction-and-extend-by-bottom. This is the cleanest structural connection between the predicate category and the fiber algebras: specialization at the predicate level becomes restriction at the fiber-algebra level.

## Composition

Specialization morphisms compose by composition of inclusions: $f : q \to p$ and $g : p \to r$ compose to $g \circ f : q \to r$ if $\mathrm{dom}(q) \subseteq \mathrm{dom}(p) \subseteq \mathrm{dom}(r)$. Composition with the other three generator classes — [converse](predicate-converse.md), [subject-projection](predicate-subject-projection.md), [object-projection](predicate-object-projection.md) — yields morphisms that may not themselves be specializations: the composition rules are part of the predicate category's structure.
