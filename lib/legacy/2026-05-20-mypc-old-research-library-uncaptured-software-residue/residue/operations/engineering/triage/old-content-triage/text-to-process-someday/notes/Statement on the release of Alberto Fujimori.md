My commitment to opposing all forms of [domination\|domination](domination\|domination) aligns with calls for [prison abolition\|abolishing carceral systems](prison abolition\|abolishing carceral systems); yet, acknowledging the deep wounds inflicted upon countless families during Fujimori's regime demands accountability—a form we often expect these flawed penal institutions to deliver.
