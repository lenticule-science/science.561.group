---
title: My Green Demon
date-created: "[2003](2003)"
---

Out my window  
Across the way  
A light so bright  
And far away  
  
It keeps flashing  
To show a sign  
Encouraging  
Me to find  
  
The green light  
Across the bay  
Holds so much  
In a heartfelt way  
  
I reach for it  
Across the sky so blue  
Hoping that  
My dreams come true
