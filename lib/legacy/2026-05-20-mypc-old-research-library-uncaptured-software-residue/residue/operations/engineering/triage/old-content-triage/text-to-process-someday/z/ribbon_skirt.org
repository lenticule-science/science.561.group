A */[[file:ribbon_skirt.org][ribbon skirt]]/* is a piece of [[file:apparel.org][apparel]] that symbolizes [[file:native_survivance.org][Native survivance]], especially the [[file:resilience_of_native_women.org][resilience of Native women]].

* History
** 2020 Ribbon Skirt Solidarity Movement

   On [[file:d/2020-12-24.org][December 24th, 2020]], a student in [[file:saskatchewan.org][Saskatchewan]] was scolded by her teacher for wearing a [[file:ribbon_skirt.org][ribbon skirt]] to her school's "formal day," suggesting the student should have worn a store-bought matching skirt and shirt.

   By [[file:d/2021-01-05.org][January 5th]], hundreds of [[file:indigenous_people.org][Indigenous people]] posted photographs of themselves wearing ribbon skirts on the [[file:web.org][Web]], in a demonstration of [[file:solidarity.org][solidarity]].
