A */note/* is a type of [[file:information.org][information]], consisting of, at a minimum, an /identifier/ and /content/. In many [[file:information_management_system.org][information management tools]], a /note/ is the most basic unit of information.

Notes are implemented within [[file:groundhog_emacs.org][Groundhog Emacs]], as the most-basic information [[file:schema.org][schema]]. Notes are also an important part of the [[file:zettelkasten_method.org][Zettelkasten method]] of [[file:note_taking.org][note-taking]].
