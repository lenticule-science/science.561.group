 So I want to post this here because every time someone seems me do it, they go =:O=

 In the [[file:../org_mode.org][Org-mode]] manual it's well documented that you can name source blocks with a line starting with =#+name:= above them:

 
 #+name: org-block-1
 #+begin_src org
   ,#+name: demonstration-code-block-1
   ,#+begin_src elisp
   (print "practice gratitude")
   ,#+end_src
 #+end_src

 (see [[https://orgmode.org/manual/Structure-of-Code-Blocks.html#Structure-of-Code-Blocks][15.2 Structure of Code Blocks]] in the manual.)

 You can also name tables, I found that documented in [[https://orgmode.org/manual/Environment-of-a-Code-Block.html][15.4 Environment of a Code Block]], and lists.

 #+name: alpha-paragraph
 What I didn't find documented anyplace is that you can name quote blocks, and also just /ordinary paragraphs/.

 I named that paragraph =alpha-paragraph=, now I'll work with it in Elisp:

 #+name: working-with-alpha 
 #+begin_src elisp :var alpha=alpha-paragraph :results raw :wrap quote
   (print alpha)
 #+end_src

 That, behind the scenes, looks like this:

 #+name: org-block-2
 #+begin_src org :noweb yes
   ,#+name: working-with-alpha 
   ,#+begin_src elisp :var alpha=alpha-paragraph :results raw :wrap quote
     (print alpha)
   ,#+end_src
 #+end_src
 
 Evaluating that results in the following output:

 #+RESULTS: working-with-alpha
 #+begin_quote
  What I didn't find documented anyplace is that you can name quote blocks, and also just /ordinary paragraphs/.
 #+end_quote

 Conveniently, this also means I can use Org-mode's inclusion system to bring in paragraphs from other documents. The definition of [[file:../groundhog_emacs.org][Groundhog Emacs]] should be pulled in below:
 #+begin_quote
 #+include: "../groundhog_emacs.org::definition"
 #+end_quote
 That appears to work, at least at the time of writing. You can also use Org-modes link-handling system to easily link to a specific paragraph this way. Hope y'all find this useful info!

 As requested by [[file:../benjamin_slade.org][Benjamin Slade]], here's the Org-mode all together:

#+begin_src org :noweb yes
  ,#+name: alpha-paragraph
    What I didn't find documented anyplace is that you can name quote blocks, and also just /ordinary paragraphs/.

  <<org-block-1>>

  <<org-block-2>>
#+end_src

   Put this in an empty Org-mode buffer and press =C-c C-c= over the last block and you should get the following:

   #+begin_src org
     #+RESULTS: working-with-alpha
     #+begin_quote
       What I didn't find documented anyplace is that you can name quote blocks, and also just /ordinary paragraphs/.
     #+end_quote
 #+end_src
