The prefermentation basins are located between the south fence and the carbon heap.

You'll need something to stir the basins, i.e. a bedding fork.

  1. Open every basin
  2. Stir the western-most open basin
    1. Scoop the solid contents of the basin above the water level and let them drip.
    2. Repeat that a few times
    3. Close the basin
