Burrow Aquaria

The Burrow Aquaria is a small aquaponics setup inside the Burrow.

Aquaponics is the combination of aquaculture and hydroponics; uniting systems for cultivating plants and fish.

The 561 Burrow is what me and my roommates call our house, where we practice cottage industry.

The primary goal of the Aquaria is to sustain populations of select plants through our winter, for use in the Garden come spring.

A goal is something an individual or group has choosen to put energy toward.

Winter is the season that occurs when a part of the Earth is tilted furthest away from the Sun.

The 561 Garden is the name of the backyard garden I help steward.

Spring is the season that occurs when a part of the Earth is tilting toward the Sun.

Aquaria

Mobile Aquarium

The Mobile Aquarium is an aquarium holding about 2 quarts of water, with glass marble beads as substrate. Azolla floats on the water's surface, and two ghost shrimp, two bladder snails and three rosy-red minnows share the space.

An aquarium is a vivarium filled with water, where aquatic plants and animals are kept.

Aquarium substrate is the substrate used on the bottom of an aquarium.

Azolla is a genus of aquatic plant that floats and looks like a fern.

Palaemonetes paludosus, commonly called ghost shrimp or glass shrimp, are small shrimp.

Physidae, commonly called bladder snail, is a family of pulmonate gastropods.

Pimephales promelas 'Rosy Red', commonly called rosy-red fathead minnow, is a xanthic strain of Pimephales promelas, having a rosy-golden color.

Hardening Basin

The Hardening Basin is an aquarium holding about 5 gallons of water with calcium grit & seashells as substrate, planted with azolla.

Calcium grit is a supplementary poultry feed which helps birds' digestion and egg-laying.

A seashell is a hard mineralized outer layer that some sea critters possess. It usually outlives them, and can be an important crafting supply.

Shrimp Basin

The Shrimp Basin is an aquarium holding about 5 gallons of water. Azolla floats on the water's surface, and approximately 40 ghost shrimp live in the water.

Minnow Basin #1

Minnow Basin #1 is an aquarium holding about 3 gallons of water with calcium grit as substrate, planted with azolla. X rosy-red minnows live in it.

Minnow Basin #2

Minnow Basin #2 is an aquarium holding about 5 gallons of water with calcium grit as substrate, planted with azolla. X rosy-red minnows and eight ghost shrimp share the space.

Biofilter Basin #1

Biofilter Basin #1 is an aquarium holding about 5 gallons of water with calcium grit as substrate, planted with azolla and water lettuce.

Pistia stratiotes, commonly called water lettuce, is a floating aquatic plant.

Biofilter Basin #2

Biofilter Basin #2 is an aquarium hodling about 2.5 gallons of water with calcium grit as substrate, planted with azolla and filamentous algae.

Filamentous algae is algae that grows as intertwined filaments.

Operations

Routines

ROUTINE Top up the hardening basin

Every day, top up the hardening basin with water from the tap. This'll let the existing bacteria neutralize the chloramine in the water, before it goes into any of the basins with life.

Chloramine is a chemical used to make water potable.
