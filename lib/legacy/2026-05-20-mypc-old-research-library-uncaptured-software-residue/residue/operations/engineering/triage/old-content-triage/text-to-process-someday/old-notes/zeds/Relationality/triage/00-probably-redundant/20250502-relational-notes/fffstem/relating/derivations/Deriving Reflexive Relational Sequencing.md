- [summary](summary):: Building on the [domain of relating](domain of relating) and its foundational [ternary relational structure](ternary relational structure), it cannot not be [derive|derived](derive|derived) that this structure undergoes self-relation via the act of [reflex](reflex), generating the [reflexive relational sequence](reflexive relational sequence) that deepens relational structure through recursive layering.

- [relational expression](relational expression):: The [reflexive relational sequence](reflexive relational sequence) is: `{ R(0), R(1), R(2), ... }`, where `R(0)` is the foundational [ternary relational structure](ternary relational structure) and `R(n+1) := reflex(R(n))`.

## Derivation

- [postulate-grounding](postulate-grounding):: In [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/derivations/Deriving the Domain of Relating](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/derivations/Deriving the Domain of Relating), the minimal [ternary relational structure](ternary relational structure) `{ relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating) }` was established as the first complete unit of relationality—both defining the domain and forming its intrinsic content. This structure is not static; as a relational unit, it inherently participates in the generative logic of relation, where structures relate further.

- [postulate-claim](postulate-claim):: It cannot not be [derive|derived](derive|derived) that this foundational relational structure undergoes self-relation—being related to itself through the act of [reflex](reflex)—and that recursive application of this act induces an infinite sequence of progressively deeper relational structures.

- [derivation](derivation)::
    1. **Foundational Structure as Starting Point:**
       - The [domain of relating](domain of relating) contains the foundational [ternary relational structure](ternary relational structure)—a cohesive relational unit denoted as `R(0)`.
       - This unit is not merely a collection of acts but a structured whole: `{ relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating) }`.
    2. **Defining Reflex:**
       - The act of [reflex](reflex) is defined as:
         - `reflex(S) := relating(S, S)`
       - Here, `S` is treated as a **relational structure in its entirety**—the full ternary unit is related to itself as a single operand.
    3. **Generating the Sequence:**
       - Applying reflex to `R(0)` produces the next layer:
         - `R(1) := reflex(R(0))`
       - Each successive application deepens the relational layering:
         - `R(2) := reflex(R(1))`
         - ...
         - For all `n ≥ 0`:  
           `R(n+1) := reflex(R(n))`
    4. **Nature of the Sequence:**
       - Each `R(n)` retains the essential relational type of `R(0)` but gains an additional layer of **reflexive depth**—embedding its predecessor within a new self-relation.
       - The resulting sequence `{ R(0), R(1), R(2), ... }` forms a **recursive hierarchy** of relational structures, each wrapping the last, constructing a progressively deeper relational field that extends indefinitely.

## Formalism

- Let:
    - `R(0) := { relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating) }` (the foundational [ternary relational structure](ternary relational structure))
    - `reflex(S) := relating(S, S)`
- For all `n ≥ 0`:
    - `R(n+1) := reflex(R(n))`
- The [reflexive relational sequence](reflexive relational sequence) is:
    - `{ R(n) | n ≥ 0 }`

## Clarification

- **What Is Reflexed:**  
   Reflex applies to the **entire ternary relational structure as a unit**. Although this structure contains internal relational acts, reflex treats it as a **single relational operand** in the recursive process.

- **Structural Continuity:**  
   Each `R(n)` remains a **ternary relational structure in form**, maintaining the fundamental logic of `R(0)` while adding depth by embedding prior layers reflexively.
