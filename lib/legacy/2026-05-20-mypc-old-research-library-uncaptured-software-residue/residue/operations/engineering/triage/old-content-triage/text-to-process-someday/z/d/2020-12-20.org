** [2020-12-20 Sun 09:38]
 I think I have an idea. My [[file:../org_roam_records.org][Org-roam records]] contain the [[file:../information.org][information]] I've captured about something. I wonder if I could have those records then create a virtual representation of the thing, the way my MUD engine creates virtual things.

 So like, part of a very-well developed term card for a species of bean is a section on its photoperiodism, and the table with that information can be read by Org-babel to create the recipe for "make a fake bean plant" in some virtual environment.

 There's reality, and then there's my personal world I live in, and then there's my records, and then there could be a virtual world based on those records!

 This would let me like, literally test my knowledge of things: does the way I understand a bean plant's relationship with the sun /actually/ work? Virtualize both and test!
** [2020-12-20 Sun 21:40]
 I spent most of today mucking with the [[file:../groundhog_emacs.org][Groundhog Emacs]] configuration, which meant I spent most of the day with my [[file:../emacsen.org][Emacsen]] broken, and so I haven't had my [[file:../org_roam_capture_template.org][Org-roam capture templates]] available, and so haven't been [[file:../note_taking.org][taking notes]] like I was the past few days. Which is fine; I don't think it's like previous times when I'm fallen off my writing habits because I just fell off them. With [[file:../org_roam.org][Org-roam]], I really do think the friction between all the different parts of my [[file:../computer.org][computer]] use is low enough that I'll probably actually stick with it!

 I'll definitely have to refamiliarize myself with [[file:../elisp.org][Elisp]], but I'm also hoping that by structuring the configuration, largely, as a resource of the [[file:../webgaz.org][WebGAZ]], other folk will be able to benefit from it and so add onto it in ways that help me use it better, too.

 I think this means I'm /finally/ ready to start planning copying from my personal Org-roam database into a website database...
** [2020-12-20 Sun 23:12]
 So now that I have my [[file:../emacsen.org][Emacsen]] functioning, I'm left trying to think about how to move forward with... cultivating my [[file:../digital_garden.org][digital garden]]. I think stuff will probably tend to start in my [[file:../daily_record.org][daily record]], and then migrate into the more "formal" records as necessary

 I also want to make sure I have a way for this information to get into my [[file:../website.org][Website]] in a meaningful way. I was talking with [[file:../benjamin_slade.org][Benjamin Slade]] about my plans and I think it might work to export my personal records as Org-mode files, and then use that export directory as the source directory for building the Website. So then I just export a file and republish my website, to share something.

 This'll require adding in tags to prevent exporting on the relevant headlines, to make sure there's not any personal information leaking. Though perhaps it'd be better to tag which headlines /do/ get exported, and leave the rest private...

 Another concern will be links: what'll happen when I link from a public record to a private one?
