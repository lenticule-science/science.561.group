## Claim

Within the [domain of relating](domain of relating), once unary [relational structures](relational structures) are formed through the [reflexive sequence](reflexive sequence) and related across the [reflective sequence](reflective sequence), a further act is required to bind multiple distinct unary structures into a single, ordered composite. This act preserves the individuality of each component while inducing explicit positional structure.

## Derivation

### Postulate

> If multiple unary [relational structures](relational structures) at shared [sequence](sequence) are available and are held in [relational distinction](relational distinction) via [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect), then a composite structure can be [induce|induced](induce|induced) that maintains their order and enables positional access.

### Steps

1. Let `{x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)}` be unary [relational structures](relational structures) at [sequence](sequence) level `n`.
2. Define a new act:  
   `compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`  
   → relates these structures into a single ordered composite.
3. This yields a new structure:  
   `Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`  
   which preserves:
   - The [difference](difference) between components  
   - The [order](order) of their composition
4. Each component `x(i)` remains accessible within the tuple by its position.
5. This composite requires the condition of [ordinal](ordinal):  
   - Each element occupies a fixed relational position within the composite structure.

## Formalism

Let:

- `x(i)` ∈ unary [relational structures](relational structures) at [sequence](sequence) level `n`, where `1 ≤ i ≤ p`
- `compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)` := `Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`

The resulting [reflexive relational tuple](reflexive relational tuple) satisfies:

- Positional access: `Index(i, Tuple(...)) = x(i)`  
- Ordered integrity: order is preserved under [reflex](reflex) and [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect)
