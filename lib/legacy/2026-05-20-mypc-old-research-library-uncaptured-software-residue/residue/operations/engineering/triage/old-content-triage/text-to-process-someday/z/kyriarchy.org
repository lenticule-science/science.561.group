* Introduction

  This record is some of what I know abut /kyriarchism/.
* Kyriarchy

  #+include: "kyriarchy.org::definition"

  The term was coined by [[file:elisabeth_schussler_fiorenza.org][Elisabeth Schussler Fiorenza]] in 1992.
  
  Over time, I've come to use /kyriarchy/ as the most general descriptor for any social order based on domination, regardless of its mechanisms.

  One benefit of the word is that it encorporates [[file:intersectionality.org][intersectionality]] at the foundation of its conception of social orders.
