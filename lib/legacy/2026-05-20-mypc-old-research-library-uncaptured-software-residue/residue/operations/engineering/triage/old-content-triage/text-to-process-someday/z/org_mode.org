* Introduction

  This record is some of what I (emsenn) know about */Org-mode/*. You may use it without limits or promises.

* Org-mode

  #+include: "org_mode.org::org-mode-definition"
  
  Whenever I'm using a [[file:computer.org][computer]], I'm probably using [[file:org_mode.org][Org-mode]]. I use it to keep notes, draft essays, keep ledgers, write software, and more.
