This catalog gathers the synthetic relational-worlding corpus into one
triage-side location of record. Hevi Mala-specific triage files have
been physically consolidated under `triage/personal/writing/fiction/
hevi-mala/`, but the synthetic corpus remains bracketed off from the
published Hevi Mala intake.

## Scope

This catalog includes Praxonic, Navonic, Allovonic, and adjacent
synthetic files, including mixed overlays such as `praxonic hevi
mala.md`.
