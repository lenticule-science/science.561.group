Blue of Shore  
crisp clean blue  
on the crest of  
inch-high waves  
cracking onto beaches  
shimmering  
  
Blue of Glass  
reflective shiny dirty blue  
covering windows  
on thousand-story  
high rises  
  
Blue of Space  
drowning crushing blue  
no action here  
just silence  
meditation  
but most of all  
there is an end
