- [summary](summary):: [relating](relating) and [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate) are structurally distinct; their [difference](difference) is a [necessary condition](necessary condition) for [induce|inducing](induce|inducing) the [domain of relating](domain of relating).
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [relating](relating) has [difference](difference) from [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate)
- [proof](proof)::
	- the [domain of relating](domain of relating) includes:
		- [relating|relating](relating|relating)
		- [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate|unrelating](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate|unrelating)
		- relating relating and unrelating
	- [assume](assume) relating has no [difference](difference) from unrelating
		- relating relating and unrelating and relating relating and relating would be the same
		- this is a reflexive form
	- but the domain of relating is induced by irresolvable including and excluding, not reflex
	- therefore, relating and unrelating must be different
- [corollaries](corollaries):
	- [difference](difference) is a [necessary condition](necessary condition) for [relational structure](relational structure)
	- the [domain of relating](domain of relating) cannot be [induce|induced](induce|induced) from [include|including](include|including) alone.
