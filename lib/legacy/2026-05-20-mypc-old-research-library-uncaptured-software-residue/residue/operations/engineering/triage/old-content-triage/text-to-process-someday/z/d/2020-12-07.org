** Nunavut introduces bill for civilian oversight of RCMP
   :LOGBOOK:
   - Note taken on [2021-01-06 Wed 13:15] \\
     [[file:../royal_canadian_mounted_police.org][Royal Canadian Mounted Police]] in [[file:../nunavut_canada.org][Nunavut, Canada]] may receive civilian oversight for some instances, if a bill introduced by the territory's government passes. The bill ultimately allows the RCMP to investigate themselves, if they choose, however; I view the bill as a gesture toward appeasing [[file:../police_abolitionist.org][police abolitionists]], not a step toward [[file:../abolition.org][abolition]] or even [[file:../reform.org][reform]].
   :END:
