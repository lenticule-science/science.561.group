Striking Ground on a Digital Garden

Ahh, here we are again: striking ground on a new digital garden. This one lives at ~/.emacs.d/org/ - this specific file lives under that at web/com/striking-ground-on-a-digital-garden.org.

Interestingly, this digital garden is being cultivated on my phone, extended with a Bluetooth keyboard and some choice software1. A certain type of hacker calls such a setup a "cyberdeck,"2, and take great pains to make it out of custom hardware. Myself, I needed to be pragmatic: for a bit I wasn't able to access an outlet regularly, so had to be able to sustain my computing off of a relatively small solar panel and battery setup. Being on a phone also means that Org-roam, at least the first version, was too slow to use like how I had been using it on my laptop. So, I've been thinking about how to start over, and given a few different attempts to it during the fall. But now that winter is officially here, it's time to try and officially start something.

So, here I am.
