- [summary](summary):: **self-relation** is the [condition](condition) in which a [relational structure](relational structure) is [relating|related](relating|related) to itself through the [act](act) of [reflex](reflex).

Self-relation does not assume identity or self-similarity. It is induced when a structure performs the act of [relating](relating) upon itself.

This condition enables the formation of nested structural layers and recursive participation.

## Expression

self-relation(S): reflex(S) = relating(S, S)

## Properties

- Requires a structure to be an admissible input to [relating](relating).
- Cannot be derived from [relating](relating) or [triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate](triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate) alone.
- Enables layered depth: a structure becomes part of itself again.
