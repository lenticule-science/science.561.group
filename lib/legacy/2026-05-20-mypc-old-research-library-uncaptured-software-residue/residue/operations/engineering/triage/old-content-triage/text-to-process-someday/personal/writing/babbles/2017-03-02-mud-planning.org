** a fresh look at things
I've been away from working on the MUD for a bit as I think about what a decolonial MUD would look like.

I've been wondering if the concept of a /thing/, or "entity" in more common game-design lingo, is something I should leave behind

it's encouraged to view lakhotiyapi - and other languages of the cultures indigenous to Turtle Island - as an action-based language

in coding this could be viewed as, perhaps, centering the function (if not meeting the definition of functional programming)

so, lets say we've got a rabbit, Sniffs Often

what makes it a rabbit is that it can do rabbit-like actions

perhaps that's the difference:

rather than qualities of things (components of entities), actions?

=(sniff air)= would return a scent, but what that scent would be perceived as would change based on the perceiver

and other actions, =(dig dirt)=, return a scoop of dirt - this time the volume would change depending on what did the digging

...well, that's still not...

the scent would be meant to be its own "action," right? as would the dirt that got dug
