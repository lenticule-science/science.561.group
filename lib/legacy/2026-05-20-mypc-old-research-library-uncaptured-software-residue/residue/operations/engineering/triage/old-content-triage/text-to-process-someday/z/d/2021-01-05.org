** Looking at Portland's new pro-Indigenous directives
   :LOGBOOK:
   - Note taken on [2021-01-06 Wed 13:28] \\
     Back on [[file:2020-12-12.org][December 12th]], the [[file:../portland_city_council.org][Portland City Council]] approved a series of directives designed to facilitate a better relationship between the city and its [[file:../indigenous.org][Indigenous]] residents and neighbors. The biggest directive is an official [[file:../land_acknowledgement.org][land acknowledgement]] that the city will give sometime this year. The directives also establish [[file:../indigenous_peoples_day.org][Indigenous Peoples Day]] as a paid holiday for city employees, an exploration of park fee waivers for Indigenous [[file:../ceremony.org][ceremonies]], strategizing to increase how many Indigenous employees the city has, creating a recurring training for all city employees, and departments have to create plans on developing collaboration with sovereign tribal nations in providing services to urban Indigenous communities.
     
     While any appreciation of the need to change our relationship is good, I'm unsure if Portland appreciates that... well, it's a city, and it's a bit telling of the relationship they want to have with Indigenous communities that "city departments" and "sovereign tribal nations" are supposed to communicate as peers. I see glimmers of a future that's much like the relationship between [[file:../anthropology.org][anthropologists]] and Indigenous peoples, where any old colonial student feels fully entitled to consult with the leaders of tribal nations.
   :END:
