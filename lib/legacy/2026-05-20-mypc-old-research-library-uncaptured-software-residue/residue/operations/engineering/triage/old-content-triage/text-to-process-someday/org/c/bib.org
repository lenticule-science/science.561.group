#+name: 2021-10-11-pic-review
Before a space has a trail, it has landmarks, and folk orient their journey through the space by their distance from these landmarks. /Pollution is Colonialism/ exists as a wonderful navigator through the novel space of anti-colonial science.

The liminal edges of academe form a frontier, and like all frontiers, colonialism brings it to a state of war, immediately setting up supply chains, skirmishing lines, and all the other trappings of conflict. (Of course, in academia, these look like grants and dissertation committees.) /Pollution is Colonialism/, as an anti-colonial work, doesn't work so hard to attack anything. Instead, it brings us, the readers, into this frontier. Here, in the space beyond the well-worn trails of establishment discourse, Liboiron serves as a deft navigator, pointing to and orienting with the landmarks that exist in this space: pollution, land use, cosmology.
