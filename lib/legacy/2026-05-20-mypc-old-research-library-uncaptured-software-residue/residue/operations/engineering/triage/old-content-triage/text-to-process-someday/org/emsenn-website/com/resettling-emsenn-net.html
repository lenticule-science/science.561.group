Resettling emsenn.net

I'm coming back to emsenn.net after quite a bit of time away. I explored different ways of working with my computer, and exploring the Web, over the past few seasons. After it all, I'm making the choice (for now) to go back to using Emacs and Org-mode to handle what I'm constructing as my digital garden.1

I have some snippets of information from past seasons spent in my digital garden, but they're more… seeds, than saplings I can transplant, to continue the metaphor. But they're enough, with what I've learned from the past, to probably get things built back up pretty quickly. Right now, the garden is organized into a few sections - each broken down to a three-letter abbreviation for the sake of my fingers while they type.

  • bul/, or bulletins, are timely updates about me, my communities, and the world.
  • com/, or communications, are things I've written for other folk to read. This bit of writing is at com/resettling-emsenn-net.org, for example.
  • per/, or personal, are personal documents.
  • def/, or definitions, are definitions for terms that exist in my worldview.
  • ref/, or references, are references to external things (books, people.)

There's also a projects listing, which for now just lists ter/, or Teraum, a fantasy world I made up. That's actually the project I'd like to focus on now: bringing in the old source material I have for Teraum into this new garden.

One thing that gives me pause in diving right back in is that I remember in the past using a complex assembly of Org-mode macros to assist my writing, and now (as far as I know) I don't have those same tools on-hand. Maybe I'll find them! As-is, I plan on starting off pretty dang manual.
