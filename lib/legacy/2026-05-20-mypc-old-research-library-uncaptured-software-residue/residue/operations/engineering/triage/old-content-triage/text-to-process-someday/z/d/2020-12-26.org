** On Dennis Schep's "Freeing space…"
   :PROPERTIES:
   :EXPORT_DATE: [2020-12-26 Sat 11:32]
   :END:

   #+index: Neo-pioneerism!Propaganda critiques!On Dennis Schep's "Freeing space…"
   
   I'm not going to bother forming a well-structured critique of [[file:../dennis_schep.org][Dennis Schep]]'s [[https://roarmag.org/essays/freeing-space-building-worlds-outside-of-state-and-capital/]["Freeing space: building worlds outside of state and capital"]]this piece; it isn't worth it. The article is [[file:../neo_pioneer.org][neo-pioneer]] nonsense. Building [[file:../world.org][worlds]] outside of the [[file:../state.org][state]] is something [[file:../indigenous.org][Indigenous]] people do regardless of where they live, inside [[file:../kyriarchy.org][kyriarchism]] or not. [[file:../colonial.org][Colonizers]] moving "outside the state," is usually just a [[file:../larp.org][LARP]] of [[file:../anarchism.org][anarchism]], supported by larger systems of [[file:../colonialism.org][colonialism]] than the closest state. Going out into the woods to make [[file:../art.org][art]] isn't building a better world, it's moving where [[file:../resource_scarcity.org][resource scarcity]] isn't too severe to secure [[file:../profit.org][personal prosperity]], which is the same thing colonials have been doing for centuries.
     
   Take a lesson from me and other [[file:../indigenous.org][Indigenous]] people, who are building our indigenity within the shoals of [[file:../settler_colonialism.org][settler-colonialism]]: You don't need to move anywhere to build a better world, you just have to move your hands.
     
   And, ignoring the neo-pioneerism expressed, I think solutions like those proposed by Dennis gravely underestimate how much of our [[file:../resources.org][resources]] we've been using. Moving out of a [[file:../city.org][city]] isn't going to do anything on its own, even if we all stopped making [[file:../trash.org][trash]] and using [[file:../water.org][water]] and [[file:../energy.org][energy]]. People need to be actively making [[file:../soil.org][soil]], undoing [[file:../generation.org][generations]] of damage to our [[file:../freshwater_resevoirs.org][freshwater resevoirs]] and [[file:../topsoil.org][topsoil]], not pursuing personal passions. There's a reason I'm mostly active on the [[file:../web.org][Web]] during [[file:../rainstorms.org][rainstorms]] and the [[file:../winter.org][winter]]: I'm busy making [[file:../soil.org][soil]] the rest of the time!
     
   To put it really clearly: moving to another place because it has resources you don't have where you are isn't anything different than kyriarchism. If you want to build a better world, imitate the people who live in a better world. [[file:../decolonization.org][Decolonize]], [[file:../indigenize.org][indigenize]].
