My name is [[file:emsenn.org][emsenn]] and this is a catalog of my personal records. It's a collection of /hypertext records/ created through my experiences with /decolonizing/ and finding /indigeneity/ within /settler-colonial society/.

I'm 30 years-old, /Itázipčo/ of the /Lakota/, /queer/, and I have permanent health and mobility issues. I've developed skills in /land stewardship/ and /urban direct action/.

The goal of this catalog is to cultivate support for applying these skills, through providing information about my relationship to the various concepts involved. 

There are currently call_garden-file-counter() records in the catalog, containing a total of call_garden-word-counter() words. To help folk find there way, there are a few pages which provide various listings and overviews:

- There's a [[file:d/index.org][list of my daily records]]. These are things that might go on a conventional /blog/ or /social media profile/.
- There's an [[file:records_index.org][list of all the records]], in alphabetical order. This is useful if you know the name of what you want to look up, or you want to dive into a random page.
