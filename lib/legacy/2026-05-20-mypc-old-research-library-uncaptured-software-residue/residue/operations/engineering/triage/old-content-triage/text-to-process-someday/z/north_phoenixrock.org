** Trade

   /North Phoenixrock/ uses a gift economy with outsiders. Gifts are made available at the base of the settlement. Here's a list of gifts the settlemnet likes:

   - Dirt :: Since they live on the side of an active volcano, the settlement has no natural dirt.
   - Ores :: Again, living on a volcano, they have no access to metal.

   Here's a list of gifts that are frequently abundant for visitors:

   - Stone :: As the settlement is constantly terracing the volcano and expanding into its walls, there is usually a great volume of stone available.
   - Gravel :: Same reason as above.
   - Hay :: The green-spaces of North Phoenixrock are, for now, mostly cultivated for grass for hay, which is used extensively by the settlement as a building material and fuel.
