- [summary](summary):: **unrelating** is the act of [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) a [relational structure](relational structure) from a [domain of relating](domain of relating).


Unrelating is the complementary act to relating. It asserts structural non-participation — a refusal of relation.

Unrelating is not negation of existence, but negation of inclusion.

## Expression

unrelating(a, b): a excludes b  
unrelating(b): b is withheld from relation

## Properties

- Asymmetric: `unrelating(a, b) ≠ unrelating(b, a)`
- Opposed to relating: `unrelating(a, b)` structurally resists `relating(a, b)`
