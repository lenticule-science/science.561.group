* Procedures
** Burning Brush
- PLAN:
  - Check weather conditions
  - Consider fire service availability
  - Plan for resultant ash
  - Schedule firepit time 1h before dusk
- PREP:
  - Collect brush approx. 2 meter west of firepit.
  - Ready fire extinguisher, 2 5gal buckets of soil, 1 2gal bucket of water, firestick.
- DO:
  1. Start a small fire in the pit, build with wood until there are some coals flaming.
  2. Add brush, slowly growing the flame
  3. Once the flame fills the bottom of the pit, add brush more rapidly, even if it reduces the fire to nearly-coals.
  4. Once the flames flare up again, add another layer to damp the the flame.
  5. Repeat step 4 twice.
  6. Stir the fire thoroughly with the fire stick
  7. Repeats step 4-5.
  8. When you're out of brush, stir the fire to keep flares from growing too large.
  9. Keep stirring occassionally as it goes to ash.
  10. Come back in the morning and collect.
** Rake leaves off the front lawn
SCHEDULED: <2021-11-25 Thu>
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   3:00
:END:
- Qualification
  - It must have frosted 3-6 times.
  - It must not have rained in the past 2 days.
- Requirements
  - Tools
    - Long-tine rake
    - Bedding fork
    - Tarp
  - Labor
    - One or more physically-able person
- Instructions
  1. Rake the leaves into rows from east-to-west, approximately 2 meters apart.
  2. Rake those rows into piles.
  3. Lay the tarp out next to the pile closest to the back gate.
  4. Fork the litter onto the tarp.
  5. Drag the tarp to the Soilworks and add it to the Carbonheap.
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 until complete.
- Records
  - How long did it take? (multiplied by how many people did it)
  - How much litter did you add? (estimated in pounds)
