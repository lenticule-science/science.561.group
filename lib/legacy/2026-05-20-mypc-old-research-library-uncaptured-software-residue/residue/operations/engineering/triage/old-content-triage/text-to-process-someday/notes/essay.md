An ***essay*** is (definition:: an [artifact\|artifact](artifact\|artifact) that results from [writing\|writing](writing\|writing), usually a few hundred to several thousand [word\|words](word\|words), usually focused on one or just a few [topic\|topics](topic\|topics).)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:

- [expository essay\|Expository essays](expository essay\|Expository essays) are for [informing\|informing](informing\|informing) people about a [concept\|concept](concept\|concept) or [topic\|topic](topic\|topic).
- [descriptive essay\|Descriptive essays](descriptive essay\|Descriptive essays) are for [describing\|describing](describing\|describing) a [thing\|thing](thing\|thing), often focusing on one [enc/def/quality\|quality](enc/def/quality\|quality) or set of qualities.
- [narrative essay\|Narrative essays](narrative essay\|Narrative essays) are for [narrative storytelling\|narrative storytelling](narrative storytelling\|narrative storytelling).
- [argumentative essay\|Argumentative essays](argumentative essay\|Argumentative essays) are for [arguing\|arguing](arguing\|arguing) a [truth\|truth](truth\|truth) about a [thing\|thing](thing\|thing).
- [Analytical essay\|Analytical essays](Analytical essay\|Analytical essays) are for [relating\|relating](relating\|relating) a [concept\|concept](concept\|concept) or [topic\|topic](topic\|topic) to [event\|events](event\|events) or [information\|information](information\|information).
- [comparative essay\|Comparative essays](comparative essay\|Comparative essays) are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [thing\|things](thing\|things).
- [critical essay\|Critical essays](critical essay\|Critical essays) apply a [concept\|concept](concept\|concept) or [synthesis\|synthesis](synthesis\|synthesis) of concepts to events or information.
- [reflective essay\|Reflective essays](reflective essay\|Reflective essays) explore my [relationship\|relationship](relationship\|relationship) to a [thing\|thing](thing\|thing).
