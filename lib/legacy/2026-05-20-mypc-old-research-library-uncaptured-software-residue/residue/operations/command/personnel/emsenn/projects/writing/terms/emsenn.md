***emsenn*** is a Lakota land steward and independent researcher. The central project of emsenn's work is [relationality](../../relationality/index.md) — a [metaphysical](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) and philosophical-mathematical framework, grounded in Lakota [epistemologies](../../philosophy/terms/epistemology.md), that treats relations as ontologically prior to entities.

This site, [emsenn.net](../../index.md), is emsenn's research vault. See [About emsenn](../about/index.md) for more.
