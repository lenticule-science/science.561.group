In [emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s writing, ***my friend*** is a relational term used to refer to people with whom emsenn maintains bonds of mutual care, trust, and intellectual exchange. Friendship, in emsenn's usage, is not casual — it names a relation constituted through sustained attention and reciprocal obligation.
