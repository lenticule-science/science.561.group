The ***emsdeavor*** is the name for the *endeavor* of ***emsenn***.

The emsdeavor houses the work emsenn does for their own joy, which includes agroecology, math, and philosophy.

Currently, as of 2026-03-10, the emsdeavor is being re-organized, through the establishment "emsemioverse," a specialized agential knowledge system.

The emsdeavor inherits the qualities of an endeavor, as they're described in the emsemioverse.

The emsemioverse inherits the qualities of an agential semioverse, as its described in the emsemioverse, as well as specific semioverse and other specifications required for its operations.
