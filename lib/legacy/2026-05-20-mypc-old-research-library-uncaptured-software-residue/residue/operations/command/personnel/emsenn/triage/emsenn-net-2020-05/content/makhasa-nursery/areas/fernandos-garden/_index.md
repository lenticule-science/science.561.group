There are two beds adjacent to a tax accountant's office, near the southwest corner of the residential building on-site.


## west Fernando bed {#west-fernando-bed}

Itself divided into two halves, it's planted with tomatoes, peppers, and basil on the northern side, and corn, beans, and squash on the southern side.


## south Fernando bed {#south-fernando-bed}

From east to west, this bed is planted with sweet basil, Silene coronaria, lambs ear, stinging nettle, virginia creeper, and blackberries
