## Content
According to the Romance Writers of America, the romance genre includes novels which contain both of two defining characteristics: the novel must center a love plot as its primary or major plot, and the novel must have an ending that is “emotionally satisfying and optimistic” (RWA, [About the Romance Genre](https://www.rwa.org/Online/Romance_Genre/About_Romance_Genre.aspx)). The romance genre has seen monumental growth over the decade since the release of E.L. James’ _Fifty Shades of Grey_, and with that has come an increase in critical conversations surrounding the genre. Romance criticism as a field covers a wide range of topics--—from titles that helped create the genre as it is today, to issues of representation of BIPOC in romance publishing and genre-specific awards, to discussions about the role of queer representation in historical romance novels.


## Scope
This annotated bibliography is intended to provide an introduction to critical discussions about the romance genre. To support this goal, this bibliography includes resources covering a wide range of romance-related topics, and materials include nonfiction books, novels, interviews, podcasts, and online articles. These resources have been selected in order to provide a broad overview of relevant issues that appear in critical conversations and writing about the romance genre. This annotated bibliography is not intended to serve as a starting point or a list of recommendations for new readers to the genre, but instead is meant to help existing readers better understand the genre’s past and present, as well as where it may be headed in the future.

Sections of this annotated bibliography:

- ***[Broad Guides](#broad-guides)*** covers resources that broadly cover tropes and trends in the genre.
- ***[Historical Perspective](#historical-perspective)*** includes resources that look at the history of the romance genre.
- ***[Influential Texts](#influential-works)*** includes romance novels that changed or shaped the genre.
- ***[Interviews](#interviews)*** includes interviews with influential authors and publishers speaking to trends or the big picture in the genre.
