[2021-12-31 Fri 12:33]

The land here is so clearly freaking out, due to the unprecedented weather, and I feel deeply like I should be doing something about it. But when it comes to stewarding the land, human labor is only a lever between the push of our Will and the fulcrum of material reality, and I do not see any way in which my labor can be sufficient.

I don't know what to do with my days, and I have spent my days to this point figuring out what to do with them.

After two cold snaps, the Land seems willing to accept this weather as Spring, but I fear a future snap will kill this growth. Stored energy in the soil resulting in very little except the disruption of hormone cycles.

[2021-12-31 Fri 17:23]

A few things in my life have been pushing me to feel like I should write some sort of... science-fiction fantasy novel. But I don't know if I have it me. Like, someone should do it, but... I don't think I'd be happy if anyone but me did it.

I have the conceit: "There is no liberation without community."
