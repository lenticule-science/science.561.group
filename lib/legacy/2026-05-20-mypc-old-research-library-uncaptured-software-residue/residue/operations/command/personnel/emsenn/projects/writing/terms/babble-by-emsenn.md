A ***babble*** is a short, unplanned, generally unedited written thought by [emsenn](./emsenn.md). Babbles are published in the [babbles](../writing/babbles/index.md) section of the site. They serve as a low-friction way to capture observations, reactions, and working thoughts without the structure required of essays or formal writing.
