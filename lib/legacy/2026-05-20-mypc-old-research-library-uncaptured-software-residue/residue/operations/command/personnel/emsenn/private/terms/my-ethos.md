emsenn's ethos is the set of ethical commitments and intellectual traditions that inform how emsenn approaches research, [relating](../../relationality/index.md), and lived practice.

It draws on the following ethical traditions:

- [care ethics](../../philosophy/concepts/care-ethics/index.md), existential ethics, consequentialism, [dialectical](../../philosophy/terms/dialectics.md) ethics, Levinasian moral attention, capability ethics, the Parable of the Good Samaritan, ordo amoris, virtue ethics, relativist ethics, pragmatist ethics, [phenomenological](../../philosophy/terms/phenomenology.md) ethics, feminist ethics, post-structuralist ethics, structural ethics

And the work of:

- [Carol Gilligan](../../general/people/carol-gilligan.md), [Nel Noddings](../../general/people/nel-noddings.md), [Virginia Held](../../general/people/virginia-held.md), Simone de Beauvoir, Emmanuel Levinas, Maurice Merleau-Ponty, John Dewey, Amartya Sen, Martha Nussbaum, [Emma Goldman](../../general/people/emma-goldman.md), Ivan Illich, Audre Lorde, Dorothy Day, [Gregory Bateson](../../general/people/gregory-bateson.md), [[donna-haraway|Donna Haraway]], [Judith Butler](../../general/people/judith-butler.md), María Lugones, [Michel Foucault](../../general/people/michel-foucault.md), [bell hooks](../../general/people/bell-hooks.md), [Paulo Freire](../../general/people/paulo-freire.md), [Édouard Glissant](../../general/people/edouard-glissant.md), Roy Bhaskar, Edward Said, White Buffalo Calf Woman, Black Elk
