## Introduction {#introduction}

This document contains the complete curriculum for studying "eco-industrialism," a philosophy of agriculture and industry which emphasizes democracy and sustainability.

The curriculum is being created by me, [emsenn](https://emsenn.net), for myself, to learn about these topics outside of a formal education institution.

**_Please note_** that this whole thing is in early development. If you have any input, especially recommended things to learn and things to learn them from, please let me know!

#### Bio-1: Introductory Biology {#bio-1}

This course is a basic coverage of biology. It is required for [Introductory Plant Biology](#introductory-plant-biology) and [Introductory Animal Biology](#introductory-animal-biology).

This course is based in part on the public notes for the [Introductory Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/biology/7-013-introductory-biology-spring-2018/index.htm) course taught at MIT in Spring 2018 by Prof. Angelika Amon, Prof. Hazel Sive, and Dr. Diviya Ray


##### Lessons {#lessons}


###### Bio-1-A: Cellular Anatomy {#bio-1-a-cellular-anatomy}

-   Cells: plasma membrane, cytoplasm, and a concentration of DNA
-   Eukaryotes and Prokaryotes: nucleated and not cells.
-   Eukaryotic organelles
    -   nucleus
    -   endoplasmic reticiculum
    -   golgi apparatus
    -   mitochondria
