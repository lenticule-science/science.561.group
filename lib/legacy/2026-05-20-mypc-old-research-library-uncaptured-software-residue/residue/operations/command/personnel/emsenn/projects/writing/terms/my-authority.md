In [emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s writing, ***my authority*** refers to the domains in which emsenn claims standing to speak — grounded in lived experience, sustained study, and relational accountability rather than in institutional credentials. emsenn's authority derives from practice as a Lakota land steward, independent researcher, and community participant, not from positions within academic or governmental hierarchies.
