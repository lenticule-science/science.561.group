as the society I live in adjusts to the realities of a pandemic, I've found myself with less time to be online

I've been spending a lot of time working with food: gardening, cooking, and distributing free food in my community[^fn:1]

most of the time I've been on a computer has been to take quick notes, like this one

I'll be working on different ways to make communicating online a little easier on me, but for now I'm going to use my website to share stuff, and my [email](mailto:emsenn@emsenn.net) to talk to folk

[^fn:1]: I've been working with [Food Not Bombs 919](https://www.facebook.com/FoodNotBombs919/), a local chapter of [Food Not Bombs](http://foodnotbombs.net/new%5Fsite/)
