<p class="verse">
When judgement day comes I'll be writing a novel<br />
about all the things which haven't happened to me.<br />
I'll write about love, and I'll write about triumph<br />
Confidence, clarity, certainty, absolution.<br />
I won't say a word about the caustic fear<br />
that brought us headlong to the end<br />
There'll be no mention of the little deaths<br />
the false justice or apprehension.<br />
Gods don't need that.<br />
</p>
