The ***2025 relaunch of emsenn.net*** refers to [emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s project of rebuilding the research site using Quartz 4 and restructuring the underlying Obsidian vault as an [Agential Semioverse Repository](../../technology/specifications/agential-semioverse-repository/index.md). The relaunch involved migrating content from earlier site formats, establishing the current directory structure, and developing the formal specifications that govern how the vault is organized.
