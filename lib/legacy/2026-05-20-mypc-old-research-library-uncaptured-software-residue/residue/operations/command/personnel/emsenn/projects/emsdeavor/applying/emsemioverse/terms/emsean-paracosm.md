The ***emsean paracosm*** is [emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s term for the imagined world that accompanies and informs the research project. A [paracosm](../../media/terms/paracosm.md) is a detailed imaginary world; the emsean paracosm is the speculative domain in which [relational](../../relationality/index.md) structures, [semiotic universes](../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md), and formal architectures are explored before they are committed to rigorous formalization. It functions simultaneously as a paracosm in the psychological sense (a sustained imaginative engagement) and as a [secondary world](../../media/terms/secondary-world.md) in the literary sense (a self-consistent fictional reality that generates narratives), with its formal properties explored through the [Reproducible Paracosm](../../media/disciplines/literature/reproducible-paracosm.md) framework.
