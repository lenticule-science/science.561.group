[emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s Humble Choice membership is a subscription to the Humble Choice game bundle service. Humble Choice delivers a monthly selection of PC games, and emsenn uses it as one source of [games](../../games/index.md) for recreation and as material for thinking about game design, narrative structure, and interactive systems.
