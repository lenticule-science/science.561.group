<p class="verse">
Roses are red,<br />
violets are blue.<br />
In the post-modern world,<br />
the poems read you.<br />
<br />
Do you want ads<br />
for fresh-fruit bouquets?<br />
Or to have misconstrued<br />
things your opponents say?<br />
<br />
Twitter doesn't care<br />
if privacy is harmed.<br />
It's quite profitable<br />
for your data to be farmed.<br />
<br />
Reddit is orange,<br />
Facebook is blue.<br />
Your friends are the product,<br />
and so are you.<br />
</p>
