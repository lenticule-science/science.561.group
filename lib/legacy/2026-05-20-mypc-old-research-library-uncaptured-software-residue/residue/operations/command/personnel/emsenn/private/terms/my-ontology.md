*/My [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md)/*, that is, the [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md) of emsenn, is a hybrid of Lakota and Taoist ontologies, formalized with liberal [epistemology](../../philosophy/terms/epistemology.md).

- my [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md) isn't a taxonomy but a map of derivatives
  - not "what is being" but "what must necessarily derive from being"
- dynamic and procedural
  - existing induces changing, necessitating more existing
- formal
- relational
  - the fundamental unit is a relation
- differential
  - see derivatives
- recursive
- reflexive
- aporiatic
  - a la deconstructionism, there is no resolving the fundamental claims
- paraconsistent or inconsistent
  - influenced by dialetheism
- semantic deflationism
- negative [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md)
- abyssal [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md)
- process [ontology](../../philosophy/terms/ontology.md)
- haecceity
