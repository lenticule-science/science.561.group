Deeply Unserious Koward, or DUK, encapsulates a disposition characterized by an avoidance of earnest engagement with critical issues and responsibilities. This term implies a retreat from the necessary courage to confront or commit to actions that uphold one's ethos, often in favor of superficial or evasive behavior that undermines collective efforts toward meaningful change. It suggests a lack of depth in both thought and action, reflecting an unwillingness to participate authentically in the relational universe where Mitákuye Oyás'iŋ calls for interconnected responsibility and universing demands active presence within the web of existence.
