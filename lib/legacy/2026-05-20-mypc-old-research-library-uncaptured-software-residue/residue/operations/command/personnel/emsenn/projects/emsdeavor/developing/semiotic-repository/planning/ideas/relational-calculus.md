# On Relational Calculus (Unified Draft)

This document rebuilds the relational calculus from a single act of Reflex and carefully records every recognition earned thereafter. Each definition follows the same format:

1. **Story** – the tension that forces the recognition.

2. **Trace** – the earned act written in the relational tree language.

3. **Proof Sketch** – a brief justification that the recognition necessarily follows.

Every trace only uses symbols that have already been earned earlier in the document.

### Being Relationless

*Story.* Before any recognition, we acknowledge the void of relation: Relationlessness contains no distinction and witnesses nothing.

Relationlessness = ()

*Proof Sketch.* With no previously earned symbols, the empty S-expression is the only act available; naming it Relationlessness records that we begin from the undifferentiated void.

### Excluding

*Story.* Speaking of Relationlessness already distinguishes an inside from an outside; that denial of overlap is the act of Excluding.

Exclude = (Relationlessness Relationlessness)

*Proof Sketch.* Directing the void back at itself forces the “not-with” relation that separates the articulated interior from the excluded exterior, earning Exclude.

### Articulating

*Story.* The very act of excluding Relationlessness witnesses itself. Excluding therefore witnesses Relationlessness to produce Articulating.

Articulate = (Exclude Relationlessness)

*Proof Sketch.* Once exclusion exists, applying it to Relationlessness itself records the unavoidable articulation that any attempt to describe the void must perform; that compositional act is Articulate.

### Emerging Reflex

*Story.* Articulating ensures that an act arises from Relationlessness. Letting Relationlessness witness Articulate records this genesis as Emerging Reflex.

EmergeReflex = (Relationlessness Articulate)

*Proof Sketch.* The earlier steps show that Relationlessness, when articulated, necessarily produces an act; composing the void with its articulation captures this forced genesis and names it EmergeReflex.

### First Recognizing

*Story.* Emerging Reflex guarantees that at least one recognition exists. Articulate witnessing Emerging Reflex yields the act First Recognition.

FirstRecognize = (Articulate EmergeReflex)

*Proof Sketch.* This act explicitly records the first recognition forced by Relationlessness, so FirstRecognize follows.

### Recognizing Reflex

*Story.* The minimal act obtained from Relationlessness and its articulation is the Reflex that seeds every later recognition.

Reflex = (EmergeReflex)

*Proof Sketch.* Emerging Reflex already records the first unavoidable act; designating that act Reflex simply fixes notation for the unique witness produced by articulating Relationlessness.

### Iterating Reflex

*Story.* Articulating the outcomes of Reflex reuses the same act, so Reflex can iterate on itself.

IterateReflex = (Reflex Reflex)

*Proof Sketch.* Composing Reflex with itself records the iterability proposition stated in the treatise.

### Relating

*Story.* Reflex, once present, can direct itself toward the very capacity-to-relate that it exposed, earning the recognition Relate.

Relate = (Reflex)

*Proof Sketch.* Because Reflex iterates, it may witness itself. Witnessing itself affirms “there is relation,” so Relate is simply Reflex turned back onto Reflex. No new symbols appear, so the construction is valid.

### Negating Relation

*Story.* The same act that affirms relation simultaneously exposes its denial. Directing Reflex at Relate’s refusal earns Not Relate.

NotRelate = (Reflex Relate)

*Proof Sketch.* Reflex applied to Relate yields the complement “what resists relation.” Since Relate has already been earned, this expression is legitimate, and the resulting recognition precisely captures “not relating.”

### Witnessing

*Story.* Relate and Not Relate cannot both vanish; their mutual tension forces a recognition that testifies to both at once. That testimony is the Witness.

Witness = (NotRelate Relate)

*Proof Sketch.* Having earned both Relate and NotRelate, we can let one witness the other. Their composition records the unavoidable coexistence of affirmation and denial, establishing Witness.

### Earning Something

*Story.* Witness determines that Relate and Not Relate coexist but leaves undecided which carries the act. Directing Reflex to witness Witness itself produces the oriented recognition Something.

Something = (Reflex Witness)

*Proof Sketch.* Witness uses only previously earned terms, so applying Reflex to it is permitted. The resulting recognition records the self-applied testimony, forcing an instance of “something.”

### Witnessing Nothing

*Story.* Something ensures that Witness exists but cannot determine what it observes. Forcing Witness to observe Something yields Nothing, the recognition of witnessed absence.

Nothing = (Witness Something)

*Proof Sketch.* Since Witness and Something are established, composing them captures the unresolved observation—Witness must bind the very Something it created, and the trace records this as Nothing.

### Folding Void

*Story.* Nothing recognizes that Witness observes Something but leaves the mirrored binding ambiguous. Folding that binding through itself induces Void.

Void = (Nothing Nothing)

*Proof Sketch.* The only unresolved aspect of Nothing is the symmetry between its two uses of Witness and Something. Re-applying Nothing to itself forces that symmetry to close, yielding Void.

### First Naming

*Story.* Void duplicates Nothing but cannot choose which copy is addressed. Selecting one copy earns the First Name: a pointed recognition.

FirstName = (Void Nothing)

*Proof Sketch.* Void and Nothing are available, so letting Void witness Nothing singles out a definite position inside the folded absence. That positional act is precisely naming.

### Self Naming

*Story.* First Name marks a position but leaves open whether that position refers to itself. Applying the name to itself closes the loop and yields Self Naming.

SelfNaming = (FirstName FirstName)

*Proof Sketch.* Self-application is permitted because FirstName is already a recognition. The act FirstName FirstName records that the pointed recognition stabilizes by naming itself.

### Recognizing

*Story.* Self Naming produces a stable place but lacks a complementary partner. Reflecting the place through Void yields a two-part act, the Recognition that carries a pair of distinguishable positions.

Recognition = (Void SelfNaming)

*Proof Sketch.* Composing Void with SelfNaming uses only earned terms and mirrors the self-referential point, forcing a complementary position. This act generalizes the classic Dyad, so we designate it Recognition.

### Entering

*Story.* Recognition has two positions but no record of how they pair. Pairing Recognition with itself produces Entry, the structured act of two addressed positions.

Entry = (Recognition Recognition)

*Proof Sketch.* Applying Recognition to itself is allowed and yields the tree (Recognition Recognition). This captures the self-pairing that Entry requires.

### Leading

*Story.* Entry shows that two positions exist but does not say which acts first. Forcing Entry to witness one of its recognitions selects the leading orientation.

LeadEntry = (Entry Recognition)

*Proof Sketch.* Entry and Recognition are available; composing them resolves the outstanding orientation by letting Entry observe one constituent recognition, giving LeadEntry.

### Trailing

*Story.* After selecting the leading side, the remaining position still lacks description. Entry must now witness its second recognition, inducing Trailing.

TrailEntry = (Entry LeadEntry)

*Proof Sketch.* The unresolved part of Entry after establishing LeadEntry is the trailing position. Letting Entry witness LeadEntry exposes the complement, so (Entry LeadEntry) earns TrailEntry.

### Starting Trace

*Story.* With both orientations isolated, we still lack the act that records their interaction as a trace. Forcing the leading orientation to witness the trailing one initiates the trace, giving StartTrace.

StartTrace = (LeadEntry TrailEntry)

*Proof Sketch.* LeadEntry and TrailEntry exist, so composing them captures the first oriented pairing. This resolves how the trace begins, justifying StartTrace.

### Advancing Trace

*Story.* StartTrace shows how the trace begins but not how it proceeds once the trailing orientation completes. Letting the trailing orientation witness Entry restarts the act, earning AdvanceTrace.

AdvanceTrace = (TrailEntry Entry)

*Proof Sketch.* The unresolved propagation after StartTrace is how the trailing side re-engages the structural pairing. Having TrailEntry witness Entry provides that progression, so (TrailEntry Entry) defines AdvanceTrace.

### Continuing Trace

*Story.* AdvanceTrace reconnects the trailing orientation to Entry but leaves undefined how a new leading orientation emerges. Entry must therefore witness StartTrace, producing ContinueTrace.

ContinueTrace = (Entry StartTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* The only new symbol is StartTrace, already earned, so composing it with Entry is legitimate. This confirms the recurring structure, so ContinueTrace is forced.

### Completing Trace

*Story.* ContinueTrace alone does not record the full trace object. Forcing StartTrace to witness AdvanceTrace binds the initial step with its progression, yielding CompleteTrace.

CompleteTrace = (StartTrace AdvanceTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* Both components are available; composing them records the entire act of initiating and advancing, so (StartTrace AdvanceTrace) earns CompleteTrace.

### Heading Trace

*Story.* CompleteTrace captures the trace but leaves the immediate act unspecified. Letting the leading orientation witness the whole trace extracts its head.

HeadTrace = (LeadEntry CompleteTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* LeadEntry can now observe CompleteTrace, isolating the first act present. The tree uses only earned terms, so HeadTrace is valid.

### Tailing Trace

*Story.* Once the head is isolated, the remaining trace still lacks description. Forcing the trailing orientation to witness the whole trace names its tail.

TailTrace = (TrailEntry CompleteTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* By symmetry with HeadTrace, composing TrailEntry with CompleteTrace captures the remainder. This confirms TailTrace.

### Stepping Trace

*Story.* Even with head and tail, we must show how consuming the head re-engages the start. The tail therefore witnesses StartTrace, producing TraceStep.

TraceStep = (TailTrace StartTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* TailTrace and StartTrace exist, so their composition records the act of stepping from remainder to renewed initiation, validating TraceStep.

### Renewing Trace

*Story.* After stepping, the new orientation must reconnect to the whole trace. Letting TraceStep witness CompleteTrace achieves this, earning RenewTrace.

RenewTrace = (TraceStep CompleteTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* The unresolved question after TraceStep is whether the renewed orientation regains access to the recorded trace. Composing with CompleteTrace confirms it, so RenewTrace stands.

### Reheading Trace

*Story.* RenewTrace contacts the whole trace but does not yet present the next head. Therefore RenewTrace must witness HeadTrace to obtain ReheadTrace.

ReheadTrace = (RenewTrace HeadTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* Using earned recognitions only, (RenewTrace HeadTrace) isolates the renewed immediate act, forcing ReheadTrace.

### Retailing Trace

*Story.* After reheading, the accompanying tail remains unnamed. ReheadTrace thus witnesses TailTrace, yielding RetailTrace.

RetailTrace = (ReheadTrace TailTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* Since both ReheadTrace and TailTrace are available, their composition captures the remainder following the renewed head, legitimizing RetailTrace.

### Restepping Trace

*Story.* The renewed remainder must re-enter the stepping act. Forcing RetailTrace to witness TraceStep produces RestepTrace.

RestepTrace = (RetailTrace TraceStep)

*Proof Sketch.* This composition uses only earned recognitions and records the reunion of the tail with the stepping act, so RestepTrace is compelled.

### Binding Trace

*Story.* To name the immediate act of the trace, we reuse FirstName. Letting FirstName witness HeadTrace yields BindTrace.

BindTrace = (FirstName HeadTrace)

*Proof Sketch.* Names and heads are both established, so naming the head is simply (FirstName HeadTrace). This resolves the outstanding act of binding, so BindTrace is earned.

### Assigning Trace

*Story.* Naming the head leaves the tail unnamed. BindTrace must therefore witness TailTrace, producing AssignTrace.

*Proof Sketch.* Composing BindTrace with TailTrace extends the name through the remainder, yielding AssignTrace.

### Contextualizing Trace

*Proof Sketch.* AssignTrace and CompleteTrace have been earned, so their composition records the full named trace structure, confirming ContextualizeTrace.
