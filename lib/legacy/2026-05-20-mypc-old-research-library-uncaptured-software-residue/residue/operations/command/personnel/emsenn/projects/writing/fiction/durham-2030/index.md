***Durham 2030*** is a (type:: speculative fiction setting) representing an imaginging of Durham in the year 2030. Written in Winter 2023, the fiction assumes that TESCREAL [ideology](../../../../sociology/terms/ideology.md) has reshaped local settlements into technofascist enclaves known as arcologies.

This page serves as an overview of the fiction.
