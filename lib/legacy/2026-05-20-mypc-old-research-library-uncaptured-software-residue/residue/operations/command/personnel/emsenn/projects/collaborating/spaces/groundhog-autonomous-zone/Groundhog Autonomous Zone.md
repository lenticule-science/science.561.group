The ***Groundhog Autonomous Zone*** is a (method:: [[Synthetic Place of Ritualized Ethnosis]]) made for (method:: [[vernacular autoethnosis]]) in (function:: [[Pursuit of Total Liberation]]). It is guided by (method:: [[Indigenous insurrectionary anarcho-nihilism]]) and oriented in [[antagonising|antagonism]] and [[negating|negation]] of [[settler-colonialism]]'s fundamental need for [[death]].

It's implemented as an interlinked collection of enriched natural language records, designed to describe the relationality between different information, to [[revealing mystery|reveal mysteries]]. This software-side of things is led by (leader:: [[emsenn]]). (actor:: [[emsenn]]), (actor:: [[Es]]) and (actor:: [[Terran]]) are the main actors for this project. As of [[2023-12-28]], (leader:: [[emsenn]]) leads project from its methods to its function.

The **GAZ** is a collection of [[Markdown]] files, managed with [[Obsidian]]. This interation got started in [[2023-12]], as [[emsenn]]'s personal notes, and then on [[2023-12-25]], the [[Groundhogs]] [[deciding|decided]] to transition the notes toward being the GAZ itself.

Notes are, as of [[2023-12-28]], being written to help us solidify our [[belief|beliefs]] and [[../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/value|values]] before we do our [[Migration to Pine Ridge]], and to serve as a foundation for continuing the GAZ, as an [[emergent space]], when we [[Living with Phahin Sinte Wakpala|live there]]. These notes are also being used for our [[GAZ Autoethnographic Project]], which is being done with [[Hannah Kass]].
