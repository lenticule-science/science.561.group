[emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s intellectual practice is the set of habits, methods, and commitments that structure emsenn's research work. This includes daily reading, writing, vault maintenance, formal specification development, and the ongoing work of articulating [relationality](../../relationality/index.md) across multiple disciplines. The practice is grounded in Lakota [epistemologies](../../philosophy/terms/epistemology.md) and informed by the ethical traditions described in [my ethos](./my-ethos.md).
