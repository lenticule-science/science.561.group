*Emsean thought* is a school of thought developed by [emsenn](./emsenn.md) (me).

It exists as a way to distinguish what's "my" thinking compared to what is me pulling in the ideas from "elsewhere," but the quotes there should make it clear that concept isn't so simple.

More precisely, it's where I put things that I'm expressing in a way that can only be understood through the lens of my other thinking, which isn't to say that it isn't expressed somewhere else in a much better way.
