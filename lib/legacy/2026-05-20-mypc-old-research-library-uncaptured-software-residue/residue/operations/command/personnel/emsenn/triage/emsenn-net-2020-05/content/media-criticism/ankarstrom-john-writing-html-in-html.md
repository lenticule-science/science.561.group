A few days ago I wrote and responded briefly to a piece; I'm adding those notes here. The piece was John Ankarstrom's ["Writing HTML in HTML"](http://john.ankarstrom.se/html/). I immediately disagree with his reasoning, even though I agree with handwriting HTML. A static site generator _adds_ a layer of redirection between you and your _layout_. It _removes_ a layer of redirection between you and your _content_. Also, he straight-up advocates copying boilerplate and that way lies madness. One shouldn't copy a boilerplate framework but add boilerplate to a content, or else you end up with more and more legacy detritus.  I don't wanna pile on but his site has like a half-dozen pages and at least a couple broken links, which is a "time capsule" but eh, that's what the back entries in a git repo are for!
