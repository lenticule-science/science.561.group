[emsenn.net](https://emsenn.net) is the top-level Internet domain I've used for my personal website. As of 2023-12-24, it is used to host [my Webplace](emsenn's-webplace.md).

During Arab Spring, ***emsenn.net*** was used for syndicating the [writing](../../writing/index.md) of [anarchists](tri/anarchist.md) in the Middle East and North Africa region, specializing in Kurdish perspectives.
