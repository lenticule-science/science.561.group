Most proprietary systems are proprietary because they're taking advantage of temporal
arbitrage: the fact no one else is offering the same thing as them for free, yet.  That's
kind of a greedy way to make money, so we'd rather put our resources into people who share
their information for free, and only charge for their actual work - just like us.
