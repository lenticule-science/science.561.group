[emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s ***corporate person*** refers to the legal and institutional identity that [the state](../../sociology/terms/the-state.md) and market systems assign to emsenn — the person who files taxes, holds accounts, and is recognized by bureaucratic structures. emsenn distinguishes this from the relational person who exists through bonds of [kinship](../../sociology/terms/kinship.md), land stewardship, and intellectual community. The distinction matters because [settler-colonial](../../sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) governance operates primarily through corporate personhood, and emsenn's work in [relationality](../../relationality/index.md) is partly an articulation of what personhood looks like outside that framework.
