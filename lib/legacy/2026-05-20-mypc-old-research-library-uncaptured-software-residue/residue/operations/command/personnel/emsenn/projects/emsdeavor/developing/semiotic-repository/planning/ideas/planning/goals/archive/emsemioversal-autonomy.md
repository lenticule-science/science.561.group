## Objective

The emsemioverse incites the next "cycle" of deriving its own fixed point as the "end-point" of its current cycle.

That is, without human-in-the-loop, the emsemioverse orchestration system is able to continue proceeding through its established order-of-operations.

## Plain Summary

In order to properly understand this objective, one would need to understand what it means for an agential semioverse to derive its own fixed point, but put plainly:

The emsemioverse is a "knowledge system" created by emsenn that uses a special set of mathematical operators to help it measure which knowledge is true.

This is done by intaking signals, such as emsenn's writing or external research papers, and iteratively serializing and formalizing them from their hetereogeneous form into standardized semantic information that the systems syntactical operators can process.

This processing results in new information which guides future autonomous intake systems, such as those querying the latest articles from science journals.
