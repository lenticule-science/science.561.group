[emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s ***[anarchist](../../sociology/terms/anarchism.md) lineage*** refers to the tradition of [anarchist](../../sociology/terms/anarchism.md) thought and practice that informs emsenn's political and ethical commitments. This includes both the historical lineage of [anarchist](../../sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) theory ([mutual aid](../../sociology/terms/mutual-aid.md), [prefigurative politics](../../sociology/terms/prefigurative-politics.md), anti-state organizing) and the specific relationships and communities through which emsenn came to these commitments.
