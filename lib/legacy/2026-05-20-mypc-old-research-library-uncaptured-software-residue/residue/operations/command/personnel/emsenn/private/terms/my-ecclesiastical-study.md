[emsenn](./emsenn.md)'s ***ecclesiastical study*** refers to emsenn's engagement with religious institutions, spiritual traditions, and the organizational structures of organized religion. This is studied not as a practitioner within a specific church but as a researcher interested in how [spiritual](../../spirituality/index.md) and ceremonial structures relate to governance, community formation, and the broader relational framework.
