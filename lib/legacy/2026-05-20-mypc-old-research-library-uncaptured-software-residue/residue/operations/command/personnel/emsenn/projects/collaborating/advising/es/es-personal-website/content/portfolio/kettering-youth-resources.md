Regardless of what you want - from volunteer opportunities, to activities for making new friends, to taking charge of your health - consider this your one-stop shop. Below, you'll find suggested resources and an introduction to why they might be relevant to your life.

# Community Engagement
## [Kiwanis Kettering-Centerville](https://ketcenkiwanis.org/?page_id=39)
Whether you want to join a group at your school or work with the wider Kettering-Centerville community, the local Kiwanis International branch gives you the opportunity to plan events that benefit local and international youth. In Kettering, high school students can join Key Clubs at Alter High School and Dayton Regional STEM to build leadership skills and plan and execute events, and middle school students can join the Builders Club at Dayton Regional STEM to grow and share your creativity and community focus. If you want to bring these clubs to your school, reach out to the Kettering-Centerville Kiwanis branch and ask how you can get one started. Alternatively, if you're looking for a way to get involved outside of school, the branch also allows teens to volunteer and support events they put on every year, including Summer Fun at the Trails, which provides meals and outdoor activities for local children.

## [United Way of the Greater Dayton Area](https://dayton-unitedway.org/)
If you're looking for volunteer opportunities but are unsure of where to start, or if you're looking for activities and programs, check out United Way. The local branch covers Dayton and the areas surrounding it, but there are plenty of opportunities specific to Kettering. Whether you want to get involved in a foodbank or if you're trying to find a new after-school program, United Way can give you some direction. Plus, volunteers with the group give feedback on proposed activities, opportunities, and more, so you get to help decide what happens in Kettering.

## [Little Miami Watershed Network](http://lmriverkleeners.org/index.html)
Whether you're already environmentally conscious or trying to learn more about the natural world around you, check out the Little Miami Watershed Network. This group is interested in protecting and improving the health of the local watershed to ensure long-term vitality and sustainability. You can get involved by signing up for classes and activities to find out why it's so important to protect the watershed, or you can volunteer with the group to clean up waterways or even lead activities.

## [YWCA Dayton](https://www.ywcadayton.org/)
If you're interested in working alongside like-minded people toward social justice, the YWCA would be a great fit. This organization isn't a gym - it's a group of community members from the greater Dayton area, working to empower women and end racism. If you're new to activism or looking to brush up on information, you can attend trainings and workshops on social justice. They also offer community service programs so that you can help end bigotry and oppression in your community, and leadership opportunities so you can make choices to help your neighbors. After-school and summer programs can help develop the skills you need to lead - public speaking, decision-making, planning, and collaboration, as well as hands-on experience with social justice work.

## [Kettering Youth Council](https://www.playkettering.org/kettering-youth-council/)
If you're interested in gaining leadership experience, honing your decision-making and planning skills, and deciding what happens in your community, consider joining the Kettering Youth Council. This opportunity through the city government is open to students in grades 6-12, and allows you to work with a group of your peers to brainstorm and plan events and to give feedback on some city proposals. On the Youth Council, you can voice your ideas and opinions and help shape the future of Kettering.

# Recreation
## [Dayton-Metro Library: Kettering-Moraine Branch](http://www.daytonmetrolibrary.org/locations/kettering-moraine)
Want a space to hang out after school or on the weekends, or fun events to get to know other teens in town? Check out the Kettering-Moraine library! With a space designed for teens, access to video games, and programs that give you the chance to hang out, play some games, talk about books, or craft, the library is no longer just a silent study space. Plus, while you're here, you can discover the newest books and audiobooks, and get personalized recommendations regardless of what you enjoy. And for those looking to contribute to the library and impact your community, you can get involved on the youth board and give feedback, so that the library can continue to reflect the interests and needs of the entire community.

## [South YMCA (Kettering)](https://southy.playerspace.com/)
The local YMCA offers a huge variety of clubs and sports, all of which let you meet and spend time with friends and meet your community members. Swimming, volleyball, and basketball are among the sports available, but in addition to physical health, YMCA works to support mental health and community connection. Their programs emphasize health and growth across all facets of life, so beyond physical education, the they offer camps, weekend programs, and classes to develop skillsets like babysitting - all while getting to know other teens and meeting new community members.

## [Girls on the Run Dayton](https://www.gotrdayton.org/)
Girls on the Run is only open to teen girls and young women and, like the YMCA, gives you the chance to grow emotionally and connect to your community as you learn about sustainable, life-long physical health. If you're in middle school, you can join the group as a runner and, in the process, learn about mental and emotional health, as well as make lasting connections with teens like you. High schoolers can join as mentors and coaches, learning valuable leadership skills and making a difference in the health of others.

## [WKET 98.3](https://onlineradiobox.com/us/wket/) & [Dayton Youth Radio](https://www.wyso.org/podcast/dayton-youth-radio)
If you're interested in learning more about broadcast radio, public speaking, music, or storytelling, consider applying for one of the two local teen-run radio stations. Kettering Fairmont High School students can apply for WKET 98.3, which is a rock music radio station that has operated for over 50 years. If you attend another school or are interested in storytelling rather than music, the Dayton Youth Radio would be a better fit: every year, young adults from across the greater Dayton area work together under Basim Blunt to learn about broadcasting and storytelling. Either way, you'll get to decide what your community listens to, and broadcasting provides great experience with public speaking.

# Health & Growth
## [Kettering Backpack Program](https://www.ketteringbackpack.org/)
Eligible middle school students can receive a bag every Friday that provides meals for the weekend; if you're not currently having your dietary needs met, consider reaching out to your guidance counselor and asking if you're eligible for assistance. The bags are given out discreetly, and help ensure you have the energy to focus on what matters - spending time with friends and family, catching up on games or books, and catching up on sleep before the school week starts again.

## [Youth Resources Center](https://www.mcohio.org/departments/human_services_planning_and_development/youth_resource_center.php)
Whether you're looking for a job, homework help, mental health support, or legal assistance, the Montgomery County Youth Resources Center can listen and provide the right service. Their services are wide-ranging and available virtually or in-person, so you can get the help you want or need to thrive and grow. No matter how small it seems, the Youth Resources Center is happy to connect you with peers and connect you with services that meet your needs and help you grow into the person you want to be.

## [South Community, Inc.](https://www.southcommunity.com/services/youth-care/#youth-care)
After-school or over the summer, individually or with a group, you can find emotional and mental health support with South Community. This organization offers a wide variety of options for teens navigating health, self-image, family life, and more, and South Community can meet you at your home, at your school, or in their office, so that they can help you wherever you are and whatever you need.
