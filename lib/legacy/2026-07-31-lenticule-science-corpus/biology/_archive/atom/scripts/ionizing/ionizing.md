# ionizing

Generates ions from [`atom`](../../atom.md) by gain or loss of electrons — e.g. the [hydrogen-ion](content/hydrogen-ion/hydrogen-ion.md) (a bare proton, the unit of acidity) and the bases that define an [alkali](content/alkali/alkali.md). Ionic chemistry sets the pH and reactivity that biomolecules and cells must hold in homeostasis.
