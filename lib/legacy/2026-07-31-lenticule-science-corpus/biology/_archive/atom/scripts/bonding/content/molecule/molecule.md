A **molecule** is two or more [atoms](../../atom.md) held together by chemical bonds. In the life tree the molecules that matter are the **biomolecules**: proteins — including [enzymes](scripts/folding/content/enzyme/enzyme.md), the catalysts that make metabolic reactions fast enough for life — and nucleic acids such as [DNA](scripts/encoding/content/dna/dna.md), the informational polymer. Bonding atoms into reactive, foldable, information-bearing structures is the step that makes a self-sustaining reaction network — and so a [cell](scripts/assembling/content/cell/cell.md) — possible. Molecule is generated by [`bonding`](../../scripts/bonding/bonding.md) atoms; it is the substrate floor's second storey, between bare matter and the living cell.
