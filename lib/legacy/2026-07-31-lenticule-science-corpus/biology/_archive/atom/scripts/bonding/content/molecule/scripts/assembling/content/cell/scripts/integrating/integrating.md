# integrating

Generates [organism](content/organism/organism.md) from [`cell`](../../cell.md): one or many cells integrate into a discrete, self-maintaining individual — the living agent. The organism is the apex: homeostasis is its self-maintaining settling, response-to-stimulus its perceive→act loop. Physiology, development, heredity, behaviour, evolution, and ecology are all read off the living agent.
