# bonding

Generates [molecule](content/molecule/molecule.md) from [`atom`](../../atom.md): atoms share or transfer electrons into bonded structures. The bonded, reactive macromolecules — proteins ([enzyme](content/molecule/scripts/folding/content/enzyme/enzyme.md)) and nucleic acids ([dna](content/molecule/scripts/encoding/content/dna/dna.md)) — are the chemistry from which life is built. This is the substrate floor: matter bonding into the molecules life will settle into agents.
