A **fungus** is a kind of [organism](../../../organism.md): a **heterotroph** with a chitin cell wall whose body is not a bounded shape but a **network**. It feeds by pushing digestion *outside* itself and absorbing the products — life run as a distributed, growing front rather than a discrete individual.

## How a fungus is built and lives

- [`threading`](scripts/threading/threading.md) → [hyphae](scripts/threading/content/hyphae/hyphae.md): the tubular filament (chitin wall, septa or coenocytic, anastomosing) → `networking` → [mycelium](scripts/threading/content/hyphae/scripts/networking/content/mycelium/mycelium.md), the autopoietic network → `cognizing` → fungal-intelligence (distributed problem-solving) and `fruiting` → fruiting-body → `sporing` → spores (basidium, ascus, zoospore).
- [`digesting`](scripts/digesting/digesting.md) → extracellular-digestion (heterotroph, saprotroph; substrate, root-exudate).
- [`decomposing`](scripts/decomposing/decomposing.md) → fungal-decomposition (recycling cellulose/lignin; nutrient-cycling) — the planet's carbon recycler.
- [`symbiosing`](scripts/symbiosing/symbiosing.md) → mycorrhiza (hartig-net, arbuscule, mycorrhizal-networks), lichen.
- [`classifying`](scripts/classifying/classifying.md) → fungal-taxonomy.
