# fibering

Generates [hyperdoctrine](content/hyperdoctrine/hyperdoctrine.md) from [`functor`](../../functor.md): a functor P : C^op → Lat assigning to each context a fiber of propositions, with reindexing along substitution carrying adjoint quantifiers ∃ ⊣ f* ⊣ ∀ (Lawvere). This is the bridge to the corpus's own category theory — the **predicate-category** is the hyperdoctrine whose fibers are the bilattice FOUR, generated downstream at positing. Standard category theory is the degenerate shadow of which this is the realized instance.
