# simplicializing

Generates [simplicial-set](content/simplicial-set/simplicial-set.md) from [`presheaf`](../../presheaf.md): take the presheaf on the simplex category Δ (finite ordinals, order-preserving maps). It is the combinatorial skeleton — n-simplices with face and degeneracy maps — from which the whole ∞-branch is built.
