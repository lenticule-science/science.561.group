# filling

Generates [quasi-category](content/quasi-category/quasi-category.md) from [`simplicial-set`](../../simplicial-set.md): require every inner horn Λⁿₖ (0 < k < n) to admit a filler. Composites then exist but only up to a contractible space of choices — composition becomes coherent rather than unique.
