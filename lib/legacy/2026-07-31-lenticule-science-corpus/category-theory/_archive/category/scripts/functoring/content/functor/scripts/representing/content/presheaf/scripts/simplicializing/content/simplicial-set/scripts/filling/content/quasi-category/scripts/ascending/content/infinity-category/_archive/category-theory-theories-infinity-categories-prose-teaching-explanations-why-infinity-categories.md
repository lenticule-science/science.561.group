Ordinary categories capture objects and [morphisms](../../ordinary-categories/morphisms/morphism.md), but many constructions in
homotopy theory require higher [morphisms](../../ordinary-categories/morphisms/morphism.md) that witness equivalences.

Infinity categories add [morphisms](../../ordinary-categories/morphisms/morphism.md) in every dimension so that composition and
equivalence are tracked up to higher homotopies, not strict equality.

This gives a setting where the objects of interest are equivalent in a flexible
way while still supporting categorical reasoning.
