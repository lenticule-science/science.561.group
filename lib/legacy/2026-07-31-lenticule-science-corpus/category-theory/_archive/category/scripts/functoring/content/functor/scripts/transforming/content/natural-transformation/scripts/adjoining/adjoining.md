# adjoining

Generates [adjoint-pair](content/adjoint-pair/adjoint-pair.md) from [`natural-transformation`](../../natural-transformation.md): a unit η : 1 ⇒ GF and counit ε : FG ⇒ 1 — two natural transformations witnessing F ⊣ G through the triangle identities. The left adjoint freely adds structure, the right adjoint forgets it. "Adjoints arise everywhere" (Mac Lane); in this corpus the instance ∃ ⊣ ∀ on hyperdoctrine fibers is the one that matters.
