# pointing

Generates [object](content/object/object.md) from [`presheaf`](../../presheaf.md): the Yoneda recovery. The representable presheaf Hom(−, A) determines an object A fully and faithfully — distinct objects induce distinct representables, nothing lost or added. This is where objects re-enter the arrows-first tree: an object is not primitive, it is *pointed to* by its bundle of incoming arrows.
