# puncturing

Generates [horn](content/horn/horn.md) from [`simplicial-set`](../../simplicial-set.md): the simplicial set Λⁿₖ obtained from the boundary of Δⁿ by removing its k-th face. A horn is "almost" a simplex; whether its missing face can be filled is what the higher-categorical structure turns on.
