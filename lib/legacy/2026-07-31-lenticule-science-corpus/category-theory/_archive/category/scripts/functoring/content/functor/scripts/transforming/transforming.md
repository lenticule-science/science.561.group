# transforming

Generates [natural-transformation](content/natural-transformation/natural-transformation.md) from [`functor`](../../functor.md): a choice-free family of morphisms comparing two parallel functors, coherent with every arrow of the source category (the naturality square). Mac Lane: categories were invented to study functors, and functors to study natural transformations — naturality is what makes a construction canonical.
