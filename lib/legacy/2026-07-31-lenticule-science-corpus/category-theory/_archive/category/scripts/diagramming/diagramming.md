# diagramming

Generates [commutative-diagram](content/commutative-diagram/commutative-diagram.md) from [`category`](../../category.md): a diagram of objects and morphisms in which all directed paths between the same two objects compose to the same morphism. Commutation is how the corpus states every definition, theorem, and universal property — an equation drawn as a spatial pattern.
