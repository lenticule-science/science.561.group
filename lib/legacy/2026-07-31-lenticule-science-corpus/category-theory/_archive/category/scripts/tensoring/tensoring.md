# tensoring

Generates [monoidal-category](content/monoidal-category/monoidal-category.md) from [`category`](../../category.md): equip a category with a tensor product ⊗ and a unit object I, coherent up to the pentagon and triangle. A monoidal category is the many-object generalization of a monoid — objects combined by ⊗ with a unit, where a monoid had elements combined by an operation with an identity. It is the base over which enrichment happens.
