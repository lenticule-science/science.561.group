# enriching

Generates [enriched-category](content/enriched-category/enriched-category.md) from [`monoidal-category`](../../monoidal-category.md): replace hom-*sets* with hom-*objects* of the base V, with composition given by the tensor and identities by the unit. An ordinary category is enriched over Set; richer V (abelian groups, topological spaces, simplicial sets) lets the morphisms themselves carry structure — the principle central to higher category theory.
