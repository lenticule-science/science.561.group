# uniting

Generates [unit-object](content/unit-object/unit-object.md) from [`monoidal-category`](../../monoidal-category.md): the object I that is neutral for the tensor product (I ⊗ A ≅ A ≅ A ⊗ I). It plays the role of the monoid identity one categorical level up, and is the source of identity maps for enriched composition.
