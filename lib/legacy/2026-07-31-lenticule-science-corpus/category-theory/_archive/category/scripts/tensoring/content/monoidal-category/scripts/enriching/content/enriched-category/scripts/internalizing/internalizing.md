# internalizing

Generates [hom-object](content/hom-object/hom-object.md) from [`enriched-category`](../../enriched-category.md): the object Hom(A, B) of the base V that plays the role of "morphisms from A to B." In an ordinary category the hom is a set; internalizing it into V (a chain complex, a space, a simplicial set) is what gives the morphisms structure.
