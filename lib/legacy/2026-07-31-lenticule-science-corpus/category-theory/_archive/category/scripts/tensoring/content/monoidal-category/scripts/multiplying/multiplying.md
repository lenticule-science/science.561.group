# multiplying

Generates [tensor-product](content/tensor-product/tensor-product.md) from [`monoidal-category`](../../monoidal-category.md): the bifunctor ⊗ : C × C → C that combines two objects into one, with the unit object as its neutral element. It is the categorical lift of a monoid's multiplication.
