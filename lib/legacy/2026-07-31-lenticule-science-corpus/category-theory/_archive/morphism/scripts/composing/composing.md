# composing

Generates [composition](content/composition/composition.md) from [`morphism`](../../morphism.md): the step that requires arrows to chain associatively, turning a bare collection of arrows into something a category can be built on. A generator lives under its domain's `scripts/`, and the object it yields lives under the morphism's own `content/`.
