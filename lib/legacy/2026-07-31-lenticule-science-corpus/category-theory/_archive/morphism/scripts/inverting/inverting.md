# inverting

Generates [isomorphism](content/isomorphism/isomorphism.md) from [`morphism`](../../morphism.md): a morphism equipped with a two-sided inverse, so the round trip is the identity in both directions. This is the categorical notion of sameness — objects connected by an isomorphism are interchangeable for every categorical purpose.
