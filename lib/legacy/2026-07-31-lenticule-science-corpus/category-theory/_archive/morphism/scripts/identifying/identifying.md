# identifying

Generates [identity-morphism](content/identity-morphism/identity-morphism.md) from [`morphism`](../../morphism.md): the distinguished do-nothing arrow on each object, neutral for composition. It is the categorical name for "doing nothing," and an object is recovered from its identity morphism — so identifying is where objects re-enter the arrows-first reading.
