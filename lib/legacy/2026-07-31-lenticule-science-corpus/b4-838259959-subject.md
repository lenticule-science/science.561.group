In the [relationality](../_index.md) framework, **subject** names a position in a directed act of [relating](relating.md): the relatum *of which* the predicate is asserted. The subject and the [object](object.md) are the two sides of any directed relating; what makes them subject and object — rather than two interchangeable poles — is the directionality of the relating itself.

The subject is not a substance, a thing-with-properties, or an agent in the philosophy-of-mind sense. A subject in this framework is constituted by the relating in which it stands — it is what the predicate is asserted *of*, and it would not be that subject (or any subject at all) outside the relating. There is no Cartesian *res cogitans* underneath, no grammatical-subject as a property the world has independently of being read; the subject is a *position*, and the position is constituted by the directed relating that gives it content.

## Subject and object are not symmetric

In substance metaphysics, the subject is the bearer of properties; the object is what is acted upon. In relational ontology, the directionality is more austere: subject and object are the two coordinates of a directed [predication cell](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/predication-cell.md), structurally distinct, with the predicate running *from* the subject *toward* the object. Swapping the two does not give the same relating with the poles relabeled; it gives a different relating, related to the original by [predicate converse](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/predicate-category.md).

This asymmetry is what allows relating to constitute its terms in the first place. A symmetric primitive could not produce two distinct positions; the directed primitive does, and the *subject* position is one of the two positions it produces.

## Distinction from grammatical and Cartesian subject

Three readings of *subject* should be kept apart:

- **Grammatical subject** — the noun phrase in subject position of a sentence. A surface feature of language, not the ontological category the framework names.
- **Cartesian subject** — the *res cogitans*, the thinking thing as substrate of mental properties. Substance-metaphysical; the relationality framework refuses the substrate while keeping the term *subject* for the position.
- **Relationality subject** — a position in a directed relating, constituted by the relating itself. *This* reading.

The three coincide in particular cases (a sentence whose grammatical subject names a Cartesian subject who is occupying a relationality subject position) but the framework's term means the third.

## Subjects are entities

A subject is always a [relational entity](relational-entity.md) — a stable pattern occupying the subject coordinate of one or more predication cells. The same entity can occupy the subject position of one relating and the object position of another; *being a subject* is not a property of the entity but of the position the entity is in within a particular relating. The framework has no inherent subjects, only entities that are subjects within specified relatings.
