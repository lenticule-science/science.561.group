# General Mathematics

## What this node is
This concept is generated by the **predicate** morphism in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. To predicate is to assert structure of a subject before that structure is indexed or specialized; general mathematics is exactly that assertion left general — the unindexed material that holds for mathematics as such, prior to splitting into algebra, topology, number theory, or any particular subject. It is not a subject filed under mathematics but the predicative base from which those subjects are later distinguished, which is why it carries the Mathematics Subject Classification top class msc-00 (general).

## What it generates / contains
There are no canonicalized subconcept directories under `content/` yet; the body presently lives in `_archive/` as source material awaiting canonicalization. That material covers:

- **History of mathematical ideas** — the historical development of the function concept from Dedekind's *Abbildung* through Frege, Church, and Eilenberg–Mac Lane.
- **Mathematical communication and practice** — accountable closure, framed units, medium-specific rigor, and transport across representational registers.
- **Foundational surveys** — algebraic foundations of learning systems (closure operators, bilattices, Galois connections, formal concept analysis, persistence).
- **General vocabulary** — term entries for carrier, signature, state, morphism, function, operation, domain, duality, derivation, factorization, and related primitives.
- **Method and calibration** — the test for situating claims, the calculus for cross-discipline claims, and reflexive calibration theory.
- **Visualizations** — Collatz and affine figures generated from the computing toolkit.

## Orientation
This node sits beneath the **predicate** morphism in the predication chain; the parent node holds the morphism that produces it, and indexed mathematical subjects (order theory, type theory, category theory, and the rest) are its siblings — the same predicative base specialized by a further index. Un-canonicalized source material sits in `_archive/` until promoted into a `content/` subconcept here.
