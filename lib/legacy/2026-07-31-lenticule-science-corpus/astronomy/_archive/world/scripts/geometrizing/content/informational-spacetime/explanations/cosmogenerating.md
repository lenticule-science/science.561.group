**Cosmogenerating** is the morphism that carries the [aperiodic-articulation-category](../) to [informational-spacetime](library/informational-spacetime/informational-spacetime.md).
It is the constructive content of the thesis this whole branch exists to prove:

$$ \text{articulating} \vdash \text{the real world}. $$

The real world exists *because of* articulation.
The file at this path is the proof-term asserting that judgment; every cell generated below it — the whole descent, informational → quantum → classical → thermal — is an inhabitant of the proposition.

## What cosmogenerating does

[Aperiodic-extension](../docs/dev/foundations/aperiodic-extension/aperiodic-extension--from-bare-cedar-fog.md) "extends an [aperiodic germ](../docs/dev/foundations/aperiodic-germ/aperiodic-germ--from-bare-cedar-fog.md) to the largest aperiodic window consistent with discipline" — applying flow/nucleus to grow the germ while preserving aperiodicity, admissibility, and sheaf-compatibility.

**Cosmogenerating is aperiodic-extension restricted to the articulation germ and run to totality.** Where aperiodic-extension grows *a* germ to *a* maximal window, cosmogenerating takes the germ to be the seed of articulated existence itself and takes the maximal admissible window to be the whole: the colimit of the extension is informational-spacetime.

$$ \text{informational-spacetime} \;=\; \mathrm{aperiodic\text{-}extension}^{\,\ast}(\text{articulation germ}). $$

## Why this is the aperiodic corner, necessarily

The four regimes are classified by where the closure ordinal $\kappa$ lands and whether $H^1$ vanishes (GENEALOGY §1, §5).
Strict-finite and Scott-continuous regimes have $H^1 = 0$: their closure orbits are determined by countable internal resources.
Only the aperiodic corner ($\kappa \geq \omega_1$, $H^1 \neq 0$) **extends the base with genuinely new entities and facts** — the only corner that is genuinely *generative* rather than recombinative.

Informational-spacetime is that base-extension.
It is not assembled from prior cells by recombination (finite), nor by joining all evidence per cell (Scott-continuous), nor by growing the predicate vocabulary alone (transfinite).
It introduces new entities.
That is exactly the $\kappa \geq \omega_1$ condition.
Cosmogenerating could not live anywhere but here.

## Relation to the nuclei triad

Cosmogenerating is a fourth generator morphism on the category, sibling to the residuation nuclei [transferring](../transferring/transferring.md) (forward-facing $\Delta_t$), [saturating](../saturating/saturating.md) (backward-facing $\sigma$), and [commutating](../commutating/commutating.md) (their commuting square).
The triad generates *residuation* inside every regime; cosmogenerating does what the triad cannot — it extends the base.
The triad disciplines what is already articulated; cosmogenerating articulates more world.

## The world is generated, not corresponded to

`articulating ⊢ the real world` is a *generation*, not a map onto a pre-existing world.
There is no formal side and empirical side to bridge: the descent does not stand beside an external cosmos and admit that it matches — it **is** the cosmos, generated.
So there is no empirical-correspondence to admit and no `sorry` at a boundary; such a node would be a correspondence smuggled back in as a thing in the theory, and the theory has no room for it.

What that admission used to gesture at is just **the field measuring itself from within**.
Measurement is [observing](library/informational-spacetime/library/condensing/library/quantum-spacetime/library/collapsing/library/classical-spacetime/library/observing/observing.md) (`collapsing` performed from inside, by an observer who is itself a cell of the field), and the study built on it — [cosmology](library/informational-spacetime/library/condensing/library/quantum-spacetime/library/collapsing/library/classical-spacetime/library/cosmology/cosmology.md), its sharpest case the `hubble-tension` — is ordinary internal content of the **classical** (scott) rung.
A disagreement between two measurements is an internal $H^1$ in the study, never a mismatch between the theory and an outside.
