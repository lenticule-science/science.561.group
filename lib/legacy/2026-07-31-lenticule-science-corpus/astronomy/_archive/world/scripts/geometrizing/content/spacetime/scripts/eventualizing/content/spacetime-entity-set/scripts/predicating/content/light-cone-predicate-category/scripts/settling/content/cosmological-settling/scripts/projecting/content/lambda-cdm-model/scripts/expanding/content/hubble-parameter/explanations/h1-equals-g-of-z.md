# The g(z) = ‖H¹‖ identification

> **Reframe (merge):** "cosmos"/"cosmos-cell" = the **classical-spacetime** field and its cells; cosmic-scott = classical (golden φ).
> The H¹-axis disambiguation below holds unchanged.

The predecessor cosmology papers center a scalar, the **residuated sufficiency gap**

$$
g(z) = 1 - \frac{V\big(\mathrm{Together}(E_{\text{early}}(z), E_{\text{late}}(z))\big)}
                 {V\big(\mathrm{Either}(E_{\text{early}}(z), E_{\text{late}}(z))\big)}.
$$

This branch claims $g(z)$ is the **value of an $H^1$ class**.
The claim has two readings, on the two strictly-commuting axes of the bigraded diamond (GENEALOGY §1, §5).
Keeping them apart is the whole point of the post-2026-05-29 disambiguation — collapsing them is the old error.

## Static reading — fibration axis, H¹_top

Two pipelines thermalize the *same* cosmos-cell (e.g. $H(z)$) at *one* epoch and fail to glue.
The obstruction to gluing local thermalizings into a global section is a degree-1 class on the **predication fibration**, $H^1_{\text{top}}$.
This is what [`hubble-tension`](../../library/hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) carries.
The overlap-ratio formula above is its value: $g=0 \iff$ the two thermalizings glue (the Čech 1-cocycle is a coboundary) $\iff$ residuated sufficiency holds.

## Dynamic reading — closure/temporal axis, H¹_t

One recognition propagated across redshift via [`flow`](../../library/flow/flow.md) fails to glue with the recognition obtained at the later epoch: $g(z, \Delta z)$.
The obstruction to the closure orbit reaching a fixed point ($\mathrm{Close}\circ\mathrm{Flow}(x)=x$) is $H^1_t$.
Flow-stability is $H^1_t = 0$.

On scope ($H^1=0$, the residuated/Scott-continuous corners) the two readings coincide — the diamond's 2-cells are trivial.
Off scope ($H^1 \neq 0$, where the tension actually lives) they diverge: the static class can be nonzero while the dynamic one closes, and vice versa.
