# Is quantum gravity a non-classical predication fiber?

The [cosmological settling](../objects/cosmological-settling.md) at its founding uses a value bilattice $\mathbf{L}$ that is implicitly classical: a single bilattice into which every predicate's evidence is recorded.
But the parent [predication categories](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) program also studies non-classical [predication fiber algebras](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md): bilattices with non-distributive joins, bilattices over non-Boolean truth-spaces, [continuous-valued settlings](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/continuous-valued-settling.md) over residuated lattices.

**Question:** Is the failure of general relativity at the Planck scale — the fact that the universe at $t \lesssim 10^{-43}$ s and $\ell \lesssim 10^{-35}$ m is not adequately described by classical [spacetime](../../../terms/spacetime.md) — diagnosable in the predicated framing as a failure of the cosmological settling's bilattice to remain classical at small scales?

Concretely: does the predication fiber algebra at the Planck scale require a *non-distributive* bilattice, where joining two values does not yield a single resultant value but a quantum superposition of values?
And if so, does this match — formally, perhaps via [Heyting structure](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/) on the bilattice — the quantum-mechanical structure that classical general relativity refuses to incorporate?

## Why this might be productive

Two suggestive bridges:

1. **Bilattice negation as time reversal.** The [bilattice negation](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/negation.md) generator swaps positive and negative evidence axes.
  In quantum-mechanical contexts, this resembles complex conjugation acting on amplitudes.
  A bilattice whose negation is realized via a $\mathbb{C}$-valued amplitude algebra rather than a $\{T, F\}$-valued truth algebra would naturally accommodate quantum interference.
2. **Non-globally-hyperbolic spacetimes need non-classical fibers.** The [light-cone predicate category](../objects/light-cone-predicate-category.md) on a generic curved spacetime has [Beck-Chevalley](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/beck-chevalley-commutation-on-predication-bilattices.md) failures whose categorical fingerprint is a non-trivial cohomology of the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bitopological-settling-site.md).
  The Planck-scale quantum-gravity regime is precisely where non-global-hyperbolicity is expected (foam structure, virtual black holes); the same cohomological obstructions might be the classical-bilattice's signal that it cannot model the regime.

## Why this might be unproductive

The match between bilattice non-distributivity and quantum amplitude structure is suggestive but unworked.
Quantum mechanics has a very specific structure — Hilbert space, unitarity, Born rule — and there is no guarantee that non-classical bilattices furnish the right one.
Worst case: the predicated framing turns out to be Boolean-classical at every scale, and quantum gravity requires a different formal extension of the parent theory.

## Status

Open.
The question is genuinely open and is the predicated reformulation of "is there a quantum theory of gravity?"
The reformulation does not claim to answer it but does identify a place to look: the bilattice structure of the cosmological settling at the Planck scale.
