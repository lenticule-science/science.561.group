**The weighted-tropical reading** develops one resolution of the [hubble-tension](../../../hubble-tension.md): that $H_0$ is the **weighted tropical eigenvalue** of the cosmic-scott thermalization's Fibonacci skeleton, and the tension is the gap between that eigenvalue's two dual readings.
Its load-bearing identification is "$H_0$ = a weighted eigenvalue"; the calibration that carries it to physical units lives at [`scale-factor-calibration`](hacking/library/scale-factor-calibration/scale-factor-calibration.md).

It is grounded in the predication-categories corpus's `verified` [continuous-V step-n benchmark](../../../../../../../../../../docs/dev/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md) (via the n-bonacci grounding note): cosmic-scott = λ_2 = the Fibonacci skeleton, whose growth is the golden φ only at uniform weights.

## The objects of this reading (`library/`)

- [`cosmic-scott-skeleton`](library/cosmic-scott-skeleton/cosmic-scott-skeleton.md) — the object $H_0$ is read *on*: the Fibonacci (step-2) substitution skeleton of the cosmic-scott thermalization, carrying per-letter weights $w_a, w_b$.
  Bare φ is its uniform-weight shadow.
- [`duality-gap`](library/duality-gap/duality-gap.md) — the object that **is** the Hubble tension: the gap $\lambda_w^{\max} - \lambda_w^{\min} = |w_a - w_b|/2$ between the skeleton eigenvalue's two duals.
  Vanishes iff the thermalizing has converged.
- [`conflating`](library/conflating/conflating.md) — the morphism that forces the two pipelines to be the two duals: $\lambda_w^{\min}(w) = -\lambda_w^{\max}(-w)$, i.e. min-plus is the knowledge-axis (De Morgan) conflate of max-plus.
- [`dequantizing`](library/dequantizing/dequantizing.md) — the morphism relating the additive (tropical) and multiplicative (real) readings: Maslov dequantization, $\lambda_{\text{trop}} = \ln \lambda_R$.
  Fixes the units: $H$ is an e-fold rate, so the tension is fundamentally additive.

## The residual (`hacking/`)

- [`scale-factor-calibration`](hacking/library/scale-factor-calibration/scale-factor-calibration.md) — the map from abstract skeleton-letter weights to physical scale-factor e-folds.
  The naive "weights = the skeleton's own Perron eigenvector" is *ruled out* (recorded there).
  This is the remaining content of the "$H_0 = \lambda_w$" gap.

## Relation to the cell

This reading is the *quantitative refinement* of the cell's [stage-offset](../../../hubble-tension.md): the stage-offset says "two stages of one golden spiral"; this reading says "the two stages carry unequal weights, and the eigenvalue gap is the tension."
