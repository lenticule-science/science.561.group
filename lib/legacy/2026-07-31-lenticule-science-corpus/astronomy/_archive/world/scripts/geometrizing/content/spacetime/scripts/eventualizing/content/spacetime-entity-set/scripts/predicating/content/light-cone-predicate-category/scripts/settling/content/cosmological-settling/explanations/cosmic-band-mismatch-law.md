- **late-time** (local distance ladder, present stage τ_now) → later cosmic-scott, λ = φ;

**Prediction:** the Hubble tension is the *expected* offset between two stage-readings of the **one** golden-φ spiral of an unconverged (κ=ω) thermalization.
