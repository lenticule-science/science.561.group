# Does baryogenesis correspond to truth-axis symmetry breaking?

The Sakharov conditions for [baryogenesis](../../theories/evolution/terms/baryogenesis.md) — baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, departure from thermal equilibrium — are conditions on the closure-rule system of the early universe.
Each Sakharov condition has a candidate predicated translation:

- **Baryon number violation.** A closure rule whose output cell has different baryon-number content than the input cells.
  In bilattice terms, the rule changes the *truth-axis projection* of the baryon-number predicate's value.
- **C and CP violation.** The closure-rule system is asymmetric under the [bilattice negation](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/negation.md) (which swaps positive and negative evidence axes — reversing matter and antimatter labels).
  A symmetric closure-rule system would have every matter-creating rule matched by an equally fast antimatter-creating one.
- **Departure from thermal equilibrium.** A closure-rule firing whose forward and reverse rates differ — not a [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md) violation (forward rates are still upward in $\leq_k$), but an *imbalance* in which firing direction dominates the bilattice value's truth-axis evolution.

**Question:** Is the matter-antimatter asymmetry of the universe a symmetry breaking on the *truth axis* of the value bilattice — analogous to the [dark-sector-fiber](../objects/dark-sector-fiber.md)'s pattern of evidence-axis asymmetry, but on a different bilattice axis?

If the answer is yes, then baryogenesis would be the predicated content of a structural feature: the cosmological settling at very early cosmic time underwent a transition from a bilattice-negation-symmetric phase to a negation-asymmetric phase.
The mechanism would be analogous to a Higgs-style phase transition: the "vacuum" of the closure-rule system had a degenerate symmetric configuration that broke into a non-symmetric ground state, and the universe got stuck on the matter side of the broken symmetry.

## Why this might be productive

The bilattice has two independent axes — the truth axis and the knowledge axis.
Standard physics treats matter-antimatter asymmetry as a feature of the standard model's CP-violating sector, with no obvious connection to "asymmetry of evidence."
The predicated framing makes a structural prediction: if the matter-antimatter asymmetry corresponds to a truth-axis breaking, then the *knowledge* axis remains symmetric — i.e., positive evidence and negative evidence still accumulate at equal rates.
This is testable in principle by examining whether the closure-rule system has a residual knowledge-axis symmetry even where it has lost truth-axis symmetry.

## Why this might be empty

The two-axis bilattice structure may simply not have anything to do with matter-antimatter asymmetry.
The Sakharov conditions are usually phrased in field-theoretic terms (C, P, T discrete symmetries on quantum fields), and the bilattice axes are epistemic constructs.
The translation may turn out to be a coincidence of structural-algebraic vocabulary that does not survive contact with the actual physics.

## Status

Open.
Distinguishes itself from the [dark-energy-as-closure-rate](../theorems/dark-energy-as-closure-rate.md) conjecture in being framing-only — the question proposes a translation but does not yet propose a derivation.
Productive answer would be either a derivation of the observed baryon asymmetry from a definite truth-axis breaking, or a clean argument that the bilattice axes are not the right structure to capture C/CP-violation.
