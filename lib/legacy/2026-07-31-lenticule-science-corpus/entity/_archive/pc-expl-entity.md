An element of an [entity set](../../../../code/structures/entity-set.md), denoted $e$. Entities are the individuals or nodes that [predicates](predicate.md) discuss.

An entity may occupy the [subject](subject.md) or [object](object.md) position of a [predication cell](predication-cell.md).
