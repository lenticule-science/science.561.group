# Algebra

## What this node is
Algebra is generated by **predicate**, the second step in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. To predicate is to assert that an operation holds of its arguments and obeys laws; an algebraic structure is exactly a set whose elements carry such a law-bearing operation. A monoid is a one-object category — its elements are morphisms, its operation composition — and a group is a monoid in which every morphism is invertible. Each structure here is a predication category: composition is the predicate, and associativity, identity, and distributivity are the laws the predicate must satisfy.

## What it generates / contains
- **linear-algebra** — vector spaces, bases, linear maps, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues; algebra over a field made geometric.

The node's own body (collected in `_archive`) develops the algebraic hierarchy and its laws:
- The chain magma -> semigroup -> monoid -> group, each step adding one axiom (associativity, identity, inverses).
- Two-operation structures: rings, integral domains, fields, ideals, polynomials.
- Order-flavored algebra: lattices, boolean and De Morgan algebras, residuated lattices, bilattices, twist products.
- Structure-preserving maps: homomorphisms, group and ring homomorphisms, involutions.
- The defining properties: associativity, commutativity, distributivity, closure, identity elements, idempotence, congruence, cosets, quotients.
