🌊 Unveiling the Ocean's Hidden Climate Threat 🚨

Scientists [have uncovered](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-023-01333-w) a startling truth beneath our ocean floors. [[marine methane hydrate|Marine methane hydrate]], once thought safely entombed in icy depths, is now known to be vulnerable even in [[deep ocean|deep waters]]. As bottom-water temperatures rise, this substance will [[melting|melt]] and release [[methane]]—a [[greenhouse gas]] with a [[global warming]] potential that dwarfs [[carbon dioxide|CO2]].

Here's what we've learned:

1. Marine methane hydrate isn't just an issue at shallow [[continental margin|continental margins]]; it threatens from the deep.
2. [[climate weirding|Climate weirding]] is setting off a [[chain reaction]] that could liberate vast amounts of this potent gas.
3. The risk extends far beyond previous estimates! Deep-sea reserves are migrating and venting into our oceans.

This revelation isn't just alarming—it's a call to action for [[degrowing|degrowth]] and [[decolonizing|decolonization]]. We must dismantle [[kyriarchism|systems of exploitation]] and rethink our relationship with Earth’s resources.

🛑 Push back against policies fueling [[extractivism|unsustainable resource extraction]] 
♻️ Adopt lifestyles centered around [[sustainability]] and respect for natural limits
🗳 Support leaders who prioritize [[climate justice]] and [[systemic change]] over short-term gains
