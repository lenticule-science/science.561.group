## Full citation

A. Carboni, R. F. C. Walters. "Cartesian Bicategories I." *Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra* 49, 11–32, 1987.

## Status

Not directly accessed; cited for the standard categorical statement of the modular law as one of the defining axioms of a cartesian bicategory of relations.

## Annotation

Introduces cartesian bicategories of relations, axiomatizing the structural properties that $\mathbf{Rel}$ enjoys: a symmetric monoidal bicategory with diagonals and codiagonals in which every object carries a canonical commutative comonoid structure, and the composition–intersection interaction is governed by the modular law. The modular law in this setting is the relational form of Frobenius reciprocity for conjunction.

Cited in the [Frobenius-as-modular-law remark](prose/remarks/frobenius-as-modular-law.md) as one of the two standard references — alongside [@freyd-scedrov1990] — for the allegorical/relational lineage in which Frobenius for $\wedge$ is stated as a separate modular axiom rather than bundled into the conjunction's integrality.
