# Equalization locus

For two operational endomaps $F,G : X \to X$ on a common class $X$ of predication valuations or settlings, the **equalization locus** is

$$\mathrm{Eq}(F,G) := \{x \in X \mid F(x) = G(x)\}.$$

When $X$ is organized as a category with enough subobjects, this is the set-level carrier of the ordinary categorical equalizer. When $X$ is merely the preorder category $\mathbf{PS}$, the categorical equalizer of parallel arrows is trivial; the equalization locus keeps the computational content by moving the question to operational endomaps rather than hom-set elements.

The associated defect complement is computed by the [commutation defect](../morphisms/commutation-defect.md) or by paired [evidence residuals](../morphisms/evidence-residual.md) when the two outputs are comparable in $\leq_k$.
