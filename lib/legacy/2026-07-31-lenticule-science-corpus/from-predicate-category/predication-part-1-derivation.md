# Derivation

This part develops the central object — the *predication settling* — and the structural apparatus around it. The derivation runs through seven chapters whose anchors form a single chain in the dependency graph: each chapter's central object is built from the previous chapter's results.

The chain is:

1. **Predication structure**: a [predicate category](../../objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ over an entity set $E$, with [predication fiber algebras](../../objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_q = \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E}$ assembled by the [predication fiber functor](../../theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md). Anchored on the fiber algebra, closed by the [closure operator](../../morphisms/closure-operator.md).

2. **Hyperdoctrine layer**: the 2-categorical [predication fibration](../../objects/predication-fibration.md), refined to a [predication hyperdoctrine](../../objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) with quantifier adjoints, Frobenius reciprocity, Beck–Chevalley, and comprehension. Closed by the [closure 2-monad](../../morphisms/closure-2-monad.md).

3. **Settling dynamics**: the [predication settling](../../objects/predication-settling.md) — a transfinite settling of valuations driven by closure and increment, classified by the [four-regime dichotomy](../../theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md). Closed by the [settling endofunctor](../../morphisms/settling-endofunctor.md)'s orbit LFP.

4. **Priestley duality**: the entire structure dualizes — fiberwise via [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../../theorems/jung-rivieccio-duality.md), globally via [Priestley dualizability](../../theorems/priestley-dualizability.md). The [dual predication hyperdoctrine](../../objects/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine.md) is the natural site for cohomology. Closed by the [dualized closure 2-comonad](../../morphisms/dualized-closure-2-comonad.md).

5. **Cohomology**: the [bitopological settling site](../../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md), the [settling-associated sheaf](../../objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md), and [settling cohomology](../../objects/settling-cohomology.md) as derived global sections. Vanishing above degree 1, regime-by-regime structure, [aperiodic-regime classification](../../theorems/aperiodic-regime-classification.md) by an $H^1$-class.

6. **Moduli**: the [settling fiber groupoid](../../objects/settling-fiber-groupoid.md), the [coarse moduli](../../objects/coarse-settling-moduli.md), the [settling moduli stack theorem](../../theorems/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md) over the [parameter base category](../../objects/parameter-base-category.md). The classifying invariants of cohomology refine to a stack-level statement.

7. **Realizability**: the moduli classifying function $\mathcal{I}_B$ is injective on each regime stratum (Chapter 6); realizability characterizes its *image* — which cohomology classes are realized by some MLD-class. Per-regime theorems plus three structural axes (bigraded value-type, $\mathcal{C}$-naturality coupling, rule-language fragment) refine $\mathcal{I}_B$ from injection to bijection onto an explicit image, completing the moduli classification.

Each chapter introduces and develops the next link of the chain. By the end of the Derivation, the central objects and the chief structural results connecting them are in place. The Study-and-use part then turns to topics built on this apparatus.
