## Annotation

Accessible modern summary of tripos theory. Tripos = "**T**opos **R**epresenting **I**ndexed **P**artially **O**rdered **S**et" — Pitts's mnemonic. The construction unifies Higgs's $\Omega$-valued sets and Hyland's realizability toposes as instances of a single tripos-to-topos construction.

**§4** identifies a comprehension property weaker than Lawvere's that still yields a topos via tripos-to-topos. Useful for the hyperdoctrinal layer where the predication hyperdoctrine's relationship to tripos theory is positioned.

Why our predication hyperdoctrine is **not** a tripos: bilattice-valued rather than Heyting-valued fibers; genuine 2-categorical fiber structure; no postulated generic predicate. The hyperdoctrinal layer makes the disambiguation explicit.
