## Status

Not directly accessed; cited for the systematic textbook treatment of N4-lattices, twist-product representation, and duality with bi-intuitionistic Kripke frames (Ch. 8).

## Annotation

Systematic monograph on Nelson-type paraconsistent logics. Chapter 8 develops the algebraic and duality theory of N4-lattices: the twist-product representation due to [@vakarelov1977], the bi-intuitionistic Kripke duality, and the relationship between Nelson's strong negation and the bilattice-style negation that the predication hyperdoctrine inherits.

Cited in [equality is derived, not postulated](prose/teaching/explanations/equality-derived-not-postulated.md) as the standard textbook reference for the propositional Nelson lattices that underlie the program's value algebra, alongside the original [@vakarelov1977].
