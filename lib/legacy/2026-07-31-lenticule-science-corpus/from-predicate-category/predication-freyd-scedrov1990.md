## Full citation

P. J. Freyd, A. Scedrov. *Categories, Allegories*. North-Holland Mathematical Library 39, North-Holland, 1990.

## Status

Not directly accessed; cited for the allegorical formulation of the modular law and for the standard form of the relational axioms governing Frobenius reciprocity in a non-integral setting.

## Annotation

The canonical reference on allegories — categorical structures abstracting the intersection-and-composition behavior of relations. Allegories carry intersection $\cap$ as a separate operation from composition $;$ together with a converse $(-)^\circ$, with the *modular law* $\phi \cap (R\,;\,S) \subseteq R\,;\,(R^\circ\!\cap\!\phi\,;\,S)$ as an axiom. The modular law is the relational form of Frobenius reciprocity for conjunction.

Cited in the [Frobenius-as-modular-law remark](prose/remarks/frobenius-as-modular-law.md) for the standard placement of the bilattice's $\wedge_k/\wedge_t$-Frobenius identities inside the allegorical lineage rather than the integral-hyperdoctrine lineage.
