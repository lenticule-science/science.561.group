## Annotation

The standard textbook reference for term rewriting. The comparison-operations packet uses its abstract-reduction-system vocabulary: a one-step rewrite relation $\to$, its reflexive-transitive closure $\to^*$, confluence as the diamond-up-to-closure property, local confluence as one-step divergence joinability, and normal forms as terminal points of reduction.

The PredCat confluence results are not imported wholesale. They specialize the abstract notion to the [predication settling category](../objects/predication-settling-category.md), where knowledge-order joins give a canonical common successor for monotone settling steps.
