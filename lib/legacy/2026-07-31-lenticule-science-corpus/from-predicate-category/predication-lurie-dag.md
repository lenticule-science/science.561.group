## Annotation

Comprehensive treatment of spectral / derived algebraic geometry: spectral schemes, derived stacks, derived deformation theory, formal moduli problems. Builds on [@lurie2009] (Higher Topos Theory) and [@lurie-ha] (Higher Algebra).

**Relevance to the corpus.** The future-direction narrative on $(\infty, 1)$-extensions cites this as the framework where predication categories' deformation theory would be naturally generalized:

- The corpus's first-order Ext-classification of [SKH deformations](../theorems/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md) becomes a derived-tangent-complex statement.
- The corpus's [vanishing-above-degree-1 theorem](../theorems/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) becomes a derived-vanishing assertion (tangent complex concentrated in degree 1).
- The settling moduli stack would lift to a derived stack in this sense, with formal completions giving derived deformation theory.

This is a deep prerequisite, not a current corpus result.
