## Annotation

The conference paper that established the Kripke semantics later expanded in Rivieccio–Jung–Jansana 2017. Sets up:

- Algebraic semantics + Priestley duality on a modal expansion of Arieli–Avron's bilattice logic (itself based on Belnap's four-valued logic).
- Soundness and completeness results for the Kripke semantics derived via duality.

Useful for our program as the historical record showing the algebraic-to-Kripke move; for current technical content prefer [@rivieccio-et-al2017] which has the full development with the twist translation, decidability proof, and finite model property.
