## Annotation

The foundational paper analyzing how Ricci flow develops singularities. Classifies singularity types (Type I, Type II, Type III) by the rate of curvature blowup, and identifies the "model singularities" that finite-time singularities approach under rescaling. The classification is the structural template predication categories' [closure-flow singularity-formation question](questions/closure-flow-singularity-formation.md) parallels in the discrete-ordinal-time / bilattice-valued setting.

The corpus's analog: non-Scott-continuous firings as singularities of the closure flow, with truth-only / falsity-only / $\top$ as the model-singularity classification. Hamilton's blowup analysis (rescaled limit near a singular time) corresponds to the "local structure of the closure flow at a NSC firing" — the substantive sub-question of the singularity-formation question.
