## Annotation

Street's foundational paper on fibrations in a 2-category, identifying the colax-idempotent 2-monad whose pseudo-algebras are cloven Grothendieck fibrations. With [@gray1966], gives the [Gray–Street fibration 2-monad](../theorems/gray-street-fibration-monad.md) $\mathbb{M}$ used to frame the [predication fibration](../objects/predication-fibration.md). For the program, the technical 2-categorical backbone of the hyperdoctrine layer.
