# Predicate category

A **predicate category** is a small category, denoted $\mathcal{C}$, whose objects are [predicates](../concepts/predicate.md) and whose morphisms are generated by four canonical classes — [predicate specialization](../morphisms/predicate-specialization.md), [predicate converse](../morphisms/predicate-converse.md), [predicate subject-projection](../morphisms/predicate-subject-projection.md), and [predicate object-projection](../morphisms/predicate-object-projection.md). Each class is induced by a specific manipulation of the entity-pair index $E \times E$ over the [entity set](entity-set.md) $E$, and each induces a [bilattice homomorphism](../morphisms/bilattice-homomorphism.md) between the [predication fiber algebras](predication-fiber-algebra.md) at the predicates it relates. Under a $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$-equivariance hypothesis with $|E| \geq 3$, the four classes exhaust the natural bilattice homomorphisms between fiber algebras — see [predicate-generator exhaustiveness](/predication-categories/library/predication-cell-complex-site/content/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md).

The object set $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is the [predicate set](predicate-set.md) $\Pi$. Specific theorems may require additional structure on $\mathcal{C}$:

- **Finite limits / pullbacks.** Required for [Beck–Chevalley commutation](../theorems/beck-chevalley-commutation-on-predication-bilattices.md) on the fiber algebras.
- **$\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$** (cohomological dimension as a poset / Alexandrov-topology category). Required for [settling-cohomology vanishes above degree 1](../theorems/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) and all downstream theorems that use $H^{\geq 2} = 0$ or $\mathrm{Ext}^{\geq 2} = 0$ — including [SKH deformation Ext-classification](../theorems/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md), [meta-moduli deformation theory](../theorems/meta-moduli-deformation-theory.md), and the d-frame-valued analogs. The hypothesis is automatic when $\mathcal{C}$ is finite (Alexandrov-finite posets have cohomological dimension equal to the order-complex dimension), when $\mathcal{C}$ is a directed poset ($\mathrm{cd} = 0$), and in all explicit corpus instances built from the four predicate generators on finite predicate sets. Higher-cd predicate categories — e.g., higher-arity predicates with non-trivial composition structure, or modal-operator-extended predicate categories — would require re-verification of the downstream vanishing.
