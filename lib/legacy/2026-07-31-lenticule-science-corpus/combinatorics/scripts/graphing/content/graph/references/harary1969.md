# Harary 1969 — Graph Theory

The foundational mid-20th-century textbook that brought graph theory to a wide mathematical audience. Now historical rather than current, but cited for terminological priority and for several constructions (notably the [line graph](../morphisms/line-graph.md), Ch. 8) that received their classical treatment here.

## Why this is cited

Harary is the corpus's reference for *historical* terminology and for results whose canonical exposition predates the modern textbooks. The directory's [line-graph](../morphisms/line-graph.md) note follows Harary's Ch. 8 convention. Cited when terminology variation across the literature matters, since Harary's choices set many of the standards [Diestel 2017](diestel2017.md) and [Bondy–Murty 2008](bondy-murty2008.md) inherited.

## Notable claims

- Ch. 1 — graphs, subgraphs, automorphisms.
- Ch. 4 — trees and forests.
- Ch. 8 — line graphs, total graphs, related constructions.
- Ch. 11 — planarity, Euler's formula.

## Status

Out of print but widely available. Superseded for current research by the modern textbooks; remains the canonical reference for several specific constructions.
