# graphing

Generates [graph](content/graph/graph.md) from [`combinatorics`](../../combinatorics.md): predication restricted to a finite carrier, where an edge is a predicate asserted of a pair of vertices. The graph is the base discrete relational structure — the floor every order (a transitive acyclic digraph) and small category (a graph closed under composition) stands on.
