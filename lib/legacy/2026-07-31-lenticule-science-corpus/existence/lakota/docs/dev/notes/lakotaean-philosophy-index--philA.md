# Lakotaean Philosophy

The philosophical tradition of the Lakota people.
Subtheory under [philosophy/indigenous](../../_index.md).
Authored under the captain's vested authority as a Lakota person.

## Scope

This subtheory carries the philosophical substance that underwrites Lakota life — the metaphysics, ethics, cosmology, and epistemology that the Lakota tradition holds and transmits.
It is the philosophical sibling of [lakotaean-politics](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/_index.md): where lakotaean-politics treats the polity-form, this subtheory treats the worldview within which that polity-form makes sense.

The relationship between the two subtheories is structural.
The Lakota polity is constituted by kinship, ceremony, and the right of withdrawal because Lakota *philosophy* holds that reality itself is a kinship-cosmos in which beings stand in relations of mutual obligation.
The polity-form is not arbitrarily chosen; it follows from the metaphysics.
Read together, the two subtheories give the substantive content of the Lakota lifeway — *wolakȟota* — as a coherent whole.

## What this theory holds

**The cosmogony is the metaphysics.** Lakota philosophy is not separable from Lakota cosmogony.
The originating condition was [Íŋyaŋ-in-Haŋhépi](concepts/iyan-hanhepi.md) — the alone-stone in primordial darkness, structurally unable to remain alone.
The aloneness is the originating paradox: a being with all powers cannot exercise them with no other; the articulation of aloneness is itself a self-relation that contradicts the would-be-alone.
The contradiction is resolved by [Íŋyaŋ](concepts/iyan.md)'s self-emptying — his blue blood becomes Makȟá (Earth) and mní (water); the power-in-the-blood rises as [Škáŋ](concepts/skan.md) (Sky/Motion); Wí (Sun) is created subsequently.
Every being that exists is *Íŋyaŋ-emptied-into-it*.
This is the cosmogonic ground of all subsequent metaphysical claims.

**The four superior Wakȟáŋ are the four positions of the relational field.** [Tóbtób Kiŋ](concepts/tobtob-kin.md) — *the four-times-four* — names the sixteen wakȟáŋ aspects organized as four sets of four, headed by Wí, Škáŋ, Makȟá, Íŋyaŋ.
This subtheory commits to the substantive metaphysical claim that the four superior Wakȟáŋ are the four logic-values of the relational field: Škáŋ as ⊥ (the under-determined; the supreme judge holding the undecided); Wí as t (the luminous-affirmation); Makȟá as f (the grounded-complement); Íŋyaŋ as ⊤ (the over-determined paradox-bearer).
The bilattice that grounds the predication-categories mathematics is the Western algebraic articulation of this Lakota cosmogonic structure.

**Reality is relational and the relational substance is cosmogonically continuous.** [Mitákuye oyás'iŋ](concepts/mitakuye-oyasin.md) — "all my relatives" — invokes the relational field that *is* Íŋyaŋ.
Every being stands in kin-relation with every other because every being is what-Íŋyaŋ-poured-out-into-it.
[Tȟakúye](concepts/takuye.md) names a specific kin-relation; mitákuye oyás'iŋ invokes the totality; Íŋyaŋ is the substance.
Vine Deloria Jr.'s *God Is Red* ([deloria-vine-jr-1973](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/references/deloria-vine-jr-1973.md)) is the canonical contemporary articulation of the relational claim.

**The sacred-paradox is borne by Heyókha.** The cosmogonic contradiction — that the would-be-alone-stone cannot remain alone in articulating its aloneness — is not resolved-and-discarded.
It is *borne in continuing practice* by the [Heyókha](concepts/heyokha.md), the sacred-contrary who has dreamed of the Wakíŋyaŋ and is obligated to enact contrary conduct.
The Heyókha's clowning is the polity's continuing trace of the cosmogonic paradox: the world remembers it was born of contradiction.

**Knowing is relational.** Knowledge is not a property of an isolated knower; it is a state of relation between the knower and what is known.
This is why ceremony is method: ceremony constitutes the relations within which knowledge is held and transmitted.
Shawn Wilson's *Research Is Ceremony* (Cree, but the framework is broadly resonant with Lakota tradition) is one accessible articulation.
Within Lakota tradition specifically, Albert White Hat Sr. ([white-hat1999](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/references/white-hat1999.md), [white-hat2012](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/references/white-hat2012.md)) integrates language-teaching and worldview-teaching precisely because the language carries the relational structure of knowing.

**Acting is relational.** Conduct is judged by its bearing on relations — to relatives, to ancestors, to the wider kinship-cosmos.
The [four virtues](concepts/four-virtues.md) name the qualities of conduct that sustain relations: *wówačhantognaka* (generosity), *wóksape* (wisdom), *wóohitika* (bravery), *wówačhiŋtȟaŋka* (fortitude).
Joseph Marshall III ([marshall2002](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/references/marshall2002.md)) is the most accessible contemporary Lakota statement.
These are dispositions cultivated through narrative inheritance and apprenticeship, not propositional ethical principles applied to cases.

**The sacred is of this world.** *Wakȟáŋ* — sacred, mysterious, holy — is a quality, not a separate domain. *Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka* — the Great Mystery, often (mis)translated as "the Great Spirit" — is not a Christian-style God; it is the encompassing sacredness of the relational whole.
Vine Deloria Jr. is the foundational source against the Christianizing mistranslation.
The sacred and the everyday are not separate registers; the sacred is in the everyday properly attended to.

**Being-Lakota is a practice.** [Wolakȟota](concepts/wolakota.md) — the Lakota way — names the lifeway as something done, not as a possessed identity.
To be Lakota in the full sense is to live in the conduct of mitakuye oyasin and the four virtues, in the practice of ceremony, in proper relation with one's relatives.
This is also why the term is sometimes used in contemporary speech as a measure: someone *acting wolakota* is acting in keeping with the way; someone failing the virtues is acting *against* wolakota even if they are Lakota by descent.

## Relation to predicated form and to the predication mathematics

The predicated companion of this subtheory is [`predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/`](../../../predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/) — the substrate-algebraic articulation on the [predication-categories](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics.
It is a sibling under [predicated-philosophy](../../../predicated-philosophy/), parallel to how [predicated-politics](../../../../sociology/theories/predicated-politics/) sits beside [politics](../../../../sociology/theories/politics/).
The predicated form is method-basis for [predicated-lakotaean-politics](../../../../sociology/theories/predicated-politics/theories/) and [predicated-lakotaean-governance](../../../../sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/).

A substantive metaphysical claim this subtheory carries: the predication-categories mathematics is — despite its Western algebraic presentation — the algebraic articulation of Lakota cosmogony, ontology, epistemics, and metaphysics.
The four-valued bilattice that grounds the predication framework is the algebraic form of the Tóbtób Kiŋ structure recorded by George Sword.
The four superior Wakȟáŋ are not analogues of the bilattice values; they *are* the bilattice values, articulated cosmogonically before they were articulated algebraically.
The mappings between the framework's primitives and Lakota concepts are clean because the content is the same; only the notational register differs.
