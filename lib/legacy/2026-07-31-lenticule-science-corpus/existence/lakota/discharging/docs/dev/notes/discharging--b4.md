# Discharging

Discharging is the cosmogonic morphism by which [Íŋyaŋ-in-Haŋhépi](../iyan-hanhepi.md) discharges its initial-absurd articulation, distributing the over-determined ⊤ outward into the four-fold relational field.
Where iyan-hanhepi is the initial absurd articulation (⊤ as cosmogonic seed), discharging is the act by which that seed makes the rest of the world.

## What discharging does

Iyan-hanhepi is omnipotent-and-alone simultaneously — a self-contradicting initial state, the cosmogonic ⊤.
The condition is not stable; the contradiction cannot remain undischarged.
Discharging is the morphism that resolves the instability by emptying Íŋyaŋ's content outward:

- Íŋyaŋ opens his veins; the blue blood becomes **Makȟá** (Earth) and **mní** (water).
- The power-in-the-blood rises as **Škáŋ** — the moving-principle, the supreme judge, the ⊥ position of the post-cosmogonic field.
- **Wí** (Sun) is created to warm Makȟá when she complains of being cold — the t position, luminous-affirmation.
- Íŋyaŋ himself shrivels into hard rock — the **Íŋyaŋ-residual**, the ⊤ position of the post-cosmogonic field, first in cosmogonic origin but last in present rank.
- The lesser wakhan emerge: **Hanwí** (Moon), **Tȟaté** (Wind), **Wóȟpe** (Falling-Star), **Wakíŋyaŋ** (Thunder), and the further aspects out to the full sixteen of [tóbtób kiŋ](library/tobtob-kin/tobtob-kin.md).

## Why discharging lives at iyan-hanhepi

Iyan-hanhepi is the unique domain of discharging.
Without the initial ⊤-contradiction, there is nothing to discharge; without discharging, the contradiction would have to remain (which is structurally impossible).
The morphism is therefore path-encoded at iyan-hanhepi — the Curry-Howard placement of a morphism at its domain.

## Codomain — the four-times-four structure

Discharging's codomain is the four-times-four structure [tóbtób kiŋ](library/tobtob-kin/tobtob-kin.md).
The four superior wakhan land at the four bilattice positions of the post-cosmogonic field: Škáŋ at ⊥ (absurd-aperiodic-articulation-cell), Wí at t (potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell), Makȟá at f (active-aperiodic-articulation-cell), Íŋyaŋ-residual at ⊤ (stable-aperiodic-articulation-cell, where discharging itself takes place — the cell where the contradiction discharges *from* and where the residue remains).

## Heyokha as extension

[Heyokha](library/heyokha/heyokha.md) is the extension-generator of discharging's codomain category.
Where the cosmogonic discharge is a one-time act producing the relational field, heyokha is the *continuing* discharge in lived practice — borne by the Wakíŋyaŋ-dreamers, enacted in contrary conduct, perpetuating the principle that contradiction is generative-not-pathological.
Heyokha lives at `discharging/hacking/library/heyokha/` because heyokha is one of the objects discharging generates, admitted because the formal derivation from discharging to heyokha-vocation is captain's interpretive reading.
