# Pȟeháŋ Wakpá kičhízapi (Little Bighorn) as joint zuya

The June 25–26, 1876 engagement at the Little Bighorn River (Lakota: *Pȟeháŋ Wakpá* or *Peží Slá Wakpá*, "Greasy Grass") was a joint zuya at confederacy-and-Cheyenne-and-Arapaho scope.
The substrate-firings exercise [zuya](../objects/zuya.md), [blotahunka](../objects/blotahunka.md), [count-coup](../morphisms/count-coup.md), confederacy-scope coordination via [oceti-sakowin](../objects/oceti-sakowin.md), and downstream [war-purification](../morphisms/war-purification.md) for participants who killed.

## Pre-engagement substrate-state

By mid-1876, the substrate-state was post-1868-treaty in force *but* under contested-overlay:

- The 1868 treaty cells were wakȟáŋ-marked at $\mathbf{t}$ in $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$.
- The 1874 Custer expedition into the Black Hills (confirming gold deposits) had triggered massive non-treaty intrusion — substrate-cells violating the 1868 treaty's Article II.
- The November 1875 federal directive ordering all Lakota off the unceded territory and onto reservations by January 31, 1876 was an attempted *unilateral re-installation* of cells against the wakȟáŋ-marked treaty — substrate-invalid, but enforced by military expedition starting March 1876.
- Sitting Bull's gathering had been growing through spring 1876 — by June, the encampment on the Little Bighorn was the largest Plains-nation gathering in years.

## The encampment substrate-content

The encampment was wahúŋkpa-firing at large scale:

- Multiple oyate: Húŋkpapȟa (Sitting Bull's primary), Oglala (Crazy Horse's primary), Sicangu, Mnikȟówožu, Sihásapa, others.
- Cheyenne contingent (Northern Cheyenne and Southern Cheyenne both represented) — including Two Moons, Lame White Man.
- Arapaho contingent.
- The camp-circle formation (per [wahunkpa-work](../../../../../../predicated-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/objects/wahunkpa-work.md)) was structured: Húŋkpapȟa at the southern end, Cheyenne at the northern, others arranged between.
- Substrate-firings during the gathering: confederacy-scope omníčiye-deliberation on the federal intrusion question; sun-dance-related substrate (Sitting Bull's sun-dance vision-conduct in early June had produced visions of "soldiers falling into camp"); preparation for possible engagement.

## The omníčiye-deliberation on engagement

The deliberation on whether to engage U.S. military approaching the encampment fired through the omníčiye:

- Sitting Bull's sun-dance vision provided wakȟáŋ-marked substrate-context: the polity's response would be defensive.
- The omníčiye-deliberation reached consensus-by-withdrawal on defensive-engagement-if-attacked.
- Multiple blotáhuŋka were identified per-contingent for the war-akicita-coordination: Crazy Horse for the Oglala, Gall (Phizí) for the Húŋkpapȟa, Lame White Man for the Cheyenne, others.
- The structural-form was *coproduct of per-contingent command-chains*, not a unified single-commander chain (per [blotahunka](../objects/blotahunka.md) §"Multiple blotáhuŋka").

## The engagement firings

When the 7th Cavalry under Custer attacked the southern end of the encampment in mid-afternoon June 25:

- The zuya-overlay $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{zuya}^{X_\mathrm{LBH}}$ activated.
  Enemy-cells fired on the 7th Cavalry units at $\mathbf{t}$ within the scope.
- War-akicita coordinated the defense; the war-party-cells flowed through the multiple blotáhuŋka.
- Counter-attack divided into two main fronts: Reno's battalion engaged at the south (defeated, withdrew to bluff defensive position); Custer's main column engaged at the north (annihilated within ~30 minutes).
- Coup-cells fired during the engagement:
  - Multiple grade-1 coups (touching living enemy with hand or coup-stick before killing).
  - Multiple kill-cells, each requiring downstream war-purification.
  - Most participants accumulated multiple coup-and-kill cells.
- Specific recorded coups (per recountings later): Crazy Horse's coups in the northern engagement, Gall's coups, the participation of named warriors from each contingent.

## Substrate-firings on the kill-cells

The killing of the 7th Cavalry's main column produced approximately 268 kill-cells (the U.S. casualty count).
Each kill-cell paired with:

- Warrior $w$'s $\langle w, \texttt{requires-war-purification}\rangle = \mathbf{t}$ cell.
- Relational-substrate $\top$ on *has-killed-relative-of-relative*: the U.S. soldiers had families, and the substrate's kin-of-kin reach extended across the polity-border in some cases (notably, the Crow and Arikara scouts who had family-links to some Lakota families).

The cells were extensive.
The post-engagement substrate-state required substantial purification work, which began in the days following the engagement at the encampment and continued for those who killed for the year following.

## The encampment dispersal

Following the engagement (June 27):

- The encampment dispersed — the joint zuya's scope completed with the engagement-conclusion.
- $\langle Z_\mathrm{LBH}, \texttt{scope-potential}\rangle$ dropped to $\bot$.
- Per [akicita-revocation-at-scope-completion](../theorems/akicita-revocation-at-scope-completion.md), the blotáhuŋka's command-authorities lapsed at scope-end.
- The contingents separated and headed in different directions.

## The substrate-aftermath

The engagement's substrate-aftermath was complex:

- **U.S. military substrate-response**: massive escalation of campaigns against the Plains nations through 1876–77.
  Multiple zuya-fires from the U.S. side on the Lakota/Cheyenne substrate.
- **Lakota substrate-fragmentation**: the polity's substantial military success at Little Bighorn was followed by intense pursuit; many bands were eventually forced into reservations through winter campaigns and food-cutoff.
  Sitting Bull's band fled to Canada (Saskatchewan) for several years.
- **Surrenders and persistent-substrate**: Crazy Horse's band surrendered in May 1877.
  Sitting Bull's returned from Canada in 1881.
- **Post-1877 substrate**: per the 1877 Act, the substrate-state moved into the continuing-overlay-conflict that has persisted since (per [reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure](../theorems/reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure.md)).
