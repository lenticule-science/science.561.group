The ethnographic, ceremonial, legal, and social context surrounding Lakotaean aerophones.
Where the parent theory `lakotaean-aerophones` is a music theory — pitch, scale, mode, acoustics, ornamentation, phrase, ensemble — this sub-theory studies who plays what, in what setting, addressed to whom, and under what regulatory regime.

This separation is intentional.
The music has its own theoretical content, derivable on musical terms.
The culture has its own theoretical content, derivable on cultural terms.
Conflating the two — analyzing the music only through its ceremonial frame — is a category error.
