# Predicated Lakotaean theory survey — governance/work/tiospaye

This survey records a first pass at the mathematical and conceptual structure needed to enrich the governance and work theories for understanding the unfolding existence of a tiyóšpaye as it exists in its world, governs, works, repairs, withdraws, and re-settles.

The governing correction for this pass is:

> not "predicated-X-as-Lakota-Y" but "predicated-X-is-Lakota-Y."

That is not a stylistic correction.
It is the mathematical constraint of this leaf.
The Lakotaean system is not a metaphorical interpretation of a predication-categorical substrate.
The system is the substrate as instantiated in the Lakota polity-cell-system, with the Tóbtób Kiŋ / `FOUR` value domain, the Lakota predicate category, and the Wolakȟota closure-rule subsystem.
The survey therefore treats governance and work as slices of one predication-settling, not as external social-science domains translated into Lakota terms.

## 1. Mathematical substrate: the common cell-system

The basic mathematical object is the predication cell

```text
(s, p, o) ∈ E_lak × Π_lak × E_lak.
```

The source `polity-cell-system.md` fixes the Lakota polity-cell-system as

```text
(E_lak, Π_lak, C_lak, w_lak),
```

where:

- `E_lak` is the extended entity-set: persons, ancestors, four-leggeds, wingeds, standing-ones, rooted-ones, stones, places, wakȟáŋ-beings, ceremonial objects, offices, polity-units, and related entity-classes.
- `Π_lak` is the predicate-set: kin-relations, place-relations, conduct-relations, witness-relations, wakȟáŋ-relations, standing-relations, office-relations, withdrawal-relations, and covenant-relations.
- `C_lak` is the predicate category on `Π_lak`.
- `w_lak` marks cells and rules by wakȟáŋ-bearing quality.

The important point for governance/work theory is that this is not merely a classification schema.
It is a category-indexed bilattice-valued predication system.
A polity state is a valuation over cells, and the existence of a polity-unit, office, ceremony, kin-obligation, work-action, treaty, or withdrawal is expressed by how the corresponding cells settle under the knowledge order.

The predicate category file makes `C` small, with objects predicates and morphisms generated by four canonical classes: specialization, converse, subject-projection, and object-projection.
The Lakota leaf imports this directly: `C_lak` is generated by those same classes.
This means that Lakotaean governance/work should not invent extra reindexing operations when a governance or work concept feels socially complex.
It should ask how the concept lives through:

1. specialization of predicates,
2. converse of relations,
3. subject projection,
4. object projection,
5. and closure rules natural in the predicate variable.

This is the first protection against fake mathematization.
The math is not notation placed on top of terms.
The math controls what counts as a legitimate construction.

## 2. FOUR is Tóbtób Kiŋ, not an analogy

The governance index commits to the four-element bilattice `FOUR = {t, f, ⊥, ⊤}` as realized in the Lakota polity as the four superior Wakȟáŋ:

```text
t = Wí
f = Makȟá
⊥ = Škáŋ
⊤ = Íŋyaŋ
```

This is a decisive identity-claim for the leaf.
The algebraic values are not a generic many-valued logic later decorated with Lakota names.
The value domain of the Lakota polity-form is the Tóbtób Kiŋ articulated algebraically.

Consequences for future theory-writing:

- A governance decision at `⊥` is not merely "unknown"; it is the polity's not-yet-pronounced underdetermination in the Škáŋ position.
- A treaty breach or conflict at `⊤` is not an ordinary inconsistency to normalize away; it is a load-bearing overdetermination in the Íŋyaŋ position.
- Knowledge-order increase is not bureaucratic evidence accumulation; it is increased settledness of the polity-cell-system.
- Truth-order claims must not erase knowledge-order conflict.
  A cell can be both politically load-bearing and contested; `⊤` is not a defect to hide.

This matters for a tiyóšpaye because a tiyóšpaye is not a group whose facts are fully crisp.
It is an unfolding settlement: who is related, who owes what, who witnessed what, who withdrew from which engagement, which obligations have been discharged, which harms require repair, and which offices are recognized all occupy bilattice values that change through closure.

## 3. Closure and settling: existence as unfolding

`predication-settling-category.md` defines a category `PS(L, C, E, λ)` whose objects are predication settlings over fixed parameters and whose morphisms exist when one settling is pointwise and stepwise below another in the knowledge order.
This gives a direct model of unfolding existence: a polity does not merely have a static state; it moves through an ordered family of more-settled valuations.

For the Lakota leaf, the closure-rule subsystem is `S_wolak`, the Wolakȟota closure-rule system.
It fires on the polity-cell-system.
Its rule-classes include:

- kin-propagation,
- conduct-evaluation,
- kin-obligation,
- witness-bearing,
- office-installation,
- withdrawal,
- restorative repair.

The source says these rule-shapes are stated uniformly over the predicate-category, and so closure is natural in `C_lak`: reindexing by the predicate-category generators commutes with closure.
This gives the mathematical criterion for whether a Lakotaean governance/work operation has been properly stated: it must be a closure operation compatible with the predicate-category structure, not merely a social description with formulas attached.

A tiyóšpaye, then, should be understood as a dynamically maintained sub-settling or sub-cell-system of the full polity-cell-system.
Its existence unfolds through closure:

```text
kin-cells fire → obligation-cells fire → work-cells fire → standing-cells update
witness-cells fire → wakȟáŋ-marking propagates → ceremonial/political validity settles
deliberation-cells fire → withdrawal/restriction cells update → engaged decision settles
harm-cells fire → repair-required cells fire → restorative morphisms seek quiescence
```

The tiyóšpaye exists as these cells keep settling, not as a list of members plus rules.

## 4. Governance and work are slices of one substrate

The `predicated-lakotaean-politics` index states that governance and work are political activities and are not separable: how the people decide is how they work is how they govern themselves.
This should become a formal principle for future development.

Current placement is split by repository convention:

- `predicated-lakotaean-governance` is housed under the Western governance tree for substrate reasons.
- `predicated-lakotaean-work` lives as a sibling under the predicated Lakotaean politics tree.

But the Lakota-structural claim is that both share:

```text
(E_lak, Π_lak, C_lak, w_lak)
```

and both fire under

```text
S_wolak.
```

Therefore governance and work should not be developed as two ontologies later connected by analogies.
They should be developed as two subfibrations / slices / predicate-class regions of the same valuation.
Governance is the normative-substructure slice; work is the operational-substrate slice.
The same cell can have consequences in both.

Example:

```text
(a, kin-of, b) = t
```

is not just a family fact.
Under W3 it installs owes-virtue-to cells.
Those cells originate work.
Under W2, work/discharge updates standing-as-wolakȟota.
Under governance, standing-as-wolakȟota helps condition recognition, office, counsel, or trust.
The same kin-cell is thus already governance/work/politics.

## 5. Tiyóšpaye as a predicated polity-unit

The `tiyospaye.md` concept identifies the tiyóšpaye as the extended family group, the basic political and economic unit of Lakota life: multiple households related through descent, marriage, and ceremony, moving and residing together.
It is explicitly political, not a pre-political kinship substrate.

A useful formal reading:

A tiyóšpaye is a polity-unit entity `T ∈ E_lak^pol` together with a region of the polity-cell-system determined by:

- membership / kin cells involving `T` and its persons,
- place / camp cells connecting `T` to its dwelling and movement,
- work-cells whose object is `T`, its members, its camp, its ceremonial objects, or its relations,
- office / counsel / recognition cells by which its internal leadership and deliberation settle,
- withdrawal cells by which `T` or its members restrict participation in engagements without severing kinship,
- covenant and witness cells connecting it to oyate-scale or Oceti Sakowin-scale action.

This suggests a future object:

```text
tiyospaye-settling
```

with an approximate shape:

```text
Tiy(T) ⊆ v_lak
```

where `Tiy(T)` is not a set-theoretic subset alone, but a sub-settling closed under the relevant `S_wolak` rule-classes.
The closure condition matters: if kin-cells exist but their obligation/work/standing consequences are not allowed to fire, then the object is only a fragment of a tiyóšpaye, not the unfolding political-economic unit.

## 6. Work originates in kin-obligation, not dispatch

The `predicated-lakotaean-work` index and `work-cell-system.md` make a major formal distinction from general predicated-work:

```text
Western/general predicated-work: task → assignment/dispatch → capability → completion
Lakotaean work: kin-position → owes-cells → work-cells → standing-cells
```

The central Lakotaean work statement is that work flows through kin-obligation, not dispatch.
The closure rule is W3:

```text
kin-of(a,b) ≥_k t  ⇒  owes-virtue-to(a,b) ≥_k t
```

The owes-cell is the origination-cell of work.
This has immediate consequences:

1. A work theory centered on `dispatch` cannot be the parent of predicated Lakotaean work except as a comparison target.
2. Capability must be kin-position-and-gender-position embedded, not a standalone transferable skill bundle.
3. Completion must be obligation-discharge, not task-done.
4. Work-class must be predicate-class-distinguished, not task-type distinguished.
5. The absence of a surplus-cell is constitutive: the Lakotaean work closure system does not install extraction over the worker.

A foundational next step is to make `kin-obligation-firing` the central work morphism in any Lakotaean work calculus, and to treat dispatch as an external or non-Wolakȟota subsystem unless specifically recovered through a Lakotaean authority relation.

## 7. Work-classes as predicate-class regions

The work-cell-system defines:

```text
Φ_lak^work = E_lak × Π_lak^work × E_lak
```

where `Π_lak^work` decomposes into five work-predicate subclasses:

```text
Π^wówašake
Π^wakȟáŋ-w
Π^wótakuye-w
Π^káğapi
Π^wahúŋkpa
```

The survey finding is that these should be developed categorically as predicate-class regions, not merely as a typology of labor.
Each region should have:

- specialization structure internal to the region,
- converses where meaningful,
- projection behavior into agent/object/standing predicates,
- closure rules that fire differently by class,
- interaction rules for multi-classed work-cells.

For example, ceremony-preparation can be both wakȟáŋ-work and wahúŋkpa-work.
That should not be represented as an ambiguous label.
It should be represented as a cell or linked cells whose predicate-class projections land in two regions and whose closure consequences are the union / pullback / fibered interaction of the relevant rule-classes.

This is where `n`-ary predication may become necessary.
A binary cell `(a, prepares, ceremony)` may not carry enough structure to remember relation among agent, object, ceremonial role, collective, season, witness, and obligation-source.
The `n-ary-predication.md` object says the closure-2-monad structure is independent of arity, while closure-shapes become richer.
This makes higher arity a legitimate mathematical enrichment for work/governance, not an ad hoc extension.

Possible ternary or higher cells:

```text
(a, performs-work-for-under-witness, b, w)
(a, discharges-obligation-to-through, b, work_cell)
(a, prepares-ceremony-for-at-season, ceremony, season)
(T, migrates-from-to-under-counsel, old_place, new_place, council)
```

These should be introduced only where the binary cell system cannot preserve the needed closure/naturality information.

## 8. Governance as binding, recognition, withdrawal, and repair

The governance index gives the core commitments:

- the polity-cell-system is kin-relational at the root,
- Wolakȟota is the closure-rule subsystem,
- Omniciye settles by withdrawal rather than majority,
- naca office is double-settlement under four-virtue conditions,
- akicita authority is bounded delegation with revocation,
- treaty is wakȟáŋ-bearing declaration,
- Pȟahá Sápa is a kin-cell, not a property-cell,
- restorative accountability replaces punitive substrate machinery.

The mathematical enrichment should make these into operations on settlings:

### Omniciye / withdrawal

Withdrawal should be modeled as restriction of engagement-local standing cells to `⊥`, while preserving kin-cells.
This is not negation.
It is a morphism that changes the engagement slice without destroying the relation in the polity substrate.

### Omniciye as engagement-local settling

The first enrichment of this survey is to treat omníčiye and iyúha-šni as the governance mechanism by which a tiyóšpaye's decision exists only in the engagement-slice in which it is actually settled.

The `omniciye` source describes council as an assembly around a council fire whose membership is dynamic and matter-appropriate, not a fixed-membership legislature.
The `consensus-by-withdrawal` source says that a council decision binds those who consent; those who cannot consent may withdraw rather than be coerced.
The `iyuha-sni` source supplies the deeper act-form: withdrawal is refusing-to-be-bound in this engagement without severing relation.

Mathematically, an engagement `ω` should be treated as a local context, not as the whole polity.
For a matter `m` before a council, the relevant participant region is not a permanent electorate but a matter-indexed sub-settling:

```text
Part_ω := { a ∈ E_lak | v(appropriate-to-matter)(a, ω) ≥_k t
                      and v(iyuha-sni-from)(a, ω) ≠ t }.
```

This should not be read as a literal set-comprehension replacing the cell-system.
It is a guide to the predicates needed in the cell-system: `appropriate-to-matter`, `engaged-in`, `consents-to`, `iyuha-sni-from`, and `bound-in-engagement`.
The actual structure is a valuation over cells in the engagement-local predicate region.

The decision rule is not majority aggregation:

```text
majority(Part_ω, δ) ⇒ bound(Everyone, δ)
```

The Lakotaean closure-shape is instead:

```text
consents-to(a, δ, ω) ≥_k t  ⇒  bound-in-engagement(a, δ, ω) ≥_k t
iyuha-sni-from(a, ω) ≥_k t  ⇒  standing-for-engagement(a, ω) = ⊥
```

while the deeper kin and polity-unit cells are preserved:

```text
kin-of(a,b) is unchanged by iyuha-sni-from(a,ω)
member/relative-standing in the wider tiyóšpaye is not deleted by iyuha-sni-from(a,ω)
```

This makes withdrawal a restriction morphism on an engagement-indexed slice, not a negation, punishment, exile, or schism.
It is not `¬ bound`; it is refusal of the binding morphism for `ω`.
The relation remains available for later re-entry, later counsel, later repair, or a different engagement.

For tiyóšpaye theory, this means internal governance should be modeled as repeated local settlings around matters, not as a permanent legislative state.
A tiyóšpaye continues as the kin/work/world sub-settling that survives across engagements; a decision exists as the closure of the matter-specific omníčiye slice.
The political unit is therefore both durable and non-coercive: durable because kin-relations persist beyond engagements; non-coercive because engagement-binding is not forced onto those who have iyúha-šni'd from that engagement.

This enrichment suggests a future object:

```text
omniciye-engagement-settling.md
```

and a future morphism:

```text
iyuha-sni-engagement-restriction.md
```

The key theorem to attempt later:

```text
Iyúha-šni preserves the tiyóšpaye substrate iff it restricts only the
engagement-local standing/binding cells and leaves kin, witness, and
polity-unit membership cells invariant.
```

That theorem would give the exact predication-categorical distinction between withdrawal and severance.

### Naca / office installation

Office installation should remain a double-settlement:

```text
recognition-cell(a,o) ≥_k t
induction-cell(a,o) ≥_k t
⇒ holds-office(a,o) ≥_k t
```

The stronger Lakotaean condition is that relevant cells are wakȟáŋ-marked and four-virtue conditioned.
Future files should specify which predicate-category reindexings preserve office validity.

### Akicita / bounded delegation

Akicita authority is delegation over a finite scope with self-revocation at completion.
It should be studied using the existing governance theory of delegation, revocation, and Beck-Chevalley coherence, but instantiated through `S_wolak`: the authority is not generic command power; it is scoped collective-coordination under the polity's recognized rule-subsystem.

### Restorative repair

Harm does not close to punishment.
Under W7:

```text
harmed(x,y) ≥_k t ⇒ relation-requires-repair(x,y) ≥_k t
```

This should be developed as a quiescence-restoration theory: repair is a path toward a settled relation, not a sanction-cell over an offender.
The absence of punitive closure rules is positive mathematical content.

## 9. World-unfolding: tiyóšpaye in environment

The user's target phrase, "the unfolding existence of a tiyóšpaye as it exists in its world," should be read literally in the cell-system.
The world is already in `E_lak`: places, stones, animals, plants, ceremonial objects, wakȟáŋ-beings, and polity-units are all entities.
A tiyóšpaye exists in its world by settling relations with all of these, not by occupying a neutral container called environment.

This suggests a future `tiyospaye-world-settling` object with at least these predicate regions:

- kin-to-place,
- comes-from / buried-at / stands-in-relation-with,
- relation to buffalo, horses, plants, stones, water, and ceremonial objects,
- seasonal and annual-cycle predicates,
- camp-circle / migration / hunt coordination,
- witness and wakȟáŋ-marking predicates,
- harm/repair predicates involving places and nonhuman relatives.

The annual-cycle matters because work cadence in the Lakotaean work leaf is annual, not project-based.
Thus the temporality of a tiyóšpaye should be a periodic/seasonal settling structure, possibly a temporal base category or 2-category over which the same polity-cell-system is reindexed.

A future theorem could state:

```text
A tiyóšpaye is operationally alive at time τ iff its kin, work,
witness, place, and repair sub-settlings remain closed under the
seasonally available fragment of S_wolak at τ.
```

This is only a sketch, but it points to real predication-category content: temporal base change, closure under a restricted rule subsystem, and quiescence/standing conditions.

## 10. Deontic/governance fiber: obligations as cells, not mental states

Earlier governance work in the corpus uses jural relations, deontic modality, delegation as authority residuation, jurisdiction as base change, and Beck-Chevalley coherence.
The Lakotaean leaf already has the key bridge: W3 installs owes-virtue-to cells.

A deontic reading should therefore not model obligation as a mental attitude or a rule in a separate logic.
Obligation is a settled predication cell whose force comes from closure:

```text
kin-cell ⇒ owes-cell ⇒ work/conduct-cell ⇒ standing-cell
```

The deontic fiber can be read as a structured region of the predication fibration: obligation, permission, authority, recognition, withdrawal, repair, and office are predicate-classes whose modal behavior arises through closure and reindexing.
For Lakotaean governance/work, this keeps deontic content relational and cell-based.

This also clarifies why work and governance cannot separate.
A work discharge changes deontic status.
A governance recognition can depend on virtue-standing accumulated through work.
A withdrawal changes the deontic scope of an engagement without erasing kinship.
These are all morphisms in one predication-settling ecology.

## 12. Acceptance gates for future development

A new governance/work file in this leaf should pass the following gates:

1. It names the relevant cells, predicate-classes, and closure rules.
2. It states whether it lives in governance, work, or their shared slice, without treating them as separate substrates.
3. It explains how its construction respects the predicate-category generators.
4. It uses `FOUR` / Tóbtób Kiŋ as the actual value domain, not as metaphor.
5. It identifies whether the operation is knowledge-order increasing, truth-order changing, a restriction/withdrawal, or a repair/quiescence operation.
6. It records whether wakȟáŋ-marking is preserved, introduced, required, or irrelevant.
7. It avoids importing dispatch, surplus, property, punitive, or external-authority rules unless it is explicitly modeling conflict with a non-`S_wolak` subsystem.
8. It treats tiyóšpaye, oyate, place, animal, ceremonial object, and office as entities in the same cell-system when relevant.
9. It distinguishes binary-cell sufficiency from cases requiring `n`-ary predication.
10. It says what closure/naturality fact would fail if the file's claim were false.
11. If it discusses council, consent, or decision, it distinguishes engagement-local binding from polity-wide coercion.
12. If it discusses withdrawal, it states explicitly which cells are restricted and which kin / witness / polity-unit cells are preserved.

## 13. Current survey conclusion

The foundational move is to articulate a tiyóšpaye as an unfolding closed sub-settling of the Lakota polity-cell-system.
Governance and work are two readable slices of that same unfolding, not separate domains.
Work originates from kin-obligation cells installed by `S_wolak`; governance binds through recognition, witness, withdrawal, office, treaty, and repair cells; both update standing and quiescence in the same bilattice-valued settlement.

The first enrichment sharpens the withdrawal part of that claim: omníčiye is a matter-local settling whose participant region is dynamically generated, and iyúha-šni is the restriction morphism that refuses binding in that engagement without deleting kin-relation.
This makes a tiyóšpaye durable across disagreements without making it coercive.

Predication category theory is therefore not a representational layer for Lakotaean mathematics.
In this leaf, the Lakotaean polity-form is the predication category theory instantiated through its own entity set, predicate category, value domain, and closure-rule subsystem.
The next development should make this identity operational by adding explicit tiyóšpaye-settling, world-settling, omníčiye-engagement-settling, iyúha-šni-restriction, and work/governance-slice objects.
