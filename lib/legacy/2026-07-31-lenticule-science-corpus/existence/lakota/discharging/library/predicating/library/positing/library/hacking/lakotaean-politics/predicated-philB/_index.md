# Predicated Lakotaean Politics

The Lakota political form on the predication-categories mathematics.
In Lakota structural ordering, this theory carries Lakota political life as the parent, with [predicated-lakotaean-governance](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/) (currently housed under the Western governance tree for substrate reasons) and [predicated-lakotaean-work](theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/) as sibling sub-theories under it.

## The two sub-theories

In the Lakota organizing, both governance and work are *political* activities, both grounded in kinship and ceremonial-substrate.
They are not separable: how the people decide is how they work is how they govern themselves.
The substrate-algebraic distinction:

- **Predicated-lakotaean-governance** is the *normative-substructure* slice: offices, councils, deliberation, treaty, sanction, restorative repair, the polity's authority-cells and recognition-cells.
  What the polity *binds itself to.* Currently housed at [`predicated-work/predicated-governance/predicated-lakotaean-governance/`](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/) — a Western-tree placement.
  The Lakota-structural placement is here, as a sub-theory of politics, sibling to work.
- **Predicated-lakotaean-work** is the *operational substrate* slice: kin-obligation labor, ceremonial work, the annual-cycle of subsistence and craft, the gift-economy as the polity's substrate-redistribution.
  What the polity *does.* Lives at [`theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/`](theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/).

Both share the polity-cell-system $(E_\mathrm{lak}, \Pi_\mathrm{lak}, \mathcal{C}_\mathrm{lak}, w_\mathrm{lak})$ (see [polity-cell-system](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/objects/polity-cell-system.md)) and both fire under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ (see [wolakhota-closure-system](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/objects/wolakhota-closure-system.md)).
They are slices of the same substrate, not separate substrates.

## Why this scaffolding exists

The Western-tree placement of predicated-lakotaean-governance under predicated-work/predicated-governance follows the library's general convention (governance is a sub-domain of work; the predicated form follows).
The Lakota-structural placement reverses this: work is under politics, governance is next to work.
The captain's authority commits this subtheory to the Lakota placement; the substrate-algebraic content does not depend on which tree-position the leaf lives in, but the conceptual organization does.

A future reorganization may move predicated-lakotaean-governance into this tree as a sibling of predicated-lakotaean-work.
For now, the cross-references make the connection explicit while keeping the substantive content in its current location.
