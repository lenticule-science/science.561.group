# Claim. The whistle–drum ensemble produces heterophony — two layers articulating a shared meter with different timbres.

**Claim.** In ceremonial song contexts (Sun Dance, peyote ceremonies, grass dance), the whistle and the drum together produce **heterophony**: pulses on the whistle align with strokes on the drum, articulating a shared underlying meter with two distinct timbres and slightly offset temporal placements.
The drum supplies the metric pulse the whistle adopts; the whistle adds a pitched and timbrally-distinct layer on top of the drum's percussive pulse.

This is the **only metric context** in the Lakotaean aerophone repertoire.
The flute is non-metric (see [cedar-flute-temporal-substrate-is-non-metric](./cedar-flute-temporal-substrate-is-non-metric.md)); the whistle in isolation is irregular-rhythm; the whistle-with-drum imports the drum's pulse and becomes metric.

**Argument.**

*Pulse alignment.* In documented ceremonial recordings, whistle pulses coincide with drum strokes (within timing-perception tolerance).
The whistle is not playing against an independent rhythmic scheme; it is articulating the drum's pulse with added pitch and timbre.

*Heterophony, not unison.* The two layers are timbrally distinct (drum: low-frequency, broad-spectrum percussion; whistle: high-frequency, narrow-band tone).
The temporal alignment is approximate, not exact — the whistle's attack typically follows the drum's by a few tens of milliseconds, producing a heterophonic "thickening" rather than a unison.

*Imported meter.* The whistle does not generate the meter; it adopts it.
Whistle pulses without the drum (rare in practice but observable in interview recordings) are irregular and rhythm-free.
The drum is the meter source.

*Compatibility with stochastic clusters.* When multiple whistles play simultaneously with the drum, the resulting texture is a stochastic-cluster-with-shared-pulse: many slightly-different pitches, all articulating the same drum-driven meter.
The two music-theoretic objects (stochastic cluster, heterophony) compose: the whistles' pitch dimension is clustered, their rhythmic dimension is heterophonic with the drum.
