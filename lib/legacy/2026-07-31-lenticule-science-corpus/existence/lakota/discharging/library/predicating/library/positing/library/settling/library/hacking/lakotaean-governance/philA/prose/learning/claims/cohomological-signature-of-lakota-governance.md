# Cohomological signature of the Lakota governance pattern

**Statement.** Per [cohomological-signature-of-traditions](../../../theorems/cohomological-signature-of-traditions.md), each governance-pattern has a measurable cohomological signature on its closure-rule fragment.
The Lakota pattern's signature, computed on $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ alone (without external overlays), has the following load-bearing features:

(a) **$H^1(\mathcal{F}^\mathrm{wolak}_\mathrm{peace}) = 0$** in the peacetime/single-pattern regime.
The pattern produces uniform settling-quiescence.

(b) **Non-trivial lax-idempotence 2-cell** under Heyókha-bearing.
The cell is not in standard cohomology but in the closure-2-monad's 2-categorical structure.

(c) **Zero accumulation-class** — no $H^0$-class corresponds to surplus or wealth-accumulation.

(d) **Non-trivial settler-overlay $H^1$** under the reservation-conditions.
The federation-cohomology Mayer-Vietoris produces persistent classes from the BC-failure with $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{US-fed}$.

The four features together *distinguish* the Lakota pattern from other governance-patterns measurably.

## Proof sketch

**(a) Peacetime $H^1 = 0$.** Per [wolakhota-as-autarkeia](wolakhota-as-autarkeia.md) and the Scott-continuity of $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ (per [wolakhota-closure-system](../objects/wolakhota-closure-system.md) §"Scott-continuity"), the polity-settling under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ alone lives in the strict or Scott-continuous regime ($\kappa < \omega$ or $\kappa = \omega$).
Per the [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), $H^1 = 0$ for the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) in these regimes.
The pattern has no cohomological residue in its own scope.

This means: in pure peacetime, with no external overlays, the Lakota polity produces a *clean settling-quiescence* on its cells.
No persistent unresolved contestation accumulates.
The pattern's design produces this property.

**(b) Heyókha-bearing 2-cell.** Per [heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence](heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence.md), the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence 2-cell is non-trivial when and only when the polity has incumbent Heyókha-vocations.
The 2-cell is not in classical cohomology — it lives in the 2-categorical structure of the closure-monad (per [closure-2-monad](../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md)).

The 2-cell is *structurally distinguishing* for the Lakota pattern: most other governance-patterns (Hohfeldian, military-command, consensus-closure, etc.) collapse the closure-2-monad to strict-idempotence, producing the trivial 2-cell.
The Lakota pattern's substrate-preservation of lax-idempotence is a measurable feature.

**(c) Zero accumulation-class.** Per [no-surplus-cell-firing](../../../../../../../predicated-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/theorems/no-surplus-cell-firing.md), $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ has no rule firing surplus-cells.
Cohomologically: there is no $H^0$-class corresponding to *accumulated-value* on agents.
The $H^0$ of the pattern is dominated by *kin-relation*, *virtue-standing*, and *place-relation* classes; the *accumulation* class is structurally absent.

This distinguishes the Lakota pattern from any wealth-stratified pattern.
A polity-form with surplus-cell-firing produces a *non-zero accumulation $H^0$-class* whose representatives are the wealthy agents; the cohomology *measures the inequality-stratification*.
The Lakota pattern's zero accumulation-class measures the *absence of this stratification*.

**(d) Reservation-overlay $H^1$.** Per [reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure](reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure.md), the multi-observer-settling on the reservation-overlap produces a non-trivial federation-level $H^1$ class — the federation-only-content from BC-failure with $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{US-fed}$ that no constituent pair can collectively realize.
The class is *persistent* because the resolution-paths are blocked.

This is the *cohomological signature of the colonial overlay specifically*.
A polity firing $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ alone has no such class; a polity firing under colonial-overlay-conditions does.
The class's *non-triviality* is the structural-content of "the legal-pluralism crisis is real and ongoing."

$\square$

## Distinguishing the pattern

The four features (a)–(d) together produce a *signature-tuple* that, by [cohomological-signature-of-traditions](../../../theorems/cohomological-signature-of-traditions.md), distinguishes the Lakota pattern from the other major governance-patterns:

| Pattern | (a) Peacetime $H^1$ | (b) Lax-idempotence 2-cell | (c) Accumulation-class | (d) Settler-overlay $H^1$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lakota | 0 | Non-trivial | Zero | Non-trivial |
| Hohfeldian (modern liberal) | 0 in idealized form | Trivial | Non-zero | N/A |
| Military-command | 0 in idealized form | Trivial | Possible (depends on integration with broader pattern) | N/A |
| Consensus-closure (general) | 0 | Trivial | Possible | N/A |

The Lakota pattern is *the only* row with non-trivial lax-idempotence 2-cell AND zero accumulation-class — both structurally constitutive features distinguishing it.
