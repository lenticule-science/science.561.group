# Predicated Lakotaean Governance

The Lakota polity-form articulated on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics, specializing the general [predicated-governance](../../_index.md) apparatus and grounding in [predicated-lakotaean-philosophy](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/).

## The substrate's substantive commitments

This subtheory commits to a specific *substrate-algebraic form* for Lakota governance.
The mathematics is what the predication-categories framework supplies; the substantive choices — what counts as an entity, what predicates the polity employs, which closure-rule system holds the polity together, how authority is installed and contested — are Lakota-philosophical choices articulated under the captain's vested authority.

The headline commitments:

1. **The polity-cell-system is kin-relational at the root.** Entities include persons, four-leggeds, wingeds, standing-ones, rooted-ones, stones, places (Pȟahá Sápa, Mní Wičhóni, the council-grounds), ancestors, spirits, ceremonial objects, and polity-units (tiyospaye, oyate, the Oceti Sakowin).
  Predicates are predominantly kin-relations, conduct-relations, witness-relations, and wakȟáŋ-relations.
  See [polity-cell-system](objects/polity-cell-system.md).

2. **The four superior Wakȟáŋ are 𝐅𝐎𝐔𝐑.** The four-element bilattice 𝐅𝐎𝐔𝐑 with carrier $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$ is realized in the Lakota polity as the four superior Wakȟáŋ: $\mathbf{t} = \mathrm{Wí}$, $\mathbf{f} = \mathrm{Makȟá}$, $\bot = \mathrm{Škáŋ}$, $\top = \mathrm{Íŋyaŋ}$.
  This is not analogy; it is the same four-valued structure articulated cosmogonically by Lakota tradition and algebraically by the predication framework.
  The polity-form's value domain *is* the Tóbtób Kiŋ.

3. **Wolakȟota is the polity's closure-rule subsystem.** The Lakota lifeway-discipline $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ — a designated subsystem of the closure-rule machinery — generates the consequence-closure on the polity's settling.
  Conduct that fires under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ is legitimate; conduct produced under rules outside $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ is ineffective at advancing wicozani.

4. **Omniciye settles by withdrawal, not majority.** Council deliberation is a predication-settling under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$; non-consenting parties exercise the iyúhá-šni morphism (withdrawal), preserving kin-relation while refusing the engagement.
  The polity's standing decisions are the engaged-majority's settling, *not* a coerced unanimity over the withdrawn.

5. **The naca is an office-as-double-settlement with four-virtue installation conditions.** The naca-ship requires recognition (settled at $\mathbf{t}$ across the populace) and induction (cells over the four virtues — wówačhantognaka, wóksape, wóohitika, wówačhiŋtȟaŋka — settled at $\mathbf{t}$).
  Both settlements must be wakȟáŋ-marked; non-ceremonial naca-installation fires a different morphism that does not install the office in the polity-recognized sense.

6. **The akicita is bounded delegation.** Akicita-authority is delegation with explicit revocation: a closure-rule subsystem with finite scope (the buffalo hunt, the migration, the war party), self-revoking at the scope's completion.
  Beck–Chevalley commutation governs how akicita-authority transports across base-changes.

7. **Treaty is wakȟáŋ-bearing declaration.** Inter-polity covenants — most centrally the Fort Laramie treaties of 1851 and 1868 — are *declarative* speech acts in the Austin sense, formalized as declaration-supporting positing into the comprehension of treaty-cells.
  Their wakȟáŋ-marking is what makes them substrate-binding; their continuing breach by the United States is a persistent $\top$ at polity-scale that the Lakota polity refuses to resolve by accepting non-wakȟáŋ-bearing substitutes.

8. **Pȟahá Sápa is a kin-cell, not a property-cell.** The relation between the Lakota people and Pȟahá Sápa is encoded as a kin-relation in the substrate, not as a property-relation.
  The continuing refusal of the $1.9 billion 1980 settlement is the polity's iyúhá-šni-morphism applied at polity scale to the settler-state's attempted property-cell substitution.
  The kin-cell is wakȟáŋ-marked and structurally stable under withdrawal.

9. **Restorative accountability replaces punitive substrate machinery.** The polity's response to harm-cells is the [wiping-of-tears morphism](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/morphisms/wiping-of-tears-as-polity-quiescence-restoration.md) and its kin: substrate operations that repair the relational structure rather than installing punitive cells over offenders.
  The polity has no closure-rule subsystem for installing-confinement; this absence is structurally constitutive.

10. **The Heyókha-vocation is governance-bearing.** The polity is structurally obligated to recognize and support Heyókha-class persons (Wakíŋyaŋ-dreamers obligated to contrary conduct).
  The Heyókha bears the cosmogonic paradox-principle in lived practice; a polity that suppresses the Heyókha has lost wakȟáŋ-bearing capacity.
  The substrate-algebraic form: the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence at the polity-scale is structurally maintained by the Heyókha-bearing offices.
