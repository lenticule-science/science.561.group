# Wolakota as closure-rule discipline

[Wolakȟota](../../../../indigenous/theories/lakotaean-philosophy/concepts/wolakota.md) — the Lakota way — is realized in the substrate as a *closure-rule discipline*: a coherent subsystem of closure rules that, when potential on an agent's substrate, produces conduct-cell settlings consistent with the [four virtues](four-virtues-as-evaluation-predicates.md), with mitakuye oyasin's relational obligations, and with proper participation in ceremony.

## The discipline as subsystem

Formally, the wolakȟota closure-rule discipline is a subsystem

$$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}} \;\subseteq\; \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{lak}}$$

— a designated subset of the full closure rule system on the Lakota substrate.
The subsystem is *coherent* in the technical sense that its rules do not contradict one another: an agent whose substrate operates under $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ does not produce $\top$ cells through internal rule conflict (they may still produce $\top$ cells through external witness conflict, which is a different matter).

Membership of a rule in $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ is *recognized*, not declared.
The subsystem is the historical accumulation of closure rules that the Lakota tradition has recognized as wolakȟota over generations of practice.
New rules can be added by the tradition's own recognition processes (a teaching becomes wolakȟota when the elders carry it); rules can be revised or removed by the same processes.

## Acting wolakota

An agent $a$ is *acting wolakȟota* at time $\tau$ when their conduct cells in the time interval around $\tau$ have been settled by rules in $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ rather than by rules outside it.
Formally: the set of closure rules whose firings produced the values of $a$'s conduct cells in the relevant interval is a subset of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$.

This is a substrate-algebraic version of *being-Lakota as practice*.
An agent's descent determines what substrate they have access to; their conduct determines which rules of that substrate are potential.
A Lakota person whose conduct cells are settled by rules outside $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ is not acting wolakȟota even though they remain Lakota by descent.
A non-Lakota person acting in accordance with rules in $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ is acting wolakȟota in the partial sense the tradition recognizes (the [hunka](../morphisms/hunka-as-kin-cell-installation.md) ceremony is the substrate operation that admits a person formally into the populace under $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$).

## The integration claim

The substantive content the discipline integrates:

- The [four virtues evaluation system](four-virtues-as-evaluation-predicates.md) — the evaluation predicates the conduct cells settle under.
- The [kin-relational predicate primitive](kin-relation-as-primitive.md) — the predicate-category structure the rules operate over.
- The [wakhan cell-and-rule quality](wakhan-as-cell-quality.md) — the rules that bear the sacred quality.
- Ceremony participation as substrate operation — proper performance of the seven rites generates witness-bearing cells that the discipline reads on.
- Language: speaking Lakȟótiyapi is not a separate addendum; it is part of the substrate that the rules of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ operate over, because the language *carries* the relational distinctions the rules quantify on.

A coherent operation under all of these together is what makes the discipline a discipline rather than a list.
The closure-rule subsystem holds together because its rules are mutually consistent and jointly sufficient to generate the lifeway.

## Disruption and reclamation

A century and a half of settler-state pressure — boarding schools, language suppression, religious suppression, dispossession — has been pressure on $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$: pressure to remove rules from the subsystem (suppress ceremony, suppress language, suppress kinship organization) and to install rules outside it (the closure-rule discipline of Christian-individual personhood, of bureaucratic-citizenship, of property-relations to land).
The polity's response has been to maintain $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ across this pressure and, more recently, to *reclaim* rules that had been pushed out (language revitalization, ceremony return, kinship-rebuilding).

The substrate machinery makes this concrete.
Reclamation is *closure-rule re-installation*: a teaching, a ceremony, a language fluency, a kinship practice returns to potential membership in $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ through being learned, performed, and recognized.
The reclamation is not symbolic; it is the substrate's own operation, conducted by the people who hold the substrate.
