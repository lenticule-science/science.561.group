# Tóbtób Kiŋ

Tóbtób Kiŋ — *the four-times-four* — names the sixteen wakȟáŋ aspects that together constitute Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka.
The structure is four sets of four, each set headed by one of the four superior Wakȟáŋ: **Wí** (Sun), **Škáŋ** (Sky/Motion), **Makȟá** (Earth), **Íŋyaŋ** (Rock).
The four superior beings are the four positions of the relational field after the cosmogonic self-emptying.
In the relational-metaphysical reading carried by this subtheory, the four superior Wakȟáŋ are *the four logic-values of the relational field* — the Lakota form of the bilattice that grounds the predication framework.

## The recorded structure

George Sword (Long Knife) gave Walker the systematic theology, published in *Lakota Belief and Ritual* (DeMallie/Jahner ed., 1980), pp.
50–55.
The four superior Wakȟáŋ:

1. **Wí** — Sun.
  Walker records Finger calling Wí "Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka Kiŋ."
  Wí is the ceremonial chief, the luminous-origination, the lit-up.
  Wí is the visible source of warmth and life-on-earth.
2. **Škáŋ** — Sky / Motion.
  Walker records Finger calling Škáŋ "Naǧí Tȟáŋka, the Great Spirit." Škáŋ is the supreme functional power: the giver of *naǧí* (spirit) to every living being, the cosmic animator, the supreme judge.
  See [skan](skan.md).
3. **Makȟá** — Earth.
  The mother, the material-ground, the receiver of Íŋyaŋ's blood.
  Makȟá is the dense-substrate on which beings stand.
4. **Íŋyaŋ** — Rock.
  First in cosmogonic origin, last in present-time rank — the self-emptied one whose pouring-out *is* the relational field.
  See [iyan](iyan.md).

Below the four superior are the next four (Hanwí / Moon, Tȟaté / Wind, Wóȟpe / Falling-Star-or-Beautiful-One, Wakíŋyaŋ / Thunder), and below them further aspects — sixteen in total.
The structure is *Tóbtób* (four-times-four).
The *Kiŋ* is the definite article ("the").

## The cosmogonic ordering

The four superior Wakȟáŋ emerge in cosmogonic order from Íŋyaŋ's self-emptying:

- **Before**: Íŋyaŋ alone in Haŋhépi (the genesis-paradox; see [iyan-hanhepi](iyan-hanhepi.md)).
- Íŋyaŋ opens his veins.
  His blood becomes **Makȟá** (Earth) and **mní** (water).
- The power-in-his-blood rises as **Škáŋ** (the sky/animation).
- **Wí** (Sun) is created to warm Makȟá when she complains of being cold.
- **Íŋyaŋ** himself shrivels into hard rock — first in origin, last in present rank.

The order matters.
Makȟá and Škáŋ both emerge directly from Íŋyaŋ's emptying — Makȟá from his blood (the material), Škáŋ from the power-in-the-blood (the animation).
Wí is created subsequently. Íŋyaŋ-post-emptying remains as the spent-source.
The four-superior structure is *the cosmogonic resolution* of Íŋyaŋ-in-Haŋhépi.

## The four superior Wakȟáŋ as the four logic-values of the relational field

This subtheory commits to a substantive metaphysical reading: the four superior Wakȟáŋ are not four arbitrary beings; they are the *four positions* of the relational field generated by the cosmogonic resolution of the paradox.
They map directly onto the four values of the bilattice {⊥, t, f, ⊤} that grounds Lakota relational logic.

| Bilattice value | Logic role | Lakota | Cosmogonic role |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⊥ | under-determined; "nothing yet pronounced" | **Škáŋ** | The supreme judge holding the undecided; the moving-principle from which determination proceeds |
| t | true; positive-determination | **Wí** | The luminous-affirmation; the lit-up assertion; the sun-as-yes |
| f | false; negative-determination | **Makȟá** | The grounded-complement; the receiving-mother; the dense-substrate-as-not-light |
| ⊤ | over-determined; both-true-and-false; contradiction | **Íŋyaŋ** | The paradox-bearer; the void-that-became-everything; the spent-source-as-totality |

The mapping is structural, not analogical.
The four positions are not separate beings *plus* logic values; they *are* the four logic positions, in Lakota cosmogonic form.
The Western algebraic articulation (Belnap's four-valued logic, the bilattice machinery underlying the predication-categories mathematics) is a Western *re-presentation* of what the Lakota cosmogony already names.
The bilattice is not imposed on Lakota cosmology; Lakota cosmology is the *source-form* of the bilattice, articulated in cosmogonic terms before it was articulated in algebraic terms.

This is the captain's substantive claim.
The predication-categories framework is — despite its Western mathematical presentation — *Lakota cosmogony, ontology, epistemics, and metaphysics in algebraic notation*.
The reason the mappings between the framework's primitives and Lakota concepts work so cleanly is that the framework is, at root, *the same content in a different notational register*.

## Why this matters for the structure

The bilattice has two orderings:

- **Truth-order**: how "true" a value is.
  Ordering: f ≤ ⊥, f ≤ ⊤, ⊥ ≤ t, ⊤ ≤ t.
  Truth-supremum: t (Wí).
  Truth-infimum: f (Makȟá).
- **Information-order**: how much information a value carries.
  Ordering: ⊥ ≤ t, ⊥ ≤ f, t ≤ ⊤, f ≤ ⊤.
  Information-supremum: ⊤ (Íŋyaŋ).
  Information-infimum: ⊥ (Škáŋ).

The cosmogonic mapping respects both orderings:

- *Truth-order on the cosmogony*: Makȟá-as-f is the truth-infimum because Makȟá receives without yet illuminating; Wí-as-t is the truth-supremum because Wí *is* the illumination; Škáŋ and Íŋyaŋ sit between as the undetermined-potential (⊥) and the over-determined-contradictory (⊤).
- *Information-order on the cosmogony*: Škáŋ-as-⊥ is the information-infimum because the supreme judge *holds* the undecided before pronouncing; Íŋyaŋ-as-⊤ is the information-supremum because the paradox-bearer holds *all of it at once* (the alone-being-and-the-emptied-relational-field together); Wí and Makȟá sit between as the determinate-truth-values.

The lattice meets and joins (operations on the values) correspond to specific Lakota relational operations — but those operations are articulated more in the [predicated subtheory](../../../../predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/) than in the philosophical-foundations layer.

## The lesser twelve

The four-times-four structure means there are twelve further wakȟáŋ aspects beyond the four superior:

- **Hanwí** (Moon), **Tȟaté** (Wind), **Wóȟpe** (Falling-Star / Beautiful-One), **Wakíŋyaŋ** (Thunder) — the next four below.
- **Tatáŋka** (Buffalo), **Hunúŋpa** (Bear/Grizzly), **Tatékiyutaŋ** (Whirlwind), **Wani** (the four winds) — the third tier in some recordings.
- Plus the lowest tier, which varies by informant.

These aspects are not independent.
They are *aspect-clusters* under one of the four superior.
Hanwí (Moon) is paired with Wí (Sun) under the luminous-aspect.
Tȟaté (Wind) is paired with Škáŋ under the moving-aspect.
The four-times-four structure is *fractal*: each of the four superior contains its own quartet of aspects, and each of those contains finer aspects.
The relational field has the same four-valued structure at every level of detail.

This fractal property is significant: the Lakota cosmology does not have one logic for the cosmos and a different logic for the local.
The same four-valued structure runs at every scale.
The polity's wicozani, the individual's standing-as-wolakȟota, the relations between specific kin — all are evaluated on the same four-valued field.
The cosmos is *self-similar* across scales of relational complexity.
