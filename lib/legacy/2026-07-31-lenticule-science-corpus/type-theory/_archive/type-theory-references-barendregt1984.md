# Barendregt 1984 — The Lambda Calculus: Its Syntax and Semantics

Henk Barendregt, *The Lambda Calculus: Its Syntax and Semantics* (Studies in Logic and the Foundations of Mathematics, vol. 103), revised edition, North-Holland, 1984.

The standard reference for the [untyped-lambda-calculus](../objects/untyped-lambda-calculus.md) and a substantial reference for the typed extensions. Covers [beta-reduction](teaching/explanations/beta-reduction.md), [eta-equivalence](teaching/explanations/eta-equivalence.md), [confluence](teaching/explanations/confluence.md), [normalization](teaching/explanations/normalization.md), Böhm trees, and the lambda cube.

## Why cited here

- Definitive treatment of [alpha-equivalence](teaching/explanations/alpha-equivalence.md), [substitution](teaching/explanations/substitution.md), and the [substitution-operator](../morphisms/substitution-operator.md).
- Standard proof of [church-rosser](../theorems/church-rosser.md) via parallel reduction.
- The Barendregt convention on bound variables (used implicitly throughout the directory).
- Concrete examples: [church-numerals-untyped](../examples/church-numerals-untyped.md), [y-combinator-untyped](../examples/y-combinator-untyped.md).

## Used by

Cited in essentially every concept and object note here, plus the [church-rosser](../theorems/church-rosser.md) and [strong-normalization-stlc](../theorems/strong-normalization-stlc.md) theorems.
