Type theory is the mathematics of typed terms — formal systems in which expressions are *typed* before they are *evaluated*, the type system rules out ill-formed combinations before reduction begins, and the propositions-as-types correspondence makes a proof and a program the same kind of object. From Church's 1932 simply-typed [λ-calculus](objects/simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md) through [Martin-Löf 1984](references/martin-lof1984.md)'s dependent type theory and the homotopy-theoretic extensions of the 2010s, type theory has carried a double role: as a foundation for constructive mathematics, and as the operational substrate underneath every modern proof assistant. This directory carries type theory as a working library, not a textbook: a disciplined population of typed notes that earns the theory's load-bearing role by being explicitly cited from the corpus's `type:` frontmatter convention and from the [predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system](../../../../../science/theories/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/specifications/frontmatter-relations.md)'s typed-predication structure.

## Position relative to math

Type theory sits *under* [logic](../logic/) — every typing rule is a derivation rule in some logic, and the [curry-howard-correspondence](theorems/curry-howard-correspondence.md) makes the alignment exact — and *alongside* [sets](../sets/) and [categories](../categories/) as a foundation for the rest of mathematics. It supplies the formal language proof assistants ([Lean](objects/dependent-type-theory.md), Agda, Coq) use to record verified mathematics, and supplies the discrete syntactic substrate that underwrites the corpus's `type:` and `class:` frontmatter conventions: a `type: theorem` value is a type assignment in the informal type-theoretic sense, and a `status: proved` claim is exactly an inhabited type in the corresponding proof system.

## Load-bearing role

Type theory underwrites the corpus's `type:` frontmatter field — every typed note (concept, definition, theorem, question, narrative, example, reference) carries a `type:` value that classifies it in the informal type-theoretic sense, and the [predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system frontmatter-relations spec](../../../../../science/theories/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/specifications/frontmatter-relations.md) treats the `class:` refinement as a typed-predication structure on top of those `type:` values. The [curry-howard-correspondence](theorems/curry-howard-correspondence.md) theorem makes the load-bearing claim explicit at the semantic level: a `status: proved` theorem is exactly an inhabited type in the corresponding proof system — proving and inhabiting are the same activity. The downstream `depends-on: [pi-type]` edge added to [logic/concepts/quantifier.md](../logic/concepts/quantifier.md) replaces a prior silent coupling: universal quantification along a projection *is* a Π-type, both as adjoint to substitution (the categorical view) and as a typing rule (the syntactic view).

## Deferred sub-areas

The following sub-areas of type theory are *named-as-deferred*: they are recognized as part of the textbook external theory but are not written here, and SHOULD become child theories under `type-theory/theories/` when the corpus needs them.

- **Cubical type theory** — explicit interval-based identity types; the computational reading of [univalence](theorems/univalence-axiom.md).
- **Linear and substructural type theory** — type theories that restrict weakening, contraction, or both. The logic-side counterparts already live at [logic/concepts/linear-logic](../logic/concepts/linear-logic.md) and [logic/concepts/substructural-logic](../logic/concepts/substructural-logic.md); the type-theoretic side is named-as-deferred here.
- **Effect systems and graded modal types** — typed tracking of computational effects.
- **Refinement types and liquid types** — types refined by predicates, decidable subtyping.
- **Realizability and categorical semantics of type theory** — should arrive as a child theory once the [predication-categories](../categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) treatment of typed predication is mature enough to receive it.
- **Lean formalisation** — initial `lean:` bindings on this directory's theorems against `Mathlib.Logic` and the Lean kernel are tracked in [questions/lean-binding](questions/lean-binding.md).
