## What this node is
This node is generated by the **predicate** morphism in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. The natural numbers are the initial algebra of the successor operation: starting from zero and asserting "and one more" again and again is exactly the act of predicating structure onto a bare carrier. Number theory is what that predication generates — ℕ characterized by the Peano axioms, arithmetic defined by recursion over successor, and the commutative semiring of addition and multiplication. It is not a "subject filed under math"; it is the body that the predicate morphism produces when applied to the inductive carrier.

## What it generates / contains
- **collatz-conjecture/** — the 3n+1 dynamic studied as an irreducible relational reflex, including the navonic/relational modeling material.
- Peano foundations: zero, successor, the Peano axioms, induction, and recursion as the universal property of the natural numbers object.
- Arithmetic and algebraic structure: addition, multiplication, and ℕ as the prototypical semiring (no additive inverses).
- Divisibility and primes: divisibility, congruence, greatest common divisor, prime.
- Combinatorics: binomial coefficient, combination, permutation, counting principles.
- Relational proofs and dynamics: Agda treatments of the Collatz, Goodstein, and Riemann/zeta dynamics; an RSA cryptography toy.
