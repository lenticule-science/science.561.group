## The method, now explicit (and it makes soft words tractable)

A soft word is soft because ordinary language smears it across all four regimes at once.
Its cloud of near-synonyms is not noise — it is the **four regime-faces tangled together** with drift.
So deriving a soft word = **sort its cloud onto the four regimes and name each face precisely.** The hard math (the closure-ordinal tower) does the cutting; I don't invent meanings, I disambiguate by regime.

Worked example — the doxastic family:

## relating moved into the fractal — it was never the primitive

1. bare directed `(s,p,o)` giving a *single* existent its structure = **doing / predicating**, the person-scale transfinite base (already derived; relating ≠ this);

Relating is (2).
And (2) needs ≥2 **individuals** to be between — and the individual is **governing's finite fixed point**.
An act that presupposes settled selves cannot be the seed that produces them.
So relating is **post-individual**: the **doing (transfinite act) of the social-scale descent**, re-entered on the individual.
