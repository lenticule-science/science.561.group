## Departure from Substack: The Nexus of Civil Liberties and Censorship

In the ever-evolving landscape of the internet, it has become crucial to grasp how certain legislation, like FOSTA/SESTA, has led to an unsettling chill effect on sex work online, impacting the lives and safety of many. This understanding forms part of the backdrop to my decision to withdraw from Substack and channel my writing through a more autonomous platform, [[Groundhog Autonomous Zone|my Webplace]].

For those who wish to set sail with me and direct their support elsewhere, I recommend Ko-fi as an alternative platform. My reasons for transitioning away from Substack have been spurred by a statement from Hamish McKenzie, one of the co-founders:

> "I just want to make it clear that we don’t like Nazis either—we wish no-one held those views. But some people do hold those and other extreme views. Given that, we don't think that censorship (including through demonetizing publications) makes the problem go away—in fact, it makes it worse. We believe that supporting individual rights and civil liberties while subjecting ideas to open discourse is the best way to strip bad ideas of their power. We are committed to upholding and protecting freedom of expression, even when it hurts."

This stance conveys an ironclad allegiance to the ideals of individualism and civil liberties, whatever the consequences may be. It fails, however, to explicitly address the implicit prohibition of sexual content on their platform, setting a precedent worthy of scrutiny.

Within the cacophony of voices championing the banner of free speech are those who paradoxically silence sexual expression while lending a megaphone to more destructive forms of discourse. Christian nationalists figure prominently in this cohort, promoting a dichotomy where the dissemination and financing of Nazi propaganda are permissible, yet the representation of sexuality on the internet is purged. This is not an approach exclusive to Substack, but rather an insidious trend across payment processors and platforms—a trend borne out of and emboldened by legislative actions like FOSTA/SESTA.

While some individuals cloak their ideologies in the garb of Progressivism, their actions facilitate the insidious spread of Christian nationalism, a phenomenon designed, perhaps inadvertently, by the technocratic dreams of our age. Recognizing how events like these weave into the broader fabric of societal change is imperative for those who aspire to be architects of change rather than mere bystanders.

I extend my gratitude to the venture capitalists for the unintended consequence of amplifying my readership. Nonetheless, the time has come to voyage beyond these waters.

The irony is not lost on me that my departure leads to [[Groundhog Autonomous Zone|my Webplace]], a domain comparably more receptive to free expression and unencumbered by orthodox structures dictating the dissemination and sharing of knowledge. It operates akin to a virtual mycelium network, free from the imposition of what ought to be.

I cordially invite you to join me there, where the fertile ground for discourse and exploration remains untamed by the predilections of the few. To journey together to this new haven, follow the link to my websiteTK.

Your engagement is not just welcome, but integral to the growth of a community where ideas can flourish authentically, and where the tapestry of change is woven with threads of unfettered expression and shared human experience.
