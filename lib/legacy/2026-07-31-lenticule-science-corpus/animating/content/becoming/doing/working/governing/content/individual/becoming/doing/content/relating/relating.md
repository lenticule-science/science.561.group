**Relating** is the act one settled self directs toward another: a conversation, a gift, a witnessing, a promise.
It is what selves do *to and with each other* — the social act, the directed deed between someones.
Relating is the **doing** of the social-scale descent: the acting that the re-entered [individual](../../../../../docs/individual.md) carries out among other individuals.

## The act between selves — and the relation prior to terms

There are two things the word "relating" can name, and keeping them apart is the point of seating this term where it sits.
There is the constitutive relating that is *prior to its terms* — the bare directed structure by which anything is determined as *this* over against *that* at all.
Relational ontologies press this priority: Buber opens *I and Thou* with "In the beginning is the relation"; Barad's *intra-action* holds that relata do not precede their relating but emerge through it.
That priority is real, but it belongs at the base of the descent, with [doing](../../../../../../../../../docs/doing.md) and predication — the moment that gives a single existent its structure.

Relating *between individuals* is the other thing, and it presupposes the selves it runs between.
Here the relata are already settled persons, and what passes between them is a social act.
This is the relating of Buber's lived *I–Thou* encounter — the meeting of two who already stand as someones — and of Lévinas's face-to-face, where the Other's face calls the self into responsibility.
Before *this* relating there are individuals; "before relating there are no terms" is true of the constitutive sense and false of the social one.
An act that runs between settled selves cannot be the act that first produces them.

## What generates it

Relating is generated by the *doing* of the re-entered descent — the acting of an individual, now directed not at a sub-personal content but at another individual.
Its directedness is real: to give and to receive, to address and to be addressed, are structurally distinct positions, and relating runs from one self to another.

## What it generates

The settled form relating leaves behind is the **bond** — friendship, kinship, contract.
Accumulated relating among individuals builds **community**, the shared social field, which settles in turn into the institution or collective.
Relating is, for the social descent, what speaking is for the personal one: the act that feeds the whole.
Simondon's *transindividual* names the result — the collective that is neither a mere aggregate of individuals nor a fusion of them, but the relation in which individuals are further individuated together.
