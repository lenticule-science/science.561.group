- [[postulate]]:: A null reflexive derivation must induce the minimal negation of relational presence.
- [[derivation]]:: Reflexing zero times derives negation, because no reflexive act is performed, preserving relational absence. The most minimal absence of relation is thus induced by a reflex count of zero.
- [[example]]::
    - false(n) is reflect(0, n)
    - zero reflexive acts induce absence, so reflect(0, n) derives false.
- [[relational expression]]:: [[false]](n) is [[oldreflect]](0, n)
