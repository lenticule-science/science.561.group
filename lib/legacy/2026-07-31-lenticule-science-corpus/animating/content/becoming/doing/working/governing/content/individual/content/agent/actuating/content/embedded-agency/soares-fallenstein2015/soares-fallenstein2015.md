# Soares & Fallenstein 2015 — Toward Idealized Decision Theory

Soares, N., & Fallenstein, B.
(2015). *Toward Idealized Decision Theory*.
MIRI Technical Report.

The technical statement of **Functional Decision Theory** as the successor to Yudkowsky's TDT.
The agent's choice variable is the *output of its decision algorithm*, treated as a function across all physical instantiations of the algorithm (including predictors that simulate it).
The expected utility is computed under counterfactuals over this output, requiring a theory of *logical counterfactuals* — counterfactual reasoning over mathematical/algorithmic facts.

Soares and Fallenstein develop FDT enough to recover the intuitive verdicts on Newcomb, Smoking Lesion, Prisoner's Dilemma against a copy, Parfit's Hitchhiker, and several other test cases.
They acknowledge the open problem of logical counterfactuals and connect FDT to the broader agent-foundations program (logical induction, embedded agency, reflective stability).
