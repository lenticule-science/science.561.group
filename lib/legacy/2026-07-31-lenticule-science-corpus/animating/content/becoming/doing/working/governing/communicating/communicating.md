**Communicating** is governing read as the settlement of addressable meaning between agents.
Where knowing settles claims, communicating settles the shared forms by which claims, commands, questions, promises, signs, and texts can move between knowers without dissolving into private noise.

Communication is not merely transmission.
It is a governed relation: utterances have force, signs have standing, grammar constrains composition, discourse carries commitments forward, and writing makes communicative order durable across time.

## What generates it

Communicating is generated by governing because language only functions when a shared field of marks is bounded by rules of recognition, uptake, correction, and reference.
Those rules may be grammatical, rhetorical, semantic, social, technical, or institutional, but they are rules all the same: they govern how a mark can count as meaning.

## What it generates

Communicating generates language, linguistics, semiotics, speech acts, grammar, syntax, semantics, discourse, rhetoric, writing, technical writing, and mathematical communication as particular ways of settling meaning for use.
