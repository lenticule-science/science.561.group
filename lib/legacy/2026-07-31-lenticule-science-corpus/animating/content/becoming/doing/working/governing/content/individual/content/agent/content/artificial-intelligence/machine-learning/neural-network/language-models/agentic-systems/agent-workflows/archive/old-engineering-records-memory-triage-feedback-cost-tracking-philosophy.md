Don't enforce budget limits or report budget utilization. The budget is "cheaper over time for the same workunit."

**Why:** The captain pointed out we don't know our absolute budget objectively. Today's $182 was expensive but produced 28 research texts, infrastructure, and architectural improvements that enable future cost reduction. There isn't a way to do it cheaper without the infrastructure this session built.

**How to apply:** Track and report cost-per-tool-call, cost-per-output-token, context-overhead-ratio — efficiency metrics that should trend downward. Never gate or block work based on cost. The cost-tracker should analyze efficiency, not enforce limits.
