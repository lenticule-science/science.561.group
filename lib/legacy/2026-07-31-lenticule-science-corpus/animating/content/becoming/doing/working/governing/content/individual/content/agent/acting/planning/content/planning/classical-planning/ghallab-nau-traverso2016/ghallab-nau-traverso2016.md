# Ghallab, Nau & Traverso 2016

**Automated Planning and Acting.** Cambridge University Press.

## What the book does

The standard textbook of automated planning (a major revision of the authors' earlier *Automated Planning: Theory and Practice*, 2004).
Comprehensive coverage of classical planning, heuristic-search planning, hierarchical planning, planning under uncertainty (MDP, POMDP), temporal planning, and the integration of planning with execution and acting in the real world.

## Why this book matters

The book defines the consensus shape of academic planning research.
Notation, terminology, problem decompositions, algorithmic conventions are largely those of this book and its 2004 predecessor.
When a result in the planning theory needs a *standard* reference for the field's mainstream framing, this book is it.

The book also self-consciously addresses the gap between *planning* (a research subdiscipline) and *acting* (the real-world deployment problem), introducing the planning–acting integration framework that contemporary deployed-planning systems rely on.
