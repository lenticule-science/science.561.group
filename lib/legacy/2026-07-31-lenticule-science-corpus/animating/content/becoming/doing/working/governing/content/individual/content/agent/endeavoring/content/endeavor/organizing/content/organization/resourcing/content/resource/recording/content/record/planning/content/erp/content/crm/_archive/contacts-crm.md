# Idea: Contacts, Relationships, and Simple CRM

Treat people, organizations, and interactions as recognitions so “contact list” and “CRM” become just another slice of the relational graph.

## 1. Recognitions

- `PersonRecognition`: name, handles, contact info, roles.
- `OrganizationRecognition`: groups, companies, collectives.
- `InteractionRecognition`: meetings, messages, collaborations.
- `RelationshipRecognition`: ongoing ties (friend, collaborator, client).

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - social graph, affiliation graph, project membership.
- Measurement:
  - frequency/recency of contact, responsiveness, open threads.

Runbooks like `log_interaction`, `plan_checkins`, `review_relationships` help you stay in touch without leaving the relational universe.
