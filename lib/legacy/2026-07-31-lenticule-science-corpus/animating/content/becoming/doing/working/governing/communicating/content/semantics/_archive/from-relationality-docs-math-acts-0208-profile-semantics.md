# Profile Semantics

## Interpretation

The profile’s recognitions form a semantic structure in their own right.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Profile Recognitions* yields ***Profile Semantics*** ([ProfileSemantics](0208-profile-semantics.md)).

This composition restricts the algebraic laws to the profile recognitions, so *Profile Semantics* is valid.
