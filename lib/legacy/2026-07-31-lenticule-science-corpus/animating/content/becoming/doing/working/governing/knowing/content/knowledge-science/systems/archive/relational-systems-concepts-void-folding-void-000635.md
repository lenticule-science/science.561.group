# Folding Void

*Story.*  Nothing recognizes that Witness observes Something but leaves the mirrored binding ambiguous.  Folding that binding through itself induces Void.


Void = (Nothing Nothing)


*Proof Sketch.*  The only unresolved aspect of Nothing is the symmetry between its two uses of Witness and Something.  Re-applying Nothing to itself forces that symmetry to close, yielding Void.
