**Research certification** is idempotent stabilization of a research act. A
research act `r : X -> C Y` is certified when it factors through a active
`C`-coalgebra on `Y`, equivalently when its modal residual has vanished:

```text
C r ~= r
```

Certification is therefore not external approval added after the fact. It is the
internal proof condition that the act has become stable under the closure it
invokes.

## What generates it

Certification is generated by [research iteration](../research-iteration/research-iteration.md).
Only an act that can be iterated can show that further closure no longer changes
it. The archive states this as the equivalence between on-shell idempotence,
factorization through a `C`-coalgebra, and closure under composition with other
certified cells.

## What it generates

Certified research cells compose with other certified research cells. That
closure is what lets a research program become more than a list of results: it
becomes a space of stable forms, and so generates the
[witness topos](../witness-topos/witness-topos.md).
