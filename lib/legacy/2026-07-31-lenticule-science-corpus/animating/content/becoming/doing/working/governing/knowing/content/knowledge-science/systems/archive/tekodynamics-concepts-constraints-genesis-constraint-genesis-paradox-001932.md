The ***constraint genesis paradox*** arises when [[constraint]] (`C`) is [[induce|induced]] by [[exclusion]] (`∅`), yet only validated via [[completion]] (`Ω`).

> `∅ ⊢ C`
> `Ω ⊣ C`
> `⇒ C ⊂ Ω`
> `∧ Ω ⊂ C`

That is, constraint exists only if it is already complete, yet completion presupposes constraint — a circularity that cannot resolve from within.
