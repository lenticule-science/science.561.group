**Study and use** is the peripheral branch generated after a research spine is
stable enough to be consumed. It collects applications, refinements, and bridges
to outside literature whose results depend on the derivation but do not feed
back into the central proof tree.

This is not a junk drawer. By Curry-Howard directionality, a study-and-use leaf
must already inherit the research machinery it consumes. By Yoneda, it is known
by the arrows it offers back: decision procedures, alternate formulations,
refined invariants, and literature connections.

## What generates it

Study and use is generated by researching when the derivation has stable
machinery available for downstream work. It is later than certification because
there must be something certified to consume; it is separate from the researching
morphism because using a theory is not the same as extending the theory.

## What it generates

It generates application chapters and refinement chapters. The archived examples
include modal calculus on bilattice fibers, distributed-systems applications,
paraconsistent variants, bigraded and bilattice-valued cohomology refinements,
and Verdier-duality work. Each belongs here only insofar as it consumes the
research spine rather than becoming the spine itself.
