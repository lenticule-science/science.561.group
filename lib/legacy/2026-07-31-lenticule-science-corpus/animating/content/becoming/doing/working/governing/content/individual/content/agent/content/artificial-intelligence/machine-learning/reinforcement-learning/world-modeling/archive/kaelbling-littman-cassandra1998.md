# Kaelbling, Littman, Cassandra 1998 — POMDPs

Kaelbling, L. P., Littman, M. L., & Cassandra, A. R. (1998).
Planning and Acting in Partially Observable Stochastic Domains.
*Artificial Intelligence*, 101(1-2), 99–134.

The standard reference for partially observable Markov decision
processes. Formalizes the framework — a state space hidden behind
an observation function $O(o \mid s)$ — that motivates the
distinction between latent state and observation in modern world
modeling. Develops the belief-state formulation, the value-
iteration algorithm for finite-state POMDPs, and the
intractability results that motivate approximate methods.
