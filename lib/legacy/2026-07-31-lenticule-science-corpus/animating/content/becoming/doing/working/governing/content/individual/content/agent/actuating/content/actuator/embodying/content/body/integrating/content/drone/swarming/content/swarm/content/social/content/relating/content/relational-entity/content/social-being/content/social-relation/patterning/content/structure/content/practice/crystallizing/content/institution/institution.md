# institution

A stable, organized pattern of practices that persists beyond the individuals who fill it and structures how they act in a domain — hospitals, courts, marriage, peer review (archive: sociology/terms/institution).
Generated by: **practice** (crystallizing — practices that recur and outlast their practitioners).
Institutions don't only constrain; they *produce the categories* through which people understand themselves (Foucault: the site where power/knowledge operates).
Generates: **role**, **office**.
