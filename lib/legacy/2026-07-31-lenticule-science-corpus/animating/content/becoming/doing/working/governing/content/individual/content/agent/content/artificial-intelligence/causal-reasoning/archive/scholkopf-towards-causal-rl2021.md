# Schölkopf et al., "Toward Causal Representation Learning" (2021)

Position paper laying out the program of causal
representation learning.

## What it argues

- Standard ML representations capture statistical
  features; causal-aware applications need
  representations tied to causal factors.
- Causal representations would be:
  - Robust under intervention.
  - Transferable across environments.
  - Manipulable by intervention.
  - Compositional.
- Identifiability theory and algorithm development
  are needed; identifies open problems.
- Lays out research directions: independent causal
  mechanisms, multi-environment training,
  intervention-aware learning.

## Why it matters

Defining statement of the causal-representation-
learning agenda. Connects Pearl-style causality with
deep representation learning. Influential in shaping
post-2021 research direction.

## How to read

Position paper, not technical treatise. Read for
framing and directions, not detailed methods.
