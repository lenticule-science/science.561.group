**Genre coherence** is the structural pressure to keep the cast of social-narrative folk types active once [genring](genring.md) has produced them.
When a nation or social formation imagines itself through a set of genre figures — the Noble Indian, the Hearty Bavarian, the Salt-of-the-Earth Farmer, the Silicon Valley Founder — the integrity of its story depends on those characters remaining as assigned.
Anyone who does not fit the genre becomes a problem for the story: not necessarily an ideological enemy, but a *narrative disruption* — a character who breaks the fourth wall.
A subject who occupies a genre figure's social position but whose actual attributes deviate from the canonical assignment is both confirming the genre and denying it.
Genre coherence is the pressure that resolves this tension — either by correcting the deviant subject or by excluding them from the figure's membership.

## Mechanism of enforcement

Genre coherence enforces itself through social affect rather than explicit rule.
The feeling that something is "wrong" when a genred person deviates from type — the sense of inauthenticity, the discomfort with a Lakota mathematician or a Kurdish anarchist feminist revolutionary — is genre coherence operating as distributed social correction.
No coordinator is required.
The affect is produced by the structural mismatch between the subject's actual predicate-fiber values and the genre-canonical assignment; it propagates through the same [genre calibration](../morphisms/genre-calibration.md) feedback loops that maintain the genre's affective stickiness.

The mechanism connects genring to exclusion.
Genring produces the genre figures; genre coherence is the ongoing pressure to keep them stable.
The severity of the exclusion — social marginalization, legal disenfranchisement, physical elimination — depends on circumstances.
The structural pressure itself is present wherever genre figures are operative as the narrative units of a social formation.

## Relation to fascist grammar

Griffin (1991) identifies *palingenetic ultranationalism* — the mythic narrative of national rebirth from decadence — as fascism's structural constant.
That narrative requires a stable cast of authentic folk types who have been corrupted and who will be reborn.
Genre coherence is what makes this cast load-bearing: the rebirth story cannot function if the folk types deviate from their assigned attributes, because the deviation destabilizes the narrative's account of what was pure before corruption and what will be restored.
Genre coherence is therefore a necessary component of [fascist grammar](../morphisms/fascist-grammar.md): without a stabilized community stage, the sequence cannot proceed.

## Heteroglossia as formal contrast

Bakhtin (1981) names the alternative to genre coherence *heteroglossia*: the multiplicity of voices, genres, and subject-positions that any living social formation contains and that the monologic pressure of genre coherence suppresses.
Heteroglossia is the condition in which genre-canonical attributions are held open — the community's self-description is a site of ongoing negotiation rather than narrative stabilization.
Genre coherence is what converts heteroglossic openness into monological closure. ---
