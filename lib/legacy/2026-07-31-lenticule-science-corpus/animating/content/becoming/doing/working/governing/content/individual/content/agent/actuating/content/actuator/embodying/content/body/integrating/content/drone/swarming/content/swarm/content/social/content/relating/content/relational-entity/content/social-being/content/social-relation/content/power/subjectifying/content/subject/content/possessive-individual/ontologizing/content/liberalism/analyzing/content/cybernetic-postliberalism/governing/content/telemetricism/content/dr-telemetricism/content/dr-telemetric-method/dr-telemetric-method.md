A ***telemetric method*** is a structured behavior that engages a [telemetric signal|signal](telemetric signal|signal) within a [telemetric field|field](telemetric field|field) to produce a [telemetric yield|yield](telemetric yield|yield).
It is not a tool, not a plan, and not a function.
It is a ***relational gesture***—a repeatable, role-performing structure used in casting.

Methods may include writing, diagramming, slowing, refusal, tracing, witnessing, crafting, farming, co-voicing, etc. A method becomes telemetric when:

- It accepts signals without asserting their meaning
- It operates situationally, not universally
- It responds with coherence, not conclusion

Methods do not resolve contradiction.
They metabolize it.

A method is not *what you do to the signal*.
It is *how the system agrees to engage it*.
