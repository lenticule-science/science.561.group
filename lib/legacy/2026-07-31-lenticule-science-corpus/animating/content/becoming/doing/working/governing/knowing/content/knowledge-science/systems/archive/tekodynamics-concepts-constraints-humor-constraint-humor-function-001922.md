- `x, x′ ∈ dom(𝒮)` be stabilized symbolic expressions

> ℍ(x, x′) := ∆[s](x, x′) ∧ Ξ(x, x′) ∧ Μ(x) ∧ Μ(x′) ∧ Φ(x ∧ x′) ≠ 0

That is, humor emerges when two stabilized myths are semantically distinct, cohere without collapse, and generate interference at their overlap.
