Durkheim's first major work and the companion to *The Rules of Sociological Method* (Durkheim 1895): the dissertation that introduced the distinction between *mechanical solidarity* (cohesion through shared sentiments and a strong collective conscience, characteristic of segmentary societies) and *organic solidarity* (cohesion through functional interdependence in a differentiated division of labor, characteristic of modern societies).
The argument grounds the analytical pair *anomie* and *integration* that runs through Durkheim's later work on suicide and on religion, and is a load-bearing prior for the entire functionalist tradition in sociology.
