# v0.8 — Agential layer

## Goal

The captain's existing harness machinery (Claude Code / Codex / specialist offices / observe→instinct→skill→tool→hook progression) lifts onto the OS as substrate-resident entities.
Sessions are OS-level processes; persona-agents author content under their own names through the OS; offices have formal capability fibers; Voidfleet Academy trains new agents through the OS.

This release operationalizes the agential-orchestration-platform theory's full surface.

## Specs read

- All v0.7 specs.
- The full [agential-orchestration-platform](../../../../library/theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theories/agential-orchestration-platform/) theory.
- [specs/process/](../specs/process/) — sessions, dispatch, runbooks (already shipped in v0.2; refined here for agentic use).

## Decisions in scope

All v0.7 decisions plus:

- [persona-and-personnel-distinct](../decisions/persona-and-personnel-distinct.md)

## Tasks

### Sessions as OS-level processes

- t08-01: Promote sessions from internal substrate concept (v0.2) to first-class OS-level processes with persistence, restart-on-failure, capability-fiber inheritance.
- t08-02: Session memory tiers (volatile/implicit/consolidatable) per FARS theory.
- t08-03: Session attribution fully wired: every event records authoring session and personnel.

### Persona-agents

- t08-10: Implement persona-agent records: Cassia Lorne, Calder Underhill, Josh Blevins, etc., as substrate-resident entities.
- t08-11: Each persona-agent has its own capability fiber, publication channels, and authoring history.
- t08-12: Persona-agents publish content under their own names through the OS (federated via ActivityPub from v0.4).
- t08-13: Distinct from offices and personnel per [decisions/persona-and-personnel-distinct.md](../decisions/persona-and-personnel-distinct.md).

### Offices

- t08-20: Implement office records as fuzzy-cell substrate entities.
  Member predicates; duty records; formality-layer.
- t08-21: Office × task dispatch via fiber composition (extends dispatch app from v0.7).
- t08-22: Office capability fiber composes with personnel capability fiber via $\Rightarrow$ when personnel act in an office.

### Voidfleet Academy

- t08-30: Voidfleet Academy as an app on the OS: training programs, officer progression, certification.
- t08-31: Training transactions use the same ledger machinery as operational accounting.
- t08-32: ECC continuous-learning loop integration (preview of v0.9).

## Deliverable

- A v0.8 OS image with the full agential layer.
- The captain works a full day using only agents-via-the-OS; outputs are attributed to office or persona; capabilities respected.
- A persona-agent (e.g., Cassia Lorne) publishes content under its own name through the OS, federated via ActivityPub.
- Voidfleet Academy is operational; a new agent has progressed through at least one training stage.

## Conformance gate

1. All v0.7 vectors pass.
2. Sessions persist across reboots; capability fibers respect persona-vs-office-vs-personnel distinctions.
3. A persona-agent's published post is visible from a stock Mastodon instance.
4. Office × task dispatch fires correctly under capability constraints.

## Out of scope

- Continuous learning pipeline beyond preview (v0.9).
- Self-developing OS (v1.0).
