# Aboutness (semiotic reading)

The *semiotic reading of what an artifact is about*: an artifact is about an entity $e$ when the artifact participates as a sign-vehicle in [sign-relations](sign-relation.md) whose [sign-object](sign-object.md) is $e$, possibly via the [interpretant](interpretant.md) chains the artifact's [code](code.md) licenses.

This concept is the semiotic-side counterpart of the substrate's [aboutness](../../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/aboutness.md), and the bridge between them is the discipline's load-bearing self-application: an artifact's *predication-participation* (substrate side) and its *sign-relation-participation* (semiotic side) are the same structural relation read in two registers. The substrate-side reading was authored against Topic Maps' subject-identifier discipline; the semiotic-side reading is authored against Eco's encyclopedia (per [Eco 1976](references/eco1976.md) §2.7) and Peirce's triadic sign.

Three operational consequences mirror the substrate-side aboutness:

- **Aboutness is multi-valued.** An artifact can be clearly about, clearly not about, ambiguously about (multiple incompatible readings), or under-determined about an entity. The bilattice-valued substrate reading directly absorbs this.
- **Aboutness is community-relative.** The same sign-vehicle is about different things in different [semiospheres](semiosphere.md); the substrate's per-scope reading directly absorbs this.
- **Aboutness is closed under the interpretant chain.** An artifact about $e$ is also about every $e'$ that the code's interpretant chain reaches from $e$. The chain's bound (per [unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint](../theorems/unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint.md)) determines how far aboutness propagates.

The two aboutness concepts are deliberate doubles: one sits in the predicated-knowledge-systems theory, one sits here, and the [composability narrative](teaching/explanations/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md) records that they are the same structural fact under two presentations rather than two separate concepts. Editing either should consult the other.
