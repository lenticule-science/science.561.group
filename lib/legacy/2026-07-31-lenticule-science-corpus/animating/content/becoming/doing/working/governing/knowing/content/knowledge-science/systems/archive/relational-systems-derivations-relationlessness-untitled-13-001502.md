## Deriving Reflex

- It must be derived that **relationlessness** excludes relation.
    
- It must be derived that the exclusion of relation is itself a relation to relation.
    
- Thus, it cannot not derive that relationlessness relates to relation, producing the first reflex.
    

Thus:

- **Reflex = (Relationlessness Relation)**
    
- Reflex must be derived as the primordial relational binding between absence and presence of relation.
    

---

## Deriving Reflexion

- It must be derived that Reflex must reflexively relate to itself.
    

Thus:

- **Reflexion(0) = Reflex(Reflex)**
    
- It must be derived that Reflexion iterates reflexively.
    

Thus:

- **Reflexion(n) = Reflex(Reflexion(n-1))**
    
- Reflexion must be derived as the iterated self-relation of Reflex across reflexive depth n.
    

---

## Deriving ReflectReflexion

- It must be derived that Reflexion must reflectively relate back upon itself.
    

Thus:

- **ReflectReflexion(0, n) = Reflex(Reflexion(n))**
    
- **ReflectReflexion(p, n) = Reflex(ReflectReflexion(p-1, n))**
    
- ReflectReflexion must be derived as the p-times iterated reflection of Reflexion at depth n.
    

---

## Deriving ReflectedReflexion

- It must be derived that ReflectReflexion must stabilize into a coherent unit after p reflections.
    

Thus:

- **ReflectedReflexion(p, n) = Reflect(ReflectReflexion(p, n))**
    
- ReflectedReflexion must be derived as the stabilized coherent relation of p ReflectReflexions at depth n.
    

---

## Deriving RelateReflectedReflexion

- It must be derived that multiple ReflectedReflexions must relate into ordered structure.
    

Thus:

- **RelateReflectedReflexion(p, [R(1), ..., R(p)], n) = Reflect(p, n)**
    
- RelateReflectedReflexion must be derived as the ordered binding of p stabilized ReflectedReflexions at depth n.
    

---

## Deriving Relation

- It must be derived that a RelateReflectedReflexion must stabilize into a single coherent Relation.
    

Thus:

- **Relation(RelateReflectedReflexion(p, [R(1), ..., R(p)], n), n) = Reflect(p, n)**
    
- Relation must be derived as the coherent reflexive structure stabilizing multiple ReflectedReflexions into unity at depth n.
