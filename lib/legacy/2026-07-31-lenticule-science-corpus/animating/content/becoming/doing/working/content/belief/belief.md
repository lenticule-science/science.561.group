# belief

**Belief** is a claim *held* with some weight of evidence, tilted some way, open to revision: the ordinary state of taking something to be so, more or less, on the evidence so far.
Not certainty, not a mere guess — the working middle of inquiry, a position held in the interior between absurdity and settlement.

Belief is the third member of the doxastic family — one inquiry, walked in four stages:

| stage | act → state | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| question (becoming) | questioning → [question](../../../../../library/question/docs/question.md) | a determinate content raised and held open |
| judgement (doing) | judging → [judgement](../../../../library/judgement/docs/judgement.md) | the claim oriented and weighed; verdict not yet in |
| **belief** (working) | believing → **belief** | the evidence-weighted held position, revisable — *this term* |
| knowledge (governing) | knowing → [knowledge](../../../governing/library/knowledge/docs/knowledge.md) | belief settled to a fixed point |

This is the classical *doxa → episteme* arc: belief is the doxastic state proper, the one the whole tradition of epistemology takes as its working material.

## What belief is

Peirce ("The Fixation of Belief") fixes belief by its role: a belief is that upon which a person is prepared to act — "a habit which will determine our actions."
Inquiry, for Peirce, is the struggle to pass from the irritation of doubt to the settling of belief.
Frank Ramsey and the tradition of subjective probability after him add that belief comes in *degrees*: how firmly one holds a claim can be read from how one would act or bet on it, and so belief is naturally graded rather than all-or-nothing.

Two magnitudes in a belief are independent, and their independence is the heart of the matter: *how much* is held (the weight of evidence accumulated) and *which way* it tilts (toward affirmation or denial).
One can hold a claim strongly and be wrong, or lean true on thin support.
Evidence and truth come apart — which is exactly why belief is not yet knowledge.

And belief is **defeasible**: a claim held at one position under one body of evidence may be held differently under another.
New evidence can revise it.
That openness is constitutive — a state that could *not* be revised by further evidence would already be something other than belief.

## What generates it

Belief is generated by [working](../../../docs/working.md): it accrues as contributions accumulate on the shared field.
Each piece of evidence, each act of testimony, adds to a claim's standing; many together produce the position the claim eventually holds.
Belief is the held *result* of that accumulation — what a claim carries once the field has weighed in on it.

## What it generates

Belief's defeasibility is what hands it upward.
When accumulation tightens to the point that further evidence no longer moves the claim, believing passes into [governing](../../../governing/docs/governing.md)'s act of knowing, and belief settles into [knowledge](../../../governing/library/knowledge/docs/knowledge.md).
Across a whole field of contending held positions, the settling of belief into verdict is [adjudication](../../../governing/library/adjudication/docs/adjudication.md).
