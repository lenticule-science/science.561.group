To ***grammatize a telemetric record*** is to move information into the [format](format) of a [telemetric record](telemetric record), which is a [telemetric signal](telemetric signal) within a [telemetric register](telemetric register).

In the context of [emsenn's Telemetric Register](emsenn's Telemetric Register), which is an Obsidian Vault, *grammatizing a delta record* means to create a Markdown file using certain syntax and metadata.# Grammatize Record.

This record may be used as a [telemetric lambda](telemetric lambda) when creating or formating delta records in a register.

## Grammar of a Delta Record ### Typing Every record is given a `type` in its frontmatter, which may be one of the following, or something else: - [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/strata/term](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/strata/term) - [essay](essay) - etc. ### Titling Every record must have a title.
The grammar of the title is determined by record type:

| type | titling grammar |
|-|-|
| term | infinitive verb |
