Predicated sociology is an analytical framework for understanding how liberal governance reproduces itself under contemporary conditions — specifically under conditions of digital platforms, declining net energy, and post-institutional subject-formation.
It synthesizes cybernetic systems theory, cultural sociology, settler-colonial analysis, and political economy to describe a circuit that no single-field analysis has previously captured whole.

The circuit: structural contradictions in liberal political philosophy → formatted through platforms as personally manageable affect → metabolized by subjects performing self-regulation → whose self-regulation constitutes the governance mechanism that maintains the apparatus.

## The central operation

[Californication](../teaching/explanations/californication.md) names the central operation: the systematic formatting of structural contradictions as personally manageable affect, such that subjects' self-regulation manages contradictions the system cannot resolve.
The contradictions are not hidden.
The apparatus is transparent about climate collapse, inequality, democratic dysfunction, and mental-health crisis.
What the apparatus provides is not information management but *affect management*: a market of genres through which subjects can experience the contradictions as personally meaningful, personally significant, and personally addressable — through choices, practices, positions, and identities.

Californication is not propaganda.
Propaganda argues that the condition is better than it appears.
Californication formats the subject's *response* to the condition.
Whether the condition is acknowledged as bad, very bad, or catastrophic is secondary; the primary operation is the formatting of the response.
The subject who meditates through ecological collapse, journals through institutional breakdown, and advocates earnestly through platformal channels is doing what the apparatus requires, regardless of whether their beliefs about the severity of the conditions are accurate.

## The subject-formation operations

[Genring](../teaching/explanations/genring.md) and [ontogenring](../teaching/explanations/ontogenring.md) are the subject-formation operations through which californication operates.

Genring is the reduction of a living people to a fixed literary type — the production of a genre figure that stands for the people in the apparatus's imaginary.
Genring operates at the population level: it produces the Noble Indian, the Mountain Kurd, the Authentic Peasant.
It operates by selecting specific features for their narrative utility, assembling them into a template, and circulating the template until it substitutes for the people.
The [temporal middle](../teaching/explanations/temporal-middle.md) — the position every living person actually occupies, inheriting, continuing, and transforming a tradition — is what genring systematically eliminates.

Ontogenring is the internalized counterpart: the process by which subjects adopt genres of selfhood and inhabit them as forms of life.
The apparatus provides a market of self-genres — wellness-self, activist-self, productive-self, resilient-self — through [socially ontogenic media](../teaching/explanations/socially-ontogenic-media.md).
Each genre provides feeling rules, recognized practices, and community membership.
Each genre metabolizes structural contradiction rather than addressing it.

## The five subject-formations

The apparatus's operation produces five principal subject-formations:

The [savior-slave subject](../teaching/explanations/savior-slave-subject.md) is simultaneously mandated to manage global crises and structurally incapable of transforming the conditions producing them.
This is the foundational position.

The [neurotic platformal intellectual](../teaching/explanations/neurotic-platformal-intellectual.md) is the savior-slave subject operating through platform-intellectual performance — navigating dual-audience pressure, performing expertise under uncertainty, and doing emotional labor at scale.

[Zen fascism](../teaching/explanations/zen-fascism.md) is the affective-governance mechanism that enforces which affects are genre-correct — pathologizing distress, eroticizing detachment, and locating authority in serenity rather than in truth.

[Harm governance](../teaching/explanations/harm-governance.md) is the collapse of care into risk management — the private reproduction of the state's administrative logic by people who have lost faith in state institutions.

[Coherent confusion](../teaching/explanations/coherent-confusion.md) is the transversal condition within which the other formations operate — the state of being confused but functional, sustained by the apparatus's continuous production of just-resolvable confusion.

## The apparatus's architecture

The apparatus operates through three interlocking structures:

**The platform** as feedback architecture in the cybernetic sense — a Viable System Model implementation that maintains stability through nested feedback loops across multiple timescales, uses requisite variety to match subjects' varied conditions, and extracts behavioral surplus from subjects' interactions.

**The energetic substrate** as material foundation — the declining net-energy surplus that drove the institutional thinning and platformalization that characterizes the contemporary apparatus, distinguishing it from prior instantiations of the same operational circuit.

**The settler-colonial structure** as territorial and historical ground — the elimination operation that made California available as a laboratory, that continues to operate in the apparatus's [crisis settlerism](../teaching/explanations/crisis-settlerism.md), and that is embedded in the subject-formations the apparatus produces.

## The closure claim

The apparatus is operationally closed in the cybernetic sense.
No coherent subject-position — no political identity, lifestyle choice, spiritual practice, or analytical framework that can be articulated within the apparatus's discursive infrastructure — produces an outside.
The apparatus's feedback architecture metabolizes coherent subject-positions as content, genres, and engagement, sustaining the apparatus regardless of the content's critical intent.

This is a structural finding, not a verdict.
The closure does not preclude all practice; it specifies the structural conditions that practice must engage with to operate otherwise than as apparatus-sustaining content.
What practices might survive the metabolism — practices that do not produce engagement, relational practices that resist legibility, ancestral and ceremonial obligations that operate outside the platform's readability — is the open question the theory holds.

## What the theory is for

Predicated sociology is analytical.
It does not produce prescriptions.
But precision has its own use: it lets those who want to act differently do so with greater structural understanding of what the apparatus is doing, how it operates, and where its structural weaknesses might lie.
The precision is a contribution to practice, even though it is not itself practice.
