The julenek (Christmas sheaf) is a Norwegian tradition of hanging oats or wheat for birds at Christmas.
It tracks through three historical phases: pre-Christian harvest offering (associated with Freya and fertility), Christian reinterpretation as charity toward creation, and modern folk tradition.

The concept is interesting as a case study in cultural continuity through religious transformation — the same material practice persists while its meaning is reinterpreted within successive worldviews.
