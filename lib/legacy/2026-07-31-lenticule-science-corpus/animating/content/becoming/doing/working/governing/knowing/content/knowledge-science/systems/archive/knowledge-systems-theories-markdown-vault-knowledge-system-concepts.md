# Concepts

The parent theory commits to five things. Each is recorded here as a concept in its own right, because each is a load-bearing commitment that more specific theories (such as the [predicated markdown-vault knowledge system](theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/_index.md)) inherit and build on.

These concepts are deliberately *pre-predication*. They describe what any markdown-vault knowledge system is committed to, regardless of whether the vault layers a predication discipline on top.

## The five commitments

- [files-are-source.md](files-are-source.md) — the canonical form of a note is its file on disk; everything else is derived.
- [filenames-are-active-identifiers.md](filenames-are-active-identifiers.md) — a note's slug is its identity within its directory; renaming is editorial.
- [frontmatter-is-structured-data.md](frontmatter-is-structured-data.md) — anything to be queried, sorted, rendered, or validated lives in YAML frontmatter; body text is for prose.
- [plain-text-first.md](plain-text-first.md) — a vault must be useful with no tooling beyond a text editor and `grep`; tooling adds power, not access.
- [directory-tree-carries-meaning.md](directory-tree-carries-meaning.md) — where a file lives is part of what it says.

## Adjacent

- [vault.md](vault.md) — the artifact itself: the directory tree of markdown files that the five commitments collectively describe.
