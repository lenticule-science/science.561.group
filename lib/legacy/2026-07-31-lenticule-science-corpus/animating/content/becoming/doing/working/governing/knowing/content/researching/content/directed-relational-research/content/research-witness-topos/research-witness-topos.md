The **research witness topos** is the cohesive field generated by the category of
directed research cells.

## Definition

Let:

```text
R := Research(C)
```

Define:

```text
DRR_* := Shv(R^op, Spaces)
```

This is the witness topos of directed relational research.

## Proposition: the witness topos realizes research by its arrows

The Yoneda embedding sends each research cell into the sheaf topos as the
representable witness of its incoming and outgoing relations. Certified cells
appear as stable sections or points because they factor through coalgebras.

## Proof

Yoneda embeds `R` fully and faithfully into presheaves on `R^op`. Sheafification
adds the gluing conditions needed for local research witnesses to cohere across
overlapping contexts. Because certified cells are stable under composition, they
define the stable local data that can be glued. Thus the witness topos is not an
external archive of the research program; it is the categorical space generated
by the research program's own arrows.

When this space supports a strict advance in settled predicates, it can induce a
[researching morphism](../../../researching-morphism/researching-morphism.md) of
the host theory.
