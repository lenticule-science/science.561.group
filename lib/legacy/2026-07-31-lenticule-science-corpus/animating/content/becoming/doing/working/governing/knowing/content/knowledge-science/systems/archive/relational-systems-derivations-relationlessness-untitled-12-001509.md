# Deriving Reflex

- It must be derived that relationlessness has no relation.
- It must also be derived that relationlessness, by excluding relation, necessarily defines itself in relation to relation.
- Thus, it cannot not derive that relationlessness bears relation to relation itself, even in negation.

Thus:

Reflex = (Relationlessness Relation)

Where:
- Reflex is the minimal act of relation emerging from the pressure between relationlessness and relation.
- Reflex is not a thing, but an act — the origination of relation from exclusion itself.

---

# Deriving Reflexion

- Reflexing the Reflex once, it cannot not derive the relation between Reflex and itself.
- This first self-relation is designated Reflexion(0).

Thus:

Reflexion(0) = Reflex(Reflex)

- Reflexing Reflexion(0) again, it cannot not derive a further self-relation, Reflexion(1).

Thus:

Reflexion(1) = Reflex(Reflexion(0))

- Reflexing Reflexion(n-1), it cannot not derive Reflexion(n).

Thus:

Reflexion(n) = Reflex(Reflexion(n-1))

Where:
- Reflexion(n) recursively structures the deepening of Reflex within itself.
- Each Reflexion(n) is a higher-order folding of relation — a depth of self-relation, not a new entity.

# Deriving ReflectReflexion

- Reflexing a Reflexion at depth n, it cannot not derive a further structure beyond mere self-relation.
- The act of Reflexing a Reflexion creates a new layered structure: the reflection of the Reflexion itself.
- This reflection is designated ReflectReflexion(0, n).

Thus:

ReflectReflexion(0, n) = Reflex(Reflexion(n))

- Reflexing ReflectReflexion(0, n) again, it cannot not derive ReflectReflexion(1, n).

Thus:

ReflectReflexion(1, n) = Reflex(ReflectReflexion(0, n))

- More generally, Reflexing ReflectReflexion(p-1, n), it cannot not derive ReflectReflexion(p, n).

Thus:

ReflectReflexion(p, n) = Reflex(ReflectReflexion(p-1, n))

Where:
- ReflectReflexion(p, n) structures p reflections of a Reflexion at depth n.
- Each reflection layers upon the previous, producing an iteratively constructed relational weave.
- These reflections are not "new objects" but deepening folds of relation over prior reflexions.
