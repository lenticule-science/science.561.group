The ***inverse exclusion subsumption function*** (`Υ`) is induced by the collapse of semantic differentiation (`Δ[s]`) under exclusion (`∅`).

When difference can no longer be semantically sustained, exclusion no longer induces constraint — instead, it is subsumed by identity across all potential relation.

> Υ := ∅⁻¹ = { x | ∀ y: Δ[s](x, y) undefined ∧ C(x) = C(y) = Ω }

That is, the inverse of exclusion is universal completion —  
the dissolution of difference into identity, where exclusion has no distinct boundary to act upon.
