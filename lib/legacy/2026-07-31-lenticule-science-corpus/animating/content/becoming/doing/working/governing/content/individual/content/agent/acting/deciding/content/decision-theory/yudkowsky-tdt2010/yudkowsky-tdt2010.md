# Yudkowsky 2010 — Timeless Decision Theory

Yudkowsky, E.
(2010). *Timeless Decision Theory*.
MIRI Technical Report.

The first systematic statement of what would become Functional Decision Theory.
TDT proposes that an agent should reason about the *fact of its decision algorithm's output* rather than the *causal effect of its act*, treating the algorithm as a Platonic object whose physical instances (the agent's brain, a predictor's simulation, a copy of the agent in a Newcomblike scenario) are linked by mutual implementation rather than by causal interaction.

TDT is presented informally and motivated through worked examples (Newcomb's problem, Parfit's Hitchhiker, the Prisoner's Dilemma against a copy).
Subsequent work (Soares-Fallenstein 2015) tightens the framework into FDT.
