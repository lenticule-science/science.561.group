This program models metaphors and figurative language as recognitions to keep
their mappings and effects explicit.

## Behaviour

- Represent source/target mappings, frames, and figurative constructions as
  recognitions with context and provenance.
- Link metaphors to domains and discourse settings; track how they shape
  interpretation.
- Measure prevalence, spread, and shifts of figurative patterns.

## Intended operators

- Annotate text for metaphor/figurative structures.
- Compare metaphor usage across domains or audiences.
- Support generation/translation that respects figurative intent.
