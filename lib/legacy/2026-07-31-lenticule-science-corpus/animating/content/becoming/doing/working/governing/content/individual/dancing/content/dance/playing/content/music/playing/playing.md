# playing

**Playing** here is the live act that carries [music](../../docs/music.md) into [game](../library/game/docs/game.md).
It is the same hinge as the playing that took dance into music — play as the verb (Huizinga's *homo ludens*; the in-repo `…/game/library/vocabulary/library/play/play.md`) — now operating one turn further down: playing *the music*, which under structure settles into bounded, rule-governed play.

## Definition

Playing the music is the enactment of the shared pulse as live activity.
Recall the in-repo distinction: play "becomes a game when it acquires structure — rules, objectives, constraints — but the underlying quality of voluntary engagement persists" (`play.md`).
Playing is that persisting live engagement; it is the medium in which the open musical field can take on rules without ceasing to be play.
As before, playing is the **hinge**, not a face of its own — the doing that keeps the spiral in motion.

## Generated by

Playing is generated by **[music](../../docs/music.md)**: the shared pulse exists to be played in, danced to, joined.

## Generates

Playing generates **[game](../library/game/docs/game.md)**: when the play of the musical field acquires rules and bounds — Caillois's paidia hardening into ludus — it crystallizes into a game, the settled individual turn that closes (and re-seeds) the cycle.
