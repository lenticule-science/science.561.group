---
name: contractions-consistency
description: Enforce consistent contraction use in a piece. Use when writing with a friendly or informal tone.
---

- If you use contractions, use them consistently throughout the piece.
- Do not mix "can't" and "cannot" in the same piece.
- Avoid ambiguous contractions such as "there'd" and "it'll".
- Never form contractions from a noun and verb (e.g., "the client's arrived").
