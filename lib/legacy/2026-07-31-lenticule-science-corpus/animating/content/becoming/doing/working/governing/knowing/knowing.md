# knowing

**Knowing** is governing read as the settlement of claims.
Where governing closes a shared field into a bounded order, knowing closes the field of signs, evidence, memory, and testimony into claims that can be held, revised, taught, disputed, or acted from.

This makes knowing a collective fixed point before it is a private possession.
A knower is bounded by what their world lets them take as settled, and a world is bounded by the practices through which its claims can be learned, researched, reasoned over, and revised.

## What generates it

Knowing is generated by governing: it is the law-bearing closure of inquiry.
It is not mere attention, memory, or representation, though it may use each of those.
Its essence is the governed status of a claim: who may assert it, what counts as evidence for it, how disagreement is adjudicated, how revision is admitted, and when the result can stand.

## What it generates

Knowing generates knowledge as settled claim, learning as revision of the knower, researching as governed inquiry, thinking and reasoning as the internal operations of claim settlement, and cognition, perception, and neuroreflexivity as embodied ways a knower participates in that settlement.
