# Agentic Swarm Theory

Agentic swarm theory is the local study of agent populations whose global behavior is produced by bounded local action, rule-bearing observation, and auditable witness propagation rather than by a single central controller. In this library, the term names the bridge between classical swarm questions, multi-agent reinforcement learning, ruled multi-observer systems, and the control/governance requirements that appear in drone-swarm work.

The point is not merely that many agents act together. The point is that the population must remain legible as a system: local policies, closure rules, witness records, safe envelopes, and human authorization records have to compose into a global behavior that can be inspected after the fact and constrained before dispatch.

## Local Commitments

- A swarm is agentic when its members are not only particles or automata but action-bearing processes with policy, memory, witness, or role structure.
- A swarm is controllable only when its local decisions can be related to an admissible global envelope, not when a supervisor merely watches aggregate motion.
- A swarm is governable only when its observations and decisions leave witness records that can be traced through the archive of action.
- Learning systems and swarm systems meet at the deployment boundary: MARL can train local policies, while swarm theory asks whether the converged policies satisfy bounded local-rule and scalability premises.

## Relationship To Neighboring Terms

[[Witness Propagation]] supplies the audit discipline: observations and decisions have to travel as usable witnesses rather than as private agent state.

[[Context Spacesuit]] names the boundary equipment an agent needs in order to act inside a larger environment without smearing context across incompatible tasks.

[[Meaningful Human Control]] becomes the drone-swarm governance test: a swarm action is not meaningful merely because a human approved a mission; the action must preserve witness chains, safe-envelope checks, authorization records, and certifiability conditions.

[[Verified Safe Envelope]] is the control-side companion concept: the swarm remains admissible only while the action stays inside a prechecked safety region.
