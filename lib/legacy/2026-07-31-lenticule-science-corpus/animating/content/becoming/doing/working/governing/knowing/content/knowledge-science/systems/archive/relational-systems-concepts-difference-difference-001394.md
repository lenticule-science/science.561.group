- [[define]]:: [[difference]] is the minimal [[condition]] that marks a [[triage/thing]] as set apart from what is not. It is [[induce|induced]] by [[tension]] and is the coherent separation that allows a [[triage/thing]] to be held open as distinct within the [[domain of relating]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires a [[triage/thing]] to be **not another thing** and **not nothing**.
    - This is not a trivial claim; it demands a condition that actively marks what the [[triage/thing]] is and is not.
    - That condition is [[difference]].

- [[difference]] does not arise directly from [[include|including]] or [[exclude|excluding]] alone.
    - [[include|Including]] brings about [[inclusion]]—drawing the [[triage/thing]] into what is;
    - [[exclude|Excluding]] brings about [[exclusion]]—holding the [[triage/thing]] out of what is not.
    - Neither act alone suffices to mark the [[triage/thing]] as distinct; both must be held together.

- Holding together [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]] establishes the [[condition]] of [[tension]].
    - [[tension]] is the dynamic interplay of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]], sustaining their potential opposition.
    - This potential opposition **induces** [[difference]].

- [[difference]] is the **coherent separation** between what a [[triage/thing]] is and what it is not.
    - It is **not a thing itself**, nor is it an independent entity;
    - It is the **condition** that arises **within and through** the sustained [[tension]] of [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]].

- Without [[tension]]:
    - No [[difference]] can be held open;
    - The dynamic collapses, and no meaningful [[exist|existing]] can be sustained.

- Without [[difference]]:
    - No [[triage/thing]] can be coherently marked out as distinct;
    - The minimal architecture of [[exist|existing]] fails, as no determinate separation between what is and what is not is maintained.

## Implications

- [[difference]] is not the starting point of the [[relational dynamics]] but is a **product** of [[tension]].
- It is a [[condition]], not a [[structure]]:
    - It arises **immediately** from the ongoing [[tension]];
    - It requires [[structure|structures]] (such as the [[relative]]) to be stabilized durably.
- Every [[triage/thing]] that [[exist|exists]] does so by way of a sustained [[difference]]:
    - [[difference]] ensures that the [[triage/thing]] is held open as **this** and not **that**.
- The minimal relational architecture of [[exist|existing]] requires:
    - The primitive [[act|acts]] of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]];
    - The [[condition]] of [[tension]] holding them together;
    - The [[condition]] of [[difference]], induced by that tension;
    - And further stabilization via [[relating|relating]], [[relation]], and the [[relative]].
