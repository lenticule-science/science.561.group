# Garcez & Lamb, "Neurosymbolic AI: The 3rd Wave" (2022)

Position and survey paper on neurosymbolic AI.

## What it covers

- History of neurosymbolic research.
- Taxonomy of neurosymbolic architectures.
- Survey of contemporary methods.
- Argument that neurosymbolic represents a "third
  wave" of AI after symbolic and connectionist.
- Open challenges and research directions.

Authors are major figures in the neurosymbolic
research community.

## Why it matters

Frames the contemporary neurosymbolic agenda.
Provides architectural taxonomy used in subsequent
work. Useful overview for entering the field.

## How to read

Position paper/survey; readable. Good orientation to
neurosymbolic research.

## Related works

- Kautz 2022 — alternative architectural taxonomy.
- Various technical papers on specific methods.
