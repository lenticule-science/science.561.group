# auto-immunity

Generated by: biopower and immunization - the protection of life turning against the life it protects.

Auto-immunity names the reversal internal to protective power: the apparatus that secures a body, population, or polity can become the means by which that body is damaged.
Within a biopower spine, the concept marks a limit of governance by protection.
Life is defended through boundaries, exclusions, prophylaxis, and managed exposure; but those same operations can negate the relational conditions that make life livable.
