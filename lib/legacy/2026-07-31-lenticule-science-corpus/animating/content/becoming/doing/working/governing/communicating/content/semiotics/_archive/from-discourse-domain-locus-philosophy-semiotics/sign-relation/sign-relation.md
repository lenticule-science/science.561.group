# Sign-relation

The *triadic structure $(V, O, I)$ in which a sign-vehicle $V$ stands for an object $O$ for an interpretant $I$*, with the irreducibly third place that distinguishes semiotic standing-for from any dyadic relation.

The Peircean commitment is that the relation is *irreducibly triadic*: removing the interpretant collapses the structure into a name-and-thing pair (which is metaphysics, not semiotics), and removing the object collapses it into a name-and-mind pair (which is psychology, not semiotics). The three positions are not independent variables; they are the three roles a single relational structure occupies. Any analysis that reduces a sign-relation to two of its three places loses the structural content the discipline names.

The Saussurean reading takes a dyadic *signifier–signified* (per [signifier](../terms/signifier.md), [signified](../terms/signified.md)) and locates the third place implicitly in the *system of differences* the langue supplies; the structural content is the same once the system-side third place is made explicit. The Hjelmslevian reading splits each of the two Saussurean places into *form* and *substance*, giving four positions whose two-by-two tensor recovers a structurally enriched sign-relation. The Lotmanian reading places the sign-relation against the [semiosphere](semiosphere.md) so that the third place is the semiosphere's interpretive horizon.

A sign-relation's three positions admit substrate-level readings:

- $V$ ↔ a substrate cell's *predicate position* (per the [predicated-knowledge-system](../../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/objects/predicated-knowledge-system.md) substrate).
- $O$ ↔ a substrate cell's *subject and object positions*.
- $I$ ↔ the cell's placement in a *fiber* corresponding to a [discourse community](../../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/discourse-community-as-fiber.md), whose closure operator iterates the cell into further interpretants per [unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint](../theorems/unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint.md).

The bridge is the discipline's load-bearing self-application: predication is a special case of sign-relation, and so the substrate's $(s, p, o)$-cell triad is exactly the Peircean triad with the predicate playing the sign-vehicle role.
