An ***essay*** is (definition:: an [[artifact]] that results from [[writing]], usually a few hundred to several thousand [[word|words]], usually focused on one or just a few [[topic|topics]].)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:
```dataview
table definition
where file.name = "analytical essay" OR
  file.name = "argumentative essay" OR
  file.name = "comparative essay" OR
  file.name = "critical essay" OR
  file.name = "descriptive essay" OR
  file.name = "expository essay" OR
  file.name = "narrative essay" OR
  file.name = "reflective essay"
```


- [[expository essay|Expository essays]] are for [[informing]] people about a [[concept]] or [[topic]].
- [[descriptive essay|Descriptive essays]] are for [[describing]] a [[old-notes/domains/tekodynamics/glossary/thing]], often focusing on one [[quality]] or set of qualities.
- [[narrative essay|Narrative essays]] are for [[narrative storytelling]].
- [[argumentative essay|Argumentative essays]] are for [[arguing]] a [[truth]] about a [[old-notes/domains/tekodynamics/glossary/thing]].
- [[Analytical essay|Analytical essays]] are for [[zeds/Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating]] a [[concept]] or [[topic]] to [[event|events]] or [[information]].
- [[comparative essay|Comparative essays]] are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [[old-notes/domains/tekodynamics/glossary/thing|things]].
- [[critical essay|Critical essays]] apply a [[concept]] or [[synthesis]] of concepts to events or information.
- [[reflective essay|Reflective essays]] explore my [[relationship]] to a [[old-notes/domains/tekodynamics/glossary/thing]].

Check the [[Essay Catalog]] if you want to see all the essays that are a part of [[Groundhog Autonomous Zone|my virtual mycelium]].
