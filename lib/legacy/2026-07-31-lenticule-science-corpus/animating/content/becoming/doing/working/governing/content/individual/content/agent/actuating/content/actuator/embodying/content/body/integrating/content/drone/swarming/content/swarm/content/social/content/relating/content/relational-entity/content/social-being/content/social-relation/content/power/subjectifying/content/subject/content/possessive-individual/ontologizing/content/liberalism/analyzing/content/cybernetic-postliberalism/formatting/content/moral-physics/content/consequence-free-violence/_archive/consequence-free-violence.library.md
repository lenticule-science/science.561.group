**Consequence-free violence** is a mechanism within an artifact's moral physics: visually maximal harm staged with zero narrative residue.
A character is hit, exploded, dismembered, beaten, or killed; the next scene proceeds as if it did not happen.
The harm is not hidden or aestheticized — it is fully shown, often elaborately so.
What is suppressed is not the visibility of the violence but its *persistence* — its capacity to alter the world that follows from it.

The diagnostic feature is the **reset**.
A character who fell from a building and exploded into ash is upright in the next shot.
A city destroyed in the third act is rebuilt by the credits, or never spoken of again.
A casualty count goes up and the protagonist's mood does not change.
The scene of harm and the scene that follows are decoupled: the artifact's moral physics has decided that violence happens in a sealed compartment from which nothing leaks.

## Three mechanisms of the reset

**Bodies do not accumulate damage.** Each scene begins with the protagonist's body restored to its baseline.
Even when injuries are acknowledged, the acknowledgment is decorative: the limp does not constrain subsequent action.
The body is treated as a renewable resource rather than a finite one.

**Death is downgraded to absence.** Characters who die do not haunt the survivors.
Their absence is registered briefly and then the narrative continues as if the dead had left town.
The moral weight of a death lasts approximately as long as the scene in which it occurs.

**Catastrophe is reversible.** When harm reaches the level of disaster, the reset expands to fit: cities burn and are rebuilt; civilizations end and a new generation lives in the ruins without trauma.
The catastrophe registers as spectacle rather than as a permanent change in what the world can contain.

## Political effects

Consequence-free violence is not politically neutral.
A population trained on it imports its grammar into how they read off-screen events.

**Structural harm becomes hard to perceive.** A moral physics in which damage does not accumulate trains subjects in a worldview where each event begins fresh.
This makes structural harm — ongoing, compounding, intergenerational — difficult to register.
The structural register requires a moral physics where wounds carry forward; that physics has been eroded by entertainment that operates on resets.
The [savior-slave subject](savior-slave-subject.md) who cannot recognize cumulative injury as structural has been partly formed by this erosion.

**Reversibility expectations migrate from fiction to politics.** Subjects whose entertainment moral physics treats catastrophe as reversible carry that expectation into political reasoning.
Climate destabilization, mass incarceration, colonial displacement — these resist reversibility, but the expectation that they should be reversible (and that the failure to reverse them is a failure of cleverness or will) is itself a product of the moral physics the subject has been trained in through [socially ontogenic media](socially-ontogenic-media.md).

**Harm-as-spectacle becomes the default register.** When images of actual war, atrocity, or disaster reach audiences, the moral physics of the entertainment they are accustomed to shapes how the images are processed.
The same audience that has consumed thousands of consequence-free explosions encounters images of actual destruction inside a frame that has already established what destruction *is for*: it is something you watch.

## Relation to rubber-hose realism

[Rubber-hose realism](rubber-hose-realism.md) is the moral-physics regime in which consequence-free violence is the dominant mechanism.
The rubber-hose animation style (Fleischer, early Disney) is the explicit origin: limbs bent without joints, bodies stretched, flattened, and reassembled within seconds.
That technical convention is the most explicit instantiation, but the mechanism extends far beyond cartoons into live-action film, video games, superhero narrative, and certain genres of news coverage.
Rubber-hose realism is the full regime; consequence-free violence is its operative mechanism. ---
