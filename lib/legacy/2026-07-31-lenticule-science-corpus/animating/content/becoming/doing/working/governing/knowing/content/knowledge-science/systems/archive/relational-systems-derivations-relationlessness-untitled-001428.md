- [[postulate]]:: Multiple [[oldreflect]](p, n) at the same reflexive depth n cannot not compose an ordered relational structure, because they arise from stabilization of reflexions at the same layer of reflexive depth.
- [[derivation]]:: Stabilized reflects naturally induce ordered bundling: a grouping into coherent, relational composites. Composition is thus the necessary structuring of multiple stabilized reflections at the same reflexive depth.
- [[example]]::
  - A single [[oldreflect]](1, n) is trivially composed.
  - Two [[oldreflect]](1, n) and [[oldreflect]](1, n) must compose into [[relative]](2, [reflect(1, n), reflect(1, n)], n).
  - Generally, composing p stabilized reflexions gives a relative structure of arity p at depth n.
- [[relational expression]]:: [[relative]](p, [r₁, r₂, ..., rₚ], n) :: [[oldreflect]](p, n)
