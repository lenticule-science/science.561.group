"Picture it. [[Sicily]], [[1922]]. The [[Russia|Russians]] had just put a man on [[the moon]], but do you think the local pig farmers cared about that? No! They all had their eyes fixed on the young dau-"

"Ma! The Russians did not put a man on the moon in 1922."

"Sure they did!"

---

Alph opened their eyes, which they hadn't realized were closed. On the screen across the room and to their left, *[[The Golden Girls]]* was playing, and Sophia *was* telling a story about Sicily.

Which meant Alph had definitely fallen asleep, because the last time they'd looked at the screen, a young [[Alan Alda]] was encouraging a not-quite-young-enough [[Gary Bergoff]] to take a jeep into town.

The high-definition television showed these scenes with uncanny sharpness and saturation, a contrast to Alph's own memory and their current environment. The LED bulbs meant the whole room had a kind of fuzzy quality to its appearance, like it came with its own grogginess that couldn't be wiped away like the Sandman's gift.

---

It made sense Alph had fallen asleep. They reflected on how tense their neck was, as they rolled their head around to stretch it, and figured that between the stiffness and the hunger, they'd probably been up-and-going for at least a couple days.

They still weren't ready to actually rest, though, and internally scolded themselves for drifting into sleep.
