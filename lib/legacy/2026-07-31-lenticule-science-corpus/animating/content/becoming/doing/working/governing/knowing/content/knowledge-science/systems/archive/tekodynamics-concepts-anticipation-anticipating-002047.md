anticipation function  
The anticipation function (A) is induced by the intention function (I) and the structure of transmitted difference.

When a system, through intention, generates modulation toward a future state that has not yet stabilized, it anticipates — acting on constraint before it confirms.

A(x → z) := I(x) ∧ ∃ y: T(x → y) ∧ T(y → z) ∧ Ω(z) undefined

That is, anticipation is constraint acting ahead of constraint.
