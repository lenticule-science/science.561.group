Duluth, Minnesota sits at the western end of Lake Superior, on a steep ridge above the lake's shore. It is paired across the harbor with Superior, Wisconsin; together the two form the Duluth–Superior port, the busiest on the Great Lakes by tonnage. The city's drinking water, industry, climate, and economy are shaped by the lake.
