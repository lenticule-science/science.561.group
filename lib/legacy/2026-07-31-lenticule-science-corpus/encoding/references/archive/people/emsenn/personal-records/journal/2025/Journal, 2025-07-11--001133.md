One thing that keeps me looking toward [[Org-mode]] is how it makes it more possible to publish a space on the [[Web]] that is well-and-truly mine. However, this is in contrast to so much of what I've [[studying|studied]], about how the Web is now [[platformized]], and thus my [[knowledge production method]] needs to result in platformable [[post|posts]], not the development of a space. That is, rather than build a Website, I need to build something that lets me generate good posts for the specific platforms I'm on.

It might be useful to take some time today to work on an essay, [[Accepting the Web as a Platform, Not a Place]], that explains the historical shift and my relationship to it.

That would mean that [[my research notes]] are going to stay private, and instead the goal is create... not quite [[atomic]] bits of content, but bits of content that are translated out of this space and into the profile of whatever platform they're destined for.

Which I think is an idea I was [[developing]] when I was [[considering]] things like [[digital paraform|digital paraforms]] and how to use [[AI agent|AI agents]] in my work.

It's funny to think of what another person might think if they saw me doing all this work, to decide how to work, at this work that is entirely [[self-directing|self-directed]] and [[self-determining|self-determined]]. I've read books from other [[author|authors]] and while they all struggle some with [[writing technology]] and [[writing tool|tools]], none of them seem to have fallen into the professional trap of writing about writing about writing about.. ....

One thing that's a [[difficulty]] is that because of the complexity of the ideas, and the complexity being about how to shape ideas themselves, is that it can be easy to forget the values and decisions that led me to doing research how I am, and so I'll derive new values and decisions from that position, that just add [[complexity]].

The difficulty is resolving itself through the development of a class of files that list [[my ideal things]]: literally, files that describe how I've determined I think I'd like things to be. For example, I have [[[my ideal software]], which is for planning the software that actually resolves these difficulties about platforms and stuff. The trick is, I'm not sure how to describe this thing I'm looking for formulate. It's writing... it's also research. It's also propaganda, and publishing. It's also being an influencer (albeit an influencer of anarchist and indigenous radicalism, not consumerism (directly)). It's a few other things. It's vernacular autoethnosis, but it's also the production of stories from that. But specifically, the production of stories for communication into the world that encloses me, materially and ontologically. It is communication, that's accurate: but also imprecise; to call it "my ideal communication method" more readily implies it's about how to cant my accent depending on audience than how to craft novels and political discourse.

The closest [[verb]] I can find might be [[articulating]], but I think no one-word is going to capture what I mean. It may be that "my ideal liberalized self-performance" is the closest term; that phrasing has me wondering if the [[jargon]] of [[Judith Butler|Butler]] or [[bell hooks|hooks]] might be relevant. Thinking on it, we could talk about [[commodification]] or [[affecting]], but [[my ideal liberalized self-performance]] really seems to (at least in this moment) hit the key ideas I need to be [[containing|contained]] in the [[term]].

Which feels like a good step in doing this work: I now have two files, one for my ideal software and one for my ideal liberalized self-performance. The two serve as hubs for the ideas I've developed around them in [[my journal]] and other [[my writing|writing]], and the latter can help inform the former.

This is a pretty soft application of the type of [[deriving]] and [[determining]] I use in [[tekodynamics]], but even in its softness can help show the direction of future work:

If my software determines my writing, and my self-performance determines my software, what determines my self-performance?

(Also, I'm realizing it may be helpful to have hubs for [[my current software]] and [[my current liberalized self-performance]]. The page for [[my writing]] serves as a hub for my current writing, but begs for [[my ideal liberalized writing]]. And all of this implies other potential ideals, but are at present too murky to name: my ideal radical writing, perhaps? Insurgent writing? Perhaps both, or neither.)

Through developing this relational map - current practices and various ideals - I can help develop a sense of the [[tekic force]] that is coming from [[my positionality]], [[my values]], [[my desires]], into [[my researching]], [[my writing]], [[my self-performance]], and how I can guide that force toward [[ideal|ideals]] in a way that [[reinforcing|reinforces]] my values.

Connecting that to specific things: I need to set up [[my research notes]] so that there is an inflow of information (external news events, published papers, event logs), an interpretative layer (encyclopedia, journals, essays, world-building), and the communicative layer.

In more tekodynamic terms, I want to set it up so that destabilizing information is being captured into the structure of my notes, which, in attempting to cohere them, emit posts. I'm struggling to say just how I'm thinking of it, because these ideas are so interstitched and my language isn't quite firm yet, but, like: the posts essentially are waste byproducts of the research system, and the platforms are the environments in which I'm trying to process that pollution?

That isn't quite right, but it touches on something: there are specific flows that will emerge, where certain types of input end up resulting in certain types of posts, after certain workings through the research method.

Journal entries, like this one, are interesting, because they're both inputs and interpretative, but I imagine in practice a lot of things will serve multiple roles - posts already serve as references for future interpretation.
