** January 31st, 2021
*** Opening address

    Welcome to my office hours, y'all. My name is emsenn. Before I explain how this will work, let me begin by recognizing some things that I think are important.

    This is the 30th winter I've lived through. I grew up on the unceded traditional territory of the Shawnee, Miami, and Wyandotte Nations, among others, and I'm living today on the unceded traditional territory of the Shakori-Eno people, alongside Indigenous people from many Nations, tribes, and bands, as well as people brought here through diaspora, displacement, and settler-colonization. I was raised within settler-colonialism; my relationship to the Itazípčo band of the Lakota was hidden from my family until I was in my early 20s: my mom was stolen from her mother as an infant and relocated into a White Catholic household.

    Prior to knowing my relationship to the Lakota people, I'd spent several years working, in various roles, as an activist, both in anti-authoritarian insurgencies in other places, as well as against white supremacy and kyriarchism here on Turtle Island. Those actions caused me to be de-personed by the US government; I became regarded as a terrorist and that limited my abilities to continue my work.

    In the time since I've focused on developing my relationship with the Lakota and other Indigenous peoples, and re-teaching myself the radicalism I learned earlier in life through a decolonial lens.

    This has led to a skillset in urban direct action and land stewardship, which was almost entirely taught to me through the hard work of others, especially Black and Indigenous women, who have maintained these sacred stories for generations, through many atrocities.

    It's my hope in this time to share some of what I've learned with y'all. Please, submit your questions in the chat, and I'll get to them roughly in order. You don't need to have your mic or camera on to participate.
