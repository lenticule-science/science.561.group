# Relating

A conversation is not a thing that exists and then, separately, involves people talking. A conversation *is* the talking. If the turns stop being taken and understood, the conversation doesn't pause — it ends. Relating is this kind of activity: the ongoing work that holds things together.

In [relational dynamics](./relational-dynamics.md), relating is the core process. It is not a state but an activity — something that must keep happening for the thing it sustains to persist. The stone on the desk exists through the ongoing relating of gravity, friction, and surface. The school club exists through the ongoing relating of meetings called, rooms opened, members showing up. When the relating stops, the thing it held together comes apart.

This is a stronger claim than saying "things are connected." Connection implies entities that exist first and relate second. Relating reverses this: the entities are constituted by the relational activity. There is no stone-on-desk without the gravity-friction-surface relation. There is no club without the meeting-room-member relation. What exists, exists through relating.

In the [Interactive Semioverse](../../mathematics/objects/universes/interactive-semioverse/interactive-semioverse.md), relating corresponds to interaction: the evaluation of interaction terms against the semantic field. An interaction term that is evaluated produces semantic content (a generator in the Heyting algebra).
