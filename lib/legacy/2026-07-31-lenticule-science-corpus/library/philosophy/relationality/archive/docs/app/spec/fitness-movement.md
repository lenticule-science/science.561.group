# Idea: Fitness & Movement Tracking

Represent movement as recognitions to see patterns and plan sessions.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ActivityRecognition`: walks, workouts, stretches, chores-as-movement.
- `WorkoutPlanRecognition`: structured plans or programs.
- `LocationRecognition`: routes, gyms, home spaces.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Measurement:
  - duration, intensity, heart rate, perceived exertion, recovery.
- Topology:
  - activities ↔ locations ↔ projects (e.g., training for a hike).

Runbooks: `plan_session`, `log_activity`, `review_training_block`.
