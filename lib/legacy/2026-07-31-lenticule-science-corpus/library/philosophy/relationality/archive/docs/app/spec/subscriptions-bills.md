# Idea: Subscriptions & Bills

Track recurring financial obligations as recognitions.

## 1. Recognitions

- `SubscriptionRecognition`: service, cost, billing cycle, purpose.
- `BillRecognition`: individual bills/invoices.
- `RenewalEventRecognition`: renewals, cancellations, changes.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Measurement:
  - monthly/annual spend, changes over time, value vs usage.
- Topology:
  - subscriptions ↔ projects, people, accounts (ties into finance/budget).

Runbooks: `review_subscriptions`, `plan_cancellations`, `negotiate_bill`.
