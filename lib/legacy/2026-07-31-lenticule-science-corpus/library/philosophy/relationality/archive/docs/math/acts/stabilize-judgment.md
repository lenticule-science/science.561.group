## Interpretation

*Close Judgment Trace* shows that observationally aligned triadic judgments close, yet we still lack the identification collapsing judgments with the same closed behaviour.  Forcing *Recognize Judgment Triad* to witness *Close Judgment Trace* yields ***Stabilize Judgment*** ([StabilizeJudgment](stabilize-judgment.md)).

This composition identifies triadic judgments that close in the same way, so *Stabilize Judgment* follows directly from earned recognitions.
