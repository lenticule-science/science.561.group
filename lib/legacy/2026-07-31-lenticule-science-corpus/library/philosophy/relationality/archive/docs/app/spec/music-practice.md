# Idea: Music Practice & Composition

Treat music learning and composing as recognitions to understand and shape your
practice over time.

## 1. Recognitions

- `PieceRecognition`: works you’re studying or composing.
- `MotifRecognition`: small melodic/rhythmic/harmonic ideas.
- `PracticeSessionRecognition`: when/what/how you practiced.
- `RecordingRecognition`: takes, with metadata and links to pieces/motifs.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Measurement:
  - tempo stability, error rates, endurance, perceived difficulty.
- Topology:
  - motifs across pieces, fingerings, technical exercises linked to passages.

Runbooks like `plan_practice_session`, `review_recording`, `compose_variant`
turn practice into relational exploration rather than grind.
