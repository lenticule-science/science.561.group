# Idea: Liminal Spaces & Glitch Topology

Represent liminal places and glitches as recognitions to explore “near-normal
but off” phenomena.

## 1. Recognitions

- `LiminalSpaceRecognition`
  - places or states that feel in-between (hallways, hotel lobbies at night,
    3am supermarkets, waiting rooms).
- `GlitchRecognition`
  - unexpected discontinuities: visual, audio, UI, narrative, or social.
- `ContextRecognition`
  - surrounding circumstances (time, company, prior events).

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - graph of liminal spaces and glitches connected by shared features (lighting,
    emptiness, repetition, echoing patterns).
- Measurement:
  - subjective metrics: uncanny/comfort, presence/absence, clarity/confusion.

This can tie into dreams, inner work, creative writing, and MUD worldbuilding.
