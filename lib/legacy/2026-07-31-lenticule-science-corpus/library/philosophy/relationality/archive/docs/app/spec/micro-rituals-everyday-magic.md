# Idea: Micro-Rituals & Everyday Magic

Represent small personal rituals as recognitions to see how they support you.

## 1. Recognitions

- `RitualRecognition`
  - the practice: making tea, stretching, going outside, checking a window.
- `ContextRecognition`
  - when/where/with whom a ritual happens.
- `EffectRecognition`
  - reported effects on mood, focus, anxiety, etc.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Measurement:
  - frequency, adherence, subjective benefit, interaction with other metrics
    (sleep, work, social).
- Topology:
  - links between rituals, days, projects, and health recognitions.

This makes everyday “magic” a first-class citizen in your relational universe,
without reducing it to mere habit tracking.
