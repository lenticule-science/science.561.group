# Idea: Deconstruction & Textual Traces

Make deconstructive reading into explicit operations on recognitions: binaries,
traces, and sliding meanings.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `BinaryOppositionRecognition`
  - paired terms/positions in a text or discourse:
    - nature/culture, man/woman, center/margin, normal/abnormal, etc.

- `TraceRecognition`
  - what is present as absence:
    - events/subjects/senses that haunt a text but are unnamed or marginal.

- `DifferanceRecognition`
  - places where meaning is deferred or unstable:
    - key terms whose meanings slide depending on context,
    - unresolved tensions in definitions.

- `DeconstructiveReadingRecognition`
  - a pass over a slice of text/discourse that collects:
    - binaries found,
    - traces noted,
    - sites of différance.

## 2. Runbooks and uses

- `deconstruct(text_slice)`: produce a `DeconstructiveReadingRecognition` with
  identified binaries, traces, and meaning slippages.
- `compare_readings(text_slice, lens_ids)`: see how different lenses highlight
  different traces/binaries.
- `feed_back_to_agents`: let author/analyst agents incorporate this into how
  they summarize, critique, or recommend.

## 3. Connections

Deconstruction recognitions link directly to:

- semiosis and sign processes,
- critical lenses and assemblages,
- newsfeeds, policy docs, contracts, MUD lore, and any other text the system
  handles.

They provide a structured way to say: “here is how this text undoes or troubles
its own surface claims,” in a form agents and runbooks can actually work with.
