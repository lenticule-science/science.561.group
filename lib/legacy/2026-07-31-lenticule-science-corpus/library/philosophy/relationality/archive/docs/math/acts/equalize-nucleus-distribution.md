# Equalizing Nucleus Distribution

## Interpretation

Closure-like nuclei satisfy ν(x meet ν(y)) = ν(x meet y) and ν(ν(x) join y) = ν(x join y).  *Include Recognition* witnessing *Nucleate* yields ***Equalize Nucleus Distribution*** ([EqualizeNucleusDistribution](equalize-nucleus-distribution.md)).

This composition asserts the equalities using *Include Recognition*, so *Equalize Nucleus Distribution* is established.
