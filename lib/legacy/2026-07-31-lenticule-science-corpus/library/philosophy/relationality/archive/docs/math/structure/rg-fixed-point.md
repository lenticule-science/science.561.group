Sections or profiles invariant under repeated renormalisation steps.

## Definition
- Carrier: sections/profiles `x` such that `RGStep(x) = x` up to nucleus
  equivalence.
- Role: phases or universality classes in relational physics models.

## Invariants
- Stability: applying [RG Step](../operator/rg-step.md) leaves the element
  unchanged up to earned equivalence.
- Attraction: nearby sections may flow toward the fixed point under RG.

## Proof
- Uses: [RG Step](../operator/rg-step.md) to define invariance.
- Shows that: fixed points of renormalisation form a carrier capturing
  large-scale phases.
