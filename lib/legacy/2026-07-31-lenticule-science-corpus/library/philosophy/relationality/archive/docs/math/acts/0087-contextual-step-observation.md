## Interpretation

The stabilized head must still be witnessed by the surrounding context.  *Complete Trace* therefore witnesses *Step Observation Head*, producing ***Contextual Step Observation*** ([ContextualStepObservation](0087-contextual-step-observation.md)).

The composition keeps to earned recognitions and records contextual witnessing, so *Contextual Step Observation* is valid.
