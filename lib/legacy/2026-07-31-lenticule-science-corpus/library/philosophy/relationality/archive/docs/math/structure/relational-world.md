Profile endowed with its earned topos and geometry, treated as a “world” for
multi-scenario reasoning.

## Definition
- Carrier: [Profile Topos](profile-topos.md) equipped with
  [Context Geometry](context-geometry.md) describing spatial/temporal extent.
- Structure: inherits flow/nucleus, logical structure, and geometric relations;
  closed under earned recognitions in the profile.

## Invariants
- Stability: closed under profile flow/nucleus and internal logical operations.
- Geometric compatibility: geometry morphisms respect the profile topos maps.
- Functorial: world morphisms preserve both geometry and logical structure.

## Proof
- Uses: [Profile Topos](profile-topos.md) and [Context Geometry](context-geometry.md)
  to package logic and geometry into a single carrier.
- Shows that: a profile with geometry/topos data forms a active world suitable
  for combination and transport.
