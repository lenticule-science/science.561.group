The semiotic universe's history object (H, ≤) is a thin category — a [partial order](../domains/objects/domains/posets/terms/partial-order.md) viewed as a [category](../domains/objects/domains/categories/domains/ordinary-categories/terms/category.md). A [morphism](../domains/objects/domains/categories/domains/ordinary-categories/terms/morphism.md) from a to b is the unique witness that a ≤ b. There is at most one morphism between any two objects, and its existence expresses the ordering relation.

The Heyting algebra H is a [complete lattice](../domains/objects/domains/posets/terms/lattice.md) with the Heyting implication — the algebraic structure underlying the semiotic universe. Its elements are semantic values and its ordering expresses entailment: a ≤ b means "a entails b."
