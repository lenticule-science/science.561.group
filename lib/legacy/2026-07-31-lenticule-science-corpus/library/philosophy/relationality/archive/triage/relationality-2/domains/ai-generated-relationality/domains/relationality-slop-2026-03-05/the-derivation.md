## What the derivation is

Relationality unfolds through a single sustained argument. It begins with one
claim — that something exists — and shows that this claim, if coherent, compels
structures that cannot not arise. Each structure's coherence leaves something
undetermined, and that indeterminacy compels the next structure. The argument
does not import external axioms. Everything is earned from the initial claim and
the requirement that what is claimed must cohere.

The argument follows a recurring pattern: each phase produces three dynamics.
An **act** (a dynamic of determination), a **condition** (a dynamic of
relation), and a **structure** (a dynamic of form). The structure resolves the
current indeterminacy but exposes a new one, which incites the next phase.

This pattern — act, condition, structure, incitement — is the **triadic engine**.
It operates at every scale of the derivation.

Source: draft 1, "main derivations.md"; draft 7, "Minimal Ontology for
Deriving Coherence" (Act → Condition → Structure → Unit → Act cycle).

## Phase 1: Existing (→ Movement I)

**What is claimed**: Something exists.

**What this compels**: To claim something exists is to claim it is not what it
is not. This is a distinction — the minimal act of carving something from
everything else. The claim has three dynamics that must be derived:

- **Including** (act of determination): Something holds itself within what it is.
  Including actively establishes presence — it is not passive containment but
  ongoing activity.
- **Excluding** (act of determination): Something holds out everything it is not.
  Excluding actively establishes absence — not a mere lack but a maintained
  boundary.
- **Coherence** (structure): The minimal unit that stabilizes the interplay
  between including and excluding. This coherence ensures type-homogeneity:
  while including and excluding are oppositional, they are the same KIND of act
  on the same field.

**What remains undetermined**: How is this coherence sustained? Including and
excluding are acts — they must be continuously maintained. What maintains them?

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Existential Coherence"; draft 7, acts/include.md,
acts/exclude.md; draft 0, "terse derivations.md" (ternary structure).

### The phenomenological layer

Between the raw claim "something exists" and the formal operators, there is a
sequence of philosophical recognitions that the algebra later axiomatizes:

1. **Existing** — claiming something is
2. **Including** / **Excluding** — the twin acts of holding-in and holding-out
3. **Inclusion** / **Exclusion** — the conditions these acts maintain (not
   states but ongoing situations requiring active maintenance)
4. **Tension** — the condition of mutual opposition when including and excluding
   are held together. Neither act can operate alone; each requires the other
   for its meaning.
5. **Difference** — the condition induced by tension. Not a thing itself but
   what arises through sustained opposition. "Induced by tension, not directly
   by including or excluding."
6. **Relating** — the act that sustains tension productively. Relating holds
   including and excluding in ongoing opposition without collapsing either.
7. **Relation** — the condition that binds a thing to its ongoing existing.
8. **Relative** — the first structure. The minimal durable entity that holds
   relation open. "Every thing that is said to exist must, at minimum, be
   stabilized as a relative."

Source: draft 7, system/acts/ and system/conditions/ (all files); draft 0,
files 04-05 (Domain of Relating).

### The formal operators (Movement I proper)

The phenomenological sequence earns the operations that the formal framework
names:

- **Differentiating**: The product of reflexing — the first recognition. What
  the PRE calls Differentiate; the act of making a distinction.
- **Including** / **Excluding**: Partial order (a ≤ b) and incompatibility.
  Including establishes "a is within b"; excluding establishes "a is apart
  from b."
- **Togethering** / **Eithering**: Conjunction (a ∧ b) and disjunction
  (a ∨ b). Combining recognitions and choosing between them. These arise
  because if you can include A in B and B in C, you need consistent ways to
  combine and compare.
- **Negating**: Complement. The maximum of what is excluded from a given
  recognition. Negating(a) = Inducing(a, Bottom).
- **Inducing**: Implication / sufficiency. Inducing(a, b) is the greatest
  recognition c such that Togethering(a, c) ≤ b. This arises because
  conditional relating — "if this, then that" — must be expressible.
- **Iterating**: Self-application within the algebra. Iterating(a) =
  Togethering(a, Inducing(a, Top)). A recognition combined with the
  sufficiency for everything. This is the formal engine of self-confirmation.

These operations form a complete Heyting algebra — a logical structure where not
every question has a determinate answer. This is not Boolean logic: Negating
Negating(a) ≥ a but not necessarily Negating(Negating(a)) = a. The relational
field admits genuine indeterminacy.

Source: draft 3 PRE, Part I (Logic); draft 12, movement-1.md; draft 3 treatise,
"Relational Structure" section. For Iterating definition: PRE Appendix A.2,
line 2775.

## Phase 2: Relating (→ Movement I/II transition)

**What is claimed**: The coherence from Phase 1 must be sustained.

**What this compels**: Including and excluding do not maintain themselves —
something must hold them in productive opposition. This is **relating**: the
continuous act that maintains the interplay between including and excluding.

- **Relating** (act): The ongoing act sustaining tension. Relating introduces
  reflexive sequence: each relational unit can relate to itself, producing a
  sequence of deepened units.
- **Relation** (condition): The stabilized condition in which inclusion and
  exclusion are coherently maintained. Relation induces positional invariance:
  the roles of including and excluding are preserved through reflexive deepening.
- **Relational form** (structure): The minimal configuration formalizing the
  interplay of relation and relating. This establishes reflexive compositionality:
  relational forms at any depth can be composed side-by-side.

**What remains undetermined**: What coheres relational form itself?

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Relational Coherence"; draft 7, acts/relate.md,
conditions/relation.md, structures/relative.md.

## Phase 3: Stabilizing (→ Movement II)

**What is claimed**: Relational form must cohere.

**What this compels**: The act of relating and the condition of relation must be
jointly sustained. This self-maintenance has its own dynamics:

- **Relating-relation** (act): The act that mediates between relating and
  relation, ensuring their joint persistence.
- **Relational self-coherence** (condition): The condition in which relating and
  relation are unified as a single dynamic.
- **Relational closure** (structure): The structured configuration formalizing
  self-maintenance — the system achieves internal consistency.

In the formal framework, this phase earns:

- **Closing**: The closure operator. Closing(a) = Join of Iterating^n(a) over
  all n. The least element stable under iteration. What survives all self-
  confirmation.
- **Opening**: The interior operator. The dual of closing — what remains
  coherent when you look WITHIN a boundary rather than beyond it.
- **Balancing**: The equilibrium of closing and opening. Balancing(a) =
  Opening(a) ∧ Closing(a). The compatibility between inward consolidation
  and outward release. The Frobenius law ensures that what is closed inward
  coheres with what is opened outward.

Closing and opening earn modal logic: what must be (Necessitating = Closing)
and what may be (Possibilizing = Opening). These are the S4 modal axioms,
derived rather than assumed.

**What remains undetermined**: Being is now stable but not static. Stability
says nothing about how recognitions MOVE.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Sustained Relational Dynamics"; draft 12,
movement-2.md (Closure/Interior, Frobenius Law, Balance, Modal Translation);
PRE Part II (Structure), §4.8 (Transition).

## Phase 4: Bounding (→ Movement II/III transition)

**What is claimed**: The self-sustaining unit must distinguish itself from
what it is not.

**What this compels**: The coherent relational unit needs a boundary — a
distinction between itself and what lies outside relation.

- **Boundary-excluding** (act): Enacting exclusion of what lies beyond the
  relational unit — relationlessness itself.
- **Relational negation** (condition): The condition maintaining the
  distinction between relation and non-relation.
- **Relational boundary** (structure): The structural form stabilizing the
  division between the relational unit and its outside.

**What remains undetermined**: What coheres the boundary? The boundary must
itself be related — but to relate the boundary is to fold it back into the
system it bounds.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Bounded Relational Coherence."

## Phase 5: Directing (→ Movement III)

**What is claimed**: The relational boundary must be related back to the field.

**What this compels**: Relating the boundary to the field is a reflexive act —
the system relates to its own limits. This produces directed motion: the
boundary is not merely drawn but engaged, producing orientation.

- **Reflexive relating** (act): Relating the boundary back to the relational
  field. This is the act that folds exclusion into the system.
- **Reflexive relation** (condition): The boundary becomes part of the system
  through its own self-engagement.
- **Reflexive form** (structure): The folding of boundary into system,
  formalized.

In the formal framework, this phase earns:

- **Flowing**: The directed comonadic dynamic. Flowing is a KZ-lax idempotent
  comonad: Flowing(Flowing(a)) ≤ Flowing(a). Motion contracts under iteration
  rather than escalating. Flowing preserves the modal core — what must be
  stays stable under motion.
- **Dynamic residuation**: The logic along flow. Flowing(a ∧ b) ≤ c if and
  only if a ≤ Flowing(b ⇒ c). This Galois connection means that doing-then-
  reasoning is equivalent to reasoning-about-doing.
- **Arithmetic of processes**: Addition (combining flows) and multiplication
  (composing flows) of directed motion.

**What remains undetermined**: All motion has occurred within a single field.
But relations cluster locally and extend globally — how does local behavior
translate to global structure?

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Reflexive Relational Coherence"; draft 12,
movement-3.md (Flow, Dynamic Residuation, Arithmetic); PRE Part III (Arithmetic).

## Phase 6: Differentiating externally (→ Movement IV)

**What is claimed**: The reflexive relational unit must distinguish itself
from OTHER such units.

**What this compels**: If one relational unit exists, it differentiates from
what it is not — and what it is not may include other relational units with
the same structure. This establishes co-presence: multiple units maintained in
differentiated yet shared existence.

- **External differentiating** (act): Distinguishing one reflexive unit from
  another within the shared field.
- **Co-presence** (condition): Multiple units maintained in differentiated
  existence. This generates relational tension between distinct units.
- **Relational tension** (structure): The structure formalizing and stabilizing
  the interplay between distinct units. Supports composites, chains, networks.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Multirelational Coherence."

## Phase 7: Integrating (→ Movement IV proper)

**What is claimed**: Multiple co-present units must cohere both locally
(between units) and globally (across the whole field).

**What this compels**: Two simultaneous acts of integration:

- **Inter-unit relating** (act): Direct relational engagement between distinct
  units. Addresses local coherence.
- **Field-integrating** (act): Maintaining coherence of the entire field.
  Addresses global coherence.

In the formal framework, this phase earns:

- **The cohesive chain**: Shape ⊣ Flat ⊣ Sharp ⊣ Crisp — four adjoint
  functors connecting immediate and extended perspectives.
  - Shape: the "outline" — what local structure looks like from a distance
  - Flat (Discrete): purely local, no global connections
  - Sharp (Codiscrete): purely global, no local distinctions
  - Crisp (Global): the fully coherent integration
- **Local-global correspondence**: What holds locally translates coherently
  to what holds globally, and vice versa.
- **Residuation**: The adjunction between doing-then-stabilizing and
  stabilizing-then-doing.

**What remains undetermined**: The relational field has expressed logic,
stability, motion, and spatial coherence. But to understand what these
operations DESCRIBE, they must be read as describing physical reality.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Relational Field Coherence"; draft 12,
movement-4.md (Cohesive Chain, Adjoint Unity); PRE Part IV (Geometry).

## Phase 8: Meta-bounding (→ Movement V)

**What is claimed**: The entire integrated relational field must distinguish
itself from what lies beyond it.

**What this compels**: The meta-boundary — the distinction between the
relational field as a whole and what is outside it. This recapitulates
Phase 4 at the scale of the entire field.

- **Meta-boundary excluding** (act): Excluding what lies beyond the
  integrated field.
- **Meta-negation** (condition): The distinction between relation and
  non-relation at the field level.
- **Meta-boundary** (structure): The structural form stabilizing this
  division. Preserves positional differentiation and type-homogeneity at the
  meta level.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Relational Meta-Boundary Coherence."

## Phase 9: Meta-relating (→ Movement V proper)

**What is claimed**: The meta-boundary must be related back into the field.

**What this compels**: Meta-reflexive relating folds the field's boundary back
into the field, producing the system's ability to observe and describe itself.

- **Meta-reflexive relating** (act): Folding the meta-boundary back into the
  relational system.
- **Meta-transcending** (act): Engaging with what the meta-boundary posits as
  beyond itself.
- **Meta-reflexive relation** / **Meta-expansion** (conditions): The meta-
  boundary becomes part of the field; the field extends to accommodate what
  was excluded.
- **Meta-reflexive form** / **Meta-extension** (structures): Formalizing
  self-relation and expansion at the meta level.

In the formal framework, this phase earns:

- **States**: Recognitions read as physical configurations.
- **Observables**: Measurements as nuclei applied to states.
- **Invariants**: What is conserved under flowing. The active envelope
  Env_F(a) = Necessitating(Flowing(a)) ∧ Possibilizing(Flowing(a)) captures
  the "bandwidth of stable variation."
- **Conservation**: Every consistency in the logic finds expression as a
  measurable invariant (Noether-like correspondence).

**What remains undetermined**: Meta-reflexive form and meta-extension induce
recursive domain unfolding — closure at the meta-relational level exposes new
dynamics, compelling a subsequent domain. The derivation points toward further
determination. The meta-relational unit's coherence requires ongoing
resolution.

Source: draft 1, "Deriving Meta-relational Coherence"; draft 12,
movement-5.md (States, Observables, Conservation, Stable Envelope);
PRE Part V (Physics).

## The self-grounding claim

The derivation is self-grounding: the structures it derives contain the means
to reconstruct the derivation. This is not a metaphor — it is a formal
property. Every Profile (a well-behaved substructure) supports the full
four-part chain: RelationalStructure → RelationalLanguage →
RelationalGeometry → RelationalPhysics. The system IS the fixed point of its
own generative closure.

This is what RITM (Relational Infinity-Topos Modality) formalizes: a
quadruple adjunction that, once realized, generates two internal sub-modalities
(Relational Dynamics and Relational Topology) that reproduce the RITM axioms.
Every RITM contains the structure to reconstruct itself.

The philosophical meaning: the relational field does not rest on an external
ground. It is not built on a substrate, derived from a creator, or dependent
on a transcendental subject. It grounds itself through the very operations it
contains — because those operations, when applied to themselves, produce
exactly the structure from which they arose.

Source: draft 4, ritm.md (self-stabilizing property); draft 3 treatise,
"Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction" theorem; draft 4,
deriving-a-reflexive-infinity-topos.md (Reflexive Infinity-Topos as fixed
point of composed modalities).

## Mathematical correspondences

The derivation is philosophical. The mathematics validates it by showing that
the structures earned at each phase correspond to established formal objects:

| Movement | Philosophical content | Mathematical correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| I | Including/excluding, tension, coherence | Complete Heyting algebra |
| II | Closing, opening, balancing, necessitating/possibilizing | Closure/interior operators, S4 modal logic |
| III | Flowing, dynamic residuation | KZ-lax idempotent comonad, Galois connections |
| IV | Cohesive chain, local-global correspondence | Cohesive toposes, adjoint quadruples |
| V | States, observables, conservation | Spectral theory, operator algebras, Noether's theorem |

The mathematics is ONE correspondence — a validation that the philosophical
structures have the properties they claim. It is not the thing itself. Other
correspondences exist: the framework aligns with Lakota cosmology (the
grounding epistemology), with certain patterns in sociology (cybernetic
postliberalism), with neurocognitive dynamics (neuroreflexivity). Each
correspondence validates from a different direction.
