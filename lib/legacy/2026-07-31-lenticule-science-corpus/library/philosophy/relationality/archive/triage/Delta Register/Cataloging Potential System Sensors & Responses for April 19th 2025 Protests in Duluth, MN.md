---
title: "Cataloging Potential System Sensors & Responses For April 19Th 2025 Protests In Duluth, Mn"
---
**Fūsus** aggregates live video from Duluth PD bodycams, city-owned CCTV, port authority surveillance, and partner business cameras. Detects crowd density and movement shifts near protest zones and port-adjacent infrastructure. Sends alerts to city command and MNJAC.

**Banjo (SafeXai)** processes real-time location and traffic data. Flags clustering near Duluth Courthouse, Canal Park, lift bridge, and Port of Duluth-Superior terminals. Infrastructure proximity boosts priority tier in MN fusion center workflows.

**Port of Duluth-Superior / Maritime Domain Awareness** operates under federal critical infrastructure oversight. Any protest activity or broadcast referencing shipping routes, grain silos, or dock operations triggers notifications to MNJAC, U.S. Coast Guard, and DHS Infrastructure Protection.

**MNJAC (Minnesota Fusion Center)** receives feeds from Fūsus, Banjo, and LPRs. Elevates SAR generation when activity intersects supply chain, economic zones, or public employee mentions. Sends profiles to HSIN and FBI. Alerts Port Authority and regional shipping partners under CI/KR threat management.

**License Plate Readers (LPRs)** positioned on inbound routes (e.g., I-35, US 2, Bong Bridge) log protestor vehicle data. Scans include access roads near port terminals and fuel depots. Data shared with Palantir and fusion center partners.

**Clearview AI** scrapes protest livestreams, Instagram reels, and bystander posts. Identifies faces and matches against port personnel records, protest history, and law enforcement datasets. Outputs synced to Palantir Gotham.

**Palantir Gotham** links faces, plates, and mobile IDs to known associations, attendance patterns, and flagged ideologies. Port-linked disruption or critical infrastructure adjacency increases risk weighting.

**Geofence Warrants** cover the protest perimeter and port-adjacent zones (e.g., DECC, Harbor Drive, Rice’s Point). Device pings tied to Google/Apple user data. Requests drafted in advance by Duluth PD and MNJAC.

**Echosec / ShadowDragon** monitor Telegram, TikTok, and Twitter for Duluth protest references. Special attention to keywords involving port shutdown, bridge blockage, or “economic strike.” Network maps generated and shared with fusion analysts.

**HSIN (Homeland Security Information Network)** stores full protest footage, SARs, and entity data. Port-related references flagged for Infrastructure Protection workflows and shared across CI/KR networks.

**Microsoft 365 Purview / Slack Grid / Zoom IQ** flag protest planning or attendance discussion within city departments, port-linked logistics companies, and educational institutions. Flags routed to HR or compliance officers.

**CLEAR / Skopenow** assemble identity profiles from donation platforms, protest flyers, or live chat handles. Match names and phone numbers to Duluth, Superior, and Iron Range employment records, housing data, and civil court histories.

**CrowdTangle / Meta Trust & Safety** detect viral protest clips or port disruption messaging on Facebook and Instagram. Escalated for downranking or removal. May trigger platform-based visibility suppression.

**X (Twitter)** flags coordinated resharing of Duluth protest links, especially if tied to anti-infrastructure or economic messaging. Activates suppression queues for known activist accounts.

**YouTube CSAI Match** scans protest footage for confrontation, emotional content, or repeat port symbols (e.g., lift bridge, grain elevators). Auto-removal and demonetization apply under internal escalation thresholds.

**Stripe / PayPal / FinCEN** track clustered donations to Duluth-focused bail funds, port protest supplies, or mutual aid groups. Spiky transaction patterns trigger suspicious activity reports. Reports shared with financial partners and federal risk teams.
