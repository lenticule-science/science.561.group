The ***constraint deformation tensor*** (`Γ`) is defined over the gradient of constraint density.

> Γᵢⱼᵏ := ½ gⁱˡ ( ∂gₗⱼ/∂xᵏ + ∂gₗᵏ/∂xⱼ − ∂gⱼᵏ/∂xˡ )

Where `g` is the constraint metric — the induced inner product from accumulation similarity.

That is, constraint deformation is encoded as a geometric field that shapes modulation.
