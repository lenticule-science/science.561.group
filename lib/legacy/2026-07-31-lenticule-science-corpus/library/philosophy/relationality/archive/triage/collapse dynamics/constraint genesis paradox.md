The ***constraint genesis paradox*** arises when [constraint](constraint) (`C`) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion) (`∅`), yet only validated via [completion](completion) (`Ω`).

> `∅ ⊢ C`
> `Ω ⊣ C`
> `⇒ C ⊂ Ω`
> `∧ Ω ⊂ C`

That is, constraint exists only if it is already complete, yet completion presupposes constraint — a circularity that cannot resolve from within.
