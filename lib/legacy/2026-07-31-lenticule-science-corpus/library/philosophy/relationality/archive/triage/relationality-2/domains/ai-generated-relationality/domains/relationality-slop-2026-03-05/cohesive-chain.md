The adjoint quadruple connecting local and global perspectives. Four idempotent operators forming a chain of adjunctions.

## What it is

PRE: Shape (Π, connected-components), Discrete (Disc, discrete embedding), Codiscrete (Codisc, codiscrete embedding), Global (Γ, global sections).

Foundations: Π→ ⊣ □ ⊣ ◇ ⊣ ∇←, arising from decomposition of the reflexive operator R. "The abstract reflexive operation R has unfolded into a modal chain. Its internal directions correspond to four roles of coherence: collecting, fixing, releasing, and dispersing."

Draft 12: Shape ⊣ Flat ⊣ Sharp ⊣ Crisp (using Lawvere–Schreiber terminology in some places).

Draft 1: Phase 7 discusses "inter-unit relating" (local coherence between distinct units) and "field-integrating" (global coherence across the entire field). "Depth alignment: multi-relational units must align their reflexive sequences to maintain coherent engagement within the integrated field."

## What it produces

- Cohesion: local behavior and global structure translate without loss
- Residuation: the interplay between doing-then-stabilizing and stabilizing-then-doing
- The ground for stratified geometry (PRE's Stratify operator)
- Networks, nodes, edges (draft 1's vocabulary for complex relational architectures)

## Relationships

- Arises from: decomposition of the reflexive operator R (Foundations Phase IV)
- Includes: Necessary (□) and Possible (◇) as the middle pair
- Flow = □◇ (composition of the middle two)
- Mathematical correspondence: Lawvere's axiomatic cohesion
