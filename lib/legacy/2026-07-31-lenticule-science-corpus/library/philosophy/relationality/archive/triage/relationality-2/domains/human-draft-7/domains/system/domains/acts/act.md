- [define](define):: An [act](act) is the thing by which a [triage/thing](triage/thing) brings about any [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) or [../structures/structure](../structures/structure). Every [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) and every [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) requires an [act](act) to [exist](exist).

## Derivation

- [derive|deriving](derive|deriving) [exist|existing](exist|existing) derives [act|acts](act|acts)
    - Every claim of anything being the case involves something having been done.
- An [act](act) is what introduces [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) into a field.
    - Without an [act](act), there is no way to set apart what is from what is not.
    - This makes the [act](act) a necessary condition for any [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) to be realized.
- Every [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) presupposes an [act](act).
    - To [relate|relating](relate|relating) requires an [act](act) that enacts the [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation).
    - Without an [act](act), no [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) can be established, sustained, or held open.

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](triage/thing) that is [exist|existing](exist|existing) does so by way of at least one [act](act).
- An [act](act) is the point of minimal [induce|induction](induce|induction) for all [relational dynamics](relational dynamics).
- No [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) or [../structures/structure](../structures/structure) arises without an [act](act) that brings it into being.
