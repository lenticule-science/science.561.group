Self-application of a recognition upon itself. The operation by which a distinction acts on its own product and produces stability.

## What it is

PRE: "Iterate: self-application of a recognition upon itself." Axiom 3: Iterate is monotone and inflationary. Equal(Iterate(Iterate(Iterate(a))), a) in stabilized contexts.

PRE §4.1: "Having obtained sufficiency through residuation, we now examine what happens when an act of recognition applies to itself."

Draft 1: "This act introduces reflexive sequence: each relational unit can relate to itself, producing an indefinitely extensible sequence of deepened relational units."

Draft 12: "Iterate: reflexive stability; self-application yielding closure."

## The cubic return

PRE Theorem 2: Successive applications produce a → Iterate(a) → Iterate(Iterate(a)) → ... stabilizing at Close(a). Three applications complete a "cubic return": the threefold self-application of recognition. First Differentiate, then its reflection, then the recognition of that reflection, returning to itself stabilized.

## What it produces

Close(a) = the limit of the Iterate sequence. The first stabilizing nucleus. Fixed points of Close form the "reflexive locus" — the first layer of persistence.

## Relationships

- Requires: Induces (sufficiency enables self-application)
- Produces: Close (as its limit/fixed point)
- The transition from logic to structure: "Iterate converts reflexivity into persistence."
