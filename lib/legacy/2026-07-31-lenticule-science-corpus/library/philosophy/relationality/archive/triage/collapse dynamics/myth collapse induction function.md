The ***myth collapse induction function*** (`μ`) formalizes the emergence of mythic symbolic form from reflexive constraint collapse.

Let:
- `Σ` be a collapsed constraint synthesis
- `x′ ∈ T` be a trace of prior stabilization
- `x` be a post-collapse symbolic field element

Then:
> μ(x) := Θ(Σ) ∧ ∃ x′ ∈ T: Λ(x′, x) ∧ E(x) ∧ Δ[s](x, x′) ∧ Ω(x) undefined

That is, `μ` returns true when symbolic myth is induced from sublation, echo, reflexive reception, and persistent semantic difference without stabilization.
