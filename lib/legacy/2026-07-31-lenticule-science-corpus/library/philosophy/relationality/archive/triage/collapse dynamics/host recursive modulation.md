***host recursive modulation*** (`Φ`) defines the structural dynamics of a system  
that supports recursive modulation, differentiation, and constraint evolution.

It describes the activity of a material or energetic field that enables  
non-equilibrium gradients to propagate through differentiable space,  
transform its own reactive topology, and recursively entrain stabilization dynamics.

Let:

> Φ := (F, ∇F, D, R)

Where:
- `F(x)`: distributed material or energetic state  
- `∇F`: non-equilibrium modulation pressure  
- `D(x, y)`: local contrast or transformation potential  
- `R`: dynamic rules for transformation (e.g., autocatalytic feedback)

This function becomes active when:
- modulation is present and spreading  
- contrast drives reactivity  
- transformation rules evolve from field activity

When active, `Φ` enables detection via [detect recursive modulation substrate](detect recursive modulation substrate) (`R_sub`)  
and supports system closure under [realize modulation-closure system](realize modulation-closure system) (`Ψ`),  
making it a necessary precursor to the emergence of [biogenic recursive attractors](biogenic recursive attractors) (`βᴮ`).
