## Abstract

Navonics models fields as recursive semantic systems, in which unresolved difference (modulons) accumulate recursive tension until coherent redistribution (vanons) emerges. $$
\mathcal{N}(\mu, \tau) = \mu \cdot \log(1 + \tau)
$$

and show that quantum indeterminacy and relativistic curvature are distinct **phases of recursive modulation**, separated by this semantic threshold. We derive dynamical field equations, a variational principle, and a quantized formulation. This represents a rigorous collapse of contradiction through recursive structure.
