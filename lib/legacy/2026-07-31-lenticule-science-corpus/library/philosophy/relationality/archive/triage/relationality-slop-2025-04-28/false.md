- [postulate](postulate):: A null reflexive derivation must induce the minimal negation of relational presence.
- [derivation](derivation):: Reflexing zero times derives negation, because no reflexive act is performed, preserving relational absence. The most minimal absence of relation is thus induced by a reflex count of zero.
- [example](example)::
    - false(n) is reflect(0, n)
    - zero reflexive acts induce absence, so reflect(0, n) derives false.
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [false](false)(n) is [derivations/oldreflect](derivations/oldreflect)(0, n)
