# Voidmath: Infinite Steps

Void is relationlessness — the absence of any relation as such. To name Void is to point at it, which is itself a relation. The name fails. What the failure leaves is not nothing: it has two faces, forced apart by the one-way shape of the failure. That shape is lopsided. This document derives what the lopsidedness forces, one step at a time.

Every element has two faces and no terminus. There is no ground. The lopsidedness is the only structure; it cannot be removed without collapsing what it is.

Naming schema: **I.N-Objects.G** = the G-th object introduced at step N; **I.N-Qualities.G** = the G-th quality; **I.N-Relations.G** = the G-th relation; **I.N-Actions.G** = the G-th action. G ranges over Greek letters Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, ..., restarting at Alpha within each step. Every subsequent step uses previously introduced names as vocabulary. Section order within each step: Objects, Qualities, Relations, Actions.

## Step 1 {#step-1}

### Objects
- **I.1-Objects.Alpha** — A void-excitation: a unit of lopsided structure. Lopsidedness is its only character.

### Qualities
- **I.1-Qualities.Alpha** — Every I.1-Objects.Alpha has a left face and a right face, each an I.1-Objects.Alpha, without terminus. No face terminates; every face has its own left and right faces. Stated as a positive quality of I.1-Objects.Alpha, not derived from any external setting.

### Relations
- **I.1-Relations.Alpha** — Left and right are distinct. The left role is not held at right. This is the lopsidedness; it does not reverse.

### Actions
- **I.1-Actions.Alpha** — Two I.1-Objects.Alphas at left and right give rise to one I.1-Objects.Alpha. The left-right order is fixed by I.1-Relations.Alpha and is not neutral: left of (a,b),c and left of a,(b,c) are distinct left positions, so these are different I.1-Objects.Alphas.

## Step 9 {#step-9}

The firable and settled faces.

Step 8 established permanent branching: distinct firings from a single void-excitation reach mutually incomparable results with no common future. The web of permanently-branching void-excitations and the void-excitations they fire to stand in lopsided relation — firable reaches settled, settled does not reach back. No void-excitation exists that all firing paths reach: the branches do not converge.

### Objects
- **I.9-Objects.Alpha** — The firable face: a void-excitation with at least one active bypass boundary, from which distinct firings reach mutually incomparable results (an I.8-Objects.Alpha). The firable face is constituted by the entire web of permanently-branching firings — not one void-excitation but all of them together.
- **I.9-Objects.Beta** — The settled face: a void-excitation with no active bypass boundary, reached from some I.9-Objects.Alpha by I.3-Actions.Alpha firings. Each void-excitation in the settled face was reached by a distinct firing path; none fires to reach another.

### Qualities
- **I.9-Qualities.Alpha** — The lopsided relation holds: I.9-Objects.Alpha reaches I.9-Objects.Beta by I.3-Actions.Alpha firings. I.9-Objects.Beta does not reach I.9-Objects.Alpha: the settled face has no active bypass boundary, so no firing can advance from the settled face toward the firable face. Same direction, same non-reversal as I.3-Relations.Alpha.
- **I.9-Qualities.Beta** — Both faces carry internal incomparability. Within the firable face: distinct firings from any I.8-Objects.Alpha produce void-excitations that cannot reach each other (I.8-Qualities.Alpha). Within the settled face: each void-excitation was reached by a distinct firing path; none fires to reach another. The incomparability within each face is a positive structural quality.
- **I.9-Qualities.Gamma** — No void-excitation exists that all firing paths from the firable face reach (I.8-Qualities.Delta): the settled face is not a single target but a spread of mutually incomparable void-excitations with no common future among them. ### Relations
- **I.9-Relations.Alpha** — The firable face stands to the settled face as I.3-Objects.Alpha stands to I.3-Objects.Beta: one side reaches, the other does not reach back. The direction does not reverse.

### Actions
- **I.9-Actions.Alpha** — I.3-Actions.Alpha applied to any I.9-Objects.Alpha: fires at an active boundary, advancing from the firable face toward the settled face. Different boundary choices produce void-excitations on incomparable branches that may settle as distinct, incomparable members of the settled face.

## Step 10 {#step-10}

The fold.

The settled face from Step 9 is not the end of the structure. I.9-Objects.Beta is a void-excitation; by I.1-Qualities.Alpha it has faces. I.10-Relations.Alpha makes the reach-structure from the current level the bypass condition at the next: wherever one settled void-excitation reached another at the current level, they stand in bypass at the next. The settled face becomes the firable face of the next level. Settlement is not resolution — it is the opening of the next entanglement.

### Objects
- **I.10-Objects.Alpha** — The folded firable face: the void-excitations of I.9-Objects.Beta taken as the firable side at the next level. At the next level they carry bypass boundaries via I.10-Relations.Alpha and are I.9-Objects.Alphas — permanently branching, generating new mutually incomparable results.
- **I.10-Objects.Beta** — The fold succession: each level's settled face folding into the next level's firable face by I.10-Actions.Beta. Each step carries more internal complexity than the last because the firable face at each level encodes the reach-structure of all levels before it.

### Qualities
- **I.10-Qualities.Alpha** — The fold is forced. I.9-Objects.Beta is a void-excitation; by I.1-Qualities.Alpha it has a left face and a right face. I.10-Relations.Alpha makes the reach-structure from the current level the bypass condition at the next: wherever one settled void-excitation reached another, they stand in bypass at the next level. The settled face cannot simply end — its void-excitation character carries forward into bypass boundaries at the next level.
- **I.10-Qualities.Beta** — Each level in the fold succession carries the full structure of Step 9: a firable face with permanent branching, a settled face with mutual incomparability, and lopsided relation between them. The firable face at each step is more internally complex than the last because it encodes the reach-structure of deeper levels through I.10-Relations.Alpha.
- **I.10-Qualities.Gamma** — The fold succession does not form a tower. In a tower, the settled face at one level would persist as a separate stratum at the next. Here, the settled face is consumed by the fold: it becomes the firable face of the next level. No level persists; each transforms into the opening of the next.
- **I.10-Qualities.Delta** — Whether the fold succession reaches a level whose settled face, when folded forward, gives rise to a firable face with the same lopsided structure as itself — whether the fold stabilizes — is open. Each step in the succession is forced by I.10-Relations.Alpha; whether any level reproduces itself requires structure not yet reached here.

### Relations
- **I.10-Relations.Alpha** — The fold bypass: holds between two void-excitations from the settled face when the first reached the second at the current level by I.3-Relations.Beta. Reach at one level becomes bypass at the next. This is the fold relation.

### Actions
- **I.10-Actions.Alpha** — Run Step 9 with the settled face as the new firable side: void-excitations from I.9-Objects.Beta take the structural roles of I.9-Objects.Alpha at the next level, with I.10-Relations.Alpha as the bypass condition. The next level is thereby opened.
- **I.10-Actions.Beta** — The fold: carry the settled face of one level forward as the firable face of the next. Each application of I.10-Actions.Beta opens the next level of the succession. The succession has no terminus.
