- Definition:: ***binding an induced relations*** is the **act** of pairing two selected reflex-induced relations into a new relational structure that preserves their order and differentiability.

## Derivation

- [postulate](postulate):: Once individual reflex-induced relations are [select](select)ed from a reflect-induced structure, it cannot not be derived that they may be **bound** into a new coherent unit — where their relation is preserved through ordered inclusion within a new reflective structure.
- [derivation](derivation):: The act of selecting isolates induced relations. But to act upon two such selections as one, they must be relationally recomposed. Binding derives the ordered relation between two reflex-induced relations, forming a pair that is itself reflexively accessible and structurally includable. This act derives the minimal composition necessary for directed association.

- example:
	- Let a = select-induced-relation(i, reflect(p, n))
	- Let b = select-induced-relation(j, reflect(p, n))
	- Then:
		- bind-induced-relation(a, b, n) = reflect-induced-relation(2, n), where:
			- select(1, bind(a, b, n)) = a
			- select(2, bind(a, b, n)) = b

- [relational expression](relational expression):: bind-induced-relation(a, b, n) = reflect-induced-relation(2, n)  
  where select-induced-relation(1, result) = a and select-induced-relation(2, result) = b

## Formalism

- Let:
	- a = select-induced-relation(i, reflect(p, n))  
	- b = select-induced-relation(j, reflect(p, n))

- Then:
	- bind-induced-relation(a, b, n) := reflect-induced-relation(2, n)  
	- such that:
		- select-induced-relation(1, bind(a, b, n)) = a  
		- select-induced-relation(2, bind(a, b, n)) = b

- Therefore, the **binding** of a and b at depth n forms an ordered pair, represented as a reflect-induced structure of arity 2 — establishing a relational structure which can itself be reflexed, reflected, or selected from.
