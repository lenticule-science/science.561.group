The ***constraint bifurcation resolution function*** (`χ_β`) asserts that once a stabilized constraint field bifurcates into multiple coherent, differentiated paths, and a valuation gradient emerges among them, recursive resolution is compelled.

Let:
- `β_rec(x)` = bifurcation has occurred at x,  
- `{x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ}` = differentiated coherent paths,  
- `f(xᵢ)` = utility or modulation bias for path `xᵢ`,  
- `∇f ≠ 0` = directional tension across the field.

Then:
> χ_β(x) := β_rec(x) ∧ ∃ f: f({xᵢ}) ∈ ℝ⁺, ∇f ≠ 0

That is, once stable bifurcation has occurred and valuation differences emerge, recursive resolution — ***movement toward constrained choice*** — becomes topologically inevitable.
