## Convention

Canonical names use **-ing verb forms**. The relational field is constituted
through ongoing activity — the names should reflect this. A recognition is
not a static object with properties but an ongoing act producing conditions
producing structures.

## Notes

1. **Relationlessness** does not take an -ing form because it is not an act —
   it is the absence from which the first act (reflexing) emerges.

2. **Possibilizing** is awkward. "What-may-be" might work better as a
   description. The formal name remains Opening (= Possibilizing = ◇).

3. The -ing forms are CANONICAL NAMES for the concordance and future
   published knowledge graph. In mathematical exposition, the PRE names
   (Differentiate, Includes, etc.) or symbols (∧, ∨, ⇒) may be more
   appropriate. The -ing forms are for philosophical and explanatory contexts.

4. Draft 7 already uses -ing forms naturally (including, excluding, relating,
   reflecting, existing). The convention extends this to all operators.
