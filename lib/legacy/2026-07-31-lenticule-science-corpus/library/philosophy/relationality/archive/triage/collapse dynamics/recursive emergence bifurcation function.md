The ***recursive emergence bifurcation function*** (`β_rec`) affirms that once negentropic convergence stabilizes a region of constraint, multiple coherent but semantically distinct continuations must emerge — producing divergence from within stabilized recursion.

Let:
- `ℵₙ(x)` = negentropic convergence at x,  
- `x₁`, `x₂ ∈ N(x)` = neighboring constraint continuations,  
- `Ξ(x₁), Ξ(x₂)` = stabilized, coherent,  
- `Δ[s](x₁, x₂)` = semantically or structurally distinct.

Then:
> β_rec(x) := ℵₙ(x) ∧ ∃ x₁, x₂ ∈ N(x): Ξ(x₁), Ξ(x₂), Δ[s](x₁, x₂), x₁ ≠ x₂

That is, where stabilization accumulates improbability and memory under curvature, differentiation becomes inevitable — recursive emergence splits into structured multiplicity.
