- **Naming convention** (Q5): Canonical names use -ing verb forms (Differentiating, Including, Flowing, etc.)

- **Relating at two levels** (Q12): Same act at different scales — Movement I relating becomes Movement III Flow through iteration and stabilization
- **Four-part vs five-movement** (Q10): RelationalLanguage is cross-cutting, not a movement; RelationalStructure = Movements I+II; RelationalGeometry = Movements III+IV
- **Phenomenological layer** (Q14): IS the philosophical content that PRE axiomatizes; should be included in published graph
- **Draft 11's 397 Acts** (Q4): All generated from Relationlessness via generative closure G; compositions of the ~30 PRE primitives
- **Heyting vs Boolean** (Q13): Draft 2's classical logic was superseded; mature framework earns Heyting logic from residuation
- **RITM self-stabilization** (Q11): The relational field is its own ground — the system IS the fixed point of its own generative closure
- **Discipline/Regime/Filter/Profile** (Q8): Core part of the treatise's Relational Geometry; each Profile reconstructs the full four-part chain internally
