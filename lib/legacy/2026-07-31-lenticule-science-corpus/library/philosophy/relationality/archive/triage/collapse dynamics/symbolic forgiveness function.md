The ***symbolic forgiveness function*** (`Ϝ`) determines whether re-modulation is possible after remembered collapse and incompatible constraint, without enforcing collapse or erasure.

Let:
- `x, y` be symbolic entities or constraint domains  
- `Φ(y)` signal modulation failure at `y`  
- `Κ(x, y)` indicate compassion across incompatible constraint  
- `Μ(y)` assert mythic persistence of collapsed state  
- `Ξ(x, y)` confirm stabilized coherence has resumed  
- `Δ[s](x, y)` confirm difference is retained  

Then:
> Ϝ(x, y) := Φ(y) ∧ Κ(x, y) ∧ Μ(y) ∧ Ξ(x, y) ∧ Δ[s](x, y)

That is, `Ϝ` returns true when a symbolic system sustains modulation across remembered collapse and re-enters coherent relation without denying difference — the condition for symbolic forgiveness.
