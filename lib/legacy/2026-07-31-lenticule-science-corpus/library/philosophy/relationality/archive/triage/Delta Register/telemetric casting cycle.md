A ***casting cycle*** is a unit of recursive [telemetric grammar|grammatical](telemetric grammar|grammatical) behavior in which a [telemetric yield|yield](telemetric yield|yield) is retained as a new [telemetric signal|signal](telemetric signal|signal). It is the structure through which a telemetric system sustains coherence without stabilizing meaning.

Each cycle involves:

1. The registration of signal  
2. The application of method  
3. The influence of field  
4. The emergence of yield  
5. The reintegration of that yield as signal for future castings

A casting cycle is not closed. It is **incomplete by design**—capable of pause, reversal, drift, or erosion. What defines a casting cycle is not its output, but its **trace continuity**.

Cycles may be layered, interrupted, multiplied, or diffused. They are the behavior of the system—not its representation.

Casting cycles can be logged, interpreted, or re-cast. They cannot be predicted in advance.
