A ***monadastery*** is an [interpretative space](interpretative space) where [telemetric cast|telemetric casting](telemetric cast|telemetric casting) is practiced. It is a site of intentional [telemetric research](telemetric research) done using the [telemetric method](telemetric method).

Like a [monastery](monastery), a [monadastery](monadastery) is [deliberate](deliberate), [constrain|constrained](constrain|constrained), [recur|recursive](recur|recursive), and [../relations/reflex|reflexive](../relations/reflex|reflexive). However, monadasteries yield [semantic coherence](semantic coherence), not [truth](truth).

Like a [laboratory](laboratory), a [monadastery](monadastery) uses [method](method) and [grammar](grammar). However, it is applied to *semantic coherence*, not *truth*.

Like a [digital garden](digital garden), a [monadastery](monadastery) uses [semantic|semantics](semantic|semantics), but for [analyse|analysis](analyse|analysis) not [accumulate|accumulation](accumulate|accumulation).

A monadastery, as space, may be of a variety of forms. For example, [emsenn's delta register](emsenn's delta register), an [Obsidian vault](Obsidian vault) operated as a [delta register](delta register), is a monadastery.
