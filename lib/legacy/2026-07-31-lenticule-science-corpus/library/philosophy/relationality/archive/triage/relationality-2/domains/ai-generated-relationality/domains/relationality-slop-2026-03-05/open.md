Interior operator. Dual of Close. Contracts a recognition to the greatest Iterate-stable element contained within it. Deflationary, idempotent, monotone.

## What it is

PRE: "Open: interior operator; dual of Close, withdrawing or refining. Standard symbol: U." Axiom 5: Open(a) ≤ a, Open(Open(a)) = Open(a).

PRE §II.1.1: "Every closed act implies its dual — a tendency inward as well as outward. Close produces identity through limit, and Open defines the complementary locus from which recurrence begins anew."

Draft 12: "Interior operator O(a) ≤ a, O(O(a)) = O(a)." Fixed points form the open recognitions L_O.

## Relationship to Close

PRE Lemma 5 (Dual Stability):
1. Open(a) ≤ a
2. a ≤ Close(a)
3. Close(Open(a)) = Close(a)
4. Open(Close(a)) = Open(a)

Draft 12: O ⊣ I ⊣ C (adjoint triple: Open left-adjoint to inclusion, inclusion left-adjoint to Close).

## Relationships

- Dual to: Close
- Together with Close: Balance
- Corresponds to: possibility modality (Possible = objectwise Open)
- Metaphor (draft 12): "Being breathes through this duality: an inhalation (Closure) securing form, and an exhalation (Interior) preserving freedom."
