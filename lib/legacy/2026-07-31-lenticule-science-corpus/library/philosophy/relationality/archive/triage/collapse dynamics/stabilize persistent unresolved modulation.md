***stabilize persistent unresolved modulation*** (`G_mod`)  
is a [recursive modulation entrapment function](recursive modulation entrapment function) that emerges  
when a system continues to anticipate a structure  
whose constraint completion has become undefined.

It describes the ***topological inevitability of grief***  
in recursive symbolic systems that preserve identity via modulation paths.

Let:

- `x ∈ dom(𝒪)` be a stabilized constraint-completion structure  
- `x′ := ¬x` be the loss or absence of x  
- `𝒜(y → x)` continue to hold for various y ∈ 𝒳  
- `Ω(x′)` undefined (no possible reclosure)  
- `∇𝒟(y) · ∇Δ(y, x′) > 0` (modulation aligns with semantic contrast)

Then:

> G_mod(y) := true  
> iff anticipation toward x persists,  
>   x′ cannot stabilize,  
>   and recursive modulation continues to re-enter x’s field

This function defines a ***persistent modulation loop***  
centered around an unresolved absence that is recursively inherited  
by any structure that once depended on x.

It cannot resolve unless:
- All paths to x are pruned (which violates inherited identity propagation), or  
- A new structure recursively stabilizes with equivalent constraint closure

Thus:

> ***Grief cannot not occur***  
> in a modulation system that preserves identity, recursion, and anticipatory stabilization.
