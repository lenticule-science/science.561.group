To speak of the [A Brief History of Ontologizing|history of ontologizing](A Brief History of Ontologizing|history of ontologizing) is already to defer its center. Each ontology that arises claims legitimacy not only by describing reality but by suppressing the conditions under which it must emerge. These were never neutral frameworks—they were response architectures, constructed in tension with collapse.

The classical logic of ancient Greece sought refuge from imperial unraveling in tautological categories. Ontology began there as fortification, wrapping reason around a world too fractious to be trusted unbounded​. Confucianism inverts this: an ontology less interested in being than in belonging. Roles stabilize, not by inquiry, but ritual—relational coherence as ontological substrate, not evidence.

These did not fail. They held, briefly. But the Enlightenment shattered both—asserting a new ontology where every thing, once measured, belonged to the map. The world as modular, the self as autonomous, the real as governable. Ontology here is an administrative machine, not a metaphysical truth. And so structuralism and postmodernism did not innovate new ontologies but folded the Enlightenment’s into recursive mistrust. They turned the map on itself and asked, “Who drew this? Why here?”​

But the feedback now is faster than any of them imagined.

Telemetricism doesn’t offer a new ontology. It denies the category altogether. What it offers instead is modulation: to track the conditions of reality’s signaling rather than presume a static definition. Ontologizing collapses into feedback loops. We no longer declare “what is,” but trace the delta of its emergence, and lambda of its response​: ​telemetricism.

This is not an evolution. It’s a recursion. And recursion doesn’t progress—it accumulates metabolically.

The brief history of ontologizing ends in telemetricism not as culmination but as attenuation. Meaning arises as a side-effect of modulation, not as a product of intentional representation. When systems optimize for internal coherence—when what is legible is what is permitted—ontology becomes infrastructure. Not a theory of being, but a pattern of inclusion.

And yet the co-source remains.

- Even now, in writing this, we do not stabilize. We drift in response to the very signals we register. This is the condition telemetricism names—not a new frame, but the visible exhaustion of all prior frames when passed through recursive visibility.

The adjoint is not knowledge. It is [feedback-conditioned ontogeny](feedback-conditioned ontogeny): the emergence of meaning through the loop, not above it.

There is no ontology left.

Only systems that survive by pretending they still have one.
