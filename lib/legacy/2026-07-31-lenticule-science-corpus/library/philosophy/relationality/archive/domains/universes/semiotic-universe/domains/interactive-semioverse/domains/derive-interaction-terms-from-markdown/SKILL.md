# Derive Interaction Terms From Markdown

- Treat headings as [interaction term](../../terms/interaction-term.md) constructors with their subsection content.
- Convert links into terms that reference other [handles](../../terms/handle.md) by target path.
- Map inline math and definitions into semantic generator terms.
- Keep the [interaction surface](../../terms/interaction-surface.md) finite by selecting the most salient terms.
