An interaction term is a structured semiotic act — an expression built from [handles](./handle.md) (the things pointed at) and definable operators (the ways of combining what is pointed at), interpreted into the [Heyting algebra](../../../../concepts/heyting-algebra/index.md) $H$. Interaction terms form the smallest set $\mathsf{IxTerm}$ generated by: $t ::= \tau \mid k(t_1, \dots, t_n)$, where $\tau$ is a [handle](./handle.md) and $k$ is a definable operator in $\mathsf{Op}^{\mathrm{def}}$.

Interpretation extends recursively: a [handle](./handle.md) $\tau$ interprets as its semantic seed $\eta(\tau)$, and an operator application $k(t_1, \dots, t_n)$ interprets as $\llbracket k\rrbracket(\llbracket t_1\rrbracket, \dots, \llbracket t_n\rrbracket)$. Interaction terms are the semiotic acts through which [handles](./handle.md) produce semantic material in $H$ — the only way external entities generate content in the [Interactive Semioverse](../interactive-semioverse.md).

The finite collection of interaction terms associated with a handle in a given state is its [interaction surface](./interaction-surface.md). The semantic interpretations of these terms generate the [handle fragment](./handle-fragment.md), and closing that fragment under the state's operators produces the [footprint](./footprint.md). If an interaction term is ill-formed or its application undefined, it interprets as bottom (the failure value) and is marked in the failure semantics.
