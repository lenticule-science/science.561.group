Secondary contradictions are contradictions within a process that do not
occupy the decisive position at a given stage. They can intensify,
recede, and under changed conditions become principal
[@mao-on-contradiction-1937].

Treating secondary contradictions as irrelevant is an error; the concept
marks subordination, not insignificance.
