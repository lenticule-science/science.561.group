---
title: "Phenomenology"
description: "The study of experience as it is lived, before theories are applied to it — from Husserl's 'back to the things themselves' through Heidegger's being-in-the-world and Merleau-Ponty's embodied perception."
enables: relational-ontology
---

The philosophical tradition that studies the structure of experience itself. Husserl's phenomenological reduction, Heidegger's being-in-the-world, Merleau-Ponty's embodied perception. Phenomenology insists that experience is potential relating, not passive recording — which makes it a natural ally of relational ontology.
