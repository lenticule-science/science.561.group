Immanence is the claim that causes, structures, and standards of
critique are internal to the forms being analyzed.

In dialectical method, immanent critique evaluates a position using that
position's own claims and commitments, showing how internal tensions
produce revision or breakdown.

In metaphysics, immanence is often contrasted with transcendence:
explanation is sought within being, relation, or process itself, not in
an external principle standing outside the world.
