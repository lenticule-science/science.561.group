The theory of pramāṇas (valid means of cognition) is the epistemological framework within which Indian formal logic operates. Dignāga and Dharmakīrti developed the Buddhist version, recognizing two pramāṇas: perception (pratyakṣa) and inference (anumāna). The tradition asks the same fundamental question as Western epistemology — what counts as knowledge and how is it produced? — but arrives at different answers through different methods.
