**Evidence** in the [relationality](../_index.md) framework is the structural record that [witnessing](witnessing.md) produces: what supports or denies a claim, accumulated by the system's relating activity. Evidence is the substance that fills the form a [recognition](recognition.md) has registered — the records that determine where in [settlement](settlement.md) space a claim ends up sitting.

Evidence is *two-sided*: every claim accumulates evidence both for and against, and the two sides are independent rather than dual. Affirming evidence is recorded when relating produces witnessings that support the claim; denying evidence is recorded when relating produces witnessings that work against it. These are separate accumulations, not a single signed quantity.

## Two kinds of evidence, not one

A common error is to treat evidence as a single quantity — a thumb on a scale that can tilt one way or the other. The framework treats evidence as *two parallel quantities*: how much affirming evidence has been recorded, and how much denying evidence has been recorded. The two quantities together determine the claim's settlement, and they can take any combination of values.

The framework recognizes the distinction because the four resulting configurations are structurally different:

- **Affirming evidence high, denying low** — the claim is settled toward [true](true.md).
- **Affirming evidence low, denying high** — the claim is settled toward [false](false.md).
- **Both high** — the claim is settled with evidence in both directions; the system has [recognized](recognition.md) genuine contradiction or controversy. The framework treats this as a positive datum rather than as a defect to be resolved by collapsing one side.
- **Both low** — the claim is unsettled; no evidence has accumulated. This is the [absurd](absurd.md) corner, where the claim holds (if it holds at all) only on bare assertion without evidential support.

These four configurations correspond to the four corners of the framework's [settlement](settlement.md) structure, and to the four canonical evidence values of the framework's mathematical [bilattice](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/bilattice.md).

## Evidence is positive

Evidence in the framework's sense is always *positive achievement*. Affirming evidence is something witnessed *for* the claim; denying evidence is something witnessed *against*. The mere absence of affirming evidence is not denying evidence — it is just absence, the [absurd](absurd.md) state of un-witnessed-ness. The framework's distinction between *no evidence* and *negative evidence* rests on this: both are achievements, not defaults, and the two are structurally distinct.

This positivity is what distinguishes a claim that has been refuted (denying evidence has been recorded) from a claim that has not yet been examined (no evidence has been recorded). Refutation is achieved by witnessing-against; un-examination is the default that no witnessing has yet altered. The framework's [false](false.md) terms develops the implications of this distinction in detail.

## Evidence and the math layer

The framework's mathematical articulation makes the two-sided structure precise. In the [predication fiber algebra](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/predication-fiber-algebra.md), the value at each [predication cell](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/predication-cell.md) is a pair $\langle a_+, a_- \rangle$ recording affirming evidence in the first component and denying evidence in the second. The two parallel orders of the [bilattice](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/bilattice.md) — knowledge and truth — read these components dually:

- The knowledge order rises with both kinds of evidence — it measures how much evidence has accumulated, regardless of direction.
- The truth order rises with affirming evidence and falls with denying evidence — it measures the direction of tilt.

This dual structure is what the framework calls [evidence in the math voice](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/evidence.md): the role borne by the two components of a twist-product value.

## Evidence and time

Evidence accumulates monotonically in the framework's mathematical layer, via the [settling monotonicity](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/settling-monotonicity.md) condition: knowledge-order positions rise as inquiry proceeds, never fall. This is the formal expression of an honesty commitment: once evidence has been recorded, the framework does not erase it. New evidence can shift the truth-axis tilt — pushing toward affirmation or denial — but cannot retroactively un-witness what has been witnessed.

The practical consequence: a claim that was settled toward affirmation can become settled paraconsistently if new denying evidence arrives, but it cannot be unsettled to the un-witnessed state. The framework's evolution under settling is irreversible in this sense.
