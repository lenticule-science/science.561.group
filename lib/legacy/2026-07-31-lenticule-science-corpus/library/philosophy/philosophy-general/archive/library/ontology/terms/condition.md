---
title: Condition
aliases:
  - conditions
definition: a quality of the state of a thing at a given time
---

A condition is a [quality|quality](quality|quality) of the [state](state) of a [thing](thing) at a given [time](time). That is, at any moment, a thing is in a state, defined by its qualities, and the state of any one of those qualities in that moment is a condition of that thing.
