A **relational entity** is a stable pattern constituted by acts of [relating](relating.md). It is not a substance that possesses intrinsic properties and then enters into relations. It is what it is because of the relations it sustains, and it would be something different if those relations were different.

The relational entity is the [relationality](../_index.md) framework's answer to the question *what kinds of things are there?* The answer is structurally minimal: there are stable configurations of relating, and entities are those stable configurations.

## What stability means

For an entity to be a *stable* pattern of relations, the relating that constitutes it must reach local equilibrium: further acts of relating do not dissolve or significantly transform the configuration. A stone on a desk is stable because the relating of gravity, friction, and surface continues to reproduce the same configuration. A school club is stable because the relating of meetings, members, and rooms continues to reproduce its structure. When the relating that produces an entity ceases or radically changes, the entity is no longer there — not because something has been removed from the world, but because the configuration that *was* the entity is no longer being produced.

In the framework's mathematical layer, stability has a formal counterpart: a relational entity's [settlement](settlement.md) reaches a local fixed point, where additional witnessing through [predication](../../../../math/theories/voidmath/terms/predication.md) leaves the settlement position unchanged. The entity persists when its settlement persists; it changes when its settlement moves under new relating; it ends when its settlement disperses.

## Stability is not finality

Stability under relating is not closure against further inquiry. An entity that is stable now may settle differently when new relations are introduced — its constituent relating extends, the configuration shifts, the entity becomes a different (or no) entity. Substance metaphysics treats persistence as a property of the substrate; relational ontology treats persistence as ongoing achievement. The persistence is real; it is also doing work.

## Contrast with substance

In substance metaphysics, an entity has an essence independent of its relations. Properties are predicated of a substance that persists through change. In [relational ontology](../../western/theories/metaphysics/theories/ontology/terms/relational-ontology.md), there is no substrate behind the relations. The entity *is* the stable configuration of relations. Remove the relations and there is no residual thing — there is nothing.

This is not a softening of realism. Relational entities are no less real than substances; they are real in a different way. A school club is not less of a thing because it is constituted by ongoing meetings rather than by an essence; it is *that kind of thing*. The category of *stably-related-pattern* is a category the framework treats as fully populated by real things.

## Scale and composition

A relational entity can be at any scale. The same structural account covers a stone, a club, a person, a friendship, a galaxy: each is a stable pattern of relating at its own scale, with its own kinds of constituting acts. The framework does not privilege one scale or kind. Composition — multiple entities forming a larger entity — is itself a configuration of relating that, when stable, is its own entity.
