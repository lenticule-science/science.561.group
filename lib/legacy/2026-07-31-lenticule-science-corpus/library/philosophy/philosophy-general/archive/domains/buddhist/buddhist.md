Philosophical traditions rooted in Buddhist thought. The library's current content centers on logic (the catuṣkoṭi, pramāṇa theory) but the tradition encompasses metaphysics, ethics, epistemology, and philosophy of mind developed across South, East, and Southeast Asian contexts over two and a half millennia.
