# Existentialism

The philosophical tradition organized around the claim that existence precedes essence. Spans Kierkegaard's confrontation with faith, Heidegger's being-in-the-world, Sartre's radical freedom, de Beauvoir's existentialist feminism, and Camus's absurdism.

Existentialism overlaps with nihilism — both confront the absence of inherent meaning — but existentialism insists meaning is made through action rather than given or absent. Absurdism (Camus) occupies a third position: the absurd persists regardless of what one chooses, and the response is revolt rather than meaning-creation.
