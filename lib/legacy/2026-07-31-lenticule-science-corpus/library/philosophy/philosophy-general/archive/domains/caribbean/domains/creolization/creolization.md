# Creolization

Édouard Glissant's central concept. Creolization names the process by which cultures, languages, and identities form through encounter — not the melting pot's synthesis or colonialism's assimilation, but the production of something irreducibly new and unpredictable. Opacity — the right to not be made transparent — is the epistemological counterpart: the insistence that creolized knowledge cannot be extracted from the relations that sustain it.
