A loose cluster of 20th-century French thinkers whose work, often in translation, reshaped the Anglophone humanities from the 1970s onward. The label is imperfect — it's an Anglophone reception category, not a school — but it names a real grouping: Bataille's general economy, Baudrillard's simulation, Deleuze and Guattari's schizoanalysis, Lyotard's postmodern condition.
