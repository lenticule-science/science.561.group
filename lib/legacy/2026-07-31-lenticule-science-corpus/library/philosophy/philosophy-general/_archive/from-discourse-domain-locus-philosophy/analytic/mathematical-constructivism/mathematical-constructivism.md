Mathematical [constructivism](../../epistemology/terms/constructivism.md) is a philosophy of mathematics that holds mathematical objects exist only when they have been constructed — that is, when a procedure, proof, or algorithm for producing them has been given. This contrasts with mathematical Platonism (mathematical objects exist independently of any construction) and formalism (mathematics is the manipulation of symbols according to rules, with no [ontological](../../metaphysics/theories/ontology/terms/ontology.md) commitment). Constructivism insists that existence claims require witnesses: to prove that a number with a certain [property](../../../../../terms/property.md) exists, one must produce it or give a method for producing it.

The strongest and most developed form of [constructivism](../../epistemology/terms/constructivism.md) is intuitionism, founded by [L. E. J. Brouwer](../../../../../../../../references/people/l-e-j-brouwer.md) in the early twentieth century. [Brouwer](../../../../../../../../references/people/l-e-j-brouwer.md) held that mathematics is a constructive mental activity grounded in the intuition of temporal succession. Mathematical statements are meaningful only to the extent that they correspond to constructions the mind can perform. From this position, the [law of excluded middle](./law-of-excluded-middle.md) — for any proposition P, either P or not-P — fails for infinite domains, because there are propositions for which neither a proof nor a refutation has been constructed.

[Arend Heyting](../../../../../../../../references/people/arend-heyting.md) formalized [intuitionistic logic](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/logic/texts/intuitionistic-logic.md), producing the logical system that results from constructive principles. In this logic, the connectives have computational meaning: a proof of "A and B" is a pair of proofs; a proof of "A or B" is a proof of one disjunct plus an indicator of which; a proof of "if A then B" is a method for transforming proofs of A into proofs of B. The algebraic semantics of this logic are [Heyting algebras](../../mathematics/concepts/heyting-algebra/index.md), which generalize Boolean algebras by replacing complementation with pseudo-complementation.

For this research program, [constructivism](../../epistemology/terms/constructivism.md) provides the philosophical rationale for the [semiotic universe](../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md)'s formal architecture. The [semiotic](../../../science/theories/linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) universe is built on a complete [Heyting algebra](../../mathematics/concepts/heyting-algebra/index.md) because meaning, like mathematical truth under [constructivism](../../epistemology/terms/constructivism.md), is not given in advance as a binary partition but constructed through relational processes — interpretation, closure, fixed-point iteration. The initial [semiotic](../../../science/theories/linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) structure is the [least fixed point](../../mathematics/concepts/fixed-point/index.md) of a composite closure: a structure that is constructed through its own operations, not discovered as a pre-existing entity. This constructive character connects the [semiotic](../../../science/theories/linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) formalism to [relational ontology](../../metaphysics/theories/ontology/terms/relational-ontology.md): both hold that what exists is constituted through activity, not independent of it.
