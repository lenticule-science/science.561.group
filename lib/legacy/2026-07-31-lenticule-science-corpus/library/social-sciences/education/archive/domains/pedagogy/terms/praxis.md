[Praxis](./praxis.md) is the unity of reflection and action — thought that leads to action, action that is informed by thought, and the ongoing cycle between them. The concept is central to [Paulo Freire](../../../../general/people/paulo-freire.md)'s pedagogy and to the broader [critical pedagogy](../schools/critical-pedagogy/index.md) tradition [@freire_PedagogyOppressed_1970].

Freire argued that reflection without action is verbalism — mere intellectualism that changes nothing. Action without reflection is activism — doing without understanding, which cannot sustain transformation. Praxis is the synthesis: people reflect on their conditions, act to change them, reflect on the results of their action, and act again. This cycle is the engine of [conscientization](./conscientization.md).

Praxis has implications for education:

- **Learning is not preparation for action but a form of action.** Education that separates knowing from doing — that treats learning as theory to be applied later — breaks the cycle of praxis. [Dialogic education](./dialogic-education.md) restores it by posing problems drawn from the learners' actual conditions and pursuing understanding through engagement with those conditions.
- **Teaching is itself a practice.** The teacher is not an authority dispensing knowledge but a practitioner engaged in praxis — reflecting on and transforming their own practice. [Engaged pedagogy](./engaged-pedagogy.md) makes this explicit: the teacher is changed by the encounter.
- **Knowledge is produced through practice**, not discovered in abstraction and then applied. This aligns with [situated learning](../../learning/terms/situated-learning.md) and the traditions of [apprenticeship](../../knowledge-transmission/terms/apprenticeship.md), which understand knowledge as inseparable from the practice that produces it.

The concept extends beyond Freire. [Anarchist pedagogies](../schools/anarchist-pedagogies/index.md) practice praxis through [prefigurative education](./prefigurative-education.md) — building the world they want through the educational structures they create. [Black radical pedagogies](../schools/black-radical-pedagogies/index.md) practice it through [freedom schools](./freedom-school.md) and study groups that connect analysis to organizing. [Indigenous pedagogies](../schools/indigenous-pedagogies/index.md) practice it through [land-based education](./land-based-education.md), where knowledge and practice are never separated.
