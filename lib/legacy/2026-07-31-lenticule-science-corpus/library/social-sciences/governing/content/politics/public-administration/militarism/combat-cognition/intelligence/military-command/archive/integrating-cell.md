An integrating cell is a staff grouping that connects multiple functions
so a headquarters can coordinate current or future operations across
specialties [@reeves2018; @staffintegration2025].

The concept matters because command depends on more than functional
expertise. A headquarters also needs places where intelligence,
operations, sustainment, protection, and other staff functions are made
to converge around the same operational problem.
