# Predicated Kyriarchial Work

A theory of [kyriarchial work](../../_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics: kyriarchial work structures as specific predication settlements over a kyriarchial-work-themed predicate category. Workplace hierarchies, exploitation patterns, valorization regimes, and resistance assemblages — the kyriarchial work vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this predication substrate.

Sibling to [predicated-work](../predicated-work/) (also a theory of work). Predicated-kyriarchial-work is the bilattice-substrate-native synthesis; it combines the formal rigor of predicated-work with the critical power analysis of kyriarchial-work.

Following the predication-categories research convention. See the [predication-categories index](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) for frontmatter/relation details.

## What this theory commits to

Predicated-kyriarchial-work analyzes how mathematical structures of work intersect with systems of domination:

1. **Formalization of power** — kyriarchial relations are encoded as specific structures within the predication-categories mathematical framework
2. **Intersectional settling dynamics** — how different axes of domination interact in the settling dynamics of work predications
3. **Valorization as closure structure** — how societal determinations of work value emerge from closure rules operating on intersecting identity fibers
4. **Exploitation as network propagation** — how workplace exploitation spreads through bilattice-network structures patterned after kyriarchal distributions
5. **Resistance as cohomological obstruction** — how collective action emerges as non-trivial cohomology classes in the kyriarchial work complex

## Key Structural Commitments

- **Predicate fiber of identity**: Each worker's position in intersecting hierarchies (gender, race, class, sexuality, ability, citizenship) forms an identity fiber over which work predications vary
- **Closure rules of domination**: Societal rules that determine what work counts as "valuable", "skilled", or "appropriate" for different identity positions
- **Exploitation networks**: How surplus extraction propagates through kinship, workplace, and market networks structured by kyriarchal principles
- **Resistance sheaves**: How local actions of resistance glue together to form global obstruction classes against kyriarchial work structures
