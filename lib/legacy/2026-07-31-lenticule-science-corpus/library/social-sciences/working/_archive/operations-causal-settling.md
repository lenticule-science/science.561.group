# Operations causal settling

The substrate-algebraic structure of *operational time* — which dispatch events causally precede which others, which are independent (parallelizable), which are unreachable from each other across the operations base. Instantiates the [Lorentzian causal-structure settling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/lorentzian-causal-settling.md) on the operations base's event set.

The corpus-wide apex/void cohomology, applied here, gives a clean structural reading: the **apex** of the operations causal settling is the set of *causally-related task pairs* (one task's completion enables or constrains the other); the **void** is the set of *operationally-independent task pairs* (parallelizable, no enable/constrain relation); and the **apex of the void** is the set of *structurally-independent work-streams* — task pairs that *never* become causally related across any settlement of the operations base.

## Definition

Let $E_{\mathrm{ev}} \subseteq E_{\mathrm{op}}$ be the subset of *dispatch events* — entity-cells of class `event` representing concrete operational acts (a task being dispatched, a decision being recorded, a release being shipped, a ratification being voted). The dispatch events are the "spacetime points" of the operations base.

Let $\prec_0 \subseteq E_{\mathrm{ev}} \times E_{\mathrm{ev}}$ be the **direct-enablement relation**: $(e_1, e_2) \in \prec_0$ iff a closure rule of the operations settling has fired with antecedent containing a cell about $e_1$ and consequent containing a cell about $e_2$. Operationally: $e_1$ directly enables $e_2$ iff completing or recording $e_1$ was a direct prerequisite for $e_2$ to be dispatched, recorded, or settled.

The **operations causal settling** $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}$ is:

- *Cells*: pairs $(e_1, e_2) \in E_{\mathrm{ev}} \times E_{\mathrm{ev}}$.
- *Initial*: $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}, 0}(e_1, e_2) = \mathbf{t}$ if $(e_1, e_2) \in \prec_0$, $\bot$ otherwise.
- *Closure*: $T_{\mathrm{caus}}(v)(e_1, e_3) := v(e_1, e_3) \vee \exists e_2 \in E_{\mathrm{ev}}.\,[v(e_1, e_2) \wedge v(e_2, e_3)]$ — Boolean transitive closure.
- *Settling*: $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}, \kappa}(e_1, e_2) = \mathbf{t}$ iff there's a directed $\prec_0$-chain $e_1 \to \cdots \to e_2$.

The settling encodes the **full causal precedence order** $\preceq_{\mathrm{op}}$ on $E_{\mathrm{ev}}$ generated by direct enablement. By the [timelike/spacelike apex/void identification](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/lorentzian-apex-void-as-timelike-spacelike.md), the apex/void structure of $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the timelike/spacelike dichotomy of operational events.

## The apex: causally-related work

The apex cone $\mathrm{Apex}_{v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}}$ is the set of pairs $(e_1, e_2)$ with $e_1 \preceq_{\mathrm{op}} e_2$ — the events for which one is in the operational past of the other. Every cell in the apex records a *causal-precedence-acquisition event*: the closure firing at which transitive precedence between $e_1$ and $e_2$ became settled.

Operationally:

- A task $T_2$ that depends on the output of task $T_1$ has $T_1 \preceq_{\mathrm{op}} T_2$ — the dispatch of $T_1$ causally precedes the dispatch of $T_2$, even if many intermediate events sit between them.
- The full ancestry chain of any settlement is the apex restricted to that settlement's antecedents.
- Every closure-rule firing extends the apex by at least one cell: the consequent's enabling pair becomes $\mathbf{t}$.

By the [void-cone cohomology theorem](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/void-cone-cohomology.md), the apex's $H^1$ counts these firing events. For an operations settling, $H^1(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}})$ is the **causal-acquisition cohomology** — the cohomological tally of every moment when one operational event became (transitively) settled-as-prior-to another.

## The void: operationally-independent work

The void cone $V_\infty := \{(e_1, e_2) : v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}, \kappa}(e_1, e_2) = \bot\}$ restricted to $e_1 \neq e_2$ — the cells *neither* of which becomes settled — is the set of pairs $(e_1, e_2)$ with $e_1 \not\preceq_{\mathrm{op}} e_2$ and (by symmetric application) $e_2 \not\preceq_{\mathrm{op}} e_1$. These are pairs of events that *neither causally precede each other nor depend on each other*.

In Lorentzian terms (per the [apex/void = timelike/spacelike theorem](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/lorentzian-apex-void-as-timelike-spacelike.md)), these are **spacelike-separated** — there is no operational signal that propagates between them. In operational terms: these pairs are *parallelizable*. They can be dispatched in either order, or simultaneously, without one constraining the other.

The void cone is the **parallelization budget** of the operations base. Two facts follow:

1. *Increasing the void increases parallelism.* An organizational design choice that decouples two task streams (removes the closure rules that would have created direct enablement) increases the void cone, expanding the parallelization budget.
2. *Decreasing the void removes coordination overhead but creates structural fragility.* A redesign that makes two tasks causally-related (introduces a direct enablement closure rule) removes them from the void; this often increases coordination quality but eliminates the option to dispatch them in parallel.

## The apex of the void: structural work-streams

The most structurally-loaded invariant: $H^0_{\mathrm{apex of Void}}(v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}) := \{\text{cells permanently } \bot\}$ — pairs of events that *never* become causally connected, no matter how the closure dynamics unfold to their fixed point.

These pairs are the **structurally-independent work-streams** of the organization: events that belong to operationally-disconnected sub-systems with no closure rule bridging them. Concretely:

- An accounts-payable event and a software-deployment event in a typical company are *probably* in distinct apex-of-void components — there is no closure rule that ties them causally.
- A code-review approval event and a payroll-processing event in the same company are also probably in distinct components.
- The HR division's grievance-filing event and the engineering division's incident-response event are in distinct components iff no governance or operational rule cross-links them.

The number of distinct connected components in $H^0_{\mathrm{apex of Void}}$ — equivalently, the number of operationally-independent sub-systems — is the **work-stream count** of the operations base. This is a structural invariant of organizational design: dimension-flavored, recording how many independent operational "spatial directions" exist.

For a unitary, fully-coupled organization (every event eventually causally-related to every other through some chain): work-stream count = 1.

For a holding company or federated polity where divisions operate independently: work-stream count = number of divisions.

For a project-based structure with cross-cutting infrastructure: work-stream count is intermediate, depending on how thoroughly the infrastructure causally bridges the projects.

This is the operational analog of *spatial dimension* in the Lorentzian setting (per the [apex/void theorem](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/lorentzian-apex-void-as-timelike-spacelike.md) §*What dimensions mean*). The apex-of-void measures *operational extent in independent directions*.

## The diamond example, operationally

The diamond causal-set example from [lorentzian-causal-settling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/lorentzian-causal-settling.md) has a clean operational reading:

$E_{\mathrm{ev}} = \{a, b, c, d\}$, $\prec_0 = \{(a, b), (a, c), (b, d), (c, d)\}$ — event $a$ enables both $b$ and $c$; both $b$ and $c$ are required to enable $d$; the diamond structure of a *parallelized prerequisite chain*.

Operational instance: $a$ is "kickoff meeting"; $b$ is "design review"; $c$ is "infrastructure setup"; $d$ is "integration test." The kickoff enables both review and setup independently; the integration test requires both completed.

- *Apex* (5 cells): $\{(a, b), (a, c), (b, d), (c, d), (a, d)\}$ — the kickoff causally precedes everything, both branches precede the integration, kickoff transitively precedes integration.
- *Void apex* (2 cells): $\{(b, c), (c, b)\}$ — the design review and the infrastructure setup are *operationally independent*, parallelizable, neither causally precedes the other.
- *Causal-acquisition $H^1 = \mathbb{Z}^1$*: one transitivity firing, $(a, d)$ via either branch. The cohomological generator records the acquisition-of-dependency event.
- *Apex-of-void $H^0_{\mathrm{apex of Void}}$ at the fixed point*: the cells $\{(b, c), (c, b)\}$ — $b$ and $c$ are *permanently* causally-disconnected from each other, even after the full settling closes. This is a fixed-point invariant of $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}$, not a stage-indexed quantity: $b$ and $c$ never become causally related, no matter how the operations base evolves under its closure rules.

Two distinct readings to keep separate:

- The **fixed-point apex-of-void** above is the structural-spacelike content — pairs that *no* closure trajectory will ever connect.
- A separate, **stagewise parallelism metric** — at intermediate stage $\tau$, the number of $\bot$-cells in $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}, \tau}$ — measures *currently-available parallelism* and shrinks monotonically as the settling closes. For the diamond, counting off-diagonal ordered pairs (so $|E_{\mathrm{ev}} \times E_{\mathrm{ev}}| - |\Delta_{E_{\mathrm{ev}}}| = 16 - 4 = 12$ pairs in scope), the metric starts at $12 - |{\prec_0}| = 12 - 4 = 8$ off-diagonal $\bot$-cells before any closure firing and shrinks to $12 - 5 = 7$ at the fixed point — the single transitivity firing settles $(a, d)$, removing one cell from the $\bot$-count. Including diagonal cells (which are $\bot$ throughout under the strict precedence convention) shifts both numbers by $4$ but does not change the dynamics. This metric is operationally useful but is *not* the apex-of-void invariant; conflating them is a category error the corpus warns against (per the [void-cone structure narrative](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/prose/teaching/explanations/void-cone-structure.md) on transient-vs-permanent void).

## Multi-observer operations causal settlings

By the multi-observer reading of [lorentzian-causal-settling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/lorentzian-causal-settling.md), each operational role-bearer (each office-holder, each team, each individual) has their own *operational past* — the events causally upstream of them under their visible substrate. Different role-bearers have different operational pasts, hence different observer-relative operations causal settlings.

Two role-bearers' shared past is the **operational causal diamond**: events both can causally trace to. Their disjoint pasts are *operationally hidden* from each other — the substrate-algebraic content of "you and I worked on different things this quarter."

The **operational consensus structure** across role-bearers is the agreement of their causal settlings on the shared past — what events both bearers see as having causally enabled their current settlements. Disagreement on the shared past is operationally serious: it is the substrate-algebraic content of "we are working from different histories of how we got here."

## Where used

- [witness-as-increment](../morphisms/witness-as-increment.md) — every increment is a dispatch event $e \in E_{\mathrm{ev}}$; the operations causal settling is the closure of these increment-events under direct enablement.
- [handoff-cobordism](handoff-cobordism.md) — a handoff cobordism's closure ordinal is bounded below by the longest causal chain in the handoff's bulk: the bulk's settling cannot complete faster than its causal-precedence depth.
- [retrospect-via-cosettling](../morphisms/retrospect-via-cosettling.md) — retrospect on $v^{\mathrm{caus}}_{\mathrm{op}}$ produces backward causal pre-images: the cosettling's preimages are alternative causal histories consistent with the same outcome. This is the substrate-algebraic content of "what could have caused this outcome other than what actually did?"
- [cadence-as-step-shift-rg-flow](../theorems/cadence-as-step-shift-rg-flow.md) — at each cadence boundary, the operations causal settling restricted to events at depth $\leq n$ is the step-$n$ causal substructure. The step-shift extends this by one cadence-level, adding a new layer of causal events that the next-finer cadence cannot resolve.
- [restructure-as-visible-automorphism](../theorems/restructure-as-visible-automorphism.md) — a restructuring is causally-coherent iff it preserves the operations causal settling's apex/void structure. A restructuring that introduces a new direct-enablement edge changes the void cone (parallelism budget) and is thus substantive even if its relabeling component is trivial.

## Open downstream

- *Causal-set physics ↔ operations-management.* The Myrheim-Meyer dimension estimator from causal-set physics gives a discrete-causal-set analog of spatial dimension. Applied to operations causal settlings, it should give a *parallelism-dimension estimator* — a single scalar measuring how parallelizable the operational base is, independent of its event count. Open whether this matches existing organizational-design metrics (e.g., decoupling indices, modularity scores).
- *Black-hole horizons in operations.* A black-hole horizon in causal-set physics is a void-cone-flavored boundary (events outside causally disconnected from inside). The operational analog: a *closed sub-organization* (a black-box subsidiary, a classified division, an opaque vendor) whose internal events are causally disconnected from the rest of the operations base. The cohomological signature of such a horizon — and what it costs the larger organization in lost causal content — is a downstream computation.
- *Quantum-superposition operational events.* The corpus's continuous-valued / stochastic settlings allow events with amplitude rather than Boolean values. The operational analog would be an event whose enablement of downstream events is *probabilistic* — e.g., a vote whose passage probabilistically enables an action. Whether the existing infrastructure suffices, or whether amplitude-valued events need new machinery, is open.
