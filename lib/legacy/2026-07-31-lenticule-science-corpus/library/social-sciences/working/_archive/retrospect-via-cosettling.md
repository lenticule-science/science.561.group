# Retrospect via cosettling

The substrate-algebraic operation behind retrospective inference, post-hoc audit, and after-action review on an [operations settling](../objects/operations-settling.md) $v^{\mathrm{op}}$.

A **retrospect** of an operations settling at closure stage $\kappa$, looking back to a stage $\tau < \kappa$, is the [cosettling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/cosettling.md) of $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$ at depth $\kappa - \tau$ under the closure operator $T$ generated by the operations closure-rule system $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{op}}$:

$$\rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa) \;:=\; (T^{-1})^{\kappa - \tau}(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa).$$

This is *not* an inverse of the forward operations dynamics. It is the multi-valued backward operation that asks: "of the operational states from which the closure could have produced $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$ in $\kappa - \tau$ steps, which are they?"

## What the retrospect computes

By [cosettling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/cosettling.md) and [iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells.md), the iterated cosettling at depth $\kappa - \tau$ is the set of operational states $v$ such that $T^{\kappa - \tau}(v) = v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$ — every state that, run forward through $\kappa - \tau$ closure steps, would have produced the closure state.

This set always contains the actual stage-$\tau$ state $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\tau$ (the recorded prior state lies in $(T^{-1})^{\kappa - \tau}(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$, by construction of the forward trajectory: see [backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory.md)). It also contains **off-trajectory pre-images** — states with cells from "the future" inserted into a present, where the missing precursor is then re-derived by a closure step. The presence of such off-trajectory pre-images is the substrate-algebraic content of *what the records could have looked like and still produced the same closure*.

The retrospect therefore distinguishes:

- **Cells whose presence at stage $\tau$ was necessary.** Every $v \in \rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$ contains these cells. They are forced by the closure to have been present.
- **Cells whose presence at stage $\tau$ was contingent.** Some elements of $\rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$ contain them, others do not. The settling could have proceeded with or without them at stage $\tau$ and still arrived at the same $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$.
- **Cells whose presence at stage $\tau$ was forbidden.** No element of $\rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$ contains them — they would have changed the trajectory's destination.

## Operational reading

The retrospect is the substrate-algebraic foundation of the after-action review:

- "What did we *have to* know by stage $\tau$ for things to have ended this way?" — the meet of all $v \in \rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$, the necessary content.
- "What could we *also* have known by stage $\tau$ without changing the outcome?" — the join of all $v \in \rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa)$ minus the meet, the contingent content.
- "What could we *not* have known by stage $\tau$?" — the complement in $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$, the cells that could not yet have appeared without altering the trajectory.

Per [cosettling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/cosettling.md), the retrospect is a **finite join-subsemilattice with top** in the strict regime — its maximum is $v^{\mathrm{op}}_\kappa$ itself (the trivial backward step that says "the closure was already present"), and there is no minimum in general. The lack of a canonical minimum is the substrate-algebraic content of the everyday observation that *retrospects do not have a canonical earliest moment of foreseeability*: there are typically multiple incomparable minimal pre-images, each a distinct possible "earliest stage at which we could have known."

## The conflation generator's role

The retrospect uses the **conflation generator** $-$ from the [work symmetry group](../objects/work-symmetry-group.md) natively. The conflation generator swaps the roles of $\bot$ and $\top$: silence becomes maximal evidence, contradiction becomes maximal absence. This is unusable as a forward operations dynamic (it reverses $\leq_k$), but the retrospect is *not* a forward dynamic — it is a multi-valued backward operation. In the cosettling, the conflation generator becomes a meaningful re-reading: a cell at $\bot$ in a backward pre-image $v$ is one whose "absence at stage $\tau$" is being asserted; under conflation, that absence is read as "maximal evidence of nothing-happening." This is the substrate-algebraic content of [audit-conflation](teaching/explanations/audit-conflation.md) — the audit reads silence as a recorded fact about the state.

The retrospect's compatibility with conflation distinguishes audit reasoning structurally from forward operations: forward operations live in the gauge subgroup $\langle \sim \rangle$ of $K_4$ (which preserves $\leq_k$), while audit reasoning lives in the full $K_4$ (which uses $-$ on backward states). The two are formally distinct symmetry groups acting on overlapping data, and the audit's freedom to use $-$ is what gives audit conclusions structural content that forward operations cannot generate.

## Composition with handoff cobordisms

For a [handoff cobordism](../objects/handoff-cobordism.md) $H : \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{prior}} \to \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{next}}$ with out-boundary value $v^+_H$, the receiving settling $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next}}$ has opening valuation $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,0} \supseteq v^+_H$ on $B_H$ by the cobordism's boundary compatibility. By settling-monotonicity, every stage of the receiving settling satisfies $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,\tau} \supseteq v^+_H$ on $B_H$.

Two distinct retrospect claims need separating:

**(realized-trajectory claim).** The actual stage-$\tau$ valuation $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,\tau}$ contains $v^+_H$ on $B_H$. This is forced by boundary monotonicity — the realized trajectory cannot fall below the handoff content. Since $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,\tau}$ lies in $\rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,\kappa})$ by [backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory.md), the retrospect always contains *at least one* element witnessing the handoff content.

**(meet claim — false in general).** It is *not* the case that every $v \in \rho_\tau(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next},\,\kappa})$ contains $v^+_H$ on $B_H$. The cosettling can include off-trajectory pre-images that lack handoff cells, provided the missing cells are re-derivable in the remaining $\kappa - \tau$ closure steps. The meet $\bigcap \rho_\tau$ is therefore not bounded below by $v^+_H$ in general.

The structural distinction is the substrate-algebraic content of an everyday operational tension. Two readings of the same handoff are formally available to a retrospective reviewer:

- *Trajectory-faithful retrospect.* The actual stage-$\tau$ state contains the handoff content. A reviewer who insists "what was actually known at $\tau$" is reading the trajectory and is bounded below by $v^+_H$.
- *Counterfactual retrospect.* Some hypothetical stage-$\tau$ state consistent with the closure has *less* than the handoff content. A reviewer who admits counterfactuals — "could we have arrived here by a different path?" — finds preimages without $v^+_H$ in $\rho_\tau$, witnessing that the handoff is not a closure-necessary feature of the destination.

The retrospect formalism does not adjudicate between these readings; it exposes both as well-defined operations on $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next}}$, and the difference between them is itself observable in the structure of $\rho_\tau$. The reviewer's *choice* of reading is the substrate-algebraic content of the audit's epistemological stance — whether it treats actual records as fixed or as one trajectory among consistent possibilities.

Going behind the handoff to the prior bearer's settling is a separate operation: retrospect on $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{prior}}$, not on $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{next}}$. By [restructure-as-visible-automorphism](../theorems/restructure-as-visible-automorphism.md), the two retrospects may sit in different visible-automorphism classes.

## Limits

The retrospect inherits all the structural limitations of the cosettling:

- **Multi-valued.** There is typically no unique "what we should have known by stage $\tau$" — only a poset of consistent possibilities.
- **No minimum in general.** No canonical "earliest possible knowledge" — incomparable minimal pre-images are typical.
- **Off-trajectory pre-images.** The retrospect can include pre-images that contain cells the actual settling did not have at stage $\tau$ (because the missing precursors are re-derivable in one closure step). These are not *what happened* — they are *what could have happened consistent with the closure*. Treating off-trajectory pre-images as if they were the actual stage-$\tau$ state is the substrate-algebraic form of *retrospective reconstruction error*.
- **Strict-regime only by default.** In Scott-continuous and aperiodic regimes, the cosettling at limit ordinals has additional structure that the strict-regime retrospect does not capture.

## Where used

- [audit-conflation](teaching/explanations/audit-conflation.md) — the retrospect is the formal mechanism of audit-conflation; the audit reads $\bot$-cells in backward pre-images as recorded facts via the conflation generator.
- [work-symmetry-group](../objects/work-symmetry-group.md) — the conflation generator $-$ becomes meaningful when applied to retrospect pre-images, even though it is not a valid forward dynamic.
- [handoff-cobordism](../objects/handoff-cobordism.md) — the handoff out-boundary $v^+_H$ is a floor on the *realized* trajectory of the receiving settling, but not on every preimage in $\rho_\tau$; the trajectory-faithful and counterfactual readings of the retrospect diverge at the handoff (see the *composition with handoff cobordisms* section).
- [restructure-as-visible-automorphism](../theorems/restructure-as-visible-automorphism.md) — retrospects on different bearers of the same operational role may sit in different visible-automorphism classes.
