Distributed sensemaking is the collective production of shared
understanding across multiple actors and organizations under conditions
where no single actor has complete control over information or action
[@kendrawachtendorf2006sensemaking].

Within emergent disaster response, the term matters because coordination
often depends on many participants discovering meaning and capacities
for action through their emerging relationships rather than receiving a
single authoritative picture from above [@kendrawachtendorf2006sensemaking].
