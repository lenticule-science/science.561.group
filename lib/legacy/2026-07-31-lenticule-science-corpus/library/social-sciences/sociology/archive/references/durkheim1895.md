# Durkheim 1895 — *The Rules of Sociological Method* (pending direct read)

Émile Durkheim. *Les Règles de la méthode sociologique*. Paris: Félix Alcan, 1895. Standard English: *The Rules of Sociological Method*, ed. Steven Lukes, trans. W. D. Halls (Free Press, 1982).

## Status

**Pending direct read.** This entry is an honest stub. *The Rules* is the founding methodological statement of the social-fact concept and one of the discipline's constitutive texts. The annotation here is to be promoted from a direct read.
