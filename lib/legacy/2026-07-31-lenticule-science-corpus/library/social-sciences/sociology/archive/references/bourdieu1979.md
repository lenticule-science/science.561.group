The companion canonical Bourdieu work to *Outline of a Theory of Practice* (Bourdieu 1972): the empirical-and-theoretical study of taste that operationalizes the *habitus*–*field*–*capital* triad through a survey-based analysis of French cultural consumption. The book reads taste — in food, music, dress, sport, the arts — as the practical expression of a class-positioned habitus, and reads cultural distinction as a primary mechanism through which class structure reproduces itself across generations. The empirical argument is the load-bearing demonstration of the analytical apparatus the earlier *Outline* introduced.
