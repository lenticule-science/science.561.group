# Predicated Kyriarchial Politics

A theory of [kyriarchial politics](../../_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics: kyriarchial politics structures as specific predication settlements over a kyriarchial-politics-themed predicate category. Authority relations, decision-making processes, sovereignty claims, political institutions, and polis formation — the kyriarchial politics vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this predication substrate.

Sibling to [predicated-politics](../predicated-politics/) (also a theory of politics). Predicated-kyriarchial-politics is the bilattice-substrate-native synthesis; it combines the formal rigor of predicated-politics with the critical power analysis of kyriarchial-politics.

Following the predication-categories research convention. See the [predication-categories index](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) for frontmatter/relation details.

## What this theory commits to

Predicated-kyriarchial-politics analyzes how mathematical structures of politics intersect with systems of domination:

1. **Formalization of power** — kyriarchial relations are encoded as specific structures within the predication-categories mathematical framework
2. **Intersectional authority dynamics** — how different axes of domination interact in the settling dynamics of authority and legitimacy predications
3. **Legitimacy as closure structure** — how societal determinations of legitimate authority emerge from closure rules operating on intersecting identity fibers
4. **Sovereignty as network propagation** — how self-determination claims and recognition/refusal dynamics spread through bilattice-network structures patterned after kyriarchal distributions
5. **Resistance as cohomological obstruction** — how collective political transformation emerges as non-trivial cohomology classes in the kyriarchial politics complex

## Key Structural Commitments

- **Predicate fiber of identity**: Each actor's position in intersecting hierarchies (gender, race, class, sexuality, ability, citizenship) forms an identity fiber over which political predications vary
- **Closure rules of domination**: Societal rules that determine what authority counts as "legitimate", what sovereignty claims count as "valid", or what decision-making counts as "democratic" for different identity positions
- **Authority networks**: How legitimacy propagates through kinship, community, market, and state networks structured by kyriarchal principles
- **Sovereignty sheaves**: How local self-determination practices glue together to form global obstruction classes against kyriarchial political structures
