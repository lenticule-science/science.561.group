# Bondy & Murty 2008 — Graph Theory

The standard alternative graduate textbook to [Diestel 2017](diestel2017.md); equally comprehensive, with somewhat heavier emphasis on the algorithmic and applied side of graph theory. The successor to the authors' earlier 1976 *Graph Theory with Applications*, with substantially expanded treatment.

## Why this is cited

Bondy & Murty serves as the corpus's secondary reference, useful when Diestel's treatment is sparse on a topic — particularly directed graphs (Bondy & Murty Ch. 2 is heavier than Diestel's §1.10), weighted graphs and shortest-path algorithmics (Ch. 7), and the algorithmic / matroid / connectivity perspective (Ch. 8 onwards). The [directed-graph](../objects/directed-graph.md), [weighted-graph](../objects/weighted-graph.md), [dag](../objects/dag.md), [topological-sort](../morphisms/topological-sort.md), and [transitive-closure](../morphisms/transitive-closure.md) notes follow Bondy & Murty's conventions where they diverge from Diestel.

## Notable claims

- §1.1–1.2 — graphs and isomorphism; bipartite graphs.
- §1.7 — line graphs.
- Ch. 2 — directed graphs, including DAGs, topological sort, and reachability.
- §4.1 — trees, forests, equivalent characterizations.
- §4.2 — bipartite graphs, the no-odd-cycle characterization.
- Ch. 7 — flow theory and weighted-graph optimization.
- §10.2 — Euler's formula and the planarity bound $E \leq 3V - 6$.
