# Nordström, Petersson & Smith 1990 — Programming in Martin-Löf's Type Theory

Bengt Nordström, Kent Petersson, and Jan M. Smith, *Programming in Martin-Löf's Type Theory: An Introduction* (International Series of Monographs on Computer Science 7), Oxford University Press, 1990.

A textbook treatment of [dependent-type-theory](../objects/dependent-type-theory.md) emphasising the *programming* reading: types as specifications, terms as programs, type-checking as verification. The companion volume to [Martin-Löf 1984](martin-lof1984.md) for those approaching from a software-engineering direction.

## Why cited here

- Practical worked examples of [pi-types](../objects/pi-type.md), [sigma-types](../objects/sigma-type.md), and [inductive-types](../objects/inductive-type.md) used as programming constructs.
- Source for the dependent-data-type tradition that produced [vec-as-dependent-type](../examples/vec-as-dependent-type.md) and similar examples.
- Out of print but freely available from the authors' homepages.
