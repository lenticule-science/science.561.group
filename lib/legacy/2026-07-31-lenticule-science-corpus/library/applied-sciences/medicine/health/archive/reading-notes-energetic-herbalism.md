## Chapter 1: The Language of Energetics
- [[energetic herbalism]] "is based on the truth that the [[essence|vital force]] of [[nature]] and the vital force of an individual [[human]] are one and the same" (p. 16)
- "[[framework]] that matches the pattern or spirit (warming, cooling, moving, sedating) of an herb to that of the imbalance (tension, stagnation, dryness) in the body" to help body restore balance "via the path of least resistance" (p. 18)
	- aim: treat whole person
	- "Through observations of Nature's patterns, the inherent self-regulating systems of the body are acknowledged and supported." (p. 19)
- elements of Nature: fire, water, earth, air, earth, wood, metal, ether
