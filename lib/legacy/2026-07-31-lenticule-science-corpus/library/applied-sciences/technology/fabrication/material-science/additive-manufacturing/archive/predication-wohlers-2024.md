# Wohlers Report 2024

Annual industry survey, the canonical source for AM market data: revenue, machine installed base, materials shipments, by region and process family. Available only by paid subscription; figures cited here are from public summaries and press releases.

**Headline figures (2023 calendar year).**

- Total AM industry revenue: ~$20.0 B (8.2% YoY growth, well below the ~25% sustained 2014–2019).
- Metal AM systems revenue: ~$3.7 B; system unit sales: ~3,750.
- Polymer AM systems revenue: $1.4 B.
- Materials revenue: ~$3.4 B (now growing faster than systems revenue, indicating maturation toward production use).
- Installed base of industrial AM systems crossed 100,000 units worldwide in 2023.

**Trends to chart.**

- Slowing system-revenue growth (post-COVID normalization, end of speculative boom).
- Rising materials-to-systems revenue ratio (sign of production-volume usage, not prototyping).
- Geographic shift: US share declining, China share rising sharply (now ~16% of global AM revenue, up from <8% in 2018).
- Polymer-process consolidation around HP MJF, EOS SLS, Carbon DLS for production; FFF dominant in prototyping by unit count but small in revenue.

**Why it matters here.** Industry data grounds the strategic claim that AM is past the hype peak and is now a real-but-niche manufacturing modality. Frames the [AM economic crossover question](questions/economic-crossover.md).
