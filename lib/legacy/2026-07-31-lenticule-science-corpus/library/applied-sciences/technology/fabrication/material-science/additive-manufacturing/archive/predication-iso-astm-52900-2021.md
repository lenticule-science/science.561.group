# ISO/ASTM 52900:2021

The international standard fixing the AM vocabulary, including the seven process families and the term "additive manufacturing" itself (formerly "rapid prototyping"). Replaces ASTM F2792-12a and ISO 17296-2.

**The seven process families.**

1. **Vat photopolymerization (VPP)** — selective curing of a liquid photopolymer (SLA, DLP, MSLA).
2. **Material extrusion (MEX)** — selective deposition through a heated nozzle (FFF/FDM, paste extrusion).
3. **Material jetting (MJT)** — selective deposition of droplets (PolyJet, NanoParticle Jetting).
4. **Binder jetting (BJT)** — selective adhesive deposition into a powder bed.
5. **Powder bed fusion (PBF)** — selective melting/sintering of powder by laser or electron beam (SLS, SLM/LPBF, EBM, MJF).
6. **Sheet lamination (SHL)** — selective bonding and cutting of sheet stock (LOM, UAM).
7. **Directed energy deposition (DED)** — focused thermal energy melts feedstock as it is deposited (LMD, WAAM, EBAM).

**Why it matters here.** This standard is the source of the [process-family taxonomy](../_index.md) used throughout the AM theory. Anything calling itself "the AM process" is one of these seven, and any new process should be classified against this list.

**Companion standards.** ISO/ASTM 52901 (purchase orders), 52902 (test artefacts), 52911 series (DED, PBF design), 52915 (AMF format), 52920/52921 (qualification of AM facilities and personnel).
