A toot is a post published on the Mastodon social network, analogous to a tweet. Toots can contain text (up to 500 characters by default, though instances may configure different limits), images, video, audio, polls, and content warnings. Toots federate across instances: when a user follows someone on a different [instance](./instance.md), that person's toots appear in their timeline.
