title: "Tool"
---
A tool is an [object](object) that extends the [capability|capabilities](capability|capabilities) of a [thing](thing).

It can be as simple as a [hammer](hammer) aiding in driving [nails](nails) or as complex as a [digital computer](digital computer) managing vast amounts of [information](information). Tools are [../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating|relational extensions](../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating|relational extensions) of our [intending|intentions](intending|intentions); they carry out [tasks](tasks) that amplify our efforts, facilitate new possibilities, and enhance our ability to shape and understand the [cosmos|world around us](cosmos|world around us).
