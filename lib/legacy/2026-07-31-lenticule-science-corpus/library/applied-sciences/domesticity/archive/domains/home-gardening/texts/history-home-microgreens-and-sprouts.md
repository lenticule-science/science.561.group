Home sprouting became popular as a low-cost, year-round source of fresh food.
Microgreens followed as a kitchen-scale adaptation of greenhouse baby greens.
Food-safety guidance tightened over time as sprout-related outbreaks highlighted
the need for careful rinsing and sanitation.
