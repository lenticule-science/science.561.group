The process of gradually exposing indoor-grown seedlings to outdoor
conditions over the course of a week before transplanting them
permanently. Seedlings are placed outside for increasing periods each
day, allowing them to adjust to direct sunlight, wind, and temperature
fluctuations. Skipping this step often results in transplant shock.
