How quickly water moves through soil. Drainage depends on soil texture
and structure. Well-drained soil allows water to pass through within a
few hours; poorly drained soil holds water for a day or more, which can
suffocate roots. Drainage is tested by filling a 30 cm deep hole with
water and timing how long it takes to empty.
