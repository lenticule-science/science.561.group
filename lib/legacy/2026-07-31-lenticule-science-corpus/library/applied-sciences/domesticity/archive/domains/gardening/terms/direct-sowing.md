---
name: direct sowing
aliases:
  - "direct sowing"
  - "direct sow"
date-created: 2026-03-06
tags: []
---

Planting seeds directly into the soil where the plant will grow to
maturity, without starting them indoors or in a separate container first.
Direct sowing is the simplest propagation method and works well when the
plant tolerates local conditions at planting time and the growing season
is long enough for it to reach maturity from an outdoor start.
