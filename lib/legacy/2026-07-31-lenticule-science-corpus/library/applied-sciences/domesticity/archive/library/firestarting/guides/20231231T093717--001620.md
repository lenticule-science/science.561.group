## Firestarting
### Materials
bucket (for transporting water)
ash pot (for holding water under fire)
pizza pan (holes under fire for letting embers fall through to water)
log (may stay lit if we accidentally let )
bricks 
cardboard

### Process 
1. Add three gallons of water to ash pot
2. Cover ash pot with pizza pan
3. Stack bricks on pizza pan so that they will prevent gusts of wind from blowing away the firy cardboard 
4. Lay log upright in corner created by bricks
5. Hold wad of shredded cardboard on pan, lighting away from fingers
  1. Manipulate cardboard until cardboard is well lit
