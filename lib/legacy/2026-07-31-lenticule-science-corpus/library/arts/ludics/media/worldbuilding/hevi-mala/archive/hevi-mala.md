# Hevi Mala

A paracosm distinct from the [emsean paracosm](../emsean-paracosm/) and
[groundhog autonomous zone](../groundhog-autonomous-zone/), centered on
a clinic and relay outpost called **Hevî Mala** (the building stencil
reads **MARKAZ 9**) in the Hala Basin — a fictionalized northeast-Syria
border region modeled on post-2011 conflict collapse.

The fiction is built as a story mesh rather than a single plot line:
every story either passes through the clinic, depends on it, or feels
its effects at a distance. Characters do not grow. The world does not
advance. It holds.

## Layout

- `texts/` — worldbuilding reference, abridged framing texts, stories.
- `beings/` — characters extracted as standalone entries (currently:
  Nadim, the fabricated presence). Core characters (Leyla, Bissan,
  Amina, Haval, Ciwan) and recurring secondary cast (Salim, Samir,
  Dr. Shouq, Julien, Fatemeh, Rana, Nassif) are described in
  [`texts/worlding-guide.md`](texts/worlding-guide.md) and can be
  promoted to standalone entries as concrete stories require.
- `places/` — to be created when individual locations get their own
  entries (Hasur, Ayna Bara, Sifir Station, Maqam al-Nahr, Zinar, the
  Flatline, Markaz 9, etc.).

See [the schema](../SCHEMA.md) for entry conventions.
