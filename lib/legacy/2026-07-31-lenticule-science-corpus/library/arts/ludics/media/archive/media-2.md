The study of narrative, fictional, and communicative forms -- how stories are built, worlds are constructed, and meaning is transmitted through various media. Content covers narrative theory (canon, continuity, diegesis), worldbuilding, literature, television, scripts, and zine-making.

**Teleology:** To understand how fictional worlds are constructed, maintained, and experienced -- and how narrative forms produce meaning across different media.

**Method:** Structural and semiotic analysis of narrative forms, combined with practical inquiry into how fictional worlds achieve internal consistency, how canons are negotiated, and how different media afford different kinds of storytelling.
