# Arts

The arts are practices of making — of shaping form, sound, movement, and narrative into objects of aesthetic and cultural significance. This root approaches the arts through theory, practice, and play rather than a medium-by-medium catalog.

This root holds three domains:

**Arts theory** covers aesthetics, criticism, and the philosophy of art: what art is, how it works, and how it is evaluated across traditions.

**Ludics** covers games and play as a mode of human activity — game design, play theory, the cultures of role-playing and tabletop games, and the relationship between rules, fiction, and interactivity. Digital games, MUDs, and multi-user fictional worlds appear here.

**Music** covers the theory, history, and practice of music: scales, harmony, composition, ethnomusicology, and the social life of musical cultures.
