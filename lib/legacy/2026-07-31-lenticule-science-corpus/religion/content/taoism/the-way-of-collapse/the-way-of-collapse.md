## Preface

As an [[anarcho-nihilist]], I work to make my own [[meaning]] for the things in my world. One [[tool]] I've found useful for this is [[Taoism]], and one domain I've applied it to is the notion of [[Collapse]]: the dissolution of our familiar [[Earth]] and the [[settler-colonial civilization|civilization]] that [[plundering|plundered]] it.

This text was created after acquiring, and beginning to read, a copy of [[John Milton]]'s *[[The Essential Tao Te Ching]]*. It was created with no planned form, only an intention to share it with people curious about some potential [[relationship|relationships]] between Taoism and Collapse.

There's nothing [[truth|true]] in this text; it is not meant to [[teaching|tech]], but connect ideas in my head to ideas in your head.

## Stepping Toward Mystery

The Way and the Power is 5000 words of laconic poetry written by the Old Master while he was in Qin during the Zhou Dynasty. *(Or it wasn't.)* As a text, it has served and serves many purposes for many people. Coming from a childhood in Western culture, I most easily see it as an exploration of a cosmology, but most commonly use it as a guide to teaching myself from my daily experiences.

The Way is the center of the cosmology the text explores. The Way is beyond what can be Known or Described: it is the Source of the Dimensionality that makes things Knowable and Describable. *(Yes: The Way and the Power is 5000 words about something that can't be put in words.)*

The Power is the manifestation of The Way, creating the World. It's the energy that makes up things, animating the universe, oriented by The Way.

We cannot know Collapse, in any meaningful way. The rate of change already being experienced by our ecology is beyond anything the practiced ways of knowing can know. What we can know is things like slowing of the Atlantic Meridian Oceanic Current.
