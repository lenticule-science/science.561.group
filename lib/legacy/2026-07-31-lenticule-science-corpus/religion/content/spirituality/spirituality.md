Traditions and practices oriented toward transcendence, meaning, and the relationship between human experience and what exceeds it. Current content covers Christianity and its theological lineages.

**Teleology:** To understand spiritual traditions on their own terms -- their claims about reality, their practices for engaging the sacred, and the forms of knowledge they produce.

**Method:** Close reading of theological and liturgical texts, analysis of ritual practice, and examination of how spiritual traditions construct and transmit their understanding of the sacred across communities and generations.
