Preprint at arXiv:2202.03659.

## Annotation

Resolves a technical issue in cosheaf theory: cosheafification (the cosheaf analogue of sheafification) doesn't exist in general, so cosheaf homology can't be defined as the formal dual of sheaf cohomology in the usual way.

The authors show: given a cellular cosheaf $F$, there is a natural way to associate a cosheaf $\widehat{F}$ for which homology can be defined as the formal dual of sheaf cohomology. The Borel-Moore homology of $F$ is isomorphic to the homology of $\widehat{F}$ when the underlying CW complex is a simplicial complex.

## Position in predication categories

Companion reference for the [Verdier duality chapter](../narratives/verdier-duality-opener.md). Specifically: handles the cosheafification subtlety in the cellular setting, ensuring that the cellular cosheaf homology used in the Curry derived equivalence is well-behaved and computable. Used as a black box for the cosheaf-side technical machinery in the Q-enriched generalization.
