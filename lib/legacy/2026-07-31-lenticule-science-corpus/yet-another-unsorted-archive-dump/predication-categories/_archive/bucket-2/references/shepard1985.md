## Annotation

Develops a cellular-sheaf model of the derived category of a stratified space, with Verdier duality realized at the cellular level. For the program, the methodological precursor to the [Curry cellular-sheaf lineage](../narratives/curry-cellular-sheaf-lineage.md): the cellular-cosheaf side of Curry's framework — and the Verdier-duality bridge that Curry recovers — comes from this work. A paired-Priestley analogue of cellular Verdier duality is open as [Verdier duality on the bitopological site](../questions/verdier-duality-on-bitopological-site.md).
