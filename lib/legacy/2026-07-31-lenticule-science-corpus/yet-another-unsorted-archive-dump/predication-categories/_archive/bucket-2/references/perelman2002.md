## Full citation

G. Perelman. *The Entropy Formula for the Ricci Flow and Its Geometric Applications*. Preprint, 2002. arXiv:math/0211159.

## Status

Standard reference; not held as PDF. Used as a structural template for the Ricci-flow-with-surgery framework. The full surgery program is developed in subsequent papers (math/0303109, March 2003); the 2002 paper introduces the foundational entropy formula and the analytic framework that makes surgery analysis possible.

## Annotation

Perelman's first preprint on the Ricci flow program. Introduces the entropy functional and the reduced volume that control the flow's behavior near singularities, plus the analytic framework that supports surgical continuation past singular times. Subsequent papers develop the surgery procedure explicitly (math/0303109) and complete the program to a proof of the geometrization conjecture (math/0307245).

For the corpus, Perelman's framework is the technical antecedent for the [surgical-continuation question](../questions/surgical-continuation-past-firings.md): the closure operator past a non-Scott-continuous firing as a Hamilton-Perelman-style surgical modification — cut at the directed-limit, replace with the firing's standard piece, continue.
