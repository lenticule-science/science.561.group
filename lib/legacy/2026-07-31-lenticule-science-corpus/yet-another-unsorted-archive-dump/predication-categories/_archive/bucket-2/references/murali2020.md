## Annotation

Extends Verdier duality to overconvergent sheaves on separated, paracompact rigid analytic spaces, analogous to Lurie's treatment of Verdier duality for locally compact, Hausdorff topological spaces. Establishes a sheaf-cosheaf Verdier duality equivalence theorem in this setting.

## Position in predication categories

Reference for the [Verdier duality chapter](../narratives/verdier-duality-opener.md) — illustrates that Verdier duality between sheaves and cosheaves extends beyond the classical CW-complex / locally-compact setting to richer geometric contexts, supporting predication categories' extension to bitopological-Grothendieck-site setting. The bitopological settling site is structurally different from rigid analytic spaces but shares the feature of being a non-classical site where standard Verdier needs adaptation; Murali's adaptation pattern (overconvergent sheaves, paracompactness conditions) is a methodological precedent.
