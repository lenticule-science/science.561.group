# SKH-FOL ↔ BD∆ correspondence

The internal classical first-order logic of the [predication hyperdoctrine](../objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) under the $\leq_t$-adjoint system, with [classical equality](../objects/classical-equality.md) $=_E$ and [comprehension](../theorems/lawvere-comprehension-classical.md) $\{-\}_\mathbf{t}$, is conjecturally equivalent to BD∆ — Omori–Wansing's first-order paraconsistent logic with a designated-truth modality $\Delta$.

A correspondence theorem would specify the translation in both directions: SKH formulas to BD∆ formulas (and conversely), and prove that derivability is preserved. The argument is non-trivial — quantifier semantics and the role of $\Delta$ in BD∆ both demand careful matching against the [two-internal-logics](../theorems/two-internal-logics.md) structure here. A standalone appendix or a separate short note is the likely shape.

Related: [Belnap-designated comprehension](belnap-designated-comprehension.md) — both questions concern the predication hyperdoctrine's internal logic under designated-value semantics. A working BD∆ translation would likely interact with the third (Belnap-designated) comprehension schema; the two questions are partially coupled.
