# Gray–Street fibration 2-monad

The 2-functor $\mathbb{M} : \mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C}$ on the slice 2-category over a [predicate category](../objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ — sending $F : A \to \mathcal{C}$ to the comma construction $\mathcal{C}/F \to \mathcal{C}$ — extends to a colax-idempotent 2-monad $(\mathbb{M}, \mu, \eta)$. Pseudo-algebras for $\mathbb{M}$ are cloven Grothendieck fibrations over $\mathcal{C}$.
