## Annotation

Surveys completeness and complexity results for the modal logics of knowledge and belief — single-agent (S5, K45) and multi-agent variants — with attention to common knowledge and the complexity blowup it induces. For the program, the canonical complexity reference for modal-knowledge logic alongside [@blackburn-et-al2001]; the program's [modal complexity bounds](../theorems/modal-complexity-bounds.md) inherit from this lineage via the [twist-modal translation](../objects/twist-modal-translation.md).
