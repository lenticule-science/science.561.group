## Annotation

Develops cellular sheaves on the face posets of regular cell complexes, with cellular cosheaves as the dual. Used as the methodological backbone for the cohomological-layer's site theory.

Sections used:
- **§§5, 6** — cellular sheaves on face posets, cellular sheaf cohomology.
- **Chapter 5** — enough projectives and injectives in $\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathrm{cell}}(X, R\text{-Mod})$, a non-trivial convenience.
- **§11** — Verdier duality for cellular sheaves: a derived equivalence between cellular sheaves and cellular cosheaves, recovering Shepard 1985 as a special case.

The cohomological layer does not directly invoke Verdier duality but notes its availability — see [Verdier duality on the bitopological site](../questions/verdier-duality-on-bitopological-site.md).

For the cohomological layer: Curry's correspondence between Alexandrov-topology sheaves on a poset and functors out of the poset is the conceptual move that lets us assemble fiberwise paired-Priestley duals into a single sheaf on a 2-categorical site.
