## Annotation

The textbook treatment of the topology of aperiodic-tiling spaces: inverse-limit constructions over Anderson–Putnam complexes, mutual local derivability (MLD) as the equivalence relation classifying tilings up to local recoding, and the Čech cohomology of tiling spaces as a complete topological invariant in many cases. For the program, this is the methodological template the [aperiodic-regime classification](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-classification.md) imports: settlings stand to MLD as tilings do, and the [aperiodic classifying invariant](../objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) plays the role of the Čech-cohomology invariant in tiling theory.
