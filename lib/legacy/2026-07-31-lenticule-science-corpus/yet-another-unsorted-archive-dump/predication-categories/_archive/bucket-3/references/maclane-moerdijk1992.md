## Annotation

The standard introductory text on topos theory and sheaves on sites: Grothendieck topologies, sheafification, sites, geometric morphisms, classifying topoi, and the topos-theoretic perspective on logic. For the program, supplies the site-theoretic infrastructure underlying the [bitopological settling site](../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md) — Grothendieck topology axioms, sheaf condition formulations, and standard sheafification machinery.
