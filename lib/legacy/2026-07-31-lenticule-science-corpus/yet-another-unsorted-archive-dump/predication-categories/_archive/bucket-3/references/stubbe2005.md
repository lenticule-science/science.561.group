## Full citation

I. Stubbe. "Categorical structures enriched in a quantaloid: categories, distributors and functors." *Theory and Applications of Categories* 14(1), 1–45, 2005.

## Status

Standard reference for quantaloid-enriched category theory.

## Annotation

The systematic treatment of category theory enriched in a quantaloid. Specializing to a single-object quantaloid (i.e., a quantale), this is the right reference for $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched category theory in its full generality — including weighted limits, weighted colimits, distributors, and the structure theory of complete weighted lattices.

For the corpus's [Lawvere Laplacian extension theorem](../theorems/lawvere-laplacian-grothendieck-sites.md), Stubbe's §5 (or thereabouts — exact section depends on the version) supplies the analogue of Kelly's "$\mathsf{Q}$-functors preserve weighted limits" result in the quantale-enriched setting specifically. This is the technical fact used to show the Lawvere Laplacian preserves the sheaf-condition equalizer.

Stubbe's quantaloid-enriched setting is also the natural home for an eventual *quantaloid*-enriched generalization of the Lawvere Laplacian — a direction explicitly identified as future work in [@ghrist-et-al2025] §7.

## Position in the program

Foundational reference for $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched category theory in the cellular extension work. Companion to [@kelly1982] for the more specialized quantale-enriched setting; needed where the quantale's specific structure (commutative, unital) and the value category's specific structure (complete weighted lattice) interact in ways the general enriched category theory abstracts over.
