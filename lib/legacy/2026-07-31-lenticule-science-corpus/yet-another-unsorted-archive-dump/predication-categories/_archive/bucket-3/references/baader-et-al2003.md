## Annotation

The canonical reference on description logics — the model-theoretic family of decidable fragments of first-order logic underlying OWL and other knowledge-representation frameworks. For the program, the relevant content is the *ABox* (assertions about individuals): a description-logic ABox is a paradigmatic instance of the kind of partial, accumulating, possibly conflicting assertion store that the spatiotemporal knowledge presheaf models.
