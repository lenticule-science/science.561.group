## Annotation

Develops a sequent-calculus presentation of first-order ideal paraconsistent four-valued logic with completeness and cut-elimination. For the program, supplies the proof-theoretic side of the four-valued treatment of equality and identity — equality as a derived predicate rather than a postulated primitive — that [equality derived not postulated](../narratives/equality-derived-not-postulated.md) draws on.
