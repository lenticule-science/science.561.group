## Full citation

## Status

Foundational lecture notes for quasi-category theory. Cited at training-knowledge level for the framework's existence; specific results not directly used in current corpus theorems.

## Annotation

Joyal's foundational treatment of quasi-categories (also called $(\infty, 1)$-categories or simplicial-set $\infty$-categories) and their interaction with logic. Predates and informs [@lurie2009]; develops the model-theoretic and logical side of higher categorical foundations more directly than Lurie's books.

**Relevance to the corpus.** The future-direction narrative on $(\infty, 1)$-extensions cites this as an early framework for higher-categorical interpretations of predicate logic. The corpus's [predication hyperdoctrine](../objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) — currently 2-categorical with quantifier adjoints satisfying Beck-Chevalley — would naturally lift to an $\infty$-doctrine in Joyal's sense, accommodating the higher-coherence machinery the corpus's logical layer would inherit from the $(\infty, 1)$-direction.

This is a future-direction reference; no current corpus theorem depends on it.
