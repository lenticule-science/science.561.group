## Annotation

A standard graduate text on homological algebra: derived functors, spectral sequences, Tor and Ext, group and Hochschild cohomology, mapping cones and triangulated categories. For the program, supplies the homological-algebra background for [bigraded cohomology via mapping cone](../theorems/bigraded-cohomology-via-mapping-cone.md) — mapping-cone presentation of long exact sequences and the relationship to comparison morphisms between sheaves.
