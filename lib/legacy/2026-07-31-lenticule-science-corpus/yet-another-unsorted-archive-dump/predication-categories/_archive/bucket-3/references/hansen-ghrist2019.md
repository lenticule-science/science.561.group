## Annotation

Develops a Hodge Laplacian on cellular sheaves, building a spectral theory that parallels classical graph Laplacian theory but for sheaf-valued data on cell complexes.

Cited in the cohomological layer (Computational Content) as a candidate framework if a spectral / numerical-analytic perspective on the settling-associated sheaf cohomology is pursued. Currently a secondary reference; the main cohomology computations in the cohomological layer use derived-functor and Čech methods rather than spectral methods.
