## Annotation

The standard reference on classical modal logic: Kripke semantics, completeness via canonical models, the finite model property, complexity classifications (PSPACE-complete local consequence, EXPTIME-complete global), filtration, bisimulation. For the program, the classical apparatus is what the four-valued bilattice modal calculus generalizes; the FMP and the complexity bounds are inherited via the [twist-modal translation](../objects/twist-modal-translation.md) from the underlying classical modal logic.
