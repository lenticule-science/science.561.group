## Full citation

R. F. Williams. "Expanding attractors." *Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS* 43, 169–203, 1974.

## Status

Foundational reference for inverse-limit modeling of dynamical attractors. Cited at training-knowledge level for the framework's existence; specific results not directly used.

## Annotation

Models hyperbolic attractors as inverse limits $\varprojlim(\Gamma, f)$ of branched-manifold approximants $\Gamma$ under expanding self-maps $f$. The construction is the historical predecessor of [@anderson-putnam1998]'s tiling-space inverse-limit and the broader Williams / Bellissard / Sadun framework.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified in the literature stress-test of the corpus's [step-n fixed-point object](../objects/step-n-fixed-point.md): Williams's inverse-limit construction takes a *fixed* substitution rule and forms a limit over iteration. The corpus's $\mathscr{M}^{(\infty)}$ goes in the opposite direction — direct limit over the alphabet-size index $n$ at the moduli level. The two limits are structurally orthogonal; whether either commutes with the other is open.
