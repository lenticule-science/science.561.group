## Annotation

The result that licenses the hyperdoctrinal-layer's KZ-lax-idempotent diamond as a concrete construction.

**Theorem 3.4.** Cloven Grothendieck fibrations over a fixed base $B$ are exactly the **pseudo-coalgebras** for a lax-idempotent 2-comonad $\mathbb{N}$ on $\mathbf{Cat}/B$. Moreover, the well-known colax-idempotent 2-monad $\mathbb{M}$ on $\mathbf{Cat}/B$ (whose pseudo-algebras are also cloven fibrations; Gray 1966 / Street 1974) has $\mathbb{N}$ as its **right 2-adjoint**.

The 2-adjunction $\mathbb{M} \dashv \mathbb{N}$ has canonical unit, counit, multiplication, and comultiplication 2-cells. Together with the (co)lax-idempotent coherence, these form a **diamond of 2-cells** at each iteration level — this is the structural object the hyperdoctrinal layer uses to upgrade the temporal axis from a linear ordinal to a 2-category $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}, \lambda)$.

A side benefit (called out in the paper): a cofree construction of a fibration on a functor.

Primary source for the hyperdoctrinal layer (Theorems 4.2.1, 4.2.2 cite directly).
