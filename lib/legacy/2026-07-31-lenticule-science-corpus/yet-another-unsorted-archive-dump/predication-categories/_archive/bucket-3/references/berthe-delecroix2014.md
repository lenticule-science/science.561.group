## Full citation

V. Berthé, V. Delecroix. "Beyond substitutive dynamical systems: $S$-adic expansions." *RIMS Kôkyûroku Bessatsu* B46, 81–123, 2014. arXiv:1309.3960.

## Status

Foundational survey of S-adic systems — symbolic dynamical systems generated by infinite compositions of substitutions, with the substitution allowed to vary at each step. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

An **S-adic shift** is generated by a sequence of substitutions $(\sigma_k)_{k \geq 0}$ where $\sigma_k : A_{k+1}^* \to A_k^*$. The system reads infinite words by composing $\sigma_0 \circ \sigma_1 \circ \cdots$ from the outside in. This generalizes single-substitution systems by allowing substitution-rules to vary across iteration.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified by the literature stress-test as the closest "varying-alphabet substitution" framework to the corpus's [step-$n$ family](../concepts/step-n-substitution-recurrence.md), and the natural setting in which the corpus's [step-$n$ fixed-point object](../objects/step-n-fixed-point.md) might be embedded. Critical structural difference: S-adic compositions go in the *inverse* direction (an S-adic system reads $\sigma_0 \circ \sigma_1 \circ \cdots$ as a projective limit), while the corpus's step-shift goes in the *direct* direction (embedding $\mathscr{M}^{(n)} \hookrightarrow \mathscr{M}^{(n+1)}$ at the moduli level).
