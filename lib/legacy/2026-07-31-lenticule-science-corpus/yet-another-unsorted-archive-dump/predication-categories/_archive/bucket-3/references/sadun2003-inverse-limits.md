## Full citation

L. Sadun. "Tiling spaces are inverse limits." *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 44, 5410–5414, 2003. arXiv:math/0210179.

## Status

Standard reference for inverse-limit modeling of FLC tiling spaces. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Generalizes [@anderson-putnam1998]'s substitution-tiling inverse limit to arbitrary FLC (finite-local-complexity) tilings: every FLC tiling space is an inverse limit of branched manifolds under self-maps. The branched manifolds are generalizations of the Anderson-Putnam complex $\Gamma_\sigma$.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified in the literature stress-test of the [step-n fixed-point object](../objects/step-n-fixed-point.md). Cited adjacent to [@williams1974] and [@anderson-putnam1998] when discussing inverse-limit-versus-direct-limit constructions in the substitution-tiling literature: the corpus's $\mathscr{M}^{(\infty)}$ direct limit is structurally distinct from the inverse-limit constructions standard in this literature.
