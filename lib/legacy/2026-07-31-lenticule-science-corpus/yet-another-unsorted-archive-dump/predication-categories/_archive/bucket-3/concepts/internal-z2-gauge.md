# Internal Z/2 gauge

The **internal gauge** of [$\mathbf{FOUR}$](../objects/four-bilattice.md) is the $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ by [negation](negation.md) $\sim$: the non-trivial element of $\mathbb{Z}/2$ acts as $\sim$, the identity element acts as $\mathrm{id}$.

The gauge action:

- preserves the [knowledge order](knowledge-order.md) $\leq_k$;
- reverses the [truth order](truth-order.md) $\leq_t$;
- fixes the two "symmetric" values $\bot, \top$ while swapping $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$.

The [paired Priestley dual](../objects/paired-priestley-space.md) of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ inherits the corresponding $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action: the swap involution exchanging the two components $\{+, -\}$ of the paired space realizes the $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action dually under [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../theorems/jung-rivieccio-duality.md).
