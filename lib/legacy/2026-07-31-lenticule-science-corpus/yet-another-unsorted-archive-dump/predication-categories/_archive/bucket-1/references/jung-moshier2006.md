## Annotation

The d-frame foundations paper. Introduces a Stone duality for **bitopological** spaces, axiomatising the two open-set lattices via a *d-frame*. Develops:

- **d-sobriety** (a more inclusive concept than usual sobriety in this bitopological setting).
- **Reasonable d-frames** — additional axioms suggested by spatial d-frames.
- Alternative presentation of d-frames as **partial frames**.
- Notions of regularity and compactness for bitopological spaces.

For our program: the technical foundation underlying the cohomological-layer's bitopological site construction. Together with [@bezhanishvili-et-al2010] (pairwise Stone spaces) and [@jakl2018] (d-frame thesis development), this is the bitopological backbone of the cohomology paper.

The cohomological layer cite for the d-frame foundations supporting the bitopological pairwise-Stone site $(\mathbf{T}^*, J)$.
