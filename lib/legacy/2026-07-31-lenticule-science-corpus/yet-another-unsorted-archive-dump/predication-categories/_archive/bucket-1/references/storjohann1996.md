## Annotation

A near-optimal algorithm for computing Smith normal forms of integer matrices. For the program, supplies the polynomial-time complexity ingredient used in [cohomology class equality in polynomial time](../theorems/cohomology-class-equality-polynomial.md): comparing $H^1$-classes in the strict regime reduces to a $\mathbb{Z}$-linear-system solvability question, and Smith normal form gives a polynomial-time decision procedure.
