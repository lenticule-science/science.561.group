## Annotation

Comprehensive thesis treatment of d-frames building on [@jung-moshier2006]. Develops d-frame theory in detail, including:

- The **Priestley embedding** in Chapter 2.6.
- Comparison of d-frames with related bitopological/lattice frameworks.
- Spatial-vs-algebraic correspondences for bitopological spaces.

Used as the comprehensive d-frame reference when the cohomological-layer's site-theoretic moves need fuller technical backing than the original Jung–Moshier 2006 tech report provides. Especially useful for the cohomological layer (open problem on bilattice-valued sheaves; Jakl's d-frame chapter on bilattice connections is the bridge candidate).

Closely related: [@jakl2025] which extends to four imprints of Belnap logic in CS, and connects d-frames to bilattices explicitly.
