Richard Lanham reframed rhetoric for an information economy where attention, not content, is scarce. His prose-revision method (the paramedic method) is widely taught.

## Core ideas

- **Economics of attention**: in an information glut, style is the technology that wins or loses readers
- **Paramedic method**: concrete, sentence-level revision steps to shorten and clarify
- **Looking AT vs THROUGH style**: rhetoric oscillates between transparent and opaque language

## Key works

- *Revising Prose* (1979)
- *The Electronic Word* (1993)
- *The Economics of Attention* (2006)
