---
name: Richard Mayer
description: "American educational psychologist, originator of the cognitive theory of multimedia learning."
date-created: 2026-04-28
---

Richard Mayer's research at UC Santa Barbara established a set of evidence-based principles for combining text, image, and narration so that learners build coherent mental models without overload.

## Core ideas

- **Multimedia principle**: people learn better from words and pictures than from words alone
- **Coherence principle**: extraneous material hurts learning
- **Modality and redundancy**: narration with picture beats on-screen text with picture; both is worse than one

## Key works

- *Multimedia Learning* (2001)
- *Applying the Science of Learning* (2010)
