name: Louis Hjelmslev
description: "Danish linguist (1899–1965), founder of glossematics and a key relay between Saussure and structuralist semiotics."
date-created: 2026-04-28
---

Hjelmslev formalized Saussurean structural linguistics into a more rigorous theory of expression and content planes, each with form and substance. Eco, Greimas, and Deleuze all build on his apparatus.

## Core ideas

- **Expression and content planes**: the sign as a relation between two stratified planes
- **Form and substance**: every plane has formal structure and material substance
- **Connotative semiotics**: a semiotic system whose expression plane is itself another semiotic

## Key works

- *Prolegomena to a Theory of Language* (1943)
