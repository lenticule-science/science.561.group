Donald J. Trump (born 1946) is an American real-estate developer and politician, the 45th (2017-2021) and 47th (2025-) President of the United States. The *who-benefits* study uses Trump as the orbit-anchor for mapping documented connections across three threads of post-Soviet organized crime, oligarch networks, and sexual-content / blackmail infrastructure.

The study does not advance new claims; it maps documented connections drawn from court filings, FBI investigations, the Mueller Report, *American Kompromat* (Craig Unger), *Putin's People* (Catherine Belton), and contemporaneous press reporting. Trump's role across the three threads is documented:

- The Mogilevich → Sater → Bayrock → Trump thread: Felix Sater, an FBI informant with documented ties to Semion Mogilevich's organization, served as a Bayrock Group executive responsible for several Trump-branded real-estate projects in the 2000s. The 2018 *Cohen* criminal information records false statements about a 2016 Trump Tower Moscow project that Sater was developing.
