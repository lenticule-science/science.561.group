Karen Schriver bridges cognitive psychology, rhetoric, and visual design. Her research treats text and graphics as a single integrated message and grounds design choices in evidence about reader comprehension.

## Core ideas

- **Integrated text-and-graphic design**: words and images must be planned together, not laid out after
- **Reader-protocol research**: observing readers think aloud reveals where documents fail
- **Genre-aware design**: form follows reader expectations of the document type

## Key works

- *Dynamics in Document Design* (1997)
