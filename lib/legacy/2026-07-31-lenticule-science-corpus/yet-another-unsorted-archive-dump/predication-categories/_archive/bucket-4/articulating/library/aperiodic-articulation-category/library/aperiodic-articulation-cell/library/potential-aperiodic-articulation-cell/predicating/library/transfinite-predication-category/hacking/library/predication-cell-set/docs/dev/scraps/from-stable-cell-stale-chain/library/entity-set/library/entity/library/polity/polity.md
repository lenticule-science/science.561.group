A **chartered institutional collective**: an [agent-community](agent-community.md) that has additionally constituted itself as a polity by adopting a charter, declaring offices, and maintaining a personnel roster. The 561 Group is a polity. RSC Quiescent is a ship — a particular kind of polity (or a polity-projection at one specific scope).

A polity is more than its agent-community: it has a constitution. Charter-amendment, office-constitution, personnel-recognition, peer-confederacy-membership are polity-level acts that an unchartered community cannot perform.

## Distinguishing predication-shape

- `charter(polity, charter-record)` — the constitutional document.
- `personnel-roster(polity, {personnel, ...})` — the entities recognized as members.
- `offices(polity, {office, ...})` — the constituted office-positions.
- `recognition-cell(polity, fuzzy-cell)` — e.g., the 561's `Pop_561` recognition cell with its fuzzy-membership values.
- `peer-confederacy(polity, {polity, ...})` — federation relations.

## Step-induction role

Institutional acts: amending charter, formally recognizing new personnel, constituting new offices, issuing peer-credentials for federation. These are polity-level acts; without polity as a distinct form, these are not distinguishable from arbitrary community-level acts.

Cross-polity federation: when the 561 polity recognizes another polity's personnel, that's polity-to-polity, not community-to-community. The personnel-recognition cell carries the recognizing polity's authority.

## Not synonymous with

- **Agent-community** — community is structural; polity is constituted (chartered).
- **Ship** — ship may be a kind of polity (RSC Quiescent), or a polity-projection at one operational scope.
