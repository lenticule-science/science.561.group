The phase of liberal governance where the system acknowledges its colonial, racial, and environmental violence — and manages that acknowledgment through reconciliation commissions, sustainability frameworks, and diversity programs rather than structural transformation. The quasi-event is its characteristic political form. Crisis ordinariness is the lived experience it produces.
