---
title: "Deconstruction"
description: "Derrida's practice of reading that exposes the unstable binary oppositions structuring a text — showing how each text undermines its own claims to stable meaning."
---


Derrida's practice of close reading that shows how texts depend on oppositions (speech/writing, presence/absence, nature/culture) they cannot sustain. Auto-immunity, as Derrida develops it in his later political work, applies deconstructive logic to sovereignty and democracy.
