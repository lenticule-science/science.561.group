---
title: "Poststructuralism"
description: "The tradition that challenges structuralism's stable binaries and fixed codes — showing how meaning is produced through difference, deferral, and power rather than through stable structures."
---

The movement that destabilized structuralism's confidence in stable codes and binary oppositions. Where structuralism found fixed systems of difference, poststructuralism found those systems undermining themselves from within. Derrida's deconstruction, Foucault's genealogy, and Baudrillard's simulation each extend this insight in different directions.
