# Bataille

Georges Bataille (1897–1962). Theorist of excess, expenditure, and transgression. His general economy inverts political economy: the fundamental problem is not scarcity but surplus — what to do with the energy that cannot be reinvested in growth and must be spent through sacrifice, war, luxury, or festival.
