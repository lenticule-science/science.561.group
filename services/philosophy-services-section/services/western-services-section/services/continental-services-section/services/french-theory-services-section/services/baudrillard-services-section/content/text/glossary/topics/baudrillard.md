# Baudrillard

Jean Baudrillard (1929–2007). Theorist of simulation, hyperreality, and the sign-system's progressive detachment from any referent. His central claim: contemporary culture operates in a regime of simulacra — signs without originals — where the distinction between representation and reality has collapsed.
