Intra-action, as theorized by [Karen Barad](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/karen-barad.md), names the process by which agencies emerge through encounter rather than preceding it. Where "interaction" presupposes that two distinct entities meet, intra-action holds that the entities themselves are constituted through the encounter — they do not exist as determinate agencies prior to the intra-action that produces them.

[Barad](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/karen-barad.md) develops the concept from Niels Bohr's philosophy-physics: in quantum mechanics, the properties of a particle are not intrinsic to the particle but are constituted through the specific measurement apparatus used to observe them. A photon does not possess the [property](../../sociology/terms/property.md) of "wave" or "particle" independently — it manifests one or the other through the specific experimental arrangement in which it participates. The apparatus and the photon are not separate entities that interact; they are co-constituted through the phenomenon of measurement.

This is not a claim limited to quantum mechanics. [Barad](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/karen-barad.md) extends it into [ontology](../../../../../ontology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md): all agencies, all boundaries, all properties are produced through intra-actions. What counts as an "object" and what counts as a "subject" are not given in advance but cut from the relational field by specific material-discursive practices. The "agential cut" — the production of determinate boundaries from an entangled field — is the basic [ontological](../../../../../ontology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md) act.

Intra-action is a form of [relational ontology](../../../../../ontology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md) arrived at through physics: relations are ontologically prior to relata, and what we call entities are patterns stabilized through ongoing intra-actions. This parallels [process philosophy](../disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md)'s claim that [actual occasions](./actual-occasion.md) constitute themselves through [prehension](./prehension.md), and the [semiotic universe](../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md)'s derivation of determinate structure from relational process.
