Audience: readers who have completed the [societies and endurance](./societies-and-endurance.md) lesson.

Learning goal: connect [process philosophy](../index.md) to [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md) and to the broader concerns of this research program.

The preceding lessons have built up [Whitehead](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s system from its elements: [actual occasions](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/actual-occasion.md) as the basic units, [prehension](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) as the basic relational act, [concrescence](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/concrescence.md) as the process of self-constitution, [creativity](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/creativity.md) as the principle of novelty, and [societies](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/society.md) as the explanation of endurance. The common thread is that relations are constitutive: an occasion is what its prehensions make it, a society is what its pattern of inheritance makes it, and apart from these relations, neither is anything.

This is [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md): the position that relations are ontologically prior to entities. [Process philosophy](../index.md) is the most developed Western [metaphysical](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/metaphysics.md) system in which this claim is worked out systematically. But it is not the only tradition that arrives at this position. Many Indigenous philosophical traditions — including the Lakota epistemologies that ground this research program — understand beings as constituted through relations, responsibilities, and processes of reciprocal becoming.

The convergence is significant but must be stated carefully. [Process philosophy](../index.md) and Indigenous relational ontologies arise from different traditions, address different concerns, and occupy different political positions. Whitehead wrote as a Cambridge mathematician constructing an alternative [metaphysics](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/metaphysics.md); Indigenous thinkers speak from lived traditions of relation to land, kin, and more-than-human beings. The structural parallel — both hold that relations constitute beings, that experience pervades reality, and that substance thinking distorts what it describes — supports the claim that [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md) is not a philosophical novelty but a widely attested understanding of reality that Western [substance metaphysics](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/substance-metaphysics.md) has obscured.

[Process philosophy](../index.md) also provides conceptual vocabulary that connects to the mathematical formalisms of this research program. [Prehension](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) — the constitution of entities through relational acts — is what the [semiotic universe](../../../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md) formalizes algebraically. [Concrescence](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/concrescence.md) — the achievement of determinate structure from open potentiality — is what closure operators model. [Societies](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/society.md) — patterns sustained by ongoing inheritance — are what the dynamical framework describes. The philosophical concepts and the mathematical structures are not identical, but they illuminate one another.

The [fallacy of misplaced concreteness](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/fallacy-of-misplaced-concreteness.md) names the diagnostic that ties these threads together. Wherever abstractions are mistaken for concrete realities — the individual for the relation, the substance for the process, the commodity for the ecosystem — [process philosophy](../index.md) identifies an error that [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md) corrects and that the mathematical formalism makes precise.

Check for understanding: Why is the convergence between process philosophy and Indigenous relational ontologies significant for this research program?
