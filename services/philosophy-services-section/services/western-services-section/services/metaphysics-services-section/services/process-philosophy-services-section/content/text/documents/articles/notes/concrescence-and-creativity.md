Audience: readers who have completed the [actual occasions and prehension](./actual-occasions-and-prehension.md) lesson.

Learning goal: explain how [concrescence](../../../glossary/terms/concrescence.md) produces determinate occasions and how [creativity](../../../glossary/terms/creativity.md) names the universe's ongoing novelty.

An [actual occasion](../../../glossary/terms/actual-occasion.md) begins as an open potentiality — defined by its initial data (the [prehended](../../../glossary/terms/prehension.md) past) and its [subjective aim](../../../glossary/terms/subjective-aim.md) (a lure toward a particular form of integration) — and concludes by achieving a definite, novel unity: its [satisfaction](../../../glossary/terms/satisfaction.md). The internal process of moving from potentiality to definiteness is [concrescence](../../../glossary/terms/concrescence.md) (from the Latin *concrescere*, to grow together).

Concrescence proceeds in phases. In the initial phase, physical [prehensions](../../../glossary/terms/prehension.md) supply the raw data from prior occasions. In supplementary phases, conceptual [prehensions](../../../glossary/terms/prehension.md) introduce [eternal objects](../../../glossary/terms/eternal-object.md) (possibilities) that shape how the data is valued, contrasted, and organized. The [subjective aim](../../../glossary/terms/subjective-aim.md) guides the integration, but the occasion is not rigidly determined by its aim — it may depart from the initial lure and achieve a form of definiteness that was not originally aimed at. This capacity for departure is the basis of freedom in [Whitehead](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s system.

At [satisfaction](../../../glossary/terms/satisfaction.md), the occasion is fully determinate. It perishes as a subject — ceasing to be an experiencing process — and becomes an object: a datum available for [prehension](../../../glossary/terms/prehension.md) by future occasions. This transition from subjective immediacy to objective availability is what generates the forward movement of time.

[Creativity](../../../glossary/terms/creativity.md) names the general principle at work in every concrescence: the many become one and are increased by one. Each occasion takes in a multiplicity of data and synthesizes a novel unity that did not exist before, adding itself to the multiplicity for future occasions. Creativity is not an agent or a force; it is the abstract principle that novelty is real — that each moment of experience is a genuine creation, not merely a rearrangement of what already exists.

Together, concrescence and creativity describe a universe that is an ongoing adventure of self-creation. There is no external agent making the world; each actual occasion makes itself through the creative integration of what it inherits. This is how [process philosophy](../index.md) avoids both determinism and arbitrary chance: every occasion is conditioned by its past but not determined by it, because the creative act of concrescence always introduces something new.

Check for understanding: What is the difference between concrescence and creativity?
