## World-sim math enrichment (from predication-world-sim + old-draft mining, 2026-04-06)

The old-draft relational math (52K lines) IS foundational ontology research. Not "relevant to" — IS. Specific connections:

**Action and change:** The composition gap (I.6) gives action a precise meaning — an action IS a composition whose boundary carries bypass, producing new reachable states that neither entity contained alone. Change IS settlement — the transition from firable to settled. The asymmetry requirement (F3.4) is WHY change has a direction: if bypass were symmetric, nothing would ever settle.

**Quality and property:** The four shadow classes (H*_t, SatShadow, TrShadow, FreeShadow) ARE quality grades. A property in H*_t is fully determined. A property in SatShadow is memory without destiny. A property in TrShadow is destiny without memory. A property in FreeShadow is pure potential. Quality IS shadow class membership.

**State, time, and event:** The tower/fold IS temporal structure. Each tower level is an epoch. The fold (I.10) is the transition between epochs — the settled face becoming the firable face of the next level. Events are composition-gap firings (I.6). Time is the depth filtration. The partition function gives time a temperature.

**Relation and identity:** Entangled pairs (F8) ARE relations — the geometric residue of past compositions. Identity IS the fiber — the totality of predicates. Two entities are "the same" when their fibers produce identical respond() results. The quasicrystal structure of H*_t shows that identity has an aperiodic texture — ordered but never repeating.

**The Padovan correction matters here.** The old draft's Fibonacci assumption was wrong because it assumed FreeShadow was empty. But FreeShadow is the largest class — pure potential is the DEFAULT state. Foundational ontology must account for this: most of what exists is not yet determined in either direction. Potentiality is not absence but the mathematically dominant condition.
