Relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md) is the philosophical position that relations are ontologically prior to entities. Things do not first exist independently and then enter into relations; they are constituted through relations. The self, the object, the concept — each is what it is because of the relations it sustains, and it would be something different if those relations were different.

This position has multiple genealogies. In Western philosophy, it appears in [Alfred North Whitehead](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/general/people/alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s [process philosophy](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md) (actual entities are constituted by their [prehensions](../../../../../process-philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) of other entities), in [Charles Sanders Peirce](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/general/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md)'s [semiotics](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) ([signs](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md) are constituted by the triadic relation of [sign](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md), object, and [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md)), and in various strands of structuralism and post-structuralism. In Indigenous philosophies, relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md) is not a theoretical position arrived at through argument but a description of how the world is understood through lived practice — what [Glen Coulthard](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/glen-coulthard.md) calls [grounded normativity](../../../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/grounded-normativity.md).

The [relationality](/relationality/index.md) framework developed in this library provides a formal mathematical articulation of relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md), grounded in Lakota epistemologies. The framework demonstrates through a forced derivation that starting from the impossibility of nothing, relational structure necessarily emerges — producing logic, dynamics, geometry, and the full architecture of the [semiotic universe](/mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/). The derivation does not assume entities and then add relations; it produces entities as stable patterns within a relational field.

The political implications of relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md) are significant: if entities are constituted by relations, then [ontologization](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontologization.md) — treating contingent social arrangements as fixed features of reality — is not just a conceptual error but a form of violence. It severs the relations that constitute things and presents the severed product as natural and self-sufficient.

Relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/philosophy/terms/ontology.md) is the philosophical claim at the foundation of relationality: relations are ontologically prior to entities. Things do not exist first and then enter into relations. Rather, things are constituted through their relations — what something *is* reduces to how it relates.

This is not merely an alternative philosophical stance adopted for its consequences. The derivation demonstrates it: beginning from Relationlessness, the primitive act of [differentiation](../../../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/differentiation.md) produces [recognition](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md) before any entity-like structure exists. [Closure](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/closure.md) produces self-maintaining units, but those units are constituted by the [closure](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md) of relational activity, not by substance or [property](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/sociology/terms/property.md).

The derivation's logic is intuitionistic (a [Heyting algebra](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/mathematics/concepts/heyting-algebra/index.md) with [Together](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/terms/together.md), [Either](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/terms/either.md), [Implies](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/terms/implies.md), and [Negate](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/complement.md)), not classical. This reflects the relational stance: not every question has a determinate answer. Some matters are genuinely indeterminate until relational activity resolves them. The [law of excluded middle](../../../../../../../analytic-philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/law-of-excluded-middle.md) fails because existence is relational, not substantial — there is no fact of the matter independent of the relations that constitute it.

Relational [ontology](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/philosophy/terms/ontology.md) is grounded in Lakota epistemologies, where the primacy of relations over entities is not a theoretical innovation but a lived understanding. The derivation formalizes what that understanding requires: that relating precedes and constitutes what is related.
