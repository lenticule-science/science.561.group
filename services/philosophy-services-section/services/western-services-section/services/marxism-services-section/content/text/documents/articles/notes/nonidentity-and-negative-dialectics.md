Negative dialectics refuses the claim that contradiction is resolved into
a final conceptual reconciliation. Its core safeguard is
[nonidentity](../terms/nonidentity.md): objects are not fully identical
to the concepts that organize them [@adorno-sep-2025].

This matters methodologically. Without nonidentity, dialectical analysis
can become system-confirming: every conflict is narrated as a moment on
the way to closure. With nonidentity, critique remains open to remainder,
injury, and historical specificity that concepts fail to absorb.

In practical terms, nonidentity is a discipline against premature
synthesis. It requires analysts to track what their conceptual scheme
cannot capture without distortion.

## Relation to reification and totality

Within critical theory, nonidentity does not replace totality. It
qualifies it. Totality remains necessary for relational analysis;
nonidentity prevents totality from becoming a closed metaphysical system.

Likewise, nonidentity deepens critique of reification: if reification
turns social relations into things, nonidentity helps disclose the
suppressed mediations and particularities hidden by that thing-form.
