The "thesis-antithesis-synthesis" formula remains pedagogically popular,
but it often obscures what [sublation](../terms/sublation.md) actually
names. Sublation is not a third-step compromise. It is the structured
movement by which a form is negated, preserved, and transformed
[@hegel-dialectics-sep-2019].

## Why the triad fails

The triad model encourages template thinking: find two opposites, then
announce a synthesis. This can produce elegant narratives with weak
method. Sublation, by contrast, requires determinate transition.

A true sublation claim must show:

1. What in the prior form becomes untenable.
2. What is retained as functional content.
3. How retention is reorganized at a new level.

Without these, "synthesis" is often just rhetorical closure.

## Methodological payoff

Treating sublation rigorously helps prevent two errors:

- **Erasure error**: assuming development requires total break.
- **Continuity error**: assuming development requires no break.

Sublation gives a language for broken continuity: continuity through
transformation.
