In twentieth-century critical theory, [totality](../terms/totality.md)
and [reification](../terms/reification.md) form a paired diagnostic
framework. Reification names how social relations appear as thing-like
facts; totality names the method required to undo that appearance by
reconnecting parts to the structured whole [@lukacs-sep-2023].

The pairing is methodological, not decorative. If reification is named
without totality, critique stops at denunciation. If totality is named
without reification, analysis risks abstract system-talk detached from
lived forms of domination.

## Lukácsian contribution

Lukács's contribution is to make this pairing central to social
intelligibility under capitalism: fragmented appearances are not merely
epistemic mistakes but socially produced forms of experience
[@lukacs-sep-2023].

## Adornian correction

Adorno's negative dialectics corrects a different risk: that appeals to
totality become reconciliatory or overly closed. Nonidentity keeps open
the gap between concept and object, protecting critique from premature
closure [@adorno-sep-2025].

## Practical implication

For contemporary analysis, use totality as a relational method and
reification as a symptom concept. Then use nonidentity as a safeguard
against system closure.
