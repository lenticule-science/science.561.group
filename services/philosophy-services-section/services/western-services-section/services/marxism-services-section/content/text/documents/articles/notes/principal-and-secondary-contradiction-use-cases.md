Mao's distinction between principal and secondary contradiction is most
useful when handled as a stage-specific analytic tool, not as a rigid
formula [@mao-on-contradiction-1937].

## Use case 1: Policy conflict analysis

When multiple policy conflicts coexist, the method asks which
contradiction structures the overall field at that stage. Secondary
contradictions still matter, but they are interpreted through the
principal one.

## Use case 2: Movement strategy

In movement situations, principal contradiction identifies strategic
priority while secondary contradictions identify alliance constraints,
internal tensions, and tactical sequencing.

## Use case 3: Institutional diagnosis

Inside institutions, principal contradiction can shift rapidly. A
financial contradiction may dominate one period; a legitimacy
contradiction may dominate the next. Historicization is therefore
mandatory: ranking without periodization is methodologically weak.

## Error conditions

- Treating principal contradiction as permanent.
- Ignoring secondary contradictions as irrelevant.
- Ranking contradictions without specifying temporal scope.
