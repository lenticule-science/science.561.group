# Western Services Section

The Western Services Section holds philosophical traditions originating
in Europe and its colonial/settler extensions — from ancient Greece
through the contemporary academy. Its own topic file
(`content/text/glossary/topics/western.md`) is explicit that this is a
name for a tradition, not a claim to be "philosophy proper": the bulk of
the library's philosophical content lives here because the
library was built by people trained in Western academic traditions, a
fact about provenance rather than completeness.

The section is organized into eight `services/` subsections, each with
its own topic entry and further subdivisions:

- `analytic-philosophy-services-section/` — logical form, analysis,
  law of excluded middle, mathematical constructivism, provenness.
- `continental-services-section/` — phenomenology, deconstruction,
  poststructuralism, existentialism, and French theory, the last split
  further into named-thinker subsections for Deleuze/Guattari, Bataille,
  and Baudrillard.
- `epistemology-services-section/` — constructivism and knowledge/
  documentation, including a triage entry on reflexive-calibration theory.
- `ethics-services-section/` — presently developed almost entirely
  through care ethics, itself split into named-thinker subsections for
  Gilligan, Held, Noddings, and Tronto.
- `logic-services-section/` — classical, intuitionistic, and
  paraconsistent logic as three separate subsections, each with topics
  and cited terms (bivalence, tautology, decidability, dialetheia,
  explosion). This is the fuller logic taxonomy; the philosophy
  section's own direct `logic-services-section/` sibling holds a
  smaller, separate starting glossary and an unrelated triage backlog.
- `marxism-services-section/` — dialectics and contradiction, with a
  substantial set of notes on dialectical method (contradiction and
  mediation, principal/secondary contradiction, sublation, reification
  and totality) alongside the core glossary terms.
- `metaphysics-services-section/` — the section's largest subtree,
  split into `ontology-services-section/` (a dated 2026-04 research
  line on foundational ontology and the "definability tower") and
  `process-philosophy-services-section/` (a full Whiteadian vocabulary:
  actual occasion, concrescence, prehension, nexus, eternal object,
  and more).
- `political-philosophy-services-section/` — liberalism (including a
  late-liberalism subsection on crisis-ordinariness and quasi-events),
  queer theory, and republicanism.

Each subsection generally pairs a `topics/` entry describing its scope
with `terms/` entries carrying full citations, and several carry their
own `notes/` or `triage/` material recording work still in progress.
