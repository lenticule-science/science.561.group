# Dao Services Section

The Dao Services Section holds the estate's Daoist-philosophy material and is currently the active repository home for questions provisionally framed as Chinese philosophy.
Its developed works live at `text/documents/articles/books/`, as two books rather than a glossary or a triage backlog: a 2024 personal essay and a 2026 formal treatise, both original writing rather than commentary on the *Daodejing* or *Yijing* as external objects.
First-class questions live at `text/documents/articles/research-articles/questions/`; their placement here does not itself establish that a question or its eventual answer is Daoist.

`the-way-of-collapse.md` (dated 2024-02-28) is the earlier and shorter of the two: an anarcho-nihilist reading of the *Daodejing*'s Way and Power against ecological collapse, written after the author's first reading of a translation and stated plainly as connective, not scholarly — "there's nothing true in this text; it is not meant to teach, but connect ideas in my head to ideas in your head."

`2026-book-of-changes.md` is a nineteen-chapter work reading the *Yijing* (rather than a third pass at the *Daodejing*, which the estate had already rewritten twice in 2025) through obsmetrologia's mathematics — Belnap four-valued logic, bilattices, closure operators — keyed to the *Yijing*'s own line/trigram/hexagram structure.
Each chapter pairs a verse with a commentary and closes with an explicit grading line in the estate's own vocabulary (**[B]** background fact, **PROVED** mechanically checked, **FRAMING** an offered interpretive move, **VALVE** deliberately ungraded speculation), and a back-matter appendix restates every formal claim with file citations.
The book states its own limits directly: it sets a seven-week-old formal apparatus beside a three-thousand-year commentarial tradition (the Ten Wings, Wang Bi, Cheng Yi, Zhu Xi) and offers itself as *a* reading, not an account of what the *Yijing* "really" means.
