Ziran (自然), "self-so" or "so of itself," names in chapter 25 the standard the way itself follows once earth, heaven, and the way have each in turn been named as modeling something else: 道法自然, "the way models itself on ziran" — the one step in that chain that models itself on nothing further.

Ziran's classical sense (a thing's own spontaneous, uncaused way of being) is distinguished from the modern loan sense "nature" as the physical or biological environment; translators who refuse "Nature" as a rendering — Waley's "the Self-so," Ames & Hall's "self-derived" — are marking exactly this distinction, while a looser translation that substitutes "Nature['s laws]" imports the modern sense chapter 25 does not use.

## Renderings across published translations

Chapter 25's closing chain (人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然) is rendered:

- Legge (1891): "Man takes his law from the Earth. Earth takes its law from Heaven. Heaven takes its law from the Tao. The law of the Tao is its being what it is." [@legge1891]
- Waley (1934): "The ways of men are conditioned by those of earth. The ways of earth, by those of heaven. The ways of heaven by those of Tao, and the ways of Tao by the Self-so." [@waley1934]
- Ames & Hall (2003): "Man is derived from nature, nature is derived from Heaven, Heaven is derived from Tao. Tao is self-derived." [@ames2003]

Legge's "its being what it is" refuses to give ziran a noun at all, folding it back into a description of the Tao's own self-relation; Waley's capitalized "the Self-so" and Ames & Hall's "self-derived" both mint an English term for the concept rather than paraphrasing it away, though they mint different ones.

## The section's own translation

No existing carrier in this section formalizes ziran against the estate's own mathematical apparatus, and this entry does not manufacture one. Offered at FRAMING grade, plainly: this section's own reading is that ziran is the chain running out, honestly, rather than being secretly infinite — the *Daodejing* naming the place its own explanatory chain stops, rather than pretending some further thing the way models itself on.
