# Lau 1963 — Tao Te Ching

Harmondsworth: Penguin Classics.

## Why this is central to the glossary

Combines philological precision with idiomatic, unornamented English; its introduction is widely read as a short, reliable guide to Laozi's philosophy independent of confessional or comparative-religion framing. "The way that can be spoken of is not the constant way" is this glossary's baseline plain-register rendering of chapter 1.

## What it does not settle

Lau's 1963 edition works from the received Wang Bi-tradition text and predates both the 1973 Mawangdui silk-manuscript discovery and the 1993 Guodian bamboo-slip discovery; his own later (1982, revised) edition incorporates Mawangdui readings, and this glossary cites the 1963 wording specifically, not the revision.
