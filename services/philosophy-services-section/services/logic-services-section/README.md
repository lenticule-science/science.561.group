# Logic Services Section

The Logic Services Section, as a direct child of Philosophy Services
Section, holds a small starting glossary plus an active triage backlog —
it is not where the estate's fuller logic taxonomy lives. That fuller
taxonomy (classical, intuitionistic, and paraconsistent logic, each with
its own topics and terms) is organized instead under
`western-services-section/services/logic-services-section/`, as part of
the Western tradition's own subdivisions; this section holds material
that predates or sits outside that organization.

`content/text/glossary/terms/` holds two fully cited entries: `true.md`
and `false.md`, covering the classical semantic properties and their
non-classical treatments (truth-value gaps, gluts, and Belnap's
bilattice), each contrasting with the other and cross-referencing
material in the Western logic subsection.

`content/text/triage/` holds an unprocessed backlog on dialetheia
recognition: `research-dialetheia.md` is a fully worked term (a
proposition both true and false, per Graham Priest's dialetheism, tied
to the estate's relational-derivation research program), while
`old-archive-dialetheia-recognition.md`, `operations-dialetheia-recognition.md`,
and `research-dialetheia-recognition.md` are three near-duplicate,
skeletal operator specifications (`role: operator`, empty `inputs`/
`outputs`) for a "dialetheia recognition" transformation — captured in
different triage passes.
