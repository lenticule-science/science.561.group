## Start from the current, source-owned knowledge

- [FOUR: bilateral evidence reading](content/text/glossary/terms/four.md)
  — two independently admitted witness-presence sides of exactly one
  typed predication under one scope. `absurd` = neither; `potential` =
  for only; `active` = against only; `stable` = both. These are not
  epistemic inquiry stages or measures of external truth.
- [Closure-rule language](content/text/glossary/concepts/closure-rule-language.md)
  — distinguish positive, knowledge-upward thresholds on finite cells
  from quantified infinitary schemata. A syntactic universal block
  **may**, but need not, cause non-Scott-continuous firing.
- [Π₂ rule-language schemas and realizability](content/text/glossary/concepts/rule-language-pi2-schemas-realizability.md)
  — speculative transfinite bounds **plus** an explicit, implementable,
  finite FOUR-valued `∀∃` restriction and a separate classical
  counterexample for a genuine directed-limit obstruction.
- [Paraconsistent aperiodic classification](content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-paraconsistent-classification.md)
  — one consolidated canonical research note, not three copies.
  Its bigraded-classification biconditional is **not yet proved**;
  it requires actual invariant-completeness and native-formalization
  arguments before receiving as a theorem.
- [Specialization adjoints](content/text/glossary/concepts/specialization-hyperdoctrinal-adjoints.md)
  — a related finite-fiber restriction to distinguish from arbitrary
  hyperdoctrine quantifier schemas.

## How knowledge becomes computing

A mathematical result is grounded in the Group's generated
relational foundation, then qualified through Obsmetrologia. Only
after the corresponding Science meanings, proof conditions and
Engineering requirements are identified does the reusable runtime
implementation belong with Computer Data Engineering.

The finite, admitted parts currently realized by Engineering are
[Metrologic positive-rule settling](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-data-engineering-services-section/blob/main/lib/metrologic/src/articulation/predicating/positing/settling/analysis/closure-rule-system.mjs)
and the [FOUR hyperdoctrine adjoints](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-data-engineering-services-section/blob/main/lib/metrologic/src/hyperdoctrine/adjoints.mjs).
Schema-carried quantifier programs derive finite index maps from
explicit tables and execute through the **existing** adjoints.
Neither a schema nor a passing property test recovers actual
transfinite computation or the consumption discipline of linear
logic. Keep these open obligations separate.

Operations owns parameters, procedures and receiving for particular
consumers; engineering source commits are not installations.

## Working in this corpus

Before extending it, read the relevant glossary and primary research
notes, compare related variants, keep the strongest distinct
arguments and merge duplicate tellings into one canonical file.
Use cross-links rather than copying a whole research explanation
into a term. Label incomplete theorem arguments as hypotheses or
sketches, retain unique historical evidence, and leave old revisions
recoverable through Git history.
