- Set monthly income and expense targets for each account category.
- Ensure budgeted expenses plus savings do not exceed budgeted income.
- At period close, compare actual income statement lines to budget targets.
- Identify categories consistently over or under budget.
- Adjust budget or spending based on patterns, not single-period anomalies.
- Review budget quarterly and adjust for changed circumstances.
