## Annotation

A standard graduate-level text on sheaf cohomology, with extensive treatment of Čech cohomology and its derived-functor comparison, flabby and soft resolutions, Godement's construction. For the program, the technical backbone for the [Čech-derived comparison on the bitopological site](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/cech-derived-comparison-on-bitopological-site.md): the Godement flabby resolution serves as the universal acyclic resolution when [Husainov acyclicity](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/concepts/husainov-acyclicity.md) of the bitopological site fails.
