## Annotation

**Direct predecessor for the hyperdoctrinal-layer's substructural-hyperdoctrine move.** Generalizes Lawvere's hyperdoctrine framework to first-order logics over the **Typed Full Lambek Calculus**: fibers carry a residuated monoidal structure $\otimes \dashv \multimap$ with the monoidal unit $I$ not necessarily the lattice top.

Maruyama's **Frobenius reciprocity** uses $\otimes$:

$$\exists_f(f^*\alpha \otimes \phi) \cong \alpha \otimes \exists_f \phi$$

generalizing Lawvere's $\wedge$-Frobenius. The Typed Full Lambek setting covers linear, relevant, intuitionistic, and classical first-order logic uniformly.

**Where the hyperdoctrinal layer departs.** On $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ the fusion $\odot$ is non-integral, so Frobenius-for-$\odot$ fails for three of the four generator classes here. The hyperdoctrinal layer instead uses the **bilattice knowledge-meet** $\wedge_k$ (and truth-meet $\wedge_t$) for Frobenius, recovering the Lawvere-style statement with bilattice-native conjunctions whose absorbers align with $\bot$ and $\mathbf{f}$ respectively.

Also introduces a **Lawvere–Tierney analogue** on hyperdoctrines for unified treatment of logical translations. Action item: acquire full text.
