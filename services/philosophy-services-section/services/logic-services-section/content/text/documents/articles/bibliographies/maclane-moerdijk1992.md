## Annotation

The standard introductory text on topos theory and sheaves on sites: Grothendieck topologies, sheafification, sites, geometric morphisms, classifying topoi, and the topos-theoretic perspective on logic. For the program, supplies the site-theoretic infrastructure underlying the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md) — Grothendieck topology axioms, sheaf condition formulations, and standard sheafification machinery.
