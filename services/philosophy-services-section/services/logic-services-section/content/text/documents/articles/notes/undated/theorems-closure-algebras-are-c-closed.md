# Closure 2-monad algebras are $\mathcal{C}$-closed presheaves

Pseudo-algebras of the [closure 2-monad](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/hacking/library/closure-2-monad/docs/dev/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) $\mathrm{Cl}$ are exactly the [$\mathcal{C}$-closed](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predicate-category/content/predicate-category-naturality/predicate-category-naturality.md) [predication fibrations](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/hacking/library/closure-2-monad/docs/dev/objects/predication-fibration.md).

## Proof

A pseudo-algebra structure on $\mathcal{E}$ is a morphism $a : \mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal{E}) \to \mathcal{E}$ inverse to $\eta_\mathcal{E}$ up to canonical iso. By inflationarity, $\eta_\mathcal{E}$ is an inclusion $\mathcal{E} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal{E})$; for $a$ to be inverse requires $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathcal{E}$ — i.e., $\mathcal{E}$ is already closed. Conversely, every $\mathcal{C}$-closed predication settling admits the identity as its canonical $\mathrm{Cl}$-algebra structure. $\square$

## Remark

This recovers the [predicate-category naturality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predicate-category/content/predicate-category-naturality/predicate-category-naturality.md) property exactly: "$\mathcal{C}$-closure is the characteristic property that the closure operator produces" is upgraded here to "being $\mathcal{C}$-closed is a $\mathrm{Cl}$-algebra structure, which is unique-up-to-iso by Kelly–Lack (K2)."
