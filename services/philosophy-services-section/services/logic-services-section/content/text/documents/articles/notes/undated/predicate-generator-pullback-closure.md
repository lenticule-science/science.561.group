# Predicate generator pullback closure

The four [predicate-category](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predicate-category.md) generator classes — [specialization](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-specialization.md), [converse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md), [subject-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [object-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-object-projection.md) — are closed under pullback formation in $\mathcal{C}$.

## Proof

Ten unordered pullback pairs verified case-by-case: spec × spec → spec on intersection of sub-supports; spec × converse → specialization with swap of sub-support; spec × subject-projection → specialization of rows; converse × subject-projection → diagonal; subject-projection × object-projection → diagonal; and analogous cases. All pullbacks yield objects in the four-generator closure. $\square$
