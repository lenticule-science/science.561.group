## Annotation

The "Tôhoku" paper: introduces the AB-axiom hierarchy for abelian categories (AB1–AB5, AB3*–AB5*), establishes existence of injective resolutions in AB5 categories with a generator, and grounds modern sheaf cohomology in homological algebra. For the program, AB5 — exact filtered colimits — is the property that lets derived functors commute with the filtered colimits used in Scott-continuous regime cohomology computations.
