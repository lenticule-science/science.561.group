# MLD-equivalence is trivial in the strict regime

For two settlings $v, v'$ in the [strict regime](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md) over a common base, [MLD-equivalence](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/docs/dev/concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md) $v \sim_{\mathrm{MLD}} v'$ is decidable in polynomial time and reduces to $H^0$-matching.

## Proof

By [strict-regime cohomology vanishing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md), $H^1 = 0$ in the strict regime, so the [aperiodic classifying invariant](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/docs/dev/objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) (the $H^1$-class detecting MLD-equivalence in transfinite regimes) is identically zero for every strict settling.

The MLD-classification reduces to its $H^0$-component: two strict settlings are MLD-equivalent iff their designated-cell sets at quiescence agree up to settling-shift and entity-relabeling — an equality of finite sets of cells, computable in polynomial time by [finite-settling $H^0$](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/finite-settling-h0-polynomial-time.md). $\square$

## Note

This theorem says the strict regime is *cohomologically uninteresting* and *combinatorially trivial*: every strict settling is MLD-equivalent to every other settling with the same $H^0$, with no further refinement available. The richness of the MLD-classification only emerges in the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes — see [combinatorial classification across regimes](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/docs/dev/theorems/combinatorial-classification-across-regimes.md).
