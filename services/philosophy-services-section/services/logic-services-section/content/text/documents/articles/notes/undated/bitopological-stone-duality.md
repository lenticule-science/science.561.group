# Bitopological Stone duality

The category $\mathbf{PStone}$ of [pairwise Stone spaces](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/pairwise-stone-space/pairwise-stone-space.md) is isomorphic to the category $\mathbf{Spec}$ of spectral spaces and to the category $\mathbf{Pries}$ of [Priestley spaces](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/priestley-space.md):

$$\mathbf{PStone} \;\cong\; \mathbf{Spec} \;\cong\; \mathbf{Pries}.$$

## Proof

[@bezhanishvili-et-al2010] (Theorems 2.9, 3.4). $\square$

## Why this matters here

The bitopological framing is mathematically equivalent to the Priestley framing but treats the two topologies symmetrically. For the [paired Priestley space](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/paired-priestley-space.md) duals of the [predication fiber algebras](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) — where the two topologies arise naturally from the positive and negative coordinates of the [twist product](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md) — pairwise-Stone is the natural framing for sheaf-theoretic constructions. Covers can be specified pairwise-open without privileging either coordinate, and dualization of [predicate](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/concepts/predicate.md) generators (specialization, converse, subject- and object-projection) acts symmetrically on both topologies.
