## Full citation

J. A. Kalman. "Lattices with Involution." *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 87(2), 485–491, 1958.

## Status

Pending direct access. Cited as the historical origin of the Kleene-lattice construction that became the twist product.

## Annotation

Introduces **Kleene lattices** — De Morgan lattices satisfying $x \wedge x' \leq y \vee y'$. The Kalman construction $L \times L^{\mathrm{op}}$ for a bounded distributive lattice $L$, with the diagonal involution $(a, b) \mapsto (b, a)$, is the direct ancestor of the twist product $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ used throughout our program.

The bilattice formulation of the same construction (with two compatible orderings rather than a single order with involution) appears in [@ginsberg1988] and is given its modern form in [@fitting2006] and [@rivieccio2010].

The foundational layer cites for historical attribution of the twist-product construction.
