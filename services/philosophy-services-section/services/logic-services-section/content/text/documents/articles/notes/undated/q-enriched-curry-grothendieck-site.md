# Q-enriched Curry equivalence: Grothendieck sites with cellular fibers

The Grothendieck-site lift of the per-fiber Q-enriched Curry equivalence. Where the per-fiber theorems handle individual cell complexes, this theorem assembles the per-fiber equivalence across a fibered site whose fibers are cell complexes — the structural shape exhibited by the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md). The lift adds one new ingredient — base-change fuzziness — and one new structural verification — that fiber-level Curry maps assemble compatibly across base morphisms.

The theorem covers both **finite-cellular fibers** (using the [CW-complex theorem](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) as per-fiber input) and **transfinite-cellular fibers** (using the [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) as per-fiber input). The proof structure is identical in both cases; only the cited per-fiber theorem and the per-fiber fuzziness profile differ.

## Setup: fibered cellular sites

A **fibered cellular site** is the data of:

- a small category $\mathbf{B}$ (the **base**) carrying the trivial Grothendieck topology;
- a small category $\mathbf{T}$ (the **total site**) with a Grothendieck topology $J$;
- a functor $\pi : \mathbf{T} \to \mathbf{B}$ (the **projection**);

subject to two structural conditions:

1. **Fiber-cellular condition.** For each $b \in \mathbf{B}$, the fiber $\mathbf{T}_b := \pi^{-1}(b)$ (with the induced category structure) carries the structure of a regular CW complex's face-incidence poset — either a [finite regular CW complex](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) or a [transfinite cell complex](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/objects/transfinite-cell-complex.md) (countable cells indexed up to $\omega_1$). Write $\mathrm{cells}_k(b)$ for the $k$-cells.

2. **Fiber-only covers.** The covering families in $J$ at any object $(b, \sigma) \in \mathbf{T}$ are exactly the families generated by face inclusions within the fiber $\mathbf{T}_b$. There are no covering families crossing base objects.

The bitopological settling site $(\mathbf{T}^*, J)$ realizes this with $\mathbf{B} = \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$, and $\mathbf{T}_{(q, \tau)}$ the **order complex of the lattice of pairwise-Stone clopens of the [paired Priestley space](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/paired-priestley-space.md) $X_q^\tau$**: cells are clopens (objects of the bitopological site at $(q, \tau)$, per the [bitopological settling site object](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md)), face incidences are the inclusion chains $U_0 \subset U_1 \subset \cdots \subset U_k$. For the standard Belnap setting ($\mathbf L = \mathbf 2$, $X_q^\tau = \{+, -\}$), the lattice has 4 elements and its order complex is the contractible subdivided square (geometrically Δ¹) with 4 vertices, 5 edges, and 2 triangles. Covering sieves at $(q, \tau)$ are pairwise-Stone refinements, equivalently face-inclusion families in the order complex, matching predication categories' [why-fiber-covers-only](why-fiber-covers-only.md) reasoning.

The per-fiber Curry equivalence on this fiber has been concretely computed for the d-frame-valued sheaf in the simplest Belnap placement: see [per-fiber d-frame Curry on the Belnap fiber example](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/examples/per-fiber-d-frame-curry-on-belnap-fiber.md). The result agrees with what [Framework 2's strict-regime theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/strict-regime-d-frame-cohomology.md) predicts in its discrete-points setting — first concrete cross-framework agreement evidence for the theorem chain assembled here.

## Q-enriched data over a fibered cellular site

A $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched cellular sheaf $F$ on $\mathbf{T}$ packages two pieces of data:

- **Per-fiber data.** For each $b \in \mathbf{B}$, the restriction $F_b := F|_{\mathbf{T}_b}$ is a $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched cellular sheaf on the cell complex $\mathbf{T}_b$ — the [finite version](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/content/quantale-enriched-sheaf/quantale-enriched-sheaf.md) or [transfinite version](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/commutating/content/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category/content/anderson-putnam-direct-limit/content/transfinite-cell-complex/content/q-enriched-transfinite-cellular-sheaf/q-enriched-transfinite-cellular-sheaf.md) according to the fiber's cellularity. Stalks $F_b(\sigma)$ for $\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)$, restrictions $f_{b, \sigma}^\tau$ along face incidences in $\mathbf{T}_b$.

- **Base-change data.** For each $\beta : b' \to b$ in $\mathbf{B}$ and each $\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)$, a $\mathsf{Q}$-functor $F(\beta, \sigma) : F_b(\sigma) \to F_{b'}(\beta^*\sigma)$ where $\beta^*$ is the cellular pullback (the morphism in $\mathbf{T}$ over $\beta$).

The cosheaf $\check{F}$ is structured dually: per-fiber cosheaves $\check{F}_b$ via the [CW-complex theorem](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md)'s fuzzy adjoint extensions, and base-change *extensions* $\check{F}(\beta, \sigma) : \check{F}_{b'}(\beta^*\sigma) \to \check{F}_b(\sigma)$ — the **fuzzy right adjoints** of the sheaf-side base-change Q-functors, with quantale-graded fuzziness $q_\beta^\sigma \in \mathsf{Q}$.

## Global cohomology and homology

The site's fiber-only covers mean global cohomology decomposes as **base-presheaf cohomology of fiber cohomologies**.

**Sheaf cohomology.** Define the **fiber-cohomology presheaf**

$$\underline{H}^k(F) \,:\, \mathbf{B}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}, \qquad b \mapsto H^k(F_b),$$

with restriction along $\beta : b' \to b$ given by the fiber-cohomology functoriality of the base-change $F(\beta, -)$. The global sheaf cohomology is then the **base-presheaf cohomology** of $\underline{H}^k(F)$:

$$H^n(\mathbf{T}, J;\, F) \,=\, \mathbb{H}^n(\mathbf{B},\, \underline{H}^\bullet(F)),$$

where $\mathbb{H}^n$ is the hypercohomology of the cochain complex $\underline{H}^\bullet(F) = (\underline{H}^k(F))_{k \geq 0}$ as a presheaf of cochain complexes on $\mathbf{B}$. Since $\mathbf{B}$ has trivial topology, $\mathbb{H}^n$ reduces to the limit-and-equalizer construction over $\mathbf{B}$ — the **functor cohomology** of the presheaf.

**Cosheaf homology.** Dually, define the **fiber-homology copresheaf**

$$\underline{H}_k(\check{F}) \,:\, \mathbf{B} \to \mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}, \qquad b \mapsto H_k(\check{F}_b),$$

with extension along $\beta : b' \to b$ given by the fiber-homology functoriality of the base-change *extensions* $\check{F}(\beta, -)$. The global cosheaf homology is the **base-copresheaf homology**:

$$H_n(\mathbf{T}, J;\, \check{F}) \,=\, \mathbb{H}_n(\mathbf{B},\, \underline{H}_\bullet(\check{F})).$$

Since $\mathbf{B}$ has trivial topology, $\mathbb{H}_n$ reduces to the colimit-and-coequalizer construction — the functor homology of the copresheaf.

## The theorem

**Theorem (Q-enriched Curry equivalence on fibered cellular sites).** Let $\pi : \mathbf{T} \to \mathbf{B}$ be a fibered cellular site, $F$ a $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched cellular sheaf on $\mathbf{T}$, and $\check{F}$ its associated cosheaf via per-fiber and base-change fuzzy right adjoints. Then for each degree $n$, there is a q-fuzzy equivalence

$$H^n(\mathbf{T}, J;\, F) \,\simeq_{q_n^{\mathrm{glob}}(F)}\, H_n(\mathbf{T}, J;\, \check{F})$$

with **global fuzziness profile**

$$q_n^{\mathrm{glob}}(F) \,=\, \Bigl(\bigwedge_{b \in \mathbf{B}} q_n(\mathbf{T}_b, F_b)\Bigr) \;\wedge\; \Bigl(\bigwedge_{\beta \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathbf{B})} \prod_{\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)} q_\beta^\sigma\Bigr),$$

where $q_n(\mathbf{T}_b, F_b)$ is the per-fiber fuzziness from the [CW-complex theorem](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) and $q_\beta^\sigma$ is the base-change fuzziness on the stalk $F_b(\sigma)$.

The first meet is the **fiber contribution** (per-fiber Curry fuzziness, accumulated over base objects). The second is the **base-change contribution** (fuzziness from the assembly across base morphisms).

## Proof

The argument has three steps: per-fiber lift, base-presheaf compatibility, and global assembly via the trivial-topology cohomology decomposition.

**Step 1: per-fiber Curry equivalence.** For each $b \in \mathbf{B}$, the appropriate per-fiber theorem applies — the [CW-complex theorem](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) for finite-cellular fibers, the [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) for transfinite-cellular fibers — giving

$$H^k(F_b) \,\simeq_{q_k(\mathbf{T}_b, F_b)}\, H_k(\check{F}_b)$$

for all relevant $k$. Write $\Phi_b : H^k(F_b) \to H_k(\check{F}_b)$ for the per-fiber forward map and $\Psi_b$ for the inverse, both established by the cited theorem. The fuzziness $q_k(\mathbf{T}_b, F_b)$ is finite-cellular when the fiber is finite, transfinite (a meet over finite sub-complexes) when the fiber is transfinite.

**Step 2: base-presheaf compatibility (explicit).** The per-fiber maps $\{\Phi_b\}$ assemble into a fuzzy-natural transformation between the fiber-cohomology presheaf $\underline H^k(F)$ and the fiber-homology copresheaf $\underline H_k(\check F)$ on $\mathbf B$. Concretely, for $\beta : b' \to b$, the diagram

$$\begin{array}{ccc} H^k(F_b) & \xrightarrow{\;\Phi_b\;} & H_k(\check F_b) \\ \downarrow{\scriptstyle F(\beta)_*} & & \uparrow{\scriptstyle \check F(\beta)_*} \\ H^k(F_{b'}) & \xrightarrow{\;\Phi_{b'}\;} & H_k(\check F_{b'}) \end{array}$$

commutes up to fuzzy equivalence, with fuzziness bounded by $\bigwedge_{\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)} q_\beta^\sigma$ (the meet of stalk-level base-change fuzzinesses across cells of $b$).

**Why naturality holds.** The argument has the same structural shape as Step 2 of the [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md), but with two layers of fuzzy adjunction (per-fiber + base-change) composed multiplicatively.

For an element $[c] \in H^k(F_b)$ with cocycle representative $c \in C^k(F_b)$, both routes through the diagram act on $c$ as follows:

- *Route 1 ($\Phi_b$ then $\check F(\beta)_*$).* $\Phi_b([c])$ is a class in $H_k(\check F_b)$ built from $c$ via the per-fiber Curry forward map of the [CW-complex theorem](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) (or [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) for transfinite fibers). $\check F(\beta)_*$ then transports along the cosheaf-side base-change extensions $\check F(\beta, \sigma) : \check F_{b'}(\beta^* \sigma) \to \check F_b(\sigma)$ — which are the *fuzzy right adjoints* of the sheaf-side base-change Q-functors $F(\beta, \sigma)$.

- *Route 2 ($F(\beta)_*$ then $\Phi_{b'}$).* $F(\beta)_*([c])$ pulls back to $H^k(F_{b'})$ via the sheaf-side base-change Q-functors. $\Phi_{b'}$ then applies the per-fiber Curry forward map at $b'$.

The two routes differ exactly by the base-change fuzzy unit-counit composition at each cell $\sigma$ of $b$: $\check F(\beta, \sigma) \circ F(\beta, \sigma) \simeq_{q_\beta^\sigma} \mathrm{id}$. The per-fiber Curry maps $\Phi_b, \Phi_{b'}$ commute with the cell-wise structure (by the per-fiber theorem's chain-fuzziness formula), so the diagram's failure to commute strictly is exactly the base-change unit-counit fuzziness, accumulated as a meet over cells $\sigma$ of $b$ where the routes differ.

So Route 1 and Route 2 agree on $[c]$ up to fuzziness $\bigwedge_\sigma q_\beta^\sigma$.

Identical argument for the inverse maps $\{\Psi_b\}$ — a fuzzy-natural transformation in the other direction with the same fuzziness bound.

**Step 3: global assembly via limit / colimit.** Because $\mathbf B$ has trivial topology, base-presheaf cohomology / copresheaf homology of $\underline H^\bullet$ and $\underline H_\bullet$ are computed as limits / colimits over $\mathbf B$ in $\mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}$:

$$\mathbb H^n(\mathbf B, \underline H^\bullet(F)) \;=\; \lim_{b \in \mathbf B} \underline H^n(F)(b) \;=\; \lim_b H^n(F_b),$$
$$\mathbb H_n(\mathbf B, \underline H_\bullet(\check F)) \;=\; \mathrm{colim}_{b \in \mathbf B} \underline H_n(\check F)(b) \;=\; \mathrm{colim}_b H_n(\check F_b).$$

Limits and colimits in $\mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}$ exist by [@kelly1982] Theorem 3.73 + Proposition 3.75 (specialized to $\mathsf V = \mathsf Q$): $\mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}$ is a reflective full subcategory of the $\mathsf Q$-presheaf category $[\mathsf Q^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$, hence complete and cocomplete, with limits computed pointwise and colimits computed via reflection.

By the universal properties: the fuzzy-natural transformation $\{\Phi_b\}$ from Step 2 induces a unique Q-functor

$$\Phi^{\mathrm{glob}} : \lim_b H^k(F_b) \to \mathrm{colim}_b H_k(\check F_b)$$

via the limit-colimit composition (compose with the limit projections on the source, then with the colimit injections on the target). Symmetrically for $\Psi^{\mathrm{glob}}$.

**Fuzziness bound for $\Phi^{\mathrm{glob}}$.** The fuzziness of $\Phi^{\mathrm{glob}}$ at any element is determined by the worst-case fuzziness encountered along the limit-colimit assembly. Two contributions:

- *Per-fiber.* For an element $[c] \in \lim_b H^k(F_b)$ with components $\{[c_b] \in H^k(F_b)\}_b$, the per-fiber forward map applied at each $b$ has fuzziness $q_k(\mathbf T_b, F_b)$. The worst-case across $b$ is $\bigwedge_b q_k(\mathbf T_b, F_b)$.
- *Base-change.* The fuzzy-naturality from Step 2 contributes $\bigwedge_{\beta \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathbf B)} \bigwedge_{\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)} q_\beta^\sigma$, the worst-case fuzziness over all base morphisms.

Combined:

$$q_n^{\mathrm{glob}}(F) \;=\; \Bigl(\bigwedge_{b \in \mathbf B} q_n(\mathbf T_b, F_b)\Bigr) \;\wedge\; \Bigl(\bigwedge_{\beta \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathbf B)} \bigwedge_{\sigma \in \mathrm{cells}(b)} q_\beta^\sigma\Bigr),$$

matching the formula in the theorem statement.

**Round-trip verification.** $\Phi^{\mathrm{glob}} \circ \Psi^{\mathrm{glob}}$ at any element factors through per-fiber round-trips at each $b$ (each contributing fuzziness $q_n(\mathbf T_b, F_b)$) and base-change unit-counit pairs at each $\beta$ (each contributing $\bigwedge_\sigma q_\beta^\sigma$). The total fuzziness is the meet of all contributions, which equals $q_n^{\mathrm{glob}}$. Identical for $\Psi^{\mathrm{glob}} \circ \Phi^{\mathrm{glob}}$.

$\square$

## Specialization to the bitopological settling site

For the bitopological settling site $(\mathbf{T}^*, J)$ with $\mathbf{B} = \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$ and fibers the paired Priestley spaces $X_q^\tau$:

**Strict regime ($\kappa < \omega$).** Each fiber is a finite cell complex; the base $\mathbf{B}$ is a finite category. The CW-complex theorem applies per fiber; finitely many base-changes contribute to $q^{\mathrm{glob}}$. The fuzziness is a finite meet of finite-products, in $\mathsf{Q}$.

**Scott-continuous regime ($\kappa = \omega$).** Each fiber is finite (countable cell complex via Husainov acyclicity); base is countable. The CW-complex theorem applies; $q^{\mathrm{glob}}$ is a countable meet, well-defined in $\mathsf{Q}$ (which has all meets / joins).

**Transfinite-bounded regime ($\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$).** Fibers may be transfinite-cellular (with countable cell sets indexed by ordinals $< \omega_1$). The [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) supplies the per-fiber Curry equivalence; the global theorem assembles it across the base. The per-fiber fuzziness is itself a meet over finite sub-complexes of the fiber, and the global fuzziness is a meet of these per-fiber meets together with the base-change contribution. For predication categories' typical Boolean d-frame data, all per-finite-sub-complex fuzzinesses are 1, so the global fuzziness $q^{\mathrm{glob}} = 1$ — strict equivalence in this regime.

**Aperiodic regime ($\omega_1 \leq \kappa$).** Fibers are $\omega_1$-cofinal transfinite cell complexes. The same per-fiber transfinite-CW input applies; the base $\mathbf{B}$ now has $\omega_1$-many ordinal strata as well, but all base-changes (predicate-changes via $\mathcal{C}$, settling-advancements within $\lambda$) are themselves Boolean-projection Q-functors with strict adjoints in predication categories' typical setting. So $q^{\mathrm{glob}} = 1$ in the aperiodic regime too.

So the theorem now covers all four regimes of the bitopological settling site, with the per-fiber input switching between [finite-CW](q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) (strict, Scott-continuous) and [transfinite-CW](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) (transfinite-bounded, aperiodic) as appropriate. The Verdier duality conjecture is reduced to "established at draft level across all four regimes" — a substantial completion of the cellular extension plan.

## What's open (beyond this theorem)

**Non-Boolean value categories.** The Layer 1+2+3 chain has been verified in predication categories' typical Boolean d-frame setting; whether non-Boolean value categories (Lawvere-quantale-enriched, MV-algebra-enriched) yield non-degenerate transfinite fuzzinesses is open and depends on per-finite-sub-complex fuzziness behavior under the directed colimit (see the [transfinite-CW theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/quantale-enriched-sheaf/docs/dev/theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md)'s non-degeneracy criterion).

## What this theorem gives

With this theorem in hand:
