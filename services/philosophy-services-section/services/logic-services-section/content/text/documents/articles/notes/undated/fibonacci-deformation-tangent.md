# Deformation tangent of the Fibonacci substitution settling

The L5a instance of the [Ext-classification of SKH deformations](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md) on the aperiodic Fibonacci case. **Lineage 5a** — first concrete instance of an *uncountable* deformation tangent space.

## Ext^1 computation

For the Fibonacci settling at $E = \omega_1$:

$$\mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}^{|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)|} \;=\; \mathbb{Z}^{2^{\aleph_0}}$$

(or more precisely, the deformation space at the per-firing level, with cardinality matching $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}|$ which is uncountable). **Uncountable deformation tangent space.**

## Deformation rank progression

| Lineage | $\dim \mathrm{Ext}^1$ |
|---|---|
| L1 (strict, single-cell) | 0 |
| L2 (strict, multi-entity) | 0 |
| L3 (Scott-continuous) | 0 |
| L4 (transfinite-bounded, two-firing) | 2 |
| L4 (transfinite-bounded, $\omega$ firings) | $\omega$ |
| L5 (this file: aperiodic Fibonacci) | $2^{\aleph_0}$ |

The progression peaks at L5, where the deformation tangent space reaches its maximal cardinality (matching the realizability cardinality bound).

## Ext^2 vanishing

By [vanishing-above-degree-1](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md), $\mathrm{Ext}^2 = 0$. Unobstructed: every first-order deformation extends. **The Fibonacci-substitution moduli stack is formally smooth at this point.**
