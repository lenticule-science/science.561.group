# predication settling merge is a semilattice operation

The pointwise [knowledge-order](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/teaching/explanations/knowledge-order.md) join $\vee_k$ on [predication settlings](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-settling.md) — the merge operation of a [distributed settling system](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/distributed-settling-system.md) — is commutative, associative, and idempotent.

## Proof

Pointwise lift of the knowledge-order join being a semilattice operation on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$. Commutativity, associativity, and idempotence of $\vee_k$ on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ pass to pointwise application across the entity-pair index $E \times E$, predicates, and settling steps. $\square$
