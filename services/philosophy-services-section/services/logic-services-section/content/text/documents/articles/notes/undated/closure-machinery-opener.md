# Closure machinery

The settling's saturation step is performed by a [closure operator](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/morphisms/closure-operator.md) $\mathrm{cl} = (\mathrm{cl}_q)_{q \in \mathcal{C}}$ — a family of monotone, inflationary, idempotent maps on the [predication fiber algebras](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), one per predicate, satisfying a [naturality condition](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predicate-category/content/predicate-category-naturality/predicate-category-naturality.md) under the four-generator reindexing. Concretely: closures are generated by inference rules; firing the closure exhaustively at a valuation produces the smallest saturated extension under those rules.

Two views of the same machinery sit at different levels:

- **As a fiberwise operator.** Each $\mathrm{cl}_q : A_q \to A_q$ is a closure operator in the classical sense — its fixed points form a sub-meet-semilattice ([post-closure bilattice closure](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/formalization/infomaterial/lib/infomaterial/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/post-closure-bilattice-closure.md)). The settling orbit $v_{\tau+1} := \mathrm{cl}(v_\tau \vee_k u_\tau)$ at successor steps is the iteration that drives saturation.

- **As a 2-monad.** The fiberwise closure family lifts to a [closure 2-monad](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) $\mathrm{Cl}$ on the 2-category $\mathbf{PrFib}(\mathcal{C})$ of [predication fibrations](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/predication-fibration.md). The 2-monad is *lax-idempotent* in the Kelly–Lack sense — see [closure 2-monad lax-idempotence](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md) — meaning its algebra structure is a *property*, not a choice. A predication fibration is a $\mathrm{Cl}$-algebra iff every fiber is closed under the closure family. This is the [predicate-category naturality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predicate-category/content/predicate-category-naturality/predicate-category-naturality.md) condition stated 2-monadically.

The lax-idempotent property is what makes the framework structurally robust: settling quiescence is exactly $\mathrm{Cl}$-algebra status at the fixed ordinal — see [quiescence as closure algebra](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/quiescence-as-closure-algebra.md) — and the algebra structure being a property rather than a choice means there is no ambiguity in what "saturated" means. The Priestley-dualization machinery ([dualized closure 2-comonad](dualized-closure-2-comonad.md)) turns this lax-idempotent monad into a colax-idempotent comonad on the dual side, which is what licenses the cohomological invariants downstream. Under this duality, $\mathrm{Cl}$-algebras correspond to $\mathrm{Cl}^*$-coalgebras — see [closure-algebra/coalgebra correspondence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/closure-algebra-coalgebra-correspondence.md). The algebra structure on individual fibers is restricted by the [post-closure adjunction restriction](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/post-closure-adjunction-restriction.md): the residuated structure on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ does not always pass through quiescence cleanly.
