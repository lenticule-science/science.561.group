# Predicate object-projection

A morphism in the [predicate category](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predicate-category.md), denoted $f : q \to p$, induced by the collapse of the entity-pair index to the object-diagonal:
$$(s, o) \mapsto (o, o).$$

The induced fiber-algebra map $f^* : A_p \to A_q$ acts by:

$$f^*(v)(s, o) = v(o, o).$$

A predicate object-projection is the dual of [predicate subject-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md): the value of $q$ at $(s, o)$ depends only on $p$'s value at $(o, o)$, broadcasting from the object-diagonal.
