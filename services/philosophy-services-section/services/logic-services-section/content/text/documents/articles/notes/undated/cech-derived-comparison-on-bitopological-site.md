# Čech computes derived cohomology under Husainov acyclicity

Let $F \in \mathrm{Sh}^{\mathrm{KZ}}(\mathbf{T}^*, J)$ and $\sigma < \infty$. If for every $\tau \leq \sigma$ the poset $(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \{\tau\}, \leq)$ — equipped with the restriction of $F$ to stage $\tau$ — satisfies the [[@husainov2026] acyclicity condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/bitopological-settling-site/docs/dev/concepts/husainov-acyclicity.md), then

$$\check{H}^n(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \sigma}, F) \;\cong\; H^n(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \sigma}, F) \qquad \text{for all } n \geq 0.$$

Sufficient: $\mathcal{C}$ is finite, or $F$ is [predicate-locally-constant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-local-constancy/settling-local-constancy.md).

## Proof

The topology $J$ on the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md) decomposes into a fiber part (pairwise-Stone covers at fixed $(q, \tau)$) and a cross-ordinal/cross-predicate part (encoded entirely in morphisms, not in covers — see [why fiber covers only](why-fiber-covers-only.md)).

**Fiber part.** The pairwise-Stone clopens of a paired Priestley space form a Boolean algebra. For any constant sheaf $\underline{A}$ and any pairwise-Stone cover, $\check{H}^n(\mathcal{U}, \underline{A}) = A$ for $n = 0$ and $0$ for $n \geq 1$ — the standard Stone-space Čech vanishing in positive degree ([@bredon1997] Ch. III §2). Fiber Čech agrees with fiber derived-functor cohomology in this setting.

**Cross-ordinal / cross-predicate part.** Across the ordinal-predicate base, the sheafification acts as a *poset-sheafification* (the Alexandrov-topology equivalence between finite posets and finite topological spaces extends to bounded-ordinal cases). The [@husainov2026] Dedekind–MacNeille acyclicity condition is exactly what makes poset-Čech agree with poset-derived-functor cohomology on the base.

Combining: under the hypothesis, the fiber and cross-ordinal Čech cohomologies separately compute the corresponding derived-functor cohomologies, and the full $\check{H}^n = H^n$ comparison holds. $\square$
