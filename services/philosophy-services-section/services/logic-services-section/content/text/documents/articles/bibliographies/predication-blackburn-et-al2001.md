## Annotation

The standard reference on classical modal logic: Kripke semantics, completeness via canonical models, the finite model property, complexity classifications (PSPACE-complete local consequence, EXPTIME-complete global), filtration, bisimulation. For the program, the classical apparatus is what the four-valued bilattice modal calculus generalizes; the FMP and the complexity bounds are inherited via the [twist-modal translation](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-bound/content/world-set/content/bilattice-accessibility-relation/content/twist-modal-translation/twist-modal-translation.md) from the underlying classical modal logic.
