# Higher-categorical extensions

The [temporal 2-category](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/temporal-2-category/temporal-2-category.md) and the closure structure are 2-categorical, treated modulo strictification where coherence becomes intricate. Whether the construction extends to $(\infty, 1)$-categorical settings — replacing strict 2-categories with quasicategories and the [Emmenegger comonad](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/emmenegger-fibration-comonad.md) with a higher adjoint — is open.

Potentially useful for formal coherence arguments now handled by appeal to strictification. The downstream cohomology work may force the issue if the derived-functor machinery prefers $\infty$-categorical sheaves.

The [realizability program](realizability-program.md)'s within-corpus proofs depend critically on the closure 2-monad's lax-idempotence ([closure 2-monad lax-idempotence](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md)) and on $\mathcal{C}$-naturality of the closure operator. Any $(\infty, 1)$-extension would need to preserve enough of this structure to support the realizability characterizations — specifically, the "antecedent monotonicity" lemma (closure preserved under $\leq_k$-orbit advancement) and the "no early closure stability" lemma (chain advancement past every limit below $\kappa$). These are stated 2-categorically; their $(\infty, 1)$-analogues would need higher-coherence formulations.
