## Status

Bibliographic and publisher pages checked at Springer: <https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4612-9839-7>. Cited for standard category-theoretic definitions rather than for a novel claim.

## Annotation

The classical reference for categories, functors, natural transformations, universal constructions, limits, colimits, and equalizers. For the comparison-operations packet, Mac Lane supplies the formal baseline:

- a commutative diagram is an equality of composites with common source and target;
- an equalizer of parallel arrows is the universal arrow into their domain on which the two composites agree;
- comparison maps and naturality squares are categorical equations, not informal analogies.

Predication categories use these notions directly, but the [predication settling category](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-settling-category.md) is a preorder, so ordinary equalizers collapse there. Nontrivial equalization must be stated for operational endomaps on valuations or for richer ambient categories.
