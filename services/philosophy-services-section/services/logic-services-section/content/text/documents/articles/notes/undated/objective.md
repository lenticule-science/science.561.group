## Pure form

An objective is a decidable target. The defining predication: `(objective, has-target, target-description)`. The defining property is decidability: at the deadline, the target is either met or not — there is no ambiguous middle. This distinguishes objective from [goal](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/entity-set/library/entity/library/endeavor/library/goal/library/objective/goal.md) (directional, never strictly settled) and from [task](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/entity-set/library/entity/library/endeavor/library/goal/library/objective/task.md) (goal-directed, but without a required metric or deadline).

## Characteristic predication-shape

- `has-target(objective, target-description)` — what counts as achieved; the individuating predication.
- `serves(objective, goal)` — the goal whose direction this target advances.
- `has-metric(objective, metric)` — the measurement by which progress is evaluated.
- `has-deadline(objective, date)` — the moment at which achievement is assessed.
- `has-status(objective, {open | on-track | achieved | missed | abandoned})` — current lifecycle state.

## SMART invariants

Conformance to the SMART criteria is the structural condition for an objective to function as an actionable target rather than a vague aspiration: specific (target is determinate), measurable (metric exists), achievable (structurally possible), relevant (advances the served goal), time-bounded (deadline is specific).

## Decidability and friction

An objective met at one point may become unmet if conditions change. Achievement is assessed *at the deadline*, not earlier — earlier readings are progress signals, not closure events.
