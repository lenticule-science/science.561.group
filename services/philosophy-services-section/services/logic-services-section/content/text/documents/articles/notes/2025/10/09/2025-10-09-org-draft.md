# Treatise on Stratified Directed ∞-Topos Geometry

## Preliminaries and Model Framework

### Ambient Model

We model ∞-categories by **quasi-categories**, i.e. simplicial sets with the Joyal model structure.

External (co)limits, Kan extensions, and fibrations are computed in this setting.

Internal reasoning occurs within a fixed **cohesive ∞-topos** $\mathfrak{E}$.

Let $(\Sigma \dashv \square \dashv \Pi)$ be the standard cohesive adjoint triple of modalities satisfying:

1.  $\Sigma$ and $\Pi$ are left- and right-exact;
2.  $\square : \mathfrak{E} \to \mathfrak{E}$ is a left-exact reflective modality with unit $\eta : \mathrm{id} \to \square$;
3.  Each modality is definable in the internal language of $\mathfrak{E}$.

All internal ∞-categorical constructions below are interpreted in $\mathfrak{E}$ via its homotopy type theory (HoTT).

### Logical Conventions

- $\|{-}\|$ denotes propositional truncation.
- $\simeq$ denotes homotopy equivalence (internal to $\mathfrak{E}$).
- “Locally constant” and “coherent” are defined with respect to the internal logic of $\mathfrak{E}$.

### Reference Frameworks

## Components

### Stratified Directed Bases

1.  Axiom, Stratified Directed ∞-Site (A1)

    <div class="definition">

    A **stratified directed ∞-site** is a tuple $(\mathcal{X}, S, p, \square)$ where:

    1.  $\mathcal{X}$ is an ∞-category with Grothendieck topology $\mathcal{J}$;
    2.  $S$ is a finite poset (of **strata**);
    3.  $p:\mathcal{X}\to S$ is a **stratification functor**, whose fiber $\mathcal{X}_\sigma:=p^{-1}(\sigma)$ is a full sub-∞-category;
    4.  $\square:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}$ is a left-exact reflective endofunctor with unit $\eta:\mathrm{id}\Rightarrow\square$ satisfying:
        1.  **(Directedness)** $\mathrm{Im}(\square)$ is a wide subcategory stable under pullback and composition, with all non-identity morphisms non-invertible;
        2.  **(Strata Monotonicity)** if $\sigma\le\tau$ in $S$, then $\eta$ restricts $\mathcal{X}_\sigma\to\square(\mathcal{X}_\tau)$ and is $p$-monotone.

    A **morphism** $(\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)\to(\mathcal{Y},T,q,\square')$ is a continuous functor $f:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{Y}$ preserving covers, strata (via a monotone map $S\to T$), directed maps, and equipped with a modal natural isomorphism $f\circ\square\simeq\square'\circ f$.

    </div>

2.  Lemma, Exit ∞-Functor

    <div class="lemma">

    Let $(\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)$ be a stratified directed ∞-site. Define the **exit ∞-category** $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})$: $$
    \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})}(\sigma,\tau)
     := \underset{x\in\mathcal{X}_\sigma,\,y\in\mathcal{X}_\tau}{\mathrm{colim}}
        \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{X}}(x,\square y).
    $$ Then $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty$ extends to a functor $$
    \mathrm{Exit}_\infty:\mathbf{StratDir}_\infty\to\mathbf{Cat}_\infty.
    $$

    </div>

3.  Lemma, Skeletal Filtration

    <div class="lemma">

    If $S$ carries a rank function $\dim:S\to\mathbb{N}$, then $\mathcal{X}$ admits a filtration $$
    \mathrm{Sk}_0\mathcal{X}\subset
    \mathrm{Sk}_1\mathcal{X}\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal{X},
    $$ where $\mathrm{Sk}_n\mathcal{X}:=p^{-1}(\{\sigma\mid\dim(\sigma)\le n\})$.

    </div>

### Part III. Constructible and Coherent Diagrams {#part-iii.-constructible-and-coherent-diagrams}

1.  Definition, Constructible Diagram

    <div class="definition">

    Given $(\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)$ and a stable presentable $\infty$-category $\mathcal{E}$ internal to $\mathfrak{E}$, define $$
    \mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E})
     := \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{loc}}_{\square}
        (\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X}),\mathcal{E}),
    $$ the ∞-category of **locally constant** and $\square$-covariant diagrams.

    </div>

2.  Definition, Coherent Diagram

    <div class="definition">

    A diagram $F\in\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E})$ is **coherent** if for every object $x$ in $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})$ the canonical map $$
    \eta_x : F(x) \to \mathrm{hocolim}\,F
    $$ is an equivalence in $\mathcal{E}$.

    </div>

3.  3.3 Assumption (Exit Cofinality)

    <div class="assumption">

    $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})$ is weakly contractible, for instance if $S$ has a maximum stratum and every $\sigma$ admits a directed morphism $\sigma\to\tau_{\max}$.

    </div>

4.  3.4 Theorem (Coherence Criterion)

    <div class="theorem">

    If $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})$ is weakly contractible and $F$ is locally constant, then for any $x$, $$
    F(x)\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,F.
    $$

    </div>

### Part IV. The ∞-Topos of Coherence {#part-iv.-the--topos-of-coherence}

1.  4.1 Definition (∞-Topos of Coherence)

    <div class="definition">

    Let $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}$ be the full sub-∞-category of $\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E})$ consisting of coherent diagrams. It is an ∞-topos, realized as the left-exact accessible localization $$
    \mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}
     \;\simeq\;
     L_S\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}),
    $$ where $L_S$ inverts the set of canonical maps $\{\eta_x\}$.

    </div>

2.  4.2 Proposition (Stability Under Directed Morphisms)

    <div class="proposition">

    For any directed natural transformation $\Phi:F\to F'$ in $\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}$, $$
    \eta_x\ \text{equivalence for }F
     \;\Rightarrow\;
     \eta_x\ \text{equivalence for }F',
    $$ and $\mathrm{hocolim}\,F\to\mathrm{hocolim}\,F'$ is a modal equivalence.

    </div>

3.  4.3 Definition (Field Object)

    <div class="definition">

    Assuming $\mathcal{E}$ complete, define the **field object** $$
    \mathcal{F}:=\mathrm{holim}_{F\in\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}}F.
    $$

    </div>

4.  4.4 Axiom A2 (Ambidextrous Invariance)

    <div class="axiom">

    If $\mathcal{E}$ is symmetric monoidal stable with dualizable objects of $F$, then $$
    \mathrm{holim}\,\mathcal{F}\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathcal{F},
    $$ expressing modal fixed-point equivalence for $\square$.

    </div>

5.  4.5 Corollaries

    <div class="corollary">

    1.  (Global–Local Equivalence) For coherent $D$, $\mathrm{holim}\,D\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,D$.
    2.  (Stability Under Kan Extension) Coherence is preserved by directed Kan extensions.

    </div>

### Part V. Duality and Ambidexterity {#part-v.-duality-and-ambidexterity}

1.  5.1 Definition (Dual Diagram)

    <div class="definition">

    Let $\mathcal{E}$ be symmetric monoidal stable. For $F:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathcal{E}$ with pointwise dualizable values, define $$
    F^{\vee}:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal{E},
    \quad
    F^{\vee}(x):=\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(F(x),1).
    $$

    </div>

2.  5.2 Theorem (Verdier Ambidexterity)

    <div class="theorem">

    If $\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})$ is finite and $F$ pointwise dualizable, then $$
    \mathrm{hocolim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{holim}\,F^{\vee}.
    $$ **Proof.** By coend–end duality in a symmetric monoidal stable category and the adjunction $$
    \mathrm{Map}(1,\mathrm{holim}\,F^{\vee})
     \simeq
     \mathrm{Map}(\mathrm{hocolim}\,F,1).
    $$

    </div>

3.  5.3 Corollaries

    <div class="corollary">

    1.  $F$ coherent ⇔ $F^{\vee}$ coherent;
    2.  Dualization induces a contravariant equivalence

    $$
    (-)^{\vee}:\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}.
    $$

    </div>

### Part VI. Dynamics (Kan Extensions and Fibrations) {#part-vi.-dynamics-kan-extensions-and-fibrations}

1.  6.1 Definition (Homotopy Kan Extensions)

    <div class="definition">

    Given $\Phi:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{Y})$ and $F:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathcal{E}$, define $\mathrm{LKan}_\Phi F$ and $\mathrm{RKan}_\Phi F$ by the standard universal properties.

    </div>

2.  6.2 Lemma (Stability Under Cofinal Maps)

    <div class="lemma">

    If $\Phi$ is cofinal (resp. coinitial), then $$
    \mathrm{hocolim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathrm{LKan}_\Phi F
    \quad
    (\text{resp. }\mathrm{holim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{holim}\,\mathrm{RKan}_\Phi F).
    $$ Hence coherence is preserved under such $\Phi$.

    </div>

3.  6.3 Definition (Condition Fibration)

    <div class="definition">

    Define the **condition fibration** $$
    \mathrm{Cond}:\mathfrak{E}_{(-)}\to\mathbf{StratDir}_\infty
    $$ as the Grothendieck construction of the pseudofunctor $\mathcal{X}\mapsto\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}$ with reindexing $f_!:={\mathrm{LKan}}_f$.

    </div>

4.  6.4 Theorem (Dynamic Invariance of $\mathcal{F}$)

    <div class="theorem">

    Under A2 and 6.2, $\mathrm{holim}\,\mathcal{F}\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathcal{F}$ is stable up to modal equivalence for all directed $\Phi$.

    </div>
