## Annotation

Foundational paper on tripos theory. Introduces the framework that generalizes Higgs's $\Omega$-valued sets and gives the tripos-to-topos construction as a unified account of how various toposes (sheaf, realizability, Boolean-valued) arise.

For our program: cited primarily for historical attribution of the tripos framework; technical content used through Pitts 2002. The predication hyperdoctrine differs from a tripos in not having a generic predicate and being bilattice-valued rather than Heyting-valued.
