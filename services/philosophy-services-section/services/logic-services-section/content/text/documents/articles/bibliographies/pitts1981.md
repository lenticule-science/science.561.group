## Annotation

The original development of triposes — *topos-representing indexed posets* — as a structure that captures higher-order logic over a base via a fibration of posets with sufficient structure to construct an associated topos. For the program, the relevant point of contact is negative: the [predication hyperdoctrine](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) is *not* a tripos because the bilattice fibers are not Heyting/Boolean and the Heyting-poset fiber requirement of triposes fails — see [not a tripos](../notes/undated/not-a-tripos.md).
