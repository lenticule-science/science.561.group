## Annotation

Informal but comprehensive guide to 2-categories and bicategories. Used as the background reference for the hyperdoctrinal-layer's 2-categorical preliminaries: limits and colimits, 2-dimensional universal algebra, formal category theory, nerves of bicategories, 2-monads and their algebras.

Pulled when a definition or basic property in the hyperdoctrinal layer needs a citation that isn't already covered by Kelly–Lack 1997 (which is more specific to property-like 2-monads).
