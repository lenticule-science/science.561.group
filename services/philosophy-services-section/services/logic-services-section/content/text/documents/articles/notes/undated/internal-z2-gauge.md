# Internal Z/2 gauge

The **internal gauge** of [$\mathbf{FOUR}$](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/four-bilattice.md) is the $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ by [negation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/concepts/negation.md) $\sim$: the non-trivial element of $\mathbb{Z}/2$ acts as $\sim$, the identity element acts as $\mathrm{id}$.

The gauge action:

- preserves the [knowledge order](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/concepts/knowledge-order.md) $\leq_k$;
- reverses the [truth order](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/truth-order/truth-order.md) $\leq_t$;
- fixes the two "symmetric" values $\bot, \top$ while swapping $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$.

The [paired Priestley dual](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/paired-priestley-space.md) of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ inherits the corresponding $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action: the swap involution exchanging the two components $\{+, -\}$ of the paired space realizes the $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action dually under [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/references/jung-rivieccio-duality.md).
