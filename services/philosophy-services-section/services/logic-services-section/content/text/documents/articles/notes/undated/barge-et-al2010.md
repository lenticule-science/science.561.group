## Full citation

M. Barge, B. Diamond, J. Hunton, L. Sadun. "Cohomology of substitution tiling spaces." *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 30, 1607–1627, 2010. arXiv:0811.2507.

## Status

Standard reference for Čech cohomology of substitution tiling spaces, including the constant-length / non-Pisot cases. Cited at training-knowledge level for the established results on Thue-Morse and other constant-length substitutions.

## Annotation

Develops the cohomology of substitution tiling spaces beyond the original [@anderson-putnam1998] framework, with explicit computation in cases including non-primitive and constant-length substitutions. Provides the standard $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]$ result for Thue-Morse cohomology, reflecting the dyadic structure of the constant-length-2 substitution.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Cited from [benchmark 2 (Thue-Morse)](examples-benchmark-2-thue-morse.md) as the literature reference confirming $\check{H}^1(\Omega_{\mathrm{TM}}) \cong \mathbb{Z}[1/2]$ — the result the corpus's framework recovers (after correcting an earlier corpus claim of $\mathbb{Z}^2$). Also adjacent to the constant-length case of the [singular substitution variety](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/questions/singular-substitution-variety.md) question.
