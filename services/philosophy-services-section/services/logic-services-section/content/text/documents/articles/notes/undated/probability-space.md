A **probability space** is a triple (Ω, 𝓕, P) where (Ω, 𝓕) is a measurable space and P: 𝓕 → [0, 1] is a *probability measure* — a measure with P(Ω) = 1. Specializes [measure-space](measure-space.md) by constraining the total mass to 1. Events are elements of 𝓕; the probability of event A is P(A). For events A, B with P(B) > 0, the *conditional probability* of A given B is P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B); this defines a new probability measure P(·|B) on 𝓕.
