## Full citation

V. Berthé, W. Steiner, J. M. Thuswaldner. "Multidimensional continued fractions and symbolic codings of toral translations," in *Substitution and Tiling Dynamics: Introduction to Self-inducing Structures*, Springer Lecture Notes in Mathematics 2273, 2020. HAL hal-01070551.

## Status

Comprehensive treatment of S-adic shifts, multidimensional continued fractions, and the geometric / arithmetic / dynamical content of S-adic systems. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Connects S-adic shifts to multidimensional continued fraction algorithms; develops the conjectural Pisot S-adic theorem (S-adic shifts with Pisot incidence matrices are measurably conjugate to torus rotations). The "geometry, dynamics, and arithmetic of S-adic shifts" framework.
