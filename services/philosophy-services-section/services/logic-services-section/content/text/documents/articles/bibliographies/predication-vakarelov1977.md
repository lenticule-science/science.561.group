## Full citation

## Status

Not directly accessed; covered through [@odintsov2008] (Ch. 8). Cited for the propositional twist-product representation of N4-lattices.

## Annotation

Foundational paper representing N4-lattices — Nelson's paraconsistent extension of intuitionistic logic — as twist products of Heyting algebras by themselves, with strong negation realized as the coordinate-swap involution. The construction is the propositional precursor to the bilattice-valued indexed setting: the N4-lattice itself is the value algebra, and the indexed-categorical promotion to a fibration with quantifier adjoints in both orders is the structural lift the [predication hyperdoctrine](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) carries out.

Cited in [equality is derived, not postulated](../notes/undated/equality-derived-not-postulated.md) as the propositional precursor to the indexed-Nelson story, alongside [@odintsov2008]'s textbook treatment.
