## Status

Standard reference in topological data analysis; not held as PDF. Used as a structural template for the bottleneck-distance metric on persistence-diagram spaces.

## Annotation

Foundational paper on the stability of persistence diagrams under perturbation: the bottleneck distance between persistence diagrams is bounded by the $L_\infty$-distance between the underlying functions / filtrations. This is the structural foundation of topological data analysis as a *quantitative* framework — persistence diagrams are not just topological invariants but *Lipschitz-continuous* invariants with respect to the input data.

For the corpus's [typed-barcoding question](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/questions/typed-barcoding-of-settling-cohomology.md): persistence diagrams are the natural target of the corpus's classifying invariants when re-presented over the ordinal filtration. The Cohen-Steiner–Edelsbrunner–Harer stability theorem is the established analog the corpus's deformation-theoretic stability would parallel.

For the corpus's [failure-event moduli](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/questions/failure-event-moduli-stack.md): the persistence-diagram space is the closest established precedent for a "moduli of typed-barcode-like objects" — generalized in the corpus to typed firing-events on Grothendieck sites.
