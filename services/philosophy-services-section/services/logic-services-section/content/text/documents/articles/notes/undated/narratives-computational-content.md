# Computational content of knowledge presheaves

For finite parameters $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}, E, \mathcal{C}$, this framework reduces to concrete polynomial-time algorithms on small data structures.

**Decision procedures.** Membership $v \in A_p$ is array access, $O(|E|^2)$. A single closure firing $\mathrm{cl}_p(v)$ is $O(|\mathrm{cl}\text{-rules}| \cdot |E|^2)$ for typical logic-programming-style closures; the settling-convergence cost is bounded above by the finite-chain height $O(4^{|E|^2})$ in the worst case, but the per-cell two-step bound on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ ([four-bilattice residuation convergence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/four-bilattice/references/four-bilattice-residuation-convergence.md)) gives much tighter convergence in practice. The [modal necessity](modal-necessity-operator.md) and [possibility](modal-possibility-operator.md) operators aggregate over worlds at $O(|W|)$ per cell. Local modal consequence is PSPACE; global consequence is EXPTIME ([modal complexity bounds](modal-complexity-bounds.md)).

**Enumeration-based verification.** The [four-element bilattice](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/four-bilattice.md) $\mathbf{FOUR}$ has only four elements, so any algebraic identity in $\mathbf{FOUR}^\bowtie$ reduces to a finite case-enumeration of at most $4^n$ cases for $n$ variables. For $n \leq 4$ — the typical identity — that is at most 256 cases, tractable by hand or by a short script. The [Nelson identity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/references/nelson-identity.md) (16 cases), the [contrapositive failure](contrapositive-failure.md) counterexamples (16 cases each direction), the $\odot$-properties on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ (16 cases for monoid laws, 64 for residuation; see [four-bilattice residuation properties](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/four-bilattice/references/four-bilattice-residuation-properties.md)), and the residuation adjointness on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ (64 triples) all submit to this method.

**Effective representation.** A finite-base knowledge presheaf with convergence ordinal $|\tau^*| = n$ admits a compact array-based representation: the entity set as a finite list; the predicate category as an object list plus generator-morphism specifications (sub-support inclusions for specialization, and the corresponding cellwise actions for converse and projections); valuations as a four-dimensional array indexed by $(p, s, o, \tau) \in \mathcal{C} \times E \times E \times [n]$ with two bits per cell (the four states of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ encoded as $\langle a_+, a_- \rangle$); and the [closure operator](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/morphisms/closure-operator.md) as a list of rewrite rules. Total space: $O(|\mathcal{C}| \cdot |E|^2 \cdot n)$ for the valuation array, with polynomial overhead for the category and closure data.

**Distributed implementation.** The merge in a [distributed settling system](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/distributed-settling-system.md) is the simplest possible CRDT update: cellwise $\vee_k$ on the two-bit representation,

$$\langle a_+, a_- \rangle \vee_k \langle b_+, b_- \rangle = \langle a_+ \vee b_+,\ a_- \vee b_- \rangle$$

— two bitwise ORs per cell. Per-cell cost is $O(1)$; per full-state merge is $O(|\mathcal{C}| \cdot |E|^2)$. The [CRDT convergence theorem](../../../../glossary/concepts/crdt-convergence.md) guarantees correctness without consensus.

**Reference implementation.** A complete reference implementation is small in scope — perhaps a thousand lines in a modern language — with data structures for entities, predicates, and fiber algebras; closure-operator compilation from rewrite-rule specifications; settling execution with fixed-point detection; merge and delta-state representation; and query evaluation for modal satisfaction. Correctness reduces to the formal theorems already proved.

**Continuous values.** For $\mathbf{L} \neq \mathbf{2}$ — for example $\mathbf{L} = [0, 1]$ with MV-structure — values are continuous and the finite-enumeration arguments do not directly apply. The structural theorems still hold, but numerical stability and termination become application-specific concerns. See [continuous-$\mathbf{L}$ knowledge presheaves](continuous-l-knowledge-presheaves.md).
