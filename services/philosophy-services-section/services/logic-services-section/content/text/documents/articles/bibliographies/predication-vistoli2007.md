## Full citation

A. Vistoli. *Notes on Grothendieck topologies, fibered categories and descent theory*. arXiv:math/0412512v4, 24 May 2007. 114 pp.

## Status

Read in part; PDF at `sources/vistoli2007.pdf`. §§3.1–3.4 and §§4.1.1–4.1.3 read in Phase 2 of the moduli paper.

## Annotation

The definitive modern reference for fibered-category descent. Used as the central technical reference for the moduli layer (descent and the stack property).

**Core definitions used:**
- **Definition 3.1** cartesian arrow.
- **Definition 3.5** fibered category.
- **Proposition 3.11** equivalence between fibered categories over $\mathcal{C}$ and pseudo-functors $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Cat}$.
- **Definition 3.21** categories fibered in groupoids — what moduli points naturally form.
- **Definition 4.6** prestack and stack: $F(U) \to F(\{U_i \to U\})$ fully faithful (prestack) / equivalence (stack).
- **Proposition 4.7** reduces the prestack property to Hom-functors being sheaves.
- **Definition 4.8** effective descent.

What's **not** in Vistoli: 2-stacks. The moduli-layer's 2-stack content draws from Breen 1994 and Hollander 2008 instead.
