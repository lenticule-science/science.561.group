Alfredo Maria Bonanno (4 March 1937 – 6 December 2023) was an Italian [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) and prominent theorist of insurrectionary [anarchism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md). He was imprisoned multiple times for his political activities and was an editor of Anarchismo Editions, producing texts in Italian that have been translated and circulated internationally across [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) networks.

## Significance for This Research

Bonanno's contribution to the [anti-work](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/dev/terms/anti-work.md) tradition is his insistence on *informal organization* — resistance that does not crystallize into party structures, unions, or formal organizations that can be captured, co-opted, or bureaucratized. For Bonanno, the revolutionary act is immediate and relational: it happens between specific people in specific situations, not between abstract classes and historical forces.

This emphasis on immediacy and informality connects to the [relational framework](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/relationality/index.md): resistance is itself a relational act, constituted through the specific encounters between people and the conditions they resist, rather than through adherence to a pre-given program or [ideology](../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ideology.md).

Bonanno's analysis of work as a structure of domination — a fundamental restructuring of one's relationship to time, land, and other people, beyond the Marxist category of exploitation — is foundational to the [anti-work](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/dev/terms/anti-work.md) research here. His Italian autonomist roots, his engagement with the *Refusal of Work* tradition, and his critique of leftist organizational forms that reproduce the hierarchies they claim to oppose all inform emsenn's analysis of the colonial dimensions of waged [labor](../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md).

## Key Texts

Bonanno's most circulated texts in English translation include *Armed Joy* (1977), written in response to what he saw as the left's fetishization of the party and the program, and *The Anarchist Tension* (1996), a short lecture on why [anarchism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/alfredo-bonanno/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) is uncomfortable to all sides.
