## Full citation

J. Lurie. *Higher Topos Theory.* Princeton University Press, Annals of Mathematics Studies vol. 170, 2009.

## Status

Standard reference for $(\infty, 1)$-category theory and higher topos theory. Cited at training-knowledge level for the framework's existence and its standard results on filtered colimits and $\infty$-stacks; specific corpus theorems do not currently depend on it.

## Annotation

Foundational treatment of $(\infty, 1)$-categories via the quasi-category model: Joyal's model structure, presentable / accessible $\infty$-categories, $\infty$-topoi, and the basic theory of filtered colimits and limits in the $\infty$-categorical setting.

**Relevance to the corpus.** The future-direction narrative on $(\infty, 1)$-extensions cites this for:

- **Filtered colimits in $(\infty, 1)$-categories** (§5.3) — the natural setting for predication categories' transfinite-CW direct-limit constructions and the [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md) theorem.
- **$\infty$-stacks** — the natural lift target of predication categories' [settling moduli stack](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md) and [meta-moduli stack](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/meta-moduli-stack-theorem.md), with strict-vs-lax 2-cell coherence handled automatically.
