Serafinski is the pseudonym of the anonymous author of *Blessed is the Flame: An Introduction to Concentration Camp Resistance and Anarcho-Nihilism* (2016). The text examines acts of resistance within Nazi concentration camps and uses them to develop an anarcho-nihilist politics — one that does not depend on the possibility of victory or a better future to justify action.

## Core ideas

- **Anarcho-[nihilism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/serafinski/docs/philosophy/disciplines/nihilism/index.md)**: a political orientation that acts against domination without requiring hope, progress, or the expectation of success. Resistance is justified by the conditions it responds to, not by its prospects.
- **Concentration camp resistance**: Serafinski documents sabotage, revolt, and [refusal](../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/services/strategy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) within the camps as political acts that cannot be understood through frameworks that measure action by its outcomes.
