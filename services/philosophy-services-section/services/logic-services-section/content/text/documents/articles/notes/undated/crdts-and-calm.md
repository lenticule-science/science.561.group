# predication settlings as a structurally enriched CRDT class

The [CRDT convergence theorem](../../../../glossary/concepts/crdt-convergence.md) establishes that a [distributed settling system](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/distributed-settling-system.md) forms a CRDT class in the sense of [@shapiro-et-al2011]. The CALM theorem ([@hellerstein-alvaro2020] — that monotone computations are coordination-free — applies directly via the [predication settling CALM corollary](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/formalization/infomaterial/lib/infomaterial/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-calm-corollary.md).

This places predication settlings in the distributed-systems literature as a *structurally enriched* CRDT: more algebraic than a plain join-semilattice, but retaining the same convergence guarantees. The bilattice values give a richer state space than a simple semilattice; the settling structure gives a notion of inferential propagation absent from generic CRDTs; the predicate-category structure gives a naturality condition on the morphisms between fibers. Yet the CRDT machinery operates on the underlying $\leq_k$-semilattice, so the convergence theorem of Shapiro et al. lifts unchanged via the [predication settling merge semilattice](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-merge-semilattice.md) result.

Related lattice-theoretic distributed-programming models — Kuper and Newton's LVars (2013, 2014) and Conway, Marczak, Alvaro, Hellerstein, and Maier's Bloom$^L$ (2012) — apply the same monotonicity principle at the language level. predication settlings provide the corresponding bilattice-valued model-theoretic substrate: any distributed system whose state can be modeled as a predication settling inherits eventual consistency without coordination. This includes distributed knowledge-graph stores, multi-agent belief systems, and decentralized reasoning systems where inference rules — encoded in the [closure operator](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/morphisms/closure-operator.md) — can be applied locally without cross-replica synchronization.

The delta-state CRDTs of [@almeida-et-al2018] specialize naturally: practical predication settling implementations ship the individual writes $u \in U$ rather than full states, with merge invariant under representation choice.
