## Annotation

Establishes **pairwise Stone spaces** as bitopological Priestley duals.

**Theorem (2.9, 3.4).** The categories are isomorphic:

$$\mathbf{PStone} \cong \mathbf{Spec} \cong \mathbf{Pries}$$

— pairwise Stone spaces, spectral spaces, and Priestley spaces are equivalent. Bitopological duality extends to Heyting / co-Heyting / bi-Heyting algebras (§4 of the paper).

For our program: the bitopological framing is what the cohomological-layer's site theory adopts. The two topologies at each fiber come from the paired Priestley structure of the foundational layer (lifted to the hyperdoctrinal layer) — $\tau_+$ tracks positive evidence, $\tau_-$ tracks negative evidence — and the pairwise-Stone framework of this paper is what makes them compatible with sheaf-theoretic moves.

The cohomological layer cites for the bitopological / pairwise-Stone framing of the site $(\mathbf{T}^*, J)$.
