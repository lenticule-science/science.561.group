The originating predicate of the predicate category $\mathbf{C}$. $p^* :=$ has-no-relation is the predicate of the cell $\phi^* = (X, p^*, R)$ — the cell that articulates *relationlessness* as the relation it is. Every other predicate in $\mathbf{C}$ is reachable from $p^*$ by iterated application of the four canonical morphism classes: [predicate-specialization](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-specialization.md), [predicate-converse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md), [predicate-subject-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [predicate-object-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-object-projection.md).
