# Synonymy criterion

Let $v, v'$ be [predication settlings](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-settling.md) over a common base $B = (\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda)$, and let $\mathrm{reg}(v) = \mathrm{reg}(v')$ be their common [regime label](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md) (invariant under [synonymy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/mutually-locally-derivable/content/synonymy/synonymy.md) by [regime-MLD-invariance](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/regime-mld-invariance.md)). Then $v \sim_{\mathrm{syn}} v'$ iff the regime-specific condition holds:

| Regime | $v \sim_{\mathrm{syn}} v'$ iff |
|---|---|
| Strict | there exists a stage-by-stage bijection of designated-cell sets, up to ordinal shift |
| Scott-continuous | same condition; bijection at every stage up to ordinal shift |
| Transfinite-bounded | $H^1$-classes agree: $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) = \mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v')$, where $H^1$ has one $\mathbb{Z}$-generator per non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal |
| Aperiodic | $H^1$-classes agree: $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) = \mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v')$ in $H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \omega_1}, \mathcal{F}_U^{\mathrm{univ}})$ |

## Proof

This is a direct corollary of [biequivalence-cohomological-collapse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/biequivalence-cohomological-collapse.md), reframed in terms of the [synonymy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/mutually-locally-derivable/content/synonymy/synonymy.md) equivalence relation $\sim_{\mathrm{syn}} = \sim_{\mathrm{MLD}} = \sim_H$.

**Strict and Scott-continuous.** $H^1(\mathcal{F}_U) = 0$ for the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md) by [settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md). Cohomological equivalence therefore reduces to sheaf isomorphism without $H^1$ data, which is precisely a bijective matching of the designated-cell evolution stage-by-stage. By biequivalence-cohomological-collapse, this matches the MLD condition. $\square$

**Transfinite-bounded.** By [transfinite-bounded-regime $H^1$-generators](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md), the $H^1$ of the settling-associated sheaf has one $\mathbb{Z}$-generator per non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal. Two settlings have isomorphic sheaves iff their non-Scott-continuous firing-ordinal sets agree up to settling-shift, iff their $H^1$-classes agree. By biequivalence-cohomological-collapse, sheaf-iso implies MLD and vice versa on this regime. $\square$

**Aperiodic.** By [aperiodic-regime classification](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-classification.md), $H^1$-class agreement in $H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \omega_1}, \mathcal{F}_U^{\mathrm{univ}})$ is equivalent to MLD. Sheaf-iso of $\mathcal{F}_U$ implies $H^1$-class agreement by functoriality; the converse is the substantive direction of the aperiodic classification proof. By biequivalence-cohomological-collapse, this completes the equivalence. $\square$

## Interpretation

The synonymy criterion makes precise in which sense the theory's four regimes correspond to four different "languages" for the same underlying structure. In the strict and Scott-continuous regimes, synonymy is entirely combinatorial — two settlings express the same thing iff they have the same pattern of designated cells, possibly offset in time. In the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes, synonymy has genuine cohomological content: the $H^1$-class is the complete invariant, and settlings that share it encode the same structure in the same formal sense as synonymous words in natural language — same inferential role, possibly different surface derivation.

The aperiodic regime is where cohomology is alive: $H^1 \neq 0$ generically, so cohomological synonymy is strictly more discriminating than in the lower regimes. This is not a failure of synonymy in the aperiodic regime but the full realization of what synonymy requires: genuine cohomological agreement.
