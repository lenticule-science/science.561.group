# Tangent dimension on the settling moduli is constructible but non-semicontinuous

The [SKH deformation Ext-classification theorem](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md) (proved) gives the tangent space at a settling $v$ as $\mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v)$, with the [vanishing-above-degree-1 theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) (proved) giving unobstructed deformation theory. The [regime-stratification non-semicontinuity theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/regime-stratification-non-semicontinuity.md) (proved) shows that $\dim H^1$ is not semicontinuous in either direction along $\leq_k$-monotone chains of MLD-classes.

These three theorems combine to give a structurally distinctive feature of the settling moduli stack $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$ ([settling-moduli-stack theorem](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md), proved): the tangent-dimension function is **locally constant on each regime stratum** (constructible) but **jumps discontinuously in both directions across regime boundaries** (non-semicontinuous). The deformation theory is unobstructed at every settling, so each regime stratum is formally smooth; but the moduli as a whole is not semicontinuously smooth — its tangent dimension does not vary monotonically along $\leq_k$-deformation paths.

## Statement

**Theorem (tangent-dimension constructibility, non-semicontinuity).** *Let $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$ be the [settling moduli stack](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md). Define the tangent-dimension function*

$$T : \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}} \to \mathrm{Card}, \qquad T(v) := \dim_\mathbb{Z} \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v,\, \mathcal{F}_v).$$

*Then:*

(a) *(Per-regime values.) For each settling $v$ with closure ordinal $\kappa(v)$:*

$$T(v) = \begin{cases}
0 & v \in \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}_{\mathrm{strict}} \quad (\kappa(v) < \omega) \\
0 & v \in \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}_{\mathrm{Scott\text{-}continuous}} \quad (\kappa(v) = \omega) \\
|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))| & v \in \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}_{\mathrm{transfinite\text{-}bounded}} \quad (\omega < \kappa(v) < \omega_1) \\
|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))| & v \in \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}_{\mathrm{aperiodic}} \quad (\kappa(v) \geq \omega_1)
\end{cases}$$

*where $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa)$ is the set of [non-Scott-continuous firing ordinals](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-continuity/content/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal.md) below $\kappa$. The strict and Scott-continuous values follow from $T(v) = \dim H^1(\mathcal{F}_v) = 0$ on those regimes (per [strict-regime cohomology vanishing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md) and [Scott-continuous-regime cohomology vanishing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/scott-continuous-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md), both proved); the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic values follow from the [transfinite-bounded-regime $H^1$-generators theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md) (proved) and [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md) (proved). The identification $T = \dim H^1$ on the corpus's standard sheaves is established in Part (a)' below.*

(a') *(Ext-via-cohomology identification.) For the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_U$, the structural identification*

$$\mathcal{H}om(\mathcal{F}_U,\, \mathcal{F}_U) \;\cong\; \mathcal{F}_U$$

*holds at each fiber $(q_0, \tau_0)$ of the bitopological settling site, by the following construction-internal fact: $\mathcal{F}_U|_{(q_0, \tau_0)} = \bigoplus_{x \in \Delta^{+,*}_{\tau_0}(q_0)} \mathbb{Z}_x$ is a direct sum of skyscraper sheaves at distinct $+$-points on the paired Priestley space $X_{q_0}^{\tau_0}$ (per the [settling-associated sheaf construction](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md), citing [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/references/jung-rivieccio-duality.md) for the cell-to-point correspondence and [vanishing-above-degree-1](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) Step 1 for the skyscraper-direct-sum form). For skyscrapers at distinct points $x \neq y$, $\mathcal{H}om(\mathbb{Z}_x, \mathbb{Z}_y) = 0$ (pointwise-disjoint supports give zero local Homs); for $x = y$, $\mathcal{H}om(\mathbb{Z}_x, \mathbb{Z}_x) = \mathbb{Z}_x$. Hence $\mathcal{H}om(\bigoplus_x \mathbb{Z}_x, \bigoplus_y \mathbb{Z}_y) = \bigoplus_x \mathbb{Z}_x$, equal to $\mathcal{F}_U$ at the same fiber.*

*Applying the standard Ext-via-cohomology identification $\mathrm{Ext}^n(F, F) = H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{H}om(F, F))$ (which holds in the bitopological-site Q-enriched sheaf category by the closed-monoidal structure per [closed monoidal structure on Q-enriched sheaves theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/content/quantale-enriched-sheaf/references/q-enriched-sheaf-closed-monoidal-structure.md), proved):*

$$\mathrm{Ext}^n(\mathcal{F}_U,\, \mathcal{F}_U) \;=\; H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{H}om(\mathcal{F}_U, \mathcal{F}_U)) \;\cong\; H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_U).$$

*This justifies the per-regime values in Part (a) as the regime cohomology theorems' $H^1$ values, applied to $\mathrm{Ext}^1$ via this identification. The identification's structural ingredient — that the settling-associated sheaf's fiber is a sum of skyscrapers at distinct cells — is corpus-internal to the settling-associated sheaf's construction.*

(b) *(Stratification with $T$ constant on each substratum.) The function $T$ descends to the [settling moduli stack](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md) (i.e., $T$ is [MLD](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/teaching/explanations/mutually-locally-derivable.md)-invariant), and $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$ admits a partition by regime + firing-cardinality on which $T$ is constant. Specifically:*

- *Strict stratum: $T \equiv 0$ (single substratum).*
- *Scott-continuous stratum: $T \equiv 0$ (single substratum).*
- *Transfinite-bounded stratum: $T(v) = |L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$, taking values in countable cardinals; partitioned into substrata $\{v : |L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))| = \aleph\}$ for $\aleph$ ranging over countable cardinals, with $T \equiv \aleph$ on each.*
- *Aperiodic stratum: $T(v) = |L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$, taking values $\geq \aleph_1$; partitioned into substrata indexed by $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa)|$ values.*

*MLD-invariance of $T$: on the transfinite-bounded stratum, the [transfinite-bounded-regime moduli classification](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/transfinite-bounded-regime-moduli-classification.md) (proved) establishes that MLD-equivalence is equivalent to agreement of $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-sets up to settling-shift, which preserves $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$. On the aperiodic stratum, the [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md)'s "Combinatorial restatement of the classification" section gives the same statement. Hence $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$ is MLD-invariant on both strata, and $T(v) = 0$ on the strict and Scott-continuous strata is trivially MLD-invariant. Since $T(v)$ is determined by regime (MLD-invariant: settling-shift and bilattice/entity automorphisms preserve the closure-ordinal-classifying invariants) and $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$ alone, $T$ is MLD-invariant.*

(c) *(Non-semicontinuity at boundaries.) There exist $\leq_k$-monotone chains $v_B \leq_k v_C \leq_k v_{\mathrm{pre}}$ of distinct MLD-classes with $T(v_B) = 0$, $T(v_C) = 1$, $T(v_{\mathrm{pre}}) = 0$ (per the [regime-stratification non-semicontinuity theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/regime-stratification-non-semicontinuity.md), Section "Settling analysis"). Consequently, no partial order on MLD-classes extending $\leq_k$ admits $T$ as a semicontinuous function in either direction.*

(d) *(Unobstructed at each point but non-globally-smooth.) Define the **formal-deformation set** at $v \in \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$ as*

$$\mathrm{FD}(v) \;:=\; \varprojlim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \, \mathrm{Def}_n(v) \big/ \!\sim_{\text{gauge}},$$

*where $\mathrm{Def}_n(v)$ is the set of $n$-th order deformations of $v$ (deformations over $\mathbb{Z}[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{n+1})$ in the corpus's settling-coalgebra category, per [SKH deformation Ext-classification](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md) §"First-order deformations as Ext-classes" extended to higher orders), and $\sim_{\text{gauge}}$ identifies deformations differing by an automorphism of $\mathcal{F}_v$ trivial modulo $\epsilon$.*

*By Part (a')'s identification, $\mathrm{Ext}^{\geq 2}(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) \cong H^{\geq 2}(\mathcal{F}_v) = 0$ (the latter by [vanishing-above-degree-1](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md)). Per the [SKH deformation Ext-classification theorem](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md)'s "Obstructions in $\mathrm{Ext}^2$" + "Vanishing of obstructions" sections, every $n$-th order deformation extends to an $(n{+}1)$-st order deformation, with the lift unique up to gauge (gauge-uniqueness follows from the $\mathrm{Ext}^2 = 0$-driven obstruction-class triviality plus the Ext-classification's bijection between first-order classes and $\mathrm{Ext}^1$, applied to each lifting step). The inverse limit over $n$ is therefore non-empty at every $v$, with $\mathrm{Def}_1(v)/\!\sim_{\text{gauge}}\, \cong\, \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v)$ as the first-order layer and each subsequent layer adding no new gauge-classes. Hence $\mathrm{FD}(v) \cong \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v)$ as $\mathbb{Z}$-modules, and*

$$\dim_\mathbb{Z} \mathrm{FD}(v) \;=\; \dim_\mathbb{Z} \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) \;=\; T(v).$$

*This gives a local formal-smoothness property at each moduli point: the formal-deformation set is a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module of dimension $T(v)$. Globally, however, $T$ jumps non-monotonically across regime-boundaries (Part c) — no $\leq_k$-extending partial order on MLD-classes makes $T$ semicontinuous in either direction, so the moduli's tangent dimension is not a semicontinuous function of the deformation order.*

## Proof sketch

The four parts assemble from existing corpus theorems plus the structural identification in Part (a').

**Part (a):** the per-regime $H^1(\mathcal{F}_v)$ values are proved corpus theorems ([strict-regime cohomology vanishing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md), [Scott-continuous-regime cohomology vanishing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/scott-continuous-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md), [transfinite-bounded-regime $H^1$-generators](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md), [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md)). Part (a')'s identification $\mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) \cong H^1(\mathcal{F}_v)$ then transports these values to $T = \dim \mathrm{Ext}^1$. ✓

**Part (a'):** see the statement section above. The structural fact $\mathcal{H}om(\mathcal{F}_U, \mathcal{F}_U) \cong \mathcal{F}_U$ at each fiber follows from the settling-associated sheaf's construction (direct sum of skyscrapers at distinct $+$-points on the paired Priestley space) plus the standard $\mathcal{H}om$-of-skyscrapers identity. The Ext-via-cohomology identification then follows from the closed-monoidal structure theorem. ✓

**Part (b):** MLD-invariance of $T$ has the following per-stratum decomposition:

- *Strict and Scott-continuous strata.* $T \equiv 0$ on these strata (Part a), so MLD-invariance is trivial.
- *Transfinite-bounded stratum.* The [transfinite-bounded-regime moduli classification](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/transfinite-bounded-regime-moduli-classification.md) (proved) establishes MLD-equivalence ⟺ agreement of $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-sets up to settling-shift, which preserves $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$.
- *Aperiodic stratum.* The [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md)'s "Combinatorial restatement of the classification" section establishes the same MLD ⟺ firing-ordinal-set-agreement criterion.

Combined with regime-MLD-invariance (settling-shift, bilattice automorphism, and entity-permutation — the three sources of MLD per [mutually-locally-derivable](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/teaching/explanations/mutually-locally-derivable.md) — all preserve the regime-classifying closure-ordinal data), $T$ is MLD-invariant uniformly. The substratification then partitions strict / Scott-continuous strata as single substrata ($T \equiv 0$) and transfinite-bounded / aperiodic strata into substrata indexed by $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa(v))|$ values, with $T$ constant on each. ✓

**Part (c):** The [regime-stratification non-semicontinuity theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/regime-stratification-non-semicontinuity.md) constructs explicit $\leq_k$-monotone chains $v_B \leq_k v_C \leq_k v_{\mathrm{pre}}$ in distinct MLD-classes with $\dim H^1$ values $0, 1, 0$. Since $T(v) = \dim_\mathbb{Z} \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) = \dim H^1$ on the standard sheaf (Ext-via-cohomology identification, Part (a)), $T$ takes the same $0, 1, 0$ pattern. This refutes both upper- and lower-semicontinuity of $T$ along $\leq_k$-extending partial orders. ✓

**Part (d):** Vanishing-above-degree-1 gives $\mathrm{Ext}^{\geq 2}(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) = 0$ at every settling $v$. By the Ext-classification's "Vanishing of obstructions" section, every $n$-th order deformation extends to an $(n{+}1)$-st order deformation with lift unique up to gauge. The inverse limit $\mathrm{FD}(v) = \varprojlim_n \mathrm{Def}_n(v)/{\sim_{\text{gauge}}}$ is therefore well-defined; the first-order layer $\mathrm{Def}_1(v)/{\sim_{\text{gauge}}}$ is $\mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v)$ (Ext-classification, "First-order deformations as Ext-classes" section); subsequent layers add no new gauge-classes (Ext^2 = 0). Hence $\mathrm{FD}(v) \cong \mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v)$ as $\mathbb{Z}$-modules, with $\mathbb{Z}$-rank $T(v)$. The $0, 1, 0$ chain of Part (c) refutes both upper- and lower-semicontinuity of $T$. $\square$

## Structural cause

The constructible-but-non-semicontinuous shape of $T$ on $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$ is the joint consequence of two corpus-internal features:

1. **Regime stratification's non-semicontinuity.** $\leq_k$-enhancement (adding information to a settling) interacts with closure rules in two competing ways: antecedent-satisfaction can introduce new non-Scott-continuous firings (raising $T$); consequent-preemption can pre-empt non-Scott-continuous firings (lowering $T$). Neither dominates universally — see the [regime-stratification non-semicontinuity theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/regime-stratification-non-semicontinuity.md)'s "structural diagnosis" section.

2. **Each regime stratum has its own unobstructed deformation theory.** Per the Ext-classification's "Strict regime", "Scott-continuous regime", "Transfinite-bounded regime", "Aperiodic regime" subsections, each stratum's deformation theory is locally formally smooth.

The two features combine to produce a moduli whose deformation theory is *locally* well-behaved (formally smooth at each point) but *globally* non-semicontinuous (tangent dimension jumps up and down across regime boundaries).

## Cycling back into cohomology

The deformation-theoretic finding cycles back into the cohomology side of the corpus in three ways:

1. **The moduli's tangent-dimension function $T$ is itself a constructible cohomological invariant on $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$.** $T$ records the rank of the per-fiber $H^1$ as a function on the moduli; Part (b)'s constructibility says this rank is locally constant on strata. The function $T$ is then a stratified-rank cohomological invariant of the moduli, refining the regime stratification with quantitative content.

2. **The non-semicontinuity constrains the corpus's classification machinery.** Within the corpus, classification of settling moduli has to refine via the regime stratification + orbit-type data ([strong-MLD via composed duality theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/strong-mld-via-composed-duality.md), sketch), not via tangent-dimension semicontinuity — Part (c) shows the latter is unavailable. The orbit-type structure is the corpus-internal refinement layer below regime + $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}|$.

3. **The constructibility-not-semicontinuity feature is preserved under the [Q-enriched](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/content/quantale-enriched-sheaf/content/q-enriched-cohomology/q-enriched-cohomology.md) lift.** The Q-enriched cohomology vanishing-above-degree-1 ([q-enriched-cohomology higher-degree vanishing theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/content/quantale-enriched-sheaf/references/q-enriched-cohomology-higher-degree-vanishing.md), sketch) gives unobstructed Q-enriched deformation theory (per the [d-frame Ext-classification](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/skh-deformation-ext-classification.md) Part 2). The d-frame tangent refinement adds further constructible structure (truth-only / falsity-only / $\top$-firing tangent directions, per Part 4 of skh-deformation-ext-classification), which inherits the non-semicontinuity of the underlying regime stratification. So the d-frame deformation-tangent function $T_{\mathbf{dFrm}}$ is similarly constructible-but-non-semicontinuous on $\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{SKH}}$.
