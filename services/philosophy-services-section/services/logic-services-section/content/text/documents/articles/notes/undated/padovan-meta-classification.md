# Meta-classification of the Padovan substitution rule

The L5d meta-classification instance. **Padovan lives in the step-3 sub-stack $\mathscr{M}^{(3)} \subset \mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{meta}}_{\mathrm{aper}}$.**

## Meta-stratum

By the [step-n meta-stratification theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/references/step-n-meta-stratification.md), the Padovan closure rule $[\mathcal{S}_{\text{Padovan}}]$ is in $\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$ — the step-3 sub-stack.

## Other points in $\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$

$\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$ contains other step-3 substitutions distinct from Padovan (modulo closure-equivalence):

- **Tribonacci** ($\sigma : a \mapsto ab, b \mapsto ac, c \mapsto a$): different substitution matrix from Padovan, hence distinct meta-point in $\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$. See [tribonacci-substitution-settling](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/examples/tribonacci-substitution-settling.md) (forthcoming).
- Various other 3-letter substitutions encoded as closure rules.

Each is a distinct meta-point in $\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$ — same step parameter, different substitution structure.

## Step-shift comparison

The [step-shift morphism](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/morphisms/step-shift-morphism.md) $\sigma_2^3 : \mathscr{M}^{(2)} \to \mathscr{M}^{(3)}$ takes Fibonacci ($\mathscr{M}^{(2)}$) to *some* step-3 rule in $\mathscr{M}^{(3)}$. Whether this image is Padovan, Tribonacci, or yet another step-3 rule depends on the step-shift's specific extension-vector choice. Concrete computation in [fibonacci-to-padovan-step-shift](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/examples/fibonacci-to-padovan-step-shift.md) (forthcoming).
