# Configuration space vs. trajectory

The data of a [spatiotemporal knowledge presheaf](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf.md) decomposes into two structurally distinct parts.

The first is the [predication fiber functor](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/references/predication-fiber-functor.md) $F : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{DBiLat}(\mathbf{L})$, which assigns to each predicate its [predication fiber algebra](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_p = \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E}$. The fiber functor depends only on the base parameters $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$ — not on any specific settling — and is shared across every knowledge presheaf over the same base.

The second is the settling trajectory $(v_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$ of valuations in the product $\prod_p A_p$. Two knowledge presheaves over the same base differ only in their trajectories.

This is the configuration-space-versus-trajectory split familiar from physical state-space dynamics: the fiber functor specifies the configuration space — the set of all possible valuations — and is fixed by the parameters of the system. The settling is a path through this space, evolving in the [knowledge order](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/concepts/knowledge-order.md) $\leq_k$ as evidence accumulates and inference fires.

The split is not just expository: it determines what is parameterized in the moduli layer. The base parameters $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$ give a family of knowledge presheaves over a common configuration space; varying the base is a second order of variation — the moduli of configuration spaces themselves — handled by the [settling moduli stack](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md) over the [parameter base category](parameter-base-category.md).
