# Positing-generated settling paths are confluent under knowledge join

Any two finite positing-generated paths from a common predication settling have a common successor given by the [confluence join](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/morphisms/confluence-join.md).

## Statement

Let $v$ be a predication settling. Suppose finite composites of [positing morphisms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/positing-morphism/positing-morphism.md) produce

$$v \Rightarrow^* u, \qquad v \Rightarrow^* w.$$

Then $u$ and $w$ have a common positing-generated successor:

$$u \Rightarrow^* u \vee_k w, \qquad w \Rightarrow^* u \vee_k w.$$

## Proof

A finite composite of positing morphisms is a batch positing morphism:

$$x \longmapsto x \vee_k W$$

for some finite positing collection $W$. Therefore there are finite collections $U,W$ with

$$u = v \vee_k U,\qquad w = v \vee_k W.$$

Their join is

$$u \vee_k w = (v \vee_k U) \vee_k (v \vee_k W) = v \vee_k U \vee_k W,$$

using associativity, commutativity, and idempotence of $\vee_k$.

From $u = v \vee_k U$, applying the remaining posit collection $W$ yields

$$u \vee_k W = v \vee_k U \vee_k W = u \vee_k w.$$

Symmetrically, applying $U$ to $w$ yields the same join. Thus both branches reach the same successor by finite positing. $\square$

## Reading

This theorem is the predication-categorical counterpart of abstract rewriting confluence for the update fragment generated by positing. It is stronger than mere order confluence because the common upper bound is reachable by the same operational primitive that generated the branches.
