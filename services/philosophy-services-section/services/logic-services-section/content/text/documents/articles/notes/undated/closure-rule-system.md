A **closure-rule system** is a monad T on a category of cell-collections whose unit η encodes "a cell asserts itself" and whose multiplication μ composes derivation chains (cells derived from cells derived from cells collapse to single derivations). Closure rules are the Kleisli morphisms of T: each rule has a premise pattern (matching incoming cells) and an action that produces additional cells in the monadic context. Firing a closure rule is Kleisli composition. In the 561 OS, the closure-rule system is the kernel of the FOUR-based computer — the firing process IS what the computer computes.
