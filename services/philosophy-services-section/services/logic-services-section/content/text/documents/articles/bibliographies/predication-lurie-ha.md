## Annotation

Companion to [@lurie2009] (Higher Topos Theory). Develops $\infty$-categorical algebra: stable $\infty$-categories, symmetric monoidal $\infty$-categories, $\mathbb{E}_n$-algebras, and the derived-functor / six-functor packages in the $\infty$-categorical setting.

**Relevance to the corpus.** The future-direction narrative on $(\infty, 1)$-extensions cites this as the natural setting for: (a) derived enhancements of predication categories' six-functor formalism, (b) active $\infty$-categorical sheaf theory generalizing predication categories' Q-enriched sheaves, (c) derived deformation theory generalizing predication categories' Ext-classification.

The specific corpus structures the lift would touch — closure 2-monad, settling moduli stack, predication hyperdoctrine — would each require corresponding $\infty$-categorical analogues developed in this framework.

The lift is not currently undertaken; this reference scaffolds the future-direction narrative.
