Entity record for vague-token. A vague-token is a token that the watchlist scanner has classified as a stretch-indicator — a use that signals the emitter lacks precise context. The data shape extends [token](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/system/library/token/library) with two fields: a *trigger-reason* (free-text recorded on the watchlist entry that fired the match) and a list of *exempt-cues* (words whose presence in the surrounding window suppresses the flag because they indicate the token is being used precisely). The full data shape is at [the JSON schema](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/system/library/token/library/vague-token/vague-token.schema.json); the EAVT log at [vague-token.jsonl](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/system/library/token/library/vague-token/vague-token.jsonl).

The transformer that recognizes a token as vague is `noticing-a-vague-token`. The transformer that responds is `injecting-context-after-noticing-a-vague-token`. Both are recorded under [the injecting subtree](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/system/library/doing/working/injecting).
