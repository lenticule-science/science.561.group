# Predication structure

The bilattice supplies the value domain. The *predication structure* supplies the indexing on which values live: a small [predicate category](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ whose objects are predicates, an [entity set](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/entity-set.md) $E$, and the cell index $E \times E$ that tracks subject–object pairs at each predicate.

The central object built from these pieces is the [predication fiber algebra](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) at predicate $q$:

$$A_q \;:=\; \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E},$$

a bilattice of bilattice-valued functions on the cell index. A [predication valuation](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predication-valuation.md) — an element $v \in A_q$ — assigns a four-state value to every cell $(s, o) \in E \times E$, recording the program's knowledge of the predicate's instantiation at every entity pair.

The predicate category's morphisms are not arbitrary. By a structural-equivariance hypothesis under the symmetric group on $E$, the natural [bilattice homomorphisms](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/morphisms/bilattice-homomorphism.md) between predication fiber algebras decompose into four classes — [specialization](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-specialization.md), [converse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md), [subject-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [object-projection](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-object-projection.md) — and the predicate category is generated by these. See [predicate-generator exhaustiveness](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/predication-fibration/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md).

The [predication fiber functor](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/references/predication-fiber-functor.md) packages this: the assignment $q \mapsto A_q$ is a contravariant functor $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{DBiLat}(\mathbf{L})$ from the predicate category to distributive bilattices, with the four-generator action providing the reindexing. This is the static spatial structure on which the settling will run.
