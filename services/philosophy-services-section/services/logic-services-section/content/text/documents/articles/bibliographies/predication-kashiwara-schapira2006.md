Pending direct access. Cited via secondary literature.

## Annotation

A modern reference for category theory and sheaf theory at graduate level: Grothendieck topologies and sites, abelian categories, derived functors, six-functor formalism, and the homological-algebra apparatus needed for sheaf cohomology in generality. For the program, the standard reference for sheaf-theoretic infrastructure used in [settling cohomology](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/settling-cohomology.md) and across the cohomology layer.
