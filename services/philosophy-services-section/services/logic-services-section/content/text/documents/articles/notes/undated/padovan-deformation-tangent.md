# Deformation tangent of the Padovan substitution settling

The L5d deformation tangent — uncountable rank, parallel to Fibonacci.

## Ext^1 computation

For the Padovan settling at $E = \omega_1$:

$$\mathrm{Ext}^1(\mathcal{F}_v, \mathcal{F}_v) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}^{|L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)|} \;=\; \mathbb{Z}^{2^{\aleph_0}}.$$

Same cardinality as Fibonacci's deformation tangent — both aperiodic-regime substitutions reach the corpus's maximal deformation rank.

## Distinction from Fibonacci

Despite the same cardinality, Padovan's deformation tangent has *different structural realization* from Fibonacci's:

- **Fibonacci's tangent:** parameterized by perturbations of Fibonacci-substitution-rule structure (matrix entries, etc.).
- **Padovan's tangent:** parameterized by perturbations of Padovan-substitution-rule structure.

Step-3 substitutions have a 3 × 3 matrix; perturbations of $M_{\text{Padovan}}$ have a different structural shape than perturbations of Fibonacci's 2 × 2 matrix. The tangent spaces are *isomorphic-as-groups* but *structurally distinct* under the step-$n$ family's morphisms.
