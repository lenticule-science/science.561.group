## Annotation

Relates Kellendonk and Putnam's **pattern-equivariant (PE) cohomology** to the inverse-limit structure of a tiling space. The headline result is

**Theorem 4 (§3).** $H^*_{\mathrm{PE}}(T, G) \cong \check{H}^*(\Omega_T, G) \cong \varinjlim_n \check{H}^*(\Gamma_n, G)$ for any abelian group $G$.

The direct-limit presentation (the rightmost equality) is the methodological ancestor for the cohomological layer: settling cohomology at the closure ordinal $\kappa$ is the direct limit of finite-stage cohomologies under the settling endofunctor's action. The analogy is structural — tiling cohomology uses abelian-group coefficients on simplicial-complex inverse limits; we use abelian-group coefficients on bitopological-site direct limits — but the underlying direct-limit framework transfers cleanly.

**§4 (PE representation variety).** Non-abelian generalization, non-trivial even when $\pi_1(\Omega_T) = 1$. A concrete analogue of how direct-limit invariants can detect what inverse-limit invariants cannot. The moduli layer records this as a future direction.

**Important distinction.** Tiling theory does not construct a moduli stack. The moduli-layer's moduli is structurally a different object; tiling theory is methodological ancestry, not structural template. The moduli layer makes the disambiguation explicit.
