## Annotation

The textbook reference for fibred categorical logic. Used systematically throughout the hyperdoctrinal layer.

Sections relied on:
- **Chapter 1** (Introduction to fibred category theory) — fibration definitions; cleavages; reindexing functors.
- **§3.4** (Fibred equality) — templates the hyperdoctrinal layer (equality and comprehension).
- **§4.2** (Fibrations for first-order predicate logic) — templates the three-strata structure (predication fibration / predication hyperdoctrine / settling-equipped predication hyperdoctrine).
- **Chapter 9** (Advanced fibred category theory) — quantification, locally small fibrations, definability.

The hyperdoctrinal-layer's choice of the **fibration-over-indexed-category** framing for the predication hyperdoctrine is based on Jacobs's usage. The alternative indexed-category form is equivalent via the Grothendieck construction but less direct for the 2-categorical structure on $\mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C}$ that the hyperdoctrinal layer uses.
