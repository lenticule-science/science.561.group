## Annotation

**Primary source** for the residuation pair $\odot, \Rightarrow$ on the twist product of an integral commutative residuated lattice. Gives the equational characterization of the subvariety of commutative residuated lattices represented by twist-products.

The foundational layer cite as the primary source for the existence of the adjunction $\odot \dashv \Rightarrow$, with the proof itself adapted from [@chajda-langer2021] (which gives a cleaner direct argument).

Co-credited: [@tsinakis-wille2006], "Minimal varieties of involutive residuated lattices," for an independently-developed version of the same construction.
