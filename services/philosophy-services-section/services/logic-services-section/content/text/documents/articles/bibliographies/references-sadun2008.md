## Annotation

The textbook treatment of the topology of aperiodic-tiling spaces: inverse-limit constructions over Anderson–Putnam complexes, mutual local derivability (MLD) as the equivalence relation classifying tilings up to local recoding, and the Čech cohomology of tiling spaces as a complete topological invariant in many cases. For the program, this is the methodological template the [aperiodic-regime classification](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-classification.md) imports: settlings stand to MLD as tilings do, and the [aperiodic classifying invariant](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) plays the role of the Čech-cohomology invariant in tiling theory.
