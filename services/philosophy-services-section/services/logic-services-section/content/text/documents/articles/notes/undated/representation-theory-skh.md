# What the predication hyperdoctrine is a representation of

Every algebraic object presents itself as a representation of something — Lie algebras of Lie groups, modules of rings. Open: identify what the [predication hyperdoctrine](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) is a representation of.

Candidate: the free residuated 2-category generated by the [predicate category](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ — with [the four predicate generators](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/predication-fibration/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md) as the generating 1-cells, the bilattice operations as fiber-level structure, and the closure 2-monad as the 2-cell content. Confirming or refuting this candidate would situate the predication hyperdoctrine in a representation-theoretic landscape and open the door to character-theory-style analysis.

## Connection to the realizability program

The [realizability program](realizability-program.md) gives concrete tools for what the answer would look like. Two axes of the program are directly representation-theoretic:

- **$\mathcal{C}$-naturality coupling axis.** The [$\mathcal{C}$-naturality coupling refinement](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/c-naturality-coupling-realizability-refinement.md) characterizes how each of the four predicate-generator classes (specialization, converse, subject- and object-projection) couples per-predicate firing patterns. This is the *predicate-category-action* of the predication hyperdoctrine — the structure a representation theory would record.
- **Rule-language fragment axis.** The [rule-language fragment refinement](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-2/theorems/rule-language-fragment-realizability-refinement.md) parametrizes the realizable cohomology classes by rule-language expressivity. Which classes are accessible from finite-rule presentations vs richer rule systems is a representation-theoretic question about the predication hyperdoctrine's "complexity of representation" — an answer would specify the classes' representation-theoretic invariants.

The candidate "free residuated 2-category" answer is consistent with the per-axis realizability characterizations: the realizable family decomposes along the structural axes that a representation-theoretic answer would predict. Confirming the candidate would now mean showing that the per-axis decomposition exhibits the universal-property structure of a free 2-category.
