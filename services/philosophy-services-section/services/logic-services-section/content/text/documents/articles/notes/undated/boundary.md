The **holographic identity-surface** of an entity: the surface onto which the entity's bulk projects, exhausted by four canonical fibers (auth, authz, attention, history). The records' primitive identity object — upstream of [persona](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/entity-set/library/entity/library/boundary/persona.md), [personnel](personnel.md), [officer](../../../../../../../relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/03/18/2026-03-18-officer.md), [agent](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/entity-set/library/entity/library/boundary/agent.md), all of which are projection-shapes of the boundary's fibers.

Per the holographic principle (applied to identity per the user's source idea, `user-as-boundary`): a real bulk has indefinite complexity that the records cannot model; the records model only the boundary, the projection of the bulk onto the system's surface. What the records carry about the entity is exhausted by the four fibers.

## The four fibers

- **Auth fiber** — who is this key (the boundary's keypairs and key-derivation cells).
- **Authz fiber** — what can this entity do (capability-cells, granted positions).
- **Attention fiber** — what is this entity looking at (subscriptions, foci).
- **History fiber** — what has this entity done (the operative-record accumulator).

## Distinguishing predication-shape

- `four-fiber-bundle(boundary, auth-fiber, authz-fiber, attention-fiber, history-fiber)` — the canonical projection-bundle.
- `keypair-on-surface(boundary, surface-tag, keypair)` — the per-projection-surface keys.
- `bulk-of(boundary, bulk?)` — for boundaries with a real bulk (a real human); absent for synthetic boundaries.

## Step-induction role

Session-open: a session must open *as a boundary* before any further form can be selected. The session-record's `boundary` slot is filled by a boundary, not directly by a persona or personnel. Then `acting-as` selects a projection (personnel, office, persona).

This is the upstream of *all* identity disciplines. Capability composition, audit-attribution, persona-publication-quotient — all are operations on the boundary's fibers.

## Relation to other forms

- **Persona** is a projection-shape of the boundary at the publication surface (with the persona-projection-quotient: downstream consumers cannot recover the operating boundary without privileged-cross-projection).
- **Personnel** is the polity-membership relation; for a real-bulk entity (emsenn) personnel and boundary correspond closely; for synthetic entities the boundary is more abstract.
- **Body-natural** is a session's incarnation — it's bound to a boundary acting through a persona projection.
