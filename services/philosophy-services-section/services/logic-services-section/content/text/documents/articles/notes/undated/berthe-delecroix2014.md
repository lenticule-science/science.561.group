## Full citation

V. Berthé, V. Delecroix. "Beyond substitutive dynamical systems: $S$-adic expansions." *RIMS Kôkyûroku Bessatsu* B46, 81–123, 2014. arXiv:1309.3960.

## Status

Foundational survey of S-adic systems — symbolic dynamical systems generated by infinite compositions of substitutions, with the substitution allowed to vary at each step. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

An **S-adic shift** is generated by a sequence of substitutions $(\sigma_k)_{k \geq 0}$ where $\sigma_k : A_{k+1}^* \to A_k^*$. The system reads infinite words by composing $\sigma_0 \circ \sigma_1 \circ \cdots$ from the outside in. This generalizes single-substitution systems by allowing substitution-rules to vary across iteration.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified by the literature stress-test as the closest "varying-alphabet substitution" framework to the corpus's [step-$n$ family](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/concepts/step-n-substitution-recurrence.md), and the natural setting in which the corpus's [step-$n$ fixed-point object](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/content/step-n-moduli-substack/content/step-n-fixed-point/step-n-fixed-point.md) might be embedded. Critical structural difference: S-adic compositions go in the *inverse* direction (an S-adic system reads $\sigma_0 \circ \sigma_1 \circ \cdots$ as a projective limit), while the corpus's step-shift goes in the *direct* direction (embedding $\mathscr{M}^{(n)} \hookrightarrow \mathscr{M}^{(n+1)}$ at the moduli level).
