David Graeber (1961–2020) was an American anthropologist and [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/david-graeber/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) based at the London School of Economics. His work combined anthropological fieldwork (particularly in Madagascar), historical analysis, and [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/david-graeber/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) political commitment to challenge the narratives that mainstream economics and political theory treat as self-evident.

## Core ideas

- **Debt as social relation**: in *[Debt: The First 5,000 Years](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/debt-the-first-5000-years.md)* (2011), Graeber argues that debt is not a neutral economic mechanism but a moral and political relationship — one that has historically been enforced through violence. The standard economics narrative (barter → money → credit) is historically backwards: credit and debt came first, and money emerged from state and military operations, not from market exchange.
- **Mutual aid as anthropological norm**: building on [Kropotkin's](/general/people/pyotr-kropotkin.md) work, Graeber argued from anthropological evidence that cooperation, sharing, and [mutual aid](/sociology/terms/mutual-aid.md) are the baseline of human economic life — not the exception to a competitive norm but the norm that markets and states disrupted.
- **Bullshit jobs**: Graeber identified a large class of jobs that even the people holding them consider pointless — administrative bloat, corporate compliance, make-work — and argued that their existence contradicts the efficiency claims of market [capitalism](../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalism.md).
- **[Prefigurative politics](../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/services/organization-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prefigurative-politics.md)**: Graeber practiced and theorized the [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/david-graeber/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) commitment to building the social relations you want in the present, rather than deferring them to a post-revolutionary future. He was active in Occupy Wall Street and other direct-action movements.
