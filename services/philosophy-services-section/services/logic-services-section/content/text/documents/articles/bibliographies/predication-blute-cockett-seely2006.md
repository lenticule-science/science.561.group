## Annotation

Differential categories provide the categorical calculus of derivatives used in differential linear logic. The comparison-operations packet cites this reference chiefly as a boundary condition: a PredCat "differentiation" primitive that measures residual evidence between two settlings is not, by itself, a differential category. To claim a genuine differential-category structure, PredCat would need the relevant additive monoidal, coalgebra-modal, and differential-combinator data.
