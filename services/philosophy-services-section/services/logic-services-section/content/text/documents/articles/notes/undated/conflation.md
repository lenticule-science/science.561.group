# Conflation

An optional unary operation $-$ on a [bilattice](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/code/objects/bilattice.md) satisfying:

- $a \leq_k b \Rightarrow -b \leq_k -a$ (antitone in the [knowledge order](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/code/transformers/teaching/explanations/knowledge-order.md));
- $a \leq_t b \Rightarrow -a \leq_t -b$ (monotone in the [truth order](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/truth-order/truth-order.md));
- $-(-a) = a$ (involutive).

When both [negation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/code/transformers/negation.md) and conflation are present on a bilattice, they are typically required to commute: $\sim\, - = -\, \sim$.
