## Full citation

P. J. Cameron. *Permutation Groups*. London Mathematical Society Student Texts 45, Cambridge University Press, 1999.

## Status

Standard textbook reference. Not directly accessed for the program; cited for the standard fact that $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$ acting on $E \times E$ yields a coherent configuration with two basic relations $\Delta, \Delta^c$ and the four equivariant index maps among them.

## Annotation

Cameron's textbook treats permutation groups, their orbits on $X^k$, coherent configurations and association schemes (Ch. 3), and the orbital-relation algebra of a transitive action. The fact relevant here — that the natural action of $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$ on $E \times E$ is doubly transitive on $\Delta^c$ for $|E| \geq 3$ and produces exactly two orbits — is folklore in algebraic combinatorics; Cameron is a clean reference.
