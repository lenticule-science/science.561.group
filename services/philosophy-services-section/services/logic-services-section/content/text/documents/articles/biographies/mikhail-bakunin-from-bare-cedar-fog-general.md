Mikhail Alexandrovich Bakunin (1814--1876) was a Russian revolutionary and the principal theorist of collectivist [anarchism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/terms/anarchism.md). His participation in uprisings across Europe, his imprisonment, his exile to Siberia, and his escape through Japan and the United States made him a figure of legend within revolutionary movements. His political legacy, however, rests on his arguments rather than his adventures.

## Core ideas

- **Critique of [the state](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md)**: Bakunin argued that [the state](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) is not a neutral instrument that can be seized and redirected but a structure that generates domination regardless of who holds it. A revolutionary state would reproduce hierarchy, bureaucracy, and coercion under new management. This critique applied to [Marx](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/dev/journal/karl-marx.md)'s program directly: Bakunin predicted that a dictatorship of the proletariat would become a dictatorship over the proletariat.
- **The [anarchist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md)-[communist](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/terms/communism.md) split**: Bakunin's conflict with [Karl Marx](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/dev/journal/karl-marx.md) in the First International (the International Workingmen's Association) defined the central division in revolutionary politics. [Marx](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/dev/journal/karl-marx.md) argued that the working class must seize [state power](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) as a transitional step; Bakunin argued that seizing [state power](../../../../../../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) reproduces domination. [Marx](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/dev/journal/karl-marx.md) expelled Bakunin from the International in 1872. The split established [anarchism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md) and authoritarian socialism as distinct and often opposed traditions.
- **Collectivist [anarchism](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/schools/anarchism/index.md)**: Bakunin advocated the collective ownership of the means of production, organized through free federation of workers' associations. Unlike later anarcho-communists such as [Pyotr Kropotkin](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/dev/journal/pyotr-kropotkin.md), Bakunin retained a principle of remuneration according to [labor](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/references/people/mikhail-bakunin/docs/sociology/terms/labor.md) performed.
- **Revolutionary federalism**: social organization should proceed from the bottom up -- from the commune to the federation -- with no central authority empowered to compel obedience. Coordination without command.

## Notable works

- *God and the State* (*Dieu et l'État*, written 1871, published posthumously 1882)
- *Statism and Anarchy* (*Gosudarstvennost' i anarkhiya*, 1873)
