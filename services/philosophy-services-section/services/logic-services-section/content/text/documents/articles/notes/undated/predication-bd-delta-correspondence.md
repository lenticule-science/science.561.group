# SKH-FOL ↔ BD∆ correspondence

The internal classical first-order logic of the [predication hyperdoctrine](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md) under the $\leq_t$-adjoint system, with [classical equality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/classical-equality.md) $=_E$ and [comprehension](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/predication-hyperdoctrine/references/lawvere-comprehension-classical.md) $\{-\}_\mathbf{t}$, is conjecturally equivalent to BD∆ — Omori–Wansing's first-order paraconsistent logic with a designated-truth modality $\Delta$.

A correspondence theorem would specify the translation in both directions: SKH formulas to BD∆ formulas (and conversely), and prove that derivability is preserved. The argument is non-trivial — quantifier semantics and the role of $\Delta$ in BD∆ both demand careful matching against the [two-internal-logics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/theorems/two-internal-logics.md) structure here. A standalone appendix or a separate short note is the likely shape.

Related: [Belnap-designated comprehension](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/belnap-designated-comprehension.md) — both questions concern the predication hyperdoctrine's internal logic under designated-value semantics. A working BD∆ translation would likely interact with the third (Belnap-designated) comprehension schema; the two questions are partially coupled.
