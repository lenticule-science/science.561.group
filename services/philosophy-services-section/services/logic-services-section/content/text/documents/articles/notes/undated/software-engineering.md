Software engineering is treated here as a discipline within [computing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/category/hacking/library/computing/theories/type-theory/_index.md) — the practice-side counterpart to the substrate-side studies of [predicated-computing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/category/hacking/library/computing/theories/type-theory/theories/predicated-computing/_index.md). Computing studies what computers *are*; software engineering studies what humans (and increasingly agents) *do* to produce, evolve, and operate them.

The discipline's content decomposes into the *activities* that produce or change software (development, refactoring, testing, deployment, observation, deletion) and the *paradigms* that organize those activities (waterfall, agile, continuous-delivery, kernel-and-modules, monorepo-and-trains, etc.). The fusion theory — [predicated-software-engineering](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/predication-cell-set/docs/dev/scraps/from-stable-cell-stale-chain/library/predicate-set/library/predicate-category/hacking/library/object/library/thing/library/category/hacking/library/computing/theories/type-theory/theories/predicated-computing/theories/predicated-software-engineering/_index.md) under predicated-computing — re-presents these activities as operations on a substrate-algebraic codebase: a codebase as a predication-cell-system, a change as a witness-increment, a refactor as an invisible automorphism.
