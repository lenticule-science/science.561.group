# Continuous-$\mathbf{L}$ knowledge presheaves

> **Progress.** The framework now has continuous value categories built in: the [continuous-valued settling object](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/continuous-valued-settling/continuous-valued-settling.md) covers Lawvere quantale $[0, \infty]$, fuzzy $[0, 1]$, real-valued, and general residuated-lattice $\mathcal{V}$. The [continuous-state regime classification theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/continuous-state-regime-classification.md) confirms the four-regime dichotomy survives, with a continuous-time variant (strict-c / asymptotic-c / non-asymptotic-countable / aperiodic-c). The [Lawvere-quantale benchmark](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/examples/benchmark-lawvere-quantale-settling.md) gives concrete computations. Open: MV-algebra-specific axiomatization, Priestley-style topology on $[0,1]$ fibers, bigraded-cohomology in the continuous case.

The corpus's main results take $\mathbf{L}$ to be a finite or countable [residuated lattice](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/residuated-lattice.md) (often $\mathbf{2}$ or $\mathbf{FOUR}$). Open: rebuild the framework with $\mathbf{L} = [0, 1]$ (an MV-algebra) or other continuous residuated lattices. Which structural results survive, which fail, and what replaces the failures?

## What survives unconditionally

The pure-algebra layer is value-type-agnostic: anything stated for general residuated lattices and twist products carries through.

- [Twist-product residuation](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/references/twist-product-residuation.md), [twist-product right residual](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/morphisms/twist-product-right-residual.md), [Avron representation](avron-representation.md) — all stated for general residuated lattices.
- [Predication fiber algebra](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), [predication fiber functor](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/references/predication-fiber-functor.md) — pure algebra over $\mathbf{L}$.
- [Predicate-generator exhaustiveness](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/predication-fibration/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md) — structural-equivariance argument independent of $\mathbf{L}$'s cardinality.

## What needs revisitation

- **[Settling convergence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/settling-convergence.md)** assumes finite-or-Scott-continuous closure on a finite-fiber base. With $\mathbf{L} = [0,1]$ the fiber algebra is uncountable; settling convergence holds only under topological assumptions on the closure (continuity, possibly Hausdorffness on $[0,1]$). Specifically: when does the transfinite settling converge in the order topology on $[0,1]^\bowtie$?
- **[Modal finite model property](modal-finite-model-property.md)** fails: there is no finite model property for continuous-$\mathbf{L}$ modal calculi in general. The decidability questions of the modal-calculus chapter ([modal-complexity-bounds](modal-complexity-bounds.md)) are entirely different — likely undecidable for full continuous $\mathbf{L}$, with decidable fragments only under heavy restriction.
- **[CRDT convergence](../../../../glossary/concepts/crdt-convergence.md)** depends on $\vee_k$-merge being a join-semilattice. On $[0,1]$ this is fine algebraically, but the convergence theorem's *finite-orbit* proof technique requires re-examination: continuous-$\mathbf{L}$ orbits are no longer eventually constant, only convergent in the order topology.
- **[Settling cohomology vanishing above degree 1](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md)** — the proof's 3-axis argument may or may not survive: the fiber-Boolean-algebra-dimension axis is what changes (from 0-dimensional Boolean to a continuous bilattice). Specifically: on a continuous fiber, what's the analogue of the Boolean-algebra cohomological dimension?

## What changes structurally

Continuous $\mathbf{L}$ unlocks numerical applications — fuzzy logic, Łukasiewicz semantics, MV-algebra-based knowledge representation — but introduces three structurally different concerns:

- **Stability.** Numerical settling on $[0, 1]$ produces evolving real-valued cells that may converge slowly, oscillate, or accumulate floating-point error. The settling becomes an analytic object as well as algebraic.
- **Topology of fibers.** $[0, 1]^\bowtie$ has the order-product topology; the closure operator is continuous in this topology, but the [Priestley dualization](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/references/priestley-dualizability.md) becomes Stone-style for compact Hausdorff distributive lattices. The dual predication hyperdoctrine inherits this topology.
- **MV-algebra structure.** Continuous Łukasiewicz logic (Hájek's *Metamathematics of Fuzzy Logic*, Cignoli–D'Ottaviano–Mundici on MV-algebras) gives published infrastructure: MV-algebra duality, complete axiomatization, decidable fragments. The corpus's continuous-$\mathbf{L}$ work would be the bilattice-2-monadic refinement of this.
