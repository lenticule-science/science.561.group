## Annotation

Systematizes **property-like 2-monads**: 2-monads for which an algebra structure is essentially unique when it exists at all. Introduces the term *lax-idempotent 2-monad* and *fully property-like*.

Theorems used in the hyperdoctrinal layer:
- **(K1)** Kock's characterization: a 2-monad is lax-idempotent iff $\mu \dashv \eta\mathbb{T}$ in $[\mathcal{K}, \mathcal{K}]$ with counit $\mu \cdot \eta\mathbb{T} = 1_\mathbb{T}$.
- **(K2)** *Algebras are properties*: for every pseudo-$\mathbb{T}$-algebra $a: \mathbb{T} A \to A$, the action $a$ is left adjoint to $\eta_A$ with identity counit. Equivalently, $\mathbb{T}$-algebra structure on an object is unique up to unique iso if it exists at all.
- **(K3)** *Unique lax morphism structure*: for every 1-cell $f: A \to B$ between strict $\mathbb{T}$-algebras, there is a unique 2-cell making $f$ a lax $\mathbb{T}$-morphism.

Hierarchy: lax-idempotent + colax-idempotent $\Rightarrow$ fully property-like $\Rightarrow$ property-like. Finitary reflection: each class is coreflective in 2-monads.

The hyperdoctrinal layer primary reference for the KZ structure of the closure 2-monad $\mathrm{Cl}$. The (K2)/(K3) characterizations are what license the hyperdoctrinal-layer's claim that the predication hyperdoctrine's hyperdoctrinal properties are "structure-as-property" — verifying or failing rather than chosen.
