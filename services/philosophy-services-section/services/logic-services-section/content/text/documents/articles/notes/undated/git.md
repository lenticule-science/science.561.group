**git** is a content-addressed distributed version-control system organized as a Merkle DAG of objects (blobs, trees, commits, tags) addressed by SHA-1 (legacy) or SHA-256 (modern) hashes. Specializes [system](system.md) as a version-control system; intermediate `version-control-system` parent may be earned later. Its lifecycle hooks (pre-commit, commit-msg, post-commit, pre-push, etc.) are the events the 561 OS gates on to keep its substrate conformant.
