## Annotation

Comprehensive exposition of Bénabou's fibration framework, much of which is otherwise unpublished. The relevant sections for our program:

- **§4 closure properties of fibrations** — used in the hyperdoctrinal layer for verifying that the relevant constructions stay inside the cloven-fibration class.
- **§13 preservation under change of base** — this is the hyperdoctrinal-layer's Beck–Chevalley story stated fibrationally.
- **§14 adjoints to change of base** — quantifier adjoints in fibrational language; matches the knowledge presheaf $\exists_f \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f$ pattern.
- **§§15–18 geometric morphisms as fibrations** — relevant for the cohomological-layer's Priestley route to cohomology.
