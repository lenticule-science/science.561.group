## Full citation

L. Sadun. "Tiling spaces are inverse limits." *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 44, 5410–5414, 2003. arXiv:math/0210179.

## Status

Standard reference for inverse-limit modeling of FLC tiling spaces. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Generalizes [@anderson-putnam1998]'s substitution-tiling inverse limit to arbitrary FLC (finite-local-complexity) tilings: every FLC tiling space is an inverse limit of branched manifolds under self-maps. The branched manifolds are generalizations of the Anderson-Putnam complex $\Gamma_\sigma$.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified in the literature stress-test of the [step-n fixed-point object](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/content/step-n-moduli-substack/content/step-n-fixed-point/step-n-fixed-point.md). Cited adjacent to [@williams1974] and [@anderson-putnam1998] when discussing inverse-limit-versus-direct-limit constructions in the substitution-tiling literature: predication categories' $\mathscr{M}^{(\infty)}$ direct limit is structurally distinct from the inverse-limit constructions standard in this literature.
