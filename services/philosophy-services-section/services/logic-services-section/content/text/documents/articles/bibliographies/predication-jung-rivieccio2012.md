## Annotation

Develops a Priestley-style duality theory for several classes of algebras with a bilattice reduct. The previous duality theory (Mobasher, Pigozzi, Slutzki, and Voutsadakis 2000) was abstract category-theoretic; this paper gives the concrete topological spaces — *paired Priestley spaces*.

**Core result.** The category $\mathbf{DBiLat}$ of distributive bilattices is dually equivalent to a category of paired Priestley spaces: each distributive bilattice $\mathbf{B} \cong \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ corresponds to the pair $(\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L}), \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L}))$ with a swap involution encoding the bilattice negation.

**The dual of $\mathbf{FOUR}$.** Since $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{2})$ is a single point, the Priestley dual of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ is a two-point space $\{+, -\}$ with a swap map $+ \leftrightarrow -$. This is the simplest non-trivial paired Priestley space and the source of the internal $\mathbb{Z}/2$ gauge in the framework.

Primary source for the foundational-layer's gauge and duality content and for the hyperdoctrinal layer's Priestley dualizability. The cohomological layer builds sheaf cohomology on the dualization this paper establishes fiberwise.
