## Annotation

A standard graduate text on homological algebra: derived functors, spectral sequences, Tor and Ext, group and Hochschild cohomology, mapping cones and triangulated categories. For the program, supplies the homological-algebra background for [bigraded cohomology via mapping cone](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/content/bigraded-settling-cohomology/content/assets/legacy/bigraded-cohomology-via-mapping-cone.md) — mapping-cone presentation of long exact sequences and the relationship to comparison morphisms between sheaves.
