## Annotation

The W3C Recommendation for RDF 1.1 semantics: model-theoretic semantics for triple-store knowledge representation, including simple, RDF, and RDFS interpretations. For the program, RDF triple stores are a paradigmatic instance of the kind of partial, accumulating, multi-source assertion store that the predication settling models — the (subject, predicate, object) triple is the [predication cell](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/teaching/explanations/predication-cell.md) under another name.
