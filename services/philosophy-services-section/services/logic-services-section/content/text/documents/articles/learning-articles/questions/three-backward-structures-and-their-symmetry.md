# Three backward structures and their symmetry

The corpus has three structurally-distinct "backward direction" operations:

1. **σ** ([sigma-as-axis-rotation](sigma-as-axis-rotation.md)): rotates between $\leq_k$-theory and $\leq_t$-theory. Cellwise: 90° rotation of FOUR. Single-valued, involutive. Preserves time-direction.

2. **Conflation $-$** ([conflation-as-knowledge-axis-reversal](conflation-as-knowledge-axis-reversal.md)): reflects the knowledge axis. Conjugates $T_k$ to a $\leq_k$-deflationary kernel operator $K_k = -T_k-$. Single-valued, involutive. Reverses the value-axis dynamics.

3. **Cosettling $T_k^{-1}$** ([cosettling-backward-direction](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/questions/cosettling-backward-direction.md), [cosettling object](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/cosettling/cosettling.md)): reverses ordinal time. Multi-valued, no canonical inverse. Reverses the temporal dynamics.

These are independent: $\sigma$ acts on values without touching ordinal time; conflation reflects values within the knowledge axis without rotating axes; cosettling reverses ordinal time without acting on values. They commute on the operations they act on, in the sense that the conjugate of $T_k^{-1}$ by $\sigma$ is $T_t^{-1}$ (parallel-theory cosettling), and the conjugate by $-$ is $K_k^{-1}$ (the cosettling of the kernel operator).

## The value-category symmetry group

The involutions $\{\sigma, -, \sim\}$ on FOUR generate $D_4$ (dihedral of order 8). Specifically:

- $\sim$ ([negation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/morphisms/negation.md)) swaps $\mathbf{t} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{f}$, fixes $\bot, \top$ — the truth-axis reflection.
- $-$ swaps $\bot \leftrightarrow \top$, fixes $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$ — the knowledge-axis reflection.
- $\sigma$ is the 90° rotation between them.

Compute $\sigma \circ -$: takes $\bot \to \mathbf{t} \to \top \to \mathbf{f} \to \bot$, a 4-cycle. So $\sigma$ and $-$ generate $D_4$. The Klein four $K_4 = \langle \sim, - \rangle$ (already noted in the corpus) is the normal subgroup of $D_4$ corresponding to the rotations and the two axis reflections; $\sigma$ extends $K_4$ by a diagonal reflection. Equivalently: $\sim = \sigma - \sigma$, so $\sim$ and $-$ are $\sigma$-conjugate.

This gives a cleaner generator presentation than the corpus's existing "8-element extended symmetry group" remark in the [bilattice two-orders duality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/theorems/bilattice-two-orders-duality.md): the group is $D_4$, with $K_4$ as the rotational/axis-reflection subgroup and $\sigma$ as the diagonal generator.

## Combining with the temporal backward direction

Adding $T_k^{-1}$ to the picture is more delicate because $T_k^{-1}$ is multi-valued. So we don't have a group action by extension. But we have:

- *Commutation with σ*: $\sigma_*$ intertwines $T_k$ and $T_t$, hence $T_k^{-1}$ and $T_t^{-1}$.
- *Commutation with $-$*: conflation conjugates $T_k$ to $K_k$, so $T_k^{-1}$ corresponds to $K_k^{-1}$ under $-$. But $K_k$ is deflationary, so $K_k^{-1}$ runs *forward* in $\leq_k$ — making $- \circ T_k^{-1}$ structurally a forward operation in the conflated frame.
- *Iteration vs. composition*: $\sigma$ and $-$ are involutions (order 2). $T_k^{-1}$ is not invertible. Iterating $T_k^{-1}$ goes backward in ordinal time.

A natural object: the **trajectory category** $\mathrm{Traj}(T_k)$ of $T_k$-trajectories with morphisms generated by:
- *forward step* $T_k$,
- *backward step* $T_k^{-1}$ (multi-valued, so a span),
- the involutions $\sigma, -, \sim$ acting on objects.

The $D_4$ acts on $\mathrm{Traj}(T_k)$ by relabeling, conjugating $T_k$ to its $D_4$-conjugates ($T_t = \sigma T_k \sigma$, $K_k = -T_k -$, etc.). Iteration of $T_k^{-1}$ is the "time" direction.

This trajectory category is the candidate setting for a unified theory of backward direction.

## Sub-questions

1. **Construct $\mathrm{Traj}(T_k)$ explicitly.** What are the right morphisms? Spans (because $T_k^{-1}$ is multi-valued)? 2-cells encoding the relationship between forward and backward iterates? This is a category-theoretic design question.

2. **$D_4$-orbits of trajectories.** Group the corpus's trajectory examples by $D_4$-orbit. The current $K_4$-orbit examples ([Klein-four self-duality on named substitutions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/examples/klein-four-self-duality-on-named-substitutions.md)) are within $D_4$; what are the additional $D_4$-orbits coming from $\sigma$?

3. **Cohomology of the trajectory category.** $\mathrm{Traj}(T_k)$ has a natural sheaf structure (cells, firings, ordinal time). Sheaf cohomology over the whole trajectory category — combining forward, backward, and value-symmetry data — is the unified invariant. The settling cohomology $H^*(\mathcal{F}_v)$ already exists; this would extend it.

4. **Conflation as adjoint to closure?** If $K_k$ is the right adjoint of $T_k$ in some natural setting (sub-question 2 of the conflation question), then $D_4 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ (with $\mathbb{Z}/2$ for forward/backward in the $K_k$-cosettling sense) acts on trajectories. This would make the temporal direction also single-valued and canonical, removing the multi-valuedness of $T_k^{-1}$.

5. **Lorentzian benchmark.** On a Lorentzian causal-set settling, all four operations are available: $\sigma$ (knowledge↔truth), $-$ (knowledge axis reversal), $T_k^{-1}$ (ordinal-time reversal), and the Lorentzian cone swap (base-time reversal). The interactions among them — particularly between conflation and the Lorentzian time-reversal — should give a sharp test of whether the corpus's "value time-reversal" matches Lorentzian "physical time-reversal." Concrete prediction: conflation does *not* match the Lorentzian cone swap (different categories), but their composition might give a candidate "CPT-equivalent" symmetry.

## Why it matters

The corpus has been treating "backward direction" as a single open question, but the analysis above shows it splits cleanly into three independent structures plus their interactions. Recognizing the split clarifies:

- $\sigma$ is *cohomologically silent* (proved via the duality theorem) — a structural symmetry without classifying content.
- Conflation is *value-dynamical* — gives a real backward operator $K_k$, single-valued.
- Cosettling is *temporal* — multi-valued, lossy, but the only structure that genuinely reverses ordinal time.

The interesting unifying questions live in the trajectory category, and in whether conflation gives the missing "canonical backward selection" inside the cosettling.
