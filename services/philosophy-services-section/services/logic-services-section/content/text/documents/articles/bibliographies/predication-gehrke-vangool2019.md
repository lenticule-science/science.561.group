## Annotation

The methodological template for the cohomological-layer's site theory.

**Theorem 5.7.** For a distributive lattice $L$, soft sheaf representations of $L$ correspond bijectively to continuous decompositions of $\mathrm{Spec}(L)$ satisfying an interpolation property.

**Theorem 3.10** (more general). For a stably compact space $Y$ (compact, locally compact, coherent), sheaf representations of an algebra $A$ over $Y^\uparrow$ correspond to frame homomorphisms from $(\mathcal{K} Y^\uparrow)^{\mathrm{op}}$ into subframes of pairwise-commuting congruences $\mathrm{Con}\, A$.

For the program: the cohomological layer extends Gehrke–van Gool's single-lattice statement to the 2-categorical assembly over $\mathcal{C}$ and $\lambda$.
