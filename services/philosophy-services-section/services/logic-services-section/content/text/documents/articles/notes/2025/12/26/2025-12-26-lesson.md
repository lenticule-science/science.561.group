## Entry conditions

Use an $\infty$-site only when you can:

- Describe an $\infty$-category of objects and higher [morphisms](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md).
- Define which families of [morphisms](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) count as covers.
- Require higher descent (gluing up to homotopy).

## Definitions

An **$\infty$-site** is an $\infty$-category equipped with a Grothendieck topology, used to define higher sheaves.

## Vocabulary (plain language)

- **Higher [morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md)**: a [morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) between [morphisms](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md), and so on.
- **Higher cover**: a covering family where compatibility is up to homotopy.

## Intuition

An $\infty$-site generalizes a site so that gluing is homotopy-coherent rather than strictly equal.

## Worked example

Sheaves of spaces on a topological space are defined using an $\infty$-site of opens with higher descent.

## How to recognize the structure

- You can specify higher [morphisms](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md).
- You can state a covering rule with homotopy coherence.

## Common mistakes

- Using an $\infty$-site when ordinary sites suffice.
