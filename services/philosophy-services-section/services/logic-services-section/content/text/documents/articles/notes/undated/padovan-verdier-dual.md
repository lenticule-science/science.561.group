# Verdier dual of the Padovan substitution settling

The L5d Verdier-dual instance, parallel to Fibonacci's L5a Verdier dual.

## Setup

The L5d [Padovan substitution settling](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/examples/padovan-substitution-settling.md) at $E = \omega_1$.

## Cellular fiber and Layer 3

The Padovan settling's cellular fiber at the limit is transfinite-cellular with cells indexed by $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)$. For Boolean d-frame data, $q^{\mathrm{transfin}} = 1$ — strict equivalence.

## Verdier identification

By the [aperiodic co-classification theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/theorems/aperiodic-co-classification.md), sheaf cohomology and cosheaf homology agree:

$$H^1(\mathcal{F}_v) \;\simeq\; H_1(\check{\mathcal{F}}_v) \;=\; \mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v_{\text{Padovan}}).$$

After settling-shift quotient: both $\mathbb{Z}^3$.

## Position in Lineage 5d

Same structural pattern as L5a Fibonacci's Verdier dual, applied at $n = 3$. The $\mathbb{Z}^3$ classifying-invariant data is presented from both sheaf and cosheaf sides via Verdier duality.
