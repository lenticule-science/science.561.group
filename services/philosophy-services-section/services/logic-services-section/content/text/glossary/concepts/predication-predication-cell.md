# Predication cell

The role of an *assertion-position* — the location at which a [predication valuation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predication-valuation.md) records evidence about a predicate's instantiation on a pair of entities. Concretely, a predication cell is an ordered triple $\phi = (s, p, o)$ whose first coordinate is an [entity](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/entity.md) (the [subject](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/subject.md)), second is a [predicate](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/predicate.md), and third is an entity (the [object](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/object.md)); but the role's content lies in the *orientation* the triple carries, not in its tuple structure.

## The asymmetry of the role

The predication cell is not a generic relational triple. It is *oriented from subject to object through the predicate*: the cell $(s, p, o)$ asserts something *of $s$ in relation to $o$*, not symmetrically about the pair $\{s, o\}$. Three structural consequences of this orientation distinguish the role from a flat $(\text{node}, \text{label}, \text{node})$ triple of an RDF or property-graph framework:

- **Subject and object positions are not interchangeable.** The cell $(s, p, o)$ and the cell $(o, p, s)$ are *distinct* assertion-positions, with independent values. Their relationship is mediated by the [predicate converse](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md) generator, not by any symmetry on the cell index.
- **The predicate is a typed coordinate, not a label.** Predicates live in a small category $\mathcal{C}$ — the [predicate category](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predicate-category.md) — with morphisms in four classes ([specialization](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-specialization.md), [converse](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md), [subject-projection](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [object-projection](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-object-projection.md)). The predicate coordinate of a cell carries the typing that licenses reindexing along these morphisms; "label" is too thin a word for the role.
- **The entity-pair index is the diagonal of two transversal projections.** Cells of the form $(s, p, s)$ — same entity in both positions — are the fixed points of both projection generators, and the projection morphisms collapse $E \times E$ along the two distinct diagonals. This asymmetry is what makes [n-ary predication](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/n-ary-predication.md) decompose into binary cells with explicit orientation rather than into a symmetric set-of-relations.

This is the orientation that the [cohomology layer](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/prose/teaching/explanations/cohomology-relations-to-prior-work.md) calls "the s→o-oriented predication cell." Stripping the orientation off — treating cells as elements of a generic simplicial complex, or values as held at unoriented vertices — loses exactly the content the program's theorems are about.

## Where values live

A predication cell is *the location at which* a value is held; the value itself lives in the [predication fiber algebra](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_p = \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E}$ at the cell's predicate coordinate. A [predication valuation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predication-valuation.md) $v$ assigns each predicate $p$ an element $v(p) \in A_p$, which evaluates at the entity-pair $(s, o)$ to give the cell's value $v(p)(s, o) \in \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ — a bilattice value tracking [evidence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/evidence.md) for and against the predication. The cell is therefore the *carrier* of the role, not the value: many cells share a fiber algebra (every cell with predicate $p$ draws its value from $A_p$), and many valuations can be defined on the same cell.

## As an element of the cell set

Set-theoretically, a predication cell is an element of the [predication cell set](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-cell-set.md) $\Phi = E \times \Pi \times E$ — the Cartesian product of an [entity set](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/entity-set.md), a [predicate set](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predicate-set.md), and the entity set. The predication cell set is the discrete index over which valuations range; the predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ is the *typed* index that licenses reindexing. The two indices are compatible: a morphism $f : q \to p$ in $\mathcal{C}$ acts on values in $A_p$ to produce values in $A_q$, and at the cell level this is the reindexing that aligns $(s, q, o)$-cells with $(s, p, o)$-cells through the typing on the second coordinate.

## Under settling and adjudication

Across a [predication settling](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/predication-settling.md), each cell carries a *trajectory* in $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ — the sequence of values $(v_\tau(p)(s, o))_{\tau < \lambda}$ as the settling unfolds. [Settling monotonicity](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/settling-monotonicity.md) makes the trajectory $\leq_k$-monotone cellwise, and [closure-drivenness](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/closure-drivenness.md) determines its successor steps from the increment sequence and the closure operator. Cells are therefore the granularity at which [adjudication](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/adjudication.md) happens: every assertion-position in the system has its own evidence-trajectory, its own current state, and its own contribution to the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md). The framework's compositional structure — fibers indexed by predicates, reindexing by the four generators, monotone evolution under closure, and merge across replicas — is what licenses treating each cell as locally adjudicated while the global structure remains coherent.
