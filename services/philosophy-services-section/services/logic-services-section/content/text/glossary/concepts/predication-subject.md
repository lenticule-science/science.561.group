# Subject

The [entity](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/entity.md) occupying the first coordinate of a [predication cell](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/predication-cell.md), denoted $s$. The subject of a cell $(s, p, o)$ is the entity *of which* the predicate $p$ is being asserted; the cell's value $v(p)(s, o) \in A_p$ records predication categories' epistemic state on the proposition "$p$ holds of $s$ in relation to $o$."

The [predicate subject-projection](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md) generator class is named for this coordinate: collapsing $E \times E$ along the diagonal $(s, o) \mapsto (s, s)$ extracts a unary predicate that depends only on the subject. Together with the [object](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/object.md) coordinate, the subject indexes the entity-pair structure $E \times E$ over which fiber algebras are built.
