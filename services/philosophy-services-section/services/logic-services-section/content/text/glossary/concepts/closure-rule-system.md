# Closure-rule system

A *closure-rule system* $\mathcal{S}$ is a finite or countable set of [closure rules](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/hacking/library/closure-rule-system/docs/dev/scraps/closure-rule.md). The **closure operator generated by $\mathcal{S}$** is the least operator $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}} = (\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}_p)_{p \in \mathcal{C}}$ satisfying:

- $v \leq_k \mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}_p(v)$ (inflationary);
- $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}_p \circ \mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}_p = \mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}_p$ (idempotent);
- for every rule $r \in \mathcal{S}$ and every instantiation $\vec{y}$ of $r$'s free variables, the consequent of $r[\vec{y}]$ holds in $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}(v)$ whenever the antecedent of $r[\vec{y}]$ holds in $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}}(v)$.

Existence and uniqueness follow from the standard fixpoint construction: the family of operators satisfying the three conditions is closed under pointwise meet, so the meet is the least.

The [closure-rule language](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/hacking/library/closure-rule-system/docs/dev/scraps/closure-rule-language.md) specifies which formulas $\phi$ are admissible in rule antecedents; the language fragment determines the regime-class of the generated closure operator (strict / Scott-continuous / transfinite-bounded / aperiodic), per the [four-regime dichotomy](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md).

The set $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\mathcal{S})$ of [non-Scott-continuous firing ordinals](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-continuity/content/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal.md) of a system $\mathcal{S}$ — the limit ordinals at which the closure adds content not derivable from any single earlier stage — is the realizability-determining invariant: the [realizability program](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/realizability-program.md) characterizes which subsets of countable limit ordinals arise as $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\mathcal{S})$ for some system $\mathcal{S}$ in a given rule-language fragment.
