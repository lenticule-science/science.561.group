# Entity

An element of an [entity set](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/entity-set.md), denoted $e$. Entities are the individuals or nodes that [predicates](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/predicate.md) discuss.

An entity may occupy the [subject](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/subject.md) or [object](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/object.md) position of a [predication cell](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/from-predicate-category/predication-cell.md).
