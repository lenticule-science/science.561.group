# Aperiodic classifying invariant

The cohomology class in $H^1$ that classifies [aperiodic-regime](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md) [predication settlings](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/objects/predication-settling.md) up to [mutually-locally-derivable](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md) equivalence.

Let $v$ be a aperiodic settling over $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda)$ with [closure ordinal](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/scraps/closure-ordinal.md) $\kappa$ and [increment sequence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/scraps/increment-sequence.md) $U$. The **aperiodic classifying invariant** of $v$ is

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) \;:=\; [\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{F}_U}] \;\in\; H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \kappa}, \mathcal{F}_U),$$

where $\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{F}_U}$ is the canonical *identity class* of the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agentic-relation-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md) — the cohomology class of the identity cochain assigning to each object $(q, \tau, U) \in \mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \kappa}$ the sum of generator elements in $\mathcal{F}_U(q, \tau, U)$ indexed by newly-added designated cells at stage $\tau$.

## Why this lives where it does

[Settling cohomology vanishes above degree 1](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md): all obstruction content sits in $H^0$ and $H^1$. $H^0$ counts converged-state cells (a quantitative datum, not a structural one); $H^1$ carries the obstructions that distinguish structurally inequivalent aperiodic orbits.

For [strict and Scott-continuous regimes](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md) settlings the cohomology stabilizes at the closure ordinal ([settling-locally-constant cohomology](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-locally-constant-cohomology.md)) and $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}$ is forced to be zero. For [aperiodic regime](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/aperiodic-settling.md) the cohomology *does not* stabilize at any countable ordinal — non-Scott-continuous firings at countable limit ordinals continually contribute new $H^1$-generators — and $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}$ can be non-zero.

## Distinguishing power

By the [aperiodic-regime classification](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-classification.md), $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}$ is the *complete* invariant for aperiodic settlings up to MLD: $v \sim_{\mathrm{MLD}} v'$ iff $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) = \mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v')$. Two settlings with the same closure ordinal but structurally different closure rules — so that no invertible [settling-morphism](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/morphisms/settling-morphism.md) exists — have distinct classes.

The [transfinite-induction settling](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/examples/transfinite-induction-settling.md) realizes the canonical "all-ones" diagonal class — see [transfinite-induction classifying invariant](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/references/transfinite-induction-classifying-invariant.md).

## Step-$n$ parameterization

For aperiodic settlings arising from substitution-recurrence rules, the classifying invariant has additional structure refined by the [step-$n$ parameter](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/concepts/step-n-substitution-recurrence.md). When the underlying rule is a [step-$n$ substitution rule](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/scraps/step-n-substitution-rule.md), the classifying invariant — quotiented by the substitution-shift MLD relation — becomes the [step-$n$ classifying invariant](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/scraps/step-n-classifying-invariant.md):

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}^{(n)}(v) \;\in\; \mathbb{Z}^n,$$

with rank exactly $n$ by the [step-$n$ cohomological rank theorem](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/references/step-n-cohomological-rank-theorem.md). The pre-quotient invariant remains uncountable-rank (one generator per firing ordinal, as in the [Fibonacci L5a computation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/docs/dev/examples/fibonacci-classifying-invariant.md)); the post-quotient $\mathbb{Z}^n$ recovers the Anderson-Putnam Čech cohomology and aligns with the bilattice's value-structure at $n = 2, 3, 4$ via the [step-$n$ / bilattice correspondence](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/references/step-n-and-bilattice-correspondence.md).

The aperiodic classifying invariant therefore stratifies, on the substitution-recurrence subset of meta-aperiodic, into a step-$n$-tower of $\mathbb{Z}^n$-valued invariants — predication categories' substitution-recurrence refinement of $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}$.
