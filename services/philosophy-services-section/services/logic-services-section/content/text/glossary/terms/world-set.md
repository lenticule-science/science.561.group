A set, denoted $W$, of *worlds* over which the modal structure on a knowledge presheaf is indexed. The world set may be the [settling bound](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-3/objects/settling-bound.md) itself — that is, $W = \lambda$, taking settling ordinals as worlds — or a separate index set chosen for the modal application.
