# Increment sequence

A family $U = (u_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$ indexed by a [settling bound](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/increment-sequence/docs/dev/scraps/settling-bound.md) $\lambda$, where each $u_\tau$ is a [predication valuation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predication-valuation.md) that is [$\mathcal{C}$-natural](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predicate-category/content/predicate-category-naturality/predicate-category-naturality.md) — that is, $f^*(u_\tau(p)) = u_\tau(q)$ for every morphism $f : q \to p$ in the [predicate category](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/increment-sequence/docs/dev/scraps/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$.

The increment sequence is the input data driving a [closure-driven](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/increment-sequence/docs/dev/concepts/closure-drivenness.md) [predication settling](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-4/articulating/library/aperiodic-articulation-category/library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/library/transfinite-predication-cell/library/potential-transfinite-predication-cell/positing/library/scott-continuous-posit-category/library/scott-continuous-posit-cell/library/potential-scott-continuous-posit-cell/settling/library/finite-settlement-algebra/hacking/library/increment-sequence/docs/dev/scraps/predication-settling.md): each $u_\tau$ supplies the new evidence introduced at settling step $\tau$, joined into the previous settling state and then closed under the closure operator.
