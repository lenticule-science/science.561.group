# Difference comparison morphism

A **difference comparison morphism** compares two valuations or settlings by producing the evidence that separates them under the selected standard.

When the standard is directional and $v \leq_k w$, the morphism returns the [evidence residual](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/code/transformers/evidence-residual.md):

$$\partial_k(v,w).$$

When the inputs are not comparable and the value domain is $\mathbf{FOUR}$, the morphism returns the [FOUR symmetric evidence difference](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/yet-another-unsorted-archive-dump/predication-categories/_archive/bucket-1/code/transformers/four-symmetric-evidence-difference.md):

$$\delta^{\oplus}_k(v,w).$$

For a general bilattice, the existence of a canonical total difference comparison depends on additional structure on the underlying lattice.
