**Kleene's first algebra** $\mathcal{K}_1 = (\omega, \cdot)$ is the PCA with underlying set $\omega = \{0, 1, 2, \ldots\}$ (natural numbers) and application $a \cdot b = \varphi_a(b)$, where $\varphi_a$ is the $a$-th partial recursive function in a fixed standard enumeration (Kleene's $T$-predicate or equivalent).

The combinators $k$ and $s$ exist in $\mathcal{K}_1$: they are the Kleene codes of the constant-function and substitution programs.

## What K₁ is

$\mathcal{K}_1$ is the PCA of Turing computation. Its elements are program codes (Gödel numbers); its application computes the output of running program $a$ on input $b$; non-termination is the undefined case.

It is the **initial Turing-complete PCA**: every Turing-complete PCA $A$ admits an applicative morphism $\mathcal{K}_1 \to A$. See [k1-is-initial-turing-complete-pca](../concepts/k1-is-initial-turing-complete-pca.md).

The Church-Turing thesis is the claim that $\mathcal{K}_1$ is the unique (up to PCA isomorphism) minimal Turing-complete PCA — that the partial recursive functions are exactly the effectively computable partial functions over $\mathbb{N}$.

## The realizability topos it generates

The realizability topos $RT(\mathcal{K}_1) = \mathbf{Eff}$ (the effective topos, Hyland 1982) is the canonical mathematical structure of the theory of discrete deterministic Turing-complete computing. See [eff](eff.md).

## What K₁ is not

$\mathcal{K}_1$ does not model:
- **Continuous computation**: $\mathcal{K}_2$ (Scott's graph model) is needed; it realizes Brouwer's fan functional and other non-recursive total functions.
- **Oracle computation**: $\mathcal{K}_1^{0'}$, $\mathcal{K}_1^{0''}$, etc. extend $\mathcal{K}_1$ with oracles for progressively harder halting problems.
- **Name-passing / interactive computation**: no PCA models nominal sets; the nominal structure requires the Fraenkel-Mostowski topos.

The existence of an applicative morphism $\mathcal{K}_1 \to \mathcal{K}_2$ (every partial recursive function is continuous on Baire space, so $\mathcal{K}_1$-computation embeds into $\mathcal{K}_2$) gives a geometric morphism $\mathbf{Eff} \to RT(\mathcal{K}_2)$ — but no morphism in the reverse direction exists.
