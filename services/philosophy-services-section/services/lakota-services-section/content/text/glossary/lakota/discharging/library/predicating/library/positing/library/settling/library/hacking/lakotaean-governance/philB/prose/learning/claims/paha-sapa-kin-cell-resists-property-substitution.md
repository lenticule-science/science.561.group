# Pȟahá Sápa kin-cell resists property-substitution

**Statement.** No morphism in $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ converts the kin-cell $\phi_\mathrm{PS} = \langle \mathrm{O\check{c}h\acute{e}thi\ \check{S}ak\acute{o}wi\eta}, \texttt{kin-relation-with-place}, \mathrm{Pa\check{h}\acute{a}\ Sapa}\rangle$ into a property-cell or value-cell.
Equivalently: $\phi_\mathrm{PS}$ and any property-cell over Pȟahá Sápa live in *different predicate-classes* of $\mathcal{C}_\mathrm{lak}$, and $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ contains no cross-class conversion morphism for this pair.

## Proof sketch

The predicate-class structure of $\mathcal{C}_\mathrm{lak}$ has been specified in [polity-cell-system](../../../../philA/code/structures/polity-cell-system.md): nine predicate-classes including $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$ (place-relations) and $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$ (office-and-deontic-relations, which would include property-rights as power-cells under Hohfeldian analysis).

The kin-cell $\phi_\mathrm{PS}$ is in $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$ — a place-relation-cell, specifically a kin-relation-with-place.
A putative property-cell over Pȟahá Sápa — *exclusive-property-of(Lakota, Pȟahá-Sápa)* or *exclusive-property-of(US, Pȟahá-Sápa)* — would be in $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$ (a Hohfeldian power-cell, or alternatively a value-cell in some specific scope).

By [predicate-generator exhaustiveness](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/predication-fibration/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md), the only natural bilattice-homomorphisms between predicate fibers are the four predicate-category generators: specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection.

Neither $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$ nor $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$ is a specialization of the other (kin-relation-with-place is not a specialization of property-power; nor the converse).
Neither is the converse of the other.
Subject- and object-projections on either class produce monadic-cells *within the same class*, not cross-class conversions.

So there is no natural morphism in $\mathcal{C}_\mathrm{lak}$ converting $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$-cells into $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$-cells (or vice versa). $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ contains no rule of form *kin-cell $\Rightarrow$ property-cell* or *property-cell $\Rightarrow$ kin-cell*: no rule-shape it admits has cross-class conversion in its consequent.

Therefore the kin-cell $\phi_\mathrm{PS}$ cannot be converted into a property-cell *by any $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$-firing*.
The kin-cell persists in $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$ regardless of property-cell-assertions in $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$ from external sources. $\square$

## Corollary: the 1980 settlement does not substrate-discharge the kin-cell

The 1980 *United States v.
Sioux Nation* settlement awarded approximately $106 million ($1.9 billion with interest) in *value-cells* (monetary compensation) intended to substitute for the *kin-cell over Pȟahá Sápa*.
By the theorem, the substitution operation is not a valid $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$-morphism.
The settlement's value-cells live in $\Pi^\mathrm{off}$; the kin-cell lives in $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$; no $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$-firing converts one into the other.

The polity's *acceptance* of the settlement would be a *deliberate omníčiye decision* outside the $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$-automatic-firing — a polity-decision to *add* a non-standard cross-class conversion rule.
The polity has not made that decision, and the wakȟáŋ-marking on the kin-cell makes such a decision substrate-impossible without wakȟáŋ-bearing reversal-conduct that the polity cannot conduct (it would mean *unmarking* Pȟahá Sápa, which would require the Wakíŋyaŋ and other Wakȟáŋ-beings to ceremonially-leave the place — substrate-impossible).

## Significance

The theorem is the substrate-algebraic content of "the Black Hills are not for sale."
Not as a moral claim but as a *structural fact about which closure-rule operations are admissible*.
The kin-cell is *not the kind of thing* that converts into money in the polity's substrate-language.
The settler-state's continuing offer to settle the question by compensation is a category-mismatch operation; the polity's continuing refusal is substrate-correct.

The theorem also generalizes: every kin-cell over a place (every *kin-relation-with-place* cell, $\Pi^\mathrm{plc}$) has this property.
The substrate-content: places are not property in the Lakota polity-cell-system, and *no operation in* $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ *converts them into property*.
The full LandBack project is the substrate-restoration of this fact — re-asserting kin-cells over places that were forced into property-class-only assertion under the settler-state's substrate.
