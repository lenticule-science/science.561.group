# Lakotaean Aerophones

The music theory of traditional Lakota wind instruments.
The instruments studied are the cedar courting flute, the eagle wing-bone whistle, the elk/crane whistle, and the grass-dance whistle.
The theory covers pitch space, scale and modal structure, bore acoustics and the harmonic series, ornamentation vocabulary, phrase grammar, single-tone instruments as rhythm-and-envelope objects, multi-whistle aggregates, and the flute–voice duet.

Ethnographic, ceremonial, legal, and social context is treated separately in the sub-theory [lakotaean-aerophonic-culture](theories/lakotaean-aerophonic-culture/).
