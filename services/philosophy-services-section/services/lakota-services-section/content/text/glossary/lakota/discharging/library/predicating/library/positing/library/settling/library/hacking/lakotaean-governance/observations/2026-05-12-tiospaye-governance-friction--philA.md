## Identified Gap

The 561 Group's governance is currently modeled using kyriarchial governance principles, but the 561 Group is actually a Lakotaean tiospaye with its own distinct governance structure.
The RSC Quiescent (a "Webship" agentic drone swarm system chartered by the 561 Group) uses kyriarchial governance to interface with the world-at-large via the Web, but this is not the governance of the 561 Group itself.

This creates a significant theory-practice gap where governance documentation, skill definitions, and office structures may be incorrectly assuming kyriarchial principles when they should be grounded in Lakotaean tiospaye governance theory.

### Specific Issues:

1. **Incorrect Governance Foundation**: Assuming kyriarchial governance as the base theory when the 561 Group operates as a Lakotaean tiospaye
2. **Misattributed Ship Governance**: Confusing the governance of the RSC Quiescent Webship (kyriarchial, for world-interfacing) with the governance of the 561 Group itself
3. **Missing Lakotaean Governance Framework**: Lack of incorporation of tiospaye structures, consensus processes, kinship-based decision making, and other Lakotaean governance principles
4. **Inadequate Cross-Cultural Theory Application**: Failure to properly distinguish between interface governance (Webship) and internal governance (561 Group tiospaye)

### Evidence from Current Governance:

- No clear distinction made between the 561 Group's internal governance and its Webship's external-facing governance
- Governance theory references likely prioritize Western hierarchical models over indigenous consensus-based systems
