## Finding

The [predicated-governance](../../theories/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-work/governance/) theory is already richly populated with substrate-algebraic content drawn from Indigenous, kinship-based, and consensus-oriented governance traditions.
The Lakota leaf bootstrap (planned for a future session) will sit cleanly on existing parents; substantial inheritance is available without any need to extend the parent theory first.

## What is *not* a friction

None of the items above represent contradiction with the existing predicated-governance content.
The parent theory's existing absorption of Indigenous governance traditions (Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe, Aboriginal Australian, Ubuntu, ayllu) means the structural patterns are already articulated; the Lakota leaf adds the Oceti Sakowin's specific instance.

## What is potentially a friction (minor)

The only adjustment worth flagging: the predicated-governance treatment of [consensus-closure](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/consensus-closure.md) treats a non-consenting `f` cell as a blocking condition that requires further deliberation.
In Lakota practice, withdrawal is the *resolution* of persistent non-consent — the dissenting tiyospaye or person leaves the populace rather than the council deliberating indefinitely.
This is a structural refinement, not a contradiction: it changes who is in $\mathrm{Pop}_\mathcal{P}$ for purposes of the consensus rule rather than changing the consensus rule itself.
The Lakota leaf will need to introduce a *populace-mutation morphism* (`withdraw-from-populace`?) that the consensus rule then operates on.

This refinement is also relevant for the broader theory: other consensus-with-withdrawal traditions (some Quaker meeting practice, some communalist anarchist tradition, possibly some Anishinaabe practice) may share the structural pattern.
Worth a brief survey at the time of leaf bootstrap.

## Conclusion

Predicated-governance is leaf-ready.
No prerequisite extension of the parent theory is required before predicated-lakotaean-governance can be authored.
The leaf will be substantive (the Lakota content is rich and specific) but the substrate scaffolding is already in place.
