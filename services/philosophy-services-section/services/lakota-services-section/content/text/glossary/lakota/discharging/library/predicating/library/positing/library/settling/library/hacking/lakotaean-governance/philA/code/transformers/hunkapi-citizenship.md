# Huŋkápi as citizenship-installation

The [huŋkápi-as-kin-cell-installation morphism](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/morphisms/hunka-as-kin-cell-installation.md) of predicated-lakotaean-philosophy is the substrate-mechanism by which non-kin become kin.
Applied at the *polity scope*, the same morphism functions as *citizenship-installation*: a person not previously a member of the polity becomes a full member by huŋkápi-adoption, with all the rights and obligations of polity-membership.

## The polity-scope morphism

For a person $a$ (not previously a member of polity $P$) and a sponsoring kin $b \in \mathrm{pop}(P)$:

$$\mathrm{hunkapi\text{-}cit}_{(a, P, b)} : v \;\longmapsto\; v'$$

with effects:

1. $\langle a, \texttt{kin-of}, b\rangle$ settles at $\mathbf{t}$, wakȟáŋ-marked.
2. Per (W1) of $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ (kin-propagation), the kin-cells propagate: $a$ becomes kin-of-kin to $b$'s relatives, and via (W1) to most of $b$'s tiyóšpaye and to portions of the oyate.
3. Per (W3) of $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ (kin-obligation), owes-virtue-to cells install on $a$ toward their new kin and on their new kin toward $a$.
4. $\langle a, \texttt{member-of}, P\rangle$ settles at $\mathbf{t}$ — the polity-membership-cell, which is a *consequence* of the kin-cell-installation rather than a separate cell.
5. $a$'s standing-cells are *fresh* — no prior virtue-standing-history; standing begins to accumulate from $a$'s post-huŋkápi conduct.

## Why this is *not* naturalization

The Western polity's *naturalization* is a contract-style installation: the person undertakes oaths or obligations, signs documents, has the membership-cell installed by polity-decree.
The cells fired are *polity-membership* cells; *kin* is not affected.

The Lakota huŋkápi-citizenship is structurally different:

- The primary cells fired are *kin* cells.
  The polity-membership cell is a *consequence* of kin-installation, not the primary content.
- The morphism requires a *sponsoring kin* — a specific existing polity-member who undertakes the relation.
  There is no "polity sponsors as a corporate body" version.
- The morphism is *wakȟáŋ-bearing* — requires a wičháša wakȟáŋ, the pipe, ceremonial preparation.
  Non-wakȟáŋ-bearing attempts (a verbal "welcome to the people" statement) do not fire the closure-rule.
- The morphism is *irreversible-by-default*.
  Kin-cells, once installed by huŋkápi, are kin-cells in full force.
  They can be broken only by banishment (per [banishment-as-relation-severance](banishment-as-relation-severance.md)) — the same severity as a born-kin can have their kin-cells severed.

## Historical cases

The huŋkápi-citizenship has been used:

- **Joseph Epes Brown** was adopted by Black Elk and given the name *Chanumpa Yuha Mani* (Walking with Sacred Pipe) — see Black Elk's *The Sacred Pipe* (1953).
  Brown became kin to Black Elk's family and substrate-recognized as a member of the Oglala oyate in a specific sub-relational position.
- **Stephen Feraca**, anthropologist, similarly adopted.
- **Lieutenant Colonel John Gibbon** (US Army, 1876), adopted by Cheyenne in the period after Little Bighorn — illustrating the morphism's use in inter-polity diplomacy.
- Many cross-polity adoptions during the Plains-confederation era: Lakota members adopted into Cheyenne tiyóšpaye and vice versa; the morphism extended the confederation-substrate.

## Polity-scope variants

The morphism scales:

- **Person-into-tiyóšpaye**: the standard case.
  A new person becomes member of one tiyóšpaye.
- **Person-into-oyate**: typically through person-into-tiyóšpaye-of-the-oyate.
- **Person-into-confederacy**: through person-into-oyate-of-the-confederacy.
- **Group-into-polity**: in some recorded cases, an entire small band has been adopted into a larger polity through joint-huŋkápi ceremonies.
  The morphism fires kin-cells across the whole group simultaneously.

## The contemporary application

Contemporary applications of the huŋkápi-citizenship morphism:

- **Adoption of non-Lakota children** by Lakota families — the children become full-substrate-kin via huŋkápi-or-equivalent contemporary practice.
- **Honorary citizenship** of non-Lakota persons who have served the polity (allies in treaty-defense, scholars whose work has been substantive-help to the polity, etc.) — contemporary huŋkápi ceremonies are sometimes conducted for such cases.
- **The Native American Church inter-tribal adoption** patterns: members of one tribe being adopted into another tribe's NAC chapter, with the substrate-content paralleling the traditional huŋkápi.

## Distinction from federal tribal-enrollment

The U.S. federal-Indian-law system has *tribal-enrollment* — a statutory mechanism for determining who is a "member" of a federally-recognized tribe.
Enrollment is based on blood-quantum (typically a fraction of Lakota ancestry) plus other criteria.
The substrate fires under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{US-fed}$, not under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$.

The enrollment-cells and the kin-cells coexist in the multi-observer-system.
They typically *correlate* (most enrolled members are huŋkápi-or-born-kin), but they can *diverge*:

- A person huŋkápi-adopted but not blood-related: kin-cell at $\mathbf{t}$, enrollment-cell may or may not be at $\mathbf{t}$ (depends on whether the federal-system recognizes huŋkápi-adoption, which it generally does not).
- A person blood-related but never participating in tiyóšpaye conduct: enrollment-cell may be at $\mathbf{t}$ if blood-quantum threshold is met; kin-cell substantively at lower $\leq_k$-value because the kin-conduct is not firing.

The divergence is one of the substrate-state's accountability-disposition $\top$-residues on the reservation-overlay (cf. [reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure](../../prose/learning/claims/reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure.md)).
Who counts as "Lakota" has different answers in $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ and $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{US-fed}$, and the conflict-of-laws is structural.
