# Wówašake-work

Wówašake-work is the work-class whose cells fire on predicates requiring physical power or skill — hunting, butchering, hide-processing, fighting in zuya, riding, horse-care, tipi-pole-cutting, the construction-work of camp-establishment.
The class is *men's-primary* in the complementary-gendering structure, though many wówašake-w predicates have meaningful women's-primary firing (women hunt small game, do most hide-processing, etc.).

## Cells in the class

$\Pi^\mathrm{wówašake}$ contains:

- *Hunts* (large game, primarily men), *hunts-small-game* (mixed-gender), *butchers*, *processes-game-meat*.
- *Tans-hide*, *finishes-hide* (primarily women — overlap with káğapi).
- *Fights-in-zuya* (men), *counts-coup* (men — see [count-coup](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/morphisms/count-coup.md)).
- *Rides-horse*, *trains-horse*, *cares-for-horse* (men's-primary for war-horses, mixed for general).
- *Cuts-tipi-pole*, *constructs-tipi-frame* (men), *raises-and-erects-tipi* (women), *moves-tipi* (women).
- *Cuts-firewood* (mixed, with children participating), *carries-water* (women's-primary).
- *Makes-bow*, *makes-arrows*, *makes-shield* (men, with káğapi-overlap).

## The hunt-cycle as primary wówašake-w-firing

The buffalo-hunt-cycle (cf. [buffalo-hunt-coordination](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/morphisms/buffalo-hunt-coordination.md)) is the polity's largest periodic wówašake-w firing-event.
The cells fired during a major hunt:

- *Hunts-buffalo* cells across multiple hunters.
- *Butchers-kill* cells, often immediate-after on the hunt-site.
- *Tans-hide* and *processes-meat* cells in the camp following the hunt.
- *Distributes-meat-to* cells across the gift-economy redistribution.

The cycle is scope-bounded (per [akicita-revocation-at-scope-completion](../../../../../work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/predicated-lakotaean-governance/theorems/akicita-revocation-at-scope-completion.md)); the wówašake-w cells fire densely during scope-active and the standing-cells they install on warriors and hunters are durable.

## Capability-installation through apprenticeship

Wówašake-w capability-cells install through apprenticeship-under-kin, parallel to wakȟáŋ-work but without the wakȟáŋ-dream condition.
The chain is *teaches-X-to* relations:

- *Teaches-hunting-to* — typically father to son, uncle to nephew, occasionally elder hunter to younger non-kin.
- *Teaches-horse-care-to* — typically through observation-and-participation from young age.
- *Teaches-hide-tanning-to* — mother to daughter primarily.
- *Teaches-fighting-to* — uncle to nephew (in the *húŋka* uncle-position), father to son, occasionally war-leader to young warrior.

The capability-cell-firing rule:

$$\langle b, \texttt{teaches-}p\texttt{-to}, a\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t} \;\wedge\; \langle a, \texttt{practices-}p\texttt{-with-witnesses}\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t} \;\Longrightarrow\; \langle a, \texttt{has-capability-}p\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t}$$

The teaching-cell and the practice-with-witnesses cell *both* fire before the capability cell installs.
This is the substrate-content of "you learn by doing, with someone older watching."
The witness-step is what fires the capability — *not* a formal certification, but the older-kin's witness-cell-firing.

## Wówašake-w and standing-as-wóohitika

Wówašake-w cells firing on zuya-scoped predicates (*fights-in-zuya*, *counts-coup*) install standing-as-wóohitika (bravery) cells on the agent via (W2) of $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$.
Wówašake-w cells firing on hunt-predicates install standing-as-wówašake (skill/strength) cells.
Wówašake-w cells firing on hide-tanning install standing-as-good-tanner cells.

The naming-of-virtues makes the substrate-record visible: a man called *Wakhúwa* (a great hunter, lit.
"good chaser") is named for his accumulated wówašake-w standing-cells on hunt-predicates.
The naming is a substrate-recognition operation reading off the cell-accumulation.

## Reservation-period attack on wówašake-w

The settler-state attack on wówašake-w was multi-pronged:

- **Buffalo extermination** removed the largest wówašake-w-cell-firing-site, evacuating the structural-substrate of summer wówašake-w work.
- **Disarmament** removed war-related wówašake-w-firing capacity (made-bow, made-arrows, made-shield cells).
- **Confinement** removed wówašake-w around migration (cuts-tipi-pole, raises-tipi-on-new-site, etc.) — the lodges moved less, the wówašake-w-cells fired less.
- **Forced agricultural labor** redirected wówašake-w into a different work-class entirely (settler-state agricultural-work cells under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wage}$).

The contemporary substrate-restoration includes buffalo-restoration (return of the hunt-cell-firing-site), revival of horsemanship traditions (the Big Foot Memorial Riders annually), and traditional-craft revival (bow-and-arrow making, traditional-hide tanning workshops).
