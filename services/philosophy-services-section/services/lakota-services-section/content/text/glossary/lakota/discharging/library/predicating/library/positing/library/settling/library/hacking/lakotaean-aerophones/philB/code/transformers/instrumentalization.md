# instrumentalization

The morphism Voice → Flute sending a vocal courting-song to its cedar-flute realization.

## Definition

The **instrumentalization** morphism is

ι : Voice → Flute

acting on a vocal courting-song V to produce a flute realization F = ι(V).

The morphism is defined on three levels:

1. **Pitch**: V's pitch content is quantized to the flute's pentatonic-minor scale degrees (V's pitch is continuous in principle; the flute's is discrete).
  Quantization is to the nearest scale degree.
2. **Phrase grammar**: preserved structurally — phrase boundaries, phrase shapes, strophic structure.
3. **Linguistic content**: dropped — text is not carried by the flute.
4. **Timbre and ornament**: V's vocal timbre and ornament are replaced by the flute's six-ornament vocabulary (slide, pop, breath-vibrato, sustained-warble, breath-pulse, octave-overblow).

## Properties

- **Structure-preserving on pitch and phrase**: π_S(ι(V)) is determined by the nearest-scale-degree quantization of V's pitch.
- **Information-dropping on text**: text content is not in the image of ι.
- **Information-adding on flute timbre**: the flute's spectral and ornamental palette is added at ι.
- **Not in general injective**: distinct vocal songs with similar pitch content may collapse to the same flute realization (see [is-instrumentalization-injective-on-the-courting-song-corpus](../../questions/is-instrumentalization-injective-on-the-courting-song-corpus.md)).
