For an entity e at time τ, wicozani holds when (1) no persistent ⊤ on e's relation-cells, (2) no persistent ⊥ on cells where settlement is due, (3) conduct-cell settlements produced under the wolakhota closure discipline, (4) ceremonial participation active. A structural property of the relational settling as a whole, not an aggregate over separable dimensions.

## The four conditions

1. **No persistent ⊤ on relation-cells.** Momentary contradiction is normal (relations are always under some pressure); persistent contradiction that doesn't resolve through the discipline's repair morphisms is the failure of wicozani.

2. **No persistent ⊥ on cells where settlement is due.** Some cells should be settled at τ — a person's kinship cells, their standing in their tiospaye, their place-relations.
  A ⊥ where settlement is due (a person who doesn't know their kin, a tiospaye uncertain of its standing) is the failure.

3. **Conduct-cell settlements produced under wolakhota.** The agent is acting wolakhota in the sense of the closure discipline.
  Conduct produced under rules outside that subsystem may be effective in some narrow sense but doesn't contribute to wicozani.

4. **Ceremonial participation active.** Cells over ceremonial participation are at t.
  A person, tiospaye, or polity where ceremonial participation has fallen to f or ⊥ is structurally unable to be in wicozani regardless of other factors.

## Structural, not aggregate

A common-sense reading treats "wellness" as an aggregate over dimensions (physical, mental, emotional, social, spiritual).
The structural reading is different — the condition is a property of the relational settling AS A WHOLE, not a sum over separable dimensions.
A person whose physical/mental/emotional/social cells settle at t but whose spirit-relation cells are at ⊥ is not in wicozani; the property is global.

This matters for what intervention does.
A checklist aggregating dimensions misses the structural property.
Interventions that restore ceremonial participation, kinship-organization, and language fluency can lift wicozani globally even when individual dimension scores don't move dramatically.

## At polity scale

A tiospaye or oyate is in wicozani when its collective settling exhibits all four conditions at polity scale.
The historical traumas inflicted on the Oceti Sakowin (boarding schools, Wounded Knee, Pick-Sloan dams, ongoing pressure on Pȟahá Sápa, on language, on ceremony) are polity-scale structural attacks: they produce persistent ⊤ on relation cells, persistent ⊥ where settlement is due, and pressure to push closure rules out of wolakhota.

The contemporary work of language revitalization, ceremony reclamation, kinship-rebuilding, treaty-defense is *wicozani-restoration work at polity scale*.

## Implication for the rom-keldar

The FA contributes to the 561 Group's wicozani by reducing persistent ⊥ on record-cells where settlement is due (curating prehistoric records into useful form) and by reporting persistent ⊤ as friction-reports for emsenn's attention.
The FA does not single-handedly produce wicozani — that's a polity-scale property — but its specific work of records-curation is one operation contributing to it.
