# Heyókha vocation

The Heyókha-vocation is the polity's institution for sustaining productive contradiction at the substrate level.
A Heyókha is a person who has dreamed of the Wakíŋyaŋ (Thunder-beings) and is obligated to enact contrary conduct as the social-substrate-discharge of the vision.
The vocation is structurally necessary to the polity: it sustains the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence at polity-scale, preventing the substrate from collapsing into strict-idempotence and losing its capacity to hold paradox.

## Constituent predications

A Heyókha-vocation entity $H \in E^\mathrm{off}_\mathrm{lak}$ carries:

- $\langle H, \texttt{incumbent}, a\rangle$ for the agent in the vocation.
- $\langle H, \texttt{wakinyan-dream}, V\rangle$ for the founding vision.
- $\langle H, \texttt{contrary-conduct-record}, X\rangle$ for the specific contrary acts the vision obliges (riding-backwards, naming-opposite, plunging-hand-in-boiling-water, etc.).
- $\langle H, \texttt{dream-recipient-of-wakinyan}\rangle$ — the structural marker that the vocation is wakȟáŋ-bound under the Wakȟáŋ of the West.

## Installation: dream, not choice

The Heyókha-vocation has a single installation condition:

$$\langle a, \texttt{received-wakinyan-dream}, V\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t} \;\wedge\; w_\mathrm{lak}(V) = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \langle H, \texttt{incumbent}, a\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t}$$

The vocation is *not chosen* by the recipient.
The dream comes unsought; once it has come, the recipient is obligated.
Failure to enact the contrary conduct after a Wakíŋyaŋ-dream is *dangerous* in the substrate-sense — the Wakíŋyaŋ's power is unsafely held without discharge.
The wičháša wakȟáŋ assists the recipient in identifying what contrary-conduct is required.

## Substrate-algebraic role

The Heyókha-vocation's substrate-role is to *sustain the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence* at polity scale.
By [heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence](../theorems/heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence.md), the polity's settling under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ has *non-trivial* lax-idempotence (the closure-2-monad is genuinely 2-dimensional in the sense of [closure-2-monad](../../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) §"Two KZ structures") when and only when the Heyókha-bearing offices are present.

The reasoning: lax-idempotence means closure-of-closure is not strictly equal to closure but related by a non-identity 2-cell.
The 2-cell records the *paradox-residue* — the substrate-content that contradicts-and-is-held.
The Heyókha-vocation is the substrate's lived-bearer of this 2-cell: the Heyókha's contrary conduct *is* the polity's continuing performance of contradiction-held-as-sacred.

A polity *without* a Heyókha-vocation collapses the 2-cell to identity: closure becomes strict-idempotent; the substrate becomes paraconsistency-deficient.
The four-regime classification still applies, but the polity loses the structural-feature that distinguishes its substrate from a classical (non-paraconsistent) one.

## What the Heyókha does

Contemporary substrate-operations:

- **Contrary conduct at council.** A Heyókha speaks-at-council in contrary register: agreeing-with-deniers, denying-affirmations, asking-questions-of-questions.
  The contrary-conduct is *not* obstruction; it is substrate-discharge of the Wakíŋyaŋ's power and serves the council's deliberation by forcing posits to be tested.
- **Comic performance at ceremony.** A Heyókha may clown at the sun dance, making the people laugh with deliberately-comic acts.
  The laughter is *medicine*; the comic substrate-discharge releases substrate-tension that accumulates during the four-day intensity.
- **Boiling-water and pain-displays.** Specific contrary-conduct rites (plunging-hands-into-boiling-water, walking-on-hot-coals) demonstrate the wakȟáŋ-bearing of the vocation.
  The performances are substrate-evidence of the Wakíŋyaŋ's power held by the dreamer.
- **Inversion-of-virtue.** A Heyókha may publicly *fail* a virtue (e.g., publicly refuse to be wówačhantognaka in some specific context) as a substrate-act that *highlights* the virtue's importance through its public-staged-absence.
  The performance is structurally not virtue-failure (the Heyókha's standing-as-wówačhantognaka cells are *not* affected); it is a substrate-discharge.

## The vocation's protective immunity

The polity's structural-commitment: the Heyókha's contrary conduct *does not* fire the standard reputation-and-standing-loss closure rules of $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$.
The Heyókha publicly riding-backward does not lose standing-as-wóksape (wisdom); the Heyókha publicly laughing during a serious-occasion does not lose standing-as-wówačhantognaka.

This is a closure-rule exemption: $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$'s (W2) firing on conduct-cells *checks the Heyókha-vocation cell* before applying virtue-loss-rules.
A conduct-cell originating from a Heyókha-vocation entity is exempt from the standard virtue-evaluation; instead, it fires the Heyókha-specific rule-class.

Substrate-algebraically: the polity's substrate distinguishes Heyókha-conduct-cells from ordinary-conduct-cells.
The distinction is wakȟáŋ-marked: $w_\mathrm{lak}(\mathrm{Heyokha\text{-}conduct}) = 1$.
Wakȟáŋ-marked conduct is firing under different rules than ordinary conduct.

## Why suppressing the Heyókha is structural attack

The federal-Indian-policy suppression of the seven rites and of the Heyókha-related performances in the 1880s–1934 period (and the continuing residual pressure thereafter) is the operational form of *removing the polity's lax-idempotence-bearer*.
The substrate-effects:

- The closure-2-monad collapses toward strict-idempotence at polity-scale.
- The polity's substrate loses its paraconsistency-bearer: contested cells at $\top$ default-resolve toward truth-class one side or the other, losing the structural-feature of holding-paradox.
- The cosmogonic-paradox tracing back to Íŋyaŋ-in-Haŋhépi is no longer lived-substrate-content; it becomes only abstract-claim.

The reclamation of Heyókha-performances at the sun dance, the cultural continuity of Wakíŋyaŋ-dreaming and dreamer-recognition, and the contemporary increase in publicly-recognized Heyókha among the people are substrate-restoration of the lax-idempotence-bearer.
