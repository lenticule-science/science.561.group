# White Hat 2012 — Life's Journey—Zuya

**White Hat, Albert, Sr. (2012). *Life's Journey—Zuya: Oral Teachings from Rosebud*.
Edited by John Cunningham.
Salt Lake City: University of Utah Press.**

## Annotation

The companion volume to *Reading and Writing the Lakota Language* ([white-hat1999](white-hat1999.md)).
Where the earlier book taught language alongside worldview, *Life's Journey—Zuya* presents the oral teachings directly: White Hat's transmission of Sicangu Lakota understanding of life-journey, ceremony, family, leadership, and the conduct of being-Lakota.

*Zuya* in the title carries the sense of an organized journey — historically, a war-party expedition, more broadly a purposeful undertaking with companions.
Life as zuya is a Lakota framing of personhood as journey-with-relatives rather than self-actualization.

## Why this is a political reference

White Hat's oral teachings on leadership and council are primary-source material for Lakota political life.
As an elder, language teacher, and ceremonial figure from Rosebud (Sicangu), White Hat held standing to transmit these teachings, and the book preserves them in his own voice (with Cunningham's editorial work).
For the lakotaean-politics subtheory, this is a primary Lakota source on the contemporary form of traditional teachings — including teachings on leadership, conduct, and the proper bearing of one who would be recognized as a naca.
