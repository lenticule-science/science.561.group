# Lakota work-cadence is the annual cycle

**Statement.** The cadence-modal-frame governing work-cell-firing in $\Phi_\mathrm{lak}^\mathrm{work}$ is the [annual-cycle](../../../../../../../../settling/library/hacking/lakotaean-governance/philA/code/structures/annual-cycle.md) $\mathcal{Y}$, in the [cadence-as-modal-frame](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cadence-as-modal-frame.md) sense of predicated-work.
The annual cycle's four ceremonial-and-seasonal markers (Wíiyaȟpeyatuya-wí, Bloketu, Pteȟíŋčhaŋ-wí, Waníyetu) function as the modal-frame accessibility relation determining when specific work-classes are *due* to fire.

## Proof sketch

The cadence-modal-frame structure (cf. predicated-work [cadence-as-modal-frame](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cadence-as-modal-frame.md)) takes a periodic recurrence-pattern and treats it as a modal accessibility relation $R^c$ on the temporal index.
For a work-cell of class $p$ with a natural-cadence-completion window, the modal possibility operator

$$\Diamond_{R^c} \langle a, p, b\rangle = \mathbf{t}$$

evaluates to $\mathbf{t}$ at times $\tau$ within the cadence-window for class $p$.

For the Lakota substrate, the cadence-windows are keyed to the annual-cycle:

| Work-class | Primary annual-cycle window |
|---|---|
| Wówašake-work (hunt) | Bloketu (summer) for big hunts; small hunts year-round |
| Wakȟáŋ-work (sun dance) | Bloketu, at sun-dance gathering |
| Wakȟáŋ-work (vision-quest) | Bloketu primarily, after spring storms (Wakíŋyaŋ-return) |
| Wótakuye-work (food-prep) | All seasons, with pre-Waníyetu storage emphasis in Pteȟíŋčhaŋ-wí |
| Káğapi-work (regalia) | Waníyetu (winter) primarily — long indoor time |
| Wahúŋkpa-work (sun-dance-prep) | Bloketu, before the dance |
| Wahúŋkpa-work (migration) | Wíiyaȟpeyatuya-wí (spring) and Pteȟíŋčhaŋ-wí (autumn) |
| Wahúŋkpa-work (camp-circle) | Bloketu, during gathering |

The modal-frame accessibility relation $R^\mathrm{annual}$ connects temporal-index $\tau$ to the work-classes whose cadence-windows include $\tau$.
For work-class $p$ and time $\tau$:

$$R^\mathrm{annual}(\tau, p) \iff \tau \in \mathrm{cadence\text{-}window}(p)$$

The modal-frame is *cyclic* with period one year.
By [cadence-as-step-shift-rg-flow](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cadence-as-step-shift-rg-flow.md), the cyclic structure produces a renormalization-group-flow on the year-indexed settling.

## Modal non-normality

Per [cadence-modal-non-normality](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cadence-modal-non-normality.md), cadence-modal-frames in the predicated-work setting are non-normal: $\Box_{R^c} u \not\geq_t u$ in general.
Applied to the Lakota cadence: a work that *would normally fire in this window* may not actually fire (delayed by weather, by other ceremony, by death of a key participant); the substrate accommodates the deviation without producing $\top$-residue, *unless* the work was structurally required at that window.

The structural-required cases:

- Sun dance: structurally required at Bloketu.
  A year without sun-dance gathering produces substrate-residue.
- Wanáǧi yuhápi keeping-of-the-soul: structurally required at the one-year anniversary of a death.
  Missing it produces $\top$-residue on the grief-cells.
- Naming-of-child: structurally required within a window after birth.

For these classes, the cadence-modal-frame functions more like a $\Box$-style universal-requirement than a $\Diamond$-style possibility.
The substrate distinguishes them: high-wakȟáŋ-marked cadence-cells are required; ordinary cadence-cells are permitted-with-flexibility.

## The reservation-period cadence disruption

The settler-state's various impositions disrupted the annual-cycle cadence:

- Reservation confinement broke the migration-cadence (Wíiyaȟpeyatuya-wí and Pteȟíŋčhaŋ-wí migrations).
- Buffalo extermination broke the summer-hunt cadence.
- Sun-dance criminalization (1881–1934) broke the Bloketu confederacy-convocation cadence.
- Boarding-school confinement of children broke the Waníyetu winter-teaching cadence.

The substrate-state: the cadence-modal-frame's accessibility relation $R^\mathrm{annual}$ remained installed, but the *firing-of-work-cells at the indicated windows* was prevented by external constraint.
The structural-required cells (sun dance, naming, wanáǧi yuhápi) that could not fire produced accumulated substrate-residue.
The contemporary reclamation is restoration of firing at the windows the cadence-frame still indicates.
