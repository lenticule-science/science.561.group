# Governance-pattern (Lakota)

The Lakota governance-pattern is one fragment in the [governance-pattern](../../teaching/explanations/governance-pattern.md) coproduct of the wider predicated-governance theory.
The pattern's substrate-content:

- **Closure-rule fragment**: $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ (kin-propagation, conduct-evaluation, kin-obligation, witness-bearing, office-installation, withdrawal, restorative).
- **Witness language**: *witnessed-by*, *bears-witness-for*, *kept-by*, *ceremony-conducted-by*, *recounted-at*, *sees-the-coup-of*.
- **Recognition discipline**: *recognizes-as-naca*, *recognizes-as-relative*, *recognizes-as-wakȟáŋ-bearing*, settled via populace's individual recognition-cells aggregating to office-and-relation-cells.

## The pattern's distinctives

Comparing to other governance-patterns in the predicated-governance coproduct:

| Feature | Lakota | Hohfeldian | Military-command | Consensus-closure (general) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary cells | Kin + virtue | Power/duty/privilege/immunity | Command-rank | Decision-quorum |
| Primary morphism | Kin-obligation (W3) | Hohfeldian operations | Chain-of-command modal-composition | Quorum-aggregation |
| Authority source | Virtue-standing | Office-installation | Rank-installation | Voter-aggregation |
| Compliance mechanism | Substrate-recorded standing | Sanction-installation | Hierarchical enforcement | Majority-vote-binding |
| Withdrawal-mechanism | Iyúhá-šni (preserves relation) | Dissent (procedural) | Insubordination (sanctioned) | Minority-bound by vote |
| Surplus-cell firing | Absent | Possible (contract) | Possible | Possible |
| Wakȟáŋ-marking required | Yes for major operations | Not present | Not present | Not present |

The structural distinctives:

1. **Kinship-primary**: The kin-relation predicate-class $\Pi^\mathrm{kin}$ is the substrate-foundation.
  Other patterns build on entity-relations that may or may not be kin; the Lakota pattern's foundation *is* kin.
2. **Virtue-witnessed authority**: Authority is computed from virtue-standing-cell-accumulation, not from formal-position-installation independently of conduct.
3. **No-surplus-cell**: $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ does not fire accumulation-cells (cf. [no-surplus-cell-firing](../../../../../../../predicated-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-politics/theories/predicated-lakotaean-work/theorems/no-surplus-cell-firing.md)).
4. **Iyúhá-šni-availability**: Withdrawal preserves relation; the pattern's compliance does not depend on majority-coercion.
5. **Restorative-not-punitive**: The pattern's response-to-harm is relation-repair-firing (W7), not sanction-installation.
6. **Wakȟáŋ-marking-conditional rule-firing**: Many major operations (treaty, office-installation, ceremony) condition on wakȟáŋ-marking; non-wakȟáŋ-marked attempts do not produce substrate-binding.

## The pattern's cohomological signature

Per [cohomological-signature-of-traditions](../../../theorems/cohomological-signature-of-traditions.md), each governance-pattern has a measurable cohomological signature on its closure-rule fragment.
The Lakota pattern's signature has the following load-bearing features:

- **$H^0(\mathcal{F}^\mathrm{wolak})$**: dominated by kin-relation cells, virtue-standing cells, and place-relation cells.
  Reflects the substrate's primary content.
- **$H^1(\mathcal{F}^\mathrm{wolak})$ in peacetime** (single-pattern firing, no external overlay): low — the strict-or-Scott-continuous regime gives $H^1 = 0$ per [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md).
  Adjudication-mode obligations are low; the pattern produces uniform settling-quiescence.
- **$H^1(\mathcal{F}^\mathrm{wolak})$ under colonial overlay** (the reservation overlap): non-trivial — the BC-failure with $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{US-fed}$ produces overlap-content not in the constituent-pair image (cf. [reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure](../../../theorems/reservation-overlay-as-bc-failure.md)).
  The federation-level $H^1$ class records the *persistent unresolvable* substrate-state of the legal-pluralism crisis.
- **Heyókha-bearing**: a structurally distinct contribution to the cohomological structure via the lax-idempotence 2-cell (cf. [heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence](../../../theorems/heyokha-bearing-preserves-lax-idempotence.md)).
  The 2-cell records the polity's continuing performance of the cosmogonic-paradox.

The signature *distinguishes* the Lakota pattern from the Hohfeldian, military-command, consensus-closure, and other patterns.
This is what makes the patterns measurably-different rather than just terminologically-different.

## Coproduct compatibility with other patterns

Per [tradition-pluralism-as-fragment-coproduct](../../../theorems/tradition-pluralism-as-fragment-coproduct.md), multiple governance-patterns can coexist as fragments in a polity's $\mathcal{S}_\mathcal{P}$ when their compatibility-conditions hold.
The Lakota pattern's coproduct-compatibility:

- **With Cheyenne/Arapaho patterns (Plains-nation confederation)**: largely-compatible.
  The shared structural features (kinship-based, restorative-not-punitive, council-with-withdrawal-availability, wakȟáŋ-bearing ceremony) make the patterns substantially-coproductible.
  The Plains-confederation has historically run these as compatible fragments.
- **With Cheyenne military-society pattern**: partially-compatible.
  The Cheyenne *Dog Soldier* military-society is a more-structured military-command sub-pattern than the Lakota blotáhuŋka.
  In the joint Plains-confederation, the patterns interleave during joint zuya without contradiction.
- **With U.S. federal-Indian-law pattern**: incompatible (per the BC-failure noted above).
  The accountability-disposition cells go to $\top$; resolution-paths are blocked.
  The patterns do *not* form a clean coproduct; their coexistence is forced-overlay, not compatible-coproduct.
- **With Christian missionary pattern** (historical overlay): partially-compatible-by-syncretism.
  Some Lakota practice has blended elements (the Native American Church combining Christian and Lakota substrate); some has refused syncretism.
  The substrate-state varies by tiyóšpaye, by oyate, by family.

The coproduct-compatibility-or-not is a *measurable structural feature*, not a stipulation.
Compatibility means the closure-rule fragments' joint firing produces consistent settlings without $\top$-residue; incompatibility means $\top$-residue accumulates.
