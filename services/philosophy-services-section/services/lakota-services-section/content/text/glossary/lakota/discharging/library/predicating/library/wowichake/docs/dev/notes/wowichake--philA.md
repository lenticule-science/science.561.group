# Wówičhake

Wówičhake — *truth*, *firm-standing*, *the holding-together-truly* — names the achieved coherence of a relational unit: the condition in which acts, conditions, and supporting structures hold together stably.
It is what the polity, the tiyospaye, the relative achieves when their relational substrate is in working order.
In the relational framework, wówičhake is *coherence* — the family-of-conditions whose incompleteness drives the framework's derivation forward.

## The word's range

*Wówičhake* is most commonly glossed as *truth*, but the gloss undersells the relational substance.
The form *wówičhake* is built on *wičháka* — *to be true, to be firm, to stand-as-genuine*.
The wó- nominalizer turns the stative into an abstract noun.
So wówičhake is *the condition of being-firmly-standing*.
Truth in Lakota is not primarily a property of propositions (statement-matches-fact); it is a property of *things-and-relations that hold together properly*.

A relation is wówičhake when it holds.
A council's decision is wówičhake when its grounding and its implementation hold together coherently.
A person is wówičhake when their conduct is firm — when their acts, their relations, and their standing-as-Lakota are in keeping.
A story is wówičhake when its telling holds together with the people's experience and with what they know to be the case.

This is closer to the older English sense of *truth* as *troth*-keeping (covenantal firmness) than to the modern sense of *correspondence-with-fact*.
To be wówičhake is to *stand firm in keeping with* the relational substrate.

## Wówičhake as coherence

The framework's *coherence* names the holding-together of acts, conditions, and structures as a unified whole.
The three components must be present *and* held together:

1. **Acts do the work.**
2. **Conditions are held open.**
3. **Structures stabilize and durably maintain the results.**

Wówičhake is the Lakota form of this holding-together.
A relational unit has wówičhake when:

1. The acts of [woótakuye](wotakuye.md) are being done.
2. The conditions of tȟakúye (kin-link) are being held open.
3. The structures (the polity, the tiyospaye, the ceremonial calendar) are stabilizing the conduct durably.

When all three are present and integrated, the relational unit *stands firm*.
When one fails, the wówičhake fails proportionally: a polity whose ceremonial calendar has been disrupted (acts not done) loses wówičhake at the ceremonial scale; a polity whose tȟakúye-links have been frayed (conditions not held) loses wówičhake at the kinship scale.

## Wówičhake and wicozani

Wówičhake and [wicozani](wicozani.md) are distinct but tightly related:

- **Wówičhake** — the *achieved* coherence; the relational unit *standing firm*.
- **Wicozani** — the *settling* of the relational unit's substrate to its quiescence condition; the *flow-condition* of well-being.

A relational unit can be wówičhake (standing firm in its structure) without yet being in full wicozani (settled to quiescence) — the firmness is the condition for the settling, but settling takes its own time.
Conversely, wicozani depends on wówičhake: a unit cannot settle to quiescence if its underlying substrate is not standing firm.

In contemporary public-health and clinical work with Lakota communities, this distinction matters.
An intervention can restore wówičhake (re-establishing the structural conditions for relational firmness — the ceremonial calendar, the language teaching, the kinship rebuilding) without yet producing wicozani at the population level.
The settling-toward-quiescence may take generations to follow.
But without wówičhake at the structural layer, no amount of dimensional intervention will produce wicozani.

## Wówičhake and the family-of-conditions

The framework names *coherence* as a family-of-conditions rather than a single condition — each more comprehensive than the last, and each's incompleteness driving the next.
Wówičhake admits the same family-structure in Lakota practice:

- **Person-scale wówičhake**: a person's conduct holds firm with the four virtues.
- **Tiyospaye-scale wówičhake**: a tiyospaye's relational structure holds firm.
- **Oyate-scale wówičhake**: an oyate stands firm as a people.
- **Oceti-Sakowin-scale wówičhake**: the confederate Seven-Council-Fires standing firm in their confederation.
- **Cosmogonic wówičhake**: the polity-and-cosmos standing firm together — the human polity in keeping with mitakuye oyasin and with the four superior Wakȟáŋ.

Each scale is wówičhake in its own right, and each scale's wówičhake is *incomplete* without the next scale's wówičhake also being achieved.
A person can stand firm but if the tiyospaye is fractured, the person's standing-firm is precarious.
The polity can stand firm but if its cosmogonic relations are unattended, the polity-firmness is precarious.
The family-of-conditions is structural.

## Wówičhake and yúȟóȟa-šni

Yúȟóȟa-šni — non-breaking under strain — is the *dynamic* aspect; wówičhake is the *condition* aspect.
A relational unit that is yúȟóȟa-šni *is* wówičhake under strain: the firm-standing is being *demonstrated* by the not-breaking.
Outside of strain-conditions, a relational unit may be wówičhake without exhibiting yúȟóȟa-šni in any visible way — the firmness is at rest.
Under strain, yúȟóȟa-šni *is* what wówičhake looks like in action.

## What wówičhake is not

- *Not* propositional truth alone.
  Wówičhake includes the holding-together of acts and relations, not only the agreement of statements with facts.
- *Not* stasis.
  A wówičhake polity is *active*, conducting woótakuye continually; firmness is not stillness.
- *Not* rigidity.
  A wówičhake polity is *adaptive* — capable of holding firm under varying strains, including by absorbing or withdrawing-from (iyúhá-šni) particular engagements that don't keep with the substrate.
- *Not* universal-true.
  Wówičhake is *of-this-relation*, *of-this-unit*; it is relational, not absolute.
