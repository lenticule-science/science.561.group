# Seven rites as witness-bearing morphisms

The [seven rites](../../../../indigenous/theories/lakotaean-philosophy/concepts/seven-rites.md) are realized in the substrate as a family of *witness-bearing morphisms*: each rite, performed under proper conditions, produces a specific witness-increment on specific cells.
The rites are not symbolic actions accompanying a separate political-substrate reality; they are operations *on* the substrate.

## The morphism structure

For each rite $R \in \{\text{inipi}, \text{haŋbléčheyapi}, \text{wanáǧi yuhápi}, \text{wiwáŋyaŋg wačhípi}, \text{huŋkápi}, \text{išnáthi awíčhalowaŋpi}, \text{tȟapá wakȟáyap}\}$, the substrate carries a morphism

$$R : \mathrm{State}_\tau \times \mathrm{ProperConditions}(R) \to \mathrm{State}_{\tau+1}$$

that takes the substrate state at time $\tau$ together with the rite's proper conditions (wičháša wakȟáŋ standing, proper preparation, proper place, proper time, proper participants) and produces a successor state in which specific cells have received witness-bearing increments.

The proper-conditions argument is structural.
A rite-form performed without proper conditions is not the rite-morphism — it is a different morphism (no rite at all, or a morphism on the substrate that fails to fire the wakȟáŋ-bearing closure rule).
The substrate distinguishes the form from the rite.

## Each rite's substrate effect

**Iníkağapi (sweat lodge / inipi).** Witnesses purification cells on participants.
Effect: cells of the form $\langle p, \texttt{purified-of}, x\rangle$ for the matters carried into the lodge (grief, illness, confusion, accumulated burden) receive $\mathbf{t}$ witness-increments.
Closure rules over the participant's substrate that were firing on the carried matters fire to discharge them.

**Haŋbléčheyapi (vision quest).** Witnesses vision-cells on the quester.
Effect: a vision-cell $\langle p, \texttt{given-vision}, V\rangle$ for some vision content $V$ may receive $\mathbf{t}$ — but only if the vision is given; the rite does not guarantee a vision.
The proper conditions include proper preparation and proper place; the witness comes from the spirit-relations to which the quester reaches.

**Wanáǧi yuhápi (keeping of the soul).** Maintains ancestor-relation cells.
Effect: a cell $\langle \text{living family}, \texttt{keeps-relation-with}, \text{the deceased}\rangle$ is kept at $\mathbf{t}$ over the rite's period (typically a year); the rite then concludes with a release-morphism that retires the cell.
The rite is a maintenance-and-release operation, not a one-step.

**Wiwáŋyaŋg wačhípi (sun dance).** Witnesses the people's standing as a people.
Effect: the rite produces $\mathbf{t}$ on the cell $\langle \text{the people}, \texttt{stands-as-people-before}, \text{Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka}\rangle$.
The dancers' offering on behalf of the people produces witnesses on the polity-scale cells of [wicozani](../objects/wicozani-as-quiescence.md) — the polity's settling moves toward quiescence through the rite.

**Huŋkápi (making-of-relatives).** Installs kin-cells across the populace.
See [hunka-as-kin-cell-installation](hunka-as-kin-cell-installation.md).
Effect: cells of the form $\langle p, \texttt{kin-of}, q\rangle$ for the rite's participants receive $\mathbf{t}$, and the closure rules over kin-obligation fire to install the rights and duties of the new kin-relation.

**Išnáthi awíčhalowaŋpi (preparing-of-a-young-woman).** Witnesses the standing-as-woman cells on the young woman.
Effect: the cells over her position in the polity's adult-women-of-the-people group settle at $\mathbf{t}$, with the kin-relations and ceremonial-eligibility cells the new standing entails.

**Tȟapá wakȟáyap (throwing of the ball).** Witnesses the wisdom-coming cells.
The ball — itself wakȟáŋ — is thrown to participants; those who reach for it receive wisdom.
Effect: cells of the form $\langle p, \texttt{reaches-for-wisdom}\rangle$ that settle at $\mathbf{t}$ trigger closure rules producing wisdom-cells.

## Why this is a morphism family, not isolated rites

The rites compose.
A vision received in haŋbléčheyapi is brought into the wiwáŋyaŋg wačhípi as the dancer's offering.
A relation made in huŋkápi participates in the polity's wicozani that the sun dance maintains.
The wanáǧi yuhápi's release closes a mourning period that the wiping-of-tears morphism (related to but distinct from the seven rites) may further repair.
The family operates as a coherent substrate-discipline, not as separable events.

## Why these are wakhan-bearing morphisms

Each rite, properly performed, fires wakȟáŋ-bearing closure rules (see [wakhan-as-cell-quality](../objects/wakhan-as-cell-quality.md)).
The morphism's effect is therefore not merely a settled cell but a *wakȟáŋ-marked settled cell* — the rite-produced witnesses bear the sacred quality, and downstream closure rules that operate on wakȟáŋ-marked cells fire on the rite-produced increments.
The polity's substrate is thereby maintained as wakȟáŋ-bearing across time.
