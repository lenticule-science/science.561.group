# Wicozani as substrate quiescence

[Wicozani](../../../../indigenous/theories/lakotaean-philosophy/concepts/wicozani.md) — well-being, wholeness — is realized in the substrate as a *quiescence condition* on the relational settling under the [wolakȟota closure discipline](wolakota-as-closure-discipline.md).

## The condition

For an entity $e \in E_{\mathrm{lak}}$ (a person, a tiyospaye, an oyate, the Oceti Sakowin) and a time index $\tau$, $e$ is in the wicozani condition at $\tau$ when:

1. **No persistent $\top$ on relation-cells.** The relation cells in which $e$ stands — kin-cells, place-relations, spirit-relations, ancestor-relations — are not in persistent contradiction.
  A momentary $\top$ is normal (relations are always under some pressure); a $\top$ that does not resolve through the discipline's repair morphisms (most prominently the [wiping-of-tears](../morphisms/wiping-of-tears-as-polity-quiescence-restoration.md)) is the failure of wicozani.

2. **No persistent $\bot$ on cells where settlement is due.** Some relation cells should be settled in $e$'s history at $\tau$: cells about kinship to one's parents, about one's standing in one's tiyospaye, about one's place-relations to where one is.
  A $\bot$ on a cell that should be settled (a person who does not know their kin, a tiyospaye uncertain of its standing) is the failure of wicozani.

3. **Conduct-cell settlements are produced under $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$.** The agent is acting wolakȟota in the sense of the [closure discipline](wolakota-as-closure-discipline.md).
  Conduct produced under rules outside the wolakȟota subsystem may be effective in some narrow sense but does not contribute to wicozani.

4. **The ceremonial substrate is active.** The seven rites (or whichever rites are appropriate to the entity's situation) are participated in.
  Cells over ceremonial participation are settled at $\mathbf{t}$.
  A person, tiyospaye, or polity in which ceremonial participation has fallen to $\mathbf{f}$ or $\bot$ is structurally unable to be in the wicozani condition, regardless of other factors.

## Structural property, not aggregate

A common-sense reading of "wellness" treats it as an aggregate over dimensions (physical, mental, emotional, social, spiritual).
The substrate-algebraic content of wicozani is *structural*, not aggregate: the condition is a property of the relational settling as a whole, not a sum over separable dimensions.
A person whose physical, mental, emotional, and social cells all settle at $\mathbf{t}$ but whose spirit-relation cells are at $\bot$ is not in wicozani — the property is global to the relational structure.

This matters for clinical and public-health applications.
An intervention that aggregates well-being on a checklist of dimensions misses the structural property; an intervention that restores ceremonial participation, kinship-organization, and language fluency can lift wicozani globally even when no individual dimension's score moves dramatically.
The substrate-algebraic reading explains why.

## Wicozani at the polity scale

A tiyospaye, oyate, or the Oceti Sakowin as a whole is in wicozani when its collective settling exhibits the four conditions above at the polity scale: no persistent contradiction on the polity's kin and place relations, no persistent unsettlement of polity-constitutive cells, conduct produced under the polity's $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$, ceremonial substrate active.

The historical traumas inflicted on the Oceti Sakowin — most explicitly the boarding-school assimilation project, the massacre at Wounded Knee, the Pick-Sloan dams, the ongoing pressure on Pȟahá Sápa and on language and ceremony — are substrate-level attacks on polity-wicozani: they produce persistent $\top$ on relation cells (the polity's relation to land is in contradiction; the people's relation to their ancestors is in contradiction with the boarding-school dispossession of language and ceremony), persistent $\bot$ on cells where settlement is due, and pressure to push closure rules out of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$.

The contemporary work of language revitalization, ceremony reclamation, kinship-rebuilding, treaty-defense, and political refusal — work the polity is conducting now — is *wicozani-restoration work* at the polity scale.
The substrate machinery names what this work is doing and why no narrower-scope intervention is sufficient: a polity-scale substrate condition is restored only by polity-scale substrate operations.

## Sources

The substrate-algebraic articulation of wicozani as quiescence under the wolakȟota discipline is original to this subtheory.
The substantive content is the Lakota tradition's understanding of wicozani as substantively related health/wholeness, as taught by Albert White Hat Sr. ([white-hat2012](../../../../../sociology/theories/politics/theories/lakotaean-politics/references/white-hat2012.md)) and the wider contemporary Lakota public-health and language-revitalization literature, including the Sicangu Lakota Oyate's *Wicozani* initiative on the Rosebud Reservation.
