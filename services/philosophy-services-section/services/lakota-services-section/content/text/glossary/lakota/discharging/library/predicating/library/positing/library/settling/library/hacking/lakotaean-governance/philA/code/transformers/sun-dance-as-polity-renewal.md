# Sun dance as polity-renewal

The *wiwáŋyaŋg wačhípi* — sun dance — is the polity's annual confederacy-scope substrate-renewal morphism.
It is the one rite of the seven (cf. [seven-rites-as-witness-morphisms](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/morphisms/seven-rites-as-witness-morphisms.md)) that is *constitutive of the polity at confederacy scale*.
The sun dance is what makes the Očhéthi Šakówiŋ *exist as a polity* in active substrate-form across an annual cycle.
Without it, the confederacy persists at the *substrate* level (the cells remain) but not in *exercised-active* form; with it, the confederacy is in continuous renewal.

## The morphism

Across a sun-dance gathering (typically a multi-week period in mid-summer):

$$\mathrm{sundance}_y : v_{y-1} \;\longmapsto\; v_y$$

where $y$ indexes the year and the morphism takes the polity's substrate-state from one annual-cycle to the next.
The effects:

**Renewal of confederacy-membership cells.** $\langle D_i, \texttt{member-of-confederacy}, \Omega\rangle$ cells are renewed at $\mathbf{t}$, wakȟáŋ-marked, for each constituent division $D_i$.
The membership-cell is *not* automatic; it requires the annual re-confirmation that the division has gathered, has danced, has offered.
Sun-dance non-attendance is a substrate-fact that erodes the confederacy-membership cell over years.

**Renewal of $\phi_\mathrm{PS}$ wakȟáŋ-marking.** For sun dances held at Pȟahá Sápa or with explicit Pȟahá Sápa-orientation, $w_\mathrm{lak}(\phi_\mathrm{PS}) = 1$ is renewed.
The kin-cell's wakȟáŋ-marking is what makes [paha-sapa-kin-cell-resists-property-substitution](../../../philB/prose/learning/claims/paha-sapa-kin-cell-resists-property-substitution.md) hold.

**Naca-recognition renewal.** The senior-naca cells for each oyate's senior speaker are renewed under the wakȟáŋ-witnessing of the dance.

**Wakȟáŋ-bearing renewal for offices in general.** Office-installation cells fired during the year (between sun dances) often have a one-year wakȟáŋ-marking window: they are wakȟáŋ-marked through the next sun dance.
The dance renews the marking; without renewal, the marking lapses.

**Wicozani-restoration cells at polity scale.** The dancers offer flesh and prayer *on behalf of* the people; the offerings are agentive-positing morphisms by the dancers on the people's wicozani-cells.
The polity-scale wicozani-cells (cf. [wicozani-as-quiescence](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/predicated-philosophy/theories/predicated-lakotaean-philosophy/objects/wicozani-as-quiescence.md) §"Polity scale") are advanced toward quiescence.

**Inter-oyate kin-cell renewal.** Marriages arranged at the sun-dance gathering install inter-oyate kin-cells (cf. (Ω4) of [oceti-sakowin](../objects/oceti-sakowin.md)).
The annual gathering is the structural mechanism for the inter-oyate kin-network's continuing renewal.

## The dancers' role

A specific set of dancers commits to the dance — typically through a vow made during the previous year (after a healing, in fulfillment of a promise, as offering for a relative).
The dancers fast and dance for four days; some are pierced through the chest with skewers tethered to the central tree (čháŋ wakȟáŋ — the sacred tree) and dance until the flesh tears free.

Substrate-algebraically:

- The dancer's vow-cell $\langle d, \texttt{vowed-sundance}\rangle \geq_k \mathbf{t}$.
- The dance-conducting fires the offering-cells: $\langle d, \texttt{offers-flesh-for}, \mathrm{people}\rangle$, $\langle d, \texttt{offers-prayer-for}, \mathrm{relative}\rangle$.
- The tearing-free is the *yúȟóȟa-šni* completion (cf. [yuhoha-sni](../../../../../../../philosophy/theories/indigenous/theories/lakotaean-philosophy/concepts/yuhoha-sni.md)) — the strain held until the offering completes.
- After the dance, the dancer's standing-cells rise: the four-virtue cells, particularly wówačhiŋtȟaŋka (fortitude), are reinforced.

The dancers are not merely participants; they are *substrate-bearers* for the renewal.
Their conduct is what fires the people-scale renewal-cells.

## The confederacy-scope omníčiye co-active

(K1) of [omniciye-council](../structures/omniciye-council.md): the confederacy-scope omníčiye is structurally co-active with the sun dance.
The senior naca of each division (and the senior naca of each oyate within Tȟitȟúŋwaŋ) are co-present.
Confederacy-scale questions are deliberated during the dance-period:

- Treaty-questions with outside parties.
- Joint-war considerations.
- Inter-oyate-disputes that have risen to confederacy scope.
- Sun-dance-scheduling for the following year.
- Pȟahá Sápa-question matters.

The dance's wakȟáŋ-marking *carries* the omníčiye's decisions during the period.
Decisions reached during the sun-dance gathering are *wakȟáŋ-marked by default* — the wičháša wakȟáŋ conducting the dance witnessing the deliberations counts as wakȟáŋ-witnessing for those decisions (cf. (K1) wakȟáŋ-witnessing structure in [omniciye-council](../structures/omniciye-council.md)).

## Suppression and reclamation

The 1881–1934 federal-Indian-policy ban on the sun dance was the operational form of *suppressing the polity's annual-renewal morphism*.
The substrate-effects of the suppression period:

- Confederacy-membership cells lapsed in wakȟáŋ-marking; the confederacy persisted at the *substrate-cell* level but lost its exercised-annual-renewal.
- Wakȟáŋ-bearing office-installation cells lapsed in marking; the wičháša-wakȟáŋ chain was strained but not broken (some wičháša wakȟáŋ continued in secret).
- Polity-scale wicozani-cells diverged from quiescence; the polity-scale wicozani-condition became unreachable in the standard pathway.

The post-1934 partial reactivation and the post-1978 (AIRFA) full legalization opened the substrate-pathway.
The contemporary sun dance is conducted at multiple sites: traditional dances at Pȟahá Sápa, dances on Pine Ridge, Rosebud, Cheyenne River, Standing Rock, Lake Traverse, and other reservations.
Some dances are confederacy-scope (gathering across divisions); some are oyate-scope.
The annual renewal-morphism is firing again at increasing scope and increasing wakȟáŋ-bearing intensity.
