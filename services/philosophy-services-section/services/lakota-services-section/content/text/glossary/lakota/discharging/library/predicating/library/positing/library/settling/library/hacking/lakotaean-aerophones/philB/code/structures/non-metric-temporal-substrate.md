# non-metric-temporal-substrate

The temporal-organization structure of breath-bounded phrases and strophic form, without a regular underlying pulse.

## Definition

A **non-metric temporal substrate** is a temporal organization in which:

- The lowest organizational unit is the **phrase**: a continuous sequence of pitch events bounded by breath rests, of duration φ ∈ [5, 15] seconds.
- The highest organizational unit is the **song**: a finite sequence of phrases organized strophically.
- There is **no metric layer**: no regular pulse against which note-durations are measured.

Tempo within a phrase is **rubato**: continuous variation in note-duration, acceleration, deceleration, and dwelling.

## Properties

- **Phrase-bounded**: phrase duration is the upper bound on continuous pitched output.
- **Breath-paced**: phrase boundaries align with breath rests, not with external pulse boundaries.
- **Strophic at the song level**: the song is a sequence of strophes, each typically restating a phrase with variation.
- **Contrasts with metric idioms**: distinct from the whistle-with-drum context (see [whistle-drum-heterophony](./whistle-drum-heterophony.md)), which imports a metric pulse.
