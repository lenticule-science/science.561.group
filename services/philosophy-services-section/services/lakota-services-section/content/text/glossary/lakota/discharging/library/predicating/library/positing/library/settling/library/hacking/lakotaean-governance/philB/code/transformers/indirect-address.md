# Indirect-address

The substrate-mechanism by which $a$ fires a cell on $b$ without firing the speech-substrate as $a \to b$ directly.
The firing routes through an intermediary $c$: $a$ speaks to $c$ with $b$ in receive-range, or $a$ speaks to $c$ who later carries the firing to $b$.

## Factorization-form

The indirect-address firing factorizes the speech-substrate firing $a \to b$ as a composite:

$$a \xrightarrow{u_1} c \xrightarrow{u_2} b$$

where $u_1$ is a speech-firing with $c$ as nominal-addressee, and $u_2$ is either: - $b$'s overhearing-firing (when $b$ is in receive-range during $u_1$), or - $c$'s subsequent carrying-firing to $b$ (when $b$ is not present during $u_1$).

The substrate-energy expended on the direct $a$-$b$ relational-cell is absorbed by the $c$-mediation.
The kin-cells $\rho_{a,b}$ are still fired (via the $u_2$ completion) but at lower direct-cell-energy.

## Substrate-context for indirect-mode

Closure-rule W4 (kin-context dependent firing-mode selection) determines when indirect-address is substrate-required vs substrate-permitted vs substrate-malformed:

**Indirect-required contexts**: - High wakȟáŋ-marking on $\phi$: direct firing on wakȟáŋ-cells expends substrate-capacity disproportionately and risks malformation. - Avoidance-relation pairs $(a, b)$: certain kin-relations carry avoidance-marking; the substrate forbids direct $a \to b$ firing under these relations. - High relational-tension between $a$ and $b$: direct firing risks escalation; indirect-mode absorbs the tension into the $c$-mediation.

**Indirect-permitted contexts**: - Children-in-presence: indirect-mode supports children's substrate-development by allowing them to receive substrate-content through overhearing rather than direct-address (children's substrate fires receive-side cells under different wakȟáŋ-marking than adults'). - Routine substrate-update: when the firing's content is not high-energy, indirect-mode is the substrate's default-economy choice.

**Direct-permitted contexts**: - Joking-relation pairs $(a, b)$: certain kin-relations carry joking-marking; the substrate permits and even structures direct firing under specific high-energy modes. - Urgent-substrate-update: when delay through intermediary would produce substrate-decay or harm-cell-firing, direct-mode is permitted.

## Substrate-pace implications

The indirect-mode's $c$-mediation introduces *substrate-time* between firing-initiation and firing-completion.
The substrate-pace of polity-update under indirect-address is intrinsically slower than under direct-address.
This is not inefficiency; it is the substrate's natural-pace for high-energy or high-wakȟáŋ-marking firings — the substrate-time allows the substrate-state to compute toward proper-firing before the receive-side update fires.
