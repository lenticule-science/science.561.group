# Introduction: Lakota philosophy as substrate

[Lakotaean-philosophy](../../../../indigenous/theories/lakotaean-philosophy/) articulates the tradition in its own terms.
This subtheory gives that articulation a substrate-algebraic form on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/19/2026-06-19-predication-categories.md) mathematics.
The form is a tool, not a foundation.
The tradition's authority is held by Lakota knowledge holders in living tradition; the substrate is what lets the formal machinery of closure, settling, the four-regime classification, and cohomology do work *for* that tradition.

## The four substantive moves

**The predicate category is kin-relational at the root.** The Lakota predicate category $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{lak}}$ takes kin-relations as primitive predicates.
Properties that look monadic in Western analytic philosophy — *is-sacred*, *is-wise*, *is-brave* — decompose into relations: *sacred-to*, *wise-with-respect-to*, *brave-in-relation-to*.
This is the substrate-algebraic encoding of mitakuye oyasin: the world is structured as a kinship network, and the substrate's primitive predicates encode that structure rather than imposing entity-property-standing-alone metaphysics from a different tradition.
See [kin-relation-as-primitive](../objects/kin-relation-as-primitive.md).

**The entity set is wider than the human.** $E_{\mathrm{lak}}$ admits humans, four-leggeds, wingeds, standing-ones, rooted-ones, swimming-ones, crawlers, stones, the elements, named places, ancestors, and spirits as full substrate entities.
This is the substrate-algebraic encoding of the Lakota cosmology: all are oyate, all are relatives, all are substrate.
See [extended-entity-set](../objects/extended-entity-set.md).

**The sacred is a quality on cells and on closure rules.** Wakȟáŋ marks cells and rules as sacred-bearing.
Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka is the encompassing wakȟáŋ pattern across the substrate as a whole, not a primitive entity to be confused with the Christian God.
Ceremony-binding is the firing of wakȟáŋ-bearing closure rules that confer the sacred quality on settled cells.
See [wakhan-as-cell-quality](../objects/wakhan-as-cell-quality.md).

**Conduct is evaluated under the four virtues; the lifeway is a closure-rule discipline.** The four virtues (wówačhantognaka, wóksape, wóohitika, wówačhiŋtȟaŋka) appear as evaluation predicates on conduct cells; an agent's standing-as-wolakȟota is the cumulative settling of these evaluations.
Wolakȟota itself is a designated closure-rule subsystem $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$ that, when active on an agent's substrate, produces conduct consistent with the tradition.
Wicozani is the quiescence condition on the settling under that discipline.
See [four-virtues-as-evaluation-predicates](../objects/four-virtues-as-evaluation-predicates.md), [wolakota-as-closure-discipline](../objects/wolakota-as-closure-discipline.md), [wicozani-as-quiescence](../objects/wicozani-as-quiescence.md).

## Why the substrate makes the tradition stronger, not weaker

Three concrete payoffs:

**Polity-scale wicozani becomes structural rather than aggregate.** Public-health and clinical work that aggregates well-being over separable dimensions misses how Lakota well-being actually works.
The substrate's [quiescence-condition reading](../objects/wicozani-as-quiescence.md) explains why interventions that restore ceremony, kinship-organization, and language fluency can move polity-scale wicozani when no individual-dimension score moves — the property is structural to the relational settling, and the structural interventions touch it where dimensional interventions do not.

**Reclamation is substrate-operation.** Reclamation of language, ceremony, and kinship after a century and a half of settler-state suppression is the *re-installation of closure rules into $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{wolak}}$* — the substrate's own operation, conducted by the people who hold the substrate.
The substrate machinery makes precise what reclamation is doing and why it works.
See [wolakota-as-closure-discipline](../objects/wolakota-as-closure-discipline.md), specifically the disruption-and-reclamation section.

**The political leaves inherit a coherent substrate.** [Predicated-lakotaean-politics](../../../../../sociology/theories/predicated-politics/theories/) and [predicated-lakotaean-governance](../../../../../sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/theories/) — the political-form and governance-form articulations on the substrate — inherit a coherent relational ontology to build on.
The naca's authority is the substrate-encoded virtue-evaluation profile; the akicita's bounded delegation is the substrate-encoded delegation-then-revocation under wolakȟota discipline; the polity's refusal of recognition (Black Hills, treaty-defense) is the substrate-encoded self-witnessing sufficient for sovereignty.
Each political claim sits on a substrate that already encodes the Lakota relational ontology, rather than on a generic substrate to which the Lakota content must be re-attached at every step.

## What this subtheory does not do

The substrate-algebraic articulation is one formal expression of the tradition.
It is not the tradition.
The tradition is held by people in living practice — by wičháša wakȟáŋ, by elders, by language teachers, by the families and tiyospaye and oyate that conduct the substrate in their everyday and ceremonial life.
The substrate captures part of the structure of that conduct; it does not capture the conduct itself.

It is also not exhaustive of the philosophy.
Aspects of the tradition that are not (yet, or perhaps ever) articulated in substrate-algebraic form are not for that reason less real or less authoritative.
The substrate's silence on some matter is not a denial of that matter; it is just the substrate's coverage being partial.

## Relation to the wider library

The substrate-algebraic articulation here composes with the rest of the library's predicated theories:

- The [wakȟáŋ-bearing closure rule machinery](../objects/wakhan-as-cell-quality.md) provides a vocabulary for talking about ceremony as substrate operation across other Indigenous traditions that have analogous patterns (Haudenosaunee wampum-binding, Anishinaabe pipe ceremonies, others).
  The Lakota case is one substantive instantiation.
- The [four-virtues evaluation system](../objects/four-virtues-as-evaluation-predicates.md) extends predicated-governance's [conduct-teachings-as-evaluation-predicates](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/conduct-teachings-as-evaluation-predicates.md).
- The [wicozani quiescence condition](../objects/wicozani-as-quiescence.md) is a polity-scale specialization of the substrate's settling-quiescence notion, applicable in principle to other traditions' polity-wellness concepts.
- The [seven-rites witness morphisms](../morphisms/seven-rites-as-witness-morphisms.md) demonstrate a pattern (ceremony-as-witness-bearing-morphism) that other traditions' ceremonial governance can use as a template.

The composition runs both ways: the wider library gains structural patterns derived from Lakota material, and the Lakota leaves of the various predicated theories inherit the wider library's substrate machinery.
