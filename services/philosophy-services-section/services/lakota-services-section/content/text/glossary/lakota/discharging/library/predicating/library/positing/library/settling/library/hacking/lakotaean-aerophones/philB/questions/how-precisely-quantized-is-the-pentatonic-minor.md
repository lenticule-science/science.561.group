# Question. How precisely quantized is the cedar flute's pentatonic minor scale to 12-TET?

The interval signature ⟨3, 2, 2, 3, 2⟩ is stated in 12-tone-equal-temperament semitones, but the cedar flute is not constructed to a temperament.
Hole placement is calibrated by ear, by finger-width, or by maker tradition.

Open: do measured flutes in the Lakotaean source corpus realize 12-TET pentatonic intervals exactly, or do they realize a just-intonation pentatonic (6:5, 9:8, 9:8, 6:5, 9:8), or do they realize a tradition-specific tuning that is neither equal nor just?

Sub-survey needed: acoustic measurement of fundamentals and finger-hole intervals on a representative set of Densmore-era flutes and contemporary Lakotaean-tradition flutes; comparison to 12-TET and just-intonation predictions.

If the answer is "tradition-specific," that tuning is itself a music-theoretic object deserving its own structure file.
