# Theology

Theology is the disciplined study of the divine and of the relations —
worship, grace, judgment, hope, abandonment — that traditions hold to
obtain between the divine and creaturely life. It proceeds by close
argument from authoritative texts, reasoned reflection on doctrine,
and sustained attention to practice, and it has, across the scholastic,
patristic, Jewish, and Islamic traditions, produced some of the most
exact vocabulary available anywhere for states — hope, despair,
acedia, dereliction, self-abandonment — that secular idiom names only
loosely.
