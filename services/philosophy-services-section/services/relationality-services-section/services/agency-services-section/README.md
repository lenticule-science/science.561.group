# Agency Services Section

The Agency Services Section is the Lenticule Science home for agency: agents
and their activities as an object of scientific study within relationality.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

Social science sits directly under `science-services-section/services/` as a
peer scientific domain. Relations between social-scientific and agency work
remain conceptual relations, not directory ownership.

Founded 2026-08-21. Paired with engineering's
`agent-computer-system-services-section` (nine verb packages: `relating`,
`animating`, `communicating`, `knowing`, `playing`, `computing`, `gaming`,
`socializing`, `organizing`), science-side at
`engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-services-section/`.
