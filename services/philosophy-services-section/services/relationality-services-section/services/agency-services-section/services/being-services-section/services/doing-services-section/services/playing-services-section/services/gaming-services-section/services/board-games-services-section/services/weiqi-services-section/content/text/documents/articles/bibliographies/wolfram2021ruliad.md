## Why this is central

Wolfram's names for the objects this section reached from the other side.
*Ruliology* is the pure study of what rules do. A *multiway system* follows
every possible update of a rule at once, so histories branch; a *rulial
multiway system* follows every possible rule as well, and a slice of it is
*rulial space*, "a space in which different branches can correspond not
just to different histories, but to different rules for history." The
*ruliad* is "the entangled limit of all possible computations," what is
generated by carrying out all possible rules in every possible way. An
*observer* is part of the multiway system, a subgraph spread across
branches, and what it perceives is a sampling of the whole.

Read against the house: a multiway system is a prime event structure with
conflict (branches) and independence (commuting moves), the arena of the
2026-07-07 arena paper; rulial space is the space of rulesets the weiqi
kernel already carries as data and the space of strategies read as an
arena of its own; the observer is the reader, the whole-board reader
office seated 2026-09-04; and *causal invariance*, that all branches yield
the same causal graph, is the arena's law that independent moves commute
(the CALM shape, `Obsmetrologia/Cohomology/CRDTCohomologicalCALM.lean`).
The section's rule-space article states which of these are identities and
which are correspondences.

## What it does not settle

Wolfram's substrate is hypergraph rewriting; the house's is sheaf and
bilattice settlement over event structures. The bridge is the theorem that
multiway rewriting systems are event structures, which is in the
Gorard–Arsiwalla line ([arsiwalla2022homotopies](arsiwalla2022homotopies.md)), not in this essay.
