A handicap in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is a rule adjustment, usually extra Black stones placed before play begins, that lets players of unequal strength compete in a meaningful game [@britgo2026whatisgo; @britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Handicap is one reason weiqi supports play across large skill differences without becoming a different game. The stronger player does not receive more pieces or special powers during play. Instead, the weaker player begins from a materially stronger board position.

## Related terms

- [komi](./komi.md) - Komi balances even games, while handicap balances unequal players.
- [territory](./territory.md) - Handicap stones reshape the territorial balance from the first move.
- [connection](./connection.md) - Handicap stones are useful only if the weaker player can connect them into efficient whole-board structure.
