## The idea

Voidfleet Academy: the formal training and certification system for officers. Not just "dispatch an agent with a prompt" but a structured progression:

1. **Curriculum as duties** — each office has required duties. Duties define what an officer must be able to do. Training = demonstrating capability on each duty.

2. **Training as transactions** — when an officer completes a training exercise, that's a transaction: effort debits the training budget, competency credits the officer's capability account. Failed training debits more but credits the learning system.

3. **Certification as balance** — an officer is "certified" for a duty when their competency balance exceeds a threshold. The books record the evidence.

4. **The ECC continuous learning loop** — observer detects patterns → instincts form → instincts mature into skills → skills become duty training material. The learning system feeds the academy.

5. **Grounded in the Void** — "Voidfleet" (like Starfleet but grounded in Void philosophy from the relational-hyperverse voidmath). The Void is the empty history (Thing F1.3 in finite voidmath) — the ground term from which all structure grows. Officers grow from the Void upward through the nuclear quartet stages (Object → Quality → Relation → Act).

Discussed with a human officer (outranks first officer). This is a directive to develop, not just an idea to record.
