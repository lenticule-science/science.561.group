A liberty in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is an empty intersection directly adjacent to a stone or connected group of stones [@nagofed2026thisisgo]. Liberties determine whether a group remains on the board. When the last liberty is removed, the group is subject to [capture](./capture.md).

Liberties matter because they convert board geometry into tactical pressure. A move may look quiet in territorial terms while still being urgent if it reduces a group from two liberties to one. This is why local fights in weiqi often turn on counting available adjacent points rather than on the nominal size of a group.

## Related terms

- [atari](./atari.md) - Atari is the urgent state in which only one liberty remains.
- [capture](./capture.md) - Capture happens when a stone or group loses its final liberty.
- [connection](./connection.md) - Connections matter because they let stones share liberties.
- [territory](./territory.md) - Securing territory often depends on keeping enough liberties to prevent invasion and capture.
- [ko](./ko.md) - Ko can arise when a single-stone capture would otherwise repeat because liberties are exchanged back and forth.
