# Playing Services Section

The Playing Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for play as the
link between doing and gaming in the relationality taxonomy.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

The glossary carries the activity's own vocabulary — `play`, `playing`,
`lusory-attitude` — with the topic at
`content/text/glossary/topics/playing.md` stating the scope. The formal
treatment (playing as the two-party interaction faculty, Girard's cut) is
derived in the computer-science section's 2026-06-21 note and implemented
as `@red-cup-engineering/agentic-play-engine` on the registry; the cultural theory of
play (Huizinga, Caillois, Sutton-Smith) is social-theory custody at the
ludics-science section's `game-ludics-services-section`. Structured play
— games and their traditions — continues below at
`services/gaming-services-section/`.
