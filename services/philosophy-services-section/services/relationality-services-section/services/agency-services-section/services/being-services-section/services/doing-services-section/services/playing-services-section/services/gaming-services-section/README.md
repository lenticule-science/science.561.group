# Gaming Services Section

The Gaming Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for gaming —
structured play: games, their analytic vocabulary, and their traditions.
It sits under playing in the relationality taxonomy; the unstructured
activity (play, the lusory attitude) is the parent section's custody.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`: the glossary
(topic `games`, terms from `game` and `gaming` through `rules`, `player`,
`game-state`, `mechanic`, `turn`, `win-condition`, `house-rule`), dated
research notes (the ludic-theory frontier surveys of 2026-06-26, the
design notes of the April games campaign), and teaching articles (the
games curriculum beginning at
`content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/reference-on-game.md`).
Domain ontology lives at `data/ontology/games/`.

Traditions continue under `services/`: board games (including weiqi and
its kernel/LSP apps), card games, dice games, role-playing games (with
tabletop RPGs beneath), and gambling. The Group's formal treatment of
gaming — concurrent game systems, strategies, ludics — is engineering
custody, published as `@red-cup-engineering/agentic-game-engine`; its proof root is
the math section's Lean locus
`Obsmetrologia.GameTheory.VerificationTournament` (Nash stability bundled
with survivor correspondence); the cultural theory of play is
social-theory custody at the ludics-science section's
`game-ludics-services-section`.
