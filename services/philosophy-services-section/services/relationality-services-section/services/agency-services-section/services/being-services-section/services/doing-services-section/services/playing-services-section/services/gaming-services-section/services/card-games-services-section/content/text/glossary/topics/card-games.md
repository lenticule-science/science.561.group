Card games gathers the rule systems built around a bounded, orientable deck
of information-carrying cards: the shared vocabulary of shuffling, dealing,
hands, tricks, melds, and trumps that lets trick-taking, shedding, matching,
and betting games be compared as variations on the same structural family.
It sits under [games](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/topics/games.md)
as the card-medium branch, distinct from
[tabletop role-playing games](../../../../../role-playing-games-services-section/services/tabletop-role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/tabletop-role-playing-games.md)
and other gaming subtopics that share the parent but not the medium.

The full structural specification — constitutive and non-constitutive
features, boundary cases, and family subtopics — lives in the section's
note:
[Card Games Specification](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/03/06/2026-03-06-card-games.md).
Historical and genealogical treatments live alongside it:
[History of Card Games](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/12/31/2025-12-31-card-games-history.md)
and
[Genealogy of Card Games](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/12/31/2025-12-31-card-games-genealogy.md).
