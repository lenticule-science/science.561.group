Life and death in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is the analysis of whether a group can secure enough stable shape, usually through [eyes](./eye.md), to avoid [capture](./capture.md), or whether the opponent can force it off the board [@britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Life-and-death reading is one of the game's central skills because local shape decides global outcomes. A large territorial framework can collapse if one key group dies. A small group can become strategically decisive if it lives inside the opponent's area.

## Related terms

- [eye](./eye.md) - Eyes are the main structural feature used to determine whether a group lives.
- [connection](./connection.md) - Connected stones defend and build eye shape more effectively than scattered stones.
- [atari](./atari.md) - Atari is the immediate tactical pressure that often precedes life-and-death resolution.
