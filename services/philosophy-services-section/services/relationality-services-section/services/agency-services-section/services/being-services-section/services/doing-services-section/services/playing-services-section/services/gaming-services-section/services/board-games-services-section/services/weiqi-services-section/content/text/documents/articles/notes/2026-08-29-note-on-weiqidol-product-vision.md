## WXWQ / Wu(x)weiqi — October 14, 2026 continuation of this original vision

**Leader's direction, recovering rather than superseding the August 29 source:** Wu(x)weiqi / Wuxweiqi / WXWQ reintroduces Go moves as **tactics read within strategies**, and strategies as a fictional world of agentic characters inhabiting sects, teaching, challenging and recruiting through recognizable Weiqi lineages. *WeiqIdol* is the existing idol-wuxia product hypothesis; WXWQ is an additional working program/title and may coexist. Neither label is a verified brand clearance or a guarantee that a Chinese/Japanese/Korean historical tradition has been represented.

**A substantive intellectual progression:**

1. **Accepted event:** One legal Go move, color, ruleset, known history, stones/captures and source-identified semantic observation. A tactical invitation from a captured group is grounded in precisely this fact; a legal alternative can re-create or refute its target under the same rules.
2. **Tactic under counterplay:** A move is a tactic when the position, the opponent's threatening replies and the relevant local objective support that reading. A semantic event such as capture, simple-eye completion or giving atari is evidence of a *local effect*, not by itself evidence of good strategy.
3. **Strategy:** Global objective, initiative/sente, influence/territory, tenuki, timing and risk are readings of *sequences, alternatives, constraints and actual opposing decisions*. Distinguish a rhetorical character doctrine from a warranted position evaluation. Derive compatible formal meanings and tests under Obsmetrologia and the existing SCA/SEN/SEAVT language work rather than using labels as ungrounded strength scores.
4. **Fictional sect and character lineage:** A sect's strategy doctrine is authored IP, its human/agent practitioners have distinct actor identities and optional affiliative history, and an invitation changes playable missions, rivalries, lesson access or narrative consequences. Let sects notice *evidence* in a player's recorded actions, offer a contradictory doctrine or send the player to a rival. Membership is an actual elected relationship, not automatically granted by a score. Red Cup Cast/Runner and Qwzspace are incumbent agent/presentation suppliers, not things to rewrite in a downstream web page.
5. **Teaching lineage and documented real-world traditions:** Real teacher→student lineage comes only from actual consented instruction (see the existing Red Cup **Lineage and Clubs** specification); **historical playing traditions** are a separate source-based education corpus. We can teach Japanese, Korean, Chinese and much more specific named-player/school histories through their original languages, contextual matches, openings, joseki and tactics, but we must research and source these lessons rather than classify a person's nationality or expertise from one game. The player may choose the historical or language-study route through encounters with a fictional visitor.

**Actual present fabrication rung (2026-10-14):** Red Cup's original Weiqi engine now exports a deterministic [planTacticalTryouts](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/blob/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section/services/weiqi-engine/src/tactics.mjs). It takes accepted Study frames, isolates actual Black/White source moves, and offers event-verified one-move tryouts with valid replay positions. The incumbent Play and Study pages carry visible visitor invitations and their original engine-backed branch and glossary path. This **does not yet** constitute persistent NPC agents, reciprocal sect relationships, AI-authored episodic characters, historical-style recognition, richer strategic evaluation, source-qualified culture curricula, native end-to-end tests or a public deployment. Retain those as substantive work, not simulated UI success. Reuse the existing eight authored Opponent Cast personalities and the full WeiqIdol noodle-bar/recruitment vision rather than opening a rival game.

**Commercial relation:** An interactive character world could sell narrative seasons, rival campaigns, creator/teacher content and premium clubs; teaching creates separate retail, institutional, language study, real mentor and certification/assessment *opportunities*. The actual seller and price choices belong to the corrected [Causeway Public Games portfolio](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/blob/main/services/public-games-services-section/README.md), not Science speculation. No "$2.99 first sale" condition precedes WXWQ research or gameplay. Each offer still needs real rights, paid receiving and net contribution. One match may generate educational, entertainment and media products without duplicating its Go record.

**Research and disconfirmation owed:** Differentiate historical claims from fictional schools; model at least two counterposed lines under consistent Go rules before attributing a strategic doctrine; analyze teaching outcomes against unseen games rather than counting animation; study culture-specific written Go source and named historical figures before public claims. An engine-detected atari can support a playable challenge, but cannot establish a learner is "Korean-style", a winning strategy, or an actually enrolled sect follower. These are the claims for Lenticule to test, not excuses to postpone rich actual gameplay.

### Proposed path-category semantics for Wu(x)weiqi tactics and sects — law versus fiction

**Status: formal research candidate under Lenticule Science, not a completed Obsmetrologia or Lean theorem, software design admission, cultural authentication or market result.** This is a precise model by which to *test* the Leader's observation that nearly every work/game noun and verb denotes an existing structure, type, relation or morphism. Recover actual owner vocabulary before mapping its elements; never assert that a user utterance became a formal law merely by labeling it a functor.

**Move objects and arrows.** Fix the *actual* accepted Weiqi configuration `c = (board size, ruleset, handicap and inherited ko/scoring law)`. Let `G_c` be the directed graph whose vertices are **fully accepted rules-session histories**, not bare stone arrays: include the entire commanded prefix, next color, ko/superko-relevant positions, active counting window and result. An edge `h -[m]-> h'` exists *only* if Red Cup's one `admitWeiqiGameCommand(h, h.version, m)` accepts the command under `c`. A refused suicide, superko, out-of-range move, post-result command or invalid dead-group agreement has **no such edge**. The graph's free path category `Path(G_c)` has these source-verified histories as objects and composable finite legal-move paths as arrows; identity is the empty path and composition is concatenation at exactly matching endpoints. Associativity and identities follow for the free-path construction at the mathematical-definition level, but any claimed correspondence to Red Cup's concrete implementation requires a separately executed/reproduced conformance assay and relevant proof in the actual Science/Obsmetrologia owner. Different histories reaching the same visible board remain *different objects* when their move/ko provenance differs. There is no general inverse move and no assertion that concurrent Go moves commute.

**An actor-bound match is a different category candidate.** In a real two-principal match, the additional source object carries authenticated player identity, ordered Cartridge entries, a **match revision** and possibly a disputed `pendingSettlement`. One participant's settlement *proposal* is an accepted actor-attributed log transition that advances `match.revision` even while `session.version` and the board stay unchanged. A forgetful *candidate* projection from the actor/log paths to ordinary Go rules-session paths sends that offer to a session identity (the eventual joint agreement to a genuine `settle` move), but collapses distinctions and is not faithful. Its preservation of identities, legal composition, replay and authorized responsibilities is a **proof/test obligation**, not inferred from the matching integers or a diagram. A stale expected match revision must be refused even if it names the currently unchanged board session version. Neither a game position nor its winner alone is evidence of the actor who authored a move.

**Tactics, strategies, and semiotic articulation are different readings:**

- An engine semantic event `(version, mover, acceptedCommand, event.type, termRef)` is an articulated observation of a legal *local effect*. The projection to a glossary term or factual invitation must retain that exact source/context/actor-side address and allow contradictory gameplay evidence. A capture or atari is not a function yielding a winning strategy.
- A **tactic candidate** names a bounded objective/predicate of legal continuations and a specified opponent response class, e.g. `exists legal move producing capture` contrasted with `all permitted defenses prevent that capture`. A one-move WXWQ tryout currently assays only a narrow event predicate; it does *not* settle the full adversarial reading. Source-owned Go engine and actual opposing decisions supply admissible paths; invented model narration has no authority to alter them.
- A **strategy candidate** is an admissible observation-conditioned decision policy `pi: (accepted history, evidence, actor goal) -> legal move/distribution`, together with explicit utility/regret, policy/compute budget and rival counterstrategies. A five-tier seeded bot policy is an implemented *decision procedure*, not yet evidence of a historical Japanese/Korean style, a mathematically optimal policy or a character's remembered experience. For formal comparison, hold out opponent seeds and games, and measure wins/losses, risk, initiative, territory/influence and actual learning rather than count how often a dramatic word appears.
- A **sect doctrine** is an attributed, authored fictional claim or preference over tactic/strategy policies. An encounter is an *actor-message and accepted playable mission*; membership/standing requires a separate voluntary, authorized change in the actual game-world actor record. An automatic classifier may suggest whom to invite but cannot commit the player to a society, confer real teaching credentials or transfer money.
- A **human teaching lineage edge** is a distinct consented student/teacher act sourced to a genuine completed teaching game (Red Cup's existing Lineage and Clubs design). A **documented historical Go lineage** references attributed teachers, game records, language, traditions and relevant usage rights in an evidence-bearing cultural corpus. Neither is a morphism into the other without an explicitly defined, consented and source-qualified relation. A player can voluntarily explore Japanese, Korean, Chinese and much narrower named traditions without being assigned an ethnicity, nationality or expertise by an AI profile.

**Bilateral conformance and falsification:** On the **left**, recover exact Obsmetrologia/CHL/SCA/SEN/SEAVT source terms where warranted, check the path/category definitions against the admitted engine's actual history semantics, state assumptions, prove/refute the projected mappings and keep open obligations labelled conjectural. On the **right**, select tests of genuine player recognition, rematches, learning transfer to unseen matches, historical source accuracy, teacher/learner feedback, licensed course completion, institutional buyers, creator income and attributable game profitability. Basic examples: (1) replay legal paths under two distinct rulesets and verify source/target and history-sensitive ko behavior; (2) contrast two visually identical boards with different histories; (3) two-pass actor settlement offer versus actual agreed result and stale revision; (4) a legitimate capture that does *not* imply sound long-range strategy; (5) a player declines a sect without losing their game; (6) a historical-style lesson cannot be labeled culturally authenticated without actual attributed sources; (7) a sponsor-funded earning cannot arise from a client-side character invitation alone.

**Bounded source-level assay on 2026-10-14 (evidence, not a new theorem):** Exact Red Cup Weiqi engine `board.mjs` (`5c4ab325`), `zobrist.mjs` (`9dd52c9b`), `rulesets.mjs` (`17f445eb`), `position.mjs` (`4353e9f1`), `scoring.mjs` (`dc7d37b6`), `events.mjs` (`faa70fb3`), `game.mjs` (`da390439`) and `session.mjs` (`96557752`) were evaluated as their retrieved JavaScript with only the unavailable host `structuredClone` operation substituted. Under 9×9 Chinese rules, an initial empty game and the accepted path `pass; pass` had **exactly the same 81 board cells and the same next color**, yet the initial state had `passStreak=0` and refused `settle(dead=[])` with `main-phase-open`, while the passed state had `passStreak=2` and accepted the same settlement. A further placement after the accepted settlement was refused with `game-ended`. Two groupings of the same admitted `C3, G7, pass, pass` path produced the same complete source-engine state, consistent with concatenation associativity. The alternative Japanese ruleset produced **territory** rather than Chinese **area** scoring for a completed no-stone game. This is direct support for keeping histories, path endpoints, legal transition effects and ruleset identity distinct; the result alone does **not** prove the proposed Obsmetrologia functor, general semantic faithfulness, distributional game skill or historical lineage. The complete native Node suite and independent live receiver were not executed in this assay.

**Engineering handoff boundary:** The original WX-01/WX-03/WX-04 and WX-07 system requirements consume these **candidate predicates** only where separately warranted. Red Cup's [single Game Systems source](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-engineering-services-section/tree/main/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-game-system-services-section) owns the existing legal game, tactics, battle/agent policies and match-log revision. As of this note there is **no** independent formal path-category-to-Obsmetrologia theorem, empirically qualified national play-style classifier, received persistent sect runtime, authenticated online service, delivered paid course or observed Lightning payout. The lawful next proof/fabrication questions remain explicitly open.

## Keya (mascot)

A turtle named Keya (Lakota: *kéya* = turtle) with a go board for a shell. Two cosmological resonances were noted in session, deliberately both held rather than one chosen: Lakota/Turtle Island (the world resting on the turtle's back) and the Chinese Luoshu (the magic-square diagram said to appear on a turtle's shell from the Luo River; oracle-bone divination was historically performed on turtle plastrons). The relationality program this section sits under is Lakota-grounded; the game itself is Chinese; the turtle image is read as belonging to both rather than as a choice between them.

Keya's role is learn-track companion and teacher, with a deliberately anti-Duolingo voice — patient, ancient, glad-you-came, no streak-shaming. "Turtle pace" is meant to describe the pedagogy, not just the character. A mascot-science research note has been commissioned separately (in the game-science section, not this one) to carry cultural-care flags for public use of a Lakota-derived name — that research is to **report** those flags, not resolve them; this note does not attempt to resolve them either.

## WeiqIdol (fiction layer over the competitive ladder)

The name was verified unclaimed in app stores and on the web as of 2026-08-29. The concept: a wuxia martial world meets idol culture, where a player's real play feeds an idol persona's renown in a jianghu fiction. Named precedents from the session: *Hikaru no Go* (Sai as a channeled idol figure, with a documented real youth-Go revival attributed to the series), Jin Yong's Go-master tropes, and VTuber/idol-media mechanics generally.

**Core mechanic:** a player's *play style* gets them solicited and recruited by AI idols representing different playstyle sects — the rivals scout the player, not the other way around. The engineering basis for this already exists in outline: the kernel's semantic event stream (atari, capture, connection, eye events) plus engine statistics (aggression, territory-vs-influence balance, fighting frequency) yields a style profile; sects are style archetypes (fighting/power, territorial/calm, light/fast, thick/patient, and so on); recruitment is a personalized solicitation by a named agent idol whose sect matches — or deliberately opposes — the player's profile.

AI idols are conceived as agent-peers, using the same pattern named in [Upstream Integration](2026-08-29-note-on-upstream-integration.md) for durante's `entrantKind: human | agent` distinction: named rivals with LLM voices, persistent memory of the player's games, and glossary-grounded in-character commentary — not a special-cased bot tier bolted onto a human-only game. Titles and progression are concept-mastery based (the [Weiqi Market Teardown](weiqi-market-teardown.md) addendum's ranked differentiator #4 — concept-mastery progress tracking as an alternative to ladder rating), styled in-fiction as sect ranks and techniques rather than as a bare numeric ladder. Tournament arcs run on `footing`'s daily-tournament plumbing (see [Upstream Integration](2026-08-29-note-on-upstream-integration.md)); spectating carries fan-cheering mechanics.

## The noodle bar (Reigns-style hub loop)

A later addition to the same session's vision, staged as a longer-term direction rather than immediate work: the player sits at a table in a noodle bar, and opportunities arrive as cards — challenges from NPCs, invitations, events — which the player swipes left or right to deny or accept, in the manner of the game *Reigns*. Normal gameplay (actual Go) happens **outside** this loop; the noodle bar is the frame around it, not a replacement for it.

The recruitment flow: the AI watches the player's style; an idol representing the house or sect that matches that style comes to the player's table in person — *"we're seeing how you play! We want you to play a match against [this other NPC]."* Recruitment and match proposals are delivered diegetically, as visitors arriving at the table, never as menu notifications or system toasts.

A session observation worth recording alongside the direction itself: the noodle bar could in principle absorb the platform's entire lobby surface — matchmaking offers, daily tournament reminders (`footing`), correspondence "your opponent moved" notices, SRS tsumego reviews coming due (delivered by Keya), arcade ticket prompts — with every system event rendered as a character arriving at the table rather than as UI chrome. The swipe interaction is native to mobile-first design, consistent with the [Weiqi Market Teardown](weiqi-market-teardown.md) addendum's table-stakes note on mobile-responsive play.

## Ticket arcade ("go-slots" idle lane)

A lower-effort, social-casino-style side lane: near-slot-machine-simple Go problems as idle gaming. Tickets gate play in this lane; tickets recharge over time or can be purchased.

**Hard boundary: real gameplay is never gated behind tickets or purchases.** The arcade is explicitly a side lane, aimed at the — described as large — audience overlap between Go players and social-slots players, not a monetization layer over the core game. There is house precedent for this genre: the fleet already runs `gambling-trainer`, and a `gambling-services-section` exists, so this is not the organization's first contact with slot-style mechanics.

The session's guardrail for this whole vision, retained explicitly: the fiction and arcade layers are opt-in, sitting atop the serious platform rather than replacing or gating it, with transparent, non-dark-pattern monetization throughout — the [Weiqi Market Teardown](weiqi-market-teardown.md) addendum's ranked differentiator #7. This boundary is the one piece of this note that reads as a constraint rather than a feature idea, and it should be treated that way by whoever eventually implements any of the above: the ticket arcade may be built, expanded, or dropped, but it may not be built in a way that puts real gameplay behind it.
