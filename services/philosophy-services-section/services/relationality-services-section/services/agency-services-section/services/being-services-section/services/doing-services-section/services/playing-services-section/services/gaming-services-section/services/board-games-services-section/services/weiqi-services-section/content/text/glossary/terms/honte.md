Honte in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is the solid, "proper" move — the one that leaves no [aji](./aji.md) behind, at the cost of a little tempo [@kageyama1978lessons]. Kageyama's *Lessons in the Fundamentals of Go* is the standard source on honte, alongside shape and direction of play, and the concept exists specifically to name the trade a greedier, more aji-leaving move refuses to make [@kageyama1978lessons].

Honte is a judgment call rather than a rule: playing honte everywhere would waste tempo on threats the opponent could never have exploited, while never playing honte leaves aji that eventually costs more than the tempo it would have taken to remove.

As a proposal, the section's own [Weiqi Strategic Theory](../../documents/articles/research-articles/2026-08-29-note-on-weiqi-strategic-theory.md) (§6) places "honte's trade" in the modal, option-valued row of its classification, alongside aji and aji-keshi — the knowledge order, not an ordinary board-position evaluation or a move-relation CGT already solves — status: proposal.

## Related terms

- [aji](./aji.md) - Honte is the move that spends tempo specifically to avoid leaving aji.
- [aji-keshi](./aji-keshi.md) - Honte trades tempo for safety; aji-keshi trades aji for a smaller local gain, and is the error honte is meant to avoid on the other side.
- [sabaki](./sabaki.md) - Honte and sabaki are opposite responses to local pressure: solidity everywhere versus expendable lightness.
