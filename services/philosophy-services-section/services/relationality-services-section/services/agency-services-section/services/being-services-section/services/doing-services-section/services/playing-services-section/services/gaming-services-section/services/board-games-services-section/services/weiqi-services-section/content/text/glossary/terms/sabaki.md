Sabaki in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is making a light, flexible, quickly-settled shape inside the opponent's sphere of [influence](./influence.md), playing so that any individual stone is expendable [@senseislibrary]. It contrasts with heavy (*omoi*) shape, which must be saved at all costs and therefore becomes a target the opponent can attack repeatedly for profit [@kageyama1978lessons].

Sabaki is a posture more than a single move: a group played for sabaki accepts that some of its stones may be sacrificed, and treats that as acceptable so long as the group as a whole settles with enough shape to survive or to have profited elsewhere while the opponent was attacking it. It is a compressed way of getting an approximate answer to a local fight without doing the full life-and-death calculation a heavier group would demand.

## Related terms

- [thickness](./thickness.md) - Sabaki is the light-shape alternative to confronting an opponent's thickness directly.
- [influence](./influence.md) - Sabaki is specifically the posture for operating inside the opponent's influence rather than outside it.
- [honte](./honte.md) - Honte and sabaki are opposite responses to the same pressure: honte spends a move to remove all weakness, sabaki accepts weakness as expendable.
