A false eye in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is an eye-shaped empty point that looks like a protected internal [liberty](./liberty.md) but does not meet the diagonal-control and connection requirements of a real [eye](./eye.md), so the opponent can eventually force the surrounding group to fill it [@senseislibrary2026falseeye].

An eye stays real only if the stones around it are firmly connected and enough of the diagonal points next to it are also controlled by the same color: one diagonal for an eye in the corner, two on the edge, and three in the center of the board. A false eye fails one of these conditions. Often the surrounding stones can be cut apart, or too many of the diagonal points belong to the opponent. Once the opponent captures a cutting stone or occupies enough diagonals, the eye point stops being a safe internal liberty and becomes an ordinary point that the surrounding group may eventually have to fill itself, or that the opponent can play into without dying [@senseislibrary2026falseeye].

False eyes matter because they are easy to miscount during a fight. A group that appears to have two eyes may actually have one real eye and one false eye, which means it has only one secure eye and is not unconditionally alive. Reading whether an eye is true or false, especially in a group with a single large or oddly shaped internal space, is a core part of [life and death](./life-and-death.md) work: the difference between a true eye and a false eye is often the difference between a living group and a dead one.
