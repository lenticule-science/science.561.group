board.mjs

A plain page loading ui/board.mjs directly — no bundler, no kernel import. Theme follows the OS prefers-color-scheme via the five CSS custom properties board.mjs documents at the top of its own file; try switching your system theme with this page open.

Click/tap an empty point to arm a pending ghost stone; click it again (or press Enter twice on the keyboard cursor) to fire onIntent — logged below the board. Escape clears a pending ghost. Tab to the board to drive it with arrow keys.
