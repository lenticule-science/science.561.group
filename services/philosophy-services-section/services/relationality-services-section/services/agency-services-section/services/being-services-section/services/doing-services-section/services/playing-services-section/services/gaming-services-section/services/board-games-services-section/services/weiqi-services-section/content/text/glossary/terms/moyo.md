Moyo in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is a large-scale framework of potential territory, outlined by stones' [influence](./influence.md) rather than enclosed by them [@senseislibrary]. A moyo is [influence](./influence.md) organized at whole-board scale and aimed at a specific outcome — growing into secured space — where bare influence only constrains the opponent's options without claiming any of them.

A moyo is not territory. It remains open to invasion or reduction until the framework is actually closed, and a large moyo that cannot be defended once entered is the classical trap of the style: the framework looked large on the board but could not survive contact [@kageyama1978lessons]. Whether a given moyo is worth more than the [territory](./territory.md) an opponent secures while invading it is a genuine, position-dependent judgment, not a rule of thumb.

## Related terms

- [influence](./influence.md) - Moyo is influence organized into one large, whole-board framework rather than a local effect.
- [territory](./territory.md) - Moyo is territory-in-potential; it is not secured until the framework survives invasion.
- [thickness](./thickness.md) - A moyo often grows outward from a thick wall, though the wall's value and the framework's value are judged separately.
