## Why this is central

Cho Hun-hyun (born 1953) is the section's verified source for the
founding figure of modern Korean professional Go. The article records him
turning professional at nine in 1962, studying in Japan under Kensaku
Segoe and Hideyuki Fujisawa, becoming the first Korean 9-dan in 1982, and
accumulating 150 professional titles — more than any other player in the
game's recorded history. Most load-bearing for this survey: the article
confirms he began mentoring Lee Chang-ho in 1984, the direct teaching
line this survey's §3 traces from Cho through Lee Chang-ho to the
generation (Lee Sedol among them) that followed.

## What it does not settle

The Wikipedia article itself does not characterize Cho's playing style as
fighting-oriented or aggressive despite his "God of War" nickname; it
records the nickname without unpacking it. The style content this
survey's §3 draws on — the "Swallow" and "God of War" style shift, and
Korean Go's break from Japanese convention — comes instead from
[cho-hunhyun-gomagic](cho-hunhyun-gomagic.md), not from this source.
