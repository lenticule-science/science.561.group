Playing gathers the vocabulary of the activity itself, before it hardens into games: [play](../terms/play.md) (the voluntary, intrinsically motivated quality), [playing](../terms/playing.md) (the activity read as an agent's two-party boundary faculty, formalized through Girard's ludics), and the [lusory attitude](../terms/lusory-attitude.md) (Suits's name for the rule-acceptance that makes any game possible).

The topic sits here — under doing, under being, in the relationality taxonomy — because playing is a mode of an agent's doing, distinguished from its siblings by acting through a second party. What lies below is the structured form: the [gaming](../../../../services/gaming-services-section/README.md) section and its traditions (board, card, dice, role-playing, gambling). What lies alongside, in social-theory custody, is the cultural theory of play — Caillois's categories, deep play, flow — at the ludics-science section's game-ludics subsection.
