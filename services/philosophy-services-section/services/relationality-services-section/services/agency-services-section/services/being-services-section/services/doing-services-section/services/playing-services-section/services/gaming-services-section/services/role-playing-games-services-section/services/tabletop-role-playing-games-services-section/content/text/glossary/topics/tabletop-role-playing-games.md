Tabletop role-playing games gathers the medium-specific vocabulary for
[role-playing games](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/topics/role-playing-games.md)
played through real-time human conversation and adjudication rather than
software: the session-level apparatus of dice mechanics, reference
sheets, handouts, session zero, and table culture that distinguishes
play at a table (physical or virtual) from a digital RPG. It sits under
role-playing games as the human-adjudicated branch, gathering the
material and social practices — actual play, campaign frames, factions,
scenarios — that recur across systems and settings.

The full structural specification — constitutive and non-constitutive
features, the relationship to digital RPGs, and political implications —
lives in the section's note:
[Tabletop Role-Playing Games Specification](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/01/01/2026-01-01-note-on-tabletop-role-playing-games.md).
A genealogical treatment lives alongside it:
