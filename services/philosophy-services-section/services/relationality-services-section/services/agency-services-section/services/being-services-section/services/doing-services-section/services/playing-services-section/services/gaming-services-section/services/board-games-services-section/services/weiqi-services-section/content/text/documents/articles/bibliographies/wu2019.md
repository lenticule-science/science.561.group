## Why this is central

This is the KataGo paper: it introduces a set of architectural and
training improvements over the AlphaZero process ([silver2017](silver2017.md))
that together achieve roughly a 50x reduction in the computation needed
to reach a comparable playing strength — the paper's own framing is that
"much of the speedup involves non-domain-specific improvements that might
directly transfer to other problems," i.e. it is presented as general
self-play-learning engineering, not a Go-only trick. First posted to
arXiv on 2019-02-27, with a fifth revision on 2020-11-09, which is why
this corpus's own notes refer to it as the "2019/2020" KataGo paper.

KataGo is the specific engine this section names repeatedly as its own
AI dependency: the [Go-Science History](../notes/2026-08-29-note-on-goscience-history.md)
note's implementation ladder for in-app opponents runs "scripted
heuristics now → shaped GnuGo Phase 2 → KataGo human-style nets Phase 3,"
and [Reframe and Standards](../notes/2026-08-29-note-on-reframe-and-standards.md)
names "the KataGo JSON analysis protocol" as one of the section's three
canonical outside-format interchange boundaries, alongside SGF and GTP
(see [katago-analysis-engine](katago-analysis-engine.md)). [Obsmetrologia and Go Strategy](../research-articles/obsmetrologia-and-go-strategy.md)
also treats KataGo's shipped per-point ownership standard deviation and
score variance as the baseline scalar statistic its own aji-formalization
conjecture is measured against.

## What it does not settle

The paper is an engineering and machine-learning result about
self-play-learning efficiency; it makes no claims about Go strategy
vocabulary or about what its evaluation numbers mean strategically. The
gap [Obsmetrologia and Go Strategy](../research-articles/obsmetrologia-and-go-strategy.md)
identifies — that KataGo's scalar ownership/score statistics blur "this
resolves with deeper reading" from "this structurally depends on
undetermined context elsewhere" — is a limit of the tool this paper
describes, not something the paper itself addresses.
