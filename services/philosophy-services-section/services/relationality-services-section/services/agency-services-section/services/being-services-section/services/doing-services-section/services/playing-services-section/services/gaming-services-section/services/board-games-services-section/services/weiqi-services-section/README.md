# Weiqi Services Section

The Weiqi Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for this service domain, and is the constituted home for the weiqi web platform build described below.

## What is here

**Corpus** (`content/text/`), in the house science-topic-section shape:

- `documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/` — the five-lesson sequenced curriculum ([Weiqi Overview](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/weiqi-overview.md), [Living Groups and Eyes](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/living-groups-and-eyes.md), [Komi and Handicap](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/komi-and-handicap.md), [Ending the Game](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/ending-the-game.md), [History of Weiqi](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/weiqi-history.md)) plus [Weiqi Rulesets](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/tutorials/weiqi-rulesets.md), the club/server-facing companion lesson to the implementer-facing reference below.
- `documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/` — the rules-completeness reference set kernel implementers read: [Rulesets](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/rulesets.md) (stable ruleset identifiers) and [Regression Positions](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/regression-positions.md) (thirteen hand-verified board positions), plus the operational tables [How Ranks Work](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/how-ranks-work.md), [Handicap Placement and Komi Values](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/handicap-placement-and-komi-values.md), [Ko and Superko](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/ko-and-superko.md), and [Seki and False Eyes](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/references/seki-and-false-eyes.md).
- `documents/articles/research-articles/` — [Obsmetrologia and Go Strategy](content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/obsmetrologia-and-go-strategy.md) (the 2026-08-19 inquiry into whether Obsmetrologia's mathematics contributes novel Go strategy), its 2026-08-29 complement [Weiqi Strategic Theory](content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/2026-08-29-note-on-weiqi-strategic-theory.md) (the classical strategic vocabulary the rules-level glossary does not cover), and the 2026-09-03 [Weiqi as Arena, Chu Space, Predication, and Manifold](content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/2026-09-03-note-on-weiqi-as-arena-chu-space-predication-and-manifold.md) (a survey of the house theories that bear on weiqi, with per-lineage status).
- `documents/articles/notes/` — this section's operating and historical notes: the [Weiqi Platform Plan](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-platform-plan.md), [Provenance Policy](content/text/documents/articles/notes/provenance-policy.md), [Weiqi Market Teardown](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-market-teardown.md), the [Weiqi Kernel](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-kernel-constitution.md), [Weiqi Platform](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-platform-constitution.md), [Weiqi Learn](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-learn-constitution.md), and [Weiqi LSP](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-lsp-constitution.md) constitutions, and the [Weiqi LSP Authorization](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-19-note-on-weiqi-lsp-authorization.md). A 2026-08-29 working session added seven further notes recording that session's findings, each written to stand as a complete record in its own right: [Platform State](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-platform-state.md) (verified state of every app, tests, deployment, and open decision gates), [Upstream Integration](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-upstream-integration.md) (reusable infrastructure elsewhere in the repository and the gaps it doesn't cover), [Reframe and Standards](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-reframe-and-standards.md) (the owner's course-correction on scope, and standards decisions for SGF/GTP/KataGo integration), [Engineering History](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-engineering-history.md) and [Go-Science History](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-goscience-history.md) (the full recovered project chronology, split engineering/doctrine, including every bug's exact mechanics, dated test-count arcs, and one still-open owner action in the weiqi-lsp governance saga), [Weiqi/Go App Landscape](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-go-app-landscape.md) (the full 2026 competitive-landscape study behind the market teardown's addendum), and [WeiqIdol Product Vision](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-weiqidol-product-vision.md) (owner-originated fiction-layer and arcade-lane direction, not yet built).
- `glossary/terms/` — thirty-seven dependency-linked glossary terms in four clusters (tactical: liberty, atari, capture, ko, superko, suicide; scoring: territory, komi, handicap, area scoring, territory scoring, pass, dead stones; structural: connection, eye, life-and-death, seki; strategy: influence, thickness, sente, gote, aji, aji keshi, miai, sabaki, honte, joseki, fuseki, moyo, geta, sute-ishi, kikashi, tobi, keima, dango, semeai, zenkyoku).
- `glossary/topics/weiqi.md` — the start-here index linking the lessons and terms.

**Platform build.** This corpus is the foundation for a weiqi web platform — real-time and correspondence play, an interactive learn track built from the lesson template, and a glossary served as both pages and an API — fusing playing and learning around one concept graph, per house doctrine that "the engine is the executable form of the glossary." Full thesis, architecture, roadmap, and board-decision gates are in the [Weiqi Platform Plan](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-platform-plan.md) — read it before adding code here, alongside the 2026-08-29 [Reframe and Standards](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-reframe-and-standards.md) note, which corrects the plan's education-first emphasis toward a fuller feature web. The rules kernel is the published engine `@red-cup-engineering/weiqi-engine` (seated in the engineering tree at `computer-game-system-services-section/services/weiqi-engine`, formerly this section's `apps/weiqi-kernel`), constituted by the [Weiqi Kernel Constitution](content/text/documents/articles/notes/weiqi-kernel-constitution.md); every app here consumes it by exact registry pin. [`apps/weiqi-web`](apps/weiqi-web) is deployed and live at weiqi.561.group as its own Cloudflare Worker (normal 9×9 play against four local bot tiers, Capture Go, an online two-player match admitted and replayed by a Durable Object, tsumego with spaced repetition, one interactive lesson, Study replay, and the glossary); the earlier parallel Learn face under `services/weiqi-platform` has been retired; and [`apps/weiqi-lsp`](apps/weiqi-lsp), a corpus doctrine checker (not a Go language server, despite its name), enforces this section's own conventions against the kernel. See [Platform State](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-29-note-on-platform-state.md) for the verified detail, including the unreconciled two-parallel-Learn-apps situation and the status of every open decision gate. Both constitutions and the plan's remaining open decisions still gate any further code here.

## Tooling

- `scripts/check-links.mts` — zero-dep Node script (run via `npm run check-links` or `node scripts/check-links.mts`) that walks this section's `content/` tree and verifies every relative markdown link resolves to a real file. Scoped strictly to this section.

Node runs `.mts` files directly via type stripping; no build step, no external npm dependencies (house doctrine: zero-dep). Tests, when added, follow the house `node --test` convention with `.test.mts` files.
