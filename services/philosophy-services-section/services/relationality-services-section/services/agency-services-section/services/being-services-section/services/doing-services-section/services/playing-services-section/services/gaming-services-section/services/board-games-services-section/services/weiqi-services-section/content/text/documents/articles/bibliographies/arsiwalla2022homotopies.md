## Why this is central

The formal shape of rulial space. Homotopies between evolution paths of a
multiway rewriting system are "induced by the inclusion of appropriate
rewriting rules taken from an abstract rulial space," and a multiway
system with homotopies up to order n is an n-fold category whose infinite
limit, with inverses, is an ∞-groupoid. In the house's terms: adding a
rule is a higher cell between strategies, so the space of rules is the
space of homotopies between plays, and "rules as strategies" is a
statement about which dimension a move lives in. Two branches of play
that a rule identifies are the same play up to that rule; the rule is the
2-cell. This is the construction the rule-space article needs to say
"rules as strategies" as a theorem shape rather than an analogy, and it
is where the house's braided tower (rows, saturated, settled, served) and
the n-fold categories should be compared.

## What it does not settle

Whether the house's settlement nuclei (σ, Δ) are the homotopies of a
particular rulial inclusion is not stated here; it is the comparison the
house has to make.
