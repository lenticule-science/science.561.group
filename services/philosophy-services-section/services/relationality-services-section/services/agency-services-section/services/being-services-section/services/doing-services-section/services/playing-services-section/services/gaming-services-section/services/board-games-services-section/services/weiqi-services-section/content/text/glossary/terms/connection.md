A connection in weiqi is a relation between stones that makes them function as one group, sharing liberties and supporting each other's defense.

Connection is structurally important because isolated stones are weak. A move that does not gain immediate territory may still be strong if it joins two unsettled groups, prevents a cutting attack, or turns a fragile shape into one that can live.

## Related terms

- [liberty](./liberty.md) - Connected stones share liberties, which is why connection increases strength.
- [eye](./eye.md) - A connected group often tries to make eyes as one unit rather than as isolated stones.
- [life and death](./life-and-death.md) - Many life-and-death problems turn on whether stones can stay connected.
