An eye in [weiqi](../index.md) is an empty point or enclosed space inside a group's shape that functions as an internal [liberty](./liberty.md) and helps determine whether the group can live [@britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Eyes matter because they change the status of a group.
A surrounded group with no secure eye shape may still be dead, while a well-connected group with two separate eyes is usually safe.
This is why players often defend shape even when no immediate [capture](./capture.md) is on the board.

## Related terms

- [liberty](./liberty.md) - An eye is a protected internal liberty.
- [connection](./connection.md) - Strong connection makes it easier for a group to form active eyes.
- [life and death](./life-and-death.md) - Life-and-death reading asks whether a group can make enough eyes to survive.
