## Why this is central

This is the section's verified source for the specific fighting-style
content this survey's §3 attributes to Cho Hun-hyun and, through him, to
the Korean tradition generally. The article names two style periods, not
one: an early "Swallow" style — "light, sharp, leaving groups dangling
while seizing territory elsewhere only to return and narrowly escape
death" — later succeeded by the "God of War," "a flame-thrower who
balanced aggression with patience" after Lee Chang-ho's methodical,
territory-securing "Stone Buddha" play had "clipped the Swallow's wings."
The article frames this as one instance of a broader national pattern:
Korean Go developed by "breaking one by one the rules and the taboos
defined by the players in Japan," treating the game as "a competitive
brain sport" driven by "a strong instinct for survival and the thirst for
victory" rather than by the accumulated courtesies of the Japanese
professional tradition.

The "Swallow" description — light, sharp shape; groups left dangling as
bait; territory seized while the opponent is occupied elsewhere — is the
closest documented historical parallel this survey found to the Lakota
hypothesis's combination of shape lightness and sacrifice (§1
"Decoys, Feigned Retreats, and Sacrifices" and "Mobility, Light Shape,
and Dispersion" in
[Note on Lakota Weiqi Strategy](../notes/2026-09-04-note-on-lakota-weiqi-strategy.md)),
though attributed here to one player's early style, superseded by his own
later "God of War" period, not to a whole national lineage's steady-state
signature.

## What it does not settle

This is a secondary, narrative source (a Go Magic instructional
retrospective) describing Cho's own account of his style shift, not a
move-level measurement of either period's games. It gives no numeric
opening-line distribution, sacrifice rate, or forcing ratio, and its
"Swallow"/"God of War" framing describes one player's career arc, not a
stable Korean national signature — [cho-hunhyun-wikipedia](cho-hunhyun-wikipedia.md)
and [lee-sedol-wikipedia](lee-sedol-wikipedia.md) are needed for the
broader lineage claim.
