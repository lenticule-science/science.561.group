Geta ("net") in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) is a technique for [capturing](./capture.md) a stone or small group by surrounding it loosely, from a distance, with a small number of stones placed so that any attempt to extend toward a [liberty](./liberty.md) runs into a stone already in place [@senseislibrary]. The captured stones are never chased [liberty](./liberty.md) by liberty; the net is set once, and the group inside it cannot escape by any single move.

A net differs from a direct chase in exactly this way: it needs no follow-up reading once it is set correctly, whereas a ladder-style pursuit must be read all the way to its end to confirm it works [@kageyama1978lessons]. A poorly placed net leaves a gap the enclosed stones can escape through, so confirming a geta still requires reading the specific shape — it is a shape recognized and verified, not merely assumed from the general pattern.

## Related terms

- [capture](./capture.md) - A net is one specific technique for forcing a capture without a direct chase.
- [atari](./atari.md) - The enclosed stones need never be placed in atari for a net to work; the net closes off their escape before that stage.
- [liberty](./liberty.md) - A net is set by occupying the liberties an escaping stone would otherwise extend toward.
