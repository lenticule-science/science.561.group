## Why this is central

Otake Hideo (born 1942, joined the Kitani Minoru school at age nine,
professional from age fourteen) is the section's verified source for the
"thickness style" named in this survey's task scope, distinct from
Takemiya's whole-board cosmic style though from the same teaching
lineage. The article records his nickname "Aesthetics Go Player" (*Otake
Bigaku*) and describes a preference for "thick, tasteful shapes that
emphasized aesthetic harmony over aggressive confrontations" — 44 career
titles including four Meijin, seven Gosei, and five Judan titles across
a career running to his 2021 retirement.

## What it does not settle

"Aesthetic harmony over aggressive confrontations" is a qualitative,
secondary-source characterization, not a move-level measurement; the
article gives no data on Otake's opening-line distribution, sacrifice
rate, or forcing ratio. It also does not explain the relationship between
his thickness style and Takemiya's cosmic style beyond the shared
Kitani-school teacher — whether one influenced the other, or both drew
independently on the same teaching lineage's emphasis on strong shape, is
not stated.
