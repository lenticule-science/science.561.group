A capture in [weiqi](../topics/weiqi.md) occurs when a move removes the last [liberty](./liberty.md) from an opposing stone or connected group, after which those stones are removed from the board [@britannica2026go; @nagofed2026thisisgo].

Capture is the game's basic tactical enforcement rule. Without it, enclosure would carry no force. But weiqi is not a pure capture race, because players usually capture in order to secure shape, reduce influence, or stabilize [territory](./territory.md), not only to remove material.

## Related terms

- [atari](./atari.md) - Atari is the immediate threat state that often precedes capture.
- [liberty](./liberty.md) - Capture is defined by the removal of the last liberty.
- [life and death](./life-and-death.md) - Many life-and-death problems end by proving whether capture can be forced.
- [territory](./territory.md) - Captures often matter because they change which empty points can become territory.
- [ko](./ko.md) - Some captures trigger ko and cannot be answered by immediate recapture.
