Komi in [weiqi](../index.md) is the point compensation added to White's final score to offset Black's first-move advantage [@britgo2025default; @britgo2026whatisgo].

Komi matters because weiqi is asymmetrical at the start: Black moves first.
Without compensation, that initiative would distort results across many even games.
Komi is therefore a scoring adjustment, not a tactical rule inside local fights.

## Related terms

- [territory](./territory.md) - Komi is added during final scoring after territory and other points are counted.
- [capture](./capture.md) - Captures may affect the final score that komi then adjusts.
- [handicap](./handicap.md) - Handicap changes the starting position, while komi adjusts the score.
