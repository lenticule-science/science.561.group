Atari in weiqi is the state in which a stone or connected group has only one liberty remaining and is therefore under immediate threat of capture.

Atari matters because it turns a broad positional game into a forced tactical problem. When a group falls to one liberty, the next move often becomes urgent for both players: one side tries to capture, while the other tries to extend, connect, or create shape.

## Related terms

- [liberty](./liberty.md) - Atari is defined by the reduction to a single remaining liberty.
- [capture](./capture.md) - A capture follows if the threatened group cannot answer the atari.
- [connection](./connection.md) - Connecting to another group is one common way to escape atari.
