# Hjelmslevian semiotic system

A worked instance of [semiotic-system](../../../../glossary/terms/semiotic-system.md) under Hjelmslev's *Prolegomena* (per [Hjelmslev 1943](../../bibliographies/hjelmslev1943.md)).

## Components

- $V$ — *expression-plane*, split by Hjelmslev into *expression-form* (the system's structural shape on the sign-vehicle side) and *expression-substance* (the material instantiation). The system operates at the form level; substance is its parameter.
- $O$ — *content-plane*, similarly split into *content-form* (the system's structural shape on the sign-object side) and *content-substance* (the conceptual material).
- $I$ — *interpretant repertoire*: the *purport* the system makes available, derived from the joint articulation of expression-form and content-form.
- $K$ — *code* (Hjelmslev's *function*): the relation that pairs expression-form positions with content-form positions; the heart of the system's structural commitment.
- $R$ — *rule system*: the rules of *commutation* (substitution-test for distinguishing sign-positions) and the *connotative iteration* (taking a denotative system as the expression-plane of a further connotative system).
- $\Sigma$ — *semiosphere*: implicit, supplied by the discourse community whose system is being analysed.

## Distinctive features

- **Form-substance split on both faces.** The two-by-two structure (expression-form, expression-substance, content-form, content-substance) is the system's load-bearing analytical commitment.
- **Connotative iteration as primitive.** A *connotative semiotic* takes an entire denotative semiotic as its expression-plane and re-relates it to a further content-plane. The iteration composes: connotations of connotations are admissible.
- **Metasemiotic.** The system can take *itself* as object — a metasemiotic has a denotative semiotic as its content-plane. This recursion is what makes the discipline possible: semiotics studies semiotic systems metasemiotically.

## Substrate reading

The Hjelmslevian system instantiates predicated knowledge system under a *layered Scott-continuous* regime: each layer's closure terminates at $\omega$, and the connotative iteration produces a tower of layers indexed by ordinals up to the encyclopedia's bound. The connotative tower's collapse-point (the level at which connotation stops generating new content) is the substrate's settled fixpoint at the corresponding ordinal.

## Bridge to substrate operations

- *Specialise* — within a single layer, refines the form-positions along the system's commutation hierarchy.
- *Translate* — across two Hjelmslevian systems with different forms, requires explicit form-mapping; the translation reads as substrate federate at the form-level with the substance-level absorbed into the federation.
- *Codeswitch* — between a denotative and its connotative reading; ubiquitous in literary discourse, where the same utterance is read denotatively and connotatively in alternation.
