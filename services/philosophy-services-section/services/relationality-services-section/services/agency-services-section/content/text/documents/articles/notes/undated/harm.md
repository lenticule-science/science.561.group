# harm

Damage to a body, a capacity, or a relation.
Generated by: **body** (harming — a body's integrity is breached).

A low primitive.
Harm is not derived from social structure; the social *governs* and *distributes* harm (who is exposed, whose harm is grievable), but the damage itself is prior to the arrangement that distributes it.
Imports **body** from the agency module (`agent/actuating/library/actuator/embodying/library/body`).
