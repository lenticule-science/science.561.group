***Intertheoretic reduction*** is a [thing](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md) that is [induced](inducing.md) by [reducing](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/reducing.md) a [concept](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md) in two [different](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/different.md) [theories](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/theory.md) to one [concept](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md).
