Geontologies names the governance of the distinction between Life and Nonlife — the [ontological](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/science/theories/information/terms/ontology.md) division that structures late liberal power.
[Elizabeth Povinelli](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/references/people/elizabeth-povinelli.md) develops the concept in *Geontologies: A Requiem to Late Liberalism* (2016) to identify what she argues is the more fundamental operation beneath [biopolitics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/biopolitics/terms/biopolitics.md): not the management of living populations but the prior determination of what counts as alive at all.

The concept works through three figures — the Desert, the Animist, and the Virus — each of which represents a threat to the Life/Nonlife boundary that late liberalism must manage.
The Desert is the figure of Nonlife threatening to overwhelm Life (ecological collapse, resource exhaustion).
The Animist is the figure that refuses the distinction altogether — that insists rocks, rivers, and ancestors are alive and agentive.
The Virus is the figure that crosses the boundary, neither fully alive nor fully inert, disrupting the classification from within.

These are not merely philosophical categories.
They name the concrete mechanisms through which [settler-colonial](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) governance classifies land, water, and geological formations as inert matter available for extraction.
When Indigenous ontologies recognize the personhood of a river or the agency of a landscape, they do not merely disagree with Western [metaphysics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/philosophy/theories/western/theories/metaphysics/terms/metaphysics.md) — they challenge the geontological infrastructure that makes [property](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/anarchism/terms/property.md) law, [extractivism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/services/ecology-services-section/services/climate-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/extractivism.md), and resource governance possible.

Geontologies thus extends [Foucault](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/references/people/michel-foucault.md)'s analysis of [biopower](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/foucauldian/terms/biopower.md) by showing that the governance of Life presupposes a prior governance of the Life/Nonlife distinction itself.
Biopolitics asks: how are living populations managed?
Geontologies asks: how is the category of "living" produced and maintained in the first place?
