The **modal research setting** is the ambient structure required before directed
relational research can be stated.

Let `E` be a cohesive research universe carrying a chain of adjoint idempotent
modalities:

```text
Pi^right -| Box -| Diamond -| Nabla^left
```

The middle composite is:

```text
C := Box Diamond
```

DRR uses `C` as the closure in which a dynamic inquiry and a stable form can be
held together.

## Definition

A modal research setting is a pair `(E, C)` where:

- `E` is the ambient field in which experiences, claims, and research acts live;
- `C : E -> E` is the middle modal composite;
- `C` is read comonadically when research dynamics are being extended;
- `C` is read by its stable coalgebras when research dynamics have certified.

## Lemma: closure is part of the codomain

For DRR, a claim is not the bare object `Y`; it is `C Y`, the claim under modal
closure. Therefore a research act never targets an ungoverned proposition. It
targets a claim-form already placed inside the closure by which it may stabilize.

## Proof

If the codomain were only `Y`, composition of research acts would leave no
record of the closure that makes later certification meaningful. Placing the
codomain at `C Y` means every research arrow carries both its dynamic movement
and the topology in which it can settle. This is the minimal structure needed to
state directed relational research.
