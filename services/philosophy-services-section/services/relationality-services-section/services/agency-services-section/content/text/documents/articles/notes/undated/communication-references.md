# References

The science-side communication theory's local references. Currently seven load-bearing entries, organised by the tradition each anchors:

**Mathematical / cybernetic tradition.**
- [shannon-weaver1949](../../bibliographies/shannon-weaver1949.md) — Shannon & Weaver, *The Mathematical Theory of Communication*. The source-channel-receiver formalisation that anchors [shannon-channel](../../../../glossary/terms/shannon-channel.md).

**Linguistic-pragmatic tradition.**
- [grice1975](../../bibliographies/grice1975.md) — Grice, "Logic and Conversation". The Cooperative Principle and the four maxims of conversation; load-bearing for [pragmatics](../../../../glossary/concepts/pragmatics.md).
- [halliday1985](../../bibliographies/halliday1985.md) — Halliday, *An Introduction to Functional Grammar*. The systemic-functional account of register and the metafunctions of language; load-bearing for [register](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/references/teaching/explanations/register.md).
- [austin1962](../../bibliographies/austin1962.md) — Austin, *How to Do Things with Words*. The locutionary/illocutionary/perlocutionary triple; founding reference for [speech-act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/objects/speech-act.md).
- [searle1969](../../bibliographies/searle1969.md) — Searle, *Speech Acts*. The systematic theory of felicity conditions and illocutionary types that elaborates Austin into the structural model the [speech-act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/objects/speech-act.md) object inherits.
- [clark1996](../../bibliographies/clark1996.md) — Clark, *Using Language*. The joint-action program and the canonical contemporary statement of common ground as pragmatic substrate; founding reference for [common-ground](../../../../glossary/terms/common-ground.md).

**Social-rhetorical tradition.**
- [bitzer1968](../../bibliographies/bitzer1968.md) — Bitzer, "The Rhetorical Situation". The exigence/audience/constraints triple; founding reference for [rhetorical-situation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/objects/rhetorical-situation.md).

## Further open work

References the theory will need as it develops past minimum-viable status (Bell 1984 on style-shifting, Sperber & Wilson 1986 on relevance, Stalnaker 1978 on the original common-ground update rule, Vatz 1973 as the counter-position to Bitzer, Lewis 1969 on convention as game-theoretic equilibrium) are tracked as open work in the parent [`_index.md`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/_index.md). Adding them is gated on downstream load — when a downstream theory's `depends-on:` would point to one of them, it lands here at that point.
