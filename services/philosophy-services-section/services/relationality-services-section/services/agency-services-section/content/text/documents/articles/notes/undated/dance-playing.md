**Playing** is the live act — play as the verb — that carries one form of the spiral into the next.
Here it is the act of playing a [dance](dance.md): enacting the composed danced form in voluntary, bounded, shared activity.
Playing a dance *with others* is what makes the dancers fall into common time — and a shared pulse is [music](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/dancing/content/dance/library/music/docs/music.md).

## Definition

Playing is grounded in the play literature already derived in-repo under the game tree: Huizinga's *homo ludens* (play as voluntary, bounded, rule-governed activity that is constitutive, not decorative — `homo-ludens.md`) and the distinction of **play** from mere structured activity (play is "voluntary, intrinsically motivated activity pursued for its own sake" — `…/game/library/vocabulary/library/play/play.md`).
Playing is not a new existential face of its own; it is the **hinge** — the live doing that takes a settled form and re-animates it into the next shared field.
It is why the spiral *moves* rather than freezing at any one form.

## Generated by

Playing is generated by **[dance](dance.md)**: a composed dance exists to be played.
The artifact calls for its live enactment.
