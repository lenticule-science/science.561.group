# Sutton 1991 — Dyna

Sutton, R. S. (1991). Dyna, an Integrated Architecture for
Learning, Planning, and Reacting. *ACM SIGART Bulletin*, 2(4),
160–163.

The architectural founding paper of model-based reinforcement
learning. Introduces the Dyna pattern: interleave model-learning
with policy-learning, using each real-environment sample three
ways — to update the policy directly, to update an empirical
model, and to drive multiple additional policy updates from
synthetic experience generated by the model. Establishes the
template that all subsequent model-based RL inherits.
