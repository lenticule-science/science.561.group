The **witness topos** is the cohesive field generated by certified research
cells. If `R = CoKl(C)` is the category of research dynamics, the archived
directed relational account writes the witness topos as sheaves on the opposite
research category:

```text
RITM_* := Shv(R^op, Spaces)
```

This records the Yoneda reading of research: a research program is recovered
from the certified arrows that witness it. The stable claims are not isolated
objects in a container; they are sections and points in the space generated by
their morphisms.

## What generates it

The witness topos is generated by [research certification](research-certification.md).
Certification gives stable cells; sheafing those cells gives a space where local
research acts can glue, compare, and propagate.

## What it generates

The witness topos generates a coherent research field. When that field produces
strictly more informed settled predicates for some resource or theory, it can
support a [researching morphism](researching-morphism.md)
from the prior host theory to a stronger one.
