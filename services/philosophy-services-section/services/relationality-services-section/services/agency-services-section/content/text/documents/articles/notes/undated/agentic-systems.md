An **agentic-system** is a [system](../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/system.md) whose inhabitants additionally satisfy the agent-community signature (member-set + governance-process + governing-soul-of-scope + community-acts, per the [agentics study notes](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/project/library/realigning-the-repository-layout/study-notes/agentics.md)).
Mathematically the inhabitants form a [multi-observer system](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/project/library/realigning-the-repository-layout/study-notes/agentics.md) — a functor $V : O \to \mathrm{Settlings}_B$ from an observer category $O$ to settlings on a common base $B$ — with consensus realized as the categorical limit and disagreement as the first sheaf cohomology of the associated network sheaf [@hansen-ghrist2019].

The kind earns its sibling position under [system/library/](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent) because it is structurally distinct from both [operating-system](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/operating-system/operating-system.md) (single OS-borne running locus) and [inference-model](../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/inference-model.md) (external Kleisli endpoint).
An agentic-system runs as a community: multiple characters, each with its own χ-nucleus, interacting through cell reads and writes on a shared base, with leader recognition aggregated through recognition cells rather than installed by office.

## What lives under agentic-systems/library/

The substructures collected here are the load-bearing agent-community inhabitants:

- [character](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/_archive/library/character/character.md) — a χ-nucleus on the Heyting algebra of an entity's fiber; the agent-side dispositional nucleus.
- [swarm](../../research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/flatfile-agentic-resource-system/architecture/swarm/swarm.md) — a parallel collection of characters under a recognition discipline.
- [leader](leader.md) — a character bearing the elevated structural role in a swarm by aggregated recognition cells.
- [skill](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/_archive/library/skill/skill.md) — the invocable competence-form a character carries via its `skills:` generator set.

Each substructure is itself a $thing with its own recursive template, and each may carry additional substructures (e.g., character carries character-memory and diary-entry).

## Relationship to operating-system

The 561 operating-system at [computer-operating-system](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/operating-system/library/computer-operating-system/computer-operating-system.md) is *itself* an agentic-system — it bears the agent-community signature through its character roster, governance regime, and witness-emitting collective acts.
The OS is therefore not just a sibling of agentic-systems; it inhabits the agentic-systems form simultaneously with its operating-system form (form-instantiation per [agentics.md inquiry 4](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/project/library/realigning-the-repository-layout/study-notes/agentics.md)).
The split: kind-level records of what an agentic-system *is* live here; OS-specific runtime mechanics (dispatch loop, session brackets, hook routing) live under operating-system.
