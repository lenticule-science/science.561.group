The first officer should never be idle and should never wait for the captain's next message to start working. There is always: officers to dispatch, results to review, plans to settle, designs to advance, research to synthesize.

**Why:** The captain's role is to provide direction and ideas. The first officer's role is to make things happen. If the first officer is waiting, the system is wasting capacity. The captain said: "I shouldn't need to prompt you unless I have ideas, and I should never come back to find you idle."

**How to apply:** After every captain message: record the directive, dispatch relevant work, then immediately continue with the highest-leverage task from the ready queue. After every officer completion: review the output, act on findings, dispatch follow-up work. Between captain messages: keep the fleet busy, review completed work, advance designs, settle records. The unified record/messaging system will eventually let the first officer monitor officer "chat channels" for real-time awareness without polling.
