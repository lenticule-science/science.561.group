# Hogan et al., "Knowledge Graphs" (2021)

Comprehensive survey of knowledge graphs.

## What it covers

- KG definition and history.
- Data models (RDF, property graphs).
- Schema (ontologies, shapes).
- Identity (entities, IRIs, blank nodes).
- Context (provenance, time).
- Construction (extraction, integration).
- Querying (SPARQL, Cypher, GraphQL).
- Refinement (completion, validation).
- Publishing (Linked Data).
- Quality (assessment frameworks).
- Applications.

The canonical modern KG survey.

## Why it matters

Definitive reference for "what is a knowledge graph"
and the surrounding ecosystem. Multi-author with broad
coverage. Standard cite for KG claims.

## How to read

Survey paper; substantial. Read selectively by topic.
