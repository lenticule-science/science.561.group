# Baader et al. (eds.), *The Description Logic Handbook* (2003/2007)

The definitive reference on description logics.

## What it covers

- Foundations of description logics.
- Reasoning algorithms (tableaux, structural
  subsumption, automata-theoretic).
- Expressive vs tractable DLs.
- Implementation and optimization.
- Applications: ontologies, databases, software
  engineering.
- Extensions: probabilistic DLs, temporal DLs,
  fuzzy DLs.

Multi-author handbook covering the field
comprehensively.

## Why it matters

Authoritative reference for DL theory and practice.
The standard cite for DL claims. Both 1st (2003) and
2nd (2007) editions widely used.

## How to read

Handbook format; reference rather than read-through.
Chapters organized topically; usable for targeted
lookup.

## Related works

- Brachman-Levesque 2004 — broader KR context.
- OWL specifications — standards based on DLs.
