## Content summary

*Pending direct read.* Representative of the LLM-multi-agent-orchestration framework genre.
MetaGPT's published pitch: assign LLM agents to *roles* drawn from a software-engineering team (product manager, architect, project manager, engineer, QA), have them collaborate on a project specification through a structured workflow that produces standardized intermediate artifacts (PRDs, design documents, task lists, code, tests).
The framework's load-bearing engineering claim is that *Standard Operating Procedures* (SOPs) lifted from human software-engineering practice can be encoded as prompt-templates and inter-agent message-formats, and that LLM agents constrained by these SOPs produce more coherent multi-agent output than free-form agent collaboration.
