# Byzantine-fault-tolerant clusters present a finer replica cover

**Claim.** A Byzantine-fault-tolerant (BFT) consensus cluster of $3F+1$ members tolerating up to $F$ malicious members is, cohomologically, the same engineering surface as a crash-fault-tolerant (CFT) cluster of $2F+1$ members tolerating up to $F$ crashes — read on a *finer* [replica cover](../../../../glossary/concepts/cluster-as-replica-cover.md). The BFT quorum-size discipline is the substrate-level realization of cover refinement: each member's clopen $U_i$ is split into a smaller honesty-resolving sub-cover, and the protocol's larger quorum (typically $2F+1$ of $3F+1$) is the discipline that ensures every honesty-resolving sub-clopen is touched by at least one honest member.

## Why this holds

**Part 1: Adversarial votes are well-formed sections.** Per [vote-as-section](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf/content/distributed-settling-system/content/replica-cover/_archive/teaching/explanations/vote-as-section.md), every signed vote is a well-formed section of $\mathcal{F}_U$ over the voting member's clopen. Adversarial content does not break the section's well-formedness — the signature still verifies, the section is still a function, the cocycle calculation still completes. What adversarial content breaks is the *honesty* of the section: the section's value at points of $U_i$ may not match what an honest observer at $U_i$ would record.

**Part 2: Honesty is a finer cover.** Refine each member $m_i$'s clopen $U_i$ into $U_i = U_i^H \cup U_i^A$, where $U_i^H$ is the honesty-resolving sub-clopen on which $m_i$'s recorded value matches what honest observers record, and $U_i^A$ is the residual on which $m_i$'s recorded value may be adversarial. (For an honest member, $U_i^A = \emptyset$.) The refined family $\{U_i^H, U_i^A\}_i$ is a finer replica cover of $U$ in the sense of the math entity [replica-cover](../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/replica-cover.md): every $U_i$ is the union of the refined pieces, and the cover condition $U = \bigcup_i U_i$ propagates.

**Part 3: BFT quorum-size discipline ensures every $U_i^H$ is hit.** With $N = 3F+1$ members and at most $F$ adversarial, at least $2F+1$ members are honest. A quorum of $2F+1$ members must intersect the set of honest members in at least $F+1$ members (pigeonhole: $2F+1 + 2F+1 > 3F+1$ forces overlap of $F+1$). The recorded quorum's vote-family contains at least $F+1$ honest local sections; cohomologically, at least $F+1$ of the refined cover's $U_i^H$ pieces are touched by recorded sections.

**Part 4: Mayer–Vietoris on the refined cover lifts the honest sub-family.** Per [consensus-as-h0-construction](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf/content/distributed-settling-system/content/replica-cover/_archive/consensus-as-h0-construction.md), Mayer–Vietoris applied to the refined cover lifts the honest sub-family $\{\sigma_i|_{U_i^H}\}_{i \in \text{honest}}$ to a unique global section $s_H \in H^0(U, \mathcal{F}_U)$ — the section the honest members agree on. The BFT protocol's discipline is to commit $s_H$ as the decided value; the additional quorum size is the substrate's mechanism for guaranteeing the recorded vote-family contains enough honest sections to identify $s_H$ via majority overlap-agreement.

**Part 5: Adversarial sub-clopens don't lift.** The adversarial sub-family $\{\sigma_i|_{U_i^A}\}_{i \in \text{adversarial}}$ has at most $F$ members; it cannot constitute a quorum, cannot satisfy the protocol's threshold, and so does not produce a committed decision. The cover-refinement reading makes this concrete: adversarial sections live on a sub-cover too small to produce a valid lift.

## What this theorem buys

- **One conceptual frame for CFT and BFT.** Both are global-section constructions on a replica cover; BFT is the same construction on a refined cover with a quorum-size discipline that guarantees the refinement's honesty-resolving pieces are touched.
- **Quorum-size formulas have a cohomological derivation.** The $3F+1$ membership and $2F+1$ quorum sizes are not arbitrary — they are the smallest sizes for which the cover refinement guarantees Mayer–Vietoris lifts the honest sub-family. Variants (e.g., flexible Paxos quorum-spec where read and write quorums have different sizes) have analogous cohomological readings.
- **BFT audit is a refined-cover audit.** Per [audit-via-cech-cocycle-evaluation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf/content/distributed-settling-system/content/replica-cover/_archive/audit-via-cech-cocycle-evaluation.md), the audit cocycle calculation extends to the refined cover: the audit must check both the per-pair cocycle on the recorded sections *and* a separate honesty check (typically: cross-validation of evidence chains against external trust anchors). The cohomological component is unchanged; the additional honesty check is BFT-specific.

## Engineering consequence

- **Protocol design is dimensional.** Choosing the quorum size for a Byzantine-resistant protocol is choosing how fine a cover refinement to commit to. Coarser refinements (smaller quorums, fewer members) tolerate fewer adversaries; finer refinements tolerate more. The trade-off is structural, not heuristic.
- **Hybrid protocols compose.** A protocol that uses CFT for low-stakes decisions and BFT for high-stakes decisions is operating on two different cover-refinement levels of the same cluster. The cohomological reading makes the hybrid composition well-typed: both protocols produce global sections of the same $\mathcal{F}_U$; the difference is the cover they evaluate over.

## Limits

- **The honesty refinement is observer-relative.** $U_i^H$ depends on what an "honest observer" would record, which depends on the substrate's predicate semantics. For substrates with fully determined predicates (e.g., signed deterministic computation), $U_i^H$ is well-defined; for substrates with under-determined predicates, the refinement is fuzzier and the BFT guarantee correspondingly weaker.
- **Adversaries colluding on coordinated sections.** Malicious members coordinating to produce a coherent set of adversarial sections that together pass per-pair cocycle checks can defeat the audit (per [audit-via-cech-cocycle-evaluation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf/content/distributed-settling-system/content/replica-cover/_archive/audit-via-cech-cocycle-evaluation.md)'s soundness limit). The BFT quorum-size discipline guarantees the *recorded family contains* enough honest sections; it does not guarantee that an *audit* run later can distinguish honest from adversarial sections without separate evidence-validation. Byzantine-resilient audit is a follow-on direction.
- **Membership-change interaction.** BFT protocols handle membership changes via consensus on the new membership; the cover refinement re-derives at each membership change. The interaction between membership-change consensus and decision consensus is the substrate's view-change discipline, which the cover-refinement reading does not by itself address.
