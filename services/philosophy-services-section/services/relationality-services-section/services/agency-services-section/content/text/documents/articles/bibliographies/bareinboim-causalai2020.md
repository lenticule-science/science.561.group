# Bareinboim, Correa, Ibeling & Icard, "On Pearl's Hierarchy and the Foundations of Causal Inference" (2020)

Formal treatment of Pearl's causal hierarchy.

## What it covers

- Formal definition of the causal hierarchy
  (associational, interventional, counterfactual).
- The **causal hierarchy theorem**: for almost all
  SCMs, rung-$k$ questions cannot be answered from
  rung-$(k-1)$ data alone.
- Implications for AI: pure observational learning is
  bounded above by rung-1.
- Connections to identifiability and
  transportability.
- Categorization of "seven tasks of causal AI"
  spanning the hierarchy.

## Why it matters

The formalization of the hierarchy that Pearl
informally introduced. The hierarchy theorem clarifies
exactly what observational data does and doesn't
determine.

## How to read

Mathematically substantial. Read after Pearl 2009 for
formal grounding.

## Related works

- Pearl 2009 — original framework.
- Pearl-Mackenzie 2018 — popular hierarchy

- Shpitser-Pearl 2008 — identification completeness.
