# Demski & Garrabrant 2019 — Embedded Agency

Demski, A., & Garrabrant, S.
(2019).
Embedded Agency. *arXiv:1902.09469*.
(Also published as a four-part essay at the Alignment Forum.)

The systematic survey of the embedded-agency research program.
Identifies the four central problems:

1. **Embedded decision theory** — rational choice for an agent that is part of the world it decides about.
2. **Embedded world models** — representing a universe that contains the agent doing the representing.
3. **Robust delegation** — building successor agents (or self-modifications) that share the original's values.
4. **Subsystem alignment** — ensuring the agent's own subprocesses do not optimize against it.

Each problem is discussed with worked examples and pointers to partial-result literature (logical induction, Functional Decision Theory, reflective oracles, Cartesian frames).
The paper does not propose solutions; it organizes the open problems and articulates why the standard Cartesian framework of decision theory and Bayesian inference does not suffice for agents that are physical subsystems of their environments.

The four-problem structure has been adopted as the de facto table of contents for the agent-foundations subfield.
