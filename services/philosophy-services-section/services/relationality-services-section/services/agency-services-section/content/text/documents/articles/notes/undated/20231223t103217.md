I've come to the decision that I will no longer be updating my Substack. For those who wish to continue following my work, you are welcome to join me at [emsenn's Webplace](http://www.emsenn.net), where I will be posting my latest writings for the foreseeable future.

For those among you who have been contributing and would prefer to support me on a different platform, I recommend migrating to [Ko-fi](http://www.ko-fi.com), where my work will also be accessible.

Allow me to explain my departure:

A recent statement from Hamish McKenzie, a cofounder of Substack, has caused considerable controversy:

> “I just want to make it clear that we don’t like Nazis either—we wish no-one held those views. However, the unfortunate reality is that such extreme views exist. Censorship, or the denial of funding to publications, in our opinion, doesn't eradicate such ideologies; in fact, our stance is that it serves to exacerbate the issue. It is our belief that the most effective method to undermine harmful ideologies is to ensure they are openly debated, thereby placing civil liberties and individual rights at the forefront. Our commitment to preserving the freedom of speech remains resolute, even when the implications are painful.”

This assertion effectively communicates, "Our dedication to upholding the principles of civilization and individualism outweighs all other costs."

Yet, what remains unsaid is a critical point: sexual content faces prohibition on the platform.

There exists an outspoken faction that champions the idea of free speech while actively suppressing sexual expression and bolstering expressions of violence: Christian nationalists are such a group.

The decisions taken to provide a platform and financial backing to Nazi sympathizers while purging sexual content online is not a mere coincidence but a deliberate design choice.

It needs to be said that this issue extends beyond Substack—it permeates the entire spectrum of payment processors influenced by legislation such as FOSTA/SESTA.

These regulations may be cloaked in the language of Progressivism , but the truth is that these systems are inadvertently more effective in advancing the agenda of Christian nationalism than achieving any kind of technological utopia.

Understanding the interplay between these events and the broader narrative of societal change is crucial for anyone who aspires to engage proactively rather than merely reacting to the currents of the time.

Thus, I extend my gratitude to the venture capitalists for inadvertently broadening my readership. Nonetheless, the time has come for me to part ways and venture forth on my own path.
