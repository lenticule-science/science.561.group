## Annotation

The *Prolegomena* is the founding document of glossematics (Hjelmslev's structural-semiotic framework) and the most rigorously structural exposition of sign-theory in the Saussurean lineage. For the structural-semiotic framing, three Hjelmslevian threads are load-bearing:

1. **Form-substance split on each face.** Both the expression-plane and the content-plane split into *form* (the structural shape) and *substance* (the material instantiation). The form is what the discipline studies; the substance is the parameter the form is realised in. This grounds the [hjelmslevian-system](../notes/undated/hjelmslevian-system.md) and the discipline's commitment that *what is structural is invariant under substance*.
2. **Connotative iteration as primitive.** A *connotative semiotic* takes an entire denotative semiotic as its expression-plane and re-relates it to a further content-plane. The iteration composes: connotations of connotations are admissible. This grounds the [denotation](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/semiotics/references/teaching/explanations/denotation.md) / [connotation](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/semiotics/references/teaching/explanations/connotation.md) concepts and the iterated-closure reading of unlimited semiosis.
3. **Metasemiotic.** The system can take itself as object — a metasemiotic has a denotative semiotic as its content-plane. This is what makes the discipline possible: semiotics studies semiotic systems metasemiotically.

Hjelmslev's *function* (the structural relation between expression-form and content-form) is structurally derivative of the system's components; this is one of the two readings the [code-vs-system-distinction](../learning-articles/questions/code-vs-system-distinction.md) open question records.
