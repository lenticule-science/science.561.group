An object $q \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ of the predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ fixed by the [predication-fibration](predication-fibration.md).
A transformer is itself an [entity](entity.md) — it inhabits `transformers/` in the substrate directory.

Transformers appear as the second coordinate of [predication-cells](predication-cell.md): the predicate $q$ in $(e_1, q, e_2)$.
In the FARS encoding, a frontmatter entry `q: e₂` in file $e_1$ asserts the cell $(e_1, q, e_2)$ at $\mathbf{t}$.

The valid relationships between transformers are the morphisms of $\mathcal{C}$, generated by four canonical classes encoded as predication cells using [specializing](../transformers/specializing.md), [conversing](../transformers/conversing.md), [projecting-to-subject](../transformers/projecting-to-subject.md), and [projecting-to-object](../transformers/projecting-to-object.md).
These four classes are, by the predicate-generator-exhaustiveness theorem, the complete set of natural bilattice homomorphisms between predication fiber algebras under $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$-equivariance with $|E| \geq 3$.
