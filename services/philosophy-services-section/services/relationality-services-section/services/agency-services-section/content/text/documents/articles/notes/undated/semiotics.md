Semiotics is the study of *sign-relations* — the structure by which a [sign-vehicle](../../../../glossary/concepts/sign-vehicle.md) stands for a [sign-object](../../../../glossary/concepts/sign-object.md) for an [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) under a [code](../../../../glossary/concepts/code.md), organised within [semiotic systems](../../../../glossary/terms/semiotic-system.md) and composed across systems by [translation](../../../../glossary/terms/translation.md) and [codeswitching](../../../../glossary/terms/codeswitching.md). The discipline is older than linguistics-as-science and broader than philosophy-of-language; it is the substrate-level theory of *standing-for*.

This theory commits to the predicated-knowledge-systems substrate. The committed bridges are made explicit in [composability with predicated-knowledge-systems](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/semiotics/narratives/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md):

- The [sign-vehicle](../../../../glossary/concepts/sign-vehicle.md) reads as the substrate's predication-cell unit.
- The [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) chain reads as the substrate's [closure operator](../../../../glossary/concepts/closure-as-fitting-fixpoint.md) iterating over a starting cell.
- The [semiosphere](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semiosphere.md) reads as the substrate's [discourse-community-as-fiber](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/discourse-community-as-fiber.md).

These bridges are not metaphor; they are the substrate-level reading the lift this theory underwent in the [rubric-application-2026-05-03 audit](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/audits/rubric-application-2026-05-03.md) committed to.

## Position relative to neighbours

- **Linguistics**: this theory is one of linguistics' children; it neighbours [semantics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/semantics/_index.md) (which fixes how *meaning* attaches to a sign) and [languages](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/languages/_index.md) (which catalogues specific natural-language systems). Semiotics is the broader discipline; semantics is its specialisation to sign-systems whose interpretants are propositions.
- **Communication**: this theory shares the *medium* and *register* vocabulary of [humanities/communication](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/humanities/theories/communication/_index.md) and [science/communication](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/communication/_index.md), but its primitive object is the *sign-relation*, not the *channel*. The [inheritance ledger](../../teaching-articles/explanations/what-semiotics-inherits.md) records what is borrowed and what is genuinely added.
- **Predicated knowledge systems**: this theory is *upstream* of the substrate in the discipline's order of explanation — predication is a special case of sign-relation — but *downstream* in the corpus's actual order of construction (the substrate was named first; semiotics is now retroactively typed against it). The composability narrative records both directions.
