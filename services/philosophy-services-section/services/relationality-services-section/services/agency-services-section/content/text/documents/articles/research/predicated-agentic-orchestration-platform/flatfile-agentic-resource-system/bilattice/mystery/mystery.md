The element $\mathbf{\bot} \in \mathbf{FOUR}$.
In the twist-product representation, $\mathbf{\bot} = \langle 0, 0 \rangle$: both evidence coordinates absent.

Knowledge-order position: minimum.
Mystery knowledge-precedes every bilattice-value.
A predication-cell at mystery is one the substrate has not been told anything about.

Truth-order position: strictly between false and true, incomparable with contradiction.
Mystery and contradiction share the same truth-order interval but are incomparable with each other.

In the FARS encoding, absence of a frontmatter entry leaves the predication-cell at mystery.
Mystery is the default.
