**A game is the individual form of becoming** — play settled into a bounded, rule-governed someone: a **player inside a magic circle**.
It is the most-settled turn of the cycle, and the seed of the next.

## Definition

The grounding is the real play literature already derived in-repo under the game tree (`theories/agency/.../swarm/library/multi-agent/concurrent-game-system/library/game/`):

- Huizinga's **homo ludens** — play is voluntary, bounded, rule-governed, and *constitutive of culture*, not decorative (`…/ludics/library/vocabulary/library/homo-ludens/homo-ludens.md`).
- Caillois's **paidia → ludus** spectrum — free, spontaneous play hardening into formal, rule-structured play (`…/paidia/paidia.md`, `…/ludus/ludus.md`); and his four categories **agon / alea / mimicry / ilinx** (competition, chance, simulation, vertigo).
- The **magic circle** — the bounded space within which the game's rules hold and ordinary life is suspended.
- The in-repo distinction that play "becomes a game when it acquires structure — rules, objectives, constraints" (`…/game/library/vocabulary/library/play/play.md`).

Read existentially: a game is play **glued to its fixed point** — a self bounded by rules and a circle, persisting under law.
That is precisely the **individual** face of the quartet (animate / agential / agentic / **individual**), the same phenomenon [individual.md](individual.md) names as "a self that has settled into a distinct, bounded existent."
Music's open shared pulse, given rules, crystallizes into the bounded player; that crystallization is the game.

## Generated by

A game is generated by **music** through **[playing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/dancing/content/dance/playing/content/music/docs/playing.md)**: the played musical field, taking on rules and bounds (paidia → ludus), settles into rule-governed play.
The shared pulse becomes a shared *game*.

## Generates — the spiral closes

The game **re-seeds becoming**.
This is not a new claim but the re-entry [individual.md](individual.md) already establishes: the settled individual carries a charge of pre-individual potential that can drive further individuation, and re-enters as the seed of its own four-fold descent.
The game is exactly such a settled individual — a bounded player full of residual potential — and so it is the **seed of the next becoming**.
Caillois's own spectrum says it: paidia never disappears into ludus; the free play held inside the bounded game is the charge that opens the next turn.

So the cycle bites its tail:

> **becoming → dancing → music → game → becoming → dancing → …**

One turn through all four faces (animate → agential → agentic → individual), then the individual game re-seeds the animate becoming of the next stratum.
That return is what makes the structure a **spiral**, not a line — and it is why dancing, the agential form of *music*, is at the same time the agential form of *becoming itself*.
