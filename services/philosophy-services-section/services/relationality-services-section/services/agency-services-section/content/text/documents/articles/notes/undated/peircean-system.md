# Peircean semiotic system

A worked instance of [semiotic-system](../../../../glossary/terms/semiotic-system.md) under Peirce's commitments (per [Peirce CP](../../bibliographies/peirce-cp.md)).

## Components

- $V$ — *representamen inventory*: anything that can stand for something for someone in some respect; not restricted to linguistic items.
- $O$ — *object range*: open; admits both immediate object (as the sign represents) and dynamic object (as it is independent of the sign).
- $I$ — *interpretant repertoire*: open; admits immediate, dynamic, and final interpretants. Each interpretant is itself a sign, available as the sign-vehicle of a further sign-relation.
- $K$ — *code*: the threefold trichotomy of sign-typology (icon/index/symbol on the sign-vehicle/object face; rheme/dicent/argument on the interpretant face; qualisign/sinsign/legisign on the sign-vehicle face), giving 10 admissible classes of sign.
- $R$ — *rule system*: the inferential rules of pragmatistic grounding — the meaning of a sign is the totality of its practical bearings, with practical bearings derived through the interpretant chain.
- $\Sigma$ — *semiosphere*: implicit; Peirce did not name the semiosphere as a primitive but presupposed a community-of-inquiry whose convergent reading determines the final interpretant.

## Distinctive features

- **Triadic and irreducible.** The interpretant is not optional; without it the sign-relation collapses into a name-and-thing pair.
- **Open-chain interpretant.** Each interpretant generates further interpretants without a structural cap; the chain runs to its convergent reading in the indefinite-prolongation of inquiry.
- **Pragmaticist closure.** The rule system grounds meaning in practical consequences; the closure operator's iterates are the practical bearings the sign-relation generates.

## Substrate reading

The Peircean system instantiates [predicated knowledge system](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/objects/predicated-knowledge-system.md) under the *transfinite-bounded* regime: the open-chain interpretant corresponds to a closure-rule system whose iterates converge at a countable ordinal $\kappa < \omega_1$ rather than at $\omega$. The "indefinite prolongation of inquiry" reads as the substrate's transfinite settling under monotone-but-not-Scott-continuous rules. The convergent final-interpretant is the bilattice-$\leq_k$-least fixpoint at $\kappa$.

## Bridge to substrate operations

- *Specialise* — runs the icon-index-symbol gradient and the qualisign-sinsign-legisign gradient as paradigmatic specialisations.
- *Translate* — Peirce's writings on the relation between sign-systems (especially the transition between phaneroscopic categories) read as the federate-with-identity-mapping operation.
- *Codeswitch* — Peirce's existential graphs and algebraic notation are two codes operated together; mathematical work routinely codeswitches between them.
