A "psyop aesthetic reading" is the practice of reading a cultural or political artifact for the operations it performs on its subjects' affect, self-understanding, and political grammar — without assuming that these operations are coordinated by a central agent.
The term *psyop* (psychological operation) is used here not to assert that the artifact is the product of a military or state information-operation, but to name the analytical posture: reading for what the artifact does to minds and feelings, rather than reading for what it means.

This tutorial walks through a complete psyop aesthetic reading from start to finish.
As with the rubber-hose tutorial, perform the reading on an artifact you choose before and alongside reading the demonstration.

## Choose your artifact

Choose one of the following for your first psyop aesthetic reading:

1. A wellness influencer's content series — 5–10 posts or videos from a single account that addresses a theme (burnout, healing, boundaries, growth)
2. A political movement's social media campaign — 5–10 posts from a coordinated hashtag or campaign
3. A technology company's brand campaign — not a product launch but a brand identity campaign (the ones that say who the company "is")
4. A charity or nonprofit's fundraising appeal

Each of these types is a reliable site for the operations predicated sociology analyzes: [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/reading/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md), [moral displacement](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/reading/content/teaching/explanations/moral-displacement.md), [fascist grammar](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fascist-grammar.md) moves, and [ontogenric](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontogenring.md) subject-formation.
Each produces specific affective and self-understanding effects on its audiences.

## The reading: a worked demonstration

For this demonstration, I am reading a hypothetical but entirely typical wellness-influencer content series — the kind organized around the theme of "burnout recovery" or "healing from hustle culture."
I will mark the analytical moves.

---

**[Initial setup]** The account's handle contains words like "heal," "root," "grounded," or "rest."
The visual aesthetic is consistent: warm tones, natural textures (linen, wood, plants), a single human figure in a calm domestic setting or in nature.
The account has a large following and strong engagement.

**[Move 1: Identify the structural conditions being addressed]** Burnout.
The content addresses burnout as a widespread condition — the influencer acknowledges it, names it, validates the audience's experience of it.
Burnout is a real condition.
Its structural causes: excessive work demands relative to compensation and autonomy, the erosion of clear work-life boundaries in gig-economy and professional-class labor, the chronic activation produced by constant connectivity and the threat of economic precarity.
These structural causes are real, empirically documented, and increasingly severe.

**[Move 2: How does the content address the structural conditions?]** The content addresses burnout through a personal-healing arc: the influencer narrates their own burnout and recovery, provides practices (morning routines, boundary-setting scripts, rest practices, nourishing foods), and invites the audience into the recovery arc.
The structural causes of burnout — the labor market conditions, the platform economy's demands, the erosion of protections — do not appear in the content.
The content's address to burnout is entirely at the level of the individual's relationship to their experience of the structural conditions.

**[Moral displacement identification]** The content performs [moral displacement](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/reading/content/teaching/explanations/moral-displacement.md) by locating the accountability for burnout's resolution in the individual's practice.
The structural conditions that produce burnout are not addressed; the individual's relationship to those conditions is what can be changed.
"You can't pour from an empty cup" is the displacement in slogan form: the problem is the individual's depletion, not the structural demand for continuous pouring.

**[Move 3: Identify the [ontogenric](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontogenring.md) genre being distributed]** The content distributes the *healing-self* genre: the subject who is recovering from burnout, on a trajectory from depleted-and-overextended to nourished-and-boundaried.
The genre has specific features: the healing subject prioritizes rest, practices nourishment, sets limits, invests in self-knowledge.
The genre's trajectory is personal growth; the structural conditions are the background against which the personal growth occurs.

**[Move 4: Identify [zen fascism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zen-fascism.md)]** The account's feeling rules are typical zen-fascist: the authorized affective registers are calm, grounded, gentle, soft, and compassionate-toward-self.
The unauthorized affective registers are visible in what the content never shows: rage at the conditions producing the burnout, indignation at the structural arrangement, despair that is not formatted as a step in the healing arc.
Comments that express rage or structural critique are not responded to or are gently redirected toward the healing practice.
Serenity is the authorized register; the structural recognition that would require a different register is foreclosed by the genre's feeling rules.

**[Move 5: Identify [fascist grammar](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fascist-grammar.md) moves if present]** The wellness influencer content is not standard fascist grammar, but several moves are embedded:

- **Crisis:** burnout is named as a crisis.
  "Hustle culture" is named as the corrupting force (the nearest thing to a corrupt-enemy move in this genre: hustle culture and the toxic workplace are the enemy of the healing community).
- **Pure community:** the healing community — the people who have chosen to prioritize rest and recovery — is implicitly defined as pure against the hustle culture they are recovering from.
- **Suffering as sacrifice:** the burnout experience is represented as a necessary passage — "your burnout was your body's wisdom asking for a different way."
  The suffering is meaningful, a signal, a teacher.

The grammar is attenuated and inverted compared to political fascist grammar, but the structural moves are recognizable.

**[Move 6: Identify the [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/reading/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md) circuit]** The full californication circuit is running:

- Structural contradiction: the labor conditions of late-liberal platformal economy produce burnout at scale
- Formatted as personal condition: the burnout is addressed as the individual's experience of depletion
- Metabolized through personal self-regulation: the healing practice is the self-regulation that manages the experience
- The self-regulation sustains participation: the healed/healing subject returns to the conditions that produced the burnout, now better equipped to manage them — the circuit completes

---

## What the reading establishes

The reading establishes: the wellness content series addresses real structural conditions (burnout under late-liberal labor conditions) through a genre that performs moral displacement, distributes a specific ontogenric self-genre (the healing-self), enforces zen-fascist feeling rules, embeds attenuated fascist grammar, and closes the californication circuit by routing the structural conditions through personal self-regulation.

## What the reading does not establish

- That the influencer is acting in bad faith or is aware of these operations
- That the practices distributed (rest, boundary-setting, nourishment) are without value
- That the audience members are deceived or without agency
- That following the account is wrong

The reading establishes structural operations.
The political and ethical conclusions from the structural operations are for the reader.

## Your reading

Perform the six-move analysis on your chosen artifact.
Where in the content are the structural conditions being addressed?
How is the address performing moral displacement?
What ontogenric genre is being distributed?
Are zen-fascist feeling rules operating?
What grammar moves are present?
Where does the californication circuit close?

Write the analysis out before returning to the how-to procedures to check completeness.
Your first reading will miss things; the procedure is for identifying what.
What your first reading catches unaided is your starting point.
