Communication theory studies how meaning moves between agents — what makes a transmission legible, what conventions a channel imposes on what can be said over it, what happens when sender and receiver hold different priors, what the *form* of a message contributes beyond its content.

Three live traditions sit under this heading:

1. **Mathematical / cybernetic.** Shannon's source-channel-receiver model, information-theoretic limits, error correction, feedback. Closely related to [information theory](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/information/theories/information-theory) and [cybernetics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/information/theories/cybernetics); this corpus treats communication theory and information theory as cousins, not parent-child.
2. **Linguistic / pragmatic.** Speech acts, Gricean maxims, deixis, register, genre. Closely related to [linguistics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/linguistics) and [semiotics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/linguistics/theories/semiotics).
3. **Social / rhetorical.** Audience, ethos/pathos/logos, situated address, the rhetorical situation. Closely related to [rhetoric and composition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/humanities/theories/writing/theories/rhetoric-and-composition) under humanities.

## Relation to humanities/communication

The corpus also carries a sibling theory at [humanities/theories/communication/](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/humanities/theories/communication/_index.md) which develops a different axis: the McCloud / Andersen / Avigad / Macbeth closure-discipline framework — units, gaps, [accountable-closure](../../../../glossary/concepts/accountable-closure.md), [medium-specific-rigor](../../../../glossary/concepts/medium-specific-rigor.md), [transport](../../../../glossary/concepts/transport.md) across registers. That theory explicitly distinguishes itself from Shannon (see [communication-is-not-shannon](../../../../glossary/concepts/communication-is-not-shannon.md)).

The two theories are not rivals; they cover different questions. The science-side theory carries the source-channel-receiver tradition (where this corpus's [audience](../../../../glossary/concepts/audience.md), [channel](../../../../glossary/concepts/channel.md), [register](../../../../glossary/concepts/register.md), [genre](../../../../glossary/concepts/genre.md), [pragmatics](../../../../glossary/concepts/pragmatics.md) anchors live). The humanities-side theory carries the closure-discipline anchors. Whether they should be unified into one theory, federated under a shared parent, or remain as parallel registers is an open captain-decisions question — see the [predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system curation-conformance audit](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/audits/curation-conformance-audit.md), section "Captain decisions required", row C-1.

## Status

The science-side theory has lifted past minimum-viable: it now carries its load-bearing concepts (audience, channel, register, genre, pragmatics), four formal objects (one per tradition plus the pragmatic substrate), seven references, and one captain-tagged question. Downstream theories' `depends-on:` edges resolve to real content rather than to a stub.

The substantive *development* of each tradition (deeper theorems, more morphisms, the conversation-as-game and dialogue-act extensions) is still open. The theory does not pretend to settle the linguistic-pragmatic / social-rhetorical / mathematical-cybernetic split; instead it carries one canonical formal model from each and lets downstream theories pick which one they load on.
