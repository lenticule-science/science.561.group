# Meta-moduli prestack of closure-rule systems

The pseudo-functor

$$\mathscr{M}^{\mathrm{meta}}_{\mathrm{pre}} \;:\; \mathbf{B}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Gpd}, \qquad (\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda) \;\mapsto\; \mathrm{Sys}^{\sim}(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda),$$

sending each base to the **groupoid of closure-rule-systems over that base, modulo closure-equivalence**.

This is the substrate's analogue one level up: where `M^pre` classifies settlements up to MLD-equivalence (models), `M^meta_pre` classifies **closure-rule-systems** up to closure-equivalence (theories). The **Lindenbaum-Tarski move** from "models" to "theories" at the moduli level.

## The fiber groupoid `Sys^~(B)`

For each base `B`:

- **0-cells**: closure-rule-systems over `B`.
- **1-cells**: closure-equivalences — pairs of rule-systems `(S, S')` such that they generate the same closure-operator `cl^S = cl^{S'}`.
- **2-cells**: identities (the groupoid is effectively 1-truncated by the closure-equivalence-1-truncation theorem).

The 1-cells of `Sys^~(B)` are exactly the witnesses that two rule-systems are extensionally equivalent (generate the same closure operator) — possibly differing syntactically (different rule-counts, different exact rule shapes) but agreeing on what they produce.

## Lindenbaum-Tarski analogue

In classical model theory, the Lindenbaum-Tarski algebra of a theory `T` is `T/~` where `~` identifies formulas with the same truth-value across models. This passes from "syntactic theories" to "equivalence-classes of theories" — the move from `T` to `T/~` is structurally similar.

Here:

- **Classical**: theory `T` → equivalence-class `[T]` modulo provability.
- **Substrate `M^meta_pre`**: closure-rule-system `S` → equivalence-class `[S]` modulo closure-equivalence.

Both moves classify "theories that say the same thing" as single moduli points.

## Two-tier moduli structure

The two prestacks fit into a **two-tier classification**:

| Tier | Prestack | Classifies | Modulo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model-tier | `M^pre` (settlement-moduli-prestack) | settlements | MLD-equivalence |
| Theory-tier | `M^meta_pre` (this $thing) | closure-rule-systems | closure-equivalence |

Each settlement is generated by *some* closure-rule-system; each closure-rule-system generates *many* settlements (one per initial valuation). So `M^pre` and `M^meta_pre` are related by a forgetful map (forget which rule-system generated a settlement, remember only the settlement itself).
