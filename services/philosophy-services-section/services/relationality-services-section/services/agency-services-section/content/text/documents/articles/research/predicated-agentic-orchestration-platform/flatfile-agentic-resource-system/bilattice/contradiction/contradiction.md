The element $\mathbf{\top} \in \mathbf{FOUR}$.
In the twist-product representation, $\mathbf{\top} = \langle 1, 1 \rangle$: both evidence coordinates present.

Knowledge-order position: maximum.
Every bilattice-value knowledge-precedes contradiction.
It carries the maximum amount of information — both for and against evidence received — but that information is in conflict.

Truth-order position: strictly between false and true, incomparable with mystery.
Contradiction is not directly expressible as a frontmatter assertion; it arises when the bilattice algebra accumulates conflicting evidence over a cell.
