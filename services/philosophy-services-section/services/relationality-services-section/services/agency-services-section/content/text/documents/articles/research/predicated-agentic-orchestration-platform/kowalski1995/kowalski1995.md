## Content summary

*Pending direct read.* Representative of Kowalski's broader logic-programming-for-agents program (the "Computational Logic and Human Thinking" line, including the *agents-as-logic-programs* approach and the LPS / KELPS reactive-rational-agent framework).
The chapter's load-bearing claim: the apparent tension between *reactive* agents (event-driven, condition-action rules) and *rational* agents (deliberative, goal-directed, plan-following) is resolvable through *meta-logic* — a logical framework in which the agent's reactive behavior is itself the logical consequence of higher-level reasoning principles.
The unification puts logic programming forward as the agent-architecture substrate that admits both the reactive and rational modes uniformly.
