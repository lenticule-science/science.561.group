**Moral physics** is the set of rules an artifact's world quietly enforces about consequence, persistence, and causal weight — the substrate that determines what *can happen* in the world the artifact constructs.
These rules are not declared; they are taught through repetition: the way a body bounces back, the way a death is or is not registered, the way the next scene does or does not remember the last.
Moral physics is the load-bearing layer beneath genre: a film can declare itself a war film and operate in a moral physics where soldiers reset between scenes; a wellness app can declare itself therapeutic and operate in a moral physics where structural conditions cannot exert force on individual feelings.

## Five axes

**Persistence of harm.** Does damage stay?
When a character is injured, is the injury present in the next scene, the next episode, the next season?
A moral physics in which harm persists treats consequence as cumulative.
A moral physics in which harm dissolves — [consequence-free violence](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/30/2026-04-30-consequence-free-violence.md) — treats each scene as fresh.
The reset is the primary diagnostic.

**Locus of causation.** Where do events come from?
In some moral physics, events arise from structures (institutions, economies, ecologies).
In others, they arise from individual choices (the hero's pluck, the villain's malice).
In others still, events arise from no traceable source — they simply happen, and characters react.
The locus of causation determines what kind of action makes sense to take in response: structural causation implies structural response; individual causation implies individual response; traceless causation implies endurance without response.

**Reversibility of catastrophe.** Can the worst be undone?
A moral physics with reversible catastrophe — disasters reset, regimes can be voted out, bodies reassemble — invites a different ethical posture than one in which catastrophe leaves a permanent mark.
The political subject trained on reversible catastrophe carries the expectation into their reading of actually-irreversible structural conditions: climate destabilization, colonial harm, carceral accumulation.

**Weight of bodies.** Do bodies have mass, friction, fragility?
A body that cannot be permanently broken cannot be the subject of a permanent injustice; the moral physics of weightless bodies makes certain moral claims literally illegitimate within the artifact's world.
[Rubber-hose realism](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/essays/rubber-hose-realism.md) is the extreme version: bodies that cannot accumulate harm.

**Memory of the world.** Does the world remember what happened?
A moral physics with memory carries forward the accumulated state.
A moral physics without memory begins each scene fresh, unencumbered, as if prior events had not occurred.
The latter approaches the structure of dream or comedy of repetition; the former approaches the structure of history and of genuine grievance.

## Moral physics is not realism

Moral physics is orthogonal to stylistic realism.
A surrealist film can have a heavy, persistent moral physics in which every catastrophe leaves residue.
A photorealistic war film can have a featherlight moral physics in which protagonists fall from buildings and crack jokes by the next reel.
The look is genre; the rule is physics.
The most politically significant moral physics often runs underneath the most realistic-looking surfaces — the documentary aesthetic that presents a genre figure's suffering without registering it as a permanent alteration of the world.

## Relation to other terms

Moral physics interlocks with every other term in this corpus that concerns what an artifact does to its audience.

- [Genring](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genring.md) reduces a living people to a literary type.
  Moral physics determines what *can happen* to that type once produced.
  A genre figure rendered inside a consequence-free moral physics cannot be the subject of a real grievance, because the physics has decided that grievances do not accumulate.
- [Genre calibration](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genre-calibration.md) tunes audience expectation about how situations unfold.
  Moral physics is the substrate that makes calibration possible: the audience knows a setup will resolve into a punchline because they have been trained in a physics where setups always do.
- [Harm governance](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/harm-governance.md) collapses care into risk management.
  Moral physics is the cultural training that prepares subjects to accept this collapse: a population accustomed to consequence-free violence on screen has already been given the affective grammar for harm-without-accountability off screen.
- [Affective looping](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/formatting/content/moral-physics/_archive/affective-looping.md) holds the viewer in a feeling-state through repetition.
  The feeling-state must be legible within the artifact's moral physics: a loop sustaining dread only works in a moral physics where the threat is persistent enough to sustain dread across repetitions.

## Political significance

The political significance of moral physics analysis is that it reveals the ideology operating beneath propositional content.
An artifact can argue explicitly for justice while its moral physics treats injustice as reversible.
An artifact can feature sympathetic marginalized characters while its moral physics forecloses the structural causation of their marginalization.
The propositional layer is what the audience consciously engages with; the physics layer is what they are trained in through repetition.
Ideology analysis that stops at the propositional layer has stopped one layer too soon. ---
