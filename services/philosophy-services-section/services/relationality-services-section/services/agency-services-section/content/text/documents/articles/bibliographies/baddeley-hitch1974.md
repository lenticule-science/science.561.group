# Baddeley & Hitch 1974

Baddeley, A. D., & Hitch, G. (1974). Working Memory. In G. H.
Bower (Ed.), *Psychology of Learning and Motivation* (Vol. 8,
pp. 47-89). Academic Press.

The introduction of *working memory* as an active manipulation
system, distinguished from passive short-term storage. The
model includes a *phonological loop* (verbal rehearsal), a
*visuospatial sketchpad* (visual/spatial manipulation), and a
*central executive* (control and coordination). Later
extensions added an *episodic buffer* (Baddeley 2000) to
explain integration of multimodal information.

The framework reframed short-term memory as *substrate for
cognition* rather than just a passive holding buffer. The
analogy to LLM in-context state and to recurrent network
hidden state is direct, though imperfect.
