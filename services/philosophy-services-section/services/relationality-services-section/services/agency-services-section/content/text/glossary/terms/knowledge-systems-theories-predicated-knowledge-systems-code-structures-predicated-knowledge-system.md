# Predicated knowledge system

A **predicated knowledge system** is a tuple $\mathcal{K} = (\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \mathcal{S}, \Sigma)$ where:

- $\mathbf{L}$ is a [residuated lattice](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/residuated-lattice.md), the per-coordinate value-domain.
  The standard reading takes $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$, giving the four-element bilattice $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie = \mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2} = \mathbf{FOUR}$ on each predication fiber.
- $\mathcal{C}$ is a [predicate category](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) — the system's predicate vocabulary together with the morphisms (specialisation, inversion, composition, fusion) generated by the [predicate-category generator theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/predication-fibration/explanations/exhaustiveness-of-four-predication-cell-complex-site-generators.md). $\mathcal{C}$ is typically stratified; the upper-ontology stratum (continuant/occurrent or endurant/perdurant/quality/abstract) and the domain stratum (per discourse community) sit in $\mathcal{C}$ as nested sub-categories.
- $E$ is the system's entity set, the carrier of the substrate's [predication-cell-set](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-cell-set.md) $\Phi = E \times \mathcal{C} \times E$.
- $\mathcal{S}$ is a [closure-rule system](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-system.md) over the substrate, classified by the [four-regime dichotomy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md).
- $\Sigma = \{S_i\}_{i \in I}$ is a family of [scopes](../../documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/scope/scope.md), each of which restricts $\mathcal{C}$, $\mathcal{S}$, and $E$ to a sub-system corresponding to a [discourse community](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/teaching/explanations/discourse-community-as-fiber.md).

A *state* of $\mathcal{K}$ is a [predication-settling](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) — a fiber-algebra-valued history over an [increment sequence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/increment-sequence.md) of asserted cells under $\mathcal{S}$'s closure operator.
The system's *knowledge* is the settling's converged content; its *coverage* is the entity-and-predicate region in which any settling is non-$\bot$; its *consistency* is the absence of $\top$-valued cells in scopes that reject contestation.

## What this object is

A predicated knowledge system is the named structural object that the [predicated-knowledge-systems](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/_index.md) subtheory's synthesis identifies as the substrate of any knowledge system that adopts the predication-cell.
The five components correspond, respectively, to: the value-domain choice (Belnap → Fitting → Busaniche-Cignoli line), the predicate-vocabulary choice (Frege → Lawvere → upper-ontologies line), the entity-set choice (Topic Maps' subject-identifier discipline), the closure-rule-language choice (Datalog → Lawvere-hyperdoctrine → Maruyama line), and the scope discipline (Cyc microtheories → discourse-communities → Knorr-Cetina epistemic cultures).

A predicated knowledge system is not a *theory of all possible knowledge*; it is a *substrate parameterisation* — a specific choice of $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \mathcal{S}, \Sigma)$ on which a knowledge community runs its work.
Different communities adopt different parameterisations; the substrate machinery (predication-fibration, fiber algebra, closure-driven settling, four-regime classification, federation discipline) is invariant.

## What this object is not

A predicated knowledge system is not:

- A first-order theory in the model-theoretic sense.
  The substrate's value-domain is the bilattice, not classical $\mathbf{2}$; the substrate admits paraconsistent and intuitionistic readings that classical first-order theories exclude.
- A description-logic knowledge base.
  A DL KB is a special case of a predicated knowledge system in which $\mathcal{S}$ is the closure-rule encoding of the chosen DL fragment and $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$.
  The substrate is general over DL fragments and over the value-domain choice.
- An ontology in the BFO/DOLCE/SUMO/Cyc sense.
  An upper ontology is a *choice of $\mathcal{C}$*; the substrate is what the choice is made *on*.
  Per [upper-ontologies-as-predicate-vocabularies](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/texts/upper-ontologies-as-predicate-vocabularies.md), an upper ontology selects the predicate-category schema, and the substrate then runs against that schema.
