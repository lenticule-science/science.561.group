# @561-group/theories/existence

**A theory of existence, derived as a four-fold descent.** The method is structural: the directory tree *is* the argument.
Each term lives at the path where it is generated — nested inside the term it comes from — so that reading inward *is* following the derivation.
There are no essays standing in for the structure; the nesting is the structure.

## The descent

Existence is read in four moments, running from the most open to the most settled — from the bare event of coming-to-be down to bounded, persisting order.
Each moment is generated *within* the one before it, not set beside it:

| moment | what it is | agency-face | grounded in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[becoming](becoming.md)** | that anything comes to be at all — the event before the thing | animate | process philosophy: Heraclitus, Bergson, Whitehead, Deleuze |
| **[doing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/becoming/doing/docs/doing.md)** | the someone acting — the bounded deed | agential | philosophy of action (Anscombe, Davidson); Arendt |
| **[working](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/becoming/doing/working/docs/working.md)** | acting made shared and ongoing on a common field | agentic | Marx, Arendt; coordination through a field (Hayek, activity theory) |
| **[governing](governing.md)** | the open field brought under a settled, bounded order | individual | political order; Ostrom on the commons |

This is the classical reversal of substance metaphysics.
Where that tradition begins from the finished individual and treats becoming as something that happens to it, this theory begins from becoming and *earns* the individual last, as the fixed point at which the descent comes to rest.
The path `becoming/doing/working/governing` is that descent, and its final term, the **[individual](individual.md)**, is settled below and open above: a bounded self that, carrying its unspent potential (Simondon), re-seeds the same four-fold descent at the *social* scale — relating, community, institution.

## The two families

Two strands run the length of the descent.

The **agency** strand is the spine above: becoming's animate someone is specified into an actor (doing), a contributor on a shared field (working), and a bounded self under law (governing).
Its native act is **[speaking](speaking.md)** — Austin's speech act, anatomized into locution, illocution, and perlocution.

The **doxastic** strand runs the same four moments as the stages of an inquiry, and tracks Peirce's passage from doubt to settled belief: **[question](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/becoming/library/question/docs/question.md)** (a content raised and held open) → **[judgement](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/becoming/doing/library/judgement/docs/judgement.md)** (the claim weighed) → **[belief](belief.md)** (the evidence-weighted held position) → **[knowledge](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/becoming/doing/working/governing/library/knowledge/docs/knowledge.md)** (belief settled to a fixed point — determination, in Peirce's fallibilist sense, not certainty).
A settled answer re-opens as the next question; inquiry spirals.

## How to read each document

Every `<term>/docs/<term>.md` does three things: it **defines** the term on its own philosophical ground, names what **generates** it (the term it is nested within), and names what it **generates** in turn (its issue, and the next moment nested inside it).
The terms are drawn from the actual traditions that study them — process philosophy, the philosophy of action, epistemology, speech-act theory, the phenomenology of world — and stated as such.
The four-fold descent and its four agency-faces (animate, agential, agentic, individual) are the theory's own organizing claim, the thread on which those traditions are strung.
