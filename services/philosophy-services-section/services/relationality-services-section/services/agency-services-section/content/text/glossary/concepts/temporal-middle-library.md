**The temporal middle** is the position of inheriting, continuing, and transforming a tradition without either renewing it wholesale or abandoning it.
Every living person occupies this position.
No one is purely "traditional" — frozen in the past, expressing an original culture unchanged since ancient times — or purely "modern" — severed from the past, operating free of inherited formation.
People inherit what they inherit, transform what they can, and continue.
The temporal middle names this universal condition of living culture.

The concept's analytic work is not to name an obvious fact but to name what [settlerism](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/04/27/2026-04-27-settlerism.md) and [genring](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genring.md) systematically eliminate.
Settler-colonial formations and the genre figures they produce offer only two positions: the traditional subject (who matches the assigned genre — the Noble Indian, the Mountain Kurd, the Hearty Bavarian, the Authentic Peasant, the folk-type unchanged since ancient times) and the assimilated subject (who has abandoned the genre and joined the dominant culture).
The temporal middle — where every actually-living person stands — is excluded from the available subject-positions.

## How the elimination works

A Lakota mathematician occupies the temporal middle: inheriting Lakota intellectual traditions, engaging with European mathematical traditions, continuing both.
A Mohawk steelworker who carries a Haudenosaunee passport occupies it.
A Kurdish filmmaker who makes Cannes-winning cinema in Kurmanji occupies it.
But genre figures cannot accommodate these people because genre requires stasis.
The genre figure of the Noble Indian is historically prior and therefore presently absent; a contemporary Lakota who is also a mathematician is not legible within the figure.
The genre offers the traditional Indian or the assimilated Indian.
The middle position, where the living person actually stands, is the one the genre eliminates.

The elimination operates at multiple scales simultaneously:

**Physical.** Land seizure and population displacement sever the relational network within which cultural inheritance-and-transformation occurred.
The temporal middle requires a land-based relational web to continue; destroying the web destroys the condition of the middle's possibility.

**Legal.** Blood-quantum legislation and citizenship frameworks define indigeneity as a fixed condition (you are or you are not, by degree of documented ancestry) rather than as a living relational practice.
Legal definition substitutes the traditional/assimilated binary for the temporal middle.

**Epistemic.** Boarding schools, residential schools, and assimilationist educational programs explicitly target the inheritance-and-transformation structure — cutting the subject off from the tradition they were inheriting, installing the dominant culture's tradition in its place, making the cut itself difficult to name as a violence rather than as an improvement.

**Linguistic.** Capturing the language of cultural continuity as evidence of inauthenticity.
Indigenous people who speak their language fluently are read as performing authenticity rather than practicing inheritance; those who do not are read as having assimilated.
The genre forecloses the actually-lived condition of partial inheritance, transformation, and continuation in any given generation.

## Refusal as the temporal middle's practice

Audra Simpson's study of Kahnawake Mohawks (2014) documents the temporal middle through the practice of *refusal* — declining to perform the genre, carrying Haudenosaunee passports, asserting sovereignty by withdrawing from the recognition framework.
Refusal is the temporal middle practiced in a world built to eliminate it: it does not claim the traditional position (which would require performing the genre) or the assimilated position (which would require abandoning sovereignty).
It claims the third position that the binary is structured to foreclose.

The recognition framework that Coulthard (2014) critiques is the liberal-political form of the same binary: the state offers recognition of indigeneity on the condition that the recognized indigeneity matches the state's genre figure.
A recognition framework operating through [liberal ontologizing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-ontologizing.md) can only recognize what the ontology makes legible.
The temporal middle, which is a relational-inheritance structure rather than a substance with inherent properties, is not legible within the liberal ontology.
Refusal is the practice that holds the temporal middle open against the framework's foreclosure.

## Relation to settlerism and genre-coherence

[Settlerism](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/04/27/2026-04-27-settlerism.md) requires the elimination of the temporal middle as its metaphysical condition.
The legitimation-through-erasure operation depends on rendering indigenous presence as historically prior and therefore presently absent.
A living person who inherits, continues, and transforms their tradition refutes the erasure by existing.
The temporal middle is not a theoretical position but an empirical refutation of the settler genre — which is why the settler formation must continuously re-eliminate it rather than eliminating it once.

[Genre coherence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/representing/content/liberal-ontologizing/representing/content/genring/content/temporal-middle/_archive/genre-coherence.md) provides the mechanism by which the elimination is distributed and enforced.
The genre-figure of the traditional Indian exercises pressure on any living Indigenous person to either perform the figure's assigned attributes (unchanged since ancient times, spiritually connected to nature, pre-modern) or be read as assimilated.
The pressure is not applied by any single authority; it operates through the distributed social affect that genre-coherence generates.
The Lakota mathematician does not fit the figure and the feeling of inauthenticity this produces in audiences trained on the genre is genre-coherence at work.

[Californian projection](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/27/2026-04-27-californian-projection.md) names the specific apparatus through which the temporal middle's elimination has been exported globally as a subject-formatting template.
Each iteration of the projection (the Missions, the Gold Rush, Hollywood, Silicon Valley) requires the cleared space that the temporal middle's elimination produces, and each iteration exports a new version of the traditional/modern binary as the governing framework for understanding cultural difference.
