## Annotation

The international standard defining Topic Maps as a knowledge-representation paradigm. A *topic map* consists of *topics* (representations of subjects), *associations* (typed n-ary relations among topics), and *occurrences* (links from a topic to information resources that are about it, optionally typed). Subject identity is established by *subject identifiers* (URIs) or *subject locators* (URIs of resources that *are* the subject). The standard's central invariant is the *Topic Naming Constraint* — within a single scope, a topic name uniquely identifies its topic.

The predicated framing recognises Topic Maps as a substrate that is *almost* the predication-cell substrate, with three structural restrictions: (i) every association is *symmetric in its role-players* up to role-typing, which makes its fiber algebra a *symmetric* monoidal substructure rather than the full residuated lattice; (ii) the value-domain is implicitly $\mathbf{2}$ (a topic either has an association or it does not), not $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$ — there is no separate negative-evidence channel; (iii) closure is *reified subject identification* (the merging of two topics with the same subject identifier) rather than rule-driven settling. The [topic-maps-as-degenerate-predication-system](../../../glossary/concepts/topic-maps-as-degenerate-predication-system.md) theorem makes these three restrictions precise and shows Topic Maps is the canonical *strict-regime, $\mathbf{2}$-valued, symmetric* sub-substrate of the full predication-cell.

The standard is the load-bearing comparison for the framing's expressivity claim: the predication-cell is *strictly more expressive* than Topic Maps, recoverable by these three restrictions, and the cost of going from $\mathbf{2}$-valued to $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$-valued is the introduction of the [knowledge-order](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md) on top of the truth-order.
