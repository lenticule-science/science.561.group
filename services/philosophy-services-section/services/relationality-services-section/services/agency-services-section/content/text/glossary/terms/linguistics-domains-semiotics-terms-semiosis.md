Semiosis is the process by which a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) produces an [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md), which, being itself a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md), produces a further [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md), and so on. It is the dynamic, iterative process through which meaning is generated, propagated, and transformed.

[Peirce](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md) treated semiosis as the fundamental operation of [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) systems. A [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) does not &ldquo;have&rdquo; a meaning in a static sense — it *generates* meaning through the process of being interpreted. This generation is open-ended: each [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) is a new [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md), capable of entering into further [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) relations. The chain of semiosis is constrained by habit, context, and purpose, but it has no necessary termination point [@short_PeirceTheorySigns_2007].

Umberto Eco coined the phrase &ldquo;unlimited semiosis&rdquo; to emphasize that interpretation is never final — every sign points to further [signs](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md), every definition uses terms that themselves require definition. This is not a deficiency of sign systems but their constitutive character [@eco_TheorySemiotics_1976].
