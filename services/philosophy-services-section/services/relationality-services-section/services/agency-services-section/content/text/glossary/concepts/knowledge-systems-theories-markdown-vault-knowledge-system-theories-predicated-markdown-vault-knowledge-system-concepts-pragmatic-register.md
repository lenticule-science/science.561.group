# Pragmatic register

The **pragmatic register** of a typed [note](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/note.md) is the relationship the note's body assumes with its [audience](audience.md): expository, claim-and-evidence, position-and-stake, rhetorical-and-ordering, concrete, terse-and-authoritative, bibliographic. Each [note-type](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/note-type.md) commits to a register, and that commitment governs what the body must (and must not) do.

This concept binds upstream to *both* communication theories: the [science-side communication register](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/communication/concepts/register.md) anchors the linguistic / pragmatic / systemic-functional notion (Halliday's field-tenor-mode, genre theory in rhetoric and composition), and the [humanities-side communication register](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/communication/concepts/register.md) anchors the closure-discipline notion (a medium with its own native panels, native rigor, native foreclosures, after McCloud / Andersen / Avigad). The substantive specification of what each note-type's register commits to is held open at [audience-and-pragmatics-of-each-note-type](../../documents/articles/learning-articles/questions/audience-and-pragmatics-of-each-note-type.md).

## The eight implicit registers (per current per-type specs)

- **Concept** → expository. The body explains the role the anchor plays and how various objects realize it. Avoids claim-and-evidence; defers proofs to theorems.
- **Object (definition)** → terse and authoritative. The body fixes a structure with the precision required to check a proof or build a construction.
- **Morphism** → operational. The body specifies a signature, an operational semantics, and invariants the operator must satisfy.
- **Theorem** → claim-and-evidence. The body states one claim and gives a proof, a sketch, or a refutation.
- **Question** → position-and-stake. The body names what is being inquired into, why the answer matters, what would count as an acceptable answer, and what bounds the question.
- **Narrative** → rhetorical and ordering. The body walks a path through other notes, linking concepts/objects/morphisms/theorems into a story.
- **Example** → concrete. The body presents a particular instance and traces its substrate-properties.
- **Reference** → bibliographic. The body annotates an external source: form, content summary, position in the corpus.

## Why pragmatic register is its own anchor

Register confusion is the largest source of voice-drift across the corpus. A theorem note that drifts into narrative loses the reader who came to check what is proved; a concept note that drifts into theorem loses the reader who came for orientation. The discipline's response is to name the eight registers as anchors so that each note-type spec can declare its register explicitly and so that contributors can audit drafts against a known register rather than against an implicit one.
