## Annotation

Generalises FCA from the binary to the *residuated-lattice-valued* case. Where classical FCA constructs a concept lattice from a Boolean object–attribute incidence $I \subseteq G \times M$, Belohlavek's residuated FCA constructs a concept lattice from a residuated-lattice-valued incidence $I: G \times M \to \mathbf{L}$, with the two derivation operators (extent and intent) becoming residuated-lattice-valued Galois connections. The resulting concept lattice carries enriched-categorical structure indexed by the residuated lattice.

Two structural results matter most for this subtheory:

- **Belohlavek's basic theorem** (per [theorems/belohlavek-bridge](../../../glossary/concepts/belohlavek-bridge.md)): the residuated concept lattice is a complete $\mathbf{L}$-lattice; every complete $\mathbf{L}$-lattice arises this way. The classical Wille-Ganter theorem is the case $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$.
- **Parametric uniformity**: the choice of $\mathbf{L}$ (Boolean, Heyting, Łukasiewicz, product-of-chains, etc.) is a parameter rather than a separate construction. The same proof works at every choice.

For the predicated framing: Belohlavek's construction is the technical bridge between FCA and the bilattice-valued substrate. Specifically, when $\mathbf{L}$ is taken to be (a projection of) the bilattice $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$ that the predicated substrate uses, the residuated concept lattice becomes the substrate's [knowledge-fiber-algebra](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/predicated-knowledge-systems/objects/knowledge-fiber-algebra.md) restricted to the trivial single-attribute predicate vocabulary. This is the structural content of the [belohlavek-bridge](../../../glossary/concepts/belohlavek-bridge.md) theorem.

Belohlavek's paper also matters because it shows the residuated generalisation *preserves* the lattice-theoretic structure (the Galois connection, the lattice operations, the basic theorem) while *enriching* the value-domain. The same conservativity holds for the predicated framing's bilattice generalisation: the substrate's machinery does not destroy the FCA-internal structure when the FCA fragment is identified.
