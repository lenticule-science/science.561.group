**FINDING 2 — SERIOUS. The daemon's tick is inherited from a wall-clock loop.**
- Element attacked: Wave 1, body 3 — "Its operational shape is generalized from newswire's existing tick loop."
- Argument: Way 2: "A tick is an ordinal advance, not an interval... wall-clock is testimony, never sequencing." Newswire's tick loop is an interval scheduler from the old kernel era — exactly the artifact the plan elsewhere calls a prototype to be discarded. "Generalized from" imports interval-driven sequencing into the bedrock's one service, where it becomes the operational definition of time for every mounted topology (Way 7 makes this a single point of doctrinal failure). The program map even says regimes are operational facts requiring different scheduling — an interval loop is regime-blind.
- Amendment: Specify the daemon's tick as witness-driven ordinal advance: admission events trigger incremental settling steps; any timer exists only as a conduit that admits time-testimony witnesses; scheduling policy is a function of the mounted topology's regime classification.

**FINDING 3 — SERIOUS. "At rest it is the topology on disk" persists the value, not the trace.**
- Element attacked: Wave 1, body 3, closing sentence.
- Argument: Way 1: "State is never a value; it is a log... the trace is the truth and the value is a reading." A daemon whose at-rest identity is the settled topology has made fold(log) the durable object and demoted the log. This is the persistence-format decision for the entire ecosystem and Wave 1 fixes it; if the topology-on-disk is authoritative, replays, re-settlings under widened charge (Finding 1), and audit of admission history all become impossible or lossy — a Landauer-default erasure (Way 2).
- Amendment: At rest it is the **witness trace** on disk (plus checkpointed readings as declared caches, per Way 1's cache clause). The sentence should read: at rest it is the trace; running, it is the flow; the topology is always a reading.
