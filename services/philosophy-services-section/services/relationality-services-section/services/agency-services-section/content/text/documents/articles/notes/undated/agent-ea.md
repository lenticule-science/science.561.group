Relational agent with epistemic and normative profiles and local flow/nucleus.

## Definition - Components: an [Epistemic Profile](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/epistemic-profile.md), a [Norm Regime](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/norm-regime.md), and local flow/nucleus data from [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/flow-nucleus-adjunction.md). - Behaviour: epistemic updates and norm enforcement act on the agent’s local recognitions and contexts.

## Invariants - Commutation: epistemic updates and norm enforcement commute with the local flow/nucleus. - Stability: agent state remains inside admissible recognitions given its norm regime. - Monotonicity: new observations refine, not expand, epistemic possibilities.

## Proof - Uses: [Epistemic Profile](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/epistemic-profile.md), [Norm Regime](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/norm-regime.md), and [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/flow-nucleus-adjunction.md) to define the agent’s state/evolution. - Shows that: agents can evolve under observations and norms without breaking earned flow/nucleus structure.
