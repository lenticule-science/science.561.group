# Three upstream clusters

The curation theory does not invent its substantive vocabulary. It assembles it from three upstream clusters whose load-bearing literature is already present in the corpus's reference web — chiefly under [predicated-knowledge-systems references](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references) and at the two [communication theories](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/_index.md). This narrative names the three clusters, says what each contributes, says where the curation theory borrows wholesale and where it diverges, and grounds each citation in a reference note that resolves on disk.

## Cluster I — Knowledge organization

The library-and-information-science tradition that asks: *given a body of recorded knowledge, what structures admit reliable retrieval?*

Load-bearing references already in the corpus:

- [Dahlberg 1978](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/glossary-science-services-section/services/term-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/dahlberg1978.md) — *A Referent-Oriented, Analytical Concept Theory for INTERCONCEPT*. The conceptual basis for treating the *concept* (not the term, not the definition) as the unit of organisation. The curation theory's [note-type](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/teaching/explanations/note-type.md) commitment, with its distinction between `concept` and `definition`, is a Dahlberg-derived move: the concept names the load-bearing role, the definition realizes it.
- [Hjørland 2002](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/hjorland2002.md) — *Domain analysis in information science: Eleven approaches – traditional as well as innovative*. The argument that knowledge organisation is *domain-specific*: there is no domain-neutral classification, and the discipline's structure must be calibrated to the domain it organises. The curation theory's [theory-directory](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/teaching/explanations/theory-directory.md) commitment — that each theory is its own organisation locale — is the domain-analytic reading.
- [Ranganathan 1933](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/ranganathan1933.md) and [Ranganathan 1967](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/ranganathan1967.md) — *Colon Classification* and *Prolegomena to Library Classification*. The faceted-classification tradition: a single object can be classified along multiple independent facets (Personality, Matter, Energy, Space, Time). The curation theory's typed-edge system (`depends-on`, `see-also`, `realizes`, `instantiates`, `defines`) is faceted in this sense — the same note carries multiple independent edges that classify it along different relational dimensions.
- [Foskett 1962](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/foskett1962.md) — *Science, humanism, and libraries*. The argument that classification is a *political-epistemic* act, not a neutral cataloguing one. The curation theory borrows this honesty: its discipline is a *commitment*, not a neutral observation about how things are.

**What the curation theory borrows.** Concept-as-unit (Dahlberg); domain-specificity (Hjørland); facet-as-edge (Ranganathan); discipline-as-commitment (Foskett).

**What the curation theory diverges on.** It does not adopt any single classification scheme. Where the library-science tradition built specific schemes (Dewey Decimal, UDC, Colon Classification), the curation theory builds *a discipline for building schemes within theories*: the directory tree is the scheme, the discipline tells you how to build it. This is closer in spirit to Hjørland's domain-analytic position than to Dewey's universalist one.

## Cluster II — Knowledge representation

The AI-and-formal-ontology tradition that asks: *what formal structures admit machine-checkable reasoning over a body of represented knowledge?*

Load-bearing references already in the corpus:

- [W3C RDF 2014](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/w3c-rdf2014.md) and [W3C OWL 2012](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/w3c-owl2012.md) — the Resource Description Framework and the Web Ontology Language. The (subject, predicate, object) triple is the canonical formalisation of the kind of typed edge the curation theory's relation graph carries.
- [Brachman & Levesque 2004](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/brachman-levesque2004.md) — *Knowledge Representation and Reasoning*. The textbook articulation of the KR tradition (description logics, frames, semantic networks) that grounds OWL and any typed-edge knowledge graph aiming at decidable reasoning. The curation theory's [theory-discipline-conformance-decidable](../../../../glossary/concepts/theory-discipline-conformance-decidable.md) theorem is in the spirit of this decidability discipline.
- [Sowa 2000](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/sowa2000.md) — *Knowledge Representation: Logical, Philosophical, and Computational Foundations*. The synthetic textbook treatment that bridges Peirce's existential graphs, FOL, conceptual graphs, and the modern KR machinery.
- [ISO 13250 / Topic Maps 2003](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/predicated-knowledge-systems/references/iso13250-2003.md) — Topic Maps as an alternative knowledge-graph formalism (with `topics`, `associations`, and `occurrences`). The curation theory's typed-note plus typed-edge structure is a Topic-Maps-style design more than an RDF-style one: notes are first-class identified things, not anonymous nodes.
- [Peirce CP](../../bibliographies/peirce-cp.md) — Peirce's *Collected Papers*, in particular the existential-graphs material. Peirce's category theory (firstness, secondness, thirdness) and his predication-as-graphical work is the deepest upstream influence on the [predication-categories](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories) theory the curation discipline generalises.

**What the curation theory borrows.** Typed triples (RDF/OWL); decidability discipline (DL); the `topic`-as-first-class-identified-thing design (Topic Maps); the predication-as-graphical reading (Peirce).

**What the curation theory diverges on.** It does not commit to any specific formal logic. RDF/OWL, DL, and Topic Maps are all *machine-readable* knowledge representations; the curation theory is *human-readable-first* (markdown is the source, the formal graph is derived). The discipline's claim is that the human-readable form *is itself* a knowledge representation, not that it is a markup over one. The bilattice machinery in [predication-categories](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories) is the curation discipline's preferred formal substrate when one is needed; that substrate is more permissive than DL (it admits unverifiable and inconsistent valuations) and is closer in spirit to four-valued logics than to classical KR.

## Cluster III — Communication and closure

The communication-theory tradition that asks: *what makes a transmitted artifact legible to its intended audience, and what disciplines authorial closure-design?*

This cluster is split across two sibling theories on disk; the split is honest about the cluster's two axes:

- [`humanities/communication/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/communication/_index.md) carries the McCloud / Andersen / Avigad / Macbeth closure-discipline framework: every artifact frames units explicitly and asks the reader to perform [accountable-closure](../../../../glossary/concepts/accountable-closure.md) between them; the defect is unaccountable closure; the discipline is [medium-specific-rigor](../../../../glossary/concepts/medium-specific-rigor.md). This is the substantive home of *which closures each note-type asks the reader to perform*.
- [`science/communication/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/communication/_index.md) carries the source-channel-receiver / linguistic-pragmatic / rhetorical tradition: [audience](../../../../glossary/concepts/audience.md), [channel](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/communication/concepts/channel.md), [register](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/communication/concepts/register.md), [genre](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/communication/concepts/genre.md), [pragmatics](../../../../glossary/concepts/pragmatics.md). This is where the curation theory's [audience](../../../../glossary/concepts/audience.md), [channel](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/teaching/explanations/channel.md), and [pragmatic-register](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/teaching/explanations/pragmatic-register.md) anchors load on.

The humanities-side theory explicitly distinguishes itself from Shannon's mathematical theory of communication — see [communication-is-not-shannon](../../../../glossary/concepts/communication-is-not-shannon.md). The science-side theory carries the Shannon/Wiener/Halliday tradition. The curation theory loads on both, citing the closure-discipline anchors via the humanities side and the audience-channel-register anchors via the science side.

**What the curation theory borrows.** Closure-as-unit-of-communicative-work (McCloud / humanities-side); medium-specific-rigor as the per-register discipline commitment (Andersen / Avigad); audience-design as a per-note-type pragmatic anchor (Bell / science-side); register-as-situated-configuration (Halliday / science-side); genre-as-stable-artifact-type (Miller / science-side).

**What the curation theory diverges on.** It does not adopt Shannon's mathematical theory as its model of communication. Following the humanities-side theory's explicit position, the curation theory treats the corpus as a *medium with native rigor*, not as a noisy channel with a code. Shannon's framing is appropriate for the [shannon-channel](../../../../glossary/terms/shannon-channel.md) object but not for the per-note-type discipline.
