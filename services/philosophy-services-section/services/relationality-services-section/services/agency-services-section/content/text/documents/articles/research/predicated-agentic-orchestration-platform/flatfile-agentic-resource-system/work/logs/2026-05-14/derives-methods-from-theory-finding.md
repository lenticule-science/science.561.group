# Finding — what is a valid value of `derives-methods-from-theory`

## Question

`derives-methods-from-theory` is already in use in `flatfile-agentic-resource-system/_index.md` (value `[agential-orchestration-platform]`). emsenn proposed elevating it to a key declared in `561/specifications/theory.md`.
The valid-value question is to be settled by what predication category theory says, not by convention.

## Answer (short form)

**A valid value of `derives-methods-from-theory: X` is a theory** — meaning, an entity that satisfies "is a theory" in the predication-categories sense: a predication settling over a 4-tuple base $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda)$ in the parameter base category $\mathbf{B}$, with a closure operator.
In repo terms: a directory presenting such a settling.

**The relation asserted** is the existence of a **predication-settling base-morphism** $T_X \to T_{\text{this}}$ whose predicate-category component $\pi : \mathcal{C}^X \to \mathcal{C}^{\text{this}}$ is faithful and generator-preserving — i.e., this theory imports X's value-domain (or a residuated-lattice extension), X's predicate generators (specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection) and the closure rules built on them, and may extend by adding new structure.

## Grounding

### What "is a theory" means

From `objects/parameter-base-category.md`: a predication settling's *base parameters* are an object of $\mathbf{B} = \mathrm{ResLat} \times \mathrm{PredCat} \times \mathbf{Set} \times \mathbf{Ord}$ — a 4-tuple $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda)$ where:

- $\mathbf{L}$ is an integral commutative residuated lattice (the value-domain; e.g., the four-bilattice).
- $\mathcal{C}$ is a predicate category — a small category whose objects are predicates and whose morphisms are generated by the four canonical classes.
- $E$ is the entity set.
- $\lambda$ is the settling bound (an ordinal).

From `concepts/theory-relative-ideal.md`: a "host theory $T$" is then a settling $(v^T_\tau)_{\tau<\lambda}$ over this base with closure operator $\mathrm{Cl}^T$.

### What morphisms between theories exist

From `morphisms/predication-settling-base-morphism.md`, three distinct morphism notions exist on predication settlings:

| Morphism | Base | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| **base-morphism** $\phi$ | cross-base | "imports parameters/methods" |
| **preorder morphism** | same-base, $\leq_k$ | "has learned everything parent has" |
| **modeling morphism** $\models$ | same-base, $\leq_t$ | "satisfies parent's truth commitments" |

Plus the strict same-base **settling-morphism** (equality with ordinal shift + endofunctor commutation; generates MLD-equivalence per `concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md`).

`derives-methods-from-theory` is the cross-base one.
The other relations would warrant their own structural keys if/when needed (candidates: `models-theory:` for $\models$; `extends-knowledge-of:` for $\leq_k$-preorder).

### The structure of the base-morphism

From `morphisms/predication-settling-base-morphism.md`, a base-morphism $\phi : v \to v'$ consists of:

1. $\ell : \mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{L}'$ — residuated-lattice homomorphism.
2. $e : E \to E'$ — function on entity sets.
3. $\pi : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ — functor preserving the four predicate generators.
4. $\phi_\lambda : \lambda \to \lambda'$ — order-preserving on settling bounds.
5. A family $(\phi_{p,\tau})$ of bilattice homomorphisms on fiber algebras such that $\phi_{p, \tau}(v_\tau(p)) \leq_k v'_{\phi_\lambda(\tau)}(\pi(p))$ for all $p, \tau$.

The 4-tuple $(\ell, e, \pi, \phi_\lambda)$ is the morphism in $\mathbf{B}$; the fiber-algebra family is the cellwise lift.

### Why "methods" maps to this

"Methods" of a theory in the corpus include:

- The closure operator $\mathrm{Cl}^T$ (closure rules).
- The four predicate generators (specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection).
- The hyperdoctrinal adjoints (quantifiers, comprehension) — see `objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md`.
- Settling endofunctors and dispatch composition.

These are exactly what a base-morphism preserves when $\pi$ is faithful and generator-preserving and $\ell$ is a residuated-lattice homomorphism — the child theory inherits operations and rules, and may extend.

## Implications for the structural register

This finding settles one floor key for `561/specifications/theory.md`:

- **`derives-methods-from-theory: [X, ...]
  `** — list of theories.
  Each entry must denote a theory; the assertion is the existence of a base-morphism with faithful generator-preserving $\pi$ from X's base into this theory's.

It also surfaces three sibling structural keys that the same framework supports (not in scope to file today, but named for the record):

- `models-theory:` — assertion of a modeling morphism $\models$ (truth-order satisfaction; the classical $\mathcal{M} \models T$).
- `extends-knowledge-of:` — assertion of a $\leq_k$-preorder morphism (knowledge-order learning).
- `mld-equivalent-to:` — assertion of a settling-morphism in the strict same-base sense (relevant for aperiodic-regime classification).

These should not be added pre-emptively; they are noted because the predication category theory cleanly distinguishes them and the structural register should grow under that distinction rather than collapsing them into a single `derives-from`.
