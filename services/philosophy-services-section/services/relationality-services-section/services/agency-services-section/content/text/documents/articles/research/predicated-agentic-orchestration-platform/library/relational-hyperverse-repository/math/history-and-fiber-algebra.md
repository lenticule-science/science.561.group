### Definition — Histories and Fiber Algebras {#history-and-fiber-algebra}

*What exists and what it is made of.*

Two axioms define the scaffolding and the fibers.
The scaffolding is a thin preorder of relational histories — the history category $T$.
The fibers are finite intuitionistic logics with two commuting settling operators — the Heyting algebras $(H_t, \sigma_t, \Delta_t)$.
No topology, no dynamics, no language yet.
The Axiom of Histories gives the scaffold; the fiber doctrine furnishes each node with an algebra of meanings.
