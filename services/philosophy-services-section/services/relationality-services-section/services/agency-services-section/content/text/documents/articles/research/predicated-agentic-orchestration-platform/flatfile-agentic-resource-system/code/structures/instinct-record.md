# Instinct record

The substrate-record holding one *instinct* — a settled pattern recognized across [`Observation`](observation-record.md)s, with a declared trigger pattern, a declared outcome pattern, and a confidence-as-closure-measure indicator.
An `InstinctRecord` is the *settlement-path output* of consolidation per [consolidation-as-settlement](../../settlement/consolidation-as-settlement/consolidation-as-settlement.md): observations compose under closure rules; concordant patterns become $t$-cells in the instinct's fiber; the instinct entity is what the substrate holds about the recognized pattern.

An instinct is an F2 entity in the [formality-progression](../../formality-progression/formality-progression.md) sense: structured predicates capturing the pattern's content, not yet a deterministic skill but more than a one-off observation.
Per the CL pipeline path *observation → instinct → skill → tool*, an instinct is the second arrow's destination and the third arrow's source.

`InstinctRecord` is FARS's realization of AgOP's [instinct-record](../../../objects/instinct-record.md) at the bilattice value-domain.

## Required fields

Every conforming realization SHALL hold, for each `InstinctRecord`:

### Identity

- **`name`** — canonical identifier, typically content-derived plus a human-readable suffix (`grep-then-edit-on-test-failures`, `dispatch-stalls-on-thin-fiber`).
- **`description`** — human-readable account of the recognized pattern.

### Pattern content

- **`trigger`** — the predication-pattern that activates the instinct: which substrate states it recognizes.
  May reference a single entity's predicates ("any task with `status: blocked` AND `last-activity` older than 7 days") or cross-entity patterns.
  Premise-shape per [closure-rule-record](closure-rule-record.md).
- **`outcome`** — the predication-pattern the instinct claims about the trigger: what the instinct *says* about cases the trigger matches.
  Conclusion-shape.
- **`why`** — the substrate-resident *diagnosis* — the reason the instinct holds, in narrative form per Reflexion's diagnosis-is-load-bearing claim.
  Used by dispatch and downstream skills to know *why* this pattern matters, not just that it does.

### Confidence

- **`confidence`** — confidence-as-closure-measure, *not* count.
  The substrate-level statement of "applying further consolidation passes does not change this instinct's pattern" — i.e., idempotence under the training operator.
  Realizations MAY express confidence as a value-domain element ($t$ for fully settled, $\bot$ for "not yet stable," $\top$ for "contested across observations"), as a numeric scalar in [0, 1], or as a structured bilattice projection.
  The discipline is that confidence reflects *settledness*, not raw observation count.
- **`observations`** — list of [`ObservationRecord`](observation-record.md) references the instinct was settled from.
  The instinct's substrate provenance.
- **`contradicting-observations`** — list of observations that *would* match the trigger but *do not* match the outcome.
  A small contradicting-set (relative to observations) sustains $t$-confidence; a large contradicting-set produces $\top$ on the instinct's confidence cell.

### Relevance & dispatch

- **`relevance-fiber`** — the predicates that determine *when this instinct applies*.
  Used by [`dispatch`](../morphisms/dispatch.md) when an agent's response involves matching against learned patterns: `respond(message, instinct.relevance-fiber)` produces a value-domain ranking.
- **`channel-emitted`** — the channel an event matching this instinct's trigger emits to.
  Typically `analysis` (the instinct surfaces a recognized pattern for human-or-agent attention) or `learning` (the instinct accumulates as an internal substrate signal).

### Lifecycle

- **`status`** — one of `proposed`, `validated`, `promoted`, `retired`.
  The instinct's settlement-path position.
  `proposed` instincts have been generated by consolidation but not validated; `validated` instincts have been ratified (by a closure rule or by human review) as substrate-trustworthy; `promoted` instincts have been used to derive a skill (the next settlement-path step); `retired` instincts have been replaced or invalidated.
- **`promoted-skill`** — optional reference to the [`SkillRecord`](skill-record.md) that was derived from this instinct.
  Used to trace the settlement path forward; nullable for non-promoted instincts.

### Provenance

- **`witness`** — the consolidation witness that produced this instinct.
  Cites the rule-system version, the observations consumed, the consolidation operator, the resulting instinct hash.
  Per [`consolidate`](../transformers/consolidate.md).

## Invariants

- **Substrate entity.** An `InstinctRecord` SHALL be a substrate entity, addressable like any other.
- **Observations-cited.** Every instinct SHALL cite at least one source observation.
  Instincts without observational provenance cannot be replayed and lack substrate-coherence.
- **Settled-output of consolidation.** An instinct SHALL be the output of a [`consolidate`](../transformers/consolidate.md) run; instincts created by hand without going through consolidation are non-conformant.
  (A realization MAY admit *seeded instincts* — manually-authored patterns that are explicit fixtures for testing or for substrate bootstrapping — but these SHALL be tagged as such, distinct from consolidation-derived instincts.)
- **Confidence-tracked.** The `confidence` field SHALL be present and reflect settledness under the consolidation operator.
  Confidence as raw observation-count is non-conformant per the FARS-internal CL design.
- **Trigger/outcome separable.** Trigger and outcome SHALL be distinct predication-patterns.
  An instinct whose trigger and outcome collapse into one predicate degenerates to a tautology and provides no learning value.

## How instincts compose with skills

The promotion arc *instinct → skill* is one substrate edit:

1. The instinct reaches `confidence: t` AND `status: validated`.
2. A closure rule on the `learning` channel matches: "promote validated, settled instincts into skills."
3. The rule's conclusion creates a [`SkillRecord`](skill-record.md) whose `body` references the instinct's pattern, whose `preconditions` derive from the instinct's trigger, whose `postconditions` derive from the instinct's outcome.
4. The instinct's `promoted-skill` predicate is set to the new skill's reference.
5. The substrate emits the promotion event on the `learning` channel; an auditor can replay both the consolidation that produced the instinct and the promotion that produced the skill.

The promotion is governed by substrate content (the closure rule), not by realization-side automation.
Different realizations MAY apply different promotion rules; the substrate-level discipline is that the promotion IS a substrate edit and IS recorded.

## What an `InstinctRecord` is not

- **Not a tag.** An instinct is *substrate content with structure*, not a label.
  It cannot be applied to an entity by adding a tag; it is *recognized in* an entity by composition with the instinct's trigger fiber.
- **Not a hard rule.** An instinct does not block actions or enforce constraints.
  Instincts feed dispatch and analysis; closure rules enforce.
  An instinct's outcome that "this dispatch is risky" surfaces the risk; the substrate's `guard` rules decide whether to admit.
- **Not the same as a memory.** Memory is the broader substrate of all retained content (per [three-tier-memory](../../architecture/three-tier-memory/three-tier-memory.md)); an instinct is one *kind* of memory — the pattern-shaped kind.
  Observations are also memory; offices are also memory; not all memory is instinct.

## Position

`InstinctRecord` is the substrate-record under which *learned patterns* are first-class.
Without instinct records, the substrate's accumulated observations would have no settled-form representation; consolidation would have nowhere to write its outputs; the *learning* channel of the seven-channel decomposition would have no record-shape.

The record composes with [`ObservationRecord`](observation-record.md) (its source), [`SkillRecord`](skill-record.md) (its potential promotion-target), [`ClosureRuleRecord`](closure-rule-record.md) (the rules that consolidate it and the rules that promote it), and [`Witness`](witness.md) (its provenance chain back to the settling consolidation run).
