# Non-Hohfeldian governance traditions in predicated-governance

The library's [indigenous-governance-traditions survey](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/library/theories/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/governance/texts/indigenous-governance-traditions-survey.md) and [black-radical-governance survey](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/triage/black-radical-governance-survey.md) document governance traditions that operate by structurally different principles than the Hohfeldian rights-and-duties frame.
This narrative names what predicated-governance imports from each — substrate-algebraic patterns the math admits but the Hohfeldian apparatus alone does not surface.

The math program is *frame-neutral*: it provides closure-rule subsystems, jural-relation entities, settling regimes, cohomological invariants.
What governance pattern a community implements is a choice of which closure rules go in $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{op}}$; the math discriminates patterns by their cohomology but does not privilege any.

This means non-Hohfeldian patterns are not "exceptions to" the framework; they are *first-class citizens* of it.
The framework imports patterns from indigenous and Black radical traditions because those traditions provide structurally well-defined governance content that the math can carry — content the Hohfeldian apparatus alone does not.

## What each tradition contributes structurally

### Haudenosaunee Confederacy: consensus, removal, generational obligation

Substrate-algebraic content imported:

- *Consensus closure* per [consensus-closure](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/consensus-closure.md).
  Universal $\bigwedge_a$ over populace alignment cells, not majority-counting.
  More substrate-native than majority-rule because it uses only the bilattice's primitive $\wedge_t$, not cardinality reasoning.
- *Clan-mother removal* — a power-jural-relation held by clan-mothers over the action of removing-a-chief.
  Not an erosion of consensus; a separate closure rule operating on incumbency cells.
  The substrate carries both.
- *Seventh-generation projection* — closure rules whose antecedents read projected substrate state seven generations forward.
  Lifts decision-cells into the [transfinite-bounded regime](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-recurrent-practice.md) — non-trivial $H^1$.
  Substrate-algebraic substantive content of *intergenerational obligation*.

What predicated-governance does *not* import (and cannot, structurally): the spiritual / ceremonial / cosmological dimensions of the Great Law of Peace.
The math reads the closure-rule structure; the lived practice exceeds the structure.
The framework recognizes this gap explicitly: substrate-readability is not the totality of governance.

### Anishinaabe Governance: clan structure, seven teachings, seasonal calendar

Substrate-algebraic content:

- *Clan-doodem-based authority* — the [office](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/relationality-repo/sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/objects/office-as-double-settlement.md)'s eligibility predicate restricted to a specific clan-membership cell.
  Substrate-algebraic content: closure rules that gate office-bearing on $\langle a, \texttt{member-of-doodem}, D\rangle = \mathbf{t}$.
- *Seven teachings as closure-rule constraints* — love, respect, bravery, truth, honesty, humility, wisdom each expressed as evaluation predicates a leader's conduct must satisfy.
  The substrate carries cells $\langle a, \texttt{exhibits-teaching}, t_i\rangle = v$ for each teaching $t_i$; closure rules check these for $\mathbf{t}$ before admitting authority-exercising acts.
  Failure-witness on conduct violating a teaching produces removal-pressure cells.
- *Seasonal authority* — the [recurrent practice](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/recurrent-practice.md) cadence is *ecological*, not calendrical.
  Governance closure rules fire in alignment with harvest, hunt, and ceremonial cycles rather than abstract fiscal-year boundaries.
  The substrate's cycle-boundary ordinals $T_R$ are determined by ecological-state cells, not by clock-ticks.

The seasonal-authority point is structurally significant: the substrate's *cadence* is not assumed to be calendrical.
Different polities can have different cadence-structures — calendrical (Western fiscal year), ecological (Anishinaabe seasonal), generational (Haudenosaunee multi-generational).
Each is admissible; the math discriminates them by their closure-rule firing patterns.

### Ubuntu: communitarian dignity, restorative accountability

Substrate-algebraic content:

- *Communitarian dignity* — dignity is a relational predicate, not an individual property.
  The cell encoding dignity is $\langle a, \texttt{exhibits-community-capacity}, \tau\rangle$, not $\langle a, \texttt{has-individual-rights}\rangle$.
  The substrate-algebraic content: dignity-cells are *fiber values over relational predicates* — they're cells in the fiber of `exhibits-community-capacity-toward`, indexed by pairs of agents.
- *Restorative accountability* — when harm occurs, closure rules fire that aim toward *reintegration* of the wrongdoer and *healing* of relationships.
  The substrate's response to a violation-witness is not (only) a punitive cell-update; it's a closure rule that asserts reparation-needed cells, opens a deliberative process via consensus closure, and seeks restoration of the broken relational cells.
  Punishment-cells, if they exist, are downstream and conditional.

Ubuntu is structurally distinct from Hohfeldian-individual-rights: the basic substrate fact is *relation*, not *individual claim*.
Predicated-governance imports this by allowing closure rules whose primary subject is a *relational pattern* across multiple agents, rather than only individual-cell predicates.
The substrate's bilattice algebra accommodates either; the choice of which to make primary is a structural commitment.

### Andean Ayllu: reciprocity, complementarity, rotation

Substrate-algebraic content per [rotational-authority](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/rotational-authority.md):

- *Ayni reciprocal exchange* — labor provided creates correlative reciprocal-labor-debt cells via [correlate-of](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/correlate-of.md).
  The substrate maintains a *reciprocal-debt graph* across the populace.
- *Minka collective labor* — rotational leadership of shared tasks; each event's incumbency drawn from the populace and retired at completion.
- *Rotational authority* — no permanent positions; cycle through eligible members.
  Substrate-algebraically a [recurrent practice](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/recurrent-practice.md) on incumbency cells.
- *Complementarity* — authority distributed across complementary positions (often gender-balanced); decisions emerge from balance rather than hierarchy.
  Substrate-algebraic content: closure rules whose firing requires both complementary-position alignments, not single-position decision.

The ayllu's *essentially-egalitarian* structure is a substrate-algebraic property: incumbency-history cells are broadly distributed across the populace; reciprocal-debt cells form a connected graph; authority does not concentrate.

### Aboriginal Australian: kinship as infrastructure, songlines as law

Substrate-algebraic content:

- *Kinship-determined authority* — each agent belongs to multiple overlapping groups (moiety, section, subsection); closure rules fire conditioned on kinship-cell patterns.
  An agent's authority over a context is determined by their kinship position, not by individual-attribute predicates.
- *Songlines as law* — sacred routes encoding law, history, geography, ceremony in narrative-and-song.
  Substrate-algebraic content: closure rules whose antecedents reference *path-cells* through the substrate's cell-pattern graph — like *cohomological-classes* in the math program's settling cohomology, but indexed by physical landscape and sung verses rather than abstract dimensions.
- *Age-grade authority* — leadership tied to life stages; authority passes through age cohorts.
  Substrate-algebraic content: incumbency cells gated on $\langle a, \texttt{age-cohort}, k\rangle$ at appropriate $k$ values; rotation by ageing.
- *Ceremonial transmission* — authority is exercised through ceremonies that maintain Songlines and teach.
  A break in ceremony is a break in transmission.
  Substrate-algebraic content: closure rules whose firing depends on *prior ceremony cells* being settled.
  Skipping a ceremony breaks the chain.

The ceremonial-transmission point is structurally important: governance is *ongoing maintenance work*, not a static structure.
The substrate's settling continually extends and refreshes the closure-rule firings; without the maintenance increments, the structure decays.

### Black Radical Tradition: refusal, fugitivity, abolition as positive theory

Substrate-algebraic content per the [black-radical-governance survey](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/triage/black-radical-governance-survey.md):

- *Refusal of inherited normative grammars* — the function's hard-core $C$ contains rules that *block* certain status-function-declarations from binding the working community.
  Substrate-algebraic content: closure rules whose consequent is "this institution's rules do not fire on our cells" — a structural claim of non-applicability for institutions implicated in the conditions the function aims to abolish.
- *Fugitive practice* — practices that operate outside prevailing institutions' jurisdictions without thereby being illegitimate.
  Substrate-algebraic content: lusory closures (per [lusory-constraint-and-the-magic-circle narrative](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/lusory-constraint-and-the-magic-circle.md)) whose consent-cells are settled by the community's own recognition, independent of external institutional recognition.
- *Abolition as positive theory* — replacing structures organized around abandonment and control with structures organized around life-affirmation.
  Substrate-algebraic content: the function's closure rules systematically retire cells that produce abandonment/control patterns and assert life-affirming alternatives.
  Not a negation of governance; an alternative governance structure.

Per the [function-grounded-in-critical-tradition narrative](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/function-grounded-in-critical-tradition.md), the Black radical tradition's specific closure rules produce specific $H^1$-classes — distinguishable from liberal-procedural traditions by the math program's regime classification.

## Common patterns across traditions

The library's survey identifies several governance patterns that recur across these traditions and substantively differ from Hohfeldian-frame defaults:

**Consensus or thick agreement** — substrate-algebraic content: universal-quantifier closure rules over populace alignment cells.

**Authority tied to community membership and kinship** — substrate-algebraic content: incumbency-eligibility cells gated on community/kinship-membership predicates rather than abstract qualifications.

**Rotation and term limits** — substrate-algebraic content: recurrent-practice incumbency cycling, distributing authority-experience cells across populace.

**Ecological embeddedness** — substrate-algebraic content: closure-rule cadence determined by ecological-state cells; intergenerational projection lifts decision regimes higher.

**Restorative accountability** — substrate-algebraic content: violation-witness response patterns favor relation-restoration cells over individual-punishment cells.

**Integration with normativity beyond law** — substrate-algebraic content: closure rules drawn from teachings, ceremonies, philosophical principles, and reciprocal obligations — not only from formal-law sources.

Each pattern is well-defined substrate-algebraically.
None are exceptions to the framework.
The framework's openness to them is the point: predicated-governance does not assume Hohfeldian-rights as the universal substrate; it admits the Hohfeldian apparatus as one structural option among several.

## What this means for predicated-governance's scope

The Hohfeldian eightfold (claim/duty/privilege/no-claim and power/liability/immunity/disability) remains a useful vocabulary for *some* governance patterns — particularly those derived from common-law traditions and their formal-state analogs.
The eightfold is not, however, a universal frame.
Patterns from indigenous and Black radical traditions structurally don't reduce to it.

Predicated-governance's commitment is to provide substrate-algebraic content for *whichever* governance pattern a community implements.
The Hohfeldian operations ([assignment](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/relationality-repo/sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/morphisms/assignment.md), [novation](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/novation.md), [waiver](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/waiver.md), etc.) are tooling for one family of patterns.
The non-Hohfeldian objects ([consensus-closure](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/consensus-closure.md), [rotational-authority](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/rotational-authority.md), and others to be developed) are tooling for others.
Both are first-class.

A community choosing a governance tradition is choosing which structural toolings will be primary in its closure-rule subsystem.
The substrate carries either; the cohomology distinguishes the choices.

## Open: more traditions

This narrative engages five indigenous traditions and the Black radical tradition.
Other traditions named in the library's broader survey — feminist organizational theory, cybernetic governance, the cybernetic-postliberalism critique of liberal governance — also provide substrate-algebraic content predicated-governance can import.
Each warrants its own development.
The framework's openness is the point: as the library's research expands, predicated-governance gains additional structural content from each tradition's accumulated findings.

The math is the constant; the traditions are what the math carries.
The richness of governance comes from the traditions; the math discriminates them by their cohomology and provides a uniform substrate for their formalization.
