## Scope

This directory contains *annotated reference notes* — one note per upstream work whose contribution the subtheory inherits, extends, or deliberately departs from. Each note records the bibliographic data, the work's status (directly read versus covered via secondary literature), the substantive annotation explaining the work's contribution to the predicated framing, and back-links to the synthesis [texts](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/texts) and downstream notes that cite it.

For citations to upstream foundational works on bilattices, predication categories, fibrations, and four-valued logic, see the canonical reference notes in the [predication-categories references](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/references) — the entries here are *supplementary*, focused on the knowledge-organisation, knowledge-representation, upper-ontology, and process-and-situated-learning literatures the subtheory adds on top of the predication-categories substrate.
