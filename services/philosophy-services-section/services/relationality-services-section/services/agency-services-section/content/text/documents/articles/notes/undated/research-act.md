A **research act** is the local arrow of researching. In the directed relational
archive it is written as a coKleisli arrow

```text
r : X -> C Y
```

where `X` is experience, evidence, or an inquiry situation; `Y` is the claim or
form sought; and `C` is the modal closure that holds dynamic inquiry and stable
topology together.

This is the first proper child of researching because no certification, witness
topos, or theory extension can exist until there is an arrow that tries to carry
unsettled material toward claim-closure.

## What generates it

A research act is generated when knowing cannot yet close a claim but can direct
an inquiry toward closure. It is not identical with observation, reading, or
experimentation. Those may supply `X`; the research act is the directed morphism
from that input into a claim-space under closure.

## What it generates

A research act generates [research iteration](research-iteration.md)
when it is composed with further acts, and
[research certification](research-certification.md)
when iteration stabilizes. A field of certified acts generates the
[witness topos](witness-topos.md) of the research program.
