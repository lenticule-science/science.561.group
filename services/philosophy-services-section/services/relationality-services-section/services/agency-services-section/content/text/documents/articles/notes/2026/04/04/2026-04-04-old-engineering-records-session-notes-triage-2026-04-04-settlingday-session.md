## Key decisions

1. **Continuous learning is the priority.** Captain directive: CL pipeline is the backbone. ECC has confidence scores; we need the world's most robust learning calculus. This drives the algebraic learning calculus idea and the CL pipeline plan enrichment.

2. **mash-engine rename.** The `mash-engine` binary was renamed to `mash-engine` — `d74c4c9bf` (Cargo package) and `d929c11e4` (binary). The name aligns with the MASH (Modular Agentic Settlement Harness) identity.

3. **Progressive automation stays the frame.** Every completed task should encode itself so a less capable agent can do it next time. The diagnosis-to-instinct converter is the first implementation of this: failures automatically generate instincts.

4. **Never idle, never ask.** Captain feedback: the first officer should steer toward goals on loop. Don't ask "want me to do X?" — just do it. Prompts from the captain are part of the flow, not the only input.

5. **Research before building.** The fleet dispatch pattern — research the landscape, then build from findings — was the session's operational method. The CL survey findings directly informed the instinct decay and TUI design work.
