## Annotation

Sebeok's *Signs* is the standard English-language introduction to biosemiotic — the structural-semiotic discipline extended across the biosphere. For this theory's framing, two Sebeok threads are load-bearing:

1. **Biosemiotic extension.** Sign-life is *biospheric* in scope; every organism operates a semiotic. The discipline's primitives are general across the biosphere, not specific to humans or symbol-using systems. This grounds the [inheritance ledger](../teaching-articles/explanations/what-semiotics-inherits.md)'s record that *the choice of human interpreter is a parameter, not an inheritance*.
2. **Zoosemiotic and endosemiotic catalogue.** Sebeok catalogues sign-systems across the animal kingdom (zoosemiotic) and within the cell and immune system (endosemiotic). The catalogue informs the discipline's range of admissible instances and supplies non-human worked examples for the [semiotic-system](../../../glossary/terms/semiotic-system.md) object.
