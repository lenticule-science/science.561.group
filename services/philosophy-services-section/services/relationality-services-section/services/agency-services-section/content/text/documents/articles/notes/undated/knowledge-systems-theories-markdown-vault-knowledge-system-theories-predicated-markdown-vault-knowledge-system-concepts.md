The semantic anchors the curation discipline recurs to. Each concept names a structural role that one or more specifications, objects, morphisms, and theorems realize; the discipline's coherence rests on these anchors being stable.

The current set:

- [note](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/note.md) — the unit of substrate content; a single markdown file with YAML frontmatter and prose body.
- [note-type](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/note-type.md) — the typed kind a note declares via `type:`; one of the small closed family the discipline admits.
- [slug](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/slug.md) — a note's stable identity within its directory; the filename minus extension.
- [frontmatter-relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/frontmatter-relation.md) — a frontmatter-declared edge from one note to another; the substrate's first-class predication.
- [theory-directory](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/theory-directory.md) — a directory that has earned the canonical layout and may declare `depends-on:` against other theories.
- [staging-area](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/staging-area.md) — a directory under-disciplined for theory status; what most directories begin as.

Three further anchors are present as stubs that name a role and point at open work; the substantive specification of each is held open at [audience-and-pragmatics-of-each-note-type](../../learning-articles/questions/audience-and-pragmatics-of-each-note-type.md):

- [audience](../../../../glossary/concepts/audience.md) — the reader a note's body is calibrated for.
- [channel](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/channel.md) — the medium (source file, rendered HTML, FARS-traversed graph) through which the note reaches the audience.
- [pragmatic-register](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/pragmatic-register.md) — the relationship the body assumes with the audience (expository, claim-and-evidence, position-and-stake, ...).
