**Action:** Survey assignment and delegation theory across organizational design, agent systems, and scheduling.
Cover mutual adjustment, direct supervision, standardization, kanban pull, capability matching, and load-aware routing.

**Why:** Dispatch is a distinct operational problem—who does what work, when, and based on what capability assessment.
This differs from planning (deciding work exists) and execution (actually doing it).

**Key sources:** - Mintzberg: coordination mechanisms and organizational design - Fayol: management functions and delegation - Barnard: authority and communication in organizations - Kanban: pull-based work assignment - Job shop scheduling: routing, load balancing - Multi-agent task allocation literature - Capability maturity and skill routing in teams
