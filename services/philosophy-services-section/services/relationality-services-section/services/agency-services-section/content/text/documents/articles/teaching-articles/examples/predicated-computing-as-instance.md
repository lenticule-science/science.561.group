# Predicated-computing as instance

The [predicated-computing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing) theory is the second of the two gold-standard precedents the curation theory takes itself to be generalizing from. Where [predication-categories](predication-categories-as-instance.md) demonstrates the discipline applied to *purely-mathematical* content (with the bilattice as the substantive specialisation), predicated-computing demonstrates the discipline applied to *engineering-substrate* content — the FARS toolkit itself, plus the surrounding computing-discipline material — and demonstrates the [relation-graph-update](../../../../glossary/terms/relation-graph-update.md) morphism in its most-stressed form.

## What predicated-computing already does

Inspecting the predicated-computing theory and its FARS subtree directly:

- **Typed notes for engineering content.** Predicated-computing carries typed `concept`, `object`, `morphism`, `theorem`, `example`, `reference` notes for engineering substrate (programming language semantics, type systems, the predicated-knowledge-systems FARS implementation). The discipline's per-type spec is satisfied for non-mathematical content, demonstrating that the discipline is not specific to mathematics.
- **Validation rules under engineering-volume churn.** The FARS subtree under predicated-computing churns: notes are added, edited, deleted, and relocated as the toolkit develops. The vault remains discipline-conformant under this churn — which is the [relation-graph-update](../../../../glossary/terms/relation-graph-update.md) morphism's incrementality claim, in observable practice.
- **Substrate-side instances of every commitment.** Where predication-categories instantiates the discipline at the *content* level (notes about predication categories), predicated-computing instantiates it at the *substrate* level (notes about the toolkit that enforces the discipline). The recursion — the toolkit's own documentation conformant to the discipline the toolkit checks — is one of the discipline's most demanding tests, and predicated-computing passes it.

## Why two examples, not one

Predication-categories alone would leave open the question: *is the discipline only a discipline of mathematical content?* It is not. Predicated-computing demonstrates the discipline on engineering-substrate content with no formal-mathematical core. This is what licenses the curation theory's [scope claim](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/_index.md): the discipline is for *typed-note knowledge systems in markdown vaults*, full stop, not for any narrower content domain.

The two examples also stress different parts of the discipline:

- Predication-categories stresses the bilattice valuation reading (see the [predication-categories theory](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md)) (which the curation theory abstracts away as one specialisation among several).
- Predicated-computing stresses the [relation-graph-update](../../../../glossary/terms/relation-graph-update.md) morphism (which the curation theory carries directly), the [staging-area-to-theory promotion morphism](../../../../glossary/terms/staging-area-to-theory-promotion-decision.md) (visible in the toolkit's frequent additions of new typed notes), and the engineering-substrate side of the [pragmatic-register](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/teaching/explanations/pragmatic-register.md) anchor (engineering-substrate content has its own register conventions, distinct from the mathematical ones predication-categories exemplifies).

Together they pin down the discipline from two ends: the discipline is general enough to admit both, narrow enough that conformance is the same property in both.

## What this example demonstrates

That the discipline survives engineering-substrate churn — the highest-volume class of vault edits and the one most likely to expose latent defects in the discipline. If the discipline only worked on slow-moving mathematical content, it would be too narrow to commit to as a corpus-wide standard. Predicated-computing is the existence proof that it works at engineering speed.
