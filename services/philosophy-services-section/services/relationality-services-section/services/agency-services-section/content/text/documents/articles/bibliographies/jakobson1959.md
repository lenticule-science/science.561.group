## Annotation

Jakobson's short paper introduces the threefold typology of translation that the discipline still uses:

1. **Intralingual translation** (rewording) — translation within a single language between alternative formulations.
2. **Interlingual translation** (translation proper) — translation between two languages.
3. **Intersemiotic translation** (transmutation) — translation between two semiotic systems of different kinds (e.g., a novel into a film, an equation into a verbal exposition).

The threefold typology is structural rather than empirical; each kind is the substrate-level [translation](../../../glossary/terms/translation.md) operation parameterised by where the source and target sit in the [semiosphere](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semiosphere.md).
