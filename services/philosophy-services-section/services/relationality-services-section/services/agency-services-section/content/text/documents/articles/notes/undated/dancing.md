**Dancing is the agential form of becoming.** It is what becoming looks like when a distinguishable someone is read not as a static existent but as a **body in motion that means by moving** — agency exercised *as* movement and *between* moving bodies.

## Definition

Dancing is agency lived from the inside of movement.
The grounding is gathered in [movement phenomenology](movement-phenomenology.md): Maxine Sheets-Johnstone's claim (*The Primacy of Movement*, 1999) that **kinesthesia** is a primary sense and that there is a genuine **"thinking in movement"** — the dancing *is* the thinking, not its delivery; Merleau-Ponty's **motor intentionality** and **body schema** (*Phenomenology of Perception*, 1945), the lived "I can" that orients without representing; and Yeats's "**How can we know the dancer from the dance?**" — in dancing, agent and act are not separable.
Its play-root is Caillois's **ilinx** (vertigo, the controlled surrender of stable perception), already derived in-repo under the [ludics vocabulary](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/agency/library/agent/actuating/library/actuator/embodying/library/body/integrating/library/drone/swarming/library/swarm/library/multi-agent/concurrent-game-system/library/game/library/ludics/library/vocabulary/library/ilinx/ilinx.md).

This is why dancing is the **agential** face of the quartet (animate / **agential** / agentic / individual): agency here is neither the bare animate someone (becoming) nor a settled bounded self (the individual) — it is **directed activity between**, the live relating of bodies that is the existential meaning of "agential."

## Generated by

Dancing is generated by **[becoming](individual.md)** — more exactly, by the re-entry of becoming at the **[individual](individual.md)**.
`individual.md` establishes that a settled self carries a charge of pre-individual potential and re-enters as the seed of its own four-fold descent.
Dancing is the first turn of that fresh descent: the re-seeded someone, set in motion.
Where becoming opens *that there is a someone at all*, dancing opens *that the someone moves, and means by it*.

## Generates

Dancing generates **music** — through **[playing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/library/dance/playing/docs/playing.md)**.
A single body moving is dancing; bodies *playing* a dance together fall into common time, and that shared pulse **is** music.
So dancing's codomain, reached across the `playing` hinge, is the agentic shared field: [music](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/library/dance/playing/library/music/docs/music.md).
Its own immediate product is the danced artifact, [dance](dance.md) — the composed, transmissible form (Langer's "virtual gesture"; Laban's analyzable, notatable movement).

## Placement in the spiral

Dancing is the agential turn of the cycle **becoming → dancing → music → game → becoming**.
One full turn passes through all four faces of the quartet — the same descent existence already runs as `becoming → doing → working → governing` (animate → agential → agentic → individual).
Dancing seats where `doing`/agential seats.
The tail-bite that makes this a spiral rather than a line: the most-settled form the cycle reaches, [game](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/library/dance/playing/library/music/playing/library/game/docs/game.md), re-seeds the next becoming — so dancing, the agential form of *music*, is at the same time the agential form of *becoming itself*.
