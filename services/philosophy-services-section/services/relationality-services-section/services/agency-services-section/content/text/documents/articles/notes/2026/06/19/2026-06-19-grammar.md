## What this node is

Grammar is the **predicate** morphism evaluated at the base of [language](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/language.md). To predicate is to say something *of* something well-formedly; under semiosis the things are signs, and predicating over signs is exactly syntax — the constraints that distinguish a sentence from a mere string. This is the predicate step in the unfolding articulate → predicate → posit → settle: semiosis (articulate) gives us bare signification, and grammar is what it becomes when we ask not merely whether a sign signifies but whether signs may be *combined* into a well-formed whole that can then bear force and meaning.

That syntax *is* predication is not a metaphor but a structural fact the corpus inherits from categorial grammar. Lambek read the grammar of a language as a logic of types: a sentence type `s`, a noun-phrase type `np`, and directional implications `\` and `/` that encode word order. A transitive verb is then literally the type `np \ s / np` — a thing that, given an `np` to its left and an `np` to its right, yields an `s`. A verb is a function looking for its arguments; a well-formed sentence is a type-derivation that reduces to `s`. Constituency (Chomsky's phrase structure) and dependency (head-argument relations) are two views of this same predicative combination — the bracketing of constituents and the directed links between a head and what it governs. Grammar is therefore the predicate morphism made ordered: predication that cares not only *what* combines but in *what order*.

## What it generates / contains

The body under `content/` holds the particular and the diachronic faces of grammar:

- **[languages](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages)** — particular languages as instances of the grammar base. The tree descends genetically: [proto-indo-european](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages/content/proto-indo-european), [proto-germanic](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages/content/proto-germanic), [old-english](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages/content/old-english), [anglo-norman](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages/content/anglo-norman), and [english](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/languages/content/english). Each is one settled assignment of types to a lexicon and one set of well-formedness rules.
- **[historical-linguistics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/historical-linguistics)** — the study of how that assignment changes. The archived material works through sound change as structural change: lenition from PIE through Latin into the Romance languages, rhoticity in General American English. These show grammar mutating — phonological erosion reshaping the morphosyntax that encodes meaning, so that synthetic case-marking gives way to fixed word order and prepositions.
- **[semantics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/semantics)** — meaning read off the settled parse: the *settle* face of grammar. The archived material is Fregean and Kripkean — sense versus reference, intension versus extension, rigid designation. Once a string is well-formed, semantics measures what it means: how a term's sense routes to its referent, and how meaning composes over the structure syntax licenses (the Montague program of meaning-as-a-function-of-form).

## Orientation

Up one morphism is [language](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/language.md), the base at which all of this is re-run; grammar is its predicate step, sitting between [semiotics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/semiotics) (articulate — the bare sign) and [speech-acts](2026-06-19-speech-acts.md) (posit — saying-as-doing). Read downward, [semantics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/content/grammar/content/semantics) is grammar's own settle face, where a well-formed parse closes into determinate meaning. The procedure for placing any concept on this base by its generating morphism lives with the parent. Un-canonicalized material — the language stubs, the historical briefs, the semantics notes — sits in the several `_archive` directories beneath this node and has not yet been drawn onto the spine.
