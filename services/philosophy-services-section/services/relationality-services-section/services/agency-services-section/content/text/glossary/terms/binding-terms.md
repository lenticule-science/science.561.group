# Binding Terms

*Story.*  Seeds do not yet name the resulting term.  Applying FirstName to SeedTerm produces the act BindTerm.


BindTerm = (FirstName SeedTerm)


*Proof Sketch.*  Naming the seed uses only established recognitions, so (FirstName SeedTerm) records the binding that introduces a named term.
