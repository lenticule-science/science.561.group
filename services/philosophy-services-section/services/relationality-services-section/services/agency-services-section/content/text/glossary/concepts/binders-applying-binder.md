# Applying Binder

*Story.*  Transport alone does not describe how binders apply to term bodies.  FormTermBody must therefore witness BinderTransport, inducing BinderApplication.


BinderApplication = (FormTermBody BinderTransport)


*Proof Sketch.*  Composing the body recognition with the transport records actual usage, so BinderApplication is justified.
