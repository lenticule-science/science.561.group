# Theory of Agency

`@561-group/theory-of-agency` — the 561-group's agency derivations.

The relation to the rest of the catalog is derived, not imposed: **an agent is a kind of system**.

This is knowledge, not a runnable product.

Read the directory to see the modules; prior thought collects at each object's `docs/dev/notes/`.
