A **dance** is the composed, bounded form that dancing produces — the danced artifact, the pattern that can be held, repeated, taught, and shared.
Where [dancing](dancing.md) is the live agency-in-movement, a *dance* is its work: the noun to dancing's verb.

## Definition

The grounding is in [movement phenomenology](movement-phenomenology.md).
Susanne Langer (*Feeling and Form*, 1953) reads a dance as a play of **"virtual powers"** — gesture lifted out of its practical errand and made to *appear* as feeling-in-motion; a dance presents the form of a feeling without denoting a proposition.
Rudolf Laban's movement analysis (*Choreutics*, 1966; Labanotation) shows that this appearance has **analyzable structure** — effort qualities of weight, space, time, and flow, recordable in a notation.
Together they fix what a *dance* is: a patterned appearance of movement, composed and transmissible, distinct from the live dancing that enacts it.

## Generated by

A dance is generated by **[dancing](dancing.md)**: it is dancing's immediate codomain, the settled form precipitated out of the live activity — exactly as, one tier up, *being* is the existent precipitated out of *becoming*.

## Generates

A dance generates **[playing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/dancing/content/playing/docs/playing.md)**: a composed dance is not inert, it is *for* being played.
The danced form opens onto the live act of playing it — and playing a dance *with others*, in shared time, is the hinge that carries the dance into [music](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/dancing/content/playing/library/music/docs/music.md).
