## Annotation

Suchman's distinction between *plans-as-resources-for-action* and *plans-as-prescriptions-for-action* is the canonical critique of the AI-planning tradition's assumption that plans determine activity.
For predicated-work, Suchman's reading aligns precisely with the [plan](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/work/theories/predicated-work/objects/plan.md) object as a closure-rule subsystem: the plan does not *execute* — it constrains the closure rules that fire as situated witnesses arrive.
The plan-completion condition is not "the plan ran to its end" but rather [the meet of subtask completions](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/plan-completion-as-meet.md) — a substrate-state, not a prescription-following.

The relevance to [closure-driven-coordination](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/closure-driven-coordination.md): Suchman's point that coordination emerges from situated action rather than from plan-execution is structurally what predicated-work's closure-driven account formalizes.
Coordination rules fire on the evidence the working community records; the plan is the rule-subsystem, not a prescription.
