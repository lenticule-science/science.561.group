- It must be derived that Relationlessness has no Relation, thus it cannot not derive that Relationlessness Relates to Relation, deriving Relating, the Relation between Relationlessness and Relation.

- Deriving Relate-to-Relating, it cannot not derive that the meaning of Relate is transformed, such that Relate now always includes the self-relating of Relating.
