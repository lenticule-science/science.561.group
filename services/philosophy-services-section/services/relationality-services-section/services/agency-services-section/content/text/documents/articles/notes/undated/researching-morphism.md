A **researching morphism** is the theory-level operation of research. Where a
research act moves evidence toward a claim, a researching morphism moves the host
theory itself:

```text
rho : T -> T'
```

The archived predication-category account gives three conditions. The extension
must preserve the old generator structure faithfully, be monotone in the
knowledge order on shared predicates, and strictly advance at least one resource
ideal. In plainer terms: it may add predicates and settle more, but it may not
destroy what the old theory already knew.

## What generates it

A researching morphism is generated when certified research is strong enough to
change the ideal by which resources are evaluated. It is not the same as
refactoring a resource, and it is not the same as moving a resource toward its
current ideal. It changes the ideal.

## What it generates

Because `T'` is stricter or more informed than `T`, a resource that was settled
under `T` can show a new deficit under `T'`. That is the productive force of
research: advancing the theory creates new work without falsifying the old
resource. Researching followed by idealization is the basic cycle of
theory-driven knowledge production.
