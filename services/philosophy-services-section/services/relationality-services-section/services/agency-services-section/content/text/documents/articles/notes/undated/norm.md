# norm

A rule of conduct backed by sanction — a structure experienced as binding.
Generated by: **structure** (normativizing — a regularity that deviation is sanctioned for).
Informal norms arise from structure alone; formal norms (**law**) additionally require **authority** to declare them and **power** to enforce them.
