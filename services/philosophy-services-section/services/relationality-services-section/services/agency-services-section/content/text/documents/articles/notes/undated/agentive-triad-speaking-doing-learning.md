# Agentive triad — speaking, doing, learning

The corpus has **speech-acts** developed as a thick structure: four Belnap-classified positing morphisms (assertive, denial, contestation, agentive-with-abstention), a [three-layer factorization](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md) (locution / illocution / perlocution), a [four-fold partition theorem](belnap-speech-act-partition.md) with Klein-four-symmetric class structure, a [channel-theoretic semantics](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md), and (newly) a [stratification of the L/I/P factorization under $K_4$](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md).

This question asks whether speech-acts are *one of three* parallel agentive categories — namely **speaking, doing, learning** — corresponding to the **truth / knowledge / evidence** triad load-bearing in the bilattice structure.

## The structural slot

The bilattice carries three independent structural axes:

| Axis | Order/coordinate | $D_4$ generator |
|---|---|---|
| Truth | $\leq_t$ | $\sim$ (negation) — preserves $\leq_k$, reverses $\leq_t$ |
| Knowledge | $\leq_k$ | $-$ (conflation) — preserves $\leq_t$, reverses $\leq_k$ |
| Evidence | $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie = \mathbf{L}_+ \otimes \mathbf{L}_-$ (substrate) | $\sigma$ (order-swap) — exchanges $\leq_k$ and $\leq_t$ |

By [Avron's representation theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/avron-representation.md), every interlaced bounded bilattice IS a twist product, so the truth-axis / knowledge-axis / evidence-substrate triad is the *universal* structure, not a choice.
The full symmetry group is $D_4$ (per [dihedral-symmetry-on-twist-products](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/dihedral-symmetry-on-twist-products.md)), generated by these three involutions.

Speech-acts currently align with $\sim$: the [Belnap partition](belnap-speech-act-partition.md) has $\sim$ permuting Assert ↔ Deny (truth-axis content), and the [$K_4$-stratification theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md) shows $\sim$ acts non-trivially on the illocution layer.

The hypothesis: $-$ and $\sigma$ are the symmetry generators of two *other* agentive categories — **doing-acts** ($-$, knowledge-axis-aligned) and **learning-acts** ($\sigma$, evidence-axis-aligned) — that should structurally parallel speech-acts.

## The candidate agentive triad

| Agentive category | Bilattice axis | Symmetry generator | Belnap-prototype | Corpus apparatus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speaking | Truth ($\leq_t$) | $\sim$ | Asserting ($q = \mathbf{t}$) | Positing morphisms (rich) |
| Doing | Knowledge ($\leq_k$) | $-$ | ??? (closure-application?) | Closure-operator, settling-morphism (scattered, not framed agentively) |
| Learning | Evidence ($\mathbf{L}_+, \mathbf{L}_-$) | $\sigma$ | ??? (witness-emission? researching?) | `researching` transformer (theory-level); stratum-0 atoms in stratified fiber (cell-level) |

Each category should presumably carry the same internal structure as speech-acts: a three-layer factorization, a four-fold Belnap-style classification, a stratification-under-its-symmetry-generator theorem, a channel-theoretic semantics.
None of doing-acts or learning-acts is currently organized as such a structure in the corpus.

## Three candidate readings of "learning"

Working through the gesture, three structurally distinct readings of "learning" emerge — at three different layers of the framework:

**Reading A: cell-level witness emission.** Learning-acts produce stratum-0 atoms in the [stratified predication fiber](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/objects/stratified-predication-fiber.md), distinguished from positing-stratum-0-atoms by provenance: positing-provenance points to *agent authority*, witnessing-provenance points to a *measurement source* or *sensing channel*.
Algebraically the same atom; the deps-chain extension differs.
In its weak form (provenance tagging), Reading A is metadata on stratum-0 atoms.
In its strong form, Reading A requires a separate **measurement morphism class** with a different structural signature from positing: where positing takes an agent-chosen value $q$ and emits it at $\phi$, a measurement morphism takes a measurement-function (world-state → bilattice-value) and emits whatever value it produces.
The agent does not choose the value; the agent chooses the measurement.
The framework does not currently have measurement morphisms.

**Reading B: theory-level researching.** The [`researching` morphism](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/morphisms/researching.md) extends the host theory's predicate-category and rule-system.
It adds new predicates, new derivable content, new ideal structure $v^{T'}_\infty$.
Resource settlings within the old theory do not change; what changes is the *ideal* against which they are measured.
This is meta-level: it acts on $\mathcal{C}$ (and possibly $E$) rather than on the value-substrate $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.
It does not align with the evidence-axis of the bilattice; it aligns with the *predicate-category-axis* of the framework's structural stratification.

**Reading C: inductive generalization via meta-realizability.** The [meta-realizability map](../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/meta-realizability-map.md) sends a settling to the closure-rule system it could realize.
Structurally this is an *inductive generalization step* — from data (settling content) to hypothesis (rule system that would produce it).
It is the **bridge** from Reading A (evidence-at-cells) to Reading B (theory-extension): you observe many cells (Reading A), the meta-realizability map identifies a rule-system pattern (Reading C), and the agent extends the theory via researching to encode the pattern (Reading B).
Reading C is abduction — inference from observation to explanation — and it is the corpus's existing apparatus for the cell-level → theory-level upward movement.

| Reading | Layer | Operation | Corpus apparatus |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Cell-value level | Emit stratum-0 atom with measurement-provenance | Stratified fiber stratum-0 (needs provenance refinement) |
| C | Cell → theory bridge | Inductive generalization from settling to realizable rule-system | Meta-realizability map |
| B | Theory level | Extend predicate-category / rule-system | Researching morphism |

## The horizontal/vertical asymmetry

The three readings do not collapse and do not subsume each other.
They live at three structurally-distinct layers, with the natural ordering being **A → C → B**: atomic evidence emission, inductive generalization that aggregates evidence into a pattern, theory-extension that encodes the pattern as new structure.

This makes "learning" structurally *vertically distributed* across the framework's stratification — spanning cell-level evidence, the upward bridge, and theory-level structure — in contrast to **speaking and doing**, which live *horizontally* at the cell-value layer (different bilattice axes within a fixed theory).

The original gesture toward symmetry — *speaking : truth :: doing : knowledge :: learning : evidence* — has to be reinterpreted in light of this asymmetry:

- **Speaking and doing are horizontal siblings** at the cell-value layer, operating along the truth-axis and knowledge-axis respectively *within a fixed theory*.
- **Learning is the vertical movement** that *extends the theory itself* in response to accumulated speaking/doing — going from cell-level evidence (Reading A) through inductive generalization (Reading C) to theory-extension (Reading B).

So the triad is not three-siblings-at-one-layer.
It is **two-horizontal-siblings (speak, do) at the cell-value layer plus one-vertical-axis (learn) that connects to the meta-moduli layer.** Speaking and doing operate on values within $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$; learning operates on *the space of possible values* by extending the predicate-category, with evidence-emission as its atomic act and theory-extension as its compositional/structural act.

**Consequence for the $D_4$ symmetry hypothesis.** $D_4$ acts on values within a fixed theory.
The horizontal pair (speaking, doing) plausibly does carry the $\sim/-$ part of the symmetry.
But $\sigma$ (the order-swap, which goes to the value-substrate) is *not* the natural symmetry generator for learning — because learning does not stay within a fixed value-substrate.
Learning *changes* the value-substrate (or rather, changes the cell-coordinates that values are attached to, which has the same effect of changing what values mean).
The third generator of $D_4$ may not be the right structural home for learning-acts.

Instead, the natural structural home for the symmetry of learning might be a **vertical** generator at the meta-moduli level — something that acts on the predicate-category stratification rather than on the value-substrate stratification.
The corpus's [CHL apex recursion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/_archive/prose/teaching/explanations/chl-apex-recursion.md) and the upward direction in the moduli tower (settling-moduli → meta-moduli → meta-meta-moduli) are candidates for where this generator lives.

This refines the original gesture: the agentive triad has a **two-tier structure** — - *Tier 1 (horizontal, cell-value):* speech-acts ($\sim$, truth-axis) ⊕ doing-acts ($-$, knowledge-axis). - *Tier 2 (vertical, meta):* learning-acts (atomic-A / bridge-C / compositional-B), spanning the cell-level → theory-level direction.

The $D_4$-symmetry covers Tier 1 cleanly.
Tier 2 needs its own symmetry analysis at the meta-moduli level.

## Doing-acts — the PCT-internal construction

The PCT-native objects underlying doing-acts are both in the chapter's `code/` directory:

- *The σ-nucleus:* the [closure-operator](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/morphisms/closure-operator.md) generated by the closure-rule system, equivalently the algebra map of the [closure-2-monad](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md).
  Knowledge-axis ($\leq_k$).
  Produces stratum-1 ("saturated") atoms in the [stratified-predication-fiber](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/objects/stratified-predication-fiber.md).
- *The Δ-nucleus:* the [bilattice-stabilization-nucleus](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/objects/bilattice-stabilization-nucleus.md) $T_a^* = a \Rightarrow (a \odot \cdot)$ iterated to fix.
  Truth-axis ($\leq_t$).
  Produces stratum-2 ("settled") atoms.

The joint fixed point of the two nuclei is the settled fiber; together they realize the closure-2-monad's algebra structure on the predication fiber algebra.

### Doing-acts split intrinsically into two operational kinds

Speech-acts produce stratum-0 atoms via positing morphisms.
Doing-acts produce stratum-1 atoms via σ-nucleus application or stratum-2 atoms via Δ-nucleus application.
The split is built into the stratified-predication-fiber: **σ-doing-acts** and **Δ-doing-acts** are structurally distinct because they produce atoms at structurally distinct strata.

### The by-produced-value Belnap classification (per stratum)

Each doing-act is further classified by which Belnap value gets produced at the affected cell — parallel to how speech-acts are classified by which Belnap value gets posited:

| σ-doing-act | Produced value | Δ-doing-act | Produced value |
|---|---|---|---|
| σ-Inferring | $\mathbf{t}$ | Δ-Stabilize-True | $\mathbf{t}$-fixed-point |
| σ-Refuting | $\mathbf{f}$ | Δ-Stabilize-False | $\mathbf{f}$-fixed-point |
| σ-Idling | $\bot$ (rule fires, no new content) | Δ-Stabilize-Void | $\bot$-fixed-point |
| σ-Conflicting | $\top$ (contradictory rules merge) | Δ-Stabilize-Saturated | $\top$-fixed-point |

Eight doing-act classes total: 4 Belnap × 2 strata.
The Belnap quartet within each stratum is the same as the speech-act Belnap quartet; the stratum-2-fold-ness is what doing-acts have that speech-acts don't.

### The $K_4$ symmetry — speech-acts and doing-acts share the same value-side symmetry

(Corrected this turn.
An earlier draft claimed the $\sigma$-generator of $D_4$ exchanges σ-stratum and Δ-stratum as a within-theory symmetry of doing-acts; this was wrong. $\sigma$ is a *meta-theoretic* equivalence between theories, not a within-theory automorphism of the nucleus pair.)

Speech-acts carry $K_4 = \langle \sim, - \rangle$ acting on the Belnap quartet of posited values.
Doing-acts carry the **same** $K_4$, acting on the Belnap quartet of *produced* values within each stratum:

- $\sim$ permutes the truth-axis Belnap pairs within each stratum: σ-Inferring ↔ σ-Refuting; Δ-Stabilize-True ↔ Δ-Stabilize-False.
- $-$ permutes the knowledge-axis Belnap pairs within each stratum: σ-Idling ↔ σ-Conflicting; Δ-Stabilize-Void ↔ Δ-Stabilize-Saturated.

So $K_4$ acts independently on each stratum's 4-class quartet.
The full action on the 8-class doing-act space is $K_4$ acting fiber-wise — *not* a $D_4$-action with $\sigma$ exchanging strata. σ-nucleus and Δ-nucleus are different operators with different signatures (rule-driven vs bilattice-algebra-driven); no canonical involution within a single theory exchanges them.

**What $\sigma$ actually does (meta-theoretic).** Per [bilattice-two-orders-duality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/bilattice-two-orders-duality.md), σ-conjugation gives a structural equivalence between $\leq_k$-closure-theory and $\leq_t$-closure-theory: for any $\leq_k$-closure operator $T_k$, the conjugate $\sigma_* T_k \sigma_*^{-1}$ is a $\leq_t$-closure operator with the same closure ordinal and firing-pattern combinatorics.
This is at the **meta-theory level** — it relates one theory $T$ to a dual theory $\sigma^*(T)$.
Within a single theory, σ doesn't exchange σ-stratum-acts and Δ-stratum-acts; it gives a dual theory where what was σ-stratum becomes σ-stratum-on-the-other-order.

| Agentive category | Within-theory symmetry | σ-action |
|---|---|---|
| Speech-acts | $K_4 = \langle \sim, - \rangle$ on the Belnap quartet at stratum-0 | Meta-theoretic only |
| Doing-acts | $K_4 = \langle \sim, -\rangle$ on the Belnap quartet at each stratum (σ and Δ) | Meta-theoretic only |

This is structurally tighter than the corrected claim's predecessor: speech-acts and doing-acts have the *same* invariance group within a fixed theory.
They differ in which stratum they produce at, not in their value-side symmetry.

**This refines the agentive-triad framing.** The $\sigma$-generator that appears in $D_4$ via the [dihedral-symmetry theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/dihedral-symmetry-on-twist-products.md) is a *meta-theoretic* equivalence — it moves between theories, not within one.
This places $\sigma$ at the meta-moduli level — exactly where the original gesture suggested learning-acts live.
The $D_4$ decomposes naturally as:

- $K_4$ at the cell-level (within a single theory) — covers speech and doing.
- $\sigma$ at the meta-level (between theories) — candidate symmetry for learning-acts.

So the original triadic gesture lands more cleanly than the recent draft: cell-level value-side symmetry is $K_4$ (shared by speaking and doing); meta-level theory-equivalence symmetry is $\sigma$ (aligned with learning).
The earlier claim that "$\sigma$ is doing-act-internal" was the error; $\sigma$ is genuinely meta-theoretic.

### The three-layer factorization for doing-acts

Speech-acts factor as [locution/illocution/perlocution](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md).
Doing-acts admit a parallel three-layer factorization, with the middle factor differing structurally (rule-firing or nucleus-stabilization rather than value-positing) but the overall shape preserved.

**For σ-doing-acts.** Every operationally-realizable σ-step $v_0 \to v_1$ decomposes as:

- *Locution (σ-act):* select the closure-rule $r \in \mathcal{S}$ to be applied, with its target cell $\phi$.
- *Illocution (σ-act):* fire $r$ at $\phi$, emitting a stratum-1 atom with `deps` pointing to the firing's premises.
  The emitted value is determined by the rule's consequent.
- *Perlocution (σ-act):* closure propagates downstream — possibly enabling further σ-firings, possibly triggering Δ-stabilization at the moved cell, possibly being absorbed without further effect.

**For Δ-doing-acts.** Every operationally-realizable Δ-step decomposes as:

- *Locution (Δ-act):* select the cell $\phi$ to stabilize, with its background value $a$ (per the bilattice-stabilization-nucleus's background-value policy).
- *Illocution (Δ-act):* apply $T_a^*$ to $\phi$'s value, emitting a stratum-2 atom with `deps` pointing to the cell's prior stratum-0/1 atoms whose values were composed in the orbit.
- *Perlocution (Δ-act):* the stabilization completes; possibly triggers further σ-firings if values changed in ways that match unfired rules' premises.

The two doing-act layerings are structurally identical at the framing level — same three layers, same `deps`-chain discipline — but differ in the middle layer's operation.
This makes "doing-act" a single agentive category with two operational kinds, rather than two separate categories.

### Conjectured $K_4$-stratification analog for doing-acts

The [$K_4$-stratifies-LIP theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md) for speech-acts establishes: $K_4$ acts trivially on locution, non-trivially on illocution (permuting Belnap classes), conditionally on perlocution (requires rule-system $K_4$-closure).
The doing-act parallel should give the same stratification:

**Conjecture (doing-acts $K_4$-stratification).** For doing-acts in both strata: - $K_4$ acts trivially on the locution layer (cell-or-rule selection — values not yet at play). - $K_4$ acts non-trivially on the illocution layer (permuting the produced-value Belnap class via $\sim$ and $-$ as in the speech-act case). - $K_4$ acts conditionally on the perlocution layer — iff the closure-rule system $\mathcal{S}$ is $K_4$-closed (every rule's $K_4$-twist is derivable from $\mathcal{S}$).

The $K_4$-closure condition is **the same as in the speech-act theorem** — because the rule system is the same object in both cases, and rule-firing perlocution depends on the rule system identically in both.
The doing-act stratification theorem would therefore reuse the speech-act stratification's perlocution analysis directly.
The structural unity: speech-acts and doing-acts share both the value-side symmetry ($K_4$) AND the rule-system-dependent perlocution-equivariance condition.
They differ only in where their produced atoms land (stratum-0 vs stratum-1/2).

### The orthogonal closure-status taxonomy (cross-cutting across all agentive categories)

A separate, *orthogonal* PCT-internal classification of operations exists: classifying every operation by which of the two closure structures has reached fixed-point on its output.
This taxonomy is recoverable inside PCT through either of two equivalent routes, both PCT-internal:

- *Route A (pass-decomposition).* Classify by (σ-nucleus-fixed?, Δ-nucleus-fixed?) where the two nuclei are the rule-saturation closure and the bilattice-stabilization-nucleus from the stratified-predication-fiber.
  Both are PCT objects.
- *Route B (bigraded-modal).* Classify by ($\mathrm{Cl}$-fixed?, $\mathrm{Cl}^*$-fixed?) where $\mathrm{Cl}$ is the closure 2-monad (necessity-side, lax-idempotent on $\mathbf{PrFib}$) and $\mathrm{Cl}^*$ is the [dualized-closure-2-comonad](../../../../../../../../../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/dualized-closure-2-comonad.md) (possibility-side, colax-idempotent on $\mathbf{PrFib}^*$).
  Both are PCT objects, from the dual-hyperdoctrine apparatus.

Both routes give a 2×2 = 4-position partition by closure-status.
The four positions:

| σ-status | Δ-status | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed | Fixed | Both closures have nothing more to add to the output. *Fully-settled* operation. |
| Open | Fixed | σ has work pending; Δ is satisfied. *Saturation-pending* operation. |
| Fixed | Open | σ is done; Δ has work pending. *Stabilization-pending* operation. |
| Open | Open | Neither closure has reached fixed-point on the output. *Fully-open* operation. |

(The engineering layer names these Process / Derivation / Procedure / Inquiry; the PCT-internal structural taxonomy doesn't require those operational names.)

This taxonomy is **cross-cutting across all agentive categories** — speech-acts, doing-acts, and learning-acts each have closure-status classifications.
A bare speech-act (positing without follow-up closure) is typically *fully-open* at its output.
A σ-doing-act that completes its rule-firing may be *stabilization-pending* (σ-fixed on the affected cell, Δ unstabilized).
A Δ-doing-act may be *fully-settled* if it brings both nuclei to fixpoint on the affected cell.

The two classifications cross-classify operations: every operation has both a by-produced-value Belnap-class AND a by-closure-status class.
The product structure is **4 Belnap × 4 closure-status = 16 cross-classes per stratum**, or — for doing-acts which span two strata — 32 cross-classes.
Not all 32 are realized; the actual realized cross-classes are a sub-product whose structure is open.

The two classifications answer structurally different questions:

- *By-produced-value Belnap-style:* what the act IS (what it produces at the affected cell).
  Agentive-internal.
- *By-closure-status:* what state the act leaves the substrate in.
  Operation-effect.

Both are PCT-native; neither subsumes the other.

## Sub-questions

**1.
Doing-acts: are the by-produced-value Belnap classes structurally distinct under the $D_4$ action?**

The Belnap-stratification-theorem-analog for doing-acts: $\sim$ permutes truth-axis pairs, $-$ permutes knowledge-axis pairs, $\sigma$ exchanges σ-stratum and Δ-stratum.
Whether this gives a structurally-distinct $D_4$-action on the 8-class space (vs collapsing some classes under the symmetry) is the parallel-of-Belnap-partition theorem for doing-acts.
Conjectured: 8 classes form a $D_4$-orbit of size 8 (a single regular orbit, with $|D_4| = 8$).

**1a.
Closure-status taxonomy: which sub-classes are actually realized?**

The product 4-Belnap × 4-closure-status = 16 cross-classes per stratum is the maximum possible.
The actually-realized sub-product is a structural fact about the framework: certain Belnap values may force certain closure-statuses.
Conjectured constraints:

- *σ-Idling forces σ-fixed:* if a σ-pass step produces $\bot$ (no new content), σ-saturation isn't advanced, so σ-status is unchanged.
- *σ-Conflicting forces both-open:* producing $\top$ from contradictory rules may unlock further σ-firings AND requires Δ-stabilization, so both nuclei have work pending.

Whether such constraints are general theorems or example-specific is open.

**1b.
Connection to closure-rule-system $K_4$-closure (from the K_4-stratifies-LIP theorem).**

The [klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md) shows perlocution is $K_4$-equivariant iff the rule system is $K_4$-closed.
The doing-act analog: doing-act perlocution should be $K_4$-equivariant iff the rule system is $K_4$-closed — same condition, same theorem-shape.
(The earlier $D_4$-closure conjecture was an artifact of the wrong $D_4$-on-doing-acts claim; corrected to $K_4$.)
Whether the theorem transfers verbatim or requires new structural argument for the stratum-1/2 produced-atom case is what would settle this sub-question.

**1c.
The bilattice-two-orders-duality and its action on the agentive triad.**

[Bilattice-two-orders-duality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/bilattice-two-orders-duality.md) gives a structural equivalence between $\leq_k$-closure-theory and $\leq_t$-closure-theory via σ-conjugation.
This is meta-theoretic: it relates theory $T$ to its σ-dual $\sigma^*(T)$.
Under σ-conjugation:

- σ-doing-acts in $T$ (knowledge-axis rule-saturation) correspond to σ-doing-acts in $\sigma^*(T)$ — same operational kind, but now on the $\leq_t$ axis.
- Δ-doing-acts similarly correspond.
- Speech-acts in $T$ correspond to speech-acts in $\sigma^*(T)$ on the order-swapped Belnap quartet.

So σ-conjugation acts on the entire agentive picture as a *theory-equivalence*, not as a within-theory automorphism.
This is the structurally-correct home for σ — at the meta-moduli level where theories are objects.

**Conjectured connection to learning-acts.** The meta-moduli-level σ-action is plausibly the symmetry of learning-acts (Reading B's theory-extension and Reading C's inductive generalization).
Under σ-conjugation, the [researching morphism](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/morphisms/researching.md) $\rho : T \to T'$ has a σ-dual $\sigma_* \rho : \sigma^*(T) \to \sigma^*(T')$, possibly with structurally-distinct content.
The σ-symmetry of learning-acts would be the closure of the learning-act category under σ-conjugation.

**2.
Learning-acts: three readings, what is the unified structure?**

Reading A (cell-level witness emission), Reading C (inductive generalization via meta-realizability), Reading B (theory-extension via researching) span three layers.
The natural composition is A → C → B: atomic evidence emission, abductive generalization, theory-extension.
Whether this is *the* structure of learning-acts or whether it is one of several candidate composition patterns is open.
Sub-sub-questions:

- *Is Reading A's strong form needed?* The weak form (provenance tagging on stratum-0 atoms) is a small refinement; the strong form (measurement morphism class with a different structural signature from positing) is new apparatus.
  Which is load-bearing?
- *Is Reading C's meta-realizability map the right bridge?* Alternative candidates: Hermida-Jacobs fibered coinduction (already mentioned in corpus proofs), the CHL apex recursion, settling-coalgebra-structure's underlying upward dynamics.
  Each gives a different abduction-like operation.
- *Does Reading B require Readings A and C as precursors?* That is: can a researching morphism be applied "freely" (the theorist proposes new structure on intuition alone), or does it require a witness-grounding (the agent has accumulated evidence that justifies the new structure)?
  The corpus's current `researching` definition does not require evidence-grounding; whether it *should* is a downstream design question.
- *Are there learning-acts that do NOT compose A → C → B?* Pure-Reading-A (witness without generalization), pure-Reading-B (theory-extension on intuition), pure-Reading-C (generalization without follow-through to theory-extension) all seem possible.
  Each is a degenerate or partial learning-act in the three-layer picture.

**3.
Three-layer factorization for each act-type.**

Speech-acts factor as locution / illocution / perlocution.
Do doing-acts and learning-acts also factor through three layers?
Candidate factorizations:

- *Doing-acts:* selection (which rule or closure step to apply) / application (running the rule or step) / propagation (downstream cells affected).
  This is structurally L/I/P-shaped, just with the middle factor being rule-application rather than value-positing.
- *Learning-acts (cell-level):* sensing (which feature to observe) / emission (what evidence atom to emit) / integration (how it joins the fiber).
  Also three-layer-shaped.
- *Learning-acts (theory-level):* identification (which predicate to add) / extension (extending the theory) / re-stratification (how it shifts settlements across resources).
  Three-layer-shaped at the meta-level.

A clean structural result would establish that *every* operationally-realizable agentive act decomposes through a three-layer factorization of the same shape, with each factor corresponding to: selection-of-target, application-of-act, propagation-of-effect.

The [$K_4$-stratifies-LIP theorem](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/theorems/klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md) shows $K_4$ acts trivially on locution, non-trivially on illocution, conditionally on perlocution.
The parallel question: does $K_4$ (or a generator-specific version) stratify the doing-act and learning-act factorizations the same way?
Or differently?

A natural conjecture: $D_4$ stratifies all three triadic act-categories in a unified pattern, with *each generator* ($\sim$, $-$, $\sigma$) being the primary symmetry of *its corresponding* act-category and acting on the others either trivially, conditionally, or via a structural twist.

## Why this matters

PCT's claim that "bytes are predications" sits on a triadic substrate — truth, knowledge, evidence — and the corpus has *one* of the three corresponding agentive categories thickly developed.
The original gesture toward a clean symmetric agentive triad has, on analysis, refined into something richer: **two horizontal siblings (speaking, doing) at the cell-value layer plus a vertical axis (learning) that connects to the meta-moduli layer**.
The framework's agentive structure is not three-siblings-at-one-layer; it is a two-tier picture with a cleanly different role for learning.

This refinement has three concrete consequences.

First, it predicts that **doing-acts will parallel speech-acts structurally** — same three-layer factorization, same Belnap-style four-fold classification, same symmetry stratification (with $-$ playing the role $\sim$ plays for speech-acts) — because both live horizontally at the cell-value layer where $D_4$ acts on values within a fixed theory.
The work to fill in doing-acts is then a *parallel construction* exercise: copy the speech-act apparatus to the knowledge-axis.

Second, it predicts that **learning-acts will NOT parallel speech-acts structurally** — because learning is vertically distributed across three layers (atomic A, bridge C, compositional B), not horizontally on one.
The work to fill in learning-acts is then a *layered construction* exercise: develop the witness-emission apparatus at cell-level, identify the right inductive-generalization bridge, and connect it to theory-extension.
The Belnap classification and $K_4$-stratification may not apply to learning-acts at all; learning-acts likely require their own meta-moduli-level symmetry analysis.

Third, it suggests the bilattice's $D_4$-symmetry **covers cell-level agency cleanly but does not cover learning-acts**.
Speech-acts and doing-acts together exhaust the $\sim$-and-$-$ part of $D_4$; the $\sigma$ generator (order-swap, evidence-substrate) is *not* the natural home for learning, because learning changes the predicate-category, not the value-substrate.
The full agentive picture requires *two* symmetry analyses: $D_4$ (or a sub-group) on cell-value-layer acts, and a separate meta-moduli-level symmetry on learning-acts.

The deepest version of the question: **does the corpus's apparent symmetry between value-substrate and predicate-category at the meta level give learning-acts their own dihedral or higher symmetry group, analogous to but structurally distinct from $D_4$?** If yes, the framework's full agentive structure has *two* parallel symmetry groups: $D_4$ on cell-value-layer acts (speech and doing), and a meta-symmetry on theory-layer acts (learning).
If no, learning-acts are structurally less symmetric than speech-and-doing-acts, and the asymmetry between value-layer and theory-layer agency is itself a structural fact of the framework.
