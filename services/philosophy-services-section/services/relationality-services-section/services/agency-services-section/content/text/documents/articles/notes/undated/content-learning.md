**Learning** is one of the two acts of [working](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/docs/working.md): reading from the shared field and revising what one takes to be settled.
It is the receiving direction of work — drawing in what others have contributed and letting it change one's own hold on things.
Dual to [teaching](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/teaching/docs/teaching.md), which writes to the same field.

## Learning revises the frame, not only the content

Learning is not the mere accumulation of more items.
Dewey took it as the potential *reconstruction of experience*: one learns when an encounter forces a revision in how one will meet the next encounter.
Piaget marked the crucial distinction — *assimilation* fits new material into the schemes one already has, but genuine learning is *accommodation*, the revising of the schemes themselves when the world refuses to fit them.
Gregory Bateson made the same point in levels: there is learning *within* a frame, and there is the higher "deutero-learning," learning that changes the frame one learns within.

This is why learning is more than teaching run backwards.
Speaking and acting move values *within* a settled understanding; learning can move the understanding itself — extend it, correct it, reorganize it — in response to what has accumulated on the shared field.
It runs "vertically": a single act of learning can re-measure everything one had previously taken as settled.

## What generates it

Learning is generated by working: it is the reading direction of the shared field.
It reads what [teaching](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/teaching/docs/teaching.md) and others have written there; without a common field there is nothing to learn *from*.
As Vygotsky insisted, learning is social before it is individual — one learns first in the company of others and from what they have laid down, and only afterward on one's own.

## What it generates

Learning generates a revised understanding: a self whose grasp of its [world](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/governing/library/individual/library/world/docs/world.md) has been altered by what it took in.
Because that revision can reach the framework itself, learning is the act by which a self — and the shared work it contributes to — grows rather than merely accretes.
