## Form

Textbook.
The standard university-level introduction to multi-agent systems and the consolidated reference for the classical-MAS tradition.

## Content summary

Develops the multi-agent-systems (MAS) field across its constituent topics: the agent abstraction (autonomy, reactivity, pro-activeness, social ability); deductive, practical, and reactive reasoning architectures (logic-based, BDI, subsumption); communication (speech-act theory, agent communication languages — KQML and FIPA-ACL); cooperation (task and resource sharing, contract net); coordination (joint plans, joint intentions, mutual modeling); negotiation (auction protocols, bargaining); argumentation; trust and reputation; methodologies (Gaia, Tropos); and applications.
The textbook consolidates the field's vocabulary, formalisms, and engineering practices into a single reference.
