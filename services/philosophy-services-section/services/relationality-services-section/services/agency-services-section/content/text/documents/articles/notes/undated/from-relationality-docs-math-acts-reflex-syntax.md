## Interpretation

The syntactic calculus inherits the *Reflex* action on recognitions.  *Reflex* witnessing *Construct Terms* produces ***Reflex Syntax*** ([ReflexSyntax](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/reflex-syntax.md)).

Applying our primitive act to *Construct Terms* records the syntactic ρ-operator, so *Reflex Syntax* follows.
