# geontopower

Generated by: biopower under late liberalism, corrected by the governance of the distinction between Life and Nonlife.

Geontopower names a power that governs not only living populations but the boundary through which Life is separated from Nonlife.
Where biopower takes biological life as its object, geontopower asks how governing arrangements decide what counts as life, what counts as inert matter, and which relations between people, lands, ancestors, substances, and worlds become politically intelligible.

In this spine, geontopower is placed as a correction and extension of biopower rather than as a free-floating topic.
Its archive preserves Povinellian, late-liberal, and older predication-category materials.
