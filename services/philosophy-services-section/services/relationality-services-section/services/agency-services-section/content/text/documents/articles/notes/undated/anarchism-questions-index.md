Open work for anarchism at the level of the school as a whole.
Strand-specific open work lives in each strand's own subdomain.
What is held here are the questions whose scope crosses strands.

## Open structural questions

- **What is the operative theory of organization for the school today?** The internal disagreement between insurrectionary, anarcho-nihilist, social-anarchist, and platformist currents on the organizational question (see [`organization/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/questions/theories/organization), [`insurrectionary-anarchism/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/questions/theories/insurrectionary-anarchism), [`anarcho-nihilism/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/questions/theories/anarcho-nihilism)) has not converged in over a century.
  The structural question is whether it should and what convergence would look like.
- **What does the encounter with Indigenous political traditions require of European-inheritance anarchism?** The [`indigenous-anarchism/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/questions/theories/indigenous-anarchism) subdomain registers the question; the substantive answer requires sustained engagement with the Indigenous traditions on their own terms rather than as confirmation of anarchism's a priori commitments.
- **How does anarchism read the cybernetic-postliberal analysis of platform-mediated organization?** Contemporary platforms have absorbed the language of horizontal organization while reproducing extractive command at the infrastructure level.
  The question is what anarchist analysis can say about platform form, and whether the existing anarchist organizational vocabulary is adequate to it.
- **Where does the analysis of the carceral state fit?** The abolitionist analysis (in [`abolition/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/abolition)) is structurally adjacent to anarchism's analysis of the state, but the two have not fully converged — abolitionism has its own lineage (Black-radical, primarily), and the question of whether anarchism should adopt the abolitionist analysis wholesale, contribute to it, or maintain a distinct position is open.
