Zork-on-FARS is a standalone project, not superseded by the predication world sim. The world sim is wildly speculative; Zork is a known artifact with known behavior that can empirically test whether the FARS algebra works.

**Why:** Speculative projects can't be tested. Zork has deterministic expected outputs — you can verify respond() produces the right result for every command. It's a test harness for the algebra.

**How to apply:** Never fold Zork into broader speculative projects. Treat it as its own plannable, taskable project with empirical success criteria. When prioritizing, favor Zork over speculative sim work because it produces testable results.
