**Genring** is the reduction of a living people to a fixed literary type — a *genre figure*.
It is a political operation in three steps: *selection* (some features of a people are chosen as representative), *codification* (those features are fixed into a active type), and *substitution* (the type displaces the living people as the operative unit in political, cultural, and institutional discourse).
The result is a genre figure: the Noble Indian, the Hearty Bavarian, the Salt-of-the-Earth Farmer, the Silicon Valley Founder.
The genre figure is not a stereotype in the loose sense of an overgeneralization; it is a narrative role with prescribed attributes, affective valences, and a place in the national story.

In predication-categories terms: genring is a fiber-collapsing operation on the social settling.
A living people $P \subseteq E$ has a rich, contested, historically evolving predicate-fiber bundle $\{F(e)\}_{e \in P}$.
Genring maps this bundle to a single canonical predicate-value assignment $\hat{F}$ — the genre figure — and substitutes $\hat{F}$ for the actual fiber bundle in the relevant discourse.
The substitution is an act of forced convergence: the overdetermined ($\top$) and unknown ($\bot$) fibers of actual social reality are collapsed to $\mathbf{t}$ or $\mathbf{f}$ according to the genre's script, erasing the settling dynamics that produced them.

## The three-step operation

**Selection.** From the heterogeneous reality of a people — contradictory internal positions, contested histories, diverse practices — a subset of features is selected as representative.
The selection is never neutral: it reflects the interests of the selecting apparatus (the state, the intellectual formation, the nationalist movement) and forecloses the features that do not serve the genre's narrative function.

**Codification.** The selected features are frozen into a active type: the Noble Indian is wise, nature-connected, stoic, and temporally prior to modernity.
These attributes are fixed, not as descriptions that could be revised by new evidence, but as constitutive features of the type.
Evidence that does not fit the type is noise; evidence that confirms it is ground-truth.
The codification is genre-coherent: the attributes form a narrative whole, with an emotional register, a place in the national story, and appropriate behavior scripts.

**Substitution.** The genre figure is substituted for the living people in institutional, cultural, and political discourse.
Policy addresses the genre figure; historical narrative features the genre figure; political movements invoke or oppose the genre figure.
The living people — heterogeneous, contesting, exceeding the genre — remain, but they are not what the discourse is about.
When they deviate from the genre (when a Lakota person writes mathematics, a Bavarian reads Marx, a Kurdish woman leads an anarchist revolution), the deviation registers as inauthenticity rather than as evidence against the type.

## Relation to liberal-ontologizing

Genring requires [liberal ontologizing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-ontologizing.md) as its metaphysical precondition.
Only if peoples are treatable as substances with inherent attributes does the reduction to a fixed type make sense.
The predication-categories substrate shows exactly why: genring is coherent only if the fiber values are treated as the entity's properties rather than as settling-results.
If the social settling is the primary ontological fact — if what a people *is* is a function of historically contested predication dynamics — then genring is visibly a distortion, not a description.
Liberal ontologizing makes genring *look* like description.

## Relation to genre-coherence

[Genre coherence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/representing/content/liberal-ontologizing/representing/content/genring/_archive/teaching/explanations/genre-coherence.md) is the structural pressure genring produces once established: the demand that the genre figure remain active.
Anyone who does not fit the assigned genre becomes a narrative problem — not necessarily an ideological enemy, but a disruption of the national story's cast.
The feeling that something is "wrong" when a genred person deviates from type is genre coherence enforcing itself as social affect.

## Relation to ontogenring

Where genring operates on *peoples* (reducing them to genre figures in collective discourse), [ontogenring](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontogenring.md) operates on *individuals* (installing a being-template in a specific subject).
Genring provides the available genre-figure inventory from which ontogenring draws; ontogenring is the process by which individual subjects come to occupy those genres as their ontological form.
