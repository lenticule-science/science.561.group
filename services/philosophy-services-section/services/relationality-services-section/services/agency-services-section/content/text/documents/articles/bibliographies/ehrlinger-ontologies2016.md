# Ehrlinger & Wöß, "Towards a Definition of Knowledge Graphs" (2016)

Definitional/terminological paper on KGs vs
ontologies vs related concepts.

## What it argues

- "Knowledge graph" used loosely; needs clearer
  definition.
- Reviews definitions across literature.
- Proposes: a KG acquires and integrates information
  into an ontology and applies a reasoner to derive
  new knowledge.
- Distinguishes KGs from databases, ontologies, and
  semantic networks.

## Why it matters

Useful clarification of overlapping terminology.
Frequently cited as a reference for what KGs are
(or aren't). Influences how KGs are described.

## Caveat

Other definitions exist; the Ehrlinger-Wöß
formulation is more restrictive than common
practical use (which often calls any large
entity-relation graph a KG).

## How to read

Short paper; readable in one sitting.
