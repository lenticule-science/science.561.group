## Annotation

The most comprehensive single-volume treatment of knowledge representation as a research field: covers symbolic logic, modal and intensional logic, ontology, contexts, processes, agents, and the conceptual-graph notation Sowa himself developed (a graphical syntax for first-order logic with a sortal type hierarchy). Sowa's framing organises KR around four dimensions: (i) the *logic* used to express assertions, (ii) the *ontology* used to type the entities, (iii) the *computation* used to derive conclusions, and (iv) the *language* used as the human-readable surface.

The predicated framing recognises Sowa's four dimensions as a near-isomorphic decomposition of the predication-cell substrate: (i) the bilattice value-domain plus the closure-rule language is the *logic*; (ii) the [predicate-category](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) plus its [stratification](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/teaching/explanations/predicate-category-stratification.md) is the *ontology*; (iii) the [predication-settling](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) dynamics is the *computation*; (iv) the surface syntax (markdown notes, JSON-LD, S-expressions) is the *language*. Sowa keeps the four dimensions independent; the framing makes them algebraically coordinated by the substrate.

Sowa's *conceptual graphs* notation — a bipartite graph of concept-nodes and relation-nodes — is recoverable as a presentation of the [predication-fibration](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md) where each relation-node is a predicate and each concept-node is a subject. The framing's claim is that conceptual graphs are *a particular surface syntax* for the predication-cell substrate, not an alternative substrate.
