# Sowa, *Knowledge Representation: Logical, Philosophical, and Computational Foundations* (2000)

A philosophically-rich KR textbook.

## What it covers

- Logical foundations of KR.
- Conceptual graphs (Sowa's formalism).
- Ontologies and ontological categories.
- Linguistic and philosophical foundations.
- Knowledge acquisition and engineering.
- Reasoning and inference.

Distinctive philosophical depth; covers KR's
intellectual foundations as well as its formal tools.

## Why it matters

Brings philosophy of language and ontology to bear on
KR practice. Sowa's conceptual graphs influence
linguistic and KR work. Useful for understanding the
*why* of KR design choices.

## How to read

Substantial textbook; philosophically engaged.
Worthwhile for those wanting depth beyond formalisms.

## Related works

- Brachman-Levesque 2004 — more standard textbook.
- Conceptual graphs literature.
