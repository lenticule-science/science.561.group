# Witnessing

**A WIT is one witnessed distinction-step, a cover of FOUR's knowledge
order.** This source term belongs to Obsmetrologia's
[mathematical and monetary denomination](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/wit.md).
It is neither a synonym for an eyewitness, nor for an entire narrated
incident, testimony, perception, acknowledgment, attention, or social
approval. To call something *one wit*, exhibit one particular knowledge-order
0→1 cover (bottom→true, bottom→false, true→both, or false→both) and the
evidence-bearing claim or cell on which that cover occurs. Bottom→both
crosses **two** covers; the shortcut is not one wit.

Obsmetrologia's
[`Record`](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Record.lean)
keeps two natural-number evidence tallies, `pos` and `neg`; its
[`Charge` readout](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Four.lean)
forgets multiplicity by retaining whether each tally is positive.
A witness can provide new evidence without creating a *new* FOUR cover
when that polarity is already present. Conversely, a single direct
bottom-to-both change consists of two covers. A social testimony can
bear many different claims; do not count it as exactly one wit without
specifying the claims and readings.

## The other turns of the spiral

**ACK** is another party's uptake of a WIT across a boundary, minted
on the receiving side; an asserter's self-congratulation does not
mint an ACK. Receipt or recognition of *contested* evidence can be a
valid ACK without resolving it. The [ACK glossary](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ack.md)
specifies the border. Where release and admission instruments agree on
the exact crossing, they compose a [tally](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tally.md);
one half is not settlement.

**ATT** is a WIT **of an ACK**: the originating face witnesses
the other's uptake. This is the second-order move called attention
in the [ATT glossary](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/att.md).
It is not defined as subjective interest, time spent reading, or
general-purpose expenditure of capacity. Attention-allocation proofs
in Obsmetrologia give constraints on scarce budget; they do not by
themselves prove the proposed WIT→ACK→ATT equivalences with
Landauer bits or the `H⁰/H¹` index balance. The
[2026-10-06 audit](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/docs/history/quot-sound-as-residue.md)
records the remaining bridges honestly.

**Recognition** in relationality is again a *different* act:
registering a claim under a category or form. The existing
[recognition definition](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recognition.md)
is not overwritten by economic ACK or by interpersonal assent.

## Social testimony is a case, not the definition of WIT

In testimony and trauma studies, *bearing witness* can describe
speaking of an event, making it available to an addressee, or
taking responsibility for listening. Felman and Laub's
*Testimony* (1992) studies the difference between witnessing within
an event, receiving testimony, and the act of testifying.
Fricker's *Epistemic Injustice* (2007) studies unequal credibility
given to speakers and the injustice of discounting testimony.

These accounts raise questions about which evidence can travel
between agents and who admits it. They do not imply that testimony
already has a counterparty ACK, that receiving it supplies a
second-order ATT back to the witness, or that the witnessed obligation
was discharged. One may attest an event that another party refuses
to take up. An addressee can acknowledge a claim yet classify or
adjudicate it differently. Both its evidential reading and its
normative obligations can remain contested.

For the political case where electoral participation is used to
claim closure on a **different** witnessed obligation, see the
[Political Science analysis of electoral receipts](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/14/2026-10-14-witness-acknowledgment-attention-obligation-electoralism.md).
The central question there is whether an electoral ACK/ATT is
substituted for a still-unmatched obligation-specific settlement.

## Formalization boundary

The mathematical source is **not** this prose essay. Check the actual
FOUR carrier, `Record`, and `WitLedger` before making mechanized claims.
There is no established blanket isomorphism between ordinary
eyewitness testimony, FOUR WIT covers, `Quot.sound` occurrences,
border receipt pairs, and the Landauer/`H¹` counts; the denomination
bridge remains under research.
