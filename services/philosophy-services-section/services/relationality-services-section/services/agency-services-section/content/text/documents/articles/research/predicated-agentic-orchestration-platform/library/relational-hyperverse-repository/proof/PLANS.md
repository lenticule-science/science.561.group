# Proof Plans

Plans for the `proof/` directory — machine-checked proofs of the Relational Hyperverse theorems.

## Role in the Repository Fourfold

`proof/` is the **Language phase** of the repository's own fourfold: it is where the mathematical meanings established in `math/` settle into verified canonical form.
Just as FML₀ terms reduce to unique canonical representatives, proof terms settle the meanings of the axioms and theorems into machine-checked normal form.
The output of `proof/` is the **Syntactic Relational Universe as initial model** — the free model generated by the axioms, verified to exist.

## What Machine Proofs Do

Machine proofs are not just verification — they are the primary diagnostic for rigor gaps.
A Lean proof term must exhibit every object it claims exists.

Gaps surfaced so far:

- **`noncomputable` on `H_syn V`** — comes from `propext` in `synLE`; removable once normalization completeness is formalized (no math gap, pure engineering).
- **Initiality uniqueness** — `proof_sketch` in `math/initiality.md` admits "extension uniqueness asserted not verified"; Lean cannot close what the math has not established.
  Needs Relational Faithfulness axiom in `RelationalUniverse`.
- **`nucleus_neg_fixed`** — discovered while closing `StableEnvelope.lean`: any nucleus with j(⊥)=⊥ fixes all Heyting negations.
  The cross-nucleus conditions in `stableEnvelope_mem_fixedFiber` follow from ⊥-preservation alone, simplifying the math.
  Both `StableEnvelope.lean` and `math/stable-envelope.md` updated.
