## Form

Survey paper.
One of the canonical surveys of the LLM-agent field at the 2023–2024 inflection point.

## Content summary

Surveys the LLM-based autonomous-agent literature across its constituent components and applications.
Organizes agent architectures around four modules: (1) *profile* — the agent's role, capabilities, and persona, typically expressed as a prompt prefix; (2) *memory* — short-term context (the LLM's conversation buffer / scratchpad) and long-term memory (vector-database retrieval, summarization-based memory, structured external stores); (3) *planning* — task decomposition (single-pass, iterative, tree-of-thoughts, plan-and-execute), with or without human / model feedback; (4) *action* — tool-use (function calling, API invocation, code execution), embodied action in simulation or robotics, communication with other agents.
Surveys application domains (software engineering, scientific research, gaming, social simulation, recommendation, autonomous driving) and evaluation methodologies.
The survey's load-bearing claim is that the field has converged on a roughly common four-component architecture (profile / memory / planning / action) across otherwise-divergent systems.
