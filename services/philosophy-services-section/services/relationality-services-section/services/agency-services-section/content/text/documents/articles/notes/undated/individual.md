The **individual** is the self at the close of the descent: one bounded someone, glued out of the shared field and persisting under law.
It is the agency-face of [governing](governing.md) — the settled self that the settled order recognizes.
In plain terms, a self that has come to rest as a distinct, bounded existent.

## The individual is an outcome, not a starting point

Gilbert Simondon's *L'individuation* makes the decisive correction to substance metaphysics: the individual is not where one begins but where a process arrives. *Individuation* is the process by which a distinct entity emerges from a field of potential; the individual "is not a starting point but an outcome."
Substance metaphysics takes the bounded individual as given and asks how it relates to others; this theory earns the individual *last*, as the fixed point at which becoming, acting, working, and governing have finally settled a someone into one definite, persisting self.

## The fractal hinge — the individual re-seeds

The individual has a double aspect, and it is the structurally pivotal point of the whole theory: **the individual is settled below and open above.** Below, it is the fixed point of the descent — a bounded self, nothing left unsettled.
Above, it is the *seed of a new descent*, this time at the **social** scale.

Simondon supplies the mechanism.
The individuated being is never exhausted by its individuation; it carries a charge of pre-individual potential — its *associated milieu* — that can drive further individuation.
And collectives, for Simondon, are not aggregates of pre-formed individuals but *fields of potential from which individuals and groups continuously emerge*.
So the settled individual, full of residual potential, is exactly the kind of thing that can begin again — re-entering as the seed of its own four-fold descent, now among other selves:

- **becoming** — the individual re-posed as a someone at the social scale;
- **doing** — [relating](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/becoming/doing/library/relating/docs/relating.md): the act one self directs toward another;
- **working** — community: the shared field *among* individuals;
- **governing** — the institution or collective: the social order, itself re-enterable at a higher scale (federation).

Distinct from that social descent is the individual's [world](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/library/world/docs/world.md) — not a rung of the descent but the ambient totality the individual emerges from and reproduces, the setting its descent runs in.
And the play of a settled self — its [dancing](dancing.md), music, and games — is the same re-entry read as the forms a someone takes up when it begins again.
