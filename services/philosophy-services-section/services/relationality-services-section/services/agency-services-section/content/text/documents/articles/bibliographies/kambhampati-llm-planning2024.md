# Kambhampati et al. 2024 — LLMs can't plan

Kambhampati, S., Valmeekam, K., Guan, L., Verma, M., Stechly, K.,
Bhambri, S., Saldyt, L., & Murthy, A. (2024). Position: LLMs
Can't Plan, But Can Help Planning in LLM-Modulo Frameworks.
*ICML 2024*.

Position paper arguing that LLMs do not internally perform
planning or maintain coherent world models in the technical
sense. Documents systematic failure of frontier LLMs on
PlanBench (a classical-planning benchmark with small instances).
Proposes the LLM-Modulo framework: use LLMs as candidate
generators and natural-language interfaces, with classical
verifiers and planners providing the soundness. The dominant
articulation of the "LLMs are not world models" position.
