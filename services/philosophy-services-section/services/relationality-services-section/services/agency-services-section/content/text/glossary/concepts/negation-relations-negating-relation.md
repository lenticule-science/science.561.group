# Negating Relation

*Story.*  The same act that affirms relation simultaneously exposes its denial.  Directing Reflex at Relate’s refusal earns NotRelate.


NotRelate = (Reflex Relate)


*Proof Sketch.*  Reflex applied to Relate yields the complement “what resists relation.”  Since Relate has already been earned, this expression is legitimate, and the resulting recognition precisely captures “not relating.”
