Open work for critical theory at the school level.
The strand-specific questions live in each strand's own subdomain (the per-thinker subdomains and the topic subdomains under `theories/`).
What is held here is the work whose scope crosses strands.

## Open structural questions

- **What is the relation between the Frankfurt-school analysis of culture-and-consciousness and the Foucauldian analysis of power/knowledge?** The two were posed against each other (Foucault explicitly distanced himself from the Frankfurt-school inheritance; Habermas explicitly criticized Foucault).
  The contemporary critical-theoretical question is whether the two are reconcilable or whether the choice between them remains substantive.
- **How does the contemporary affect-theoretic strand (Berlant, Ahmed, Massumi) relate to the older critique-of-ideology tradition?** Affect theory was developed in part as a refusal of the cognition-and-belief framing that ideology critique relies on.
  The structural question is whether ideology critique can absorb the affect-theoretic insights without abandoning its own commitments.
- **Where does the Black-radical tradition sit relative to "critical theory" as a label?** Robinson's *Black Marxism* explicitly argues that the Black radical tradition precedes and exceeds Marxism (and, by implication, Frankfurt-school critical theory).
  The structural question for this subdomain is whether to read Black-radical analysis as a strand of critical theory, as a parallel tradition that critical theory must reckon with, or as something else again.
- **What does Povinelli's analysis of late liberalism mean for the older Frankfurt-school analyses of administered society?** Povinelli's work on the management of disturbance through the production of quasi-events looks structurally like an extension of the Frankfurt-school analysis of administered society into the late-liberal phase.
  The structural question is whether the extension is continuous or whether it changes the analysis.

## Where these questions are worked

Each of the questions above is pursued partially in the subdomains that touch on it (the per-thinker lineages, the affect-theory school, the black-radical-tradition lineage, the povinellian lineage).
The discipline-level home for the questions, when the work crosses subdomains, is here.
