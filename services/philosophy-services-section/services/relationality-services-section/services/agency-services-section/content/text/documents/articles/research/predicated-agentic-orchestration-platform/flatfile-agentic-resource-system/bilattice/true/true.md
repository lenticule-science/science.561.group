The element $\mathbf{t} \in \mathbf{FOUR}$.
In the twist-product representation, $\mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$: positive evidence present, negative evidence absent.

Truth-order position: maximum.
Every bilattice-value truth-precedes $\mathbf{t}$.

Knowledge-order position: strictly between mystery and contradiction.
True and false are incomparable under knowledge-preceding — they carry the same amount of information, pointing in opposite directions.

In the FARS encoding, a frontmatter entry `q: e₂` in file `e₁` asserts the predication-cell $(e_1, q, e_2)$ at $\mathbf{t}$.
True is the only bilattice-value directly expressible by a frontmatter assertion.
