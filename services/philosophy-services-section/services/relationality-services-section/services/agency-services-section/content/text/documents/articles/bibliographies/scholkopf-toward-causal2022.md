# Schölkopf & von Kügelgen, "From Statistical to Causal Learning" (2022)

Survey of the integration of causality and machine
learning.

## What it covers

- Survey of causal inference and ML connections.
- Independent causal mechanisms principle.
- Identifiability results across causal-discovery
  and causal-representation-learning.
- Practical methods: invariant prediction, IRM,
  causal disentanglement, multi-environment
  learning.
- Applications: robustness, fairness, transfer,
  agent design.
- Open problems and research directions.

## Why it matters

Up-to-date (as of 2022) survey of the field. Connects
classical causality with contemporary ML practice.
Useful for orientation in fast-moving area.

## How to read

Survey/position paper; readable by ML researchers
without deep causality background.
