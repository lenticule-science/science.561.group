## [[zeds/old-notes/Delta Register/emsenn]] to [[Filler PGH]], [[2024-01-11]]
Hau kola (hello accomplices)  
  
My name is emsenn, I'm a Lakota insurrectionary anarcho-nihliist who focuses on doing regenerative horticulture. I've been asked to bring my knowledge and experience as an anarchist to my people's traditional territory, to enrich their practices of autonomy. Anarchist texts were foundational in me developing these skills myself, and so bringing those texts to my people is something I want to do.  
  
My family be migrating from North Carolina in mid-March, and are organizing a donation drive to bring as much gift to my people as we can. I'm writing to ask if y'all would be able to send us any good books and zines. We have the facilities to set up a lending library when we get there.  
  
Additionally, we'd be interested in participating in your subscription program, to keep this library updated.

## Filler to emsenn, [[2024-03-07]]

Hey emsenn! 

This is bent (not broken) from the filler crew. We’re currently reorganizing after a winter hiatus, and I’m just now checking the emails to see what we’ve missed - thanks for your patience in waiting for our reply.

The lending library sounds sick! I feel that we may come to find that our projects share a great deal of affinity, and so I’d love to bottom-line the logistics of sending free zines and other resources. 

Right now, our project is in the process of *greatly* expanding our capacity for free or at-cost printing and distribution. After we’re done moving our infrastructure into a new cooperative warehouse, I’ll send a catalog and some free filler your way. 

I’m not sure exactly how exactly long it will take (up with punk time! down with standard time!), but sometime in the near future we will be able to share hundreds of free zines with yinz. Speaking as an individual within the informal cluster of distro projects called “Filler,” my personal goal is to have our new printing infrastructure up and running before the spring.  

I function at a slow pace with all correspondence, and I am here for the long-haul. Feel free to hit me up at this collectively-accessible email, on my personal signal (@get_bent.412), or via pen and paper.

Solidarity n’at,

- bent, not broken
