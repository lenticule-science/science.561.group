{ .block-language-dataview}


- Expository essays are for informing people about a concept or topic.
- Descriptive essays are for describing a [thing](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/thing.md), often focusing on one [quality](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/quality.md) or set of qualities.
- Narrative essays are for narrative storytelling.
- [Argumentative essays](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/essay/_archive/argumentative-essay.md) are for arguing a truth about a [thing](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/thing.md).
- Analytical essays are for [relating](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/index.md) a concept or topic to [events](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/event.md) or [information](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/science/theories/information/_index.md).
- Comparative essays are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [things](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/thing.md).
- Critical essays apply a concept or synthesis of concepts to events or information.
- Reflective essays explore my relationship to a [thing](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/philosophy/terms/thing.md).

Check the Essay Catalog if you want to see all the essays that are a part of [my virtual mycelium](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/writing/content/essay/_archive/emsenn's-webplace.md).
