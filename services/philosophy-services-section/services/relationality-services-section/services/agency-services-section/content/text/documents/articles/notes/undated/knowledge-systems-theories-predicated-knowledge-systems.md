The named theory of how this repository itself works as a knowledge system: a system whose unit of storage is the [predication-cell](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predication-cell.md), whose value-domain is the four-element [bilattice](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice.md) $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$, whose internal structure is a [predication-fibration](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md) over a [predicate category](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) of named relations, and whose dynamics are the [closure-driven settling](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) of a [closure-rule system](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-system.md).

The subtheory synthesizes three upstream literatures — knowledge organization (Ranganathan, Hjorland, Needham/Feibleman/Foskett, Dahlberg, Wille/Ganter, BCG/CRG), knowledge representation (RDF/OWL, description logics, Topic Maps, Sowa's conceptual graphs, Datalog/Prolog, FCA, property graphs), and predication logic (Frege → Lambek → Lawvere → Belnap → Fitting → Busaniche-Cignoli → Jakl → Maruyama) — into a single set of structural commitments that any knowledge system inherits when it adopts the predication-cell as its substrate.

The companion subtheory [`formal-concept-analysis/`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/formal-concept-analysis) builds out the FCA lineage at full subtheory depth; this subtheory cross-links into it where the synthesis touches Galois-closure constructions.
