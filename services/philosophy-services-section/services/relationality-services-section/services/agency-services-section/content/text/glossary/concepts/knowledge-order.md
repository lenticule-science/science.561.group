# Knowledge order

The order, denoted $\leq_k$, associated with the second lattice $\langle B, \wedge_k, \vee_k \rangle$ of a [pre-bilattice](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/pre-bilattice.md).
When the lattice is bounded, the knowledge order has bottom $\bot$ and top $\top$.

In a [twist product](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md) $\mathbf{L}_1 \bowtie \mathbf{L}_2$, the knowledge order is $\langle a_1, a_2 \rangle \leq_k \langle b_1, b_2 \rangle$ iff $a_1 \leq_1 b_1$ in $\mathbf{L}_1$ and $a_2 \leq_2 b_2$ in $\mathbf{L}_2$.
The knowledge order raises both positive and negative [evidence](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/learning/_archive/from-agent-acting-learning/_archive/evidence.md).

On a [bilattice](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/learning/_archive/from-agent-acting-learning/objects/bilattice.md), [negation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/learning/_archive/from-agent-acting-learning/_archive/negation.md) is monotone in the knowledge order: $a \leq_k b \Rightarrow \sim a \leq_k \sim b$.

The [settling monotonicity](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/learning/_archive/from-agent-acting-learning/_archive/settling-monotonicity.md) condition asserts that knowledge values accumulate monotonically in $\leq_k$, growing as evidence is added and never retracted.
