## Theorem: directed relational research

In a modal research setting `(E, C)` with `C = Box Diamond`, research is a
coKleisli dynamics whose certified cells form active coalgebraic witnesses; the
Yoneda/sheaf completion of those witnesses is the research witness topos; and
strict certified advance can induce a researching morphism of the host theory.

## Proof

1. [Modal research setting](modal-research-setting.md)
   gives the closure `C` needed to hold dynamics and stable topology together.

2. [Directed research cell](directed-research-cell.md)
   types a research act as `r : X -> C Y`, so inquiry is a directed typed arrow
   from situated input to claim under closure.

3. [CoKleisli research category](cokleisli-research-category.md)
   proves these arrows compose as research arrows, so multi-step inquiry is a
   category rather than a loose sequence.

4. [Certified research cell](certified-research-cell.md)
   proves certification is idempotent stabilization, equivalently factorization
   through a `C`-coalgebra.

5. [Research residual](research-residual.md) shows the
   remaining proof obligation of a cell is exactly the residual `C r - r`; when
   it vanishes, the cell is certified.

6. [Research invariant](research-invariant.md) shows
   modal symmetries preserve readouts through certified composition, giving the
   conserved quantities of a research program.

7. [Research witness topos](research-witness-topos.md)
   applies Yoneda and sheafification to the coKleisli research category, making
   the stable research field generated by the cells themselves.

8. A certified field that strictly advances settled predicates for some resource
   gives the conditions for a
   [researching morphism](researching-morphism.md)
   `rho : T -> T'`: faithful preservation of old generators, monotone knowledge
   order on shared predicates, and strict advance for at least one resource.

Therefore DRR is not a metaphorical research style. It is a proof path from
directed relation to certified claim-settlement to theory extension.

## Corollary: representation agnosticism

prose papers, and local paths are projections of the same research cells when
they preserve the typed arrow `X -> C Y`, the certification condition
`C r ~= r`, and the compositional structure of `Research(C)`.
