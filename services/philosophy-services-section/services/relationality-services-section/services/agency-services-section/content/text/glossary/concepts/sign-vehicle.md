# Sign-vehicle

The *position in a sign-relation occupied by what does the standing-for* — the perceptible thing (mark, sound, gesture, configuration) that, under a code, stands for an object for an interpretant.

Peirce's *representamen* (per the [representamen](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/representamen.md) term) is the canonical name for this position; Saussure's *signifier* (per the [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) term) is the same position viewed against the langue. The discipline's preferred name is *sign-vehicle* because it is neutral between Peircean and Saussurean readings and emphasises that the position is a *role* rather than a substance: the same physical mark can be a sign-vehicle in one [code](code.md) and not in another.

A sign-vehicle is *not* the sign itself — the sign is the whole [sign-relation](sign-relation.md). Common usage often elides the distinction ("the word *cat* is a sign of the animal"); the discipline keeps the distinction because the sign-relation's structural content lives in the relating, not in any of the three positions.

The substrate-level reading places the sign-vehicle as a *predicate* in a predication-cell. The cell $(s, p, o)$ has $p$ in the sign-vehicle role: $p$ is what does the relating; $s$ and $o$ are what the relating is about; the cell's placement in a [discourse-community fiber](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/discourse-community-as-fiber.md) is the [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md). This is the structural bridge the [composability narrative](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/semiotics/_archive/from-discourse-domain-locus-philosophy-semiotics/sign-vehicle/teaching/explanations/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md) commits to.
