A structure entity declares one [entity](entity.md)-form the substrate commits to: a named type, its role in the agentic algebra, and its [predication-cell](predication-cell.md) commitments.
In the [predication-fibration](predication-fibration.md) $(\mathbf{FOUR}, \mathcal{C}, E)$, every member of $E$ inhabits a kind-classifier directory; entities whose kind-classifier is `structures/` are structure entities.

The declaration is authoritative: if `structures/foo.md` exists in a [filestore](filestore.md), then the theory commits to the entity-form "foo" — that instances of foo can exist in that filestore's $E$ and carry the predicates the structure entity names.

Per AgOP's agential-self-generation invariant, every operative element of the substrate must be a substrate entity.
Structure entities are the operative declarations of entity-forms.
Each one is itself an entity in $E$, subject to the same predication-fibration algebra as everything else in the filestore.

Self-application: this file, `structures/structure.md`, is a structure entity declaring the entity-form "structure."
The kind that classifies structure entities is the structure entity itself.
