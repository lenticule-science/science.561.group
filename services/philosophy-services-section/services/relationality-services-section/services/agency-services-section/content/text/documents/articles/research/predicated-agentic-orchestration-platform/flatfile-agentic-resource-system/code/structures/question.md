A question entity records one open inquiry the theory has not yet settled.
In the [predication-fibration](predication-fibration.md) $(\mathbf{FOUR}, \mathcal{C}, E)$, questions are members of $E$ whose kind-classifier is `questions/`.

A question differs from a [claim](claim.md) in bilattice value: a claim is settled-true ($\mathbf{t}$); a question is explicitly at mystery ($\mathbf{m}$) — the substrate acknowledges the inquiry but has no settled answer.
Filing a question is the substrate's act of making the gap explicit, accountable, and subject to dispatch.

Questions are operative: closure rules may match on open questions to trigger research work; auditing processes flag unanswered questions as substrate debt; making processes may scaffold a claim entity as the intended resolution of a question.
The substrate uses question entities to track its own known unknowns rather than leaving them implicit in prose.

Per AgOP's agential-self-generation invariant, the gaps in the substrate's knowledge are themselves substrate entities.
A question entity is the operative record that the substrate has acknowledged this gap — it is not external to the theory, it is the theory's honest accounting of what it does not yet know.
