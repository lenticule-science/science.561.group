## Content summary

Develops ConGolog: a programming language for agents whose primitive is the situation-calculus action and whose structuring constructs (sequence, conditional, while-loop, non-deterministic choice, concurrency, prioritized concurrency, interrupt) form a complete imperative-like language with a denotational semantics in the situation calculus.
A ConGolog program is, formally, a situation-calculus formula whose models are the program's possible execution traces; program execution is theorem proving over the underlying situation-calculus action theory ([Reiter 2001](../../references/reiter2001.md)).
ConGolog and its extensions (IndiGolog for incremental on-line execution, sIndiGolog for sensing) provide a programming-language register for situation-calculus agent design, in which the agent's program is a logical specification rather than imperative code.
The result: agent programs whose execution traces are first-order logical terms, whose pre/postconditions are situation-calculus formulas, and whose reasoning is decidable for relevant fragments of the action theory.
