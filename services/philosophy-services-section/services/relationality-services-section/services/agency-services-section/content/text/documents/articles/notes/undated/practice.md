# practice

The actual doing of a patterned activity — action carried out according to a method, always concrete and situated (Bourdieu; Dewey; Foucault).
Generated by: **structure** (a structure realized in the doing).
Imports **action** from the agency module: a practice is where agency meets structure — the point at which the action⇄structure circle is cut, rather than directed.
Generates: **institution** (when practices crystallize into a persisting cluster).
