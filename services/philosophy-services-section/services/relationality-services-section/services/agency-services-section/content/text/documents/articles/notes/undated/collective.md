# collective

A plural social-being — many bound into one that can act and be addressed as a unit (a community, a people, an organization).
Generated by: **membership** (bundling — the members' belonging constitutes the collective).
A collective is itself a *social-being*: it holds relations, can be harmed, bears a name.
