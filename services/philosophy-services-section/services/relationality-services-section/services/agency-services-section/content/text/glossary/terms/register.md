The register is GnuCash's primary interface for viewing and entering transactions within a single account.
It resembles a checkbook register — each row is a transaction showing date, description, transfer account, and amount.
The register displays the running balance for the account.

Under the hood, each entry in the register is one side of a [double-entry](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/services/accounting-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/double-entry-bookkeeping.md) transaction; the "transfer account" column shows where the other side posts.
Users can switch between "basic ledger" (one line per transaction), "auto-split ledger" (expands the selected transaction to show all [splits](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/endeavoring/content/endeavor/organizing/content/organization/resourcing/content/resource/recording/content/record/accounting/content/accounting-software/content/gnucash/content/register/split-transaction.md)), and "transaction journal" (shows all splits for every transaction) views.
