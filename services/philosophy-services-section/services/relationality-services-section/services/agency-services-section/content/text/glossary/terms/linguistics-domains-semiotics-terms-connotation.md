Connotation is second-order signification: the process by which a denotative sign becomes the signifier for a further, culturally determined signified — the mechanism through which ideology operates in sign systems.

Connotation is second-order signification: the process by which a [denotative](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/denotation.md) [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) becomes the [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) for a further, culturally determined [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md).

The concept was formalized by [Louis Hjelmslev](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/louis-hjelmslev.md) and made central to cultural critique by [Roland Barthes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/roland-barthes.md). In Hjelmslev&rsquo;s framework, a connotative semiotic is one whose expression plane is itself a semiotic system — that is, a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) system built on top of another [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) system. The first-order [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) ([denotation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/denotation.md)) provides the material for a second-order sign whose [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) is cultural, ideological, or affective.

[Barthes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/roland-barthes.md) used this framework to analyze how culture naturalizes its values. In *Mythologies* (1957), he showed that myth is a connotative system: it takes a first-order [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) (a photograph, a news report, a sporting event) and drains it of its particular, historical content to fill it with a new, naturalized meaning. The Black soldier saluting the French flag does not simply denote that scene — at the connotative level, it signifies &ldquo;French imperialism is just and universal.&rdquo; The operation of myth is to transform history into nature, contingency into necessity [@barthes_Mythologies_1957].

## The mechanism

The connotative chain works as follows:

- **First order ([denotation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/denotation.md))**: a [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) (a sound, image, or mark) is linked to a [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) (a concept) to produce a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md).

- **Second order (connotation)**: that first-order [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) now functions as a [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) in a new system, linked to a new [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) — a cultural value, an ideological position, an emotional register.

This layering can continue: a connotative [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) can itself become the [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) for a third-order meaning, and so on. The effect is that meaning accumulates in layers, each layer appearing more &ldquo;natural&rdquo; than the last because it is built on the apparent transparency of the lower levels [@barthes_ElementsSemiology_1964].
