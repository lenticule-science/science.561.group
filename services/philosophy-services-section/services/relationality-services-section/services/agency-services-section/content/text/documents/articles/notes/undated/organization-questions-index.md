The discipline-level open work for the `organization` subdomain.
Questions whose answers, once worked out, would bring the subdomain closer to theory status under [theory-discipline.md](../../specifications/theory-discipline.md).
Subdomain-internal substantive questions (about the subject the subdomain studies) live in the subdomain's bodies and texts; what is held here is the structural / curation work.

## Standing structural questions

- **What does this subdomain commit to substantively?** An earlier curation pass classified most sociology subdomains as staging-areas — collections of analytical resources that have not yet stabilized into a single research program with shared method and shared object.
  The first-order question for `organization` is whether the existing material can stabilize into such a program, or whether what currently lives here is better understood as a regional collection routed through the school whose name it carries.
- **What is the relation between this subdomain's vocabulary (`terms/`) and the discipline's broader concept layer (`sociology/concepts/`)?** Where the same analytical role is occupied by a term here and a concept at the discipline level, the relation should be made explicit (via `realizes:` or `see-also:` edges).
- **Which writers and works should the subdomain cite, and which of those need notes in `sociology/references/` rather than ad-hoc inline mentions?** A future pass should harvest the ad-hoc figure-mentions in this subdomain and produce reference notes for the load-bearing ones.
- **What relations to neighboring subdomains are structural rather than incidental?** A future curation pass should make the subdomain's `see-also:` edges to its sibling subdomains explicit and bidirectional.

## Where these questions are worked

These questions are worked here, in pull-requests against this subdomain's `_index.md`, and in the subdomain's own `_index.md`.
