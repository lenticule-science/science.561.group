# knowledge presheaf merge is a semilattice operation

The pointwise [knowledge-order](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf/content/distributed-settling-system/concepts/knowledge-order.md) join $\vee_k$ on [spatiotemporal knowledge presheaves](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf.md) — the merge operation of a [distributed settling system](distributed-settling-system.md) — is commutative, associative, and idempotent.

## Proof

Pointwise lift of the knowledge-order join being a semilattice operation on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.
Commutativity, associativity, and idempotence of $\vee_k$ on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ pass to pointwise application across the entity-pair index $E \times E$, predicates, and settling steps. $\square$
