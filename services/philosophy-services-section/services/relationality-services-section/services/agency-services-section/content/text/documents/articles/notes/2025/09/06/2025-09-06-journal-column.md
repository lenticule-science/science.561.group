A ***journal column*** is a regularly published piece in a [newspaper](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/newspaper.md), [magazine](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/magazine.md), or such. Usually [opinion](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opinion.md) and written in an [affective writing style](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/affective-writing-style.md).
