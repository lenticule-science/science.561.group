Learning goal: explain how [geontologies](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/geontologies/terms/geontologies.md) and [geontopower](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/geontologies/terms/geontopower.md) operate through three figures and how they extend [biopolitics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/sociology/terms/biopolitics.md).

[Povinelli](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/general/people/elizabeth-povinelli.md) argues that [late liberal](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/geontologies/terms/late-liberalism.md) governance manages the Life/Nonlife boundary through three figures:

- **The Desert**: Nonlife threatening to overwhelm Life.
  This is the figure of ecological catastrophe, resource exhaustion, and extinction — the fear that the inert world will reclaim the living one.
  Climate [discourse](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/discourse.md) often operates through this figure.
- **The Animist**: the figure that refuses the Life/Nonlife distinction altogether.
  Indigenous ontologies that recognize rivers, rocks, and ancestors as alive and agentive represent the Animist — not as a "belief" to be tolerated but as an ontological challenge to the framework that makes [extractivism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/services/ecology-services-section/services/climate-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/extractivism.md) possible.
- **The Virus**: the figure that crosses the boundary, neither fully alive nor fully inert.
  The Virus disrupts classification from within — it is the uncanny entity that the Life/Nonlife distinction cannot accommodate.

[Geontopower](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/biopower/content/geontopower/content/geontologies/terms/geontopower.md) is the form of power that manages these threats to the boundary.
It operates not through dramatic acts of sovereignty but through the quiet maintenance of [ontological](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/philosophy/terms/ontology.md) categories — the classifications embedded in [property](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/legitimating/content/authority/monopolizing/content/the-state/governing/content/sociology/terms/property.md) law, environmental regulation, and scientific taxonomy.
