# Predicated Work

A theory of [work](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/work/_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories) mathematics: operational work as a [predication settling](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) over a work-themed predicate category.
Tasks, plans, workflows, capabilities, dispatch, completion, recurrence — the work vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this substrate.

Sibling to [governance](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/work/theories/governance) (also a theory of work).
Predicated-work is the bilattice-substrate-native theory; the existing work texts ([formal-structure-of-office](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/documents/notes/2026-04-03-note-on-formal-structure-of-office.md), [formal-structure-of-delegation](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/documents/notes/2026-04-03-note-on-formal-structure-of-delegation.md), [algebraic-structure-of-work](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/algebraic-structure-of-work.md)) provide parallel formalizations.

## What this program is for

Tasks, plans, workflows, recurrence, completion are the *operational* layer of work — what gets done, when, by whom.
The predicated-governance subtheory studies the *normative* layer — who has the standing to do what, what authority configures dispatch, what counts as charter-compliant.
Both layers live on the same operations-settling substrate; the two together cover the work vocabulary's content.

The math program's machinery — bilattice-valued predication, settling dynamics under closure, four-regime classification, cohomology and moduli, d-frame-valued sheaves — is the substrate the program runs on.
Predicated-work does not re-derive the math; it instantiates it.
