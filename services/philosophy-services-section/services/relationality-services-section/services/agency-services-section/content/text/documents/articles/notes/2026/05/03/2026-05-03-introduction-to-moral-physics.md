Moral physics is the investigation of how moral beliefs are structured, how they interact, and how they produce effects — approached not as philosophy (the normative question of which beliefs are true) but as sociology and history (the positive question of how beliefs actually function).
The term *physics* is deliberate: it names the aspiration to describe the lawlike regularities in moral-belief systems without presupposing the normative validity of any particular system.

Predicated sociology uses moral-physics methods to analyze how [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md) operates through moral belief — specifically how [moral displacement](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/moral-displacement.md) works, how ideologies can be measured by their statistical prerequisites, and how the interaction of moral beliefs with material conditions produces the subject-formations the apparatus requires.

## What moral physics is

Moral physics rests on three methodological commitments.

**The separation of descriptive from normative analysis.** Moral physics describes how moral beliefs function without committing to the normative validity of any belief.
This is not a claim that normative validity is unimportant; it is a disciplinary commitment that the descriptive and normative questions are separate and must be answered separately.

**The treatment of moral beliefs as having structure.** Beliefs are not isolated propositions.
They form systems with internal consistency requirements, entailment relations, and incompatibility constraints.
Understanding how a particular belief functions requires understanding its position in the belief system.

**The treatment of moral belief systems as having material correlates.** Ideologies do not float free of social conditions.
They have institutional bases, distributional effects, and structural prerequisites.
Analyzing an ideology requires identifying what social conditions its operation presupposes.

## Moral displacement

[Moral displacement](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/moral-displacement.md) is moral physics' most analytically important contribution to predicated sociology.
The concept names the operation by which accountability for structural conditions is shifted from the structural to the personal: instead of the structural cause being held responsible, the individual acting within the structure is held responsible.

The shift has several forms:

**Individualization.** Structural causes (industrial pollution) are reformatted as individual choices (consumer behavior).
The accountability that would fall on the institution is displaced onto the individual.

**Temporalization.** The historical cause is displaced by the contemporary agent.
"The problem is not historical settler-colonial policy but current individual racism" is a temporalization displacement; it is accurate in identifying individual racism as a problem but performs the displacement of accountability away from the structural conditions individual racism operates within.

**Modality-shift.** The actual cause (structural violence, legal coercion, economic compulsion) is reformatted as moral failure (lack of virtue, character flaw, improper belief).
The accountability that would fall on the structure that compels action is displaced onto the agent's character.

Moral displacement is not always ideologically motivated.
It can be an honest cognitive error — the tendency to see agents rather than structures as causes, which cognitive research (Kahneman, 2011) has documented as a widely distributed bias.
But under the conditions of [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md), the cognitive error is *systematically amplified* by the apparatus's affective formatting.
The apparatus provides genres that route structural accountability through personal cultivation; moral displacement is the moral-cognitive mechanism through which this routing operates.

## Regime analysis

Moral-physics analysis identifies *regimes* — stable configurations of moral belief and institutional form that co-evolve across historical periods.
A regime is a moral-belief system that has achieved sufficient institutional embedding to reproduce itself across generations.

Several regimes are analytically relevant to predicated sociology:

**The liberal-individualist regime.** Moral individualism — the belief that individual agents are the appropriate unit of moral accountability — is the bedrock belief of the liberal moral system.
It is institutionally embedded in law (individual legal personality), economics (rational-choice theory), and psychology (cognitive-behavioral therapy).
Its statistical prerequisites include a certain level of material prosperity (moral individualism is more coherent when individuals have genuine agency) and a certain level of institutional reliability (moral individualism is more coherent when institutions are predictable enough for individuals to plan around).

**The pastoral-progressive regime.** The moral regime of progressive political culture — whose beliefs include collective accountability for structural injustice, the priority of the most-harmed perspective, and the importance of continuous consciousness-raising — has its own institutional embedding and statistical prerequisites.
Its prerequisites include sufficient institutional infrastructure to support professional advocacy, sufficient surplus to sustain people in analysis-and-advocacy roles without direct production, and sufficient platform infrastructure to distribute consciousness-raising at scale.

**The wellness regime.** The moral regime of contemporary wellness culture — whose beliefs include the priority of personal health, the body as moral barometer, the importance of practices that maintain optimal function — is institutionally embedded in the wellness industry, the therapeutic profession, and the platform's self-help content economy.
Its prerequisite is sufficient surplus to fund the consumption of wellness products and sufficient institutional failure to make personal wellness the available response to structural conditions.

## The measurement of ideology

Measuring ideologies by their statistical prerequisites is the methodological core of moral physics.
An ideology's *statistical prerequisites* are the social conditions that must obtain for the ideology's operation to be coherent.
If the conditions are absent, the ideology cannot function as it claims to function; it functions differently, or it fails.

The liberal-individualist ideology presupposes that individuals have genuine agency — genuine capacity to make choices that determine their outcomes.
This presupposition requires certain statistical conditions: a distribution of genuine options that differs across individuals' choices, a threshold of institutional reliability that makes individual planning possible, a level of material sufficiency above which basic needs are not the primary constraint on choice.
Where these conditions are absent, the liberal-individualist ideology's operation changes character: it no longer describes a system in which individual choices produce individual outcomes, but rather a system in which the ideology of individual choice provides the moral vocabulary that displaces accountability for collective conditions.

This is what moral physics contributes to predicated sociology's analysis: the precise specification of when an ideology is functioning as described and when it is functioning as [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md).
When the statistical prerequisites are met, the ideology may be describing something real.
When they are absent, the ideology is metabolizing the gap between its description and reality through the subjects who inhabit it.
[Structural determination](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/structural-determination.md) — the degree to which a subject's outcomes are determined by their structural position rather than their choices — is the key measurement.
As structural determination rises above what the ideology's presuppositions can accommodate, the ideology's operation shifts from description to [californication](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/content/relating/content/relational-entity/content/social-being/content/social-relation/content/power/subjectifying/content/subject/content/possessive-individual/ontologizing/content/liberalism/analyzing/content/cybernetic-postliberalism/teaching/content/teaching/explanations/californication.md).
