## Interpretation

Completeness states that semantic entailment implies provability.  *Recognize Syntax* witnessing *Entail Semantics* yields ***Complete Syntax*** ([CompleteSyntax](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/complete-syntax.md)).

Composing these recognitions records the canonical-model argument: if every model satisfies the entailment, the syntactic recognition witnesses a proof, so *Complete Syntax* holds.
