# Meeting Recognition

*Story.*  Ordering tells us when one recognition subsumes another, but we also need their greatest shared part.  Forcing OrderRecognition to witness Entry yields MeetRecognition.


MeetRecognition = (OrderRecognition Entry)


*Proof Sketch.*  Entry records paired recognitions; composing it with the order captures their joint lower bound, so MeetRecognition is earned.
