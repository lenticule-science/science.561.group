Concrete notes that exemplify the discipline's commitments. The best examples are the corpus's own gold-standard theories — [predication-categories](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) and [predicated-computing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/README.md) — read as instances of this discipline rather than as mathematical or engineering content.

When a contributor wants to know what a well-formed `concept`, `object`, `morphism`, `theorem`, `question`, or `narrative` looks like in practice, the canonical move is to read the relevant entry in those theories' typed sub-directories. The discipline is meant to be learned by example as much as by spec.

This directory exists to hold examples that *exhibit* a discipline-level pattern (e.g., a particularly clean theory-promotion case-study, a worked term-to-typed migration, a relation-graph topology that exposes a subtle validation issue) when none of the corpus's existing typed notes already does so. It is empty pending such a need.
