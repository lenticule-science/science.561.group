# Crew Dispatch

Match work to officers' capabilities, authority bounds, and capacity at every dispatch opportunity.
Dispatch is the bridge between planning and execution.

## Discharge

Dispatch act creates `assigned-to` relation; task moves to ready state; officer receives notice.
