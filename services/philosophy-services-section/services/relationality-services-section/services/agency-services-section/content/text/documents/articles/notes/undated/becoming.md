Becoming is the first and most open moment of existence: that anything comes to be at all.
Before a thing is *this* thing — bounded, named, possessed of properties — there is the bare event of its arising, the coming-into-being that every existent presupposes and none escapes.

## The primacy of process

Most of Western metaphysics begins from *being*: from the thing already there, the substance that persists, with change treated as something that happens to it.
Process philosophy reverses the order.
Heraclitus held that everything flows — one cannot step into the same river twice.
Bergson made duration (*durée*) primary: the real is a continuous becoming, and the intellect only freezes it into discrete things after the fact.
Whitehead built the reversal into a system: the basic unit of reality is not an enduring substance but an **actual occasion**, a momentary event of coming-to-be he calls *concrescence* — "the many become one, and are increased by one" (*Process and Reality*).
Deleuze pushes furthest, taking becoming as prior to and productive of every fixed identity.

Becoming is that priority made the first term of the theory: existence read from the side of the event rather than the thing.
It is the most open moment because nothing about the existent is yet settled — not its bounds, not its qualities, not which someone it is.
There is only that there is an arising.

## What it generates

What becoming yields is **[being](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/existence/README.md)** — the existent, the someone that has come to be.
Being is not set against becoming here; it is its issue, what the event leaves standing.
Everything that follows in the theory — the act ([doing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/doing/docs/doing.md)), shared work ([working](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/doing/working/docs/working.md)), settled order ([governing](governing.md)) — is a further determination of what becoming first lets be.

## The animate face

Read as agency, becoming wears the face of the **animate**.
To come to be, here, is not to be deposited as an inert object to which agency is later added — it is already to be a someone, however indeterminate.
This is what animist thought holds onto: personhood is not the rare property of a special class but the default condition of whatever is.
A.I. Hallowell, working with Ojibwe ontology, found a world organized around *other-than-human persons*; Graham Harvey generalizes it — animists "recognize that the world is full of persons, only some of whom are human."
At the moment of becoming there is a someone at all, and nothing yet narrows which: the bare animacy out of which every more specific agency — the actor, the worker, the citizen — is afterward drawn.
