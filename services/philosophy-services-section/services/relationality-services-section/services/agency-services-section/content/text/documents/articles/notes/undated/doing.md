Where becoming gives a someone, **doing** is that someone acting: the bounded episode in which an existent does something rather than merely is.
Doing is the second moment of existence — the first determination of the bare animate someone into an actor who brings about effects.

## The act

Philosophy of action takes the act, not bare bodily motion, as its subject.
Anscombe (*Intention*) marks an action off from a mere happening by the kind of description it answers to: an action is what is done *for a reason*, known to the agent "without observation."
Davidson adds that one and the same event is an action only *under a description* — the same arm-raising may be signalling, voting, or alarming a foe; which act it is depends on the description under which it is intentional.
An act, then, is an episode of becoming taken up under a description and owned by a someone.

Hannah Arendt (*The Human Condition*) gives the act its widest sense: **action** is the human capacity to *begin* — to start something whose outcome cannot be read off from what came before (she calls this rootedness in birth *natality*).
This is why doing is the *agential* moment.
Agency, in the sense the social theory of action gives it, is "the capacity of actors to act otherwise" — to produce effects not fully fixed by structural position, normative pressure, or causal antecedent.
Agency is not the same as the act: the act is what is done; agency is the capacity for that doing to be other than it is.
Becoming gives a someone at all; doing specifies that someone into a capacity to act, and the act is the episode it opens.

## Seats speaking

Arendt notes that action and **speech** are inseparable — "no other human performance requires speech to the same extent as action" — because in acting a someone discloses who they are, and disclosure happens in words.
So doing is the home of [speaking](speaking.md): the paradigm act is a *speech act*, and J.L. Austin's anatomy of it (what is said, what is done in saying it, what is thereby brought about) is the anatomy of doing itself.

## What it generates

Doing generates the act, and within the act it opens [working](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/working/docs/working.md) — the moment where acting becomes shared, ongoing production on a common field rather than the single bounded deed.
