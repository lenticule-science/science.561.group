# Source-grounded exemplars, derived practices, and the feminist problem of masks

**Originating Leader demand, 2026-10-19:** "Make money make money make money. I want us to have more characters, formal Characters, that we can use when doing certain work. ... point to real people, real sets of qualities (i.e. business sense) and then derive their qualities, and then project an arrangement of real people and selected qualities into a character ... 'let's make a character that has the business, comedy, and media sense of Seth MacFarlane' ... next person is Guy Fieri's business and media sense." This research preserves the demand in its own words as a source, not an authorization to borrow any person's identity, rights, or powers.

## 1. Different objects MUST remain different

A publicly identified **person** is a source-referent; a documented **act or practice** is an attributed observation; a **practice class** is an analytical concept; a **disposition or skill** is a revisable hypothesis inferred across observations; a **Character** is a selected arrangement of commitments and practices in an independent agent; a **mask/persona** is its situated public-facing or role-facing presentation; an **office** is a normative institutional position; an **incumbency** seats an actual actor in that position; an **organ** or model-provider capacity is an actual means of acting; and a **commercial outcome** is an independent receiving observation. Neither reference to a successful person nor an attractive representation is an incumbency, license, contract, skill admission, or successful sale.

Direction of explanation: identifiable sources -> exact source statements -> attributed observable actions -> constrained, revisable interpretations of transferable operations -> candidate practice selection -> authored Character/mask -> bounded work decision -> authorized act -> externally received outcome. Backwards promotion (observing revenue and asserting every inferred inner trait) is invalid: fame, distribution privilege, capital, teams, legal control, timing, structural advantage and luck are live alternative causes.

Use existing SignCell for typed/source-linked statements, SignChain for changes and positive/negative tellings, SignGraph for actor/practice/source/consumer relations and SignCraft for pure candidate composition. Knowing researches the practice inference; Organizing composes behaviorally useful, non-authorizing masks and dispatch; Animating performs only operations supplied by the admitted organ/capability and institutional authority. Keep program-specific monetization below the scientific mathematical substrate. Do not mint new mathematical Marks or pretend source IDs prove observational equivalence.

## 2. Evidence structure and negative law

For each claimed transfer, retain:

- Exemplar's *public identity reference*, which never becomes the newly authored Character's identity.
- Exact source URI, publisher, authored/published date when known, extractable proposition and attribution (source claims, not truth of the person's inner intent).
- Observed action or institutional arrangement, period and scope. For a public-company press release, do not promote advertiser self-description to independent verified profitability.
- Candidate **practice** stated as a reusable operation of bounded inputs, work situation, actions, deliverable, cost and falsifying receiving observation. Do not use "business genius", "comedic genius" or "the character IS Guy" as operation labels.
- Evidence **for** and **against** the proposed practice attribution; absence remains neither, disagreement remains both (FOUR knowledge, not majority vote), and a thin positive witness does not justify high confidence.
- Transfer conditions: rights, human collaborators, distribution channels, capital, team size, audience, platform, geography, age, material constraints. Unknown transfer conditions explicitly remain unknown.
- Originality/ethics limits: no misleading claims of affiliation, false endorsements, borrowed personas or distinctive likenesses/voices, private psychological inference, appropriation of protected creative expression, or erased collective labor.
- Economic receiving tests: paying customer/revenue (not views alone), incremental attributable leads or contracts, work cost, gross/contribution margin, retention, and adverse outcomes. A hypothesized market is not a signed contract.

**A source observation and an inferred disposition are not equal propositions.** A verified broadcast and brand partnership may justify studying production/distribution methods; it does not prove a stable cognitive faculty or that those methods cause any revenue for 561 Group. Opposed sourcing must remain visible and may suspend automatic projection.

## 3. Test exemplars: document the business *moves*, not personalities

### A. Seth MacFarlane — explicitly limited evidence

Fuzzy Door's company profile identifies a production organization led by MacFarlane and **president Erica Huggins**; its public portfolio spans formats, genres and cultural subjects. This supports investigating an *ensemble/co-production and portfolio-development practice*, not an unsupported claim that one man's solitary personal traits caused a production company's output. Source: https://fuzzydoor.com/about/ (accessed 2026-10-19).

NBCUniversal's 2026-09-30 announcement states that the *Ted* franchise's existing film characters are carried into an additional animated series, created by MacFarlane **with Paul Corrigan and Brad Walsh**, with multiple producers and partner studios; debut was **announced** for December 17, 2026, not observed to have occurred by this note's date. Transferable candidate: **portfolio-format extension**, a rights-qualified decision to expand a proven creative concept to a new format/team/audience with a measurable receiving channel. Source: https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/peacock-releases-premiere-date-first-look-seth-macfarlanes-ted-animated-series .

The company's August 2025 interview reporting about *The Naked Gun* describes negotiation/greenlighting hurdles for feature comedy, and publicly credits MacFarlane and Huggins. Transferable candidate: **comedy market validation**, deliberately testing audience appetite for a recognizable genre, not copying scripts, jokes, voice, animation, titles, or protected IP. This is only a *candidate*; no numerical elasticity or ROI is supplied by this interview. Source: https://fuzzydoor.com/news/p/naked-gun-producers-seth-macfarlane-and-erica-huggins-talk-greenlight-hurdles-and-sequel-possibilities/ .

A stronger characterization of the proposed 'media sense' is *reusable production pipeline + differentiated formats + credited collaborator network + distribution contracts*. A stronger characterization of 'comedy sense' is a *procedural portfolio of joke development, editorial iteration, audience feedback, and medium-specific constraints*. Neither is the essence of MacFarlane, and both require independent experimental evidence for transferability. **Counterfactual confounders** include pre-existing audience, advertising budgets, licensed original material, established studio relationships, collaborators, financing and broadcaster access.

### C. Cross-exemplar composition (hypothesis, not attributed psychology)

Both exemplars provide publicly observable instances of: (i) repeatable formats, (ii) franchise continuity, (iii) collaborators and distribution channels, and (iv) connecting audience attention to some downstream offering. *Select* elements from each, preserving their independent origin and institutional conditions. Possible original Character: **Audience-to-Enterprise Producer**, tasked to find worthy producers, test an original story/comedy format, publish with consent and attribution, then measure voluntary inquiries and profitably repeat the useful format. This is NOT 'Seth MacFarlane x Guy Fieri', a voice imitation, celebrity endorsement, or automatic entitlement to either franchise's audience. It cannot claim such a title or access.

The first buyer hypothesis is paying **independent producers and micro-enterprises** seeking rights-respecting discovery, storytelling and customer conversion. Counter-hypothesis: free self-publishing or ordinary agency services satisfy them more efficiently. Minimum external test is a named opt-in receiver, a separately priced and scoped deliverable, real fulfillment cost, and a payment/contract or explicit rejection; social impressions or theoretical projected revenue do not establish market necessity. Group must qualify demand, rights and acceptance; Science offers only possible constraints.

## 4. Feminist analysis: masks are not just a costume library

Joan Rivière, 'Womanliness as a Masquerade' (1929), investigated feminine performance as a socially situated defence, with particularly contestable psychoanalytic assumptions. The paper's claim about masquerade challenged a simple division between 'authentic interior' and a worn feminine exterior; it does **not** provide a ready-made programming API or general psychological profile of women. Primary text: https://lacanianworksexchange.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/19290101WomanlinessasamasqueradeJoanRiviereInternationalJournalofPsycho-AnalysisVol9p303-313-1.pdf .

Feminist critiques and reinterpretations of this inheritance include Luce Irigaray, Michèle Montrelay, Mary Ann Doane and Judith Butler. See scholarly critical literature: https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7591/j.ctt207g6z5.7 . Their accounts are NOT interchangeable. Butler's performativity is not an individual's entirely free daily choice of outfit/persona. Identity categories are constituted through repeated socially regulated acts and unequal power, rather than simply expressing a pre-existing internal essence. Overview and disagreement: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-gender/ .

**Engineering consequence:** A mask is not a psychological 'real self' cloned into code. Record who authored a mask, who can wear/revoke it, under which institutional relationships and constraints, what labor makes it possible, how an affected person can challenge a misleading attribution, and who benefits financially. A rich Character can hold different audience-facing performances without pretending an underlying person has one isolatable 'true' personality. The expression 'emulates MacFarlane' means *selects independently described professional practices as hypotheses*, NOT falsely presenting as him, assuming his gendered/racialized privileges transfer, taking ownership of his team's work, or copying his creative expression.

The feminist question is also **distributional**: who is seen, credited, paid and allowed to refuse. A celebrity-centered profile risks erasing co-creators (e.g. Huggins, Corrigan, Walsh) and the small business people whose labor the series exhibits. New Characters should be required to expose attributable contributors and consent/rights at the point of real publication and commercial use. Do not equate transparent evidence recording with a surveillance mandate: public actions are much safer research data than private inferred traits.

## 5. Candidate formalism and falsifiers

Let E be source-attributed observations, P candidate practice classes, C a *new* Character and W a concrete work kind. Observation relation e ⊧+ p / e ⊧− p is **revisable testimony**, not physical identity or theorem; combine independently addressed for/against evidence by the existing FOUR knowledge join. A projection S ⊆ P can be selected only after (i) source link exists, (ii) the correct exemplar is named, (iii) counterevidence is preserved, (iv) an explicit applying procedure with deliverable and outcome measure is defined, and (v) no authorizing institution or skill is inferred from selection. A conflicted candidate should remain visible but not be selected automatically.

A **hypothesis** is that repeated situated acts of C under source-qualified constraints may be regular enough to characterize a dispositional operator χ. To call χ a frame nucleus, Science must establish monotonicity, inflationarity, idempotence **and preservation of binary meets**, and compatibility with restriction when asserted. These conditions are not consequences of a few biographical stories, a finite trait matrix, or a successful test campaign. Keep empirical behavioral regularity and algebraic characterization distinct.

For an actual frame nucleus, 1 is fixed, so two nuclei's fixed-point sets both contain 1 and cannot have empty intersection. The nuclear fixed subframe generally takes meets as inherited but joins through χ applied to ambient joins; 'sub-Heyting algebra with inherited operations' requires qualification. **Negative law:** the current 'Character' glossary's proposed fully-disjoint normative/character case is mathematically inadmissible under the stated nucleus axioms. This is a theory correction, not reason to discard the research program.

**Discriminating counterexamples:**
1. Two independent public sources describing the same act with opposite quality interpretations must retain both and withhold automatic endorsement.
2. A richly described famous person with no direct source link must yield no admitted practice.
3. One individual performs the same technique but lacks the originals' distribution rights; an output can be a candidate workflow but not a publishing right.
4. Selecting a professional practice may modify a Character mask but **must not** create office, power, skill admission or authenticated agent identity.
5. Original Character authors change selected practices without losing provenance of previous compositions and without merging with real people's identity.
6. A media product receives views and zero customers; revenue claim fails independently of successful content deployment.
7. Another source shows the referenced success involved teams and institutional access; explanations credit these factors and reduce attribution to one person's mind.
8. A purported fixed-space pair with empty intersection must be rejected by frame reasoning, not celebrated as a countercultural persona.

## 6. Downstream receiving obligations

- **Engineering:** reuse existing SignCell source-statement, Computer Data FOUR, SignChain witness composition, SignGraph associations, and AOE Organizing agency and cast; no branch-local hash, proof engine, or public-figure likeness generator. Implement source-labeled practice candidates -> selective independent Character/mask records -> work-specific procedures. Preserve source materials in usable fields after normalization, not just a descriptive biography. Actual operation contracts should be callable and independently testable.
- **Operations:** define source-bearing procedures/work kinds and revocable mask selection as reusable data bound to the Engineering consumer. Research summaries are not installed actors.
- **561 Group:** choose a non-impersonating new Character name, qualified users, priced commercial service, legal rights and independent market evidence. Do not appoint its office from an exemplar profile.
- **Causeway/public:** deliver an actual contracted useful result, credit real beneficiaries/collaborators, retain their corrections, and measure customer-received value rather than self-reported output.
- **Feedback:** earnings, requests, disputes and missed contracts should improve *practice hypotheses* with counterevidence rather than recursively decorating the persona prompt.

This note establishes **bounded research possibilities** and named falsifiers; it does not prove χ exists for either public person, verify profitability, settle publicity law in all jurisdictions, authorize use of a person's image/voice, or claim an installed running Character.
