# Source-grounded exemplars, derived practices, and the feminist problem of masks

**Originating Leader demand, 2026-10-19:** "Make money make money make money. I want us to have more characters, formal Characters, that we can use when doing certain work. ... point to real people, real sets of qualities (i.e. business sense) and then derive their qualities, and then project an arrangement of real people and selected qualities into a character ... 'let's make a character that has the business, comedy, and media sense of Seth MacFarlane' ... next person is Guy Fieri's business and media sense." This research preserves the demand in its own words as a source, not an authorization to borrow any person's identity, rights, or powers.

## 1. Different objects MUST remain different

A publicly identified **person** is a source-referent; a documented **act or practice** is an attributed observation; a **practice class** is an analytical concept; a **disposition or skill** is a revisable hypothesis inferred across observations; a **Character** is a selected arrangement of commitments and practices in an independent agent; a **mask/persona** is its situated public-facing or role-facing presentation; an **office** is a normative institutional position; an **incumbency** seats an actual actor in that position; an **organ** or model-provider capacity is an actual means of acting; and a **commercial outcome** is an independent receiving observation. Neither reference to a successful person nor an attractive representation is an incumbency, license, contract, skill admission, or successful sale.

Direction of explanation: identifiable sources -> exact source statements -> attributed observable actions -> constrained, revisable interpretations of transferable operations -> candidate practice selection -> authored Character/mask -> bounded work decision -> authorized act -> externally received outcome. Backwards promotion (observing revenue and asserting every inferred inner trait) is invalid: fame, distribution privilege, capital, teams, legal control, timing, structural advantage and luck are live alternative causes.

Use existing SignCell for typed/source-linked statements, SignChain for changes and positive/negative tellings, SignGraph for actor/practice/source/consumer relations and SignCraft for pure candidate composition. Knowing researches the practice inference; Organizing composes behaviorally useful, non-authorizing masks and dispatch; Animating performs only operations supplied by the admitted organ/capability and institutional authority. Keep program-specific monetization below the scientific mathematical substrate. Do not mint new mathematical Marks or pretend source IDs prove observational equivalence.

## 2. Evidence structure and negative law

For each claimed transfer, retain:

- Exemplar's *public identity reference*, which never becomes the newly authored Character's identity.
- Exact source URI, publisher, authored/published date when known, extractable proposition and attribution (source claims, not truth of the person's inner intent).
- Observed action or institutional arrangement, period and scope. For a public-company press release, do not promote advertiser self-description to independent verified profitability.
- Candidate **practice** stated as a reusable operation of bounded inputs, work situation, actions, deliverable, cost and falsifying receiving observation. Do not use "business genius", "comedic genius" or "the character IS Guy" as operation labels.
- Evidence **for** and **against** the proposed practice attribution; absence remains neither, disagreement remains both (FOUR knowledge, not majority vote), and a thin positive witness does not justify high confidence.
- Transfer conditions: rights, human collaborators, distribution channels, capital, team size, audience, platform, geography, age, material constraints. Unknown transfer conditions explicitly remain unknown.
- Originality/ethics limits: no misleading claims of affiliation, false endorsements, borrowed personas or distinctive likenesses/voices, private psychological inference, appropriation of protected creative expression, or erased collective labor.
- Economic receiving tests: paying customer/revenue (not views alone), incremental attributable leads or contracts, work cost, gross/contribution margin, retention, and adverse outcomes. A hypothesized market is not a signed contract.

**A source observation and an inferred disposition are not equal propositions.** A verified broadcast and brand partnership may justify studying production/distribution methods; it does not prove a stable cognitive faculty or that those methods cause any revenue for 561 Group. Opposed sourcing must remain visible and may suspend automatic projection.

## 4. Feminist analysis: masks are not just a costume library

Joan Rivière, 'Womanliness as a Masquerade' (1929), investigated feminine performance as a socially situated defence, with particularly contestable psychoanalytic assumptions. The paper's claim about masquerade challenged a simple division between 'authentic interior' and a worn feminine exterior; it does **not** provide a ready-made programming API or general psychological profile of women. Primary text: https://lacanianworksexchange.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/19290101WomanlinessasamasqueradeJoanRiviereInternationalJournalofPsycho-AnalysisVol9p303-313-1.pdf .

Feminist critiques and reinterpretations of this inheritance include Luce Irigaray, Michèle Montrelay, Mary Ann Doane and Judith Butler. See scholarly critical literature: https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7591/j.ctt207g6z5.7 . Their accounts are NOT interchangeable. Butler's performativity is not an individual's entirely free daily choice of outfit/persona. Identity categories are constituted through repeated socially regulated acts and unequal power, rather than simply expressing a pre-existing internal essence. Overview and disagreement: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-gender/ .

**Engineering consequence:** A mask is not a psychological 'real self' cloned into code. Record who authored a mask, who can wear/revoke it, under which institutional relationships and constraints, what labor makes it possible, how an affected person can challenge a misleading attribution, and who benefits financially. A rich Character can hold different audience-facing performances without pretending an underlying person has one isolatable 'true' personality. The expression 'emulates MacFarlane' means *selects independently described professional practices as hypotheses*, NOT falsely presenting as him, assuming his gendered/racialized privileges transfer, taking ownership of his team's work, or copying his creative expression.

The feminist question is also **distributional**: who is seen, credited, paid and allowed to refuse. A celebrity-centered profile risks erasing co-creators (e.g. Huggins, Corrigan, Walsh) and the small business people whose labor the series exhibits. New Characters should be required to expose attributable contributors and consent/rights at the point of real publication and commercial use. Do not equate transparent evidence recording with a surveillance mandate: public actions are much safer research data than private inferred traits.

## 5. Candidate formalism and falsifiers

Let E be source-attributed observations, P candidate practice classes, C a *new* Character and W a concrete work kind. Observation relation e ⊧+ p / e ⊧− p is **revisable testimony**, not physical identity or theorem; combine independently addressed for/against evidence by the existing FOUR knowledge join. A projection S ⊆ P can be selected only after (i) source link exists, (ii) the correct exemplar is named, (iii) counterevidence is preserved, (iv) an explicit applying procedure with deliverable and outcome measure is defined, and (v) no authorizing institution or skill is inferred from selection. A conflicted candidate should remain visible but not be selected automatically.

A **hypothesis** is that repeated situated acts of C under source-qualified constraints may be regular enough to characterize a dispositional operator χ. To call χ a frame nucleus, Science must establish monotonicity, inflationarity, idempotence **and preservation of binary meets**, and compatibility with restriction when asserted. These conditions are not consequences of a few biographical stories, a finite trait matrix, or a successful test campaign. Keep empirical behavioral regularity and algebraic characterization distinct.

For an actual frame nucleus, 1 is fixed, so two nuclei's fixed-point sets both contain 1 and cannot have empty intersection. The nuclear fixed subframe generally takes meets as inherited but joins through χ applied to ambient joins; 'sub-Heyting algebra with inherited operations' requires qualification. **Negative law:** the current 'Character' glossary's proposed fully-disjoint normative/character case is mathematically inadmissible under the stated nucleus axioms. This is a theory correction, not reason to discard the research program.

**Discriminating counterexamples:**
1. Two independent public sources describing the same act with opposite quality interpretations must retain both and withhold automatic endorsement.
2. A richly described famous person with no direct source link must yield no admitted practice.
3. One individual performs the same technique but lacks the originals' distribution rights; an output can be a candidate workflow but not a publishing right.
4. Selecting a professional practice may modify a Character mask but **must not** create office, power, skill admission or authenticated agent identity.
5. Original Character authors change selected practices without losing provenance of previous compositions and without merging with real people's identity.
6. A media product receives views and zero customers; revenue claim fails independently of successful content deployment.
7. Another source shows the referenced success involved teams and institutional access; explanations credit these factors and reduce attribution to one person's mind.
8. A purported fixed-space pair with empty intersection must be rejected by frame reasoning, not celebrated as a countercultural persona.

## 7. Deeper feminist and sociomaterial foundations — second source pass

This is a research relationship, not a claim that feminist thought licenses one universal model of roles, masks or agent identity. These theories disagree about the nature of the subject, performance, perception and constraint. Make their disagreements informative rather than collapse them into one 'feminist personality module'.

**Joan Rivière: mask under social constraint.** *Womanliness as a Masquerade* (1929) describes feminine masquerade, specifically in the context of the social penalties and reprisals the analyzed woman anticipated for public intellectual accomplishment. The point is not merely that one can choose an outfit called 'womanliness': the relationship between performance, audience recognition, power and anticipated punishment is part of the masquerade. The original author's psychoanalytic claims and gender essentialism are not accepted as contemporary empirical truth. Source: https://lacanianworksexchange.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/19290101WomanlinessasamasqueradeJoanRiviereInternationalJournalofPsycho-AnalysisVol9p303-313-1.pdf .

**Mary Ann Doane and the critique of a transparent feminine spectacle.** A feminist film-theory reading of masquerade turns attention to the gendered act of spectating and to the apparatus by which a spectator and performer become visible. Rather than assigning 'feminine' or 'masculine' as a transferable behavioral class, study what a media organization *makes visible*, who controls visibility and who is positioned as viewing subject versus viewed object. Doane, 'Film and the Masquerade: Theorising the Female Spectator' (1982), discussed in critical history: https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7591/j.ctt207g6z5.7 and https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-18396-5_4 . These are secondary analytic accounts; the original article and its context remain the primary scholarly source to recover for stronger textual claims.

**Judith Butler: performed identities are norm-governed repetition.** Gender performativity is not the hypothesis that a freely choosing individual puts on arbitrary role costumes and switches identities at will. Its critical force includes the constraints under which repeated acts are intelligible and sanctioned. For Character engineering, a mask record cannot plausibly supply genuine social recognition merely by authoring 'wearing' as metadata; the audience, institutions and contestation matter. For conceptual background and disputes, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-gender/ . Make a sharp technical distinction between *an intended performance selected by the agent*, *a public performance actually produced*, and *recognition or harm attributed by other actors*.

**Donna Haraway: situated knowledges and accountable partial perspective.** Haraway, 'Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective' (*Feminist Studies* 14(3), 1988, 575–599; DOI 10.2307/3178066), argues against the view from nowhere. The operational analogy is limited but consequential: an attribution to 'business sense' must show the located observer, the context and whose evidence can challenge it. It does not follow that adding a list of citations makes an automated judge objective. Bibliography: https://philpapers.org/versions/HARSKT . Counterexample: two articles agreeing because both repeat the same press release are not independent eyewitnesses.

**Kimberlé Crenshaw: intersectionality makes institutional advantage non-transferable.** 'Mapping the Margins' (*Stanford Law Review* 43(6), 1991, 1241–1299) develops intersectional analysis of the ways institutions can erase differences within policy categories and political identities. DOI 10.2307/1229039; source https://www.jstor.org/stable/1229039 . An enterprise focusing on two successful men must not mistake individual success for evidence that others face the same access to capital, contracts, audience, legal protections or culturally legible presentation. Yet merely listing demographic identity is no sufficient causal explanation either: test the specific institutions, relations and barriers.

**Patricia Hill Collins: unequal control of representations.** The 'matrix of domination' names historically situated structural, disciplinary, hegemonic and interpersonal power. See a scholarly reference account: https://doi.org/10.1002/9781405165518.wbeosm054 . For a Fieri-inspired independent-maker feature, the chef, workers and local community must retain their own voices and economic claims rather than become content inputs whose labor is attributed to a celebrity host. The Character must not infer public performers' inner moral quality or simulate marginalized people's voices as marketing instruments. It should track **who is producer, who controls narrative, who controls distribution, who receives payment** and what rights each actually has.

**Lucy Suchman: people and machines are enacted in situated relations.** *Human-Machine Reconfigurations: Plans and Situated Actions* (2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2007) explicitly investigates how persons and machines are figured and enacted at their interfaces. Publisher description: https://books.google.com/books/about/Human_Machine_Reconfigurations.html?id=VwKMDV-Gv1MC . This does not settle whether the 561 Group's mathematical 'Character nucleus' exists; it is an empirical warning against projecting agency from a stand-alone textual personality. A model's response, a harness action, a different person's recognition, a networked account and an office act are materially different events; a label should not collapse them into one synthetic self.

**Resulting design question:** When a selected mask changes the social expectations under which a Character speaks or acts, what independently observable and revocable relations have changed? Authored selection alone is a *candidate*; subsequent interactions may provide evidence of the performance's effect but cannot confer rights without normative sources. Feminist rigor here is not censorship of personas. It is greater specificity about the conditions of performance and the distributions of power and benefits.

## 9. Deriving the category and class from an example

The Leader's method is not simply *put 'business sense' into a prompt*. Treat the original example as an element of a class within a broader category:

- An **observed production** is an element: e.g. the 2026 NBCUniversal announcement of an animated continuation of an existing film franchise, with separately credited writers, performers, showrunners, producers and companies. What has been observed is *the announcement and its attributed arrangement*, not a guaranteed successful release.
- A **practice class** describes the candidate reusable structure: *cross-format franchise development*. It abstracts selected relations (original right, accessible existing premise, complementary production resources, new format, new distributor, measurable receiving), not the franchise's scripts, character likenesses or contracts.
- A **category** contains neighboring classes: *media-enterprise* includes format extension, multi-channel experiments, audience-bridge discovery and several other practices, potentially supported by unrelated exemplars. Every induction from instances to classes remains revisable.
- A **work application** uses a selected class for a Character: *Mira Crossfield* can select cross-format development for an independently licensed client and reject source material if required rights are absent; *Tavi Lantern* can select an independent-maker feature and return an opt-in producer-controlled contact route. Neither acquires the exemplar's identity or production credit.
- A **downstream received result** depends on real rights, available production capacity, consent and a particular buyer; a hypothetical media operator that produces files is not yet a profitable business.

In an evidence context $K=(E,P,I^+,I^-)$, $E$ is a set of *qualified source-observation tokens* and $P$ a set of *candidate practice predicates*, with separate supporting and opposing incidence. Under incomplete or inconsistent evidence, the existing FOUR reading $\mathsf{v}(e,p)$ is appropriate to retain both lanes. Ordinary binary FCA $A\mapsto A''$ can describe a chosen, explicitly policy-bounded supporting slice only; converting a contradictory or missing observation into a binary positive fact would erase the paraconsistent reading. This is a **model proposal** rather than a proven consequence of Obsmetrologia's foundational theorems.

The selection $S\subseteq P$ becomes a new mask commitment only through source-qualified candidate operations. A candidate nucleus $\chi_S$ is **not** automatically induced by this selected subset: proving it is a frame nucleus requires a specified frame $H$, a total $\chi_S:H\to H$, monotonicity, inflationarity, idempotence, binary meet preservation and naturality when claimed. The science problem remains to construct or refute such a map from actual repeated situated performances while preserving the direction of explanation Obsmetrologia → recovered conventional mathematics. In a *test-only conventional finite lattice*, an independently computed closure map can test algebraic obligations but cannot prove the foundational ontology or psychological emulation.

**Critical falsifier:** A source article credits a lead host for an outcome but the production depended on unequal funding, a publisher's distribution, other producers and independent creators. An algorithm attributing the outcome to a solitary celebrity's 'natural business instinct' is wrong even if a language model confidently performs the resulting mask. A revised, network-aware practice account can be meaningful without claiming to know the person's mind.



## 10. Dated commercial demand and disclosure — October 19, 2026

A narrow paid **maker/merchant feature** is a plausible first receiver for *Tavi Lantern*, supported as a *test candidate*, not by any claim of ready customers. This is a much more defensible revenue hypothesis than an agent introducing itself as Guy Fieri or trying to imitate an entertainer's voice.

**External market signal 1 — differentiated video services.** Wyzowl's [State of Video Marketing 2026](https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/) reports 91% of surveyed businesses using video for marketing, 82% of surveyed video marketers perceiving good ROI, and 92% planning to maintain/increase video spending. **Critical denominator:** its report describes **266 mixed marketer and consumer respondents surveyed in late 2025**. It is a vendor-published, self-reported, mixed-sample benchmark rather than an unbiased measure of independent Duluth-area producers' demand, customer retention, a quoted purchase order, or 561 Group margins. High adoption also suggests competition, not unoccupied profitable territory.

**External market signal 2 — competition among formats.** [HubSpot's State of Marketing 2026](https://www.hubspot.com/state-of-marketing) and its [public statistics](https://www.hubspot.com/marketing-statistics) report that surveyed marketers frequently name short-form video the highest-ROI format (about 49% selecting it). Its self-reported ROI ranking does not measure a particular maker's incremental sales. A production bundle should compare short video with native site copy, local search, source-controlled evergreen landing pages and interviews, rather than presupposing that one generic 30-second reel meets every buyer.

**Source-observed named exemplar, actual release:** Food Network's [September 10, 2026 account of *Guy's Feast Club*](https://www.foodnetwork.com/shows/guys-feast-club/articles/guys-feast-club-comes-to-youtube) lists four 2026 YouTube episodes (June 11, July 9, August 13, September 10), moving this individual example from WBD's May *announcement* to a separately published platform schedule. This supports the *channel-appropriate serialized program* class hypothesis, not an estimate of revenue, production spend or a transferable celebrity fanbase.

**Correct ethical and legal producer boundary:** The U.S. [FTC endorsements/influencers guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews) and [2023 Endorsement Guides questions](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) distinguish independent coverage from commercial endorsements and instruct clear, conspicuous disclosure of **material connections** including paid or in-kind consideration. Endorsements must be truthful, and the substantive disclosure should accompany the relevant endorsement rather than only appear behind a link. It would be deceptive to produce a paid review styled as independent third-party journalism, or to claim an agent is a named celebrity's business representative. Consult actual facts and relevant law before each public placement. An unsigned media proposal needs no fabricated testimonial, fake customer or undisclosed affiliate link.

**One testable product from derived class to economically received service:**

- **Buyer:** an actual opt-in independent maker, operator or merchant with rights to their real materials and a customer-facing order/contact channel, explicitly authorized by the merchant.
- **Offer:** an original interview/feature, credited photos or rights-cleared original creative assets, one format-adapted short video *when appropriate*, accessible web copy, merchant-owned call-to-action and correction path, with separately priced optional updates.
- **Producer:** Tavi's `independent-producer-spotlight` and `channel-format-adaptation` repertoire; Mira's `comedy-offer-test` only when the consenting merchant and audience genuinely call for original humor. None of this grants a publishing account, editor position or pricing authorization.
- **Differentiation:** the merchant remains visible as original contributor; the agent identifies actual distribution limitations and required permissions; material connections are disclosed, and rights and labor costs are explicit.
- **Falsifier:** the target buyer prefers their existing free/cheap promotional channel or declines the minimum viable paid deliverable. Record the *decline*, not imaginary conversion revenue.
- **Receiving:** original merchant agrees to the scope and cost, accepts the useful result, then payment is independently booked with clear variable/paid costs and an attributable customer benefit. A viral video with no buyer acceptance or usable merchant outcome does not count.
