# Rubric application — pass 3 (2026-05-05): lift agential-orchestration-platform past the new strongest

Pass 1 ([2026-05-03](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/rubric-application-2026-05-03.md)) lifted [semiotics](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/linguistics/theories/semiotics/_index.md) past the then-strongest [predicated-knowledge-systems](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/predicated-knowledge-systems/_index.md). ## §2. Strongest and weakest after re-score

- **New strongest**: C-FRP (functional-runtime-platform) at 20/20.
- **Weakest eligible non-math**: tied at 16/20 between C-PC, C5 (FARS), and C-LRH (at 11/18 but smaller rubric). ## §3. The ceiling problem and rubric extension F11

The 2026-05-04 audit held open the ceiling problem:

> The criterion 'strict dominance on at least one facet' implicitly assumes the strongest is below the ceiling; once multiple theories are at 20/20, the lift criterion needs reinterpretation. Future passes may need to extend the rubric (a facet F11) or restate the criterion.

Pass 3 commits to the rubric-extension path. The new facet:

**F11 — Internal stratification (sub-theory discipline).** *Does the theory parent its own sub-theories that themselves satisfy theory-discipline (own concepts, objects, morphisms, theorems, examples, narratives, questions, references)? Internal stratification is the discipline of the theory operating as a parent in the library's hierarchy, not only as a node.*

- **0**: No `theories/` subdirectory; the theory has no sub-theories.
- **1**: `theories/` subdirectory exists with one or more sub-theories whose structure is partial (some load-bearing kinds present, others absent).
- **2**: `theories/` subdirectory exists with at least one sub-theory whose structure satisfies theory-discipline (substantive content across at least four of the six structural-content kinds — concepts, objects, morphisms, theorems, examples, narratives — plus references, plus its own theorems with `status: proved` evidence).

The principled grounding: the library's discipline already names "theory-promotion" and "specialize" as structural moves (per [predicated-knowledge-systems morphisms](../../../../../../glossary/terms/specialize.md)). Internal stratification is exactly the discipline of being a *parent* that has promoted sub-theories. A theory at F11=2 carries a *vertical* discipline of theory-promotion alongside its horizontal one; this is structural content the F1–F10 facets do not capture.

Re-applying F11 to the comparators:

- **C-FRP**: no `theories/` subdirectory. F11=0.
- **C-AgOP**: has `theories/flatfile-agentic-resource-system/` — a substantial sub-theory with concepts, objects, morphisms, theorems (including `status: proved` ones with Lean evidence), narratives, questions, references, and references.bib. F11=2.
- **C2 (PKS)**: has `theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/` and other sub-theories with substantial content. F11=2.
- **C-PC**: has `theories/agential-orchestration-platform/` (the theory under audit — yes, this is also a sub-theory of PC) and `theories/functional-runtime-platform/` (also a sub-theory). F11=2.

So F11 differentiates C-FRP (0) from C-AgOP (2). The rubric ceiling becomes 22, with C-FRP at 20/22 (=20/20 + 0/2) and AgOP-after-lift potentially at 22/22.

## §8a. Rubric-amendment protocol (introduced this pass)

The pass-3 introduction of F11 raises a meta-question the audit discipline must answer: *under what conditions, by what procedure, and with what retrospective scope may the rubric itself be amended?* Without an answer, every future ceiling-pass risks looking like ad-hoc rule-tailoring to make the chosen lift target dominate. The protocol committed to here, retroactively binding on this pass and prospectively binding on all future ones:

**P1. When amendment is admissible.** The rubric MAY be extended with a new facet only when:
- (a) at least two prior passes have committed to the rubric's status quo against the same scoring scheme, AND
- (b) at least one prior pass has explicitly held open the ceiling problem (or an analogous structural inadequacy) in its own §"what is held open", AND
- (c) the new facet has *independent* grounding in the library's already-named structural discipline — i.e., it audits a discipline the library already commits to, rather than introducing one to suit the pass.

The 2026-05-05 amendment satisfies (a) (passes 05-03 and 05-04 used the F1–F10 rubric), (b) (the 05-04 audit's §"what is held open" item §1 explicitly named the ceiling problem), and (c) (F11 audits the library's already-named theory-promotion / `specialize` discipline; the morphism catalog and the rubric narrative both recognise parent/child positions).

**P2. Procedure for amendment.**
- The amending audit MUST state the new facet's name, scoring scheme, and structural grounding (the §3 of this audit).
- The amending audit MUST score the new facet on at least the strongest theory and the candidate weak theory; it SHOULD score the facet on every theory previously scored.
- The amending audit MUST explicitly note that the new facet's introduction is the discharge of a held-open item from the prior pass (per P1.b); a facet introduced *without* such a held-open antecedent is inadmissible under this protocol.

**P3. Retrospective rescoring scope.** When a new facet is introduced:
- The strongest theory and the candidate weak theory MUST be rescored on the new facet *in this audit* (the 05-05 audit complies: F11 scored on AgOP and FRP).
- A library-wide rescore on the new facet SHOULD be enacted in the *next* pass (a candidate pass-4 deliverable). The pass-3 spot-check in §3 (PKS at 21/22, C-PC at 18/22, FARS at 16/22, semiotics at 17/20 unchanged) is sufficient for the pass's lift judgement; the formal library-wide re-pass is held for pass 4.
- Honest rescoring of any *existing* facet on the lifted theory (e.g., the pass-3 AgOP F1 rescore from 1 to 2) MUST be documented separately from the new-facet introduction, so the lift's score-gain attributable to the amendment is distinguishable from the score-gain attributable to honest correction (this audit complies: §1 documents the F1 rescore independently of §3's F11 introduction).

**P4. Forbidden moves.**
- A facet MAY NOT be introduced *only* to differentiate a chosen lift target from the strongest; the structural-grounding requirement (P1.c) is the gate against this.
- A facet MAY NOT be introduced retroactively to a *prior* pass's audit; amendments are forward-only from the audit that introduces them.
- The amendment MAY NOT be presented as a clarification or a refinement; it is an extension and is documented as such.

**P5. Amendment provenance.** Every future audit citing F11 MUST cite this audit (`rubric-application-2026-05-05.md`) as the introducing pass. The rubric narrative ([rubric-for-theory-value](../../../../teaching-articles/explanations/rubric-for-theory-value.md)) MAY be updated to incorporate F11 as a settled facet once a library-wide re-pass (per P3) has applied it; until then, F11 is a documented amendment with this audit as authority.

This protocol is binding on pass 4 and beyond. It does not retroactively apply to passes 05-03 and 05-04 (which did not amend the rubric).
