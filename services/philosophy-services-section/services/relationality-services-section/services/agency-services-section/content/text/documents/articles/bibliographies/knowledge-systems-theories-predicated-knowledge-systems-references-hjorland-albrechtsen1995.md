## Annotation

The founding statement of the *domain-analytic* turn in information science. The paper argues against treating information retrieval and knowledge organisation as user-centric or cognitivist activities and re-grounds them in the *discourse community* (Fleck's *thought collective*, Kuhn's *paradigm community*, Wenger's *community of practice*) whose terminology and inferential commitments the system serves.

For the predicated framing this is the load-bearing argument that *fibers are discipline-shaped*. A predicate $p$ does not have a universal meaning; it has a meaning relative to the discourse community whose [discourse-community-as-fiber](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/archive/teaching/explanations/discourse-community-as-fiber.md) it lives in. The fiber algebra $A_p$ is therefore not a universal mathematical object but a community-relative one, and federation across fibers is exactly federation across discourse communities. The [discipline-vs-school-as-fibers](../learning-articles/questions/discipline-vs-school-as-fibers.md) question is the live research issue Hjørland and Albrechtsen surface for us: what *index* does the fibration take its values in — disciplines, schools, projects, or all three at once?

The framing departs from Hjørland and Albrechtsen by insisting the discourse-community structure is *internal to* the substrate (an index of the fibration) rather than external to it (a contextual layer the system happens to operate within). This is what lets [federate](../../../glossary/terms/federate.md) be an algebraic operation rather than a social negotiation.
