Peircean semiotics is the tradition of [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) theory founded by [Charles Sanders Peirce](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md) (1839–1914). It treats the [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) as an irreducible triadic relation among [representamen](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/representamen.md), object, and [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md), and [semiosis](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/semiosis.md) as the open-ended process by which [signs](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) generate further [signs](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md). This is the primary tradition developed in this vault&rsquo;s [semiotics](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/index.md) module.

## Methods and approach

Peirce&rsquo;s semiotics is grounded in logic and the philosophy of categories. His three universal categories — Firstness (quality, possibility), Secondness (reaction, brute fact), and Thirdness (mediation, law, habit) — structure the entire sign system. The [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) itself is a phenomenon of Thirdness: it mediates between object and interpreter [@peirce_CollectedPapers_1931].

### Key contributions

- **Triadic sign relation** — the [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) is a three-place relation among [representamen](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/representamen.md) (the sign vehicle), object (what the sign stands for), and [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) (the meaning produced). The relation is irreducible: it cannot be decomposed into three dyadic relations without losing its character.

- **Sign classification** — [Peirce](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md) classified [signs](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md) along multiple dimensions. The most widely known is by the relation between sign and object: [icon](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/icon.md) (resemblance), [index](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/index-sign.md) (causal or spatial connection), [symbol](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/symbol.md) (convention). Three trichotomies combine to produce ten fundamental sign types.

- **Semiosis** — the dynamic, iterative process of sign-mediated meaning-making. Every [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) is itself a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/sign.md), producing a chain of interpretation that is potentially unlimited. [Semiosis](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/semiosis.md) is constrained by habit, context, and purpose.

- **Three modes of inference** — deduction, induction, and abduction. [Peirce](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md) identified abduction — the generation of explanatory hypotheses — as the engine of [semiosis](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/semiosis.md).

- **Pragmaticism** — [Peirce](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md)&rsquo;s mature doctrine that the meaning of a concept consists in the practical consequences it entails. The [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) is the sign&rsquo;s meaning in the sense of what it enables the interpreter to do or infer.

## Role in this vault

Peircean semiotics provides the foundational vocabulary for the [semiotics](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/index.md) module. The [curricula](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/curricula/index.md) develop the triadic sign model, [semiosis](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/terms/semiosis.md), and sign classification in detail. The [mathematical formalization](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/from-signs-to-formal-structure.md) of the [semiotic universe](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md) takes Peirce&rsquo;s framework as its starting point.

## Contemporary formal Peircean work

Several contemporary programs formalize Peircean semiotics using category theory. [Joseph Goguen](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/joseph-goguen.md)&rsquo;s algebraic semiotics (1999–2006) treats sign systems as algebraic theories and representations as semiotic morphisms. Guerino Mazzola&rsquo;s functorial semiotics (2020–2022) models signs as presheaves in a topos-theoretic framework. Tohme, Gangle, and Caterina (2024) show that machine learning processes form a topos whose internal logic captures the structure of semiosis. See the [formal semiotics survey](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/formal-semiotics-survey.md) for detailed analysis.

The Reduction Thesis — that triadic relations are irreducible to combinations of dyadic relations — has received renewed mathematical attention from Koshkin (2022, 2024), who introduced &ldquo;ternarity&rdquo; as a numerical measure of triadic complexity, and from Haydon (2025), who connected Peirce&rsquo;s Existential Graphs to the string diagrams of category theory.
