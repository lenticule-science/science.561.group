# Disagreement is the inconsistency-sheaf cohomology of the scope cover (sketch)

**Claim** (sketch). Let $\mathcal{K} = (\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \mathcal{S}, \Sigma)$ be a [predicated knowledge system](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/knowing/content/knowledge-science/systems/objects/predicated-knowledge-system.md) and let $\mathcal{U} = \{S_i\}_{i \in I} \subseteq \Sigma$ be a *scope cover* of a target scope $S = \bigcup_\iota S_i$ — a family of pairwise federable scopes whose colimit (per [federation-as-bilattice-colimit](federation-as-bilattice-colimit.md)) is $S$. Then the structural shape of disagreement among $\mathcal{U}$'s discourse communities is exactly the cohomology of the [inconsistency sheaf](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/inconsistency-sheaf/inconsistency-sheaf.md) over the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md) the cover induces:

- $H^0(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{F}^{(\top)})$ = **resolved local contestations** — $\top$-valued cells that occur in some $S_i$ but glue consistently across the cover (the contestation is local, the cover agrees on its localness).
- $H^1(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{F}^{(\top)})$ = **substantive structural disagreement** — $\top$-valued cells whose contestation cannot be glued from local data; the disagreement is a global obstruction the cover cannot resolve from its parts.
- $H^{n \geq 2}(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{F}^{(\top)})$ = **higher coherence obstructions** — disagreements about what the disagreement *is*, surfaced when subcovers of $\mathcal{U}$ disagree on what counts as a contested cell at all.

## Setup

Per [federation-as-bilattice-colimit](federation-as-bilattice-colimit.md), the federated scope $S$ has knowledge fiber algebra $K_p^S = \mathrm{colim}_i K_p^{S_i}$ in $\mathbf{BiLat}_{\mathbf{L}^\bowtie}$, with values at identified cells combined under $\vee_k$ — producing $\top$ exactly where two covering scopes assert designated values that disagree on the truth coordinate.

The bitopological settling site $X_S$ associated to $S$ (per the [bitopological settling site axioms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/bitopological-settling-site/content/assets/legacy/bitopological-settling-site-axioms.md)) carries the [paraconsistent settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_{X_S}^{(k)}$, which decomposes per the inconsistency-sheaf definition as

$$\mathcal{F}_{X_S}^{(k)} \;\cong\; \mathcal{F}_{X_S}^{(t)} \,\oplus\, \mathcal{F}_{X_S}^{(\top)}.$$

The scope cover $\mathcal{U}$ is a Grothendieck cover on $X_S$ (a pairwise-Stone clopen cover, since each $S_i \subseteq S$ corresponds to a clopen of the bitopological site, and federability ensures the pairwise meets are clopens too).

## Argument

**(0) The cover is a Grothendieck site.** Each $S_i$ corresponds to a clopen $U_i \subseteq X_S$ of the [Priestley dual cover topology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/priestley-dual-cover-topology/priestley-dual-cover-topology.md); pairwise meets $U_i \cap U_j$ correspond to the federation-intersection $S_i \cap S_j$ of [federation-as-bilattice-colimit](federation-as-bilattice-colimit.md)'s pushout diagram. The cover is subcanonical per [priestley-cover-topology-subcanonical](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/references/priestley-cover-topology-subcanonical.md).

**(1) $H^0$ as resolved local contestations.** The Čech complex's degree-0 piece is $\prod_i \mathcal{F}^{(\top)}(U_i)$, modulo the equalizer condition $\mathcal{F}^{(\top)}(U_i)|_{U_i \cap U_j} = \mathcal{F}^{(\top)}(U_j)|_{U_i \cap U_j}$. A class in $H^0$ is a family of $\top$-cells $(\phi_i)_i$ with $\phi_i$ supported in $U_i$ and agreeing on overlaps. Operationally: every covering scope agrees that the same cells are contested, with the same contestation content. Such contestations are *local* — they live inside individual scopes and the cover endorses them as such.

**(2) $H^1$ as structural disagreement.** A 1-cocycle is a family $(\phi_{ij})_{i,j}$ on overlaps satisfying the cocycle condition $\phi_{jk}|_{ijk} - \phi_{ik}|_{ijk} + \phi_{ij}|_{ijk} = 0$ on triple overlaps. Non-trivial $H^1$ classes record contestation-data that exists pairwise but cannot be glued to any global section: scope $S_i$ and $S_j$ agree on what is contested in their overlap, $S_j$ and $S_k$ agree on theirs, $S_i$ and $S_k$ agree on theirs, yet no single global $\top$-cell pattern restricts to all three pairwise records consistently. This is the substrate-level structural fact: the communities' disagreements are not reducible to local mismatches; the cover itself imposes a global obstruction.

**(3) Higher cohomology and meta-disagreement.** $H^{n \geq 2}$ classes appear when the cover admits subcovers whose intersections disagree on the triple-overlap data. Read operationally: when the discourse communities disagree on *which contestations count as global obstructions* — a meta-level disagreement about what the substantive disagreements are — that meta-disagreement is an $H^{\geq 2}$ class of the inconsistency sheaf, witnessing a coherence failure one degree deeper than the structural disagreement at $H^1$.

**(4) Vanishing criterion.** When the cover satisfies [husainov-acyclicity](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/husainov-acyclicity.md) (per [settling-cohomology-vanishing-via-husainov-cech](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/references/settling-cohomology-vanishing-via-husainov-cech.md)), all higher inconsistency-sheaf cohomology vanishes and the disagreement collapses to its $H^0$ content — every contestation is local, every disagreement is a localized $\top$-cell with no global obstruction. Husainov-acyclic covers are the substrate's characterization of *cohesive federations*: the federation has no structural disagreement beyond its localized contestations.

## Reading

The substrate gives a structural distinction the disciplinary literature does not formalize:

- The **knowledge-organization literature** (Bowker & Star 1999, Knorr-Cetina 1999) registers cross-community disagreement as a sociological-empirical fact requiring case-by-case description, with no formal apparatus distinguishing local mismatch from structural obstruction.
- The **knowledge-representation literature** treats cross-source contradiction as a model-theoretic inconsistency triggering classical explosion, fixed by paraconsistent annotations or repair operators that flatten the structural shape into a single error class.
- The **paraconsistent logic tradition** (Belnap 1977, Fitting 1994) admits $\top$ as designated but treats every $\top$-cell as the same kind of evidential-conflict atom, without distinguishing local from globally obstructive contestations.

The inconsistency-sheaf cohomology gives the substrate-level distinction. A community auditing its federation's content can compute (in principle, by any standard Čech machinery on the bitopological site) which of its $\top$-cells are local contestations the cover already explains, and which are $H^1$-obstructions requiring substrate-level resolution — schema change, scope splitting, identity-mapping revision, or admission that the federation is structurally inconsistent and must be restricted.

## Engineering implication

The engineering counterpart is [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md): a [predicated computer](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predicated-computer.md) implementing the substrate can compute the $H^0/H^1$ profile of its $\top$-cells over its scope cover. The predicated-knowledge-systems claim is that this is not merely an engineering audit move — it is the substrate's structural reading of *what a community's disagreement is*, applicable to any predicated knowledge system whether or not it is realized on a predicated computer.

## Status: sketch

`status: sketch` because:

- The Čech-complex argument relies on the bitopological site being well-behaved enough that Čech cohomology agrees with the derived functor cohomology (per [cech-derived-comparison-on-bitopological-site](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/cech-derived-comparison-on-bitopological-site.md)); the precise hypotheses on the scope cover under which this comparison holds in the predicated-knowledge-systems setting need to be stated.
- The interpretation of higher cohomology degrees as "meta-disagreement" is heuristic; sharpening it would require examples (small finite covers exhibiting non-trivial $H^2$) and a category-theoretic characterization of the meta-relation as a 2-categorical structure on $\mathbf{Scope}_{\mathcal{K}}$.
- The Husainov-acyclic vanishing is stated for the inconsistency-sheaf component; the analogous statement for the truth-side $\mathcal{F}^{(t)}$ component (which would govern *agreement* cohomology) is the dual claim worth a separate theorem.
