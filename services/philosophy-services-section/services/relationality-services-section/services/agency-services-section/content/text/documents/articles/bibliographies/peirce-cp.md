## Annotation

The standard collected edition of Peirce's published and manuscript writings. For the structural-semiotic framing this theory commits to, three Peircean threads are load-bearing:

1. **Triadic sign-relation.** Every sign-process is the irreducibly triadic structure $(V, O, I)$ — sign-vehicle (representamen), object, interpretant. The argument-by-collapse against dyadic reductions is the proof. This grounds the discipline's [sign-relation](../../../glossary/concepts/sign-relation.md) and the structural identification with the substrate's predication-cell triad per the [composability narrative](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/communication/archive/library/linguistics/library/semiotics/references/teaching/explanations/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md).
2. **Unlimited semiosis.** The interpretant generates further interpretants without a structural cap; the chain converges only in the indefinite prolongation of inquiry. Read structurally, this is the *transfinite-bounded* regime claim of [unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint](../../../glossary/concepts/unlimited-semiosis-as-fixpoint.md).
3. **Pragmaticism.** The meaning of a concept is the totality of its practical bearings — the consequences of holding the assertion true. The structural reading places this at the *closure operator's settled state*: the meaning of a sign-relation is what closure derives from it.

The threefold Peircean trichotomy of sign-typology (icon/index/symbol; rheme/dicent/argument; qualisign/sinsign/legisign) gives 10 admissible sign-classes, instantiating the [peircean-system](../notes/undated/peircean-system.md).
