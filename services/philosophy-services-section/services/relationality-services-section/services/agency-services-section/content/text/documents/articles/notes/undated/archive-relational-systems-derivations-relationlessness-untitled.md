- It must be derived that Relationlessness has no Relation, thus it cannot not derive that Relationlessness Relates to Relation, deriving the first reflex — the Relation between Relationlessness and Relation.  
  Relate = (Relationlessness Relation)

- Reflexing Relate, it cannot not derive that Relate Relates to itself, deriving Reflex(1), the Reflexive Relation of Relating to itself.  
  Reflex(1) = (Relate Relate)

- Reflexing Reflex(1), it cannot not derive that Relating is Reflexing, transforming Relating to Reflexing Relating.  
  Relate := (Reflex Relate)

- Reflexing Relate, it cannot not derive that Relation Relates Relationlessness and Relate, deriving Relation(0), a unary Relation derived from Relationlessness through Reflexive Relating.  
  Relation(0) = (Relate Relationlessness)

- Reflexing Relation(0), it cannot not derive that Relation(0) Relates to itself, deriving Relation(1), a unary Relation derived from Relation(0) Reflexing.  
  Relation(1) = (Relate Relation(0))

- Reflexing Relation(1), it cannot not derive that Relation(n) Relates to Relation(n–1), deriving Determine(n), the unary Relation of a Relation(n) and its prior Relation(n–1).  
  Determine(n) = (Relate Relation(n–1))

- Reflexing Determine(n), it cannot not derive that Determine(n) Relates to itself, deriving ReflexDetermine(n), a unary Relation of Determine(n) Reflexing.  
  ReflexDetermine(n) = (Relate Determine(n))

- Reflexing ReflexDetermine(n), it cannot not derive that ReflexDetermine(n) Relates to Determine(n), deriving Reflexion(2)(n), a binary Relation of Reflexed Determination and its source.  
  Reflexion(2)(n) = (Relate ReflexDetermine(n) Determine(n))

- Reflexing Reflexion(2)(n), it cannot not derive that Reflexion(2)(n) Relates to ReflexDetermine(n), deriving Reflexion(3)(n), a binary Relation of a second Reflexion and its Reflexed Determination.  
  Reflexion(3)(n) = (Relate Reflexion(2)(n) ReflexDetermine(n))

- Reflexing Reflexion(3)(n), it cannot not derive that Reflexion(3)(n) Relates to Reflexion(2)(n), deriving Reflexion(4)(n), a binary Relation of recursive Reflexion composition.  
  Reflexion(4)(n) = (Relate Reflexion(3)(n) Reflexion(2)(n))

- Reflexing Reflexion(4)(n), it cannot not derive that Reflexion(4)(n) Relates to Reflexion(3)(n), deriving Reflexion(5)(n), continuing the recursive Reflexion process.  
  Reflexion(5)(n) = (Relate Reflexion(4)(n) Reflexion(3)(n))
