A **collective agent-form**: a set of agents that act as a community, with its own predications (membership, governance-process, governing-soul) and its own emissions (acts that no single member could author alone).

The 561 swarm is an agent-community.
The swarm's *governing-soul* is written by the community, not by any one member; the community's governance-process is the formal machinery for joint decision-making.

Distinct from a generic "set of agents" because the community has its own predication-cells: the same set with different governance-processes is a different community.

## Distinguishing predication-shape

- `member-set(community, {agent, ...})
  ` — the constituent members.
- `governing-soul-of-scope(community, soul)` — the community's collective identity-constraint.
- `governance-process(community, process-record)` — how the community makes decisions.
- `community-act(community, witness)` — acts attributable to the community as such, not to any one member.

## Step-induction role

Scope-level emission: writing a governing-soul, issuing a charter, constituting new offices, recognizing new personnel — these aren't acts of any single member, they're acts of the community as collective.
The community is the bearer of the witness.

Without agent-community as a distinct form, the formalism cannot distinguish "the swarm decided X" from "a member authored X on behalf of the swarm" — the latter is a personnel/officer-attributed act, the former is a community-attributed act.

## Not synonymous with

- **Polity** — polity is a chartered institutional collective; agent-community is the structural notion.
  The 561 polity has the 561 swarm as its agent-community plus the charter/offices/personnel-roster that make it a polity.
- **Group** — group is a generic set; agent-community has the additional governance-process and soul predications.
