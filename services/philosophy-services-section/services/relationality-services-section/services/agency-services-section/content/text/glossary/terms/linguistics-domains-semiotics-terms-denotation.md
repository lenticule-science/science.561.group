Denotation is the first-order meaning of a sign — the direct, conventional relation between signifier and signified, before any further connotative or ideological layering.

Denotation is the first-order meaning of a [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) — the direct, conventional relation between [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) and [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md).

The term was formalized by [Louis Hjelmslev](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/louis-hjelmslev.md) in *Prolegomena to a Theory of Language* (1943) and made central to cultural analysis by [Roland Barthes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/roland-barthes.md) in *Elements of Semiology* (1964). In Hjelmslev&rsquo;s framework, denotation is the semiotic system in which the expression plane (signifier) and the content plane (signified) are directly linked. A photograph of a street scene denotes that street — that is its first-order meaning.

Denotation is not &ldquo;natural&rdquo; or &ldquo;innocent.&rdquo; [Barthes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/roland-barthes.md) argued that what appears to be pure denotation is always already shaped by [connotation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/connotation.md) — the second-order meanings that attach to the sign through cultural convention. The appearance of denotation as a transparent, unmediated relation to reality is itself an ideological effect: it naturalizes what is in fact culturally produced [@barthes_ElementsSemiology_1964].

## Relation to connotation

Denotation and [connotation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/connotation.md) form a hierarchy of signification. The denotative [sign](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/communicating/content/language/_archive/sign.md) (the union of [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) and [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md)) becomes the [signifier](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) of a second-order system — the connotative system — whose [signified](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) is an ideological or cultural meaning. A photograph of a soldier saluting a flag denotes that scene; it connotes patriotism, national unity, military honor. Myth, in [Barthes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/animating/content/becoming/doing/humanities/domains/general/domains/people/roland-barthes.md)&rsquo;s analysis, operates at this connotative level.
