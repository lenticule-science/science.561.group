# Meaningful human control (AOP sub-theory level)

The sub-theory's semantic anchor for the **charter-authority concept** that governs embodied actuation in the polity-as-swarm.
The polity-root [meaningful-human-control object](../../../../../../../../../../objects/meaningful-human-control.md) realizes this concept as a substrate-decidable composite closure rule; the [meaningful-human-control-as-charter-c3-extension theorem](meaningful-human-control-as-charter-c3-extension/meaningful-human-control-as-charter-c3-extension.md) establishes the four-condition substrate-algebraic form.

## What the concept names

The polity's commitment that, for any workitem involving embodied actuation:

1. The workitem's outcome is auditable back to a captain-attested cell.
2. The workitem's actuation passes the safety-envelope precheck.
3. The workitem's authorization composes with a charter fragment of at least the workitem-class's required tier.
4. The dispatched office maintains persistent state continuity allowing captain interrupt-and-resume.

These four conditions are *not* a checklist of regulatory compliance items.
They are the substrate-algebraic decomposition of "meaningful human control" into Π₁-decidable closure rules, composed by knowledge-meet.
The polity declares what counts as MHC for *this* polity via charter-tier discipline; the substrate verifies it mechanically.

## Why this concept exists at the sub-theory level

The polity-root object adopts MHC as boundary discipline for the polity's `Cob_settling`.
The sub-theory's concept is the *upstream* statement at the AOP level — the agential-orchestration-platform's charter-authority concept that the polity instantiates.
Other polities (other instances of AOP) may adopt MHC differently (different fragment-tier table, different captain-attestation discipline); the AOP concept is parameterized over those choices, the polity object is one specific parameterization.

## Position

The concept anchors the sub-theory's central regulatory thesis: *governed autonomy by construction*.
Without MHC the polity has no substrate-side definition of the surveys' "appropriate human judgment"; with it, the definition is mechanical and audit-exportable.
