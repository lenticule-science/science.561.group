- [[define][[exclusioo]]]:: [[exclusion]] is the [[condition]] brought about by the [[act]] of [[exclude|excluding]]. It specifies that a [[triage/thing]] is held out of what is, marking it as absent within the [[domain of relating]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires that a [[triage/thing]]:
	- Be included in what it is (producing [[inclusion]]);
	- And be excluded from what it is not (producing [[exclusion]]).

- The [[act]] of [[exclude|excluding]] establishes the [[condition]] of [[exclusion]]:
	- This [[condition]] holds the [[triage/thing]] outside of the field of what is;
	- It is not merely a lack of presence but an **actively maintained absence**;
	- [[exclusion]] is therefore a **positive condition**—it marks and holds the [[triage/thing]] as not within.

- [[exclusion]] is transient unless stabilized by a [[structure]]:
	- As long as the [[act]] of [[exclude|excluding]] is active, [[exclusion]] persists;
	- To achieve durability, [[exclusion]] must be stabilized within a [[relative]] or other [[structure]].

- [[exclusion]] gains its coherence by being held together with [[inclusion]]:
	- This holding induces [[tension]];
	- [[tension]] ensures that [[exclusion]] is not isolated or meaningless but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [[inclusion]].

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that [[exist|exists]] is conditioned by both [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]];
- [[exclusion]] is essential for the maintenance of [[difference]]:
	- Without [[exclusion]], no [[triage/thing]] can be marked as distinct from what is not;
- [[exclusion]] participates in the basic [[relational dynamics]] that sustain [[exist|existing]] and all further relational architectures.
