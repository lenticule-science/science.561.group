# Mialon et al. 2023 — Augmented LM survey

Mialon, G. et al. (2023).
Augmented Language Models: a Survey. *Transactions on Machine Learning Research*. arXiv:2302.07842.

The comprehensive survey of *augmented language models* — LLMs equipped with reasoning enhancements (CoT, scratchpads), tool use (function calls, retrieval, code execution), and acting capacities.
The survey provides:

- **Taxonomic framework.** Reasoning, tools, acting as three augmentation classes.
- **Literature consolidation.** Coverage of major augmentation approaches as of early 2023.
- **Capability and risk analysis.** What augmentation enables and what risks it introduces.
- **Open problems.** Identification of gaps.

The survey is the standard reference for the augmented-LM landscape and serves as orientation for researchers entering the field.
It predates much subsequent work (function-calling APIs, computer use) but the taxonomy remains useful.
