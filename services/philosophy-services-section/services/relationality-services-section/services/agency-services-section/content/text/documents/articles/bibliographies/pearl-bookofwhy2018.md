# Pearl & Mackenzie, *The Book of Why* (2018)

Popular exposition of Pearl's causal framework. The
introduction-to-the-revolution book.

## What it covers

- The causal hierarchy: associational,
  interventional, counterfactual.
- Why statistics excluded causation and why that was
  a mistake.
- Causal graphs and the do-operator (informally).
- Counterfactual reasoning and its applications.
- Pearl's own intellectual history and the AI/ML
  context.
- Argument that AI cannot achieve robust intelligence
  without explicit causal machinery.

## Why it matters

Brought Pearl's framework to a broad audience and
framed the contemporary discussion of AI and
causality. The "ladder of causation" terminology
became standard. Pearl's challenge to ML — that scale
alone won't produce causal reasoning — has structured
the debate.

## How to read

Accessible prose. Worth reading for the framing and
historical context even if you've read Pearl's
technical work. Some technical claims are stated
loosely; check the textbook for precision.

## Related works

- Peters-Janzing-Schölkopf 2017 — modern textbook.
