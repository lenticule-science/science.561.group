The **hypertensor product** is a monoidal operation on [recognition fibers](./recognition-fiber.md) induced by trace concatenation in the [trace category](./trace-category.md). When two traces $t_1$ and $t_2$ are concatenated to form $t_1 \cdot t_2$, the hypertensor product combines their fiber algebras into a single fiber algebra for the concatenated trace.

Axiom A4 of the [QCHTTopos specification](../../documents/articles/notes/quasicrystalline-hypertensor-topos-specification.md) defines this operation. The hypertensor product gives the topos its characteristic "hypertensor" structure — the monoidal geometry induced by trace composition.
