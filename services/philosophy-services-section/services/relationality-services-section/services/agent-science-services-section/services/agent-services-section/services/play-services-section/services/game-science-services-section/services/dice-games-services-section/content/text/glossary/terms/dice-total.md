A dice total in [dice games](../index.md) is the sum of the face values shown after a roll [@britannica2026craps; @britannica2026backgammon].

Dice totals matter because many games read combinations through addition rather than through each die separately. That simple arithmetic creates the probability structure players learn to anticipate: some totals appear more often than others, and the game can attach meaning to that pattern.

## Related terms

- [roll](./roll.md) - The dice total is produced by a specific roll.
- [pip](./pip.md) - Totals are computed from the pip values on the dice.
- [Craps](../craps/index.md) - Craps assigns central meaning to specific totals such as 7 and 11.
