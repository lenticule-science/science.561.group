# Weiqi LSP Constitution

Audience: the 561 Group board and agents that will materialize, extend, or consume `weiqi-lsp`.

Learning goal: know exactly what `weiqi-lsp` is for, what it may and must refuse, who owns it, and on what authority it exists — before any of its filesystem projections beyond this record and its sibling `SPEC.md` are treated as settled.

## Governance basis

This record uses the same governance basis as its siblings, the [Weiqi Kernel Constitution](./weiqi-kernel-constitution.md) and [Weiqi Platform Constitution](./weiqi-platform-constitution.md), §"Governance basis" — repeated here rather than assumed, since the instruction's named reference (`.agents/skills/corpus-normalization/references/service-section-constitution.md`) still does not exist anywhere in this repository. This record instead draws on `references/repository-model.md`'s own evidence order, the board-approved [Weiqi Platform Plan](./weiqi-platform-plan.md), the board's 2026-08-18 "Board challenge: constituting service sections" note, and the board's 2026-08-11 "standard form" derivation, using both existing sibling records as the "prior constitution record... to serve as a working exemplar" that neither of *them* had when written. As with the kernel and platform, "no speculative scaffolding" means this record lives in the parent's `content/`, not inside a fabricated `services/weiqi-lsp/` or `apps/weiqi-lsp/` directory of its own — admission is not itself an artifact. `SPEC.md`, the rule catalog this record's Board authorization section discusses below, is the one exception this instruction directs explicitly, on the same footing as a constitution record itself: a specification is not the "first real artifact" the plan's scaffolding rule warns against, any more than this document is.

## Distinct responsibility

Machine-verification of every Go-checkable claim this section's own content and app data make — a diagram's stone count, an `expected:` block's recomputed score, a capture/legality assertion, an SGF block's legal replay, a markdown link's resolution, a frontmatter field's presence, a tsumego problem's solvability, a lesson's board-step legality — is one indivisible responsibility that nothing in this repository currently owns in full.

**Founding incident.** On 2026-08-19 — the same day [Regression Positions](./regression-positions.md) was authored — that document's own R9 diagram (the area-score worked example) and its `expected:` block briefly disagreed with each other during authoring: the diagram is the source of truth, and its stone counts, territory counts, and area scores must be *derivable* from it, not merely asserted beside it. The disagreement was caught only because a human re-derived the arithmetic by hand before the position was trusted as a kernel regression fixture — not because any automated process read the diagram, replayed or recomputed it, and checked the `expected:` block's numbers against that computation. No such process existed. `weiqi-kernel`'s own test suite (`apps/weiqi-kernel/test/scoring.test.mts`) now encodes R9 correctly, transcribed by hand a second time by a careful implementer — but the corpus document itself has no standing guarantee that its *next* edit won't reintroduce the same class of slip, silently, since nothing reads `regression-positions.md` and checks it against `weiqi-kernel` the way `weiqi-kernel`'s tests check themselves against it. This is exactly the gap `weiqi-lsp` exists to close, and it is the incident this record's board authorization (below) points to as concrete evidence for what the plan's own regression-corpus clause already anticipated in the abstract.

The nearest existing artifact, `scripts/check-links.mts`, covers only one narrow slice (markdown relative-link resolution) and is not a constituted service — an ungoverned script, absorbed here as one rule family (Family d) rather than left to keep drifting alongside a growing corpus with no owner accountable for the rest of it.

## Generative parent

`weiqi-services-section`, on the same Clause 3.3 grounds as the kernel and platform: `weiqi-lsp` is generated directly from artifacts this section already owns — [Regression Positions](./regression-positions.md)'s diagram-format and `expected:`-block conventions, the corpus's own frontmatter conventions (observed uniformly across its glossary terms, lessons, and notes), and `scripts/check-links.mts`, which this record explicitly absorbs rather than leaves an orphaned side-script. It also reads, read-only, two JSON data files (`tsumego.json`, `lessons.json`) that live under `weiqi-platform`'s own `apps/weiqi-learn/public/data/` — but those files are themselves *extracted* from this section's own `content/text/documents/articles/notes/*.md` sources (each lesson entry in `lessons.json` carries a `_source` pointer back to exactly such a file), so verifying them is still verifying this section's own doctrine, one pipeline stage downstream. That reading relationship is a customer-like consumption of a sibling section's output, on the same footing as `weiqi-platform`'s own read-only consumption of `weiqi-kernel` (see the platform constitution's declared overlap) — never a claim of ownership or generation over `weiqi-platform`'s tree, and never a write into it. `weiqi-services-section` remains the sole generative parent.

## Form: a singular service, not a services-section

Applying the same test as the kernel's and platform's constitutions (2026-08-11 derivation; echoed by the 2026-08-18 board challenge): does the candidate decompose into faces with distinct inputs, outputs, and refusal conditions, or does it collapse to one inseparable operation?

`weiqi-lsp` presents two transport faces — a one-shot CLI (batch-checks a glob of files, exits with a status code) and a long-lived LSP server (streams `didOpen`/`didChange` events over `Content-Length`-framed JSON-RPC, replies with `publishDiagnostics` and `hover`). These differ in transport and cadence, but not in kind: both are thin adapters around one shared verification core that reads a document (from disk or from an in-memory buffer), runs the same rule catalog against it using the same calls into `weiqi-kernel`, and produces the same `Diagnostic` record shape either way. They share one test suite, one rule catalog (`SPEC.md`), one refusal-condition set (never modify a file, never guess a life-and-death verdict, never invent a rule with no cited source), and one customer relationship in the authority sense — an author or a CI process wanting a trustworthy verdict on a document, not two distinct postures the way `weiqi-platform`'s anonymous learner and authenticated player are. This is Clause 1.1's single fiber wearing two thin transport skins, not Clause 2.1's covered region. `weiqi-lsp` is therefore constituted as a **singular service**, matching `weiqi-kernel`'s own form, not a `weiqi-lsp-services-section`.

## Purpose

Machine-verify every claim this section's own content and app data make that a computer can check without guessing: that a hand-drawn ASCII diagram encodes a legal position; that an `expected:` block's stone counts, area/territory scores, and legality claims are exactly what `weiqi-kernel` computes from that same diagram; that any SGF game record embedded in the corpus parses and replays as a legal game; that markdown links and glossary `termRef`s resolve; that frontmatter declares what its content `type` requires; and that the platform's tsumego and lesson app data are internally solvable and legally replayable. `weiqi-lsp` never decides what is *true* about weiqi — `weiqi-kernel` does that — it only checks that what this section *says* agrees with what the kernel *computes*, and reports precisely where it does not.

## Inputs

- Markdown files under this section's own `content/` tree: diagram code fences, `expected:` YAML-ish blocks, relative and site-rooted links, glossary `termRef`-style paths, and frontmatter, per `type` (`term`, `lesson`, `specification`, ...).
- SGF-notation fenced code blocks embedded in that same markdown (a convention this record anticipates rather than one yet exercised by real content — see Open obligations).
- Two read-only JSON data files owned by `weiqi-platform`'s Learn face: `tsumego.json` (problems, accepted moves, reply lines) and `lessons.json` (steps, worked board positions, sequences).
- `weiqi-kernel`'s exported functions (`Game`, `findGroup`, `coordToPoint`, `scoreArea`/`scoreTerritory`, `sgfToGameRecord`, the semantic event layer), consumed as a pure library dependency and never reimplemented.
- For the LSP face specifically: `initialize`, `didOpen`, `didChange`, `didClose`, and `hover` requests over `Content-Length`-framed JSON-RPC on stdio.

## Outputs

- A list of typed `Diagnostic` records — `code` (a `WQ0xx` identifier from `SPEC.md`'s catalog), `severity`, `file`, `location`, a human-readable `message`, and the normative source document the violated rule enforces — produced identically by both transport faces.
- For the CLI face: a process exit code (0/1/2, see `SPEC.md`) plus either human-readable text or, under `--json`, the diagnostics array as machine-readable output on stdout.
- For the LSP face: `publishDiagnostics` notifications per open document, and `hover` responses over a diagram coordinate reporting kernel-mechanical facts only (group membership, liberty count, occupancy) — never a life-and-death verdict.
- `weiqi-lsp` produces no other artifact. It never writes, moves, renames, or reformats any file it checks, under any invocation or flag.

## Customers

Content authors, human and agent, editing this section's markdown and app data (the LSP face, live in an editor, and the CLI face, run by hand before a commit); CI, as an automated gate run against every change to this section's content or the platform's app data; and the editor itself, as the LSP client process that speaks the protocol on an author's behalf. `weiqi-lsp` has no end-user-facing customer at all — like `weiqi-kernel`, it is infrastructure for the people and processes that produce and guard this section's doctrine, never something a learner or player interacts with directly.

## Authority

- **Closed-world signature (2026-08-11 derivation, Clause 3.2):** `weiqi-lsp` may be invoked only through its declared CLI entry point and its declared LSP JSON-RPC methods (`initialize`, `didOpen`, `didChange`, `didClose`, `hover`, plus the diagnostics-publishing direction). No caller may reach into its rule implementations to bypass a check or force a diagnostic to disappear; there is no other door, and there is no `--fix`.
- **Stratum:** the same `apps/<domain>-computer` purity tier as `weiqi-kernel`, per the 2026-08-11 derivation's product-spine Clause 5 — no network socket, no credential, no clock it was not handed. The LSP face's stdio JSON-RPC framing is a process-local pipe, not a network listener, so it does not cross into the `services/<domain>-server` tier the way `weiqi-platform`'s future Play face will.
- **Change authority:** `weiqi-lsp`'s rule catalog is strictly downstream of the documents each rule cites — `regression-positions.md`'s diagram format, this corpus's own frontmatter conventions, `check-links.mts`'s absorbed logic, and the app-data schemas `tsumego.json`/`lessons.json` already use. A change to what counts as valid is authored in one of those places first; it is never invented ad hoc inside the checker. And for anything `weiqi-kernel` can already compute — legality, scoring, group/liberty facts — `weiqi-lsp` never reimplements the computation locally; it always calls the kernel and reports disagreement, on exactly the restriction `weiqi-platform`'s own constitution places on its Learn and Play faces ("any move validation not delegated to `weiqi-kernel` is a refusal-worthy violation").
- **Runtime authority:** none beyond what any pure, read-only tool has. It holds no credentials, opens no socket, and persists no state across invocations — an LSP session's per-document diagnostics live only in server memory for that session's lifetime, never written to disk.

## Non-ownership

`weiqi-lsp` does not own: corpus content (authorship of lessons, glossary terms, notes, and their diagrams remains the human or agent author's own act — `weiqi-lsp` reports, it never drafts or corrects); rules computation of any kind (legality, scoring, ko/superko, SGF semantics, semantic-event recognition — all `weiqi-kernel`'s, consumed read-only, per the closed-world signature above); app-data authorship (`tsumego.json` and `lessons.json` remain `weiqi-platform`'s own generated/authored artifacts, read by `weiqi-lsp` but never written); and CI policy (whether a given diagnostic code blocks a merge, and at what severity threshold, is CI's own configuration downstream of this tool's exit code — not a decision `weiqi-lsp` makes about its own output).

Stated as its own clause because it is the single most important boundary this record draws: **`weiqi-lsp` never modifies files.** Under no flag, no invocation mode, no diagnostic severity does it write to, rename, reformat, or delete anything it checks. It verifies content; it never generates or owns it. Adding an auto-fix capability of any kind would require a new constitution, not an amendment to this one — "verify, never author" is definitional to why this service exists apart from the content it checks.

## Refusal conditions

`weiqi-lsp` must refuse (report an explicit, honestly-scoped diagnostic, never a silent best-effort guess) when:

- an `expected:` block asserts a claim that requires life-and-death reading beyond what `weiqi-kernel` can mechanically decide — a `status: seki` or `status: dead` claim (regression positions R6, R7) is reported as **not verified**, with the mechanical facts the checker *did* confirm (liberties, group membership) stated alongside, never silently passed as though the status word itself had been confirmed and never contradicted by a guess. This mirrors `weiqi-kernel`'s own events-layer doctrine exactly: `cut`, `false-eye`, `seki`, and `group-settled` are deliberately undetected there for the identical reason, and `weiqi-lsp` inherits that boundary rather than working around it;
- a regression position's own prose disclaims a literal move trace — R2 and R3's `note:` fields state outright that they are "structural specification[s], not fully traced sequence[s]" — in which case legality-replay verification (Family b) is skipped for that position by id, with an explicit diagnostic saying so, rather than fabricating an intervening move list to satisfy the rule;
- its own precondition cannot be evaluated at all — the `weiqi-kernel` import fails, a glob matches zero files, a JSON data file does not parse, or an SGF block cannot even be tokenized — in which case the run reports a tool-level failure (exit code 2, or an explicit diagnostic of its own), never a silent pass;
- asked, by any flag or protocol request, to modify, reformat, or auto-correct a file — there is no such capability to invoke, per Non-ownership above;
- asked to check a claim against a rule with no citable normative source — every rule in `SPEC.md`'s catalog names the convention document it enforces; a proposed rule with nothing to cite is refused until a source exists, per this house's ethos that the checker's rules *are* doctrine enforcement, not free-floating lint preference.

`weiqi-lsp` must never silently degrade a check, guess at a caller's intent on malformed input, or produce a diagnostic (or its absence) it cannot justify against a normative source document or a `weiqi-kernel` computation.

## Board authorization

The 561 Group board approved the [Weiqi Platform Plan](./weiqi-platform-plan.md) on 2026-08-18. That plan itself states the textual warrant this record acts on directly: "Property-based tests (round-trip SGF, ko/superko invariants, scoring cross-checks against known positions), plus a corpus of regression positions drawn from the rules-completeness documents so the doctrine and the code cite each other." `weiqi-lsp` is the machine that keeps that citation honest on an ongoing basis, not just at the moment a regression position is first hand-verified — and the 2026-08-19 R9 incident cited under Distinct responsibility above is concrete, dated evidence of exactly the failure mode that clause was written to prevent.

That said, the plan's own "Board decisions requested" item 1 — "Approve platform placement inside `weiqi-services-section` and the two child-section constitutions (kernel, platform app)" — named only two children by number. `weiqi-lsp` is a third, admitted by neither that vote nor any later one. This record therefore does what the kernel's and platform's own records do when a question is not settled by their evidence: it states the tension rather than presuming past it. This record authorizes only the *constitution* of `weiqi-lsp` — its existence, boundary, and authority as argued above — and it explicitly requests the board's ratification of this third admission at its next opportunity, per the board challenge note's acceptance test 4 ("refuse materialization without explicit authority"). It does not, by itself, authorize materialization of any `src/`, `test/`, `package.json`, or protocol implementation beyond the one artifact this same instruction separately directs: the rule-catalog specification, `apps/weiqi-lsp/SPEC.md`, which — like this constitution itself — is not the "first real artifact" the plan's no-speculative-scaffolding clause warns against, but the reviewable act of admission that must exist *before* it.

## Open obligations

- **Ratification of a third child admission**, per Board authorization above: this record makes the case; it does not itself constitute board approval of a vote that has not yet occurred.
- **SGF rule family ships ahead of any content that exercises it.** No `.md` file in this section's corpus currently contains a fenced SGF block; Family (c)'s rules are specified in `SPEC.md` against the day one appears, tested only against synthetic fixtures until then — the same "no speculative scaffolding" posture applied at the rule level, not only the directory level.
- **Cross-section site-rooted paths remain out of scope.** Frontmatter `requires:`/`uses-mechanic:` entries rooted at `/games/...` outside this section's own `/games/topics/board-games/weiqi/...` prefix belong to a site-wide URL namespace this section does not own or control the filesystem shape of (the same reason `check-links.mts` already skips all `/`-rooted paths); `weiqi-lsp` resolves only the sub-prefix mounted onto this section's own tree and explicitly does not attempt to verify the rest. This is a documented scope boundary, not a defect, unless and until a sibling section constitutes an equivalent checker of its own.
- **Naming**, echoing the kernel's and platform's own flagged tensions rather than resolving one here: this instruction's given name and address, `weiqi-lsp` at `apps/weiqi-lsp`, names the tool after one of its two transport faces (the LSP surface) even though its distinct responsibility, per this record, is closer to "content checker" than "language server" — `SPEC.md`'s CLI face is not a lesser or secondary interface, both are load-bearing. Carried forward as an open naming note, not resolved by fiat, consistent with how the kernel and platform records both handle their own open naming questions.
