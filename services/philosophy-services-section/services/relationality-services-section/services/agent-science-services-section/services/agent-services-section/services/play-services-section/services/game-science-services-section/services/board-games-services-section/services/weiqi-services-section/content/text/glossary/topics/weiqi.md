# Weiqi

Weiqi is a domain within Board Games.

This page is the start-here index for everything this section holds about weiqi — the sequenced lesson curriculum and the dependency-linked glossary — per the [Weiqi Platform Plan](../../documents/articles/notes/weiqi-platform-plan.md)'s "engine is the executable form of the glossary" thesis.

## Lessons

Read in this order; each lesson names what comes next.

1. [Weiqi Overview](../../documents/articles/notes/weiqi-overview.md) — liberties, capture, territory, and ko, via one worked example.
2. [Living Groups and Eyes](../../documents/articles/notes/living-groups-and-eyes.md) — why connection and eye shape determine whether a group lives or dies.
3. [Komi and Handicap](../../documents/articles/notes/komi-and-handicap.md) — how even games and uneven games are balanced.
4. [Ending the Game](../../documents/articles/notes/ending-the-game.md) — passing, dead-stone settlement, scoring both ways, and why area and territory rarely differ.
5. [History of Weiqi](../../documents/articles/notes/weiqi-history.md) — origins in China, transmission across East Asia, and modern international play. (A parallel entry, not a prerequisite for the other four.)
