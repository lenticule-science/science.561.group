The [semiotic](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2026-06-17-emsemioverse-terms-index.md) footprint of a [handle](handle.md) $\tau$ in state $X$ is the closed partial state obtained by applying the composite [closure operator](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2026-06-17-emsemioverse-terms-index.md) $\mathcal{S}$ to the [handle fragment](handle-fragment.md): $\mathrm{Foot}_X(\tau) = \mathcal{S}_X(F_{\tau,X})$. The footprint formalizes [Peirce's](../../../../../../../../../../../../../library/references/people/charles-sanders-peirce.md) pragmatic maxim: the meaning of a [handle](handle.md) is the totality of its conceivable practical effects — the full semantic, syntactic, and [fusion](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/semiotic-universe/terms/fusion.md) closure of everything generated by the handle's interactions.

The footprint is always bounded by the core semioverse state X*: for any X <= X* and any tau, Foot_X(tau) <= X*. This boundedness ensures that no single handle's interactions can produce semantic material beyond what the semioverse's [least fixed point](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2026-06-17-emsemioverse-terms-index.md) contains. The footprint is also monotone in the [interaction surface](interaction-surface.md): more interactions produce a larger or equal footprint.

Footprints are the objects compared by the [delta](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/delta.md) operation (Delta_foot(tau, tau'; X) = S_X(Delta_frag(F_{tau,X}, F_{tau',X}))) and diminished by [subtraction](subtraction.md). They can be quantified by an evaluation valuation mu(tau; X) = mu(Foot_X(tau)), allowing quantitative comparison of how much semantic material different handles contribute to the semioverse.
