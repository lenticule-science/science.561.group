With [interaction surfaces](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-surface.md) in place, we can define how a handle affects the meaning domain and how to compare or remove that influence. These three operations formalize the pragmatic dimension of [semiosis](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/semiosis.md): meanings have consequences (footprints), those consequences can be compared (delta), and influence can be disciplinedly removed (subtraction).

## Footprint

The [footprint](../../../glossary/terms/footprint.md) of a handle is the closure of its [handle fragment](../../../glossary/terms/handle-fragment.md) under the state's operators — the total meaning the handle acquires through all its [semiotic](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) encounters. As a physical footprint [indexes](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/index-sign.md) the passage of a body, the semiotic footprint indexes the passage of a handle through the sign system.

## Delta

[Delta](../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/delta.md) formalizes semiotic difference at three levels: syntactic (which interactions differ), semantic-local (which fragment elements differ), and semantic-closed (what difference remains after closure). This captures how [signs](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) acquire value through contrast.

## Subtraction

[Subtraction](../../../glossary/terms/subtraction.md) formalizes semiotic forgetting — disciplined removal of semantic influence. It is defined using [Heyting](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/arend-heyting.md) pseudo-complement and fragment generation so that removal stays within intuitionistic logic and the closure discipline.
