The interactive semioverse draws on both semantic and [semiotic](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) traditions, specifically those that align with a relational ontology — where relations are prior to entities. The mathematical structures correspond to concepts in [Peircean](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/peircean-semiotics.md) semiotics: indexicality, habit, unlimited semiosis, and the pragmatic maxim.

## Semantics

The Heyting algebra $H$ provides the semantic domain — a constructive logic of meanings. The implication $a \Rightarrow b$ captures constructive entailment; the modal closure $j$ formalizes [habit](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/habit.md)-formation; the trace $G$ formalizes semiotic provenance. These are the semantic structures that the interactive layer operates on.

## Semiotics

The interactive layer formalizes relational engagement through semiotic concepts: [handles](../../../glossary/terms/handle.md) as Peircean [indices](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/index-sign.md) (signs connected by existential relation), [interaction surfaces](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-surface.md) as relational boundaries, failure as [Peirce's](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/charles-sanders-peirce.md) doubt (interruption of [habit](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/habit.md)), provenance as unlimited [semiosis](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/semiosis.md), evaluation as the pragmatic maxim (meaning as practical consequences), and subtraction as disciplined removal of relational influence.

## Application

[Handles](../../../glossary/terms/handle.md), [interaction terms](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-term.md), and [footprints](../../../glossary/terms/footprint.md) formalize the triadic structure of [semiosis](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/semiosis.md) in an interactive context: the handle is the [representamen](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/representamen.md) (sign vehicle), the interaction term is the act of interpretation, and the footprint is the [interpretant](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) (meaning produced) — closed under the sign system's full discipline.
