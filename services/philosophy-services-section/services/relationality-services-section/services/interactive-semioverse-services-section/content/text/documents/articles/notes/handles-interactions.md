The [interactive semioverse](../index.md) introduces handles as [indexical](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/index-sign.md) references and defines a calculus of interactions that generate semantic material — formalizing how [signs](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) engage with what lies outside the sign system.

## Handles

A [handle](../../../glossary/terms/handle.md) is a persistent [indexical](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/index-sign.md) reference to a thing. It is not itself an element of the meaning domain $H$; it is an external pointer mapped into $H$ by a seed function $\eta$. This formalizes Peirce's [index](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/index-sign.md): a [sign](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) connected to its object by existential relation rather than convention or resemblance.

## Interaction terms

[Interaction terms](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-term.md) are structured acts of [semiosis](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/semiosis.md): expressions built from [handles](../../../glossary/terms/handle.md) and definable operators whose interpretation produces elements of $H$. They are the [semiotic](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) encounters through which handles generate meaning.

## Interaction surfaces

An [interaction surface](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-surface.md) assigns a finite set of [interaction terms](../../../glossary/terms/interaction-term.md) to each handle. It is the relational boundary across which a handle's engagement with what lies outside the sign system generates meaning within it.
