## Ideas for Skills

- resolve-context: Determine the active semantic and normative context for a given path or file by identifying applicable READMEs, specifications, and agent contracts.
- locate-governing-docs: Traverse the repository to find which readmes, SPECIFICATIONS.md files, or contracts govern a given document or directory.
- enumerate-applicable-specs: List all specifications that constrain a document or task based on location, kind, and declared scope.
- check-spec-compliance: Validate a document against its governing specifications and report missing required fields or structural violations.
- query-library-by-metadata: Query the library using structured metadata such as kind, id, tags, authors, roles, goals, and dates instead of raw text search.
- find-agents-by-role-goal: Identify agents that declare specific roles or goals for use in planning and task assignment.
- analyze-role-goal-coverage: Compare roles and goals required by plans against those declared by agents to surface gaps or redundancies.
- inventory-library-structure: Produce a high-level map of the library showing domains, kinds, and counts to support orientation and auditing.
- distinguish-interface-vs-spec: Determine whether a document is normative (specification), operational (skill), contextual (readme), or non-normative (interface).
- synthesize-documents-under-constraints: Create a new document by synthesizing multiple artifacts while respecting applicable specifications and declared goals.
- infer-document-kind: Infer the appropriate kind for a document based on its location, structure, and content when frontmatter is missing or ambiguous.
- normalize-frontmatter: Rewrite or repair frontmatter to conform to applicable specifications while preserving author intent.
- detect-duplicate-ids: Scan the library to identify duplicate or colliding ids and report their locations and contexts.
- resolve-id-references: Verify that all id-based links and references resolve to existing documents and report broken or ambiguous targets.
- extract-skill-dependencies: Identify which skills are implicitly or explicitly required by a plan, specification, or agent definition.
- trace-skill-usage: Find where a given skill is referenced, required, or implied across plans, agents, and specifications.
- audit-tag-consistency: Analyze tag usage across the library to surface drift, redundancy, and undocumented tags.
- infer-missing-tags: Suggest tags for documents based on content, kind, and neighboring artifacts.
- detect-orphan-documents: Identify documents that are not referenced by any readme, plan, specification, or index.
- generate-index-readme: Produce or update a README that accurately summarizes and links the contents of a directory.
- diff-semantic-changes: Compare two versions of a document and describe changes in terms of meaning and specification impact, not just text.
- detect-stale-plans: Identify plans whose assumptions, required roles, or referenced artifacts no longer exist or have changed.
- validate-agent-contracts: Check that agent definitions satisfy the requirements of their governing agent specifications.
- simulate-skill-execution: Dry-run a skill to report intended reads, writes, and effects without mutating the library.
- surface-spec-gaps: Identify areas where behavior is implied by practice or interface but not specified normatively.
- track-document-lineage: Record and query the provenance of a document, including source, transformations, and agent actions that produced it.
- annotate-assumptions: Extract or require explicit assumptions from plans, specifications, and designs to support later invalidation.
- detect-assumption-drift: Identify when referenced assumptions no longer hold due to changes elsewhere in the library.
- summarize-domain-intent: Produce a concise statement of purpose for a domain by synthesizing its readmes, specs, and plans.
- align-plan-to-spec: Verify that a plan’s stated goal and steps are permitted and supported by applicable specifications.
- estimate-plan-scope: Infer rough scope, dependency count, and risk signals for a plan based on referenced artifacts.
- identify-cross-domain-couplings: Detect when a document in one domain implicitly depends on artifacts or assumptions in another.
- flag-spec-implementation-gaps: Identify specifications that have no corresponding skills, scripts, or enforcement mechanisms.
- map-skill-to-script-coverage: Report which skills are purely declarative versus backed by executable scripts.
- monitor-library-health: Produce a periodic report summarizing integrity issues, drift, missing coverage, and growth trends.
- gate-writes-by-context: Prevent or warn on writes that violate the active context’s specifications or contracts.
- generate-review-queues: Create prioritized queues of documents needing review based on drift, age, or dependency changes.
- detect-normative-conflicts: Identify conflicting requirements across multiple applicable specifications.
- reconcile-conflicting-specs: Propose resolution strategies or precedence rules for overlapping or conflicting specifications.
- snapshot-semioverse-state: Capture a labeled snapshot of the library for reference, rollback, or comparison.

## 3D Printed Seed Drying Box

A 3D-printed box with small perforations and a stable base so it can stand upright in drafty places like a windowsill.
See `/library/ideas/3d-printed-seed-drying-box.md`.

## 3D Printed Gridfinity/Skadis Triangle Plate System

Two plates, one Gridfinity and one Skadis, that slot together to form triangles.
The bottom edges follow Gridfinity and the sides follow Skadis, enabling angled drawers and hanging storage that interface with Skadis boards.

See `/library/ideas/gridfinity-skadis-triangle-plate-system.md`.
