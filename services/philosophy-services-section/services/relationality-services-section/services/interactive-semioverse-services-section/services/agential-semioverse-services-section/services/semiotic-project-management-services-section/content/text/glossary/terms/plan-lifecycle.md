The **plan lifecycle** is the sequence of states a plan passes
through: draft, proposed, accepted, active, completed. It is an
evidence chain, not an authority chain — each transition requires
evidence, not permission.

- **Draft -> Proposed** (agent gate): the plan has clear steps,
  measurable acceptance criteria, and an identified
  [closure condition](./closure-condition.md).
- **Proposed -> Accepted** (emsenn gate): the goal is worth pursuing
  and the approach is reasonable.
- **Accepted -> Active** (agent gate): dependencies are met, the
  agent has context and tools.
- **Active -> Completed** (evidence gate): acceptance criteria are
  met with evidence, outcome is assessed.

The lifecycle format is specified in
[plans/index.md](../../../agential-semioverse-repository/plans/index.md).
