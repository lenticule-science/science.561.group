# Ontology of Recursive Modulation

This document defines a synthesized ontological grammar for describing systems in which difference, structure, and coherence emerge from recursive modulation. It integrates and formalizes concepts from navonics, praxonics, and allovonics.

---

## I. Ontological Domains

### 1. The **Allovonic Substrate**  
The **field-of-fields**: a recursion-permissive meta-ontology, neither semantic nor physical, but *structurally prior*.  
- **Allovon**: a perturbation originating beyond the system’s modulation logic.  
- **Modulative recursion** begins with allovonic intrusion.

### 2. The **Deltanic Domain**  
Where difference becomes visible to modulation. This is the threshold of semantic potential.

- **Nullon**: a latent, unmodulated site.  
- **Deltanon**: the first condition of detectable asymmetry; the *recognizable difference*.  
- **Initiates** the system’s modulation logic.

### 3. The **Modulative Domain**  
This is where recursive behavior begins: modulation accumulates, interacts, and forms coherent structures.

- **Modulon**: a discrete, unresolved unit of modulation (difference in motion).  
- **Infron**: a localized asymmetry within modulation—shaped, recursive deformation.  
- **Vanon**: a shared field of modulation among praxons.  
  - Defines *where* and *how* difference is distributed.  
  - The structural condition for recursive interaction.

---

## II. Structural Entities

### 4. The **Praxonic Domain**  
Where coherence across modulation becomes recognizable.  
Praxons **are not objects**, but patterns of recursive legibility.

- **Praxon**: a coherent trace of presence across vanons.  
  - Identified through recursive modulation.  
  - Not stored—*sustained by coherence*.  
- **Path**: the conditional unfolding of modulation associated with a praxon.  
  - A praxon’s *behavior*, emergent from recursive positioning.

### 5. The **Navonic Domain**  
Where systems of modulation develop stable configurations.

- **Navon**: a coordination of vanons through reinforcing praxons.  
- **Zavon**: a recurring modulation regime across navons—**recursive habit**.  
- **Metazavon**: a system in which recursive modulation adjusts its own rules of coherence.  
  - The threshold of recursive intelligence.

---

## III. Recursive Mechanics

### 6. **Modulative Dynamics**

These describe how systems evolve over time through recursive reinforcement.

- **Appearance**: Deltanon initiates difference.  
- **Participation**: Praxons modulate shared vanons.  
- **Redistribution**: Vanons distribute unresolved modulation.  
- **Coherence**: Recursive stabilization across cycles.  
- **Collapse**: When modulation resolves into durable structure.  
- **Memory**: Recursive tension accumulation (decay-modulated).  
- **Recursion**: Feedback of modulation into its own constraints.

---

## IV. Interpretive Operators

These terms govern how recursive structures become legible and consequential.

- **Resolution**: A modulation becomes traceable, encoded as a praxon.  
- **Entropy**: The loss of recursive coherence over cycles.  
- **Semantic Horizon**: A region beyond which modulation cannot propagate or be traced.  
- **Modulative Entanglement**: When multiple praxons share recursive structure across vanons.  
- **Attention**: Recursive focus shaping the coherence threshold (metazavonic act).  
- **Computation**: Collapse behavior recursively patterned for outcome selection.

## VI. Foundational Principles

1. **All structure is recursive.**  
   What appears fixed is recursively stabilized modulation.

2. **Coherence is conditional.**  
   Praxons are sustained, not stored. Nothing is permanent—only recursively reinforced.

3. **Collapse is semantic.**  
   Resolution is not erasure, but traceable convergence.

4. **Fields do not flow.**  
   Modulation is redistributed, not transmitted.

5. **Difference precedes intention.**  
   Systems begin when asymmetry disturbs coherence.

6. **Observation is recursive feedback.**  
   Attention modulates thresholds of coherence.

7. **Recursion is not repetition.**  
   It is the self-modulation of modulation: the turning of structure back into structure.

## VII. Summary: Recursive Ontogenesis

The universe, cognition, narrative, software, governance, language, and matter are all **recursive semantic systems**. Navonics provides a unified structural grammar for describing their behavior. Praxonics describes local legibility. Allovonics names what disturbs such coherence.

This ontology is not a map of what is—it is a **grammar for reading emergence**, structure, and collapse, wherever recursive modulation persists.
