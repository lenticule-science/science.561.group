# Theorem (Context Dynamics)

With acts

- `ContextFlow = (FlowProfile RecognizeContext)`,
- `ContextNucleus = (NucleateProfile RecognizeContext)`,
- `ContextGeometry = (ContextFlow ContextNucleus)`,
- `ContextDynamics = (ContextNucleus ContextFlow)`,

we have:

1. **Contextual evolution.**  
   `ContextFlow` evolves contexts along the induced profile flow.

2. **Context collapse.**  
   `ContextNucleus` collapses contexts whose variation lies entirely in
   nucleus-stable trace shuffles.

3. **Geometric structure.**  
   `ContextGeometry` packages the flow–nucleus compatibility into a coherent
   contextual geometry.

4. **Invariant contextual dynamics.**  
   `ContextDynamics` identifies the stabilised contexts: those unchanged by
   further flow–nucleus interplay.

## Proof sketch

These statements follow from the profile compatibility between flow, nucleus,
and trace shapes established earlier; restricting the construction to contexts
preserves the same coherence laws.
