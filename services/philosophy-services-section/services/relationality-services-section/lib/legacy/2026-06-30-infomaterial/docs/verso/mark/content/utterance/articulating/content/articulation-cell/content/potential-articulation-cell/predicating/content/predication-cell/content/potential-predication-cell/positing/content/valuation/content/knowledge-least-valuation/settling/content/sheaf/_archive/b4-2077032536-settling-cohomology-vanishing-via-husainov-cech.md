# Settling cohomology vanishes above degree 1 — Husainov–Čech path

Alternative proof of [settling cohomology vanishes above degree 1](settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) via direct Čech computation under Husainov acyclicity, complementing the [Leray spectral sequence proof](settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md). Distinct chain of reasoning: where the Leray approach uses derived functors and projects through the base poset, this approach uses Čech cohomology directly and identifies it with derived cohomology only at the end via the Čech-derived comparison.

## Statement

The same statement: for the [settling-associated sheaf](../objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_U$ (and its paraconsistent variant) on the [bitopological settling site](../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md), under the standing hypothesis $\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$,

$$H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_U) \;=\; 0 \qquad \text{for all } n \geq 2.$$

## Proof

The argument has three steps: (i) compute Čech cohomology directly and show it vanishes above degree 1, (ii) verify Husainov acyclicity holds on the bitopological site, (iii) apply the Čech-derived comparison to conclude.

### Step 1: direct Čech computation

The bitopological settling site has **fiber-only covers** (per [why fiber covers only](../narratives/why-fiber-covers-only.md)): covering sieves at $(q, \tau, U)$ are families of pairwise-Stone clopen inclusions $\{U_i \hookrightarrow U\}$ at fixed $(q, \tau)$, generated by joint covering of $U$ in the pairwise-Stone topology of $X_q^\tau$. There are no covering families crossing base objects.

For a cover $\{U_i \to U\}$ at $(q, \tau)$ and a sheaf $\mathcal{F}_U$, the Čech complex is

$$\check{C}^n(\{U_i\}, \mathcal{F}_U) \;=\; \prod_{i_0 < \cdots < i_n} \mathcal{F}_U(q, \tau, U_{i_0} \cap \cdots \cap U_{i_n}).$$

By the [settling-associated sheaf](../objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md)'s definition, $\mathcal{F}_U(q, \tau, V) = \mathbb{Z}^{\Delta^{+,*}_\tau(q) \cap V}$ — free abelian on the *discrete* set of newly-added designated points lying in $V$.

For a discrete subset $S \subseteq X_q^\tau$ and a pairwise-Stone cover $\{U_i \to U\}$ of $U$, the Čech complex of the sheaf $V \mapsto \mathbb{Z}^{S \cap V}$ decomposes as a direct sum over points of $S \cap U$:

$$\check{C}^\bullet(\{U_i\}, \mathbb{Z}^{S \cap -}) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{x \in S \cap U} \check{C}^\bullet(\{U_i\}, \mathbb{Z}_x)$$

where $\mathbb{Z}_x$ is the skyscraper at $x$. For each such $x$, $x$ lies in some collection of cover elements $\{U_i : x \in U_i\} = \{U_{i_1}, \ldots, U_{i_{k_x}}\}$. The Čech complex of $\mathbb{Z}_x$ on the cover restricted to this collection is

$$\check{C}^n(\{U_{i_1}, \ldots, U_{i_{k_x}}\}, \mathbb{Z}_x) = \binom{k_x}{n+1} \mathbb{Z}$$

with the alternating-sum coboundary. This is the cochain complex of the simplex $\Delta^{k_x - 1}$, whose cohomology is concentrated in degree 0:

$$\check{H}^n(\{U_i\}, \mathbb{Z}_x) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & n = 0 \\ 0 & n \geq 1. \end{cases}$$

Direct-summing over all $x \in S \cap U$:

$$\check{H}^n(\{U_i\}, \mathcal{F}_U|_{(q,\tau)}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}^{|S \cap U|} & n = 0 \\ 0 & n \geq 1. \end{cases}$$

So *fiber* Čech cohomology of $\mathcal{F}_U$ vanishes in every positive degree, regardless of regime. $\square$ (Step 1, fiber direction)

For the **base direction** ($\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$), there are no covers, so the Čech complex degenerates to functor cohomology: $H^p$ of the base poset with values in the fiber cohomology presheaf. By the standing hypothesis $\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$ and the linearity of $\lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$ (with $\mathrm{cd}(\lambda^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$), the base contributes at most degree 1.

Combining: total Čech vanishes for $n \geq 2$. $\square$ (Step 1)

### Step 2: Husainov acyclicity on the bitopological site

The [Husainov-acyclicity](../concepts/husainov-acyclicity.md) condition for Čech-derived comparison requires the Dedekind–MacNeille cut posets of the underlying base poset to be cohomologically acyclic.

For $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$:

- $\lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a linear ordinal; its Dedekind–MacNeille completion is a chain (with $\infty$ added at the top), and chains have trivial sub-poset cohomology in degree $\geq 1$. Husainov acyclicity holds in the temporal direction.

- $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ for predication categories' standard predicate categories (built from the four predicate generators on a finite predicate set): $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a finite poset under the dependency-induced order, with order-complex dimension $\leq 1$. Its Dedekind–MacNeille completion is again a finite poset, whose cuts are again chains and antichains. Both are acyclic. Husainov acyclicity holds.

The product $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$: Husainov acyclicity is preserved under products of acyclic posets (Dedekind–MacNeille completions distribute over products up to natural identification, and acyclicity of factors implies acyclicity of products at the level of cuts). So Husainov acyclicity holds on the base.

For the *fiber* direction: pairwise-Stone clopens of a paired Priestley space form a Boolean algebra, which is the simplest case of a Dedekind–MacNeille-complete poset (every clopen has a complement). Husainov acyclicity is automatic on Boolean algebras. $\square$ (Step 2)

### Step 3: Čech-derived comparison

By the [Čech-derived comparison theorem](cech-derived-comparison-on-bitopological-site.md), under Husainov acyclicity (verified Step 2),

$$\check{H}^n(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \sigma}, \mathcal{F}_U) \;\cong\; H^n(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \sigma}, \mathcal{F}_U)$$

for every bounded truncation $\sigma$. The Čech vanishing from Step 1 transfers to derived vanishing: $H^n(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \sigma}, \mathcal{F}_U) = 0$ for $n \geq 2$ at every bounded stage.

By the direct-limit definition of unbounded settling cohomology and AB5-exactness of filtered colimits in $\mathbf{Ab}$, the unbounded $H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_U)$ is the colimit of bounded $H^n$'s, hence also vanishes for $n \geq 2$. $\square$ (Step 3)

Combining Steps 1–3: the Husainov–Čech path establishes the same vanishing as the Leray spectral sequence path, by an independent chain of reasoning. $\blacksquare$

## Where the two paths agree and differ

Both paths use:
- The fiber-only covers structure of the bitopological settling site.
- The discrete-support structure of $\mathcal{F}_U$ (skyscraper-on-discrete decomposition).
- The standing hypothesis $\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$.

The Leray path: uses derived functors and the Leray spectral sequence for the projection $\pi : \mathbf{T}^* \to \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$. Vanishing of $R^q\pi_*$ via skyscraper-cohomology lemma; collapse of the SS at $E_2$.

The Čech path: computes Čech directly via the simplex-cochain decomposition for skyscraper sheaves on cover refinements; identifies Čech with derived via Husainov acyclicity (separately verified).

The two paths use mutually independent technical machinery (spectral sequences vs. Čech complexes), arrive at the same conclusion, and depend on different chains of cited lemmas. If a future audit finds an error in one of the paths, the other gives independent confirmation of the result.

## Open

- **Verifying Husainov acyclicity for higher-dimensional predicate categories.** The proof's Step 2 uses $\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$ to get acyclicity on the base. For predicate categories with $\mathrm{cd} \geq 2$ (modal extensions, higher-arity), Husainov acyclicity needs separate verification, and the Čech-derived comparison may fail.
- **Cosheaf-side Čech computation.** A dual statement for the Verdier-dual cosheaf $\check{\mathcal{F}}_v$ would close the picture. The cosheaf side has its own Čech-coČech machinery; the dual vanishing should hold by the same skyscraper-supported argument, but with chains in place of cochains.
