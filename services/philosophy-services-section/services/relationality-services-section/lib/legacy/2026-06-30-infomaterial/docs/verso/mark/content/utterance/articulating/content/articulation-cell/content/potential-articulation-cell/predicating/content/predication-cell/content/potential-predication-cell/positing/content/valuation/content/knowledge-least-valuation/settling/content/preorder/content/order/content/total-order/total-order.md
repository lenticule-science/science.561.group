# Total order

## What this node is

A **total order** is an [order](../../order.md) in which every pair of elements is
**comparable**: for any $a$ and $b$, either $a \le b$ or $b \le a$. Order already
gives reflexivity, antisymmetry, and transitivity; total order adds the one further
demand that no two elements are left side by side with no relation between them.

It is also called a **linear order** or a **chain** — "chain" especially for a
totally-ordered *subset* sitting inside a larger order. The integers under $\le$
are totally ordered; the reals are; every subset of a totally-ordered set is.

## Generated from order

Order permits incomparability — two elements may have no $\le$ either way (sets
under $\subseteq$: $\{1,2\}$ and $\{2,3\}$ are incomparable). A total order is order
with that freedom removed: comparability is required everywhere. This is a
**restriction** — fewer orders qualify as total — so order is the generator and
total order the generated refinement underneath it. It is the order-side sibling of
the lattice refinement: where the [lattice](../lattice/lattice.md) adds *bounds*,
the total order adds *comparability*; both are order with one more law.

## Why settling already exhibits it

The total order is not imported here by resemblance; settling instantiates it.
[Settling](../../../../../../settling.md)'s closure orbit — the graded ascent
possibilize∘necessitate runs until the valuation stops changing — is "a
totally-ordered ascent indexed by the closure ordinal." That ascending **chain** is
a total order living *inside* the partial order of values: the value space is a
[lattice](../lattice/lattice.md) (incomparable values exist), but the trajectory
settling traces through it is linear. Order is the shape of the destination space;
total order is the shape of the path.

The bilattice's own truth axis shows the same collapse: after settling closes the
four valued charges, the truth ordering on $\{f, \top, t\}$ becomes the chain
$f <_t \top <_t t$ — the diamond flattens to a total order along the axis settling
has decided.

## Orientation

The object that generates this one is [order](../../order.md), itself generated by
[settling](../../../../../../settling.md). Total order strengthens order by comparability;
the [lattice](../lattice/lattice.md) strengthens it by bounds. The two strengthenings
are independent — a lattice need not be a chain, a chain need not have all subset
bounds — and meet again only in special structures.
