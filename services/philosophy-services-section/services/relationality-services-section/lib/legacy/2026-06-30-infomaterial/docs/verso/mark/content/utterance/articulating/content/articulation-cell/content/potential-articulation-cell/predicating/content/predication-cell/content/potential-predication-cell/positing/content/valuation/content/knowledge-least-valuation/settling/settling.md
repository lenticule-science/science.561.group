## What this node is

Settling is the morphism out of the
[knowledge-least valuation](../knowledge-least-valuation.md) that brings a trace
to its **fixed point**. It runs the value-saturation coherence (commutate),
splitting the value category into the settled substrate and the unglued gluing,
then **iterates the closure** possibilize∘necessitate on the trace until the last
valuation stops changing. Termination is by **Knaster–Tarski**: each step is
monotone in the bounded knowledge lattice, so the orbit stabilizes.

Because the closure is monotone, the fixed point a least upper bound, and the
trace a graded chain, **this is the morphism that generates order**. Order theory
is not filed near settling by resemblance; order is the structure settling reaches.
Settling lays this down in two steps. The S4 modal closure first gives a
reflexive-transitive accessibility frame — a [preorder](content/preorder/preorder.md)
(reflexivity = the T axiom, transitivity = the 4 axiom). Then settling's *leastness*
— the fixed point is the unique least one — adds antisymmetry, quotients the ties,
and refines the preorder to [order](content/preorder/content/order/order.md). Order
sits one law beneath the preorder that generates it.

## What it generates

Settling packages the fixed point as a [sheaf](content/sheaf/sheaf.md)
`{base, section}` — the value-saturated substrate together with its agreeing
[section](content/sheaf/content/section/section.md). At the finite regime the
comparison 2-cell becomes invertible (the distributive bilattice is present to
force ◇□ = □◇), confluence holds, the fixed point is unique and the gluing
succeeds: the residue **drains to zero**, $H^1 = 0$, nothing left to descend. The
cascade stops here. Sheaf theory and cohomology — measuring how local settlements
fail to glue — are generated at this same morphism.

## Orientation

The domain is the [knowledge-least valuation](../knowledge-least-valuation.md);
descend to [sheaf](content/sheaf/sheaf.md) for the fixed point it packages. This
is the terminus of the four-regime descent articulate → predicate → posit →
settle.
