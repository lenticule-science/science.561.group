# Distributive lattice

## What this node is

A **distributive lattice** is a [lattice](../../lattice.md) in which
[meet](../meet/meet.md) distributes over [join](../join/join.md):
$$a \wedge (b \vee c) = (a \wedge b) \vee (a \wedge c).$$
In a lattice this law is self-dual — it holds if and only if join distributes over
meet — so the single demand fixes both. It says the two operations interleave
without interference: combining $a$ with a join is the same as joining the
combinations.

Distributivity is exactly the absence of the two minimal obstructions. A lattice
is distributive if and only if it contains neither the diamond $M_3$ nor the
pentagon $N_5$ as a sublattice; these are the two smallest non-distributive
lattices, and ruling them out rules out all failure.

## Generated from the lattice

The bare [lattice](../../lattice.md) guarantees a meet and join for each pair but
says nothing about how the two operations compose across one another — and in
general they do not compose cleanly. The distributive lattice adds precisely the
law that makes them compose: it is a refinement by an *equation on the operations*,
parallel to and independent of the
[bounded](../bounded-lattice/bounded-lattice.md) and
[complete](../complete-lattice/complete-lattice.md) refinements, which constrain
which bounds *exist* rather than how the operations interact.

[Settling](../../../../../../../../settling.md) needs this coherence. The two orders it
crosses — the knowledge axis and the truth axis of the bilattice — must agree on
how their meets and joins interleave, or the closure on one axis would corrupt the
other. Distributivity on each axis is what keeps the two-dimensional ascent
coherent; it is also what the
[twist-product](../../../../../../../../../../../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md)
construction of the bilattice requires of its input lattice.

## What it generates

Distributivity is the substrate two deeper structures stand on:

- **Priestley duality** — every bounded distributive lattice is dually equivalent
  to a compact ordered (Priestley) space; the lattice is recoverable from a
  topology on its prime filters. This recovery of an order from its topology is
  derived as a child once the topological machinery it needs is in place.
- the [Heyting algebra](content/heyting-algebra/heyting-algebra.md) — a bounded
  distributive lattice carrying an implication right-adjoint to meet. Distributivity
  is the precondition for that residuation to exist, which is why the constructive
  implication is derived *here*, under distributivity, and not under a bare lattice.

Every [Heyting algebra](content/heyting-algebra/heyting-algebra.md) and every
Boolean algebra is distributive; the converse fails in both cases — distributivity
is strictly weaker than carrying an implication.

## Orientation

Generated by [lattice](../../lattice.md), generated in turn by
[order](../../../../order.md) and [settling](../../../../../../../../settling.md). It is
the sibling of the [bounded](../bounded-lattice/bounded-lattice.md) and
[complete](../complete-lattice/complete-lattice.md) refinements and the parent of
the [Heyting algebra](content/heyting-algebra/heyting-algebra.md).
