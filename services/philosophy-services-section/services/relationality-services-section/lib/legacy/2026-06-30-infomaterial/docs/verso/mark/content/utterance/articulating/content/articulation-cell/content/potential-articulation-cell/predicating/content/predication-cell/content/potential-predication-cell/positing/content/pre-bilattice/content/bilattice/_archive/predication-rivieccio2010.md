## Annotation

The canonical modern reference on bilattice algebra. Replaces the original Ginsberg 1988 and Fitting 1991 sources for our purposes whenever a polished modern statement is preferable.

**Definitions 1.3.1–1.3.8** give bilattices, distributive bilattices, interlaced bilattices, twist products, and negation/conflation. **Theorems 1.3.9–1.3.10** are the representation theorems: every bounded interlaced (pre-)bilattice is isomorphic to $\mathbf{L}_1 \bowtie \mathbf{L}_2$ for some bounded lattices $\mathbf{L}_1, \mathbf{L}_2$, and every bounded interlaced bilattice is isomorphic to $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ for some $\mathbf{L}$. This is what licenses our reduction-to-twist-products discipline throughout.

**Chapter 3** treats implicative bilattices; **Chapter 4** gives the representation of implicative bilattices via classical implicative lattices.

Notation in the source: $\wedge, \vee$ for $\leq_t$; $\otimes, \oplus$ for $\leq_k$; $\odot$ for the bilattice product of lattices; $\mathbf{f}, \mathbf{t}, \bot, \top$ for the four-valued bilattice. Our program follows Fitting on $\mathbf{f}, \mathbf{t}, \bot, \top$ and Jakl on $\bowtie$ (rather than $\odot$) for the twist product, freeing $\odot$ for the twist-product residuation product.
