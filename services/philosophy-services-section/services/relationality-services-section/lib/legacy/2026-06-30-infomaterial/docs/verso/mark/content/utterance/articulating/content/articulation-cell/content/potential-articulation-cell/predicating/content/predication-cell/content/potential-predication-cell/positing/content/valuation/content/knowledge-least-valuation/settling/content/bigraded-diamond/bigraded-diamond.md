# Bigraded Diamond

## What this node is

The bigraded diamond is the first math structure **below** [settling](../../settling.md).
Settling brings a trace to a single fixed point — a [sheaf](../sheaf/sheaf.md) whose
[section](../sheaf/content/section/section.md) is a settled, bilattice-valued
assignment. But the bilattice carries **two** orders, knowledge and truth, and so
the settled section admits **two closures** — a knowledge **nucleus** $j_k$ and a
truth **nucleus** $j_t$ on its underlying Heyting algebra. When those two nuclei
**commute**, their composite is again a nucleus, and the structure is no longer a
single fixed point but a **diamond**: a commuting square of four canonical
fixed-point sub-algebras.

Grading that diamond by the (truth, knowledge) Klein-four — stacking the diamonds
as the closures are applied at successive grades — gives the **bigraded diamond
tower**, the post-settling structure the corpus's richer theory is built on.

## The diamond: a nuclear quartet

The diamond at each grade is a [nuclear-quartet](content/nuclear-quartet/nuclear-quartet.md):
two commuting nuclei $j_k, j_t$ on a Heyting algebra $A$ give exactly four
canonical sub-algebras —

$$\bigl(\,A,\;\; \mathrm{Fix}(j_k),\;\; \mathrm{Fix}(j_t),\;\; A^*\,\bigr),\qquad A^*=\mathrm{Fix}(j_k)\cap\mathrm{Fix}(j_t)$$

— the free carrier (neither closed), the two singly-closed algebras, and the
doubly-closed joint fixed point, arranged in a commuting square whose two paths
from $A$ to $A^*$ agree exactly because the nuclei commute. This is the same
four-corner slot-presence shape met at the articulation phases and the bilattice
charge, now lifted to *sub-algebras under closure*: apply neither, $j_k$ alone,
$j_t$ alone, or both.

## Why it matters

This is where the descent stops being a single chain and becomes a **tower**.
Settling produced one fixed point per order; the bigraded diamond holds *both
orders' closures at once* and grades them, which is the structure rich enough to
carry the predication hyperdoctrine, the closure 2-monad's higher cells, Chu-style
dualities, and the Lawvere-algebraic-theory machinery. The corpus's more complex
objects — agents, systems, computers — each **require at least one settling** and
a richer fiber algebra, so they are derived **deep under this tower**, not as
siblings of the spine: once the bigraded structure is in place, "an agent is
*this*, a system is *this*, a computer is *this*" can be read off it.
