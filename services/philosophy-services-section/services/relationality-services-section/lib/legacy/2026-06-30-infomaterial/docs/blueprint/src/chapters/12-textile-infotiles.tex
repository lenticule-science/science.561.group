\chapter{Information projection: Tiles, Beads, InfoHair}\label{ch:textile}

This chapter records the information-projection subgraph.  Its base claim-site is a
local-compatibility constraint system on a discrete carrier, coloured by the FOUR
bilattice, with defects tracked as held inconsistency.  Tiles name the finite
local rules and sofic factors.  Beads name ported condensates and the
load-bearing topology they create.  InfoHair names the visible invariants of the
settled carrier: growth, diffraction, curvature, and serial order.  Format
carriers name the serialization surfaces on which those settled carriers are
read and completed.

The included regions are one dependency graph.  Their
\texttt{\textbackslash uses} edges are the incidence data; exposition belongs to the
Verso profile of the same claim-sites.
