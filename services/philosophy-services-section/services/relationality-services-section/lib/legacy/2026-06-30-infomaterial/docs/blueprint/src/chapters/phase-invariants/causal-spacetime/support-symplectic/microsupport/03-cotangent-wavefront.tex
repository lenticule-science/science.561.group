\subsubsection{Cotangent wavefront}

\begin{theorem}[Cotangent microsupport --- the wavefront set in codirection space]\label{thm:cotangent-microsupport}\lean{Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Support.Cotangent.cotangent_microsupport}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-microsupport, thm:clinv-dichotomy}
The cotangent refinement of \cref{thm:causal-microsupport}: the discrete
covectors are directed causal edges, and the wavefront set of an edge-cochain
$\varphi$ is its support. A codirection points into the causal future, the base
singular support is the projection of the wavefront, an empty wavefront forces
a trivial coboundary class, and a nonzero $\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}$ coordinate
forces a wavefront point. Cohomological charge is therefore cotangent
microlocal data on the causal edge carrier.
\end{theorem}
