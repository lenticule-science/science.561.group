# Lorentzian causal-structure settling

A **Lorentzian causal-structure settling** is a settling whose cells are pairs $(e_1, e_2)$ of spacetime events and whose closure operator is *causal transitivity*: if $e_1$ is in the causal past of $e_2$ and $e_2$ is in the causal past of $e_3$, then $e_1$ is in the causal past of $e_3$. The settling fixed-point is the transitive closure of seed causal precedences — the full causal order on the event set.

The natural setting: **causal sets** (Sorkin-style discrete spacetime models), where spacetime is replaced by a locally-finite partial order $(P, \preceq)$ representing causal precedence. The settling formalism applies directly with Boolean (or bilattice) values.

## Definition

Fix a (finite or countable) set $E$ of spacetime events and a binary relation $\prec_0 \subseteq E \times E$ of "direct causal links" (e.g., elementary timelike connections). The **causal-structure settling on $(E, \prec_0)$** is:

- *Cells*: pairs $(e_1, e_2) \in E \times E$.
- *Initial settling* $v_0(e_1, e_2)$: $\mathbf{t}$ if $e_1 \prec_0 e_2$, $\bot$ otherwise.
- *Closure operator*: $T_{\mathrm{caus}}(v)(e_1, e_3) := v(e_1, e_3) \vee \exists e_2 . [v(e_1, e_2) \wedge v(e_2, e_3)]$ — Boolean transitive closure step.
- *Settling*: $v_\kappa(e_1, e_2) = \mathbf{t}$ iff there's a directed $\prec_0$-path $e_1 \to e_2$ in $E$.

The settling encodes the **full causal order** $\preceq$ on $E$ generated by the seed relation $\prec_0$.

## Regime classification

For finite $E$: $\kappa = \lceil \log_2 D \rceil$ where $D$ is the longest directed path length, by the parallel-relaxation argument (cf. [benchmark-lawvere-quantale-settling](../examples/benchmark-lawvere-quantale-settling.md)). **Strict regime**, $H^1 = 0$, no NSC firings.

For countably-infinite $E$ (e.g., a sprinkling of Minkowski spacetime): $\kappa = \omega$ in the Scott-continuous sense — every cell that becomes $\mathbf{t}$ does so at some finite stage, and the $\omega$-supremum is the full causal order. **Scott-continuous regime.**

For genuinely-uncountable causal sets (continuum-spacetime): the closure is taken in the topological/measure-theoretic sense. The closure ordinal is $\omega_1$ or beyond depending on cofinality. **Aperiodic regime in pathological cases**, otherwise asymptotic-c.

## Apex / Void cones in the Lorentzian setting

The corpus's [apex / void cone duality](../narratives/void-cone-structure.md) acquires a clean physics interpretation:

### Apex cone (settled cells)

A cell $(e_1, e_2)$ becomes settled (in apex $\mathrm{Apex}_v$) iff $v_\kappa(e_1, e_2) = \mathbf{t}$ — iff $e_1$ is in the causal past of $e_2$.

In Lorentzian terms: the cell is **causally connected** — there's a directed timelike-or-null curve from $e_1$ to $e_2$. The apex generators (per [void-cone-cohomology](../theorems/void-cone-cohomology.md)) record causal-content acquisition events.

### Void cone (permanently-$\bot$ cells)

A cell $(e_1, e_2)$ remains in the void $V_\infty$ iff neither $v_\kappa(e_1, e_2)$ nor $v_\kappa(e_2, e_1)$ are $\mathbf{t}$ — iff $e_1$ and $e_2$ are **causally disconnected**.

In Lorentzian terms: $e_1$ and $e_2$ are **spacelike separated**. No causal curve connects them in either direction.

The structural identification:

| Corpus side | Lorentzian side |
|---|---|
| Apex cone (settled cells) | Causally-connected pairs (timelike/null) |
| Void cone (permanently $\bot$) | Spacelike-separated pairs |
| Apex cohomology $H^1$ | Causal-acquisition events |
| Void cohomology $H^0(\mathrm{Void})$ | Structural causal-impossibility events |

**The apex/void duality of the corpus is the timelike/spacelike duality of Lorentzian geometry.** This identification is established formally in the [timelike/spacelike as apex/void theorem](../theorems/lorentzian-apex-void-as-timelike-spacelike.md).

## Causal-set substitution dynamics

A *causal-set substitution* is a rule that grows a causal set by adding new events with prescribed causal links to existing events. The settling formalism applies: each substitution step is a closure operator producing a richer causal-set settling.

For a primitive non-singular causal-set substitution (analog of Fibonacci substitution at the spacetime level), the cohomology of the resulting settling is governed by predication categories' [aperiodic-regime cohomology theorem](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md). The Perron eigenvalue of the substitution matrix gives the *growth rate* of the causal set, which physically corresponds to *spacetime expansion / volume growth*.

This connects causal-set physics to predication categories' substitution-dynamics infrastructure.

## Multi-observer Lorentzian settlings

The [multi-observer system](multi-observer-system.md) framework applies: each observer at a particular event has their own causal past (their *backward light cone*) and assigns settlings only on cells reachable from that past. Different observers with different worldlines have different causal-pasts, hence different observer-relative settlings.

The consensus / disagreement structure across observers gives the **observer-dependent reality structure** in predication categories' framework: each observer sees a different settling, but all observers' settlings agree on cells in their common past.

This is structurally the *causal diamond intersection* in Lorentzian geometry, recast as a multi-observer-system limit.

## Connection to existing structure

The Lorentzian causal settling is a *binary, Boolean-valued, bitopological-site-flavored* settling — fully within predication categories' existing framework. What's specific to the Lorentzian context:

- The cell space $E \times E$ has *spacetime* interpretation (events).
- The closure operator is *causal transitivity*, a special case of transitive closure.
- The apex/void duality has *timelike/spacelike* interpretation.

All structural theorems apply: closure 2-monad, regime dichotomy, MLD-classification, no-tunneling, etc.

## Worked example sketch

A minimal causal set: $E = \{a, b, c, d\}$ with seed $\prec_0 = \{a \prec b, a \prec c, b \prec d, c \prec d\}$. This is a "diamond" pattern.

Closure: transitive closure adds $a \prec d$.

Apex: $\{(a, b), (a, c), (b, d), (c, d), (a, d)\}$ — 5 cells settled.
Void: cells $(b, c)$ and $(c, b)$ — $b$ and $c$ are spacelike separated. 2 cells in $V_\infty$.

This is the simplest causal-diamond example: 4 events, 2 spacelike pairs, 5 timelike pairs. The apex/void counts match a basic Lorentzian-2D diamond structure.
