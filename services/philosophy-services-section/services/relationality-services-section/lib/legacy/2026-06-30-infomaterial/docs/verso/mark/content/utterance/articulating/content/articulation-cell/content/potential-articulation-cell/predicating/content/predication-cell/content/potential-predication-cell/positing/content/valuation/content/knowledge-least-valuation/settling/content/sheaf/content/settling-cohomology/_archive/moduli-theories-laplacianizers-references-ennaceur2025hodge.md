# Hodge Laplacians on Weighted Simplicial Complexes (2025)

arxiv:2510.15546 (Oct 2025).

## Why this matters

Treats the **weights** on simplices as the *Hilbert-pairing data* underlying the Hodge Laplacian. This is the clearest exhibit that the choice of inner product on each chain space is not incidental but is one of the inputs to the Laplacianization functor — different weighting schemes are different liftings $\mathcal C \to \mathbf{HilbCh}$.

In the language of `concepts/laplacianizability.md`: this paper *factors* the Laplacian construction as (combinatorial structure) + (per-simplex weighting) → (Hilbert chain complex) → (Laplacian). The factorization is laplacianizers' central object — Laplacianizability is the *first* arrow being functorial.
