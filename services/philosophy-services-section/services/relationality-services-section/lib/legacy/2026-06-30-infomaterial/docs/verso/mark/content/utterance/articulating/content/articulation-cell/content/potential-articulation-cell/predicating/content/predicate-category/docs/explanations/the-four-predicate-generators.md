# The four predicate generators

The [predicate category](../../predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ is the small
category whose objects are [predicates](../../../predicate/predicate.md) — so
$\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is the
[predicate set](../../content/predicate-set/predicate-set.md) $\Pi$ — and whose
morphisms are generated by **four canonical classes**. Each class is induced by a
specific manipulation of the entity-pair index $E \times E$ over the entity set
$E$, and each induces a **bilattice homomorphism** between the predication fiber
algebras at the predicates it relates. The root node names the four; this is what
each one *is*.

## Specialization — $f_{\mathrm{spec}} : q \to p$

A pair of predicates $q, p$ together with a sub-support inclusion
$$\iota_f : \mathrm{dom}(q) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{dom}(p),$$
where $\mathrm{dom}(p), \mathrm{dom}(q) \subseteq E \times E$ are the structurally
designated sub-supports attached to $p, q$. The induced fiber-algebra map is
restriction along $\iota_f$:
$$
f^*(v)(s, o) = \begin{cases} v(s, o) & (s, o) \in \mathrm{dom}(q) \\ \bot & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
$$
Specialization encodes that $q$ is a **sub-predicate** of $p$: $q$'s values are
$p$'s values on the sub-class $\mathrm{dom}(q) \subseteq \mathrm{dom}(p)$, and
$\bot$ outside. Concretely, `is-dog` specializes `is-mammal`.

## Converse — $f_{\mathrm{conv}} : p^c \to p$

Induced by the swap of the entity-pair index
$$(s, o) \mapsto (o, s),$$
with induced fiber-algebra map
$$f^*(v)(s, o) = v(o, s).$$
The converse encodes the **relational converse** of $p$: the predicate $p^c$ holds
at $(s, o)$ exactly where $p$ holds at $(o, s)$. Concretely, the converse of
`is-parent-of` is `is-child-of`. The swap is its own inverse, so converse is
involutive at the morphism level.

## Subject-projection — $f_{\mathrm{sp}} : q \to p$

Induced by the collapse of the entity-pair index onto the subject-diagonal
$$(s, o) \mapsto (s, s),$$
with induced fiber-algebra map
$$f^*(v)(s, o) = v(s, s).$$
It **broadcasts** $p$'s subject-diagonal content across all object coordinates:
$q$'s value at $(s, o)$ depends only on $p$'s value at $(s, s)$. Concretely, a
unary `is-tall` stored at $(s, s)$ projects to a binary $q$ with
$q(s, o) = \text{is-tall}(s)$ for every $o$. This is one of the two ways a unary
predicate embeds into the binary-cell framework.

## Object-projection — $f_{\mathrm{op}} : q \to p$

The dual of subject-projection, induced by the collapse onto the object-diagonal
$$(s, o) \mapsto (o, o),$$
with induced fiber-algebra map
$$f^*(v)(s, o) = v(o, o).$$
It broadcasts $p$'s object-diagonal content across all subject coordinates:
$q(s, o)$ depends only on $p$'s value at $(o, o)$. Concretely, a unary
`is-target-of-attention` stored at $(o, o)$ projects to $q(s, o)$ equal to that
value for every $s$.

## Why exactly four

Under a $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$-equivariance hypothesis with $|E| \geq 3$, these four
classes **exhaust** the natural bottom-preserving bilattice homomorphisms between
fiber algebras. The four-ness is not arbitrary; it falls out of the
symmetric-group analysis of the operations available on the entity-pair index
$E \times E$. The subject and object coordinates of the
[predication cell](../../../predication-cell/predication-cell.md) are named for the
two projection classes.

## Generators determine the category

The four classes are a **generating set**: every morphism of $\mathcal{C}$ is a
finite composition of class members. The category is therefore fully determined by
the object-set $\Pi$ together with the generator-data — the list of
$(\text{source}, \text{target})$ pairs in each class. Composition is recovered by
graph closure on the generator-pair edges, modulo the identities each class's
semantics forces: specialization is reflexive and transitive, converse is
involutive, and the two projections compose into a constant unary predicate when
iterated.

## What downstream theorems demand of $\mathcal{C}$

The bare predicate category needs no extra structure, but specific downstream
theorems require more:

- **Finite limits / pullbacks** — for Beck–Chevalley commutation on the fiber
  algebras.
- **$\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$** (cohomological dimension as a
  poset / Alexandrov-topology category) — for settling-cohomology vanishing above
  degree 1, and every theorem downstream of it that uses $H^{\geq 2} = 0$ or
  $\mathrm{Ext}^{\geq 2} = 0$.

Both hypotheses are automatic when $\mathcal{C}$ is finite (an Alexandrov-finite
poset has cohomological dimension equal to its order-complex dimension), when
$\mathcal{C}$ is a directed poset ($\mathrm{cd} = 0$), and in every explicit corpus
instance built from the four generators on a finite predicate set. Higher-cd
predicate categories — higher-arity predicates with non-trivial composition
structure, or modal-operator-extended predicate categories — would require
re-verification of the downstream vanishing.
