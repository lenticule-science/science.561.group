## Annotation

Develops the propositional logic of distributive bilattices — the logical companion to the algebraic theory of bilattices that the program inherits via [@avron1996] and [@rivieccio2010]. For the program, supplies the proof-theoretic side that supports the four-valued modal extensions developed downstream.
