\subsubsection{Causal involutivity}

\begin{theorem}[Causal involutivity --- coisotropy of the wavefront]\label{thm:causal-involutivity}\lean{Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Causal.Involutivity.causal_involutivity}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-cochain-complex}
The cocycle condition makes causal 1-cochains additive along every composable
bicharacteristic triangle. A singular composite edge therefore decomposes:
at least one causal leg is singular, so the wavefront propagates along the
causal cone rather than sitting as an isolated composite.
\end{theorem}
