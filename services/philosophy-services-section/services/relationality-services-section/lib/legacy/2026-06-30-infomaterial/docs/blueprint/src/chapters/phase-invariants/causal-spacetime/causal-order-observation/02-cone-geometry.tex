\subsection{Cone geometry}

\begin{theorem}[Causal reachability is a cone geometry]\label{thm:cone-field-geometry}\lean{Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Causal.Cone.causal_reachability_is_cone_geometry}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-settling-transitive-closure}
Causal reachability promotes a finite causal set to cone-field geometry:
future and past cones, reflexive causal precedence, spacelike separation, and
causal diamonds. The reflexive causal relation is a partial order, spacelike
separation is symmetric, and future cones are transitive.
\end{theorem}
