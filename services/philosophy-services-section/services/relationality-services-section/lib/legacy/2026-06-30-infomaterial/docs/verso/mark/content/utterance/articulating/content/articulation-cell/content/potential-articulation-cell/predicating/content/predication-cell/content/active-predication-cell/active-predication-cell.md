# Active Predication Cell

The **active** corner of the [predication cell](../../predication-cell.md)'s
recurring slot-presence partition — positive — one side committed. It is one of the four refinements the
predication cell comes in, the same four-point object met at the
[articulation cell](../../../../../potential-articulation-cell.md)'s phases and,
one regime down, at the bilattice charge. The generative descent continues from
the [potential](../potential-predication-cell/potential-predication-cell.md)
corner (via [positing](../potential-predication-cell/positing/positing.md)); this
corner is reached by the predication category's internal morphisms.

The morphism that produced the cell is
[predicating](../../../../predicating.md).
