## What this node is

Settling restricts [positing](../../positing.md) to its **fixed points**. Once value is in play, a claim is run through an ordinal closure process — a monotone ascent that drops no designated cell — until it stabilizes. The corpus's research locates here the settlement filtrations, ordinal closure processes, fixed-point readings, sheaves, cohomology, and valuation dynamics — and classifies every such settling into one of four regimes (strict, Scott-continuous, transfinite-bounded, aperiodic) by its closure ordinal. And because the closure is *monotone*, the fixed point a *least upper bound*, and the filtration a *graded chain*, **this is the morphism that generates order**. Order theory is not filed near settling by resemblance; order is the structure settling *is*.

## What it generates

- [order theory](content/order-theory/order-theory.md) — posets, lattices, Heyting algebras, Priestley duality, formal concept analysis: the study of the closure order that settling climbs and the fixed points it reaches.
- [sheaf theory](content/sheaf-theory/) — sheaves and cohomology, which measure how local settlements fail to glue into a global one; generated at the same morphism because they too are about agreeing fixed points over a base.
