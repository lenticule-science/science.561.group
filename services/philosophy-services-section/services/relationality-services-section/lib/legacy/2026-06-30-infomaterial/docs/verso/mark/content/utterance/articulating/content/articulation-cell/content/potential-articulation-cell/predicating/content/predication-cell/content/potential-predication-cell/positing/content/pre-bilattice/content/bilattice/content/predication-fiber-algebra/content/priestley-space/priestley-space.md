# Priestley space

A **Priestley space** is a compact, totally order-disconnected, partially ordered topological space — for any two points $x \not\leq y$ there is a clopen up-set containing $x$ but not $y$.
Morphisms are continuous order-preserving maps.

Priestley duality ([@priestley1970], 1972) gives the contravariant equivalence

$$\mathbf{DLat}^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \mathbf{PriSp},$$

sending a bounded distributive lattice to its space of prime filters topologized by the spectral basis, and a Priestley space to its lattice of clopen up-sets.

In the spatiotemporal-knowledge program, Priestley duals appear at the [bilattice](bilattice.md) level via the [twist product](twist-product.md): the [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../theorems/jung-rivieccio-duality.md) lifts $\mathbf{DLat}^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \mathbf{PriSp}$ to $\mathbf{DBiLat}^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \mathbf{PairedPriSp}$.
