Probability in this vault treats uncertainty as a formal object: a [sample space](./terms/sample-space.md) equipped with a measure that assigns values in [0, 1] to events, satisfying the Kolmogorov axioms. Specifications may address:

- The axiomatic foundation (sigma-algebras, measure functions, conditional probability as a derived operation)
- Interfaces between probability and other vault modules, particularly [statistics](../statistics/index.md) (which applies probabilistic models to data) and the [semiotic universe](../../objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md) (where probabilistic reasoning may inform inference under uncertainty)
