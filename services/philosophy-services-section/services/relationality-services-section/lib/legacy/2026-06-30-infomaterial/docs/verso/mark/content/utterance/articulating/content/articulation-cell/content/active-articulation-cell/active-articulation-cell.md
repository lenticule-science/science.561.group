# Active Articulation Cell

## What this node is

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **token side
is articulated and the claim side is not** — `(token: yes, claim: no)`. The
identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has been
committed; the content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md)
remains open.

## Position in the progression

Under the token-before-claim working theory, active is the **first step out of
the [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
cell**: articulating naturally proceeds by articulating the token side first, so
potential → active is the opening move of the natural progression, which
continues active → [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md)
when the claim side is articulated too. Active is "on the path" — the *which-one*
is fixed while *what-is-said* is still to come. (Whether token-before-claim is the
right ordering is open work; under the reversed reading this corner's role and
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md)'s would swap.)

Material from the corpus's aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in
[`_archive/`](_archive).

## Orientation

Read [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
for the seed it steps from, [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md)
for where it continues, and the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole partition. The
morphism that generated the cell is [articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
