## Content summary

Introduces *bilattices* as a uniform algebraic framework for AI inference systems: default reasoning, truth-maintenance systems, ATMS-style assumption tracking, probabilistic reasoning, fuzzy inference, and modal reasoning are all instances of inference over a bilattice-valued model. The paper argues that the diversity of AI inference paradigms reflects different choices of bilattice (the four-valued $\mathcal{FOUR}$ for paraconsistent reasoning; the unit interval as a chain bilattice for probability/fuzziness; lattices of contexts for assumption-based reasoning) rather than fundamentally different logical machinery. A single inference operator on the bilattice (cellwise meet for combining sources, fixed-point iteration for forward chaining) instantiates the AI inference paradigm under each bilattice choice.
