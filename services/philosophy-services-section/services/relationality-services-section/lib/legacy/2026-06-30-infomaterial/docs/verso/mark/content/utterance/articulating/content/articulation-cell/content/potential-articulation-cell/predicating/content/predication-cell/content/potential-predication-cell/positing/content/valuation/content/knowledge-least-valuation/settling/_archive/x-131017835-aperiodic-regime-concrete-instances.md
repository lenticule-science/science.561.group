# Concrete naturally-occurring aperiodic-regime instances

The [aperiodic-settling sketch](../examples/aperiodic-settling-sketch.md) is qualitative: concrete [aperiodic settlings](teaching/explanations/aperiodic-settling.md) require proper-class entity sets or inaccessible cardinals. Open: identify naturally-occurring knowledge-representation scenarios — open-world ontologies, certain dynamical systems, large cardinal logical settings — where the aperiodic regime arises in practice rather than only in proper-class constructions.

Finding such scenarios would situate the aperiodic regime in application and motivate the cohomological invariants beyond their structural justification. The [Priestley-route narrative](prose/teaching/explanations/priestley-route-significance.md) argues that the aperiodic regime is where the dualization apparatus pays off; instances of practical interest would make that payoff concrete.
