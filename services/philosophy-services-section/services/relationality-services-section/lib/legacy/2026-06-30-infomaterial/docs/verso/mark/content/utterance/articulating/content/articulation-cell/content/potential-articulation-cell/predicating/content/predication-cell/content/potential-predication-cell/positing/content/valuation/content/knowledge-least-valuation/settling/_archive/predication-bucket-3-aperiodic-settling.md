# Aperiodic settling

A settling of a [spatiotemporal knowledge presheaf](../objects/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf.md) is **aperiodic** iff its [closure ordinal](../objects/closure-ordinal.md) satisfies $\kappa \geq \omega_1$, i.e., no countable ordinal is a strict fixed point.

Aperiodic settlings are the natural home for sheaf cohomology over the Priestley dual: orbits that do not stabilize but are coherent — strictly $\leq_k$-ascending, carrying all 2-cell information of the KZ diamond — and whose invariants are not accessible via fixed-point theorems.
