\subsection{Step 3 refinement}

\begin{theorem}[MLD refines cohomological equivalence (Step 3)]\label{thm:mld-refines-cohequiv}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.mld_refines_cohEquiv}\leanok\uses{thm:clinv-dichotomy}
The formalized relation \(\mathrm{CohEquiv}\) is equality of classifying
invariants.  Mutual local derivability is finer: an invertible
settling-morphism induces the sheaf isomorphism carrying
\(\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{F}_U}\) to \(\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{F}_{U'}}\), hence
preserves \(\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}\).  Therefore
\(\mathrm{MLD}\Rightarrow\mathrm{CohEquiv}\).
\end{theorem}
