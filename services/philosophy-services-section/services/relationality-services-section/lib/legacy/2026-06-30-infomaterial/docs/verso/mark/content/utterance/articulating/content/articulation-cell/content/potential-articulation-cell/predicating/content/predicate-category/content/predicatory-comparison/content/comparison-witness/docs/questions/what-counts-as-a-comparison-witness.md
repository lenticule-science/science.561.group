# What counts as a comparison witness?

What data must a comparison return to be a mathematical witness rather than a Boolean projection?

The current answer is deliberately stronger than "a value" and weaker than "a full proof transcript." A comparison witness should record the context, standard, agreement locus, divergence profile, residuals where they exist, and certificates required by the standard. This is enough to distinguish:

- disagreement from missing data;
- contested evidence from simple absence;
- exact disagreement from directional residual;
- order-theoretic confluence from operational confluence.

## Open points

- For non-Boolean bilattices, what is the canonical divergence profile when inputs are incomparable?
- Does every comparison witness decompose into agreement plus divergence plus residual, or do confluence and argumentation require additional proof-object components?
- Which witness projections are functorial in the comparison context?
- Is there a universal comparison witness for a fixed standard, analogous to an equalizer or pullback in ordinary category theory?
