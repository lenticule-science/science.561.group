\subsection{Geometric completeness}

\begin{theorem}[The geometric MLD is complete --- the coalgebraic resolution]\label{thm:geometric-mld-complete}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.geometric_mld_complete}\leanok\uses{thm:aperiodic-settle-frame, thm:aperiodic-mld-classification-reduction}
The geometric local-derivability structure is the coalgebraic relation from the
aperiodic settling frame: \(v\sim v'\) iff some \texttt{BisimUTC} relates
\((v,0)\) and \((v',0)\).  The settled-value kernel constructs the required
morphism from \(\mathrm{CohEquiv}\), so the genuine geometric structure is
complete.  The residue of \cref{thm:aperiodic-mld-classification} is only the
primitive-transfinite-closure identification.
\end{theorem}
