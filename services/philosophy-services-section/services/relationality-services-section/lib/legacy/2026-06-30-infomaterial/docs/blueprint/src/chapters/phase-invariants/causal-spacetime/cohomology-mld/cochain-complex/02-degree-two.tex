\subsubsection{Degree two}

\begin{theorem}[Causal $\check{H}^2$ --- the degree-2 cohomology]\label{thm:causal-cohomology-h2}\lean{Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Causal.Cohomology.H2.causal_cohomology_h2}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-cochain-complex}
The causal order complex extended one degree.
\cref{thm:causal-cochain-complex} stopped at $\delta^1$
($C^0 \to C^1 \to C^2$, giving $H^1$); here 3-simplices, composable chains
$a \leq b \leq c \leq d$, carry the degree-2 coboundary
$\delta^2 \psi(a,b,c,d) = \psi(b,c,d) - \psi(a,c,d) +
\psi(a,b,d) - \psi(a,b,c)$. The identity
$\delta^2 \circ \delta^1 = 0$ telescopes, so
$\operatorname{im}\delta^1 \subseteq \ker\delta^2$ and
$H^2 = \ker\delta^2 / \operatorname{im}\delta^1$ is well-defined. This is
where a closed 2-form's class would live. But the cotangent symplectic form is
exact ($\omega = \delta^1\lambda$), so the symplectic content is not an $H^2$
class but the Liouville form and polarization nondegeneracy of
\cref{thm:cotangent-symplectic-flow}.
\end{theorem}
