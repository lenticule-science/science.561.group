## What this node is
This node is generated by **settle**, the final morphism in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. Once a predicate has been posited and its truth allowed to converge over a base, the question that remains is whether locally-consistent data can be glued into globally-consistent data — and the residue when it cannot. Algebraic topology is exactly the measurement of that residue: homology, cohomology, and homotopy as invariants of the global obstruction to settling local sections into a global one. It is generated by settle because the settling direction *is* the base over which sheaves are formed, and cohomology is the derived functor that records where settling fails to be coordination-free.

## What it generates / contains
- **`sheaf-cohomology/`** — the body of this node: cohomology of KZ-coherent and Q-enriched sheaves on the bitopological settling site. Material here includes the settling-cohomology object itself (right-derived bounded global sections plus a direct limit over the settling bound), the replica Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence, CRDT convergence stated as vanishing of an H^1 gluing obstruction, Verdier duality between sheaves and cosheaves (with the d-frame's two-dualities-composing refinement), the assembled six-functor formalism, Q-enriched and bigraded variants, and the aperiodic-regime classification by H^1 / H_1 invariants. The directory presently holds un-canonicalized source material under its own `_archive/`.

## Orientation
Up one level sits **settling**, the morphism node whose convergence supplies the base this topology is taken over; alongside it under the same parent live the other settle-generated faces. Un-canonicalized source material for this node lives in `sheaf-cohomology/_archive/` and awaits canonicalization into stable subconcepts.
