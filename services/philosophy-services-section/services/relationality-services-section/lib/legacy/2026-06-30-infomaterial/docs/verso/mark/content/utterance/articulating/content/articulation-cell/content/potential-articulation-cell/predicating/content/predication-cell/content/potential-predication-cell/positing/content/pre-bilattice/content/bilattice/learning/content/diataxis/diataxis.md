## What this node is

**Diátaxis** is the four-mode documentation structure — *tutorial*, *how-to*,
*explanation*, *reference* — read here as the **documentation-face of the recurring
FOUR**. It is generated by [learning](../../learning.md): the morphism that reads the
[bilattice](../../../../bilattice.md)'s four charges as four *orientations to
acquiring knowledge*. Diátaxis is not a documentation convention laid over the
mathematics; it is the same four-point object the bilattice already carries —
[charge](../../../content/charge/charge.md), the articulation phases, the modal four
— met once more under documentation names.

This claim is argued in full, and discharged as an axiom-free Lean theorem, in this
node's own surfaces:

## The forced bijection

Diátaxis's own two axes are the two articulation coordinates `(token, claim)`:
`token`-articulated **is** *application* (orienting to use/work), `claim`-articulated
**is** *cognition* (orienting to the proposition/why). That identification reproduces
Daniele Procida's compass exactly:

| Diátaxis | charge | `(token, claim)` | Procida axes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **tutorial** | `⊥ₖ` (potential) | `(no, no)` | acquisition + action |
| **how-to** | `t` (active) | `(yes, no)` | application + action |
| **explanation** | `f` (stable) | `(no, yes)` | acquisition + cognition |
| **reference** | `⊤ₖ` (absurd) | `(yes, yes)` | application + cognition |

## Diátaxis is a bilattice, not a flat 2×2

Because the bijection lands on [charge](../../../content/charge/charge.md), Diátaxis
inherits the bilattice's [two orders](../../../content/two-orders-duality/two-orders-duality.md):

- the **knowledge order** `≤ₖ` reads as the *scaffolding / decontextualisation*
  gradient: `tutorial (⊥ₖ) <ₖ how-to, explanation <ₖ reference (⊤ₖ)`. Ascending it is
  exactly the move cognitive-load theory's worked-example and expertise-reversal
  effects prescribe as a learner gains expertise;
- the **truth order** `≤ₜ` reads as the *actionability / definiteness* gradient:
  `explanation (f) <ₜ tutorial, reference <ₜ how-to (t)`.

The bilattice's [Klein-four symmetry](../../../content/klein-four-symmetry/klein-four-symmetry.md)
`K₄ = ⟨~, −⟩` becomes the two Diátaxis **diagonals**:

- `conflate −` = **tutorial ↔ reference** (the *learning↔lookup* duality; reverses `≤ₖ`);
- `negate ~` = **how-to ↔ explanation** (the *doing↔understanding* duality; reverses `≤ₜ`).
