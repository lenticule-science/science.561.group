# Priestley-dual cover topology

The Grothendieck topology on the category $\mathrm{ResLat}_{\mathrm{dist}}$ of distributive integral commutative [residuated lattices](residuated-lattice.md) defined via [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../theorems/jung-rivieccio-duality.md): a family is a cover iff its dual on Priestley spaces is jointly surjective.

For $\mathbf{L} \in \mathrm{ResLat}_{\mathrm{dist}}$, write $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L})$ for its [Priestley space](priestley-space.md) of prime filters.

## Definition

A family $\{\ell_i : \mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{L}_i\}_{i \in I}$ in $\mathrm{ResLat}_{\mathrm{dist}}$ is a **Priestley cover** of $\mathbf{L}$ iff the dual family

$$\{\mathrm{Spec}(\ell_i) : \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L}_i) \to \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L})\}_{i \in I}$$

is *jointly surjective* — every prime filter of $\mathbf{L}$ is the image of a prime filter of some $\mathbf{L}_i$.

The **Priestley-dual cover topology** $J_{\mathrm{ResLat}}$ is the topology generated by Priestley covers.

Equivalently in $\mathrm{ResLat}_{\mathrm{dist}}$ terms: $\bigcap_i \ker(\ell_i) = \{1\}$ — the joint kernel is trivial — together with Priestley-side cocycle conditions on overlaps.

## Why this topology

The natural cover topology is already in the program: [Jung–Rivieccio duality](../theorems/jung-rivieccio-duality.md) and the [Priestley dualizability theorem](../theorems/priestley-dualizability.md) together make distributive residuated lattices dualize to Priestley spaces, and Stone-style clopen covers transport via duality to the lattice category.
This is intrinsic — not an externally imported "fpqc" or "canonical" topology — and it integrates directly with the [bitopological settling site](bitopological-settling-site.md) of the cohomology layer.

## Subcanonicality

By [Priestley-cover topology is subcanonical](../theorems/priestley-cover-topology-subcanonical.md), every representable functor $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{ResLat}}(-, \mathbf{M})$ is a sheaf for $J_{\mathrm{ResLat}}$.
The proof reduces the sheaf condition to the standard topological gluing-of-continuous-maps condition on Priestley spaces under jointly-surjective covers.

## Example covers

- $\mathbf{2}$: only the singleton iso (trivial), since $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{2})$ is a point.
- Finite distributive lattice with $|\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{L})| = n$: covers correspond to coverings of the $n$-point poset by sub-posets.
  Many non-trivial covers, e.g., singletons giving residuated-lattice quotients $\mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{L} / p_i$.
- Heyting algebra $H$: covers correspond to clopen partitionings of $\mathrm{Spec}(H)$.
  Rich topology.
- MV-algebra: same picture via the bounded-distributive-lattice underpinning.

The topology is non-trivial enough to make the [settling moduli stack theorem](../theorems/settling-moduli-stack-theorem.md) genuinely a stack — not a discrete groupoid.

## Restriction to distributive

Non-distributive residuated lattices do not admit Priestley duality directly; the topology is defined only on $\mathrm{ResLat}_{\mathrm{dist}}$.
Extension to non-distributive $\mathbf{L}$ is a future-work item.
