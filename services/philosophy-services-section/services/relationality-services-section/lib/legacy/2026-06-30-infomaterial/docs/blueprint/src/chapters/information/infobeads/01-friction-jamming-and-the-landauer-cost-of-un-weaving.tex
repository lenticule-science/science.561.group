\section{Friction, jamming, and the Landauer cost of un-weaving}

Infomaterial identifies Landauer erasure cost with cohomological coordination cost:
$\texttt{landauerBits} = \texttt{cells} - (\texttt{entities} - \texttt{components})$ = cycle
rank $\check H^1$, zero iff the contact graph is a forest
(\cref{thm:coordination-cost,thm:coordination-h1-iff-forest}).  This subgraph
uses that carrier to state the jamming, friction, and homogenized-energy nodes.

\begin{theorem}[Un-weaving cost is Landauer cost]\label{thm:unweaving-is-landauer}\notready\uses{thm:coordination-cost, thm:coordination-h1-iff-forest, thm:weave-entropy-collapse}
\emph{(Open frontier --- stated, not formalized.)} The Landauer cost of un-weaving a
fabric --- erasing its woven information --- equals the cycle rank $\check H^1$ of its
contact graph, $\texttt{cells} - (\texttt{entities} - \texttt{components})$. A floatless
plain weave is near-forest (low cost, slips apart); a jammed or knotted fabric is
cycle-rich (high cost, sticks hard). Reversible un-weaving holds iff the contact graph is a
forest --- the textile face of \texttt{landauer\_zero\_iff\_reversible}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Friction metastability is a held state]\label{thm:friction-metastability-held}\notready\uses{thm:unweaving-is-landauer, thm:miscrossing-top, thm:kcm-activation-closure, thm:glass-mobility-zero-agreement, thm:basin-no-descending-cycle}
\emph{(Open frontier --- stated, not formalized.)} The continuum of dry-friction
metastable rest-states of a simple knit (Crassous--Poincloux--Steinberger) and beadwork
superjamming (Dreier) are held, non-unique settled configurations: the fabric state space
carries a positive-dimensional family of friction-locked fixed points --- the mechanical
projection of the held $\top$ (\cref{thm:miscrossing-top}), a contradiction borne without
explosion. Friction is the dissipative price of holding $\top$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Homogenized energy is the settled action]\label{thm:homogenized-energy-settled}\notready\uses{thm:friction-metastability-held, thm:weave-infotiles-entropy}
\emph{(Open frontier --- stated, not formalized.)} The homogenized elastic energy
$W_{\mathrm{hom}}(F)$ of a periodic draft is the settled (least-fixed-point) value of the
yarn-level bending action $E_{\mathrm{bend}} = \tfrac{B}{2}\int|\partial_s\hat t|^2\,ds$
under the interlacement constraints, and the Tiles measured entropy
(\cref{thm:weave-infotiles-entropy}) bounds the $W_{\mathrm{hom}}$ a stitch family can
realize. Mechanics is the settling of the action; the entropy is its capacity.
\end{theorem}
