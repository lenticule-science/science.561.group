\subsection{Chromatic Lorentzian carrier}

\begin{theorem}[Chromatic-Lorentzian carrier --- a genuinely non-idempotent transfinite MLD carrier]\label{thm:chromatic-lorentzian-carrier}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.ChromaticLorentzian.chromatic_lorentzian_classification}\leanok\uses{thm:transfinite-ladder-climb}
The classification needs only closure-absorption, not idempotence.  The
chromatic Lorentzian carrier is a causal chain with a one-tick propagation step
and a past-closure \(\mathrm{settle}\).  The step is non-idempotent
(\(\kappa=\omega\)), but past-closure absorbs it, so the cross-base
classification holds on a concrete non-idempotent carrier.
\end{theorem}
