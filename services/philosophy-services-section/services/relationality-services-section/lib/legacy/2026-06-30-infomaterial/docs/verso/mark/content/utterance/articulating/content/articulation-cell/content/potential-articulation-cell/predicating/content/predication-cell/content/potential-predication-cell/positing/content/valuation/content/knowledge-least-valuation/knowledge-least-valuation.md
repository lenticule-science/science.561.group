# Knowledge-Least Valuation

## What this node is

The **knowledge-least valuation** is the seed [positing](../../../../positing.md)
deposits: **bottom charge on every cell** of the predication base $\Phi$ — the
knowledge order's least element, assigned pointwise. It is emphatically *not* the
empty graph but bottom-on-every-cell, one fiber-algebra per predicate derived
from the fiber's own cell-set, never seeded by hand. Bottom is the identity of
joining-knowledge: the value positing posits before any is asserted.

## What descends from it

It is the base of every trace and the point the modal dynamic begins from.
[**Settling**](settling/settling.md) descends from this seed valuation: it
iterates the closure possibilize∘necessitate on the trace built atop this bottom
until the last valuation stops changing, reaching the fixed point.

## Orientation

Read [valuation](../../valuation.md) for the general assignment this is the least
of, and [settling](settling/settling.md) for the closure that climbs from it. The
morphism that seeded it is [positing](../../../../positing.md).
