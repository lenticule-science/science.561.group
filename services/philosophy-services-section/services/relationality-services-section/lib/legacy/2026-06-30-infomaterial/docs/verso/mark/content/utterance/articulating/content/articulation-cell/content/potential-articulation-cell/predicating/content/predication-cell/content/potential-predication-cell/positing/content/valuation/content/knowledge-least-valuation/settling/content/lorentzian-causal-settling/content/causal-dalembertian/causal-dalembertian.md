# Causal d'Alembertian

The **causal d'Alembertian** $\Box_\rho$ is a nonlocal operator on scalar fields over a [Lorentzian causal settling](../../lorentzian-causal-settling.md), generated by the *layered order-interval abundance* of the causal order.
It is the **local, weighted** reading of the same abundance the [Myrheim–Meyer dimension](../myrheim-meyer-dimension/myrheim-meyer-dimension.md) reads *globally*: where $d_\mathrm{MM}$ inverts the single ordering fraction $r(v) = |\mathrm{Apex}|/\binom{N}{2}$ to recover a *dimension*, $\Box_\rho$ weights the abundance of every past interval of an event to recover a *wave operator and its curvature*.
It is the operator through which the causal order — with no metric, no coordinates — carries **geometry-as-curvature**, the way $d_\mathrm{MM}$ carries geometry-as-dimension.

## The order interval and its past layers

For a causal order $\preceq$ on events $E$, the **order interval** between $x \preceq y$ is $$I(x,y) \;:=\; \{\, z \in E : x \prec z \prec y \,\},$$ the events strictly causally between them, and its **abundance** is $|I(x,y)|$ — the grade of the pair.
Reading the abundance of every past pair $y \prec x$ sorts the causal past of $x$ into **layers** $$L_k(x) \;:=\; \{\, y \prec x : |I(y,x)| = k \,\}.$$ Layer $L_0(x)$ is the **covering relation** — the irreducible links, no event between, the *nearest* past; layer $L_k(x)$ the past separated from $x$ by exactly $k$ intermediate events.
The layers partition the causal past: every $y \prec x$ sits in exactly one $L_k(x)$, indexed by $|I(y,x)|$.

This is the same interval abundance the settling already counts.
The [Myrheim–Meyer dimension](../myrheim-meyer-dimension/myrheim-meyer-dimension.md) reads its total ($|\mathrm{Apex}|$, the relation count); the [settling Gauss–Bonnet identity](../theorems/settling-gauss-bonnet.md) $\chi = |\mathrm{Apex}| - |L_\mathrm{ns}|$ reads its global Euler character.
The causal d'Alembertian reads the *per-event layer profile* $(|L_0(x)|, |L_1(x)|, \dots)$ — a strictly finer datum.
The layer decomposition is a finite, decidable construction of the strict regime: **proven** at the substrate level.

## The operator

Fix a discreteness scale $\ell$ (the sprinkling density $\rho = \ell^{-d}$).
The **causal d'Alembertian** of a scalar field $\phi : E \to \mathbb{R}$ at $x$ is a weighted alternating sum over the nearest past layers: $$\Box_\rho \phi(x) \;:=\; \frac{1}{\ell^2}\left[\, c_{-1}\,\phi(x) \;+\; \sum_{k \geq 0} c_k \sum_{y \in L_k(x)} \phi(y) \,\right].$$ It is **retarded** (it reaches only into the past cone of $x$) and **nonlocal** (it sums over a band of layers, not nearest neighbours alone) — the discrete face of the wave operator $\Box$ on a Lorentzian manifold, whose own nonlocality on a causal set is forced: there is no local stencil Lorentz-invariantly, so the operator must average over a causal band.

## The coefficients are the order's own Möbius inversion

The generative claim — and the point at which this operator is *derived*, not *imported*:

> The layer coefficients $c_k$ are **not** fitted to Minkowski space.
> They are the values of the **incidence-algebra Möbius function** $\mu$ of the causal order, summed over intervals of cardinality $k$ — the alternating inclusion–exclusion that inverts the interval abundance.

The incidence algebra of a locally finite poset carries a canonical Möbius function $\mu(y,x)$, defined by the inversion $\sum_{y \preceq z \preceq x} \mu(y,z) = [\,y = x\,]$.
Grouping its values by interval cardinality gives the layer coefficients $$c_k \;=\; \sum_{|I(y,x)| = k} \mu(y,x),$$ a finite alternating sum — the second-difference pattern $(c_0, c_1, c_2) = (1, -2, 1)$ at the 2-cell, the signed-binomial pattern $(1,-3,3,-1)$ at the next, each an entry of the poset's own incidence inversion.
So the d'Alembertian's nonlocality is **generated by the causal order's incidence algebra**: the same Möbius inversion that, in the settling cohomology, measures the gluing obstruction here measures the curvature.
The operator is a reading of the order, not a structure laid onto it. *(Maturity: the layer substrate is proven; the identification of $c_k$ with the poset Möbius inversion is the research-grade claim this node opens — the obligation to be discharged before $\Box_\rho$ is asserted, not assumed.)*

The incidence algebra has since been built and its core laws proven.
The Möbius function `μ` of the causal order satisfies the **orthogonality** `μ * ζ = δ` (`mobius_orthogonality_causal`), and — **Philip Hall's theorem** — equals the reduced Euler characteristic of the order complex, `μ(x,y) = ∑_k (−1)^k · #{k-chains x ⤳ y}` (`mobius_eq_hallSum`).
So the operator's weights are genuinely the order's own incidence data, and the same alternating count is what the order-complex cohomology measures — the two faces of Hall's theorem.
The operator `□` itself is built over the reals and is **linear**; its sprinkling expectation **commutes with it**, `⟨□ φ⟩ = □ ⟨φ⟩` (`dalembertian_expectation`) — the expected Benincasa–Dowker operator. *(Built+green; the exact identification of $c_k$ with the layer-grouped BD coefficients remains the research-grade obligation above.)*

## Continuum limit: the d'Alembertian carries curvature

In the Scott-continuous limit $\rho \to \infty$ (the $\kappa = \omega$ regime of the [four-regime dichotomy](four-regime-dichotomy.md) — every cell settling at a finite stage, the $\omega$-supremum the continuum), the mean of $\Box_\rho$ over sprinklings converges to the manifold wave operator *shifted by curvature*: $$\langle \Box_\rho \phi \rangle \;\xrightarrow{\ \rho \to \infty\ }\; -\tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(\Box + \tfrac{1}{2} R\right)\phi,$$ where $\Box$ is the Lorentzian d'Alembertian and $R$ the Ricci scalar.
Applied to the constant field $\phi \equiv 1$, the wave term vanishes and $$\langle \Box_\rho 1 \rangle \;\to\; -\tfrac{1}{4}\,R$$ — the **Ricci scalar recovered from the causal order alone**.
Summed over events, this is the discrete **Einstein–Hilbert action** $S = \sum_x \Box_\rho 1(x) \to -\tfrac14 \int R\sqrt{-g}$, the order's intrinsic gravitational action.

That the limit *exists* is a regime statement: it is the Scott-continuity of the settling at $\kappa = \omega$ ("no new content at the limit"), the same continuity through which the [reals close the value-settling hub](continuous-valued-settling.md).
Where the settling is aperiodic ($\kappa \geq \omega_1$), no such continuum geometry converges — the horizon sector. *(Maturity: roadmap — the continuum limit and the Ricci identity are the downstream obligation, gated by the Scott-continuous convergence of the sprinkled settling.)*

## Position in the program

The causal d'Alembertian refines the corpus's **spacetime portrait** of a settling.
The [Myrheim–Meyer dimension](../myrheim-meyer-dimension/myrheim-meyer-dimension.md) gave it geometry as a single *dimension* $d_\mathrm{MM}$; the [Gauss–Bonnet identity](../theorems/settling-gauss-bonnet.md) gave it a *global* curvature $\chi$.
The d'Alembertian gives it a **local, per-event** curvature $R(x) \sim \Box_\rho 1(x)$ — the global $\chi$ resolved into a curvature field over the events.
The portrait becomes $$\bigl(\,\underbrace{d_\mathrm{MM}}_{\text{dimension}},\ \underbrace{R(x)}_{\text{local curvature}},\ \underbrace{\chi^{(n)}}_{\text{global / topology}},\ \underbrace{\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}^{(n)}}_{\text{classifying}}\,\bigr).$$

This is the operator the **faithful-embedding** question turns on: a causal order embeds faithfully in a Lorentzian manifold only if its intrinsic invariants — dimension *and curvature* — match the manifold's. $d_\mathrm{MM}$ supplies the dimension match; $\Box_\rho \to -\tfrac12(\Box + \tfrac12 R)$ supplies the curvature match.
The two together are the geometric content of the embedding; the uniqueness of the embedding (the Sorkin *Hauptvermutung*) remains the open conjecture downstream.

### Worked sketch: the diamond

The corpus diamond ($a \prec b, a \prec c, a \prec d, b \prec d, c \prec d$) at its top event $d$ has past $\{a, b, c\}$ with intervals $I(b,d) = I(c,d) = \varnothing$ and $I(a,d) = \{b, c\}$ — so the layer profile is $(|L_0|, |L_1|, |L_2|)(d) = (2, 0, 1)$, partitioning the past of size $3$.
The d'Alembertian at $d$ reads $$\Box_\rho \phi(d) \;=\; \frac{1}{\ell^2}\bigl[\, c_{-1}\,\phi(d) + c_0(\phi(b) + \phi(c)) + c_2\,\phi(a) \,\bigr],$$ the two covering links $b, c$ entering at weight $c_0$ and the doubly-bracketed $a$ at weight $c_2$ — the smallest causal set on which the *layered* (not merely relational) abundance is non-trivial, and so the smallest witness that the d'Alembertian sees more than $d_\mathrm{MM}$ does.
