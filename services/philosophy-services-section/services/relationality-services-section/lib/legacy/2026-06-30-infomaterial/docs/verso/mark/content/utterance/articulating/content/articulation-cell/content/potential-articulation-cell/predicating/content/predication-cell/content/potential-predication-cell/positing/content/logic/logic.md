# Logic

## What this node is

**Logic** is what [positing](../../positing.md) generates: predication *with
evidence admitted*. A bare predication says only *that* something is predicated;
positing lets value enter as a charge, and the structure of "what that admitted
evidence licenses" is **entailment** — $\Gamma \vdash A$, "from the assumptions
$\Gamma$ the conclusion $A$ follows, by licensed steps." Logic is where entailment
is studied as itself: its objects are propositions, sequents, and proofs; its one
relation is consequence.

It is not the discipline "mathematical logic" shelved by topic — it is the object
positing makes. The corpus reads a filepath as a Curry–Howard coordinate precisely
because descending a path *is deriving*: each segment a licensed step,
`recognize_child = recognize_parent ∧ local` the conjunction rule building a
sequent. An arrow is an entailment; that single law is read here.

## Generated from positing

Evidence is admitted at positing as a [bilattice](../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md)
charge — and *how* that evidence is allowed to behave is what splits logic into its
systems. Three commitments of this corpus are three such behaviours:

- **intuitionistic** — constructive content is preserved ($\lnot\lnot a \ne a$);
- **substructural** at the language base — whether an assumption may be reused
  (contraction), discarded (weakening), or reordered (exchange) are *options*, not
  laws; dropping them makes resources and order first-class (the Lambek face);
- **paraconsistent** at the evidence base — a contradiction is *carried*, not
  exploded, because the admitted charge has a $\top$ ("told both") that does not
  trivialize its neighbours.

## What it generates

The systems, each a discipline on the admitted evidence, derive under `content/`:
**intuitionistic-logic**, **modal-logic** (the $\Box/\Diamond$ of accessibility),
**many-valued-logic** (the bilattice charge itself), **paraconsistent-logic**,
**substructural-logic** (with the Lambek calculus and linear logic beneath it),
**propositional-** and **first-order-logic**, and **decidability**. Their shared
proof theory — the **derivation** (a finite licensed proof tree), sequent calculus,
natural deduction, cut-elimination, normalization — is the machinery the whole
cluster runs on, and the place the Curry–Howard–Lambek identification of proof,
program, and morphism is stated.

sibling carriers under positing are the [bilattice](../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md)
