# Sheaf

## What this node is

A **sheaf** is the fixed point [settling](../../settling.md) packages:
`{base, section}` — the value-saturated predication base together with a
[section](content/section/section.md) that **glues** the local settlements into a
globally agreeing whole. It is the crystal the four-regime descent deposits, the
finite projection the whole cascade was draining toward.

Because settling reaches a *unique* fixed point under confluence, the gluing
**succeeds with no obstruction** — the cohomology vanishes, $H^1 = 0$. A sheaf
whose section failed to glue would carry nonzero cohomology, the measure of
disagreement among local settlements; at the terminus of a converged descent
there is none.

## Orientation

Descend to [section](content/section/section.md) for the agreeing assignment over
the base. The morphism that packages the sheaf is [settling](../../settling.md).
