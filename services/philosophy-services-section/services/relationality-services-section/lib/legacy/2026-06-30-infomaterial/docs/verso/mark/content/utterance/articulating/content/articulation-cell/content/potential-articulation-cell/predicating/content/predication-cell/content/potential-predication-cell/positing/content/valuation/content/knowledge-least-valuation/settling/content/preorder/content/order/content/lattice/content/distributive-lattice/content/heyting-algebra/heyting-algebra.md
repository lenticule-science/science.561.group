# Heyting algebra

## What this node is

A **Heyting algebra** is a bounded
[distributive lattice](../../distributive-lattice.md) carrying one further
operation — an **implication** $a \Rightarrow b$ — fixed by the residuation law
$$c \le (a \Rightarrow b) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c \wedge a \le b.$$
That is: $a \Rightarrow b$ is the *greatest* element whose [meet](../../../meet/meet.md)
with $a$ stays below $b$. The law says implication is the **right adjoint** of
$a \wedge -$; the operation is determined by the order, not added freely, so a
distributive lattice is a Heyting algebra in at most one way.

From implication comes the **pseudo-complement** $\lnot a := a \Rightarrow \bot$,
the largest element disjoint from $a$. It is here that the algebra parts ways with
the classical one: in general $a \vee \lnot a \neq \top$ and $\lnot\lnot a \neq a$.
Excluded middle is *not* a law. A Boolean algebra is the degenerate collapse —
a Heyting algebra in which every element has a true complement, $\lnot\lnot a = a$
restored — and is the classical projection of this constructive object, not a thing
beneath it.

## Generated from the distributive lattice

Distributivity is exactly what makes the residual exist. The right adjoint
$a \Rightarrow -$ to $a \wedge -$ requires $a \wedge -$ to preserve all joins, and
that preservation *is* the (infinitary) distributive law; without distributivity
there is no largest $c$ with $c \wedge a \le b$ to name. So the implication is
derived **here**, one step below
[distributive lattice](../../distributive-lattice.md), and not under a bare
[lattice](../../../../lattice.md): the generating move is "adjoin the residual that
distributivity makes available," and boundedness supplies the $\bot$ that turns
the residual into a negation.

This is the order-side of why the corpus's truth is **constructive**. The
[settling](../../../../../../../../../../settling.md) ascent reaches values that may be
partial — told-something-but-not-everything — and a partial value need not have a
complement that joins with it to $\top$. The failure of $\lnot\lnot a = a$ is the
order-theoretic image of the Curry–Howard discipline that a proof of $\lnot\lnot
P$ is not a proof of $P$: evidence does not double-negate into existence. The
implication $\Rightarrow$ is the entailment of that constructive truth read on the
value lattice.

## What it generates

The Heyting structure is where the order side meets the proof side:

- its implication is the semantics a logical connective points *down* to — the
  intuitionistic implication of a constructive [logic](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../logic/logic.md)
  is interpreted as this residual, a downward reference from positing's logic to
  this object under settling, not a generation of logic by it;
- the open sets of any space form a complete Heyting algebra (the interior of a
  complement is the pseudo-complement), and the subobject classifier of a topos is
  a Heyting algebra — the same object reached through the
  [sheaf](../../../../../../../../../sheaf/sheaf.md) side of settling.

## Orientation

Generated by [distributive lattice](../../distributive-lattice.md), and through it
by [lattice](../../../../lattice.md), [order](../../../../../../order.md), and
[settling](../../../../../../../../../../settling.md). It assumes the
[bounded](../../../bounded-lattice/bounded-lattice.md) refinement for its $\bot$;
when the underlying lattice is also
[complete](../../../complete-lattice/complete-lattice.md) it is a frame. The worked
construction of the implication and the failure of excluded middle are in
[`tutorials`](tutorials).
