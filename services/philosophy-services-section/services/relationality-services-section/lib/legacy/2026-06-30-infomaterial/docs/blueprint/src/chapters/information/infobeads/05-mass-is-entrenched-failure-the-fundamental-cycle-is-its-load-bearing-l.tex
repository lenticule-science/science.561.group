\begin{theorem}[Each entrenched failure is one rest-mass quantum]\label{thm:mass-quantum}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.mass_strict_of_entrench}\leanok\uses{thm:condensate-is-top}
Driving one more lenticule into the condensed regime adds one unit of mass
(\texttt{mass\_quantum\_condensed}); a floppy site adds none
(\texttt{mass\_quantum\_floppy}); and adding material never sheds mass
(\texttt{mass\_mono\_sublist}).
\end{theorem}

$\bot \to t \to \top \to f \to \bot$

held-$\top$ (\texttt{one\_failure\_per\_cycle}), and gives a single
load-bearing $\check H^1$ generator (\texttt{fundamentalNerve\_h1} $=1$).
\end{theorem}
