\section{The finite local MLD base case}

\begin{theorem}[The MLD classification at the bilattice base --- the converse, complete]\label{thm:bilattice-base-mld}\lean{Infomaterial.Positing.LocalMld.bilattice_base_mld}\leanok\uses{thm:negate-tle, thm:negate-duality}
At the single-charge base, the local MLD classification is complete in both
directions. Gauge equivalence under charge negation is exactly equality of
the local classifying invariant, because the invariant fibers are precisely
the finite negation orbits.
\end{theorem}
