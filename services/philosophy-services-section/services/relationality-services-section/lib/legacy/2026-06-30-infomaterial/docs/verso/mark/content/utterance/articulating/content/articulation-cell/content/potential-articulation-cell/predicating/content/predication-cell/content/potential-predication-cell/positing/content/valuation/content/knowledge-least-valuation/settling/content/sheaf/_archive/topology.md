# Topology

## What this node is
Topology is generated by **settle**, the final morphism in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. A topology on a set is its lattice of open sets, and — by De Morgan and the Kuratowski axioms — equivalently a closure operator `cl` that is extensive, idempotent, and join-preserving. Settling is that closure: an idempotent process that takes a posited collection of points or relations to its smallest closed completion. The closed sets are exactly the fixed points of settling, and any operator satisfying the four Kuratowski axioms determines a unique topology. This node is what settle produces when applied to spatial structure.

## What it generates / contains
There is no canonical `content/` subconcept directory under this node yet; the body sits as un-canonicalized source in `_archive/`. That material covers:

- **Point-set foundations** — topological space, open set, closed set, basis, cover, compactness, continuous map, homeomorphism.
- **Closure** — the Kuratowski closure operator and its equivalence with the open-set axioms (the settle morphism made explicit).
- **Frames and locales** — the open-set lattice as a complete Heyting algebra, and pointfree topology where the frame of opens is primary and points are secondary (Isbell locales, sublocales as nuclei, Stone duality).
- **Sheaf and topos material** — presheaf, sheaf, sheaf condition, site, geometric morphism, subobject classifier, Lawvere-Tierney topology, infinity-toposes.
- **Homotopy** — paths, homotopy, homotopy equivalence, fundamental and homotopy groups, homotopy type, directed homotopy.
