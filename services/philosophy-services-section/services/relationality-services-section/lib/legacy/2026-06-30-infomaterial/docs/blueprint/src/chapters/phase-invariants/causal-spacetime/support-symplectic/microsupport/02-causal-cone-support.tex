\subsubsection{Causal cone support}

\begin{theorem}[Causal microsupport unifies propagation and locality]\label{thm:causal-microsupport}\lean{Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Support.Causal.causal_microsupport_unifies}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-laplacian, thm:causal-alexandrov-topology}
The spatial companion of \cref{thm:microlocal-support}: the microsupport of an
event section is its causal closure, exactly the union of the future cones of
its support. From this cone-confinement fact come propagation of singularities
and spacelike locality: a point source propagates along $J^+(a)$, never to a
distinct spacelike event, and re-propagating adds nothing. The PDE reading and
the locality reading are the same causal-closure theorem viewed from different
surfaces.
\end{theorem}
