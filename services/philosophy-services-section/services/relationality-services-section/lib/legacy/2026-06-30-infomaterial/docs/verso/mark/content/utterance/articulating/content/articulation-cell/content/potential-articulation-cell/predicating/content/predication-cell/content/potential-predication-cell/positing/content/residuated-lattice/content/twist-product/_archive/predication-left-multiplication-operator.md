# Left multiplication operator

For a fixed element $a \in \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ of a [twist product](../objects/twist-product.md), **left multiplication by $a$** is the unary operator

$$\mathrm{L}_a : \mathbf{L}^\bowtie \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie, \quad \mathrm{L}_a(x) := a \odot x,$$

where $\odot$ is the [twist-product fusion](twist-product-fusion.md). Iterated application gives orbits $(\mathrm{L}_a^n(x))_n$ in $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.
