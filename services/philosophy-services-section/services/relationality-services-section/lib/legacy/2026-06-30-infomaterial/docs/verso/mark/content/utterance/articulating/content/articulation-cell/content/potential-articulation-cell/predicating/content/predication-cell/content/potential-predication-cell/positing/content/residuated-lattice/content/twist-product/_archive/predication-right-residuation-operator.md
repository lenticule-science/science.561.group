# Right residuation operator

For a fixed element $a \in \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ of a [twist product](../objects/twist-product.md), **right residuation by $a$** is the unary operator

$$\mathrm{R}_a : \mathbf{L}^\bowtie \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie, \quad \mathrm{R}_a(x) := a \Rightarrow x,$$

where $\Rightarrow$ is the [twist-product right residual](twist-product-right-residual.md). Iterated application gives orbits $(\mathrm{R}_a^n(x))_n$ in $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.
