\subsection{Limit-stage ladder climb}

\begin{theorem}[Transfinite ladder-climb --- the up-to-closure coinduction climbs the limit stage]\label{thm:transfinite-ladder-climb}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.UpTo.transfinite_ladder_climb}\leanok\uses{thm:coalgebraic-crossbase-mld}
The up-to-closure coinduction extends from successor orbits to the
\(\mathrm{coalgebraOrbitLimit}\) join.  For the realized carrier, the limit join
is squeezed between \(E\) and \(\mathrm{Cl}\,E\), so settling collapses it:
\(\mathrm{settle}(v_\mu)=\mathrm{settle}\,E\).  The remaining residue is not the
coinduction but a genuinely non-idempotent transfinite carrier.
\end{theorem}
