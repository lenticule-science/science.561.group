# Section

## What this node is

A **section** is the globally agreeing assignment over a [sheaf](../../sheaf.md)'s
base — the settled value at each cell that the local settlements **glue into**. It
is the content of the fixed point: what the descent finally says, cell by cell,
once the modal closure has stabilized and all disagreement is resolved. A global
section exists exactly when the gluing succeeds ($H^1 = 0$); it is the settled
valuation read as one coherent whole over the base.

## Orientation

Read [sheaf](../../sheaf.md) for the `{base, section}` package this completes. The
morphism that settles the section is
[settling](../../../../settling.md).
