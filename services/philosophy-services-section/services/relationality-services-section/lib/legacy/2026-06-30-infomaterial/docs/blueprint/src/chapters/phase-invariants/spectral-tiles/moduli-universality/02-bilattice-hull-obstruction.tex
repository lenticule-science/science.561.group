\subsection{Bilattice hull obstruction}

\begin{theorem}[The lax-idempotence obstruction is antisymmetry]\label{thm:lax-slack-is-antisymmetry}\lean{Infomaterial.Valuation.ModalFrame.lax_idempotence_obstruction_is_antisymmetry}\leanok\uses{thm:kz-iff-strict}
The contradiction dynamics need the closure 2-monad's $\mu$ to be a genuine
non-identity 2-cell: the lax slack
$\mathrm{Cl}(\mathrm{Cl}\,v) <_k \mathrm{Cl}\,v$ with
$\mathrm{Cl}(\mathrm{Cl}\,v)\neq\mathrm{Cl}\,v$, the home of a stable $\top$.
Abstractly (\texttt{kz\_strict\_of\_antisymm},
\texttt{lax\_slack\_witnesses\_antisymmetry\_failure}) a genuine lax cell is a
witnessed failure of antisymmetry; concretely on the thin valuation poset
$\le_k$ the KZ inequality is equivalent to strict idempotence and there is no
lax slack (\texttt{closureOp\_no\_lax\_slack}). So the
contradiction-dynamics frontier is red not merely for want of reals: it needs
the value carrier to stop being antisymmetric. This is the order-parametric
form of \cref{thm:kz-iff-strict}, naming the obstruction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The inverse-limit hull carries the classifying invariant]\label{thm:inverse-hull-pairs-clinv}\lean{Infomaterial.Persistence.Aperiodic.hull_pairs_clinv}\leanok\uses{thm:clinv-dichotomy}
The geometric dual of the Anderson--Putnam direct limit (cohomology): the
tiling hull $\Omega = \varprojlim \mathrm{stage}$ is built as the type of
threads (coherent families) of an \texttt{InverseSystem}, well-defined
(\texttt{Thread.ext}) and totally-disconnected-separated by its finite-stage
projections (\texttt{hull\_separated\_by\_stages}). The seam
$\Omega \to H^1$ is surjective and realizes the aperiodic witness's nonzero
class $[1]\neq0$ by a thread (\texttt{hull\_pairs\_clinv}): the hull and the
colimit are the two halves of one Anderson--Putnam node, the hull carrying the
$\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}$ the colimit classifies. The constant $H^1$-valued
hull here is countable; the uncountable Cantor odometer and the
$\mathrm{hullLeaf}\cong\mathbb R$ accretion remain frontier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Bilattice consensus admits stable $\top$-conflict (the signed-Laplacian frontier)]\label{thm:bilattice-stable-top-conflict}\notready\uses{thm:lawvere-harmonic-clinv-zero, thm:aperiodic-local-constancy-failure, thm:lax-slack-is-antisymmetry, thm:inverse-hull-pairs-clinv}
\emph{Open frontier, stated but not formalized.} Over the FOUR bilattice the
Lawvere/Tarski Laplacian admits the ``told-both'' charge $\top$ as a stable
harmonic state: an antagonistic interaction settles to a localized $\top$-seam
rather than diverging. This is the regime where the signed real sheaf
Laplacian of Hansen--Ghrist--Riess is reported unstable for antagonistic
interactions, and where the nonlinear sheaf-diffusion identifiability of Riess
et al.\ requires strong convexity and $H^1(G;\mathcal F)=0$. The claim-site is
a settling/consensus convergence theorem whose obstruction is the nonzero
classifying invariant $\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}\neq0$ localized to the
$\top$-seam (the aperiodic regime,
\cref{thm:aperiodic-local-constancy-failure}), recovering the strong-convex
and $H^1=0$ case as the vanishing diagonal
(\cref{thm:lawvere-harmonic-clinv-zero}). Red until the bilattice consensus
dynamics and the $\top$-localized obstruction are modeled past the
unconditionally-gluing \v{C}ech model.
\end{theorem}
