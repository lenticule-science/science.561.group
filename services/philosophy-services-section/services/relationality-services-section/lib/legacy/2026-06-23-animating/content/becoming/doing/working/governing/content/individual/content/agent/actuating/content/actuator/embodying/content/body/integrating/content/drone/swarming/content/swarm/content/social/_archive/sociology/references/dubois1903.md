## Why this stub exists

The discipline cannot honestly leave Du Bois unreferenced. *The Souls of Black Folk* is the originating text of the analytic the [`black-radical-tradition/_index.md`](theories/critical-theory/theories/black-radical-tradition/_index.md) summarizes ("the color line" as the problem of the twentieth century; double consciousness; the standpoint that organizes the lineage).
Du Bois's later *Black Reconstruction in America* (1935) is equally constitutive — its analysis of the general strike of the enslaved during the Civil War is the move Robinson and the racial-capitalism tradition extend.

Both works should be promoted to full annotations from a direct read. *Black Reconstruction* will get its own reference note (`dubois1935.md`) when it does.

Person-level reference notes for Du Bois may live at `library/references/people/w-e-b-du-bois.md` (currently absent — also a follow-up).
