- [[define]]:: [[coherence]] is the [[condition]] in which an [[act]], its corresponding [[condition|conditions]], and supporting [[structure|structures]] hold together in a stable, self-sufficient dynamic. It is what marks that a relational system is **complete enough** to persist as a real part of the [[domain of relating]].
    

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] shows:
    
    - A [[triage/thing]] is brought about by the primitive [[act|acts]] of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]];
        
    - These generate [[condition|conditions]] of [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]];
        
    - Held together by the [[condition]] of [[tension]], which induces [[difference]];
        
    - Sustained by [[relating|relating]], which maintains the dynamic;
        
    - Clarified and stabilized through [[relation]] and the formation of a [[relative]] as a minimal [[structure]].
        
- Each of these elements is necessary but not sufficient on its own:
    
    - [[act|Acts]] do the work;
        
    - [[condition|Conditions]] are held open;
        
    - [[structure|Structures]] stabilize and durably maintain the results.
        
- But for the system to be **self-sufficient**:
    
    - These elements must not only be present but also **held together as a unified whole**;
        
    - Their interplay must resolve without collapse, dissipation, or irresolvable contradiction.
        
- This unified holding-together is the [[condition]] of [[coherence]]:
    
    - It ensures that all necessary components of the relational system **mutually support** one another;
        
    - It marks the point at which the system achieves **closure**: nothing further is needed to sustain its being.
        

## Implications

- [[coherence]] is the minimal indicator that a relational architecture is **real** within the [[domain of relating]]:
    
    - Without [[coherence]], even if acts and conditions arise, they fail to persist meaningfully;
        
    - Coherence is what allows a [[triage/thing]] to maintain its integrity over time.
        
- Any system lacking [[coherence]]:
    
    - Breaks down into unintelligibility or dissolution;
        
    - Cannot serve as a foundation for further [[act|acts]] or [[structure|structures]].
        
- [[coherence]] is what makes further derivations possible:
    
    - Once coherence is established at one level, new layers of [[relational dynamics]] can be built upon it;
        
    - This recursive building is what allows complex relational systems to emerge from minimal foundations.
