MALE (medium-altitude long-endurance) is a classification for UAVs designed to operate at altitudes of 3,000–9,000 m (10,000–30,000 ft) for extended periods, typically 12–30+ hours.
MALE platforms are the workhorse of military UAV operations, combining enough altitude to remain above small-arms fire and short-range air defenses with enough [endurance](./endurance.md) for persistent surveillance and strike missions.

Representative MALE platforms:

| Platform | Country | Weight (kg) | Endurance (hr) | Wingspan (m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MQ-9 Reaper | USA | 4,760 | 27 | 20 |
| Bayraktar TB2 | Turkey | 650 | 24 | 12 |
| Wing Loong II | China | 4,200 | 20 | 20.5 |
| Heron TP | Israel | 5,300 | 30+ | 26 |
| TAI Anka | Turkey | 1,600 | 24 | 17.5 |

MALE design is characterized by: - High [aspect ratio](./aspect-ratio.md) wings (12–25) for cruise efficiency - Turboprop or piston [propulsion](../concepts/uav-propulsion-systems.md) for fuel economy at moderate altitude - Conventional [planforms](../concepts/wing-planform-selection-for-uavs.md) with separate wing and tail - [Composite](./composite.md) construction for low structural weight fraction - Satellite datalinks for beyond-line-of-sight control - Full sensor suites (EO/IR, SAR radar, SIGINT)

The engineering constraints of MALE UAVs differ fundamentally from small or expendable drones: [fatigue](./fatigue.md) is a primary design driver (thousands of flight hours), [safety factors](./safety-factor.md) approach certified aircraft levels, and aerodynamic efficiency at altitude — where air density is 30–60% of sea level — demands careful [airfoil](./airfoil.md) selection and high [L/D](./lift-to-drag-ratio.md).
