Open work for ludics.
The subdomain's first-class question is the [Girard–Caillois substrate-correspondence question](girard-caillois-substrate-correspondence.md), which asks how Caillois's four-fold typology of play (agon, alea, mimicry, ilinx) relates to Girard's analysis of mimetic desire and ritual.

Beyond that question, the subdomain's open structural work includes:

- **The status of the magic circle.** Huizinga's bounded ludic space remains the discipline's most-cited and most-contested concept.
  The question of whether it does positive analytical work or merely names a historically contingent feature of Western leisure has not been settled.
- **The structure of contemporary platform-mediated play.** Digital games and platform-mediated ludic practice have reorganized many of the relations the classical typologies analyzed.
  The analytical question is what survives the reorganization, what is genuinely new, and what — if anything — that means for the typologies themselves.
- **The relation between play and political action.** The Situationist and contemporary game-design traditions both engage the question of whether play can be a site of political work.
  The structural question is what conditions are required for the relation to be operative rather than recuperative.

The open work is currently registered in this directory and in the substantive texts (especially [`texts/ludic-analysis`](../texts/ludic-analysis.md) and [`texts/the-magic-circle-and-its-critics`](../texts/the-magic-circle-and-its-critics.md)).
Further question files should be added here as the work matures.
