# The substrate's three acts are the three structural advances of the temporal-2-category lifted along the predication fibration

**Claim.** Every substrate state advance — every operation that moves the [predication-graph store](../objects/predication-graph-store.md) from one state to another — is one of three structurally distinct moves, and the three exhaust the advance modes available to a conformant predicated computer:

- **Sequential act.** [Tick-advance](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/morphisms/tick-advance.md) on a single entity. Math content: an $\mathbb{M}$-step 1-cell $(\tau, X) \to (\tau{+}1, X)$ in the [temporal 2-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/temporal-2-category.md) $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}, \lambda)$, where $\mathbb{M}$ is the [Gray–Street fibration 2-monad](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/gray-street-fibration-monad.md). One ordinal step at fixed parameter object.
- **Concurrent act.** [Merge](../morphisms/merge.md) of two replica stores. Math content: the cellwise [knowledge-order](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md) join $\vee_k$ on the [predication fiber algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), pointwise across the entity index — the semilattice operation of [settling-merge-semilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/settling-merge-semilattice.md). Horizontal in the temporal 2-category at a fixed ordinal layer.
- **Generative act.** [Settle](../morphisms/settle.md) on a region. Math content: an iterate of the [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ acting fiberwise on the [predication fibration](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md), per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md). Vertical: replaces a fiber's value with its $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-saturate without advancing the temporal 2-category's 0-cell.

The three acts decompose the substrate's advance because they live on three structurally distinct axes of the structure obtained by lifting $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}, \lambda)$ along the predication fibration with $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ acting fiberwise: $\mathbb{M}$-step (temporal axis), $\vee_k$-join (replica axis), $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-iterate (saturation axis). The trichotomy is the substrate's *engineering interface schema* — the classification by which conformant operators declare and witness their advance content, with normal forms and audit consequences as the resolving content. The claim that every conformant composite is a finite word in these three letters is an engineering-conformance commitment on operator design, not a derived universal-property theorem on a free 2-category; substrates whose operators step outside this schema are non-conformant per the rules-out section.

## Why this holds

Three observations together force the trichotomy.

**Sequential is the temporal-2-category's $\mathbb{M}$-step.** Per [temporal-2-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/temporal-2-category.md), the 1-cells of $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}, \lambda)$ from $(\sigma, X)$ to $(\tau, X)$ for $\sigma \leq \tau$ are the iterated-$\mathbb{M}$-step morphisms $\mathbb{M}^\beta \mathbb{N}^\alpha X \to X$ with $\tau - \sigma = \alpha + \beta$. The substrate's tick-advance moves one entity from its state at history $t$ to its state at $s \star t$ — an $\mathbb{M}$-step at $\alpha = 0, \beta = 1$ on the entity's parameter object. Per the temporal 2-category's strict-idempotent collapse (the **strict regime**, where $\mathbb{M}$ and $\mathbb{N}$ are strict-idempotent and $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}, \lambda)$ collapses to the [settling bound](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-bound.md) $\lambda$ as a 1-category), the substrate's tick on a strict-regime closure-rule system is a 1-category morphism; in the Scott-continuous and higher regimes the tick retains its 2-cell coherence content per the [regime-classification](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), and the substrate's witness records the 2-cell data when it is non-trivial.

**Concurrent is the predication-fiber-algebra semilattice join.** Per [merge](../morphisms/merge.md) and [crdt-convergence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence.md), the substrate's merge is cellwise $\vee_k$ followed by re-settle. The $\vee_k$-component is the [settling-merge-semilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/settling-merge-semilattice.md) operation — commutative, associative, idempotent — pointwise on the entity-pair index. This is the substrate-content version of horizontal composition in $\mathbf{T}$: merging two settlings at a common ordinal layer combines their replica content without advancing the layer.

**Generative is the closure-2-monad iterate.** Per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md) and [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), settle on a region is the iterated functor $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ applied fiberwise to the region's predication valuation. This is structurally distinct from $\mathbb{M}$-step: $\mathbb{M}$-step changes the temporal-2-category 0-cell while leaving the fiber valuation policy fixed; $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-iterate saturates the fiber valuation under the closure-rule system while leaving the temporal-2-category 0-cell fixed. The two operate on orthogonal axes of the lifted structure.

**Adjoint relations across the three axes.** The math content predicts (and the substrate confirms) three composability laws:

- **Sequential then concurrent = concurrent then sequential, up to per-replica tick alignment.** Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), tick-advance commutes with merge whenever the two replicas' rule-systems agree: $\mathrm{merge}(\mathrm{tick}(G_1), \mathrm{tick}(G_2)) = \mathrm{tick}(\mathrm{merge}(G_1, G_2))$. This is the substrate-content shadow of the Beck–Chevalley square for $\mathbb{M} \dashv \mathbb{N}$ on $\mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C}$.
- **Concurrent then generative absorbs into generative.** Per the closure-merge commutation lemma underlying [crdt-convergence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence.md), $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}(\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}(X) \vee_k Y) = \mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}(X \vee_k Y)$. Re-settle after merge is structurally one generative act, not two.
- **Generative idempotent on settled input.** Per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}} \circ \mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}} \cong \mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$. Two generative acts in succession compose to one; the substrate's settle on settled input is a no-op-witness per [settle](../morphisms/settle.md).

The trichotomy is the conformant-operator schema for the lifted structure: each of the three axes — temporal ($\mathbb{M}$), replica ($\vee_k$), saturation ($\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$) — admits its own primitive advance, and the conformant-operator discipline (per the witness contracts on [tick-advance](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/morphisms/tick-advance.md), [merge](../morphisms/merge.md), [settle](../morphisms/settle.md)) requires every composite operator to be presented as a finite word in these three primitives with the per-axis composition laws above. The schema is closed under the conformant composition rules; advances outside the schema are ruled non-conformant per the rules-out section, not non-existent.

## Engineering consequence

- **Mode classification on operations is structural, not stylistic.** Each substrate operation declares its act-mode by its axis content: a closure-rule application is $\mathbb{M}$-step at $\beta = 1$ (sequential), a CRDT replica merge is $\vee_k$ (concurrent), a settle pass is $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-iterate (generative). The audit can extract the act decomposition from the witness's recorded operator, without needing the operator to self-declare.
- **Per-mode termination discipline is regime-conditioned.** Sequential ticks terminate per [fuel-bound](teaching/explanations/fuel-bound.md). Concurrent advance terminates when all replicas converge per [sync](../morphisms/sync.md) — bounded by the diameter of the federation graph times the per-replica tick cost. Generative advance terminates per the closure ordinal $\kappa$ of the active closure-rule system, classified by [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md): finite in strict, $\omega$ in Scott-continuous, transfinite in non-Scott-continuous regimes.
- **Audit can detect mode confusion.** A "sequential advance" whose witness records a $\leq_k$-jump only achievable by closure saturation (a generative act mislabeled as sequential) is structurally inconsistent — the witness's seed-to-result valuation gap is bigger than one $\mathbb{M}$-step can provide. The audit surfaces such mismatches as conformance failures per [substrate-operations-are-bigraded-modal](substrate-operations-are-bigraded-modal.md).
- **Composite operators have a canonical normal form.** Every substrate composed operator reduces, by the three composability laws above, to a finite word in $(\mathbb{M}, \vee_k, \mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}})$ in normal form: all $\vee_k$ first (replica join), then all $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ (settle), then all $\mathbb{M}$ (tick). The substrate's [canonical-form](teaching/explanations/canonical-form.md) discipline can normalize composite-operator witnesses into this form for content-addressing.
- **Federation reduces to act-by-act reconciliation.** Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), two substrates' state difference decomposes as a difference along each of the three axes; reconciliation runs the corresponding inverse on each axis ($\mathbb{M}$-step the lagging replica forward, $\vee_k$ the divergent fiber values, $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ the merged result). The act trichotomy makes this a finite procedure rather than an open negotiation.

## What the theorem rules out

- **A "fourth act."** A substrate operation that is not expressible as a finite word in $(\mathbb{M}, \vee_k, \mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}})$ — for example, a non-monotone "advance" that decreases $\leq_k$, or a "merge" that is not $\vee_k$ on the fiber — is non-conformant. Per [closure-driven-settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-driven-settling-monotonicity.md), no conformant operator decreases $\leq_k$; the trichotomy is closed under composition.
- **Implicit acts (advance with no act-mode witness).** A substrate that "just settles" without recording which act-axis its advance occupied has lost the structural decomposition. Per the witness contract on each of [settle](../morphisms/settle.md), [merge](../morphisms/merge.md), and [tick-advance](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/morphisms/tick-advance.md), the operator emits a witness that locates the advance on its axis; an operator whose witness omits this is non-conformant.
- **Ordering policies that violate composability.** A substrate that requires merge-then-tick to mean something different from tick-then-merge (beyond per-replica alignment) has broken the Beck–Chevalley shadow above. Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), conformant federation requires the commutation; non-commuting designs are non-conformant.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's [predication fibration](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md) admits the [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) action (per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md)).
- The substrate's regime is classified per [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md) so that the temporal-2-category's behavior at the substrate's regime is determinate.
- The substrate's value-domain admits the knowledge-order semilattice join $\vee_k$ per [predication-fiber-algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) and [settling-merge-semilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/settling-merge-semilattice.md).

## Position

This theorem resolves the set-aside [three-acts-decomposition](questions/three-acts-decomposition.md) by identifying the math structure the three acts decompose: the lift of the [temporal 2-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/temporal-2-category.md) along the [predication fibration](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md), with the [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) acting fiberwise. The three substrate acts are the three structural advances of that lifted structure; their composability is the substrate-content shadow of the Gray–Street/Emmenegger 2-adjunction's Beck–Chevalley square paired with the closure 2-monad's lax-idempotence.

Companion theorem: [access-patterns-as-temporal-2-category-limit-colimit-triad](access-patterns-as-temporal-2-category-limit-colimit-triad.md) classifies the substrate's *observation* triad (Directed Shape, Dynamics, Cosections) as the limit/atom/colimit triad of diagrams in the same lifted structure. The two trichotomies — advance and observation — are dual on the temporal axis.
