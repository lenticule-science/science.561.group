* Existential Stratified Directed Infinity-Homotopy Geometry Model of Assemblage Theory
** Abstract
This paper develops an *Existential Stratified Directed Infinity–Homotopy Geometry* (ESDIHG) model of *Assemblage Theory*, interpreting Deleuze and Guattari’s notions of territorialization, deterritorialization, and consistency as internal modal phenomena within a cohesive ∞-topos.
Assemblages are realized as coherent homotopy-colimiting diagrams in a stratified directed ∞-site; their stability and transformation correspond to ambidextrous equivalences between limits and colimits in the *Field of Existence*.
This construction provides a higher-categorical semantics of relational composition, grounding assemblage theory in the formal language of directed homotopy and cohesive geometry.

** 1. Introduction
*** 1.1 Motivation
Assemblage theory describes social, material, and affective formations as emergent multiplicities rather than substances.
Each assemblage is a dynamic configuration of heterogeneous elements, stabilized locally yet open to transformation.
While originally philosophical, this notion can be formalized within higher category theory: an assemblage becomes a *coherent diagram* whose existence consists in the stability of its directed relational data.

The *Existential Stratified Directed Infinity–Homotopy Geometry* (ESDIHG) introduced in prior work provides a precise setting for this intuition.
Within a stratified directed cohesive ∞-topos $(\mathcal{X}, \triangleright)$, existence corresponds to inhabited homotopy colimits of constructible diagrams.
We extend this structure to model the dynamics of territorialization and deterritorialization as Kan-extension flows, thus producing a fully formal model of assemblage formation, transformation, and consistency.

** 2. Background: Existential Stratified Directed Geometry
Let $(\mathcal{X},\triangleright)$ be a *stratified directed ∞-site*, with strata $\Sigma$ and directed modality $\triangleright$.
Objects of $\mathcal{X}$ represent differentiated regions of a cohesive field; arrows classified by $\triangleright$ encode forward (noninvertible) morphisms, such as causal or temporal flow.

#+BEGIN_LaTeX
\begin{definition}[Derived Relational Object]
A \emph{Derived Relational Object} (DRO) in $(\mathcal{X},\triangleright)$
is a coherent diagram
\[
\mathcal{C} : \mathrm{Exit}_\infty(U) \longrightarrow \mathcal{E},
\]
where $\mathcal{E}$ is a stable $\infty$--category of conditions inside the ambient cohesive $\infty$--topos.
Existence of $\mathcal{C}$ is given by inhabitedness of the propositional truncation
\[
\|\,\operatorname{hcolim}\mathcal{C}\,\|.
\]
\end{definition}
#+END_LaTeX

The full sub-∞-topos of all such coherent diagrams forms the *∞-Topos of Existence* $\mathfrak{E}_x$.
Its global homotopy limit
\[
\mathcal{F} := \operatorname{hlim}_{F\in \mathfrak{E}_x} F
\]
is the *Field of Existence*, satisfying the ambidextrous equivalence
\[
\operatorname{hlim}\mathcal{F} \;\simeq\; \operatorname{hcolim}\mathcal{F}.
\]
This fixed-point expresses the self-consistency of being.

** 3. Assemblages as Coherent Stratified Diagrams
*** 3.1 Definition
#+BEGIN_LaTeX
\begin{definition}[Assemblage]
An \emph{Assemblage} $A$ in ESDIHG is a pair $(\mathcal{C}_A, F_A)$ where
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item $\mathcal{C}_A$ is a Derived Relational Object in $(\mathcal{X},\triangleright)$;
    \item $F_A : \mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{C}_A)\to \mathcal{E}$ is a constructible diagram satisfying local constancy and directed covariance;
    \item its coherence $\|\,\operatorname{hcolim}F_A\,\|$ is inhabited.
\end{enumerate}
Assemblages form the full $\infty$--subcategory $\mathrm{Asmbl}(\mathcal{X})\subseteq \mathfrak{E}_x$.
\end{definition}
#+END_LaTeX

An assemblage therefore *is* a coherent relational configuration; its "components" are strata, its "relations" directed morphisms, and its *consistency* the inhabited homotopy colimit.

*** 3.2 Existence and Heterogeneity
The multiplicity of an assemblage corresponds to the stratification $\Sigma$ of the site.
Each layer contributes a partial condition; the directed modality $\triangleright$ imposes causal or processual ordering.
Global existence of the assemblage is not primitive but an emergent coherence across these heterogeneities.

** 4. Dynamic Processes and Modal Flows
Assemblages persist and transform through modal flow.
Within ESDIHG, these are described by homotopy Kan extensions and their stability properties.

#+BEGIN_LaTeX
\begin{definition}[Territorialization]
Given $F : \mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathcal{E}$,
the \emph{territorialization} of $F$ is the formation of its coherent homotopy colimit.
\[
\operatorname{Terr}(F) := \operatorname{hcolim}F,
\quad
\text{with }\;\|\operatorname{hcolim}F\|\text{ inhabited.}
\]
It represents contraction of directed relations into a local fixed--point of the modality.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Deterritorialization]
For a directed morphism
$\Phi : \mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{C}')$,
the \emph{deterritorialization} of $F$ is its left homotopy Kan extension
\[
\operatorname{Deterr}(F) := \mathbf{LKan}_\Phi F.
\]
This process transports coherence across strata:
\[
\|\operatorname{hcolim}F\|
\;\simeq\;
\|\operatorname{hcolim}\mathbf{LKan}_\Phi F\|.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reterritorialization]
The corresponding right Kan extension
\[
\operatorname{Reterr}(F) := \mathbf{RKan}_\Phi F
\]
recovers necessary conditions in the new stratum, reinstating coherence.
\end{definition}
#+END_LaTeX

#+BEGIN_LaTeX
\begin{proposition}[Dynamic Stability]
For every directed transformation $\Phi$,
territorialization, deterritorialization, and reterritorialization preserve modal coherence:
\[
\|h\!\operatorname{colim}F\|
\simeq
\|h\!\operatorname{colim}\mathbf{LKan}_\Phi F\|
\simeq
\|h\!\operatorname{colim}\mathbf{RKan}_\Phi F\|.
\]
\end{proposition}
#+END_LaTeX

Hence the “deterritorialized” flow of an assemblage is not loss but redirection of coherence.

** 5. Plane of Consistency and the Field of Existence
In Deleuzian terms, the *plane of consistency* or *immanence* unifies all assemblages.
In ESDIHG, this corresponds precisely to the Field of Existence $\mathcal{F}$.

#+BEGIN_LaTeX
\begin{theorem}[Plane of Consistency]
The Field of Existence $\mathcal{F}$ satisfying
$\operatorname{hlim}\mathcal{F}\simeq\operatorname{hcolim}\mathcal{F}$
is the universal limit of all assemblages.
For each coherent $F$, there exists a canonical map
\[
F \longrightarrow \mathcal{F}
\]
classifying its inclusion in the global modal equilibrium.
\end{theorem}
#+END_LaTeX

This equivalence ensures that all territorializing and deterritorializing flows occur within a single cohesive field; assemblages are localizations of its global coherence.

** 6. Discussion and Implications
*** 6.1 From Philosophy to Geometry
Assemblage theory conceptualizes entities as contingent gatherings of relations.
ESDIHG renders this insight geometrically: relations are directed morphisms; gathering is homotopy colimiting; contingency is encoded as modal inhabitation.
The resulting system provides a formal semantics for composition, emergence, and transformation.

*** 6.2 Applications
- *Cognitive and Social Systems:* Assemblages of belief, habit, and infrastructure can be modeled as coherent DROs whose transformations follow Kan-extension flows.
- *Physics and Biology:* Reaction networks or morphogenetic processes can be described as deterritorializing flows within a cohesive field of conditions.
- *Computational Ontology:* Knowledge graphs enriched with directional modalities instantiate finite truncations of the ESDIHG schema.

*** 6.3 Outlook
Further work will develop quantitative measures of coherence and apply spectral enrichment to express intensities of territorialization.
A type-theoretic formalization in modal homotopy type theory will enable machine-verifiable reasoning about assemblage formation.

** Conclusion
The ESDIHG model interprets assemblage theory through the precise language of stratified directed homotopy geometry.
Territorialization and deterritorialization become dual Kan-extension flows preserving modal coherence; the plane of consistency is the ambidextrous fixed-point of the Field of Existence.
Being, relation, and transformation thus appear as internally coherent operations of a single stratified cohesive field.
