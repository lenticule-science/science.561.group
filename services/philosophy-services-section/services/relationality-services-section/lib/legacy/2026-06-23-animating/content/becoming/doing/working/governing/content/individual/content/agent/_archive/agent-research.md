Relational agent with epistemic and normative profiles and local flow/nucleus.

## Definition - Components: an [Epistemic Profile](epistemic-profile.md), a [Norm Regime](norm-regime.md), and local flow/nucleus data from [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](flow-nucleus-adjunction.md). - Behaviour: epistemic updates and norm enforcement act on the agent’s local recognitions and contexts.

## Invariants - Commutation: epistemic updates and norm enforcement commute with the local flow/nucleus. - Stability: agent state remains inside admissible recognitions given its norm regime. - Monotonicity: new observations refine, not expand, epistemic possibilities.

## Proof - Uses: [Epistemic Profile](epistemic-profile.md), [Norm Regime](norm-regime.md), and [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](flow-nucleus-adjunction.md) to define the agent’s state/evolution. - Shows that: agents can evolve under observations and norms without breaking earned flow/nucleus structure.
