# von Neumann & Morgenstern 1944

von Neumann, J., & Morgenstern, O.
(1944). *Theory of Games and Economic Behavior*.
Princeton University Press.

The founding statement of expected-utility theory.
Introduces the four axioms (completeness, transitivity, continuity, independence) and proves the representation theorem: any preference relation over lotteries satisfying the axioms is representable as expected-utility maximization for some real-valued utility function unique up to positive affine transformation.

The book is also the founding statement of game theory; the utility theorem is developed as the substrate for analyzing games of strategy.
Subsequent work (Savage, Jeffrey, etc.) develops, generalizes, and contests the framework, but the basic axiomatic move originates here.
