A ***log entry*** is an [item](item) in a [log](log), a log being a [record](record) consisting of a [timestamp](timestamp) and [information](information) about the way some [thing](thing) was at that [time](time).

One [type](type) of *log entry* is a [personal log entry](personal log entry).
