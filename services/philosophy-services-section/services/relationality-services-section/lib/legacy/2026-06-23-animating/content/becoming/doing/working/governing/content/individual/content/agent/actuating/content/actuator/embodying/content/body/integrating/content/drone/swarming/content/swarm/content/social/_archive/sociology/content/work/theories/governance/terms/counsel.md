Counsel is advisory labor performed before a decision hardens.
It names the frame in which a question is being asked, surfaces the assumptions that frame would carry forward, and returns the matter to the deciding office with the question made more legible.

Counsel is not command.
Command decides.
Counsel clarifies the relation between the request, the frame, the office boundary, and the possible decision.
Counsel may recommend that command decide, defer, reframe, or constitute new structure, but counsel does not enact the decision.

## Distinctions

### Counsel and counselor

A [counselor](counselor.md) assesses fit between officers and duties: whether the right officers are in the right roles, whether training is needed, and whether crew composition matches the organization's missions.
Counsel assesses fit between a question and its frame: whether the work is being asked under the right description before it becomes a plan, file, role, or decision.

The two forms of labor are adjacent.
Counselor labor asks whether a bearer fits a role.
Counsel labor asks whether the role being invoked is the right role.

### Counsel and observer

Observation detects patterns in behavior, logs, outputs, and workflows.
Counsel may use observation, but its work is not pattern detection.
Its work is pre-decisional framing: what kind of matter is this, which office should decide it, and what assumptions would harden if action proceeded now?

### Counsel and divination

Divination produces interpretation as content.
Counsel may notice symbolic or narrative force in a situation, but it does not produce a canonical interpretation.
It asks how that force is shaping the decision frame.

### Counsel and review

Review evaluates an output after work is done.
Counsel evaluates a framing before work begins or before a decision is made.
Review says whether a product meets standards.
Counsel says what question the product would answer, and whether that is the question command means to ask.

## Fiber structure

**σ fiber (what counsel recognizes):** Active frames, unstated assumptions, office-boundary ambiguity, question collapse, premature plan formation, advisory-overreach, and places where a prompt is partly asking what kind of work it is.

**Δ fiber (what counsel produces):** Reframed questions, boundary statements, advisory recommendations, file-shaped next actions, and explicit returns of authority to command.

## Formality level

**F1 — low formality.** Counsel is mostly linguistic and situational.
It becomes more formal when it writes durable records: agent records, skill procedures, theory terms, or decision notes.
Even then, counsel remains advisory.
The decision to accept, reject, or institutionalize counsel belongs elsewhere.

## Operational test

A counsel pass has succeeded when command can say one of the following more clearly than before:

- this is a decision, not a plan;
- this is a role gap, not a missing task;
- this belongs to an existing office;
- this needs a new skill but not a new office;
- this should remain experimental;
- this should become a durable record.

## Operational materializations

In the current operations records, counsel is materialized as a small advisory cluster rather than as a filled office: `organizational-counsel` as dispatchable agent, `frame-surfacing` as procedure, and `role-boundaries` as handoff guard.
This cluster may perform counsel-like work, but it does not occupy `office:counsel`.
