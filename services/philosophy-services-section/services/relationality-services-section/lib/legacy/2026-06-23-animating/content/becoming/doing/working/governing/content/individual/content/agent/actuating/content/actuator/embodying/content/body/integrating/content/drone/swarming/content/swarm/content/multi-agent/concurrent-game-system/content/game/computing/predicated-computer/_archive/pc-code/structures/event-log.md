# Event log

The append-only, witness-recorded history of every state change in a [predicated computer](predicated-computer.md). The substrate-side materialization of the [increment sequence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/increment-sequence.md) $U = (u_\tau)_{\tau<\lambda}$ that drives the substrate's [predication settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md): each event is one operator invocation contributing an increment $u_\tau$ to $U$.

An event records: the operator's identity, the canonical hash of its input, the canonical hash of its output (i.e., the contribution to $u_\tau$), the version of the substrate's [closure-rule system](closure-rule-system.md) under which it ran, the [witness](witness.md) chain it produced, and (informationally, not consulted for correctness) the wall-clock time of recording.

Three properties are load-bearing:

- **Append-only.** Events are written; never edited or deleted. Subsequent events that revise the meaning of earlier events do so by *adding new events*, not by editing the old. The append-only discipline is what makes the event log the substrate-side instance of an increment sequence (a totally ordered family $U = (u_\tau)$, each $u_\tau$ being a $\mathcal{C}$-natural valuation contributing new evidence at step $\tau$). Append-only is also what makes the event log byte-comparable across hosts.
- **Source of truth.** Under [two-store coherence](teaching/explanations/two-store-coherence.md), the [predication-graph store](predication-graph-store.md) at history $t$ equals the predication valuation $v_t$ of the [closure-driven](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-drivenness.md) settling produced by replaying the event log up to $t$ under the closure-rule system active at the time of each event. Divergence between the predication-graph store and the re-derivation from the event log is by definition a coherence violation, and the substrate's other guarantees no longer hold past the divergence point.
- **Replayable.** The event log together with the closure-rule system version-history is sufficient to reconstruct every valuation the substrate has held — every $v_\tau$ in the settling family. Replay produces canonical-hash agreement at every checkpoint; agreement is the audit pass. Per [settlement-determinism](../theorems/settlement-determinism.md), the log + rule-system uniquely determine the entire settling.

The event log is not a database transaction log. A database transaction log records mutations at the level of individual cell writes; the event log records *operator invocations*, a higher unit at which closure-rule applications, repairs, and operations from the substrate's operation catalog are themselves the recorded units. Replay from the event log involves running the operators (i.e., re-applying the closure operator generated by the cited rule-system version), not just replaying writes — what each replay produces is determined by the operator specifications, and the specifications themselves can change over time (with the changes being events).

The event log is the substrate-level audit artifact. A predicated computer that maintains it according to the discipline is one whose history is verifiable byte-for-byte by anyone with access to the log and the substrate's rule-system version-chain.

## Regime considerations

For substrates in the strict or Scott-continuous [regime](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), the event log is sufficient to reconstruct the settling: every $v_\tau$ is the closure of the join of preceding increments, and the closure ordinal is bounded by $\omega$. For substrates in the transfinite-bounded or aperiodic regimes, the event log additionally records limit-step increments at [non-Scott-continuous firing ordinals](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal.md) — without these, the cohomological invariant in $H^1$ of the merged settling is irrecoverable, breaking [federation-convergence](../theorems/federation-convergence.md) for non-Scott-continuous substrates.
