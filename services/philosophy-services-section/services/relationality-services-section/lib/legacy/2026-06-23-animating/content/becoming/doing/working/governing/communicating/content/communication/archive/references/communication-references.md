# References

The science-side communication theory's local references. Currently seven load-bearing entries, organised by the tradition each anchors:

**Mathematical / cybernetic tradition.**
- [shannon-weaver1949](shannon-weaver1949.md) — Shannon & Weaver, *The Mathematical Theory of Communication*. The source-channel-receiver formalisation that anchors [shannon-channel](../objects/shannon-channel.md).

**Linguistic-pragmatic tradition.**
- [grice1975](grice1975.md) — Grice, "Logic and Conversation". The Cooperative Principle and the four maxims of conversation; load-bearing for [pragmatics](teaching/explanations/pragmatics.md).
- [halliday1985](halliday1985.md) — Halliday, *An Introduction to Functional Grammar*. The systemic-functional account of register and the metafunctions of language; load-bearing for [register](teaching/explanations/register.md).
- [austin1962](austin1962.md) — Austin, *How to Do Things with Words*. The locutionary/illocutionary/perlocutionary triple; founding reference for [speech-act](../objects/speech-act.md).
- [searle1969](searle1969.md) — Searle, *Speech Acts*. The systematic theory of felicity conditions and illocutionary types that elaborates Austin into the structural model the [speech-act](../objects/speech-act.md) object inherits.
- [clark1996](clark1996.md) — Clark, *Using Language*. The joint-action program and the canonical contemporary statement of common ground as pragmatic substrate; founding reference for [common-ground](../objects/common-ground.md).

**Social-rhetorical tradition.**
- [bitzer1968](bitzer1968.md) — Bitzer, "The Rhetorical Situation". The exigence/audience/constraints triple; founding reference for [rhetorical-situation](../objects/rhetorical-situation.md).

## Boundary with humanities/communication

Per the captain-decisions row C-1 (lifted into the typed [unify-or-federate question](questions/unify-or-federate-with-humanities-communication.md)), the relationship between this theory and [humanities/communication](../../../humanities/theories/communication/) is open. Some canonical references (McCloud, Andersen, Hamami & Morris, Avigad, Macbeth, Ashton) live with the humanities-side theory and are cited from there; this directory does not duplicate them.

## Further open work

References the theory will need as it develops past minimum-viable status (Bell 1984 on style-shifting, Sperber & Wilson 1986 on relevance, Stalnaker 1978 on the original common-ground update rule, Vatz 1973 as the counter-position to Bitzer, Lewis 1969 on convention as game-theoretic equilibrium) are tracked as open work in the parent [`_index.md`](../_index.md). Adding them is gated on downstream load — when a downstream theory's `depends-on:` would point to one of them, it lands here at that point.
