## Annotation

Strauss's framework treats work as an ongoing negotiation among differently positioned actors, with the division of labor itself as an emergent and contested outcome rather than a fixed structure.
For predicated-work, this aligns with the [normative-operational layering](teaching/explanations/normative-operational-layering.md) commitment: the operational layer (who is doing what) and the normative layer (who has standing to do what) are *both* substrate-recordable and *both* subject to revision through witness increments.

Strauss's *arc of work* — the trajectory of an actionable matter through phases of definition, planning, execution, and closure — corresponds to the trajectory of a [task cell system](../objects/task-cell-system.md) through the regimes characterized by [regime-of-task](../theorems/regime-of-task.md).
The arc is not a script; it is the closure-rule cascade.
