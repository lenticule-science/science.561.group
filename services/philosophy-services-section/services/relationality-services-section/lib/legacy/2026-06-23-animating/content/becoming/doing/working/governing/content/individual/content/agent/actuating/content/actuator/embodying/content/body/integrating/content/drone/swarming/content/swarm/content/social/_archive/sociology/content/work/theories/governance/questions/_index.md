Open work for governance at the subdomain level.
The sub-subdomain-specific questions live in each sub-subdomain.
What is held here are the questions whose scope crosses them.

## Open structural questions

- **Can the Hohfeldian operations (assignment, succession, waiver, ratification, revocation, estoppel) be lifted to characterization theorems?** The terms are richly developed; the question is whether the structural relations between them are stable enough to admit theorem-grade statements.
  See [`hohfeldian/`](theories/hohfeldian/).
- **How are jurisdictional disputes analytically structured?** [`jurisdiction/`](theories/jurisdiction/) names the operations (invocation, declination, submission, excess, vires) but the structural question of how disputes between concurrent or overlapping jurisdictions get resolved — and what the discipline's analysis of those resolutions should look like — is open.
- **What is the relation between the formal analyses here and the empirical sociology of how governance actually operates inside organizations?** The subdomain currently develops the analytical apparatus; the empirical sociology of how the apparatus is enacted (and distorted) in real organizations is a separate strand of work.

## Where these questions are worked

Each question is partially worked in the relevant sub-subdomain.
The cross-cutting home is here.
