The duty of status maintenance is the obligation to keep [records](../../../terms/record.md) accurate.
Every work entity — plans, tasks, projects — has a status.
The status must reflect reality.
When status drifts from reality, every system that reads it (dispatch, briefing, reporting, review) operates on fiction.

## The five-tuple

| Component | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Bearer** | The office responsible for operational tracking |
| **Position** | The tracking office |
| **Normative system** | The governance framework's commitment to visible labor |
| **Required act** | Every work entity has a current, accurate status; every change is recorded as an event |
| **Correlative party** | All offices that read work status (dispatchers, briefers, reviewers, plan owners) |

## Why this is a distinct duty

The [duty of execution](./duty-of-execution.md) says: do the work.
The [duty of plan stewardship](./duty-of-plan-stewardship.md) says: ensure the plan converges.
This duty says: ensure the RECORD of work and plan status is accurate.
These three duties can fail independently:

- Work advances but status isn't updated (execution without maintenance — invisible labor)
- Status is updated but work hasn't actually advanced (maintenance without execution — fiction)
- Plan converges but individual task statuses are stale (stewardship without maintenance — unclear picture)

## Discharge

[Discharged](./discharge.md) when zero drift exists between actual state and recorded state.
This is a continuous duty — unlike execution (which terminates when the task ends), status maintenance persists as long as work entities exist.
