Terms specific to black-feminism — the lexical anchors the subdomain commits to.
The current entries:

- [`intersectionality`](intersectionality.md) — Crenshaw's term for the analytic, developed out of Black feminist legal theory, that Collins systematizes as a critical social theory.
- [`matrix-of-domination`](matrix-of-domination.md) — Collins's name for the structure the Combahee Collective called interlocking.
- [`interlocking-systems`](interlocking-systems.md) — the Combahee framing the matrix and intersectionality both extend.
