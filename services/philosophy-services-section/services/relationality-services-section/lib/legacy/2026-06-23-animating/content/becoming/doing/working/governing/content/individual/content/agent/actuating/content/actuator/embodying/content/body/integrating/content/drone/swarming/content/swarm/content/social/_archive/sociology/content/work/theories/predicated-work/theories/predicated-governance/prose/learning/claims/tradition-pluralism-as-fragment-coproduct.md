# Tradition pluralism is fragment coproduct, with $H^1$ obstruction to compatibility

A polity that runs *several* [governance patterns](teaching/explanations/governance-pattern.md) simultaneously — Haudenosaunee Confederacy with consensus for council decisions, clan-mother removal-power as a Hohfeldian pattern, kinship-positional admission, ecological-cadence recurrent practices — has its closure-rule subsystem $\mathcal{S}_{\mathcal{P}}$ as the *coproduct* (union) of the participating patterns' fragments.
Pattern-coexistence is not a meta-political choice; it is a substrate-algebraic configuration.
Compatibility between any two patterns is the absence of an $H^1$ obstruction class in their fragment intersection.

## Statement

**Theorem.** *Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a polity carrying [governance patterns](teaching/explanations/governance-pattern.md) $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_n$ with fragments $(\mathcal{S}_{\pi_i}, \mathcal{W}_{\pi_i}, \mathrm{Rec}_{\pi_i})$.
Then:*

(a) *(Coproduct.)
The polity's full closure-rule subsystem decomposes as $\mathcal{S}_{\mathcal{P}} = \bigcup_{i=1}^n \mathcal{S}_{\pi_i}$, with the polity's witness language $\mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{P}} = \bigcup_i \mathcal{W}_{\pi_i}$ and recognition discipline $\mathrm{Rec}_{\mathcal{P}} = \bigcup_i \mathrm{Rec}_{\pi_i}$.
Closures fire by union of antecedent-matching across all participating patterns.*

(b) *(Compatibility.)
Patterns $\pi_i, \pi_j$ are* compatible *— their joint operation produces no contradictory cells, and either pattern's outputs are admissible inputs to the other — if and only if the [Mayer–Vietoris connecting morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/replica-mayer-vietoris.md) on the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) restricted to the intersection $\mathcal{W}_{\pi_i} \cap \mathcal{W}_{\pi_j}$ vanishes.*

(c) *(Localization of the obstruction.)
When $\pi_i, \pi_j$ are not compatible, the obstruction class lies in $H^1$ of the restricted sheaf and is generated by specific cells in $\mathcal{W}_{\pi_i} \cap \mathcal{W}_{\pi_j}$.
Those cells are exactly the cells the polity must adjudicate — by adopting a third pattern, a meta-rule, or a deliberate exception.*

## Sketch

**(a)** A polity-level closure rule fires when its antecedent matches a substrate cell-pattern; the antecedents of the patterns' closure rules are independent specifications.
The polity's full closure-rule subsystem is the union $\bigcup_i \mathcal{S}_{\pi_i}$ — antecedents are matched in parallel (a cell-pattern triggering multiple antecedents fires multiple consequents, in the truth-monotonic accumulation of [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md)).
The witness language is the union of what any pattern reads or writes; the recognition discipline is the union of binding-rules.

**(b)** Compatibility means the joint closure-rule firing on shared cells is *consistent* — no cell is asserted both $\mathbf{t}$ by one pattern and $\mathbf{f}$ by another (which would settle to $\top$, recording an inconsistency).
Restrict the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_U$ to the cells in $\mathcal{W}_{\pi_i} \cap \mathcal{W}_{\pi_j}$ and apply [replica Mayer–Vietoris](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/replica-mayer-vietoris.md) to the cover $\{\pi_i$-fragment, $\pi_j$-fragment$\}$ of the joint fragment.
The connecting morphism $\delta^0$ vanishes iff the pattern-restrictions glue to a consistent global section on the intersection — i.e., compatibility.
Higher-degree obstructions encode iterated compatibility (compatibility of compatibility-resolutions, etc.); for first-order coexistence, $\delta^0 = 0$ suffices.

**(c)** The obstruction class $[\delta^0(s)] \in H^1$ for a section $s$ on the intersection is supported on a specific subset of cells in $\mathcal{W}_{\pi_i} \cap \mathcal{W}_{\pi_j}$ — the cells where the two patterns' rules disagree on a consequent.
By the locality of the Mayer–Vietoris construction, the support is exactly these cells.
Cells outside the support are unaffected; the polity can run both patterns on them without adjudication.

(All three statements are direct corollaries of [cohomological CALM](../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/cohomological-calm.md) restricted to the patterns' intersection: pattern compatibility is exactly the CALM condition on the intersection sub-sheaf.) $\square$

## Worked examples

**Haudenosaunee.** Patterns: consensus (council deliberations), Hohfeldian-restricted (clan-mother removal-power as a delegated power), kinship (clan membership gates), conduct-teachings (Great Law's stipulations on chiefly conduct), ecological-cadence (council convocation pattern).
Compatibility holds because the witness languages are largely disjoint — consensus reads `aligns-with` on decision cells; clan-mother power reads `is-clan-mother-of` on incumbency cells; the few overlapping cells (a chief's `currently-bears`-office cell read by both consensus-on-removal and clan-mother removal-power) are explicitly resolved by the Great Law's removal procedure, which the math reads as an explicit adjudication closure rule.

**Settler-state-with-Indigenous-jurisdiction overlap.** Patterns: settler-state Hohfeldian (statutory law), Indigenous kinship-and-consensus (treaty nation governance).
Compatibility *fails* in general: the two patterns assert contradictory cells on `who has authority over` shared land cells.
The obstruction class is supported on those land cells.
The polity must adopt a meta-pattern — treaty-recognition, federation, jurisdictional-deference — to resolve, or the contradiction settles cells to $\top$, recording the colonial-imposition fact directly in the substrate.
The math does not adjudicate the substantive question; it diagnoses the obstruction precisely.

**Modern federated state.** Patterns: federal Hohfeldian, state Hohfeldian, municipal Hohfeldian, jurisdictional federation (federalism rules).
Compatibility holds because the federation's jurisdictional-base-change pattern (per [jurisdiction-as-base-change](jurisdiction-as-base-change.md)) explicitly decomposes the witness language into per-jurisdiction sub-bases.
Conflicts that do arise (preemption disputes) are exactly cells where the BC structure breaks — testable on the substrate.

**Worker cooperative.** Patterns: consensus (membership decisions), rotational (officer cycling), conduct-teachings (cooperative principles as evaluation predicates), Black-radical refusal (rejection of inherited corporate-law bindings on certain decisions).
Compatibility holds within the membership; the refusal-pattern produces an obstruction with the surrounding state's Hohfeldian pattern, exactly localized to the cells the cooperative refuses to cede.

## What this earns

The narrative claim that "patterns are first-class, not exceptions" becomes a structural claim:

- **Pluralism is not a special case.** Single-pattern polities are the degenerate $n = 1$ case of the coproduct decomposition.
  Multi-pattern is generic; "purely Hohfeldian" or "purely consensus" polities are rare in practice and structurally unprivileged in the math.

- **Compatibility is testable.** A community can ask, of any proposed pattern-addition, whether the new pattern's fragment intersects existing patterns' fragments on cells where their rules disagree.
  The math reduces the question to a sheaf-cohomology computation on the intersection.

- **Adjudication is named.** When compatibility fails, the obstruction class names the cells requiring meta-rule resolution.
  Adjudication mechanisms (a third pattern, an explicit precedence, an exception) are themselves rule-fragments added to the coproduct, and their compatibility with the existing patterns is testable in turn.

## Relation to legal-pluralism literature

What the legal-pluralism literature (Griffiths, Merry, Tamanaha) names as "the simultaneous co-existence of multiple legal orders within a single social field" this theorem makes substrate-algebraic.
The literature's central question — how do plural orders interact, conflict, and resolve — gets a uniform answer: as a coproduct of fragments, with conflicts localized to cohomology classes in fragment intersections, and with adjudication mechanisms appearing as additional fragments.
The math does not predict which adjudication mechanism a community will adopt; it gives the community a uniform vocabulary for describing what they have adopted and what its structural consequences are.
