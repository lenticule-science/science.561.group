## Content summary

*Pending direct read.* Standard handbook-level survey of the CRDT field a decade after the founding [Shapiro et al. (2011)](shapiro-et-al2011.md) paper, consolidating the state-based / operation-based / delta-state CRDT taxonomy, the catalog of standard CRDT data types (counters, sets, registers, maps, sequences, JSON documents, sequences with editing), the engineering practices for building CRDT-based systems, the relationship to other eventual-consistency disciplines, and the open research questions (transactions over CRDTs, security, garbage collection of historical state, bounded-staleness guarantees).
