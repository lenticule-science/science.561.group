# Witness Propagation

Witness propagation is the movement of a witnessed cell-pattern from one local agent view into the wider team topology. In the local research program, a witness starts as an increment in one cell-system; propagation tries to transport that increment across communication channels so the team can share or adjudicate it.

The source theorem [Witness-network propagation as fibration-comonad descent](/library/social-sciences/economics/work/archive/witness-network-propagation-as-fibration-comonad-descent.md) states the stronger reading: witness-sharing protocol design is descent-data design. Broadcast, gossip, BFT consensus, and eventual consistency are choices about when and how the team enforces cocycle compatibility.

The operational companion is [Organizations as bilattice network sheaves](/library/social-sciences/economics/work/archive/organizations-as-bilattice-network-sheaves.md), where teams are communication covers whose local valuations either glue into consensus or expose disagreement cells.

## Use

Use this term when an agent, office, team, or subsystem submits a claim that must become visible to other local views. The question is not only whether the witness is true, but whether its propagation is descent-clean, descent-failed, or awaiting adjudication.
