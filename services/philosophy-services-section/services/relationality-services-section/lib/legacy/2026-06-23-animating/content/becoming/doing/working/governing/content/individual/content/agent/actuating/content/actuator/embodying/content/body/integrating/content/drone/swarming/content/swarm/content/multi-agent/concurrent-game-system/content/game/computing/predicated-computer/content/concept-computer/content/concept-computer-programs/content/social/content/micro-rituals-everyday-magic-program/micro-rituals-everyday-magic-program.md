# Micro Rituals and Everyday Magic Program

This program captures small rituals and everyday acts of meaning as
recognitions so they can be nurtured and related to other practices.

## Behaviour

- Represent rituals, contexts, participants, and intentions as recognitions
  with provenance and policy (consent/boundary) bindings.
- Link rituals to moods, events, or domains; observe how they propagate or
  anchor experience.
- Measure frequency, felt impact, and change over time.

## Intended operators

- Record and schedule rituals; relate them to well-being measures.
- Share or adapt rituals across contexts with explicit consent.
- Reflect on which rituals sustain or destabilize desired states.
