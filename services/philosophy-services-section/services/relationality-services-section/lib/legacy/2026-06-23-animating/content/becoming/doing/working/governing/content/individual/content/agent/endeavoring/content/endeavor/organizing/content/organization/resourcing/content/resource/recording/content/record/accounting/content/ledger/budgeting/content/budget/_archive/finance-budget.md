# Idea: Finance / Budget & Resource Flows

Treat money and other resources as recognitions flowing through a graph, not just ledger lines.

## 1. Recognitions

- `AccountRecognition`: wallets, bank accounts, envelopes.
- `TransactionRecognition`: movements between accounts with purpose and tags.
- `BudgetRecognition`: planned allocations over time.
- `ObligationRecognition`: recurring bills, subscriptions, debts.

## 2. Measurement and views

- Measurement:
  - cashflow, volatility, savings rates, risk exposure.
- Views:
  - budget vs actual, scenario planning via counterfactual runbooks.
