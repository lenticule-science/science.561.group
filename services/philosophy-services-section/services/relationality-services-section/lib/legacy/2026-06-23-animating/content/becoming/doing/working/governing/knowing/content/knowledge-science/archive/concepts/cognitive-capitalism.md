Cognitive [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md) is a concept from post-[operaismo](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/marxism/theories/autonomism/terms/operaismo.md) thinkers — Yann Moulier-Boutang, Carlo Vercellone, and others — describing the phase of [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md) in which knowledge, attention, and affective [labor](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/terms/labor.md) become the primary sources of value. Under cognitive [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md), the production of ideas, communication, and interpretation is the central economic activity, not a supplement to material production. Value extraction shifts from the factory floor to the nervous system.

The concept builds on the [operaismo](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/marxism/theories/autonomism/terms/operaismo.md) tradition's analysis of how capital reorganizes the [labor](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/terms/labor.md) process across historical phases. Where industrial [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md) extracted value from manual [labor](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/terms/labor.md) disciplined by the assembly line, cognitive [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md) extracts value from mental and relational activity that resists the same forms of measurement and control. The workplace dissolves into life itself: every conversation, every act of attention, every moment of creative problem-solving becomes potentially productive for capital.

Franco "Bifo" [Berardi](../../../../references/people/franco-berardi.md) extended this analysis in *The Soul at Work* (2009) [cite:@berardi_SoulWork_2009], arguing that under cognitive [capitalism](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md), attention becomes both commodity and exhaust. The mind is put to work not occasionally but continuously, and burnout is not a failure of the system but a proof of yield. The worker's exhaustion is the measurable trace of value successfully extracted.

emsenn draws on this framework in "Governing by confusion" to describe how curiosity and interpretive [labor](../../../humanities/theories/sociology/terms/labor.md) are harvested as system resources. The organic intellectual becomes a class of material, and their burnout registers not as dysfunction but as credentialed intensity — evidence that the system has successfully converted cognitive capacity into stabilizing output.
