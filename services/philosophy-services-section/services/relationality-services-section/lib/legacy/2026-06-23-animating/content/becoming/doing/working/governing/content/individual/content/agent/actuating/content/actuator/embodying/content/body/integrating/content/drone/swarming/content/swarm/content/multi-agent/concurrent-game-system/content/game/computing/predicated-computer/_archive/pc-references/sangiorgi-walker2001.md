## Content summary

*Pending direct read.* Standard advanced reference for the π-calculus, complementary to [Milner (1999)](milner1999.md). Develops the calculus's mathematical theory in depth: the variants of bisimulation (early, late, open, weak, ground), the algebraic theory of channel restriction and replication, the type-theoretic developments (linear, session, polymorphic, generic types), the higher-order π-calculus, and the encodings between the π-calculus and other concurrency formalisms. The standard reference for π-calculus theorem-level results.
