# Boot completion is the $H^0$ fixpoint of the substrate-readiness sheaf

**Claim.** Let $\mathcal{B}$ denote the substrate's [paraconsistent settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md) restricted to the *boot region* — the substrate-content cells that the boot sequence (per [boot-sequence](teaching/explanations/boot-sequence.md)) is required to produce or admit before the substrate is declared *ready*. Let $B : \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{B}$ be the *boot-sequence functor*: the composition of the per-phase actions (container-attach, persistent-set-attach, bootstrap-load, closure-rule-system activation, fiber-cache reconcile, multi-user bootstrap) viewed as a single endofunctor on the readiness sheaf.

Then:

1. **Boot completion is a fixpoint of $B$.** A substrate is *ready* iff the boot-region valuation $v_B \in \mathcal{B}$ satisfies $B(v_B) = v_B$ — re-running the boot sequence against the post-boot state produces no further closure. The fixpoint exists by the lax-idempotence of the substrate's [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), and is unique up to canonical iso by the same lax-idempotence (being-settled is a property, not extra structure).

2. **The fixpoint is the $H^0$ class of the readiness sheaf.** Under the truth-side / inconsistency-side decomposition $\mathcal{B}^{(k)} \cong \mathcal{B}^{(t)} \oplus \mathcal{B}^{(\top)}$ per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), the post-boot truth-side valuation $v_B^{(t)}$ represents the unique class
   $$[v_B^{(t)}] \in H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)}).$$
   $H^0$ is exactly the substrate-content "what readiness was achieved": the global sections of the truth-side sheaf over the boot-region cover.

3. **Boot failure has cohomological signature on the inconsistency side.** A boot that records contradictions during its phases populates the boot-region inconsistency cohomology $H^*(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ rather than aborting:
   - $H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ — the truth-side global sections of the inconsistency-side sheaf restricted to the boot region — represents the boot region's *globally-coherent contradiction content*: the $\top$-cells admitted during boot that survive the boot-region's covering relations (e.g., two `bootstrap_users` paths claiming different admin manifests, when both witness-chains reach the post-boot fixpoint).
   - Higher cohomology $H^n(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ for $n \geq 1$ records boot-region *gluing failures* — local consistency on a sub-cover that does not extend to a coherent global section. The substrate's audit-cohomology framing per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) treats these as the structural failure-record of the boot region; the precise correspondence between higher cohomology classes and concrete failure modes is the substrate's audit-side specialisation, not a separate result.

The substrate's *self-test* operation (the substrate-content readiness query whose result is part of the substrate's [self-representation](teaching/explanations/self-representation.md)) is therefore the structural pair: $H^0$ of the truth-side as the readiness invariant; $H^*$ of the inconsistency-side as the failure record. *Reaching ready* and *failing to reach ready* are not separate predicates — they are the two parts of the same cohomological computation on the boot-region readiness sheaf.

## Why this holds

**Part 1 — boot is one closure-2-monad iteration.** Per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), the substrate's settle operator realizes the closure 2-monad $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ as substrate content. Per [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md), Phase 6 (closure-rule-system activation) reaches [settling quiescence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-quiescence.md) on the bootstrap content. The remaining boot phases (container-attach, persistent-set-attach, bootstrap-load, fiber-cache reconcile, multi-user bootstrap) each contribute a $\leq_k$-monotone increment to the boot-region valuation per [closure-driven-settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-driven-settling-monotonicity.md).

The composite $B = \mathrm{phase}_n \circ \dots \circ \mathrm{phase}_1$ is an inflationary monotone operator on the boot-region valuation lattice. By the lax-idempotence of the closure 2-monad per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), the iteration $v_0 \leq_k B(v_0) \leq_k B^2(v_0) \leq_k \dots$ stabilises at a unique fixpoint $v_B$ (up to canonical iso). For finite bootstrap content the closure ordinal is finite per [closure-ordinal-realization-finite](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-ordinal-realization-finite.md); the boot phase reaches $v_B$ in a bounded number of phase-applications.

**Part 2 — the fixpoint is $H^0$ of the truth-side readiness sheaf.** Per [paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md), the settling-associated sheaf at quiescence carries its content as global sections: $H^0(\mathcal{F}^{(t)}) = \Gamma(\mathcal{F}^{(t)})$ is the truth-side global section group. Restricting to the boot region $B$, $\Gamma(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$ is exactly the post-boot truth-side valuation $v_B^{(t)}$ — the substrate-content "what is true after boot."

A substrate is *ready* when $v_B^{(t)}$ contains every cell the boot-sequence's freeze-gates require (per the substrate's self-test catalogue, declared as substrate content per [self-representation](teaching/explanations/self-representation.md), which lists the required cells per phase). Per the cohomological framing, this readiness is the existence of the global section $[v_B^{(t)}] \in H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$ satisfying the freeze-gate predicate. Because the freeze-gate predicate is $\mathcal{C}$-natural per [closure-rule-system](../objects/closure-rule-system.md), its satisfaction is preserved under the canonical isos that lax-idempotence guarantees; *being-ready* is a property of the substrate-content valuation, not of the path the boot took to reach it.

**Part 3 — boot failure is cohomological in the inconsistency-side.** Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), the substrate's $\top$-cells live in $\mathcal{F}^{(\top)}$, separated from the truth-side via the short exact sequence
$$0 \to \mathcal{F}^{(t)} \to \mathcal{F}^{(k)} \to \mathcal{F}^{(\top)} \to 0.$$
Restricting to the boot region: phase failures that produce $\top$-cells (a boot phase recording a contradiction with a prior phase's witness, a `bootstrap_users` step admitting a manifest that contradicts the on-disk users record, a `fiber_reconcile` step admitting two divergent fiber caches) populate $\mathcal{B}^{(\top)}$ rather than aborting boot.

By the same audit-cohomology framing:

- **$H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ is the global-section content of the inconsistency side.** It carries the boot-region's globally-coherent contradiction record: the $\top$-cells whose presence is consistent across the boot-region's covering relations. Per [paraconsistent-equality](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-equality.md), boot does not trivialise on contradictions; the global-section content is well-defined regardless of how many $\top$-cells appeared.

- **$H^n(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ for $n \geq 1$ is a gluing obstruction.** Boot phases each producing locally-consistent witnesses whose joint state cannot be glued to a global section produce non-trivial higher cohomology — the structural form of a *non-reconcilable boot*. The precise classification of which higher cohomology degree corresponds to which boot-phase-disagreement pattern is the substrate's audit-side specialisation per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), not a separately-derived result of this theorem.

A boot whose post-boot $H^*(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ is non-trivial is *failed at the cohomological surface* — the truth-side $H^0$ may still be defined (the substrate has *some* readiness state), but the inconsistency-side carries the failure record that downstream operations (per [repair-as-top-cell-resolution](repair-as-top-cell-resolution.md)) must resolve before the substrate is declared ready by the captain or by the freeze-gate audit.

**Part 4 — the proof object.** Per [witness-chain-as-proof-object](witness-chain-as-proof-object.md), the boot's per-phase witness chain is the Curry-Howard proof object of the boot-region derivation. The chain's hashes record the trajectory $v_0 \to B(v_0) \to B^2(v_0) \to \dots \to v_B$; the post-boot canonical hash is the substrate-content fingerprint of $[v_B^{(t)}] \in H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$. Replaying the witness chain (per [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md)) re-derives the same $H^0$ class on a fresh boot — the cohomological identity is what makes boot byte-identical across replays per [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md).

## Engineering consequence

- **Self-test is a cohomology computation.** A substrate's `self-test` operation evaluates two cohomology groups on the boot-region readiness sheaf: $H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$ for the readiness invariant, $H^*(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ for the failure record. Both are well-defined regardless of which phases recorded contradictions, because the substrate's value-domain is paraconsistent.

- **Failure-recording is not exception-throwing.** Per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md), boot failures populate $\mathcal{B}^{(\top)}$; they do not crash the boot. The substrate completes Phase 9 with a possibly-non-trivial inconsistency-side cohomology and surfaces the failure record for the captain or downstream `respond` calls to resolve. A boot that aborts on the first contradicted phase has *destroyed* the cohomological audit content — the substrate's self-test cannot then enumerate which phases failed.

- **Freeze-gate audit is a sheaf-section query.** The v0.2a freeze-gate "boot completeness on a single node" (per the active plan) reduces to: does $H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$ contain every required-cell class declared in the substrate's freeze-gate catalogue? This is a substrate-content query (per [self-test](teaching/explanations/self-test.md)), not a host-runtime probe.

- **Multi-user boot bootstraps the cover.** A `bootstrap_users` phase per [boot-sequence](teaching/explanations/boot-sequence.md) §4.1 admits multiple per-user readiness sheaves and glues them via the cover of the boot region; the joint $H^0$ is the multi-user readiness. A `fiber_reconcile` phase (§4.2) is the structural dual — the joint $H^*(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$ records per-fiber divergences; reconciliation is repair on the inconsistency-side per [repair-as-top-cell-resolution](repair-as-top-cell-resolution.md).

- **Boot determinism is a $H^0$-class identity.** Per [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md), two boots from the same $(D, P)$ produce the same post-boot state; per this theorem, the more precise statement is *they produce the same $H^0$ class*. Implementations may differ in the per-phase trajectory (cache strategies, parallel readers, fixture ordering) without violating boot-determinism — what is preserved is the cohomological class, not the path.

- **Boot regimes determine what crosses federation.** Per [witness-lifecycle-trajectory-is-regime-conditional-federation-content](witness-lifecycle-trajectory-is-regime-conditional-federation-content.md) and [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md), in strict and Scott-continuous regimes only the post-boot $H^0$ class needs to ship across federation; in transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes the per-phase intermediate-hashes ship as $H^1$-relevant content. Boot's federation surface is regime-conditional in the same way every other closure-step's federation surface is.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Boot defined as a flat sequence with no fixpoint check.** A boot that runs each phase once and declares ready without verifying $B(v_B) = v_B$ may be in a non-quiescent state — Phase 6's closure may still have rules that fire on cells admitted by Phase 7. Per the theorem, *ready* is the fixpoint, not the end of the phase list.

- **Crash-on-contradiction boot.** A boot that aborts when any phase produces a $\top$-cell destroys the inconsistency-side audit content. Per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md), boot must accumulate $\mathcal{B}^{(\top)}$ rather than crash — the cohomology then surfaces the failure for resolution.

- **Self-test that returns a single boolean.** A self-test that returns "ready / not ready" without exposing the cohomological structure has thrown away the diagnosis: the captain (or the operator, or the auditor) cannot tell *which* freeze-gate class is missing from $H^0(\mathcal{B}^{(t)})$ or *which* contradictions populate $H^*(\mathcal{B}^{(\top)})$. Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), audit operations consult cohomology classes; self-test is no exception.

- **Boot trajectory shipped across federation in regimes that mandate $H^1$-only.** A federation in the strict regime that ships per-phase intermediate-hashes is over-shipping; in the strict regime the $H^0$ class is sufficient per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md) §"Regime hypotheses". Conversely, a federation in the transfinite-bounded regime that ships endpoint-only loses $H^1$ classes that distinguish [mutually-locally-derivable](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md) boot trajectories.

- **Boot-completion check that consults host-runtime probes.** A self-test that pings the network, checks free disk space, or polls process state is consulting host-runtime conditions outside the substrate-content readiness sheaf. Per [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md), boot-completion depends only on $(D, P)$; per the theorem, it depends only on the cohomology classes of $\mathcal{B}$. Host-runtime probes belong to the driver layer (per [driver-substrate-split](teaching/explanations/driver-substrate-split.md)), not to the readiness check.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's [closure-rule system](../objects/closure-rule-system.md) realises the closure 2-monad per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), so each boot phase contributes a monotone $\leq_k$-increment whose composition is itself a closure-2-monad iteration.

- The substrate's value-domain admits the bilattice $\top$ value (per [four-bilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md)) and is paraconsistent (per [paraconsistent-equality](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-equality.md)) so boot can record contradictions without trivialising.

- The substrate maintains the boot-region as a sub-cover of the [paraconsistent settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md) per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), so the boot-region cohomology is a restriction of the substrate's existing sheaf-cohomology machinery, not a new structure.

- The boot phases are individually deterministic per [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md) — the readiness fixpoint identity depends on each phase's deterministic action.

- The substrate's [self-test](teaching/explanations/self-test.md) catalogue declares the freeze-gate predicate as substrate content, so the *required* $H^0$ class is itself substrate content rather than implementation-internal logic.

## Position

This theorem is the cohomological corollary of [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) restricted to the boot region, refined by [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md) into a fixpoint statement and by [boot-determinism](boot-determinism.md) into a uniqueness statement. It discharges the placeholder named *boot-as-h0-fixpoint* in the dev-doc placeholder audit (`pypredcomp/docs/dev/surveys/placeholder-theorems-2026-05-03.md` row #3) by exhibiting the named result as a corollary of the existing cohomology theorem rather than as a separately-authored theorem.

The substrate's claim that *boot is a structurally well-defined operation that either reaches a unique readiness state or records its failure as substrate content* is one cohomological statement in the boot region of its existing readiness sheaf. There is no separate "boot algebra" — the algebra is the substrate's closure-2-monad, restricted to the boot region; the readiness invariant is $H^0$ of the truth-side sheaf, restricted to that region; the failure record is $H^*$ of the inconsistency-side, restricted likewise. The substrate's `self-test` operation is one cohomology query; the freeze-gate audit is another; the captain's "is the substrate ready?" question is the same query, asked at the outermost surface.

This positions the substrate's boot machinery alongside its other uniformity claims: one runtime per [substrate-runtime-as-closure-driven-settling](substrate-runtime-as-closure-driven-settling.md), one authorization per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), one contradiction discipline per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md), one audit cohomology per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md). Boot completion is not a separate architectural concern — it is the boot-region instance of the substrate's audit-cohomology machinery, with the readiness invariant on the truth-side and the failure record on the inconsistency-side, structurally guaranteed.
