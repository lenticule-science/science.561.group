# Atkinson & Shiffrin 1968

Atkinson, R. C., & Shiffrin, R. M. (1968). Human Memory: A
Proposed System and its Control Processes. In K. W. Spence &
J. T. Spence (Eds.), *Psychology of Learning and Motivation*
(Vol. 2, pp. 89-195). Academic Press.

The *multi-store model* of human memory: a pipeline of
*sensory register* (very brief, modality-specific, large
capacity), *short-term store* (seconds-to-minutes, limited
capacity, the substrate of working memory), and *long-term
store* (durable, effectively unlimited). Items transition
between buffers via attention (sensory→short-term) and
rehearsal (short-term→long-term).

The model dominated cognitive-psychology textbooks for
decades and influenced AI memory architecture (the Soar
cognitive architecture's memory decomposition, replay buffers
in deep RL, the multi-tier cache hierarchy of conversation
buffer + vector store + LLM weights in modern agents).
