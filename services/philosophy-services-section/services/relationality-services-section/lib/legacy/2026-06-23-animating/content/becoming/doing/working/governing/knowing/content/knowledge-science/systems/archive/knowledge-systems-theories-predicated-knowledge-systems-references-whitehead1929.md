## Full citation

## Annotation

Whitehead's mature systematic statement of *process metaphysics*. The foundational claim: the basic units of reality are not enduring substances but *actual occasions* — momentary acts of becoming, each of which *prehends* (selectively inherits from) prior occasions and contributes to the prehensive horizon of subsequent ones. Substances, properties, and identities are *abstractions* over patterns in the underlying flux of actual occasions; they are *real* but they are *derivative*, not foundational.

For the predicated framing, Whitehead is the philosophical anchor for treating the [predication-cell](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predication-cell.md) as the *unit of becoming* rather than as a static record. An assertion is a *cell-occasion*: it inherits from prior cells (the [evidence-accumulation](teaching/explanations/evidence-accumulation.md) chain), it contributes to subsequent settling ([predication-settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md)), and the *thing* being asserted-about is the *abstraction* over the cells whose subject it is ([aboutness](teaching/explanations/aboutness.md)). The framing's commitment that *identity is by relations rather than by essence* is Whitehead's commitment that substance is derivative; the framing's algebraic apparatus is what makes the derivation precise.

The framing departs from Whitehead in one important respect: Whitehead treats the temporal succession of occasions as the *primary* structure and spatial/structural relations as derivative; the framing treats the predicate-category structure (which includes structural relations) and the closure-regime dynamics (which include temporal settling) as *coequal* — neither structure nor dynamics is primary, and the [knowing-as-settling](teaching/explanations/knowing-as-settling.md) concept names the joint dynamics-structure unit.
