**Memetic warfare** is the doctrinal use of meme-form content — short, recombinable, emotionally-loaded units circulated through algorithmic platforms — as tactical influence.
The term originates in late-2010s U.S. and NATO discussion, where it names a recognized capability area within information operations: the production and distribution of low-cost, high-virality content designed to shape perception, recruit attention, harass adversaries, or shift the affective register of an information environment.
Within predicated sociology, memetic warfare is treated less as a novel doctrine than as the *open avowal* of a longer state-aesthetic capacity — the moment when the [state-aesthetic continuum](state-aesthetic-continuum.md) named itself in one of its registers.

## What the doctrine names

Standard doctrinal discussion identifies several features that make meme-form content suited to influence operations.
None is unique to memes; their convergence in the platformal substrate is what makes the capability coherent.

**Low-cost production.** Meme-form content can be produced rapidly, in large quantities, by small teams.
The economics differ from broadcast propaganda, which required centralized production capacity and expensive distribution.

**High-virality distribution.** Algorithmic platforms amplify content that performs well by their engagement metrics.
Meme-form content is engineered to perform well by these metrics, making distribution largely free.

**Recombinant payloads.** Memes carry their content in a format that invites variation.
Adversaries, supporters, and bystanders all become potential redistributors and modifiers.
Content propagates through a population of remixers rather than from a single broadcast source.

**Affective rather than propositional address.** Meme-form content delivers a feeling, a stance, or an in-group cue rather than a propositional claim.
The doctrinal interest is precisely that memes bypass propositional argument and operate at the level of affect and identity — the level that [affect-governance](affect-governance.md) and [genre calibration](../morphisms/genre-calibration.md) shape.

**Plausible deniability.** A meme that spreads through a population cannot easily be attributed to its origin.
The doctrinal frameworks recognize this as a feature: influence operates without a return address.

## The predicated-sociology reframing

Three moves distinguish the predicated-sociology reading from the standard doctrinal framing.

**Memetic warfare is the doctrinal capture of a pre-existing operation.** Long before the doctrine, the [state-aesthetic continuum](state-aesthetic-continuum.md) was producing recombinable, emotionally-loaded, low-cost content through commercial entertainment, advertising, and brand culture.
Memetic warfare is the state's open arrival inside a substrate it had already been operating in by other means.
The novelty is doctrinal rather than operational.

**The audience is not principally a foreign adversary.** Doctrinal framing presents adversary populations as targets.
In predicated-sociology analysis, the more significant audience is often the domestic one.
Recruitment videos that adopt memetic registers — such as the *Ghosts in the Machine* series produced by the U.S. Army's 4th Psychological Operations Group — are addressed to potential recruits and to online culture more broadly, not principally to foreign adversaries.
The subject-formation work happens at home.

**The form enforces moral physics prior to any content.** The meme-form itself enforces a [moral physics](moral-physics.md): short duration, no narrative arc, [consequence-free violence](consequence-free-violence.md) of statement, [affective looping](affective-looping.md) as the temporal structure of the feed.
The form trains audiences in a particular physics before any specific content arrives.
This is why the doctrinal interest in memes is analytically telling: the doctrine is choosing a form whose moral physics already does the primary work.

## Doctrinal openness as a finding

One analytically distinctive feature of memetic warfare is its comparative transparency.
Most operations that the predicated-sociology corpus analyzes — [genring](genring.md), [californication](../morphisms/californication.md), [harm-governance](harm-governance.md) — are unspoken.
The system does not advertise that it is doing them.
Memetic warfare has been openly discussed in military journals and NATO publications.
The doctrinal openness is itself a finding: the capability is openly avowed while the deeper operations remain tacit, and avowing one capability provides cover for the others by positioning the state as operating only where it has admitted to operating.

## Relation to other concepts

Memetic warfare is one register of the [state-aesthetic continuum](state-aesthetic-continuum.md): the register where the state operates openly inside the platformal substrate using meme-form content.
Other continuum registers deliver similar content under different institutional cover.

[Affective looping](affective-looping.md) is memetic warfare's temporal structure.
The feed holds the viewer in a feeling-state across many meme-form units; the individual unit is a single loop iteration.

[Soft surrealism](soft-surrealism.md) is memetic warfare's preferred aesthetic surface when it operates in recruitment and influence registers — the collage, glitch, and retro-cartoon forms that constitute the contemporary influence-operation vocabulary.

[Socially ontogenic media](socially-ontogenic-media.md) names what memetic warfare produces at scale: the population of subjects who have internalized the meme-form's being-templates as their operating posture toward the information environment.
