A ***poem*** is an artifact that results from [writing](../_index.md) in which the material qualities of language — sound, rhythm, [line break](./line-break.md), spacing, image — are compositional elements alongside (or instead of) propositional content. Where an [essay](../../../terms/essay.md) is organized by argument and a narrative by plot, a poem is organized by the [line](./line-break.md).

The term *verse* (from Latin *versus*, a turning of the plow, hence a line) is sometimes used as a synonym for poetry and sometimes more narrowly for metrically regular poetry, in contrast to [free verse](./free-verse.md) or prose poetry.

Poems range from the briefest fixed forms ([haiku](./haiku.md), epigram) to book-length works (epic, verse novel). The major modal categories are [lyric](./lyric.md) (subjective, expressive), narrative (story-telling), and dramatic (voiced by characters). See [Poetry](../disciplines/poetry/index.md) for the discipline of composing poems.
