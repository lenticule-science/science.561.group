## Scope

This directory contains *worked examples* — concrete instantiations of the [predicated-knowledge-system](../objects/predicated-knowledge-system.md) object as it appears in (i) the literatures the subtheory synthesises and (ii) this repository as a self-instance. Each example identifies which substrate restrictions recover the example from the full predication-cell substrate, what the restrictions make visible, and the failure modes that come from misreading the example.

## What is here

- [this-repository-as-predicated-knowledge-system](this-repository-as-predicated-knowledge-system.md) — this FARS repository as a self-instance, with the markdown notes as the cell-set, the front-matter `type:` predicates as the predicate vocabulary, and the cross-link graph as the fiber structure.
- [topic-maps](topic-maps.md) — ISO 13250 Topic Maps as a strict-regime, $\mathbf{2}$-valued, symmetric sub-substrate of the predication-cell, recovered by three structural restrictions.
- [fca-concept-lattice](fca-concept-lattice.md) — a single Wille-Ganter concept lattice ([@ganter-wille1999], [@belohlavek2004]) as a degenerate fiber in the predication-fibration.
- [rdf-owl-as-restricted-substrate](rdf-owl-as-restricted-substrate.md) — RDF + OWL as a restricted substrate recovered by three independent restrictions: bilattice collapse to $\mathbf{2}$, federation collapse to set union, and externally-applied closure.
- [datalog-as-strict-regime-substrate](datalog-as-strict-regime-substrate.md) — Datalog as the strict-regime corner of the substrate, with Horn-clause closure rules and a trivial fibration index.
- [cyc-microtheory-as-scoped-fiber](cyc-microtheory-as-scoped-fiber.md) — Cyc microtheories ([@lenat-guha1990]) as the engineering precedent for the discourse-community-as-fiber reading, with the genlMt lattice as the fibration base.
