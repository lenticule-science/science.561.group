The **labor process** is the concrete activity by which human labor power is transformed into value-producing work — the actually-occurring conjunction of worker, object worked on, and instruments through which the work is performed.
Marx introduces the term in [Capital Volume I, Chapter 7](marx1867) to distinguish the *labor process in general* (a feature of every social form) from the *valorization process* specific to capitalism.
The labor process is the primary unit of analysis for the [labor-process-theory tradition](../texts/labor-process-theory-survey.md) that Braverman reanimated in 1974.

## The three simple elements (Marx, Capital I, ch. 7)

Marx defines the labor process as comprising three elements that any work activity exhibits:

1. **Purposive activity** (*zweckmäßige Tätigkeit*) — the labor itself, the worker's directed expenditure of capacities.
2. **The object on which the activity is performed** — the raw material, the workpiece, the case to be processed, the document to be drafted.
3. **The instruments through which the activity is mediated** — tools, machines, software, infrastructures.

Every social form requires a labor process; what makes capitalism distinctive is not the labor process as such but its *subsumption under capital*.
Capital purchases labor power as a commodity; during the period of purchase, the worker's activity belongs to the capitalist; the product of the activity belongs to the capitalist; the value produced exceeds the value paid for the labor power, generating surplus value.

## Labor process vs. valorization process

The conceptual distinction is analytical, not empirical: in any concrete capitalist labor process, the two are inseparably entangled.
The distinction lets the analyst ask: which features of the work organization are *technical requirements of producing the thing* and which are *political requirements of extracting surplus value from labor power*?

Braverman's [Labor and Monopoly Capital](braverman1974) is, methodologically, an extended demonstration that many features of twentieth-century work — the assembly line, time-and-motion methods, Taylorist deskilling, the separation of conception from execution — that present themselves as technical necessities of efficient production are in fact political features of the valorization process.
The same product could be produced under different organizations of the labor process; the actual organization was selected by capital's need to control labor.

## The "core theory" version

The "second-wave" labor-process tradition formulated by Paul Thompson [thompson1983] and the International Labour Process Conference made four commitments organizing the labor process as analytical object:

- The labor process has a *privileged role* in social analysis as the site of surplus-value production and the concrete living of capital-labor antagonism.
- Capital-labor relations have a *structured antagonism* but no *necessary form* — multiple control strategies are possible.
- The labor process is *relatively autonomous* from broader political and ideological structures and requires its own analysis.
- Workers retain *agency* in shaping how the labor process unfolds; the analysis is not technologically deterministic.

## Distinguished from

- **Work** (the umbrella concept) — the labor process is the *internal organizational form* of work under conditions of wage labor; "work" includes activity outside the wage relation that no labor-process analysis covers.
- **Production** — the labor process is the activity-side of production; production also includes the circulation, distribution, and realization of value that the labor process feeds.
- **Job** — a job is a contractually-bounded employment relationship; one job typically encompasses many distinct labor processes (and one labor process may be performed across many jobs).
- **Operation** (in this corpus) — an [operation](operation-item.md) is a unit of executable work in our predicated-work substrate; a labor process is the surrounding capitalist-organizational form within which operations are organized when they are performed under wage labor.
