# Hernán & Robins, *Causal Inference: What If* (2020)

Textbook on causal inference from the potential-
outcomes (Rubin) tradition. Open-access PDF.

## What it covers

- Potential outcomes framework.
- Confounding, exchangeability, positivity.
- Standardization and inverse probability weighting.
- Instrumental variables, with LATE result.
- Time-varying confounding and g-methods (Robins'
  contributions).
- Causal inference in epidemiology applications.
- Mediation analysis.

## Why it matters

The standard reference for the potential-outcomes
tradition. Complements Pearl's graphical approach.
Strong on time-varying treatments, common in
epidemiology and policy.

The g-methods (g-formula, g-estimation, marginal
structural models) developed largely by Robins are
the primary tools for time-varying causal inference.

## How to read

Accessible; many worked examples. Open-access
encourages broad use. Good complement to Pearl 2009
for the alternative formal apparatus.

## Related works
