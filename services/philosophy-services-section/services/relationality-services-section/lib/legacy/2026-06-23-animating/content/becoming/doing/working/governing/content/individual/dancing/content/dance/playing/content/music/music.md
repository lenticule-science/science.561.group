# music

**Music is the agentic form of the spiral** — coordination **through a shared field, not point-to-point**.
Where dancing is agency between particular moving bodies, music is what those bodies constitute *together* when they fall into common time: a **shared pulse** that no one of them commands and all of them read and feed.

## Definition

The grounding is in [entrainment and groove](../library/texts/entrainment-and-groove.md).
Musical **entrainment** — independent rhythmic processes adjusting to and locking with a shared periodicity (Clayton, Sager & Will, "In time with the music," 2005; the phenomenon first recorded by Huygens's coupled pendulums, 1665) — is the mechanism by which many separate agents become **one field in time**.
Dynamic attending (Large & Jones, 1999) shows the listener's own attention entrains to that pulse; **groove** and **participatory discrepancy** (Keil 1987; Iyer 2002; Janata et al. 2012) show the field's life comes from the timing differences *between* participants reading and contributing to it.

That is exactly the **agentic** face of the quartet, and it matches the repository's own stigmergic reading of `working` ("coordinating **through the shared field, not point-to-point**" — `theories/existence/becoming/doing/working/docs/working.md`).
Music is the existential shared field made audible: agents contributing to and reading a common medium.

## Generated by

Music is generated by **[dancing](../../../../../../docs/dancing.md)** through **[playing](../../../docs/playing.md)**: when bodies play a dance together, their movement entrains to a common pulse, and that shared field is music.
Dancing supplies the moving bodies; playing locks them into shared time; the lock *is* music.

## Generates

Music generates **game** — through **[playing](../playing/docs/playing.md)** again.
When the shared field acquires rules, constraints, and stable structure — Caillois's movement from free play (paidia) to formal play (ludus) — the open pulse crystallizes into bounded, rule-governed play: [game](../playing/library/game/docs/game.md), the individual turn.

## Placement in the spiral

Music is the agentic middle of **becoming → dancing → music → game → becoming**, seating where `working`/agentic seats in the existence descent.
It is the field between the agential act (dancing) above it and the settled bounded player (game) below it.
