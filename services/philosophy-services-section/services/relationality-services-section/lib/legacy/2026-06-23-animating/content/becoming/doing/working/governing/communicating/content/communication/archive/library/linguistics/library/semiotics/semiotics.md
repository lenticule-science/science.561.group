Semiotics is the study of *sign-relations* — the structure by which a [sign-vehicle](concepts/sign-vehicle.md) stands for a [sign-object](concepts/sign-object.md) for an [interpretant](concepts/interpretant.md) under a [code](concepts/code.md), organised within [semiotic systems](objects/semiotic-system.md) and composed across systems by [translation](morphisms/translation.md) and [codeswitching](morphisms/codeswitching.md). The discipline is older than linguistics-as-science and broader than philosophy-of-language; it is the substrate-level theory of *standing-for*.

This theory commits to the predicated-knowledge-systems substrate. The committed bridges are made explicit in [composability with predicated-knowledge-systems](narratives/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md):

- The [sign-vehicle](concepts/sign-vehicle.md) reads as the substrate's predication-cell unit.
- The [interpretant](concepts/interpretant.md) chain reads as the substrate's [closure operator](../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/theorems/closure-as-fitting-fixpoint.md) iterating over a starting cell.
- The [semiosphere](concepts/semiosphere.md) reads as the substrate's [discourse-community-as-fiber](../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/discourse-community-as-fiber.md).

These bridges are not metaphor; they are the substrate-level reading the lift this theory underwent in the [rubric-application-2026-05-03 audit](../../../information/theories/knowledge-systems/theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/audits/rubric-application-2026-05-03.md) committed to.

## Position relative to neighbours

- **Linguistics**: this theory is one of linguistics' children; it neighbours [semantics](../semantics/_index.md) (which fixes how *meaning* attaches to a sign) and [languages](../languages/_index.md) (which catalogues specific natural-language systems). Semiotics is the broader discipline; semantics is its specialisation to sign-systems whose interpretants are propositions.
- **Communication**: this theory shares the *medium* and *register* vocabulary of [humanities/communication](../../../../humanities/theories/communication/_index.md) and [science/communication](../../../communication/_index.md), but its primitive object is the *sign-relation*, not the *channel*. The [inheritance ledger](narratives/what-semiotics-inherits.md) records what is borrowed and what is genuinely added.
- **Predicated knowledge systems**: this theory is *upstream* of the substrate in the discipline's order of explanation — predication is a special case of sign-relation — but *downstream* in the corpus's actual order of construction (the substrate was named first; semiotics is now retroactively typed against it). The composability narrative records both directions.
