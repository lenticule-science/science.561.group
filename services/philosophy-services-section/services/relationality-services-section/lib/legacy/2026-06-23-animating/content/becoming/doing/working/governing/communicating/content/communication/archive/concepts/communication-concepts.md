# Concepts — communication

The portable, parameterized disciplines that communicative practices instantiate. Each concept is parametric: it states a pattern, the parameters that pattern accepts, the shape of a conformance check, and its lineage to the source literature (McCloud, Eisner, Cohn, Groensteen on comics; Andersen, Hamami and Morris, Ashton, Avigad, Macbeth on mathematical communication; the broader philosophy of mathematical practice and the diagrammatic-reasoning literature; semiotics and multimodality).

Subtheories — for now [mathematical-communication](../../math/theories/mathematical-communication/_index.md), with more to follow — bind the parameters to the apparatus of one practice, inheriting the conformance shape and adapting it to the practice's own registers, audiences, and apparatus.

A concept here is *not* a stylistic preference. It is a structural commitment a communicative practice makes about how it constrains itself. Two practices may instantiate the same concept with very different parameter values and remain mutually legible as members of the same family.

## Inventory

- [panel](panel.md) — a bounded communicative unit with a recognizable status; the framed material the artifact stipulates explicitly.
- [gutter](gutter.md) — the transition between panels where the reader is required to perform closure.
- [communicative-closure](communicative-closure.md) — the reader's disciplined completion of a transition between framed units.
- [icon](icon.md) — a compressive sign that removes detail in order to preserve manipulable structure.
- [sequence](sequence.md) — the ordered path through which panels make particular closures possible (and others impossible).
- [transport](transport.md) — the preservation of identity across representational registers, with disciplined account of what is preserved, lost, and gained.
- [audience](audience.md) — the relative ground for gap acceptability; gaps are licensed or unlicensed only relative to a stated audience.
- [register](register.md) — a medium with its own native panels, native rigor, and native foreclosures.
- [accountable-closure](accountable-closure.md) — the meta-discipline: an artifact is legitimate when its medium-specific form makes its required closures inspectable.
- [medium-specific-rigor](medium-specific-rigor.md) — the per-register criterion: a medium is rigorous when it makes accountable the closures proper to that medium, not by imitating another.
- [plan-reconstruction](plan-reconstruction.md) — the criterion that an artifact communicates when the reader can reconstruct the plan that makes its parts belong together.
- [frame-discipline](frame-discipline.md) — the requirement that the reader can tell what kind of unit each panel is, what it asserts, what it assumes, and what closure it asks for.
