# Fudenberg & Tirole 1991 — Game Theory

Fudenberg, D., & Tirole, J.
(1991). *Game Theory*.
MIT Press.

The standard graduate-level textbook on non-cooperative game theory.
Covers extensive-form and normal-form games, Nash equilibrium and refinements (subgame-perfect, sequential, trembling-hand, perfect Bayesian), games of incomplete information, repeated games, reputation effects, and bargaining theory.

The book remains widely used in economics, theoretical computer science, and multi-agent systems graduate programs as the reference for equilibrium-concept theory.
Its treatment of refinements is particularly thorough, explaining the various refinements and their motivations in a unified way.
