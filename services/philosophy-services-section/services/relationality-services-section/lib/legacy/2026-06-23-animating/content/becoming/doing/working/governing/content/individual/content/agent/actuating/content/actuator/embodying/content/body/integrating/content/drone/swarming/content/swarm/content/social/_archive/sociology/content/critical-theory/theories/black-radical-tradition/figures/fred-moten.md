Fred Moten (b.
1962) is the figure through whom the [black-radical-tradition](../_index.md) holds its analysis of fugitive thought, Black study, and the aesthetic-political register that exceeds institutional capture.
Professor at NYU's Tisch School of the Arts.

The full biographical note lives at [`library/references/people/fred-moten`](../../../../../../../../references/people/fred-moten.md).
This page records what the subdomain in particular takes from him.

## What this subdomain takes from him

- **The undercommons.** With Stefano Harney: a maroon space within and against the institution (paradigmatically the university) where intellectual labor happens in the cracks of the institutional apparatus.
  The full development is in the subdomain's term [undercommons](../terms/undercommons.md) and in Moten and Harney's *The Undercommons: Fugitive Planning and Black Study* (2013).
- **Black study.** Distinguished from Black studies (the institutional formation): black study is the ongoing intellectual-political-aesthetic practice of the Black radical tradition.
  Study as a form of life, prior to and continuing alongside any institutional credentialing.
- **Fugitivity.** A mode of being that refuses the institutional capture-and-recognition that resistance-within-institutions presupposes.
  Drawn from the historical practice of fugitive enslaved persons and extended into a general analytical category for thought and practice that cannot be brought back into institutional grammar.
  Moten's fugitivity converges with [Saidiya Hartman](saidiya-hartman.md)'s and intersects with [Glen Coulthard](../../../../../../../../references/people/glen-coulthard.md)'s critique of recognition.
- **The break.** The space-time of Black aesthetic-political production where the dominant categories break down and other modes of being become available.
  The break is jazz's improvisatory moment, the slave song's moment of refusal, the underground railroad's moment of departure.
  The framing comes through *In the Break: The Aesthetics of the Black Radical Tradition* (2003).
- **Phonic materiality.** The force of sound in Black performance that precedes and exceeds semantic content.
  Read in this subdomain (see the text [black-radical-tradition-and-russian-semiotics](../texts/black-radical-tradition-and-russian-semiotics.md)) as adjacent to Russian Formalist concerns with the materiality of the sign.

## Key works

- *In the Break: The Aesthetics of the Black Radical Tradition* (2003)
- *The Undercommons: Fugitive Planning and Black Study* (with Stefano Harney, 2013)
- *Black and Blur*, *Stolen Life*, *The Universal Machine* (the *consent not to be a single being* trilogy, 2017–2018)
- *All Incomplete* (with Stefano Harney, 2021)
