**Teaching** is one of the two acts of [working](../../../docs/working.md): writing a settled claim to the shared field so that others may read it.
Where a bare [speech act](../../../../library/speaking/docs/speaking.md) puts a charge into a content, teaching does so *into a common field* and over one's own name — it is a contribution others can take up.
Dual to [learning](../../learning/docs/learning.md), which reads the same field.

## Teaching is dialogical, not deposit

Paulo Freire (*Pedagogy of the Oppressed*) attacked the "banking" picture of teaching — the teacher depositing fixed knowledge into passive receivers — and replaced it with a *dialogical* one: teaching and learning are reciprocal, the teacher is also taught in dialogue, the student also teaches.
This theory's pairing of teaching and learning as two directions on *one* shared field is that reciprocity made structural.
To teach is not to transmit point-to-point into an empty vessel but to write into a field that other teachers have also written, and that will be read and answered.
Vygotsky's complementary point is that this shared field is where understanding is built in the first place: what a learner can do today in the company of a teacher, they can do alone tomorrow.

## What generates it

Teaching is generated by working: it is the writing direction of the shared field.
It presupposes that field — one cannot teach into a vacuum, only into a medium others share and will read.

## What it generates

Teaching generates a contribution to the common record, and that contribution takes its character from what the field already holds.
Where one's contribution agrees with what is there, it confirms and strengthens it; where it diverges — because another has taught something incompatible — the field carries the disagreement as a [contested](../../../../library/speaking/library/illocution/library/forces/docs/forces.md) state rather than silently overwriting.
The standing record that many acts of teaching and learning build, with its agreements and its live conflicts, is what [governing](../../../governing/library/adjudication/docs/adjudication.md) must settle.
