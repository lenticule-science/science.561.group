# The substrate's three access patterns are the colimit / atom / limit triad of diagrams over the temporal 2-category

**Claim.** Every substrate observation — every operation by which an external observer or an internal operator engages substrate content without advancing it — is one of three structurally distinct moves, and the three exhaust the access modes available to a conformant predicated computer:

- **Directed Shape (colimit).** A [snapshot](../objects/snapshot.md) of a region at a moment, an audit-export collapsing a region's history into one canonical valuation. Math content: the colimit $\mathrm{colim}_T \, v$ over the [temporal 2-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/temporal-2-category.md)-indexed [predication settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) $v = (v_\tau)_{\tau<\lambda}$ — the merged join of all per-step valuations under $\leq_k$.
- **Dynamics (single atom).** A read of one cell at one moment, the inputs and outputs of a single closure-rule application, a single tick witness. Math content: the value $v_\tau(p)$ at one $T$-0-cell — the predication fiber algebra element at one (ordinal, parameter, predicate) coordinate.
- **Cosections (limit).** A [validate-against-shape](../morphisms/validate-against-shape.md) check, a substrate-coherence query, a "would this admission preserve invariants?" governance pre-check. Math content: the limit $\lim_T \, v$ over the temporal-2-category-indexed settling — the fiber elements compatible with every per-step valuation, i.e., the constraint-satisfying global sections.

The three patterns decompose the substrate's observation surface as the conformant-query schema for $T$-indexed diagrams in the predication fibration: the colimit projection ("what has happened" — pointwise $\bigvee_k$ over the chain), the atom read ("what is happening here-now" — the value at one $T$-0-cell), the limit projection ("what is admissible across all positions" — pointwise $\bigwedge_k$ over the chain). The three projections are well-defined per the predication fiber algebra's $\leq_k$-completeness; the substrate's query language commits its observations to this schema (per the per-pattern witness contracts below), and queries outside it are ruled non-conformant per the rules-out section.

## Why this holds

Per [predication-settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md), the substrate's runtime trajectory is a $T$-indexed family $v = (v_\tau)_{\tau<\lambda}$ of [predication valuations](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-valuation.md) satisfying [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md): $\sigma \leq \tau \Rightarrow v_\sigma(p) \leq_k v_\tau(p)$. Three projections of this family are well-defined and underwrite the three access patterns:

- **Colimit cone $\mathrm{colim}_T \, v$.** The pointwise $\bigvee_k v_\tau(p)$ — the supremum of the chain. This is the substrate-content meaning of "snapshot": collapse the trajectory to its supremum, present that supremum as the canonical settled valuation. Per [settling-convergence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/settling-convergence.md), the colimit exists in every regime; per [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md), its closure ordinal determines the snapshot's cost class.
- **Atom (object inclusion at $\tau$).** The value $v_\tau(p)$ at one position. The trivial cone — picking out a single fiber element. Operationally a read; structurally the unit of the colimit/limit pair, at the "current position" of the substrate.
- **Limit cone $\lim_T \, v$.** The pointwise $\bigwedge_k v_\tau(p)$ — the infimum of the chain, the value compatible with every per-step valuation. Per [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md), the chain is monotone, so the limit collapses to $v_0(p)$ in the strict regime — the constraint a value must satisfy to be admissible across every substrate state. This is the substrate-content meaning of "validate against shape": check whether a proposed valuation lies within the limit cone, equivalently within every settled state's $\leq_k$-bound.

The trichotomy is the substrate's conformant-query schema: the predication fiber algebra's $\leq_k$-completeness (per [predication-fiber-algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md)) makes all three projections always defined; the substrate's query language commits its observation operators to one of these three roles per the witness contracts on [snapshot](../objects/snapshot.md), atom-read, and [validate-against-shape](../morphisms/validate-against-shape.md). Per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md), the colimit projection is computable in the regime-classified cost class; per [governance-decision-modal-decidability](governance-decision-modal-decidability.md), the limit projection's decidability is regime-conditioned likewise.

## Duality with the three acts

The three access patterns are dual to the [three substrate acts](three-acts-as-temporal-2-category-decomposition.md) on the temporal axis:

- **Sequential act ↔ Atom access.** The act produces a new $T$-0-cell ($\tau \to \tau{+}1$); the access reads at one $T$-0-cell. The two are paired: every sequential act emits an atom-access witness recording the new 0-cell's value.
- **Concurrent act ↔ Directed Shape.** The act $\vee_k$-joins replica content (colimit on the replica axis); the access $\bigvee_k$-collapses temporal content (colimit on the temporal axis). The two share the colimit structure on different axes of the lifted structure of the [companion theorem](three-acts-as-temporal-2-category-decomposition.md).
- **Generative act ↔ Cosections.** The act runs $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$ to fixed point — producing the $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-algebra value at the current 0-cell. The access checks whether a valuation is compatible with every settled state — the limit-cone version of the same fixed-point property. Per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), the $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-algebra structure is property-like, so the limit cone collapses to the unique $\mathrm{Cl}^{\mathcal{S}}$-fixed-point at quiescence.

## Engineering consequence

- **Audit lens per pattern.** An auditor consulting the substrate selects access pattern by question kind: "what happened?" requests a Directed Shape colimit (snapshot or audit-export); "what is happening at this cell?" requests an atom read; "what would be admissible if I proposed this?" requests a Cosections limit (validate-against-shape). The substrate's [witness](../objects/witness.md) emits the access-pattern label so the audit chain records observation kind alongside operator kind.
- **Cost class per pattern.** Atom reads are O(1) on indexed storage per [index-as-alexandrov-up-set](index-as-alexandrov-up-set.md). Directed Shape (colimit) reads are O(region-size) for in-cache regions, with the closure-ordinal cost when the colimit is not yet materialized. Cosections (limit) reads are constraint-satisfaction checks: per [governance-decision-modal-decidability](governance-decision-modal-decidability.md), polynomial in the strict and Scott-continuous regimes, PSPACE in the non-Scott-continuous regimes.
- **Cache discipline per pattern.** Per [cache-coherence-via-functorial-projection](cache-coherence-via-functorial-projection.md), the substrate's cache is a functorial projection of the predication-graph store. The colimit cone admits aggressive caching — once materialized, the snapshot is content-addressed (per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md)) and reusable until the next sequential act. The limit cone admits conservative caching — invalidated whenever any closure-rule application changes a constraint-relevant cell. Atom reads are cache-bypass-able when the underlying fiber is hot.
- **Modal correspondence.** The three access patterns map onto the substrate's bigraded modal structure per [substrate-operations-are-bigraded-modal](substrate-operations-are-bigraded-modal.md): atom = neither $\Box$ nor $\Diamond$ (the bare value), Directed Shape = $\Box \sigma$ (knowledge-saturated), Cosections = $\Box \Delta$ (truth-constrained). The triad therefore composes with the closure-kind taxonomy: a Process-kind operation's natural observation is Directed Shape; an Inquiry-kind operation's natural observation is Cosections.
- **Mixed-pattern queries surface their components.** A query that traverses regions (atom-by-atom) while accumulating an aggregate (colimit) and checking compatibility (limit) is structurally a composite. The substrate's query language ([query-language](teaching/explanations/query-language.md)) decomposes such queries into per-pattern phases for cost accounting and audit, rather than treating them as a single opaque operation.

## What the theorem rules out

- **A "fourth access pattern."** An observation that is neither an atom read, nor a colimit projection, nor a limit projection of the $T$-indexed settling — for example, a "median state" projection that does not factor through the colimit/limit/atom triad — is non-conformant per the substrate's query-language commitment above. Conformant compound queries (e.g., a delta between two snapshots) are colimit differences, expressible in the triad.
- **Snapshots without colimit-witness.** A "snapshot" that records a valuation without certifying it as the $\bigvee_k$ over a $T$-segment cannot be replayed deterministically (per [snapshot-replay-fidelity](snapshot-replay-fidelity.md)) and cannot serve as a Directed Shape. The substrate's snapshot witness records the segment endpoints and the colimit hash, not just the value.
- **Validate-against-shape implementations that are not limit-cone checks.** A "validation" that checks the proposed valuation against one settled state but not against the limit over the trajectory is missing the constraint-satisfaction content. Per the limit cone's structure, such a check certifies admissibility at one position only; the substrate's [conformance-test-suite](../objects/conformance-test-suite.md) requires the limit-cone discipline.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's [predication fibration](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md) is fiberwise $\leq_k$-complete (the predication fiber algebra admits arbitrary $\bigvee_k$ and $\bigwedge_k$).
- The substrate's runtime trajectory is a [predication settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) per [substrate-runtime-as-closure-driven-settling](substrate-runtime-as-closure-driven-settling.md).
- The substrate's regime is classified per [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md) so the colimit's closure ordinal is well-defined.

## Position

This theorem resolves the set-aside [modal-triad-access-patterns](questions/modal-triad-access-patterns.md) by identifying the math structure the three patterns realize: the universal-cone trichotomy (colimit / atom / limit) for $T$-indexed diagrams in the predication fibration. The companion theorem [three-acts-as-temporal-2-category-decomposition](three-acts-as-temporal-2-category-decomposition.md) classifies the *advance* triad on the same temporal axis; the two are dual — acts produce diagrams, accesses consume them — and together exhaust the substrate's surface for every conformant operation.
