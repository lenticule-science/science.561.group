## Annotation

Lotman's *Universe of the Mind* is the major English-language exposition of the Tartu-Moscow school's cultural semiotics, and the founding document of the *semiosphere* concept. For this theory's framing, three Lotman threads are load-bearing:

1. **Semiosphere as primitive.** The semiosphere is the *bounded cultural-historical region within which sign-processes are intelligible*; outside the semiosphere, sign-vehicles are inert. This grounds the [semiosphere](teaching/explanations/semiosphere.md) concept and Bridge 3 of the [composability narrative](teaching/explanations/composability-with-predicated-knowledge-systems.md): semiosphere ↔ discourse-community-as-fiber on the substrate.
2. **Boundary and translation.** The semiosphere has a boundary regulated by translation activity; the semiosphere is alive in proportion to its translation. This grounds the [translation](../morphisms/translation.md) morphism's status as load-bearing rather than incidental.
3. **Core-periphery asymmetry.** Central codes are stable and prestigious; peripheral codes are productive and unstable; new sign-relations characteristically arise at the periphery and migrate inward. This grounds the [lotmanian-system](../examples/lotmanian-system.md) example and informs the substrate-level reading of fiber-density gradients.
