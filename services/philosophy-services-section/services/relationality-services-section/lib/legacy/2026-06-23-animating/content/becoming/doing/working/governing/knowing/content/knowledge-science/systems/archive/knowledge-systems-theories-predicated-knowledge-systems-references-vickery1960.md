## Annotation

The Classification Research Group's working manual for designing faceted schemes for *special* (single-discipline) collections. Generalises Ranganathan's PMEST programme by allowing the number and identity of facets to vary by discipline rather than fixing them at five universal categories. Vickery's procedure: (i) collect the operative terms of the discipline, (ii) cluster them into facets by *kind* of attribute, (iii) order the facets by *citation order* (the canonical sequence in which a document's facet values are concatenated), (iv) ensure each facet is *mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive* within its scope.

The predicated framing recognises Vickery's procedure as the *empirical specification of a [predicate-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) for a domain*. The terms become objects of the category, the facets become collections of named predicates (a sub-vocabulary), and the citation order becomes a strict-regime [closure-rule-system](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-system.md) governing how cells compose into a class number. Mutual exclusion within a facet is the *coherence condition* — no cell may simultaneously assert two values for the same single-valued predicate of the same subject.

Vickery's recognition that *different disciplines need different facet sets* anticipates the framing's core claim that the predicate vocabulary is community-relative ([discourse-community-as-fiber](teaching/explanations/discourse-community-as-fiber.md)). What Vickery treats as an editorial decision the framing treats as a structural one: the choice of facets *is* the choice of fibration index.
