# A device substrate is conformant on its declared sub-fibration

**Claim.** For any [device substrate](teaching/explanations/device-substrate.md) $D$ with declared conformance profile listing a sub-vocabulary $V_D$, sub-operation-set $O_D$, and sub-capability-set $C_D$, the device is *conformant on the sub-fibration* $\mathcal{F}_D$ generated by $V_D$ — the device's runtime exactly realizes the substitution functors $f^* : A_{\sigma'} \to A_\sigma$ for morphisms $f \in O_D$ on the predicate-fibers in $V_D$. Per [conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor](conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor.md) restricted to $\mathcal{F}_D$, the device passes a sub-fibration conformance test.

The federation discipline accommodates restricted-conformant devices by routing only $V_D$-vocabulary content to $D$ and explicitly disclaiming operations outside $O_D$.

## Why this holds

Per [predication-fibration](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md), the substrate's full fibration is a functor $\mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{C}$ where $\mathcal{C}$ is the [parameter-base category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/parameter-base-category.md). A *sub-fibration* is the restriction of this functor to a subcategory $\mathcal{C}' \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ — fewer base objects, fewer morphisms, but otherwise the same fibration structure.

For a device substrate $D$:

- $\mathcal{C}'_D$ is the parameter-base category whose objects are the predicates in $V_D$ and whose morphisms are the operations in $O_D$.
- $\mathcal{F}_D$ is the restriction of the fibration to $\mathcal{C}'_D$ — predication fiber-algebras over $V_D$, with substitution functors for the $O_D$ morphisms.
- The capability declarations $C_D$ are the modal-restriction outputs the device admits per [governance-as-modal-restriction](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/theorems/governance-as-modal-restriction.md).

Per [conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor](conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor.md), conformance is exact functorial realization. Restricted to $\mathcal{F}_D$, the device's runtime must:

1. **Object-conformance on $V_D$.** For every predicate $p \in V_D$, the device's fiber realization is faithful per the value-domain (per [bilattice-operation-table-correctness](bilattice-operation-table-correctness.md)).
2. **Morphism-conformance on $O_D$.** For every operation $f \in O_D$, the device's runtime produces canonical-form-hash-identical outputs to the spec.
3. **Composition law on $O_D$.** Operations compose per the fibration's composition.
4. **Identity law on $V_D$.** Identity operations are identity functors.

If $D$ satisfies these four conditions on $\mathcal{F}_D$, $D$ is conformant on the sub-fibration. Operations outside $O_D$ are *disclaimed* — the device explicitly does not implement them, and federation peers are informed via the conformance profile.

## Engineering consequence

- **Federation routes only relevant content.** Per [shared-vocabulary-federation](teaching/explanations/shared-vocabulary-federation.md), peers federating with $D$ negotiate vocabulary; only $V_D$-vocabulary content reaches $D$. Per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md), $D$'s accessibility cells admit only operations within $O_D$.
- **Dispatch picks devices by declared capability.** Per [graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion](graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion.md), the dispatcher matches work-items to candidates by capability; per the theorem, $D$'s declared $C_D$ is the matchable capability set. A work-item requiring a capability outside $C_D$ doesn't reach $D$.
- **Restricted runtime is explicit, not implicit.** Per the conformance profile, peers know $D$'s limitations. A federation operation expecting capability outside $D$'s profile fails at the federation handshake, not deep inside $D$'s runtime.
- **Compute and storage budgets are declared.** Per [device-substrate](teaching/explanations/device-substrate.md), the conformance profile includes compute-budget; per the substrate's cost discipline (per [cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale](cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale.md)), peers can predict the device's load and pace dispatch accordingly.
- **Restricted conformance composes with full conformance.** A federation of fully-conformant substrates plus restricted-conformant devices is well-defined: the agreed vocabulary across peers is the intersection of all participating vocabularies; the per-peer operation set is the per-peer profile.

## Sub-fibration structure

Per [parameter-base-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/parameter-base-category.md), $\mathcal{C}'_D \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is a subcategory if it's closed under composition (the operations in $O_D$ compose to operations in $O_D$) and contains identities. The device's profile declaration must satisfy:

- $V_D$ contains all predicates referenced in $O_D$'s morphisms (no dangling references).
- $O_D$ is closed under the device's intended composition (e.g., a printer's "queue print job" composes with "advance queue" — both operations must be in $O_D$).
- $C_D$'s capabilities are realizable by the device's $O_D$ — no claimed capability that requires unimplemented operations.

Per [scope-cover-via-grothendieck-site](scope-cover-via-grothendieck-site.md), the device's sub-fibration corresponds to a sub-cover of the substrate's parameter-base site. Federation operates on the cover; the device's coverage is its declared scope.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Devices claiming full conformance.** A device declaring full conformance but only implementing a sub-fibration is misrepresenting; per [conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor](conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor.md), it fails the conformance test on operations outside its actual implementation. The substrate's federation handshake catches this discrepancy at link establishment.
- **Devices implementing operations not in their profile.** A device that admits operations outside its declared $O_D$ surprises peers — federation peers expected the device to disclaim those operations. The substrate's discipline is conservative: declared operations are the device's commitment, not a lower bound.
- **Federation peers expecting operations the device disclaims.** A peer that dispatches work requiring capability outside $C_D$ to $D$ has misread the profile. Per [graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion](graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion.md), the dispatcher checks capability compatibility before dispatch; mismatched dispatches fail at the matcher.
- **Device-substrate compromise affecting full-substrate operations.** A compromised device-substrate can affect operations within its sub-fibration (per its capability), but cannot affect operations outside (because the full-substrate has no expectation that the device handles them). Per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md), revocation of a device's accessibility moves the federation trust to $\mathbf{f}$ for that device's domain.

## What the theorem requires

- The device's conformance profile is recorded as substrate content (per [self-representation](teaching/explanations/self-representation.md)).
- The device's runtime exactly realizes the operations in $O_D$ on the predicates in $V_D$ (per [conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor](conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor.md) restricted to $\mathcal{F}_D$).
- The sub-fibration $\mathcal{F}_D$ is well-defined — composition-closed, identity-containing.
- Federation peers respect the profile — don't dispatch work requiring undeclared capabilities.

## Position

This theorem grounds the substrate's *peripheral-as-peer* discipline in the predication-categories' sub-fibration framework. Together with [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md) (bilattice-graded trust), [graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion](graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion.md) (capability-graded dispatch), and [shared-vocabulary-federation](teaching/explanations/shared-vocabulary-federation.md) (vocabulary handshake), it lets the substrate's federation scale from $1000-substrates to $50-printers without losing structural conformance properties. Devices are first-class peers; their restrictions are declared structurally; the substrate's federation handles them with the same machinery as full peers.
