# LeCun 2022 — JEPA / autonomous machine intelligence

LeCun, Y. (2022). A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence.
*OpenReview*. Version 0.9.2.

Position paper proposing the joint-embedding predictive
architecture (JEPA) family as the principal alternative to
generative-reconstruction approaches for world-model learning.
Argues that next-token prediction and pixel reconstruction are
both misdirected — capacity is wasted on irrelevant detail —
and that prediction *in latent space* of latent representations
is the right objective. Also articulates the position that
LLMs trained on text cannot be world models in any deep sense.
