A single deed is doing; **working** is what acting becomes when it is shared, sustained, and laid onto a common field.
Where the act is one bounded episode owned by one someone, the work-process is an actor contributing to — and drawing from — a field that other actors also work.
Working is the third moment of existence: the act made social and ongoing.

## Work is social

For Marx, labour is never the isolated exertion of a single body; production is from the start *cooperative*, a metabolism between people and world carried on together, each worker's contribution presupposing and feeding others'.
Arendt's *homo faber* names the complementary point: **work** (as against mere labour) builds a durable common *world* of artifacts that outlast any single act and form the shared stage on which further work goes on.
In both, the defining feature is that the worker acts *into a standing common medium*, not into the void.

## Coordination through a shared field

What distinguishes working from a chain of separate deeds is *how* the many are coordinated.
They are not coordinated point-to-point, each actor commanding the next; they are coordinated *through the field itself* — each reads the current state of the common work and contributes a move that others will in turn read.
This is the structure Hayek described in "The Use of Knowledge in Society": no mind holds the whole, and coordination is achieved through a shared medium that aggregates dispersed, partial contributions.
It is the structure activity theory (Vygotsky, Leontiev) calls *mediation* — joint activity organized through common artifacts — and that biology calls *stigmergy*: agents leaving and reading traces in a shared environment.

This is why working is the **agentic** face.
Where doing is the bare capacity to act, working is that capacity settled into a *contributor* — an actor whose moves carry definite values others can read off the common field, and whose own moves are answers to what the field already holds.
Agency here is, as the social theory of it observes, "structurally distributed": not a uniform possession but something exercised unevenly across positions in a shared field.

## Seats teaching and learning

Because working is coordination through a common field, its native acts are the two directions of transfer across that field: [teaching](../library/teaching/docs/teaching.md), writing a settled contribution *to* the field for others to read, and [learning](../library/learning/docs/learning.md), reading *from* the field to revise one's own.
The held positions that move across it are [beliefs](../library/belief/docs/belief.md).

## What it generates

Working generates the work-process, and within it opens [governing](../governing/docs/governing.md): the moment where the open, revisable shared field is brought under a bound — a settled order that caps and stabilizes the work.
