# Ji et al., "A Survey on Knowledge Graphs: Representation, Acquisition, and Applications" (2021)

Survey focused on KG embeddings, acquisition, and
applications.

## What it covers

- Knowledge representation learning (KG embeddings):
  TransE, DistMult, ComplEx, RotatE, ConvE, R-GCN,
  many more.
- Knowledge acquisition: entity discovery, relation
  extraction, KG completion.
- Temporal KGs.
- Applications: NLP, recommendation, QA.
- Future directions.

Complementary to Hogan et al. 2021's broader survey.

## Why it matters

Comprehensive coverage of KG embedding methods, which
are central to KG ML. Useful taxonomy and citation
list.

## How to read

Survey paper; readable selectively by topic.

## Related works

- Hogan et al. 2021 — broader KG survey.
- Wang et al. 2017 — earlier KG embedding survey.
