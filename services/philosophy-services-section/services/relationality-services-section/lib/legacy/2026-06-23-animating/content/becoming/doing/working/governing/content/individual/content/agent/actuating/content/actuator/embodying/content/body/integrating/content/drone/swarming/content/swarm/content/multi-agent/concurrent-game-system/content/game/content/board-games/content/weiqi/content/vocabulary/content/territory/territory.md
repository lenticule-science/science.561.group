Territory in [weiqi](../index.md) is the set of vacant points effectively enclosed by one player's stones and unavailable to the opponent except at unacceptable cost [@britannica2026go; @nagofed2026thisisgo].

Territory is the strategic heart of the game because it turns placement into scoring.
A move may increase territory directly by closing space, or indirectly by making an invasion weak.
This is why players often choose moves that neither attack nor defend a single group immediately but instead reshape the balance of large empty regions.

## Related terms

- [liberty](./liberty.md) - Territory remains meaningful only if the surrounding stones retain enough liberties to hold it.
- [capture](./capture.md) - Captures can secure or destroy territory by changing which stones remain on the board.
- [komi](./komi.md) - Komi adjusts final scores after territory has been counted.
- [ko](./ko.md) - Ko fights can determine whether a key territorial area remains secure.
