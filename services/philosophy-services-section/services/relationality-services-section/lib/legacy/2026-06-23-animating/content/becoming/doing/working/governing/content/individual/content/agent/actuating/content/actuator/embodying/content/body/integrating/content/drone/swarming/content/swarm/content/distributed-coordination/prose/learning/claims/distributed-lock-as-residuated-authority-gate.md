# Distributed locking is residuated authority-gating composed with modal lease-restriction; safety, lease auto-release, and reentrancy follow structurally under the $\alpha = \mathbf t$ admissibility regime

**Claim.** *(Under the authority-row admissibility regime stated in Setup: ownership is granted only at $\alpha_\ell = \mathbf t$; the rows $\bot, \mathbf f, \top$ route to refusal-witness or audit, never to lock issuance.)* A [distributed-lock](teaching/explanations/distributed-lock.md), as recorded by a [distributed-lock-record](../objects/distributed-lock-record.md) and operated by [acquire-distributed-lock](../morphisms/acquire-distributed-lock.md) and [release-distributed-lock](../morphisms/release-distributed-lock.md), is *not* a coordination primitive sui generis: it is the composition of two operators already present in the math parent, applied to a single predication cell. Concretely, write the lock state as the predication cell

$$\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell) \;\in\; \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathrm{holds}}(h, \ell)$$

with subject $h$ a candidate [identity-record](../../../objects/identity-record.md), object $\ell$ the lock-id, and predicate $\mathrm{holds}$ unary on (holder, resource). Then:

0. **Authority-row admissibility is a precondition.** Lock issuance is admissible only when the round's authority element $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf t$. The other three rows ($\bot, \mathbf f, \top$) are mapped to refusal or audit by the acquire rule itself, per the Setup admissibility table; they do not produce lock-tokens. This is not an optional convention — it is required by [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), since rows 3 and 4 of the table produce uniformly-permissive ($\mathbf f$) or identity-passthrough ($\top$) gating that would dissolve the lock.

1. **Acquire is residuated authority-gating, conditional on the admissibility regime.** The substrate-content evolution of $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell)$ under [acquire-distributed-lock](../morphisms/acquire-distributed-lock.md) is the [right residuation operator](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/right-residuation-operator.md) $\mathrm{R}_{\alpha_\ell}$ on the lock's [four-bilattice](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md) fiber, with authority element $\alpha_\ell \in \mathbf{FOUR}$ supplied by the cluster's quorum-witnessed authority on $\ell$ per [propose-quorum-value](../morphisms/propose-quorum-value.md). The lock-token returned by `acquire` is the witness recording the residual evaluation; per [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), its behavior on payloads is determined by the row $\alpha_\ell$ of the four-row table — and three of the four rows have substrate-design consequences different from the prose intuitions attached to their names.

2. **Lease validity is modal necessity along a heartbeat-shaped accessibility sub-region.** The substrate-recorded `lease-expires-at` field is the boundary of a sub-region $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ of the substrate's [accessibility relation](../../teaching/explanations/substrate-accessibility-relation.md). Lease validity at substrate-clock window $t$ is exactly $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}} \mathrm{holds}(h, \ell)$ — the [modal necessity operator](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/modal-necessity-operator.md) on the holds-predication, parameterized by the lease sub-region of the same $R$ that drives [failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion](failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion.md). Lease auto-release is *not* a separate cleanup step: it is the $\mathbf f$-collapse of $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ at $t > \text{lease-expires-at}$ (matching the Setup table value for the post-expiry interval), which makes $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}} \mathrm{holds}$ no longer derivable and the *derived* `current-holder` projection on [distributed-lock-record](../objects/distributed-lock-record.md) recompute to absent. Per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), the lease check is one more application of the substrate's single $\Box_R$ evaluator, parameterized by $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ rather than by trust-boundary or governance sub-regions.

3. **Mutual exclusion is the cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merge of the holder predication, plus quorum-overlap.** Two distinct active holders $h_1 \neq h_2$ on the same lock-id $\ell$ would contribute $\mathrm{holds}(h_1, \ell) = \mathbf t$ and $\mathrm{holds}(h_2, \ell) = \mathbf t$ as conflicting sections of the lock's [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) on the cluster's [replica cover](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/replica-cover.md). On the holder-axis, the cellwise $\vee_k$-merge per [bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md) evaluated on the unary predicate "$\ell$ is held by $\cdot$" admits at most one truth-side surviving witness, because [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md) forces the quorum-overlap discipline of [replica-mayer-vietoris](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/replica-mayer-vietoris.md) on the acquire round. Two simultaneously-surviving holders would correspond to two non-isomorphic global sections of the same sheaf — ruled out by the same gluing argument that grounds [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md).

4. **Reentrancy and idempotent re-acquire are closure-2-monad lax-idempotence.** A holder $h$ that re-issues `acquire(\ell)` on a lock it already holds gets back the same lock-token (modulo witness-chain extension). This is the substrate-content reading of [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md): the closure-rule that derives $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell)$ from a prior $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell)$ has counit $\mu \cdot \eta_{\mathrm{Cl}} = 1_{\mathrm{Cl}}$ on the holds-fiber. Re-acquire is not a separate "reentrancy" code path; it is the structural property of the same closure operator that handled the first acquire.

5. **No oscillation between holders.** Per [closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits.md), the holder-predication state cannot enter a period-$\geq 2$ orbit under the lock's closure-rule system. The "ping-pong" failure mode — two candidates each acquiring, releasing, and re-acquiring the lock in a cycle the protocol cannot exit — is structurally impossible whenever the acquire/release rules are inflationary, monotone, and idempotent on the substrate's lock-state predications. The same theorem already grounds [leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits](leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits.md) for elections; locks inherit it without re-derivation.

The substrate's distributed-lock module is therefore an instance of two existing modal/residuation operators in composition, not a fresh primitive. The four engineering invariants — mutual exclusion, lease auto-release, reentrancy, and termination — are each one corpus-level theorem away.

## Setup

Fix a [cluster-record](../objects/cluster-record.md) $C$ of $N$ members with [cluster-as-replica-cover](teaching/explanations/cluster-as-replica-cover.md) presentation $\{U_i \to U\}$. Fix a lock-id $\ell$ and let $H = \{h_1, \ldots, h_N\}$ range over candidate [identity-records](../../../objects/identity-record.md). Take the predicate $\mathrm{holds} \in \mathcal C$ to be unary on $(\text{holder}, \text{resource})$ with predication cells $(h, \ell) \in H \times L$.

Per [propose-quorum-value](../morphisms/propose-quorum-value.md), the cluster's quorum on $\ell$ at acquisition round $r$ produces an **authority element** $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} \in \mathbf{FOUR}$:

- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf t$ when the quorum has agreed that $h$ holds and the lease is fresh — the "affirming authority" row of [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md).
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \bot$ when the cluster has not yet decided in round $r$ — the "silent authority" row.
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf f$ when the quorum has decided to *deny* the request (e.g., the resource is administratively quiesced) — the "denying authority" row, which by the table produces uniformly $\mathbf t$ residuation; the substrate must read this row through the table, not through the prose name (see Engineering consequence below).
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \top$ when the cluster carries paraconsistent evidence about ownership — for example, two partitions both claim a holder. Per the table, $\top$ is the right-residual *unit*, so $\mathrm{R}_\top$ is the identity: the residual gate vanishes in this case, and the lock is structurally ungated. The substrate's discipline is to surface the $\top$-cell to [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../theorems/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), not to grant the lock.

**Authority-row admissibility (acquire policy).** Per [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), only one of the four authority values is admissible as an *ownership-granting* row of $\mathrm{R}_{\alpha_\ell}$:

- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf t$ — **the only ownership-granting row.** The acquire rule writes $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell) = \mathbf t$ to the holds-fiber and emits a lock-token.
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \bot$ — **undecided.** The acquire rule writes nothing on the holds-fiber and routes to the refusal-witness path of [acquire-distributed-lock](../morphisms/acquire-distributed-lock.md). The substrate must re-run the round; lock-tokens issued under $\bot$ are non-conformant.
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf f$ — **deny-row.** Per the residuation table, $\mathrm{R}_{\mathbf f}$ is uniformly $\mathbf t$-valued (paraconsistent ex-falso). Using this row to gate ownership would grant the lock on every payload — the opposite of the prose intuition. The acquire rule must route this row to the refusal-witness path; ownership cannot be issued under $\alpha_\ell = \mathbf f$, regardless of the residuation output.
- $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \top$ — **paraconsistent (split) authority.** Per the table, $\mathrm{R}_\top$ is the identity, so the residual gate vanishes. The acquire rule must route this row to [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../theorems/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) and emit a refusal-witness with the $\top$-cell attached; ownership cannot be issued under $\alpha_\ell = \top$.

This admissibility discipline is **mandatory**: without it, the row-3 and row-4 readings of [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md) make the lock structurally ungated. The clauses below assume this admissibility regime; under any other regime the lock-safety invariants do not follow.

Form the **lease accessibility relation** $R_{\mathrm{lease}} : W \times W \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ over the observation-window world set $W = T \times H$ of substrate-clock windows and candidate holders, by analogy with [failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion](failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion.md) clause "Setup":

$$R_{\mathrm{lease}}\bigl((t_1, h), (t_2, h)\bigr) \;:=\; \begin{cases} \mathbf t & \text{if a lease for } h \text{ on } \ell \text{ covers the interval } [t_1, t_2], \\ \bot & \text{if the lease state at } [t_1, t_2] \text{ is not yet recorded}, \\ \mathbf f & \text{if the lease has expired or been released by } [t_1, t_2]. \end{cases}$$

Off-diagonal $(h \neq h')$ entries are $\bot$: a lease for $h$ does not give evidence about $h'$. Per [bilattice-accessibility-relation](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice-accessibility-relation.md), $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ is by construction $\leq_k$-monotone in window time.

## Statement

**Theorem (distributed lock as residuated authority-gating composed with modal lease-restriction).** *With $C$, $\ell$, $\alpha_\ell^{(r)}$, $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ as above, and provided fewer than $\lceil N/2 \rceil$ voters fail simultaneously per [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md):*

1. **Acquire = residuated authority-gating.** *The substrate update issued by [acquire-distributed-lock](../morphisms/acquire-distributed-lock.md)$(\ell, h, \delta)$ at round $r$ is the cellwise application of $\mathrm{R}_{\alpha_\ell^{(r)}}$ to the candidate's request payload, on the holds-fiber. The returned lock-token is the witness pair $(\alpha_\ell^{(r)}, \mathrm{R}_{\alpha_\ell^{(r)}}(\text{payload}))$ signed per [signature-binds-witness-to-identity](../../../theorems/signature-binds-witness-to-identity.md).*

2. **Lease check = $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}}$.** *The substrate's lease-validity check at clock window $t$ is exactly $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}} \mathrm{holds}(h, \ell)$, evaluated by the same $\Box_R$ implementation that discharges the six surfaces of [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md). Lease auto-release is the $\mathbf f$-collapse of $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ at $t > \text{lease-expires-at}$, after which $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}} \mathrm{holds}$ is no longer satisfied — the holder predication ceases to be modally supported, and the substrate's closure-rule for "release on lease expiry" fires automatically.*

3. **Mutual exclusion.** *At every round $r$ in which $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} \in \{\mathbf t\}$ (the only authority value under which a non-trivial residual grants ownership-as-truth-side), at most one holder $h$ has $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell) = \mathbf t$ in the cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merged section of the lock-sheaf on the cluster cover. Two surviving holders would force the merged section to violate quorum-overlap per [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md) (clause "Cohomological source"), or to glue inconsistently per [replica-mayer-vietoris](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/replica-mayer-vietoris.md).*

4. **Reentrancy.** *For any holder $h$ that re-issues `acquire`$(\ell)$ while holding $\ell$, the substrate update is the identity: per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), $\mathrm{Cl}^2 = \mathrm{Cl}$ on the holds-fiber, so the second application of the acquire closure-rule produces the previously-witnessed lock-token (with extended witness chain). The substrate hosts no separate reentrancy state machine.*

5. **No-oscillation termination.** *Per [closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits.md), the holder-state predication cannot enter a period-$\geq 2$ orbit under inflationary-monotone-idempotent acquire/release rules. Lock-handoff oscillation between two contending holders is structurally impossible in the substrate's regime.*

## Proof

Each clause is a one-step reduction to a single corpus-level theorem.

**(1) Acquire = residuated authority-gating, under the admissibility regime.** The substrate's acquire rule, written in the [closure-rule wire format](../../teaching/explanations/closure-rule-wire-format.md), takes the form "if quorum-authority on $\ell$ is $\alpha$ and request-payload on $\ell$ is $x$, *first dispatch on $\alpha$* per the Setup admissibility table, then on $\alpha = \mathbf t$ write $\mathrm{R}_{\mathbf t}(x)$ to the holds-fiber cell $(h, \ell)$ and emit the witness $(\mathbf t, \mathrm{R}_{\mathbf t}(x))$; on $\alpha \in \{\bot, \mathbf f\}$ emit a refusal-witness; on $\alpha = \top$ emit a refusal-witness and surface the $\top$-cell to inconsistency-sheaf audit." The on-$\mathbf t$ branch is the substrate-content surface of [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), specialized to the subject $h$ as recipient and the lock-id $\ell$ as resource (and, by row 2 of the table, $\mathrm{R}_{\mathbf t}$ acts as the identity on $\{\bot, \mathbf t, \mathbf f\}$ payloads and demotes $\top$-payloads — a substrate-content discipline the acquire-rule inherits from the table). The witness is signed per [signature-binds-witness-to-identity](../../../theorems/signature-binds-witness-to-identity.md), making the residual evaluation non-repudiable. $\square$

**(2) Lease check = $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}}$.** $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ is, by construction, a sub-region of the substrate's [accessibility relation](../../teaching/explanations/substrate-accessibility-relation.md): its world-pair indexing is $(t_1, h) \times (t_2, h)$ over the $W = T \times H$ window-holder world set. By [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), every $\Box_R$ evaluation on a sub-region of the substrate's $R$ is one application of the substrate's single $\Box_R$ implementation. The lease-validity check $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{lease}}} \mathrm{holds}$ is therefore the standard $\Box_R$ evaluation, parameterized by $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$. The auto-release behavior follows because $R_{\mathrm{lease}}\bigl((t_1, h), (t_2, h)\bigr) = \mathbf f$ for $t_2 > \text{lease-expires-at}$ collapses the modal support: per [modal-necessity-basic-properties](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/modal-necessity-basic-properties.md), $\Box_R u$ is non-trivial only on the $\leq_k$-image of cells where $R$ carries non-$\mathbf f$ value. The substrate's lease-expiry handling is *not* a destructive write to the holds-fiber: it is the recomputation of the *derived* `current-holder` projection on [distributed-lock-record](../objects/distributed-lock-record.md) under the new $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ values, which yields *absent* exactly when no holds-witness in the chain is currently lease-modally supported. The holds-witness chain is preserved (audit-relevant); only the derived view changes. This preserves the inflationary closure-discipline that clause (5) below depends on. $\square$

**(3) Mutual exclusion.** Suppose for contradiction that distinct holders $h_1, h_2$ both have $\mathrm{holds}(h_i, \ell) = \mathbf t$ at the same round $r$ with $\alpha_\ell^{(r)} = \mathbf t$. Per [propose-quorum-value](../morphisms/propose-quorum-value.md) and [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md) (clause "Cohomological source"), the two acquire-quorums $Q_1, Q_2$ supporting $\mathrm{holds}(h_1, \ell)$ and $\mathrm{holds}(h_2, \ell)$ are sections of the lock-sheaf over $V_1, V_2 \subseteq C$ with $|V_i| \geq \lceil N/2 \rceil + 1$, hence $V_1 \cap V_2 \neq \emptyset$ (pigeonhole). Per [replica-mayer-vietoris](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/replica-mayer-vietoris.md), the two sections glue to a global section of the lock-sheaf iff their restrictions to $V_1 \cap V_2$ agree. Per the no-double-vote rule signed by [signature-binds-witness-to-identity](../../../theorems/signature-binds-witness-to-identity.md), no member in $V_1 \cap V_2$ has signed both for $h_1$ and for $h_2$ on $\ell$ in round $r$. Therefore at least one of the two sections does not extend to the overlap — contradicting the assumption that both achieved quorum-merged truth-side support. The cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merge per [bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md) collapses $\mathbf t \vee_k \mathbf t = \mathbf t$ to a single survivor; the loser's section is recorded as $\bot$ on the holder-axis. $\square$

**(4) Reentrancy.** The acquire closure-rule on the holds-fiber is, by construction, inflationary, monotone, and idempotent — these are the standard closure-operator axioms for the rule to be a closure-rule per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](../../../theorems/closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md). Per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), the closure 2-monad satisfies $\mu \cdot \eta_\mathrm{Cl} = 1_\mathrm{Cl}$. Re-applying the acquire rule to a holds-cell that already carries $\mathrm{holds}(h, \ell) = \mathbf t$ is the composite $\mathrm{Cl} \circ \eta$, which equals $\mathrm{Cl}$ by lax-idempotence; the substrate writes back the same value, extends the witness chain by one signed re-acquire entry, and emits the prior lock-token. $\square$

**(5) No oscillation.** Per [closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits.md), iterating $\mathrm{Cl}$ on any seed produces an ascending chain that stabilizes; no orbit of minimal period $\geq 2$ exists. The holds-predication state, evolved under the union of acquire-rules and release-rules (both of which must be inflationary on their target predications to be admissible closure-rules), inherits the no-periodic-orbit property. A genuine ping-pong $h_1 \to h_2 \to h_1 \to h_2 \to \cdots$ would require a release-rule that is non-inflationary on the holds-predication — i.e., a rule that *retracts* the holds-cell to its prior state — which falls outside the closure-2-monad frame. The substrate's release rule is instead a *new* closure on the conjugate "released" predication; the holds-cell is not retracted but is succeeded in the witness chain. Per [closure-driven-settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-driven-settling-monotonicity.md), the resulting trajectory is $\leq_k$-monotone, and per the no-periodic-orbit theorem, it terminates at a fixed point — either a single stable holder (or no-holder, in the released-and-not-yet-reacquired state). $\square$

## Engineering consequence

- **Locks have no separate primitive in the substrate.** A substrate that already implements the right-residuation operator $\Rightarrow$ on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ (per [residuation-orbit-monotonicity](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuation-orbit-monotonicity.md) and [four-bilattice-residuation-properties](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-bilattice-residuation-properties.md)), the modal necessity $\Box_R$ (per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md)), and quorum consensus (per [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md)) gets distributed locking *for free*. There is no `lock-manager` subsystem to ship; there is one residuation evaluator, one $\Box_R$ evaluator, and one quorum protocol, parameterized by lock-id and lease-window for the lock surface.
- **The four authority rows are the lock's substrate-policy choices.** Per [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), the four values of $\alpha_\ell^{(r)}$ have *determinate, non-intuitive* behaviors. Substrates that name an authority element after its prose intuition (e.g., calling $\mathbf f$ "deny" or $\top$ "admin") will mis-specify the lock's gating: $\mathbf f$ produces uniformly $\mathbf t$ output (ex-falso permissiveness), and $\top$ is the residual *unit* (no gating at all). The substrate's lock-policy must read the four-row table directly.
- **Lease auto-release is structural.** Implementations should not write lease-expiry watchdogs as separate timers; per clause (2), the existing $\Box_R$ evaluator on $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ produces $\bot$ at expiry and the existing closure-rule engine fires the release. A substrate with a separate lease-watchdog is doing the same work twice and risks the watchdog falling out of sync with the modal evaluator.
- **Reentrancy is not a feature, it is the closure being a closure.** Implementations that special-case "is this a re-acquire by the current holder?" to skip work are correct but redundant: per clause (4), the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence already produces the same outcome from the unchanged closure-rule path. Re-acquire and first-acquire are one code path.
- **Mutex composes with leader-election and failure-detection.** Per [leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits](leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits.md) and [failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion](failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion.md), election termination, suspicion of a failed leader, and lock acquisition are three closure-rule systems on the same predication fiber-algebra. Per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](../../../theorems/closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), their union is itself a closure 2-monad. A holder that fails (suspicion crosses threshold) drops out of the holds-cell via the lease-modal collapse of clause (2); the next acquire round runs as a fresh closure on the now-vacant cell. The "failover-reacquire" sequence is one closure-rule evaluation across the union of failure-detection, lease, and acquire predicates — not a sequential handoff between three subsystems.
- **Audit cost is bounded by $H^1$.** Per [coordination-cohomologically-bounded-by-h1](coordination-cohomologically-bounded-by-h1.md), every coordination obstruction the lock-sheaf can carry lives in $H^0$ or $H^1$ of the cluster cover. A lock acquisition decision is an $H^0$-section construction; a contention-disagreement that does not glue is an $H^1$-class. The audit machinery for distributed locks is the same audit machinery that handles every other coordination obstruction — no $H^{\geq 2}$ machinery is required.
- **Non-amplification of the lock-token follows the modal-restriction discipline.** Per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md) (engineering consequence 2, "authorization composes structurally"), composing a lock-token with downstream capability checks is non-amplifying: the token cannot grant more on transit than it grants at issuance. A holder cannot delegate a "stronger" lock-token than the one the substrate issued; the residual evaluation that produced it is the substrate-recorded ceiling.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Lock implementations that retract the holds-cell.** A release-rule that *removes* the prior holds-predication from the substrate (rather than succeeding it in the witness chain) is non-inflationary and falls outside the closure-2-monad frame. Such implementations may oscillate per clause (5); the substrate's discipline is to record release as a new fact on the conjugate predication, not as deletion of the prior fact.
- **Locks that admit two simultaneous holders by quorum-overriding.** A protocol that allows a "split-lock" outcome where two holders both receive valid tokens breaks clause (3). This is the same failure mode that breaks [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md): a voter signing for both candidates on the same lock-id in the same round.
- **Lease implementations that depend on synchronized clocks.** Per [failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion](failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion.md), $R_{\mathrm{lease}}$ is window-based, not timestamp-based; substrates that read lease expiry from wall-clock comparison rather than from the modal evaluator are subject to clock-skew attacks. The modal apparatus reads only the substrate's recorded window state, which is itself a quorum-witnessed fact.
- **"Fast path" lock acquisitions that bypass quorum.** A protocol that grants the lock on a single peer's say-so (e.g., "if the leader knows it's the only candidate, skip the round") breaks clause (3): the cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merge can no longer rule out a contending holder elected on the partition's other side. Per [quorum-safety-under-partition](quorum-safety-under-partition.md), only quorum-witnessed acquires are partition-safe.
- **Locks named after intuitive authority words.** A substrate-policy that uses $\mathbf f$ as "the deny authority" inadvertently specifies uniformly-permissive gating per [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md), row 3. A substrate that uses $\top$ as "the admin override" inadvertently specifies the identity residual — no gating — per row 4. Lock-policy declarations must read the four-row table; the corresponding row-3 / row-4 readings must be made via different operators (e.g. an explicit projection).

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's lock-state predication $\mathrm{holds}$ is settled under a closure-rule system per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](../../../theorems/closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md).
- The acquire rule is presented in the [closure-rule wire format](../../teaching/explanations/closure-rule-wire-format.md) as residuation by a quorum-witnessed authority element.
- The lease-validity check is implemented as $\Box_R$ evaluation on the lease sub-region of [substrate-accessibility-relation](../../teaching/explanations/substrate-accessibility-relation.md), per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md).
- The cluster runs [propose-quorum-value](../morphisms/propose-quorum-value.md) for the authority element, with the safety hypotheses of [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md) and [quorum-safety-under-partition](quorum-safety-under-partition.md) (fewer than $\lceil N/2 \rceil$ simultaneous voter failures, fixed participant set).
- Acquire and release rules are inflationary, monotone, and idempotent on their target predications; release writes a successor to the conjugate "released" predication rather than retracting the holds-cell.

## Scope and limits

The theorem holds **only** under the authority-row admissibility regime stated in Setup. A substrate that issues lock-tokens on $\alpha_\ell \in \{\bot, \mathbf f, \top\}$ — for example, a "fast path" that grants on undecided rounds, a deny-by-default policy that mis-reads row 3, or an admin override that mis-reads row 4 — falls outside the theorem's scope, and clauses (3)–(5) do not follow. The admissibility regime is the load-bearing precondition; it must be enforced inside the acquire rule itself (by dispatch on $\alpha$ before any write to the holds-fiber), not as a downstream check.

## Position

This theorem closes the open distributed-lock theorem gap in the platform. Together with [leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits](leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits.md), [quorum-consensus-safety](quorum-consensus-safety.md), [quorum-safety-under-partition](quorum-safety-under-partition.md), and [failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion](failure-detection-as-bilattice-modal-suspicion.md), it grounds the four named coordination surfaces — election, consensus, partition behavior, failure detection, locking — in a single shared apparatus: the closure 2-monad, the modal necessity operator, the right-residuation operator on $\mathbf{FOUR}$, the cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merge of the cluster sheaf, and the replica Mayer–Vietoris gluing constraint. None of these is lock-specific; each is a general operator whose lock-surface specialization is one parameterization away.

The platform's distributed locking is, on this reading, not a coordination primitive that the substrate provides alongside the others. It is the residual gating operator from [residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/residuated-restriction-as-authority-gate.md) composed with the modal restriction from [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), parameterized by a lock-id and a lease window. The substrate's claim to *be simpler than what it does* — already invoked for runtime, authorization, recovery, and event-log — extends here to coordination, and to the lock primitive specifically.
