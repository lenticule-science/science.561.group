# Predicated Politics

A theory of [politics](../politics/_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics: political life as a [predication settling](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) over a polity-themed predicate category.
Standing, claim, sovereignty, authority-installation, contestation, recognition, refusal, legitimation — the politics vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this substrate.

Sibling to [predicated-sociology](../predicated-sociology/) (the general social layer) and to [predicated-work](work/theories/predicated-work/) / [predicated-governance](work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/) (operations and normative substructure of work).
Predicated-politics is the substrate-native theory of the *political* layer: how polities constitute themselves, how authority is installed and contested, how sovereignty is exercised, how legitimation regimes settle and destabilize.

Following the [predication-categories research convention](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md).
Same frontmatter, same status markers, same relation set.
Notes here depend on predication-categories' substrate objects and morphisms.

## What this subtheory will commit to

The substantive commitments are sketched in advance here; their detailed substrate articulation accrues as downstream leaves require:

- **A polity is substrate content.** A polity is a [predication-cell-system](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-cell-set.md) whose entities include persons, places, offices, councils, ceremonies, and claims, related by predicates like *member-of*, *bound-by*, *speaks-for*, *witnessed-as*, *holds-standing-with-respect-to*.
  This is not metaphor — it is the substrate's primary reading of what a polity is.

- **Political activities are morphisms.** Council deliberation, decision-binding, ceremony, authority-installation, refusal, withdrawal, recognition, contestation are substrate operations: witness-bearing increments to the polity's settling.
  The [four-regime classification](../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md) applies to political-stability questions in a structured way.

- **Polity-form is closure-rule structure.** Different polity-forms correspond to different closure-rule families on the political substrate.
  The state-form has closure rules that produce centralized command-cells; state-refusing polity-forms (Clastres' anti-state polities, Lakota political life) have closure-rule families that structurally prevent command-cell formation.
  The mathematics of which closure-rule families produce which polity-forms is a substantive research question for this subtheory.

- **Sovereignty is a property of the polity's self-witnessing.** A polity is sovereign with respect to a domain when the witnessing for cells in that domain is internal to the polity.
  Recognition by another polity is a witness contribution; refusal of recognition is the polity's claim that external witness is not constitutive of its own settling.
  The Indigenous politics-of-refusal analyzed in [politics/references](../politics/references/) becomes a structural feature of the substrate: refusal is the polity asserting that its own witnessing is sufficient.

- **Legitimation is closure-rule consent.** A polity's authority-cells are legitimately settled when the closure rules that fire on them have the consent of the affected parties — where consent itself is a substrate-recorded condition.
  Weber's traditional, charismatic, and legal-rational legitimation types become specific configurations of how that consent is recorded; kinship-based legitimation (Ella Deloria's Dakota/Lakota material) becomes a fourth configuration with its own substrate structure.

## Why scoped this way

A predicated theory does not derive its substance from mathematics.
The mathematics provides shape; the host discipline provides content.
For predicated-politics, the host discipline is [politics](../politics/_index.md), and the substantive choices — which polity-forms to treat as primary, which closure-rule families to study, what counts as a polity-cell-system — are political-sociological decisions.
The mathematics tells us *what follows from* those decisions, structurally; it does not make them.

This is especially important for the lakotaean leaf.
The substrate-algebraic shape will be developed; the Lakota political content it shapes is held under Lakota authority and is not negotiable by the formalism.
