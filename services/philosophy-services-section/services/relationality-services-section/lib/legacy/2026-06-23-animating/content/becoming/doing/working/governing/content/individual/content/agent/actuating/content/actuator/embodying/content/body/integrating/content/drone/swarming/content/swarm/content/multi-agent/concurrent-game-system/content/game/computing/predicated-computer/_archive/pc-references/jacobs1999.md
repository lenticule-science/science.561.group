## Content summary

Develops the fibred-categorical formulation of first-order predicate logic and dependent type theory: a *predicate* over a context $\Gamma$ is an object in the fiber over $\Gamma$ in a fibration $\mathcal{P} \to \mathcal{B}$ of predicates over contexts; substitution along a context map $f: \Gamma \to \Gamma'$ is the reindexing functor $f^*$; existential and universal quantifiers along a projection are the left and right adjoints of $f^*$ (Lawvere's "quantifiers as adjoints"); equality on a context is the left adjoint of contraction; the Beck–Chevalley condition expresses the substitution-quantifier coherence that the engineering side calls *uniformity*. Comprehension extends the apparatus to dependent types via a right adjoint to the truth-predicate functor; the resulting structure (a *comprehension category* / *category-with-attributes*) is the categorical record of "every predicate carves out a sub-context."
