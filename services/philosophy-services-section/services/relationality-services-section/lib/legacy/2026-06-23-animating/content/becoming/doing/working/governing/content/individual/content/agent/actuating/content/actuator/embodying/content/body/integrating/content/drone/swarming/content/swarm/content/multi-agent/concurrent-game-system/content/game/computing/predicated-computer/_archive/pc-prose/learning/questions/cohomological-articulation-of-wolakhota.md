# What is the cohomological articulation of wolakȟota?

**Question.** Which substrate sectors stabilize under wolakȟota? How do the four virtues — wówačhantognaka (generosity), wóksape (wisdom), wóohitika (bravery), wówačhiŋtȟaŋka (fortitude) — correspond as substrate-content predicates whose stabilization implements the tiyóšpaye's instance of [sector-selective stabilization](../../teaching/explanations/sector-selective-stabilization.md)?

## What is established

- Wolakȟota is the tiyóšpaye's closure-rule subsystem (per [`tiyospaye-is-agentic-drone-swarm-may-2026`](../surveys/tiyospaye-is-agentic-drone-swarm-may-2026.md), §3.5). Its substantive content is the Lakota lifeway: practice rather than possession, a substantive evaluative standard, resilient to disruption.
- The four virtues are wolakȟota's load-bearing content. Each is taught through story and apprenticeship rather than as propositional principle; the virtues are also the qualifications for naca (recognized polity leadership).
- The framework's general structural feature of sector-selective stabilization (per [`aperiodic-regime-sector-selective-convergence`](../claims/aperiodic-regime-sector-selective-convergence.md)) requires that *some* sectors of the aperiodic-classifying-invariant stabilize and others remain live; *which* sectors play which role is governance-regime-specific.
- In the kyriarchical regime, the stabilizing sectors are $H^1_{\chi_-}, H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$ (the safety-critical sectors gating dispatch certifiability). In the kin-relational regime, the stabilizing sectors are different and not yet identified.
- The informant material at `theories/.../predicated-lakotaean-work/governance/objects/wolakhota-closure-system.md` has begun the substrate-algebraic articulation of wolakȟota as a closure-rule subsystem.

## What remains open

Specifying *which* $K_4$-equivariant sectors stabilize under wolakȟota, and how the four virtues correspond to substrate-content predicates whose stabilization implements that sector-vanishing.

Three sub-questions:

1. *Per-virtue substrate-content correspondence.* Each virtue is, structurally, a class of conduct-cells. Wówačhantognaka stabilizes when generosity-conduct holds; wóksape stabilizes when situated practical reason is exercised; and so on. The substrate-content predicates that encode each virtue — and the cohomological sectors their stabilization vanishes in — need to be identified.
2. *Conjunction-cell mechanics for naca-recognition.* The naca's standing in a tiyóšpaye is the conjunction of member-recognition cells across the membership, with the substantive content of each recognition-cell being the member's judgment on the candidate's conduct in the four virtues (per the tiyóšpaye atom at `theories/.../predicated-lakotaean-work/governance/objects/tiyospaye.md`). The cohomological articulation: under what conditions does this conjunction-cell stabilize, in what sector, and how does its stabilization correspond to wolakȟota's general stabilization?
3. *Distinction from kyriarchical sectors.* The kin-relational regime's stabilizing sectors are *not* the kyriarchical safety-critical sectors $H^1_{\chi_-}, H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$ (that conflation was the failure mode the corpus names at [`dual-governance-context`](../../teaching/explanations/dual-governance-context.md)). Articulating *which* sectors the kin-relational regime stabilizes — and how those sectors structurally differ from the kyriarchical case — is the load-bearing distinction the answer needs to make.

## What an answer would look like

A satisfactory answer would:

- Identify the four virtues as substrate-content predicates, with each virtue's predicate-class structurally defined in the polity's predication-fiber algebra.
- Specify the cohomological sectors in which the virtues' stabilization vanishes — likely a subset of $\{H^1_\mathrm{id}, H^1_{\chi_\sim}\}$ or a refinement of these, distinct from the kyriarchical sectors.
- Establish the relationship between virtue-stabilization and naca-recognition-cell conjunction — how the membership's recognition of conduct manifests as cohomology vanishing in the appropriate sectors.
- Show how the kin-relational regime's stabilization corresponds to the framework's general sector-selective-stabilization claim without importing kyriarchical apparatus.

## Why it matters

The aperiodicity-as-tractability finding ([`aperiodicity-as-tractability`](../claims/aperiodicity-as-tractability.md)) requires governance-regime-specific rate-limiting on consultations. The kin-relational regime's rate-limiting mechanism is consensus-by-withdrawal and kin-recognition cycles. The cohomological articulation of wolakȟota is what would make this rate-limiting mechanism formally specifiable — what counts as the kin-relational regime's instance of "aim-frontier dispatch," in cohomological terms.

More broadly: the tiyóšpaye's continuity-through-disruption (Estes 2019) — its capacity to maintain wolakȟota across settler-state pressure — is the political-historical observation that this question would give cohomological content to. The settler-state's various pressures aim at forcing wolakȟota's stabilizing sectors into the unstable region; specifying which sectors those are makes the resistance precisely articulable.
