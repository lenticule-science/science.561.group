## Derivation
- Postulate:: It must be derived that relationlessness is without relation, yet relationlessness is the exclusion of relation: a relation to relation.
- Derivative:: Relationlessness has relation to relation, thus it cannot not derive there is a *reflex* between relationlessness and relation.
- Statement:: Reflex = (Relationlessness Relation)
## Commentary

Reflex is the primordial structure of relation emerging from the absence of relation. It defines the fundamental act by which relations arise from relationlessness.

Reflex is a binary construction: it relates relationlessness to relation, bridging exclusion and coherence.
