## Scope

This survey records the structural definitions at the categorical-theoretic core of predication categories as they stand in the library at `theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/`. It covers the predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ and its four canonical morphism generators; the predication fiber algebra $A_p = \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E}$ and its underlying twist-product structure over a residuated lattice $\mathbf{L}$; the smallest non-trivial bilattice $\mathbf{FOUR}$; the predication fibration $p : \mathcal{E} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{C}$; the refinement to a predication hyperdoctrine via two parallel quantifier-adjoint systems; and the predication settling $(v_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$ as a $\leq_k$-monotone family of valuations indexed by an ordinal bound $\lambda$. It excludes — and refers to companion surveys — the bitopological / Stone-dual side, the cohomology of settlings, the moduli theory, the speech-act and adjudication reading, the CRDT correspondence, the physical specializations, the cobordism category of boundary predication systems, and the named-theorem corpus.

## Content

### The predicate category $\mathcal{C}$

A predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ is a small category whose objects are predicates (drawn from a fixed predicate set $\Pi$) and whose morphisms are generated by four canonical classes: predicate specialization, predicate converse, predicate subject-projection, and predicate object-projection. Each generator is induced by a manipulation of the entity-pair index $E \times E$ over a fixed entity set $E$, and each induces a bilattice homomorphism between the predication fiber algebras at the predicates it relates. Under a $\mathrm{Sym}(E)$-equivariance hypothesis with $|E| \geq 3$, the four classes exhaust the natural bilattice homomorphisms between fiber algebras (predicate-generator exhaustiveness theorem).

Specific theorems impose additional structure on $\mathcal{C}$: finite limits are needed for Beck–Chevalley commutation, and cohomological dimension $\mathrm{cd}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \leq 1$ is needed for the degree-bound $H^{\geq 2} = 0$ and the downstream $\mathrm{Ext}$-classifications. The latter is automatic for finite $\mathcal{C}$, for directed posets, and for every $\mathcal{C}$ built from the four predicate generators on a finite predicate set.

### The fiber algebra $A_p$ and the twist product

Fix a residuated lattice $\mathbf{L}$ and an entity set $E$. For each predicate $p$, the predication fiber algebra at $p$ is the pointwise twist product on the entity-pair index:

$$A_p := \mathbf{L}^{\bowtie\, E \times E}.$$

An element of $A_p$ is a function $E \times E \to L \times L$ assigning a twist-product value $v(s, o) \in \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ at each predication cell $(s, p, o)$ with predicate coordinate fixed. As an abstract algebra, $A_p$ does not depend on $p$ — the subscript records which predicate's values are held.

The twist product $\mathbf{L}_1 \bowtie \mathbf{L}_2$ has carrier $L_1 \times L_2$ with two interleaved orders: the truth order $\leq_t$ raises the first coordinate and lowers the second (positive evidence rises, negative evidence falls); the knowledge order $\leq_k$ raises both. The four binary operations $\wedge_t, \vee_t, \wedge_k, \vee_k$ are defined coordinate-wise on these two orders. When $\mathbf{L}_1 = \mathbf{L}_2 = \mathbf{L}$, the resulting algebra $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ carries a canonical negation $\sim \langle a, b \rangle = \langle b, a \rangle$ exchanging the two coordinates; under this negation $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ is a bilattice. If $\mathbf{L}$ is distributive, so is $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.

Specializing to $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$ recovers $\mathbf{2}^\bowtie \cong \mathbf{FOUR}$, the smallest non-trivial bilattice. Its four values, with the epistemic reading the library assigns them:

- $\mathbf{f} = \langle 0, 1 \rangle$ — *told false*.
- $\bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle$ — *told nothing*.
- $\top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle$ — *told both*.
- $\mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$ — *told true*.

The truth order on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ is generated by $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \top \leq_t \mathbf{t}$; the knowledge order by $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$ and $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t} \leq_k \top$. The negation $\sim$ fixes $\bot$ and $\top$ and swaps $\mathbf{t} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{f}$.

The bilattice operations on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ lift pointwise to $A_p$: $(v \wedge_k v')(s, o) := v(s, o) \wedge_k v'(s, o)$, and analogously for the other three operations and for negation. When $\mathbf{L}$ is distributive, $A_p$ is a distributive bilattice on the entity-pair index.

### The predication fibration $p : \mathcal{E} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{C}$

Fix base parameters $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$ with $\mathbf{L}$ an integral commutative residuated lattice, $\mathcal{C}$ a small predicate category with finite limits, and $E$ an entity set. A predication fibration over $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$ is a cloven Grothendieck fibration $p : \mathcal{E} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with three axioms:

- **(A1) Fiber uniformity.** For every $q \in \mathcal{C}$, the fiber $\mathcal{E}_q := p^{-1}(q)$ is canonically isomorphic to the predication fiber algebra $A_q$, viewed as a thin category via its $\leq_k$-poset.
- **(A2) Generator action.** For each of the four predicate-category generators, the reindexing functor $f^* : \mathcal{E}_p \to \mathcal{E}_q$ for $f : q \to p$ acts on fiber elements by the specified cellwise reindexing of the entity-pair index.
- **(A3) Fiber structure preservation.** For every $f$ in $\mathcal{C}$, $f^*$ is a bilattice-residuated homomorphism: it preserves $\wedge_t, \vee_t, \wedge_k, \vee_k$, negation $\sim$, twist-product fusion $\odot$, and twist-product right residual $\Rightarrow$, pointwise.

Cloven fibrations over $\mathcal{C}$ are exactly pseudo-coalgebras for the Emmenegger lax-idempotent 2-comonad $\mathbb{N}$ on $\mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C}$; the definition above is equivalently *a pseudo-$\mathbb{N}$-coalgebra satisfying (A1)–(A3).* The Grothendieck transpose is the predication fiber functor $F : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{DBiLat}(\mathbf{L})$, the pseudo-functor restricted to land in distributive bilattices.

The full sub-2-category of $\mathbf{Cat}/\mathcal{C}$ on predication fibrations is denoted $\mathbf{PrFib}(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$; it is the ambient 2-category for the closure 2-monad.

### The predication hyperdoctrine $\mathcal{E}$

A predication hyperdoctrine is a predication fibration that additionally satisfies four properties — *not* additional structural data, since Kelly–Lack's property-like theorem rules out independent choice.

- **(B1k) and (B1t) Two parallel quantifier-adjoint systems.** For every $f : q \to p$ in $\mathcal{C}$, the reindexing $f^*$ has both a left and a right adjoint in each of the two enrichments:

$$\exists_f^k \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f^k \qquad \exists_f^t \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f^t.$$

- **(B2k) and (B2t) Frobenius reciprocity for the two meets.** For every $f$, $\alpha \in \mathcal{E}_p$, $\phi \in \mathcal{E}_q$:

$$\exists_f^k(f^*(\alpha) \wedge_k \phi) \cong \alpha \wedge_k \exists_f^k(\phi), \qquad \exists_f^t(f^*(\alpha) \wedge_t \phi) \cong \alpha \wedge_t \exists_f^t(\phi).$$

- **(B3) Beck–Chevalley coherence.** For every pullback square in $\mathcal{C}$ and every $\phi \in \mathcal{E}_B$, the canonical natural transformations $\exists_{p_A}^{k/t}(p_B^*\phi) \to f^*(\exists_g^{k/t}\phi)$ in $\mathcal{E}_A$ are isomorphisms for both adjoint systems. Dually for $\forall$.

- **(B4) Comprehension.** For every $p \in \mathcal{C}$, the extension functor $\mathrm{Ext}^t : \mathcal{C}/p \to \mathcal{E}_p$, $(g : q \to p) \mapsto \exists_g^t(\mathbf{1}_q^t)$, has a right adjoint $\{p : -\}_\mathbf{t} : \mathcal{E}_p \to \mathcal{C}/p$, where $\mathbf{1}_q^t$ is the $\leq_t$-terminal of $\mathcal{E}_q$ (the constant-$\mathbf{t}$ valuation). The analogous right adjoint $\{p : -\}_\top$ for the $\leq_k$-system also exists.

The two parallel adjoint systems — knowledge-order and truth-order — and the two comprehension schemas yield the two derived equality predicates: classical equality from the $\leq_t$-Lawvere image of the constant-$\mathbf{t}$ valuation, paraconsistent equality from the $\leq_k$-Lawvere image of the constant-$\top$ valuation.

Frobenius reciprocity for twist-product fusion $\odot$ *fails* for three of the four generator classes. The criterion is alignment between the connective's absorber and the existential adjoint's extension default: $\wedge_k$'s absorber $\bot$ aligns with specialization's $\exists_f^k$-extension by $\bot$; $\wedge_t$'s absorber $\mathbf{f}$ aligns with $\exists_f^t$-extension by $\mathbf{f}$; $\odot$'s absorber $\mathbf{f}$ does not align with $\bot$. The failure is constructive — explicit counterexamples on $|E| = 2$ confirm it.

### The predication settling $(v_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$

A predication valuation assigns to every predicate $p \in \mathcal{C}$ an element $v(p) \in A_p$ — a function on the entity-pair index taking values in $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.

Fix additionally a settling bound $\lambda$, an ordinal. A predication settling over $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E, \lambda)$ is an ordinal-indexed family $v = (v_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$ of predication valuations satisfying settling monotonicity: for every $\sigma \leq \tau < \lambda$ and every predicate $p$,

$$v_\sigma(p) \leq_k v_\tau(p)$$

pointwise in $A_p$. Knowledge accumulates along $\lambda$; it does not retract.

The settling is the central indexed object of the library. Specific theorems impose three further axioms when they apply: *settling continuity* ($v_\mu(p) = \bigvee_k \{v_\sigma(p) : \sigma < \mu\}$ at limit ordinals — limits are the colimit of preceding stages); *settling quiescence* ($v_\tau = v_{\tau^*}$ for all $\tau \geq \tau^*$ — the trajectory stabilizes at some $\tau^*$); and *closure-drivenness* ($v_{\tau+1}(p) = \mathrm{cl}_p(v_\tau(p) \vee_k u_{\tau+1}(p))$ for an increment sequence $(u_\tau)$ and a closure operator $\mathrm{cl}_p$ on each fiber). A settling satisfying all four is *well-behaved*; relaxations of continuity and quiescence stratify the regime classification.
