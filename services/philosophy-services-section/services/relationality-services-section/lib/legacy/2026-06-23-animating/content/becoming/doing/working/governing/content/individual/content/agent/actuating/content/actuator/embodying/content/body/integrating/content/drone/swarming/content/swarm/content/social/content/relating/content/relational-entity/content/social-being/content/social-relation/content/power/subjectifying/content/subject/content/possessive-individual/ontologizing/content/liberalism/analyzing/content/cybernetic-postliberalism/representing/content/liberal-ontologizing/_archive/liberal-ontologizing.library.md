**Liberal ontologizing** is the foundational operation of liberal thought: it treats the entities of the social world — persons, peoples, properties — as *substances with inherent attributes existing prior to and independently of the relations they participate in*.
It produces a world of pre-political individuals and pre-relational units that *then* enter into the social.
This operation is not a description of how things are; it is a metaphysical posture with the appearance of a neutral default.

In predication-categories terms: liberal ontologizing is the closure rule that treats each entity $e \in E$ as possessing a fixed predicate-fiber $F(e)$ independently of the relational structure of the settling $\mathcal{S}$.
It forecloses the reading of predicates as emerging through settling dynamics, insisting instead that each entity *has* its properties first, and that the settling merely registers what was already there.
The predication-categories substrate makes the error precise: it is the misidentification of a *settled* fiber value ($\mathbf{t}$ or $\mathbf{f}$ at the end of a convergent settling sequence) with an *initial condition* present prior to any settling.

## Structural role

Liberal ontologizing performs three operations simultaneously.

**Substance-priority.** The discourse treats *what something is* as fixed prior to how it relates.
The rights-bearing individual is a rights-bearer before any legal order recognizes them; the property-owner owns before any market transaction.
Relations are subsequent; entities are first.

**Inherent attribution.** Attributes — rights, identities, capacities, dignities — are properties of the substance, not features of the relations the substance participates in.
This makes it coherent to speak of the rights of an isolated person; in the predication-categories reading, it is an attribution that refuses to acknowledge the settling sequence that produced the attributed value.

**Discrete countability.** The world is made of countable, discrete things.
Populations are sums of individuals; markets are aggregates of participants.
Relational fields, gradients, and settling dynamics are collapsed into the entities that can be enumerated.
This is the operation that Bourdieu's field theory, Luhmann's systems theory, and predication-categories each criticize from different directions.

## Enabling role for genring

Liberal ontologizing is the metaphysical prerequisite that makes [genring](genring.md) possible.
Genring — the reduction of a living people to a fixed literary type — requires that peoples be treatable as substance-bearing units with inherent, stable attributes.
If the social settling were the primary ontological category (as it is in the predication-categories substrate), then a people's attributes would be understood as settling-results: historically contingent, contested, and subject to overdetermination.
Liberal ontologizing forecloses this by treating the genre-figure as the entity's inherent type, the settled value as the prior fact.

The link is also operative in the other direction: genring *reinforces* liberal ontologizing by producing subjects who experience their genre-template as their natural substance.
The [ontogenring](ontogenring.md) process installs a being-template that subjects then report as their authentic self — precisely the substance-with-inherent-attributes that liberal ontologizing posits.

## Critical lineage

Macpherson (1962) traced *possessive individualism* — the liberal self-conception in which the person owns themselves and their capacities as property — as the historically specific form this operation takes in Hobbes and Locke.
Wynter (2003) showed how liberal ontologizing is a racial-colonial operation: *Man* — a particular Western, secular, bourgeois figure — becomes the overrepresented stand-in for the human, with everyone outside its template registering as defective instances.
Barad (2007) argued from physics that 'things' are intra-actively constituted, not pre-existing; the predication-categories substrate formalized this precisely — entities and their predicate values co-arise through settling, not prior to it.
Indigenous relational epistemologies (Cajete 2000) hold the relational alternative at the level of cosmology.
