# Untapped predication-category machinery and where it lands in the engineering theory

The math directory carries roughly 340 atomized entities — concepts, objects, morphisms, theorems. The engineering directory under predicated-computing presently grounds about a dozen of those heavily: [bilattice](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice.md), [predication-fiber-algebra](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), [closure-rule-system](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-system.md), [closure-2-monad](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md), [predication-fibration](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fibration.md), and a few more. The other ~320 entities — almost the entire cohomological apparatus, the regime-classification machinery, the Priestley/d-frame chart of the value-domain axis, the modal operators on the bilattice fibers, and several CRDT-convergence variants — appear in **no** engineering note's `depends-on:` chain.

This is not a defect of the engineering theory. It is a frontier inventory: the engineering content the substrate already commits to is mostly grounded; the *next* engineering content the substrate could commit to has its mathematical apparatus already developed and waiting. This narrative surveys what is waiting, and the engineering theorems and objects each piece would naturally land at.

The five families.

## 1. The cohomological apparatus and tampering / repair / inconsistency

The math has a complete cohomological framework over the bitopological settling site: the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-associated-sheaf.md) and its [paraconsistent companion](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md), a short exact sequence decomposing the latter into the truth-side sheaf and the [inconsistency sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/inconsistency-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$, the [bigraded settling cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bigraded-settling-cohomology.md), the [d-frame cohomology comparison theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/d-frame-cohomology-comparison.md), and the [bitopological-settling-site axioms](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bitopological-settling-site-axioms.md).

The substrate already has the engineering shadows of two of these — [inconsistency-sheaf-record](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md) and the [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md) morphism — but the orphaned engineering theorems on tampering, repair, and audit do not yet cite them. The natural landing sites:

- [substrate-tampering-detection](../theorems/substrate-tampering-detection.md) — tampering produces $\top$-cells (cells with both positive and negative evidence); the auditor's task is precisely to interrogate $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$. The cohomology class $H^1(\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)})$ ranks tamper events by their structural signature, distinguishing local corruption (a single $\top$-cell) from coordinated forgery (a non-trivial cohomology class).
- [apply-repair](../morphisms/apply-repair.md) — repair is the engineering-side reduction of an [inconsistency-sheaf-record](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md) towards the zero sheaf. The math's [bilattice negation on d-frame-valued sheaves theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-negation-on-d-frame-valued-sheaves.md) gives the algebraic moves a repair operator can compose without leaving the d-frame.
- Federation conflict ranking — when two substrates federate and disagree, the disagreement is exactly the [bilattice network-sheaf consensus theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md)'s con-failure cells, which the math classifies as $\top$-witnesses in the joint d-frame. The engineering side currently uses [federation-convergence](../theorems/federation-convergence.md); adding the d-frame structure would let the substrate distinguish *cohomologically equivalent* disagreements (genuine paraconsistent evidence) from *cohomologically trivial* ones (transient race conditions).

## 2. The regime classification and substrate behavior typing

The math classifies settlings into four regimes via the [four-regime dichotomy](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md): *terminating-finite*, *terminating-transfinite*, *aperiodic*, and *non-Scott-continuous*. For each regime there is a classifying invariant (the [aperiodic regime classification](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/aperiodic-regime-classification.md), the [continuous-state regime classification](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/continuous-state-regime-classification.md), [step-n cohomological rank](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/continuous-valued-step-n-cohomological-rank.md)), and the [closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits.md) rules out everything outside this classification.

The substrate has a [regime-record](../objects/regime-record.md) object and a [regime-discipline](regime-discipline.md) narrative but no engineering theorem yet asserts what regime a conformant substrate must inhabit. The natural landing sites:

- [boot-determinism](../theorems/boot-determinism.md) — boot is exactly the case where the substrate must converge to a [settling quiescence](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-quiescence.md) point in finite settling time. The closure-monad-no-non-trivial-periodic-orbits theorem is the mathematical fact that boot *can* converge — there are no closure-driven oscillations that would prevent it.
- [leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits](../theories/distributed-coordination-platform/theorems/leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits.md) — already cites the no-periodic-orbits theorem in spirit (per its slug), but a full grounding chain through the four-regime dichotomy would let the distributed-coordination subtheory state which regime its leader-election protocols inhabit.
- A new engineering theorem *substrate-regime-conformance* — every conformant substrate inhabits the terminating-finite or terminating-transfinite regime in steady state; the aperiodic regime is admissible only for streaming-substrate variants whose [settling-bound](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-bound.md) is unbounded by design; the non-Scott-continuous regime is non-conformant. This would tighten what the [regime-record](../objects/regime-record.md) is recording and what conformance tests are checking.

## 3. The Priestley / d-frame / bitopological chart of the value-domain axis

The math has three coordinate charts on the value-domain axis: [bilattices](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice.md), [paired Priestley spaces](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/paired-priestley-space.md), and [d-frames](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/d-frame.md), related by the [bitopological Stone duality theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bitopological-stone-duality.md). The substrate uses the bilattice chart exclusively. The other two charts buy specific engineering content the bilattice chart does not give cleanly:

- The d-frame chart's $\mathrm{con}, \mathrm{tot}$ relations are the algebraic objects the substrate would interrogate to rank consistency and totality of a query result. The [query-language](../teaching/explanations/query-language.md) concept and the [database-platform](../theories/database-platform/_index.md) subtheory's query-planning content are the natural landing sites: a query plan that needs to assert "no totality gap remains" or "this consistency join is now witnessed" is making a d-frame-level claim, not a bilattice-cell claim.
- The Priestley chart is the dual presentation in which the substrate's content can be displayed as a clopen lattice of a paired Priestley space. The [interactive-application-platform](../theories/interactive-application-platform/_index.md) subtheory's view-projection content (documents, lists, forms) is the natural landing site: each view is a clopen extraction from the substrate's paired Priestley dual, and the math's [closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence.md) is the mathematical guarantee that view extraction commutes with closure.

## 4. Modal operators on the bilattice fibers and sessions / scopes / capabilities

The math has [modal-necessity](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/modal-necessity-operator.md) and [modal-possibility](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/modal-possibility-operator.md) operators on the bilattice fibers, plus the [twist-product](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/twist-product.md) and its [right-residual](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/twist-product-right-residual.md) — the algebraic apparatus for asserting "in every accessible world, $p$ holds" and "$p$ holds in some accessible world" against a [bilattice accessibility relation](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice-accessibility-relation.md).

The substrate has [scope](../teaching/explanations/scope.md), [principal-session](../teaching/explanations/principal-session.md), and [scope-as-predication](scope-as-predication.md) (narrative). The accessibility relation is the natural mathematical home of "what content this principal can see in this session" — the modal-necessity operator is the algebraic meaning of "this predication holds across all sessions a principal authorizes" and modal-possibility is "this predication holds in at least one authorized session". The natural landing sites:

- A new engineering theorem *scope-modal-decomposition* — every scope decomposes the substrate's bilattice valuation into a modal-necessity part (what every session in the scope agrees on) and a modal-possibility part (what some session in the scope asserts). This would give the [interactive-application-platform](../theories/interactive-application-platform/_index.md) and [agential-orchestration-platform](../theories/agential-orchestration-platform/_index.md) subtheories a single algebraic object to refer to when speaking of "what the scope says".
- The [identity-by-relations](../teaching/explanations/identity-by-relations.md) concept is the substrate-side of the accessibility relation; tying it explicitly to [bilattice-accessibility-relation](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice-accessibility-relation.md) would let the substrate's identity-management discipline inherit the modal-operator algebra rather than reconstruct it.

## 5. The CRDT-convergence variants and distributed coordination

The math has three convergence theorems: [crdt-convergence](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence.md), [crdt-convergence-via-cohomological-calm](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence-via-cohomological-calm.md), [crdt-convergence-via-mayer-vietoris](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence-via-mayer-vietoris.md), and [crdt-convergence-with-per-observer-rules](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/crdt-convergence-with-per-observer-rules.md), plus the [bilattice network-sheaf consensus theorem](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md) on agent graphs. The substrate's [federation-convergence](../theorems/federation-convergence.md) cites only the bare existence of CRDT convergence; it does not lift the *variant* structure (cohomological calm vs. Mayer–Vietoris vs. per-observer rules) into engineering content.

The natural landing sites are in the [distributed-coordination-platform](../theories/distributed-coordination-platform/_index.md) subtheory:

- The Mayer–Vietoris variant is the local-to-global statement: federation through an arbitrary topology converges if and only if the cover's Mayer–Vietoris sequence vanishes at $\delta^0$. This is what a substrate's federation-graph topology has to satisfy for [federation-convergence](../theorems/federation-convergence.md) to apply non-vacuously — a structural condition on federation topologies the engineering theory currently states only informally.
- The per-observer-rules variant is the engineering reality: different substrates run different closure rules (different versions, different rule-system extensions). The math says convergence holds anyway, provided the rules commute pairwise on shared content. This is the foundation for *rolling-upgrade federation* — substrates running version $n$ and version $n+1$ of the rule system can federate during the upgrade window. No engineering theorem currently asserts this; it would be a natural follow-up to [migrate-rule-system](../morphisms/migrate-rule-system.md).
- The agent-graph consensus theorem is the specific case where the federation topology *is* an agent graph, which lands in the [agential-orchestration-platform](../theories/agential-orchestration-platform/_index.md) subtheory — the math has the theorem, the engineering has the agent graph, and the bridge is not yet stated.

## What this survey is and is not

The survey is an inventory of unused load-bearing math, with the engineering destinations each piece would naturally land at. It is not a project plan and it is not a claim that every piece *should* land — some of these wirings will turn out to require engineering content the substrate is not yet prepared to commit to (e.g., the d-frame chart's full integration would demand a query-language refactor on the database-platform side that may or may not be worth the cost).

The narrative's value is to surface the alignment so that, when an engineering claim wants to be tightened, the math is already there to tighten with rather than approximated locally. The five families above cover the largest concentrations of unused math; smaller pockets exist (the [Klein-four symmetry](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/klein-four-symmetry.md) and its [internal $\mathbb{Z}/2$-gauge](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/internal-z2-gauge.md) realization, the [Husainov acyclicity concept](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/husainov-acyclicity.md) for path-independence claims, the [moduli prestack](../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/settling-moduli-prestack.md) for content-addressed substrate identification under symmetry) but each lands in a smaller engineering region than the five above.

The improvements the [transaction-atomicity](../theorems/transaction-atomicity.md), [boot-determinism](../theorems/boot-determinism.md), [monotonic-clock-discipline](../theorems/monotonic-clock-discipline.md), [streaming-replay-equivalence](../theorems/streaming-replay-equivalence.md), and [substrate-tampering-detection](../theorems/substrate-tampering-detection.md) theorems carry — adding direct math grounding via these families — are the first concrete consumption of this inventory.
