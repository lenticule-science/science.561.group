# Communication

## What this node is

Communication is [language](../../language.md) read at its **applied settle-face**: the point where speech-acts performed by many agents close into a *shared* common ground rather than a single speaker's force. The corpus's own language node names "applied `communication`" as a larger body generated downstream of the spine and not yet derived onto it; this node derives it.

What generates communication is the [speech act](../speech-acts/speech-acts.md) — an utterance given illocutionary force — under repeated multi-agent **settling**. You cannot have communication without first having forced utterances to circulate and accept; communication is what their settlement produces. Reading the path left to right: a mark becomes a token-and-claim utterance (articulate), something is said of it (predicate), force enters (posit, the speech act), and many such forced assertions are accepted, contested, corrected, and glued into a public record (settle) — that record, mutating in time across agents, is communication.

## What it generates

Communication is the base from which applied public-discourse forms unfold: [journalism](content/journalism/journalism.md) (the disciplined public settlement of attributed, time-indexed claims), and beneath it the provenance/warrant invariants that survive settlement and the circulation surfaces that distribute it. Online-community forms, social media, and federated publication are sibling settle-face bodies; as their generators are derived they settle here rather than into topical buckets.

## Why it matters

Placing communication here keeps the corpus's law intact: news, publishing, and public exchange are not a separate "media" subject but the *settle* morphism re-run over forced utterances among many agents. The stable primitive is therefore not "article" or "post" but the attributed, time-indexed claim with provenance and status — exactly the [claim](../../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) the base morphism first brings to stand.

## Orientation

Descend [journalism](content/journalism/journalism.md) for the public settlement of claims into reportable artifacts. Un-derived sibling bodies (social media, online communities, federated publication) remain in interim topical homes until their generators are named.
