Ko in [weiqi](../index.md) is a repetition constraint that forbids a player from making the immediate recapture that would recreate the previous board position [@britgo2025default].

Ko matters because it prevents endless local loops while preserving the tactical possibility of capture.
Once a ko appears, the fight usually stops being purely local: the blocked player must move elsewhere first, which can turn the rest of the board into leverage for the eventual return to the ko point.

## Related terms

- [capture](./capture.md) - Ko arises from a specific kind of capture and attempted recapture.
- [liberty](./liberty.md) - Ko positions usually appear when liberties are reduced to a narrow exchange.
- [atari](./atari.md) - Many ko fights begin from repeated atari and recapture threats.
- [territory](./territory.md) - Ko fights often matter because the threatened capture would change important territory.
