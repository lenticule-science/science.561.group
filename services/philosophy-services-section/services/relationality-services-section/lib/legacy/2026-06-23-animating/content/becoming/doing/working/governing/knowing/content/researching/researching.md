# researching

**Researching** is knowing as governed inquiry: a directed process that takes
unsettled evidence, experience, or testimony and moves it toward claims that can
stabilize, be certified, and then revise the standards by which later work is
judged.

This node is not a bucket for "research about things." It is the proof-tree home
for research as an operation. Read by Curry-Howard directionality, each descent
in this directory adds one conjunct to the type inhabited by the file at the
leaf. Read by Yoneda, a research object is recovered from the arrows that enter
and leave it: the evidence it admits, the claim it targets, the certification it
can bear, and the theory extension it makes possible.

## What generates it

Researching is generated by [knowing](../../knowing.md). Knowing settles claims;
researching is the governed form of inquiry that can change which claims are
settleable. It consumes questions, observations, instruments, methods, and
resources, but none of those alone generate it. What generates researching is the
need to move from an unsettled field to a more informed closure.

## Proof tree

The tree separates two levels that the archive previously braided together:

- [research act](content/research-act/research-act.md): the local arrow from
  experience or evidence into modal claim-closure.
- [researching morphism](content/researching-morphism/researching-morphism.md):
  the theory-level extension `rho : T -> T'` that moves the ideal itself.
- [directed relational research](content/directed-relational-research/directed-relational-research.md):
  the named modality where research acts are directed relational cells and
  certification is idempotent stabilization.
- [study and use](content/study-and-use/study-and-use.md): the peripheral growth
  of applications and refinements that consume the derived machinery without
  changing the central spine.

This is the slow derivation: local research acts stabilize into certified cells;
certified cells compose; a coherent field of certified cells becomes a witness
topos; and enough certified advance can induce a researching morphism of the
host theory. The path order records that proof.

## What it generates

Researching generates settled claims, new questions, new resources, stricter
ideals, and theory extensions. Its characteristic result is not only "more
knowledge"; it is a changed field in which prior resources can reveal new
deficits because the governing theory now asks more of them.
