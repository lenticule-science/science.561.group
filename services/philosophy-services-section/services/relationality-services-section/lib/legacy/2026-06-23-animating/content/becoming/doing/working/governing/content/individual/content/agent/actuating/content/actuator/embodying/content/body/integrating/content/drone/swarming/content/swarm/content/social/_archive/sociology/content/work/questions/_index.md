Subdomain-specific questions live in each subdomain.
What is held here are the questions whose scope crosses subdomains.

## Open structural questions

- **What is the relation between the predicated-work formalism and the Hohfeldian analysis of governance positions?** The [`predicated-work/`](theories/predicated-work/) subtree formalizes work as predication cells; the [`work/theories/governance/theories/kyriarchial-governance/theories/hohfeldian/`](theories/work/theories/governance/theories/kyriarchial-governance/theories/hohfeldian/) subdomain develops a structured analysis of legal positions at the term level.
  The question is whether the Hohfeldian operations (assignment, succession, waiver, ratification, revocation, estoppel) can be lifted to typed morphisms in the predicated-work substrate, and whether characterization theorems can be proved against that substrate.
- **How does the analysis of jurisdiction in [`governance/theories/jurisdiction/`](theories/governance/theories/jurisdiction/) relate to the predicated-governance treatment in [`predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/`](theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/)?** Both are studies of the scope-conditions under which authority is operative.
  The question is whether the predicated-governance formalism subsumes the sociological analysis or whether the two operate at distinct registers that require translation.
- **What is the substantive relation between the military-command substrate the project uses operationally and the broader sociology of command authority?** [`governance/theories/military-command/`](theories/governance/theories/military-command/) develops the substrate the project uses for orchestrated multi-agent work.
  The question is what generalizations from that specific substrate hold for command authority more generally and which are artifacts of the substrate.

## Where these questions are worked

Each question above is partially pursued in the subdomain that touches on it.
The cross-subdomain home for the questions is here.
