## Annotation

The mature theoretical statement of Ranganathan's faceted classification programme. Adds to [@ranganathan1933] the explicit *Five Laws of Library Science*, the *canon of relevant succession*, the *postulates and principles* governing facet formation, and an articulated *plane–verbal–notational* three-plane methodology that separates conceptual analysis from naming from notation.

The three-plane separation is the move the predicated framing inherits most directly: it corresponds to the [predicate-category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) (idea plane, the abstract structure of relations), the [predicate vocabulary](../objects/upper-ontology-as-predicate-vocabulary.md) (verbal plane, the discipline-specific names of predicates), and the [closure-rule language](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-language.md) (notational plane, the syntactic surface used to assert and federate cells). Where Ranganathan keeps the three planes coordinated by editorial judgement, the predicated framing makes the coordination algebraic: the verbal plane is a contravariant functor on the idea plane, and the notational plane is a closure-rule signature over the verbal plane.

The Five Laws — books are for use, every reader his book, every book its reader, save the time of the reader, the library is a growing organism — translate cleanly into the substrate's design constraints: assertions must be reachable (the *epistemic order on the bilattice*), federation must terminate (the [closure-as-fitting-fixpoint](../theorems/closure-as-fitting-fixpoint.md) regime), and the system must support [reified-retraction](teaching/explanations/reified-retraction.md) so it can grow without losing its history.
