## The question

A flatfile agentic resource system (FARS) is a substrate-rooted directory of frontmatter-markdown atoms organized so that each atom bears one role-commitment of the system's underlying theory. The draft claim [`fars-directory-is-predication-categorical-theory`](../claims/fars-directory-is-predication-categorical-theory.md) argues that the eleven directory components listed there are *forced* by what a theory must be (a hyperdoctrine has a signature, structures, transformations, propositions, an open frontier, models — and each is a distinct role).

The argument-from-forcing is not complete. FARS, as a working theory, extends past public categorical-logic literature; the precise hyperdoctrine that grounds the forcing — and the proofs that each commitment lives in exactly one canonical location — have not been written.

So: **which directories are lawful in a FARS?** Which are forced by the underlying theory, which are convention that may be reorganized, and which are misfilings to be corrected?

## What is settled

The 561 Group's own structuration is settled separately, by decision (see [`work/governance/rules/decisions/canonicalize-561-structuration`](../../../work/governance/rules/decisions/canonicalize-561-structuration.md)). The polity does not wait on the theoretical answer to operate. The decision names specific paths as canonical for 561; new paths may be added by ordinary edit, and their canonical status is judged by their consistency with the substrate's role partitioning.

This question concerns the **general** answer — what FARS forces *as a theory* — not 561's particular instance. They will coincide when the theory is fully written; until then, the polity's structuration is authority for 561, and this question is authority for the theory.

## Open sub-questions

- What is the precise predication-hyperdoctrine that FARS instantiates? The claim cites three depended-on atoms (`predication-hyperdoctrine`, `predicated-computer`, `application-sublocale`) that are not yet authored as substrate atoms.
- Is the partition by *role* (work/governance/, code/, prose/, references/, theories/) the only forced top-level partition, or are there alternative role-partitions that also satisfy the hyperdoctrine?
- Which subdirectories under each top-level kind are forced, and which are merely conventional? The current claim asserts forcing on the five top-level kinds but is silent on subdirectories.
- How does sub-FARS recursion (`theories/<sub>/` being itself a FARS) interact with the partition? Is the recursion criterion just "carries its own `_index.md` and satisfies the partition," or is more required?

## Where progress would come from

- Completing the substrate atoms for `predication-hyperdoctrine`, `predicated-computer`, and `application-sublocale`.
- Lifting the claim into the FARS theory under `theories/.../flatfile-agentic-resource-system/` once its formulation stabilizes (the claim's own "Promotion path").
- Examples and counterexamples: directories that *do* satisfy the claimed partition, and proposed directories that don't — to see what the partition admits and excludes.
