To call in [poker](../index.md) is to match the current wager so the player can remain in the [hand](./hand.md) [@britannica2026poker].
Calling neither ends the action nor increases the price for later players.
It accepts the current price of continuation.

Calling matters because poker decisions are comparative.
A player calls when the expected value of continuing at the current price is better than [folding](./fold.md), but not strong enough to justify a [raise](./raise.md).
This relation between hand strength, draw potential, and price is one of poker's central analytical problems [@sklansky1999].

## Related terms

- [fold](./fold.md) - Folding is the alternative to paying the current price to continue.
- [raise](./raise.md) - Raising both continues the hand and changes the price for everyone else.
- [pot](./pot.md) - Calling adds chips to the pot at the current wager size.
