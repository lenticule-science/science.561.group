## V. Genres of Rupture: Controlled Dissensus in Ontogenric Form

Rather than opening toward rupture as liberation, this section identifies how _rupture itself_ is now formatted and integrated.

- **Therapeutic Genres**: trauma recovery, reconciliation commissions, restorative justice

- **Academic and Cultural Genres**: critique, deconstruction, satire, “radical” art

- **Insurgent Genres Captured**: revolution, resistance, reparation as narrative forms

- Rupture is allowed, but only when it loops back into structure—genre of the anomaly

## VII. Non-Ontogenric Logics: Grammars of Otherwise

Not aporetic or speculative—this is an analytic tracing of extant formations that do not operate by ontogenric closure.

- **Recursive relation without resolution**: certain Indigenous metaphysics, mystic practices, and relational cosmologies

- **Incoherence as structure**: languages, rituals, or kin systems that do not arc

- **Forms that do not translate**: relations that cannot be owned or anticipated

- **Anonto-epistemes**: not just different ways of knowing, but knowing that refuses being as genre

This section should stay rigorous—grounded in systems (not dreams) that already exceed genre _without becoming its anomaly_.
