**Viable system model (VSM)** is Stafford Beer's architectural realization of an aperiodic-autopoiesis-cell at organizational scale.
An organization is viable when it satisfies the autopoiesis-conditions through five recursively-nested subsystems.

## The five subsystems

| | Subsystem | Substrate role | Cybernetic function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | Operations | Multiple instances of the autopoiesis-cell's metabolic processes | Carry out the organization's primary activity |
| **S2** | Coordination | Inter-cell σ-compatibility checks | Prevent oscillation between S1 instances |
| **S3** | Control | Internal regulator over S1 + S2 | Maintain operational variety |
| **S4** | Intelligence | Δ-driven observation of environment | Track external perturbations |
| **S5** | Policy | Joint-quiescent layer overseeing all sub-systems | Set the system's identity |

Each subsystem is itself a viable-system-model: an organization is a recursive nesting of viable systems.

## Recursion and viable subsystems

The VSM's recursive structure realizes the substrate's regime-step pattern at the organizational level.
Each subsystem must satisfy the same autopoiesis-conditions as the whole:

- S1 (operations): each operation-unit is itself a small autopoiesis-cell with its own σ, Δ.
- S5 (policy): the policy layer's $H^*$ is the organization's identity-invariant.

When all five subsystems satisfy the substrate's autopoiesis-conditions individually and their interactions satisfy the commutativity-axiom at the organizational level, the organization is viable.

## When VSM fails

A VSM organization fails to be viable when:

- One subsystem lacks [requisite-variety](../../../../../../../library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/variety/library/lattice/library/closure-operator/library/closure-rule-system/library/requisite-variety/requisite-variety.md) against its sub-domain.
- The inter-subsystem coordination (S2) fails: distinct S1 cells produce incompatible $H^*$.
- The policy layer (S5) loses its joint-quiescent character: the organization's identity becomes contested.

In substrate terms: VSM-failure is the collapse of one or more of the four corners of the organization's nuclear-quartet.

## Connection to aperiodic-autopoiesis-cell

VSM is the substrate-instance of [aperiodic-autopoiesis-cell](../../aperiodic-autopoiesis-cell.md) applied to multi-component organizations.
Beer's five subsystems are the operational decomposition of the four-corner structure plus the environmental observation channel — extending the four corners (free, σ-quiescent, Δ-quiescent, joint-quiescent) into a richer organizational typology while preserving the underlying nuclear quartet.
