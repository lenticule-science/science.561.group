# information

The reduction of uncertainty; the substrate a regulating system senses, models, and acts on.
An import candidate from the system module's cybernetics, kept **low** in social.

**The ! resource (freely-reproducible).** In the linear-logical typology (see social-relation), information — and with it knowledge, culture, language — is the **!** ("of course") resource: it can be copied and shared *without depletion*, non-rivalrous, the one thing relating can reuse freely.
That is why it sits low and pervasive: record, signal-memory, telemetricism, and informational-malfunction all descend from it.

The drama of the late-liberal formation is the **enclosure** of this ! commons — surveillance-capital enclosing behavioural data, IP enclosing knowledge, genring enclosing meaning into fixed types — converting the freely-copiable into linear private property (see **enclosure**, under capital).
Its reciprocal defence is **knowledge-sovereignty** (under autonomy).
