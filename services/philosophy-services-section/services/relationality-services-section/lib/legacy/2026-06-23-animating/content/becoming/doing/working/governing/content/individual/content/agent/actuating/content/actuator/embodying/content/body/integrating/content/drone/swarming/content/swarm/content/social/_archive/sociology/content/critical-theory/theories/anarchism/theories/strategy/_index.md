The anarchist analysis of strategy is the analysis that asks: given that the state cannot be captured for liberatory purposes, given that representation and electoral mediation reproduce what they claim to challenge, given that mass organizations historically tend toward the bureaucratic forms they were meant to replace — what does it mean to act effectively?

The subdomain's terms name four of the answers the tradition has worked out. *Direct action* — acting directly on the conditions one wishes to change rather than petitioning some authority to act on one's behalf.
The principle is methodological as much as tactical: it presupposes that representation itself is part of what produces the condition of dependence the political action is meant to refuse. *Dual power* — the building of autonomous infrastructure (mutual-aid networks, popular assemblies, self-defense, alternative production) capable of operating independently of the state, with the long-term project of displacing the state's social functions through capacity rather than through direct overthrow. *Propaganda of the deed* — the historically loaded term for political action whose argument is carried by the act itself: at the late-nineteenth-century pole, individual attacks on representatives of power; at the contemporary pole, the broader practice of acting in public in ways that make the political claim by enacting it. *Refusal* — the strategic recognition that not all effective political action is constructive, and that the refusal to participate (in conscription, in collaboration, in the labor relation, in the platform economy) is itself a political form whose accumulation can shift what the dominant order can do.

The four terms are not a unified strategic doctrine.
They name different positions inside an internal disagreement that has run through the tradition for over a century.
The insurrectionary current ([`insurrectionary-anarchism/`](../insurrectionary-anarchism/)) emphasizes attack and the propaganda of the deed; the social-anarchist current emphasizes dual power and prefigurative organization ([`organization/`](../organization/)); the anarcho-nihilist current ([`anarcho-nihilism/`](../anarcho-nihilism/)) emphasizes refusal.
The subdomain holds the analytical work that lets these positions argue with each other.
