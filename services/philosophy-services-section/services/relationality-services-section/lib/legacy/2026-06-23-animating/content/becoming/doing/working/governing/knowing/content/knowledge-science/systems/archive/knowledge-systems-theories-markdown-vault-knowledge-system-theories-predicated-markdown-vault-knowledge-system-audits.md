## Audit conventions

Audits SHOULD use `type: audit` (or `type: triage` for older audits authored before the audit type was named) with a `class:` slug indicating what they audit. Audits MUST cite the discipline they are auditing against; the discipline lives in the theory's `specifications/` and `narratives/` directories, not in the audit itself.

A library-wide audit (one that walks `theories/**`) is a different discipline from a within-theory audit; the [rubric-application-2026-05-03](rubric-application-2026-05-03.md) is the library-wide kind, the others are within-theory.
