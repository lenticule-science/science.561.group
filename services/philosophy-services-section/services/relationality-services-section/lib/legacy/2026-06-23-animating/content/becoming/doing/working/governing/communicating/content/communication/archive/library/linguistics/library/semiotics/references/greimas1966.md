## Annotation

Greimas's *Sémantique structurale* is the founding document of structuralist semantics in the Saussurean lineage and the most influential exposition of paradigmatic analysis after Hjelmslev. For this theory's framing, two Greimasian threads are load-bearing:

1. **Semantic square.** A four-position structure on a binary semantic axis, with the positions related by *contrariety* (the two original poles), *contradiction* (each pole's logical denial), and *implication* (each contradictory implies the other pole's contrary). The square's structural completeness is the proof: no further positions can be added on a single axis without redundancy. This grounds the [paradigm](teaching/explanations/paradigm.md) concept's substantive operationalisation.
2. **Generative trajectory.** Subsequent Greimasian work (extended in *Du sens* 1970 and *Sémiotique et sciences sociales* 1976) developed a *parcours génératif* organising sign-content from deep semantic structures up through narrative and discursive surface. The trajectory is a paradigmatic-to-syntagmatic generative chain; informs the discipline's commitment to layered structural analysis.
