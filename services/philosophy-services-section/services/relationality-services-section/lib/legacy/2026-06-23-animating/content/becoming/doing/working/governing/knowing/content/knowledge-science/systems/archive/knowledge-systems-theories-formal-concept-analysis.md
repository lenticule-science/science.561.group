The named theory of knowledge systems whose substrate is the **formal context** $(G, M, I)$ — a triple of an object set, an attribute set, and an incidence relation — and whose primary structural invariant is the **concept lattice** extracted by the Galois connection $({-})' \dashv ({-})'$ between $\mathcal{P}(G)$ and $\mathcal{P}(M)$.

The companion sibling [`predicated-knowledge-systems/`](../predicated-knowledge-systems/) reads FCA as a degenerate sub-substrate of the predication-cell framework (per its [concept-lattice-as-degenerate-fiber](../predicated-knowledge-systems/objects/concept-lattice-as-degenerate-fiber.md) object). This subtheory does not duplicate that reading; it carries the FCA-internal structural development — Galois connections, the basic theorem, attribute exploration, fuzzy/many-valued generalisations, the lattice operations — at its own depth, with cross-links into the predicated framing where the two meet.
