PyFARS is an enterprise `(C, Γ, O)` in the sense of PredWork's `enterprise` definition: a temporally open, aim-generative organized activity whose pursuit of current aims continuously generates new aims.

## Hard Core C

The five constitutive commitments in the charter's rules tier.
Amending any requires novation.
The `hard-core:` slugs above are placeholder identifiers; formal commitment records should be written as part of `rewrite-pyfars-charter-as-enterprise-founding-document` — each slug becomes a proper `type: concept` or `type: commitment` record that the charter's rules tier references.

## Generative Mechanism Γ

The theory-derivation loop (`theory-derivation-loop`).
The closure rule: new material in predication-categories → derivation aims → engineering aims → implementation aims.
The aim-set refills on each cycle; the enterprise cannot reach a fixed point as long as the mathematical research program is active.

Γ is in the Scott-continuous regime: each cycle's new aim-set depends on the immediately prior theory state (Markov aggregation — not full history, not limit-aggregating).
This places the enterprise at the boundary between strict and Scott-continuous settling; it will migrate to transfinite-bounded if the theory produces limit-aggregating results (e.g., cross-generational deepened understanding of what predication means for computation).

## Operative Structure O

Six offices comprise O.
Three govern the enterprise; three run it:

**Governing offices** (presiding over C, Γ, O): - `pyfars-steward` — guards C; presides over novation; installs and revokes personnel. - `pyfars-executive-officer` — operational interface; dispatch; watch management. - `pyfars-theory-officer` — audits Γ soundness; reports degeneration signals.

**Functional offices** (doing the work): - `pyfars-records-officer` — creates and maintains governance/ and work/ records. - `theory-derivation-officer` — runs the theory-derivation loop Phases 1–5. - `pyfars-developer` — implements code from FARS spec and dev docs.

## Relation to the Polity and Ship

RSC Quiescent is the ship.
The 561 Group is the polity.
PyFARS is an enterprise operating aboard the ship under the polity's authority.
The steward holds PyFARS on behalf of the polity; the captain (emsenn) is the immediate authority above the steward in the chain.

The ship carries a *function* (Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty) — aim-open and cross-generational.
PyFARS is an enterprise in service of that function: it provides the computational substrate through which the polity does its work.
PyFARS's Γ generates new engineering aims; the ship's function generates new mission aims.
The two aim-generation mechanisms are nested, not competing.

## Degeneration Indicators

The theory names three degeneration modes.
Their PyFARS manifestations:

- **Exhaustion of positive heuristic**: the theory-derivation loop stops generating new predicated- computing entities — predication-categories has been "worked through" at the current level of formalization.
  Substrate signal: `auditing-theory-derivation-coverage` returns empty repeatedly.
- **Institutional corruption**: the operative structure O comes to dominate; work shifts from building the predication substrate (internal goods) to maintaining the tooling and harness scaffolding (external goods).
  Substrate signal: tasks in `work/tasks/engineering/` overwhelmingly concern harness maintenance rather than substrate capability.
- **Closure of domain**: genuine completion.
  Rare and unlikely — the predication-categories theory is research-active and the domain is open.

The pyfars-theory-officer monitors for these signals and reports to the steward.
