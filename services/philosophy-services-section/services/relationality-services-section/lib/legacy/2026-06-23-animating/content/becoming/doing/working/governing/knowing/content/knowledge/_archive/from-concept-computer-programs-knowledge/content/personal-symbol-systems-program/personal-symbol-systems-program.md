This program models personal symbol systems as recognitions to relate them to
other domains.

## Behaviour

- Represent symbols, associations, and narratives as recognitions with
  provenance.
- Link symbols to experiences, dreams, and practices; map how they shift over
  time.
- Measure recurrence, salience, and transformation.

## Intended operators

- Capture and relate personal symbols to other recognitions.
- Trace evolution of symbols across contexts.
- Use symbol mappings to inform interpretation or generation tasks.
