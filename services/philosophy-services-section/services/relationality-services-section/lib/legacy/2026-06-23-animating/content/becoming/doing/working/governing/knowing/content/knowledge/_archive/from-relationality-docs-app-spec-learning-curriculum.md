# Learning & Curriculum Graph

Model concepts, skills, and resources as recognitions with prerequisite
topology and mastery measurement.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ConceptRecognition`: definition, examples, relations.
- `SkillRecognition`: practical abilities, linked to concepts.
- `ResourceRecognition`: books, videos, exercises, notes.
- `AssessmentRecognition`: quizzes, projects, reflections.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - prerequisite graph (what you need before tackling something),
  - thematic clusters, learning paths.
- Measurement:
  - mastery estimates, spaced repetition scores, practice frequency.

## 3. Runbooks

- Adaptive study plans:
  - `plan_study_session`, `review`, `test`, `update_mastery`.
- Views:
  - curriculum map, personalized learning path, “next best thing to learn”.
