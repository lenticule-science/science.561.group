An ***essay*** is (definition:: an [artifact](artifact) that results from [writing](writing), usually a few hundred to several thousand [word|words](word|words), usually focused on one or just a few [topic|topics](topic|topics).)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:

```dataview
  file.name = "reflective essay"
```


- [expository essay|Expository essays](expository essay|Expository essays) are for [informing](informing) people about a [concept](concept) or [topic](topic).
- [descriptive essay|Descriptive essays](descriptive essay|Descriptive essays) are for [describing](describing) a [thing](thing), often focusing on one [quality](quality) or set of qualities.
- [narrative essay|Narrative essays](narrative essay|Narrative essays) are for [narrative storytelling](narrative storytelling).
- [argumentative essay|Argumentative essays](argumentative essay|Argumentative essays) are for [arguing](arguing) a [truth](truth) about a [thing](thing).
- [Analytical essay|Analytical essays](Analytical essay|Analytical essays) are for [../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating](../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating) a [concept](concept) or [topic](topic) to [event|events](event|events) or [information](information).
- [comparative essay|Comparative essays](comparative essay|Comparative essays) are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [thing|things](thing|things).
- [critical essay|Critical essays](critical essay|Critical essays) apply a [concept](concept) or [synthesis](synthesis) of concepts to events or information.
- [reflective essay|Reflective essays](reflective essay|Reflective essays) explore my [relationship](relationship) to a [thing](thing).
