# Concepts

The shared semantic vocabulary of the knowledge-systems theory — load-bearing anchors that subtheories ([predicated-knowledge-systems](theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/), [markdown-vault-knowledge-system](theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/), [knowing-semioverse](theories/knowing-semioverse/)) cite by `depends-on:` rather than redefine.

## Current concepts

These three were promoted from the parent's [`terms/`](../terms/) directory under the [term-to-typed-migration](theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/theories/predicated-markdown-vault-knowledge-system/morphisms/term-to-typed-migration.md) discipline. The historical glossary entries remain in `terms/` as redirect stubs; the typed concepts live here.

- [domain-analysis](domain-analysis.md) — Hjørland & Albrechtsen's framework for studying knowledge domains as discourse communities with shared epistemological commitments.
- [faceted-classification](faceted-classification.md) — Ranganathan's PMEST decomposition of any subject into orthogonal facets that combine to express compound subjects.
- [integrative-levels](integrative-levels.md) — Needham/Feibleman/Foskett's stratification of order in a scale of complexity, where each level organizes the level below and adds emergent qualities.

These are *historical* anchors: they name positions taken by the 20th-century knowledge-organization literature that the [predicated-knowledge-systems](theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/) subtheory recasts in substrate-level terms (domain-as-fiber, facets-as-orthogonal-sub-fibrations, integrative-levels-as-predicate-stratification). Citing the concepts here keeps the historical lineage one citation away from the substrate readings.

## What is *not* here

- The substrate-level concepts ([discourse-community-as-fiber](theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/discourse-community-as-fiber.md), [predicate-category-stratification](theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/concepts/predicate-category-stratification.md), etc.) live with the [predicated-knowledge-systems](theories/predicated-knowledge-systems/) subtheory that introduces them. The promoted concepts here cite those by `see-also:` for the substrate translation.
- The vault-shape concepts ([vault](theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/concepts/vault.md), [filename-as-identifier](theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/concepts/filenames-are-stable-identifiers.md), etc.) live with the [markdown-vault-knowledge-system](theories/markdown-vault-knowledge-system/) subtheory.

The grandparent `concepts/` directory is reserved for the *cross-subtheory* shared vocabulary — concepts that all the knowledge-systems subtheories cite, not concepts owned by any one of them.
