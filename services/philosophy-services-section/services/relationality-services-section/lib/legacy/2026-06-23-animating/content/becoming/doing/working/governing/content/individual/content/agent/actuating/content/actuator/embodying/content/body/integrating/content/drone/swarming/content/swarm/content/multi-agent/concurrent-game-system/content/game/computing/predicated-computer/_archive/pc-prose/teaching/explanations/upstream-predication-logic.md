# Upstream stream: predication / logic

This narrative situates the predicated-computing engineering theory against the upstream stream of *predication-and-logic engineering* — the published lineage of fibred categorical logic, paraconsistent and bilattice-based reasoning, recursive-query languages, knowledge-graph engineering, and situation-calculus action representation. The math foundation rooted at the [predication cell](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predication-cell.md) cites this stream extensively from the math side; the engineering corpus inherits and extends it through specific commitments.

## What the stream consists of

Five sub-currents that the engineering theory stands on:

- **Fibred categorical logic.** [Jacobs (1999)](../references/jacobs1999.md) is the canonical textbook; the substrate's hyperdoctrinal apparatus — fiber algebras over a predicate category, reindexing functors as substitution, adjoints to substitution as quantification, Beck–Chevalley as substitution-quantifier coherence — is exactly what the textbook formalizes. The substrate's [predication fiber algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), [extend-restrict-adjoint](../teaching/explanations/extend-restrict-adjoint.md) discipline, and [join-reordering-via-beck-chevalley](../theorems/join-reordering-via-beck-chevalley.md) are direct engineering applications.
- **Bilattice-based knowledge representation.** [Belnap (1977)](../references/belnap1977.md) introduces the four-valued logic ($\bot$, $\mathbf{t}$, $\mathbf{f}$, $\top$); [Ginsberg (1988)](../references/ginsberg1988.md) generalizes to arbitrary bilattices as a uniform AI-inference framework; [Fitting (2006)](../references/fitting2006.md) develops bilattice-fixed-point semantics for logic programs. The substrate's [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](../theorems/paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md) and [bilattice-operation-table](../teaching/explanations/bilattice-operation-table.md) operationalize these claims at the substrate level, with the [adjoint-structure-spo-bilattice](../references/adjoint-structure-spo-bilattice.md) text supplying the residuation that makes the bilattice domain compose with substitution.
- **Recursive-query / Datalog.** [Abiteboul–Hull–Vianu (1995)](../references/abiteboul-hull-vianu1995.md) is the textbook foundation; the modern recursive-query renaissance ([Hellerstein–Alvaro 2020 on Bloom/Dedalus](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/references/hellerstein-alvaro2020.md), already cited on the math side) extends it. The substrate's [closure-rule system](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-system.md) is structurally a generalization of stratified Datalog over a bilattice value-domain rather than $\mathbf{2}$; the [recursive-query-closure](../teaching/explanations/recursive-query-closure.md) concept names the lift.
- **Provenance and knowledge-graph engineering.** [Green–Karvounarakis–Tannen (2007)](../references/green-et-al2007.md) introduce provenance semirings as the algebraic framework for tracking *why* a derived tuple holds; [Hogan et al. (2021)](../references/hogan-et-al2021.md) supply the canonical knowledge-graph survey; the W3C [RDF 1.1](../references/w3c-rdf11.md) and [SPARQL 1.1](../references/w3c-sparql11.md) specifications anchor the wire-format and query-language register. The [provenance-semiring-witness](../teaching/explanations/provenance-semiring-witness.md) concept names the relationship between the semiring framework and the substrate's [witness](../teaching/explanations/witness.md) chain.
- **Situation calculus / action representation.** [McCarthy–Hayes (1969)](../references/mccarthy-hayes1969.md) introduces the situation calculus and the frame, qualification, and ramification problems; [Reiter (2001)](../references/reiter2001.md) develops the mature treatment with successor-state axioms and the GOLOG family of agent programming languages. The substrate's [event log](../objects/event-log.md) is structurally the situation sequence; the substrate's [closure rules](../teaching/explanations/closure-rule.md) are structurally Reiter's successor-state axioms. The synthesis-bridge stream picks up GOLOG/ConGolog/IndiGolog at the AOP layer; the foundational situation-calculus references live here on the engineering side because the substrate-level commitment (event log = state) is the load-bearing commitment.

## What the substrate borrows

- **The fibred-categorical apparatus.** The substrate's storage and operator semantics are structured *as* a hyperdoctrine (per the math parent's [predication hyperdoctrine](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-hyperdoctrine.md)). Without Jacobs's framework, the substrate's claims about substitution coherence (Beck–Chevalley), quantifier-as-adjoint (existential closure operators), and predicate pushdown (Frobenius reciprocity) would have no precise statement.
- **The bilattice value-domain.** The substrate's commitment to four-valued cells at the storage layer — and to the knowledge order $\leq_k$ as the convergence-direction — is Belnap's, Ginsberg's, and Fitting's commitment lifted from logic programming to the systems-engineering register.
- **The Datalog fixed-point computation.** The substrate's settling discipline is the bilattice-valued analog of stratified Datalog's least-fixed-point evaluation. Closure-rule application is rule firing; quiescence is the fixed point; the substrate's locality bound is Datalog's bounded-recursion-depth analog.
- **The provenance-semiring algebra.** The substrate's witness chain composes under closure-rule application the way how-provenance polynomials compose under join — the algebraic shape Green–Karvounarakis–Tannen identify is the shape the substrate's witness algebra obeys.
- **The situation-as-state commitment.** The substrate's event log is the situation sequence; recovery from the log is regression in the situation calculus. McCarthy and Hayes named the data structure; Reiter fleshed it out with successor-state axioms; the substrate runs on it.

## Where the substrate diverges

- **Bilattice as substrate primitive, not specialized semantics.** Belnap, Ginsberg, and Fitting use the bilattice as the value domain for *one kind of system* (paraconsistent logic, AI inference, logic programs). The substrate uses it as the value domain for *all* substrate primitives — storage, replication, dispatch, governance, federation. The bilattice is not a specialized semantics for one application; it is the substrate's universal value-domain.
- **Closure-rule system as engineering surface, not just inference engine.** Datalog is a query language; its rules denote queries to evaluate. The substrate's closure rules are a *substrate-content first-class entity* — every rule is a predication-graph entity, editable through substrate operations, witnessed through the event log. Per [self-representation](../teaching/explanations/self-representation.md), the inference engine is in the substrate, not over it. Datalog has no analog of this.
- **RDF/OWL as projection, not source.** The W3C RDF/OWL specifications take the open-world two-valued model; the substrate carries four-valued predications and projects to RDF when needed. The substrate's storage is *strictly more informative* than the RDF view — contested cells become $\top$ in the substrate, but a SPARQL projection has to choose a truth-order policy at the boundary. The substrate is upstream of RDF, not a triple store with a SPARQL frontend.
- **Provenance as substrate primitive, not annotation layer.** Green–Karvounarakis–Tannen treat provenance as an *annotation* added to relations to enable a separate question ("why is this tuple here?"). The substrate makes provenance *substrate content* — every predication is born with its witness, and the question "why is this predication here?" is just a substrate traversal. There is no separate provenance store to maintain.
- **Frame problem dissolved at the storage layer.** Reiter's successor-state axioms solve the frame problem for systems whose state representation is "the current value of every fluent". The substrate solves it differently: the state representation is the event log itself, and the closure of the log gives the current state. There are no frame axioms to write because there is no parallel state representation to keep consistent.

## What the stream lacks that the substrate supplies

The published stream has each of the five elements separately, but no published work in the stream commits to all five *at the substrate level simultaneously*. Datalog systems use $\mathbf{2}$, not bilattices. Bilattice logic-programming systems don't make the rule system substrate content. Knowledge-graph systems use external provenance layers. Situation-calculus implementations don't run over a bilattice domain. The substrate's engineering content is the simultaneous commitment to all five — a fibred bilattice-valued recursive-query store with first-class provenance, where the rule system itself is substrate content.

The [composite-theory positioning narrative](../theories/agential-orchestration-platform/narratives/predicated-agentic-computing.md) returns to this point at the agential-platform level: the predication-and-logic stream supplies the substrate; the agential-platform commitment supplies the agents-on-the-substrate. The two halves together compose into "predicated agentic computing".
