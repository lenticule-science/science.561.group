Research dispatches are creating detailed findings in operations/engineering/records/sessions/research/ — but these contain general claims that belong in the library as public texts. The library is published on the web; operations records are internal.

**Why:** Policy 006 (Research Produces Texts) requires research output to be "citable texts with provenance — files in the library with proper frontmatter, citations, and sources." Session logs are not citable texts. The general insights (e.g., "the partition function IS the trial balance," "cells are models," "the holographic boundary principle applies to user accounts") are true beyond our specific system and should be accessible to anyone reading the library.

**How to apply:** After a research crew member completes:
1. Read their session output
2. Extract general claims that are true independently of our specific system
3. Write those claims as library texts with proper frontmatter, citations, and domain placement
4. The session log remains as the operations record of how the claim was discovered
5. The library text is the public-facing, general, citable version

Example: "Double-entry accounting instantiates the detailed balance condition from Markov chain theory" is a general claim. It belongs in library/theories/engineering/theories/domesticity/theories/bookkeeping/texts/ as a text, not just in a session log.
