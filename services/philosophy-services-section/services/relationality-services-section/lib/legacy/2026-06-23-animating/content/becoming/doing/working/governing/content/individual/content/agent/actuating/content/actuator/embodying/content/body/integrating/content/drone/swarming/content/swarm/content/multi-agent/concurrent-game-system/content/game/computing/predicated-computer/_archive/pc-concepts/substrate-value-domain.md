# Substrate value-domain

The algebraic structure that the substrate's predications carry as their *values* — the underlying [bilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice.md) whose elements appear in every predication's value slot, whose operations the substrate's algebra is built on. In the math reformulation, this is the [predication fiber algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_p$ at each [predicate](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predicate.md) $p$ — the pointwise [twist product](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/twist-product.md) on the entity-pair index. The substrate's value-domain is not an implementation detail; it is what determines what claims can be expressed, how partial information composes, how contradictions surface, and what convergence properties the substrate's [merge](../morphisms/merge.md) and [federation](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/concepts/federation.md) inherit.

## Structural commitments

A substrate's value-domain is fixed by four properties:

- **A set of values.** The discrete elements that may appear in a predication's value slot. The corpus's central case is the [four-valued bilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md) $\mathbf{FOUR} = \{\bot, \mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \top\}$ (per [@adjoint-structure-spo-bilattice]): $\bot$ for "no information," $\mathbf{t}$ for "told true," $\mathbf{f}$ for "told false," $\top$ for "told both" (contested). The four values arise as the [twist product](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/twist-product.md) of two Booleans tracking [evidence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/evidence.md): the first coordinate carries positive evidence, the second negative. Other value-domains the substrate may use are obtained by varying the [residuated lattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/residuated-lattice.md) $\mathbf{L}$ that the twist product is taken over: weighted lattices for confidence-weighted predications, more general $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ for fine-grained epistemic states.
- **A pair of orders.** The [truth order](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/truth-order.md) $\leq_t$ (orders by claim-strength: raises positive evidence and lowers negative — $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ in the four-valued case) and the [knowledge order](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md) $\leq_k$ (orders by information-content: raises both — $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$ in the four-valued case). The substrate's [closure rules](closure-rule.md) advance values monotonically up the knowledge order (per [settling monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md)); the substrate's [wavefront-termination](../theorems/wavefront-termination.md) bound depends on the orders being upper-bounded.
- **A residuated pair $\odot \dashv \Rightarrow$.** An *extending* operation — realized as [twist-product fusion](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/twist-product-fusion.md) $\odot$ on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ — that composes two values' evidence, and a *restricting* operation — realized as [twist-product right residual](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/twist-product-right-residual.md) $\Rightarrow$ — that tests one value's claim against another. The pair satisfies the Lambek adjunction $a \odot p \leq_t q$ iff $p \leq_t (a \Rightarrow q)$ — verified exhaustively for the four-valued case in [@adjoint-structure-spo-bilattice] (Theorem 1). The pair is the substrate's mechanism for *composition*: predications combine through $\odot$, predications test through $\Rightarrow$.
- **A commutative-associative-idempotent join.** The substrate's value-domain admits a join (the truth-order's join, the knowledge-order's join, or both) whose properties make the substrate's [merge](../morphisms/merge.md) operator a CRDT-style operation. The properties are what make [federation-convergence](../theorems/federation-convergence.md) operative.

## What the value-domain is not

- It is not a *type system*. The value-domain is a single lattice (or pair of lattices); every predication's value slot is in the same domain. There is no per-predicate choice of value-domain. Domain-specific structure is captured in the substrate's predicate vocabulary and shapes, not in per-predicate value-domains.
- It is not *Boolean*. A two-valued $\{t, f\}$ Boolean lattice does not admit the partial-information state $\bot$ or the contested state $\top$; the substrate's response to multi-source conflict is structurally different from Boolean logic's "one of them must be wrong."
- It is not the *value type* of an external system. The substrate's value-domain is the substrate's algebra; bytes, strings, numbers, and other concrete payload types live in the predication's *object* slot, not in the value slot. The object slot is the witnessing configuration; the value slot is the algebraic value.

## Engineering consequences

- **Composition and conflict are first-class.** A substrate that needs to combine two parties' claims about the same predication (in [federation](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/concepts/federation.md), in [replication](replication.md), in multi-author editing) does so through the value-domain's join. Agreement produces $t$ at $t \vee t$; disagreement produces $\top$ at $t \vee f$; absence-of-claim produces no change. The substrate's response to conflict is the value $\top$ (contested), not a runtime exception.
- **Closure rules' monotonicity is structural.** Per [closure-rule](closure-rule.md) and [wavefront](wavefront.md), each rule application monotonically advances some predication's value up the relevant order. The value-domain's bounded order is what makes settlement terminate; the value-domain's join is what makes wavefront cascade order-independent.
- **Per-predication transitions are bounded.** For the four-valued bilattice, [@adjoint-structure-spo-bilattice] Proposition 8 establishes that orbits under iterated left-$\odot$ or left-$\Rightarrow$ reach fixed points in at most 2 steps. This bounds wavefront cost and underwrites [wavefront-termination](../theorems/wavefront-termination.md).
- **Contradiction surfaces don't crash.** A substrate operation that produces a contested value $\top$ does not raise an exception; the value $\top$ is admissible in the substrate's algebra. The substrate's [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md) discipline handles $\top$-valued predications through declared repairs.

## What replacing the value-domain costs

The value-domain is not a swap-out detail. Changing the substrate's value-domain — replacing the four-valued bilattice with a different lattice — affects every closure rule, every shape, every operator specification, every dispatch composition. The substrate's algebra is parameterized by the value-domain; a new value-domain produces a different substrate, even if the surface vocabulary is unchanged. Particular platform realizations are typically built around one value-domain and stay there.

A substrate that admits multiple value-domains simultaneously (a "multi-sorted" substrate) is a research direction more than a stable engineering pattern; the math-program work for such substrates is partial (per [hyperversal-meta-operators](questions/hyperversal-meta-operators.md)).

## Position in the substrate

The substrate value-domain is the substrate's *algebraic foundation*. Every other discipline — [closure rules](closure-rule.md), [witness](witness.md) chain, [merge](../morphisms/merge.md), [federation](theories/agential-orchestration-platform/concepts/federation.md), [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md), [hash-discipline](hash-discipline.md), [interfacer](interfacer.md) round-trips — operates over the value-domain's elements and depends on the value-domain's algebraic properties.

The pattern composes with [identity-by-relations](identity-by-relations.md) (the value-domain's elements are themselves predication-graph entities in some realizations, where the substrate's self-representation reaches into its own algebra), with [canonical-form](canonical-form.md) (canonical-form serializes value-domain elements to bytes deterministically), and with [hash-discipline](hash-discipline.md) (hashes are computed over canonical-form-serialized value-domain content).
