**Research iteration** is repeated composition of research acts. In the directed
relational notes, if `r : X -> C Y`, then an iterate is formed by applying the
modal pull-push law again, so the next act does not leave the dynamic/topological
closure behind.

Iteration matters because researching is not a single assertion. It is the
movement by which an inquiry explains more of its input, exposes residual
defect, and either stabilizes or shows where the governing theory is still too
weak.

## What generates it

Research iteration is generated by a research act that remains open under its own
closure. If applying the modal closure still changes the act, the act has not
yet certified itself and must be read dynamically.

## What it generates

Research iteration generates certification when the residual vanishes:

```text
C r ~= r
```

In archive language, the act is then on-shell. In knowing-language, the inquiry
has reached a fixed point under the evidence and closure currently governing it.
