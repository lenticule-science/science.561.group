Treat sequences, variants, annotations, and experiments as recognitions, linked
to health and lab domains.

## 1. Recognitions

- `SequenceRecognition`: DNA/RNA/protein sequences.
- `VariantRecognition`: SNPs, indels, structural variants.
- `AnnotationRecognition`: functional annotations, domains, motifs.
- `OmicsExperimentRecognition`: transcriptomics, proteomics, etc.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - gene networks, pathways, regulatory relationships.
- Measurement:
  - expression levels, effect sizes, confidence scores.

This ties into health metrics, methods from papers, and experiments in your lab
notebook graph.
