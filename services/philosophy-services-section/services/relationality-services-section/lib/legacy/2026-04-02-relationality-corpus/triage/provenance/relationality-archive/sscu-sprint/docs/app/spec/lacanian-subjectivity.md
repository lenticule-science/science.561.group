Represent aspects of Lacanian theory so subjectivity, desire, and “lack” can be
talked about in the relational graph without flattening them.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `RegisterRecognition`
  - the three registers as lenses on a slice:
    - Symbolic (language, law, structure),
    - Imaginary (images, identifications),
    - Real (what resists symbolization).

- `SubjectPositionRecognition`
  - positions like:
    - subject of enunciation vs subject of enounced,
    - “I who speak” vs “I that is spoken about.”
  - linked to texts, dreams, interactions.

- `LackRecognition`
  - structural absences/impossibilities:
    - what cannot be said/owned/filled in a given discourse or self‑story.

- `DesireChainRecognition`
  - chains of desire and drive:
    - objects, others, ideals pursued across recognitions and time.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - relations between registers for a given situation,
  - links from desire chains to events, symptoms, and choices.

- Measurement:
  - recurrence and intensity of certain lacks/desires,
  - shifts in subject positions in different contexts (work, MUD, therapy).

## 3. Runbooks and uses

- `analyze_subject_positions(text_or_session)`: annotate positions in a
  narrative or conversation.
- `trace_desire_chain(subject_slice)`: follow recurring objects/ideals.
- `identify_lack(slice)`: mark what seems structurally excluded or impossible.

These structures tie into dreams, inner work, interpersonal communication,
symbol systems, and how agents choose to speak back to you.
