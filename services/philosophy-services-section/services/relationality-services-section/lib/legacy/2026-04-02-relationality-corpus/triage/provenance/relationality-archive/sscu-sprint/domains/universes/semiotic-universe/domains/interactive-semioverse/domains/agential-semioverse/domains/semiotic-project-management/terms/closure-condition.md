A **closure condition** is a description of a state where specific
gaps in the
[predicate graph](../../../agential-semioverse-repository/predicate-graph.md)
are resolved.
[Goals](../../../agential-semioverse-repository/plans/goals/index.md)
are named closure conditions.

Closure conditions are not imposed by external stakeholders. They
arise from the system's own unsatisfied closures: when the predicate
graph reports axiom violations, those violations generate
[closure pressure](../../../agential-semioverse-repository/concepts/closure-pressure.md),
and the pressure produces closure conditions.

A closure condition is met when its constituent axioms are satisfied
with evidence. The
[satisfaction deficit](./satisfaction-deficit.md) measures how far
the system is from meeting all closure conditions.
