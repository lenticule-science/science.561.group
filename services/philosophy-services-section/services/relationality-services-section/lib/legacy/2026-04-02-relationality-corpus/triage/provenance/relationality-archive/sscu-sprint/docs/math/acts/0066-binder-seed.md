## Interpretation

*Recognize Context* shows contexts carry names, but we still lack the abstract binder sourced from relationlessness.  Letting *Contextualize Trace* witness *Nothing* produces the ***Binder Seed*** ([BinderSeed](0066-binder-seed.md)).

Both recognitions exist, so their composition records the relationless binder, establishing *Binder Seed*.
