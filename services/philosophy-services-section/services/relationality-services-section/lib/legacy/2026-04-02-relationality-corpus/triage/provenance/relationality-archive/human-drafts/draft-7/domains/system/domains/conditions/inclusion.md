- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: [inclusion](inclusion) is the [condition](condition) brought about by the [../acts/act](../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including). It specifies that a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is held within what is, marking it as present within the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating.

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](triage/thing):
    - Be included in what it is (producing [inclusion](inclusion));
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [exclusion](exclusion)).

- The [../acts/act](../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) establishes the [condition](condition) of [inclusion](inclusion):
    - This [condition](condition) holds the [triage/thing](triage/thing) **inside** the field of what is;
    - [inclusion](inclusion) is not a passive state but an **actively maintained presence**;
    - It marks and holds the [triage/thing](triage/thing) as part of the relational field.

- [inclusion](inclusion) is transient unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure):
    - As long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) is active, [inclusion](inclusion) persists;
    - To sustain it durably, [inclusion](inclusion) must be stabilized within a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) or other [../structures/structure](../structures/structure).

- [inclusion](inclusion) gains its coherence by being held together with [exclusion](exclusion):
    - This mutual holding induces [tension](tension);
    - [tension](tension) ensures that [inclusion](inclusion) is not trivial or self-dissolving but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [exclusion](exclusion).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](triage/thing) that [../acts/exist|exists](../acts/exist|exists) is conditioned by both [inclusion](inclusion) and [exclusion](exclusion);
- [inclusion](inclusion) is essential for affirming what a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is:
    - Without [inclusion](inclusion), no [triage/thing](triage/thing) can be marked as present within the field of what is;
- [inclusion](inclusion) participates in the fundamental [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) that generate and sustain [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) and all further relational architectures.
