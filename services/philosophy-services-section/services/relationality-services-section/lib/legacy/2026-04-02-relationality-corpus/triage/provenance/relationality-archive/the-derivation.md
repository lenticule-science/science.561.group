Relationality unfolds as a single sustained argument. It begins with one claim — that something exists — and shows that the claim, if coherent, compels structures that cannot not arise. Each structure's coherence leaves something undetermined, and that indeterminacy compels the next structure. Nothing external is imported. Everything is earned from the initial claim and the requirement that what is claimed must cohere.

The argument follows a recurring pattern: each phase produces an **act** (a dynamic of determination), a **condition** (a dynamic of relation), and a **structure** (a dynamic of form). The structure resolves the current indeterminacy but exposes a new one, which incites the next phase. This pattern — act, condition, structure, incitement — is the **triadic engine**, operating at every scale of the derivation.

The full derivation has nine phases, organized into five **movements**. The movements correspond to qualitative shifts in what is being derived: Movement I is logic, II is stability, III is motion, IV is spatiality, V is physics.

## Phase 1: Existing → Movement I

**Claim.** Something exists.

**Compelled.** To claim something exists is to claim it is not what it is not — a distinction. Three dynamics are forced: *including* (something holds itself within what it is), *excluding* (something holds out everything it is not), and *coherence* (the minimal unit stabilizing the interplay). Coherence ensures type-homogeneity: including and excluding are oppositional, but they are the same kind of act on the same field.

**Undetermined.** Including and excluding must be continuously maintained. What maintains them?

**Phenomenological layer.** Between the raw claim and the formal operators is a sequence of recognitions the algebra later axiomatizes: existing → including/excluding → inclusion/exclusion → tension → difference → relating → relation → relative.

**Formal operators (Movement I proper).** The phenomenological sequence earns: [Differentiating](../terms/differentiate.md) (the product of reflexing — the first recognition); Including/Excluding (partial order $a \leq b$ and incompatibility); Togethering/Eithering ($\land$, $\lor$); Negating (complement, $\text{Negating}(a) = \text{Inducing}(a, \bot)$); Inducing (implication, $\text{Inducing}(a, b)$ is the greatest $c$ with $\text{Togethering}(a, c) \leq b$); Iterating (self-application, $\text{Iterating}(a) = \text{Togethering}(a, \text{Inducing}(a, \top))$). These form a complete Heyting algebra — *not* Boolean: $\text{Negating}(\text{Negating}(a)) \geq a$ but not necessarily $=$. The relational field admits genuine indeterminacy.

## Phase 2: Relating → Movement I/II transition

**Claim.** The coherence from Phase 1 must be sustained.

**Compelled.** Including and excluding do not maintain themselves. *Relating* is the continuous act that holds them in productive opposition; it introduces reflexive sequence (each relational unit can relate to itself, deepening). *Relation* is the stabilized condition. *Relational form* is the minimal configuration formalizing the interplay.

**Undetermined.** What coheres relational form itself?

## Phase 3: Stabilizing → Movement II

**Claim.** Relational form must cohere.

**Compelled.** Relating and relation must be jointly sustained. *Relating-relation* mediates them; *relational self-coherence* is the unified dynamic; *relational closure* is the structured configuration of self-maintenance.

**Formal operators.** *Closing* (the closure operator: $\text{Closing}(a) = \bigvee_n \text{Iterating}^n(a)$ — the least element stable under iteration). *Opening* (the interior operator, dual to closing). *Balancing* ($\text{Opening}(a) \land \text{Closing}(a)$). The Frobenius law ensures inward consolidation coheres with outward release. Closing/opening earn modal logic: *Necessitating* = Closing, *Possibilizing* = Opening — the S4 axioms, derived not assumed.

**Undetermined.** Stability says nothing about how recognitions *move*.

## Phase 4: Bounding → Movement II/III transition

**Claim.** The self-sustaining unit must distinguish itself from what it is not.

**Compelled.** *Boundary-excluding* (enacting exclusion of what lies beyond the unit — relationlessness itself); *relational negation* (the condition maintaining the distinction); *relational boundary* (the structural form).

**Undetermined.** What coheres the boundary? To relate the boundary is to fold it back into the system it bounds.

## Phase 5: Directing → Movement III

**Claim.** The relational boundary must be related back to the field.

**Compelled.** *Reflexive relating* (folding exclusion into the system); *reflexive relation* (boundary becomes part of the system through self-engagement); *reflexive form* (the folding formalized).

**Formal operators.** *Flowing* — the directed comonadic dynamic; KZ-lax idempotent: $\text{Flowing}(\text{Flowing}(a)) \leq \text{Flowing}(a)$. Motion contracts under iteration. Flowing preserves the modal core. *Dynamic residuation*: $\text{Flowing}(a \land b) \leq c$ iff $a \leq \text{Flowing}(b \Rightarrow c)$ — doing-then-reasoning equals reasoning-about-doing. *Arithmetic of processes* — addition (combining flows) and multiplication (composing flows).

**Undetermined.** All motion has occurred within a single field. How does local behavior translate to global structure?

## Phase 6: Differentiating externally → Movement IV

**Claim.** The reflexive relational unit must distinguish itself from *other* such units.

**Compelled.** *External differentiating* (distinguishing one reflexive unit from another within the shared field); *co-presence* (multiple units in differentiated existence); *relational tension* (the structure stabilizing the interplay; supports composites, chains, networks).

## Phase 7: Integrating → Movement IV proper

**Claim.** Multiple co-present units must cohere both locally and globally.

**Compelled.** Two simultaneous integrations — *inter-unit relating* (local) and *field-integrating* (global).

**Formal operators.** *The cohesive chain*: Shape ⊣ Flat ⊣ Sharp ⊣ Crisp — four adjoint functors connecting immediate and extended perspectives. Shape is the outline; Flat (Discrete) is purely local; Sharp (Codiscrete) is purely global; Crisp (Global) is the fully coherent integration. *Local-global correspondence* — what holds locally translates coherently to what holds globally. *Residuation* — the adjunction between doing-then-stabilizing and stabilizing-then-doing.

**Undetermined.** Logic, stability, motion, and spatial coherence have been earned. To understand what these operations *describe*, they must be read as describing physical reality.

## Phase 8: Meta-bounding → Movement V

**Claim.** The entire integrated relational field must distinguish itself from what lies beyond it.

**Compelled.** *Meta-boundary excluding*; *meta-negation*; *meta-boundary*. Recapitulates Phase 4 at the scale of the field.

## Phase 9: Meta-relating → Movement V proper

**Claim.** The meta-boundary must be related back into the field.

**Compelled.** *Meta-reflexive relating* (folding the meta-boundary into the system); *meta-transcending* (engaging with what the meta-boundary posits as beyond); *meta-reflexive relation* / *meta-expansion* (the field extends to accommodate what was excluded); *meta-reflexive form* / *meta-extension* (formalizing self-relation and expansion at the meta level).

**Formal operators.** *States* (recognitions read as physical configurations); *observables* (measurements as nuclei applied to states); *invariants* (what is conserved under flowing — the active envelope $\text{Env}_F(a) = \text{Necessitating}(\text{Flowing}(a)) \land \text{Possibilizing}(\text{Flowing}(a))$ captures the bandwidth of stable variation); *conservation* (every consistency in the logic finds expression as a measurable invariant — Noether-like correspondence).

**Undetermined.** Closure at the meta-relational level exposes new dynamics, compelling a subsequent domain. The derivation points toward further determination — coherence requires ongoing resolution.

## Self-grounding

The derivation is [self-grounding](../terms/self-grounding.md): the structures it derives contain the means to reconstruct the derivation. Every Profile (a well-behaved substructure under a Filter) supports the full four-part chain — RelationalStructure → RelationalLanguage → RelationalGeometry → RelationalPhysics. This is what RITM formalizes: a quadruple adjunction that, once realized, generates two internal sub-modalities (Relational Dynamics, Relational Topology) reproducing the RITM axioms. Every RITM contains the structure to reconstruct itself.

The relational field does not rest on an external ground. It grounds itself through the very operations it contains — because those operations, when applied to themselves, produce exactly the structure from which they arose.
