- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is any [structure](structure)—specifically, any [relative](relative)—that is held open as coherent within the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating. A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is not a special kind of entity but the name we give to any **stabilized** relational unit that maintains [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) through ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension.

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) produces the **minimal relative**: the simplest [structure](structure) that holds open the dynamic of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion, ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion, and their ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension.
    - This minimal relative is the **first [triage/thing](triage/thing)**.

- However, the system does not limit what counts as a [triage/thing](triage/thing) to this minimal case:
    - Any stabilized [relative](relative)—whether simple or complex—is a [triage/thing](triage/thing);
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) can be a single basic relative or a **nested/compound** relative assembled through further relational dynamics.

- Therefore, a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is **defined structurally**:
    - It is a [structure](structure) that holds at least one ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation open durably;
    - It achieves ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence by stabilizing the acts and conditions (such as [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including), ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding, ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension, [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference), and ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) that bring it about.

- The [triage/thing](triage/thing) **cannot be separated** from the relational architecture that constitutes it:
    - It is not a primitive “given” outside the system;
    - It is **generated and maintained** through the interplay of [../acts/act](../acts/act), [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition), and [structure](structure).

- In this way, every [triage/thing](triage/thing):
    - Is sustained by a **coherent set** of relational dynamics;
    - Derives its **identity and distinctness** from the conditions of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension that define it relationally.

## Implications

- The minimal [triage/thing](triage/thing) is the first [relative](relative) formed in the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing);
    - More complex [triage/thing|things](triage/thing|things) arise by recursively applying relational dynamics, building up nested or composed relatives.
- Every [structure](structure) that stably holds a ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation is, by definition, a [triage/thing](triage/thing):
    - Thus, the entire architecture of relationality is populated **only by things** of varying complexity and depth.
- There is no “bare” [triage/thing](triage/thing) outside of relationality:
    - To be a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is to be a **relationally-constituted, stabilized structure**;
    - The system admits no thing **without** relational grounding.
