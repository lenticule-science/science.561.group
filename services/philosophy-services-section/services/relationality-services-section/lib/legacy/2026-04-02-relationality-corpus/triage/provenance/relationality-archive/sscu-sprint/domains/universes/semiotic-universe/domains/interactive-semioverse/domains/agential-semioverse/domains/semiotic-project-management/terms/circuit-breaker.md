A **circuit breaker** is the mechanism that stops work when
[appetite](./appetite.md) is exhausted without completion. When a
plan consumes its declared appetite and its acceptance criteria are
not met, the circuit breaker fires: stop, reassess whether the
approach is right, reshape the plan or abandon it.

The concept comes from Ryan Singer's *Shape Up* (Basecamp, 2019),
where it prevents projects from consuming unbounded attention.

The circuit breaker prevents plans from growing without bound. It
forces a choice: is the plan worth reshaping with a new appetite,
or should the work be abandoned and the closure pressure addressed
differently?
