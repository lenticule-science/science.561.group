## Interpretation

Flows alter arguments before implication is tested.  *Flow* witnessing *Imply Recognition* yields ***Flow Index Implication*** ([FlowIndexImplication](flow-index-implication.md)).

This composition records the implication whose premise is transformed by the flow, so *Index *Flow* Implication* is valid.
