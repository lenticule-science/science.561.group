- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: A [condition](condition) is the immediate outcome of an [../acts/act](../acts/act). It is what is brought about, held open transiently, by the [../acts/act](../acts/act) that produces it. Every [../structures/structure](../structures/structure) holds a [condition](condition) durably; every [relation](relation) requires at least one [condition](condition).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) shows that every [../acts/act](../acts/act) necessarily brings about a [condition](condition).
    - For example, the [../acts/act](../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) brings about the [condition](condition) of [inclusion](inclusion);
    - The [../acts/act](../acts/act) of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding brings about the [condition](condition) of [exclusion](exclusion).

- A [condition](condition) is distinct from the [../acts/act](../acts/act) that produces it.
    - An [../acts/act](../acts/act) is what **does**; a [condition](condition) is what **is held open** by that doing.
    - The [condition](condition) only persists **as long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act) is active**, unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure).

- [condition|Conditions](condition|Conditions) are necessary because without them:
    - The effects of [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) would have no persistence, even transiently;
    - No differentiation between states could be identified or held open, even momentarily.

- In the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing):
    - [inclusion](inclusion) and [exclusion](exclusion) are the [condition|conditions](condition|conditions)nditions]] that arise from the primitive [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding;
    - [tension](tension) is the [condition](condition) that holds [inclusion](inclusion) and [exclusion](exclusion) in mutual opposition;
    - [difference](difference) is the [condition](condition) induced by that [tension](tension), marking a [triage/thing](triage/thing) as set apart from what is not;
    - [relation](relation) is the [condition](condition) that specifies the binding of the [triage/thing](triage/thing) to its ongoing [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) via ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating.

- Thus, [condition|conditions](condition|conditions):
    - Arise **immediately** from [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts);
    - Are **maintained** transiently by those acts;
    - Require [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) to achieve durability and coherence.

## Implications

- No [condition](condition) can arise independently of an [../acts/act](../acts/act).
- No [../structures/structure](../structures/structure) can exist without holding at least one [condition](condition) durably.
- [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) depend on a layered interaction:  
    - [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) bring about [condition|conditions](condition|conditions);  
    - [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) hold those [condition|conditions](condition|conditions) open durably, forming the basis for further [relation|relations](relation|relations).
- The minimal architecture of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) necessarily includes multiple [condition|conditions](condition|conditions): [inclusion](inclusion), [exclusion](exclusion), [tension](tension), [difference](difference), and [relation](relation).
