Model relationships, boundaries, and conversations as recognitions to see
patterns of contact and care.

## 1. Recognitions

- `RelationshipRecognition`: type, boundaries, history.
- `ConversationRecognition`: threads across channels.
- `AgreementRecognition`: explicit agreements or expectations.
- `RuptureRecognition` / `RepairRecognition`: conflicts and their working
  through.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Topology:
  - social graphs, overlapping communities.
- Measurement:
  - contact frequency, imbalance, conflict frequency, repair attempts.

Runbooks like `check_in`, `hold_boundary`, `repair` can align gameplay/real
life with your relational ethics.
