Represent values, norms, and ethical constraints as recognitions so conflicts
can be seen and worked with explicitly.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ValueRecognition`: core values (care, autonomy, justice, etc.).
- `NormRecognition`: contextual norms for specific domains (research, MUD,
  social, finance).
- `ConflictRecognition`: when values/norms clash in a situation.
- `ResolutionRecognition`: a particular way a conflict was navigated.

## 2. Uses

- Attach value/norm recognitions to runbooks and recognitions across domains.
- Use conflicts/resolutions as training data for future decisions, and as
  anchors for how you want the computer to behave.
