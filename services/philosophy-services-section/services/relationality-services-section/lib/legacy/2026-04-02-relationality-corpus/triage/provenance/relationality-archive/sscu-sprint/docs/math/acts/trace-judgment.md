Observational content exists, yet we must record how such stabilized judgments evolve under evaluation.  *Stabilize Trace* witnessing *Observe Judgment* yields ***Trace Judgment*** ([TraceJudgment](trace-judgment.md)).

Using only earned stable recognitions, this composition builds the trace of judgmental acts, so *Trace Judgment* follows.
