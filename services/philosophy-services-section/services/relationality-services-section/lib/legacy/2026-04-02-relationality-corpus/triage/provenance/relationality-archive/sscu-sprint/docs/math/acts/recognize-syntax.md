## Interpretation

We interpret types modulo the provability preorder to obtain syntactic recognitions.  *Construct Types* witnessing *Order Provability* produces ***Recognize Syntax*** ([RecognizeSyntax](recognize-syntax.md)).

This composition records the equivalence classes of types under provability, so *Recognize Syntax* is valid.
