Treat astrological charts and similar systems as recognitions, without forcing
them into or out of “truth” claims—just as structured relational systems.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ChartRecognition`: a natal or event chart (date, time, location).
- `BodyPositionRecognition`: positions of planets/points.
- `AspectRecognition`: relationships between bodies (conjunction, square,
  trine, etc.).
- `ConfigurationRecognition`: larger patterns (T-square, grand trine, etc.).
- `InterpretationRecognition`: text or structured interpretations.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - aspects/configurations as edges between bodies and houses,
  - charts linked to people, events, cycles.
- Measurement:
  - frequencies of patterns, correlations with other recognitions (mood,
    events, health metrics), without assuming causality.

This lets you explore “aquarian math” relationally and rigorously: you can see
how symbolic systems relate to lived data (health, mood, weather, social
events) without collapsing them into or out of validity.
