Treat vocabulary, grammar, and usage as recognitions across languages.

## 1. Recognitions

- `LexemeRecognition`: word stems, meanings, inflections.
- `PhraseRecognition`: multi-word expressions, idioms.
- `GrammarPatternRecognition`: constructions (tenses, aspects, cases).
- `UsageContextRecognition`: where/how a phrase is used (register, domain).

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Topology:
  - cognate graphs, semantic fields, syntax trees, cross-lingual links.
- Measurement:
  - recall (SRS), interference between languages, domain coverage.

Runbooks for `plan_review`, `add_phrase`, `link_cognates` let you navigate and
grow a living language graph.
