## Interpretation

*Structure Semantics* interprets *Construct Types*, *Construct Contexts*, and *Construct Terms*.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Construct Types* produces ***Model Semantics*** ([ModelSemantics](model-semantics.md)).

Composing these recognitions records how each type is interpreted as a recognition with its constructors, so *Model Semantics* follows.
