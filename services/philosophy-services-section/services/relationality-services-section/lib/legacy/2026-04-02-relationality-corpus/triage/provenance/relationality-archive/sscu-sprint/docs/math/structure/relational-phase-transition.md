Families of recognitions undergoing abrupt topological/structural changes under
parameterised flow.

## Definition
- Parameter space: drawn from [Parametric Semantics](parametric-semantics.md).
- Transition: critical parameter values where recognition topology changes
  (e.g., merges/splits).

## Invariants
- Criticality: transitions marked by non-smooth change in invariants.
- Stability: phases stable away from critical points.

## Proof
- Uses: parametric flow and invariants to define phases and transitions.
- Shows that: critical behaviour can be captured recognitionally.
