Represent care relationships and exchanges as recognitions to support
transparent, humane mutual aid.

## 1. Recognitions

- `PersonRecognition`: with consent, needs, capacities.
- `NeedRecognition`: concrete requests (rides, money, listening, research).
- `OfferRecognition`: what someone can provide.
- `ExchangeRecognition`: when a need and an offer meet.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - care networks, affinity groups, households, organizations.
- Measurement:
  - load (how much someone is giving/receiving), unmet needs, burnout risk.

Runbooks:

- `match_needs_offers`, `check_in`, `rebalance_load` become operational ways to
support actual communities.
