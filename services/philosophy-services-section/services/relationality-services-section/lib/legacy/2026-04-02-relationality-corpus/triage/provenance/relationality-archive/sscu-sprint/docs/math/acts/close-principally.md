## Interpretation

Joining with a fixed recognition k yields the principal closure ν_k(x) = x join k.  *Join Recognition* witnessing *First Name* produces ***Close Principally*** ([ClosePrincipally](close-principally.md)).

*First Name* designates the chosen recognition k; composing with joins records ν_k, so *Close Principally* follows.
