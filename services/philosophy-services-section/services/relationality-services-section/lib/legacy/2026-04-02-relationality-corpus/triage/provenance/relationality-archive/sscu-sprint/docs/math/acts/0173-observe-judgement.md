## Interpretation

Classified judgemental acts still need their stabilized observational content.  *Stabilize Observation* witnessing *Witness Judgement* produces ***Observe Judgement*** ([ObserveJudgement](0173-observe-judgement.md)).

This composition records the observational report of stabilized judgements, so *Observe Judgement* is valid.
