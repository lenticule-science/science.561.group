This program models relationships, boundaries, and conversations as
recognitions to see patterns of contact and care.

## Behaviour

- Represent relationships, conversations, agreements/expectations, and ruptures
  or repairs as recognitions with provenance.
- Map social graphs and overlapping communities; attach policies and
  boundaries to relationships.
- Measure contact frequency, imbalance, conflict frequency, and repair
  attempts.

## Intended operators

- Check in, hold boundaries, and run repair processes via runbooks.
- Analyse relationship health using the recorded measurements.
- Align interpersonal behaviour with stated values and policies.
