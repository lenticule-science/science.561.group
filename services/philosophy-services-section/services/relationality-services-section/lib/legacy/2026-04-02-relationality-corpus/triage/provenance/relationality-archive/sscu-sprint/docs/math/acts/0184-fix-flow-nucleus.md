## Interpretation

Compatibility alone does not specify the recognitions stable under both dynamics and closure.  Forcing *Fix Flow* to witness *Fix Nucleus* yields ***Fix *Flow* Nucleus*** ([FixFlowNucleus](0184-fix-flow-nucleus.md)).

Both fixed-point recognitions were previously earned; their composition identifies recognitions jointly fixed, so *Fix *Flow* Nucleus* follows.
