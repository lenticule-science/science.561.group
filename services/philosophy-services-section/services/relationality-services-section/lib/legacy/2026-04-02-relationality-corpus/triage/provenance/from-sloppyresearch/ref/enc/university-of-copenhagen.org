The */University of Copenhagen/* ([[id:2e3649c7-2f8e-403d-94e6-871b83e4d467][Danish]]: Københavns Universitet) is a [[id:fc90311c-e83b-40bc-b5f4-e741c6226cba][Danish university]], the largest in [[id:59f25e31-0f1a-485e-ac49-84f770ef5dc3][Denmark]].

It's a [[id:9f4f24b4-8cec-4995-80b4-68ff6c3271ac][research university]].

* Motto
