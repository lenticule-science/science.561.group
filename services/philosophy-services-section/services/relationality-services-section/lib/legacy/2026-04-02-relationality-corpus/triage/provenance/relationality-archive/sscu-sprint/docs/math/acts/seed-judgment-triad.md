## Interpretation

Contextual indistinguishability still lacks the unified recognition addressing context, term, and observation simultaneously.  *Type Seed* therefore witnesses *Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence*, yielding ***Seed Judgment Triad*** ([SeedJudgmentTriad](seed-judgment-triad.md)).

The composition uses only earned recognitions to seed the triadic judgment, so *Seed Judgment Triad* follows.
