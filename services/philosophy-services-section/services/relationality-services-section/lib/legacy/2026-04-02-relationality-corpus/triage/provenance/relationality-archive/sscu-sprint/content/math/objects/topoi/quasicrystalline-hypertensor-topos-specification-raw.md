1. Primitive Data
Axiom A0 — The Trace Category

There exists a small category T, called the trace category, whose objects represent finite traces and whose morphisms represent admissible normalizations between such traces. This category satisfies the following conditions:

Objects
Each object of T is a finite sequence of abstract trace-steps.
No algebraic structure is imposed on step symbols beyond identity.

Morphisms (normalizations)
A morphism f : t → t′ exists exactly when t′ can be obtained from t by a finite sequence of normalization moves, each of which is one of:

deletion of a neutral or redundant step,

commutation of two adjacent steps that are declared independent,

reassociation or rebracketing when permitted by independence.

These moves encode the fundamental equivalences of trace-shapes.

Free partially commutative monoid
The collection of objects forms the free partially commutative monoid on the set of trace-steps, subject only to the declared independence relations.
Morphisms are the congruence classes generated by these commutations and deletions.

Composition
Composition of morphisms corresponds to concatenation of normalization sequences followed by normalization.
Confluence of normalization guarantees associativity.

Identities
The identity morphism on a trace t is the empty normalization sequence.

Finiteness
For any pair of traces t, t′, the hom-set T(t, t′) is finite.

Interpretation.
Axiom A0 provides the purely combinatorial temporal scaffold of the hypertensor universe. Traces encode the shapes along which information, geometry, or logical structure may flow, while morphisms capture when two such shapes should be regarded as equivalent. No spatial, metric, or analytic structure is present yet—only the algebra of allowed temporal rearrangements.
