Model artifacts, exhibits, and loans as recognitions to support curation and
storytelling.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ObjectRecognition`: artifacts with provenance, materials, condition.
- `ExhibitRecognition`: curated arrangements around themes.
- `LoanRecognition`: movement of objects between institutions.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - thematic links, historical connections, physical layouts.
- Measurement:
  - visitor engagement, environmental conditions, conservation status.

Runbooks: `plan_exhibit`, `rotate_objects`, `assess_conservation` integrate
museum work into the relational universe.
