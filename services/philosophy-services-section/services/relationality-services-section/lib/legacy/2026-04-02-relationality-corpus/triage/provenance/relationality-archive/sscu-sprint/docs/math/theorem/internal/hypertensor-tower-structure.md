  - acts/nucleate# Hypertensor Tower Structure

There exists a carrier `Tower` earned by:

- [Build Profile Tower](../../acts/build-profile-tower.md),
- [Build Composite Profile Tower](../../acts/build-composite-profile-tower.md),
- on the stabilised recognitions from [Profile Recognitions](../../acts/profile-recognitions.md),
  respecting the flow/nucleus acts [Flow](../../acts/flow.md) and
  [Nucleate](../../acts/nucleate.md).

Each `Tower` attaches to a single `Fiber` (from
[Hypertensor Fiber Structure](hypertensor-fiber-structure.md)) and records its
profile levels.
