Make contradictions, mediations, and ideological formations explicit so the
system can surface tensions and structural patterns.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `ContradictionRecognition`
  - two or more recognitions/tendencies that clash:
    - stated values vs measured impacts,
    - rights vs actual access,
    - growth vs ecological limits.

- `MediationRecognition`
  - recognitions that explain how opposed poles are linked (e.g., markets,
    law, cultural norms, infrastructures).

- `TotalityRecognition`
  - a slice of relations treated as a whole:
    - a project, supply chain, media ecosystem, mode of production.

- `IdeologicalFormationRecognition`
  - patterns of signs/practices that:
    - normalize some relations,
    - obscure others,
    - reproduce certain power structures.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - networks of contradictions and their mediations,
  - how ideological formations sit across domains (news, policy, MUD,
    personal finance).

- Measurement:
  - frequency and severity of certain contradictions,
  - how often mediations resolve vs deepen tension,
  - where ideological formations correlate with harm/benefit.

## 3. Runbooks and uses

- `surface_contradictions(slice)`: find mismatches between values and
  measurements.
- `trace_mediations(contradiction_id)`: follow systems that link the poles.
- `analyze_ideology(slice, lens_id)`: apply a critical lens to describe what’s
  naturalized/hidden.

These recognitions connect to:

- value & ethics, governance, markets, socio-ecology, MUD narrative structure.
