# Idea: Open-Source Communities

Model projects, contributors, issues, and governance decisions as recognitions.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ProjectRecognition`: repositories, scope, ecosystem.
- `ContributorRecognition`: people, roles, histories.
- `IssueRecognition`: bugs, features, RFCs.
- `ReleaseRecognition`: versions, changesets.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - dependency graphs, maintainer networks, governance structures.
- Measurement:
  - activity, bus factor, responsiveness, diversity.

Runbooks: `triage_issue`, `review_pr`, `cut_release`, `update_governance` make
open-source work first-class in your relational universe.
