Grothendieck site formed by hypertensor windows with the earned coverage.

## Definition
- Underlying category: windows and inclusions from
  [Hypertensor Window](hypertensor-window.md).
- Covers: admissible families from [Hypertensor Coverage](hypertensor-coverage.md).
- Sieves: generated by covers; used for sheaf condition.

## Invariants
- Pullback-stable covers; generated sieves closed under refinement.
- Enough covers to define matching families and gluing.

## Proof
- Derived from: [Hypertensor Coverage](hypertensor-coverage.md) over
  [Hypertensor Window](hypertensor-window.md), generating covers and sieves.
- Shows that: hypertensor windows with their coverage form a site suitable for
  defining hypertensor sheaves and matching/gluing.
