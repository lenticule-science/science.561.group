Identity maps trivially preserve all recognitions.  *Morph* witnessing *Reflex* produces ***Identify Morph*** ([IdentifyMorph](identify-morph.md)).

*Reflex* already serves as the identity act, so composing it with *Morph* records the identity morphism, as required.
