## Derivation

- [derive|Deriving](derive|Deriving) [exist|existing](exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](triage/thing) be:
    - Brought into what it is (by [include|including](include|including));
    - And set apart from what it is not (by [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding)).

- [exclude|Excluding](exclude|Excluding) is the [act](act) that enacts this **holding-out**:
    - It does not merely assert that something is absent; it actively **establishes absence** by preventing the [triage/thing](triage/thing) from being within the scope of what is.
    - Without [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding), nothing is positioned as absent, and no coherent claim of [exist|existing](exist|existing) can be held open.

- [exclude|Excluding](exclude|Excluding) is primitive:
    - It cannot be reduced to other acts;
    - It operates in necessary **complementarity** with [include|including](include|including).

- [exclude|Excluding](exclude|Excluding) brings about the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion:

    - This [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) persists only transiently unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure).

- To sustain the dynamic of [exist|existing](exist|existing), [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) must be held open **together with** [include|including](include|including):
    - This mutual holding induces ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension;
    - ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension ensures that ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion is not void but is **active and bounded**, forming a coherent opposition to ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion.
