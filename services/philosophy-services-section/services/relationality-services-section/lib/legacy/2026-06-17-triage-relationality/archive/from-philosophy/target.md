## What this is

The **target** of a directed edge e : u → v is v — the vertex or object the edge arrives at.

In directed graph theory: target = head of the edge. The edge goes from tail to head.
In category theory: target of a morphism f : A → B is B. Synonym for *codomain*.
In Petri nets: the target places of a transition t are its postset t• — the places that receive tokens.

Target is the same concept as Codomain of a function, applied to the directed-graph setting where edges have explicit endpoints.
