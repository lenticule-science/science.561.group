# Idea: Freudian Psychology – Drives, Defenses, and Repetition

Capture aspects of Freudian psychoanalysis (drives, defenses, repetition) as
recognitions to see how they manifest across domains.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `DriveRecognition`
  - basic drives (life/libido, death/aggression) as they appear in patterns of
    action or fantasy.

- `DefenseMechanismRecognition`
  - defenses (projection, repression, denial, rationalization, displacement,
    etc.) inferred from discourse/behavior.

- `RepetitionCompulsionRecognition`
  - repeated patterns of behavior/situation that echo earlier conflicts or
    traumas.

- `TransferenceRecognition`
  - when feelings/expectations from past relationships are mapped onto present
    figures (people, agents, institutions).

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - links between drives, defenses, transference events, and repeated
    situations.
- Measurement:
  - frequency/contexts of specific defenses,
  - where repetition diminishes or intensifies over time.

## 3. Runbooks and uses

- `annotate_defenses(discourse_slice)`,
- `detect_repetition(subject_slice)`,
- `trace_transference(subject_slice)`.

These structures connect to your interpersonal communication, dreams, inner
work, and agent-dialogue behavior.
