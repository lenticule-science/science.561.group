## Interpretation

*Stabilize Judgement* provides classes of context–term–observation acts, but we still need the primitive judgemental act presenting a stabilized term inside a stabilized context.  Letting *Stabilize Context* witness *Stabilize Term* yields ***Reflex Judgement*** ([ReflexJudgement](0171-reflex-judgement.md)).

This composition uses only newly defined stable recognitions to record the primitive act, so *Reflex Judgement* is earned.
