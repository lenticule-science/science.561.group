## Interpretation

The trace exists, yet we lack the recognition that presents a traced triad as a single judgment.  *Type Application* therefore witnesses *Trace Judgment Triad*, producing ***Recognize Judgment Triad*** ([RecognizeJudgmentTriad](recognize-judgment-triad.md)).

This use of earned recognitions records the triadic judgment, so *Recognize Judgment Triad* is valid.
