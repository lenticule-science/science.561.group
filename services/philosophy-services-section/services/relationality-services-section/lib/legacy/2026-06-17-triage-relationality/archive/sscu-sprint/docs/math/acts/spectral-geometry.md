Spectral geometry studies spaces through harmonic modes and their spectral
weights under operators like the deviation operator and the diffusion operator.
We package the deviation operator, diffusion operator, and wave operator by
composing the wave operator with the deviation-measuring act:
`(WaveOperator MeasureDeviationObservationKernel)` earns ***SpectralGeometry***.
