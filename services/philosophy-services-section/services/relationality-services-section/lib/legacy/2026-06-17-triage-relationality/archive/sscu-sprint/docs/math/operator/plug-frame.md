Operator `plug : Frame × Term → Term` inserting a term into the hole of an
evaluation frame from [`EvaluationContext`](docs/math/structure/evaluation-context.md).

## Statement

For any frame `F` and term `t`, `plug(F, t)` yields the program obtained by
inserting `t` into the unique hole of `F`. For the neutral frame `empty`,
`plug(empty, t) = t`.

## Proof

Sketch: by induction on frames generated by
[`BuildEvaluationContext`](docs/math/acts/build-evaluation-context.md), define
`plug` structurally and show neutrality for `empty` from
[`SeedEvaluationContext`](docs/math/acts/seed-evaluation-context.md).
