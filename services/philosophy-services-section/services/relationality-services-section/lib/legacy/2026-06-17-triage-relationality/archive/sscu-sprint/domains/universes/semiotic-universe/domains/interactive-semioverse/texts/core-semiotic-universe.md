The [interactive semioverse](../index.md) extends a [semiotic universe](../../semiotic-universe/semiotic-universe.md) — a self-sustaining [sign](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md) system in which everything sayable is meaningful and everything meaningful is sayable. The base layer provides:

- a meaning domain $H$ that is a complete [Heyting algebra](../../../../concepts/heyting-algebra/index.md) (intuitionistic, because not every question about a [sign](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md)'s meaning resolves to yes or no),
- a modal [closure operator](../../../../concepts/closure-operator/index.md) $j$ formalizing [habit](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/habit.md)-formation,
- a [trace comonad](../../semiotic-universe/terms/trace-comonad.md) $G$ formalizing semiotic provenance — the [sign](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md) chains that produced each meaning,
- definable operators interpreted into $H$ (the grammar of the sign system),
- fragments (bounded interpretive contexts) and a composite [closure operator](../../../../concepts/closure-operator/index.md) $\mathcal{S}$.

This layer ensures that all semantic work is closed under logic, [habit](../../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/habit.md)-formation, and semiotic provenance before any handle-facing interaction is added.
