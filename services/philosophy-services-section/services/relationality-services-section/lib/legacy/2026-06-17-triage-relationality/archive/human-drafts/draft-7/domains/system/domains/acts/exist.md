- [define](define):: [exist|existing](exist|existing) is the minimal [act](act), required for a [triage/thing](triage/thing) to [act](act), be [../conditions/condition|conditioned](../conditions/condition|conditioned), or have [../structures/relative](../structures/relative). It is [derive|derived](derive|derived) that to [exist|exist](exist|exist), a [triage/thing](triage/thing) must [relate|relating](relate|relating) to existing, entering the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating.
    

## Derivation

- To [claim](claim) _something exists_ is not trivial. It specifies:
    
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is not some other thing;
        
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is not nothing.
        
- Therefore, [exist|existing](exist|existing) must be derived.
    
- To be a thing and not another, a thing must exclude what isn't itself, and therefore must include what it is.
    
    - This establishes two acts: [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).
        
    - [include|including](include|including): the [act](act) by which a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is brought into what is.
        
    - [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding): the [act](act) by which a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is held out of what is not.
        
    - These are primitive [act|acts](act|acts): each brings about a corresponding [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition)—../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion.
        
    - Neither reduces to the other, and both are required for anything to be meaningfully said to [exist|exist](exist|exist).
        
- The acts of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) must be held open together. This introduces the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension.
    
    - ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension is the dynamic holding of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion in potential opposition.
        
    - ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension is necessary because [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) are fundamentally opposed yet inseparable within the dynamic of existing.
        
    - Critically, to include, one must also include **the inclusion of inclusion as a condition**—which is only meaningful if exclusion of that condition is also a condition. But that itself is a condition, triggering a recursion: every inclusion demands exclusion, and every exclusion introduces a new condition requiring inclusion. This generates an **irreducible asymmetry** that ensures the derivation is never final.
        
    - As a result, to [exist|exist](exist|exist) is to be sustained not just in tension but in a **perpetual process of derivation**—an ongoing act that holds open what cannot be fully closed.
        
- The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension.
    
    - [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) is the minimal condition that marks a [triage/thing](triage/thing) as set apart from what is not.
        
    - It does not exist independently; it is **held open by the ongoing tension** between [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).
        
    - [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) is the **coherent separation** that makes existing internally stable within the relational dynamic.
        
    - Without ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension, there can be no [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference); without [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference), existing fails to achieve coherence.
        
- To sustain ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension over time, there must be an overarching [act](act) that maintains it.
    
    - Otherwise, the opposing acts would lose their mutual relation and the condition of existing would collapse.
        
    - We name this act [relate|relating](relate|relating): the minimal [act](act) by which a [triage/thing](triage/thing) sustains its own ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).
        
    - [relate|Relating](relate|Relating) is compelled by the requirement for ongoing coherence within the act of existing.
        
- The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) introduces a further requirement: the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation.
    
    - [relate|Relating](relate|Relating) engages the [triage/thing](triage/thing) in sustaining its own existing, but this engagement must itself have a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) to ensure that the [triage/thing](triage/thing) is not merely involved abstractly but is **bound determinately** to its [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).
        
    - This produces the minimal [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) we name ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation: the holding-open of the connection between the [triage/thing](triage/thing) and its acts of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) in a coherent and sustained way.
        
    - Without the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation, the act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) remains transient and fails to stabilize the dynamic.
        
- The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) together with the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation necessarily produces a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative).
    
    - A [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) is the minimal [../structures/structure](../structures/structure) that holds a ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation open durably.
        
    - While [relate|relating](relate|relating) is an [act](act) and ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation is a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition), acts and conditions alone are transient.
        
    - The [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) is the **first structure**: the stable outcome in which the [triage/thing](triage/thing) is maintained as existing within the relational field.
        
    - Only with the [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) does the architecture of existing achieve **closure**: the coherent stabilization of the entire dynamic.
        
- The formation of the [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) brings the minimal architecture of existing into full ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence.

        - The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating), sustaining the ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension;
