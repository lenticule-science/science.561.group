## Interpretation

Both observation and judgment traces now flow through evaluation contexts, but we still need the single act that witnesses this shared dynamics.  Forcing *Flow Observation* to witness *Flow Judgment* yields ***Flow*** ([Flow](flow.md)).

These recognitions already record how observations and judgments traverse contexts.  Composing them binds the shared motion into one act, so *Flow* is earned.
