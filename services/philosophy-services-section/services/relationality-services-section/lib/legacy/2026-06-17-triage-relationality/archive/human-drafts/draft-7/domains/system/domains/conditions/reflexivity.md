- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: [reflexivity](reflexivity) is the minimal [condition](condition) under which a [relational structure](relational structure) sustains its own ../../../../../triage/relations/reflex without alteration. It holds open the dynamic by which a [relational structure](relational structure) remains coherent when [related](related) to itself, ensuring that the operation of [reflexing](reflexing) neither introduces nor eliminates any relational determination already present.

## Derivation

- [reflexivity](reflexivity) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by the act of [reflexing](reflexing).

- To ../../../../../triage/relations/reflex is to perform `relating(S, S)` for a [relational structure](relational structure) `S`.

- This act deepens the [../structures/structure](../structures/structure), but for coherence, the [condition](condition) of [reflexivity](reflexivity) is required:

    - It holds the [relation](relation) between `S` and `relating(S, S)` open as **internally stable.**

- Without [reflexivity](reflexivity), the result of [reflexing](reflexing) would lack coherence, causing the [relational structure](relational structure) to fail closure under its own reflex.

- Therefore, [reflexivity](reflexivity) is necessary for sustaining [reflexive](reflexive) acts as **structurally coherent operations** within the [../relational system](../relational system).
