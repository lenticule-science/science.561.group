# Idea: Generative Art & Algorithmic Textures

Treat generative art systems as recognitions so you can explore styles and
textures relationally.

## 1. Recognitions

- `AlgorithmRecognition`
  - code/parameters for a generative process.
- `SeedRecognition`
  - random or structured seeds that drive variations.
- `ArtworkRecognition`
  - outputs: images, animations, audio, etc.
- `AestheticJudgementRecognition`
  - your assessments: pleasing, unsettling, balanced, chaotic.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - style neighborhoods, morph paths between outputs, shared motifs.
- Measurement:
  - complexity, symmetry, noise/structure balance, diversity.

Runbooks: `generate_batch`, `explore_style_space`, `curate_set` align generative
art with your broader relational practice.
