## Interpretation

*Close Judgement Trace* shows that observationally aligned triadic judgements close, yet we still lack the identification collapsing judgements with the same closed behaviour.  Forcing *Recognize Judgement Triad* to witness *Close Judgement Trace* yields ***Stabilize Judgement*** ([StabilizeJudgement](0167-stabilize-judgement.md)).

This composition identifies triadic judgements that close in the same way, so *Stabilize Judgement* follows directly from earned recognitions.
