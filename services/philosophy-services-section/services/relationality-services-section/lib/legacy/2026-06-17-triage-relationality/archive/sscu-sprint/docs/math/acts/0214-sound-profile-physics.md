## Interpretation

Likewise, the profile’s physics respects its geometry and judgements.  *Geometry Profile* witnessing PhysicsProfile produces ***Sound Profile Physics*** ([SoundProfilePhysics](0214-sound-profile-physics.md)).

This act records that the restricted flow and nuclei agree with the declared physics, so *Sound Profile Physics* is valid.
