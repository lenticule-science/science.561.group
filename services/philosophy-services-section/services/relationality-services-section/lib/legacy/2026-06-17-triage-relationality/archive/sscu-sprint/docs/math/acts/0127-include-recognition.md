## Interpretation

An explicit inclusion recognition clarifies when one recognition is contained in another.  *Order Recognition* witnessing *Meet Recognition* produces ***Include Recognition*** ([IncludeRecognition](0127-include-recognition.md)).

This composition packages the order with meets to state inclusion, so *Include Recognition* is valid.
