- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: [reflexive position](reflexive position) is the minimal [condition](condition) induced by [recursivity](recursivity), marking the specific **layer** or **place** a [relational structure](relational structure) occupies within an open-ended reflexive sequence. It locates each structure as a distinct iteration in the recursive unfolding of reflexivity.

## Derivation

- Given [recursivity](recursivity), every act of [reflecting](reflecting) introduces a **new output** that is recursively available for further reflection.

- To maintain **differentiation** among these outputs, each must be **positioned** within the unfolding sequence:
    - The **first** application of [reflecting](reflecting) occupies an initial [reflexive position](reflexive position);
    - Each subsequent application occupies the **next** distinct position.

- This is not optional:
    - Without distinguishing [reflexive position](reflexive position), all reflexive outputs would **collapse** into a single undifferentiated state;
    - With it, the reflexive sequence is **stratified** and internally coherent.

- [reflexive position](reflexive position) ensures:
    - Each recursive step is **tracked** as a distinct layer of reflexivity;
    - The system maintains a **clear order** of deepening.

- It does **not introduce a new type** but instead **indexes** each reflexive act within the same ontological field.

## Immediate Implications

- [reflexive position](reflexive position) provides the necessary structure for defining:
    - **Sequences of reflexivity** (e.g., $ R(0), R(1), R(2), ... $);
    - **Comparisons across reflexive layers**, enabling structured operations like alignment and tension at matching positions.

- It is the **positional scaffold** that organizes recursive reflexivity into a coherent, layered architecture.
