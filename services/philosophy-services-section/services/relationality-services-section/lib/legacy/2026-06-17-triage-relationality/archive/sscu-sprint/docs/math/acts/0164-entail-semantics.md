## Interpretation

To prove completeness we relate typing derivations to semantic orderings.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Equalize Terms* produces ***Entail Semantics*** ([EntailSemantics](0164-entail-semantics.md)).

This act records when every semantic calculus forces the entailment, so *Entail Semantics* is established.
