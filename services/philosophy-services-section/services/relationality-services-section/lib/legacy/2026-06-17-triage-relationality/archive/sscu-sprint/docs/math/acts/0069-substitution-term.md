## Interpretation

Substitution steps are judged but still need the term that records them inside the trace.  Forcing *Seed Term* to witness *Substitution Step* yields ***Substitution Term*** ([SubstitutionTerm](0069-substitution-term.md)).

The composition uses only earned recognitions and records the desired term, so *Substitution Term* arises.
