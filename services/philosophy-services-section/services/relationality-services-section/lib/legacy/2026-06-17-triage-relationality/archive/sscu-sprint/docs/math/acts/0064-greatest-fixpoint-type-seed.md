## Interpretation

The maximal invariant extends the least fixpoint, but we must seed the judgement guaranteeing that maximality.  Forcing *Form Greatest Fix Term* to witness *Fixpoint Typing Step* yields ***Greatest Fixpoint Type Seed*** ([GreatestFixpointTypeSeed](0064-greatest-fixpoint-type-seed.md)).

The terms involved are already earned, so their composition records the needed guarantee, giving *Greatest Fixpoint Type Seed*.
