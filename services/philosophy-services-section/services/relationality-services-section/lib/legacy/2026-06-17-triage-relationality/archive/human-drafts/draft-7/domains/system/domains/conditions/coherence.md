- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: [coherence](coherence) is the [condition](condition) in which an [../acts/act](../acts/act), its corresponding [condition|conditions](condition|conditions), and supporting [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) hold together in a stable, self-sufficient dynamic. It is what marks that a relational system is **complete enough** to persist as a real part of the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating.
    

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) shows:
    
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is brought about by the primitive [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding;
        
    - These generate [condition|conditions](condition|conditions) of [inclusion](inclusion) and [exclusion](exclusion);
        
    - Held together by the [condition](condition) of [tension](tension), which induces [difference](difference);
        
    - Sustained by ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating, which maintains the dynamic;
        
    - Clarified and stabilized through [relation](relation) and the formation of a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative) as a minimal [../structures/structure](../structures/structure).
        
- Each of these elements is necessary but not sufficient on its own:
    
    - [../acts/act|Acts](../acts/act|Acts) do the work;
        
    - [condition|Conditions](condition|Conditions) are held open;
        
    - [../structures/structure|Structures](../structures/structure|Structures) stabilize and durably maintain the results.
        
- But for the system to be **self-sufficient**:
    
    - These elements must not only be present but also **held together as a unified whole**;
        
    - Their interplay must resolve without collapse, dissipation, or irresolvable contradiction.
        
- This unified holding-together is the [condition](condition) of [coherence](coherence):
    
    - It ensures that all necessary components of the relational system **mutually support** one another;
        
    - It marks the point at which the system achieves **closure**: nothing further is needed to sustain its being.
        

## Implications

- [coherence](coherence) is the minimal indicator that a relational architecture is **real** within the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating:
    
    - Without [coherence](coherence), even if acts and conditions arise, they fail to persist meaningfully;
        
    - Coherence is what allows a [triage/thing](triage/thing) to maintain its integrity over time.
        
- Any system lacking [coherence](coherence):
    
    - Breaks down into unintelligibility or dissolution;
        
    - Cannot serve as a foundation for further [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) or [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures).
        
- [coherence](coherence) is what makes further derivations possible:
    
    - Once coherence is established at one level, new layers of [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) can be built upon it;
        
    - This recursive building is what allows complex relational systems to emerge from minimal foundations.
