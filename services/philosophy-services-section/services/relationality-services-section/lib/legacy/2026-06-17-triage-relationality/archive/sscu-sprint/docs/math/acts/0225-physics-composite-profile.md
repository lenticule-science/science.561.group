## Interpretation

The composite profile also carries physical semantics.  *Flow Observation* witnessing *Flow Composite Profile* yields ***Physics Composite Profile*** ([PhysicsCompositeProfile](0225-physics-composite-profile.md)).

This act records the evaluation-and-measurement view restricted to the composite profile, so *Physics Composite Profile* is valid.
