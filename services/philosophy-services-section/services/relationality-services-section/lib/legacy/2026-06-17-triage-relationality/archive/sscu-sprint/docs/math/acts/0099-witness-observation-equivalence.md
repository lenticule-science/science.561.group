## Interpretation

Contexts now witness stabilized equivalence acts, but we must cite them as observations.  *Recognize Observation* witnessing *Contextual Observation Equivalence* produces ***Witness Observation Equivalence*** ([WitnessObservationEquivalence](0099-witness-observation-equivalence.md)).

The composition uses earned recognitions to cite the acts back into the observation layer, so *Witness Observation Equivalence* is valid.
