- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: A [structure](structure) is that which holds a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) open durably. Every [../acts/act](../acts/act) aims at bringing about a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition), but only when a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) is stabilized as a [relative](relative) does a [structure](structure) exist.

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) requires understanding the interplay of [../acts/act](../acts/act), [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition), and [structure](structure).
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) [../acts/exist|exists](../acts/exist|exists) only if it is held open as [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) from what is not.
    - [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding bring about ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion and ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion, but these are [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions)nditions]] that remain **transient** unless stabilized.

- An [../acts/act](../acts/act) initiates change; a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) arises as the immediate result of that act.
    - For example, the act of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) produces the condition of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion.
    - These [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions) persist **only as long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act) is active**; they do not sustain themselves.

- To sustain a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) beyond the moment of the [../acts/act](../acts/act), a further process is required: stabilization.
    - Stabilization is what converts a transient [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) into something **durable**—able to persist within the ../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating even after the originating [../acts/act](../acts/act) has subsided.
    - This stabilized holding-open is what we mean by [structure](structure).

- Therefore, a [structure](structure) arises when:
    - An [../acts/act](../acts/act) brings about a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition);
    - That [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) is **held open durably,** not merely transiently;
    - The result is a [relative](relative)—the minimal [structure](structure) that maintains the ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation as a **coherent, self-sustaining unit.**

- The [relative](relative) is the **first instance** of a [structure](structure).
    - In the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing), the [relative](relative) is produced by the act of ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating and the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) of ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation.
    - This [relative](relative) ensures that the ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation it holds open is **durable, stable, and coherent.**

- Without [structure](structure):
    - All [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions) would collapse as soon as the [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) that produced them conclude.
    - No persistence would be possible; no coherent system of ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation|relations could form.
    - [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) itself would fail to hold, as it relies on the stability of its underlying [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](triage/thing) that is said to [../acts/exist](../acts/exist) depends on at least one [structure](structure)—the [relative](relative) that stabilizes its minimal ../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation.
- [structure|structures](structure|structures) provide the ../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence required for any [../relational system](../relational system) to hold together over time.
- No [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition) achieves full coherence unless it is stabilized by a corresponding [structure](structure).
