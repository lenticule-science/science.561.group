Recognized triads must report their observational content back to the observation layer.  *Recognize Observation* witnessing *Recognize Judgement Triad* yields ***Witness Judgement Triad*** ([WitnessJudgementTriad](0110-witness-judgement-triad.md)).

This composition records that report, so *Witness Judgement Triad* follows.
