States stable in distribution under repeated stochastic (Markov-like) evolution.

## Definition
- Carrier: states/recognitions invariant under
  [Hypertensor Stochastic Evolution](hypertensor-stochastic-evolution.md) up to
  distribution (fixed invariant measures).
- Relation: aligns with [Fixed Invariant State](fixed-invariant-state.md) but
  phrased for distributional stability.

## Invariants
- Stationarity: evolution leaves the distribution unchanged.
- Stability: small perturbations return to the fixed distribution under
  evolution.

## Proof
- Uses: stochastic evolution kernel and fixed invariant states.
- Shows that: distributional fixed points are a natural carrier for stochastic
  dynamics.
