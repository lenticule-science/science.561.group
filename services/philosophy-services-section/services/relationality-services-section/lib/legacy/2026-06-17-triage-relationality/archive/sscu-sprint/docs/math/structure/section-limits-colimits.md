Limits/colimits of section diagrams in the hypertensor sheaf category.

## Definition
- Limits: products, equalizers of [Hypertensor Section](hypertensor-section.md)
  diagrams computed pointwise over the sheaf.
- Colimits: coproducts, coequalizers computed pointwise with sheaf gluing.

## Invariants
- Sheaf compatibility: limits/colimits respect restriction/gluing; computed
  sectionwise.
- Preservation: sheaf morphisms preserve these limits/colimits when exact.

## Proof
- Uses: sheaf structure to compute sectionwise (co)limits.
- Shows that: section categories admit small limits/colimits needed for
  program/data composition.
