Brave Old World is a tabletop role-playing game ruleset built on Apocalypse World and tuned for adventures set in Teraum.

## Brave Old World Podcast
The same name was used for a podcast where emsenn served as host and dungeon master for a Dungeons & Dragons campaign exploring Teraum.
