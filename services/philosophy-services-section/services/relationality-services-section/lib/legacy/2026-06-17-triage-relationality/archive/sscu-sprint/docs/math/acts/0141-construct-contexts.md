## Interpretation

Contexts are traced collections of types.  *Contextualize Trace* witnessing *Construct Types* produces ***Construct Contexts*** ([ConstructContexts](0141-construct-contexts.md)).

Since contexts already arise as traces, composing with *Construct Types* records how contexts aggregate types, so *Construct Contexts* follows.
