Set of “almost identity” flows: flow steps that deviate only within
nucleus-equivalence.

## Definition
- Elements: flows whose effect on recognitions is nucleus-equivalent to
  identity, possibly with small deviations.
- Structure: subset of flow context annotated with deviation witnesses.

## Invariants
- Identity up to nucleus: composition with quasi-identity is equivalent to
  identity after nucleus collapse.
- Closed under composition where deviations remain within nucleus bounds.

## Proof
- Uses: flow context and identity paths to define near-identity behaviour.
- Shows that: quasi-identity flows form a meaningful substructure for
  perturbative reasoning.
