# Idea: Jungian Psychology – Archetypes, Shadow, and Individuation

Represent key Jungian structures as recognitions so archetypal patterns, shadow
material, and individuation paths can be related to your wider graph.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `JungArchetypeRecognition`
  - archetypes (Self, Shadow, Anima/Animus, Hero, Trickster, etc.).
  - fields: descriptions, typical motifs, light/shadow expressions.

- `ShadowAspectRecognition`
  - traits/impulses/figures that are disowned or repressed.
  - linked to events (overreactions, projections, dreams).

- `ComplexRecognition`
  - emotionally charged clusters of associations (mother complex, authority
    complex).
  - connected to personal history, symbols, and recurring situations.

- `IndividuationPathRecognition`
  - a narrative of individuating moves:
    - confrontations with shadow, integration of opposites,
    - symbolic milestones (dreams, encounters, choices).

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - links between archetypes, complexes, dreams, symbols, and life events.
- Measurement:
  - frequency/intensity of certain complexes in different domains,
  - gradual integration (shadow aspects acknowledged/related to differently).

## 3. Runbooks and uses

- `map_dream_to_archetypes(dream_id)`,
- `identify_complex_triggers(event_slice)`,
- `update_individuation_path(subject_slice)`.

These recognitions align with your mythic, dreams, and inner-work notes.
