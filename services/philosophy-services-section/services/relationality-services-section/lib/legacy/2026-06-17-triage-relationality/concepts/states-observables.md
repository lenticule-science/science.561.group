The physical reading of the relational field. Recognitions become *states*, [Flow](flow.md) becomes *evolution*, [invariants](conserving.md) become *conserved quantities*.

## What they are

- A **state** is an object $a \in \mathcal{C}$ — a stable recognition in the field.
- An **observable** is a morphism $\rho: a \to \Omega$ measuring the truth or presence of a recognition within state $a$.
- A **Flow evolution** is a family of morphisms $F_t: a \to F_t(a)$.
- An **invariant** is an observable $I$ with $I \circ F_t = I$.

A reflexive symmetry — a natural transformation $\theta: FC \to CF$ — produces the *Categorical Noether Theorem*: each symmetry of the comonad generates an invariant. The *residual* $\rho(r) = C^* r - r$ measures deviation from stability; the *defect energy* $S[r] = \tfrac{1}{2}\langle \rho(r), \rho(r)\rangle$ is minimized exactly at stabilization.

At the meta level, the framework constructs systems explicitly: a meta-Flow comonad, and a *Reflexive Infinity* — the stabilized universe where $\text{MetaFlow}(\text{MetaClose}(S)) = \text{MetaClose}(S)$. Movement V proper begins from the meta-boundary: the entire relational field must be distinguished from what lies beyond it; meta-relational coherence is what the field achieves when it folds that boundary back in.

## Key results

- **Conservation**: stabilized morphisms commute (the categorical conservation lemma).
- **Noether theorem**: reflexive symmetries → invariants.
- **Energy = deviation from coherence**: $\rho(r) = 0$ iff stabilized.
- **Entropy decreases monotonically** under iteration until stabilization.
- **Self-containment**: $R^{**} \cong R^*$ — reflexion terminates in identity.

States and observables are how Movement V earns the *physical* reading. The mathematics carried through Movements I–IV produces, at the meta-relational level, a structure isomorphic to spectral physics. The deepest result is that the physics of [RITM](ritm.md) is the logic of reflexive existence — the same content read in two registers.
