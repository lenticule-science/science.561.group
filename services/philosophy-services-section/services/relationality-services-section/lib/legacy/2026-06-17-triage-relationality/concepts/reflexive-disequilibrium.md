---
title: "Reflexive Disequilibrium"
kind: term
---
The failure of residuated sufficiency between distinct recognitions of the same field. The opposite of [reflexive equilibrium](reflexive-equilibrium.md).

When two inference systems share a relational field but fail the residuation law, the result is a structural imbalance: each system cannot reconstruct the other through the shared field operations. The systems make mutually incompatible demands on the same field.

## Order parameter

The residuated-sufficiency gap $g(z)$, evaluated as a function of the temporal parameter $z$, serves as the order parameter:

- $g(z) = 0$: reflexive equilibrium — the inference systems are mutually entailing.
- $g(z) > 0$: reflexive disequilibrium — residuation fails; the systems make incompatible demands on the shared field.

## Application: cosmological phase change

Applied to the Hubble tension, reflexive disequilibrium reinterprets the discrepancy between early-universe (CMB-derived) and late-universe (distance-ladder) measurements of $H_0$ not as a measurement error or missing physics, but as a *structural phase transition*. The field passes from a closed phase (matter-dominated, where inference structures mutually entail) to an open phase (dark-energy-dominated, where residuation fails).

The framework predicts the disequilibrium emerges where the field's reflexive coherence breaks — not at a particular redshift by accident, but at the boundary between the closed and open phases of the relational field.
