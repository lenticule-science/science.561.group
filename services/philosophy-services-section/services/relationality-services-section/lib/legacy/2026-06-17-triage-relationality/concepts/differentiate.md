The first recognition — the *product* of [Reflex](reflex.md) acting on [Relationlessness](relationlessness.md). Differentiate names what has been produced: a boundary with two sides, the first distinction. Reflex is the engine; Differentiate is what the engine generates.

## What it does

To claim that something exists is to claim it is different from what it is not. This claim has dynamics — *including* (the thing within what it is) and *excluding* (everything it isn't) — and Differentiate is the primitive act that produces them. It divides existence into marked and unmarked; the boundary it draws is what makes entities possible.

Distinguishing presupposes three notions: difference, negation, and coherence. Formally, Differentiate produces a set with an involution and a closure operator. Categorically, it appears as a monomorphism $m: X \hookrightarrow Y$ in the category of recognitions, inducing a subobject classifier $\Omega$.

## What it produces

- The field of recognition: all acts of Differentiate form a preorder $(\text{Rel}, \text{Includes})$.
- The first polarity: interior vs. exterior, [inclusion vs. exclusion](includes-excludes.md).
- The basis for further logical structure: [Together / Either](together-either.md), [Negate](negate.md), [Induces](induces.md).
- Relation as Top: `Relation = (Differentiate Relationlessness Relationlessness)` — the first "something," the complement of Relationlessness.

## On the primitive question

Older treatments are sometimes ambiguous about which of {Relationlessness, Reflex, Differentiate} is "primitive." All three describe one chain. Relationlessness is logically prior — the absence of relation. Reflex is the act that arises from its self-contradiction (see [Articulation Theorem](../theorems/articulation-theorem.md)). Differentiate is what Reflex produces.

Treating Differentiate alone as primitive collapses the act and its product into one concept; treating Relationlessness alone as primitive omits the bridge act between the void and its first determination. The clean reading keeps all three as distinct: void → engine → product.
