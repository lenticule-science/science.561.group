`Nothing = (Witness Something)`

Having "[something](something.md)" forces the next question: what does *[Witness](witness.md)* see when it looks directly at it? The composition captures the unresolved situation where testimony points to its own bearer, producing the recognition called *Nothing*.

This is not [relationlessness](relationlessness.md) (the void prior to all acts). It is the derived recognition of the chain looking at its own product and finding nothing yet definite there — a moment of internal symmetry that the next act, [Void](void.md), folds back on itself.
