---
`Recognition = (Void SelfNaming)`

A self-named point lacks a partner. Having [Void](void.md) witness [Self Naming](self-naming.md) produces a two-place recognition — a complementary position is created. This closes a structural gap left by self-application: the self-naming step on its own has no other to relate to; the void supplies that other.

The act is named *Recognition* because it is the first proper two-place act in the chain — the first place where the act-algebra produces something with a binary structure rather than a self-referential single position.
