Relationality is organized into five phases, each earning a kind of relational [coherence](coherence.md) whose incompleteness incites the next.

## The five movements

| Movement | Name | What it earns |
|---|---|---|
| I | Logical Origination | Complete Heyting algebra: [Differentiate](differentiate.md) → [Includes/Excludes](includes-excludes.md) → [Together/Either](together-either.md) / [Negate](negate.md) → [Induces](induces.md) → [Iterate](iterate.md) |
| II | Structural Stabilization | Modal closure: [Close/Open](close.md) → [Balance](balance.md) → [Necessary/Possible](necessary-possible.md) |
| III | Directed Dynamics | Comonadic [Flow](flow.md) → CoKleisli → stabilization → [Dynamic Residuation](dynamic-residuation.md) |
| IV | Geometric Cohesion | [Cohesive chain](cohesive-chain.md) (Shape ⊣ Discrete ⊣ Codiscrete ⊣ Global) → [Stratification](stratifying.md) |
| V | Emergent Containment | Self-contained modality: [States/Observables](states-observables.md) → [Conservation](conserving.md) → self-containment |

## Finer-grained articulations

Some treatments use finer divisions. The 9-phase view (one common articulation) maps to the five movements as:

- Movement I ← Phases 1–2 (Existential Coherence, Relational Coherence)
- Movement II ← Phases 3–4 (Sustained Relational Dynamics, Bounded Relational Coherence)
- Movement III ← Phase 5 (Reflexive Relational Coherence)
- Movement IV ← Phases 6–7 (Multirelational Coherence, Relational Field Coherence)
- Movement V ← Phases 8–9 (Relational Meta-Boundary Coherence, Meta-relational Coherence)

An 8-phase variant maps similarly:

- Movement I ← Phases I–II (Existential Differentiation, Relational Determination)
- Movement II ← Phase III (Reflexive Stability)
- Movement III ← Phases IV–V (Modal Induction, Comonadic Dynamics)
- Movement IV ← Phase VI (Geometric Reflection)
- Movement V ← Phases VII–VIII (Reflexive Physics, Reflexive Quantization)

The mature articulation is the five-movement structure. The flat-closure view (the [generative closure](generative-closure.md) operator $G$ applied to the seed) does not organize into movements at all — it presents the universe of acts as a single fixed point with no philosophical narrative overlay.
