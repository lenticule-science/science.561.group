The minimal condition that marks a thing as set apart from what it is not. Induced by [tension](tension.md); precedes [relating](relating.md).

## What it is

Difference is the condition of coherent separation produced by tension. With including and excluding held in mutual opposition, the thing emerges as definitely apart from its negation: $x \neq \text{not-}x$.

Difference is not directly produced by including or excluding alone. Each generates its own condition (inclusion, exclusion). It is the *tension* between them that produces difference — a structural distinction generated by the holding-together of opposed acts.

## Why it matters

Difference is the structural product of tension. Once it is established, relating becomes possible — the sustaining act that maintains the difference over time. Without difference, there is nothing for relating to relate.

In the formal vocabulary, difference is the substrate of [Includes / Excludes](includes-excludes.md): the partial order on Rel rests on the assumption that distinct recognitions remain distinct. Difference is what makes the field of recognition more than a single point.
