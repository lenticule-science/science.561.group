title: "Void"
kind: term
---
`Void = (Nothing Nothing)`

[Nothing](nothing.md) shows that Witness observes Something in two symmetric positions; folding that symmetry back on itself produces *Void*. The trace reuses Nothing as both witness and object, so the symmetry between the two appearances of Witness and Something collapses, yielding a closed loop.

This is a derived act in the chain — *not* the same as [Relationlessness](relationlessness.md), which is the originating absence prior to any act. Void here is a structural fold that emerges much later in the derivation, after Something and Nothing have been earned.
