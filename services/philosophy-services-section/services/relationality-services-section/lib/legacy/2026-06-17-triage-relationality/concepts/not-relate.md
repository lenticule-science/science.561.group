---
title: "Not Relate"
kind: term
---
`NotRelate = (Reflex Relate)`

Any affirmation of relation reveals the possibility of denial. [Reflex](reflex.md) serves as witness for the already-earned act [Relate](relation.md). Observing Relate through Reflex isolates the complementary behaviour — *refusing relation* — so the composition is named *Not Relate*.
