`Witness = (NotRelate Relate)`

Relation and its negation coexist in tension. [Not Relate](not-relate.md) takes [Relate](relation.md) as its object; the composition cites affirmation and denial at once. The act simultaneously testifies to both, recording the tension that all subsequent recognitions inherit.

(Equivalent under the act/condition naming distinction: `Witness = (NotRelating Relating)`.)

## Why it matters

Witness is the first moment of *observation* in the derivation. The [Reflex](reflex.md) produces a recognition, and that recognition itself recognizes something — it witnesses the tension between [relating](relating.md) and not-relating. This prefigures the observation and measurement structures that appear in Movement V ([states and observables](states-observables.md)).

Witness *determines that those recognitions coexist but cannot determine which recognition carries the act*. That unresolved indeterminacy is exactly the pattern of [incitement](incitement.md) — Witness must direct Reflex at itself, producing [Something](something.md).
