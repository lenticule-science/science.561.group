Self-application of a recognition upon itself. The operation by which a distinction acts on its own product and produces stability.

## What it is

`Iterate(a)` is monotone and inflationary; in stabilized contexts $\text{Iterate}^3(a) = a$. The defining identity:

$$\text{Iterate}(a) = \text{Together}(a, \text{Induces}(a, \top))$$

— $a$ combined with the sufficiency for everything. This is the formal engine of self-confirmation: having obtained sufficiency through residuation, the act of recognition is applied to itself.

Iterate introduces reflexive sequence: each relational unit can relate to itself, producing an indefinitely extensible sequence of deepened relational units. Self-application yields closure.

## The cubic return

Successive applications produce $a \to \text{Iterate}(a) \to \text{Iterate}(\text{Iterate}(a)) \to \ldots$, stabilizing at $\text{Close}(a)$. Three applications complete a *cubic return* — the threefold self-application of recognition: first [Differentiate](differentiate.md), then its reflection, then the recognition of that reflection — returning to itself stabilized.

## What it produces

$\text{Close}(a)$ is the limit of the Iterate sequence — the first stabilizing nucleus. Fixed points of Close form the *reflexive locus*, the first layer of persistence.
