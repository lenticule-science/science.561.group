# Triage Relationality (2026-06-17 archive capture)

2,019 files: the original single-author "relationality" formal
philosophy/mathematics research program — the corpus's Cone 2 (its own apex,
its own foundational concepts — relational entity, relational operation, dual
nuclei, nuclear quartet — building outward into the relational computer, the
semiotic universe, the generative comonad). Cone 2 sits *under* Cone 1 (the
standard-disciplines cone the rest of the library assumes): the standard
disciplines assume entities exist and then relate; relationality says the
relating is prior, entities are constituted by their relations.

## Known internal breakage

siblings that did **not** move with it — most importantly the parent
`triage/content/{speculative,non-speculative}/` trees and the
`foundational-ontology-research-brief.md` cited above, both of which remain
at the old `engineering-services-section` location. Those relative links will
now 404. Per this corpus's move discipline, breakage from a completed move is
accepted and left for a follow-up pass, not treated as a reason to defer the
move itself.
