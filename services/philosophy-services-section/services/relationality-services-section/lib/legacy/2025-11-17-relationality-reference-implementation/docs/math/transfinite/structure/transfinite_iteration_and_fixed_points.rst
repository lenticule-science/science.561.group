Transfinite iteration and fixed points
======================================

.. meta::
   :phase: someday

The transfinite iteration and fixed-point development will extend the
finite Reflex-step iteration and fixed-point schemas to ordinal-indexed
and transfinite chains of recognitions. It is intended to generalise:

- the Reflex-step iteration and eventual-equivalence results from
  ``math/infinite/structure/iteration_and_equivalence``; and
- the fixed-point existence schemas for monotone endomaps from
  ``math/infinite/structure/monotone_endomaps_and_fixed_points``.

At present this file is a placeholder describing the intended scope;
no formal transfinite theorems are stated yet.
