Proposition — Dual Interior from Closure
----------------------------------------

Suppose negation :math:`\neg(\cdot)` is an antitone operation
satisfying De Morgan-style laws with :math:`\wedge, \vee` (as in a
Heyting setting with relative negation). For any closure-like
:math:`\nu`, define :math:`\delta(x) := \neg \nu(\neg x)`. Then
:math:`\delta` is interior-like and satisfies the interior
compatibility laws.

**Proof.** Standard nucleus dualisation: antitonicity of
:math:`\neg` and De Morgan laws transfer closure properties to
interior properties under double application of :math:`\neg`. ∎
