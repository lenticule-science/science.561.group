Standard Model and Semantics
============================

Definition — ***Standard Model***
---------------------------------

Given a *:doc:`Relational Structure <math/infinite/structure/relational_structure>`*
(:class:`relationing.core.structure.RelationalStructure`) ***R***,
interpret:

- types as recognitions (elements of :math:`R`) with
  :math:`\wedge, \vee, \Rightarrow` as defined in the Structure spec;
- contexts as finite meets of their types;
- terms as monotone operations respecting constructors;
- ``ρ`` as the Reflex action (monotone endo-operation).
