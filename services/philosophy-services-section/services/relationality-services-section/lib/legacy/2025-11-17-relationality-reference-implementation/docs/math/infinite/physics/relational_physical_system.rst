Statement
---------

***Relational Physical System*** (*RelationalPhysicalSystem*) is the
bundle of a *Relational Geometry*
(:class:`relationing.core.geometry.RelationalGeometry`) with a flow,
chosen nuclei, and observables:

- a carrier *Relational Structure*
  (:class:`relationing.core.structure.RelationalStructure`) with
  Reflex and flow-compatible nuclei;
- a *Relational Flow* describing evolution on recognitions; and
- a family of Observables describing measurements or readings.

The basic well-formedness requirement is that evolution and nuclei
respect the underlying RelationalGeometry axioms, and that observables
are defined on all states. The detailed relational laws and profile
specialisations are developed in the surrounding Infinite Physics
notes; this file serves as the atomic definition of
***Relational Physical System***.
