Finite Relational Geometry
==========================

Finite Relational Geometry notes will specialise the infinite Geometry
definitions to finite nuclei, flows, and profile carriers. Concretely,
they describe how to filter an infinite ***Relational Geometry*** and
its nuclei/flow along finite Filters and Disciplines to obtain finite
substructures realised by the library helpers.
