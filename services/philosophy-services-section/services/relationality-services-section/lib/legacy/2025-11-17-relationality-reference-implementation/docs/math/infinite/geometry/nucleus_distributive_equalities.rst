Proposition — Nucleus Distributive Equalities
---------------------------------------------

For a closure-like :math:`\nu` and recognitions :math:`x, y`,
:math:`\nu(x \wedge \nu(y)) = \nu(x \wedge y)` and
:math:`\nu(\nu(x) \vee y) = \nu(x \vee y)`. Dual equalities hold for
an interior-like :math:`\nu`.

**Proof.** Use monotonicity, idempotence, and the algebraic
compatibility axioms; both directions follow from
:math:`\nu(y) \ge y` (closure-like) and
:math:`\nu(\nu(y)) = \nu(y)`. ∎
