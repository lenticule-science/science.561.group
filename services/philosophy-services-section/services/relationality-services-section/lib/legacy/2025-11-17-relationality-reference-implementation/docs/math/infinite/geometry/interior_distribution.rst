Proposition — Interior Distribution
-----------------------------------

For an interior-like :math:`\nu` and recognitions :math:`x, y`,
:math:`\nu(x \wedge y) = \nu(x) \wedge \nu(y)` and
:math:`\nu(x \vee y) \le \nu(x) \vee \nu(y)`. If :math:`x, y` are
:math:`\nu`-open, then :math:`\nu(x \vee y) = x \vee y`.

**Proof.** By the interior compatibility axioms and idempotence. ∎
