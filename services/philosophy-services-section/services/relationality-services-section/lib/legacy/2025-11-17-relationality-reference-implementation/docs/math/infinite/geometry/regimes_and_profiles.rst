Regimes and Fixed-Point Profiles
================================

.. meta::
   :xref-spec: math/infinite/geometry/index (Regimes and Profiles)

This file isolates the notions of ***Regimes*** and finite profiles
from the Relational Geometry notes:

- Regimes as fixed-point sets of Disciplines or Filters; and
- finite profile constructions induced by Filters or Disciplines on
  finite carriers.

The full development of Regimes and ProfileGeometry remains in
``docs/math/infinite/geometry/index.rst``; this file provides an
atomic anchor for the library-level Regime and Profile helpers and
their tests.
