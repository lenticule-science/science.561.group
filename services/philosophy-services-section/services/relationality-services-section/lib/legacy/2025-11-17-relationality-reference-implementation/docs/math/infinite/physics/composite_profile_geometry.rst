Composite ProfileGeometry
=========================

Proposition — Composite ProfileGeometry
---------------------------------------

Let each :math:`\mathrm{Filter}_i` commute with all nuclei
:math:`\{\nu_j\}` and the flow :math:`F`. Then the restricted nuclei
:math:`\nu_j|_{\mathrm{Profile}(\{\mathrm{Filter}_i\})}` and restricted
flow :math:`F|_{\mathrm{Profile}(\{\mathrm{Filter}_i\})}` are
well-defined and satisfy the nucleus and flow axioms. Thus

.. math::

   \mathrm{ProfileGeom}(\{\mathrm{Filter}_i\}) :=\\
   (\mathrm{Profile}(\{\mathrm{Filter}_i\}),
    \le, \wedge, \vee, \Rightarrow, \bot, \top;\ \{\nu_j|\}, F|)

is a *Relational Geometry*.
