Library-level math fragments
============================

This section collects small, library-level mathematical fragments
whose executable witnesses live under ``relationing.library.*``.

Currently these fragments are documented under the finite math
specialisations in ``docs/math/finite``; see in particular the finite
proof-search and model notes and their unary refinements.
