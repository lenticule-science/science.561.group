Statement
---------

***Profile Physics*** (*ProfilePhysicalSystem*) is the restriction of
 a *Relational Physical System* (*RelationalPhysicalSystem*) to a
*Profile Geometry* (*ProfileGeom*) induced by a Filter:

- states live in a finite profile :math:`\mathrm{Profile}(\mathrm{Filter})`;
- evolution is given by the restricted flow :math:`F|_{\mathrm{Profile}}`;
- nuclei are the restricted nuclei :math:`\nu_i|_{\mathrm{Profile}}`; and
- observables are restricted (or redefined) on the profile.

These structures form the Profile-level instances of
***Relational Physical System*** over Profile Geometry. The detailed
reconstruction story and tower results are developed in the Infinite
Physics notes; this file states the ProfilePhysics notion that those
results refer to.
