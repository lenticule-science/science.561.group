Finite Unary Disciplines
========================

.. meta::
   :xref-spec: math/finite/library/structures/unary_triangle_and_disciplines

This note records that the unary ``A ⇒ A`` operators studied in the
finite unary triangle are *Geometry-level* entities: they are simply
Disciplines (and endomaps) on finite RelationalGeometry carriers.

The detailed finite spec and its tests are currently documented under
``docs/math/finite/library/structures/unary_triangle_and_disciplines.rst``,
which describes how unary λ_R operators induce compatible Disciplines
and how those Disciplines interact with Reflex, nuclei, and flows on
finite carriers.
