Proposition — Closure Absorption
--------------------------------

For a closure-like :math:`\nu` and recognitions :math:`x, y`,
:math:`x \wedge \nu(y) \le \nu(x \wedge y)` and
:math:`\nu(x) \vee y \le \nu(x \vee y)`. Moreover, if :math:`y` is
:math:`\nu`-closed, then
:math:`x \wedge y \le \nu(x \wedge y) = x \wedge y` and
:math:`\nu(x) \vee y = \nu(x \vee y) \ge y`.

**Proof.** From algebraic compatibility and idempotence. ∎
