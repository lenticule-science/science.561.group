Finite Relational Physics
=========================

Finite Relational Physics notes specialise the infinite Physics
definitions to finite RelationalPhysicalSystem and ProfilePhysics
carriers. They describe how to take an infinite
***Relational Physical System*** together with Filters/ProfileGeometry
data and restrict it to finite profiles of the system that our code
and tests instantiate concretely.
