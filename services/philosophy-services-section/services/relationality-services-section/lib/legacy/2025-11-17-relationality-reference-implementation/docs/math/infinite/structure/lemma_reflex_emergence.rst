Reflex Emergence Lemma
======================

Informal picture
----------------

Assume *Relationlessness*. Then there exists at least one
recognition; consequently there exists an act that brings a
recognition forth from *Relationlessness*. We call this act
***Reflex***.

By the Articulation theorem, articulating *Relationlessness* induces a
recognition (an *Exclusion Recognition*). Therefore the field is not
empty of recognitions. Let *Reflex* be the minimal act witnessing this
genesis: the act that, from *Relationlessness*, yields that first
recognition.


Formal statement
----------------

Assume *Relationlessness* and the Articulation theorem. Then there
exists at least one recognition; consequently there exists an act
that brings a recognition forth from *Relationlessness*. We call this
act *Reflex*.

In particular, there is a minimal witness ***Reflex Emergence***
(``ReflexEmergence``) from the Relationlessness void to a first
recognition.


Proof
-----

By the Articulation theorem, articulating *Relationlessness* induces a
recognition (an *Exclusion Recognition*). Therefore the field is not
empty of recognitions: the collection *R* of recognitions is
nonempty.

Define *Reflex* to be a minimal act witnessing this genesis: an act
that, from *Relationlessness*, yields that first recognition. By
construction, any Articulation from *Relationlessness* produces at
least one *Exclusion Recognition* distinguishing interior from
exterior; *Reflex Emergence* is the minimal such Articulation. :math:`\square`
