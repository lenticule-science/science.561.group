Join
====

Definition — ***Join***
----------------------

.. meta::
   :induced-by: math/infinite/structure/preorder_and_order

Let a *Relational Structure* (``RelationalStructure``) *R* carry a
preorder :math:`\leq` on its recognitions. For recognitions
:math:`x, y \in R`, a *join* :math:`x \vee y` is a least recognition
:math:`z` such that :math:`x \leq z` and :math:`y \leq z`.
