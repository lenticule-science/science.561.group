Lemma — Residuation Law
-----------------------

For all :math:`a, b, c \in R`, we have
:math:`c \leq (a \Rightarrow b)` iff :math:`c \wedge a \leq b`.

**Proof.** Immediate from the definition of :math:`\Rightarrow` as the
greatest such *c*; the two inequalities are equivalent by
construction. ∎
