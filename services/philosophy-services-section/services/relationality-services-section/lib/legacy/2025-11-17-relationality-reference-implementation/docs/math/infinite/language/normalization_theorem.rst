Normalization Theorem
======================

Theorem — Normalization
-----------------------

Every well-typed ``λ_R`` term reduces to a normal form.

**Proof (via reducibility candidates).** We work with closed terms;
the open-term case is handled by environments mapping variables to
reducible terms. The detailed argument proceeds via strong
normalisation, reducibility candidates, and a fundamental theorem of
logical relations as sketched in the surrounding fragments. ∎
