Bounded Distributive Lattice
============================

Proposition — *Bounded Distributive Lattice*
-------------------------------------------

.. meta::
   :induced-by: math/infinite/structure/preorder_and_order,
                math/infinite/structure/definition_meet,
                math/infinite/structure/definition_join

Let a *Relational Structure* (``RelationalStructure``) *R* have a
preorder :math:`\leq` together with finite meets and joins
(:math:`\wedge`, :math:`\vee`, :math:`\bot`, :math:`\top`) as in
*Meet* and *Join*.

Then :math:`(R, \leq, \wedge, \vee, \bot, \top)` forms a *bounded
lattice*: both :math:`\wedge` and :math:`\vee` are commutative and
associative and satisfy the absorption laws. When distributivity of
meets over joins (or dually) holds, :math:`R` is a *bounded
distributive lattice*.

*Proof.* Standard order-theoretic arguments using the universal
properties of finite meets and joins. :math:`\square`
