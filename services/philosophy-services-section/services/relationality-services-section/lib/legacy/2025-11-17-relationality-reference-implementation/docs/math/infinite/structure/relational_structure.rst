Relational Structure
====================

Definition — ***Relational Structure*** (*RelationalStructure*)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

A *Relational Structure* (*RelationalStructure*) is a tuple

.. math::

   \mathrm{RStruct} = (R, \le, \wedge, \vee, \Rightarrow, \bot, \top, \mathrm{Reflex})

such that:

1. :math:`(R, \le, \wedge, \vee, \bot, \top)` forms a bounded
   distributive lattice;
2. :math:`\Rightarrow` satisfies the Residuation Law making
   :math:`(R, \le, \wedge, \vee, \Rightarrow, \bot, \top)` a Heyting
   algebra;
3. a designated ***Reflex*** endomap (when provided) is monotone and
   interprets the :math:`\rho`‑operator.
