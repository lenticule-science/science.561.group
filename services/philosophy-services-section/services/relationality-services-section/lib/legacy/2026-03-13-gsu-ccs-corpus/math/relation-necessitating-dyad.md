### Definition — The Relation–Necessitating Dyad {#relation-necessitating-dyad}

The **relation–necessitating dyad** pairs two aspects of every structural
connection in $R$: the connection as a static geometric fact
(relation) and the constraining force that connection exerts on
the structures it links (necessitating, §necessitating).

The prefix order $t \leq t'$ illustrates both poles simultaneously. As a
relation, it IS the comparison: it specifies which pairs of histories stand in
prefix position. This is a geometric fact about the history monoid — a static
partial order that does not change as the mathematics proceeds. As necessitating,
the prefix order DOES something to the fibers: it compels the existence of
restriction maps $\rho_{t,t'} : H_{t'} \to H_t$, and it compels those maps to
compose. The prefix order both IS a relation and necessitates a consequence.
These are two aspects of the same structure, not two separate structures.

The nucleus pair $(\sigma_t, \Delta_t)$ illustrates the same split at the
fiber level. Each nucleus IS a connecting structure: $\sigma_t$ connects a
proposition to its saturation-stable upper bound; $\Delta_t$ connects a
proposition to its transfer-stable upper bound. These connections are relations
— mappings that specify how each element of $H_t$ relates to its closure.
Each nucleus also necessitates: $\sigma_t(a) = a$ is not a choice but a
compelled stability once $\sigma_t$ is given; the fixed-point layer
$\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t)$ is entailed by the nucleus, not posited alongside it.
Again, one structure; two aspects.

The commutation topology $J$ repeats the pattern at the sheaf level. The collection of
covering sieves IS the relational structure that specifies which sub-history
families cohere. It necessitates the gluing property: compatibility over a
cover forces a unique global section.

The dyad is a being/becoming pair: relation is the being-pole (what the
connection IS — the static geometric structure that can be read off without
further development) and necessitating is the becoming-pole (what the
connection DOES — the modal force it actively exerts on what must follow).
The being-pole can be described without describing its effects; the
becoming-pole cannot be described without presupposing the connection that
exerts it. Relation is prior; necessitating is what relation does when the
construction is set in motion.
