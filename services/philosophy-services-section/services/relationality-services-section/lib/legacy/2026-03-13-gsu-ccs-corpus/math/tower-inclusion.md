### Definition — Tower Inclusion {#tower-inclusion}

The hyperverse tower (§hyperverse-tower) is a sequence of relational universes
$R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)} \hookrightarrow R^{(2)} \hookrightarrow \cdots$ with a
specified inclusion morphism at each step. The **tower inclusion** names this morphism and
records its defining properties.

#### Definition {#def-tower-inclusion}

For each $n \geq 0$, the **tower inclusion** is the universe morphism
$$
\iota_n \;:\; R^{(n)} \;\hookrightarrow\; R^{(n+1)} \;:=\; U_\mathcal{H}(R^{(n)})
$$
given by the canonical embedding of $R^{(n)}$ into the image of one application of the
hyperverse closure operator $U_\mathcal{H}$ (§meta-universe-closure).

#### Properties {#tower-inclusion-properties}

The tower inclusion is not merely a set-theoretic inclusion. It is a morphism with
four distinct structural properties:

**1. Universe morphism.** $\iota_n$ is a morphism in the category of relational universes
$\mathbf{RU}$ (§universe-morphism): a geometric morphism of Grothendieck toposes
$(\iota_n)_* \dashv (\iota_n)^*$ that preserves the history site structure.

**2. Essential morphism.** $\iota_n$ is **essential** (§modal-adjoint-chain): it has both
a left adjoint $(\iota_n)_!$ and a right adjoint $(\iota_n)_*$ in addition to the
adjoint pair of the geometric morphism. This makes the modal adjoint chain at each level
a four-term sequence: $(\iota_n)_! \dashv (\iota_n)^* \dashv (\iota_n)_* \dashv (\iota_n)^!$.
The Beck-Chevalley conditions (§beck-chevalley-tower) hold: the adjoints commute with
base change along the tower inclusions.

**3. Nuclear preservation.** $\iota_n$ commutes with both nuclei:
$$
\iota_n \circ \sigma^{(n)} \;=\; \sigma^{(n+1)} \circ \iota_n,
\qquad
\iota_n \circ \Delta^{(n)} \;=\; \Delta^{(n+1)} \circ \iota_n.
$$
Equivalently, $\iota_n$ maps stable propositions at level $n$ to stable propositions at
level $n+1$: $\iota_n(H_t^{(n)*}) \subseteq H_t^{(n+1)*}$.

**4. Fiber-lifting.** For each history $t \in T$, the tower inclusion induces a
Heyting algebra morphism on fiber algebras:
$$
(\iota_n)_t \;:\; H_t^{(n)} \;\hookrightarrow\; H_t^{(n+1)}.
$$
The fiber $H_t^{(n+1)}$ is strictly richer than $H_t^{(n)}$ in general: new one-step
extensions of $t$ exist in $R^{(n+1)}$ (generated by $U_\mathcal{H}$), making the
transfer nucleus $\Delta^{(n+1)}_t$ more constraining and adding new propositions that
are witnessed by the new extensions but not by extensions visible at level $n$.

The tower-fiber-seeding theorem (§tower-fiber-seeding) says more precisely:
$(H_t^{(n)})^*$ is canonically identified with the free carrier $H_t^{(n+1)}$ of the
fiber nuclear quartet at level $n+1$ (§fiber-nuclear-quartet). The stable layer of level
$n$ is the starting point for level $n+1$.

#### What the Tower Inclusion Adds {#tower-inclusion-content}

At each step $n \to n+1$, the tower inclusion adds exactly what $U_\mathcal{H}$ generates
from $R^{(n)}$: new objects (relational universes generated by one meta-closure step), new
morphisms between them, and new histories witnessing relationships between those objects.
For any $\mathbf{U} \in R^{(n)}$, the new objects are built from the atoms of $\mathbf{U}$
(§generative-basis) via the meta-generative act $U_\mathcal{H}$.

The "residue" — the content of $R^{(n+1)}$ not already in $\iota_n(R^{(n)})$ — is the
collection of genuinely new universe morphisms and relational structures that did not exist
at level $n$. It is precisely this residue that creates the unsettled elements in
$H_t^{(n+1)} \setminus (H_t^{(n)})^*$: propositions about the new objects that the level-$n$
nuclei had no way to address.

#### Colimit and Convergence {#tower-inclusion-colimit}

The tower inclusions form a filtered diagram in $\mathbf{RU}$:
$$
R^{(0)} \xrightarrow{\iota_0} R^{(1)} \xrightarrow{\iota_1} R^{(2)} \xrightarrow{\iota_2} \cdots
$$
The colimit of this diagram is the Relational Hyperverse:
$$
\mathcal{H} \;=\; \varinjlim_n R^{(n)}.
$$
Each tower inclusion is a component of the universal cone: $\iota_{\infty,n} : R^{(n)} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{H}$
is the canonical map from the $n$-th stage to the colimit. The hyperverse is the
"limit" of all the tower inclusions assembled — the structure where every finite
tower step has been taken simultaneously.
