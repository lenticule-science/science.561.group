### Construction — Universe Closure at the Meta-Level {#meta-universe-closure}

The **meta-level Universe Closure** $U_\mathcal{H}$ is the analogue of $U_G$
(§closure-operator) one level up: where $U_G$ closes a subclass of
*objects inside* a fixed topos $R$, $U_\mathcal{H}$ closes a relational
universe $\mathbf{U}$ under the full nuclear quartet cycle applied to its own
internal algebra, producing a strictly richer relational universe as output.

#### The Tensor Product of Relational Universes {#ru-tensor}

Before defining $U_\mathcal{H}$, we need a monoidal structure on $\mathbf{RU}$.
Given two relational universes
$\mathbf{U} = (T_\mathbf{U}, J_\mathbf{U}, H_\mathbf{U})$ and
$\mathbf{V} = (T_\mathbf{V}, J_\mathbf{V}, H_\mathbf{V})$, define their
**tensor product**
$$
\mathbf{U} \otimes \mathbf{V}
\;:=\;
\bigl(T_\mathbf{U} \times T_\mathbf{V},\; J_\mathbf{U} \boxtimes J_\mathbf{V},\;
H_{(t,s)} := H_{\mathbf{U},t} \otimes_{\mathbf{HA}} H_{\mathbf{V},s}\bigr),
$$
where $T_\mathbf{U} \times T_\mathbf{V}$ is the product history category with
componentwise order and monoidal structure, $J_\mathbf{U} \boxtimes J_\mathbf{V}$
is the product topology, and $H_{(t,s)}$ is the tensor product of Heyting algebras.
The unit object is the **trivial universe** $\mathbf{1}$ with $T_\mathbf{1} = \{e\}$
(one history) and $H_\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{2}$ (the two-element Heyting algebra).

This makes $(\mathbf{RU}, \otimes, \mathbf{1})$ a symmetric monoidal category.
The **prefix order** on $\mathbf{RU}$ is: $\mathbf{U} \leq \mathbf{V}$ if and only
if there exists a relational universe $\mathbf{W}$ such that
$\mathbf{V} \cong \mathbf{U} \otimes \mathbf{W}$. This mirrors the prefix order on
the history category $T$ (§history): a history $t \leq t'$ iff $t' = t \star u$ for
some extension $u$. The structure $(\mathbf{RU}, \leq, \otimes)$ is itself a
history system at the meta-level.

#### The Topology on $\mathbf{RU}$ {#ru-topology}

The **profile topology** $J_\mathbf{RU}$ on $\mathbf{RU}$ is the Grothendieck
topology generated by the covers
$$
\mathbf{U} \;\text{is covered by}\;\{\mathbf{U}_i \hookrightarrow \mathbf{U}\}_{i \in I}
\quad\text{whenever}\quad
\bigsqcup_i \mathbf{U}_i \;\text{is dense in } \mathbf{U},
$$
where density means the embedding $\bigsqcup_i T_{\mathbf{U}_i} \hookrightarrow T_\mathbf{U}$
covers all finite-depth histories (§universe-as-object). This is the meta-level
analogue of the commutation topology $J$ on $T$.

#### Definition — $U_\mathcal{H}$ {#def-universe-closure-meta}

$U_\mathcal{H}$ is a **step operator in a cumulative construction**, not an endofunctor
on a fixed $\mathbf{RU}$. At each level $n$, it takes the current-level universe
category $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ (all relational universes constructed up to level $n$) and
produces the next level:
$$
U_\mathcal{H}^{(n)}
\;:=\;
\mathbf{Sh}\!\bigl(\mathbf{RU}^{(n)},\; J^{(n+1)}\bigr),
$$
with the following fiber data over an object $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$:

- **Trace objects**: $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ with the prefix order and tensor product of
  §ru-tensor serves as the meta-history category for level $n+1$.

- **Fiber algebras**: the fiber at $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the
  **fixed fiber** $H^*_\mathbf{V}$ of stable propositions inside $\mathbf{V}$:
  $$
  H^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{V} \;:=\; H^\ast_\mathbf{V}
  \;=\; \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_\mathbf{V}) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_\mathbf{V}),
  $$
  the doubly-stable Heyting subalgebra of $\mathbf{V}$ (§fixed-fiber).

- **Reindexing**: a morphism $(\phi, \alpha) : \mathbf{V} \to \mathbf{W}$ in
  $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ induces a restriction map $H^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{W} \to H^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{V}$
  via the pullback of $\alpha$ to the fixed fibers (§universe-morphism, Nuclei Are
  Automatically Preserved).

- **Nuclei**: $\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{V} = \mathrm{id}$ and
  $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{V} = \mathrm{id}$ on $H^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{V}$:
  $H^*_\mathbf{V}$ is already doubly-stable, so both nuclei act as the identity.

**Level zero.** $\mathbf{RU}^{(0)} = \mathbf{RU}$ is the full category of all
relational universes built from primitive history data. Applying $U_\mathcal{H}$ at
level 0 gives
$$
R^{(1)} \;=\; U_\mathcal{H}^{(0)} \;=\; \mathbf{Sh}\!\bigl(\mathbf{RU},\; J_\mathbf{RU}\bigr).
$$
The formula in earlier references to "$U_\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{U}) = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}, J_\mathbf{RU})$"
describes this level-0 step; $\mathbf{RU}$ there means $\mathbf{RU}^{(0)}$, not a
fixed ambient category independent of level.

**The canonical embedding** $R^{(n)} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)}$ is the Yoneda embedding:
$R^{(n)}$ (as an object of $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$) maps to the representable sheaf
$y(R^{(n)}) \in R^{(n+1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}, J^{(n+1)})$.

#### Why U_H Has the Right Properties {#universe-closure-properties}

**Extensivity.** Each $R^{(n)} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)}$ is the Yoneda embedding,
which is fully faithful. Every level embeds properly into the next.

**The site grows strictly.** $\mathbf{RU}^{(n+1)}$ strictly contains $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$:
the new objects are the level-$(n+1)$ relational universes, in particular $R^{(n+1)}$
itself, which were not constructible from the data at level $n$. This is the
meta-level analogue of the depth filtration within a single universe (§depth-filtration).

**Fixed points are self-universal.** A fixed point $\mathbf{F}$ — satisfying
$U_\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{F}) \cong \mathbf{F}$ — has a site that is closed under the
operation of adding "all relational universes constructible from $\mathbf{F}$." Since
$\mathbf{F}$ is its own output, the site for $\mathbf{F}$ must already contain all
relational universes at all finite levels. This makes $\mathbf{F}$ self-universal in
the strong sense of §relational-hyperverse.

#### Relation to the Object-Level $U_G$ {#closure-comparison}

The object-level $U_G$ (§closure-operator) operates on subclasses of
$\mathrm{Obj}(R)$ within a fixed topos $R$: it is the closure operator that
produces $R$ from its atoms in one complete pass.

$U_\mathcal{H}$ operates one level up: it takes the *entire* relational
universe $R$ as input and produces a *new* relational universe as output, by
treating $R$'s structure as the algebra for a fresh application of the
nuclear quartet cycle. The relation is:
$$
U_\mathcal{H}(R) \;=\; \text{``one full nuclear quartet cycle applied to } R\text{''},
$$
just as $U_G(X)$ within $R$ is "one full nuclear quartet closure of $X$ within $R$."

Both operators satisfy the same abstract schema: they are closure operators on
a category that is itself a history system, closing subclasses (or sub-universes)
under all structurally-permitted operations. The fixed points of $U_G$ are
$U_G$-closed subclasses of $\mathrm{Obj}(R)$; the fixed points of $U_\mathcal{H}$
are self-universal relational universes.

#### Set-Theoretic Caveat {#closure-set-theory}

$\mathbf{RU}$ is a *large* category — its objects are all relational universes,
which do not form a set. Working with $\mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}, J_\mathbf{RU})$
therefore requires one universe level higher than the objects of $R$. We follow
the convention of §convention-foundations and work in a background set theory with
Grothendieck universes: $\mathbf{U}$ (for first-turn data) and $\mathbf{U}^+$
(for $\mathbf{RU}$ and $U_\mathcal{H}$). The Relational Hyperverse $\mathcal{H}$
is a $\mathbf{U}^+$-object; its study from the external perspective requires
$\mathbf{U}^{++}$, and so on indefinitely — the universe stratification is
itself aperiodic in the same sense as §aperiodicity.
