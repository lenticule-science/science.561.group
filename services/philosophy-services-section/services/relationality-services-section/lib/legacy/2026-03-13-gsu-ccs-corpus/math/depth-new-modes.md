### Definition — The Depth-New-Modes Sheaf {#depth-new-modes}

The **depth-new-modes sheaf** at depth step $k$ is
$$
N_{[k]} \;:=\; E_0^{[k+1]} \big/ E_0^{[k]},
$$
a sheaf of $K$-modules on the canonical history site $(T,J)$.

#### Construction {#depth-new-modes-construction}

The depth filtration $R_{[0]} \subset R_{[1]} \subset \cdots \subset R$ (§depth-filtration)
of the relational universe refines which propositions are in scope at each depth. As
depth increases, the stable fixed fiber grows: $H_t^{[k]*} \subseteq H_t^{[k+1]*}$ at
each history $t$ (new propositions settle at each depth step). The stable mode sheaves
$E_0^{[k]}(t) = K^{H_t^{[k]*}}$ therefore form a growing family on $(T,J)$, with
fiberwise inclusions $E_0^{[k]}(t) \hookrightarrow E_0^{[k+1]}(t)$.

This gives a short exact sequence of sheaves on $(T,J)$:
$$
0 \;\to\; E_0^{[k]} \;\to\; E_0^{[k+1]} \;\to\; N_{[k]} \;\to\; 0.
$$

Sections: $N_{[k]}(t) = K^{H_t^{[k+1]*} \setminus H_t^{[k]*}}$, freely generated by the
propositions first settling at depth $k+1$.

#### Fiberwise Dimension — The Fibonacci Step {#depth-new-modes-fibonacci}

By the Nuclear Fibonacci Recurrence (§nuclear-fibonacci-recurrence), the fiberwise
stable-mode counts $d_k := |H_t^{[k]*}|$ satisfy $d_{k+1} = d_k + d_{k-1}$. Therefore:
$$
\dim_K N_{[k]}(t) \;=\; d_{k+1} - d_k \;=\; d_{k-1}.
$$

Each depth step contributes exactly $d_{k-1}$ new stable modes per fiber.

#### The Presheaf and the Sheaf {#depth-new-modes-presheaf-vs-sheaf}

The sections formula $N_{[k]}(t) = K^{H_t^{[k+1]*} \setminus H_t^{[k]*}}$ describes
the **raw new-modes presheaf** $N_{[k]}^{\mathrm{raw}}$ (§raw-new-modes-presheaf):
the pointwise cokernel with all restriction maps zero. The depth-new-modes sheaf
$N_{[k]}$ is the sheafification of this presheaf.

The two objects may differ. Sheafification imposes the covering compatibility of
$(T,J)$; with zero restriction maps, covered objects may acquire different fibers or
have their fibers forced to zero. Whether $N_{[k]}(t) \cong N_{[k]}^{\mathrm{raw}}(t)$
for all $t$ — the Sheafification Fiber Preservation question — is open (§raw-new-modes-presheaf).

**Local versus global.** The fiberwise dimension $\dim_K N_{[k]}(t) = d_{k-1}$ (if
fiber preservation holds) is a local datum at each history $t$. The global cohomological
quantity $\dim_K H^1(T,J;\,N_{[k]})$ is a separate number, depending on the topology.
The equality $\dim H^1_{\mathrm{depth}}(k) = d_{k-1}$ is a claim to be proved, not a
direct consequence of the fiber dimension formula.

#### Level Contrast {#depth-new-modes-contrast}

The hyperverse-tower analog is §hyperverse-new-modes: there the "step" moves
between universes $R^{(k)} \hookrightarrow R^{(k+1)}$ rather than between depths within
a single universe, and the two sheaves live on different base toposes.
