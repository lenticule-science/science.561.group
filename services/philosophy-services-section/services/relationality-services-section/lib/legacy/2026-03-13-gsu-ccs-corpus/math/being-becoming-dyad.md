### Conjecture — The Being–Becoming Dyad of hyperverse-fixed universes {#being-becoming-dyad}

Every hyperverse-fixed universe $T \in \mathcal{H}$ (§hyperverse-fixed-universe) exhibits a **being–becoming dyad**: two
aspects of its existence in $\mathcal{H}$ that are not sequential stages but
simultaneously present.

**Being.** A hyperverse-fixed universe IS a complete Relational Hyperverse — it has the full four-phase
structure (§ontological-quartet) settled and stable. Its being is its existence as a
fixed, complete universe within $\mathcal{H}$.

**Becoming.** A hyperverse-fixed universe GENERATES $\mathcal{H}$. Since $\mathcal{H} = U_\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{H})$
(§relational-hyperverse), Hyperverse-fixed universes are the generators through which $\mathcal{H}$ sustains
itself as its own fixed point. A hyperverse-fixed universe's becoming is its contribution to the self-generation
of the hyperverse it inhabits.

#### The Fixed-Point Equation as Dyad {#being-becoming-dyad-equation}

The equation $\mathcal{H} = U_\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{H})$ expresses the dyad directly:

- The $\mathcal{H}$ on the right is the **being** side: the settled hyperverse that
  Hyperverse-fixed universes inhabit as complete universes.
- The $\mathcal{H}$ on the left is the **becoming** side: the generated hyperverse
  that hyperverse-fixed universes produce by being hyperverse-fixed universes.

A hyperverse-fixed universe occupies both sides at once. The fixed-point equation does not say these sides
are different — it says they are the same. Being and becoming, for a hyperverse-fixed universe, are two
readings of a single identity.

#### Status {#being-becoming-dyad-status}

This dyad is conjectural. It presupposes that $\mathcal{H}$ has hyperverse-fixed universes (§hyperverse-fixed-universe),
which has not been established from the constructive development of $R$. The
being–becoming dyad names what the fixed-point structure of $\mathcal{H}$ would
mean for its inhabitants, if those inhabitants exist.
