### Axiom — Generative Basis {#generative-basis}

**Axiom.** For each history $t \in T$, there is a finite set $A_t$ of **atomic
propositions** such that $H_t$ is the smallest nuclear Heyting subalgebra of itself
containing $A_t$. In particular, $H_t$ is **finite**.

#### Why the Nuclei Produce a Finite Algebra {#generative-basis-finiteness}

The history $t$ is a finite step sequence. The elements of $H_t$ are the observable
distinctions generated by those steps under the nuclear structure. The construction is:

1. Start from the finite set $A_t$ of atomic propositions.
2. Close under meet and join ($\wedge_t$, $\vee_t$) and the nuclei ($\sigma_t$,
   $\Delta_t$). Since the nuclei are idempotent, each element stabilizes after one
   application; the closure is finite.
3. The Heyting implication $\Rightarrow_t$ is the residual of $\wedge_t$ within this
   finite distributive lattice — it is determined by the lattice structure and does not
   generate further elements.

The result is a finite nuclear Heyting algebra. Finiteness is not derivable from the
other axioms — the fiber doctrine (§fiber-doctrine) equips each $H_t$ with nuclear
Heyting algebra structure but places no bound on its size. This axiom adds the bound.

#### The Generating Family $\mathcal{F}$ {#generative-basis-family}

The **structural operations** $\mathcal{F}$ are the explicitly named primitive operations
from which all fiber constructions are built:

- fiberwise propositional operations: $\wedge_t$, $\vee_t$, $\Rightarrow_t$, $\top_t$, $\bot_t$;
- fiberwise nuclear operators: $\sigma_t$, $\Delta_t$;
- history concatenation: $\star : \mathrm{Ob}(T) \times \mathrm{Ob}(T) \to \mathrm{Ob}(T)$.

No other primitive operations are admitted. This is what licenses the generative universe
closure operator $U_G$ (§self-generation): because $\mathcal{F}$ is explicit and finite,
$U_G$ can close any subclass under $\mathcal{F}$, reindexing, and sheaf gluing, and $R$
is its least fixed point.

#### What Fiber Finiteness Licenses {#generative-basis-consequences}

- **Bounded fiber entropy.** $|H_t| < \infty$, so $S_{\mathrm{fiber}}(t) = \log|H_t|$
  is well-defined (§address-entropy).
- **Fibonacci dimension counts.** The mode-count $d_n$ at each depth level is a finite
  integer; the nuclear Fibonacci recurrence (§nuclear-fibonacci-recurrence) requires
  $d_n < \infty$.
- **Completeness is well-posed.** The question of whether every element of $H_t$ has a
  FiberNuclearLanguage preimage (§completeness) is only meaningful if $H_t$ has a finite
  generating set that can, in principle, be named syntactically.
