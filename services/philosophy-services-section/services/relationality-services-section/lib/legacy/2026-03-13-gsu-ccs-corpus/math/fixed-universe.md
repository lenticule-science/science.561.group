### Definition — Fixed Universe {#fixed-universe}

The **fixed universe** of $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the joint fixed-point
algebra of both meta-nuclei on the universe fiber (§universe-fiber):
$$
(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^* \;=\; \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \cap
\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \;\subseteq\; H_\mathbf{U}^*.
$$

An element of $(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$ is a proposition about $\mathbf{U}$ that is
simultaneously saturation-stable (fixed by the inter-universe relational context,
§saturation-stable-universe) and transfer-stable (fixed by all future tower extensions,
§transfer-stable-universe). These are the propositions for which neither meta-nucleus has
any further work to do: the content of $\mathbf{U}$ that has fully settled at the
meta-level.

#### Fixed Universe and Universe Closure {#fixed-universe-closure}

The joint meta-projection $\pi^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} = \sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} \circ
\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ is a nucleus on $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ with fixed-point set
$(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$ (§joint-projection, [CommutativeNuclearSquare](commutative-nuclear-square.md)). It is the canonical reflection
of $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ onto the fixed universe.

The fixed universe is operationally identified with the universe closure $U_{G_\mathbf{U}}$
of $\mathbf{U}$: the operation that generates the next level from $\mathbf{U}$'s atoms.
The doubly-stable layer $(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$ is where this generation has reached its
fixed point — where $U_{G_\mathbf{U}}(\mathbf{U}) = \mathbf{U}$ holds and the closure
has nothing further to produce.

The meta-transfer nucleus $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ is the forward-facing component:
it stabilizes what the next tower extension will demand. The meta-saturation nucleus
$\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ is the backward-facing component: it stabilizes what the
current inter-universe context has already determined. The fixed universe is the
intersection: what both directions have fully settled.

#### When the Fixed Universe Equals the Universe Fiber {#fixed-universe-thing}

A universe $\mathbf{U}$ is a **hyperverse-fixed universe** (§hyperverse-fixed-universe) when
$(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^* = H_\mathbf{U}^*$, i.e., when the meta-nuclear-square at
$\mathbf{U}$ is trivial: $\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} = \mathrm{id}$ and
$\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} = \mathrm{id}$ on $H_\mathbf{U}^*$. This is the condition
$U_\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{U}) = \mathbf{U}$: $\mathbf{U}$ is a fixed point of the
meta-Universe Closure (§meta-universe-closure). The fixed universe coincides with the
universe fiber; the universe does not expand further.
