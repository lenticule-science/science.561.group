### Theorem — The Limit–Colimit Dyad {#limit-colimit-dyad}

The hyperverse tower $R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)} \hookrightarrow \cdots$ carries
two canonical limits:

| Pole | Object | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Homotopy colimit** | $\mathrm{hocolim}_n R^{(n)} = \mathcal{H}$ | Assembles all finite-depth data; the forward limit |
| **Homotopy limit** | $\mathrm{holim}_n R^{(n)}$ | Compatible systems across all levels; the backward limit |
| **Agreement** | $\mathrm{hocolim} \simeq \mathrm{holim}$ | The Ambidexterity Conjecture (§ambidexterity) |

The limit–colimit dyad is the tower-level instance of the saturation–transfer dyad
(§saturation-transfer-dyad) at the fiber level: $\sigma_t$ is the backward nucleus,
$\Delta_t$ is the forward nucleus, and $H_t^*$ is their common fixed point. The
ambidexterity conjecture (§ambidexterity) is the statement that the same pattern holds
one level higher — the backward limit and the forward limit of the tower agree in the
colimit $\mathcal{H}$.

The 1-categorical shadow of this agreement is the self-generation equation $R = U_G(R)$
(§self-generation): the colimit of the depth-filtration tower equals the fixed point of
the closure operator. The conjecture extends this to the hyperverse tower and the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical setting.
