### Definition — Transfer-Stable Universe {#transfer-stable-universe}

The **transfer-stable universe** of $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the
$\Delta^{(n+1)}$-stable sub-algebra of the universe fiber (§universe-fiber):
$$
\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \;\subseteq\; H_\mathbf{U}^*.
$$

A proposition $a \in H_\mathbf{U}^*$ belongs to $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$
when it has settled under the meta-transfer nucleus — when all one-step extensions of
$\mathbf{U}$ in $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ agree on $a$. These are the propositions about
$\mathbf{U}$ that are preserved as the tower advances: the relational profile of
$\mathbf{U}$ across all higher universe levels.

#### Transfer-Stable Universe and Universe Morphisms {#transfer-stable-universe-morphisms}

The meta-transfer nucleus $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ stabilizes with respect to
one-step extensions in $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$: for each universe morphism
$f : \mathbf{U} \to \mathbf{V}$ in $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$, the restriction map
$H^{(n+1)}(f) : H_\mathbf{V}^* \to H_\mathbf{U}^*$ (§universe-doctrine) contributes
to determining $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$. A proposition is transfer-stable when it is
coherent with all such restrictions — when it names something preserved under universe
morphisms.

This is the meta-level analogue of the transfer-stable fiber at the object level: just as
$\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_t) \subseteq H_t$ (§transfer-stable-fiber) consists of propositions
committed to by all one-step history extensions, $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$
consists of propositions about $\mathbf{U}$ committed to by all one-step universe
extensions.

#### Position in the Meta-Level Nuclear Square {#transfer-stable-universe-nuclear-square}

In the meta-level nuclear square at $\mathbf{U}$ (§universe-fiber, [CommutativeNuclearSquare](commutative-nuclear-square.md)), the
transfer-stable universe occupies the $\Delta^{(n+1)}$-quiescent corner. The inclusion
$\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \hookrightarrow H_\mathbf{U}^*$ has left adjoint
$\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$: for any $a \in H_\mathbf{U}^*$,
$\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}(a)$ is the least transfer-stable-universe element above $a$.

The saturation-stable universe (§saturation-stable-universe) and transfer-stable universe
together specify $\mathbf{U}$'s position in the hyperverse: the saturation-stable universe
is what can be said about $\mathbf{U}$ from the same level; the transfer-stable universe
is what is preserved as the level advances. Their intersection is the fixed universe
(§fixed-universe).
