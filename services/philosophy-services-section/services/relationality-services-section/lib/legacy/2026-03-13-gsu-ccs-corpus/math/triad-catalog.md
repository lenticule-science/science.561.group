### Definition — Triads of the Relational Universe {#triad-catalog}

A **triad** is a three-stage progression (§triad). The following is an
annotated catalog of the triads of $R$.

---

#### The Settling Triad {#triads-settling}

*See §settling-triad for the full treatment.*

| Stage | Name | Level |
|-------|------|-------|
| Free | Syntax | FiberNuclearLanguage / $\Sigma^*$ / $R_\mathrm{syn}$ |
| Quotient | Semantics | $H_t$ / $\mathbb{M}(\Sigma,I)$ / $R$ |
| Fixed | Stability | $H_t^*$ / (none) / $\mathsf{Phase}$ |

The settling triad runs at every level of the construction. At the language
level it is the passage from the fiber language FiberNuclearLanguage through the
Heyting algebra $H_t$ to the fixed fiber $H_t^*$. At the universe level it is
the passage from the syntactic relational universe through the topos
$\mathbf{Sh}(T,J)$ to the invariant locale.

The history level is the only level where the triad truncates: the history monoid
$\mathbb{M}(\Sigma,I)$ has no nuclear closure operators, so there is no stable
sublocus at that level.

---

#### The Modal Triad {#triads-modal}

*See §modal-triad for the full treatment.*

| Position | Name | Adjoint |
|----------|------|---------|
| Left | Directed shape | $\Pi_\to$ |
| Middle | Dynamics | $G_s = \Box \dashv \Diamond$ |
| Right | Cosections | $\nabla_\leftarrow$ |

The modal triad is the operational complement to the settling triad: where
the settling triad describes how the universe is *built*, the modal triad
describes how it is *accessed*. The left and right positions are global
(colimit and limit over all of $T$); the middle position is local (one
atom $s$ at a time).

Under the finite-step-set hypothesis, this triad extends to the four-adjoint
chain (§modal-adjoint-chain): $\Pi_\to \dashv \Box \dashv \Diamond \dashv
\nabla_\leftarrow$. The tetrad at the internal level is a natural extension of
the constructive-level triad.

---

#### Open: Hyperversal Triad {#triads-hyperversal}

At the hyperversal level, a third triad is expected: the passage from the
initial hyperversal model $\mathcal{H}_\mathrm{syn}$ through the Relational
Hyperverse $\mathcal{H}$ to its stable subtopos $\mathsf{Phase}_\mathcal{H}$.
This is the settling triad at the spiral level. It requires the second-turn
content (§on-relational-hyperverse) to be in place before it can be stated precisely.
