### Construction — The Syntactic Relational Universe {#syntactic-relational-universe}

The previous results show that, once a relational universe $(R, H, \sigma, \Delta, H^\ast)$ exists, it
is completely determined by its primitive data. We now give an explicit *syntactic*
construction of such a model: a term model built purely from the FiberNuclearLanguage
signature. This is the canonical initial form of the language — the settled first
instance of the relational universe.

This construction proceeds in two layers:

1. a **syntactic history site** $(T_{\mathrm{syn}}, J_{\mathrm{syn}})$, and
2. a **syntactic recognizer sheaf** $H_{\mathrm{syn}} : T_{\mathrm{syn}}^{\mathrm{op}}
   \to \mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ whose fibers are Lindenbaum–Tarski algebras of
   FiberNuclearLanguage formulas.

Together these yield a canonical relational universe
$$
R_{\mathrm{syn}} \;\coloneqq\; \mathbf{Sh}(T_{\mathrm{syn}}, J_{\mathrm{syn}}),
\qquad H_{\mathrm{syn}} \in R_{\mathrm{syn}},
$$
which realizes the theory on the nose.

#### Syntactic Trace Site {#syntactic-history-site}

Fix a step set $\Sigma$ and a symmetric commutation relation
$I \subseteq \Sigma \times \Sigma$. Define the **syntactic history category**
$T_{\mathrm{syn}}$ as follows.

Objects are equivalence classes of finite step sequences $w = a_1 \dots a_n$ over $\Sigma$ under the
smallest congruence generated by:
1. **commutations**: if $(a_i, a_{i+1}) \in I$, then
   $\dots a_i a_{i+1} \dots \sim \dots a_{i+1} a_i \dots$.

This is exactly the free partially commutative monoid $\mathbb{M}(\Sigma, I)$ from §history;
concatenation is already associative and has unit $\varepsilon$ (the empty step sequence). We write $[w]$
for the class of $w$ and call such classes **syntactic histories**.

A Grothendieck topology $J_{\mathrm{syn}}$ on $T_{\mathrm{syn}}$ is generated by
**commutation covers**: for a history $t$ and a partition of its steps into commuting sub-histories $t \simeq t^{(1)} \cdot \dots \cdot t^{(n)}$, declare the family
of inclusions $\{u_i : t^{(i)} \hookrightarrow t\}$ to be a covering family of $t$.
By construction, $(T_{\mathrm{syn}}, J_{\mathrm{syn}})$ satisfies the Axioms of Histories and Locality.

#### Syntactic Fibers and Modalities {#syntactic-fibers}

For each syntactic history $t \in T_{\mathrm{syn}}$, define:

- $\mathrm{Form}_t$: the set of closed $\mathsf{Prop}$-terms at history $t$ in
  FiberNuclearLanguage;
- $\equiv_t$: provability of $\vdash_t M \equiv N : \mathsf{Prop}$ in the propositional-modal
  equational theory of FiberNuclearLanguage (Heyting algebra laws plus nucleus axioms).

Define the **Lindenbaum–Tarski algebra**
$$
H^{\mathrm{syn}}_t \;\coloneqq\; \mathrm{Form}_t \big/ {\equiv_t},
$$
where $[M]_t$ denotes the equivalence class of $M$. The operations are induced
syntactically:
$$
[M]_t \wedge_t [N]_t \coloneqq [M \wedge N]_t,\quad
[M]_t \Rightarrow_t [N]_t \coloneqq [M \Rightarrow N]_t,\quad
\sigma_t([M]_t) \coloneqq [\mathsf{Saturate}(M)]_t,\quad
\Delta_t([M]_t) \coloneqq [\mathsf{Transfer}(M)]_t,
$$
and similarly for $\vee, \bot, \top$. Quotienting by provable equality ensures that all
equations of the fiber doctrine hold; thus each $(H^{\mathrm{syn}}_t, \sigma_t, \Delta_t)$ is an object
of $\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$.

The **syntactic fixed fiber** is
$$
H^{\mathrm{syn}\,*}_t
\;\coloneqq\;
\{\, [M]_t \in H^{\mathrm{syn}}_t \mid
\vdash_t \mathsf{Saturate}(M) = M
\text{ and }
\vdash_t \mathsf{Transfer}(M) = M \,\},
$$
the subalgebra of elements provably fixed by both modalities.

For each morphism $f : t \to t'$ in $T_{\mathrm{syn}}$, reindexing on formulas is given
syntactically: the translation $[M]_{t'} \mapsto [M^f]_t$ makes $H_{\mathrm{syn}} :
T_{\mathrm{syn}}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ a functor, as required by the Axiom of Reindexing,
and the fixed fibers assemble into a subpresheaf $H^{\mathrm{syn}\,*} \subseteq H_{\mathrm{syn}}$.

By construction, the sheaf conditions of the Axioms of Reindexing through Locality for $H_{\mathrm{syn}}$ and
$H^{\mathrm{syn}\,*}$ reduce to locality and gluing properties of the syntactic
equivalence relation. Thus the syntactic data satisfy the axioms of §history-and-fiber-algebra, §modal-calculus, and §sheaf-construction up to and including the Axiom of Locality.

#### Proposition — Universal Property of the Syntactic Relational Universe {#prop-syntactic-universal}

The syntactic relational universe $(R_{\mathrm{syn}}, H_{\mathrm{syn}})$ is the
**initial relational universe model** in the category of Definition §relational-universe-model: given any relational universe model
$(S, K, \sigma_S, \Delta_S, K^\ast)$, there is a unique structure-preserving geometric
morphism
$$
F : (R_{\mathrm{syn}}, H_{\mathrm{syn}}, \sigma, \Delta, H^{\mathrm{syn}\,*})
\longrightarrow (S, K, \sigma_S, \Delta_S, K^\ast).
$$

The proof is given in §initiality in §sheaf-construction, where the full category of relational universe
models is in place. Here we note only that the syntactic data provides the explicit
witness: each syntactic history maps to the internal history structure of $S$; each FiberNuclearLanguage
term $M : \mathsf{Prop}$ is interpreted as an internal arrow into $K$; the modalities
$\mathsf{Saturate}$, $\mathsf{Transfer}$ are interpreted as $\sigma_S$, $\Delta_S$. This
interpretation extends uniquely to a geometric morphism by the universal property of
sheafification.

The syntactic relational universe is the free model generated by the FiberNuclearLanguage signature and
axioms — the language in its one canonical initial form.
