### Construction — The Sheaf Construction {#sheaf-construction}

*How local fiber data coheres into a single global topos $R = \mathbf{Sh}(T,J)$.*

Relations are the hardest term: they are neither the things nor the operations, but the
coherence structure that makes operations produce coherent things. In the relational universe, this is
the functor $H: T^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{HA}_{\mathrm{nucl}}$ relating fibers across
histories, the commutation topology $J$ specifying when local data assembles uniquely into
global data, and the ambient topos $R = \mathbf{Sh}(T,J)$ that is the result of that
assembly. Four axioms govern this section: Reindexing, History Separation, Locality, and
Stability. All establish coherence conditions — none introduce new objects.
