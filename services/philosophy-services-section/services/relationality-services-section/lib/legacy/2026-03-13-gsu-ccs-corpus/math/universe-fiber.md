### Definition — The Universe Fiber {#universe-fiber}

The **universe fiber** of a relational universe $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is
$H_\mathbf{U}^*$: the fiber of the universe doctrine (§universe-doctrine)
$H^{(n+1)} : \mathbf{RU}^{(n),\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ evaluated at
$\mathbf{U}$.

$H_\mathbf{U}^*$ is the fixed fiber of $\mathbf{U}$ at its own level — the joint
fixed-point algebra $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_\mathbf{U}) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_\mathbf{U})$
of the object-level nuclei within $\mathbf{U}$ (§fixed-fiber). It encodes the
constitutive data of $\mathbf{U}$: what $\mathbf{U}$ is made of, at the level where
$\mathbf{U}$'s own construction has fully settled.

#### The Universe Fiber in the Meta-Level Nuclear Square {#universe-fiber-nuclear-square}

The universe doctrine $H^{(n+1)}$ is a double-closure doctrine over $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$
(§universe-doctrine, §double-closure-doctrine), carrying two natural commuting nuclei
$\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ and $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ on each fiber $H_\mathbf{U}^*$.
These nuclei are induced by the Yoneda embedding
$\mathbf{U} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}, J^{(n+1)})$:
the meta-saturation nucleus $\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ stabilizes with respect to the
horizontal inter-universe relational context; the meta-transfer nucleus
$\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ stabilizes with respect to one-step extensions in
$\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$.

The resulting meta-level nuclear square at $\mathbf{U}$ has four corners:

| Corner | Algebra | Position |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Universe fiber** | $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ | Free — all stable propositions of $\mathbf{U}$ |
| **Saturation-stable universe** | $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$ | $\sigma^{(n+1)}$-stable |
| **Transfer-stable universe** | $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$ | $\Delta^{(n+1)}$-stable |
| **Fixed universe** | $(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$ | Doubly stable |

The universe fiber is the starting material: the full stable algebra of $\mathbf{U}$,
carrying its constitutive triple $(T_\mathbf{U}, J_\mathbf{U}, H_\mathbf{U})$ and all
the derived structure. The three other corners are sub-algebras of the universe fiber,
obtained by applying the meta-nuclei.
