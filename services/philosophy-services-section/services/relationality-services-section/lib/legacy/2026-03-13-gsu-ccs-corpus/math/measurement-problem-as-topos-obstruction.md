### Conjecture — Measurement Problem as Topos Obstruction {#measurement-problem-as-topos-obstruction}

An observer is a structure at the next spiral level looking down. The measurement
problem is the Topos-of-Topoi obstruction (see [topos-of-topoi](topos-of-topoi.md)):
$H^1(\mathcal{H}) \neq 0$ because there is no Hyper-Axiom of Locality at the
meta-level. The measurement problem is not solvable within one universe; it requires
the next spiral level.

**What a proof requires.** A precise identification of "observer" with an object in
$\mathcal{H}^{(1)}$ relative to the system in $\mathcal{H}$, and a derivation of the
$H^1$ obstruction from the absence of a meta-locality axiom.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
