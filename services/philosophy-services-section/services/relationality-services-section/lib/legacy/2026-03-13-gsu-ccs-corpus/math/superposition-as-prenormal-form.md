### Conjecture — Superposition as Pre-Normal Form {#superposition-as-prenormal-form}

A quantum superposition corresponds to a FiberNuclearLanguage formula $M$ (see
[FiberNuclearLanguage](fiber-nuclear-language.md)) that has not yet committed to which
reduction branch to take. Measurement is forcing normalization. The Born rule
probabilities are the relative weights of the reduction paths available to $M$. Quantum
collapse is: the formula took one branch.

**What a proof requires.** A probability measure on reduction paths compatible with the
Born rule. The entanglement measure $\mathsf{ent}(M)$ (see
[nuclear-reduction-measure](nuclear-reduction-measure.md)) may supply this: paths with
lower final entanglement should receive higher weight.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
