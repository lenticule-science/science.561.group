### Definition — Dyads of the Relational Universe {#dyad}

A **dyad** is a duality (§convergence-locus) with two named poles and a convergence
point. The following is an annotated catalog of the dyads of $R$, organized by the level at which they first appear.

---

#### Algebra-Level Dyads {#dyads-algebra}

##### The Nucleus Dyad {#dyads-nucleus}

*See §saturation-transfer-dyad for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Operator | Direction |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Saturation | $\sigma_t$ | Backward — what the past forces |
| Transfer | $\Delta_t$ | Forward — what the future demands |
| **Convergence** | $H_t^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_t)$ | The doubly quiescent layer |

This is the foundational dyad. Every other duality either specializes it or
is derived from it. The nucleus dyad is established at the ground level by
the fiber doctrine (§fiber-doctrine).

##### The Being/Becoming Dyad {#dyads-being-becoming}

*See §being-becoming-dyad for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Nucleus | Fourfold Axes | Sections |
|------|---------|---------------|----------|
| Being | $\sigma$ (Sat) | Object + Relation | Algebra + Geometry |
| Becoming | $\Delta$ (Trans) | Quality + Act | Language + Dynamics |
| **Convergence** | $H_t^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_t)$ | Fixed fiber | hyperverse-fixed universe fully determined |

The organizing duality of the nuclear quartet structure. Being is static — what the
accumulated relational history already forces. Becoming is dynamic — what
further extensions demand. The nucleus dyad (§saturation-transfer-dyad) is the same dyad
named from the directional (past/future) perspective; this entry names it from
the ontological (what-it-is / what-it-does) perspective. The `phase:` field on
every math unit is the external record of this dyad.

##### The Stabilization–Envelope Dyad {#dyads-stabilization-envelope}

*See §stabilization-envelope-dyad for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Map | Direction |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Stabilization | $\mathrm{Stab}_t$ | Inflationary — least stable upper bound |
| Stable Envelope | $\mathrm{Env}_t$ | Deflationary — greatest stable lower bound |
| **Convergence** | $a = \mathrm{Stab}_t(a) = \mathrm{Env}_t(a)$ iff $a \in H_t^*$ | Instability gap closes |

The stabilization–envelope dyad bounds instability from both sides. It is
the positional view of what the nucleus dyad characterizes operationally.

#### Universe-Level Dyads {#dyads-universe}

##### The Construction-Observation Dyad {#dyads-construction-observation}

*See §observation-duality for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Object | Direction |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Construction | $H$ (the sheaf of propositions) | Building from atoms upward |
| Observation | $H^\vee = [H, \Omega]$ (the observation sheaf) | Testing predicates downward |
| **Convergence** | $H^* \times (H^\vee)^* \to \Omega$ is separating | §observation-duality |

At the stable level, what can be constructed is exactly what can be observed.
This is a topos-level duality: it uses the subobject classifier $\Omega$ as
the target of the pairing.

##### The Limit-Colimit Dyad {#dyads-limit-colimit}

*See §ambidexterity for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Construction | Direction |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Colimit | $\varinjlim_n R^{(n)}$ | Assembling from below — what can be built |
| Limit | $\varprojlim_n R^{(n)}$ | Projecting from above — what persists |
| **Convergence** | $\mathrm{holim} \simeq \mathrm{hocolim}$ | Ambidexterity conjecture |

The Ambidexterity Conjecture (§ambidexterity) says what can be generated
equals what persists. This is the hyperversal version of the nucleus dyad:
at the meta-level, the two directions of closure — building up and
projecting down — converge.

##### The Bulk-Boundary Dyad {#dyads-bulk-boundary}

*See §relational-holograph for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Object | Role |
|------|--------|------|
| Bulk | $R$ (the full topos) | All propositions, stable and unstable |
| Boundary | $\mathsf{Phase}$ (the invariant locale) | Only the stable propositions |
| **Convergence** | $\mathrm{Stab} : H \xrightarrow{\sim} H^*$ at the fixed point | Holographic completeness |

At the fixed point $R = U_G(R)$, every proposition is already stable. The
bulk topos coincides with its stable boundary — the holographic projection
becomes an isomorphism.

#### Dynamics-Level Dyads {#dyads-dynamics}

##### The Restriction–Carrier Dyad {#dyads-restriction-carrier}

*See §restriction-carrier-dyad for the full treatment.*

| Pole | Direction | Map |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Restriction | Backward — $H(f) : H_{t'} \to H_t$ | Presheaf restriction maps |
| Carrier | Forward — $\gamma_t : X(t) \to X(s \star t)$ | Directed comonad structure maps |
| **Convergence** | $\varepsilon_t \circ \gamma_t = \mathrm{id}_{X(t)}$ | Counit law |

The counit law of the directed comonad $G_s$ says that extending one step
and then restricting back is the identity. Restriction and carrier cancel.
