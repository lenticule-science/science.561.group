### Definition — Saturation-Stable Universe {#saturation-stable-universe}

The **saturation-stable universe** of $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the
$\sigma^{(n+1)}$-stable sub-algebra of the universe fiber (§universe-fiber):
$$
\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \;\subseteq\; H_\mathbf{U}^*.
$$

A proposition $a \in H_\mathbf{U}^*$ belongs to $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$
when it has settled under the meta-saturation nucleus — when the inter-universe relational
context at level $n+1$ has nothing further to impose on $a$. These are the propositions
about $\mathbf{U}$ that are expressible from any universe in $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$: the
stable internal language of $\mathbf{U}$ as visible from the inter-universe perspective.

#### Saturation-Stable Universe and the Internal Language {#saturation-stable-universe-language}

The meta-saturation nucleus $\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ stabilizes with respect to the
horizontal inter-universe context: the other universes in $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ and the
Yoneda embedding $\mathbf{U} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)}$. A proposition fixed by
$\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$ is one that all other universes at the same level agree on
— it is independent of the particular choice of ambient universe at level $n+1$.

This is the meta-level analogue of the fiber-level saturation: just as $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t)
\subseteq H_t$ (§saturation-stable-fiber) consists of propositions that have settled with
respect to the accumulated relational history at $t$, $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$
consists of propositions about $\mathbf{U}$ that have settled with respect to the accumulated
inter-universe context.

The saturation-stable universe is strictly contained in the universe fiber:
$\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \subseteq H_\mathbf{U}^*$ (§universe-fiber). The
universe fiber carries all the constitutive data of $\mathbf{U}$; the saturation-stable
universe carries only what is universally expressible about $\mathbf{U}$ from the outside.

#### Position in the Meta-Level Nuclear Square {#saturation-stable-universe-nuclear-square}

In the meta-level nuclear square at $\mathbf{U}$ (§universe-fiber, [CommutativeNuclearSquare](commutative-nuclear-square.md)), the
saturation-stable universe occupies the $\sigma^{(n+1)}$-quiescent corner. The inclusion
$\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) \hookrightarrow H_\mathbf{U}^*$ has left adjoint
$\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}$: for any $a \in H_\mathbf{U}^*$, $\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}(a)$
is the least saturation-stable-universe element above $a$.
