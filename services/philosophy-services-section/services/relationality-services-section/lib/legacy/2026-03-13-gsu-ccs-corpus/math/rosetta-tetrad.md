### Conjecture — The Rosetta Tetrad {#rosetta-tetrad}

In "Physics, Topology, Logic and Computation: A Rosetta Stone" (2009), John Baez and Mike Stay exhibit a table with four columns:

| Physics | Topology | Logic | Computation |
|---------|----------|-------|-------------|
| Hilbert space | Manifold | Proposition | Type |
| Operator | Cobordism | Proof | Program |
| Composition | Gluing | Cut rule | Function composition |
| Tensor product | Disjoint union | Conjunction | Product type |
| Inner product | Pairing | Implication | Function type |

The claim: the same mathematical structures, when read column by column, are simultaneously a physical theory (Hilbert spaces and operators), a topological theory (manifolds and cobordisms), a logical theory (propositions and proofs), and a computational theory (types and programs). The Rosetta Stone (the artifact) translated the same decree into three scripts; this table translates the same mathematics into four disciplines.

#### What This Illuminates {#rosetta-tetrad-illuminates}

The Rosetta tetrad shows that the nuclear quartet pattern is cross-disciplinary: it is not specific to topos theory or to the construction of $R$. The same four-column structure appears whenever a mathematical framework is rich enough to support all four modes of description. For $R$, being a Grothendieck topos with an internal language, a site, and an action, this richness is guaranteed.

The translation metaphor is also useful: a fact visible in the language section (a proof in FiberNuclearLanguage) has a translation in the geometry section (a global section of a sheaf) and in the dynamics section (an invariant under $U_G$). The nuclear quartet is not just an organization of sections — it is a claim that these translations exist and are coherent.

#### Limits of the Correspondence {#rosetta-tetrad-limits}

The Rosetta Stone is a *correspondence*: it shows that the four columns are translation-equivalent for the specific structures listed. It does not claim (as the nuclear quartet does) that the four axes are *necessary* for exhaustive disclosure. The nuclear quartet is a structural theorem; the Rosetta Stone is an observed translation.
