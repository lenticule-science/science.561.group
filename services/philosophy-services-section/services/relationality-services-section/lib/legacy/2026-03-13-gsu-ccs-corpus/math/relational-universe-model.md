### Definition — Relational Universe Model {#relational-universe-model}

A **relational universe model** consists of: a topos $S$; an internal object $K \in S$; internal modal
operators $\sigma_S, \Delta_S : K \to K$; a fixed subobject $K^\ast \hookrightarrow K$;
and a family of representable-like objects realizing a site $(T,J)$ inside $S$ — such
that the internal structure of $(S, K, \sigma_S, \Delta_S, K^\ast)$ realizes the axioms of §history-and-fiber-algebra, §modal-calculus, and §sheaf-construction internally.

A **morphism** between relational universe models is a geometric morphism of topoi together with
structure isomorphisms that preserve the distinguished object, modalities, and fixed
subobject up to isomorphism, and respect the closure mechanism of the Axiom of Generative Basis.
