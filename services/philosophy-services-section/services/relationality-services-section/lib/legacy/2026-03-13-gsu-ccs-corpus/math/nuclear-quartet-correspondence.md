### Theorem — Nuclear Quartet Correspondence {#nuclear-quartet-correspondence}

The nuclear quartet structure of the relational universe is self-similar across
two levels simultaneously:

| Level | Algebra (Being) | Language (Becoming) | Geometry (Being) | Dynamics (Becoming) |
|-------|----------------|---------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Fiber** | $H_t$ carrier | $\sigma_t$, $\Delta_t$ acting | $H_t^\ast$ fixed layer | Reindexing / restriction |
| **Universe** | Fiber algebras $(H_t)$ | FiberNuclearLanguage / $R_\mathrm{syn}$ | $R = \mathbf{Sh}(T,J)$ | $U_G$, comonad $G_s$, tower |

At both levels, the same four actual mathematical objects arise: the free carrier,
the $\sigma$-stable sub-algebra, the $\Delta$-stable sub-algebra, and the doubly
stable joint fixed-point. The transition from one level to the next is not an
analogy — it is the content of the tower construction: $H_t^*$ at level $n$
becomes the fiber algebra at level $n+1$.

#### The Four-Adjoint Modal Chain {#correspondence-adjoints}

The four-adjoint chain (§modal-adjoint-chain)
$$
\Pi_{\to} \;\dashv\; \Box \;\dashv\; \Diamond \;\dashv\; \nabla_{\leftarrow}
$$
is the modal expression of this nuclear quartet correspondence. Each adjoint corresponds
to one phase:

| Adjoint | Modality | Phase |
|---------|----------|-------|
| $\Pi_{\to}$ | Directed shape: forward colimit | Dynamics (becoming) |
| $\Box = \Delta$ | Flat: constant extension from base | Algebra (being) |
| $\Diamond$ | Sharp: coinduction from global | Language (being/becoming) |
| $\nabla_{\leftarrow}$ | Codiscrete: all future sections | Geometry (becoming) |

The middle comonad $G_s = \Box\Diamond$ captures exactly the handshake: it is the
composite of the algebra modality (flat/constant) and the language modality (sharp),
and it drives the sequential dynamics of §closure-and-self-generation. The entire chain is the topos-
theoretic realization of the nuclear quartet at the level of $R$'s own modal structure.
