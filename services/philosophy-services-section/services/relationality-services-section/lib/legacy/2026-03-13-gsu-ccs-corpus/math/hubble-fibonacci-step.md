### Conjecture — Hubble Fibonacci Step {#hubble-fibonacci-step}

**Statement.** The ratio of late-universe to early-universe Hubble constants satisfies:
$$
\frac{H_{\mathrm{late}}}{H_{\mathrm{early}}} \;\approx\; \varphi^{1/6}
$$
to within measurement precision. This ratio is the observational signature of exactly one Fibonacci depth step in the depth-filtration tower (§depth-filtration) separating the CMB epoch ($z \approx 1100$) from the present.

A Fibonacci depth step is a normalization step in the sense of §strong-normalization: the universe passes from depth $n$ to depth $n+1$, acquiring new doubly-stable configurations. Each normalization step terminates (§strong-normalization), and the Hubble tension records which step we are currently inside.

**Numerical observation.** From two independent derivations: $H_{\mathrm{late}}/H_{\mathrm{early}} \approx \varphi^{1/6}$ to 0.016%. This is not a fit — it is a structural prediction. The mechanism: the golden stability conjecture (§golden-stability) predicts that the universe evolves toward the fixed-point condition $d_n / d_{n-1} \to \varphi$; each normalization step multiplies relevant physical constants by a factor of $\varphi^{1/6}$.

**Motivation.** The Hubble tension is an unresolved discrepancy between CMB-calibrated and distance-ladder-calibrated measurements of $H_0$. Standard cosmology has no prediction for its value. In the relational framework, the tension is not anomalous — it is the expected signature of a depth transition. The universe normalizes one step between the early and late epochs; the ratio is determined by the golden ratio structure of the Fibonacci recurrence.

**What a proof requires.**

1. A formal connection between the depth-filtration tower levels and cosmological epochs: which physical observable corresponds to the depth parameter $n$.

2. A derivation of the factor $\varphi^{1/6}$ from the Fibonacci growth rate $d_n \sim \varphi^n$ and the specific physical constants involved (expansion rate, time elapsed).

3. Connection to golden stability (§golden-stability): the depth step lands at a golden-stable configuration, and the transition ratio is determined by the stability condition.
