### Definition — Universes as Objects {#on-universe-as-object}

The construction of $R$ built one Relational Universe $R = \mathbf{Sh}(T, J)$ from the ground up: history category, fiber algebras, presheaf, nuclei, topos. Everything from §history-and-fiber-algebra through §closure-and-self-generation was about the interior of $R$ — its propositions, its stable layer, its self-generation.

The construction of $\mathcal{H}$ begins with a single question: **what kind of thing is $R$?**

Not "what is $R$ made of" — that is already answered. But: what is $R$ as a mathematical object that can appear alongside other objects of the same kind, be compared with them, and be used as raw material for another nuclear quartet cycle?

The answer is that $R$ is an object of $\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ at the universe level: it carries a history structure, a fiber algebra presheaf, and two nuclei, just as every fiber $H_t$ carries a Heyting algebra and two nuclei. The nuclear quartet construction does not know whether its input is a fiber algebra or a full universe; it only needs the algebraic shape. If $R$ has that shape, $U_\mathcal{H}$ can be applied to $R$ the same way $U_G$ was applied to the atoms of $T$.

**What this section does.** Three constructions, in order:

1. **Universe as object** (§universe-as-object) — pin down the exact algebraic data that makes $\mathbf{U} = (T_\mathbf{U}, J_\mathbf{U}, H_\mathbf{U})$ a well-formed object. This is the "single fiber" for the construction of $\mathcal{H}$.

2. **Universe morphisms** (§universe-morphism) — define what a structure-preserving map between universes is, and show that nuclei are automatically preserved. This gives the hom-sets.

3. **Category of relational universes** (§universe-category) — assemble objects and morphisms into $\mathbf{RU}$, verify it carries the history-like structure (monoidal product, commutation, Grothendieck topology) needed to serve as the meta-level history category $T^{(1)}$ for the construction of $\mathcal{H}$.

Once $\mathbf{RU}$ is verified to be a history category, the entire machinery of §history-and-fiber-algebra — histories, fiber algebras, the nuclear quartet — applies with $T^{(1)} = \mathbf{RU}$. The output is $R^{(1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}, J_\mathbf{RU})$: the first level of the hyperverse tower (§hyperverse-tower).
