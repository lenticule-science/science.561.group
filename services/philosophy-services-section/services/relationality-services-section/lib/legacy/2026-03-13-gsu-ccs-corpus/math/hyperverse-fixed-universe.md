### Definition — Hyperverse-Fixed Universe {#hyperverse-fixed-universe}

A **hyperverse-fixed universe** is a relational universe $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$
for which the meta-level nuclear square (§universe-fiber, §fixed-universe) is trivial:
$$
(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^* \;=\; H_\mathbf{U}^*.
$$

Equivalently, both meta-nuclei act as the identity on $H_\mathbf{U}^*$:
$\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} = \mathrm{id}$ and $\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U} = \mathrm{id}$.
Equivalently, $\mathbf{U}$ is a fixed point of the meta-Universe Closure:
$$
U_\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{U}) \;=\; \mathbf{U}.
$$

For a hyperverse-fixed universe, the four corners of the meta-level nuclear square at
$\mathbf{U}$ all coincide:
$H_\mathbf{U}^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) =
\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U}) = (H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$.
There is no gap between the universe fiber and its fixed universe; the meta-nuclear-square
is degenerate.

#### Hyperverse-Fixed Universes and the Hyperverse {#hyperverse-fixed-universe-hyperverse}

The Relational Hyperverse $\mathcal{H}$ is the colimit of the tower
$R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)} \hookrightarrow \cdots$ (§hyperverse-tower) and
satisfies $U_\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{H}) = \mathcal{H}$ (§relational-hyperverse). It is
the canonical hyperverse-fixed universe: the object for which the meta-Universe Closure
has no further work to do.

Hyperverse-fixed universes are the fixed points of $U_\mathcal{H}$: the universe-level
analogue of the fixed fiber $H_t^*$ at the fiber level, and of $R = U_G(R)$ at the
universe level. The three fixed-point conditions —
$H_t^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_t)$ (§fixed-fiber),
$R = U_G(R)$ (§self-generation), and $\mathcal{H} = U_\mathcal{H}(\mathcal{H})$
(§hyperverse-fixed-point) — are the three instances of the same pattern, at fiber,
universe, and hyperverse levels respectively.

#### The Four Corners of a Non-Fixed Universe {#hyperverse-fixed-universe-non-fixed}

For a universe that is not hyperverse-fixed, the four corners of its meta-level nuclear
square are genuinely distinct:
- **Universe fiber** (§universe-fiber): $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ — the full fixed-fiber algebra of $\mathbf{U}$
- **Saturation-stable universe** (§saturation-stable-universe): $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$ — stable under the inter-universe relational context
- **Transfer-stable universe** (§transfer-stable-universe): $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta^{(n+1)}_\mathbf{U})$ — stable under all one-step extensions
- **Fixed universe** (§fixed-universe): $(H_\mathbf{U}^*)^*$ — the doubly stable sub-algebra

For a hyperverse-fixed universe, these four collapse to one. The gap between universe
fiber and fixed universe is zero; the meta-Nuclear Square is the identity.
