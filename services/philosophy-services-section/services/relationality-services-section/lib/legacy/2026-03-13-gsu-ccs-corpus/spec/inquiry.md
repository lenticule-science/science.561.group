# Inquiry

## What this is

An inquiry is the type of skillful work (§skillful-work) corresponding to raw A in the nuclear quartet ([NuclearQuartetSubalgebra](../math/nuclear-quartet-subalgebra.md)) — neither meaning-closure (σ) nor execution-closure (Δ) has been applied. An inquiry explores unknown territory without a predetermined output shape. By the nuclear quartet theorem (§nuclear-quartet-theorem), every element of A maps to one of the four canonical sub-algebras via π (to H\*), j₁ (to Fix(σ)), or j₂ (to Fix(Δ)); an inquiry therefore always produces something, even if that something is another inquiry at a deeper level.

## MUST

- State the question or territory being explored — §nuclear-quartet-theorem: A is not empty; even the raw algebra has structure; the starting point must be named
- Produce some output — [NuclearQuartetSubalgebra](../math/nuclear-quartet-subalgebra.md#nuclear-quartet-subalgebra-projection): the map π : A → H\* shows every element has a canonical image; an inquiry that produces nothing has failed to apply any nucleus
- Admit that the output type is unknown in advance — §nuclear-quartet-theorem: A ≠ A\* when the nuclei are non-trivial; the raw object is genuinely larger than the settled sub-algebras; claiming a predetermined output type collapses A to H\*
- Have a termination condition — without one it is not a skill, it is an open-ended session
- Declare its type in `skill-closure-kind: inquiry` frontmatter — §skillful-work

## CAN

- Loop, backtrack, and change direction
- Spawn skills of any type as sub-skills
- Produce another inquiry as output, if the territory explored opens new questions
- Produce nothing of substance and report that the territory is empty or not yet accessible

## CANNOT

- Predetermine its output type — §nuclear-quartet-theorem: A ≠ A\* when the nuclear square is non-trivial; assuming the shape in advance collapses A to H\* and makes this a Process
- Be used when a more determined type is available — §nuclear-quartet-theorem: whenever π, j₁, or j₂ suffice to reach a determined type, apply them; Inquiry is for territory where none of the three maps is yet known to apply
