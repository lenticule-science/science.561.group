# Work

## What this is

A **work unit** is an episode of doing with a defined output. Work is characterized entirely by what it produces, not by what happens inside it or to it. The output may be an artifact, a judgment, a transformed state, or a report that nothing was found — but it must be something nameable.

**Skillful work** (§skillful-work) is the subtype of work for which a documented, repeatable method exists. The four types of skillful work (§process, §procedure, §derivation, §inquiry) are the canonical sub-algebras of a nuclear quartet ([NuclearQuartetSubalgebra](../math/nuclear-quartet-subalgebra.md)) generated by two commuting nuclei on the algebra of all work.

## MUST

- Produce a named output — a work unit that produces nothing has not done work; it has occupied time
- Be named by its output — §name (the naming rule for all units in this repo applies to work units)

## CAN

- Be skillful (have a documented method) or ad-hoc (no method yet)
- Produce an output of type "nothing found" or "territory is inaccessible" — this counts as a named output
- Call other work units as sub-steps

## CANNOT

- Be defined by what happens to it — work is active, not passive; the output is the definition
- Produce an unnamed output — an unnamed output cannot serve as input to downstream work; it is not complete
