# Predicate

An object of a [predicate category](../objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ — equivalently, an element of the [predicate set](../objects/predicate-set.md) $\Pi = \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$, denoted $p$.

A predicate occupies the second coordinate of a [predication cell](predication-cell.md). The predicate category equips predicates with morphisms in four classes — [predicate specialization](../morphisms/predicate-specialization.md), [predicate converse](../morphisms/predicate-converse.md), [predicate subject-projection](../morphisms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [predicate object-projection](../morphisms/predicate-object-projection.md) — and each predicate has an associated [predication fiber algebra](../objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) at which [predication valuations](../objects/predication-valuation.md) are assigned.
