## Annotation

Systematic treatment connecting hyperdoctrines to natural deduction, with the **Beck–Chevalley condition** formalized as the central coherence requirement on substitution-and-quantifier interaction. The principal result identifies the class of Beck-satisfying hyperdoctrines with first-order intuitionistic theories with equality.

Adds **finite coproducts to fibers** (yielding disjunction in the internal logic) on top of Lawvere's hyperdoctrine signature. The headline theorem is roughly: a hyperdoctrine satisfies the Beck condition for a pullback iff it "knows" the diagram is a pullback.

Primary source for the hyperdoctrinal layer (Beck–Chevalley as a condition on the predication hyperdoctrine).
