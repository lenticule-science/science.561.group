## Annotation

Revisits the differential-category formalism and compares two approaches to defining categorical differentiation: deriving transformations and coderelictions, showing their equivalence under the appropriate monoidal coalgebra-modality hypotheses. Predication categories' evidence-residual operation is deliberately weaker: it is a bilattice-native difference calculator, not a claim that the settling category already carries a differential category structure.
