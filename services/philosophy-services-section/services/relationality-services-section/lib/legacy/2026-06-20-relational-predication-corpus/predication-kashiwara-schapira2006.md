## Status

Pending direct access. Cited via secondary literature.

## Annotation

A modern reference for category theory and sheaf theory at graduate level: Grothendieck topologies and sites, abelian categories, derived functors, six-functor formalism, and the homological-algebra apparatus needed for sheaf cohomology in generality. For the program, the standard reference for sheaf-theoretic infrastructure used in [settling cohomology](../objects/settling-cohomology.md) and across the cohomology layer.
