## Annotation

Contemporary survey of sheaf theory with applications. Useful sections for the cohomological layer:

- **Appendix A.2 Proposition A.7.** Equivalence between arbitrary posets and Alexandrov spaces — clean modern statement of the classical result.
- **Appendix C.3.** Roos complex extension of the cellular cochain complex via the bar construction (relevant for infinite posets where the standard cellular complex is insufficient).
- **Appendix C.8 Algorithm 1.** A *minimal* cochain complex for sheaf cohomology on arbitrary finite posets — more optimal than the Roos complex.
- **Theorem C.48.** Cellular = derived: for cellular sheaves on a CW complex, cellular cohomology agrees with the derived-functor cohomology of the sheafification.

The cohomological layer cites for the cellular / poset sheaf framework that supports the bitopological site theory.
