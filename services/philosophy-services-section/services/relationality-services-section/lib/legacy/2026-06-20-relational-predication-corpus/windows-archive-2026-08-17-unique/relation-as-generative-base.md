# Relation

## What this node is

This node is the **base of a generative unfolding**, not a subject among subjects. It is **articulation** — the surface where a relation is brought into being at all: a `Mark` is arbitrary, a `Token` and `Claim` carry marks, an utterance pairs them. Nothing is yet said *of* anything; there is only the related. Everything else in this branch is generated from here by applying, one segment at a time, the morphisms of the relational substrate:

> **articulate → predicate → posit → settle**

Each `content/` step down the tree is the application of the next morphism, and each concept name is the conclusion that morphism earns. The path is therefore a **Curry–Howard proof coordinate**: to descend it is to construct the derivation of the concept at its leaf.

## What it generates

- **predicate** → [predication](content/predication/predication.md): saying something *of* the related; composing predicates generate a category. This is the generative home of [category theory](content/predication/content/category-theory/category-theory.md) and, one step in, of [type theory](content/predication/content/typed-theory/content/type-theory/type-theory.md).
- **posit** → [positing](content/predication/content/positing/positing.md): value enters predication by evidence, as a bilattice charge. The generative home of the value logics — [many-valued, paraconsistent, and substructural](content/predication/content/positing/content/mathematical-logic/mathematical-logic.md).
- **settle** → [settling](content/predication/content/positing/content/settling/settling.md): positing brought to its fixed points by ordinal closure. The generative home of [order theory](content/predication/content/positing/content/settling/content/order-theory/order-theory.md) and sheaf theory — *order is the structure settling is*.

## Orientation

Read [the generative unfolding](docs/explanations/the-generative-unfolding-of-mathematics.md) first: it derives the whole chain and shows why order theory sits under settling rather than beside category theory. Then descend the path — each directory is a morphism you apply, each file the proof the generator built it. This same chain is set out in the corpus's own research on predication categories and the four settling regimes; the library is organized from that research and from first-class reasoning, never from any program that implements it.

This branch is the first slice of the library reorganized from a flat subject taxonomy into the generative proof-path; the remaining domains migrate onto the same spine as their derivations are worked out.
