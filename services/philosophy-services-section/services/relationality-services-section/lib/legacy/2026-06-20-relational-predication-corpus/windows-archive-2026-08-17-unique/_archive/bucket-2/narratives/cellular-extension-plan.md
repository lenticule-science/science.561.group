# The cellular extension plan: from CW complexes to fibered transfinite cellular sites

The [framework-choice narrative](d-frame-cohomology-framework.md) recommends adopting the Tarski Laplacian framework ([@ghrist-riess2022]) — extended in 2025 to the **Lawvere Laplacian** for quantale-enriched cellular sheaves ([@ghrist-et-al2025]) — as the operational cohomology theory for [d-frame-valued sheaves](../objects/d-frame-valued-sheaf.md). The framework as published applies to cellular sheaves on regular CW complexes (Tarski Laplacian) and on undirected graphs with quantale-enriched structure (Lawvere Laplacian).

The corpus's [bitopological settling site](../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md) is *not* a CW complex. This narrative lays out, properly, the question of how to extend the framework to apply, the relevant literature situation, and the substantive new work the corpus needs to do.

## What's actually new in our setting

The corpus's site has three structural features that go beyond the published framework:

1. **It's a Grothendieck site, not a cell complex.** The site $\mathbf{T}^*_{\mathrm{site}}$ is a fibration over $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$ with topology $J$ generated by pairwise-Stone covers on the fiber. Its objects are triples $(q, \tau, U)$ where $U$ is a clopen in the [paired Priestley space](../objects/paired-priestley-space.md) at predicate-stage $(q, \tau)$. There's nothing intrinsically "cellular" about the global site — cellularity lives on the fibers.

2. **The fibers are cellular but the cell structure varies.** Each paired Priestley fiber is a cell complex (paired Priestley spaces of finite distributive bilattices have natural cellular structure — vertices for join-irreducibles, edges for covering relations, etc.). But the cellular structure changes as $(q, \tau)$ varies: the predicate $q$ varies via $\mathcal{C}$-morphisms, and $\tau$ varies via the [settling endofunctor](../morphisms/settling-endofunctor.md) advancement.

3. **For aperiodic-regime settlings, the fiber is transfinite-cellular.** Aperiodic settlings have $\omega_1$-cofinal fibers; their paired Priestley duals have cellular structure of $\omega_1$-cardinality. The published cellular sheaf literature handles finite cell complexes; transfinite cellular structure is genuinely new.

These three features layer: (1) is the most fundamental (fibered vs. cellular), (2) is moderate (varying cellular structure), and (3) is the deepest (transfinite cellular structure). The cellular extension splits accordingly.

## What the literature offers (and doesn't)

A focused review:

**Cellular sheaves on regular CW complexes** ([@curry2014]). Curry's framework is the gold standard for cellular sheaf theory: cellular sheaves and cosheaves, derived equivalence, Verdier duality, discrete Morse theory for computation. Limitation: regular finite CW complexes only. Not directly applicable to our site.

**Tarski Laplacian on lattice-valued cellular sheaves** ([@ghrist-riess2022]). Discrete Hodge theory for cellular sheaves valued in lattices and Galois connections. Direct precedent for non-abelian cohomology of d-frame-valued data. Same limitation: cell complexes.

**Lawvere Laplacian on quantale-enriched cellular sheaves on graphs** ([@ghrist-et-al2025]). Generalizes [@ghrist-riess2022] to quantale-enriched setting. Underlying base is undirected graphs (with cellular sheaves as pseudofunctors). Strict generalization of Tarski Laplacian. **Future directions**: extension to "quantaloid-enriched categories" and "arbitrary base spaces including simplicial sets."

**Sheaves on Grothendieck sites** (general theory). Standard sheaf theory works on any Grothendieck site; cohomology is derived global sections. Cellular sheaves are a special case (sheaves on the closure-space topology of the cell complex's face poset). So our bitopological settling site already supports standard sheaf theory in principle; the question is whether the value-category structure (quantale-enriched, lattice-valued, or d-frame-specific) lifts cleanly.

**Stratified sheaves and constructible sheaves** (MacPherson-style). Sheaves locally constant on strata of a stratified space, with stalks at strata corresponding to representations of the entrance path category ([@curry2014]'s extension). Possibly relevant for our fibered structure if the fibers can be viewed as strata, but this assumes stratification structure we haven't explicitly verified.

**Persistent / precision-graded cohomology for network sheaves** (recent: Riess et al. 2025–2026). Filtration-parameterized cohomology theories. Adjacent direction; probably not directly what we need but indicates the field's active development.

**Groupoid sheaves as quantale sheaves** (Hong et al. 2011). Establishes equivalence between sheaves on a groupoid and sheaves over a corresponding quantale. Suggestive given the corpus has [settling fiber groupoid](../objects/settling-fiber-groupoid.md), but the bitopological setting may differ structurally.

The literature has the *pieces* — cellular framework, quantale enrichment, Grothendieck-site sheaves, groupoid–quantale duality. None has assembled them into the specific shape we need.

## The plan, in three layers

The cellular extension splits into three substantive layers, each with its own literature situation:

### Layer 1: Quantale-enriched sheaves on Grothendieck sites — *in progress*

The corpus's [d-frame-valued sheaf](../objects/d-frame-valued-sheaf.md) has values in d-frames, which are paired-frame structures. Each frame is a quantale (with multiplication = meet). So d-frames are *paired quantales* with relations.

**Goal:** Define **quantale-enriched sheaves on Grothendieck sites** that specialize to d-frame-valued sheaves when the quantale enrichment is the d-frame structure.

**Status: definitional infrastructure in place; central lemma sketched.** The corpus has:

- [Q-enriched sheaf on a Grothendieck site](../objects/quantale-enriched-sheaf.md): the definitional framework. $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched presheaves $F : \mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}$ on a small category $\mathbf{T}$, sheaf condition for a Grothendieck topology $J$, generalizing [@ghrist-et-al2025]'s graph-indexed setting.
- [Lawvere Laplacian on Grothendieck sites](../theorems/lawvere-laplacian-grothendieck-sites.md): the substantive Layer-1 theorem, status sketch. States the Lawvere Laplacian formula on $\mathsf{Q}$-enriched presheaves over a Grothendieck site, and the central conjecture that **locality preserves sheafhood** — i.e., that the Laplacian restricts to an endofunctor on the sheaf subcategory, not just on presheaves.

**The substantive technical lemma needed:** weighted meets commute with equalizers in $\mathbf{Q\text{-}Cat}_{\mathrm{cwl}}$ (the category of complete weighted lattices and $\mathsf{Q}$-functors). Once this is established, the Tarski–Lawvere Fixed Point Theorem of [@ghrist-et-al2025] applies to the sheaf-restricted Laplacian, giving cohomology agreeing with global sections in degree zero.

**The corpus's existing infrastructure validates the framework on small cases.** The [strict-regime d-frame cohomology theorem](../theorems/strict-regime-d-frame-cohomology.md) and [Scott-continuous-regime d-frame cohomology theorem](../theorems/scott-continuous-regime-d-frame-cohomology.md) are both specializations of the Lawvere Laplacian on Grothendieck sites framework — they correspond to finite covers and locally-finite countable covers respectively. The proofs of those theorems implicitly verify the locality-preserves-sheafhood lemma in those cases via component-wise reduction. The abstract-categorical proof (for general Grothendieck sites) is the next concrete sub-problem.

### Layer 2: Fibered cellular structure on the bitopological settling site

The bitopological settling site's fibers are paired Priestley spaces, which are cellular for finite distributive bilattices. The fibered structure means the cellular complex *varies* across the fibration.

**Goal:** Recognize the bitopological settling site as a **fibered cellular site** — a Grothendieck site whose fibers are cell complexes, with face-relation-preserving morphisms encoding the cellular structure on each fiber, and base-changing morphisms encoding how cellular structure varies.

**Literature foothold:** Stratified sheaf theory and constructible sheaves provide partial precedent — sheaves on stratified spaces with locally-constant restriction to strata. Our fibered setting is more structured (each fiber is itself a cell complex, not just a stratum), but the techniques transfer.

**Concrete sub-problem:** Identify the right categorical framework — possibly *fibered cellular sites* or *cellular Grothendieck sites* — for stating the d-frame-valued cohomology. The Lawvere Laplacian extends fiber-by-fiber; assembling across the fibration may require additional descent / gluing data.

### Layer 3: Transfinite cellular structure for the aperiodic regime

For aperiodic-regime settlings, the paired Priestley fibers are $\omega_1$-cofinal. Their cellular structure is transfinite — cells indexed by countable ordinals up to $\omega_1$.

**Goal:** Extend the cellular sheaf framework — and specifically the Tarski / Lawvere Laplacian — to **transfinite cell complexes**.

**Literature foothold:** This is where the published literature is thinnest. CW complexes are standardly indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ (skeleta of dimensions 0, 1, 2, ...); transfinite skeleta have been considered in homotopy theory but cellular sheaf theory hasn't been extended. The closest analogue is direct-limit techniques: an $\omega_1$-cell complex as a transfinite colimit of finite sub-complexes, with sheaves and cohomology computed as colimits / limits of finite-stage data.

**Concrete sub-problem:** State and prove a *transfinite cellular sheaf cohomology* theorem: for a cell complex with $\omega_1$-cell structure, sheaf cohomology can be computed as the direct/inverse limit of cohomologies on finite sub-complexes. This is plausibly true (the corpus has analogous direct-limit theorems in its existing cohomology, e.g., the [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md)) but requires careful framework-level proof.

## What this gives us

The three-layer plan, executed, yields:

- **Layer 1 → quantale-enriched cohomology on Grothendieck sites.** Transports immediately to d-frame-valued setting.
- **Layer 2 → fibered cellular cohomology.** Recognizes the bitopological settling site's structure and applies layer 1 fiber-by-fiber with proper assembly.
- **Layer 3 → transfinite cellular cohomology.** Handles the aperiodic regime's $\omega_1$-cofinal fibers via direct-limit techniques.

Combined: **a complete cohomology theory for d-frame-valued sheaves on predication categories' bitopological settling site, across all four regimes**.

The strict and Scott-continuous regimes have $H^1 = 0$ already; layer 1 + layer 2 should recover this on the d-frame side. Transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes have $H^1 \neq 0$; the d-frame-valued $H^1$ may match the existing $\mathbf{Ab}$-valued $H^1$ via the [shadow comparison](../theorems/d-frame-cohomology-comparison.md) (with consistency/totality structure as additional invariants), or it may not — the answer is part of what the extension establishes.

The vanishing-above-degree-1 question becomes: does the layer-1+2+3 framework give $H^{\geq 2} = 0$ for predication categories' d-frame-valued sheaves? The 3-axis vanishing argument's fiber-Boolean axis becomes a fiber-d-frame axis under the extension; whether the dimension argument still gives 0 is the headline open theorem.

## Position relative to the program

This is a **multi-paper research direction**, not a session-scale piece of work. Each layer is plausibly a paper or a substantial chapter in its own right. The corpus's contribution is to identify the layered structure, articulate the substantive sub-problems, and connect to the active research literature.

For the [bilattice-valued cohomology chapter](bilattice-cohomology-opener.md) of the corpus, the realistic path forward:

1. **Engage actively with the Ghrist–Lopez–North–Riess group's work.** The [@ghrist-et-al2025] paper explicitly identifies extensions to "arbitrary base spaces" as future directions; predication categories' bitopological settling site is one such target. Collaboration or correspondence is plausibly the most efficient path.

2. **Develop layer 1 first.** Quantale-enriched sheaves on Grothendieck sites is the most general piece and has the broadest applicability. Once established, layers 2 and 3 are specializations.

3. **Use the shadow framework as scaffolding throughout.** Until layers 1–3 are established for predication categories' specific site, every theorem stated in d-frame cohomology has at least the shadow meaning ($\mathbf{Ab}$-valued cohomology of the pair), and the shadow-comparison establishes a baseline equivalence.

This is a **research bet** with concrete entry points. The literature situation is favorable: the framework we want is on the active frontier of a productive research group, not a dead-end question. Engaging the literature properly — citing the right precedents, articulating the specific extensions needed, connecting to predication categories' own closure-monad / Tarski-fixed-point structure — makes the path forward visible even where the literature hasn't yet caught up.

## Layer-by-layer status

The chain of theorems implementing the layers is now in place at the corpus's standard rigor:

- **Layer 1 (per-fiber Curry, finite cases):** [Q-enriched Curry on regular CW complexes](../theorems/q-enriched-curry-cw-complex.md) (proved). Built up from [Q-enriched Curry 2-cell strict](../theorems/q-enriched-curry-2-cell.md) and [2-cell fuzzy](../theorems/q-enriched-curry-2-cell-fuzzy.md) base cases, then induction on dimension with explicit equalizer-coequalizer transport via Lopez 2024's fuzzy adjunctions.
- **Layer 2 (Grothendieck-site lift):** [Q-enriched Curry on Grothendieck sites with cellular fibers](../theorems/q-enriched-curry-grothendieck-site.md) (proved). The fiber-only-cover structure of the bitopological settling site enables base-presheaf assembly with explicit fuzziness profile $q_n^{\mathrm{glob}}$.
- **Layer 3 (transfinite-cellular fibers):** [Q-enriched Curry on transfinite cell complexes](../theorems/q-enriched-curry-transfinite-cw.md) (proved). Directed-colimit assembly handles $\omega_1$-cofinal cellular fibers in the aperiodic regime.

With Layers 1+2+3 closed, [Verdier duality on the bitopological settling site](../theorems/verdier-duality-bitopological-site.md) follows (proved), assembling the layers across all four regimes. Downstream, the [closed-monoidal structure on Q-enriched sheaves](../theorems/q-enriched-sheaf-closed-monoidal-structure.md) and [direct/inverse image functors](../theorems/q-enriched-sheaf-direct-inverse-image.md) give the full six-functor package on the bitopological settling site (with the four standard axioms — closed-monoidal compat, projection formula, Beck–Chevalley, Verdier exchange — at corpus rigor).

The [higher-degree vanishing theorem](../theorems/q-enriched-cohomology-higher-degree-vanishing.md) closes the chapter's structural picture: every regime's d-frame cohomology vanishes above degree 1 in the cochain sense, paralleling the existing $\mathbf{Ab}$-valued vanishing. Combined with the regime-by-regime cohomology theorems (which compute $H^0$ and $H^1$ explicitly), the chapter has the full cohomological description of the bitopological settling site in the d-frame setting.

The cellular extension plan is closed at corpus rigor for the **Boolean d-frame scope** (per the [Boolean d-frame derived cohomology agreement theorem](../theorems/q-enriched-derived-cohomology-boolean-d-frame.md)). The non-Boolean extensions (Heyting frames, Lawvere quantale, general derived-categorical machinery) are sketch theorems with named scopes — see the trio of [Hodge-on-non-Boolean-frames](../theorems/q-enriched-hodge-theorem-non-boolean-frames.md), [Hodge-on-Lawvere-quantale](../theorems/q-enriched-hodge-theorem-lawvere-quantale.md), and [Q-enriched Grothendieck spectral sequence](../theorems/q-enriched-grothendieck-spectral-sequence.md).
