## Full citation

F. Durand, B. Host, C. Skau. "Substitutional dynamical systems, Bratteli diagrams and dimension groups." *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 19, 953–993, 1999.

## Status

Canonical reference for the correspondence between primitive aperiodic substitutions and stationary Bratteli–Vershik diagrams. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Establishes that primitive aperiodic substitutions on a finite alphabet correspond to **stationary** Bratteli–Vershik diagrams (every level of the diagram identical) plus stationary odometers. The stationary BV setting at fixed alphabet size $n$ is the substitution-dynamical analogue of predication categories' $\mathscr{M}^{(n)}$.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Identified by the literature stress-test as the closest BV-side analogue to predication categories' [step-$n$ moduli substack](../objects/step-n-moduli-substack.md). The corpus's $\sigma_n^{n+1}$ step-shift, taking $\mathscr{M}^{(n)} \to \mathscr{M}^{(n+1)}$, would correspond on the BV side to a *non-stationary* diagram with growing levels $|V_k|$. Whether predication categories' step-shift admits a clean BV-diagram representation, and whether $\mathscr{M}^{(\infty)}$ matches the moduli of growing-rank BV diagrams, is open — see [canonical step-shift morphism](questions/canonical-step-shift-morphism.md).
