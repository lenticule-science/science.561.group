## Annotation

Survey-quality treatment of bilattices, written as the canonical modern entry point. All the basic definitions used throughout our program appear here:

- **Pre-bilattice, interlaced, distributive, with negation, with conflation.** Definitional cleanup over Ginsberg 1988 and Fitting 1991.
- **Product construction $L_1 \odot L_2$** (in Fitting's notation; we write this as the *twist product* $L_1 \bowtie L_2$, using Jakl's convention to avoid the $\odot$-collision with twist-product residuation).
- **Interval construction**, **pointwise lift** $B^S$, exact and consistent elements.
- **Kripke fixed-point** in three variants (relevant to closure on predication fibers).

Notation in the source: $\otimes$ for $\leq_k$-meet (consensus), $\oplus$ for $\leq_k$-join (gullibility); $\wedge, \vee$ for $\leq_t$-meet/join; $\neg$ for negation; $-$ for conflation; $\mathbf{FOUR}$ for the four-element bilattice. Our program substitutes $\wedge_k, \vee_k$ for Fitting's $\otimes, \oplus$ to disambiguate from the Chu/linear-logic tensor and from the twist-product residuation product.

Source for §2.1 (bilattice definitions), §5 (Kripke–Fitting fixed point), and §8 (negation/conflation gauge).
