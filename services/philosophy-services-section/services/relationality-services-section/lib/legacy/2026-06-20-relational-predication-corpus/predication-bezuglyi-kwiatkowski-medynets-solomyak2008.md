## Full citation

S. Bezuglyi, J. Kwiatkowski, K. Medynets, B. Solomyak. "Aperiodic substitution systems and their Bratteli diagrams." *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 29, 37–72, 2009. arXiv:0807.3621.

## Status

Standard reference for aperiodic substitution systems modeled via Bratteli–Vershik diagrams. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Develops the BV-diagram model of aperiodic substitution dynamical systems, refining [@durand-host-skau1999]: any primitive aperiodic substitution is conjugate to a stationary properly-ordered BV-system, with explicit construction of the BV-diagram from the substitution rule.

**Relevance to the corpus.** Cited adjacent to [@durand-host-skau1999] when discussing how predication categories' [step-$n$ moduli substack](../objects/step-n-moduli-substack.md) and [step-$n$ fixed-point object](../objects/step-n-fixed-point.md) compare to the BV-diagram side of the literature.
