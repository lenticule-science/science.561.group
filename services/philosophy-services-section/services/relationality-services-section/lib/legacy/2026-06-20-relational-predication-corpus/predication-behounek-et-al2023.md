## Annotation

A free-logic variant accommodating both truth-value gluts and gaps, built on a suitable expansion of the Belnap–Dunn four-valued logic with a normality predicate. Uses **dual-domain semantics**: an inner domain of "existing" entities and an outer domain of all entities.

This is the closest prior art to the predication hyperdoctrine's quantifier structure: it shows how to handle existential and universal quantification when truth values include $\bot$ and $\top$, and it works out the relevant soundness/completeness results.

The hyperdoctrinal layer cites this as a shape-precedent for the two adjoint systems ($\leq_k$ and $\leq_t$). The hyperdoctrinal layer records the open problem of relating the FOL on the predication hyperdoctrine to BD∆ (Omori–Wansing's four-valued first-order system); Běhounek et al. is the bridge candidate.
