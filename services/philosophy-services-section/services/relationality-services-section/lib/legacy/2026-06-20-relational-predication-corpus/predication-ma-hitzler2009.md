## Annotation

Applies the four-valued bilattice $\mathbf{FOUR}$ to OWL 2 semantics, giving a paraconsistent description-logic framework. The framework treats inconsistent KBs as locally inconsistent (recording $\top$-valued assertions) rather than trivializing globally (as classical OWL does via $p \wedge \neg p \vdash q$).

For our program: prior art on bilattice-valued knowledge representation, working at the semantics layer rather than the structural layer. Our contribution relative to Ma–Hitzler: where they apply $\mathbf{FOUR}$ as a value domain to existing OWL semantics, the the framework develops the **structural theory** of the bilattice-valued knowledge base as an object in its own right (presheaf, hyperdoctrine, cohomology). The foundational layer cite as motivation; the foundational layer sketches a downstream "bilattice-valued OWL" construction that builds on predication settlings as the underlying KB object.
