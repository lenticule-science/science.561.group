- [summary](summary):: The **domain of relating** is the minimal relational structure induced by the tension between [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate) and [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate](../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate).

It contains the acts of inclusion and exclusion, and the act that holds them in mutual tension.

This domain is not primitive — it is induced, triadic, and minimal.

## Members

- relating  
- unrelating  
- relating(relating, unrelating)

## Properties

- Triadic: exactly three elements  
- Induced: does not exist without opposing acts  
- Minimal: contains only what is required to generate structure

## Expression

domain of relating := {relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating)}
