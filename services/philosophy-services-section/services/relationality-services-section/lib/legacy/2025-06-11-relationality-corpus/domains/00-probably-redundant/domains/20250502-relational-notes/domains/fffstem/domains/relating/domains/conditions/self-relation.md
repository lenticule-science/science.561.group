- [summary](summary):: **self-relation** is the [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/conditions/condition](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/conditions/condition) in which a [relational structure](relational structure) is [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|related](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|related) to itself through the [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/act](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/act) of [../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex).

Self-relation does not assume identity or self-similarity. It is induced when a structure performs the act of [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate) upon itself.

This condition enables the formation of nested structural layers and recursive participation.

## Expression

self-relation(S): reflex(S) = relating(S, S)

## Properties

- Requires a structure to be an admissible input to [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate).
- Cannot be derived from [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate) or [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate](../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate) alone.
- Enables layered depth: a structure becomes part of itself again.
