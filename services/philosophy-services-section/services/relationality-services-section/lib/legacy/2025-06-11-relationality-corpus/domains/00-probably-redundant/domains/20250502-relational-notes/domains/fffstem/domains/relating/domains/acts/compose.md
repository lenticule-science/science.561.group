- [summary](summary):: **Compose** is the act of [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating) multiple unary [relational structures](relational structures) into a single, ordered composite at a shared [sequence](sequence) level.

Compose performs structured inclusion of multiple distinct components into a unified relational form, preserving both their individual identities and their positional order.

## Expression

compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n):  
→ Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)

## Properties

- Arity-preserving: the composite retains `p` distinct components  
- Order-preserving: the position of each `x(i)` is fixed within the tuple  
- Compatible with [../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex) and [reflect](reflect): components remain accessible under further relational acts
