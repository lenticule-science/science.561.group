The ***relational admissibility predicate*** (R) is [[induce|induced]] by
the [[collapse induction function]] (κ).

> R(x, y) := (x, y) ∈ dom(κ)

R(x, y) holds if the [[pair]] (x, y) is [[admit|admissible]] within the [[domain]]
defined by κ. This [[evaluate|evaluates]] whether a [[relation]] can exist without
[[violate|violating]] the [[constraint]] of κ.
