Relational [closure operators](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) are the self-maintenance structures that the derivation produces at three scales: self-sustaining closure (step 3), field closure (step 7), and grand closure (step 18). Each is a map that, applied to a structure, returns the smallest self-sustaining envelope containing it.

In standard mathematics, a [closure operator](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) on a [partially ordered](../../../../mathematics/objects/posets/terms/partial-order.md) set is a function *c* that is extensive (*x* ≤ *c*(*x*)), monotone (*x* ≤ *y* implies *c*(*x*) ≤ *c*(*y*)), and idempotent (*c*(*c*(*x*)) = *c*(*x*)). A *nucleus* on a frame or locale is a [closure operator](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) that additionally preserves finite meets. See the existing learn-closure-operators skill for the standard treatment.

What distinguishes the *relational* [closure operators](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) is that they appear at three scales, each forced by what the previous leaves undetermined:

1. **Self-sustaining closure (step 3).** [Sustaining](../../../terms/sustaining.md) mediates between [Relating](../../../terms/relating.md) (act) and [Relation](../../../terms/relation.md) (condition), producing [Self-Coherence](../../../terms/self-coherence.md) and then [Closure](../../../terms/closure.md). The relational unit maintains itself through its own activity. This is the first [closure operator](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md): applied to the unit, it returns the unit.
2. **Field closure (step 7).** After multiplicity, meta-structure, and recursive domain unfolding, [Closure Criteria](../../../terms/closure-criteria.md) describe what it takes for an integrated field to be self-sustaining. This generalizes unit-level closure to the entire relational field — a [closure operator](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) on the field of units and their relations.
3. **Grand closure (step 18).** [Profile](../../../terms/profile.md)s reconstruct the full derivation internally. The derivation applied to itself yields itself — a [fixed point](../../../../mathematics/concepts/fixed-point/index.md). Grand closure is the [closure operator](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) whose [fixed points](../../../../mathematics/concepts/fixed-point/index.md) are the profiles that reconstruct the whole.

[Nucleus](../../../terms/nucleus.md) (step 16) is the generalization of closure to the full field of [Term](../../../terms/term.md)s and [Judgement](../../../terms/judgement.md)s: the smallest closed structure containing something. Where closure at step 3 is structural and closure at step 7 is field-theoretic, Nucleus operates on the formalized syntax, giving closure its algebraic character. [Flow](../../../terms/flow.md) and Nucleus form a [residuated](../../../concepts/residuation.md) pair whose commutation produces [Geometry](../../../terms/geometry.md).

For the derivation-internal account of closure across scales, see [Closure](../../../concepts/closure.md).
