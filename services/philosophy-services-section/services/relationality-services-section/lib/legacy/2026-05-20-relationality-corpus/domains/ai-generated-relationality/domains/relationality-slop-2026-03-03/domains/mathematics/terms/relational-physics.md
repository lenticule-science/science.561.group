Relational physics is the operational layer that the derivation produces at step 17 (Profiles and Physics), when [Geometry](../../../terms/geometry.md) acts on the contents of [Profile](../../../terms/profile.md)s. Four structures emerge: [Observable](../../../terms/observable.md), [State](../../../terms/state.md), [Evolution](../../../terms/evolution.md), and [Measurement](../../../terms/measurement.md). Together they constitute the physics of the relational field — not an application of the derivation to physics but a structure that the derivation forces.

In standard mathematics, the four structures correspond to elements of observable algebras, states on those algebras, dynamical semigroups (evolution), and positive operator-valued measures (measurement). The relationship between evolution and measurement — the fact that how things change and how things are observed constrain each other — appears in quantum mechanics as the Heisenberg vs. Schrodinger picture. In the relational derivation, this relationship is [Residuation](../../../concepts/residuation.md): the same [adjunction](../../../../mathematics/disciplines/higher-topos-theory/terms/adjunction.md) pattern that governs [Implication](../../../terms/implication.md) in the [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra/index.md) and [Flow](../../../terms/flow.md)-[Nucleus](../../../terms/nucleus.md) interaction in the dynamics.

The derivation produces these structures as follows:

1. A [Filter](../../../terms/filter.md) carves out a Profile — a complete relational universe with its own Terms, Judgements, Order, Flow, Nucleus, and Geometry.
2. Within a Profile, [Observation](../../../terms/observation.md) is localized: an [Observable](../../../terms/observable.md) is what can be seen from a particular position in a particular Profile.
3. A [State](../../../terms/state.md) is what remains fixed under a Profile's Flow — the structures that directed transformation does not currently change.
4. [Evolution](../../../terms/evolution.md) is Flow applied to a State: how [the state](../../../../sociology/terms/the-state.md) changes. [Measurement](../../../terms/measurement.md) is Nucleus applied to a State: how [the state](../../../../sociology/terms/the-state.md) is observed.
5. Evolution and Measurement interact through Residuation — the third and final level where this pattern recurs (after logic and dynamics).

The physics is internal to each Profile. Different Profiles may yield different Observables, States, Evolution, and Measurement from the same underlying field, because each Profile reconstructs the derivation with its own perspective. This is the relational analog of frame-dependence in physics: what you observe depends on where you stand, but the structure governing observation is the same everywhere.

The old formulation of relationality explored connections to conservation laws, transport equations, detailed balance (forward-reverse symmetry), entropy (monotonicity under flow-measure evolution), and spectral decomposition. These remain areas for further development within the canonical framework.
