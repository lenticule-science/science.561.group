# Context Geometry

A geometry requires compatible flow and nucleus.  We combine
[ContextFlow](context-flow.md) and [ContextNucleus](context-nucleus.md) to form
the contextual geometry:
`(ContextFlow ContextNucleus)` earns ***ContextGeometry***.

The flow and nucleus inherit the compatibility laws of
[FlowProfile](flow-profile.md) and [NucleateProfile](nucleate-profile.md), so
their composition yields the geometric act capturing contextual dynamics under
the profile evolution.
