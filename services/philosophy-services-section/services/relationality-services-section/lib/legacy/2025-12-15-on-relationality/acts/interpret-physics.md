# Semantizing Physics

## Interpretation

Physical semantics interpret the syntactic calculus inside a geometry endowed with observables.  *Systematize Physics* witnessing *Flow* yields ***Interpret Physics*** ([InterpretPhysics](interpret-physics.md)).

This act records the canonical physical interpretation built from geometry, flow, and observables, so *Interpret Physics* is valid.
