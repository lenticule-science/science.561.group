# Normalizing Observation

## Interpretation

Evaluated observations must normalize back to the observation witness.  Letting *Normalization* witness *Evaluate Observation* produces ***Normalize Observation*** ([NormalizeObservation](normalize-observation.md)).

This composition records the normalization of evaluations, so *Normalize Observation* follows.
