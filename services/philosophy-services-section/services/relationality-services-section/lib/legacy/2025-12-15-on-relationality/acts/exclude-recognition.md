## Interpretation

To capture orthogonality we watch the meet collapse to the bottom.  *Meet Recognition* witnessing *Ground Recognition* yields ***Exclude Recognition*** ([ExcludeRecognition](exclude-recognition.md)).

The act records exactly when the meet vanishes, so *Exclude Recognition* follows.
