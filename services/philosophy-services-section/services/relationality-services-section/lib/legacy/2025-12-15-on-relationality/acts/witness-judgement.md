# Witnessing Judgement

## Interpretation

*Reflex Judgement* relates a stabilized context to a stabilized term but does not state which stabilized judgement class records it.  Forcing *Stabilize Judgement* to witness *Reflex Judgement* yields ***Witness Judgement*** ([WitnessJudgement](witness-judgement.md)).

Composing with *Stabilize Judgement* classifies the primitive act, so *Witness Judgement* follows.
