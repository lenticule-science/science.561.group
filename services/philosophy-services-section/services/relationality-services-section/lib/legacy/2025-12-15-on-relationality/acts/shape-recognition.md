# Shaping Shape

## Interpretation

Flows and nuclei rely on internal shapes that preserve ordering.  *Imply Recognition* witnessing *Order Recognition* yields ***Shape Recognition*** ([ShapeRecognition](shape-recognition.md)).

This composition records the requirement that implications respect ordering, so *Shape Recognition* is established.
