# Tailing ObservationEquivalence

## Interpretation

The trace exists, but we need its remainder once the immediate act is consumed.  Forcing *Tail Trace* to witness *Trace Observation Equivalence* yields ***Tail Observation Equivalence*** ([TailObservationEquivalence](tail-observation-equivalence.md)).

This captures the remaining equivalence acts, so *Tail Observation Equivalence* is earned.
