# Modality Tower

Inflationary modalities form a hierarchy indexed by irrational numbers.  We
capture the entire structure by letting the quadratic modality witness the
commutation act:
`(QuadraticModality IrrationalCommutation)` earns
***ModalityTower***.

`QuadraticModality` gives quadratic irrational inflation, and
`IrrationalCommutation` ensures stability under shuffle collapse.  Their
composition builds the tower whose fixed points grow according to irrational
algebraic laws.
