# Fixing Flow

## Interpretation

A flow exists, yet we must capture the invariants it leaves untouched.  *Stabilize Trace* witnessing *Flow* produces ***Fix Flow*** ([FixFlow](fix-flow.md)).

Applying the flow to a stabilized trace uses only earned terms and records precisely those traces that remain invariant, so *Fix Flow* follows.
