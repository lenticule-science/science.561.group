# Meeting Recognition

## Interpretation

Ordering tells us when one recognition subsumes another, but we also need their greatest shared part.  Forcing *Order Recognition* to witness *Entry* yields ***Meet Recognition*** ([MeetRecognition](meet-recognition.md)).

*Entry* records paired recognitions; composing it with the order captures their joint lower bound, so *Meet Recognition* is earned.
