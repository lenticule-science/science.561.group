### Definition — Closure and Self-Generation {#closure-and-self-generation}

*How $R$ generates itself: the closure operator, its fixed point, and what the fixed point looks like.*

Three structures remain after algebra, language, and geometry have done their work. The first is
the generative machinery itself — the operations that close a universe under one complete cycle of
construction, extending it by exactly one shell. The second is the iteration of that machinery —
the tower of depth levels, its fixed point, and the aperiodic order the fixed point exhibits. The
third is the limit structure that crystallizes only when the full tower is in place — the internal
phase space, its spectral decomposition, its cohomological obstructions, and the duality between
construction and observation.

The Axiom–Theorem boundary runs through this section. The Axiom of Finite Generation states the
primitive generative vocabulary as a condition on the data. The Theorem of Least Fixed Point
(§self-generation) then proves that $R = U_G(R)$: what was stipulated from outside becomes a
provable identity inside the structure. Every claim after §self-generation is a theorem about
$R$, not a further axiom.

This section closes with the Theorem of Nuclear Quartet Closure (§nuclear-quartet-closure): $U_G$ is the nuclear quartet
operator and $R$ is its unique fixed point containing the atoms. This is not the end of the
program — it is the end of the construction of $R$. The Relational Universe $R$ is the input
for the same construction one level up. The least fixed point of that meta-level construction
is the Relational Hyperverse $\mathcal{H}$ (§relational-hyperverse).
