### Construction — The Hyperverse Tower {#hyperverse-tower}

The **hyperverse tower** is the sequence of relational universes
$$
R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)} \hookrightarrow R^{(2)} \hookrightarrow \cdots
$$
built by iterating the meta-level Universe Closure $U_\mathcal{H}$ (§meta-universe-closure).

#### Level Zero {#tower-level-0}

$R^{(0)} = R$: the canonical relational universe from the construction of R, constructed from the
primitive history data $(T, J, H)$.

#### Level One {#tower-level-1}

$R^{(1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}, J_\mathbf{RU})$: the topos of sheaves on the site
of relational universes. This is built by applying $U_\mathcal{H}$ (§meta-universe-closure)
with the category of relational universes $\mathbf{RU}$ (§universe-category) in the role of the
history category. The fiber algebras over an object $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}$ are the
Heyting algebras of propositions *about* the universe $\mathbf{U}$ — specifically, the
Heyting algebra $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ of stable propositions in $\mathbf{U}$, with nuclei induced
by the embedding $\mathbf{U} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)}$.

The embedding $R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)}$ is the Yoneda embedding: each universe
$\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}$ corresponds to a representable sheaf $y(\mathbf{U}) \in R^{(1)}$,
and $R = R^{(0)}$ is one such universe (the canonical first-turn object).

#### Level $n+1$ from Level $n$ {#tower-induction}

Given $R^{(n)}$, the next level $R^{(n+1)}$ is constructed by one application of
$U_\mathcal{H}$ (§meta-universe-closure, §meta-universe-closure):

1. **Current-level universe category**: $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the category of
   relational universes constructed up to and including level $n$, with universe
   morphisms between them. $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is defined externally at each step:
   $\mathbf{RU}^{(0)} = \mathbf{RU}$ (all primitive relational universes);
   $\mathbf{RU}^{(n+1)} = \mathbf{RU}^{(n)} \cup \{R^{(n+1)}\}$ (add the new universe).

2. **Fiber algebras at level $n+1$**: for each $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$, the
   fiber is the active layer $H_\mathbf{U}^*$ (§fixed-fiber) — propositions in $\mathbf{U}$
   fixed by both $\sigma$ and $\Delta$.

3. **Topology**: $J^{(n+1)}$ is the profile topology on $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ generated by
   the density covers (§universe-as-object).

Then $R^{(n+1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}, J^{(n+1)})$.

The embedding $R^{(n)} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)}$ is the Yoneda embedding: the
universe $R^{(n)}$ (as an object of $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$) maps to the representable
$y(R^{(n)}) \in R^{(n+1)}$. Note: $\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ is the site for $R^{(n+1)}$,
not an "internal category of $R^{(n)}$" — a relational universe $\mathbf{U}$ acts as
both a topos (the environment for its internal logic) and as an object in the growing
site (a point over which the next level sheafifies). These two roles are distinct and
should not be conflated.

#### The Tower Is Strictly Increasing {#tower-strict}

Each embedding $R^{(n)} \hookrightarrow R^{(n+1)}$ is proper: $R^{(n+1)}$ contains objects
not in $R^{(n)}$. The new objects at level $n+1$ are propositions *about* level-$n$ universes
— objects that cannot be represented within $R^{(n)}$ itself, because they require $R^{(n)}$
as data rather than just as ambient context.

This is the same mechanism as the depth filtration within a single universe (§depth-filtration):
each depth level adds genuinely new objects. The hyperverse tower is the depth filtration
operating one level up.

#### The Colimit {#tower-colimit}

The Relational Hyperverse is defined as the colimit of the tower in the $2$-category of
Grothendieck toposes:
$$
\mathcal{H} \;=\; \varinjlim_n R^{(n)}.
$$
This colimit is taken along the Yoneda embeddings; its existence as a well-defined
Grothendieck topos requires that the tower satisfies coherence conditions (the Beck–Chevalley
conditions from §beck-chevalley-tower). The claim that $\mathcal{H}$ is itself a relational universe
— satisfying all the axioms of §history-and-fiber-algebra through §closure-and-self-generation, but now at the spiral level —
is the content of §hyperverse-fixed-point.

#### Distinguishing the Two Towers {#two-towers}

The paper contains two towers that operate at different levels and must not be conflated:

**Tower 1 — Depth filtration within $R$** (§depth-filtration):
$$
R_0 \hookrightarrow R_1 \hookrightarrow \cdots \hookrightarrow R,
\qquad R_n = \mathbf{Sh}(T^{(n)}, J|_{T^{(n)}}),
$$
where $T^{(n)} = \{t \in T \mid \mathrm{depth}(t) \leq n\}$ is a sub-history-category
of the *fixed* ambient $T$. This tower lives entirely within the construction of R; its
colimit is $R$ itself. The $U_G$ of §closure-operator operates here.

**Tower 2 — Hyperverse tower** (this section):
$$
R^{(0)} \hookrightarrow R^{(1)} \hookrightarrow \cdots \hookrightarrow \mathcal{H},
\qquad R^{(n+1)} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}, J^{(n+1)}),
$$
where each step produces a *strictly new* relational universe with a larger history
category. This tower operates at the spiral level; its colimit is $\mathcal{H}$.
The $U_\mathcal{H}$ of §meta-universe-closure operates here.

The two towers are related by a self-similarity: Tower 1 describes how $R$ is built
from atomic histories by the object-level $U_G$; Tower 2 describes how $\mathcal{H}$ is
built from atomic universes (including $R$) by the meta-level $U_\mathcal{H}$. The
depth filtration within each $R^{(n)}$ is Tower 1 instantiated at level $n$.
