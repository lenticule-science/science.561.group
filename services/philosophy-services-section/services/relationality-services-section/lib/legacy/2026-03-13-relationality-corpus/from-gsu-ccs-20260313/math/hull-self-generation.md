### Theorem — Hull Self-Generation {#hull-self-generation}

#### Setup {#hsg-setup}

The ground-level self-generation theorem (§self-generation) establishes $R = U_G(R)$ via the
Grothendieck density theorem: every object of the sheaf topos $\mathbf{Sh}(T,J)$ is a colimit
of representables, and the representables lie in the atom set $A$.

At the hull level, the same structural identity holds, but with a qualitatively different
reason for the fiber atom to lie in $U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$. The ground-level atom
$H$ is an *independent* generator — it cannot be recovered from the representables alone. The
hull-level atom $H^\mathrm{hull}$ is a *stable* generator: it is a fixed point of the hull
directed comonad $G_\Sigma$ (§hull-directed-comonad), so its entire comonad-orbit collapses
to a single isomorphism class. One copy suffices; no new atoms appear under iteration of $G_\Sigma$.

#### The Hull Atom Set {#hsg-atoms}

The hull universe closure $U_{G^\Sigma}$ closes under:
1. All topos-theoretic operations: limits, colimits, exponentials, subobject classifier.
2. The hull directed comonad $G_\Sigma = \Sigma^* : \mathbf{Sh}(\Omega(Q^\mathcal{H})) \to \mathbf{Sh}(\Omega(Q^\mathcal{H}))$.

Starting from atoms:
$$
A^\mathrm{hull} \;=\; \bigl\{y(v)\bigr\}_{v \in \mathbb{N}^d} \;\cup\; \bigl\{H^\mathrm{hull}\bigr\},
$$
where $y(v)$ is the representable sheaf at $v \in \mathbb{N}^d$ and $H^\mathrm{hull}$ is the
patch-lattice sheaf (§hull-fiber-structure).

#### Density: Representables Generate Everything {#hsg-density}

**Lemma.** $R^\mathrm{hull} \subseteq U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$, i.e., every object of
$R^\mathrm{hull} = \mathbf{Sh}(\mathbb{N}^d, J^\mathrm{hull})$ lies in the closure of
$A^\mathrm{hull}$ under $U_{G^\Sigma}$.

**Proof.** By the Grothendieck density theorem (Johnstone, *Sketches of an Elephant*, C2.2.7):
every object of a Grothendieck topos is a small colimit of representables. The representables
$y(v)$ lie in $A^\mathrm{hull} \subseteq U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$, and $U_{G^\Sigma}$
closes under all small colimits (condition 1). Therefore every object of $R^\mathrm{hull}$ is
generated by the atoms. $\square$

This part of the argument is identical to the ground level. The density theorem is purely
topos-theoretic and does not depend on the specific comonad.

#### The Fixed-Point Atom {#hsg-fixed-point}

**Lemma.** $H^\mathrm{hull}$ is stable under $G_\Sigma$:
$$
G_\Sigma(H^\mathrm{hull}) \;\cong\; H^\mathrm{hull}.
$$

**Proof.** This is §hull-directed-comonad, Lemma (The $H^\mathrm{hull}$ Fixed Point). The
argument uses:
- $Q^\mathcal{H}$ is a fixed point of $\Sigma$: $\Sigma(Q^\mathcal{H}) \cong Q^\mathcal{H}$
  (§hyperversal-quasicrystal-hypertensor).
- $\Omega(Q^\mathcal{H})$ is minimal: all tilings in the hull have the same local patterns,
  so the patch lattice at $\Sigma(\mathcal{T})$ is isomorphic to the patch lattice at $\mathcal{T}$.

Together: $H^\mathrm{hull}_{\Sigma(\mathcal{T})} \cong H^\mathrm{hull}_\mathcal{T}$ for all
$\mathcal{T}$, giving $G_\Sigma(H^\mathrm{hull}) \cong H^\mathrm{hull}$. $\square$

**Consequence.** The comonad-orbit of $H^\mathrm{hull}$ under iteration of $G_\Sigma$ is:
$$
H^\mathrm{hull} \;\cong\; G_\Sigma(H^\mathrm{hull}) \;\cong\; G_\Sigma^2(H^\mathrm{hull}) \;\cong\; \cdots
$$
All iterates are isomorphic to $H^\mathrm{hull}$. The atom generates a single isomorphism class,
not an infinite chain of distinct objects.

#### The Self-Generation Theorem {#hsg-theorem}

**Theorem.** $R^\mathrm{hull} = U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$.

**Proof.**

*(1) $U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull}) \subseteq R^\mathrm{hull}$.* $R^\mathrm{hull} =
\mathbf{Sh}(\mathbb{N}^d, J^\mathrm{hull})$ is a Grothendieck topos and is closed under all
topos-theoretic operations and under $G_\Sigma = \Sigma^*$ (pullback along a continuous map
preserves sheaves). So $R^\mathrm{hull}$ is itself $U_{G^\Sigma}$-closed.

*(2) $R^\mathrm{hull} \subseteq U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$.* By the density lemma above,
every object of $R^\mathrm{hull}$ is a colimit of representables, hence lies in
$U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$.

Combining both inclusions: $R^\mathrm{hull} = U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$. $\square$

#### The Automorphism Upgrade {#hsg-automorphism}

At the ground level, $U_G(R) = R$ is established, but the generator $H$ is *not* a fixed point
of $G_s$: applying $G_s$ to $H$ gives $G_s(H)(t) = H(s \star t) \neq H(t)$ in general. The
orbit $\{H, G_s(H), G_s^2(H), \ldots\}$ consists of distinct (though related) objects.

The hull upgrade: $H^\mathrm{hull}$ *is* a fixed point of $G_\Sigma$, so the closure
$U_{G^\Sigma}$ applied to $H^\mathrm{hull}$ stays at $H^\mathrm{hull}$. This is the sense in
which the hull self-generation identity is stronger than its ground-level counterpart:

- **Ground level**: $R = U_G(R)$ holds, but the atoms are not individually stable — the
  generating set is closed as a whole, not pointwise.
- **Hull level**: $R^\mathrm{hull} = U_{G^\Sigma}(R^\mathrm{hull})$ holds, and additionally
  $G_\Sigma(H^\mathrm{hull}) \cong H^\mathrm{hull}$ — the fiber atom is a *fixed point* of
  the comonad, not merely an atom in a closed generating set.

This fixed-point stability is a direct consequence of the automorphism property of $G_\Sigma$
(§hull-directed-comonad): because $\Sigma: \Omega \to \Omega$ is a homeomorphism
(reversible dynamics), the pullback $G_\Sigma = \Sigma^*$ preserves the local structure of
$\Omega$ exactly, and the patch lattice — which records that local structure — is invariant.
