### Definition — Triad {#triad}

A **triad** is a three-stage progression in which:

1. A **free** structure is constructed first — universal, unconstrained,
   generated by the raw data without imposed equations.

2. A **quotient** structure is built from the free one by imposing
   equations — constraints that identify previously distinct elements.

3. A **fixed** structure is the terminus — the sublocus where the
   equations imposed in stage two are satisfied trivially, so that the
   construction can go no further.

The triad is not merely a sequence. It is characterized by two universal
properties: the free structure maps *into* anything (it is initial among
structured things); the fixed structure receives a map *from* everything
(it is the image of the quotient under the relevant closure). The quotient
is the mediating stage — not initial, not terminal, but the right model
of the given data.

#### The Two Triadic Patterns {#triad-patterns}

Two distinct triadic patterns appear in $R$:

##### The Settling Triad: Free → Quotient → Fixed {#triad-settling-pattern}

The settling triad is the passage from syntax through semantics to stability:

| Stage | Name | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Free | Syntax | The universal structure with no extra equations |
| Quotient | Semantics | The structure with the specific equations imposed |
| Fixed | Stability | The sublocus where equations act trivially |

This triad appears at every level of the construction (§settling-triad).
At the language level it is $\text{FiberNuclearLanguage} \twoheadrightarrow H_t
\twoheadrightarrow H_t^*$. At the universe level it is
$R_\mathrm{syn} \to R \to \mathsf{Phase}$.

The driving duality within the settling triad is the **nucleus duality**
(§saturation-transfer-dyad): the fixed stage is precisely the convergence of the two
nuclei, the locus where both settle simultaneously.

##### The Adjoint Triad: Left — Middle — Right {#triad-adjoint-pattern}

The adjoint triad is a chain of three adjoints with the middle term
being a monad or comonad:

| Position | Role | Direction |
|----------|------|-----------|
| Left | Forward shape $\Pi_\to$ | Colimit along the prefix order |
| Middle | Dynamics $G_s = \Box \dashv \Diamond$ | One-step comonad |
| Right | Backward cosections $\nabla_\leftarrow$ | Limit against the prefix order |

This triad names the three ways to relate a structure on histories to
a structure on their extensions. The middle term is the elementary
dynamics — one step forward. The left term is the full forward
propagation to the colimit; the right term is the full backward
determination from the limit (§modal-triad).

#### Triads and Dualities {#triad-duality-relation}

Every triad contains an embedded duality. In the settling triad:

- The duality is between the **free** pole (initial, builds from below) and
  the **fixed** pole (terminal image, builds from above); the **quotient**
  is the convergence where both are satisfied.

In the adjoint triad:

- The duality is between the **left** pole ($\Pi_\to$, colimit/forward) and
  the **right** pole ($\nabla_\leftarrow$, limit/backward); the **middle**
  dynamics is what mediates between them.

The triad is thus a duality with a named middle term: the stage that
makes both poles accessible from the same position.

---

#### Triads and Tetrads {#triad-tetrad-relation}

Two settling triads interlock to form the nuclear quartet ([NuclearQuartetSubalgebra](nuclear-quartet-subalgebra.md)):

$$
\underbrace{(\text{syntax} \to \text{semantics})}_{\text{Pair 1: Algebra} \to \text{Language}}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\underbrace{(\text{syntax} \to \text{semantics})}_{\text{Pair 2: Geometry} \to \text{Dynamics}}
$$

The fixed stage of Pair 1 ($R_\mathrm{syn}$) is the free stage of Pair 2.
The nuclear quartet is two settling triads chained at their handshake object.
