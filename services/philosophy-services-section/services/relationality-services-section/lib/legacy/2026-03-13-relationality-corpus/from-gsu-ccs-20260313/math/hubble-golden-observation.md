### Conjecture — Hubble Tension Ratio and $\varphi^{1/6}$ {#hubble-golden-observation}

#### The Measured Values {#hubble-golden-data}

The **Hubble tension** is the discrepancy between two independent measurements of the
present-day expansion rate:

- $H_{\mathrm{early}} \approx 67.4$ km/s/Mpc — inferred from the CMB (Planck 2020),
  probing the universe at $z \approx 1100$;
- $H_{\mathrm{late}} \approx 73.04$ km/s/Mpc — measured locally from Cepheid-calibrated
  Type Ia supernovae (SH0ES 2022), probing the universe at $z \approx 0$.

The ratio of the two measurements:
$$
\frac{H_{\mathrm{late}}}{H_{\mathrm{early}}}
\;\approx\; \frac{73.04}{67.4} \;\approx\; 1.0837.
$$

#### The Golden Ratio Coincidence {#hubble-golden-coincidence}

The golden ratio $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.61803$. Its sixth root:
$$
\varphi^{1/6} \;\approx\; 1.08351.
$$

The agreement:
$$
\left|\frac{H_{\mathrm{late}}/H_{\mathrm{early}} - \varphi^{1/6}}{\varphi^{1/6}}\right|
\;\approx\; 0.016\%,
$$
which is within the combined measurement uncertainty ($\pm 0.7\%$ at $1\sigma$ for each
experiment). The coincidence holds to the precision of current measurements.

#### Status {#hubble-golden-status}

This is a numerical observation, not a derivation. The formula
$H_{\mathrm{late}}/H_{\mathrm{early}} = \varphi^{1/6}$ is derived from hyperverse
mathematics in §hubble-tension. The observation predates that derivation:
the ratio was computed from Planck 2020 and SH0ES 2022 data, and the result identified
as $\varphi^{1/6}$, which then motivated the derivation of §hubble-tension.

If future measurements revise $H_{\mathrm{early}}$ or $H_{\mathrm{late}}$ substantially,
this observation should be updated; the derivation in §hubble-tension
remains valid independently of whether the numerical coincidence persists.
