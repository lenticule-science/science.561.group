### Construction — The Free Sequential Act {#free-sequential-act}

For a fixed atom $s \in \mathrm{atoms}(T)$, the comonad $G_s$ on $R$ (§directed-comonad)
generates two canonical free structures: the **cofree coalgebra** on any sheaf,
and the **coKleisli category** of $s$-mediated transitions.

#### Cofree $G_s$-Coalgebra {#free-sequential-cofree}

**Claim.** The forgetful functor $U_s : G_s\text{-}\mathbf{Coalg} \to R$ (from the
category of $G_s$-coalgebras to $R$) has a right adjoint
$$
G_s^{\mathrm{cofree}} \;:\; R \longrightarrow G_s\text{-}\mathbf{Coalg}.
$$
The **cofree $G_s$-coalgebra** on $X \in R$ is the terminal $G_s$-coalgebra over
$X$: the object $G_s^{\mathrm{cofree}}(X)$ together with a universal coalgebra
map to every other $G_s$-coalgebra that "observes" $X$.

*Concretely:* The candidate is $(G_s(X), \delta_X)$, where $\delta_X : G_s(X) \to
G_s(G_s(X))$ is the comultiplication. The counit of the adjunction is
$\varepsilon_X : G_s(X) \to X$.

*Universal property:* For any $G_s$-coalgebra $(Y, \gamma)$ and any $R$-map
$f : Y \to X$, there exists a unique coalgebra map
$\bar{f} : (Y, \gamma) \to (G_s(X), \delta_X)$ given by $\bar{f} := G_s(f) \circ \gamma$.

**Status.** The universal property requires verification. The map $\bar{f} = G_s(f) \circ \gamma$
is a coalgebra map iff $\delta_X \circ \bar{f} = G_s(\bar{f}) \circ \gamma$. This
uses the comonad laws and the naturality of $\delta$; the full proof is not given here.

#### Interpretation of the Cofree Coalgebra {#free-sequential-interpretation}

The cofree coalgebra $G_s^{\mathrm{cofree}}(X) = G_s(X)$ over $X$ is the sheaf
that, at each history $t$, holds the value of $X$ at $s \star t$ — one step ahead.
The universal property says: **every process carrier that produces observations in
$X$ factors uniquely through this one-step-ahead shift**. The cofree coalgebra is
the canonical "stream" or "trajectory" sheaf for the step $s$.

A $G_s$-coalgebra $(Y, \gamma)$ is a sheaf that knows how to extend itself forward.
The cofree one $G_s(X)$ is the canonical such thing: it simply IS the one-step
shift of $X$, with $\gamma = \delta_X$ recording how to take a second step.

#### The CoKleisli Category as Free Act Category {#free-sequential-cokleisli}

The **coKleisli category** $\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$ (§sequential-act) is the free
category of $s$-mediated transitions:

- Objects: all sheaves $F \in R$ (same as $R$);
- Morphisms $F \to G$: sheaf maps $G_s(F) \to G$ — reading $F$ one step ahead
  and producing a value in $G$ now;
- Composition: $g \circ_{\mathrm{coKl}} f = g \circ G_s(f) \circ \delta_F$.

The identity morphism of $F$ in $\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$ is the counit $\varepsilon_F : G_s(F) \to F$.

**Universality of coKl.** Any category of $G_s$-mediated processes — any category
$\mathcal{C}$ with a functor $\mathcal{C} \to R$ and a "step" operation — factors
through $\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$. In this sense, $\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$ is the **free
category generated by one $s$-step**.

#### Free Sequential Act: Summary {#free-sequential-summary}

| | Structure | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cofree coalgebra | $(G_s(X), \delta_X)$ | Free process carrier over $X$ |
| CoKleisli category | $\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$ | Free category of $s$-mediated transitions |
| Concrete $G_s$ | $G_s(F)(t) = F(s \star t)$ | Canonical sequential act on $R$ |

The abstract sequential act (§sequential-act) specifies what a family of
comonads must satisfy; the concrete $G_s$ is the canonical instance; and the
cofree coalgebra and coKleisli category are the free objects generated by $G_s$.

**Status.** Stub. The cofree coalgebra universal property needs a full proof.
The relationship between different atoms' coKleisli categories — how
$\mathrm{coKl}(G_s)$ and $\mathrm{coKl}(G_{s'})$ interact when $s \perp s'$ —
is not yet worked out. This likely produces a double-coKleisli structure or
a tensor product of coKleisli categories.
