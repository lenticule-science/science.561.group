### Lemma — Idempotence of $U_G$ {#closure-operator-idempotence}

A subclass $X \subseteq \mathrm{Obj}(R)$ is $U_G$-**closed** if $U_G(X) \subseteq X$. For any
$U_G$-closed subclass, $U_G(X) = X$. Together with the Monotonicity Lemma, $U_G$ is a closure
operator on $P(\mathrm{Obj}(R))$: inflationary, monotone, idempotent.

**Proof.** Inflationarity gives $X \subseteq U_G(X)$; closedness gives $U_G(X) \subseteq X$. $\square$
