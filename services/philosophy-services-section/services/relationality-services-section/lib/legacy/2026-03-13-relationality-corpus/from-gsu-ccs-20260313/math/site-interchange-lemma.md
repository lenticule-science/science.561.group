### Lemma — The Site Interchange Lemma {#site-interchange-lemma}

**Lemma.** Let $((\mathcal{C}_n, J_n), f_n: (\mathcal{C}_n, J_n) \to (\mathcal{C}_{n+1},
J_{n+1}))_{n \geq 0}$ be a filtered $\omega$-diagram of sites with continuous
transition functors. Let $\mathcal{C}_\infty = \varinjlim_n \mathcal{C}_n$ and
$J_\infty$ the finest topology on $\mathcal{C}_\infty$ making each canonical functor
$\iota_n: \mathcal{C}_n \to \mathcal{C}_\infty$ continuous. Then:
$$
\mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{C}_\infty, J_\infty) \;\cong\; \varinjlim_n \mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{C}_n, J_n).
$$

*Proof.* We construct mutually inverse functors $\Phi$ and $\Psi$.

**Functor $\Phi$ (colimit site → colimit of categories).** Given $F \in
\mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{C}_\infty, J_\infty)$, define $F_n := \iota_n^* F$ for each $n$.
Since $\iota_n$ is continuous, $F_n \in \mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{C}_n, J_n)$.
The transition: $\iota_{n+1} \circ f_n = \iota_n$ gives the isomorphism
$\phi_n: F_n(C) = F(\iota_n C) = F(\iota_{n+1} f_n C) = (f_n^* F_{n+1})(C)$.
The system $(F_n, \phi_n)$ is a compatible system in $\varinjlim_n \mathbf{Sh}(\mathcal{C}_n, J_n)$.

**Functor $\Psi$ (colimit of categories → colimit site).** Given a compatible system
$(F_n, \phi_n: F_n \xrightarrow{\sim} f_n^* F_{n+1})$, define $F_\infty(C) := F_n(C_n)$
for $C = \iota_n(C_n)$. Compatibility ensures independence of representative.
For morphisms: $F_\infty(h) := F_k(h_k)$ for $h$ eventually $h_k$ in $\mathcal{C}_k$.
Sheaf condition: any $J_\infty$-cover of $C = \iota_n(C_n)$ is generated by
$\iota_n$-images of $J_n$-covers (since $J_\infty = \bigvee_n (\iota_n)_! J_n$);
the sheaf condition for $F_\infty$ reduces to the sheaf condition for $F_n$, which holds.

**Inverses.** $(\Psi \circ \Phi)(F)$: for $C = \iota_n(C_n)$, the value is
$(\iota_n^* F)(C_n) = F(\iota_n C_n) = F(C)$; on morphisms likewise. So
$\Psi \circ \Phi = \mathrm{id}$ on the nose.
$(\Phi \circ \Psi)(F_n, \phi_n)$: $\iota_n^* F_\infty(C_n) = F_\infty(\iota_n C_n) = F_n(C_n)$;
the compatibility isomorphisms agree. So $\Phi \circ \Psi = \mathrm{id}$. $\square$

**Remark on large sites.** For the application in §hyperverse-fixed-point, the sites
$\mathbf{RU}^{(n)}$ are large. The lemma applies under the universe stratification of
§closure-set-theory: level-$n$ sites are $\mathcal{U}^{(n)}$-small; the colimit is
taken in $\mathcal{U}^\omega = \bigcup_n \mathcal{U}^{(n)}$.
