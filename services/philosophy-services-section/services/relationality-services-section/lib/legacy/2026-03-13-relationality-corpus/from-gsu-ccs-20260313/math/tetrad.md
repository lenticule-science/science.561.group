### Definition — Tetrads of the Relational Universe {#tetrad}

A **tetrad** is a four-element structure. The following is an annotated catalog
of the tetrads of $R$.

---

#### The Nuclear Quartet Tetrad {#tetrads-nuclear-quartet}

*See [NuclearQuartetSubalgebra](nuclear-quartet-subalgebra.md) for the full treatment.*

| Position | Name | Disclosure |
|----------|------|-----------|
| First | Object | What something IS — constitutive data |
| Second | Quality | What CAN BE SAID of it — properties and invariants |
| Third | Relation | How it CONNECTS to others — morphisms and maps |
| Fourth | Act | What it DOES — operations, closures, generation |

The nuclear quartet tetrad is the organizing principle of $R$. The
four sections (algebra, language, geometry, dynamics) correspond to these four
positions at the universe level. The four term-facets (§term) apply
the same four positions to individual mathematical objects.

---

#### The Closure Tetrad {#tetrads-closure}

*See §nuclear-quartet-closure for the full treatment.*

| Position | Closure | Fixed points |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Algebra | Trace closure ($\mathbb{M}(\Sigma,I)$) | Histories |
| Language | Modal closure ($\sigma_t, \Delta_t$) | $H_t^*$ |
| Geometry | Sheaf closure (sheafification) | Objects of $R$ |
| Dynamics | Universe closure ($U_G$) | $R$ itself |

Each closure operates at a strictly higher level than the previous. The
closure tetrad names the four levels of stabilization and makes precise the
claim that each is irreducible to the others: no lower-level closure can
simulate a higher-level one.

---

#### The Adjoint Tetrad {#tetrads-adjoint}

*See §modal-adjoint-chain for the full treatment.*

| Position | Adjoint | Role |
|----------|---------|------|
| First | $\Pi_\to$ | Directed shape — forward colimit |
| Second | $\Box$ | Flat (constant sheaf, left adjoint) |
| Third | $\Diamond$ | Sharp (global sections, right adjoint) |
| Fourth | $\nabla_\leftarrow$ | Codiscrete (cosections) |

The four-adjoint chain $\Pi_\to \dashv \Box \dashv \Diamond \dashv \nabla_\leftarrow$
is the modal tetrad of the relational universe. It exists at the internal level
(under the finite-step-set hypothesis) and specializes the modal triad
(§modal-triad) by adding the fourth adjoint $\nabla_\leftarrow$.

---

#### The Level Tetrad {#tetrads-levels}

| Position | Level | What it discloses |
|----------|-------|--------------------|
| First | Constructive | The ground-level construction of $R$ |
| Second | Internal | The internal logic and type theory of $R$ |
| Third | Moduli | The space of families of fiber algebras — the Fischer-Rao geometry |
| Fourth | Spiral | The hyperversal level — $\mathcal{H}$ as $R$ acting on itself |

This tetrad is a nuclear quartet of the **program**: the four levels of mathematical
engagement with the same object $\mathcal{H}$. The constructive level builds
the first instance; the spiral level is where $\mathcal{H}$ becomes its own
ground.

---

#### Open: Second-Turn Tetrads {#tetrads-second-turn}
