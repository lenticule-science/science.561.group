### Definition — Step Cohomology {#step-cohomology}

Step cohomology — measuring new stable structure at each tower step — exists at two
levels:

| Level | File | Converges to |
|---|---|---|
| Depth filtration | §depth-step-cohomology | $H^*(R, E_0)$ |
| Hyperverse tower | §hyperverse-step-cohomology | $H^*(\mathcal{H}, E_0^\mathcal{H})$ |

Both fit in long exact sequences from their respective new-modes sheaves (§depth-new-modes,
§hyperverse-new-modes). Both organize into step spectral sequences (§depth-step-spectral-sequence,
§hyperverse-step-spectral-sequence).
