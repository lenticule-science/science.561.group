### Definition — Free, Quotient, and Fixed at the Quality Level {#quality-free-quotient}

The quality phase — language — has all four faces of the free/quotient pattern in
place. This topic names them explicitly and identifies where in the paper each lives.

#### The Abstract Side: Nuclear Heyting-Modal Algebras {#quality-abstract}

The intrinsic characterization of a quality-bearing fiber is an object of
$\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ (§nuclear-heyting-algebra): a finite Heyting algebra $(A, \wedge, \vee,
\Rightarrow, \bot, \top)$ equipped with two commuting nuclei $\sigma, \Delta : A \to A$
(extensive, idempotent, meet-preserving; $\sigma \Delta = \Delta \sigma$).

This is what a fiber **is**, independently of any syntactic presentation. No terms,
no variables, no rewriting rules — just the algebraic structure.

#### The Free Object: FiberNuclearLanguage {#quality-free}

FiberNuclearLanguage ([fiber-nuclear-language](fiber-nuclear-language.md)) is the **initial object** in the category of nuclear
Heyting-modal propositional calculi: the free such structure generated by a set of
propositional variables, subject to no equations beyond the axioms of $\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$.

Every nuclear HA $(A, \sigma, \Delta)$ and every assignment of variables to elements
of $A$ extends uniquely to a nuclear HA homomorphism from FiberNuclearLanguage. This
is the content of soundness (§soundness): the interpretation map
$\llbracket - \rrbracket_t : \text{FiberNuclearLanguage} \to H_t$ is the unique such homomorphism
determined by the variable assignment.

#### Quotients: The Fiber Algebras {#quality-quotients}

Each fiber $H_t$ is a **quotient** of FiberNuclearLanguage: the kernel of
$\llbracket - \rrbracket_t$ is the congruence of all equations that hold semantically
at history $t$. Completeness (§completeness) says this quotient is not lossy — every
equation that holds in $H_t$ is derivable in FiberNuclearLanguage relative to the
semantic equations at $t$.

Different histories $t, t'$ give different quotients $H_t, H_{t'}$ — different
amounts of "additional information" about what holds. The restriction maps
$H(f) : H_{t'} \to H_t$ for $t \leq t'$ are the quotient maps between quotients:
passing from a finer to a coarser semantic context.

#### Fixed-Point Layer: $H_t^*$ {#quality-fixed}

The fixed-point layer $H_t^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_t) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_t)$
(§fixed-fiber) is the **most quotiented** fiber: the quotient of $H_t$ by all
equations of the form $\sigma_t(a) = a$ and $\Delta_t(a) = a$. It consists of
propositions that are already at their saturated and transfer-stable form —
on which neither nucleus has further work to do.

In terms of the free/quotient tower:
$$
\text{FiberNuclearLanguage}
\;\twoheadrightarrow\;
H_t
\;\twoheadrightarrow\;
H_t^*
$$
Each arrow is a quotient (surjective HA homomorphism). FiberNuclearLanguage is the
syntactically free object; $H_t$ is the semantically determined quotient at $t$;
$H_t^*$ is the doubly stable quotient where the nuclei act trivially.

#### The Correspondence Theorem {#quality-correspondence}

| Face | Object | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract (intrinsic) | $\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$: nuclear Heyting algebras | §nuclear-heyting-algebra |
| Free | FiberNuclearLanguage: the term calculus | [fiber-nuclear-language](fiber-nuclear-language.md) |
| Quotient | $H_t$: each fiber, semantically determined | §fiber-doctrine |
| Fixed | $H_t^*$: doubly stable sub-HA | §fixed-fiber |
| Concrete→Abstract | $H_t$ is an $\mathbf{HA_{nucl}}$ object | §fiber-doctrine |
| Free→Quotient | $\llbracket-\rrbracket_t : \text{FiberNuclearLanguage} \twoheadrightarrow H_t$ | §soundness |
| Quotient→Fixed | $H_t \twoheadrightarrow H_t^*$ (modding out $\sigma = \Delta = \mathrm{id}$) | §fixed-fiber |
| Completeness | Equations in $H_t$ determine quotient fully | §completeness |

#### Remark — Normal Forms as the Free Presentation {#quality-normalforms}

The reduction system of §normalization is the **canonical presentation** of
FiberNuclearLanguage as a rewriting system. A normal form is a canonical representative
of an equivalence class of terms — exactly as $\mathrm{NF}(w)$ is a canonical
representative of a commutation-equivalence class in $\mathbb{M}(\Sigma, I)$. The
normalization theorem (§normalization) says every term has a unique normal form:
FiberNuclearLanguage has **decidable word problem**, and the rewriting system is
confluent and terminating.
