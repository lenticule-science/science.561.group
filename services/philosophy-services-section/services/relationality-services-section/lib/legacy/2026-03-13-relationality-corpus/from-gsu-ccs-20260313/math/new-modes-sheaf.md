### Definition — New-Modes Sheaf {#new-modes-sheaf}

The new-modes sheaf construction — measuring stable structure first appearing at a tower
step — exists at two levels of the theory:

| Level | File | Step | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depth filtration (within $R$) | §depth-new-modes | depth $k \to k+1$ | $(T,J)$ |
| Hyperverse tower (across universes) | §hyperverse-new-modes | $R^{(k)} \hookrightarrow R^{(k+1)}$ | $R^{(k+1)}$ |

At the depth-filtration level, both sheaves live on the same site $(T,J)$ and the
construction is a quotient sheaf with fiberwise Fibonacci growth (§nuclear-fibonacci-recurrence).
At the hyperverse tower level, the sheaves live on different toposes and comparison
requires the geometric morphism $\iota_k$.
