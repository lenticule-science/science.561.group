### Conjecture — Interference as Cohomological Cancellation {#interference-as-cohomological-cancellation}

Constructive and destructive interference correspond to sections that assemble or fail
to assemble in sheaf cohomology. Destructive interference is a class in $H^1$ that
vanishes — a section whose local pieces cancelled in the gluing. The double-slit
experiment is a sheaf that fails to amalgamate when both slits are open but amalgamates
with one closed.

**What a proof requires.** An identification of the interference pattern with a Čech
1-cocycle, and a proof that path-integral cancellation corresponds to the coboundary
condition.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
