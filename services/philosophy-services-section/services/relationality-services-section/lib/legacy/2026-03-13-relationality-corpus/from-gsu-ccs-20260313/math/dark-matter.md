### Conjecture — Spectral Mass and Dark Matter {#dark-matter}

*This file maps internal mathematical structures to a physical phenomenon.
All mathematical claims are in §spectral-remanence and §spectral-schwarzschild.*

#### The Mathematical Structures {#corr-dm-math}

- **Spectral mass** $M_t(\delta) = \mathrm{Tr}(\delta_t)$: the total weight of
  unsettled modes at history $t$ (§spectral-schwarzschild).
- **Opacity of $M_t(\delta)$ to $E_{t,0}$**: the spectral mass is invisible to
  measurements that probe only the stable layer (§spectral-remanence).
- **Schwarzschild radius** $r_s(\delta_t) = 2M_t(\delta)$: the critical spectral
  scale below which mode escape is impossible.
- **Cohomological tunneling**: the mechanism by which trapped modes eventually
  settle, dissipating $M_t(\delta)$ over the depth tower (§cohomological-tunneling).

#### The Conjectural Correspondence {#corr-dm-correspondence}

**Correspondence.** In a physical system modeled as a relational universe:

- $M_t(\delta)$ corresponds to **dark matter density** in a galactic region.
- The opacity of $M_t(\delta)$ to the stable layer corresponds to the
  electromagnetic invisibility of dark matter: it gravitates but does not couple
  to photons.
- The Schwarzschild radius $r_s(\delta_t) = 2M_t(\delta)$ corresponds to the
  gravitational Schwarzschild radius $r_s = 2GM/c^2$.
- The bridge conjecture:
  $$
  M_{\mathrm{grav}} \;=\; M_{\mathrm{baryonic}} + \kappa \cdot M_t(\delta),
  $$
  where $\kappa$ is the relational-gravitational coupling and $\kappa \cdot M_t(\delta)$
  is the **spectral dark mass**.

#### Empirical Prediction {#corr-dm-empirical}

If $M_t(\delta)$ correlates with causal event density (number of gravitationally
bound interaction events per unit volume over a region's history), then galactic
regions with the same baryonic mass but richer interaction histories (denser merger
histories, cluster cores vs. isolated ellipticals) should exhibit systematically
higher gravitational lensing mass.

**Falsification.** Lensing maps of galaxy clusters showing no correlation between
lensing-mass excess and interaction history, across a statistically robust sample,
falsify this correspondence. The mathematics of §spectral-remanence is unaffected.

**Relationship to Hubble tension.** As dark matter evaporates via cohomological
tunneling, spectral energy accumulates as vacuum energy. This dark matter–dark energy
conversion is the mechanism underlying §hubble-tension.
