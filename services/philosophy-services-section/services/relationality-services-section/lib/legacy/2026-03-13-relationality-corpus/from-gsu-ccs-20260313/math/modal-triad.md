### Theorem — The Modal Triad {#modal-triad}

The **modal triad** is the three-position adjoint chain that organizes the
relationship between a sheaf and its behavior under history extensions. The three
positions are a left adjoint (forward shape), a middle comonad (elementary
dynamics), and a right adjoint (backward cosections).

#### The Poles and Middle {#triad-modal-positions}

| Position | Name | Notation | Role |
|----------|------|----------|------|
| Left | **Directed shape** | $\Pi_\to$ | Forward colimit along the prefix order |
| Middle | **Dynamics comonad** | $G_s = \Box \dashv \Diamond$ | One-step shift comonad |
| Right | **Cosections** | $\nabla_\leftarrow$ | Backward limit (global sections against the order) |

**Directed shape** $\Pi_\to$ takes a sheaf and returns its forward colimit — the
"final value" that the sheaf settles toward as history grows without bound. It
discards all transient information, retaining only what survives the entire history.

**Dynamics** $G_s$ is the one-step shift (§directed-comonad): $G_s(F)(t) = F(s \star t)$.
It shifts the sheaf forward by one atom $s$ — it is the elementary move. As a
comonad $\Box \dashv \Diamond$, it describes what the sheaf can "see" after one
step and what it "knows" from one step back. This is the middle term because the
entire dynamics of the relational universe is generated by iterating this single step.

**Cosections** $\nabla_\leftarrow$ returns the backward limit of a sheaf — the "initial
determination" that the sheaf was committed to from the very beginning of the history
history. It retains only what was forced from the start, discarding everything that
the dynamics added.

#### The Triad Is Not Symmetric {#triad-modal-asymmetry}

The three positions are not symmetric:

- $\Pi_\to$ and $\nabla_\leftarrow$ are the colimit and limit ends of the adjoint chain —
  they are determined by the global structure of $T$.
- $G_s$ is the local, step-by-step dynamics — it depends only on one atom $s$.

The middle position is *generative* in a way the outer positions are not: iterating
$G_s$ produces the full dynamics of the universe. The outer positions are *selective*:
they pick out what survives the full iteration (left) or what was already settled at
the start (right).

#### The Four-Adjoint Extension {#triad-modal-four-adjoints}

Under the hypothesis that $T$ has an initial object and the history monoid has finite
step set, the modal triad extends to a **four-adjoint chain** (§modal-adjoint-chain):

$$
\Pi_\to \;\dashv\; \Box \;\dashv\; \Diamond \;\dashv\; \nabla_\leftarrow
$$

The four-adjoint theorem names this chain precisely and identifies the middle pair
$\Box \dashv \Diamond$ with the directed comonad $G_s$. The triad is the
constructive-level picture; the four-adjoint chain is the internal picture.

#### The Modal Triad and the Settling Triad {#triad-modal-settling-relation}

The modal and settling triads are related but distinct:

- The **settling triad** (§settling-triad) is about *stages of construction* — it
  describes how the universe is built from free syntax through semantic quotient to
  stable sublocus.

- The **modal triad** is about *modes of access* to the universe once built — it
  describes how a sheaf can be projected forward (shape), shifted step by step
  (dynamics), or projected backward (cosections).

The settling triad is constructive; the modal triad is operational.
