### Construction — The Free Concurrent Act {#free-concurrent-act}

The free concurrent act over a history category $T$ is the **Day convolution**
on the presheaf category $[T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$, followed by
sheafification.

#### Step 1 — Day Convolution on Presheaves {#free-concurrent-presheaf}

The presheaf category $[T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$ carries the **Day
convolution** monoidal product:
$$
(F \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}} G)(t)
\;:=\;
\int^{u,v \in T} F(u) \times G(v) \times T(t,\, u \star v).
$$
This is the left Kan extension of the monoidal product $\star : T \times T \to T$
along the Yoneda embedding $y : T \to [T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$. The unit
is $y(e)$, the representable at the empty history.

The resulting structure $([T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}],\, \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}},\,
y(e))$ is a **symmetric monoidal biclosed category**: symmetric because $T$ is a
commutative monoid (up to the commutation relations); biclosed because the category
is a Bénabou cosmos.

#### Step 2 — Universal Property {#free-concurrent-universal}

**Theorem.** $([T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}], \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}}, y(e))$ is
the **free symmetric monoidal cocomplete category** generated by $T$ as a monoidal
category. That is, for any symmetric monoidal cocomplete category $\mathcal{C}$ and
any (strong) monoidal functor $F : (T, \star, e) \to (\mathcal{C}, \otimes, I)$,
there exists a unique (up to isomorphism) colimit-preserving symmetric monoidal
functor
$$
\widetilde{F} \;:\; ([T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}],\, \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}})
\;\longrightarrow\; (\mathcal{C},\, \otimes)
$$
extending $F$ along the Yoneda embedding. *(Status: standard result; see
Kelly, "On the operads of J.P. May", 1972, or Day's original construction.)*

This universality makes $[T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$ with Day convolution the
**abstract free concurrent act**: no locality condition, no sheaf condition — just
the monoidal structure of $T$ extended to all presheaves as freely as possible.

#### Step 3 — Sheafification as Quotient {#free-concurrent-sheafification}

Sheafification $a : [T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}] \to \mathbf{Sh}(T, J)$ is a
left exact left adjoint. Under the monoidal compatibility hypothesis (that $J$-covers
are preserved by $\star$-translation), $a$ is a **symmetric monoidal functor**, so
it carries Day convolution to the sheafified convolution $\circledast$ on $R$.

Define:
$$
F \circledast G \;:=\; a\bigl(iF \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}} iG\bigr),
$$
where $i : R \hookrightarrow [T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}]$ is the inclusion.

**Interpretation.** The sheafification is the **quotient** step:
- The free concurrent act $([T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}], \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}})$
  imposes no locality condition.
- The sheafified concurrent act $(R, \circledast)$ imposes the Grothendieck
  topology $J$ — the locality condition that makes local data cohere globally.
- The quotient map is $a$: it identifies presheaves that agree on all $J$-covers.

The canonical concurrent act on $R$ is therefore the **locality quotient** of the
free concurrent act on presheaves.

#### Summary: Free → Quotient for Concurrent Acts {#free-concurrent-summary}

| | Structure | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $([T^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set}], \otimes_{\mathrm{Day}})$ | Monoidal product of $T$, no locality |
| Quotient | $(R = \mathbf{Sh}(T,J), \circledast)$ | Locality imposed by $J$ |
| Map | Sheafification $a$ | Identifies presheaves agreeing on covers |

The concurrent act on $R$ is the unique symmetric monoidal structure on the
sheaf topos that (a) restricts to $\star$ on representables and (b) is compatible
with the commutation topology $J$.
