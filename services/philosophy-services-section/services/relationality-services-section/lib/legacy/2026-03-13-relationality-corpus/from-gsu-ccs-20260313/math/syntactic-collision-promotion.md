### Conjecture — Syntactic Collision Promotion {#syntactic-collision-promotion}

#### Setup {#scp-setup}

At each tower level $n$, there is a canonical map
$$
H_t^{\mathrm{syn},(n)} \;\longrightarrow\; H_t^{(n)}
$$
from the level-$n$ nuclear language normal forms to the level-$n$ fiber algebra. The kernel of
this map — the equivalence classes of distinct normal forms with equal denotation —
consists of the **syntactic collisions** at level $n$.

Syntactic collisions are dual to the completeness gaps (§completeness-tower-stratification):
a completeness gap is a semantic element with no syntactic preimage (surjectivity
fails); a syntactic collision is a pair of syntactic forms with the same semantic
image (injectivity fails). Both measure the mismatch between syntax and semantics,
from opposite sides.

#### The Conjecture {#scp-conjecture}

**Conjecture (Syntactic Collision Promotion).** The kernel of the level-$n$ map
determines the new generators at level $n+1$: each syntactic collision at level $n$
becomes a generator relation at level $n+1$ — the collision is resolved by adding
a new element that witnesses the equivalence.

More precisely: let $K_n$ denote the set of syntactic collisions at level $n$ (the
kernel pairs of the map above). Then:

1. Each $[f, g] \in K_n$ (a pair of the level-$n$ nuclear language normal forms with the same
   denotation) contributes a new generator $e_{[f,g]}$ to the level-$(n+1)$ nuclear language.

2. The new generator satisfies $e_{[f,g]} \leq f = g$ (it witnesses the collision
   as a canonical element).

3. Every new element of $H_t^{(n+1)}$ that is inexpressible at level $n$ arises
   from a collision in $K_n$ — there are no other sources of new generators.

#### Why Collisions Become Generators {#scp-mechanism}

At level $n$, two distinct normal forms $f$ and $g$ denote the same semantic
element. The level-$n$ language cannot distinguish them syntactically — they look
different but mean the same thing. At level $n+1$, the universe $R^{(n+1)}$
recognizes this equivalence and adds an element that names the shared denotation
directly.

This is a quotient-then-extend pattern: the level-$n$ collision defines an
equivalence on syntactic forms; the level-$(n+1)$ extension adds a canonical
representative for each equivalence class. The new representatives are the
generators that the level-$(n+1)$ nuclear language adds to the level-$n$ nuclear language.

#### The Fibonacci Count {#scp-fibonacci}

The R7 step direction (§r7-step-direction) orients the canonical nesting of nuclei:
$\mathsf{Transfer}(\mathsf{Saturate}(M))$ is the canonical form, encoding one step of the
backward-then-forward pattern. The depth of an the level-$n$ nuclear language term measures how
many times this pattern has been applied.

The number of depth-$n$ forms available for collision grows with the level-$n$
active mode count $d(n)$. By §nuclear-fibonacci-recurrence, $d(n+1) = d(n) + d(n-1)$
— the mode count follows the Fibonacci recurrence. If the number of new generators
at level $n+1$ equals $d(n)$ (the number of level-$n$ active modes, each of which
participates in some collision), then:
$$
|\mathrm{new generators at } n+1| = d(n),
$$
and the total generator count at each level follows the Fibonacci recurrence
from the R7 depth structure.

#### Connection to Completeness Tower Stratification {#scp-completeness}

§completeness-tower-stratification conjectures that the elements inexpressible at
level $n$ are exactly those first expressible at level $n+1$. Syntactic collision
promotion is the constructive mechanism behind this: the new elements at level $n+1$
are built from the collisions at level $n$. The two conjectures are complementary:

- **Completeness tower stratification**: the new elements at $n+1$ are exactly the
  old gaps.
- **Syntactic collision promotion**: the new generators at $n+1$ are exactly the old
  collisions.

Together, they say the tower is generated by alternating between resolving surjectivity
gaps and resolving injectivity gaps — the tower climbs by alternately adding what is
missing and identifying what is redundant.

#### What Remains to Prove {#scp-open}

- **Kernel structure**: a formal description of the syntactic collision kernel $K_n$
  in terms of the R7-normalized forms.
- **Generator correspondence**: that each $[f, g] \in K_n$ yields a well-defined
  generator of the level-$(n+1)$ nuclear language, with no two collisions yielding the same generator.
- **Completeness of promotion**: that every new element at level $n+1$ arises from
  some collision at level $n$ — that $K_n$ generates all of the new content.
- **Fibonacci count**: the count of new generators equals $d(n)$, connecting the
  R7 depth structure to the Fibonacci recurrence explicitly.
