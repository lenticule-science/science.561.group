### Conjecture — Uncertainty as R7 Asymmetry {#uncertainty-as-r7-asymmetry}

Saturate ($\sigma_t$) and Transfer ($\Delta_t$) commute as functions (see
[nucleus-commutation](nucleus-commutation.md)), but their measurement procedures do not
— reduction rule R7 is oriented
(see [reduction-system](reduction-system.md)). There exists a pair of observables in
this framework corresponding to "which operator ran last," and those observables are
fundamentally uncertain together: knowing the Saturate-depth or the Transfer-depth but
not both, because knowing both would require a formula simultaneously normalized in two
incompatible orders.

**What a proof requires.** A precise notion of "measuring the operator type" as a
function on normal forms, and a non-commutativity result for the two measurement
operators.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
