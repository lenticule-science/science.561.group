## Full citation

D. Ayala, J. Francis, N. Rozenblyum. *A stratified homotopy hypothesis.* arXiv:1502.01713 (2015).

## Status

Standard reference — the $(\infty, 1)$-categorical treatment of stratified spaces and exit-path categories.

## Annotation

Establishes the equivalence between the $\infty$-category of conically smooth stratified spaces and a full sub-$\infty$-category of $\mathrm{Cat}_\infty$ via the [exit ∞-category](../teaching/explanations/exit-infinity-category.md) construction $X \mapsto \mathrm{Exit}(X)$.

**Most-cited content in this subtheory:**

- The exit-path $\infty$-category construction — the basis of [`exit-infinity-category-object`](../objects/exit-infinity-category-object.md) and the [`stratified-directed-infinity-site`](../objects/stratified-directed-infinity-site.md).
- Constructible sheaves on stratified spaces — the basis of [`constructibility`](../teaching/explanations/constructibility.md) and [`sd-constructible-stack`](../objects/sd-constructible-stack.md).
- The ambidexterity-style results for constructible sheaves — the lineage of [`sd-ambidexterity`](../theorems/sd-ambidexterity.md).

This subtheory's stratified-directed material is essentially their framework specialized to the relational ∞-topos setting. Their proofs transport with the coefficient ∞-category replaced.
