## Full citation

J. Lurie. *Higher Topos Theory.* Princeton University Press, Annals of Mathematics Studies vol. 170, 2009.

## Status

Standard reference for $(\infty, 1)$-category theory and higher topos theory.

## Annotation

The foundational treatment of $(\infty, 1)$-categories via the quasi-category model. Used in this subtheory for:

- The basic theory of $(\infty, 1)$-topoi over $\infty\text{-Grpd}$ (Chapter 6).
- Filtered colimits and presentable $\infty$-categories (Chapter 5) — the technical infrastructure for the [recursive realization theorem](../theorems/recursive-realization-theorem.md)'s convergence argument.
- The general theory of accessible reflective sub-$\infty$-categories — used implicitly in the [RT self-stabilization theorem](../theorems/rt-self-stabilization.md).
