## Full citation

F. W. Lawvere. *Categories of spaces may not be generalized spaces, as exemplified by directed graphs.* Revista Colombiana de Matemáticas 20 (1986), 179–185.

## Status

Standard reference — the original paper introducing axiomatic cohesion.

## Annotation

The 1986 paper that initiates Lawvere's program of *axiomatic cohesion*: characterizing the topos of "spaces" (in some intended geometric sense) by an adjoint structure over the topos of "discrete spaces" rather than by a choice of underlying site. The paper uses directed graphs as the worked example.

The four-functor structure later canonized as the [cohesive quadruple](../morphisms/cohesive-quadruple.md) is foreshadowed here in 1-categorical form. Lawvere's 1989 *Categories in Continuum Physics* paper makes the axioms explicit, and [Schreiber 2013](schreiber-2013.md) generalizes the entire program to the $(\infty, 1)$-topos setting.

This subtheory imports the axiomatic-cohesion framework wholesale; this reference is the foundational citation.
