## Full citation

A. Kock. *Monads for which structures are adjoint to units.* J. Pure Appl. Algebra 104 (1995), 41–59.

## Status

Standard reference for the theory of KZ (Kock–Zöberlein) monads.

## Annotation

The paper formalizing the **KZ-laxity** axiom: a 2-monad $T$ is *Kock–Zöberlein* (lax-idempotent) when its multiplication $\mu : TT \Rightarrow T$ is right adjoint to $T\eta$ with identity counit. The dual notion (KZ-comonad) is the relevant one for this subtheory's [KZ comonad $G = \Box_r \Diamond_l$](../morphisms/kz-comonad-G.md).

**Most-cited content here:**

- The KZ-laxity axiom itself — the central technical tool of this subtheory.
- The reflectivity / coreflectivity of $T$-(co)algebras as a consequence — used in the [RT self-stabilization theorem](../theorems/rt-self-stabilization.md).

Independently rediscovered by Zöberlein in dissertation form 1976; jointly cited as Kock–Zöberlein. Predication categories' [closure 2-monad](../../../../theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) appeals to the same axiom in its 2-categorical formulation; see [Kelly–Lack 1997](../../../../theories/predication-categories/references/kelly-lack1997.md) for the related K1 condition.
