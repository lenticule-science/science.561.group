## Full citation

V. Zöberlein. *Doctrines on 2-categories.* Math. Z. 148 (1976), 267–279.

## Status

Historical reference — the original (with [Kock 1995](kock-1995.md)) of the KZ-laxity axiom.

## Annotation

Independently derives the lax-idempotent 2-monad notion that is now jointly cited as Kock–Zöberlein. Cited here for completeness of attribution; the substantive use of KZ-laxity in this subtheory is via [Kock 1995](kock-1995.md)'s formulation.
