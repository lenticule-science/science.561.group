## Full citation

U. Schreiber. *Differential cohomology in a cohesive infinity-topos.* arXiv:1310.7930 (2013).

## Status

Standard reference — the canonical $(\infty, 1)$-topos-theoretic treatment of cohesion. Cited at training-knowledge level.

## Annotation

Schreiber's monograph generalizing [Lawvere 1986](lawvere1986.md)'s axiomatic cohesion to the $(\infty, 1)$-topos setting. The four-functor [cohesive quadruple](../morphisms/cohesive-quadruple.md) — Shape ⊣ Flat ⊣ Sharp ⊣ Crisp — is given its $\infty$-categorical formulation; the modal reading $(\Box, \Diamond)$ is developed; the synthetic-differential-cohesion model on Cartesian spaces $\mathrm{PSh}(\mathrm{CartSp})$ is constructed in detail.

**Most-cited sections in this subtheory:**

- §3.7 — KZ-laxity of the middle pair $\Box_r \Diamond_l$ in any cohesive $\infty$-topos (used in the [RITM existence theorem](../theorems/ritm-existence.md)).
- §1–3 — the basic axiomatization of cohesive ∞-topoi (used everywhere).
- The CartSp model — used in the [presheaf-vs-CartSp question](../questions/presheaf-vs-cartsp-base.md).

The relational program imports this framework; this subtheory does not re-prove cohesive-∞-topos foundations, only the relational-side specializations and refinements.
