- [[define]]:: A [[triage/thing]] is any [[structure]]—specifically, any [[relative]]—that is held open as coherent within the [[domain of relating]]. A [[triage/thing]] is not a special kind of entity but the name we give to any **stabilized** relational unit that maintains [[difference]] through [[tension]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] produces the **minimal relative**: the simplest [[structure]] that holds open the dynamic of [[inclusion]], [[exclusion]], and their [[tension]].
    - This minimal relative is the **first [[triage/thing]]**.

- However, the system does not limit what counts as a [[triage/thing]] to this minimal case:
    - Any stabilized [[relative]]—whether simple or complex—is a [[triage/thing]];
    - A [[triage/thing]] can be a single basic relative or a **nested/compound** relative assembled through further relational dynamics.

- Therefore, a [[triage/thing]] is **defined structurally**:
    - It is a [[structure]] that holds at least one [[relation]] open durably;
    - It achieves [[coherence]] by stabilizing the acts and conditions (such as [[include|including]], [[exclude|excluding]], [[tension]], [[difference]], and [[relation]]) that bring it about.

- The [[triage/thing]] **cannot be separated** from the relational architecture that constitutes it:
    - It is not a primitive “given” outside the system;
    - It is **generated and maintained** through the interplay of [[act]], [[condition]], and [[structure]].

- In this way, every [[triage/thing]]:
    - Is sustained by a **coherent set** of relational dynamics;
    - Derives its **identity and distinctness** from the conditions of [[difference]] and [[tension]] that define it relationally.

## Implications

- The minimal [[triage/thing]] is the first [[relative]] formed in the derivation of [[exist|existing]];
    - More complex [[triage/thing|things]] arise by recursively applying relational dynamics, building up nested or composed relatives.
- Every [[structure]] that stably holds a [[relation]] is, by definition, a [[triage/thing]]:
    - Thus, the entire architecture of relationality is populated **only by things** of varying complexity and depth.
- There is no “bare” [[triage/thing]] outside of relationality:
    - To be a [[triage/thing]] is to be a **relationally-constituted, stabilized structure**;
    - The system admits no thing **without** relational grounding.
