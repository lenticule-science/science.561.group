- [[summary]]:: [[relating]] and [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate]] are structurally distinct; their [[difference]] is a [[necessary condition]] for [[induce|inducing]] the [[domain of relating]].
- [[relational expression]]:: [[relating]] has [[difference]] from [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate]]
- [[proof]]::
	- the [[domain of relating]] includes:
		- [[relating|relating]]
		- [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate|unrelating]]
		- relating relating and unrelating
	- [[assume]] relating has no [[difference]] from unrelating
		- relating relating and unrelating and relating relating and relating would be the same
		- this is a reflexive form
	- but the domain of relating is induced by irresolvable including and excluding, not reflex
	- therefore, relating and unrelating must be different
- [[corollaries]]:
	- [[difference]] is a [[necessary condition]] for [[relational structure]]
	- the [[domain of relating]] cannot be [[induce|induced]] from [[include|including]] alone.
