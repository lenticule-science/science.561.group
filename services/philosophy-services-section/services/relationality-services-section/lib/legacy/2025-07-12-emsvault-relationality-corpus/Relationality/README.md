This repository is for the development and application of a theory of relational dynamics. The repository is built as an Obsidian Vault.

There is a directory of a developed [[relational system]] which formally derives [[exist|existing]] through a [[dynamic of determination]], a [[dynamic of relation]], and a [[dynamic of form]], which together shape [[act|acts]], [[condition|conditions]], and [[structure|structures]], [[induce|inducing]] a [[relational cohesion unit]].

These derivations induce an irresolvable arc of dynamics which recursively reflex, determining earlier dynamics at the same time they necessitate further derivation.
