- [[define]]:: [[system/acts/unrelate|Unrelating]] is the minimal [[act]] of exclusion, required to sustain [[difference]] by resisting inclusion. It is derived as the necessary complement to [[relating|relating]], ensuring that relationality is not trivialized into undifferentiated inclusion.

## Derivation

- The [[act]] of [[relating|relating]] introduces inclusion:

    - It positions one [[triage/thing]] within another, generating the basis of relationality.
    
- However, [[relating|relating]] alone is insufficient to sustain meaningful relationality:

    - If everything were included without resistance, all differentiation would collapse;
    - Pure inclusion leads to triviality: no internal structure or separation can persist.

- To prevent this collapse, a complementary [[act]] is required:

    - one that **resists inclusion** and sustains separation;
    - this act cannot merely negate existence but must **actively maintain exclusion** as a relational position.
    
- We name this act [[system/acts/unrelate|unrelating]]:

    - the act that holds a [[triage/thing]] **out of relation**—maintaining its distinctness from what is included;
    - [[system/acts/unrelate|Unrelating]] is not destruction but a **positional exclusion**: it asserts that something is **not included** within a given relational context.
    
- [[relating|Relating]] and [[system/acts/unrelate|unrelating]] are [[primitive]] and mutually irreducible:

    - Neither can be reduced to or derived from the other;
    - Both are required to generate and sustain [[tension]]: the condition in which inclusion and exclusion are held together in ongoing dynamic opposition.

- [[system/acts/unrelate|Unrelating]] is necessary to:

    - Preserve [[difference]] within relationality;
    - Sustain the **internal coherence** of any relational structure by **preventing collapse into undifferentiated inclusion**;
    - Enable the minimal architecture of relationality to remain **structured, balanced, and dynamically stable**.

- Without [[system/acts/unrelate|unrelating]]:

    - [[relating|Relating]] would trivialize into total inclusion;
    - [[tension]] would dissolve;
    - The minimal coherent structure of relationality could not be sustained.

- Therefore, [[system/acts/unrelate|unrelating]] is derived as an indispensable [[act]] within the [[domain of relating]], paired with [[relating|relating]] to sustain the minimal conditions for relational coherence.
