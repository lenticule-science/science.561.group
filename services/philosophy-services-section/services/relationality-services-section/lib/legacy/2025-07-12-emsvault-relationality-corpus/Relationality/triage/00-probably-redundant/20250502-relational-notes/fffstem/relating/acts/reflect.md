- [[summary]]:: **Reflect** is the act of [[relating|relating]] a [[relational structure]] to another distinct structure while preserving the relational identity of the source.

Reflect extends [[zeds/Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating]] by enforcing that the source and target are distinct (`S ≠ T`). It performs active inclusion **across difference** while maintaining the originating structure's context.

Reflect enables lateral relational connections and induces the condition of [[relational distinction]].

## Expression

reflect(S, T): relating(S, T) with S ≠ T and S preserved

## Properties

- Asymmetric: `reflect(S, T) ≠ reflect(T, S)`
- Difference-preserving: requires `S ≠ T`
- Context-preserving: `S` retains its relational identity after reflection
