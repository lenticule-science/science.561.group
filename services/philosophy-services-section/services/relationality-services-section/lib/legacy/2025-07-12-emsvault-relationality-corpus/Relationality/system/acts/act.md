- [[define]]:: An [[act]] is the thing by which a [[triage/thing]] brings about any [[condition]] or [[structure]]. Every [[relation]] and every [[difference]] requires an [[act]] to [[exist]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|deriving]] [[exist|existing]] derives [[act|acts]]
    - Every claim of anything being the case involves something having been done.
- An [[act]] is what introduces [[difference]] into a field.
    - Without an [[act]], there is no way to set apart what is from what is not.
    - This makes the [[act]] a necessary condition for any [[difference]] to be realized.
- Every [[relation]] presupposes an [[act]].
    - To [[relating|relating]] requires an [[act]] that enacts the [[relation]].
    - Without an [[act]], no [[relation]] can be established, sustained, or held open.

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that is [[exist|existing]] does so by way of at least one [[act]].
- An [[act]] is the point of minimal [[induce|induction]] for all [[relational dynamics]].
- No [[condition]] or [[structure]] arises without an [[act]] that brings it into being.
