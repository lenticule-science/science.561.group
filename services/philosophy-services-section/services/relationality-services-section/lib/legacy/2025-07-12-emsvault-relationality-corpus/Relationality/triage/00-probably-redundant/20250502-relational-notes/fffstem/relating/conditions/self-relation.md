- [[summary]]:: **self-relation** is the [[condition]] in which a [[relational structure]] is [[relating|related]] to itself through the [[act]] of [[reflex]].

Self-relation does not assume identity or self-similarity. It is induced when a structure performs the act of [[relating]] upon itself.

This condition enables the formation of nested structural layers and recursive participation.

## Expression

self-relation(S): reflex(S) = relating(S, S)

## Properties

- Requires a structure to be an admissible input to [[relating]].
- Cannot be derived from [[relating]] or [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate]] alone.
- Enables layered depth: a structure becomes part of itself again.
