- [[define]]:: [[reflexivity]] is the minimal [[condition]] under which a [[relational structure]] sustains its own [[reflex]] without alteration. It holds open the dynamic by which a [[relational structure]] remains coherent when [[related]] to itself, ensuring that the operation of [[reflexing]] neither introduces nor eliminates any relational determination already present.

## Derivation

- [[reflexivity]] is [[induce|induced]] by the act of [[reflexing]].

- To [[reflex]] is to perform `relating(S, S)` for a [[relational structure]] `S`.

- This act deepens the [[structure]], but for coherence, the [[condition]] of [[reflexivity]] is required:

    - It holds the [[relation]] between `S` and `relating(S, S)` open as **internally stable.**

- Without [[reflexivity]], the result of [[reflexing]] would lack coherence, causing the [[relational structure]] to fail closure under its own reflex.

- Therefore, [[reflexivity]] is necessary for sustaining [[reflexive]] acts as **structurally coherent operations** within the [[relational system]].
