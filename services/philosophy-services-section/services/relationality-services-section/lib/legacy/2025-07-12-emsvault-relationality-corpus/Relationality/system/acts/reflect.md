- [[define]]:: [[reflecting]] is the minimal [[act]] by which a [[relational structure]] actively sustains its own [[reflexivity]]. It enacts the relation `relating(S, S)` for a [[relational structure]] `S`, maintaining coherence by ensuring that the result is held within the same [[relational system]] without rupture or escalation of type.

## Derivation

- The act of [[reflexing]] produces `relating(S, S)`, deepening the [[relational structure]].

- However, for this act to maintain coherence, the [[relational structure]] must not only undergo reflex but also **sustain its own reflexivity** actively.

- This sustained activity is formalized as [[reflecting]].

- [[reflecting]] differs from mere [[reflexing]] by **holding open** the reflexive relation over time, ensuring stability of the [[reflexive]] operation within the ongoing dynamics of the system.

- Without [[reflecting]], reflexive acts risk becoming transient or incoherent, failing to anchor the recursive deepening in a stable way.

- Therefore, [[reflecting]] is necessary wherever reflexivity must be maintained as an **ongoing, structurally coherent act** in the [[relational system]].
