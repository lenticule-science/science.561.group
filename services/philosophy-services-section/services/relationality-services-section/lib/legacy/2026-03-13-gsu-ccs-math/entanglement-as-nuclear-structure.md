### Conjecture — Entanglement as Shared Nuclear Structure {#entanglement-as-nuclear-structure}

Two particles are entangled when they share a formula that cannot be factored into
independent sub-formulas at each fiber. Measuring one collapses the shared formula,
reducing $\mathsf{ent}$ (see [nuclear-reduction-measure](nuclear-reduction-measure.md))
— which is why the other particle's state is instantly determined. Decoherence is the
environment running normalization steps that were not requested.

**What a proof requires.** A tensor product structure on fibers $H_t \otimes H_t$
representing two-particle states, and a factorizability criterion in terms of
$\mathsf{ent}$.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
