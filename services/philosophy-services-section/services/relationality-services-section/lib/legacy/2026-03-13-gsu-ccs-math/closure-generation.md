### Lemma — Closure Generation {#closure-generation}

The subclass $\mathrm{Obj}(R_{\mathrm{gen}})$ is $U_G$-closed and contains $A$.

**Proof.** Each $X \in \mathcal{C}$ is $U_G$-closed and contains $A$. Intersections preserve both
properties. $\square$

**Consequence.** $R_{\mathrm{gen}}$ is the **least fixed point** of $U_G$ containing the atoms: for
any $U_G$-closed subclass $X \supseteq A$, we have $\mathrm{Obj}(R_{\mathrm{gen}}) \subseteq X$.
