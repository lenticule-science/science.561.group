### Definition — Generated Subuniverse {#generated-subuniverse}

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the collection of all $U_G$-closed subclasses of $\mathrm{Obj}(R)$ containing
the atomic class $A$. Define
$$
\mathrm{Obj}(R_{\mathrm{gen}}) \;:=\;
\bigcap \{\, X \in \mathcal{C} \,\}.
$$
The corresponding full subcategory $R_{\mathrm{gen}} \subseteq R$ is the **generated sub-universe**.
