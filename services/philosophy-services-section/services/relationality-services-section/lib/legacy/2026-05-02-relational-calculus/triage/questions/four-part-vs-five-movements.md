The relational calculus has two organizational schemes that overlap but are not identical:

- The **four-part framework**: RelationalStructure (Heyting algebra) → RelationalLanguage ($\lambda_R$) → RelationalGeometry (nuclei + Flow + cohesion) → RelationalPhysics (states + observables + conservation).
- The **five movements**: Logical Origination → Structural Stabilization → Directed Dynamics → Geometric Cohesion → Emergent Containment.

The two are partially aligned but not identical. RelationalStructure roughly = Movements I+II; RelationalGeometry roughly = Movements III+IV; RelationalPhysics roughly = Movement V; RelationalLanguage *cuts across* the movements rather than living at any one of them.

## What the sources say

[four-part-framework.md](../teaching/explanations/four-part-framework.md) gives the framework as the *layered* organization and acknowledges that RelationalLanguage is "cross-cutting." [five-movements.md](../teaching/explanations/five-movements.md) gives the movements as the *narrative* organization and does not directly map onto the four parts.

## Why it matters

If the two schemes are equivalent up to a re-binning, the framework has one organizational structure with two presentation modes, and any theorem stated in one can be re-stated in the other. If they are not equivalent — if RelationalLanguage genuinely lives off the movement axis — then there are theorems about RelationalLanguage that do not have a natural home in the movement scheme, and conversely the [Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction](../teaching/explanations/discipline-regime-filter-profile.md) theorem (which produces a complete four-part chain inside every Profile) does not have a clean movement-level statement.

## Three resolutions to weigh

1. **The five movements are correct; the four parts are a cross-cut.** Movements I+II produce a Heyting algebra; that algebra's *internal language* is RelationalLanguage; that language is available at every movement once Movements I+II have closed. RelationalGeometry is what Movements III+IV produce when reading the geometric content of the modal-comonadic-cohesive structure. RelationalPhysics is Movement V. On this reading, Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction is the theorem that every Profile internally reproduces all five movements (and therefore their cross-cuts).

2. **The four parts are correct; the five movements are a derivation history.** The four-part framework is the *finished structure*; the five-movement narrative is one route to it. A different route might produce the same four parts via three or seven movements.

3. **They are different theories disguised as one.** The four-part framework is a *description of relational structures*; the five-movement narrative is a *derivation of relational structures from one paradox*. The connection between them is the meta-theorem that the derivation produces a structure of the four-part form, but the two are otherwise independent.

## Status

Open. The author's working position appears closest to (1) — the five movements are the derivation, the four parts are the resulting structure's stratification, and RelationalLanguage is the internal computational layer that the closure of Movements I+II produces and that all subsequent movements use. This should be made explicit; until then, downstream readers cannot reliably cite either scheme as canonical.
