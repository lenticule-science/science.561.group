The act-algebra trace gives two incompatible compositions for the act *Relate*:

- **Form A:** `Relate = (Reflex)` — Reflex acting as a unary witness records "this is a relating." The minimal one-place trace.
- **Form B:** `Relate = (Reflex Reflex)` — Reflex placed in both witness and witness-ee positions; the act of Reflex relating to its own iteration.

Form A appears in [relation.md §(1)](../teaching/explanations/relation.md) ("once reflex iteration is available, Reflex may bear witness to the mere fact of relating, so a dedicated relation act appears"). Form B appears in some derivation traces also in the source material; the two coexist with no resolution.

## Why it matters

The two are not stylistic variants. Form A treats Relate as a unary mark on Reflex (a witnessing of Reflex's existence). Form B treats Relate as the binary self-application `(Reflex Reflex)`, which is the same composition as `IterateReflex = (Reflex Reflex)` in [iterate-reflex.md](../teaching/explanations/iterate-reflex.md). If Form B is correct, then `Relate` and `IterateReflex` denote the same composite — the framework has two names for one act, which the trace nomenclature is designed to avoid.

Conversely, if Form A is correct, then *unary witnessing* is a primitive trace move (a single act in witness position with no witnessed). The standard `(Witness Witnessed)` template would need to be amended to allow `(Witness)` as a valid trace, which weakens the trace's structural discipline.

## Three resolutions to weigh

1. **Form A wins; allow unary witnessing.** Acts may have arity 1 or 2. The trace template generalizes. Cost: nomenclature loosens.
2. **Form B wins; collapse `Relate` and `IterateReflex`.** They are the same act under two names; rename one to the other (preferentially keeping `IterateReflex` in the granular layer and using `Relate` only as a Movement-III synonym for Flow).
3. **Both are valid, naming distinct acts.** Form A is *Relate-as-witnessing-of-Reflex*; Form B is *Relate-as-mutual-Reflex*. They occupy different positions in the trace and should be distinguished by name (e.g., `WitnessReflex` vs `RelateReflex`).

The third option is the most expensive but the most honest to the source material, which appears to use both compositions deliberately in different contexts. A close reading of the granular trace as it appears across `relationality/concepts/`, the formal-axioms doc, and the on-relationality unified draft would be needed to confirm.

## Status

Open. No source resolves this.
