The condition in which an act, its corresponding conditions, and supporting structures hold together in a stable, self-sufficient dynamic. Coherence is what marks that a relational system is complete enough to persist. It is the goal of each stage of [the derivation](../texts/the-derivation.md), and its *incompleteness* at each stage is what [incites](incitement.md) the next.

## Three components

Coherence has three parts:

1. **Acts do the work.**
2. **Conditions are held open.**
3. **Structures stabilize and durably maintain the results.**

All three must be present *and* held together as a unified whole. Their interplay must resolve without collapse, dissipation, or irresolvable contradiction.

## A family of conditions, not a single condition

Coherence is not one condition but a family, each more comprehensive than the last. Each movement of the derivation "establishes a kind of relational coherence whose incompleteness incites the next." Across the 9 phases of the derivation, the names mark distinct kinds: Existential Coherence, Relational Coherence, Sustained Relational Dynamics, Bounded Relational Coherence, Reflexive Relational Coherence, Multirelational Coherence, Relational Field Coherence, Relational Meta-Boundary Coherence, Meta-relational Coherence. Each names a kind of coherence achieved at a particular scale.

## Coherence at each movement

| Movement | Coherence achieved |
|---|---|
| I (Logical Origination) | Consistent distinguishing — the Heyting algebra |
| II (Structural Stabilization) | Sustained form — modal closure/interior balance |
| III (Directed Dynamics) | Directed motion — [Flow](flow.md) preserves the modal core |
| IV (Geometric Cohesion) | Local-global translation — the [cohesive chain](cohesive-chain.md) |
| V (Emergent Containment) | The system with its own limits — self-containment |

## Why it matters

Coherence is the central concept of the derivation for two reasons:

1. **It is what the derivation produces.** Every step, every movement, earns a coherence — a self-sufficient holding-together of acts, conditions, and structures.
2. **Its incompleteness is what drives the derivation forward.** Each achieved coherence leaves something undetermined; the undetermined remainder incites further determination.

This makes coherence the link between the [triadic engine](triadic-engine.md) (act → condition → structure) and the five movements (each achieving and exceeding a coherence). The triadic engine describes *how* coherence is achieved at a given level; the movements describe *what* coherence is achieved at each level.
