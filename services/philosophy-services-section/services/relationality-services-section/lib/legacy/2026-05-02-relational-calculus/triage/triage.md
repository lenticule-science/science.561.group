The relational calculus is the theory of *relational acts* — what acts are forced when one tries to articulate the absence of relation, and what calculus those acts close into. Its starting point is not an axiom but a paradox: speaking of *relationlessness* already enacts a distinction, and distinction is relational; the void cannot be named without being undone. The act witnessing that genesis is named [Reflex](concepts/reflex.md). Iterating Reflex (and the small family of constructors it forces — togethering, eithering, inducing, negating, closing, opening, flowing, residuating, the cohesive chain, observing, conserving) generates the universe of acts $A_\infty$ as the least fixed point of a [generative closure](concepts/generative-closure.md) operator $G$ on the seed $\{$Relationlessness$\}$.

The calculus has matured into several coordinate views of one structure:

- The **act-algebra trace** — every act is a tree of earlier acts, written `Act = (Witness Witnessed)`, beginning at the empty trace `Relationlessness = ()` and unfolding through `Exclude`, `Articulate`, `EmergeReflex`, `FirstRecognize`, `Reflex`, `Relate`, … into ~50 named granular acts (witness, recognition, void, first-name, self-naming, entry, trace, head/tail, context, term, lambda binder, judgement, …). This is the *constructive* layer.
- The **five movements** — the *philosophical narrative* organizing the closure into Logical Origination, Structural Stabilization, Directed Dynamics, Geometric Cohesion, and Emergent Containment, each earning a [coherence](concepts/coherence.md) whose incompleteness incites the next. Each movement closes into one block of standard mathematics: a complete Heyting algebra; the Kelly–Lack lax-idempotent closure / interior; a comonadic Flow with KZ-laxity; the Lawvere–Schreiber cohesive adjoint quadruple ([RITM](concepts/ritm.md)); a state/observable/conservation layer with a Noether-style correspondence.
- The **four-part framework** — the *layered* organization: RelationalStructure (Heyting algebra), RelationalLanguage (a typed lambda calculus $\lambda_R$ internal at every level), RelationalGeometry (nuclei + Flow + cohesion), RelationalPhysics (states + observables + conservation). RelationalLanguage cuts across the movements rather than living at any one of them.
- The **discipline / regime / filter / profile** hierarchy — the theory of how the calculus *specializes without breaking*. A Filter is a discipline commuting with all nuclei, with Flow, and with the cohesive adjunctions; its fixed-point Profile inherits the entire four-part chain. The Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction theorem says every well-behaved perspective on the field reproduces the entire calculus internally.

These views are not parallel theories. They are the same structure observed at different granularities and from different sides; reconciling them — deciding when *Domain*, *Movement*, *Layer*, and *Profile* refer to the same structural object and when they do not — is a load-bearing piece of unfinished synthesis. See [questions/](questions/).

## What this directory is

This is the synthesis of five overlapping triage dumps (`relationality/`, `relationality-2/`, `on-relationality/`, `relationality-inner-triage/{internal-theory,research,research-notes}`, `from-sloppyresearch/content/relationing/`) into one theory directory shaped on the precedent of [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md). The synthesis lives in `triage/` because the captain has not yet promoted it to the live library; the substantive content is current, but the framing, naming choices, and divergence-resolutions deserve review before the directory loses its `triage/` segment.

The shape mirrors predication-categories:

- `concepts/` — semantic anchors (one file per concept, lower-kebab-case slug). The 59 files here range from primitive trace acts (`relationlessness`, `exclude`, `articulate`, `void`, `first-name`, `self-naming`, …) through the load-bearing structural primitives (`reflex`, `differentiate`, `relating`, `relation`, `tension`, `coherence`, `incitement`, `irreducible-asymmetry`, `triadic-engine`, `five-movements`, `four-part-framework`, `discipline-regime-filter-profile`, `generative-closure`, `self-grounding`) up to the mathematical layer's named structures (`flow`, `induces`, `dynamic-residuation`, `cohesive-chain`, `ritm`, `stratifying`, `states-observables`, `conserving`).
- `theorems/` — the load-bearing claims. Currently: the [Articulation Theorem](theorems/articulation-theorem.md) and its Reflex Emergence Lemma. Several further theorems (Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction, RITM self-stabilization, Existence-Observation Duality / Ambidexterity) are presently stated inside concept files; they need lifting into standalone theorem notes.
- `narratives/` — connective documents that walk through the calculus rather than defining one piece of it. Currently: the unified-derivation walkthrough (lifted from `on-relationality.md`), the formal-axioms layer (lifted from `from-sloppyresearch/content/relationing/formal-axioms-of-relationing/`), and the position relative to predication-categories.
- `questions/` — the live divergences. Where two source drafts gave incompatible accounts of the same construct, the divergence is recorded here as a question rather than silently picked. These are the most important files for a reader to read first; they frame what the calculus has not yet settled.
- `references/` — external works the dumps cite (Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form*, Street, Lawvere & Schreiber, Riehl & Verity, Kelly).

## Position relative to predication-categories

The relational calculus is **not** a predecessor to [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) and not a competitor — it is a *parallel articulation* with a different starting move. Predication-categories starts from the cell — a $(s, p, o)$ predication valued in a bilattice — and asks what categorical and fibrational structure that cell forces. The relational calculus starts from Reflex and asks what acts the iteration of Reflex compels.

The two meet:

- The Heyting algebra earned by Movement I of the relational calculus is the truth-axis order-theoretic content sitting inside the [bilattice](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/bilattice.md)-valued predication fiber algebras of predication-categories.
- The closure operator earned by Movement II ($\text{Close}$ as nucleus) corresponds to the [closure operator](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-operator.md) on predication fiber algebras whose 2-monadic lift is the [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md). The lax-idempotence properties match.
- The directed Flow of Movement III plays the role the [predication settling](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) plays in the spatiotemporal-knowledge application of predication-categories — both are the dynamics through which a relational/predicational state evolves toward saturation.
- The Lawvere–Schreiber cohesive quadruple of Movement IV (RITM) is the same adjoint quadruple Lawvere uses for cohesive toposes; predication-categories' bitopological / Priestley side carries a related but not identical structure (paired-Priestley spaces, d-frame-valued sheaves).

The most likely synthesis once both theories mature: predication-categories provides the *categorical and bilattice-valued* side; the relational calculus provides the *act-algebraic and derivational* side; the load-bearing question is whether the two can be made formally equivalent (e.g. is there a faithful translation between the act-algebra trace and the predication settling's saturation orbit?). This question is open. See [questions/relation-to-predication-categories.md](questions/relation-to-predication-categories.md).

## Convention

Frontmatter follows the corpus convention (see CLAUDE.md and the predication-categories `_index.md`):

```yaml
---
type: concept | theorem | question | narrative | reference

---
```

Every note is one entity per file, lower-kebab-case slug. Math is first-class in `depends-on:` — when a relational-calculus claim cites a predication-category construct, the dependency is direct, not narrated as a sidebar.

Most concepts and theorems here are currently `status: sketch` per the novel-research-potential discipline in CLAUDE.md. The dumps that fed this synthesis carry proof-sketches and trace-derivations rather than fully checked proofs; promotion to `draft` or `proved` happens when the formal layer (`from-sloppyresearch/.../formal-axioms-of-relationing/` material, lifted here as the [formal-axioms narrative](narratives/formal-axioms-layer.md) and a few extracted theorems) is filled in.
