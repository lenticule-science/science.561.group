The condition achieved when a relational structure maintains the same form across infinite reflexive layers. When a recognition is iterated through self-application — $a, S(a), S^2(a), S^3(a), \ldots$ — it either diverges, oscillates, or converges. Reflexive equilibrium is convergence: the point at which further self-application produces no new structure.

This is the relational concept underlying the formal notion of a fixed point. `Close(a) = a` means $a$ has achieved reflexive equilibrium — iterating the closure operator produces nothing beyond $a$.

## Criteria

- **Asymptotic equilibrium** — the form approaches a limit as reflexive depth increases. The limit exists even if no finite depth achieves it exactly.
- **Uniqueness** — if equilibrium exists, it is unique. The monotonicity of iteration gives a single ascending chain whose join is unique.
- **Depth cutoff** — in practice, equilibrium may be treated as achieved when further iteration produces no detectable change at a given resolution. This is an approximation; the formal definition requires the limit of the infinite sequence.
- **Total reflexive closure** — every substructure has achieved reflexive equilibrium with every other substructure it relates to.

## Where it appears

- **Philosophically**: being requires sustained coherence — not merely single acts, but ongoing maintenance of the interplay. The form must be maintained, not just achieved once.
- **At the framework level**: [RITM](ritm.md)'s self-stabilizing property *is* reflexive equilibrium applied to the whole structure. Once realized, RITM generates internal sub-modalities that reproduce the RITM axioms — the framework has reached reflexive equilibrium with itself.
- **At the order level**: the transfinite closure tower formalizes how reflexive equilibrium is approached. Each stage of the tower includes all previous stages; the limit (if it exists) *is* the equilibrium.
- **At the structural level**: a relative that sustains itself — that maintains its own relating without external support — achieves [coherence](coherence.md). This is reflexive equilibrium as a philosophical condition.
