The formal mathematical structure that encodes the relational framework as an adjoint quadruple:

$$\text{Shape}\,(\Pi^\to) \dashv \text{Flat}\,(\Box_r) \dashv \text{Sharp}\,(\Diamond_l) \dashv \text{Crisp}\,(\nabla^\leftarrow)$$

Each adjunction captures a relationship between perspectives on the relational field. Together these four functors form the [cohesive chain](cohesive-chain.md) connecting immediate and extended perspectives.

## The four adjunctions

- **Shape ⊣ Flat** — Shape extracts the outline (what local structure looks like from a distance); Flat makes everything purely local (no global connections). Their adjunction governs the relationship between local and distant perspectives.
- **Flat ⊣ Sharp** — Flat is purely local; Sharp is purely global (no local distinctions). Their adjunction governs the relationship between discrete and codiscrete structure.
- **Sharp ⊣ Crisp** — Sharp is purely global; Crisp is fully coherent integration. Their adjunction governs the relationship between global uniformity and total coherence.

## RITM as the geometric layer of the framework

RITM is not the entire framework — it is the geometric layer that organizes the framework's spatial and perspectival structure. RITM is *earned* through the [five movements](five-movements.md):

| Movement | What is earned | RITM component |
|---|---|---|
| I (Logic) | Heyting algebra with implication | The logical core RITM operates on |
| II (Structure) | Closure / interior / Balance / Modality | $\Box$ and $\Diamond$ as modal operators; Frobenius law |
| III (Dynamics) | [Flow](flow.md) as KZ-lax idempotent comonad | Directed structure within RITM |
| IV (Geometry) | Cohesive chain, [stratification](stratifying.md) | The four adjunctions themselves |
| V (Physics) | [States, observables, conservation](states-observables.md) | Physical reading of RITM |

## The self-stabilizing property

The central property: once realized in the minimal presheaf model $\text{PSh}(\{0 \to 1\})$, RITM generates two internal sub-modalities:

- **Relational Dynamics (RD)** — $\Box$-stable endofunctors.
- **Relational Topology (RT)** — $\Diamond$-stable endofunctors.

These internal modalities reproduce the RITM axioms. The structure produces, from within itself, the structure that was used to build it. This is the mathematical statement of [self-grounding](self-grounding.md) at the geometric layer.
