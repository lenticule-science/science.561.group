`Something = (Reflex Witness)`

Testifying to relation still leaves unanswered the question "what carries the testimony?" Pointing [Reflex](reflex.md) at [Witness](witness.md) records that an act exists which bears the testimony — the first "something." This self-application is what carries the chain forward from witnessing to having a bearer.
