The adjoint quadruple connecting local and global perspectives. Four idempotent operators forming a chain of adjunctions:

$$\text{Shape} \dashv \text{Flat} \dashv \text{Sharp} \dashv \text{Crisp}$$

Equivalently, in modal notation: $\Pi^\to \dashv \Box \dashv \Diamond \dashv \nabla^\leftarrow$.

## What it is

The four functors:

- **Shape** ($\Pi^\to$) — connected-components; collapses local structure to its outline.
- **Flat / Discrete** ($\Box$) — discrete embedding; everything purely local, no global connections.
- **Sharp / Codiscrete** ($\Diamond$) — codiscrete embedding; everything purely global, no local distinctions.
- **Crisp / Global** ($\nabla^\leftarrow$) — global sections; the fully coherent integration.

The chain arises from decomposition of the reflexive operator $R$. The abstract reflexive operation unfolds into a modal chain whose internal directions correspond to four roles of coherence: collecting, fixing, releasing, and dispersing. Local-global correspondence: what holds locally translates coherently to what holds globally, and vice versa. Depth alignment: multi-relational units must align their reflexive sequences to maintain coherent engagement within the integrated field.

## What it produces

- **Cohesion** — local behavior and global structure translate without loss.
- **Residuation** — the interplay between doing-then-stabilizing and stabilizing-then-doing.
- The ground for stratified geometry (the [Stratify](stratifying.md) operator).
- The vocabulary for complex relational architectures: networks, nodes, edges.

## Key relationships

- Includes [Necessary](necessary-possible.md) ($\Box$) and [Possible](necessary-possible.md) ($\Diamond$) as the middle pair.
- [Flow](flow.md) is $\Box\Diamond$ — composition of the middle two.
- Mathematical correspondence: Lawvere's axiomatic cohesion.
