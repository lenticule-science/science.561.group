What incomplete structure compels. The pattern by which each achieved [coherence](coherence.md)'s incompleteness drives the derivation forward. Incitement is the engine of the entire derivation — why the argument cannot stop at any finite stage.

## Three formulations of the same idea

The same pattern is named three ways across the framework's articulations:

1. **"It cannot not derive"** — the explicit formula. Given that $X$ has been derived, it cannot not derive $Y$. Not logical necessity in the classical sense — there is no axiom system being applied — but *philosophical necessity*: the coherence of $X$ requires $Y$, and the absence of $Y$ would leave $X$ incoherent.
2. **"Determines $X$ but cannot determine $Y$"** — the indeterminacy pattern. Each step resolves one indeterminacy and reveals another. Witness, for instance, *determines that those recognitions coexist but cannot determine which carries the act*. Each resolved indeterminacy reveals a new one.
3. **"The incompleteness of [achieved structure] incites [next structure]"** — the mature formulation. Each movement achieves a coherence; the achieved coherence's incompleteness — what it leaves undetermined — incites the next movement.

## The mechanism

Incitement is not external motivation. It is structural: the achieved structure *itself* requires further determination. The gap is internal, not imposed.

At each stage the relational field has structure (operators, properties, laws) that is self-consistent but *incomplete* — questions the structure can pose but cannot answer with the resources it has. Answering them requires new structure, which in turn poses new questions.

This is why the derivation is Heyting (non-Boolean): $\neg\neg a \geq a$ but $\neg\neg a$ need not equal $a$. There are recognitions whose double negation exceeds the recognition itself. The gap between $a$ and $\neg\neg a$ is the space in which incitement operates.

## Examples at each movement transition

- **I → II**: The logical core (Heyting algebra) earns consistent distinguishing, but recognitions can distinguish locally without global stability. The need for sustained, global coherence incites closure and the modal operators.
- **II → III**: Modal closure and interior provide stability, but stability is static. The boundary between closed and open must itself be related, inciting [Flow](flow.md).
- **III → IV**: Directed dynamics operate within a single field, but relations cluster locally and extend globally. The need for local-global translation incites the [cohesive chain](cohesive-chain.md).
- **IV → V**: Cohesive geometry provides architecture, but the system has not yet confronted its own boundary. The distinction between the relational field and what lies beyond incites emergent containment.

## Relationship to induction

Incitement has a dual: **induction** — what completed structure produces. Where incitement is what drives forward (the gap, the incompleteness), induction is what stabilizes (the achievement, the completion). Together they describe the oscillation: achieve → incite → achieve → incite.
