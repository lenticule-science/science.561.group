The framework uses *relating* at two distinct levels:

1. **Movement I sense.** The minimal act that sustains tension between including and excluding, holding a thing in coherent existing. Produces the condition of [relation](../teaching/explanations/relation.md), which produces the [relative](../teaching/explanations/relative.md) — the first stabilized form.

2. **Movement III sense.** The ongoing directed dynamics of the relational field, identified with [Flow](../teaching/explanations/flow.md) — a comonad with KZ-laxity and core preservation.

[relating.md](../teaching/explanations/relating.md) explicitly notes the two scales and does not commit on whether they are the same activity at different scales or genuinely distinct acts.

## Three resolutions

1. **Same activity, different scales.** Movement I relating produces coherence; coherence iterated and stabilized through enough recursive depth produces Flow as its mature form. The Movement III "relating" is the same act seen at the level where its iteration has stabilized into a comonad. On this reading, the act-algebra trace should connect Movement I `Relate` directly to Flow via a sequence of compositions.

2. **Genuinely distinct acts.** Movement I relating is the *micro* act that maintains a single tension; Flow is the *macro* dynamics of the field. They share a name because they share a structural role (sustaining a tension over time) but do different work and carry different formal structure (Movement I relating produces difference; Flow produces a CoKleisli category).

3. **Movement III subsumes Movement I.** Movement I relating is a degenerate case of Flow (Flow restricted to two-element tensions). On this reading, the framework should drop the Movement I sense and re-derive Movement I phenomena as Flow-on-trivial-objects.

## Why it matters

The translation from the act-algebra trace to the formal layer has to know which sense is operative when the trace says "relating." If the two are the same (Position 1 or 3), the trace and the formal layer are talking about one thing under two names, and the granular composition `Relating = …` should connect to the comonadic Flow. If the two are distinct (Position 2), the trace's `Relating` is *not* the formal Flow, and there is a separate Movement III act-name needed.

The current source material does not commit. Position 2 (genuinely distinct) is the safest reading of the texts and the easiest to defend technically — Movement I relating produces a *condition* (relation) while Flow is an *operator* on already-stabilized recognitions. But Position 1 (same activity) is more elegant and is suggested by the framework's overall self-similarity.
