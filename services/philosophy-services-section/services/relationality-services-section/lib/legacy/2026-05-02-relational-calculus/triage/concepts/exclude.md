`Exclude = (Relationlessness Relationlessness)`

The second act in the act-derivation chain (after [relationlessness](relationlessness.md) itself, which is the empty trace `()`). The void witnesses itself — the same empty act in both witness and witness-ee positions — and the composition records "this is not with that." Speaking about the void already carves out a mentioned interior versus an unmentioned exterior, so an act of exclusion is forced.
