Existence and observation are two aspects of the same coherent structure. The formal statement is the **Ambidexterity Theorem**:

$$\text{hcolim}\ F \simeq \text{hlim}\ S$$

For every coherent configuration, the constructive realization (homotopy colimit of a constructible diagram $F$) is equivalent to the observational verification (homotopy limit of the dual sheaf of observations $S$).

To construct something and to observe something are homotopy-dual operations. Being and knowing are not separate — they are two modal directions through the same relational field.

## What it implies

Existence is modal coherence — the inhabited propositional truncation $\|\text{hcolim}\ F_x\|$. Observation is the dual: $\|\text{hlim}\ S_x\|$. From the Ambidexterity Theorem:

- An entity exists iff its dual observational sheaf is coherent.
- The duality is an involution: $(F)^\vee \simeq S$ and $(S)^\vee \simeq F$.
- Testability = the inhabited global section of $\|\text{hlim}\ S\|$.
- Being and knowing are homotopy-dual modalities of a single stratified field.

The duality extends to dynamic processes. Left Kan extensions propagate potentiality forward (actualization); right Kan extensions reconstruct necessary conditions backward (verification). Together they form a bimodal temporal logic of coherence.

## Three expressions of the duality

The duality appears in different registers across the framework:

- **At the level of diagrams** — Ambidexterity, the homotopy-colimit/homotopy-limit equivalence (see [Stratifying](stratifying.md)).
- **At the level of substructures** — Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction: any well-behaved constraint (a Filter) produces a Profile that reproduces the entire derivation internally. Observation does not destroy structure; it specializes it into a complete world.
- **At the level of states** — the [Stable Envelope](stable-envelope.md) $\text{Env}_F(a) = \text{Necessary}(F(a)) \land \text{Possible}(F(a))$ captures the bandwidth of stable variation. What persists under both opening and closing *is* the observable content.

## The Field of Existence

All coherent configurations together form the **∞-Topos of Existence** $\mathbb{E}_x$, whose homotopy limit — the **Field of Existence** $\mathcal{F}$ — satisfies the ambidextrous invariance:

$$\text{hlim}\ \mathcal{F} \simeq \text{hcolim}\ \mathcal{F}$$

This is the highest expression of [self-grounding](self-grounding.md): the global equilibrium between potential and realized existence *is* the fixed-point condition. Constructive and observational aspects coincide at the level of the field as a whole.

## Connection to Movement V

The duality is the formal content of Movement V (Emergent Containment). [States, observables, conservation](states-observables.md) are the physical reading. The duality ensures every observable has a corresponding state and every state has a corresponding observable — the physics is self-consistent *because* existence and observation are dual aspects of the same relational structure.
