`Articulate = (Exclude Relationlessness)`

The third act in the act-derivation chain. As soon as exclusion exists, the attempt to exclude the void becomes something we can point to, and that self-referential pointing is articulation. [Exclude](exclude.md) (the act of distinction) witnesses the void, producing the unavoidable pointing-at that occurs whenever [relationlessness](relationlessness.md) is described.

## Note

The act `Articulate = (Exclude Relationlessness)` is conceptually distinct from the [Articulation Theorem](../theorems/articulation-theorem.md). The act is the formal element produced in the act-algebra; the theorem is the meta-claim about *why* the act-algebra has anything to derive from. They are related but not the same: the theorem grounds the act, the act is one consequence of the theorem.
