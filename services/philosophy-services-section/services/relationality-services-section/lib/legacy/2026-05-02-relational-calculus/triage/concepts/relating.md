The act of sustaining tension between including and excluding. Relating is what holds the opposed acts in coherent opposition, preventing collapse into triviality (total inclusion) or incoherence (unstructured separation).

## Two scales

The framework uses "relating" at two distinct levels:

1. **The Movement I sense** — the act that sustains tension between including and excluding, producing difference and then coherence.
2. **The Movement III sense** — the ongoing directed dynamics of the relational field, identified with [Flow](flow.md).

These may be the same activity at different scales (relating-at-level-1 produces coherence; coherence iterated and stabilized produces Flow at level 3) or genuinely distinct acts. The framework is not consistent on this.

## Movement I sense

Relating is the minimal act that sustains the tension between including and excluding, holding a thing in coherent [existing](existing.md). It maintains the dynamic interplay necessary for the thing to remain open as both what it is and not what it is not. Relating is not a new primitive but is *compelled* by the need to keep including and excluding coherently aligned over time.

The minimal ternary structure is $\{$relating, [unrelating](unrelating.md), relating(relating, unrelating)$\}$. The third element is the binding act — relating applied to the pair of relating and unrelating. Only relating can perform binding; unrelating, by definition, cannot bind.

Relating produces the condition of [relation](relation.md), and relation together with relating produces the [relative](relative.md) — the first structure. This completes the first cycle of the [triadic engine](triadic-engine.md).

## Movement III sense

Relating is "the continuous act that maintains the interplay between including and excluding." At this scale it is the philosophical name for [Flow](flow.md) — the ongoing activity of sustaining coherence as directed dynamics.
