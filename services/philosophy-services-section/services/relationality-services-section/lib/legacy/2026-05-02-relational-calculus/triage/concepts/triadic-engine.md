The pattern by which each phase of [the derivation](../texts/the-derivation.md) unfolds. Each phase shows that a given structure's coherence requires three dynamics: **act** (determination), **condition** (relation), **structure** (form). The new structure leaves something undetermined, *inciting* the next phase.

## The pattern

Each phase of the derivation follows the same four-beat:

1. *"Given that X is determined by Y, it cannot not derive there is a dynamic of determination"* — names the **act**.
2. *"Given that the act is determined by how it engages Z, it cannot not derive there is a dynamic of relation"* — names the **condition**.
3. *"Given that the condition is determined by how it structures W, it cannot not derive there is a dynamic of structure"* — names the **structure**.
4. *"Determining [act], [condition], and [structure] provides the minimal dynamic determination for cohering [the unit], but leaves unresolved dynamics which must be derived"* — **incites** the next phase.

The same triad appears in the algebra: act = formation of a [Differentiate](differentiate.md); condition = application of [Induces](induces.md)$(a, -)$; structure = composition $T(a) = \text{Together}(a, \text{Induces}(a, \top))$.

## The incitement

Each phase's structure is incomplete — its coherence requires something not yet given. This incompleteness is not a deficiency but the generative engine of the derivation: "leaves unresolved dynamics which must be derived."

This grounds two complementary relational methods:
- **[Incitement](incitement.md)** — what an incomplete structure compels.
- **Induction** — what a completed structure produces.

## Where it organizes

- The 9 phases of [the derivation](../texts/the-derivation.md) (and their reduction to the 5 [movements](five-movements.md)).
- Each Act in the [generative closure](generative-closure.md), where every Act has a top, a bottom, and a semantics — the same triadic shape.

**Exceptions**: phases 7 and 9 of the derivation produce *two* acts, *two* conditions, *two* structures (the field splits into local and global).
