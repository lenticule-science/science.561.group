**Unrelating** is the minimal act of exclusion: holding a thing out of relation. It is not destruction but positional exclusion, asserting that something is not included here; it sustains [difference](difference.md) by resisting inclusion.

Unrelating is the complement of [relating](relating.md). Without it relating would trivialize into total inclusion, [tension](tension.md) would dissolve, and there would be nothing to hold in opposition. The minimal structure of the relational field has three parts: the act of binding (relating), the act of resisting binding (unrelating), and the act that binds these two, relating(relating, unrelating).

Formally, unrelating becomes excluding, the disjointness of two elements whose meet is the bottom ([preorder](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/preorder.md)), and excluding becomes the Heyting pseudocomplement $\neg a = a \to \bot$, the strongest element incompatible with $a$ ([complement](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/complement.md)). These are one concept at increasing levels of formalization. The pseudocomplement is not the framework's [negation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/negation.md), which swaps evidence for and against.
