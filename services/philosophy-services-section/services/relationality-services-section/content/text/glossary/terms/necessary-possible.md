Modal operators derived from Close and Open applied objectwise. Necessary: what survives all closure (what must be). Possible: what can be opened from within (what may be).

## What they are

PRE: "Necessary: objectwise modality of Close. Standard symbol: J (objectwise)." "Possible: objectwise modality of Open. Standard symbol: U (objectwise)."

Foundations: Part of the adjoint quadruple Π→ ⊣ □ ⊣ ◇ ⊣ ∇←, where □ = necessity (Close applied pointwise) and ◇ = possibility (Open applied pointwise).

Draft 11: BoxModality / DiamondModality, mapping to PRE's Necessary / Possible.

Draft 12: S4 modal logic — □ and ◇ satisfying the axioms of reflexive transitive modal logic.

## What they produce

- The composition □◇ = Flow's comonadic structure (Foundations: C = □◇)
- The ground for directed dynamics (Movement III)
- Internal modal logic within the relational field

## Relationships

- Derived from: Close (→ Necessary) and Open (→ Possible), applied objectwise
- Compose to: Flow (as □◇ or comonad C)
- Part of: adjoint quadruple Π→ ⊣ □ ⊣ ◇ ⊣ ∇← (Foundations, draft 12)
