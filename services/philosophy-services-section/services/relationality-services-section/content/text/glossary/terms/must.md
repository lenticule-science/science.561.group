Must is necessity: what survives all [closure](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/closure.md). When Must is applied to a [recognition](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md), it picks out the part that persists under every consolidation — the core that cannot be undone by further distinction. What Must identifies is what the structure's own inward consolidation secures.

Must arises in [Movement II: Structural Stabilization](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/structural-stabilization.md) when the equilibrium between [Close](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md) and [Open](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md) gives rise to two ways of qualifying what holds of a recognition. The inward motion — consolidating relation until no further addition changes its form — manifests as necessity. The outward motion manifests as [possibility](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/may.md). These are not imposed logical categories. They emerge from the structure's own self-[stabilization](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/processes/stabilization.md).

Applying Must twice changes nothing beyond the first application — what is necessarily the case is necessarily necessarily the case. This self-consistency reflects the idempotence of the [closure](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/closure.md) from which Must arises.

Must and [May](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/may.md) interlock: what is necessarily possible is possible, and what is possibly necessary is necessary. Every recognition carries two boundaries — a necessary boundary that secures its [coherence](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/coherence.md), and a possible boundary that secures its openness. Understanding how these boundaries interact allows one to discern what holds firmly in a relational system from what remains open to further determination.

Directed motion ([Flow](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/flow.md)) must preserve both Must and May — this is the law that [flow preserves the modal core](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flow.md). Directed evolution respects the boundaries of necessity and possibility, never collapsing either.

## Mathematical correspondence

Must corresponds to the necessity modality (□) in S4 modal logic, arising from the closure operator on a Heyting algebra. The S4 axiom (□□p = □p) is earned here from the idempotence of closure.
