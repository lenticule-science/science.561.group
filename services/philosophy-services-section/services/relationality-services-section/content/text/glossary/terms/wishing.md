***Wishing*** is [desiring](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/desiring.md), usually with a [feeling](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/feeling.md) that the [desired thing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/desired-thing.md) is not [attainable](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/attainable.md).

It probably relates to something to do with [agency](agency-psychology.md) and [subjectivation](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/subjectivation.md).
It might also have to do with [aestheticization](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aestheticization.md) of desire as a means of [deferring](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/deferring.md) it.
