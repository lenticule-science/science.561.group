# Relational hyperverse

We conjecture that **our universe is one generation of a relational hyperverse**. A universe is not a final container standing outside relations. It can be an inhabited, internally structured formation, and its relations can become the objects and conditions of another universe.

The proposed tower begins with a relational universe \(R^{(0)}\) and repeats a meta-level universe-closure construction:

\[
R^{(n+1)}=U_{\mathcal H}(R^{(n)}),\qquad
R^{(0)}\longrightarrow R^{(1)}\longrightarrow
R^{(2)}\longrightarrow\cdots .
\]

Its proposed hyperverse is a suitable colimit \(\mathcal H\), with a fixed-point condition \(U_{\mathcal H}(\mathcal H)\simeq\mathcal H\) when the required structure exists. The fixed-point condition describes completion *at that level*, not a boundary that can never again be related.

Two generations must be distinguished. **Internal depth** builds a universe from its admitted marks, histories, relations, valuations, and closures. **Meta-level depth** takes universes themselves as inputs to the next relational construction. The second depth is the hyperverse spiral, not an assertion that the first construction was already performed twice.

The [generative spiral](../concepts/generative-spiral.md) supplies our proposed physical reading: kinematic possibilities, quantum relations, causal spacetime, finite settlement, and residual re-articulation. We conjecture that a settled cosmos \(R^{(n)}\) occupies one level of this larger process. Naming the generation number or a specific cosmological observation requires the generating maps, embeddings, and their empirical readout.

The original [hyperverse tower](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DVu1No9lHAXUP8fG-ANblQyzQrCZTmgW/view) and [relational hyperverse](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nmU20fywblF3YFH00IiaXoP9Cs2AFH3D/view) sources give the proposed sheaf and topos structures; [Obsmetrologia](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia) is the mathematical proof owner. The governing conjecture is not that a named tower makes a cosmos, but that the next generation can be *derived from relations generated in the previous one*.
