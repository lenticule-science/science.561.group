Together is combining: the shared refinement of two [recognitions](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md). If you can distinguish A and distinguish B, then [coherence](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/coherence.md) demands a way to recognize what is both A and B. Together formalizes that demand.

Together arises in [Movement I: Logical Origination](../concepts/movements-logical-origination.md) from the requirement that multiple distinctions be combinable. Once acts of including and excluding have produced recognitions, those recognitions must be able to interact. If A is recognized and B is recognized, the question "what is recognized under both?" must have an answer — otherwise the field of recognitions is incoherent. Together is that answer.

Combining through Together is commutative (combining A with B is the same as combining B with A), associative (combining three recognitions does not depend on the order of combination), and idempotent (combining a recognition with itself produces itself — there is no amplification).

Together participates in the central relationship that gives the logical core its constructive character. The interaction between Together, [Implies](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/implies.md), and [Entails](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/entails.md) is governed by a residuation law: asking whether combining A with B [entails](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/entails.md) C is the same as asking whether A alone entails that B [implies](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/implies.md) C. This connection — between combining, ordering, and conditional relating — is the structural signature of the relational logic.

## Mathematical correspondence

Together corresponds to the meet (greatest lower bound) operation in a lattice. The residuation law connecting Together, Implies, and Entails is what makes the relational algebra a Heyting algebra rather than a mere lattice. In set-theoretic terms, Together is intersection.
