The ***Old English language*** was a [language](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/language.md) used by the [Anglo-Saxon people](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anglo-saxon-people.md) in what is now [England](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/england.md) and [southern Scotland](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/southern-scotland.md) from roughly [500ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/500ce.md) to the [Norman Conquest](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/norman-conquest.md). It's the earliest form of [English language](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/english-language.md).

Its roots are in the [West Germanic](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/west-germanic-languages.md) branch of the [Indo-European languages](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/indo-european-languages.md), [developing](developing.md) from the [Anglo-Frisian language](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anglo-frisian-language.md) of the [Angles](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/angle-people.md), [Saxons](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/other-social-science-services-section/services/ancestry-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/saxon-people.md), and [Jutes](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jute-people.md).

**Old English** was a collection of regional dialects:

- [West Saxon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/west-saxon-language.md) was spoken in [southern England](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/southern-england.md) and became the literary standard.
- [Mercian](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mercian-language.md) was spoken in [central England](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/central-england.md)
- [Northumbrian](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/northumbrian-language.md) was spoken in [northern England](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/northern-england.md) and [southern Scotland](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/southern-scotland.md)
- [Kentish](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kentish-language.md) was spoken in [southeastern England](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/southeastern-england.md)

Originally, **Old English** was written with [Runic script](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/runic-script.md), but transitioned to the [Latin alphabet](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/latin-alphabet.md).

The most famous work in **Old English** is [Beowulf](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/beowulf.md).
