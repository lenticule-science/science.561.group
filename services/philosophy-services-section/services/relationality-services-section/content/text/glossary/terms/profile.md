A **profile** is a self-contained relational subsystem: the recognitions that survive a filter, carrying the full relational structure restricted to them. It is a viewpoint from which the relational field is seen, with everything needed to do relational dynamics inside the viewpoint. Profiles are reached in two steps, each a stronger constraint.

**Discipline.** A discipline is an endomap on the relational field that commutes with every [nucleus](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nucleus.md) and with [flow](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flow.md): a constraint that respects the logic, the closure and the dynamics already in place. Its fixed points, the recognitions a discipline leaves unchanged, inherit the full structure; they are sometimes called the discipline's regime, a sense distinct from the closure [regime](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/regime.md). Disciplines compose, and a composite discipline governs a composite set of fixed points.

**Filter.** A filter is a discipline that also respects the geometry: where the cohesive chain (shape ⊣ discrete ⊣ codiscrete ⊣ global) is in place, it commutes with that too. In the acts derivation a filter is a discipline read through the observational equivalence of flowed observations.

**Profile.** A profile is the set of recognitions fixed by a filter, equivalently the recognitions fixed jointly by flow and nucleus within the filter. Everything in the relational field restricts to it: a profile has its own traces, contexts, terms and judgements, its own semantics and syntax (sound for it), its own flow, its own nucleus, and so its own geometry, and a profile read physically is a [relational physics](relational-physics.md). Profiles carry enough structure to support a typed λ-calculus within themselves.

**Towers.** A profile can be refiltered, nesting one profile inside another; iterating gives a profile tower. Profiles compose through composite disciplines, and a composite profile has its own semantics, flow, nucleus, geometry and physics in turn.

Disciplines, filters and profiles are not yet formalized in Obsmetrologia; they are relationality's account of how structure can be specialized without being destroyed.
