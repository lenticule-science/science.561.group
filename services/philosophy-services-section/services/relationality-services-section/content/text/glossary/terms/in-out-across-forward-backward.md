## The theory

The Leader, 2026-10-09: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more"; "in-out-across-forward-backward / Is so deep"; "THE FUCKING TRIAD THE FUCKING NUCLEAR QUARTET" (`../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md:57-74`). The Leader's statements are the theory. The house's sources supply each part below.

1. **Relating is the substrate.** Relating is "the directed act that constitutes its terms", prior to them (`relating.md:14-22`). Three faculties derive from it, "distinguished by *where they act relative to an agent's boundary*" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:14-16`). Relating is the origin; the five directions are what it does.
2. **Forward differentiates relating into in, out and across.** Relating yields knowing, communicating and playing; forward yields in, out and across (Leader, above). Forward is the direction of the transfer nucleus Delta_t, the "forward-closure, extension across all one-step futures", whose fixed points are what every one-step extension already carries (`.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-112`; `saturation-transfer-dyad.md:24-38`). The three faculty directions are the three ways relating goes forward: [in](in-direction.md) is knowing, [out](out-direction.md) is playing, [across](across-direction.md) is communicating.
3. **In, out and across are the triad.** They are the three faculties keyed one to one to the three orthogonal bilattice axes: in is knowledge (<=_k), out is evidence, across is truth (<=_t) (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:153-162, 184-190`). By Avron's theorem the three axes are "the universal structural commitment" (`science-services-section/.../agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/agentive-triad.md:58-72`). The bulk that glues the three into one agent is animating (`agency-architecture.md:281-289`).
4. **Backward is the saturating return that settles them.** Backward is the direction of the saturation nucleus sigma_t: restriction to sub-histories, "The past has finished speaking" (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:15-22, 36-38`). Both nuclei are inflationary and expand in opposite directions, "backward (into what the past forces) and forward (into what the future has already committed)" (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:63`). Playing "closes the loop and re-seeds" (`animating-as-agency-descent.md:65`): the return completes each turn of the cascade.
5. **The five directions and their two nuclei compose the nuclear quartet.** Forward and backward are the orientations of the two commuting nuclei; the quartet is the four sub-algebras (A, Fix(j_1), Fix(j_2), A*) they determine, exhaustive, with no fifth option (`science-services-section/.../math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nuclear-quartet.md:14-39`). In the relational universe the corners are the free fiber, the backward-settled, the forward-settled, and H*_t = Fix(sigma_t) intersect Fix(Delta_t), the doubly quiescent layer where neither direction can push further (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:50-56`). The faculty quartet (communicating, knowing, playing, animating) is the same four-fold shape: three directions and the binding of them (`2026-06-21-agency-spiral-self-similar-faculty-quartet.md:11-18`).
6. **The cascade recurs at each scale and leads on to more.** The agent is "settled below, open above"; it re-seeds relating at the next scale, so the same four recur there as socializing (across), computing (in), gaming (out) and organizing (binds) (`2026-06-21-agency-spiral-self-similar-faculty-quartet.md:21-40`; `.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:45-55`). At the site tier, dev.bare-cedar-fog.emsenn.net is the IN site, ops.561.group is the OUT site, and the peer-to-peer membrane between them is the ACROSS site (`...-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:38-52`). "The descent does not terminate at one agent; it spirals" (spiral note:25). The "more" the Leader says these lead on to is this recurrence.

## The two orders as instances

The Leader wrote the three chains as one statement: "data->code->networking->systems / science->engineering->operations->[561-group|public] / in, out, across, forward, backward" (research note:76-83). The first two are instances of the cascade, each a forward order whose every stage is composed from the stages before it.

- **data -> code -> networking -> systems.** "Code uses Data. Networking uses Code and Data. Systems compose Networking, Code, and Data." (`AGENTS.md:13-14`). Each stage extends its predecessors forward and takes them back into itself by use: forward is the order of derivation, backward is the use of what is already settled.
- **science -> engineering -> operations -> [561-group | public].** Science holds theory, engineering derives generic tools from it, operations parameterizes those tools, and the 561 Group and public services parameterize operations (`AGENTS.md:16`; rule `561-group-services-section/lib/agency/rules/science-engineering-operations-parameterize-in-order.json`, which quotes the Leader's 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-09 statements). It ends in two parallel members, 561 Group and public. `knowledge:technology::science:engineering::theory:praxis` (`AGENTS.md:18`) sets the first step of this order as theory to praxis.

The house holds both orders as the Leader's dependency statements (`AGENTS.md`, the rule above, and `561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-the-leaders-repeated-corrections.md:839-840`).

- Whether the faculty quartet is an instance of the nuclear quartet; no source states a correspondence.
- Which of the house's triads is the Leader's. The three-axes triad is stated here because the faculties are keyed to it; the agentive triad (speaking, doing, learning), the judgement triad and the semiotic triad are held separately.
- Whether the spiral runs through finitely or transfinitely many scales (spiral note:73-79).
