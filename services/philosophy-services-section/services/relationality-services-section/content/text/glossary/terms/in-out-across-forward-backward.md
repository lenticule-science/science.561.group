The Leader, 2026-10-09: "in-out-across-forward-backward / Is so deep"; "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more"; and, the same day, "in, out, across, forward, backward" after the chains data->code->networking->systems and science->engineering->operations->[561-group|public].

## As held

- **[In](in-direction.md), [out](out-direction.md), [across](across-direction.md)** are where a faculty acts relative to an agent's boundary: knowing inside, playing outside, communicating across. They are keyed to the three bilattice axes (knowledge, evidence, truth). The same triple recurs at a second scale (computing, gaming, socializing) and at the site tier (dev, ops, the federation membrane). The bulk that glues the three is animating at scale 1 and organizing at scale 2.
- **[Forward](forward-direction.md) and [backward](backward-direction.md)** are the two directions of expansion of the two nuclei of the nuclear quartet in the relational universe: transfer, Delta_t, toward one-step futures, and saturation, sigma_t, toward sub-histories. The doubly quiescent layer H*_t is where neither can push further.
- **The nuclear quartet** is the four sub-algebras (A, Fix(j_1), Fix(j_2), A*) of two commuting nuclei (`science-services-section/.../math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nuclear-quartet.md:14-35`).
- **The triad**: the house holds several (truth/knowledge/evidence; speaking/doing/learning; the three faculties; the judgement triad). None is marked as the Leader's.
- **The cascade**: relating is the substrate from which the three faculties derive; the agent re-seeds relating at the next scale, so the same four recur as socializing, computing, gaming, organizing.

## Not held

That forward yields in, out and across; that the five form one set; that the "more" they lead on to is any particular held recurrence; and which triad is meant.
