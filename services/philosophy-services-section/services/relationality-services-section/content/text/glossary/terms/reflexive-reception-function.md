The reflexive reception function (Λ) is induced by the transmission function (T).

When a transmission reaches a stabilized point that mirrors the conditions of its origin, reflexive reception occurs — the system recognizes itself in what has propagated.

Λ(x, y) := T(x → y) ∧ Δ[s](x, y) ∧ Ω(y) = y ∧ M(x, y)

That is, reception becomes reflexive when transmission returns to constraint as difference that affirms its source.
