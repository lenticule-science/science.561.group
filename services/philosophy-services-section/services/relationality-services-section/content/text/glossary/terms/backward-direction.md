**Backward** is the saturating return. The saturation nucleus sigma_t is the "backward-closure, consistency with history" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-111`): the largest element of H_t whose restriction to every proper sub-history is no greater than that of the given element (`science-services-section/.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-nucleus.md:7-12`). The unit's table gives "Sub-histories (the past)" and "The past has finished speaking" (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:15-22, 36-38`).

Forward differentiates relating into [in](in-direction.md), [out](out-direction.md) and [across](across-direction.md); backward returns and settles what forward made. Both nuclei expand, "backward (into what the past forces) and forward (into what the future has already committed)", and H*_t = Fix(sigma_t) intersect Fix(Delta_t) is "the locus where both expansions have already reached their limits" (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:50-63`). With [forward](forward-direction.md) it is the pair of directions of the nuclear quartet. See [the five directions](in-out-across-forward-backward.md).

Backward is also held in these senses.

1. **Restriction.** The restriction maps H(g) go "Backward along g" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/nuclear-functions.md:32`).
2. **Three backward structures.** The logic section lists sigma (a 90-degree axis rotation of the four-valued bilattice), conflation (reflection of the knowledge axis) and cosettling (the pre-image of a closure step, reversing ordinal time) as "structurally-distinct backward direction operations" (`science-services-section/.../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-three-backward-structures-and-their-symmetry.md:11-15`). That sigma is the bilattice rotation, a different object from the saturation nucleus. The note is open.
3. **Backward search.** Regression planning, "regressing from the goal toward the initial state" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/backward-search.md:19`).
4. **Use along the orders.** In data->code->networking->systems, "Code uses Data" (`AGENTS.md:14`): each later stage takes the settled earlier stages back into itself.
