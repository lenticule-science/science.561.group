**Backward** is held in four senses.

1. **Saturation.** The saturation nucleus sigma_t is the "backward-closure, consistency with history" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-111`): the largest element of H_t whose restriction to every proper sub-history is no greater than that of the given element (`.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-nucleus.md:7-12`). The unit's table gives "Sub-histories (the past)" and "The past has finished speaking" (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:15-22, 36-38).
2. **Restriction.** The restriction maps H(g) go "Backward along g" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/nuclear-functions.md:32`).
3. **Three backward structures.** The logic section lists sigma (a 90-degree axis rotation of the four-valued bilattice), conflation (reflection of the knowledge axis) and cosettling (the pre-image of a closure step, reversing ordinal time) as "structurally-distinct backward direction operations"; the note is open (`science-services-section/.../logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-three-backward-structures-and-their-symmetry.md:11-15`). This sigma is not the saturation nucleus.
4. **Backward search.** Regression planning, "regressing from the goal toward the initial state" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/backward-search.md:19`).

## Not held

Backward as a fifth member beside in, out and across is the Leader's statement of 2026-10-09 and is not developed in the corpus. See [the research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
