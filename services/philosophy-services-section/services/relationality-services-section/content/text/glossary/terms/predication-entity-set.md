A set, denoted $E$, whose elements are [entities](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/_archive/teaching/explanations/entity.md). The entity set carries no required structure beyond set-hood in general; specific theorems and applications may impose finiteness, and the entity set may be sorted.

Pairs of entities form the index $E \times E$ over which [predication fiber algebras](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/_archive/predication-fiber-algebra.md) are built; the first and second coordinates of such pairs are the [subject](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/_archive/teaching/explanations/subject.md) and [object](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/_archive/teaching/explanations/object.md) positions of [predication cells](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/_archive/teaching/explanations/predication-cell.md).
