A **relative** is the minimal structure that holds a [relation](relation.md) open durably. It is the first stabilized form within the domain of relating, ensuring that the relation between a thing and its own existing is held and not merely performed.

The relative is where the derivation of [existing](existing.md) crosses from transient acts and conditions into durable structure. Acts bring about conditions, but conditions persist only as long as the acts are active; the relative is the structure that keeps them. With it the minimal architecture of existing closes: the primitive acts of including and excluding, the conditions of inclusion, exclusion, [tension](tension.md) and [difference](difference.md), the act of [relating](relating.md) and the condition of relation, stabilized together.

Every thing that exists is, at minimum, a relative: a stabilized relational unit that maintains difference through tension. It is the first instance of structure.
