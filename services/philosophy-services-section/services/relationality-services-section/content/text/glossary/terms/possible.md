# Possible — relational-inquiry sense

**Possible** names the opening of a *candidate relational continuation* under the premises and relations available to one inquiry. In the original March 28, 2026 philosophical account, something initially [Absurd](absurd.md) becomes **Possible** as it is brought into a more articulated configuration. This does not assert that the candidate actually happened or that all required constraints have been established. [Necessary](necessary.md) concerns the requirements that bind a continuation; [Determination](determination.md) concerns its locally witnessed result.

An inquiry's possible continuation must identify **the given datum, the proposed relation, the context in which it is admitted, and its unmet requirements**. Otherwise “it is possible” supplies no reproducible relational content. These stages are roles in an *iterated epistemic/articulation proposal*, not four mutually exclusive truth values or automatically time-ordered physical events.

## Frontmatter example

A Markdown file presents \`author: someone\`. That presentation can motivate several **possible readings**: a literal metadata key; a claim by the file about a creator; a term resolved under a controlled bibliographic vocabulary; or an unqualified author-name token. Which readings are admitted depends on the declared vocabulary and source authority. The presence of the key does not choose the correct reference, prove authorship or create a publisher's permission. If the predicate or source identity cannot be resolved, report the **open candidate**, not a made-up assertion.

## Do not borrow another world's operator

- [Modal possibility \`◇\`](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/logic-terms-possibility.md) quantifies over accessible worlds in a nominated modal model. It is **not** this inquiry-role named Possible.
- An opportunity, forecast, permission, feasible execution, nonempty FCA incidence and a positive FOUR witness are other questions. Neither “Possible” nor any of these implies the others.
- A possibility can remain **unsupported by independent empirical evidence**; absence of evidence does not make the candidate Absurd, and support does not make it Determined.

This glossary item records the **relational-inquiry sense** of the word, with its source in the March 28 authored personal log and its cautious [Language Science interpretation](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/information-science-services-section/services/language-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-about-l-space.md#march-28-the-utterance-as-a-locally-witnessed-relation-source-recovered-2026-10-14). Do not introduce an unproved theorem that all candidates pass through this stage or that the word is synonymous with an existing modal operation.
