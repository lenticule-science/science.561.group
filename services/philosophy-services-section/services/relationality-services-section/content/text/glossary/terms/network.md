A **network** is what [chains](chain.md) become when they intersect and branch: relational structures connected in patterns that no single ordered sequence can hold.

- A **node** is a relational structure together with its [relational depth](relational-depth.md).
- An **edge** between two nodes is the [tension](tension.md) of the two structures after each is aligned to the greater of their depths. The tension's order is the edge's direction.
- A **network** is a finite collection of nodes and edges.

## Properties

- **Closed under relating.** A network is a relational structure. Deepening it as a whole holds the entire network within itself one depth deeper ([reflexive sequencing](reflexive-sequencing.md)).
- **Merging.** Two networks merge into one by taking the union of their nodes and of their edges.
- **Reduction to chains.** A network whose edges form a single path is a chain.
- **Propagation.** A change at one node or edge reaches every structure connected to it, so a local change runs through the network until the whole is coherent again.

Deepening preserves which nodes are connected by which edges; only depth changes ([reflexive equilibrium](reflexive-equilibrium.md)).
