***inciting*** is an [act](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) where a [complex system](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/complex-system.md) causes a [subsystem](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/subsystem.md) to act.

I use the term in a way influenced by [Michel Foucault](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/michel-foucault.md)'s [l'incitation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/l-incitation.md), but to refer to the more general [relational dynamic](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relational-dynamic.md) represented.
