**In** (also inside, inward) is the direction of the **knowing** faculty: the faculty "inside: transforms the agent's interior state (reflection)", and "does work *inside* the informed thing" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:21, 25`). The engineering document gives it as the `direction: "inside"`, `axis: knowledge` row of the faculty table, with a comonadic reading algebra (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 186-187`). At the second scale, the in-direction is `computing`; at the site tier, the IN site is the reflective dev instance (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:47`; `...-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:42`).

## A second held use

The relational-hyperverse unit says "Saturation looks inward to the history already accumulated" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:48`). The corpus does not state that this inward and the knowing faculty's inside are the same relation.

## Not held

That in is derived from forward is the Leader's statement of 2026-10-09 and is not developed in the corpus. See [the research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
