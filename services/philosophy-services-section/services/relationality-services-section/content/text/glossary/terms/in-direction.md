**In** (also inside, inward) is the direction of the **knowing** faculty: the faculty "inside: transforms the agent's interior state (reflection)", which "does work *inside* the informed thing" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:21, 25`).

1. **Forward yields it.** The Leader: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more". Relating going forward yields knowing, and in is the direction knowing takes. See [the five directions](in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
2. **It is the knowledge axis.** The engineering document gives the row `direction: "inside"`, `axis: knowledge`, order <=_k, with a comonadic reading algebra (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 186-187`). It is one of the three members of the triad.
3. **It recurs.** At scale 2 in is `computing`, cognition across many agents; at the site tier the IN site is the reflective dev instance (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:47`; `...-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:42`).
4. **Backward settles it.** The saturation nucleus is the backward return, and the hyperverse unit says "Saturation looks inward to the history already accumulated" (`science-services-section/.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:48`). Saturation settles what the accumulated past forces, which is the work of knowing inside.
