# Reflexivity

**Reflexivity is the ability of relating to relate to its own activity.** An act generates a distinction; that distinction can enter another act as a term; the process does not need a foundation standing outside all relating.

The [relationlessness paradox](relationlessness.md) is its originating case. Exclusion tries to determine the absence of relating, but the exclusion itself is an oriented act. What has been articulated can itself be considered, challenged, composed, or excluded in a further turn.

Reflexivity is not the same as **symmetry**. A directed relation from subject to object need not also hold in the opposite direction. Nor does a relation becoming the object of another relation erase the distinction between the earlier and later acts.

## From reflexivity to settlement

A field can recognize and revisit its own judgments. Closure gives a settled state under a particular rule family; reflexivity gives that closure a place within the next inquiry. Thus the result of one cycle may be complete **as a settlement** without exhausting the possibility of further relation.

We conjecture that this is the general pattern behind the [generative spiral](../concepts/generative-spiral.md): reflexive re-entry of the unabsorbed [residue](../concepts/residuation.md) after a finite turn becomes the next kinematic field.

The formal work proceeds through typed predications, trace and witnessing operations, closure, and two-dimensional coherence. A recursion on an already chosen data type models one instance of reflexivity; it does not automatically derive the type's own origin.
