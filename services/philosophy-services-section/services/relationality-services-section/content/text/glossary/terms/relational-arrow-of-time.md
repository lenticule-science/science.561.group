# Relational arrow of time

**The arrow of time is a relational structure to be generated, not an external clock with which the first act can be measured.** The original [relationlessness paradox](relationlessness.md) produces directed distinction: excluding and excluded have different roles. Relating then deposits distinguishable results in a trace, and later acts can take those results as material.

We conjecture that **this directional accumulation and noninvertible re-articulation constitute a deeper arrow of time**. One settled formation can be carried forward without requiring the next formation to be recoverable by simply undoing its relations. A history and the constraints by which it was admitted give temporal orientation its meaning.

This claim is distinct from two other arrows:

- **Causal order** states which events or acts may precede and influence others. A causal set expresses an order once the events and reachability relation have been constructed.
- **Thermodynamic irreversibility** concerns entropy production and coarse-graining under actual physical dynamics. It is a possible downstream manifestation of the relational arrow, not its definition.

In a [Turn](../concepts/turn.md), a completed settlement can become material for the next Turn's articulation. That directed re-entry is the proposed deeper source of temporal orientation.

The mathematical bridge will construct a partial order or time-orientation from admitted relational transitions, transport it through quantum and causal readings, and derive appropriate entropy observables for the thermal regime. The [physics terminology](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/natural-science-services-section/services/physical-science-services-section/services/physics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thermal-spacetime.md) keeps the thermodynamic meaning separate.
