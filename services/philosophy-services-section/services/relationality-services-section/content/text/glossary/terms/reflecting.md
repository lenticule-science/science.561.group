**Reflecting** is the act of sustaining reflexivity over time. It holds the reflexive relation $\mathrm{relating}(S, S)$ open durably, so that the reflexive operation stays stable within the ongoing dynamics of the system.

Reflexing and reflecting are distinct in the same act/sustained pattern that runs through relationality:

- **Reflexing** is the instantaneous act of turning an act back on itself, $\mathrm{reflex}(S) = \mathrm{relating}(S, S)$: one self-application.
- **Reflecting** is the ongoing maintenance of reflexivity, sustaining the reflexive relation over time so that it can accumulate.
- **Reflexivity** is the condition that results, as inclusion results from including and exclusion from excluding.

Reflexive sequences, $R(0) = S$ and $R(n+1) = \mathrm{reflect}(R(n))$, need each step's reflexivity kept rather than performed once and forgotten; without reflecting, single self-applications do not add up to a sequence. In the formal framework reflexing is one application of the reflex endomap and reflecting is its iteration, whose limit is a closure ([articulating](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/articulating.md), [closure](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure.md)).
