Meta-Iterate is systemic self-renewal: the act of applying a [system](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/make-system.md) to itself, transforming the triad as a whole. Three applications return the system to its starting configuration — the system grows through self-application but does not depart from itself.

Meta-Iterate arises in [Movement V: Emergent Containment](../concepts/movements-emergent-containment.md) as the system-level counterpart of [Iterate](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure.md). Where Iterate applies an operation to its own output on individual [recognitions](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md), Meta-Iterate applies the entire triadic system to itself. The result is not an accumulation of changes but a cycle of renewal: after three steps, the system returns to where it started.

This triple-return law parallels [Meta-Rotate](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/meta-rotate.md)'s cyclic behavior and reflects the deeper pattern that triadic structure in relationality is governed by three-fold symmetry at every level — from the cubic return of object-level iteration to the cyclic exchange of system-level roles.
