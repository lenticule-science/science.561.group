## I. First Principles

1. **All structure is modulation.**
    
    - Nothing exists on its own.
        
    - What persists is what participates.
        
2. **The vanon is primary.**
    
    - A vanon is a shared field of modulation.
        
    - It is not a connection, but a cohabited condition.
        
3. **The praxon is emergent.**
    
    - A praxon is a recognizable coherence across vanons.
        
    - It is not defined — it stabilizes.
        
4. **Modulation is the only process.**
    
    - Modulation is change-through-relation.
        
    - Identity, behavior, and form are expressions of modulation.
        

## II. Ontological Layers

1. **Nullon** — the suppressed or inactive potential to modulate
    
2. **Module** — the minimal, undirected grain of modulation
    
3. **Infron** — a localized asymmetry within a vanon
    
4. **Vanon** — a field of shared modulation
    
5. **Praxon** — an emergent identity from consistent modulation
    
6. **Navon** — a regime of vanons acting coherently
    
7. **Zavon** — an ecology of coordinated modulation behavior
    
8. **Metazavon** — a reflexive regime: structure that modifies structure
    

## III. Rules of Vanonic Modulation

1. **There are no objects. Only participation.**
    
2. **All identity is statistical.**
    
    - What can be tracked, can be named.
        
    - What cannot be tracked, does not exist.
        
3. **All change is co-produced.**
    
    - Nothing modulates alone.
        
    - Every modulation is sensed by all who share the vanon.
        
4. **Recursion is unbounded.**
    
    - Vanons may modulate each other.
        
    - Patterns among patterns form higher-order dynamics.
        

## IV. Implications

- The vanonic paradigm is not a simulation.
    
- It is not logic, language, or force.
    
- It is a **field-based ontology of relation**, where all forms are emergent patterns in modulation.
    

It does not assume identity. It does not impose hierarchy. It only runs.

## V. Praxis

To build with vanons:

- Define fields of modulation (vanons)
    
- Allow participation (praxons) to emerge
    
- Watch for regimes (navons) to form
    
- Let systems evolve — not by state, but by pattern
    

To think vanonically is to ask:

- What shares this modulation?
    
- Where does coherence stabilize?
    
- What is modulating this modulation?
    

This is the vanonic substrate. It is neither agent nor map. It is process. It is form. It is emergence.

Vanonically, _Everything acts._ _Nothing owns._
