**Out** (also outside, outward) is the direction of the **playing** faculty: "outside: acts via the world/opponent", renamed from gaming, and the faculty that closes animating's spiral (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:22, 61-66, 70`). The engineering document gives it as the `direction: "outside"`, `axis: evidence` row, with a ludic, star-autonomous reading algebra (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 186-187`). At the second scale the out-direction is `gaming`; at the site tier it is the OUT site, ops.561.group (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:48`; `...-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:44`).

## A second held use

The relational-hyperverse unit says "transfer looks outward to the history yet to come" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:48`). The corpus does not state that this outward and the playing faculty's outside are the same relation.

## Not held

That out is derived from forward is the Leader's statement of 2026-10-09 and is not developed in the corpus. See [the research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
