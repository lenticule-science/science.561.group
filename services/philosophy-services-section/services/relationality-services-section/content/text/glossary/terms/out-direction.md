**Out** (also outside, outward) is the direction of the **playing** faculty: "outside: acts via the world/opponent", renamed from gaming, the faculty that closes animating's spiral and re-seeds (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:22, 61-66, 70`).

1. **Forward yields it.** The Leader: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more". Relating going forward yields playing, and out is the direction playing takes. See [the five directions](in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
2. **It is the evidence axis.** The engineering document gives the row `direction: "outside"`, `axis: evidence`, the pair <a+, a->, with a ludic, star-autonomous reading algebra (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 186-187`). It is one of the three members of the triad.
3. **It recurs.** At scale 2 out is `gaming`, many agents playing concurrently; at the site tier it is the OUT site, ops.561.group (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:48`; `...-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:44`).
4. **It faces the forward nucleus.** The transfer nucleus, the forward nucleus, "looks outward to the history yet to come" (`science-services-section/.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:48`). Playing acts into the world where the next step is taken, and closes the loop that begins the next turn.
