Flow-Comult is propagation: the capacity of a directed process to be viewed as containing further directed processes within it. A flow that flows further does not expand — it converges. Flow-Comult tracks the layering of directed evolution without amplifying it, ensuring that the structure remains self-consistent at every depth.

Flow-Comult arises in [Movement III: Directed Dynamics](../concepts/directed-dynamics.md) as one of the two structural components of [Flow](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/flow.md). Together with [Flow-Counit](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/flow-counit.md) (resolution), it gives Flow its convergent character. Under the [KZ-Lax](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/kz-lax.md) condition, propagation does not produce something larger — the layered flow is contained within the original flow. This makes Flow-Comult a structural tracking device rather than an amplifier.

## Mathematical correspondence

Flow-Comult corresponds to the comultiplication of a comonad — the natural transformation from the comonad to its square. In lax-idempotent (KZ) comonads, the comultiplication is contractive: the square is contained in the original.
