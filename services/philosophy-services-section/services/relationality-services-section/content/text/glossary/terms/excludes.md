Excludes expresses incompatibility between two [recognitions](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md). Two recognitions exclude each other when they share no common refinement — what is recognized under one cannot simultaneously be recognized under the other.

Excludes arises in [Movement I: Logical Origination](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/logical-origination.md) as the complement of inclusion. [Distinguishing](../concepts/differentiation.md) produces not only what is included but also what is excluded — the two dynamics are inseparable. To recognize something as A is simultaneously to exclude it from not-A. Excludes formalizes this inseparability.

Two recognitions exclude each other precisely when one [entails](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/entails.md) the [negation](negate.md) of the other. Equivalently, their [combining](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/together.md) yields [Bottom](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/bottom.md) — the empty recognition. These are not two separate conditions but two ways of saying the same thing, reflecting the internal consistency of the logical core.
