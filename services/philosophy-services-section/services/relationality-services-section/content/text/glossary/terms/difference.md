**Difference** is the minimal condition that marks a thing as set apart from what it is not. It is induced by [tension](tension.md) and is the coherent separation that lets a thing be held open as distinct within the domain of relating.

Difference does not arise from including or excluding alone. Including brings about inclusion, drawing the thing into what is; excluding brings about exclusion, holding it out of what is not; neither alone marks the thing as distinct. Both must be held together, and holding them together is tension, which induces difference. So difference is not the starting point of the derivation of [existing](existing.md) but a product of it: the derivation does not assume distinction, it derives it.

To assert that something exists is to assert differentiated existence: for any $x$, $x$ is not not-$x$. Without difference nothing can be marked out as distinct and everything collapses into the undifferentiated; without tension no difference can be held open. Difference precedes [relating](relating.md), which sustains the tension for difference's sake, and it is stabilized by the [relative](relative.md).
