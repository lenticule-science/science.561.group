**The relational derivation** is relationality's central argument. It begins from a single claim, that something exists, and shows that each structure's coherence leaves something undetermined, and that the undetermined remainder compels the next structure. It starts with the [derivation of existing](existing.md).

## The engine

Each step follows a **triadic engine**. Given a structure whose coherence has been established:

1. its persistence "cannot not derive" a *dynamic of determination*: an **act**;
2. the act, determined by how it engages what is around it, cannot not derive a *dynamic of relation*: a **condition**;
3. the condition, determined by how it structures what it holds, cannot not derive a *dynamic of structure*: a **structure**;
4. act, condition and structure together give the minimal determination for the new unit's coherence, "but leave unresolved dynamics which must be derived", and that remainder starts the next step.

**Incitement** is that remainder's force: incomplete structure compels further determination, not as an outside requirement but as the gap itself demanding resolution. Formally it is the gap between the current configuration and the next application of the generative [closure](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md): while the configuration is not yet a fixed point, the closure has more to do. Its complement is **induction**, what a completed structure produces. **Predictive determination** makes incitement a procedure: take a domain's acts, conditions, structures and closure; identify the dynamics its closure leaves implicit; classify and formalize them; and check that the next domain is of the same type. Each stabilized structure anticipates and necessitates its own unfolding into a higher-order form.

## Phases and movements

The argument was first written as nine phases, each earning a kind of coherence: existential, relational, sustained relational dynamics, bounded relational coherence, reflexive relational coherence, multirelational, relational field, relational meta-boundary and meta-relational coherence. It was then organized as five **movements**:

| Movement | Phases | What it earns | Where it is now |
|---|---|---|---|
| I, Logical Origination | 1–2 | the Heyting algebra of recognitions: order, meet and join, implication, complement, iteration | [positing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/positing.md)'s value algebra |
| II, Structural Stabilization | 3–4 | modal closure: closure and interior, balance, necessity and possibility | [settling](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settling.md) |
| III, Directed Dynamics | 5 | flow and its stabilization | [flow](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flow.md) |
| IV, Geometric Cohesion | 6–7 | local and global structure, the cohesive chain | [cohesion](cohesion.md), [profile](profile.md) |
| V, Emergent Containment | 8–9 | states, observables, conservation, self-containment | [relational physics](relational-physics.md) |

The movements' own arguments are [I](../concepts/movements-logical-origination.md), [II](../concepts/movements-structural-stabilization.md), [III](../concepts/movements-directed-dynamics.md), [IV](../concepts/movements-geometric-cohesion.md) and [V](../concepts/movements-emergent-containment.md).

## The acts

The derivation was also formalized as a generative system of numbered **acts**. Each act is an ordered pair of earlier acts, one witnessing the other, beginning from [relationlessness](relationlessness.md): exclusion, articulation, the reflex, and on. The acts fall into strata, and the strata run in the order of the language spine:

| Strata | Stage | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| foundation, trace | articulating | [mark](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mark.md), [utterance](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/utterance.md), [articulating](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/articulating.md), [articulation cell](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/articulation-cell.md), [witness](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/witness.md), [trace](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/trace.md) |
| term, judgement | predicating | [predication](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/predication.md), [λ-calculus](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lambda-calculus.md), [typing judgment](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/typing-judgment.md) |
| observation, semantics | positing | [positing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/positing.md), [evidence](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/evidence.md) |
| stability, geometry | settling | [settling](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settling.md), [nucleus](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nucleus.md), [flow](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flow.md) |
| profile, physics, dynamics | the models | [profile](profile.md), [relational physics](relational-physics.md), [cohesion](cohesion.md) |

Each stage is formalized in Obsmetrologia, in the order articulate → predicate → posit → settle, and each has its own [regime](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/regime.md). The models are partly formalized (settling as gradient flow of a free energy, the ledger's Noether pair) and partly not yet (disciplines, filters, profiles, cohesion).
