Relationality has historically been organized into five phases, each earning a kind of relational coherence whose incompleteness incites the next.

## The five movements

| Movement | Draft 12 Name | PRE Part | What it earns |
|----------|--------------|----------|---------------|
| I | Logical Origination | Logic | Complete Heyting algebra (Differentiate → Includes/Excludes → Together/Either/Negate → Induces → Iterate) |
| II | Structural Stabilization | Structure | Modal closure (Close/Open → Balance → Necessary/Possible) |
| III | Directed Dynamics | Arithmetic | Comonadic flow (Flow → CoKleisli → stabilization) |
| IV | Geometric Cohesion | Geometry | Cohesive structure (Shape ⊣ Discrete ⊣ Codiscrete ⊣ Global → stratification) |
| V | Emergent Containment | Physics | Self-contained modality (states → observables → conservation → self-containment) |

## Mapping to draft 1's 9 phases

- Movement I ← Phases 1–2 (Existential Coherence, Relational Coherence)
- Movement II ← Phases 3–4 (Sustained Relational Dynamics, Bounded Relational Coherence)
- Movement III ← Phase 5 (Reflexive Relational Coherence)
- Movement IV ← Phases 6–7 (Multirelational Coherence, Relational Field Coherence)
- Movement V ← Phases 8–9 (Relational Meta-Boundary Coherence, Meta-relational Coherence)

## Mapping to Foundations' 8 phases

- Movement I ← Phases I–II (Existential Differentiation, Relational Determination)
- Movement II ← Phase III (Reflexive Stability)
- Movement III ← Phases IV–V (Modal Induction, Comonadic Dynamics)
- Movement IV ← Phase VI (Geometric Reflection)
- Movement V ← Phases VII–VIII (Reflexive Physics, Reflexive Quantization)
