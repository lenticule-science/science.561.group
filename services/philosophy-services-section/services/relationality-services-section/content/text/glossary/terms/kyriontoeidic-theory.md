**Kyriontoeidic theory/** is the [theory](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/theory.md) of [kyriontoeidic analysis](kyriontoeidic-analysis.md).

It's [theoretical lineage](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/theoretical-lineage.md) is in [post-structuralist theory](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/post-structuralist-theory.md) and [genre theory](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genre-theory.md) (among others, probably).

It was [created](creating.md) by [emsenn](../../triage/emsenn/index.org.md) as a way of discussing the [commodity](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/services/accounting-services-section/services/gnucash-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commodity.md) that appears to be produced by certain [capitalist labor processes](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalist-labor-process.md) related to [neurotic liberalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/neurotic-liberalism.md), [magical nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/magical-nominalism.md), and [californication](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/triage/exploring-californication/californication.org.md).

It's a part of my broader thinking on how [formalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/formalism.md) has shaped the world:

A [kyriontoeidon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kyriontoeidon.md) forms when [anticipation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anticipation.md) ([incited](inciting.md) by [linear temporality](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/linear-temporality.md)) modifies an [ontoeidon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontoeidon.md), an [eidon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/eidon.md) formed by [ontologizing](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ontologizing.md).

I'm having a hard time describing it, but it's something to do with how the idea of things should be a certain way comes to be, because linear time gives us expectations, proto-possession, etc.
