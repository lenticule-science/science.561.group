# Twistoring

**Twistoring** is our name for a candidate geometrical reading of the two-sided structure of relating. The research brings three precise constructions into contact:

1. **FOUR twistoring.** Negation and conflation are commuting involutions on a four-valued carrier. Their square has a Klein-four structure, and Obsmetrologia compares it with a commuting square of nuclei at a later level of the derivation.
2. **Void-cone twistor.** An earlier bilattice vocabulary uses *twistor* for saturation followed by transfer. Its remaining path or concept-sequence data motivates re-articulation.
3. **Penrose twistors.** In twistor theory, a twistor \(Z=(\omega^A,\pi_{A'})\) is incident with a spacetime point \(x\) through \(\omega^A=i x^{AA'}\pi_{A'}\) (up to convention). A spacetime point corresponds to a projective twistor line, and the intersections of those lines carry null-separation information.

These are **three defined objects**. We conjecture that a suitable realization maps the relational two-sidedness of the first two into the incidence structure of the third, preserving orientation and the information a residual carries. We do not define such a realization merely by renaming the operators.

## From subject–predicate–object to space

A predication has directed subject and object positions. One candidate realization assigns its relational data to a \(2\times 2\) matrix. A symmetric matrix over a field has three independent entries; appropriate real Hermitian/spinorial matrix geometry additionally supplies a determinant and light-cone structure. The proposed spatial reading must specify which symmetry, coefficient field, reality condition, and incidence map turn a predication into such geometry.

We conjecture that this is part of the same [residuation](../concepts/residuation.md) and [KZ](kz-lax.md) construction. The noncommuting left and right conditions of a relation become geometric information that can matter after a finite settlement and through the next [generative turn](../concepts/generative-spiral.md).

Penrose's spacetime–twistor correspondence is mathematically established in its own setting; our proposed transport from FOUR and predication into it is the work of this research program.
