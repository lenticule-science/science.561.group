Stabilizes-Between is the condition for self-persistence between dual poles. It names when something can persist as itself rather than collapsing to one extreme or the other — bounded by [closure](close.md) above and interior ([Open](open.md)) below, with these bounds compatible.

Stabilizes-Between arises in [Movement II: Structural Stabilization](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/structural-stabilization.md) as the full condition on [Include](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/include.md). Where [Balance](../concepts/balance.md) ensures that [Close](close.md) and [Open](open.md) do not interfere with each other, Stabilizes-Between ensures that the mediating identity (Include) sits coherently between them. A [recognition](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md), taken as it is, occupies a stable position: its interior lies below it, its closure lies above it, and these bounds are compatible.

This condition presupposes Balance — without the compatibility of the dual pair, there is no coherent midpoint to occupy. It recurs at every level of the derivation wherever triadic structure appears: at the stabilization layer (Open, Include, Close), the modal layer ([May](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/may.md), [Identity](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/identity.md), [Must](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/must.md)), and the meta level.
