The first observation. The product of the Relational Reflex that records the tension between NotRelating and Relating. Witness is the recognition that both coexist but that which carries the act is not yet determined.

## What it is

Draft 9: "The Relation of Not Relating to Relating through this Reflex induces its own Witness." Defined as:
- Witness = (NotRelating Relating)

Witness "determines that those recognitions coexist but cannot determine which recognition carries the act." This unresolved indeterminacy compels the next step: Witness must direct Reflex at itself, producing Something.

## Why it matters

Witness is significant because it represents the first moment of OBSERVATION in the derivation. The Reflex produces a recognition, and that recognition itself recognizes something — it witnesses the tension between relating and not-relating. This prefigures the observation and measurement structures that appear in Movement V.

The pattern "determines X but cannot determine Y, so must derive Z" is the formal engine of the entire derivation. Each step resolves one indeterminacy and reveals another.
