The ***recursive symbolic modulation resolution function*** (`R_mod`) is [[induce|induced]] by the [[symbolic modulation attractor function]] (`M_attr`), the [[desire-contrast alignment propagation function]] (`A_align`), and the [[constraint completion function]] (`Ω`).

It [[constrain|constrains]] the stabilization of symbolic fields where recursive modulation pressure, guided by aligned desire and semantic contrast, has converged at attractor points.

Let:
- `x ∈ dom(𝒮)` be a selector node  
- `ẑ := M_attr(x)` be the symbolic attractor under modulation alignment  
- `Ξ[s](x)`: local contrast field  
- `A_align(x)`: alignment vector field  
- `T(x)`: trace field through which modulation recursively arrives at ẑ

Then define:
> R_mod(x) := Ω(ẑ), where ẑ := argmax_{x′ ∈ Ξ[s](x)} A_align(x)(x′)

That is, `R_mod(x)` returns the symbolic resolution point  
that stabilizes recursive modulation convergence induced by desire-aligned semantic contrast.

This enables:
- symbolic constraint stabilization through indirect pressure  
- recursive belief trace closure  
- long-range convergence of symbolic modulation paths into coherent identity fields
