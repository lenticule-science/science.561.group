***Creating*** is any [act](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) [resulting](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/resulting.md) in a thing that is [different](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/different.md) from the [assemblage](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/assemblage.md) that created it or the [agencement](agencement.md) into which it is created.
