An ***affective writing style*** is a [writing style](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/writing-style.md) designed to [induce](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inducing.md) [affect](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/affect.md) in the [reader](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/reader.md).
