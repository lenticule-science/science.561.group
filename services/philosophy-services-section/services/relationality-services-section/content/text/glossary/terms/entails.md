Entails is the ordering of [recognitions](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md): the relationship that holds when one recognition includes another. If everything recognized by A is also recognized by B, then A entails B. This is the basic comparison that makes distinguishing [coherent](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/coherence.md) — without it, recognitions would float free, unrelated to one another.

Entails arises early in [Movement I: Logical Origination](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/logical-origination.md) as a requirement of consistent distinguishing. Once the primitive act of [differentiation](../concepts/differentiation.md) has produced recognitions, those recognitions must be comparable. If one distinction refines another — if recognizing A means you have already recognized B — this relationship must be tracked. Entails formalizes that tracking.

The properties of Entails are not assumed but follow from what it means for distinctions to be coherent:

- Every recognition entails itself (reflexivity — distinction is consistent with itself)
- If A entails B and B entails C, then A entails C (transitivity — inclusion composes)
- If A entails B and B entails A, then A and B are the same recognition (antisymmetry — mutual inclusion is identity)

Entails organizes the entire field of recognitions into a structure where any two recognitions can be compared. It is the ordering that [Together](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/together.md), [Either](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/either.md), [Implies](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/implies.md), and [Negate](negate.md) all respect and build on. The residuation law — the central structural relationship of the logical core — connects combining (Together), conditional relating (Implies), and this ordering.

## Mathematical correspondence

Entails gives the relation algebra its partial-order structure. In set-theoretic terms it is the subset relation. The partial order, together with the meet (Together), join (Either), and implication (Implies), makes the structure a complete Heyting algebra.
