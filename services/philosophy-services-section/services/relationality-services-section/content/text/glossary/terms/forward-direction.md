**Forward** is the direction in which relating goes on. The Leader: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more". Forward differentiates relating into the three faculty directions [in](in-direction.md), [out](out-direction.md) and [across](across-direction.md), and those lead on to more at the next scale. See [the five directions](in-out-across-forward-backward.md).

Forward is held in three senses, which the theory composes.

1. **Transfer.** The transfer nucleus Delta_t is the "forward-closure, extension across all one-step futures" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-112`). Its fixed points are the propositions already present, as restrictions, in every one-step extension; the table names its direction "Extensions (the future)" and says "The future has already sent its information back" (`science-services-section/.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:24-38`, `transfer-nucleus.md:7-15`). The unit names the two directions: "backward (into what the past forces) and forward (into what the future has already committed)" (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:63`). Delta_t is inflationary and is the forward half of the nuclear quartet. The older name "continuation" was dropped because Delta_t acts by pulling information back from extensions (`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:75`).
2. **Trajectory.** The forward trajectory of a closure rule system is the sequence v_0, v_1, ..., v_kappa; it is contained in the iterated cosettling, and the inclusion is generally strict (`science-services-section/.../logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory.md:11-21`).
3. **Forward search.** Planning "progressing from the initial state toward a goal state" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/forward-search.md:19`).

Relating is the substrate and the three boundary faculties derive from it (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:14-16`). The orders data->code->networking->systems and science->engineering->operations->[561-group|public] are forward orders (`AGENTS.md:13-16`).
