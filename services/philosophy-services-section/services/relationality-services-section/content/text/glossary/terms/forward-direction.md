**Forward** is held in three senses.

1. **Transfer.** The transfer nucleus Delta_t is the "forward-closure, extension across all one-step futures" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-112`). Its fixed points are the propositions already present, as restrictions, in every one-step extension; the table names its direction "Extensions (the future)" and says "The future has already sent its information back" (`.../relational-hyperverse-repository/math/saturation-transfer-dyad.md:24-38`, `transfer-nucleus.md:7-15`). The same unit says the two nuclei are "two directions of expansion: backward ... and forward (into what the future has already committed)" (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:63), and that the older name "continuation" was dropped because it suggested moving forward in time when Delta_t pulls information back from extensions (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:75).
2. **Trajectory.** The forward trajectory of a closure rule system is the sequence v_0, v_1, ..., v_kappa; it is contained in the iterated cosettling, and the inclusion is generally strict (`science-services-section/.../logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory.md:11-21`).
3. **Forward search.** Planning "progressing from the initial state toward a goal state" (`science-services-section/.../relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/forward-search.md:19`).

Where the work-science terms use Delta_t they read it as execution-closure, not future (`science-services-section/.../work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/procedure.md:67`); the corpus does not say the two readings are one.

## Not held

The Leader's "forward->in,out,across" says forward yields the three faculty directions. No source states it. See [the research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md), sections 5 to 7.
