**Reflexive sequencing** is [relating](relating.md) turned on what relating has already produced. Relating is the one act, and nothing in the relational field is exempt from it, including a structure's relation to itself.

## The reflex

The **reflex** of a relational structure $S$ is relating applied to $S$ and $S$:

$$\mathrm{reflex}(S) := \mathrm{relating}(S, S).$$

It is a new structure that holds $S$ within itself, one [relational depth](relational-depth.md) deeper. It adds no new act: it is relating, applied where relating already is.

## The reflexive sequence

Iterating the reflex from a starting structure gives the reflexive sequence:

$$R(0) := S, \qquad R(n+1) := \mathrm{reflex}(R(n)) = \mathrm{relating}(R(n), R(n)).$$

Each $R(n+1)$ embeds $R(n)$ with its content and order intact. The sequence is open-ended: every structure in it can be deepened again.

## Type-homogeneity

Relating keeps one ontological type at every depth. Relating two primitive structures and relating two whole structures built from them are the same act on things of the same kind. Three consequences follow:

- No structure is lifted into a higher type by being related, so the sequence never needs a new sort of act to continue.
- Self-reference does not produce a stratification of types, which is how the relational field avoids Russell-style paradox without a type hierarchy.
- The reflex is always available. A structure that could not relate to itself would be a structure outside the relational field.

## Everything deepens

Every relational structure has a reflex, not only the primitive ones. The reflex of a [tension](tension.md) is a tension at the next depth that keeps its two structures in the same order. The reflex of a [chain](chain.md) is a chain at the next depth that keeps its components and their order. Deepened structures can be composed and nested again ([composition](composition.md)), so depth and breadth grow together.

Structures that behave the same under reflexive deepening form reflexive equivalence classes. Two structures at different depths are compared by aligning them first ([relational depth](relational-depth.md)).
