The trace site (T, J) is a small [category](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/categorical-services-section/services/ordinary-categories-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/category.md) T equipped with a [Grothendieck topology](./grothendieck-topology.md) J that indexes [traces](./trace.md) and their refinements in the [GFRTU](../../documents/articles/notes/generative-fibered-recognition-trace-universe.md). Objects of T are traces; [morphisms](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/categorical-services-section/services/ordinary-categories-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) are refinement maps (a [morphism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/categorical-services-section/services/ordinary-categories-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) t → t' means t' refines or extends t). The topology J specifies which families of refinements count as [covers](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/topology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cover.md).

The trace site is one of the three primitive inputs to the GFRTU construction (alongside the [recognition fibers](./recognition-fiber.md) and their dynamics). The choice of trace site determines the granularity and structure of the universe: different trace sites produce different GFRTUs, just as different [topological spaces](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/topology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/topological-space.md) produce different sheaf topoi.

The category of [sheaves](../../documents/articles/notes/sheaf.md) on the trace site, Sh(T, J), is the [sheaf universe](./sheaf-universe.md) — a Grothendieck topos where recognitions assemble as sheaves. The three closure principles of the GFRTU (sheaf completion, fiber stabilization, generative closure) all operate relative to the trace site.
