**Across** is the direction of the **communicating** faculty: "across the boundary: meme in / meme out. Endomorphic on the meme carrier; transports/transforms meaning", the faculty that "provides information" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:18-19, 25`). The engineering document gives it as the `direction: "across"`, `axis: truth` row, a meme-endomorphism / negate reading, whose face "pools same-subject memes with joinKnowledge" so that an assertion and its denial land at the top value, a held contradiction (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 165`). At the second scale the across-direction is `socializing`. At the site tier, "the ACROSS site = the peer-to-peer membrane between" the IN and OUT sites, which the note found missing and identified with the WMAP server-to-server federation (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:46-52`).

## Not held

That across is derived from forward is the Leader's statement of 2026-10-09 and is not developed in the corpus. See [the research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-research-on-in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
