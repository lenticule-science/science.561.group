**Across** is the direction of the **communicating** faculty: "across the boundary: meme in / meme out. Endomorphic on the meme carrier; transports/transforms meaning", the faculty that "provides information" (`science-services-section/.../computer-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:18-19, 25`).

1. **Forward yields it.** The Leader: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing / foward->in,out,across / and then those lead on to more". Relating going forward yields communicating, and across is the direction communicating takes. See [the five directions](in-out-across-forward-backward.md).
2. **It is the truth axis.** The engineering document gives the row `direction: "across"`, `axis: truth`, order <=_t, a meme-endomorphism / negate reading whose face "pools same-subject memes with joinKnowledge", so an assertion and its denial land at the top value, a held contradiction (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:157-162, 165`). It is one of the three members of the triad.
3. **It recurs.** At scale 2 across is `socializing`. At the site tier "the ACROSS site = the peer-to-peer membrane between" the IN and OUT sites, which is the WMAP server-to-server federation (`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:46-52`).
4. **It joins the other two.** The three faces are pieces of a three-cover whose pairwise overlaps are the edges where local sections must agree, and the bulk, animating, is the gluing of the three (`engineering-services-section/.../agent-organization-engine/lib/relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:264-289`).
