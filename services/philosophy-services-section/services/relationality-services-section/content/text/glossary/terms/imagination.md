***Imagination*** has been described as a [mode of intuition](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mode-of-intuition.md). ([Kant](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/immanuel-kant.md)) [Imagining](imagining.md) is [interfacing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interfacing.md) with imagination.

# Modes of Imagination

Imagination has been described as having its own [modes of imagination](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/modes-of-imagination.md).

- [Reproductive imagination](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/reproductive-imagination.md) is [remembering](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/remembering.md) and [modifying](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/modifying.md) [experience](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experience.md).
- [Productive imagination](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/productive-imagination.md) is [creating](creating.md) [experience](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experience.md). (Kant)

# Differentiating Imagination

Imagination has been [differentiated](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/differentiating.md) from [perception](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/perception.md) for [including](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md) [intention](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intention.md). ([Sartre](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jean-paul-sartre.md))

Imagination may focus on imagining that which is [unreal](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/unreal.md). ([James Rowland Angell](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/james-rowland-angell.md))

Imagination may be a [mode of making meaning](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mode-of-making-meaning.md).

# Science

Imagining physical experience has been demonstrated to create similar neural activations to the real experience.

[Perky effect](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/perky-effect.md): people [mistaking](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mistaking.md) [perception](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/perception.md) for imagination.
