# Caring as complete relational Attending

**We conjecture that Caring is the whole act of [Attending](attending.md): articulating, predicating, positing, and settling an encountered relation.** Caring is not an emotion inserted into a fourth-stage output, nor a fifth morphism added after closure. It is the relational activity under which each stage receives and preserves what the preceding stage made available.

The [Turn](turn.md) is the field of codomains this complete act produces. Attending is the transformation through those stages. Caring is the proposed metaphysical identity of *the fully oriented act*, considered in its relation to what it attends.

## How the stages belong to care

- **Articulation** lets a distinction count as something that may concern the relation.
- **Predication** situates that distinction without stripping its subject/object handedness.
- **Positing** admits and records what can be told, including supporting and opposing evidence.
- **Settlement** gives the relation the stability its presently admitted conditions warrant, retaining the difference that cannot yet be absorbed.

A completed local settlement need not abolish the relation's future. We conjecture that **care preserves the conditions of further relating**: a remaining difference may become the next Turn's articulation, and a settled result can become material for a further relation.

The hypothesis is stronger than saying humans sometimes pay attention while caring. It says the four-part relational closure may be what **caring is at the metaphysical level**. This is a generative definition to be formalized through admissible stage maps and their receiving conditions, not an etymological argument or a physiological measurement.

## Ethics and receiving are not automatic

Care ethics studies a distinct but related question: when does an act of care become a caring *relationship*? In [Nel Noddings's caring relation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QfnhJg1VD3gqV1grnoMhMSGk47HfWBeu/view), receptive attention and motivational displacement toward a particular other are complemented by the cared-for's recognition or response. A carer's internal judgment that the act was helpful cannot by itself establish that the care was received.

Our metaphysical conjecture does not erase that distinction. For human and institutional applications, the cared-for's independent reception, consequences, and remaining needs belong to the relevant empirical and ethical theory. **A closure that declares itself received is not thereby caring in the relational-ethical sense.**

Similarly, the specific WIT/ACK/ATT accounting semantics of Group institutions remain their own typed operations. Metaphysical Attending does not automatically instantiate an institutional ATT, establish an ACK, or discharge a particular obligation.

## From Caring to the next Turn

A local Attending can be settled while a relationship remains unfinished. Its unabsorbed residue, held claims, and independently received effects can shape another Attending. We conjecture that recurring, situated care is an important realization of the [generative spiral](generative-spiral.md): repeated completion of a four-part act without repetition of identical contents.

Mathematically, the open work is to derive the Attending maps from Marks, specify which provenance is retained and which is identified, and prove when the resulting Turn can be received and lawfully carried into another. Obsmetrologia owns the mathematical constructions; the respective care-ethics and institutional fields own their distinct receiving criteria.
