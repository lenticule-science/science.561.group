Relational [closure operators](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) are the self-maintenance structures that the derivation produces at three scales: self-sustaining closure (step 3), field closure (step 7), and grand closure (step 18). Each is a map that, applied to a structure, returns the smallest self-sustaining envelope containing it.

In standard mathematics, a [closure operator](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) on a [partially ordered](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/partial-order.md) set is a function *c* that is extensive (*x* ≤ *c*(*x*)), monotone (*x* ≤ *y* implies *c*(*x*) ≤ *c*(*y*)), and idempotent (*c*(*c*(*x*)) = *c*(*x*)). A *nucleus* on a frame or locale is a [closure operator](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) that additionally preserves finite meets. See the existing learn-closure-operators skill for the standard treatment.

What distinguishes the *relational* [closure operators](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) is that they appear at three scales, each forced by what the previous leaves undetermined:

1. **Self-sustaining closure (step 3).** [Sustaining](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/sustaining.md) mediates between [Relating](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/relating.md) (act) and [Relation](../terms/relation.md) (condition), producing [Self-Coherence](../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/terms/self-coherence.md) and then [Closure](phenomena-closure.md). The relational unit maintains itself through its own activity. This is the first [closure operator](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md): applied to the unit, it returns the unit.
2. **Field closure (step 7).** After multiplicity, meta-structure, and recursive domain unfolding, [Closure Criteria](../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/terms/closure-criteria.md) describe what it takes for an integrated field to be self-sustaining. This generalizes unit-level closure to the entire relational field — a [closure operator](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) on the field of units and their relations.
3. **Grand closure (step 18).** [Profile](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/profile.md)s reconstruct the full derivation internally. The derivation applied to itself yields itself — a [fixed point](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/fixed-point/index.md). Grand closure is the [closure operator](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) whose [fixed points](../../triage/relationality-2/mathematics/concepts/fixed-point/index.md) are the profiles that reconstruct the whole.

[Nucleus](../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/nucleus.md) (step 16) is the generalization of closure to the full field of [Term](../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/terms/term.md)s and [Judgement](../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/terms/judgement.md)s: the smallest closed structure containing something. Where closure at step 3 is structural and closure at step 7 is field-theoretic, Nucleus operates on the formalized syntax, giving closure its algebraic character. [Flow](../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/terms/flow.md) and Nucleus form a [residuated](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/residuation.md) pair whose commutation produces [Geometry](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/geometry.md).

For the derivation-internal account of closure across scales, see [Closure](phenomena-closure.md).
