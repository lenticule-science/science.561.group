# Residuation

**Residuation asks what relation remains necessary for a composition to hold.** If a composite can be formed only under certain conditions, the residual gives the greatest compatible condition. For directed relationships, the subject-side and object-side conditions can differ.

For a product \(\cdot\) in an ordered Lambek algebra, left and right residuals obey

\[
a\cdot b\le c\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
b\le a\backslash c\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
a\le c/b.
\]

The operations \(a\backslash c\) and \(c/b\) retain different orientations. In the relational semantics, ordinary composition \(R;S\) has residuals defined by universal conditions on which pairs could enter a composite without exceeding the target relation. The two residuals coincide in the commutative case under the appropriate algebraic laws; noncommutative composition can keep them distinct.

## The difference becomes generative

We conjecture that the difference between the two residual directions is not only an account of what a finite relational composition could not absorb. It can be **the next turn's generative condition**. One settled form leaves a question about which further relation, observation, or rule would allow its boundary to be crossed.

That is the mechanism proposed in the [generative spiral](generative-spiral.md): the result of one finite descent supplies the material for a renewed kinematic or aperiodic articulation. This is not an instruction to apply the same idempotent map forever. The residual can change the space of admissible relations.

## Curvature, KZ, and twistoring

In the [Obsmetrologia Lambek algebra](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Calculus/Logic/LambekCalculus.lean), left and right residuation describe the handedness of a noncommutative product. A separate causal-set development investigates the residual gap through curvature. We conjecture that this algebraic defect can be transported to the two-dimensional [KZ comparison](../terms/kz-lax.md) and to the proposed [twistor incidence](../terms/twistoring.md) geometry without losing its direction.

The carrier, maps, and preservation law matter: an algebraic asymmetry is not automatically a physical curvature tensor. Its geometric realization is a construction we intend to derive.
