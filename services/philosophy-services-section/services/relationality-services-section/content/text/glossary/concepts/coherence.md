**Coherence** is the condition in which an act, its corresponding conditions and its supporting structures hold together in a stable, self-sufficient dynamic. It marks a relational system as complete enough to persist. Acts do the work, conditions are held open, and structures stabilize and durably maintain the results; each is necessary and none suffices, and for the system to be self-sufficient all three must be present and held together as one whole, their interplay resolving without collapse, dissipation or irresolvable contradiction.

Coherence is not the absence of contradiction. A claim held with evidence both for and against it is a legitimate, stable position; what breaks coherence is a contradiction that cannot be held, not one that is.

In emsenn's words, deriving [existing](../terms/existing.md) shows that a thing is brought about by the primitive acts of including and excluding; these generate the conditions of inclusion and exclusion; these are held together by the condition of tension, which induces difference; the dynamic is sustained by relating and stabilized through relation and the formation of a relative as a minimal structure. Coherence is what holding all of that together achieves.

## Coherence drives the derivation

Coherence is what the [relational derivation](../terms/relational-derivation.md) produces and what moves it. Every step earns a coherence, a self-sufficient holding-together of act, condition and structure, and each achieved coherence leaves something undetermined, which incites the next. Coherence is never final.

Each movement earns its own kind:

- **I, Logical Origination:** consistent distinguishing, the Heyting algebra of recognitions.
- **II, Structural Stabilization:** sustained form, the [balance](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md) of closure and interior.
- **III, Directed Dynamics:** directed motion, [flow](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flow.md) preserving the modal core.
- **IV, Geometric Cohesion:** local–global translation, the [cohesive chain](../terms/cohesion.md).
- **V, Emergent Containment:** a system coherent with its own limits, self-containment.

At finer grain, existential coherence is including and excluding stabilizing, relational coherence is relating and relation stabilizing, field coherence is many units stabilizing in one field, and meta-coherence is the integrated field stabilizing its own boundary.

The formal mechanism that produces coherence is closure: applying a [closure operator](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-operator.md) settles a configuration into its coherent form. At the smallest scale coherence is a genuinely higher-dimensional condition, the coherence path of [minimal cohesion](../terms/cohesion.md).
