# Turn: the codomains of Attending

A **Turn** is the *stage-indexed family of codomains* generated by one complete [Attending](attending.md). The four-part composition is the **act**; the Turn is the structured field of what its constituent acts can produce. It carries the four codomains, their order of generation, their connecting morphisms, and the particular images reached in an occurrence. A Turn is therefore more than the last settled value, but is not the same object as the operation that settles it.

Let \(X_0\) be the admitted utterance structure built from Marks, and take the following typed transformations under a declared context \(\Gamma\):

\[
X_0 \xrightarrow{\alpha} X_1
\xrightarrow{\pi} X_2
\xrightarrow{\nu_\Gamma} X_3
\xrightarrow{\sigma_\Gamma} X_4 .
\]

Here \(X_1\) is the **articulation codomain**, \(X_2\) the **predication codomain**, \(X_3\) the **positing codomain**, and \(X_4\) the **settlement codomain**. The context \(\Gamma\) records whichever evidence and closure operators the present realization still requires as supplied inputs.

The single composite \(\sigma_\Gamma\circ\nu_\Gamma\circ\pi\circ\alpha\) is **Attending**. The **Turn** is the *whole diagram of codomains and connecting maps*. A stage-tagged carrier may be written as the dependent sum \(\sum_{i=1}^{4}X_i\), with a tag preserving each codomain's type. This notation presents an already constructed family; it does not import a four-way coproduct as an unexplained primitive of Obsmetrologia.

For a particular utterance \(u\), the sequence of attained outputs \((\alpha u,\pi\alpha u,\nu_\Gamma\pi\alpha u,\sigma_\Gamma\nu_\Gamma\pi\alpha u)\) is a **trace through the Turn**. It must not be confused with the codomain objects themselves. The mathematical program aims to generate the whole Turn from Mark rather than simply assume its codomain types and operators.

We conjecture that **Turns constitute the spiral of existence**. What is settled at one depth may leave a lawful residual whose articulation opens a new Turn. The repeated pattern of Attending does not require repetition of the same carrier or the same result.

The stronger conjecture that this entire attending closure is a constitutive form of **[Caring](caring.md)** belongs to the caring concept. Neither an ordinal regime nor an ethical reception rule is silently identified with one of the four stage outputs.

## What makes the first Turn necessary

A Turn does **not** originate because someone has already selected four state types. The [irresolvability of relationlessness](../terms/relationlessness.md) is the proposed generating pressure.

- Trying to complete the exclusion of relation **performs an exclusion**. That act gives rise to the roles of excluding and excluded; the roles do not wait as two self-identical atoms before the act.
- The attempt cannot declare those roles nonexistent while relying on the act that produced them. A distinction persists between the claim of completed absence and the performed exclusion.
- **Attending** admits and follows that distinction. If the act remains unresolved and its witness cannot be erased, attempting settlement supplies *new material for articulation*. One completed local determination can therefore produce the conditions of another Turn.
- The positions in a Turn's codomain diagram are the mathematical **record of those compelled acts**. The diagram is the result to derive; it is not an initial container in which to place an already chosen relation.

The concrete obstruction remains at the source: `Obsmetrologia.Articulating.pair u a` strictly continues `a`, but its utterance `u` is still externally supplied. The missing generator must construct **the next utterance from the unresolved relation in the preceding Attending**. Until it does, the four-stage program is the correct type of mathematical question, but it is not yet a completed derivation of the first act from relationlessness.

This also explains why **graphs are downstream**. At first, the source has acts, generated roles, retained attempts and comparisons; it has not provided independently given vertices, adjacency, a graph cycle or a Galois context. A graph becomes a possible *lossy readout* only once continuations and their observably supported identifications exist. A graph theorem cannot substitute for the derivation of those continuations.

## The codomains of a Turn

| Operation | What it does | Regime at this depth | Physical reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Articulate** | Generate directed relational distinction from the admitted utterance | Aperiodic-facing | Kinematic possibility |
| **Predicate** | Compose subject–predicate–object incidence and its conditions | Transfinite-facing | Quantum relations |
| **Posit** | Assign evidential positions and valuations; establish compatible readings | Scott-facing | Effective causal spacetime |
| **Settle** | Close the admitted field and obtain its current determinations | Strict-finite-facing | Finite space and thermodynamics |

The *four operations* and the *four closure-ordinal regimes* are two distinct structures. We conjecture the correspondence shown here arises through a common generative carrier. The closure ordinal is a measurement of a particular rule-driven process; the operation does not acquire an ordinal simply because we have named its phase.

Attending orders its stage maps by mathematical dependence, not by a pre-existing clock. The Turn retains every codomain, including unsettled and settled stages; different occurrences may traverse the same diagram with different traces and different evidence.

We use **positing** for the third verb, including the establishment of **position** and valuation: this recovers the existing Obsmetrologia verb without treating the intended position as a wholly independent process.

## The regime–Pisot selection conjecture

We conjecture that each Turn's regime-specific, *source-generated* substitution dynamics select a characteristic Pisot inflation. This is the correspondence the old `residuation-forces-a-spiral`, `settlement-spiral`, and `regime-pisot-tower` corpus was reaching for:

| Regime (closure ordinal) | Recurrence / substitution | Spectral equation | Inflation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strict-finite** \(\kappa<\omega\) | Padovan/Perrin | \(x^3=x+1\) | Plastic \(\rho\approx1.3247\) |
| **Scott-continuous** \(\kappa=\omega\) | Fibonacci | \(x^2=x+1\) | Golden \(\varphi\approx1.6180\) |
| **Transfinite-bounded** \(\omega<\kappa<\omega_1\) | Tribonacci | \(x^3=x^2+x+1\) | Tribonacci \(\tau\approx1.8393\) |
| **Aperiodic** \(\kappa\geq\omega_1\) | \(n\)-bonacci as \(n\to\infty\) | \(x^n=x^{n-1}+\cdots+1\) | Limit \(2\) |

The ordering is \(\rho<\varphi<\tau<\cdots\to2\). Our proposal is more precise than “Pisot numbers seem related to settlement.” A Turn is a way to derive *which* recurrence describes the closure of a field from what its previous articulations generated.

**Why Padovan at the finite end?** Its recurrence \(P_k=P_{k-2}+P_{k-3}\) skips the immediately preceding term. Its plastic inflation is the smallest Pisot number greater than one: the tightest self-similar inflation in that class. We conjecture this is the spectral signature of a maximally settled, finite Turn. The original Collatz shape study reported growth closer to plastic than golden and used that result to challenge the earlier Fibonacci assignment.

**Why the expanding family upward?** Fibonacci and Tribonacci progressively enlarge the active predecessor set; the \(n\)-bonacci limit approaches two. We conjecture that the growing recurrence depth expresses how much relational history a Turn must retain before a new settlement can form.

The **classification by closure ordinal** and the **aperiodicity of a spatial substitution** are separate measurements. A geometrically aperiodic Padovan or Fibonacci tiling need not have an aperiodic *closure ordinal*. Our conjecture instead requires that a *particular generated Turn* supply both the admissible substitution matrix and the closure process whose ordinal is measured. This is how the old table can be mathematically meaningful without conflating two different meanings of “aperiodic.”

The [original computed tower](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1J9Ow0IUNR2SmeyXCvEBn7dzdsMi5m8hL/view) computed the stated matrices and numerical inflation factors. Obsmetrologia separately has [Padovan and Fibonacci matrix identities](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/AperiodicOrder/Tiling/StepN/SubstitutionMatrix.lean), including \(M_{\rho}^{3}=M_{\rho}+I\) and \(M_{\varphi}^{2}=M_{\varphi}+I\), and a [pointwise recurrence comparison](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Closure/Collatz/PadovanShape.lean). These are concrete pieces of the proposed law; the universal selection map is the conjecture.

## Distinct indices behind the towers

A **Turn index** \(n\) counts generative re-entry; a **stage index** \(i\) selects one of the four codomains of Attending; a **closure ordinal** \(\kappa\) classifies a specified settling process; a **substitution order** \(r\) specifies the recurrence's letter count and incidence matrix \(M_r\); and a **hyperverse level** \(d\) counts universe-generating operations. These are different types of indices. A grade \(g\in K_4\) additionally records the two-choice symmetry of a quartet, while a gradient requires a potential and a flow on its own carrier.

In particular, both **Padovan** and **Tribonacci** have cubic characteristic polynomials, but different polynomials and different permitted look-back steps:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Padovan:}&\quad P_k=P_{k-2}+P_{k-3},\quad x^3-x-1,\\
\text{Tribonacci:}&\quad T_k=T_{k-1}+T_{k-2}+T_{k-3},
\quad x^3-x^2-x-1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus a degree-three matrix is not by itself evidence for the transfinite regime. The proposed correspondence concerns the **substitution rule actually generated by the Turn**, the closure process applied to that rule, and the measured \(\kappa\). The prior constraint that a Turn's generating maps be admitted from Mark is part of that classification.

The [Obsmetrologia four-regime source](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/explanations/the-four-regimes.md) holds the complete typed coordinate inventory and the distinction between dyads, quartets, ascension, descension, gradients, and stage closure.

### Why these recurrences must be generated, not selected

The regime–Pisot correspondence is a conjecture about **what failed attempts at settlement force a Turn to retain and revisit**. A recurrence rule such as Padovan's \(P_{n+3}=P_{n+1}+P_n\) becomes a *derived law of relating* only if the native residual re-entry specifies why the two predecessor obligations at those precise depths are indispensable and why neither can be collapsed into the other. Selecting the Padovan substitution matrix in advance would simply manufacture its eigenvalue.

Similarly, the Fibonacci, Tribonacci and \(n\)-bonacci predecessor sets must arise as distinct ways in which the generative process preserves irresolvable relations. Their growth factors are then **consequences of the native recursion**. The first proof obligation is therefore the recurrence of unresolved *witness dependencies*, **before** any substitution matrix, Galois algebra, topology or spectrum is introduced.

## What the recurrence measures

There are **two mathematically different interpretations** of the historical statement that finite settlement “spirals by Padovan.”

**Within-Turn recurrence.** One strict-finite closure may fire only a *finite prefix* of a Padovan-shaped sequence. It can demonstrate the predicted local growth ratios and interval structure, but a finite completed orbit alone has no limiting eigenvalue measured along infinitely many of its own steps.

**Between-Turn recurrence.** A sequence of Turns can have \(\kappa(T_n)<\omega\) for **every** \(n\), yet their generated carriers or increments can grow across \(n\) by a Padovan substitution. For instance a family may satisfy \(a_{n+3}=a_{n+1}+a_n\) even though each separate \(T_n\) settles in one or several internal steps. Its cross-Turn growth approaches the plastic factor \(\rho\) while no individual finite closure runs indefinitely.

Both readings preserve the proposed finite–Padovan relationship. They concern different indexing operations, and a proof must say which is used. The same distinction applies to Fibonacci/Scott and Tribonacci/transfinite assignments.

The \(n\)-bonacci family has an exact, useful mathematical progression. For \(n\ge2\), its root \(\lambda_n>1\) is the unique solution of

\[
x^n=x^{n-1}+\cdots+x+1.
\]

Its roots increase, and multiplication by \(x-1\) gives \(2-\lambda_n=\lambda_n^{-n}\). Hence \(\lambda_n\to2\), with \(0<2-\lambda_n\le\varphi^{-n}\). This supplies the increasing spectral ladder; it **does not** alone establish an aperiodic closure with uncountable cofinality. The source-generated bridge must explain how the countably indexed recurrence limit corresponds to \(\kappa\ge\omega_1\), rather than assuming the two limits are one.

The explicit derivation, the two different cubic Padovan/Tribonacci laws, and an independent-matrix/closure countermodel are in [Obsmetrologia's four-regime explanation](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/explanations/the-four-regimes.md).

## Why successive Turns form a spiral

Write \(F_n\) for the settlement of Turn \(n\), with its locally admitted closure operator \(c_n\). At the end of the Turn,

\[
c_n(c_n(x))=c_n(x)
\]

can hold without ending generation. The missing structure is the passage from the **residual of the settled field** to a new admissible articulation:

\[
F_n\xrightarrow{\operatorname{residue}}
\operatorname{Residue}(F_n)
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{precipitate}}
\operatorname{Articulations}(T_{n+1}).
\]

We conjecture that the second arrow is a derived operation, constrained by the original marks, admissibility, and history, rather than an unexplained creation of new primitives. Because the *carrier* can change between Turns, another Turn is not an illegal periodic orbit of an idempotent closure on the same state space.

[Lambek residuation](residuation.md) gives the distinct left and right conditions for directed composition. [KZ lax idempotence](../terms/kz-lax.md) gives the coherent closing structure at one depth. [Twistoring](../terms/twistoring.md) is the proposed incidence and geometric presentation of what remains between the two directions. We conjecture these are three readings of one relational transition, realized within the Turn.

## From Turn to kinematic, quantum, causal, and thermal existence

The physical reading is

\[
K_n\to Q_n\to C_n\to F_n\to K_{n+1}.
\]

We conjecture that the **aperiodic articulation of a Turn is kinematic space before quantum physics**; the generated quantum relations support causal/general-relativistic spacetime; the effective spacetime supports thermodynamic settlement; residual conditions create the next kinematic Turn.

“Before” here means generative dependence. It is not a claim that ordinary time existed before spacetime. An already-defined [causal diamond kinematic space \(K(C)\)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/CausalSets/BlackHoles/HoleDiamondCausalSet.lean) is a useful mathematical construction; deriving an originary kinematic field from our Turn's articulation requires another morphism.

The [relational hyperverse](../terms/relational-hyperverse.md) is the meta-level reading: a universe completed in one Turn can enter a new generation as a relational object. The hyperverse's own universe tower and a single universe's internal Turn sequence are related, not identical.

## The one mathematical weld this conjecture asks for

To derive the regime–Pisot law, take one admitted history, not four externally labeled examples. Let \(H\) be an admissible Mark-generated history with witnessed residuals, and derive from it **together** the relevant closure \(F_H\), its stage-indexed Turn, and a substitution matrix \(M_H\). Measure the ordinal of that very closure and the Perron spectrum of that very matrix. The missing mathematical construction is

\[
G:H\longmapsto
\bigl(F_H,\ \kappa(F_H),\ M_H,\ \lambda(M_H)\bigr).
\]

The conjecture says these generated measurements obey the recurrence–regime correspondence above **within the class of admitted histories**. Deriving admissibility from Mark, observational quotient witnesses, and Attending's admitted evidence is part of the hypothesis.

The countercase must also be generated by the same admissibility rules. An arbitrary non-Pisot matrix introduced by hand tests a broader claim than ours; an internally generated non-Pisot history would test the conjecture itself.

This is the original mathematical ambition in a form we can continue to build. One Turn is complete when it settles *its* field; the spiral continues when what it cannot absorb generates the next.
