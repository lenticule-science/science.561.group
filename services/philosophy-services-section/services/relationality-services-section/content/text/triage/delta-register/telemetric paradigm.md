A ***telemetric paradigm*** is a [[system of coherence]] maintained through recursive registration of [[telemetric signal|signal]] within situated [[telemetric field|fields]]. It replaces [[ontological paradigm|ontological]] or [[representational paradigm|representational paradigms]] with *processual coherence systems*, in which [[knowledge]], [[action]], and [[interpret|interpretation]] emerge through [[casting cycles]], rather than [[symbolic alignment]].

Where other paradigms assume discrete [[category|categories]], internal consistency, or [[truth]]-anchored [[model|modeling]], the **telemetric paradigm** assumes [[telemetric role|roles]], [[telemetric trace|traces]], and [[telemetric recursion|recursions]]. It does not model what *is*, it describes *apparent movement*.

A telemetric paradigm operates through the [[telemetric grammar|grammar]] of [[telemetric cast|casting]] signal into [[telemetric method|methods]] situated within [[telemetric field|fields]], which result in [[telemetric yield|yields]]. This grammar is [[metabolize|metabolic]], not [[epistemology|epistemic]]: it does not stablize [[meaning]], it surfaces [[coherence]] by aligning methods and fields around signals.
