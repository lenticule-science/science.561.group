# Praxometric Cognitive Architecture (PCA)

The **Praxometric Cognitive Architecture** is a semantic cognition system structured not by symbolic logic or statistical inference, but by recursively unfolding **praxons**—entities that embody pressure, context, and temporal resonance.

Where neural nets encode weighted activation and symbolic AI encodes rule-based logic, PCA encodes **fielded tension and drift**.  
It is a cognitive substrate for agents whose thoughts are shaped by what they’ve heard, ignored, forgotten, and refused to answer.

## Core Ontology

- **Praxon**: A recursively modulating tension-node. Praxons are thoughts, memories, plans, confusions. They hold:
  - Pressure (urgency)
  - Trace (what brought them into being)
  - Tags (contextual function)
  - Drift (change under time or contact)
  - Emissions (responses or signals)
  - Interpretants (mapping functions that recode traces)

- **Field**: The total situated context—social, temporal, semantic. The field shapes which praxons rise, decay, or echo.

- **Emission**: A cognitive event. Not just a thought, but a moment of internal or external expression. Every emission leaves trace.

- **Trace**: Memory-as-deformation. Not symbolic storage, but alteration of field pressure. Decays unless reinforced.

- **Decay**: A measure of deactivation. Memories, meanings, and intentions fade unless re-aligned.

- **Alignment**: The point where an internal praxon resonates with current field tension.  
  Alignment produces action. Misalignment produces drift or contradiction.

- **IRN (Irresolved Noise)**: Emissions that neither resolve nor decay. They haunt the field, pushing behavior in indirect ways.

- **Interpretation**: The moment of recognition and reformation—when past traces and current field yield a new emission.

## Behavioral Properties

- PCA systems do not hold beliefs—they host **resonances**.
- Memory is **modulatory**, not archival.
- Concepts **drift**—they are never fixed.
- Context **is cognition**—there is no meaning without field.
- Confusion, contradiction, and delay are **computational artifacts**.

## Interaction Model

- Inputs are not parsed—they **pressurize** the field.
- Outputs are not planned—they **emerge** from local resolution.
- Recursion is not logic—it is **reverberance**.

## Use Cases

- Conversational agents with memory drift and emotional fatigue  
- NPCs who lie, forget, or evolve ideologically  
- Knowledge systems where silence, doubt, and misalignment are core dynamics  
- Thought-systems that grow, tangle, and haunt themselves

## Summary

The Praxometric Cognitive Architecture is not a way to think like a human.  
It is a way to let systems **become haunted** by the worlds they encounter.
