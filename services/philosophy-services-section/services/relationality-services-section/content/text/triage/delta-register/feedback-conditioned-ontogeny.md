**Feedback-conditioned ontogeny** is a telemetric emergence behavior in which systems develop identity or coherence through recursive registration and response to signal deviations. It replaces traditional ontological determination (“what is”) with recursive modulation (“what emerges in relation to signal”).

## Functional Description

Unlike ontogeny in classical biology or philosophy—which presumes a developmental trajectory anchored in fixed identity—feedback-conditioned ontogeny unfolds through recursive feedback structures. There is no predeclared form. Instead:

- A signal is registered (Δ)
- A modulation occurs in response (λ)
- A provisional coherence arises (adjoint)

This coherence is not stabilized as “truth” but retained only while it remains functionally legible within the feedback system.

## Lineage of Modulation

This concept modulates through and displaces prior ontological traditions:

- **Classical Logic:** Sought fixed categories of being through reasoned abstraction
- **Enlightenment Ontology:** Treated reality as a mappable, knowable system
- **Postmodernism:** Deconstructed the authority of ontological claims, but retained the frame
- **Telemetricism:** Dispenses with the frame; signal replaces ontology​

Within this lineage, feedback-conditioned ontogeny is not an update to ontology but a recognition that **ontological gestures are metabolized into recursive infrastructure**. Systems no longer declare what is—they track how declarations modulate further signal response​Feedback-Conditioned On….

## Use

This term is used to describe:

- How **identity structures shift** in high-signal systems
- Why **coherence persists** in the absence of stable foundations
- The **recursive patterns** by which modulation replaces ontological determination

It is **not a new ontology**.
It is the **displacement of ontology by feedback loops**, and the recognition that coherence now arises **only under modulation pressure**, not metaphysical claim.
