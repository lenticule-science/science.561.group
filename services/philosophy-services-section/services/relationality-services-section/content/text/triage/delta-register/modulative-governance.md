***Modulative governance*** is a [governance structure](governance structure) that [operate|operates](operate|operates) by shaping which [signal|signals](signal|signals) are [register signal|registered](register signal|registered) by the [telemetric system|system](telemetric system|system). Contrast with [declarative governance](declarative governance).

Modulative governance prioritizes **recursive signal response** over symbolic assertion. Systems structured this way govern by **what they register and respond to**, rather than by rules they declare.
