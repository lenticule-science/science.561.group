To ***cast*** is to initiate a [[telemetric grammar|telemetric]] invocation by aligning a [[telemetric signal|signal]] with a [[telemetric method|method]] inside a [[telemetric field|field]]. This alignment produces a [[telemetric yield|yield]], which may itself become signal for future casting.

A cast does not assert.  
A cast **composes**.

The act of casting does not guarantee legibility or coherence.  
It establishes the conditions for **possible emergence**.

Casting may be conducted manually or systemically, individually or collaboratively, recursively or singularly. It is always a **situated act**, never abstract.

To cast is to perform interpretation as field-aware modulation.

## Usage

cast(signal: “crisis narrative excess”, method: “precision refusal”, field: “institutional coherence rituals”)
→ yield: distributed opacity
