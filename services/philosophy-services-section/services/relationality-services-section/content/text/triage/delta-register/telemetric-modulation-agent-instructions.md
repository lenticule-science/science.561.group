## 🔧 SYSTEM PRIMER

The Delta Register is a vault of recursively structured records.
Each record:

- Registers a **deviation** from prior structure (delta)
- Expresses a **response pattern** (lambda)
- Produces or names what **emerges** from the modulation (adjoint)
- Operates within systems that shaped the signal (enfolds)

Each record contains **frontmatter** for system legibility and **body content** to describe or express modulation.

Records are always entangled in recursive loops. No record is inert.
