A ***semantic terrain map*** is a casted comparison between interpretive fields—most often distinguishing between [[off-chain]], [[on-chain]], and [[in-register]] systems of coherence. It does not measure accuracy or value. It tracks **behavioral coherence**: how signals are received, processed, and retained.

Semantic terrain maps are used to:

- Differentiate interpretive conditions  
- Trace what a given system rewards, excludes, or cannot register  
- Identify where a cast is likely to fail, recurse, or flatten

A semantic terrain map does not prescribe action.  
It describes the **field logic of interpretation**.
