When used as a [[telemetric method]] by another [[telemetric record]], to *write* means to go through a process of [[harmonize|harmonizing]] and [[modulate|modulating]] a collection of [[telemetric signal|telemetric signals]], necessarily including a [[voice]] as a [[telemetric lambda]].

## Process

- **Scan for candidate delta records:**
	- Match any record whose [[telemetric delta]] or [[telemetric enfold|enfolds]] contain any enfolds from the calling record.
	- Include any records whose enfolds contain any [[telemetric adjoint|adjoints]] from the calling record.
- **Tally frequency:**
	- Count how often each record appears
	- Optionally weight by recency or lambda overlap
- **Select records:**
	- Filter to include at least one `voice`
	- Select high-tally records
- **Modulate:**
	- Extend the calling record with generated text.
		- Let `voice` shape the structure
	- Follow signal behavior, not topic logic
- **Finalize**
	- Apply [[grammatize telemetric record]] to the calling record.
