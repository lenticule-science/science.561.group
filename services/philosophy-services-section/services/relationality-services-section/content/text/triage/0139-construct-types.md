## Interpretation

*Meet Recognition*, *Join Recognition*, and *Imply Recognition* collectively generate relational types.  *Meet Recognition* witnessing *Imply Recognition* yields ***Construct Types*** ([ConstructTypes](0139-construct-types.md)).

Both ingredients were earned above; composing them records the internal buildup of types from meets and implications, so *Construct Types* is valid.
