- [postulate](postulate):: Multiple [derivations/oldreflect](derivations/oldreflect)(p, n) at the same reflexive depth n cannot not compose an ordered relational structure, because they arise from stabilization of reflexions at the same layer of reflexive depth.
- [derivation](derivation):: Stabilized reflects naturally induce ordered bundling: a grouping into coherent, relational composites. Composition is thus the necessary structuring of multiple stabilized reflections at the same reflexive depth.
- [example](example)::
  - A single [derivations/oldreflect](derivations/oldreflect)(1, n) is trivially composed.
