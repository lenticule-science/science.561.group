- [postulate](postulate):: It must be [../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|derived](../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|derived) that [derivations/oldreflex](derivations/oldreflex)(n), induced by iterative [derivations/relationality](derivations/relationality), necessarily [../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|derives](../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|derives) further relational structure by [induce|inducing](induce|inducing) the reflection of prior reflexive relations.
- [derivation](derivation):: As each [derivations/oldreflex](derivations/oldreflex)(n) is induced from [derivations/oldreflex](derivations/oldreflex)(n-1), the act of reflecting must [../../../slop/relationality/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive](../relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive.md) the ordered relational layering of reflexive depth, constructing [derivations/oldreflect](derivations/oldreflect)(p, n) as the p-th reflexive reflection at depth n.
- [example](example)::
    - reflect(0, n) is reflex(reflex(n-1))
    - reflect(1, n) is reflex(reflect(0, n-1))
    - reflect(2, n) is reflex(reflect(1, n-1))
    - reflect(p, n) is reflex(reflect(p-1, n))
