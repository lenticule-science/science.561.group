- [postulate](postulate):: A single reflexive derivation must induce the minimal affirmation of relational presence.
- [derivation](derivation):: Reflexing once derives the affirmation that relationality is present. This is because inducing one reflexive act composes a first positive presence of relation, which is the most minimal unit of affirmation.
- [example](example)::
    - true(n) is reflect(1, n)
    - reflex(reflex(0)) induces presence of relation, so reflect(1, n) derives true.
