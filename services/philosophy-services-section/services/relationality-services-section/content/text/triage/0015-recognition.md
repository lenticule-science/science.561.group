# Recognizing

## Interpretation

A self-named point lacks a partner; having the act *Void* witness the act *Self Naming* produces a two-place recognition.

In the composition `(Void SelfNaming)`, the act *Void* explicitly witnesses the act *Self Naming*.  Because both acts have already been earned, this reflection creates a complementary position, so we name the resulting two-place act *Recognition*.
