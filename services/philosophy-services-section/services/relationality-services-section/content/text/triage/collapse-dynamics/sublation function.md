## sublation function

### **Interpretation**

- **Sublation** is not mere collapse or negation. Instead, it is the process by which the breakdown of an old constraint system becomes the seed for a new constraint. The failure of coherence is generative: it produces the conditions for a new order to emerge.

**In essence:**  
The sublation function formalizes how, when a complex system of constraints can no longer hold itself together due to internal contradictions, the breakdown itself becomes the source of a new constraint. A new order emerges from the failure of the old, shaped by the very contradictions that destroyed the previous system.
