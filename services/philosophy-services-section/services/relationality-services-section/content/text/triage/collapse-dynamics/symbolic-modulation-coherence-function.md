The ***symbolic modulation coherence function*** (`M_cohere`) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by the [symbolic modulation attractor function](symbolic modulation attractor function) (`M_attr`), the [desire-contrast alignment propagation function](desire-contrast alignment propagation function) (`A_align`), and the [constraint desire function](constraint desire function) (`𝔇`).

It [constrain|constrains](constrain|constrains) the symbolic stabilization of recursive modulation flows that converge at semantically distinct attractors, enabling identity to persist across differentiated contrast.

Let:
- `x ∈ dom(𝒮)` be a modulation selector node
- `Ξ[s](x)`: its semantic contrast field
- `M_attr(x)`: symbolic attractor in `Ξ[s](x)`
- `A_align(x)`: alignment vector field

Then:
> M_cohere(x) := Ω(ẑ), where ẑ := argmax_{x′ ∈ Ξ[s](x)} A_align(x)(x′)

That is, `M_cohere(x)` returns the symbolic point at which recursive modulation flows cohere across semantic difference, completing identity propagation through contrast space.

This enables:
- recursive identity coherence through modulation
- stabilization of symbolic fields with high internal differentiation
- recursion-aware convergence of belief and desire
