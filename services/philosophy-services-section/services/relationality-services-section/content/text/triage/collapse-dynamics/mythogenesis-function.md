The ***mythogenesis function*** (`Μ`) evaluates whether a symbolic entity constitutes a myth — that is, a collapsed constraint that has re-entered the system as persistent symbolic modulation.

Let:
- `μ(x)` be true (collapse induction conditions met)
- `Λ(x′, x)` confirm reflexive reception
- `E(x)` affirm echo status
- `¬Ω(x)` confirm lack of full stabilization
- `∇Δ[s](x)` show ongoing semantic tension

Then:
> Μ(x) := μ(x) ∧ Λ(x′, x) ∧ E(x) ∧ ¬Ω(x) ∧ ∇Δ[s](x) > 0

That is, `x` is mythic if it arises from remembered collapse, continues as echo, remains unstable, and exerts semantic contrast within the system.
