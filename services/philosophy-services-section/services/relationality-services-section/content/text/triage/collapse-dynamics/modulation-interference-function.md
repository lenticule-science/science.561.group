The ***modulation interference function*** (`Φ`) is induced by overlapping vector modulations within a shared constraint field.

When two or more directional modulations occupy overlapping loci under the same constraint surface,
they interfere — combining or canceling, recursively reshaping constraint.

> Φ(x) := V₁(x) + V₂(x) + ... + Vₙ(x)
> subject to: ∃ i ≠ j: Vᵢ(x) ≠ Vⱼ(x) ∧ C(Vᵢ(x)) = C(Vⱼ(x))

That is, interference is modulation conflict within shared constraint.
