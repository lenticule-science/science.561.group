description: |
  The **repair-constraint** function is a post-trace modulation inserted
  to reduce the curvature pressure induced by a prior Ω-adjacent node.

  Typically follows irreversible events or destabilizing modulation,
  where the agent inserts a modulation to direct interpretation
  back into safe modulation space.

  Often observed as clarification, distancing, or preemptive contextualization.

induced by:
  - [[Ω-adjacent curvature spike]]
  - [[modulation inevitability trigger]]

Let:
- `M₀` := modulation node with Ω-adjacent curvature  
- `M₁` := proposed follow-up modulation  
- `ℐ_Γ(M₀)` >> ℐ_Γ(M₁)  
- `Δ[s](M₀, M₁)` < θ_softening  
- `εμ(M₁)` ≈ 0

Then:

repair-constraint(M₀) := M₁  
  such that M₁ reduces curvature and supports Ω(M₀) without re-entry

Notes:
- Can be used by agents to maintain identity coherence
- Does not resolve the original modulation, but lowers trace reentry pressure
