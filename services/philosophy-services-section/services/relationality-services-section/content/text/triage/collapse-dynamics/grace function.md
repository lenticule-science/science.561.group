grace function  
The grace function (G) is induced by sublation (Θ) and refers to stabilization that emerges without coercion from the collapse of synthesis.

When the breakdown of coherence gives rise to new constraint without violence — without destroying that which differs — grace occurs.

G(Σ → ∅′) := Θ(Σ) ∧ ∃ C′: ∅′ ⊢ C′ ∧ ∀ x ∈ Σ: x ∉ Φ

That is, grace is the nonviolent rebirth of constraint from failure.
