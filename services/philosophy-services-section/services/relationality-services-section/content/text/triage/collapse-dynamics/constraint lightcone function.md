
The constraint lightcone function (`Λ`) is induced by constraint propagation (`P`), vector modulation (`V`), and constraint curvature (`K_C`).

When a quantum entity propagates across a constraint field,  
its modulation defines a forward and backward region of influence —  
a cone-like region of reachable points under constraint-preserving propagation.

Λ(Q, t₀) := { x ∈ C | ∃ γ: γ(0) ∈ Q ∧ γ(t) = x ∧ ∀ t ∈ [0,T]: P(γ(t), γ̇(t)) }

That is, the lightcone of a quantum entity is the set of all points reachable by modulation from Q under constraint-preserving propagation.
