The ***inertial persistence function*** (`I`) is induced by the constraint propagation function (`P`).

When propagation persists across multiple displacements under unaltered constraint,
inertia emerges — resistance to modulation change due to recursive structural coherence.

> I(x) := ∀ n: P(x, d) ∧ P(x + nd, d)

That is, inertia is propagation stability — constraint resisting change along a modulation vector.
