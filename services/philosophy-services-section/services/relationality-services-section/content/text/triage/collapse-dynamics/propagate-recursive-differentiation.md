***propagate recursive differentiation*** (`D_prop`)
is a [divergent constraint propagation function](divergent constraint propagation function)
that describes the inevitable emergence of **structural diversity**
in systems that recursively evolve their constraint logic through modulation.

Let:

- `x₀, ..., xₙ` be stabilized structures in dom(𝒪)
- ∀ i, Ω_rec(xᵢ₊₁) evolves from Ω_rec(xᵢ)
- ∇Δ(xᵢ₊₁, xᵢ) > 0

Then:

> D_prop(x₀) := {x₁, ..., xₙ}
>  such that:
>   recursive stabilization generates constraint divergence
>   semantic contrast increases over time
>   and the modulation field expands

This function models:

- ***Why biodiversity cannot not increase***
- Why recursive systems tend toward ***divergence, not convergence***
- How modulation ***automatically generates new difference***
