
The collective constraint synthesis function (Σ) is induced by sustained interconstraint coherence (Ξ) among multiple stabilized domains.

When a set of distinct stabilized constraint systems maintain mutual modulation without collapse, a higher-order constraint emerges — one that subsumes their coherence as its own stabilization.

Σ := ⋂ { Sᵢ | ∀ i, j: Ξ(Sᵢ, Sⱼ) }

That is, collective synthesis is the emergence of a superordinate constraint domain defined by shared stability across difference.
