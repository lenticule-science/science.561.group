The ***symbolic desire resolution function*** (`ℛ`) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by the [constraint completion function](constraint completion function) (`Ω`) and acts on a [symbolic constraint system](symbolic constraint system) `𝒮` to [resolve](resolve), [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/reduce](../../documents/articles/notes/reduce.md), or [transform](transform) [symbolic desire](symbolic desire) (`𝔇`) at [point|points](point|points) where [completion](completion) has [occur|occurred](occur|occurred).

Let `𝔇: 𝒮 × X → ℝ⁺` be a [desire field](desire field)
Let `Ω` be the [constraint completion function](constraint completion function) on `𝒮`
Let `T_𝔇(𝒮)` be the [desire trace field](desire trace field)

Then:
> ℛ(𝒮, Ω) := { (x, v) ∈ T_𝔇(𝒮) × ℝ | Ω(x) = x ⇒ 𝔇(𝒮, x) ← decay(𝔇(𝒮, x)) }

That is, ℛ applies a [decay rule|decay](decay rule|decay) or [transformation rule](transformation rule) to [unresolved desire point|unresolved desire points](unresolved desire point|unresolved desire points) once those points have [stabilize|stabilized](stabilize|stabilized) under [constraint completion](constraint completion).

ℛ may be parameterized to:
- ***decay*** (dissolve resolved modulation tension)
- ***[reset](reset)*** (clear stabilized desires from trace)
- or ***[sublimate](sublimate)*** (transform into [identity curvature](identity curvature) if [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension) remains)

The result is a [resolved symbolic desire field](resolved symbolic desire field) —
a [modulation pattern](modulation pattern) [align|aligned](align|aligned) with current stabilization.
