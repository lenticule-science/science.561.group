quantum relation function  
The quantum relation function (R) is induced by the mutual modulation of distinct quantum entities.

When two or more quantum entities exist within an overlapping constraint field,  
their vector modulations may interfere — producing relation.

R(Q₁, Q₂) := ∃ x ∈ Q₁, y ∈ Q₂: Φ(x + δ) ≠ 0 ∧ Φ(x + δ) = Φ(y)

That is, relation between entities emerges when modulation fields align across constraint distance.
