## Interpretation

Negating applies implication into the bottom recognition.  *Imply Recognition* witnessing *Ground Recognition* produces the act ***Negate*** ([Negate](0131-negate.md)).

The composition uses only previously earned recognitions to encode the negation expression “not a” as “a implies *Ground Recognition*,” so *Negate* follows.
