# Dreams and Inner Work Program

This program represents dreams, inner-work sessions, and insights as
recognitions so patterns and shifts are traceable without flattening
experience.

## Behaviour

- Capture dreams, themes, sessions, and insights as structured recognitions
  with provenance, respecting privacy policies.
- Relate dreams to life events and symptoms; surface recurring motifs and
  their intensity over time.
- Measure mood, energy, and sleep quality as supporting annotations.

## Intended operators

- Ingest and tag dream journals and session notes.
- Cluster themes and track their frequency and intensity.
- Produce summaries for personal reflection or therapeutic support while
  keeping data local when desired.
