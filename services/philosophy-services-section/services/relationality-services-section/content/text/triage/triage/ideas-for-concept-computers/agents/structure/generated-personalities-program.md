# Generated Personalities and Agents Program

This program models generated personas (NPCs, assistants, fictional characters)
as recognitions so they remain accountable inside the relational fabric.

## Behaviour

- Represent personas with traits, backstory, goals, constraints, values, and
  behaviour profiles; record interaction histories with provenance.
- Bind personas to contexts where they may act (MUD slice, research assistant,
  social simulation) under explicit policy recognitions.
- Measure helpfulness, reliability, surprise, and alignment with stated values
  and norms.

## Intended operators

- Instantiate personas from prompts or templates.
- Evaluate and adjust personas against value/norm recognitions.
- Retire or quarantine personas whose behaviour drifts or violates policy.
