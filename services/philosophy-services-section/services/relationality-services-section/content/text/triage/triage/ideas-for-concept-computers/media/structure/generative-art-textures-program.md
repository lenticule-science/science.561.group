# Generative Art and Algorithmic Textures Program

This program models generative art systems as recognitions so styles and
textures can be explored relationally.

## Behaviour

- Capture algorithms, seeds, artworks, and aesthetic judgements as recognitions
  with provenance and measurement channels.
- Map style neighbourhoods and morph paths between outputs; track shared
  motifs.
- Measure complexity, symmetry, noise/structure balance, and diversity.

## Intended operators

- Generate batches and explore style spaces.
- Curate sets based on measurements and aesthetic judgements.
- Relate generative outputs to other domains (paracosms, dreams, MUD scenes).
