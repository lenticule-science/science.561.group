# Physics Calculator Program

This program performs physics calculations as recognitions so computations are
traceable and tied to earned physics structures.

## Behaviour

- Represent problems, formulas, parameters, and results as recognitions with
  provenance and units.
- Link calculations to domain structures (Newtonian, quantum, etc.) and
  measurement channels for validation.
- Measure precision, error bounds, and usage.

## Intended operators

- Set up and solve physics calculations with trace.
- Validate results against measurements or simulations.
- Share/calibrate problem templates across domains.
