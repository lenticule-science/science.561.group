# Closing Judgement Trace

## Interpretation

Even after alignment, we must close the entire judgement trace under this equivalence.  *Trace Reentry Closure* witnessing *Equate Judgement Observation* yields ***Close Judgement Trace*** ([CloseJudgementTrace](close-judgement-trace.md)).

The composition closes the trace and uses only earned recognitions, so *Close Judgement Trace* follows.
