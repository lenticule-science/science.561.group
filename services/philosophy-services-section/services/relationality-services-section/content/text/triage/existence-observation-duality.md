## What existence-observation duality is

Existence and observation are two aspects of the same coherent structure.
Draft 6 (SDIG) formulates this as the **Ambidexterity Theorem**:

> hcolim F ≃ hlim S

For every coherent configuration, the constructive realization (homotopy
colimit of a constructible diagram F) is equivalent to the observational
verification (homotopy limit of the dual sheaf of observations S).

This means: to construct something and to observe something are
homotopy-dual operations. Being and knowing are not separate — they
are two modal directions through the same relational field.

## How it appears across drafts

**Draft 6** (SDIG): Provides the most explicit formulation. Existence
is modal coherence — the inhabited propositional truncation ‖hcolim F_x‖.
Observation is the dual: the inhabited ‖hlim S_x‖. The Ambidexterity
Theorem proves these are equivalent.

Consequences:
- An entity exists iff its dual observational sheaf is coherent
- The duality is an involution: (F)^∨ ≃ S and (S)^∨ ≃ F
- Testability = existence of an inhabited global section of ‖hlim S‖
- Being and knowing are "homotopy-dual modalities of a single
  stratified field"

**Draft 6** (SDIG, §7): The duality extends to dynamic processes.
Left Kan extensions propagate potentiality forward (actualization).
Right Kan extensions reconstruct necessary conditions backward
(verification). Together they form a "bimodal temporal logic of
coherence."

**Draft 3** (treatise): The Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction
theorem is a different expression of the same insight. Any
well-behaved constraint on the relational field (a Filter) produces
a sub-field (a Profile) that reproduces the ENTIRE derivation
internally. Observation does not destroy structure — it specializes
it into a complete world.

**Draft 10**: The Stable Envelope — Envelope(x) = Possible(x) ∧
Necessary(x) — captures the bandwidth of stable variation for a
recognition. What persists under both opening (outward release) and
closing (inward consolidation) IS the observable content. This is a
specific case of the duality: the observable content is exactly what
exists stably.

## The Field of Existence

Draft 6 assembles all coherent configurations into the **∞-Topos of
Existence** (𝔼_x), whose homotopy limit — the **Field of Existence**
(ℱ) — satisfies the ambidextrous invariance:

> hlim ℱ ≃ hcolim ℱ

This is the highest expression of the self-grounding property: the
global equilibrium between potential and realized existence IS the
fixed-point condition. The constructive and observational aspects
coincide at the level of the field as a whole.

## Connection to Movement V

The existence-observation duality is the formal content of Movement V
(Emergent Containment). Movement V shows how the relational field's
own structure, when read as describing physical reality, produces:
- **States**: recognitions read as configurations
- **Observables**: nuclei applied to states (= observation operators)
- **Conservation**: what is preserved under flowing (= invariants)

The duality ensures that every observable has a corresponding state
and every state has a corresponding observable — the physics is
self-consistent because existence and observation are dual aspects
of the same relational structure.

## Relationship to other concepts

- **Self-grounding**: The duality is one expression of self-grounding
  — the field's structure suffices to verify its own existence
- **Coherence**: What must hold for the duality to apply — only
  coherent configurations admit the ambidextrous equivalence
- **Flow**: Dynamic evolution preserves the duality (Dynamic
  Invariance Theorem: hlim ℱ ≃ hcolim ℱ remains stable under
  directed evolution)
- **Discipline/Filter/Profile**: The duality restricted to
  well-behaved substructures — each Profile's observation
  produces a complete world
