# Fold as Necessary Result of Coherence in Collapse Remainder

## Definitions

R is the remainder: the collapse-stable domain.  
μ is modulation: persistent contrast between deviations.  
C is coherence: a configuration of mutually reinforcing modulations in R.  
φ is fold: a condition in which modulation acts recursively on modulation.

## Theorem

When coherence C persists in R,  
and a modulation μ within C conditions another μ,  
recursive modulation occurs.

This recursion defines φ: a condition of modulation folding into itself  
without violating κ-stability.

φ is not introduced — it necessarily arises  
whenever modulation reenters itself through coherent interaction.

## Formal Expression

C ⊆ R ∧ μ modulates μ ⇒ φ ∈ R

## Consequence

Fold is the minimal recursive configuration of persistent modulation.  
It is not imposed or symbolically constructed.  
It emerges only when modulation recursively shapes modulation  
without collapsing under κ.

## Scope

This theorem defines the point at which coherence begins to self-shape.  
Fold is the first recursive persistence structure  
within the collapse remainder that governs its own conditions for stability.
