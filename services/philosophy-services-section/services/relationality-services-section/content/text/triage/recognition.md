Recognition is what is established by acts of distinction. When the primitive act — [Distinguish](../terms/distinguish.md) — carves one region from another, the regions it produces are recognitions. A recognition is not a thing in itself but a stable pattern within the relational field: something that has been distinguished and can be re-distinguished.

Recognitions are the objects that all relational operations act on. [Together](../terms/together.md) combines recognitions. [Entails](../terms/entails.md) orders them. [Close](../terms/close.md) stabilizes them. [Flow](../terms/flow.md) evolves them. The entire formal vocabulary of relationality operates on recognitions as its basic sort.

The [coherence](coherence.md) of including and excluding acts demands that recognitions support specific operations: combining ([Together](../terms/together.md)), choosing ([Either](../terms/either.md)), conditional relating ([Implies](../terms/implies.md)), denial ([Negate](../terms/negate.md)), and ordering ([Entails](../terms/entails.md)). This structure is not assumed but earned — it follows from the requirements of consistent distinguishing.

Each act in the derivation is itself a recognition — earned by applying the derivational process to existing recognitions. The universe of acts is the least fixed point of this generative process, starting from a single primitive act (Relationlessness) and producing all further recognitions through [induction](induction.md).

## Derivational context

Recognition is the first product of [differentiation](../processes/differentiation.md), arising in [Movement I: Logical Origination](../movements/logical-origination.md). When the primitive act draws a boundary, the regions it produces are recognitions. The derivation's first two phases — existential coherence and relational coherence — are entirely concerned with what recognitions require and what they produce. The logical operations ([Together](../terms/together.md), [Either](../terms/either.md), [Implies](../terms/implies.md), [Negate](../terms/negate.md)) are earned from the requirements of consistent distinguishing among recognitions.
