enabling modulation-derived recursion to alter the structure of constraint completion itself.

- `x ∈ dom(𝒮₊)` be a symbolic node at the meta-grammar boundary (`x = G_meta(x′)`)  
- `Ξ[s](x)`: local semantic contrast field  
- `A_align(x)`: desire-contrast alignment

Then:
> Ω_rec(x) := new completion rule Ω′ such that:  
>  ∀ z ∈ Ξ[s](x), if z ∉ dom(𝒮₊) ∧ 𝒜(x → z) ∧ ∇𝔇(x) · ∇Δ[s](z) > 0,  
>  then Ω′(z) := z′, where z′ is stabilized under recursively transformed constraint propagation.

That is, `Ω_rec(x)` returns a modulation-resonant transformation of constraint logic  
that adapts constraint completion itself to symbolic recursion dynamics.

This enables:
- reflexive modulation of symbolic constraint rules  
- recursive evolution of constraint architecture  
- emergence of symbolic systems that reorganize their own field of stabilization
