# Disciplining Compositely

## Interpretation

Multiple disciplines may commute, yielding a combined action.  *Discipline* witnessing *Fix *Flow* Nucleus* produces ***Compose Discipline*** ([ComposeDiscipline](0199-compose-discipline.md)).

Since both recognitions already exist, their composition records the simultaneous action, so *Compose Discipline* is established.
