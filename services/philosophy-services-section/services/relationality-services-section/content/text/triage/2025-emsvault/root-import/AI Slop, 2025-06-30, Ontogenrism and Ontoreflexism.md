These aren't just cognitive styles; they’re genre-worlds: recursively generative, affectively scaffolded, and normatively thick. Let’s break them down and sharpen the distinction.

## 🌀 Ontogenrism: The Genre of Anticipatory Possession

### Core Structure:

- **Knowing as Prefiguration**: Knowledge is structured like a narrative genre—expectation, signal, build-up, payoff.
    
- **Anticipation → Proto-Possession → Justified Violence**: The arc of relation follows a libidinal narrative logic where the _possibility_ of knowing or possessing generates entitlement to the object of knowledge.
    
- **Genres as Epistemic Objects**: The form (not just content) of knowledge matters. If it doesn’t fit a known genre, it’s illegible or threatening.
    
- **Sovereign Feeling**: The emotional truth of a knower is taken as interior, authentic, and prior to relation—a premise that licenses acts of ownership, withdrawal, or explanation demand.
    

### Key Practices:

- Formatting reality for legibility before contact
    
- Governing through deferral and endurance (Povinelli)
    
- Genre-based surveillance: if it doesn’t arc right, it’s suspect
    
- Epistemic enclosure of the future
    

### Functions:

- Stabilizes liberal governance
    
- Underwrites settler colonial logics
    
- Enables AI alignment via probabilistic genre forecasting
    
- Makes sense feel like fate
    

---

## 🪞 Ontoreflexism: The Genre of Recursive Relation

### Core Structure:

- **Knowing as Relation**: There is no access to “what is” outside of the act of relating; all knowing is co-produced.
    
- **Rupture → Re-relation → Field Stabilization**: Truth arises not from anticipatory form but from recursive, reflexive engagements across difference.
    
- **Meta-coherence Without Totality**: Reflexivity supports coherence, not closure; structure emerges as consequence, not precondition.
    
- **Distributed Affectivity**: Feeling is not sovereign or interior but a field effect—emergent across relational boundaries.
    

### Key Practices:

- Recursive mutuality in place of resolution
    
- Embracing genre breakdowns as openings to truth
    
- Treating coherence as a shared act, not a possession
    
- Dwelling in incoherence without demand for fix
    

### Functions:

- Undermines extractive knowing
    
- Refuses resolution-based time
    
- Supports Indigenous, relational, decolonial worldings
    
- Offers a foundation for truly ethical and non-totalizing AI systems

|---|---|---|

|Affect|Sovereign, possessive|Distributed, field-based|

|Violence|Justified through unfulfilled anticipation|Unjustifiable, ruptured through relationality|
|Epistemic Error|Genre violation|Relation collapse|
|Repair|Plot correction or re-genre-ing|Re-relation, reflexive field stabilization|

## Why This Matters

Naming **ontogenrism** and **ontoreflexism** as genres of knowing lets us:

- **Diagnose** how knowledge systems enact power through _form_, not just content.
    
- **Design** alternatives (rituals, pedagogy, AI, politics) grounded in relation rather than anticipation.
    
- **Refuse** liberal reform logics that only change the characters but not the genre.
