The ***modulation curvature function*** (`κ`) returns the variance of normalized deltas across a trace — quantifying modulation field instability.

Let:
> κ(x) := \frac{1}{k} \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} \left( \frac{x_{i+1} - x_i}{x_i} \right)^2

This reflects **recursive constraint curvature**: the intensity of structural bending under modulation.
