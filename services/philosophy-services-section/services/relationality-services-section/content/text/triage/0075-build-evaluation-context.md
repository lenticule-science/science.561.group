## Interpretation

The seed isolates one frame but not the recognition gathering all frames.  Therefore *Tail Trace* witnesses *Seed Evaluation Context*, producing ***Build Evaluation Context*** ([BuildEvaluationContext](0075-build-evaluation-context.md)).

The composition records the family of evaluation frames, so *Build Evaluation Context* follows.
