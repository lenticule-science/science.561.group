## Interpretation

Dually, we require the maximal recognition that witnesses every trace.  *Recognition* witnessing *Complete Trace* produces ***Cap Recognition*** ([CapRecognition](0124-cap-recognition.md)).

The act uses existing recognitions and records the trivial recognition that all traces satisfy, so *Cap Recognition* is valid.
