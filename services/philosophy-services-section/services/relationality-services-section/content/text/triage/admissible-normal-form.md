Canonical normal forms on admissible morphisms (`Adm`), earned by strong
normalization and reducibility acts.

## Components

- Rewrite relation `⇒` on `Adm` generated by eta/beta/tau-style steps from the
  normalization acts.
- Well-founded measure on `Adm` ensuring termination of `⇒`.
- Confluence up to coherent cells, yielding unique normal forms in the 1-truncation.
- Normal-form map `N∞ : Adm → Adm` sending each admissible morphism to its
  canonical normal representative.

## Proof

Sketch: use `NormalizeStrongly`, `NormalizeSyntax`, and `SummarizeReduction` to
define the rewrite relation on `Adm` (eta/beta/tau steps). Show termination via
the earned measure and confluence via critical-pair joins, yielding unique
normal forms. Define `N∞` accordingly.
