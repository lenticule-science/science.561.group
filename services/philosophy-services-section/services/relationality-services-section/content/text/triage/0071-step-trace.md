## Interpretation

A single reduction step exists, but we need to see its effect along the entire trace.  Hence *Complete Trace* witnesses *Reduce*, producing ***Step Trace*** ([StepTrace](0071-step-trace.md)).

The composition records the propagation of reduction steps through the trace, so *Step Trace* is earned.
