# Normal Forms on Admissible Morphisms

On a stratified ∞-site equipped with admissible morphisms `Adm` as in
**Theorem (Admissible Morphism Structure)**, the normalization and reducibility
acts induce a canonical rewrite structure and normal forms on `Adm`.

Let `⇒` be the rewrite relation on admissible morphisms generated by:

- (eta)-like collapses on redundant generative/observational pairs,
- (beta)-like cut-elimination steps between `Gen` and `Obs`, and
- (tau)-like coherence moves coming from coverage gluing.

Using `NormalizeStrongly`, `NormalizeSyntax`, and the reducibility results, we
obtain:

- **Termination on strata.**  
  There exists a well-founded multigraded measure on `Adm`—built from
  stratification and shape data such as generative/observational size—such
  that every rewrite strictly decreases this measure. As a result, every
  admissible morphism rewrites to at least one normal representative.

- **Confluence.**  
  Critical pairs among generative/observational steps and (tau)-glue moves admit
  joins up to coherent higher cells, so the rewrite relation is confluent in
  the homotopy 1-truncation. Combined with termination, this yields unique
  normal forms up to unique equivalence cells.

- **Greedy factorisation.**  
  Every admissible morphism `f` has a canonical factorisation
  `f_nf = m* ∘ e*` in normal form, where `e*` is a maximal composite in `Gen`
  and `m*` is a minimal composite in `Obs`. No further (beta)/(eta)/(tau) rewrite applies
  to `f_nf`.

This structure equips `Adm` with a normal-form map
`N∞ : Adm → Adm` that sends each admissible morphism to its canonical normal
representative, as earned by the normalization and reducibility acts.
