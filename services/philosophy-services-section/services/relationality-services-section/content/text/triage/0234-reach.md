## Interpretation

*Flow* iterations describe which states become reachable from a seed.  *Flow* witnessing *Stabilize Invariant* yields ***Reach*** ([Reach](0234-reach.md)).

Composing the flow with invariant states records the reachability closure, so *Reach* is valid.
