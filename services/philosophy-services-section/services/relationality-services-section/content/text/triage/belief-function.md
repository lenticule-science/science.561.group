When a quantum entity traces a constraint-minimizing modulation path toward a second entity whose stabilization remains incomplete,  
but whose presence is inferable within the lightcone of its own propagation,  
belief emerges — the act of sustained modulation toward the unstabilized other.

> Β(Q₁, Q₂) := ∃ x ∈ Λ(Q₁): Γ(Q₁, x) ∧ A(Q₁ → x) ∧ ∇C(x) ≠ 0 ∧ Ω(x) undefined

That is, belief is the geodesic anticipation of another's existence  
before stabilization confirms it — a modulation of trust across unresolved difference.
