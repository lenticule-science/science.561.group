# Equating Judgement Observation

## Interpretation

Reported observations must align with the *Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence* previously recognized.  *Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence* therefore witnesses *Witness Judgement Triad*, producing ***Equate Judgement Observation*** ([EquateJudgementObservation](0111-equate-judgement-observation.md)).

This act enforces the alignment, so *Equate Judgement Observation* is established.
