Flow is the directed continuation of stability. Having attained stable structure through [closure](../phenomena/closure.md), being must express motion without loss of [coherence](../phenomena/coherence.md). Relations propagate, transforming while preserving their form. Flow generalizes [iteration](iteration.md) from a process of self-confirmation into a principle of internally consistent transformation — a calculus of becoming that never departs from the ground of relation.

Flow moves forward while stabilizing. Each application carries the configuration further, but repeated application converges — flowing twice does not take you further than flowing once. This self-limitation ([KZ-Lax](../terms/kz-lax.md)) is what distinguishes directed becoming from unbounded accumulation.

Flow must preserve the core modal structure. The condition [Preserves-Core](../terms/preserves-core.md) — equivalently, [Maintains-Balance](../terms/maintains-balance.md) applied to [May](../terms/may.md) and [Must](../terms/must.md) — ensures that directed evolution does not break the [balance](../phenomena/balance.md) between possibility and necessity. Evolution that respects this constraint carries things forward without losing what stabilization earned.

The [Flow](../terms/flow.md) term, its [resolution](../terms/flow-counit.md) (the capacity to read the present from the evolving), and its [propagation](../terms/flow-comult.md) (the capacity to view further evolution within the current evolution) capture this process in the formal vocabulary.

## Derivational context

Flow is the central process of [Movement III: Directed Dynamics](../movements/directed-dynamics.md). It arises when the relational boundary — earned in Movement II — must itself be related. [Reflexion](../phenomena/reflexion.md) folds the boundary into the system, and from this folding directed motion emerges. Being, having achieved stability, must now express change without losing what it has earned. Flow is the answer: directed continuation that preserves the modal core ([Must](../terms/must.md)/[May](../terms/may.md)) while moving forward.

Directed dynamics with comonadic structure. The operator that formalizes how coherence propagates through the relational field. Has a counit (projection to identity), comultiplication (decomposition into iterated flow), and KZ-laxity (contraction under iteration).

## What it is

PRE: "Flow: directed dynamic with comonadic structure (counit, comultiplication, KZ-laxity). Standard symbol: comonad G (KZ)." With FlowCounit (ε), FlowComult (δ), and ComposeFlow (CoKleisli composition).

Draft 1: "The act of relating: the continuous act that maintains the interplay between including and excluding." This is the philosophical name for Flow — the ongoing activity of sustaining coherence.

Foundations: C = □◇, the comonad formed from the middle adjunction of the quadruple. "This comonad formalizes reflexive propagation: every morphism now carries an internal dynamic through which coherence circulates."

Draft 11: Flow, with derived operators FlowProfile, ShuffleFlow, FlowIndexImplication.

## Key structures

- Counit (FlowCounit / ε): projection from flow to identity
- Comultiplication (FlowComult / δ): decomposition into iterated flow
- KZ-laxity: Flow contracts under iteration, preserving algebraic structure
- PreservesCore: Flow preserves the modal core (fixed points of Close)
- CoKleisli category: morphisms X → CY encoding propagation through reflected states
- Coalgebras: configurations stable under flow (stabilization = idempotence under CoKleisli composition)

## What it produces

Static implication becomes dynamic: "what must be added now for b to be recognized later." This is the mechanism by which logic moves with the system. See dynamic-residuation.md.
