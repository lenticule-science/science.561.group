# Idea: Dreams, Inner Work, and Therapy Notes

Treat dreams and inner work as recognitions so patterns and shifts are
traceable without flattening experience.

## 1. Recognitions

- `DreamRecognition`: narrative, symbols, emotions.
- `SessionRecognition`: therapy sessions, journaling rounds, inner dialogues.
- `ThemeRecognition`: recurring motifs (abandonment, safety, transformation).
- `InsightRecognition`: moments of understanding or reframe.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - links between dreams, themes, life events, symptoms.
- Measurement:
  - frequency/intensity of themes, mood/energy trends, sleep quality.

This lets you explore inner patterns relationally, with strong privacy policies
and local-only storage if desired.
