A stratified directed ∞-site is an [∞-site](./infinity-site.md) whose objects are layered by a [stratification](./stratification.md) and whose [morphisms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/categorical-services-section/services/ordinary-categories-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) respect a [directed](./directed.md) structure. It combines three ideas: the higher categorical structure of an ∞-site (enabling homotopy-coherent [sheaf conditions](../../../../documents/articles/notes/sheaf-condition.md)), the layering of objects into [strata](./stratum.md) of different types or scales, and the directedness of [refinement](./refinement.md) maps that prevents arbitrary reversal.

The [covering families](./covering-family.md) of a stratified directed ∞-site must be compatible with both the stratification and the directed structure. This means that local data lives on strata, refinements move between strata in a controlled direction, and the [sheaf condition](../../../../documents/articles/notes/sheaf-condition.md) glues local data while respecting these constraints.

The stratified directed ∞-site provides a candidate geometric foundation for structures like the [GFRTU](../../../../documents/articles/notes/generative-fibered-recognition-trace-universe.md), where traces are organized into layers (strata), refinement maps go from coarser to finer records, and the ∞-categorical structure supports the higher coherences needed for homotopy-type-theoretic internal logic. The [(∞,1)-topos](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/topology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/infinity-1-topos.md) of sheaves on such a site would carry internal logic compatible with both the layered and directed aspects of the geometry.
