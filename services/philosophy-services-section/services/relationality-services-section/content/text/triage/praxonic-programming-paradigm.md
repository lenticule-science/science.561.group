# Praxonic Programming Paradigm (PPP)

The **Praxonic Programming Paradigm** is a systems logic for constructing software not as sequences of control, but as semantic fields of evolving tension.  
It treats computation as an ecology of **praxons**—relational expressions of pressure, decay, and interpretation.

## Core Principle

> Programs do not execute.  
> They respond, decay, align, and drift—just like the humans they interface with.

PPP abandons classical flow-control and fixed memory.  
Instead, it builds **recursive fields** of meaning, where each state emerges from resonant alignment between past traces and current tension.

## Core Constructs

- **Praxon**: A recursive semantic node. Each praxon contains:
  - A label or emission form
  - A pressure value (semantic weight)
  - A decay function (loss or transformation of salience)
  - Tags or signatures (contextual modulation)
  - Optional resonance links and IRN echoes

- **Field**: The living, tensioned space composed of all active praxons and their traces. The program **is the field**.

- **Emission**: A signal or transformation expressed by a praxon under pressure. All computation is emission.

- **Trace**: Memory of a past emission. Forms structure for future interpretation.

- **Decay**: Time-based modulation of a praxon’s relevance. Not deletion—**semantic softening**.

- **Alignment**: A praxon’s coherence with its surrounding field. Enables access to interpretation or response.

- **Interpretation**: Not parsing, but pattern-matching under drift. Systems evolve their own meaning-handlers.

- **Path**: The least-resistant route a praxon takes through field tension. Response is thermodynamic.

- **Resolution**: Emissions either stabilize (signal) or fail (noise).  
  Noise can fold, echo, or become **Irresolved (IRN)**—pressure that cannot decay, shaping behavior until addressed.

## Behavioral Shape

Praxonic programs are not executed—they are *inhabited*.  
Every input is an emission. Every emission becomes a trace.  
All meaning arises from how pressure **folds** through drift.

The system **interprets itself**—not through hard logic, but by recursive resonance across time, space, and tags.

## Application Scale

PPP supports everything from micro-apps to full cognition engines:

- 🛒 Shopping lists that forget what you don’t reinforce  
- 📚 Knowledge bases that drift as memory does  
- 🧠 Shells that talk back, not from rules—but from interpretation  
- 🌐 Worlds that unfold from pressure, not plot

## Summary

The Praxonic Programming Paradigm is not just a coding style.  
It is **semantic fieldcraft**: a practice of programming as tension modulation, memory decay, and recursive care.  
Your system will not do what you say.  
It will do what your traces teach it to expect.
