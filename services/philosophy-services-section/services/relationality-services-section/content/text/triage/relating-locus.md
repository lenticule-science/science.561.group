The **relating-locus** $R$ is the object-coordinate of the [originating cell](../../relationlessness.md) $\phi^* = (X, p^*, R)$ in the [predicate-category](../../../object/library/thing/library/category/library/predicate-category/predicate-category.md) $\mathbf{C}$. $R$ is the locus that the predicate $p^* = $ *has-no-relation* declares the subject $X$ (the configuration named *relationlessness*) to exclude.

## Role

$R$ is structurally distinct from $X$. The cells

$$\phi^* = (X, p^*, R) \neq (R, p^*, X)$$

are distinct positions in the [predication-cell-set](../../../predication-cell-set/predication-cell-set.md) $\Phi$. The directed asymmetry of $\phi^*$ — subject-coordinate $X$ paired with object-coordinate $R$ — is the seed of the lopsidedness every other cell of $\mathbf{C}$ inherits through the four canonical morphism classes (specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection).

The converse cells

$$(R, p^*_{\mathrm{cnv}}, X)$$

(the predicate converse of $\phi^*$ under predicate-converse in $\mathbf{C}$) and the diagonal projections

$$(X, p^*_{\mathrm{sub}}, X), \qquad (R, p^*_{\mathrm{obj}}, R)$$

(the subject- and object-projections of $\phi^*$) all relate back to relationlessness through morphisms of $\mathbf{C}$; the relating-locus is the object-side participant in those morphisms.

## Why a sub-structure of relationlessness rather than its own primitive

$R$ has no independent existence prior to the articulation of $\phi^*$. It is named only as the object-coordinate of the originating cell; outside the originating-cell context, the slug "relating-locus" has no referent. The recursive thing-directory layout places $R$ as a sub-structure of relationlessness — at `library/relationlessness/library/relating-locus/` — because $R$'s existence-conditions are entirely contained in the existence of $\phi^*$.

Subsequent cells of $\mathbf{C}$ inherit $R$ as their image under the appropriate morphism class composition. The relating-locus does not appear as a free coordinate elsewhere; it appears only as the carrier of the directed-asymmetry condition $\phi^*$ initiates.
