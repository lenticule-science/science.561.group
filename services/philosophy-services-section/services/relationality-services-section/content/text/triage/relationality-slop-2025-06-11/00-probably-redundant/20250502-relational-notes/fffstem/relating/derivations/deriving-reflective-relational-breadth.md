- [summary](summary):: This derivation introduces the act of [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect](../acts/reflect.md), which relates a structure to another while preserving its own relational context. This induces the condition of [relational distinction](relational distinction) and forms the structure of [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/reflective relational breadth](../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/reflective relational breadth).

## Claim

If a structure can relate to itself via [../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex), then it must also be able to relate to something **distinct** from itself — while retaining awareness of itself as the origin of relation. This requires a new act and induces a new structural axis: **relational breadth**.

## Derivation

### Postulate

> If a structure `A` exists, and `A ≠ B`, and `relating(A, B)` is permitted, then a new act is required that relates `A` to `B` *while maintaining `A`'s structural context*. This act — [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect](../acts/reflect.md) — induces horizontal relational differentiation: **breadth**.

### Steps

1. Begin with `R(0)`, the [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating) — containing `relating`, `unrelating`, and `relating(relating, unrelating)`.
2. From `R(0)` we derived `reflex(R(0)) := relating(R(0), R(0))` — vertical depth.
3. Now consider two distinct structures: `S := R(n)`, `T := R(m)` where `n ≠ m`.
4. Define a new act:
   `reflect(S, T) := relating(S, T)`
   **under the condition that `S ≠ T` and that the relation preserves S’s identity.**
5. This is not unqualified `relating` — it is **relating with distinction**:
   - `S` acts upon `T`
   - But `S` is not collapsed into `T`
6. This requires the condition of [relational distinction](relational distinction):
   - The relation occurs across difference
   - The source structure (`S`) remains intact

7. By applying `reflect` across pairs of distinct structures in a shared space, a horizontal structure emerges:
   - `reflect(R(0), R(1))`
   - `reflect(R(0), R(2))`
   - `reflect(R(1), R(2))`
   - etc.

This forms a **lateral structure of active, non-collapsing relations** — a **breadth** of differentiated relational acts, each preserving their origin and direction.

## Formalism

Let:

- `R(n)`, `R(m)` ∈ reflexive relational depth
- `n ≠ m`
- `reflect(S, T) := relating(S, T)` under constraint: `S ≠ T` and `S` is preserved

Then:

$$
\text{reflective relational breadth} := \{ reflect(R(i), R(j)) \mid i ≠ j \}
$$

This structure is induced by difference-preserving relation between distinct layers.
