- [summary](summary):: **Reflexive relational depth** is the structure formed by applying [../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex) repeatedly to a [relational structure](relational structure), producing a layered sequence of self-relations.

This structure arises from the act of a structure [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating) to itself, once, then again, and again — each time generating a deeper layer of internal inclusion.

## Members

R(0): domain of relating
R(1): reflex(R(0))
R(2): reflex(R(1))
...
R(n): reflex(R(n–1))

## Properties

- Ordered by depth: each R(n) contains all previous layers.
- Induced, not assumed: arises from repeated application of [../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../../../../../../../../../../triage/relations/reflex).
- Enables structural position: depth becomes a relational coordinate.

## Expression

reflexive relational depth := { R(0), R(1), R(2), … }
where R(n+1) := reflex(R(n))
