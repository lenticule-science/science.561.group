So I was thinking about [[id:6fcbf1ea-4e4f-4210-a4eb-a5cacab5dc20][Lakotaean philosophy]] and specifically [[id:9d1c6af9-60ce-427e-ad2c-318a68e88f29][rock people]], who are [[id:d7649d6a-66cc-4efb-b20f-5f2b960bcab8][known]] to [[id:e2de581c-f4e8-403b-aeb7-5fcbccc1d1df][sense]] and [[id:ef3433a0-d9d2-453b-82de-f5b2afe9bfa6][remember]] the [[id:81791140-d846-43cc-88b0-f66f951b3576][relations]] of those [[id:ecbaf64a-b85c-44a7-9337-069e562112f6][relatives]] they are related to.

Anyway, I was thinking about this and realized, that it might be cool for a [[id:de5cda39-6fa8-4f17-a11e-3c6aca2b66e8][MUD engine]] to use [[id:78d17d43-2a5a-40a0-b0c7-6846b303d8b4][stones]] as [[id:42cdf664-5d8c-4337-a3b9-2b3625cd48ca][omniscient]], and then admins can query stones for what the stone knows about whatever. Any stone that can answer can answer, and the admin-ness of an admin is defined by how far they can query into the stone-network. (Stones can perfectly transmit all knowledge between all stones they encounter, instantly, basically - though that language doesn't reflect [[id:a48dbfa8-e338-4cab-9259-8467271c95f6][Lakotaean metaphysics]].)
