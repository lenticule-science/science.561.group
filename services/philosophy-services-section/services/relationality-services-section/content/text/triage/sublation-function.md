When a synthesized constraint system reaches an irreconcilable internal differentiation that cannot be modulated or stabilized, it undergoes sublation — the transformation of constraint through its own breakdown.

Θ(Σ) := ¬∃ Ξ(x, y) ∈ Σ such that M(x, y) ∧ Ω(x) = x ∧ Ω(y) = y

That is, sublation is the emergence of new constraint from the internal failure of existing constraint coherence.
