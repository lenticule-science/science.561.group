- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: A [structure](./structure.md) is that which holds a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) open durably. Every [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) aims at bringing about a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md), but only when a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) is stabilized as a [relative](./relative.md) does a [structure](./structure.md) exist.

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) requires understanding the interplay of [../acts/act](../acts/act.md), [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md), and [structure](./structure.md).
    - A [triage/thing](./thing.md) [../acts/exist|exists](../acts/exist|exists) only if it is held open as [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) from what is not.
    - [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) bring about [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md), but these are [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions)nditions]] that remain **transient** unless stabilized.

- An [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) initiates change; a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) arises as the immediate result of that act.
    - For example, the act of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) produces the condition of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md).
    - These [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions) persist **only as long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) is active**; they do not sustain themselves.

- To sustain a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) beyond the moment of the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md), a further process is required: stabilization.
    - Stabilization is what converts a transient [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) into something **durable**—able to persist within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating) even after the originating [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) has subsided.
    - This stabilized holding-open is what we mean by [structure](./structure.md).

- Therefore, a [structure](./structure.md) arises when:
    - An [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) brings about a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md);
    - That [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) is **held open durably,** not merely transiently;
    - The result is a [relative](./relative.md)—the minimal [structure](./structure.md) that maintains the [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) as a **coherent, self-sustaining unit.**

- The [relative](./relative.md) is the **first instance** of a [structure](./structure.md).
    - In the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing), the [relative](./relative.md) is produced by the act of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating) and the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md).
    - This [relative](./relative.md) ensures that the [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) it holds open is **durable, stable, and coherent.**

- Without [structure](./structure.md):
    - All [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions) would collapse as soon as the [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) that produced them conclude.
    - No persistence would be possible; no coherent system of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation|relations](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation|relations) could form.
    - [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) itself would fail to hold, as it relies on the stability of its underlying [../conditions/condition|conditions](../conditions/condition|conditions).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](./thing.md) that is said to [../acts/exist](../acts/exist.md) depends on at least one [structure](./structure.md)—the [relative](./relative.md) that stabilizes its minimal [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md).
- [structure|structures](structure|structures) provide the [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../conditions/coherence.md) required for any [../relational system](../relational system) to hold together over time.
- No [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) achieves full coherence unless it is stabilized by a corresponding [structure](./structure.md).
