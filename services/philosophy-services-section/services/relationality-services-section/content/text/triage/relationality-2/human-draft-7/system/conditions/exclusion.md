- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: [exclusion](./exclusion.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) brought about by the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding). It specifies that a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is held out of what is, marking it as absent within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md):
    - Be included in what it is (producing [inclusion](./inclusion.md));
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [exclusion](./exclusion.md)).

- The [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) establishes the [condition](./condition.md) of [exclusion](./exclusion.md):
    - This [condition](./condition.md) holds the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) outside of the field of what is;
    - It is not merely a lack of presence but an **actively maintained absence**;
    - [exclusion](./exclusion.md) is therefore a **positive condition**—it marks and holds the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) as not within.

- [exclusion](./exclusion.md) is transient unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md):
    - As long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) is active, [exclusion](./exclusion.md) persists;
    - To achieve durability, [exclusion](./exclusion.md) must be stabilized within a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) or other [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

- [exclusion](./exclusion.md) gains its coherence by being held together with [inclusion](./inclusion.md):
    - This holding induces [tension](./tension.md);
    - [tension](./tension.md) ensures that [exclusion](./exclusion.md) is not isolated or meaningless but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [inclusion](./inclusion.md).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) that [../acts/exist|exists](../acts/exist|exists) is conditioned by both [inclusion](./inclusion.md) and [exclusion](./exclusion.md);
- [exclusion](./exclusion.md) is essential for the maintenance of [difference](./difference.md):
    - Without [exclusion](./exclusion.md), no [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) can be marked as distinct from what is not;
- [exclusion](./exclusion.md) participates in the basic [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) that sustain [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) and all further relational architectures.
