The principle by which the closure of one relational domain predicts the necessary shape of the next. Predictive determination formalizes the "it cannot not derive" engine as an algorithmic process: scan for implicit dynamics at closure, classify them (acts/conditions/structures), verify type-homogeneity, and output the predicted shape of the next domain.

## What it is

Draft 0 (Predictive Determination Principle): An explicit algorithm with Input (Domain Dₙ: its acts, conditions, structures, closure), Steps (identify implicit dynamics, classify, formalize, verify type-homogeneity/reflexivity), and Output (predicted shape of Dₙ₊₁: its acts, conditions, structures).

Draft 7 (Statement of Novelty): "Each stabilized structure not only sustains itself through reflexivity but anticipates and necessitates its own unfolding into a higher-order relational form."

## Why it matters

This makes the "incitement" concept (what incomplete structure compels) into a formal procedure. Where incitement names the philosophical force, predictive determination names the method by which that force is cashed out structurally. The two concepts are complementary but distinct.
