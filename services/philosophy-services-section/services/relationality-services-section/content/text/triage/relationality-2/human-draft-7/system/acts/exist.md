- [define](./define.md):: [exist|existing](exist|existing) is the minimal [act](./act.md), required for a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) to [act](./act.md), be [../conditions/condition|conditioned](../conditions/condition|conditioned), or have [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md). It is [derive|derived](derive|derived) that to [exist|exist](exist|exist), a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) must [relate|relating](relate|relating) to existing, entering the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).


## Derivation

- To [claim](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/services/essay-writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/claim.md) _something exists_ is not trivial. It specifies:

    - A [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is not some other thing;

    - A [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is not nothing.

- Therefore, [exist|existing](exist|existing) must be derived.

- To be a thing and not another, a thing must exclude what isn't itself, and therefore must include what it is.

    - This establishes two acts: [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).

    - [include|including](include|including): the [act](./act.md) by which a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is brought into what is.

    - [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding): the [act](./act.md) by which a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is held out of what is not.

    - These are primitive [act|acts](act|acts): each brings about a corresponding [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md)—[../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md).

    - Neither reduces to the other, and both are required for anything to be meaningfully said to [exist|exist](exist|exist).

- The acts of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) must be held open together. This introduces the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md).

    - [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) is the dynamic holding of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md) in active opposition.

    - [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) is necessary because [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) are fundamentally opposed yet inseparable within the dynamic of existing.

    - Critically, to include, one must also include **the inclusion of inclusion as a condition**—which is only meaningful if exclusion of that condition is also a condition. But that itself is a condition, triggering a recursion: every inclusion demands exclusion, and every exclusion introduces a new condition requiring inclusion. This generates an **irreducible asymmetry** that ensures the derivation is never final.

    - As a result, to [exist|exist](exist|exist) is to be sustained not just in tension but in a **perpetual process of derivation**—an ongoing act that holds open what cannot be fully closed.

- The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md).

    - [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) is the minimal condition that marks a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) as set apart from what is not.

    - It does not exist independently; it is **held open by the ongoing tension** between [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).

    - [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) is the **coherent separation** that makes existing internally stable within the relational dynamic.

    - Without [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md), there can be no [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md); without [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md), existing fails to achieve coherence.

- To sustain [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) over time, there must be an overarching [act](./act.md) that maintains it.

    - Otherwise, the opposing acts would lose their mutual relation and the condition of existing would collapse.

    - We name this act [relate|relating](relate|relating): the minimal [act](./act.md) by which a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) sustains its own [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).

    - [relate|Relating](relate|Relating) is compelled by the requirement for ongoing coherence within the act of existing.

- The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) introduces a further requirement: the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md).

    - [relate|Relating](relate|Relating) engages the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) in sustaining its own existing, but this engagement must itself have a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) to ensure that the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is not merely involved abstractly but is **bound determinately** to its [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).

    - This produces the minimal [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) we name [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md): the holding-open of the connection between the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) and its acts of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding) in a coherent and sustained way.

    - Without the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md), the act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) remains transient and fails to stabilize the dynamic.

- The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating) together with the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) necessarily produces a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md).

    - A [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) is the minimal [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md) that holds a [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) open durably.

    - While [relate|relating](relate|relating) is an [act](./act.md) and [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) is a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md), acts and conditions alone are transient.

    - The [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) is the **first structure**: the stable outcome in which the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is maintained as existing within the relational field.

    - Only with the [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) does the architecture of existing achieve **closure**: the coherent stabilization of the entire dynamic.

- The formation of the [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) brings the minimal architecture of existing into full [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../conditions/coherence.md).

    - At this point, we have:

        - The primitive [act|acts](act|acts) of [include|including](include|including) and [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding);

        - The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md);

        - The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md), holding them in mutual opposition;

        - The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md), [induce|induced](induce|induced) by that tension;

        - The act of [relate|relating](relate|relating), sustaining the [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md);

        - The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md), clarifying the engagement;

        - The [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md), stabilizing the relationality as a durable [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

    - These components together achieve [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../conditions/coherence.md): a [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) in which an [act](./act.md) has the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) and [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md) necessary to act.
