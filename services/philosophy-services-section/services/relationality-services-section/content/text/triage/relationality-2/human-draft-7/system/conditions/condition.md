- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: A [condition](./condition.md) is the immediate outcome of an [../acts/act](../acts/act.md). It is what is brought about, held open transiently, by the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) that produces it. Every [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md) holds a [condition](./condition.md) durably; every [relation](./relation.md) requires at least one [condition](./condition.md).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) shows that every [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) necessarily brings about a [condition](./condition.md).
    - For example, the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) brings about the [condition](./condition.md) of [inclusion](./inclusion.md);
    - The [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) brings about the [condition](./condition.md) of [exclusion](./exclusion.md).

- A [condition](./condition.md) is distinct from the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) that produces it.
    - An [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) is what **does**; a [condition](./condition.md) is what **is held open** by that doing.
    - The [condition](./condition.md) only persists **as long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) is active**, unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

- [condition|Conditions](condition|Conditions) are necessary because without them:
    - The effects of [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) would have no persistence, even transiently;
    - No differentiation between states could be identified or held open, even momentarily.

- In the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing):
    - [inclusion](./inclusion.md) and [exclusion](./exclusion.md) are the [condition|conditions](condition|conditions)nditions]] that arise from the primitive [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding);
    - [tension](./tension.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) that holds [inclusion](./inclusion.md) and [exclusion](./exclusion.md) in mutual opposition;
    - [difference](./difference.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) induced by that [tension](./tension.md), marking a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) as set apart from what is not;
    - [relation](./relation.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) that specifies the binding of the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) to its ongoing [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) via [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating).

- Thus, [condition|conditions](condition|conditions):
    - Arise **immediately** from [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts);
    - Are **maintained** transiently by those acts;
    - Require [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) to achieve durability and coherence.

## Implications

- No [condition](./condition.md) can arise independently of an [../acts/act](../acts/act.md).
- No [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md) can exist without holding at least one [condition](./condition.md) durably.
- [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) depend on a layered interaction:
    - [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) bring about [condition|conditions](condition|conditions);
    - [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) hold those [condition|conditions](condition|conditions) open durably, forming the basis for further [relation|relations](relation|relations).
- The minimal architecture of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) necessarily includes multiple [condition|conditions](condition|conditions): [inclusion](./inclusion.md), [exclusion](./exclusion.md), [tension](./tension.md), [difference](./difference.md), and [relation](./relation.md).
