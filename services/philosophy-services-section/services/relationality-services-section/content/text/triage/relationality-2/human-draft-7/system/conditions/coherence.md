- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: [coherence](./coherence.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) in which an [../acts/act](../acts/act.md), its corresponding [condition|conditions](condition|conditions), and supporting [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures) hold together in a stable, self-sufficient dynamic. It is what marks that a relational system is **complete enough** to persist as a real part of the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).


## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) shows:

    - A [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is brought about by the primitive [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding);

    - These generate [condition|conditions](condition|conditions) of [inclusion](./inclusion.md) and [exclusion](./exclusion.md);

    - Held together by the [condition](./condition.md) of [tension](./tension.md), which induces [difference](./difference.md);

    - Sustained by [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating), which maintains the dynamic;

    - Clarified and stabilized through [relation](./relation.md) and the formation of a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) as a minimal [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

- Each of these elements is necessary but not sufficient on its own:

    - [../acts/act|Acts](../acts/act|Acts) do the work;

    - [condition|Conditions](condition|Conditions) are held open;

    - [../structures/structure|Structures](../structures/structure|Structures) stabilize and durably maintain the results.

- But for the system to be **self-sufficient**:

    - These elements must not only be present but also **held together as a unified whole**;

    - Their interplay must resolve without collapse, dissipation, or irresolvable contradiction.

- This unified holding-together is the [condition](./condition.md) of [coherence](./coherence.md):

    - It ensures that all necessary components of the relational system **mutually support** one another;

    - It marks the point at which the system achieves **closure**: nothing further is needed to sustain its being.


## Implications

- [coherence](./coherence.md) is the minimal indicator that a relational architecture is **real** within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating):

    - Without [coherence](./coherence.md), even if acts and conditions arise, they fail to persist meaningfully;

    - Coherence is what allows a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) to maintain its integrity over time.

- Any system lacking [coherence](./coherence.md):

    - Breaks down into unintelligibility or dissolution;

    - Cannot serve as a foundation for further [../acts/act|acts](../acts/act|acts) or [../structures/structure|structures](../structures/structure|structures).

- [coherence](./coherence.md) is what makes further derivations possible:

    - Once coherence is established at one level, new layers of [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) can be built upon it;

    - This recursive building is what allows complex relational systems to emerge from minimal foundations.
