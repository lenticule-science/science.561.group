- [define](./define.md):: An [act](./act.md) is the thing by which a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) brings about any [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) or [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md). Every [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) and every [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) requires an [act](./act.md) to [exist](./exist.md).

## Derivation

- [derive|deriving](derive|deriving) [exist|existing](exist|existing) derives [act|acts](act|acts)
    - Every claim of anything being the case involves something having been done.
- An [act](./act.md) is what introduces [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) into a field.
    - Without an [act](./act.md), there is no way to set apart what is from what is not.
    - This makes the [act](./act.md) a necessary condition for any [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) to be realized.
- Every [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) presupposes an [act](./act.md).
    - To [relate|relating](relate|relating) requires an [act](./act.md) that enacts the [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md).
    - Without an [act](./act.md), no [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) can be established, sustained, or held open.

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) that is [exist|existing](exist|existing) does so by way of at least one [act](./act.md).
- An [act](./act.md) is the point of minimal [induce|induction](induce|induction) for all [relational dynamics](relational dynamics).
- No [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) or [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md) arises without an [act](./act.md) that brings it into being.
