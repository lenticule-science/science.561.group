- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: [reflexivity](./reflexivity.md) is the minimal [condition](./condition.md) under which a [relational structure](relational structure) sustains its own [../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../acts/reflex.md) without alteration. It holds open the dynamic by which a [relational structure](relational structure) remains coherent when [related](related) to itself, ensuring that the operation of [reflexing](reflexing) neither introduces nor eliminates any relational determination already present.

## Derivation

- [reflexivity](./reflexivity.md) is [induce|induced](induce|induced) by the act of [reflexing](reflexing).

- To [../../../../../triage/relations/reflex](../acts/reflex.md) is to perform `relating(S, S)` for a [relational structure](relational structure) `S`.

- This act deepens the [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md), but for coherence, the [condition](./condition.md) of [reflexivity](./reflexivity.md) is required:

    - It holds the [relation](./relation.md) between `S` and `relating(S, S)` open as **internally stable.**

- Without [reflexivity](./reflexivity.md), the result of [reflexing](reflexing) would lack coherence, causing the [relational structure](relational structure) to fail closure under its own reflex.

- Therefore, [reflexivity](./reflexivity.md) is necessary for sustaining [reflexive](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/order-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/reflexive.md) acts as **structurally coherent operations** within the [../relational system](../relational system).
