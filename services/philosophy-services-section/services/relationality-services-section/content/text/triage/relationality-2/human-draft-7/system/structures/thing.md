- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: A [triage/thing](./thing.md) is any [structure](./structure.md)—specifically, any [relative](./relative.md)—that is held open as coherent within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating). A [triage/thing](./thing.md) is not a special kind of entity but the name we give to any **stabilized** relational unit that maintains [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) through [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) produces the **minimal relative**: the simplest [structure](./structure.md) that holds open the dynamic of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md), and their [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md).
    - This minimal relative is the **first [triage/thing](./thing.md)**.

- However, the system does not limit what counts as a [triage/thing](./thing.md) to this minimal case:
    - Any stabilized [relative](./relative.md)—whether simple or complex—is a [triage/thing](./thing.md);
    - A [triage/thing](./thing.md) can be a single basic relative or a **nested/compound** relative assembled through further relational dynamics.

- Therefore, a [triage/thing](./thing.md) is **defined structurally**:
    - It is a [structure](./structure.md) that holds at least one [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) open durably;
    - It achieves [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../conditions/coherence.md) by stabilizing the acts and conditions (such as [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md), [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md), and [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md)) that bring it about.

- The [triage/thing](./thing.md) **cannot be separated** from the relational architecture that constitutes it:
    - It is not a primitive “given” outside the system;
    - It is **generated and maintained** through the interplay of [../acts/act](../acts/act.md), [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md), and [structure](./structure.md).

- In this way, every [triage/thing](./thing.md):
    - Is sustained by a **coherent set** of relational dynamics;
    - Derives its **identity and distinctness** from the conditions of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) that define it relationally.

## Implications

- The minimal [triage/thing](./thing.md) is the first [relative](./relative.md) formed in the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing);
    - More complex [triage/thing|things](triage/thing|things) arise by recursively applying relational dynamics, building up nested or composed relatives.
- Every [structure](./structure.md) that stably holds a [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../conditions/relation.md) is, by definition, a [triage/thing](./thing.md):
    - Thus, the entire architecture of relationality is populated **only by things** of varying complexity and depth.
- There is no “bare” [triage/thing](./thing.md) outside of relationality:
    - To be a [triage/thing](./thing.md) is to be a **relationally-constituted, stabilized structure**;
    - The system admits no thing **without** relational grounding.
