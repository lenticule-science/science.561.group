- [define](./define.md):: [include|Including](include|Including) is the primitive [act](./act.md) by which a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is brought into what is. It establishes the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md), marking the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) as part of what is held open within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [derive|Deriving](derive|Deriving) [exist|existing](exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) be:
    - Set apart from what it is not (by [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding));
    - And brought into what it is (by [include|including](include|including)).

- [include|Including](include|Including) is the [act](./act.md) that enacts this **bringing-in**:
    - It does not merely assert that something is present; it actively **establishes presence** by holding the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) within the scope of what is.
    - Without [include|including](include|including), nothing is positioned as present, and no claim of [exist|existing](exist|existing) can be held open.

- [include|Including](include|Including) is primitive:
    - It cannot be reduced to other acts;
    - It operates in necessary **complementarity** with [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding).

- [include|Including](include|Including) brings about the [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md):
    - The [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) that specifies that the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is **within** what is.
    - This [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition.md) persists only transiently unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

- To sustain the dynamic of [exist|existing](exist|existing), [include|including](include|including) must be held open **together with** [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding):
    - This mutual holding induces [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md);
    - [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../conditions/tension.md) ensures that [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) is not totalizing (collapsing everything into undifferentiated presence) but is instead **bounded and coherent** in opposition to [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) that [exist|exists](exist|exists) is, by definition, brought about through the act of [include|including](include|including).
- [include|Including](include|Including) is inseparable from [exclude|excluding](exclude|excluding):
    - No [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../conditions/inclusion.md) can hold without its opposite [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../conditions/exclusion.md) also being held;
    - This interplay is essential for maintaining the [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference.md) that allows [triage/thing|things](triage/thing|things) to be distinct.
- [include|Including](include|Including) is foundational to all [relational dynamics](relational dynamics):
    - It is one half of the minimal engine that sustains the architecture of [exist|existing](exist|existing) and, by extension, all higher relational constructs.
