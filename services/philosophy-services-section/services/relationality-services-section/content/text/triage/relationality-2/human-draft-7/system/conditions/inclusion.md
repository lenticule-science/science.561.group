- [../acts/define](../acts/define.md):: [inclusion](./inclusion.md) is the [condition](./condition.md) brought about by the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including). It specifies that a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is held within what is, marking it as present within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md):
    - Be included in what it is (producing [inclusion](./inclusion.md));
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [exclusion](./exclusion.md)).

- The [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) establishes the [condition](./condition.md) of [inclusion](./inclusion.md):
    - This [condition](./condition.md) holds the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) **inside** the field of what is;
    - [inclusion](./inclusion.md) is not a passive state but an **actively maintained presence**;
    - It marks and holds the [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) as part of the relational field.

- [inclusion](./inclusion.md) is transient unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md):
    - As long as the [../acts/act](../acts/act.md) of [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including) is active, [inclusion](./inclusion.md) persists;
    - To sustain it durably, [inclusion](./inclusion.md) must be stabilized within a [../structures/relative](../structures/relative.md) or other [../structures/structure](../structures/structure.md).

- [inclusion](./inclusion.md) gains its coherence by being held together with [exclusion](./exclusion.md):
    - This mutual holding induces [tension](./tension.md);
    - [tension](./tension.md) ensures that [inclusion](./inclusion.md) is not trivial or self-dissolving but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [exclusion](./exclusion.md).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) that [../acts/exist|exists](../acts/exist|exists) is conditioned by both [inclusion](./inclusion.md) and [exclusion](./exclusion.md);
- [inclusion](./inclusion.md) is essential for affirming what a [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) is:
    - Without [inclusion](./inclusion.md), no [triage/thing](../structures/thing.md) can be marked as present within the field of what is;
- [inclusion](./inclusion.md) participates in the fundamental [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) that generate and sustain [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) and all further relational architectures.
