- Definition:: ***relationality*** is the [domain](domain) [induce|induced](induce|induced) by the [act](act)s of [including](including) (relation) and [excluding](excluding) (relationlessness), inducing coherent [relational structure](relational structure).

## Derivation
- [postulate](postulate):: It must be derived that as [relation](relation) is the act of [including](including) within a domain, and [relationlessness](relationlessness) is the act of [excluding](excluding) relation, their mutual necessity [induce|induces](induce|induces) [induce relationality](induce relationality) itself.
- [derivation](derivation):: As [relationlessness](relationlessness) and [relation](relation) are mutually acting within and upon the same domain, the [existence](existence) of relationality necessarily derives its [relation](relation) to itself, inducing the first act of [reflexive relational derivation](reflexive relational derivation).
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [induce relationality](induce relationality) is [induce](induce)([relationlessness](relationlessness), [relation](relation))

## Formalism
- Let r denote the act of [including](including) relation, and not(r) denote the act of [excluding](excluding) relation (relationlessness).
- Then:
	- not(r) ≠ r
	- The act of excluding (not(r)) necessarily relates back to the act of including (r).
- Therefore, define Relationality as the domain induced by these acts:
	- Relationality = {r, not(r), relate(r, not(r))}
- Thus, Relationality is the domain composed of the acts of [including](including) and [excluding](excluding) relation, bound together by their necessary mutual relation.
