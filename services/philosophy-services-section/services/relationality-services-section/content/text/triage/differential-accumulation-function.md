The ***differential accumulation function*** (A) is induced by the constraint inheritance function (Η).

When stabilized difference is inherited across successive constraint formations,  
accumulated difference forms gradients within constraint.

> A(x) := Σ { Δₛ(x, x′) | x′ ∈ Η(Θ(Σ)) }

That is, accumulation is the sum of inherited difference under persistent constraint.
