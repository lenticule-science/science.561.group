Commuting filters have a shared stable region.  *Profile Recognitions* witnessing *Compose Discipline* produces ***Profile Composite*** ([ProfileComposite](0219-profile-composite.md)).

The composition identifies recognitions preserved by all commuting filters, so *Profile Composite* is valid.
