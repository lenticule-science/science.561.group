A thing is anything that can be distinguished from everything else. If you can point at it, name it, or talk about it, it is a thing. A rock is a thing. A color is a thing. A number, a feeling, a country, a mistake — all things. "Thing" is the most general word there is. Everything in this library is a thing.

A thing has qualities — properties that make it distinguishable. A red ball is distinguished from a blue ball by color. A heavy box is distinguished from a light box by weight. Without at least one quality, there is nothing to distinguish, and so nothing to call a thing.

Things can be physical or not. A chair is a physical thing — it has mass, takes up space, you can sit on it. A promise is a non-physical thing — it exists, it matters, it can be broken, but it has no weight. Both are things. The word makes no commitment about physicality.

Things can contain other things. A box contains books. A room contains furniture. A country contains cities. Containment is one of the most basic relations between things — see [container](./container.md).

Things can be parts of other things. A wheel is part of a car. A chapter is part of a book. A cell is part of an organism. Parthood is different from containment — the wheel IS part of the car, but a book inside a box is NOT part of the box.

In TAB (Things with Attributes and Behaviors), a thing is the base model from which all other models derive. A thing has a name and grammar (how to refer to it in language). An [object](./object.md) is a thing with mass and notability. A [container](./container.md) is a thing with contents. A person is a thing that can move and act. Everything starts from thing.

- [../acts/define](../acts/define):: A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is any [structure](structure)—specifically, any [relative](relative)—that is held open as coherent within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating). A [triage/thing](triage/thing) is not a special kind of entity but the name we give to any **stabilized** relational unit that maintains [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) through [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing) produces the **minimal relative**: the simplest [structure](structure) that holds open the dynamic of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion), and their [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension).
    - This minimal relative is the **first [triage/thing](triage/thing)**.

- However, the system does not limit what counts as a [triage/thing](triage/thing) to this minimal case:
    - Any stabilized [relative](relative)—whether simple or complex—is a [triage/thing](triage/thing);
    - A [triage/thing](triage/thing) can be a single basic relative or a **nested/compound** relative assembled through further relational dynamics.

- Therefore, a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is **defined structurally**:
    - It is a [structure](structure) that holds at least one [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) open durably;
    - It achieves [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence) by stabilizing the acts and conditions (such as [../acts/include|including](../acts/include|including), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding), [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension), [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference), and [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation)) that bring it about.

- The [triage/thing](triage/thing) **cannot be separated** from the relational architecture that constitutes it:
    - It is not a primitive “given” outside the system;
    - It is **generated and maintained** through the interplay of [../acts/act](../acts/act), [../conditions/condition](../conditions/condition), and [structure](structure).

- In this way, every [triage/thing](triage/thing):
    - Is sustained by a **coherent set** of relational dynamics;
    - Derives its **identity and distinctness** from the conditions of [../conditions/difference](../conditions/difference) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension) that define it relationally.

## Implications

- The minimal [triage/thing](triage/thing) is the first [relative](relative) formed in the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../acts/exist|existing);
    - More complex [triage/thing|things](triage/thing|things) arise by recursively applying relational dynamics, building up nested or composed relatives.
- Every [structure](structure) that stably holds a [../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation](../../../../../mathematics/objects/sets/terms/relation) is, by definition, a [triage/thing](triage/thing):
    - Thus, the entire architecture of relationality is populated **only by things** of varying complexity and depth.
- There is no “bare” [triage/thing](triage/thing) outside of relationality:
    - To be a [triage/thing](triage/thing) is to be a **relationally-constituted, stabilized structure**;
    - The system admits no thing **without** relational grounding.
