The ***recursive negentropic convergence potential*** (`ℵₙ`) quantifies the presence of localized substrate regions within a constraint field that exhibit sustained improbability — areas where entropy decreases, trace accumulates, curvature bends coherence, and stabilization persists.

Let:
- `κ(x)` = local constraint curvature,
- `Ξ(x)` = coherence without collapse,
- `(∂𝒮/∂t)(x) < 0` = declining modulation entropy,
- `ρ_T(x) > τ` = trace mass density exceeds threshold,
- `Ω(x) = x` = field completes stabilization.

Then:
> ℵₙ(x) := κ(x) ∧ Ξ(x) ∧ (∂𝒮/∂t)(x) < 0 ∧ ρ_T(x) > τ ∧ Ω(x) = x

That is, `ℵₙ` holds when a region within the constraint field bends, coheres, accumulates memory, lowers entropy, and stabilizes — a topological basin of emergent potential.
