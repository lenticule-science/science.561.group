# Theory of Relationality

Relationality is the philosophical register of the theory whose mathematical register is [Obsmetrologia](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/content/text/documents/learning-documents/theories/theory-of-obsmetrologia.md). It answers the question every later theory presupposes: why is there relation at all, and what is a thing?

The answer is a derivation, not a postulate. Relationlessness has no relation. But relationlessness is the exclusion of relation, and excluding is a relation to relation. So it cannot not derive the first distinction between relationlessness and relation (emsenn, 2025-04). That first distinction is the reflex; the formal theory calls it articulating. From it everything else unfolds, and no thing exists prior to the relations that constitute it.

This is a Lakotaean metaphysics stated mathematically: being is derived, not posited, and personhood is the default condition of whatever is. The mathematics carries the principles. The tradition's substance stays with its holders and is not a library module.

## Primitives

1. **Relationlessness.** The void of relation. It is the ground the engine starts from and cannot start in: to start is already to draw a distinction.
2. **Reflex (articulating).** The primitive act that draws a distinction out of relationlessness. "Reflex" is the pre-formalization name. The formal name is articulating (Leader, 2026-09-21).
3. **Including and excluding.** Two primitive acts held open together. Including brings a thing into what is held open. Excluding marks what cannot be produced together with it. Their tension keeps inclusion from totalizing into undifferentiated presence.
4. **Difference.** Relating and unrelating are different. If they were not, the domain of relating would be induced by including alone, and it is not. Difference is a necessary condition of relational structure.
5. **Trace.** A relational history: a finite sequence of relational steps in which independent steps commute. Traces are the elements of a free partially commutative monoid M(Σ, I), ordered by prefix extension.

## Laws

- **R1. The first distinction is forced.** No choice produces it. Speaking of relationlessness already carves an inside from an outside.
- **R2. Entities are constituted by their relations.** A mark has no identity but its role (Obsmetrologia L-primitive 1). Two things that stand in all the same relations are one thing.
- **R3. Difference is held open in tension.** A difference is an ordered composite of two, neither collapsed into sameness nor exploded into contradiction.
- **R4. Histories are partial orders.** Independent steps commute. A history records only the order that dependency forces, never an order that arrival happened to impose.
- **R5. Meaning is local and glues.** At each history t there is an algebra H_t of the propositions available there. A longer history restricts to a shorter one by forgetting later steps. Propositions that agree on a cover of a history glue to a unique proposition on it: meaning is a sheaf on histories.
- **R6. Two closures settle meaning.** Each H_t carries two nuclei. Saturation σ is habit: a proposition is σ-fixed when further engagement at that history no longer changes its interpretation (Peirce's final interpretant). Continuation Δ is temporal stability: a proposition is Δ-fixed when one more step of history does not change its value. The doubly quiescent layer H*_t = Fix σ ∩ Fix Δ is what has settled in the strongest sense.
- **R7. Construction and observation agree at fixed points.** A settled proposition is determined by the observations it satisfies, and an observation by the settled propositions it accepts. What is constructible is exactly what is observable (observation duality).
- **R8. Faithfulness.** No nontrivial symmetry of histories is invisible to settled meaning. A universe whose modal data could not tell two histories apart would be relational only in decoration.
- **R9. Being equals the act of its own distinction.** The world does not exist apart from its self-differentiation: Equal(World, Differentiate(World)) (emsenn, 2025-11).

## Relation to Obsmetrologia

Relationality is the same theory read philosophically. Obsmetrologia's two hands (token, claim) are relationality's two sides of the first distinction. Its knowledge order is relationality's no-retraction of history. The two nuclei σ and Δ reappear as the two strata of a witness journal: saturated (Fix σ) and settled (Fix σ ∩ Fix Δ). See [theory of Obsmetrologia](../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/content/text/documents/learning-documents/theories/theory-of-obsmetrologia.md).

## Consequences for the Group's systems

Required:

1. **A work history is a trace, not a list.** Records of what agents did form a partial order. Two agents' independent steps commute, and a system that forces them into one total order invents an order that does not exist. This is the formal reason "dozens of agents committing into one checkout" fails: a single mutable checkout imposes a total order on work that is only partially ordered.
2. **Every actor reads a restriction.** An actor sees the restriction of the shared history to what it can reach. Shared views are glued from local ones, never a copy of one universal interior.
3. **Done means doubly settled.** A unit of work is settled when further engagement (σ) and further steps (Δ) no longer change its value. This is computed from the record.
4. **Identity is relational.** A resource is identified by what it stands in relation to and how it was derived, not by where it is filed. A copy filed elsewhere is not a second thing; a thing filed nowhere still exists in its relations.

## Open

- Open: whether the two commuting nuclei (σ, Δ) hold at every level of the tower, or only at finite levels with one lax-idempotent nucleus above (the relational-hyperverse voidmath derivation argues the latter).
- Open: the precise functor from the trace-sheaf presentation (the Generative Sheaf Universe) to Obsmetrologia's settling presentation.
