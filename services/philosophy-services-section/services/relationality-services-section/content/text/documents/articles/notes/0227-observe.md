## Interpretation

*Recognize Observation* records contextual indistinguishability, but an observable must address a concrete traced term.  Forcing *Recognize Observation* to witness *Seed Term* yields ***Observe*** ([Observe](./0227-observe.md)).

Both recognitions were previously earned, so their composition records a designated observable act on a traced term, establishing *Observe*.
