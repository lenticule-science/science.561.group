constraint identity continuity function  
The constraint identity continuity function (I) is induced by structural coherence between evolving quantum constellations.

When two constellations of resonance nodes exhibit alignment in modulation structure and transformation trajectory,  
identity is preserved — the entity persists through change, maintaining coherence under deformation.

I(Q₁, Q₂) := similarity(Q₁, Q₂) ≥ θ

That is, identity continuity is satisfied when two quantum constellations remain sufficiently aligned in constraint and modulation,  
according to a threshold θ of structural correspondence.
