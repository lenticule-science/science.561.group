The constraint periodicity function (Π) is induced by the dynamic recurrence of quantum constellations.

When a quantum entity undergoes structural recurrence across time without loss of coherence,  
periodicity emerges — a stable return of form, possibly subharmonic or nested within a greater dynamic.

Π(Q, T) := Q(t) = Q(t + T)

That is, periodicity is the reappearance of an entity constellation at a temporal interval T, with preserved identity.
