## Interpretation

Open terms reduce under environments assigning reducible terms to variables.  *Construct Contexts* witnessing *Select Reducibility Candidate* yields ***Build Reducible Environment*** ([BuildReducibleEnvironment](./build-reducible-environment.md)).

Composing contexts with candidates records environments mapping each hypothesis to a reducible term, so *Build Reducible Environment* follows.
