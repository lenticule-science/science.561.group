Considering [On Fediverse narrating contradictions into aesthetic cruel optimism](babble-20250720094800.md), if we consider [postliberal cybernetics](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberal-cybernetics.md) as the analytical frame, then I might say:

The [affect economy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/affect-economy.md) and [libidinal economy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/libidinal-economy.md) are [complementary postliberal complexes](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/complementary-postliberal-complex.md) that [process](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberal-processing.md) [experience](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experience.md) into [liberal subjectivity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-subjectivity.md).

By complementary complexes, I mean two distinct but interdependent [postliberal complexes](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberal-complex.md) acting in response to the same [pressures](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberal-pressure.md): the libidinal economy incites investment in response to [desiring](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/desiring.md), the affect economy orients feedback, and together they stabilize the [postliberal circuit of liberal subjectivity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberal-circuit-of-liberal-subjectivity.md).

I think I'm drawing most on [Jean-François Lyotard](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jean-francois-lyotard.md)'s [Libidinal Economy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/libidinal-economy.md), which explicitly treats [libido](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/libido.md) as an [energetic flow](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/energetic-flow.md) that moves through [social](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-system.md) and [economic systems](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/economic-system.md), but I'm also influenced by [Jacques Lacan](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jacques-lacan.md), who discussed desire not as a lack to be filled but a circulating structural function of investing in the [Other](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/other.md), and [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md), which also rejected [Freudian lack](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/freudian-lack.md) to look at [generative desiring](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/generative-desiring.md). The three share the claim that desire is not simply personal or interior, but circulates through system, and those systems are animated and sustained by that circulation.

Looking at the Fediverse, we can say that these investments are in a sense of preserving ethics, cultivating them in community, and doing it ([using social media](../../../../glossary/terms/using-social-media.md)) "right:" the desire is routed into a system that generates it into an [affect of ethicalness](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/affect-of-ethicalness.md), which is shaped by the [feeling rules of the Fediverse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/feeling-rules-of-the-fediverse.md).

On [Twitter](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/twitter.md), the same desire is captured the enjoyment of competing for visibility, and the system regulates toward that [affective of virality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/affective-of-virality.md).
