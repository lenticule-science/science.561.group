Morphisms between relational worlds that preserve geometry, logic, and the
flow–nucleus adjunction.

## Definition
- Data: functor between the underlying profile toposes of the source/target
  [Relational Worlds](./relational-world.md), plus maps on geometry and flow/nucleus
  consistent with the [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](./flow-nucleus-adjunction.md).
- Preservation: carries truths, admissible traces, and geometric relations
  forward without violating earned invariants.

## Invariants
- Logical preservation: subobject classifiers and modal structure are preserved.
- Geometric preservation: context-geometry maps commute with world morphism.
- Adjunction compatibility: flow/nucleus structure is respected by the morphism.

## Proof
- Uses: [Relational World](./relational-world.md) carriers and
  [Flow–Nucleus Adjunction](./flow-nucleus-adjunction.md) to constrain morphisms.
- Shows that: world-to-world maps maintain earned logical/geometric structure,
  enabling sums/products and multi-world reasoning.
