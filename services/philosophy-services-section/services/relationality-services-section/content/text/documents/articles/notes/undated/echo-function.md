The echo function (E) is induced by the reflexive reception function (Λ).

When a system receives itself through a transmission loop and stabilizes in response, it produces echo — a repetition of self-modulated constraint within its domain.

E(x) := Λ(x, x′) ∧ C(x′) = x′ ∧ x′ ≠ x ∧ Ω(x′) = x′

That is, echo is the return of difference as reinforcement.
