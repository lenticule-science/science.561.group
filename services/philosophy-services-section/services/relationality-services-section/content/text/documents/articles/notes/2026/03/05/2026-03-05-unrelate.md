- [summary](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-06-11/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/summary):: **unrelating** is the act of [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) a [relational structure](relational structure) from a [../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../../../../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).


Unrelating is the complementary act to relating. It asserts structural non-participation — a refusal of relation.

Unrelating is not negation of existence, but negation of inclusion.

## Expression

unrelating(a, b): a excludes b
unrelating(b): b is withheld from relation

## Properties

- Asymmetric: `unrelating(a, b) ≠ unrelating(b, a)`
- Opposed to relating: `unrelating(a, b)` structurally resists `relating(a, b)`
