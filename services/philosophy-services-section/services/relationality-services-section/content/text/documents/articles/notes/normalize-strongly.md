# Normalizing Strongly

## Interpretation

A term is strongly normalizing when no infinite reduction can occur.  *Normalization* witnessing *Step Trace* yields ***Normalize Strongly*** ([NormalizeStrongly](./normalize-strongly.md)).

This composition records that normalization eliminates all infinite traces, so *Normalize Strongly* follows.
