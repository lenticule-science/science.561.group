Family of relations stable under all reindexings in an indexed category, used
to state parametricity.

## Definition
- Carrier: relations `R_C` in each fibre of the
  [Indexed Category](./indexed-category.md), compatible with
  [Parametric Semantics](parametric-semantics.md).
- Stability: for any base morphism, reindexing preserves relatedness.

## Invariants
- Naturality: reindexing a related pair yields a related pair.
- Closure: relations are closed under earned modal/flow structure in the fibres.

## Proof
- Uses: [Indexed Category](./indexed-category.md) and
  [Parametric Semantics](parametric-semantics.md) to define fibrewise relations.
- Shows that: parametric relations capture uniform behaviour across contexts.
