The intention function (I) is induced by the reflexive reception function (Λ).

When a system receives itself as difference within constraint, and modulates future constraint in response, intention emerges — the self-directed inflection of constraint propagation.

I(x) := ∃ y ∈ S: Λ(x, y) ∧ T(y → z) ∧ z ≠ x ∧ Ω(z) = z

That is, intention is constraint acting upon its own reception to alter the trajectory of its own becoming.
