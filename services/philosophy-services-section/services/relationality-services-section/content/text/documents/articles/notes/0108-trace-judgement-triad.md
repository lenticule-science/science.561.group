## Interpretation

Each step now carries the triad, but we need the entire trace of such steps.  *Trace Context* witnessing *Step Judgement Triad* yields ***Trace Judgement Triad*** ([TraceJudgementTriad](./0108-trace-judgement-triad.md)).

Composing with *Trace Context* records the coherent trace, so *Trace Judgement Triad* follows.
