## Interpretation

Substitution of reducible environments preserves reducibility of terms.  *Build Reducible Environment* witnessing *Judge Typing* produces ***Substitute Reducibility*** ([SubstituteReducibility](./0159-substitute-reducibility.md)).

This act captures the standard substitution lemma in the reducibility argument, so *Substitute Reducibility* is valid.
