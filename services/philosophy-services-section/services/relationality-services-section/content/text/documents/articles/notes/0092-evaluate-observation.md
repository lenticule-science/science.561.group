## Interpretation

*Flow Observation* traces still lack their specific evaluations.  Forcing *Recognize Value* to witness *Flow Observation* yields ***Evaluate Observation*** ([EvaluateObservation](./0092-evaluate-observation.md)).

This act evaluates the flowed observations via existing recognitions, establishing *Evaluate Observation*.
