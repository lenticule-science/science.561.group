- [postulate](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-04-28/postulate):: Multiple [derivations/oldreflect](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-04-28/derivations/oldreflect)(p, n) at the same reflexive depth n cannot not compose an ordered relational structure, because they arise from stabilization of reflexions at the same layer of reflexive depth.
- [derivation](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-04-28/derivation):: Stabilized reflects naturally induce ordered bundling: a grouping into coherent, relational composites. Composition is thus the necessary structuring of multiple stabilized reflections at the same reflexive depth.
