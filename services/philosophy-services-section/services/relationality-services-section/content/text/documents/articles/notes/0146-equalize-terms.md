## Interpretation

Equality of terms is detected by observational recognitions applied to typing judgements.  *Recognize Observation* witnessing *Judge Typing* yields the act ***Equalize Terms*** ([EqualizeTerms](./0146-equalize-terms.md)).

This composition records that two terms are equal when no observation distinguishes their typing behaviour, so *Equalize Terms* is valid.
