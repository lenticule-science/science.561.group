# Hypertensor Tower Structure

There exists a carrier `Tower` earned by:

- [Build Profile Tower](./build-profile-tower.md),
- [Build Composite Profile Tower](./build-composite-profile-tower.md),
- on the stabilised recognitions from [Profile Recognitions](./profile-recognitions.md),
  respecting the flow/nucleus acts [Flow](./flow.md) and
  [Nucleate](./nucleate.md).

Each `Tower` attaches to a single `Fiber` (from
[Hypertensor Fiber Structure](./hypertensor-fiber-structure.md)) and records its
profile levels.
