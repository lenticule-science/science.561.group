## Interpretation

Nucleus-closed recognitions inherit the semantic structure.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Fix Nucleus* yields ***Nucleus Semantics*** ([NucleusSemantics](./0181-nucleus-semantics.md)).

This composition records that the closed recognitions form a complete substructure, so *Nucleus Semantics* is valid.
