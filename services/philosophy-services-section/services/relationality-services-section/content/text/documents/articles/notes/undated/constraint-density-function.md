The ***constraint density function*** (`ρ_C`) measures the local rate of modulation change within constraint.

Where modulation changes sharply under stable constraint,  
constraint is said to be dense — tightly curved, high potential for deformation.

> ρ_C(x) := ‖∇V(x)‖  
> where V is the vector modulation function

That is, constraint density is the rate of modulation distortion within constraint.
