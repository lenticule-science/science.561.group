# Free Phase

The free phase lists recognitions exactly as they are earned—no quotienting or
identifications have been imposed yet.  Future quotient phases will collapse
acts along declared equalities, but for now every recognition appears here in
its raw presentation.

Acts presented in the **free** phase grouped by stratum.
