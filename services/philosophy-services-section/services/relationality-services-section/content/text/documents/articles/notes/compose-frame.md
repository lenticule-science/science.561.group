Partial composition `compose : Frame × Frame ⇀ Frame` for frames from
[`EvaluationContext`](./evaluation-context.md).

## Statement

Whenever `compose(F1, F2)` is defined,

```
plug(compose(F1, F2), t) = plug(F1, plug(F2, t))
```

and composition is associative where defined, with `empty` as neutral.

## Proof

Sketch: define `compose` by nesting holes along the trace spine, using frames
generated by [`BuildEvaluationContext`](./build-evaluation-context.md).
Show associativity and neutrality of `empty` from
[`SeedEvaluationContext`](./seed-evaluation-context.md) by
structural induction on frames and the definition of `plug`.
