Set of recognitions such that all admissible flow paths in a region converge to
them (attractor basin representative).

## Definition
- Attractor: recognition/set stable under flow/nucleus; nearby states converge
  to it.
- Basin: recognised via [Recognition Attracting Basin](./attracting-basin.md).

## Invariants
- Stability: attractor is fixed under flow/nucleus.
- Attraction: neighbourhood under flow maps into basin converging to attractor.

## Proof
- Uses: flow dynamics and nucleus to define fixed points/basins.
- Shows that: attractors can be identified as convergence targets for flows.
