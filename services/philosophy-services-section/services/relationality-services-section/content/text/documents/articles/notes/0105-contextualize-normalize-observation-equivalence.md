## Interpretation

Even normalized equivalence observations must be indistinguishable to all contexts.  *Equate Contexts* witnessing *Normalize Observation Equivalence Witness* yields ***Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence*** ([ContextualizeNormalizeObservationEquivalence](./0105-contextualize-normalize-observation-equivalence.md)).

This act ensures contextual indistinguishability, so *Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence* is earned.
