## Interpretation

*Type Function* and *Type Application* collectively present how terms inhabit types under contexts.  Letting *Type Function* witness *Type Application* produces ***Judge Typing*** ([JudgeTyping](./0145-judge-typing.md)).

Both recognitions were previously earned; their composition records the judgement that a context recognises term t at type A in native form, so *Judge Typing* follows.
