## Interpretation

Transport alone does not describe how binders apply to term bodies.  *Form Term Body* must therefore witness *Binder Transport*, inducing ***Binder Application*** ([BinderApplication](./binder-application.md)).

Composing the body recognition with the transport records actual usage, so *Binder Application* is justified.
