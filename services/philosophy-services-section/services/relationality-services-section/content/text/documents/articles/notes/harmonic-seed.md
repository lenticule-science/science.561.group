A harmonic arises from comparing a state to its flow-advanced image.
[OrbitSeed](./orbit-seed.md) isolates the initial position of an
orbit, and [FixedInvariant](./fixed-invariant.md) provides the invariant
states.  Letting the seed observe the invariants,
`(OrbitSeed FixedInvariant)`, earns ***HarmonicSeed***.

Their composition records the primary oscillatory component: the deviation
between the invariant and the initial shift, seeding the harmonic.
