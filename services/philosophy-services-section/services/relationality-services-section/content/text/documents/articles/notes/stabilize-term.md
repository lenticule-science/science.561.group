# Stabilizing Term

## Interpretation

*Equate Contexts* tells us when two terms are indistinguishable to every recognized context, but we must quotient those terms.  Letting *Seed Term* witness *Equate Contexts* produces ***Stabilize Term*** ([StabilizeTerm](./stabilize-term.md)).

By composing an earned seed with the equivalence, we identify contextually indistinguishable terms, so *Stabilize Term* is established.
