This program treats generated images and environments as recognitions so visual
worlds sit inside the same relational lattice as everything else.

## Behaviour

- Define visual generators (procedural terrain, GANs, diffusion), prompts,
  scenes/images, and visual styles as recognitions with provenance.
- Measure complexity, symmetry, colour distributions, affect, and reactions
  (what people linger on or reuse).
- Map style spaces and morph paths; link scenes to other recognitions (places,
  dreams, symbols, MUD rooms).

## Intended operators

- Generate images or scenes from prompts and context.
- Explore style spaces and relate styles across domains.
- Weave visuals into narrative, research, or gameplay with traceable outputs.
