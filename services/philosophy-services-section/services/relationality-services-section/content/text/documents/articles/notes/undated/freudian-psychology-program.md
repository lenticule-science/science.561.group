This program models drives, defenses, repetition, and transference as
recognitions so psychoanalytic patterns can be traced across domains.

## Behaviour

- Represent drives (life/libido, death/aggression), defense mechanisms,
  repetition compulsions, and transference events as recognitions with
  context and provenance.
- Link these recognitions to discourse, behaviour, dreams, and relationships to
  see how they manifest and change over time.
- Measure frequency and contexts of defenses and repetitions; track whether
  patterns intensify or diminish.

## Intended operators

- Annotate discourse/behaviour for defenses and transference.
- Detect repetition across situations and timelines.
- Surface how psychoanalytic motifs intersect with inner-work and interpersonal
  programs.
