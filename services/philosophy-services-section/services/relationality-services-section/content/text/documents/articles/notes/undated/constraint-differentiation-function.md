The ***constraint differentiation function*** (`∇C`) is induced by the accumulation surface (`Aˢ`).

When difference accumulates unequally across constraint-stable domains,  
constraint differentiates — modulating itself in response to internal topography.

> ∇C(x) := ∂C/∂A(x)

That is, constraint varies as a function of accumulated difference.
