* Abstract
We define and construct a *Relational ∞-Topos Modality* (RITM): a quadruple of
adjoint, idempotent endofunctors internal to a (Grothendieck) cohesive ∞-topos,
encoding directed relationality.  
Two concrete realizations are exhibited:
(1) a presheaf ∞-topos on a directed poset (the *relational cell*), and  
(2) a cohesive sheaf ∞-topos on smooth spaces (the *relational site*).  

We further prove:
- minimal and essential axiom sets and their logical independence,
- constructive realization via *Lawvere–Tierney nuclei* corresponding to
  Grothendieck topologies,
- a recursive RITM on the functor ∞-category \(\End(\mathfrak E)\),
- and a smooth cohesive model ensuring strict ∞-adjunctions.  
All cohesivity claims are verified through (−1)-connected / effective epi
arguments (HTT §6.5).

* 1. Introduction
Grothendieck’s theory of topoi situates sheaf semantics over sites (SGA4).  
Lurie’s *Higher Topos Theory* upgrades this to ∞-topoi; Johnstone’s
*Elephant* systematizes subtopoi via local operators.  
Cohesive ∞-topoi (Lawvere; Schreiber) equip an adjoint chain
\(\Pi\dashv\Delta\dashv\Gamma\dashv\nabla\).  
RITM internalizes *directionality* by inserting a commuting middle pair into
that cohesive paradigm. Our contributions:

- Axiomatization (minimal vs. convenient) of RITM and a homotopy-invariant
  notion of *directed ∞-connectedness*;
- A *Grothendieck* construction in \(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)\) with commuting
  nuclei \(j_f,j_b\) yielding essential geometric inclusions and an explicit
  cohesive outer adjunction;
- A *recursive* RITM on \(\End(\mathfrak E)\) via sandwich (whiskering)
  modalities;
- A smooth cohesive model ensuring internal mapping objects and strict
  ∞-adjunctions.

* 2. Axioms of the Relational ∞-Topos Modality
** 2.1 Minimal Axioms (MA)
#+begin_definition
A *minimal RITM* on a cohesive ∞-topos 𝔈 is a quadruple
\[
\Pi^{→}\dashv\Boxr\dashv\Diaml\dashv\nabla^{←}
\]
satisfying:
(MA1) \(\Pi^{→}\) (a left-exact localization) preserves all colimits and finite limits;  
\(\nabla^{←}\) (a right-exact coreflection) preserves all limits and finite colimits.  
These preservation conditions follow from their adjoint roles within the cohesive
quadruple, though only the finite (co)limit cases are used in later proofs.
(MA2) \(T:=\Boxr\Diaml\) and \(S:=\Diaml\Boxr\) are idempotent monads on 𝔈.  
(MA3) There is a canonical equivalence \(\Boxr\Diaml\simeq\Diaml\Boxr\).  
(MA4) *Cohesion (outer pair):* the unit
\(\eta_X: X\to\nabla^{←}\Pi^{→}X\) is (−1)-connected (effective epi) and the
counit \(\epsilon_X:\Pi^{→}\nabla^{←}X\to X\) is an effective mono (HTT §6.5).
#+end_definition

** 2.2 Essential / “Convenient” Axioms (EA)
#+begin_definition
A *convenient RITM* assumes additionally:  
(EA1) \(\Boxr,\Diaml\) are fully faithful inverse-image functors of essential
geometric inclusions of subtopoi \(a_f⊣i_f⊣\Pi_f\) and \(a_b⊣i_b⊣\Pi_b\);  
(EA2) These arise from *Lawvere–Tierney nuclei* \(j_f,j_b\) on 𝔈.
#+end_definition

#+begin_proposition
(EA) ⇒ (MA): essential inclusions yield left exact \(a_f\), limit-preserving
\(\Pi_b\), fully faithful \(i_f,i_b\), and idempotent \(i_fa_f,i_b\Pi_b\).
Cohesion then follows by (−1)-connected / effective epi stability.
#+end_proposition

** 2.3 Directed ∞-Connectedness
#+begin_definition
Fix a *directed interval object* \(I_d\) in a cohesive setting.
Let \(\mathcal S_d\) be the class of *directed spheres*—finite colimits of
iterated suspensions of \(I_d/\partial I_d\).  
An object X is *directed ∞-connected* iff for all \(S∈\mathcal S_d\),
\(\mathrm{Map}(S,X)\to\mathrm{Map}(\ast,X)\) is an equivalence.
#+end_definition

#+begin_proposition
If (MA4) holds and \(I_d\) generates \(\mathcal S_d\), then
\(X\) is directed ∞-connected ⇔ \(X\simeq\Pi^{→}X\simeq\nabla^{←}X\).
#+end_proposition

*Proof sketch.* Both shape and codiscretion fix X iff all directed homotopy
groups vanish.

* 3. Relational Morphisms
#+begin_definition
A *directed relation* \(x\rightsquigarrow y\) is a morphism \(r:x\to\Boxr\Diaml y\).
Composition is the modal pull–push
\[
r_2\circ r_1:=(\Boxr r_2)\circ(\Diaml r_1),
\]
characterized by the universal 2-cell against the counit of
\(\Diaml\dashv\nabla^{←}\). Associativity follows from (MA3).
#+end_definition

* 4. Constructive Presheaf Model (Relational Cell)
** 4.1 Ambient Topos
Let \(\mathcal D=\{0\to1\}\) (thin), \(\mathfrak E=\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)\).

** 4.2 Grothendieck Topologies and Commuting Nuclei
Define \(J_f,J_b\) by sieves:
- \(J_f(0)\): maximal; \(J_f(1)\): identity (forward cover),
- \(J_b(1)\): maximal; \(J_b(0)\): identity (backward cover).  
These yield Lawvere–Tierney nuclei \(j_f,j_b\) satisfying \(j_fj_b=j_bj_f\)
(thinness ⇒ no 2-cell obstructions).

** 4.3 Essential Inclusions and Adjunctions
Let \(i_f:\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal D^{op},J_f)\hookrightarrow\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)\),
\(i_b:\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal D^{op},J_b)\hookrightarrow\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal D)\),
with left adjoints (sheafifications) \(a_f,a_b\) and right adjoints
\(\Pi_f,\Pi_b\). Then:
\[
\Pi^{→}:=a_f,\quad \Boxr:=i_f,\quad \Diaml:=i_b,\quad \nabla^{←}:=\Pi_b
\]
define a convenient RITM.

** 4.4 Cohesion in Presheaves
#+begin_theorem
The outer pair \(\Pi^{→}\dashv\nabla^{←}\) is cohesive on \(\mathfrak E\).
#+end_theorem
*Proof.* \(X\to i_fa_fX\) is objectwise surjective (−1)-connected);  
\(\Pi_b\) preserves limits and (−1)-connectedness; hence
\(\eta_X:X\to\Pi_ba_fX\) is an effective epi, and
\(\epsilon:a_f\Pi_bX\to X\) is an effective mono. □

** 4.5 Explicit Computation
For \(X=(X_0,X_1,u:X_1→X_0)\):
\[
(a_fX)_0=\mathrm{coeq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0),\quad (a_fX)_1=X_1,
\]
dually for \(a_b\). This is the minimal nontrivial directed realization.

* 5. Recursive RITM on \(\End(\mathfrak E)\)
Let \(\End(\mathfrak E)=\mathrm{Fun}(\mathfrak E,\mathfrak E)\), presentable and enriched.

** 5.1 Sandwich (Whiskering) Modalities
Define
\[
\Pi^{→}_{\End}(F)=a_f∘F,\quad
\Box^{→}_{\End}(F)=i_f∘F∘a_f,\quad
\Diaml_{\End}(F)=i_b∘F∘\Pi_b,\quad
\nabla^{←}_{\End}(F)=F∘\Pi_b.
\]
These form a minimal RITM on \(\End(\mathfrak E)\) by whiskering adjunctions.

** 5.2 Stable Subcategories
Let \(T:=i_f(-)a_f\), \(S:=i_b(-)\Pi_b\).  
Both are idempotent monads/comonads.  
Define fixed-point subcategories:
\[
\mathrm{RD}:=\{F\mid F∘i_f\simeq i_f∘F\},\quad
\mathrm{RT}:=\{F\mid F∘i_b\simeq i_b∘F\}.
\]
RD is reflective, RT coreflective; restricted functors yield a *secondary RITM*
whose inner adjunction  
\[
\Boxr_{RD}\dashv\Diaml_{RT}
\]
arises as the restriction of the sandwich modalities of §5.1 to the stable
subcategories RD and RT. This makes explicit the adjunction internal to
\(\End(\mathfrak E)\) that underlies the recursive RITM structure.

* 6. Smooth Cohesive Model (Relational Site)
Let \(\mathfrak E=\mathrm{Shv}_\infty(\mathcal C,J)\) for cartesian closed \(\mathcal C\)
(e.g. CartSp). Let \(I_d\) be the directed unit interval.
Define:
\[
\Pi^{→}(X)=\mathrm{colim}_{I_d→X}\!\Hom(I_d,X),\quad
\Boxr(X)=\mathrm{Map}(I_d,X),\quad
\Diaml(X)=\mathrm{Map}(X,I_d),\quad
\nabla^{←}(X)=\mathrm{lim}_{X→I_d}\!\Hom(X,I_d).
\]
These satisfy (MA) and yield strict ∞-adjunctions (HTT §5.5.3).  
Idempotence follows from concatenation \(I_d×I_d→I_d\);  
cohesion from (−1)-connected stability. □

* 8. Conclusion
RITM provides a minimal yet fully constructive framework for directed
relationality in ∞-topoi.  
We gave minimal and essential axioms, explicit Grothendieck realization,
recursive extension, and cohesive smooth model.  
The RD/RT split is both algebraic (idempotent (co)monads) and geometric
(directed shape/codiscretion).  
Future directions: modal HoTT encodings, AWFS formulations, and enriched
probabilistic topoi.
