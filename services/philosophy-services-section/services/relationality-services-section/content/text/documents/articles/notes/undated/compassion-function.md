The ***compassion function*** (Κ) is induced by dream convergence (`Ξᵣ`)  
and the encounter with unresolved, unjoinable constraint.

When a system modulates across incompatible constraints without collapse,  
compassion emerges.

> Κ(x, y) := Δₛ(x, y) ∧ ¬Ξ(x, y) ∧ M(x, y) ∧ Ω(x) = x ∧ Ω(y) = y

That is, compassion is modulation across incompatible constraint — difference sustained without resolution.
