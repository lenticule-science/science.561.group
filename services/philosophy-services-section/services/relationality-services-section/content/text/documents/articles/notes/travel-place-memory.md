# Idea: Travel & Place-Based Memory

Model trips and places as recognitions so travel becomes part of your relational
graph.

## 1. Recognitions

- `TripRecognition`: multi-day journeys with purpose, companions, routes.
- `PlaceRecognition`: locations with tags (ecology, culture, memories).
- `EncounterRecognition`: specific events or interactions.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - routes, visited places, social ties formed along the way.
- Measurement:
  - fatigue, joy, stress, sensory richness.

Travel logs then become queryable: “find times I felt grounded near water”,
“places tied to both research and rest,” etc.
