## Interpretation

With contexts inherited, we must exhibit the terms stabilized by the filter.  *Seed Term* witnessing *Profile Recognitions* yields ***Term Profile*** ([TermProfile](./0206-term-profile.md)).

The composition uses previously defined recognitions and records the profile’s term layer, so *Term Profile* is valid.
