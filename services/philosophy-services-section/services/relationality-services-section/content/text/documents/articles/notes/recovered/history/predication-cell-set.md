name: predication cell set
description: The Cartesian product $\Phi := E \times \Pi \times E$ — the discrete index over which predication valuations range.
---

The Cartesian product, denoted $\Phi$, of an [entity set](entity-set.md), a [predicate set](predicate-set.md), and the entity set:
$$\Phi := E \times \Pi \times E.$$

The elements of the predication cell set are [predication cells](../../prose/teaching/explanations/predication-cell.md) at which predication valuations assign values.
