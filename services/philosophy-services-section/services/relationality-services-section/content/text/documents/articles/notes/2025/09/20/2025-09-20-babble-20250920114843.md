So I was thinking about [Lakotaean philosophy](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-philosophy.md) and specifically [rock people](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-rock-people.md), who are [known](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowing.md) to [sense](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/sensing.md) and [remember](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/remembering.md) the relations of those [relatives](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotean-relative.md) they are related to.

Anyway, I was thinking about this and realized, that it might be cool for a [MUD engine](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mud-engine.md) to use [stones](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/stone.md) as [omniscient](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/omniscient.md), and then admins can query stones for what the stone knows about whatever. Any stone that can answer can answer, and the admin-ness of an admin is defined by how far they can query into the stone-network. (Stones can perfectly transmit all knowledge between all stones they encounter, instantly, basically - though that language doesn't reflect [Lakotaean metaphysics](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-metaphysics.md).)
