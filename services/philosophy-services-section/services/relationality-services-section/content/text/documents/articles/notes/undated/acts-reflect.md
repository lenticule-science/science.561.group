- [define](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/define.md):: [reflecting](../../../../glossary/terms/reflecting.md) is the minimal [act](act.md) by which a [relational structure](relational structure) actively sustains its own [../conditions/reflexivity](reflexivity.md). It enacts the relation `relating(S, S)` for a [relational structure](relational structure) `S`, maintaining coherence by ensuring that the result is held within the same [../relational system](../relational system) without rupture or escalation of type.

## Derivation

- The act of [reflexing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/reflexing) produces `relating(S, S)`, deepening the [relational structure](relational structure).

- However, for this act to maintain coherence, the [relational structure](relational structure) must not only undergo reflex but also **sustain its own reflexivity** actively.

- This sustained activity is formalized as [reflecting](../../../../glossary/terms/reflecting.md).

- [reflecting](../../../../glossary/terms/reflecting.md) differs from mere [reflexing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/reflexing) by **holding open** the reflexive relation over time, ensuring stability of the [reflexive](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/reflexive.md) operation within the ongoing dynamics of the system.

- Without [reflecting](../../../../glossary/terms/reflecting.md), reflexive acts risk becoming transient or incoherent, failing to anchor the recursive deepening in a stable way.

- Therefore, [reflecting](../../../../glossary/terms/reflecting.md) is necessary wherever reflexivity must be maintained as an **ongoing, structurally coherent act** in the [../relational system](../relational system).
