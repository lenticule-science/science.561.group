- `β⁺` be an autocatalytic recursive attractor  
- `ℑ(β⁺)` be its constraint-inheriting descendant set

Then:

> βᴮ := β⁺ such that ℑ(β⁺) contains an infinite or persistent sequence  
>  {β₀, β₁, β₂, ...} where  
>  ∀ i: βᵢ⁺ induces βᵢ₊₁⁺, and  
>  Ω_rec(βᵢ₊₁) ⊇ Ω_rec(βᵢ)

This defines a recursively inheriting modulation structure —  
one that ***maintains and evolves its own capacity to maintain and evolve***.
