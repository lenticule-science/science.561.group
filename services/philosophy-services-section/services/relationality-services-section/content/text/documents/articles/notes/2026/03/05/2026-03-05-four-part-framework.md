## What the four-part framework is

The treatise (draft 3) organizes the relational framework into four
layers, each building on the previous:

1. **RelationalStructure** — A complete Heyting algebra with modal
   closure and interior. The logical and structural bedrock: recognitions,
   their partial ordering, and the operations of combining, choosing,
   conditioning, and denying.

2. **RelationalLanguage** — A typed lambda calculus (λ_R) internal to
   RelationalStructure. The means of expressing and manipulating
   recognitions computationally. Proven sound and complete for
   RelationalStructures.

3. **RelationalGeometry** — Nuclei (closure operators), flow (directed
   dynamics), and cohesion (the adjoint chain connecting local and
   global). The spatial and dynamic architecture of the relational field.

4. **RelationalPhysics** — States (recognitions read as configurations),
   observables (nuclei applied to states), conservation (invariants
   under flow). The physical reading of relational structure.

Each layer presupposes the previous one: you cannot have Language without
Structure, Geometry without Language, or Physics without Geometry. The
layers are not independent modules but a progressive deepening of the
same relational field.

## How the four parts map to the five movements

The treatise's four parts and draft 12's five movements organize the
same content from different perspectives:

| Four-Part (Treatise) | Five Movements | Content |
|---|---|---|
| RelationalStructure | Movement I + Movement II | Heyting algebra + closure/interior/modality |
| RelationalLanguage | (cross-cutting) | Typed lambda calculus; internal at every level |
| RelationalGeometry | Movement III + Movement IV | Flow + cohesion + stratification |
| RelationalPhysics | Movement V | States + observables + conservation |

Key differences:
- The four-part framework groups logic (I) and stability (II) into a
  single layer (Structure). The five-movement framework separates them
  because the INCITEMENT between logic and stability is philosophically
  significant.
- RelationalLanguage is NOT a movement — it is cross-cutting, providing
  the internal calculus at every level.
- The four-part framework groups dynamics (III) and cohesion (IV) into
  a single layer (Geometry). The five-movement framework separates
  them because directed motion (Flow) is philosophically prior to
  spatial extension (cohesion).

The PRE's five Parts (Logic, Structure, Arithmetic, Geometry, Physics)
are closest to the five-movement structure, with a direct 1:1
correspondence.

## The Filter-Induced Reflexive Reconstruction theorem

The deepest property of the four-part framework: every well-behaved
constraint (Filter) produces a sub-field (Profile) that reproduces ALL
FOUR PARTS internally.

A Filter is a Discipline (constraint on the field) that commutes with
all nuclei, all flow, and with reflexing itself. Its fixed set — the
Profile — inherits:

1. ProfileStructure (a Heyting algebra)
2. ProfileLanguage (a typed lambda calculus)
3. ProfileGeometry (nuclei + flow)
4. ProfilePhysics (states + observables + conservation)

This means: ANY perspective on the relational field, if it is
sufficiently coherent, reproduces the ENTIRE architecture. Observation
does not destroy structure — it specializes it into a complete world.

This is one of three demonstrations of self-grounding (see
self-grounding.md). The four-part framework is not just a convenient
organization — it is a NECESSARY feature of any coherent relational
structure.

## Why four parts and not some other number

The four parts correspond to four qualitative shifts in what the
relational field can do:

1. **Structure**: distinguish, order, combine, condition, deny
   → the field can RECOGNIZE
2. **Language**: express, bind, abstract, apply, normalize
   → the field can COMPUTE
3. **Geometry**: close, flow, stratify, cohere
   → the field can MOVE AND ORGANIZE
4. **Physics**: state, observe, conserve
   → the field can DESCRIBE ITSELF AS PHYSICAL

Each shift is irreducible — no combination of the previous operations
achieves what the new layer provides. The number four is not arbitrary;
it is the number of qualitatively distinct capabilities that the
relational axioms produce.
