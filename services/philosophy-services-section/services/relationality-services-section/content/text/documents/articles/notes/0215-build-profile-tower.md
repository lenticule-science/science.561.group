## Interpretation

Profiles recursively reconstruct the entire relational chain.  PhysicsProfile witnessing *Calculate Profile Syntax* yields ***Build Profile Tower*** ([BuildProfileTower](./0215-build-profile-tower.md)).

Composing these recognitions records the internal chain *Profile Recognitions* to *Profile Semantics* to *Calculate Profile Syntax* to *Geometry Profile* to *Physics Profile*, so *Build Profile Tower* follows.
