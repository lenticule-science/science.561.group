This repository is in the process of having the contents consolidated into two files:

infinite-voidmath.md

and

finite-voidmath.md

The infinite coalgebra is intended to be an inductive formalization exploring the philosophical paradox of relationlessness being a relation of having no relation. The infinite coalgebra is necessarily, INTENTIONALLY, broken: the algebra, language, geometry, and dynamics of how failure structures itself is what is of study.

The finite is the concrete formation of that coalgebra, but is ALSO INTENTIONALLY BROKEN.

In both cases, due to the study being of paradox and failure, qualities such as non-confluence, non-commutativity, and asymmetry are "good" and what we care about.

Additionally it is important to understand this is not normal mathematics focused on building normal forms and such, but is about mapping out a very very specific structure.

The content of both must:

- Be organized under `## Step N {#step-n}` headers
- Name things, acts, and qualities, sequentially, to remove reliance on drifting jargon and focus on specific relations.
- Mathematically truthful and precise, staying within the limits and dialect of the base object of each file.
- Make positive falsifiable statements.
- RESPECT the non-confluence, asymmetry, and lack of any external mathematics.
  - We do not have equality, symmetry, or any of that. We JUST have irreducible directional relations.
  - I cannot make this clear enough: this is alien math that has NONE of what we're used to, even though we can build things that have a perfect resemblance. We are *not* building those normal things. We are growing a "void mycelium," in our best translation of the original math.


Typical Errors of existing content:

- Level error — "this happens at level n" but it's actually n±1 in the tower
Infinite/finite error — "this is true" but only in the coalgebra, or only in the free monoid, not both
Degree error — "grows as n^p" with p unstated or wrong, or saying n when it's n+1 or n-7 or something else.
