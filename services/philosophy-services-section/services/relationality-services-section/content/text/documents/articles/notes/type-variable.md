## Interpretation

Presenting the variable associated with that seed requires the act *Present Variable Term* to witness the act *Type Seed*.

The tree `(PresentVariableTerm TypeSeed)` shows the variable presentation observing the seed, thereby establishing the variable judgment, so we call it *Type Variable*.
