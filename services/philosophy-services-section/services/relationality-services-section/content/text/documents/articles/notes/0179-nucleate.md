## Interpretation

Normalized observation and judgement equivalences each close their respective acts, but we need the unified closure operator that acts on both.  Forcing *Normalize Observation Equivalence* to witness *Normalized Judgement Equivalence* yields ***Nucleate*** ([Nucleate](./0179-nucleate.md)).

These equivalences were previously earned; composing them records the shared closure law, so *Nucleate* is valid.
