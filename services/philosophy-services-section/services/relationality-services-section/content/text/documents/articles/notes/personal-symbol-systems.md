# Idea: Personal Symbol Systems

Model your own symbols as recognitions so you can see how they move across
domains and time.

## 1. Recognitions

- `SymbolRecognition`
  - specific symbol/images (e.g., river, door, machine).
- `UseInstanceRecognition`
  - occurrences of symbols in dreams, notes, art, conversations, MUD events.
- `AssociationRecognition`
  - links to feelings, people, places, archetypes.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Topology:
  - networks of symbols and associations.
- Measurement:
  - frequency over time, shifts in associated meanings, co-occurrence patterns.

This can tie inner work, creative writing, and gameplay together via your own
symbolic vocabulary.
