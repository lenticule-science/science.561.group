I was thinking about [professionalization](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/professionalization.md) and want to explore the concept that the [genre of the contemporary American worker](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genre-of-the-contemporary-american-worker.md) with any "management" rule actually [rewards](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rewarding.md) [administrating](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/administrating.md), not [managing](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/managing.md).

Distinguishing the two as i mean them:

administrating  
rooted in [bureaucratic theory](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bureaucratic-theory.md) and public adminstration, involves [standardizing](../../../../../../glossary/terms/standardizing.md) and [proceduralizing](../../../../../../glossary/terms/proceduralizing.md), [measuring](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/measuring.md) and [quantifying](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/quantifying.md), focused on [compliance](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/compliance.md), [documentation](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/documentation.md)

managing  
rooted in [industrialism](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/industrialism.md) and [scientific management](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/scientific-management.md), involves directing [persons](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/person.md) and [processes](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/process.md) toward [producing](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/producing.md), focused on interpersonal [authority](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authority.md) and [responsibility](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/responsibility.md)

[Sociology of labor](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sociology-of-labor.md) has looked at how [professions](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/profession.md) move through [cycles of rationalization](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cycle-of-rationalization.md), and this occured with the managerial role in the U.S. labor economy, [schooles of business](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/school-of-business.md) and MBA programs teach [business frameworks](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/business-framework.md), business analysis, for administrating metrics across contexts: generating, interpreting, and translating records in some way I'm having difficulty describing, but which serves to allow the managerial layer of a business to serve as a administrative relay of commands coming from above or without.[^1]

Managers aren't rewarded for managing the systems they're a part of, but administrating the workflow assigned by higher systems, using the tools provided, into those systems.

Weirdly this process, while part of [professionalization of management](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/professionalization-of-management.md), has also been part of [deskilling](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/deskilling.md) the role. Rather than having any sort of [craft knowledge of managing people](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/craft-knowledge-of-managing-people.md)

[^1]: Are managers [the slime of many heads](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physarum-polycephalum.md)?
