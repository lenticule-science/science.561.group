Program that identifies, aligns, and stabilises cross-profile patterns between
inner symbolic life and external experiential worlds.

## Definition
- Inputs: inner (dreams, symbols, narratives) and outer (events, projects,
  relationships) artefacts recognised via computer
  [Recognition](../../../../glossary/concepts/phenomena-recognition.md) into
  [Computer Recognition](../computer-recognition.md)
  carriers; initial [Computer Evaluation State](../computer-evaluation-state.md).
- Plan: maintain separate profile towers and correspondences as a
  [Computer Wiring Plan](../computer-wiring-plan.md);
  evaluate/adjust via [Evaluate Step](../evaluate-step.md).
- Persistence: save/replay via [Computer Storage](../storage.md)
  and [Computer Snapshot](../computer-snapshot.md).
- Outputs: stabilised wiring plan/recognitions, or failures.

## Behaviour
- Map correspondences between inner and outer recognitions via earned
  part–whole, association, and flow relations.
- Detect unstable or recursive patterns across profiles (discontinuities,
  loops, attractors).
- Propose stabilisations by adjusting flows or contexts in either profile.

## Invariants
- Profile separation: inner/outer profiles remain distinct but linked via
  earned relations.
- Stability: accepted interventions increase stability of cross-profile
  invariants.
- Failure purity: rejected correspondences emit computer failures; no partial
  mutations.

## Proof
- Derived from: [Computer Kernel](../../../../triage/triage/computer/structure/computer-kernel.md).
- Shows that: multi-profile renormalisation across inner/outer domains can be
  expressed via computer recognition/flow/trace operators without extending
  machine semantics.
