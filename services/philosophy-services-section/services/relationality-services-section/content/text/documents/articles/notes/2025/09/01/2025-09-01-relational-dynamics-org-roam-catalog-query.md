***Relational dynamics*** are the [modes of relation](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mode-of-relation.md) that [induce](../../../../../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inducing.md), [cohere](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cohering.md), and [sustain](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/sustaining.md) [existing](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/existing.md).

The idea is a part of [emsean thought](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsean-thought.md), and the product of [emsean analysis](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsean-analysis.md).
