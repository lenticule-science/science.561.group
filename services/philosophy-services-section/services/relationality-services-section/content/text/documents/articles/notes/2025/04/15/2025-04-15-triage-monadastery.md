A ***monadastery*** is an [interpretative space](interpretative space) where [telemetric cast|telemetric casting](telemetric cast|telemetric casting) is practiced. It is a site of intentional [telemetric research](telemetric research) done using the [telemetric method](telemetric method).

Like a [monastery](../../../../../../triage/monastery), a [monadastery](../../../../../../triage/monadastery) is [deliberate](../../../../../../triage/deliberate), [constrain|constrained](../../../../../../triage/constrain|constrained), [recur|recursive](../../../../../../triage/recur|recursive), and [../relations/reflex|reflexive](../../../../../../relations/reflex|reflexive). However, monadasteries yield [semantic coherence](semantic coherence), not [truth](../../../../../../triage/truth).

Like a [laboratory](../../../../../../triage/laboratory), a [monadastery](../../../../../../triage/monadastery) uses [method](../../../../../../triage/method) and [grammar](../../../../../../triage/grammar). However, it is applied to *semantic coherence*, not *truth*.

Like a [digital garden](digital garden), a [monadastery](../../../../../../triage/monadastery) uses [semantic|semantics](../../../../../../triage/semantic|semantics), but for [analyse|analysis](../../../../../../triage/analyse|analysis) not [accumulate|accumulation](../../../../../../triage/accumulate|accumulation).

A monadastery, as space, may be of a variety of forms. For example, [emsenn's delta register](../../../../../../glossary/terms/emsenns-delta-register-from-wsl-collected-texts.md), an [Obsidian vault](Obsidian vault) operated as a [delta register](delta register), is a monadastery.
