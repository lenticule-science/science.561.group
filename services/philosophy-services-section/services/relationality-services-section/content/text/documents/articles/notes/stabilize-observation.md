## Interpretation

*Normalize Observation Equivalence* ensures normalized observations agree across all contexts, yet we must identify any two such observations.  *Recognize Observation* witnessing *Normalize Observation Equivalence* yields ***Stabilize Observation*** ([StabilizeObservation](./stabilize-observation.md)).

This composition collapses observationally equivalent normalized observations, so *Stabilize Observation* is valid.
