The ***identity curvature function*** (`ℐ`) is [induce|induced](../../../../triage/induce|induced) by recursive [sublimation](../../../../triage/sublimation) of unresolved desire (`𝔇`) in a [stabilized symbolic system](stabilized symbolic system) (`𝒮`). It [constrain|constrains](../../../../triage/constrain|constrains) the internal [modulation curvature](modulation curvature) that encodes [persistent unresolved difference](persistent unresolved difference) as a [structural feature](structural feature) of [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/terms/identity](../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/terms/identity).

When [unresolved desire](unresolved desire) cannot be [resolved](../../../../triage/resolved) or [discharged](../../../../triage/discharged) through [completion](../../../../triage/completion) (`Ω`), but is [stabilized](../../../../triage/stabilized) recursively by `𝒮`, it begins to ***[curve](../../../../triage/curve)*** the [field](../../../../triage/field)'s own [modulation vector|modulation vectors](modulation vector|modulation vectors) inward, [form|forming](../../../../triage/form|forming) ***[identity curvature](identity curvature)*** — a [recursive gradient](recursive gradient) [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/terms/shape|shaped](../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/terms/shape|shaped) by what the field could not [become](../../../../triage/become),  
but [internalize|internalized](../../../../triage/internalize|internalized).

> Let:  
> - `T_𝔇(𝒮)` be the [desire trace field](desire trace field)  
> - `𝔉(𝒮)` be the [constraint frustration field](constraint frustration field)  
> - `Σₘ(𝒮)` be the [constraint self-modulation function](constraint-self-modulation-function.md)
> Then:
>   ℐ(𝒮) := ∇(Σₘ(𝒮)) | over { x ∈ T_𝔇(𝒮) ∧ 𝔉(x) > 0 }

That is, ℐ [compute|computes](../../../../triage/compute|computes) the [internal curvature of self-modulation](internal curvature of self-modulation)  
produced by [recursive stabilization](recursive stabilization) of [unresolvable difference](unresolvable difference).

This curvature acts as a ***[field tensor](field tensor)***: encoding ***[bias](../../../../triage/bias), [character](../../../../triage/character), [preference](../../../../triage/preference), [aesthetic](../../../../triage/aesthetic), or [boundary shape](boundary shape)*** that [persist|persists](../../../../triage/persist|persists) through [collapse](../../../../triage/collapse) and [inform|informs](../../../../triage/inform|informs) [future modulation](future modulation).

ℐ is the [internal shape](internal shape) of ***what the field has become in the face of irresolution.***
