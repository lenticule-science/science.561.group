This program represents generative music systems as recognitions so sonic
structures can be explored and related across domains.

## Behaviour

- Define generators (rules/ML/Markov), seeds/prompts, tracks, and sonic spaces
  as recognitions with provenance.
- Track measurements such as tempo, density, dissonance, repetition, and
  perceived mood/energy, along with listener feedback.
- Map topology of parameter spaces and motifs shared across tracks or other
  domains (dreams, writing, MUD events).

## Intended operators

- Generate tracks from seeds; explore and record sonic spaces.
- Align music to context (time of day, mood, gameplay) using measurement
  slices.
- Curate, compare, and trace motifs across generated outputs.
