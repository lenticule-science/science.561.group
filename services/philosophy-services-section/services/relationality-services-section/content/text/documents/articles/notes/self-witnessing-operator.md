Generates recognitions that witness the profile witnessing itself; fixed points
approximate reflexive awareness.

## Definition
- Inputs: a [Reflexive Profile](./reflexive-profile.md) and the
  [Nucleus](../../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/nucleus.md) operator.
- Output: updated [Reflexive Profile](./reflexive-profile.md)
  containing self-witnessing recognitions.
- Behaviour: apply nucleus to self-referential recognitions to stabilise
  self-witnessing; iterate to approach fixed points.

## Invariants
- Reflexivity: results remain self-referential.
- Stability: fixed points represent stable self-awareness states.

## Proof
- Uses: [Reflexive Profile](./reflexive-profile.md) and
  [Nucleus](../../../../../services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/nucleus.md).
- Produces: refined [Reflexive Profile](./reflexive-profile.md).
- Shows that: self-witnessing is expressible within the earned logic/nucleus
  framework.
