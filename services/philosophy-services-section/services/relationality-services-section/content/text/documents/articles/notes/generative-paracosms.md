# Idea: Generative Paracosms (Imagined Worlds)

Represent rich, evolving imaginary worlds—paracosms—as recognitions, and let
them grow generatively inside the relational computer.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `ParacosmRecognition`
  - a particular imagined world.
  - fields: premise, themes, origin (childhood, project, dream), boundaries.

- `RegionRecognition`
  - spatial/relational areas within a paracosm (countries, planes, networks).

- `EntityRecognition`
  - beings, groups, artifacts unique to the paracosm.
  - fields: roles, capabilities, relationships.

- `RuleRecognition`
  - rules of physics, magic, social order, metaphysics for the paracosm.

- `HistoryEventRecognition`
  - in-world events that shape the paracosm’s timeline.

## 2. Generative machinery

- `ParacosmTemplateRecognition`
  - seeds/grammars for generating new regions, entities, histories.

- `ParacosmProcgenRunbookRecognition`
  - runbooks that:
    - expand unexplored regions,
    - introduce new entities or conflicts,
    - simulate consequences of events according to `RuleRecognition`.

- `ParacosmSessionRecognition`
  - specific sessions of engaging with the paracosm (solo play, writing,
    MUD-like exploration, agent-driven simulation).

## 3. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - intra-paracosm:
    - maps of regions, kinship/social graphs, causal/historical links.
  - inter-paracosm:
    - links to real-world recognitions (parallels with places, people, issues),
    - links to other paracosms (shared motifs, crossovers).

- Measurement:
  - how often you visit/use a paracosm,
  - which motifs/structures recur,
  - “health” of a paracosm (stagnant vs generative, overwhelming vs grounding).

## 4. Runbooks and uses

- `seed_paracosm`: create a new paracosm from prompts, dreams, or themes.
- `expand_paracosm_region`: generatively add detail where it’s needed.
- `trace_paracosm_motifs`: surface connections between a paracosm and your
  dreams, writing, or life events.
- `co_simulate_paracosm`: let agents run constrained simulations to explore
  consequences of changes without overwriting your core canon.

Generative paracosms become a structured way to let worlds unfold alongside
your math, MUD, ecology, and personal life—coherent enough to relate to
everything else, open enough to stay genuinely imaginative.
