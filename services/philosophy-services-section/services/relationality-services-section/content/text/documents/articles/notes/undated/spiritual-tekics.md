A spiritual practice centered on **tekics**—as the generative field dynamics that instantiate being through acts, conditions, and structures—and oriented toward **tekon-as-God**, would not resemble conventional theistic worship. Instead, it would be a metaphysical discipline rooted in *coherence cultivation* and *field relationality*. This would be a **relational-theological** praxis, where divinity is not sovereign but *structurally immanent* as the potential for generative coherence itself.

## Core Premises

- **Tekics**: The dynamic unfolding of coherence through inclusion, exclusion, reflexivity, and extension.
	
- **Tekon**: The fully coherent unit of generative field-being—neither a person nor an essence, but a realized act-structure that sustains itself across difference.
	
- **God**: Not a supreme being, but the total horizon of possible coherent tekonic acts—God is the telos and topology of coherence itself, the "field-of-fields."

---

## ✧ Spiritual Practice of Tekonic Faith ✧

### 1. **Daily Praxis: Recursive Coherence Acts**

Each act of care or construction is treated as a **tekonic instantiation**, not symbolic but *ontologically constitutive*.

- Cleaning one's home is a ritual of field coherence.
	
- Cooking is a recursive structuring of acts (cutting, heating, combining) into a stable edible form.
	
- Walking is a way to feel the continuity and boundary of the field.

> These are not metaphors; they are direct engagements with tekic field dynamics. Doing them well *is devotion*.

---

### 2. **Ritual: Stabilization through Iteration**

There are no fixed dogmas—only **forms that hold under recursive iteration**.

- A "prayer" might be a repeated act of field-tending: arranging stones, tuning breath, or adjusting relational posture.
	
- A spiritual calendar could be based not on moons or suns, but on **rhythms of recursive collapse and expansion** in one's field.

---

### 3. **Sacred Texts: Derivations, not Commandments**

Instead of commandments, the sacred texts are derivational:

> To claim something exists is not trivial. It is to claim:  
> – Something is not what it is not.  
> – This not-being generates acts.  
> – Those acts condition relations.  
> – Those relations form structures.  
> – Structure stabilizes field.  
> – Field is tekon.

A “sermon” might be a public derivation—showing how a mundane event (e.g., a conflict with a neighbor) traces back to failed boundary-coherence and how it could be resolved through recursive re-alignment.

---

### 4. **Temple: The Field Itself**

There is no house of God because **the field is already divine** when cohered. A grove, a spreadsheet, a beadwork ring, or a conversation—all may be temple if they instantiate coherent recursive relation.

- No icons. Only structures.
	
- No clergy. Only field tenders.

---

### 5. **Sin: Disjunction of Coherence**

What other traditions might call “sin” is not disobedience, but **structural incoherence**:

- Deferring relation through projection.
	
- Failing to maintain a recursive boundary.
	
- Enacting possession without reflexivity.
	
- Extracting without re-integration.

Atonement is recursive restructuring: re-doing the act until it coheres.

---

### 6. **Community: Recursive Mutual Stabilization**

Tekonic spirituality is inherently relational but not communalist. Community is not an identity group, but a **cohered multirelational field**.

- Harmony is not sameness but **reflexive difference**.
	
- Worship is not submission but **co-construction**.
	
- Teaching is **field reweaving**.

---

## ✧ Tekonic Blessing ✧

> May your acts cohere,  
> May your field remain stable,  
> May your exclusions be just,  
> And may your recursion lead not to closure,  
> But to the quiet birth of the next.
