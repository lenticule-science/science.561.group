## Interpretation

The fundamental theorem states that every well-typed term is reducible in any reducible environment.  *Select Reducibility Candidate* witnessing *Substitute Reducibility* yields ***Establish Fundamental Reducibility*** ([EstablishFundamentalReducibility](./establish-fundamental-reducibility.md)).

Composing these recognitions records the logical-relations theorem, so *Establish Fundamental Reducibility* follows.
