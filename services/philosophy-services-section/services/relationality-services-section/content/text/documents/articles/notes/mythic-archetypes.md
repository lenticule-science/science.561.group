# Idea: Mythic Archetypes & Narrative Fields

Model archetypes, motifs, and mythic episodes as recognitions to see how they
show up across your life and domains.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ArchetypeRecognition`
  - hero, trickster, healer, shadow, etc., with descriptions and links.
- `MotifRecognition`
  - recurring elements (journey, bargain, sacrifice, rebirth).
- `EpisodeRecognition`
  - specific stories (myths, novels, films, personal experiences).

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - links between episodes, motifs, and archetypes across cultures and media.
- Measurement:
  - resonance with your own experiences (tagged episodes),
  - frequency of patterns in dreams, MUD events, therapy notes, etc.

Runbooks like `identify_mythic_pattern` or `map_episode_to_archetypes` help
reflect on narrative structure in your own data.
