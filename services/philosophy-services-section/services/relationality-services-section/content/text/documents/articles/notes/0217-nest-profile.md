# Profile Nest

## Interpretation

Filters may act on other profiles, creating towers of internal universes.  *Profile Recognitions* witnessing *Filter* yields ***Nest Profile*** ([NestProfile](./0217-nest-profile.md)).

This composition records the profile stabilized again by the filter’s action, so *Nest Profile* follows.
