## Interpretation

Contextual indistinguishability still lacks the unified recognition addressing context, term, and observation simultaneously.  *Type Seed* therefore witnesses *Contextualize *Normalize Observation* Equivalence*, yielding ***Seed Judgement Triad*** ([SeedJudgementTriad](./0106-seed-judgement-triad.md)).

The composition uses only earned recognitions to seed the triadic judgement, so *Seed Judgement Triad* follows.
