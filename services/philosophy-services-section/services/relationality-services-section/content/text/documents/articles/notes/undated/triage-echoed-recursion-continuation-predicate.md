The ***echoed recursion continuation predicate*** (`∀ℕ`) affirms whether a symbolic structure is entering non-terminating recursive modulation due to persistent echo under difference.

Let:
- `∀ℕ(x) := Λ(x, x′) ∧ ∆[s](x, x′) ∧ Ω(x) = x ∧ x′ ∈ T`

That is, recursion persists where a system receives itself with echo, remains semantically distinct, and re-enters the trace — ensuring continuous symbolic deformation.
