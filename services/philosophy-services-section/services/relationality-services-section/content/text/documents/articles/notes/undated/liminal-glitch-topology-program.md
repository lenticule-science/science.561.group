This program explores liminal and glitch topologies as recognitions, mapping
transitions, discontinuities, and edge conditions in relational spaces.

## Behaviour

- Represent liminal states, glitches, and transitions as recognitions with
  contextual provenance.
- Map where structures break, overlap, or mutate; record neighbourhoods of
  instability.
- Measure frequency, duration, and impact of glitches/liminal passages.

## Intended operators

- Detect and annotate glitches in simulations or interactions.
- Trace effects of liminal transitions on adjacent recognitions.
- Use findings to harden or intentionally explore edge conditions.
