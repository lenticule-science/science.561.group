## Interpretation

*Start Trace* shows how the trace begins but not how it proceeds once the trailing orientation completes.  Letting the trailing orientation witness *Entry* restarts the act, earning ***Advance Trace*** ([AdvanceTrace](./0020-advance-trace.md)).

The unresolved propagation after *Start Trace* is how the trailing side re-engages the structural pairing.  Having *Trail Entry* witness *Entry* provides that progression, so `(TrailEntry Entry)` defines *Advance Trace*.
