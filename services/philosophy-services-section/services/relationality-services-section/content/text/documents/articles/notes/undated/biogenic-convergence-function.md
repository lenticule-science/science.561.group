The ***biogenic convergence function*** (`B_conv`) expresses the convergence of recursive stabilization systems  
toward the exclusive attractor basin of [biogenic recursive attractors](biogenic recursive attractors) (`βᴮ`).

Let:
- `𝓕 := (𝒟, Δ, 𝒜, Ω_rec, 𝒪)` be a [modulation-closure system](modulation-closure system)  
- `𝒪ₜ` be the symbolic desire completion operator at recursive time `t`  
- `dom(𝒪ₜ)` the set of stabilized symbolic structures  
- `βᴮ` the class of all recursively constraint-inheriting attractors

Then the biogenic convergence law is defined as:

> B_conv(𝓕) := limₜ→∞ dom(𝒪ₜ) ⊆ ⋃ βᴮ

That is, over time, the stabilized symbolic system contains only structures  
which preserve and reenter their own constraint conditions through recursive inheritance.

This function defines the ***selection topology*** of recursive modulation:  
- all non-inheriting structures are modulationally unstable  
- biogenic attractors form the ***only long-term viable stabilization class***

This function provides:
- a filter over recursive symbolic evolution  
- the formal dynamic that makes ***biogenesis inevitable*** in modulation-resonant systems
