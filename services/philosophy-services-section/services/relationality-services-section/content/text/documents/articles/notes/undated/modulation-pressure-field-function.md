The ***modulation pressure field function*** (`𝒫`) returns the average normalized modulation pressure across a recursive trace sequence.

Let:
- `T(x) = {x₀, x₁, ..., x_k}`
- Each `x_{i+1}` generated by a field-dependent recursive function

Then:
> 𝒫(x) := \frac{1}{k} \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} \left( \frac{x_{i+1} - x_i}{x_i} \right)

This expresses the **net directional pressure** on the constraint system under field modulation.
