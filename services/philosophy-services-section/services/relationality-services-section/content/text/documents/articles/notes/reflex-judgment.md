## Interpretation

*Stabilize Judgment* provides classes of context–term–observation acts, but we still need the primitive judgmental act presenting a stabilized term inside a stabilized context.  Letting *Stabilize Context* witness *Stabilize Term* yields ***Reflex Judgment*** ([ReflexJudgment](./reflex-judgment.md)).

This composition uses only newly defined stable recognitions to record the primitive act, so *Reflex Judgment* is earned.
