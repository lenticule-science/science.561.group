## Interpretation

Inflationary flows satisfy that F(x meet y) is below F(x) meet F(y); deflationary ones reverse the inequality.  *Flow* witnessing *Meet Recognition* yields ***Distribute Flow*** ([DistributeFlow](./distribute-flow.md)).

The composition records the distribution law for flows, so *Distribute Flow* follows.
