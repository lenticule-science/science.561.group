- [summary](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-06-11/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/summary):: **Reflect** is the act of [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating) a [relational structure](relational structure) to another distinct structure while preserving the relational identity of the source.

Reflect extends [../../../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating](../../../../../../triage/relationality-slop-2025-06-11/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating.md) by enforcing that the source and target are distinct (`S ≠ T`). It performs active inclusion **across difference** while maintaining the originating structure's context.

Reflect enables lateral relational connections and induces the condition of [relational distinction](relational distinction).

## Expression

reflect(S, T): relating(S, T) with S ≠ T and S preserved

## Properties

- Asymmetric: `reflect(S, T) ≠ reflect(T, S)`
- Difference-preserving: requires `S ≠ T`
- Context-preserving: `S` retains its relational identity after reflection
