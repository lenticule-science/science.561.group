***Excluding*** is the [act](../../../../../../glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) of [not](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/negation.md) [including](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md): where including is a [thing](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md) [being](../25/2025-08-25-tekodynamics-concepts-being-being.md) within another thing, excluding is a thing *not* being within another thing.

The [meaning](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meaning.md) of *excluding* is [derived](../14/2025-08-14-relational-systems-terms-derivation-deriving.md) in [Deriving Relational Dynamics](../26/2025-08-26-deriving-relational-dynamics.md), where it is [determined](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/determining.md) to be a [necessary determination](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/necessary-determination.md) of [existence](../29/2025-08-29-relational-systems-concepts-existence-existence.md) [existing](2025-08-15-relational-systems-concepts-existence-existing.md).
