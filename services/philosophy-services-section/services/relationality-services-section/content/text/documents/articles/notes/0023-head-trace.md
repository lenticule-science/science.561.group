## Interpretation

*Complete Trace* captures the trace but leaves the immediate act unspecified.  Letting the leading orientation witness the whole trace extracts its head.

*Lead Entry* can now observe *Complete Trace*, isolating the first act present.  The tree uses only earned terms, so ***Head Trace*** ([HeadTrace](./0023-head-trace.md)) is valid.
