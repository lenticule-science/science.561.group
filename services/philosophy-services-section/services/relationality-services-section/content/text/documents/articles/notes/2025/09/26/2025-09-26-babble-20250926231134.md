A lot of [my writing](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-writing.md) recently has been focused on [relational dynamics](../01/2025-09-01-relational-dynamics-org-roam-catalog-query.md) in the [fields](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/field-of-study.md) of [sociology](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sociology.md) and [psychology](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychology.md), and I thought it might be [fun](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fun.md) to look at something else: [Physarum polycephalum](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physarum-polycephalum.md). It's a [slime mold](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/slime-mold.md) that's been called the "many-headed slime," that's what [polycephalum](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/polycephalum.md) means.

First described by [Johann Heinrich Freidrich Link](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/johann-heinrich-freidrich-link.md), a [German](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/german-person.md) [naturalist](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/naturalist.md) back in [1833ce](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1833ce.md).

I should write a history of study of Physarum polysephalum and I'm tempted to but I actually want to explain how it's been an [object of study](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/object-of-study.md) in other fields like [mass transit](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mass-transit.md) and logic.

And through that study, led to things like the [Tero–Kobayashi–Nakagaki equation](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/tero-kobayashi-nakagaki-equation.md), which is what I think might actually be useful in my studies of how [social media platforms](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-media-platform.md) interact with [imagination](../../../../../../glossary/terms/imagination.md).

I got here specifically because I've been thinking of the concept of [pressure](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pressure.md), where I've been elsewhere describing [change](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/change.md) or [input](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/input.md), especially in the context of relational dynamics.

I think the [homotopy-type theory paths](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/homotopy-type-theory-path.md) and P. polycephalum's tubes might also relate to how edges/boundaries form with relational dynamics, but that's… a vaguer shape.

And, it's also, like, late, so I'm not really going to get into any of this tonight. I think I want to instead pivot toward thinking about how to get better about making more kinds of nodes in the database, so I can more easily make nodes for specific claims, questions, ideas, postulates, etc.

I recently added "field" and "instantiates" as two properties most of my GnoponEmacs nodes have (or should have), but I'm not… loving that. I've been thinking, as always, about [theory of MUD design](../../../../../../../../services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/theory-of-mud-design.md), and am wondering about bringing in a map of contemporary engine/systems jargon to see which are the most applicable for the kind of weird system that GnoponEmacs is, where it's not [automatic](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/automatic.md), but it ias [autonomic](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/autonomic.md): that is, it does internal processing, but only directly in response to input. (Maybe that'll change down the line if I integrate a [Fediverse client](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fediverse-client.md) or something.)
