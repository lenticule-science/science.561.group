## The problem

The system has two disconnected layers of temporal coverage. The philosophical layer is strong: `time` as a foundational term, the `state-time-and-event` survey covering McTaggart through Whitehead through Buddhist momentariness, and `temporal-autonomy` and `temporal-middle` in critical theory. The operational layer has some terms: `deadline`, `schedule`, `cadence`. But there is no bridge between them.

The consequence: when the system reasons about temporal aspects of work, endeavors, governance, or normative systems, it has either abstract metaphysics or concrete scheduling vocabulary — nothing in between. There is no concept of how long something takes (duration), how far ahead to plan (horizon), how entities move through temporal phases (lifecycle), how events return (recurrence), or how time creates pressure (urgency). The endeavor term has a temporal-structure section, but it's isolated — nothing else in the work domain connects to it.

Meanwhile, the operational infrastructure lacks temporal grounding for concepts that are inherently temporal: norms have validity periods, mandates have terms, duties persist across role-holders, governance cycles repeat, reviews happen periodically. The governance terms written recently (norm, duty, mandate, accountability, charter) don't address their temporal dimensions.

## What exists

**Philosophical foundations (strong):**
- `library/terms/time.md` — A-series/B-series, duration, arrow of time, cyclical/spiral time
- `library/.../texts/state-time-and-event-survey.md` — deep survey of substance vs. process accounts
- `library/.../terms/event.md`, `state.md`, `condition.md` — foundational ontology with temporal aspects
- `library/.../process-philosophy/terms/` — concrescence, satisfaction, event (Whiteheadian)

**Work-domain temporal terms (thin but present):**
- `deadline` — temporal constraint converting priority to urgency
- `schedule` — time-bound mapping of work to calendar
- `cadence` — rhythm of recurring operational events
- `endeavor` — has temporal-structure section (aim-terminated, indefinite, aim-open)
- `milestone` — named checkpoint (time-adjacent but not deeply temporal)

**Critical theory temporal concepts (isolated):**
- `temporal-autonomy` — anarchist concept of self-determined time
- `temporal-middle` — cybernetic-postliberal concept
- `speed` — Virilio's dromology

**Operational infrastructure (scheduling done, concepts missing):**
- Planning-infrastructure plan completed (frontmatter schema, compute-plan-status temporal)
- `ops-multi-scale` objective open — needs grounded temporal vocabulary to progress
- Review commands exist (/weekly-review, /standup) but without temporal theory behind them

## What's missing

1. **Bridging terms** — concepts that connect philosophical time to operational time: duration, horizon, lifecycle, recurrence, urgency
2. **Temporality of normative systems** — how norms, duties, mandates, governance structures depend on and relate to time
3. **Temporality of endeavors** — how the temporal structure of an endeavor shapes its governance, planning, and operational rhythms
4. **Infrastructure connections** — linking the enriched library concepts to operational tools
