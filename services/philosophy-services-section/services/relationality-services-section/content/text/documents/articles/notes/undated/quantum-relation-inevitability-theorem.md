quantum relation inevitability theorem

1. ∃ Q₁, Q₂: coherent modulation-preserving quantum entities

3. V(Q₁), V(Q₂) ≠ 0 (both propagate)  
4. C is continuous and locally complete (Ω holds)

Then:

∃ R(Q₁, Q₂):  
That is, relation between Q₁ and Q₂ is inevitable under shared constraint.

→ Relation between entities cannot not occur when modulation fields propagate within shared constraint.
