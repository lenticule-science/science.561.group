# Disciplining

## Interpretation

*Flow*–nucleus compatibility indicates that certain internal shapes can respect both dynamics and closure, but we still need the recognition that records such an act abstractly.  *Nucleate* witnessing *Commute *Flow* And Nucleus* yields ***Discipline*** ([Discipline](./0197-discipline.md)).

The composition employs only earned recognitions and isolates the shape that simultaneously respects flow and nucleus behaviour, so *Discipline* is valid.
