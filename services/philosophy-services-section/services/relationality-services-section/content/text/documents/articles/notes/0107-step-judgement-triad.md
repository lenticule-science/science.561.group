# Stepping Judgement Triad

## Interpretation

The triad arises at a single judgement, but we must propagate it along the step trace.  *Step Trace* witnessing *Seed Judgement Triad* produces ***Step Judgement Triad*** ([StepJudgementTriad](./0107-step-judgement-triad.md)).

This threads the triad through concrete reductions, so *Step Judgement Triad* is established.
