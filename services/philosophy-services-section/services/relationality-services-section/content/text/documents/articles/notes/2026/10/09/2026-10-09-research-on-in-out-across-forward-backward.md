# Research on in, out, across, forward, backward

## The theory

1. **Relating is the substrate.** Relating is "the directed act that constitutes its terms" (`REL`content/text/glossary/terms/relating.md:14-22). Knowing, communicating and playing derive from it, distinguished by where they act relative to an agent's boundary (`CS`2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:14-16). The Leader: "relating->knowing,communicating,playing".
2. **Forward differentiates relating into in, out and across.** The Leader: "foward->in,out,across". Forward is the direction of the transfer nucleus Delta_t, "extension across all one-step futures" (`CS`2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-112; `HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:24-38). In is knowing, out is playing, across is communicating (`CS`animating-as-agency-descent.md:18-25).
3. **In, out and across are the triad.** They are keyed one to one to the knowledge, evidence and truth axes (`AOE`relating/docs/agency-architecture.md:153-162, 184-190), the three orthogonal axes that Avron's theorem makes "the universal structural commitment" (`AGY`content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/agentive-triad.md:58-72). Animating binds the three into one agent (`AOE`agency-architecture.md:281-289).
4. **Backward is the saturating return that settles them.** Backward is the direction of the saturation nucleus sigma_t, restriction to sub-histories: "The past has finished speaking" (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:15-22, 36-38). Playing closes the loop and re-seeds (`CS`animating-as-agency-descent.md:65).
5. **The five directions and their two nuclei compose the nuclear quartet.** Forward and backward are the orientations of the two commuting nuclei. The quartet is the four sub-algebras (A, Fix(j_1), Fix(j_2), A*), exhaustive (`MATH`content/text/glossary/terms/nuclear-quartet.md:14-39). Its corners are the free fiber, the backward-settled, the forward-settled, and H*_t, "the locus where both expansions have already reached their limits" (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:50-63). The faculty quartet (communicating, knowing, playing, animating) has the same shape: three directions and the binding of them (`CS`2026/06/21/2026-06-21-agency-spiral-self-similar-faculty-quartet.md:11-18).
6. **The cascade recurs at each scale and leads on to more.** The agent is "settled below, open above" and re-seeds relating at the next scale, where the quartet recurs as socializing (across), computing (in), gaming (out) and organizing (binds) (`CS`2026/06/21/2026-06-21-agency-spiral-self-similar-faculty-quartet.md:21-40; `EMS`2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-codecs-on-faces-activitypub-across-the-agency-scales.md:45-55). At the site tier the IN site is dev.bare-cedar-fog.emsenn.net, the OUT site is ops.561.group, and the ACROSS site is the WMAP federation membrane between them (`EMS`2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-the-four-strata-and-the-missing-across-site.md:38-52). "The descent does not terminate at one agent; it spirals" (spiral note:25).
7. **The two orders the Leader paired with it are instances.** Section 5.

Sections 1 to 3 give the Leader's words and the sources. Section 4 gives the joins. Section 6 lists what is not yet derived.

### 3.1 In, out, across: the three faces of an agent

- knowing: "inside: transforms the agent's interior state (reflection)"
- playing: "outside: acts via the world/opponent"; renamed from gaming
- animating: not a boundary faculty but the descent that binds the three

(`CS`2026/06/21/2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md:14-25; frontmatter

### 3.2 Forward and backward: the two nuclei and the backward family

**Saturation looks backward, transfer looks forward.** The relational-universe
unit defines two nuclei on each fiber H_t of the history presheaf:

- sigma_t, saturation: the largest element of H_t whose restriction to every
  proper sub-history t_0 < t is no greater than that of a
  (`HYP`saturation-nucleus.md:7-12). "Looks toward: Sub-histories (the past)";
  "The past has finished speaking" (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:15-22, 36-38).
- Delta_t, transfer: the smallest transfer-stable element above a, where
  transfer-stable means present, as a restriction, in every one-step extension
  s*t with s independent of t (`HYP`transfer-nucleus.md:7-15). "Looks toward:
  Extensions (the future)"; "The future has already sent its information back"
  (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:24-32, 36-38).

The same unit states the direction in words: "Saturation looks inward to the
history already accumulated; transfer looks outward to the history yet to come"
(saturation-transfer-dyad.md:48), and "two directions of expansion: backward
(into what the past forces) and forward (into what the future has already
committed)" (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:63). It also corrects a name: the
operator was once called "continuation", which "suggests moving forward in time,
whereas Delta_t acts by pulling information *backward* from extensions. Transfer
correctly names the direction" (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:75).

The mash note of 2026-04-21 says the same with the labels attached: sigma is
"backward-closure, consistency with history" and Delta is "forward-closure,
extension across all one-step futures" (`CS`2026/04/21/mash-as-4d-spatiotemporal-knowledge-manifold-and-oqra-as-nuclear-quartet.md:109-112).

The convergence H*_t = Fix(sigma_t) intersect Fix(Delta_t) is "the doubly
quiescent layer" (saturation-transfer-dyad.md:50-56). The relational-universe
note on nuclear functions lists direction for each map: sigma_t and Delta_t act
"Within H_t"; the restriction maps H(g) go "Backward along g"; natural
transformations act "Fiberwise, coherent" (`REL`content/text/documents/articles/notes/nuclear-functions.md:28-33).

**The backward family in the logic section.** The corpus names "three
structurally-distinct backward direction operations": sigma as a 90-degree axis
rotation between the knowledge theory and the truth theory; conflation as
reflection of the knowledge axis; and cosettling, the pre-image T^{-1} of a
closure step, which "reverses ordinal time without acting on values" (`S`philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-three-backward-structures-and-their-symmetry.md:11-15).
Here "sigma" is a different object from the saturation nucleus (it rotates the
four-valued bilattice FOUR; it is not a nucleus), and the note is `status: open`.
The theorem that the forward trajectory sits inside the iterated cosettling and
that the inclusion is generally strict is the concept
`S`philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/backward-closure-recovers-forward-trajectory.md:2-11
(`status: proved`); it lists "Backward-MLD" as open downstream (same file:108).

**Forward and backward search.** The agency section's planning notes define
forward search (from the initial state toward a goal) and backward search
(regression from the goal toward the initial state) (`AGY`content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/forward-search.md:19-20 and backward-search.md:19-20). They are
planning techniques from Ghallab, Nau and Traverso; nothing in them mentions in,

### 3.3 The nuclear quartet

Defined in two places that agree:

- Math glossary: the nuclear quartet is the four sub-algebras
  (A, Fix(j_1), Fix(j_2), A*) determined by two commuting nuclei j_1, j_2 on a
  Heyting algebra A, with A* = Fix(j_1 o j_2) = Fix(j_1) intersect Fix(j_2). It is
  exhaustive ("There is no fifth option"), and commutativity is "the hinge"
  (`MATH`content/text/glossary/terms/nuclear-quartet.md:14-39).
- Obsmetrologia blueprint, with Lean names: the Nuclear Quartet Theorem
  (`NuclearSquare.compNucleus`, `NuclearSquare.fix_pi_iff`) and
  `NuclearSquare.corner`, which reads the four sub-algebras off the four
  articulation phases (absurd, potential, active, stable) (`OBS`docs/blueprint/src/chapters/recurring-four/03-the-nuclear-quartet-two-commuting-nuclei.tex:78-85).

**The pair is read three ways in the house.** The blueprint states that the
nuclear pair "is read either as the two orders (j_k, j_t) of the bilattice or
as the temporal pair (sigma, Delta) --- saturation and transfer" (same file:79).
Elsewhere the pair is read as meaning-closure and execution-closure:

- In the 2026-06-25 skillful-work paper, sigma settles "what a produces" (the
  type of its output) and Delta settles "how a runs" (its control flow); the four
  sub-algebras are Inquiry (neither closed), Procedure (sigma closed), Derivation
  (Delta closed) and Process (both closed) (`EMS`2026/06/25/2026-06-25-research-on-the-nuclear-quartet-of-skillful-work.md:112-113, 170-173, 220-225; status `paper-draft`).
- The work-science glossary uses that reading with the relational-universe
  notation: Procedure is an element of Fix(sigma_t) not in H*_t with
  sigma_t(W)=W and Delta_t(W) different from W (`WORK`content/text/glossary/terms/procedure.md:63-67);
  the runbook's step sequence is in Fix(Delta_t) and its trigger norm in
  Fix(sigma_t) (`WORK`content/text/glossary/terms/runbook.md:21, 90, 124); a duty
  is discharged when sigma_t and Delta_t both fix the performance, i.e. it lies in
  H*_t (`WORK`services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/duty.md:49, 98).

So the same symbols sigma_t, Delta_t, H*_t are read as past and future in the
hyperverse units and as meaning and execution in the work-science terms. Whether

**The shadows.** The aperiodic-autopoiesis cell has a fiberwise nuclear pair

and strict-finite-nuclear-collapse-shadow.md:3-8, 14-16).

**Other fours called a quartet in the corpus.** The relational entity's growth

### 3.4 The triad

The house holds several things called a triad. None is marked as the triad.

1. **Truth, knowledge, evidence.** The three orthogonal bilattice axes; by
   Avron's theorem every interlaced bounded bilattice is a twist product, so the
   triad is "the universal structural commitment" (`AGY`content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/agentive-triad.md:58-72). Their D_4 generators are
   negation, conflation and sigma (agentive-triad.md:80-82).
2. **The agentive triad: speaking, doing, learning.** The conjecture
   "speaking : truth :: doing : knowledge :: learning : evidence"
   (agentive-triad.md:152), refined to two tiers: speaking and doing are
   horizontal siblings within a fixed theory, learning is the vertical movement
   that extends the theory (agentive-triad.md:88-95). Doing-acts split into a
   sigma-nucleus kind and a Delta-nucleus kind, "Eight doing-act classes total:
   4 Belnap x 2 strata" (agentive-triad.md:97-110). The statement is an open
   conjecture in its own words.
3. **The three faculties** communicating, knowing, playing, with axes truth,
   knowledge, evidence (section 3.1), which is the same three axes as item 1.
4. **The judgement triad** (context, term, observation recognitions along a
   trace), built by the seed, step and trace acts (`REL`content/text/documents/articles/notes/judgment-triad.md:12-24; status `plan`).
5. **The semiotic triad model** has a six-line glossary file (identifier, name, date) and no content
   (`S`information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semiotic-triad-model.md).

Items 1 and 3 line up by axis: communicating with truth, knowing with
knowledge, playing with evidence. Item 2 pairs speaking with truth, doing with
knowledge and learning with evidence. The animating note seats the faculties
differently again: "speaking (communicating) at doing, teaching/learning
(knowing) at working, and playing closes the loop and re-seeds"
(animating-as-agency-descent.md:65). Learning is paired with evidence in item 2
and with knowing in that note; the corpus does not reconcile them.

## 4. The joins

1. **In, out, across and the triad.** The three directions are the three faculties, and the faculties are keyed to the three axes (`AOE`agency-architecture.md:157-161): across is truth, in is knowledge, out is evidence. The faculty list and the axis list are the same three, so the triad of section 3.4 item 1 is the triad of the directions. The speaking, doing, learning triad (item 2) pairs with the same three axes.
2. **Forward, backward and the nuclear quartet.** The nuclei point backward (sigma_t, saturation, the past) and forward (Delta_t, transfer, the future); the corners are the free algebra, the sigma-quiescent, the Delta-quiescent and the doubly quiescent H*_t (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:36-56; MATH nuclear-quartet.md:24-29).
3. **Forward before in, out, across.** Relating yields the faculties (section 3.5) and forward yields the directions (Leader). Delta_t's fixed points are what every one-step extension already carries (`HYP`transfer-nucleus.md:7-15). The Leader's statement is the theory of the join.
4. **Backward after in, out, across.** Backward settles what forward differentiates: H*_t is the layer both nuclei fix (`HYP`saturation-transfer-dyad.md:50-56), and the triad's glue, animating, is the settled agent (`AOE`agency-architecture.md:281-289).
5. **The faculty quartet and the nuclear quartet** are both fours with a binding element: three directions and their binding, two nuclei and their joint fixed layer.
