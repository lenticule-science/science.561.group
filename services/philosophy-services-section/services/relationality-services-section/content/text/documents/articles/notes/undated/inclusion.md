- [../acts/define](../../../../acts/define):: [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) is the [condition](../../../../triage/condition) brought about by the [../acts/act](../../../../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../../../../acts/include|including). It specifies that a [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) is held within what is, marking it as present within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../../../../acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../../../../acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing):
    - Be included in what it is (producing [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion));
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [exclusion](../../../../triage/exclusion)).

- The [../acts/act](../../../../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../../../../acts/include|including) establishes the [condition](../../../../triage/condition) of [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion):
    - This [condition](../../../../triage/condition) holds the [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) **inside** the field of what is;
    - [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) is not a passive state but an **actively maintained presence**;
    - It marks and holds the [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) as part of the relational field.

- [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) is transient unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../../../../structures/structure):
    - As long as the [../acts/act](../../../../acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../../../../acts/include|including) is active, [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) persists;
    - To sustain it durably, [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) must be stabilized within a [../structures/relative](../../../../structures/relative) or other [../structures/structure](../../../../structures/structure).

- [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) gains its coherence by being held together with [exclusion](../../../../triage/exclusion):
    - This mutual holding induces [tension](../../../../triage/tension);
    - [tension](../../../../triage/tension) ensures that [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) is not trivial or self-dissolving but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [exclusion](../../../../triage/exclusion).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) that [../acts/exist|exists](../../../../acts/exist|exists) is conditioned by both [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) and [exclusion](../../../../triage/exclusion);
- [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) is essential for affirming what a [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) is:
    - Without [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion), no [triage/thing](../../../../triage/triage/thing) can be marked as present within the field of what is;
- [inclusion](../../../../triage/inclusion) participates in the fundamental [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) that generate and sustain [../acts/exist|existing](../../../../acts/exist|existing) and all further relational architectures.
