## Interpretation

A recognized context should resolve back into a trace.  Consequently, *Recognize Context* witnesses *Contextualize Trace* to produce ***Retrace Context*** ([RetraceContext](./0039-retrace-context.md)).

The act mirrors *Trace Context* and records the converse direction, so *Retrace Context* is forced.
