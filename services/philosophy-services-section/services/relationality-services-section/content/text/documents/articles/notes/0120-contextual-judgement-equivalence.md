# Equating Judgement Contextually

## Interpretation

The stabilized head must be witnessed by the surrounding context.  *Complete Trace* witnessing *Head Judgement Equivalence* yields ***Contextual Judgement Equivalence*** ([ContextualJudgementEquivalence](./0120-contextual-judgement-equivalence.md)).

This act records the contextual witnessing, so *Contextual Judgement Equivalence* follows.
