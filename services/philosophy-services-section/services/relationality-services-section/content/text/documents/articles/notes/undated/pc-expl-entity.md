An element of an [entity set](../../research/2026/07/code/structures/entity-set.md), denoted $e$. Entities are the individuals or nodes that [predicates](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/_archive/predicate.md) discuss.

An entity may occupy the [subject](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/_archive/subject.md) or [object](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/_archive/object.md) position of a [predication cell](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/_archive/predication-cell.md).
