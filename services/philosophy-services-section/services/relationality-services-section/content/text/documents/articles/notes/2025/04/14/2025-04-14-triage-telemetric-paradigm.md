A ***telemetric paradigm*** is a [system of coherence](system of coherence) maintained through recursive registration of [telemetric signal|signal](telemetric signal|signal) within situated [telemetric field|fields](telemetric field|fields). It replaces [ontological paradigm|ontological](ontological paradigm|ontological) or [representational paradigm|representational paradigms](representational paradigm|representational paradigms) with *processual coherence systems*, in which [knowledge](../../../../../../triage/knowledge), [action](../../../../../../triage/action), and [interpret|interpretation](../../../../../../triage/interpret|interpretation) emerge through [casting cycles](casting cycles), rather than [symbolic alignment](symbolic alignment).

Where other paradigms assume discrete [category|categories](../../../../../../triage/category|categories), internal consistency, or [truth](../../../../../../triage/truth)-anchored [model|modeling](../../../../../../triage/model|modeling), the **telemetric paradigm** assumes [telemetric role|roles](telemetric role|roles), [telemetric trace|traces](telemetric trace|traces), and [telemetric recursion|recursions](telemetric recursion|recursions). It does not model what *is*, it describes *apparent movement*.

A telemetric paradigm operates through the [telemetric grammar|grammar](telemetric grammar|grammar) of [telemetric cast|casting](telemetric cast|casting) signal into [telemetric method|methods](telemetric method|methods) situated within [telemetric field|fields](telemetric field|fields), which result in [telemetric yield|yields](telemetric yield|yields). This grammar is [metabolize|metabolic](../../../../../../triage/metabolize|metabolic), not [epistemology|epistemic](../../../../../../triage/epistemology|epistemic): it does not stablize [meaning](../../../../../../triage/meaning), it surfaces [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence](../../../../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/coherence) by aligning methods and fields around signals.
