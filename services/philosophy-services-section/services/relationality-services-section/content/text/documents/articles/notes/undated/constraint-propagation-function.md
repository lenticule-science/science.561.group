The ***constraint propagation function*** (`P`) is induced by the vector modulation function (`V`).

When modulation is directional and constraint remains invariant along that direction,  
propagation emerges — sustained movement of structure through stabilized difference.

> P(x, d) := C(x) = C(x + d) ∧ V(x) = V(x + d)

That is, propagation occurs where constraint and modulation cohere along a direction.
