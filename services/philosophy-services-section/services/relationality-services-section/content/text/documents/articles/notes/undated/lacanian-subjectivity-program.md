This program models Lacanian concepts (symbolic, imaginary, real; desire;
subject positions) as recognitions to trace how they manifest in discourse and
relation.

## Behaviour

- Represent subject positions, objet petit a, signifiers, and mirror-stage
  constructs as recognitions with context and provenance.
- Link to discourse, dreams, and interpersonal recognitions to observe shifts
  and repetitions.
- Measure patterns of desire, lack, and symbolic/imaginary interplay over time.

## Intended operators

- Annotate discourse for Lacanian motifs.
- Trace subject positions across interactions.
- Compare Lacanian readings with other psychoanalytic programs.
