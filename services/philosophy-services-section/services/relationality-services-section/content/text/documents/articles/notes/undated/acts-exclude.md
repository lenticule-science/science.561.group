- [define](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/define.md):: [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) is the primitive [act](act.md) by which a [triage/thing](thing.md) is held out of what is. It establishes the [../conditions/condition](condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md), marking the [triage/thing](thing.md) as not part of what is held open within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [derive|Deriving](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|Deriving) [exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](thing.md) be:
    - Brought into what it is (by [include|including](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/include|including));
    - And set apart from what it is not (by [exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|excluding)).

- [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) is the [act](act.md) that enacts this **holding-out**:
    - It does not merely assert that something is absent; it actively **establishes absence** by preventing the [triage/thing](thing.md) from being within the scope of what is.
    - Without [exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|excluding), nothing is positioned as absent, and no coherent claim of [exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing) can be held open.

- [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) is primitive:
    - It cannot be reduced to other acts;
    - It operates in necessary **complementarity** with [include|including](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/include|including).

- [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) brings about the [../conditions/condition](condition.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md):
    - The [../conditions/condition](condition.md) that specifies that the [triage/thing](thing.md) is **outside** of what is.
    - This [../conditions/condition](condition.md) persists only transiently unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](structure.md).

- To sustain the dynamic of [exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing), [exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|excluding) must be held open **together with** [include|including](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/include|including):
    - This mutual holding induces [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](tension.md);
    - [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/tension](tension.md) ensures that [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is not void but is **active and bounded**, forming a coherent opposition to [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](thing.md) that [exist|exists](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|exists) is, by definition, set apart through the act of [exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|excluding).
- [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) is inseparable from [include|including](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/include|including):
    - No [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) can hold without its opposite [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md) also being held;
    - This interplay is essential for maintaining the [../conditions/difference](difference.md) that allows [triage/thing|things](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/triage/thing|things) to be distinct.
- [exclude|Excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exclude|Excluding) is foundational to all [relational dynamics](relational dynamics):
    - It is one half of the minimal engine that sustains the architecture of [exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing) and all further relational structures.
