# Idea: Research Reading & Literature Review

Model reading, comprehension, and synthesis as recognitions to make your
literature workflow explicit and traceable.

## 1. Recognitions

- `PaperRecognition`: as in the papers/measurement idea (title, authors, ID,
  domains, links).
- `SectionRecognition`: major sections or key passages, linked to the parent
  paper.
- `ClaimRecognition`: specific claims or results you care about.
- `ReadingPassRecognition`: a particular session of reading (skim, deep read,
  revisit) with notes and timestamps.
- `SynthesisRecognition`: your own summaries, comparisons, or meta-analyses.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Topology:
  - citation graph; theme clusters; “concept → paper → claim” chains.
  - links from claims/summaries to other domains (experiments, code, MUD).
- Measurement:
  - coverage (what fraction of a topic you’ve touched),
  - recency (how stale a recognition is),
  - confidence (how well you think you understand a claim).

Runbooks like `plan_reading`, `annotate_paper`, `synthesize_topic` can drive
your literature work through the same relational engine.
