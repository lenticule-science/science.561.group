# Entity set

A set, denoted $E$, whose elements are [entities](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/concepts/entity.md).
The entity set carries no required structure beyond set-hood in general; specific theorems and applications may impose finiteness, and the entity set may be sorted.

Pairs of entities form the index $E \times E$ over which [predication fiber algebras](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/entity-set/predication-fiber-algebra.md) are built; the first and second coordinates of such pairs are the [subject](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/concepts/subject.md) and [object](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/concepts/object.md) positions of [predication cells](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/entity/content/concepts/predication-cell.md).
