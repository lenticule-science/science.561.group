## Interpretation

Contextual witnessing still lacks the value recognized by those stabilized observations.  Forcing *Tail Trace* to witness *Contextual Step Observation* yields ***Valued Step Observation*** ([ValuedStepObservation](./0088-valued-step-observation.md)).

This composition binds the contextual observation to its value, so *Valued Step Observation* is earned.
