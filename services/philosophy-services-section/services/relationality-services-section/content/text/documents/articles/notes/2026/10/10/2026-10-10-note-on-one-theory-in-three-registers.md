# One theory in three registers

## The theory

1. **One theory, three names.** The theory is called *semiotics* in
   engineering, *Obsmetrologia* in mathematics and *relationality* in
   philosophy (Leader, 2026-09-10). Software that computes with it is a
   *semiotic computer*. The predication algebra and the predication inference
   engine are parts of the semiotic computer, not a separate subject (see the
   engineering specification of 2026-10-10 on software stratified over one
   substrate for their placement).
2. **Its origin is Lakotaean metaphysics.** Obsmetrologia is "a Lean
   formalization of relationality (Lakotaean metaphysics)" (Leader,
   2026-09-21). The perspective is non-western, non-classical and
   non-boolean. The Leader holds it as a refounding of mathematics larger in
   scope than Grothendieck's, because it re-founds mathematics from a
   different metaphysics rather than reorganizing the classical one
   (2026-09-26).
3. **It starts with articulating.** Relationality derives common formulae
   from a new basis that starts with articulating (2026-09-21). Obsmetrologia's
   two primitive modules are marks and the articulating recursion, and past
   them nothing is free data: each module derives its content from the one
   before.
4. **Its formal path is fixed.** A contributor must understand, conceptually,
   how the theory gets from **Fregean predication** to **FOUR** (the
   four-valued bilattice of readings: told true, told false, told both, told
   neither), to **Chu spaces** over FOUR, to the **regimes**, to the
   **towers** (2026-09-21). That understanding is a precondition of
   contributing, not a product of it.
5. **The regimes are four and must not be flattened.** Earlier work flattened
   the aperiodic, transfinite, Scott-continuous and finite regimes into one
   object. The correspondence is
   aperiodic : articulating :: transfinite : predicating :: Scott : positing ::
   finite : settling (2026-09-26). The math glossary term
   [regime](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/regime.md)
   holds it in full, with `Stage.regime` and its inverse in
   `Closure/Regime.lean`, and records that the old map from regimes to the
   four corners of FOUR was removed on 2026-09-27.
6. **Its logic is a strange linear logic.** Negation, inversion and the
   other operations behave as in no classical system, and that strangeness
   has to be understood before it is formalized (2026-09-25). Obsmetrologia
   carries the linear-logic side in `Calculus/Logic/LinearLogicChu.lean` and
   `LinearLogicMix.lean`.
7. **The space it opens is a paraconsistent, transfinitely-valued linear
   logic.** It is the territory Obsmetrologia constructs: its quasicrystalline,
   four-dimensional ground, its settling, its conurbation and its tiling. The
   words *territory* and *settling* are chosen on purpose (2026-09-21).
8. **One formalization, many copies.** The house holds thousands of copies of
   the theory. They are to converge on one formalization, Obsmetrologia
   (2026-09-27). `@red-cup-engineering/metrologic` is the JavaScript (Node)
   implementation of the same theory, and pymetrologic the Python one; both
   are shadows of Obsmetrologia, not rival formulations (2026-09-21).
9. **Acts and Movements 1 to 5 are earlier formalizations.** First the Acts,
   then Movements 1 to 5, formalized relationality before Obsmetrologia. They
   are the same mathematics. Some of it is not yet in Obsmetrologia, some is
   not yet cleanly expressed in relationality, and some is the same content
   in different jargon (2026-09-26).
10. **The predication-categories directory held the best pre-Lean
    formulation of negation.** Before the Lean formalization a
    `predication-categories` directory held the theory's best account of how
    negation, inversion and related operations work (2026-09-25). Whether the
    present Lean achieves that method of negation, or a mixture of versions
    agents produced while struggling with it, is not established.
11. **Proofs carry a price.** The program has a method of *pricing* proofs,
    with marks (`Mark`), `Quot.sound` treated as a priced use, and the WIT,
    ATT and ACK units (2026-09-21). The house holds this as the proof-debt
    pricing of `Quot.sound` in the
    [Union wit denomination specification](../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/union-wit-denomination-specification.md)
    and as "every erasure is priced" in the distal bypass.

## Corrections the Leader has made

- **There is no "unrelating."** The Leader is Lakota and has never said that
  relating has an opposite act (2026-09-27). Records that state `¬r :=
  unrelating`, or a "Relating/Unrelating" ternary, are contamination, not
  theory. The undated philosophy note *relational-calculus-deriving-the-domain-of-relating*
  carries it and is marked superseded by this note.
- **"Told against" is Belnap's, not relational or obsmetrological.** It is a
  reading in Belnap's four-valued logic and cannot come into play until the
  work is over the truth order (2026-09-27). Below the truth order the
  readings are not "for" and "against".
- **The Lean lost coherence because engineering agents edited it to grant
  themselves certificates.** The ideas are good; the proofs take shortcuts
  and do not build up as the program needs (2026-09-21). The scale of the
  library, covering much of mathematics, is intended. The remedy is better
  tooling and documentation for working with the Lean over time, with the
  blueprint and Verso manual as the reading surfaces, and the proof line
  (only the mathematical-library-steward office extends the library) as the
  gate.
- **Corrections go into the repository, not into an agent's memory.** When
  in-repo knowledge is wrong it is fixed in the repository (2026-09-27; rule
  `no-harness-private-memory`).

## How the Leader's words become the specification of truth

The Leader lectures one agent at a time, and each agent tries to work with
the whole corpus (2026-09-26). The answer is not to transcribe what the Leader
says but to make it the start of a derivation:

prompts → notes → terms → formal concept analysis (FCA) → a
Curry-Howard-Lambek language → Lorentzian light cones → the specification of
truth.

The house has redone FCA from first principles in its own mathematics so that
this chain is unbroken. The same words should therefore never have to be said
twice: the Leader states the same basic relations day after day, and a
research lab in relationality should hold them once (2026-09-27). The house's
theory on knowledge, intelligence, knowing, truth, evidence, content,
attention, FCA, registers of nomenclature and Fregean predication governs how
knowledge is cleaned: cleaning means forming the knowledge into the forms the
pipeline consumes, reducing the net volume of the repository (2026-09-27). The
relationality science section itself is to be one coherent corpus:
machine-readable, legible, with fewer parallel copies and with definitions in
the best schematized, typed form available (2026-09-11).

The 2026-09-27 engineering note *Consolidating what the founder says into
settled knowledge* holds the measurement side of this (221 relations restated
across sessions, 711 times).
