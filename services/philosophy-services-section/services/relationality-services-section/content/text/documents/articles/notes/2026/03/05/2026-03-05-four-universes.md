## The problem

Relationality is formalized through two mathematical systems, each
validated against established mathematics:

- **Semiotic Universe** — process-based. Formalizes signs, agents,
  meanings. Mathematical correspondence validated against category
  theory and type theory.
- **Quasicrystalline Hypertensor Topos (QCHTTopos)** — structure-based.
  Formalizes traces, fibers, geometric coherence. Mathematical
  correspondence validated against sheaf theory and spectral geometry.

Neither is built to validate against empirical observation. Each has
a natural domain: the semiotic universe fits agential and information
systems (finance, governance, web); QCHTTopos fits computing, metric,
and quantified systems.

But what serves as the mathematical paradigm for the natural and quantum
world?

## The grid

emsenn proposes a 2x2 classification:

The two filled cells are validated by showing their formal structures
correspond to established mathematical theorem. The two empty cells
need formalisms validated against empirical observation — the world as
actually encountered, not as mathematically modeled.

## Implications

- There may be a third (or fourth) mathematical universe whose language
  brings us closest to discussing lived reality without the baggage of
  existing paradigms or the incommensurability with Western mathematics
  that relationality (as philosophy) brings.
- Cross-universe correspondence work is necessary: showing that all
  roads lead back to relationality, regardless of which formal system
  you enter through.
- The GFRTU (Generative Fibered Recognition Trace Universe) is meant
  to be deprecated — its ideas should be captured within QCHTTopos.

## Relevance to the emsemioverse

This grid shapes how the vault organizes formal content. Work in
`mathematics/objects/universes/` should respect these distinctions:
the semiotic universe lineage handles process, QCHTTopos handles
structure, and the empirical cells remain open problems.
