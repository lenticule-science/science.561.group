The ***constraint inheritance function*** (`Η`) is induced by the sublation function (`Θ`).

When sublation seeds a new constraint from collapse,  
residual structures from the previous stabilization are carried forward as inherited tendencies.

> Η(Θ(Σ)) := { x ∈ Σ | Δₛ(x, x′) for some x′ ∉ Σ but x′ ⊢ C′ }

That is, even after collapse, constraint does not forget —  
difference persists across generations of stabilization.
