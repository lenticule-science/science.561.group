- [../acts/define](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/define.md):: [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is the [condition](condition.md) brought about by the [../acts/act](act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding). It specifies that a [triage/thing](thing.md) is held out of what is, marking it as absent within the [../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating](../../../Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/structures/domain of relating).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing) requires that a [triage/thing](thing.md):
    - Be included in what it is (producing [inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md));
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md)).

- The [../acts/act](act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) establishes the [condition](condition.md) of [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md):
    - This [condition](condition.md) holds the [triage/thing](thing.md) outside of the field of what is;
    - It is not merely a lack of presence but an **actively maintained absence**;
    - [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is therefore a **positive condition**—it marks and holds the [triage/thing](thing.md) as not within.

- [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is transient unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](structure.md):
    - As long as the [../acts/act](act.md) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) is active, [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) persists;
    - To achieve durability, [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) must be stabilized within a [../structures/relative](relative.md) or other [../structures/structure](structure.md).

- [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) gains its coherence by being held together with [inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md):
    - This holding induces [tension](tension.md);
    - [tension](tension.md) ensures that [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is not isolated or meaningless but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md).

## Implications

- Every [triage/thing](thing.md) that [../acts/exist|exists](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|exists) is conditioned by both [inclusion](conditions-inclusion.md) and [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md);
- [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) is essential for the maintenance of [difference](difference.md):
    - Without [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md), no [triage/thing](thing.md) can be marked as distinct from what is not;
- [exclusion](conditions-exclusion.md) participates in the basic [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) that sustain [../acts/exist|existing](../../../../triage/relationality-2/human-draft-7/system/acts/exist|existing) and all further relational architectures.
