The ***constraint stabilization function*** is [induce|induced](../../../../triage/induce|induced) by the [exclusion constraint function](exclusion constraint function).

The constraint stabilization set (`S`) contains all values for which the constraint does not induce further difference.

> `C(x) = x ⇒ x ∈ S`

That is, constraint constrains until it cannot.
