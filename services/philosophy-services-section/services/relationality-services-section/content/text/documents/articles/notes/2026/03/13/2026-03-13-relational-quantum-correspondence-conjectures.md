Three open conjectures proposing a correspondence between quantum-mechanical phenomena and structures of the [generative fibered recognition trace universe](../../../generative-fibered-recognition-trace-universe.md)/[hypertensor topos](../../../hypertensor-topos-specification.md) formalism. None has an identified proof route; each states precisely what a proof would require.

These three were originally grouped, along with two further conjectures — *superposition as prenormal form* and *uncertainty as R7 asymmetry* — under a single "FML quantum correspondence" unit that was later dissolved into five separate conjecture units. Only these three survived into the snapshot this document was recovered from; the other two are not accounted for anywhere in the live corpus and are not reconstructed here.

## Entanglement as shared nuclear structure

Two particles are entangled when they share a formula that cannot be factored into independent sub-formulas at each fiber. Measuring one collapses the shared formula, reducing the nuclear-reduction measure — which is why the other particle's state is instantly determined. Decoherence is the environment running normalization steps that were not requested.

**What a proof requires.** A tensor product structure on fibers H_t ⊗ H_t representing two-particle states, and a factorizability criterion in terms of the nuclear-reduction measure.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.

## Interference as cohomological cancellation

Constructive and destructive interference correspond to sections that assemble or fail to assemble in sheaf cohomology. Destructive interference is a class in H¹ that vanishes — a section whose local pieces cancelled in the gluing. The double-slit experiment is a sheaf that fails to amalgamate when both slits are open but amalgamates with one closed.

**What a proof requires.** An identification of the interference pattern with a Čech 1-cocycle, and a proof that path-integral cancellation corresponds to the coboundary condition.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.

## Measurement problem as topos obstruction

An observer is a structure at the next spiral level looking down. The measurement problem is the topos-of-topoi obstruction: H¹(ℋ) ≠ 0 because there is no hyper-axiom of locality at the meta-level. The measurement problem is not solvable within one universe; it requires the next spiral level.

**What a proof requires.** A precise identification of "observer" with an object in ℋ⁽¹⁾ relative to the system in ℋ, and a derivation of the H¹ obstruction from the absence of a meta-locality axiom.

**Status.** Open conjecture. No proof route is currently identified.
