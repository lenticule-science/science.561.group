This program envisions a continuously evolving “infinity site” that renders the
relational universe as an explorable web experience.

## Behaviour

- Map recognitions and runbooks to navigable web views with provenance-aware
  links.
- Provide progressive disclosure: shallow overviews that drill into full
  traces, measurements, and policies.
- Support live updates from the relational graph while preserving traceability
  of what was shown when.

## Intended operators

- Generate and refresh site pages from recognition queries.
- Record user navigation as interactions for audit and learning.
- Export snapshots for offline or archival viewing.
