# Idea: Disaster Preparedness & Response

Represent hazards, assets, plans, and incidents as recognitions to reason about
resilience.

## 1. Recognitions

- `HazardRecognition`: flood, fire, storm, quake risk.
- `AssetRecognition`: shelters, vehicles, supplies, staff.
- `PlanRecognition`: preparedness and response plans.
- `IncidentRecognition`: actual events and responses.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - geography, infrastructure, social networks.
- Measurement:
  - readiness, response time, coverage, unmet needs.

Runbooks: `run_drill`, `activate_plan`, `review_incident` connect prep and
response into an auditable loop.
