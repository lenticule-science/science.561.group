I'm [thinking](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/thinking.md) about different facets of why, in [American idiom](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-idiom.md), [post-](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/post.md) is often [interpreted](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpreting.md) as meaning [anti-](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti.md) rather than "[after](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/after.md)" or [beyond](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/beyond.md)."

# On American translation of post- to anti- {#on-american-translation-of-post--to-anti--1}

## Basic Idea

### Linguistics

In [English language](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/english-language.md), post- has historically, and in dictionaries, just means "after:" it's a [temporal condition](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/temporal-condition.md), not an [opposing relation](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opposing-relation.md): [postwar](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postwar.md), [postnatal](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postnatal.md).

But in [political](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/political-discourse.md) and [theoretical discourse](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/theoretical-discourse.md), it often has connotations of "[against](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/against.md):" [postmodernism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postmodernism.md) is seen as not just after [modernism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/modernism.md), temporally, but is seen as [rejecting the claims of modernism](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/rejecting-the-claims-of-modernism.md), usually specifically [modernist universalism](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/modernist-universalism.md).

In the American idiom, the *post-* prefix is often still temporal, but [signifying](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifying.md) a [rupture](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/rupture.md) in a [zero-sum state](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zero-sum-state.md).

### Philosophy

In [European thought](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/european-thought.md), [philosophers](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/philosopher.md) in "post-" [schools of thought](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/school-of-thought.md) maintained [continuity of thought](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/continuity-of-thought.md) with what they were "after:" [Jacques Derrida](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jacques-derrida.md)'s [post-structuralism](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/post-structuralism.md) is not [anti-structure](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-structure.md) but an exploration of [structuralist assumptions](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/structuralist-assumption.md). But in the U.S., post-structuralism is often equated with [anti-truth](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-truth.md), as a result of the former's reduction into [anti-structuralism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-structuralism.md)

### Culture

[American public discourse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-public-discousre.md) is basically nothing but [opposing binaries](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opposing-binary.md): [liberal](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal.md) and [conservative](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conservative.md), and frames [progress](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/progress.md) in terms of [rupture](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/rupture.md) rather than [continuity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/continuity.md), so "post-" becomes a story of breaking free from whatever it is post.

This was especially true during the [Cold War](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cold-war.md), when [Continental theory](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/continental-theory.md) arrived in [American academia](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/28/2025-09-28-american-academia.md), when almost all [thought](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-academic-thought.md) was filtered into these lens: liberal and [Marxist](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxist.md), [realist](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/realist.md) and [relativist](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/relativist.md).

### Loops

Because [American readers](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-reader.md) assume "post-" means "anti-," [Continental theory texts](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/continental-theory-text.md) are taught, cited, and mobilized in ways that utilize that reading: when looking for a term to signal their opposition to a thing, [American thinkers](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-thinker.md) use "post-," for example [postliberalism](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postliberalism.md).[^1]

This induces a [connotative drift](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/connotative-drift.md) in the [idiom of American discourse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/idiom-of-american-discourse.md), compared to the [idiom of global discourse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/idiom-of-global-discourse.md) (which I might also say includes a [idiom of international discourse](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/idiom-of-international-discourse.md) with a different drift.)

For example, while in the [global idiom](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/global-idiom.md), [post-socialism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/post-socialism.md) generally signifies that which comes after [state socialism](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/state-socialism.md), while in the American idiom, it generally signifies that which is opposing socialism or proving its failure.

## Postmodernism in America

In [France](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/france.md), postmodernism, and post-structuralism, were parts of a wider, dense debate about Marxism, [phenomenology](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/phenomenology.md), and [structural linguistics](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/structural-linguistics.md). They weren't about [negating](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/negating.md), but about [folding](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/folding.md) and [complicating](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/complicating.md) structures: Derrida's [différance](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/differance.md), [Michel Foucault](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/michel-foucault.md)'s [geneaology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/foucaultian-geneaology.md).

In America, the first institutional interpretation of these texts was in [literary studies](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/literary-study.md): folk like Derrida were read through [Paul de Man](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/paul-de-man.md) and the [Yale school](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/yale-school-of-thought.md), where the "undeccidability" of meaning was emphasized. This made postmodernism less about examining philosophical structures and more as an attitude, or orientation: anti-truth, anti-foundation, anti-grand narrative.

Locked into their Cold War binaries, postmodernism itself became a thing to decide: a [pole of debate](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pole-of-debate.md): is it destroying truth? relativizing science? undermining morality.

Once "postmodernism = anti-truth" got traction, it became an interpretative shorthand in both [conservative critique](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conservative-critique.md), with things like [cultural Marxism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cultural-marxism.md), and in [progressive identity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/progressive-identity.md), as a "against [Enlightenment rationalism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/enlightenment-rationalism.md)."

Curricula, anthologies, and polemics reinforced this, and by the end of the Cold War era, "post-" had become heavily encoded as a symbol of ideological stance, not symbol of chronological position.

I keep hand-waving to the Cold War as a mechanism for inciting this translation and [drift to dualism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/drift-to-dualism.md), but in order for that mechanism to exist, it had to have been selected, and this is likely reflecting a longer historical drift. But the erason I'm looking this way is mostly because of two texts: [François Cusset](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/francois-cusset.md)'s French Theory: How Foucault, Derrida, Deleuze, & Co. Transformed the Intellectual Life of the United States ([2003ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2003ce.md)) and [Alan Sokal](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/alan-sokal.md)'s parody [Social Text](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-text.md) ([1996ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1996ce.md)).

## American Drift to Dualism

[American political theology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-political-theology.md) has structured itself with binaries for a long time: [liberty](../../../../../../services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberty.md) and [tyranny](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tyranny.md), [civil](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/civil.md) and [savage](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/savage.md).

While these [concepts](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md) are very [abstract](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abstract.md), they were the explicit grounds for the [structure](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/structuring.md) of things like [American sovereignty](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-sovereignty.md), [citizenship](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/28/2025-09-28-american-citizenship.md), and [national legitimacy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-national-legitimacy.md). And these structures were seen as incommensurate with the things from which they came: American governance was a rupture from the English monarchism that organized it and the Indigenous governance of land that provided its material base.

European thinkers like [Georg Hegel](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/georg-wilhelm-friedrich-hegel.md) and [Karl Marx](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/karl-marx.md) developed and elaborated [dialectical reason](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectical-reason.md), where [contradiction](../../../../../../../western-services-section/services/marxism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/contradiction.md) was seen as [generative](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/generative.md), [producing synthesis](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/producing-synthesis.md). [American liberal thought](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-liberal-thought.md), though its [conceptual lineage](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conceptual-lineage.md) was in European thought, usually truncated that move: contradiction wasn't used as generative [tension](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/services/fiction-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tension.md), but a [sorting operation](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/sorting-operation.md).

When looking toward what has made America different than Europe, there's a few different dynamics that have been clearly identified by different schools of thought, but the idea most relevant here is the [Transatlantic cybernetic circuit](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/transatlantic-cybernetic-circuit.md).

What I mean by that is how the earliest American settlers arrived with a way of life that was compatible with European ways of living; specifically the early settlers of New England arrived with a way of life that was compatible with England: they both had the same [political referents](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/political-referent.md): the [Bible](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bible.md), English legal code, [Anglican](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anglican-doctrine.md) and [Calvinist doctrine](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/calvinist-doctrine.md).

In America and Europe, these references were produced into [texts](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md): [charters](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/charter.md), [catechisms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/catechism.md), [sermons](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sermon.md). These texts referenced the same foundations, but in different environments and situations.

I'm not doing a good job explaining what I'm thinking of here, but it's close maybe to the concepts in [The American Jeremiad](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/literature-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/the-american-jeremiad.md) by [Sacvan Bercovitch](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/sacvan-bercovitch.md) ([1978ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1978ce.md)), but that's not quite it. But you're either in fidelity to the covenants, or you're in betrayal.

But the idea that [American intellectualism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-intellectualism.md) and political formations have a drift to dualism is something that's been diagnosed a few ways in a few frames. Here are some others that come to mind:

- [Louis Hartz](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/louis-hartz.md) in [The Liberal Tradition in America](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/the-liberal-tradition-in-america.md) ([1955ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1955ce.md))
  - America never had [feudalism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/feudalism.md) or [aristocratic estates](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aristocratic-estate.md): it was born as a [liberal nation](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-nation.md).
  - Without deep counter-traditions ([monarchism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/monarchism.md), [socialism](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/socialism.md), [aristocraticism](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/aristocraticism.md)) to structure [conflict](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conflict.md), especially [intellectual conflict](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intellectual-conflict.md), everything was an [intra-liberal conflict](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intra-liberal-conflict.md).
- [Patrick Wolfe](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/patrick-wolfe.md) in [Settler Colonialism and the Elimination of the Native](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settler-colonialism-and-the-elimination-of-the-native.md) ([1999ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1999ce.md))
  - [settler-colonialism](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settler-colonialism.md) is a structure, not an [event](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/event.md):
    - its fundamental logic is elimination: [excluding](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/excluding.md) the [Indigenous](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/indigenous.md), [including](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md) the [settler](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settler.md)
  - the system preserves European heterogeneity but eliminates non-European heterogeneity.
- [Maurice Duverger](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/maurice-duverger.md) in Political Parties: Their Organization and Activity in the Modern State ([1954ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1954ce.md))
  - [Duverger's Law](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/duverger-s-law.md): first-past-the-post voting tends to produce two-party systems
- [Alexis de Tocqueville](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/alexis-de-tocqueville.md) in [Democracy in America](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/democracy-in-america.md) ([1835ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1835ce.md))
  - [American democracy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/american-democracy.md) arises from lack of aristocracy and leads to individualism.
  - cultural style of stance-taking: issues are processed as personal commitments.
  - Tocqueville warned of a [tyranny of the majority](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tyranny-of-the-majority.md) that comes from this.

So, lack of material structuration derived from epistemic structuration that was translated into a more compressed form, and myths that made these absences feel good, led to the American drift to dualism, might by how I'd say it.

[^1]: Postliberalism is a perhaps interesting case study here, because it was taken up by Europeans who did a similar translation, resulting in using an antagonist thought as structuring thought, which is a demonstration of a certain dynamic of this phenomenon, in which American codes are taken into the European or global code but in doing so limit the code's potential results to what the American code facilitates.

## Earlier material

This induces a connotative drift in the [[id:8ffcdaaf-748c-4c0a-bb9d-444350785f5d][idiom of A…4943 chars truncated…, producing synthesis. American liberal thought, though its conceptual lineage was in European thought, usually truncated that move: contradiction wasn't used as generative tension, but a sorting operation.
