## Statement

domain of relating = {relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating)}

## Proof

- The domain is derived from:
  - relating
  - unrelating
  - their mutual interaction

- No additional acts are induced by this derivation.
- Removing any element collapses the relational possibility:
  - Without relating: no inclusion
  - Without unrelating: no distinction
  - Without their tension: no structure

Therefore:

- The domain is complete and minimal.

## Corollaries

- **Any act outside the domain of relating must be separately derived.**
- **Relating and unrelating are necessary and sufficient to induce relational structure.**
