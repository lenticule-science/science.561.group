# Idea: Socio-Ecological Systems (Land, Community, Policy)

Model land, species, communities, and policies as recognitions to explore
stewardship and impact.

## 1. Recognitions

- `ParcelRecognition`: land units with use, ownership, habitat types.
- `SpeciesRecognition`: populations, ranges, trophic roles.
- `CommunityRecognition`: local groups, institutions, customary practices.
- `PolicyRecognition`: regulations, incentives, zoning.
- `InterventionRecognition`: burns, plantings, protections, developments.

## 2. Measurement and topology

- Topology:
  - spatial adjacency, food webs, social networks, policy jurisdictions.
- Measurement:
  - ecological indicators (biodiversity, soil, water),
  - human wellbeing indicators (housing, health, livelihoods).

Runbooks for `plan_intervention`, `simulate_impact`, `evaluate_outcome` become
gameplay loops and real-world planning aids.
