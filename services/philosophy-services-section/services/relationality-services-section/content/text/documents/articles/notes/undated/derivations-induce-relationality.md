- Definition:: ***relationality*** is the [domain](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/domain) [induce|induced](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/induce|induced) by the [act](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/act)s of [including](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/including) (relation) and [excluding](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/excluding) (relationlessness), inducing coherent [relational structure](relational structure).

## Derivation
- [postulate](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/postulate):: It must be derived that as [relation](derivations-relation.md) is the act of [including](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/including) within a domain, and [relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-05/relationlessness.md) is the act of [excluding](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/excluding) relation, their mutual necessity [induce|induces](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/induce|induces) [induce relationality](induce-relationality.md) itself.
- [derivation](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/derivation):: As [relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-05/relationlessness.md) and [relation](derivations-relation.md) are mutually acting within and upon the same domain, the [existence](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/existence) of relationality necessarily derives its [relation](derivations-relation.md) to itself, inducing the first act of [reflexive relational derivation](reflexive relational derivation).
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [induce relationality](induce-relationality.md) is [induce](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/induce)([relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationality-2/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-05/relationlessness.md), [relation](derivations-relation.md))

## Formalism
- Let r denote the act of [including](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/including) relation, and not(r) denote the act of [excluding](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/excluding) relation (relationlessness).
- Then:
	- not(r) ≠ r
	- The act of excluding (not(r)) necessarily relates back to the act of including (r).
- Therefore, define Relationality as the domain induced by these acts:
	- Relationality = {r, not(r), relate(r, not(r))}
- Thus, Relationality is the domain composed of the acts of [including](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/including) and [excluding](../../../../triage/relationality/relationality-slop-2025-05/derivations/excluding) relation, bound together by their necessary mutual relation.
