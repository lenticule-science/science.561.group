## Interpretation

Normalized observations still require the recognition that any two such observations remain equivalent across contexts.  Forcing *Equate Contexts* to witness *Normalize Observation* yields ***Normalize Observation Equivalence*** ([NormalizeObservationEquivalence](./normalize-observation-equivalence.md)).

Composing these recognitions captures the indistinguishability of normalized observations, so *Normalize Observation Equivalence* is established.
