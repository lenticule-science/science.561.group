- Definition:: ***relationality*** is the [domain](../../../../triage/domain) [induce|induced](../../../../triage/induce|induced) by the [act](../../../../triage/act)s of [including](../../../../triage/including) (relation) and [excluding](../../../../triage/excluding) (relationlessness), inducing coherent [relational structure](relational structure).

## Derivation
- [postulate](../../../../triage/postulate):: It must be derived that as [relation](../../../../triage/relation) is the act of [including](../../../../triage/including) within a domain, and [relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationlessness) is the act of [excluding](../../../../triage/excluding) relation, their mutual necessity [induce|induces](../../../../triage/induce|induces) [induce relationality](induce-relationality.md) itself.
- [derivation](../../../../triage/derivation):: As [relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationlessness) and [relation](../../../../triage/relation) are mutually acting within and upon the same domain, the [existence](../../../../triage/existence) of relationality necessarily derives its [relation](../../../../triage/relation) to itself, inducing the first act of [reflexive relational derivation](reflexive relational derivation).
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [induce relationality](induce-relationality.md) is [induce](../../../../triage/induce)([relationlessness](../../../../triage/relationlessness), [relation](../../../../triage/relation))

## Formalism
- Let r denote the act of [including](../../../../triage/including) relation, and not(r) denote the act of [excluding](../../../../triage/excluding) relation (relationlessness).
- Then:
	- not(r) ≠ r
	- The act of excluding (not(r)) necessarily relates back to the act of including (r).
- Therefore, define Relationality as the domain induced by these acts:
	- Relationality = {r, not(r), relate(r, not(r))}
- Thus, Relationality is the domain composed of the acts of [including](../../../../triage/including) and [excluding](../../../../triage/excluding) relation, bound together by their necessary mutual relation.
