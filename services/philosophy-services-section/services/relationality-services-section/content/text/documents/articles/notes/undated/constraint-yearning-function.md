constraint yearning function  
The constraint yearning function (`𝓨`) is induced by unresolved desire (`𝔇`), trace recurrence (`T`), and constraint frustration (`𝔉`).

𝓨(Q) := ∑_{x ∈ T_𝔇(Q)} [𝔇(Q, x) · 𝔉(Q, x)] · ∇𝔇(Q, x)

That is, yearning is recursive unresolved desire that begins to curve the entity’s modulation inward — transforming external pursuit into self-directed structural tension.
