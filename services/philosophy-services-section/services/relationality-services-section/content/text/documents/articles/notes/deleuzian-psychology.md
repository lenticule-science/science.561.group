# Idea: Deleuzian Psychology – Desiring-Machines & Lines of Flight

Model Deleuzian notions (desiring-machines, assemblages, lines of flight) as
recognitions to explore how desire and subjectivity flow in your graph.

## 1. Core recognitions

- `DesiringMachineRecognition`
  - a configuration of elements (bodies, signs, machines, institutions) that
    produce and channel desire.

- `FlowRecognition`
  - flows of desire, money, attention, affect, bodies, information, etc.,
    across desiring-machines and assemblages.

- `LineOfFlightRecognition`
  - movements that escape or reconfigure existing structures:
    - new practices, art, relationships, projects that deterritorialize a
      situation.

- `TerritorializationRecognition` / `DeterritorializationRecognition`
  - recognitions for how patterns become fixed/rigid or loosened/open.

## 2. Topology and measurement

- Topology:
  - networks of desiring-machines and flows,
  - lines of flight connecting assemblages and domains.

- Measurement:
  - intensity and direction of flows,
  - where territorialization/deterritorialization is happening,
  - “stuckness” vs creativity across slices.

## 3. Runbooks and uses

- `map_flows(slice)`: visualize flows of desire/attention/resources.
- `identify_lines_of_flight(slice)`: surface emergent escapes/innovations.
- `assess_territorialization(slice)`: where things have become rigid and may
  need loosening.

These recognitions deepen your existing `AssemblageRecognition` work and link
to creative, political, and personal transformation domains.
