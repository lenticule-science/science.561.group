**Reading along the bias grain** is Marisa J. Fuentes's method for analyzing how an archive's omissions, distortions, classifications, and document forms were structured by the power relations that produced them. A record that marginalizes an enslaved woman can still disclose what officials criminalized, what an institution needed to know, and whose injury its categories made unintelligible.

Reading the bias is not filling absence with invented interiority. The method presses a hostile document for evidence about its own production while preserving the difference between an institutional classification, a reported act, and the historical actor's unstated thought.
