**Bodymind** names the material entwining of processes conventionally divided into mental and physical domains. Sami Schalk develops the term within Black feminist disability studies to analyze disability, race, gender, and speculative representation without treating the mind and body as independent substances whose social meanings can be studied separately.

For labor inquiry, bodymind makes pain, exhaustion, trauma, cognition, illness, impairment, and care needs internal to the analysis of capacity. It also imposes a boundary: disability cannot function only as a metaphor for slavery's injury. The lives and knowledge of disabled people require analysis beyond figurative use.
