# Reproductive Racial Capitalism

**Reproductive racial capitalism** is a contemporary framework that places conception, gestation, birth, childrearing, and the reproduction of social relations within racialized accumulation rather than outside the economy as natural or private support. Jennifer L. Morgan and Alys Eve Weinbaum use the term to organize scholarship on hereditary racial slavery and its afterlives, including both exploitation and refusals of reproductive logics.

The framework can relate Atlantic slavery to later formations, but it does not establish an unchanged system across periods. A source must specify the legal rule, labor relation, medical practice, market, welfare arrangement, or technology through which reproduction is racialized and accumulated. The phrase is not antebellum actors' own vocabulary.
