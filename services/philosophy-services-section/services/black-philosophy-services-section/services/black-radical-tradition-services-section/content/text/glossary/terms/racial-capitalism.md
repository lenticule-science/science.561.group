Racial [capitalism](./capitalism.md) is the argument, originating with [Cedric Robinson](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/cedric-robinson.md) in *Black Marxism* (1983) and developed by [Ruth Wilson Gilmore](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/ruth-wilson-gilmore.md) [cite:@gilmore_GoldenGulag_2007], that [capitalism](./capitalism.md) did not emerge as a racially neutral economic system that later incorporated racial hierarchy. Capital accumulation was from its origins structured through racial differentiation — the production of categorical distinctions among populations in order to cheapen [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md), justify dispossession, and organize people into hierarchies of value.

Robinson traced this to feudal Europe, where intra-European racial logics — the racialization of the Irish, the Slavs, the Roma — predated colonial encounter. Capitalism inherited and extended these logics. The Atlantic slave trade did not introduce race into an otherwise neutral economic system; it scaled a logic already present in European social organization. The concept challenges orthodox Marxism's treatment of race as superstructural — as [ideology](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ideology.md) layered over a class base. Robinson argued that race was not a distortion of [capitalism](./capitalism.md) but a condition of its possibility.

[Gilmore](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/ruth-wilson-gilmore.md) extends the concept to show how racial [capitalism](./capitalism.md) operates through [organized abandonment](organized-abandonment.md): the uneven distribution of life chances across racialized geographies. Her definition of racism — "[the state](./the-state.md)-sanctioned or extralegal production and exploitation of group-differentiated vulnerability to premature death" — is a definition of racial [capitalism](./capitalism.md)'s output. It locates the harm not in attitudes or individual acts but in the political organization of differential mortality.

The concept refuses the separation of economic analysis from racial analysis. Any account of accumulation that treats race as incidental misidentifies how value is produced, distributed, and destroyed.
