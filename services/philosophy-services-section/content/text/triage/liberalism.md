The dominant political tradition of Euro-American modernity. Grounded in individual rights, consent of the governed, and procedural neutrality. The library's content here focuses on procedural liberalism (Rawls) and its intensification into late liberalism (Povinelli) — the phase where liberalism manages its own crises through recognition and inclusion rather than structural transformation.
