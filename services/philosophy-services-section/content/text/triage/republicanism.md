# Republicanism

The political tradition that grounds legitimacy in civic participation and public action rather than in rights and procedural neutrality. The library's content here centers on Hannah Arendt, whose recovery of the Greek and Roman political experience reframes politics as the human capacity to begin something new (natality) rather than the management of private interests.
