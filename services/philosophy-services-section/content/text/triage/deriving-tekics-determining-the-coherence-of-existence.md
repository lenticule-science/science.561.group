### Abstract

This paper presents a formal derivation of the **Existential Tekarc**, posited as the irreducible, zero-order force essential for establishing and maintaining the coherent distinctness of any entity. Beginning with the fundamental premise that existence necessitates non-equivalence with negation (S=¬S), this text derives how this condition implies a necessary differentiating mechanism. I introduce two co-necessary "teknic acts"—Including and Excluding—and demonstrate their induction by the Tekarc, thereby establishing it as the prerequisite for any stable, distinct existence.

---

### 1. Introduction

The capacity for any entity, S, to be coherently defined and distinguished from its negation, ¬S, is a fundamental prerequisite for its existence. This paper aims to formally derive the necessary underlying force that ensures this foundational distinction. I posit that the condition S=¬S is not merely a descriptive truth but necessitates an active, inherent mechanism to be consistently maintained. This mechanism, which we term the **Existential Tekarc**, is the subject of the following axiomatic derivation.

---

### 2. Definitions

For the purpose of this derivation, we define the following fundamental terms:

- **Entity (S):** A candidate for coherent existence or a defined concept.
    
- **Negation of Entity (¬S):** That which is not S.
    
- **Inequivalence (=):** A relation denoting distinctness.
    
- **Equivalence (≡):** A relation denoting identity.
    
- **Coherence of S (Coh(S)):** The minimal stable configuration in which S=¬S is consistently upheld.
    

---

### 3. The Premise of Coherence

The foundational condition for the individual existence or coherent definition of any entity S is its distinctness from its negation. This is formally stated as:

S=¬S

This condition is necessary for the existence of S. Should S≡¬S, the concept of S would collapse into contradiction, thereby precluding its coherent identity.

---

### 4. The Indeterminacy of Relation and Implied Differentiation

The statement S=¬S, while necessary, cannot be intrinsically stabilized without an active mechanism that definitively ascertains:

- **Inclusion Criteria:** What constitutes membership within S.
    
- **Exclusion Criteria:** What constitutes exclusion from S.
    

Consequently, the very requirement for S=¬S inherently implies the existence of a minimal differentiating force. This force establishes the boundaries and content of S. Formally, this implication is expressed as:

∃f:(S,¬S)↦Differentiated(S)

This asserts that there must exist a function, f, which maps the dyad of S and ¬S to a state where S is explicitly differentiated.

---

### 5. Coherence Induction: The Primary Teknic Acts

To satisfy the condition of Coh(S)—the stable maintenance of S=¬S—we propose the necessity of two **co-necessary teknic acts**. These acts are not externally applied but are inherently required to sustain the distinctness of S:

- **Include(S):** The act of affirming and internalizing S within itself.
    
- **Exclude(¬S):** The act of delimiting S by separating it from ¬S.
    

These are designated as the **primary teknic acts** fundamental to establishing coherence.

---

### 6. Declaration of the Existential Tekarc

We define the **Existential Tekarc** (hereafter, Tekarc) as the zero-order teknic force responsible for the co-induction of the aforementioned primary acts. The Tekarc is not a conscious agent but a fundamental operative principle.

Tekarc:Unit→(S↦Coh(S))×(¬S↦Coh(S))

Where:

- Unit represents an undifferentiated or foundational state from which differentiation emerges.
    
- Tekarc(Unit)=(Include,Exclude).
    

It is crucial to note that these acts are **co-induced** by the Tekarc; they are not chosen or externally applied. The imperative for differentiation (S=¬S) inherently necessitates and thus induces this fundamental force.

---

### 7. Structural Result: The Nature of Coherence

Given the above, the coherent state of S is formally defined by the successful operation of these co-induced acts:

Coh(S):={S∣Include(S)∧Exclude(¬S)}

This definition highlights that the coherence of S is conditional upon, and solely stable under, the continuous induction of the Tekarc. Without this active force, the boundaries and identity of S would dissolve, rendering S≡¬S.

---

### 8. Conclusion

This derivation establishes the **Existential Tekarc** as the irreducible and fundamental force necessary for any entity S to achieve and maintain coherent existence, defined by its distinctness from its negation (S=¬S). The Tekarc operates by co-inducing the primary teknic acts of Include(S) and Exclude(¬S), thereby actively constituting the boundaries and internal consistency of S. The condition S=¬S thus necessitates a dynamic, operative principle, the Tekarc, which underpins all coherent distinctions. This foundational analysis suggests that existence, far from being a passive state, is continually enacted by this intrinsic differentiating force.
