## **Binding the Real: Liberal Worlding and the Ontology of Linearity**

“Liberal worlding functions by binding the real to genre via a doctrine of linearity.” This is not merely a statement about how time is governed. It is a statement about how **being itself** is structured — how liberalism formats the real through anticipatory form, rendering emergence intelligible only to the extent that it follows a preconditioned logic of **order, separation, and progression**. This logic is what we call linearity. It is not just a matter of when something happens. It is a question of **how something can exist at all**.

---

### **Linearity as an Ontological Doctrine**

Linearity, in the liberal world, is not confined to timelines. It is **the condition of coherence** itself. An object must be **itself**, distinct, stable, and directed. A relation must be **between** entities, not constitutive of them. A transformation must have a **before and after**, not recursive becoming. In this regime, things are only permitted to exist if they do so **as objects with extension**, **as identities with persistence**, or **as subjects with developmental arcs**.

In other words, linearity is a metaphysics — one that presumes the real is made of **discrete entities** traveling along discrete paths, bound by cause-effect chains, and intelligible only through stable naming and progressive action. It is a logic of **is-ness**, not **with-ness**.

---

### **Genre as Ontological Apparatus**

Within this ontology, **genre** operates as a **mediating schema** — a kind of scaffold that formats not only how events are told, but how beings must be. Genre is not just aesthetic. It is **disciplinary**. It instructs subjects and objects alike on how to appear, how to relate, how to complete themselves.

In liberal worlding, genre absorbs difference not by annihilating it, but by formatting it into recognizable structures: the queer who finds love, the victim who heals, the Native who reconciles. These are not merely plots — they are **ontological permissions**. Genre tells the real how it is allowed to be.

And crucially, genre is **linear**: it moves from inciting incident to resolution, from rupture to restoration. Only those beings who can **fit this arc** are allowed to persist within the world it governs.

---

### **Against Recursive Being**

What is excluded from this regime is **recursion** — being that does not progress but folds back on itself; coherence that arises through **relation rather than identity**; existence that does not narrate, but resonates. Indigenous metaphysics, queer opacity, disabled dependency, spiritual immanence — these modes of life do not follow the rules of linear ontology. They do not seek completion; they **persist through entanglement**.

In your own relational calculus, the real is not a thing that unfolds; it is a **structure of ongoing relation**, recursively stabilizing coherence across difference, rupture, and time. The liberal world cannot process this kind of being. It reads it as incoherence, pathology, or premodern error.

---

### **Liberal Worlding as Ontological Capture**

To “bind the real to genre via a doctrine of linearity” is to constrain existence itself. It is to say: you may exist, but only as a **thing with a function**, a **life with a plot**, a **subject with resolution**. Everything else is misrecognized — either as noise (if too chaotic) or background (if too ambient).

This is not just a politics of recognition. It is an **ontological sorting algorithm**. It decides what exists, not through annihilation, but through formatting. It doesn’t kill relation; it **prevents it from appearing**.

---

### **Conclusion: Toward Nonlinear Being**

To interrupt liberal worlding is not just to disrupt progress narratives or capitalist time. It is to reject linear ontology — to reclaim the possibility of being that is recursive, entangled, and non-teleological. This is the promise of relational worlding: not an escape from form, but a reformation of form **as relation, not container**.

It is not enough to say that the future must be different. We must say that **the real must be allowed to become again** — not as object, not as spectacle, not as arc, but as **coherence-with**, recursively alive.
