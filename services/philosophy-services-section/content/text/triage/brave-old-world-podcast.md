The */Brave Old World Podcast/* as a [[id:47d513fe-99ed-41ab-acb0-c577679f0037][podcast]] where [[id:40cbaad9-af3c-4573-b356-ae116687e483][emsenn]] was the [[id:cdd75b6c-3543-4256-8124-27c718f6c8f4][podcast host]] and [[id:366d2365-48ea-43ef-be50-f67d89e9320e][dungeon master]] in a [[id:1697283a-e3a8-42bc-b010-113810baa831][Dungeons & Dragons campaign]]

* Original Copy

Brave Old World is a [[id:23f14ce6-3cb9-4212-8bec-25558f857c64][make-believe world]] world that [[id:aeec8d1e-0d5e-4dfa-8030-a6b644c585d1][my friends]] [[id:eac7bd24-9a1b-4de6-9c65-8698b128b7c9][hang out]] in while I serve as the [[id:e73aa716-5a75-4482-9602-eff0001083a3][narrator]]. We use [[id:dd58b9e9-dee6-45d4-99aa-035d8d1d191b][numbers]] and [[id:fa9438b2-1fd1-471a-a6d3-26fed711d0a0][dice rolls]] to help [[id:bd75f456-2ba1-4e4e-a9f9-0c66f33e44e0][make decisions]] but mostly it’s up to our [[id:ccd641fc-17d9-4b9c-9fcf-914af8f7687b][collective imagination]]. Put another way, we’re a [[id:0be2588a-9943-4c6c-b9a4-2f733866e4f0][Dungeons & Dragons podcast]].

Whenever we play, we [[id:2c168470-9709-4df3-b76a-25703996b198][stream]] the session live on [[id:d2e99d13-272e-4d20-b5dd-788fbd4c61e3][Twitch]]. Those get edited and get released as a podcast.

Brave Old World is like one of those old-time [[id:a38ecf1a-109c-4124-bb0b-0c73354a2625][radio plays]], only we use chance to decide where we’re going, instead of a script. Six of my friends are cast as the heroes, and I’m playing the extras.

** The story so far

The [[id:7f527251-b5fc-4546-9fc7-8b6af33451e0][Brave Old World]] used to be a land of [[id:87b75e38-7814-45a8-ba28-b9fd59a93d1f][magic]] and wonder. [[id:4020e332-87d9-4347-939e-7b2813ad5641][Humanity]] was entering a [[id:b774c653-dc51-4451-9266-504883d69995][golden age]] after discovering a [[id:ce0071b4-89a6-4e92-87a2-e856efd7f33e][Eastland]] populated with elves and halflings. Their wars with the dwarves and orcs of the Old World ended.

swords-shield-bow80 years ago, magic vanished in what would be called the Collapse. The dwarves disappeared and the elves became feral. Humanity was alone and cascaded into war.

wrench-scales-briefcaseWar and wealth created the spark of an industrial revolution. Industrialization brought bureaucracy and a new peace, held by debt and credit. Humanity successfully coped with the Collapse.

That’s where our story begins.
