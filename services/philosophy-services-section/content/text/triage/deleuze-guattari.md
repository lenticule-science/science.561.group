# Deleuze and Guattari

Gilles Deleuze (1925–1995) and Félix Guattari (1930–1992). Their collaborative work — *Anti-Oedipus* (1972) and *A Thousand Plateaus* (1980) — reworked psychoanalysis, political economy, and ontology. Central claims: desire is productive (not lack-based), social formations are desiring-machines, and the proper unit of social analysis is the assemblage (agencement) of heterogeneous components in contingent relation.
