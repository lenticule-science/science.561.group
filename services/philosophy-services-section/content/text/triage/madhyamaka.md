# Madhyamaka

The philosophical school founded by Nāgārjuna (c. 2nd century CE). The central claim: all phenomena are empty (śūnya) of inherent existence. The catuṣkoṭi (tetralemma) is the logical tool used to demonstrate this — showing that a thing cannot be said to exist, not exist, both, or neither in any inherent sense.
