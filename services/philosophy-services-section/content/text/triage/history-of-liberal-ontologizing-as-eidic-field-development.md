The ontological foundation of what is now referred to as _liberalism_ can be described as the cumulative effect of a recursive world-structuring grammar, in which the legibility and coherence of being are conditioned through projective alignment with idealized image-forms, or **eidolons**. These eidolons function as organizing templates for intelligibility, actionability, and systemic incorporation. The process by which beings, systems, and events are induced into alignment with eidolons is governed by a persistent, directional force here termed the **eidogenic vector**.

Historically, this process begins with early cosmological formalism. The **Milesian philosophers** introduced the premise of ontological reducibility: that multiplicity could be derived from an underlying _archê_. This introduced both an explanatory structure and a regulatory constraint: to understand and to govern required ordering being through abstraction and derivation. This ontological model rendered certain operations—measurement, boundary-setting, hierarchy formation—structurally viable. The act of naming became a mechanism of ontological stabilization.

**Platonic metaphysics** formalized eidic structuration: the material world was real insofar as it approximated immaterial, ideal Forms. The act of recognition became a projection of alignment: to be seen was to be seen _as_ a participant in form. This introduced a recursive ontological condition in which proximity to eidolon governed inclusion within the domain of coherent being. The **eidogenic vector**, although unnamed, operated structurally as the force by which instances were perceived, evaluated, and conditionally stabilized.

**Aristotelian hylomorphism** integrated this structure into a temporal grammar: form exists within matter as telos. The movement of matter toward form was not epistemic but constitutive of the entity’s being. This enabled the development of ontologically-justified governance across domains such as biology, ethics, domestic organization, and civic structure. Material life was ordered according to its anticipated alignment with functionally determined forms.

The **Christian scholastic synthesis** reframed eidic structuration as a theologically ordered hierarchy. Ontological alignment became moralized. The logic of coherence through form persisted, now tied to divine ordinance. The eidolon was reified in sacrament, virtue, and hierarchy. Feudal governance, sacramental embodiment, and calendrical regulation operated as technologies of eidic normalization, producing differentiated modes of being through alignment to stabilized projections.

The **Enlightenment** abstracted this structure into rational formalism. Cartesian and Kantian systems retained eidic grammar while reframing coherence as a function of reason, cognition, and categorical alignment. Being was constrained by epistemological conditions, which in turn stabilized permissible material forms: subjects, legal personhood, mechanical time, and spatialized jurisdiction. Here, the **eidocratic systematization** of material life through transport, measurement, urban design, and work discipline became operational.

With **colonial expansion**, eidic structuration became globalized. The ontological non-coherence of the colonized was not contingent but structural—failure to align with the eidic norms of European formalism instantiated their exclusion from legal, economic, and biopolitical intelligibility. Infrastructural impositions—roads, education systems, taxonomies, property law—acted as enforcement mechanisms for eidogenic alignment.

Modern **liberalism** emerges not as a rupture but as a recursive formalization of these processes. It posits coherence through autonomy, agency, and self-determination, but these are constrained by the same grammar: _the real must appear as aligned to form_. Governance under liberalism is the regulation of eidic alignment across domains: civil, biological, architectural, affective, and informational. Even nominally disruptive or pluralist categories are structured as **eidies**—instances that fulfill alignment criteria within pre-established eidic fields.

Thus, the history of liberal ontologizing is the history of **eidic world-production**. It is not confined to discursive or ideological domains but materialized in infrastructure, classification, embodiment, and temporal regulation. The **eidogenic vector** is the infrastructural operator through which beings, systems, and practices are recursively formatted into coherence with projected eidic regimes.
