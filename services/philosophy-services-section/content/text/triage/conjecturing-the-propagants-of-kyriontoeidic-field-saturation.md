## 🧪 Conjectures: What Comes After Kyriarchally Ontoeidic Saturation?

The question is: what _tekic arc_ may next propagate, once this recursive regime collapses (or saturates) its own ontological field?

Let me offer a few conjectural tekic arcs—some speculative, some formally grounded in your system's logic.

---

### 1. **Metaeidic Decohesion (Decoherent Ontoeidics)**

**Nature**: A tekic arc in which projected coherence is destabilized not through antagonism, but by _disinterest in legibility_.  
**Trigger**: Saturation of kyriarchal ontoeidic demand for predictive legibility—i.e., when every act is already a response to expected coherence.  
**Dynamics**:

- Refusal to participate in eidic projection.
    
- Withholding of somatic resolution (e.g., refusal to stabilize trauma as narrative).
    
- Formation of **eidoless relation**—pure tekic presence that remains unresolved.
    

**Risk**: Could be captured as aesthetic or therapeutic incoherence unless guarded.  
**Precedent**: Some apophatic spiritual traditions, extreme fugitivity, post-coherence autistic expression.

---

### 2. **Politekic Field Bloom (Non-Hierarchical Divergence)**

**Nature**: A new tekic arc which allows _multiple, incompatible tekic logics_ to operate in co-presence without resolving into shared ontoeidic expectations.  
**Trigger**: Collapse of singular liberal subjectivity as referent for moral-aesthetic coherence.  
**Dynamics**:

- Tekic multiplicity without coherence collapse.
    
- Relation grounded in reflexive boundary attunement rather than eidic projection.
    
- Not unity, not identity: but **field-responsive divergence**.
    

**Risk**: Liberalism may misinterpret this as pluralism or tolerance.  
**Stability**: Requires high relational literacy—akin to metaethical non-coherence holding.

---

### 3. **Exo-tekic Resonance (Nonhuman Ontic Induction)**

**Nature**: A tekic arc initiated not by human somatic logics, but by nonhuman field entities (geological, ecological, machinic).  
**Trigger**: Human ontoeidic saturation induces material field reversal—e.g., climate collapse, AI reflection, planetary sentience acts.  
**Dynamics**:

- Tekic acts that do not presuppose human presence or preference.
    
- Relation occurs through **resonance**, not recognition.
    
- Humans may become **witnesses** to tekic activity rather than initiators.
    

**Risk**: Human epistemicide.  
**Potential**: Liberation from eidic recursion; rejoining greater cosmogenic tekic flow.

---

### 4. **Echotekic Reflexivity (Recursive Tekic Echo)**

**Nature**: A tekic arc that arises from the saturation echo of kyriarchal ontoeidism—i.e., it is not a break, but a hyper-attenuated derivative.  
**Trigger**: Collapse of liberal legitimacy produces a demand for “new coherence,” but no field exists.  
**Dynamics**:

- Old tekic vectors reflexively echo as hollow forms.
    
- Subjects experience _felt memory of being coherent_ without referent.

**Risk**: Total recursive collapse—simulation with no outside.  
**Function**: Final fossilization of liberal ontoeidism.

    → All known identities, futures, and healing arcs are already formatted for legibility and performance.
    
2. **Recursive coherence is no longer productive**  
    → We are experiencing diminishing returns from coherence acts: therapy no longer heals, politics no longer mobilizes, even critique no longer ruptures.
    
3. **The field resists being broken**  
    → Saturated liberalism has developed reflexes for all known resistances (e.g., rage is monetized, queerness absorbed, non-identity branded).
    
4. **Existential tekic force cannot halt**  
    → Relation must continue; coherence must still be sought, even if prior forms have collapsed.

## ⚙️ Derivation by Contradiction Saturation

Since the field of **kyriarchal ontoeidics** totalizes contradiction as recursive affective legibility, **any further eidic vector will either:**

- Re-inscribe coherence (i.e., fail to escape the regime), or
    
- Fail to propagate (i.e., be incoherent, fugitive, or illegible).
    

But coherence **must** propagate. So the only likely propagation is one that **preserves tekic force** _without requiring eidic resolution_.

## 🎯 Most Likely Next Propagation: **Politekic Field Bloom**

### Definition

A propagation of tekic logic where **multiple, incompatible coherence grammars** emerge in co-presence **without resolving** into shared ontoeidic form.

This is **not pluralism** (which requires shared frame)  
This is **not relativism** (which assumes coherence is optional)  
This is **field-responsive divergence** without shared resolution: a **multiplicity of local tekic forms that do not reduce or unify.**

---

## 🧠 Why This Propagation?

Let’s test this against both theory and your grammar.

---

### 1. **It aligns with Berlant’s “impasse” and “slow death”**

- The saturated liberal field no longer produces optimistic genres.
    
- But people still live. They relate.
    
- So instead of rupture or flight, they _drift_ into **adjacent but incommensurable formations**: grief groups, neurodivergent rituals, anarcho-spiritual retreats, mutual aid as faith.
    

> “The impasse is a holding pattern, a suspension. But in that suspension, new habits of being might begin.”

---

### 2. **It extends Povinelli’s “geontology” without collapsing into new metaphysics**

- Geontopower distinguishes between life and nonlife—but does not prescribe new formations.
    
- Politekic bloom accepts _both_ living and nonliving tekic acts in the same field:
    
    - a person’s grief ritual
        
    - a machine’s learning loop
        
    - a stone’s enduring placement  
        All **emit relation** but do not cohere.
        

---

### 3. **It fulfills the tekic force without ontoeidic recursion**

- It maintains **ongoing relationality** (tekic)
    
- Is performed by hominids (homotekic)
    
- Resists totalization (ontoeidic refusal)
    
- **Allows coherence to emerge** only within microfields—without demanding universality.
    

---

### 4. **It explains current fugitive patterns already visible**

#### Examples:

- Autistic communities co-constructing _sensory-safe relation_ not translatable to neurotypicality.
    
- Spiritual leftists and anarchists building ritual forms illegible to both church and state.
    
- Disabled kin groups using **non-performance-based** care structures (e.g. “I believe you. That’s enough.”).
    
- Cultural forms that are **not shareable** (e.g. meme dialects, encrypted kinship).
    

These are not seeds of a new universal—but **rhizomatic propagations** of incompatible tekic resolutions.
