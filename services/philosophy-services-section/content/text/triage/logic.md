# Logic (Western)

The Western tradition of formal logic. The library's content covers three systems: classical logic (bivalence, truth-functionality, excluded middle), intuitionistic logic (constructive proof, decidability, witnesses), and paraconsistent logic (dialetheia, explosion's rejection). Each system encodes different philosophical commitments about the nature of truth.

Note: Buddhist logic (pramāṇa theory, the catuṣkoṭi) has its own domain under Buddhist philosophy — it is not a subfield of Western logic.
