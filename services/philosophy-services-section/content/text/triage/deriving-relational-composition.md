## Claim

Within the [[domain of relating]], once unary [[relational structures]] are formed through the [[reflexive sequence]] and related across the [[reflective sequence]], a further act is required to bind multiple distinct unary structures into a single, ordered composite. This act preserves the individuality of each component while inducing explicit positional structure.

## Derivation

### Postulate

> If multiple unary [[relational structures]] at shared [[sequence]] are available and are held in [[relational distinction]] via [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect]], then a composite structure can be [[induce|induced]] that maintains their order and enables positional access.

### Steps

1. Let `{x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)}` be unary [[relational structures]] at [[sequence]] level `n`.
2. Define a new act:  
   `compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`  
   → relates these structures into a single ordered composite.
3. This yields a new structure:  
   `Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`  
   which preserves:
   - The [[difference]] between components  
   - The [[order]] of their composition
4. Each component `x(i)` remains accessible within the tuple by its position.
5. This composite requires the condition of [[ordinal]]:  
   - Each element occupies a fixed relational position within the composite structure.

## Formalism

Let:

- `x(i)` ∈ unary [[relational structures]] at [[sequence]] level `n`, where `1 ≤ i ≤ p`
- `compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)` := `Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)`

The resulting [[reflexive relational tuple]] satisfies:

- Positional access: `Index(i, Tuple(...)) = x(i)`  
- Ordered integrity: order is preserved under [[reflex]] and [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect]]
