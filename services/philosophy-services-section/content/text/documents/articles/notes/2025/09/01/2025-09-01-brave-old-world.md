***Brave Old World*** is a [tabletop role-playing game ruleset](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tabletop-role-playing-game-ruleset.md), based on Apocalypse World. It was built for playing [tabletop role-playing games](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tabletop-role-playing-game.md) set in Teraum.
