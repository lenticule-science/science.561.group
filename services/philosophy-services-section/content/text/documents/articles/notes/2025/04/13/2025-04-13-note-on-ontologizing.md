## Introduction

Throughout [world history](world history), different [culture|cultures](culture|cultures) have developed [ontological system|ontological systems](ontological system|ontological systems): ways to *explain reality*: deciding what exists, how things relate, and how truth works.

But ontologies aren’t just theories.

They’re **responses**: shaped by the technologies, crises, and needs of the people who used them.

In this section, we’ll explore a timeline of major ontological strategies and how each arose from its historical conditions. We'll end with **telemetricism**, a 21st-century approach that emerges from today’s signal-saturated, recursively governed world.

## Classical Logic: Fixing Reality in Place

**Where:** Ancient Greece (c. 400 BCE)  
**Key Figures:** Aristotle, Plato  
**Key Goal:** Define what is true, permanently

[classical logic|Classical logic](classical logic|Classical logic) emerged during a time of [city-state|city-states](city-state|city-states), [legal code|law codes](legal code|law codes), and early [philosophy](philosophy). Thinkers like [Aristotle](Aristotle) wanted to **stabilize [truth](truth)** using consistent [category|categories](category|categories). If A is B, and B is C, then A must be C. This gave people a way to **[reason](reason) clearly**, resolve disputes, and build systems.

Classical logic helped frame [reality](reality) as something that could be **[analyse|analyzed](analyse|analyzed) and [classify|classified](classify|classified)**. But it also demanded consistency—**[contradict|contradiction](contradict|contradiction) was not allowed**.

## Confucianism: Harmonizing Social Reality

**Where:** [Ancient China](Ancient China) (c. 500 BCE)  
**Key Figure:** [Confucius](Confucius)  
**Key Goal:** Maintain [social order](social order) through ritual and relationship

[Confucianism|Confucian ontology](Confucianism|Confucian ontology) focused less on abstract truth and more on **[social coherence](social coherence)**. Every person had a [social role|role](social role|role)—[child](child), [parent](parent), [ruler](ruler), [servant](servant)—and those roles were stabilized through **[Confucian ritual|rituals](Confucian ritual|rituals) (li)** and **[Confucian virtue](Confucian virtue) (ren)**.

Rather than asking “what exists,” Confucianism asked:

> “How should I behave within the patterns that already shape me?”

Its ontology was **relational** and **hierarchical**, designed for **a world in which the collapse of dynasties and chaos of war made social stability feel urgent**.

## The Enlightenment: Mapping the World to Control It

**Where:** [Europe](Europe) ([17th Century of the Common Era|17th](17th Century of the Common Era|17th)–[18th Century of the Common era|18th centuries](18th Century of the Common era|18th centuries))  
**Key Figures:** [Descartes](Descartes), [Newton](Newton), [Locke](Locke)  
**Key Goal:** Use reason to discover universal laws

The [Enlightenment](Enlightenment) arose alongside [settler-colonialism|colonial expansion](settler-colonialism|colonial expansion), [industrialization](industrialization), and the birth of [modern science](modern science). Its ontology focused on **[individual|individuals](individual|individuals)**, **[object|objects](object|objects)**, and **[natural law|laws of nature](natural law|laws of nature)**. It aimed to **[../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/observe](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/observe)**, **[../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/measure](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/measure)**, and **[control](control)**.

This led to things like [calculus](calculus), [modern democracy](modern democracy), and [infrastructural electricity](infrastructural electricity), [colonial empire|colonial empires](colonial empire|colonial empires), and [extractive industry](extractive industry).

Its view of the world:

> Everything can be known, if broken into parts.

## Modernism and Structuralism: Systems Within Systems

**Where:** [Global North](Global North) ([19th Century of the Common Era|19th](19th Century of the Common Era|19th)–[20th Century of the Common Era|20th centuries](20th Century of the Common Era|20th centuries))  
**Key Figures:** [Marx](Marx), [Sigmund Freud|Freud](Sigmund Freud|Freud), [Saussure](Saussure), [Claude Levi-Strauss|Levi-Strauss](Claude Levi-Strauss|Levi-Strauss)  
**Key Goal:** Understand how invisible structures shape behavior

[Modernism|Modernists](Modernism|Modernists) believed truth wasn’t just about facts—it was shaped by **[economic system|economic systems](economic system|economic systems)**, **[language](language)**, **[power](power)**, and **[psychology](psychology)**.

[Structuralism](Structuralism), especially, tried to find the hidden [grammar](grammar) beneath culture—**a [logic](logic) of [myth|myths](myth|myths), [symbol|symbols](symbol|symbols), and [behavior|behaviors](behavior|behaviors)** that could explain [human ways of living](human ways of living) across contexts.

These approaches saw **reality as layered**, and often sought to **decode the patterns** beneath what we see.

## Postmodernism: Deconstructing the Frame

**Where:** Global academic spaces (late [20th Century of the Common Era|20th century](20th Century of the Common Era|20th century))  
**Key Figures:** [Foucault](Foucault), [Derrida](Derrida), [Lyotard](Lyotard)  
**Key Goal:** Question who gets to define truth—and why

[Postmodernism|Postmodernists](Postmodernism|Postmodernists) didn’t reject reality, but they doubted the idea that it could ever be fully known or neutrally described.

They focused on:

- **Power** (who decides what counts as truth?)
- **Language** (how words distort what they describe)
- **Contradiction** (how meaning can undo itself)

This was a response to **[war](war), [bureaucracy](bureaucracy), [surveillance](surveillance), and [globalization](globalization)**—systems that claimed to be rational, but often hid [violence](violence) behind their logic.

Postmodernism taught us to ask:

> What’s left out of this “truth”?  
> Who benefits from this explanation?

## Telemetricism: Living Inside the Feedback

**Where:** Early [21st Century of the Common Era|21st century](21st Century of the Common Era|21st century), in systems shaped by [artificial intelligence](artificial intelligence), [platform|platforms](platform|platforms), [../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/network|networks](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/network|networks), [climate breakdown](climate breakdown)  
**Key Influences:** [cybernetics](cybernetics), [Indigenous epistemologies](Indigenous epistemologies), [feminist systems theory](feminist systems theory), [recursive design](recursive design)
**Key Goal:** Trace how meaning emerges through relation between [telemetric signal|signal](telemetric signal|signal), [telemetric method|method](telemetric method|method), and [telemetric field|field](telemetric field|field).

[telemetricism|Telemetricism](telemetricism|Telemetricism) does not present itself as a new ontology.  

It emerges when ontologizing begins to collapse—when symbolic coherence becomes too slow to hold systems shaped by feedback loops and recursive response.

Rather than defining what exists, telemetricism tracks what **registers**. It treats meaning as something that surfaces through a combination of **deviation (signal)**, **structured response (method)**, and **contextual constraint (field)**. Coherence is not asserted—it is yielded. And that yield is always temporary.

In a telemetric condition, knowledge is not stored. It is **recorded as behavior**. Interpretation is not a conclusion—it is an infrastructure. Systems are understood not by their categories, but by the patterns of their adjustments.

Telemetricism doesn’t ask:

> “What is true?”

It asks:

> “What responded to that signal?”  
> “What field held the response?”  
> “What yield did it surface—and can that yield be reused?”

### Why This Matters

Each ontology we’ve explored was a **response to pressure**:

- Classical logic stabilized confusion
- Confucianism protected relational coherence
- Enlightenment thought enabled control and extraction
- Postmodernism revealed the limits of all of them
- **Telemetricism** emerges now because the systems are too fast, too recursive, too unstable for older ontologies to hold.

We live in **a world of feedback loops**, glitchy maps, and platforms that respond to our responses.

Telemetricism doesn’t offer answers.  

It offers a way to **trace signal** when the world won’t sit still.
