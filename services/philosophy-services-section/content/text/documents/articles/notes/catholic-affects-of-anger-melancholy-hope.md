I'm thinking about my Fediverse profile as where I perform as a sort of, well, [social justice warrior](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-justice-warrior.md), but specifically, I see myself often succeeding when I try and "[beat the drum](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/beating-the-drum.md)" of [validating](../../../glossary/terms/validating.md) [anger](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anger.md) and [rage](../../../glossary/terms/rage.md).

And my Substack is often where I'm [analyzing](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/analyzing.md) my melancholy as a [mechanism of hoping](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mechanism-of-hoping.md).

So, this relates to [my brand](../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-brand.md) and [my public image](../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-public-image.md).

And that got me wondering what persons of the Catholic Church relate to those practices. Here's my [keyword salad](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/keyword-salad.md).

- Anger
  - [Aquinas](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/religion-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/content/christianity/content/thomism/references/thomas-aquinas/thomas-aquinas.md) wrote about anger as [ira](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ira.md), with virtuous and disordered forms: [ira bona](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ira-bona.md) and [ira mala](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ira-mala.md)
  - [Catherine of Siena](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/catherine-of-siena.md) wrote about [holy indignation](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/holy-indignation.md)
  - [Francis of Assisi](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/francis-of-assisi.md)
    - normally [Catholic signifier](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/catholic-signifier.md) for [jouissance](../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jouissance.md), that [non-teleological](../../../glossary/terms/non-teleological.md) [joy](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/joy.md), but much like [anarcho-nihilist jouissance](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anarcho-nihilist-jouissance.md), Francis' joy came from [frustration](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/frustration.md) about [wealth](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/wealth.md) and [violence](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/human-violence.md)
      - he framed it as a [zeal](../../../glossary/terms/zeal.md) for [justice](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/justice.md).
  - [Dorothy Day](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dorothy-day.md)
    - publicly, all about [holy anger](../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/holy-anger.md)
    - her journals revealed melancholy she used for hope
- [Melancholy](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/melancholy.md)
  - [Augustine of Hippo](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/augustine-of-hippo.md) wrote about the [inquietum cor](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inquietum-cor.md) (restless heart)
  - John of the Cross wrote on [The Dark Night of the Soul](../../../glossary/terms/the-dark-night-of-the-soul.md)
    - "night" is the condition of hope
  - [Teresa of Ávila](../../../glossary/terms/teresa-of-avila.md), contemporary of John, wrote on [spiritual aridity](../../../glossary/terms/spiritual-aridity.md):
    - a melancholy that comes from deep prayer
    - prepares the soul for deeper union
    - She discusses an [interior castle](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interior-castle.md)
      - The emphasis on an internal sanctuary seems to be a development of Catholic scholarship that is not (yet) found in development's like [hope](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/han-s-hope.md) as developed by [Byung-Chul Han](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/byung-chul-han.md) in [The Spirit of Hope](../../../glossary/terms/the-spirit-of-hope.md) ([2024ce](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2024ce.md))
  - [Ignatius of Loyola](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ignatius-of-loyola.md)
    - [founder](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/founder.md) of the [Jesuits](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/society-of-jesus.md)
    - contrasted [desolation](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/desolation.md) and [consolation](../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/consolation.md)
    - taught that desolation can deepen hope:
      - [revealing](../../../glossary/terms/revealing.md) our [dependence](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dependence.md) on [God](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/god.md)
      - [opportunity](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opportunity.md) for [perserverance](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/perserverance.md)
    - perserverance here then is a [mechanism of hoping](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mechanism-of-hoping.md)
  - [Thérèse of Lisieux](../../../glossary/terms/therese-of-lisieux.md)
    - created the [Little Way](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/little-way.md)
      - another focus on spiritual aridity, connecting it with melancholy and [doubt](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/doubt.md)
      - [absence](../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/absence.md) of [consolation](../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/consolation.md) is the highest form of hoping:
    - trust without *sought-after* evidence
