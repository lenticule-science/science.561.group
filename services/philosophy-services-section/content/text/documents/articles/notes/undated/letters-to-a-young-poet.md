***Letters to a Young Poet*** is a collection of ten [letters](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/letter.md) [written](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/writing.md) by [Rainer Maria Rilke](../../../../glossary/terms/rainer-maria-rilke.md) to [Franz Xaver Kappus](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/franz-xaver-kappus.md), between [1903ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1903ce.md) and [1908ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1908ce.md).
