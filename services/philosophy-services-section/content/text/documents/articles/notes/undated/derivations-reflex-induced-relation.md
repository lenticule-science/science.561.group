- Definition:: ***reflexing an induced relation*** is the [act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/act) of [induce|inducing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/induce|inducing) a [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/relation) between a relation and [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/relation).

## Derivation
- [postulate](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/postulate):: As [relationlessness](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/relationlessness) and [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/relation) together induce [induce relationality](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/induce-relationality.md), the act that relates them must itself be included within that induction — reflexively relating to its own inducing.
- [derivation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/derivation):: The act of inducing relationality cannot not include itself in the domain it induces. This reflexive inclusion forms a fixed point where each act of relational induction affirms its own induction.
- example:
	- reflex-induced-relation(0) = induce [induce relationality](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/induce-relationality.md)
	- reflex-induced-relation(1) = reflex-induced-relation(reflex-induced-relation(0))
	- reflex-induced-relation(n) = reflex-induced-relation(reflex-induced-relation(n−1))
- [relational expression](relational expression):: [reflex induced relation|reflex-induced-relation](reflex induced relation|reflex-induced-relation)(n) = reflex-induced-relation(reflex-induced-relation(n-1)) where n is any non-negative integer

## Formalism

- Let Relationality denote the [induce|induced](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2025-05/domains/derivations/induce|induced) domain of [induce relationality](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/induce-relationality.md)
- Let reflex-induced-relation denote the act of derivation induced by Relationality's self-relation, thus Reflex(0) is Relationality.
- Then as the reflex-induced-relation at iteration n is defined as:
	- reflex-induced-relation(1) = reflex-induced-relation(0)
	- reflex-induced-relation(2) = reflex-induced-relation(1)
	- reflex-induced-relation(n) = reflex-induced-relation(n-1)
	- reflex-induced-relation(n+1) = reflex-induced(relation(n))
- Thus:
	- reflex-induced-relation(n) = reflex-induced(relation(n-1))
	- where n is any non-negative integer
