*(Note: I didn't have [[Obsidian]] set up when I started [[reading]].)*

---
sapphic, Jewish, Christmas-themed (on a Christmas tree farm)
Noelle - fat, butch, sober, twice orphaned (parents, then Cass), does the tree-related stuff
Miriam - Jewish, queer (bi?), parents are shitheads, artist, engaged to Tara, 35
unconvincing chemistry
Miriam seems too... quirky to be Tara's "society wife" (p. 115)
Cole feels very one-dimensional (makes me think of Drama John from The Great North)
- Tara just drops that Cole spends his weekends "doxxing white nationalists" (p. 126) at Thanksgiving dinner. is this gonna come back up??
Cass - previous owner of Carrigan's (farm), Miriam's aunt (?)
Miriam loved spending time at Carrigan's as kid, couldn't go back for 10 years after leaving dad's control because she feared his "ghost," didn't talk to Hannah (cousin, best friend) about why
Noelle has a hard time trusting Miriam because of her alleged flightiness (evidence: not coming back to farm for a decade)
Cass convinced a bunch of people she liked to move to Advent (town Carrigian's is in) and start businesses
