- [../acts/define](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/define):: A [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) is the immediate outcome of an [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act). It is what is brought about, held open transiently, by the [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) that produces it. Every [../structures/structure](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/structures/structure) holds a [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) durably; every [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/relation) requires at least one [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition).

## Derivation

- [../acts/derive|Deriving](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/derive|Deriving) [../acts/exist|existing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/exist|existing) shows that every [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) necessarily brings about a [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition).
    - For example, the [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) of [../acts/include|including](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/include|including) brings about the [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) of [inclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/inclusion);
    - The [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) of [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding) brings about the [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) of [exclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/exclusion).

- A [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) is distinct from the [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) that produces it.
    - An [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) is what **does**; a [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) is what **is held open** by that doing.
    - The [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) only persists **as long as the [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act) is active**, unless stabilized by a [../structures/structure](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/structures/structure).

- [condition|Conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|Conditions) are necessary because without them:
    - The effects of [../acts/act|acts](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act|acts) would have no persistence, even transiently;
    - No differentiation between states could be identified or held open, even momentarily.

- In the derivation of [../acts/exist|existing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/exist|existing):
    - [inclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/inclusion) and [exclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/exclusion) are the [condition|conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|conditions)nditions]] that arise from the primitive [../acts/act|acts](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act|acts) of [../acts/include|including](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/include|including) and [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/exclude|excluding);
    - [tension](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/tension) is the [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) that holds [inclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/inclusion) and [exclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/exclusion) in mutual opposition;
    - [difference](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/difference) is the [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) induced by that [tension](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/tension), marking a [triage/thing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/triage/thing) as set apart from what is not;
    - [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/relation) is the [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) that specifies the binding of the [triage/thing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/triage/thing) to its ongoing [../acts/exist|existing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/exist|existing) via [../../../ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/acts/relate|relating).

- Thus, [condition|conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|conditions):
    - Arise **immediately** from [../acts/act|acts](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act|acts);
    - Are **maintained** transiently by those acts;
    - Require [../structures/structure|structures](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/structures/structure|structures) to achieve durability and coherence.

## Implications

- No [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) can arise independently of an [../acts/act](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act).
- No [../structures/structure](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/structures/structure) can exist without holding at least one [condition](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition) durably.
- [relational dynamics](relational dynamics) depend on a layered interaction:  
    - [../acts/act|acts](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/act|acts) bring about [condition|conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|conditions);  
    - [../structures/structure|structures](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/structures/structure|structures) hold those [condition|conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|conditions) open durably, forming the basis for further [relation|relations](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/relation|relations).
- The minimal architecture of [../acts/exist|existing](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/acts/exist|existing) necessarily includes multiple [condition|conditions](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/condition|conditions): [inclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/inclusion), [exclusion](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/exclusion), [tension](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/tension), [difference](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/difference), and [relation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/human-draft-7/domains/system/domains/conditions/relation).
