# Predicated Philosophy

A theory of [philosophy](../../../../../_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories) mathematics: philosophical traditions as predicate-category configurations, closure-rule disciplines, and regimes over the entities the tradition holds to populate the world. Metaphysical ontologies, epistemic stances, ethical systems, and cosmologies are exhibited as specific structures on this substrate.

Sibling-as-approach to the tradition-organized subtheories of [philosophy/theories/](../../../..) ([western/](../../../../western), [buddhist/](../../../../buddhist), [caribbean/](../../../../caribbean), [indigenous/](../../../../indigenous), [logic/](../../../../logic), [ontological-turn/](../../../../ontological-turn), [relationality/](../../../../relationality)). Predicated-philosophy is not itself a tradition; it is the substrate-algebraic shape that traditions take when formalized on the predication mathematics.

Following the [predication-categories research convention](../../../../../../math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md).

## What this subtheory commits to

- **A philosophical tradition is substrate content.** A tradition's metaphysics is a choice of predicate category and entity set; its epistemic stance is the witness discipline on cells; its ethics is the closure-rule discipline that produces admissible conduct-cells; its cosmology is the settling structure on the relational substrate.

- **Traditions differ in substrate, not only in claims.** Western analytic philosophy, Buddhist Madhyamaka, Lakota relational ontology, and Caribbean creolization are not just different answers to the same questions — they install different predicate categories, different closure rules, and different entity sets. The substrate makes these differences visible at the structural level rather than only at the propositional level.

- **Formalization does not flatten.** A predicated form of a tradition does not commit the tradition to the formalism; it gives the tradition a tool. The substantive content remains the tradition's, held under whatever authority the tradition's own custom recognizes.
