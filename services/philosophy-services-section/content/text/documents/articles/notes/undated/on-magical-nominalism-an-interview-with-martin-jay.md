- [interview](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interview.md) between [Howard Prosser](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/howard-prosser.md) and [Martin Jay](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/martin-jay.md), focusing on [conceptual elements](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conceptual-element.md) from Magical Nominalism: The Historical Event, Aesthetic Reenchantment, and the Photograph.
- Prosser asks about the origins of the book
  - Jay points back two decades or more, to:
    - his writing a review of [Hans Blumenberg](../../../../glossary/terms/hans-blumenberg.md)'s [The Legitimacy of the Modern Age](../../../../glossary/terms/the-legitimacy-of-the-modern-age.md)
      - That got him into [William of Ockham](../../../../glossary/terms/william-of-ockham.md) and [nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nominalism.md), [14th Century](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/14th-century.md)
      - nominalism [asserted](../../../../glossary/terms/asserting.md) [divine omnipotence](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/divine-omnipotence.md)
    - against [rationalist philosophy](../../../../glossary/terms/rationalist-philosophy.md) of [Classical Era](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/classical-era.md)
    - instead, world shaped by [will](../../../../glossary/terms/will.md) and [caprice](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/caprice.md)
      - Blumenberg asserted nominalism led to modernity through asserting… [individualism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/individualism.md)? "human self-assertion" is what Jay says.
    - Jay studied [Weimar culture](../../../../glossary/terms/weimar-culture.md)
      - interested in origins of [magical realism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/magical-realism.md)
    - began as synonym for [Neue Sachlichkeit](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/neue-sachlichkeit.md) ("New Objectivity")
    - morphed years later into a [literary movement](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/literary-movement.md)
      - identified with [Latin American literature](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/latin-american-literature.md), i.e. [Gabriel García Márquez](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gabriel-garcia-marquez.md)
  - Jay was asked to contribute an essay celebrating [W. J. T. Mitchell](../../../../glossary/terms/w-j-t-mitchell.md), whose study of visual culture had inspired Jay's work on the French denigration of vision
    - Jay decided to write about the philosophical implications of photography
    - Wanted to use "magical"
      - not to modify "realism"
    - which had meant belief in the ontological existence of real universals during Ockham's challenge
      - but to modify "nominalism"
    - First text was Magical Nominalism: Photography and the Re-enchangement of the World ([2009ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2009ce.md))
  - From there, Jay left it on the back burner
    - Encountered work of [Agata Bielik-Robson](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agata-bielik-robson.md)
      - Polish scholar of Jewish studies
      - Coined "[Jewish nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jewish-nominalism.md)"
    - more recently "[mystical nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mystical-nominalism.md)"
    - ***emsenn***: sounds like space for [Lakotaean nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-nominalism.md), which could be the [anthropological field](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anthropological-field.md) of [relational process theory](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/01/2025-09-01-relational-dynamics-org-roam-catalog-query.md)?
- Prosser asks, "Why consider the [magical](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/magical.md) now when [life](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/life.md) (modernity, still?) seems, so, well [mundane](../../../../glossary/terms/mundane.md) or, following [Weber](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/max-weber.md), [disenchanted](../../../../glossary/terms/disenchanted.md).

  - Jay highlights he began with "all of the typcial modern prejudices against revivals of [belief](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/belief.md) in [supernatural](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/supernatural-practice.md) or [occult practices](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/occult-practice.md), and still largely retain them.-

    - Jay "imbibed" [Horkheimer](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/max-horkheimer.md) and [Adorno](../../../../glossary/terms/theodor-w-adorno.md)'s [Dialectic of Enlightenment](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectic-of-enlightenment.md)
      - So, [Frankfurt School](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/frankfurt-school.md)
    - But Horkheimer and Adorno focused on the negative effects:
      - Jay wondered about the potentially positive ones:
    - Paraphase: *What if I [decoupled](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/decoupling.md) [critique](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/critique.md) and [bigotry](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bigotry.md), in this specific way?*
      - Looked to [Walter Benjamin](../../../../glossary/terms/walter-benjamin.md).
      - Went along in that way study
    - Jay found quote from [Ludwig Wittgenstein](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ludwig-wittgenstein.md)'s Tractatus that labeled metaphysics as a "kind of magic."
- Prosser asks about nominalism: why now, and why magical.
  - Jay got especially fascinated with the history of nominalism when he realized it wasn't just an esoteric philosophical debate within realism, but central to larger, often very heated, disccussions about "the most fundamental and abiding of human concerns."
    - Our understanding of [God](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/god.md)
    - our [relationship](../../../../glossary/terms/relationship.md) to Him
    - our belief in the human ability to know, make sense of, and explain the workings of His creation.
    - our capacity to have aesthetic experiences with privileged instances of that creation
    - our hopes for improving an imperfect world and creating modes of living together that nuture human flourishing
  - Some concerns are theological, others philosophical, others are social or cultural
  - All are affected by nominalist critique of real universals
  - Blumenberg argues one big critique was the "upsurge of human self-assertion" that came in when "belief in a harmonious cosmos ruled by divinely mandated reason" faded.
    - In Horkheimer/Adorno's terms, this set up the hegemony of humans over the real, most practically domination of nature.
  - Now we're seeing the costs of that human-self assertion
  - Why now?
    - Living at the dawn of the [Anthropocene](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anthropocene.md) (if that's where we are) makes it imperative to reflect on the limits of the self-assertion abetted by the nominalist revolution.
      - ***emsenn***: That feels like a statement I agree with.
  - Jay says other attempts to restore faith in the real universal haven't convinced him:
    - [natural law theory](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/natural-law-theory.md) or [Neo-Scholasticism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/neo-scholasticism.md)
    - "do not succeed… in relieving the anxieties unleashed by the… ideological comfort… real universals once provided."
      - ***emsenn***: This surely ties into [neurotic intellectual](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/neurotic-intellectual.md) stuff.
  - Jay says nominalism can't be easily refuted
    - suggests there may be variants that are "less subject-centered, less intent on subssuming particulars under concepts, less complicit with the domination of nature"
      - ***emsenn***: does my relational process theory meet the criteria of a maginal nominalism?
    - that nominalism would be magical because it would "recover some of the awe-inspiring [mystery](../../../../glossary/terms/mystery.md) of our contact with the world prior to its being reduced… to a 'standing reserve' for technological purposes."
      - Okay, that sounds a lot like [Lakotaean mystery](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-mystery.md), at least when explained to a nominalist perspective.
- Prosser highlights the book's scope and outlines its movements, asking why the process described is important:
  - Jay presents nominalism (as [via moderna](../../../../glossary/terms/via-moderna.md)) displacing [Scholastic realism](../../../../glossary/terms/scholastic-realism.md) ([via antiqua](../../../../glossary/terms/via-antiqua.md)), foreshadowing [modern thinking](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/modern-thinking.md) and perhaps modernity
  - Jay highlights history of nominalism:
    - originating in theological disputes in the Middle Ages
    - occuring from stress betewen Hebraic and Greek components of Christian theology
      - As [Tertullian](../../../../glossary/terms/tertullian.md) put it, [Jerusalem](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jerusalem.md) over [Athens](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/athens.md)
    - God's will over the cosmic order previously ordained.
    - Ockham was a [Franciscan](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/franciscan.md)
      - instrumental in overthrowing [Aristotelian theology](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aristotelian-theology.md) associated with [Dominicanism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dominicanism.md), i.e. [Thomas Aquinas](../../../../glossary/terms/thomas-aquinas.md) and [Scholasticism](../../../../glossary/terms/scholasticism.md).
    - Privileging will over reason (according to Blumenberg, according to Jay) became the defining characteristic of human self-assertion
      - That term, human self-assertion, keeps coming up. I am very curious what it's formal meaning is. It seems somewhere between [individualism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/individualism.md) and [anthropocentricism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anthropocentricism.md).
      - conventional nominalism is "subjective imposing of conceptual order of bestowing of arbitrary names on a world of radical contingency"
    - this is where [kyriontoeidonic theory](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kyriontoeidonic-theory.md) might be relevant
    - math as nominalist, when applied to the world.
      - magical nominalism
    - rather than displace divine omnipotence/God's will onto humans
      - celebrate resistance to "subjective imposition in unique singularities that escape subsumption under the general rules of either [rationalist realism](../../../../glossary/terms/rationalist-realism.md) or [conventional nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conventional-nominalism.md).
    - magincal nominalism maps to [mystical nominalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mystical-nominalism.md) ([Ernst Bloch](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ernst-bloch.md)) and Jewish nominalism (Bielik-Robson), but Jay prefers magical:
      - challenges the foundational premise of political theology:
        - that religious idiom is substantively prior and thus superior
      - Jay wants to show that religion itself ([Jewish](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jewish-religion.md), [Christian](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/christian-religion.md), or [other](../../../../glossary/terms/religion.md)) "derives at least some of its original impulses from earlier human relations to the world, in which certain singularities-things, events, words-possess whatever causes a sense of wonder and awe in those who encounter thing."
        - religious and theological intermediates "preserve" a "transfigured form"

        -"when they are recovered in our own age, sparks of their earlier magical origins remain available for reignition."
- Prosser highlights that Jay situates [critical theory](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/critical-theory.md) within a larger [philosophical arc](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/philosophical-arc.md).
  - Prosser points to [Reason after Its Eclipse](../../../../glossary/terms/reason-after-its-eclipse.md) ([2016ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2016ce.md)) doing it in relation to [Immanuel Kant](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/immanuel-kant.md) and [Hegel](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/georg-wilhelm-friedrich-hegel.md), but *Magical Nominalism* looks past [Marxism](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxism.md).
  - Jay highlights that every time he returns to the history of critical theory, he finds new ways of [framing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/framing.md) its narrative, due to what Jay says Prosser was right to call different [throughlines](../../../../glossary/terms/throughline.md) (so I guess I'll make that a term).
  - Way back in [1984ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1984ce.md), in [Marxism and Totality](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxism-and-totality.md), Jay situated critical thought within the ["Western Marxist](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/western-marxist.md) struggle to defend a viable concept of totality, which concluded with \[\[id:44ef1f73-71ca-4a0c-89c7-75643116f115\]\[\[Jürgen\] Habermas\]\]'s decentered, non-expressive view of the social whole."
  - *Reason after Its Eclipse* began with Kant and Hegel, passed through Marx and [Georg Lukács](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/georg-lukacs.md), continued to Horkheimer's "defense of 'objective' reason against its 'instrumental debasement,'" and finished with Habermas's [communicative rationality](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/communicative-rationality.md).
  - In *Magical Nominalism*
    - No Habermas
      - Horkheimer and [Marcuse](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/herbert-marcuse.md) are cameos
      - [Benjamin](../../../../glossary/terms/walter-benjamin.md), [Adorno](../../../../glossary/terms/theodor-w-adorno.md), and [Siegfried Kracauer](../../../../glossary/terms/siegfried-kracauer.md) represent critical theory and the Frankfurt School
      - But a lot "protagonists of the story" come from Jay's work on [20th-century French interrogation of visuality](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/20th-century-french-interrogation-of-visuality.md), esp in the [post-structuralist](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/post-structuralist.md) chapters of the book.
  - Jay says magical nominalism can help make sense of an issue in critical theory:
    - dual nature of [concreteness](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concreteness.md) and [abstraction](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abstraction.md)
      - concreteness is [epistemological](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/epistemological.md) and [normative](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/normative.md) alternative to abstraction
      - abstraction is often identified with:
    - [real abstractions](../../../../glossary/terms/real-abstraction.md) ([Alfred Sohn-Rethel](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/alfred-sohn-rethel.md)) (hegemony of exchange relations in capitalism)
    - [conceptual abstraction](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conceptual-abstraction.md) ([identitarian thought](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/identitarian-thought.md))
    - Jay says that [dialectical reason](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectical-reason.md) understood concreteness in a specific way
      - Jay's lineage of [dialectic theory](../../../../glossary/terms/dialectic-theory.md):
    - [Hegel](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/georg-wilhelm-friedrich-hegel.md)
    - [Marxism](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxism.md)
    - Lukacs
    - [Frankfurt School](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/frankfurt-school.md)
    - [Herbert Marcuse](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/herbert-marcuse.md)'s [Reason and Revolution](../../../../glossary/terms/reason-and-revolution.md) is its "highpoint"
      - [dialectic concreteness](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectic-concreteness.md): "a '[moment](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/moment.md)' in a complexly mediated, dynamically developing whole"
      - [dialectic abstraction](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectic-abstraction.md): a thing extracted from its contexts.
    - classic example of abstraction in [Hegelian Marxism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hegelian-marxism.md):
      - [fetishism of commodities](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fetishism-of-commodities.md)
        - commodity:
          - [fungible thing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fungible-thing.md)
          - [origin](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/origin.md) in [capitalist labor process](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalist-labor-process.md) is [forgotten](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/forgetting.md) or [repressed](../../../../glossary/terms/repressing.md).
    - Jay says adherents of dialectic reason, and the practices it informed, would overcome that [fetishization](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fetishization.md) with [negation](../../../../glossary/terms/negation.md): undermine the [erasing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/erasing.md) of origin and thus false "solidity"
      - "Ultimately, at least for Hegel and Hegelian Marxism, that negativity would be sublated in a concrete totality that would overcome abstraction and end the alienation experienced in the labor process and elsewhere in ccapitalism."
  - Jay says this version of concreteness was an implicit premise of Frankfurt school, but there was another impulse he sensed early on
    - before [The Dialectical Imagination](../../../../glossary/terms/the-dialectical-imagination.md) ([1972ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1972ce.md))
    - Jay noticed a "reluctance to adopt the rhetoric of alienation and the restitution of holistic unity, which they attacked as inherently idealist rather than materialist"
      - Genuine materialism entailed the resistance of objects to their subjecetive constitution.
    - Implication:
    - always an excess:
      - a surplus beyond the reconciled, integrated whole
      - can never be fully recuperated
      - Jay understood one meaning of Adorno's negative dialectics was resisting the incorporation of different things into a totality.
      - Jay learned Walter Benjamin inspired this version of concreteness, which valorizes unassimilable singularity.
    - Benjamin's distrust of Hegelian dialectics meant he didn't prioritize the cconcept or accept history "as a progressive negation of negations culminating in a grand synthesis of the identical and non-identical."
      - Adorno was more like Hegel than Benjamin, but valued "non-conceptuality"
    - grounded in nominalist critiques of real universals.
    - Jay uses Adorno's attitude toward nominalism in music to highlight Adorno's criticism of conventional nominalism, while "tacitly embracing" magical nominalism.
