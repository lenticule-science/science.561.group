***Conceptual analysis*** is the [philosophical analysis](../../../../glossary/terms/philosophical-analysis.md) of a [concept](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-concept.md); finding the [description](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/description.md) or definition that is true for that concept: what is the [meaning](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meaning.md) that is [referenced](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/referencing.md) when a concept is reference?

Conceptual analysis has [utility](../../../../glossary/terms/utility.md) because [philosophy](../../../../glossary/terms/philosophy.md) often deals with [abstractions](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abstraction.md) and [ideas](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/idea.md): structures that aren't necessarily coherent or [legible](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/legiblity.md) without [describing](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/describing.md) them.

Conceptual analysis essentially involves [examining](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/examining.md) a concept's [relationships](../../../../glossary/terms/relationship.md) to other concepts. There are different [modes of conceptual analysis](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mode-of-conceptual-analysis.md):

- [two-dimensionalist conceptual analysis](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/two-dimensionalist-conceptual-analysis.md)
