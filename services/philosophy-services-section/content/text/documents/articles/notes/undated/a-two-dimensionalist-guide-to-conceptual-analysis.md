***A Two-Dimensionalist Guide to Conceptual Analysis*** is a [book](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/book.md) by [Jens Kipper](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jens-kipper.md), [published](../../../../glossary/terms/publishing.md) as volume 25 of [Epistemic Studies](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/epistemic-studies.md) ([2012](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2012ce.md)). It presents an [analytical framework](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/analytical-framework.md) for [conceptual analysis](conceptual-analysis.md) [developed](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/developing.md) from [two-dimensionalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/two-dimensionalism.md), a [semantic framework](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semantic-framework.md) that [differentiates](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/differentiating.md) two classes of [meaning](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meaning.md) [induced](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inducing.md) by [linguistic communication](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/linguistic-communication.md): primary intension (descriptive reference to a priori epistemic possibilities) and secondary intension (defined reference to a posteriori metaphysical possibilities).

Kipper's central thesis is that [two-dimensional semantic theory](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/two-dimensional-semantic-theory.md) provides a [methodology](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/methodology.md) for [conceptual analysis](conceptual-analysis.md) across [epistemic](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/epistemic-mode.md) and [metaphysical modes](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/metaphysical-mode.md).

By distinguishing intension into two functional classes, Kipper argues that conceptual analysis can be metaphysically robust and descriptively adequate:

primary intensions  
functions from possible worlds considered as factual scenarios.

secondary intensions  
functions from worlds considered as counterfactual scenarios.

The book applies the theory to canonical philosophical problems, including [necessity](../../../../glossary/terms/necessity.md), [referencing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/referencing.md), and [analyticity](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/analyticity.md). For example, Kipper looks at [natural kind terms](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/natural-kind-term.md), and how their primary intensions [encode](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/encoding.md) [description](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/description.md), while the secondary intensions mapping [metaphysical structure](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/metaphysical-structure.md). "Water is a clear, drinkable liquid in lakes and rivers," is a primary intension, while "water is H2O," is a secondary intension.

<div class="aside">

This is incredibly similar to the common-practice in [LPMUDs](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lpmud.md) of rooms having hand-written descriptions, and often only hand-written descriptions, with [metaphysicality](../../../../glossary/terms/metaphysicality.md) being introduced through adding optional attributes, i.e. inventories.

</div>

Kipper also establishes a typology of roles in conceptual analysis:

epistemic profile  
set of conditions under which a concept is applicable

metaphysical anchor  
entity or property that satisfy those conditions in the actual world.

Sounds like my [relational structure](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-structure.md) and [relational instantiation](../../../../glossary/terms/relational-instantiation.md), as much as my metaphysics has [entities](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/metaphysical-entity.md).

Kipper emphasizes the utility of two-dimensionalist conceptual analysis, especially in contexts where [intuition](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intuition.md) about possibility and [necessity](../../../../glossary/terms/necessity.md) diverge.
