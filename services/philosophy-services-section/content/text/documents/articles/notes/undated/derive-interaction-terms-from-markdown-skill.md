# Derive Interaction Terms From Markdown

- Treat headings as [interaction term](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/interactive-semioverse-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interaction-term.md) constructors with their subsection content.
- Convert links into terms that reference other [handles](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/domains/universes/semiotic-universe/domains/interactive-semioverse/terms/handle.md) by target path.
- Map inline math and definitions into semantic generator terms.
- Keep the [interaction surface](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/interactive-semioverse-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interaction-surface.md) finite by selecting the most salient terms.
