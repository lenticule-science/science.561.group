So back in the [1830s](../../../../glossary/terms/1830s.md), [Auguste Comte](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/auguste-comte.md), a [French philosopher](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/french-philosopher.md), proposed a [hierarchy of the sciences](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hierarchy-of-the-sciences.md). This was part of a wider idea of his, [positivism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/positivism.md), which suggested that [human knowledge](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/human-knowledge.md) progressed from theological to [metaphysical](../../../../glossary/terms/metaphysical.md) to [physical](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physical.md). In other terms, from [abstract](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abstract.md) to [concrete](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concrete.md). Thus, [mathematics](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mathematics.md) was the foundation, with [astronomy](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/astronomy.md) coming next, then [physics](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physics.md), then [chemistry](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/chemistry.md), [biology](../../../../glossary/terms/biology.md), and finally [sociology](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sociology.md), a term Comte [coined](../../../../glossary/terms/coining-a-term.md) himself.[^1][^2]

Comte called sociology the "queen of sciences," if I remember, because it was the "crown" on top of all of them, that brought humans, the doers of science, into alignment with mathematics, the foundations of science.

In the nearly-200 years since Comte's work, there's been a lot of thought on how to frame sciences. Comte's [positivism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/positivism.md) and [reductionism](../../../../glossary/terms/reductionism.md) hasn't ever left, but the way it's been handled don't (usually) operate from the same ecclesiastical position, so have in their own way refuted the central claim, that sciences are cleanly founded upon each other.

By the late [19th Century](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/19th-century.md), [knowledge production](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowledge-production.md) had [professionalized](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/professionalizing.md), with [universities](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/university.md) and academic journals, which grouped sciences based on what they studied: [formal](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/formal-science.md), [natural](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/natural-science.md), or [social](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-science.md). In [1938ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1938ce.md), the [International Encyclopedia of Unified Science](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/international-encyclopedia-of-unified-science.md) was published, and by [1948ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1948ce.md), the [Hempel-Oppenheim model](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hempel-oppenheim-model.md) attempted to codify the relations between sciences.

In [1961ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1961ce.md), [Ernest Nagel](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ernest-nagel.md) published [The Structure of Science](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/the-structure-of-science.md), which further solidified the idea of [intertheoretic reduction](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intertheoretic-reduction.md) via [intertheoretic bridge laws](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intertheoretic-bridge-law.md).

Then, the [next year](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1962ce.md), [Herbert Simon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/herbert-simon.md) [published](../../../../glossary/terms/publishing.md) The Architecture of Complexity, which used cybernetics to soften the claim, pointing to different levels of complexity having different [principles](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/principle.md).

The same year, [Thomas Kuhn](../../../../glossary/terms/thomas-kuhn.md), with [Structure](../../../../glossary/terms/structure-book.md), further undercut the idea of a unified hierarchy of sciences, looking at how some sciences had [incommmensurable paradigms](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/incommmensurable-paradigm.md). And in [1970ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1970ce.md), [Donald Davidson](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/donald-davidson.md), with [Mental Events](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mental-events.md), proposed [anomalous monism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anomalous-monism.md): [mental events](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cognitive-operation.md) [supervene](../../../../glossary/terms/supervening.md) on physical events, but are not reducible themselves to [physical laws](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physical-law.md).

Then in [1972ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1972ce.md), [Philip Anderson](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/philip-anderson.md) used physics itself to change intertheoretic reduction, by highlighting [emergence](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emergence.md) that produces novel [concepts](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-concept.md) and [laws](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/law.md) at higher levels that cannot be derived from [microphysical laws](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/microphysical-law.md).

[Jerry Fodor](../../../../glossary/terms/jerry-fodor.md) generalized this non-reductionist stance in [1974ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1974ce.md) with [Special Sciences](../../../../glossary/terms/special-sciences.md), claiming [multiple realizability](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/multiple-realizability.md) precludes reducting "special sciences" lilke [psychology](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychology.md) and [sociology](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sociology.md) to [physics](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/physics.md).

[Paul Feyerabend](../../../../glossary/terms/paul-feyerabend.md), the [next year](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1975.md), in [Against Method](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/against-method.md), argues fro [methodological pluralism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/methodological-pluralism.md), establishing what [non-reductionist scientific practice](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/non-reductionist-scientific-practice.md) might look like.

[Jaegwon Kim](../../../../glossary/terms/jaegwon-kim.md) took these ideas and voiced a necessary concern, the [causal exclusion problem](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/causal-exclusion-problem.md): if higher-level sciencees aren't reducible, are they causally impotent?

So, causal exclusion. Imagine we're working with two levels of properties, the physical (neurons firing, molecules colliding, etc.) and mental (beliefs, desires, etc.)

Davidson and Fodor said that mental supervenes on physical - no mental change without physical change. But… they also claimed that mental changes are causal. For example, seeing rain outside is physical. But it's your belief that it's raining that makes you grab a jacket.

The physical stuff has proper "[causal closure](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/causal-closure.md)," and mental stuff depends on the physical. This leads to an issue of [overdetermination](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/overdetermination.md): under Davidson/Fodor modeling, deciding to grab an umbrella has two full and independent causes, physical and mental.

Which would mean the mental isn't actually doing any causal work - it's [epiphenomenal](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/epiphenomenal.md): along for the ride, but not making anything happen.

This issue creates an impasse within [irreductive physicalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/irreductive-physicalism.md): mental causes are real but not reducible, which is unstable: either the mental can be reduced to the physical (so it has real causal power) or the mental is a impotent byproduct.

Essentially, higher-level causes seem excluded by the sufficiency of their physical bases. Put another way, the causality described by things like sociology or psychology is excluded by that causality's own supervenience on physical causality.

Or, put another way, irreducible supervenience causes epiphenomenalism. It forces philosophers to choose reduction or explain away supervenience.

This critique sparked a few directions. Reductionism accepts Kim's critique as a conclusion: higher-level causes are nothing over and above physical causes.

[Emergentism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emergentism.md) argues that higher levels can have novel cause powers that are irreducible and not supervenient.

[Interventionism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interventionism.md) argues that causation should be understood as an explanatory practice. That is, higher-level causes are real because they allow us to track regularities that may be, themselves, manipulable, even if they are underpinned physically.

And [pluralism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pluralism.md) says causality is a context-dependent story, so the exclusion problem is precluded.

These views were explored through the second-half of the [20th Century](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/20th-century.md): [Bruno Latour](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bruno-latour.md) intorduced [actor-network theory](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/actor-network-theory.md), [John Dupré](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/john-dupre.md) advanced [promiscuous realism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/promiscuous-realism.md), and [Nancy Cartwright](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nancy-cartwright.md) wrote [The Dappled World](../../../../glossary/terms/the-dappled-world.md), which framed the [cosmos](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cosmos.md) as a mosiac of local orders, not governed by universal laws.

Early in the [21st Century](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/21st-century.md), the understanding of sciences took a [mechanistic turn](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mechanistic-turn.md). The philosophizing of this probably peaked in [2007ce](../../../../glossary/terms/2007.md), with [Carl Craver](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/carl-craver.md) writing Explaining the Brain, and [Every Thing Must Go](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/every-thing-must-go.md) from [James Ladyman](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/james-ladyman.md) and [Don Ross](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/don-ross.md).

Basically, the understanding became that the sciences were unified not through a hierarchy of causality, but at the level of mathematical structure, which was manifest in the mechanisms present in each field.
