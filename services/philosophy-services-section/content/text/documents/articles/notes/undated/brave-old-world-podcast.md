# Original Copy

Brave Old World is a make-believe world world that my friends [hang out](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hanging-out.md) in while I serve as the narrator. We use [numbers](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/number.md) and [dice rolls](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rolling-dice.md) to help [make decisions](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/decision-making.md) but mostly it’s up to our [collective imagination](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/collective-imagination.md). Put another way, we’re a [Dungeons & Dragons podcast](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dungeons-dragons-actual-play-podcast.md).

Whenever we play, we [stream](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/livestreaming.md) the session live on [Twitch](../../../../glossary/terms/twitch.md). Those get edited and get released as a podcast.

Brave Old World is like one of those old-time [radio plays](../../../../glossary/terms/radio-play.md), only we use chance to decide where we’re going, instead of a script. Six of my friends are cast as the heroes, and I’m playing the extras.

## The story so far

The Brave Old World used to be a land of magic and wonder. Humanity was entering a [golden age](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/golden-age.md) after discovering a [Eastland](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/eastland.md) populated with elves and halflings. Their wars with the dwarves and orcs of the Old World ended.

swords-shield-bow80 years ago, magic vanished in what would be called the Collapse. The dwarves disappeared and the elves became feral. Humanity was alone and cascaded into war.

wrench-scales-briefcaseWar and wealth created the spark of an industrial revolution. Industrialization brought bureaucracy and a new peace, held by debt and credit. Humanity successfully coped with the Collapse.
