## What reflexive equilibrium is

Reflexive equilibrium is the condition achieved when a relational
structure maintains the same form across infinite reflexive layers.
When a recognition is iterated through self-application —
a, S(a), S²(a), S³(a), ... — it either diverges, oscillates, or
converges. Reflexive equilibrium is convergence: the point at which
further self-application produces no new structure.

This is the relational concept underlying the formal notion of a
fixed point: Close(a) = a means that a has achieved reflexive
equilibrium — iterating the closure operator produces nothing beyond a.

## Draft 0's formalization

Draft 0 (file 02) provides the most detailed analysis of reflexive
equilibrium, identifying several criteria:

**Asymptotic equilibrium**: The form of a structure approaches a
limit as reflexive depth increases. The limit exists even if no
finite depth achieves it exactly.

**Uniqueness**: If reflexive equilibrium exists, it is unique — there
is only one form that is active under infinite self-application. This
follows from the monotonicity of the iteration: each S^n(a) ≤ S^(n+1)(a),
and the join of an ascending chain is unique.

**Depth cutoff**: In practice, equilibrium may be treated as achieved
when further iteration produces no detectable change within a given
resolution. Draft 0 notes that this is an approximation, not the true
equilibrium — the formal definition requires the limit of the infinite
sequence.

**Total reflexive closure**: Everything fully embedded — every
substructure has achieved reflexive equilibrium with every other
substructure it relates to.

coherence." This is reflexive equilibrium restated as a philosophical
condition: the structure holds together through its own activity.

## Relationship to other concepts

- **Closing**: The formal operator that computes reflexive equilibrium:
  Close(a) = Join over n of Iterate^n(a)
- **Coherence**: The philosophical name for the condition of being in
  reflexive equilibrium
- **Self-grounding**: The relational field's reflexive equilibrium with
  its own generative process
- **Balance**: The compatibility of closing (inward equilibrium) and
  opening (outward equilibrium) — reflexive equilibrium in both
  directions simultaneously
- **Alignment**: The preliminary step — bringing structures to
  compatible depth before testing whether equilibrium holds
- **Irreducible asymmetry**: What prevents GLOBAL reflexive equilibrium
  — each local equilibrium produces conditions that are not in
  equilibrium at the next level
