## Observations

1. **Draft 1's vocabulary is the most philosophical** — it names phenomena rather than operators. "Including" is an act, "inclusion" is a condition, "coherence" is a structure. This matches the triadic engine directly.

2. **PRE's vocabulary is the most complete** — it names every operator and gives each a signature, symbol, and Tarski truth condition. 31+ operators defined axiomatically.

3. **The treatise's vocabulary is the most architecturally layered** — it builds four levels of structure (RelationalStructure → RelationalLanguage → RelationalGeometry → RelationalPhysics) and introduces the Discipline/Regime/Filter/Profile hierarchy for constraining and specializing the field. It also uniquely names Reflex as distinct from Differentiate.

4. **Draft 4's vocabulary is the most formal-system-oriented** — it uses S-expressions (RES syntax), positions everything within RITM, and decomposes Flow into RD/RT. It is closest to an implementation specification.

5. **Draft 11's vocabulary is the most operational** — it names constructors for a generative closure algorithm. Many of its terms (Trace, Profile, Harmonic, Spectral) don't appear in other drafts because they're implementation-level.

6. **Draft 12's vocabulary is the most mathematical** — it uses standard category-theoretic notation (∧, ∨, ¬, ⊣) alongside relational names (Differentiate, Induce, Balance).

7. **The core derivation sequence is stable**: Relationlessness → Reflex → Differentiate → Includes/Excludes → Together/Either/Negate → Induces → Iterate → Close → Open → Balance → Necessary/Possible → Flow → Cohesion → Physics. This sequence appears in all declared required drafts with varying levels of detail and different names. The treatise and draft 4 clarify the opening: Reflex is the engine, Differentiate names its product.

8. **The treatise/draft 4 era contributes unique architectural concepts** — Discipline/Regime/Filter/Profile, Dynamic Residuation, the four-part framework, and the explicit separation of Reflex from Differentiate. These concepts are not clearly present in PRE, Foundations, draft 11, or draft 12, and may represent either a parallel development or a layer that the later drafts absorbed without naming.

9. **Draft 0 and 7 contribute a phenomenological layer** between genesis and formal operators — tension, relating (as distinct from include/exclude), difference, relative, coherence. These are philosophical concepts that name what is happening between the acts and the operators. PRE skips this layer.

10. **Draft 2 constructs classical (Boolean) logic**, while the mature framework insists on Heyting (non-Boolean) logic. This may represent an earlier framework refined into the algebraic approach, or may show that classical logic IS derivable but is WEAKER than what the framework actually earns.

11. **Draft 9 gives the most granular opening sequence** — 17+ named steps from Reflex to TraceStep. Each step resolves one indeterminacy and reveals another. This matches the "it cannot not derive" pattern but with much finer resolution than any other draft.

12. **Draft 10 is the most citation-heavy** — every construct is shown equivalent to an established theorem with specific references (Johnstone, Lurie, Street, Riehl-Verity, Shulman, Lawvere, Cisinski, Ayala-Francis). This is the version closest to a publishable mathematical paper.

13. **Draft 7's Cubical Agda formalization** is unique — it represents MinimalCohesion as a Higher Inductive Type with point, include (path), and coherence (2-path). The coherence constructor gives the type non-trivial homotopy structure, which is philosophically significant: coherence is not just holding things together but adds genuine higher-dimensional structure.

14. **Draft 0 contributes a pre-algebraic dynamics layer** that is richer than previously recognized. The four-domain progression (Relating → Propagation → Alignment → Cross-Dynamics) names relational phenomena that the algebraic framework later absorbs: propagation becomes Flow, alignment becomes the cohesive chain, cross-equilibrium becomes the self-grounding property. Draft 0 also uniquely names irreducible asymmetry, the recursive dynamic preventing final closure — a concept that pervades the mature framework without being explicitly named.

15. **Draft 1's granular derivation is a 37-step expansion** of the 15-operator canonical sequence. Each canonical operator compresses multiple granular steps: Differentiating absorbs Match/Compare/Orient/Differentiate; Closing absorbs Return/Retract/Strain; Balancing absorbs Modulate/Sustain; etc. The compression loses the philosophical justification for each step but gains formal clarity. Both levels of description are needed for a complete presentation.

16. **Draft 6 provides the geometric interpretation** of the self-grounding property: the ambidextrous equivalence hlim ℱ ≃ hcolim ℱ. This is the formal statement that construction (building upward by colimits) and observation (reconstructing by limits) are equivalent — being and knowing are homotopy-dual. This is the most sophisticated mathematical expression of the framework's central philosophical claim.

17. **Draft 1's meta-relational ascent** (Relational Derivatives.md) extends the granular derivation beyond Reset to ~61 steps, culminating in MetaRelate → Fix → Elevate → Extend → Refer → Name → Abstract → Reinstantiate. This sequence shows how the relational field earns the capacity for REFERENCE (relating to its own fixed structure), NAMING (identifiable reference to relational action), and ABSTRACTION (extracting behavior from identity). These are not metalinguistic additions — they are compelled by the system's need to relate to its own completed closure. The meta-relational ascent is the granular-level evidence for the self-grounding property.

18. **Draft 1's program calculus** (Untitled 10.md and oldDeriving Relationlessness.md) demonstrates that computation is EARNED, not imported. Program/Run/Branch/Recurse/Accumulate/Filter/Map/Link/Chain — the complete substrate of sequential, conditional, recursive, and compositional computation — arises from the same "it cannot not derive" pattern. The Module/Layer/TraceField architecture then organizes all these derived structures into a self-describing layered field, anticipating the mature framework's four-part organization.

19. **Draft 6 uses a cohesive TRIPLE** (Σ ⊣ □ ⊣ Π) rather than RITM's QUADRUPLE (Shape ⊣ Flat ⊣ Sharp ⊣ Crisp). The draft 6 treatise defines an adjoint triple on directed ∞-categories with an Exit ∞-functor governing transitions between strata. This represents either an earlier formulation refined into the quadruple, or a genuinely different geometric realization of the same relational dynamics. The concordance does not resolve this tension.
