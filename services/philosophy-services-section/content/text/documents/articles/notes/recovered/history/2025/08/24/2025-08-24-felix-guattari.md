***Félix Guattari*** ([1930](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1930ce.md)-[1992](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1992ce.md)) was a [French](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/french-person.md) [psychoanalyst](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychoanalyst.md), [philospher](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/philospher.md), and [political activist](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/political-activist.md). He is best known for [collaborating](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/25/2025-08-25-collaborating.md) with [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) to [author](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring--002211.md) [Anti-Oedipus](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-oedipus.md) ([1972](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1972ce.md)) and [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md) ([1980](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1980ce.md)), which form the two volumes of [Capitalism and Schizophrenia](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalism-and-schizophrenia.md). These texts challenged traditional [psychoanalysis](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychoanalysis.md), [Marxism](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxism.md), and [structuralism](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/structuralism.md), introducing concepts like [schizoanalysis](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/schizoanalysis.md), [deterritorialization](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/deterritorialization.md), and [assemblage theory](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agencement.md).

**Félix Guattari** [trained](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/training.md) under [Jacques Lacan](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jacques-lacan.md) and worked at [La Borde psychiatric clinic](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/la-borde-psychiatric-clinic.md).
