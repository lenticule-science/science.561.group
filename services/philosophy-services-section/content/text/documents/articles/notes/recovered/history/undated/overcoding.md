***Overcoding*** is a term for a process by which systems [erase](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/erasing.md) or [invisibilize](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/invisibilizing.md) [difference](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/difference.md). [Coined](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/coining-a-term.md) in [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md).

Interestingly, [my files](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsenn-s-files.md) may be a form of overcoding, done for [sabotaging](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sabotaging.md) [liberal ideology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-ideology.md).
