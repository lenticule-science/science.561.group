***My life***, or the [life](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/life.md) of [emsenn](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/triage/emsenn/index.org.md), is the [result](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/result.md) of me [living](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/living.md).

Depending on [perspective](../../../../../../glossary/terms/perspective.md), it may be seen as the [timeline](../../../../../../glossary/terms/timeline.md), the [sequence](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sequence.md) of [moments](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/moment.md) that [occured](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/occuring.md) [involved](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/involving.md) me between my birth and [my death](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/06/2025-09-06-my-death.md).
