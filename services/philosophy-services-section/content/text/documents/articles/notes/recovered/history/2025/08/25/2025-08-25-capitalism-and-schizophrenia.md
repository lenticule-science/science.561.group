***Capitalism and Schizophrenia*** is a [text](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md) [authored](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring--002211.md) by [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) and [Félix Guattari](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-felix-guattari.md), having two volumes: [Anti-Oedipus](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-oedipus.md) and [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md).

Together, they [reject](../../../../../../glossary/terms/rejecting.md) [Freudian psychoanalysis](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/freudian-psychoanalysis.md) and [establish](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/establishing.md) [schizoanalysis](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/schizoanalysis.md); [methodologies of subjectivity analysis](../../../../../../glossary/terms/methodologies-of-subjectivity-analysis--002490.md)
