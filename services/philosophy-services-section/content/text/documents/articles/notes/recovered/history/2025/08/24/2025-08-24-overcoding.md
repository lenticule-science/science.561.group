***Overcoding*** is a term for a [process](../../../../../../../../services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/process.md) by which [systems](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/system.md) [erase](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/erasing.md) or [invisibilize](2025-08-24-invisibilizing.md) [difference](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/difference.md). [Coined](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-coining-a-term.md) in [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md).

Interestingly, [my files](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsenn-s-files.md) may be a form of overcoding, done for [sabotaging](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sabotaging.md) [liberal ideology](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-ideology.md).
