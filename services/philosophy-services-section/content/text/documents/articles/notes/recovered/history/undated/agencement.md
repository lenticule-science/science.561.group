***Agencement*** is the the [relational process](../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-process.md) that [results](../../../../glossary/terms/resulting.md) in an [assemblage](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/assemblage.md), a [dynamic network](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dynamic-network.md) of [heterogeneous elements](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/heterogeneous-element.md) whose relations to each other are [external](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/external.md) to their own [form](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/form.md).
