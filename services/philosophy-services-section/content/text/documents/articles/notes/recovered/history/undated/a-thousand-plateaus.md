***A Thousand Plateaus*** is a text [authored](../../../../glossary/terms/authoring.md) by [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) and [Félix Guattari](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/felix-guattari.md), [published](../../../../glossary/terms/publishing.md) in [1980](../../../../../../../history-and-archaeology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-1980.md).

In it, they [coined a term](../../../../glossary/terms/coining-a-term.md), [overcoding](../../../../glossary/terms/overcoding.md).
