***A Thousand Plateaus*** is a [text](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md) [authored](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring--002211.md) by [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) and [Félix Guattari](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-felix-guattari.md), [published](../../../../../../glossary/terms/publishing.md) in [1980](../../../../../../../../../history-and-archaeology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-1980.md).

In it, they [coined a term](../../../../../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-coining-a-term.md), [overcoding](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-overcoding.md).
