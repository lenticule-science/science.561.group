# Hot Dishes

## Mary Jane's Veal Stroganoff

1.  1 1/2 lbs veal in 1-4 inch stripes
2.  Juice of one lemon
3.  4 tbs. butter
4.  1 onion chopped fine
5.  1 cup sliced mushroom
6.  1 tsp. salt
7.  dash of pepper
8.  1 cup sour cream
9.  flour
10. stuffed olives

Cover veal with lemon juice. melt 2 tbs. butter in large heavy skillet. Add onion and mushroom, cook for about 5 minutes or until onion is golden. Remove onion and mushroom from pan. Toss veal in enough flour to cover it on all sides. Stir 2 tbs. butter into skillet. When it bubbles add the veal, salt and pepper. Cook quickly until veal is a lovely, rich brown. Turn down heat, add onions and mushroom. Stir in sour cream and heat slowly. Garnish with stuffed olives. A fine, tasty meal along with string beans and broiled tomatoes. Serves 4.

**Mrs. Alexander E. Ostoff VA 5-7656**
