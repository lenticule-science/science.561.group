Concrescence (from the Latin *concrescere*, to grow together) is [Alfred North Whitehead](../../../../general/people/alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s term for the process by which an [actual occasion](./actual-occasion.md) constitutes itself as a determinate unity from the multiplicity of its [prehensions](./prehension.md). It is the internal process of becoming: the occasion begins as an open potentiality defined by its initial data (the prehended past) and its subjective aim (a lure toward a particular form of integration), and it concludes by achieving a definite, novel unity — its "satisfaction."

Concrescence has a temporal structure but is not in physical time. The occasion does not persist through time while concrescing; rather, the concrescence is the occasion's entire existence as a subject. Once satisfaction is achieved, the occasion perishes as a subject and becomes an object — a datum for future occasions to prehend. Physical time is the serial order of completed concrescences, not a container in which they occur.

The process involves phases of integration. In the initial phase, physical [prehensions](./prehension.md) of past occasions provide the raw data. In supplementary phases, conceptual [prehensions](./prehension.md) introduce [eternal objects](./eternal-object.md) (possibilities) that shape how the data is valued and organized. The subjective aim — initially derived from the primordial nature of God in [Whitehead](../../../../general/people/alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s system, though the concept can be secularized — guides the integration toward a particular form of definiteness. The result is a novel entity that is more than the sum of its data: it is a creative synthesis.

For this research program, concrescence models what the [closure operators](../../../../mathematics/concepts/closure-operator/index.md) of the [semiotic](../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) universe formalize: the achievement of a determinate [semiotic](../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) structure from the open field of possible interpretations. The semantic, syntactic, and fusion closures of the [semiotic universe](../../../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md) are, in process-philosophical terms, the formal structure of concrescence applied to [signs](../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/sign.md).
