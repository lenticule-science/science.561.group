- [[definition]]:: ***Bind*** is the [[act]] of [[induce|inducing]] a [[relation]] between two [[reflexive]] relations, forming a bound relational structure.

## Derivation
- [[postulate]]:: As [[reflexive]] structures emerge from the [[domain of relationality]], it must be derivable that two reflexive structures can be [[induce|induced]] into an ordered coupling.
- [[derivation]]:: Deriving two [[reflexive]] relations together [[induce|induces]] a new composite relation where each relation is structurally ordered relative to the other, forming a [[binding]].
- [[relational expression]]:: 
  - bind(a, b) is [[reflect]](2, reflex(a), reflex(b))
