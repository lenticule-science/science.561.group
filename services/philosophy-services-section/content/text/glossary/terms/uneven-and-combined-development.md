Uneven and combined development names a historical pattern in which
different developmental tempos coexist and interpenetrate, producing
hybrid social forms rather than linear stage succession
[@trotsky-hrr-1930].

In contemporary theory, it functions as a general historical-dialectical
category for relational development under asymmetry
[@rosenberg-ucd-2013].
