An ***ontological process*** is a process that [does](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/doing.md) [ontologizing](ontologizing.md). An ontological process is a form of [conceptual analysis](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/conceptual-analysis.md) where the [concept](../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-concept.md) being [analyzed](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/analyzing.md) is related to [being](being.md) or [knowing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowing.md) about being.
