The [[collapse induction function]] (κ) [[induce|induces]] its own [[domain]] through [[self-negating constraint]] caused by [[relationlessness]] requiring [[relation]] to relation itself.

By using the [[relational admissibility predicate]] on relationlessness, a [[collapse]] (κ) [[emerge|emerges]].


> κ := R(¬R, ¬R)

This [[expression]] [[evaluae|evaluates]] whether two [[instances]] of relationlessness
can be admitted into relation. The result is inadmissible under
[[relational admissibility]], as it re-invokes the condition it negates.
