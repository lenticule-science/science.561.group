***Heraclitus*** was a [Proto-Classical Greek philosopher](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/proto-classical-greek-philosopher.md) who [posited](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postulating.md) that [change](change.md) in [existence](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/existence.md) happened through [logos](../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/logos.md). His [philosophizing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/philosophizing.md) who followed [Anaximander](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anaximander.md) and preceded [Pythagoras](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pythagoras.md).
