Nonidentity names the mismatch between concept and object: objects are
never exhausted by the concepts that classify them. In Adorno's negative
dialectics, this is not a minor epistemic inconvenience but a structural
condition for critique [@adorno-sep-2025].

Nonidentity blocks premature reconciliation. It keeps thought attentive
to what identity-thinking suppresses: particularity, remainder, and the
historical violence embedded in forced conceptual sameness.
