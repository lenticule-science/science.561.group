- [[define]]:: A [[condition]] is the immediate outcome of an [[act]]. It is what is brought about, held open transiently, by the [[act]] that produces it. Every [[structure]] holds a [[condition]] durably; every [[relation]] requires at least one [[condition]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] shows that every [[act]] necessarily brings about a [[condition]].
    - For example, the [[act]] of [[include|including]] brings about the [[condition]] of [[inclusion]];
    - The [[act]] of [[exclude|excluding]] brings about the [[condition]] of [[exclusion]].

- A [[condition]] is distinct from the [[act]] that produces it.
    - An [[act]] is what **does**; a [[condition]] is what **is held open** by that doing.
    - The [[condition]] only persists **as long as the [[act]] is active**, unless stabilized by a [[structure]].

- [[condition|Conditions]] are necessary because without them:
    - The effects of [[act|acts]] would have no persistence, even transiently;
    - No differentiation between states could be identified or held open, even momentarily.

- In the derivation of [[exist|existing]]:
    - [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]] are the [[condition|conditions]]nditions]] that arise from the primitive [[act|acts]] of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]];
    - [[tension]] is the [[condition]] that holds [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]] in mutual opposition;
    - [[difference]] is the [[condition]] induced by that [[tension]], marking a [[triage/thing]] as set apart from what is not;
    - [[relation]] is the [[condition]] that specifies the binding of the [[triage/thing]] to its ongoing [[exist|existing]] via [[relate|relating]].

- Thus, [[condition|conditions]]:
    - Arise **immediately** from [[act|acts]];
    - Are **maintained** transiently by those acts;
    - Require [[structure|structures]] to achieve durability and coherence.

## Implications

- No [[condition]] can arise independently of an [[act]].
- No [[structure]] can exist without holding at least one [[condition]] durably.
- [[relational dynamics]] depend on a layered interaction:  
    - [[act|acts]] bring about [[condition|conditions]];  
    - [[structure|structures]] hold those [[condition|conditions]] open durably, forming the basis for further [[relation|relations]].
- The minimal architecture of [[exist|existing]] necessarily includes multiple [[condition|conditions]]: [[inclusion]], [[exclusion]], [[tension]], [[difference]], and [[relation]].
