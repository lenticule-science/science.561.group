Immanent critique is criticism from within: it evaluates practices,
institutions, or theories using norms they themselves claim to uphold.
In critical theory, this method is central because it links diagnosis to
historically situated contradictions rather than external ideal models
[@critical-theory-sep-2023].

Immanent critique differs from merely internal critique. It is not only
consistency checking; it is transformative analysis of crisis-tendencies
within a form of life.
