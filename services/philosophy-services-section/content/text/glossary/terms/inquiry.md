**Inquiry** is the directed activity of moving claims from [absurd](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/absurd.md) toward [determination](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/determination.md) through accumulating [witnessings](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/witnessing.md). It is the broader frame within which the framework's other concepts — [relating](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relating.md), witnessing, [settlement](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settlement.md) — all do their work. Where relating is the primitive activity and witnessing is its evidentiary unit, inquiry is the trajectory: a directed motion through the framework's joint space of evidence.

Inquiry is not a method, a procedure, or a discipline. The framework's term is structural: inquiry is *what is happening* whenever claims are being moved toward determination. Empirical research, philosophical argument, mathematical proof, ordinary perception, even the habitual practices that produce settled stances about the world — all are inquiries in the framework's sense, distinguishable not by content but by the kinds of witnessings they accumulate.

## The trajectory: absurd → possible → necessary → determined

Inquiry has a characteristic shape. A claim begins [absurd](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/absurd.md): held only on its own assertion, with no relational structure constraining it. Inquiry's first moves make the claim [possible](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/possible.md): some witnessings have begun to accrue, opening up the relational structure within which the claim could be true. Continued inquiry tightens this into [necessity](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/necessary.md): the relations have become structured enough to *force* the claim's shape. The trajectory's terminus, locally, is [determination](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/determination.md): the relations are tight enough that the claim is settled, and adding more witnessings does not change what it says.

This four-mode trajectory (absurd → possible → necessary → determined) is one reading of the framework's structure, the modal one. It is parallel to the four readings of the [bilattice](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bilattice.md), which describe *positions* rather than *trajectory states*: the trajectory describes how a claim moves, a reading where it sits at a given moment. A claim's trajectory is the sequence of readings it holds as inquiry proceeds, and its local terminus is a [settlement](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settlement.md), the reading further closure no longer changes.

## Inquiry is local and revisable

Inquiry's progress is not toward a single global terminus. Different starting [absurdities](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/absurd.md) — different sets of a priori claims that the inquiry takes as given — produce different trajectories and different determinations. The framework treats this as honest description, not as a defect to be patched out by additional axioms. There is no view from nowhere; there is only the view from this configuration of accepted absurdities.

A claim that has been determined locally can become unsettled if new relations are introduced. Inquiry does not foreclose its own continuation: the structural stability of a determination is local under the witnessings the inquiry has so far performed. New witnessings produce new positions; new positions may be more, less, or differently determined.

## Inquiry as the framework's account of knowledge

The relationality framework's account of *knowledge* is its account of inquiry. To know a claim is for the claim to have been determined within an inquiry whose absurdities one accepts; to know more is for the inquiry to have proceeded further along its trajectory; to know wrongly is for the inquiry to have determined a claim that, under additional witnessings, becomes unsettled. There is no separate epistemology bolted onto the ontology; the structure of [relating](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relating.md) *is* the structure of how knowledge is produced.

This collapses a distinction that is load-bearing in much of Western philosophy — between what *is* and what *is known*. The relationality framework does not need the distinction because what *is* (in the framework) is whatever has been structurally settled by inquiry, and what is *known* (in the framework) is the same thing. Substance metaphysics keeps the two apart by positing a substrate that exists independently of being known; relational ontology has no such substrate, and inquiry both produces and tracks what there is to know.
