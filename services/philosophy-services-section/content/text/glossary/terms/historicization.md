Historicization is the method of treating an object as historically
produced rather than timelessly given.

To historicize a concept is to ask when it emerged, through which
conflicts or practices it stabilized, and how its meaning shifts across
periods. To historicize an institution is to track the sequence of
forces, decisions, and constraints through which it was formed and
reorganized.

In dialectical work, historicization prevents abstraction from freezing
social forms into essences. It keeps analysis tied to determinate
conditions and temporal development.
