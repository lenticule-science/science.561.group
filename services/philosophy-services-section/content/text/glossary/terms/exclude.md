- [[define]]:: [[exclude|Excluding]] is the primitive [[act]] by which a [[triage/thing]] is held out of what is. It establishes the [[condition]] of [[exclusion]], marking the [[triage/thing]] as not part of what is held open within the [[domain of relating]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires that a [[triage/thing]] be:
    - Brought into what it is (by [[include|including]]);
    - And set apart from what it is not (by [[exclude|excluding]]).

- [[exclude|Excluding]] is the [[act]] that enacts this **holding-out**:
    - It does not merely assert that something is absent; it actively **establishes absence** by preventing the [[triage/thing]] from being within the scope of what is.
    - Without [[exclude|excluding]], nothing is positioned as absent, and no coherent claim of [[exist|existing]] can be held open.

- [[exclude|Excluding]] is primitive:
    - It cannot be reduced to other acts;
    - It operates in necessary **complementarity** with [[include|including]].

- [[exclude|Excluding]] brings about the [[condition]] of [[exclusion]]:
    - The [[condition]] that specifies that the [[triage/thing]] is **outside** of what is.
    - This [[condition]] persists only transiently unless stabilized by a [[structure]].

- To sustain the dynamic of [[exist|existing]], [[exclude|excluding]] must be held open **together with** [[include|including]]:
    - This mutual holding induces [[tension]];
    - [[tension]] ensures that [[exclusion]] is not void but is **active and bounded**, forming a coherent opposition to [[inclusion]].

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that [[exist|exists]] is, by definition, set apart through the act of [[exclude|excluding]].
- [[exclude|Excluding]] is inseparable from [[include|including]]:
    - No [[exclusion]] can hold without its opposite [[inclusion]] also being held;
    - This interplay is essential for maintaining the [[difference]] that allows [[triage/thing|things]] to be distinct.
- [[exclude|Excluding]] is foundational to all [[relational dynamics]]:
    - It is one half of the minimal engine that sustains the architecture of [[exist|existing]] and all further relational structures.

## Earlier material

Speaking about the void already carves out a mentioned interior versus an unmentioned exterior, so an act of exclusion is forced.

The trace `(Relationlessness Relationlessness)` places the same empty act in both witness and witness-ee positions.  Directing the void back at itself expresses the relation “this is not with that,” so the composition precisely captures exclusion and earns the name *Exclude*.
