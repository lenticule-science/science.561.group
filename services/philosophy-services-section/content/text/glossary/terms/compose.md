- [[summary]]:: **Compose** is the act of [[relate|relating]] multiple unary [[relational structures]] into a single, ordered composite at a shared [[sequence]] level.

Compose performs structured inclusion of multiple distinct components into a unified relational form, preserving both their individual identities and their positional order.

## Expression

compose(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n):  
→ Tuple(p, [x(1), x(2), ..., x(p)], n)

## Properties

- Arity-preserving: the composite retains `p` distinct components  
- Order-preserving: the position of each `x(i)` is fixed within the tuple  
- Compatible with [[reflex]] and [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/reflect]]: components remain accessible under further relational acts

## Earlier material

Compose is the basic mechanism for combining operations: applying one and then the other. It is the structural glue that turns individual operations into a coherent system.

Compose operates across all five movements as a utility term. Every compound operation in the relational framework is built from composition. [Close](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/close.md) is composed from simpler operations; [Iterate](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/iterate.md) feeds an operation back into itself through composition; [Flow](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/flow.md) is defined by composing resolution and propagation. Compose appears as early as [Movement I: Logical Origination](../concepts/logical-origination.md), where the logical operations ([Together](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/together.md), [Either](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/either.md), [Implies](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/implies.md), [Negate](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/negate.md)) are combined to form more complex recognitions, and it remains active through [Movement V: Emergent Containment](../concepts/emergent-containment.md) where meta-level operations are composed.

Composition is associative — grouping does not matter — and has an identity element (the operation that returns its input unchanged). These two properties ensure that the order of doing things is well-defined, even as the framework builds increasingly complex structures from simple parts.
