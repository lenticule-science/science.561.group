The ***exclusion constraint induction function*** is [[induce|induced]] by the [[emerge|emergence]] of [[exclusion]].

*Exclusion* (`∅`), a [[condition]] of no [[relation]], has relation with *relation* itself, [[induce|inducing]] [[constraint]](`C`), a condition of [[induced difference]].

> `∅ ⊢ C`

That is, [[relationlessness]] induces the conditions for [[relationality]].
