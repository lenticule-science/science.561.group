A session zero is the initial meeting of a [tabletop role-playing game](../_index.md) group, held before the first [session](session.md) of actual play. Its purpose is to align expectations, establish boundaries, and create [characters](../../../terms/character.md) that work together.

A typical session zero covers: the genre and tone of the [campaign](campaign.md), content boundaries using [safety tools](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/safety-tool.md), scheduling logistics, platform choices for remote groups such as a [virtual tabletop](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/services/tabletop-role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/virtual-tabletop.md), and the kind of stories the group wants to tell. Players then create characters with this shared context, which reduces the chance of a character concept that clashes with the group's agreed direction.

Session zero is also where the [game master](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/game-master.md) communicates house rules, system modifications, and expectations about player engagement, including attendance, preparation, and spotlight sharing. The conversation is not a contract. It establishes a starting point that the group can revisit as the campaign develops and preferences shift.
