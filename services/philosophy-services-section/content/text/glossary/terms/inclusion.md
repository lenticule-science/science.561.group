- [[define]]:: [[inclusion]] is the [[condition]] brought about by the [[act]] of [[include|including]]. It specifies that a [[triage/thing]] is held within what is, marking it as present within the [[domain of relating]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires that a [[triage/thing]]:
    - Be included in what it is (producing [[inclusion]]);
    - And be excluded from what it is not (producing [[exclusion]]).

- The [[act]] of [[include|including]] establishes the [[condition]] of [[inclusion]]:
    - This [[condition]] holds the [[triage/thing]] **inside** the field of what is;
    - [[inclusion]] is not a passive state but an **actively maintained presence**;
    - It marks and holds the [[triage/thing]] as part of the relational field.

- [[inclusion]] is transient unless stabilized by a [[structure]]:
    - As long as the [[act]] of [[include|including]] is active, [[inclusion]] persists;
    - To sustain it durably, [[inclusion]] must be stabilized within a [[relative]] or other [[structure]].

- [[inclusion]] gains its coherence by being held together with [[exclusion]]:
    - This mutual holding induces [[tension]];
    - [[tension]] ensures that [[inclusion]] is not trivial or self-dissolving but is maintained in **coherent opposition** to [[exclusion]].

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that [[exist|exists]] is conditioned by both [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]];
- [[inclusion]] is essential for affirming what a [[triage/thing]] is:
    - Without [[inclusion]], no [[triage/thing]] can be marked as present within the field of what is;
- [[inclusion]] participates in the fundamental [[relational dynamics]] that generate and sustain [[exist|existing]] and all further relational architectures.

***Inclusion*** is the [condition](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/condition.md) [induced](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inducing.md) by [including](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md). It describes whether a [thing](thing.org.md) is held within what is; it is true when some thing is present within the [domain of relating](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/domain-of-relating.md).

- Every [thing](thing.org.md) that [exists](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/existing.md) is [conditioned](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conditioning.md) by both [inclusion](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/inclusion.md) and [exclusion](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/exclusion.md).
