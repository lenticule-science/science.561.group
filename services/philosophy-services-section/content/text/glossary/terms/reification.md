Reification is the transformation of historically produced social
relations into apparently natural or thing-like facts.

In Marxist and Lukácsian analysis, reification obscures mediation: what
is socially produced appears as if it were simply given, autonomous, and
beyond alteration [@lukacs-sep-2023].

The critical task is de-reification: recovering the processes, relations,
and institutions that produce the appearance of objectivity.
