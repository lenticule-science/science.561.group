- [[define]]:: A [[structure]] is that which holds a [[condition]] open durably. Every [[act]] aims at bringing about a [[condition]], but only when a [[condition]] is stabilized as a [[relative]] does a [[structure]] exist.

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires understanding the interplay of [[act]], [[condition]], and [[structure]].
    - A [[triage/thing]] [[exist|exists]] only if it is held open as [[difference]] from what is not.
    - [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]] bring about [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]], but these are [[condition|conditions]]nditions]] that remain **transient** unless stabilized.

- An [[act]] initiates change; a [[condition]] arises as the immediate result of that act.
    - For example, the act of [[include|including]] produces the condition of [[inclusion]].
    - These [[condition|conditions]] persist **only as long as the [[act]] is active**; they do not sustain themselves.

- To sustain a [[condition]] beyond the moment of the [[act]], a further process is required: stabilization.
    - Stabilization is what converts a transient [[condition]] into something **durable**—able to persist within the [[domain of relating]] even after the originating [[act]] has subsided.
    - This stabilized holding-open is what we mean by [[structure]].

- Therefore, a [[structure]] arises when:
    - An [[act]] brings about a [[condition]];
    - That [[condition]] is **held open durably,** not merely transiently;
    - The result is a [[relative]]—the minimal [[structure]] that maintains the [[relation]] as a **coherent, self-sustaining unit.**

- The [[relative]] is the **first instance** of a [[structure]].
    - In the derivation of [[exist|existing]], the [[relative]] is produced by the act of [[relate|relating]] and the [[condition]] of [[relation]].
    - This [[relative]] ensures that the [[relation]] it holds open is **durable, stable, and coherent.**

- Without [[structure]]:
    - All [[condition|conditions]] would collapse as soon as the [[act|acts]] that produced them conclude.
    - No persistence would be possible; no coherent system of [[relation|relations]] could form.
    - [[exist|existing]] itself would fail to hold, as it relies on the stability of its underlying [[condition|conditions]].

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that is said to [[exist]] depends on at least one [[structure]]—the [[relative]] that stabilizes its minimal [[relation]].
- [[structure|structures]] provide the [[coherence]] required for any [[relational system]] to hold together over time.
- No [[condition]] achieves full coherence unless it is stabilized by a corresponding [[structure]].
