Principal contradiction identifies which contradiction is decisive in a
conjuncture when multiple contradictions coexist. In Mao's formulation,
this role can shift as conditions change; principal status is historical,
not eternal [@mao-on-contradiction-1937].

This concept is strategic: it orders analytic and political priority.
