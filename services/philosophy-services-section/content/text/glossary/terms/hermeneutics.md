***Hermeneutics*** is the [theory](theory.md) and [methodology](../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/methodology.md) of [interpreting](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpreting.md) [meaning](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meaning.md).

Originally, **hermeneutics** was limited to the theory and methodology of [exegesis](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/exegesis.md), or interpretation of [scripture](../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/religious-text.md), but contemporarily may include all [communication](../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/communication.md) and [knowledge](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowledge.md).
