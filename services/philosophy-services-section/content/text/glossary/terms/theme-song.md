A ***theme song*** is the [song](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/music-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/song.md) that [accompanies](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/accompanying.md) another [thing](thing.md) as part of its [theme](../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/services/software-services-section/services/mkdocs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/theme.md), such as a song being used during a [television show](television-show.md)'s [introduction](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/services/essay-writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/introduction.md).
