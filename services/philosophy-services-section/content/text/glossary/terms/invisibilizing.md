***Invizibilizing*** is an [act](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) [resulting](resulting.md) in a [thing](thing.md) [being](being.md) [invisible](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/invisible.md).

When the result of an act includes a thing becoming invisible, that act can be said to be invisibilizing that thing.

The term is often used in [sociology](../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sociology.md), and [critical theory](../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/critical-theory.md), especially [criticism of tradition](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/criticism-of-tradition.md), [criticism of liberalism](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/criticism-of-liberalism.md).
