There's been reports that *Immigration and Customs Enforcement* is going to be conducting mass immigrant round-ups on Sunday, and a lot of people in the Fediverse are sharing posts about it. I'm concerned some of them aren't considering the risks they're taking as American residents sharing anti-law enforcement sentiment in public channels. I know I publicly express that sentiment myself, but I'm already known to dislike law enforcement so the damage is done. A lot of these folk are young folk, even kids.
