A ***mystery*** is a [thing](thing.md) that is [unknowable](unknowable.md), regardless of [epistemic method](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/epistemic-method.md).

Mystery is a [central concept](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/central-concept.md) in [Lakotaean ideology](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lakotaean-ideology.md).
