***A Thousand Plateaus*** is a [text](../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md) [authored](authoring.md) by [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) and [Félix Guattari](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/felix-guattari.md), [published](publishing.md) in [1980](../../../../../history-and-archaeology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-1980.md).

In it, they [coined a term](coining-a-term.md), [overcoding](overcoding.md).
