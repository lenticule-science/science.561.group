The transmission function (T) is induced by the resonance induction function (R).

Resonance, when extended beyond its original cycle while preserving constraint integrity, initiates transmission — the propagation of structured difference across stabilized constraint.

T(x → y) := R(x, x′) ∧ ∃ path {x′, ..., y} ⊆ S where ∀ i: M(xᵢ, xᵢ₊₁) ∧ C(xᵢ) = xᵢ

That is, transmission is the outward continuity of resonance — difference sustained across constraint without collapse.
