***Ontogenrism*** is an [ontological process](ontological-process.md) in which [ontologizing](ontologizing.md) is [genring](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genring.md). [Coined](coining-a-term.md) by [emsenn](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsenn.md), *ontogenrism* describes the [contemporary global kyriarchy](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/contemporary-global-kyriarchy.md)'s [dominant](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dominating.md) [ontological mode](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontological-mode.md), which may be an [elementary process](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/elementary-process.md) of [liberalism](liberalism.md), as a [recursive process](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recursive-process.md) by which [kyriarchist systems](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kyriarchist-system.md) [format](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/formatting.md) [existence](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/existence.md) into [legible](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/legible.md), [governable](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/governable.md) subjects through a narrative of [anticipation](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anticipation.md) and [entitlement](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/entitlement.md) [affirmed](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/affirming.md) by its [a priori determination](a-priori-determination.md).

Contrasted with ontological processes outside the [lineage of contemporary kyriarchist ontologizing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lineage-of-contemporary-kyriarchist-ontologizing.md), *ontogenrism* is [differentiated](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/differentiating.md) by what it must [assume](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/assuming.md) to be true: existence is [immanently](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/immanently.md) [coherent](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/coherent.md).

This simple [assumption](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/assumption.md) has [developed](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/developing.md) alongside the contemporary global kyriarchy from [reflections](reflection.md) on the [precondition](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/precondition.md) of the [real](real.md) as experienced by [Thales of Miletus](thales-of-miletus.md) into the [experience of being](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experience-of-being.md) of the [liberal subject](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-subject.md).
