***Collaborating*** is when two [things](thing.md) [intentionally](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intentionally.md) [do](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/doing.md) the same [act](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) or [process](../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/process.md) in the same [situation](situation.md), usually toward the same [result](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/result.md).
