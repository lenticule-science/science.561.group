***Ontologizing*** is [conceptualizing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conceptualizing.md) the [mode of existence](mode-of-existence.md) of a thing.

In [relational dynamics](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/01/2025-09-01-relational-dynamics-org-roam-catalog-query.md), *ontologizing* is [conceptualizing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/conceptualizing.md) a possible secondary intension of a thing, [expressing](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/expressing.md) that intension as a [Fregean first-order predicate](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fregean-first-order-predicate.md), testing it as a Tarskian model-theoretic truth, and reflexively revisiting earlier stages of the ontologizing process as necessary to achieve [ontological closure](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontological-closure.md).
