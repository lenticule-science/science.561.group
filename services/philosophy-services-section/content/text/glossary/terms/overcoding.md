***Overcoding*** is a term for a [process](../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/process.md) by which [systems](../../../../services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/system.md) [erase](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/erasing.md) or [invisibilize](invisibilizing.md) [difference](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/difference.md). [Coined](coining-a-term.md) in [A Thousand Plateaus](a-thousand-plateaus.md).

Interestingly, [my files](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emsenn-s-files.md) may be a form of overcoding, done for [sabotaging](sabotaging.md) [liberal ideology](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal-ideology.md).
