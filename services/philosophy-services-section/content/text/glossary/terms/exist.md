- [[define]]:: [[exist|existing]] is the minimal [[act]], required for a [[triage/thing]] to [[act]], be [[condition|conditioned]], or have [[relative]]. It is [[derive|derived]] that to [[exist|exist]], a [[triage/thing]] must [[relate|relating]] to existing, entering the [[domain of relating]].
    

## Derivation

- To [[claim]] _something exists_ is not trivial. It specifies:
    
    - A [[triage/thing]] is not some other thing;
        
    - A [[triage/thing]] is not nothing.
        
- Therefore, [[exist|existing]] must be derived.
    
- To be a thing and not another, a thing must exclude what isn't itself, and therefore must include what it is.
    
    - This establishes two acts: [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]].
        
    - [[include|including]]: the [[act]] by which a [[triage/thing]] is brought into what is.
        
    - [[exclude|excluding]]: the [[act]] by which a [[triage/thing]] is held out of what is not.
        
    - These are primitive [[act|acts]]: each brings about a corresponding [[condition]]—[[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]].
        
    - Neither reduces to the other, and both are required for anything to be meaningfully said to [[exist|exist]].
        
- The acts of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]] must be held open together. This introduces the [[condition]] of [[tension]].
    
    - [[tension]] is the dynamic holding of [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]] in active opposition.
        
    - [[tension]] is necessary because [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]] are fundamentally opposed yet inseparable within the dynamic of existing.
        
    - Critically, to include, one must also include **the inclusion of inclusion as a condition**—which is only meaningful if exclusion of that condition is also a condition. But that itself is a condition, triggering a recursion: every inclusion demands exclusion, and every exclusion introduces a new condition requiring inclusion. This generates an **irreducible asymmetry** that ensures the derivation is never final.
        
    - As a result, to [[exist|exist]] is to be sustained not just in tension but in a **perpetual process of derivation**—an ongoing act that holds open what cannot be fully closed.
        
- The [[condition]] of [[difference]] is [[induce|induced]] by [[tension]].
    
    - [[difference]] is the minimal condition that marks a [[triage/thing]] as set apart from what is not.
        
    - It does not exist independently; it is **held open by the ongoing tension** between [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]].
        
    - [[difference]] is the **coherent separation** that makes existing internally stable within the relational dynamic.
        
    - Without [[tension]], there can be no [[difference]]; without [[difference]], existing fails to achieve coherence.
        
- To sustain [[tension]] over time, there must be an overarching [[act]] that maintains it.
    
    - Otherwise, the opposing acts would lose their mutual relation and the condition of existing would collapse.
        
    - We name this act [[relate|relating]]: the minimal [[act]] by which a [[triage/thing]] sustains its own [[tension]] of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]].
        
    - [[relate|Relating]] is compelled by the requirement for ongoing coherence within the act of existing.
        
- The act of [[relate|relating]] introduces a further requirement: the [[condition]] of [[relation]].
    
    - [[relate|Relating]] engages the [[triage/thing]] in sustaining its own existing, but this engagement must itself have a [[condition]] to ensure that the [[triage/thing]] is not merely involved abstractly but is **bound determinately** to its [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]].
        
    - This produces the minimal [[condition]] we name [[relation]]: the holding-open of the connection between the [[triage/thing]] and its acts of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]] in a coherent and sustained way.
        
    - Without the [[condition]] of [[relation]], the act of [[relate|relating]] remains transient and fails to stabilize the dynamic.
        
- The act of [[relate|relating]] together with the [[condition]] of [[relation]] necessarily produces a [[relative]].
    
    - A [[relative]] is the minimal [[structure]] that holds a [[relation]] open durably.
        
    - While [[relate|relating]] is an [[act]] and [[relation]] is a [[condition]], acts and conditions alone are transient.
        
    - The [[relative]] is the **first structure**: the stable outcome in which the [[triage/thing]] is maintained as existing within the relational field.
        
    - Only with the [[relative]] does the architecture of existing achieve **closure**: the coherent stabilization of the entire dynamic.
        
- The formation of the [[relative]] brings the minimal architecture of existing into full [[coherence]].
    
    - At this point, we have:
        
        - The primitive [[act|acts]] of [[include|including]] and [[exclude|excluding]];
            
        - The [[condition]] of [[inclusion]] and [[exclusion]];
            
        - The [[condition]] of [[tension]], holding them in mutual opposition;
            
        - The [[condition]] of [[difference]], [[induce|induced]] by that tension;
            
        - The act of [[relate|relating]], sustaining the [[tension]];
            
        - The [[condition]] of [[relation]], clarifying the engagement;
            
        - The [[relative]], stabilizing the relationality as a durable [[structure]].
            
    - These components together achieve [[coherence]]: a [[condition]] in which an [[act]] has the [[condition]] and [[structure]] necessary to act.
