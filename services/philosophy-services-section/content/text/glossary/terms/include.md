- [[define]]:: [[include|Including]] is the primitive [[act]] by which a [[triage/thing]] is brought into what is. It establishes the [[condition]] of [[inclusion]], marking the [[triage/thing]] as part of what is held open within the [[domain of relating]].

## Derivation

- [[derive|Deriving]] [[exist|existing]] requires that a [[triage/thing]] be:
    - Set apart from what it is not (by [[exclude|excluding]]);
    - And brought into what it is (by [[include|including]]).

- [[include|Including]] is the [[act]] that enacts this **bringing-in**:
    - It does not merely assert that something is present; it actively **establishes presence** by holding the [[triage/thing]] within the scope of what is.
    - Without [[include|including]], nothing is positioned as present, and no claim of [[exist|existing]] can be held open.

- [[include|Including]] is primitive:
    - It cannot be reduced to other acts;
    - It operates in necessary **complementarity** with [[exclude|excluding]].

- [[include|Including]] brings about the [[condition]] of [[inclusion]]:
    - The [[condition]] that specifies that the [[triage/thing]] is **within** what is.
    - This [[condition]] persists only transiently unless stabilized by a [[structure]].

- To sustain the dynamic of [[exist|existing]], [[include|including]] must be held open **together with** [[exclude|excluding]]:
    - This mutual holding induces [[tension]];
    - [[tension]] ensures that [[inclusion]] is not totalizing (collapsing everything into undifferentiated presence) but is instead **bounded and coherent** in opposition to [[exclusion]].

## Implications

- Every [[triage/thing]] that [[exist|exists]] is, by definition, brought about through the act of [[include|including]].
- [[include|Including]] is inseparable from [[exclude|excluding]]:
    - No [[inclusion]] can hold without its opposite [[exclusion]] also being held;
    - This interplay is essential for maintaining the [[difference]] that allows [[triage/thing|things]] to be distinct.
- [[include|Including]] is foundational to all [[relational dynamics]]:
    - It is one half of the minimal engine that sustains the architecture of [[exist|existing]] and, by extension, all higher relational constructs.

## Earlier material

Include is self-persistence: holding a [recognition](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/recognition.md) as it is, between the dual motions of inward consolidation and outward release. While [Close](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/close.md) gathers inward and [Open](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/open.md) releases outward, Include names the recognition as itself — bounded above by its closure and below by its interior, occupying a stable position between the two.

Include arises in [Movement II: Structural Stabilization](../concepts/structural-stabilization.md) as the recognition that something can persist as itself between the dual motions of [closure](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/phenomena/closure.md) and interior. Include is the formal expression of the idea that a thing can be recognized as itself without yet being reduced to its stable core (Open) or expanded to its full closure (Close).

Because [Open](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/open.md)(a) is contained in a and a is contained in [Close](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/close.md)(a), Include sits at the midpoint of the Open-Close pair. It is the identity — it does not transform its input. What makes Include non-trivial is its structural position: it satisfies the [Stabilizes-Between](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/stabilizes-between.md) condition, holding the thing steady between both poles. This mediating position is only possible because [balance](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/balance.md) holds — because the dual motions do not interfere with each other.

- [Close](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/close.md) — the upper pole that Include mediates toward
- [Open](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/open.md) — the lower pole that Include mediates toward
- [Stabilizes-Between](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/philosophy/relationality/archive/triage/relationality-2/domains/ai-generated-relationality/domains/relationality-slop-2026-03-04/stabilizes-between.md) — the condition that Include satisfies
- [Balance](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/balance.md) — the compatibility between Open and Close that makes mediation possible
