***Excluding*** is the [act](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) of [not](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/negation.md) [including](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md): where including is a [thing](thing.md) [being](being.md) within another thing, excluding is a thing *not* being within another thing.

The [meaning](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meaning.md) of *excluding* is [derived](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/deriving.md) in [Deriving Relational Dynamics](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/26/2025-08-26-deriving-relational-dynamics.md), where it is [determined](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/determining.md) to be a [necessary determination](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/necessary-determination.md) of [existence](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/existence.md) [existing](../../../../services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/existing.md).
