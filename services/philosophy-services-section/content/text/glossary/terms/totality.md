Totality is the methodological demand to analyze parts through the
structured whole that conditions their meaning and function.

In dialectical usage, totality does not mean a closed or final system. It
means that phenomena are relationally constituted: labor, law, state,
and culture are not self-explanatory in isolation.

In Lukácsian terms, totality is central to overcoming fragmented or
reified appearances in social analysis [@lukacs-sep-2023].
