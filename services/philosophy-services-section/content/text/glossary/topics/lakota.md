---
title: "Lakota Philosophy"
description: "The philosophical tradition of the Lakota people — relational epistemology, place-based knowledge, and the understanding that knowledge lives in relationships rather than in individual minds."
---


The library's grounding epistemology. Lakota philosophy holds that knowledge is relational, situated, and constituted through responsible participation in relationships — with land, other-than-human beings, ancestors, and ceremony. This is not a supplement to the formal architecture but its foundation: the relational universe is built on relational ontology arrived at through Lakota thought.
