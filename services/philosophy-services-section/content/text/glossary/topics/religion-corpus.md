- `religion.md`, the Christianity root, the spirituality root, Solomonic occultism, and Thomism are topic files under this philosophy service.
- `acedia`, `curiositas`, `spes`, `studiositas`, and `glasya-labolas` are canonical glossary terms. The source's three July-corpus revisions are preserved in the active `acedia`, `studiositas`, and spirituality/triggers files.

Their authored variants remain separately headed and are not treated as a single doctrine.
- The four dated routine readings are already represented by dated Cassia Lorne entries in the divination service's character legacy library; they remain four readings, not four definitions of a “routine read.”
- Post-conversion medallions and anti-Christian Bruvik are canonical Norway-history notes; Julenek and post-conversion Christmas practices are now adjacent notes in that same dated collection.
- The Taoist collapse essay already has a canonical Dao-services destination, with the July archive serving as its historical byte record.
