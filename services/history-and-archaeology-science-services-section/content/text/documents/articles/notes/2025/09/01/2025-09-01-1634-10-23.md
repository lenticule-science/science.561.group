***October 23rd, 1634*** was the [day](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/day.md) [October 23rd](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/october-23.md) in [1634ce](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1634ce.md).

# On This Day

The [Pequot people](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pequot-people.md) sent a messenger promising tribute to [Roger Ludlow](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/roger-ludlow.md), then [deputy governor](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/political-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/01/2025-09-01-deputy-governor.md) of the [Massachusetts Bay Colony](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/massachusetts-bay-colony.md).
