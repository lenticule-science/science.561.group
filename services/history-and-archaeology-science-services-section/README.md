# History and Archaeology Science Services Section

This section implements OECD FORD 6.1, history and archaeology. Public history
and social history are narrower historical fields here rather than peer social
sciences.

## Settler social reproduction research (2026-10-17)

Historical evidence for eugenic population selection, racial law, U.S. births into slavery, captive domestic trade, and settler migration is curated in the [Sociology Science commissioned corpus](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/README.md), including its original sources and evidence grades. This History section links that corpus as a cross-disciplinary historical research consumer rather than creating a second unsynchronized bibliography. Source-specific history and archaeological records remain under this field when a separate historical inquiry has its own scope and evidence.
