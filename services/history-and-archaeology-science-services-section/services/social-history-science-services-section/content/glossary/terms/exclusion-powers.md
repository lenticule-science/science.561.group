An exclusion-power is a dispositional property of a social arrangement: not what the arrangement *is*, but what it *can make happen* — specifically, the removal, denial, or violence it stands ready to bring down on whoever falls outside it. The philosophy glossary's [property](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/property.md) entry carries the underlying metaphysics (Molnar's powers: fragility is the power-to-shatter, solubility the power-to-dissolve); this term names the political application. A deed does not describe dirt; it describes who may summon enforcement against whoever stands on the dirt without permission. A border does not mark a place; it holds the power to detain, deport, and wall. The arrangement is inert most of the time — like fragility, an exclusion-power is real even when unmanifested — and that latency is exactly what lets it pass as neutral fact rather than standing threat.

Cheryl Harris's "Whiteness as Property" (1993) supplies the sharpest worked case: whiteness functioning as a bundle of exclusionary powers held simply by being read as white, enforceable the way a land title is. The Gadsden-line corpus supplies the others. The [San Rafael de la Zanja grant](./san-rafael-de-la-zanja-grant.md) shows a communal holding converted into a single enclosing power through litigation and cattle-as-occupation. The [Roosevelt Reservation](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/glossary/terms/roosevelt-reservation.md) is a 60-foot exclusion-power proclaimed in 1907 and left dormant for a century before the 2026 wall energized it. The [§102 waiver](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/glossary/terms/real-id-act-section-102-waiver.md) is a second-order case: the power to switch off every legal constraint on exercising the first-order power.

The corpus's recurring observation is that exclusion-powers are *selectively energized*. The same state that would not enforce treaty fishing rights administered starvation to stop the Sun Dance ([Code of Indian Offenses](./code-of-indian-offenses.md)); the same border that walls people moving north let 275 armed strikebreakers cross south ([Cananea](./cananea-strike.md)). Asking of any arrangement "what exclusion-powers does it hold, who can energize them, and against whom have they actually fired?" is this program's basic analytic move — and the answer is never symmetrical.

The concept's best worked example of *reversed* polarity is the [Medicine Line](./medicine-line.md): for four years (1877–1881) the 49th parallel's exclusion-power pointed at the United States Army, sheltering Sitting Bull's Lakota in Grandmother's Land — until Canada's own filing system (no reserve, no rations, wait out the buffalo) reasserted the familiar direction. An exclusion-power is a mechanism, not a moral; it runs whichever way the holding institution points it, and the corpus now attests both directions.
