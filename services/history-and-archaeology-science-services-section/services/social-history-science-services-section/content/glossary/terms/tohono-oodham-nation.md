The Tohono O'odham Nation — the Desert People — is the federally recognized government of O'odham whose ancestral lands ran from the Gila River to the Sea of Cortez, bisected without consultation by the [Gadsden Purchase](./gadsden-purchase.md) line in 1854. Roughly 34,000 enrolled members; the Nation's 2026 issue brief counts about 3,000 residing on ancestral lands in Sonora, still crossing for kinship, subsistence, and ceremony (the Magdalena pilgrimage) under the post-9/11 Enhanced Tribal Card regime. The reservation — 2.8 million acres, second-largest in the U.S. — carries 62 miles of the international border, more than any other tribal nation.

The Nation's institutional voice on the border is unambiguous and predates the current wall by decades: the 2017 film *There's No O'odham Word for Wall*; then-Vice Chairman Verlon Jose's "over my dead body"; the Legislative Council's formal opposition (February 2017). The record since is a chronicle of the [filing system](./settlerism-as-filing-system.md) overriding that voice: the February 2020 blasting of Monument Hill (Hia-Ced O'odham burial ground), the drawdown of Quitobaquito Springs (A'al Vaipia), the 2020 arrests of Amber Lee Ortega and Nellie Jo David that catalyzed the O'odham Anti Border Collective, and — current as of August 2026 — Judge Richard Leon's denial (Aug 13–14, 2026) of the Nation's injunction against "Smart Wall" construction on its boundary, reading the 1907 [Roosevelt Reservation](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/glossary/terms/roosevelt-reservation.md) strip as sufficient authority. Now-Chairman Jose's response — contractors had already "bladed right through" sites DHS promised to protect — states the pattern exactly; the Nation was weighing appeal as of the latest reporting.

For this corpus: the Nation is the [Sobaipuri](./sobaipuri.md)'s successor polity on the western O'odham lands, the standing counterparty the line has never consulted and never dissolved, and — through its trespass notices to contractors during the 2026 [Lochiel](./lochiel.md) events — a live demonstration that Indigenous jurisdiction remains an exclusion-power of its own, asserted against the state's. Its northern mirror is the [Wood Mountain Lakota First Nation](./wood-mountain-lakota.md) — another community with relatives on both sides of a line that arrived after them — with the decisive legal difference that the northern line carries a treaty crossing right ([Jay Treaty](./jay-treaty-border-crossing-right.md)) where the southern one offers only revocable administrative accommodation.
