Property-as-disposition is the move of analyzing ownership with the same instrument the philosophy glossary's [property](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/property.md) entry applies to matter: the distinction between categorical properties (what a thing is) and dispositional ones (what it can do). Ownership presents itself as categorical — this land *is* mine, the way a knife *is* sharp — but nothing in the land carries the mineness. What ownership actually consists of is a disposition welded onto the thing by an enforcing structure: the standing capacity to have trespassers removed, occupants evicted, users charged. Read this way, "I own this" unpacks to "an apparatus stands ready to act against others on my signal," and the metaphysical question "where does the property reside?" gets its honest answer: in the enforcement relation, not the object.

The move matters because categorical presentation is how the relation hides. A fact about a thing seems natural, pre-political, discovered rather than made; a disposition maintained by an apparatus invites the questions who built the apparatus, who feeds it, and what it costs to keep the disposition live. The Gadsden-line corpus runs the analysis at every scale: a land grant whose communal disposition was re-welded into a single enclosing one ([San Rafael de la Zanja](./san-rafael-de-la-zanja-grant.md)); a surveyed line whose placement is demonstrably wrong ([la gran línea](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/la-gran-linea.md)) yet whose dispositions fire regardless — because the line was never about *where*, only about *who*; whiteness itself as a title-like bundle (Harris 1993).

The named product of the analysis is the [exclusion-power](./exclusion-powers.md): once ownership is read dispositionally, the corpus can treat deeds, borders, waivers, and racial categories as one family of objects and compare them — which is what the Lochiel research program does throughout.
