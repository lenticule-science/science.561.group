Settlerism-as-filing-system names the observation, developed across this corpus's Gadsden-line research, that settler colonialism does much of its work not through force directly but through categorization that determines where force and money flow. A wall is filed as "infrastructure" and "border security" — categories that receive $46.5 billion; a two-century cottonwood grove is filed as "the environment" — a category that receives an impact statement, and under the [§102 waiver](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/glossary/terms/real-id-act-section-102-waiver.md), not even that. A 170-year-old real-estate transaction is filed as *reality*; the living watershed it bisects is filed as *scenery*. The filing precedes and licenses the bulldozer.

The Lochiel corpus supplies the term's worked examples at every scale. The [Gadsden Purchase](./gadsden-purchase.md) filed O'odham lands as purchasable emptiness. The [Court of Private Land Claims](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/glossary/terms/court-of-private-land-claims.md) filed a communal grant as an oversized individual claim to be trimmed. The [Code of Indian Offenses](./code-of-indian-offenses.md) filed ceremony as "offense," making rations contingent on the filing. The 1939 de Niza monument filed a doubted report as origin; [la gran línea](../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/la-gran-linea.md) shows the line itself misfiled 4.41 miles from its own treaty coordinates, with nobody in power ever caring — because the line's function was never *where* but *who*. The counting failures the corpus keeps finding (cattle books at half of reality, deportation figures inflated threefold, the Colibrí Center's dissolution) are the filing system's signature: settler accounting inflates what justifies power and loses what indicts it.

The term is the bridge between this corpus's metaphysics and its politics: filings are how [exclusion-powers](./exclusion-powers.md) get selectively energized while presenting as neutral administration, and unfiling — insisting that the tree is infrastructure, that the treaty is law, that the dead be counted — is what the corpus's subjects, from treaty fishers to the Grandmother Camp, are materially doing. The filing system also operates in refusal's clothing: Canada never charged the exiled Lakota, never sent cavalry — it simply *declined to file them* as entitled to a reserve or rations ([Medicine Line](./medicine-line.md); Grandmother's Land), and the buffalo collapse did the rest. Starvation-by-category is the filing system's quietest instrument, attested on both of the corpus's lines.
