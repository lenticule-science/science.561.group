# Consumer Goods Science Services Section

This section is Lenticule Science's consumer-goods custody, managed through
the [561 Group consumer-goods program](../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-goods-services-section/services/consumer-goods-services-section/README.md).
It owns the evidence for product, user, safety, and claim questions; it does
not write sales copy or operate fulfillment.
