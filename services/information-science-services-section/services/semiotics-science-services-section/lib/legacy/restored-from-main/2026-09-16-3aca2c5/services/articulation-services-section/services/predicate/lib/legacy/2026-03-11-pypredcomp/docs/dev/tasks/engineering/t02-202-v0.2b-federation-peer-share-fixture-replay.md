## Grounding

Theory row 10 (federation merge as $\odot$) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md).
The fixture-replay surface is the v0.2-shaped *surrogate for live peering*: two substrates are the two peers; the fixture event-logs are the wire payloads; the cross-merge is the federation handshake.
Convergence to byte-identical fibers IS the proof of the federation algebra.
Library grounding:

- [`federation-convergence`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/federation-convergence.md) — the structural guarantee this fixture replays exercises; under $\odot$ the joint state of two substrates that merge each other's logs is the same regardless of merge order.
- [`cross-peer-trajectory-ordering-is-content-addressed`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/cross-peer-trajectory-ordering-is-content-addressed.md) — the merge order is determined by content-addressed event ords, not wall-clock; the fixture exercises this property by interleaving peer-A's and peer-B's events in two different orders and asserting identical post-merge fibers.
- [`closure-rule-wire-format-canonicalization`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/closure-rule-wire-format-canonicalization.md) — the wire format defers per [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md), but the canonicalization theorem says any wire format that survives canonicalization replays identically; the fixture-replay surface tests *the algebra*, not *a wire choice*.

## Description

[`tests/conformance/test_fixture_replay.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_fixture_replay.py) currently exercises single-substrate replay-from-event-log.
This task extends it with a peer-share variant: two substrate instances are seeded from disjoint fixture slices (peer A from `fixtures/federation/peer-A.jsonl`, peer B from `fixtures/federation/peer-B.jsonl`), then each merges the other's event log via $\odot$.
Acceptance is byte-identical fibers across both substrates after cross-merge, regardless of merge order.

**What this consumes from v0.2a + v0.2b.**

- v0.2a `t02-42` (kernel-shipped bilattice ops) supplies $\odot$ — the federation merge primitive this fixture exercises.
- v0.2a `t02-43` (event log + history fiber) supplies the replay surface this fixture extends.
- v0.2b `t02-150` (federation-protocol stub) supplies the algebraic spec the fixture conforms to.
- v0.2b `t02-155`/`t02-200`/`t02-201` (peer-trust, mTLS, rate-limit stubs) are *not* exercised — this is pure fixture-replay, no wire format.
  Those stubs are the structural spec; this task is the algebraic conformance.
