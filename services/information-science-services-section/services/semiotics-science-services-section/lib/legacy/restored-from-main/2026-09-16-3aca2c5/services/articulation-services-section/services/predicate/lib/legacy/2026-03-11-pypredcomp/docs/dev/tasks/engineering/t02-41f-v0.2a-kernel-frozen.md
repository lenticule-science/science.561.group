## Description

Per [decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md](../../decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md), every `t02-NN` task that ships normative substrate behaviour has a paired `t02-NNf` fixture-authoring task with its own conformance gate.
This is that pair for [t02-41](t02-41-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md) — the **authz-fiber slice** of the four boundary projections, sibling to [`t02-40f`](t02-40f-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md).

The authz fiber is the projection that answers *what this sender can do*, per [specs/security/user-as-boundary.md §2.2](../../specs/security/user-as-boundary.md).
The substrate composes the sender's authz fiber with the requested predicate via the modal restriction $\Box_R$ ([`substrate.authorization.box_R`](../../../../src/substrate/authorization.py)), the surface form of the $\Rightarrow$ adjoint to the Phase-3 fusion $\odot$ (row 2 of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md)).
The canonical *projection cell* `(user:<slug>, projects-authz-fiber) = TRUE` is emitted onto the boundary entity's fiber alongside per-capability `(user:<slug>, granted:<cap>) = TRUE` cells by [`init_boot.bootstrap_users`](../../../../src/init_boot/bootstrap_users.py) per [boot-sequence.md §4.1](../../specs/init-boot/boot-sequence.md).
The umbrella [`test_t02_34f_multi_user_authoring.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_t02_34f_multi_user_authoring.py), the per-role slices [`test_t02_30f_admin_authoring.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_t02_30f_admin_authoring.py) / [`test_t02_31f_member_authoring.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_t02_31f_member_authoring.py), and the existing structural fixtures [`test_modal_authz_box.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_modal_authz_box.py) / [`test_capability_permissions.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_capability_permissions.py) cover overlapping ground; this paired fixture is the **per-fiber refinement** asked for by [t02-41](t02-41-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md)'s acceptance — authz alone, isolated from the other projections and framed as projection-emission + non-amplification.

The fixture lives at [`tests/conformance/test_t02_41f_authz_fiber.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_t02_41f_authz_fiber.py) and witnesses the load-bearing acceptance items from [t02-41](t02-41-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md):

- *"the authz-projection cell exists in the boundary entity's fiber after bootstrap; per-capability `granted:<cap>` cells materialise the role-default fiber content"* — pinned by walking three roles (admin/member/observer) and asserting both `projects-authz-fiber` and every `granted:<cap>` from `ROLE_CAPABILITIES[role]` resolve to TRUE on the boundary's fiber.
  Iterating across all three catches a regression that role-gates the projection cell, drops a role-default grant, or de-syncs `ROLE_CAPABILITIES` from the persisted fiber content.
- *"sender's authz says yes → cell composes"* — pinned by installing a TRUE accessibility cell on the `(user:captain, task:demo)` edge, calling `respond`, and asserting the Phase-0 gate auto-enabled, `authz_decision.allowed=True`, the licensing cell id appears in `matched_cells`, and Phases 1-4 ran (cell emitted, event log advanced, target fiber carries the predicate).
- *"sender's authz says nothing → restricts to $\bot$, no event"* — pinned by installing a grant on an unrelated edge (so the gate auto-enables) and calling `respond` from a sender with no grant on the requested edge; asserts the call returns a vacuous `RespondResult` (no cell, no event, target fiber byte-identical before and after) carrying `authz_decision.allowed=False` and `box_value=NONE` (the algebraic content of "restrict to bottom").
  The substrate raises NO exception per `decisions/capability-not-acl.md` — restriction is algebraic.
  This is the no-amplification claim of `functional-runtime-platform/theorems/capability-non-amplification` in load-bearing form.
- *"byte-identical fiber state across two runs"* — same determinism pinning as t02-40f; monkeypatch `process.user.secrets` to a counter, run bootstrap on two fresh roots, assert byte-equality of `events.jsonl` plus a SHA-256 belt-and-braces check.

## Notes on scope

- The "no-raise discipline" framing in [t02-41](t02-41-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md)'s acceptance ("No code path in `src/substrate/` raises on insufficient authority — restriction is algebraic") IS enforced by Leg 3 of this fixture: the test calls `respond` on a denied edge and asserts a successful return with `authz_decision.allowed=False` and `cell_id is None` — an implementation that raised would fail the leg.
  `AuthzGatePhase` was rewritten to record the decision on the respond context and short-circuit the pipeline (rather than raise `PCC_AUTHZ_DENIED`); the constant is retained in [`src/substrate/error_codes.py`](../../../../src/substrate/error_codes.py) for driver wire compatibility but is no longer raised anywhere in `src/substrate/`.
  Two pre-existing tests that asserted the raise (`test_modal_authz_box.py::test_accessibility_relation_present_but_no_grant_denies` and `test_stratum_capability_t02_126.py::test_retract_at_saturated_denied_when_only_asserted_grant`) were rewritten to assert the restriction shape.
- The fixture deliberately does NOT extend [`test_modal_authz_box.py`](../../../../tests/conformance/test_modal_authz_box.py); the structural Box_R laws (truth-meet preservation, non-amplification, constant-true preservation) are an algebraic-property suite.
  This fixture is the projection-emission-and-respond-integration suite — different hypothesis surface, different failure mode, different file per the t02-30f/t02-40f sibling pattern.

## Acceptance

- The fixture file exists, is collected by pytest, and all four tests pass against the current implementation.
- The fixture exercises the spec's acceptance claims (authz-projection cell on every role + per-capability grants, $\Rightarrow$-admits, $\Rightarrow$-restricts with no state advance, byte-identical replay), not just the implementation's incidental behaviour, per [decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md](../../decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md) §"Why".
