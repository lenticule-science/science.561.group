## Theory grounding

[`predicated-computing/theorems/doctrine-fidelity-via-faithful-interfacer-functor`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/doctrine-fidelity-via-faithful-interfacer-functor.md) plus row 13 of [theory-for-developers.md](../references/theory-for-developers.md) (self-representation everywhere).
A driver is *not* a Python module that registers itself at import; it is a substrate cell of `kind: driver` whose interface contract is encoded in its predications.
The faithful-interfacer-functor is the proof obligation that the driver's substrate-side surface and external-side surface are connected by a functor preserving the operations the doctrine claims they share.

## Procedural steps

Per [implementation/next-stretch.md §2A](../implementation/next-stretch.md#2a-driver-model-under-faithful-interfacer-functor):

1. Author [specs/drivers/driver-model.md](../specs/drivers/driver-model.md) §"Faithful interfacer functor" as the normative obligation.
  Cite the theorem; name the four operations (`open`, `respond`, `close`, `audit`) a driver-cell MUST expose.
2. Author the per-driver conformance vector under [specs/conformance-vectors.md](../specs/conformance-vectors.md) §"Driver fidelity": for any driver, `external → substrate → external` MUST agree with `external → external` modulo the faithful functor's image.
3. Implement the driver-cell schema in `src/drivers/driver_cell.py` (new module) with pydantic round-trip per row 3.
4. Pick three concrete lowest-bandwidth drivers first (filesystem, hledger, in-memory MCP stub) and implement under `src/drivers/<name>/`.
5. Each driver gets a paired fixture per [workflows.md recipe 2](../implementation/workflows.md#2-writing-a-paired-fixture-tnn-nnf).

## Enables next

Every v0.3 bundled app (email, calendar, chat, ledger, file-browser, etc.) is a (driver-cell + app-cell + verb-extensions + dashboard view) tuple per [next-stretch.md §3B](../implementation/next-stretch.md#3b-first-bundled-apps-as-driver--verb--dashboard-tuples).
Without the driver model normative, every v0.3 app re-invents its substrate boundary and the cross-app properties (an email becomes a calendar invite becomes a chat reply, all preserving capability fiber) are unprovable.
