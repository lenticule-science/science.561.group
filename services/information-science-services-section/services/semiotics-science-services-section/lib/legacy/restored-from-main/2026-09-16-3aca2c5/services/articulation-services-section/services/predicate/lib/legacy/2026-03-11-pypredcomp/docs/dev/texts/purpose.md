The **561 group** is a confederacy whose function is **Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty**.
Captain emsenn operates the **RSC Quiescent**, one ship in that confederacy.
This OS is the operational substrate that work runs on — not a research artifact, not an AI-systems vehicle, not a SaaS product.
The place the captain runs the ship from.

## What the group has been doing without it

Operational state has been scattered across separate tools the group integrates by hand: Slack-style chat, ad-hoc kanban, hledger, markdown-in-git, agent conversations that vanish at session end, library-as-files. **Each works in isolation; connecting them does not.** The connections are integration glue — webhooks, CSV exports, custom scripts.
Cross-system queries reconstruct relationships that aren't encoded structurally anywhere.

## What the computer does

The substrate makes those connections **structural**: one predication-graph store, many projection views.
A task-completion record shows up in the chat projection, the ledger projection (as a credit), the fleet projection (as session progress), and the knowledge projection (as authored work) — same record, with relationships encoded as predications, not glue.

The captain's stack collapses: chat, tasks, ledger, dashboards, wikis, decisions, integration scripts all become projections of the substrate.
It also adds what the previous stack couldn't natively do: federation across sovereign nodes; industrial-control wiring physical infrastructure; continuous learning that promotes patterns through observation → instinct → skill → tool → hook; persona-agents authoring under their own names.

## Why predicated

The bilattice handles four values natively, all first-class:

- **$\bot$** — no evidence yet (not the same as "false").
- **$t$** — affirmed.
- **$f$** — denied.
- **$\top$** — paraconsistent: two sources disagree, both retained.

Federation merge produces $\top$ on disagreement; closure rules see $\top$; the captain's actual work — incomplete, contested, multi-source — has somewhere to live.
A two-valued substrate forces every claim to true-or-false and treats absence as falsehood.
The captain has spent years living with that cost; the predicated substrate is the answer.

## What this is not

- Not a knowledge graph for AI agents.
  Agents are one class of operator alongside humans and external services.
- Not a research platform.
  The substrate is real infrastructure; research happens *on* it.
- Not a SaaS product.
  The 561 group operates it for themselves; if other confederacies want it, they fork.
