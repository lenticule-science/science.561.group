## Outcome

The substrate supports multiple users on a single node under the boundary-projection identity scheme.
Each user has its own capability fibers (auth / authz / attention / history).
Inter-session messaging flows under the multi-regime-session discipline.
Boot ships `bootstrap_users` and `fiber_reconcile` routines.

## Acceptance

- Multi-user authoring (admin, member, observer roles) per the boundary-projection identity scheme works against fixtures (gate item 4 of v0.2a: admin asserts, member retracts, observer reads).
- Capability fibers (auth / authz / attention / history) are per-user and projection-derived per [specs/security/user-as-boundary.md](../specs/security/user-as-boundary.md).
- Inter-session messaging flows per [specs/process/multi-regime-session.md](../specs/process/multi-regime-session.md).
- `bootstrap_users` and `fiber_reconcile` boot-phase routines exist (gate item 3) and produce divergence-free reconcile against a multi-user manifest of ≥3 users.
