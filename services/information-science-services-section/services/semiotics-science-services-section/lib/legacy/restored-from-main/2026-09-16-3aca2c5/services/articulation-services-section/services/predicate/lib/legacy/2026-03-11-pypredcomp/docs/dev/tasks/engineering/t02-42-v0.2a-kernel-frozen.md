## Description

Spec and source-cite the **attention fiber** projection from the boundary entity.
The attention fiber is the *what this session is focused on* dimension — the read-side companion to the authz fiber's write-side restriction.
Where authz answers "what may you author?", attention answers "what are you reading?"
Both are projections of the same boundary entity per [decisions/identity-is-boundary-projection.md](../../decisions/identity-is-boundary-projection.md).

At v0.2a, the attention fiber is the substrate cell-set the sender's session has subscribed to or queried — the input domain of the read endpoints (`/cell/`, `/fiber/`, `/events`) and the WebSocket subscription.
Per row 11 of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md), sessions are substrate cells with stable slugs across restarts; the attention fiber is itself a substrate cell over the session entity.
