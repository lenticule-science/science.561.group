## Outcome

Three dashboards consume H¹ audit cohomology from a live multi-user node:

- Log aggregation per [specs/observability/log-aggregation.md](../specs/observability/log-aggregation.md).
- Error investigation per [specs/observability/error-investigation.md](../specs/observability/error-investigation.md), including the diagnostic projection extension from [pt-03](../tasks/engineering/pt-03-diagnostic-projection.md).
- Incident management per [specs/governance/incident-management.md](../specs/governance/incident-management.md).

The diagnostic projection answers "why does this saturated cell exist?" by exposing `project_derivation(cell_id) -> WitnessChain` on `substrate.projection`, rendered as `/cells/<id>/why` in the web GUI shell.

## Theory grounding

[`predicated-computing/theorems/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) plus row 9 of [theory-for-developers.md](../references/theory-for-developers.md).
The H¹ surface is the *content* the audit pass returns — a $\top$ cell appears on a dashboard *because* the algebra produced it, not because a logging side-effect was attached.
Logs themselves are a stratum projection (row 4): logs aggregate by filtering the event log on `kind: log-line` predications, not by a sidecar collector.

  Subscribe to the heartbeat (row 11) — never poll.

## Enables next

v0.4 federation needs the H¹ surface to *cross node boundaries* — a dashboard on node A renders an obstruction whose witness chain points at events on node B.
That cross-node walk requires the dashboard surface to already exist locally and to be witness-chain-aware.
Building dashboards in v0.4 instead of v0.2b would force federation work to also re-design the audit UX.
