## Grounding

## Description

The mqtt-driver spec is normative.
This task implements the MQTT 5.0 client binding — the IoT pub/sub exemplar. v0.5 industrial-control work (modbus, etc.) consumes this driver as the network transport; landing it at v0.2b unblocks the v0.5 critical path.

**What this consumes from the rest of v0.2b.** Driver framework from [`task:t02-50`](t02-50-v0.2b-driver-model-and-three-drivers.md) + lifecycle from [`task:t02-53`](t02-53-v0.2b-driver-model-and-three-drivers.md).
Counit discipline from [`task:t02-65`](t02-65-v0.2b-drivers-lossy-counit-discipline.md) — MQTT counit is `lossy`.
Heartbeat-fiber-subscription pattern from [`task:t02-72`](t02-72-v0.2b-heartbeat-fiber-subscription-pattern.md) — MQTT subscriptions surface as heartbeat-driven fiber subscriptions on the substrate side.
