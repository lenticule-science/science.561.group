## Theory grounding

[`predicated-computing/theorems/federation-convergence`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/federation-convergence.md) plus row 10 of [theory-for-developers.md](../references/theory-for-developers.md) (federation as $\odot$).
The algebraic federation spec — that merge is commutative-associative-idempotent and therefore convergent — does not depend on any specific wire format.
It can be specified and tested against an in-memory transport before the wire spec exists.
The capability-flow spec inherits row 8 (modal restriction) crossing nodes; per [`federation-fuel-as-tensor-distributivity`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/federation-fuel-as-tensor-distributivity.md), fuel composes across the federation tensor and MUST be specified now or it cannot be retrofitted at v0.4.

## Procedural steps

Per [implementation/next-stretch.md §2E](../implementation/next-stretch.md#2e-network-protocol-stubs-algebraic-specs-eager):

1. Stub `specs/network/federation-protocol.md`, `peer-trust.md`, `mtls.md`, `federation-rate-limit.md` each citing [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md).
2. Author the algebraic specs eagerly: `specs/network/federation-by-construction.md` (the $\odot$ merge contract) and `specs/network/capability-flow-across-federation.md` (row-8 modal restriction crossing nodes).
3. Land [pt-01](../tasks/engineering/pt-01-federation-peer-share-fixture-replay.md) — the in-memory peer-share fixture replay.
  This proves the algebra without committing to a wire format.

## Enables next

v0.4's wire-format work picks up an *already-validated* algebraic contract and only has to translate it.
Without 2E, v0.4 risks designing a wire format whose semantics quietly diverges from $\odot$ — and once that ships, no amount of CRDT theory recovers it.
The federation-fuel-as-tensor-distributivity theorem is the canary: if the wire format is designed without the tensor obligation in mind, federation rate-limiting becomes ad-hoc rather than algebraic.
