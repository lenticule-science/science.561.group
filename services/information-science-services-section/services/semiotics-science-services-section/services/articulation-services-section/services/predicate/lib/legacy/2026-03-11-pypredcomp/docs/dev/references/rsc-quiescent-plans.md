## How to use this archive

Most of these plans have been absorbed into [milestones/](../milestones/) at the project-phase level.
The agent reads individual archive plans only when:

- Implementing a specific feature where the captain already worked through design choices.
- Looking for predicates the captain has already named (the planning vocabulary is rich).
- Understanding why a particular spec section says what it says.

The new [milestones/](../milestones/) is the working sequencing; this archive is the captain's design library.
