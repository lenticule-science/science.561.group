## Goal

After v0.2a ships, the substrate gains: a driver model with the faithful-interfacer-functor discipline; observability + governance dashboards; a Nix-flake skeleton sufficient for reproducible builds (the full trusting-trust machinery lands in v0.3 per [decisions/trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md](../decisions/trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md)); the stdlib verb specs and dispatch shell; and network-protocol **stubs** only per [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md).
This is the second of two milestone halves that together replace the original v0.2 plan.

v0.2b does **not** ship: full federation/network wire formats (deferred until implementer named), self-hosting (v0.3), the full reproducibility-witness machinery for trusting-trust defence (v0.3).

## Scope

### Drivers - Driver model per [specs/drivers/driver-model.md](../specs/drivers/driver-model.md) — faithful-interfacer-functor discipline. - At least three concrete drivers (kept minimal; per-driver authoring follows the same paired-fixture discipline per [decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md](../decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md)).

### Observability + governance - Log aggregation per [specs/observability/log-aggregation.md](../specs/observability/log-aggregation.md). - Error investigation per [specs/observability/error-investigation.md](../specs/observability/error-investigation.md). - Incident management per [specs/governance/incident-management.md](../specs/governance/incident-management.md). - These three consume the H¹ audit cohomology surface from v0.2a.

### Stdlib - Verb specs and per-verb fixtures per [specs/stdlib/_index.md](../specs/stdlib/_index.md), including the §"Per-verb fixture discipline" appendix. - Dispatch shell per [decisions/single-respond-primitive.md](../decisions/single-respond-primitive.md) — every verb reduces to `respond`.

### Packaging-prep - Nix-flake skeleton (`flake.nix`, `default.nix`, lockfile) sufficient for `nix build .#default` to produce a reproducible Python wheel. - The flake is **packaging-prep, not trusting-trust defence** — the diverse-double-compiling discipline and witnessed-toolchain cell schema land in v0.3.

### Network (stubs only) - `specs/network/federation-protocol.md` exists as a stub citing [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md). - `specs/network/peer-trust.md`, `specs/network/mtls.md`, `specs/network/federation-rate-limit.md` exist as stubs. - Algebraic specs (federation-by-construction, capability-flow across federation) are written eagerly; wire formats are deferred.

## Ship targets

v0.2b ships when:

1. The driver model spec is normative and at least three drivers conform via paired fixtures.
2. The observability dashboards consume H¹ audit cohomology from a live multi-user node.
3. The stdlib dispatch shell exists and every verb's per-verb fixtures pass.
4. `nix build .#default` produces a reproducible wheel (bit-identical across two builds on the same host).
5. Network stubs exist and explicitly cite the deferral decision.

## Successor
