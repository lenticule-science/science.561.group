## Grounding

Theory rows 8 (capability/authz as modal restriction) and 10 (federation merge as $\odot$) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md). mTLS is mutual attestation: each side proves possession of a private key bound to a public identity, and each side's accepted-identity set is restricted by its trust fiber for the other.
The algebraic surface is two capability fibers composed under $\Rightarrow$; the cipher-suite selection is the wire-level concern that defers per [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md).
Library grounding:

- [`signature-binds-witness-to-identity`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/signature-binds-witness-to-identity.md) — the per-connection identity binding is structurally a witness-signature predicate; mTLS is the wire shell around this predicate.
- [`federation-trust-bilattice-graded`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md) — peer trust is bilattice-graded, not boolean; the mTLS spec must accommodate non-binary acceptance (e.g., trusted-but-untested).
- [`modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md) — mTLS authorization at the connection IS modal restriction at the network boundary; the spec inherits the substrate's authorization primitive rather than re-inventing one.
- [decisions/federation-signatures-ed25519](../../decisions/federation-signatures-ed25519.md) names the signature primitive the mTLS spec composes with.

## Description

`specs/network/mtls.md` does not exist.
By Unix reflex the v0.2b implementer would author "mTLS = openssl s_server with client-cert verification" and bake in cipher choices that have to be re-negotiated at v0.4 federation.
The substrate's discipline is that mTLS is structurally a *paired capability fiber predicate* over the connection: the algebraic shape is normative now; the wire-level cipher choice is what defers.

This task creates the stub.
The stub names the algebraic shape (paired capability fibers, bilattice-graded acceptance), names the deferred surface (cipher suite, key-rotation cadence, certificate transparency), and cites the deferral decision.
The structural pre-condition is essential: without it the v0.4 implementer will face a tangle of ad-hoc cipher choices baked into peer-trust assumptions.

**What this consumes from v0.2a + v0.2b.**

- v0.2a `t02-40`/`t02-41` (kernel-shipped capability + authz fibers) supply the algebraic primitive — an mTLS connection is a capability-fiber over the remote-node identity.
- v0.2b `t02-155` (peer-trust stub) supplies the trust-fiber primitive this stub composes with; mTLS is the wire-level instance of the trust algebra.
