## Grounding

Theory rows 6 (the respond primitive) and 11 (sessions, dispatch, heartbeat) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md).
Cross-shell coherence is the *primary instance* of the heartbeat-fiber-subscription pattern: when two shells (TUI, web GUI, headless) subscribe to the same substrate fiber, both observe the same state at the same heartbeat tick.
Without explicit spec authoring, the v0.2b shells would each implement a per-shell read protocol that diverges silently under load.

- [`monotonic-clock-discipline`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/monotonic-clock-discipline.md) — the heartbeat ord is the coherence index.
