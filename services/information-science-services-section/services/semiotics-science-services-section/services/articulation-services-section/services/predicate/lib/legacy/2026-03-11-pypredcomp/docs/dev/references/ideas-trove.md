# Ideas trove

Path: `operations/engineering/triage/operations/command/ideas/triage/`.

50+ markdown files of the captain's design ideas, organized roughly by theme.
Each file is one idea, sometimes 100 lines, sometimes 800+.
They are the captain's working notes — the place where ideas got worked out before being distilled into plans, specs, or library theory.

> **Catalog status (v0.8+).** This catalog is a queue projection of the `pipeline-card` substrate fiber per [specs/governance/triage-workflow.md](../specs/governance/triage-workflow.md) §6 and the [triage-promotes-via-pipeline-stages](../decisions/triage-promotes-via-pipeline-stages.md) decision.
> The pipeline-card cells are canonical; the catalog markdown is regenerated by the v0.8 render script (t08-66).
> The inline `**(Integrated into ...)**` markers below are the legacy form of `pipeline-card.integrated-into-files`; they remain valid for human reading.
> Pre-v0.8 markers are migrated to retroactive pipeline-cards in stage `integrated` by the t08-65 sweep.
> New integrations from v0.8 onward MUST flow through the pipeline (`idea` → `review` → `plan-draft` → `spec-edit` → `integrated`), with the two narrow bypasses declared in the decision (captain-direct edits and incident-response amendments).

## How to use

The agent does not read the trove en masse.
When a spec or plan needs an idea the captain has already worked out, this reference points to the file.
Many of these ideas are absorbed into the new specs; others remain as captain notes.

The trove is also where the captain has been evolving vocabulary in real-time.
New ideas often appear here first.
