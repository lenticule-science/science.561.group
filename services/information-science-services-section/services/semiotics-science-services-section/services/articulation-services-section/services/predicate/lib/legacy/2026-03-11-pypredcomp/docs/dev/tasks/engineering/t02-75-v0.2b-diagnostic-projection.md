## Grounding

Theory rows 4 (stratified facts), 5 (closure rules), and 6 (projections as natural transformations) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md).
Per [specs/kernel/projection-naturality.md](../../specs/kernel/projection-naturality.md) the diagnostic projection is a natural transformation $\Pi_\mathrm{deriv}: \mathrm{Sat} \Rightarrow \mathrm{WitChain}$ from the saturated-stratum presheaf to the witness-chain presheaf, sending each $(s,p,o,v)$ at stratum `"saturated"` to its $(\mathrm{derived\\_from}, \mathrm{derived\\_via})$ chain back to `"asserted"` antecedents.
Library grounding:

- [`witness-chain-as-proof-object`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/witness-chain-as-proof-object.md) — the chain returned by this projection IS a proof object; the projection's faithfulness is the proof-checker discipline.
- [`witness-as-cell`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/witness-as-cell.md) — every link in the chain is itself a substrate cell, so the projection is closed under the substrate's own representation discipline (no out-of-band data structure).
- [`audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) — the H¹-audit panel that consumes this projection IS the cohomology read; the projection supplies the $\check{C}^1$ chains.

## Description

`substrate.projection` exposes the four read-only projections that v0.2a shipped (`project_stratum`, `project_peer`, `project_at_ord`, `project_history`).
Surprised captains today get *no* explanation of why a cell saturated to a particular value — the `derived_from`/`derived_via` fields exist on each cell but there is no projection that traverses them.
This task adds the fifth projection and its two consumers (web-GUI route, dashboard panel).

**What this consumes from v0.2a + v0.2b.**

- v0.2a `t02-43` (history fiber + event log) supplies the per-cell `derived_from`/`derived_via` provenance fields the projection traverses; without it there is nothing to project.
- v0.2a `t02-110` (H¹ audit pass) supplies the cohomology computation the dashboard panel renders — `t02-80` is the *panel*, this task is the *projection it reads*.
- v0.2b `t02-74` (dashboard frame) supplies the widget plumbing the `/cells/<id>/why` view embeds into.
