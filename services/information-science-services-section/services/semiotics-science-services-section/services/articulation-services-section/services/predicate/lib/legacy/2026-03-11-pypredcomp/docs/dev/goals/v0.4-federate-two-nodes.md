# Goal: v0.4 two nodes federate

## Outcome

Two OS instances exchange events under the federation protocol; $\odot$-merge produces consistent fibers where peers agree and $\top$ where they disagree.
The OS edges into the wider fediverse via ActivityPub and into the wider Matrix federation via S2S.
Trust graphs and selective sync work end-to-end.
