# t02-44 — Fuel-composition byte-identical replay (gate #6)

## Description

Verifies the load-bearing assertion of [milestones/v0.2a-multi-user-single-node.md](../../milestones/v0.2a-multi-user-single-node.md) §"Ship targets" #6 and [goals/v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md](../../goals/v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md) §"Acceptance" gate-item-6:

> The fuel-composition gate: a `respond` invocation with explicit `F = top-level ceiling` produces byte-identical replay across two fresh boot cycles.

The implementation surface is already normative per [specs/kernel/fuel-composition.md](../../specs/kernel/fuel-composition.md) (status: normative, version 0.2) and is realised in [`src/substrate/respond.py`](../../../src/substrate/respond.py) (top-level `fuel: int = 1024` parameter; Phase 4d cost-cell emission) and [`src/substrate/cost.py`](../../../src/substrate/cost.py) (the `LawvereQuantale` interface, `CostCell`, `emit_cost`, `query_total_cost`).
Cost cells are content-addressed via `cid(canonical_body)`, so byte-identical replay does not require monkeypatching beyond the wall-clock-free event-log initialisation already used by `t02-34f`.

This task does not introduce new substrate behaviour — it only authors the conformance fixture that locks gate #6 against regression.
The paired fixture record is [t02-44f](t02-44f-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md) per [decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md](../../decisions/fixture-authoring-paired-task.md).

## Acceptance

- The conformance fixture at `tests/conformance/test_t02_44f_fuel_composition_replay.py` exists and passes against `pypredcomp/src/`.
- The fixture's replay leg asserts byte-equality of the full `events.jsonl` stream across two fresh boot cycles under an explicit top-level `F`.
- The fixture also covers the §3 cost-class assignments per-invocation (a no-op respond emits no settlement cell — fold-invariant under the additive quantale's unit `0`; a contradicting respond emits exactly one settlement cell tagged with the additive quantale, with a finite non-negative value) and the reads-are-free invariant at the spec level (`query_total_cost` does not change the entity's quantale-fold across a read; the spec permits read-class cells to be materialised provided they contribute the additive unit `0`).
- The paired task [t02-44f](t02-44f-v0.2a-kernel-frozen.md) is authored and marked `status: complete`.
