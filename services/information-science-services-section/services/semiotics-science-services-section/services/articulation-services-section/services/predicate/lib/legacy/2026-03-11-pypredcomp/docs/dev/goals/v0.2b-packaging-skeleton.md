# Goal: v0.2b packaging skeleton

## Outcome

A Nix-flake skeleton (`flake.nix`, `default.nix`, lockfile) exists at the repo root sufficient for `nix build .#default` to produce a reproducible Python wheel.
Reproducibility means bit-identical output across two builds on the same host.

This is **packaging-prep, not trusting-trust defence**.
The diverse-double-compiling discipline and witnessed-toolchain cell schema land in v0.3 per [decisions/trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md](../decisions/trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md).

## Acceptance

- Gate item 4 of v0.2b: `nix build .#default` produces a reproducible wheel (bit-identical across two builds on the same host).

## Backed by tasks

- Intra-milestone IDs `t02-100`…`t02-102` (Nix-flake skeleton).

## Theory grounding

[`predicated-computing/theorems/deployment-determinism`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/deployment-determinism.md) plus row 12 of [theory-for-developers.md](../references/theory-for-developers.md) (boot-determinism).
A reproducible build is the *outer envelope* of boot-determinism: the same image produces the same boot-determinism trajectory.
Without reproducibility, two captains running "the same" v0.3 disagree on what their substrate is — and no downstream witness chain can paper over that.

## Procedural steps

Per [implementation/next-stretch.md §2D](../implementation/next-stretch.md#2d-packaging-skeleton-nix-flake):

1. Author `flake.nix`, `default.nix`, `flake.lock` at the repo root.
2. The flake's `.#default` output produces a Python wheel via poetry2nix or equivalent.
3. Bit-identical across two builds on the same host.
  Use `diffoscope` to assert.
4. CI runs the build twice and asserts hash equality.

## Enables next

v0.3's trusting-trust full machinery (per [next-stretch.md §3A](../implementation/next-stretch.md#3a-trusting-trust-full-machinery), grounded in [`witness-as-cell`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/witness-as-cell.md) and [`witness-chain-as-proof-object`](../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/witness-chain-as-proof-object.md)) requires the reproducible build *first* — the witnessed-toolchain cell schema records the SHA of the toolchain that produced the SHA of the wheel.
If either step is non-reproducible, the witness is meaningless. v0.3's self-hosting threshold (3C) closes the loop: the substrate's own dispatch shell builds the substrate, and the diverse-double-compiling check from 3A verifies the result.
