# Split v0.2 into v0.2a and v0.2b

## What

The v0.2 milestone in [milestones/v0.2-os-class.md](../milestones/v0.2-os-class.md) is split into two sub-milestones with separate ship dates and conformance gates:

**v0.2a — Multi-user single-node** (target ~3 weeks from v0.1 shipping):

- Multi-user authoring (personnel, sessions per personnel, capability fibers per personnel).
- Authoring-boundary recording on every cell.
- Per-session sandbox-fuel and per-personnel daily-fuel budgets per [decisions/cost-is-lawvere-quantale](cost-is-lawvere-quantale.md).
- Inter-session messaging (the binary-cell path per [specs/kernel/binary-cell-emission.md](../specs/kernel/binary-cell-emission.md)).
- Per-stratum projection materialisation per [specs/kernel/projection-naturality.md](../specs/kernel/projection-naturality.md).
- Inconsistency-repair lifecycle per [specs/kernel/inconsistency-repair.md](../specs/kernel/inconsistency-repair.md).
- Bootstrap-users and fiber-cache reconcile in the boot sequence.
- The σ↔Δ outer-iteration discipline (already in [specs/kernel/sigma-delta-passes.md](../specs/kernel/sigma-delta-passes.md)).
- Conformance fixtures (t02-NNf paired) for each of the above.

**v0.2b — Drivers, observability, packaging-prep** (target ~4 weeks after v0.2a ships):

- Driver model and the `extract` / `apply` / `compose` faithful-interfacer-functor discipline.
- Observability: log-aggregation, error-investigation, governance/incident-management.
- Packaging-prep: Nix flake skeleton (without the trusting-trust-bootstrap-path full machinery, which is v0.3).
- Stdlib verb specs ([stdlib/_index.md](../specs/stdlib/_index.md) §"Specs to be written").
- The federation-stub specs per [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md) (stubs only).

The federation full-spec, self-hosting, and trusting-trust-bootstrap-path machinery are explicitly **out of scope for both v0.2a and v0.2b**; they are v0.3+ per [decisions/trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md](trusting-trust-bootstrap-path.md).

## Why

The original v0.2 plan listed ~25 task IDs across six concern areas.
Three problems with shipping it as one milestone:

1. **The ship date depends on the slowest concern.** Drivers (which require the faithful-interfacer-functor machinery and a real driver implementation per substrate) and observability (which require a UI surface) are the long-pole concerns; multi-user (which is mostly substrate-discipline and capability-fiber work) is shorter.
  Tying multi-user's ship target to the long pole delays the captain's testing of the most-impactful surface.
2. **The conformance gate set is mixed.** Multi-user's gates are about authoring discipline (every cell carries `authored-by-boundary`, `authored-in-session`, `authored-by-personnel`); drivers' gates are about doctrine fidelity (the counit law on `extract ∘ apply`).
  A single milestone ship pass must clear both; partial passes leave the milestone in limbo.
3. **The review surface is too large.** Reviewing 25 task IDs at once is a half-day to a day of captain attention; reviewing 12 then 13 separately is two ~2–3 hour reviews, each with sharper focus.

The split aligns with the user's review feedback (critique #1 in the gap-survey Round 2 appendix). v0.2a is the "we can run multi-user workflows" milestone; v0.2b is the "we can plug in real drivers and observe what's happening" milestone.

The split is **convenience-class** per [decisions/decision-precedence.md](decision-precedence.md) — it does not change any substrate algebra or capability claim, only the milestone packaging.

## What it rules out

- **Mixing v0.2a and v0.2b task IDs in a single ship pass.** The ship target for v0.2a is independent of v0.2b; promoting a v0.2b spec to "normative" before v0.2a ships is non-conformant with this decision.
- **Promising v0.2 deliverables without specifying a or b.** External communications (release notes, captain status updates, library writeups) reference v0.2a or v0.2b explicitly; "v0.2" alone is ambiguous.
- **Adding new task IDs to v0.2a after its ship date.** Post-ship work goes to v0.2b or a follow-up.

## What it does not constrain

- **The exact task ID partition.** [milestones/v0.2a-multi-user-single-node.md](../milestones/v0.2a-multi-user-single-node.md) and [milestones/v0.2b-drivers-observability-packaging.md](../milestones/v0.2b-drivers-observability-packaging.md) carry the partition; this decision says *to split*, not *which IDs go where*.
- **The ship dates.** The "~3 weeks" and "~4 weeks after" are targets; the captain may adjust per implementation reality.
- **Whether v0.2b can ship in parallel with v0.3 algebra work.** Once v0.2a ships, v0.3 algebra work (federation full-spec, trusting-trust-bootstrap-path implementation) MAY proceed in parallel with v0.2b; the milestones are not strictly sequential.
- **Renaming.** This decision uses "v0.2a" / "v0.2b"; the captain may rename to "v0.2.1" / "v0.2.2" or other scheme without amending this decision.
