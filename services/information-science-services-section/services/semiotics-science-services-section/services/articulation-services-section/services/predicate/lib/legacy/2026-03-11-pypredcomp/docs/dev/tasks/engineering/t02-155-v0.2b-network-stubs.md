## Grounding

Theory row 8 (capability/authz as modal restriction) and row 10 (federation merge as $\odot$) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md).
Peer trust is "which other nodes' witness-signatures count as authority for which cells" — a per-peer scope predicate over the federation merge.
The algebra is normative now (a peer's contribution is bounded by the local node's trust-fiber for that peer, restricted via $\Rightarrow$); the wire-level trust-bootstrapping protocol defers to v0.4.
Per [decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md](../../decisions/network-spec-defer-until-implementer-named.md).
