## Grounding

Theory rows 6 (the respond primitive), 7 (fuel composition / cost), and 11 (sessions, dispatch, heartbeat) of [references/theory-for-developers.md](../../references/theory-for-developers.md).
A dashboard is structurally a *subscription to a fiber of the substrate* — the fiber projecting "things the captain looks at".
Its refresh discipline is the fiber's heartbeat-driven coherence.
Library grounding:

- [`predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra`](../../../../../../../../../theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md) — the subscription target IS a fiber-algebra object; the spec inherits the fiber's coherence laws.
- [`monotonic-clock-discipline`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/monotonic-clock-discipline.md) — the heartbeat is monotonic; refresh ticks are content-addressed against monotonic ord, not wall-clock.
- [`telemetry-as-continuous-valued-settling-projection`](../../../../../../../../../theories/engineering/theories/tech/theories/computing/theories/predicated-computing/theorems/telemetry-as-continuous-valued-settling-projection.md) — what the dashboard renders IS the projection's settled image at the latest heartbeat.
- Decision: round-4 §1.6 named the dashboard-as-subscription discipline; without recording it, v0.2b dashboards spec authoring would (by reflex) ship "WebSocket pushes JSON" — a per-dashboard ad-hoc subscription protocol that loses the unified heartbeat-fiber surface.
