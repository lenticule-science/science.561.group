# Tabasheer

TABASHEER entered the bestiary as the shard left in the hollow of the cut
CULM — the piece from which the whole endeavor can be read. The
alchemical-line note found it is a real materia medica, 天竺黄
tiānzhúhuáng. This note goes to the molecular structure, where three
properties bear directly on the role the bestiary assigned the material.
The mineralogy inquiry note filed today carries the literature checks;
its opal-kinetics correction is incorporated below.

## What it is

Tabasheer is amorphous hydrated silica, SiO₂·nH₂O, with substantial water
content — opal-A, the least ordered opal — and one of the only
macroscopic mineral concretions any plant produces. It forms when
silica-supersaturated fluid in a bamboo's internodal cavity, the same
transpiration-stream silica that elsewhere becomes phytoliths,
precipitates in place over the culm's life.

So the shard is not a crystal: no lattice, no long-range order. The
bestiary's phrase "the crystal left in the hollow" is materially wrong,
and the error turns out to matter — tabasheer is glass-family, young,
wet, disordered silica, and being pre-crystalline is what puts it on the
road described in the diagenesis section below.

## Porosity

Tabasheer is strikingly porous — an open silica network threaded with
voids, structurally akin to synthetic silica gel, and like silica gel it
adsorbs liquids and vapors into its pore network. The stone drinks; this
is much of why it interested both traditional pharmacology and early
modern chemists.

For the bestiary: the evidence-shard a finished endeavor leaves is not
inert — it keeps absorbing after harvest. The design consequence, so far
unwired: tabasheer-carriers in the grove product should have an append
surface, not just a read surface, so that when a harvested skill is
re-invoked, the new run's witness soaks into the shard rather than
scattering.

## Optics

Tabasheer's most celebrated property, studied by David Brewster in the
1810s: its refractive index is extraordinarily low for a solid, close to
water's. Dry, the porous shard is opaque white — the silica-air pore
interfaces scatter light. Soaked, water fills the pores, the index
mismatch at the internal interfaces nearly vanishes, and the stone turns
translucent; drained, it clouds again, reversibly.

In the grove, water is attention, and the mapping needs no forcing: dry
evidence is opaque, attended evidence goes clear, and content is lost in
neither state. This is the phenomenology of every archive — intact and
unreadable until someone soaks it — and it relocates the payoff of
provenance from write-time (we recorded it) to read-time (it clears when
consulted). The bamboo note already made re-invocation-with-evidence the
test of the extraction claim; the optics restate it: unsoaked tabasheer
stays cloudy.

## Diagenesis, corrected

Opal-A matures: under burial and time it reorganizes to opal-CT
(cristobalite-tridymite stacking, the first order) and eventually to
chalcedony or microcrystalline quartz. Water is expelled; disorder
anneals into lattice. Tabasheer is early-stage material on the same road
whose far end the fourth regime names — jade's larval form in the sense
that the difference between the shard and the deeply settled stone is
pressure and time.

The timescales constrain the reading, per the mineralogy inquiry's
literature check: ambient marine-regime maturation runs 10–65 million
years; the ~1,900-year figure sometimes quoted applies only under
sustained hydrothermal heat. So the earlier suggestion that an archive's
old records "mature like opal" is wrong by about six orders of magnitude
unless the archive has a heat source — some analogue of sustained
re-working — and whether any honest analogue exists (recompression,
repeated consultation and re-settling of old records) is an open
question, not an assumption.

## The TCM cross-link

As tiānzhúhuáng, tabasheer's traditional indications are clearing heat
and transforming phlegm — dealing with turbid accumulation by clarifying
it. The material's actual behaviors — adsorption, and turning clear
under fluid — are physical enactments of those verbs. Nothing here bears
on clinical validity, which belongs to the medical sections; the point is
that the tradition and the bestiary assigned this material the same role
independently, and the molecular structure cooperates with both.

## Open questions

Whether re-consulted, re-compressed archives actually consolidate — the
missing heat analogue. Whether *Arundinaria* forms tabasheer at all (the
rivercane note carries this). And the append-surface design: whether
re-invoked skills accrete witness in practice, which is the condition
under which the porosity reading earns its keep.
