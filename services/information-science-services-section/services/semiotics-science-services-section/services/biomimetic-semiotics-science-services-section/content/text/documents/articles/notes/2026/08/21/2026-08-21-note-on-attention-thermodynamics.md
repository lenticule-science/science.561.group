## Correction: water ≠ ATT ≠ heat

The assertions “water is attention,” “attention is heat” and “a witnessed archive is necessarily hydrothermal” are **not mathematical identities in Obsmetrologia**. Water mass/flux, energy/heat flow Q (joules), inference compute, WIT knowledge-order covers and ATT have different carriers and units. A shared narrative about scarce resources does not supply an isomorphism or equality. This note's original propositions are retained below solely as **historical conjectures/metaphors**, not 561 Group formal law.

Landauer's kT ln 2 is a lower bound per logically irreversible bit erasure in the specified thermodynamic regime; it does not prove “one ATT equals one joule,” “one WIT equals one erased physical bit” or “a transformer attention head is canonical ATT.” Transformer softmax uses normalized exponentials and can admit a Gibbs-form mathematical comparison under selected parameters; this is a distinct algorithmic construction, not a proof that water, human focus or canonical ATT are heat.

The later [2026-08-26 audit](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/services/biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/26/2026-08-26-note-on-attention-thermodynamics-audited.md) already reports overreach. Only a specified, dimensionally consistent map to the canonical WIT/ACK/ATT carriers and checked proof may promote these conjectures.

## The archive's heat source

The tabasheer note left one question open. Opal-A matures to opal-CT and
onward to chalcedony — amorphous silica annealing toward crystal — but
the timescales split by regime: 10–65 million years under ambient marine
conditions, on the order of 1,900 years under sustained hydrothermal
heat. If archives mature like opal, they need a heat source, and none was
named.

Attention is the heat source. An attended archive is a hydrothermal
system: re-consultation is hot water circulating through the porous
record, carrying dissolved witness, accelerating the anneal by four to
five orders of magnitude. And the hydrothermal number lands on a
recognizable scale — roughly two thousand years is the age of the mature
textual traditions. Hanzi, continuously read, copied, and commented for
three millennia, is the jade-stage script of the writing-systems note,
and this gives its jade a mechanism: uninterrupted hydrothermal
attention. The scripts that lost their readers — Linear A, rongorongo —
stayed opal: intact, cloudy, unreadable, exactly as the tabasheer optics
predict for the unsoaked stone. Decipherment is soaking; Linear B went
transparent the year attention flooded back.

## Glass, quench, anneal

Glass science supplies the estate's failure taxonomy directly. Glass
forms when a melt cools faster than it can crystallize — quenching
freezes disorder in place. Crystal needs slow cooling under sustained
conditions. So: an abandoned project is quench glass, frozen
mid-arrangement when attention-heat was withdrawn suddenly, and brittle
the way glass is brittle. The lib/legacy staging areas are a quench
archive. Annealing — reheating and cooling slowly to relieve internal
stress — is what a patient revisit does to old work, and tempering is why
work revisited on a schedule outlasts work finished once and never
touched.

The vitrimer strand acquires its missing variable: the topology-freezing
transition below which a dynamic-covalent network stops exchanging bonds
is an attention temperature. Above it, the estate rearranges; starved
below it, it ossifies. The Deborah-number doctrine — hold the system
viscoelastic, keep the mempool molten, protect the whirling defect — is a
heat-budget requirement: something must keep circulating attention or the
lattice crystallizes shut.

Latent heat carries two facts about people that the physics states
exactly. Crystallization releases heat: when an endeavor settles, its
bound attention discharges into the surroundings, which is why a finished
thing warms everyone near it. Melting absorbs heat: un-settling a settled
position consumes attention with nothing visible to show for it, the
latent cost of unfreezing that every mediator and every reviser knows.

## The machine's side of the identity

For a transformer, attention is heat by equation, not analogy. Attention
weights are computed by softmax, and softmax is the Boltzmann
distribution: attention over keys is a Gibbs measure, thermal occupancy
of memories at a temperature set by the scaling factor, and the sampling
parameter called temperature is the same T doing the same
exploration-versus-settling work as in annealing. Deeper: modern Hopfield
theory (Ramsauer et al. 2020) identifies transformer attention with the
retrieval step of an associative memory performing energy descent, and
Hopfield networks are spin glasses. Spin-glass frustration is defined on
loops — a cycle of couplings that cannot all be satisfied — which is
holonomy failing to close, the obstruction class realized in magnets.
Parisi's Nobel-cited result is that such systems have not one ground
state but a rugged ultrametric hierarchy of metastable states: a felted
landscape, robust because it is not monocrystalline, which is the jade
note's toughening-obstruction question as fifty years of glassy-systems
physics. When the owner says attention is heat to a silicon agent, the
agent's own update rule is the proof.

One practical corollary: spaced repetition is a kiln schedule. FSRS-style
