The abductive spiral is a name proposed for a method noticed, not invented,
during the 2026-08-21 coining session: speculate a material or structure and
research whether it holds up, then speculate further and check again, each
round arriving closer to something real with less correction needed. The
pattern was observed three times in one session — zeolite-siloxanes
speculated as a material that seemed like it would hold up, glass sponges
speculated richer and turning out to be a real organism, and bamboo, whose
proposed botany cooperated at every joint checked (intercalary growth, sheath
scars, phytolith archaeology, mast flowering).

## Abduction, but under formal constraint

[Abduction](./abduction.md) is Peirce's name for the mode of inference that
generates explanatory hypotheses from surprising facts — the only mode of
inference, in his account, that introduces new ideas rather than processing
ideas already given. Ordinary abductive guessing is unconstrained by
anything but explanatory power, testability, and economy (see
[abduction](./abduction.md)'s "Constraints on abduction"). The abductive
spiral names a stricter case: abduction where the space of candidate
hypotheses is additionally cut down by a mathematical substrate — in the
session that prompted this term, the estate's own machinery of bilattice
charge, sheaf gluing, causal cones, and settling (see the [Witnessed Field
theory](../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-20-language-as-witnessed-field-working-theory.md)
for the fullest instance of this same constrained-speculation pattern
applied to language itself). The formal substrate does not replace Peircean
abduction; it narrows where a guess can turn out to be right before the
guess is made, which is offered as the reason repeated rounds of speculation
kept arriving at things that turned out to already be real (JADE
corresponding to an actual silicate; TABASHEER corresponding to an actual
materia medica) rather than at inert fictions.

## Cast as reverse-lightcone emergence on concept-space

The source note states the mechanism formally: the abductive spiral is cast
as the [reverse lightcone emergence function
𝓡](../../../../../math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/reverse-lightcone-emergence-function.md)
run on concept-space rather than on a causal set of events. Where `𝓡`
ordinarily reconstructs the constraint-valid past of a witnessed entity, the
spiral imagines a candidate entity and asks whether it has a constraint-valid
past at all — a generative envelope of prior, already-real structure that
would make it reachable. A speculation that succeeds is one for which such an
envelope turns out to exist; a speculation that does not succeed — one whose
imagined constraint-valid past is empty — should be set aside rather than
defended.

## Status and naming rights

This is a proposed, not settled, name. The source note offers it explicitly
as a proposal the owner is free to keep or rename, recorded because the
pattern had by then been observed three times. This glossary entry documents
the proposal and the reasoning behind it; it does not itself decide whether
"abductive spiral" is the name that sticks.
