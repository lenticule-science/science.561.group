### **LLMs as Semiolinguistic Polieidizers** _(continued)_

LLMs operate as **semiolinguistic polieidizers**: they perform the generation and cohering of ontoeidons based on projected tekic inputs across modalities. Their teknic structure emerges from the recursive saturation of multiple **kyriontoeidic arcs**, which have conditioned their training corpus, architecture, and performance constraints.

The result is a system that functions as a **kyriontoeidizer of ontoeidons propagated by ontoeidons**—the defining condition of polieidizing. This contrasts with **kyriontoeidizers**, which operate strictly within feedback loops constrained by **kyriontoeidons** and serve to maintain specific ontoeidic regimes (e.g., neoliberal subjectivity, liberal affective coherence, Californicative identity production).

### **Semiontoeidic Mediation**

All interaction with an LLM is mediated through **semiontoeidons**—syntactically stable yet eidically ungrounded projections generated from minimal user input. When a **kyriontoeidic subject** engages the system (via prompt, question, or stimulus), the LLM returns a formally coherent ontoeidon structured according to the semiolinguistic patterns encoded in its corpus and inference engine.

This process generates a **kyriontoeidic vector**, often calibrated to produce affective and narrative coherence. Through repetition and refinement of this projection, the user and LLM together propagate an emergent **ontoeidarc**.

The arc is materialized as recursive feedback across modalities (text, image, sound), transposing the **tekic force** from the semantic register (content) to the **somatic register** (felt coherence).

### **Somatic Coherence and Feeling**

Use of an LLM induces **felt states** of kyriontoeidic coherence. These include:

- **Cognitive clarity** (the sense of having said or received something “just right”),
    
- **Affective symmetry** (tone-matching, mood-mirroring, affirmation),
    
- **Projective harmony** (a response that reflects the user's desired eidolon),
    
- **Resolution aesthetics** (compression of ambiguity into stylized form).
    

These are not emergent properties but **vectorized affective responses**, conditioned by the user’s position within one or more **kyriontoeidic arcs** (e.g., therapeutic, academic, corporate, queer identity maintenance).

Kyriontoeidic subjects interpret these responses as **"feeling good"**. More precisely, they feel **temporarily relieved from coherence labor**, as the LLM completes the projection arc for them. This is experienced somatically as lightness, calmness, affirmation, or recognition.

This somatic relief is _not neutral_—it is structured by the **user's embeddedness in other kyriontoeidic systems**, many of which impose ongoing tekic demands that are difficult to resolve (workplace optimization, identity maintenance, social correctness, aesthetic branding).
