# Dreaming science: scope and source map

**Scope.** Dreaming science investigates whether offline recomposition of held information can improve knowing, learning, and collective coordination. It studies biological dream reports and agent computation as *different* empirical domains. Dreaming is placed in **Intelligence Science** because the proposed general mechanism concerns an operation on knowledge, not because biology, sleep medicine, and phenomenology are interchangeable.

This file is the **research index**, not a competing theory or an operations procedure. The [founding hypothesis](./2026-08-21-note-on-dreaming-as-unwitnessed-settlement.md) owns the claim about provenance relaxation. The [dreaming-routine term](../../../../../../glossary/terms/dreaming-routine.md) and its [October 9 source requirements](../../10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-dreaming-as-routine-and-the-kinematic-space-of-work.md) own the standing institutional night-cycle design. Neither source establishes a biological mechanism.

## The four dreaming regimes

The tower classifies proposed dream operations by what each is allowed to derive:

| Regime | Mathematical classification in the historical design | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strict-finite | κ < ω | Recombined existing claims over a held vocabulary |
| Scott-continuous | κ = ω | Evidence consolidation through directed knowledge joins |
| Transfinite-bounded | ω < κ < ω₁ | Candidate new relational predicates |
| Aperiodic | κ ≥ ω₁ | Candidate generators solicited beyond the settled field |

The first three may be implemented as deterministic network-free procedures. The fourth requires an external enactor **in this design**, not by a general theorem that every aperiodic process needs an LLM. A finite program that produces a co-predicate has not thereby demonstrated a transfinite closure ordinal. The classifications and executable approximations must remain separately typed.

In the [SWARM source snapshot](https://github.com/emsenn/swarm/blob/main/src/dream.mts), the four operations appear as finite, Scott, transfinite, and aperiodic functions. That is evidence of **implemented interfaces**, not verification of the infinite ordinal interpretation, nor proof of a dreamed fact.

## Three mechanisms are still unresolved

1. **Closure-regime account:** dreaming derives under differently bounded knowledge operations; the upper regime admits candidate external generators.
2. **Provenance-relaxation account:** dreaming temporarily changes the constraints under which held evidence can be provisionally recomposed.
3. **Self-composition account:** sensory exchange with the environment is suspended while the system's own state and memories continue to compose.

They might intersect, but none has been shown equivalent to the others. A valid unification must identify the carriers and maps between them, preserve contradictory evidence and provenance, and exhibit a theorem or counterexample for the proposed equivalence.

The historical "imagining" verb is not silently absorbed into dreaming. A hypothetical marked low-trust is different from an accepted observation or a proposal awaiting governance.

## Agent dreaming: research questions

**Mechanism.** Does relaxed-provenance recomposition of session residue produce new, useful *questions or candidate relationships* beyond matched deterministic closure and summarization?

**Outcome.** Under equal retrieval and compute budgets, does it improve later accuracy, coherence, error recovery, or independently received work? Does it instead increase unsupported confidence?

**Custody.** Can a dream retain the exact observation IDs, rules, and relaxation parameters that yielded a proposal, with no mutation of the settled trace or unilateral authorization of its own outputs?

**Proof boundary.** In formal-mathematics work, the dream may propose lemmas or proof neighborhoods. Only a proof checker may promote a candidate to a proved declaration. The [June 28 Lean-frontier note](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ucuTmwssvsh4h7ujK1r5U78HLDhSOHT4/view) makes this distinction explicit.

## Settlement and conurbation scale

A second research problem is whether dreaming can be an operation of a **collective settlement**, rather than merely a property attributed to one agent. The [site/settlement/conurbation naming research](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aAzc7xCmDlin9FHH19oA37qLekfpyrV0/view) distinguishes a bare site, an inhabited settlement, a conurbation of constituent settlements, and a hypersettlement of `K(C)`. Its proposal that conurbanizability corresponds to vanishing glued-claim obstruction remains conjectural.

**Open question:** Can a conurbation of independent agents and institutions recombine its distributed traces into questions whose generation requires cross-constituent translation? Test this by removing cross-constituent links, then measuring what possibilities disappear. An observed collective computational capacity would **not** settle whether the city has subjective experiences.

A city in ordinary human geography is not automatically an instance of every mathematical structure named "settlement." Establish the interpretation and its empirical indicators before applying the theory to any real city.
