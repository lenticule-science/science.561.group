## Statements: dreaming as the Group's routine

**D1.** Dreaming is routine. It is not an experiment or an event. The Group requires each admitted MetroPlat node to run it as a scheduled service
loop over the records that node holds. This is an obligation, not a report that
all nodes have already deployed it. (The Leader, 2026-10-09; the founding
note's "real experiment" at `$D/.../2026-08-21-note-on-dreaming-as-unwitnessed-settlement.md:44-49`
becomes the standing practice.)

**D2.** The Group's trace of witnessed records is its semiosphere: by day work
writes cells; by night dreaming re-reads the field and opens questions; by day
again governance answers some of them (`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:56-58`).
The routine is this night cycle.

**D3.** There are four dreams, one per descent regime, ordered by generativity
(`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:40`; `index.mjs:1-27`):

| Regime | Closure | Act | Network | Emits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| finite | κ < ω | recombines held cells to the least fixed point of a closure over a fixed vocabulary | none | proposed cells |
| scott-continuous | κ = ω | consolidates repeated cells by knowledge-join to the directed-supremum limit | none | proposed consolidated claims |
| transfinite | ω < κ < ω₁ | derives new predicates from co-occurrence of predicates on a subject | none | proposed predicates (vocabulary) |
| aperiodic | κ ≥ ω₁ | channels new generators from outside the settled fibers, through an enactor | the enactor's | proposed cells with no internal fixed point |

**D4.** The three inward regimes are deterministic, replayable and cost no
money; the same window of records and the same rule set give the same proposals
(`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:46`; `2026-05-28-2026-05-28-dreaming-tick.md:23-28`).
Only the aperiodic regime calls an enactor. An enactor call is an act with a
cost, so it is admitted work with a budget and a fuel cap, carried by an
office-projected agent, and, being heavy, it runs off the laptop where the
laptop is the node (rules `sessions-dispatch-only-office-projected-agents`,
`heavy-work-runs-off-the-laptop`).

**D5.** A dream writes proposals and its own record, and nothing else. A
proposal is an inert, bottom-opened cell: a second fact that the cell was
asked, never a fifth truth value and never a determination of the cell
(`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:72-74`, citing `ontology.mjs` `openPotential`).
A dream may also leave candidate neighborhoods, decompositions, probes and
research-queue notes (`2026-06-28-dreaming-as-lean-proofing-frontier.md:26-28`).
Its own record states the window read, the regime, the rule set, the enactor if
any, and each proposal with the terms that raised it, so a dream leaves
witnessed residue and the rest evaporates rightly
(`2026-08-21-note-on-dreaming-as-unwitnessed-settlement.md:50-52`).

**D6.** A dream does not settle the trace (`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:56`).
It does not write a settled cell, articulate its own proposal, mark a theorem
proved, close an admitted declaration, or upgrade a capability without the
oracle that certifies it (`2026-06-28-dreaming-as-lean-proofing-frontier.md:29`).
The inward dreaming state narrows the dreamer's authorization to internal
composition (`2026-05-28-2026-05-28-dreaming-tick.md:49`): it sends nothing
outside the node, spends nothing and touches no credential. The separately
admitted, budgeted aperiodic enactor of D4 is not an inward dream-tick.

**D7.** Governance articulates. The office whose duty covers a proposal's
subject accepts it into the trace, declines it, or lets it lapse, and records
the judgment (rules `existing-work-is-judged-before-it-is-discarded`,
`receiving-decisions-belong-to-the-receiving-office`). The dreamer never
receives its own proposals, as the Senior Scientist never receives its own
outcome (`science-services-section/lib/agency/duties/realizing-allocated-science-work.json`, `receiver-condition`).

**D8.** Compaction and summarization can abbreviate or discard context;
the dreaming routine instead retains its input records and emits proposals.
The phrase "dreamless sleep" in the August founding note is an analogy, not
a scientific identity between memory compaction and human sleep. The routine
keeps its dream record and deletes nothing.

**D9.** A dream reads a window bounded by marks on the ledger. Until the
monotone-transaction-time migration lands, the sound window is the whole log;
an earlier `asOf` boundary is opt-in for stores that already file a sortable
time (`2026-06-05-seam-memory-dreaming.md:70`).

**D10.** A node's dream is a bounded run, not a tick stream. Its witnesses grow
with the proposals it makes, not with the clock. The node's scheduler already
wrote about 3.4 `run-due` RPCs per second, each witnessed three times, 5.8 GB on
2026-10-08, into the disk whose saturation crashes the laptop
(`561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-the-leaders-repeated-corrections.md:59-64`).
A scheduled pass that finds nothing due makes no run and writes no witness.

## Statements: the kinematic space of the Group's history and work

**K1.** A run is a diamond: its lower endpoint is its ingress (a prompt, an
allocation, a scheduled firing admitted), its upper endpoint is its settled exit
(its final witnessed effect), and its interior is the witnessed events between
(`2026-07-01-research-on-agent-run-as-charge.md`, the derivation section;
`2026-07-01-research-on-the-kinematic-space-of-runs.md:30`).

**K2.** The kinematic space of the Group's history and work is the space of its
runs. It is a causal set under sequencing: run a precedes run b when a's exit
reaches b's ingress through witnessed events. Holes, horizons and shrouds come
with it one level up, and the four closure laws are inherited
(`2026-07-01-research-on-the-kinematic-space-of-runs.md:30-34`). It is a causal
set in the sense the physics glossary states: a locally
finite partial order (`causal-set.md`).

**K3.** Nesting is not the order. A session containing subagents, a project
containing tasks, a campaign containing projects are containment, which is
carried as grouping over the parent run and never as causal precedence; a
board that draws a sub-run as a causal child draws a falsehood
(`2026-07-01-research-on-the-kinematic-space-of-runs.md:35-38`). Runs of runs
(campaigns, epics) carry the same structure at every level (`:39-40`).

**K4.** A live run is not yet a diamond. It is a lower endpoint and a growing
witnessed interior. A run whose session is killed, whose peer goes dark or
whose readout disconnects keeps its lower endpoint and interior and acquires no
invented exit; the structure requires no upper endpoint at admission
(`2026-07-01-research-on-the-kinematic-space-of-runs.md:55-62`).

**K5.** A move is one run of one skill by one office holder: a skill is one step
of judgment with a declared output or a structured error, and every dispatch is
witnessed with its verb and subject
(`science-services-section/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/skill.md`;
`2026-07-01-research-on-agent-run-as-charge.md:62`). Sessions, runbooks, tasks and
projects are runs composed of moves.

**K6.** The space contains exactly the moves that are witnessed. Work that was
done and not recorded is a hole in the space, not an absence of work. The Leader:
"you'd run the command, the output would be a content addressed Resource
associated with the Project, and thus when you hand off Work from the Project to
other Agents, they can find it just the same"
(`2026-10-09-note-on-the-leaders-repeated-corrections.md:56-58`). The work board
"saw almost no moves after about 2026-10-08 07:00" (`:64`) while sessions
worked: that stretch of the Group's history is a hole in its kinematic space,
and the space of the work done then is recoverable only from what the sessions
left in the corpus.

**K7.** A scheduled firing that does nothing is not a run. A dream, a receiving
of a pull request, a dispatch of an agent and a Leader message that opens an
allocation are runs, and each is a point in the space.

## Statements: the causal set of an agent's moves from its capabilities

**C1.** A move type is the source quadruple (incumbency, office, duty, skill).
An incumbency binds its holder to an office
(`science-services-section/lib/agency/incumbencies/bare-cedar-fog-claude-as-senior-scientist.json`);
the office bears duties (`senior-scientist.json`, `bears-duty`) and provides
skills (`provides-skill`); a duty names its discharging skills
(`realizing-allocated-science-work.json`, `skills`); the skill states its
required capabilities and input/output contract (`research/SKILL.md`). The
source’s rule `no-work-outside-an-office-duty-and-skill` remains the admission
condition: a session does no Group work that no office it holds or dispatches
bears as a duty realized by a skill. The applicable rule’s current identity
and scope must be resolved by its owner; the mathematical correction here
does not remove that institutional constraint. Capability, authority, input
and resource conditions additionally constrain the occurrence’s admission. A bounded source snapshot yields a
finite candidate type set; its actual registry must be derived from the
agency records rather than copied into a second hand-kept list.

**C2.** A duty's `to` can establish another duty's `from`, including receiving
conditions. These dependencies define a type graph, not automatically a
partial order. Review and revision can revisit the same types. Distinct
holders do not rule out cycles. Use either a demonstrated acyclic type graph
or explicitly indexed occurrences whose evidenced prerequisites are acyclic.
Containment remains grouping, as in K3.

**C3.** Each real move retains its type reference and its actual prerequisite
occurrences. An order-preserving instance map into a type-level poset requires
that poset and preservation law to be constructed; it does not follow from
the quadruple alone. An available candidate occurrence discharges a particular
open obligation, has its own received prerequisites and satisfied admission
conditions, and has no equivalent active occurrence. A previous instance of
the same type does not suppress a new cycle or revised-input occurrence.

**C4.** The board reads admitted obligations, agency types and recorded
occurrences together. It distinguishes available candidates, in-flight runs,
outputs awaiting the required receiving, and received outcomes. A failed
terminal attempt does not fulfill its parent goal. The applicable receiver
must be read from the current contract; a self-check cannot silently count as
independent receiving. Source conflict: the Science research skill says its
producer does not receive its own outcome, while the current
`realizing-allocated-science-work.json` describes producer check/merge as
receiving with later CSO review. This unresolved policy difference must remain
visible to the consumer rather than be decided by this mathematical note.

The active [kinematic term](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kinematic-space-of-work.md)
states the occurrence, containment and capability-slice contract. The previous
type-poset assertion remains in the [prior source revision](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/aa46e2f11aff49635bfbd7a381ee4c8b261db63c/services/information-science-services-section/services/intelligence-science-services-section/services/dreaming-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-dreaming-as-routine-and-the-kinematic-space-of-work.md) as provenance.

**C5.** A dream is a move under a dreaming duty held by an office on the node. It
precedes the governance move that articulates its proposals (D7), so
dreaming sits in the causal set before the work its proposals open and never
inside it as a settled step.

# Requirements for engineering

## Requirements (EARS)

Dreaming as a routine of each MetroPlat node:

- **R1** (D1) Where a MetroPlat node is running, the node shall run a dreaming
  routine on a schedule over the records it holds.
- **R2** (D3) When the routine fires, the node shall run the finite, scott-continuous
  and transfinite dreams in that order over the window of D9.
- **R3** (D4) While a dream of the finite, scott-continuous or transfinite regime
  runs, the node shall make no network call and no enactor call.
- **R4** (D4) The node shall produce byte-identical proposals when it re-runs an
  inward dream over the same window with the same rule set.
- **R5** (D4) Where an aperiodic dream is configured, the node shall run it only
  as admitted work with a declared budget and fuel cap, under an office-projected
  agent, and shall not run it on the laptop host.
- **R6** (D5) When a dream produces a proposal, the node shall record it as a
  bottom-opened cell carrying the terms that raised it, its regime, and the dream
  record that produced it.
- **R7** (D5) When a dream finishes, the node shall write one dream record naming
  the window, the regime, the rule set, the enactor if any, and every proposal.
- **R8** (D6) The node shall leave every settled section of the trace
  byte-identical across a dream, apart from the dream record and its proposals.
- **R9** (D6) The node shall refuse a dream's attempt to settle a cell, articulate
  its own proposal, mark a declaration proved or upgrade a capability, and shall
  record each refusal.
- **R10** (D6) While a dream runs, the node shall confine the dreamer to
  internal composition rules, with no outbound message, spend or credential change.
- **R11** (D7) When a proposal is recorded, the node shall make it receivable by
  the office whose duty covers its subject, and shall not let the dreaming office
  receive it.
- **R12** (D7) When an office accepts, declines or lets a proposal lapse, the
  node shall record the judgment with the proposal.
- **R13** (D8) The node shall delete no record in consequence of a dream.
- **R14** (D9) Where the store does not file a monotone transaction time, the
  node shall read the whole log as the dream's window.
- **R15** (D10, K7) When the routine fires and finds nothing to dream over, the
  node shall write no run and no witness.
- **R16** (D10) The node shall bound the witnesses a dream writes by the number of
  proposals it makes plus a constant, independent of how long the dream runs.
- **R17** (C1, C5) Where the node is to run the routine, the Group's agency
  records shall hold an office, a duty and a skill under which the node's dream is
  a move; until they exist the routine shall not be enabled.

The work board as the causal set:

- **R18** (K1, K4) When an agent run is admitted, the board shall record its lower
  endpoint, and shall not require an upper endpoint.
- **R19** (K1, K4) When a run is killed, a peer goes dark or a readout disconnects,
  the board shall keep the run's lower endpoint and interior and shall not
  record an exit it did not witness.
- **R20** (K2) The board shall order runs by sequencing, meaning one run's exit
  reaches another's ingress through witnessed events.
- **R21** (K3) The board shall present containment (session, subagent, project,
  campaign) as grouping and shall never present it as precedence.
- **R22** (K5) When an agent dispatches a skill, the board shall record the move
  with its verb, its subject, the incumbency, the office, the duty and the skill.
- **R23** (K6) When a command's output is produced inside a project's work, the
  board shall bind it to the project as a content-addressed resource.
- **R24** (K6) While a stretch of time holds sessions' work and no board moves, the
  board shall show that stretch as a hole in the space and shall not show it as
  idle.
- **R25** (C1) The board shall derive the set of move types for each incumbency
  from the agency records (incumbency, office, duty, skill, required capabilities)
  without a second hand-kept list.
- **R26** (C2) The board shall derive type dependencies from duty `to`/`from`
  contracts, preserve their source basis, and distinguish a cyclic type graph
  from the acyclic causal order of actual occurrences. Required receiving
  shall be represented as its own occurrence under the applicable contract.
- **R27** (C3) When a move is recorded that is an instance of no move type, the
  board shall mark it as outside method and open a revision against the owning
  record.
- **R28** (C3, C4) The board shall show a candidate occurrence as available
  when a specific open obligation calls for it, its own prerequisites and
  admission conditions hold, and no equivalent active occurrence carries it.
  An earlier occurrence of its type shall not suppress legitimate recurrence.
- **R29** (C4) The board shall classify each card as available, in flight,
  awaiting receipt or received from the witnessed records alone.
- **R30** (C5) The board shall place a dream before the move that articulates its
  proposals.

## Specification

1. **Run record.** `run-id` (content address), `incumbency`, `office`, `duty`,
   `skill`, `ingress` (event reference), `exit` (event reference or absent),
   `interior` (ordered witnessed event references), `parent-run` (grouping only),
   `resources` (content addresses). A run with no `exit` is in flight.
2. **Sequencing.** `a` precedes `b` iff `a.exit` reaches `b.ingress` in the
   witnessed-event causal set; `parent-run` is never read as precedence.
3. **Move type and occurrence.** `(incumbency, office, duty, skill)` comes from
   the agency records. Type dependencies retain the relevant duty `to`/`from`
   contract. An occurrence names its input, open obligation, actual prerequisite
   occurrences and applicable receiver. Cyclic type dependencies require
   occurrence indexing before being read as causal precedence.
4. **Dream record.** `window` (start mark, end mark), `regime`, `rule-set`
   (content address), `enactor` (or absent), `proposals` (list), `refusals`
   (list), `witness-count`.
5. **Proposal.** `cell` (subject, predicate, object), `status` bottom-opened,
   `raised-by` (terms or witnesses), `dream` (dream record address), `judgment`
   (absent, then accepted, declined or lapsed, with the judging office).
6. **Board position.** `available` is assessed per open obligation and candidate
   occurrence under R28; `in-flight` means an admitted occurrence lacks an
   evidenced exit; `awaiting-receipt` means its required assessment is missing;
   `received` requires that assessment. Terminal attempt outcome, successful
   goal satisfaction and commitment state remain separately readable.

## Design constraints

- Science fixes the semantics here. Engineering derives generic tools from them;
  operations parameterizes per node (schedule, window, enactor budget); the
  Group and public services parameterize operations. No engineering artifact
  carries a section's data or a brand (rule
  `science-engineering-operations-parameterize-in-order`).
- The routine reuses the existing scheduler and the existing knowing dreaming

## Receiving conditions

The CEngO, or the office it names, receives the work when all of these hold on a
running node, observed from its records:

1. Two consecutive scheduled firings over the same window yield identical
   proposals for each inward regime, and the process made no network call (R3, R4).
2. The hash of every settled section before and after a dream is equal apart from
   the dream record and proposals; an attempt to settle through a dream is
   refused and recorded (R8, R9).
3. A node idle for a day writes no dream run and no scheduler witness for the
   firings that found nothing (R15), and a dream's witness count does not grow
   with its duration (R16).
4. A proposal is receivable by a non-dreaming office, and its judgment is stored
   with it (R11, R12).
5. For the Senior Scientist incumbency, derived move types resolve to the
   actual agency records. A review→revision→review example repeats types
   without creating a cycle among occurrences; the receiver-policy conflict
   is reported rather than silently resolved (R25, R26).
6. A session whose work left witnesses but no board moves appears as a hole, and
   a recorded move with no move type is marked outside method (R24, R27).
7. A killed run keeps its lower endpoint and has no exit (R19), and a session
   drawn with subagents shows grouping, not precedence (R21).
8. The cards of a project classify into available, in flight, awaiting receipt
   and received from the records alone (R28, R29).
