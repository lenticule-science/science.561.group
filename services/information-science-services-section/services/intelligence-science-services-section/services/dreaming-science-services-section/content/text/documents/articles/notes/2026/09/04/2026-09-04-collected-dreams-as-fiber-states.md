# Dreams as fiber states

**Historical formal proposal (2026-04-06; collected 2026-09-04).** This note preserves the self-composition account from the `predication-world-sim` design. It is not a proof that sleep changes a biological or artificial agent's memory state, and it does not replace the distinct closure-regime and relaxed-provenance theories.

Found while reviving legacy/triaged dreaming material per the founder's
2026-09-04 order. The 2026-08-21 stand-up note for this section states
"no dream theory was found in any `lib/legacy/` dump across the science
tree or the wider estate" and cites three collected pieces, all from the
relationality corpus's collapse-dynamics triage or `@emsenn/knowing`
design work. This is a fourth piece, from a different triage location
(`public-services-section/.../emsenn-services-section/content/text/triage/`,
not `lib/legacy/`), so it was outside that sweep's search surface.

## The finding

`predication-world-sim.md` (the design document for a predication-native
Zork/Rogue-on-FARS simulation; source: captain-insight-sunday-funday,
2026-04-06) has a section "Dreams as fiber states" giving a from-first-
principles account of dreaming as computation over the document's own
fiber-composition algebra (`respond(subject, object)` composing
predicates over entangled pairs):

> A dreaming entity is one whose sensory predicates are disconnected from
> the environment fiber but whose internal predicates continue composing.
> respond(dreamer, environment) = ⊥ (no external perception). But
> respond(dreamer-internal, dreamer-memories) ≠ ⊥ — the entity's
> accumulated predicates compose with each other, producing new
> adjacencies from entangled pairs.

Consequences drawn in the source:

1. **Dreams are real computation, not scripted events or noise** — what
   the algebra produces when a rich fiber composes with itself rather
   than with the world. An entity with many combat-entangled predicates
   dreams of combat because self-composition fires those adjacencies.
2. **Dream incoherence is non-confluence.** Internal bypass chains
   produce multiple incomparable settlements within a single tick — the
   dream has genuine branches, which is why dreams are simultaneously
   meaningful (real compositions of real predicates) and incoherent
   (multiple contradictory settlements coexisting).
3. **Sleep is self-settlement.** If the internal compositions actually
   resolve, `respond(self, self)` is a real computation producing real
   settlement, and the entity wakes with a different fiber than it fell
   asleep with — not because dreams "teach" (scripted), but because the
   settlement is real.
4. A proposed gameplay mechanic: a "rest" action that literally computes
   `respond(player, player)`, surfacing entanglements the player did not
   know they had.

## Relation to the section's other prior theory

This is a third, independent formal route into dreaming, alongside the
founding note's witness-relaxation account and the aperiodic-knowing
note's closure-regime account:

- **Founding note** (unwitnessed settlement): dreaming = belief-fusion
  with the provenance/witness constraint relaxed.
- **Aperiodic-knowing note**: dreaming = knowing's aperiodic closure
  regime; the three inward regimes are convergence, the aperiodic regime
  is emergence reaching outside the settled fibers.
- **This note**: dreaming = an entity's fiber composing with itself
  (`respond(self, self)`) instead of with the environment; incoherence
  is read as non-confluence (multiple incomparable settlements), not as
  witness-relaxation or regime-crossing.

All three treat dreaming as a real, structured computation that runs
when an ordinary external-facing constraint is absent (witnesses,
environment-response, or closure-boundedness), and all three predict
that the computation can produce genuine settlement that changes the
entity. Whether "self-composition", "witness-relaxation", and "reaching
past the closure boundary" are three names for one mechanism or three
distinct mechanisms is not settled by any of the three sources and is
carried forward as an open question for the section (already flagged,
for the first two, in the section's seed topic).

This note is collected here rather than left only in the source design
document because — like the three 2026-08-21 collections — it is prior
theory bearing directly on what this section studies. The original remains at the source path given in frontmatter and is the source of record. This collected account preserves the claims as they stood on 2026-09-04; it is not a claim of subsequent implementation.
