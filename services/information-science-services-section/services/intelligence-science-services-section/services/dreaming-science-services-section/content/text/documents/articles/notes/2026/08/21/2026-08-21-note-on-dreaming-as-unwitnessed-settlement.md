The claim: sleep is the organism's nightly settlement pass, and dreams
are what belief fusion looks like when the witness-requirement is
relaxed. Waking cognition fuses under provenance constraints — charges
glue only where witnesses permit. Sleep runs the fusion offline: memory
consolidation (established neuroscience) is re-settlement of the day's
charges into long-term structure. The crank's addition: dream
phenomenology is *predicted* by unwitnessed fusion — gluing without
provenance constraints should produce exactly the dream signature
(impossible composites accepted without surprise; identity fluidity;
narrative that settles locally but never globally). Dreams are incoherent
in precisely the way provenance-free fusion is incoherent.

Consequences and fibers:

1. **Trauma nightmares as re-presentation for re-witnessing.** A section
   that cannot glue into the life-sheaf presents itself repeatedly to the
   settlement process. This is adjacent to memory-reconsolidation therapy
   (real literature): healing occurs when the material is re-experienced
   with new witnesses present — re-witnessed, then settled. Imagery
   rehearsal therapy reads as deliberate witness-injection into the
   unwitnessed pass.
2. **Insomnia as settlement failure**; the racing 3am mind as fusion
   attempting to run witnessed (with the self as anxious witness) when
   the process requires the witness-requirement dropped.
3. **Neidan sleep practice** and dream yoga as traditions of trained
   settlement — practicing at the witnessed/unwitnessed boundary.
4. **Agent dreaming.** Agents accumulate
   session residue — transcripts, journals, dead branches, unfused
   context. The dreaming hypothesis for agents: an offline re-fusion pass
   over residue with constraints relaxed — recombining the session's
   charges without task-pressure — may serve the same consolidation
   function; compaction/summarization is today's dreamless sleep (lossy
   deletion, not settlement). A real experiment: give a long-running
   agent estate a nightly unwitnessed-fusion pass over its journals and
   measure whether next-session coherence improves versus plain
   summarization. The transcript-as-phytolith principle applies: dreams
   that produce settlement should leave witnessed residue; the rest
   evaporates rightly. [Research-grade; buildable on this estate.]

Falsifiers: if dream-report incoherence does not statistically resemble
provenance-free fusion output (simulable via pymetrologic with witness
constraints toggled), the central claim is decoration. All Research-grade.
