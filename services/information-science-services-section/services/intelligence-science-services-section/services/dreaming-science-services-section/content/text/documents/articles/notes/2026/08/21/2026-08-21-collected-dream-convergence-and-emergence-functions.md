# Dream convergence and emergence: historical formal sketches

**Source status.** This is a preserved reading of two older relationality notes, not a proved theorem or a typed scientific model. The source notes have no known creation date. The collection date is **not** the date the original formulas were derived. The original symbols are retained below; the missing typing and interpretation obligations are made explicit.

## Convergence: Ξᵣ

The source proposes that multiple systems can continue to orient toward a compatible but unrealized future without collapsing their differences. Its informal interpretation is **mutual stabilization toward a shared unrealized possibility**.

Original expression, retained as written:

```text
Ξᵣ := ∃ {xᵢ → z} : ∀ i, j: Dᵣ(xᵢ → z) ∧ M(xᵢ, xⱼ)
```

**Formalization gap.** The source does not define the type of `xᵢ` or `z`, what an arrow `xᵢ → z` denotes, whether `Dᵣ` is a predicate or a process, or the domain and properties of `M` (mutual compatibility). The quantifier scope also needs to be stated unambiguously. Until then, `Ξᵣ` is a candidate condition, not a well-formed theorem.

## Emergence: Dᵣ

The source proposes that anticipation persists in the presence of mismatch or error even when the desired state cannot be realized under the current constraints. Its informal reading is **persistence of an anticipated possibility without available settlement**.

Original expression, retained as written:

```text
Dᵣ(x → z) := A(x → z) ∧ ε(x → z) ∧ I(x) ∧ Φ(z) ∧ Ω(z) undefined
```

**Formalization gap.** `A`, `ε`, `I`, `Φ`, and `Ω` have no declared carriers or codomains here. In particular, “`Ω(z) undefined`” cannot be conjoined as a Boolean predicate without a specified partiality or definedness logic. The original expression is preserved as historical notation, **not** silently corrected into a different formula.

## Relationship to the four dreaming regimes

The [June 21 account of dreaming as knowing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1skfWCe9ew1kzLsd28UkoQytYax4FYha-/view) interprets finite, Scott-continuous, and transfinite inward dreaming as *convergence*, and the externally supplied aperiodic regime as *emergence*. This is a proposed interpretation of `Ξᵣ` and `Dᵣ`, not a demonstrated equivalence.

A proper bridge requires two explicitly defined maps:

1. From a regime-specific dream result to a family of anticipatory relations suitable for `Ξᵣ`.
2. From externally admitted candidate generators to a typed condition suitable for `Dᵣ`.

The bridge must establish what it preserves (supporting observations, uncertainty, compatibility, and the distinction between a proposed generator and an admitted fact). A failure to construct either map is a legitimate counterexample to the proposed interpretation.
