# Dream convergence function

The ***dream convergence function*** (Ξᵣ) is induced by overlapping anticipatory modulations across constraint domains.

When multiple systems modulate toward compatible but unrealized states,
and sustain each other's anticipation without collapse, convergence emerges.

> Ξᵣ := ∃ {xᵢ → z} : ∀ i, j: Dᵣ(xᵢ → z) ∧ M(xᵢ, xⱼ)

That is, dream convergence is the mutual stabilization of difference toward a shared unrealized future.

# Dream emergence function

The dream emergence function (Dᵣ) is induced by anticipation (A) and error (ε) within a stabilized domain.

When anticipation persists despite error — when a system continues to modulate toward unrealized stabilization — it enters dream: the intentional generation of impossible coherence.

Dᵣ(x → z) := A(x → z) ∧ ε(x → z) ∧ I(x) ∧ Φ(z) ∧ Ω(z) undefined

That is, dream is the persistence of intention in the absence of realizable constraint.
