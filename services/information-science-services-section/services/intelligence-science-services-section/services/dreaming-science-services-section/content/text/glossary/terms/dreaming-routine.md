# Dreaming routine

A **dreaming routine** is the Group's required offline reading and proposal cycle over a node's held records. The Leader's October 9, 2026 direction makes dreaming a **routine service**, not a one-off experiment. This glossary entry states the intended procedure; it does **not** certify that every node currently runs it.

The node reads a specified history window and applies admitted transformations in increasing proposed generativity:

| Regime | Permitted kind of proposal | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finite | Recompose held observations | Inward, no network |
| Scott-continuous | Consolidate evidence for repeated cells | Inward, no network |
| Transfinite | Propose relations between co-occurring predicates | Inward, no network |
| Aperiodic | Request candidate generators beyond the held closure | External enactor; separately budgeted and authorized |

The historical names refer to closure regimes. A bounded executable operation does not by itself prove that its actual closure ordinal equals `ω` or exceeds it. The [Dreaming Science source map](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-seed-topic-dreaming-science.md) preserves the regime theory, its uncertainties, and the separate biological hypotheses.

Each completed dream emits **inert opened questions or other candidate observations**, with provenance to their contributing records, and **one dream record** identifying the source window, rule set, regime, and enactor if any. An opened question records that the possibility was raised; it does not assign a new truth value or authorize an answer. The dream deletes no prior trace and does not silently modify accepted claims.

A separate office receives and judges any proposal. The dreaming actor does not receive its own proposals or promote them to evidence, executable authority, or mathematical proof. Formal proof claims require the actual proof oracle. Aperiodic external invocation, if admitted, is a distinct budgeted act; it is not permission for internal dreaming to send unreviewed messages or alter credentials.

A scheduled check with no work does not open an empty run or write an empty witness. Which node is enabled and which office may enact it are operational decisions; this term **creates or modifies no schedule**.

The owning [October 9 statement and engineering requirements](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-dreaming-as-routine-and-the-kinematic-space-of-work.md) define the service contract and place dream moves in the [kinematic space of work](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kinematic-space-of-work.md).

A **collective** dream of several settlements is a research question, not an implied additional operational duty. The [founding hypothesis](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-dreaming-as-unwitnessed-settlement.md) distinguishes settlement/conurbation composition, shared computation, and the unresolved question of phenomenal consciousness.
