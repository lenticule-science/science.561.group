A **dreaming routine** is the scheduled pass in which a node of the Group's
MetroPlat ecosystem dreams over the records it holds. The Leader holds that AI
dreaming is routine (2026-10-09). It is a service loop of every node, not an
experiment.

By day work writes cells to the trace. By night the routine re-reads a window of
the trace, bounded by marks on the ledger, and runs up to four dreams in
order of generativity: the **finite** dream recombines held cells; the
**scott-continuous** dream consolidates repeated cells; the **transfinite** dream
derives new predicates from co-occurrence; the **aperiodic** dream brings new
generators from outside the settled fibers through an enactor. The first three
are deterministic, replayable and network-free. Only the aperiodic dream calls an
enactor, and it does so as admitted work with a budget.

A dream writes two things: inert **proposals**, each a bottom-opened cell that
records only that the cell was asked, and one **dream record** naming the
window, regime, rule set, enactor and proposals. A dream does not settle the
trace, articulate its own proposal, mark a theorem proved, close a declaration or
upgrade a capability, and in the dreaming state its authorization is narrowed to
internal composition. By day an office whose duty covers a proposal's subject
accepts, declines or lets it lapse, and records the judgment; the dreamer never
receives its own proposals. A dream deletes nothing, which separates the routine
from compaction.

A scheduled pass that finds nothing to dream over is not a run and writes no
witness.

ordered before the governance move that articulates its proposals.
