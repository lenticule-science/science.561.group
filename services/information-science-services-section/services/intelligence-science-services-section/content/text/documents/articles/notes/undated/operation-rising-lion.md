Operation Rising Lion was a [[military operation]] conducted by [[Israel]] against [[Iran]] on [[2025-06-13]]. Using a combination of [[war drone|drones]] and [[Mossad operative|Mossad operatives]] previously [[deploy|deployed]] into Iran, and [[missile strike|missile strikes]], Israel attacked [[Iranian nuclear facility|Iranian nuclear]] and [[Iranian military facilities|military facilities]]. The operation led to a series of responsive missile strikes between teh two nations.
