## Current research

- [Formation Duration and Protective Choice](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/16/2026-10-16-formation-duration-and-protective-choice.md) (2026-10-16) is an **unreviewed working research paper** on whether long object-formation time independently changes protective choices, and how to separate this hypothesis from legitimate irreplaceability, object history, production effort and goal shielding. It proposes randomized tests; it reports no new experimental result. The [formation-duration protective prioritization](content/text/glossary/terms/formation-duration-protective-prioritization.md) glossary term is explicitly a candidate construct, not an established bias.
