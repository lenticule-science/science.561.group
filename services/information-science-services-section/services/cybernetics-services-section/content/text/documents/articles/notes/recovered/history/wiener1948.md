# Wiener 1948 — Cybernetics

**Wiener, Norbert. (1948). *Cybernetics: Or, Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine*. MIT Press. (2nd ed. 1961.)**

## Annotation

The founding text of cybernetics as a discipline. Wiener defines cybernetics as the study of control and communication in animals and machines — specifically, the study of feedback mechanisms by which systems maintain stability against perturbation. The core concept is the *feedback loop*: a system that monitors its output against a target state and adjusts its behavior accordingly, without requiring a central controller.

For predicated sociology, Wiener's cybernetics is the upstream theoretical resource for the feedback mechanism at the heart of [affect governance](teaching/explanations/affect-governance.md) and cybernetic postliberalism. The cybernetic postliberalism framework (see [overview](../../../cybernetic-postliberalism/texts/overview.md)) explicitly cites Wiener alongside Stafford Beer and W. Ross Ashby as the intellectual lineage for its claim that late-liberal governance operates through distributed feedback without central control.

In substrate-algebraic terms, the cybernetic feedback loop is a self-sustaining closure-rule circuit: the settling's closure rules monitor cell states and adjust future cell values to restore a target configuration, without any central authority issuing commands. The californication circuit — structural crisis → affective formatting → continued system participation → structural condition reproduction → structural crisis — is a Wienerian feedback loop realized as a closure-rule family in the [late-liberal social base](../objects/late-liberal-social-base.md).

The key Wienerian concept for predicated sociology is *homeostasis*: the tendency of a cybernetic system to maintain a target state against perturbation. The late-liberal social system's homeostatic target is *functional subject participation*: it does not target any particular ideological belief but targets the continued active participation of subjects in the social system. Perturbation (structural crisis, political contestation) is absorbed by the cybernetic mechanism (californication, coherent confusion) that returns subjects to functional participation. This is what makes the system reproducing without repression: the feedback mechanism does the work that coercion would otherwise require.
