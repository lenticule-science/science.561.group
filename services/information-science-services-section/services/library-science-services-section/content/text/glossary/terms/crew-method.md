The CREW method (Continuous Review, Evaluation, and Weeding) is the most widely used guide to weeding a public library collection in the United States. Joseph P. Segal wrote it in 1976, Belinda Boon revised it in 1995, and Jeanette Larson revised it in 2012 as *CREW: A Weeding Manual for Modern Libraries* for the Texas State Library and Archives Commission. In 2025 the Commission archived the manual with no plan to update it; copies are requested from ld@tsl.texas.gov and remain mirrored by library systems.

Each class of material has a formula of three parts, X/Y/MUSTIE (p. 57). X is the number of years since the item's latest copyright, Y the number of years since its last circulation, and MUSTIE the negative factors. "8/3/MUSTIE" means an item is considered for discard "when its latest copyright is more than eight (8) years ago; and/or, when its last circulation or in-house use was more than three (3) years ago; and/or, when it possesses one or more of the MUSTIE factors." An "X" in place of a number means that test does not apply to the class.

MUSTIE (p. 58): **M**isleading or factually inaccurate; **U**gly, worn beyond mending; **S**uperseded by a new edition or a better book; **T**rivial, of no discernible literary or scientific merit; **I**rrelevant to the community's needs and interests; **E**lsewhere available, through interlibrary loan, reciprocal borrowing or electronically.

For children's and teen collections (pp. 80–82) picture books and easy readers are X/2 ("every title should go out at least once in a two-year period"; weed books that reflect racial and gender bias), juvenile fiction X/2, young adult fiction 3/2, and children's and teen nonfiction take the adult formula of their Dewey class, "but look especially for inaccuracy and triviality." Examples of adult formulas: computers 3/X, medicine 5/3, astronomy 5/3, folklore X/3, literature X/3, history 10/3, biography X/3.

The formula nominates; a person decides. Binding Generations' youth collection audit applies the manual's formula for each class and names the page (`binding-generations-services-section/src/collection-audit.mjs`).
