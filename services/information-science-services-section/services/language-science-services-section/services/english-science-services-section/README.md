# English Science Services Section

**Parent:** [Language Science Services Section](../../README.md).  
**Child:** [PTGAE Science Services Section](services/ptgae-science-services-section/README.md).

English Science studies English usage, grammars, registers, interpretation, technical vocabulary, sociolinguistic variation, document structures, and accessible communication. An English-language standard is a proposal about a defined register and audience; it is not a statement that one form of English is inherently better or that its speakers are deficient.

The PlainEng program treats contemporary General American English as an **initial written register/localization target (en-US)**, not a universal speaking norm. Models and empirical protocols must declare audience, purpose, document type, locale and available evidence. Neither universal comprehension nor preservation of meaning follows from grammatical simplification alone.

Research includes comparison with the U.S. federal plain-language tradition and independent controlled-language and accessibility methods. See the [PTGAE/PlainEng science section](services/ptgae-science-services-section/README.md).

This section is currently a private, source-owned path within `lenticule-science/science-services-section`; separate repo formation is tracked in [science issue #53](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/53).


## Research program

[English for people and agents](research/english-language-research-program.md) studies English itself (morphology, syntax, semantics, discourse, pragmatics, dialects, accessibility and change) and the actual competing English-algebra implementations before specifying constrained PlainEng. The [PTGAE research section](services/ptgae-science-services-section/README.md) is a consumer of this science, not its replacement.
