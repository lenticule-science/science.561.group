## Purpose

Develop an explicit, evidence-grounded, standards-aware science of clear technical and institutional English. PlainEng is intended as a *spiritual successor* to the public-interest U.S. federal Plain Language program, not a government program or a claim of literal succession.

The research program must explain what makes a message useful to a given reader doing a given task, under what conditions a revision preserves its intended interpretation, and which aspects of that claim can be checked by machines, domain experts, or readers. Scientific theories and empirical outcomes must remain distinct from adopted writing rules.

## Joint work and authority

The existing [private Red Cup PTGAE engineering section](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/ptgae-engineering-services-section) implements versioned language fibers, selection and prose analysis; the planned rewrite service needs evidence and bounded semantic safety. Science supplies theories, annotations, counterexamples, conformance proposals and empirical outcomes. Engineering supplies typed implementations and falsifiable tests. Neither partner may promote an unreviewed proposal into a universal Group requirement through repository prose alone.

Source history exists under the older [Communication → Writing → PTGAE section](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/tree/main/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/services/plain-technical-general-american-english-services-section), [Lenticule docs](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/docs/plain-technical-general-american-english.md), and emsenn's earlier writing-theory corpus.
