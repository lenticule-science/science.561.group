# Language Science

Language Science studies situated utterances, interpretation, and evidence downstream of marks and articulations. It belongs to [Information Science](../../README.md) within Lenticule Science.

- [L-Space concept note](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-about-l-space.md) — the single scientific account of the Semioverse language space of content-addressed tokens and claims, with CHL, Yoneda, FCA, causal/lightcone, Grothendieck-site and optional Hilbert-space readings distinguished.
- [L-Space glossary term](content/text/glossary/terms/l-space.md) — the concise definition and concept relationships.
- [Marks, utterances and contextual semantics](research/marks-utterances-and-contextual-semantics.md) — source, interpretation, evidence and authorization boundaries.
- [English Science](services/english-science-services-section/research/english-language-research-program.md) — research on English and its technical registers.

The [Linguistics Services Section](https://github.com/lenticule-science/linguistics-services-section) develops separate observation and parsing work. L-Space's knowledge-garden site, CAIRN document storage and Markdown indexes are applications or carriers, not the language space itself.
