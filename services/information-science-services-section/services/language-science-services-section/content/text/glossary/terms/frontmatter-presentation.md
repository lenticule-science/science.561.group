# Frontmatter presentation — language/document world

**Frontmatter presentation** is a *documentary fact about how one file revision represents key/value data*. For a source revision \`s\`, a delimited source span \`ℓ\`, a parsed key \`k\` and a parsed value \`v\`, the initial evidence is that **the specified source contains that presentation**. A parser's successful reading does not, by itself, determine the key's sense, the referent of \`v\`, the truth of a world claim or the actor authorized to make it.

The original March 28, 2026 personal blog/log explores why \`key: value\` appears locally determined **because a file witnesses its own presentation**, even though its interpretation begins as something taken as Absurd in the relational inquiry. This is not a declaration that the YAML colon is a universal RDF relation or that the source has certified its own metaphysical truth.

## Separate the complete chain

1. **Bytes and occurrence.** Read one source revision and exact key/value span, with an encoding, parse boundary and a parse result. Use [token occurrence](token-occurrence.md), not the bare spelling \`author\`, as the source-local position.
2. **Lexical and contextual interpretation.** Select an actually declared vocabulary, sense or [world-indexed term](world-indexed-term-sense.md) for \`k\`. Unknown or wrong-typed keys remain **uninterpreted**; another world's \`author\` cannot simply be treated as an alias.
3. **Candidate predication.** Resolve documentary subject, predicate and object types, and any reference to an external person, office or event. Multiple candidates can remain possible.
4. **Qualified admission and evidence.** An admitted predication can receive independently identified support and refutation under the existing SignCell/FOUR rules. The frontmatter **presentation** is evidence about *what the document declares*, not the external proposition it appears to state.
5. **Lawful effect and receiving.** An \`incumbent: X\`, \`paid: true\` or \`done: yes\` field never appoints, settles, pays or completes work. Those require the actual owning system and independent receiver.

The intermediate forms are *not synonyms*. Keep source identity, syntactic observation, interpreted term sense, typed claim, evidential charge, and effect in distinct carriers.

## Required negative controls

- Unknown or untyped key: return source presentation and a **refusal to assert a typed claim**.
- Valid field claiming unauthorized office status: show the attributed declaration without creating a real incumbency.
- Key omitted: absence of a declaration is **not** negative witness about an external claim.
- Same visible YAML in two different source revisions: distinguish the two occurrences even if the semantic bytes are identical.
- Two differently attributed source values: retain separate source tellings and do not fabricate a single resolved value.
- A question about an absent claim: report what would have to be witnessed without inserting the proposed claim into the current store.

This is a lexical and scientific contract, not a second Markdown parser or RDF engine. Engineering should use its existing Markdown parsing, SCA/SEN/SEAVT, SignCell and CAIRN providers and verify any actual consumer.
