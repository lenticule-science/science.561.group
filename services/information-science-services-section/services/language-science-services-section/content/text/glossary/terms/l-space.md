**L-Space** is the Semioverse's shared language space: the structure of content-addressed tokens, uttered articulations, typed claims, evidence, and their admissible relationships. It provides a common referential and semantic substrate across contexts. A content address identifies a canonical representation; a claim's truth, provenance, authorization, and acceptance require separate judgments.

Categorical, FCA, causal and potential Hilbert-space structures are mathematical **readings or proposed realizations** of this language space, not interchangeable definitions. The Lenticule knowledge-garden application called L-Space is a document-facing service, not the space itself.

The [L-Space concept note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-about-l-space.md) develops the mathematical structure, CHL/Yoneda and light-cone connections, research status, and relationship to a possible quantum Hilbert representation.
