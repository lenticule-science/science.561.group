**L-Space** is the Semioverse's shared language space of content-addressed tokens and the attributable claims made with, about and between them. Its vocabulary arises downstream of **marks and uttered articulations**. Typed predications, proof paths, contexts, supporting or opposing evidence and admitted relationships give tokens interpretive use. A content hash identifies an encoding, not an established meaning, a proved proposition or the authority to act.

L-Space admits several *mathematical readings*, not competing definitions: CHL concerns typed proofs/programs/morphisms; Yoneda characterizes categorical objects through their morphisms; FCA derives concept lattices from sourced incidence contexts; a Grothendieck site can model local interpretations and conditional gluing; causal order supplies kinematic reachability; a Hilbert-space representation requires additional amplitudes, operators and interpretation. None is entailed by content addressing alone.

The linguistics [categorical Language Cone (A8)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/linguistics-services-section/blob/main/services/obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commuting-cone-mastery.md) relates coherent representations of language; the distinct [causal language light cone (A6)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/linguistics-services-section/blob/main/services/obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/language-light-cone.md) concerns possible influence among witnessed utterances. Their empirical and L-Space-specific combination remains research.

Markdown notes, archive search, frontmatter graph projections, CAIRN storage and the L-Space-named knowledge-garden application are **sources or interfaces**, not L-Space itself. See the single [L-Space concept note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-about-l-space.md) for the proposed structures, formal carriers and proof obligations.
