# Documentary source — text/knowledge world

A **documentary source** is one identifiable carrier of written or otherwise recorded presentation: a content-addressed or revision-qualified file, dated page, message or artifact, together with its **retrieval and rights context**. The source is where an observer can check what was said or encoded. It is not necessarily the actual subject *denoted* by the text, the author claimed by the text, or evidence that the text's assertions are true.

For example, the original March 28, 2026 personal blog/log can be the source for “the author proposed an Absurd → Possible → Necessary → Determined progression.” Its frontmatter's \`authors\` field is an **authored claim of authorship**, not independent proof about the real creator. A later Science research synthesis is another documentary source about the first; it must not replace, impersonate, publicly republish or silently overrule the original.

## What a qualified reading owes

1. Source identity: owner/repository or archival custodian, exact version/content and acquisition provenance.
2. Content coordinates: selected text or structured field and source-local spans under the actual encoding.
3. Attested scope: whether the document **declares**, **argues**, **observes**, **reports** or **verifies** the statement.
4. Rights and attribution: a personal unpublished source is not made public by being cited in a Science model.
5. Separate claims: mapping to a vocabulary/world must be explicit; an unknown key stays uninterpreted.
6. Independent receiving: if the document says an action happened, check the actual provider and recipient before saying it did.

## Explicitly different senses of “source”

A [graph-theoretic source](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/source.md) is the departure vertex of a directed edge. The **source of institutional authority** is a law, office or mandate with its own validity conditions. **Science source ownership** is the custody of definitions and proofs. None is the documentary source merely because the same English noun is used. Keep source type, provenance, and authorization as distinct properties.

A mutable URL, filename or note title can help retrieve a documentary source, but does not uniquely identify the original bytes. A cryptographic hash commits to chosen bytes, **not** the source's intent or veracity. Existing CAIRN/SignCell/NI and source-admission providers should do the actual capture and typed claim reading; this glossary item does not implement a new source registry.
