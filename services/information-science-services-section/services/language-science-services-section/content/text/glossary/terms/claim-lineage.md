**Claim lineage** is a typed relation between an attributed source claim and a later telling of it. A faithful rendering retains the source claim's proposition, context and time scope, revision, and whether the utterance is an observation, attribution, commitment, forecast, scenario, or unresolved claim. A promise reported as a completed effect is a **change of speech act**, not a faithful translation.

This is a **structural provenance test**, not evidence that the source was true, the published words semantically entail the source, the actor was authorized, or the recipient accepted an outcome. Recording a promise and performing it remain separate.

The [source-owned claim-lineage research and executable assay](../../../../research/claim-lineage/CAUSEWAY-TO-LANGUAGE-SCIENCE-2026-10-11.md) defines the supported fields, refusals, coverage measure and research limits. [L-Space](l-space.md) supplies the broader language-space context; source text and claim identity are not interchangeable.
