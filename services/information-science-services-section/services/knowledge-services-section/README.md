# Knowledge Services Section

Knowledge science studies warranted, situated, revisable knowing and the
systems through which knowledge is formed, organized, transmitted, and used.

## Unfolding

- `epistemology-services-section` owns accounts of warrant, standpoint,
  differentiation, epistemic communities, and knowledge practices.
- `knowledge-organization-services-section` owns classification, ontologies,
  library structures, vocabularies, and domain arrangement.
- `knowledge-systems-services-section` owns architectures that operationalize
  knowledge, including stewarded semiotic universes and vault models.
- `research-services-section` owns methods for producing and evaluating new
  knowledge.
- `identifier-services-section` owns persistent naming and identity schemes.
- `adr-services-section` owns architectural decision knowledge.

Scientific ownership is separate from representation status, provenance,
privacy, and publication visibility. L-Space preserves all four distinctions.
