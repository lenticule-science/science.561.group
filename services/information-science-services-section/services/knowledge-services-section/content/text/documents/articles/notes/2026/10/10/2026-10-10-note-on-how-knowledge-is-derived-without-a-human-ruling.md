# Statements

1. **The form of data is SPO.** All data in existence is subject-predicate-object. A file is a triple (file, key, value). A token and a claim form a subject and an object whose predicate is an unsaturated function; the key supplies the function but is itself another term, so the graph is a meshy directed acyclic graph (2026-09-25 17:50). The Leader asks agents to think hard about this when they judge that the house lacks graphing tooling.
2. **Truth is derived, not ruled.** No source in the corpus is human-authored in the sense of authoritative: the human wrote prompts, the agents authored the text, the prompts are gone and the text remains (2026-09-25 15:59). Where no source is authoritative, correctness is decided by mathematics, standards conformance and research, which is the purpose of the Group's mathematics (16:00). An agent that searches for the one document that will lead it correctly makes the opposite error. The concept of a "ruling" hides that derivation and sustains the idea of a supreme human in the loop; the Group is a mesh of humans and agents seeking mathematical lawfulness and standards conformance (17:41, item 3).
3. **Formal concept analysis begins at marks.** The Group does FCA from bare principles, marks, outward. The more settled the corpus is, the more confidently agents derive truth from what is there, so agents need not rely on the Leader's rulings (17:41, item 5).
4. **Derivation replaces annotation.** Relations, provenance and grades derived by the graphing tools are the record; hardcoded or handwritten grade tags are theatre and help no one (item 2). Provenance tracking of the kind that governs complex processes goes beyond any software written today and intersects with item 2 (item 3). Relative links that must be maintained by hand are a symptom that the house is a search over markdown and not a knowledge system (2026-09-21 01:39).
5. **Attention raises care.** If a resource receives attention, it percolates toward receiving different attention: when humans keep opening a note's page, and the page views are tracked, that motivates the Group's systems to make that resource as good as they can get it (item 4).
6. **Knowledge tooling is semantic.** The Group's knowledge system uses OWL and SPARQL and the tools available in 2026. The ontology file FORD is not assumed to be the right taxonomy (2026-09-21 01:39). Turtle is generated from schemas and no handwritten copy of the same data is kept (2026-09-11 03:07). The Daoist studies are made richer, denser, more machine-readable and more useful to knowledge graphing and to forming an OWL by using upstream code, and specialized Daoist-only projection code is unnecessary (2026-09-11 02:33, 2026-09-21 01:47). The Group's science research is made more legible, visible and machine-readable by improving the tools already used (2026-09-17 01:32).
7. **Content normalization is not knowledge.** The normalized content is weak against what the Group's theory supports for knowledge graphing, knowledge, truth and evidence (2026-09-24 21:48).
8. **Numbers.** The Group generally wants ordinals and fractions over integers, whatever context a given use sits in (2026-09-25 23:13).
9. **Thing and Cell.** A Thing is a different data structure from a Cell, with qualities, relations, structures and conditions as subobjects; the Leader thinks that is the wrong list of subobjects. It is not "Cells versus Things". The software moves to one identifier system by breaking migration (2026-09-12 01:35).
