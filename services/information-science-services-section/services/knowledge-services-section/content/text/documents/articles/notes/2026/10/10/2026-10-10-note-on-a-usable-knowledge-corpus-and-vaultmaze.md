# A usable knowledge corpus, and VaultMaze

## The Leader's statements, taken as theory

1. What the agent found were **thin AI summaries of real research**. The research is real mathematics and external work, real enough that an agent can validate it itself, by reading and recomputing it.
2. The agent was wrapped up in **performative "settled claims"**. That distracts from the broader issue: the Group's knowledge is **not yet a usable corpus, for agents or for humans**.
3. The cause is the **tooling the agents are building: it is "very very silly"**. Machinery (term-engineering, 93 predicates, "D6", a "paid predicate-identification ledger") that the Leader had never heard of stands between a reader and the knowledge. A Leader's unfamiliarity with the machinery is evidence the machinery is not the Group's design.
4. **VaultMaze is meant to be like Encarta MindMaze**, a castle of wings with a question at every door, not what it was turned into.

## What the house holds

- Statement 4: held fully by the 2026-09-27 VaultMaze note (Encarta MindMaze row: wings are vaults, doors ask rule-based questions) and the VaultMaze glossary term and design note.
- Statements 1–3: not held before this note. The house has term-engineering-services-section and relation predicates in its glossary schema, but no statement that corpus usability, not claim machinery, is the measure.

## Consequences

- A knowledge record is judged by whether a human or agent reading it finds the real source and the real result; a summary that cannot be checked against the research it summarizes is not knowledge.
- A claim is not "settled" by being labelled settled; it is held when the source is cited and the result can be recomputed.
- Tooling that introduces vocabulary or ledgers the Leader's design does not contain (the "paid predicate-identification ledger", "D6") is judged under `existing-work-is-judged-before-it-is-discarded` and removed or reduced if it does not serve a reader.
- VaultMaze is rebuilt toward the MindMaze form named in 2026-09-27's design, and the Leader's later word (Encarta MindMaze with other games, federated across the Semioverse) is the brief.

## What remains open

- Which of the 93 predicates and "D6" survive judgment is not decided; the Leader has not seen them.
- A measure of usability for a reader (a task a reader can finish from the corpus alone) is to be specified.
