An ***in-register*** condition is one in which signal is interpreted through **structured, recursive recordkeeping**—like within a [[delta register]] or a casting-based system of role-bearing documents.

Registers:

- Accept signals without requiring consensus  
- Log role behavior over ontological claim  
- Preserve interpretive memory **without freezing it**
- Allow yields to become new signals

In-register conditions prioritize:

- **Trace continuity** over irreversibility  
- **Interpretive modulation** over transactional finality  
- **Role-behavioral logic** over identity assertion

They support **multi-caster, non-binding coherence** with recursive potential.
