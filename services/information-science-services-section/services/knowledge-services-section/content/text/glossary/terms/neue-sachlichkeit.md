***Neue Sachlichkeit*** ([English](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/english-language.md): "New Objectivity") was a [cultural](cultural-movement.md) and [art movement](art-movement.md) in [Germany](germany.md) in the [1920s](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1920s.md) and early [1930s](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1930s.md) that rejected [Expressionism](expressionism.md).

Itself was divided into [socially critiquing](critique-of-society.md) Verists and the non-critiquing Classicists.
