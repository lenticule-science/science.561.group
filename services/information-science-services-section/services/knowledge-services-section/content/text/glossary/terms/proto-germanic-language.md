The ***Proto-Germanic language*** was a [language](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/language.md) spoken between approximately [500bce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/500bce.md) and [200ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/200ce.md) in [southern Scandinavia](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/southern-scandinavia.md) and [northern Germany](northern-germany.md).

**Proto-Germanic language** developed from [Proto-Indo-European language](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/proto-indo-european-language.md), differentiated by several features, described by the following:

- [Grimm's Law](grimm-s-law.md) indicated how consonants shifted.
- [Verner's Law](../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/verner-s-law.md) indicated exceptions to Grimm's Law.
