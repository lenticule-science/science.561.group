# Introduction

# Judgement Day {#judgement-day-5}

When judgement day comes I'll be writing a novel  
about all the things which haven't happened to me.  
I'll write about love, and I'll write about triumph  
Confidence, clarity, certainty, absolution.  
I won't say a word about the caustic fear  
that brought us headlong to the end  
There'll be no mention of the little deaths  
the false justice or apprehension.  
Gods don't need that.
