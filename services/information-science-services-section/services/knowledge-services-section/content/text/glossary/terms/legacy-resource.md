A **legacy resource** is a resource of the ecosystem that has not been integrated into the living resources. It is a valid resource. It is not preserved for its own sake, ignored, or excused from treatment as a resource. The Leader, 2026-09-27: "Legacy is meant to be *consumed* into living resources. I want, ideally, 0% legacy resources in the entire ecosystem."

A legacy resource MUST be [revived](revival.md) when its class of resource is the focus of attention. A session that keeps, skips or finds reasons to leave legacy resources untreated, or that holds a memory rule to that effect, mishandles them. The target proportion of legacy resources in the ecosystem is zero. See [living resource](living-resource.md).
