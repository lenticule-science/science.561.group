***Blacksmithing*** is [smithing](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/smithing.md) that is focused on [forging](forging.md) [iron](../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/iron.md) and [steel](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/steel.md).

The "[black](black.md)" in blacksmith comes from the black [oxide](oxide.md) scale that forms on hot iron.
