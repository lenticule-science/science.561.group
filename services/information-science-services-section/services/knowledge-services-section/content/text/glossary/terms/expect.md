- [[definition]]:: ***Expect*** is the [[act]] of structuring a [[reflexive]] relation to [[bind]] with a future relation, forming a relational anticipation.

## Derivation
- [[postulate]]:: As [[binding]] two relations [[induce|induces]] an ordered structure, it must be derivable that a [[reflexive]] relation can be structured to anticipate and bind a future input.
- [[derivation]]:: Deriving a [[reflexive]] relation with an empty placeholder [[induce|induces]] an expected binding, preparing the relation to couple with another relation when provided.
- [[relational expression]]:: 
  - expect(f) is bind(f, x)
