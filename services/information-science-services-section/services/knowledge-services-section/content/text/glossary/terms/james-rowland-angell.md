***James Rowland Angell*** was an [American functionalist psychologist](american-functionalist-psychologist.md) and the 16th [President of Yale University](president-of-yale-university.md), the 15th President of the American Psychological Association, and developer of [functionalist psychology](functionalist-psychology.md), [influenced](influencing.md) by [John Dewey](john-dewey.md).
