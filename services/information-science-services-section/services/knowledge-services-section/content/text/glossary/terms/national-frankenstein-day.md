***National Frankenstein Day*** is an annual day of appreciation that has [occured](occuring.md) on [August 30th](august-30th.md), the same day [Mary Shelley](mary-shelley.md), the [author](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/author.md) of [Frankenstein, or the Modern Prometheus](frankenstein-or-the-modern-prometheus.md), was born in [1797](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1797ce.md), since [1997](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1997ce.md), when the Day was [inaugurated](inaugurating.md) by Ron MacCloskey of Wakefield, New Jersey.
