Folding is the process by which a linear chain acquires the three-dimensional conformation that carries its function. A protein folds into a shape that catalyzes a reaction ([protein folding note](../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/folding.md)); Anfinsen's thermodynamic hypothesis is that the native state is the global free-energy minimum under folding conditions (Anfinsen 1973).

The Leader's statement is that realization is protein folding of proofs. A proof folds into working software as a polypeptide folds into a protein: the proof is primary structure, a linear chain, and working software is its native fold, the minimum-energy conformation reached by settlement as least fixed point. A wrapper, certificate or subsystem deposited beside the territory instead of folded from the proof is a misfold ([folding-protein note](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/24/2026-07-24-the-union-evm-chain-as-a-folding-protein.md), lines 20-27; [framing note](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 46-50). On 2026-10-09 the Leader named folding with [electronic design automation](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/electronic-design-automation.md) and [kinetic proofing](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kinetic-proofreading.md) as the research on code fabrication ([recovery index](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-recovering-the-fabrication-research.md), line 59).

The same two readings of one molecule recur in the chain. The history, an append-only log, is primary structure: knowledge order, never retracted. The state is the native fold: truth order, revisable by later transitions. They are the two Galois faces of one value object, so state is not a database bolted onto the log. Folding is settling is free-energy descent: settling is the least fixed point of a monotone closure, and the closure is proved to be gradient flow of a free energy with a strictly decreasing Lyapunov potential (`free_energy_is_lyapunov`, `energy_gives_unique_equilibrium`, cited by the note and not rechecked here). The terminus is H1 = 0, with nothing left to descend. The landscape is rugged, with many metastable basins, and strictly descending dynamics cannot cycle. Closure regimes read as folding kinetics: a strict-finite closure is a two-state fast folder with no trapped intermediates ([note](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/24/2026-07-24-the-union-evm-chain-as-a-folding-protein.md), sections 1-3). A [zeolite](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zeolite.md) is the catalytic framework of the fold.

Fidelity of the fold is the fidelity of the fabrication: the work order sets it and independent assay with an irreversible reject path buys it ([fabrication fidelity](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fabrication-fidelity.md), [kinetic proofreading](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kinetic-proofreading.md)).
