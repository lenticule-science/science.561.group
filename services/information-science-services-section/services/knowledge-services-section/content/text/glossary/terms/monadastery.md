A ***monadastery*** is an [[interpretative space]] where [[telemetric-cast|telemetric casting]] is practiced. It is a site of intentional [[telemetric research]] done using the [[telemetric-method]].

Like a [[monastery]], a [[monadastery]] is [[deliberate]], [[constrain|constrained]], [[recur|recursive]], and [[reflex|reflexive]]. However, monadasteries yield [[semantic coherence]], not [[truth]].

Like a [[laboratory]], a [[monadastery]] uses [[method]] and [[grammar]]. However, it is applied to *semantic coherence*, not *truth*.

Like a [[digital garden]], a [[monadastery]] uses [[semantic|semantics]], but for [[analyse|analysis]] not [[accumulate|accumulation]].

A monadastery, as space, may be of a variety of forms. For example, [[emsenn's delta register]], an [[Obsidian vault]] operated as a [[delta register]], is a monadastery.
