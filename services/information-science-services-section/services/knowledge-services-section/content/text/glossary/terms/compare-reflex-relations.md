- [[definition]]:: ***Compare reflex relations*** is the act of relating two [[reflex relation]]s to determine whether their relational compositions are identical.

## Derivation
- [[postulate]]:: As reflex relations are structured derivations, it must be derivable to relate two reflex relations to each other based on their internal relational composition.
- [[derivation]]:: Comparing two reflex relations determines whether they affirm identical relational derivations at a shared depth.
- [[relational expression]]:: compare(r.1, r.2) affirms if r.1 and r.2 are structurally identical reflex relations, otherwise negates.
