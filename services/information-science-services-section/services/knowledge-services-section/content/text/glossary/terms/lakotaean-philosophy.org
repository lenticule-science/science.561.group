* Number

Documented Lakota sacred numerology centers on four and seven (Očhéthi
Šakówiŋ, the four directions, the "six directions" set phrase of the pipe
prayer with the center unenumerated) — not five. The widely-circulated claim
that the five-pointed star in cottonwood pith is "the Lakota morning star"
traces to an unattributed 1970 sentence by a non-Native author, not to
Densmore, Walker, Black Elk/Brown, or (so far as could be checked) Goodman;
where the pith star appears in sources, its five is descriptive only. The
provenance work, with sources and open leads, is in the 2026-08-22 note:
