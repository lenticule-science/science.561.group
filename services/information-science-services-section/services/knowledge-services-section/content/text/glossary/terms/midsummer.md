***Midsummer*** is a holiday on the Red Union calendar used for [tracking](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tracking.md) time on Teraum. It comes after the month of [Summertide](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/summertide.md) and is followed by the month of Highsun, except every four years, when the holiday of [Shieldmeet](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/shieldmeet.md) occurs, after Midsummer, and is then followed by Highsun.
