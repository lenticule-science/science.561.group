***Advising*** is [communicating](communicating.md) between an [advisor](advisor.md) and [recipient of advice](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recipient-of-advice.md) that is about [advice](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/advice.md).

As a term, *advising* usually [implies](implying.md) an [ongoing relationship](ongoing-relationship.md) between the advisor and recipient.
