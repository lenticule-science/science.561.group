***Félix Guattari*** ([1930](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1930ce.md)-[1992](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1992ce.md)) was a [French](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/french-person.md) [psychoanalyst](psychoanalyst.md), [philospher](philospher.md), and [political activist](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/political-activist.md). He is best known for [collaborating](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/collaborating.md) with [Gilles Deleuze](gilles-deleuze.md) to [author](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring.md) [Anti-Oedipus](anti-oedipus.md) ([1972](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1972ce.md)) and [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md) ([1980](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1980ce.md)), which form the two volumes of [Capitalism and Schizophrenia](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalism-and-schizophrenia.md). These texts challenged traditional [psychoanalysis](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychoanalysis.md), [Marxism](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marxism.md), and [structuralism](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/structuralism.md), introducing concepts like [schizoanalysis](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/schizoanalysis.md), [deterritorialization](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/deterritorialization.md), and [assemblage theory](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agencement.md).

**Félix Guattari** [trained](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/training.md) under [Jacques Lacan](jacques-lacan.md) and worked at [La Borde psychiatric clinic](la-borde-psychiatric-clinic.md).
