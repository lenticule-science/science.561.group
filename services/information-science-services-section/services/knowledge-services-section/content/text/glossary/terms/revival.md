**Revival** is the integration and synthesis of a [legacy resource](legacy-resource.md) into the [living resources](living-resource.md). It is done by reasoning through what is appropriate to migrate and what has been superseded. It is specific integration and synthesis; moving, copying, preserving or deleting a legacy resource is not revival. The Leader, 2026-09-27: "REVIVAL means very specific integration, synthesis, into our living resources, through reasoning through what is appropriate to migrate, what has been superseded, etc."

The trigger is attention: revival is required when the class of resource to which the legacy resource belongs is the focus of the work. The result is a living resource that holds what the legacy resource held and that was not superseded; the legacy resource is then consumed.

Record `existing-work-is-judged-before-it-is-discarded` holds the judging step; revival adds the integration step.
