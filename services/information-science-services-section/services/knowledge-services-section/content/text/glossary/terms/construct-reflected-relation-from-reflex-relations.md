- [[definition]]:: ***Construct reflected relation from reflex relations*** is the act of combining multiple [[reflex relation]]s into a new [[reflected relation]] at a shared depth.

## Derivation
- [[postulate]]:: As reflex relations are derivable individually, it must be derivable to combine them into a single reflected structure.
- [[derivation]]:: Constructing a reflected relation aggregates p given reflex relations into an ordered composition at depth n, deriving new relational coherence.
- [[relational expression]]:: construct([r.1, r.2, ..., r.p], n) is reflect(p, n), with each r.i reflexed into the structure.
