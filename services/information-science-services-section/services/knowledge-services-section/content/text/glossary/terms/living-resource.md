A **living resource** is a resource of the ecosystem that is integrated with the rest of the living ecosystem and in current use. There are two classes of resource in the ecosystem: living and legacy. All resources of both classes are valid resources. The Leader, 2026-09-27: "There are two classes of resources in this ecosystem: living, legacy. ALL ARE VALID RESOURCES."

The ecosystem's aim is that every resource be living. See [legacy resource](legacy-resource.md) and [revival](revival.md); see also [living](living.md).
