To ***grammatize a telemetric record*** is to move information into the [[format]] of a [[telemetric-record]], which is a [[telemetric-signal]] within a [[telemetric register]].

In the context of [[emsenn's Telemetric Register]], which is an Obsidian Vault, *grammatizing a delta record* means to create a Markdown file using certain syntax and metadata.# Grammatize Record.

This record may be used as a [[telemetric lambda]] when creating or formating delta records in a register.

## Grammar of a Delta Record
### Typing
Every record is given a `type` in its frontmatter, which may be one of the following, or something else:
- [[term]]
- [[essay]]
- etc.
### Titling
Every record must have a title. The grammar of the title is determined by record type:

| type | titling grammar |
|-|-|
| term | infinitive verb |
### Relationing
#### Signaling
The [[telemetric signals]] which a record contains are tracked as `delta` in the frontmatter.
- A *telemetric signal* is...
#### Functioning
The [[telemetric lambda]] which a record uses are tracked as `lambda` in the frontmatter.
- A *telemetric lambda* is...
#### Adjointing
The [[telemetric adjoints]] of a record are tracked as `adjoints` in the frontmatter.
- A *telemetric adjoint* is...
#### Enfolding
The [[telemetric enfolds]] of a record are tracked as `enfolds` in the frontmatter.
- A *telemetric enfold* is...
### Voicing
Every record must have a voice.

| type | standard voice |
|-|-|
| [[term]] | [[structured reference voice]] |
