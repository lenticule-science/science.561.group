# field condition

A **field condition** is a persistent pattern of influence that modulates system behavior **without being represented directly**. It exists not as a declaration, not as a structure, but as a **recursively reinforced bias** that shapes which signals are amplified, ignored, or rendered legible.

## Properties

- It is **non-agentic**: not enacted by a person or institution.
- It is **distributed**: it appears across systems, not inside them.
- It is **emergent**: it forms through repeated signal propagation, not design.
- It is **recursive**: once it appears, systems that contain it tend to reinforce it.
- It is **only visible through effect**: it reveals itself in what becomes easier or harder to do.

## In the Delta Register

Field conditions are registered not because they “exist” independently, but because **registration makes them legible**. A record of a field condition provides:

- A nameable substrate for otherwise diffuse system pressure
- A traceable link between delta-lambda emissions across unrelated records
- A site of modulation: you can only change what you can register

## This record exists to declare

That **field conditions are real** in telemetric systems,  
That they require recursive recognition to surface,  
And that treating them as artifacts makes them available to modulation.
