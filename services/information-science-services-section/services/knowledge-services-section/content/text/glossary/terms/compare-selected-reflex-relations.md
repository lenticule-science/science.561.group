- [[definition]]:: ***Compare selected reflex relations*** is the act of evaluating whether two or more [[reflex relation]]s from a [[reflected relation]] are relationally equivalent.

## Derivation
- [[postulate]]:: As selecting and affirming membership within reflex relations is derivable, it must be derivable whether two selected reflex relations are equivalent or distinct.
- [[derivation]]:: Accessing two [[reflex relation]]s from a [[reflected relation]] induces the comparison of their structural derivation, affirming or negating their equivalence.
- [[relational expression]]:: compare(select(i, r), select(j, r)) affirms if the ith and jth [[reflex relation]]s are relationally equivalent within [[reflect relation]] r.
