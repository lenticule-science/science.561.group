The ***Heritage Foundation*** is an [American conservative think tank](american-conservative-think-tank.md), founded in [1973ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1973ce.md) by [Edwin Feulner](edwin-feulner.md) and [Paul Weyrich](paul-weyrich.md). The Heritage Foundation was [influential](influential.md) in [Reagonomics](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/reagonomics.md) and [Project 2025](project-2025.md).
