# The organization of science: a top-down taxonomy and a DAG of terms

## The organizing theory

- Science is organized top-down by the standard taxonomies of the real world. The classification baseline is in `561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/20/2026-09-20-note-on-classification-standards-across-science-engineering-and-operations.md`.
- The keywords and keys of a science resource are terms. Terms form their own directed acyclic graph of categorical relations.
- The theory that carries those relations is developed: Fregean predication, Chu spaces and the predication terms of `math-services-section` and `knowledge-services-section` (`chu-space`, `fregean-first-order-predicate`, `predication-cell`, `predication-hyperdoctrine`). Lenticule Science also holds theories of knowledge, of language, of CHL language cones and of directed relational research, and a theory of theory.
- A knowledge resource is a Markdown vault note with YAML frontmatter. The Leader's 2026-03-28 personal log states the "absurd and YAML frontmatter" ideas the vault is meant to follow (`public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/` log of 2026-03-28). The Leader's own vault follows its own rules poorly.

## Roles in MAPE-K

- Science holds the (K)nowledge of the MAPE-K system distributed across the Group's ecosystem.
- Science stands alone as a consumer of Engineering. Engineering provides the M, A and E of MAPE-K and has found dogfooding hard.
- The simple-planning-engine under `dev/` is standalone and dependency-free, and provides the P. Evidence: `561-group-services-section/data/records/knowing/namings.jsonl` and the 2026-09-20 note above.

## What the work is for

- The local copy of the content and the remote copy at `l-space.561.group` are federated, using upstream technologies. The remote copy is private on the web.
- Lenticule Science's knowledge resources are standardized and normalized, with durable tools. The result must meet and then beat the current agentic tooling for providing knowledge as context to language models, including the models and agents the Group runs.
- All science, from the root to medicine, is made more legible to agents by aligning its formats, schemas and protocols with standards. The better ground is for highly useful resources on the topic of the day, produced by the Group's own directed relational research process, which its systems enact.
- An agent in such a session asks the Leader a question only when the answer is not in the science services section. An engineering fact read there is held as unreliable, because engineering is built by less-sophisticated agents and does not reflect design.
- The weekday themes of those sessions are the Group's: Sunday Funday (the fun is earning the wins of knowledge and research into the Group's content) and Theorsday, Theory Thursday (attention on knowledge and on engineering and operating knowledge systems). Terms: `sunday-funday`, `theorsday` in `561-group-services-section`.
- Qigong is held at `traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section` in the notes of 2026-09-24 on qigong as a practice system and on the clinical evidence for qigong and taiji; the Leader judged the science on qigong not nearly enough, and those notes are the answer to that request.
