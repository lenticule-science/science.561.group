Louis Hjelmslev (1899–1965) was a Danish linguist. His *Prolegomena to a Theory of Language* (1943) developed Saussurean structural linguistics into a rigorous formal system he called glossematics. His distinction between expression plane and content plane, and between [denotation](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/denotation.md) and [connotation](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/connotation.md) as orders of signification, became foundational to [French semiology](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/french-semiology.md).

## Core ideas

- **Expression and content planes**: Hjelmslev reformulated Saussure's [signifier](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md)/[signified](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) as the expression plane and the content plane, each with its own form and substance. This allowed a more rigorous structural analysis of [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) systems.
- **[Denotation](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/denotation.md) and [connotation](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/terms/connotation.md)**: a denotative semiotic links expression and content directly; a connotative semiotic uses an entire denotative system as its expression plane. This layering mechanism became [Roland Barthes](./roland-barthes.md)'s primary tool for analyzing myth and ideology.
- **Glossematics**: Hjelmslev's formal linguistic theory, which aimed to describe language as a self-contained system of pure relations (form) independent of the physical or conceptual material (substance) that realizes them.

## Notable works

- *Prolegomena to a Theory of Language* (1943)
- *Résumé of a Theory of Language* (1975, posthumous)
