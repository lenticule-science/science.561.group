# contributes-to-goal

`contributes-to-goal` is declared on a project's (or objective's) frontmatter to name the goal it serves. Inversion cascades emit the reciprocal `contributed-to-by-project` claim at the goal subject.

Note: inversion negates values — see `contributes-to-plan.md` for the same caveat.
