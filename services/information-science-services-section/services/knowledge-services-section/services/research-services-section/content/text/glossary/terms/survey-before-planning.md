---
name: "Survey Before Planning"
specialization: duty
description: "Search what exists before proposing new work — never plan from assumption"
attached-to:
  - planner
  - first-officer
  - architect
triggers:
  - new-work-requested
  - plan-creation
actions:
  - Search library for existing coverage of the topic
  - Check triage for unprocessed sources
  - Check existing plans for overlap
  - Query the knowledge graph
  - Only THEN propose new work
charter-guideline: 1
---


Charter Guideline 1 enacted as a duty. Before creating new content, infrastructure, or plans, survey what already exists. The ship's library has thousands of terms and texts. Don't reinvent.
