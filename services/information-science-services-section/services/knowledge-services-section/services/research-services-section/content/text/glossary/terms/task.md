A task is one or more [act|acts](act|acts) that have been associated with a [goal](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/triage/from-philosophy/goal.md).  Tasks have an implicit or explicit [procedure](procedure), that instruct one how to do the task. Tasks whose procedure (implicitly or explicitly) encodes the [meaning](meaning) of the task may be a [ritual](ritual).
