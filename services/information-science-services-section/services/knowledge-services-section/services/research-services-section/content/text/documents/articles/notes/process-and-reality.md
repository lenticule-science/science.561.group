*Process and Reality: An Essay in Cosmology* [cite:@whitehead_ProcessReality_1929] (1929) is [Alfred North Whitehead](./alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s magnum opus, presenting a comprehensive [metaphysical](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) system — the "philosophy of organism" — in which [actual occasions](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/actual-occasion.md) (momentary events of experience) are the fundamental constituents of reality, constituting themselves through [prehension](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) and [concrescence](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concrescence.md).

The book develops a systematic alternative to [substance metaphysics](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/substance-metaphysics.md), arguing that relations are ontologically prior to relata, that experience is the basic character of reality (not limited to conscious organisms), and that the universe is a creative advance into novelty. [Whitehead](./alfred-north-whitehead.md)'s "categoreal scheme" — his formal statement of the ultimate categories of reality — constitutes one of the most ambitious attempts in Western philosophy to build a [metaphysics](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) in which [process](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/terms/process.md), relation, and [creativity](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/creativity.md) replace substance, [property](../../sociology/terms/property.md), and persistence.

The text is notoriously difficult, and its reception has been uneven: celebrated in process theology and the [process philosophy](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md) tradition, largely marginalized in analytic philosophy, and increasingly rediscovered by scholars working on [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md), the [ontological turn](../../philosophy/terms/ontological-turn.md), and the intersection of Western and Indigenous [metaphysics](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md).
