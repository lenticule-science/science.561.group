Charles Sanders Peirce (1839–1914) was an American philosopher, logician, and scientist. He is the founder of pragmatism and of modern [semiotics](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) as a formal discipline.

## Core ideas

- **Triadic [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) theory**: a [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) is a three-place relation among a [representamen](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/representamen.md) ([sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) vehicle), an object, and an [interpretant](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md). This triadic structure distinguishes Peirce's [semiotics](../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) from Saussure's dyadic model.
- **Pragmatism**: the meaning of a concept is the sum of its practical consequences. Peirce later renamed his position "pragmaticism" to distinguish it from William James's popularization.
- **Abduction**: a third mode of inference alongside deduction and induction — the generation of explanatory hypotheses. Peirce considered abduction the engine of scientific discovery.
- **Categories**: Firstness (quality, possibility), Secondness (reaction, brute fact), Thirdness (mediation, law, habit). These categories structure Peirce's classifications of [signs](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md).
- **Unlimited [semiosis](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semiosis.md)**: [interpretants](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) are themselves [signs](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md), producing further [interpretants](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interpretant.md) in an open-ended chain of meaning.

## Notable works

- *Collected Papers of Charles Sanders Peirce* (8 volumes, published posthumously)
- "On a New List of Categories" (1867)
- "How to Make Our Ideas Clear" (1878)
- "A Guess at the Riddle" (c. 1890)
