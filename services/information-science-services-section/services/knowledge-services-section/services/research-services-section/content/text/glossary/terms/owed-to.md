# owed-to

`owed-to` names the correlative party of a duty — the entity whose claim the duty's required-act satisfies. Inversion populates `has-claim-on` at the correlative-party subject, carrying the Hohfeldian correlativity axiom (D(a, φ, r) entails C(r, φ, a)) directly into the geometry: walking any party's claim-fiber returns the duties that entity may invoke.
