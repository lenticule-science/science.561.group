# preference

A preference is a default that can be changed without justification. Preferences represent what works well under normal conditions.

Preferences outrank instructions: where an instruction contradicts a preference, the instruction must justify the override. Preferences are outranked by guidelines and rules. The rank-cascade (rule > guideline > preference > instruction) is carried at the type level; specializations of `preference` inherit the override relation via the predicate-specialization morphism.
