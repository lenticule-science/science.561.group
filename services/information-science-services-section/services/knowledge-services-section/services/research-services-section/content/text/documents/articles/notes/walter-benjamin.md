Walter Benjamin (1892–1940) was a German-Jewish philosopher, literary critic, and essayist associated with the Frankfurt School. He died by suicide at the Spanish border while fleeing the Nazis. His work spans literary criticism, philosophy of history, aesthetics, and cultural theory, and continues to generate new readings decades after his death.

## Core ideas

- **Theses on the philosophy of history**: Benjamin's "Theses on the Concept of History" (1940) — written shortly before his death — argue that historicism (the view that history progresses toward a better future) is complicit with the victors. The angel of history faces backward, seeing not progress but catastrophe piling wreckage upon wreckage. The revolutionary does not ride the train of history but pulls the emergency brake. Benjamin opposed the idea that time passing means things improve — that the future will redeem the suffering of the present.
- **Weak messianic power**: Benjamin wrote that each generation possesses a "weak messianic power" — a claim on it made by past generations. This is not religious messianism but a [materialist](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/metaphysics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/materialism.md) one: the dead have a claim on the living, and [historical materialism](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/marxism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/historical-materialism.md) is the practice of honoring that claim by refusing to let the past be forgotten or instrumentalized.
- **The [dialectical](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/marxism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectics.md) image**: Benjamin developed the concept of the [dialectical](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/marxism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dialectics.md) image — a constellation in which past and present flash together in a moment of recognizability. History is not a continuous narrative but a field of fragments; the task of the historical [materialist](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/metaphysics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/materialism.md) is to seize these fragments at the moment of danger and read them against the grain.
- **Mechanical reproduction**: "The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction" (1935) argues that the capacity to reproduce artworks (through photography, film, and print) destroys the "aura" — the unique presence in time and space — that traditional artworks possessed. This destruction is politically ambivalent: it democratizes access to art but also makes aesthetics available for political manipulation. Fascism aestheticizes politics; [communism](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/marxism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/communism.md) politicizes art.
