# work-type is functional

One work-type per task (inquiry / derivation / procedure / process).

The engine's constraint morphism derives a `work-type-violation` claim at any subject that carries more than one distinct object with positive evidence under `work-type`. Violations are witnessing, not rejection — the claims all stay; the substrate just flags the conflict.
