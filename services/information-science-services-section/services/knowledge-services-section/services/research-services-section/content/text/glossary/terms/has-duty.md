# has-duty

`has-duty` names a duty a position (office) bears. Inversion populates `duty-of` at the duty subject so a query at a duty returns the position(s) it's attached to, without anyone authoring the reverse claim by hand. Per positional derivation, duties attach to positions, not to bearers; the office's has-duty claims persist across succession.
