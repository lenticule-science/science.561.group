Serafinski is the pseudonym of the anonymous author of *Blessed is the Flame: An Introduction to Concentration Camp Resistance and Anarcho-Nihilism* (2016). The text examines acts of resistance within Nazi concentration camps and uses them to develop an anarcho-nihilist politics — one that does not depend on the possibility of victory or a better future to justify action.

## Core ideas

- **Anarcho-[nihilism](../../philosophy/disciplines/nihilism/index.md)**: a political orientation that acts against domination without requiring hope, progress, or the expectation of success. Resistance is justified by the conditions it responds to, not by its prospects.
- **Concentration camp resistance**: Serafinski documents sabotage, revolt, and [refusal](../../sociology/terms/refusal.md) within the camps as political acts that cannot be understood through frameworks that measure action by its outcomes.
