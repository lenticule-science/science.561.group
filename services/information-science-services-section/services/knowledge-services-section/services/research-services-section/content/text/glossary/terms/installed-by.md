# installed-by

`installed-by` names the authority that installed an office's incumbent (or installed an office at all). Inversion populates `installs` at the installer subject so a query at a captain returns the offices and personae they have installed. The relation persists even though the installation act was once: installation is a power, and the inversion lets accountability for current installations be traced upward at any time.
