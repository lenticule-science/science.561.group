# guideline

A guideline is a strong default within a charter. Overriding a guideline requires demonstrated understanding of why it exists and explicit reasoning for why the override better serves the function. Guidelines cannot be overridden silently.

Guidelines outrank preferences: where a preference contradicts a guideline, the preference yields. Guidelines are outranked by rules. The rank-cascade (rule > guideline > preference > instruction) is carried at the type level; specializations of `guideline` inherit the override relation via the predicate-specialization morphism.
