Recuperation is the process by which radical ideas, practices, and aesthetics are absorbed into dominant culture, stripped of their oppositional content, and repurposed to serve the systems they originally challenged. The term comes from the Situationist International ([Guy Debord](../../documents/articles/notes/guy-debord.md), Raoul Vaneigem), who argued that [capitalism](../../sociology/terms/capitalism.md) has the capacity to turn any critique into a commodity — including the critique of commodification itself.

The mechanism is not suppression but incorporation. Rebellion becomes fashion. Resistance becomes a brand. Revolutionary slogans become advertising copy. The content of the critique is emptied out; the form is preserved and sold back. This process does not require conspiracy — it follows from the logic of a system that can absorb anything that can be marketed, and the market's indifference to the meaning of what it circulates.

In the context of this library, [recuperation](./recuperation.md) names a specific danger: the adoption of terms like "[decolonization](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-services-section/services/povinellian-services-section/services/settler-colonialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/decolonization.md)" as metaphors for institutional reform, the reduction of [anarchism](/sociology/terms/anarchism.md) to an aesthetic, the transformation of [Indigenous resurgence](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-services-section/services/povinellian-services-section/services/settler-colonialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/indigenous-resurgence.md) into a curriculum module. Each of these is a form of [recuperation](./recuperation.md) — the radical demand is acknowledged, defanged, and incorporated into the system it challenged. [Settler moves to innocence](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-services-section/services/povinellian-services-section/services/settler-colonialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settler-moves-to-innocence.md) are [recuperation](./recuperation.md) applied to the colonial question. The [relational framework](/relationality/index.md) treats [recuperation](./recuperation.md) as a specific failure mode: the severing of a [sign](../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) from its relational context and its reinsertion into a context that neutralizes it.
