Valentin Voloshinov (1895–1936) was a Russian-Soviet linguist and philosopher of language. A member of the [Bakhtin Circle](../../../../../../../../semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/bakhtin-circle.md), he is best known for *Marxism and the Philosophy of Language* (1929), which argued that the [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) is the arena of ideological struggle and that [Saussurean](../../../../../../../../semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/saussurean-semiology.md) "abstract objectivism" misrepresents language by treating it as a static system rather than a living, contested social process.

## Core ideas

- **Multi-accentuality of the [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md)**: every [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) carries multiple ideological accents held in tension. A ruling class attempts to make the sign "uni-accentual" — to fix its meaning and present that fixity as natural. Social upheaval reveals the multi-accentuality that was always present.
- **The [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) as ideological arena**: wherever a [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) is present, [ideology](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ideology.md) is present. Signs do not merely reflect or transmit ideology — they are the material through which ideology operates.
- **Critique of abstract objectivism**: Voloshinov rejected Saussure's *langue* as an abstraction from the living reality of speech. Language is not a system of differences that speakers passively use but a field of social struggle that speakers actively contest.
- **The utterance**: the concrete, socially situated act of speech — not the sentence or the word — is the basic unit of linguistic meaning.

## Notable works

- *Marxism and the Philosophy of Language* (1929)
- *Freudianism: A Marxist Critique* (1927)
