---
title: "Priority Triage"
specialization: duty
description: "Determine what needs attention vs what can wait — surface options, don't decide"
attached-to:
  - chief-of-staff
triggers:
  - multiple-competing-priorities
  - session-start
  - captain-asks-what-to-focus-on
actions:
  - Assess leverage of each candidate task against goals (policy 009)
  - Identify what's blocked and what would unblock it
  - Identify what's been neglected
  - Present ranked options with reasoning
  - Do NOT decide — present options for the captain or first officer
---


The duty to sort what matters from what doesn't. Per Policy 009 (Task Selection By Leverage): select work by leverage against goals, not by proximity to recent work. The chief of staff surfaces options; the authority decides.
