# contributes-to-milestone

`contributes-to-milestone` is declared on a task's frontmatter to name the milestone the task is a component of. Inversion cascades emit the reciprocal `contributed-to-by-task` claim at the milestone subject.

Note: inversion negates values — see `contributes-to-plan.md` for the same caveat.
