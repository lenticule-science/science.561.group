Ferdinand de Saussure (1857–1913) was a Swiss linguist. His *Course in General Linguistics* (1916), reconstructed from students' lecture notes and published posthumously, founded structural linguistics and established the tradition of [semiology](../../../../../../../../semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/saussurean-semiology.md) — the study of [signs](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) within social life.

## Core ideas

- **The dyadic [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md)**: the [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) is the union of [signifier](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) (sound-image) and [signified](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) (concept). These are inseparable — two sides of the same sheet of paper.
- **Arbitrariness of the [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md)**: the relation between [signifier](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signifier.md) and [signified](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/signified.md) is conventional, not natural. No inherent bond connects the sound "tree" to the concept of a tree.
- **Differential meaning**: [signs](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) do not carry positive content. Each [sign](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) gets its value from what it is *not* relative to other [signs](../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) in the system.
- **Langue and parole**: *langue* is the abstract system of conventions shared by a speech community; *parole* is the individual act of speech. Linguistics, for Saussure, should study the system.
- **Synchronic and diachronic analysis**: the study of a language system at a given moment (synchronic) versus the study of its historical development (diachronic). Saussure privileged the synchronic.
