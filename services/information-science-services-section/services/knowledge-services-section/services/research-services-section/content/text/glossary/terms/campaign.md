A campaign in a command context carries the library definition (time-bounded coordinated push across multiple projects/plans) plus operational predicates: what goal it serves, what projects and plans it coordinates, and its time-bound.

Campaigns are the coordination mechanism between projects. A campaign says "these projects, coordinated together during this period, advance this goal." The campaign does not own the projects — it coordinates them.
