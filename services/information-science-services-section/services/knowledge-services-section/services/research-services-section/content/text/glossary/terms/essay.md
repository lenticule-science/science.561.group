An ***essay*** is (definition:: an [artifact](artifact) that results from [writing](../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/writing.md), usually a few hundred to several thousand [word|words](word|words), usually focused on one or just a few [topic|topics](topic|topics).)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:
```dataview
table definition
where file.name = "analytical essay" OR
  file.name = "argumentative essay" OR
  file.name = "comparative essay" OR
  file.name = "critical essay" OR
  file.name = "descriptive essay" OR
  file.name = "expository essay" OR
  file.name = "narrative essay" OR
  file.name = "reflective essay"
```


- [expository essay|Expository essays](expository essay|Expository essays) are for [informing](informing) people about a [concept](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md) or [topic](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/topic.md).
- [descriptive essay|Descriptive essays](descriptive essay|Descriptive essays) are for [describing](describing) a [thing](./thing.md), often focusing on one [quality](./quality.md) or set of qualities.
- [narrative essay|Narrative essays](narrative essay|Narrative essays) are for [narrative storytelling](narrative storytelling).
- [argumentative essay|Argumentative essays](argumentative essay|Argumentative essays) are for [arguing](arguing) a [truth](truth) about a [thing](./thing.md).
- [Analytical essay|Analytical essays](Analytical essay|Analytical essays) are for [../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating](../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/relating) a [concept](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md) or [topic](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/topic.md) to [event|events](event|events) or [information](./information.md).
- [comparative essay|Comparative essays](comparative essay|Comparative essays) are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [thing|things](thing|things).
- [critical essay|Critical essays](critical essay|Critical essays) apply a [concept](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/concept.md) or [synthesis](../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/services/essay-writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/synthesis.md) of concepts to events or information.
- [reflective essay|Reflective essays](reflective essay|Reflective essays) explore my [relationship](relationship) to a [thing](./thing.md).

Check the [Essay Catalog](Essay Catalog) if you want to see all the essays that are a part of [Groundhog Autonomous Zone|my virtual mycelium](Groundhog Autonomous Zone|my virtual mycelium).
