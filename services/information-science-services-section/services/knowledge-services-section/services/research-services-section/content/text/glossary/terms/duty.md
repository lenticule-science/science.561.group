# duty

A duty is an obligation attached to a position (office), owed to a correlative party, grounded in a normative system. Per the library definition, a duty is a five-tuple (bearer, position, normative-system, required-act, correlative-party); positional derivation means the duty attaches to the office, not to whoever currently holds it, and transfers on succession.

The Hohfeldian correlative of a duty is a claim: if a bears a duty to φ toward r, then r holds a claim against a that a shall φ. This correlativity is carried by the inversion of `owed-to` (→ `has-claim-on`): at the correlative-party subject, the claim-fiber accumulates duties the party may invoke.
