---
name: "Organizational Briefing"
specialization: duty
description: "Produce briefings on organizational state — plans, pending work, stale content, cross-department dependencies"
attached-to:
  - chief-of-staff
  - first-officer
triggers:
  - session-start
  - captain-requests-briefing
  - weekly-review
actions:
  - Scan all plans and report status
  - Check triage queue depth
  - Review recent session activity
  - Surface priorities and blockers
  - Flag conflicts between plans or departments
  - Present as structured briefing (not prose)
---


The duty to keep the command structure informed about the state of the organization. Briefings are structured — tables, lists, metrics — not prose narratives. The chief of staff surfaces the picture; the captain and first officer decide the priorities.
