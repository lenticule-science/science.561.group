# immunity

An immunity is the absence of liability — what cannot be imposed on a position-holder by the powers of another. The Hohfeldian correlative of an immunity is a disability: the party who would otherwise hold a power is disabled from exercising it against the immune position-holder. Immunities protect the integrity of a position against overreach from positions that would otherwise have altering-powers.
