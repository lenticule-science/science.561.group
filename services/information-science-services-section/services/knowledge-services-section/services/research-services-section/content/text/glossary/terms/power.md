# power

A power is the capacity of a position-holder to alter the normative position of another party. The Hohfeldian correlative of a power is a liability: if a holds a power to change r's normative relations, r bears a liability to that change. Powers are scope-bounded and limit-bounded — both scope (whom, what) and limit (what cannot be done via the power) are named explicitly.

Inversion: `creates-liability-in` populates `liable-to` at the liable-party's subject, so walking a party's fiber returns the powers that can affect them.
