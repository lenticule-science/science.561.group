Mass is a fundamental property of [object|objects](object|objects), [quantification|quantifying](quantification|quantifying) the amount of [matter](./matter.md) they contain.

It is a [measuring|measure](measuring|measure) of an object's [resistance](resistance) to [acceleration](acceleration) when a [force](./force.md) is applied, often referred to as [inertia](inertia). Mass also determines the strength of an object's [gravity|gravitational attraction](gravity|gravitational attraction) to other objects with mass. Unlike [weight](weight), which can vary depending on gravity, mass is constant regardless position in [space](space). In [../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/strata/physics](../../../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-02/strata/physics), it is typically measured in [grams](grams) and plays a crucial role in [Newtonian mechanics](Newtonian mechanics) and [Einstein's theory of relativity](Einstein's theory of relativity).
