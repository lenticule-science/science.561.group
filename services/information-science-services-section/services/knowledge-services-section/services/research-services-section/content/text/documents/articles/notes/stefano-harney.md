Stefano Harney is a scholar whose work, developed primarily in collaboration with [Fred Moten](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/fred-moten.md), examines the possibilities for collective intellectual life that persist within and against the university and other institutions.

## Core ideas

- **[The undercommons](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/black-philosophy-services-section/services/black-radical-tradition-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/undercommons.md)**: developed with [Moten](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/fred-moten.md) in *The Undercommons: Fugitive Planning and Black Study* (2013), the [undercommons](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/black-philosophy-services-section/services/black-radical-tradition-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/undercommons.md) names the spaces of study, care, and collective life that exist in [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) of institutional [legibility](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/black-philosophy-services-section/services/abolition-services-section/services/state-repression-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/legibility.md) — not a place but a condition, inhabited through fugitive relation.
- **Logistics and governance**: Harney's work examines how logistical thinking — the management of populations and flows — has become a dominant mode of governance, and how the [undercommons](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/black-philosophy-services-section/services/black-radical-tradition-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/undercommons.md) operates as its [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md).

## Notable works
