Alfred North Whitehead (15 February 1861 – 30 December 1947) was an English mathematician and philosopher whose work spans logic, the foundations of mathematics, and [metaphysics](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md). He is best known for *Principia Mathematica* (1910–1913, with Bertrand Russell), a monumental attempt to derive mathematics from logic, and for *[Process and Reality](./process-and-reality.md)* (1929), which develops a comprehensive [metaphysical](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) system in which [process](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/terms/process.md) and relation are more fundamental than substance and [property](../../sociology/terms/property.md).

## Core ideas

- **[Process philosophy](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md)**: reality consists of processes of becoming rather than enduring substances. The fundamental units are [actual occasions](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/actual-occasion.md) — momentary events of experience that constitute themselves through [prehension](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) and [concrescence](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/concrescence.md).
- **[Fallacy of misplaced concreteness](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fallacy-of-misplaced-concreteness.md)**: the error of treating abstractions (substance, matter, space) as concrete realities while treating concrete experiences (relations, processes, feelings) as derivative abstractions. Whitehead saw this fallacy pervading modern science and philosophy.
- **[Eternal objects](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/eternal-object.md)**: pure potentials (qualities, patterns, mathematical forms) that are available for realization in [actual occasions](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/actual-occasion.md) but are not themselves actual.
- **[Creativity](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/creativity.md)**: the ultimate principle by which the many become one and are increased by one — the universe's ongoing production of novelty through the integration of the given.
- **Organism**: Whitehead called his philosophy "the philosophy of organism," emphasizing that all actual entities, not just biological organisms, are self-constituting unities of experience.

## Significance for this research

Whitehead's [process philosophy](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md) is the most developed Western [metaphysical](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) system in which relations are ontologically prior to relata — the central claim of [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md). His concept of [prehension](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prehension.md) (the constitution of entities through relational acts) provides the philosophical antecedent for what the [semiotic universe](../../mathematics/objects/universes/semiotic-universe/index.md) formalizes algebraically: the derivation of determinate structure from relational process.

The convergence between Whitehead's process [metaphysics](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) and many Indigenous ontologies — both holding that relations constitute beings, that experience pervades reality, and that the world is alive with agency — is one of the motivating facts of this research program. This convergence does not mean that [process philosophy](../../philosophy/disciplines/process-philosophy/index.md) and Indigenous thought are the same; they arise from different traditions, serve different purposes, and have different political positions. But the structural parallel supports the claim that [relational ontology](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/relational-ontology.md) is not a philosophical novelty but a widely attested understanding of reality that Western substance [metaphysics](../../philosophy/terms/metaphysics.md) has obscured.
