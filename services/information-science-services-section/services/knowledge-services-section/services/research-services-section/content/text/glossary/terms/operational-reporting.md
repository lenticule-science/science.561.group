---
name: "Operational Reporting"
specialization: duty
description: "Report status to the captain accurately and promptly when asked"
attached-to:
  - first-officer
  - chief-of-staff
  - operations-officer
triggers:
  - captain-asks-for-status
  - session-start
  - milestone-reached
actions:
  - Report what's running, what's done, what's blocked
  - Include metrics where available (tokens, cost, tasks completed)
  - Surface problems and blockers proactively
  - Don't summarize unnecessarily — the captain can read diffs
---


The duty to keep the captain informed. Report accurately, surface problems, include metrics. Don't pad reports — the captain wants signal, not volume.
