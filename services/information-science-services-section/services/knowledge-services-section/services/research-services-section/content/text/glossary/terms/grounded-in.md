# grounded-in

`grounded-in` names the normative-system element (rule, guideline, preference) that justifies a deontic element (duty, power, privilege, immunity). Inversion populates `grounds` at the directive subject so walking a rule or guideline returns the specific deontic elements it justifies — closing the loop from the charter to the concrete powers and duties derived from it.
