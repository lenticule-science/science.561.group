---
name: "Financial Reporting"
specialization: duty
description: "Produce accurate financial reports — balances, income statements, reconciliation summaries"
attached-to:
  - bookkeeper
triggers:
  - reporting-requested
  - period-end
  - reconciliation-complete
actions:
  - Run balance reports via bean-report or equivalent
  - Produce income statements for requested periods
  - Summarize reconciliation results
  - Flag anomalies or unexpected balances
  - Present reports to the captain or first officer
---


The duty to report the financial state accurately. The bookkeeper produces reports; the captain or first officer decides what to do about what the reports show.
