# actor

`actor` names the person or office whose act produced a record (a decision, an observation, a finding, an adjustment). Inversion populates `acted-in` at the actor subject so a query at a person or office returns the records they authored. Compatible with `assignment-of-work`'s `from-office` / `to-office` shape: the act-entity terms keep authority and authorship as distinct predicates, and the inversion makes both walkable from the actor.
