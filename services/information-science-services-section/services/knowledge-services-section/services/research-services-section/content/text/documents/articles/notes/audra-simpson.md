Audra Simpson is a Mohawk scholar from Kahnawake and a political anthropologist at Columbia University. Her work theorizes [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) as both a political practice and a methodological commitment — Indigenous peoples' active declining of [settler-colonial](../../sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) categories, borders, and knowledge-extraction.

## Core ideas

- **[Refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md)**: the Mohawk practice of refusing Canadian and American citizenship, border regimes, and state-defined Indian identity is not mere non-participation but an assertion of Mohawk sovereignty. Refusal is generative: it creates political space that the settler state cannot govern.
- **Nested sovereignty**: Kahnawake exercises sovereignty within and against the settler states that claim jurisdiction over it, producing a condition of layered and contested authority that cannot be resolved on the settler state's terms.
- **Ethnographic [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md)**: as a methodological commitment, Simpson refuses to make all of her research legible to the academy, withholding knowledge that the community considers private. This challenges the extractive assumptions of social science research.

## Significance for this research

Simpson's theorization of [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) connects to the [relational framework](/relationality/index.md) in its insistence that political subjects are constituted through their relationships — to land, to community, to the structures they refuse — rather than through the identities [the state](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) assigns them.
