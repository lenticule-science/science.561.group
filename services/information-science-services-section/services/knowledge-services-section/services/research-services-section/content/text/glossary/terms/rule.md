# rule

A rule is the highest-ranking directive class in a charter. Rules are non-negotiable; violating a rule is a breach of the charter, not a mistake to be corrected. Rules can only be changed by charter amendment.

Rules outrank guidelines: where a guideline contradicts a rule, the guideline yields. The rank-cascade (rule > guideline > preference > instruction) is carried at the type level here rather than restated at every directive instance, so specializations of `rule` inherit the override relation via the predicate-specialization morphism.
