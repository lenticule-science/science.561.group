A schedule in a command context carries the library definition (time-bound mapping of work units to calendar intervals) plus operational predicates: what work it maps, over what period, and who is responsible.

Schedules turn plan structure into temporal commitments. A plan says "A before B"; a schedule says "A on Monday, B on Wednesday." The schedule is the bridge between dependency ordering and calendar time.
