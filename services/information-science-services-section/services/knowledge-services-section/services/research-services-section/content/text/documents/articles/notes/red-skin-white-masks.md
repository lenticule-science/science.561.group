*Red Skin, White Masks: Rejecting the Colonial Politics of Recognition* [cite:@coulthard_RedSkinWhiteMasks_2014] is a 2014 book by [Glen Coulthard](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/glen-coulthard.md) (Yellowknives Dene). The title inverts [Frantz Fanon](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/frantz-fanon.md)'s *Black Skin, White Masks* [cite:@fanon_BlackSkinWhiteMasks_1952] to situate the analysis in the context of [settler colonialism](../../sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) in northern Turtle Island (Canada).

[Coulthard](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/glen-coulthard.md) argues that the politics of recognition — the demand that the [settler-colonial](../../sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) state acknowledge Indigenous rights, title, and identity — has become a mechanism of colonial governance rather than a path to liberation. Recognition, when granted by the colonizer on the colonizer's terms, reproduces the colonial relationship by making Indigenous freedom dependent on settler approval. [Coulthard](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/glen-coulthard.md) proposes [grounded normativity](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/grounded-normativity.md) as an alternative: ethical and political frameworks rooted in Indigenous relationships to specific lands and practices, rather than in the terms the colonial state offers.

The book is central to contemporary debates on [settler colonialism](/sociology/terms/settler-colonialism.md), [Indigenous resurgence](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/povinellian-services-section/services/settler-colonialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/indigenous-resurgence.md), and the [refusal](../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/services/strategy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) of liberal recognition frameworks.
