A ***log entry*** is an [item](item) in a [log](log), a log being a [record](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/record.md) consisting of a [timestamp](timestamp) and [information](./information.md) about the way some [thing](./thing.md) was at that [time](time).

One [type](type) of *log entry* is a [personal log entry](personal log entry).
