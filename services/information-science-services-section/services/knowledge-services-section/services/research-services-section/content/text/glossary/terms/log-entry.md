A ***log entry*** is an [item](item) in a [log](log), a log being a [record](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/record.md) consisting of a [timestamp](timestamp) and [information](./information.md) about the way some [thing](./thing.md) was at that [time](time).

One [type](type) of *log entry* is a [personal log entry](personal log entry).
