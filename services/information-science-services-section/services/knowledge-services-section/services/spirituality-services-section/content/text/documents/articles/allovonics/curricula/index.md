This curriculum introduces the recursive modulation grammar through three lessons. It begins with the foundational vocabulary — the structural entities that compose the grammar — then moves to the praxonic lens (how to read systems under pressure), and concludes with allovonic reading (how to recognize when systems are shaped by what they cannot contain).

1. [Foundations of recursive modulation](/spirituality/disciplines/allovonics/curricula/foundations-of-recursive-modulation.md)

3. [Reading systems allovonically](/spirituality/disciplines/allovonics/curricula/reading-systems-allovonically.md)
