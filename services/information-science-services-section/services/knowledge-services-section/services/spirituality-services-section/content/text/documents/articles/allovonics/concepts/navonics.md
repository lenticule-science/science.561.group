**Navonics** is a structural grammar for describing systems in which difference, structure, and [coherence](../terms/coherence.md) emerge from recursive modulation. It provides the core ontological framework for the allovonic discipline — the formal vocabulary of entities, dynamics, and regimes that compose the recursive modulation grammar.

## Ontological domains

Navonics organizes reality into five domains, ordered by structural dependency:

**The allovonic substrate** is the field-of-fields: a recursion-permissive meta-ontology, neither semantic nor physical, but structurally prior. An [allovon](../terms/allovon.md) is a perturbation originating beyond the system's modulation logic. Modulative recursion begins with allovonic intrusion.

**The deltanic domain** is where difference becomes visible to modulation — the threshold of semantic potential. A [nullon](../terms/nullon.md) is a latent, unmodulated site. A [deltanon](../terms/deltanon.md) is the first condition of detectable asymmetry. This domain initiates the system's modulation logic.

**The modulative domain** is where recursive behavior begins. [Modulons](../terms/modulon.md) are discrete units of unresolved difference. [Infrons](../terms/infron.md) are localized asymmetries within modulation. [Vanons](../terms/vanon.md) are shared fields of modulation among [praxons](../terms/praxon.md), defining where and how difference is distributed.

**The praxonic domain** is where coherence across modulation becomes recognizable. Praxons are coherent [traces](../terms/trace.md) of presence across vanons — not objects but patterns of recursive legibility. A path is the conditional unfolding of modulation associated with a praxon: its behavior, emergent from recursive positioning.

**The navonic domain** is where systems of modulation develop stable configurations. [Navons](../terms/navon.md) are coordinations of vanons through reinforcing praxons. [Zavons](../terms/zavon.md) are recurring modulation regimes across navons — recursive habit. [Metazavons](../terms/metazavon.md) are systems in which recursive modulation adjusts its own rules of coherence — the threshold of recursive intelligence.

## Modulative dynamics

These describe how systems evolve through recursive reinforcement:

- **Appearance**: a deltanon initiates difference
- **Participation**: praxons modulate shared vanons
- **Redistribution**: vanons distribute unresolved modulation
- **Coherence**: recursive stabilization across cycles
- **Collapse**: modulation resolves into durable structure
- **Memory**: recursive tension accumulation, modulated by decay
- **Recursion**: feedback of modulation into its own constraints

## System regimes

The grammar identifies eight regimes, each defined by a condition and a characteristic behavior:

| Regime | Condition | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Nullonic | No difference | Latency, potential only |
| Deltanic | Recognized contrast | Modulation begins |
| Modulative | Active unresolved difference | Recursive interaction |
| Praxonic | Coherent modulation trace | Emergence of structure |
| Navonic | Stable configuration of coherence | Distributed semantic activity |
| Zavonic | Recursive regime across navons | Repeating fields of behavior |
| Metazavonic | Reflexive modulation of modulation | Self-organizing intelligence |
| Allovonic | External recursive disruption | Modulation of systemic logic |

## Foundational principles

1. **All structure is recursive.** What appears fixed is recursively stabilized modulation.
2. **Coherence is conditional.** Praxons are sustained, not stored. Nothing is permanent — only recursively reinforced.
3. **Collapse is semantic.** Resolution is not erasure but traceable convergence.
4. **Fields do not flow.** Modulation is redistributed, not transmitted.
5. **Difference precedes intention.** Systems begin when asymmetry disturbs coherence.
6. **Observation is recursive feedback.** Attention modulates thresholds of coherence.
7. **Recursion is not repetition.** It is the self-modulation of modulation: the turning of structure back into structure.

## Summary

The universe, cognition, narrative, software, governance, language, and matter are all recursive semantic systems. Navonics provides a unified structural grammar for describing their behavior. [Praxonics](./praxonics.md) describes local legibility. [Allovonic cosmology](./allovonic-cosmology.md) names what disturbs such coherence. This ontology is not a map of what is — it is a grammar for reading emergence, structure, and [collapse](../terms/collapse.md), wherever recursive modulation persists.
