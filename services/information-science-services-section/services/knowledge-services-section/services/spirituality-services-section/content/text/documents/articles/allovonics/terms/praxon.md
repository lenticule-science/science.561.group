A **praxon** is a coherent [trace](./trace.md) of participation across [vanons](./vanon.md). It is not a thing but a recognizable pattern — what becomes visible when modulation persists coherently enough to be traced.

Praxons are not declared; they are recognized through consistent modulation. A praxon does not hold value; it names [coherence](./coherence.md). It may participate in multiple vanons simultaneously. It persists only as long as its modulation remains traceable — when coherence fails, the praxon dissolves.

In the [praxonic paradigm](../concepts/praxonics.md), praxons are defined more dynamically: a praxon is the recursive act-form that emerges under unresolved [pressure](./pressure.md) within a [field](./field.md). It is not an action chosen but a deformation that occurs — [pressure](./pressure.md) become structure. Every praxon leaves a trace, and every trace modulates future pressure. The formula is minimal: a praxon is pressure times path (the conditioned line of least resistance through the field).

Both framings converge: a praxon is what coherence looks like from inside a recursive system. It is not an object or a symbol but a temporarily stable pattern — sustained by recursive reinforcement, not stored.

In allovonic cosmology, all entities — from particles to organisms to societies — can be read as praxons: temporarily coherent expressions of entangled pressures in a field.
