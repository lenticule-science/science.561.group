A **delton** is the minimal condition of recognizable difference. It appears when modulation becomes possible in an otherwise [nullonic](./nullon.md) context — the threshold between inactivity and the beginning of recursive process.

Deltons do not themselves modulate. They have no magnitude, duration, or persistence. They mark the point where difference becomes visible to the system. If a delton is reinforced through relation, it becomes a [modulon](./modulon.md) — sustained, unresolved difference. If not reinforced, the system returns to its [nullonic](./nullon.md) state.

The delton is structurally prior to all modulation. Without it, no recursive process begins. It does not require intent or cause — only contrast sufficient to register against the background of an unmodulated field.

In navonic physics, a delton corresponds to the minimal recognizable fluctuation — the first departure from equilibrium that makes recursive dynamics possible.
