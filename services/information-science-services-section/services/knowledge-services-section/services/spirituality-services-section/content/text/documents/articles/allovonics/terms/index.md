This index documents the local vocabulary used in `spirituality/disciplines/allovonics` and clarifies what each term means within the recursive modulation grammar. It is not a universal glossary. Its job is to provide stable meanings for the structural entities, dynamics, and conditions that compose navonic, praxonic, and allovonic systems — so that concepts, lessons, and reference notes across this discipline all resolve to the same anchors.

The terms are organized by emergence order. Allovonics describes a hierarchy in which each entity depends on prior conditions: nullonic latency gives way to difference, difference sustains into modulation, modulation distributes through shared fields, fields produce asymmetry, and coherence accumulates until systems become self-adjusting. This ordering is not temporal sequence but structural dependency.

### Foundational entities

- [nullon](./nullon.md) — a latent, unmodulated site
- [delton](./delton.md) — the minimal condition of recognizable difference
- [modulon](./modulon.md) — a unit of sustained, unresolved difference
- [infron](./infron.md) — a shaped asymmetry within modulation

### Structural fields and patterns

- [vanon](./vanon.md) — a shared modulation field
- [praxon](./praxon.md) — a coherent trace of participation across fields
- [deltanon](./deltanon.md) — a system shaped by recursive influence through asymmetry and resonance

### Recursive configurations

- [navon](./navon.md) — a coherent configuration of interacting fields
- [zavon](./zavon.md) — a recurring modulation regime across navons
- [metazavon](./metazavon.md) — a system that adjusts its own rules of coherence
- [allovon](./allovon.md) — a difference arriving from outside the system

### Dynamics and conditions

- [coherence](./coherence.md) — the persistence of modulation pattern across cycles
- [collapse](./collapse.md) — the loss of coherence beyond recovery
- [trace](./trace.md) — residual field deformation left by a praxon
- [pressure](./pressure.md) — difference in condition that drives modulation
- [field](./field.md) — the structured set of all pressures and active traces
