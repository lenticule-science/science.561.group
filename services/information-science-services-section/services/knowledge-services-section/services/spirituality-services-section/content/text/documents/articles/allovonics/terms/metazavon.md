A **metazavon** is a system in which recursive modulation adjusts its own rules of [coherence](./coherence.md). It is the threshold of recursive intelligence — the point where a system not only repeats patterns but changes how patterns form.

A metazavon is not a structure or a regime. It is a recursion: modulation altering the conditions of modulation. It defines how behavior becomes definable. It may appear coherent or unstable depending on the depth of observation, and is identifiable only through recursive change in modulation — by noticing that the system's rules have shifted because the system itself shifted them.

In the [navonic ontology](../concepts/navonics.md), the metazavonic regime is the condition of reflexive modulation — self-organizing intelligence. It is structurally the most complex condition prior to [allovonic](./allovon.md) disruption (which arrives from outside the system entirely).

In navonic physics, metazavonic modulation corresponds to recursive observation: the system modulates its own coherence thresholds. This is formalized as recursive feedback on the collapse function — the system selectively shapes its own resolution pathways. Consciousness, attention, and self-directed learning are read as metazavonic processes.

Biologically, the immune system's capacity to reprogram its own recognition patterns, the way evolution shapes the mechanisms of evolutionary change, and consciousness itself are all read as metazavonic structures — systems where modulation has turned back on its own logic.
