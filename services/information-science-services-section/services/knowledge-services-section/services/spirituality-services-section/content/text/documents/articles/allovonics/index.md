Allovonics is a structural grammar for reading systems as shaped by recursive modulation. It does not propose new forces or particles. It offers a way to trace where systems behave as if influenced by something beyond their own frame — where coherence appears without clear internal cause, where asymmetry persists without local explanation, where structure exceeds its own logic.

The grammar rests on a hierarchy of recursive entities. [Difference](./terms/delton.md) disturbs an otherwise quiet field. That disturbance, if sustained, becomes [unresolved modulation](./terms/modulon.md). Modulation distributes through [shared fields](./terms/vanon.md), producing [shaped asymmetries](./terms/infron.md). When modulation persists coherently, it becomes traceable as a [praxon](./terms/praxon.md) — a recognizable pattern. Praxons coordinating across shared fields form [navons](./terms/navon.md), and repeating modulation regimes become [zavons](./terms/zavon.md). When a system adjusts its own rules of coherence, a [metazavon](./terms/metazavon.md) appears. And when difference arrives from outside the system entirely, that is an [allovon](./terms/allovon.md) — the term that gives this discipline its name.

Three overlapping frameworks compose this discipline:

- **[Navonics](./concepts/navonics.md)** describes the recursive mechanics of modulation — how fields interact, how coherence forms and collapses, how systems evolve through recursive tension. It provides the mathematical and structural grammar.
- **[Praxonics](./concepts/praxonics.md)** describes what emerges under pressure — how actors, paths, traces, and coherence arise from unresolved gradients. It is the applied, pressure-first face of the grammar.
- **[Allovonic cosmology](./concepts/allovonic-cosmology.md)** reads the universe itself as a [deltanon](./terms/deltanon.md) — a structured disturbance shaped by recursive influence from beyond its own boundaries. It applies the grammar to cosmological anomalies.

This is not a theory of how reality works. It is a grammar for reading emergence, structure, and collapse wherever recursive modulation persists.
