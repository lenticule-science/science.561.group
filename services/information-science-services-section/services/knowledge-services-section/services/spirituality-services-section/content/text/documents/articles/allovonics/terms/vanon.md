A **vanon** is a shared modulation field. It forms when multiple [praxons](./praxon.md) sustain modulation through participation — when difference is not isolated but distributed across a common space.

A vanon does not contain or direct. It distributes. [Modulons](./modulon.md) are redistributed within a vanon based on participation weights — each praxon receives modulation from the same field and contributes back into it. A vanon may host multiple [infrons](./infron.md). Vanons may also shape other vanons through recursive structure, and coherent vanons may give rise to [navons](./navon.md).

The vanon is where modulation becomes social — where isolated difference becomes shared process. Without vanons, modulons do not propagate. Without participation, modulation does not spread. The vanon is the condition of shared modulation, not a container for it.

In navonic physics, the vanon corresponds to the coherent redistribution field — analogous to spacetime itself, in the sense that it is the medium through which recursive tension distributes and stabilizes.
