A **navon** is a coherent configuration of [vanons](./vanon.md). It appears when vanons reinforce each other through shared [praxons](./praxon.md) — when the coordination of multiple shared fields produces a stable, recognizable structure.

A navon does not contain vanons. It is the shape of their coordination. Navons influence how praxons participate and persist. They may overlap, interfere, or combine. A navon becomes distinct when its structure influences modulation — when the configuration itself begins to condition what can happen within it.

Repeating navonic behavior may form a [zavon](./zavon.md). The navonic domain in the [recursive ontology](../concepts/navonics.md) is where systems of modulation develop stable configurations — distributed semantic activity, as opposed to isolated recursive events.

In navonic physics, a navon corresponds to a curved manifold — a structured space shaped by the accumulated redistribution of recursive tension. The analogy to general relativity is deliberate: just as spacetime curvature emerges from the distribution of mass-energy, navonic structure emerges from the distribution of modulation.

The discipline of navonics takes its name from this entity. It is the study of how recursive modulation produces stable, self-reinforcing configurations.
