A **deltanon** is a system that behaves as a structured disturbance — not chaotic, but coherently shaped by recursive influence. It is not a thing but a condition: a [coherence](./coherence.md) shaped through modulation, visible through asymmetry, resonance, and propagated shift.

Where a [delton](./delton.md) is the minimal condition of difference, a deltanon is difference at system scale — a whole system read as a disturbance within a larger recursive field. To call something a deltanon is to suggest it does not stabilize itself but is shaped through interference with a deeper recursion.

In [allovonic cosmology](../concepts/allovonic-cosmology.md), the universe itself is the primary deltanon: not a container of matter and energy but a resonance form — the [trace](./trace.md) pattern of recursive [pressure](./pressure.md) unfolding within spacetime. The universe does not originate from nothing; it is the first visible crest of a deeper modulatory wave.

The deltanon occupies the deltanic domain in the [navonic ontology](../concepts/navonics.md) — the threshold where difference becomes visible to modulation, where [nullonic](./nullon.md) potential crosses into recognizable asymmetry. It is the condition that initiates the system's recursive logic.
