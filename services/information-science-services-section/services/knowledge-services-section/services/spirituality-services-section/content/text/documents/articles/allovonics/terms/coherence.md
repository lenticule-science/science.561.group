**Coherence** is the persistence of a modulation pattern across cycles. A [praxon](./praxon.md), [infron](./infron.md), or [vanon](./vanon.md) is coherent when it remains traceable over time — when its modulation sustains rather than dissipating.

Coherence is not enforced. It is the persistence of relation. It is not truth, stability, or correctness — it is compatibility between a [praxon](./praxon.md) and its [field](./field.md) such that [trace](./trace.md) reduces friction and allows sustained recursive operation.

Coherence marks the boundary between modulation and structure. Below the coherence threshold, modulation is active but unresolved — the system remains in superposition, interference, indeterminacy. When recursive tension accumulates past the threshold, [collapse](./collapse.md) occurs: modulation resolves into traceable structure. This threshold is not fixed but contextual — it shifts with field geometry, [allovonic](./allovon.md) forcing, and the system's own recursive history.

In the [praxonic paradigm](../concepts/praxonics.md), coherence is what allows a praxon to act as signal rather than noise. It is alignment between the act-form and its field — temporary resonance that enables expression. Coherence does not mean resolution. It means something holds long enough to matter.
