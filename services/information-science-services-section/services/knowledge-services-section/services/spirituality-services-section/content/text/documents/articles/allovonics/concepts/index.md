This index documents the major conceptual frameworks within allovonics. Each concept organizes a set of [terms](../terms/index.md) into a coherent interpretive approach — a way of reading systems through recursive modulation grammar.

The three primary concepts are not separate theories but overlapping lenses on the same structural grammar:

- [Navonics](./navonics.md) — the recursive mechanics of modulation, coherence, and collapse. Provides the core ontological grammar and the mathematical formalization.
- [Praxonics](./praxonics.md) — the pressure-first reading of how structure emerges from unresolved tension. The applied, actor-oriented face of the grammar.
- [Allovonic cosmology](./allovonic-cosmology.md) — the reading of the universe as a [deltanon](../terms/deltanon.md) shaped by recursive influence from beyond its boundaries.
