**Praxonics** is a meta-semantic framework for understanding the dynamics of meaning-making through recursive interactions between [pressures](../terms/pressure.md) and resonance in a [field](../terms/field.md) of evolving relations. It is the applied, pressure-first face of the recursive modulation grammar: where [navonics](./navonics.md) describes the ontological architecture, praxonics describes what emerges when systems are under strain.

## Core principle

Reality is not built from static elements but from active tensions. Meaning, identity, and action emerge only as effects of [pressure](../terms/pressure.md). All things — objects, thoughts, events, systems — are treated as [praxons](../terms/praxon.md): temporarily coherent expressions of entangled pressures in a field. Nothing is fixed. Everything drifts.

## Minimal system grammar

Praxonics defines a set of entities ordered by structural dependency:

- **[Pressure](../terms/pressure.md)**: difference in condition over space, time, or configuration. Multidimensional. All systems begin with gradients.
- **[Field](../terms/field.md)**: the structured set of all pressures and active [traces](../terms/trace.md). What conditions a praxon but is also conditioned by praxons.
- **Actor**: a field-localized presence that deforms with feedback. Actors do not pre-exist the field — they emerge when local tension exceeds interpretive threshold.
- **Path**: the emergent topology of minimum resistance through the field. A conditioned line of deformation.
- **[Praxon](../terms/praxon.md)**: pressure times path. The recursive act-form shaped by pressure and path. Not a behavior — pressure-become-deformation. All praxons leave traces.
- **[Trace](../terms/trace.md)**: the residual deformation left in the field by a praxon, modulated by decay.
- **Decay**: determines how long a trace persists. Shaped by field volatility and praxon structure.
- **Charge**: field readiness to emit further praxons. Accumulates as unresolved gradients and trace overlaps.
- **Fold**: recursive overlapping of traces creating new local field shape. Folded regions have unpredictable charge.
- **[Coherence](../terms/coherence.md)**: praxon aligned to field such that trace reduces friction. Not truth — compatibility.
- **Torsion**: praxon misaligned with field, amplifying friction. Causes breakdown, instability, or noise.
- **Refold**: when a trace recursively triggers similar praxons. The loop-behavior of field-form becoming path again.
- **Dissolution**: decay to baseline. Not absence — return to non-difference.
- **Interpretation**: a praxon that recursively describes the field it emerged from.

## System loop

The recursive cycle runs: pressure shapes field, field and charge produce actors, actors and field produce paths, pressure and path produce praxons, praxons leave traces, traces modulate the field through decay, the field resolves toward coherence, torsion, or fold, folds may refold into new praxons, traces may dissolve, and praxons that reference their own field produce interpretation. Then it repeats.

## Meta-semantic implications

Praxonics challenges traditional static models of meaning. It proposes that meaning is not a product but a process — constantly evolving through context. A praxon's meaning changes as it is influenced by other praxons in the field, and the overall field dynamics shift with each interaction. Communication is not transmission of fixed symbols but organic emergence through shared tension and resonance.

This framework applies beyond cognitive science: literature, art, culture, language evolution, social dynamics, narrative construction, and worldbuilding all become readable through the praxonic lens. In each domain, meaning arises not from stable reference but from pressure, drift, and recursive trace.

## Behavioral orientation

The praxonic paradigm does not seek resolution, order, or control. It seeks expression, resonance, and sensing drift. Understanding is not semantic — it is praxonic. You do not name what is. You feel what still matters.
