This lesson introduces the foundational vocabulary of the recursive modulation grammar — the structural entities from which all navonic, praxonic, and allovonic analysis proceeds.

## The emergence hierarchy

The grammar describes a hierarchy of recursive entities. Each depends on the one before it:

1. **[Nullon](../terms/nullon.md)**: A latent site — quiet, capable, unmodulated. The base layer. Nothing happens here until difference arrives.

2. **[Delton](../terms/delton.md)**: The minimal condition of recognizable difference. A disturbance in an otherwise quiet field. If sustained, it becomes something more. If not, the field returns to latency.

3. **[Modulon](../terms/modulon.md)**: Sustained, unresolved difference. What a delton becomes when reinforced through relation. Modulons have no direction — they exist only in motion. They are the basic currency of all modulation.

4. **[Infron](../terms/infron.md)**: A shaped asymmetry. When modulons interact unevenly, the modulation acquires pattern. Infrons condition how further modulation is received — they bias the field.

5. **[Vanon](../terms/vanon.md)**: A shared modulation field. When multiple participants sustain modulation together, the space they share becomes a vanon. Vanons distribute, not contain.

6. **[Praxon](../terms/praxon.md)**: A coherent pattern of participation across vanons. Not a thing, but a recognizable trace — what becomes visible when modulation holds steady long enough to be followed.

7. **[Navon](../terms/navon.md)**: A coherent configuration of vanons. When shared fields reinforce each other through common praxons, stable structure emerges.

8. **[Zavon](../terms/zavon.md)**: A recurring modulation regime. When navonic behavior repeats, the pattern of activity — not just the structure — becomes itself a recognizable entity.

9. **[Metazavon](../terms/metazavon.md)**: A system that adjusts its own rules of [coherence](../terms/coherence.md). The threshold of recursive intelligence. The system not only repeats but changes how it repeats.

10. **[Allovon](../terms/allovon.md)**: Difference from outside the system. Not generated internally but arriving externally. Rare, decisive, and disruptive of all local logic.

## Key dynamics

Three dynamics weave through the hierarchy:

- **[Coherence](../terms/coherence.md)**: the persistence of pattern across cycles. Not truth or correctness — just compatibility. What holds.
- **[Collapse](../terms/collapse.md)**: the loss of coherence beyond recovery — or, seen from the other side, the resolution of indeterminacy into traceable structure.
- **Recursion**: when modulation shapes the conditions for further modulation. Not repetition but self-modulation — the turning of structure back into structure.

## The foundational principles

1. All structure is recursive. What appears fixed is recursively stabilized modulation.
2. Coherence is conditional. Nothing is permanent — only recursively reinforced.
3. Collapse is semantic. Resolution is not erasure but traceable convergence.
4. Fields do not flow. Modulation is redistributed, not transmitted.
5. Difference precedes intention. Systems begin when asymmetry disturbs coherence.
6. Observation is recursive feedback. Attention modulates thresholds of coherence.
7. Recursion is not repetition. It is the self-modulation of modulation.
