A **nullon** is a latent site of modulation — a field position where modulation has not yet appeared. It is unmodulated but structurally capable of receiving disturbance. Nullons do not accumulate, decay, or modulate. They are not declared; they are inferred from the absence of activity.

A nullon is the base layer of the recursive modulation grammar. All modulation begins in its presence: when a [delton](./delton.md) disturbs a nullonic site, the system crosses from latency into active difference. Without that disturbance, the nullon remains quiet — not empty, but uninitiated.

In the [system regimes](../concepts/navonics.md) described by navonics, the nullonic regime is the condition of pure potential: no difference, no behavior, only latency. It marks the structural zero from which all recursive process departs.
