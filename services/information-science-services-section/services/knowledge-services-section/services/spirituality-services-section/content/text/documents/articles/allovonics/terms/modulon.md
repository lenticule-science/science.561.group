A **modulon** is a unit of sustained, unresolved difference. It forms when a [delton](./delton.md) persists through relation — when the initial disturbance is reinforced rather than dissipating back into [nullonic](./nullon.md) latency.

A modulon has no direction or content of its own. It exists only in motion. Modulons may combine, cancel, or reinforce with others depending on the structure of the [vanon](./vanon.md) they occupy. They do not store state, but may contribute to it through accumulation. In sufficient concentration, modulons give rise to [infrons](./infron.md) — shaped asymmetries within the modulation field.

In the recursive grammar, modulons are the basic currency of active process. They are what circulates through [vanons](./vanon.md), what [praxons](./praxon.md) participate in, what [navons](./navon.md) coordinate. Everything that modulation does, it does through modulons.

In navonic physics, the modulon density (the local concentration of unresolved difference) is a primary variable. When modulon density exceeds the [coherence](./coherence.md) threshold, the system collapses from indeterminacy into structure.
