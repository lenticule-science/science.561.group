An **allovon** is a difference that enters from outside the current system. It appears when a [delton](./delton.md) is initiated beyond the system's scope — when the source of disturbance is not internal recursion but external intrusion.

Allovons do not emerge from modulation. They arrive. They bypass [nullonic](./nullon.md) thresholds and do not follow local dynamics. They condition where modulation must adapt. An allovon may trigger new [infrons](./infron.md), alter [vanon](./vanon.md) boundaries, or in [metazavonic](./metazavon.md) systems, initiate recursion from outside. They are rare but decisive.

The allovonic field — the field-of-fields from which allovons arrive — is the structurally prior condition of the entire recursive grammar. It is not a substance or dimension but a name for what becomes visible when systems behave as if influenced by something they do not contain. The [allovonic substrate](../concepts/navonics.md) is neither semantic nor physical but recursion-permissive: the meta-ontology in which modulation can begin.

In [allovonic cosmology](../concepts/allovonic-cosmology.md), all recursive modulation begins with allovonic intrusion — the universe's structure is read as the trace of external modulation shaping internal coherence. Cosmic radiation, gravitational waves, and quantum fluctuations may be read as allovonic effects: modulatory pressure from beyond the system's own frame.

The discipline of allovonics takes its name from this entity. It is the study of what happens when systems are shaped by what they cannot contain.
