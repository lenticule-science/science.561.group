Divination practices interpret patterns or [signs](../../../../../../../../../../communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semiotics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/sign.md) as sources of insight.

Key elements include:

- a medium (cards, stars, lots, patterns),
- a question or focus,
- a method for interpreting results.
