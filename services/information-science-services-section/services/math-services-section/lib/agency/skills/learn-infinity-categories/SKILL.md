# Learn Infinity Categories

Ordinary category theory has objects and morphisms, with morphisms either equal or not. Infinity categories add coherent higher morphisms — paths between morphisms (2-morphisms), paths between paths (3-morphisms), and so on — making homotopy data part of the structure rather than an afterthought.

## What you will be able to do

- Explain why ordinary categories are insufficient for homotopy theory: equality of morphisms is too strict; equivalence-up-to-coherent-homotopy is what you actually want.
- Describe the role of higher morphisms: 2-morphisms are paths between morphisms, 3-morphisms are paths between those paths, and so on. Coherence relates the layers.
- Identify quasi-categories (simplicial sets satisfying the inner Kan extension property) as one working model, and locate alternatives (complete Segal spaces, Joyal's $\infty$-categories, model-categorical presentations).
- Read introductory formal statements about $\infty$-categorical limits, colimits, adjunctions, and equivalences without conflating them with their 1-categorical versions.

## Prerequisites

- `learn-category-theory` — ordinary categories.

## Verification

State, in your own words, why a 2-morphism in an ordinary category (where morphisms have no internal structure) is necessarily an equality. Then describe what a 2-morphism is in a quasi-category model, and explain why this is what's wanted for tracking homotopy.
