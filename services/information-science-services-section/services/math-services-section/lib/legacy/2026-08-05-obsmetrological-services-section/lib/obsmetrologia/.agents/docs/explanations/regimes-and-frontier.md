# Regimes and Frontier

Obsmetrologia documentation must say not only what an object is, but where it
lives in the project's maturity and regime structure.

The site guidance names regime signatures such as strict finite, Scott
continuous, transfinite bounded, and aperiodic. The documentation surfaces also
use status distinctions: foundation, recovery, frontier, provenance, archived,
proved, and open.

These are not labels added after the fact. They determine how a claim may be
spoken.

## Regime Is Mathematical Information

A finite charge calculation, a Scott-continuity test, a transfinite collapse, and
an aperiodic classification frontier do not have the same documentation voice.

For each page, name:

- the potential regime, if the local source names one;
- the carrier being measured in that regime;
- the claim status;
- the downstream surfaces that use the regime reading.

When the regime is not known, say that the regime is not yet identified rather
than guessing from the subject matter.

## Frontier Is Not Failure

The local proof discipline allows a precise frontier to remain visible. In
Blueprint, `\notready` marks stated but unsettled obligations. In Lean, a
frontier proof can remain visible when the real statement is not yet proved, but
exported or green claims must be clean.

For agent docs, the rule is:

- teach proved results as proved only when Lean and Blueprint support that;
- teach red nodes as frontier obligations;
- teach exploratory notes as provenance or frontier pressure;
- do not rewrite a frontier into a weaker statement just to make prose smoother.

## Recovery Is Downstream

Many familiar objects appear in the current project: orders, adjunctions,
equality, sheaves, cohomology, dynamics, probability-like carriers, spacetime
readings, contextuality, reaction networks, thermodynamics, and computing
formats.

Document them as recovered or projected surfaces unless the local source says
they are foundational. A recovered object should name:

- the familiar comparison;
- the Obsmetrologia construction that generates it;
- the Lean and Blueprint carrier;
- the missing stronger external theorem, if any.

## Provenance Is Not Normative Documentation

Corpus and development notes can be the source of a term without already being
the stable manual account. When using provenance:

- cite the path;
- summarize the promoted reading;
- keep old language visibly old if it is not the current term;
- do not treat an archive note as a proof.

## Newly Revealed Obligation

Regime/status needs to be part of every coverage entry. A page that says what a
thing is but not whether it is proved, frontier, recovery, or provenance is
incomplete.
