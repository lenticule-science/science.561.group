# Concept Glossary

This glossary is a starting point for agent navigation. Check live modules and
Blueprint nodes before using any entry in final mathematical prose.

## Mark

Primitive admission. Do not identify it with a point, set element, proposition,
symbol, or object unless a local carrier recovers that reading.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Primitive/Mark.lean`.

## Kind

The token/claim positional distinction for marks.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Primitive/Mark.lean`.

## Utterance

The paired token/claim carrier that feeds articulating.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Articulation/Articulating/Utterance.lean`.

## Articulating

The recursive articulating carrier generated from utterances.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Articulation/Articulating.lean`.

## Four-Corner Cell

The first finite fourfold carrier with potential, active, stable, and absurd
corners.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Articulation/Articulating/Cell.lean`.

## Charge

The positing/value reading of the fourfold surface, with knowledge and truth
operations.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Charge.lean`.

## Knowledge Order

The generated order surface for information accumulation over charge and
valuation.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Settling.lean`.

## Truth Order

The second order surface tracking truth-facing polarity and its closure
behavior.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/TruthOrder.lean`.

## Valuation

A charge assignment over predication cells, used by reindexing, quantification,
settling, sheaf, and cohomology surfaces.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Valuation.lean`.

## Predication Cell

The subject-predicate-object cell generated after articulation, not a generic
import of external predicate logic.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Predicating/Cell.lean`.

## Settling

Closure/fixed-point behavior generated over charge and valuation. Do not reduce
it to ordinary truth evaluation.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Settling.lean`.

## Quartet Recurrence

The recurring fourfold shape across articulation, charge, modal, nuclear, and
regime surfaces. Some recurrence links are proved carriers; some are frontier
obligations.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Quartet.lean`.

## Sheaf

A downstream site/section surface generated after valuation and settling. Do
not use it as an initial topological foundation.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Site/Sheaf.lean`.

## Cohomology

Obstruction and coordination surfaces downstream of settling, sheaves, and
closure. Check each concept's status before saying what vanishes or classifies.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Cohomology/`.

## Aperiodic Persistence

Persistence and classification surfaces for aperiodic, transfinite, finite
witness, diffraction, and moduli behavior.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Persistence/Aperiodic/`.

## Relational Boundary Sector

Downstream physics-facing projections such as contextuality, material states,
dense matter, plasma, fluid, reaction, and kinetic surfaces.

Carrier hint: `Obsmetrologia/Relational/`.

## Information Projection

Textile, tile, bead, InfoHair, and format-facing projections named by Blueprint
and persistence/computing carriers.

Carrier hint: `docs/blueprint/src/chapters/information/` and
`Obsmetrologia/Computing/`.
