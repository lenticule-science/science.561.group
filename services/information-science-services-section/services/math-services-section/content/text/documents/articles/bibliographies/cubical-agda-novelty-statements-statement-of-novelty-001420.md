This work presents a novel formalization of recursive relational dynamics within Cubical Agda, synthesizing concepts from homotopy type theory (HoTT), coinductive structures, and higher inductive types to articulate a multi-layered architecture of coherence and reflexivity. While coinductive types and greatest fixpoints are established tools for modeling infinite data structures and behaviors, the present formalization introduces a distinctive advancement through its integrated treatment of **predictive unfolding, layered meta-reflexivity, and infinite self-coherence.**

Key novel contributions include:

1. **Recursive Relational Architecture:**  
    A systematic progression from minimal cohesion units to multi-relational, boundary-aware, and meta-relational constructs, each defined via minimal higher inductive types that formalize acts, conditions, and structures across successive layers. This modular build-up mirrors a philosophical grammar of persistence, grounding each new level of coherence in reflexive relations to prior stages.
    
2. **Predictive Determination Formalized:**  
    The framework embeds a principle of _predictive determination_, wherein each stabilized structure not only sustains itself through reflexivity but anticipates and necessitates its own unfolding into a higher-order relational form. This dynamic, formalized in dependent path types, encodes a logic of recursive openness rarely made explicit in type-theoretic frameworks.
    
3. **Infinite Coinductive Unfolding with Meta-Reflexive Closure:**  
    Extending the recursive system, an infinite stream of domain unfoldings is coinductively constructed, reflecting the system’s perpetual openness. Crucially, this is not left as an unstructured infinity: the framework introduces a **meta-reflexive fixpoint theorem,** proving that the infinite unfolding process itself is **self-similar and self-stabilizing.** This establishes a novel formal model in which infinite progression and reflexive closure coexist, ensuring that open-ended relational expansion remains coherently grounded.
    
4. **Philosophical Minimalism with Formal Precision:**  
    Each stage of the architecture adheres to a strict principle of minimalism: no unnecessary constructors or coherence conditions are introduced beyond what is required by the internal logic of relational persistence. This achieves a tight correspondence between philosophical clarity and formal elegance.
    

To the best of our knowledge, while coinductive streams and reflexivity are well-established in type theory, no prior formal system has integrated these elements into a **hierarchically layered architecture that both anticipates its own infinite extension and reflexively proves its own coherence at the meta-level.** This work therefore bridges foundational questions in philosophy and mathematics with rigorous constructive formalism, opening new possibilities for reasoning about persistent, evolving systems that are both **infinitely extensible and internally coherent.**
