# Pierce 2002 — Types and Programming Languages

Benjamin C. Pierce, *Types and Programming Languages*, MIT Press, 2002.

The standard graduate textbook on type theory from the programming-languages perspective. Covers untyped λ-calculus, [simply-typed-lambda-calculus](../../../glossary/terms/simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md), subtyping, recursive types, polymorphism (System F), higher-order types, and a brief introduction to [dependent-type-theory](../../../glossary/terms/dependent-type-theory.md).

## Why cited here

- Definitive presentation of [subject-reduction](../research-articles/subject-reduction.md), [progress](../research-articles/progress.md), and the type-safety proof structure.
- Standard treatment of [type-checking](../../../glossary/terms/type-checking.md), [type-erasure](../../../glossary/terms/type-erasure.md), and Hindley-Milner-style [principal-typing-stlc](../research-articles/principal-typing-stlc.md).
- Introduces the [curry-howard-correspondence](../research-articles/curry-howard-correspondence.md) (§9.4) and the propositions-as-types view in a compact form.

## Used by

Cited from most theorem and morphism notes in this directory. The companion volume *Advanced Topics in Types and Programming Languages* (Pierce ed., 2005) is the natural follow-on.
