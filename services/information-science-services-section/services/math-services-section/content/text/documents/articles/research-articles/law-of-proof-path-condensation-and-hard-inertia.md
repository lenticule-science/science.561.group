# The law of proof-path condensation and hard inertia

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** Human Leader operational directive and working mathematical synthesis  
**Scope:** The Union Semioverse, Semiotic Exchange Chain, Wuxian Word World, and every byte-bearing software, research, protocol, game, interface, organizational, and historical artifact available to them

## Directive

The Union does not suffer primarily from missing software. It suffers because the dimensions of already-created capabilities are scattered across many disconnected generations, carriers, repositories, packages, notes, transcripts, deployments, and broken or abandoned artifacts. New agents encounter a low-dimensional local projection and fabricate another implementation of what the wider body already knows.

The governing repair is therefore **proof-path condensation with hard inertia**:

1. admit every relevant set of bytes as a candidate carrier of capability;
2. articulate the transformations and distinctions latent in those bytes;
3. derive the typed domain, morphism, and codomain;
4. normalize them to their earned proof-path address;
5. condense every unique capability dimension into the owning implementation;
6. redirect every consumer, release, deployment, and protocol edge to that owner;
7. remove redundant executable, generated, broken, disabled, archived, and otherwise misleading copies once their unique information has been absorbed;
8. publish and settle the content-addressed bound state through the existing network.

This is not a request to add a coordinate ontology, preferred wrapper, compatibility layer, policy package, or another software generation. It is a law for reducing scattered generations into richer, addressable particles.

## Physical inertia

For a coupled system with generalized coordinates \(q\), momentum and force are:

\[
p=M\dot q,
\qquad
F=\frac{d}{dt}(M\dot q).
\]

Its kinetic energy is:

\[
T=\frac12\dot q^{\mathsf T}M(q)\dot q.
\]

The mass matrix \(M\) determines which coordinates move together when force is applied. The repository contains enormous material mass, but its effective mass matrix is nearly block-diagonal: HTML rendering, semantic hypertext, three-dimensional worlds, parsing, authority, settlement, cloud fabrication, games, content mills, and interfaces occupy disconnected local modes. Force applied to one implementation moves only that implementation.

If local capability sections are \(s_i\), with restriction maps assembled into a sheaf-like coboundary \(\delta\), their disagreement energy is:

\[
E(s)=\lVert\delta s\rVert^2=s^{\mathsf T}L_{\mathcal F}s.
\]

Compatible global sections lie in the kernel of \(L_{\mathcal F}\). Disconnected duplicate implementations can nevertheless each occupy a zero-energy local state because no edge compares them. For an ordinary graph Laplacian:

\[
\dim\ker L=\text{number of connected components}.
\]

Thus hundreds of locally green gateways, parsers, renderers, or agents can form a highly degenerate ground state rather than one organism. Local verification cannot heal components that do not share restrictions or consumers.

A useful coupled dynamics is:

\[
M\ddot q+C\dot q+L_{\mathcal F}q=f.
\]

Here \(L_{\mathcal F}\) expresses cross-locus compatibility, \(M\) expresses the inertia and coupling of capability coordinates, \(C\) expresses damping or settlement, and \(f\) is applied demand or pressure. Proof-path condensation changes both topology and coupling: it moves independent basis vectors into one owner, redirects consumer edges, and deletes obsolete degrees of freedom. Separate zero modes become a collective mode. A change at the owner then propagates through the body.

Hard inertia is therefore not a declaration that one package is canonical. It is a physical change to ownership, dependency, release, deployment, and protocol topology that makes future work move through the corrected implementation.

\[

\[

Individual photons are massless. A bound configuration of radiation can have nonzero invariant mass when the total energy and momentum form a timelike system. ### Coordinate charts, affine holonomy, and Burgers residue

Every filesystem, package graph, Git graph, protocol graph, registry, and chain is a local coordinate chart, not the whole Semiotic Coordinate System. If two charts describe a shared region, write

\[
\kappa_i:U_i\to A_i,
\qquad
g_{ij}=\kappa_j\circ\kappa_i^{-1}.
\]

Transport around a closed route \(\gamma\) has affine holonomy

\[
H_\gamma=(R_\gamma,b_\gamma),
\]

where \(R_\gamma\) is rotational or frame residue and \(b_\gamma\) is signed translational residue in a declared coordinate frame. Affine transports compose and invert by

\[
(R_2,b_2)\circ(R_1,b_1)
=(R_2R_1,\;R_2b_1+b_2),
\]

\[
(R,b)^{-1}=(R^{-1},\;-R^{-1}b).
\]

This forces four distinct assay results:

- \(R_\gamma=I\) and \(b_\gamma=0\): the observed loop closes;
- \(R_\gamma=I\) and \(b_\gamma\ne0\): a translational dislocation, whose Burgers vector is \(b_\gamma\);
- \(R_\gamma\ne I\) and \(b_\gamma=0\): rotational residue, which may be a lawful protected charge rather than a repair target;
- \(R_\gamma\ne I\) and \(b_\gamma\ne0\): a mixed defect whose two components must be treated separately.

The distinction is executable law, not descriptive vocabulary. `Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.Defect.Disclination.dipole_burgers_linear` proves a concrete family whose frame closes while its developed position is displaced by \((0,-n)\). `config_burgers_additive` proves that the translational obstruction adds. `config_burgers_zero_iff_contact` proves, for that nonnegative dipole carrier, that it vanishes exactly when every separation reaches contact. `obstruction_descent` proves that the witnessed slide lowers this obstruction strictly off the vacuum and reaches zero within the configuration diameter. Conversely, `unpaired_disclination_survives_descent` proves that the same descent must preserve an unpaired rotational quantum. A repair system that records only rotation cannot see a Burgers defect; a repair system that forces all holonomy to zero destroys protected structure.

The software bridge must therefore carry, rather than infer, every input needed to compute the affine product: ordered route, orientation, frame identity or explicit transition, signed translation, and the declared border law. ## Feizian legalist mechanics

Hard inertia requires law, administrative technique, and positional power together:

- **Fa — law:** transformations have earned proof-path addresses; operations have typed domains and codomains; chain settlement is canonical; cloud consumers do not depend on a laptop workspace; a new locus requires evidence that no adequate basis already exists.
- **Shu — technique:** inexpensive clerks assay bytes; Gemini Notebook and inference endpoints recover dimensions; deterministic tools compare generations; registry and cloud runners fabricate and distribute; receipts expose what changed.
- **Shi — positional power:** consumers, releases, deployments, and protocol routes point to the corrected owner, while obsolete paths disappear. Correct behavior follows from graph position rather than exhortation.

Prose laws without positional force do not govern the body. Renaming a rejected doctrine while executable gates continue enforcing it is not repeal. Naming a canonical package while other plausible implementations remain is not consolidation. Adding a wrapper while consumers retain old imports is not repair.
