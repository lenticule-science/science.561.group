# functoring

Generates [functor](../2025/12/26/2025-12-26-functor.md) from [`category`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/category.md): the structure-preserving map between categories — the homomorphism of categories. Functors between two categories themselves form a category (with natural transformations as its morphisms), so the construction recurs, and the whole apparatus above is generated by descending through `functor`'s own `scripts/`.
