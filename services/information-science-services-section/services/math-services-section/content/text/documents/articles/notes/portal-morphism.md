# Portal morphism

A **portal morphism** is a morphism $\phi : P \to W$ in the ambient relational category $\mathsf{R}$ from a [hole](../../../glossary/terms/hole.md) $P$ in some host nucleus into a [world](../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/physical-science-services-section/services/astronomy-services-section/services/cosmology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/world-as-nucleus.md) $W$, satisfying:

- **(PM1) Boundary determination.** For all $p, q \in P$ with identical germs at $\partial P$, $\phi(p) = \phi(q)$.

That is, the value of $\phi$ at any interior point of $P$ depends only on that point's behaviour at the hole's boundary. This is what licences the cosmological reading of $\phi$ as *a controlled connection*: nothing about the deep interior of the hole reaches the destination world; only the boundary information does.

## What this morphism is doing

Portal morphisms are the inter-world moves of the framework. They are the categorical correlate of the informal multiverse-cosmology image of *a way of getting from one place to another across worlds*, made precise by (PM1). Two consequences follow immediately:

1. The portal morphism pre-composes with [hole](../../../glossary/terms/hole.md) inclusions, so a hole's outgoing data is already factored through its boundary; (PM1) is the additional condition that the *portal*-typed outgoing data behaves coherently with that factoring.
2. The traveller-jump construction [portal-jump](../../../glossary/terms/portal-jump.md) is exactly the pushforward $\phi_*$ of portal morphisms along a chosen [traveller fibration](../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/physical-science-services-section/services/astronomy-services-section/services/cosmology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/traveller-fibration.md).
