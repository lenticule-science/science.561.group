# simplicializing

Generates [simplicial-set](../../../../glossary/terms/simplicial-set.md) from [`presheaf`](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-presheaf.md): take the presheaf on the simplex category Δ (finite ordinals, order-preserving maps). It is the combinatorial skeleton — n-simplices with face and degeneracy maps — from which the whole ∞-branch is built.
