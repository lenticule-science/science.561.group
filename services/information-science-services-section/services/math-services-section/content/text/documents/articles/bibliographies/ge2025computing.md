# Computing the p-Laplacian eigenpairs of signed graphs (2025)

arxiv:2501.07929 (Jan 2025).

## Why this matters

Establishes an **equivalence between the graph p-Laplacian eigenproblem and the tensor eigenproblem when p is even**. Two consequences for the framework:

1. The p-Laplacian on signed graphs is genuinely nonlinear (for $p \neq 2$), so the operator-theoretic form (which is built around graded *linear* operators) does not directly apply. The p-Laplacian track is one of the cleanest pieces of evidence that **a nonlinear analogue of Laplacianizability is needed** — a nonlinear extension is needed.
2. The tensor-eigenproblem reformulation is itself a *cross-formalism identification* of the same kind that supports the unifying claim: the same spectral data arises in two different mathematical objects with no a-priori reason to expect equivalence. That alignment is the kind of structural evidence the framework is collecting.
