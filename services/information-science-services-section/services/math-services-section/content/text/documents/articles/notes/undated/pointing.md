# pointing

Generates [object](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/pointing/content/object/object.md) from [`presheaf`](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-presheaf.md): the Yoneda recovery. The representable presheaf Hom(−, A) determines an object A fully and faithfully — distinct objects induce distinct representables, nothing lost or added. This is where objects re-enter the arrows-first tree: an object is not primitive, it is *pointed to* by its bundle of incoming arrows.
