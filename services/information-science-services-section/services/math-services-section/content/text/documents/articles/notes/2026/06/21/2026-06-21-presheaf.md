## What this node is

A **presheaf** on a category C is a contravariant [functor](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/functor.md)
`F : C^op → Set`. It assigns a set `F(c)` of data to each object `c` and, to each
morphism `f : c' → c`, a restriction map `F(f) : F(c) → F(c')` going the other
way. Presheaves are generated by [`representing`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/scripts/representing/representing.md)
a functor: fixing the codomain at Set and reversing direction.

## Why it is the recovery point for objects

The **representable presheaf** `𝒴(A) = Hom(−, A)` records every morphism into `A`.
The Yoneda lemma says `A` is recovered, fully and faithfully, from `𝒴(A)`: distinct
objects induce distinct representables, and nothing about `A` is lost or added in
passing to its arrows. This is why [`object`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/pointing/content/object/object.md)
is generated *below* presheaf in this tree rather than assumed at the root — in the
corpus's arrows-first reading an object has no internal substance; it *is* its
bundle of incoming arrows. A node is its hom-set.

## What it generates

- [`object`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/pointing/content/object/object.md) via [`pointing`](../../../undated/pointing.md) — the Yoneda recovery.
- the **fiber** `F(t)` at a base object, recorded in this node's
  [`explanations/fiber-of`](../../../../../../glossary/terms/fiber-of.md) (the `fiber-of` / `base`
  relation: evaluation of a presheaf at an object).
- the simplicial machinery: a presheaf on the simplex category Δ is a
  `simplicial-set` (generated below this node by `simplicializing`), which feeds
  the ∞-branch.

## Orientation

The morphism that packages a presheaf is [`representing`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/scripts/representing/representing.md);
descend through [`pointing`](../../../undated/pointing.md) to see the object it
recovers. Gluing a presheaf over a covering yields a **sheaf** — generated in-chain
at `settling`, where the gluing succeeds with H¹ = 0.
