# tensoring

Generates [monoidal-category](../../../../glossary/terms/monoidal-category.md) from [`category`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/category.md): equip a category with a tensor product ⊗ and a unit object I, coherent up to the pentagon and triangle. A monoidal category is the many-object generalization of a monoid — objects combined by ⊗ with a unit, where a monoid had elements combined by an operation with an identity. It is the base over which enrichment happens.
