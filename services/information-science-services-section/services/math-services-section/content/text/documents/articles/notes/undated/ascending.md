# ascending

Generates [infinity-category](../../../../glossary/terms/content-infinity-category.md) from [`quasi-category`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/quasi-category.md): read the inner-horn-filling simplicial set as a category with morphisms in every dimension — arrows between arrows at every height, with coherent composition at each level.
