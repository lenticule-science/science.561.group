# Sørensen & Urzyczyn 2006 — Lectures on the Curry-Howard Isomorphism

Morten Heine Sørensen and Paweł Urzyczyn, *Lectures on the Curry-Howard Isomorphism* (Studies in Logic and the Foundations of Mathematics, vol. 149), Elsevier, 2006.

The definitive monograph on the [curry-howard-correspondence](../research-articles/curry-howard-correspondence.md). Covers intuitionistic propositional logic, [simply-typed-lambda-calculus](../../../glossary/terms/simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md), System F, the lambda cube, and the proof-theoretic correspondences for each fragment.

## Why cited here

- Definitive reference for the [curry-howard-translation](../../../glossary/terms/curry-howard-translation.md) and the [curry-howard-correspondence](../research-articles/curry-howard-correspondence.md) theorem.
- Detailed proof of [strong-normalization-stlc](../research-articles/strong-normalization-stlc.md) via Tait's reducibility-candidates technique.
- Treatment of the structural rules for [typing-context](../../../glossary/terms/typing-context.md), [eta-equivalence](../../../glossary/terms/eta-equivalence.md), and the type-inhabitation problem.
