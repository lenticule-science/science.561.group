# identifying

Generates [identity-morphism](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/morphism/scripts/identifying/content/identity-morphism/identity-morphism.md) from [`morphism`](../../../../glossary/terms/morphism.md): the distinguished do-nothing arrow on each object, neutral for composition. It is the categorical name for "doing nothing," and an object is recovered from its identity morphism — so identifying is where objects re-enter the arrows-first reading.
