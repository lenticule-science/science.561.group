# extending

Generates [kan-extension](../../../../glossary/terms/kan-extension.md) from [`functor`](../2025/12/26/2025-12-26-functor.md): the universal way to extend a functor along another. Mac Lane called it the concept from which all other categorical concepts derive — limits, colimits, adjunctions, and sheafification are each a Kan extension. The general notion of extension (dual to restriction) is recorded in this node's `explanations/`.
