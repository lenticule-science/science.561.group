# factoring

Generates [factorization](../../../../glossary/terms/factorization.md) from [`commutative-diagram`](../../../../glossary/terms/commutative-diagram.md): the converse reading of composition. A morphism f : A → C factors through an intermediate B when f = h ∘ g — a commuting triangle. When every instance of a process passes through the same intermediate, that waypoint is doing real structural work.
