# Crystals

Python mirror of `Core/Foundation.lean`. One seed; the axioms are the
methods; the structure grows transfinitely without settling.

Nonconfluence is the point: extending the seed in different orders
gives different histories. DefectCollapse, zones, and the rest of the
foundation appear at greater depth from the same axioms.
