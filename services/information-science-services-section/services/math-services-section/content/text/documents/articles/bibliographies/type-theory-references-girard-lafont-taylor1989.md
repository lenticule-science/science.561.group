# Girard, Lafont & Taylor 1989 — Proofs and Types

Jean-Yves Girard, Yves Lafont, and Paul Taylor, *Proofs and Types* (Cambridge Tracts in Theoretical Computer Science 7), Cambridge University Press, 1989. Translated and adapted from Girard's 1987 lecture notes.

A compact, idiosyncratic, deeply influential introduction to proof theory and type theory from the perspective of the [curry-howard-correspondence](../research-articles/curry-howard-correspondence.md). Covers natural deduction, [simply-typed-lambda-calculus](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md), System F, intuitionistic sequent calculus, and the original Girard-Tait proof of [strong-normalization-stlc](../research-articles/strong-normalization-stlc.md) via reducibility candidates.

## Why cited here

- Original source for the *reducibility candidates* technique used to prove [strong-normalization-stlc](../research-articles/strong-normalization-stlc.md) and (with extensions) the consistency of stronger systems.
- Authoritative presentation of the [curry-howard-correspondence](../research-articles/curry-howard-correspondence.md) at the level of intuitionistic propositional logic and STLC.
- Out of print but freely available at <http://www.paultaylor.eu/stable/Proofs+Types.html>.
