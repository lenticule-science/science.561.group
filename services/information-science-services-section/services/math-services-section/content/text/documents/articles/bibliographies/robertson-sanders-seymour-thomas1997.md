# Robertson, Sanders, Seymour & Thomas 1997 — The four-colour theorem

A substantially simplified proof of the [four-colour theorem](../research-articles/four-colour.md), reducing the configuration list from [Appel & Haken's](appel-haken1977.md) ~1900 to 633 and producing a quadratic-time colouring algorithm. The standard modern reference.

## Why this is cited

The simpler 1997 proof is what most subsequent treatments cite when the four-colour theorem is invoked. The corpus cites both [Appel–Haken 1977](appel-haken1977.md) (for the original proof and the foundational discussion of computer-assisted mathematics) and Robertson et al. 1997 (for the modern, more accessible exposition).

## Notable claims

- A reformulated proof of the four-colour theorem with 633 unavoidable reducible configurations.
- A quadratic-time algorithm for 4-colouring planar graphs.
- A cleaner discharging argument; the structural / combinatorial side (independent of computer verification) is more checkable by hand than Appel–Haken's.

## Status

The 1997 *JCTB* paper is the canonical modern reference. The same authors' "graph minors" research program (in which Robertson and Seymour proved the wide-ranging graph-minors theorem over many papers) is one lineage in which this proof sits; that broader program is itself one of the corpus's *named-as-deferred* sub-areas (see the _index).
