# groupoidifying

Generates [infinity-1-category](../../../../glossary/terms/infinity-1-category.md) from [`infinity-category`](../../../../glossary/terms/content-infinity-category.md): require every k-morphism for k > 1 to be invertible, so the higher morphisms witness homotopies rather than directed transformations. Most ∞-categories arising in practice (spaces, spectra, derived/sheaf categories) are (∞,1)-categories.
