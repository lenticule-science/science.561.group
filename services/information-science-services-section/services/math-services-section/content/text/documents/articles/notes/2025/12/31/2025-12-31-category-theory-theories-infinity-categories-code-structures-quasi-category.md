A quasi-category is a [simplicial set](../../../../../../glossary/terms/simplicial-set.md) in which every inner [horn](../../../../../../glossary/terms/horn.md) has a filler: for every map Λⁿₖ → X with 0 < k < n, there exists an extension Δⁿ → X. This condition ensures that composites of [morphisms](../../../../../../glossary/terms/morphism.md) exist but are not required to be unique — they are unique only up to a contractible space of choices.

Quasi-categories are the most widely used combinatorial model of [∞-categories](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/_archive/infinity-category.md). Every ordinary [category](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/ordinary-categories/objects/category.md) embeds into quasi-categories via its nerve (the simplicial set whose n-simplices are chains of n composable [morphisms](../../../../../../glossary/terms/morphism.md)), and the inner horn condition generalizes the fact that composites exist in a category. A quasi-category where all horns (not just inner ones) have fillers is a Kan complex — the model of an ∞-groupoid, where every [morphism](../../../../../../glossary/terms/morphism.md) is invertible up to [homotopy](../../../../../../glossary/terms/homotopy.md).

The theory of quasi-categories, developed extensively by André Joyal and Jacob Lurie, provides the combinatorial foundation for [(∞,1)-categories](../../../../../../glossary/terms/infinity-1-category.md) and [(∞,1)-topoi](../../../../../../glossary/terms/infinity-1-topos.md). Within this framework, the classical concepts of [category theory](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/_index.md) — [functors](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/ordinary-categories/morphisms/functor.md), [natural transformations](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/ordinary-categories/morphisms/natural-transformation.md), [adjunctions](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/topology/morphisms/adjunction.md), limits — all have well-defined ∞-categorical analogues.
