# Univalent Foundations Program 2013 — Homotopy Type Theory

The Univalent Foundations Program, *Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics*, Institute for Advanced Study, 2013. Available from <https://homotopytypetheory.org/book/>.

The collective output of the special year on Univalent Foundations at the Institute for Advanced Study (2012–2013), led by Vladimir Voevodsky. The first book-length treatment of homotopy type theory, the [univalence-axiom](../../../glossary/terms/univalence-axiom.md), and the homotopical reading of [identity-types](../../../glossary/terms/identity-type.md).

## Why cited here

- Definitive reference for the [univalence-axiom](../../../glossary/terms/univalence-axiom.md).
- Modern presentation of the [universe-level](../../../glossary/terms/universe-level.md) hierarchy and [universe-lifting](../../../glossary/terms/universe-lifting.md) (Appendix A).
- Comprehensive treatment of [pi-types](../../../glossary/terms/pi-type.md), [sigma-types](../../../glossary/terms/sigma-type.md), [identity-types](../../../glossary/terms/identity-type.md), and [inductive-types](../../../glossary/terms/inductive-type.md) in the homotopical setting.
- Open-access — the book is freely downloadable, which makes it a default reference for the corpus.

Cited from most of the dependent-type-theory notes in `objects/`, the [univalence-axiom](../../../glossary/terms/univalence-axiom.md) theorem, the [questions/cubical-vs-book-hott](../research-articles/questions/cubical-vs-book-hott.md) question, and the [type-theory-as-foundation](../teaching-articles/explanations/type-theory-as-foundation.md) narrative.
