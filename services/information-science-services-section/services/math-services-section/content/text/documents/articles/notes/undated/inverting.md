# inverting

Generates [isomorphism](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/morphism/scripts/inverting/content/isomorphism/isomorphism.md) from [`morphism`](../../../../glossary/terms/morphism.md): a morphism equipped with a two-sided inverse, so the round trip is the identity in both directions. This is the categorical notion of sameness — objects connected by an isomorphism are interchangeable for every categorical purpose.
