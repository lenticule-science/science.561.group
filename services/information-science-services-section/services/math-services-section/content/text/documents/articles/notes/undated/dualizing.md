# dualizing

Generates [opposite-category](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/opposite-category.md) from [`category`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/category.md): reverse every morphism. A statement true in a category is true in its opposite with all arrows reversed — products become coproducts, limits colimits, monos epis. Every theorem proved gives a dual theorem for free. The node body, inherited from the corpus's `duality` relation, explains the phenomenon.
