# Tutte 2001 — Graph Theory

W. T. Tutte's authoritative graduate-level treatment, with characteristic emphasis on the deep structural side: connectivity, matroid theory, planarity (Tutte's own theorem on planar graphs), the Tutte polynomial. A reference for results that originate with or were brought to maturity by Tutte himself.

## Why this is cited

Tutte is the corpus's reference for *structural* graph-theoretic results — particularly the deep planarity theorems that go beyond [Kuratowski's theorem](../research-articles/kuratowski.md), and the connectivity / matroid perspective. Cited mostly via `see-also:` from notes on planarity and from the [kuratowski](../research-articles/kuratowski.md) note's discussion of the Tutte path-decomposition. Originally published 1984 as part of the Encyclopedia series; the 2001 paperback edition is the current standard.

## Notable claims

- Connectivity decompositions (3-connectivity, the Tutte synthesis).
- Tutte's planarity theorem (a cleaner alternative characterization to Kuratowski).
- The Tutte polynomial and its specializations.
- Matroid theory in graph-theoretic form.

## Status

The 2001 Cambridge paperback is the current edition; the original 1984 hardcover Encyclopedia of Mathematics edition is otherwise identical.
