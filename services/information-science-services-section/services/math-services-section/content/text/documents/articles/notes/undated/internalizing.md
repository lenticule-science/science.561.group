# internalizing

Generates [hom-object](../../../../glossary/terms/hom-object.md) from [`enriched-category`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/tensoring/content/monoidal-category/scripts/enriching/content/enriched-category/enriched-category.md): the object Hom(A, B) of the base V that plays the role of "morphisms from A to B." In an ordinary category the hom is a set; internalizing it into V (a chain complex, a space, a simplicial set) is what gives the morphisms structure.
