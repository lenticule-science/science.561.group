# Hindley & Seldin 2008 — Lambda-Calculus and Combinators: An Introduction

J. Roger Hindley and Jonathan P. Seldin, *Lambda-Calculus and Combinators: An Introduction*, second edition, Cambridge University Press, 2008.

A modern, accessible textbook on the [untyped-lambda-calculus](../../../glossary/terms/untyped-lambda-calculus.md), combinatory logic, simple types, and the basics of polymorphism. Less encyclopedic than [Barendregt 1984](barendregt1984.md), more pedagogical.

## Why cited here

- Clear treatment of [confluence](../../../glossary/terms/confluence.md) and the [church-rosser](../research-articles/church-rosser.md) theorem.
- Standard introduction to combinators (S, K, I) and their connection to the λ-calculus.
- Useful entry point for the [untyped-lambda-calculus](../../../glossary/terms/untyped-lambda-calculus.md).
