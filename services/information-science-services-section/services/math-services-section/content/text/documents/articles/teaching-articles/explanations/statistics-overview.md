Audience: beginners learning to summarize data.

Learning goal: describe what statistics measures and why samples are used.

Statistics summarizes data and estimates properties of a population using
samples. Summaries like averages and spreads help compare groups or track change.

Check for understanding: Why do statisticians use samples instead of measuring
an entire population?
