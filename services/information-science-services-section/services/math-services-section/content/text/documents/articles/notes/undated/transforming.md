# transforming

Generates [natural-transformation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/transforming/content/natural-transformation/natural-transformation.md) from [`functor`](../2025/12/26/2025-12-26-functor.md): a choice-free family of morphisms comparing two parallel functors, coherent with every arrow of the source category (the naturality square).
