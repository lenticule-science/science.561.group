# adjoining

Generates [adjoint-pair](../../../../glossary/terms/adjoint-pair.md) from [`natural-transformation`](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/transforming/content/natural-transformation/natural-transformation.md): a unit η : 1 ⇒ GF and counit ε : FG ⇒ 1 — two natural transformations witnessing F ⊣ G through the triangle identities. The left adjoint freely adds structure, the right adjoint forgets it. "Adjoints arise everywhere" (Mac Lane); in this corpus the instance ∃ ⊣ ∀ on hyperdoctrine fibers is the one that matters.
