# Noncommutative Laplacian and numerical approximation (2025)

arxiv:2510.09909 (Oct 2025).

## Why this matters

Replaces the function algebra of a surface with a sequence of **finite-dimensional matrix algebras** and defines a Laplacian via **commutators of quantized coordinate functions**, which converges consistently to the classical Laplace–Beltrami operator. This is a Laplacianization of a *noncommutative* input — the matrix algebra has no underlying point set, no chain complex in the usual sense, and yet a Laplacian is constructed and shown to recover the classical operator in the commutative limit.

For the framework this is one of the most testing data points so far. None of the candidate forms (A: Hilbert chain complex; B: graded operator with $d^2 = 0$; C: functor to $\mathbf{HilbCh}$) immediately accommodate a $C^*$-algebraic / matrix-algebra input. Either the framework needs a noncommutative extension of the Laplacianizer, or this construction has to be reframed inside one of the existing forms — for example, by exhibiting an associated chain complex (analog of Hochschild / cyclic complex) the Laplacian acts on.
