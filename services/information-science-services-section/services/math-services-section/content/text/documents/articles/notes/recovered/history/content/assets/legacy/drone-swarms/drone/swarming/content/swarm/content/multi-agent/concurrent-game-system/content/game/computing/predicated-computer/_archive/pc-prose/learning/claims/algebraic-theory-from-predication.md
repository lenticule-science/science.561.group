# A Lawvere algebraic theory is the clone of functional, settled-state-admissible cells of a clone-closed predication theory

**Claim.** Let $T = (\mathbf L, \mathcal C, E, \mathrm{Cl})$ be a predication theory in the sense of the [predication-categories corpus](../../../theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-theory.md), extended to its arity-stratified form $\{\mathcal C^{(n)}\}_{n \geq 0}$ via $n$-ary-predication. For each arity $n \geq 0$ define $\mathrm{Adm}_n(T) := \{f : E^n \to E \mid \text{the functional cell of } f \text{ is at the settled state } v^T_\infty\}$, where the *functional cell of $f$* is the unique $\leq_t$-classical cell in $\mathbf L^{\bowtie E^{n+1}}$ taking value $\mathbf t$ on the graph $\{(e_1, \ldots, e_n, f(e_1, \ldots, e_n))\}$ and $\mathbf f$ elsewhere. Call $T$ **clone-closed** when $\mathrm{Adm}(T) := \bigsqcup_n \mathrm{Adm}_n(T)$ is a clone on $E$ — i.e., contains every coordinate projection $\pi_i^n$ and is closed under composition $(f, g_1, \ldots, g_n) \mapsto f \circ (g_1, \ldots, g_n)$.

Then the assignment

$$L(T) := \text{the Lawvere theory of the clone } \mathrm{Adm}(T) \text{ on } E$$

is well-defined and produces a Lawvere algebraic theory in the standard sense (small category with strict finite products, distinguished object $x = [1]$, every object $[n] \cong x^n$, products generated by $x$). $L(T)$ has $L(T)([n], [m]) = \mathrm{Adm}_n(T)^m$ with composition inherited from $\mathbf{Set}$.

The construction uses only predication-categorical primitives: bilattice fibers (to define functional cells via the $\leq_t$-classical sub-poset), settling (to define $T$-admissibility), arity-stratified $n$-ary predication, and a structural condition on the closure 2-monad (clone-closure).

## Argument

Three pieces compose:

1. **Functional cells biject with functions.** A cell $v \in \mathbf L^{\bowtie E^{n+1}}$ is *functional in the last coordinate* iff (a) every value $v(e_1, \ldots, e_n, e)$ lies in $\{(\mathbf t, \bot), (\mathbf f, \bot)\} \subset \mathbf L^\bowtie$ — i.e., the cell is $\leq_t$-classical with trivial knowledge value — and (b) for each $(e_1, \ldots, e_n) \in E^n$ there is a unique $e \in E$ with $v(e_1, \ldots, e_n, e) = (\mathbf t, \bot)$. Such cells canonically biject with functions $E^n \to E$ via $v \mapsto f_v$, $f_v(e_1, \ldots, e_n) :=$ the unique $e$ with $v(e_1, \ldots, e_n, e) = (\mathbf t, \bot)$. Inverse: $f \mapsto v_f$, $v_f(\vec e, e) := (\mathbf t, \bot)$ if $e = f(\vec e)$, else $(\mathbf f, \bot)$.

2. **Lawvere–Linton: clones biject with single-sorted Lawvere theories at a chosen carrier.** Classical (Lawvere 1963; Linton 1966): a clone $\mathcal{O}$ on a set $E$ is the same data as a Lawvere theory $L$ together with a faithful finite-product-preserving functor $L \to \mathbf{Set}$ sending $[1]$ to $E$. The Lawvere theory of $\mathcal{O}$ has objects $\{[n]\}_{n \geq 0}$ and morphisms $L([n], [m]) = \mathcal{O}_n^m$ (here $\mathcal{O}_n$ is the $n$-ary part of the clone), with composition the clone's substitution. The finite-product structure is automatic from clone axioms: $\pi_i^n \in \mathcal{O}_n$ gives projections, and pairing $\langle f_1, \ldots, f_m \rangle$ is tuple-formation.

3. **$\mathrm{Adm}(T)$ is a clone on $E$ when $T$ is clone-closed.** By the definition of clone-closure, $\mathrm{Adm}(T)$ contains all coordinate projections and is closed under composition. The composition is associative and the projections act as identities at arity-1 (the projection $\pi_1^1 = \mathrm{id}_E$ is settled by clone-closure). Hence $\mathrm{Adm}(T)$ satisfies the clone axioms.

Combining: applied to $\mathcal O = \mathrm{Adm}(T)$, Lawvere–Linton yields the Lawvere theory $L(T)$. The bijection in (1) supplies the predication-categorical interpretation: $L(T)([n], [m])$ is realized internally to the predication theory as the set of $m$-tuples of functional, $T$-admissible cells at arity $n + 1$.

## Significance

The corpus's apparatus is set up for bilattice-valued, paraconsistent, transfinitely-settling predications. Lawvere theories are the classical universal-algebra side — clones on a set, finitary monads on $\mathbf{Set}$, varieties of algebras. The claim establishes that **classical universal algebra lives inside the predication apparatus as its $\leq_t$-classical, functional-cell, clone-closed-closure fragment**.

Three downstream consequences:

- *Universal algebra is the classical fragment of settling.* The clone of $T$-admissible operations is recovered from $v^T_\infty$ by projecting to the $\leq_t$-classical functional cells. The closure dynamics that drive settling include, as a special case, the closure under composition that defines a clone.
- *Bilattice and knowledge axes parameterize a refinement.* The bilattice's non-classical region (cells with non-trivial knowledge value, paraconsistent cells, partial functions) is what $L(T)$ throws away. A bilattice-enriched Lawvere theory — using non-classical functional cells — is the natural enrichment, plausibly an instance of Lucyshyn-Wright's enriched-Lawvere-theory framework over $\mathbf L^\bowtie$.
- *The bigraded diamond grades the Lawvere theory.* Stratifying $\mathrm{Adm}(T)$ by the knowledge axis of $\mathbf L^\bowtie$ gives a knowledge-graded clone and hence a graded Lawvere theory in the sense of Smirnov–Katsumata (2020). The corpus's bigraded diamond is the source of the grading.

Discharge of obligations 4, 5 is essentially trivial given clone-closure (they reduce to clone axioms); obligation 1 is a corpus-internal extension (arity-stratification for $n \in \{0, 1\}$) and is sketched in [`n-ary-predication.md`](../../../theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/n-ary-predication.md); obligation 6 is the graded refinement and follows once 1–5 are in place.

## What this is *not*

- Not the predication theory $T$ itself. $T$ has bilattice values and explicit closure; $L(T)$ has neither, after projection to the $\leq_t$-classical functional fragment.
- Not Lawvere comprehension. The corpus's [`lawvere-comprehension-classical.md`](../../../theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/lawvere-comprehension-classical.md) and [`lawvere-comprehension-paraconsistent.md`](../../../theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/lawvere-comprehension-paraconsistent.md) realize Lawvere's *comprehension scheme* — the right adjoint to extension. The present claim concerns Lawvere's *algebraic theories* — categories with finite products encoding clones. These are different Lawverian constructions; both pass through Lawvere comprehension (which picks out objects from predicates) but build different structures.
- Not a subtheory of $T$ in the corpus's sense. Subtheories are induced by type embeddings of predicate categories preserving fiber structure; $L(T)$ is a quotient onto the classical fragment, not a sub-embedding.
