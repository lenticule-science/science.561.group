# invertibilizing

Generates [equivalence](../../../../glossary/terms/equivalence.md) from [`adjoint-pair`](../../../../glossary/terms/adjoint-pair.md): an adjunction whose unit and counit are natural *isomorphisms*. The round trip returns not the same object but one naturally isomorphic to it — the right notion of sameness for categories, and the seed of the weaker ∞-equivalences that replace strict isomorphism higher up.
