# Kuratowski 1930 — Sur le problème des courbes gauches en topologie

The original proof of [Kuratowski's planarity theorem](../research-articles/kuratowski.md): a finite [graph](../../../glossary/terms/graph.md) is [planar](../../../glossary/terms/planar-graph.md) iff it contains no [subgraph](../../../glossary/terms/subgraph.md) that is a subdivision of $K_5$ or $K_{3,3}$. The founding result of the *forbidden-substructure* characterization tradition in graph theory and topology.

## Why this is cited

This is the canonical citation for [kuratowski](../research-articles/kuratowski.md), where the theorem is stated with `status: sketch` and the proof is not reproduced. The historical paper is the standard reference; modern treatments (e.g. [Diestel 2017](diestel2017.md) §4.4) provide the proof in modern notation.

## Notable claims

- A finite graph is planar iff it has no $K_5$- or $K_{3,3}$-subdivision as a subgraph.
- The proof technique — assume non-planarity, locate one of the two forbidden subdivisions — set the template for forbidden-subgraph results.
- The minor reformulation (Wagner 1937) and the generalization to surfaces of higher genus (Robertson–Seymour graph-minors program; see [Robertson–Sanders–Seymour–Thomas 1997](robertson-sanders-seymour-thomas1997.md)) all trace lineage to this paper.

## Status

The 1930 *Fundamenta Mathematicae* paper is the original, in French. English translations and modern presentations are available in any standard graph-theory text; [Diestel 2017](diestel2017.md) §4.4 is the corpus's preferred working reference.
