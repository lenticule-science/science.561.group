# Church 1940 — A formulation of the simple theory of types

Alonzo Church, *A formulation of the simple theory of types*, Journal of Symbolic Logic, 5(2):56–68, 1940.

The first formulation of the [simply-typed-lambda-calculus](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md). Church introduced types as a response to the inconsistency of his original [untyped-lambda-calculus](../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/objects/untyped-lambda-calculus.md) when used as a foundation for logic.

## Why cited here

- Origin of STLC.
- Origin of the function-type notation $A \to B$.
- The earliest connection between typed terms and propositions, predating Curry's and Howard's explicit identification.
