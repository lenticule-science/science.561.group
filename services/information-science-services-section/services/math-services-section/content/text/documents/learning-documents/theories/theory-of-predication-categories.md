# Theory of Predication Categories

Predication category theory is the mathematics of the Group. Predicating is the morphism that follows articulating, and it produces the predication cell: an oriented position (subject, predicate, object) that carries no value yet. Predicates compose, so predicating *generates* a category. Category theory is not a language the Group chooses to encode predication in. It is a structure predication produces.

The theory studies FOUR-valued predication cells fibered over a small predicate category, refined to a hyperdoctrine with two internal logics, and run forward by settling. Classical logic, Datalog and plain RDF are projections of it.

## Primitives

1. **Predication cell** φ = (s, p, o) ∈ E × Π × E. A position, not a truth. It is oriented: (s, p, o) and (o, p, s) are distinct cells with independent values. Value enters one morphism later, at positing.
2. **Parameter base** (L, C, E, λ): a residuated value lattice L (FOUR when L = 2), a small predicate category C, an entity set E, and an ordinal settling bound λ.
3. **Fiber algebra** A_p = L^⋈(E×E) at each predicate p: one charge per entity pair, operations pointwise.
4. **Valuation** v: an element of A_p for each predicate p.
5. **Settling** v = (v_τ)_{τ<λ}: a family of valuations, monotone in the knowledge order.

## Laws

- **P1. Four generators, derived.** The predicate category's morphisms are generated by exactly four classes: specialization (restricting support), converse ((s,o) ↦ (o,s)), subject-projection ((s,o) ↦ (s,s)) and object-projection ((s,o) ↦ (o,o)). They are derived, not listed: a mark's surface is not its identity, so a lawful reindexing must commute with every relabeling of entities, and from four entities on the only such reindexings are these four (`orbital_exhaustive`, proved; three entities admit a fifth map, so the earlier bound of three was wrong).
- **P2. Reindexing is a strict homomorphism.** Each generator acts on valuations by precomposition and preserves both orders, both meets and joins, negation, fusion and residual.
- **P3. Two internal logics.** The fibration carries two full Lawvere quantifier systems, ∃^k ⊣ f* ⊣ ∀^k over knowledge and ∃^t ⊣ f* ⊣ ∀^t over truth, each with Frobenius reciprocity and Beck–Chevalley. One logic is classical, one paraconsistent; neither translates into the other.
- **P4. Equality is derived twice.** Lawvere's diagonal construction in the truth system gives classical equality =_E (t on the diagonal, f off it). In the knowledge system it gives paraconsistent equality Δ^k_E (⊤ on the diagonal, ⊥ off it: "known to be the same"). Neither is postulated.
- **P5. Frobenius fails for fusion.** Fusion ⊙ is non-integral, so the resource logic lives in the fiber algebra beneath the two first-order logics. The substrate is stratified: substructural below, two logics above.
- **P6. Classical logic is a projection.** Take L = 2, keep only {t, f}, use only the truth adjoints and the truth equality: what remains is Boolean first-order logic. Datalog and asserted-or-absent RDF are the same projection. They cannot distinguish "no evidence" from "false" or hold a conflict, because the projection forgot the axis that does.
- **P7. Settling monotonicity.** For σ ≤ τ, v_σ(p) ≤k v_τ(p) at every cell. Knowledge accumulates and nothing is retracted; conflicts accumulate at ⊤. Time is monotone in knowledge and not in truth: learning more can flip a verdict, which is why the two orders are kept apart.
- **P8. Coordination-free merge.** The knowledge join ∨k is the structural merge of a state-based CRDT; convergence is proved. Consistency without coordination holds exactly for monotone computation (cohomological CALM).
- **P9. Property-like structure.** The hyperdoctrine conditions are properties of a fibration, not added data: a fibration has them or it does not.
- **P10. Theory-internal discipline.** A result belongs to the theory only if its statement and its proof derive from the theory's primitives. Work that reuses the vocabulary on objects without the oriented cell loses exactly what the theorems are about.

## What the theory generates

- **Category theory.** The predication category, its Yoneda embedding ("an object is nothing over and above its bundle of incoming arrows", `YonedaArrowsFirst.lean`), and the Curry–Howard–Lambek correspondence read at the level of the hyperdoctrine (`CurryHowardLambek.lean`).
- **A Chu reading.** The evaluation matrix PredicationCell × Valuation → FOUR is a Chu space: claims are points, valuations are states (`fregean_predication_forces_four_and_chu`).
- **Settling geometry.** Priestley duality onto bitopological settling sites, settling cohomology concentrated in degrees 0 and 1, a moduli of settlings up to mutual local derivability, and realizability.

## Consequences for the Group's systems

Required:

1. **A fact is a cell with a charge.** A work fact is (subject, predicate, object) with a FOUR charge measured from utterances, for example (task, fulfilled-by, change), (agent, promised, request), (change, passes, suite). A row with a `status` column is the classical projection of this and has lost the information the theory keeps.
2. **The four generators are the only natural views.** Converse ((change, fulfills, task) ↔ (task, fulfilled-by, change)), the two projections, and specialization are the lawful ways to reindex relational data. A view that is none of these, or a composite of them, is not natural and will disagree with the data under relabeling.
3. **Merging needs no coordination.** Federated instances merge by ∨k and converge in any order. A protocol that needs a global lock or a leader to merge monotone records is doing unnecessary work. "Nothing blocks anything" is a theorem here, not a policy.
4. **Two answers to "is it so?"** A system answers both "how true" (≤t) and "how much is known" (≤k). A dashboard that shows one bit per item cannot show a conflict or a gap.
