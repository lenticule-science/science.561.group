---
title: "Bilattice Modal Possibility Operator"
description: "The truth-order join of accessibility-fused values across worlds in a bilattice-valued modal model."
aliases:
  - "bilattice possibility operator"
  - "Diamond_R"
date-created: 2026-03-30

---

For a world-indexed valuation $u=(u_w)_{w\in W}$ over a bilattice-valued predication fiber, the bilattice modal possibility operator is defined cellwise by

$$
(\Diamond_Ru)_w(s,o)=\bigvee_t\{R(w,w')\odot u_{w'}(s,o):w'\in W\}.
$$

Here $\bigvee_t$ is truth-order join, $R$ is a bilattice-valued accessibility relation, and $\odot$ is twist-product fusion. When the value algebra and accessibility relation are Boolean, this reduces to the classical Kripke possibility operator.
