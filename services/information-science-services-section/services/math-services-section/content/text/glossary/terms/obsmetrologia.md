**Obsmetrologia** is the foundational mathematics that grew out of the Leader's research notes. Its name joins observation and obstruction with metrology. It is a vast program and not a device for flagging contradictions. From one relational foundation it reaches logic and order, category theory, sheaf cohomology, aperiodic order, causal sets, quantum contextuality and kinetic theory, and it is a reformation of mathematics from non-western, relational principles.

It is a **metrology of identification**:

- The only primitive data are [marks](mark.md), admitted on the two hands of an [utterance](utterance.md): (token, claim).
- Marks are developed along the spine of [articulating](articulating.md), [predicating](predication.md), [positing](positing.md) and [settling](settling.md).
- The only admitted axiom is `Quot.sound`, used once at the root as the biextensional collapse of the measured utterance space.
- `sorry` marks a frontier theorem: stated, with its proof to come. It is counted and never hidden, and it is not a violation.

The library is the Lean 4 package at `services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia`, licensed Apache-2.0 like Lean and Mathlib. It holds **only pure mathematics**. An application of it is a separate package that imports it. Mathematics never bends to technology: no software need changes how Obsmetrologia works.

[Metrologic](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/metrologic.md) is its one software realization.
