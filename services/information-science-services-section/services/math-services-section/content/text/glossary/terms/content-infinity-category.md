An ∞-category is a category-like structure with [morphisms](morphism.md) in all dimensions: objects, [morphisms](morphism.md) between objects, 2-[morphisms](morphism.md) between [morphisms](morphism.md), 3-[morphisms](morphism.md) between 2-[morphisms](morphism.md), and so on, with coherent [composition](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/scripts/ascending/ordinary-categories/terms/composition.md) at every level. Where an ordinary [category](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/scripts/ascending/ordinary-categories/terms/category.md) has only objects and arrows, an ∞-category has arrows between arrows at every height.

Several equivalent models make this idea precise. A [quasi-category](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/scripts/ascending/content/infinity-category/quasi-category.md) (or weak Kan complex) is a [simplicial set](simplicial-set.md) with inner [horn](horn.md) fillers. A Segal category or complete Segal space uses simplicial spaces. A simplicially enriched category uses [enrichment](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/scripts/ascending/enriched-categories/terms/enriched-category.md) over simplicial sets. These models are all equivalent in a precise sense and form the basis of higher [category theory](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/category/scripts/functoring/content/functor/scripts/representing/content/presheaf/scripts/simplicializing/content/simplicial-set/scripts/filling/content/quasi-category/scripts/ascending/index.md).

An [(∞,1)-category](infinity-1-category.md) is an ∞-category where all k-[morphisms](morphism.md) for k > 1 are invertible — the higher [morphisms](morphism.md) witness [homotopies](homotopy.md) rather than genuinely directed transformations. Most of the ∞-categories arising in practice (spaces, spectra, derived categories, sheaf categories) are (∞,1)-categories. An [(∞,1)-topos](infinity-1-topos.md) is an (∞,1)-category satisfying descent conditions that make it a context for homotopy-coherent logic and geometry.
