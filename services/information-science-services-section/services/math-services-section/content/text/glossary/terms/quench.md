The report states: during active settling, "a **'Quench'** occurs. This is a cohomological obstruction where a record source feeding a truth cell refreezes into a distinct record (`src -> mid -> sink`, with charges `top, t, top`). The failure of these records to agree creates a connecting class that is not a coboundary" (`GR` section 7). In the static-mask case where the vertical differential `d_v = 0`, the long exact sequence of record and truth data degenerates, with all content in degree 0.

The report gives no construction of the complex, the charges' derivation or an example computed from a repository. The source is a provider-generated report that disclaims authority; `content/research/2026-10-15-study-lorentzian-causal-settling.md` records it.
