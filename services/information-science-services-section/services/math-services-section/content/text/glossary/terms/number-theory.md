Number theory studies arithmetic over the natural numbers and the structures
generated from them. Its foundations here are zero, successor, the Peano
axioms, induction, recursion, and the commutative semiring formed by addition
and multiplication.

The service's canonical material covers:

- Peano foundations and recursive arithmetic;
- divisibility, congruence, greatest common divisors, and primes;
- combinatorial counting, binomial coefficients, combinations, and
  permutations;
- integer and rational constructions; and
- discrete dynamics, including Collatz and related relational models.
