# Typed Theory

## What this node is

Typed theory is [predication](./predication.md) read **syntactically**: consequence over predication base objects — inference rules, derivations, typing judgments, and predicate words acting over predication base objects, not over a separate logic bucket. Where category theory takes the predication category's *arrows* as primitive, typed theory takes its *terms and types*: a typing judgment `Γ ⊢ t : A` is a derivation in the predication category, and the two are the same structure seen from two sides — the Curry–Howard correspondence at the base of predication.

## What it generates

- [type theory](./type-theory.md) — typed terms and the calculi they inhabit; the propositions-as-types correspondence that makes a proof and a program one object.
