The **predication cell set**, denoted $\Phi$, is the Cartesian product of an [entity set](entity-set.md) $E$, a [predicate set](predicate-set.md) $\Pi$, and the entity set $E$ again:

$$\Phi := E \times \Pi \times E.$$

Its elements are the [predication cells](predication-cell.md) — triples $(s, p, o)$ at which a [predication valuation](predication-valuation.md) assigns a value in the [predication fiber algebra](predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_p$.

The cell set makes explicit what space the framework's valuations are functions on: not the entities alone, not the predicates alone, but the full subject-predicate-object index. A valuation is a section over $\Phi$ — assigning a bilattice value to every triple — and it is this sectional structure that the rest of the framework's machinery (the [predicate category](predicate-category.md), the [predication fibration](predication-fibration.md)) operates on.
