# Combinatorics

## What this node is

Combinatorics is what [predication](./predication.md) generates over a *finite* carrier. The [predicate](./predication.md) morphism — the second stage of the unfolding articulate → predicate → posit → settle — says something *of* the related, and an edge is exactly that: a predicate asserted over a pair of vertices. A graph is a relation made finite and explicit; a category is a graph whose arrows compose. This node holds the discrete structures that arise when predication ranges over finitely many entities, so the predicate morphism is not a label filed near here — it is the arrow that produces vertices, edges, and the claims they support.

## What it generates / contains

- [graph-theory](./graph.md) — the populated heart of this node: vertices and edges as the substrate of every discrete relational structure. It carries the *roles* (vertex, edge, adjacency, incidence, path, cycle, connectivity, degree, reachability, acyclicity, bipartition), the *objects* (graph, directed-graph, simple-graph, multigraph, weighted/labelled-graph, tree, forest, DAG, quiver, complete-graph, planar-graph), the *operators* (homomorphism, isomorphism, transitive-closure, topological-sort, line-graph, quotient-graph), and proved claims (handshake lemma, Eulerian-circuit criterion, tree-equivalences, dependency-soundness-iff-DAG). It is load-bearing: the corpus's own `depends-on:` reading order and the predication-fibration's four-generator quiver are both directed graphs this theory types.
- Extremal/Ramsey material lives in [_archive](_archive/) for now (a constructive Agda formalization of finite Ramsey clique dynamics), named-as-deferred rather than canonicalized into its own subconcept.

## Orientation

This node sits under the [predicate](./predication.md) morphism, alongside siblings [category-theory](../category-theory/), [set-theory](../set-theory/), [number-theory](../number-theory/), and [algebra](../algebra/) — all faces of the same generating arrow. Graph theory is the floor the others stand on: every poset is a transitive acyclic digraph and every small category a graph closed under composition, so descending here is descending toward the most concrete reading of predication. Un-canonicalized source material (the Ramsey Agda proof) sits in [_archive](_archive/).
