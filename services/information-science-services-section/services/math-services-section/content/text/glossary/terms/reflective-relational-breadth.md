Reflective relational breadth extends the [[reflexive ]]

This structure extends the reflexive sequence by relating distinct layers or structures to one another while maintaining their individual identities.

## Members

Examples include:

- reflect(R(0), R(1))  
- reflect(R(0), R(2))  
- reflect(R(1), R(2))  
- … for all pairs (R(i), R(j)) where i ≠ j

## Properties

- Non-reflexive: all pairs satisfy i ≠ j  
- Asymmetric: reflect(R(i), R(j)) ≠ reflect(R(j), R(i))  
- Lateral: forms a breadth of relational connections between distinct structures

## Expression

reflective relational breadth := { reflect(R(i), R(j)) | for all i ≠ j }
