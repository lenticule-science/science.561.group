---
name: "Congruence"
date-created: 2026-03-05
---

Two integers $a$ and $b$ are congruent modulo $n$ (written a≡b(modn)a \equiv b \pmod{n}) if $n$ [divides](divisibility.md) a−ba - b.

Congruence modulo $n$ is an equivalence [relation](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/concepts/relation/index.md) on $\mathbb{Z}$. It is compatible with [addition](addition.md) and [multiplication](multiplication.md): if a≡a′(modn)a \equiv a&#x27; \pmod{n} and b≡b′(modn)b \equiv b&#x27; \pmod{n}, then a+b≡a′+b′(modn)a + b \equiv a&#x27; + b&#x27; \pmod{n} and ab≡a′b′(modn)ab \equiv a&#x27;b&#x27; \pmod{n}. This compatibility is what makes the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ a [ring](ring.md).
