description: "The math of relationality — the formal articulation, in standard categorical and bilattice-theoretic vocabulary, of what the relationality framework commits to philosophically. Where relationality cribs its language from philosophy, voidmath cribs from math: predication, fiber algebra, fibration, hyperdoctrine, Priestley duality, sheaf cohomology."

---

Voidmath is the mathematical layer of the relationality framework. The two domains are paired: relationality articulates the philosophical commitment that things exist through acts of relating; voidmath articulates the same commitment in the vocabulary mathematics already has. The two domains share the same primitive under different names — *relating* in relationality, *predication* in voidmath — and develop in parallel from there.

The vocabulary inside voidmath is real math, drawn from existing literature: Lawvere–Seely hyperdoctrines, Belnap–Ginsberg–Fitting bilattice logic, Hermida–Jacobs cloven Grothendieck fibrations, Priestley duality, Kelly–Lack property-likeness. The synthesis is what is new; the parts are not bespoke.
