# High Energy Physics of Information

## Note

The High Energy Physics of Information is not currently a literal `HEP` namespace
inside Infomaterial.  It is the physics-facing reading of the Lean library's
derived relational layer: finite causal order, settling, cohomology, charge,
boundary, thermodynamics, and contextual obstruction all appear downstream of the
same primitive/articulation spine.

The key correction is that continuum physics should not be treated as merely the
analytic projection of the finite structure.  It is also the forward projection of
the arrow of time.  The continuum equations are what the directed settling
process looks like when its irreversible orientation is carried forward into an
analytic regime.

In this reading, continuum physics is both:

- an **analytic projection**: a smooth, real-valued, differential, or measure-level
  expression of finite settling, closure, cohomology, causal order, and
  Lyapunov descent;
- a **forward projection of the arrow of time**: the image of the library's
  monotone settling orientation when the finite proof-bearing dynamics are
  pushed into continuum language.

So the continuum is not an external physical substrate added after the fact.  It
is a projection of the already-oriented informational process.  The arrow is not
introduced by thermodynamics after geometry exists; the arrow is already present
in settling, and thermodynamics/continuum physics project that direction forward.

The live Infomaterial spine says that past `Mark` and `Articulating` there is no
free data: value, settling, order, cohomology, dynamics, persistence, relational
physics, and thermodynamics are derived downstream.  The physics-facing surface
is gathered by `Infomaterial.Relational`, which imports spacetime causal order,
d'Alembertian/action/curvature, material constraints, plasma, kinetic and liquid
models, dense matter, quantum contextuality, reaction networks, and thermodynamic
projections.

That gives the following derivation route:

1. `Primitive` and `Articulation` supply the permitted marks and recursion.
2. `Language.Positing` admits value as FOUR charges.
3. `Language.Settling` and `Closure` orient the process by monotone ascent in
   the knowledge order toward a fixed point.

The high-energy reading begins at step 6, but it is not independent of steps 1-5.
The physics is not a new axiom layer.  It is the relational/material projection
of settling and cohomology.

## Causal spacetime

`Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Causal.Order` is the base spacetime carrier.
A Lorentzian causal structure is presented as a Boolean settling over pairs of
events.  The closure step is transitive closure: a pair is causally related when
there is a directed path through the finite event graph.

The settled valuation is the causal order.  Timelike relation is an apex pair;
spacelike separation is causal incomparability.  This is important for the HEP
reading because geometry is not primary.  Geometry appears as the settled order
of causal reachability.

The finite causal set is strict-regime: closure reaches a fixed point in finite
time, and the associated first cohomology obstruction vanishes in that strict
case.  That makes finite causal spacetime an unobstructed local-to-global
settling of causal data.

## Action, curvature, and the continuum projection

retarded d'Alembertian is a backward difference, and its Green's function is the
cumulative causal past-sum.  The chromatic-Lorentzian Boolean settling is the

The Einstein-Hilbert action reading is therefore not "add a continuum action."
It is:

- causal-chain settling as retarded propagation;

This is where the continuum correction matters.  The analytic d'Alembertian,

continuum equation carries the finite arrow into an analytic expression:

process.

## Plasma and charge

`Infomaterial.Relational.Plasma.Quasineutrality` gives a finite Debye-screening
skeleton.  A plasma cell is a list of integer charges.  Its net charge is their
sum.  A screening step adds one opposite charge when the net charge is nonzero.
The imbalance, the absolute value of net charge, is a natural-number Lyapunov
function.

The proved content is:

- screening never increases imbalance;
- screening strictly lowers imbalance when the cell is not neutral;
- quasineutrality is exactly the fixed point of screening;
- iterating for at least the initial imbalance reaches neutrality.

The continuum screened potential, such as the exponential radial Green's
function, is therefore not the primitive plasma fact.  It is the analytic
forward projection of a finite directed relaxation to neutrality.

## Dense matter and high-energy admissibility

`Infomaterial.Relational.DenseMatter.EOS` treats neutron-matter equation of
state constraints as finite sampled admissibility rather than full nuclear
physics.  A finite EOS has sampled pressure, energy, and a sample list.
Stability is monotone pressure over energy-ordered samples.  Causality bounds
pressure increment by a sound-speed parameter times energy increment.

The module proves:

- stable plus zero causal speed forces uniform pressure;
- admissibility gives the causal speed bound;
- the stiff causal EOS `P = energy` is admissible at the causal limit;
- the superluminal EOS `P = 2 * energy` is not causal at that limit;
- stability is preserved by pointwise maximum under the expected monotonicity
  condition.

For HEP-of-Information, this says dense matter begins as lawful finite
constraint propagation.  TOV equations, many-body Hilbert spaces, and detailed
nuclear interactions are not erased; they are later analytic projections of the
finite admissibility skeleton.

## Quantum contextuality

`Infomaterial.Relational.Quantum.ContextualObstruction` reads quantum behavior
through finite sheaf-style gluing.  A local section extends when there is a
global assignment agreeing with it and lying in every support.  Logical
contextuality is the existence of a supported local section that cannot extend.

The PR box inhabits the logical contextuality rung because a witnessed local
support element has no global section.  This is high-energy-relevant because it
places quantum obstruction in the same local-to-global vocabulary as causal

The probabilistic inequalities and contextual fractions are not the base layer.
They are the later real/stochastic projection of a finite extension obstruction.

## Infoholes and boundary readout

`Infomaterial.Relational.Spacetime.Boundary.Infohole` gives the cleanest local
route toward black-hole information language without making black holes the
primitive metaphor.

Given a causal set and a readout region:

- an event reaches readout if it causally precedes some readout event;
- the infohole region consists of events that do not reach readout;
- the readout exterior consists of events that do reach readout;
- the shroud is the boundary on the infohole side adjacent to the exterior;
- effects in the bundled `Infohole` carrier have sources on the shroud.

The no-readout region is future-closed, the exterior is past-closed, and no
interior event causally precedes an exterior event.  This is the information
horizon before gravitational metaphor: a causal readout obstruction with a
boundary that can source effects.

## Thermodynamics, temperature, and time's arrow

`Infomaterial.Dynamics.Flow.FreeEnergyLyapunov` is the thermodynamic hinge.
Free energy is rendered as a finite deficit to the settled valuation, measured
by knowledge-order height.  The closure orbit ascends in the knowledge order,
so the deficit descends.

This gives an H-theorem-like reading:

- free energy never increases along the settling orbit;
- a firing step strictly decreases free energy on a support cell;
- convergence to the fixed point is bounded by the initial deficit;
- the closure ordinal is the ordinal projection, and free energy is the analytic
  projection.

`Infomaterial.Thermodynamics.Temperature` then adds the first bridge from
zero-temperature hard closure to stochastic settling.  Held contradiction
creation is counted through gauge orbit or causal collision.  Free-energy drop
is discharge.  Positive temperature appears as balanced creation/discharge flux
inside a stochastic carrier.

This is where the corrected sentence should live:

> Continuum physics is the analytic projection and the forward projection of the
> arrow of time at the same time.

The arrow of time is the direction of settling.  Energy, entropy, action,
screening, retarded propagation, stochastic balance, and continuum equations are
ways that this orientation becomes visible after projection.  They are not
separate explanations for the arrow; they are analytic expressions of the arrow
already present in the informational process.
