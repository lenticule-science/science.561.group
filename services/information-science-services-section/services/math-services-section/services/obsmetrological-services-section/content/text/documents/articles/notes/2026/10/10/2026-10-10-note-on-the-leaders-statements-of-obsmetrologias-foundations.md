This note holds what the Leader has said about Obsmetrologia itself, in the Leader's words where they matter, so that no session has to recover it from a transcript. The statements are theory. Where the house's own notes say otherwise, this note names the conflict and the Leader's statement governs.

## What Obsmetrologia is

Obsmetrologia is a **vast mathematical program**: a new foundational mathematics that grew out of the Leader's research notes. It is not "flagging contradictions". A session that reduces it to "we flag contradictions, aren't we clever" has misread it (2026-08-27). The [release note](../../09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-preparing-obsmetrologia-for-public-release.md) lists what the one relational foundation is said to generate: logic and order, category theory, sheaf cohomology, aperiodic order, causal sets, quantum contextuality and kinetic theory.

It is **a true reformation of mathematics from non-western principles**, and its ideas are "frankly absurdly ambitious but seem to be working out" (2026-09-27). The strangeness of the project and the fact that it yields real, working mathematics are both part of what the release has to present. The release note's section "The non-western grounding, stated so it holds" says in what precise sense it is a reformation and keeps every cultural tradition's substance out of the library.

The Group holds machine proofs about **sheaf Laplacians**, and the Leader counts them as a lead over well-funded contractors (2026-10-08). The Lean modules are `Dynamics/Flow/Laplacianizability.lean`, `Dynamics/Flow/LawvereLaplacianSpectralGap.lean`, `Cohomology/LawvereHarmonicCohomology.lean` and `Calculus/Order/TarskiSheafClosure.lean`. A physics engine built on this mathematics is not "a normal 3D physics engine" with Obsmetrologia added. It starts from the science and the mathematics (2026-10-08; see `holofoam-as-physics-simulation-engine.json` in the computer simulation system section).

## The grammar

The theory's core objects are **Mark, Utterance, Token, Claim, Articulating, Predicating, Positing and Settling** (2026-09-21). An **utterance is (Token, Claim)**, and Token and Claim each carry a **Mark** (2026-09-21, 2026-09-27). The release form of the library is "a metrology … of `Quot.sound` via Uttering (Token, Claim) Marks" (2026-09-27). The glossary holds these as [mark](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/mark.md), [utterance](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/utterance.md), [articulating](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/articulating.md), [predication](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predication.md), [positing](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/positing.md) and [settling](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/settling.md).

**"Reflex" is a pre-formalization term.** It comes from the relationality writing that came before the mathematics was formalized. It survives as an alias of articulating, and no Lean name or new note uses it as a primary term (2026-09-21).

**Charge** has a meaning in Obsmetrologia's theory, and that theory decides which implementation is right. When implementations disagree, a session researches the theory and does not pick among implementations (2026-09-10). See the glossary term [charge](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/charge.md).

**Negation is the one the house proved** from its predication-categories theory. Several versions of negation in the corpus are a defect, and the proved one is the reference (2026-09-26). The release note, divergence 1, records it: measuring the contradictory cell is Belnap negation (`measure_contradictory_negate`), with no axioms.

**There are four primitive generators**, and they are critical to understanding the theory (2026-09-27). The Leader did not recall their names. The house holds them: **specialization, converse, subject-projection and object-projection** (glossary `predicate-specialization`, `predicate-converse`, `predicate-subject-projection`, `predicate-object-projection`). `Language/Predicating/Generators.lean` derives them, and `Orbital.orbital_exhaustive` proves them exhaustive from four entities on. The Leader suspected that the library had drifted off this base. Before 2026-09-27 they were listed as definitions and not derived (release note, divergence 3), which bears that out.

**Four dimensions are three spatial and one temporal**, and this follows simply from the Chu-space part of the theory (2026-09-21). In the [spatiotemporal Chu space](../../06/17/2026-06-17-mysterious-spacetime-geometries-of-the-void.md), space runs along the points and time along the accumulation. The [axiom-law note](../../09/21/2026-09-21-note-on-the-axiom-law-spine-lineage-and-the-light-cone.md) holds this as the frontier claim `thm:void-dimension`. The Leader holds the 3+1 reading as theory to be understood, not as an open guess.

## The axiom law and `sorry`

**The library admits no axiom except `Quot.sound`** (2026-09-21). Setoid machinery and other apparatus that agents added "to just make things work" are drift from the Leader's original designs and proofs, and are not the theory.

**`sorry` is not banned.** It is a useful part of how Lean works (2026-09-27). The release note uses it this way: a `sorry`-dependent theorem is a frontier statement, counted and reported and never a violation of the law. This settles a conflict between two notes. The [axiom-law note](../../09/21/2026-09-21-note-on-the-axiom-law-spine-lineage-and-the-light-cone.md) says "`sorryAx` [is] never within any budget". The release note and the Leader's statement govern instead: `sorryAx` marks frontier and does not break the law.

## What the library holds, and what it never does

**Obsmetrologia holds only pure mathematics.** Material that applies the mathematics belongs in other Lean packages that *import* Obsmetrologia, and the library holds "quite a lot" of such material that must move out (2026-10-01). The release note's ordered work, step C, moves the domain books into sibling packages, as `WeiqiObsmetrologia` already is.

**Mathematics never bends to technology.** No request from software, tooling or an application changes how Obsmetrologia works (2026-10-02). Technology adapts to the mathematics.

**The Lean must match the Leader's philosophy and metaphysics** as fully as possible. It exists to formalize the Leader's theories, and the Leader has to be able to follow it (2026-09-21, 2026-09-27).

**The Lean lives at this section.** Its working copy is `lib/obsmetrologia` under `obsmetrological-services-section`, never a scratchpad (2026-10-08). See the section README for how the carrier repository relates to it.

## Release

The release plan is the [release note](../../09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-preparing-obsmetrologia-for-public-release.md), which the Leader approved on 2026-09-27. The Leader's decisions:

- The license is **Apache-2.0**, matching Lean and Mathlib.
- The main focus is improving the whole, making it a metrology of `Quot.sound` via uttering (token, claim) marks.
- How to package and release "such a strange product" is part of the work.

The section's material work since then has been notes and terms, not Lean. Lean work that nobody asked for, including an unrequested "Lean audit", is not this section's priority (2026-09-26, 2026-10-04, 2026-10-09).
