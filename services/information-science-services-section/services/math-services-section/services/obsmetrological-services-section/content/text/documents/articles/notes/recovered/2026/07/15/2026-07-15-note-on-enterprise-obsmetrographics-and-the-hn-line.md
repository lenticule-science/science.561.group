## 1. The naming family, ruled

**Obsmetrologia is the name.** Two other spellings have now circulated without a referent: `infometrilogia` (2026-07-14, recorded in the huidu-guanli note §7.3 as Calder Underhill's remit, zero hits then) and `Infometrologia` (2026-07-15, the title of the lenticule-science conjectures note and the evening session's usage). Both are the same 名 with no 實. **Ruled by emsenn, 2026-07-15: a slip for Obsmetrologia.** No new name is minted. One reconciliation is owed: the conjectures note is titled "Conjectures on Infometrologia" and should either be retitled against this ruling or carry a header note deferring to it. Recorded here so the next 循名責實 sweep closes on this ruling instead of re-deriving the confusion.

**The -logia/-graphia split, proposed.** The session tried out *obsmetrologia* against *obsmetrographics*, and the split is real Greek doing real work — the geology/geography, history/historiography distinction. Read that way: **obsmetrologia** is the theory of enterprise observation-measurement (the Lean corpus is its formalization); **obsmetrographics** is the recorded measurement-portrait of a particular enterprise — what MetroPlat's journal and settled tiers actually hold about a firm. The pair gives the firm a clean answer to "what is the relation between the math library and a running instance's data": theory and portraiture. Offered for canonization, not yet canon; if adopted, both terms enter the glossary as term nodes with this note as source.

**The false-cognate discipline.** The *metro-* in *metroplex* and *metropolis* is **mētēr** (mother); the *metr-* in *metrologia* is **metron** (measure). MetroPlat's name puns across two unrelated roots. This is recorded as a discipline, not trivia: the measurement reading and the territorial reading of any "metro-" claim are homophones, not one concept, so every claim made in the territorial frame (§3) must be derived independently of the measurement frame, and vice versa. A claim that holds only because the two frames share a syllable is not a claim.

## 2. The Hⁿ organizational ladder

The huidu-guanli note already fixed two rungs: **H⁰** is the captain's non-transferable accountability — the invariant that survives every delegation cascade (§5.2, the 内方); and **H¹ ≠ 0** is the mask-gluing obstruction — locally consistent commitments that fail to glue, as in the Calder identity drift (§7.3).

The evening session's reading extends the ladder upward, and it aligns exactly with structure MetroPlat's architecture already prices. The HLD's AR-041 states: H¹ prices disagreement *between views* and selects consensus; H² prices disagreement *between reconciliations* and selects higher arbitration. Read organizationally:

- **H⁰ — accountability.** One person answers. Already canon.
- **H¹ — management.** Disagreement between views inside one enforced interior, resolved by consensus machinery: committees, rotating chairs, the standing relaxation apparatus of a single integrated firm. **This is the Huawei mode.** The firm pays continuous internal relaxation cost to keep its interior obstruction-free.
- **H²⁺ — market.** Disagreement between reconciliations, priced at interfaces and resolved by arbitration: contracts, 对赌 wagers, chain-group agreements between units that do not share an interior. **This is the Rendanheyi mode.** Haier's move, read this way, is deliberate: shrink the enforced interiors until they are nearly trivial (小微), so almost all obstruction sits on priced boundaries instead of being relaxed by management.

Huawei and Rendanheyi are then not two philosophies but two poles of one parameter — how much of the complex you enforce the gluing condition over. Rendanheyi is the fine-triangulation limit; Huawei is the single-large-interior limit; the 561 Group's 外圆内方 is the explicit commitment to run both poles at once, with the math as the hard interior and the units at the priced boundary.

**Status of this reading.** It is a correspondence, not a theorem. The alignment with AR-041 is exact at the level of what is priced and what resolves it, and it should enter MetroPlat's HLD C-6 table as a `correspondence`-labeled row — never as a claim that the organizational reading is derived. The prior "H¹ line = Huawei, H² and up = Rendanheyi" phrasing from the session is hereby pinned to this table; anything sharper waits on actual derivation.

## 3. Territory, de-metaphorized

The session asked for the metroplex/territorial frame without metaphor drift. The frame is available without metaphor because the conjectures note's Section IV corollary already proves the load-bearing fact in the tiling setting: local relaxation cancels obstruction *within* a super-tile but cannot cancel it *between* super-tiles — holonomy concentrates on boundaries.

Read enterprise-territorially:

- An **enterprise** is a region of the complex over which the gluing condition is enforced — an interior kept consistent at continuous relaxation cost.
- The **market** is the ambient complex where sections are permitted to disagree. That is what "wilderness" means, precisely: not absence of structure, but absence of an enforced gluing obligation.
- A **border** is where the uncancelled obstruction concentrates — which is why prices, contracts, and arbitration live at interfaces and nowhere else.
- **Territory** is the region over which a firm has committed to pay relaxation cost. **Expansion is taking on obstruction load**, not drawing lines. The Coase boundary of the firm re-derives as the energy-optimal frontier: internalize a region when relaxing it internally costs less than paying its priced obstruction at the border.

This gives Feizi's militarism its teeth without a single metaphor: what a firm defends at its border is exactly the obstruction charge it has refused to internalize, and 勢 — positional power — is literally position in the complex.

**One collision recorded.** MetroPlat's AD-19 ruled "settlement, not territory" for a *storage site's* inhabited state. The enterprise-tier "territory" above is a different referent at a different tier. One word, two tiers, both recorded — the same treatment the spec set already gives "site" (R-29) and "ground" (R-31). If territory enters the glossary, its term node says this explicitly.

## 4. The node micro-enterprise resolution of the root-technology tension

The kernel-offices note ends: "The kernel says how the work is done. It does not say who pays for it" — leaving the 根技术/中央研究院 framing (the lab does not earn; never starve it) unreconciled against the Rendanheyi law (a node's income passes through an internal customer under a chain-group contract).

The evening session's resolution, offered for canonization: **the lab stays root technology and becomes demand-coupled.** Obsmetrologia is constituted as a node micro-enterprise whose internal customer is MetroPlat (Red Cup Engineering). Its 单 is ordered mathematics: the theorems MetroPlat's specification set actually cites as forcing its requirements. Under this constitution:

- "Math we can't use" stops being an invisible failure mode and becomes a visible no-单 condition — a node producing output no internal customer ordered.
- The Penrose barrier is addressed at its actual location. The huidu-guanli note's diagnosis was that the 能人 to be converted into 流程 is emsenn; the demand-coupling relocates absorption from emsenn's attention into the chain-group contract, where the order itself says what math is needed and the citation trail says whether it was delivered.
- The watermelon incident (infomaterial cold for 22 hours while 25 of 26 commits went to harness work, huidu-guanli §5.3) becomes structurally impossible to miss: an unserved 单 is a standing, visible obligation, not a vibe.

What makes this a resolution rather than a compromise: 根技术是命根子 concerns *funding and protection* — the lab is never starved and never dollar-scored against ventures; 人单合一 concerns *direction* — what the lab works on next is pulled by its internal customer's orders. The two principles bind different variables, and the contradiction in the record came from letting one word ("pays") carry both.

The concrete instrument already exists as of tonight: MetroPlat's software requirements specification cites forcing declarations from the Obsmetrologia corpus by name. Every such citation is a delivered order; every SRS requirement whose Source says `corpus-reading` instead of a named declaration is an open 单 on the lab. The demand-coupling needs no new machinery — it is read off the traceability column.
