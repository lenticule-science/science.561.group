# RelMath roadmap

RelMath's stated goal was the complete dependency-free Lean formalization of the 561 corpus math (including `content/_archive`; math only, not the agent/operations application domain), re-deriving where the four verbs conflate and filling the connective tissue isolated research had left open. It was derived from the `relmath-corpus-map` workflow: 34 parallel deep-readers over the in-tree spine plus `_archive` math disciplines, deduplicating to roughly five layers. A direct enumeration of the settling subtree alone found 85 node directories against the 34-cluster map, so the roadmap tracked a "frontier backlog" of settling-generated mathematics past the public literature that the cluster map had collapsed.

## The keystone insight

The four verbs were read as **one recurring four-corner coherence cell** at successive depths: `{potential,active,stable,absurd}` (articulation) ≅ `{⊥,t,f,⊤}` (charge) ≅ `{free, j₁-fix, j₂-fix, both}` (nuclear quartet) ≅ `{neither,◇,□,both}` (modal) ≅ `{strict,Scott,TB,aperiodic}` (regime). `QuartetRecurrence` was the keystone module proving each conflation as a theorem, including that `twistoring`-at-articulating and `commutating`-at-aperiodic-settling were the same coherence 2-cell at two depths.

## Connective-tissue gaps RelMath left open

The roadmap tracked eleven cross-module bridges as its longer-term target, most already realized inside individual modules by the fold: the FourCorner identity across articulation/positing/modal/regime; twistoring = commutating as one coherence 2-cell; ◇∘□ as closure operator and 1-truncated 2-monad; two equalities from one diagonal; σ-conjugation as ≤ₖ→≤ₜ transport; `H¹=0 ⟺ confluence ⟺ CRDT ⟺ unique fixed point`; κ as the sole regime classifier; fiberwise nuclear variation implying aperiodicity; the fiber algebra as a sheaf of equational algebras implying the 1-stack property; the negation gauge cohomologically invisible; `Two` as the universal residuated base.
