\subsection{Causal settling as transitive closure}

\begin{theorem}[Causal settling is the transitive closure]\label{thm:causal-settling-transitive-closure}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.Order.CausalSet.causal_settling_is_transitive_closure}\leanok\uses{thm:settling}
Settling a finite event set under the causal-step rule values a pair
$(e_1,e_2)$ at truth-top exactly when $e_2$ is reachable from $e_1$ by a
directed causal chain. The settled relation is therefore the transitive
closure of generating precedence.
\end{theorem}
