# Post-closure bilattice closure

The set $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \top\} \subseteq$ [$\mathbf{FOUR}$](../objects/four-bilattice.md) — the values of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ excluding $\bot$ — is closed under $\wedge_t, \vee_t, \wedge_k, \vee_k$ and the [negation](../morphisms/negation.md) $\sim$.

## Proof

Direct inspection of the operation tables on the 9-element product $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \top\}^2$. $\square$
