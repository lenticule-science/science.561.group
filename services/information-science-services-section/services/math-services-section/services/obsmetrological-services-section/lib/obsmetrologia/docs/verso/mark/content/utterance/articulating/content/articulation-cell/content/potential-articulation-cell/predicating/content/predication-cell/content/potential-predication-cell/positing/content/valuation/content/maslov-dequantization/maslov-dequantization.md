# Maslov dequantization — the two faces of one settling semiring

The settling spine runs over an abstract value semiring (`Probability.Stochastic.StochSemiring`). Two
of its instances are the two ends of the `L^p` / Maslov family, and the boundary between them is the
green/open boundary of the whole curvature programme.

## The two faces

- **`p = 1` — the magnitude face.** The counting semiring `natStoch = (ℕ, +, ×)`: the *measure* side.
  The [action](../knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/lorentzian-causal-settling/content/einstein-hilbert-action/einstein-hilbert-action.md),
  the curvature, the apex count, the Poisson expectation all live here. Its addition is a **genuine
  sum** — `natStoch_not_idempotent` (`1 + 1 ≠ 1`).
- **`p = ∞` — the shape face.** The Boolean semiring `boolStoch = (𝔹, ∨, ∧)`: the *existence / order*
  side. Its addition is **idempotent** — `boolStoch_idempotent` (`b ∨ b = b`) — so `∨` *is* the lattice
  join. This is why settling-as-join, the [colimit](../knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/lorentzian-causal-settling/content/causal-dalembertian/content/sprinkling-average/sprinkling-average.md),
  the [time section](../knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/lorentzian-causal-settling/content/time-section/time-section.md),
  and the curvature *structure* all settle green and reals-free: they live at `p = ∞`, where the
  semiring is order-theoretic.

This is exactly the corpus's existing two value categories: `Optimization` (idempotent improvement =
min/shortest-path = the tropical `p = ∞` face) and `stochastic-settling` (the sum `p = 1` face). The
whole settling machinery has, all along, been computing in the idempotent (`p = ∞`) semiring — which is
why its outputs are order/shape, not magnitude.

## The exact dequantization

The bridge is the **support map** `supp n = (n > 0)`. It is an *exact* semiring homomorphism
`natStoch → boolStoch` (`countingDequantization`, `Obsmetrologia.Dequantization`):

$$\mathrm{supp}(a + b) = \mathrm{supp}\,a \,\vee\, \mathrm{supp}\,b, \qquad
  \mathrm{supp}(a \cdot b) = \mathrm{supp}\,a \,\wedge\, \mathrm{supp}\,b,$$

preserving `0`, `1`, and the order. It sends `+ ↦ ∨` and `× ↦ ∧` — the discrete heart of **Maslov
dequantization**, magnitude to support, with **no `ℏ`-limit and no real powers**. It forgets the
magnitude and keeps the support. The diamond's apex magnitude `|Apex| = 5` dequantizes to the existence
projection `true` (`supp_apexCount_diamond`): `5` is the data discarded, `true` (has causal order) is the
shape kept.

## Why this is the green/open boundary

The curvature arc's split is *this* homomorphism's split. Everything green — the colimit shape, the
curvature structure (tensor, trace, Bianchi), the order time-section — is the `supp`-image, the `p = ∞`
existence/order face. Everything open — the Ricci scalar `R` with its `−¼∫R√g` — is the `ℕ`-preimage
data `supp` discards: the magnitude, the `p = 1` measure-integral.

So the remaining frontier is precisely the **continuous interpolation** between the two faces: the
`ℏ`-graded power-mean family that deforms `boolStoch` back to `natStoch` through real `p`, recovering
the magnitude from the support. That deformation needs real powers `a^p` — the genuine analytic wall,
and the same wall as `R`'s magnitude. The two **endpoints** and the **exact** dequantization between
them are green; the continuous `p`-bridge is the open work.

## Position

Dequantization is generated by the valuation: it is the relationship between two value semirings the
positing stage admits. It reframes the whole curvature frontier — not "we lack real analysis," but "we
have built the `p = ∞` end and the exact collapse onto it; the magnitude lives at `p = 1`, reachable
only across the continuous power-mean bridge that needs real powers."
