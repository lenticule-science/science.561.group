## Statement

The earned acts show that nuclei and flows distribute over finite meets/joins
and shuffles.

## Definition
- Inputs: [Admissible Morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/admissible-morphism.md), [Trace Shuffle Class](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/trace-shuffle-class.md).
- Outputs: [Admissible Morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/admissible-morphism.md) after distributing flow/nucleus over joins/meets/shuffles.
- Behaviour: applies the earned distribution acts ([Absorb Closure](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/absorb-closure.md), [Distribute Interior](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/distribute-interior.md), [Equalize Nucleus Distribution](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/equalize-nucleus-distribution.md), [Distribute Flow](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/distribute-flow.md)) to show flow/nucleus commute with lattice operations and trace shuffles on admissible morphisms.

## Invariants
- Distribution holds: applying flow/nucleus before or after joins/meets/shuffles yields equivalent admissible morphisms.
- Stability: resulting morphisms remain admissible and respect flow–nucleus compatibility.

## Proof
- Derived from: [Distribute Flow](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/distribute-flow.md) and related distribution acts.
- Shows that: flow/nucleus distribute over earned lattice operations and shuffles on admissible morphisms, fixing the distributive laws up to earned equivalence.
