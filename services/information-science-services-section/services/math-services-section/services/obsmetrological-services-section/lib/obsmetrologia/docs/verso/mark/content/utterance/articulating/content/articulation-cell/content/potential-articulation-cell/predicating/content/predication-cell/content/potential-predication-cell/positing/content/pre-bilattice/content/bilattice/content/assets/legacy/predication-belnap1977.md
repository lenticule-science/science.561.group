## Annotation

Introduces the four epistemic states $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$ that became the canonical motivation for bilattices. Belnap's setting is question-answering by a knowledge system that receives information from multiple sources: a single proposition can be *told true*, *told false*, *told both* (sources disagree, $\top$), or *told neither* (no information, $\bot$). Gives the De Morgan algebra structure on these four values.

The framework Belnap introduces is what every bilattice paper since has refined. The program inherits the four states unchanged: every cell of every fiber takes a value in $\mathbf{FOUR}$ when the base lattice $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$.

Cited in the foundational layer for the bilattice motivation and in the [multi-source epistemic settling](../examples/multi-agent-epistemic-settling.md) example.
