# Inconsistency-sheaf record

A **inconsistency-sheaf record** is the substrate-entity formal definition of the [inconsistency sheaf](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/inconsistency-sheaf/inconsistency-sheaf.md) $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$ as a *first-class substrate object*: the predication-graph entity that materializes the substrate's accumulated $\top$-cells (cells receiving contradictory predications) as a queryable, version-tracked, cohomology-equipped record, separate from the truth-side content.

Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), the substrate's audit-relevant content lives in $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$ — contradictions, attack-surface exploitations, federation conflicts, repair invocations all surface as $\top$-cells. The inconsistency-sheaf record is where that content lives as substrate-readable structure rather than a derived view computed on demand from the [event log](event-log.md). Implementations that compute $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$ on demand from the event log conform to the same engineering contract; this object names the contract.

## Structural fields

An inconsistency-sheaf record carries:

- **`id`** — kebab-case identifier (commonly `default`, the substrate's single inconsistency-sheaf record per scope).
- **`scope`** — reference to the [scope-record](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/scope-record.md) whose $\top$-cells this record tracks. A substrate may host several inconsistency-sheaf records, one per scope.
- **`top-cells`** — references to the substrate's $\top$-valued [predication cells](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predication-cell.md), indexed by `(predicate, subject, object, settling-step-ordinal)`. The free abelian group $\mathbb{Z}^{\Delta^{+, *, \top}_\tau(q) \cap U}$ of the math reformulation is materialized as the substrate's catalogue of $\top$-cells together with their step ordinals.
- **`witness-citations`** — for each $\top$-cell, references to the [witnesses](witness.md) in the [event log](event-log.md) whose application produced it. Per [bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md), $\top$-cells arise from contradictory information from multiple sources; the citations identify the sources.
- **`source-classification`** — for each $\top$-cell, a classification of how it was produced: `federation-conflict` (per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](../theorems/federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md)), `attack-surface` (per [attack-surface](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/attack-surface.md)), `repair-source` (per [repair-as-top-cell-resolution](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/repair-as-top-cell-resolution.md)), `local-inference` (per [closure-rule-application](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-rule-application.md)), or `cross-source` (multiple sources in tension).
- **`cohomology-cache`** — optional cached values of $H^0$ and $H^1$ over the scope, with a version hash matching the inconsistency-sheaf record's content. Per [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md), audit queries on the cached cohomology are reads-free; recomputation is required when the underlying $\top$-cell set changes.
- **`repair-resolutions`** — for each $\top$-cell that has been resolved by a [repair](teaching/explanations/repair.md), a back-reference to the resolving witness. Per [repair-as-top-cell-resolution](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/repair-as-top-cell-resolution.md), repairs operate on $\top$-cells; the record tracks which $\top$-cells have outstanding resolution status and which have been resolved.
- **`version-hash`** — canonical-form hash, citable in audit witnesses and federation handshakes.

## Invariants

- **$\top$-cell membership is the bilattice fact.** A cell is in `top-cells` iff its [predication valuation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predication-valuation.md) at quiescence equals the bilattice $\top$ (per [four-bilattice](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md) or richer bilattices). Membership is determined by valuation, not by classification.
- **Witness-cited provenance.** Every $\top$-cell cites at least one witness in the event log whose execution contributed to it. A $\top$-cell with no witness citation is non-conforming — it represents a contradiction the substrate cannot trace.
- **No silent absorption.** Per [paraconsistent-equality](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/predication-hyperdoctrine/content/paraconsistent-equality/paraconsistent-equality.md), the substrate does not silently resolve $\top$-cells. Adding a $\top$-cell to the record is the substrate's structural response to contradiction; the record's monotone growth (modulo repair-resolutions) is the substrate's audit-truth.
- **Cohomology cache version-coherent.** The `cohomology-cache`, when present, has a `version-hash` matching the record's current content. A cache with a stale hash is invalid and must be recomputed on next query.
- **Source-classification exhaustive.** Every $\top$-cell carries a classification — none of `federation-conflict`, `attack-surface`, `repair-source`, `local-inference`, `cross-source` is a default; the classification is determined per cell at the time it enters the record.

## Operations

- *Add.* When a [closure-rule application](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/closure-rule-application.md), [merge](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/merge.md), or federation handshake produces a $\top$-cell, the substrate appends the cell to the potential inconsistency-sheaf record together with its witness citation and source classification. The cohomology cache is invalidated.
- *Query.* Per [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md), audit operations query the inconsistency sheaf's $H^0$ and $H^1$. With the cache valid, queries are reads-free per [cost-discipline-reads-free](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cost-discipline-reads-free.md); without it, queries trigger recomputation.
- *Resolve.* When a [repair](teaching/explanations/repair.md) succeeds on a $\top$-cell, the cell is updated to its resolved valuation (typically $\mathbf{t}$ or $\mathbf{f}$); the record adds a `repair-resolutions` entry citing the resolving witness. The $\top$-cell remains in the record's history (for audit) but is marked resolved.
- *Federate.* When two substrates federate, their inconsistency-sheaf records merge: the union of $\top$-cells is the federated substrate's inconsistency sheaf, with cross-source $\top$-cells (cells in dispute between peers but not within either) added to the merged record per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](../theorems/federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md).
- *Audit.* External auditors consult the record to detect attack patterns ($\top$-cells from a particular source), to enumerate federation conflicts, to inspect repair history, and to query the cohomology classes connecting distant contradictions.

## Position in the substrate

The inconsistency-sheaf record is the substrate's *audit-and-paraconsistency object*: the place where the substrate's structural response to contradiction lives as queryable content. Without it, contradictions are scattered across the event log and reconstructable only by replay; with it, the substrate's contradictions are one substrate entity, equipped with cohomology, classifiable by source, and queryable in audit time.

The pattern composes with [witness](witness.md) (whose chain produces the $\top$-cells the record catalogues), with [event-log](event-log.md) (the durable accumulation), with [repair-catalog](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/repair-catalog.md) (whose entries resolve $\top$-cells), with [scope-record](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/scope-record.md) (whose scope the record's coverage respects), and with [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md) (the audit-query primitive that operates on the record).
