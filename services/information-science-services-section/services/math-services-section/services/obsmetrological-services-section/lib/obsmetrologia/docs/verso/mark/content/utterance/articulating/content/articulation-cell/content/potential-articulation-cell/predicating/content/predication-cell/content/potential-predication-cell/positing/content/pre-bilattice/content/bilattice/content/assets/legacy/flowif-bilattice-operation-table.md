# Bilattice operation table

The substrate's *canonical operation table* fixing the [four-bilattice](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md) $\mathbf{FOUR}$'s six operations — $\vee_k, \wedge_k, \vee_t, \wedge_t, \neg, \odot$ plus the strong implication $\Rightarrow$ — as 16-entry tables on the value set $\{\bot, \mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \top\}$. Per [bilattice-operation-table-correctness](../theorems/bilattice-operation-table-correctness.md), these tables are the unique operations satisfying the bilattice axioms; per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), fixing the canonical byte-encoding $\bot = 0, \mathbf{t} = 1, \mathbf{f} = 2, \top = 3$ makes operation results hash-deterministic across implementations.

A substrate that uses a different operation table — or a different byte-encoding — is not a conformant substrate; per [conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/conformance-via-fiber-algebra-functor.md), conformance is exactness of the value-domain functors, and the operation tables are the structural data those functors realize.

## Why this concept exists

PredCat establishes the bilattice's order structure but doesn't provide a single "here is the table" reference for substrate implementers. Two implementations independently deriving operation tables from the order definitions could differ on edge cases (especially around $\top$-handling for $\odot$, where the math shows the answer follows from the twist-product construction but the arithmetic isn't trivially evident). This concept fixes the tables canonically, derives them from PredCat's twist-product construction, and serves as the implementation-of-record reference.

## Canonical byte-encoding

The substrate's canonical byte representation of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ values:

| value | byte | epistemic reading | twist-product pair $\langle +, - \rangle$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\bot$ | `0` | uninformed (told nothing) | $\langle 0, 0 \rangle$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | `1` | told true | $\langle 1, 0 \rangle$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | `2` | told false | $\langle 0, 1 \rangle$ |
| $\top$ | `3` | told both (contested) | $\langle 1, 1 \rangle$ |

This encoding is fixed substrate content. Implementations that use a different byte-encoding produce different canonical hashes and are non-conformant. Per [canonical-form-via-settling-quiescence](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/canonical-form-via-settling-quiescence.md), the byte-encoding is the substrate-side enumeration of the Priestley-dual presentation; this concept records the choice.

## Knowledge-order operations

Per [knowledge-order](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md), the lattice generated by $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t} \leq_k \top$ and $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$.

**Knowledge join $\vee_k$** (least upper bound in $\leq_k$ — combine information):

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\top$       | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\top$   | $\top$ | $\top$       | $\top$       | $\top$ |

**Knowledge meet $\wedge_k$** (greatest lower bound in $\leq_k$ — extract agreed information):

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\bot$ | $\bot$       | $\bot$       | $\bot$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$       | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\bot$ | $\bot$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\top$   | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |

## Truth-order operations

Per [truth-order](../../../../truth-order/truth-order.md), the lattice generated by $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \top \leq_t \mathbf{t}$.

**Truth join $\vee_t$** (least upper bound in $\leq_t$ — classical disjunction):

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$       | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\top$   | $\top$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\top$       | $\top$ |

**Truth meet $\wedge_t$** (greatest lower bound in $\leq_t$ — classical conjunction):

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\bot$ | $\bot$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\bot$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\top$   | $\bot$ | $\top$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |

## Negation

Per [negation](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/negation.md), $\neg$ fixes $\bot$ and $\top$, swaps $\mathbf{t} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{f}$.

| $a$ | $\neg a$ |
|---|---|
| $\bot$ | $\bot$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\top$ | $\top$ |

## Twist-product fusion

Per [twist-product-fusion](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/twist-product-fusion.md), $\odot$ is the bilinear fusion via the twist-product construction $\mathbf{FOUR} \cong \mathbf{2}^\bowtie$. Componentwise: $\langle a_+, a_- \rangle \odot \langle b_+, b_- \rangle = \langle a_+ \wedge b_+, a_- \vee b_- \rangle$ in $\mathbf{2}$.

**Twist-product fusion $\odot$** ($\mathbf{f}$-absorbing for component-wise reasons):

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\bot$ | $\bot$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\top$   | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$       | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |

This is the operation [dispatch-composes-authorization](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/dispatch/dispatch-composes-authorization/dispatch-composes-authorization.md) and [graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/graded-dispatch-via-q-enriched-fusion.md) compose with. Note $\mathbf{f}$ is absorbing — any $\odot$ involving $\mathbf{f}$ collapses to $\mathbf{f}$ on the negative component, then to $\mathbf{f}$ overall when the positive component is $0$ (everything except $\mathbf{t}, \top$ paired with $\mathbf{t}, \top$).

## Strong implication (twist-product right residual)

Per [twist-product-right-residual](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/twist-product-right-residual.md), $a \Rightarrow b$ is the right residual: $a \odot c \leq_k b \iff c \leq_k (a \Rightarrow b)$.

**Strong implication $\Rightarrow$**:

|         | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| $\bot$   | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\top$   | $\bot$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\top$ |

This is the operation [modal-necessity-operator](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-necessity-operator.md) uses to compose accessibility relations with valuations — the operation [trust-boundary-modal-validity](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/trust-boundary-modal-validity.md), [governance-decision-modal-decidability](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/governance-decision-modal-decidability.md), and the substrate's modal infrastructure depend on.

## Engineering implementation

The substrate's runtime implements each operation as a $4 \times 4$ lookup table. Per [bilattice-operation-table-correctness](../theorems/bilattice-operation-table-correctness.md), the table values are uniquely determined by the bilattice axioms; an implementation that produces different values on any cell is non-conformant. Per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), the canonical byte encoding makes operation outputs byte-comparable across hosts.

A reference Rust implementation:

```rust
const VEE_K: [[u8; 4]; 4] = [
    [0, 1, 2, 3],  // bot ∨_k {bot, t, f, top}
    [1, 1, 3, 3],  // t   ∨_k ...
    [2, 3, 2, 3],  // f   ∨_k ...
    [3, 3, 3, 3],  // top ∨_k ...
];
```

(Tables for the other operations follow the same pattern — direct transcription from the tables above.)

## Position

This concept is the substrate's foundational value-domain spec. Every other substrate operation that involves the bilattice — [closure-rule](closure-rule.md) application, [merge](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/merge.md), [dispatch-matching](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/dispatch/dispatch-matching/dispatch-matching.md), [trust-boundary](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/trust-boundary.md) admission, [repair](repair.md) — uses these operation tables. The substrate's claim that two conformant implementations produce byte-identical outputs (per [determinism-via-fiber-functoriality](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/determinism-via-fiber-functoriality.md)) reduces, at the value-domain layer, to using these tables.
