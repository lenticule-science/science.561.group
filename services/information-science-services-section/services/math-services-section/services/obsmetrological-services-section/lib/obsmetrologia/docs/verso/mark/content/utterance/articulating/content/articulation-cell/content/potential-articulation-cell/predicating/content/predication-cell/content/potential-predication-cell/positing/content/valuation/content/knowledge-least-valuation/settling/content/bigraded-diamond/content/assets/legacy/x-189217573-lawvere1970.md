## Annotation

Pins down two structural facts central to the hyperdoctrinal layer:

**Frobenius reciprocity** (Definition-Theorem, p. 6). For a substitution functor $f^*$ with left adjoint $\exists_f$:

$$\exists_f(f^*\alpha \wedge \phi) \cong \alpha \wedge \exists_f(\phi)$$

Equivalent to substitution preserving implication. The hyperdoctrinal layer instantiates this on bilattice-valued fibers using $\wedge_k$ (and dually $\wedge_t$); it explicitly does **not** hold for the twist-product fusion $\odot$ because $\odot$ is non-integral on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$.

**The Beck condition** (p. 8). For each pullback square in $T$, substitution-of-$\Sigma$ equals $\Sigma$-of-substitution up to canonical iso. The quantifier-reducible-to-projection-plus-equality theorem also appears here (p. 8).

**Comprehension schema** as a right adjoint to an extension functor $(T, B) \to P(B)$ that carries a $T$-morphism to its $\Sigma$-image (p. 12). The hyperdoctrinal layer generalizes this to derive the predication hyperdoctrine's two parallel comprehension schemas (one classical, one paraconsistent).
