## What this node is
This concept is generated by the **settle** morphism, the final step in the unfolding articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle. A predicated, posited algebraic structure becomes geometry exactly when it is *settled* over a base: a sheaf of rings is fastened onto a space, local data is glued into a global object, and the result is a scheme. Algebraic geometry is what settling produces — the settling/sheaf morphism carrying ring-valued structure over a topology. It is not a field filed under mathematics but the standing object that the settle morphism yields when it acts on posited algebra.

## What it generates / contains
- **moduli-theory** — the study of when settlings of a fixed base are equivalent and how those equivalence classes assemble into a space. The corpus develops settling fiber groupoids whose components are MLD-classes, the coarse moduli as the set of those classes, the fine-versus-visible distinction (full vs. quotiented automorphism data, related as an $N$-gerbe), settling-associated sheaves and their isomorphism classes as the classifying datum, deformation theory of the settled structures, and the moduli of Laplacianizers realized as a stratified orbifold with strata indexed by spectral-multiplicity invariants.
