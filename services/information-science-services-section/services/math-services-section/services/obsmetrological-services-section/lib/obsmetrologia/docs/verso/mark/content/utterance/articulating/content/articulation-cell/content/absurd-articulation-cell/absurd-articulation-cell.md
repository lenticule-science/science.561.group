## What this node is

The **absurd** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where **neither hand's
slot holds a witness**: `(token: not articulated, claim: not articulated)`. It is
the **un-articulated position**, where a cell would sit before any articulation
reaches it.

## Never articulating's output

To articulate an [utterance](../../../../../utterance.md) is already to place its
own [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) in the token slot, so
`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell` never returns the empty cell
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner_ne_absurd`). Every cell articulating
produces is [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
or [stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md)
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner`).

Articulation reads nothing as true, false or both; polarity and evidence enter only at positing, where the four corners are read as values.

## Orientation

Read the sibling corners for the cells articulating reaches and the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the partition entire. The
morphism whose codomain this position sits outside of is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
