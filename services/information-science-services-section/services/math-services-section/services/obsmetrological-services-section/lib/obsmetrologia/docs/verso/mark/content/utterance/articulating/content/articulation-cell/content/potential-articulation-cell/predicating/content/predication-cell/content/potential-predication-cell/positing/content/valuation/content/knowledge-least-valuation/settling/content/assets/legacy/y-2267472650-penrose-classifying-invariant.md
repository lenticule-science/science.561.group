# Aperiodic classifying invariant of the Penrose substitution settling

The L5c instance of the corpus's [aperiodic classifying invariant](../objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) on the most-celebrated aperiodic substitution system.

## Penrose Anderson-Putnam invariant (literature)

The Penrose tiling space's Čech cohomology, computed via Anderson-Putnam:

$$\check{H}^0(\Omega_{\text{Penrose}}) \cong \mathbb{Z}, \qquad \check{H}^1(\Omega_{\text{Penrose}}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^5.$$

The rank-5 $H^1$ is the most distinctive feature — the Penrose tiling has 5 generating cohomology classes. (See Sadun 2008 §4 for the standard computation.)

## Corpus computation

The corpus's pre-quotient classifying invariant for the L5c Penrose settling:

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v_{\text{Penrose}}) \in \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)} \mathbb{Z}$$

— uncountable rank.

After quotient by settling-shift / substitution-translation equivalence, the corpus's invariant should reduce to $\mathbb{Z}^5$ matching Anderson-Putnam.

## What this exhibits

The L5c instance is the corpus's most demanding test of the framework's correctness — Penrose has the richest $H^1$ structure (rank 5), and the corpus's framework should reproduce this.
