# Settling cobordism category

The **settling cobordism category** $\mathrm{Cob}_\mathrm{settling}$ is the symmetric monoidal category whose objects are *boundary predication systems* — finite cell-systems with assigned bilattice values representing the "in/out state" of a settling — and whose morphisms are *settling cobordisms* between such boundaries. It is the corpus's analog of Atiyah's $\mathrm{Cob}_n$ category, with predication settlings playing the role of $n$-dimensional cobordisms.

## Objects

A **boundary predication system** is a triple $\mathcal{B} = (B, \mathcal{C}_B, v_B)$ where:

- $B$ is a finite set of *boundary cells* — a finite subcategory of cells from the underlying predication category $\mathcal{C}$.
- $\mathcal{C}_B \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is the full subcategory generated by $B$ (boundary cells together with their reindexing morphisms).
- $v_B : B \to \mathbf{FOUR}$ is a *boundary valuation* assigning a bilattice value to each cell.

The boundary valuation must be **closure-stable**: applying the closure operator to $v_B$ produces $v_B$ unchanged. This means boundary configurations are already in a "settled" state — they are closed under the closure dynamics restricted to $\mathcal{C}_B$.

The **empty boundary** $\emptyset$ has $B = \emptyset$, $\mathcal{C}_\emptyset = \emptyset$, and the unique empty valuation. It is the monoidal unit of $\mathrm{Cob}_\mathrm{settling}$.

## Morphisms

A **settling cobordism** $S : \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{in} \to \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{out}$ from boundary $\mathcal{B}_\mathrm{in} = (B_\mathrm{in}, \mathcal{C}_\mathrm{in}, v_\mathrm{in})$ to boundary $\mathcal{B}_\mathrm{out} = (B_\mathrm{out}, \mathcal{C}_\mathrm{out}, v_\mathrm{out})$ is the data of:

- A **bulk cell-system** $\mathcal{C}_S \supseteq \mathcal{C}_\mathrm{in} \cup \mathcal{C}_\mathrm{out}$ (the bulk extends both boundaries).
- A **settling trajectory** $v : \kappa_S \to \mathrm{Val}(\mathcal{C}_S)$ — a [predication settling](predication-settling.md) on $\mathcal{C}_S$.
- A **boundary compatibility**: $v_0 |_{B_\mathrm{in}} = v_\mathrm{in}$ (initial value restricts to the in-boundary) and $v_{\kappa_S} |_{B_\mathrm{out}} = v_\mathrm{out}$ (closed value restricts to the out-boundary).

Two settling cobordisms are equivalent if they have isomorphic settling trajectories — same closure ordinal, same intermediate values, modulo cell-system isomorphism rel boundary.

The **identity cobordism** $\mathrm{id}_\mathcal{B} : \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{B}$ is the trivial settling on $\mathcal{C}_B$ alone, whose trajectory is constant at $v_B$ (justified because $v_B$ is closure-stable by definition).

## Composition

The composition of $S_1 : \mathcal{B}_0 \to \mathcal{B}_1$ and $S_2 : \mathcal{B}_1 \to \mathcal{B}_2$ is the **glued settling** $S_2 \circ S_1 : \mathcal{B}_0 \to \mathcal{B}_2$ obtained by:

1. Identifying the out-boundary of $S_1$ with the in-boundary of $S_2$ — both are $\mathcal{B}_1$, so the identification is canonical.
2. The bulk cell-system is $\mathcal{C}_{S_1} \cup_{\mathcal{C}_1} \mathcal{C}_{S_2}$ (union along the shared boundary).
3. The settling trajectory is the *concatenation*: run $S_1$'s trajectory until it closes at $v_{B_1}$, then start $S_2$'s trajectory from $v_{B_1}$ and run it until $v_{B_2}$. The closure ordinal of the composition is $\kappa_{S_1} + \kappa_{S_2}$ (ordinal sum).

Composition is associative because ordinal sum is associative.

## Symmetric monoidal structure

The **monoidal product** $\otimes$ on objects is *disjoint union of boundary systems*:
$$\mathcal{B}_1 \otimes \mathcal{B}_2 \;:=\; (B_1 \sqcup B_2,\; \mathcal{C}_{B_1} \sqcup \mathcal{C}_{B_2},\; v_{B_1} \sqcup v_{B_2}).$$

On morphisms, $S_1 \otimes S_2$ is the disjoint union of settling trajectories — they happen "in parallel" on disjoint cell-systems, with independent closure ordinals.

The **monoidal unit** is the empty boundary $\emptyset$.

The **symmetry** $\sigma_{\mathcal{B}_1, \mathcal{B}_2} : \mathcal{B}_1 \otimes \mathcal{B}_2 \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathcal{B}_2 \otimes \mathcal{B}_1$ is the swap of the two disjoint summands — well-defined since the underlying cell-systems are disjoint.

## Duality

The **dual** of a boundary $\mathcal{B} = (B, \mathcal{C}_B, v_B)$ is:
$$\mathcal{B}^\vee \;:=\; (B,\; \mathcal{C}_B^\mathrm{op},\; \sim \! v_B),$$
where:
- $\mathcal{C}_B^\mathrm{op}$ is the opposite category (reindexing morphisms reversed).
- $\sim \! v_B$ is the [Klein-four-negated](teaching/explanations/internal-z2-gauge.md) valuation: $\sim \mathbf{t} = \mathbf{f}$, $\sim \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{t}$, $\sim \bot = \bot$, $\sim \top = \top$.

Duality on morphisms: a cobordism $S : \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{in} \to \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{out}$ has dual $S^\vee : \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{out}^\vee \to \mathcal{B}_\mathrm{in}^\vee$ obtained by *reversing the settling trajectory* (replacing $v$ by $v$ run from $v_{\kappa_S}$ back to $v_0$ via the [Verdier-dual settling](../theorems/verdier-duality-for-settlings.md), then negating values).

This is the corpus's "orientation reversal" for cobordisms.

## Special objects

### The disk-like cobordism

A **disk cobordism** $D_v : \emptyset \to \mathcal{B}$ is a settling cobordism whose in-boundary is empty. Concretely: a settling that starts from "no information" (empty cell-system or all-$\bot$ valuation) and produces the boundary state $v_B$ at closure. This is the corpus's analog of a "filled-in disk" with boundary $\mathcal{B}$.

### The pair-of-pants cobordism

A **pair-of-pants cobordism** $P : \mathcal{B}_1 \otimes \mathcal{B}_2 \to \mathcal{B}_3$ is a settling cobordism that takes two disjoint input boundaries and produces a single output boundary. Concretely: a settling that starts from $v_{B_1} \sqcup v_{B_2}$ on $\mathcal{C}_{B_1} \sqcup \mathcal{C}_{B_2}$, runs the closure dynamics on a bulk cell-system $\mathcal{C}_P$ that connects the two boundary subcategories, and closes to a state $v_{B_3}$ on the third boundary.

The pair-of-pants is the corpus's *multiplication-cobordism* — it gives the algebraic structure on the cohomology, as analyzed in [settling-frobenius-structure](../../settling-frobenius-structure/settling-frobenius-structure.md).

### The closed cobordism

A **closed settling cobordism** $\Sigma : \emptyset \to \emptyset$ is a settling that starts and ends with the empty boundary — a "self-contained" settling. Its associated invariant under the TQFT functor is the *partition function* — a number (in $\mathbb{Z}$ or some ground ring) characterizing the closed settling globally.

## Position in predication categories

The settling cobordism category is the natural target for the TQFT structure: it organizes settlings as morphisms between boundary states, with composition by gluing and tensor product by disjoint union. Both operations are already present in the corpus (as [settling tensor product](../../../references/settling-tensor-product.md) and as the closure operator's iterative composition); the cobordism category packages them into a single symmetric monoidal structure.

Settling cobordism is the right framework for the [TQFT structure theorem](../../settling-frobenius-structure/references/tqft-structure.md): the cohomology functor $Z = H^\bullet(\mathcal{F}_{(-)}) : \mathrm{Cob}_\mathrm{settling} \to \mathrm{Mod}_\mathbb{Z}$ is the corpus's TQFT, satisfying Atiyah's axioms with the Frobenius structure of [settling-frobenius-structure](../../settling-frobenius-structure/settling-frobenius-structure.md).

The cobordism category also opens the higher-categorical direction: settlings have "settlings between settlings" (deformations of the trajectory), which is the structure of a 2-cobordism category. The corpus's $(\infty, n)$-categorical refinement of $\mathrm{Cob}_\mathrm{settling}$ is open.
