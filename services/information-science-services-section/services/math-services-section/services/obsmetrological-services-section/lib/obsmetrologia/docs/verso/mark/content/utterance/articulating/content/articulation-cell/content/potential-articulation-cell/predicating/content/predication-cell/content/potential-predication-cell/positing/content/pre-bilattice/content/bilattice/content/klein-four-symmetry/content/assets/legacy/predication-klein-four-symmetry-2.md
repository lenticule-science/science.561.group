# Klein-four symmetry

The **Klein-four group** $K_4 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ acting on a [bilattice](../objects/bilattice.md) equipped with both [negation](../morphisms/negation.md) $\sim$ and [conflation](../morphisms/conflation.md) $-$, generated by these two commuting involutions:

$$K_4 \;:=\; \langle \sim,\, - \rangle \;=\; \{\mathrm{id},\, \sim,\, -,\, \sim\circ-\}.$$

The four elements act distinctly on the bilattice:

- $\mathrm{id}$ — identity.
- $\sim$ — preserves $\leq_k$, reverses $\leq_t$. The [internal $\mathbb{Z}/2$ gauge](internal-z2-gauge.md).
- $-$ — preserves $\leq_t$, reverses $\leq_k$.
- $\sim\circ-$ — reverses both orders.

The two generators commute: $\sim\circ- = -\circ\sim$, by the standard bilattice axiom that negation and conflation commute when both are present (recorded in the [bilattice definition](../objects/bilattice.md)).

## On $\mathbf{FOUR}$

For the [four-element bilattice](../objects/four-bilattice.md):

| element | $\sim$ | $-$ | $\sim\circ-$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle$ | $\bot$ | $\top$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf t = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$ | $\mathbf f$ | $\mathbf t$ | $\mathbf f$ |
| $\mathbf f = \langle 0, 1 \rangle$ | $\mathbf t$ | $\mathbf f$ | $\mathbf t$ |
| $\top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle$ | $\top$ | $\bot$ | $\bot$ |

In twist-product coordinates on $\mathbf 2^\bowtie$ with $\sim\langle a, b\rangle = \langle b, a\rangle$ and $-\langle a, b\rangle = \langle \neg b, \neg a\rangle$:

- $\sim\circ-\langle a, b\rangle = \sim\langle \neg b, \neg a\rangle = \langle \neg a, \neg b\rangle$ — coordinatewise complement.

The orbits of $K_4$ on $\mathbf{FOUR}$:
- $\{\mathbf t, \mathbf f\}$ (a 2-orbit under $\sim$, fixed by $-$).
- $\{\bot, \top\}$ (a 2-orbit under $-$, fixed by $\sim$).

Each orbit is the *extreme pair* of one order and the *incomparable middle pair* of the other (cf. [bilattice two-orders duality](../questions/bilattice-two-orders-duality.md)).

## Generalization to twist-products

For a [twist product](../objects/twist-product.md) $\mathbf L \otimes \mathbf L$ where $\mathbf L$ is a de Morgan algebra (a bounded distributive lattice with an involutive order-reversing automorphism $\neg_L$), the same $K_4$ acts:

- $\sim\langle a, b\rangle = \langle b, a\rangle$ — coordinate swap (defined for any $\mathbf L \otimes \mathbf L$).
- $-\langle a, b\rangle = \langle \neg_L b, \neg_L a\rangle$ — coordinate-complemented swap (requires de Morgan structure on $\mathbf L$).
- $\sim\circ-\langle a, b\rangle = \langle \neg_L a, \neg_L b\rangle$ — coordinatewise complement.

The Klein-four extends to a dihedral symmetry $D_4$ when the order-swap involution $\sigma$ is included; see the [dihedral symmetry on twist-products theorem](../theorems/dihedral-symmetry-on-twist-products.md).

## Role

$K_4$ is the natural symmetry group of a bilattice with both negation and conflation. Its orbits on settlings produce refinements of [mutually-locally-derivable](mutually-locally-derivable.md) equivalence: each non-trivial element of $K_4$ gives a duality whose orbit-type is preserved by [strong-MLD](../theorems/strong-mld-via-composed-duality.md)-style refined invariants.
