The **active** corner of the [predication cell](../../predication-cell.md)'s
recurring slot-presence partition — negative: the against-slot present, told
false. It is one of the four refinements the predication cell comes in, the
same four-point object met at the
[articulation cell](../../../../../../../articulation-cell.md)'s phases and, one
regime down, at the bilattice charge. The generative descent continues from the
[potential](../potential-predication-cell/potential-predication-cell.md) corner
(via [positing](../potential-predication-cell/positing/positing.md)); this
corner is reached by the predication category's internal morphisms. (Corner
names follow the evidence reading, ruled 2026-08-05; see
`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`. The partition is now derived —
**valuation-relative**, not cell-intrinsic: `Valuation.phase` reads a claim's
corner directly off a [valuation](../potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/valuation.md)
(`phase v φ := v φ`), exhaustive (`Valuation.phase_cases`) and pairwise
distinct (`Articulating.Corner.pairwise_ne`) across the four corners. This
corner specifically is reached, from the born-absurd seed, by *denying* $\phi$
— positing the truth-bottom charge $f$ puts its phase at exactly active
(`Valuation.phase_denying_knowledgeLeast`) — and positing only ever raises
phase in the knowledge order, never lowers it back out
(`Valuation.phase_posit_le`). What remains open is a *cell-intrinsic* corner —
one property of the triple $\phi$ alone, independent of any valuation; the
schema forbids a value field, so no theorem claims one. See
`Language/Predicating/CellPartition.lean`.)

The morphism that produced the cell is
[predicating](../../../../predicating.md).
