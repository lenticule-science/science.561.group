The **biological settlement** $\mathcal{B}$ is a settlement whose entity-set is the union of organisms together with their components (cells, organelles, tissues, organs) and aggregations (populations, communities, ecosystems), whose predicates are biological observables (metabolic rates, gene expression, phenotypic traits, ecological roles, reproductive states), whose closure-rules are the metabolic, regulatory, ecological, and evolutionary dynamics linking them.

## Multi-scale structure

The biological settlement carries a multi-scale fiber-structure: each scale presents a sub-settlement with its own characteristic predicates and closure-rules.

| Scale | Sub-settlement | Predicates | Closure-rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molecular | genomic-sub-settlement | nucleotide sequences, methylation patterns, gene expression | replication, transcription, regulation, mutation |
| Cellular | cellular-sub-settlement | metabolite concentrations, organelle states, cell types | metabolic pathways, signaling cascades, cell-cycle progression |
| Organismal | organism-sub-settlement | phenotypic traits, behavior, life-stage | ontogeny, sensorimotor loops, homeostasis |
| Population | population-sub-settlement | allele frequencies, demographic structure | natural selection, drift, migration |
| Ecological | ecological-sub-settlement | species abundances, interaction networks, biogeochemical cycles | predation, competition, mutualism, succession |

Each sub-settlement is itself a settlement; the biological settlement $\mathcal{B}$ is the multi-scale composite.

## Connection to autopoiesis

Individual organisms — at the cellular and organismal scales — are realizations of [aperiodic-autopoiesis-cells](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/aperiodic-autopoiesis-cell.md). The biological settlement contains autopoiesis-cells as its primary entities; the closure-rules between them (metabolic exchange, signaling, ecological interaction) are the substrate-realization of the structural-coupling between distinct autopoiesis-cells.
