\subsubsection{Microlocal projection}

\begin{theorem}[Microlocal support projection]\label{thm:microlocal-support}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Support.Microlocal.microlocal_support_propagation}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-laplacian, thm:curvature-regime-correspondence}
The Obsmetrologia analogue of microlocal singular support is carried by closure-flow
singularity. A settling-continuous flow has empty singular support; an NSC
firing marks failure of settling-continuity at a limit ordinal. The forward
causal closure of an event support is a propagation closure operator, and the
$H^1$ regime invariant classifies the singular-support regime: vanishing
$H^1$ is flat, nonvanishing $H^1$ is curved or singular. This is a bridge, not
an identity with smooth microlocal analysis; the formal content is the
NSC/closure-flow and curvature-regime theorem package.
\end{theorem}
