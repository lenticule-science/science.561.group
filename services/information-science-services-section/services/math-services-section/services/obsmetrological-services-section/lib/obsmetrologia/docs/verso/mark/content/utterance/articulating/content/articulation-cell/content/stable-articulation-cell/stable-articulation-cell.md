## What this node is

The **stable** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where **both
hands' slots carry a witness** — `(token: yes, claim: yes)`, *told both*.
Both the identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md)
and the content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md)
have been witnessed; the cell holds the most of any corner.

## Terminus of articulating's own progression

**Engaging** a potential cell — a cell already witnessed on the token slot —
with a claim-side witness (`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.engage`) reaches
stable directly
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.engage_articulate_corner`). Articulating's
own progression is exactly
[potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md) →
stable: the token witnessed first by articulation itself, the claim
witnessed second by engaging. Neither
[absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) (the
un-articulated position) nor
[active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md) (a claim
witnessed with no prior token) lies anywhere on this path — both are reached,
if at all, only by the value algebra downstream, never by articulating.
Stable is the only corner besides potential that
`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell` ever returns
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner`).

The name "stable" marks what both hands standing witnessed at once *does*:
nothing further can move the cell. One level down, this is the held
contradiction the paraconsistent value logics will carry — told-both does not
explode into triviality, it **absorbs**; no further telling changes it. That
absorption, not saturation-as-breakdown, is why the corner is stable rather
than absurd: reading contradiction as absurdity is the classical projection
this corpus declines. Because the slots are **lossless**, stable also carries
both witnesses forward exactly — nothing engaging deposits is ever discarded
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.mem_claimSlot_engage`). At the articulation
layer the cell is read plainly as **both-witnessed**; polarity, evidence, and
contradiction proper enter only downstream when value is measured, not here.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material named this corner
`absurd` and is preserved unrewritten in archives. Material from the corpus's
aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in [`_archive/`](_archive).

## Earlier material

The **stable** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **claim side
is articulated and the token side is not** — `(token: no, claim: yes)`. The
content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) has been
committed while the identity-carrying
[token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has not: *what-is-said* without a
*which-one* to anchor it.

Stable is the corner that the **token-before-claim ordering puts off the natural
progression**. If articulating proceeds by articulating the token side first,
then potential → [active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md)
→ [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) is the on-path
sequence, and stable — a claim articulated *before* its expected prior token — is
reached only off that path. This is exactly why the ordering of the
[utterance](../../../../../utterance.md) pair is load-bearing:
which primitive is "first" decides which corner is the off-path one. Under the
reversed claim-before-token reading, stable and active exchange roles; which
reading is right is open work.

Read [active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md) for the
on-path counterpart it mirrors, the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole partition, and
[utterance](../../../../../utterance.md) for why the pair
ordering decides on-path versus off. The morphism that generated the cell is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
