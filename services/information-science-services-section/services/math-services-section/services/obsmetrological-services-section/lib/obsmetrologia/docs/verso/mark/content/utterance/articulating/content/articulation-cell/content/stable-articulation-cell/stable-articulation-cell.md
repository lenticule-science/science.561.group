## What this node is

The **stable** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where **both sides are
articulated** — `(token: yes, claim: yes)`, *told both*. Both the
identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) and the
content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) have been
committed; the cell holds the most of any corner.

## Terminus of the progression

Under the token-before-claim working theory, stable is the **terminus of the
natural progression**: [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md)
→ [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md) →
stable, the token articulated first and the claim second. Both statuses are now
positive, leaving nothing further to articulate at this cell — reached on-path
through potential rather than off-path through
[active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md).

The name "stable" marks what both sides standing articulated at once *does*:
nothing further can move the cell. One base down, this is the held
contradiction the paraconsistent value logics will carry — told-both does not
explode into triviality, it **absorbs**; no further telling changes it. That
absorption, not saturation-as-breakdown, is why the corner is stable rather
than absurd: reading contradiction as absurdity is the classical projection
this corpus declines. At the articulation layer the cell is read plainly as
**both-articulated**; polarity, evidence, and contradiction proper enter only
downstream when value is posited, not here.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material named this corner
`absurd` and is preserved unrewritten in archives. Material from the corpus's
aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in [`_archive/`](_archive).
