\subsection{The held-\(\top\)/\(H^1\) exchange}

Local sections of a scenario, held as charge records and merged by the
knowledge join, convert the degree-1 gluing obstruction into degree-0 held
\(\top\): the merge is inconsistent exactly at the measurements where the
family disagrees. For parity models this makes the coefficient choice --- the
two-valued outcome object versus the FOUR bilattice --- the choice between
carrying a disagreement as a nonvanishing \v{C}ech class and carrying it as a
held contradiction.

\begin{theorem}[The merge holds \(\top\) exactly at disagreements]\label{thm:merge-inconsistency-disagreement}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.mergeRecord_inconsistency_iff}\leanok\uses{thm:possibilistic-model}
The knowledge-join merge of a family's local charge records is inconsistent
at a measurement \(m\) iff two contexts of the scenario containing \(m\)
assign it different outcomes. Corollary
(\texttt{mergeRecord\_topfree\_iff\_compatible}): the merge is \(\top\)-free
everywhere iff the family is compatible.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The held-\(\top\)/\(H^1\) exchange for parity models]\label{thm:top-h1-exchange}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.topfree_realizing_family_iff_coboundary}\leanok\uses{thm:merge-inconsistency-disagreement, thm:pr-cohomological-contextuality}
For a parity model whose contexts are all pairs, a realizing family whose
knowledge-join merge is \(\top\)-free everywhere exists iff the parity target
is a \(\delta^0\)-coboundary of the context graph. So a nonvanishing
\(\mathbb{Z}/2\) class obstructs every \(\top\)-free merge --- the value-level
converse, for parity models, of the rank count of
\ref{thm:coordination-value-converse}: the class is not only nonzero but
priced, in held \(\top\), by any settling that merges the data.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The PR exchange rate]\label{thm:pr-top-h1-exchange}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.pr_top_h1_exchange}\leanok\uses{thm:top-h1-exchange}
Every family realizing the PR box mints at least one held-\(\top\)
measurement under the knowledge-join merge; the witness family attains
exactly one; and the cycle rank \(\check H^1 = |E| - (|V| - |C|)\) of the PR
context-graph Euler counts (four measurements, four contexts, one component)
is one. The minimum \(\top\)-count and the cycle rank agree at the PR box.
Axiom profile: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}, inherited from
\texttt{List.countP\_pos\_iff} (no axiom-free replacement exists in this
core-only project).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The merged record's \(\top\)-support is lossy]\label{thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.fig8_merge_top_below_rank}\leanok\uses{thm:pr-top-h1-exchange}
On the figure-eight --- two triangle contexts sharing one measurement, one
odd context per triangle --- both triangle classes are nonzero and the
cycle rank is \(2\), yet every realizing family's merge holds at least one
\(\top\)-cell and a witness family attains exactly one: both disagreements
land on the shared measurement, where the idempotent knowledge join
collapses them. The per-contact carriers price the same multiplicity at the
rank (each excess contact its own held \(\top\):
\texttt{fundamental\_cycle\_one\_bit},
\ref{thm:coordination-h1-iff-forest}); the vertex-keyed merged Belnap
record undercounts it. This is a finite quantitative witness of the corpus
statement that the bare charge is the lossy shadow of the record sector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The merged-support norm]\label{thm:top-h1-exchange-rate}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.merged_support_norm}\leanok\uses{thm:pr-top-h1-exchange, thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}
The general exchange rate, proven as its two constructions. \emph{Cover to
family} (\texttt{payFamily\_realizing}, \texttt{payFamily\_top\_bound}):
for any reference section and any picked endpoint per context, the family that
plays the reference everywhere and flips the pick on each frustrated context
realizes the model, and its merge mints held \(\top\) only at picked
endpoints of frustrated contexts, so the \(\top\)-count is at most the
pick-count. \emph{Family to cover}
(\texttt{top\_set\_covers\_frustrated},
\texttt{top\_measurement\_two\_contexts}): for any realizing family,
relative to the merge's readout the \(\top\)-set covers every frustrated
pair context, and every \(\top\)-measurement lies in at least two contexts
--- degree-one measurements never mint, so they are free in the cover weight.
Read at the minimum the two bounds meet: the least merged \(\top\)-support
equals the least vertex-cover weight of the frustrated edge set over the coset
\([t] + B^1\), degree-one measurements free.
Axiom profile: inherited
from the merge machinery (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}); this
characterizes the support of the vertex-keyed idempotent merge --- the lossy
shadow --- with the per-contact count remaining the cycle rank
(\cref{thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The bid--ask spread between the two merge prices]\label{thm:two-spread-ledger}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.two_spread_ledger}\leanok\uses{thm:top-h1-exchange-rate, thm:record-merge-ground-state, thm:record-mass-vs-rank}
The idempotent Belnap merge (\texttt{topCount} of \texttt{mergeRecord}) and the
counting record merge (\texttt{massSum} of \texttt{mergeTally}) price the same
obstruction, and the prices order. Pointwise
(\texttt{incons\_iff\_one\_le\_topMass}) the Belnap merge holds \(\top\) at a
measurement iff the record carries at least one unit of contradiction mass
there: the idempotent merge is the counting merge read through a \(0/1\)
indicator, discarding multiplicity. Summed
(\texttt{topCount\_le\_massSum}), the Belnap cover price never exceeds the
record mass at the same family; the difference is the erased multiplicity, one
bit per collapsed distinction (\cref{thm:mass-is-landauer}). The gap is
realized on two carriers: the figure-eight opens the cover/mass spread (least
Belnap price one, least record mass two,
\cref{thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}) and the theta graph opens the mass/rank
spread (least record mass one, cycle rank two,
\cref{thm:record-mass-vs-rank}). Belnap price, record mass, and cycle rank are
three distinct prices for one obstruction, and which spread is nonzero
diagnoses the frustration: a shared odd edge opens mass \(<\) rank, a
high-degree paying measurement opens cover \(<\) mass. Axiom profile: inherited
from the merge machinery (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The currency cocone]\label{thm:currency-cocone}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.PricedNerve.legs_order_into_rank}\leanok\uses{thm:two-spread-ledger, thm:mint-register-is-top-support, thm:causal-mod-two-reduction}
The five \(H^1\) prices bundle into a cocone over one cover nerve. The apex
\texttt{PricedNerve} carries, over a base \texttt{CoverNerve}, the merge held-
\(\top\) support, the record mass, the ledger mint and jump registers, the causal
wavefront, and the (vanishing) propositional \v{C}ech price, constrained by the
three proved edges: \(d \le b\) (\cref{thm:two-spread-ledger}), \(c = d\) as
mints plus jumps (\cref{thm:mint-register-is-top-support}), and \(e \ge d\)
(\cref{thm:causal-mod-two-reduction}). Each currency is a leg
(\texttt{legCech}, \texttt{legRank}, \texttt{legMint}, \texttt{legTop},
\texttt{legCausal}); the cocone law \texttt{legs\_order\_into\_rank} (no axioms) is
that every leg factors through the base cycle rank consistently ---
\(\texttt{cech} = 0 \le \texttt{mint} \le \texttt{top} \le \texttt{mass} \le
\texttt{rank}\) and \(\texttt{top} \le \texttt{causal}\). At zero rank the whole
priced diagram collapses to \(0\) (\texttt{coordinationFree\_collapses}): the
shared vanishing point. The apex sits over the held-\(\top\)-as-contact base of
rigidity percolation (\cref{thm:mass-is-landauer}) --- between the held-\(\top\)
invariant and the settling ledger. The PR box instantiates a non-forest cocone
with every price one (\texttt{prPricedNerve\_nontrivial}, no axioms).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The currency cocone is the colimit]\label{thm:currency-colimit}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.PricedNerve.colimit_universal}\leanok\uses{thm:currency-cocone}
The cocone \cref{thm:currency-cocone} is initial. In the poset category
\((\mathbb N, \le)\) --- where a morphism \(m \to n\) is the order \(m \le n\) ---
a cocone over the currency legs is an upper bound, and the colimit is their join.
The join is \(\mathrm{colimitPrice} = \max(\mathrm{massPrice},
\mathrm{causalPrice})\): by the edge order the mint, top and mass legs sit below
the mass and the causal leg above the top, so the two maximal legs are the mass
and the causal. It is a cocone (\texttt{legs\_le\_colimitPrice}: every leg factors
into it) and it is \emph{least} (\texttt{colimit\_universal}: for every \(n\), all
five legs are \(\le n\) iff \(\mathrm{colimitPrice} \le n\)) --- the universal
property, the unique mediating map to any competing cocone being the order
\(\mathrm{colimitPrice} \le n\). The mediating value is unique
(\texttt{colimitPrice\_unique}: any value with the same universal property equals
it, by antisymmetry). This upgrades ``the five \(H^1\)'s order into one cycle
rank'' to ``there is one universal currency, and every disagreement cost factors
through it uniquely'' --- the single price of disagreement as a universal
property, not only a shared bound. Axiom profile: \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Quot.sound} (\(\mathbb N\) order arithmetic).
\end{theorem}
