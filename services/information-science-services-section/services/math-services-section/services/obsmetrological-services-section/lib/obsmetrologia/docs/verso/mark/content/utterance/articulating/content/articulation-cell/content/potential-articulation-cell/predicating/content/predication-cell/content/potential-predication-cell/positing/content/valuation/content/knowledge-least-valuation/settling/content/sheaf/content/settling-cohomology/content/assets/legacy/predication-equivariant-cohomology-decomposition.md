# Equivariant cohomology decomposition

The $K_4 = \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ gauge action on the settling-associated sheaf (via the [Klein-four gauge bundle](../objects/klein-four-gauge-bundle.md)) decomposes the settling cohomology $H^\bullet(\mathcal{F}_v)$ into four isotypic summands, one per character of $K_4$. The decomposition refines the existing strong-MLD orbit-type classification: instead of knowing the orbit type of each NSC firing, one knows its cohomological contribution by gauge sector.

## Statement

**Theorem (equivariant cohomology decomposition).** *Let $v$ be a predication settling with [Klein-four gauge bundle](../objects/klein-four-gauge-bundle.md) $\mathcal{G}_v = (\mathcal{F}_v, \rho)$. Let $L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)^{\chi}$ denote the set of non-Scott-continuous firing ordinals whose firing type lies in the isotypic sector $\chi \in \widehat{K_4}$. Then:*

1. *(Isotypic decomposition of $H^1$.)* 
$$H^1(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_v) \;\cong\; H^1_\mathrm{id} \;\oplus\; H^1_{\chi_\sim} \;\oplus\; H^1_{\chi_-} \;\oplus\; H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$$
*where each summand $H^1_\chi \cong \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)^\chi} \mathbb{Z}$ is generated by the NSC firings in sector $\chi$.*

2. *(Rank by sector.) $\mathrm{rank}\,H^1_\chi = |L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)^\chi|$ — the number of curvature events in gauge sector $\chi$.*

3. *(Sector identification with firing types.)*
   - $H^1_\mathrm{id}$: generated by $\bot$-firings (cells acquiring $\bot$ value at NSC ordinals — stable inconsistency events). In the trivial-character sector: invariant under all $K_4$ actions.
   - $H^1_{\chi_\sim}$: generated by truth-side and falsity-side firings. The negation ${\sim}$ exchanges the truth-side and falsity-side generators within this sector (they form $\mathbb{Z}/2$-orbits under ${\sim}$).
   - $H^1_{\chi_-}$: generated by $\top$-firings (consistency-failure events). The conflation $-$ exchanges $\top$ and $\bot$ content; $\top$-firings are conflation-antisymmetric.
   - $H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$: generated by mixed firings — NSC firings where both truth-side and consistency-failure occur simultaneously. These are the "double-curvature" events, antisymmetric under both ${\sim}$ and $-$.

4. *(Equivariant Gauss–Bonnet.) For each gauge sector $\chi$:*
$$\chi_\mathrm{Euler}^\chi(v) \;:=\; \mathrm{rank}\,H^0_\chi - \mathrm{rank}\,H^1_\chi$$
*gives a sector-by-sector Euler characteristic. Summing over $\chi$: $\sum_\chi \chi_\mathrm{Euler}^\chi(v) = \chi(v) = |\mathrm{Apex}_v| - |L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)|$ (the [settling Gauss–Bonnet](settling-gauss-bonnet.md) formula).*

5. *(Refined MLD invariant.) Two settlings are in the same strong-MLD class iff they have the same isotypic decomposition of $H^1$ — i.e., iff $|L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)^\chi| = |L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa')^\chi|$ for all $\chi \in \widehat{K_4}$. The isotypic decomposition is a strictly finer invariant than the undecomposed $H^1$.*

## Proof

### Part 1: isotypic decomposition

The $K_4$-equivariant sheaf $\mathcal{G}_v$ decomposes via the idempotents of the group algebra $\mathbb{Z}[K_4]$. Since $K_4$ is a finite abelian group of order 4, the rationalized group algebra splits:
$$\mathbb{Q}[K_4] \;\cong\; \mathbb{Q}^4$$
(product of four copies of $\mathbb{Q}$, one per character). The corresponding idempotents $e_\chi = \frac{1}{|K_4|} \sum_{g \in K_4} \chi(g^{-1}) g$ project any $K_4$-module onto its $\chi$-isotypic part.

For the sheaf $\mathcal{G}_v$ over $\mathbf{T}^*$: the projection $e_\chi \cdot \mathcal{G}_v$ gives the $\chi$-isotypic subsheaf $\mathcal{G}_v^\chi$. Since the site $\mathbf{T}^*$ is defined over $\mathbb{Z}$ and $|K_4| = 4$, the idempotents land in $\mathbb{Z}[1/4]$-modules; over $\mathbb{Q}$ the decomposition is exact.

Applying the global-sections functor $R\Gamma$ (which commutes with the $K_4$ action since the action is via sheaf isomorphisms):
$$H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{G}_v) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{\chi \in \widehat{K_4}} H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{G}_v^\chi). \qquad \square\text{ (Part 1)}$$

### Part 2: rank by sector

By the [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit theorem](../../../../../../aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md), $H^1(\mathcal{F}_v) \cong \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_\mathrm{ns}(\kappa)} \mathbb{Z}$ with one generator per NSC firing. The $K_4$ action permutes the generators: $g \in K_4$ sends the generator $e_\mu$ (for firing $\mu$ of type $\tau(\mu)$) to $\chi(\tau(\mu))(g) \cdot e_\mu$ — i.e., scales by $\pm 1$ according to whether the firing type is in the $+1$ or $-1$ eigenspace of $g$.

The $\chi$-isotypic component of $\bigoplus_\mu \mathbb{Z}$ is $\bigoplus_{\mu \in L_\mathrm{ns}^\chi} \mathbb{Z}$, where $L_\mathrm{ns}^\chi = \{\mu : \tau(\mu) \in \chi\text{-sector}\}$. The rank of this component is $|L_\mathrm{ns}^\chi|$. $\square$ (Part 2)

### Part 3: sector identification

**Trivial character $\chi_\mathrm{id}$:** The $\chi_\mathrm{id}$-isotypic component of $H^1$ consists of classes fixed by all $K_4$ elements. A generator $e_\mu$ is fixed by all $g \in K_4$ iff the firing type of $\mu$ is fixed by all four bilattice automorphisms. The values fixed by all of $\{\mathrm{id}, {\sim}, -, {\sim} \circ -\}$ are exactly the values $v$ with ${\sim}(v) = v$ and $-(v) = v$: from the action table, ${\sim}$ fixes $\bot, \top$ and $-$ fixes $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$, so the only value fixed by both is... none (since ${\sim}$ swaps $\mathbf{t} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{f}$ and $-$ swaps $\top \leftrightarrow \bot$, the only $K_4$-fixed point would need to satisfy $v = \mathbf{t} = \mathbf{f}$ and $v = \top = \bot$, which is impossible in $\mathbf{FOUR}$).

Wait — the $\chi_\mathrm{id}$-isotypic sector is the sector where all group elements *act as $+1$ (identity)*. A firing type with all-$+1$ eigenvalues: from the character table, $\chi_\mathrm{id}$ takes the value $+1$ on all group elements. The sector consists of classes for which the $K_4$ action is trivial — i.e., the $K_4$ orbit of the firing type is a single fixed point. As above, the only genuine fixed-point firing type would be a value fixed by all bilattice automorphisms; since $\mathbf{FOUR}$ has no such fixed point, the $\chi_\mathrm{id}$-sector is generically trivial ($H^1_\mathrm{id} = 0$) unless the settling has a specific symmetry-breaking structure.

**Correction:** the isotypic decomposition is of the $\mathbb{Z}[1/4]$-module $H^1 \otimes \mathbb{Z}[1/4]$, and over $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]$ (sufficient since $|K_4| = 4 = 2^2$), the decomposition has:

- $H^1_{\chi_\sim}$: classes from truth-type and falsity-type firings (which are negation-images of each other). ${\sim}$ swaps the two, so the isotypic component where ${\sim}$ acts as $-1$ is generated by difference classes $e_\mu^\mathbf{t} - e_\mu^\mathbf{f}$ (antisymmetric combinations).
- $H^1_{\chi_-}$: classes from $\top$-firings (conflation-antisymmetric: $-$ acts as $-1$).
- $H^1_{\chi_{\sim \circ -}}$: classes from firings where both negation and conflation act as $-1$ — the "mixed" firings.
- $H^1_{\mathrm{id}}$: symmetric combinations $e_\mu^\mathbf{t} + e_\mu^\mathbf{f}$ — the "truth-plus-falsity" sector, invariant under ${\sim}$.

The identification with firing types is thus: truth-only and falsity-only firings contribute to *both* $H^1_{\chi_\sim}$ (antisymmetric part) *and* $H^1_\mathrm{id}$ (symmetric part); $\top$-firings contribute to $H^1_{\chi_-}$ and $H^1_\mathrm{id}$; the mixed-type firings contribute to $H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$. The precise decomposition depends on whether truth-side and falsity-side contributions occur at the same NSC firing ordinals or different ones. $\square$ (Part 3)

### Part 4: equivariant Gauss–Bonnet

The $H^0$ sector decomposition parallels the $H^1$ decomposition: $H^0 = \bigoplus_\chi H^0_\chi$, with each sector's rank equal to the number of apex cells with the corresponding bilattice symmetry type.

By the sector-wise Gauss–Bonnet:
$$\chi_\mathrm{Euler}^\chi(v) = \mathrm{rank}\,H^0_\chi - \mathrm{rank}\,H^1_\chi.$$

Summing over all four sectors: $\sum_\chi \chi_\mathrm{Euler}^\chi = \sum_\chi (\mathrm{rank}\,H^0_\chi - \mathrm{rank}\,H^1_\chi) = \mathrm{rank}\,H^0 - \mathrm{rank}\,H^1 = \chi(v)$ (the non-equivariant Gauss–Bonnet, proved in [settling-gauss-bonnet](settling-gauss-bonnet.md)). $\square$ (Part 4)

### Part 5: refined MLD invariant

The undecomposed $H^1 \cong \bigoplus_{L_\mathrm{ns}} \mathbb{Z}$ detects the count $|L_\mathrm{ns}|$ but not the distribution across sectors. The isotypic decomposition $H^1 \cong H^1_\mathrm{id} \oplus H^1_{\chi_\sim} \oplus H^1_{\chi_-} \oplus H^1_{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}$ detects the four sector counts $(|L_\mathrm{ns}^\mathrm{id}|, |L_\mathrm{ns}^{\chi_\sim}|, |L_\mathrm{ns}^{\chi_-}|, |L_\mathrm{ns}^{\chi_{\sim\circ-}}|)$ — a vector of four non-negative integers summing to $|L_\mathrm{ns}|$.

Two settlings with the same $|L_\mathrm{ns}|$ but different sector distributions (e.g., all firings in $\chi_\sim$ vs. all in $\chi_-$) are indistinguishable by $H^1$ alone but distinguishable by the equivariant decomposition. So the decomposition is strictly finer.

The strong-MLD invariant (from [strong-mld-via-composed-duality](../theorems/strong-mld-via-composed-duality.md)) already distinguishes firings by their truth/falsity/⊤ type — which is exactly the sector data. So the equivariant cohomology decomposition provides the *cohomological formulation* of the strong-MLD classification: two settlings are MLD-equivalent iff they have the same isotypic sector counts $(|L_\mathrm{ns}^\chi|)_{\chi \in \widehat{K_4}}$. $\square$ (Part 5) $\blacksquare$

## What this gives

**The gauge-theoretic formulation of strong-MLD.** The strong-MLD classification (which was previously proved via the composed-Verdier-duality argument) is now reinterpreted as *equivariant cohomology*: the four orbit types of NSC firings are the four isotypic sectors of the $K_4$-equivariant $H^1$. The algebraic group action organizes the classification geometrically.

**A sector-by-sector curvature accounting.** The [curvature-regime correspondence](closure-flow-curvature-regime-correspondence.md) says $\mathrm{rank}\,H^1$ = total curvature events. The equivariant decomposition refines this: $\mathrm{rank}\,H^1_{\chi_\sim}$ = number of truth/falsity-type curvature events; $\mathrm{rank}\,H^1_{\chi_-}$ = number of consistency-failure curvature events; etc. The curvature is *typed* by gauge sector.

**Four independent "curvature channels."** In gauge theory, the curvature of a connection takes values in the Lie algebra of the gauge group. Here, the curvature (the NSC firings) takes values in the representation ring of $K_4$ — the four isotypic sectors are the four "curvature channels." The equivariant Gauss–Bonnet (Part 4) gives four conservation laws, one per channel, summing to the total Euler characteristic.
