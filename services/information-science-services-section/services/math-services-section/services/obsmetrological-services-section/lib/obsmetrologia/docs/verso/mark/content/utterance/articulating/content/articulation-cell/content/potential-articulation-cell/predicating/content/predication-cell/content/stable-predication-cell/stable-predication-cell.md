The **stable** corner of the [predication cell](../../predication-cell.md)'s
recurring slot-presence partition — both evidence slots present, told both: the
knowledge-top, absorbing corner that no further telling can move. It is one of
the four refinements the predication cell comes in, the same four-point object
met at the [articulation cell](../../../../../../../articulation-cell.md)'s
phases and, one regime down, at the bilattice charge. The generative descent
continues from the
[potential](../potential-predication-cell/potential-predication-cell.md) corner
(via [positing](../potential-predication-cell/positing/positing.md)); this
corner is reached by the predication category's internal morphisms — only ever
reached, never born into. (Corner names follow the evidence reading, ruled
2026-08-05; see `docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`. The partition is
now derived — **valuation-relative**, not cell-intrinsic: `Valuation.phase`
reads a claim's corner directly off a
[valuation](../potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/valuation.md)
(`phase v φ := v φ`), exhaustive (`Valuation.phase_cases`) and pairwise
distinct (`Articulating.Corner.pairwise_ne`) across the four corners. This
corner specifically is reached, from the seed, by *contesting* $\phi$
(`Valuation.phase_contesting_knowledgeLeast`), and once reached no further
posit at that claim can move it out — $\top_k$ is $\vee_k$-absorbing
(`Valuation.phase_stable_absorbs`). Together with the born-absurd fact
(`Valuation.phase_knowledgeLeast`) this makes precise the line above: only
ever reached, never born into. What remains open is a *cell-intrinsic*
corner — one property of the triple $\phi$ alone, independent of any
valuation; the schema forbids a value field, so no theorem claims one. See
`Language/Predicating/CellPartition.lean`.)

The morphism that produced the cell is
[predicating](../../../../predicating.md).
