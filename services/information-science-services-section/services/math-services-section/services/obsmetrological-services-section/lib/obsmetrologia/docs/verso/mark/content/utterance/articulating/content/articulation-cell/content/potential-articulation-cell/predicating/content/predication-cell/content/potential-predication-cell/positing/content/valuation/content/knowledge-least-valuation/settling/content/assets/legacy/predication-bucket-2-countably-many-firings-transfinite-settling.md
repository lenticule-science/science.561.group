# Countably-many-firings transfinite settling

A [transfinite-bounded-regime](../theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md) settling with $\aleph_0$-many non-Scott-continuous firings — the natural step beyond [two-firing transfinite settling](two-firing-transfinite-settling.md), still using the same uniform $\Pi_1$-rule, with the entity set scaled up to $\omega^2 + 1$.

## Construction

$\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$, [entity set](../objects/entity-set.md) $E = \omega^2 + 1$, [predicate set](../objects/predicate-set.md) $\Pi = \{P\}$ with $\mathrm{dom}(P) = \Delta_E$, [predicate category](../objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$ the one-object category on $P$, [settling bound](../objects/settling-bound.md) $\lambda = \omega^2 + 2$. Single [closure rule](../concepts/closure-rule.md) — the transfinite-induction $\Pi_1$-schema:

$$(\forall \alpha < \beta.\; v(P)(\alpha, \alpha) \geq_k \mathbf{t}) \;\Longrightarrow\; v(P)(\beta, \beta) \geq_k \mathbf{t}, \qquad \beta < \omega^2 + 1.$$

[Increment sequence](../objects/increment-sequence.md): $u_0(P)(0, 0) = \mathbf{t}$, all other increments $\bot$.

## Settling stages

By transfinite induction (mirroring [transfinite-induction settling](transfinite-induction-settling.md)'s argument), the $\mathbf{t}$-support at stage $\tau \leq \omega^2$ is exactly $\{(\alpha, \alpha) : \alpha \leq \tau\}$.

## Closure ordinal

$\kappa = \omega^2 + 1$. The settling strictly increases through every successor stage and gains a new diagonal cell at every limit + 1 stage; at $\tau = \omega^2 + 1$ the rule fires for $\beta = \omega^2$, yielding $v_{\omega^2 + 1}(P)(\omega^2, \omega^2) = \mathbf{t}$. Thereafter no $\beta \in E$ exceeds $\omega^2$, so no further firing.

Since $\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$, the settling lies in the [transfinite-bounded regime](../theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md).

## Non-Scott-continuous firings

The closure rule fires non-Scott-continuously at exactly the countable limit ordinals $\mu$ with $\mu \leq \omega^2$:

$$L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa) \;=\; \{\omega \cdot n : 1 \leq n < \omega\} \;\cup\; \{\omega^2\}.$$

At each such $\mu$ the antecedent $\forall \alpha < \mu.\, v(P)(\alpha, \alpha) \geq_k \mathbf{t}$ is satisfied at the directed join $\bigvee_{\sigma < \mu} v_\sigma$ but not at any individual $v_\sigma$ — the limit-continuity step gathers infinitely many earlier consequents, and only then does the rule's antecedent at $\beta = \mu$ commit.

Cardinality: $|L_{\mathrm{ns}}| = \aleph_0$. The firing pattern is order-isomorphic to $\omega + 1$ — countably many firings cofinal at $\omega^2$ together with the limit firing at $\omega^2$ itself.

## Classifying invariant

By [transfinite-bounded-regime $H^1$-generators](../theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md):

$$H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \omega^2 + 1},\, \mathcal{F}_U) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}} \mathbb{Z} \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}^{(\omega + 1)},$$

with one generator $e_\mu$ per non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal. The [aperiodic classifying invariant](../objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) is the all-ones element

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) \;=\; \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e_{\omega \cdot n} \;+\; e_{\omega^2}.$$

This is the smallest example with $\aleph_0$-many non-trivial cohomology generators.

## Realizability observation

The example completes the cardinality progression for the realizability mechanism described in [two-firing transfinite settling](two-firing-transfinite-settling.md): the same uniform $\Pi_1$-rule, parametrized only by entity-set choice, realizes $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-sets of every cardinality up to $\aleph_1$:

| $E$ | $\kappa$ | $\|L_{\mathrm{ns}}\|$ | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\omega + 1$ | $\omega + 1$ | $1$ | [quantifier-induced transfinite settling](quantifier-induced-transfinite-settling.md) |
| $\omega \cdot 2 + 1$ | $\omega \cdot 2 + 1$ | $2$ | [two-firing transfinite settling](two-firing-transfinite-settling.md) |
| $\omega^2 + 1$ | $\omega^2 + 1$ | $\aleph_0$ | this file |
| $\omega_1$ | $\omega_1$ | $\aleph_1$ | [transfinite-induction settling](transfinite-induction-settling.md) |

For each ordinal $\eta \leq \omega_1$, taking $E = \eta + 1$ realizes $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\eta + 1) = \{\mu \leq \eta : \mu \text{ a countable limit ordinal}\}$. The realizable subsets generated by base-shift alone are exactly the *initial segments* of countable limit ordinals.

**Scope.** As with [two-firing transfinite settling](two-firing-transfinite-settling.md), this observation concerns the truth-side $L_{\mathrm{ns}}^{(t)}$ of a consistent single-predicate $\{\bot, \mathbf{t}\}$-valued settling. The bigraded, multi-predicate, and $H^0$-pattern dimensions of the [realizability question](../questions/realizability-of-cohomology-classes.md) are separate.

## Role

Smallest example with countably-many non-trivial $H^1$-generators. Bridges the finite-firing examples ($|L_{\mathrm{ns}}| = 1, 2$) and the canonical aperiodic example ($|L_{\mathrm{ns}}| = \aleph_1$). Demonstrates that base-shift alone realizes a one-parameter family of $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-sets indexed by ordinals — the simplest realizability mechanism in the closure-rule formalism.
