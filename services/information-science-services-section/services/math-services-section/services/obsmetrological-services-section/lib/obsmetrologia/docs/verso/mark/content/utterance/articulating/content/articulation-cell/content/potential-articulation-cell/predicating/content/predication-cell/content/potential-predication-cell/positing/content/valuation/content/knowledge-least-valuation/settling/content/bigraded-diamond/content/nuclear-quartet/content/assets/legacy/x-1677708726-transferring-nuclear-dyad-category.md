The **transferring-nuclear-dyad-category** is the category generated by [transferring](../../transferring.md). An object is a [nuclear-dyad](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/nuclear-dyad.md) $(A, \Delta)$ where $\Delta$ is specifically a transfer nucleus — forward-facing, oriented toward extensions.

## Objects

Each object is a Heyting algebra $A$ together with a transfer nucleus $\Delta : A \to A$. The dyad presents two corner-cells:

- **Free corner**: $A$. All elements; $\Delta$ may still have work to do.
- **Δ-quiescent corner**: $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta) = \{a \in A : \Delta(a) = a\}$. The extension-witnessed layer.

Connected by the adjoint reflection $\Delta : A \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta) : \iota$. Strict unit $a < \Delta(a)$ for $a \notin \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta)$.

## Morphisms

A morphism $(A, \Delta_A) \to (A', \Delta_{A'})$ is a Heyting algebra homomorphism $f : A \to A'$ satisfying

$$
f \circ \Delta_A \;=\; \Delta_{A'} \circ f.
$$

The Δ-preservation condition: $f$ commutes with the transfer nucleus. This forces $f(\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_A)) \subseteq \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_{A'})$, so morphisms restrict to maps between Δ-quiescent sub-algebras.

## Composition and identity

Composition is composition of Heyting homomorphisms; identity is the identity homomorphism. The Δ-preservation condition is closed under composition.

## Connection to articulation

The two corner-cells of a transferring-nuclear-dyad correspond to two phase-cells of articulation along the claim-axis (with the token-axis left free):

| Corner       | Sub-algebra | Phase-cell along claim-axis |
|--------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Free         | $A$ | potential along claim (token-axis free) |
| Δ-quiescent  | $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta)$ | active along claim (token-axis free) |

The transferring-nuclear-dyad records the claim-axis closure dynamic alone; the token-axis is left untouched. Pairing this dyad with the [saturating-nuclear-dyad-category](../../../../../../aperiodic-settling/saturating/content/saturating-nuclear-dyad-category/saturating-nuclear-dyad-category.md) on the same carrier under [commutating](../../../commutating/commutating.md) produces the full [aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category](../../../../../../aperiodic-settling/commutating/content/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category.md).

## Pairwise instance

The fiber Heyting algebras $H_t$ over a history category yield specific transferring-nuclear-dyad objects $(H_t, \Delta_t)$, one per history $t$. Morphisms between $(H_t, \Delta_t)$ and $(H_{t'}, \Delta_{t'})$ for $t \leq t'$ are exactly the restriction homomorphisms that preserve transfer.
