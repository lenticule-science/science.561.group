## What this node is

The category generated by [transferring](../../transferring.md). Its **objects**
are Heyting algebras $A$ equipped with a single transfer nucleus $\Delta$; its
**morphisms** are the Heyting-algebra homomorphisms that commute with $\Delta$.

Each object is a **nuclear dyad**:

$$
A \;\underset{\iota}{\overset{\Delta}{\rightleftarrows}}\; \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta),
\qquad \Delta \dashv \iota,
$$

the free carrier $A$ reflecting onto its extension-witnessed layer
$\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta)$.

## Orientation

The mirror of the
[saturating-nuclear-dyad-category](../../../saturating/content/saturating-nuclear-dyad-category/saturating-nuclear-dyad-category.md):
one closes toward the future, the other toward the past. When the two nuclei
commute, [commutating](../../../commutating/commutating.md) fuses both dyads into
the four-corner
[aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category](../../../commutating/content/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category.md).
