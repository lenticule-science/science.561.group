## What this node is

The category generated by [saturating](../../saturating.md). Its **objects** are
Heyting algebras $A$ equipped with a single saturation nucleus $\sigma$; its
**morphisms** are the Heyting-algebra homomorphisms that commute with $\sigma$.

Each object is a **nuclear dyad** — two poles connected by an adjunction:

$$
A \;\underset{\iota}{\overset{\sigma}{\rightleftarrows}}\; \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma),
\qquad \sigma \dashv \iota,
$$

the free carrier $A$ reflecting onto its sub-history-determined layer
$\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma)$, with the inclusion $\iota$ right adjoint to the closure.

## Orientation

This is one of the two single-nucleus dyads an [aperiodic
settling](../../../aperiodic-settling.md) carries; the other is the
[transferring-nuclear-dyad-category](../../../transferring/content/transferring-nuclear-dyad-category/transferring-nuclear-dyad-category.md).
When the two nuclei commute, [commutating](../../../commutating/commutating.md)
fuses the dyads into the four-corner
[aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category](../../../commutating/content/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category.md).
