## Annotation

Textbook reference for coalgebraic methods. Used in our program for the hyperdoctrinal-layer's framing of the settling as a coalgebra in a 2-category.

Most relevant sections:
- **§1.3 temporal logic of coalgebras** — supports the modal-temporal framing of settling dynamics.
- **§2.3 final coalgebras** — used when settlings fail to stabilize at a countable ordinal (the aperiodic regime); the indexed final coalgebra theorem of Aczel 1988 + van den Berg–de Marchi 2007 underwrites the aperiodic-regime existence claims.
- **Chapter 5 monads, comonads, distributive laws** — reference for the closure-as-KZ-2-monad treatment in the hyperdoctrinal layer.
- **Chapter 6 coalgebra invariants via predicate lifting** — Jacobs's $\Box P$ ("greatest invariant contained in $P$") is dual on the Priestley side to our closure operator $\mathrm{Cl}$. The cohomological-layer's $H^0$ interpretation uses this duality.
