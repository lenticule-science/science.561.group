Packages gluing and locality for the hypertensor sheaf over the hypertensor
site.

## Definition
- Gluing: every [Matching Family](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/matching-family.md) over a cover has a unique
  amalgam ([Sheaf Glue](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/sheaf-glue.md)).
- Locality: sections equal on a cover are equal globally ([Sheaf Locality](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/sheaf-locality.md)).

## Invariants
- Sheaf axioms hold for the hypertensor site.
- Stable under cover refinement.

## Proof
- Derived from: [Hypertensor Site](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/hypertensor-site.md).
- Uses: [Sheaf Glue](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/sheaf-glue.md) and [Sheaf Locality](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/sheaf-locality.md).
- Shows that: the hypertensor presheaf satisfies the sheaf condition.
