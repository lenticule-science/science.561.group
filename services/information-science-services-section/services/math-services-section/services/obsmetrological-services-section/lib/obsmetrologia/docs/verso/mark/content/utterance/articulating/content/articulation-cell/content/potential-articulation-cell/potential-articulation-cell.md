## What this node is

The **potential** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **token side
is articulated and the claim side is not** — `(token: yes, claim: no)`, *told
true*. The identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has
been committed; the content-carrying
[claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) remains open.

## Position in the progression

Under the token-before-claim working theory, potential is the **first step out
of the [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) cell**:
articulating naturally proceeds by articulating the token side first, so absurd
→ potential is the opening move of the natural progression, which continues
potential → [stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md)
when the claim side is articulated too. Potential is "on the path" — the
*which-one* is fixed while *what-is-said* is still to come. (Whether
token-before-claim is the right ordering is open work; under the reversed
reading this corner's role and
[active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md)'s would swap.)

"Potential" is exact: with an articulated token in hand, the cell has something
to say something *of* — it is the corner from which
[predicating](predicating/predicating.md) proceeds, seeding the reflexive
predication cell from the token it now carries. A cell still in the absurd
corner has nothing to seed from; a potential cell is unspent capacity for
predication.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material used this name for
the (no, no) initial corner and is preserved unrewritten in archives. Material
from the corpus's aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in
[`_archive/`](_archive).

## Orientation

Read [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) for the
seed it steps from,
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md) for where it
continues, and the [articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole
partition. The morphism that generated the cell is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md); the morphism that proceeds from
this corner is [predicating](predicating/predicating.md).
