## What this node is

The **potential** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **token
slot carries a witness and the claim slot does not** — `(token: yes, claim:
no)`, *told true*. The identity-carrying
[token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has been witnessed; the
content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) remains
free.

## What articulating always produces

To **articulate** an utterance (`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.articulate`)
is to place the utterance's own token as the witness on the token slot and
leave the claim slot empty. That is not one route to potential among several;
it is the **only** cell a bare articulation ever produces
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.articulate_corner`), and it is the corner
articulating's own codomain lands on at every floor of the recursion. This is
settled by construction — owner ruling 2026-09-21 — not a working theory
about which side goes first: the token side goes first because articulating's
one operation witnesses it first, full stop.

"Potential" is exact: with a witnessed token in hand, the cell has something
to say something *of* — it is the corner from which
[predicating](predicating/predicating.md) proceeds, seeding the reflexive
predication cell from the token it now carries. The
[absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) position —
neither slot witnessed — is not a cell articulating ever visits on the way
here; potential is where articulating starts and stays until engaged.

## Position in the progression

**Engaging** a potential cell with a claim-side witness
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.engage`) reaches
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md) — both
slots witnessed
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.engage_articulate_corner`). Potential →
stable is therefore the whole of articulating's own progression; the
[active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md) corner —
claim witnessed with no prior token — sits entirely off that progression,
reached only by the value algebra downstream, never by articulating.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material used this name for
the (no, no) un-articulated corner and is preserved unrewritten in archives.
Material from the corpus's aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held
in [`_archive/`](_archive).

## Orientation

Read [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) for
the un-articulated position this corner is never reached from (articulating
starts here directly),
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md) for where
engaging continues, and the [articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md)
for the whole partition. The morphism that generates the cell is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md); the morphism that proceeds from
this corner is [predicating](predicating/predicating.md).

## Earlier material

The **potential** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where **neither side has
been articulated** — `(token: no, claim: no)`. It is the **initial presentation**
of [articulating](../../../../articulating.md)'s codomain: the cell the act
carries the instant it has an [utterance](../../../../../utterance.md)
but has articulated neither its [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md)
nor its [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md). Everything else in the
category is reached *from* here.

"Potential" is exact: the cell holds an utterance whose two sides *could* be
articulated but are not yet. It carries the full pair structure — so the four-cell
partition is already determined — while committing to none of it. The transition
morphisms internal to the category move the potential cell to the other corners:
under the token-before-claim reading, potential → [active](../active-articulation-cell/active-articulation-cell.md)
(articulate the token) is the first step of the natural progression, continuing to
[absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md). The fourth
corner, [stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md), is
reached off that path.

Because it is the initial object, potential is where the recursion of articulating
**rests before descending**: it is the cell present at the bottom of every
articulation before any side is committed. Material from the corpus's aperiodic-
regime development of this cell is held in [`_archive/`](_archive).

[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md),
[absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) — and the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the partition as a whole. The
morphism that carries this initial cell is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
