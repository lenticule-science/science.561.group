## What this node is

This node holds dynamical systems: the study of how a state evolves under repeated application of a map, and where that iteration goes — fixed points, attractors, and the ordinal at which an orbit ceases to change. In the corpus's unfolding `articulate -> predicate -> posit -> settle`, this concept is generated by the **settle** morphism: settling is precisely the act of running a closure operator over a valuation until it reaches a fixed point, so the temporal axis it traces — the transfinite orbit $(v_\tau)_{\tau<\lambda}$ ascending by monotone, continuous steps toward saturation — *is* a dynamical system. The valuation dynamics and the ordinal closure that settle runs to a fixed point are not analogous to dynamics; they are dynamics, and this node is where that identification lives.

## What it generates / contains

- **`settling-dynamics/`** — the core body: the settling as a temporal orbit of valuations, closure ordinals, settling monotonicity and continuity, the four-regime dichotomy (strict / Scott-continuous / transfinite-bounded / aperiodic), settling-as-coalgebra in a temporal 2-category, cosettling and forward-trajectory structure, and the stochastic / continuous-state / higher-arity extensions of the framework.
- **`content/recursive-dynamical-systems/`** — the recursive subconcept: least-fixed-point iteration on Scott domains, the tower $\bot \sqsubseteq f(\bot) \sqsubseteq f^2(\bot) \sqsubseteq \cdots$ converging to $\mathrm{lfp}(f)$, where the state of a recursive computation is a domain element and settling is the directed join.
