## What this node is

The **bilattice** is the value structure [positing](../../../../positing.md) opens: the
distributive bilattice $L^{\mathrm{twist}}$ built over the two-element residuated
lattice $\mathbf{2}$ — where **bottom = unarticulated slot** and **top =
articulated slot**. It is explicitly *not* Belnap's FOUR by stipulation but four
**slot-presence** values by construction; its [charge](content/charge/charge.md)
is the same four-point object as the articulation phases, met here as *value*.

## Two orders, six operations

The bilattice carries **two orders** over the charges:

- the **knowledge order** $\le_k$ — both slots increasing — with
  **joining-knowledge** (presence-union, the lub) and **meeting-knowledge**
  (presence-intersection, the glb);
- the **truth order** $\le_t$ — positive up, negative down — with
  **joining-truth** (positive union, negative intersection) and **meeting-truth**
  (positive intersection, negative union).

Two further operations complete the six: **negating** (swap the two slots —
reverses the truth order, preserves the knowledge order, self-inverse) and
**comparing-knowledge** (the $\le_k$ witness).

## The modal pair

Built *from* these operations — metacircular by design — is the modal pair:
**possibilize** ($\Diamond$) raises each cell's charge to the knowledge-**join**
across the valuation's fiber-algebras (inflationary, "what could become known");
**necessitate** ($\Box$) lowers to the knowledge-**meet** (deflationary, "what
must hold"). They form an adjunction $\Diamond \dashv \Box$, and their composite
is the closure **settling** iterates to a fixed point.

## Orientation

Descend to [charge](content/charge/charge.md) for the four-point value the orders
range over. The morphism that opened the bilattice is
[positing](../../../../positing.md); the closure on its modal pair is what settling
runs.
