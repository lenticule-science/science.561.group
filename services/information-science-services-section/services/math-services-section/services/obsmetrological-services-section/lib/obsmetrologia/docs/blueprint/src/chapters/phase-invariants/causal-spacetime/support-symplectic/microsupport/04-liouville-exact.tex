\subsubsection{Liouville exactness}

\begin{theorem}[Discrete light-cone symplectic structure --- exact (Liouville form + polarization)]\label{thm:cotangent-symplectic-flow}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.Liouville.causal_liouville}\leanok\uses{thm:cotangent-microsupport, thm:causal-cohomology-h2, thm:causal-polarization, thm:causal-involutivity}
The light-cone symplectic structure is exact. The Liouville 1-form of a
position coordinate $c$ on a codirection $(a,b)$ is the tautological pairing
$c(b)-c(a)$, matching both the cotangent coboundary and the polarization form.
The symplectic 2-form $\omega=\delta^1\lambda$ is therefore a 2-coboundary and
hence a 2-cocycle; for $\lambda=\delta^0 c$ it collapses outright by
$\delta^1\delta^0=0$. The genuine nondegenerate datum lives at the 1-form
level: $\lambda_c$ is nonzero on every codirection exactly when $c$ properly
colours the causal graph, and factor maps preserve it.
\end{theorem}
