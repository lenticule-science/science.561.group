The **absurd** corner of the [predication cell](../../predication-cell.md)'s
recurring slot-presence partition — both evidence slots empty, told none: the
knowledge-bottom corner, the charge state a value-free cell holds before any
positing, constrained by nothing. It is one of the four refinements the
predication cell comes in, the same four-point object met at the
[articulation cell](../../../../../../../articulation-cell.md)'s phases and,
one regime down, at the bilattice charge. The generative descent continues from
the [potential](../potential-predication-cell/potential-predication-cell.md)
corner (via [positing](../potential-predication-cell/positing/positing.md));
positing is precisely what raises a cell out of this corner. (Corner names
follow the evidence reading, ruled 2026-08-05; see
`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`. The partition is now derived —
**valuation-relative**, not cell-intrinsic: `Valuation.phase` reads a claim's
corner directly off a [valuation](../potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/valuation.md)
(`phase v φ := v φ`), the four phases are exhaustive
(`Valuation.phase_cases`) and pairwise distinct
(`Articulating.Corner.pairwise_ne`), and this corner specifically is where
*every* valuation is born: the knowledge-least valuation puts every claim's
phase at absurd, with no exception (`Valuation.phase_knowledgeLeast`). What
remains open is a *cell-intrinsic* corner — one property of the triple $\phi$
alone, independent of any valuation; the schema forbids a value field, so no
theorem claims one. See `Language/Predicating/CellPartition.lean`.)

The morphism that produced the cell is
[predicating](../../../../predicating.md).
