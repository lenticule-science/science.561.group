# Four-element bilattice

The smallest non-trivial [bilattice](bilattice.md), denoted $\mathbf{FOUR}$, with carrier $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$.
The [truth order](../../../truth-order/truth-order.md) is generated by $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \top \leq_t \mathbf{t}$; the [knowledge order](../concepts/knowledge-order.md) is generated by $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$ and $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t} \leq_k \top$.
The [negation](../morphisms/negation.md) $\sim$ fixes $\bot$ and $\top$ and swaps $\mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f}$.

As a [twist product](../../../../../residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md), $\mathbf{FOUR} \cong \mathbf{2}^\bowtie$ — the twist of the Boolean lattice $\mathbf{2}$ with itself:

$$\mathbf{f} = \langle 0, 1 \rangle, \quad \bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle, \quad \top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle, \quad \mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle.$$

The four values carry the canonical epistemic interpretation: $\mathbf{t}$ as *told true*, $\mathbf{f}$ as *told false*, $\bot$ as *told nothing*, and $\top$ as *told both*.
