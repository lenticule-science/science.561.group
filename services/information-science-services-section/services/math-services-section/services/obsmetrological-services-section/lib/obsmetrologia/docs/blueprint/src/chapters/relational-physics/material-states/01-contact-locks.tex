\subsection{Contact locks}

\begin{proposition}[Keratin lock]\label{thm:keratin-lock}\lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.Lock}\leanok\uses{thm:tarski-sheaf-closure}
A keratin contact lock is an inflationary monotone closure step on a complete
lattice of contact states.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Keratin settling is the least compatible lock]\label{thm:keratin-settle-lock}\lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.settle_lock_is_least_compatible}\leanok\uses{thm:keratin-lock}
Running lock histories over a keratin complex yields the least compatible
settled contact assignment above the seed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Keratin formation streams are finite-prefix joins]\label{thm:keratin-lock-stream-limit}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.LockStream}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.lockStreamLimit}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.runLockPrefix_le_limit}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.runLockPrefix_le_of_le}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.runLockPrefix_directed}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.lockStreamLimitSeed}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.lockStreamPrefixSeed_le_limitSeed}
  \lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.MaterialSectors.Contact.settle_lockStreamLimit_is_least_compatible}
  \leanok
  \uses{thm:keratin-settle-lock}
An infinite keratin formation is represented as the directed join of its finite
lock prefixes. Every finite prefix lies below the formed seed, and settling
that formed seed is the least compatible state above the stream limit.
\end{theorem}

The Collatz no-stream complement has a keratin carrier: the prefix-event lock
stream over the powerset lattice admits every finite prefix event in its formed
limit, and certification on that limit is exactly a positive infinite Collatz
barrier stream. Equivalently, it is the cylinder-avoiding barrier stream, and
equivalently a positive seed with no finite contracting prefix. The no-stream
frontier excludes all such formations; in particular, the parent-barrier gap
together with the named one-lock collapse prevents this keratin formation.
Conversely, under the parent-barrier gap, one such formation refutes the named
one-lock collapse. Absence of all such formations is exactly the pointwise
double-negated finite-contraction frontier. Using the existing decidable-core
machinery, a decision for each contracting-prefix assertion produces the
corresponding Collatz prefix core and collapses this boundary to the no-stream
frontier; a seed-indexed finite search bound gives the same collapse through
bounded decidable cores. The existing anti-matter barrier-boundary carrier
already stores the cylinder-lift-to-contraction map, so with the cylinder-lift
face supplied it produces the same prefix cores directly; the full shared
κ-carrier does so from the cylinder-lift and one-lock data carried by its
Collatz projection.
\end{theorem}
