The **Klein-four group** $K_4 \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$ acting on a [bilattice](bilattice.md) equipped with both [negation](../transformers/negating.md) $\sim$ and [conflation](../transformers/conflating.md) $-$, generated by these two commuting involutions:

$$K_4 \;:=\; \langle \sim,\, - \rangle \;=\; \{\mathrm{id},\, \sim,\, -,\, \sim\circ-\}.$$

The four elements act distinctly on the bilattice:

- $\mathrm{id}$ — identity.
- $\sim$ — preserves $\leq_k$, reverses $\leq_t$.
- $-$ — preserves $\leq_t$, reverses $\leq_k$.
- $\sim\circ-$ — reverses both orders.

The two generators commute by the bilattice axiom $\sim\, - = -\, \sim$ when both are present.

## On FOUR

For the [four-element bilattice](four-bilattice.md):

| element | $\sim$ | $-$ | $\sim\circ-$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\bot$ | $\bot$ | $\top$ | $\top$ |
| $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ |
| $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ | $\mathbf{f}$ | $\mathbf{t}$ |
| $\top$ | $\top$ | $\bot$ | $\bot$ |

The orbits of $K_4$ on $\mathbf{FOUR}$ are $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}\}$ (a 2-orbit under $\sim$, fixed by $-$) and $\{\bot, \top\}$ (a 2-orbit under $-$, fixed by $\sim$). Each orbit is the extreme pair of one order and the incomparable middle pair of the other.

## Characters

Since $K_4$ is abelian, its irreducible representations are the four characters $\widehat{K_4} = \{\chi_\mathrm{id}, \chi_\sim, \chi_-, \chi_{\sim\circ-}\}$, each a homomorphism $K_4 \to \{+1, -1\}$:

| Character | $\chi(\mathrm{id})$ | $\chi(\sim)$ | $\chi(-)$ | $\chi(\sim\circ-)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\chi_\mathrm{id}$ | $+1$ | $+1$ | $+1$ | $+1$ |
| $\chi_\sim$ | $+1$ | $-1$ | $+1$ | $-1$ |
| $\chi_-$ | $+1$ | $+1$ | $-1$ | $-1$ |
| $\chi_{\sim\circ-}$ | $+1$ | $-1$ | $-1$ | $+1$ |

These four characters index the four isotypic components of any $K_4$-equivariant object — the structural origin of the four-sector decomposition of equivariant cohomology.

## Position

$K_4$ is the natural symmetry group of a bilattice with both negation and conflation. It extends the [internal $\mathbb{Z}/2$ gauge](../transformers/negating.md) — the negation-alone action — to the full involutive-automorphism group of the bilattice. Its action extends pointwise to predication fiber algebras and to settlings, where the four isotypic components of the equivariant decomposition correspond to four mathematically distinct sectors of settling content.
