**Learning** is the morphism that reads the [bilattice](../bilattice.md) — the value
structure positing opens, the recurring FOUR carrying two orders and the Klein-four
symmetry — as a structure of **acquiring knowledge**. It does not add data; it
*reads* the four charges as the four orientations a knower can take while acquiring,
and the two orders as the two gradients along which acquisition is organised.

The corpus already named the bilattice "the bilattice of learning" in its own learning
docs (`../explanations/bilattice-learning-readme.md`); this morphism makes that reading
a node. What learning generates is the [diataxis](content/diataxis/diataxis.md)
documentation quartet — the four charges met as tutorial, how-to, explanation, and
reference — argued and formally proven to be the same object as the charges
themselves.

Under learning's reading:

- the **knowledge order** `≤ₖ` becomes the *scaffolding / decontextualisation*
  gradient — the variable cognitive-load theory manipulates (worked-example effect at
  the `⊥ₖ` pole, expertise-reversal driving ascent toward `⊤ₖ`);
- the **truth order** `≤ₜ` becomes the *actionability / definiteness* gradient — from
  the discursive `f` to the definite recipe `t`;
- the **Klein-four** `K₄ = ⟨~, −⟩` becomes the two pedagogical dualities
  *learning↔lookup* (`−`) and *doing↔understanding* (`~`).

A sequel reading, the [learning cycle](content/learning-cycle/learning-cycle.md), is
generated by the bilattice's closure **dynamics** rather than by its static algebra: a
learning episode is iteration of a closure operator, the cycle is a spiral (monotone
ascent, no nontrivial periodic orbits), and the closure ordinal stratifies learning into
four depths at `ω`/`ω₁`. It sits as a sibling of [diataxis](content/diataxis/diataxis.md)
under learning — the dynamic face to diataxis's static one.
