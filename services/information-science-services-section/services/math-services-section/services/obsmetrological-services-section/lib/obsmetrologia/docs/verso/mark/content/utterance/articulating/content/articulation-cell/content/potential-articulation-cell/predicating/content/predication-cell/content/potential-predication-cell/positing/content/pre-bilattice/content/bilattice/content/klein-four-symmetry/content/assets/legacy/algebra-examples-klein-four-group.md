The Klein four-group $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ is the group with four elements: the identity $e$, two independent [involutions](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/involution.md) $a$ and $b$, and their composition $ab = ba$. Every non-identity element has order 2 ($a^2 = b^2 = (ab)^2 = e$). It is the smallest non-cyclic group.

On the four-valued [bilattice](../objects/bilattice.md) $\{\bot, t, f, \top\}$, the Klein four-group is generated by negation $\mathord{\sim}$ (swaps $t \leftrightarrow f$) and conflation $-$ (swaps $\bot \leftrightarrow \top$). The four group elements are: identity, negation, conflation, and their composition (which swaps both pairs simultaneously). This is the symmetry group of the bilattice viewed as a square.

Neither the extending operation $\odot$ nor the restricting operation $\Rightarrow$ of the Busaniche-Cignoli [residuation](../objects/residuated-lattice.md) commutes with negation or conflation. The residuation is asymmetric with respect to the full symmetry group.
