## What this node is

The **potential** predication cell is the seed phase of the
[predication cell](../../predication-cell.md): the oriented triple
$\phi = (s, p, o)$ **present but value-free**. The cell schema forbids a value
field — admitting one would make cell-identity valuation-dependent and collapse
the fibration — so at birth the predication category carries only this potential
cell, the location at which evidence *will* be recorded but is not yet.

The name "potential" is inherited from the
[potential articulation cell](../../../../../potential-articulation-cell.md) the
seed was read off — the told-true corner whose articulated token predicating
consumed. The cell's *value* state at birth is a different coordinate of the
same recurring slot-presence four-point form: value-free means both evidence
slots empty, the **absurd** charge (⊥ₖ, told none), until positing raises it.
Its siblings —
[active](../active-predication-cell/active-predication-cell.md),
[stable](../stable-predication-cell/stable-predication-cell.md),
[absurd](../absurd-predication-cell/absurd-predication-cell.md) — are the
refinements reached as the partition fills. (Corner names follow the evidence
reading, ruled 2026-08-05; see `docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`. The
value-free birth claim above is now derived, valuation-relative: the
knowledge-least [valuation](positing/content/valuation/valuation.md) that
[positing](positing/positing.md) seeds puts every claim's phase at absurd with
no exception (`Valuation.phase_knowledgeLeast`), and a single assertion from
that seed raises the phase to exactly potential
(`Valuation.phase_asserting_knowledgeLeast`) — see
`Language/Predicating/CellPartition.lean`.)

## What descends from it

[**positing**](positing/positing.md) is the morphism out of this seed: it admits
value into the cell as a bilattice **charge**, seeding the knowledge-least
valuation (bottom on every cell) and opening the value structure. Value enters
the whole descent here, from this potential cell.

## Orientation

Read [predication-cell](../../predication-cell.md) for the cell whose seed phase
this is, and [positing](positing/positing.md) for the morphism that admits value.
The morphism that produced the predication category is
[predicating](../../../../predicating.md).
