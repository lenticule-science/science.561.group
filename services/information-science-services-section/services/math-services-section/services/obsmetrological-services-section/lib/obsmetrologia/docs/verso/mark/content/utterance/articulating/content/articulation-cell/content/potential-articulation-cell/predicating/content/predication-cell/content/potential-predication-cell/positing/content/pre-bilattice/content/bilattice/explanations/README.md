# Explanations

Understanding-oriented. *Why* the kernel is shaped the way it is — the mathematics and the code held
together. Read these when the reference tells you *what* a morphism does and you want to know why.

Planned:

- **the-bilattice-and-the-four-states** — `FOUR`, the two bits, absurd/potential/active/stable, and
  why predication needs a bilattice rather than a single lattice. (math: Belnap/Ginsberg/Avron;
  code: the `value` pair and the four charge ops.)
- **measured-not-seeded** — why a cell's state is *read* from its givens (token given → potential)
  and absurd is only ever *reached*, never stamped. (math: fixed points you can't put a cell at;
  code: `posit`, the deletion of the seeds.)
- **the-two-cones-and-the-void** — each order's unit/counit (LFP/GFP) adjoint pair, and the void as
  the slack between them. (math: Knaster–Tarski, adjunctions; code: `settle`/`brim`,
  `adjudicate`/`presume`, `gap`/`vanish`.)
- **evidence-the-carrier** — evidence as the twist-product carrier the two orders order; lossless
  accumulation; why ⊤/stable is reachable. (math: twist product, transverse aggregation; code: the
  `evidence` lists, `_union`, every op.)
- **knowledge-and-truth-are-sigma-dual** — the order-swap σ, the two orders as one structure seen
  twice, the Klein-four/G₈ gauge. (math: two-orders duality; code: `negate`, `conflate`, the
  σ-conjugation of the truth ops.)
- **one-adjunction-three-faces** — `FOUR ≅ Chu(2,0)`, `*`-autonomous: the Galois orders, the modal
  ◇⊣□, and the residual as three views of one adjoint object. (math: Jakl 2025; code: where each
  face shows up.)
- **the-four-regimes** — articulating/predicating/positing/settling as the descent's regimes, and
  what each measures. (math: the ordinal classification; code: the four-stage pipeline.)
