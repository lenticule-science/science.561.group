## Annotation

Introduces the bilattice formulation of multi-valued reasoning for AI: a value space with two compatible orderings (truth and knowledge), unifying treatments of partial, conflicting, and default information. Together with [@belnap1977] this is the AI-side origin of the bilattice apparatus that the program inherits via [@fitting2006] and [@rivieccio2010].
