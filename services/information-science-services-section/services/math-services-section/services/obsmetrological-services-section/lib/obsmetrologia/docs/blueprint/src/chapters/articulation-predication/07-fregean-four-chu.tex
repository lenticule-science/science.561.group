\section{Fregean predication forces FOUR and presents a Chu space}

\begin{proposition}[Independent predication evidence is FOUR]\label{thm:predication-independent-evidence-four}\lean{Obsmetrologia.independentEvidenceReading_exhaustive}\leanok\uses{thm:predication, thm:four-bilattice, thm:valuation}
An oriented subject--predicate--object claim has no intrinsic truth field.  Its
standing is supplied by evidence for the claim and evidence against the claim.
When those two observations are preserved independently, their carrier is
$\mathrm{Bool}\times\mathrm{Bool}$.  The maps
\texttt{independentEvidenceCharge} and
\texttt{independentEvidenceCharge\_slots} are inverse to the charge-coordinate
readout, so the four values are forced rather than chosen: neither, for only,
against only, and both.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The predication evaluation Chu space]\label{thm:predication-chu}\lean{Obsmetrologia.predicationChu}\leanok\uses{thm:predication-independent-evidence-four, thm:valuation}
The canonical evaluation matrix has claims as points, valuations as states, and
$r(\varphi,v)=v(\varphi)$ as its FOUR-valued entry.  This is a Chu space over
FOUR.  Passing from the predication fiber to the Chu presentation neither
changes the claim nor manufactures a new value.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Fregean predication forces FOUR and Chu]\label{thm:fregean-predication-four-chu}\lean{Obsmetrologia.fregean_predication_forces_four_and_chu}\leanok\uses{thm:predication-independent-evidence-four, thm:predication-chu}
For every independently observed positive/negative evidence pair, the
coordinate-to-charge and charge-to-coordinate maps round-trip, and the
predication evaluation Chu matrix reads that same charge at that same claim.
This is the compact formal carrier of the Obsmetrologia result whose statement
had become distributed through the predication, bilattice, fibration, and Chu
developments.  Belnap FOUR, twist products, Fregean predication, and Chu spaces
have separate prior histories; their forced composition in this theorem is the
lab's result.
\end{theorem}
