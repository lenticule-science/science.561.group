# Bigraded cohomology refinements

The standard [settling cohomology](../../../settling-cohomology.md) $H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_U)$ collapses the truth-order and knowledge-order data into a single graded invariant. A finer development resolves them separately into a *bigraded cohomology* $H^{m,n}_{k,t}$ on the [bitopological settling site](../../../../../../bitopological-settling-site/bitopological-settling-site.md), with one grading axis tracking knowledge-order ($\leq_k$) sheaves and the other tracking truth-order ($\leq_t$) sheaves.

The chapter develops:

- The [bigraded settling cohomology](../../bigraded-settling-cohomology/bigraded-settling-cohomology.md) — the bigraded chain complex and its derived functors.

- The [truth–knowledge comparison long exact sequence](../../../references/truth-knowledge-cohomology-long-exact-sequence.md) — the fundamental tool relating the two adjoint systems' cohomologies.

- [Bigraded aperiodic-regime trichotomy](../../../../../../../references/bigraded-aperiodic-regime-trichotomy.md) — a refinement of the aperiodic-regime classification that distinguishes three bigraded patterns within the aperiodic stratum.

- [Bigraded cohomology as a mapping cone](../../bigraded-settling-cohomology/content/assets/legacy/bigraded-cohomology-via-mapping-cone.md) — a presentation of the bigraded cohomology as the mapping cone of the comparison map between $\leq_k$- and $\leq_t$-cohomologies.

The bigraded refinement is structurally finer than the standard cohomology — it resolves invariants that the single-graded cohomology averages out. For settlings with non-trivial paraconsistent content, the bigraded version is what carries the full classifying data.
