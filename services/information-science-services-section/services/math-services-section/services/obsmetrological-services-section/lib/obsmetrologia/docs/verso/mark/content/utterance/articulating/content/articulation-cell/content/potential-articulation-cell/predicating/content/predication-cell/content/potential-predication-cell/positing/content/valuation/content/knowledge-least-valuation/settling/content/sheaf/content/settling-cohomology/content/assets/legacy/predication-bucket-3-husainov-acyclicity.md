# Husainov acyclicity

A condition under which Čech cohomology on an [Alexandrov-topology](../objects/alexandrov-topology.md) poset agrees with the derived-functor sheaf cohomology of the sheafification.

[@husainov2026] (arXiv:2310.05577) gives a necessary-and-sufficient condition phrased in terms of acyclicity of *Dedekind–MacNeille cuts* of the poset: roughly, every Dedekind–MacNeille completion's cut sub-poset must be cohomologically acyclic. For finite posets the conditions are algorithmically computable.

The condition is the natural acyclicity hypothesis for poset settings, and it is what justifies Čech computation as a substitute for derived-functor cohomology on the bitopological site of the [dual predication hyperdoctrine](../../../../../../predication-hyperdoctrine/content/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine.md). When Husainov acyclicity holds — as it does on the bounded sub-sites of the cohomology layer — Čech cochain calculations are not merely informative approximations but compute the right derived-functor answer on the nose.
