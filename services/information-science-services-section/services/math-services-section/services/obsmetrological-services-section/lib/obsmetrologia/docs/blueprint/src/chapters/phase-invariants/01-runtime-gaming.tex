\section{Runtime games as settled certificate projections}

\begin{theorem}[Verification tournament runtime certificate]\label{thm:verification-tournament-runtime-certificate}\lean{Obsmetrologia.GameTheory.VerificationTournament.runtime_tournament_settled_of_reading}\leanok\uses{thm:no-periodic-orbits}
A finite gaming verification tournament carries seven runtime obligations: the Dialectica
object-map, the libp2p/FSym roster, once-only matchmaking, pairwise streamed claim/refutation
plays, plus the finite survivor reading of the settled claim/refutation bracket. Given a returned
monotone closure orbit for the dominance seed, the no-periodic-orbits law turns the orbit into a
fixed point; the survivor reading derives Nash no-deviation, witness survival, and
equilibrium-survivor correspondence; and the operational witnesses assemble with those derived
facts into a settled Nash certificate. The theorem is still certificate-level for transport and
matchmaking, but the survivor correspondence is no longer an opaque runtime proposition.
\end{theorem}
