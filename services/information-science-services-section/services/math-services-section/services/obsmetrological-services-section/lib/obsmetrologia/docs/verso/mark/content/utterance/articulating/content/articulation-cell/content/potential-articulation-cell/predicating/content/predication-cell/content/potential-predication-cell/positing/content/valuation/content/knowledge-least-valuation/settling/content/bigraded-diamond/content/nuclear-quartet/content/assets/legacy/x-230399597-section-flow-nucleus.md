Flow and nucleus acts specialise to section operators

```text
flow_section    : Section ⟶ Section
nucleus_section : Section ⟶ Section
```

with commutation up to earned equivalence by
[`commute-flow-and-nucleus`](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/commute-flow-and-nucleus.md).

- [`flow`](../../math/acts/flow.md) acts on sections to evolve them.
- [`nucleate`](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/nucleate.md) contracts sections to nuclei.

These operators keep sections within the earned sheaf and satisfy the earned
commutation law.

## Definition
- Inputs/Outputs: [Quasicrystalline Hypertensor Sheaf Section](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/quasicrystalline-hypertensor-sheaf-section.md).
- Behaviour: apply flow and nucleus to sections with commutation up to earned
  equivalence: `flow ∘ nucleus ≃ nucleus ∘ flow`.

## Invariants
- Flow–nucleus commutation holds on sections.
- Sections remain within the earned sheaf; no new carriers introduced.

## Proof
- Uses: [Commute Flow and Nucleus](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/commute-flow-and-nucleus.md) to justify the
  commutation law on sections.
- Shows that: lifting flow/nucleus to sections preserves the commutation law
  and keeps sections within the sheaf.
