## What this node is

**Necessitating** is the modal operation $\Box$ of the
[valuation](../valuation.md): it lowers each cell's
[charge](../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/charge/charge.md) to the
**knowledge-meet** of the charges assigned at that cell across the valuation's
fiber-algebras, and appends the result as a new valuation on the trace:

$$
\Box : v \;\longmapsto\; v' , \qquad v'(c) \;=\; \bigwedge\nolimits_{k}\; \{\, v(c) : \text{over } \Phi \,\}.
$$

It reuses the bilattice's meet along the **knowledge order**, the dual of
[possibilizing](../possibilizing/possibilizing.md)'s join.

## What it means

Necessitating is **deflationary** — "what *must* hold." It keeps each cell only
to the floor its fellow assignments across $\Phi$ jointly force. It is the box of
the modal pair $\Diamond \dashv \Box$; the order of the two is what the closure
runs. By the
[two-orders duality](../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/two-orders-duality/two-orders-duality.md)
it has a truth-order variant $\Box_t$ on the same carrier.

## What it serves

The closure [settling](../content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/settling.md)
iterates is $\Diamond \circ \Box$ — possibilize then necessitate — to a fixed
point. Necessitating supplies the lowering half. The two halves agree to a
genuine closure only because their coherence
[commutating](../commutating/commutating.md) is invertible at this regime
($\Diamond\Box = \Box\Diamond$), which is what makes the iterated composite a
well-defined nucleus.

## Orientation

Acts on the [valuation](../valuation.md). Adjoint:
[possibilizing](../possibilizing/possibilizing.md). Coherence:
[commutating](../commutating/commutating.md). Closed to a fixed point by
[settling](../content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/settling.md).
