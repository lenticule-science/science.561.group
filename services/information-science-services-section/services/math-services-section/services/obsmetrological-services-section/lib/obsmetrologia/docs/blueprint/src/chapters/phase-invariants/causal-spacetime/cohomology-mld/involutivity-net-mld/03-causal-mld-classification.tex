\subsubsection{Causal MLD classification}

\begin{theorem}[Causal MLD classification --- the causal $H^1$ is the classifying invariant]\label{thm:causal-mld-classification}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.MLDClassification.causal_mld_classification}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-manifoldlikeness, thm:causal-polarization, thm:mld-refines-cohequiv, thm:aperiodic-mld-classification}
The causal light-cone $H^1$ realizes the abstract MLD classifying invariant
concretely. Same-class cochains differ by a coboundary, this relation is an
equivalence relation, and pullback along factor maps preserves it. The proved
content is the Step-3 invariance/refinement side; the converse completeness
remains the same red frontier as in the abstract aperiodic classification.
\end{theorem}
