\subsection{Light observation}

\begin{theorem}[Light observation reconstructs the causal order]\label{thm:light-observation}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.LightObservation.full_light_observation_reconstructs_causal_order}\leanok\uses{thm:cone-field-geometry}
For the full observer family, past and future light signatures recover the
causal order and separate events. Identical past and future signatures force
mutual reflexive precedence, hence equality by antisymmetry.
\end{theorem}
