\subsection{Analytic and cohomological contextuality frontier}

\begin{theorem}[The CHSH/Tsirelson tier]\label{thm:chsh-tsirelson-tier}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.chsh_tsirelson_tier}\leanok\uses{thm:pr-box-strong-contextual, thm:dervish-kcbs-value}
The value layer of the Alice×Bob cover, on the internal Bishop reals, with every
level scaled by the context count rather than a bare numeral. Classically an
assignment satisfies strictly fewer than \(|C|\) of the PR parities
(\texttt{pr\_parities\_at\_most\_three} --- the CHSH score is at most
\(|C|\cdot\tfrac12 = 2\)); every single context is satisfiable (the PR box
scores \(|C| = 4\)); the quantum value is one correlator \(1/\sqrt2 =
\sqrt2\cdot\tfrac12\) per context, summed over \texttt{prContexts} itself
(\texttt{sumConst}), equal to \(2\sqrt2\) exactly
(\texttt{chshQuantumValue\_eq\_two\_sqrt2}); and the strict tier
\(|C|\cdot\tfrac12 < |C|\cdot\tfrac1{\sqrt2} < |C|\) holds in the
constructive order with explicit witnesses --- `\(1 < \sqrt2\)` with excess
\(1/3\) past tail index 8, `\(\sqrt2 < 2\)` with deficit \(1/2\) and no
tail (\texttt{sqrt\_two\_lower}, \texttt{sqrt\_two\_upper}). Axiom
profile: the internal-reals quotient stack.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The Hardy paradox probability is strictly positive]\label{thm:hardy-value-positive}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.hardyValue_pos}\leanok\uses{thm:chsh-tsirelson-tier, thm:dervish-kcbs-value}
The value layer of the Hardy phenomenon: the maximal paradox probability
\((5\sqrt5 - 11)/2 = (5/2)\cdot(\sqrt5 - 11/5)\) is strictly positive in
the constructive order (\texttt{hardyValue\_pos}), with margin
\(\sqrt5 - 11/5 \ge 1/50\) past tail index \(61\) and integer heart
\(111m \le 50\lfloor\sqrt5 m\rfloor\) (\texttt{sqrt\_five\_margin}).
The event the possibilistic layer proves unextendable
(\cref{thm:hardy-probabilistic-logical-not-strong}) carries strictly positive
weight. Open continuations: \(\mathrm{hardyValue} < 1\), and the golden-field
identity \(\mathrm{hardyValue} = \varphi^{-5} = 1/(5\varphi+3)\) welding it
to \cref{thm:dervish-golden-field} via \(\varphi^5 = 5\varphi + 3\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Fine/CHSH and cohomological contextuality frontier]\label{thm:quantum-contextuality-analytic-frontier}\notready\uses{thm:chsh-tsirelson-tier, thm:pr-support-strong-contextual, thm:hardy-probabilistic-logical-not-strong, thm:magic-square-strong-contextual, thm:leggett-garg-real-separation, thm:pr-cohomological-contextuality}
The support-level and no-signaling finite projections are built.
\ref{thm:pr-cohomological-contextuality} discharges the
Abramsky--Mansfield--Barbosa cohomological obstruction for the \emph{parity}
(XOR-constraint, all-versus-nothing) case, concretely for the PR box, as an
ordinary Čech \(H^1\) of the measurement-context graph with
\((\mathrm{Bool},\mathrm{xor}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\) coefficients; the
underlying \texttt{ParityModel}/\texttt{Coboundary}/cycle-sum machinery is
scenario-generic and not PR-box-specific, but the magic square and GHZ are not
themselves instantiated against it here. An unrestricted \(\mathbb{Z}\)-linear
coboundary criterion over the full space of assignments is \emph{not} an
obstruction: a signed integer combination reproducing the PR box's own
per-context joint distributions exists (the linear system is consistent, rank
9 of 16), which is why the parity/\(\mathbb{Z}/2\) scoping is load-bearing,
not incidental. Still open are Fine's no-global-distribution theorem for the
probabilistic PR model, numerical CHSH/Bell inequalities over the internal
reals, contextual fraction, the general (non-parity, multi-valued-support)
Abramsky--Mansfield--Barbosa relative cohomology of the full
\(\mathbb{Z}\)-linear distribution presheaf, Kochen--Specker finite
hypergraph witnesses, the magic square/GHZ instances of the graph-cycle
obstruction, and the Hilbert/Born/CPTP layer.
\end{theorem}
