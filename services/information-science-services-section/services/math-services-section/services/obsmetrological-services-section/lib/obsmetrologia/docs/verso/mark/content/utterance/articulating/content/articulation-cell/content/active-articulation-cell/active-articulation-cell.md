## What this node is

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **claim
slot carries a witness and the token slot does not** — `(token: no, claim:
yes)`, *told false*. The content-carrying
[claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) has been witnessed while the
identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has not:
*what-is-said* without a *which-one* to anchor it.

## Never articulating's own output

**Active is never a cell
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md) itself produces
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner_ne_active`). Every articulation
witnesses its own token first — the definition of
`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.Cell.articulate` leaves no other order to choose
from — so a claim witnessed with no prior token is not a *reading* of the
cell partition that articulating could have taken the other way; it is a
position articulating structurally cannot reach. Active is reached only
downstream, by the value algebra: negation (which swaps the token and claim
witnesses of a charge) or converse, applied after articulating has already
run. This is exactly why
[`Language.Positing.ArticulationMeasure`](../potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/positing.md)'s
worked example of the active corner builds it **by fiat** — a bare cell with
an empty token slot and a supplied claim-side witness — and documents that
construction as a value-algebra move, not an articulation.

This supersedes the earlier "token-before-claim working theory," under which
active was merely the reading that a *reversed* convention would put on the
natural path instead of potential. There is no longer a live reversed
alternative: token-before-claim is what the one articulating operation does,
not a choice among readings, so active is off the path unconditionally.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material used this name for
the (yes, no) told-true corner and is preserved unrewritten in archives.
Material from the corpus's aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in
[`_archive/`](_archive).

## Earlier material

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **token side
is articulated and the claim side is not** — `(token: yes, claim: no)`. The
identity-carrying [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has been
committed; the content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md)
remains open.

Under the token-before-claim working theory, active is the **first step out of
the [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
cell**: articulating naturally proceeds by articulating the token side first, so
potential → active is the opening move of the natural progression, which
continues active → [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md)
when the claim side is articulated too. Active is "on the path" — the *which-one*
is fixed while *what-is-said* is still to come. (Whether token-before-claim is the
right ordering is open work; under the reversed reading this corner's role and
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md)'s would swap.)

Read [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
for the seed it steps from, [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md)
for where it continues, and the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole partition. The
morphism that generated the cell is [articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
