## What this node is

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **object is
filled and the subject is not**: something the relation is toward, with no subject
standing in it.

## Never articulating's own output

Active is never a cell [articulating](../../../../articulating.md) produces
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner_ne_active`). Every articulation fills its
subject with its own [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) first, so an
object without a subject is a position articulating cannot reach; it is reached
only downstream of articulating.

Articulation reads nothing as true, false or both; value enters only at positing, where the four corners are read as values.

## Orientation

Read [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
for the corner articulating reaches first and the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole partition.
