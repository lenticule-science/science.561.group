## What this node is

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **claim side
is articulated and the token side is not** — `(token: no, claim: yes)`, *told
false*. The content-carrying [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md)
has been committed while the identity-carrying
[token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has not: *what-is-said* without
a *which-one* to anchor it.

## Off the natural path

Active is the corner that the **token-before-claim ordering puts off the
natural progression**. If articulating proceeds by articulating the token side
first, then [absurd](../absurd-articulation-cell/absurd-articulation-cell.md) →
[potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md) →
[stable](../stable-articulation-cell/stable-articulation-cell.md) is the
on-path sequence, and active — a claim articulated *before* its expected prior
token — is reached only off that path. This is exactly why the ordering of the
[utterance](../../../../../contents/utterance/utterance.md) pair is
load-bearing: which primitive is "first" decides which corner is the off-path
one. Under the reversed claim-before-token reading, active and potential
exchange roles; which reading is right is open work.

This labeling is the authoritative convention ruled 2026-08-05
(`docs/history/corner-naming-convention.md`); older material used this name for
the (yes, no) told-true corner and is preserved unrewritten in archives.
Material from the corpus's aperiodic-regime development of this cell is held in
[`_archive/`](_archive).

## Orientation

Read [potential](../potential-articulation-cell/potential-articulation-cell.md)
for the on-path counterpart it mirrors, the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) for the whole partition, and
[utterance](../../../../../contents/utterance/utterance.md) for why the pair
ordering decides on-path versus off. The morphism that generated the cell is
[articulating](../../../../articulating.md).
