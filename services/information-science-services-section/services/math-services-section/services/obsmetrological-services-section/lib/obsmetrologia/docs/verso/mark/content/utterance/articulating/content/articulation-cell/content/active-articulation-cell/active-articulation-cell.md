## What this node is

The **active** articulation cell is the corner of the
[articulation-cell](../../articulation-cell.md) partition where the **claim slot
holds a witness and the token slot does not**: `(token: not articulated, claim:
articulated)`. The [claim](../../../../../contents/claim/claim.md) has been
witnessed while the [token](../../../../../contents/token/token.md) has not: what
is said, without a which-one to anchor it.

## Never articulating's own output

Active is never a cell [articulating](../../../../articulating.md) produces
(`Obsmetrologia.Articulating.cell_corner_ne_active`). Every articulation places its
own token first, so a claim witnessed without a token is a position articulating
cannot reach; it is reached only downstream of articulating.

Articulation reads nothing as true, false or both; polarity and evidence enter only at positing, where the four corners are read as values.
