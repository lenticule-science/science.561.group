\subsection{Common-base construction}

\begin{theorem}[Common-base MLD classification (the converse, proved)]\label{thm:common-base-mld-classification}\lean{Obsmetrologia.AperiodicOrder.Tiling.CommonBase.common_base_classification}\leanok\uses{thm:aperiodic-mld-classification-reduction}
On a common base, a settling is carried by its firing-ordinal pattern.  A mutual
local derivation is an explicit firing-ordinal bijection, and
\(\mathrm{cl\text{-}inv}\) is the relabeling-invariant stage-count multiset.
Equal stage-counts construct the permutation, so \texttt{BaseMLD} coincides with
cohomological equivalence on the shared carrier.  Cross-base transport is left
to \cref{thm:coalgebraic-crossbase-mld}.
\end{theorem}
