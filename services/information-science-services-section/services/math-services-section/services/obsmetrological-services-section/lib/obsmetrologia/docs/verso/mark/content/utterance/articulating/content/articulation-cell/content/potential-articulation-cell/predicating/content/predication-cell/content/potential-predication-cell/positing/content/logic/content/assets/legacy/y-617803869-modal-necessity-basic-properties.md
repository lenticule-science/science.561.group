# Modal necessity basic properties

The [modal necessity operator](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-necessity-operator.md) $\Box_R$ satisfies:

1. $\Box_R \mathbf{t} = \mathbf{t}$, where $\mathbf{t}$ denotes the constant-$\mathbf{t}$ valuation.
2. $\Box_R(u \wedge_t u') = \Box_R u \wedge_t \Box_R u'$.
3. $\Box_R(c \to u) = c \to \Box_R u$ for constants $c \in \{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$, where $\to$ denotes the strong implication on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ (the [twist-product right residual](../morphisms/twist-product-right-residual.md) $\Rightarrow$ per-cell).

## Proof

Pointwise consequences of the corresponding identities on $\mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ established by Rivieccio, Jung, and Jansana (2017, Proposition 3.2). $\square$
