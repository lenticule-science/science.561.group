## What this node is

The category generated by [commutating](../../commutating.md). Its **objects**
are commutative nuclear squares $(A, \sigma, \Delta)$ — a settled fiber Heyting
algebra with the past nucleus and the future nucleus, subject to
$\sigma\Delta = \Delta\sigma$ — and its **morphisms** are Heyting homomorphisms
preserving both nuclei.

Each object carries the four canonical sub-algebras

$$
\bigl(\,A,\;\; \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma),\;\; \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta),\;\; A^*\,\bigr),
\qquad A^* = \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma) \cap \mathrm{Fix}(\Delta),
$$

— free carrier, the two singly-closed layers, and the doubly-quiescent joint
fixed point — an exhaustive commuting square of inclusions. This is the **KZ
diamond** at one grade of the aperiodic orbit.

## Why this lives in the aperiodic regime

For strict and Scott-continuous settlings the orbit stabilizes at a countable
ordinal and the quartet degenerates (the joint fixed point is reached and stays).
In the [aperiodic settling](../../../aperiodic-settling.md) regime the orbit is
strictly $\leq_k$-ascending and does **not** stabilize below $\omega_1$, so a
fresh quartet appears at each grade and the diamonds **stack** — carrying the
2-cell content whose direct limit is the
[aperiodic classifying invariant](../../../content/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md).
