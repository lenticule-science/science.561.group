**Void** is the named instance of [knowledge-bottom](../../knowledge-bottom.md). For each [bilattice](../../../../../../../../../../object/library/thing/library/category/library/variety/library/bilattice/bilattice.md) $\mathbf{B}$, the knowledge-bottom $\bot \in |\mathbf{B}|$ — the bottom of the [knowledge-order](../../../../knowledge-order.md) $\leq_k$ — carries the system-wide name *void*. The pair $\langle \mathbf{B}, \bot \rangle$ is at once a [bilattice-value](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/flatfile-agentic-resource-system/bilattice/bilattice-value.md), a [knowledge-bottom](../../knowledge-bottom.md), and (by this naming) a *void*.

A [predication-cell](../../../../../../../../../../predication-cell-set/library/predication-cell/predication-cell.md) $\phi = (s, p, o)$ has *void value* under a valuation $v$ when

$$v(p)(s, o) = \bot \in A_p$$

at the [predication-fiber-algebra](../../../../../../../../../../predication-fibration/library/predication-fiber-algebra/predication-fiber-algebra.md) $A_p$. The cell exists as a syntactic triple in the predicate category; its valuation deposits no evidence supporting or refuting the cell's assertion.

## Role as admissible evidence-bottom

Every fiber-algebra value $w \in A_p$ satisfies $\bot \leq_k w$ by definition of the knowledge-order bound. Equivalently, every cell's valuation is at least void; evidence accretion is movement in $\leq_k$ from $\bot$ toward the classical values $\mathbf{t}$ (told true) or $\mathbf{f}$ (told false), or toward the held-contradiction $\top$ (told both).

Void is not a deficiency to be eliminated. It is the floor of every cell's evidence-trajectory — the value any cell may take when no evidence has accreted in its fiber. The closure-operator dynamics on the predication-settling treat $\bot$ as the legitimate starting state for any cell.

## Why a named-instance of knowledge-bottom

Void's existence-conditions are entirely contained in knowledge-bottom's: there is a void exactly when there is a bilattice with a knowledge-bottom, and every bilattice has one (the knowledge-order is bounded). The specialization chain that places void at its lawful position records exactly this dependency:

$$\text{void} \to \text{knowledge-bottom} \to \text{knowledge-order} \to \text{bilattice-value}$$

Each link is a [specializing](../../../../../../../../../../object/specializing/specializing.md) inclusion. Void is not a free primitive; it is the *named instance* of knowledge-bottom — the value-name the predication system uses to refer to a knowledge-bottom in narration.

## Distinction from relationlessness

[Relationlessness](../../../../../../../../../../relationlessness/relationlessness.md) is a cell — the originating predication cell $\phi^*$ of the predicate-category. Void is a value — the no-evidence element $\bot$ of the bilattice fiber algebra. The two live at distinct strata:

| | What it names | Stratum |
|---|---|---|
| relationlessness | the originating predication cell $\phi^*$ | cell-level (an element of $\Phi$) |
| void | the no-evidence value $\bot \in A_p$ | value-level (an element of $A_p$) |

A configuration in which the originating cell exists but no further evidence has been deposited at any fiber is the all-void state — the valuation that assigns $\bot$ to every cell other than $\phi^*$. It is *not* relationlessness; relationlessness IS the originating cell, value already above $\bot$ at $p^*$ (supplied by [articulating](../../../../../../../../../../relationlessness/articulating/articulating.md)).

## Position

The cell-internal counterpart to the originating theorem. With [relationlessness](../../../../../../../../../../relationlessness/relationlessness.md), void grounds the predication-cell-with-bilattice-value as a primitive of predication category theory: cells relate (by the originating theorem), their values include the admissible no-evidence $\bot$ (by void's identification as the named knowledge-bottom).
