## Annotation

The founding paper of categorical logic. Introduces **hyperdoctrines** as indexed cartesian-closed categories with adjoint quantifiers along every base morphism. The four-part hyperdoctrine signature:

1. A cartesian closed category $T$ of "types".
2. For each $X \in T$, a cartesian closed category $P(X)$ of "attributes of type $X$".
3. For each $f: X \to Y$ in $T$, a substitution functor $f \cdot (-): P(Y) \to P(X)$.
4. For each $f$, left and right adjoints $\Sigma_f \dashv f \cdot (-) \dashv \Pi_f$ giving existential and universal quantification.

**Equality** as the diagonal-image construction $\Delta_X = 1_X \Sigma_{\langle X, X \rangle}$ appears at p. 12 of the TAC reprint. This is what the hyperdoctrinal layer generalizes to derive both classical $\mathbf{t}/\mathbf{f}$-valued equality and paraconsistent $\top/\bot$-valued equality from the predication hyperdoctrine's two adjoint systems.

Cited in the hyperdoctrinal layer for the hyperdoctrine signature and for the equality-as-diagonal construction.
