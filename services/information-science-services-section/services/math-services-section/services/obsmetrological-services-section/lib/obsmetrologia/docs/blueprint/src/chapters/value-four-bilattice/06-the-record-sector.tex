\section{The record sector}

Under the charge sits the record: a pair of evidence tallies rather than a
pair of flags. The carrier is the twist product of the unbounded lattice
$(\mathbb{N}, \min, \max)$ with itself, and the charge is its positivity
shadow. This section records the carrier, the theorems that make the shadow's
lossiness a property rather than a choice, and the escape from the
strict-finite regime.

\begin{proposition}[The record carrier]\label{thm:record-carrier}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record}\leanok\uses{thm:four-bilattice, thm:interlaced-bilattice}
A \texttt{Record} is a pair of tallies (told-true count, told-false count),
with knowledge order both-tallies-up and truth order positive-up/negative-down,
componentwise $\max$/$\min$ lattice operations for each, the empty record as
knowledge bottom, and the merge \texttt{add} by pointwise addition ---
carrying multiplicity, unlike the idempotent joins.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[The record laws are interlaced but unbounded]\label{thm:record-interlaced}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.record_interlaced}\leanok\uses{thm:record-carrier}
Both orders are lattices, the knowledge bottom is the empty record, and each
operation is monotone in the other order --- every field shape of
\ref{thm:interlaced-bilattice} that does not mention a missing bound holds on
records (\texttt{record\_interlaced}). But \texttt{no\_kTop},
\texttt{no\_tTop}, \texttt{no\_tBot} (each axiom-free): no record absorbs the
knowledge join, and the truth order has neither top nor bottom. The carrier
fails the boundedness hypothesis of \ref{thm:four-initial}, which therefore
does not apply to it: with \texttt{InterlacedBilattice.joinK\_kTop}, held
$\top$ is what a value carrier buys by demanding a bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The record carrier escapes the strict-finite regime]\label{thm:record-escapes-strict-finite}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.record_escapes_strict_finite}\leanok\uses{thm:record-carrier, thm:settling}
The tally increment \texttt{succPos} is knowledge-inflationary and monotone,
yet no iterate is a settling stage: \texttt{iter succPos (k+1) r $\neq$ iter
succPos k r} for every $k$ and seed (axiom-free).
\texttt{Reading.settles\_by\_three} fails on records: the $\kappa \le 3$
strict-finite collapse is a boundedness artifact of the four-element carrier,
and the $\kappa = \omega$ regime is inhabited by a derived carrier rather than
by asserted regime data. The counterpoint \texttt{joinClosure\_one\_step}:
every join-bounded closure on records still settles in one step, so
unboundedness of the carrier, not failure of the closure laws, is what admits
the escaping dynamic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The charge is the record's shadow]\label{thm:record-charge-shadow}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.reading_add_eq_reading_joinK}\leanok\uses{thm:record-carrier, thm:four-bilattice}
The readout \texttt{charge} (tally positivity) is a homomorphism for all four
lattice operations (\texttt{charge\_joinK}/\texttt{meetK}/\texttt{joinT}/%
\texttt{meetT}), surjective and not injective, and it cannot distinguish the
counting merge from the idempotent join
(\texttt{charge\_add\_eq\_charge\_joinK}): multiplicity is exactly what the
shadow forgets. Contradiction mass is the matched-pair count
\texttt{topMass}; a record reads out as held $\top$ iff its mass is positive
(\texttt{charge\_eq\_kTop\_iff}), and the counting merge is superadditive in
mass (\texttt{topMass\_add}) where the idempotent charge join collapses it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The collapse is forced]\label{thm:record-collapse-forced}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.bottom_reflecting_hom_eq_reading}\leanok\uses{thm:record-charge-shadow}
Any map from records to charges that preserves the four lattice operations,
sends the empty record to $\bot_k$, and sends only the empty record to
$\bot_k$ is the charge readout, pointwise
(\texttt{bottom\_reflecting\_hom\_eq\_charge}). The Belnap flags are the only
bottom-faithful four-valued reading of the tallies: the lossiness measured by
\ref{thm:record-charge-shadow} is a property of reading records four-valuedly
at all, not a defect of the particular readout.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The record-coefficient exchange rate is the ground-state energy]\label{thm:record-merge-ground-state}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.record_merge_ground_state}\leanok\uses{thm:record-charge-shadow, thm:top-h1-exchange}
The measurement-scenario merge rebuilt over record coefficients: the
counting merge \texttt{mergeTally} tallies one tick per context-incidence,
and its total contradiction mass over a complete duplicate-free measurement
list prices the obstruction with multiplicity. For any pair-context parity
model, the minimum over realizing families of the merged mass equals the
minimum over reference sections of the frustrated-context count --- the
ground-state energy of the target's coset. Below: every realizing family's
mass dominates the energy of its majority readout (each frustrated context
has a dissenting endpoint incidence, distinct contexts are distinct
incidences, and dissent from the majority is the smaller tally). Above: the
pay family of any reference attains its energy (each frustrated context
pays one tick at its picked endpoint). There is no vertex-cover correction:
unlike the idempotent norm of \ref{thm:top-h1-exchange-rate}, contexts
sharing a paying measurement still pay separately, because the counting
merge carries multiplicity (\texttt{topMass\_add}).
Axiom profile: \texttt{propext}, inherited from the generic-carrier
membership decisions in \texttt{mergeTally}/\texttt{localRecord}
(\texttt{Decidable} for \texttt{List} membership over an arbitrary
\texttt{DecidableEq} carrier is core-Lean-tainted at that generality).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Record mass and cycle rank are distinct invariants]\label{thm:record-mass-vs-rank}\lean{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.theta_record_mass_below_rank}\leanok\uses{thm:record-merge-ground-state, thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}
The instance comparisons of the ground-state energy against the cycle rank.
On the figure-eight the counting merge restores the exchange rate the
idempotent merge undercounts in \ref{thm:fig8-merge-top-below-rank}: the
mass minimum is two, one per independent odd cycle
(\texttt{fig8\_record\_mass\_attains\_rank}), where the idempotent
$\top$-support minimum is one. On a theta graph whose two independent odd
cycles share their one odd edge, a single frustrated context services both
topological debts: the mass minimum is one, strictly below the cycle rank
two (\texttt{theta\_record\_mass\_below\_rank}). Both lower bounds are one
lemma applied per odd cycle: a triangle with an odd target always carries a
frustrated edge (\texttt{odd\_triangle\_frustrated}).
Axiom profile: \texttt{propext}, inherited from
\ref{thm:record-merge-ground-state}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Record-coefficient settling over the hull]\label{thm:record-settling-h1}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Odometer.hull_settling_gluing_dichotomy}\leanok\uses{thm:tower-gluing-dichotomy, thm:record-escapes-strict-finite}
The settling sheaf over the dyadic hull's cylinder covers, valued in a
pre-value carrier (\texttt{CylinderDatum}): one value per depth-$n$
cylinder, knowledge-monotone along refinement --- nested cylinders are the
cover system's only overlaps, so monotonicity along refinement is the
agreement condition. A global section is a value at each hull point
dominating every cylinder value its thread passes through. With charge
coefficients every datum glues ($\top$ absorbs,
\texttt{charge\_cylinder\_datum\_glues}); with record coefficients the
depth-tally datum glues to none
(\texttt{record\_cylinder\_datum\_not\_glues}: any candidate value at the
all-minimal thread is outrun by the depth). The equality-agreement \v{C}ech
$H^1$ vanishes for every datum (\texttt{Cohomology.h1\_vanishes}), so this
obstruction is invisible to it: it lives in the order-valued reading of
the cover and opens exactly at the topless coefficient carrier --- the
coefficient carrier gates the settling sheaf over an actual point-set
cover. Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The completed record]\label{thm:completed-record}\lean{Obsmetrologia.ORecord.oRecordInterlaced}\leanok\uses{thm:record-interlaced, thm:interlaced-bilattice}
Adjoining the point at infinity $\omega$ to each tally axis restores every
missing bound: the completed record is a bounded interlaced bilattice
(\texttt{oRecordInterlaced}), with knowledge top the double infinity
$(\omega, \omega)$ --- told both, unboundedly --- truth top $(\omega, 0)$,
and truth bottom $(0, \omega)$. Held $\top$ is literally the
compactification point of the evidence quadrant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[FOUR embeds in the completed record]\label{thm:four-embeds-completed-record}\lean{Obsmetrologia.ORecord.four_embeds}\leanok\uses{thm:four-initial, thm:completed-record}
By initiality the unique bounds map carries FOUR into the completed record;
it is injective (the four bounds are pairwise distinct) and sends $\top_k$ to
the double infinity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Settling at stage $\omega$]\label{thm:settles-at-omega}\lean{Obsmetrologia.ORecord.settles_at_omega}\leanok\uses{thm:completed-record, thm:record-escapes-strict-finite}
On the completed record the extended tally increment ($\omega + 1 = \omega$)
is inflationary, reaches no fixed point at any finite stage, and the
stage-$\omega$ value $(\omega, 0)$ is its fixed point and the least upper
bound of the orbit: the closure ordinal is exactly $\omega$ --- the
transfinite-bounded regime, inhabited by a derived carrier. The regime tower
so far: FOUR settles by three (the bounded quotient), the bare record never
settles (the topless carrier), the completed record settles at $\omega$ (the
bound restored at infinity). Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The gluing dichotomy over the dyadic tower]\label{thm:tower-gluing-dichotomy}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Odometer.tower_gluing_dichotomy}\leanok\uses{thm:record-escapes-strict-finite, thm:dyadic-tower}
Over the dyadic tower's stages, every FOUR-valued accumulation admits a
global bound ($\top$ absorbs, so the merge always glues), while some
knowledge-monotone record-valued accumulation admits no global section:
with bounded coefficients every cover settles, with topless coefficients
gluing genuinely fails. The first derived instance of the
cohomology-vanishing regime gate distinguishing carriers. Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The additive coboundary cannot close over Record coefficients]\label{thm:record-additive-coboundary-fails}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.deltaAdd_eq_kBot_iff, Obsmetrologia.Record.deltaAdd_ne_kBot_of_ne}\leanok\uses{thm:record-carrier, thm:record-interlaced}
The naive port of the classical alternating-sum coboundary to \texttt{Record} coefficients,
replacing subtraction with \texttt{add} (all the carrier offers, per \texttt{no\_kTop},
\texttt{no\_tTop}, \texttt{no\_tBot}): a triangle's composite \(\delta^1\delta^0 f\) sums every
value it reads twice, with no cancellation available, so it equals \texttt{kBot} exactly when
\(f\) vanishes at all three vertices (\texttt{deltaAdd\_eq\_kBot\_iff}) and is otherwise always
nonzero (\texttt{deltaAdd\_ne\_kBot\_of\_ne}). This is not a missing proof of
\(\delta\circ\delta=\texttt{kBot}\); it is a proof that the naive additive construction never
satisfies it on nontrivial input, for any choice of which faces to sum --- the same obstruction as
the missing bounds, sharpened to the cochain level. A genuine Record-coefficient coboundary
theory, if one exists, must therefore be order-valued, matching
\texttt{Closure.Odometer.CylinderDatum.GluesTo}'s \(\le_k\)-gluing rather than an
\(=\)-coboundary triviality condition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Monoid-coefficient cochains on the record carrier]\label{thm:record-monoid-cochain-complex}\notready\uses{thm:record-carrier, thm:record-interlaced, thm:record-additive-coboundary-fails}
An order-valued coboundary theory over \texttt{Record} coefficients that does not pass through
the group completion $\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}$, ruled out in equational form by
\cref{thm:record-additive-coboundary-fails}: a cochain complex built from the
\texttt{PreValueCategory} structure directly on the twist product, with cocycle/coboundary
conditions stated via \(\le_k\) rather than \(=\), generalizing the degree-one gluing pattern
\texttt{CylinderDatum} already uses along cover refinement (\cref{thm:record-settling-h1}) to a
genuine Čech nerve with pairwise and triple overlaps. No such complex is built past degree one;
\cref{thm:record-settling-h1} is the one instance in hand of a record-valued gluing failure,
stated as a sheaf section problem rather than a cochain boundary.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[An irrevocability class]\label{thm:irrevocability-class}\notready\uses{thm:record-monoid-cochain-complex, thm:record-settling-h1, thm:record-charge-shadow}
A class $[c]$ of \cref{thm:record-monoid-cochain-complex}, nonzero over
\texttt{Record} coefficients, whose image under group completion to
$\mathbb{Z}$ is a coboundary --- an obstruction that exists only because the
record carrier admits no subtraction (\texttt{no\_kTop}, \texttt{no\_tTop},
\texttt{no\_tBot}, \cref{thm:record-interlaced}). The degree-one instance is
already in hand under a different name: \texttt{record\_cylinder\_datum\_not\_glues}
of \cref{thm:record-settling-h1} fails to glue exactly where the
charge-valued (group-completed, bounded) version of the same datum glues
without obstruction. What is missing is the degree-two case and the general
statement that group completion of coefficients discharges exactly the
classes priced by the superadditivity of \texttt{topMass}
(\texttt{topMass\_add}) --- the counting merge's mass is not preserved by
any map that allows cancellation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The germ order is not antisymmetric]\label{thm:germ-order-not-antisymm}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.germ_order_not_antisymm}\leanok\uses{thm:record-carrier, thm:lax-slack-is-antisymmetry}
Record sequences under bounded-difference domination (\texttt{DomBy}, a
preorder: reflexive and transitive) admit mutual domination without
identity --- the tally climb and its one-step lead dominate each other and
differ. Antisymmetry fails on a derived carrier: exactly the
order-deficiency that \ref{thm:lax-slack-is-antisymmetry} shows the
aperiodic lax-slack regime requires. Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Lax slack on the germ order]\label{thm:germ-lax-slack}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.tally_lax_slack}\leanok\uses{thm:germ-order-not-antisymm}
The germ carrier is a pre-value category (\texttt{germPreValue}) and the
tally bump --- one more told-true at every stage --- is a lax closure on it
(\texttt{tallyLaxClosure}): inflationary, monotone, and Kock--Zöberlein,
with the KZ inequality holding at witness constant one. At the seed its
double differs from its single (\texttt{tally\_lax\_slack}): the first
inhabited \texttt{HasLaxSlack} in the tree --- the non-identity
$\mu$ 2-cell that \ref{thm:lax-slack-is-antisymmetry} proves cannot exist on
any antisymmetric carrier. The closure 2-monad's multiplication carries
content for the first time. Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The regime tower]\label{thm:regime-tower}\lean{Obsmetrologia.regime_tower}\leanok\uses{thm:settles-at-omega, thm:record-escapes-strict-finite, thm:germ-lax-slack}
One derived family of value carriers inhabits the regime column: FOUR
settles by stage three (strict-finite, the bounded quotient); the topless
record admits an inflationary monotone dynamic with no finite settling
stage (the $\omega$-escape); the completed record settles exactly at stage
$\omega$ (transfinite-bounded, the bound restored at infinity); and the
germ preorder carries genuine lax-idempotence slack (the aperiodic
signature, possible only where antisymmetry fails). Each regime is a
deficiency of the carrier's bound structure, exhibited by derivation.
Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The $\top$-flux is order-quiescent and never settles]\label{thm:top-bump-flux}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Record.topBump_flux}\leanok\uses{thm:germ-lax-slack, thm:record-charge-shadow}
The contradiction bump --- one more told-true and one more told-false at
every stage --- is a lax closure on the germ carrier with slack at the seed
(\texttt{topBumpLaxClosure}, \texttt{topBump\_lax\_slack}). Its orbit: every
iterate is germ-equivalent to the seed, so the whole orbit is a single
point of the germ order; consecutive iterates are never equal; and after
$k$ bumps the contradiction mass at every stage is at least $k$
(\texttt{topBump\_flux}). At the aperiodic rung the settling question
splits: the order settles instantly, the data never does, and the gap is
an unbounded held-$\top$ flux inside one germ --- the stable held-$\top$
dynamics the corpus places at $\kappa \ge \omega_1$. Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The $\omega$-escape profile]\label{thm:omega-escape-profile}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Odometer.omega_escape_profile}\leanok\uses{thm:regime-tower, thm:tower-gluing-dichotomy}
For each tower carrier, whether an $\mathbb{N}$-indexed stage accumulation
escapes every global bound: charges never ($\top$ absorbs); records do (the
tally climb); completed records never (the double infinity absorbs); and
germs never --- every $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family of germs is dominated by
its diagonal running maximum (\texttt{germ\_omega\_family\_bounded}) ---
although the germ carrier has no top (\texttt{germ\_no\_top}). The
$\mathbb{N}$-indexed escape is alive exactly at the topless $\omega$-rung.
This derived invariant does not reproduce the stipulated
\texttt{cohomologyVanishes} gate (which vanishes at strict and Scott and is
alive at transfinite-bounded and aperiodic): the escape invariant is alive
at the Scott rung and quiescent at the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic
rungs. At the aperiodic rung the obstruction exists (no top) but no
$\mathbb{N}$-indexed accumulation witnesses it: the escape requires
cofinality beyond $\omega$ --- the $\kappa \ge \omega_1$ signature, derived.
Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The regime gate is the unattained-settling gate]\label{thm:aperiodic-top-flux}\lean{Obsmetrologia.unattained_settling_gate}\leanok\uses{thm:regime-tower, thm:top-bump-flux, thm:omega-escape-profile}
The invariant the stipulated \texttt{cohomologyVanishes} table gates,
derived on the tower: a settled value that no finite stage attains. On FOUR
every inflationary step attains its settled value at stage three, and on
the record the tally increment fixes nothing, so no unattained settled
value exists --- the gate reads \texttt{true}. On the completed record the
stage-$\omega$ value is fixed and no finite stage reaches it, and on the
germ carrier the contradiction bump's orbit is a single point of the germ
order while no stage equals the next --- the gate reads \texttt{false}.
The pattern matches \texttt{cohomologyVanishes} at all four rungs, where
the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed escape invariant of
\ref{thm:omega-escape-profile} does not: the gate prices the settled
value's transcendence of its stages, not the boundedness of accumulations.
Axiom-free.
\end{theorem}
