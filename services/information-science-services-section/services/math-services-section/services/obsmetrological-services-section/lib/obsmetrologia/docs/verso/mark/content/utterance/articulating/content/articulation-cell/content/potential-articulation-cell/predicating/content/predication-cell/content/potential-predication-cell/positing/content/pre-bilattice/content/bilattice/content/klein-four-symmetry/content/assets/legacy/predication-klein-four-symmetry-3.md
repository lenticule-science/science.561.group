# Klein-four symmetry

The Klein-four group `K_4 ≅ Z/2 × Z/2` acting on a bilattice equipped with both negation `~` and conflation `−`:

$$K_4 \;:=\; \langle\, \sim,\, -\, \rangle \;=\; \{\, \mathrm{id},\, \sim,\, -,\, \sim\!\circ\!-\, \}.$$

Both generators are involutions (`~~ = id`, `−− = id`); they commute (`~ ∘ − = − ∘ ~`, a bilattice axiom when both are present). The group is therefore Abelian of order 4 with every non-identity element of order 2 — the defining structure of `Z/2 × Z/2`.

## Action of each element

| Element | Action on `≤_k` | Action on `≤_t` |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | preserves | preserves |
| `~` | preserves (monotone) | reverses (antitone) |
| `−` | reverses (antitone) | preserves (monotone) |
| `~ ∘ −` | reverses | reverses |

The four elements realize the four possible combinations of `(preserve, reverse)` on the two orders. The two generators correspond to the two flips: `~` flips the truth axis (negation), `−` flips the knowledge axis (conflation), and their composition `~ ∘ −` flips both.

## Action on FOUR

For `B = FOUR`:

| Element | `⊥` | `t` | `f` | `⊤` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | `⊥` | `t` | `f` | `⊤` |
| `~` | `⊥` | `f` | `t` | `⊤` |
| `−` | `⊤` | `t` | `f` | `⊥` |
| `~ ∘ −` | `⊤` | `f` | `t` | `⊥` |

Geometrically: `~` and `−` are reflections across the two diagonals of the `2×2` arrangement `[[⊥,t],[f,⊤]]`. Their composition is a 180° rotation. `id` is the identity.

## Orbit decomposition

The full Klein-four group acts **transitively** on FOUR: starting from any value, applying the four group elements reaches every value. So `K_4` has one orbit on FOUR — the whole carrier `{⊥, t, f, ⊤}`.

Sub-orbit structure under sub-groups:

| Subgroup | Orbits on FOUR |
|---|---|
| `⟨id⟩` | `{⊥}, {t}, {f}, {⊤}` (4 singletons) |
| `⟨~⟩` | `{⊥}, {⊤}, {t, f}` (3 orbits — truth-axis flip) |
| `⟨−⟩` | `{t}, {f}, {⊥, ⊤}` (3 orbits — knowledge-axis flip) |
| `⟨~ ∘ −⟩` | `{t, f}, {⊥, ⊤}` (2 orbits — diagonal flip) |
| Full `K_4` | `{⊥, t, f, ⊤}` (1 orbit — transitive) |

The lattice of subgroups corresponds to the lattice of orbit decompositions. This is the formal structure of the bilattice's internal symmetry.

## Z/2 × Z/2 structure

The Klein-four group is `Z/2 × Z/2` — two independent copies of `Z/2`. The two factors are:

- **Negation-Z/2**: `⟨~⟩ = {id, ~}` — the internal-z2-gauge.
- **Conflation-Z/2**: `⟨−⟩ = {id, −}`.

Each factor is one of the two unary involutions. Their product (in the group-theoretic sense) is the Klein-four group.

The Z/2 × Z/2 structure is the formal sense in which the bilattice carries a TWO-DIMENSIONAL gauge symmetry — one Z/2 per order. The truth and knowledge orders each get their own flip generator; the bilattice's two-order structure produces the Klein-four group, not just a single Z/2.

## When the bilattice carries only one generator

A bilattice always carries negation (it's part of the bilattice axioms once we move beyond pre-bilattice). It optionally carries conflation. When ONLY negation is present:

- The symmetry group is `⟨~⟩ = Z/2` — the internal-z2-gauge alone.
- No Klein-four structure — just the negation flip.

When BOTH negation and conflation are present and commute:

- The symmetry group is `K_4 = Z/2 × Z/2`.
- This $thing applies.

When negation and conflation are present but DON'T commute (rare; not in substrate practice):

- The symmetry group is the dihedral group of order 8 or larger.
- Klein-four structure FAILS; the bilattice has a more complex symmetry.

The substrate's bilattices satisfy the commutation axiom, so Klein-four is the right symmetry when conflation is included.
