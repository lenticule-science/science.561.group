\subsubsection{The cohomological MLD groupoid}

\begin{theorem}[The geometric-MLD coboundary groupoid, in every degree]\label{thm:cohomological-mld-groupoid}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.Cohomology.MLD.cohomological_mld_groupoid}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-cohomology-n, thm:causal-mld-classification}
In the full graded causal complex, the relation ``differ by a coboundary'' is a
groupoid of cohomology classes in every degree: identity is the zero
coboundary, inverse is negation, and composition is addition. This is the
cohomological groupoid side, not the completeness converse.
\end{theorem}
