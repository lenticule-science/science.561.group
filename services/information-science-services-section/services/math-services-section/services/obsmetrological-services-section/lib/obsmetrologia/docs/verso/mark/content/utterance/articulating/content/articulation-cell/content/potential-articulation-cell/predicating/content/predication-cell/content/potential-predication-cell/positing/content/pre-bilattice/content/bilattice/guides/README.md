# Guides

Task-oriented. You have a goal and know roughly what you're doing — these are the steps to a specific
end, assuming context. (How-to, not why.)

Planned:

- **assert-evidence-into-a-cell** — how to bring a real telling (a witnessed charge) onto a cell and
  re-settle, and what the cones do with it.
- **read-the-void-cone** — how to get the cells a descent left open (`vanish`/`gap`), and how to read
  the evidence interval each carries.
- **split-consistent-from-paraconsistent** — how to use `compare` to separate the `⊥/t/f` cells from
  the `⊤` (told-both) locus, and what each part means.
- **build-a-richer-category** — how to hand a multi-entity / multi-predicate category to the descent
  (beyond a single utterance) and what changes.
- **extend-the-carrier** — how the value/evidence carrier generalizes (richer `L` than the two-bit
  case), and what stays invariant.
- **add-a-morphism** — the conventions (a named export in the right module, the charge shape, plumbing `_`
  prefix) for adding to the kernel without breaking the laws.
