\subsection{Value category and stochastic internal reals}

\begin{proposition}[The value category]\label{thm:value-category}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Positing.Value.ValueCategory}\leanok\uses{thm:four-bilattice, thm:strict-finite}
A \texttt{ValueCategory}: a carrier with a reflexive, transitive, antisymmetric order and a bottom element --- the value layer that Markov-kernel stochastic settling runs over.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[The real stochastic interval is a value category]\label{thm:real-stochastic-interval-value-category}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Probability.RealStochastic.realUnitInterval_is_value_category}\leanok\uses{thm:value-category, thm:reals-close-value-settling-hub}
\texttt{Probability/Stochastic.lean} was abstract because it predated internal reals: only a
\texttt{StochSemiring} and a \texttt{Nat} witness, no genuine $[0,1]$. With the internally derived
constructive reals (\texttt{Real.Internal}) present, the unit interval is built: \texttt{UnitReal},
the subtype $\{x:\mathbb{R} \mid 0\le x\le 1\}$, is a genuine \texttt{ValueCategory} --- reflexive,
transitive, and antisymmetric under the real order (antisymmetry from the reals' antisymmetry plus
proof irrelevance on the bounds), with seed $0$. This is the value layer for stochastic settling
over real probabilities, replacing the abstract semiring. A further refinement (red) instantiates the
actual Markov-kernel stochastic settling over it and proves the probability-normalization laws.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Finite thermodynamic transition production]\label{thm:finite-thermodynamic-transition-production}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Thermodynamics.Temperature.LiftedThermoTransition.produces_creation_landauer_and_discharge}\leanok\uses{thm:real-stochastic-interval-value-category, thm:stochastic-is-value-settling, thm:measure-is-valuation}
A lifted thermodynamic transition retaining its witnesses carries positive
held-\(\top\) creation, balanced stochastic flux, positive Landauer cost, and
positive hard-flow discharge.  This is the finite churn package; it does not yet
construct a real-valued free-energy functional.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Nat-valued free energy is Lyapunov]\label{thm:free-energy-lyapunov}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.free_energy_is_lyapunov}\leanok\uses{thm:settling, thm:closure-operator}
The hard closure flow carries a \(\mathbb{N}\)-valued free-energy deficit:
successive closure steps do not increase it, firing steps strictly decrease it,
and the orbit reaches the settled lift within the retained finite bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The stage ledger: the H-theorem as exact conservation]\label{thm:stage-ledger}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.stage_ledger}\leanok\uses{thm:free-energy-lyapunov}
The Lyapunov inequality of \cref{thm:free-energy-lyapunov} becomes an equality once each
stage's flux is kept on the books. \emph{One-step ledger}
(\texttt{ledger\_step}): for an ascending sandwich $v \le_k v' \le_k w$, the energy at
$v$ is the step's knowledge-flux plus the energy at $v'$ --- discharge conserved, unit
for unit. \emph{Truncation ledger} (\texttt{ledger\_total}): at every horizon $N$, the
seed's energy equals the flux so far plus the residue at $N$ --- the invariant a cut-off
cascade still carries --- and the book closes at quiescence
(\texttt{ledger\_closes}). \emph{Itemization}
(\texttt{stepFlux\_decomposition}, per-cell law axiom-free): each step's flux is exactly
its commitments ($\bot_k$ to a truth pole) plus its mints (a truth pole to $\top_k$)
plus twice its jumps ($\bot_k$ straight to $\top_k$). \emph{Register transfer}
(\texttt{truth\_loss\_is\_record\_gain}): along any $\le_k$ step the truth register's
losses are exactly the record register's gains from truth --- the quench, in counting
form. The arrow of the flow is the monotone side of a conservation law, not a bare
inequality. \emph{The cascade instance} (\texttt{rule\_ledger},
\texttt{chain\_cascade\_receipts}): the ledger runs on the van Emden--Kowalski
immediate-consequence orbit (\texttt{ClosureRule.Tstep}), which does not settle in one
step --- relative to any closed point above the seed, at every horizon --- and the
three-rule chain witness pays exactly one commitment per stage for three genuine stages,
no mints, no jumps, quiescent at the fourth; the witness and its receipts depend on no
axioms. \emph{The collision instance} (\texttt{collision\_cascade\_receipts}): two
chains of opposite orientation collide mid-cascade --- stage one pays three commitments,
stage two pays two mints (a slow $t$ meeting a held $f$ and a slow $f$ meeting a held
$t$, both quenching to $\top_k$), the truth register's stage-two loss equals its quench
count, stage three is quiescent, and the seed's energy is the five receipts. Held-$\top$
creation by collision (\texttt{collision\_conflict}, \texttt{CollisionTopMint})
realized as ledger mints of a running settling stage; no axioms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The stage temperature is the mint fraction]\label{thm:stage-temperature}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Thermodynamics.Temperature.collision_stage_temperatures}\leanok\uses{thm:stage-ledger}
The temperature of a settling stage, read off the ledger: `\(T = \mathrm{mints}/\mathrm{flux}\)`,
the fraction of the stage's discharge that is contradiction minting rather than
commitment (\texttt{stageTemperature}, an internal rational). A mintless stage is at
zero temperature — the hard-closure pole
(\texttt{stageTemperature\_zero\_of\_no\_mints}) — and the collision cascade
realizes both poles exactly: its commitment stage has \(T = 0/3 = 0\), its quench
stage \(T = 2/2 = 1\). The creation/discharge ratio of the thermodynamics sector,
derived from receipts rather than posited on an abstract stochastic carrier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The ledger Noether pair]\label{thm:ledger-noether}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.ledger_noether}\leanok\uses{thm:stage-ledger}
Which gauge symmetries pay conserved currents: the Klein-four generators are sorted by
the ledger. The knowledge-order-preserving generator \(\sim\) (negation) conserves
every register --- flux, commitments, mints, jumps all invariant
(\texttt{stepFlux\_negate} and \texttt{kHeight\_negate} depend on no axioms) ---
the Noether current of the internal symmetry. The order-reversing generator \(-\)
(conflation) does not preserve the height (it inverts it,
\texttt{kHeight\_conflate\_add}) and instead conjugates the ledger with time
reversal: a conflated, reversed step has the same flux with commitments and mints
exchanged and jumps fixed (\texttt{ledger\_mirror}) --- the anti-descent mirror in
counting form. The conserved current belongs exactly to the symmetry respecting the
order the flow descends.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The held-\(\top\) support is the mint plus jump registers]\label{thm:mint-register-is-top-support}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.topSupport_eq_mints_add_jumps}\leanok\uses{thm:ledger-noether, thm:two-spread-ledger}
The conserved current and the priced currency are one object, and the jump
register is the defect between two of the corpus's \(H^1\)'s. Over a \(\top\)-free
predecessor step the target's held-\(\top\) support is the ledger's mints plus
jumps (\texttt{topSupport\_eq\_mints\_add\_jumps}): every held \(\top\) was reached
this step, by quenching a pole (mint) or by a \(\bot\)-to-\(\top\) jump, the two
being disjoint (\texttt{incons\_disjoint\_mint\_jump}) and jointly exhausting
inconsistency below \(\top\) (\texttt{incons\_eq\_mint\_or\_jump}, no axioms). That
count is the merge's held-\(\top\) support
\texttt{Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.topCount} --- the idempotent Belnap
price of \cref{thm:two-spread-ledger}, bounded there by the record mass and the
cycle rank. So the settling-flow register (the genuine quotient \(H^1\) of the
bigraded truth--knowledge LES, whose nonzero connecting map is the irreversible
step) and the merge's \(\mathbb Z/2\) context-graph \(H^1\) count the same held-
\(\top\), and the mint current undercounts the merge price by exactly the jump
register: the \(\bot\)-straight-to-\(\top\) transitions. On a pole-valued step both
\(\top\)-freedom and jump-freedom hold, so the mint count alone is the support
(\texttt{mints\_eq\_topSupport\_of\_poleSeed}, from \texttt{mintB\_pole}). The
held-\(\top\) whose \texttt{negate}/\texttt{conflate} invariance
\cref{thm:ledger-noether} proves as a Noether current is the held-\(\top\) the
merge cohomology prices as a currency: one invariant read at two degrees,
conserved along the settling flow and priced at the merge. Axiom profile:
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound} (from the counting split; the pointwise
register identity \texttt{incons\_eq\_mint\_or\_jump} is axiom-free).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The Minkowski signature on the ledger registers]\label{thm:ledger-minkowski-signature}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.minkQ_factors}\leanok\uses{thm:ledger-noether, thm:stage-ledger}
The flux \(\mathrm{stepFlux} = \mathrm{commits} + \mathrm{mints} +
2\,\mathrm{jumps}\) carries a Lorentzian signature, read off its gauge action.
\cref{thm:ledger-noether} splits the registers: negation fixes all three,
conflation swaps commits and mints and fixes jumps. So \(\mathrm{commits} +
\mathrm{mints}\) is the gauge-symmetric \emph{spatial} part and \(2\,\mathrm{jumps}\)
--- the irreversible \(\bot\)-to-\(\top\) diameter, twice a unit --- the
distinguished \emph{temporal} part. The register interval
\(\mathrm{regInterval} = \mathrm{spatial} - \mathrm{temporal}\) is invariant under
both gauge generators (\texttt{regInterval\_negate}, \texttt{regInterval\_conflate}):
the internal Noether symmetry and the time-reversal are Minkowski isometries. The
Minkowski norm \(\mathrm{minkQ} = \mathrm{spatial}^2 - \mathrm{temporal}^2\) factors
as \(\mathrm{stepFlux} \cdot \mathrm{regInterval}\) (\texttt{minkQ\_factors}), the
light-cone quadratic as flux times Wick-rotated interval. The light cone is the
trichotomy of the interval: a jump-free step is spacelike
(\texttt{spacelike\_of\_no\_jump}, reversible transitions dominate), a pure-jump
step is timelike (\texttt{timelike\_of\_pure\_jump}, inside the cone), the null cone
is \(\mathrm{spatial} = 2\,\mathrm{jumps}\). The irreversible jump --- the
\texttt{Quot.sound} collapse of a distinction, one Landauer bit --- is the timelike
direction; the reversible commit/mint plane is spacelike. Axiom profile:
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound} (integer arithmetic; \texttt{regInterval\_negate}
is \texttt{propext} only).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The ledger light cone is the causal cone]\label{thm:ledger-cone-is-causal-cone}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.kle_is_cone_geometry}\leanok\uses{thm:ledger-minkowski-signature, thm:causal-cochain-complex, thm:currency-cocone}

axiom bundle \texttt{causal\_reachability\_is\_cone\_geometry} proves for the
causal order on events. Under it the register decomposition is the causal
trichotomy of a step (\texttt{cell\_cone\_trichotomy}): a jump is a timelike step
of \(\mathrm{kHeight}\) gap two --- exactly the \(\bot_k\)-to-\(\top_k\) maximal
interval (\texttt{jumpB\_iff\_bot\_top}, no axioms) --- a commit or mint is a null

sign of \cref{thm:ledger-minkowski-signature} realizes the timelike/spacelike

\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The budget split --- reversible and irreversible flux under the seed energy]\label{thm:budget-split}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Valuation.irreversible_within_budget}\leanok\uses{thm:stage-ledger, thm:ledger-minkowski-signature}
The seed's free energy is a budget spent exactly at settling. In the
strict-finite regime the book closes in one step
(\texttt{ledger\_closes}: \(\Phi_0 = \mathrm{orbitFlux}\,1\)), so splitting that
closing flux by the Minkowski signature (\cref{thm:ledger-minkowski-signature})
gives the seed energy as reversible spend \(\mathrm{commits} + \mathrm{mints}\)
plus irreversible spend \(2\,\mathrm{jumps}\) (\texttt{budget\_splits}). Both
spends sit under the seed energy, and the irreversible spend exhausts it exactly
when no reversible spend occurred (\texttt{irreversible\_within\_budget}). Read as
attention economics: the free energy is the attention budget a reconciliation has
to spend; the reversible commit/mint plane is recoverable attention (relabelling,
gauge-shiftable by \cref{thm:ledger-noether}); the timelike jumps are the
irreversible decisions --- one Landauer bit each, the \texttt{Quot.sound} collapse
of a distinction, attention that cannot be refunded. A settling that is purely
decisive (no reversible spend) consumes its entire budget on unrecoverable jumps.
Axiom profile: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound} (\(\mathbb N\) arithmetic).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The attention allocation]\label{thm:attention-allocation}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.Attention.attention_allocation}\leanok\uses{thm:budget-split, thm:currency-cocone, thm:high-energy-reconciliation-gap}
The demand side of reconciliation. The budget split (\cref{thm:budget-split})
prices reconciling \emph{one} obstruction; the allocation is the
budget-constrained selection over many. An obstruction carries a reconciliation
\(\mathrm{cost}\) (its certificate size, \cref{thm:high-energy-reconciliation-gap})
and a \(\mathrm{benefit}\) (the held-\(\top\) it removes); a selection is feasible
under budget \(B\) when \(\sum \mathrm{cost} \le B\), and its residual held-\(\top\)
is the benefit not captured. The empty selection is feasible with maximal residual
(\texttt{residual\_empty}), the base case; residual is antitone in captured benefit
(\texttt{residual\_antitone}), so minimising residual is maximising captured
benefit; and greedy extension within remaining budget stays feasible and captures
benefit (\texttt{feasible\_extend}). The light cone makes this not a free knapsack:
the jump spend is one-way, so budget is consumed monotonically
(\texttt{cost\_monotone\_cons}) and feasibility is downward closed
(\texttt{feasible\_of\_cons}) --- the irreversibility partial order over the priced
obstructions. Bundled as \texttt{attention\_allocation}. The degree-\(n\)
obstructions enter as the irreducibly-global demands: an \(n\)-ary contradiction
carries an irreducible cost no pairwise skim can pay
(\cref{thm:high-energy-reconciliation-gap}). Axiom profile: \texttt{propext}
(\(\mathbb N\) arithmetic; \texttt{feasible\_extend}, \texttt{cost\_monotone\_cons},
\texttt{empty\_feasible} are axiom-free). The optimising solver is
\cref{thm:allocation-solver}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The two-handed allocation solver]\label{thm:allocation-solver}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.Attention.solveL_optimal}\leanok\uses{thm:attention-allocation}
The solver returns the optimum, and it is handed. Over the powerset of
obstructions (\texttt{subs}, exhaustive by \texttt{sublist\_mem\_subs}), the
comparator \texttt{better} keeps the max-benefit feasible candidate. A left fold
(\texttt{solveL}, foldl --- the earliest-settling cascade) and a right fold
(\texttt{solveR}, foldr --- the latest-settling cascade) each return a feasible
selection (\texttt{solveL\_feasible}/\texttt{solveR\_feasible}, axiom-free) whose
benefit dominates every feasible sublist
(\texttt{solveL\_optimal}/\texttt{solveR\_optimal}): each hand solves the knapsack.
The optimum \emph{value} is chirality-invariant (\texttt{solvers\_agree\_value}:
the two hands achieve equal benefit). But the optimising \emph{selection} is
chirality-biased: on two equal-benefit beads the left fold keeps the second and the
right fold keeps the first (\texttt{bias\_witness}, no axioms). Which bead survives
when value cannot decide is fixed by the fold direction --- the ledger's
forward/backward asymmetry, the irreversible jump of \cref{thm:ledger-noether}'s
conflate surfacing as the bead bias. Axiom profile: \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Quot.sound} (list folds; \texttt{bias\_witness} and the feasibility
theorems are axiom-free).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Bias is exactly the degeneracy of the optimum]\label{thm:bias-is-degeneracy}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.Attention.hands_agree_of_unique_optimum}\leanok\uses{thm:allocation-solver}
The two hands diverge \emph{precisely} on the optimal tie-set. Both solver
outputs are feasible optimal sublists (\texttt{solveL\_isOptimum},
\texttt{solveR\_isOptimum}, using the powerset-membership converse
\texttt{mem\_subs\_sublist} to discharge that each hand is a sublist). If the
optimum is uniquely achieved --- every feasible optimal selection equals the left
solver's --- then the two hands return the identical selection
(\texttt{hands\_agree\_of\_unique\_optimum}): a non-degenerate optimum is
chirality-free. Contrapositively, divergence forces at least two distinct feasible
optima (\texttt{divergence\_forces\_degenerate\_optimum}), witnessed on the
two-bead scenario where both single-bead selections are optima
(\texttt{bias\_degenerate\_witness}, no axioms). So the bead bias is not an
artefact of the solver but exactly the degeneracy of the optimum: value alone
cannot pick, and the fold direction --- the ledger's arrow --- decides. Axiom
profile: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}
(\texttt{bias\_degenerate\_witness} is axiom-free).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The tie-set entropy is the Landauer bit of choice]\label{thm:tie-set-entropy}\lean{Obsmetrologia.Cohomology.Attention.choiceBits_zero_iff_effectively_unique}\leanok\uses{thm:bias-is-degeneracy, thm:mass-is-landauer}
The degeneracy of the optimum is measurable, and its entropy is a Landauer bit.
The tie-set (\texttt{tieSet}: the feasible optimal selections) has cardinality the
\texttt{degeneracy}, at least one (\texttt{degeneracy\_pos}, both hands lie in it);
its base-two logarithm is the \texttt{choiceBits} --- the bits the fold direction
must supply to select one optimum. The choice entropy vanishes exactly when the
optimum is uniquely achieved (\texttt{choiceBits\_zero\_iff\_effectively\_unique}:
\(\texttt{choiceBits} = 0\) iff \(\texttt{degeneracy} = 1\)), and a unique optimum
makes the two hands agree (\texttt{hands\_agree\_of\_degeneracy\_one}) --- zero
bits, no bias. This is the allocation's exact parallel of Landauer entropy
(\cref{thm:mass-is-landauer}, \texttt{Landauer.landauer\_zero\_iff\_reversible}:
\(\texttt{landauerBits} = 0\) iff the nerve is a forest): zero choice-bits iff the
selection is reversible, value alone deciding, no bit erased. The two-bead scenario
has degeneracy two, so \(\texttt{choiceBits} = \log_2 2 = 1\)
(\texttt{witness\_one\_choice\_bit}, no axioms) --- the single bit the ledger's arrow
erases when it breaks the tie, the same Landauer bit the settling cohomology prices
per nerve cycle, here per doubling of the optimum's degeneracy. Axiom profile:
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound} (\texttt{choiceBits} and
\texttt{witness\_one\_choice\_bit} are axiom-free).
\end{theorem}
