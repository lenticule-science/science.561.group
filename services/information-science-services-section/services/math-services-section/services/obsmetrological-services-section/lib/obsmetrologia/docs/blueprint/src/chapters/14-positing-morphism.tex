\chapter{The positing morphism}\label{ch:positing-morphism}

This chapter mechanizes positing's action on a valuation: the point-mass
update, the algebra one posit and finite batches of posits obey, the four
Belnap speech-act classes, naturality under the predication-cell generators
graded by how strong a claim each one supports, and the valuation-relative
reading of the four-corner partition. It also mechanizes the one charge
coordinate that needs no caller at all: the charge measured directly off an
articulation cell's own status.
