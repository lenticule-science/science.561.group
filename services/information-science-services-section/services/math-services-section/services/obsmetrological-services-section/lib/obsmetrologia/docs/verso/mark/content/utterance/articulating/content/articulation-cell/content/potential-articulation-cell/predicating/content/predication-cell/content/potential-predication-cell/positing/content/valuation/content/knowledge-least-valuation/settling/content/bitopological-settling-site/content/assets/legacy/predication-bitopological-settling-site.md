# Bitopological settling site

The site on which the cohomology of an [predication hyperdoctrine](predication-hyperdoctrine.md) lives, assembled from the [dual predication hyperdoctrine](../../../../predication-hyperdoctrine/content/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine.md)'s fiberwise [paired Priestley spaces](../../../../../../../../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/paired-priestley-space.md) over the contravariant [predicate category](predicate-category.md) and covariant settling direction.

## Underlying category $\mathbf{T}^*_{\mathrm{site}}$

Fix an predication hyperdoctrine $\mathcal{E}$ over $(\mathbf{L}, \mathcal{C}, E)$ with [settling bound](settling-bound.md) $\lambda$ and an [increment sequence](increment-sequence.md) $U = (u_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$. Write $X_q^\tau := \mathrm{Spec}(A_q)$ for the paired Priestley space associated to the [predication fiber algebra](predication-fiber-algebra.md) at settling stage $\tau$. We use the [pairwise-Stone](../../../../../../../../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/pairwise-stone-space/pairwise-stone-space.md) framing of the bitopological structure on $X_q^\tau$ (equivalent to the paired Priestley framing by [bitopological Stone duality](../theorems/bitopological-stone-duality.md): $\mathbf{PStone} \cong \mathbf{Pries}$); a **pairwise-Stone clopen** is a subset $U \subseteq X_q^\tau$ clopen in the join of the $+$- and $-$-component topologies. Pairwise-Stone clopens form a Boolean algebra.

- **Objects:** triples $(q, \tau, U)$ with $q \in \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$, $\tau < \lambda$ ordinal, and $U$ a pairwise-Stone clopen in $X_q^\tau$.
- **Morphisms** $(q', \tau', U') \to (q, \tau, U)$: triples $(f, h, j)$ where $f : q \to q'$ is a $\mathcal{C}$-morphism, $h : \tau \to \tau'$ is a $\lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$-morphism (equivalently $\tau' \geq \tau$), and $j : U' \hookrightarrow (f \times h)^*(U)$ is an inclusion of pairwise-Stone clopens. Here $(f \times h)^* : X_q^\tau \to X_{q'}^{\tau'}$ is the composite of the dualized [predicate](teaching/explanations/predicate.md) generator along $f$ ([dualized closure 2-comonad](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/dualized-closure-2-comonad.md), generator-dualization table) and the dualized settling advancement along $h$.
- **Composition** is componentwise.

## Grothendieck topology $J$

Generated by the pretopology: a family $\{(q, \tau, U_i) \xrightarrow{(\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{id}, \iota_i)} (q, \tau, U)\}_{i \in I}$ of pairwise-Stone inclusions at fixed $(q, \tau)$ is a **covering family** iff $\bigcup_i U_i = U$ in the pairwise-Stone topology on $X_q^\tau$. Covering sieves in $J(q, \tau, U)$ are those generated by such families.

Pairwise-Stone covers are themselves closed under pullback by Priestley morphisms, so the pretopology is a Grothendieck *basis*. Verification of the Grothendieck axioms is [bitopological-settling-site axioms](bitopological-settling-site-axioms.md).

## What the topology covers and does not

Covers are *only* fiber-level pairwise-Stone refinements at fixed $(q, \tau)$. There are no temporal covers — families of the form $\{(q, \tau_i, U) \to (q, \tau, U)\}_{\tau_i \geq \tau}$ are not covering. This is deliberate: cross-ordinal settling structure is carried in *morphisms* (the $h$ component), not in covers, and cohomological cross-ordinal content is extracted at the level of cohomology via direct limits along settling morphisms ([Anderson–Putnam direct limit](anderson-putnam-direct-limit.md)). See [why fiber covers only](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/why-fiber-covers-only.md) for the structural reasoning.

## Regime-dependent complexity

- **Strict regime** ($\kappa < \omega$): $\mathbf{T}^*_{\mathrm{site}}$ is a finite category — finite Priestley fibers, finitely many ordinal strata. Standard finite-cellular-sheaf cohomology applies.
- **Scott-continuous regime** ($\kappa = \omega$): countable at the ordinal level; pairwise-Stone fibers admit acyclic refinements via [Husainov acyclicity](teaching/explanations/husainov-acyclicity.md). Computable by Čech.
- **Transfinite-bounded regime** ($\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$): countably-many ordinal strata; computable in principle via transfinite iteration of Scott-continuous techniques.
- **Aperiodic regime** ($\omega_1 \leq \kappa$): $\omega_1$-or-more ordinal strata, non-trivial 2-cells throughout. Cohomology accessible only as a direct limit over bounded sub-sites $\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \tau}$ — the [aperiodic classifying invariant](aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) lives here.
