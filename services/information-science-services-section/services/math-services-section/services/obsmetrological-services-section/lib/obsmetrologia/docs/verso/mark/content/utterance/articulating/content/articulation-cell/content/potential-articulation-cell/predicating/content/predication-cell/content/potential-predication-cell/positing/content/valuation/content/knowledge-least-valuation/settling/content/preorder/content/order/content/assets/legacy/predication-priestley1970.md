## Annotation

Priestley's original duality theorem: bounded distributive lattices are dually equivalent to *Priestley spaces* — compact totally-order-disconnected ordered topological spaces. This is the order-enriched refinement of Stone duality and the foundation on which [@jung-rivieccio2012] builds the bilattice-side duality. For the program, the structural backbone of the [Priestley duality](../narratives/priestley-duality-opener.md) chapter; the [paired Priestley space](../../../../../../../../../../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/priestley-space/content/paired-priestley-space/paired-priestley-space.md) generalizes the basic Priestley space construction.
