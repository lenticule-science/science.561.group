\subsection{Causal closure-Laplacian}

\begin{theorem}[Causal Laplacian is a propagation closure]\label{thm:causal-laplacian}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Causal.Laplacian.causal_laplacian_theory}\leanok\uses{thm:cone-field-geometry}
Over Boolean event sections, causal propagation sends a seed to its reflexive
future closure. This operator is inflationary, monotone, idempotent, and has
future-closed sections as its harmonic fixed points.
\end{theorem}
