# Evidence

The role borne by the two components of a [twist product](../../../../../../../../../../residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md) value $\langle a_+, a_- \rangle \in L_1 \times L_2$. The first coordinate $a_+$ tracks **positive evidence** (information for the assertion); the second coordinate $a_-$ tracks **negative evidence** (information against).

The two evidence kinds behave dually under the bilattice's two orders. The [truth order](../../../../../../../../truth-order/truth-order.md) raises positive evidence and lowers negative evidence; the [knowledge order](knowledge-order.md) raises both. [Negation](negation.md) interchanges positive and negative evidence: $\sim \langle a_+, a_- \rangle = \langle a_-, a_+ \rangle$.

The four canonical configurations in the [four-element bilattice](../objects/four-bilattice.md): $\bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle$ (no evidence), $\mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$ (only positive), $\mathbf{f} = \langle 0, 1 \rangle$ (only negative), $\top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle$ (both positive and negative — contradictory).
