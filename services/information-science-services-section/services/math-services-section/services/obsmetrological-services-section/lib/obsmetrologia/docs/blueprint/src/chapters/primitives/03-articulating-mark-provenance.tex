\section{Articulating carries primitive-mark provenance}

\begin{theorem}[Every articulated carrier is supported by admitted primitive marks]\label{thm:articulating-mark-provenance}\notready\uses{def:mark, def:utterance, def:articulating}
\emph{Proof-line frontier.} Every primitive mark visible at an articulating head or floor, and every mark carried by an articulating occurring in a finite trace, must occur in the corresponding recursively accumulated mark record. The record is derived only from already admitted utterances.
\end{theorem}
