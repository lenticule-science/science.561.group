A **cell-trace** is a finite ordered sequence $\tilde T = \langle r_0, r_1, \ldots, r_{n-1} \rangle$ of [cell-records](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cell-record.md) satisfying five structural conditions:

(i) **Same-cell.** All entries share their cell projection: $r_i.\mathrm{cell} = r_j.\mathrm{cell}$ for all $i, j \in [0, n)$.

(ii) **Transaction-id monotonicity.** Entries are ordered by their transaction-ids in ISO-8601 lexicographic order: $i < j \implies r_i.t \leq r_j.t$.

(iii) **Value-chain monotonicity.** Values advance in the knowledge order: $i < j \implies r_i.\mathrm{value} \leq_k r_j.\mathrm{value}$ in the shared bilattice context (which by (i) and the implicit-context-of-the-cell discipline is the same across the chain).

(iv) **Stratum monotonicity.** When the stratum field is present, it is non-decreasing along the chain in the order $\mathrm{asserted} \prec \mathrm{saturated} \prec \mathrm{settled}$. Absence of stratum is admissible at any position.

(v) **Provenance accumulativity.** When $r_i.\mathrm{derived\text{-}via}$ is set, the cell-ids in $r_i.\mathrm{derived\text{-}from}$ are well-formed identifiers; if any such cell-id resolves to a cell-record in this same trace, it must appear at an index $i' < i$ (provenance does not refer forward).

The length $n$ may be $0$, $1$, or any positive integer. The empty trace $\langle \rangle$ represents the layer-5 absence of any event at the cell; the length-1 trace $\langle r_0 \rangle$ is a singleton (every cell-record is, by inclusion, a length-1 cell-trace).

## Theorems

### T1. Layer-5 → layer-1 forgetful map

Define $U : \mathrm{CellTrace} \to \mathrm{PredicationCellTrace}$ by $U(\tilde T)(i) := r_i.\mathrm{value}$, with the layer-1 trace's bound $\lambda := n$ and its cell taken to be $r_0.\mathrm{cell}$ (consistent across the trace by (i)).

Then $U(\tilde T)$ is a well-formed predication-cell-trace.

**Proof.** $U(\tilde T)$ is a function $n \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ by construction. Monotonicity in $\leq_k$ is exactly condition (iii). The cell is fixed. The bound is finite. The layer-1 record's `cell` and `bound` fields are exactly $r_0.\mathrm{cell}$ and $n$. $\square$

### T2. U is surjective onto finite layer-1 traces

For every finite predication-cell-trace $T : n \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ at $\phi$ (with $n < \omega$), there exists a cell-trace $\tilde T$ with $U(\tilde T) = T$.

**Proof.** Construct $\tilde T = \langle r_0, \ldots, r_{n-1} \rangle$ by $r_i := \mathrm{cellRecord}(\phi, T(i), t_i)$ for any monotone sequence of ISO-8601 transaction-ids $t_0 < t_1 < \cdots < t_{n-1}$, with stratum and provenance fields omitted. Conditions (i), (ii), (iii) hold by construction; (iv), (v) hold vacuously. $\square$

### T3. Distinct from cell-record by signature arity

A cell-record is a triple $\langle \phi, w, t \rangle$ — a single position-value-time tuple. A cell-trace is a sequence of $n \geq 0$ cell-records. The forgetful **endpoint projection** $\pi_{\mathrm{end}} : \mathrm{CellTrace}_{n \geq 1} \to \mathrm{CellRecord}$ sends $\tilde T \mapsto r_{n-1}$, which discards the first $n - 1$ elements of the chain. The forgetful **singleton inclusion** $\iota : \mathrm{CellRecord} \to \mathrm{CellTrace}$ sends $r \mapsto \langle r \rangle$. Neither is a bijection; the two structures have different arities. $\square$

### T4. Provenance-DAG slice theorem

Consider the directed graph $G$ whose vertices are all cell-records in some larger event-log (a JSONL file or a union of them), and whose edges are the `derived-from` references: an edge from $r$ to $r'$ exists iff some cell-id $c \in r.\mathrm{derived\text{-}from}$ resolves to $r'$. Then:

**(a)** $G$ is a directed acyclic graph (acyclic because every edge points strictly backward in transaction-id order, which is a total order on the event-log).

**(b)** The cell-projection $\pi_{\mathrm{cell}} : V(G) \to \Phi$, $r \mapsto r.\mathrm{cell}$, partitions $V(G)$ into fibers $\pi_{\mathrm{cell}}^{-1}(\phi)$.

**(c)** For each cell $\phi$, the fiber $\pi_{\mathrm{cell}}^{-1}(\phi)$, ordered by transaction-id, is a cell-trace at $\phi$ — the *event-log cell-trace at $\phi$*.

**Proof.** (a) is acyclic-by-construction. (b) is the definition of cell-projection-quotient. For (c), the fiber is a finite set ordered by ISO-8601 transaction-ids; same-cell (i) holds by definition of fiber; transaction-id monotonicity (ii) holds by the chosen order; value-chain monotonicity (iii) is a *property* — it holds when the event-log was emitted by a settling under the closure 2-monad, since settling monotonicity at the slice gives (iii) by Theorem T1 of [predication-cell-trace](../../../predication-cell-trace/predication-cell-trace.md). When the event-log is emitted by a process that respects the closure 2-monad (which the 561 OS event-log is *required to* by the cell-record discipline), (iii) holds. $\square$

T4 is the structural content of the layer-5 → layer-1 correspondence: the event-log JSONL is one global DAG, and its per-cell slices are the cell-traces.

### T5. In-flight vs settled cell-traces

A cell-trace is *settled* iff its last entry $r_{n-1}$ has stratum `settled`. It is *in-flight* iff the last entry has stratum `asserted` or `saturated`, or the stratum field is absent.

A settled cell-trace is a fixed point of the prefix-extension operation under the current closure operator: no further cell-record at the same cell will be appended unless an external assertion lands and restarts the settling. An in-flight cell-trace admits further appends. $\square$

This corresponds to clauses (b)/(c) of the discussion of stabilization-as-fixed-point in [predication-settling.md](../../../../../../predication-settling-category/library/predication-settling/predication-settling.md).

### T6. Datomic EAVT degeneracy

Under the specialization of [predication-cell-trace's T6](../../../predication-cell-trace/predication-cell-trace.md): $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$, $\lambda = 1$ (no settling iteration), and $\mathcal{C}$ generated by identities only. Then a cell-trace at $\phi$ has length $\leq 1$, stratum collapses to `asserted` (no `saturated` or `settled` step), and provenance is empty. The cell-trace at $\phi$ is in bijection with an optional Datomic EAVT row at $\phi$ — present iff length $= 1$, absent iff length $= 0$. $\square$

## Where this fits

- **Sibling** of [cell-id](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cell-id.md). Both are layer-5 structures attached to [cell-record](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cell-record.md). cell-id content-addresses a single record; cell-trace assembles a chain of records.
- **Layer-1 partner** is [predication-cell-trace](../../../predication-cell-trace/predication-cell-trace.md). The forgetful map $U$ goes from this structure to that.
- **Parent context** is the event-log [cell-record.jsonl](../../cell-record.jsonl). The event-log, partitioned by cell, is a family of cell-traces (one per cell-projection equivalence class).
