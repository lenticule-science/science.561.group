## What this node is

Positing is the morphism out of the
[potential predication cell](../potential-predication-cell.md) that **admits
value**. Where predicating produces a value-free predication cell — the cell
schema *forbids* a value field, so identity never depends on valuation —
positing packages that value-free fiber as a **presheaf** whose stalks take
[bilattice](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md) charges,
and reads the **knowledge-least [valuation](content/valuation/valuation.md)**:
not a bottom stamped onto every cell, but the value at every cell **no
articulating has yet reached** — the reading a cell gets by default, before
any evidence has been measured at it. Value enters the whole descent here, as
what the accumulated evidence supports; assertion raises it later.

## Measured, not seeded — owner ruling 2026-09-21

**"The admission boundary, restored."** What enters is not one bit but
**standing**, and standing is admitted *by evidence*, never stamped by fiat.
A bare predication says only *that* something is predicated; positing says
*with what standing* — and that standing is a
[`Charged`](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/charge/charge.md)
value: a bilattice charge (the four-valued FOUR crossing a **truth axis**,
supported / refuted, with a **knowledge axis**, known / unknown) paired with
the **lossless evidence** — the witness slots — that charge is read off. Every
charge is measured from an evidence carrier over a cell, never admitted
directly. `Charged.Measured` names the invariant that a value is exactly what
its evidence shows; `Charged.ofCell` reads a `Charged` straight off an
articulation cell's own slots, and that reading is always measured
(`Charged.ofCell_measured`). This is why positing is the morphism that
generates the logics *of value*, and why "measured, not seeded" is load
bearing rather than a slogan: a fixed point is something a closure *reaches*
by iterating, not something a cell is stamped with in advance (see
[measured, not seeded](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/explanations/measured-not-seeded.md)).

Combining evidence (`Charged.joinK`, `meetK`, `joinT`, `meetT`) always unions
the underlying witness slots even when it changes the derived value: the
knowledge meet of a potential telling and an active telling can drive the
value down to absurd while keeping both witnesses on record
(`Charged.meetK_not_measured`) — a **cancelled** conflict, evidenced on both
sides, is not the same fact as a cell **no** evidence has yet reached, even
though both currently read as knowledge-bottom. The one sanctioned breach of
`Measured` is **presumption** (`Charged.presumeFree`): raising every
evidence-free side to present while leaving the evidence itself untouched — a
bare articulated cell presumes from potential up to stable
(`Charged.presumeFree_articulate`). This is how the counit cone below reads a
cell's *most permissive* value without fabricating a witness.

## What it generates

Positing's codomain is the value category. Its carriers, under
[`content/`](content):

- [bilattice](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md) — the value structure: the
  distributive bilattice $L^{\mathrm{twist}}$ over the two-element residuated
  lattice $\mathbf{2}$ (bottom = the un-articulated position, top = both
  hands' slots witnessed). It carries the two orders (knowledge and truth)
  and their operations, and its
  [charge](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/charge/charge.md) values are *the same
  four-point slot-presence object* as the articulation phases — met here as
  value rather than as articulation status.
- [valuation](content/valuation/valuation.md) — the assignment of a charge to
  each cell; a knowledge-monotone family of valuations is a **trace**, the
  dynamic object the modal pair (possibilize ◇ ⊣ necessitate □) moves along.
  A trace of `Articulating`s is what `measure` reads to produce a measured
  valuation: for-witnesses are the head tokens of the articulatings whose
  oriented cell matches, against-witnesses those whose oriented cell is the
  contrary predicate — never a bare denial (`no_denial`).
- [logic](content/logic/logic.md) — the value logics, generated here because they
  *are* what the admitted evidence does: many-valued and paraconsistent logic (the
  bilattice charge itself), and the bilattice linear logic whose residuation
  judgments generate the substructural and linear logics. Logic is predication
  *with evidence admitted*, not a sibling of category theory.

This is the generative home of those value logics, because they are what the
bilattice and its orders are. The next morphism, **settling**, runs the closure
◇∘□ on a trace to its fixed point — where the value category settles into order.

## Orientation

The domain is the
[potential predication cell](../potential-predication-cell.md); descend to
[bilattice](content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md) for the value structure and
[valuation](content/valuation/valuation.md) for the charge assignment. Settling is
derived from the value category this morphism produces.
