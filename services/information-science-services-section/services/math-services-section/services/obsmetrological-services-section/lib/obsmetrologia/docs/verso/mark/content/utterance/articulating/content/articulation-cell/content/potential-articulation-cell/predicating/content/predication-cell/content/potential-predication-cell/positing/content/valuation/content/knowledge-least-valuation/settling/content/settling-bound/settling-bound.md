# Settling bound

An ordinal, denoted $\lambda$, that bounds the settling index of a family of [predication valuations](predication-valuation.md).
Settling steps $\tau$ range over $\tau < \lambda$.

The settling bound is typically $\omega$ — countable settlings, the setting of finite-convergence theorems — but may be any fixed ordinal.
Bounding by a fixed $\lambda$ avoids the size-theoretic issues of indexing by the class of all ordinals while retaining flexibility for transfinite settlings.
