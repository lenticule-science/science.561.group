A relation R on a set S is transitive if it chains: whenever aRb and bRc, then aRc.

Transitivity is one of the three defining properties of a [partial order](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/partial-order.md) and one of the two defining properties of a [preorder](../objects/preorder.md). It captures the idea that the relation is coherent across intermediate steps — if a ≤ b and b ≤ c, then a ≤ c directly, without needing b as a witness.

In a poset viewed as a [category](../../categories/theories/ordinary-categories/objects/category.md), transitivity is [composition](../../categories/theories/ordinary-categories/morphisms/composition.md): a [morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) a → b followed by b → c composes to a [morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/morphism.md) a → c.

In [modal logic](../../../disciplines/modal-logic/index.md), transitivity of the [accessibility relation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/accessibility-relation.md) validates the 4 axiom: □φ → □□φ. If accessibility chains (w accesses v, v accesses u, therefore w accesses u), then what is necessary is necessarily necessary. The [modality](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/modality.md) j in the [semiotic](../../../../linguistics/topics/semiotics/index.md) universe is idempotent (j(j(a)) = j(a)), which is the order-theoretic expression of transitivity: closing and then closing again yields nothing new.
