## What this node is

A **complete lattice** is a [lattice](../../lattice.md) in which *every* subset
$S$ of the carrier — not merely every pair — has a [meet](../meet/meet.md)
$\bigwedge S$ (its greatest lower bound) and a [join](../join/join.md)
$\bigvee S$ (its least upper bound). The demand extends to infinite subsets and to
the empty one.

Joining and meeting are not independent demands here: completeness for joins
already forces completeness for meets. The meet of $S$ is the join of the set of
all its lower bounds — that join exists because joins are complete — so a lattice
with all joins has all meets, and conversely. One half of the demand carries the
other.

## Generated from the lattice

The lattice gives meets and joins of *pairs*; the complete lattice strengthens
that to meets and joins of *all subsets*. This strengthening produces a
[bounded lattice](../bounded-lattice/bounded-lattice.md) for free — the join of
the empty set is $\bot$ and the meet of the empty set is $\top$ — so completeness
is the stronger sibling of boundedness rather than a thing built on top of it.

This is the refinement [settling](../../../../../../../../settling.md) actually
inhabits. Settling iterates a monotone closure on the lattice of valuations and
must reach a *least upper bound* of an entire ascending chain, not just of a pair;
that least upper bound is a join of a subset, and its existence for every subset
is precisely completeness. The value structure settling ascends is at least a
complete lattice, because only there is the destination of the ascent guaranteed
to lie in the order.

## What it generates

Completeness is the hypothesis of the fixed-point theorem the whole descent is
built to reach:

- the **Knaster–Tarski theorem** holds on a complete lattice — every monotone map
  has a least and a greatest fixed point — and the least fixed point is the home
  of settling's destination, derived as the [fixpoint](content/fixpoint/fixpoint.md)
  child.

The pointwise order on epistemic states
$\{\bot, t, f, \top\}^n$ over the four-valued
[bilattice](../../../../../../../../../../../../pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/bilattice.md)
is a complete lattice of finite height; on it the closure's ascent terminates,
which is what makes settling effective rather than merely well-defined.

## Orientation

Generated by [lattice](../../lattice.md), generated in turn by
[order](../../../../order.md) and [settling](../../../../../../../../settling.md). It
produces the [bounded lattice](../bounded-lattice/bounded-lattice.md) as a
consequence and carries the [fixpoint](content/fixpoint/fixpoint.md) where
settling's least fixed point is proven to exist.
