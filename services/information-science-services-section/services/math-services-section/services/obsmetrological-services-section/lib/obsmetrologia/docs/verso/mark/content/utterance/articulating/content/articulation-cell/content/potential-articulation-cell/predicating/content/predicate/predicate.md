## What this node is

A **predicate** is the middle coordinate $p$ of a
[predication cell](../predication-cell/predication-cell.md) $\phi = (s, p, o)$ — *what is
asserted* of the subject [entity](../entity/entity.md) in relation to the object
entity. It is the arrow predicating asserts over a cell, the thing that orients
the triple.

## A typed coordinate, not a label

A predicate is **not a bare string label**. Predicates inhabit the small
[predicate-category](../predicate-category/predicate-category.md)
$\mathcal{C}$, whose morphisms fall in **four canonical classes**:

- **specialization** — one predicate refines another;
- **converse** — the predicate run backwards, relating $(o, p, s)$ to $(s, p, o)$;
- **subject-projection** and **object-projection** — collapsing the cell onto one
  of its entity flanks.

This typing is what licenses **reindexing** of cells: because the predicate
coordinate is categorical, a value asserted at one cell can be transported to
related cells along these morphism classes. Set-theoretically the predicates
range over the **predicate set** $\Pi$, the typed index in
$\Phi = E \times \Pi \times E$; the predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ is that index
made to compose.

## Orientation

Read [entity](../entity/entity.md) for the relata the predicate is asserted
between, [predication-cell](../predication-cell/predication-cell.md) for the oriented triple,
and [predicate-category](../predicate-category/predicate-category.md) for
the four morphism classes. The morphism that asserts a predicate over a cell is
[predicating](../../predicating.md).
