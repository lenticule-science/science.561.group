# The discrete time section

**Hawking's stable-causality theorem**: a spacetime is stably causal iff it admits a **global time
function** — a real map strictly increasing along every future-directed causal curve. This node gives
the corpus's discrete refinement, and connects the existence of a *bounded-ordinal* time function to
the settling **regime**.

## The global time function

On a causal set the canonical global time function is the **past count**
$$t(x) \;=\; |\mathrm{past}(x)| \;=\; |\{z : z \prec x\}|.$$
It is **strictly monotone** along the causal order — `x ≺ y ⟹ t(x) < t(y)`
(`timeSection_strict_mono`, `Obsmetrologia.Lorentzian.RegimeTimeSection`): transitivity embeds
$\mathrm{past}(x) \subseteq \mathrm{past}(y)$, and irreflexivity puts $x$ itself in
$\mathrm{past}(y)\setminus\mathrm{past}(x)$, so the count strictly grows. The counting engine is a
filtered-length inequality `filter_length_lt_of_implies` (a count strictly grows under a predicate
implication with a strict witness). A strictly monotone $\mathbb N$-valued function is itself a
certificate of acyclicity — no closed timelike curve — so existence of the time section *is* discrete
stable causality.

At the diamond the section is $(0,1,1,3)$ (`diamond_timeSection`): the minimal event at time $0$, the
two spacelike middles tied at time $1$ (a time function may agree on incomparable events), the maximal
event at time $3$; it increases strictly along all five causal relations (`diamond_timeSection_mono`).

## The regime reading

The section lands in $\mathbb N$ — the ordinals below $\omega$. So a **finite** causal set (the
**strict** regime, $\kappa \le 3$, `causal_strict_regime_h1_zero` / `Charge.settles_by_three`) always
carries a **sub-$\omega$ ordinal time section**. This is the green half of the refinement:

> **non-aperiodic $\Rightarrow$ ordinal time section.** A settling with closure ordinal
> $\kappa < \omega_1$ admits an ordinal-valued global time function; over a finite carrier it is
> $\mathbb N$-valued.

The converse direction — *aperiodic $\Rightarrow$ no bounded-ordinal section* — is the sharp content:
the [aperiodic regime](../../../../../../../../aperiodic-articulation-category/...) ($\kappa \ge
\omega_1$) is exactly where the time section must climb past every countable ordinal, so no section
into a fixed countable ordinal exists. Stated as the biconditional

> **an ordinal time section into a fixed countable height exists $\iff$ the settling is
> non-aperiodic,**

it is the corpus's refinement of Hawking stable causality: *stable causality is the non-aperiodicity
of the settling*. The forward (existence) direction is green; the aperiodic obstruction reuses the
[four-regime dichotomy](../../../../closure-operator/content/scott-continuous-closure/scott-continuous-closure.md)
and is stated here, its full formalization tied to the transfinite section the aperiodic regime forces
(roadmap).

## Position

The time section is generated by the causal order alone — it is a function of $(E, \prec)$, no metric
and no manifold. It refines stable causality from a topological condition on a spacetime to a
**regime** condition on a settling: the global time function exists, bounded, exactly when the
settling does not run aperiodically. Geometry's "time" is the settling's ordinal height.
