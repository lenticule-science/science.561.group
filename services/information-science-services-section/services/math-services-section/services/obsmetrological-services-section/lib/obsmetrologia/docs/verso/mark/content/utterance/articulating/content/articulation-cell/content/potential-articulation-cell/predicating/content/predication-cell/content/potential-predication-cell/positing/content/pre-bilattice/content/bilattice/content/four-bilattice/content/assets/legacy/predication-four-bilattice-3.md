The **four-element bilattice** $\mathbf{FOUR}$ is the bilattice with carrier $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$ whose truth order is generated by $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \top \leq_t \mathbf{t}$, whose knowledge order is generated by $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$ and $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t} \leq_k \top$, whose negation $\sim$ fixes $\bot, \top$ and swaps $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$, and whose conflation $-$ fixes $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$ and swaps $\bot, \top$.

$\mathbf{FOUR}$ is the smallest non-trivial bilattice, the free bilattice on no generators, and is isomorphic to the twist product $\mathbf{2}^\bowtie$ of the two-element Boolean lattice $\mathbf{2}$ with itself, with $\mathbf{f} = \langle 0, 1 \rangle$, $\bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle$, $\top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle$, $\mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$.
