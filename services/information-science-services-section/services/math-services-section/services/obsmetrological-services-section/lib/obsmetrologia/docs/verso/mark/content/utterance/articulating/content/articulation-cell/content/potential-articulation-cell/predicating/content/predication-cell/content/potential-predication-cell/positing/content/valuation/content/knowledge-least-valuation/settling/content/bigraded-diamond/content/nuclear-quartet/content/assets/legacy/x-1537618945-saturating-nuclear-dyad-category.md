The **saturating-nuclear-dyad-category** is the category generated by [saturating](../../saturating.md). An object is a [nuclear-dyad](../../../library/aperiodic-articulation-cell/library/potential-aperiodic-articulation-cell/predicating/library/transfinite-predication-category/hacking/library/variety/library/lattice/library/closure-operator/library/nucleus/library/nuclear-dyad/nuclear-dyad.md) $(A, \sigma)$ where $\sigma$ is specifically a saturation nucleus — backward-facing, oriented toward sub-histories.

## Objects

Each object is a Heyting algebra $A$ together with a saturation nucleus $\sigma : A \to A$. The dyad presents two corner-cells:

- **Free corner**: $A$. All elements; $\sigma$ may still have work to do.
- **σ-quiescent corner**: $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma) = \{a \in A : \sigma(a) = a\}$. The sub-history-determined layer.

Connected by the adjoint reflection $\sigma : A \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma) : \iota$. Strict unit $a < \sigma(a)$ for $a \notin \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma)$.

## Morphisms

A morphism $(A, \sigma_A) \to (A', \sigma_{A'})$ is a Heyting algebra homomorphism $f : A \to A'$ satisfying

$$
f \circ \sigma_A \;=\; \sigma_{A'} \circ f.
$$

The σ-preservation condition: $f$ commutes with the saturation nucleus. This forces $f(\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_A)) \subseteq \mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_{A'})$, so morphisms restrict to maps between σ-quiescent sub-algebras.

## Composition and identity

Composition is composition of Heyting homomorphisms; identity is the identity homomorphism. The σ-preservation condition is closed under composition: if $f \circ \sigma_A = \sigma_B \circ f$ and $g \circ \sigma_B = \sigma_C \circ g$, then $g \circ f \circ \sigma_A = g \circ \sigma_B \circ f = \sigma_C \circ g \circ f$.

## Connection to articulation

The two corner-cells of a saturating-nuclear-dyad correspond to two phase-cells of articulation along the token-axis (with the claim-axis left free):

| Corner       | Sub-algebra | Phase-cell along token-axis |
|--------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Free         | $A$ | potential along token (claim-axis free) |
| σ-quiescent  | $\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma)$ | active along token (claim-axis free) |

The saturating-nuclear-dyad records the token-axis closure dynamic alone; the claim-axis is left untouched. Pairing this dyad with the [transferring-nuclear-dyad-category](../../../transferring/library/transferring-nuclear-dyad-category/transferring-nuclear-dyad-category.md) on the same carrier under [commutating](../../../commutating/commutating.md) produces the full [aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category](../../../commutating/library/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category/aperiodic-nuclear-quartet-category.md).

## Pairwise instance

The fiber Heyting algebras $H_t$ over a history category yield specific saturating-nuclear-dyad objects $(H_t, \sigma_t)$, one per history $t$. Morphisms in this category between $(H_t, \sigma_t)$ and $(H_{t'}, \sigma_{t'})$ for $t \leq t'$ are exactly the restriction homomorphisms $\rho_{t,t'} : H_{t'} \to H_t$ that preserve saturation.
