The smallest non-trivial [bilattice](bilattice.md), denoted $\mathbf{FOUR}$, with carrier $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$. The truth order is generated by $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \bot \leq_t \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f} \leq_t \top \leq_t \mathbf{t}$; the knowledge order by $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{f} \leq_k \top$ and $\bot \leq_k \mathbf{t} \leq_k \top$. The [negation](../transformers/negating.md) $\sim$ fixes $\bot$ and $\top$ and swaps $\mathbf{t} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{f}$; the [conflation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/conflating.md) $-$ swaps $\bot \leftrightarrow \top$ and fixes $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}$.

As a [twist product](../../../../../../../../residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md), $\mathbf{FOUR} \cong \mathbf{2}^\bowtie$ — the twist of the Boolean lattice $\mathbf{2}$ with itself:

$$\mathbf{f} = \langle 0, 1 \rangle, \quad \bot = \langle 0, 0 \rangle, \quad \top = \langle 1, 1 \rangle, \quad \mathbf{t} = \langle 1, 0 \rangle.$$

The four values carry the canonical epistemic interpretation: $\mathbf{t}$ as *told true* [@belnap1977], $\mathbf{f}$ as *told false*, $\bot$ as *told nothing*, $\top$ as *told both*. FOUR is the free bilattice on no generators [@fitting1991].
