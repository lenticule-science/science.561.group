# Failure detection is bilattice-valued modal suspicion on the heartbeat sheaf

**Claim.** The substrate's [failure-detection](teaching/explanations/failure-detection.md) discipline is *not* an ad-hoc engineering layer atop the coordination protocols; it is the *substrate-content realization* of one fixed mathematical construction in the math parent: the [modal possibility operator](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-possibility-operator.md) $\Diamond_R$ acting on the negative-evidence ($L_-$) component of the cluster's heartbeat-indexed [predication-fiber-algebra](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md), with the [bilattice accessibility relation](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-bound/content/world-set/content/bilattice-accessibility-relation/bilattice-accessibility-relation.md) $R$ given by recent-heartbeat-arrival between observation-window worlds. Concretely:

1. The four substrate-recorded suspicion levels — `confirmed-alive`, `recently-heard-from`, `suspected`, `confirmed-failed` — are *exactly* the four values of [$\mathbf{FOUR}$](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md) on the per-cell predication $\mathrm{alive}(o, m)$ ("observer $o$ holds that member $m$ is alive"): $\mathbf t$, $\bot$, $\Diamond_R \mathbf f$, $\mathbf f$ respectively. The substrate-recorded transition table is the bilattice's $\leq_k$-Hasse diagram, restricted to the closure-driven monotone moves.
2. The Chandra–Toueg failure-detector hierarchy (P, S, $\Diamond$P, $\Diamond$S) factors as four projections of the cluster's [inconsistency-sheaf](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/inconsistency-sheaf/inconsistency-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ along bilattice-homomorphism quotients of $\mathbf{FOUR}$; *strong vs. weak* is the choice of which $L_-$-section the projection retains, *perfect vs. eventually-perfect* is the choice of which $\tau$-stage the projection retains.
3. Phi-accrual scoring is the [Lawvere-quantale-graded](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale.md) $\Diamond_R$: the $\phi$-score is a [continuous-valued settling](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/continuous-valued-settling/continuous-valued-settling.md) on the heartbeat predication, and the threshold-crossing rule is a closure-rule firing whose antecedent reads the $\mathcal V = ([0,\infty], +, \min)$ value of $\Diamond_R$ against a substrate-policy threshold.
4. Split-brain failure-detection — two observers stably disagreeing about a third member's liveness — is *exactly* a non-trivial section of $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ on the disagreement clopen, and is detected by the audit operation per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md) without any failure-detection-specific code path.

The substrate's failure-detection module is therefore *not* a distinct authorization or safety primitive: it is the same modal apparatus whose other surfaces are catalogued in [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), specialized to the heartbeat-arrival sub-region of the substrate's accessibility relation $R$.

## Setup

Fix a [cluster-record](../objects/cluster-record.md) $C$ of $N$ members $m_1, \ldots, m_N$, presented as an $N$-element [replica cover](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/replica-cover.md) $\{U_i \to U\}$ per [cluster-as-replica-cover](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cluster-as-replica-cover.md). Let $p = \mathrm{alive} \in \mathcal C$ be the unary [predicate](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predicate.md) "is alive at this instant", with predication cells $(o, m) \in E \times E$ where $o$ is the observer and $m$ the observed member.

Form the **observation-window world set** $W = T \times M$, where $T$ is a discrete sequence of recent observation windows (typically the last $k$ heartbeat intervals) and $M = \{m_1, \ldots, m_N\}$ is the member set. The **heartbeat accessibility relation** $R_{\mathrm{hb}} : W \times W \to \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ is the [bilattice accessibility relation](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-bound/content/world-set/content/bilattice-accessibility-relation/bilattice-accessibility-relation.md) given by

$$R_{\mathrm{hb}}\bigl((t_1, m), (t_2, m)\bigr) \;:=\; \begin{cases} \mathbf t & \text{if a heartbeat from } m \text{ arrived in window } [t_1, t_2], \\ \bot & \text{if no heartbeat is recorded yet on window } [t_1, t_2], \\ \mathbf f & \text{if window } [t_1, t_2] \text{ has elapsed past the substrate-policy threshold without arrival}. \end{cases}$$

Off-diagonal $(m \neq m')$ entries are $\bot$: a heartbeat from $m$ does not give evidence about $m'$. The relation is by construction $\leq_k$-monotone in the [knowledge order](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md): later windows accumulate more evidence about earlier intervals, never less.

Per [vote-as-section](teaching/explanations/vote-as-section.md), each observer's recorded liveness assertion at a window $t$ is a section of the [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agentic-relation-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F_U$ of $p$ over $U_i$. Per [paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md), this decomposes as $\mathcal F_U^{(t)} \oplus \mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ — truth-side liveness assertions and contradictory-evidence cells.

## Statement

**Theorem (failure detection as bilattice-modal suspicion).** *Let $C$, $W$, $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$, $\mathcal F_U = \mathcal F_U^{(t)} \oplus \mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ be as above. Then:*

1. **Four-state correspondence.** *For every observer $o$ and member $m$, the substrate-recorded suspicion level for $(o, m)$ at window $t$ is the value, in $\mathbf{FOUR}$, of the per-cell predication*
   $$\langle\, \Box_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}(\mathrm{alive})(t, m),\; \Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}(\neg\mathrm{alive})(t, m) \,\rangle \;\in\; \mathbf{2}^\bowtie \cong \mathbf{FOUR},$$
   *where $\Box$ and $\Diamond$ are the [modal necessity](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-necessity-operator.md) and [modal possibility](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-possibility-operator.md) operators along $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$. The four values $\langle 1,0\rangle, \langle 0,0\rangle, \langle 0,1\rangle, \langle 1,1\rangle$ are exactly the four substrate-recorded levels:*

| Substrate level         | Twist coords           | $\mathbf{FOUR}$ value | Reading                                                  |
|-------------------------|------------------------|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| `confirmed-alive`       | $\langle 1, 0 \rangle$ | $\mathbf t$           | $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\mathrm{alive}$, no negative evidence |
| `recently-heard-from`   | $\langle 0, 0 \rangle$ | $\bot$                | neither $\Box$ nor $\Diamond_{\neg}$ active yet          |
| `suspected`             | $\langle 0, 1 \rangle$ | $\mathbf f$ (modal)   | $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\neg\mathrm{alive}$ has fired |
| `confirmed-failed`      | $\langle 1, 1 \rangle$ | $\top$ as `dead`      | $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\neg\mathrm{alive}$, all windows agree |

   *(The substrate uses $\top$ for `confirmed-failed` only when the negative evidence is non-paraconsistent — every accessible window holds $\neg\mathrm{alive}$. Disagreement-induced $\top$ — split-brain — is recorded distinctly per claim 4.)*

2. **Monotonicity of suspicion is closure-driven.** *The substrate's suspicion-level transition graph is exactly the $\leq_k$-Hasse diagram of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ restricted to monotone moves. The transitions $\bot \to \mathbf t$, $\bot \to \mathbf f$, $\mathbf t \to \top$, $\mathbf f \to \top$ are admissible (knowledge accumulates); the reverse moves are inadmissible. Per [closure-driven-settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-monotonicity/references/closure-driven-settling-monotonicity.md) and [closure-2-monad](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md), this monotonicity is a structural consequence of the heartbeat predication being settled under the cluster's closure-rule system, not a separate engineering invariant.*

3. **Chandra–Toueg classes as bilattice-homomorphism projections.** *The four classical Chandra–Toueg failure-detector classes factor as four bilattice-homomorphism projections of $\mathcal F_U$:*

| Class                                  | Projection                                                                                  | What it retains                                                              |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **P** (perfect)                        | $\pi^{\mathrm{P}} : \mathcal F_U \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal F_U^{(t)}$, all $\tau$         | strong completeness $\wedge_k$ strong accuracy: every failure detected, no false positive |
| **S** (strong)                         | $\pi^{\mathrm{S}} : \mathcal F_U \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal F_U^{(t)}$ on a $\bigvee_k$-stable substack | strong completeness $\wedge_k$ weak accuracy: at least one correct member never suspected |
| **$\Diamond$P** (eventually perfect)   | $\pi^{\mathrm P} \circ \mathrm{colim}_\tau$                                                  | P holds in the colimit; transient false positives are killed by the closure |
| **$\Diamond$S** (eventually strong)    | $\pi^{\mathrm S} \circ \mathrm{colim}_\tau$                                                  | S holds in the colimit                                                       |

   *Each class is a bilattice-homomorphism quotient of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ on the per-cell value: P / S quotient by truncating the suspicion sub-stage, $\Diamond$P / $\Diamond$S quotient by passing to the closure-ordinal colimit. The classical strong-vs-weak distinction is the choice of which $L_-$-cells survive the truncation; the perfect-vs-eventual distinction is the choice of which $\tau$-stage of the [predication settling](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) the projection retains.*

4. **Split-brain failure detection is $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$-content.** *If observers $o_1, o_2$ stably disagree about whether $m$ is alive — $o_1$ records $\mathrm{alive}(o_1, m) = \mathbf t$ and $o_2$ records $\mathrm{alive}(o_2, m) = \mathbf f$ at the same settling stage — then the cellwise [bilattice network-sheaf consensus](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md) join over observers carries value $\top$ at the cell $(*, m)$, so the disagreement contributes a non-zero section to the inconsistency sheaf $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ on the disagreement clopen. Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), the audit operation surfaces the $\top$-cell as a substrate-content coherence failure without invoking any failure-detection-specific reconciliation code.*

5. **Phi-accrual is quantale-graded $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}$.** *Equip $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$ with the Lawvere quantale $\mathcal V = ([0, \infty], +, \min)$ per [cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale.md): $R_{\mathrm{hb}}\bigl((t_1, m), (t_2, m)\bigr)$ carries, beyond its $\mathbf{FOUR}$-value, a $\mathcal V$-grade equal to the elapsed-time-since-last-heartbeat. The phi-accrual score $\phi(o, m, t)$ is then exactly the value of the $\mathcal V$-graded $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\neg\mathrm{alive}$ at window $t$. The substrate's threshold-crossing rule (transition `recently-heard-from` $\to$ `suspected` when $\phi \geq \theta$) is the closure-rule firing whose antecedent reads the $\mathcal V$-grade against the substrate-policy threshold $\theta$.*

6. **Trade-off is the regime classification.** *The classical trade-off — quick detection (low $\theta$) gives high false-positive rate; slow detection (high $\theta$) gives long recovery latency — is the substrate-content realization of the [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md) applied to the failure-detection sub-system: a low-$\theta$ failure-detection runs in the strict / Scott-continuous regime (fast closure ordinal, but transient $\top$-cells from premature suspicion); a high-$\theta$ failure-detection runs in the transfinite-bounded regime (later closure ordinal, but lower $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$-content). The trade-off is therefore not a free design choice but a substrate-policy selection of regime.*

## Proof

The proof composes one definitional step (claim 1), three corpus-level reductions (claims 2, 3, 4) and two structural identifications (claims 5, 6). No new mathematics is introduced.

**(1) Four-state correspondence.** Per the [twist product](../../../../../../residuated-lattice/content/twist-product/twist-product.md) decomposition $\mathbf{FOUR} \cong \mathbf{2}^\bowtie$ (per [four-bilattice](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/four-bilattice.md)), every value of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ on a per-cell predication factors as $\langle a^+, a^- \rangle$ with $a^+ \in \mathbf 2$ recording positive evidence and $a^- \in \mathbf 2$ recording negative evidence. Per [modal-necessity-operator](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-necessity-operator.md), $\Box_R$ on a positive-evidence assertion produces a positive-evidence assertion, and per [modal-possibility-operator](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/modal-possibility-operator.md), $\Diamond_R$ on a negated assertion produces a negative-evidence assertion. The pair $\langle \Box_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\mathrm{alive},\, \Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\neg\mathrm{alive} \rangle$ is therefore an element of $\mathbf{2}^\bowtie \cong \mathbf{FOUR}$. Reading the four substrate-recorded levels by their (positive-evidence, negative-evidence) pair instantiates the table directly: `confirmed-alive` is $\langle 1, 0 \rangle$ (positive evidence, no negative evidence); `recently-heard-from` is $\langle 0, 0 \rangle$ (neither modal has fired); `suspected` is $\langle 0, 1 \rangle$ (negative possibility has fired without positive necessity); `confirmed-failed` is $\langle 1, 1 \rangle$ in the failed-but-non-paraconsistent reading (every accessible window agrees on $\neg\mathrm{alive}$). $\square$

**(2) Monotonicity of suspicion.** Per [closure-driven-settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/settling-monotonicity/references/closure-driven-settling-monotonicity.md), every closure-rule application is $\leq_k$-monotone. The heartbeat predication, settled under the cluster's closure-rule system per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](../../../theorems/closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), inherits the monotonicity: the substrate-recorded suspicion-level transitions are exactly the $\leq_k$-monotone moves in $\mathbf{FOUR}$. The substrate cannot transition `suspected` $\to$ `recently-heard-from` (a $\leq_k$-decrease) without violating closure-driven-monotonicity; the corpus-level theorem rules this out, not a separate failure-detection-specific invariant. $\square$

**(3) Chandra–Toueg classes.** Per [paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md), $\mathcal F_U \cong \mathcal F_U^{(t)} \oplus \mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$, and per [bilattice-homomorphism](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/bilattice-homomorphism.md), every truth-and-knowledge-preserving quotient of $\mathbf{FOUR}$ factors through one of four canonical projections (corresponding to the four sub-Boolean-algebras $\{\bot, \mathbf t\}, \{\bot, \mathbf f\}, \{\bot, \top\}, \{\mathbf t, \mathbf f\}$ of $\mathbf{FOUR}$). The four Chandra–Toueg classes correspond bijectively to these projections:

- **P** projects $\mathcal F_U$ to its $\mathcal F_U^{(t)}$ summand at every $\tau$ (strong completeness preserved at every stage, no false positives ever).
- **S** projects $\mathcal F_U$ to a $\bigvee_k$-stable substack of $\mathcal F_U^{(t)}$ (at least one always-correct member's section is preserved; weak accuracy).
- **$\Diamond$P** is the colimit $\mathrm{colim}_\tau \pi^{\mathrm P}$ — the closure-ordinal-eventual version of P, where transient false positives are absorbed by the closure 2-monad's lax-idempotence per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md).
- **$\Diamond$S** is the same colimit applied to S.

The classical proofs (Chandra–Toueg–Toueg 1996) that $\Diamond$P and $\Diamond$S suffice for consensus reduce to: $H^0(U, \mathrm{colim}_\tau \pi(\mathcal F_U))$ is non-trivial iff the cellwise $\bigvee_k$-merge over members produces a global section — exactly the [bilattice network-sheaf consensus](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md) result. $\square$

**(4) Split-brain.** $o_1$ and $o_2$ contributing $\mathrm{alive}(o_i, m) = \mathbf t$ and $\mathbf f$ respectively are exactly the setup of the worked instance in [bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/bilattice-network-sheaf-consensus.md), claim 2 (disagreement-join). The cellwise $\vee_k$ over observers at the cell $(*, m)$ is $\mathbf t \vee_k \mathbf f = \top$. Per the construction of the [inconsistency sheaf](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/inconsistency-sheaf/inconsistency-sheaf.md), the resulting $\top$-cell contributes to $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ on the disagreement clopen. Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), the audit operation reads $H^*(U, \mathcal F_U^{(\top)})$ directly and surfaces the $\top$-section as a coherence failure. The substrate's split-brain detection is therefore not a special-case code path — it is the same audit-cohomology operation that surfaces every other inconsistency. $\square$

**(5) Phi-accrual.** Per [cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale.md), the substrate's continuous-valued telemetry composes by Lawvere-quantale arithmetic in $\mathcal V = ([0, \infty], +, \min)$. Equipping $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$ with the elapsed-time grade extends $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}$ to a $\mathcal V$-graded operator: $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}^\mathcal V \neg\mathrm{alive}(t, m) := \min_{w'} \bigl( R_{\mathrm{hb}}^\mathcal V(t, w') + (\neg\mathrm{alive})(w') \bigr)$, which under the heartbeat semantics returns the elapsed time since the most recent heartbeat from $m$ that is still consistent with $\neg\mathrm{alive}$. This is the phi-accrual $\phi(o, m, t)$ definition (Hayashibara et al. 2004), up to the standard $\mathrm{logarithm}$-of-CDF re-scaling that makes thresholds dimensionally comparable across heartbeat distributions. The threshold-crossing rule is the closure-rule whose antecedent fires when $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}^\mathcal V \neg\mathrm{alive} \geq \theta$ in $\mathcal V$. $\square$

**(6) Trade-off as regime selection.** The substrate's failure-detection sub-system, viewed as a closure-rule-system specialized to the $\mathrm{alive}$ predicate, has its own closure ordinal $\kappa_{\mathrm{fd}}$ (the stage at which the suspicion sheaf stabilizes). Per [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md), $\kappa_{\mathrm{fd}}$ falls into one of four regimes. A low-$\theta$ choice fires suspicion rules earlier, producing a smaller $\kappa_{\mathrm{fd}}$ but more transient $\top$-cells in $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ before stabilization (false positives). A high-$\theta$ choice delays suspicion firing, producing a larger $\kappa_{\mathrm{fd}}$ but fewer transient $\top$-cells. The trade-off is therefore the substrate-policy choice of where in the regime classification the failure-detection sub-system sits — the same trade-off that governs every other closure-rule sub-system per [regime-classification-for-substrate](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/regime-classification-for-substrate.md). $\square$

## Engineering consequence

- **Failure-detection has no separate authorization surface.** Per [modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/modal-restriction-as-substrate-authorization-primitive.md), every authorization-shaped check on the substrate is one $\Box_R$ evaluator parameterized by sub-region. The failure-detection sub-region of $R$ — the heartbeat-arrival relation $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$ — slots into that single evaluator. An implementer building a substrate does not write a `failure-detector` component alongside `governance-checker`, `auth-checker`, etc.; they parameterize the existing $\Box_R / \Diamond_R$ implementation with $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$.
- **Suspicion-level transitions need no bespoke state machine.** Per claim 2, the four-state transition graph is the $\leq_k$-Hasse diagram of $\mathbf{FOUR}$, restricted by closure-driven-monotonicity. The substrate's existing closure-rule engine handles the transitions; no failure-detection-specific state machine is required.
- **Failure-detector class is a substrate-policy declaration, not a re-implementation.** Per claim 3, switching between P, S, $\Diamond$P, $\Diamond$S is the choice of one of four bilattice-homomorphism projections on $\mathcal F_U$. The substrate's [substrate-policy](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/substrate-policy.md) declares which projection the cluster uses; no bespoke detector code is shipped per class.
- **Split-brain is detected by the audit operation.** Per claim 4, observers' disagreement about a member's liveness contributes to $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$ exactly like every other substrate-content disagreement. The substrate's [audit](../morphisms/audit-quorum-witness-chain.md) operation, reading $H^*(U, \mathcal F_U^{(\top)})$, surfaces split-brain failure-detection without a special code path. The cluster's reconciliation discipline is the substrate's general inconsistency-resolution discipline, not a failure-detection-specific protocol.
- **Phi-accrual and binary timeout share one operator.** Per claim 5, phi-accrual is the $\mathcal V$-graded reading of the same $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}$ that drives binary timeout. The substrate's implementation evaluates $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}$ once and reads its value through the substrate-policy's quantale (boolean for binary timeout, $[0, \infty]$ for phi-accrual, $[0, 1]$ for confidence-bounded suspicion).
- **Failure-detection composes with leader-election structurally.** Per [leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits](leader-election-termination-via-no-periodic-orbits.md), election termination is closure-driven; per claim 2, suspicion of the current leader is closure-driven; per [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](../../../theorems/closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), the composed closure-rule system (heartbeat + election) is itself a closure 2-monad on the cluster's predication fiber. Re-election triggered by suspected-failed leader is therefore *one* closure-rule evaluation across the union of failure-detection and election predicates, not a sequential handoff between two subsystems.
- **Federation-trust grading lifts to failure detection.** Per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](../../../theorems/federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md), federation peers carry bilattice-graded trust. A cross-substrate heartbeat is read with the peer's trust grade applied to $R_{\mathrm{hb}}$: a low-trust peer's heartbeat raises $\Box_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}\mathrm{alive}$ less than a high-trust peer's. The same modal apparatus handles trust-graded failure detection without new code.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Non-monotone suspicion levels.** A substrate that allows a member to transition `suspected` $\to$ `recently-heard-from` (e.g., on receipt of a stale-but-newly-arrived heartbeat) is not running closure-driven settling on its heartbeat predication. Per claim 2, such an implementation has confused stale-arrival with retraction; the corpus-level closure-driven-monotonicity refuses it. The conformant pattern is to record the new heartbeat as evidence at its actual window $t'$, leaving the suspicion at the later window $t > t'$ unaffected (knowledge in $[t, \infty)$ does not retract; knowledge in $(-\infty, t']$ accumulates at its own cell).
- **Binary failure detection on a multi-peer cluster.** A failure-detector that reports a single boolean per peer (alive / failed) with no representation of the $\bot$-state ("don't-know-yet") cannot be the substrate-content realization of $\mathbf{FOUR}$-valued failure detection. The conformant detector represents all four states; the boolean projection is one of the bilattice-homomorphism quotients in claim 3 (specifically, the one that collapses $\bot \to \mathbf t$ — optimistic — or $\bot \to \mathbf f$ — pessimistic), and the choice is substrate-policy.
- **Failure-detection records that don't federate.** A failure-detection sub-system whose records live in non-substrate storage cannot participate in $\mathcal F_U^{(\top)}$-cohomology audit. Per claim 4, split-brain detection requires the records to be substrate-content. A substrate that runs failure-detection in an external monitoring service must federate that service's output back into the substrate per [federation-convergence](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/federation-convergence.md) before audit can detect split-brain.
- **Phi-accrual implementations that don't compose with cost-discipline.** Per claim 5, phi-accrual is one reading of $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}^\mathcal V$. An implementation that computes $\phi$ outside the substrate's quantale arithmetic (e.g., maintaining its own elapsed-time counter) loses composability with [cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/cost-composition-via-lawvere-quantale.md): the resulting $\phi$ does not compose cleanly across federation-graph paths, and `cost-aware` operations can't reason about it uniformly.
- **Detector-class polymorphism via runtime branching.** Per claim 3, the four Chandra–Toueg classes are four projections; a substrate that ships four distinct detector implementations and selects one at runtime is doing the same work four times. The conformant pattern is one $\Diamond_{R_{\mathrm{hb}}}$ implementation with the projection selected at substrate-policy level.
