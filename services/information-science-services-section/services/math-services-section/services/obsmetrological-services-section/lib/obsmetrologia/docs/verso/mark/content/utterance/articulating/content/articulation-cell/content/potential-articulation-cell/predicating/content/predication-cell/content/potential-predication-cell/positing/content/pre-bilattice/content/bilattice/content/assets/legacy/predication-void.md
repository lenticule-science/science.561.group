## Definition

Void is the bilattice value $\bot \in A_p$ at the bottom of the knowledge-order $\leq_k$ on every fiber algebra $A_p$. A predication cell $\phi = (s, p, o)$ has *void value* when

$$v(p)(s, o) = \bot.$$

The cell exists as a syntactic triple in the predicate category; its valuation deposits no evidence supporting or refuting the cell's assertion. Void is the cell-internal counterpart to the configuration-level would-be absence that [`relationlessness`](relationlessness.md) names.

## Role

Void is the admissible cell-internal evidence-bottom. Where [`relationlessness-relates`](../../prose/learning/claims/relationlessness-relates.md) establishes that relationlessness *is* the originating predication cell (with value above $\bot$ supplied by the articulation itself), void names the value any cell may take when no evidence has accreted in its fiber. Cells relate (the originating theorem); their evidence-content begins, ranges over, or returns to void. Evidence accretion is movement in $\leq_k$ from $\bot$ toward the classical values $\mathbf{t}$ or $\mathbf{f}$, or toward the held-contradiction $\top$.

The knowledge-order $\leq_k$ on the bilattice has $\bot$ as its least element by definition of [`bilattice`](bilattice.md). Every value is $\bot \leq_k v$; equivalently, every cell's valuation is at least void.

## Distinction from relationlessness

Relationlessness is a cell — the originating predication cell $\phi^*$ of the predicate category. Void is a value — the no-evidence element $\bot$ of the bilattice fiber algebra. The two live at distinct strata:

| | What it names | Stratum |
|---|---|---|
| relationlessness | the originating predication cell $\phi^*$ | cell-level |
| void | the no-evidence value $\bot \in A_p$ | value-level |

A configuration in which the originating cell exists but no further evidence has been deposited at any fiber is the all-void state. It is *not* relationlessness; relationlessness IS the originating cell, value already above $\bot$ at $p^*$. The all-void state is the valuation that assigns $\bot$ to every cell other than the originating one.

See [`relationlessness`](relationlessness.md).

## Position

The cell-internal counterpart to the originating theorem. With [`relationlessness`](relationlessness.md), void grounds the predication-cell-with-bilattice-value as a primitive of predication category theory: cells relate, their values include the admissible no-evidence $\bot$. The bilattice's $\bot$ is the floor of every cell's evidence-trajectory, not a deficiency to be eliminated.
