A function $v$ that assigns to each predicate $p$ an element $v(p) \in A_p$ of the [predication fiber algebra](predication-fiber-algebra.md) at $p$. This determines a value $v(p)(s, o) \in \mathbf{L}^\bowtie$ at each predication cell $(s, p, o)$.

When valuations are organized into a settling — a family $v = (v_\tau)_{\tau < \lambda}$ over a settling bound $\lambda$ — each $v_\tau$ is a predication valuation in this sense, recording the values assigned at settling step $\tau$. When the family satisfies settling monotonicity, it is the data of a [predication settling](predication-settling.md).
