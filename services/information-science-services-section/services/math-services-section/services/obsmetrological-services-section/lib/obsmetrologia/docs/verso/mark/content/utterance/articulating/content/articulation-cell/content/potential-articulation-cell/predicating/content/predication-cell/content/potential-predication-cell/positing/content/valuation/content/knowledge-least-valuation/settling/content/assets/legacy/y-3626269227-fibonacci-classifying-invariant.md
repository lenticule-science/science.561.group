# Aperiodic classifying invariant of the Fibonacci substitution settling

The corpus's first concrete computation of the [aperiodic classifying invariant](../objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) on a substantive aperiodic case. **L5a** instance computing $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v) \in H^1$ for the [Fibonacci substitution settling](fibonacci-substitution-settling.md).

## Firing-ordinal set

Each substitution step in the Fibonacci settling is a non-Scott-continuous firing — at limit ordinals where the substitution-iteration creates new positions not derivable from any single earlier stage.

For the L5a Fibonacci settling at $E = \omega_1$, the NSC firing ordinals are:

$$L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1) \;=\; \{\mu < \omega_1 : \mu \text{ is a countable limit ordinal where the substitution stage advances}\}.$$

For the standard substitution-stage encoding, $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$ consists of all countable limit ordinals up to $\omega_1$ — uncountably many.

## Classifying invariant

By the [aperiodic-regime cohomology via direct limit theorem](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md):

$$H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \omega_1}, \mathcal{F}_v) \;=\; \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)} \mathbb{Z}.$$

The classifying invariant is:

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}(v_{\text{Fibonacci}}) \;=\; \sum_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)} e_\mu \;\in\; \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\omega_1)} \mathbb{Z}.$$

## Comparison with Anderson-Putnam Fibonacci tiling cohomology

[@anderson-putnam1998]'s computation for the Fibonacci substitution tiling gives Čech cohomology:

$$\check{H}^1(\Omega_\sigma) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}^2$$

(rank 2 — generated by the two tile-types). The corpus's $\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}$ above is uncountable-rank — *much* larger.

The discrepancy is structural: Anderson-Putnam quotients by the substitution's dynamics — two tilings related by substitution-shift are MLD-equivalent. The corpus's classifying invariant *before* settling-shift quotient is uncountable; after quotient by settling-shift (the corpus's natural MLD relation), it should match Anderson-Putnam's $\mathbb{Z}^2$.

The full comparison is the content of [fibonacci-anderson-putnam-comparison](fibonacci-anderson-putnam-comparison.md).

## Step-$n$ refinement at $n = 2$

By the [step-$n$ cohomological rank theorem](../theorems/step-n-cohomological-rank-theorem.md), the post-settling-shift-quotient classifying invariant for the Fibonacci settling (the $n = 2$ instance) is:

$$\mathrm{cl}\text{-}\mathrm{inv}^{(2)}(v_{\text{Fibonacci}}) \;=\; \mathbb{Z}^2,$$

with generators $e_a, e_b$ corresponding to the alphabet $\{a, b\}$ — and via the [step-$n$ / bilattice correspondence](../theorems/step-n-and-bilattice-correspondence.md) at $n = 2$, to the bilattice's two partial-order axes ($\leq_k, \leq_t$). This matches Anderson-Putnam's $\mathbb{Z}^2$. Compare with [padovan-classifying-invariant](padovan-classifying-invariant.md) ($\mathbb{Z}^3$) and [tetranacci-classifying-invariant](tetranacci-classifying-invariant.md) ($\mathbb{Z}^4$).
