### Theorem — The Nuclei as Witnesses to Distinction {#nucleus-aperiodicity}

The proof of part (3) rests on the Axiom of History Separation, which in turn rests on the
fiberwise variation of the nuclear pair $(\sigma_t, \Delta_t)$. Global aperiodicity is not a
geometric hypothesis; it is a consequence of the nuclei varying across fibers in a way that
faithfully encodes the distinct relational history of each history. A relational universe with constant nuclear pair
would satisfy all axioms except the Axiom of History Separation: any automorphism of $T$ would
be invisible to $H^\ast$. The Axiom of History Separation is what converts aperiodicity from an
assumption into a theorem. The saturation nucleus and transfer nucleus are
the internal witnesses that each history is genuinely different from every other, at every scale.
