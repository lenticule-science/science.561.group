\subsection{Dimension estimators}

\begin{theorem}[Diamond ordering fraction $r=5/6$]\label{thm:mm-ordering-fraction}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Dimension.OrderingFraction.diamond_ordering_fraction_val}\leanok\uses{thm:causal-settling-transitive-closure}
For the four-event causal diamond, the Myrheim--Meyer ordering fraction
$r=2|{\prec}|/(N(N-1))$ is exactly $5/6$ in lowest terms
(\texttt{diamond\_ordering\_fraction\_val}).  This is a rational invariant of
the finite causal carrier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Myrheim--Meyer dimension]\label{thm:myrheim-meyer-dimension}\notready\uses{thm:mm-ordering-fraction}
\emph{(Open frontier --- stated, not formalized.)} The dimension estimator is
$d_{MM}=f^{-1}(r)$, where $f(d)$ is the $\Gamma$-function expected ordering
fraction of a $d$-dimensional sprinkling.  \Cref{thm:mm-ordering-fraction}
supplies the rational input $r=5/6$; the missing fiber is constructive reals,
$\Gamma$, its inverse, and the sprinkling measure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Myrheim--Meyer real interpretation]\label{thm:myrheim-meyer-real-bridge}\lean{Obsmetrologia.CausalSets.Dimension.Fingerprint.diamond_myrheim_meyer_real_bridge}\leanok\uses{thm:mm-ordering-fraction}
The rational-to-real part is green: the diamond's ordering fraction embeds in
\texttt{Real.Internal.RReal} as $5/6$.  The proof rebuilds the fraction in the
internal rationals from \texttt{apexCount} and ordered-pair count; the actual
dimension $d_{MM}=f^{-1}(r)$ remains the red fiber of
\cref{thm:myrheim-meyer-dimension}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The dimension of the void]\label{thm:void-dimension}\notready\uses{thm:myrheim-meyer-dimension, thm:mm-ordering-fraction, thm:light-cone, thm:oriented-cell}
\emph{(Open frontier --- stated, not formalized.)} Let the cells be the oriented
cells articulating generates (\cref{thm:oriented-cell}) and let the settling be the
closure generated by predication's own composition rule, from $(e_1,p,e_2)$ and
$(e_2,p,e_3)$ to $(e_1,p,e_3)$. The claim: the void of that settling --- the cells
that remain $\bot$ in both directions --- has spatial dimension three, i.e.\ its
ordering fraction, or the growth of the invariant $\mathbb Z^{V_\infty}$ of the
irreducibly spacelike cells, takes the value the Myrheim--Meyer estimator assigns to
$3{+}1$. Two sources of ``three'' exist in the corpus and are not yet one object:
the arity of the minimal relation (combinatorial and forced: a cell has three
slots) and the icosahedral cut-and-project claim for physical space (geometric,
asserted in prose with three as input). This node is where they would meet.
Promotion: a theorem computing the ordering fraction of the composition-generated
causal order on the oriented cells of articulating traces and matching it to the
$3{+}1$ value; or a counterexample showing the arity three and the geometric
three come apart, which would matter more than either. Missing carrier: the
composition-generated settling over oriented cells as a rule system rather than
a given DAG; the Myrheim--Meyer inverse (\cref{thm:myrheim-meyer-dimension}); the
$V_\infty$ invariant computed on that void. Receiving volume: Book VIII beside
\texttt{DimensionContinuumFrontier}.
\end{theorem}
