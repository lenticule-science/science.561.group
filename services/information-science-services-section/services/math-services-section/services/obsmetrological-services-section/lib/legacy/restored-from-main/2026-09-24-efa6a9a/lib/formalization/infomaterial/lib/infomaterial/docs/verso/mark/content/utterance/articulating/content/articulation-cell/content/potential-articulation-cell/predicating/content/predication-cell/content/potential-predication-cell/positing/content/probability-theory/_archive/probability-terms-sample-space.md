The sample space Ω of a probabilistic experiment is the [set](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/set.md) of all possible outcomes. For a coin flip, Ω = {heads, tails}. For rolling a die, Ω = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. For measuring a temperature, Ω might be the real numbers or some interval.

An [event](./event.md) is a [subset](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/subset.md) of the sample space — a collection of outcomes that may or may not occur. Not every subset need be an event; in general, events form a σ-algebra: a collection of subsets closed under complement, countable [union](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/union.md), and countable [intersection](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/intersection.md), and containing Ω and ∅. A [probability](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/probability.md) measure assigns a number in [0, 1] to each event.

The triple (Ω, F, P) — sample space, σ-algebra of events, probability measure — is a probability space, the formal foundation for probabilistic reasoning. Different sample spaces model different experiments, and the choice of sample space determines what questions can be asked and answered.
