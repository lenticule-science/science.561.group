# OML

The **Primary Support Node Micro-Enterprise** of the Obsmetrological
Services Section.

OML is the team whose work is developing Obsmetrologia as a mathematical
library. Its "customer" is its own frontier pressure — the unproved next
result, not an internal client. OSS derives the services it offers other
MEs through the Internal Market (e.g. proof-checking) from what OML builds.

The library OML develops seats at `../../lib/obsmetrologia`,
inside OSS's canonical mathematical-law holding.
