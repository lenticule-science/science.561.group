# What the cohomology delivers

The [bitopological settling site](../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md) is the natural site on which to do sheaf cohomology over the [dual predication hyperdoctrine](../objects/dual-predication-hyperdoctrine.md). [Settling cohomology](../objects/settling-cohomology.md) — derived global sections of $\mathbf{Ab}$-valued sheaves on this site — produces the invariants the dualized object was constructed to host.

## What is established

- The [bitopological settling site](../objects/bitopological-settling-site.md) as the natural site for the dualized object, with [Grothendieck axioms](../theorems/bitopological-settling-site-axioms.md) and [functoriality](../theorems/bitopological-settling-site-functoriality.md).
- The [settling-associated sheaf](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/apps/agentic-relation-engine/lib/relating/docs/settling-associated-sheaf.md) — central object — and its [paraconsistent variant](../objects/paraconsistent-settling-associated-sheaf.md), with the [inconsistency sheaf](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf.md) tracking $\top$-content.
- [Settling cohomology](../objects/settling-cohomology.md) defined as derived global sections, with [universal vanishing above degree 1](../theorems/settling-cohomology-vanishes-above-degree-1.md) and regime-by-regime structure: [strict-regime](../theorems/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md) and [$\omega$-Scott-regime](../theorems/scott-continuous-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md) full vanishing, [transfinite-bounded $H^1$ generators](../theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md), [aperiodic direct-limit](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-cohomology-via-direct-limit.md).
- The [aperiodic-regime classification theorem](../theorems/aperiodic-regime-classification.md): aperiodic settlings up to [mutually-locally-derivable](../concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md) equivalence are classified by their [aperiodic classifying invariant](../objects/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md) in $H^1$.
- The [CRDT Mayer–Vietoris reformulation](../theorems/crdt-convergence-via-mayer-vietoris.md) — and its [cohomological CALM](../theorems/cohomological-calm.md) extension — reformulating distributed-convergence as $\delta^0$-vanishing.
- The [bigraded two-adjoint cohomology](../objects/bigraded-settling-cohomology.md) unifying truth-order and knowledge-order cohomologies via the [comparison long exact sequence](../theorems/truth-knowledge-cohomology-long-exact-sequence.md).
- [Equivariant settling cohomology](../theorems/equivariant-settling-cohomology.md) under the [internal $\mathbb{Z}/2$-gauge](../concepts/internal-z2-gauge.md) from bilattice negation.

## The structural loop closed

The aperiodic regime — the one where primal fixed-point theorems fail because no countable ordinal stabilizes the settling — turns out to be the regime where cohomological classification has the richest content. The same $H^1$ that is identically zero in the CRDT setting carries discriminating content for aperiodic settlings. The [bitopological site design](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/why-fiber-covers-only.md) is what keeps these two uses of $H^1$ in [disjoint cohomological parts](disjoint-cohomological-parts.md) of the apparatus.

The four-regime dichotomy is mirrored cleanly in cohomology: the strict and $\omega$-Scott regimes are cohomologically trivial; the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes have $H^1$ indexed by [non-Scott-continuous firing ordinals](../concepts/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal.md). The "most intractable" regime is the one where cohomology is most informative.

## What downstream takes from this

The [settling moduli](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/coarse-settling-moduli.md) takes the settling cohomology's invariants as input: the [moduli classifying function](../morphisms/moduli-classifying-function.md) sends an MLD-class to its settling-associated-sheaf isomorphism class, and the [combined cohomology classifying morphism](../morphisms/combined-cohomology-classifying-morphism.md) refines to a stack-level statement. The [realizability program](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/realizability-program.md) characterizes which $H^1$-classes are *realized* by some MLD-class, regime-by-regime and axis-by-axis — completing the moduli classifying function from injectivity to bijectivity onto an explicit image. Moduli points stratified by $H^1$-class are natural objects of further study; infinitesimal deformations correspond to extensions in the relevant derived category; representation-theoretic interpretations classify the moduli's image.
