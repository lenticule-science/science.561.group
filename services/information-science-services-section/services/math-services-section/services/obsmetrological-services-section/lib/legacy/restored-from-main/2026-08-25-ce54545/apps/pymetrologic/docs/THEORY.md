# From theorem to module

This lab is a *realization*: every library module is grounded in a result of the 561-group Lean
library, **Obsmetrologia** (live root: `../../lib/obsmetrologia` relative to this app, i.e.
`/home/emsenn/info/lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia`
— `Obsmetrologia.lean` + `Obsmetrologia/`), or an already-ledgered theorem shadow in the
predication corpus. The table is the contract — if the code and the Lean/corpus source disagree,
the source wins.

> **History.** Obsmetrologia is a reorganized successor to an earlier library, RelMath, not a
> rename: namespaces were renested, several leaves renamed, and a few nodes merged or moved
> (`Cohomology.*` sheaf/site material moved under `Site/`, `Lorentzian` became `CausalSets`,
> `Aperiodic` became `AperiodicOrder`, …). Every citation below has been re-verified against the
> live Obsmetrologia tree (2026-08-17 remap); RelMath is retained only as historical lineage, not
> as a live source. Where a citation names more than one live file, or carries a parenthetical note
> (renamed leaf, merged, moved, split), that is the reorganization showing through, not an error.

Library modules must be Lean-derived.
Notebook-only experiments may be playful, but they stay in `notebooks/` until a real Lean theorem, definition, or frontier lemma earns them a place in `pymetrologic`.

## Why this meets-or-beats PySheaf

PySheaf is the **poset-only, metric, scalar** corner of this object:

* its base (a cell complex) is a `Site` with only specialization morphisms;
* its stalks (metric sets) are the special case where the bilattice collapses to a yes/no indicator — exactly the FOUR encoding in `pymetrologic.calculus.pysheaf_bridge`;
* its **consistency radius** is `max` over our per-value conflict degree `pro ∧_L con`, i.e. one number where we keep the whole `conflict_map`.

So we recover PySheaf's verdict on its own examples (`test_pysheaf_parity.py`, `meet`) and additionally *localize* the inconsistency as `⊤` at the exact cell and value where evidence collides (`beat`) — information the scalar discards.

The genuinely-new structure that comes for free: truth-and-knowledge-aware fibers (graded `Chain` and continuous `[0,1]` regimes), settling dynamics as the solver, and a base that is a full predicate category rather than a mere poset.
