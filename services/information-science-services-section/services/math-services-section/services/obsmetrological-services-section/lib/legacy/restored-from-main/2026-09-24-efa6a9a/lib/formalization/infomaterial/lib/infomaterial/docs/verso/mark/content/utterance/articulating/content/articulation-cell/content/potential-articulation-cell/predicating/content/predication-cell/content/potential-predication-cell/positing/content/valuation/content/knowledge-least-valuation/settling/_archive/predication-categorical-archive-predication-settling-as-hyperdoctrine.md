# predication settlings as bilattice-valued hyperdoctrines

Lawvere (1969, 1970) introduced the *hyperdoctrine* as the adjoint-theoretic skeleton of first-order logic: a functor $P : \mathcal{B}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Cat}$ (or into a specified algebraic category) with each substitution $f^* : P(Y) \to P(X)$ having left and right adjoints $\exists_f \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f$. [@seely1983] systematized the apparatus, connecting it to natural deduction. [@maruyama2021] extended the framework to substructural logics, including four-valued settings.

An predication settling is essentially a bilattice-valued hyperdoctrine over $\mathcal{C}$ parametrized additionally by a [settling bound](../../objects/settling-bound.md). The [predication fiber functor](../../theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md) $F : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{DBiLat}(\mathbf{L})$ is the non-temporal "spatial slice" of the predication settling; the settling axis adds a temporal direction on top of the hyperdoctrinal structure.

The hyperdoctrinal chain $\exists_f \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f$ is established for [predicate specialization](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-specialization.md) by the [specialization hyperdoctrinal adjoints](../../theorems/specialization-hyperdoctrinal-adjoints.md) result: extension by $\bot$ on the left, extension by $\top$ on the right. The other three generators of $\mathcal{C}$ — [converse](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-converse.md), [subject-projection](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-subject-projection.md), [object-projection](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/predicate-object-projection.md) — also admit $\exists_f \dashv f^* \dashv \forall_f$ chains, with formulas distinct from specialization's: converse acts as a self-inverse swap (both adjoints coincide with $f^*$ itself, intertwined with the swap); subject-projection's left adjoint is a row-$\vee_k$-fold onto the diagonal with $\bot$ off-diagonal, with the right adjoint dual; object-projection is dual along the object coordinate.

The uniform treatment of quantifier adjoints across all four generators is established in [Beck–Chevalley commutation on predication bilattices](../../theorems/beck-chevalley-commutation-on-predication-bilattices.md), which discharges the corresponding coherence squares uniformly.

The combined picture: predication settlings form a natural habitat for first-order bilattice logic, with the [twist-product residuation](../../theorems/twist-product-residuation.md) $\odot \dashv \Rightarrow$ pair providing the propositional-connective adjunctions and the hyperdoctrinal $\exists, \forall$ providing the quantifier adjunctions. Predicate-composition, handled via closure constraints rather than as a $\mathcal{C}$-morphism, sits outside the hyperdoctrinal structure; it plays the role of a coherence constraint that closure imposes on composite predicates' valuations.
