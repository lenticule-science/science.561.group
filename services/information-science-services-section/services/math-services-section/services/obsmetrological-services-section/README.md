course on 2026-07-18. The OSS membrane is seated here, published as the private
`emsenn/obsmetrological-services-section` repository, and admitted by the

No absence of an application from Obsmetrologia makes the application invalid,
and no application demand authorizes adding application code to the primary
mathematics package. Applications hire a separately owned OSS service and cross
the mathematical package boundary through an explicit dependency or contract.

## Service portfolio

- **Territory — OML** (`services/oml/`): offers corpus-grounded pure-mathematics
  development and an executable ActivityStreams boundary that verifies exact
  patch bytes, path envelope, application-dependency absence, and required
  scope/build evidence before returning a bundle for Obsmetrologia's own
  admission review.
- **Nation — Hoare Program Carrier / Finite Program Measurement Service**
  (`services/hoare-program-carrier/`, `services/finite-program-measurement-service/`):
  split 2026-07-30 from the former combined `hoare-program-measurement`. The
  first owns the application-specific Lean carrier removed from the primary
  mathematics library; the second is the executable ActivityStreams service
  that measures a supplied finite program relation and provenance-bearing
  customer observations, with qualified results, counterexamples, receipts,
  books, causal journals, and typed refusals.
- **Union — Mathematical Readout Exchange**
  (`services/mathematical-readout-exchange/`): offers a signed A2A 1.0 face
  plus an ActivityStreams contract face with source pinning, conformance
  evidence, local resource readouts, provider receipts and books, causal
  journals, and typed refusal.

The root `portfolio-service-admission` contract is OSS's own border. It issues
content-addressed, capability-bounded referrals to these providers and records
provider-local admissions and books. A referral does not impersonate or invoke
the provider and never writes a customer or child plate.

Each service states what remains unimplemented. In particular, ActivityStreams
over standard input is not ActivityPub transport, a resource readout or
internal reciprocal-value term is not a settled monetary price, customer
observations are not verified merely because they carry source
digests, and paired Lean/JavaScript conformance cases are not a formally
verified extraction.

The former Settlement Assurance wrapper was retired after formation. It had no
external customer and duplicated filesystem classification, planning, and
portfolio checks already exercised by each service's own conformance boundary.
Its exact bytes remain in the section's digest-addressed legacy assets; it is
not an operating service or workspace package.
