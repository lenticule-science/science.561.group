# Math Services Section

The Math Services Section is the Lenticule Science home for mathematical
services and shared mathematical resources. Its first service is the Algebra
Services Section.

The shared computer realization of mathematical transformations is Metrologic, in
the computer data section's `lib/metrologic`; research applications live in Computer Math:
`computer-math-system-services-section/apps/`. The mathematical research
carriers live here, including the Obsmetrologia Lean library and its related
formalization projects under `services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/formalization/`.

## Research

- [Laplacianizability and stratified moduli](content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/laplacianizability-and-stratified-moduli.md)
  surveys Laplacianizability and the stratified moduli of Laplacianizers. The
  work is integrated across the section's glossary, research articles,
  questions, explanations, examples, bibliographies, and computational tools.
- [Predication category theory](content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/surveys/predication-category-theory.md)
  is the mathematical account of bilattice-valued predication structures and
  settling dynamics. Its CRDT convergence results and Lean formalization live
  beside the research article, rather than with Semiotic Computer System
  Engineering's runtime specifications.
