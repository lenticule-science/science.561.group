Aim: a monograph that does for language what a new instrument does for a
science — presents the Witnessed Field theory as a foundation from which the
major twentieth- and twenty-first-century theories are recovered as
projections, and which arrives with its own measurement program and
falsifiers. The posture toward predecessors is engagement, not dismissal:
each is shown to be the best theory available to its instrument.

Working title candidates (owner decides): *Language as Witnessed Field*;
*The Witnessed Field: An Evidential Theory of Language*; a Lakota-rooted
title only if the owner chooses to give one.

## Part and chapter plan

**Part I — The Instrument Problem.**
Ch. 1 *What linguistics never had.* Thesis: linguistics has description,
formalism, and statistics but no metrology; every foundational dispute
(competence/performance, langue/parole, grammaticality, dialect vs language)
is an artifact of instrument poverty. Obligation: a fair history of
measurement attempts (judgment experiments, corpus statistics, sociolinguistic
interviews) and why each lacked fusion laws and provenance.
Ch. 2 *The instrument.* Obsmetrologia for linguists: witnessed observation,
provenance, bilattice charge, belief fusion, sheaf consistency, filtration,
cones. No linguistics yet — the chapter earns the reader's trust in the
machine before any claim about language is made.

**Part II — Foundations.**
Ch. 3 *The witnessed utterance-event* (A1; consent as axiom; contrast with
the sentence, the proposition, the token stream as false primitives).
Ch. 4 *The meme: a quantum of meaning-with-evidence* (A2; substrate
stratification mark→token→articulation→utterance; syntax as ordered binding;
Lambek calculus as the binding algebra; engagement: Peirce, Saussure,
Jakobson).
Ch. 5 *An item is its arrows* (A3; Yoneda arrows-first; meaning as presheaf
of witnessed use; the projection lemma on distributional semantics;
engagement: structuralism's "value", Firth, Harris, vector-space semantics,
LLM interpretability).
Ch. 6 *Settlement and normalization* (A7; FCA closure = settling closure;
Church-Rosser as the possibility of communication; truth-conditional
semantics as classical-closure special case; engagement: Montague, Davidson,
inferentialism/Brandom — normativity by uptake is close kin and must be
credited).

**Part III — The Community.**
Ch. 7 *A language is a gluing that generically fails* (A4; H¹ as the seat of
identity and change; vitality signatures; dialect as computed object;
engagement: Labov, Eckert's third wave, Silverstein on language ideology,
Weinreich–Labov–Herzog on the actuation problem — the theory's answer: change
nucleates at obstructions).
Ch. 8 *Grammar as gauge* (A5; orthography wars as chart confusion;
standardization as forced exactness; engagement: language planning
literature, Haugen, and the politics of standard languages).
Ch. 9 *The extraction theorem* (the witness-forgetting functor; meme grammar,
dead English, embeddings, standardization as one operation at scales;
engagement: the owner's own essays as primary sources; Indigenous data
sovereignty — OCAP and CARE principles — as the constructive requirement;
the LLC episode as case study, handled factually and with community consent).

**Part IV — The Speaker and the Learner.**
Ch. 10 *Kinematics: the martial space* (repertoire as groupoid of composable
moves; forms as derivations; sparring as charge exchange under uptake).
Ch. 11 *Mastery as a commuting cone* (A8 at speaker scale; per-item
leg-commutation; measurement by consistency-across-contexts gluing;
engagement: SLA — Krashen's i+1 recovered as cone edge, Swain's output
hypothesis as production-leg forcing, interaction hypothesis as uptake).
Ch. 12 *Acquisition as cone propagation* (𝓡-computed curricula; interlanguage
as deforming section; transport and obstruction as the learnability theory;
engagement: Selinker, processability theory, usage-based acquisition).

**Part V — The Science Program.**
Ch. 13 *Five instruments of obsmetrological linguistics* (computed dialects;
persistence over filtrations; grammaticality as fused belief; reconstruction
and neologism conformance by cones; curvature typology — with measurement
protocols for each).
Ch. 14 *First apparatus: a living Lakota corpus and a Mandarin learner*
(the two case studies as the theory's laboratory; the learning–documentation
flywheel; kin-weighted witnessing in practice).
Ch. 15 *Obligations and how the theory dies* (the five falsifiers stated as
experiments with power analyses where possible; Research-grade self-grading
as method).

**Part VI — Consequences.**
Ch. 16 *For AI* (what LLMs are and lack, by the projection lemma; what
witness-preserving language machines would be; the engine and fibers as a
reference implementation).
Ch. 17 *For revitalization and policy* (anti-standardization instruments;
community-owned metrology; consent-gated corpora as infrastructure).
Ch. 18 *For learning* (the platform as apparatus; contribution-scored play;
the polyglot game on typology space).

**Appendices.** A: Formal inventory — which claims are proven in the
estate's Lean modules (CurryHowardLambek, YonedaArrowsFirst,
FCALambekCircle, LambekCalculus, CausalSets/Curvature/Lambek) and exactly
what each theorem states, vs which claims are bridge hypotheses. B: The
engine (@lenticule-science/communicating) and fiber contract as reference
implementation, with the maturity-ledger discipline. C: Notation and
glossary. D: Related formal frameworks — Goguen institutions,
Abramsky–Brandenburger contextuality, Belnap/Ginsberg/Fitting bilattices,
Lawvere hyperdoctrines — with precise statements of overlap and difference.

## Writing discipline

Drafts live in this section with provenance frontmatter; every chapter
carries its own research-obligations block and may not claim a grade above
its evidence; formal citations name the Lean theorem and file; empirical
chapters name their instrument runs. Chapter order of writing: 3–4–5 (the
core is the quantum), then 7 and 9 (the community and the critique are the
book's heart), then the rest. Each chapter drafted in its own session
against this plan; the plan is revised by commit whenever a chapter teaches
us it was wrong.

## Standing risks

Scope creep toward proving in prose what only measurement can earn; the
"beat Chomsky" frame curdling into strawmanning (the embedding must be
generous or the book fails); cultural material handled without the owner's
and community's ongoing consent (Part III–V Lakota content passes through
the owner, always); the mathematics outrunning the reader (Part I exists so
Part II never has to apologize).
