# Should illocutionary force be modeled as an endomorphism rather than a stance value?

The current communicating package defines its language faculty as meme endomorphisms and implements illocution as an operation on a charged meme.

Its five force classes do not biject to FOUR. When projected only to the FOUR value, assertive, directive, and commissive all become potential; expressive preserves the input value; declaration becomes stable, while evidence payloads retain further distinctions.

This suggests that force belongs to a transformation algebra acting on communicative state:

[
F \curvearrowright \mathrm{Meme},
]

while epistemic/evidential stance belongs to a value fiber over propositional content.

The open question is whether this separation should become a governing RelConLang design law, and if so what algebraic laws the force operators should satisfy under composition, negation, quotation, embedding, and dialogue uptake.
