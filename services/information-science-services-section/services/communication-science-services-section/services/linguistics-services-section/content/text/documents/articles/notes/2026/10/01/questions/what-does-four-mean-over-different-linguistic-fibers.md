# What does a FOUR reading mean when indexed over different linguistic fibers?

The same four-element carrier currently appears over distinct objects: proposition evidence, parse hypotheses, membrane truth-direction, message state, visibility, and other application-specific cells.

The algebra is shared; the interpretation is not automatically shared.

This question asks for an explicit indexed account of each use:

[
(value, fiber, coordinate\ meaning, aggregation\ law).
]

A satisfactory answer must identify which uses are genuine instances of the same predication semantics, which are lawful representations related by a morphism, and which merely reuse the four labels.

It must also determine whether RelConLang should lexicalize four named values or instead grammaticalize evidence-for and evidence-against compositionally.


## Current RelConLang clue

The manifest itself contains two competing representations.

Its witness journal gives a flat particle-to-charge reading, including `ta → active` and `na → potential`.

Its Chu section instead treats the particles as points, `truth` and `falsity` as states, and assigns a charge to each point–state pair. There, `ta` has a potential reading at the truth state and absurd at the falsity state, while `na` has absurd at truth and potential at falsity.

This suggests the particle may index an orientation or relation whose evaluation yields a charge, rather than simply naming a charge. Whether that is the intended semantics is open.
