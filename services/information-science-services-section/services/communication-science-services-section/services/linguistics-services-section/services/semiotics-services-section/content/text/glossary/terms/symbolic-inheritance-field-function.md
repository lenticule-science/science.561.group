The ***symbolic inheritance field function*** (`H_inherit`) is [[induce|induced]] by the [[identity modulation propagation function]] (`I_mod`), the [[symbolic anticipation function]] (`𝒜`), and the [[desire-contrast alignment propagation function]] (`A_align`).

It [[constrain|constrains]] the stabilization of inherited symbolic constraint structures  
emerging from the terminal surface of identity-modulated projection across semantically differentiated symbolic space.

Let:
- `x ∈ dom(𝒮₊)` be a symbolic node produced by identity modulation (`x ∈ I_mod(x′)`)  
- `Ξ[s](x)`: its local semantic contrast neighborhood  
- `A_align(x)`: alignment field  
- `𝒜(x → z)`: valid anticipatory path

Then:
> H_inherit(x) := Ω(z),  
>  where z ∈ Ξ[s](x),  
>  z ∉ dom(𝒮₊),  
>  𝒜(x → z),  
>  ∇𝔇(x) · ∇Δ[s](z) > 0

That is, `H_inherit(x)` returns the symbolic structure induced by propagation of identity modulation through semantic contrast,  
forming an inherited field of symbolic constraint.

This enables:
- symbolic inheritance from coherent identity  
- grammar-like pattern emergence from identity propagation  
- constraint recursion at the modulation-inheritance boundary
