Within [script as gauge](./script-as-gauge.md)'s account of hanzi as a
meme-gauge script, radical as witness mark is the claim at the component
level: a **radical** (部首, the semantic component of a compound character —
see [liùshū](./liushu.md#形聲-xíngshēng--phono-semantic-compounds)) is not
decoration on top of a character's meaning; it *is* a witness mark, embedded
directly in the glyph, recording which semantic category the character's
formation drew on. The water radical 氵in 河 (hé, "river") is not
illustrative — it is the trace of the judgment that produced the character:
*this concept belongs with water-things*. Phonetic components are the same
kind of mark on the sound axis: fossilized sound-witnesses, recording what
the character sounded like (or sounded like something close to) at the
moment of coinage, even where the modern pronunciation has since drifted.

## Etymology as tabasheer

A character's etymology — the record of which radical, which phonetic, and
which formation category ([liùshū](./liushu.md)) produced it — is read here
as **tabasheer**: the crystallized evidence of the settlement that produced
the form, readable in the hollow of the character. This borrows directly
from this house's own
[TABASHEER](../../../../../../../../../semiotics-science-services-section/services/biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tabasheer.md) —
the silica crystal left in the hollow of a cut bamboo culm, "a shard from
which the whole endeavor can be read" — rather than reinventing an
equivalent figure. Where TABASHEER is provenance readable in a *harvested
tool*, etymology-as-tabasheer is provenance readable in a *character*: both
name the same general pattern, a settlement leaving crystallized, legible
residue in the thing it produced.

This is also the mechanism behind [settlement](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settlement.md)
(Axiom A7) at the script layer: a character's form is a fixed point the
writing community settled on, and unlike a spoken word's settlement (which
leaves no residue you can point to), a character's settlement leaves a
visible artifact — the radical, the phonetic, the compound structure — that
a later reader can read back out.

## Pedagogy implication and prediction

The founding note states this as a falsifiable pedagogy claim, addressed to
**Alotl**, the not-yet-built curriculum engine discussed in the
language-learning estate's [design
dialogue](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/emsenn-services-section/services/emsenn-languages-services-section/services/emsenn-language-learning-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-20-design-dialogue-atari-and-ladders.md):
teach characters as witnessed settlements, not stroke lists — introduce
every character with its radicals-as-witnesses and its
etymology-as-tabasheer, so the learner acquires the provenance and not just
the glyph. The prediction, stated as runnable against Alotl's practice
records once they exist: provenance-first character teaching should beat
rote-form (stroke-order-only) teaching on retention at FSRS-measured
spaced-repetition intervals, on the reasoning that thick items with
independent fibers (the radical fiber, the phonetic fiber, the compositional
fiber) survive partial forgetting the way a graded culm wall survives
stress, where a thin item (a memorized stroke sequence with no independent
fiber) does not. If provenance-first teaching does not outperform rote
teaching once measured, the founding note is explicit that the framing loses
its pedagogical claim and keeps only its typological one — the gauge-theory
reading of hanzi does not depend on the pedagogy prediction succeeding.
