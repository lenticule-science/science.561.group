# Writing systems

Writing-systems (orthography) science: the study of scripts as such — the
graphic layer that a language's utterances get coordinated onto, distinct
from the phonology, morphology, or semantics of the language itself.

This section was stood up 2026-08-21 to hold a specific claim rather than a
general survey: that scripts are [gauge
choices](../terms/script-as-gauge.md) on the witnessed field in the same
sense that [grammars are gauges](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/grammar-as-gauge.md)
(Axiom A5 of the [Witnessed Field working
theory](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-20-language-as-witnessed-field-working-theory.md)) —
and that the great typological divide between alphabets and logographic
scripts is a choice of *which stratum* a script coordinates: sound
([alphabet as phoneme-gauge](../terms/alphabet-as-phoneme-gauge.md)) or
meaning ([hanzi as meme-gauge](../terms/hanzi.md)). The founding statement
for this claim, written before this section existed to hold it, is [Hanzi as
Jade Script](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-hanzi-as-jade-script.md) —
that note is not duplicated here; the terms below elaborate pieces of its
argument and are meant to be read alongside it, not instead of it.
