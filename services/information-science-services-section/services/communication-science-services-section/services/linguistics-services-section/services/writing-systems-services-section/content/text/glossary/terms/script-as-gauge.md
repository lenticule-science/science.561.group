[Grammar as gauge](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/grammar-as-gauge.md)
(Axiom A5) treats grammars, dictionaries, and orthographies together as
coordinate charts on the field of witnessed use. Script as gauge names the
narrower claim inside that axiom that this section exists to hold: a
script — the graphic system a language's writing is coordinated onto — is
itself a gauge choice, and the question a script answers is not "how much
information does this chart carry" but *which stratum of the field it
coordinates*.

**Alphabets fix the phoneme gauge**: thin coordinates on the sound layer.
An alphabetic letter carries almost no information beyond "make roughly
this sound here" — it is cheap to learn, fast to write, and largely mute
about meaning (English spelling's partial phonemic decay is a separate,
further fact about how loosely even a phoneme-gauge chart can drift from
its territory over time).

**Hanzi fix the meme gauge**: each character coordinates a
[charge-cell](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/obsmetrological-linguistics.md)
— a unit of meaning — directly, and, the decisive property, carries its own
witnesses visibly in the glyph. See [hanzi](./hanzi.md) for the full claim
and [radical as witness mark](./radical-as-witness-mark.md) for the
component-level mechanism.

## A namespace warning, stated explicitly

"Meme" here is **not** this house's pre-existing critical-linguistics term
[meme grammar](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/meme-grammar.md)
— emsenn's 2025 term for platform vernacular that borrows AAVE and
diasporic-creole grammatical forms while discarding their histories and
obligations. That "meme" is Internet-meme, format-and-circulation. This
"meme-gauge" is Dawkins-adjacent: a meme as a minimal replicating unit of
cultural charge, the thing a hanzi character coordinates. The two senses
share a word and nothing else, and this note follows the estate's own
precedent — set by [language light
cone](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/language-light-cone.md)'s
disambiguation from [commuting-cone
mastery](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commuting-cone-mastery.md) —
of naming a same-word collision directly rather than letting a reader
conflate them.

## The projection reading of romanization

Every romanization of hanzi (pinyin included) is a lossy re-gauging: a
projection from the meme gauge back down onto a phoneme gauge, which is why
it is useful for the same reason distributional embeddings are useful and
lossy for the same reason too. This is the [arrow-bundle
meaning](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/arrow-bundle-meaning.md)
axiom's "projection lemma" applied one level down: pinyin keeps the shape of
the character's sound-coordinate while discarding the visible witness
structure (radicals, phonetic components, etymology) that the character
itself carries. The general operation — transporting content while
forgetting witnesses — is the [extraction
theorem](../../../../../obsmetrological-linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/extraction-theorem.md).
Pinyin is not therefore illegitimate; it is a chart chosen for a different
convenience (typing, sorting, teaching sound to non-readers), the same way
any gauge-fixing is a legitimate choice that is not the territory.

## Status

This is a framing extension of an already Research-grade axiom, argued by
analogy rather than by new formal citation; it inherits the source axiom's
status and its Obligations rather than adding independent ones. No claim is
made that "meme-gauge" is an established term of art in linguistics outside
this estate's own theory.
