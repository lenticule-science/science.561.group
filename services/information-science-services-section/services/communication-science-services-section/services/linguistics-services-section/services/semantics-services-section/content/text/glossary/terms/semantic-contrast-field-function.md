The ***semantic contrast field function*** (`Ξ[s]`) is [[induce|induced]] by the [[semantic differentiation function]] (`Δ[s]`) and constructs a [[field structure]] over a stabilized constraint surface, returning the local set of semantically distinct points near a reference point `x`.

Let:
- `Ω` be the [[completed constraint function]]  
- `Δ[s](x, x′)` indicate constraint-stabilized but non-collapsed difference  
- `ε` be a local modulation neighborhood radius

Then:
> Ξ[s](x) := { x′ ∈ B(x, ε) | Δ[s](x, x′) = true }

Where `B(x, ε)` is the constraint domain ball of radius ε around `x`.

That is, `Ξ[s](x)` returns the set of all nearby points within `ε`  
that are stabilized by `Ω` but remain semantically distinct under constraint.

This enables:
- field-local evaluation of unresolved contrast  
- accumulation of difference pressure  
- constraint-responsive motion and transformation

`Ξ[s]` is the semantic foundation of ***field-local identity divergence***.
