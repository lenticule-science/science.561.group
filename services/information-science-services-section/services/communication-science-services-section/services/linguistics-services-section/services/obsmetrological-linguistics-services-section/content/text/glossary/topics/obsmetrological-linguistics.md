# Obsmetrological linguistics

Obsmetrological linguistics is the reading of language through obsmetrologia
— the 561 Group's Lean 4 / pymetrologic instrument for measurement with
provenance, fusion laws, consistency conditions, and error budgets — taken as
ontologically serious rather than as a metaphor borrowed from physics.

Its founding statement is the [Witnessed Field
theory](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-20-language-as-witnessed-field-working-theory.md),
which replaces the sentence/proposition/token as base unit with the witnessed
utterance-event and derives from it a community cohomology, a gauge theory of
grammar, causal cones over acquisition and change, and a settlement account
of correctness. Five instruments follow from the theory, described in [The
Game, the Flywheel, and the Five
Instruments](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/emsenn-services-section/services/emsenn-languages-services-section/services/emsenn-language-learning-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-21-the-game-the-flywheel-and-the-five-instruments.md)
§6: dialect as computed object, semantic change as persistence,
grammaticality as fused belief, neologism conformance, and curvature
typology.

The theory is self-graded Research-grade throughout — a working axiom set
with a stated falsification program, not a settled result. See the theory
note's Obligations section for what would confirm or break each claim.

**Status:** seeded 2026-08-21 with terms drawn from the theory note's axioms
and the five-instruments note; the field itself (corpora, computed dialects,
measured H¹) does not yet exist and every term below says so where it
matters.
