The **illocutionary act** is Austin's (1962) second component of a
[speech act](speech-act.md): the force with which something is said —
asserting, promising, warning, christening — as opposed to what is said
(locution) or what results from having said it (perlocution). Searle
(1969) develops Austin's illocutionary-force indicators and felicity
conditions into a systematic account of which conditions a context must
satisfy for an illocutionary act of a given type to succeed.

## In Obsmetrologia

The factorization theorem
([`predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md))
identifies illocution with the **positing morphism**: "The illocution
factor applies the positing morphism $\mathrm{posit}_{(\phi,q)}$ at
the selected cell, injecting datum $q$." From the theorem's own
pre-unit reading, illocution is preparation for closure, not closure
itself: "The illocution $\mathrm{posit}_{(\phi,q)}$ prepares the
settling for closure; the perlocution $\eta$ is the monad unit that
closes it."

The four illocutionary force classes are fixed by the Belnap truth-order
partition of the positing datum $q \in \mathbf{FOUR}$ (legacy
[`speech-act.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/acting/speaking/_archive/speech-act.md)):
abstaining ($\bot$, no positing morphism), asserting ($\mathbf{t}$,
assertive positing morphism), denying ($\mathbf{f}$, denial positing
morphism), contesting ($\top$, contestation positing morphism) — the
same four the 2026-09-03 witnessed-field note
([`2026-09-03-note-on-the-561-group-as-a-witnessed-field.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/03/2026-09-03-note-on-the-561-group-as-a-witnessed-field.md))
names as the house's charges: "The four illocutionary forces are the four
charges: assert, deny, abstain, contest."

Under $K_4$, illocution is the one factor genuinely moved, though
lawfully: the stratification theorem
([`predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md))
proves illocution "$K_4$-equivariantly non-trivial" — each generator of
$K_4$ permutes the Belnap class of the speech act (negation $\sim$
swaps asserting and denying; conflation $-$ swaps abstaining and
contesting) without leaving the factorization.

As a Barwise–Seligman channel-theoretic object [@barwise-seligman1997]
(status proven,
[`from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md)),
an assertive positing morphism is "the **minimal** [incidence-extension]
extension" between truth-threshold classifications — it "adds exactly one
new incidence to the classification, and the identity maps on types and
tokens are the simplest possible infomorphism data." Denial and
contestation positing do not extend truth-threshold incidence at all and
are "visible in the graded generalization" — the full-bilattice reading —
rather than the binary one.
