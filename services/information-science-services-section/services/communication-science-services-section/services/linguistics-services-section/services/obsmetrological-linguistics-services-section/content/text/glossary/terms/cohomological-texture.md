Cohomological texture names the quantity Axiom A4 claims a living language
has and no predecessor theory could measure: a nonzero first cohomology
(`H¹`) over the site of a speech community, where speakers and
speech-situations hold local sections of the field and a language is the
maximal glued object over that site. `H¹ ≠ 0` means irreducible obstructions
to gluing exist — places where two communities' local sections cannot be
consistently reconciled into one global account. The theory's claim is that
these obstructions are not noise to be averaged away; they are where change
nucleates and where identity lives. Losing them is losing the language, not
tidying it.

This gives standardization a precise reading: it is the forced projection
`H¹ → 0`. A standard grammar or orthography works by picking one section as
authoritative and quotienting away the disagreements between it and every
other section. The theory's account of why this is experienced as erasure,
rather than as a neutral simplification, is that the deleted cohomology is
exactly what carried the community's distinctions of identity — the forced
projection does not just simplify description, it discards the measurable
structure the theory identifies with vitality (see [vitality
signature](./vitality-signature.md)).

A local mechanism is offered via Axiom A2's paraconsistent charge structure:
a form witnessed as correct ("pro") by one section of the community and
incorrect ("con") by another is a meme stably charged on both sides at once,
and it is exactly these irreconcilable cross-section charge assignments that
realize an obstruction class. The instrument that would compute this
directly on a real corpus is [computed dialect](./computed-dialect.md).

## Status

`H¹ ≠ 0` for a living language is stated as an empirical founding claim of
the theory, explicitly Research-grade and named as the theory's own
falsification obligation #1: a healthy speech community should show nonzero,
shrinking `H¹` under documented standardization pressure. It has not yet been
measured on any real corpus; the machinery cited (pymetrologic's sheaf
parity, filtration, and integral cohomology extension) computes the formal
object, not yet a linguistic finding.
