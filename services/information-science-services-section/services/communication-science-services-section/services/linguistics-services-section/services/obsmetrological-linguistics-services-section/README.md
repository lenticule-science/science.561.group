# Obsmetrological Linguistics Services Section

This section carries the house's own theory of language as an
obsmetrologic object: the [witnessed utterance-event](content/text/glossary/terms/witnessed-utterance-event.md)
as language's primitive unit in place of the sentence, the proposition,
or the token, and the axiom set built on it — [grammar as
gauge](content/text/glossary/terms/grammar-as-gauge.md),
[settlement](content/text/glossary/terms/settlement.md), and their
siblings — that reads a speech community's correctness as a fixed
point of its own closure operator rather than a fixed rulebook.

Its speech-act vocabulary threads the house's predicated-computing
apparatus back to Austin and Searle: a settling step's own
[locution/illocution/perlocution](content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/explanations/the-speech-act-factoring-of-a-settling-step.md)
factorization is a proven categorical decomposition, not a metaphor, and
[uptake](content/text/glossary/terms/uptake.md) — Austin's own fourth
notion, missing from that factorization's deterministic closure reading
of perlocution — is where the section's speech-act glossary currently
opens onto the rest of the corpus: the act carrier's SatShadow reading,
LAGOON's receiver disposition, incumbency's recognition predicate, and
the legacy acknowledgement cell all hold a place for it without yet
sharing its name. The 2026-09-03 witnessed-field synthesis
([`2026-09-03-note-on-the-561-group-as-a-witnessed-field.md`](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/03/2026-09-03-note-on-the-561-group-as-a-witnessed-field.md),
section 1) calls this section's territory "the communication glossary."

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/text/`:
`glossary/terms/` for the ruled vocabulary, and
`documents/articles/` for dated notes, teaching-article explanations,
and bibliography carriers.
