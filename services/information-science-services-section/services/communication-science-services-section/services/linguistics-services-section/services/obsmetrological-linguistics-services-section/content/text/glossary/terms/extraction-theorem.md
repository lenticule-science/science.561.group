The extraction theorem is the Witnessed Field theory's claim that the
house's existing critical linguistics — the essay "Marked Speech, Borrowed
Grammar" and its glossary entries for [meme
grammar](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/meme-grammar.md),
[dead English](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/dead-english.md),
and [linguistic
extraction](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/linguistic-extraction.md)
— formalizes *inside* the theory as a theorem, rather than sitting beside it
as an unrelated critical framework. This entry states the formal reading and
deliberately does not redefine those three terms; see them directly for
the critical-linguistics content itself.

The formal object is a functor, informally named here the
witness-forgetting functor: it transports a [meme's bilattice
charge](./arrow-bundle-meaning.md) forward while forgetting the witness
Marks attached to it. Charge without witnesses is bottom in the theory's
information order — present as a value but stripped of the provenance that
would let anyone check who attested it, under what consent, with what
authority.

On this reading, four things that look different at the surface are one
operation at different scales:

- **Platform meme grammar** — affective charge simulated over an empty
  witness set; the grammatical form survives, the relational obligations
  that originally carried it do not.
- **Dead English** — charge collapse; grammatical form persists while the
  agency that would let a charge be traced to a responsible speaker is
  voided.
- **Distributional embeddings** — the [projection lemma](./arrow-bundle-meaning.md)
  reading of A3: an embedding keeps the shape of an item's arrow-bundle while
  discarding who witnessed each arrow.
- **Colonial standardization** — [cohomological texture](./cohomological-texture.md)'s
  forced projection `H¹ → 0`, read here as extraction at community scale:
  the sections that disagreed with the standard are not merely
  simplified away, their witnesses are forgotten.

The theory's proposed constructive negation is a kin-witnessed corpus-and-
learning system: machinery built to only move charges together with their
witnesses, so that extraction in this sense becomes structurally
unavailable rather than merely discouraged.

## Status

This entry states a formal reading proposed by a Research-grade theory note;
it does not certify that meme grammar, dead English, distributional
embeddings, and colonial standardization are *proven* to be one operation —
only that the theory names them as such and offers a shared formal
description. The underlying critical-linguistics claims (what meme grammar
or dead English *is*, descriptively) carry whatever grading their own
glossary entries state, unchanged by this entry.
