The **locutionary act** is Austin's (1962) first component of a
[speech act](speech-act.md): the act of saying something — uttering
words with a certain sense and reference. It is what was said, prior
to any question of what was done in saying it (the illocutionary act)
or what it brought about (the perlocutionary act).

## In Obsmetrologia

The house factorization theorem
([`predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md),
status sketch) identifies locution with **cell selection**: "The locution
factor selects $\phi$ as the target cell (without yet changing any
settling value)." Formally it is the forgetful functor
$\pi : \mathbf{PS}_\Phi \to \Phi$, "the locution projection: it
extracts which cell is being targeted," and the theorem draws the Austin
consequence directly: "the locution is *not* itself an illocutionary act:
it is the prior selection of the semantic domain (which cell) in which
the illocutionary act will take place."

Under the Klein-four bilattice symmetry, locution is the one factor left
untouched absolutely. The stratification theorem
([`predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md),
status sketch) proves "Locution is $K_4$-equivariantly trivial... The
$K_4$-action commutes with $\pi$ as the identity on the codomain" —
$K_4$ acts cellwise on posited values and never touches the cell index
itself.

Read as Barwise–Seligman channel theory
([`from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md),
status proven), locution is "Selection of the type $\phi \in
\mathcal{C}$ — the channel entry point": the choice of predicate the
illocutionary act will act on, prior to any incidence being asserted at
it.
