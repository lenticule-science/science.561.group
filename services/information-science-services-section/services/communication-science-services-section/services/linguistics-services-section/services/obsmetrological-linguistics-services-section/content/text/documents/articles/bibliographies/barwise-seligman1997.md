# Barwise & Seligman 1997 — Information Flow: The Logic of Distributed Systems

*Information Flow: The Logic of Distributed Systems*. Cambridge Tracts
in Theoretical Computer Science 44. Cambridge: Cambridge University
Press, 1997.

## Why this is central

Barwise and Seligman give a general mathematical theory of how
information flows through distributed systems: a **classification** is
a set of tokens, a set of types, and an incidence relation between them;
an **infomorphism** \$f : A \rightleftarrows B\$ between classifications
is a pair of maps in opposite directions — forward on types
(\$f^\wedge\$) and backward on tokens (\$f_\vee\$) — satisfying a
fundamental adjointness property relating incidence in \$A\$ to incidence
in \$B\$; and a **channel** connects classifications through a shared
core, carrying a **theory** (a set of regularities the channel's
tokens obey) that supports information flow between otherwise separate
systems. The house's proven theorem
([\`from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md\`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/from-predicate-category-channel-theoretic-speech-acts.md))
reads the house's own [illocution](../../../glossary/terms/illocution.md)
as a minimal incidence-extension infomorphism and
[perlocution](../../../glossary/terms/perlocution.md) as information
flow through a closure channel, giving the house's speech-act
factorization a second, independently-checkable formal grounding beyond
its native category-theoretic one.

## What it does not settle

This book's classification/infomorphism apparatus is general-purpose
information-flow theory; it does not itself address speech acts,
illocutionary force, or uptake — the house's own theorem is what
establishes the correspondence, and it establishes the correspondence
only for the binary and graded incidence readings the theorem states, not
for the fourth, uptake, factor the house's factorization altogether
lacks. Whether an infomorphism's backward token map is the formal home of
acknowledgement, as raised on the house's
[uptake](../../../glossary/terms/uptake.md) term, is this corpus's own
open conjecture, not a claim this book makes.
