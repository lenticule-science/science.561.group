Vitality signature is the corollary the theory draws from [cohomological
texture](./cohomological-texture.md): if `H¹ ≠ 0` for a living language
marks irreducible, identity-carrying gluing obstructions, then a language's
vitality is not merely a qualitative judgment (speaker counts, domains of
use, intergenerational transmission) but has a measurable cohomological
signature — a shape that `H¹` takes over time, and specifically over time
under standardization pressure.

The theory states this precisely as its own first obligation to confirm:
measured `H¹` over a healthy speech community should be nonzero, and it
should shrink under documented standardization pressure. This is offered as
the falsifier of the whole cohomological-texture apparatus, not as an
already-observed result — the source note names the Lakota living corpus and
dialect continuum as the first apparatus that could take this measurement.

The significance claimed for this corollary, if it holds, is that it would
give language-vitality assessment — currently a matter of survey counts and
qualitative indices — an actual measurable quantity tied to the same
mathematics used everywhere else in the theory, rather than a separate,
ad hoc metric bolted onto it.

## Status

Explicitly unconfirmed. This is falsification obligation #1 in the theory
note's own accounting; no measurement has been taken. Treat "vitality has a
cohomological signature" as a Research-grade hypothesis with a named test,
not as an established diagnostic.
