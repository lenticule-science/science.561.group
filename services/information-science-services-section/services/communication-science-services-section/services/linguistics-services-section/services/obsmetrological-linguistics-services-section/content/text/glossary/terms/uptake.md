**Uptake** is Austin's (1962) own fourth notion, distinct from
[locution](locution.md), [illocution](illocution.md), and
[perlocution](perlocution.md), though introduced inside his discussion
of illocutionary force rather than as a fourth named "-locutionary" act.
In Lecture IX, Austin holds that the performance of an illocutionary act
requires "the securing of uptake": the hearer must understand, "to a
certain extent," the meaning and the force of the locution. Uptake is
the hearer's grasp, and Austin makes its absence a reason an
illocutionary act can *misfire* — fail to be done at all — even when
locution and the speaker's intended force are otherwise in order. Austin
distinguishes securing uptake from two further, separable things an
illocutionary act may or may not do: "taking effect" (bringing about
states of affairs, as a will or a christening does) and "inviting a
response" (as a question or an offer does). Of the three, uptake is the
one Austin makes a condition on the act's very having-been-done, not
merely on its further consequences.

Cavell (1969), "Knowing and Acknowledging" (in *Must We Mean What We
Say?*), sharpens the philosophical stakes of a hearer's response by way
of a different case — one person's suffering witnessed by another — but
the sharpening transfers directly to uptake: acknowledging is not a
further piece of knowledge added on top of knowing, and its absence is
not an epistemic gap to be closed by more accurate information.
Acknowledging is a response *owed* — a distinct act the situation calls
for, not reducible to and not repaired by knowing more. Read onto
uptake: securing uptake is not the hearer accumulating more correct
information about what was said; it is the hearer doing something —
registering, in a way that can itself be witnessed — that the speaker's
act called for.

## In Obsmetrologia

Uptake is the factor missing from the house's own three-factor
speech-act apparatus (inference, this carrier — no cited source names
the gap this way). The factorization theorem
([`predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/from-predicate-category/predication-locution-illocution-perlocution-factorization.md))
states its own idealization by name: perlocution is "rule-governed and
predictable — no unintended effects," a deliberate simplification the
theorem itself calls "perlocutionary determinism," stated as applicable
"to formal systems... where the inference rules are fixed and
mechanistic." Collapsing the receiving side of a speech act into a
deterministic closure operator is exactly what erases uptake: closure
has no author and needs none, where Austin's securing of uptake is
irreducibly an act performed *by someone other than the utterer* — the
hearer.

Four house carriers already hold a place for something like uptake
without using the word:

- The relationality triage's act carrier
  ([`act.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/triage/act.md)) puts $\varphi$'s
  $\mathrm{SatShadow}_t$ reading on the record as the Austin-named
  case: "$\mathrm{SatShadow}_t$ \| **Recognized-but-uncommitted**: the
  act is backward-acknowledged ($\sigma_t(\varphi) = \varphi$) but
  $\varphi$ is not yet forward-stable; the act has illocutionary
  uptake but the perlocutionary commitment is pending," glossed further:
  "SatShadow corresponds to the gap between illocution and perlocution:

Read together (inference, this carrier): uptake is an agentive act in
the act carrier's own sense — a four-tuple $(A, \alpha, t, \varphi)$
with its own agent $A$ distinct from the utterer, its own act-type
(acknowledging, recognizing), and its own output — sitting on the
$\mathrm{SatShadow}$ / backward-acknowledged ($\sigma$) side of the
nuclear pair, rather than folded into the $\Delta$-side deterministic
closure that perlocution already occupies. It is
[recognition](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recognition.md)
in that glossary's structural sense only where the acknowledger applies
the system's own category vocabulary to the utterance; that term's own
"Distinct from acknowledgment" section holds ordinary inter-subjective
acknowledgment apart from structural recognition, and acknowledgement is
the plainer word for what uptake, in general, secures.

## Open

Whether uptake is $K_4$-invariant, $K_4$-equivariant, or
conditionally equivariant like perlocution is open: the stratification
theorem
([`predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/from-predicate-category/predication-klein-four-stratifies-locution-illocution-perlocution.md))
proves the regime for locution, illocution, and perlocution, but treats
no fourth factor, because the factorization it stratifies has only
three. Extending the theorem to a four-factor
locution/illocution/uptake/perlocution decomposition, and proving or
refuting uptake's own $K_4$ regime, is unproved.
