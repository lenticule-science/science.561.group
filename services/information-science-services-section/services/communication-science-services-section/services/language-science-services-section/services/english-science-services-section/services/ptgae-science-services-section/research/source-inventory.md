[Plain Writing Act of 2010](https://www.congress.gov/111/plaws/publ274/PLAW-111publ274.pdf) provides distinct statutory scope. Federal documents are not a blanket license to redistribute every embedded third-party artifact.
- **External standards:** [ISO 24495-1:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html), [ISO 24495-2:2025](https://www.iso.org/standard/85774.html), [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14/) and [BCP 47](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp47/) are reference candidates.
