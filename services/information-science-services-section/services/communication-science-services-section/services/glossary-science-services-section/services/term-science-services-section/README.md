# Term science

This section studies the glossary or terminological term: a designation that a
community uses for a concept in a bounded domain. Its research spine joins
terminology and concept theory with documentation theory, then uses formal
concept analysis and formal-language theory as upstream comparisons without
identifying their different senses of *term* or *concept*.

The canonical human-facing definition is
[`content/text/glossary/terms/term.md`](content/text/glossary/terms/term.md).
The science-level record specification is
[`data/records/schema/glossary/terms/term.schema.json`](data/records/schema/glossary/terms/term.schema.json).
The dated synthesis explains the distinctions and their evidence:
[`2026-09-01-note-on-terminological-and-formal-terms.md`](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/01/2026-09-01-note-on-terminological-and-formal-terms.md).

Executable admission, rendering, writing, and indexing belong downstream in
the term-engineering service. Formal terms in lambda calculus, logic, and
universal algebra remain mathematical objects at the mathematics science seat.
