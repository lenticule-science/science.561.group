# Glossary science

Glossary science studies bounded vocabularies used by a community to make its
concepts findable and discussable. It distinguishes the concepts under study,
their designations, and the records that carry definitions; it does not own the
software that parses, renders, indexes, or validates those records.

The narrower science of a glossary term lives in
[`term-science-services-section`](services/term-science-services-section/).
