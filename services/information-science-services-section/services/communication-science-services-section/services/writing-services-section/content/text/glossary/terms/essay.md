An ***essay*** is (definition:: an artifact that results from [writing](../_index.md), usually a few hundred to several thousand words, usually focused on one or just a few topics.)

Here's a listing of various types of essays:
| File                                            | definition                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| reflective essay       | \-                                                         |
| [argumentative essay](./argumentative-essay.md) | an [essay](./essay.md) for arguing a truth about a [thing](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md) |

{ .block-language-dataview}


- Expository essays are for informing people about a concept or topic.
- Descriptive essays are for describing a [thing](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md), often focusing on one [quality](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/quality.md) or set of qualities.
- Narrative essays are for narrative storytelling.
- [Argumentative essays](./argumentative-essay.md) are for arguing a truth about a [thing](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md).
- Analytical essays are for [relating](../../slop/relationality/ai-generated-relationality/relationality-slop-2026-03-03/terms/index.md) a concept or topic to [events](../../philosophy/terms/event.md) or [information](../../../../science/domains/information/_index.md).
- Comparative essays are for looking at what is similar and different between two or more [things](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md).
- Critical essays apply a concept or synthesis of concepts to events or information.
- Reflective essays explore my relationship to a [thing](../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md).
