A short curriculum for reading poetry well, in roughly the order a new reader should take them.

1. [The Line and the Break](./the-line-and-the-break.md) — why the line, not the sentence, is poetry's basic unit.
2. [What Is Metrical Poetry](./what-is-metrical-poetry.md) — how a metrical poem keeps a bargain with the reader's ear, and what distinguishes it from free verse.
3. [Sound and Meter](./sound-and-meter.md) — the toolkit of feet, substitutions, and sonic devices.
4. [Fixed Forms and the Sonnet](./fixed-forms-and-the-sonnet.md) — how meter combines with rhyme scheme and stanza pattern, with the sonnet as the worked example.
