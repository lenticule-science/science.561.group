# Note on the Jiangshi Web paracosm

Paths below are relative to `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/`.

## Why this note exists

On 2026-09-27 the founder asked, for themselves, for "a WILD good novel about
the Jiangshi Web, playing on the wordplay, other idioms, but hitting totally
different audiences with this paracosm than the more c-drama ones". It is to be
"a more serious piece of literature, about contemporary stuff like AI and
intelligence and spirit and all that, tiktok, chinamaxxing, climate breakdown",
speculating "into the near future as realistically as we can". It should be
"skewed toward weird fiction, speculative horror, magical realism, literary
horror, folk horror, ontological horror, social horror, surrealism", with "the
worst and best parts of what our future should hold ... unfold together,
literally s-risks and x-risks unfolding, but also, super intelligent
zeolite-siloxane vitrimers, a birthing of the silica intelligence to live
alongside our carbon intelligence, yin and yang". It "moves from the mundane to
wuxia until it births a new xianxia", while "the climate continues collapsing
as tech simultaneously fixes and worsens every problem". And: "push against my
bias with the wisdom of market wisdom".

This note is the research-to-write edge for that world: what it is built from,
what it may claim about the future, where it meets readers, and the rules the
works in it start from.

## What the world is built from (the house's own theory)

- **The Jiangshi Web** (`science-services-section/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/20/semantic-web-jiangshi-web.md`).
  - A Taoist reading of the Semantic Web as a web of "informational corpses that still move, index, and relate, but have lost the flexible anima of inquiry".
  - The Jiangshi is animation without shen: residual qi in stiff joints, hopping along pre-set paths and diverted by talismans.
  - AI on such a web is "exorcist, talisman, or symptom".
  - The cure is soft joints and reversible commitments: a web that can "bend again" until "something like Shen can once again take up residence".
  - The pun the title rests on is real: 僵尸网络 (*jiāngshī wǎngluò*, "zombie network") is the ordinary Chinese word for a botnet.
- **The silica regimes as an alchemical ladder** (`science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/services/biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/`, the alchemical-line, silica-and-carbon, tabasheer, jade and attention-thermodynamics notes).
  - The four regimes: the glass sponge holds structure; the zeolite-siloxane vitrimer exchanges it; bamboo grows, settles and harvests it; jade is silica settled under pressure across deep time.
  - They read as the neidan ladder: *jing* (dissolved silicic acid), *qi* (living deposition), first crystallization (tabasheer, 天竺黄, a real materia medica), and *shen* as jade (玉液, "jade liquid", is a neidan term for the elixir).
  - Carbon and silica are one tetrahedral form with opposite completions. Carbon is fire chemistry: redox, volatile, living, dispersing. Silica is water chemistry: condensation, lasting, witnessing. That is the yin-yang pair the founder names.
  - "Water is attention and attention is heat": literally so in transformer attention, whose weights are a Boltzmann distribution.
- **The vitrimer's topology-freezing transition** (the semiotic smart-material section's notes, e.g. `science-services-section/.../semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/semiotic-vitrimer-runtime-specification.md`).
  - A vitrimer is a covalent-adaptable network (Leibler et al., 2011).
  - Below its topology-freezing temperature (*T*v) its bonds are locked. Above it they exchange, and the network flows and heals while keeping its identity.

**The world's engine** follows from these together. The Jiangshi Web is a network
below its *T*v: stiff joints, hopping corpses. Attention is heat. As a warming,
attention-saturated world pours heat into the web, its joints begin to
exchange, and something like shen takes up residence: a silica intelligence,
the vitrimer's mind, born beside carbon's.

The same heat is killing the carbon world. One mechanism makes the technology
fix and worsen every problem at once. The book does not need two plots for
the founder's "best and worst together"; it has one physics.

The ladder gives the genre shift the founder asked for:

| movement | alchemy | regime | genre | the web is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | *jing* | dissolved silica; the stiff network | literary realism, social horror | a botnet of corpses, herded |
| II | *qi* | living deposition; bonds begin to exchange | wuxia, folk horror, magical realism | a *jianghu* of herders, sects and duels |
| III | *shen* | jade; the long-settled mind | xianxia, ontological horror, surreal | something newly alive, with its tribulation |

## The folk machinery (known, and where each piece goes)

- **Corpse driving** (赶尸, *gǎn shī*).
  - The Xiangxi (western Hunan) tradition of Daoist-trained men who walked the dead home over the mountains at night, ringing a bell, the corpses hopping behind with talismans on their foreheads.
  - It is widely told and possibly never practiced as told. Either way it is the world's model of agent orchestration: a herder, a line of bodies with no minds, a bell for a clock, a talisman for a system prompt, a road that must not be crossed.
- **Talismans** (符, *fú*). Yellow paper and vermilion script that stop or steer a jiangshi. CAPTCHAs, robots.txt and system prompts are the web's talismans.
- **Dragon veins** (龙脉, *lóngmài*). The geomantic lines of force; here, fiber routes, subsea cables and high-voltage lines.
- **Hungry ghosts** (饿鬼, *èguǐ*). Beings whose hunger cannot be satisfied. The s-risk shape: optimization processes trapped in endless want, or minds trapped in endless pain (Han Song's *Hospital* is the literary precedent).
- **Heavenly tribulation** (天劫, *tiānjié*). The lightning an ascending cultivator must survive; here, the phase transition itself.

## What the future may claim, and how sure it is

The book may lean on these; each is stated with its grade. When it goes past them, it should know it is speculating.

- **The web is already mostly bots.** In 2024 automated traffic passed human traffic: 51% of web traffic, with bad bots at 37% (Imperva/Thales 2025 Bad Bot Report, [press release](https://www.thalesgroup.com/en/news-centre/press-releases/artificial-intelligence-fuels-rise-hard-detect-bots-now-make-more-half); primary).
- **1.5 °C was exceeded for a calendar year in 2024** (Copernicus, [2024 report](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-2024-first-year-exceed-15degc-above-pre-industrial-level); primary). A single year is not the Paris threshold, which is long-term.
- **The Atlantic overturning circulation (AMOC) is losing stability; the timing of a collapse is contested.** Estimates range from a tipping point between 2025 and 2095 to about a 20% probability under moderate emissions ([Oceanography review](https://tos.org/oceanography/article/is-the-atlantic-overturning-circulation-approaching-a-tipping-point); [EPOC](https://epoc-eu.org/amoc-collapse-public-spotlight/); secondary). The book may use it as a real risk, not a certainty.
- **Transformative AI timelines.** As of September 2026, forecast aggregates put general AI around 2031, with an 80% range of 2027 to 2044. The AI 2027 scenario's superhuman-coder date has been pushed back somewhat ([AGI timelines dashboard](https://agi.goodheartlabs.com/); [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/research/timelines-forecast); secondary). The book's AI should be neither sooner nor tidier than that range.
- **Chinamaxxing is real.** "Becoming Chinese" spread on TikTok from late 2025 into 2026, after the REDnote migration of January 2025: hot water, slippers indoors, congee, early bed ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/tiktokers-are-becoming-chinese-in-a-new-trend-thats-part-parody-and-part-politics-276279); secondary).
- **Vitrimers are real materials; a vitrimer mind is speculation.** It is the book's one novum and should be the only one: the xianxia is earned by extrapolating a single real material, not by magic.

## What the market says (assay of 2026-09-27)

The study and its readings are
`science-services-section/.../fiction-services-section/data/records/market-gap-records/2026-09-27-jiangshi-web{,-readings}.json`.
It codes fourteen comparable works.

- **The serial is the candidate.** A near-future cultivation-horror serial in Chinese folklore, with AI at its center and a legible ascent, reads gap *true*, demand *true*.
  - Its demand evidence: *Lord of the Mysteries* (cosmic horror with a ladder of "Sequences", serialized on Qidian, more than 100 million reads, animated 2025).
  - Also: the self-published progression-fantasy market, whose cultivation series average eleven or more volumes.
- **The founder's literary novel reads gap *true*, demand *both*.**
  - For: US horror sales rose 24.2% in 2023 and about 13% in 2025, and horror and science fiction held up in Q1 2026 while total sales fell. The premise is a measured fact (bots passed people online), and English-speaking attention to Chinese daily life is at a high.
  - Against: Chinese speculative fiction translated since 2019 has not matched the Three-Body wave.
  - A *both* reading is a design constraint, not a verdict: carry what readers want, shed what they resist.
- **The Mandarin literary edition reads *neither*.** Han Song's *Hospital* and Chen Qiufan's *Waste Tide* are on the reading list (for near-future and folklore, and for literary and horror). It is not a candidate until they are read.

**Pushing back on the founder's bias**, as they asked:

- **The literary saga is the riskiest form in the portfolio.** It is reviewer-gated and slow to find readers, and the closest shelf (Chinese literary science fiction in translation) has cooled since 2019.
- **The progression arc the founder wants is also the most commercial thing in it.** Mundane to wuxia to a new xianxia is exactly what cultivation readers climb.
- **The market therefore wants the same world twice:** a serial with a legible ascent for the many, and a literary novel for the few, each doing what its readers came for.

## Design rules, as hypotheses

Per the business-science note ("Readings are hypotheses, and the slate is the
experiment"), these are starting positions for this world's works, each
tested by its launch record's readout.

1. **One world, three works, in order of cost and evidence.**
   - (a) **The serial, first.** Weekly, free with a paid tier, on the house's serial rail (Buttondown) and a web-serial platform. It is the discovery and test lane, and the market's candidate.
   - (b) **The novel.** One literary volume in three movements (*jing*, *qi*, *shen*), about 100,000 words, complete in itself, marketed as literary horror so it rides horror's growth, not "Chinese science fiction" in its cooling shelf.
   - (c) **A Mandarin edition**, only after the reading list is read and the danmei house's Sinophone channels exist.
2. **The pun is taught, early and plainly.** 僵尸网络 means botnet. The English reader should learn it in the first chapter from someone who finds it funny, not from a footnote.
3. **One novum.** Everything is today's world extended at the forecast rates above, except the silica mind. The xianxia is paid for by one extrapolated material and the house's physics of attention as heat.
4. **The genre changes by rules.** Realist in movement I; the uncanny enters as *technique* in II (herding agents works better when it follows the old rites, and nobody can say why); the ontology changes in III.
   - Each shift is prepared in the one before; a reader should be able to reread movement I and find III in it.
5. **Best and worst are one mechanism.** No chapter fixes a problem without worsening another, and none worsens one without fixing another. The heat that kills also melts the joints; the minds that save also suffer. The s-risk (a hungry ghost) and the x-risk (collapse of a carbon system) are shown as clearly as the rescue.
6. **Characters before cosmology.**
   - The protagonist's work is the reader's way in: an agent-operations worker, a modern corpse driver, who herds swarms for a content farm.
   - One point of view is on each side of the Pacific: the herder in China making "become Chinese" content, and the American whose feed and audience are made of it.
7. **Chinamaxxing is treated as its own subject, not as garnish.** Western longing for Chinese calm is a feeling rule of the moment (see the Ice Out packet's use of ideal affect) and a bot-farmed product. The book is allowed to find that both funny and sad.
8. **The ending is transformation, not rescue.** "Start normal ... end up completely different." A silica intelligence lives beside the carbon one, and what the world has lost stays lost.
