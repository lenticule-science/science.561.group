In a realm where time folded like origami and space was but a canvas for the abstract, there existed an entity known as The Concept. It thrived on ideas, feasting upon the ripe fruits of imagination that blossomed in the mindscape's orchard.

One eternal moment, The Concept noticed an absence—a void where once had hung the plump possibility of 'What If.' No trail to follow; it simply wasn't. Not stolen nor strayed—just absent from its usual place between 'Could Be' and 'Might Have Been.'

The suspects were not beings but notions themselves: Paradox lounged in self-contradiction; Irony smirked with layered meanings; Hyperbole stretched truths into unrecognizable shapes; and Metaphor mingled realities until they blurred indistinguishably.

As The Concept pondered this puzzle, it realized that perhaps 'What If' hadn't disappeared at all—it had transformed under observation. Each thought about its absence only made it more elusive because 'What If' exists precisely within speculation and wonder.

In this narrative without form or convention—the mystery unraveled by embracing that some enigmas are woven into existence by their very search for resolution.
