The Okaga comes to Ben

Somewhere else, at some other time, the starship Okaga drifted toward the Ben starsystem, where human people lived on several planets and moons. The last starship had visited 12 years ago, and the peoepl in the system were ready for another. They had goods to gift, and problems a starship could help with. One problem was a blight on the apple trees grown on Ben-Two.

Apotei was one orchard afflicted, and they nominated a young person named Dapu to represent them when the Okaga arrived, and ask them for help. Normally Dapu's orchard would be able to treat it themselves, but their antibiotics had been contaminated.

The blight was very bad, and though the orchard knew when the Okaga would arrive, they couldn't communicate until the ship arrived in the starsystem.

When the Okaga finally arrived Dapu left the Apotei Orchard and traveled to the ship, which orbited Ben-Three. As Dapu attended the Opening Convention and the following negotiations, they became aware that some delegates represented factions that had coordinated to steal access to the interstellar communications sytem. These delegates had planned with some of the Okaga's crew to allocate resources. They'd allocated the antibiotics Dapu's trees needed, so others could store it away.

Dapu told others and there was a call to re-negotiate everything. Dapu was chosen to lead that new Congress. During one early meeting of the caucus, there was a tremendous explosion. Some delegates had planted bombs aboard the Okaga. This let waiting pirates board nad loot the ship. The pirates then set off into deep space, destroying the satellites that pushed ships between the stars.

The attention of the Congress, when it was reconvened, turned to the robbery. Delegates and crew suspected of helping were dragged in, and people hotly argued about what to do. The Okaga's crew came in to announce they wouldn't be able to fix the destroyed satellites. The Okaga was stranded, and to the rest of the galaxy, the Ben starsystem was lost.

In the chaos that immediately followed, Dapu was hanged in the Convention Hall, which led to the Okaga's crew detaining and deporting all the other delegates.

After months of little communication, the Congress that Dapu united and the crew of the Okaga negotiated the distribution of some supplies. This eased hostilities, and soon many of the crew immigrated into the Ben system. While hopes of reconnecting with the galaxy were small, the Okaga's engines and library were useful resources, and the people who lived in the system wanted to ensure they stayed operational. So, some parents sent their children to the ship, to live and learn how to maintain it. Their children usually immigrated away from the ship, but there were always new people coming to learn how to maintain things. The ship, in this way, stayed functional for generations, until its functionality was integrated more completely into the peoples' culture.
