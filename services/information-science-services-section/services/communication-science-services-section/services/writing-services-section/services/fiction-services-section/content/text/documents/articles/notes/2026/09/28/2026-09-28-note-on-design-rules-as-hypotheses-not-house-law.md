# Note on design rules as hypotheses, not house law

Paths below are relative to `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/`.

## Why this note exists

The [note on the C-drama register under American feeling rules](../27/2026-09-27-note-on-the-c-drama-register-under-american-feeling-rules.md)
(2026-09-27) reads its market-gap assay into nine numbered design rules for
*Ice Out*, including rule 4: "Consent-forward restraint... The rejected
tropes are left out, not softened." That rule was written as if settled for
the house, not for one book.

On 2026-09-28, after *Ice Out* was published under that rule, the founder
pushed back:

> "I get that american readers say they don't like wrist grabbing, but also,
> they ate up 50 shades of grey. So we should be ambitious about writing
> under multiple authors, producing multiple canons, series, etc. aggressive
> in building paracosms mathematically, using our best theories, to really
> build things to the theories of the markets, and experiment. Not to
> dismiss your research, but we should always take the research into mind
> like a business who can do testing, given how cheap production can get
> with AI."

This correction did not reach a durable resource before it was lost from
this agent's private memory (the founder's own account: that memory was
cleared because it held claims the founder had no way to check or correct,
and the house's durable record should be the repository, not an
agent's private store). This note recovers it into the repository, where it
belongs.

## The correction, stated

- **A design rule drawn from reader statements is one reading, not the
  reading.** International viewers say they reject the wrist grab, the
  forced kiss and the possessive lead (the source cited in the 09-27 note,
  `theinterlude.net`, itself flags the reception as mixed). *Fifty Shades of
  Grey*'s sales are the standing counter-fact: a stated preference and a
  revealed preference can diverge, and revealed preference is what a
  business tests for, not what a reader survey states.
- **The house should run a portfolio, not a single verdict.** Multiple
  authors (pen names), multiple canons and multiple series let the house
  hold more than one reading of a contested design question at once, each
  tested against its own readers, rather than resolving the question once
  and applying it everywhere.
- **Research is a cheap-to-run hypothesis, not a constraint.** AI-cut
  production cost means the house can treat a design rule the way a
  business treats a testable claim: run it, read the result, and let readers
  settle what a literature review could not. This restates and sharpens the
  [business-science note on finding a market gap](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-finding-a-market-gap.md)'s
  "Readings are hypotheses, and the slate is the experiment" position, this
  time naming the specific rule it applies to.

## What this changes

- **The 09-27 C-drama note's nine design rules stand for *Ice Out* and its
  line.** They are not retracted; *Ice Out* is a low-heat, consent-forward
  book by design, and that design is not itself wrong.
- **They stop being the house's one answer for the C-drama-register
  romance demand.** A second book, author, or series in the same demand
  space may test the wrist grab, the possessive lead or higher heat
  directly, as its own hypothesis, read by its own launch record, not
  inherited from *Ice Out*'s packet.
- **Every evidence packet's "design rules" section should be read as
  scoped to the one book or line it names**, unless the packet says
  otherwise. Where past or future packets in this section state a design
  rule as though it binds every future work in the same demand, that
  over-reach should be corrected on sight.

## Open measurement

*Ice Out*'s own launch record (sales, reviews, reader replies) is the first
reading of the consent-forward hypothesis. A second book testing the
opposite reading, in a different author's name or a different series, is
the first reading of its counter-hypothesis. Neither is confirmed until
both have a launch record.
