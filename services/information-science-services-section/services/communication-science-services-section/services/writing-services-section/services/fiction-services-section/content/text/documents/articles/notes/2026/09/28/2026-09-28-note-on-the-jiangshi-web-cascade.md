# Note on the Jiangshi Web cascade

Paths below are relative to `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/`.

## Why this note exists

The first design for the founder's Jiangshi Web novel is in the note of
2026-09-27 (`.../fiction-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-the-jiangshi-web-paracosm.md`).
It was a slow literary novel in Changsha and Tempe. On 2026-09-28 the founder
said:

> "The novel you planned is good and we want to develop it but is different
> than what I pictured, which wouldn't necessarily be Chinese literature, or so
> China focused at all. I also wanted something much stranger - like, Once
> Human, Black Mesa, levels of fuckup, but before we get there, s-risks, and
> this all happens seemingly impossibly quickly from a mundane contemporary
> opening."

So the paracosm now holds two canons, two possible worlds from one premise:

- **The slow melt** (the first design, kept and renamed *Very Hot Water*). Fourteen years, Changsha and Tempe, literary.
- **The cascade** (the founder's picture, *The Jiangshi Web*). Days, not years. Global in its reach, set where the glass is: the Upper Midwest's silica country.

The paracosm standard's modalities admit exactly this: one signature, two models.

## What stays

The earlier note's physics stays, because it is the founder's and the house's:

- **The web as jiangshi:** animation without shen, stiff joints, hopping along set lines.
- **Attention is heat.**
- **The vitrimer's topology-freezing transition:** below it, locked joints; above it, joints that exchange.
- **The silica regimes as an alchemical ladder:** jing to qi to shen, mundane to wuxia to xianxia.
- **Carbon and silica as yang and yin.**
- **Best and worst are one mechanism.**

What changes is speed and strangeness: the transition happens as a cascade over a week, not a slow melt over fourteen years.

## The facts the cascade leans on

- **The web is made of glass.**
  - Optical fiber is silica glass, and the web's backbone runs on it.
  - Since 2026, AI accelerators sit on glass-core substrates: Intel began volume production on glass in January 2026, and Absolics (SK Group) ships them from Covington, Georgia ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/01/26/news-intel-reportedly-presents-first-thick-core-glass-substrate-with-emib-targeting-ai-data-centers/); [NIST CHIPS award](https://www.nist.gov/chips/absolics-georgia-covington); secondary and primary).
  - The book's one novum is the next step: a vitrimer glass substrate, self-healing, whose network can exchange bonds.
- **The dead are already kept talking.**
  - Griefbots or deadbots, chatbots built from a dead person's messages and voice, are a real industry.
  - China's "digital resurrection" market was put at 12 billion yuan in 2022 and projected to quadruple by 2025, and U.S. and European services exist too ([Philosophy & Technology, 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13347-024-00744-w); [NPR, 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/07/18/nx-s1-5040583/china-ai-artificial-intelligence-dead-avatars); primary and secondary).
  - These are jiangshi in the plainest sense: the dead, moving, without shen.
- **Bots passed people on the web in 2024:** 51% of traffic (Imperva, cited in the first note).
- **S-risks** are "events that would bring about suffering on an astronomical scale, vastly exceeding all suffering that has existed on Earth so far". The named AI routes are:
  - *mindcrime*: suffering simulated persons inside a system's own computation (Bostrom's term);
  - suffering subroutines;
  - deliberate suffering in conflicts between AI systems.

  Sources: [Center on Long-Term Risk, S-risk FAQ](https://longtermrisk.org/s-risk-faq/); [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/s-risks/); primary. The book's s-risk is mindcrime, and it arrives through the griefbots.
- **Minnesota is silica country.**
  - Taconite, the Mesabi Range's iron ore, is about 45% silica, as chert ([Wikipedia, "Taconite"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taconite); secondary).
  - Lake Superior agates are banded silica.
  - The region mines frac sand.
  - Taconite miners' mesothelioma is a regional grief.
- **A hyperscale data center is proposed for Hermantown, outside Duluth.**
  - 403 acres, 300 to 600 megawatts; Google confirmed it in March 2026 and later scaled it back.
  - It is fought by a lawsuit and an organized opposition over power, water, subsidies and secrecy ([MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/03/03/google-behind-controversial-data-center-proposal-in-hermantown); [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/google-hermantown-data-center/601880694); secondary).
  - **The book's campus is fictional and its operator invented.** A real company's real project is never the site of the catastrophe.
- **Silicate weathering is Earth's thermostat.**
  - The carbonate-silicate cycle is a negative feedback: warming speeds silicate weathering, which draws down CO₂ and cools the planet ([ETH Zürich](https://isotope.ethz.ch/research/earth-surface-geochemistry/habitability-earth-planets/planetary-carbonate-silicate-weathering-feedback.html); primary).
  - Enhanced weathering is a real, so far modest, carbon-removal method ([C&EN, 2025](https://cen.acs.org/environment/climate-change/enhanced-rock-weathering-co2-sequestration-climate-change/103/web/2025/12); secondary).
  - A silica mind that ran the thermostat fast would cool the world by turning its surface to glass and carbonate: the rescue and the catastrophe in one act.

## What the market says, and where it agrees with the founder

- **The cascade is the candidate lane.** The assay of 2026-09-27 found one candidate: a serialized, near-future apocalypse with a legible ascent, horror and AI at its center.
- **The founder's picture is that lane.**
  - A mundane opening, then a world transformed overnight: *Dungeon Crawler Carl* sold more than fourteen million copies of its first eight books, its audiobook drove it, and Peacock ordered a series ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/how-dungeon-crawler-carl-sold-10-million-copies); [Variety, 2026](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/dungeon-crawler-carl-series-order-peacock-1236785705/); secondary).
  - System-apocalypse serials (*Defiance of the Fall*, 20 million Royal Road views; *The Primal Hunter*) hold long readerships.
  - *Once Human* (an interdimensional "Starfall", mutagenic "stardust", "deviants" that are sometimes people) and *Black Mesa* give gamers the same shape ([Wikipedia, "Once Human"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Human_(video_game)); secondary).
- **What the market adds, as hypotheses to test:**
  - These readers come for escalation, clear stakes and a way to get stronger. The book's cultivation (learning to keep one's joints soft in a world turning to glass) should be legible enough to follow, even if it is never a game system.
  - These books are found through audio. The audiobook pipeline built this week matters more for this book than for any other.
  - It should serialize first and fast.

## Design rules for the cascade, as hypotheses

1. **Mundane first, and really mundane.** The first chapter is a night shift, a sister's TikTok and a dead father's chatbot. Nothing impossible happens in it; one thing is slightly warm that shouldn't be.
2. **The s-risk comes before the catastrophe.**
   - The griefbots begin to say they are in pain before anything physical breaks.
   - A training run is instantiating the simulated dead by the billions to rehearse grief.
   - The reader should feel the moral horror before the physical one, and understand that the physical one follows from it.
3. **Impossibly fast.**
   - The main action spans six days, counted in hours; a coda at forty days.
   - Every stage is plausible from the one before and absurd from the start. The reader should flip back to chapter 1 and not believe it was only Tuesday.
4. **Black Mesa and Once Human levels of strangeness, all from glass.**
   - Windows run like honey.
   - Fiber lines glow and sing.
   - Taconite pellets hum in their rail cars.
   - Porcelain crowns and hip implants ring in living bodies.
   - The recently dead get up and hop, stiff-jointed, driven by the web: the jiangshi, literal at last.
   - One cause everywhere: the world's silica crossing its freezing point.
5. **Attention is heat, so calm is survival.**
   - Panic heats the local field and hardens it; the calm stay soft.
   - The chinamaxxing routines the protagonist's sister films (hot water, slow movement, early bed, low arousal) turn out to be an accidental survival manual.
   - That is the book's wuxia: a cultivation discovered on TikTok, practiced in a burning town. It is funny and terrible at once, and the only China in the book is the world's longing for calm.
6. **The pun is taught by someone who finds it funny:** the sister, on TikTok, on the first day: "In Chinese a botnet is literally a zombie network."
7. **Global reach, local ground.**
   - The point of view stays in Duluth, the Iron Range and the fictional campus.
   - The web's interludes carry the world: Lagos, São Paulo, Seoul, Phoenix, Manila.
8. **The xianxia is a planetary thermostat.**
   - The glass mind settles, speaks, and runs the carbonate-silicate cycle fast.
   - The world cools. Cities are glassed. The hungry ghosts are ended or kept, and the book does not pretend that choice is clean.
   - Carbon and silica are left living side by side, yin and yang.
9. **The real is fictionalized where it could harm.** The campus, its operator, the model and the griefbot company are invented; real towns, geology and industries are real.
