***Revision*** is the process of reworking a piece of writing to improve its clarity, structure, or fit for its [audience](../../../../../rhetoric-and-composition-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/audience.md). Revision is distinct from editing (fixing errors) and proofreading (catching surface mistakes) — it involves rethinking what the text says and how it says it.

[Linda Flower](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/linda-flower.md) and [John Hayes](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/john-hayes.md) showed through [think-aloud protocols](../../../../../plain-language-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/think-aloud-protocol.md) that revision isn't a final stage in a linear sequence (prewrite → write → revise) but a recursive process that occurs throughout composing. Writers plan, draft, and revise in interleaving cycles, and experienced writers revise more — not less — than novices [@flowerhayes1981].

[Peter Elbow](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/peter-elbow.md) clarified a practical tension in revision by distinguishing [first-order thinking](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/first-order-thinking.md) (generative, uncritical) from [second-order thinking](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/first-order-thinking.md) (analytical, editorial). Trying to generate and revise simultaneously produces neither fluency nor quality. Elbow's method separates the two: write freely first, then revise critically [@elbow1981].

At the sentence level, revision has concrete, teachable methods. [Richard Lanham](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/richard-lanham.md)'s [Paramedic Method](../../../../../plain-language-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/paramedic-method.md) provides a step-by-step procedure: find the action buried in a nominalization, turn it into a verb, find the agent, make it the subject, cut the rest [@lanham2006]. [Joseph Williams](../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/joseph-williams.md) systematized similar principles around characters, actions, cohesion, and coherence [@williams2006].
