An operational outcome of visual engineering: the artifact produces a fast first grasp of where to look and what to do next.

Glance-direction is one of five operational outcomes defined by [visual engineering practices](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/arts-theory/design/index.md). It names the artifact&rsquo;s capacity to produce a fast &ldquo;first grasp&rdquo; of where to look and what to do next. This is not the same as comprehension; it is the reduction of initial uncertainty.

Glance-direction arises when [Bauhaus](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/bauhaus-pedagogy.md) construction (clear relational field) is paired with [brutalist](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/brutalism.md) hierarchy (hard, sparse contrasts) under [Tuftean](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/tuftean-information-design.md) budgeting (few marks that matter). The eye is not asked to choose among many equal candidates; it is given a small number of structurally meaningful anchors.

The concept can be related to empirical findings on attentional deployment: attention can be guided both endogenously (by task goals) and exogenously (by salient cues) [@posner1980]. Glance-direction works by aligning both pathways — making the salient cues coincide with the structurally important elements.
