# Durham 2030

A speculative fiction setting imagining Durham, North Carolina in the
year 2030. Written in winter 2023, the fiction assumes that TESCREAL
ideology has reshaped local settlements into technofascist enclaves
known as **arcologies**, governed by Social Contribution Score regimes.

A paracosm distinct from the others under this root because it is set
in a near-future Earth — its content layer is real-world geography
under speculative political pressure rather than a wholly invented
secondary world.

## Layout

- `texts/` — Durham-2030 prose, design notes, narrative material.
- `terms/` — vocabulary specific to the setting (e.g.,
  social-contribution-score, arcology).
- `places/`, `beings/`, `things/` — to be created when individual
