To fold in [poker](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is to abandon the current [hand](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hand.md) and give up any claim to the [pot](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/archive/pot.md) [@britannica2026poker]. Folding ends the player&rsquo;s participation in that hand immediately.

Folding is one of poker&rsquo;s core strategic actions because it limits losses. A player does not need to continue just because chips are already committed. Strong poker depends as much on disciplined folding in weak positions as on aggressive betting in strong ones [@sklansky1999].
