An elder brother's wife, and by extension the wife of a close male friend or boss the speaker treats as a brother. Rendered "sister-in-law", which English viewers of C-drama accept as a form of address.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
