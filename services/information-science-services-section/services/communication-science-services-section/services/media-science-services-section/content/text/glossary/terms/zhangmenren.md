Literally the one who holds the gate: the head of a martial sect in wuxia, and in modern drama the head of a family conglomerate. Rendered "head" ("head of the Gu Group") rather than a literal "sect leader" when the setting is corporate.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
