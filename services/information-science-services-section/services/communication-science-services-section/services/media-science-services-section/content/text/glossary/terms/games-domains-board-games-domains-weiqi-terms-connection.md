A connection in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is a relation between stones that makes them function as one group, sharing [liberties](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberty.md) and supporting each other&rsquo;s defense [@britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Connection is structurally important because isolated stones are weak. A move that does not gain immediate [territory](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/territory.md) may still be strong if it joins two unsettled groups, prevents a cutting attack, or turns a fragile shape into one that can live.
