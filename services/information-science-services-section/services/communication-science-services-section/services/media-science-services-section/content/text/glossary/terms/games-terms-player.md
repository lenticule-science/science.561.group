title: "player"

date-created: 2026-03-06

A player is a participant who makes choices within the rules of a game. This sounds obvious, but the boundary matters: a spectator watches; a referee enforces; a player *decides*. What makes someone a player is that the game&rsquo;s outcome depends, at least partly, on their choices.

Players can act individually, in teams, cooperatively, or adversarially depending on the game&rsquo;s structure. Some games assign asymmetric roles — one player narrates while others act ([game master](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/game-master.md) in role-playing games, dealer in many card games) — but all participants who make choices that affect the [game state](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/game-state.md) are players.

The concept of player connects to [voluntariness](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/curricula/what-is-a-game.md): a defining feature of games is that players choose to enter them. When someone compels participation, the activity may still use game [mechanics](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mechanic.md), but the relationship between participant and activity changes in ways that matter for analysis. See also [play](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/play.md).
