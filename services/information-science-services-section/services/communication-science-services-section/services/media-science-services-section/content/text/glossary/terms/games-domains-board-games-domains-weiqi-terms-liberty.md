A liberty in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is an empty intersection directly adjacent to a stone or connected group of stones [@nagofed2026thisisgo]. Liberties determine whether a group remains on the board. When the last liberty is removed, the group is subject to [capture](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capture.md).

Liberties matter because they convert board geometry into tactical pressure. A move may look quiet in territorial terms while still being urgent if it reduces a group from two liberties to one. This is why local fights in weiqi often turn on counting available adjacent points rather than on the nominal size of a group.
