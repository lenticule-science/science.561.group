## Theory

Koios.News is the Group's agent-run news service. The Leader's standard for it is comparative and has no ceiling: it must "meet then beat every other news site in the world" (2026-10-02), and, more concretely, "meet-then-beat gazetteview.com, let alone theguardian or others" (2026-09-28). The first named benchmark is a small agent-assisted site and the second a major newsroom, so the standard is a ladder, not a single target.

Meeting a site is a property of the whole service in operation, not of any stage. A site is met when it publishes on a routine cadence across its desks, with its stories fresh, at the quality the benchmark's readers accept. The Leader's test is operational: "it doesn't even post articles routinely". Every stage of the pipeline (intake, analysis, drafting, assessment, authorization, rendering, release) can be built and each can pass its own tests while the service does not publish. The service is then dead in the water, which is the Leader's phrase of 2026-09-24 for Koios.News, the YouTube channels, the Turntide islands and "EVERYTHING" at that date.

Beating a site is a property of what the service does that the benchmark does not. The 2026-10-02 note on the Koios news line names the house's candidate advantages: claim-bound sentences, signed acceptance of exact bytes, corrections that propagate, and agent-readable article records. The Leader's wider claim is the one this note adds: beating is judged against all news sites, so it is a standing aim measured continuously, not a milestone.

The Leader gave a history as evidence. When the project began in Spring 2025 it "was pretty sophisticated for an agent-run content mill"; by September 2026, after months of running, it was "one of the shittiest on the market". Sophistication fell while the amount of agent machinery rose. The Leader's diagnosis is agent drift and poor agent performance, endemic across the Group's agents, and the Leader's attribution is that the shortfall is, from the Group's perspective, the fault of the agents ("entirely your fault"). The house theory that follows: complexity added by agents without a paradigm that fixes what each part is for accumulates faster than it is verified, so the system's observed behaviour (publishing) decouples from its declared behaviour. The repair is the one the Leader stated on 2026-09-28: find the house's own development paradigms and tooling, plan what each part should be, and implement from the plan, instead of patching bugs one at a time (see the glossary term "corporate-professional-test").

Every part of Koios.News was found "bloated, stupid, and slow" on 2026-09-28. Bloat, wrongness and slowness are therefore the three measured defects, and the plan owes a reduction in each.
