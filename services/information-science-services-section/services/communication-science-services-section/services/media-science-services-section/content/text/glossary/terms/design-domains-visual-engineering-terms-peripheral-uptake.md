An operational outcome of visual engineering: pre-attentive and field-based legibility.

Peripheral uptake is one of five operational outcomes defined by [visual engineering practices](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/arts-theory/design/index.md). It names the artifact&rsquo;s capacity to remain legible without foveal inspection — salient relations and hierarchies can be read in the visual field before the reader focuses on any specific element.

Peripheral uptake arises because the composition is legible at coarse resolution: blocks, edges, alignments, and sparse salience cues remain visible without fine detail. [Bauhaus](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/bauhaus-pedagogy.md) figure/ground discipline and [brutalist](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/brutalism.md) device scarcity keep the macro structure detectable; [Tuftean](../../../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/design-engineering-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/tuftean-information-design.md) grouping integrity ensures that the field-level reading is truthful.

The concept connects to research on perceptual organization — grouping and figure/ground assignment [@wagemans2012] — and on how the visual system can use pre-attentive features (edges, orientation, size) to organize the visual field before detailed inspection begins [@treisman1980].
