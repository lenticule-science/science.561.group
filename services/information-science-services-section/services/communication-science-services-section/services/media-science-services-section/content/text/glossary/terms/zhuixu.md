A man who marries into and lives with his wife's family, a low and mocked position; the humiliated son-in-law who is secretly powerful is a leading micro-drama plot. Rendered "live-in son-in-law".

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
