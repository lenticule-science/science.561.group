Literally white moonlight: the pure, unattainable first love the lead cannot forget, often the rival of the heroine. Rendered "first love", or kept as "white moonlight" when the series leans on the image.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
