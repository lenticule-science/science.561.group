***Neue Sachlichkeit*** ([English](../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/english-language.md): "New Objectivity") was a [cultural](../../../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cultural-movement.md) and [art movement](../../../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/art-movement.md) in [Germany](../../../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/germany.md) in the [1920s](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1920s.md) and early [1930s](../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1930s.md) that rejected [Expressionism](../../../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/expressionism.md).

Itself was divided into [socially critiquing](../../../../../../../../../../../knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/critique-of-society.md) Verists and the non-critiquing Classicists.
