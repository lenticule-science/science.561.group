Life and death in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is the analysis of whether a group can secure enough stable shape, usually through [eyes](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/archive/eye.md), to avoid [capture](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capture.md), or whether the opponent can force it off the board [@britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Life-and-death reading is one of the game&rsquo;s central skills because local shape decides global outcomes. A large territorial framework can collapse if one key group dies. A small group can become strategically decisive if it lives inside the opponent&rsquo;s area.
