A handicap in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is a rule adjustment, usually extra Black stones placed before play begins, that lets players of unequal strength compete in a meaningful game [@britgo2026whatisgo; @britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Handicap is one reason weiqi supports play across large skill differences without becoming a different game. The stronger player does not receive more pieces or special powers during play. Instead, the weaker player begins from a materially stronger board position.
