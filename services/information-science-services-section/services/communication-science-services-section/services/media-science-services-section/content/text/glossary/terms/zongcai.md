The head of a company, and the default male lead of the billionaire romance. In subtitles it is "CEO" as a noun; as a form of address English speech uses "Mr. <surname>" when the surname is known and "sir" or "ma'am" otherwise, since "Mr. CEO" is not something an English speaker says.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
