A title for a hidden sovereign of immense power, the secret identity of the despised son-in-law in a popular micro-drama formula. Rendered "Dragon King".

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
