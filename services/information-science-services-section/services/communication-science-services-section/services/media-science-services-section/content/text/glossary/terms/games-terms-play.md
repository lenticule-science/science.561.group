Play is voluntary, intrinsically motivated activity pursued for its own sake. It is broader than games: a child stacking blocks is playing without rules, objectives, or competition. Play becomes a game when it acquires structure — [rules](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rules.md), objectives, constraints — but the underlying quality of voluntary engagement persists.

The distinction matters because it marks a boundary. When an activity looks like a game but the [player](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/player.md) doesn&rsquo;t experience it as play — mandatory gamified training, exploitative reward loops, compulsive gambling — something has shifted. The [mechanics](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mechanic.md) may be identical, but the relationship between participant and activity is different. Game studies and game design both benefit from keeping this distinction visible rather than collapsing everything with rules and points into &ldquo;games.&rdquo;

Play is also the substrate of learning within games. Players learn [game states](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/game-state.md), strategies, and social dynamics through play — not by being told about them, but by encountering them through action and consequence. This is why games have been used as teaching tools across cultures, and why the most effective game-based learning preserves the voluntary, exploratory quality of play rather than instrumentalizing it.
