Crossing into another time or into a novel's character; the transmigration plot. Rendered "transmigrated" and "transmigration", the fandom's terms, or "traveled back in time" when that is all it means.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
