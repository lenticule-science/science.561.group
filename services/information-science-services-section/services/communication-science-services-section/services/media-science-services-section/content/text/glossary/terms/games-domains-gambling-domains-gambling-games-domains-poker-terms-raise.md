To raise in [poker](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is to increase the current wager [@britannica2026poker]. A raise keeps the player in the [hand](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hand.md) while forcing later players to pay more if they want to continue.

Raising is the action that gives poker much of its strategic depth. It can build the [pot](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/archive/pot.md) when the raiser expects to be ahead, pressure weaker ranges into [folding](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/archive/fold.md), or represent a stronger hand than the player actually holds. Because a raise changes both information and price, it does more than move chips [@sklansky1999].
