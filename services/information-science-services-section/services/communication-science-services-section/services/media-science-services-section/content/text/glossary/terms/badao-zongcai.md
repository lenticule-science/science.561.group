The overbearing, possessive billionaire who is the male lead of countless web novels and micro-dramas; 霸道 is high-handed or tyrannical. English-language fans call the type the "domineering CEO", and platforms often title series with it.

It is one of the terms of the [micro-drama](micro-drama.md) subtitle lexicon, which the Group's Mandarin-to-English translation supplies to the model whenever a source line uses the term.
