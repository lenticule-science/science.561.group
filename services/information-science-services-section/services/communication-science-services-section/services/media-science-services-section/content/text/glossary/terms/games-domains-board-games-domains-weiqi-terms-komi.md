Komi in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is the point compensation added to White&rsquo;s final score to offset Black&rsquo;s first-move advantage [@britgo2025default; @britgo2026whatisgo].

Komi matters because weiqi is asymmetrical at the start: Black moves first. Without compensation, that initiative would distort results across many even games. Komi is therefore a scoring adjustment, not a tactical rule inside local fights.
