An eye in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) is an empty point or enclosed space inside a group&rsquo;s shape that functions as an internal [liberty](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberty.md) and helps determine whether the group can live [@britgo2026howtoplaygo].

Eyes matter because they change the status of a group. A surrounded group with no secure eye shape may still be dead, while a well-connected group with two separate eyes is usually safe. This is why players often defend shape even when no immediate [capture](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capture.md) is on the board.
