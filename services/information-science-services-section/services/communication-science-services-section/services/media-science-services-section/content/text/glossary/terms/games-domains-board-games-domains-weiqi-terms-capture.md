A capture in [weiqi](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/arts/ludics/games/index.md) occurs when a move removes the last [liberty](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberty.md) from an opposing stone or connected group, after which those stones are removed from the board [@britannica2026go; @nagofed2026thisisgo].

Capture is the game&rsquo;s basic tactical enforcement rule. Without it, enclosure would carry no force. But weiqi is not a pure capture race, because players usually capture in order to secure shape, reduce influence, or stabilize [territory](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/board-games-services-section/services/weiqi-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/territory.md), not only to remove material.
