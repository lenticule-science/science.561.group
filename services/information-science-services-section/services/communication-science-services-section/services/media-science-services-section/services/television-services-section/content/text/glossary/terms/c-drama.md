A C-drama is a television series made in mainland China. Taiwanese and Hong Kong series are usually named apart. International viewers now use the word for a register as well as an origin: the feeling a C-drama is expected to have. Streaming carried that register abroad, most visibly the contemporary romances *Hidden Love* (2023) and *The First Frost* (2025) on Netflix.

The register viewers name has four marks:

- **Restraint.** Feeling is shown through small attentions (a meal cooked, an umbrella held, a coat left on a chair) more than said or touched. Physical intimacy is withheld to make the emotion carry further.
- **Slow burn.** The couple's declaration comes late, and the drama spends its time on the ordinary days before it.
- **Years-long hidden love** (暗恋, *an lian*). One lead has loved the other for years without saying so.
- **Healing** (治愈, *zhiyu*). The tone is warm and restorative, and viewers watch to be calmed.

The genre also carries tropes international viewers push back on: the wrist grab, the forced kiss and the possessive, jealous lead. Some series end bittersweet or tragic, which breaks the happy-ending rule of the American romance novel. Genres that borrow the register choose which of its conventions to keep.

The genre split between historical or xianxia series (古装, *guzhuang*) and contemporary ones (现代, *xiandai*) matters for writers borrowing the register in English. The historical side already has English-original novels in its register; the contemporary side, set in the United States, had none in the Group's 2026-09-27 sample ([note on the C-drama register under American feeling rules](../../../../../../../writing-services-section/services/fiction-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-the-c-drama-register-under-american-feeling-rules.md)).
