The xiao is a traditional Chinese vertical end-blown flute made of bamboo, producing a soft, mellow tone.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Material**: Bamboo (typically black or purple bamboo)
- **Structure**: Vertical flute with five or six finger holes, one blowing hole at the top end
- **Blowing Hole**: Notched or U-shaped embouchure at the upper end
- **Length**: Typically 45cm to 1.25m depending on register

## Musical Properties

- **Sound Production**: Edge tone (air splitting against the notched edge)
- **Timbre**: Soft, mellow, contemplative, less penetrating than dizi
- **Range**: Approximately 2 octaves
- **Techniques**: Finger sliding, half-holing, tonguing, vibrato, circular breathing, pitch bending

## Musical Role

- **Ensemble**: Often plays counter-melody or harmonic filler in silk-and-bamboo ensembles
- **Solo**: Meditative solo repertoire, often associated with scholarly traditions
- **Genres**: Classical, folk, religious, meditation music

## Predication Theory Perspective

From a predication theory viewpoint:
- **Subject**: The xiao instrument
- **Predicate**: "produces sound with pure edge tone"
- **Object**: Musical tone with specific timbral qualities (soft, mellow)
- **Fiber**: All xiao tones sharing the edge-tone predicate form a fiber
- **Modulation**: Finger position, breath pressure, and embouchure angle create predication space
- **Contrast with Dizi**: Lacks membrane, producing different timbral fiber structure
