This section exists to hold the charge-dynamics reading of music: dissonance
as local obstruction, cadence as audible settling, tonality as gauge, rhythm
as a causal set made audible. The founding statement of that claim is
[Music as Pure Charge Dynamics](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-music-as-pure-charge-dynamics.md),
written 2026-08-21 as valve-session crank 3. That note is not duplicated
here -- it is authoritative where it stands, and this section's job is to
give it a home, not a second copy. It relocates into this section on the
owner's schedule; until then, treat the note above as canonical and
everything below as scaffolding built to receive it.

## Where this lives, and why

A survey of the science tree before any scaffolding turned up a
**music-services-section already standing**, four levels deep:
`information-science-services-section` →
`communication-science-services-section` →
`media-science-services-section` → `music-services-section`. It already has
its own `music-theory-services-section` child, a glossary with the exact
vocabulary the charge-dynamics note reinterprets (dissonance, cadence,
tonality, rhythm, harmony, consonance), and a triage folder holding
Chinese-aerophone and Lakota-aerophone-adjacent material. This is not a
green field. `music-science-services-section` is added here as a new
sibling of `music-theory-services-section`, inside that standing section,
rather than as a new top-level section elsewhere.

**Why not semiotics.** The founding note's whole claim is that music is
charge exchange *with the predication/reference layer removed* -- language
binds substrate into memes (cells ⊗ charges), music discards the cells and
keeps the dynamics. Semiotics is the science of reference, of signs
standing for something else. Filing a "music without the reference layer"
theory under the science of reference would bury the note's central move
under the very layer it claims to subtract. `semiotics-science-services-section`
(under this same `information-science-services-section`) is a near
sibling, one level up, and worth cross-linking from music-theory's existing
vocabulary -- but it is not this section's parent.

**Why not social-science or game-science.** Neither has existing music
infrastructure to extend. `social-science-services-section` covers
sociology, business, education, critical theory -- the *social* study of
music (industry, scenes, audiences) would belong there if it existed, but
the charge-dynamics claim is not a social-science claim; it is a claim
about the acoustic/structural stratum itself, prior to any social reading
of it. `game-science-services-section`'s `ludics-services-section` covers
play and games; nothing there anticipates a music theory.

**Why here, arts-adjacent, specifically.** The engineering side of this
tree already has the mirror-image placement:
`engineering-services-section` → `media-engineering-services-section` →
`music-engineering-services-section` (the generative DSP/composition
engine, home of the guqin corpus fixture -- see below). Communication
science's `media-science-services-section` → `music-services-section` is
the science-side counterpart to that engineering-side pair. Placing
`music-science-services-section` inside the science-side `music-services-section`
keeps the engineering/science mirror intact: the DSP core lives on the
engineering side, the theory of what it's doing lives on the science side,
at the matching depth.

## A precedent already sitting here

`music-theory-services-section` already contains
[Verified Structures as Compositional Grammar](../../../../../../music-theory-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/verified-structures-as-compositional-grammar.md)
(2026-08-19), which ports Obsmetrologia's Lean-proved settling, sheaf, and
closure-flow structures directly into the generative engine's control logic
-- regime as macro-form, sheaf cover as polyphony gate, driven closure flow
as phrase logic. That is the same formal apparatus the charge-dynamics
note leans on (FCA closure as cadence, settling as resolution), arrived at
independently and from the composition-engine side rather than the
witnessed-field side. The two notes should be read together once the
founding note relocates here: one shows the settling machinery proved and
implemented: the other claims it is what music *is*, not just a convenient
control scheme for generating some of it. Neither should overclaim on the
other's behalf -- the compositional-grammar note is explicit that its
proofs are about the algorithm, not about listener-perceived tension, and
the charge-dynamics note's own falsifier condition (does the charge
vocabulary out-predict Huron's ITPRA, or is it just re-description) applies
with equal force here.
