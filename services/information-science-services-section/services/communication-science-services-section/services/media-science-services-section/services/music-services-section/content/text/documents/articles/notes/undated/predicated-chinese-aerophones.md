# Predicated Chinese Aerophones

A sub-theory of [chinese-aerophones](../../../../_index.md) that applies the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) framework to Chinese aerophone analysis.

The parent theory studies instruments organologically (physical structure, acoustics, playing technique, cultural history). This sub-theory re-examines the same instruments through the bilattice-valued predication lens: each instrument is a predication system, each producible tone is a predication cell $(s, p, o)$ (instrument × sound-production-mechanism × tone), and the instrument's tonal space has a predication fiber algebra over $\mathbf{FOUR}$.

## What this theory adds

The parent `chinese-aerophones` theory establishes the instruments, their acoustic mechanisms, and their cultural roles. This sub-theory does not re-derive those facts; it assumes them and asks:

- What is the predication fiber structure of each instrument's tonal space?
- How does instrument design (membrane, bore shape, reed type) affect the fiber's lattice properties?
- How do ensemble contexts create compound predication fibers?
- How does the bilattice truth-order distinguish canonical, contested, incorrect, and unattested tones?
