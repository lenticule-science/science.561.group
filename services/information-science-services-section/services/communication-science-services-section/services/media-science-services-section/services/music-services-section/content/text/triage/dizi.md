The dizi is a traditional Chinese transverse flute made of bamboo, characterized by a distinctive membrane (di mo) hole that creates its bright, resonant timbre.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Material**: Bamboo (typically purple or white bamboo)
- **Structure**: Transverse flute with six finger holes, one blowing hole, and one membrane hole
- **Membrane**: Thin tissue paper (di mo) coated with egg substance, covering an extra hole between the blowing hole and finger holes
- **Length**: Varies from ~30cm to over 1m depending on register

## Musical Properties

- **Sound Production**: Edge tone (air splitting against the blowing hole edge)
- **Timbre**: Bright, penetrating, with characteristic buzz from the membrane
- **Range**: Approximately 2.5 octaves
- **Techniques**: Finger sliding, finger popping, tonguing, vibrato, circular breathing, multiphonics (with membrane)

## Musical Role

- **Ensemble**: Melody instrument in Chinese orchestra, folk ensembles, opera accompaniment
- **Solo**: Virtuosic solo repertoire with programmatic elements
- **Genres**: Classical, folk, opera, contemporary, fusion
