# Worldbuilding and paracosmic science

Worldbuilding is a domain within Media. The [paracosm](../terms/paracosm.md) describes sustained world-making activity; a [secondary world](../terms/secondary-world.md) is an articulated fictional world. The Group studies both without assuming that one implies the other.

## Authoritative scientific resources

- [Paracosm content schema v0.2](../../documents/articles/specifications/paracosm-content-schema.md): source-owned typed material for worlds, works, modalities, dynamic systems, canons and disputed testimony. The dated proposed observation extension does **not** amend the implemented schema.
- [Reproducible Paracosm Specification v0.1](../../documents/articles/specifications/reproducible-paracosm-specification.md): historical categorical research program and its stated proof obligations. The broad modal encoding and reproducibility claims are unproved.
- [Relational Worlding Grammar](../../documents/articles/guides/relational-worlding-grammar.md) and [Praxonic Worldbuilding](../../documents/articles/guides/praxonic-worldbuilding-guide.md): pressure, emission, trace, local alignment, contradiction and continuing world consequences.
- [Science of Epic Paracosms](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/28/2026-09-28-note-on-the-science-of-epic-paracosms.md): genre and scale hypotheses, not categorical theorems.

## Alice in Wonderland and Looking-Glass: research case

The source-grounded [Alice paracosmic stress test](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-note-on-alice-as-a-paracosmic-stress-test.md) tests the existing theory against the Cheshire Cat's separable grin observation; changes in Alice's apparent size and identity; the Hatter's frozen clock; court orders distinct from realized sanctions; the Red Queen's relative motion; the White Queen's apparent future memory; Humpty Dumpty's attempted lexical authority; and the dream-framing alternatives. These are **fictional textual observations** modeled as scoped claims, not experimentally validated laws of physics.

The worked mathematical result is a finite observation-factorization test: if two admitted states look identical to one observer but differ for another, the second observation cannot be recovered by a function of the first. The original [reproducibility research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2025/10/10/2025-10-10-note-on-reproducible-paracosmic-models.md) now gives the proof, and the Alice case gives its motivating negative instance.

**Receiving boundary:** This Science topic owns interpretation, definitions, proposed mathematical conditions and source-bound evidence. Red Cup Engineering owns executable interpreters and tests; Coesite owns runtime procedures; each Public paracosm retains its own source/canon authority. The research case creates neither an unauthorized adaptation canon nor a new Alice-specific implementation.
