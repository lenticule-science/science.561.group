Continuity is the internal consistency of a fictional world across its multiple realizations—different works, episodes, installments, tellings, or play sessions set in the same world. A world exhibits continuity when established facts, character histories, spatial relationships, and causal sequences remain coherent from one realization to the next.

Continuity is a structural property of the world, not a social one. It describes whether the parts fit together, not whether anyone has declared them authoritative. This distinguishes it from [canon](canon.md), which is the editorial or social mechanism by which some subset of material is designated as binding. A world can have strong continuity without formal canon (as in many [role-playing game](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/role-playing-games.md) campaigns, where the group's shared memory serves as the consistency mechanism), and weak continuity despite extensive canonical declarations (as in long-running franchises with accumulated contradictions).

The historical [Reproducible Paracosm proposal](../../documents/articles/notes/2025/10/10/2025-10-10-note-on-reproducible-paracosmic-models.md) uses the gluing condition $J$ to model coherence among local world fragments. **Correction (2026-10-17):** compatibility on pairwise overlaps is not, without specified descent hypotheses, proof that a unique global world exists. Similarly, two tellings equivalent for one named receiver's observations need not be bisimilar or equivalent for all other receivers. Unresolved narrative alternatives and contradictory character testimony are not automatically continuity faults. The [Alice observational counterexample](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-note-on-alice-as-a-paracosmic-stress-test.md) distinguishes the Cheshire Cat's missing body from its still-observable grin. The formal claims in [RPS](../../documents/articles/specifications/reproducible-paracosm-specification.md) remain research obligations rather than established theorems.
