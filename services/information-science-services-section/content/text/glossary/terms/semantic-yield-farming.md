***Semantic yield farming*** is the iterative casting of interpretive structures (signals, methods, fields) to generate reusable records (yields) which may reenter the casting system. It describes a practice in which the primary goal is not insight, but **generative accumulation**—building a reservoir of role-bearing records.

The process of semantic yield farming does not produce truth. It cultivates **recastable coherence fragments**.

It may be:

- Tactical: building a mesh for future modulation  
- Relational: deepening interpretive field density  
- Infrastructural: bootstrapping a paradigm under resource constraint

Semantic yield farming is to grammar what liquidity mining is to finance:  
**A way to grow the system by engaging its structure recursively.**
