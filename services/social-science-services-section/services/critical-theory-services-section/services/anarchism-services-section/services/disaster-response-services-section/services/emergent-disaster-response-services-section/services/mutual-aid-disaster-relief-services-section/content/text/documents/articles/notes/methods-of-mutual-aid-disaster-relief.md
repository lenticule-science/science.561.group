The Mutual Aid Disaster Relief current uses a distinct cluster of
methods to organize disaster work from below. These methods include
listening to impacted communities, decentralized network mobilization,
[autonomous direct action](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/autonomous-direct-action.md),
[popular education](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../education-services-section/services/pedagogy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/popular-education.md), and the building of
[survival programs](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/survival-programs.md) that meet urgent
needs while increasing collective capacity [@madrabout2025;
@madrprograms2024; @madrprinciples2020].

## Listening as method

This current treats listening as an organizing method rather than a
public-relations gesture. Its own mission statements say that response
should work with, listen to, and be responsive to impacted communities,
especially those most marginalized [@madrprinciples2020].

## Network mobilization

Mutual Aid Disaster Relief describes itself as a decentralized network,
not a standing army of volunteers [@madrabout2025]. Methodologically,
this means mobilizing an interlocking web of relationships, affinities,
and local initiatives that can adapt quickly to different contexts
without passing everything through a single command center
[@madrabout2025].

## Survival programs

A central method in this current is the use of [survival programs](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/survival-programs.md).
The MADR network explicitly draws on the Black Panther tradition of
meeting immediate needs while building consciousness and long-term power
[@madrprograms2024]. This links supplies distribution, community
kitchens, wellness centers, and infrastructure projects to political
education rather than treating them as neutral services.

## Popular education and skill sharing

Another method is ongoing popular education. MADR's own program
descriptions present relief work as a constant learning process in which
participants share practical skills, disaster scenarios, and lessons
learned while building capacity for future response [@madrprograms2024].
