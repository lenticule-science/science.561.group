- Conjecture :: The [anarchism](../schools/anarchism/index.md) of the liberal subject derives from civic duty and is thus incommensurable with [decolonization](../../../glossary/terms/decolonization.md).

- Proof :: unproven

- claims ::
  - Given that liberal [anarchism](../schools/anarchism/index.md), that is, [anarchism](../schools/anarchism/index.md) of the liberal subject, requires itself be functional within liberalism and thus a part of liberal worlding, it must maintain compatibility with liberalism.
  - Given the lineage of liberal [anarchism](../schools/anarchism/index.md) is deviation of the lineage of liberal individualism, its a priori determinations may be derived from that lineage.
