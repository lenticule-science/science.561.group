# WIT / ACK / ATT: Electoral Receipts and Unmatched Obligations

**Claim.** Electoralism permits a distinctive form of *false absolution*: a receipt for the political act of selecting representation is socially treated as a receipt for the discharge of a **different, still-open charged obligation**. The receipt may be genuine. Its alleged discharge is not. The mechanism is **substitution of the settled object**, not the metaphysical uselessness of voting.

## I. Mathematically distinct carriers

A **WIT** is one witnessed distinction-step: a *cover* of the FOUR knowledge order. Using the two evidence coordinates, the four charges are ⊥=(0,0), true=(1,0), false=(0,1), and both=(1,1). One 0→1 cover is any of ⊥→true, ⊥→false, true→both, or false→both. The direct jump ⊥→both crosses **two** covers. An eyewitness, an utterance or a news story is not automatically one WIT: specify the cell, evidence polarity, and witnessed knowledge-order change before assigning that unit.

The finer [Obsmetrologia Record](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/Record.lean) is a pair of natural-number evidence **tallies**, (pos,neg) ∈ ℕ², merged by addition. Its four-valued charge projection retains only whether pos and neg are positive. Thus Record (2,1) and Record (1,1) both read as both/⊤; the records differ while their charge is identical. Another same-polarity attestation can increase a Record tally without producing another FOUR cover. This distinction is necessary to avoid counting repeated utterances as fresh wits.

An **ACK** is *another party's uptake of a WIT across a border*. Self-acknowledgment cannot supply its own counterparty. A receiving actor can ACK contested evidence without making it true, resolving it, or discharging an obligation. The September 4 backward-σ / forward-Δ interpretation of ACK and its conjectured H⁰ role is not an already Lean-checked theorem.

An **ATT** is a **WIT taken of an ACK**: a second-order witnessed transition in which an agent's inside face registers the outside face's actual uptake. It is not time on a screen, expressed concern, a click, or one more interchangeable attitude. The finite-budget [attention-allocation results](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Cohomology/AttentionAllocation.lean) are proved *within their stated formal domain*; they do not by themselves prove that a particular political exchange produced ATT or that each ATT is identical to one Landauer bit.

An economic **tally** is the matched release/admission receipt pair across a particular border, bound to the *same case, resource set, paths and identities*. An unmatched half is an assertion or pending receipt, **not** a settled transfer. Economic tally and the two tallies inside Record are different mathematical usages. The corpus explicitly lists this denomination bridge as unproved. A matching receipt for one case is not a receipt for an unrelated case.

An **obligation** is a charged relation with a specific owing party, beneficiary and required performance. Lenticule's [money-as-witness-forgetting founding note](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-money-as-witness-forgetting.md) interprets such a relation as witnessed by its parties and consumed once on discharge, with linear-logic aspirations. [Emergent obligation](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emergent-obligation.md) places its source in the situated relation, rather than a prefabricated universal duty. That proposed linear-logic identification is a research thesis, **not a general theorem**. Witnessing an event does not impose the same duty on every viewer.

These structures are not a WIT→ACK→ATT **ladder**. The spiral changes its object: the second face ACKs the witnessed distinction; the first face then WITs that ACK.

The WIT, ACK and ATT lenses can also be applied independently or in a configured composition. A WIT does not require the existence of a later ACK, an external ACK does not compel the original agent to take an ATT of it, and an unmatched half-tally remains unsettled. The spiral states the typed relationship **when** its turns actually exist; it is not a mandatory workflow that every witnessed claim completes.

## II. The two cases the electoralist argument conflates

Let **c** be the issue-specific claim about a harmed relation. Let **O(c)** be an obligation arising from that relation, specifying an obligor, beneficiary, grounds, performance condition and authority for discharge. Let **b** be a separate electoral act.

There may be WITs bearing on c and its Record; political parties and election institutions may also deal with b. The latter has its own possible independently issued acknowledgment, ACK(b), and, if the originator actually witnesses the acknowledgment, its own ATT(b) = WIT(ACK(b)). If independent release and admission match for b, there may be a legitimate tally concerning **b**. Do not infer that every actual ballot has a voter-identifiable receipt satisfying an economic tally contract; ballot secrecy and election procedures vary.

**Electoral absolution is the attempted inference:**

> Settled(b), or even ATT(b), therefore Discharged(O(c)).

**That inference is invalid without an additional, specific performance/transfer relation linking b to the exact O(c).** The electoral receipt witnesses an action concerning representation. Discharging the harmed relation's charged obligation would require the actual performance condition, appropriate receiving party and evidence of admission for **O(c)**. A general "responsible voter" status does not identify those objects.

This is a *type/scope error*: a fully acknowledged election-side crossing is silently used as the missing receipt for a different relation. It is possible for the electoral act to be meaningful, legitimate and legally counted **and** for the original obligation to remain wholly open.

### A small countermodel

Suppose the claim about an injured community has Record(c)=(2,1), hence FOUR charge both/⊤. The elector separately performs a ballot action b. An independent election-side receiver really acknowledges it, the elector witnesses that ACK (so ATT(b) exists), and b's relevant receipt pair matches. Meanwhile the community has not received the promised repair and O(c) is still open.

The conjunction

**ATT(b) ∧ MatchedTally(b) ∧ Open(O(c))**

does not contradict the stated WIT/ACK/ATT/tally definitions. Thus ATT(b) and settlement of b do **not entail** Discharged(O(c)). This is a clearly scoped *mathematical countermodel*, not an invented Lean-checked theorem and not an observed claim about actual voters.

The status of Record(c) also matters. Its three attestation counts are not three FOUR wits, and a contested claim being ACKed does not erase its negative tally or force the charge from both/⊤ to true.

## III. Three ways a receipt can be misread

**Self-certified completion.** Someone publicly asserts "I voted, so I have done everything required." That person's own assertion is not, by itself, an external ACK of either the ballot or the harmed community. It cannot mint its own matched tally. External applause for the declaration may ACK *that declaration* without certifying O(c).

**A real electoral receipt, wrong beneficiary.** An election authority acknowledges b and the elector takes ATT(b). This is real attention in the technical sense: the original face witnesses uptake from another face. But it is attention to the electoral ACK. It does not create ATT(c), because the receiving event refers to a different claim. A completed electoral tally can be falsely presented as a tally for O(c) without any recipient on the harmed-relation side admitting performance. This is the proposed central mechanism.

**Genuine discharge or accountable delegation.** If the relevant obligation actually is to perform b, then verified performance of b can fulfill that particular obligation. If an authorized representative takes custody of O(c), carries out its required work and is independently received by the proper beneficiary or institution, the obligation may be discharged through that chain. The specification and acceptance must be evidenced; neither the election itself nor a candidate's statement substitutes for them.

The third case prevents the argument from becoming an empty denunciation of all electoral participation. The test is which charged relation was actually settled.

## IV. Electoralism as provenance-forgetting

Our existing money-science thesis describes a bearer instrument as a charged obligation whose provenance—who owes what to whom—has been forgotten, facilitating circulation while obscuring original relations. We can investigate a structurally similar political move, without prematurely asserting a proven categorical functor.

The **electoralist projection** takes a complicated witnessed harm, with actors and unfulfilled obligations, and translates it into the legible status *participated in an election*. The second status has a real institutional receiver and simple completion criterion. It can then be made to stand for the first, although its receipt lacks the original beneficiaries, required remedy, and unfulfilled burden.

That is how recognition can produce apparent innocence without producing repair. Electoral participation is counted and acknowledged; the harmed relation is not thereby settled. The political work accomplished by the false equivalence is the **erasure of the residual charged relation from the participant's claimed ledger of obligations**.

This is also the precise attention hypothesis. **ATT(b)** is a WIT of an electoral ACK; it cannot be substituted for **ATT(c)**, which would require a witnessed ACK for the distinct claim and its own receiving boundary. An electoral completion narrative may redirect finite available attention toward the acknowledged ballot and away from additional issue-specific WIT–ACK–ATT turns or remedy work. Scarcity is mathematically motivated by the [allocation theorem](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Cohomology/AttentionAllocation.lean), but the *crowding-out effect on actual people* must be measured rather than pretended proven.

The claim is therefore stronger than "voting does not do enough": **one true tally is being advertised as another tally that does not exist**.

## V. Scientific discrimination

**Structural assay.** For any claimed public political completion, demand a specified source claim/cell, an observable FOUR cover if calling it WIT, a genuinely different party's ACK, an observed second-order WIT if calling it ATT, and independent matching receipts if calling it a tally. Then identify the exact obligation that is said to be discharged. Where did the original relation's required performance receive an independent admission? If the cited receipt instead concerns b, the discharge is not established. This assay is about typed evidence, not a judgment of voters.

**Behavioral hypothesis.** Compared with a matched framing that keeps an issue-specific obligation explicitly open, communications portraying a completed civic/electoral receipt as full settlement of that different issue will produce more incorrect attributions of Discharged(O(c)) and fewer subsequent verified issue-specific receiving/continuation events. That is a proposed causal effect of **completion framing**, not of merely casting a vote.

**Ethical experiment.** Use a fictional, nonpartisan civic situation with independent simulated issuers/receivers. Present the same harm evidence and the same simulated election-side action to every consenting participant. Randomize whether its separately verifiable receipt is described as completing all responsibility or as completing only the representation transaction while the injured relation remains open. Record independent receipt matching, participants' classification of the unfulfilled obligation, and whether they actually seek or produce the next *issue-specific* ACK or receipt. A minute spent reading is not automatically ATT; requiring the actual second-order witnessing event is the measurement advance.

**Controls and falsification.** Compare alternative explanations: crowding in from electoral participation, genuine accountable delegation, limited actor capacity, time pressure, threat, conflicting roles and institutional effectiveness. The behavioral hypothesis fails in a preregistered study if the completion message does not increase wrong-scope discharge inferences or reduce the issue-specific continuation events. If actors follow electoral receipts with stronger acknowledgment and remedial work, that is positive evidence against a universal crowding-out thesis. The existence of a mathematical non-entailment does not establish a population frequency or motivation.

**Literature with proper scope.** [Iris Marion Young's 2006 social connection model](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0265052506060043) treats political responsibility for structural injustice as forward-looking, shared, and requiring collective action: relevant for how to specify an obligation without assigning it indiscriminately to all witnesses. [Kristofferson, White and Peloza (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1086/674137) find that public/private visibility of small symbolic support can affect later helping in their experiments. Their participants did not necessarily vote; neither a click nor a petition signature is automatically a WIT, ACK, ATT or matched tally in our formal vocabulary. These are comparisons, not direct proofs of the proposed mechanism.

This work also relates to emsenn's earlier [*Between Care and Control*](https://emsenn.substack.com/p/between-care-and-control) (2025), which is an independently authored essay about liberal harm management and electoralism, not a published empirical verification of this theory.

## VI. Formal receiving

The mathematical obligation is to define and check a genuine relation between a claim-specific border receipt, an obligation-specific discharge, and the WIT/ACK/ATT spiral **using existing source-owned carriers**. A candidate non-entailment proof can start from independent obligation and electoral claims and the absence of a bridging settlement witness. It must not fabricate a new social-science tuple calculus or assume a functor whose laws remain open.

For any eventual empirical corpus, express the typed source assertions, opposing evidence, receiving events, and derived readings through the existing SignCell/SCA/SEN/SEAVT and SignChain/SignGraph carriers. Preserve observation time apart from receipt time, the provenance of each reading and its independent recipient. Do not create a parallel election-receipt format or turn the claim-scoped WIT into a generic statement counter. See [Science issue #60](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/60) for the guarded utterance→claim→evidence→settlement line.

In particular, keep separate: the [WitLedger instrument accounting](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/scripts/WitLedger.lean), FOUR knowledge-order covers, non-idempotent Record tallies, economic border matched tallies, the conjectured ACK/H⁰ correspondence, and the attention budget. The October 6 audit leaves their universal distinction-denomination equivalences **unproved**. The Political Science claim cannot silently complete that proof.

**Conclusion.** A witnessed harmed relation can remain unsettled after a completely genuine WIT–ACK–ATT turn and tally concerning a ballot. **Electoral absolution is the socially licensed substitution of that ballot's settled receipt for the unmet relation's missing receipt.** Research must establish where and when that substitution occurs—not change the definitions of WIT, ACK, ATT or tally to accommodate the rhetoric.
