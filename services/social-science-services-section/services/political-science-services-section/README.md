**Necessary distinctions for every political case:**

1. State the witnessed cell/claim, its evidence polarity and source; count WITs
   as actual covers in FOUR's knowledge order. A narrated event or a human
   witness is not automatically exactly one wit. A bottom-to-both jump
   traverses two covers.
2. Identify an ACK's *other-party* recipient and exact uptake of the asserted
   claim. An actor's own opinion or approval is not an ACK. A contested
   utterance can be acknowledged without becoming resolved.
3. ATT is a WIT **of the ACK**, not hours of cognitive focus. Use the
   attention-budget theorems for resource limits, not to redefine ATT.
4. Keep `Record.pos`/`Record.neg` evidence **tallies** separate from the
   counterparty **tally** matched across a border. The former retains
   multiplicity; the FOUR charge readout forgets it. The latter requires a
   matched release and admission, not a single receipt.
5. Identify the exact charged obligation, beneficiary, obligor, scope,
   conditions and independent discharge evidence. Read the already existing
   [Emergent Obligation](../../content/text/glossary/terms/emergent-obligation.md)
   and the [money-science witness-forgetting hypothesis](../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-money-as-witness-forgetting.md);
   do not promote its research sketch to a theorem.
6. For elections, identify whether a ballot/vote is actually evidenced,
   accepted, counted, and acknowledged. Do **not** infer from any such event
   that a different obligation was discharged. A campaign's assertion of
   completion and the affected party's acceptance are separate propositions.
7. Keep source objects, independent receiving, and counterexamples in the
   research; separate a mathematical non-entailment from a causal claim
   about people's behavior. Neither voters' personal beliefs nor real ballots
   should be collected when a nonpartisan, simulated case suffices.
