## Working research method: political responsibility and uptake

For work on political witnessing, acknowledgment, attention, obligation,
representation and participation, begin with the exact existing theory:
[relational witnessing](../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/witnessing.md),
[recognition](../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recognition.md),
and [emergent obligation](../../content/text/glossary/terms/emergent-obligation.md).
Do not collapse these into a single engagement score or confuse social
acknowledgment with structural category-recognition.

For each research claim:
1. State whether it is a normative premise, definitional distinction,
   testable mechanism, documented observation, or unresolved inference.
2. Record who witnessed what and with which evidence, who acknowledged the
   testimony or claim, who classified it into a normative category, what
   attention was actually expended, and what obligation was allegedly met.
3. Identify the relation, role, claimant, capacity, authority, and conditions
   that generate any alleged obligation. A witness report alone does not
   impose an identical obligation on every observer.
4. Do not treat voting, a public statement, or the mere assignment of a
   representative as proof of an obligation's discharge. Identify concrete
   fulfillment, valid waiver, or accepted accountable delegation, and also
   any undischarged remainder.
5. Compare electoral substitution with electoral mobilization, resource
   scarcity, burnout, institutional responsiveness, social desirability,
   and sustained collective action. State what data could falsify each.
6. Keep analysis of political mechanisms distinct from advice about any
   actual candidate, party, election, or individual's vote.

The source-owned, **unreviewed** research question is in
[Witness, acknowledgment, attention and obligation under electoralism](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/14/2026-10-14-witness-acknowledgment-attention-obligation-electoralism.md).
The paper is hypothesis-generating; its existence is not evidence for the
claimed electoral mechanism.
