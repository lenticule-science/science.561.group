Branch and sequel plans are a paradigm for planning under uncertainty.

- A **branch** is a contingency option within the current plan (if X happens, we do Y).
- A **sequel** is a subsequent plan based on a possible outcome of the current operation (if we succeed/fail/partially succeed, what next).

The value of branches and sequels is that they turn uncertainty into explicit decision points and preparation tasks.
