The discipline concerned with how knowledge is transmitted, how learning occurs, and how teaching is designed. Organized into three subdomains: knowledge-transmission (the social and institutional mechanisms by which knowledge moves between people), learning (theories of how individuals acquire knowledge and skill), and pedagogy (the design and practice of teaching).
