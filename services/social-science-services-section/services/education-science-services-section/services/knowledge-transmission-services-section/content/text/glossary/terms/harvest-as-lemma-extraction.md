**Harvest as lemma extraction** is a design proposal, developed for a self-care and task-management concept called "the grove," that treats the completion of a witnessed endeavor as licensing the extraction of a reusable artifact — concretely, a `SKILL.md`, the agent-skill format already in use elsewhere in the estate — rather than merely marking the endeavor done and setting it aside.

The formal move behind the name: under the estate's proof-pathing doctrine ("the path is the derivation; an unearned segment is a lie about a type"), a growing endeavor is treated as a derivation-in-progress toward a goal-type. Once that goal-type is witnessed inhabited — the endeavor genuinely complete, not merely abandoned — the Curry-Howard correspondence's proofs-are-programs reading licenses extraction: the endeavor's structure, emptied of the work of getting there, becomes an invocable instrument usable on future endeavors. The source note gives this a second reading in Yoneda's arrows-first framing: the completed endeavor is a morphism, and once witnessed complete it joins the person's arrow-bundle as a possession composable into every future path.

What makes this a knowledge-transmission concept rather than only a personal-productivity one is the harvested artifact's giftability. A `SKILL.md` extracted this way is proposed to be shareable along a kinship or community graph — a completed process, with its provenance intact, given the way a completed physical object might be given. The source note frames this explicitly as "the FoaF layer generalized from recordings to processes": the same kin-weighted, consent-carrying sharing structure proposed elsewhere in this design program for voice recordings, applied instead to a transmissible unit of earned competence. Read this way, the concept extends what [apprenticeship](apprenticeship.md) and [embodied transmission](embodied-transmission.md) already do — competence developed through sustained practice, passed to another person — by giving the transmitted unit a parameterizable, machine-invocable form in addition to its human-readable one.

The source note is explicit that this remains unpracticed: its own closing section grades every open question here — the concrete shape of a harvest ceremony, who authors the resulting `SKILL.md`, how grades attach to it — "All Research-grade until practiced." Nothing about this term should be read as describing a working system; it names a design proposal and the formal argument for why the proposal is coherent, not a demonstrated transmission practice.
