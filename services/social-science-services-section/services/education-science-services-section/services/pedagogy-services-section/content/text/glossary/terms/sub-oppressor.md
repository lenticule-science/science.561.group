A sub-oppressor, in Paulo Freire's *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1970 English translation by Myra Bergman Ramos), is one of the oppressed who has internalized the oppressor's model of what it is to be human, and who, given any position of relative power over other oppressed people, exercises that power the way the oppressor exercised power over them. Freire's account of the oppressor / oppressed dialectic holds that the oppressed do not automatically carry a liberatory consciousness by virtue of being oppressed: their image of what a full human being looks like has been formed by the only model of humanity the oppressive structure has shown them — the oppressor — so that acquiring any authority tends, absent a deliberate break, to reproduce oppression rather than end it.

A sub-oppressor is distinguished from the oppressor proper, who occupies the structural position that organizes the whole relation of oppression and benefits from it systemically, not merely locally. It is also distinguished from Freire's "false generosity" — the oppressor's own paternalistic concessions to the oppressed, which leave the structure of oppression intact while appearing to soften it. The sub-oppressor's violence, unlike the oppressor's false generosity, is not a concession from the top of the structure but a redirection from within it, oppressed person against oppressed person.

The consequence is for [conscientization](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/conscientization.md): liberation cannot mean only that the previously oppressed acquire power, because power acquired without a transformed model of what humanity and authority are will reproduce sub-oppression rather than end the oppressor / oppressed dialectic. Freire's alternative to the [banking model](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/banking-model.md) of imposed knowledge is meant to interrupt exactly this: education as mutual inquiry rather than domination, so that the oppressed do not simply inherit the oppressor's model of what a human relation to power looks like.
