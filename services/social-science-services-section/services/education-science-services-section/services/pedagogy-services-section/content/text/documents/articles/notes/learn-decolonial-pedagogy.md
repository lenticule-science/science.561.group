# Learn Decolonial Pedagogy

## What you will be able to do

- Identify whether an educational practice reproduces the banking model (teacher deposits knowledge into passive students) and articulate what makes it so.
- Describe problem-posing education as an alternative and explain how it differs structurally from the banking model.
- Given a knowledge system (a curriculum, a library classification, a database schema), identify whose knowledge it centers, what forms of knowledge it privileges, and what it excludes.
- Explain why "decolonize" is not a synonym for "diversify" or "critically examine" — what [decolonization](../../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/povinellian-services-section/services/settler-colonialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/decolonization.md) specifically requires (following [Eve Tuck](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/eve-tuck.md) and [K. Wayne Yang](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/k-wayne-yang.md)).

## Scope

This skill covers the major thinkers and frameworks in decolonial pedagogy as represented in the vault: [Paulo Freire](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/paulo-freire.md), [bell hooks](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/bell-hooks.md), [Linda Tuhiwai Smith](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/linda-tuhiwai-smith.md), Sandy Grande, [Leanne Betasamosake Simpson](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/leanne-simpson.md), and [Eve Tuck](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/eve-tuck.md) and [K. Wayne Yang](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/k-wayne-yang.md).

It does not cover:

- Lesson design or curriculum design (covered by separate pedagogy skills that depend on this one)
- The full breadth of critical pedagogy (Henry Giroux, Peter McLaren, and others are mentioned but not developed)
- Indigenous pedagogies in depth (the schools/indigenous-pedagogies page goes deeper, but land-based education in particular cannot be learned through text alone — it requires sustained relationship with place)
- Non-anglophone decolonial thought ([Frantz Fanon](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/frantz-fanon.md), Aníbal Quijano, Walter Mignolo, and others are not yet represented)

## Verification

After completing the lesson, try this: pick any educational practice you have experienced (a class, a textbook, an online course, a training program). Describe it in terms of the banking model: who holds the knowledge, how does it flow, what role does the learner play? Then describe what a problem-posing alternative would look like. Finally, identify one [epistemological](../../../../../philosophy/terms/epistemology.md) assumption the practice makes — what kind of knowledge does it treat as legitimate, and what does it exclude?
