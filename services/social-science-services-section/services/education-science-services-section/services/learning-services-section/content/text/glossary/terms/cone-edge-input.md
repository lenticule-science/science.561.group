**Cone-edge input** names material whose arrow-bundle — its network of grammatical and semantic dependencies — lands mostly in a learner's already-witnessed territory, leaving only a small unwitnessed remainder to be acquired. It is the computable version of "next material": not chosen by a fixed syllabus order, but read off directly from what a given learner has already demonstrably encountered.

The term restates two established ideas in the vocabulary of reverse-lightcone geometry rather than replacing either:

- **Krashen's i+1** (comprehensible input one step beyond current competence) names the same target from the standpoint of input design.
- **[Zone of proximal development](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/zone-of-proximal-development.md)** names the same target from the standpoint of the learner-in-relationship — the space where the right support enables growth that would not occur alone.

Cone-edge input adds a specific mechanism for locating that target: the forward light cone. Given a learner's witnessed history — everything they have demonstrably read, heard, or produced — an item's dependencies define a set of prerequisite arrows. When most of those arrows already fall inside the learner's witnessed cone, the item sits at the cone's edge: reachable, not yet reached. The tutoring move is to surface exactly that edge, continuously, rather than advance a learner through pre-authored levels. This is the forward-facing half of a pair with the [reverse-lightcone emergence function](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/reverse-lightcone-emergence-function.md) (𝓡): 𝓡 run backward from a desired target reconstructs the generative envelope of prior acquisitions that would make it reachable; cone-edge input is what the same geometry reads forward, moment to moment, from where the learner already stands.

This is a design hypothesis, not a settled instructional finding. The working theory that motivates it names its own falsifiable obligation directly: "Cone-edge input outperforms off-cone input in acquisition, measured in practice records," with the Mandarin learning stack proposed as the first apparatus. Until that obligation runs, the term names a design commitment and a measurement plan, not a demonstrated effect — it should not be read as an upgrade to i+1 or to ZPD, both of which remain the concepts' own canonical, independently evidenced accounts.
