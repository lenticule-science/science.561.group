# Sexual Political Economy

**Sexual political economy** is Adrienne D. Davis's framework for analyzing sexual domination under American slavery through the connected operation of property law, markets, labor command, reproduction, and constrained intimate relations. It rejects the treatment of sexual violence as private misconduct outside economic organization.

The framework does not mean every intimate relation can be reduced to price or that every enslaved woman had the same experience. It directs inquiry toward the institutional powers structuring an encounter: ownership, sale, punishment, mobility, household position, material exchange, pregnancy, kin vulnerability, and legal nonrecognition.
