# Presence of Force

**Presence of force** names Emily A. Owens's approach to sexual violence and Black women's survival under slavery. A payment, gift, promise, or repeated arrangement occurred within a structure already governed by captivity, punishment, racial law, and unequal access to material security. Evidence of exchange therefore cannot by itself establish consent.

The framework also resists making every constrained decision indistinguishable. Analysis must locate the powers and alternatives present in a particular encounter, attend to what the historical actor said, and preserve what the record cannot settle. The presence of force is a condition of interpretation, not permission to overwrite a person's strategy with a single predetermined meaning.
