# Geography and History

Geography and history are the disciplines of place and time — where things happen and when. Together they constitute the material record of human and natural events: the landscapes that shape societies and the sequences of events that explain how the present came to be.

This root holds two domains:

**Geography** covers the physical and human geography of the world: landforms, climate, regions, and the spatial organization of human settlement, including bioregionalism and place-based ecological knowledge.

**History** covers the interpretation and narration of the past: historical method, historiography, and the histories of particular places, peoples, and events. Where geography asks where, history asks when — and both ask why.
