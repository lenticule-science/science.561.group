Temporal autonomy is the condition of collectively determining one's own relationship to time — the capacity to deliberate, rest, celebrate, mourn, and organize on rhythms not dictated by capital, [the state](./the-state.md), or colonial time-discipline. For [anarchist](../_index.md) practice, temporal autonomy is not a luxury but a structural precondition: non-hierarchical organization is impossible under temporal regimes that demand the speed only hierarchy can provide.

The concept names what is destroyed when dominant institutions impose their temporalities. [Capitalism](./capitalism.md) imposes the temporality of accumulation: faster turnover, shorter cycles, just-in-time production, the compression of every interval between investment and return. The [state](./the-state.md) imposes the temporality of administration: fiscal years, electoral cycles, regulatory timelines, the deadline. [Settler colonialism](../../povinellian/theories/settler-colonialism/terms/settler-colonialism.md) imposed industrial time-discipline on Indigenous peoples as a technology of dispossession — replacing seasonal, ceremonial, and relational temporalities with the clock, the shift, and the wage. Each of these impositions narrows the temporal space in which alternatives can be organized, experienced, and sustained.

[Consensus decision-making](./horizontal-organization.md) requires temporal autonomy because genuine consensus cannot be rushed — the process of hearing all positions, working through disagreement, and arriving at a decision everyone can live with takes the time it takes. When the situation demands an immediate response, the structure that responds fastest is the structure with a command chain. This is why the demand to "be more efficient" is always implicitly a demand to accept hierarchy: efficiency is speed, and speed selects for vertical organization. The slowness of [anarchist](./anarchism.md) process is a feature, not a deficiency.

The [refusal of work](../../marxism/theories/autonomism/terms/refusal-of-work.md) is partly a claim to temporal autonomy — the insistence on not being productive on capital's schedule. [Direct action](./direct-action.md) operates on its own timeline, neither waiting for institutional permission nor racing to meet institutional deadlines. [Prefigurative politics](./prefigurative-politics.md) insists on the time needed for non-hierarchical process even when urgency demands shortcuts. The connection to [speed](./speed.md) is direct: [Virilio](../../../../../../../../references/people/paul-virilio.md)'s dromology shows that the acceleration of production, governance, and warfare serves the [state](./the-state.md) and capital; temporal autonomy is the anarchist counter-claim — the insistence that communities, not institutions, determine the rhythm of their own social life.
