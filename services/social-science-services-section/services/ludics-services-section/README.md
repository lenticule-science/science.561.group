# Ludics Services Section

The Ludics Services Section studies ludics as Jean-Yves Girard's
proof-theoretic interaction semantics (designs, daimon, orthogonality,
behaviours) — distinct from the general theory of play (Huizinga,
Caillois, Sutton-Smith) housed at fuller depth under the Game Science
Services Section.

Its one substantive document,
`content/documents/notes/questions-on-girard-caillois-substrate-correspondence.md`,
is an open research question asking whether Girard's proof-theoretic
ludics and Caillois's cultural theory of play are two presentations of
the same underlying algebraic object — a conjectured faithful embedding
of cultural-ludic structures into paraconsistent dialogical games on the
estate's predication-fiber substrate, worked out as thirteen structural
correspondences. Because that note has to ground both sides of the
comparison, `content/documents/terms/` holds a small glossary spanning
both vocabularies rather than only Girard's: `design.md` and
`daimon.md` on the proof-theoretic side, and `agon.md`, `alea.md`,
`mimicry.md`, and `ilinx.md` — Caillois's four categories of play — on
the cultural side, each cited to its source (Girard's *Locus Solum*,
2001, and Caillois's *Man, Play and Games*, 1958/2001). The note also
names several other terms (`magic-circle`, `paidia`, `ludus`, `flow`)
and a `../texts/` curriculum reference that remain unwritten here; the
note's own thirteen correspondences do not depend on them, and they are
Huizinga's, Caillois's paidia–ludus axis, and Csikszentmihalyi's
vocabulary respectively rather than the Girard/Caillois pair this
section's glossary was built to ground.

The note's conjecture is stated as open and unsettled, not established;
its own frontmatter marks it `status: open`. Several supporting math-side
claims it cites as already proved (the four-regime dichotomy, the
strong-MLD orbit stratification) live in the Obsmetrologia project under
the math services section.
