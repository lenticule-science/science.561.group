# Market Science Services Section

Market science is the social science of markets and marketing: how buyers actually behave, how brands are built, valued, and measured (brand equity, brand personality, mental and physical availability), and how branding extends to persons — self-branding, personal branding, and the influencer-marketing apparatus.
It seats under `social-science-services-section` because a market is agents in economic relation, and "market" is one of the subdomains the parent `agent-science-services-section` README already declares for social science.

## Seating derivation

Per `science-services-section`'s ownership rule, a note has one canonical owner at its most specific defensible service section.
A library survey (2026-08-22) found no marketing, brand, or market science anywhere under `science-services-section`; the only occurrences of "marketing" as a science were package names inside legacy npm-registry storage (`@lenticule-science/marketing-services-section`), which is storage artifact, not library content.
The operations forest holds `enterprise-marketing-services-section` and `enterprise-branding-services-section` — operational sections that *do* marketing for the group's enterprises — and the public forest manages actual personal and corporate brands.
Those practices have had no science section to draw on.

`agent-science-services-section`'s README (founded 2026-08-21) lists social science's subdomains as "business, critical theory, education, legalism, ludics, market, military science, organization, public history, sociology, and work."
This section takes the "market" seat, as `social-history-science-services-section` took the public-history seat.
Brand science is founded as this section's first literature rather than as a separate section, since the brand literature is the part of marketing science the group's practice most directly needs.

## Relations to existing content (not moved)

- `enterprise-marketing-services-section` and `enterprise-branding-services-section` (operations forest) remain the operational homes; this section supplies the literature they can cite.
- `social-media-science-services-section` (media-science forest) owns influencer/creator *media* science; this section owns influencer *marketing* — effectiveness, disclosure, endorsement — and the two sections cross-link at that boundary.
- The competitive-intelligence and business-persona research founded 2026-08-22 in the foraging/competitive-intelligence work remains canonical where it was seated; market-teardown method relates here.

## Scope

This section owns:

- Brand science: brand equity (Aaker, Keller's customer-based brand equity), brand personality, brand positioning.
- Empirical-generalization marketing science: the Ehrenberg-Bass tradition (double jeopardy, duplication of purchase, mental and physical availability), held distinct from the equity tradition.
- Personal branding and self-branding scholarship, corporate-brand scholarship, and influencer marketing.
- Marketing-science method: what in the field is peer-reviewed empirical generalization versus textbook canon versus trade advocacy.

It does not own: consumer psychology at large (a future social- or cognitive-science seat), advertising operations, or the group's own brand assets and campaigns (operations and public forests).
