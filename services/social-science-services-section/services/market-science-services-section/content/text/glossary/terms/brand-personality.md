Brand personality is anchored in the peer-reviewed literature by Jennifer L. Aaker's ["Dimensions of Brand Personality"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/002224379703400304) (*Journal of Marketing Research* 34(3), 1997, pp. 347–356), a psychometric scale-development study — not merely a theoretical proposal — that identifies five dimensions via factor analysis of consumer ratings of real brands: **Sincerity**, **Excitement**, **Competence**, **Sophistication**, and **Ruggedness**, explicitly modeled on the "Big Five" human-personality tradition.
This is the single most cited source underlying "brand personality" as a construct, and claims using the five-dimension framework should trace to this paper rather than to secondary textbook restatements.

That framework has a direct, peer-reviewed methodological critique.
Audrey Azoulay and Jean-Noël Kapferer's ["Do brand personality scales really measure brand personality?"](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/palgrave.bm.2540162) (*Journal of Brand Management* 11(2), 2003, pp. 143–155) argues that existing brand-personality scales, including Aaker's, in practice conflate several distinct facets of brand identity — physical attributes, user-image reflections, and self-image projections — of which personality proper is only one, so the scales do not cleanly isolate the construct they claim to measure.
**[UNVERIFIED]**: no peer-reviewed rebuttal or resolution settling this critique against Aaker's original scale was located; the methodological dispute appears open in the literature reviewed for this section.

Brand personality should be distinguished from [brand equity](brand-equity.md), which concerns a brand's overall differential value and knowledge structure rather than its perceived trait profile specifically, and it is a separate construct from [personal branding](personal-branding.md), which applies branding logic to individual people rather than to product/corporate brands.

See the [study of brand and marketing science](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-study-of-brand-and-marketing-science.md) for the fuller literature review, including the open status of the Azoulay & Kapferer critique.
