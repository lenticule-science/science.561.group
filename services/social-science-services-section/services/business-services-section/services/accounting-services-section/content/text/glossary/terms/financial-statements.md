Financial statements are the formal reports that summarize an entity's financial position, performance, and cash flows over a given period.

A complete set and its names depend on the reporting framework. Common primary
statements include a [balance sheet](./balance-sheet.md), an
[income statement](./income-statement.md) or statements of performance and
comprehensive income, a [cash flow statement](./cash-flow-statement.md), and a
statement of changes in equity, together with notes and comparative information
when required.

Financial statements are derived from controlled records, adjustments,
measurement judgments, consolidations where applicable, and presentation rules.
A [trial balance](./trial-balance.md) proves debit-credit equality, not
completeness or faithful classification. An external audit, when one is
performed, provides an opinion under a specified reporting and assurance
framework; it does not create the statements or guarantee every fact in them.
