# Game Ludics Services Section

The Game Ludics Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for the
general theory of play (Huizinga and Caillois). It is a subsection of social
theory's Ludics Science Services Section, alongside that section's
proof-theoretic reading of Girard.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

## Computer-game research portfolio

`content/text/documents/articles/` holds the portfolio on persistent worlds,
their play-boundaries, institutions, observation, time, and shared-state
settlement: six research notes, their reading guides, an annotated
bibliography, the biographies needed to read the field, and the MUD design
note that applies ludics to a game without defining a reusable runtime.
`content/text/glossary/topics/` holds the related MUD, LPMud, mudlib, and world-building
accounts. These are research materials, not a runtime or an application.

`content/text/glossary/terms/` holds the portfolio's operative vocabulary:
play-boundaries, persistent-world and MUD practices, player behavior, virtual
economy, observability, and distributed game-state settlement. A term remains
here when it describes a game-system phenomenon; a reusable implementation
contract belongs in Engineering.
