# Generation-10 pilot: automatic valuation-nursery screening

Generation 9 established that predicate existence and measurability do not imply authority to govern selection.

Generation 10 tests the next step:

> Can candidate predicates enter a zero-authority valuation nursery automatically and be screened by replay, without a human hand-picking which candidate should be tried?

## Important limitation

The current estate contains a proved vocabulary-fold theorem and open dynamic-predicate-category work, but this session found no current living runtime operator that actually emits promoted predicate-atoms from settled-partnered entities.

The Generation-10 candidate source is therefore a **controlled vocabulary-fold fixture**, not a live replay over runtime-emitted folded predicates.

The fixture's nomination rule is still automatic within the experiment:

`nominate all newly grounded measurable predicates`.

No candidate is hand-selected after the candidate list is emitted.

## Existing valuation baseline

The nursery baseline V uses two already-admitted substantive dimensions:

- information gain;
- reachable-state expansion.

On the held-out replay set, the deliberately imperfect baseline reaches 0.50 accuracy.

The purpose is not to create a production classifier. The fixture needs enough error for incremental discrimination to be observable.

## Automatically nominated predicate candidates

Four grounded/measurable predicates enter the nursery:

1. `witnessed-boundary-invocation-count`
2. `frontier-has-any-invocation`
3. `independent-discriminating-assay-pass`
4. `full-trace-semantic-reconstruction-score`

All begin with zero authority outside the nursery.

## Replay criteria

The checker keeps separate:

- held-out discrimination;
- incremental discrimination beyond baseline V;
- reactivity/manipulation susceptibility;
- redundancy;
- measurement cost.

Hard-core compatibility, lineage-diversity effect, and authority scope remain required dimensions for a fuller experiment but are fixed by construction in this fixture.

## Candidate 1: witnessed boundary invocation count

Held-out accuracy: 0.75.

Incremental accuracy over baseline: +0.25.

Measurement cost: 1.

But an actor under selection can increase the metric by repeating successful boundary plays without changing the declared substantive outcome.

Verdict: **reject-reactive**.

The nursery therefore rejects a predicate that is statistically more discriminating than the current baseline.

## Candidate 2: frontier has any invocation

Held-out accuracy: 0.75.

Incremental accuracy: +0.25.

Measurement cost: 1.

It is reactive: one gratuitous play crosses the zero/nonzero boundary.

Across the complete replay fixture it is also perfectly redundant with the nonzero truth-value of invocation count.

Verdict: **reject-reactive-and-redundant**.

## Candidate 3: independent discriminating assay pass

Held-out accuracy: 1.00.

Incremental accuracy: +0.50.

Measurement cost: 3.

The candidate under selection cannot directly write the independent assayer's result, so it survives the tested reactivity control.

Verdict: **retain-experimental-valuation**.

This is not promotion to ordinary V. It remains nursery-only pending stronger replay, hard-core, diversity, and authority evidence.

## Candidate 4: full trace semantic reconstruction score

Held-out accuracy: 1.00.

Incremental accuracy: +0.50.

Measurement cost: 80 against a nursery ceiling of 10.

Verdict: **reject-cost**.

This is the important positive-control failure: a perfectly discriminating predicate can still be institutionally unfit because acquiring it consumes more resources than the nursery allows.

## Selection result

Generation 10 provisionally selects the valuation nursery discipline:

1. vocabulary admission grants no valuation authority;
2. all eligible new grounded/measurable predicates may be nominated automatically into a bounded zero-authority nursery;
3. replay may test their marginal usefulness;
4. useful-looking predicates can still be rejected for manipulation, redundancy, cost, hard-core, diversity, or authority reasons;
5. survival means `experimental-valuation`, not production promotion;
6. demotion remains available.

## Ancestor subtraction

The broad mechanisms are ancestral:

- feature selection and held-out evaluation are standard statistical/ML practice;
- adaptive promotion ladders exist in older 561 learning machinery;
- Goodhart/Campbell and governance-indicator theory already explain consequential metric reactivity;
- vocabulary fold and dynamic predicate-category work already cover predicate creation.

The experiment-specific contribution is the institutional separation of:

`predicate existence -> zero-authority nursery -> experimental valuation -> promoted valuation authority`

with replay and governance receipts between stages.

## What remains human-authored

The four folded predicate examples are fixture-authored because the live runtime does not currently emit fold candidates.

The screening dimensions and thresholds are also inherited/experiment-authored policy.

Therefore Generation 10 does not yet demonstrate autonomous nomination in the strong sense.

## Generation-11 target

Two gaps are now separable:

1. **candidate-source gap** — find or build the current living operation that enumerates vocabulary-fold candidates from settled content without promoting them;
2. **selection-policy evolution gap** — determine whether nursery admission dimensions/thresholds themselves can evolve without allowing the nursery to weaken its own anti-Goodhart and hard-core guards.

Generation 11 should attack the candidate-source gap first. Prefer a deterministic read-only fold-candidate census over settled state. Do not automatically edit the predicate category.
