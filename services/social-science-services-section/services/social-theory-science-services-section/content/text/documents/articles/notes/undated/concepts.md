# Concepts (predicated-governance)

Semantic anchors for the normative substructure of work. Each concept names a recurring *role* that one or more substrate constructions realize — not a mathematical object in itself.

- [authority-gate](../../../../glossary/concepts/authority-gate.md) — the residuation-based filter by which an actor's authority profile constrains which incoming payloads land in the substrate. The common pattern under delegation, dispatch, installation, and revocation.
- [deontic-modality](../../../../glossary/concepts/deontic-modality.md) — the bilattice-modal reading of the Hohfeldian quartets: power as $\Diamond_R$, duty as $\Box_R$, with privilege and immunity as their Klein-four conjugates. The structural reason the four kinds form a square rather than a list.
