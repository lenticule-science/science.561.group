# Generation-5 pilot: generating mutations from selection residue

This example continues
[Generation 4](second-order-aim-generativity-generation-4.md).

Generation 4 showed a limited result:

\[
(C,N,\Gamma_0)
\;\xrightarrow{m_t}\;
(C,N,\Gamma_1)
\]

and \(\Gamma_1\) produced a different class of candidate research aim from
\(\Gamma_0\).

But the experimenter supplied \(m_t\).
The human-imagination bottleneck therefore moved from agenda selection to
variation-operator selection.

Generation 5 tests a narrower mechanism:

> Can an unresolved selection dimension emitted by a \(\Gamma\)-trial be paired
> with an already-present but unused sensing capability in the same niche to
> derive candidate \(\Gamma\)-mutations?

This is not claimed as a novel evolutionary-computation principle.
Self-adaptive evolutionary operators, adaptive operator selection,
hyper-heuristics, and agentic variation operators are prior art.
The experiment asks whether the principle can be instantiated as an auditable
institutional-culture operation with typed provenance.

## Fixed hard core and niche

Use the same local hard core \(C\) as Generation 4:

1. exact carrier identity remains the law of direct action composition;
2. derived reads may not invent semantic compatibility or organizational intent;
3. lossy representation stays distinguishable from preservation;
4. candidate generation grants no execution authority;
5. evidence retains repository/artifact provenance.

The niche remains bounded to the inspected \`@emsenn/relating\` package and the
already-admitted historical evidence around the operations-manifold trial.

## Parent

The parent is \(\Gamma_1\), the temporal-frontier aim generator.

Its added noticing rule was:

> treat frontier trajectory — persistence, closure, recurrence, migration, and
> loss of observability — as potential evidence for selecting research
> questions.

Its Generation-4 output is
\`emsenn/relating#2\`, the temporal operations-manifold history question.

## Selection residue

The Generation-4 receipt did not merely say “needs improvement.”
It exposed specific missing evidence.

### \(R_1\): etiology

A persistent frontier has ambiguous cause.

Persistence alone cannot distinguish:

- an intentional architecture boundary;
- a representation incompatibility;
- an unimplemented capability;
- irrelevant residue.

### \(R_2\): operational relevance

\(\Gamma_1\) does not know whether the actions touching a persistent frontier are
actually exercised at a scripted boundary.

The manifold is structural.
It does not by itself say which registered operations appear in observed use.

### \(R_3\): observability

The experiment found that the operations-manifold product persisted while an
older runnable example disappeared from the current tree and documentation still
named it.

\(\Gamma_1\) can notice that change but does not have a general test for whether
a topology's assay surface is maintained.

These are **selection residues**: named evidence dimensions whose absence blocks
promotion or interpretation.

## Sensor inventory

Before evaluating new aims, the experiment froze three already-existing sensors
from the same niche.

### \(S_1\): representation projector

\`src/projections/kernel.mts\` already exposes:

- representation corners \`bottom\`, \`data\`, \`prose\`, \`assertion\`;
- completion rungs;
- decidable read classification;
- \`admitsTranscode\`;
- the explicit incomparable \`data <-> prose\` feature-swap diagonal.

This sensor can answer questions about representation comparability and declared
loss. It cannot answer whether two different carrier identities are the same
semantic type.

### \(S_2\): invocation witness

\`src/running/invocation.mts\` and the invocation-ledger service already expose a
one-EAVT-row witness for each successful scripted boundary play.

A row carries:

- caller;
- verb;
- arguments;
- optional serializable morphism carrier;
- returned value;
- witness time.

The store/feed namespace scopes the observation to a package/action family.

This sensor can answer bounded questions about **observed boundary use**.
Absence of a row is not evidence that an action is globally unused.

### \(S_3\): assay surface

The package already projects public resources, docs, tests, and runnable
examples from or around its action surfaces.

This sensor can answer whether a declared assay artifact is visible at a
specific repository cut.
It cannot prove that documentation completeness equals product correctness.

## Mutation-generation rule

For this rehearsal, a sensor may generate a mutation candidate only when the
sensor's already-declared observation directly addresses a named missing
evidence dimension.

The pairing must be stated as:

\[
(R_i,\; S_j,\; q_{ij})
\]

where \(q_{ij}\) says what question the sensor can answer about the residue.

Semantic resemblance between names is not enough.

This matching discipline is still authored by the experimenter.
Generation 5 therefore does **not** close the human-imagination bottleneck.

## Frozen mutation candidates

The candidates were recorded before evaluating which generated the best agenda.

### \(M_{\mathrm{repr}}=(R_1,S_1)\)

Add **representation etiology** to temporal-frontier valuation.

For a persistent frontier, ask whether the endpoint boundary presentations are:

1. exactly carrier-compatible;
2. representation-comparable with explicit loss or enrichment;
3. representation-incomparable under the projector lattice;
4. insufficiently represented to classify.

This never upgrades representation comparability into carrier compatibility.

### \(M_{\mathrm{use}}=(R_2,S_2)\)

Add **witnessed-use evidence** to temporal-frontier valuation.

For each frontier action in a bounded package/time scope, distinguish:

- observed scripted boundary plays;
- observed zero in an explicitly supplied complete interval;
- evidence unavailable.

Invocation frequency is not importance, truth, utility, or demand.
It is one additional observation about the operational niche.

### \(M_{\mathrm{assay}}=(R_3,S_3)\)

Add **assay-observability evidence**.

Track whether the declared tests/examples/resources used to inspect a frontier
remain visible and runnable across supplied cuts.

This does not infer product failure from documentation or example drift.

## Ancestor pressure

Broad versions of all three ideas have ancestry.

- representation-loss auditing is already native to \`relating\`;
- combining static architecture and runtime traces is established software
  architecture recovery;
- test/documentation drift is ordinary software-maintenance territory.

Therefore the mutations cannot earn credit merely for combining familiar
software-analysis modalities.

The experiment-specific question is whether they improve an institutional
\(\Gamma\)'s selection of *which capability unknowns deserve investigation*.

## Candidate-aim generation

### Output of \(M_{\mathrm{repr}}\)

Candidate question:

> Do persistent operations-manifold frontiers cluster by representation
> comparability class, and does that distinction help separate likely
> representation boundaries from missing carrier morphisms?

This is testable, but receives a major penalty:
wire/projector representation is not the law that defines carrier composition.
A correlation may be real without providing frontier etiology.

Selection: **retain as a negative-control candidate; do not instantiate work yet.**

### Output of \(M_{\mathrm{use}}\)

Candidate question:

> Does witnessed boundary use add discriminating information when selecting
> operations-manifold frontiers for investigation?

This became \`emsenn/relating#3\`,
“Research witnessed-use evidence for operations-manifold frontiers.”

Its identity discipline explicitly forbids a global verb-string join.
Package/action-family scope plus morphism evidence is required where available.

The bounded discriminator uses at least two frontiers and generates scripted
plays touching one but not the other, then asks whether the added use evidence
changes a predefined research-selection decision.

Selection: **provisional survivor.**

This is not novel software architecture recovery.
Its value, if any, must come from making an existing typed/witnessed institutional
substrate provide better evidence to \(\Gamma\).

### Output of \(M_{\mathrm{assay}}\)

Candidate question:

> Should frontier selection incorporate whether its own runnable assay surface is
> decaying?

This is substantially a restatement/generalization of the observability concern
that generated \(R_3\) in the first place.

Selection: **kill as an independent mutation in this generation**.
The residue did not gain enough new machinery to justify a distinct lineage.

## Selection result

Generation 5 retains two artifacts with different roles.

### \(M_{\mathrm{use}}\): provisional survivor

Reasons:

- generated mechanically from a named blocked dimension plus a pre-existing
  sensor;
- has a lawful identity-aware join;
- admits a cheap fixture;
- failure provides information;
- generated a candidate aim not emitted by the parent static/temporal
  frontier rule.

Blocks:

- broad static+dynamic architecture analysis is prior art;
- no real invocation corpus has yet demonstrated utility;
- no downstream reuse or institutional uptake;
- the matching vocabulary was authored by the experimenter.

### \(M_{\mathrm{repr}}\): negative control retained

It is useful precisely because it may fail.
The sensor can measure representation comparability, but the causal link from
that quantity to carrier-frontier etiology is unproved.

### \(M_{\mathrm{assay}}\): killed

It did not expand the search space enough beyond its own residue.

## Stronger external ancestor correction

Generation 5 also changes how second-order aim-generativity should be described.

The general evolutionary-computation problem of adapting the variation operator
is longstanding:

- self-adaptation evolves strategy parameters;
- adaptive operator selection chooses among operators using observed state or
  credit;
- hyper-heuristics search over heuristics rather than only solutions;
- automated mutation-operator design constructs new operators;
- recent agentic variation operators use autonomous agents with lineage,
  domain knowledge, and execution feedback.

Therefore the open 561 question is not:

> Can the system evolve the process that generates goals?

It is narrower:

> Can an institution represent the missing evidence exposed by selection,
> discover already-authorized sensing affordances that can measure that evidence,
> derive bounded mutations of its cultural aim-generator, and preserve the entire
> causal/institutional lineage without conflating candidate generation,
> valuation, truth, or authority?

## What Generation 5 demonstrates

Generation 5 weakly demonstrates:

\[
\text{structured selection residue}
+
\text{frozen sensor inventory}
+
\text{explicit matching rule}
\Longrightarrow
\text{multiple candidate } \Gamma\text{-mutations}
\]

and that those mutations can then be selected at the mechanism level.

It does **not** demonstrate autonomous variation in the strong sense.

The unresolved human contribution has moved again:

> The experimenter supplied the ontology that says what evidence a residue
> requires and what a sensor measures.

That is the next target.

## Generation-6 question

Can selection residues and sensing capabilities expose **machine-readable
measurement types** such that mutation proposals arise from type-compatible
evidence obligations rather than semantic matching by an experimenter?

The desired shape is approximately:

\[
R : \operatorname{needs}(E)
\qquad
S : \operatorname{produces}(E)
\]

so that a candidate mutation can be generated because the types compose:

\[
\operatorname{produces}(S)=\operatorname{needs}(R),
\]

not because a model decided two prose descriptions sounded related.

That would make variation generation resemble the operations-manifold lesson
itself: missing evidence becomes a typed frontier, and new cultural variation
arises by closing that frontier with a lawful sensor.
