A ***dormant project*** is a [project](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/project.md) that is [dormant](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dormant.md): not [abandoned](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abandoned.md) but not [active](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/active.md) either.
