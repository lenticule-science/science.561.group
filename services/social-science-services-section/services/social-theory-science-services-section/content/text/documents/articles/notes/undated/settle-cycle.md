# Settle cycle

The morphism $\Sigma_R : v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n} \mapsto v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_{n+1}}$ advancing an [operations settling](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/operations-settling.md) by one cadence step of a [recurrent practice](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/recurrent-practice.md) $R = (\mathcal{S}_R, T_R, \alpha_R)$ — the substrate-algebraic *one-cycle-of-work* operator.

## Data

Let $\tau_n, \tau_{n+1} \in T_R$ be consecutive cadence boundaries. The settle-cycle morphism is the closure-rule firing restricted to the cycle:

$$\Sigma_R(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n}) \;=\; \mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_R}\Bigl(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n} \;\vee_k\; \bigvee_{\tau_n \leq \tau < \tau_{n+1}} u_\tau\Bigr)$$

The construction takes the valuation at the cycle start, joins all increments recorded during the cycle, and applies the closure operator generated by the practice's local subsystem $\mathcal{S}_R$.

## Properties

**Idempotence on quiescent cycles.** If no increments are recorded during the cycle and $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n}$ is already $\mathcal{S}_R$-closed, then $\Sigma_R(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n}) = v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n}$.

**Monotonicity.** $v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n} \leq_k \Sigma_R(v^{\mathrm{op}}_{\tau_n})$. Settling a cycle never decreases knowledge-order content; per [settling-monotonicity](../../research/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md), this is the inherited monotonicity of the underlying predication settling.

**Composition via cadence.** $\Sigma_R^{n} = \Sigma_R \circ \cdots \circ \Sigma_R$ ($n$ times) advances the settling by $n$ cadence steps. The transfinite case $\Sigma_R^\mu$ for limit $\mu$ is given by the limit-stage rule of the underlying predication settling:

$$\Sigma_R^\mu = \bigsqcup_{n < \mu}^{(k)} \Sigma_R^n$$

— the knowledge-order join of all prior stage-settlings.

**Regime classification via iteration.** The regime of the recurrent practice $R$ is determined by how $\Sigma_R^n$ behaves under iteration. Per [regime-of-recurrent-practice](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-recurrent-practice.md):
- If $\Sigma_R^n = \Sigma_R^{n_0}$ for some finite $n_0$, the practice is in the *strict regime*.
- If the sequence stabilizes at $\Sigma_R^\omega$ but not before, the practice is in the *Scott-continuous regime*.
- If stabilization requires a transfinite limit step $\Sigma_R^\kappa$ for some $\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$, the practice is in the *transfinite-bounded regime*.
- If no countable ordinal $\kappa$ suffices, the practice is in the *aperiodic regime*.

The settle-cycle morphism is the generator of the practice's settling orbit; the orbit's stabilization behavior *is* the regime.

## Temporal 2-category perspective

The settle-cycle morphism $\Sigma_R$ is a 1-cell in the [temporal 2-category](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/temporal-2-category/temporal-2-category.md) $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}, \lambda)$:

$$\Sigma_R \;:\; (\tau_n, \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}) \;\longrightarrow\; (\tau_{n+1}, \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}})$$

The 1-cell encodes "advance the operations settling by one cycle interval $[\tau_n, \tau_{n+1})$." Concretely: $\Sigma_R = \mathbb{M}^\beta \mathbb{N}^\alpha$ where $\alpha$ is the number of intra-cycle closure-step applications (the $\mathbb{N}$-comonad advancing the settling within the cycle) and $\beta$ is the number of cycle-boundary-crossing steps (the $\mathbb{M}$-monad encoding the new-cycle transition).

The [KZ-lax-idempotent diamond](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/temporal-2-category/temporal-2-category.md) of 2-cells — the unit $\eta : 1 \Rightarrow \mathbb{M}$, counit $\epsilon : \mathbb{N} \Rightarrow 1$, multiplication $\mu : \mathbb{M}^2 \Rightarrow \mathbb{M}$, comultiplication $\delta : \mathbb{N} \Rightarrow \mathbb{N}^2$ — governs how within-cycle settling (the $\mathbb{N}$-comonad: closure-rule-firing inside a single cycle) and across-cycle settling (the $\mathbb{M}$-monad: the new-cycle-opening rules that aggregate the prior cycle's content and initiate the next) compose coherently.

**Strict regime simplification.** In the strict regime, $\mathbb{M}$ and $\mathbb{N}$ are strict-idempotent, the 2-cells in $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}, \lambda)$ are identities, and the temporal 2-category collapses to the settling bound $\lambda$ as a 1-category. The settle-cycle morphism in this regime is simply a finite-step advance — the temporal 2-categorical machinery is not needed and the formula $\Sigma_R = \mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_R}(- \vee_k u_{[\tau_n, \tau_{n+1})})$ is the full story.

**Multi-scale cadence as 1-cell composition.** A multi-scale cadence — daily cycles within weekly within monthly — corresponds to a compositional chain of 1-cells in $\mathbf{T}$:

$$\Sigma_{\mathrm{daily}} \circ \cdots \circ \Sigma_{\mathrm{daily}} = \Sigma_{\mathrm{weekly}}$$

with the weekly cycle morphism being the sequential composition of seven daily cycle morphisms. The temporal 2-category's associativity of 1-cell composition is the substrate-algebraic content of "a week of daily cycles is a week of work" — not just a convenient notation but a structural fact about how cycle morphisms compose in $\mathbf{T}$.

## Temporal Beck-Chevalley and cycle-query coherence

The [temporal Beck-Chevalley theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/temporal-2-category/references/temporal-beck-chevalley.md) ensures that existential quantification over cycle components commutes with reindexing along cycle boundaries. Operationally: for any pseudo-pullback square in $\mathbf{T}(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}, \lambda)$ involving settle-cycle morphisms, the canonical 2-cell

$$\exists^{k/t}_{p_A}(p_B^* \phi) \;\Rightarrow\; f^*(\exists^{k/t}_g \phi)$$

is invertible. This means queries of the form "was there some sub-event within cycle $n$ satisfying condition $\phi$?" can be computed from either the pre-cycle or post-cycle states — the existential over sub-events commutes with the cycle's temporal advance. No coordination or round-tripping is needed: existential cycle-internal queries are temporally coherent.

This is the operational ground for the [operations loop's](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/operations-loop.md) phase-level analysis: each phase's existential content ("did dispatch happen?", "did review occur?") is temporally BC-coherent across the cycle boundary.

## Normative changes at cycle boundaries

Cycle boundaries $\tau_n \in T_R$ are sites where normative changes frequently occur alongside operational changes. Per [normative-operational-layering](../../../../glossary/concepts/normative-operational-layering.md), a cadence cycle carries both Type O→O rules (task lifecycle advancement) and Type O→N and N→O rules (assignments creating duties, incumbency changes affecting authority).

At the cycle-start boundary $\tau_n$, two categories of increments are typical:

1. *Operational increments*: task completions, new task openings, dispatch events, witness records. These are the standard Type O→O events.
2. *Normative increments*: office rotations (per [rotational-authority](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/rotational-authority.md)), charter-rule firing on violations detected in the prior cycle, installation of new incumbents. These are Type O→N and N→O events.

The settle-cycle morphism applies $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_R}$ over the full cycle's increments — both types — producing a settled state that updates both layers simultaneously. The normative changes at the cycle boundary propagate via the N→O rules into the next cycle's operational state (e.g., a new incumbent's authority cells are settled at the cycle boundary; dispatch-eligibility rules read those authority cells in the subsequent operational closure).

## Cycle witnesses and the audit log

Per [witness-as-increment](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/witness-as-increment.md), each increment $u_\tau$ in $\bigvee_{\tau_n \leq \tau < \tau_{n+1}} u_\tau$ is a witness. The settle-cycle morphism's application of $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_R}$ produces additional witnesses (closure-rule-derived cells) layered on top of the direct increment-witnesses.

The cycle's audit log is the sequence of these witness-cells in chronological order of their stage $\tau$. A full audit of the cycle's events is a reading of the audit log; a compliance check is a check whether any charter-rule-derived violation-record cells appear in the log.

## Cadence boundary as settling-stage boundary

The cadence boundaries $T_R = \{\tau_0, \tau_1, \tau_2, \ldots\}$ are not externally imposed timestamps — they are *substrate-algebraic facts*. Each $\tau_n \in T_R$ is a stage at which the practice's cycle-transition closure rule fires, asserting a cycle-boundary-witness cell:

$$\langle R, \texttt{cycle-boundary-at}, \tau_n\rangle = \mathbf{t}$$

This cell fires the practice's cycle-start rules (which check the preceding cycle's settled content and open the new cycle's cells). The cadence is thus *endogenous to the settling*: a cadence-boundary is a stage at which the practice's cycle-transition rules fire, determined by either (a) elapsed-stage counts for strict calendrical cadences, or (b) ecological/operational conditions for trigger-driven cadences.

A community whose cadence is ecological (per [non-hohfeldian-governance-traditions](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/non-hohfeldian-governance-traditions.md)) has cycle-transition rules whose antecedents read ecological-state cells rather than clock-stage counts. The substrate-algebraic form is identical; only the antecedent changes.
