# Evidence-record propagation as candidate descent

**Withdrawn scope:** the earlier entry called its construction
a theorem and inferred that any message sharing produces a
witness-network descent datum, and any inconsistent receiving
cell produces a nonzero Čech \`H¹\` class. Those claims were
not supported by a specified site, restrictions or
cochain calculation. The paper is a **candidate specialization**,
not a proved result in Obsmetrologia.

## Data and sorts

Let \(\mathcal T\) be an explicitly enumerated
communication category/graph; its vertices are
agents/office readers. Define local evidence Record
carrier \(R_i(c)\in\mathbb N^2\) for each
predication cell \(c\) visible at reader \(i\).
A source event \(e\) has a source identity, claim
scope, evidence polarity, provenance and stage.
Its transport along a channel \(i\to j\)
is a **message or evidence-import proposal**.

- **Source evidence** \((e,i)\) is not one
  [WIT](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/wit.md)
  merely because it crossed a network.
- At any local cell \(c\), a FOUR WIT is **one**
  knowledge-order cover induced by a *local
  charge change*, not the number of messages
  or \`Record\` increments. A replayed
  positive attestation after positive
  evidence already exists has zero new
  FOUR covers at that polarity.
- An independent receiving
  [ACK](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ack.md)
  requires actual uptake of a specified
  WIT by another party; a message arrival,
  gossip relay or local merge isn't
  automatically an ACK.
- [ATT](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/att.md)
  has a bounded-allocation sort and
  a second-order WIT-of-ACK reading.
  Neither is defined by network traffic
  count.

## Fiber and descent hypothesis

Suppose \(p:\mathcal W\to\mathcal T\) is
a *specified*, cloven fibration where the
fiber at node \(i\) contains the admissible
cell-systems/readings over that local
context. For each channel \(f:i\to j\),
specify a transport/restriction map
\(f^*\) or \(f_!\), its variance,
functoriality and the actual source
evidence retained. Do not assert a
fibration just because the deployment
has a graph of communicating agents.

Given a cover \(\mathcal U\) over
the relevant site and transport
maps \(r_{ij}\), a proposed descent
datum is compatible on overlaps when

\[
r_{ik}(s_i)=r_{jk}(s_j)
\quad \text{on all actual shared contexts }k.
\]

If a triple-overlap cocycle is defined,
test its identity by composing the
specified restriction/isomorphism
maps. A gluing theorem also needs
effective descent for the selected
category and cover. The general
theorems about fibrations or
pseudo-coalgebras do **not** supply
the required instance and proof
for an arbitrary team's evidence
system.

A compatibility failure is a failure
of **the stated descent test**.
Do not call it a nonzero \(\check H^1\)
until a coefficient system, cochain
complex, differential and
non-exact cocycle are given.
FOUR \(\top\) is local evidence
opposition; it can occur with
\(H^1=0\) on a contractible cover.

## Testable special cases

**Positive control:** a two-node
cover with an identity transport
on the same source-scoped cell and
matching Record/evidence identities.
Both local readings agree on the
intersection. Prove this instance's
compatibility without claiming
anything about all team topologies.

**Negative control:** transport two
different source-bound claims into
an allegedly common cell without
a verified correspondence of their
predicate and referent. The
proposed gluing must reject that
matching attempt. Distinct source
claims are **not FOUR contradictory
evidence** until they are actually
opposing values of the same typed
predicate under the same situation.

**Counterexample:** a network can
deliver every message but no
receiver issues an ACK. Message
delivery is therefore not by
definition a border acknowledgment.

**Cohomology assay:** construct an
actual nontrivial cover with
specified restrictions, calculate
\(\ker\delta^1/\operatorname{im}\delta^0\),
then check whether the observed
failure class is nonzero. An
assertion that \(\check H^1\)
has been measured without this
calculation is invalid.
