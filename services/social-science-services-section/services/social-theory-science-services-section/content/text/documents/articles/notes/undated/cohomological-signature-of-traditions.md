# Each governance pattern produces a distinguishable cohomological signature

The math program's [bigraded settling cohomology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/content/bigraded-settling-cohomology/bigraded-settling-cohomology.md) and [aperiodic-regime classification](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-classification.md) supply structural invariants that distinguish [governance patterns](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/teaching/explanations/governance-pattern.md) from one another at the substrate level. This theorem catalogs the signatures: which cohomological invariants each canonical pattern produces, and what operationally distinguishes the patterns through those invariants.

The point is *not* that one pattern is structurally superior. The math is frame-neutral. The point is that pattern-difference is *substrate-algebraically real*: two communities running different patterns operate in measurably different cohomology classes, and the difference is what the structural variation in their lived practice consists of.

## Statement

**Theorem.** *Each canonical governance pattern $\pi$ produces a characteristic shape in the polity's bigraded cohomology, summarized in the table below. Two patterns producing distinct signatures are not [cohomologically equivalent](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/content/cohomological-equivalence/cohomological-equivalence.md), hence operate as substrate-distinct governance.*

| Pattern $\pi$ | Cohomological signature |
|---|---|
| Hohfeldian | First-quartet rules generate $H^0_t$ classes (universally-true duty/claim cells); second-quartet rules generate $H^0_k$ classes (knowledge-axis power-availability). [Deontic-modality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/teaching/explanations/deontic-modality.md)'s $\Box$ and $\Diamond$ are dual generators on the truth axis. Vanishing $H^1$ for well-formed (non-conflicting) rule-sets. |
| Consensus | $H^0_t$ generated by universal $\bigwedge_a$ aggregations; population-cardinality dependence makes the $H^0_t$ class *populace-graded*. Non-trivial $H^1_t$ when populace boundary cells (membership/non-membership) are unsettled — a substrate-algebraic record of the *who counts as participant* question. |
| Rotational | Aperiodic-regime cohomology with period $\pi$ ([regime-of-recurrent-practice](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-recurrent-practice.md)). Specifically: limit-aggregating cycle structure produces $H^1$ classes whose generators correspond to the rotation cycle, classified by the rotation discipline $\delta$. Two rotation patterns with the same $\delta$ but different periods produce distinguishable cohomology classes. |
| Restorative | Non-trivial $H^1$ pair-classes encoding repair-paths: each unrepaired relation-rupture is a 1-cocycle on the relation-graph; the pattern's closure rules systematically attempt to write coboundaries that nullify the cocycle. The polity's *unhealed-harm load* is the dimension of $H^1$ on the restorative-cells sub-sheaf at any given stage. |
| Kinship | Base-change cohomology over the kinship partition, computed via [Beck–Chevalley](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/beck-chevalley-commutation-on-predication-bilattices.md): each kin-group is a fiber, each kin-relation a base-change morphism. The pattern's signature is the BC commutation-square data — what cells transport across kin-relation boundaries and what stays within a fiber. Witness-language cells are predominantly fiber-local; authority cells transport across selected base-changes. |
| Conduct-teachings | Bigraded fiber-cohomology over the teaching set $\Theta$: each teaching is a separate cohomology direction. The pattern's signature is the *teaching-coherence pattern* — which combinations of $H^0_t$ classes (teaching-fulfillments) coexist for a given officer. Standing-loss is an $H^1$ event when teaching-fulfillment cells fall out of consistency. |
| Military-command | Composite-modal-frame cohomology ([military-command-chain-as-modal-composition](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/triage/military-command-chain-as-modal-composition.md)): each rank in the chain contributes a frame, the composite cohomology is the Verdier-composed cohomology of the rank-by-rank frames. The pattern's signature is the chain-depth and the per-rank frame structure. |
| Jurisdictional | Sub-base cohomology with restriction-and-extension morphisms ([jurisdiction-as-base-change](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/jurisdiction-as-base-change.md)): each jurisdiction's cohomology is computed on its sub-base; cross-jurisdiction interaction generates connecting morphisms. The pattern's signature is the jurisdiction-graph structure. |
| Black-radical refusal | $H^1$ classes generated by the *non-applicability cocycles* — cells where the refusing community's rule says "this institution does not bind us" while the surrounding institution's rule says it does. The unresolved $H^1$ is the substrate-algebraic record of structural refusal. |

## Sketch

The verifications proceed pattern by pattern; a representative case shows the structure.

**Consensus-closure signature.** The pattern's characteristic rule $\bigwedge_a \langle a, \texttt{aligns-with}, \phi\rangle = \mathbf{t} \Rightarrow \langle \phi, \texttt{settled}\rangle = \mathbf{t}$ is a $\Pi_1$-style universal quantifier over the populace. In the [bigraded settling cohomology](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/content/bigraded-settling-cohomology/bigraded-settling-cohomology.md), such a rule generates an $H^0_t$ class supported on the cells $\langle \phi, \texttt{settled}\rangle$ for each decided $\phi$. The class's generator depends on the populace cardinality (it is the meet of $|\mathrm{Pop}|$ alignment cells), making the cohomology *populace-graded*: changing the populace changes the generator. When populace-boundary cells (who is in $\mathrm{Pop}$) are themselves contested ($\bot$ or $\top$ on `member-of-populace` cells), the meet is taken over an indeterminate index set, producing an $H^1_t$ class whose support is exactly the contested-membership cells. This is the structural payoff: *consensus governance carries the participant-question as cohomology*. A polity where membership is settled has trivial $H^1_t$ on this sub-sheaf; a polity with contested membership has non-trivial $H^1_t$ exactly on the contested cells.

**Rotational-authority signature.** A rotation with period $\pi$ and discipline $\delta$ is a [recurrent practice](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/recurrent-practice.md) on incumbency cells. By [regime-of-recurrent-practice](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-recurrent-practice.md), its regime is determined by the aggregation structure of $\delta$: $\delta$ depending only on prior-incumbency history is finite-history (Scott-continuous); $\delta$ depending on aggregate populace eligibility across all prior cycles is limit-aggregating (transfinite-bounded). The cohomology: aperiodic-regime classification produces an $H^1$ generator per rotation cycle, classified by $\delta$. Two rotation patterns with identical $\delta$ but different periods produce distinguishable classes via the [aperiodic-regime classification theorem](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/references/aperiodic-regime-classification.md)'s firing-ordinal-pattern invariant.

**Restorative-accountability signature.** Each rupture event $\langle V, \texttt{relation-rupture}, R\rangle = \mathbf{t}$ generates a 1-cocycle on the relation-graph: the cocycle is the obstruction-to-restoration. The pattern's repair-needed closure rule writes coboundaries (acknowledgment, mediation, restitution cells) attempting to trivialize the cocycle. A successfully restored relation has a coboundary witness; an unhealed harm leaves a non-trivial $H^1$ class. Counting unhealed harms is computing $\dim H^1$ of the restorative sub-sheaf. The substrate-algebraic content of "harm leaves a mark on the relational fabric until restored" is exactly: the cohomology class persists until a coboundary nullifies it.

**Hohfeldian signature.** Per [deontic-modality](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/teaching/explanations/deontic-modality.md), the four jural kinds are bilattice modal operators. Power as $\Diamond$ generates $H^0_k$ classes (knowledge-axis: "an authorized continuation exists"); duty as $\Box$ generates $H^0_t$ classes (truth-axis: "every authorized continuation satisfies $\phi$"). Well-formed rule-sets — those satisfying the [delegation-as-authority-residuation](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/delegation-as-authority-residuation.md) constraints — produce cohomology with vanishing $H^1$. Conflicting rule-sets (overlapping powers without precedence; contradictory duties) produce non-trivial $H^1$ exactly on the conflict cells.

The other patterns' verifications follow analogously, applying the appropriate PCT classification result to each pattern's characteristic closure-rule shape. $\square$

## What the table reveals

Three structural facts emerge:

1. **Patterns differ in cohomology *dimension*.** Hohfeldian and consensus differ in *which* cohomology direction carries their content (truth-side modal duality vs.\ universal aggregation). Rotational and military-command differ in *which* base-structure carries their content (recurrent practice vs.\ chain-depth modal composition). The dimensional difference is what makes patterns substrate-distinct.

2. **Some patterns share dimensions but differ in *generators*.** Hohfeldian and military-command both live in the deontic-modality cohomology; military-command is the chain-composition specialization. The difference is the cohomology-class generator structure, not the cohomology dimension.

3. **The cohomology localizes the pattern's *characteristic political question*.** Consensus localizes the "who counts as participant" question to membership-cell $H^1_t$. Restorative localizes the "what harms remain" question to relation-graph $H^1$. Black-radical-refusal localizes the "what bindings do we refuse" question to non-applicability-cocycle $H^1$. The pattern's characteristic concern is *cohomologically real*, not metaphorical.

## Relation to existing pieces

This theorem is the structural backbone of the [non-Hohfeldian governance traditions narrative](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/non-hohfeldian-governance-traditions.md): each tradition the narrative engages contributes a pattern whose cohomological signature is now made specific. The narrative's claim that the math program "discriminates traditions by their cohomology" is realized here as a concrete catalog. The [governance-pattern concept](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/teaching/explanations/governance-pattern.md) supplied the language; this theorem supplies the discrimination.

The cohomology computations are sketched here at the level required to demonstrate distinguishability; full computations (with explicit chain complexes, regime ordinals, and generator inventories per pattern) are downstream work. Each pattern-row of the table is, in effect, a research direction: working out the full cohomological structure of, say, the consensus pattern is its own program of study, comparable to how working out the Hohfeldian pattern's cohomology has been a multi-paper undertaking in the existing predicated-governance theorems.
