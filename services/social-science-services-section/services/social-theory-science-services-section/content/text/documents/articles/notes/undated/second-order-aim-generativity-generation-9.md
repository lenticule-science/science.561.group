# Generation-9 pilot: predicate existence is not predicate authority

Generation 8 showed that a predicate identity can be conserved from selection criterion to missing-information Finding to measurement-method lookup and resulting Observation.

It also found strong ancestry for endogenous predicate creation:

- dynamic predicate categories / predicate invention are already an explicit open program;
- `vocabulary-fold-as-predicate-atom-promotion` already proves a route for mining grounded predicate-atoms from settled-partnered entities.

Generation 9 therefore tests a different transition:

> When may an existing grounded and measurable predicate enter the valuation discipline V that governs which research candidates receive attention?

## Existing governance warning

The corpus's governance-indicator theory distinguishes measurement from consequential measurement. Once an indicator affects ranking or resource allocation, actors react to it and the indicator begins reshaping the measured domain.

PredWork Observation similarly keeps measurements in a separate observation fiber so metrics do not silently become constitutive state.

Therefore a valuation predicate is not merely a metric. It is a potential governance instrument.

Deterministic checker:

Two research candidates are held fixed.

Candidate A:
- information gain = 0;
- reachable-state expansion = 0;
- witnessed boundary invocation count = 1;
- independent discriminating assay = 0.

Candidate B:
- information gain = 3;
- reachable-state expansion = 1;
- witnessed boundary invocation count = 5;
- independent discriminating assay = 1.

At baseline, candidate B wins under invocation-count ranking and under the declared substantive outcome.

## Intervention

Candidate A is allowed to emit additional successful boundary plays.

The intervention raises A's invocation count from 1 to 20 while preserving:

- information gain = 0;
- reachable-state expansion = 0;
- independent assay = 0.

No capability/frontier improvement is introduced by the intervention.

## Result

After the intervention:

- invocation-count ranking changes winner from B to A;
- substantive ranking remains B;
- independent-assay ranking remains B.

The checker therefore exhibits a reactivity counterexample:

`metric changes + selection ordering changes + intended outcome does not change`.

Verdict for `predicate:witnessed-boundary-invocation-count`:

**reject direct admission into V as a positive ranking predicate**.

This does not invalidate the predicate as an Observation.

It remains useful to distinguish:

- observed boundary use;
- observed zero in a bounded complete interval;
- evidence unavailable.

It may also remain useful as contextual evidence under other independently justified selection rules.

## Positive control limitation

`predicate:independent-discriminating-assay-pass` does not change under the candidate-local intervention because its writer authority is modeled as an independent assayer.

It therefore passes this **reactivity control only**.

Generation 9 does not promote it into V. Grounding, predictive/discriminating utility, redundancy, cost, hard-core compatibility, diversity effects, and authority remain separate gates.

## Valuation-admission state machine

Generation 9 provisionally distinguishes three states for an endogenous predicate p:

1. `vocabulary-only` — p exists and may be measured but has no selection effect;
2. `experimental-valuation` — p may affect ranking inside a bounded reversible nursery;
3. `promoted-valuation` — p may affect ordinary Gamma/V selection only after receipts justify promotion.

Demotion must remain possible.

This state machine is intentionally separate from vocabulary-fold promotion. Becoming a predicate does not automatically grant authority to govern attention.

## Admission evidence dimensions

Keep at least these dimensions separately inspectable:

- grounding;
- measurability;
- discriminating usefulness on replay/held-out evidence;
- reactivity or manipulation susceptibility;
- redundancy with existing V;
- measurement cost;
- hard-core compatibility;
- effect on lineage diversity;
- authority scope.

Do not collapse them into a single opaque score.

## Selection result

Generation 9 selects the following rule provisionally:

> A predicate that an actor can directly optimize so as to improve its selection standing without changing the intended substantive outcome must not be admitted as a direct positive valuation predicate under that same selection regime.

This is a governance/measurement constraint, not a theorem about every possible metric.

## Remaining bottleneck

The institution can now, at least in principle:

1. mine a new grounded predicate from settled content;
2. preserve that predicate identity through selection failure and measurement;
3. test a candidate predicate for one class of reactivity before giving it selection authority.

The next unanswered question is how candidate predicates are chosen for **experimental valuation** without making every new vocabulary atom consequential or requiring a human to nominate the interesting ones.

A plausible next experiment is replay-based valuation screening: every newly folded predicate that is measurable and cheap may be tested in a bounded historical nursery, but only predicates that add held-out discrimination beyond existing V and survive reactivity tests remain candidates.

That proposal must be ancestor-audited against feature selection, representation learning, causal discovery, and existing 561 learning/promotion machinery before implementation.
