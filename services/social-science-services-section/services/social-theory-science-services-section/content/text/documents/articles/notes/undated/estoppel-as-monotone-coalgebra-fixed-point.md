# Estoppel as least fixed point of a monotone witness-coalgebra

**Estoppel** — the substrate-algebraic content of *"agent $a$ is bound by their prior assertion of $\phi$ and cannot subsequently assert $\neg \phi$ in proceedings against a party that relied on the prior assertion"* — presents as the **least fixed point** of a closure-stable monotone endofunctor $\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}$ on the closure-fixed-point lattice of the polity's [normative system](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/normative-system.md). The endofunctor is built by post-composing a "raw" witness-coalgebra (which adds estoppel-binding cells) with the closure operator, ensuring the codomain remains in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ — a complete lattice (intersection of closed sets is closed). The estoppel cell-system is then $\mathrm{lfp}(\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}) = \bigcap \{X \in \mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N}) : \widehat{F}_{a,\phi}(X) \subseteq X\}$, supplied by **classical Knaster–Tarski** on the complete lattice $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ — not by the unrestricted Tarski–Lawvere conjecture, which is **refuted** in its general form.

By [closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence.md), $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ is a complete lattice (in fact dual to a bounded distributive lattice via the Priestley correspondence) — supplying the structural prerequisite Knaster–Tarski needs. By [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), the closure operator is lax-idempotent, so $\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}$ is a well-defined endomap on $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ (re-closure does not change a closed set at the 1-categorical level). By [frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets.md), the bilattice-valued joint *"$a$ has asserted $\phi$ and $b$ has relied on it"* admits the Frobenius structure on either the truth-meet or the knowledge-meet (one of the two specific meets the cited theorem covers); we work with the **truth-meet** below — the meet that records "both assertion and reliance hold at value $\mathbf{t}$."

The substantive claim is that **estoppel is not a procedural rule but a structural fixed-point construction** — and that the practitioner-recognized doctrines of *"detrimental reliance,"* *"proprietary estoppel,"* *"estoppel by convention,"* and *"estoppel by deed"* correspond to specific choices of monotone witness-coalgebra, each producing a distinct least-fixed-point with operationally distinct binding consequences.

## Setup: prior assertions, reliance, and the witness-coalgebra

Let $\mathcal{N} = C(\mathcal{N}_0)$ be a polity's [normative system](../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/normative-system.md) with closure operator $C$. A **prior assertion** by $a$ of $\phi$ is a witnessed cell $\langle a, \mathrm{asserts}, \phi \rangle$ at value $\mathbf{t}$, recorded in the polity's witness-base.

A **reliance event** by $b$ on $\phi$ is a witnessed cell $\langle b, \mathrm{relies\text{-}on}, \phi \rangle$ at value $\mathbf{t}$, recorded subsequent to $a$'s assertion and presupposing it. By [frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets.md), the joint cell *"$a$-asserts-$\phi$ and $b$-relies-on-$\phi$"* admits a Frobenius-meet structure on the bilattice that records both assertion and reliance simultaneously.

The **raw witness-coalgebra** $F_{a, \phi}^\circ$ is the assignment

$$F_{a, \phi}^\circ(X) = X \cup \big\{ \langle a, \mathrm{estopped}, \neg\phi \mathrm{\;\;against\;\;} b \rangle : (b, \phi) \in \mathrm{Reliance}(X) \big\}$$

— add the estoppel-binding cell against every party that has relied on $\phi$ in the cell-system $X$. Note that $F_{a, \phi}^\circ$ does **not** in general land in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$: the union may have new closure-consequences not yet realized.

The **closure-stabilized witness-coalgebra** is the post-composition with closure:

$$\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}(X) := C\big( F_{a, \phi}^\circ(X) \big),$$

which lands in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ by construction. Monotonicity follows from the monotonicity of $F^\circ$ and of $C$; the codomain is $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$, a complete lattice. This is the endofunctor on which Knaster–Tarski applies.

## Statement

**Theorem (estoppel as least fixed point).** *Let $\mathcal{N}$ be a polity's normative system, $a$ an agent, $\phi$ a proposition with $\langle a, \mathrm{asserts}, \phi \rangle \in \mathcal{N}$, and $\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}$ the closure-stabilized witness-coalgebra above. Then:*

(a) **(Existence and well-definedness via Knaster–Tarski.)** *The closure-fixed-point lattice $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ is a complete lattice (intersection of closed sets is closed; this is the standard fact about closure systems, supplied here by [closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence.md)). $\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}$ is a monotone endomap on this complete lattice. By **classical Knaster–Tarski**, the least fixed point*

$$\mathrm{lfp}(\widehat{F}_{a, \phi}) = \bigcap \big\{X \in \mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N}) : \widehat{F}_{a,\phi}(X) \subseteq X \big\}$$

*exists in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$. The **estoppel cell-system** $\mathrm{Est}(a, \phi) := \mathrm{lfp}(\widehat{F}_{a, \phi})$ is the substrate-algebraic content of *"the polity recognizes $a$ as estopped from asserting $\neg\phi$ against every party that has relied on $\phi$, plus all closure-consequences."* (Note: the unrestricted [tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites.md) conjecture is **refuted** in general; the present theorem deliberately uses only the classical Knaster–Tarski path on the complete lattice $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$, which does not require the refuted machinery.)*

(b) **(Closure-stability.)** *By [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), $\mathrm{Est}(a, \phi)$ is closure-stable: $C(\mathrm{Est}(a, \phi)) = \mathrm{Est}(a, \phi)$. Operationally: the estoppel binding is consistent with all closure-rule consequences and cannot be defeated by re-deriving from the closed system.*

(c) **(Reliance-witness sufficiency via the truth-meet Frobenius.)** *Per the Frobenius structure of [frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets.md) — specifically the **truth-meet** Frobenius the cited theorem establishes — the existence of an estoppel-binding cell against $b$ requires the joint truth-meet of *"$a$-asserts-$\phi$"* and *"$b$-relies-on-$\phi$,"* not their independent truth values. The truth-meet records "both cells hold at value $\mathbf{t}$"; its Frobenius structure ensures the meet-and-extend operations compose coherently when generating the estoppel cell from the joint witness pair. A reliance witness without assertion (or assertion without reliance) has truth-meet at $\mathbf{f}$ or $\bot$ on the relevant cell, and the witness-coalgebra produces no estoppel cell from such inputs.*

(d) **(Doctrine-as-coalgebra-choice.)** *Different estoppel doctrines correspond to different choices of monotone witness-coalgebra:*
- ***Detrimental reliance estoppel*** *uses $F_{a, \phi}^{\mathrm{detr}}$, which adds the estoppel cell only when the reliance is detrimental — i.e., when there is also a witnessed cell $\langle b, \mathrm{harm}, \mathrm{from\text{-}reliance} \rangle$.*
- ***Proprietary estoppel*** *uses $F_{a, \phi}^{\mathrm{prop}}$, which adds the estoppel cell only when $\phi$ concerns a proprietary right transferred or promised.*
- ***Estoppel by convention*** *uses $F_{a, \phi}^{\mathrm{conv}}$, where the assertion need not be explicit; a shared convention recorded as joint-conduct cells suffices.*
- ***Estoppel by deed*** *uses $F_{a, \phi}^{\mathrm{deed}}$, restricted to assertions in formal deed instruments.*

*Each $F$-variant produces a distinct least-fixed-point estoppel cell-system, and the polity's choice of doctrine is the choice of which $F$-variant the closure system enacts.*

(e) **(Estoppel-defeat = removal of an antecedent witness.)** *An estoppel-defeat — a successful claim that the estoppel does not bind — corresponds to the removal of one of the antecedent witness cells (assertion, reliance, or detriment), causing the corresponding estoppel-cell to drop out of the least fixed point. Per [norm-revision-as-cocompletion](norm-revision-as-cocompletion.md), this is an order-ideal collapse on the fixed-point lattice; the defeat propagates only to estoppel cells whose generation depended on the removed witness.*

## Sketch

**(a)** $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ is a complete lattice: arbitrary intersections of closed sets are closed (a standard fact about closure systems, also recoverable from the Priestley duality of [closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/pre-bilattice/content/bilattice/content/predication-fiber-algebra/content/closure-operator/references/closure-fixed-points-priestley-correspondence.md)). $\widehat{F}_{a, \phi} = C \circ F^\circ_{a, \phi}$ is monotone (composition of monotone maps) and lands in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$ by construction. Classical Knaster–Tarski applies: every monotone endomap on a complete lattice has a least fixed point, given by the intersection of all post-fixed-points. We deliberately do **not** invoke [tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites.md), whose unrestricted form is refuted; the construction here needs only the elementary complete-lattice fact. $\square$ (Sketch (a))

**(b)** Lax idempotence of the closure 2-monad means $C \circ C \cong C$ at the 1-categorical level. Applied to a fixed-point of $F_{a, \phi}$ that is a closed set (in $\mathrm{Fix}(C_\mathcal{N})$): re-applying $C$ yields the same set. The cited [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md) ensures this re-application is well-defined and stable. Operationally: the estoppel cell-system, having been built by closing under the closure rules, remains closed under further closure — it cannot be "argued out of existence" by deriving consequences from it. $\square$ (Sketch (b))

**(c)** [frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets.md) establishes the Frobenius structure for the bilattice's knowledge-meet $\sqcap_k$ and truth-meet $\sqcap_t$ specifically. We use the truth-meet here: the joint cell *"$a$-asserts-$\phi$ and $b$-relies-on-$\phi$"* is precisely $\langle a, \mathrm{asserts}, \phi\rangle \sqcap_t \langle b, \mathrm{relies}, \phi\rangle$, with truth-meet value $\mathbf{t}$ iff both factors are at $\mathbf{t}$. The cited Frobenius structure for $\sqcap_t$ ensures meet-and-extend operations compose coherently when the witness-coalgebra extends the joint cell into the estoppel cell. The substantive point: the estoppel-generating step uses one of the two specific meet-Frobenius structures the cited theorem actually proves — not a generic bilattice operation. $\square$ (Sketch (c))

**(d)** Each doctrine specifies an additional witness-cell type (detriment, proprietary right, convention, deed-instrument) that must be present alongside the assertion-reliance pair for the estoppel cell to be generated. Encoding this as a monotone witness-coalgebra is straightforward: add the relevant filter to $F_{a, \phi}$'s union construction. The least-fixed-point of each filtered variant is its own cell-system, distinct from the others. The cited Tarski machinery applies uniformly to each; the substrate-algebraic content is that each doctrine is a different *choice* of monotone endofunctor, not different *conclusions* from the same one. $\square$ (Sketch (d))

**(e)** Removal of an antecedent witness corresponds to a cell-set revision per [norm-revision-as-cocompletion](norm-revision-as-cocompletion.md), specifically the order-ideal collapse case (c) of that theorem. The collapse propagates: estoppel cells whose generation in $F_{a, \phi}$ required the removed witness are now no longer post-fixed-points of $F_{a, \phi}$ on the revised cell-set, and they drop out of the new least-fixed-point. Estoppel cells whose generation did not require the removed witness remain. The substrate-algebraic content: estoppel-defeat is *targeted* — it removes only the estoppel cells that traced back to the defeated witness. $\square$ (Sketch (e))

## Operational consequences

### Estoppel is constructive, not declarative

The (a)-(b) reading clarifies that estoppel is a *constructive* legal phenomenon: the polity *constructs* the estoppel cell-system from the witness-coalgebra, rather than *declaring* it as an axiom. Substrates that treat estoppel as a procedural rule (a court declaring "estoppel applies here") are mis-describing the operation; the court's role is to *recognize* the least-fixed-point that the witness-coalgebra has already constructed, not to create the binding by fiat. The substrate-algebraic content: estoppel rulings are findings about the structure of the closure-fixed-point lattice, not adjudicative impositions.

### Doctrine choice is coalgebra choice

The (d) reading reframes the practitioner-recognized doctrinal differences in estoppel — *"common-law jurisdictions tend toward detrimental reliance; equity jurisdictions toward proprietary; deed-system jurisdictions toward formal deed instruments; convention-recognizing jurisdictions toward shared-conduct conventions"* — as differences in monotone-witness-coalgebra choice. Each doctrine *is* a coalgebra; the polity's doctrinal commitment is its commitment to that coalgebra. Comparative-law analysis of estoppel can then proceed at the coalgebra-level: which doctrines are subsumed by others (when one $F$-variant has its post-fixed-points contained in another's), which doctrines produce equivalent fixed points despite formally different coalgebras, etc.

### Estoppel-defeat is targeted, not categorical

The (e) reading clarifies that estoppel-defeat — *"a successful claim that estoppel does not apply"* — operates by removing an *antecedent witness* (typically: contesting whether a reliance was reasonable, whether the assertion was clear, whether the detriment was caused by the assertion). The defeat does not "remove the estoppel doctrine"; it removes the operational ground for *this particular* estoppel cell. The substrate-algebraic content: defeat is a witness-revision operation that propagates only to the closure-consequences of the removed witness, leaving other estoppel bindings intact.

### Frobenius-meet structure explains the joint-witness requirement

The (c) reading clarifies why estoppel doctrine universally requires *joint* assertion-and-reliance witnesses, not assertion alone. The Frobenius-meet structure of the bilattice means the joint cell carries operational support both component cells contribute; without one component, the joint cell does not exist as a witnessed cell, and the witness-coalgebra has nothing to operate on. The substrate-algebraic content: the joint-witness requirement is not a procedural quirk but a structural consequence of the Frobenius-meet construction's role in generating the binding cell.

## Status

`sketch` because:

- The Tarski–Lawvere fixed-point machinery applied via [tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/tarski-lawvere-fixed-point-grothendieck-sites.md) gives least-fixed-point existence for monotone endofunctors on the closure-fixed-point lattice; the precise endofunctor $F_{a, \phi}$ as constructed is monotone but the per-doctrine variants of (d) require their additional filtering to be itself monotone — a property that can fail if the additional condition is not closure-stable. The boundary cases (e.g., a "detriment" condition whose evaluation depends on contested reliance-causation) are not handled in this sketch.
- The Frobenius-meet reading of (c) is consistent with [frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/frobenius-for-knowledge-and-truth-meets.md), but the precise interpretation of the Frobenius pair $(\nabla, \Delta)$ on the assertion-reliance product is sketched rather than worked out.
- The doctrine taxonomy of (d) is illustrative, not exhaustive; jurisdictions have additional doctrinal variants (estoppel by record, equitable estoppel, promissory estoppel) that admit their own coalgebra constructions but are not enumerated.
- The (e) connection to [norm-revision-as-cocompletion](norm-revision-as-cocompletion.md) is correct in direction but treats the witness-revision as a clean order-ideal collapse; in real systems, defeat-arguments have their own closure-consequence structure that may complicate the propagation.

The directional content — that estoppel is a least-fixed-point of a monotone witness-coalgebra, with doctrines as coalgebra-variants, defeat as antecedent-witness removal, and Frobenius-meet structure underwriting the joint-witness requirement — is the substantive proposal of this sketch.

## Open downstream

- *Estoppel against the Crown / sovereign immunity.* Many polities have doctrines preventing certain agents (typically the state) from being estopped. Substrate-algebraically: the witness-coalgebra is restricted to a sub-domain of agents excluding the protected class. Whether this restriction can be presented as a uniform Beck-Chevalley structure on the agent-axis (per [capability-grant-as-base-change-along-grant-inclusion](capability-grant-as-base-change-along-grant-inclusion.md)) deserves treatment.
- *Estoppel chains.* When $b$'s reliance on $a$'s assertion is itself the basis of $c$'s subsequent reliance, and $a$ later attempts to defeat the original assertion, what propagates? The chain-of-reliance structure is a graph whose closure under the witness-coalgebra is iterated; the precise transfinite-iteration analysis is open.
- *Estoppel and time.* Some doctrines impose temporal limits (laches, statute of limitations) on estoppel claims. Encoding these as temporal-stratum filters on the witness-coalgebra produces a temporal-Beck-Chevalley analysis (per [temporal-beck-chevalley](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/temporal-2-category/references/temporal-beck-chevalley.md)) that this sketch does not pursue.
