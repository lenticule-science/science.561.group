# Generation-11 result: the valuation nursery lacks a live endogenous candidate source

Generation 10 produced a deterministic zero-authority valuation nursery and showed that candidate predicates can be retained or rejected for independent reasons including reactivity, redundancy, held-out discrimination, and measurement cost.

But its folded predicates were fixture-authored.

Generation 11 therefore asked whether the living 561 runtime can enumerate endogenous vocabulary-fold candidates from settled content.

## Ancestor theory

The current theory already gives the criterion:

- four-position substrate classification: F5.3 = settled and partnered;
- vocabulary-fold theorem: settled-partnered entities may be promoted into grounded predicate-atoms.

That is not the missing theory.

## Living runtime audit

The current @emsenn/relating repository was searched for:

- settled-partnered;
- four-position classification;
- partner completeness;
- predicate-atom promotion;
- vocabulary-fold actions.

No current implementation was found.

The live MetroPlat operation catalog was also inspected. It exposes current relating operations including `predicate`, `settle`, `induce`, `induce-membrane`, and `relate-relations`, but no four-position classifier, fold-candidate census, or predicate-fold action.

Therefore Generation 10 cannot yet truthfully say that its valuation candidates were nominated by the living endogenous vocabulary machinery.

## Stronger obstruction

Current relating does have true typed measurements over SettlementFiltration, including regime/depth/cohomology/classification reads.

But the inspected runtime does not expose a direct typed **partner-completeness** read.

That matters because closure-quiescence alone is not F5.3.

A settled-but-unpartnered entity must not be silently admitted as a fold candidate.

## Durable offspring

Opened `emsenn/relating#5`:

`Research read-only vocabulary-fold candidate census`.

The issue is deliberately read-only. It may return either:

1. deterministic fold candidates with closure-quiescence + partner-completeness evidence; or
2. explicit obstructions naming the missing runtime evidence.

It may not mutate the predicate category or grant valuation authority.

## Selection result

Generation 11 selects a negative result:

> the candidate-source leg of the autonomous valuation pipeline is not currently realized on the living runtime surface.

This prevents upgrading the Generation-10 nursery from controlled protocol rehearsal to autonomous cultural evolution.

## Current end-to-end chain

The estate now has, at different maturity levels:

1. endogenous predicate creation in theory;
2. predicate-conserved measurement lookup in a deterministic fixture;
3. valuation admission and rejection in deterministic nursery fixtures;
4. no living readout yet that supplies real endogenous predicate candidates to that nursery.

The next implementation dependency is therefore not another meta-learning layer. It is the missing read-only bridge from current settled substrate state to fold candidates.

## Success condition for the next generation

A future generation may claim autonomous candidate nomination only after `relating#5` or an equivalent living operation returns at least one candidate from actual current substrate evidence without a human naming the predicate candidate first.
