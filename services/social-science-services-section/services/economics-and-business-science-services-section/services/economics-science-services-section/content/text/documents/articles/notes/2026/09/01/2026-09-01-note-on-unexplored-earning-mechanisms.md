# Unexplored earning mechanisms

Founder observation: blockchain is under-explored relative to how much
infrastructure the house has pointed at it (MARSH, EVM wallets, x402 live
on mainnet, the forecasting/belief stack). Honest scale framing throughout:
these earn trickles of pennies to lumps of low single-digit dollars, not
fortunes — recorded so the someday survives, not because any is proven.

Note (founder correction 2026-09-01): "provenance as a product" is NOT
novel here — it is already dense in the corpus as WIT (the token of real
physics, simulable). Do not re-pitch WIT as a new idea; deepen it from
what exists.

## Blockchain-native (the under-explored lane)

- **Funding-rate arbitrage (perps, the HONEST version).** Not directional
  perp gambling — that is where small accounts die (leverage + funding +
  liquidation eat you even when you are right about price). Delta-neutral:
  long spot, short the perpetual, collect the funding spread. Mechanical,
  direction-free, exactly what sustained agentic infra is good at.
  Entanglement: the alpaca/forecasting engineering, but on a DEX/CEX perp
  venue. Gate: capital + exchange accounts + real smart-contract/venue
  risk; size the funding spread against fees before believing it.
- **Airdrop farming + incentivized testnets.** Agents systematically
  qualifying for new-protocol token drops and testnet rewards. Capital-
  light, agent-automatable. Honest: often zero, occasionally a real lump;
  enemy is sybil detection (a red-queen race). The literal "agents
  crawling blockchain for drops" idea.
- **RetroPGF / public-goods funding (the best-fit blockchain lane).**
  Optimism RetroPGF, Gitcoin — build open infrastructure (the language
  algebra, the receipts primitives) and get retroactively PAID for it.
  Mission-aligned, real money, no gambling, and it stacks with the NLnet
  lane (same artifacts, different funder).
- **Formal-verification bug bounties (the play only we can make).** Audit
  contests (Code4rena, Cantina, Immunefi) are hardened against slop and
  duplicate-farming — but the house has a LEAN proof stack. Everyone else
  eyeballs contracts; we could PROVE properties. Differentiated in a space
  where differentiation is scarce. Gate: the formal-methods-to-Solidity
  bridge is real work; scope a single contest first.
- **Prediction markets via the belief stack.** Polymarket-class event
  markets priced by our forecasting/bilattice/calibrated-belief stack —
  belief-as-input applied to real-world events koios/forecasting already
  study, arguably a more honest edge than perps (you price events, not
  leverage). Gate: US-person regulatory grey (CFTC); flag before touching.
- **DeFi small-capital yield: researched and REJECTED** (2026-08-28, net-
  negative at ~$40 spendable). Recorded so it is not rediscovered.

## Genuinely off our current map (weird relative to corpus)

- **Synthetic-data generation for training.** We have generation infra;
  sell curated synthetic datasets (with WIT provenance as the
  differentiator, since that is already ours).
- **Grant-discovery-and-drafting as a service.** We are drafting NLnet;
  the capability to find + draft grants for OTHERS (small open-source
  projects, indigenous orgs) is a service that also builds the founder's
  standing. Aligns with image, unlike gig labor.
- **"Boring data" feeds** (from the 2026-08-27 survey, never built):
  permit filings, court calendars, regulatory changes as x402-priced APIs
  — the base-chain-data-gateway pattern pointed at civic/legal data.
- **MCP tool catalog.** As agents proliferate, a catalog of small useful
  agent-payable tools (the base-chain-data-gateway generalized) is our
  x402 lane widened — sell picks-and-shovels to the agent economy.
- **GitHub Sponsors on the published packages.** The dozens of registry
  packages, once public, carry sponsor buttons — passive, mission-aligned,
  trickle.

## Standing frame

Earn-only economy (no payouts beyond verifiably earned revenue); nothing
thin sold; capital-at-risk lanes (funding-arb, perps) are paper/simulated
until a compliance + capital read, never "can't be that hard" on real
money; the mission-aligned lanes (RetroPGF, grant-drafting, formal-
verification) build the founder's nation-leader image and rank above the
pseudonymous grind lanes. Validate demand before seating any of these
(foundry law).
