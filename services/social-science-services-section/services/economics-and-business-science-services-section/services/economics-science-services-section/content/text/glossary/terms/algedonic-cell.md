An **algedonic cell** is the pool's realization of Beer's algedonic signal, the channel by which the periphery of a viable system tells the top that something hurts or works without passing through the intermediate levels. Any member may tell a pain or pleasure cell about any question or cell, bonding a charter fee. The cell jumps to the top of the [attention worklist](./attention-worklist.md) and is visible on the public face, the pool's policy surface, immediately. The bond returns if a daimon, the human office that settles contested cells, finds the signal warranted; otherwise it goes to the reserve. A member's open algedonic cells are capped by their matched pairs, so abuse is bounded by its cost and by the member's own record. In the specification's viable system map the algedonic channel is realized by a single contract and is listed apart from the five systems.
