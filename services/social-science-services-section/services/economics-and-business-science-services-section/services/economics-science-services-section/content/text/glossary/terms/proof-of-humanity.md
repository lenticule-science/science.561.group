**Proof of Humanity** is a Sybil-resistance registry on Ethereum: a person submits evidence and a deposit, must gain a required number of vouches from registered members, and is registered unless challenged within a challenge period; a lost challenge for duplicate or non-existence can penalize the vouchers. The mainnet v1 registry's parameters (0.1 ETH base deposit, two-year registration, 3.5-day challenge period, one vouch) and governor powers are in [Bonding Curves and the Token Engineering Commons](../../../../../finance-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/08/2026-10-08-bonding-curves-and-the-tec.md); [conviction voting](./conviction-voting.md) deployments do not require it.
