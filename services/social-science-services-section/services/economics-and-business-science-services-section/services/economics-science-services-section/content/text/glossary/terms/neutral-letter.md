A neutral letter asks a property owner whether they would consider selling, and how to reach them, without stating a price.
It draws more replies than a [blind offer](./blind-offer.md), 2% to 15% depending on how the owners were chosen (search synthesis: Pebble REI), because answering commits the owner to nothing; more of those owners expect a price near market value, so more conversations end without an agreement.

A business that calls owners uses the neutral letter's words for its voicemail and for owners it cannot reach by phone, so the mailed and spoken approach say the same thing.
See the [note on the Minnesota property-resale market](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-the-minnesota-property-resale-market.md).
