**Pay the meet** is the reading rule for money under disagreement. Two orders govern every reading in the pool: the knowledge order merges witnesses and sites, and the truth order decides what is paid. When two witnesses (two sites, two ledgers) report a quantity differently, the pool must pay the minimum, the meet in the truth order, and must hold the difference as a contradiction cell with mass |a − b| until it is settled. The rule makes overpayment on an unreconciled figure impossible and makes the unpaid remainder a visible, counted object rather than a silent shortfall; it is the cross-site form of the two-witness reconciliation reading and applies to the merge of site cycle totals. The held remainder is never liquidated and never reverts; it is on the [attention worklist](./attention-worklist.md) with a benefit equal to its frozen mass.
