## Why this is central

Odum supplies the house's citation for [emergy](../../../glossary/terms/emergy.md): a common-unit accounting method (solar emjoules, via a transformity factor) for comparing energy sources and even non-energy inputs (labor, ecosystem services) of very different quality on one basis, which the house reads as a candidate instrument for the cross-domain resource accounting (books, chain, traffic, compute treated as one thing) the economics-business-finance nesting note names as a goal.

## What it does not settle

Emergy's transformity values — the sej-per-joule conversion factors that make the whole method work — are themselves modeled and estimated rather than directly measured, and different researchers using different modeling assumptions have derived different transformity values for the same energy form, which is a standing methodological criticism of the emergy program from within ecological economics. Odum's book does not resolve this indeterminacy; it documents the accounting method and a wide range of applications built on transformities the reader must accept as given inputs.
