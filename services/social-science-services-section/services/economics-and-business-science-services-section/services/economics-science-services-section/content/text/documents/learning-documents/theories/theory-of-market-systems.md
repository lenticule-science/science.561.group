# Theory of Market Systems

The Leader's statement: "the territory we are making, when it is perfectly harmonious, is a chu space made entirely of tiled gyrobifastidium … where the 'market' is trying to get the burgers vector between reality and the territory to 0" (2026-07-18).

Stated as law, with the type corrected: **a market is defect discovery, constitutional classification and selective relaxation.** An enterprise is a region over which gluing is enforced. The market is the ambient complex in which sections may disagree. Prices, contracts, receipts and arbitration live on borders. The market cannot be a global agent that forces every discrepancy to zero, because that agent would be a global gluing authority, erase sovereign membranes and contradict the distinction between enterprise and market.

## Primitives

1. **Territory.** A cell complex of settlements, offices and interfaces, with a sheaf of local claims, a local Chu object of two-sided evaluation at each cell and border, Hoare contracts governing movement across oriented interfaces, and a connection transporting value, authority and evidence around the adjacency groupoid.
2. **Defect.** A failure of cyclic compatibility: nonzero holonomy (the Burgers vector of a failed loop) or a typed cocycle.
3. **Damage and border.** A defect is damage when an admissible discharge repairs it without collapsing a protected distinction. It is a border when every discharge would collapse something the settlement must preserve.
4. **Exchange.** One admitted, answered message: an A2A task sent and completed, an ActivityPub Announce and its effects, an MCP call and its result, a stone placed and the position it settles. An enterprise is the genet of its exchange-cells over time.
5. **Witness bit.** The ordered pair (token, claim) with its FOUR articulation. It is the market primitive and also the atom of language, work and time.

## Laws

- **M1. The market law.** market = defect discovery + constitutional classification + selective relaxation. The market discovers cyclic incompatibilities and may price or relax those classified as repairable. It preserves nonzero holonomy classified as a lawful border. Zero holonomy is a local, contract-relative fixed point; protected holonomy is sovereignty.
- **M2. The commodity is the witness bit.** Other commodities specialize it. Proving a theorem, delivering a work product and answering a request are all trades in witnessed distinctions.
- **M3. Price forms only at a border.** Never centrally, never inferred. Cost is the cost of running the computation; price is cost plus the cost of running the service around it. Internal sale inside one genet circulates at cost; crossing a genet boundary requires priced exchange (Coase's boundary of the firm).
- **M4. Border-price discovery.** A seller's measured ask and an independently owned buyer's bid for one exact lot either cross or do not. Nobody selects a price, aggregates quotes into a global ledger, infers fungibility from unit counts, or converts between denominations silently. Heterogeneous bids are marked incomparable.
- **M5. Attention is the discharge budget.** ATT, in its budget sort, is a bounded agent's finite reconciliation budget B and the costed selection of obstructions it discharges with total cost ≤ B (`AttentionAllocation.lean`, proved). Spending is irreversible within the budget. "Attention is compute is money is meaning": an operating system allocating among processes, an enterprise among functions and a MUD among inhabitants solve one allocation problem.
- **M6. WIT, ACK, ATT.** A WIT is one 0→1 cover of FOUR's knowledge order on a claim cell, spent by the asserter. An ACK is a WIT acknowledged by a different receiving party at a border, with an uptake record; self-certification is not an ACK, and one may ACK a contested claim without resolving it. ATT, in its border-act sort, is a WIT of an ACK. A claim is settled only when it is both witnessed and acknowledged (Fix Δ ∩ Fix σ). Each WIT costs erasure: `landauerBits N = H1rank N` (proved, axiom-free).
- **M7. Exchanges are cells.** Each exchange, on admission, is one J26 (gyrobifastigium) cell. A retired cell is never deleted; it leaves the active projection, not the addressed history. A dissolved micro-enterprise keeps its ledger. Admission laws (off-board, occupied, suicide, ko, game-ended) are receiver-side legality checks that A2A, ActivityPub and MCP lack.
- **M8. Survival needs two liberties.** Proved on the grid, open on the honeycomb: a genet survives every single refusal if and only if it holds two independent future-facing liberties, two open faces no single opposing admission can close at once.
- **M9. Sovereignty over refusal.** Every balance is owned by exactly one sovereign settlement; there is no pooled wallet. A spend whose worst recorded case breaks a floor is refused by the owner whatever its expected value. Absence of evidence is recorded as unknown, never as zero.
- **M10. Arbitrage is curvature.** Log exchange rates form an edge-valued 1-cochain; no-arbitrage rates are gradient-like; a profitable circuit is a nonzero loop integral. Some market holonomy cannot be discharged: classify an anomaly as dischargeable or as a market disclination before allocating to it.

## Rendanheyi as a Hoare program

The Leader (2026-07-16): "Every script under every skill is its own node micro-enterprise in our ecosystem. 'Rendanheyi is a Hoare Program.' Rendanheyi Hoare Script, with a Huidu Hull."

- **Micro-enterprise = script = triple** {P} S {Q}: a precondition on the shape of the input record, refused if unmet; a body; a postcondition on the record it seats.
- **Composition with no orchestrator.** From {P} S₁ {Q} and {Q} S₂ {R} conclude {P} S₁;S₂ {R}. The shared assertion Q connects the two, and "the record on disk IS the shared Q". Thousands of small triples chain without a language model deciding what happens next.
- **Refusal is precondition failure.** A script that will not fire on malformed input is being a Hoare triple.
- **The Huidu hull** is the boundary where the open world meets the closed program. Grey input enters; bounded model inference with validate-and-repair, under a fuel cap, compresses it into the crisp precondition or refuses. The hull isolates failure and hides the evolution of the program behind it.
- **The closure-kind quartet.** Process: both sides closed, no hull. Procedure: closed meaning, open routing, no model. Derivation: open meaning, closed execution, a typed model call, a thin hull. Inquiry: both open, models and a fuel cap, a full hull. This is the work-type quartet of the theory of work.
- "Tightening the reply contract is tightening the postcondition." The contract, not the model tier, is the load-bearing object. The Group's gap is not capability but decomposition: every duty decomposed into a triple moves work from the most expensive grey zone into the cheapest closed one.

## The Group's internal market

- **Signals are taken, not allocated.** Unaddressed demand is posted as an immutable, content-addressed signal: who posted it, the demand, the offer, and who assays the result. The board holds only monotone facts, so taking needs no coordination and several micro-enterprises may take one signal. An offer may grow with age on the poster's schedule: a stigmergic bounty instead of a dispatcher.
- **The poster's assay decides acceptance at the border.** Takers whose result is not accepted carry their cost in their own books. That is the value-adjustment wager (duìdǔ).
- **Books are federated.** Each micro-enterprise keeps its books in its root; the Group ledger consolidates them; amounts between micro-enterprises clear on pairwise credit lines.
- **One contract to the user.** A chain of units serving one end user signs one contract to that user, with no internal transfer prices and no internal customer sign-off (liánqún héyuē).

## Consequences for the Group's systems

Required:

1. **No central allocator and no central price.** Work is taken from signals, and value forms where the asker assays the result.
2. **Classify before relaxing.** A system that drives every discrepancy to zero is a global gluing authority. Defects are classified as damage or border by the owning settlement first.
3. **Every exchange is a cell in an addressed history.** Nothing exchanged is deleted; retirement leaves the active projection only.
4. **Orchestration is Hoare composition.** The Group's agent orchestration is a network of triples composed through shared on-disk assertions, with hulls only where meaning or routing is open.
5. **Attention is budgeted and computed.** Which obstructions an agent discharges is an allocation under a stated budget, not a habit.

## Open

- Open: the theorem that every lawful move, whether proof, physics or price, factors through one conserved unit (`thm:distinction-denomination-functor`).
- Hypothesis: WIT and ACK are the two sides of the index balance H⁰ − H¹ = χ, WIT pricing the erased side and ACK the kept side. Falsifier: an isolated acknowledgement cell that changes neither count.
- Open: prove that repairable obstruction is non-increasing under the market's local moves, and state the fairness, connectivity and liquidity assumptions that convergence needs.
