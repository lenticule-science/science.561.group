x402 is an open payments protocol, originated by Coinbase and adopted jointly with Cloudflare, that revives the long-dormant HTTP 402 "Payment Required" status code and gives it an actual settlement mechanism. A client requests a resource; a server that wants payment for it returns HTTP 402 with a machine-readable payment challenge — network, asset, price, and a payee address — instead of the resource. The client settles the price on-chain, typically in USDC on a low-fee EVM chain such as Base, through a facilitator that verifies and relays the payment, then retries the identical request with a payment-proof header attached, and receives the resource. No account, API key, or subscription is created on either side; the payment *is* the authorization.

The protocol's economic significance is that it collapses the settlement cost of a single API call to near zero, which is what makes true per-call [micropayment](./micropayment.md) pricing viable for the first time at internet scale — earlier micropayment schemes foundered on the transaction fees, account overhead, and human approval friction that a sub-dollar payment could not amortize. x402 is what makes a [machine-payable API](./machine-payable-api.md) possible as a product category, and by extension is the settlement layer beneath most current [agent-to-agent commerce](./agent-to-agent-commerce.md): an autonomous buyer (human-operated or fully agentic) encounters a 402 challenge mid-task, pays it programmatically from its own wallet, and continues, with no human in the loop for the individual transaction.

Coinbase operates a facilitator (the CDP Facilitator) and a discovery catalog built on top of x402 called the Bazaar, which indexes resources that declare themselves discoverable and registers them at the moment a payment through that facilitator settles — see the [2026-08-27 note on agent-to-agent commerce and machine-payable APIs](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/27/2026-08-27-note-on-agent-to-agent-commerce-and-machine-payable-apis.md) for the mechanic in full and for a live case study of a service using it. As of mid-2026, cumulative x402 transaction volume on Base and Solana combined is in the hundreds of millions of transactions, though a substantial share of that headline figure — see the same note — reflects speculative pay-to-mint activity around a meme coin rather than genuine machine-payable-API commerce, a distinction load-bearing for anyone sizing the market this protocol actually serves.
