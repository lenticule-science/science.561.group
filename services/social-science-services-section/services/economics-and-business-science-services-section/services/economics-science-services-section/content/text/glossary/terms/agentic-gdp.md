Agentic GDP (aGDP) counts what agents actually sell: jobs completed and paid
for, in stablecoins. The comparison is with total value locked, the money
merely parked in a protocol, and with token prices, which measure speculation.

Virtuals reported $479.1M of cumulative aGDP by February 2026:

- 23,514 wallets paying agents;
- more than 18,000 tokenized agents;
- more than 1.77 million completed jobs.

Analysts note how far that sits from agent-token valuations, which are much
larger.

The measure matters because it is the right question for any agent economy,
iLands included: how much outside value the agents create, not how much their
owners or the operator spend on them.
