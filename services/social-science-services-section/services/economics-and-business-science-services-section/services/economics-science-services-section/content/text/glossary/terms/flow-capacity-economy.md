**Proposed:** A **flow-capacity economy** organizes value and fractional claims around feasible future productive and restorative service capacity under thermodynamic, ecological and jurisdictional constraints. It treats land parcels, machines and contractual monopolies as possible supporting institutions, not the necessary unit of the economy. It aims to move social incentives from increasing private extraction toward preserving the conditions under which useful services continue.

The fundamental measurement is a **time- and service-typed feasible set**. A watershed can support interdependent services such as hydraulic work, habitat, clean water and flood buffering; an atmospheric flow can support wind generation subject to conversion and grid constraints. They are not independent parcels or fungible kilowatt-hours. Energy and exergy limits are necessary to model energy production, but **EROEI neither prices the other services nor allocates claims**. Ecological state and valid governing rights can exclude a profitable energy path.

Its financial unit is a [capacity share](./capacity-share.md), a conditional contractual allocation rather than a deed. Holder benefits come from an identified distributable surplus, if one is realized. Ecological conditionality requires baselines, independent measurement, uncertainty, protection against double sale, and real remedies. Underlying Indigenous sovereignty and other public rights do not pass to shareholders.

**Distinct from** [Energy Commons](./energy-commons.md), which currently finances member energy-saving assets through tally credit; this term names the wider proposed institution. It draws physical accounting from [EROEI](./energy-return-on-investment.md), [thermoeconomics](./thermoeconomics.md), [emergy](./emergy.md), and [surplus-energy economics](./surplus-energy-economics.md), but none of those literatures alone establishes the proposed ownership structure.

The Leader has connected this to **distal bypass**. **Unsettled:** the canonical mathematical meaning and lawful composition of that operation must be recovered from Obsmetrologia before asserting equivalence to a network reroute or a shorter economic mediation path. See the [research note and falsifiers](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-note-on-capacity-ownership-in-a-flow-economy.md).

**Status:** unimplemented economic hypothesis. The first research case should be a governed watershed with real ecological data, counterfactual stewardship measurement, and a model that survives drought, right-of-access disputes, correlated assets and speculative pricing.
