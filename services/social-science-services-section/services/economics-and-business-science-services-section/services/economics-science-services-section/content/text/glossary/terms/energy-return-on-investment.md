**Energy return on energy invested (EROEI; also EROI)** compares the usable energy delivered by a defined energy-production process with the energy directly and indirectly required to obtain and deliver it. Its interpretation requires an energy carrier, quality-conversion convention, production stage, system boundary, and time horizon. If delivered output is \(G>0\) and comparable invested energy is \(I>0\), then \(\mathrm{EROEI}=G/I\) and \(N=G-I\). An EROEI of 10 means a ratio of ten units delivered per unit invested *within that boundary*. When \(I/G=1/\mathrm{EROEI}\), the retained net fraction is \(1-1/\mathrm{EROEI}\). Do not aggregate thermal joules, delivered electricity, firm grid capacity, and ecological services without a declared conversion or service model. [@hallbaloghmurphy2009]

EROEI is not an intrinsic property of a resource independent of measurement. Extending the boundary to include upstream equipment, storage, distribution, decommissioning, restoration, or a multiproduct allocation may change the result. Low or high EROEI does not by itself establish profitability, justice, physical scarcity, a civilizational threshold, or what share any person may own. Compare the scope problems in [global EROI analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0921800916308138) and [extended multiproduct EROI](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544219323953).

Charles Hall's research, developed further by [Nate Hagens](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/nate-hagens.md), identifies a **net energy cliff**: because the energy needed to sustain a complex industrial society is not linear in the EROEI ratio, a decline from a high ratio toward a low one removes a disproportionate share of the surplus available to society. Hagens's "energy blindness" critique is that mainstream economics treats energy as one substitutable input among many rather than as this surplus-generating master resource, which leads it to systematically underprice the consequences of a falling ratio.

EROEI is distinguished from energy storage return on investment, which measures the lifetime energy cycled through a storage technology against the energy required to build it; a storage device does not itself generate the energy it later releases. EROEI enables the energy-cliff calculation: when a fraction 1/EROEI of gross energy is reinvested in energy supply, the remaining net fraction is 1 - 1/EROEI.

## Capacity economy relation (proposed 2026-10-17)

The [flow-capacity economy](./flow-capacity-economy.md) uses EROEI as one physical restriction on a **typed, feasible service-capacity envelope**. A [capacity share](./capacity-share.md) is instead a fractional right to an identified service tranche, subject to ecological constraints, valid authority, and a separate realized-surplus settlement. A watershed's ability to support fish, water quality and flood attenuation is not reducible to a power generator's EROEI. Nor does an energy surplus issue ownership rights. These quantities must remain separate in theory, contracts, and code. See [the source research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-note-on-capacity-ownership-in-a-flow-economy.md).

The house holds a declining-EROEI position: the [2025-12-25 oil-sanctions personal log](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/logs/personal-logs/2025/12/25/2025-12-25-emsenn-personal-log-about-latest-oil-sanctions.md) names "declining energy return on energy invested, brittle supply chains, ecological limits" as the base physical and thermodynamic pressure that finance manages and defers rather than resolves. The house's own [cognitive EROEI](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/01/2026-08-01-note-on-the-content-addressed-glass-sponge-semantic-compute-complex.md) concern applies the same net-return logic to scarce inference capacity rather than fossil energy. This is a separate modeled use: it must declare an inference-cost unit, a yield unit, a time horizon, an accounting boundary, and a validation path; physical EROEI does not itself supply those values.
