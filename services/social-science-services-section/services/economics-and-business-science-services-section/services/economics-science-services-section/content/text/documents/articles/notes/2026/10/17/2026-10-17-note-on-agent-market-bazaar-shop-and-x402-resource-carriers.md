# From witnessed exchange to market, bazaar, shop and x402 resource

**Question and receiver.** Which actually distinguished economic objects and relations arise when independently acting agents articulate wants and capabilities, constrain offers, locate counterparties, undertake obligations and perform independently receipted exchanges? What laws, not brand associations, would justify four different carriers named market, bazaar, shop and resource, and what can our one real merchant and AOE actually construct?

Owner: Economics Science (market formation, search and matching, measured outcomes). Pure realizing consumer: Red Cup Agent Organization Engine Gaming/Organizing, then its existing SignCell/Chain/Graph/Craft and Networking/x402 providers. Operating consumer: Coesite capability-market-services-section; specific public instance: Causeway market.561.group. No new seller, payment ledger, transport engine, market authority or external-provider registry is created by this paper.

**Scientific status (2026-10-17):** A proposed typed derivation and finite test obligations, grounded in original protocols and source. The existing AOE finite CGS constructions and simple arithmetic are executable source claims, **not Obsmetrologia kernel proofs of this entire derivation**. No live paid transaction, novel theorem, independently working Bazaar host or economic superiority has been received here.

## 2. First generate distinctions, then ask for carriers

Start with a claimant uttering a predicate about a marked subject, keeping the claimant and its conditions. This is not a seller's self-assertion becoming public truth. Distinct utterances:
1. Demand: actor D claims a wanted outcome, with requirements, beneficiary, location/jurisdiction, budget, deadline and accepting receiver.
2. Capability: actor S claims it can supply an operation or outcome with specified constraints; this is not proof of deliverability.
3. Offer: attributable S publishes contingent terms binding a capability to recipients, price/consideration, input/output, validity, legal rights and obligations; a listing is not acceptance.
4. Discovery: index B reports encountering an independently sourced Offer; this report neither transfers S's authority nor changes the Offer.
5. Quote: a particular route responds with current payment requirements; an unsigned HTTP 402 is data, not independent authority to pay S.
6. Accept/authorize: an entitled buyer and seller exercise the actual contract and payment powers; identity, scope, nonce and authorization must be preserved.
7. Execute/deliver: the operation actually occurs and returns a bound result, or fails with separate recovery.
8. Receive/settle: independently attributable delivery acceptance and money movement are separately evidenced and reconciled with measured costs.

Two different recorded transitions may refer to the same contract without being the same fact. FOUR can retain incompatible testimony; contested evidence must not be collapsed into positive admission, nor does a missing negative witness permit an effect.

**Counterexamples:** a search hit is not an offer; an offer is not a payable resource; an x402 HTTP 402 is not a signed purchase; a submitted signature is not independently settled; an on-chain EIP-3009 AuthorizationUsed is not necessarily x402; a completed transfer is not accepted work; a count of listings is not demand. The historical August 2026 Bazaar coverage estimate failed precisely because its numerators/denominators were not type-aligned.

## 3. Four candidate carriers and their composition boundaries

### Resource R: one invocable address, not a market

For HTTP, a resource reference includes the seller-controlled origin, method, route template and documented input/output schema; an individual invocation also has exact parameters and potentially authorization and time. For MCP, the official Bazaar input discriminant is an MCP tool name plus its input schema at a server endpoint. A resource reference describes *how to address an invocation*, not whether it is lawful, paid, working or fulfillable. Distinguish route template from the concrete invocation URL: different path parameters can be one indexed resource. x402 v2 publishes the **resource** in PaymentRequired, and the optional **bazaar** extension publishes discovery input/output metadata; it does not grant new payment authority.

Candidate x402 Resource identity: (seller-authoritative protocol identity, source resource URI or MCP server+tool, transport/method, route template, declaration version). **Do not change an existing NI/SKU, ActivityPub Offer identity, or recoverable transaction key to adopt these descriptors.** Quote identity additionally requires actual network, scheme, asset, amount, payee, validity and buyer-authorized payload. Two quotes with the same human name or price are not the same signed resource contract.

### Shop S: a principal-scoped offer-publishing boundary

A shop is a seller-controlled projection of that principal's authored offers and permitted ways to order or inquire. Its authority is supplied by the seller's actual actor and accounts, not the domain name or the browsing interface. One shop can expose x402 resources and human publications; different payment means are not automatically substitutable. A display on shop.561.group (historical human sales role) is not the same as a signed resource endpoint on market.561.group. Shop-specific source catalog, price and authority remain with their merchant. A third-party offer shown by a bazaar does **not** become the bazaar operator's own inventory.

### Bazaar B: the discovery morphism across shop boundaries

A Bazaar is an *indexed discovery relation* between demands/queries, independently sourced shop Offer declarations, and retrievable resource descriptions. The x402 v2 term already names a discovery extension and optional facilitator search/list API; any facilitator may operate its own index. One facilitator's snapshot is not the market universe. Distinguish a true multi-provider federation from a search page over one seller. The *discovery receipt* must identify original seller, original resource, terms revision, source index, observation time, independent qualification state and missing facts. An expired, unsigned or withdrawn resource stays historical evidence but ceases to qualify for current referral.

**Stall (proposed optional user-interface term, not x402 wire terminology):** a seller-scoped publicly authorized slice of a Bazaar. A stall has the source actor's offer identities and reference URLs, not a new wallet, global inventory or power of sale. One seller may participate in multiple indexes; never duplicate its resources or count multiple index records as multiple sales.

### Market M: a distributed exchange institution and concurrent game

A market comprises demands, qualified offered capabilities, interacting principals, rules and legal authority to match, contract, pay, deliver and accept; its outcome records are not reducible to one directory. A market may exist without an automated Bazaar; a Bazaar may index resources that never trade. Markets can include auctions, negotiation, commissioned work, order books, capacity allocations, subscriptions and contingent rights—not solely x402 fixed-price calls. The market interface at market.561.group is a partial read of that institution, not the institution itself.

**Candidate compositional typing**, to be implemented/derived from prior marks, not postulated as an Obsmetrologia axiom:

- Objects are *scoped economic positions* P carrying principals, token/claim readings, applicable jurisdiction, source offer/request identities, typed obligations and observed state. Some candidate positions are empty/open, quoted, authorized, executing, delivered, independently received.
- A morphism P → Q is a **witnessed admissible transition** (publish, qualify, propose, accept, settle, deliver, receive), carrying a source event, lawful actor, before/after state and requirements met or held. An unsupported instruction is not a morphism merely because it has a name.
- Identity is the witnessed unchanged-position transition; composition f;g exists only if the output of f and input of g are the *same admitted position* and authority/evidence conditions hold. Source equality is not label-string equality. Composition must retain distinct causes and receipts, avoid double settlement and be associative under stated hypotheses. A bare HTTP route object does not supply such composition.
- Products/parallel play are admissible only for *independent* positions/actions. Two calls sharing one-use payment nonce, finite quota, mutable inventory, beneficiary assent or seller authority do **not** commute merely because their endpoint URLs differ.
- A mapping from one evidence-bearing presentation (ActivityPub Offer, Bazaar record, MCP/OpenAPI, RDF/JSON-LD) to another is a claimed semantics-preserving representation, not a proven functor until it preserves admitted identities, composition, source/rights and negative/unknown evidence on finite cases.

This is a **candidate category after the observational distinctions**, not permission to import conventional categories/graphs/sheaves as the premises of relationality.

## 4. How the market is a computer AND a concurrent game

Existing AOE Gaming correctly has a concrete order-book *candidate* structure:
- Resting bid and ask events are negative/Opponent; a potentially crossing pair may produce positive/Proponent fill event.
- A fill is causally dependent on its bid and ask, and competing uses of the same indivisible level conflict.
- The source's price crossing uses exact graded rationals (not floating-point prices).
- Gaming symmetry is **a structural judgment** preserving polarity, causes and conflicts—not an assertion that every pair of seller identities is interchangeable.
- The separate J26 frontier detects a face-contact route to an *independently authored hypothetical quote* while explicitly denying settlement, capacity, authority and physical-tiling inference.

**Critical currently unproved claim:** AOE Gaming arena.mjs names a function *equilibrium* and its commentary repeatedly equates merely running addDesign(bid), addDesign(ask), settle with “Walras/Nash equilibrium”. Its code checks no preferences/payoffs, best responses, competitive clearing, budgets, global excess demand or deviating unilateral strategies. Therefore this operation witnesses (at most) the **game engine's terminal/settled position**, NOT a mathematical Nash or Walrasian equilibrium. Counterexample: a game terminating after a buyer and seller exchange at a price where a feasible beneficial deviation exists still terminates, but fails Nash. Engineering must retain historical callable identity for compatibility while changing advertised claims to “settled game position; equilibrium conditions unassayed”, and only expose an independently verified equilibrium predicate if it checks its own necessary conditions. This is a scientific correction, not a refactoring preference.

**Second limitation:** The current pairwise fill conflict treats each price *level* as non-copyable. A 10-unit resting order can legally be partially consumed by two different 3-unit fills, whereas the current event model marks both as in conflict if they share a level. That representation is a valid **atomic-level toy model**, not an equivalent matching engine for partial-fill order books. A proper game read must carry quantity-preserving residual states or finer-grained uniquely identified order lots and their conservation constraints. Do not assert double-auction equivalence from a finite toy trace.

**Symmetry counterexample:** Two identical-output resource descriptions owned by different sellers with distinct payees, licenses, rights or constraints cannot be permuted as principals without altering valid contracts. Finite name permutations are allowed only for provably exchangeable *uncommitted labels* while holding legal principals, payment destinations, capacities, causes, evidence and beneficiary invariant. Anonymity in an order book must respect price/time/quantity and institutional priority rules, not suppress consent or provenance.

A market **computes** in the operational sense when admitted rules take real source-told positions and eligible moves to new positions with recorded refusals, costs and conservation. It is a concurrent game when co-enabled moves, conflicts, causality, distinct agent strategies and symmetry actually appear in these transitions. A web listing page alone is neither a clearing computer nor an equilibrium witness.

## 5. Coordinates, fibers, graphs, charts and sheaf obligations

Let O_v be *only the source-qualified and listed Offers visible at fixed source version v*. Its seller/catalog projection already supplies NI SKU, original actor/enterprise, operation, input/output, producer package, category, gateway, method and public route. Define the finite observable coordinate map c_v(o) = (source seller, category, producer, gateway, operation contract). Missing seller/producer data have the distinct status “unknown”; they are not equal to another record's missing identity. Coordinates are **derived views of existing identity-bearing Offers**, never a competing registry or global SKU.

For a bounded set of explicit demand constraints q, define the **fiber** F_v(q) = {o ∈ O_v : o meets every tested hard constraint of q on actual admissible source evidence}. The “fiber” is a well-defined set-theoretic inverse-image only where every predicate and its observation contract is specified; it is not by itself a sheaf or formal dependent-type theorem. With fixed v and conjunctive narrowing q' adding a hard condition, F_v(q') ⊆ F_v(q). A regression test may confirm this finite monotonicity. If newly discovered providers change v, counts may rise even when q narrows; do not falsely compare distinct universes.

Discovery/free navigation can read the existing authoritative marketCatalogReadout, typed Sen/SCA offer identities and source citations to:
1. expose bounded coordinate faceting and seller/producer/category *fibers* without paying;
2. show capability composition compatibility (output contract of A can feed input of B), explicitly marking schema-only match versus empirically tested, licensed and authorized flow;
3. provide shortest *evidence-supported* paths from a demanded outcome through one or more qualified provider capabilities, with no implicit power to pay a middleman;
4. expose a typed provider choice: required filters → admitted options → missing evidence → objective comparison under actual total user outcome cost, latency, quality, freshness and rights → chosen/rejected reason;
5. make all views available as bounded HTTP JSON and MCP reads; publish a read-only RDF/JSON-LD named-graph projection, with proper provenance and a small SPARQL SELECT surface only when an existing RDF consumer safely parses it.

An OWL statement may say “offer has provider”; open-world OWL cannot treat silence as *proof* that another provider, price, conflict or authority does not exist. SHACL can enforce presence/typing on **a particular closed admission slice**; it does not validate seller rights or deliverability. Never expose arbitrary SPARQL SERVICE/federated remote fetches to customer input (SSRF), unbounded joins, unmetered inference, arbitrary graph mutation or secret settlement logs. A bounded query budget is itself a service contract.

For an evidence sheaf **candidate**, first specify (a) an actual base of overlapping demand/territory/contract contexts, (b) local source-authorized sections, (c) legal/economic restriction maps that preserve original actor and terms, and (d) equality/compatibility on overlaps. A global section exists only where sections actually agree on required terms; seller A quoting USDC/Base and seller B quoting HBD/Hive are not one globally payable quote, even if both claim the same output. A FOUR contested claim is not by itself a nonzero H¹; constructing a Cech complex, boundary maps and obstruction proof is a separate mathematical obligation, requiring the proper Obsmetrologia derivation and tests.

**Charts** should display historical states/time windows with distinct units: listed resources, externally attempted invocations, valid 402 quotes, authorized calls, independently settled payments, delivered outputs, customer-accepted outcomes and net contribution. A chart is an observation of those units, not a price prediction or invented “market GMV”.

## 6. Circulation, attention and the whirling-dervish hypothesis

A market can produce feedback loops: a discovered offer receives qualified demand, updates price/capacity, changes location in the search/match graph, which changes attention and future demand. That is an observed *recurrence only when the same identities and events are time-linked*, not because a circle looks appealing. The 561 Group's separate mathematical Dervish Recurrence conjecture (Obsmetrologia blueprint, recurring FOUR / pentagon work) has not been shown to apply to commercial settlement flows. Do not import its topological conclusion, an arbitrage assertion, an entropy metaphor or a liquidity theorem here.

Testable hypotheses with rival accounts:
- H1 (search-cost mechanism): source-attributed coordinate matching reduces median eligible task-to-usable-provider time against ordinary keyword Bazaar search **at fixed candidate population**, with hard-requirement false-positive rate no greater. Rival: higher recall from extra listings, not better reasoning. Negative control: queries already matched by exact names.
- H2 (concurrency mechanism): a causality/conflict-aware plan avoids double-use of one-use authorization, capacity or token while preserving independent co-enabled requests, compared to a sequential/naive graph path model. Negative control: disjoint read-only calls should compose freely.
- H3 (economic mechanism): more qualified independent **delivered and accepted outcomes per unit of buyer attention/cost** versus payapi.market and other comparable discovery surfaces, rather than raw SKU/listing/provider counts. Selection bias and external seller choice remain explicit.
- H4 (circulation mechanism): measured recurrent customer/provider loops can be distinguished from one-off speculative x402 self-tests after holding observation windows and changed inventory constant. Rival: vendor telemetry merely records retries and self-purchases, no external recurrent integration.
- H5 (sheaf mechanism): overlapping provider claims with incompatible recipients, licenses, currencies or expired terms are held contested and **not globally executable**. Negative control: two independently valid but incompatible quotes must refuse gluing, even if their descriptions match.

No hypothesis currently has a study result or kernel proof here.

## 8. Finite negative controls and receiving gates

1. Two offers have the same public label but different actual payTo: NO identity collapse, no symmetric interchange of legal principals, no buyer signature inferred.
2. Two requests share a once-only nonce or capacity lot: neither a second settlement nor a commuting parallel transition is admitted.
3. Two offers share a producer package and category, but incompatible input units or rights: related graph nodes, **not** executable composition.
4. Seller-authored “verified” flag with no independent receipt: report unassayed. Unlisted/held routes: never appear in free offer fibers or invoke flows.
5. New demand condition is added at fixed source revision: the admitted fiber is a subset or the same. Source revision changes: incomparable candidate universes unless versioned.
6. Bazaar listing without one matched live payable 402: show discovered, not currently executable. Signed payment without accepted result: record settled-but-unfulfilled independently.
7. On-chain EIP-3009 activity lacks x402 provenance: don't count as x402 customers. Cross-SKU distinct-payer totals: do not add as unique people.
8. Three-actor arena with two legitimate simultaneous read-only moves: no forged causal edge from label references; check event-structure causes only (AOE PR #19).
9. Partial-fill book of ten units with two 3-unit executions: current atomic-level conflict model fails to represent both; don't advertise real-book equivalence without finer-grained capacity.
10. Terminal game with profitable unilateral deviation: settling terminates but **fails** Nash. Never publish “equilibrium verified” without utility/strategy deviation proof.
11. Unknown legal/CARE source status: do not interpret as granted resale or consent, and do not substitute RDF/OWL entailment for original authority.
12. Provider A explicitly withdraws a signed offer: historical link may persist, but must not be ranked as current or exercised; stale index evidence remains dated.

**Source qualification complete for this research artifact only.** Deployment of bazaar.561.group, real external provider receiving, production-source cutover and accepted buyer purchases are independent gates, currently open (Causeway market issue #4; Coesite external provider issue #5). This note itself performs no commerce.

## 9. Summary of warranted names

- **x402 payable resource** — protocol-standard noun for an addressable paid endpoint; MCP **tool** is the analogous method-level target. “Resource” already has a name; don't mint one.
- **Offer** — independently authored, attributable contingent economic promise/terms about a capability; not merely an endpoint.
- **Shop** — one seller-principal's controlled sales/inquiry projection; still not a globally authorized marketplace.
- **Stall** — *proposed* optional seller-scoped slice as seen in a Bazaar; discovery only and not an x402 wire field.
- **Bazaar** — per x402 standard, source metadata and a facilitator-discovery interface; our proposed federated interpretation can display several independently qualified shops with original authority intact.
- **Market** — the broader multi-actor economic institution and play/computation of demands, offers, qualifying choices, obligations, performances, receipts and capital/attention allocation. Not a synonym for a list of API URLs.

The competitive opportunity is to compute **qualified, explainable, source-authorized paths to fulfilled outcomes**, not to describe a catalog as a “category” and a basket of untested listings as “the market”.
