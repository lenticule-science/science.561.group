What laws, not brand associations, would justify four different carriers named market, bazaar, shop and resource, and what can our one real merchant and AOE actually construct?

## 2. First generate distinctions, then ask for carriers

Start with a claimant uttering a predicate about a marked subject, keeping the claimant and its conditions. This is not a seller's self-assertion becoming public truth. Distinct utterances:
1. Demand: actor D claims a wanted outcome, with requirements, beneficiary, location/jurisdiction, budget, deadline and accepting receiver.
2. Capability: actor S claims it can supply an operation or outcome with specified constraints; this is not proof of deliverability.
3. Offer: attributable S publishes contingent terms binding a capability to recipients, price/consideration, input/output, validity, legal rights and obligations; a listing is not acceptance.
4. Discovery: index B reports encountering an independently sourced Offer; this report neither transfers S's authority nor changes the Offer.
5. Quote: a particular route responds with current payment requirements; an unsigned HTTP 402 is data, not independent authority to pay S.
6. Accept/authorize: an entitled buyer and seller exercise the actual contract and payment powers; identity, scope, nonce and authorization must be preserved.
7. Execute/deliver: the operation actually occurs and returns a bound result, or fails with separate recovery.
8. Receive/settle: independently attributable delivery acceptance and money movement are separately evidenced and reconciled with measured costs.

Two different recorded transitions may refer to the same contract without being the same fact. FOUR can retain incompatible testimony; contested evidence must not be collapsed into positive admission, nor does a missing negative witness permit an effect.

**Counterexamples:** a search hit is not an offer; an offer is not a payable resource; an x402 HTTP 402 is not a signed purchase; a submitted signature is not independently settled; an on-chain EIP-3009 AuthorizationUsed is not necessarily x402; a completed transfer is not accepted work; a count of listings is not demand. The historical August 2026 Bazaar coverage estimate failed precisely because its numerators/denominators were not type-aligned.

## 3. Four candidate carriers and their composition boundaries

### Resource R: one invocable address, not a market

For HTTP, a resource reference includes the seller-controlled origin, method, route template and documented input/output schema; an individual invocation also has exact parameters and potentially authorization and time. For MCP, the official Bazaar input discriminant is an MCP tool name plus its input schema at a server endpoint. A resource reference describes *how to address an invocation*, not whether it is lawful, paid, working or fulfillable. Distinguish route template from the concrete invocation URL: different path parameters can be one indexed resource. x402 v2 publishes the **resource** in PaymentRequired, and the optional **bazaar** extension publishes discovery input/output metadata; it does not grant new payment authority.

Candidate x402 Resource identity: (seller-authoritative protocol identity, source resource URI or MCP server+tool, transport/method, route template, declaration version). **Do not change an existing NI/SKU, ActivityPub Offer identity, or recoverable transaction key to adopt these descriptors.** Quote identity additionally requires actual network, scheme, asset, amount, payee, validity and buyer-authorized payload. Two quotes with the same human name or price are not the same signed resource contract.

### Shop S: a principal-scoped offer-publishing boundary

A shop is a seller-controlled projection of that principal's authored offers and permitted ways to order or inquire. Its authority is supplied by the seller's actual actor and accounts, not the domain name or the browsing interface. One shop can expose x402 resources and human publications; different payment means are not automatically substitutable. A display on shop.561.group (historical human sales role) is not the same as a signed resource endpoint on market.561.group. Shop-specific source catalog, price and authority remain with their merchant. A third-party offer shown by a bazaar does **not** become the bazaar operator's own inventory.

### Bazaar B: the discovery morphism across shop boundaries

A Bazaar is an *indexed discovery relation* between demands/queries, independently sourced shop Offer declarations, and retrievable resource descriptions. The x402 v2 term already names a discovery extension and optional facilitator search/list API; any facilitator may operate its own index. One facilitator's snapshot is not the market universe. Distinguish a true multi-provider federation from a search page over one seller. The *discovery receipt* must identify original seller, original resource, terms revision, source index, observation time, independent qualification state and missing facts. An expired, unsigned or withdrawn resource stays historical evidence but ceases to qualify for current referral.

**Stall (proposed optional user-interface term, not x402 wire terminology):** a seller-scoped publicly authorized slice of a Bazaar. A stall has the source actor's offer identities and reference URLs, not a new wallet, global inventory or power of sale. One seller may participate in multiple indexes; never duplicate its resources or count multiple index records as multiple sales.

### Market M: a distributed exchange institution and concurrent game

A market comprises demands, qualified offered capabilities, interacting principals, rules and legal authority to match, contract, pay, deliver and accept; its outcome records are not reducible to one directory. A market may exist without an automated Bazaar; a Bazaar may index resources that never trade. Markets can include auctions, negotiation, commissioned work, order books, capacity allocations, subscriptions and contingent rights—not solely x402 fixed-price calls. The market interface at market.561.group is a partial read of that institution, not the institution itself.

**Candidate compositional typing**, to be implemented/derived from prior marks, not postulated as an Obsmetrologia axiom:

- Objects are *scoped economic positions* P carrying principals, token/claim readings, applicable jurisdiction, source offer/request identities, typed obligations and observed state. Some candidate positions are empty/open, quoted, authorized, executing, delivered, independently received.
- A morphism P → Q is a **witnessed admissible transition** (publish, qualify, propose, accept, settle, deliver, receive), carrying a source event, lawful actor, before/after state and requirements met or held. An unsupported instruction is not a morphism merely because it has a name.
- Identity is the witnessed unchanged-position transition; composition f;g exists only if the output of f and input of g are the *same admitted position* and authority/evidence conditions hold. Source equality is not label-string equality. Composition must retain distinct causes and receipts, avoid double settlement and be associative under stated hypotheses. A bare HTTP route object does not supply such composition.
- Products/parallel play are admissible only for *independent* positions/actions. Two calls sharing one-use payment nonce, finite quota, mutable inventory, beneficiary assent or seller authority do **not** commute merely because their endpoint URLs differ.
- A mapping from one evidence-bearing presentation (ActivityPub Offer, Bazaar record, MCP/OpenAPI, RDF/JSON-LD) to another is a claimed semantics-preserving representation, not a proven functor until it preserves admitted identities, composition, source/rights and negative/unknown evidence on finite cases.

This is a **candidate category after the observational distinctions**, not permission to import conventional categories/graphs/sheaves as the premises of relationality.

### A finite path construction: where a category actually comes from

The preceding list of candidate objects and morphisms is insufficient by itself to establish a category. Here is the narrower **mathematical construction** the current observational premises permit. It is an ordinary finite/set-theoretic *derived candidate realization*; it is **not a Lean-kernel proof within Obsmetrologia**, and its starting set must eventually be constructed from Obsmetrologia's admitted mark/predication and settling structures rather than posited as a replacement foundation.

**Data first.** At a specified source revision and observation cut, let \(P\) be a collection of *scoped recorded economic positions* with actual actor/office/beneficiary, claim and contra-claim references, admissible powers, owner-controlled one-use nonces and capacities, outstanding obligations and immutable original offer/request addresses. Let \(E\) be a collection of *individually evidenced and admitted changes*, each carrying an exact source event address, witness-status, actor authority, input position \(s(e)\in P\), output position \(t(e)\in P\), and effect/status. Both sides of a contradiction remain readable; a seller quotation without the buyer's authority is never an admitted debit. These are **requirements for the data of the construction**, not a claim that the entire current Semioverse holds such a complete population.

**Paths.** A path \(p:x\to y\) is a finite ordered list \((e_1,\ldots,e_n)\) of *actually admitted* edges, with \(s(e_1)=x\), \(t(e_n)=y\) and \(t(e_i)=s(e_{i+1})\). The empty path \(\epsilon_x:x\to x\) is a formal identity (zero events): it must **not** be mistaken for a recorded or independently witnessed no-op economic transaction. Composition \(q\circ p\) is concatenation **only when** \(t(p)=s(q)\) is established as the same complete position, including original identity/capacity/authorization state, not merely similar JSON or URL. It is undefined for mismatched boundaries.

**Proof obligation and elementary derived laws.** Conditional on those typed definitions, \(\epsilon_y\circ p=p=p\circ\epsilon_x\) by the empty-list law. For composable \(p,q,r\), \((r\circ q)\circ p=r\circ(q\circ p)\) because finite list concatenation is associative. That constructs a **free path category of admitted finite observations**. Equality here is structural equality of source-addressed paths (including event identity, ordering and source/proof status), *not* equal total price, terminal output text or graph shape. One independent receipt cannot be copied into two historical paths and counted as two settlements. These elementary list laws do not prove that the admission decisions are sound or that a particular payment was authorized. The latter require separate state-transition, conservation, authority and independent-receiving invariants.

**Small exact refutations.** For seller \(s\) with unspent nonce \(n\), \(e_1:(s,n\text{ free})\to(s,n\text{ spent})\) may be admitted once. An otherwise identical \(e_2\) demanding \(n\text{ free}\) has no source position matching \(t(e_1)\), so \(e_2\circ e_1\) is **undefined**; a program composing solely on offer or payee labels violates the typed construction. An unsigned 402 is an observed quote-transition, not an authorization-transition. A proof/status change from asserted delivery to independently received output requires its own edge. Neither a terminal game position nor a priced Offer witnesses the existence of any further market-matching morphism.

**Concurrency as witnessed squares.** To assert two moves \(a,b\) commute, it is not enough that \(a\) and \(b\) share a category or appear in distinct HTTP calls. One must show a square of admitted moves from *the same* source position \(x\): \(a:x\to x_a\), \(b:x\to x_b\), \(b_a:x_a\to z\), \(a_b:x_b\to z\), with both composites enabled and equality of the final **scoped** position \(z\), retained sources and independent receipts. For independent read-only queries over frozen versioned offer data such a square can sometimes be demonstrated. For two debits against a shared balance, two writes to a versioned capability record, or two uses of nonce \(n\), the square fails. Only demonstrated commuting squares can support a causal-independence quotient or trace-style concurrency; declaring FSym-equivalence of seller/payee principals from nominal names violates the invariant. A higher category/double category of positions, concurrent plays and coherent squares remains a **future construction**, not an automatic product of the free path category.

**What the four terms denote in this construction:**

- *Resource*: an addressable seller-controlled operation/interface, possibly with multiple typed input instances and multiple quote observations; it is neither a path nor a completed edge. For MCP, x402 Bazaar defines the discovery key as the tuple **(resource URL, toolName)**. A tool name alone and a shared MCP URL alone are both insufficient. HTTP discovery additionally needs the method and input contract; a concrete x402 v2 PaymentRequired describes present resource URL and accepted payment requirements. The optional Bazaar declaration is in PaymentRequired.extensions.bazaar.info, validated by the accompanying **same-document** JSON Schema, with no external schema-ref fetching.
- *Shop*: a **principal-indexed** family of lawful author-authored offer claims and permitted resource presentations. Treat \(S_a(U)\) as the original seller \(a\)'s admissible offers visible in context \(U\); another principal cannot become owner merely because its Offer appears in this projection. The shop does not form the whole exchange category: it generally cannot discharge buyer authorization, third-party rights or independent receiving.
- *Bazaar*: an **observation-indexed discovery relation** \(B_v(q,a,o,\rho)\), relating query/demand \(q\), actual source seller \(a\), its offer identity \(o\), and a dated, provenance-bearing index observation \(\rho\) at snapshot \(v\). A search result is a reported matching **edge in a relation**, not an exchange effect. To upgrade this relation to a profunctor between categories of demand refinement and source-offer presentation one must separately specify the two variance actions, prove they preserve identity/composition and ensure stale/withdrawn/disallowed offers never reappear under restriction. The current selector only establishes finite conjunction over *one* seller. It is **not yet a proven profunctor, sheaf, federation or merchant-neutral chooser**.
- *Market*: the larger partial computation/game of multiple principals whose **admitted paths** enact solicitations, negotiations, authorizations, services, transfers, delivery and independent receiving, together with observable causal interference, governance and economic outcome. The category of admitted paths is a structural *view* of histories; a market adds incentives, actual law, alternative strategies, scarce-capacity competition and recipient outcomes, none derivable from mere path concatenation.

**Functor qualification.** A site-generated OpenAPI row, an MCP tool, an ActivityPub Offer and a Bazaar listing may all point to the **same original** seller Offer. A read-only projection preserving its original NI/actor/wire identity is a lawful *information map*; it is only a categorical functor if the source and target categories and preservation of identities, valid composition and relevant source/refusal distinctions are stated and proved. In particular, a projection that forgets original payee or consent can be useful as navigation but cannot reflect economic equivalence or authorize delegated trade. Do not claim it is faithful, full or an equivalence without witnessed counterexample tests.

**Typed example, deliberately not a sale.** Demand \(d\) requires an astronomical position in a stated frame/time with independent accuracy/rights evidence. Seller A exposes a NASA-derived endpoint with a source-addressed Offer; seller B exposes an approximate geocentric sky tool; a Bazaar may index both. Category and descriptive phrase overlap, but the *demand fiber* selects only an Offer whose measured frame, uncertainty, rights, region and current capacity satisfy the exact requirement. If A is selected and its quote has different payee/network from the independently authorized buyer intent, there is no legal payment edge. The marketplace graph can still show the source Offer and obstruction. The finite witness of eligible selection is not a proof that any buyer paid or obtained the result.

**Kernel-grade mathematical receiving target.** Recover from original Obsmetrologia source the typed observational distinction/predication state and proof-carrying event boundaries; realize \(P,E\) and source equality over those types; then machine-check (1) identity and concatenation laws on valid paths, (2) retention of original evidence under composition, (3) authority/capacity/nonce one-use under actual transition rules, (4) a non-commuting counterexample, (5) a partial-fill quantity-preservation counterexample for the current Gaming arena, and (6) proof-status-preserving projections to Gaming/Organizing/Networking. Keep the proof status **open** until declaration names, axioms and kernel receipt exist. This prevents conventional category axioms from silently becoming the foundation of the user's relationality program.

## 4. How the market is a computer AND a concurrent game

Existing AOE Gaming correctly has a concrete order-book *candidate* structure:
- Resting bid and ask events are negative/Opponent; a potentially crossing pair may produce positive/Proponent fill event.
- A fill is causally dependent on its bid and ask, and competing uses of the same indivisible level conflict.
- The source's price crossing uses exact graded rationals (not floating-point prices).
- Gaming symmetry is **a structural judgment** preserving polarity, causes and conflicts—not an assertion that every pair of seller identities is interchangeable.
- The separate J26 frontier detects a face-contact route to an *independently authored hypothetical quote* while explicitly denying settlement, capacity, authority and physical-tiling inference.

**Critical currently unproved claim:** AOE Gaming arena.mjs names a function *equilibrium* and its commentary repeatedly equates merely running addDesign(bid), addDesign(ask), settle with “Walras/Nash equilibrium”. Its code checks no preferences/payoffs, best responses, competitive clearing, budgets, global excess demand or deviating unilateral strategies. Therefore this operation witnesses (at most) the **game engine's terminal/settled position**, NOT a mathematical Nash or Walrasian equilibrium. Counterexample: a game terminating after a buyer and seller exchange at a price where a feasible beneficial deviation exists still terminates, but fails Nash. Engineering must retain historical callable identity for compatibility while changing advertised claims to “settled game position; equilibrium conditions unassayed”, and only expose an independently verified equilibrium predicate if it checks its own necessary conditions. This is a scientific correction, not a refactoring preference.

**Second limitation:** The current pairwise fill conflict treats each price *level* as non-copyable. A 10-unit resting order can legally be partially consumed by two different 3-unit fills, whereas the current event model marks both as in conflict if they share a level. That representation is a valid **atomic-level toy model**, not an equivalent matching engine for partial-fill order books. A proper game read must carry quantity-preserving residual states or finer-grained uniquely identified order lots and their conservation constraints. Do not assert double-auction equivalence from a finite toy trace.

**Symmetry counterexample:** Two identical-output resource descriptions owned by different sellers with distinct payees, licenses, rights or constraints cannot be permuted as principals without altering valid contracts. Finite name permutations are allowed only for provably exchangeable *uncommitted labels* while holding legal principals, payment destinations, capacities, causes, evidence and beneficiary invariant. Anonymity in an order book must respect price/time/quantity and institutional priority rules, not suppress consent or provenance.

A market **computes** in the operational sense when admitted rules take real source-told positions and eligible moves to new positions with recorded refusals, costs and conservation. It is a concurrent game when co-enabled moves, conflicts, causality, distinct agent strategies and symmetry actually appear in these transitions. A web listing page alone is neither a clearing computer nor an equilibrium witness.

## 5. Coordinates, fibers, graphs, charts and sheaf obligations

Let O_v be *only the source-qualified and listed Offers visible at fixed source version v*. Its seller/catalog projection already supplies NI SKU, original actor/enterprise, operation, input/output, producer package, category, gateway, method and public route. Define the finite observable coordinate map c_v(o) = (source seller, category, producer, gateway, operation contract). Missing seller/producer data have the distinct status “unknown”; they are not equal to another record's missing identity. ## 6. Circulation, attention and the whirling-dervish hypothesis

A market can produce feedback loops: a discovered offer receives qualified demand, updates price/capacity, changes location in the search/match graph, which changes attention and future demand. That is an observed *recurrence only when the same identities and events are time-linked*, not because a circle looks appealing. The 561 Group's separate mathematical Dervish Recurrence conjecture (Obsmetrologia blueprint, recurring FOUR / pentagon work) has not been shown to apply to commercial settlement flows. Do not import its topological conclusion, an arbitrage assertion, an entropy metaphor or a liquidity theorem here.

### A proposed empirical circulation assay, not an asserted Dervish theorem

There are at least three different senses of “a market whirls,” and they cannot substitute for one another:

1. A *metaphor of bustling trade* describes neither a state space nor a conserved quantity.
2. A *directed recurrent exchange process* is testable, if genuine time-ordered customer, provider, demand, quote and outcome events are linked with stable original addresses and common units.
3. A *Dervish / pentagonal obstruction* in the existing Obsmetrologia blueprint is a much stronger, explicitly conjectural connection among \(D_5\)-equivariant spectral, contextuality, quasicrystalline and algebraic readouts. **Nothing in today's marketplace observations proves that a commercial graph possesses that mathematical carrier or obstruction.**

For a bounded, fixed-version, rights-admitted empirical study, distinguish **one obligation's monotone lifecycle** (qualified → selected → authorized → delivered → independently received, which cannot legitimately rewind) from **a principal's repeated choices across multiple original Work items** (for instance, a buyer choosing provider A, then B, then A on distinct comparable independently received jobs). To study economic return/circulation, the graph states must represent a genuinely revisitable same-unit choice or allocation, NOT reverse terminal states of one contract. Define a **transition** only when an original actor's consecutive, separately attributable eligible episodes yield movement \(i \to j\) in that specified comparable state space, with original Work/beneficiary per episode, buyer consent, source event identity, clock and outcome retained. A browse click, raw 402, internal self-purchase or unrelated provider's listing is **not** automatically such a transition. Fix an actual clock interval \(I\), source universe and demand/commodity class before counting; do not combine incompatible countries, assets or observations.

Let \(N_{ij}(I)\) count time-bounded, properly deduplicated actual transitions from state \(i\) to \(j\) in **one comparable state space**, preserving actor/work source and the unit “transition.” Define antisymmetric empirical net circulation \(J_{ij}=N_{ij}-N_{ji}\). This is an ordinary finite derived statistic, **not a new category, money asset or Obsmetrologia primitive**. The incidence boundary of the directed transition counts is \((\partial N)_i=\sum_j (N_{ji}-N_{ij})=-\sum_j J_{ij}\); this detects net arrival at each state without double-counting opposite orientations. A nonzero balanced circulation requires both \(J\neq 0\) and \(\partial N=0\) on the chosen observed interior (with boundary/external flow accounted for); unbalanced flow is ordinary net migration or accumulation, **not** evidence of a closed whirl. Source gaps, missing returns and observation censoring prevent declaring the latter. A state’s signed in/out receipts are maintained separately from monetary or attention “charge”; conservation of *transition counts* is not conservation of USDC, seller capital, physical energy or ATT.

**Finite counterexamples:** one directed path A→B→C has nonzero edges but \(\partial J\neq0\) at endpoints, so cannot be claimed as a closed circulation. A→B→C→A is a balanced three-cycle, but supplies **no D5 symmetry**. Five independent seller brands arranged on a pentagon graphic also supply no genuine equvariance. A sequence A→B→A has equal opposing visits and \(J=0\), even though users revisit A; it is “repeat activity,” not nonzero directed circulation under this antisymmetric statistic. An ad click that never produced a buyer-selected provider remains attention observation, not settled economic turnover.

**Rival mechanisms to compare:** (a) a true state-dependent recurrent transition process with persistent signed circulation; (b) transient promotions and rank/fee shocks followed by return; (c) exposure and indexing artifacts from a temporarily incomplete Bazaar; (d) retry storms, test wallets or self-trading; (e) seasonality and observation-window aliasing; (f) independent but superficially similar actors falsely joined by a shared display name. Pre-register one null model preserving marginal state visitation and actor/time exposure, shuffle only time/order when lawful and meaningful, calculate a finite permutation/control distribution for \(J\), and require replicated out-of-sample recurrence rather than inferring a dynamical attractor from one loop.

**Practical next seller-to-bazaar outcome:** when the real market already has independently settled buyer-choice and accepted-delivery events, a *bounded free chart* can show transition rates, net circulation, incomplete/missing edges, cross-provider losses/repeat outcomes and provenance, with rights/PII minimization. It must use those original observed event records, not invent traffic, fake market cycles or a second ledger. A customer’s original demand and resulting independently accepted output must survive the visualization. Correlation between repeat flow and reduced search time or cost is a testable commercial hypothesis, **not** a claim that the Web has discovered the Obsmetrologia Dervish obstruction.
