## Why this is central

This paper supplies the house's citation for [HANDY model](../../../glossary/terms/handy-model.md): a minimal predator-prey-style model in which inequality of resource access between an Elite and a Commoner population, rather than resource depletion by itself, is the mechanism most reliably producing societal collapse, because elites are insulated from early scarcity signals while the commoner labor base that harvests resources is depleted.

## What it does not settle

HANDY is a stylized, low-dimensional model calibrated to illustrate a mechanism, not to reproduce any specific historical society's trajectory quantitatively; its Elite/Commoner split and shared Nature/Wealth stocks are a deliberate simplification, and the paper does not claim the model's specific parameter thresholds for collapse apply, as measured, to any named present-day economy. The paper received public criticism on release (including from NASA, which noted it had not reviewed or endorsed the paper despite its funding acknowledgment) for the gap between its media reception as a "NASA study" predicting civilizational collapse and its actual, narrower status as an independently authored theoretical model.
