# Sizing without a bank

Owner-stated on the evening of 2026-09-04, in the owner's shorthand: the house's
finance code says "Kelly" a lot, and that is good in some ways, but the work has
to generalize to moves, proof trees, strategies, and arenas, because modern
theory shows growth-optimal sizing is not available to an actor genuinely at
risk of ruin; and settler colonialism makes infinite growth look real (replenish
from the bank), which biases business practice toward a safety norm that is not
ours. The question underneath: how does a small group of poor, disabled,
struggling people turn every bit of capital they can get their hands on, and do
not need to spend at once, into as much liquid capital as they can, in the best
ways they can. Every claim below is marked as the owner's ruling, a corpus
carrier, an outside source already on disk, or this note's reading.

## 1. What Kelly is, and what it quietly assumes

The corpus already holds Kelly precisely
(`finance-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kelly-criterion.md`,
the 2026-08-27 note on growth-optimal sizing when ruin is cheap): the fraction
maximizing expected log wealth, `f* = edge/odds` for a simple bet and
`(μ − r)/σ²` for a lognormal asset, is the unique rule that beats every
essentially different rule by an unbounded factor as the horizon lengthens.

This note's reading of the assumptions that theorem carries, each of which fails
for the people this org serves:

- **Infinitely divisible stakes and no minimum bet.** The risk-of-ruin term
  (`risk-of-ruin.md`) already says ruin need not be zero: "inability to place
  the minimum trade, loss of required operating capital." With a barrier above
  zero, the wealth process is absorbed before the log objective's infinite
  penalty ever engages, and the growth-optimal fraction is strictly below Kelly
  and depends on the distance to the barrier. The 2026-08-27 note records that
  no purpose-built literature was found for a bounded disutility of ruin; the
  CRRA rescaling and the quantile reframing are the nearest tools, and both
  diverge at their limits.
- **No consumption from the stake.** Kelly's process reinvests everything. A
  household draws rent and food from the same pool. A drawn-down process with a
  barrier is a different process, and its optimal fraction is not Kelly's.
- **Known parameters.** The estimation-error argument for shading below Kelly
  never goes away (2026-08-27 note, section on what full Kelly does to an
  account); it is the one shading argument that survives even when the stake is
  written off.
- **An asymptotic guarantee.** Kelly's dominance is as the horizon lengthens.
  A person who needs the money by the first of the month is not at the limit.

So "we cannot do Kelly if we are actually at risk of ruin" is exact: the
theorem's premises are the premises of an actor with no barrier, no draw, and
unbounded time. That actor is the one who has a bank.

## 2. The bank, the frontier, and the ensemble

Peters (bibliography carrier `peters2019.md` under cybernetic-postliberalism;
glossary `ergodicity.md`) supplies the distinction: the time average is what
one trajectory experiences repeated over time; the ensemble average is what
many parallel trajectories average at one moment. Wealth growth generally lacks
ergodicity, so the two differ, and expected-value reasoning is ensemble
reasoning.

This note's reading of the owner's point about settlerism, grounded in the
carriers the corpus already holds
(`2026-08-16-note-on-561-group-as-watersheds.md`: flatness as the settler
geometry, "boundless, unpressured, settler-infinite";
`settlerism-as-filing-system.md`; `2026-08-16-note-on-561-group-as-meme-beadworkers.md`
on allotment): ensemble reasoning is rational for exactly one kind of actor,
the one who can be replenished from outside after a ruined trajectory. A
frontier is that replenishment made geographic: every failed homestead was
replaced by another settler on another parcel, so the colony as a whole realized
the ensemble average while each family faced the time average. A bank, a
bankruptcy code with discharge, deposit insurance, and a state that bails out
the largest actors are the same replenishment made institutional. Infinite
growth looks real to the actor whose ruined branches are replayed for free.

That actor can afford the safety norm: many small diversified positions,
fractional Kelly, never concentrate, wait. In the Dubins-Savage terms the
2026-08-27 note already carries, that is timid play, and timid play is optimal
only in a superfair game, one with a positive edge per unit staked. The norm
was then exported, through business schooling and financial advice, to people
with no replenishment at all, most of whose available bets are subfair after
costs. For a subfair arena with a barrier, the same mathematics says timid play
converges to certain ruin, and the goal-probability-maximizing policy is bold:
concentrate, and take the shot once. The safety norm is therefore not
conservative for the poor. It is the policy that guarantees the barrier is
reached, slowly. This is the owner's "biases business practice toward safety"
stated as a theorem about who has a bank.

The corpus's own correction of the same error, already ruled: the 2026-09-01
growth-policy note ("Poor people don't need to risk ruin." Kelly protects a
bankroll; we do not have one; split survival capital from a bounded,
re-seedable risk stake and size the stake hard). This note does not restate it;
it generalizes it.

## 3. From one bet to moves, strategies, and arenas

The house already has the objects. An **arena**
(`math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/arena.md`) is a finite
causal event structure with polarity, four-valued evidence, a site whose local
sections glue, and a settlement closure; a **position** is a configuration, an
occurred history; a **strategy** is a family of positions that is prefix-closed,
receptive to every lawful Opponent move, and deterministic on Player moves,
with maximal positions settled. Game-theoretic probability
(`game-theoretic-probability.md`) then says what capital is in such an arena: a
strategy is a capital process, and the protocol's one constraint is that the
skeptic's capital stays nonnegative. The barrier is not an add-on to the arena;
it is the protocol.

This note's reading, in those terms:

- **A move** is a Player extension of a position: an order, a cancel, a claim,
  a transfer, a forecast recorded. Opponent moves are fills, prices, resolutions,
  the tide.
- **A sleeve** (the term the meet-then-beat skill already uses for a bounded
  mandate with its own total-loss meaning) is an arena together with its own
  absorbing barrier and its own settlement: the risk stake of the 2026-09-01
  note is a sleeve whose barrier is its own zero, and whose zero is *not*
  absorbing for the arena above it.
- **The org's capital is a tree of sleeves**, and that tree is the proof tree
  the owner asked for: the root's barrier is survival capital, walled off; each
  child sleeve is a subproof whose failure discharges into its parent as a
  bounded loss, never as absorption. Bold or timid is decided per sleeve, by
  that sleeve's fairness sign and goal (Dubins-Savage), never once for the whole.
  Kelly is recovered exactly at a leaf with no barrier, no draw, and a
  positive measured edge: one bet repeated. Everything else in the tree is not
  Kelly and should not say Kelly.
- **Settlement is booking**: a fill is a row, a reconciliation is two witnesses
  agreeing (the accounting section's two-witness reading), and the booked value
  is capped at 2 while the value in circulation is √5
  (`Obsmetrologia/QuantumContextuality/KCBS.lean`; the 2026-09-04 nesting note).
  A sleeve's realized growth is booked; the tree's value is more than its
  booked sum, and the excess lives in the moves between sleeves.

The consequence for code: a sizing function takes an arena position (the edge
model, the sleeve's barrier, its draw schedule, its goal) and returns a move
and a regime, or a refusal datum naming what would be absorbed. That is the
`size-position` operation commissioned tonight in
`financial-engineering-services-section/services/position-sizing-service`,
and it is why the operation's input is a sleeve and not a bankroll.

## 4. The pool: the ensemble the poor can build

If the settler's advantage is a replenished ensemble, the question for a group
with no bank is whether an ensemble can be manufactured without one. It can,
partially, and the method is already in the corpus under another name.

N members each facing a time average, pooling bounded sleeves, face together
something closer to an ensemble: independent trajectories averaged at one
moment, with each member's ruin no longer absorbing because the pool re-seeds
it. This is Kropotkin's mutual aid (`biographies/pyotr-kropotkin.md`: cooperation
as an observed survival strategy, not altruism) stated in Peters's vocabulary,
and it is the beadworkers note's point that mutual aid is circulation value the
books cannot hold. Insurance is the captured, settler-booked version of the
same pool, priced to the insurer's ensemble and sold back to the insured's time
average at a margin; a group that runs its own pool keeps the margin and keeps
the √5.

Three constraints this note's reading puts on any pool the org runs:

1. **The pool is a sleeve too.** It has a barrier (the point below which it can
   no longer re-seed anyone) and that barrier is the group's real survival line,
   more so than any individual's. It is sized timid, because a pool of many
   small draws is the superfair case.
2. **Draws are priced on circulation, not on balance.** That is tally credit,
   the third instrument of the 2026-09-04 three-ways note: a member's line is a
   function of their matched crossings, and a default is a mismatch held in
   FOUR, not an eviction. Balance-secured credit is the bank's instrument;
   circulation-secured credit is the pool's.
3. **The pool is booked with two witnesses**, on the same ledger machinery as
   everything else (energy-bank already carries player-to-player transfer and
   pooling on a double-entry ledger; the main books carry the reconciliation
   reading). A pool whose books are not true is a bank with extra steps.

Incumbents, measured only by name tonight and to be measured properly under
the meet-then-beat skill: The Kitty App, Kitty Pot, Santander KiTTi, and
KttiPay all pool money for events and housemates onto a shared card; none holds
a risk sleeve, prices draws on circulation, or keeps a reconciled ledger of the
pool as a sleeve. The house gap is the sleeve tree and the two-witness books.

## 5. The rising barrier

The owner named the tide as relevant literally: climate breakdown raises the
water. In the arena reading, the absorbing barrier is not a constant. Survival
capital is a function of time and place that rises with hazard (a storm, a heat
month, an eviction wave), and a sizing rule that reads a fixed barrier is wrong
in the direction that kills. Every sleeve therefore carries its barrier as a
time series, not a number, and the pool's barrier rises fastest because the
pool is where correlated hazard lands (everyone's rent rises in the same
month). The public reading of that series, for the people the org serves, is
what the public economics face exists to publish: where the water is now, and
where it will be at the next spring tide.

## 6. What this asks of engineering, in the day's order

Books true, then books valued, then the capital systems (the Fintech Friday
term). For the people the group serves:

1. **A ledger each**, on the house reader (Farolfi over Beancount), with the
   two-witness reconciliation reading, so a member's own books are true before
   any sleeve is cut. A pool cannot re-seed what it cannot read.
2. **The sleeve split as an operation**: survival capital as a rising series,
   risk capital as a bounded stake, the refusal datum for any instrument with
   liability beyond the stake. Commissioned tonight as `size-position`.
3. **The pool as a sleeve on energy-bank**, with tally credit as its draw rule,
   booked into each member's ledger and the pool's own.
4. **Forecasts recorded and scored** (`forecasting-services-section`) for the
   decisions members actually face (a shift to take, a bill to defer), so
   calibration exists before any stake does; the second instrument of the
   three-ways note starts at zero cost.
5. **The venue loops** (Aagaard on Hive with real capital, Alpaca on paper, the
   internal-market peg ladder) as leaf sleeves of the tree, each with its own
   barrier and each booking fills as rows, so the tree can be read.

The size of today's balances gates none of this (owner-ruled 2026-09-04): the
tree is built so it holds when capital arrives, and a pool of small sleeves is
the one instrument that is larger for being shared.
