**Bazaar is the x402 v2 discovery extension and cataloging convention for payable HTTP resources and MCP tools; it is not one unique Coinbase-owned marketplace or a new payment primitive.** Any facilitator may implement discovery. Coinbase's public resource catalog was one facilitator-specific index, queryable without a CDP API key at the time of the 2026-08-27 observation. A merchant-scoped Coinbase endpoint has separately authenticated access. The x402 specification describes optional discovery endpoints, not a single universal or exhaustive marketplace.
Registration is gated on settlement, not on deployment: a seller opts a route in with `declareDiscoveryExtension()`, and per this estate's 2026-08-27 distribution record (cited source record `data/records/2026-08-27-agent-economy-distribution/x402-distribution-and-metered-offerings.md` is not in the tree; its figures are unrecovered, 2026-09-11) (a search-synthesis read of Coinbase's docs, not independently re-verified this pass), "the Facilitator automatically extracts metadata and registers it in the catalog **at the time of payment processing**" — meaning a correctly-implemented, opted-in endpoint that has never sold anything has also never triggered its own catalog listing, a distinction the [companion agent-to-agent-commerce note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/27/2026-08-27-note-on-agent-to-agent-commerce-and-machine-payable-apis.md) worked through in full for one live seller.

Read directly this pass from `docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/bazaar`: discovery is explicitly public ("You do not need a CDP API key to use the discovery APIs") and exposed through three methods — `searchX402Resources`, a natural-language query filterable by network, asset, payment scheme, payee address, URL, or maximum USD price, returning "at most 20 resources, ranked by a blend of query relevance and quality"; `listX402DiscoveryResources`, an offset-paginated browse of all active resources with an optional protocol-type filter; and `listX402DiscoveryMerchant`, which lists all active resources paying a given address (an unknown address returns empty results, rather than an error).
Search results carry three quality signals: call count and unique payer count over the trailing 30 days, and when the resource was last called — the same three figures this section's [calls-per-payer](./calls-per-payer.md) diagnostic is built from.

**Historical source-reported Coinbase catalog snapshot, measured by the 561 Group coordinator on 2026-08-27 (not re-observed in October)** via a complete read of `GET api.cdp.coinbase.com/platform/v2/x402/discovery/resources`: 14,841 resources total, 14,781 live and 60 dormant; 292,674 total calls and 42,316 unique payers over the trailing 30 days; live-resource prices in USDC running from a $0 minimum (only 2 free resources) through a $0.01 median to a $1,000 maximum, with the bulk of the catalog priced between $0.003 (p25) and $0.05 (p75); and an estimated $30,627 in total 30-day catalog revenue, of which a single Bitrefill gift-card payment rail — not a computation or data service — earns $21,000, and the top ten resources combined take 78%, leaving the remaining 14,771 live resources to split $6,836, about $0.46 each per month.
The catalog's highest-unique-payer resources are thin Ethereum JSON-RPC wrappers (ERC-20 balance, ENS resolution, wallet balance, block height, transaction-by-hash), each drawing roughly 1,000 unique payers and roughly 1,000 calls — a [calls-per-payer](./calls-per-payer.md) ratio near 1:1, diagnostic of broad one-off sampling rather than integration.
Full figures, including the demand-by-category breakdown, are carried in the source record at [`data/records/2026-08-27-x402-market-measurement/x402-market-observation.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-networking-engineering-services-section/services/x402-services-section/services/x402-market-observation-service/data/records/2026-08-28-x402-market-observation.json).

**The catalog is not the market.**
A separate 2026-08-27 source-reported Base USDC EIP-3009 event-rate extrapolation counted approximately 3.28 million authorizations in a 30-day estimate. The historical Bazaar figure of 292,674 reported calls divided by that unrelated extrapolation is about 9%; this is **not a protocol market-share or true visibility percentage**. Calls versus authorizations need not be the same unit, and EIP-3009 is used by non-x402 systems. The original [Bazaar-versus-settlement note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/27/2026-08-27-x402-bazaar-undercounts-settlement-by-an-order-of-magnitude.md) has been corrected in place, including its reversed conditional inequality: only if paired evidence establishes B <= X <= E for the **same** chain, unit and period, B/E is a **lower**, not upper, bound on B/X.

**Economic identity and machine behavior.** A Bazaar record describes *discovery of a seller-authored resource declaration*, not independent approval of rights, operator identity, current payee, fulfilled delivery, actual demand, equilibrium or settled commerce. The x402 v2 bazaar extension lives at PaymentRequired.extensions.bazaar and distinguishes HTTP method/request specification from MCP tool name/input; the facilitator may accept, process asynchronously or reject indexing. The payable resource and current quote remain with the seller, not with a directory. An agent should retain method, source resource URL, offer/SKU identity, declared input/output, discovery source/time, and independent verification statuses.
