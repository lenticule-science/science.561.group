# What Chinese firms going abroad buy from US residents

This note maps a market: the services that Chinese companies selling into the
United States pay US-side people and firms to perform. It was gathered on
2026-09-26 by web search across Chinese- and English-language sources. Figures
marked **(search synthesis)** come from vendor pages, trade blogs and
aggregators rather than a primary source, and are good for sizing and
pricing a first offer, not for quoting to a client as fact. The law these
services operate under is in the companion law note,
[US law for doing business with and for Chinese firms](../../../../../../../../../../../law-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-us-law-for-doing-business-with-and-for-chinese-firms.md).

## 1. The wave: 出海

[出海 (chūhǎi, "going out to sea")](../../../../../../glossary/terms/chuhai.md) is the Chinese business
term for a firm taking its products, brand or operations abroad. It names a
policy as well as a trend: the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology
opened a special service action for small and medium firms going abroad in
January 2025, more than twenty provinces and cities have their own support
programs, and the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026–2030) lists going-global support
for small firms among its priorities ([Beijing Review, 2026-09-21](http://www.bjreview.com/Latest_Headlines/202609/t20260921_800447225.html)).

The US is a shrinking share of that wave but still a very large one. China's
2025 export growth was led by electric vehicles, industrial robots and lithium
batteries, and the US share of Chinese exports fell to 11.1% while Belt and
Road markets took over half ([CBNData, 2026](https://www.cbndata.com/information/295038)).
The sectors that keep pushing into the US are cross-border e-commerce
(apparel, home goods, beauty), consumer electronics, apps, games and short
video drama, and AI applications and agents. Electric vehicles, solar cells
and batteries face the highest US barriers (Section 301 duties of 100%, 50%
and 25%+) and are the sectors least likely to need a US seller.

## 2. Why every seller now needs a US-side person

Three changes between May 2025 and July 2026 moved Chinese sellers from
shipping parcels straight to US buyers to holding stock, entities and people
in the US:

- **De minimis ended.** Duty-free entry for parcels under $800 ended for
  Chinese and Hong Kong goods on 2025-05-02 and for every country on
  2025-08-29, and CBP made the suspension indefinite in June 2026 (see the law
  note). Every parcel now needs a customs entry and pays duty.
- **China pays sellers to warehouse abroad.** Since 2025-01-27, China's tax
  authority refunds export VAT when goods leave for an
  [overseas warehouse](../../../../../../glossary/terms/overseas-warehouse.md) (海外仓), not only when they
  reach a buyer ([chukou1, 2025](https://www.chukou1.com/News/NewsDetails.aspx?id=1840)).
- **The platforms went local.** Temu began steering US shoppers to
  locally warehoused products in February 2025, stopped shipping China-to-US
  parcels in May 2025, and moved US sales to a Local Seller Program that
  fulfills from US warehouses through named third-party logistics firms
  (WINIT, Lecangs, GoodCang, ShipSage and others)
  ([CNBC, 2025-02-05](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/05/temu-steers-users-to-local-products-after-trump-ends-de-minimis.html);
  [Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/temu-walks-back-price-increases/747126/);
  [Chinese Sellers](https://chinesellers.substack.com/p/temu-adapts-with-local-us-fulfillment)).
  Shein opened US distribution centers in Illinois, California and Indiana
  ([CNN, 2025-08-03](https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/03/business/trump-suspends-duty-free-shipments-temu-shein)).

The result is what sellers call local-to-local selling: the goods, the
[importer of record](../../../../../../glossary/terms/importer-of-record.md), the returns address and increasingly the
company are in the US, and a Chinese owner needs people there to hold those
roles. One aggregator reports Chinese-owned US LLC registrations for Amazon
sellers up about 400% in a year (search synthesis; [MyWifeQuitHerJob](https://mywifequitherjob.com/chinese-amazon-sellers-us-llc/)).

## 3. What they pay for, and what it costs

### Roles US law requires a US-located person to hold

- **FDA US agent.** Every foreign food facility, and every foreign medical
  device establishment, registering with the FDA must name a
  [US agent](../../../../../../../../../../../law-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fda-us-agent.md) with a US street address, reachable in US business
  hours. The FDA charges nothing; private agents charge **$250–300 a year** at
  the low end and **$800–1,500** for staffed firms (search synthesis;
  [FDA Basics](https://www.fdabasics.com/our-faq/much-us-agent-fees/)). The
  FDA's own open data lists 42,240 device-listing records for establishments in
  mainland China as of 2026-09-14, each naming its US agent
  ([openFDA device registration](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/registrationlisting/)).
  Many of those agents are one-person LLCs; the first record read names a
  Dover, Delaware LLC with a Hotmail address. Device registration renews
  every year between October 1 and December 31.
- **FCC responsible party.** Radio and digital devices authorized by the
  Supplier's Declaration of Conformity must have a US-located responsible
  party (the importer, for imported goods) who can produce test records on
  request. Full FCC certification testing for a radio product runs
  **$3,000–5,000** (search synthesis; [Compliance Testing](https://compliancetesting.com/fcc-testing-fcc-certification-china/)).
- **CPSC certifier.** The US importer, not the Chinese factory, issues the
  certificate of compliance for a regulated consumer product and, since
  2026-07-08, files it electronically at entry. Toy test sets run
  **$380–500** (search synthesis; [AQI](https://aqiservice.com/what-is-cpc-certification/)).

### Entities, importing and warehousing

- **US company formation and registered agent**, sold to Chinese owners:
  registered-agent service **$49–300 a year**, a formed LLC with its first year
  of agent service about **$224** (search synthesis; [EntityBrief](https://entitybrief.com/llc/registered-agent-cost/)).
  The named foreign-seller formation shops advertise support in several
  languages, often without Mandarin ([AMZ LLC Prep](https://amzllcprep.com/llc-formation/)).
- **Importing.** Customs brokers charge **$90–450 an entry**, security filings
  **$35–75**, continuous bonds from **$400 a year**, and a small importer
  budgets **$2,500–5,000 a year** (search synthesis;
  [USA Customs Clearance](https://usacustomsclearance.com/process/how-much-does-a-customs-broker-cost/)).
  Filing entries for someone else requires a customs broker license; being the
  importer for one's own account does not (law note, §3).
- **Overseas warehouses and 3PLs.** Chinese sellers price 海外仓 service as
  inbound, measurement, outbound, sortation, storage and last-mile fees
  ([Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/678327501)). US pick-and-pack runs
  **$2–3 an order** at volume and **$8–15** under 200 orders a month, storage
  **$18–25 a pallet a month**, and 3PL monthly minimums rose to **$500+**
  (search synthesis; [Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/3pl-pricing)).

### Selling

- **Marketplace management.** Agencies running TikTok Shop or Amazon for a
  brand charge **$3,000–10,000 a month** or **10–30% of sales**, on three- to
  six-month minimums (search synthesis; [MediaLabs](https://medialabs-co.com/tiktok-shop-agency-cost)).
  Amazon lists vetted service providers, including 180+ China-based ones, in
  its [Service Provider Network](https://sell.amazon.com/tools/service-provider-network);
  TikTok Shop onboards agencies through its [Partner Center](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/650411a75a12ff0294023d30).
- **Product liability insurance.** From 2026-11-02 Amazon requires $1M
  coverage in children's products, cosmetics, ingestibles and lithium
  batteries regardless of sales, and mainland-China sellers must buy it through
  Amazon's own Insurance Accelerator ([EcommerceBytes, 2026-09-10](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2026/09/10/amazon-updates-insurance-requirements-for-sellers/)).
  Brokering it needs a state insurance producer license.
- **Commission sales representation.** An independent
  [manufacturer's representative](../../../../../../glossary/terms/manufacturers-representative.md) sells a maker's product in a
  territory for a commission and no salary: **5–15%** of the closed sale in
  general, **5–12%** for industrial equipment, **10–20%** for software, higher
  for infrequent large sales (search synthesis; [Kennect](https://www.kennect.io/blogs/typical-sales-commission-rates)).
  The rate is negotiated over product, customer type and territory, and split
  when several reps touch one deal ([MANA](https://www.manaonline.org/news/coming-to-agreement-on-split-commissions)).
  This is [brokerage](../../../../../../glossary/terms/brokerage.md) in the house's sense: paid on the
  match, never owning the goods.
- **English customer service.** US-based agents bill **$36–50 an hour**
  against $9–17 offshore; what a US team sells is the time zone and the
  native voice (search synthesis; [CX Harbor](https://cxharbor.com/insights/ecommerce-customer-service-outsourcing-cost/)).
- **Trade-show presence.** Convention staff bill about **$35–40+ an hour**, and
  an exhibitor's total show cost runs **$20,000–50,000** (search synthesis;
  [XS Staffing](https://www.xsstaffing.com/post/trade-show-staffing-cost-las-vegas)).
  A US team that staffs a Chinese exhibitor's booth saves it flying a crew.
- **US business development for Chinese AI and software firms.** The largest
  firms hire directly in the US ([SCMP, 2026](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3344212/chinas-tech-giants-pursue-ai-semiconductor-talent-us-competition-intensifies));
  the hundreds of funded smaller ones cannot open a US office and buy a
  contracted US voice instead.

### Short drama

Chinese-owned apps built a large US business in
[micro-drama](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/micro-drama.md): vertical episodes of one to two minutes,
sold per episode, written to web-novel formulas and shot in the US with
American casts. ReelShort (Crazy Maple Studio) and DramaBox are the largest
([Entertain Lens](https://entertainlens.com/chinese-short-drama-apps-us-market/)).
DramaBox opened a writer incubator with Stage 32 in December 2025 because, in
its words, 30 to 40 vertical series shoot in Los Angeles every month and each
needs a script; its competition's grand prize is a $5,000 writing contract
([Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/dramabox-seeks-new-u-s-writers-stage32-1236436618/);
[Stage 32](https://www.stage32.com/happy-writers/contests/Stage-32-%20-Drama-Box-Screenwriting-Competition)).
Nonunion leads earn about **$500–1,500 a day** (search synthesis;
[Stageblind](https://stageblind.com/micro-drama-actor-pay)). The platforms pay
US writers, actors and producers directly; a staffing firm that supplies them
talent takes a placement fee.

## 4. The reverse trades

**Buying from China.** Sourcing agents charge **3–10%** of the order (about
10% under $10,000, 3–5% above $100,000) or **$100–500** flat for small orders;
inspection firms charge **$250–310 per inspector-day**; Alibaba's Trade
Assurance escrow costs the supplier about 1–2% (search synthesis;
[SourcingXpro](https://sourcingxpro.com/china-sourcing-agent-fee-commission-fee/);
[QIMA](https://www.qima.com/consumer-products/quality-control/quality-control-in-china)).
Typical minimum orders are 50–500 units. Landed cost now carries base duty,
the China Section 301 list duty (7.5–100% by list) and, since 2026-07-24, a
Section 301 forced-labor duty of up to 12.5% (law note, §1).

**Selling into China.** US supplements, cosmetics, packaged foods and baby
goods sell into China through cross-border import platforms (Tmall Global, JD
Worldwide, Douyin) without a Chinese entity (search synthesis;
[Lengow](https://www.lengow.com/get-to-know-more/top-marketplaces-in-china/)).
The Upper Midwest's own case is Wisconsin ginseng: more than 90% of US
ginseng, mostly sold to China and Hong Kong, now under about a 33% Chinese
tariff, with growers holding much of their 2024 crop and Canada winning share
on price ([WXPR, 2026-09-02](https://www.wxpr.org/food-and-agriculture/2026-09-02/tariffs-and-trade-wars-impacting-wisconsin-ginseng-growers)).
Minnesota exported $1.5 billion of goods to China in 2025, its third-largest
market, mostly soybeans, corn, feed and pork ([USTR](https://ustr.gov/map/state-benefits/mn)).

## 5. Machine-to-machine trade

Chinese AI labs already sell to US software at volume: by July 2026 Chinese
models (DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot's Kimi, Z.ai, MiniMax and others) held six of
the top ten places on OpenRouter ([Foreign Policy, 2026-07-23](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/07/23/china-ai-race-moonshot-kimi-k3-alibaba-qwen-deepseek-openai-anthropic/)).
On 2026-04-02 Ant Group's blockchain arm launched Anvita, whose Anvita Flow
lets AI agents register, coordinate and settle with each other in USDC over the
[x402](../../../../../../glossary/terms/x402.md) protocol ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/02/ant-group-s-blockchain-arm-unveils-platform-for-ai-agents-to-transact-on-crypto-rails)).
Hong Kong's Stablecoins Ordinance took effect 2025-08-01 and the HKMA granted
its first licenses on 2026-04-10 ([Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/blog/hong-kong-stablecoin-regulation/)).
An x402-priced API is therefore a product that Chinese agents can already
buy, and a Chinese agent platform's API is one that US agents can already buy.

## 6. Where the deals are found

- **Rep marketplaces**: RepHunter, CommissionCrowd, Commission People and
  RepResearch match manufacturers with commission reps
  ([RepHunter](https://www.rephunter.net/)).
- **Platform directories**: Amazon's Service Provider Network and TikTok Shop's
  Partner Center (above). Xiaohongshu's e-commerce going-global program began
  with the US and recruits overseas creator agencies
  ([Sina Finance, 2025-03-10](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-03-10/doc-inepeazk0703318.shtml)).
- **Chinese hiring sites**: Liepin lists thousands of 海外销售 (overseas sales)
  roles, and Zhihu carries posts hiring Americas sales leads, some on
  commission only ([Liepin](https://www.liepin.com/zphaiwaixiaoshou/)).
- **Chambers and state offices**: AmCham China, the US-China Business
  Council, the Chinese American Chamber of Commerce of Minnesota
  ([CACC-MN](https://caccmn.org/)), the Midwest USA Chinese Chamber of
  Commerce, and the Minnesota Trade Office, which has a regional trade manager
  covering China and runs STEP export grants of up to $7,500 on a 50% match
  ([DEED](https://mn.gov/deed/business/exporting/export-financing)).
- **Trade fairs**: the 140th Canton Fair runs in three phases from 2026-10-15
  to 2026-11-04; the Global Sources Hong Kong shows run 2026-10-11 to 10-21;
  CES 2027 is 2027-01-06 to 01-09 in Las Vegas
  ([Canton Fair](https://www.cantonfair.org.cn/en-US);
  [Global Sources](https://www.globalsources.com/trade-fair/hk-show/about);
  [CES](https://www.ces.tech/)). Exhibitor lists published before each show
  are call lists for representation and booth staffing.
- **Public registrations**: openFDA's device registration data (above) names
  every Chinese device establishment and the US agent it pays.

## 7. Rendanheyi in the US

The house's own organization theory comes from Haier (see the Rendanheyi
glossary in organization science). Haier bought GE Appliances in 2016 for $5.4
billion and ran Rendanheyi there as "end-to-end", with 100+ micro-enterprises,
some over 2,000 people ([Corporate Rebels](https://www.corporate-rebels.com/blog/rendanheyi-in-usa)).
GE Appliances announced a $3 billion US manufacturing expansion on 2025-08-13
([Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20250813577880/en/GE-Appliances-Announces-Historic-%243-Billion-Investment-to-Expand-U.S.-Manufacturing)).
The Haier Model Institute runs research partnerships in Europe, Singapore,
India and the US; in the West, Rendanheyi is taught by one boutique
(Corporate Rebels) and in business-school cases. No firm sells Rendanheyi
run by software, or Rendanheyi for an agent workforce; the 2026-08-22
field-atlas note found the latter unclaimed.
