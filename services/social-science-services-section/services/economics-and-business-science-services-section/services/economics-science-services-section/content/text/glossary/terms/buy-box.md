A buy box is a buyer's standing description of what they will buy: the areas or neighborhoods, the size and kind of property, the top price, whether they close in cash and how quickly, and how they want to be sent a deal.
In [search-and-matching](./search-and-matching.md) terms it is one side of the match stated in advance, so an intermediary can filter the other side (the sellers) down to candidates that already have a buyer before spending any effort on them.

In [contract assignment](./contract-assignment.md) the buy box does two jobs.
It chooses which owners are worth calling, and it sets the price: the most the intermediary can offer a seller is the buyer's top price less the fee it needs, so the fee is known before the contract is signed.
A property-resale business that records buy boxes as data can match every parcel in a county against every buyer at once and hand a caller only matched leads.
