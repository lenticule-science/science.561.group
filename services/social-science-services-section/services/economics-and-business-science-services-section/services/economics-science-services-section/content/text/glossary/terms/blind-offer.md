A blind offer is an offer to buy a specific property at a specific price, sent to its owner by mail before any conversation.
Land investors price blind offers from comparable sales, commonly in a range of 20% to 50% of market value, and send them in volume to owners selected from county records (search synthesis: Pebble REI, REtipster, Land Academy).
Fewer owners answer than answer a [neutral letter](./neutral-letter.md), typically 1% to 2%, but an owner who answers a blind offer has already seen the number, so more of those conversations end in a signed agreement.

The blind offer screens sellers by price the way a [buy box](./buy-box.md) screens properties by fit: it moves part of the negotiation into the mailing itself.
