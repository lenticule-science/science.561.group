An absentee owner holds property they do not live on or near.
County tax records identify one by comparing the owner's mailing address with the parcel's location (another county, another state) and, where the record carries it, by a homestead flag of "No".

Absentee ownership is one of the logistical motives the 561 Group's brokerage research names as a reason a seller trades price for speed and certainty without being in distress: an heir or long-time holder who will never use a lot across the country pays its taxes every year and has no local way to sell it (see the [note on brokerage as a market mechanism](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/08/28/2026-08-28-note-on-brokerage-as-a-market-mechanism.md)).
The land business's seller score adds points for owners out of state and outside the city for that reason.
See the [note on the Minnesota property-resale market](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-the-minnesota-property-resale-market.md).
