## Why this is central

Korowicz supplies the house's citation for [financial cascade](../../../glossary/terms/financial-cascade.md): the argument that a severe financial shock could propagate through globalized just-in-time supply chains and trade finance and then feed back into the financial system, producing a cascade the 2008 liquidity-backstop response would not be a reliable guide to arresting, because 2008 did not sever the physical logistics links a worse event might.

## What it does not settle

Korowicz's cascade has not occurred as modelled: the report is a structural, scenario-based argument about coupling and fragility rather than a forecast with a specified probability or timeline, and no financial shock since 2012 (including 2020's pandemic-driven supply disruption) has produced the full self-reinforcing financial/physical cascade the report describes, though 2020 did demonstrate real, if less severe, cross-contagion between the two systems. The report is grey literature — a foundation-published study, not a peer-reviewed journal article — and its specific claims about the fragility of trade finance and just-in-time inventory have not been independently re-derived by the house from primary trade-finance data.
