# Zero-Price Economics Science Services Section

The science of zero-price economics as it applies to AI inference: why providers structure literal free tiers the way they do, what those tiers' own terms actually say, and how their quotas are rationed. It is the science root of the 561 Group's No Buy AI enterprise program and the seat of its Lenticule Science division, **Diaphragm Research** (decided 2026-09-05 under the decision law; `package.json` here is the brand package), seated beneath [economics-science-services-section](../../README.md) alongside its [participation-economy-science-services-section](../participation-economy-science-services-section/README.md) sibling.

## Why this is its own division and not Condenser Research

The platform's participation-economy science (Condenser Research) studies earning without buying in general — attention economy, digital labor, crypto faucets and earn programs. No Buy AI's own literature is a different question: not what a person earns, but why a provider gives inference away for free and on what rationed terms. The glossary already seats the founding fact one level up: `zero-price-effect` (Condenser Research, `content/text/glossary/terms/zero-price-effect.md`), the finding that pricing a good at exactly zero shifts demand out of proportion to an equivalent discount at any positive price [@shampaniermazarariely2007]. Diaphragm Research studies that instrument as providers actually deploy it in AI inference: the [2026-09-05 broad-surface measurement](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-economic-operations-services-section/services/561-group-no-buy-services-section/data/records/2026-09-05-no-buy-broad-surface-measurement.json)'s free-ai and free-compute rows (Gemini's billing-account gate, Copilot Free's 2,000-completion monthly allowance, HF ZeroGPU's tiered daily GPU quotas by account status, Oracle's card-required Always Free tier, Qwen Code's discontinued built-in quota) are exactly the provider-terms and quota-rationing literature no existing science seat reads as its own subject. An optics instrument that regulates exactly how much passes through a system is a diaphragm, the aperture stop that gates a lens's light the way a provider's quota gates a person's requests — named and conflict-scanned in [2026-09-05-note-on-naming-the-no-buy-enterprise-divisions.md](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-economic-operations-services-section/services/561-group-no-buy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/05/2026-09-05-note-on-naming-the-no-buy-enterprise-divisions.md).

## What Diaphragm Research studies

- **The zero-price effect applied to inference**, citing the founding literature already seated in Condenser Research rather than re-deriving it.
- **Provider terms**: billing-account requirements, data-use and training terms (Gemini's free tier trains on and may human-review prompts; its paid tier does not), and CORS/redistribution terms (OpenRouter's prohibition on one shared key routing many people), each cited to the provider's own page with the date it was read.
- **Free-tier quota economics**: caps, reset windows, and eligibility tiers across the measured free-ai and free-compute arenas, and the ai-training-work arenas (Prolific, Toloka, DataAnnotation, Remotasks) as a distinct, adjacent kind of "free" labor market this division holds in view without treating as a No Buy AI surface.

Its notes, glossary, and bibliography seat under `content/` here.
