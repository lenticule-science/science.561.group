## Why this is central

Albert and Hahnel's "participatory economics" (parecon) is the whole-economy planning alternative most likely to be confused with "participation economy" by name alone, so this note cites it to rule that confusion out explicitly. Parecon is a proposed replacement for both market capitalism and central planning: worker and consumer councils democratically negotiate a decentralized annual plan, balanced job complexes redistribute empowering and disempowering work, and remuneration follows effort and sacrifice rather than output or property ownership. Nothing in this note's field — earning sats from a poll, routing a free AI request, claiming a Hive account — is parecon; parecon is a macroeconomic planning system, not a way an individual earns anything without spending money.

## What it does not settle

Parecon is a normative institutional proposal, not an empirical account of any existing earning surface, platform, or crypto program; it has never been implemented at any economy-wide scale, and this note draws nothing substantive from it beyond the name collision it forecloses.
