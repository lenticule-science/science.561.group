# Five economies of earning

*The American economy pays a person through five distinct mechanisms, observed to coexist rather than to have replaced one another.*

The founder's own framing (2026-09-05, verbatim): "participation economy, gig economy, wage economy, contract economy, salary economy, are five different economies I believe the American economy to have... so we can have participation economy and American economy next to each other, and then an American participation economy." The wage economy pays for hours worked at an hourly rate. The salary economy pays a fixed period, typically annual, regardless of hours-in-task; labor economics treats it as a payment-schedule variant of the same earnings model as the wage economy rather than a separate field, so both are carried by one literature — Mincer's human-capital earnings function is the anchor result for how schooling and experience determine both. The contract economy pays an agreed price for a specified deliverable, the relationship ending at delivery; it is carried by the economics of contracting, from Coase's 1937 transaction-cost theory of why firms exist rather than contracting every task on the open market through Grossman and Hart's 1986 incomplete-contracts theory of ownership and control, the line of work that won the 2016 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences. The gig economy pays piece-by-piece for a platform-dispatched task with no ongoing employment relationship; it is carried by Schor's and Sundararajan's sharing/gig-economy literature and, in depth, by the house's own gig-work science section.

The [participation economy](../terms/participation-economy.md) is the fifth: it pays for the act of participating itself — attention, engagement, play, movement, learning, curation, resource-sharing, quests, or training data — in sats, tokens, credits, or inference rather than dollars, and it is carried by no existing literature. It is the house's own founded term, ruled by the founder on 2026-09-05 and grounded in full in [the note on earning without buying: the field and its literature](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/05/2026-09-05-note-on-earning-without-buying-the-field-and-its-literature.md), whose §13 and §14 state the definition and this five-economy table as house coinage, never as a literature's finding. The point of holding all five side by side is structural, not competitive: they are five distinct mechanisms by which the American economy pays a person, and "American participation economy" — the founder's own compound — names the scoped claim that the participation economy is one of several the American economy holds, not the only one and not a successor to the other four.

The five names are five bundles of settings on three axes, not five values of one axis, and the axes are what an optimization runs over:

| Economy | What is paid for | Who dispatches the work | What it is paid in |
|---|---|---|---|
| wage | hours | an employer | dollars |
| salary | a period | an employer | dollars |
| contract | an agreed deliverable | a client | dollars |
| gig | a platform-dispatched piece | a platform | dollars |
| participation | the act of participating | nobody, or a platform | the platform's own unit |

Wage and salary differ only on the first axis; contract and gig differ only on the second; participation differs on the third, and it is the only one of the five where what is paid is not bearer money, which is what ties it to [money science](../../../../../../../monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/money-science.md): the payout stays witnessed. The polity fixes the legal value of each axis, and that is what "American" adds to the compound: United States employment and tax law draw the line between the wage-and-salary pair and the contract-and-gig pair, and money-transmitter and tax-reporting rules draw the line around participation payouts; another polity moves the lines and the same five names denote slightly different bundles. The method for studying any one of the five resource by resource is the founder's own [reading of Hive as an attention-stake economy](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/hive-as-an-attention-stake-economy-capital-yield-labor-governance-and-liquidity.md), whose sections (tokenomics, what is actually rewarded, forms of capital, commodity forms, passive and recurring income, scalable and extractive strategies, liquidity, worked examples) are the template the participation economy's study follows over every currency, credit, commodity, and scarce input it holds.
