A no-buy names a bounded period or practice of acquiring something without paying for it. The personal-finance movement that popularized the term (a "no-buy year" or "no-buy challenge", with active Reddit and TikTok communities since roughly 2018) uses it in the abstaining sense: committing to spend nothing outside necessities for a set period. This program uses the same word for a second, related sense: not merely abstaining from a purchase, but actively routing a real need (crypto, AI inference, capital) through a channel that does not require payment at all — an earning surface, a free tier, or a reward program — rather than either buying the thing or going without it.

A no-buy is distinguished from ordinary frugality or bargain-hunting by the presence of an actual zero-cost channel that supplies the resource, not merely a cheaper one; buying the cheapest available plan is not a no-buy, because money still changes hands. It is also distinguished from a subsidy or a gift: a no-buy channel's zero cost is a structural feature of the channel itself (an attention-economy reward, a zero-price free tier, a protocol-native faucet), not a one-time favor from another person.

The engineering consequence is that a catalog of no-buy channels must record, per channel, whether any purchase is required at all and, separately, what identity a person must disclose to use it — two independent facts, since a channel can require no money and still require a government ID, or require no identity and still gate on spending through a linked card.
