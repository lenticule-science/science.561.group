*First Monday* 2(4), 1997-04-07.

## Why this is central

This is the founding statement of "attention economy" as a name for a whole economic order, not a business metaphor: Goldhaber argues that in a network saturated with information, attention itself — not material wealth or labor — becomes the scarce, allocated resource that structures production and status. It predates and is the term the later business literature (Davenport and Beck 2001; Wu 2016) and the platform-reward surfaces this note measures (polls, ads, streaks paying small amounts of attention) sit downstream of. No Buy's earn-by-attention arena (ZBD polls and ads, Brave Rewards) is, in Goldhaber's terms, a direct monetization of allocated attention.

## What it does not settle

Goldhaber writes as a former theoretical physicist making a structural argument about a coming economy, not an empirical study of any platform; it names no rate, no platform, no labor-hour figure, and predates crypto-native and app-based micro-reward surfaces by two decades. It also does not address the zero-price effect (Shampanier, Mazar and Ariely 2007) or the labor-process critique of platform "engagement" (Gray and Suri 2019; Casilli 2019) — those are separate literatures this note holds alongside it, not derivations of it.
