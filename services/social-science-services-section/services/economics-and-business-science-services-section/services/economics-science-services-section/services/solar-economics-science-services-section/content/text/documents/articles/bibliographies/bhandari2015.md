## Why this is central

This is the house's citation for the pre-harmonization literature base the later EROI disputes (Prieto and Hall's low real-world figure, Murphy et al.'s harmonized "at or above ten" figure) both draw on. Bhandari, Collier, Ellingson, and Apul systematically screened 232 candidate papers on PV energy payback time and EROI published 2000-2013, across crystalline-silicon and thin-film technologies, and found only 11 methodologically transparent enough to pass screening for EPBT/EROI specifically (23 for embedded-energy analysis). The house reads this small surviving count as evidence that the field's disagreements are not a matter of a few outlier studies against a broad consensus, but of a genuinely thin, methodologically heterogeneous base that later harmonization efforts are built on top of.

## What it does not settle

The review's screening cutoff (2013) predates both Prieto and Hall's real-world Spain study's later public defenses and the 2019-2022 storage-inclusive and harmonization literature; it says nothing about battery storage. Its meta-analysis reports EPBT/EROI ranges across technologies rather than a single reconciled figure, and does not itself adjudicate the boundary disputes (point-of-extraction versus point-of-use; which system components are included) that later papers make explicit.
