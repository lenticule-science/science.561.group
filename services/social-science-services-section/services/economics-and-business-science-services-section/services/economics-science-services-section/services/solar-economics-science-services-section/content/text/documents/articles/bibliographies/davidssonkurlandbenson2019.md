## Why this is central

This is the house's citation for the residential-scale (rather than utility-scale) companion to Raugei, Leccisi, and Fthenakis's 2020 utility-scale study: it quantifies how adding a lithium-ion battery affects the energetic performance of a typical residential photovoltaic system across a wide range of climatic conditions, which is the scale most relevant to a single household's own solar-plus-storage decision, the scale Soft Dyson's own household instruments are meant to serve.
