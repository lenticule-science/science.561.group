Soft Dyson is the 561 Group's endeavor to be the interface between humans and solar energy: the layer through which a household, a band, or an enterprise reads, prices, hedges, claims, and settles the solar energy it uses and generates, taking a share of every such transaction as a witness fee priced at cost plus the cost of the service around it, never as a spread. The founder named it on 2026-09-05 by analogy to Amazon's aim of a share of every transaction in the economy, and the house's own position that energy is the substrate of the economy makes the analogy exact: under energy descent every transaction is at base a transaction in captured sunlight, so the interface to solar energy is the interface to the economy.

A Dyson sphere is the hypothetical shell that captures a star's whole output. Soft names three departures from it. First, the protected set is not empty: in the house's mathematics of coordination a system built to converge on total capture has chosen an empty protected set and will discharge every border as damage, while the whirling dervish, the fivefold defect that cannot tile the lattice, is the protected defect that keeps the whole from crystallizing into heat death; a soft sphere certifies a household's refusal as a border and never optimizes it against its will. Second, it is a mesh, not a shell: each household is its own settlement with its own law, ledger, and contracts, joined to the others by certificate exchange, with no seizable apex. Third, it is software: the house builds no panel, inverter, or wire, only the witness layer over the energy people already own.

The endeavor is indefinite and aim-open in the work-science sense, so it is seated in a services section and pursued as projects, and it stands on the house's science of collapse, energy return on energy invested, and the distal bypass, with its instruments, the joule-denominated barrier, the consumption hedge, the claims-decay register, the option register, and joules per settled row, inherited from the finance program's energy-descent reading rather than invented for it.
