# Economics Science Services Section

The Economics Science Services Section maintains economics research under OECD FORD 5.2.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized subdomains continue under `services/`.

Founded 2026-09-05 from the No Buy program carrier, [participation-economy-science-services-section](services/participation-economy-science-services-section/README.md) studies earning without buying.

Founded 2026-09-05 from the Soft Dyson program carrier, [solar-economics-science-services-section](services/solar-economics-science-services-section/README.md) studies solar energy as an economy.

`territorial-economy-science-services-section` holds the science of territorial economies, founded 2026-09-05 from the program carrier in `561-group-territorial-economy-services-section`.

Founded 2026-09-05 from the No Buy AI program carrier, [zero-price-economics-science-services-section](services/zero-price-economics-science-services-section/README.md) studies zero-price economics as it applies to AI inference.

`lib/obsmetrologia-economics` is the Lean package `ObsmetrologiaEconomics`: attention allocation, the allocation solver and currency cocones, formalized over the pure-mathematics package Obsmetrologia, which it imports.
