person invests with help, as distinct from the finance section's science
of booking value onto positions and sizing under an absorbing barrier,
which this section consumes. Founded 2026-09-05 from the program carrier,
[561 Group Investment Services Section](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-economic-operations-services-section/services/561-group-business-operations-services-section/services/561-group-finance-operations-services-section/services/561-group-investment-services-section/README.md),

## What it holds

- [The personal quant platform](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/05/2026-09-05-note-on-the-personal-quant-platform.md),
  the founder's thesis that Alpaca Assist stands on: a person's beliefs
  are first-class data; observation, belief, forecast, valuation, policy,
  and order intent are six distinct typed records; the belief-to-capital
  algebra. It lived as a stray file in the Alpaca engineering section
  until 2026-09-05; theory seats in science.
- The field read of retail investing assistants, the regulatory line on
  what counts as investment advice, and the behavioral-finance literature
  on retail investors' beliefs and trading, each figure cited to its
  first-party source with its date, written by the research-analyst office
  the same day (`content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/05/`).
