A **pooled carbon token** is a fungible claim on a basket of tokenized carbon credits. A holder of an eligible credit token deposits it into a pool contract and receives pool tokens one-for-one per tonne; the pool applies an eligibility filter (vintage start date, registry standard, region, methodology, allow and deny lists). Because any eligible credit is worth one pool token regardless of its project or vintage, the pool token has one price while the credits behind it do not.

Where a pool lets the redeemer name which credit to take out, depositors have reason to deposit the cheapest eligible credits and redeemers to take the most valuable, so the pool's contents drift toward the low end of the eligible range unless fees or filters correct it. Pool filters and lists are controlled by the pool owner, so eligibility is a governance decision, not a property of the tonne.

The token measures tonnes admitted, not outcomes verified, and its value follows the market's view of the weakest eligible credit.
