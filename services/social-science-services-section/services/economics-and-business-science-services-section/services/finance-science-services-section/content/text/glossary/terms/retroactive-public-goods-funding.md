**Retroactive public goods funding** rewards work whose impact is already observable instead of paying for proposals. Optimism's Retro Funding is the deployed instance: early rounds had badgeholders vote on projects, later rounds used metrics and, from Season 7, monthly payouts computed by open evaluation algorithms ([Optimism Retro Lessons 2024](https://gov.optimism.io/t/lessons-learned-from-two-years-of-retroactive-public-goods-funding/9239)).

The mechanism needs an impact measure and a source of funds; it does not itself supply either. The program's own reviewers report that there is no data yet showing retroactive funding produces better outcomes than other allocation.

See [quadratic funding](../../../../../economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/quadratic-funding.md) for the contribution-weighted alternative.
