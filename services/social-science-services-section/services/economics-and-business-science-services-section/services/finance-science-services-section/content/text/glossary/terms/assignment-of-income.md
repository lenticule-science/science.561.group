aliases:
  - "anticipatory assignment of income"
description: "The federal tax rule that income is taxed to the person who owns the income-producing asset or performs the services, even if that person directs the income to another before receiving it (Lucas v. Earl; Helvering v. Horst)."
references: {}
---

**Assignment of income** is the principle of Lucas v. Earl, 281 U.S. 111 (1930), that the tax cannot be escaped by "anticipatory arrangements and contracts however skilfully devised", and of Helvering v. Horst, 311 U.S. 112 (1940), that "the power to dispose of income is the equivalent of ownership of it" so that directing it to another is "the enjoyment and hence the realization of the income by him who exercises it."

A member who owns the asset earning yield and directs the yield to a cooperative therefore realizes it. A cooperative covered by Subchapter T may deduct patronage dividends it pays under an obligation that existed before it received the amount (26 U.S.C. 1388(a)); the statute does not by its text relieve the member.
