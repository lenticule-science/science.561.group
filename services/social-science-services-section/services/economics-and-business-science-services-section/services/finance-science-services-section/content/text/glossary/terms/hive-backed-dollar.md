The **Hive-Backed Dollar (HBD)** is a chain-native asset of the Hive blockchain whose protocol targets one US dollar through a witness-published HIVE price feed. New HBD arises from converting HIVE (the collateralized conversion posts twice the HIVE needed, pays a 5 percent fee, and settles after 3.5 days) and from interest on savings balances at a rate set by the median of witness votes. The protocol limits supply by the HBD share of combined HIVE and HBD value: printing stops at 20 percent of virtual supply (treasury-held HBD excluded), and a price floor engages at 30 percent. HBD is not collateralized by a reserve asset in the sense of a fiat-reserve stablecoin; its peg rests on the conversion rules, witness feeds and an on-chain stabilizer funded by the [Decentralized Hive Fund](./decentralized-hive-fund.md).
