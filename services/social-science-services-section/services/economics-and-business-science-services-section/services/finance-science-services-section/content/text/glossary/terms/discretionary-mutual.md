A discretionary mutual is a member-owned risk pool that decides, claim by claim, whether to pay out, instead of promising payment by contract. Nexus Mutual describes its DAO as operating as a discretionary mutual, with a Foundation that is a UK community interest company; members pool capital, buy cover and underwrite by staking, and claims go to an assessment process ([On-Chain Mutuals and Insurance](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/08/2026-10-08-on-chain-mutuals-and-insurance.md)).

The discretion is the legal object: a cover holder has a right to ask the mutual's process to decide, not an enforceable claim. Nexus's process moved from member-staked assessors (until 2023-03) to a stake-weighted majority (until 2025-11) to a three-person committee with a 72-hour vote and an advisory-board override. On 2026-10-08 its pool value was 7.22 times the minimum capital requirement.
