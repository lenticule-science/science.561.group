An **impact certificate** describes work done, by whom and with what evidence, so that funders can pay for outcomes after the fact. Hypercerts began as a fractional, splittable ERC-1155 token ([Hypercerts Contracts 2025](https://github.com/hypercerts-org/hypercerts-protocol)); its current protocol stores signed records on AT Protocol and does not represent a hypercert as an onchain token ([Hypercerts Docs 2026](https://github.com/hypercerts-org/documentation)).

A record is the publisher's own account; trust comes from evaluations, evidence and funding receipts that others attach.
