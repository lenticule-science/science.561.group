A **limited cooperative association** under the Colorado Uniform Limited Cooperative Association Act (C.R.S. 7-58-104) is "an autonomous, unincorporated association of persons united to meet their mutual interests through a jointly owned enterprise primarily controlled by those persons," combining ownership and benefits for patron members with separate investments by investor members. Patron members are required or permitted by the articles or bylaws to conduct patronage with the association; investor members are not. At least two patron members are needed to begin business unless the sole member is a cooperative (7-58-501). Patron voting defaults to one vote each and may be allocated by use, equity or a combination (7-58-511, 512). Profits go to patron members in the ratio of patronage, and with investor members patrons keep at least fifty percent (7-58-1004). Patron equity instruments are exempt from the Colorado Securities Act (7-58-1009).

Wyoming's Title 17 contains no limited cooperative association act; its cooperative article is the agricultural Processing Cooperative Law.
