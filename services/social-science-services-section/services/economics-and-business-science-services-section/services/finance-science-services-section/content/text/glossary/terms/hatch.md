A **Hatch** is the opening phase of an [augmented bonding curve](./augmented-bonding-curve.md) commons. The Hatch app takes a minimum goal, a goal that closes it early, a period and an exchange rate; if the minimum is not met in the period contributors are refunded. Impact Hours then mints tokens to contributors for hours worked at a rate that rises with funds raised. The Token Engineering Commons Hatch closed 2021-08-14 at 1,571,223.57 wxDAI against an 800,000 minimum; see [Bonding Curves and the Token Engineering Commons](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/08/2026-10-08-bonding-curves-and-the-tec.md).
