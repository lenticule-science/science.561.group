A quantitative fund is not one clever trading idea; it is a pipeline of six distinct stages, each with its own failure modes and its own literature. A single strong signal is worthless if it is measured against data the strategy could not actually have seen, sized without a stated risk model, executed without a cost model, or left unreconciled once it trades real capital. This article walks the pipeline in order — data, research, portfolio, execution, booking and reconciliation, capital and fees — then names, stage by stage, which parts of it the house already has as running code and which parts remain to be wired together.

## Data

Everything downstream depends on the fund seeing, on any given historical date, only the information that would actually have been available on that date. [Point-in-time data](../../../../glossary/terms/point-in-time-data.md) is the discipline of storing history this way rather than as a present-day snapshot silently overwritten by later corrections: it corrects **survivorship bias** (dropping delisted or failed assets from the historical universe, which flatters historical returns) and **look-ahead bias** (using a restated figure, a revised index membership, or any other fact that was not yet knowable on the date being backtested). [Alternative data](../../../../glossary/terms/alternative-data.md) — satellite imagery, transaction data, web-scraped signals — is an increasingly used data source layered on top of prices and filings, and it inherits the same point-in-time discipline in a sharper form, since a novel vendor feed's "historical" file is often reconstructed after the fact rather than genuinely point-in-time.

## Research

[Alpha research](../../../../glossary/terms/alpha-research.md) is the pipeline that turns a candidate idea into a signal the fund is willing to size: a stated **hypothesis** (an economic or behavioral mechanism, not merely a correlation found by search), a **feature** built only from point-in-time-available data, **cross-validation** of the feature's predictive power on data withheld from its own discovery — typically walk-forward, chronological folds rather than randomly shuffled ones — and ongoing **decay** monitoring once the signal is live ([alpha decay](../../../../glossary/terms/alpha-decay.md)). [Backtest overfitting](../../../../glossary/terms/backtest-overfitting.md) is the central risk at this stage: searching enough rule variants over the same historical sample will eventually turn up one that looks profitable by chance, which is why the discipline holds a discovered signal to a statistical bar raised in proportion to how many variants were searched (Harvey, Liu and Zhu's multiple-testing argument), and checks its Sharpe ratio against the deflated Sharpe ratio (Bailey and López de Prado) rather than at face value.

A fund's overall skill at this stage is described by [the fundamental law of active management](../../../../glossary/terms/fundamental-law-of-active-management.md) (Grinold and Kahn): the information ratio a fund achieves scales with the accuracy of each individual forecast (the information coefficient) times the square root of the number of genuinely independent forecasts it makes (breadth). This is why a fund can be built coherently on many weak, independent signals rather than one strong one — a fund fusing dozens of small, uncorrelated public signals is not a weaker substitute for finding one big edge, it is a different and equally legitimate point on the same law.

Concrete strategy families sit downstream of this research discipline rather than outside it: [statistical arbitrage](../../../../glossary/terms/statistical-arbitrage.md), [systematic macro](../../../../glossary/terms/systematic-macro.md), [trend following](../../../../glossary/terms/trend-following.md), and [market-neutral](../../../../glossary/terms/market-neutral.md) construction are all, at root, particular choices of hypothesis, feature, and universe run through the same research pipeline.

## Portfolio

[Portfolio construction](../../../../glossary/terms/portfolio-construction.md) turns a set of validated forecasts into actual position sizes — a separate step from finding the forecasts and from filling the resulting orders. The classical formulation is Markowitz mean-variance optimization against a [risk model](../../../../glossary/terms/risk-model.md)'s covariance estimate, constrained by position, sector, and turnover limits; **risk budgeting** (of which [risk parity](../../../../glossary/terms/risk-parity.md) is one specific rule) allocates a fixed amount of risk rather than capital across positions so no single bet dominates the portfolio's variance. A construction rule that ignores expected transaction costs can recommend a trade whose cost exceeds its expected benefit, so realistic implementations optimize net of a stated [transaction cost model](../../../../glossary/terms/transaction-cost-model.md) rather than gross.

Position sizing specifically — how much to bet, given an edge and a bankroll — has its own dedicated theory: the [Kelly criterion](../../../../glossary/terms/kelly-criterion.md) sizes to maximize expected logarithmic wealth, and [fractional Kelly](../../../../glossary/terms/fractional-kelly.md) deliberately undersizes the full Kelly fraction for robustness against estimation error. [Strategy capacity](../../../../glossary/terms/strategy-capacity.md) sets a ceiling on how much of this construction a given signal can actually absorb before its own trading erodes the edge it depends on.

## Execution

Once a target portfolio exists, [optimal execution](../../../../glossary/terms/optimal-execution.md) (Almgren and Chriss) schedules the trades needed to reach it, trading off expected [market impact](../../../../glossary/terms/market-impact.md) cost against the price risk of taking longer to trade. This stage depends directly on [market microstructure](../../../../glossary/terms/market-microstructure.md) theory (Kyle; Glosten and Milgrom) for why trading moves prices and widens spreads at all, and on a stated [transaction cost model](../../../../glossary/terms/transaction-cost-model.md) covering both the explicit costs a venue's fee schedule states in advance and the implicit costs (spread, impact) a model must estimate. Execution ultimately routes through a **venue algebra** — a stated interface for placing, amending, and cancelling orders against one or more actual trading venues under an authority and risk-limit envelope — rather than an ad hoc script that calls a broker's API directly wherever a strategy happens to need it.

## Booking and reconciliation

A fill is not the end of the pipeline. Every executed order has to be **booked** — recorded as the fund's own transaction record — and that record has to be **reconciled** against independent evidence of what actually happened at the venue (an account statement, a trade confirmation, a custodian's own record), so the fund's books are never taken on the strategy's own say-so. This is a distinct discipline from [live-versus-backtest reconciliation](../../../../glossary/terms/live-versus-backtest-reconciliation.md), which compares the strategy's realized performance, once booked, against what its backtest predicted — the check that catches [alpha decay](../../../../glossary/terms/alpha-decay.md), an implementation bug, or overfitting the earlier validation missed.

## Capital and fees

Capital does not enter or leave a fund for free. Access to leverage, short borrow, and custody for an institutional fund typically comes through [prime brokerage](../../../../glossary/terms/prime-brokerage.md); the fund itself is typically compensated under a [hedge fund fee structure](../../../../glossary/terms/hedge-fund-fee-structure.md) — a management fee, a performance fee, a high-water mark preventing double-charging on the same gains, and often a hurdle rate. Every stage above is ultimately judged against this stage: a strategy's gross [Sharpe ratio](../../../../glossary/terms/sharpe-ratio.md) and [drawdown](../../../../glossary/terms/drawdown.md) are necessary but not sufficient — the same numbers net of fees, and net of the [risk of ruin](../../../../glossary/terms/risk-of-ruin.md) a sizing rule actually bounds, are what determines whether the fund is worth running or allocating to.

## Named incumbents, by what they are known for

No two of the firms most often named in this space do the same thing at the same layer of this pipeline. Renaissance Technologies' Medallion Fund is the standard example of statistical-arbitrage-style, short-horizon signals run at a capacity the firm caps by policy rather than lets grow unbounded. Two Sigma and AQR are systematic, factor- and data-driven managers spanning equities and macro, with AQR in particular a prominent publisher of the academic factor-investing and risk-parity-adjacent literature this glossary cites. Bridgewater Associates is the standard example of risk parity, through its All Weather strategy. Citadel runs a large multi-strategy hedge fund alongside its separately branded Citadel Securities market-making business; Jane Street and Jump Trading are commonly cited as high-frequency, market-making and latency-sensitive proprietary trading firms. None of this is a ranking — it is a reminder that "quant fund" names several different jobs (signal research, systematic macro allocation, risk-parity asset allocation, multi-strategy capital allocation, and market-making) that this pipeline underlies in every case, even though the emphasis a given firm places on each stage differs sharply.

## The house version

The house's own code realizes real pieces of every stage above, though not yet wired into one continuously operating live system — the gap the house's own [fintech-friday](../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fintech-friday.md) standard names directly.

- **Data.** `point-in-time-asset-universe-service` (`@red-cup-engineering/point-in-time-asset-universe-service`, in the alpaca-services-section) projects bitemporal asset observations into deterministic, survivorship-aware universe snapshots — a real point-in-time data store for the asset universe, not merely a stated intention. It is scoped to the tradeable universe rather than to point-in-time fundamentals or corporate actions more broadly.
- **Research.** `signal-fusion-alpha-engine-service` is the house's alpha-research engine: it defines a typed `Signal`, shrinks correlated signals sharing an independence tag before fusion (an honest operationalization of "independent" bets, in the spirit of the fundamental law of active management), and composes `purged-walk-forward-fold-service`'s chronological purge/split with real bilattice belief fusion into one walk-forward backtest whose reported edge is always the untouched holdout. Its own README reports a genuine, currently-nil measured edge on a BTC momentum-plus-news belief — the honest behavior of a kill switch on a refuted edge, not a bug, and itself a working instance of `backtest-overfitting`'s walk-forward discipline. The `forecasting-services-section` is a related but distinct capability: calibrated, capital-free forecasting scored by Brier and log score, the discipline this glossary's `alpha-research` needs for tracking a model's calibration, sold and operated as a separate skill-for-fee product rather than folded into a live trading system's own scoring.
- **Portfolio.** `position-sizing-service`'s `sleeve` module (`sizePosition`) is a real growth-optimal, Kelly-family sizing rule bounded by a hard risk sleeve — full Kelly on a walled risk stake by default, refusing rather than clamping when liability beyond the stake would result. `mandate-portfolio-allocation-service` converts independently supplied scores and risk measures into mandate-bounded target weights, the house's portfolio-construction step proper.
- **Execution.** `brokerage-execution-transport-service` validates a contract-bound, authority-scoped order intent and places it through a pluggable venue adapter; `pluggable-venue-adapter-service` implements that adapter concretely for Alpaca (`alpaca-connect`, `alpaca-broker`, paper and live trading), refusing to transmit anything that looks like a credential and binding every call to a revocable, time-boxed authority grant. This is a real, running venue algebra — but only one venue (Alpaca) is actually realized behind the pluggable interface today, and `signal-fusion-alpha-engine-service`'s own execution intents are still produced `simulatedOnly: true` — the wiring from a fused, sized signal into this real execution transport has not yet been armed.
- **Booking and reconciliation.** `account-evidence-reconciliation-service` normalizes a raw Alpaca account snapshot (positions, orders, activities) into a house-canonical, dated record; `brokerage-independent-witness-and-books` records an outcome or market event as witnessed by an actor distinct from whichever actor produced it, refusing to accept an actor's witness of its own output. Together these are the house's booking-and-independent-reconciliation layer. `transaction-cost-replay-service` — used inside `signal-fusion-alpha-engine-service`'s own backtest, not yet against live fills — is the house's transaction-cost model: it replays target weights over realized returns under an explicit cost model and reports hit rate and expectancy alongside the usual risk and performance measures. `live-versus-backtest-reconciliation` specifically — comparing booked live performance against the original backtest, on an ongoing basis, once a strategy trades real capital — is not yet built as a standing capability, because no strategy here has yet been armed to trade real capital.
- **Capital and fees.** No house carrier answers to hedge-fund-style external fee structures, prime brokerage, or outside capital raising named in this article; the house's finance operations custody (fintech-friday and its named program carriers) is scoped to the 561 Group's own capital rather than to a fund managing outside investors' money, so this stage of the pipeline is, correctly, absent rather than unbuilt.

The honest summary, stated the way the house's own fintech-friday standard states it: what warrants giving any of this real capital is not any one of these pieces existing — most of them already do — but "one connected, deep quantitative system: recorded point-in-time data it actually holds, parameterized features, a stated hypothesis with cross-validated edge under multiple-testing control, cost-aware portfolio construction, execution through one venue algebra, fills booked and reconciled, and live performance reconciled to its own backtest." Measured against that checklist, the house holds a real, tested instance of every capability the checklist names, but has not yet armed the execution stage or run the resulting system long enough to produce a live-versus-backtest reconciliation record — the wiring together, not any single missing capability, is what stands between the pieces above and a live key.
