## Copied: Landauer entropy as currency (frozen legacy)

All four items below come from the same legacy executive-office dump
(`lib/legacy/2026-08-03-executive-office`, under
`561-group-board-of-directors`) and form one coherent prior thread: the
Union's `wit`/`tally` denomination work, which independently arrived at
"money is a witnessed distinction-step, priced in Landauer erasure cost"
from the commodity/settlement side, in July 2026 — a month before this
section's founding note arrived at "money is witness-deletion" from the
obligation/logic side. The founding note itself flags this as unintegrated
prior estate thought (see its item 5); this index is the collection pass
that makes it available to integrate.

- **`wit`** glossary term — copied to
  `../../../../../../glossary/terms/wit.md`. The unit of witnessed change,
  denominated four ways: Landauer erasure cost (kT ln 2 per bit), the
  Euler-characteristic index balance H⁰−H¹=χ, proof-debt pricing of
  `Quot.sound`, and the market witness-bit form. Etymology load-bearing:
  *weid-* (witness) vs. "unwitting" = counterfeit, unwitnessed change —
  directly the founding note's vocabulary, arrived at independently.
- **`tally`** glossary term — copied to
  `../../../../../../glossary/terms/tally.md`. The border-reconciled,
  two-sided, content-addressed form a wit takes when it crosses a
  settlement border — i.e. the structural *opposite* of a bearer
  instrument's provenance deletion, and the most direct test case for the
  founding note's claim.
- **Union Wit Denomination Specification** (2026-07-23, draft, never
  seated) — copied to
  `2026-08-21-collected-union-wit-denomination-specification.md`. An
  RFC-style attempt to formalize wit's four-carrier grounding with
  explicit proof/thesis/ruled-practice grading.
- **Union Monetary and Financial Statistics Guide** (2026-07-23) — copied
  to `2026-08-21-collected-union-monetary-and-financial-statistics-guide.md`.
  An IMF-MFSMCG-style compilation guide proving, repeatedly and by
  section, that no Union monetary aggregate exists yet — the most
  rigorous prior articulation of what *keeping* provenance (denomination
  heterogeneity reported, never converted; absence reported as unknown,
  never zero) looks like, i.e. of a register that has not yet forgotten
  enough to function as bearer money.
- **The Union as an economy: a consolidated record** (2026-07-23) — copied
  to `2026-08-21-collected-the-union-as-an-economy-a-consolidated-record.md`.
  The narrative spine: session-transcript-sourced timeline from the
  owner's diagnosis ("there's no commodities, no trade, no economics
  happening between our enterprises") through the wit/tally naming
  session. Contains the clearest direct hit for the "Landauer entropy as
  currency" search: the owner's own idea record states **fuel should be
  reserved exclusively for the entropy/Landauer-cost currency** (glossary
  of secondary terms, "fuel" entry) — named confirmation that this line of
  thought was explicitly conceived as a currency design, not just a
  physics analogy.

## Indexed, not copied: gift economies as the witnessed control group

- **`reciprocity`** glossary term (market-services-section, this section's
  own parent) — Mauss's three gift obligations, Graeber's three baseline
  modes of exchange, and Indigenous reciprocity traditions (mitakuye
  oyasin) as a mode that "does not close the relation at the point of
  transaction" — the structural opposite of a bearer instrument closing
  a relation completely. Left in place:
  `../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/reciprocity.md`.
- **`gift-economy-redistribution`** (Lakota governance formalism,
  `philosophy-services-section/lakota-services-section`) — the richest
  single piece of witnessed-obligation material found in this collection
  pass. Formalizes the gift as a morphism that installs *standing-cells*
  and *kin-obligation-cells*, explicitly **not** a debt-cell: "the gift
  morphism does not install a debt-cell requiring b to return-something-
  to-a... reciprocity is structural to the system but is not installed at
  the cell-level as a specific debt." Pressure to reciprocate is
  reputational and substrate-recorded (visible to the whole community),
  never contractual — i.e. witnessing does the work a bearer instrument's
  provenance deletion would otherwise do. Left in place:
  `../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/lakota-services-section/content/text/glossary/lakota/discharging/library/predicating/library/positing/library/settling/library/hacking/lakotaean-governance/philA/code/transformers/gift-economy-redistribution.md`
