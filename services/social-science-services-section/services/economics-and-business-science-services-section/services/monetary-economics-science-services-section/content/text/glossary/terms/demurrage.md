Demurrage is a charge on holding money, taken as a continuous decay of balances, that gives money an incentive to circulate. On-chain forms read in this research: Circles v2 applies a daily factor 0.93 to the power 1/365.25, 7 percent a year, to balances, with new personal currency issued at one per hour; and the Grassroots Economics demurrage token decays every balance each minute and lets the decayed value be withdrawn to a sink account on a schedule to be redistributed ([UBI and Local Currency Protocols](../../../../../finance-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/08/2026-10-08-ubi-and-local-currency-protocols.md)). The Sarafu study of affordances records the holding-tax calculation as the function blockchain automated.

See the demurrage and local-currency evidence record for the measured effects.
