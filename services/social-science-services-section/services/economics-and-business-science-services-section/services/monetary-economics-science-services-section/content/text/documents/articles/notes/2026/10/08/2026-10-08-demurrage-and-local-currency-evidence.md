This note records what the sources establish about demurrage (a holding charge on money) and about measured local-currency velocity. The house terms [tally](../../../../../../glossary/terms/tally.md) and [wit](../../../../../../glossary/terms/wit.md) sit in the same monetary section.

## Fisher's design and speed claim

Fisher 1933 describes municipal stamp scrip: a dated stamp for each of 52 weeks, sold at 2 cents; no one can pass the scrip on a Wednesday without that week's stamp. A dollar of scrip returning for redemption after a year finds $1.04 waiting, so the issue is self-liquidating. Fisher states the speed as at least two transfers a week, four times the speed of an average dollar in normal times and twelve times the depression average, and says the stamp is closer to a tax on hoarding than a sales tax: a grocer turning over $60 of scrip in a week pays about one-third of one per cent of sales. This is a design estimate, not a measured series.

## Wörgl, 1932 to 1933

Ottacher 2024 gives the best-documented account in the sources read. The notes (1, 5 and 10 Schilling; 32,000 Schilling first printed) carried a stamp of 1 percent of face value per month, 12 percent a year, and could be redeemed at the municipality for a 2 percent fee. No more than ATS 12,600 had to be put into circulation; on average ATS 5,490 was out, ATS 1.3 per citizen against ATS 158.76 of Nationalbank money per citizen, and the notes passed through the municipal treasury 36.43 times a year. Tax arrears stood at ATS 118,000 at the start; arrears receipts in the first eight and a half months rose 53.8 percent over the prior year because holders paid taxes at month end to avoid the stamp. Extra municipal investment was about ATS 150,000. The widely quoted 25 percent fall in unemployment is corrected in Ottacher 2024 (citing Wendel) to 11 to 16 percent, against a 19 percent national rise. No price rise was observed because the sum was too small. The interest-rate fall that Gesell's theory predicts did not occur because circulation was local. Critics held that success depended on unemployment, business stagnation and tax arrears together. The experiment ended in autumn 1933 under court and National Bank action.

The paper by Jeremy September (September 2026) finds a data inconsistency not previously discussed in the literature: certificates redeemed for Schillings were 34,500 Schilling (one report, nine months) or 66,300 Schilling (inferred from the 1,326 Schilling collected as the 2 percent fee), against a 12,000 Schilling deposit and a peak of about 8,000 Schilling in circulation. The paper also states that acceptance for municipal taxes drove the rapid circulation from the first day, and that about 100,000 Schilling of projects were completed, 12,000 of it government emergency credit.

The sources read do not compute a Wörgl-to-Schilling velocity ratio; the figure of 36.43 passes a year is the source number.

## The Chiemgauer and the velocity table

Gelleri 2009, the issuer's own account, gives the design (euro at 1:1; demurrage 2 percent a quarter, 8 percent a year; 5 percent fee to convert back to euros; 3 percent of purchases to a chosen non-profit) and reports velocity three times the euro's after five years, turnover up 30 percent in 2008 to about 3.6 million euro, one third of it judged new (about 600,000 euro), and circulation of 300,000 in early 2009.

De La Rosa & Stodder 2015 tabulate velocity and annual turnover for nine complementary currencies: WIR 2.60 (CHF 1.55 billion, 2011), RES 1.89, Talente 4.00, Chiemgauer 11.30 (6,198,411), Berkshares 4.05, Palmas 13.50, Sol Violette 4.49, Bristol Pound 0.79 and RES Catalonia 1.56 (2013). The Chiemgauer is 4.97 times faster than the euro in Germany (2.27 in 2011); the dollar's velocity fell from 3.42 to 2.19 between 1929 and 1933 while transaction-based scrip stood at 50 or more, Okmulgee almost 100. The paper attributes differences to more than demurrage: blended loans, partly paper rather than purely virtual currency, and unregistered members.

Zeller 2020 compares the Chiemgauer and Kenya's Sarafu Credit and finds community currencies advantageous only where national-currency liquidity is seasonally insufficient.

## Theory

Keynes 1936 endorses the principle of stamped money and states the obstacle: money differs only in degree from other assets in carrying a liquidity premium, so taxing it sends liquidity into substitutes such as bank money, debts at call and precious metals. Rehme 2024 builds a Sidrauski model with love of wealth: Gesell's short-run claims hold and depreciation overcomes the zero lower bound; in the long run depreciation alone may not be beneficial, and with expansionary monetary policy it is a necessary condition for a positive Mundell-Tobin effect. Fare & Ould Ahmed 2017 review the objectives and governance of complementary currencies.

## What a pooling instrument can do with this

- The measured velocity effect in every case coexisted with a fixed redemption link to a national currency, an issuer-run reserve and a way to pay taxes or fees in the currency; Wörgl's circulation was driven by tax acceptance.
- Demurrage runs 8 percent (Chiemgauer) to 12 percent (Wörgl) a year and a reconversion fee of 2 to 5 percent funds the issuer or non-profits; both are revenue lines of the instrument.
- Keynes's substitute-liquidity objection applies to any on-chain demurrage token: holders can exit to an undecayed asset, so the exit fee and the redemption queue set the real decay.
- No source here measures a counterfactual for local output; the 600,000 euro Chiemgauer figure and the Wörgl employment figure are the issuers' or historians' estimates.
