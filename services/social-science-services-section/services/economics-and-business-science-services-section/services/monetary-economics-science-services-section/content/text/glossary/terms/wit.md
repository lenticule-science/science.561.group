A wit is the unit of witnessed change: one witnessed distinction-step in the FOUR knowledge
order (bottom to true, bottom to false, a determinate value to both). The formal, contractual
form of the same unit is the witness bit — one ordered token/claim payload together with its
separately produced formal-articulation bit, as assayed by the
witness-bit-commodity-assay-service contract. "Wit" is the unit name: what prices, ledgers,
budgets, and speech count in. Adopting the short form changes no contract; the assay service's
invariants (token first, claim second; one economic bit payload is one ordered pair;
articulation is separate production work; price forms only at a counterparty border) define
what one wit is.

The unit is not metaphorical. The same distinction-step is already denominated four ways in
this firm's own carriers: physically, one bit erased costs kT ln 2 (Landauer's principle;
Obsmetrologia's LandauerEntropy counts one erased bit per collapsed coordination cycle);
topologically, kept bits minus erased bits is the Euler characteristic (the index balance
H⁰ − H¹ = χ, machine-checked); in proof, the local law prices `Quot.sound` as one unpriced
collapsed distinction, so axiom debt is exactly unwitting change — change that occurred
without the wit being paid; in the market, the witness bit is the traded commodity form. The
open theorem tying every carrier's accounting to one ledger is the blueprint node
`thm:distinction-denomination-functor`.

The etymology is load-bearing, not decorative. Wit descends from the root *weid-*, to see or
know — the root of witness, and of the knowledge order the unit steps through. The negative
form independently means the counterfeit: unwitting change is change with no witness and no
payment. The corpus lineage runs to the mark, the library's one underived primitive — the act
of drawing a distinction — of which a wit is the witnessed, priced instance: the mark is free;
the wit never is.

A wit is spent or kept at a position; what crosses a settlement border is not a bare wit but a
reconciled pair of receipts — see tally. Known namespace collision, flagged at adoption: a
minor external cryptocurrency (Witnet's WIT) uses the ticker; the Union unit is defined by the
assay contract, not by ticker.
