ACK is acknowledgement: the uptake of a [WIT](wit.md) by a party other than
the one who asserted it. The Leader stated on 2026-10-09 that WIT, ACK and ATT
are not ambiguous; ACK names acknowledgement and nothing else.

An ACK is minted only at a border. An agent's own scale-1 self cannot
contest ($H^1 = 0$), so self-acknowledgement moves nothing. Every place the
house realizes an ACK has this border: the admission half of a
[tally](tally.md) is held by the counterparty; an incumbency's recognition is
given by the office's community, not the candidate; a receiver's disposition
is separate from the producer's; the witness-bit assay prices only at a
counterparty border. Acknowledging a contested utterance is still an ACK.

A WIT taken of an ACK is [ATT](att.md), attention.

## Settlement scope and proof status

An ACK has a *sender, receiver, particular claim, and crossing*. It is
not a count of approvals, a claim's truth charge, the submitter's
self-approval, or the mere receipt of a campaign advertisement. The
acknowledging counterparty can receive a **contested** claim without
resolving its evidence. Only a verified release/admission match for the
same crossing is the economic `tally`; a lone ACK does not manufacture
its unmatched half.

The `Fix σ`/index-balance interpretation of ACK in the
[2026-09-04 synthesis](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/04/2026-09-04-note-on-acknowledgement-beside-wit.md)
is **a mathematical research conjecture**, not a theorem in
Obsmetrologia. In particular, it is not yet established that every ACK
contributes exactly one H⁰ class. The corpus's other sense of
*recognition*, formal classification by a normative system, is not
automatically the same event as an economic border ACK.
