ACK is acknowledgement: the uptake of a [WIT](wit.md) by a party other than
the one who asserted it. The Leader stated on 2026-10-09 that WIT, ACK and ATT
are not ambiguous; ACK names acknowledgement and nothing else.

An ACK is minted only at a border. An agent's own scale-1 self cannot
contest ($H^1 = 0$), so self-acknowledgement moves nothing. Every place the
house realizes an ACK has this border: the admission half of a
[tally](tally.md) is held by the counterparty; an incumbency's recognition is
given by the office's community, not the candidate; a receiver's disposition
is separate from the producer's; the witness-bit assay prices only at a
counterparty border. Acknowledging a contested utterance is still an ACK.

A WIT taken of an ACK is [ATT](att.md), attention.
