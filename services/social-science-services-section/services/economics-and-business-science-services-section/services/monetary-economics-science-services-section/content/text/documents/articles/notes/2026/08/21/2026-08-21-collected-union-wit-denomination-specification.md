This specification defines the 'wit' denomination unit for use within the Union's financial and market-services infrastructure. It details the grounding of wit in the witness-bit commodity and the formal accounting requirements established by the executive-office.

3. Definitions
- wit: An adopted unit name used for denomination. Per the Union Monetary and Financial Statistics Guide (section 0), wit is not seated as Union legal tender, a settled allocation currency, or a monetary aggregate.
- tally: The companion term governing the counting and accumulation of wit.
- witness-bit: The commodity form tracked via the witness-bit-commodity-assay-service contract.

4. Normative Content
4.1 Status and Adoption
Wit is adopted as a unit name only (ruled-practice). It shall not be construed as Union legal tender, a settled allocation currency, or a monetary aggregate (ruled-practice).

4.2 Denomination Carriers
The wit unit relies on four-carrier grounding (ruled-practice):
a) Landauer erasure cost (ruled-practice);
b) The Euler-characteristic index balance: H0 - H1 = chi (proven: machine-checked);
c) Proof-debt pricing of Quot.sound (ruled-practice);
d) The market witness-bit form (ruled-practice).

4.3 The Distinction Denomination Functor
The blueprint node `thm:distinction-denomination-functor` is defined as an open theoretical claim tying every carrier's accounting to one ledger (thesis: unproven). This claim must not be upgraded and remains strictly unproven (thesis).

4.4 Issuance and Retirement
Issuance and retirement of wit-denominated witness-bits shall adhere to the full-reserve issuance rules defined in the Union M0 Protocol, section 2 (ruled-practice).

5. Conformance
All systems utilizing wit as a denomination must conform to the witness-bit-commodity.schema.json for assay reporting (ruled-practice). Any deviation from the H0 - H1 = chi balance constitutes a violation of the proven accounting integrity requirements (proven: machine-checked).
