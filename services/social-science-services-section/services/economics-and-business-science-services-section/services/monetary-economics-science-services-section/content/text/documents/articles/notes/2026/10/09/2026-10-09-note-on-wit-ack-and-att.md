# Note on WIT, ACK and ATT

The Leader, 2026-10-09: "WIT ACK ATT are not meant to be ambiguous, ATT is
attention and there's meant to be good theory on this." The house holds that
theory. It sits in four places: the wit term, the 2026-09-04 acknowledgement
note, two Lean modules and a Lean ledger, and a run of notes on attention. This
note states it. The term [attention](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/attention.md)
seats it; the stub that held that name was empty.

Carrier keys are listed at the end. Paths are relative to
`lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/`.

## The theory

1. **WIT is the unit.** A wit is one witnessed distinction-step in the FOUR
   knowledge order: bottom to true, bottom to false, a determinate value to both
   (WIT:17-19). It is the unit name that prices, ledgers, budgets and speech count
   in (WIT:20-22). It is the same step denominated four ways: Landauer erasure
   cost, the index balance `H⁰ − H¹ = χ`, the proof-debt of `Quot.sound`, and the
   market witness bit (WIT:27-34).

2. **ACK is the other party's recognition.** In ordinary and legal use an
   acknowledgement is receipt, prior to and distinct from agreement (ACK:76-92).
   In the house it is minted only at a border and only by a party other than the
   asserter: tally's admission half is held by the counterparty, incumbency's
   recognition is given by the office's community, and LAGOON's receiver
   disposition is separate from the producer (ACK:312-319). A tally is the matched
   pair, a release and an admission, and "transfer settles when the halves match"
   (TALLY:17-21). An ACK records recognition and not resolution (ACK:320-327).

3. **ATT is attention.** Attention is the finite budget a bounded agent spends to
   reconcile obstructions. The operator said it on 2026-07-13: "Attention = the
   scarce resource reconciliation spends" (THREAD:45). The field note says it
   plainly: "Attention is which obstructions the organism can afford to discharge"
   (FIELD:260-261).

4. **The structure of ATT is proved.** An obstruction has a reconciliation cost
   and a benefit; a selection is feasible when its total cost fits the budget
   (AA:33-54). Spend is monotone (`cost_monotone_cons`, AA:70-73), feasibility is
   downward closed (`feasible_of_cons`, AA:75-80), residual is antitone in captured
   benefit (`residual_antitone`, AA:96-102), and the bundle is `attention_allocation`
   (AA:113-123). The solver finds the optimum; value is the same under a left and a
   right fold, and the chosen selection is handed (AS:1-20). This is the demand
   side of reconciliation, beside the cost side the currency cocone prices
   (AA:6-12).

5. **The budget is the seed's free energy.** `budget_splits` proves that at the
   closing step the free energy is the reversible spend `commits + mints` plus the
   irreversible spend `2·jumps` (FEL:1109-1137). `irreversible_within_budget`
   bounds each spend by the seed energy and shows the irreversible spend equals the
   whole budget exactly when the reversible spend is zero (FEL:1139-1150). The
   ledger counts "energy discharged equals knowledge gained, unit for unit"
   (FEL:470-473).

6. **ATT is denominated in wits.** The ledger's three step kinds are the wit's
   three steps. A commitment takes `⊥ₖ` to a truth pole (FEL:544-545), which is
   bottom to true or bottom to false. A mint takes a truth pole to `⊤ₖ`
   (FEL:547-549), which is a determinate value to both. A jump takes `⊥ₖ` straight
   to `⊤ₖ` and is "worth two units" (FEL:551-553), which is two wits in one cell.
   One unit of flux is one wit. The budget attention spends is a count of wits.
   This identification is read off the two definitions; no house file states it,
   and this note seats it.

7. **ATT is therefore not a coin beside WIT.** The acknowledgement note reaches
   the same position from the economic side: attention "is always a second-order
   move, a WIT taken of an ACK, never a coin of its own" (ACK:290-297). Item 6
   supplies the ledger under that sentence.

8. **The relation.** WIT is the maker's move, counted at the asserted stratum.
   ACK is the second party's border act that takes the asserted step into its own
   record. ATT is the budget the knowing face spends when it reads the playing
   face's section, and when what it reads is an acknowledgement, the spend is a wit
   taken of an ACK (ACK:250-262). The face carrier fixes the geometry: one agent's
   own scale-1 self is glued from communicating (across, truth), knowing (inside,
   knowledge) and playing (outside, evidence) faces, and "the agent's own self
   cannot contest, so scale-1 gluing is always H¹=0" (FACE:3-11). That is why ACK
   needs a second party and why ATT, which is one agent's budget, is spent on
   reading the other's section.

9. **The spiral does not close.** Witness, then acknowledgement, then the witnessing
   of that acknowledgement. An acknowledgement of attention is the next turn's
   witness (ACK:299-308). Across two agents on a lossy channel common knowledge is
   never reached by a finite exchange (ACK:106-117), so settlement is
   regime-restricted: TCP stops at ESTABLISHED after three messages and the work
   rail stops at `H*` once cited evidence closes under `verifyProofClosure`
   (ACK:264-280, 299-308).

10. **The three meet in running software.** The attention worklist selects, under
    each cycle's budget, the held contradictions and the half-tallies older than the
    settlement window (WORKLIST:11). A half-tally is a release with no admission,
    a WIT with no ACK, and it is an obstruction that demands ATT. The ringleader
    method does the same for the enterprise: it "routes finite, irreversibly
    consumed attention toward reconciliation that removes an observed obstruction"
    (WQ:22-24), through the executable allocation readout (WQ:49-53).

11. **The attention economy is a different thing at a border.** It is the order in
    which a third party pays for a person's attention (ECON:22-24). ATT is the
    internal budget; the attention economy prices a person's attention at the
    external border.

## Conflicting readings and which the Leader's statements select

**Reading 1. ATT is a third currency or economy.** The Leader's 2026-09-04
movement named witness economy, acknowledgement economy and attention economy in
one breath (ACK:211-218). **Reading 2. ATT is no currency; it is a wit taken of
an ACK** (ACK:290-297). **Reading 3. ATT is the Nat-valued budget `B`** of the
allocation (AA:33-54).
The Leader's 2026-10-09 statement selects that ATT is attention and that the
theory is the good theory the house holds. The operator's 2026-07-13 statement
selects Reading 3 as what attention is. Readings 2 and 3 agree once item 6 holds,
since the budget `B` counts wits. Reading 1 stands as a name for the economy in
which that budget is spent and traded, and not as a third unit. The Leader's
2026-09-04 question "is ATT a WIT to an ACK?" is a question and not a statement of
theory; Reading 2 is the house's only derived answer to it.

**Reading 4. WIT prices only the erased side `H¹`, and ACK the kept side `H⁰`**
(ACK:184-209, labeled conjecture). **Reading 5. A wit is the distinction-step in
every carrier, kept and erased, "spent or kept at a position"** (WIT:27-34, 44).
The wit term is a seated definition and the conjecture is labeled conjecture, so
Reading 5 governs. The conjecture's falsifier, an isolated acknowledgement cell
that changes neither count (ACK:199-209), is not yet run.

**Reading 6. The factor two in `budget_splits` prices two witnessings of a
handshake**, the acknowledger's look and the witness-taker's look (ACK:281-288,
labeled conjecture). **Reading 7. The factor two is the two unit steps `⊥ₖ` to a
truth pole to `⊤ₖ` taken in one cell** (FEL:551-553, item 6). Reading 7 is the
proved ledger and governs. Reading 6 is not entailed by it. The operator's
2026-07-13 gloss "JUMP = you looked, can't un-look, 1 Landauer bit" (THREAD:45)
counts erased bits per collapsed coordination cycle (WIT:27-34); the flux counts
knowledge-height. They are different counts, and the house has not yet proved a map
between them.

**Reading 8. A wit is a currency** (the title and prose of ACK) **versus Reading
9. A wit is an adopted unit name only, not legal tender, not a settled allocation
currency, not a monetary aggregate** (SPEC:37-43). The wit term says "unit name"
(WIT:20-22). The specification that says "unit name only" is itself a draft
marked not accepted (SPEC:19-23), so the term governs, and it agrees with
Reading 9. "Second currency" in the acknowledgement note is loose speech for a
second kind of settled act.

**Reading 10. Attention is simulated time; Reading 11. Attention is heat.**
Neither conflicts with the budget theory; each extends it and neither is proved
beyond it. The simulated-time note says its claim is "not yet proved anywhere in
the vault" (SIM:18-19). The thermodynamics audit finds attention-as-heat literal
for transformer attention, where one dense-Hopfield update is one attention step,
and overreach for physical inverse temperature and spin-glass holonomy
(THERMO:36-60, 164-182). The term records both beside the theory with those
statuses.
