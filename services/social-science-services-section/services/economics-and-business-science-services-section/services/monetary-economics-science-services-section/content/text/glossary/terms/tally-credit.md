Tally credit is a line of credit whose limit is a function of the counterparty's record of matched tallies, the release and admission receipts that agree on each past crossing, and never of the balance the counterparty holds. A [tally](./tally.md) is the border-reconciled form of a [wit](./wit.md): not a unit held at a position but a matched pair of receipts held by two counterparties, and a counterparty with a long unbroken tally record has demonstrated circulation, not holdings. The limit is computed from that record alone: the count of matched pairs in the window times the median settled size, times a factor that falls to zero with any unmatched half-tally older than the settlement window.

It differs from balance-secured credit in that what secures the line is circulation the books cannot book, and from social collateral in that the collateral is a record rather than a sanction the lender has to guess at. Balance-secured credit lends against the quantity finance can book, which the house's contextuality reading caps at 2 on the pentagon while the value realized in circulation is √5; tally credit lends against the other quantity. The group-lending literature established that a borrower's relations can secure credit ([Besley 1995](../../../../../economics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/besley1995.md)) and that peers' observation is an asset a lender can use at the price of risk borne by the observers ([Stiglitz 1990](../../../../../economics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/stiglitz1990.md)), but left the strength of the sanction outside the model; the tally record is that strength made legible, so the risk need not be moved onto anyone.

Every draw is itself a crossing, a release receipt from the pool and an admission receipt from the member, so repayment is the matching pair and a default is a mismatch held in FOUR rather than an eviction. The line closes, the half-tally stays on the worklist, and the next limit is computed from the record including the default, which is how the house pool keeps [social collateral](../../../../../economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-collateral.md) without the contagion that [joint-liability lending](../../../../../economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/joint-liability-lending.md) transmits through a defaulter's peers. The instrument is realized in the pool operations, where a member's line is derived, a draw is fronted against it, and settlement is the matched pair; its falsifier is a default rate above that of balance-secured lines of the same size on the same market.
