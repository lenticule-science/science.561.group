# Monetary Economics Science Services Section

The Monetary Economics Science Services Section maintains research on money,
including the study of money as a witnessing technology rather than a neutral
medium of exchange.

## Founding thesis

Money is the witness-forgetting functor applied to obligation. An
obligation, natively, is a charged relation — who owes what to whom,
witnessed by the parties and their community, consumed exactly once when
discharged. A bearer instrument (cash) is that obligation with the
provenance deleted: it owes anyone, from anyone, for anything. Bearer
instruments are provenance deletion; gift economies — where the witness
set stays attached to the obligation — are the witnessed control group
against which bearer money's forgetting can be measured.
