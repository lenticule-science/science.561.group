# Selling services to Minnesota public bodies

Written for Binding Generations, whose buyers are library boards, school
districts, charter schools and regional systems. It holds for any small firm
selling services to Minnesota local government. Statutes were read at the
Revisor on 2026-09-27.

## 1. Purchasing law, and where services fall

**The Uniform Municipal Contracting Law** (Minn. Stat. 471.345) covers
counties, towns, cities, school districts and other political subdivisions.
As amended in 2023:

- **$25,000 or less:** open market or quotation, at the body's discretion
  (subd. 5).
- **Over $25,000 and up to $175,000:** sealed bids, or direct negotiation with
  at least two quotations kept on file for a year (subd. 4).
- **Over $175,000:** sealed bids after public notice (subd. 3).

**(primary: [Minn. Stat. 471.345](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/471.345))**

The law's "contract" (subd. 2) is a purchase or rental of supplies, materials or
equipment, or construction, alteration, repair or maintenance of property.
Professional services are not in that definition. A board may buy them by
negotiation, or by a request for proposals if its own policy says so. Many
bodies apply their own quote rules to services, so ask for the purchasing
policy.

**Other buyers:**

- **School districts** follow 471.345's thresholds for work, labor and
  property (Minn. Stat. 123B.52), with the same reading for services.
- **Charter schools** must adopt a procurement policy, and must procure
  competitively above $25,000. Between $25,000 and $175,000 they may negotiate
  directly with two or more bids, and the policy must forbid splitting purchases
  to stay under a threshold
  **(primary: [Minn. Stat. 124E.26, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/124E.26))**.
- **Regional public library systems** are governed by boards with exclusive
  control of their funds (Minn. Stat. 134.20) and buy as political
  subdivisions.

**Consequence:** every Binding Generations service is far below $25,000. A
director can buy it after a conversation and a board vote. The sale is a
relationship and an agenda item, not a bid.

## 2. What the buyer asks of the vendor

- **A W-9**, and often the body's own vendor form
  ([Minnesota Management and Budget vendor forms](https://mn.gov/mmb/accounting/swift/vendor-resources/vendor-forms/)).
- **A certificate of insurance.** Limits are the buyer's choice, not the
  state's. The counties' insurance trust tells members to set their own
  ([MCIT](https://mcit.org/blog/resource/certificates-of-insurance/)). One city
  example: Saint Paul's contractor standard is $500,000 combined single limit.
  $1 million per occurrence and $2 million aggregate is the common private
  default, and it is the safe amount to quote for.
- **A contract the board approves**, usually on the consent agenda of a monthly
  meeting.
- **For any contract handling the body's data**, the Government Data Practices
  notice of 13.05, subd. 11 (see the law science note on
  [government data and a contracted library administrator](../../../../../../../../../../../law-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-government-data-and-a-contracted-library-administrator.md)).

## 3. Getting paid

Minn. Stat. 471.425 requires a political subdivision to pay within **35 days**
of receiving the goods, services or invoice (whichever is later) if its board
meets monthly, or **45 days** if it meets less often. It must dispute a
defective invoice within 10 days, and owes interest on late payment
**(primary: [Minn. Stat. 471.425](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/471.425);
[Office of the State Auditor](https://www.osa.state.mn.us/audit-resources/audit-guidance/avoiding-pitfalls-articles/prompt-payment-of-local-government-bills/))**.
A small firm can plan cash on 45 days.

## 4. When to sell: the budget calendars

- **Cities, and so city library boards**, budget by the calendar year.
  - The preliminary levy is certified by September 30.
  - Truth-in-taxation hearings run late November to December.
  - The final levy is certified by December 29
    ([Minnesota Department of Revenue](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/truth-taxation)).
  - A service for next year is proposed in **summer**, before the preliminary
    levy. A service this year is paid from the current budget's discretionary
    lines.
- **School districts** run July 1 to June 30 and adopt budgets before July 1
  ([House Research, *Minnesota School Finance*](https://www.house.mn.gov/hrd/pubs/mnschfin.pdf)).
  Sell in **spring**.
- **The Arrowhead Library System's governing board** meets the second Thursday
  of February, April, June, August, October and December at 10 a.m.
  ([ALS events](https://www.alslib.info/events/)).
  - The next meetings are October 8 and December 10, 2026.
  - A system-level offer should be in the director's hands a month before.

## 6. The cost of being a vendor

Insurance for a one-person consulting and education practice, from insurers'
published averages (2026):

- **General liability:** about $32 to $50 a month
  ([Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/consulting-business-insurance/cost);
  [NEXT](https://www.nextinsurance.com/general-liability-insurance/consultants/);
  Hiscox reports a median range of $22–$109).
- **Professional liability:** about $55 to $62 a month
  ([Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/errors-omissions/cost);
  [NEXT](https://www.nextinsurance.com/professional-liability-insurance/cost/)).

Together, about **$700 to $1,500 a year**, before underwriting. Workshops with
children may raise the general liability premium.

## 7. What follows for pricing and selling

- Price for a **board vote, not a bid**. A price the director can explain in one
  line on the consent agenda sells: $1,200 flat for a policy. Very small
  libraries (operating spending under $50,000) are better served through their
  regional system than one by one.
- Offer **retainers to charter schools** only well under $25,000 a year, and
  never as split purchases.
- Put the **13.05 notice, insurance and W-9** in the first reply to an
  interested director, so the paperwork never delays the vote.
- Time **system-level offers to board calendars**. For the Arrowhead Library
  System, December 2026 is the realistic first meeting.
- Carry **insurance before the first signed engagement**. Quote about $1,000 a
  year into first-year costs.
