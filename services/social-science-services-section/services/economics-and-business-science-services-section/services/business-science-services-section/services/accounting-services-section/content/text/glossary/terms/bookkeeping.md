Bookkeeping is the systematic recording and maintenance of an entity's admitted
financial transactions. It is the record layer from which
[financial statements](./financial-statements.md) are developed, but accurate
entry alone is insufficient: recognition policy, adjustments, reconciliation,
measurement, consolidation, and presentation still govern the statements.

Operational bookkeeping includes preserving source identifiers, recording and
posting entries, maintaining receivable and payable detail, reconciling custody
and bank evidence, controlling duplicates, and retaining records needed for
reporting and tax work. Software is an implementation choice; it does not decide
the entity boundary or accounting policy by itself.
