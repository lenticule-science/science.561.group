Xero is cloud accounting software founded in New Zealand in 2006, as recorded
in its [company factsheet](https://www.xero.com/us/media/factsheet/). Product
features, plan names, and regional availability differ, so the exact regional
offering matters more than a general market-presence claim.

## What It Does

Xero product configurations can provide invoicing, bill workflows, bank-feed
intake and reconciliation, expenses, purchase orders, reporting, and
multi-currency features. Availability, automation, limits, and third-party
integrations depend on region, plan, institution, and connected service.

## Who It's For

Xero targets small-business accounting and collaboration. Fit depends on entity
count, roles, transaction volume, currencies, inventory, payroll jurisdiction,
bank connectivity, reporting, export, integrations, and control requirements.

## Double-Entry Bookkeeping

Like QuickBooks, Xero abstracts
[double-entry bookkeeping](../../../glossary/terms/double-entry-bookkeeping.md)
behind invoices, bills, bank feeds, and reconciliation workflows. Those actions
construct [debits and credits](../../../glossary/terms/debit-and-credit.md) in
the [ledger](../../../glossary/terms/ledger.md); manual
[journal](../../../glossary/terms/journal.md) entries remain available for
authorized adjustments. Interface convenience does not validate account mapping
or economic classification.

## Strengths

- Transaction-oriented invoicing, bill, bank-feed, and reconciliation workflows
- Role-based collaboration and a third-party integration ecosystem
- Plan-dependent multi-currency and reporting capabilities

## Limitations

- Payroll, tax, inventory, payments, and bank connectivity vary by jurisdiction
  and may require another service
- Plan and integration boundaries can distribute accounting evidence and control
  responsibility across several vendors
- Export, migration, attachment custody, and API completeness require testing
  against the exact operating record

## Cost Model

Xero is subscription-based. Its regional
[pricing page](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing-plans/) is the dated source for
plan names, feature limits, users, add-ons, and promotions. Total cost also
includes migration, integrations, payments, payroll, support, control operation,
and exit requirements.
