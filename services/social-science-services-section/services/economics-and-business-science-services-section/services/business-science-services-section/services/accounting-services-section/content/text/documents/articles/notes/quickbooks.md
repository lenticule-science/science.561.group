# QuickBooks

QuickBooks is a family of Intuit accounting products. Product, region, plan,
user, and usage limits matter: the current
[official product comparison](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/product-system-requirements/quickbooks-product-comparison/L5Y1oActr_US_en_US)
should be checked before treating “QuickBooks” as one feature set.

## What It Does

Depending on product and plan, QuickBooks can provide invoicing, bills and
expenses, bank feeds and reconciliation, inventory, payroll, payments, and
reporting. QuickBooks Online and current Desktop/Enterprise products have
different data models, availability, limits, and migration tradeoffs.

## Who It's For

The family serves several operating scales, but a plan name does not establish
fit. Evaluate entity count, currencies, inventory, projects, approval roles,
audit trail, integrations, data export, payroll jurisdictions, and concurrent
users against the exact product.

## Double-Entry Bookkeeping

QuickBooks abstracts [double-entry bookkeeping](../../../glossary/terms/double-entry-bookkeeping.md)
behind transaction-oriented workflows. Users can record invoices, expenses, and
payments while the configured product constructs the underlying
[debits and credits](../../../glossary/terms/debit-and-credit.md). The
[ledger](../../../glossary/terms/ledger.md) remains the accounting record, even
when a day-to-day user does not enter a manual
[journal](../../../glossary/terms/journal.md) entry. Abstraction reduces exposed
mechanics but does not remove the need to review account mapping and reports.

## Strengths

- Transaction-oriented workflows can generate the underlying double-entry
- Several products offer bank feeds, reconciliation, reports, and integrations
- Accountant access, roles, classes, locations, inventory, and user limits can
  be evaluated against published plan details

## Limitations

- Export coverage, migration fidelity, attachment custody, and API access vary by
  product and must be tested before dependence on the hosted or proprietary data
  model is accepted
- Transaction-oriented screens can obscure posting effects from users who do
  not review account mappings and ledger reports
- Product names and feature availability change, so purchasing and migration
  decisions require a dated comparison

## Cost Model

QuickBooks Online is subscription-based; Desktop and Enterprise offerings have
different commercial terms. Plans, promotions, limits, payroll, payments, and
add-ons change. Use Intuit's current
[plans and limits](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/intuit-billing-updates/upgrade-downgrade-quickbooks-online-subscription/L7LN5zzbs_US_en_US)
rather than copying prices into a durable theory note.
