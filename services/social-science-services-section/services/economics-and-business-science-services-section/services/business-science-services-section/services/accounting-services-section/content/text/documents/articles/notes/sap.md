SAP SE supplies a family of enterprise applications, including SAP S/4HANA and
SAP Cloud ERP. SAP's [company history](https://www.sap.com/about/company/history.html)
records that five former IBM employees founded SystemAnalyse
Programmentwicklung in Germany in 1972. Current product scope belongs to the
[S/4HANA product documentation](https://www.sap.com/products/erp/s4hana.html),
not to the historical name alone.

## What It Does

SAP finance applications can cover the
[ledger](../../../glossary/terms/ledger.md),
[accounts payable](../../../glossary/terms/accounts-payable.md),
[accounts receivable](../../../glossary/terms/accounts-receivable.md), asset
accounting, management accounting, and consolidation, integrated with other
configured business processes. The licensed edition and implemented scope
determine which capability is actually present.

## Who It's For

S/4HANA configurations can support multi-entity, multi-currency, and parallel
reporting needs. Suitability depends on process scope, deployment edition,
control design, implementation capacity, integrations, and total operating cost;
organization size alone does not decide it.

## Double-Entry Bookkeeping

SAP accounting configurations implement
[double-entry bookkeeping](../../../glossary/terms/double-entry-bookkeeping.md)
through postings to a [ledger](../../../glossary/terms/ledger.md). Account
determination, organizational dimensions, roles, workflow, audit logging, and
parallel ledgers can support controls and multiple reporting bases. They do not
enforce a sound control merely by existing: configuration, access assignment,
master data, interfaces, and review decide whether the control operates.

## Strengths

- Organizational dimensions, currencies, ledgers, workflows, and integrations
  can be configured for complex reporting structures
- Procurement, receipt, invoice, payment, and accounting events can be linked by
  configured process flows; a purchase order alone does not universally create
  the final accounting entry
- Roles, logs, approvals, and parallel ledgers can participate in a control
  design when configuration and operation support them

## Limitations

- Broad implementations commonly require specialized configuration and
  migration expertise
- Process and configuration breadth can create substantial implementation and
  migration work
- Customization and integrations create continuing testing and upgrade duties
- Role-specific training and operating documentation are material parts of the
  implementation

## Cost Model

SAP products are proprietary and offered under product- and deployment-specific
commercial terms. A cost comparison must include licensing or subscription,
implementation, data migration, integrations, controls, training, support, and
upgrades; no universal ratio between those costs is defensible here.
