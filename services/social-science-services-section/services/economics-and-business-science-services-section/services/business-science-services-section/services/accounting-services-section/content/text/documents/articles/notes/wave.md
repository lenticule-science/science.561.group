Wave is cloud accounting and invoicing software for small businesses. H&R Block
[completed its acquisition](https://investors.hrblock.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hr-block-announces-fiscal-2020-first-quarter-results)
of Wave in 2019. “Free” no longer describes the whole product: the current
[Wave pricing page](https://www.waveapps.com/pricing) separates a zero-price
Starter plan, a paid Pro plan, transaction fees, and optional services.

## What It Does

Wave provides bookkeeping records, invoicing, bills, dashboards, and reports.
Automatic bank import, receipt capture, users, payments, payroll, and advisory
services depend on plan, eligibility, country, or an additional fee.

## Who It's For

Wave targets small-business and self-employed workflows. Suitability depends on
entity and country eligibility, transaction volume, currencies, inventory,
payroll, payments, roles, reporting, export, integrations, and evidence-retention
requirements rather than an organization label alone.

## Double-Entry Bookkeeping

Wave implements [double-entry bookkeeping](../../../glossary/terms/double-entry-bookkeeping.md)
behind transaction-oriented screens. The underlying
[journal](../../../glossary/terms/journal.md),
[ledger](../../../glossary/terms/ledger.md), and
[chart of accounts](./chart-of-accounts.md) still determine the reports, so
categorization and reconciliation require accounting review even when debits and
credits are not shown in the primary workflow.

## Strengths

- Transaction-oriented bookkeeping and invoicing workflows
- Plan-dependent automation, bank connectivity, receipt, payment, payroll, and
  collaboration features
- Reports generated from an underlying double-entry record

## Limitations

- Inventory, currencies, reporting, roles, integrations, and payroll must be
  checked against the exact plan and country rather than assumed from the brand
- Payment, payroll, bank-feed, and advisory services introduce separate
  eligibility, fee, evidence, and control boundaries
- Export completeness, attachment custody, migration, and API access require an
  exit test before the hosted record becomes authoritative

## Cost Model

Wave uses plan subscriptions plus fees for eligible payments, payroll, receipt
capture, and advisory services. Prices and feature boundaries change; consult
the dated pricing page rather than treating this note as a quote.
