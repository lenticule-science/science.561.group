# Economics and Business Science Services Section

This is the home for OECD FORD 5.2, economics and business.

Economics, finance, monetary economics, business, and marketing are narrower
fields below this section. A market is an object of economic and business
research, not a parallel science and not a second top-level service section.
Operational sales, accounting, treasury, and marketing work remains in the
appropriate operations or enterprise section.

## Cross-field business-economics question

[Work Science's Gonzo Ghettoism research](../sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/gonzo-ghettoism-science-services-section/README.md)
transfers the established **Gonzo mode of radically involved, self-implicating
practice** into economic hustle within historically specific American ghetto
conditions. This is not an empirical *drug-plus-clowning* type or generic
entrepreneurial improvisation with a new name. The cited historical Gonzo
tradition defines the modifier; slang/informal work research defines the
base hustle and its conditions. Individual drug and clowning practices
remain economically material contexts and possible mechanisms, not admission
criteria.

**Business and economics research task:** explain whether a person who
*inhabits and changes the very economic spectacle they participate in* can
find opportunities, audiences, transactions, finance or durable value that
conventional formal institutions cannot produce or recognize. Document
the actual work, customer and receiver, attention, costs and net returns,
risks, third-party injuries, full distribution including failures, and
the distinction between short-term liquidity and long-term assets.
No superiority, causal effect, representative prevalence or profitable
micro-enterprise has been established.

The Work Science section owns the Gonzo work philosophy and source register;
Economics and Business is a potential qualified consumer of its empirical
economic results. An operations/business product, legal authority and independent
customer receiving remain distinct, not consequences of publishing this theory.
