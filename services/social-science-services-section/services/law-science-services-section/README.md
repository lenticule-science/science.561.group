# Law Science Services Section

This is the home for OECD FORD 5.5, research on law. `Legalism` is a
topic or theoretical position, not the name of the research field.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

## Captive birth and hereditary status: cross-disciplinary source law

The [*partus sequitur ventrem* glossary](content/text/glossary/terms/partus-sequitur-ventrem.md) studies the jurisdiction-specific rule of maternal inherited enslavement, grounded in the original 1662 Virginia statute, while [future increase as property interest](content/text/glossary/terms/future-increase-property-interest.md) distinguishes contingent legal rights in unborn issue from actual captive births or specific coerced conceptions. These are Law Science definitions, not copied into the Sociology field.

The original historical-demography, kinship, property and settler social reproduction case studies, including the directly relevant original source on coerced pairings, are retained under [Sociology Science](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/README.md) with original annotations, claims, and manuscripts.
