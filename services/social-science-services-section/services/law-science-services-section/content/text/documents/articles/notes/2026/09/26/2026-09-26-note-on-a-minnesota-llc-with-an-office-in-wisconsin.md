# A Minnesota LLC with an office in Wisconsin

The companion note, [Minnesota law for forming and running an LLC](2026-09-26-note-on-minnesota-law-for-forming-and-running-an-llc.md),
covers forming the company. This note covers what follows when the company's
working office is in Superior, Wisconsin while its members live in Duluth.
Items read from a statute, form or agency page are marked **primary**.

## 1. Two addresses, two rules

- **The registered office stays in Minnesota.** "A business entity shall
  continuously maintain a registered office in this state" (Minn. Stat.
  5.36, subd. 1), at an actual office location, not solely a post office box
  **(primary: [5.36](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/5.36))**. A
  Superior office cannot be a Minnesota LLC's registered office. A member's
  Duluth home, a staffed Duluth coworking address, or a commercial registered
  agent (about $50–200 a year, search synthesis) can.
- **The principal office may be anywhere.** A Minnesota LLC's principal place
  of business is its principal executive office "whether or not the office is
  located in this state" (Minn. Stat. 322C.0102, subd. 21) **(primary:
  [322C.0102](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/322C.0102))**. The
  articles and the annual renewal each ask for a principal executive office
  street address; that is where the Superior office goes.

## 2. Wisconsin registration

A company chartered in one state that transacts business in another
registers there as a foreign company. Wisconsin's LLC law (chapter 183,
effective 2023) lists what is not transacting business: litigation, internal
meetings, bank accounts, selling through independent contractors, soliciting
orders accepted outside the state, isolated transactions, interstate commerce
and similar (Wis. Stat. 183.0905) **(primary:
[183.0905](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/183/ix/0905))**.
A rented and staffed office is not on that list, so a Superior office means
registering:

- **Form 521**, Foreign Limited Liability Company Registration Statement, with
  the Department of Financial Institutions: **$100** **(primary:
  [Form 521](https://dfi.wi.gov/Documents/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Forms/CORP521.pdf))**.
  It names a Wisconsin registered agent at a Wisconsin street address; the
  Superior office itself serves if someone is there in business hours.
- **An annual report** each year by March 31: **$65 online** ($80 on paper)
  for a foreign LLC **(primary:
  [Form 5 instructions, rev. May 2026](https://dfi.wi.gov/Documents/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Forms/CORP5i.pdf))**.
- Transacting business before registering costs the fees that would have
  been paid plus a penalty of half of them or $5,000, whichever is less (Wis.
  Stat. 183.0902); members' limited liability is not lost.

## 3. Wisconsin taxes

- **Partnership return.** Every partnership, and every LLC taxed as one, with
  income from Wisconsin sources "regardless of the amount" files Wisconsin
  Form 3, including for business transacted in Wisconsin **(primary:
  [2025 Form 3 instructions](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2025/2025-Form3-Inst.pdf))**.
- **Pass-through withholding.** A partnership pays Wisconsin withholding on
  income allocable to nonresident partners (Minnesota residents are
  nonresidents) at **7.9%** on Form PW-1, due with Form 3, except for a
  partner whose Wisconsin share is under $2,000, or who files an exemption
  affidavit (Form PW-2) **(primary: same;
  [PW-1 instructions](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/TaxForms2017through2019/FormPW-1-inst-2025.pdf))**.
- **Which income is Wisconsin's.** Wisconsin sources receipts from services
  to where the purchaser receives the benefit (Wis. Stat. 71.25(9)(dh)),
  market-based sourcing **(primary:
  [71.25(9)(dh)](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/71/iv/25/9/dh))**.
  So an office in Superior makes the company file in Wisconsin, but income
  from customers outside Wisconsin is not Wisconsin income because the desk
  is there. (The provision is written for corporate apportionment; a
  Wisconsin preparer confirms how it applies to a Form 3.)
- **No reciprocity.** The Minnesota–Wisconsin income tax reciprocity
  agreement ended with tax year 2010 and has not been restored
  ([Wisconsin DOR](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/ise-mnrecipro.aspx));
  it covered commuters' wages, not business income. A Minnesota resident who
  pays Wisconsin tax on Wisconsin-source income claims Minnesota's credit
  for tax paid to another state
  ([Minnesota Revenue](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/taxes-paid-another-state-credit)).
- **Sales tax.** Wisconsin taxes specified digital goods, including e-books,
  at 5% plus county tax. The $100,000 remote-seller threshold applies only
  to sellers with no physical presence; an office in Wisconsin means a
  seller's permit from the first sale to a Wisconsin buyer, through the
  Business Tax Registration ($20 for two years)
  ([Wisconsin remote sellers](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/Businesses/remote-sellers.aspx);
  [BTR](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-btr.aspx)).

## 4. The other way round: a Wisconsin LLC

A Wisconsin LLC costs $130 online to form and $25 a year to report, and
registers in Minnesota as a foreign LLC to do its Minnesota business: a
certificate of authority at $205 online, and a free annual renewal (Minn.
Stat. 322C.0802; the Secretary of State's fee schedule). Minnesota's list of
what is not transacting business (Minn. Stat. 322C.0803) leaves out owning
income-producing property and repeated transactions, so land contracts in
Minnesota need the registration **(primary:
[322C.0803](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/322C.0803))**. The tax
filings are the same as §3, since they follow where the office and the
members are, not where the company was chartered.

## 5. What each arrangement costs a year

| Arrangement | One-time state fees | Yearly state fees | Returns each year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minnesota LLC, office in Duluth | $155 | $0 | federal 1065; Minnesota M3 |
| Minnesota LLC, office in Superior | $155 + $100 (WI registration) | $65 (WI report) + a Minnesota registered office if no member's address is used ($0–200) | federal 1065; Minnesota M3; Wisconsin Form 3 and PW-1; Wisconsin sales tax returns once selling taxable goods to Wisconsin buyers |
| Wisconsin LLC, office in Superior | $130 + $205 (MN authority) | $25 (WI report) + a Minnesota registered office ($0–200) | the same as the row above |

Rent is the comparison that decides it: the Wisconsin filings add a few
hundred dollars a year in fees and preparer time, set against the
difference in rent.
