A Robinson list records people who do not want to receive direct marketing.
The name comes from Robinson Crusoe, alone on his island.

- **Germany's Robinsonliste** is run by the I.D.I. Verband, an industry
  association. It covers mail, phone, email and fax, is free for people to
  join, and is funded by donations and association dues.
- **The UK's Mailing Preference Service** (since 1983) is funded by the
  direct-mail industry.
- **The UK's Telephone Preference Service** is legally enforceable. TPS Ltd
  runs it under contract to the Information Commissioner's Office, and it is
  funded entirely by the fees marketers pay to use it.

Together with the [National Do Not Call Registry](national-do-not-call-registry.md)
they show that such lists are free to join and paid for by the senders who must
check them.
