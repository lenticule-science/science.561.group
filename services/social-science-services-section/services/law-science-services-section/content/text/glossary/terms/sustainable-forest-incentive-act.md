The Sustainable Forest Incentive Act (SFIA, Minn. Stat. ch. 290C) pays an owner of 20 or more contiguous forested acres an annual amount per acre in exchange for a covenant not to develop the land and to manage it under a forest plan.
The Department of Revenue's 2026 rates for up to 1,920 acres are $12.13 an acre on an 8-year covenant, $16.79 on a 20-year covenant and $21.46 on a 50-year covenant; payments are taxable income, and the department expects 2027 payments to fall under legislation enacted in 2025.
Enrolled land keeps its ordinary tax class (class 2b for vacant forest) and cannot also be [managed forest land](./managed-forest-land.md).

Forty acres of northern forest at a county value near $1,000 an acre pays a few hundred dollars of property tax a year, less than the $485 an 8-year covenant pays.
The program is therefore how rural forest land can carry its own taxes.
