Property essential to self-support (PESS) is property the Social Security Administration leaves out of a Supplemental Security Income recipient's countable resources because it supports the recipient.
"Property essential to self-support used in a trade or business is excluded from resources regardless of value or rate of return effective May 1, 1990" (POMS SI 01130.501).
Nonbusiness income-producing property is excluded only up to $6,000 of equity, and only if it returns at least six percent a year (20 CFR 416.1222); property that produces goods for the recipient's own use, such as a garden plot or livestock for the table, is excluded up to $6,000 of equity (20 CFR 416.1224).

The exclusion follows the property, not the business's form.
Land a recipient holds directly and uses in a business they run (a timber stand, a sugarbush, lots held as a land dealer's stock) can be excluded as PESS.
An ownership interest in an LLC is not trade-or-business property: SSA's regional counsel treats a recipient's 100% interest in an LLC as a countable resource (POMS PS 01810.011, 2018), so the same land held through the recipient's LLC is counted through the value of the interest.
The recipient's home is excluded separately, whatever its value (20 CFR 416.1212).
