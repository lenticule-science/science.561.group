Managed forest land is property-tax class 2c under Minn. Stat. 273.13.
It takes at least 20 contiguous forested acres (up to 1,920 per taxpayer statewide) with a forest management plan registered with the Minnesota DNR and less than ten years old, applied for by May 1 for the next year's taxes, and taxes it at a class rate of 0.65% of market value against the 1% of class 2b rural vacant land.
Land in class 2c cannot also be enrolled in the [Sustainable Forest Incentive Act](./sustainable-forest-incentive-act.md), and small outbuildings under 300 square feet are allowed.
A DNR forester writes the required plan for $300 plus $10 an acre, and the DNR cost-shares much forest work at 50% to 75%.
