The Do Not Train Registry is run by Spawning. Creators find their works in
large training datasets (through its "Have I Been Trained" search over LAION-5B)
and opt them out. Signatory trainers, including Stability AI and Hugging Face,
drop opted-out files in their data pipelines. By 2023 more than 80 million
works had been opted out, most through bulk partnerships with ArtStation and
Shutterstock. Spawning also publishes `ai.txt`, a robots.txt-style file for
declaring a site's AI-use preferences.

Honoring it is voluntary, but it saves trainers money: files they skip are
files they never download. That is the lesson for an
[opt-out registry](opt-out-registry.md) that no law makes mandatory. Compliance
has to pay for itself or protect the complier.
