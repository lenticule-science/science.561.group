An assumed name (a "doing business as" or DBA name) is a name under which a business operates that is not its legal name. It creates no new entity: contracts signed and money received under the assumed name belong to the business that registered it, and share its liability.

In Minnesota a business files a certificate of assumed name with the Secretary of State before using the name ($30 by mail, $50 online) and publishes it in a qualified legal newspaper of the county of its principal place of business for two consecutive issues (Minn. Stat. 333.01). A bank will generally accept deposits payable to a registered assumed name. See [the 2026-09-26 note on Minnesota law for forming and running an LLC](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-minnesota-law-for-forming-and-running-an-llc.md), §6.
