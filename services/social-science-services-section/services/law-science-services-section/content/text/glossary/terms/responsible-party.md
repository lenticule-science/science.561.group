The responsible party is the individual that an application for an employer identification number (IRS Form SS-4, or the IRS's online application) names as the person who ultimately owns or controls the entity, or who exercises ultimate effective control over it, with enough control over its funds and assets to direct them. For an entity other than a government body it is a natural person, and the SS-4 instructions state that a nominee is never the responsible party.

The IRS's online EIN application works only when the responsible party has a Social Security number or an ITIN, and issues one EIN per responsible party per day; otherwise the entity applies on paper by fax or mail ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)). In a manager-managed LLC a manager who in fact holds authority over the company and its accounts is a responsible party. See [the 2026-09-26 note on Minnesota law for forming and running an LLC](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-minnesota-law-for-forming-and-running-an-llc.md), §8.
