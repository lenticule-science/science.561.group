A registered office is the address a business entity keeps on file with its state of formation, where it can be found and where legal papers (summonses, official notices) can be delivered to it. A registered agent is a person or firm named to receive those papers at that office.

In Minnesota every LLC has a registered office and may have a registered agent (Minn. Stat. 322C.0113). The office must be an actual office location; a post office box may be added as a mailing address but cannot be the office alone (Minn. Stat. 5.36). A home where the business is run qualifies. The address is public in the Secretary of State's business records, which is why some businesses use a commercial registered-agent service or a coworking address instead of a home. See [the 2026-09-26 note on Minnesota law for forming and running an LLC](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/26/2026-09-26-note-on-minnesota-law-for-forming-and-running-an-llc.md), §2.
