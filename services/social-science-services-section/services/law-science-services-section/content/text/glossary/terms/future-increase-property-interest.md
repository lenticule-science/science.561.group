# future increase as property interest

A legal-economic claim to possible future descendants of a person held enslaved, including legal assignments of children's value separate from the mother's currently assessed value. In antebellum disputes some courts and claimants distinguished a person's present value from potential future reproductive increase.

## Scope and counterexamples

Do not automatically treat an anticipated interest as the ownership of an actual future person across every jurisdiction and time. Alphonso's 2023 study is a preprint and underlying case law needs line-level review for a publishable legal history. The rule of maternal status and the financial treatment of future issue are distinct historical propositions.
