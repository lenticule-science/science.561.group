An opt-out registry records who has said no. Senders check it before calling,
mailing, emailing, or (for works) training on content. Working examples are the
US [National Do Not Call Registry](national-do-not-call-registry.md), Europe's
[Robinson lists](robinson-list.md), and Spawning's
[Do Not Train Registry](do-not-train-registry.md).

The working ones share four traits.

- **Joining is free.** People never pay to be left alone.
- **Senders pay.** The DNC Registry charges telemarketers per area code. The
  UK's TPS and MPS are funded by the marketing industry.
- **Checking is mandatory or made worth it.** Law requires the DNC Registry. For
  the Do Not Train Registry, contracts require it and skipping opted-out files
  saves the trainer bandwidth.
- **The list cannot be read out.** Checks are scrubs or yes/no lookups. The
  failure case is the [National Do Not Email Registry](national-do-not-email-registry.md),
  rejected in 2004 because the list itself would have been a gift to spammers.

A registry that meets these four conditions for contact by AI agents does not
yet exist.
