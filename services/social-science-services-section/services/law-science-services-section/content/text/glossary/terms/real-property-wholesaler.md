A real property wholesaler, as 2023 Wisconsin Act 208 defines the term, is a person who enters into a purchase agreement as the buyer and intends to assign the buyer's rights under it to a third party for consideration.
The act, signed 2024-03-22, requires the wholesaler to give the seller written notice of that status no later than entering the purchase agreement, and to give the assignee the same notice; a seller not given notice may rescind before closing and keep any deposit, and an assignee may rescind and recover option fees paid (search synthesis: WRA Legal Hotline, June 2024).

Minnesota has no statute of this kind as of 2026-09-26; there, assignment is governed by the purchase agreement's own terms and by the broker definition.
Several other states adopted wholesaler disclosure or licensing rules in 2024 and 2025 (see [contract assignment](../../../../../economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/contract-assignment.md)).
