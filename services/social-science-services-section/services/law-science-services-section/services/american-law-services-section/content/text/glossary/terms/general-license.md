A general license is a self-executing OFAC authorization, published in regulation or as a standalone document, that permits a defined category of otherwise-prohibited transactions with a sanctioned party without an individual application, subject to whatever conditions and expiration date the license itself states.

A general license differs from a specific license, which OFAC issues only on individual written application and which authorizes only the named applicant's stated transaction; a general license instead applies automatically to anyone whose transaction fits its published terms.

A wind-down general license authorizes closing out existing dealings, not continuing normal operations, and its expiration converts previously licensed activity back into a blocked-property violation the moment the stated deadline passes. Counter Terrorism General License 36, issued 2026-08-26 alongside Autistici/Inventati's designation and titled "Authorizing the Wind Down of Transactions Involving Autistici Inventati," authorized wind-down transactions only through 2026-09-25; A/I closed its services on 2026-09-06, before that deadline.
