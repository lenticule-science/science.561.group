A specially designated global terrorist (SDGT) is a status the Office of Foreign Assets Control assigns under 31 C.F.R. Part 594, pursuant to Executive Order 13224 as amended by Executive Order 13886, to a foreign person or entity found to have committed, or to pose a significant risk of committing, acts of terrorism, or to provide support to such persons; the assignment places the party on the Specially Designated Nationals List and blocks all of its property and interests in property within United States jurisdiction.

SDGT/SDN listing is a Treasury-administered blocked-property and prohibited-transaction status distinct from a Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) designation, which the State Department makes under a separate statute (INA §219, 8 U.S.C. §1189) and which is the list 18 U.S.C. §2339B's material-support offense attaches to; a party can be SDGT-listed without being FTO-listed, and the two regimes carry different consequences for a third party dealing with it.

Because the SDN List is a blocking status rather than a criminal-liability trigger by itself, a US person's exposure for continuing to transact with an SDGT-listed party runs through the International Emergency Economic Powers Act and 31 C.F.R. Part 594's own prohibited-transaction rules, and the only lawful path to any continued dealing is an OFAC-issued license. The August 2026 designation of Autistici/Inventati under this authority is documented in [a research note on sanctions, designation, and hosting-provider law](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/08/2026-09-08-note-on-sanctions-designation-and-hosting-provider-law.md) [@eo132242001; @eo138862019; @gtsr2026].
