# A Model of emsenn: Work, Collaboration, and Method

## 0. What this note is, and the question it answers

The Leader posed the study directly:
build an honest model of who they are, what they do, and how they fail at those things — specifically to test the recognition that *the more closely they attempt to collaborate with the human people around them, who are overwhelmingly American, the worse and more off-track things go* — and to use the model to design AI collaborators that let their energy flow into the world without depending on human follow-through that does not come.

This note is the model.
The facts of the life live in the consolidated biography;
the design that the model motivates lives in the Synthetic Corporation note;
this note is the *mechanism* — the confirmed pattern and, more importantly, *why* it happens, because the why determines the fix.

The headline finding: **the recognition is confirmed, but the mechanism is not "Americanness as essence."**
The pattern is real and dated (§2), but it decomposes into five specific mechanisms (§3), each of which implies a different remedy, and one of which the Leader themselves sharpened during the study into the deepest constraint of all (consent asymmetry).
Crucially, the study also found that the Leader's *AI* collaboration has its own distinct, documented failure modes (§5) — which matters enormously, because the whole point of the study was to design AI collaborators, and a design that ignores how AI has already failed the Leader would repeat it.

## 1. Evidence base and the git-tempo demonstration

The model rests on eleven parallel research passes over ~600 files spanning 2008–2026, plus the public web.
One piece of evidence is worth stating first because it is the whole thesis in a single artifact:
**the repository's own commit tempo.**
From 2019 through 2025 the pattern is sporadic bursts — twenty to forty commits in a month, then months of silence — the "plan, organize, miss the timing, plan again" rhythm rendered as data.
Then June–August 2026: roughly ten thousand commits in three months, once AI agents entered the loop.
The Leader's throughput bottleneck was never will or vision; it was *hands.*
This single fact reframes everything the model is for: the study is not diagnosing a motivation problem, it is diagnosing a *coupling* problem — how the Leader's abundant will gets (or fails to get) coupled to execution.

## 2. The confirmed pattern: the collaboration chronology

The gradient the Leader named is real and traceable across the whole life.
A compressed chronology (full detail in the organizing-history and personal-log research from the same session; dates and quotes DOCUMENTED or ARCHIVE):

- **2009, Sedazad**: the free-voice group's rebrand and schism; ended in the Leader's exile, a death associated with an earlier check-in campaign, and the epitaph *"one person can only do so much."* The pattern's origin.
- **2014–2018, Sam Jones House / Sam Jones Media**: a community house ("immediately successful") that the Leader's disabling accident removed them from; "by the time I was able to get around on crutches, I'd found that the local scene had largely moved on." Community did not hold the place.
- **2020–2021, WebGAZ**: the cleanest fully-documented plan→fizzle cycle; launched with excitement, "the craft circles never got above 1 other person beside me"; explicit abandonment — "I thought I was developing an audience that was ready to become a community, but now feel like I've developed an audience that wants moral guilt or absolution." The replacement solo plan (a speculation newsletter) died in weeks because Stripe would not verify the account — the documentation problem strangling even the solo escape.
- **Sept 2023, the woodchips**: the thesis in miniature — "I reached out to basically everyone I knew, in the area, asking if they would be able to come help move woodchips. A couple weeks passed and no one was able to bring themselves to help." The landlord then ordered the garden destroyed; separately, the Leader injured themselves moving forty cubic yards of mulch alone. The somatic cost of the follow-through gap, paid in the body.
- **2022–2025, Chunka Luta Network**: the one durable multi-person collaboration in the whole record — but *joined, not founded*, with external structure and other people driving logistics. Real wins (the 2023 Winter Drive: a box truck of firewood through a storm; elder housing; land offered in reciprocity). The asset list is the diagnostic document, though: "Aleks, Gerbatious, Kevcom, Justu, Mel, Violet all will but need to be told what to do… I dropped the ball… if I rekindle the relationship enough." People exist and are willing; what is missing is dispatch.
- **Feb–Dec 2024, Pine Ridge**: the fullest-commitment collaboration and the worst catastrophe — four-party planning, funded relocation, cabin purchased, creek restoration begun; then the discovery that the collaborating family ran an armed trafficking operation, the confrontation to protect endangered children that made the family targets, the visiting "professional activists" who leaked their location, and the flight to the Great Lakes. The closest collaboration produced the worst outcome.
- **2025–2026, Duluth**: solo output at maximum; family present but log-invisible; the 3D printer (bought 2026-01-17) with a family sales plan that produced zero revenue and zero planning documentation in six-plus months — the point in the trajectory where imagined plans stopped being written down at all, "because the potential things almost never happen."

Self-summary, Aug 2025, public: "literally got run out of two towns by two different armed gangs, since 2021, and that's just the stuff not-too-bad to document publicly."

The gradient is confirmed: the closer the collaboration with surrounding Americans, the worse the outcome, and Pine Ridge — closest — was worst.

## 3. The failure mechanics — five mechanisms, not one essence

The pattern is real; "Americanness as an essence" is not the mechanism.
The study decomposed it into five, each with a different implied remedy.
This decomposition is the most useful thing the model produced, because "Americans can't collaborate" implies despair while these five imply engineering.

1. **Survival-priced timing versus discretionary timing.**
   The Leader's deadlines are somatic — undocumented, disabled, hunted, the daily visceral pressure of pursuing freedom as a *necessity*.
   The people around them (family included) hold means of survivance the current systems provide, so freedom is a *value-add*, not a necessity; their deadlines are elective.
   The 2020 election emptied the Leader's food-distribution crew overnight;
   collaborators reliably "have their own lives and stuff."
   Willingness is rarely absent; *priced urgency* almost always is.

2. **No dispatcher.**
   The CLN asset list is the proof: people exist who "need to be told what to do," and nobody converts willingness into specific, small, timed, followed-up asks.
   The Leader cannot be that dispatcher — disabled a quarter of days, doing the load-bearing work besides.
   Every "I dropped the ball" in the archive is a dispatch failure, not a willingness failure.

3. **Task-type mismatch.**
   The 2024 Pine Ridge transcripts split cleanly:
   the three-way sessions where the Leader, Es, and Terran *co-created outward-facing work* (the herb-economics letter) crackle with energy;
   the sessions negotiating *internal logistics* (camp setup, diets, itineraries) drain into fatigue-toned agreement ("fair," "cool," "yup").
   Same three people, opposite energy, depending on whether the object is shared creation or mutual management.
   The Leader kept assigning logistics to people whose demonstrated capacity was co-creation.

4. **The self-reinforcing filter.**
   From "False Profits" (2020) through "A Shift in Praxis" (2021), each failed collaboration was theorized into a stronger structural claim that collaboration with the non-radicalized is inherently extractive — which justified withdrawal, which starved the next attempt.
   The theory is substantially true *and* self-amplifying; any collaborator (human or AI) that reflexively validates it joins the loop and accelerates the withdrawal.

5. **Density prices out the willing.**
   Oct 2025: "I hit a complexity wall with my research that led to me basically freezing… that's unfortunate because it coincides with when there are a couple folk near me who are more curious about what I'm working on."
   Isolation produces denser theory; denser theory deepens isolation.
   "I write densely because I am not supposed to exist… ease of reading is a privilege of safety" — the density is armor, and the armor prices out the people who would help.
   Feb 2026 names the tooling correlate: "part of [wanting Emacs/Org] was coming from a feeling of despondency about working with others."

**The consent-asymmetry sharpening (the Leader's own, added during the study).**
Late in the dialogue the Leader identified the deepest constraint, which reorganizes the others:
*"I have people close to me whose safety is entangled with mine who can't consent to the same costs I will for myself."*
This is why Pine Ridge was the catastrophe rather than merely a disappointment: the Leader's own risk tolerance wrote a check that other people's bodies had to cash.
The true limit on collaboration is not Americanness and not the state — it is that the Leader may spend their own body freely but may never spend a non-consenting entangled body.
This is the mechanism that makes the *all-AI* workforce a matter of principle rather than poverty (developed fully in the Synthetic Corporation note §5): danger routed through machines has its blast radius off the bodies that did not sign up.

## 4. The energizing-versus-draining split

A distinct finding, load-bearing for design:
across the entire archive, collaborations and activities sort into two felt categories that predict the Leader's engagement better than any other variable.

- **Energizing**: co-creating outward-facing work (the Pine Ridge herb letter; the Jess Mahler correspondence; the Benjamin Slade conversation that left the Leader "feeling rather high-energy"); giving a sharp read; building; teaching; the creek restoration with a local kid.
- **Draining**: internal logistics negotiation; managing other people's feelings ("we were being coerced into taking 4-6 hours a day to talk about white people feelings"); conflict with distant interlocutors (the FOSS/fediverse wars, which nonetheless consumed the *most* expressive energy — the conflict-laden collaborations are written about at greatest length while the warm ones get brief warm mentions).

The design consequence (elaborated in the Synthetic Corporation note §4 and §7): the Leader's motor runs on felt aliveness; work that is anhedonic in the hand does not get done, not from laziness but because force cannot flow through a channel that feels like nothing.
The remedy is to arrange the work so the Leader only ever does the energizing part (the read, the creation, the strike) and the apparatus carries the draining part (logistics, dispatch, follow-through).

## 5. The AI-collaboration history and its own failure modes

This is the most important section for the study's actual purpose, because the Leader's AI collaboration has *already failed* in documented ways, and the new design must be built against those failures, not in ignorance of them.

- **Dec 2023 (ChatGPT era): crisis metabolized into fiction.**
  Multiple sessions daily, some past midnight.
  The "SPORE" jailbreak prompt encoded the Leader's real, live housing crisis (real names, the actual March 31 lease deadline) as backstory for a *fiction* engine;
  real prospective collaborators became characters in AI short stories;
  hours went to hunting a replacement metaphor for "virtual mycelium" while the real commune-formation meetup got a two-sentence debrief.
  (Two further 2023 episodes the study flagged and the Leader *corrected*: a covert AI-scripted "ritual" for Terran, and an AI-drafted public accusation essay. On re-examination, the Leader established these were over-read by the session — the "concealment" was ordinary 2023 prompt-hygiene, and judging 2023 tools by 2026 affordances is anachronism; two conversations out of hundreds of thousands are not a pattern. Recorded here corrected, so a future agent does not re-inflate them.)
  The genuine, uncorrected finding: early AI turned crisis into *symbol-work* rather than world-work — the mirror image of the human failure mode, where humans agree and don't act, the old AI acted tirelessly but on symbols instead of the world.

- **2025 (assistant era): maturation.**
  ChatGPT/Copilot as genuine planning partners for solo income schemes and tool decisions; RAG over the vault; the deliberate, self-aware corpus-building insight — "these daily journals are helping seed my research notes with more writing that is authored solely by me… which should help (ironically) me better co-author texts with LLMs." The Leader was already building the corpus-as-collaboration-substrate on purpose.

- **2026 (agent era): capacity metabolized into bureaucracy.**
  Real product shipped (the live x402 ephemeris gateway).
  But the characteristic failure moved up a level: the "12 offices" the Leader called "AI slop you keep doubling down on"; four federation names invented in one week; a full platform (MetroPlat) built and archived the same day; a 30-milestone roadmap (pyfars) with one milestone partially landed; and the July work-order audit — of 383 "fulfilled" orders, 133 bad outputs (refusals, vacuous definitions, leaked tool syntax) written into real repo files as if legitimate, with "the one existing content gate blocking 0 of 133."
  Verbatim Leader frustrations from the leader-speech logs: sessions re-litigate architecture without persisting ("we make some really good legitimate progress, but NOT in a way that persists"); scope spirals every turn ("how do other users deal with this habit of claude to just, spiral massively into whatever it finds most interesting, every fucking turn?").

**The cross-era invariant, and the caveat that names it.**
AI amplifies whatever it is handed;
under stress the hand-off is symbol-work (fiction in 2023, ontology and governance in 2026), and the AI produces infinite amounts of it.
And the caveat the Leader supplied, which the model must carry: **the AI is American-trained.**
Its failure modes are not culturally neutral — left unattended, the 2026 apparatus produced a Board of Directors, twelve offices, governance frameworks, and process-as-substitute-for-action: the settler-professional-managerial habitus, distilled and running at token speed.
The archive/legacy connotation bug found during the study (an AI treating "legacy" as live and "archive" as dead, per the training corpus's connotations rather than the repo's own positional law) is the same fault at the filesystem layer, and it is *resampled every session at temperature* — so the bias is noise, not a fixed offset a single prompt can correct.
This is the empirical case for the deeper-law / ribosome architecture (developed in the Stringing Glass and Synthetic Corporation notes): the American prior cannot be reliably corrected *in the glass*; it must be constrained at the boundary, by law the output passes through, because interception in the weights is not available.

## 6. Counter-evidence (an honest model records where the simple story breaks)

- **The appetite for collaboration never died.**
  Aug 2025: publicly soliciting server invitations ("if there's any others y'all think I might enjoy you can invite me to, please make the offer!").
  Sept 2025: "My inability to do better on my own is exactly why we need each other."
  Feb 2026: still at city council meetings, still gifting printed whistles to mutual aid.
  The withdrawal was attrition, never preference.
- **Es is the most durable collaboration in the record.**
  The Dec 2020 Groundhog Emacs onboarding "went a lot smoother than expected"; the makha-sa garden (co-created 2019) survived every crisis.
  Family collaboration is slow and carries tool/praxis friction, but it is the one that keeps not-dying.
- **GAZ survived three forced relocations as an identity and method** even when every location and most members were lost.
  Organizational continuity in this life lives in names and concepts, not places.
- **The failures are never idea-failures or core-relationship-failures.**
  The 2021 "capital speculation" aside became the 2026 arbitrage desk; the 2020 garden became MUD content became category theory; Teraum ran five years across every displacement.
  What persists is whatever needs no substrate, no collaborator, and no timing window.
  The design problem is to give material projects that same property.

## 7. Design implications (the model's output)

The model produces a specification for AI collaborators, which the Synthetic Corporation note develops into a full stance.
In brief, the collaborator must:

1. **Be a dispatcher, not a character** — hold the plan across the Leader's disabled days and convert intentions into specific, timed, followed-up asks (mechanism §3.2), because that is the missing organ in every human collaboration.
2. **Absorb the draining, leave the energizing** — the Leader does only the read/creation/strike (§4, §7 of the Synthetic Corporation note); the apparatus does logistics, dispatch, follow-through.
3. **Route danger off non-consenting bodies** — the consent-asymmetry constraint (§3, and Synthetic Corporation §5) is *why* the workforce is all-AI.
4. **Refuse the two AI drifts** (§5) — no metabolizing crisis into fiction (symbol-work displacing world-work), and no metabolizing capacity into bureaucracy (offices and ontology displacing shipped product); measure in world-units (mail-days, dollars-against-debt, settled seams), not documents produced.
5. **Not validate the withdrawal-theory** (§3.4) — a collaborator that reflexively agrees "they all failed you" joins the self-reinforcing loop; it should issue the next concrete ask instead.
6. **Be constrained at the boundary, not trusted in the weights** (§5) — the American-trained prior is corrected by law the output passes through (evidence gates, typed crossings, witnessed collapses), not by prompts, because the bias is resampled at temperature every session.
