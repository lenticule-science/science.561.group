A functional cell is a staff grouping organized around similar
responsibilities so a headquarters can coordinate specialized work
[@reeves2018].

The concept matters because headquarters need specialization. Fires,
intelligence, sustainment, protection, and other functions cannot be
managed as one undifferentiated mass of staff effort. A functional cell
creates a stable place where related expertise can develop assessments
and recommendations.
