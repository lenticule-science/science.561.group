## Assumed audience

Someone who has understood [why universalist coordination breaks](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/notes/2026-03-31-note-on-why-universalist-coordination-breaks.md) under stress and wants to know what the alternative looks like — not as a theory but as a set of interlocking practices.

## What this is not

This is not a program. There is no step-by-step implementation guide because the whole point is that programs — plans designed in advance, generalized across contexts, ratified through universal procedures — are what collapse destroys. What follows is a description of six structural features that, when they operate together, produce a framework with high adaptive capacity. They are not steps. They are properties of a system.

## The six features

### Ground: where obligation comes from

In universalist frameworks, obligation comes from principle — you owe things to people because a moral or procedural framework says you do. In relational frameworks, obligation comes from the relation itself.

[Grounded normativity](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/grounded-normativity.md) is Glen Coulthard's name for this: ethical and political knowledge that arises from Indigenous relationships to specific places and practices. The land teaches how to live. The kinship network teaches who you owe what. You do not need a moral theory to generate these obligations. You need a relation.

This is the ground the whole system stands on. Without it, the other five features are abstract. With it, they are descriptions of how relational communities already operate.

### Asymmetry: how difference is handled

[Co-equal kinship](../../../../../../../terms/co-equal-kinship.md) holds that all relations possess inherent worth without imposing identical obligations. The elder and the child are equally real, equally valued, equally part of the kin network. They do not carry the same responsibilities. The elder carries knowledge; the child carries futurity. The elder needs physical help; the child needs instruction. The obligations flow differently because the beings are different.

This is the structural alternative to the symmetry that [cooperative reciprocity](../../../terms/cooperative-reciprocity.md) demands. Instead of treating asymmetry as a problem to be procedurally resolved, the framework treats it as the shape of the relation — the thing that tells you what to do.

### Responsiveness: how priorities shift

[Emergent obligation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/emergent-obligation.md) means obligations shift in real time as conditions change. When the storm is coming, the person who needs to be moved first is the person who cannot move themselves. You do not wait for a meeting to establish this. You do not need a shared evaluative framework to recognize it. The relation generates the priority and the priority generates the action.

This is what replaces [proceduralism](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/proceduralism.md). Instead of routing every decision through a legitimation process, the relational structure itself produces priorities that respond to the situation as it unfolds. When the situation changes — when the person who was safe is now exposed, when the resource that was available is now gone — the priorities change with it.

### Protection: how knowledge is bounded

[Opacity](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opacity.md) — Glissant's concept — is the right of knowledge, plans, and decision-making to remain bounded within the relational circles that sustain them. Not hidden, not secret. Bounded. Shared with whom the relation requires, withheld from whom the situation demands.

Under [collapse conditions](/ecology/terms/collapse-environment.md), transparency is dangerous. Plans made visible to hostile institutions invite [crisis capture](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/crisis-capture.md) — the absorption of community responses into managerial frameworks. Resources made legible to extractive actors invite seizure. Decision-making processes made public invite interference. Opacity is the condition under which adaptive work can be completed without external disruption.

This is not a failure of democratic accountability. It is the recognition that accountability to the kin network and accountability to the city council are not the same thing, and that under hostile conditions, the second destroys the first.

### Temporality: what counts as justification

An [ethics of presence](../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ethics-of-presence.md) evaluates action by its adequacy to the present situation rather than by its contribution to a projected future. You fortify the shelter because the elders need it now. You preserve the seeds because the land requires continuity now. The action does not need a destination to justify it.

This is what replaces [teleology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/teleology.md). Instead of organizing action around a future state — the cooperative society, the policy reform, the movement that will eventually win — the framework grounds action in the relations and conditions that actually exist. When the future is unavailable, this ground remains.

### Limits: how the impossible is handled

[Tragic realism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/tragic-realism.md) is the acceptance that not everyone can be saved and that this is an ecological fact rather than a moral failure. The shelter holds 350. The neighborhood holds 1,000. No procedure resolves this. No redistribution eliminates it.

Frameworks that treat capacity limits as occasions for moral crisis produce paralysis: the community cannot act because it cannot justify acting incompletely. Tragic realism permits the decision. Not because it resolves the tragedy — the tragedy is real — but because the alternative to imperfect action is no action, and no action is worse.

## How they work together

These six features are not independent. They form a system:

- **Grounded normativity** provides the relational field from which everything else emerges.
- **Co-equal kinship** structures that field so that asymmetry produces clarity rather than paralysis.
- **Emergent obligation** makes the field responsive — priorities shift as conditions change.
- **Opacity** protects the field from external disruption.
- **Ethics of presence** anchors the field in the present rather than subordinating it to a future.
- **Tragic realism** permits the field to operate under limits without freezing.

Together, they produce a coordination system that does not depend on the prerequisites that collapse destroys — time, safety, symmetry, consensus, shared evaluative ground. It depends on relations. And relations, unlike institutional infrastructure, persist through the conditions that universalist frameworks cannot survive.

## Why this matters

If you are in a community that might face ecological disruption, infrastructural failure, or institutional hostility — and in North America, Black and Indigenous communities have been facing all three for generations — this is not abstract theory. It is a description of what relational communities already do when the institutional scaffolding falls away.

The question is not whether relational adaptation works. It is whether your community has the relational density to sustain it. Grounded normativity cannot be installed. Co-equal kinship cannot be procedurally constructed. Emergent obligation cannot be turned on in an emergency if the relations that generate it were never built. The work is building the relations now — thickening the kin network, deepening the place-based knowledge, practicing opacity, exercising the capacity to act from relational ground rather than procedural authorization.

When the storm comes, you will not have time to build what you need. You will use what you already have.
