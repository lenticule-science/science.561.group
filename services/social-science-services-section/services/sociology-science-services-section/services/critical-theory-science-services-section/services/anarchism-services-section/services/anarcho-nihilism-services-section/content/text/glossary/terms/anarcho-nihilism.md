Anarcho-[nihilism](../../philosophy/disciplines/nihilism/index.md) is the political orientation that acts against domination without requiring hope, progress, or the expectation of success as preconditions for action. It combines [anarchism's](../../../terms/anarchism.md) opposition to all forms of domination with [nihilism's](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/continental-services-section/services/existentialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nihilism.md) [refusal](../../../../../strategy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) to ground action in transcendent meaning, historical progress, or faith in a better future.

The position was developed most explicitly by [Serafinski](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/serafinski.md) in *[Blessed is the Flame](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/blessed-is-the-flame.md)* (2016), which examines resistance within Nazi concentration camps — sabotage, revolt, escape, [refusal](../../../../../strategy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md) — as political acts that cannot be evaluated by their prospects for success. The concentration camp is the limit case: resistance persists where victory is impossible, where no program can be implemented, where the future offers nothing. [Serafinski](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/serafinski.md) argues that this is not an aberration but a revelation of what resistance has always been — an act grounded in present conditions, not future expectations.

Anarcho-[nihilism](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/continental-services-section/services/existentialism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/nihilism.md) is not despair. It is the insistence that despair and hope are both irrelevant to the question of whether to resist. The orientation connects to [hospicing humanity](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hospicing-humanity.md) — the recognition that the current form of human civilization may be in terminal decline and that care through that process does not require optimism about its outcome. It connects to [potential nihilism](../../../../../../../../philosophy/domains/western/domains/continental/domains/existentialism/terms/potential-nihilism.md) (Nietzsche's distinction) in its constructive [refusal](../../../../../strategy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal.md): the old values are destroyed not to produce emptiness but to clear space for action that does not depend on them.
