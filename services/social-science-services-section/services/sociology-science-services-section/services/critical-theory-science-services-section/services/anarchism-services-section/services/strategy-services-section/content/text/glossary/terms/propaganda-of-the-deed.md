Propaganda of the deed is the principle that political ideas are communicated more effectively through action than through argument. An act of resistance, an example of [self-organization](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/self-organization.md), a demonstration that life without [hierarchy](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/hierarchy.md) is possible — these are more persuasive than any pamphlet because they show rather than tell. The term originated in the 1870s among Italian and Russian anarchists and has been used to describe a wide range of actions, from insurrectionary violence to the construction of cooperative institutions.

## The original meaning

The concept emerged from a practical problem: how do you communicate anarchist ideas to people who cannot read, who have no access to anarchist publications, and who have been trained from birth to accept [authority](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/authority.md) as natural? The answer: act. Demonstrate that [domination](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/domination.md) can be resisted. Show that [self-organization](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/self-organization.md) works. Make the abstract concrete. [Errico Malatesta](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/errico-malatesta.md) and other Italian anarchists of the 1870s organized insurrections in rural villages not because they expected to overthrow [the state](../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) from a single village but because the act of collective resistance — burning the tax records, distributing landlords' grain — would demonstrate to participants and observers that another way of organizing life was possible.

## The period of attentats

In the 1880s–1900s, propaganda of the deed became associated specifically with assassinations and bombings targeting heads of state, industrialists, and symbols of [authority](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/authority.md). Several heads of state were killed by anarchists during this period: Tsar Alexander II (1881), French President Carnot (1894), Spanish Prime Minister Cánovas (1897), Empress Elisabeth of Austria (1898), King Umberto I of Italy (1900), and US President McKinley (1901).

This period produced an enormous state and media backlash that defined public perception of anarchism for decades. It also produced intense debate within the anarchist movement itself. [Kropotkin](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/pyotr-kropotkin.md) initially expressed sympathy for attentats as responses to systemic [violence](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/coercion.md), but later distanced himself. Malatesta argued that individual acts of violence, however understandable as responses to oppression, could not substitute for the collective organizing needed for [revolution](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/revolution.md). [Emma Goldman](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/emma-goldman.md) defended the attackers as individuals driven to extremes by an intolerable system while questioning whether assassination was strategically effective.

## The broader principle

The reduction of propaganda of the deed to assassination obscures the broader principle: that practice communicates more than theory. Every act of [mutual aid](../../../../../organization-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mutual-aid.md) is propaganda of the deed. Every [affinity group](../../../../../organization-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/affinity-group.md) that coordinates through [consensus](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/consensus.md) rather than command demonstrates that [hierarchy](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/hierarchy.md) is unnecessary. Every [general strike](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/the-general-strike.md) shows that [the state](../../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/the-state.md) and [capitalism](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/capitalism.md) depend on workers' [obedience](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/obedience.md), not the reverse.

[Prefigurative politics](../../../../../organization-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/prefigurative-politics.md) is, in this sense, the mature form of propaganda of the deed: the practice of building the world you want in the present, demonstrating through action that it is possible.

Propaganda of the deed is the idea that action itself communicates revolutionary analysis — that a well-chosen act demonstrates what words can only describe. The concept emerged in the [anarchist](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/schools/anarchism/index.md) movement in the 1870s and 1880s, associated with the Italian and Russian revolutionary traditions, and was initially articulated by Carlo Cafiero, [Errico Malatesta](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/errico-malatesta.md), and others at the 1876 Berne Congress of the International.

In its original formulation, the concept was broad: any act that demonstrated the possibility and desirability of a world without domination counted as propaganda of the deed. A commune that organized itself without hierarchy, a strike that seized a workplace, an expropriation that redistributed wealth — these were propaganda because they showed, rather than merely argued, that alternatives to the existing order were possible.

The concept became associated almost exclusively with assassination and bombing in the 1890s, a narrowing that has distorted its reception since. The broader meaning — that action is itself a form of communication — remains central to [anarchist](../../../../../../../../../../content/terms/anarchism.md) practice. [Direct action](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/direct-action.md) is propaganda of the deed whenever the action itself makes visible what the existing order makes invisible: that cooperation does not require hierarchy, that resistance does not require permission, that the structures of domination are sustained by compliance and can be disrupted by its withdrawal.
