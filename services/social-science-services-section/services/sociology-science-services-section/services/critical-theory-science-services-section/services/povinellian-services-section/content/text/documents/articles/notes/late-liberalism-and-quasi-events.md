Audience: readers who have completed the [economies of abandonment lesson](./economies-of-abandonment.md).

Learning goal: describe [late liberalism](../../../glossary/terms/late-liberalism.md) as a political formation and explain why [quasi-events](../../../glossary/terms/quasi-event.md) are its characteristic political form.

[Late liberalism](../../../glossary/terms/late-liberalism.md) is the phase of liberal governance that follows its own recognition of colonial, racial, and environmental violence. It does not abandon liberal commitments — it intensifies them as management techniques. Recognition, inclusion, and procedural reform become the mechanisms through which structural conditions are maintained while the appearance of self-correction is produced.

The [quasi-event](../../../glossary/terms/quasi-event.md) is the characteristic political form of this arrangement. A quasi-event looks like a crisis — it produces public [discourse](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/discourse.md), institutional response, the appearance of rupture — but it functions as maintenance. The system processes the deviation and returns to equilibrium. The commentary and analysis that follow are not separate from the containment but its final phase: the narrative closure that renders the managed disturbance [legible](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/black-philosophy-services-section/services/abolition-services-section/services/state-repression-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/legibility.md) as an event.

This connects [Povinelli](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/elizabeth-povinelli.md)'s work to [Lauren Berlant](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/lauren-berlant.md)'s analysis of [cruel optimism](../../../../../../affect-theory-services-section/services/berlantian-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cruel-optimism.md) — the attachment to objects and fantasies (reform, recognition, inclusion) that actively impede the transformation they promise. Late liberalism sustains itself through the [cruel optimism](../../../../../../affect-theory-services-section/services/berlantian-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cruel-optimism.md) of its own procedures: the belief that acknowledgment of harm will lead to redress, when acknowledgment is itself the technique of containment.
