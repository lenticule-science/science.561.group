Audience: readers who have completed the [overview](./overview.md).

Learning goal: explain how [geontologies](../../../glossary/terms/geontologies.md) and [geontopower](../../../glossary/terms/geontopower.md) operate through three figures and how they extend [biopolitics](../../../../../../sociology/terms/biopolitics.md).

[Povinelli](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/elizabeth-povinelli.md) argues that [late liberal](../../../glossary/terms/late-liberalism.md) governance manages the Life/Nonlife boundary through three figures:

- **The Desert**: Nonlife threatening to overwhelm Life. This is the figure of ecological catastrophe, resource exhaustion, and extinction — the fear that the inert world will reclaim the living one. Climate [discourse](../../../../../../foucauldian-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/discourse.md) often operates through this figure.
- **The Animist**: the figure that refuses the Life/Nonlife distinction altogether. Indigenous ontologies that recognize rivers, rocks, and ancestors as alive and agentive represent the Animist — not as a "belief" to be tolerated but as an ontological challenge to the framework that makes [extractivism](../../../../../../../../../../biological-science-services-section/services/ecology-services-section/services/climate-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/extractivism.md) possible.
- **The Virus**: the figure that crosses the boundary, neither fully alive nor fully inert. The Virus disrupts classification from within — it is the uncanny entity that the Life/Nonlife distinction cannot accommodate.

[Geontopower](../../../glossary/terms/geontopower.md) is the form of power that manages these threats to the boundary. It operates not through dramatic acts of sovereignty but through the quiet maintenance of [ontological](../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/western-services-section/services/metaphysics-services-section/services/ontology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ontology.md) categories — the classifications embedded in [property](../../../../../../sociology/terms/property.md) law, environmental regulation, and scientific taxonomy.

Check for understanding: How does the figure of the Animist challenge geontological governance?
