Audience: readers familiar with [Foucault](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/michel-foucault.md)'s [biopolitics](../../../../../../foucauldian-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/biopower.md) and basic critical theory.

Learning goal: describe [Povinelli](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/elizabeth-povinelli.md)'s central question and how her framework extends Foucauldian analysis.

[Povinelli](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/elizabeth-povinelli.md) asks: what happens when we look beneath [biopolitics](../../../../../../sociology/terms/biopolitics.md) — beneath the governance of living populations — and examine the prior operation that determines what counts as living at all? Her answer is [geontologies](../../../glossary/terms/geontologies.md): the governance of the distinction between Life and Nonlife. This distinction is not natural but governed — maintained through law, science, property regimes, and environmental regulation — and it is the ontological infrastructure on which biopolitical governance depends.

Her work is grounded in decades of collaborative engagement with Indigenous Australians, particularly the Karrabing Film Collective, which means her theoretical framework is not abstracted from politics but emerges from the concrete conditions of [settler-colonial](../../../../../../sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) governance, land management, and Indigenous persistence.

Check for understanding: What is the distinction [Povinelli](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/elizabeth-povinelli.md) identifies as more fundamental than biopolitics?
