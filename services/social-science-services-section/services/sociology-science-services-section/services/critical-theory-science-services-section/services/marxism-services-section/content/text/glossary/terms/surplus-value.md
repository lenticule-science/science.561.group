Surplus value is [Karl Marx](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/karl-marx.md)'s term for the difference between the value a worker produces during the [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md) process and the value they receive as wages. A worker employed for eight hours may produce enough value to cover their wage in four hours; the remaining four hours of [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md) produce value that the capitalist appropriates as profit. This appropriation is not theft in the legal sense — the worker has agreed to sell their [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md) power — but it is exploitation in the structural sense: the entire arrangement is organized to extract unpaid [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md).

[Marx](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/karl-marx.md) distinguished between absolute surplus value (extracted by lengthening the working day or intensifying [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md)) and relative surplus value (extracted by reducing the portion of the day needed to reproduce the worker's [labor](../../../../../anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/labor.md) power, typically through technological innovation). The drive to increase surplus value pushes capital toward automation, deskilling, and the constant reorganization of the labor process — dynamics that [operaismo](operaismo.md) later reframed as capital's response to workers' [refusal of work](../../../../services/autonomism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/refusal-of-work.md).

The concept illuminates the mechanism of [class struggle](./class-struggle.md): workers and capitalists have structurally opposed interests regarding the length of the working day, the intensity of labor, and the level of wages. But surplus value analysis, taken alone, has a specific limitation. It presupposes [wage labor](./wage-labor.md) as the primary form of exploitation. [Primitive accumulation](./primitive-accumulation.md) — the violent dispossession that creates a class of people with nothing to sell but their labor — is treated as a precondition rather than an ongoing process. [Settler colonialism](settler-colonialism.md) and the theft of Indigenous land, the Atlantic slave trade, and the [enclosure](../../../../services/political-economy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/enclosure.md) of [commons](../../../../services/political-economy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commons.md) are not anomalies or prehistory; they are constitutive of the system that produces surplus value. [Social reproduction](./social-reproduction.md) theory adds that the worker who arrives at the factory ready to labor was produced through unwaged domestic and [care work](../../../../services/autonomism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/care-work.md) that surplus value analysis renders invisible.
