Primitive accumulation is [Karl Marx](../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/karl-marx.md)'s term for the historical processes that created the preconditions for capitalist production: the separation of people from the means of their own subsistence. In England, this took the form of enclosures — the forcible conversion of common land into private [property](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/anarchism/terms/property.md), which dispossessed peasants and created a class of people with nothing to sell but their [labor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/terms/labor.md) power. Globally, it took the form of colonial conquest, the Atlantic slave trade, and the extraction of wealth from colonized peoples and lands. [Marx](../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/karl-marx.md) treated these as the "original sin" of [capitalism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md): the violence that preceded and enabled the apparently peaceful exchange of wages for [labor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/terms/labor.md).

[Marx](../../../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/karl-marx.md)'s account, however, located primitive accumulation primarily in [capitalism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md)'s past — as a stage that, once completed, gives way to the "normal" operations of surplus extraction. David Harvey's reformulation as "accumulation by dispossession" corrects this. Dispossession is not a completed stage but an ongoing requirement of capital accumulation: privatization of public goods, [enclosure](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/theories/political-economy/terms/enclosure.md) of intellectual [commons](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/theories/political-economy/terms/commons.md), seizure of Indigenous territories, structural adjustment programs that force open markets in the Global South, and the financialization of housing and debt are all contemporary forms. Capital does not simply exploit [labor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/terms/labor.md); it continuously creates new populations to exploit by destroying their existing means of subsistence.

This is where Marxist and decolonial analyses converge. [Settler colonialism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/povinellian/theories/settler-colonialism/terms/settler-colonialism.md) is not a historical event that produced present consequences; it is an ongoing structure of dispossession that continues to separate Indigenous peoples from their land. [Racial capitalism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/black-radical-tradition/terms/racial-capitalism.md) shows that this dispossession has always operated through racial differentiation — the production of populations marked as available for appropriation. [Social reproduction](../../../../services/autonomism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/social-reproduction.md) theory reveals that the unwaged [labor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/terms/labor.md) of women, particularly women of color, is another form of ongoing primitive accumulation: the appropriation of [labor](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/terms/labor.md) without compensation. The [Land Back](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/povinellian/theories/settler-colonialism/terms/land-back.md) movement names the demand that follows from this analysis: if [capitalism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/anarchism/terms/capitalism.md) requires continuous dispossession, and if [settler colonialism](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/2026-06-17-emsemioverse-terms-index.md) is its ongoing form on this continent, then the return of land is not a symbolic gesture but a structural challenge to the conditions of accumulation itself.
