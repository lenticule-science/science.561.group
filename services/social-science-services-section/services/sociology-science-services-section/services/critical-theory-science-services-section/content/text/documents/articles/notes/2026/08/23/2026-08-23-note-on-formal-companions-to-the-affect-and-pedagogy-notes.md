## 0. Purpose and division of labor

Four theory notes of 2026-08-23 are written to stand in their fields' own terms, citing only public literature:
**Hope, Care, and Fear; Attention, Grace, and Abandonment** and **Satire, Irony, and Detachment** (critical theory);
**Learning Not to Flinch** and **Pedagogy, Play, and the Missing Meme** (education).
Their claims neither need nor cite the corpus's internal mathematics — by design: field-facing claims should carry field-native warrants, and interleaving a private formalism weakens rather than strengthens them.

This companion holds the other half of the work:
the mappings from those notes' central objects into the corpus's settlement mathematics, where the mappings are exact enough to generate formalization targets.
Cross-reference is this note's *job*; the theory notes do not reference it back.
Grading: **[green]** marks results the Obsmetrologia THEORY.md ledger lists as proven in Lean; **[module]** names the source; **[target]** marks a proposed formalization; **[reading]** marks an interpretive mapping not itself a formal claim.

## 2. The told-nothing collapse

The trinities note's epistemic core of abandonment — the social treatment of the undetermined as refused — is, in the corpus's four-valued semantics, the **⊥→f collapse**: a cell at *told-nothing* processed as *told-false*.
The bilattice keeps ⊥ and ⊤ as first-class designated states [module: the Obsmetrologia Belnap-FOUR settlement semantics], so the collapse is representable as an operation, not merely a complaint:
[target] define the collapse as a (non-monotone, knowledge-order-violating) endomap, characterize the institutional operators that implement it (defaults, deadlines, silent denials), and state witness practices as its audit: re-exposure of the pre-collapse value.
The grace-period reading follows: forbearance is the *refusal* of the collapse — holding ⊥ open under pressure — and is thus formally dual to abandonment's operative clause.

## 3. Grace as the settlement path's limit

The trinities note's grace-as-standing (holding at zero marginal attention) is the corpus's settlement path evaluated at its limit:
open inquiry → settled meaning → settled execution → fully settled process, with every settled judgment migrating from attention-expensive inquiry toward zero-attention structure [module: the settlement-path note; the huidu-guanli note's relocation of 灰度 into the apparatus].
**Grace = care whose settlement is complete** [reading]:
the upstream payer's attention crystallized into structure that holds at zero marginal cost — which is why the theory note's Augustine/Pelagius resolution (unearned by the recipient, paid upstream) is not a compromise but the path's two endpoints named.
[target] state the holding-cost curve along the path and the sublinearity of maintenance in holdings (the Ostrom observation) as the formal content of "manufacturable grace."
The origin question's ground-termination (relation constitutive, edgelessness a type error) is native to the relationality framework [module: the relationality corpus; the lenticle construction], where the theory note's Simondon/Whitehead/Indigenous-philosophy citations find their machine-checked cousin.

## 4. Counterfeit catharsis

The satire note's substitution thesis — satirical laughter resolves the audience's tension while the world-cell stands — is, in the wit's accounting, **discharge without witnessed settlement**:
held-⊤ mass (the professed/actual contradiction) collapsed *in affect* with no corresponding settlement posted for the cell the contradiction is about.
The wit prices distinction-collapse and treats unwitnessed change as counterfeit [module: the Landauer-indexed distinction-accounting corpus];
catharsis-as-politics is precisely a felt receipt with no ledger entry.
[target] a two-ledger model (affect-ledger, world-ledger) in which the choir configuration provably maximizes affect-discharge at zero world-settlement, and the satire note's exemptions (informing satire; risk-bearing satire) appear as the configurations with nonzero world-ledger flux.
The choir condition itself is the pedagogy note's zone constraint inverted: delivery to holders of the meme yields no cascade, so discharge is the only available product.

## 5. The flinch and the frame

The flinch note's two-level object — startle and premature closure — is the ⊥→f collapse of §2 executed reflexively:
**the flinch is somatic early settlement**, and overgeneralized fear is a collapse whose stimulus class has widened (calibration debt).
Fear's budget behavior in the trinities note (the non-resolving allocation, the leak) gets its regime reading here:
[target] classify fear-allocations by whether their closure terminates — checkable fear as a settling allocation, unverifiable fear as non-terminating — over the regime machinery [module: the closure-regime dichotomy; green].
The play-frame argument shared by the flinch and pedagogy notes — frames suspend the consequence-structure that punishes sustained uncertainty — is formally:
**a play frame is a local modality under which the ⊥→f collapse is suspended**, and the zone of proximal development is the region of *safely-held ⊥*.
[target] state the frame as a modal operator on the settlement site; derive that learning capacity (threshold passage) is monotone in safely-held-⊥ duration; Vygotsky's head-taller finding is the intended corollary.

## 6. The click and the meme

The pedagogy note's click — threshold passage, insight, the punchline — is a **settlement cascade**:
a small delivered element triggering large-scale settlement of material already held, with the liminal state as held-⊥/held-⊤ before reorganization and irreversibility as settlement's one-way character.
The Meyer–Land marks translate exactly [reading]: transformative = large cascade basin; irreversible = settled; integrative = cross-cell welding; troublesome = designated unresolved states endured.
**The meme-leverage conjecture** [target]:
in the attention register, timed keystone delivery is the allocation class with maximal benefit-per-cost — minimal delivered mass, cascade-scale held-mass removal — with the zone condition (all-but-keystone present) as the characterization of when the maximum is attained.
Teaching-as-catalysis and the tutoring literature's outsized effects are this conjecture's phenomenology and field data respectively.
