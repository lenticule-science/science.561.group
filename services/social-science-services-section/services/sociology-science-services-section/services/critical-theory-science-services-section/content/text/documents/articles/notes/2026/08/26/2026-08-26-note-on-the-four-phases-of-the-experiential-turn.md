## 0. Abstract and plan

The owner's framing, which this note develops: **sensory, somatic, affective, cognitive** are the phases of experience — with the immediate caveat built in from the start: "obviously not a neat step by step, but spirals and feedback loops."
The note's work is threefold.
First, to define the four phases *operationally* over the settlement machinery, so they are commitments rather than intuitions (§1).
Second, to supply the feedback arrows from the literatures that have them — predictive processing, interoception, neuroimmunology — and to state the strong claim, which is not the sequence but the **nesting**: experience is loops within loops at separated timescales, and each slower loop's settlements are the standing that faster loops run on (§2–§3).
Third, to handle the quartet resonance honestly: the corpus carries four-patterns everywhere (Belnap FOUR; the four closure regimes; the K₄-graded diamond corners; the four-state tape; Caillois's four play-forms; De Caelo's four settling elements), and the owner is right that the echo is loud — but echoes are [rhyme] until a mapping earns more, and §4 tests the candidate mappings rather than declaring one (§4).
A quintessence question follows the corpus's own five-pattern and is flagged as speculation (§5).
Hypotheses in §6; objections in §7; directions in §8.
Grading follows house convention; citations are working references from assistant knowledge; verify before public use.

## 1. The phases, defined operationally

Let a *turn* be one pass of an agent through an experiential episode concerning some cell or field of cells.
The four phases are defined by what each does to settlement:

**Sensory — admission.**
The arrival of the not-yet-processed: prediction error crossing the gate.
In predictive-processing terms, sensation is never raw; what is admitted is the *residual* — the part of the signal the standing model did not already predict — weighted by precision.
Operationally: the sensory phase is the delivery of new ⊥ into the system, pre-gated by settled structure.
This gating is the first and most important anti-pipeline fact: the cognitive phase of prior turns stands at the front door of the sensory phase of this one.

**Somatic — registration.**
The body's measurement of what the admitted signal means for the organism's regulatory situation: autonomic adjustment, endocrine release, immune signaling, postural and visceral change.
In the vocabulary of the coordinate-system note: the somatic phase is where the biological readers *read* — controllability circuits, threat-axis activation, holding-state physiology — before any of it is felt as anything.
Operationally: the somatic phase is the updating of the regulatory state-variables; it is measurement, not yet readout.

**Affective — readout.**
The felt registration of the regulatory situation: interoceptive signal rendered as valenced, arousing, addressed experience — the coordinate-system note's *address*, occupied.
The interoception literature (Craig's ladder; Barrett's and Seth's constructions) supplies the mechanism: affect as the readout layer over somatic registration, core affect first, conceptualized address after.
Operationally: the affective phase is where the configuration becomes *available to the agent as a state of itself* — the derivative felt, the address lit.

**Cognitive — booking.**
Conceptualization, categorization, appraisal-as-report, narrative placement, and settlement proper: the knowledge-order operations.
Here the address gets a name, the episode gets a story, cells get booked or held open, and the products consolidate into the standing that will gate the next turn's sensory phase.
Operationally: the cognitive phase is where the turn deposits — or fails to deposit — onto the monotone coordinate.

The phase-order stated this way is a *logical* order of operations within one turn — admission before registration before readout before booking — and nothing more: the phases overlap in time, run concurrently across cells, and, decisively, feed back.

## 2. The feedback arrows

Each arrow is documented, not decorative:

**Cognitive → Sensory** (the long gate): settled priors and their precision-weighting determine what the sensory phase admits — perception as controlled hallucination corrected by error, in the current predictive-processing consensus.
The politically loaded corollary the corpus already holds: what a life has settled determines what it can subsequently notice; the frames that apportion grievability (trinities note, §7.2) operate *here*, at the front gate of experience.

**Cognitive → Somatic** (appraisal's grip): categorization shapes the registration it reports on — the same arousal registered under different bookings runs different autonomic courses (the Schachter tradition, corrected and deepened by reappraisal physiology: reframing measurably alters cardiovascular threat/challenge profiles).

**Somatic → Sensory** (interoceptive bias): body states tilt admission — inflammation shifts perceptual and attentional gating toward threat and away from reward (the endotoxin and vaccine paradigms: induced peripheral inflammation alters neural response to social stimuli within hours, before and without any cognitive attribution).

**Affective → Cognitive** (allocation steering): by the grading constraint (coordinate-system note, §4), affect cannot write bookings; it steers the *budget* — which cells get cognitive work, which verifications get funded.
The affective phase is the allocation policy's live readout, and its influence on the cognitive phase is exactly as large as the budget it routes.

**Somatic → substrate** (the slow arrow, and the deepest): the somatic phase does not only register — over its longest timescales it *edits the machinery the other phases run on*.
Peripheral immune state reaches microglia; microglia prune and remodel synapses; chronic inflammatory registration thereby rewrites the glutamatergic substrate on which settlement and admission themselves operate.
The two biomedical notes commissioned alongside this one (the glutamatergic–endocannabinoid axis; the cytokine–transcriptomic machinery) hold this bridge's mechanism.
Its theoretical weight is hard to overstate: **the somatic phase is the phase where the political economy physically enters the loop** — maintained exclusion, registered immunologically, edits the substrate of future experience.
Abandonment does not merely occupy experience; run long enough, it renovates the building.

## 3. The strong claim: nested helices

The owner's "spirals, not steps" is here made exact, using the corpus's own decomposition (spirulina: every helical process = periodic phase + monotone coordinate).

Experience is not one loop but **loops nested by timescale**, each with the four-phase structure, each depositing onto its own monotone coordinate:

| Loop | Period | What its turn deposits |
|---|---|---|
| perceptual | ~100 ms – seconds | admitted evidence; micro-updates to precision |
| affective-episodic | seconds – minutes | occupied addresses; allocation shifts |
| autonomic-endocrine | minutes – hours | regulatory set-point adjustments; stress-axis state |
| immune-transcriptomic | days – weeks | leukocyte program shifts; microglial editing; CTRA state |
| developmental | months – years | basic trust; controllability priors; the manufactured capacities |

And the nesting law, which is the note's central proposal:

**P1 (Timescale-relativity of standing).**
*Each loop's settled products are the standing — the structure held at zero marginal attention — on which the next-faster loop runs as flow.*
The perceptual loop runs on the affective loop's current address; the affective loop runs on autonomic set-points; the autonomic on the immune-transcriptomic program; all of them on the developmental deposits.
"Structure" and "flow" are therefore not kinds of thing but **timescale-relative roles**: what is flow at scale k is, once settled, substrate at scale k−1.
This is the grace analysis of the trinities note discovered *inside* the organism: manufactured standing all the way down, each layer paid for by the slower loop above it — and the faith note's manufacture claim (reliance is laid down by reliable holding, context by context) is P1 read at the developmental scale.

P1 also states precisely why damage and repair have the kinetics they do.
Fast loops cannot repair slow-loop damage by running faster: no quantity of perceptual or cognitive turns directly rewrites the immune-transcriptomic layer — which is why insight alone does not cure what maintained exclusion installed, why the reversal kinetics of the CTRA are rate-limited below any cognitive timescale, and why the clinical repairs that work (per the calibration and pharmacology notes) are all *slow-loop interventions wearing fast-loop clothes*: repeated context-by-context safety-learning, durable standing-conferral, sustained holding.
The spiral's monotone coordinate at each scale is moved only by turns *at that scale*.

## 4. The quartet question, held honestly

The corpus's four-patterns are real and numerous, and the temptation to declare S/S/A/C "another instance" must be resisted until a mapping does work.
Candidates, tested:

**Against Belnap FOUR (⊥/t/f/⊤).** A category error, cleanly: the Belnap values are *states of cells*; the phases are *operations of a turn*. Sensory is not ⊥ — sensory *delivers* ⊥. No mapping; the resemblance is arity only.

**Against the four closure regimes (strict / Scott / transfinite / aperiodic).** Also no: the regimes classify *whole settling processes* by termination behavior; every phase occurs in every regime. Arity again.

**Against the K₄ diamond corners (the holding quartet).** Closer but still wrong as identity: the corners are conditions (held-by-attention / held-by-structure / both / neither); the phases are moments. What *is* true — and the spirals note said it first (§6.3: "each turn of the spiral is one diamond traversed") — is that the phase-structure is the corpus's **turn-traversal pattern**: the generic shape of one closing operation moving through its stations.
The honest mapping is therefore to the corpus's other *phase*-quartets, not its *value*-quartets:
Tronto's care phases (attentiveness → responsibility → competence → responsiveness) and the autonomic-computing loop (monitor → analyze → plan → execute) — both already welded in the trinities note §5.2 — align station-for-station:
sensory/attentiveness/monitor; somatic/registration/analyze; affective/readout — the phase the engineering loops *lack*, and the difference is instructive: MAPE has no felt readout because servers need no allocation-steering interior; the affective phase is what a finite agent inserts between analysis and planning *because* it must route a scarce budget; cognitive/responsibility-competence/plan-execute.
The alignment is graded **[rhyme, promoted toward structural]**: three independent traditions (care ethics, autonomic computing, and now experiential phenomenology) converging on a four-ish station turn is evidence of a real generic — the turn — while the *number* four remains descriptive, not derived.
No theorem hangs on four, and this note declines to hang one.

## 5. The fifth, flagged

The corpus's five-pattern (the acedia note's quintessence: four elements settle to place, the fifth circulates) invites one speculation, recorded as such [speculation]:
if the four phases are the stations of the turn, there is a candidate for what *circulates through the stations without being one* — the attention/awareness that visits each phase, is consumed by none, and whose withdrawal collapses the turn at whatever station it abandons.
The corpus's substrate register has carried attention as the circulating quantity from the beginning (water, heat, budget); its placement as the turn's quintessence — the fifth that does not settle — is elegant, currently does no work, and is parked here so that if it ever does work, the date of the parking is on record.

## 6. Hypotheses

1. **The primacy dispute dissolves as a loop.** The Zajonc–Lazarus affective-primacy debate (affect before cognition vs. appraisal before affect) is predicted to be *unresolvable as a race and resolvable as a loop*: somatic registration precedes categorical report within a turn (supporting Zajonc's data), while prior turns' bookings gate admission (supporting Lazarus's) — so experimental outcomes should flip predictably with where in the loop the paradigm cuts in. *Killed if* a paradigm-independent fixed order survives cut-in manipulation.
2. **Somatic priority under inflammation.** Endotoxin/vaccine-induced peripheral inflammation shifts affective addresses (toward π-collapse and withdrawal readouts) and sensory gating (threat-toward, reward-away) *before and independent of* cognitive attribution, and cognitive reattribution alone does not reverse the shift while inflammation persists. Partially supported already (the Harrison/Critchley line); the sharpened prediction is the reattribution failure. *Killed if* attribution manipulation normalizes affective readout at maintained inflammatory state.
3. **Interoceptive resolution bounds affective resolution.** Individual interoceptive precision (heartbeat-discrimination and successor measures, with their psychometric caveats) upper-bounds emotion granularity: the readout cannot out-resolve the registration it reads. *Killed if* high granularity occurs stably at chance-level interoceptive precision.
4. **Timescale-matched repair (from P1).** Interventions move monotone coordinates only at their own loop's timescale: cognitive reframing shifts allocation within episodes but not CTRA state; standing-conferral shifts CTRA over monocyte-turnover timescales but shows no within-session affective signature; mismatched-timescale interventions produce the documented pattern of transient response and relapse. *Killed if* fast-loop intervention durably moves a slow-loop coordinate without the slow loop's own turns.
5. **The gate is settable.** Precision-manipulation (attentional training, or pharmacological precision-modulation) changes what the sensory phase admits from identical stimulation, measurable as shifted prediction-error responses — and the shift propagates through the turn (registration, readout, booking) in phase-order, not simultaneously. *Killed if* downstream phases shift without or before the admission shift.

## 7. Objections and replies

**O1. The phases are folk psychology's layers dressed in machinery.**
*Reply.* The folk layers do not include the operational commitments: admission as precision-gated residual, registration as coordinate-measurement, readout as interoceptive rendering, booking as knowledge-order deposit — each with named literatures and, in §6, discriminating predictions. The folk picture also has no arrows; the arrows are most of the note.

**O2. Four is doing secret numerological work despite §4's disclaimers.**
*Reply.* The note's strong claims (the arrows; P1's nesting law; the hypotheses) survive if the phase-count were revised to three or five — the affective/somatic boundary in particular is a live empirical question the interoception program may redraw. §4 grades the quartet as rhyme and refuses the theorem; the discipline is the corpus's own (the acedia note's §6 disclaimer, applied to ourselves).

**O3. P1 is unfalsifiable — any repair success can be redescribed as having engaged the right timescale.**
*Reply.* §6.4 states the exposure: the loop-timescales are independently measured (monocyte turnover, consolidation windows, set-point adjustment kinetics), so "which loop an intervention engages" is fixed by its mechanism before outcomes are seen. A reframing intervention that durably rewrote CTRA would kill P1 as stated. The prediction is risky in exactly the places current clinical data are suggestive but not decisive.

**O4. The somatic-substrate arrow proves too much: if the body edits the machinery, no phase-analysis is stable.**
*Reply.* It proves precisely the right amount: the editing is slow, rate-limited, and itself phase-structured (registration accumulating into program shifts). Stability at fast timescales with revisability at slow ones is not a bug in the analysis — it is P1, and it is also the corpus's general picture of standing (revisable, at cost, at the right level) since the trinities note.

## 8. Directions

1. **The affective-phase insertion theorem.** §4's observation — engineering loops lack the readout phase because they lack a scarce interior budget — suggests a derivation: any turn-running agent with (a) budget scarcity and (b) multiple concurrent cells *requires* an allocation-steering readout layer. If provable over the attention-allocation machinery, the affective phase stops being an empirical peculiarity of animals and becomes a theorem about finite agents — with evident consequences for the coordinate-system note's door 5.
2. **Cut-in cartography.** Systematize §6.1: catalog the classic affect-cognition experiments by where they cut into the loop, and show the primacy literature's contradictions sorting by cut-point. A review-note with real adjudicating power.
3. **The slow-arrow dossier.** When the two biomedical notes land, write the bridge note proper: microglial editing as the mechanism by which the somatic phase renovates the settlement substrate — the corpus's most concrete statement of how social conditions become cognitive constraints.
4. **Phase-diagnostics.** The clinical cartography door (coordinate-system note, §10.1) gains a second dimension: disorders as address-regions *and* as phase-lesions (admission pathologies; registration pathologies like alexithymia — readout without resolution; booking pathologies like rumination — the cognitive phase refusing to close the turn). A two-dimensional clinical map, with alexithymia and rumination as the anchor cases.
5. **The quintessence question** (§5), left parked until it can do work.
