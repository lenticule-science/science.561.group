*Decolonization is not a Metaphor* [@tuck2012] argues that decolonization, in settler-colonial contexts, means the repatriation of Indigenous land and life. Using it as a metaphor for improving schools, curricula or institutions ("decolonize your mind") settles it into the settler order it was meant to unsettle. The authors name the "moves to innocence" by which settlers seek relief from complicity without giving up land or power. It is a foundational text of contemporary decolonial thought.
