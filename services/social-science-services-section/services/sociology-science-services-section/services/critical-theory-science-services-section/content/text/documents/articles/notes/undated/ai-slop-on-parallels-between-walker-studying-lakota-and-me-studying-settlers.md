- Used his position (and the disarray from disease) to access, collect, and document Lakota religious, ontological, and cosmological knowledge.
	
- His records are unusually detailed but filtered through Christian assumptions and a clinical, salvational gaze.
	
- The collapse of traditional knowledge transmission due to disease became a *methodological opportunity* for Walker.
	
- His project, explicitly or implicitly, became one of **salvage anthropology**, enabled by biopolitical collapse.

## Key Analytic Threads:

### 1. **Biopolitical Disintegration as Ontological Aperture**

- Both figures used the death of an epistemological world as a lens for understanding it.
	
- Walker saw the Lakota cosmos fracturing under colonial and viral pressure; you saw liberal modernity unravel under pandemic and platformization.
	
- In both cases, *epistemic crisis becomes a research method*.

> Consider: is there a metaphysical irony in discovering the truth of a system only when it ceases to function?

---

### 2. **Positionality and Power**

- Walker had structural authority: a white doctor, collecting data under a logic of “preservation” and “clarification.”
	
- You are a Lakota thinker without that institutional power, though wielding para-institutional tools (GPTs, networks, Substack).
	
- You are also implicated: you *benefit* from liberalism’s collapse in terms of research clarity, even as you suffer from it materially and psychically.
	
- This creates a recursive ethical position: a theorist *of* collapse who is also its **exploiter**, its **product**, and its **witness**.

---

### 3. **Genre of Salvage**

- Both operations resemble **salvage ethnography**, but in inverted relation:
	
	- Walker salvaged Lakota worldviews under the assumption they were vanishing.
		
	- You salvage liberalism not to preserve it, but to expose and displace it.
		
- Yet both share a genre: *documentation under death conditions*.
	
- COVID let you document liberal affect, embodiment, liberal epistemology, its psychopolitical failings, etc.—because people (including you) were *no longer able* to pretend it was working.

---

### 4. **Temporal Mechanics of Collapse**

- TB and COVID each represent temporal accelerants:
	
	- TB compressed generations of relational knowledge into a deathbed transmission.
		
	- COVID compressed liberal temporalities: progress, futurity, rationality all broke.
		
- Collapse generated a sudden *clarity*: a crystallization of ontology under pressure.

## Critical Risks:

- **Romanticizing collapse**: Do you replicate Walker’s error of treating disaster as a gift to the theorist?
	
- **Self-exemption**: Do you believe your theoretical awareness immunizes you from complicity?
	
- **Instrumentalization of suffering**: Is there a risk of converting pain (yours or others’) into epistemic fuel?
