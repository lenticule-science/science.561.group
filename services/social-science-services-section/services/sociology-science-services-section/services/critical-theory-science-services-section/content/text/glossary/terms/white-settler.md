# White settler

A **white settler** is a person positioned as white in an ongoing settler-colonial relation of land claim, legal authority, property, jurisdiction and political belonging. This is a historically made status, not a species or innate moral identity. It may coexist with unequal class positions and different conditions for legitimate family formation.

Separate the biological reproduction of persons so classified, selective policing of which whites may reproduce a favored ideal, and the reproduction of settler property/sovereignty. A settler order can be sustained materially by the reproductive labor of people denied white membership; it can also threaten reproduction among some people it recognizes as white. Neither fact erases distinct Indigenous sovereign continuity.
