# A Generative Coordinate System for Affect and Belief

## 0. Abstract and plan

The teleologies note built a coordinate space for ideologies from one generating question.
This note builds the companion instrument one level down — for the affects and belief-attitudes themselves — and argues that the two vocabularies English keeps in separate books (emotion words; doxastic words) are one coordinate space read on two orders.

**The thesis.**
An affect is not an entity but an **address**: the felt registration of an agent's configuration toward a *cell* — a matter, claim, or relation whose settlement is at stake.
The configuration has few coordinates: the cell's settlement state; the agent's valenced lean; the cell's tense; whether a path exists by which the agent's action moves settlement; the holding relation engaged; and the modality under which the configuration obtains.
Doxastic attitudes — belief, doubt, certainty, conviction, credence, faith, wonder — are stances toward the *same* cells on the knowledge order.
Emotion theory and doxastics are therefore one machinery, split by the two orders of the corpus's bilattice, and the split inherits a machine-checked constraint: valence cannot operate on the knowledge order's structure; the two compose as grading and compete only on budget.

**What makes this more than taxonomy.**
The axes are not stipulated; they are *discovered twice over*.
Once by the corpus's own notes, which kept finding them one at a time (§1).
And once by the regulatory biology: the three biochemistry notes of 2026-08-26 found, in three unrelated chemistries, that what the body measures is not the material content of a situation but its **settlement structure** — controllability, safety-generalization, open-endedness of exclusion (§3).
The coordinates of this note are proposed as exactly the variables the biology already reads, which is what gives every address a predicted mechanistic signature and makes the system killable rather than merely elegant.

§1 assembles the occasion; §2 positions the ancestors; §3 states the generating principle and derives the axes with their biological readers; §4 performs the doxastic unification; §5 gives the table and discriminates the near-pairs; §6 states the lexicon predictions and the cross-linguistic test; §7 derives repair paths; §8 answers objections; §9 states the hypotheses; §10 opens the doors; §11 sources.
Citations are working references from assistant knowledge; verify before public use.

## 1. The occasion: the table was assembling itself

The corpus has been placing affects one at a time, each placement local, none noticing the grid:

hope and fear as the two valences of allocation to the undetermined (trinities note, P1) — later *derived* as a grading axis by the bigraded machinery (spirals note, §6);
despair and presumption as the two directed premature settlements of an open cell (acedia note, §1; formal companions);
faith as reliance-without-settlement, the third stance the collapse-pair was missing (faith note, §4);
solitude and loneliness as one position under opposite modalities (freedom note, §1.2);
acedia as sorrow-*at* the good against melancholy's sorrow-*toward* it (acedia note, §4.3);
lament as grief that stays in relation (acedia note, §4.3);
gratitude and safety as the unnamed interior of being-held (trinities note, §2, named in the faith note);
the energizing/draining split as the felt sign of the helix's derivative (spirals note, §5.3);
cruel optimism as an allocation signature — perpetual, non-converting, budget-holding (trinities note, §3.3).

Each of these is an *address specified by coordinates*: a settlement state here, a valence there, a modality, a relation-position, a derivative sign.
The generating principle was never stated.
This note states it, on the wager the teleologies note already validated at the ideological level: **when independent analyses keep producing points, construct the space.**

## 2. Ancestors, and what each lacks

Four research programs are genuine predecessors, and the honest positioning is the same exercise the teleologies note performed on grid-group and moral foundations.

**Dimensional models** (Russell's circumplex; core affect as valence × arousal) supply the two coordinates with the strongest biological warrant and the least discriminative power: fear, anger, dread, and disgust crowd one quadrant; the model cannot say why they are different affects with different repairs.
The present system keeps valence as one axis and treats arousal as intensity — a magnitude on the address, not an address coordinate.

**Basic emotion theory** (Ekman's program) is the wrong kind of theory for this purpose: a finite innate list, discovered rather than generated, with no principle producing the list or predicting its gaps — a periodic table with no atomic number.
Its empirical base (universal recognition of facial configurations) has meanwhile been substantially qualified by the constructionist critique.

**Appraisal theory** (Lazarus; Scherer's component-process model with its stimulus evaluation checks; Ellsworth) is the closest ancestor and deserves the credit: its dimensions — novelty, goal conduciveness, *certainty*, *coping potential*, agency, norm compatibility — are recognizably this note's axes in psychological dress.
Coping potential *is* the path coordinate; certainty *is* settlement state; agency *is* a target/modality reading.
What appraisal theory lacks is what the corpus supplies:
a **formal substrate** (the appraisal dimensions float free, unconnected to any logic of settlement, so the theory cannot say why *these* dimensions or how they compose);
the **doxastic unification** (appraisal theory generates emotions, never belief-attitudes, though its certainty dimension is begging for it);
the **distributive level** (nothing in appraisal theory connects an individual's coping-potential reading to the political economy that sets it);
and **biological anchoring of the axes as such** (appraisal dimensions are validated by self-report factor structure, not by identifying the regulatory circuits that measure each one).

**Constructed emotion** (Barrett) contributes the anthropology this note needs and will lean on in §6: emotion categories are concepts imposed on core affect plus context, learned, culturally various, and real in the way categories are real.
The generator is fully compatible — indeed it *predicts* the construction data: if affects are addresses in a continuous coordinate space, then lexicons are tilings of that space, tilings will vary culturally, and no tile is nature's own joint.
What construction lacks is the grid beneath the tilings: it can say categories vary; it cannot say *along which walls* variation will run, or which distinctions no lexicon will merge.
§6 turns exactly that into the discriminating test.

## 3. The generating principle and the axes

**The principle.**
For an agent A and a cell c (a matter, claim, or relation whose settlement bears on A's holdings):
**an affect is the felt registration of the configuration (A, c)**, and the configuration is specified by the following coordinates.
The claim is not that the agent computes these consciously; it is that the agent's regulatory systems *measure* them, and that the measurement's interoceptive readout, conceptualized, is what a language's emotion words name.

**Axis 1 — Settlement state of c** (σ ∈ {⊥ open, t settled-good, f settled-bad, ⊤ contested}).
The Belnap register, native to the corpus.
Biological reader: the prediction-error and extinction machinery — settlement events are precisely what phasic dopaminergic prediction-error signaling registers, and the settlement-pharmacology note documents the consolidation chemistry by which settled-safe becomes structural.

**Axis 2 — Valence of the lean** (v): the direction of the agent's prospective or evaluative orientation over c, toward-good or toward-bad.
By the corpus's cross-order theorem this enters as a *grade*, not an operator: leaning is not settling.
Biological reader: core affect's valence dimension; the vmPFC valuation system the neuroscience audit found applying valence to a valence-neutral construction engine.

**Axis 3 — Tense** (τ): c lies ahead (prospective), at hand (present), or behind (retrospective) on the agent's trajectory.
From the teleologies note's tense axis, applied per-cell.
Biological reader: the shared episodic construction system for prospection and memory — one engine, time-indexed.

**Axis 4 — Path** (π): does a path exist by which A's action moves c's settlement? (yes / no / unknown).
This is the axis the biology nominated loudest: Maier–Seligman controllability (the vmPFC circuit that detects *that my action moves outcomes* and inhibits the raphe stress cascade — the lean-note's center), and, at the immune depth, the abandonment-note's finding that open-ended versus resolvable exclusion grades the CTRA signature independent of isolation intensity.
π is also where the trinities note's verification analysis lives: checkable fear is fear with π = yes.

**Axis 5 — Holding** (h): the relation of the cell to the agent's standing: does c concern a mere relatum, the agent's own held standing, or the holding relation itself — and is A currently held, unheld, or is the holding at stake?
Biological readers: the social-baseline buffering pathway (holding as reduced predicted demand) and the CTRA transcriptomic program (unholding as a maintained physiological state).
The spirals note's target-class (relatum vs. relationing) is h's other face.

**Axis 6 — Modality** (μ): chosen or imposed; reversible or irreversible.
From the freedom note's schema, where it alone separated freedom from abandonment.
Biological reader: the sense-of-agency and (again) controllability machinery — Maier's paradigm *is* a chosen/imposed manipulation at the stressor level, and its downstream biology diverges by modality at fixed material exposure.

**Axis 7 — Relation-position** (ρ): in-relation or out-of-relation — is the configuration addressed *to* anyone?
The lament/despair separator: same loss, same tense, same valence; lament has an addressee and despair has none.
Biological reader: the weakest of the seven at present — social-baseline effects and the attachment system are candidates, but ρ currently lacks a dedicated circuit-level reader, and §9 marks this honestly as the axis most exposed to the charge of decoration.

Two magnitudes ride on every address without being coordinates: **intensity** (arousal) and the **derivative** — whether the configuration is improving or worsening along its coordinates, which the spirals note showed the body reads directly (energizing/draining).
Several famous affects are pure derivative-registrations: relief is the settlement event t arriving on a cell that was leaned-toward-bad; disappointment its mirror; surprise the valence-neutral settlement-against-lean event itself.

## 4. The two orders: belief as the knowledge-order reading

The bilattice that runs the corpus's semantics carries two orders — the truth order and the knowledge order — and the corpus proved (`cross_order_not_knowledge_nucleus`) that they cannot compose as two operators on one square: the second order enters as a grading axis.
This note proposes that split as the *psychological* architecture:

**Affects are stances graded on the valence/truth order. Doxastic attitudes are stances on the knowledge/settlement order. Every actual mental posture is a point in the product.**

The doxastic vocabulary then generates from the same cells:
**assent/belief** books c at t; **denial** books f;
**doubt** holds c open against pressure to settle;
**opinion** settles lightly — booking with reversibility retained (μ on the knowledge order);
**certainty** settles and *closes* — booking with the reopening path renounced (π = no, self-imposed);
**conviction** is certainty with h engaged — the booking staked to the agent's standing, which is why disconfirmation of a conviction is experienced as an attack on the self: the coordinates say it *is* one;
**credence** is the graded lean without booking;
**faith** is reliance-without-settlement (faith note, §4) — action routed across an open cell;
**suspicion** is faith's negative twin: a toward-bad lean on an open cell concerning a relation;
**wonder** is positive-valence attention to an open cell with the settlement-demand released;
**curiosity** is the lean toward the settling *event* itself — appetite for π-exercise, either valence;
**confusion** is ⊤ suffered — the contested cell without a resolution procedure;
**denialism** is the anti-flinch: the refusal of reopening, premature *permanence* rather than premature settlement.

The inherited constraint is now a psychological prediction rather than a formal nicety:
valence-order stances cannot alter knowledge-order structure directly — feeling cannot book, and booking cannot lean — **they interact only through the shared budget**.
Motivated reasoning, on this account, is not valence editing the ledger (impossible by the constraint) but valence steering the *allocation* — which cells get attention, which verifications get funded, which reopenings get resisted — exactly the mechanism the empirical motivated-cognition literature actually documents (selective exposure, differential scrutiny), as distinct from the folk picture of desire directly writing belief.
The constraint is thereby testable (§9.5), and REBUS-class interventions give it an experimental handle: pharmacological relaxation of knowledge-order settlements (priors) should move the *doxastic* coordinates first and the affect-space consequences should follow the geometry — reopened cells mean more open-cell affects, wonder and awe when held, anxiety when not — which is, verbatim, what the psychedelic phenomenology literature reports and the settlement-pharmacology note's Hypothesis 4 stakes.

## 5. The table, and the near-pairs discriminated

A sample of canonical addresses (σ, v, τ, π, h, μ, ρ as needed; magnitudes suppressed):

| Affect | Address |
|---|---|
| fear | c open, lean-bad, prospective, π unknown-or-low |
| **dread** | c *settled-bad*, prospective — the booked bad not yet arrived; no open cell, so no hope-competition: dread is fear after the loss of ⊥ |
| **anxiety** | the cell variable *unbound* — lean-bad with no c to check; π undefined because nothing is indexed, which is why reassurance (which addresses a cell) cannot land |
| hope | c open, lean-good, prospective, π > 0 |
| longing / Sehnsucht | c open or unreachable, lean-good, π ≈ 0 — the lean held without a path; melancholy's register |
| despair | ⊥ → f collapse performed (a *transition*, then its settled residue) |
| relief / disappointment | settlement events with/against the lean (derivative affects) |
| joy | positive derivative of capacity itself (dm > 0 felt; Spinoza's definition recovered as a coordinate fact) |
| grief | c = a held relation, settled-lost, retrospective; mourning = the resettling process; **lament** = grief with ρ = in-relation |
| regret | retrospective settled-bad with π-was-mine (my act moved it) |
| remorse | regret with h = the relation harmed; its repair path runs through the other |
| **guilt** | settled-bad own-act, relation reparable (π to repair exists) |
| **shame** | settled-bad *self* — h itself booked bad under witness; π unclear, which is why shame immobilizes where guilt mobilizes |
| anger | c *contested* (⊤) touching one's standing, with π > 0 — the press to settle in one's own direction; indignation adds normative warrant; **rage** is anger at π → 0 |
| envy | comparative h: other's holding registered against own lack; jealousy: a held relation threatened by a third |
| gratitude | registration of holding whose cost fell upstream on another (received grace's interior) |
| trust / safety | standing-holding relied upon (faith note) |
| loneliness / solitude | unheld, imposed / unheld, chosen (μ alone) |
| boredom | no open cell worth the lean — budget surplus with nothing indexed; flatness felt from inside |
| acedia | the good settled and offered, reception refused — sorrow-*at*, a stance toward one's own h |
| nostalgia | retrospective settled-good, π = 0 — held-in-memory, unreachable; the calibration note's thermodynamic gloss (retained non-predictive content is taxed) applies literally |
| awe | encounter with a cell that exceeds the frame — settlement-demand *defeated* rather than released; wonder's involuntary cousin, the apophatic affect |

The discriminations are the system's daily bread, and three deserve emphasis because whole literatures blur them.
**Fear / dread / anxiety** differ on σ and cell-binding alone: open cell, booked cell, unbound cell — three different repairs (verify; prepare; *bind* — give the anxiety an object, which is why naming helps anxiety and does nothing for dread).
**Guilt / shame** differ on target and path — act versus self, repair-path present versus absent — predicting their divergent motivational signatures, which the empirical guilt/shame literature (Tangney's program) documents in exactly this pattern.
**Solitude / loneliness** differ on μ only, which is why reframing interventions (restoring chosenness) can move one toward the other without changing the position at all.

## 6. The lexicon: degeneracies, gaps, and the cross-linguistic test

If lexicons are tilings of one space, three predictions follow that no ancestor makes.

**Degeneracy.** Some English words are one address twice-named (the system should say which pairs are true synonyms up to register), and some are *polysemous across addresses* — "jealousy" for envy; "hope" carrying the six referents the hope note untangled, which this system re-derives as six distinct addresses sharing a valence.

**Unnamed addresses.** The space has coherent, occupied addresses English does not lexicalize — and the prediction is that *other languages do*, at above-chance rates, at precisely the specified coordinates.
Candidates already checkable against the lexicography of untranslatables:
German **Fernweh** (unheld position, prospective, lean-good, *chosen* — the wanderer's ache; English has only borrowed "wanderlust," losing the ache);
Japanese **amae** (reliance-received: the affect of being permitted to lean — the interior of h from the held side, which English's independence-tilted tiling leaves nameless);
Portuguese **saudade** (retrospective-good, unreachable, but ρ = *in-relation* — what distinguishes it from nostalgia is that the lost is still addressed);
Welsh **hiraeth**, Korean **han**, Czech **litost** — each a plausible address-with-flag this program should place formally rather than gesture at.
The strong form of the prediction: lexical gaps are not random; they cluster where a culture's tiling runs a wall (English: the chosen/imposed wall through unheld positions is well-lexicalized on one side only).

**The computable test.**
The cross-linguistic semantics of emotion now has a dataset: colexification networks over thousands of languages (the CLICS database; Jackson et al. 2019, *Science*, which found emotion colexification universally structured by valence and arousal with substantial cultural variation in the finer structure).
The generator predicts **additional, specific structure**: controlling for valence and arousal, colexification probability should fall with distance in the settlement coordinates — σ-distance, π-distance, μ-distance — and the walls of frequent non-colexification should align with single-coordinate flips (few languages should merge guilt-address with shame-address *and* also merge their neighbors; the fear/anger colexification Jackson found variable across families should track the ⊤-vs-⊥ and π coordinates).
This is a reanalysis an existing dataset supports, and §9.1 stakes it.

## 7. Repair paths: why the resolution of an affect is written in its address

Because coordinates specify what is wrong, addresses specify what would repair — and the repairs differ *by coordinate*, not by valence:

σ-repairs: verification for open-cell fear (the checkable settles); witnessed reopening for premature collapse; binding for anxiety (index the cell before anything else is possible).
π-repairs: restore the path — experienced control, the lean note's manufactured capacity; anger's legitimate settlement procedures; the procedural-certainty interventions the audits found best-supported.
h-repairs: re-holding — social baseline, standing-conferral, the CTRA-reversal targets.
μ-repairs: modality conversion — the chosen-ness restored (solitude from loneliness; the freedom note's consent machinery).
ρ-repairs: re-addressing — lament recovered from despair; the grievance heard; witness.

Two consequences with empirical faces.
**Granularity is address-resolution**: the emotion-differentiation literature (Barrett's granularity program) finds that people who discriminate their affects finely regulate better; this system says why — finer discrimination is finer coordinate-reading, and coordinates are repair-instructions.
The prediction sharpens the finding: granularity should help *specifically* by matching repairs to coordinates, and mismatched repair (verification offered to dread; reassurance offered to anxiety; holding offered where the path is what's missing) should fail in the patterned ways clinicians already recognize.
**Therapy is coordinate movement**: exposure works σ and its consolidation chemistry; behavioral activation works π; the therapeutic alliance works h before anything else can work at all — the common-factors literature's alliance-primacy finding, re-derived as: h is the axis that gates the others' repairability, because reopening is only steerable under holding (settlement-pharmacology note, H4).

## 8. Objections and replies

**O1. Seven axes is over-fitting; any affect can be assigned somewhere post hoc.**
*Reply.* The axes were not free parameters: five of seven were forced by prior notes solving unrelated problems, and the biology nominated π, h, and σ independently (three chemistries, §3). The system's constraint shows in what it *cannot* do: it cannot distinguish affects that differ on no coordinate (it predicts true synonymy), and it cannot place an affect at an incoherent address (no settled-open cell). §9's tests are stated over pre-registered placements precisely to close the post-hoc door.

**O2. This is appraisal theory with new notation.**
*Reply.* The debt is acknowledged in §2, and the differences are the content: a formal substrate that derives the dimensions and their composition law (the grading constraint is a theorem, not a factor rotation); the doxastic unification appraisal theory never attempted; axis-anchoring in identified regulatory circuits rather than self-report structure; and the lexicon predictions of §6, which appraisal theory has never generated. If the notation were removed and these four survived, the note would stand; they are the note.

**O3. Constructionism defeats universal grids: emotion categories are cultural artifacts.**
*Reply.* The grid is not a claim about categories; it is a claim about the *space categories tile* — and construction is the system's friend, supplying the mechanism (conceptualization) by which addresses become named experiences. The discriminating question is whether tilings vary freely or along predictable walls; §6's test decides it, and a free-variation result would genuinely damage the system (§9.1's kill condition).

**O4. Introspective reports cannot carry coordinate assignments.**
*Reply.* The assignments are not carried by introspection: σ, π, μ are experimentally manipulable (that is what the three biochemistry notes' paradigms *are*), h has transcriptomic and autonomic readers, and the lexicon test (§6) uses no introspection at all. Self-report enters as the dependent variable — which category a configuration is called — exactly where the theory predicts it.

**O5. The doxastic unification proves too much: believing p and fearing p feel nothing alike.**
*Reply.* They are different orders of the same space, and the phenomenal difference is the prediction: knowledge-order stances have thin phenomenology (the "feeling of knowing" literature notwithstanding) precisely because they are structure, not grade — the constraint says valence rides *on* them, not *in* them. What the unification buys is the interaction architecture (§4): budget-competition without structural interference, testable where the folk theory of motivated belief-writing fails.

## 9. Hypotheses

Each with its kill condition; placements to be pre-registered before any test.

1. **Colexification geometry.** Controlling valence and arousal, cross-linguistic colexification probability decreases with settlement-coordinate distance, and non-colexification walls align with single-coordinate flips (CLICS / Jackson et al. reanalysis). *Killed if* coordinate distance adds no predictive power over valence-arousal, or walls fall randomly with respect to coordinates.
2. **The gap prediction.** Pre-register the addresses English leaves unnamed; survey lexicalization across a balanced language sample. *Killed if* attested "untranslatables" scatter over the space without clustering at the pre-registered gaps.
3. **Single-coordinate transitions.** Within-subject manipulation of one coordinate at fixed others shifts categorical self-report along the predicted edge: efficacy granted over a contested cell moves fear-reports toward anger; settlement of a feared outcome moves fear toward dread (with the paradoxical calm the clinic reports); chosen-ness reframing moves loneliness toward solitude. *Killed if* categories fail to track the manipulated coordinate or move along unpredicted edges.
4. **Repair-matching.** Regulation interventions succeed as a function of coordinate-match (binding for unbound cells, path-restoration for π-deficits, holding for h-deficits), and granularity's benefits are mediated by match-rate. *Killed if* mismatched repairs perform at parity.
5. **The grading constraint.** Valence manipulations alter allocation (attention, scrutiny, verification funding) without directly altering booked contents; knowledge-order interventions (REBUS-class prior relaxation) shift doxastic coordinates first, with affect-space consequences following the geometry — increased open-cell affects, direction steered by holding. *Killed if* clean valence manipulation writes bookings directly, at matched allocation.
6. **Address-specific biology.** Neighboring addresses differing on one coordinate differ on that coordinate's biological reader and not systematically on others: guilt vs. shame on repair-path/controllability circuitry; loneliness vs. solitude on CTRA trajectory at matched contact; dread vs. fear on prediction-error dynamics (the booked future generates none). *Killed if* the readers do not dissociate along the predicted axis.

## 10. Doors this opens

Marked as openings, not claims; each is a candidate next note.

1. **A clinical cartography.** Diagnosis as address-mapping and therapy as coordinate movement (§7) suggests a transdiagnostic reformulation: disorders as *characteristic regions and stuck transitions* (anxiety as chronic cell-unbinding; depression as π-collapse generalizing across cells; trauma as settlement that will not stay settled) — with treatment selection derived from coordinates rather than syndrome labels.
2. **Structures of feeling, formalized.** Because distributive conditions set coordinates (precarity imposes open-endedness; abandonment imposes unholding), a population's affect-distribution is a *social readout* — Raymond Williams's structures of feeling become measurable: the political economy level of the trinities note and this note are one system at two scales.
3. **The virtue bridge.** Affects are points; the virtues of the theology register are *policies* over the space — faith, hope, and care as standing dispositions about how to move through addresses (hold the open; lean warranted; commit the flow). The virtue-triad note owed from the faith note's §9.5 now has its formal home.
4. **Historical semantics of affect.** If addresses persist while names drift, the history of emotion vocabularies (acedia's disappearance and secular return; the invention of "boredom"; honor-culture affects fading with their institutions) becomes address-history: occupation without a name, names without occupation — a program for the history-of-emotions literature with actual coordinates.
5. **Substrate-neutral phenomenology, handled with care.** The coordinates are defined over settlement machinery, not neurons. Any agent with cells, leans, paths, and holdings has addresses. What follows for artificial agents is *not* that they feel, but that there is now a principled vocabulary for which configurations they occupy — and a research question, flagged as such, about what the interoceptive-readout layer adds.
6. **The teleologies weld.** The bearing-space (settings/weightings over settledness axes) and this space share their generator; an actor's *ideology* and *emotional life* should be jointly derivable — the doomer/prepper pair of the teleologies note differ on one weighting and should differ predictably in affect-distribution. One instrument, two projections.
