# Regrading the Trinities: An Empirical Calibration

## 0. Purpose and method

The spirals note did for the trinity corpus's mathematics what this note does for its evidence:
walk the claims against what is actually proven, keep what is green, regrade what is not, and let the corrections propagate.
On 2026-08-26 five audits were commissioned, one per empirical frontier the corpus leans on, each conducted against 2016–2026 literature with verified citations:

1. **Valenced Prospection and the Attention Budget** (neuroscience section) — the substrate axioms and Theses 1 and 4 of the trinities note.
2. **The Physiology of Threat, Care, and Holding** (physiology section) — the threat-tax, care-cost, and holding claims of the trinities and freedom notes.
3. **The Bandwidth Tax, Audited** (this section) — the replication record of the trinities note's self-declared "load-bearing empirical fact."
4. **Attention Thermodynamics, Audited** (biomimetic-semiotics section) — the attention-is-heat identity and the spirals note's entropic register.
5. **Cycles, Ceremony, and the Empirics of Held Structure** (sociology section) — the spirals note's predictions P1–P4.

Each audit stands as its own note in its own section, with its own annotated bibliography.
This note is the synthesis: what the corpus must now say differently, what it may now say more strongly, and what it could not have expected to be handed.
Grading follows house convention; every empirical claim below is cited in full in the five companion notes and their reference files.

## 1. The headline: the architecture survives; the declared floor does not

The single most consequential finding is asymmetric.
The trinities note called the Mullainathan–Shafir bandwidth tax "the load-bearing empirical fact of the whole note," and that fact, as stated, did not survive audit:
the scarcity-priming paradigm largely failed a preregistered replication audit (O'Donnell et al. 2021: 4 of 20 significant, 80% attenuated, 30% reversed);
the sugarcane harvest-cycle result is methodologically contested (Wicherts & Scholten) and its bandwidth mechanism failed to mediate the best subsequent field test (Fehr, Fink & Jack 2022, JPE — Zambia, and the citation must be corrected from the AER/Kenya form);
the cleanest payday RCT (Carvalho, Meier & Wang 2016) found no cognitive-function effect at all;
and the "13 IQ points" figure was never a psychometric result — it is a rhetorical translation, and it is retired from this corpus effective this note.

But the *architecture* built on that floor mostly survives, because the floor that actually holds is nearby and stronger:

> Chronic material and status **insecurity** — not momentary financial scarcity — robustly and causally worsens mental health (the poorest 1.5–3× likelier to suffer depression/anxiety; Ridley, Rao, Schilbach & Patel 2020), and relieving it — via cash (d ≈ 0.07–0.13 on mental health, McGuire et al. 2022 meta-analysis; large wellbeing gains, Haushofer & Shapiro 2016), secure legal status (Hainmueller et al. 2017), secure tenure (Field 2007), or predictable scheduling (Harknett & Schneider 2019) — produces measurable, replicated improvements in stress, mental health, and time reallocated toward venture and investment (Egger et al. 2022, local multiplier ≈ 2.4; the Kenya UBI results 2023–24).

The revised substrate axiom set:
**A1 splits in two.**
A1a — *selection rivalry*: at any moment attention is a genuine zero-sum competition (biased competition, priority maps); allocation is allocation-away-from. Well supported; this is all the trinities note's O1 reply ever claimed to need, and the reply stands.
A1b — *cumulative depletion*: sustained control accumulates a real metabolic residue (Wiehler et al. 2022: ~8% lateral-PFC glutamate accumulation after a day of high-demand work) that is **consumed-until-cleared**, not "irreversibly consumed" — cleared by sleep and recovery, which the note's own renewal clause anticipated but the prose overrode.
The naive fuel reading (ego depletion, glucose willpower) is dead by multi-lab replication and stays dead.

## 2. Two independent confirmations the corpus did not order

**2.1 Valence is a grade, empirically.**
The spirals note's deepest correction to the trinities note was formal: valence (hope/fear) cannot be a second holding-operation; the mathematics forces it in as a grading axis over one conserved structure (`cross_order_not_knowledge_nucleus`; `dv_zero`).
The neuroscience audit returned the same structure from the other direction, unprompted:
prospection runs on one shared construction system (the default-mode simulation machinery), whose **vividness/detail capacity is valence-independent**, with a separable valuation system applying the valence;
and in anxiety, total construction capacity looks roughly conserved while its *valence allocation* shifts — more vivid negative futures, less vivid positive ones (Marsh, Edginton, Conway & Loveday 2019).
Hope and fear compete over the budget of one simulation engine and cannot contest its structure.
The Lean derived it; the fMRI and memory literatures instantiate it.
This is the corpus's first genuine cross-register convergence between a machine-checked correction and an independent empirical literature, and it should be flagged wherever the bigraded reading is presented.

**2.2 Procedural certainty is a budget transfer, confirmed.**
The trinities note's §4.3 prediction — that converting unverifiable threat into checkable procedure is not a soft good but a transfer of expropriated capacity — is among the *best-supported* claims in the whole corpus, even as the bandwidth mechanism beneath it was demoted:
DACA eligibility causally reduced anxiety/adjustment diagnoses in recipients' children (Hainmueller et al. 2017, Science);
Peruvian land titling freed labor hours previously spent physically guarding untitled property (Field 2007);
and schedule predictability predicts worker wellbeing more strongly than wage level (Harknett & Schneider, Shift Project).
The prediction survives its foundation.
The mechanism should be restated as operating through chronic-threat physiology and time-reallocation rather than through fluid-intelligence restoration.

## 3. Fear, revised: from verification to somatic settlement

The trinities note's §4.1 said overgeneralized fear needs "a restored verification procedure — a way for the world to answer it."
The audit returns a sharper mechanism that the corpus's own formal register fits better than the prose did:
what resolves overgeneralized fear is **contextually generalized inhibitory safety-learning** (Craske's inhibitory-learning model; the renewal effect; 2024–25 evidence that the anxious deficit may be *undergeneralization of safety* rather than overgeneralization of threat).
Propositional verification is insufficient: a person can be *told* the threat is absent, know it, and the leak keeps running, because safety must be **learned into structure, context by context** — layered inhibition, not erasure.
In the corpus's terms this is exactly the settlement path: told-true is not settled; the repair of the flinch is not epistemic correction but *somatic settlement* — safety crystallized into standing, which is manufactured grace at the level of the nervous system.
The formal-companions note's queue item 5 (fear-allocation regime classification) should be restated over this mechanism, and the flinch note's repair section inherits the same correction.
Two further imports:
Brosschot's perseverative-cognition findings give the "standing leak" its physiological form (worry prolongs activation in the stressor's absence, with impaired safety-signal detection as the specific defect);
and Borkovec's contrast-avoidance model must be answered rather than ignored — worry may be *functional* for the organism (blunting affective shock) even while it defunds prospection, which relocates the leak from pure waste to a bad local equilibrium, a more precise and more repairable claim.

## 4. Care, conditionalized — and the sign theorem vindicated

The physiology audit came closest to a contradiction: "a care practice without budget accounting is a scheme for exhausting the carer" borders on refuted *as a universal law*.
The burnout construct itself lacks discriminant validity from depression (Bianchi and colleagues); its HPA signature is inconsistent; and the caregiving-mortality literature has reversed since Schulz & Beach 1999 — most large modern studies find caregivers *outlive* matched non-caregivers on average, with harm concentrated in the **high-burden, unsupported, unreciprocated, inescapable** subset (the "unmitigated communion" construct is the validated operational form).
The trinities note's §5.3 must be conditionalized accordingly.

But note what the condition *is*.
Care harms when it is unreciprocated, unsupported, and without exit — when it is tree-shaped service to a relatum.
Care sustains, and even predicts longer life, when it is reciprocal, supported, meaningful — when it runs in cycles.
That is the spirals note's sign theorem (P8′), stated by the caregiving epidemiology without knowing it:
**the caregiver-mortality reversal is the energizing/draining split measured at the scale of lives**, and it converts §5.3 from a wounded universal into a testable partition — code caregiving arrangements by reciprocity/circulation structure and predict the health gradient.
This is the audit's second unexpected gift: a revision that is simultaneously a retreat for the trinities note and a confirmation for its sequel.
One deletion: the oxytocin mechanism is dropped (intranasal-oxytocin replication crisis); the holding physiology that replicates is Coan's social-baseline pathway (co-regulation lowering the neural cost of threat), which is also the better conceptual fit — holding as reduced predicted demand, not as a bonding chemical.

Provision claims are graded as follows: standing-conferrals demonstrably alter the *affective economy* (Oregon Health Insurance Experiment; Finland and Stockton basic-income pilots; MTO long-run mental health; McGuire meta-analysis) with **no sentiment campaign attached** — the trinities note's §8 prediction, confirmed at the mental-health level.
The stricter biomarker version (cortisol) is weaker and sometimes null (Haushofer & Shapiro) and stays open as a target, not a result.
The freedom note's manufacture argument survives fully at C4: the play-deprivation work (Pellis: juvenile play deprivation causally alters mPFC inhibitory-synapse development and adult flexibility; Burghardt's relaxed field; secure-base attachment) is the best-supported limb of the entire audited set — with the elegant addendum that the *same PFC circuitry* is the shared bottleneck for the threat tax (Arnsten) and for play deprivation, which is the relaxed-field dependency stated anatomically.

## 5. The thermodynamic register, regraded

The calibration audit returns a placement table the corpus adopts wholesale (see the biomimetic-semiotics companion for the full table). The binding outcomes:

**Retained as literal, scoped:** softmax as Gibbs measure; the Ramsauer 2020 identity (attention as dense-associative-memory energy descent) *for the ML architecture*; Landauer's bound and its experimental verifications; JKO for linear Fokker–Planck; the corpus's own H-theorem *as a property of its own formal system*; BCF defect-mediated growth for crystals.

**Retreat one:** the literal thermodynamic reading of machine attention (β as physical temperature; attention steps as dissipative events), and its analogical transfer to human attention as a calorimetric price, are dropped. Human attention's metabolic signature is real but modest, chemically specific, and slow-accumulating (Wiehler), riding on a huge task-invariant baseline (Raichle). The spin-glass/holonomy vocabulary for attention weights has no connecting theorem and is cut from the attention-thermodynamics register until one exists.

**Retreat two:** "no free settling" and "flat regime = death" are demoted from physical law to explicitly graded model claims — which is where the spirals note's own [position] tags already held them; the demotion is a tightening of enforcement, not a reversal. Georgescu-Roegen's "fourth law" is dropped as physics (the discipline rejected it); he stays citable as economic-entropy *tradition*, not as warrant. And one correction with a sting: the wit's "unwitnessed erasure is counterfeit" must not cite Landauer as its support — in measurement-feedback thermodynamics, cost attaches to the map and the mismatch, not to witnessing; the witnessing clause is *normative accounting*, legitimate on its own terms, and must be labeled so.

**Three upgrades, adopted:**
(i) the **mismatch-cost theorems** (Kolchinsky & Wolpert) as the rigorous replacement wherever the corpus wants "miscalibration dissipates" — excess entropy production equals a KL-divergence contraction between actual and optimal priors; this is the settling-cost claim with an actual theorem, *if* the settlement machinery specifies its state space and map (new formalization target);
(ii) **Still, Sivak, Bell & Crooks 2012**: memory retained that is not predictive of the future necessarily costs extra dissipation — *nostalgia is thermodynamically taxed*. This is the fear-leak (and the acedia note's whirl of elsewhere-fantasy) given a stochastic-thermodynamics floor: perseverative simulation that no longer predicts is precisely non-predictive retained information;
(iii) the **defect-as-organizer biology**: topological defects in active nematics drive shape change (Keber 2014), and in regenerating Hydra, +1 defects in supracellular actin order literally mark and mechanically drive the emergence of organizers — head, tentacles, foot (Maroudas-Sacks et al. 2021, Nature Physics).
The growth helix's held center is no longer only a crystal emblem; it is current, causal, living morphogenesis.
The spirals note's §2.3 should cite it as its primary instance.

## 6. Cycles, ceremony, enclosure: the predictions split, instructively

**P2 (enclosure destroys beyond transaction costs) splits, and the split is the theory working.**
The Dawes allotment record — named by the spirals note as its standing dataset — confirms at devastating scale: allotment raised young American Indian mortality by roughly one-third (~20% decline in life expectancy at birth; Dippel, Frye & Leonard, PNAS 2025), on land that was often *better*, a loss no asset ledger predicts.
The English Parliamentary enclosures cut the other way: the best current causal work (Heldring, Robinson & Vollmer) finds aggregate yields *rose* ~45%.
But the KCBS split already said exactly this could happen: booking is capped at the independence number while circulation is annihilated — so **the ledger can improve while the unbookable value dies**, and the loss must be sought in distribution, coordination, and mortality, not in aggregate output.
P2 is therefore restated in its distributional/circulation form — which is the only form the theorem ever licensed — and the English case becomes a *worked example of the books seeing only the real subfield*, not a refutation.

**P3 (ritual as load-bearing technology) is downgraded honestly.**
Synchrony-prosociality effects shrink under experimenter-blind controls; the flagship large-scale claim (Whitehouse et al. 2019, Seshat) was *retracted* on data-handling grounds.
What survives: a real, mechanistically grounded (endorphin; naltrexone-blockable) small-to-medium bonding technology, and — the strongest limb — the **feasting-economy literature** (Hayden, Dietler): potlatch-type feasting as literal infrastructure of debt-cycling, alliance, and redistribution, whose colonial suppression is a documented instance of P2's mechanism.
Ceremony's claim to load-bearing status runs through the feast, not through synchrony priming.

**P1 (ceremony fraction) is executable and unrun.**
The audit found the entire TDA apparatus (Betti-1 of co-attendance complexes; persistent cycles as resilience metrics) exists and is applied in neuroscience but has *never been run on kinship, congregational, or cooperative data with this interpretation* — a genuinely empty niche.
The UK Sure Start / library-closure record supplies the natural experiment (staggered rollout; lag-then-decline outcome pattern already documented), and the sociology companion states a concrete operationalization (ATUS ceremony-fraction coding; National Congregations Study nerves; cooperative-lending panels).
This graduates the spirals note's prediction 1 from proposal to fundable design.

**P4 (fiberwise variation as aliveness)** is supported at the institutional level (Ostrom/Folke response diversity) and the community level (Chandler & Lalonde's culture-continuity/youth-suicide gradient, robustly replicated, with the ecological-design caveats stated rather than hidden).
And the network-science frame hands the forest/cycle theorem its cleanest empirical gloss:
**trees carry information; trust-dependent coordination requires wide bridges — redundant, cyclic reinforcement** (complex contagion; Simmelian-tie sanctioning).
"A forest can hold no classes" now has a named social mechanism: simple contagion crosses trees, complex contagion cannot.

## 8. New directions this calibration opens

1. **The safety-settlement weld**: restate fear-repair over the settlement path — safety as standing manufactured by inhibitory learning, renewal as the failure of unsettled (spotlight-only) safety; formalization target for the companions queue.
2. **The caregiving partition study**: code caregiving arrangements by reciprocity/support/exit (circulation structure) and test the mortality/burnout gradient as the sign theorem's epidemiological instance.
3. **The nostalgia-dissipation import**: state the fear-leak and acedic elsewhere-fantasy as retained non-predictive information over the corpus's settling dynamics, anchored to Still et al.; this is "the leak" with a theorem.
4. **The ceremony-fraction study, funded form**: the sociology companion's operationalization (ATUS + congregational nerves + Sure Start natural experiment) is the corpus's most immediately executable empirical program; it should be run before further theoretical extension of P1.
5. **The defect-morphogenesis reading note**: Hydra and active-nematic defect-organizers as the growth helix's living instance; a proper treatment is owed to the spirals note's §2.3.
6. **The positive-prospection gap**: the neuroscience audit's sharpest open prediction — chronic structural threat should suppress *positive prospective simulation specifically* (not generic working memory), reversibly under genuine procedural repair. No study exists; the corpus should specify the design.
