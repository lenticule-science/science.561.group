# Capacity, Pattern, and Flood

## 0. What this welds, and what it corrects

Two mechanism notes of 2026-08-26 — *The Endocannabinoid System as Holding Chemistry* and *Tone, Demand, and Flood* — were commissioned to deepen a molecular thread.
They returned something larger: the endocannabinoid system is the cleanest physical instance the corpus has found of its own central principle, and in being that, it **corrects the corpus's definition of standing.**

The trinities note defined grace-as-standing as holding that persists without ongoing attention, and illustrated it with rights, easements, kinship recognition, commons rules — durable *things*, crystallized once and thereafter held.
The tacit picture was a stored good.
On-demand signaling shows a third possibility that the corpus's vocabulary could not express, and §2 argues it is the right one.

§1 compresses the architecture; §2 states the correction; §3 derives the four-level intervention typology that is this note's principal construction; §4 reads rimonabant; §5 reads burnout at the receptor; §6 takes the inversion case; §7 propagates the corrections; §8 states the hypotheses with an explicit warning about their inheritance; §9 sources.
Mechanism claims belong to the two source notes and carry their citations. The correction, the typology, and the social readings are this note's, graded [synthesis] or [conjecture].

## 1. The architecture, compressed

Endocannabinoids are not stored. There is no vesicle, no packet, no reservoir.
2-AG is cut from membrane lipid by DAGLα at the moment postsynaptic activity calls for it; anandamide is built by NAPE-PLD and redundant routes.
The signal travels *backward* — post- to presynaptic — and is destroyed locally within seconds by enzymes at complementary subcellular addresses: MAGL presynaptic, where the signal must be cleared for the next round; FAAH postsynaptic, near where anandamide is made.
The composite fact, in the second note's words: an eCB signal "does not exist as a standing quantity anywhere in the brain outside of the instant of its synthesis."

What *is* permanent is the precursor phospholipid pool and the enzymatic machinery positioned at specific addresses.
Contrast the two familiar architectures: a vesicular transmitter is synthesized in bulk and held in a releasable pool — a reserve that can be drawn down; a circulating hormone is a bulk concentration sensed identically by every receptor-bearing cell regardless of local history — addressed to no one.
Endocannabinoid signaling is neither. It is addressed, by construction, only to the compartment and the interval that generated it.

## 2. The correction: standing is capacity, not supply

[synthesis] The corpus has been carrying an ambiguity it never had to resolve, because at social scale both readings look alike.
"Standing" could mean **a stored good that persists** — a right sitting in a statute book, drawn on when needed.
Or it could mean **a maintained capacity to generate the good on demand** — machinery kept in place, at addresses, producing nothing until called.

The eCB system is unambiguously the second, and the second is the better model for what the corpus has been describing all along.
A right is not a substance in a vault; it is an *enzyme at an address* — courts, clerks, procedures, and the practice of honoring it — that produces the holding at the moment it is invoked and is otherwise quiet.
Kinship standing is not a stored quantity of relatedness; it is the maintained readiness of people to answer when called.
The commons rule is not the parchment; it is the retained competence to apply it.

Three things follow immediately.

**It resolves the spirals note's O4.** That note was asked: if relational deposits are outside the settling flow, why do relationships die of neglect? Its answer — deposits are practice-indexed — was right but under-explained. The correction supplies the mechanism: what is deposited is *capacity*, and capacity is machinery, and machinery requires maintenance. Nothing was stored to decay; what lapses is a readiness. This is also why reunion after long estrangement is possible in a way it is not with strangers: the machinery is disused, not absent.

**It explains why standing is cheap to hold and expensive to build.** A quiet enzyme at an address costs almost nothing to keep. Installing it — building courts, transmitting a practice, raising a person into reliable expectation — is the whole cost, paid upstream. This is the Augustine/Pelagius resolution restated at the level of mechanism, and it sharpens the hysteresis finding of the slow-arrow note: what is slow to rebuild is not a stock but an apparatus.

**It disciplines the corpus's language.** "Zero marginal attention" was always right; "a holding that persists" was misleading. Nothing persists except the capacity. The good itself is generated fresh, locally, each time — which is why grace can be simultaneously unearned in the moment and paid for upstream, with no contradiction and no mysticism.

## 3. Four levels of intervention, and their relation to pattern

[synthesis; the corpus's principal construction from this weld]
The eCB literature furnishes a complete set of places to intervene in a routed system, and — this is the point — they are **not interchangeable**, because each stands in a different relation to the pattern that carries the information.

**Level 1 — Substrate. Changes the ceiling; touches neither when nor where.**
Both endocannabinoids are built from arachidonic acid esterified in membrane phospholipid, so dietary omega-6/omega-3 balance sets an upstream limit on how much signal can be synthesized at all (Alvheim et al. 2012: raising dietary linoleic acid roughly tripled tissue 2-AG and AEA). Substrate intervention raises or lowers what is *possible* without specifying a single event.

**Level 2 — Decay. Changes persistence; preserves origination.**
FAAH and MAGL inhibition extend signals *the system itself generated*, at the places it generated them. The pattern of origination is untouched; only its duration changes. This is why enzyme-level intervention is compatible with the system's own addressing in a way receptor flooding is not.

**Level 3 — Orthosteric. Overwrites pattern.**
An exogenous agonist binds the endogenous pocket and activates every receptor within reach of its systemic concentration, "entirely independent of local synaptic history." The second note's formulation is the one to keep: such a drug "cannot distinguish a synapse that just computed something worth suppressing from a synapse that did nothing at all." Whatever information lived in the restriction of the signal to a compartment and an instant is gone — overwritten by a signal that is the same everywhere.

**Level 4 — Allosteric. Amplifies pattern without originating it.**
A positive allosteric modulator binds a distinct site and does little or nothing alone; it potentiates whatever endogenous ligand is *already* bound. Where no signal is arriving, a systemic dose does essentially nothing; where a synapse is actively generating 2-AG, the same dose amplifies that local event. Negative allosteric modulators (including cannabidiol's action) run the logic in reverse: efficacy-limiting rather than occupancy-blocking, damping only where signaling is already elevated.
Honest status, which travels with the claim: the PAM case is entirely preclinical — no CB1 PAM chemotype has been reported in human trials, and some chemotypes show intrinsic efficacy that muddies the clean story.

The social mapping [conjecture — the molecular result licenses the *shape* of this typology, not its truth at social scale]:

| Level | Molecular | Social analogue |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate | precursor lipid pool | provision, capability floors — raising what is possible |
| Decay | FAAH/MAGL inhibition | procedural certainty, tenure, notice — changing how fast threat and uncertainty clear |
| Orthosteric | THC, rimonabant | mandate, imposed schedule, administered participation |
| Allosteric | PAM/NAM | matching funds, opt-in support, aid that arrives *because* someone moved |

The typology's value is that it separates goods the corpus had been lumping.
Provision and procedural certainty are *different levels*, not two flavors of one intervention — the first sets a ceiling, the second changes decay — which predicts they are complements rather than substitutes, and that neither can compensate for the absence of the other past a point (§8.4).
And it gives the corpus a name for the intervention class it has always struggled to specify: the help that does not impose.
**Genuine support is allosteric.** It cannot act unless the person has already moved; it amplifies the move rather than supplying it.

## 4. Rimonabant: manufactured standing done wrong

Rimonabant was a CB1 inverse agonist approved in Europe in 2006 for obesity. It worked exactly as designed at its target, producing real weight loss by blocking CB1 systemically. It was withdrawn worldwide by 2008 after markedly increased depression, anxiety, and suicidality, and every major CB1-antagonist obesity program halted within months.

The second note's diagnosis is the one the corpus should adopt: a molecule engaging "the CB1 receptor" as a monolithic target — without respect to which synapses were using tonic anandamide to buffer stress reactivity and which were using phasic 2-AG for local plasticity — "cannot help but produce systemic psychiatric liability, because it is blocking a signal whose entire function depended on being local and intermittent."

[synthesis] This is the freedom note's imposed-holding cell and the spirals note's booking-annihilates-circulation, occurring in a drug that reached market.
Rimonabant is the strongest available case study of a **well-intentioned, correctly-targeted, globally-applied intervention on a system whose value was its locality** — and the field's subsequent turn toward peripherally-restricted and allosteric approaches is a discipline-wide correction toward respecting routing.
Paternalism, in this frame, is not merely presumptuous; it is *orthosteric* — it supplies the good uniformly to everyone in reach, erasing the pattern of who was asking for what, and its characteristic harm is not the good it supplies but the signal it overwrites.

## 5. Burnout at the receptor: the tonic/phasic asymmetry

The pharmacology contains a dissociation the corpus should take seriously, because it maps onto a distinction the corpus already has.

Chronically elevating the **phasic** signal — repeated high-dose MAGL inhibition — produces cross-tolerance, dependence signs, impaired plasticity, and genuine CB1 downregulation. Schlosburg et al. named the endpoint precisely: *functional antagonism of the endocannabinoid system.* Push the event-signal continuously and the receptor protects itself until the system behaves as though the signal were blocked.
Chronically elevating the **tonic** signal — FAAH inhibition — sustains its effects across repeated dosing without CB1 downregulation, without cross-tolerance, without precipitated withdrawal.

[synthesis] **Background can be raised sustainably. Events cannot.**
Applied to the corpus's own terms: standing (tonic, background, low-amplitude, always-on) tolerates sustained elevation; spotlight (phasic, event-scale, high-amplitude) does not — sustained demand for elevated episodic output drives the system into functional antagonism.

That is burnout, and it is a better model than depletion.
Depletion says the carer runs out of a substance. Functional antagonism says the *receiving machinery downregulates* under chronic elevated demand, so that the same input eventually produces less than it did, and the endpoint resembles blockade of a signal that is in fact still arriving.
It predicts what the depletion model does not: that recovery requires *time at low demand* for receptor resensitization rather than replenishment of a store, that the fatigue outlasts rest proportionally to the chronicity rather than the intensity of the demand, and that the state is phenomenologically like not-caring rather than like exhaustion — which is what carers actually report, and what the acedia tradition described as sorrow-at-the-good rather than tiredness.
The physiology audit's conditionalization of the care thesis (harm concentrates in unsupported, inescapable, unreciprocated care) is consistent: those are precisely the conditions that make demand chronic and elevation non-negotiable.

## 6. Inversion: the cannabinoid hyperemesis result

THC is a well-established antiemetic — the basis of prescribed dronabinol and nabilone. Yet chronic heavy use produces, in a subset, cyclic severe nausea and vomiting. The mechanism is genuinely unresolved (regionally selective desensitization, efficacy shifts at desensitized receptors, TRPV1 involvement, pharmacokinetic accumulation are all live), and the corpus should not pretend otherwise.

What every candidate mechanism shares is the structural point [synthesis]: **continuous, non-gated supply of a good designed to arrive intermittently can produce not less of the good, and not merely tolerance, but its opposite.**

This licenses a prediction the corpus could not previously state, and it should be stated carefully, as a shape rather than a certainty (§8.3): administered goods whose endogenous form is intermittent and gated — reassurance, attention, oversight offered as care, hope supplied from above — should show, at high chronicity, a subpopulation in which the good inverts rather than merely saturating.
The corpus already has candidate instances it never connected: administered hope as pacification (hope note, referent C); surveillance as care that manufactures the distrust it manages (faith note, §6); the well-kept cell (freedom note, §4).
Inversion-at-chronicity gives them one predicted signature and a place to look for the dose-response.

## 7. Corrections propagated

- **Grace-as-standing is redefined**: not a holding that persists, but a *maintained capacity to generate holding at an address*. Every corpus location treating standing as a stored good should be revised. (Trinities §6.1; spirals §5.2; faith §5.)
- **The spirals note's O4 gains its mechanism**: deposits are capacity, capacity is machinery, machinery lapses without practice — and disuse is not absence, which is why estranged kin can resume.
- **Provision and procedural certainty are different intervention levels** (substrate and decay), predicted to be complements rather than substitutes. The freedom note's manufacture argument should be restated with the two separated.
- **Paternalism has a mechanism-name and a design alternative**: orthosteric versus allosteric. The freedom note's imposed-holding cell is no longer only a modality error; it is a routing error, and the corrective is not less help but help that cannot act unless the person has already moved.
- **Burnout is functional antagonism, not depletion** (§5), with distinct predictions about recovery kinetics and phenomenology.
- **One retreat, stated plainly.** Everything in §3's social column, §5's social reading, and §6's prediction is [conjecture]: the molecular findings license the *shape* of these claims and nothing more. A four-level typology that is exact in lipid signaling may be merely suggestive in institutional design, and the corpus's own calibration discipline — established when the bandwidth tax fell — requires that this be marked at every use rather than allowed to harden through repetition. The molecular results are not evidence for the social claims; they are the source of their form.

## 8. Hypotheses

Social-scale, distinct from the two source notes' molecular ones. Each inherits a shape, not a truth.

1. **Allosteric versus orthosteric support.** At matched resource cost, support that activates only on recipient-initiated action (matching funds, opt-in, request-triggered) produces better sustained engagement and less disengagement than support delivered uniformly on a fixed schedule. *Kill:* equal or better outcomes from uniform delivery at matched cost — in which case the routing distinction does not survive translation and §3's social column should be withdrawn.
2. **Burnout as downregulation.** Carers under chronic high episodic demand show reduced *responsiveness* to care-eliciting cues (not merely reduced output), persisting after rest in proportion to demand chronicity rather than demand intensity, and phenomenologically reported as flattened concern rather than tiredness. *Kill:* responsiveness recovers with rest proportional to intensity, with output tracking a depletable resource — the depletion model wins.
3. **Inversion at chronicity.** Continuously administered goods whose endogenous form is intermittent (monitoring-as-care, unsolicited reassurance, supplied optimism) show a dose-response with a subpopulation inverting at high chronicity, not merely saturating. *Kill:* monotonic saturation with no inverting subgroup at any dose.
4. **Substrate ceilings bound decay interventions.** Procedural-certainty interventions (Level 2) produce diminishing returns below a provision floor (Level 1) — a genuine interaction, not two additive main effects. *Kill:* additivity; procedural interventions deliver equally at any provision level.
5. **Capacity, not stock, is what lapses.** Relationships and institutions left unpracticed should show loss of *response latency and competence* while retaining recognition — the machinery-disuse signature — rather than graded loss of a quantity. Estrangement-and-reunion and dormant-institution revival are the natural corpora. *Kill:* decay looks quantitative and uniform, with no dissociation between retained recognition and lost competence.
6. **The four levels are separable in the field.** Coding real interventions by level should predict their failure modes: substrate failures look like ceilings, decay failures like persistence of threat, orthosteric failures like resentment and disengagement, allosteric failures like non-reach of those who never move. *Kill:* failure modes do not sort by level.
