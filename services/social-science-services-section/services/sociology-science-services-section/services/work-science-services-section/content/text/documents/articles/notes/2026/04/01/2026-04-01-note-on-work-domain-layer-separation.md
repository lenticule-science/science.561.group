35 files in `library/.../sociology/theories/work/terms/` conflate general work ontology (Annett & Duncan, Bratman, Dewey, PMI), FARS mathematical formalizations (free algebras, nuclear quartet morphisms, pointed presheaves), and operational format specs. A reader looking up "task" should get the organizational concept, not an initial algebra. This plan classifies each term (8 clean, 6 need splitting, 6 move to FARS entirely, 2 need rewriting), grounds the general versions in external disciplines, and extracts the philosophical content that deserves library placement (Gurwitsch/Husserl/James phenomenology from focus; officium/leitourgia/diakonia from serving). The nuclear quartet 2×2 lattice stays as the repo's own contribution noted in general terms; the σ/Δ formalization moves to triage. Classification table, per-term research findings, step-by-step procedure, and resolved open questions are at `analyses/work-domain-layer-separation-context.md`.
