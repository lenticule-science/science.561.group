# Kinematic space of work

The kinematic space of work records the Group's actual work occurrences and
their causal dependence. A settled run is modeled as a diamond with ingress,
recorded interior and an evidenced exit. A live or interrupted run retains
its ingress and available interior without an invented exit.

## Three relations to keep separate

1. **Containment:** a Group Thing contains or relates to its Endeavors and
   Missions; a project groups plans and tasks; a session groups subagents.
   Containment does not assert that one member causes another.
2. **Causal precedence:** one occurrence's recorded output or exit establishes
   a prerequisite at another occurrence's ingress. The causal-set model
   requires an acyclic, locally finite order on the relevant occurrences.
3. **Typing/admission:** a move is an occurrence of a skill under an
   incumbency, office and duty. Its capabilities, authority, input and
   resource conditions determine whether it is eligible.

A session label or timestamp alone does not prove causal dependence. Missing
records create an evidential hole; they do not establish that no work occurred.

## Move types and occurrences

The source move type is (incumbency, office, duty, skill). Its required
capabilities come from the skill and its authority from the applicable office,
duty and grant records. A bounded source snapshot can enumerate finitely many
such types. The type-dependency graph need not be acyclic: research, review,
revision and further review can revisit the same types.

The causal order therefore belongs to **occurrences with their actual
dependencies**. An acyclic type graph can also be used when its acyclicity is
established. Different holders alone do not prove it: A's production type can
depend on B's prior review, and B's review type on A's production, while the
actual occurrences A₁→B₁→A₂→B₂ remain acyclic.

Each occurrence retains its type reference. An order-preserving map into a
type-level poset requires that poset and its law to be constructed; the
four-field type label alone does not supply them. The
[October 9 source](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/intelligence-science-services-section/services/dreaming-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-on-dreaming-as-routine-and-the-kinematic-space-of-work.md)
already marks the corresponding Lean theorem as not derived.

## The available frontier

A candidate occurrence is available when an accepted open obligation calls
for it, its particular prerequisites have received evidence satisfying their
contracts, the agent's capability and authority conditions hold, and no
equivalent active occurrence already carries that obligation. Repeating a
move type is allowed for a new cycle or revised input. “This type has ever
run” cannot permanently remove it from the frontier.

An occurrence is in flight while it has no evidenced exit. After an exit, the
goal can still await the required receiver. Receiving is established by the
applicable contract and recorded assessment, not by a status chosen on a card.
Where source policies disagree about who receives, retain that conflict and
do not assert independence from a producer's self-check.

## Content-addressed work and agent slices

Required by the October 15 consumer: use the existing content-addressed Group
Thing and reusable SignCells for actors, capabilities and work references.
Bind source outputs to the relevant work as addressable resources. Preserve
source identifiers, supersession and co-reference history; a new content
address is a revision, not permission to discard old identities.

An agent slice joins necessary work with its capability and authority
conditions. It does not turn every possible action into an owed task.
Mission commissioner, executor, end-state and permitted lifecycle acts remain
explicit. Fabrication consumes the same general structure as non-code work.
Actual relation IDs and the SignCell encoding come from their existing owners;
this term does not mint replacements.

## Receiving cases and status

Exercise a non-code Mission, a fabrication Mission, repeated review/revision,
conditional branches, a failed attempt, a recalled commission, an interrupted
run and an output awaiting receipt. Verify that containment never becomes a
causal edge, repetition does not become a cycle among occurrences, and absent
evidence never becomes an invented exit or acceptance.
