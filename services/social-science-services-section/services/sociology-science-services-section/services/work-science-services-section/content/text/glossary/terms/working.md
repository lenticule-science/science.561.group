# Working

In the operational vocabulary, working performs an available
[work unit](work-unit.md) and records its output or structured failure. A queue
is one selection mechanism; a unit can also be invoked directly. Eligibility
still depends on prerequisites, capability, authority and resource conditions.
Being first in a queue does not authorize an act.

## Operational result and commitment result

A working pass records the attempted invocation, its actual effects and its
result. The attempt may end in success, error, refusal or interruption.
Recording the attempt as terminal does not fulfill its task, project or
Mission. The owning goal and receiving contract determine fulfillment.
An interrupted invocation without an evidenced exit remains incomplete in
the kinematic history, even if its worker session has stopped.

The operational default takes the next eligible unit without the enabling-work
ranking used by [improving](improving.md). Improving adds that selection bias,
feedback capture and owner-routed errors. This distinction does not prescribe
one queue policy for every form of agency.

## Shared-field sense and sustained striving

Agency's [working account](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/aa46e2f11aff49635bfbd7a381ee4c8b261db63c/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/working.md)
uses working for activity made shared, sustained and mediated by a common
field. The [existential spiral](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/existence.md)
places it between doing and governing. The older work/working survey also
reads working as directed, committed, sustained and failure-open striving.
These broader senses describe a process across many invocations. They are not
reduced to consuming one queue item, and a failed attempt can still belong to
genuine working.

State which sense and scale a claim uses. In every sense, preserve who acted,
what they attempted, what changed, what remains owed and who can receive it.
See [work](work.md), [task](task.md), [work item](work-item.md) and
[kinematic space of work](kinematic-space-of-work.md).
