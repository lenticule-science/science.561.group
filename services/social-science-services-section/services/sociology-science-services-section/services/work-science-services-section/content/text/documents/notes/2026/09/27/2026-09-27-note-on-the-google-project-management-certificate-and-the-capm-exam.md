# The Google Project Management Certificate and the CAPM exam as of September 2026

This note is the research phase of two books in the Group's study-companion
series, written to the pattern in the education science section's note on how
a study companion should teach toward a credential
(`education-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/27/2026-09-27-note-on-how-a-study-companion-should-teach-toward-a-credential.md`).
One book follows Google's Project Management Professional Certificate course
by course; the other follows PMI's Certified Associate in Project Management
(CAPM) exam domain by domain. They share one body of subject matter, so they
share this packet. It builds on this section's surveys of planning and
scheduling, tracking and measurement, execution and monitoring, and review and
retrospective (all `notes/2026-04-03-*`), which already hold CPM, PERT, earned
value, flow metrics, the Deming cycle and blameless review.

All facts about the credentials were read from their owners' pages on
2026-09-27 unless marked otherwise.

## What each credential publishes about itself

### Google Project Management Professional Certificate

Offered by Google on Coursera [@google-pm-2026]. Seven courses, 142 hours in
total by the course pages' own estimates, for beginners:

| # | Course | Hours | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of Project Management | 13 | The project manager's role and value; life cycle phases; methodologies and choosing one; organizational structure, culture and change management |
| 2 | Project Initiation: Starting a Successful Project | 15 | Benefits and costs; measurable goals, deliverables, scope and scope creep; success criteria; stakeholders, RACI charts; resources, documentation, the project charter; choosing tools |
| 3 | Project Planning: Putting It All Together | 23 | Tasks and milestones; work breakdown structure; time estimates, capacity planning, critical path, schedules; budgets and procurement, including procurement ethics; risk management plans; communication plans and documentation |
| 4 | Project Execution: Running the Project | 25 | Tracking, changes, dependencies and risks; quality management, customer satisfaction, continuous improvement and retrospectives; data-informed decisions and presenting data; team development, inclusion and influence; meetings and status reports; closing a project |
| 5 | Agile Project Management | 20 | Agile values and principles against Waterfall; Scrum's pillars, values and roles; Product Backlog, user stories, epics, the five Scrum events; value roadmaps, coaching and organizational adoption |
| 6 | Capstone: Applying Project Management in the Real World | 40 | A scenario project: charter and stakeholder analysis; a project plan with three-point estimates; quality standards, a survey instrument and a retrospective; a closeout report and an impact report [@google-pm-capstone-2026] |
| 7 | Accelerate Your Job Search with AI | 6 | A job-search course added to the certificate |

The certificate page states that completers "qualify for over 100 hours of
project management education" toward PMI credentials including the CAPM, and
that the certificate carries an American Council on Education recommendation of
up to 9 college credits. It names AI practice throughout. The subscription
price is not printed on the course page as read; the 2026-09-27 session that
planned this series recorded $49 a month from the Coursera listing, and the
books state the price only as "a monthly Coursera subscription" with the
reader told to check it.

### PMI's CAPM exam

Published in the CAPM Examination Content Outline, 2023 update
[@pmi-capm-eco-2023]:

- **Four domains:** Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts, 36% of
  items; Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies, 17%; Agile
  Frameworks/Methodologies, 20%; Business Analysis Frameworks, 27%.
- **Tasks:** five in domain 1, three in domain 2, five in domain 3, six in
  domain 4, each with example "enablers". Every exam includes every task of
  every domain. The approaches (predictive, adaptive, business analysis) are
  tested across all four domains, not only in their own.
- **Format:** 150 questions, 135 scored and 15 unscored pretest items, in 3
  hours; multiple choice, drag-and-drop matching, hot spots, and animation or
  comic-strip scenarios (comic strips only when taken online). A 10-minute
  break follows question 75, after which the first half is closed.
- **Eligibility:** a secondary diploma or equivalent and 23 contact hours of
  project management education completed before the exam.
- **Attempts:** up to three within a one-year eligibility period; after three,
  a one-year wait.
- **Reference list:** PMBOK Guide 7th edition; Process Groups: A Practice
  Guide (2022); The PMI Guide to Business Analysis (2017); Business Analysis
  for Practitioners, 2nd edition (2024); Agile Practice Guide (2017); The
  Project Management Answer Book, 2nd edition; and Effective Project
  Management, 8th edition. Every question is sourced in at least two of them.
  The outline says the task force "were not bound by the PMBOK Guide".

**Fees and renewal (secondary sources; PMI's page refused automated reading).**
Training providers report US$225 for PMI members and US$300 for others, and
renewal by 15 professional development units every three years
(`rosemet.com/capm-exam-cost`, `projeric.com`, both retrieved 2026-09-27). The
book gives these as reported figures and sends the reader to PMI for current
ones.

**PMBOK 8.** PMI released the PMBOK Guide, 8th edition, on 2025-11-13, and a
new PMP exam starts 2026-07-09. As of 2026-09-27 no new CAPM outline has been
published, so the 2023 outline and its 7th-edition reference list still govern
the CAPM (training-provider reports, retrieved 2026-09-27; PMI has announced no
CAPM change on any page read). The CAPM book says so on its first page and
names the outline's date, so a reader holding it after a change can tell.

## Where sources disagree, each reading kept

1. **"Development Team" or "Developers."** Google's Agile course describes
   the traits of an effective "Development Team". The 2020 Scrum Guide
   replaced that term with "Developers" and removed the idea of a team within
   the team; it names three accountabilities (Product Owner, Scrum Master,
   Developers) of one Scrum Team [@scrum-guide-2020]. *Reading:* both
   supported, of different editions. The books teach the 2020 terms and name
   the older one so a learner meeting it in the course is not confused.
2. **How many Scrum events.** Google's course says five. The 2020 guide
   calls the Sprint "a container for all other events" and lists four inside
   it (Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective).
   *Reading:* both, by counting the Sprint or not; the books count five and
   say why.
3. **Whether Royce proposed Waterfall.** Royce's 1970 paper draws the
   sequential model and then says it "is risky and invites failure",
   recommending iteration and early prototypes [@royce1970]. Many courses
   present him as its author. *Reading:* the paper supports "Royce described
   the sequential model", refutes "Royce recommended it". The books say both.
4. **"Over 100 hours" against 142.** Google's page states both the certificate
   total (142 hours) and "over 100 hours" of qualifying education. *Reading:*
   both: the job-search course and other material need not count toward PMI's
   hours. The CAPM needs 23, so either suffices.
5. **The CAPM's governing standard.** The outline aligns with the PMBOK 7th
   edition yet says its task force was not bound by it; PMBOK 8 exists.
   *Reading:* the outline governs the exam; the reference list governs the
   answers. The CAPM book teaches the process groups (from the 2022 practice
   guide) and the principles-and-domains view (from PMBOK 7) side by side, as
   the outline's reference list does.
6. **Fees.** Supported by two independent secondary sources, not read from
   PMI. Marked as reported.

## How the section's theory reads the same material

The theory of work (agency section,
`learning-articles/questions/what-is-the-theory-of-work.md`) gives work-acts a
cost in a commutative quantale, composed sequentially by the quantale's tensor,
with parallel acts bounded by the "parallel cost law": doing two acts in
parallel costs at most their summed cost.

- **Duration and effort are two cost models of one plan.** Take durations in
  the quantale of non-negative reals under addition. For elapsed time, the
  parallel cost law holds at its tightest: two parallel activities take the
  longer of their durations, not the sum. The critical path method computes
  exactly this model, the longest path through the activity network, which is
  why a project's duration is set by one chain, not by its total work. For
  effort (person-hours), parallel activities cost the sum. A schedule and a
  budget are the same network costed two ways. The companions teach both on
  one worked example.
- **Earned value compares the intended with the realised.** The theory's
  intention fibration separates the work that was intended from the work
  that was performed, and the 2-cell between them is the attempt. Planned
  value costs the intended work to date, earned value costs the performed work
  at the intended rates, and actual cost costs the performed work at the
  realised rates. SV = EV − PV isolates the schedule gap; CV = EV − AC
  isolates the cost gap.
- **Risks and issues.** The planning survey's Bratman reading (plans as stable
  commitments revised only on justification) is the reason a change control
  process exists. The execution survey's error model (Reason's lapses, slips,
  mistakes and violations, the theory of work's failure fibre) is a finer
  vocabulary for the "issue" the CAPM outline asks candidates to tell apart
  from a risk, an assumption and a constraint.

## What the two books are

Both follow the education science note's pattern, lint under the
`study-guide` profile, and carry `"series": { "name": "561 Group Study
Companions" }`.

1. *Project Management, Course by Course: An Independent Study Companion to
   the Google Project Management Certificate.* A chapter per course (1 to 5),
   a capstone guide (course 6) in place of a practice exam, and a study plan.
   The job-search course is out of scope.
2. *The CAPM, Domain by Domain: An Independent Study Companion to PMI's
   Certified Associate in Project Management Exam.* Front matter with the
   outline's table; a chapter per domain, with domains 1 and 4 split in two for
   length; a formula sheet; a 40-question practice exam in the outline's
   proportions (14, 7, 8 and 11 questions), each answer naming its domain and
   task; and a study plan.

Neither reproduces course material, exam items or the owners' text beyond the
public course and domain titles. Both state they are independent of, and not
endorsed by, Google, Coursera or PMI. "PMI", "CAPM" and "PMBOK" are PMI's
registered marks and are marked and attributed on the notice page.
