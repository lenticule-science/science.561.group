## The idea

We have accumulated ideas (in operations/command/ideas/, operations/engineering/ideas/, triage content, captain's verbal directives) that describe what the system is growing into. Many reference real plans: ActivityPub servers, Reiki services, Guix/Guile composition layers. But they're not connected to the planning system — they sit as idea files without tasks, plans, or goals linking them to actionable work.

We know enough about our system's trajectory now to start processing these into proper records: ideas that are ready become plans with tasks, ideas that are speculative get tagged for research, ideas that overlap get consolidated.

## Captain's words (2026-04-04)

> Somewhere there's ideas about like, activitypub servers, reiki services, all this shit. I think we have enough sense of what our system is growing into in order to start processing those things into our records more properly.

## Process

1. Survey all idea files across operations/
2. For each: is it ready for a plan? Needs research? Overlaps with existing plan?
3. Ready ideas → create plans with tasks
4. Research-needed ideas → create research tasks
5. Overlapping ideas → merge and cross-reference
