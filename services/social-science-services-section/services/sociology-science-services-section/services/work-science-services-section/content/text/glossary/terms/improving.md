**Improving** is [working](working.md) that targets skillful work and adds three
things plain working lacks: a selection bias, a feedback capture, and error
routing.

## Selection bias

Among open work items, improving takes first the one that enables the most
further work, ranking:

1. **Unblocking**: items whose completion makes blocked items available;
2. **Repair**: items that fix a broken or malformed skill;
3. **Gap-filling**: items that write a method where none exists;
4. **Ordinary work**: items that enable nothing downstream.

Plain working takes the first item regardless of rank; improving re-sorts before
it takes one.

## Feedback capture

A derivation or an inquiry ends by examining its course of work: the skills it
invoked, the records it read, and anything that produced an unexpected output or
an error. Each item that showed a problem becomes a recorded finding and, where
it needs work, a new work item; an item that worked is noted and generates
nothing. A judgment reached without examining what produced it has not closed
its loop.

## Error routing

An error a called skill emits (see [skill](skill.md), Result) is recovered in
place when it is a missing input or a failed validation with a plain remedy.
Otherwise it becomes a finding for the office that owns the work, and the failed
unit is recorded as failed with its error attached, so a later pass can take it.
An error that reaches improving and is not routed is a fault in improving.

## Open questions

- Whether the ranking yields an optimal order, and the course of work as a typed
  object, both wait on the algebra of skill work.
