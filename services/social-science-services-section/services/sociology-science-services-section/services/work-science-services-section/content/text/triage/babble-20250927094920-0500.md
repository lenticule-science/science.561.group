I was thinking about [[id:db411a11-e8ac-429b-b0ef-9befe1203c50][professionalization]] and want to explore the concept that the [[id:291cc793-52f6-481f-b412-5a4a39dd00d9][genre of the contemporary American worker]] with any "management" rule actually [[id:583cebd4-d972-4663-80bc-b9748afa7cbb][rewards]] [[id:009d3047-5db5-4bde-8a9e-a9e8a2282360][administrating]], not [[id:b91cdf78-787a-433d-8573-de4dfcbc6a46][managing]].

Distinguishing the two as i mean them:

- administrating :: rooted in [[id:1f2e5199-d4b5-4cde-ba58-92ed5ff239b3][bureaucratic theory]] and public adminstration, involves [[id:99e714e9-d17f-4bf6-8eef-2929f8cacd6d][standardizing]] and [[id:e2d02b47-d6a7-4311-b05f-67c7335b0baa][proceduralizing]], [[id:d446b935-869d-4651-bd79-8c3f2b84f940][measuring]] and [[id:07260b59-7c46-4105-bee2-f392a755f8dd][quantifying]], focused on [[id:d25ebe72-3c35-42eb-bb6a-1b8ae8a4bb34][compliance]], [[id:714f9068-5e65-4413-b35a-860728e8b0e6][documentation]]
- managing :: rooted in [[id:7ae321f2-73ff-44a7-8d3b-b470fda0fee8][industrialism]] and [[id:8f7e9ad3-def6-4279-8c72-356c9b0b7f9a][scientific management]], involves directing [[id:9fee0f36-1b36-43c7-b896-14b6700c7738][persons]] and [[id:4a53867b-afa3-437b-b979-385597e0e616][processes]] toward [[id:e4b0236c-a1e8-4f5c-8921-164b1651e302][producing]], focused on interpersonal [[id:be095971-c665-40c0-87d1-d12681f1e55e][authority]] and [[id:dae12ab6-5db6-4cfc-85bf-90aee09df85c][responsibility]]

[[id:73405a6c-826a-4823-9023-b382a48cac0c][Sociology of labor]] has looked at how [[id:e90140e0-663f-4c54-8496-604910d0361b][professions]] move through [[id:38f15a9b-efab-4a3a-9c29-231639622b8e][cycles of rationalization]], and this occured with the [[id:869698c2-914b-470f-a3d4-9f78ef8734ba][managerial role in the U.S. labor economy]], [[id:69337aca-3c87-4585-992e-8ba81a067b56][schooles of business]] and MBA programs teach [[id:d0340d44-4584-4e50-8f04-dc1ace59890f][business frameworks]], [[id:f461ea1d-4050-47c3-84c3-f68d30577f31][business analysis]], for administrating metrics across contexts: generating, interpreting, and translating records in some way I'm having difficulty describing, but which serves to allow the managerial layer of a business to serve as a administrative relay of commands coming from above or without.[fn:: Are managers [[id:410a74cb-3475-4f00-95b5-c5fe7fc92749][the slime of many heads]]?]

Managers aren't rewarded for managing the systems they're a part of, but administrating the workflow assigned by higher systems, using the tools provided, into those systems.

Weirdly this process, while part of [[id:998be859-76dd-4bfb-b71d-a8eb34703052][professionalization of management]], has also been part of [[id:dd04f88f-a63c-48f8-9021-dc0f1a9bf979][deskilling]] the role.
