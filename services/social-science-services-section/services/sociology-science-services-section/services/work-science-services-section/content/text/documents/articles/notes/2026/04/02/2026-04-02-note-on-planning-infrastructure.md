Before this plan, the system tracked dependencies but not time — no dates on tasks, no milestones, no "what should I work on today?" The plan had three parallel research tracks (scheduling theory: CPM, PERT, Goldratt, PMBOK; personal productivity: GTD, Newport, Eisenhower, Forster, Bullet Journal; review cycles: AAR, Agile retros, Deming PDCA, Haudenosaunee seven-generations) feeding into library terms (schedule, milestone, deadline, review, cadence, capacity, estimate), a temporal frontmatter schema, a CPM-aware compute-plan-status, and commands for weekly planning, daily standup, and periodic reviews. Completed 2026-04-03. Full source lists, step briefs, work-type classification, and parallel groups at `analyses/planning-infrastructure-context.md`.
