## Annotation

Orlikowski's account of *capability as enacted in practice* — not as a static asset of an organization but as the recurrent accomplishment of distributed activity — is the disciplinary precursor for predicated-work's treatment of the [capability-fiber-bundle](../objects/capability-fiber-bundle.md) as a *bilattice-valued section* updated by witness increments. A capability is not "had"; it is settled at $\mathbf{t}$ at the cells where it has been witnessed, and that settlement is revisable.

The relevance to [capability-matching](teaching/explanations/capability-matching.md) and [capability-availability-from-existential](../theorems/capability-availability-from-existential.md): Orlikowski's enacted-capability account justifies treating the existential-adjoint projection as the canonical "what's available in the community" reading, with the four-state values capturing the genuine uncertainty about what the community can currently do.
