# Work regime

A work-regime claim classifies a specified settling construction. The label of
a social practice, its duration, or its number of participants does not by
itself establish a closure ordinal.

## State the construction before classifying it

A claim must identify:

1. The ordered carrier, relevant cells and evidence scope.
2. The one-step operator F and the properties assumed of it.
3. The initial valuation and any external increment sequence.
4. The rule at successor and limit stages.
5. The fixed-point or quiescence condition and the quantifiers in the bound.

Fixed-input rule saturation and an open stream of new events are different
processes. An idempotent closure operator cl satisfies cl(cl(v))=cl(v), so
iterating that operator on a fixed input stabilizes after one application.
Transfinite construction can instead concern iterations of a preclosure or
one-step inference operator used to obtain cl. The statement must say which.

## Supported bounds and counterexamples

**Finite carrier argument (prose, not a new kernel receipt).** For N fixed
FOUR cells, each strict knowledge increase adds at least one of 2N Boolean
evidence coordinates. Iterating a fixed inflationary operator therefore has
at most 2N strict increases. With effective rule evaluation this gives a finite
saturation procedure. It does not prove the task's target is reached, a
polynomial wall-clock cost, or that no later external event can arrive.

**Scott-continuous construction.** Under the hypotheses of the applicable
fixed-point theorem, a Scott-continuous operator iterated from bottom reaches
its least fixed point by the ω supremum. Equality with ω needs a separate
argument that no finite stage is fixed. The identity operator on a countably
infinite carrier stabilizes immediately. Infinite vocabulary and recurrence
therefore do not imply an exact ω closure ordinal.

**Limit dependence.** An infinitary antecedent can defeat Scott continuity,
but its written syntax or the number of generations in a narrative does not
establish a particular closure ordinal. Name the directed family for which
preservation fails and derive the orbit's bound. Seven generations is finite;
it cannot alone demonstrate uncountable cofinality.

**Open increments.** A run v[n+1]=cl(v[n] join u[n]) can receive an event after
any currently quiet stage. Quiescence for a closed input window is different
from permanent stability under all possible future inputs. For example, no
negative evidence today does not prevent t becoming ⊤ tomorrow.

## The four-regime research vocabulary

The historical program groups closure ordinals into finite (κ<ω), exactly ω,
countable beyond ω, and at least ω₁. These are ordinal ranges for a stated
construction. The associated names strict, Scott-continuous,
transfinite-bounded and aperiodic are the program's vocabulary; they must not
be used as automatic equivalences with operator continuity, elapsed time,
aim-generativity, cultural form or combinatorial aperiodicity.

An operator-level worst-case bound also requires a specified class of admissible
inputs and a fixed carrier. A supremum need not be attained: a family of
finite bounds can have supremum ω. Thus “all runs are finite,” “there is one
uniform finite bound” and “some run requires ω” are different claims.

## Work-side use

- [Task bounds](../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-task.md)
  describe an isolated task's actual relevant cells and rules.
- [Recurrent practice](../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/regime-of-recurrent-practice.md)
  separates finite cycles from their history and aggregation.
- [Completion](../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/completion-as-quiescence.md)
  requires target satisfaction and a receiving scope in addition to saturation.
- The being→doing→working→governing spiral is an Agency research construction.
  Its cross-regime maps require their own formal definitions and proofs.

Process/procedure/derivation/inquiry classify meaning and execution openness.
They do not determine a settling regime. Composition requires a theorem for
the chosen operators and interaction; the regime of a composite is not
automatically the maximum of its separately studied components.

## Unresolved formal work

Recover the current owning closure-regime declarations and their hypotheses;
construct the work realization and verify the required map. In particular,
there is no established work-progress metric merely from the phrase
“movement in H¹”: a class space, comparison, direction and receiving criterion
must first be defined. A theorem about a specific cohomology construction
cannot certify an enterprise's progress without that bridge.

The earlier [truth–knowledge long exact sequence proposal](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/aa46e2f11aff49635bfbd7a381ee4c8b261db63c/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/work-regime.md#truth-knowledge-les-and-regime-dependent-cohomological-structure)
remains a specific research direction: construct the inconsistency sheaf,
truth/knowledge coefficient objects and exact sequence; prove when a vanishing
inconsistency object gives the claimed cohomology comparison; then establish
what, if anything, the connecting map measures about inter-cycle disagreement.
Finite saturation alone does not supply finite-dimensional cohomology, and
nonzero H¹ has no automatic equivalence with unresolved operational conflict.
The proposed H¹ trajectory for an aim-generative function likewise needs its
class space and a justified direction/ordering before it measures progress.
