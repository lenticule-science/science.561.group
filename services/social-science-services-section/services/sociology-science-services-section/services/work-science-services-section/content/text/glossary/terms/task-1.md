A task is one or more [act|acts](../../triage/act|acts) that have been associated with a [goal](../../triage/goal).  Tasks have an implicit or explicit [procedure](../../triage/procedure), that instruct one how to do the task. Tasks whose procedure (implicitly or explicitly) encodes the [meaning](../../triage/meaning) of the task may be a [ritual](../../triage/ritual).
