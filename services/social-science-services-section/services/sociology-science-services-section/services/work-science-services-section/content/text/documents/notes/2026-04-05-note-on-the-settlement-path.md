# The settlement path

Improving work can settle its intended outcome, its method, or both. The
[work taxonomy](../../glossary/terms/work.md) uses two independent axes:
whether the meaning of the output is settled, and whether its execution
path is settled. A settled output contract does not mean its actual result
value is already known. The active model is a square of possibilities, not a compulsory
four-stage linear lifecycle.

| Work kind | Meaning | Execution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry | Open | Open |
| Procedure | Settled | Open |
| Derivation | Open | Settled |
| Process | Settled | Settled |

Settling the meaning of an inquiry can yield a procedure; settling its
execution can yield a derivation. Settling the remaining axis can yield a
process. In the authored house terminology, a derivation can use a settled
execution whose meaning remains open, while a procedure has a settled meaning
but still requires an execution choice. Neither is in general a later or more
automated stage than the other. A new case may reopen an axis, requiring a new scoped judgment rather
than deletion of the historical finding.

## What an improvement must carry

A proposed settling step names its input work, the axis it settles, the
resulting contract and the evidence for that change. A repeatable method is
received by a later use with appropriate inputs, outputs and failure cases.
A settled outcome specifies an acceptance predicate; it does not assert the
outcome has occurred. A process still has an admitted domain and can fail
outside it. Deterministic syntax, a script or an F3 representation alone does
not prove totality, correctness, permission or useful effect.

Keep the different dimensions separately readable:

- Work kind describes openness of meaning and execution.
- Formality describes how a specification is expressed and checked.
- Executor choice describes demonstrated ability, availability and resources
  under the applicable authority.
- Automation describes what an installed mechanism actually performs.
- Closure regime describes a specified mathematical iteration.

The April source's table equating F0–F3, four work kinds, named model tiers
and observation/instinct/skill/hook stages was an architectural conjecture,
not an established isomorphism. Those old provider and office assignments
remain historical evidence in Git. They are not current model routing rules.
Counterexample: an algebraically specified open research question can be
expressed at high formality while its interpretation or execution remains open. Conversely,
a plainly described, well-established computation may have settled meaning
and execution without that prose itself being machine-checked.

## Closure is an additional mathematical claim

A proposed closure operator must supply a carrier, order and map and prove
extensivity, monotonicity and idempotence. Producing a better specification
is not automatically such an operator. Even two closure operators need not
compose to an idempotent map without extra conditions. On subsets of {a,b,c},
let C add b when a is present and D add c when b is present. Each is a closure;
D followed by C maps {a} to {a,b}, and applying that composite again adds c.
Thus the composite is not idempotent. The earlier note's asserted universal
chain of three closures needs compatibility laws, not only stage names.

For saturation and open increment histories, use the
[work-regime contract](../../glossary/concepts/work-regime.md). For the
being→doing→working→governing spiral, use the Agency sources linked in the
[Work Science overview](../../../../README.md); this openness square is a
different construction and has no recovered equality with that spiral.

## Corpus improvement as a receiving loop

The [Science research skill](../../../../../../../../../../lib/agency/skills/research/SKILL.md)
locates the owner, compares exact statements, revises the existing carrier,
keeps source and authorship, and supplies a typed consumer receiving case.
The next use should exercise the corrected distinction: a meaning-open derivation
with settled execution, a meaning-settled procedure with open execution, or a
recurrent workflow that revisits its type without confusing its occurrences.
An improvement is useful when the next agent can make the relevant decision
from the owning resource and its evidence, rather than needing the same
clarification again. A correction that exposes a false settled claim is also
valuable; progress is not measured only by increasing apparent determinism.
