# 561 Group — Concept of Operations

## 1. Identity

The 561 Group is "a private organization which researches, designs, and
deploys technical infrastructure," advancing "long-horizon autonomy,
collective intelligence, and strategic prosperity through political,
financial, and economic volatility" (561.group). It is led by
M. Sennhauser (emsenn, they/them), independent researcher of Lakota
Itazipcho descent, whose published research program treats relationality
as the fundamental force of existence, grounded in Lakota epistemologies,
as a framework for life, governance, and history under ecological and
social breakdown (emsenn.net). Internally, the firm's repository
describes itself as the working repository of a one-person transnational
conglomerate, its environment "a type-theoretic distributed operating
system providing agentic MAPE-K industrial enterprise resource control
systems organized under Rendanheyi principles," whose nodes provide
paraconsistent data-monitoring, content-addressed evidence-substrate
analysis, coordination-free deductive-truth planning, and database-proof
execution — as a whole, "an autonomic language-systems multiplexer
platform" (README.md, Leader-authored).

The public and internal identities are one program. The Leader's
published rejection of binary true/false frameworks is realized in the
firm's mathematics as the four-valued evidence logic FOUR, proved forced
by the structure of predication itself (voidmath forcing proof,
2026-05-27; Obsmetrologia Lean corpus). Predication theory is "the
mathematical formalization of Lakotaean cosmogeny/ontology/epistemology"
— work in the substrate is work in the firm's own framework, not
application of an external tool (rom-keldar teaching note). The
dependency order of the whole endeavor: Lakota star
knowledge → its mathematical formalization → a new form of language
system → a Rendanheyi corporation operating on it → digital services on
the Web → a new nation (2026-08-11).

**The function all operations serve.** The Group was founded 2018-04-01
under Lakota law by emsenn, who holds the authority to make the tiyóšpaye
claim the Group rests on, and who is its heyoka and its head (personnel
record `emsenn.md`; glossary term `561-group.md`). Its binding function,
declared in the RSC *Quiescent* commissioning record of 2026-04-05, is
**the pursuit of Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty** — *"all
work serves this or doesn't belong,"* the function borrowed from the
Group's own endeavor and the ship being the means. Every operation
described below is a means to that function; an operation that cannot be
traced to it is out of scope by that fact.

**The two governance registers.** The Group governs itself internally as a
**tiyóšpaye** — Lakota kin-relational governance, recognition converging
on the Leader as head of the kinship group, closure by wolakhota and
consensus-by-withdrawal — and conducts operations outside its own borders
through **commissioned ships**, each a body politic distinct from any
captain or crew (*"the dignity persists across captains"*), governed in a
command register under a captain answerable to the Group (structure
records `emsenn.md`, `rsc-quiescent.md`). The registers are not
interchangeable, and every operation below sits in one or the other. The
charter governs this document; where they disagree, the charter wins.

## 2. What the firm makes and sells

The trade doctrine (2026-07-18, "sell certified tightness"): "in an
economy where every agent can generate, the only scarce good is the
witness... The Union is not a producer of content, compute, or code. It
is a producer of tightness... a signed verdict about someone else's
claim, priced per erasure, sold across a membrane the seller provably
cannot reach through." Every product is a form of verification sold at a
boundary. The doctrine binds itself to falsifiable commercial tests:
each proposed product "either finds one outside wallet willing to pay one
real invoice this quarter, or it is confessed unrealized and goes back on
the shelf."

The first realized instance: the ephemeris gateway (ephemeris.561.group)
— astronomical moment-readings sold machine-to-machine over x402 on Base
mainnet ("Moment Essentials" $0.04 USDC; "Precision Moment" $0.32 USDC),
with a scheduled job independently requalifying the public
`402 Payment Required` offer — payee, asset, price — every six hours
without itself transacting (trade doctrine and 2026-07-24 verification
pass; treat current live status as requiring a dated record).

The internal economy prefigures the external one: no internal currency
and no global price authority — a SKU "never carries a price: price forms
only at a counterparty border" (sku.schema.json), with typed ask/bid
border-price quotes so two units can price a single lot bilaterally
(border-price-quote.schema.json).

## 3. Enterprise form

**Rendanheyi micro-enterprises.** The repository is organized as nested
service sections, each an internal enterprise with its own boundary
("hull," shaped by Huidu Guanli practice), containing its services,
documents, scripts, data, and records (README.md; tree convention).
Authority is held by offices, not persons; delegating a duty never
removes the delegator's accountability; the governance actually in force
is a derived reading of recorded relationships, not an org chart
(work-method glossary).

**Dies.** Every articulation of capability is meant to be a die: "an
atomic pure unit placed in the tessellation: exact typed input to exact
output or typed refusal, its shape doing the work" — independently
addressable, priced at its own seam, hireable on its own terms
(atomization note, 2026-07-24; die.json). Standing
correction of scale: the firm currently has far too FEW packages; every
transformation is meant to be a package in the firm's registry, because
registry seams are what make flows visible and watchable enough to price
(2026-08-11).

**The capability graph.** The package registry is the firm's Node
Operations Graph: capabilities are discovered by querying the graph, not
searching the filesystem; internal consumption resolves through the
registry at exact released versions; workspace-style source linking is
prohibited as a dark channel (2026-08-11; NOG note,
2026-08-10). The local capability graph precedes further build-out of
the networked priced layer (2026-08-11, correcting a
sequencing error made once already).

## 4. How the firm operates

**The Leader movement cycle.** Direction enters as briefings — dictated
morning and midday, naming intended changes in corporate capability —
and leaves as closeout briefings that derive what actually changed
(leader-movement-team briefings and closeout, 2026-08-08/09). The
briefing template binds each movement to "what the corporation will be
able to do after this briefing that it cannot reliably do now," stated
as actor-verb-object-result-evidence (capability-oriented briefing
template).

**The office loop.** The Codex actuation office turns "one admitted
Leader move into supported-by-evidence MAPE-over-K purchases and one
authority-bounded Codex actuation," under invariants including
only-codex-may-mutate-the-customer-filesystem,
leader-expression-is-sufficient-execution-authority, and
supplier-completion-is-not-customer-acceptance (codex-mape-office.json).
Its seven duties run monitor (leader move, repository evidence, provider
market) → analyze (pinned corpus, contradictions and obstructions) →
design (software architecture specification, then high-level design) →
decompose (low-level design, then bounded work lots) → actuate (an
actuation plan "still inert without LeaderAuthorityK," then repository
mutation) → observe receiver outcome → account for capability trade
(codex-mape-duties.json). Planning artifacts are non-authoritative until
a receiver assays them.

**The inference procurement loop.** Work orders are minted from Leader
speech and a capability registry; a five-minute timer dispatches the
oldest open order; the dispatcher discovers a signed A2A agent card,
verifies the provider's chain-qualified account, builds a typed operation
term, and sends one signed task requiring a completion disposition —
retrying against the next provider on refusal. Provider selection policy:
least-recently-hired-under-quota, across six admitted inference
providers, each separately rate-limited, priced, and substitutable
(atomization note §2.5, documented 2026-07-24; current deployment state
requires a dated record). Model routing carries a trust ordering — a
lower-trust model never overwrites a higher-trust result — and
capability-per-territory is a scheduling input: model families differ in
which subject matter they can work (distribution research, 2026-07-16;
2026-08-11).

**The record discipline.** All work leaves append-only, content-addressed
witnessed statements; status is derived by re-running goals, never
asserted; a milestone is reached when its conditions have settled;
roadmaps are dependency graphs read out of records; the test of every
record is continuation — a fresh session picks up from the records alone
(work-method glossary). Workers coordinate through the traces they leave:
overlaps are detected from records, and genuine conflicts are kept with
both sides preserved rather than overwritten by whoever wrote last.

**The verification stance toward agents.** Enactors file certificates
that validate or are refused; no enactor performs the mathematics; a
counterparty need not adopt the firm's worldview; agent and notebook
output is non-authoritative until a receiver assays it (distal-bypass;
codex office invariants). Refusal is a first-class typed outcome, and a
refused method stays refused until its record is superseded (PPA refusal
algebra; 2026-08-11).

**Growth and self-improvement.** The firm's central device is the distal
bypass: present any territory as local records with overlaps; derive the
obstruction sheet by re-running checks (never from reports); classify
each disagreement as damage or border; discharge damage at least cost
and certify borders; price every erasure; emit certificates
(distal-bypass note). Its economics: k, the ratio of value released by
discharging obstruction to the cost of finding and discharging it, is a
property of a territory, not of the engine; the criticality thesis is
falsifiable — "falsified if every bounded discharge experiment settles
at or below break-even" — and the firm's key growth service, inducing a
settlement at a path from what is already at that path, is chartered but
not yet built (price-of-saying §8; criticality-as-exchange-rate;
distal-bypass "Where it stands"). The compute doctrine that funds
scaling: heavy reasoning is spent once into a checkable derivation;
recall is near-free; the searcher is replaceable without loss of
accumulated capability (price-of-saying §10).

## 5. The substrate being built

The firm's services compose toward a content-addressed semantic
organism: "typed claims create charge, charge induces local computation,
computation settles into addressed skeletal structure, and that
structure constrains the next circulation of claims" (glass-sponge
architecture note, 2026-08-01). Concretely, in dependency order:

1. **Semiotic reduction** — all digital data is address:content, which is
   (token, claim): an utterance. Utterances articulate into a proved
   four-cell structure; predication is irreducibly triadic; charges are
   posited monotonically; settlement is a proved least fixed point
   (2026-08-11; Obsmetrologia: Articulating, Predicating,
   Positing, Closure). Reduction of arbitrary data to this form is one
   shared layer beneath everything, and its repeated mis-setup is a named
   standing problem (2026-08-11).
2. **Metrology over reduced data** — obstruction sheets, coordination
   cost as proved cycle-rank of a cover's nerve, settlement measurement
   (CoordinationCohomology.lean; obsmetrologic services).
3. **The registry as circuit board** — packages as typed morphisms with
   contracts (Hoare-shaped: precondition, effectful move, postcondition,
   honestly graded), composed along registry seams; content identity
   minted only after settlement and canonicalization, as RFC 6920 ni over
   canonical CBOR of a typed envelope (NOG note; glass-sponge invariants;
   hoare-program-carrier, whose preservation theorem is machine-checked).
4. **Settlement induction and the priced network** — territories that
   derive their own obstruction sheets, and the agent-to-agent commercial
   layer (A2A, x402) extending the same seam-pricing beyond the firm's
   hosts — explicitly sequenced after the local graph works
   (2026-08-11).

## 6. People

**The Leader** is the Group's founder, its head as tiyóšpaye, its heyoka,
and the first captain of its ship. The office holds the human authority to
state intent, supply or withhold externally acquired resources, choose
among materially different courses, and settle what may be represented as
the Leader's decision; no model, agent projection, provider, or
subordinate office may occupy it or exercise its settlement powers by
approximation (`leader.json`, recorded by emsenn). Two declarations belong
to it alone and are never derived: the admission rules for witnesses, and
the protected set — what must never be repaired, reconciled, or identified
at any price. *"The constitution shrinks to the two declarations that
cannot be derived"* (price-of-saying §7). The Leader ratifies documents by
moving them along the promotion ladder (drafting section → Leader's
section → Board); tree changes are not rulings (2026-08-11).

**Offices** carry duties, never persons. An office record names no
incumbent — a role persists when vacant and is not constituted by its
occupant — and seating is a separate record settling recognition (the
honor axis) and induction (the forward-commitment axis). Six offices are
recorded: founder, chairperson, chief-of-staff,
director-of-the-executive-office, executive-assistant, founding-assistant.
Four are vacant; the Leader holds founder and chairperson together with
offices that would ordinarily check them, which the corpus records as a
gap rather than as settled design. Delegated authority is bounded by
derivativity, accountability non-transfer, boundedness, and revocability;
accountability never transfers with the work (charter §§2.2–2.3, 3.2).

**Human members** work under the same record discipline as every actor:
find work in the records, derive completion by re-running goals, leave
the territory continuable. The work method defines dispatch — a task
carries no assignee, which is what lets it be handed to anyone — and the
firm's scale premise is multi-person and multi-machine now (2026-08-11).
[TBD: the ratified procedure by which a member presently finds and takes
an assignment; the work method defines the mechanics, but no current
document names the canonical location of open work.]

**Agent sessions** are hired labor inside instruments: bounded tasks,
typed refusals, records that transport derivations. One primary agent
per workspace; coordination through records (2026-08-11).
Meaning is held by record types and instruments — never by any model's
weights — which is what makes the workforce replaceable without loss of
capability (price-of-saying §10; distal-bypass).

## 7. Modes of operation

1. **Leader-interactive** (dominant today): the Leader directs sessions
   conversationally; briefings and closeouts record the moves
   (2026-08-08/09 artifacts).
2. **Office-mediated** (designed, partially exercised): recorded
   briefings raise work through office duty loops and the procurement
   dispatcher; the Leader rules on gates rather than performing the work
   (codex office registries; atomization §2.5).
3. **Self-operating** (aim, explicitly open): "a detected problem is
   raised as a task against the standing duty it falls under, is
   dispatched to a worker automatically, and returns a result read out
   against the task's goal... with minimal standing attention from the
   operator" — the work method's own final aim, marked not yet closed
   (work-method glossary).

## 8. Open declarations

Constitutional declarations are owed to the charter, not to this
document; its §6 carries the standing list, which the Group's own
commissioning record of 2026-04-05 acknowledged as outstanding and which
remains so — the covenant, membership and standing, decision procedures,
dispute resolution, dissolution and succession, crew articles, legal form
and jurisdiction, the Union relation, and the canonical office
vocabulary.

**Settled 2026-08-11:** the automation shutdowns of 2026-07-24
through 2026-08-04 were intentional in substance — the scheduled
dispatcher was *"causing agent drift faster than I could handle it."* The
binding constraint is the Leader's review capacity, not automation as
such. What follows: **work routing through processes is wanted;
unattended scheduled dispatch is not.** A timer produces work at a rate
independent of anyone's capacity to assay it, and supplier completion is
not customer acceptance — so a dispatcher that fires on a clock
manufactures unassayed output by construction. Dispatch that is
*requested* does not.

This bears directly on §7's modes: office-mediated operation is not
blocked on restoring a timer. It is blocked on being able to route a
piece of work through a process on demand, which the firm could do in any
session and currently does not.

Operational state — live hosts, deployed surfaces, standing offers — is
deliberately not carried here. It lives in the asset and configuration
register and the service catalog, which are dated observation records.
