# Predicated Governance

A subtheory of [predicated-work](../../../../../_index.md). Studies the *normative* substructure of work: jural relations, offices, normative systems, charters, the Hohfeldian eightfold, and the operations that modify normative state (installation, delegation, assignment, novation, waiver, revocation, etc.).

The library treats governance as a sub-domain of work — `library/.../sociology/work/theories/governance/`. This subtheory mirrors that placement: governance is the *normative-structure* slice of work-as-relational-substrate, not a separate domain.

## What is governance

Per the library's [governance index](../../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/governance/_index.md): the structures, processes, and principles through which collectives make decisions, exercise authority, and maintain accountability. Mathematically: the operations on the operations-settling that modify which closure rules fire, who has standing to invoke them, and how authority propagates through the substrate.

## Why a subtheory rather than a sibling theory

Three reasons:

1. **Library structure.** Governance is treated as a sub-domain of work in the library, not a peer; this subtheory mirrors that.
2. **Substrate inheritance.** The predicated-governance program inherits predicated-work's [operations-base](../../../../../objects/operations-base.md), [operations-settling](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/operations-settling.md), [witness-as-increment](../../../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/witness-as-increment.md), and the regime-classification machinery. It does not need to re-derive these.
3. **Co-content.** The Hohfeldian apparatus (jural relations, offices, delegation, etc.) presupposes a working substrate of agents-doing-things; without that substrate, the normative structure has nothing to modify. The subtheory shape captures this dependency.

## Convention

Following the [spatiotemporal-knowledge convention](../../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md). Same frontmatter, same status markers, same relation set. The subtheory's notes depend on predicated-work's main objects/morphisms in addition to the math program's.

## What this subtheory commits to

- **Governance is plural; patterns are first-class.** A polity's normative substructure is a [governance-pattern](../../../../triage/concepts/governance-pattern.md) coproduct — Hohfeldian, consensus, rotational, restorative, kinship-based, conduct-teachings-based, military-command, jurisdictional-federation, Black-radical-refusal, and others — each a closure-rule fragment plus witness language plus recognition discipline. No pattern is the substrate's default. Compatibility between coexisting patterns is a cohomological condition: see [tradition-pluralism-as-fragment-coproduct](../../../../../../../../../social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/tradition-pluralism-as-fragment-coproduct.md).
- **The Hohfeldian pattern is one pattern among many.** The full Hohfeldian eightfold (both quartets) is the structural vocabulary for *Hohfeldian-pattern* relations between agents on the substrate. The eightfold is the orbit of a single bilattice modal generator under the Klein-four × $\mathbb{Z}/2$ action — see [correlative-pair-as-modal-dual](correlative-pair-as-modal-dual.md). It is excellent at what it does and not the only pattern.
- **Each pattern has a structural signature.** Patterns differ measurably in cohomology — see [cohomological-signature-of-traditions](../../../../../../../../../social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/cohomological-signature-of-traditions.md). The math program's regime classification and bigraded cohomology distinguish patterns; the [non-hohfeldian-governance-traditions narrative](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/non-hohfeldian-governance-traditions.md) supplies the lived-tradition content each pattern carries.
- **Offices are double-settlement substrate entities.** Two required predicates (recognition, induction), forced by the bilattice's bigraded common-knowledge structure, not stipulated — see [recognition-as-bigraded-common-knowledge](../../../../triage/theorems/recognition-as-bigraded-common-knowledge.md).
- **Authority operations share the authority-gate pattern.** Charters are closure-rule subsystems; installation, delegation, jurisdiction, and the other authority-modifying operations all instantiate the [authority-gate](../../../../triage/concepts/authority-gate.md) residuation pattern. Delegation is made precise in [delegation-as-authority-residuation](../../../../triage/theorems/delegation-as-authority-residuation.md); jurisdiction in [jurisdiction-as-base-change](jurisdiction-as-base-change.md); chain-of-command in [military-command-chain-as-modal-composition](../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/triage/military-command-chain-as-modal-composition.md). The coupling of delegation with base-change — how a delegated grant transports under jurisdictional restriction, succession of the principal, novation of the source, or scope amendment — is [delegation-transport-via-beck-chevalley](../../../../triage/theorems/delegation-transport-via-beck-chevalley.md), which uses the predication-categories [Beck–Chevalley commutation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/beck-chevalley-commutation-on-predication-bilattices.md) result as the substrate-algebraic guarantee that "delegate then re-base" agrees with "re-base then delegate" up to canonical iso.
