military-command/ holds 53 terms; ~13 define general organizational concepts (captain, ship, crew, command, duty-officer, etc.) that happen to be grounded in naval tradition but are not military-specific. They belong in `sociology/domains/work/domains/command/`, grounded additionally in Weber, Barnard, Fayol, Mintzberg, Follett, admiralty/maritime law (ISM, SOLAS, MLC, Hague-Visby), and organizational sociology. Also brings captain + ship from the FARS project directory into the library proper. Classification, non-military sources per term, step-by-step procedure, and parallel groups are at `analyses/command-domain-extraction-context.md`.

Open: should work/domains/command/ have a texts/ directory (candidates: "Command authority and consent" drawing from Barnard + Follett + maritime; Prussian General Staff model; management-theory lineage survey)? Does "ship" need a non-metaphorical alternative word, or does the admiralty personification doctrine settle it? Does Barnard deserve his own term definition?
