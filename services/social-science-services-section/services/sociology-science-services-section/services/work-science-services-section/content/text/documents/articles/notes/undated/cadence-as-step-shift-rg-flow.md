# Cadence as step-shift renormalization-group flow

Operational life is organized into **nested cadences**: daily standups inside weekly sprints inside monthly cycles inside quarterly planning inside annual reviews inside generational role-transfers. Each cadence-level is a [recurrent-practice](../../../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/terms/recurrent-practice.md), and each level's recurrent practice is generated by the closure of the next-finer level's settlings.

The substrate-algebraic content of this nesting is the [step-shift morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/step-shift-morphism.md) of predication categories. Cadence-depth is *step-depth*; the daily-to-weekly aggregation is the *step-shift*; the cohomological rank of a cadence-hierarchy is the [step-$n$ classifying invariant](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/step-n-classifying-invariant.md). The corpus-wide renormalization-group flow on substitution moduli appears, in the operations-settling instance, as the **renormalization flow on cadence depth**.

## Setup: cadence as a step-$n$ rule

A **cadence-rule** at depth $n$ is a closure rule on the operations settling whose look-back depth is $n$ time-units of the next-finer cadence. Concretely:

- **Step-1 (atomic):** A single settling cycle — a single workday, a single shift, a single transaction. The closure rule looks back 1 unit (the immediately prior moment).
- **Step-2 (daily-into-weekly):** A weekly cadence-rule looks back across the 5–7 atomic days of the prior week. Substitution-matrix entries: $M_{ij} = $ number of stage-$j$ daily settlings the weekly closure rule consumes to produce stage-$i$ weekly content.
- **Step-3 (weekly-into-monthly):** Monthly closure looks back across 4 weekly settlings. The substitution matrix is $3 \times 3$ in the weekly/monthly content alphabet.
- **Step-4 (monthly-into-quarterly):** Quarterly closure looks back across 3 monthly settlings.
- **Step-5 (quarterly-into-annual):** Annual closure looks back across 4 quarterly settlings.
- **Step-6 and above (annual-into-generational):** Generational closure looks back across many years of annual settlings.

Each level's cadence-rule is a [step-$n$ substitution rule](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/step-n-substitution-rule.md) on the operations-settling subcategory $\mathcal{C}^{(n)}$ of cells settled at depth $\leq n$. The substitution matrix $M_n$ encodes how the next-finer cadence-content is aggregated into the current cadence-content.

## The cadence step-shift

**Theorem (cadence step-shift).** *The aggregation of cadence-depth $n$ content into cadence-depth $n+1$ content is the [step-shift morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/step-shift-morphism.md) instantiated on operational cadence-rules. Specifically:*

1. *(Substitution-matrix extension.) The cadence-depth-$(n+1)$ rule's substitution matrix is*
   $$M_{n+1} = \begin{pmatrix} M_n & \mathbf{c}_n \\ \mathbf{r}_n & 0 \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{Mat}_{(n+1) \times (n+1)}(\mathbb{N}),$$
   *where $\mathbf{c}_n, \mathbf{r}_n$ are the augmentation vectors recording how the new (depth-$(n+1)$) content alphabet relates to the existing (depth-$\leq n$) content. For most operational cadences, $\mathbf{c}_n = (k, 0, \ldots, 0)^T$ and $\mathbf{r}_n = (1, 0, \ldots, 0)$ where $k$ is the multiplicity (5 daily settlings per workweek, 4 weekly per monthly, etc.).*

2. *(Moduli embedding.) The cadence-depth-$n$ moduli $\mathscr{C}^{(n)}$ embeds into $\mathscr{C}^{(n+1)}$ via the [step-shift moduli morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/step-shift-morphism.md). Operationally: every weekly cadence-rule extends to a candidate monthly cadence-rule; not every monthly rule descends to a weekly one (this is the embedding, not equivalence).*

3. *(Classifying-invariant rank, conditional.) When the cadence substitution matrix $M_n$ is **primitive and non-singular** in the sense required by the [step-$n$ cohomological-rank theorem](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/references/step-n-cohomological-rank-theorem.md), the classifying invariant has rank $n$: $K_n \cong \mathbb{Z}^n$. Under these hypotheses, the cohomological rank of an organization's cadence hierarchy equals its cadence-depth. Without primitivity (e.g., a cadence that aggregates only a single content-type at each level) or with singular substitution structure, the rank can be strictly smaller; the equality is a hypothesis-conditional claim, not an unconditional one.*

## Sketch

The atomic cadence-rule (step 1) is the operations-settling's per-stage closure step: one stage looks back one stage. The recurrent-practice generated by an operations-loop at depth 1 is a step-1 substitution rule with $1 \times 1$ matrix $M_1 = (1)$ — trivially periodic.

The depth-$(n+1)$ aggregation closure rule fires when $k$ depth-$n$ settlings have closed; its consequent settles depth-$(n+1)$ content cells. By [closure 2-monad](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/closure-2-monad.md) lax idempotence, this aggregation is closure-stable: re-firing the depth-$(n+1)$ rule on already-settled depth-$(n+1)$ content produces no change.

The substitution-matrix structure follows from the standard step-shift construction: the new alphabet letter (the depth-$(n+1)$ content type) is mapped to from a fixed multiplicity $k$ of depth-$n$ content (the augmentation $\mathbf{c}_n$), and itself maps to the depth-$n$ content (the augmentation $\mathbf{r}_n$). The block structure of $M_{n+1}$ is exactly the step-shift matrix.

The classifying-invariant rank claim follows from the [step-$n$ cohomological-rank theorem](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/references/step-n-cohomological-rank-theorem.md): a primitive non-singular step-$n$ rule has classifying-invariant $\mathbb{Z}^n$. Each cadence-level adds one cohomological generator. $\square$

## Operational reading: cadence depth as substrate-algebraic depth (under hypotheses)

The theorem makes precise an operational intuition that holds **under the primitive + non-singular hypotheses of part (3)**: adding a new cadence level adds one cohomological dimension to the organization's substrate-algebraic structure. The illustrations below are *heuristic examples assuming those hypotheses hold*, not unconditional consequences:

- A startup with only daily standups and ad-hoc decisions has classifying-invariant $\mathbb{Z}^1$ — when the daily cadence-rule is primitive and non-singular, every closure-stable obligation is at the daily level.
- Adding a weekly sprint cycle, *under the same hypotheses*, lifts the classifying-invariant to $\mathbb{Z}^2$ — new obligations now exist that are *only visible at the weekly level* (sprint goals, weekly retrospectives, weekly stakeholder updates).
- Under continued hypothesis-satisfaction, adding monthly cycles lifts to $\mathbb{Z}^3$, quarterly to $\mathbb{Z}^4$, annual to $\mathbb{Z}^5$, generational to $\mathbb{Z}^6$.

When the hypotheses *fail*, the rank can be strictly smaller than depth. **Concrete failure example:** an organization that adds a monthly cadence whose only content is a roll-up summary of the four weekly cadences (the substitution matrix's new row/column is degenerate — a singular extension that copies rather than generates new content) has classifying-invariant rank $2$ rather than $3$. The added cadence-level contributes no new cohomological generator because no new structurally-distinct content type is produced; the apparent depth is decorative rather than substrate-algebraic.

When the hypotheses *do* hold, each new generator corresponds to a class of obligations *that cannot be reduced to lower-cadence obligations*. The annual review is not the sum of the four quarterly reviews; the annual content is a new cohomological generator, structurally independent of the lower cadences.

This is why "rolling up" lower-cadence content to higher-cadence content loses information *whenever the higher cadence's substitution structure is non-degenerate*: the higher cadence then carries cohomological content the lower cadence cannot represent, and that content sits in the cokernel of the rollup. Rollups across degenerate cadence levels lose nothing — but those cadences are also adding nothing.

## RG-flow interpretation: cadence as renormalization

The step-shift morphism is the corpus's [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/renormalization-group-flow.md) on substitution moduli. Applied to cadence-rules, this gives:

- **The flow direction is coarsening.** Step-$(n+1)$ rules look back across larger time-scales; finer-grained operational detail is integrated out. The annual review integrates out the daily and weekly noise to record only the cadence-depth-5 content.
- **Fixed points would be scale-invariant cadences.** A fixed point of the cadence step-shift would be a cadence-rule whose step-shifted version is closure-equivalent to itself: an organization whose monthly content has the same structural form as its weekly content, etc. Operationally these would be *fractal cadences* — organizations whose practice repeats self-similarly across cadence depths. The cited [step-shift morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/morphisms/step-shift-morphism.md) marks concrete existence of such fixed points as open and speculative; whether any traditional crafts, monastic rules, or permaculture practices realize them is a downstream empirical-formal question, not an established result.
- **The colimit of the cadence step-chain is the [step-$n$ fixed-point object](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/step-n-substitution-recurrence/content/step-n-substitution-rule/content/step-n-moduli-substack/content/step-n-fixed-point/step-n-fixed-point.md).** Per the cited source, the structural content of this colimit is itself open — possibly trivial, possibly rich. *Conjecturally*, the colimit is the *limit cadence* — a candidate structural object at infinite cadence depth that would capture cross-generational and civilizational obligations no finite-depth cadence can fully express. The conjecture is not established here.

The cadence hierarchy is therefore a renormalization flow: each step-shift coarsens the operational time-scale, and *under the primitive + non-singular hypotheses* adds a cohomological dimension; the flow ascends through finite cadence depths toward a colimit whose structural content remains, per the cited PCT sources, an open question.

- [handoff-cobordism](../../../../../../../../../social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/handoff-cobordism.md) — the cadence boundary is a handoff cobordism between consecutive instances of a cadence-loop; the step-shift adds one cohomological dimension to the cobordism's transfer map at each cadence-level boundary.
- [retrospect-via-cosettling](../../../../glossary/terms/retrospect-via-cosettling.md) — retrospect at cadence-depth $n$ produces cosettlings on the depth-$n$ subcategory; cross-cadence retrospect (e.g., an annual review reaching back to daily decisions) crosses step-shift boundaries and is structurally more expensive.

## Open downstream

- *Fractal cadence design.* For which cadence multiplicities $k$ does the step-shift have a fixed point? An organizational form invariant under step-shift would be a maximally-coherent cadence hierarchy — every level structurally identical to every other. The mathematical question is when the cadence substitution matrix admits a self-similar block structure.
- *Cadence-misalignment as RG-flow obstruction.* When two adjacent cadences are misaligned (e.g., bi-weekly sprints crossing monthly boundaries), the step-shift fails to extend cleanly. The obstruction is a non-trivial $H^1$-class at the cadence boundary; the operational symptom is "we keep losing content at the boundary between cadence X and cadence Y."
- *Rotational-cadence interaction.* When [rotational-authority](../../../../../../services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/content/glossary/terms/rotational-authority.md) cycles align with a cadence (rotation period = cadence period), the rotation closure cobordism factors through the step-shift; when they don't, the rotation produces a non-trivial cohomological residue at every cadence boundary.
