Nine parallel inquiry tasks producing nine survey texts for the operations loop. The foundational one (ops-ontology-mapping) grounds operations in the repo's own thing/object/quality/state/act/vessel/container ontology — a task IS a thing, a plan IS a container, acts change state — rather than building a separate system. The other eight cover the loop stages: planning (CPM/PERT/Goldratt/PMBOK/GTD/Bratman/Haudenosaunee), dispatch (Mintzberg/Fayol/Barnard/MRTA/Kanban), execution (Harel/van der Aalst/Little/Reason/TOTE), tracking (EVM/kanban/Tufte/Stevens), review (AAR/Deming/Schön/Allspaw/stage-gate/Haudenosaunee), feedback (Wiener/Ashby/Argyris-Schön/Kaizen/ADRs/experimentation), cross-cutting (event sourcing, statecharts, flow, cadence, portfolio), and work-types (Perrow/Thompson/Cynefin/Stacey — is meaning × execution the right 2×2?). Completed 2026-04-03. Full source lists per survey at `analyses/ops-research-foundations-context.md`.
