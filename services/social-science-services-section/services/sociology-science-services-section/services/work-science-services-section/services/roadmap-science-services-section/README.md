# Roadmap science services section

The study of the roadmap: what a plan is before anyone is dispatched to it, and how progress toward it is read rather than asserted.
This section is the science seat of the roadmap thread; its engineering seat is [roadmap-engineering-services-section](../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/services/agent-work-computer-system-engineering-services-section/services/roadmap-engineering-services-section/README.md), its operations seat [roadmap-operations-services-section](../../../../../../../../operations-services-section/services/project-operations-services-section/services/roadmap-operations-services-section/README.md), and the Group's own running of it [561-group-roadmap-services-section](../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-project-management-services-section/services/561-group-roadmap-services-section/README.md).
The public seat is a named debt.

The theory is already written in this section's parent and its neighbours, and this seat cites it rather than restating it:

- Planning is the production of the unsaturated form: a plan commits some fiber predicates to true and leaves the who, when, and what-was-produced slots open until dispatch and execution saturate them ([FARS algebraic readings of work terms, section 11](../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/04/08/2026-04-08-note-on-fars-algebraic-readings-of-work-terms.md)).
  A roadmap is that unsaturated form read off the record of work.
- Milestones are derived states, never asserted: a milestone is a named cell-set with target valuations, and "reached" is computed by a fold over the work-ledger cut, strata by causal height ([endeavor operating system schema synthesis](../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-project-management-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/specifications/2026-09-01-endeavor-operating-system-schema-synthesis.md)).
- A work record's horizon (Now, Soon, Later, Eventually) is derived from its modality and never stored as an independent field ([work orchestration by modality, horizon, and force](../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-services-section/services/561-group-enterprise-project-management-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/specifications/2026-09-04-work-orchestration-by-modality-horizon-and-force.md)).
- The force on a plan's predicates is deontic weight, and the deontic operators are derived from the three governance judgments ([theory of governance](../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/lakota-services-section/content/text/glossary/lakota/discharging/library/predicating/library/positing/library/settling/library/hacking/lakotaean-governance/questions/what-is-the-theory-of-governance.md)).
- The algebra of work the plan ranges over: [algebraic structure of work](../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/algebraic-structure-of-work.md), [ontology of work operations](../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/ontology-of-work-operations.md), [formal planning theory](../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/formal-planning-theory.md), [planning and scheduling survey](../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/planning-and-scheduling-survey.md).
