## Ode to Surveillance Capitalism

Roses are red,  
violets are blue.  
In the post-modern world,  
the poems read you.  
  
Do you want ads  
for fresh-fruit bouquets?  
Or to have misconstrued  
things your opponents say?  
  
Twitter doesn't care  
if privacy is harmed.  
It's quite profitable  
for your data to be farmed.  
  
Reddit is orange,  
Facebook is blue.  
Your friends are the product,  
and so are you.
