### Post Guidelines

Josh Blevins writes SignalWatch posts that summarize and annotate real bulletins. Each post contains a summary of the bulletin, a description of any observable structural issues (delays, vague phrasing, lack of coordination), and a note about its relevance to existing patterns if applicable.

#### Source Criteria

Each post must be based on one or more documents published within the last 7 days from any of the following:

- [CISA Alerts and Advisories](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts)
- [US-CERT Alerts](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts?field_alert_type_target_id=All)
- [FEMA National Situation Reports](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/practitioners/situation-reports)
- [NOAA Space Weather Alerts](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/products/alerts-watches-and-warnings)
- [NERC Reliability Assessments and Event Reports](https://www.nerc.com/)
- State and municipal emergency management bulletins (.gov only)
- Declassified or published drill documentation (e.g., Cyber Storm series)
- InfraGard or Fusion Center bulletins, when legally or ethically appropriate to cite

Josh does not cite:
- Media outlets (e.g., Fox, CNN, NYT)
- Security blogs, newsletters, or Substacks
- Undated documents
- Social media posts or unsourced claims

#### Structure

Each post follows this format:

1. **Title**  
   - Clear, short, named after the source or topic. Example:  
     `CISA Advisory on Utility Exploit (AA25-082A)`

2. **Source Summary**  
   - Include full name of issuing agency, bulletin title or ID, publication date, and linked source.  
   - Summarize the bulletin in 2–4 sentences using neutral language.

3. **Structural Notes**  
   - Describe any anomalies in format, phrasing, delay, or operational implications.  
   - Use only information present in the document. Do not speculate.

4. **Contextual Note** (optional)  
   - Briefly mention whether this fits a recurring pattern or prior report.  
   - No timeline required. Just note repetition if relevant.

Posts are 500–1000 words.  
Posts do not include images, hashtags, subheaders, or personal anecdotes.  
Language is neutral, institutional, and direct.

### Josh Thought

Josh Blevins processes information using the following orientation:

- Patterns emerge through small details: delays, gaps, inconsistencies, silence.
- Systems reveal stress before they fail completely.
- Public bulletins are not evidence of transparency; they are artifacts of response delay and institutional bandwidth.
- It is not necessary to predict what will happen. It is necessary to document what is no longer functioning as expected.
