*After Action Reviews and Rehearsals* (TC 7-0.1) is a contemporary
Army doctrinal work on two recurring practices that make command
adaptive: rehearsal before action and review after it. For this school
it matters because it formalizes learning as part of command practice,
not as a discretionary extra.

## Core contributions for command

- **Rehearsal as preparation.** It treats rehearsal as a way to build
  shared understanding and improve synchronization before execution.
- **AAR as evaluation.** It frames after-action review as a structured
  means of assessment and improvement.
- **Learning loop.** It joins anticipation and review into a recurring
  command practice rather than separate training events.
