**Californian projection** is the apparatus in which California — the actual coastal territory and the imaginative screen of the same name — has served as the historical laboratory in which the late-liberal formation iterates: prototyping subject-formatting infrastructures locally and then exporting them.
The Misiones Californianas, the Gold Rush, Hollywood, Esalen, Silicon Valley, the wellness industry are not unrelated cultural phenomena.
They are successive iterations of a continuous apparatus that converts particular features of place (climate, settler-colonial displacement, distance from East Coast institutions, Pacific orientation) into the production of inhabitable subject-positions and exportable cultural infrastructures.
Each iteration tests new subject-formatting configurations on a local social base and exports the successful results globally.
The accumulated result is [californication](../morphisms/californication.md): the global morphism that now runs on those exported configurations.

## Two senses of projection

The term operates in two simultaneous registers.
In the *cartographic* sense: a projection maps one surface onto another with systematic distortion.
In the *psychoanalytic* sense: projection attributes one's own contents to an external screen so that they can be encountered as if external.
Californian projection is both: the actual coastal territory has been continuously mapped onto by the imaginative screen, and that screen has been used to externalize American (and increasingly global) social contents — contradiction, aspiration, apocalypse, reinvention — that could not be encountered as internal.

## Three structural features

**Iteration of one apparatus.** The Missions tested conversion of indigenous populations through managed labor, belief, and daily practice.
The Gold Rush tested extraction-as-opportunity as a subject-position.
Hollywood tested industrial production of inhabitable fantasy.
Esalen and the counterculture tested the conversion of political contradiction into personal-spiritual-development.
Silicon Valley tested platform infrastructure for affect-capture and engagement optimization.
The wellness industry tests the commodification of self-regulation.
Each is a version of the same closure-rule operation: take a structural contradiction, format it as personal experience, produce a viable subject-position, export the format.

**Test-and-export logic.** The apparatus tests subject-formatting infrastructures locally and exports them globally.
The Missions exported the template of conversion-through-managed-practice across Latin America.
Hollywood exported the industrial production of inhabitable fantasy across the twentieth-century world.
Silicon Valley exported platform infrastructure as the contemporary global closing-rule apparatus.
The wellness industry is exporting self-regulation-as-lifestyle in real time.
Each iteration runs the test-and-export sequence; together they constitute the apparatus's function.

**Erasure as condition.** The projection's continuity depends on the systematic erasure of what was on the land before each iteration.
The Indigenous Californian peoples, the Mexican-Californian dispossession, the Black and Asian populations of Hollywood's era, the Indigenous and immigrant labor of Silicon Valley — each is erased in the iteration's record.
The erasure is not accidental; it is structural.
The apparatus can only install its new subject-formatting configurations on a social base that has been cleared.
[Settlerism](settlerism.md) is the structural form of this clearing; the Californian projection is settlerism's specific operation at the Pacific terminal coast.

## Relation to californication

[Californication](../morphisms/californication.md) is the global morphism that the Californian projection has produced and now distributes.
Californian projection is the apparatus (the historical laboratory, the iteration sequence, the test-and-export logic); californication is the closure-rule operation that the apparatus has exported as its current active product.
The distinction matters for analysis: californication can be identified and characterized without reference to the California geography; the Californian projection is the historical explanation of how californication came to exist and why it takes the specific form it does. ---
