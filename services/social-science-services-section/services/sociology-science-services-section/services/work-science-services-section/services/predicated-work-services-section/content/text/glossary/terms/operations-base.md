# Operations base

The base $B_{\mathrm{op}} = (\mathbf{2}, \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}, E_{\mathrm{op}}, \lambda)$ over which operational work is a [predication settling](../../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md). Each component:

- **Residuated lattice $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{2}$.** Operational evidence is binary at the bilattice's per-coordinate level; an event either witnesses a positive or negative datum, or it does not. The fiber values live in $\mathbf{2}^\bowtie \cong \mathbf{FOUR}$ — the four-element bilattice.
- **Predicate category $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{op}}$.** Objects: the predicates of the work vocabulary in [library/.../sociology/work/](../../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work), including action–agent (`assigned-to`, `completed-by`, `dispatched-by`, `failed-by`), action–capability (`requires-capability`, `provides-capability`), action–goal (`has-goal`, `terminates-on`), action–action (`decomposes-into`, `depends-on`), and Hohfeldian-position predicates (`holds-power-over`, `has-duty-to`, `has-privilege-of`, `bears-immunity-from`). Morphisms: those generated by the four [predicate-category generators](../../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predicate-generator-exhaustiveness.md) on these predicates. A finite enumeration makes the object and generator sets finite; it does not by itself make the category finite. A category freely generated by one object and one endomorphism has infinitely many arrows (the successive powers). Finiteness of this category requires relations or an explicit bound that makes the composite-arrow set finite. Smallness likewise requires an actual set-sized presentation.
- **Entity set $E_{\mathrm{op}}$.** Disjoint union of agents, offices, tasks, plans, workflows, capabilities, scopes, and the operational records (witnesses, observations, sessions). A bounded recorded snapshot can have finite $E_{\mathrm{op}}$. Ongoing admission can enlarge it. Countability of the accumulated carrier requires a specified countable admission process; a finite time interval alone is not a finiteness proof.
- **Settling bound $\lambda$.** $\omega$ for the standard reading: operations advance through countably many event-driven stages.

## Conditional regime reading

Apply the [work-regime contract](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/concepts/work-regime.md)
to an explicitly fixed carrier, knowledge order, initial valuation and one-step
rule operator. An inflationary monotone operator on N FOUR coordinates can
make at most 2N strict knowledge increases. This proves finite *internal*
saturation, not timely task execution or eventual cessation of outside events.
Scott continuity bounds the bottom-started fixed-input iteration by ω; equality
requires an orbit that does not already stabilize at a finite stage.

A period with bounded records must be distinguished from an open history that
admits new entities, rules, norms and increments. Statements that particular
charter, aim or aggregate rules require a higher regime need their exact
operator and an ordinal argument. The rule's natural-language subject or
quantifier count alone does not supply that argument.

The single-ship worked instance can be read as a chosen finite snapshot of
agents, offices, tasks, capabilities and records, with later admissions kept
as later increments. No closure-ordinal classification is inferred from the
ship's name or organizational form.
