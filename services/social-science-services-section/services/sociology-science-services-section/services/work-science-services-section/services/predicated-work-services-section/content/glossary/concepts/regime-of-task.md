# Regime of a task

A [task cell system](../../../../../../../../social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/task-cell-system.md) $T = (G_T, \mathcal{S}_T, v^T_0)$ embedded in an [operations settling](../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/operations-settling.md) is in:

- the **strict regime** iff $\mathcal{S}_T$ is finite, $G_T$ is finite, and the entity references in $\mathcal{S}_T$'s antecedents are bounded by a finite sub-set of $E_{\mathrm{op}}$;
- the **Scott-continuous regime** iff $\mathcal{S}_T$ is Scott-continuous as a closure-rule-language fragment but its antecedent-references admit countably-infinite entity sub-sets;
- the **transfinite-bounded regime** iff $\mathcal{S}_T$ contains rules with non-Scott-continuous antecedents (e.g., "if every prior cycle has shown $P$") and the antecedent-aggregation cofinality is countable;
- the **aperiodic regime** iff the antecedent-aggregation cofinality is uncountable.

## Sketch

By the [four-regime dichotomy](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/lib/obsmetrologia/docs/verso/mark/content/utterance/articulating/content/articulation-cell/content/potential-articulation-cell/predicating/content/predication-cell/content/potential-predication-cell/positing/content/valuation/content/knowledge-least-valuation/settling/references/four-regime-dichotomy.md), the regime of any settling is determined by the closure ordinal of its closure operator. For a task cell system embedded in an operations settling, the *task-restricted* closure operator is $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_T}$ — the closure generated by the task-local subsystem $\mathcal{S}_T$.

By [predicate-generator exhaustiveness](../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predicate-generator-exhaustiveness.md), the task-restricted closure operator's regime depends on:

The theorem classifies the *operator-regime* of the task-restricted closure operator $\mathrm{cl}^{\mathcal{S}_T}$: the worst-case closure ordinal over all possible increment sequences interacting with $\mathcal{S}_T$. A specific execution of task $T$ under a concrete increment sequence $u$ may achieve a *realized regime* strictly below the operator-regime.

**Example.** A task whose $\mathcal{S}_T$ contains a "if every prior attempt has failed, escalate" rule is classified as transfinite-bounded by the theorem (the rule has a limit-aggregating antecedent). But if the task succeeds on the first attempt (no prior failures), the escalation rule's antecedent is never satisfied, and the realized regime for that execution is strict. The operator-regime is an upper bound; the realized regime is the actual behavior.

For practical dispatch and scheduling, the operator-regime tells the community *what kind of work this is* (its structural complexity class). The realized regime of a specific run tells the community *what actually happened*. Both levels of classification are useful; they answer different questions. See [regime-classification-scope](../../../../../../../../social-theory-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/working/_archive/questions/regime-classification-scope.md) for the full treatment.

1. The cardinality of the task's predication-cell sub-set ($|G_T| + $ ancestor cells in the antecedents).
2. The Scott-continuity of $\mathcal{S}_T$'s closure-rule-language fragment.
3. The cofinality of the antecedent-aggregation if non-Scott-continuous.

Applying the dichotomy's equivalences regime-by-regime:

- **Strict.** Finite $G_T$, finite $\mathcal{S}_T$, finite antecedent-references give finite ascending chains in the task-restricted fiber algebras. $\kappa < \omega$.
- **Scott-continuous.** Countable antecedent-references with Scott-continuous closure rules give $\kappa = \omega$. The task settles "in the limit" — the goal cell carries its target value at the $\omega$-th stage.
- **Transfinite-bounded.** Non-Scott-continuous closure rule with countable cofinality. $\kappa$ is some specific countable ordinal $> \omega$. The task involves aggregating across countably-many prior stages.
- **Aperiodic.** Non-Scott-continuous closure rule with uncountable cofinality. $\kappa \geq \omega_1$. The task does not stabilize at any countable ordinal; it carries cohomological content. $\square$

## Examples per regime

- **Strict-regime task**: "review this PR" — the goal cell is a single `completed-by` cell, the closure rule is the trivial transition open → potential → completed, finite computation.
- **Scott-continuous-regime task**: "tag every open-source advisory in the past 30 days" — the goal cell aggregates across a countably-infinite candidate set, but each candidate's check is finitary. Settles at $\omega$.
- **Transfinite-bounded-regime task**: "after every prior weekly cycle has produced at least one Routine Read post, conclude the persona has stabilized" — the closure rule's antecedent quantifies across all prior cycles. Settles at some countable ordinal depending on cycle structure.
- **Aperiodic-regime task**: not a task in the work vocabulary's sense; this regime is for [endeavor-functions](function-as-aperiodic-settling.md), not for individual tasks.

## Corollary — most operational tasks are strict-regime

The work vocabulary's [task lifecycle](../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/lifecycle.md) (open → active → completed/failed/abandoned) terminates in finite time per the lifecycle's *constrained state machine* structure: each transition fires on a single witness, the state space is finite, no transition aggregates across an infinite history. This places typical operational tasks in the strict regime.

## Corollary — recurrence is non-strict

A [recurrent practice](../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/recurrence.md) (return of a pattern with novelty across cycles, per Prigogine's irreversible-time arrow) is *not* a single task but a closure rule that fires once per cycle, with the cycles indexed by a countable ordinal sequence. The recurrent practice's settling lives in the Scott-continuous regime if the cycles' content is independent (each cycle's settling depends on the increment for that cycle alone) and in the transfinite-bounded regime if cycles' content depends on aggregations of prior cycles (e.g., a cumulative review).
