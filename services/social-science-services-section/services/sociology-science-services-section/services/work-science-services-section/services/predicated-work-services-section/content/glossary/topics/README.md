# Predicated Work

A theory of [work](../../_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics: operational work as a [predication settling](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-settling.md) over a work-themed predicate category. Tasks, plans, workflows, capabilities, dispatch, completion, recurrence — the work vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this substrate.

Sibling to [governance](../governance/) (also a theory of work). Predicated-work is the bilattice-substrate-native theory; the existing work texts ([formal-structure-of-office](../../../../../content/documents/notes/2026-04-03-note-on-formal-structure-of-office.md), [formal-structure-of-delegation](../../../../../content/documents/notes/2026-04-03-note-on-formal-structure-of-delegation.md), [algebraic-structure-of-work](../../texts/algebraic-structure-of-work.md)) provide parallel formalizations.

Following the predication-categories research convention. See the [predication-categories index](../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) for frontmatter/relation details.

## Layout

- `concepts/` — semantic anchors for work-relevant structure (forthcoming as needed).
- `objects/` — the substrate (operations-base, operations-settling, capability-fiber-bundle), work units (task-cell-system, plan, workflow), temporal structure (recurrent-practice), aim-generative structure (enterprise).
- `morphisms/` — operations on work: dispatch, decomposition, witness/increment correspondence.
- `theorems/` — completion-as-quiescence, regime-of-task, regime-of-recurrent-practice, workflow-composition, function-as-aperiodic-settling.
- `questions/` — open questions: capacity, decomposition patterns, abandonment, regime classification scope, function closure rules, $H^1$-class operational meaning.
- `theories/` — subtheories.

## Subtheories

- [`predicated-governance/`](theories/predicated-governance/) — the *normative-substructure* slice of work: jural relations, offices, normative systems, charters, the Hohfeldian eightfold, and the operations that modify normative state (installation, delegation, assignment, novation, waiver, revocation, succession, ratification, etc.). The bilattice-substrate-native counterpart of the [governance](../governance/) theory.

## What this program is for

Tasks, plans, workflows, recurrence, completion are the *operational* layer of work — what gets done, when, by whom. The predicated-governance subtheory studies the *normative* layer — who has the standing to do what, what authority configures dispatch, what counts as charter-compliant. Both layers live on the same operations-settling substrate; the two together cover the work vocabulary's content.

The math program's machinery — bilattice-valued predication, settling dynamics under closure, four-regime classification, cohomology and moduli, d-frame-valued sheaves — is the substrate the program runs on. Predicated-work does not re-derive the math; it instantiates it.
