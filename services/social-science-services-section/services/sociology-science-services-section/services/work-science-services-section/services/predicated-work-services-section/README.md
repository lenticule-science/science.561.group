# Predicated Work Services Section

This section models work as evidence-bearing predications and their permitted
transformations. It is a realization of the [general work theory](../../README.md),
not the definition of every cultural or institutional form of working.

## Structures and transformations

- [Operations base](content/text/glossary/terms/operations-base.md): entities,
  predicates, evidence carrier and event-indexing assumptions.
- [Capability sections](content/text/glossary/terms/capability-fiber-bundle.md):
  agent provision and task requirement; dispatch compares them without
  confusing ability with authority.
- [Task decomposition](content/text/glossary/terms/decompose-task.md): relates
  parent and child goals under a declared AND, OR or other combining rule.
  Every child being terminal does not itself prove the parent target.
- [Typed workflow composition](content/glossary/concepts/workflow-composition.md):
  sequential boundaries, effects and the categorical laws still owed.
- [Operations loop](content/text/glossary/terms/operations-loop.md): planning,
  dispatch, execution, tracking, review and feedback as a recurrent practice.
- [Completion](content/glossary/concepts/completion-as-quiescence.md): scoped
  saturation, target satisfaction and receiving, kept distinct.
- [Work regimes](../../content/text/glossary/concepts/work-regime.md): explicit
  bounds and counterexamples for task and recurrent-practice claims.
- [Predicated Governance](services/predicated-governance-services-section/README.md):
  norms, authority, grants and their authorized transformations.

## Scientific status

Definitions and sketches here are prose models unless a specific checked
declaration and its hypotheses are cited. The original
[predicated-work source](https://github.com/emsenn/561-theory/tree/04a09cb4f14dfa58994db7a6864fe7771ddca882/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work)
contains additional structures and transformations whose migration must retain
their versions and meanings. A term named theorem, a `status: proved` field,
or a source file does not supply a kernel-check receipt.

The active consumer is reusable Organizing over SignCells, including non-code
Missions, actor capabilities, authority, recorded effects and causal work
frontiers. Fabrication is a consumer of that contract. Source vocabulary must
be reconciled with actual identity and relation records before code uses it.
