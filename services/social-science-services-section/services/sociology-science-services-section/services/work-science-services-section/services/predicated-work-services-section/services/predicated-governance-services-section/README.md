# Predicated Governance Services Section

This section models the normative organization of work: obligations,
permissions, claims, authority, offices and the acts that change them.
It inherits the evidence-bearing substrate of [Predicated Work](../../README.md).

## Typed transformations

| Transformation | Input and condition | Output to assess |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Norm activation | Applicable norm, antecedent, bearer and authority | A normative relation, not the performed act |
| Delegation | Valid source grant and bounded recipient scope | Derived grant with accountability and revocation conditions |
| Assignment | Assignable claim and governing notice/consent conditions | Claim-holder change with the correlative duty preserved |
| Revocation | Existing grant and valid power to revoke | An effective withdrawal record; historical grant retained |
| Succession | Office, prior bearer, successor and installation conditions | Changed incumbency with institutional continuity |
| Receiving | Named required result, evidence and authorized receiver | Acceptance, rejection or unresolved assessment in an explicit scope |

The detailed models are [norm](content/text/glossary/terms/norm.md),
[delegation](content/text/glossary/terms/delegation.md),
[assignment](content/text/glossary/terms/assignment.md),
[revocation](content/text/glossary/terms/revocation.md) and
[succession](content/text/glossary/terms/succession.md).
These drafts require their stated felicity conditions; names alone do not
grant powers. Particular legal or cultural traditions need their own sources
and cannot be inferred from the Hohfeldian model.

## Invariants for consumers

Capability and authority are different. Duty and performance are different.
Producer self-check and independent receiving are different. Unknown evidence
is not permission, and normative priority is not a free consequence of
least-fixed-point closure. Revocation cannot erase an old evidence polarity
while claiming monotonicity of that same cell.

Mission rights belong to its commission: an existing external commissioner,
a distinct executor, a defined end-state, recall by the commissioner and
executor abandonment only where the order permits. Task dispatch or a code
pipeline cannot substitute its own universal lifecycle for those rights.

## Research frontier

The [priority question](content/text/documents/notes/questions-on-precedence-in-least-fixed-point.md)
remains open. Current effective-authority projection, time/scope revision and
the map from this source vocabulary to canonical SignCell relation identities
also need explicit contracts and receiving cases. This section does not
claim that these drafts are kernel-checked or deployed.
