# Predicated Governance

A subtheory of [predicated-work](../../../../../content/glossary/topics/README.md). Studies the *normative* substructure of work: jural relations, offices, normative systems, charters, the Hohfeldian eightfold, and the operations that modify normative state (installation, delegation, assignment, novation, waiver, revocation, etc.).

The library treats governance as a sub-domain of work — `library/.../sociology/work/theories/governance/`. This subtheory mirrors that placement: governance is the *normative-structure* slice of work-as-relational-substrate, not a separate domain.

## What is governance

Per the library's [governance index](../../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/governance/_index.md): the structures, processes, and principles through which collectives make decisions, exercise authority, and maintain accountability. Mathematically: the operations on the operations-settling that modify which closure rules fire, who has standing to invoke them, and how authority propagates through the substrate.

## Why a subtheory rather than a sibling theory

Three reasons:

1. **Library structure.** Governance is treated as a sub-domain of work in the library, not a peer; this subtheory mirrors that.
2. **Substrate inheritance.** The predicated-governance program inherits predicated-work's [operations-base](../../../../../content/glossary/terms/operations-base.md), [operations-settling](../../objects/operations-settling.md), [witness-as-increment](../../../../../content/glossary/terms/witness-as-increment.md), and the regime-classification machinery. It does not need to re-derive these.
3. **Co-content.** The Hohfeldian apparatus (jural relations, offices, delegation, etc.) presupposes a working substrate of agents-doing-things; without that substrate, the normative structure has nothing to modify. The subtheory shape captures this dependency.

## Convention

Following the [spatiotemporal-knowledge convention](../../../../../../../../../../../library/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md). Same frontmatter, same status markers, same relation set. The subtheory's notes depend on predicated-work's main objects/morphisms in addition to the math program's.

## What this subtheory commits to

- The full Hohfeldian eightfold (both quartets) is the structural vocabulary for normative relations between agents on the substrate, with the second quartet (power, liability, immunity, disability) as *structural-level operations* on the closure-rule system and the first quartet (claim, duty, privilege, no-claim) as *object-level* normative facts encoded in jural-relation entities.
- Offices are substrate entities with two required predicates (recognition, induction) per the work vocabulary's [formal-structure-of-office](../../../../../../../content/text/documents/notes/2026-04-03-note-on-formal-structure-of-office.md).
- Charters are closure-rule subsystems organized by the rule-tier hierarchy.
- Installation, delegation, and the other Hohfeldian operations are specific increment+closure-rule patterns realizing structural-level changes.
