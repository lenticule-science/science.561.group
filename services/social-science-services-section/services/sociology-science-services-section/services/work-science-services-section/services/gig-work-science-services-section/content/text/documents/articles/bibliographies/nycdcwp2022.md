Published November 2022, under the mandate of Local Law 115 of 2021.

## Why this is central

This is the regulator's own study, using the pay and working-condition data Local Law 115 compelled the platforms to disclose, and it underlies the city's adopted minimum pay rate ($17.96 per hour at adoption in 2023, since risen to $22.13 per hour effective 2026-04-01, per DCWP's current delivery-worker pay page cited in the [note on American gig work as a workforce and a cost structure](../notes/2026/09/05/2026-09-05-note-on-american-gig-work-as-a-workforce-and-a-cost-structure.md), section 6). Its headline figures: delivery workers earned $14.18 per hour with tips and $7.09 without, against $3.06 per hour in expenses, for a net of $11.12 per hour with tips and $4.03 without — annualizing, at actual hours worked, to roughly the city's poverty line for a single adult. It is the only study in the [note on American gig work as a workforce and a cost structure](../notes/2026/09/05/2026-09-05-note-on-american-gig-work-as-a-workforce-and-a-cost-structure.md) produced by the regulator that then set the binding rate from it, rather than by outside academics informing a rate someone else set.

## What it does not settle
