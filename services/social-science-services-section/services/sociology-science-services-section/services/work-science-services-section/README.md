# Work Science Services Section

This section studies purposive activity, its outputs, sustained commitments,
coordination, and the normative relations that govern them. Human, collective,
and artificial agents are consumers of this theory. A software-fabrication
workflow is one application of it.

## Read the work model

The following sources have different jobs. Their names are not interchangeable
states in one mandatory lifecycle.

| Resource | Defining structure | Transformation or receiving question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Work](content/text/glossary/terms/work.md) | An attributable episode of doing with a nameable output | What changed, by whose exercised capacity, in which situation? |
| [Work unit](content/text/glossary/terms/work-unit.md) | One operation invocation with its required inputs and parameters bound | Is the invocation specified, and what output or error did it produce? |

The [Endeavor source](https://github.com/emsenn/emsenn-services-section/blob/ac826513244fe7e311cd5c8d40a016d65da88133/content/text/glossary/terms/endeavor.md)
defines directed, committed, sustained, failure-open striving. Its aim can
survive an unsuccessful attempt. The [Mission record specification](https://github.com/emsenn/emsenn-services-section/blob/ac826513244fe7e311cd5c8d40a016d65da88133/content/text/glossary/terms/emsenn-archive-org/mission.md)
restricts Endeavor by an external commissioner and a specified end-state.
Commissioner and executor are distinct existing entities. Recall is the
commissioner's act; executor abandonment depends on the order. A terminal
record is not necessarily a fulfilled commission. The [Enterprise term](../../../economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/enterprise.md)
contrasts that bounded commission with an enduring, aim-generative enterprise.
These lineage sources establish domain distinctions and record predicates;
they do not yet provide a recovered category of Missions with identity and
composition laws.

## Being, doing, working, governing

Agency's [existence carrier](../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/existence.md)
states the action tower and its recursive social-scale reading. Being yields
an agent; doing yields an attributable act; working yields a shared, sustained
work-process; governing yields the normative order under which work continues.
The [animating carrier](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/aa46e2f11aff49635bfbd7a381ee4c8b261db63c/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/animating.md)
explains how a bounded individual becomes the carrier of another social-scale
descent. Its source journals distinguish this semantic organization from four
parallel generators and mark the closure-ordinal placements as exploratory.

Use the spiral to ask what a transformation creates and what the next scale
requires. To claim a mathematical correspondence, supply its source and target
carriers, operations, preserved structure and proof. A service directory's
nesting does not prove such a correspondence. Nor does the four-valued
evidence lattice by itself classify a task's runtime or a community's culture.

## Evidence, capability, authority and completion

[Predicated Work](services/predicated-work-services-section/README.md) is a
particular mathematical realization of the general work theory. It gives
task-local goal cells, rule systems and agent/task capability sections.
Its [capability fiber bundle](services/predicated-work-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capability-fiber-bundle.md)
keeps positive, negative, absent and contradictory evidence distinct.
[Governance](services/governance-services-section/README.md) studies normative
organization; [Predicated Governance](services/predicated-work-services-section/services/predicated-governance-services-section/README.md)
represents normative relations on the work substrate. Capability does not
confer authority, obligation does not establish performance, and a quiet queue
does not establish goal achievement.

The [completion contract](services/predicated-work-services-section/content/glossary/concepts/completion-as-quiescence.md)
requires a target, a named evidence scope and a receiving criterion. The
[regime contract](content/text/glossary/concepts/work-regime.md) separates
fixed-input saturation, externally arriving evidence and recurrent work.
The [kinematic space](content/text/glossary/terms/kinematic-space-of-work.md)
orders recorded occurrences by causal dependence and groups them by
containment. These are different relations.

## Consumer contract: the Group's work in the Semioverse

Required by the Leader's October 15 direction: the 561 Group is represented by
its existing content-addressed Thing, with its Endeavors and Missions and the
necessary work they occasion. Organizing owns the reusable agency contract;
fabrication consumes it. Engineering must recover current SignCell and Thing
identity laws and relation identities before realizing this model.

1. Resolve the Group, actors, capabilities and work through reusable SignCells.
   Preserve existing content addresses, source records and co-reference
   history. A marketplace profile or code-job record cannot replace the actor
   or capability it describes.
2. Retain distinct commissioning, execution, goal, containment, causal
   dependence, capability, authority and receiving relations. Source predicate
   strings are vocabulary evidence, not fabricated canonical relation IDs.
3. Derive candidate work from the accepted purpose and unmet conditions.
   An agent's capability/authority slice filters that work; it does not make
   every technically possible move necessary. A failed attempt remains in the
   history and does not satisfy the task or Mission end-state.
4. Associate each output with its work as an addressable resource. Distinguish
   specified moves, admitted occurrences, actual effects and receiving.
   An interrupted run retains its recorded interior without an invented exit.
5. Demonstrate a non-code Mission and a fabrication Mission using the same
   Organizing contract, including recall, conditional abandonment, unavailable
   capability, authority refusal, contradictory evidence and later revision.

These are consumer requirements, not evidence that a live Group Thing or
board has been installed or received. The exact existing relation identities,
Mission category laws and cross-regime bridges remain source-recovery or
formalization obligations.
