# Work Science Services Section

This section studies work as social practice: the organization of activity,
labor, planning, coordination, review, and the normative structures that
govern them. Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.

Specialized studies continue under `services/`:

- `governance-services-section/` — the normative organization of work.
- `gig-work-science-services-section/` — American platform and gig work.
- `predicated-work-services-section/` — the formal work research program.

Engineering records that use this research to design or operate a system live
with Red Cup Engineering, not here.
