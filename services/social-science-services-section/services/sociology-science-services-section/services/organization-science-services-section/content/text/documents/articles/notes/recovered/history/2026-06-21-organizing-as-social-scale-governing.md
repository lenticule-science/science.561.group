# organizing: the social-scale governing

This identifies the domain of a fresh `organizing/` package — the second member,
after `animating`, of the boundary-binder family — the way
[the holographic-bulk note](2026-06-21-animating-as-holographic-bulk.md) identified
animating's. As there, the domain is fixed by deep `content/_archive` research, not
by analogy.

## The domain, in one statement

**organizing is the social-scale reading of `governing`: the settling of an open
plurality of agents into one bounded, law-bearing collective** — a community, an
institution, a polity. The corpus states `governing` exactly this way
(`content/_archive/animating/…/governing/governing.md`): "Where political philosophy
since Hobbes asks how a multitude becomes one body able to act and endure, the answer
named here is governing — the settling of an open plurality into a bounded,
law-bearing order," with Ostrom's *Governing the Commons* as the plainest case (a
community settles the rules that keep a shared field from exhaustion). organizing is
that move made into a package: **how many agents become one organization able to act
and endure.**

This is the **Gen-2 (inter-agent) lift of animating**. animating glues three
faculty-faces (communicating/knowing/playing) into **one agent** (the individual);
organizing glues a plurality of **agents** into **one organization**. The corpus
says the individual and the order are "two faces of one fixed point": an order is
bounded by recognizing its members as individuals, and an individual is bounded by
standing under an order. organizing is the second face.

## The bridge is the spiral (corpus-grounded, not asserted)

The link between the two scales is the corpus's own **social descent**. The
individual agent is "settled below, open above" and **re-enters `relating` among
other agents**: `content/_archive/animating/…/individual/becoming/doing/content/
relating/relating.md` seats `relating` as "the **doing** of the social-scale
descent: the acting the re-entered individual carries out among other individuals" —
Buber's lived *I–Thou*, Lévinas's face-to-face. It generates the **bond**
(friendship/kinship/contract); accumulated relating builds **community** (the shared
social field); community settles into the **institution / collective**. Simondon's
**transindividual** names the result: "the collective that is neither a mere
aggregate of individuals nor a fusion of them, but the relation in which individuals
are further individuated together."

So the social descent is the SAME four-fold descent run one scale up — `becoming →
doing(=relating among agents) → working(=community) → governing(=institution)` — over
an inter-agent carrier. That is precisely the second-generation quartet:

| Gen-1 faculty (on one agent's Meme) | Gen-2 form (among agents, over a social base) |
|---|---|
| communicating — across, endo on meme | **socializing** (convention, common ground, language-as-institution, the bond) |
| knowing — inside, comonad on meme | **computing** (distributed / repeatable knowing) |
| playing — outside, *-autonomous cut | **gaming** (concurrent games among agents — CGS is inherently multi-player) |
| **animating** — binds the three → an agent | **organizing** — binds the three → a society/organization |

The single→multi move is **diagonal → off-diagonal**: Gen-1 is operations on one
object (`communicating = End(Meme)`, a self-loop on one agent's meme; knowing a
comonad on it; playing its *-autonomous cut); Gen-2 is morphisms *between* many
(`socializing` = the hom between distinct agents' memes — the inter-subjective
field). The Gen-2 carrier is a **sheaf of agents over a social base**, and each Gen-2
faculty is its Gen-1 faculty lifted along that sheaf. So computing, gaming, and
managing-operations are **derivable from their Gen-1 faculties plus the spiral**, not
independently invented — and the quartet is one self-similar structure recurring at
every node of the agency spiral (meme → agent → society → federation → …), the same
self-similarity the manifold already has (four faces at every node).

## The structure: a holographic sheaf-gluing node at the social scale

organizing has animating's shape — a holographic bulk over three boundary faces —
lifted one scale:

- the **base** is a sheaf of **agents** over a social base (inter-agent relations),
  not memes over one agent;
- the three **boundary faces** are the Gen-2 faculties — **socializing** (across),
  **computing** (inside), **gaming** (outside) — threaded, not absorbed (organizing
  provides the organization carrier they mount on; computing and gaming already exist
  as packages, socializing is the one genuinely new sibling);
- the **interior bulk** is the **organization** (society/institution): the global
  section glued from the sheaf of agents — the first social instance of the standing
  "organizational state as a bilattice network sheaf" goal;
- the **gluing act is `adjudication`** — the corpus's own act of governing
  (`…/governing/content/adjudication/adjudication.md`): "the *receiving* dual of
  speaking," which "holds and settles the charges that many acts produce, forming a
  single state of the record from accumulating, possibly conflicting, possibly
  partial sources." It answers three pressures on one structure, and they are exactly
  our bilattice/sheaf machinery:
  - **conflict** → held as **contested** (the *stable* charge; Belnap paraconsistent,
    not collapsed),
  - **partial** → held as **open** (the *absurd* charge; silence is not denial),
  - **accumulation** → **monotone**, "converging without requiring every source to
    coordinate with every other" — which is the Mayer–Vietoris **coordination-free
    merge** / CRDT convergence (H¹ = 0) we already adopted for animating's gluing.
  So **H¹ over the social cover is the adjudication / consensus cost**: zero H¹ means
  the plurality coheres into one body coordination-free; non-zero H¹ means
  adjudication (consensus) is required to form one state of the record.
- the organization is itself "settled below, open above" and **re-seeds the next
  scale** (institution → federation) — the spiral continues.

## Relation to managing-operations (the principled home)

`managing-operations` already implements the operations chain (task → work → assign →
office → dispatch → consensus → federation → accounting) **on `@emsenn/animating`**,
and the 561 Group Operations Manifold is its running instance. Under this derivation,
that chain is **organizing's content**, not animating's: it is multi-agent governance
— assigning work across agents, office/consensus, federation — i.e. the settling of a
plurality into a law-bearing order. So organizing is the principled home that
managing-operations' capabilities belong to; the rewiring of the misplaced
`@emsenn/animating` operations imports toward organizing / managing-operations is the
companion migration (see the rewiring-plan note).

## What a fresh organizing must build (mirroring the family)

Derive on `relating-experimental` (+ `@emsenn/animating` for the agent base),
schema-first like animating/communicating/knowing/playing:

1. **The `organization` carrier** — the interior bulk: the global section glued from
   a sheaf of agents over a social base (`object.schema.json`, bare descent-path
   `$id`, type via the realize codegen). `glued`/H¹ is the adjudication condition.
2. **The three Gen-2 boundary `face` carriers** — socializing/computing/gaming
   (across/inside/outside), each a local section over the social cover; threaded, not
   re-implemented.
3. **The gluing node** — `socialize`/`compute`/`game` faces → `organize` (the
   organization as global section), with **`adjudicate`** as the gluing act carrying
   the three disciplines (contested / open / monotone) and the consensus-cost reading
   of H¹; and `project` (the organization seen only through its three social faces —
   holographic).
4. **The spiral re-seed** — organization → next-scale (federation) seed.
5. Schema-first hygiene throughout (domain/codomain `$ref`s, wire codecs, four-faced
   modules, per-package EAVT); treat any absent old feature as the principled new way.

Precision note: organizing is **not** a re-skin of managing-operations' task/office
machinery. It is the *binder* — the sheaf-gluing of agents into a collective — of
which the operations chain is one downstream reading. Build the binder; let the
operations chain mount on it.
