The analysis of how cultures, communities, and social structures adapt when the conditions that sustained them change — through ecological crisis, economic collapse, institutional withdrawal, or civilizational decline. This is not resilience discourse (bouncing back to a prior state) but adaptation analysis (becoming something different in response to conditions that will not return to normal).
