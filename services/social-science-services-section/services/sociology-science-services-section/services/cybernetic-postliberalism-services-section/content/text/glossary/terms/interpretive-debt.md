Interpretive debt is the unmet sense-making load that outlives its spender. When a population's rate of frame-generation falls below the system's rate of frame-refutation, the events left uninterpreted do not lapse, because the system that produced them persists and still requires them legible. The shortfall is therefore designated, not lost. Following [Bataille](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/bataille1949.md), every expenditure is non-recuperable *to the spender* and borne in full by a party some institution names; expenditure without a designating institution is not an alternative to transfer but its failure mode, ruin. Interpretive debt has three components: a principal (the load shed), a designation (the institution naming who carries it), and an accrual. The accrual is what makes it debt rather than transfer. Generating a frame is priced once, per event. Maintaining a mood about a frame nobody generated is priced per subject per period, indefinitely. The bearer pays more than the spender declined to pay.

## Why this is not burnout

The strongest objection is that the structural register is redundant: population-level burnout just *is* many individuals burned out, and "debt" adds a ledger metaphor to a phenomenon [Han](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/han2015.md) already describes as self-exploitation ending in depression, with no creditor and nothing conserved. Worse, a ledger is exactly the restricted-economy bookkeeping Bataille wrote against.

The answer is operational. Burnout and fatigue are measured on the spender and terminate with the spender: recovery, exit, or death closes the account. Interpretive debt is measured on the bearer and is conserved across the spender's exit. The interpreter who goes silent leaves a maintenance cost that a care laborer ([Hochschild](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/hochschild1983.md)), a later cohort, or a summarizing machine keeps paying. No individual-register term can express that conservation, which is a property of the relation, not of either party. And Bataille's denial of recuperation concerns reinvestment by the spender; potlatch is lost to the giver *and* obligates the receiver in every case he examines. The debt reading is Bataillean at precisely the point the objection says it is not.

## Falsifiable form

If the term names something real, maintenance signal attached to an interpreter's silence should persist or rise after the interpreter exits, landing on parties who never interpreted. If the signal ends with the interpreter, the account was burnout, the sink was dissipation, and this entry should be deleted. The fuller argument, including why the affective register is the system's preferred sink, is in [Interpretive debt and "the load is the system"](../../documents/articles/notes/interpretive-debt-and-the-load-is-the-system.md).
