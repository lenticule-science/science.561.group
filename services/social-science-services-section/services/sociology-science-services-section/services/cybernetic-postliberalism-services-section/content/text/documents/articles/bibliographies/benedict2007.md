## Why this is central

The note's closing section (§4) sets Han's 2024 reach for a hope "free of optimism" that "unites people and forms communities" against an ecclesial answer already specified to "which community, meeting when, doing what, obligated by what, carried how." *Spe Salvi* — Benedict XVI's 2007 encyclical on Christian hope, whose Latin title supplies the theme the note's final section trades on — is the note's implicit theological anchor for that answer.

## What it does not settle

The body does not quote or analyze *Spe Salvi* directly anywhere in its prose; the encyclical appears only in the note's frontmatter `references:` map and its closing "## References" list, not in argued text. Its role in the note is therefore thematic and implicit rather than developed: readers should not take this carrier's "why central" claim as reporting textual engagement the note itself performs, only as identifying the real, unambiguous document the citekey names and the theme (hope, as the Church's answer to Han's remedy) the note's closing section trades in without citing the encyclical's content explicitly.
