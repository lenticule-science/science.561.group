The [overview](./overview.md) says the framework "integrates" three levels and stops there. Integration is not a mechanism. This note proposes one: the levels are stages in a discharge pipeline, ordered by what each requires as input, and closed by a return path. [Cybernetic governance](./cybernetic-governance.md) generates the load. [Fascist grammar](../../../glossary/terms/fascist-grammar.md) discharges load to a mass public when explanation is politically required. [Californication](../../../glossary/terms/californication.md) absorbs the residue neither containment nor narrative discharged. The residue re-enters as fresh load. Without that last step the three are a pipe with a sink; with it, the system is recursive in the sense [recursive governance](../../../glossary/terms/recursive-governance.md) asserts but does not derive.

## Ordering by input dependency

The ordering is fixed not by history or importance but by an asymmetry in inputs. Governance-level loops, in [Beer's](../bibliographies/beer1972.md) sense, produce [structural contradiction](../../../glossary/terms/structural-contradiction.md) whether or not anyone narrates it: a market falls on a tariff before a single pundit speaks. Grammar cannot do this. Its first step declares crisis, and a declaration with no load behind it is unheard; a candidate announcing existential threat during a boom is a crank. Californication likewise requires input: a subject cannot be responsibilized for a contradiction that has not reached them as felt strain. Governance needs neither of the others to produce its output; each of the others needs load to produce theirs. That makes governance upstream.

Grammar precedes californication for a cost reason. Narrative discharge is cheap per unit of load, since one speech reaches millions, but it is politically gated: it requires an actor who must explain, which happens only when the technical loop's own accounting stops legitimating. The fascist-grammar entry already records this gate in the eurozone case, where cost-talk became sacrifice-talk when cost stopped working. Californication has no gate. It is always available because it is performed per subject, in the restricted economy of feeling the [general economy of affect](../../../glossary/terms/general-economy-of-affect.md) entry describes. It is expensive, distributed, and constant, which is exactly the profile of a residual channel: it takes whatever the cheaper, gated channel did not.

## The strongest objection: the order is reversed

The corpus's own genealogies point the other way. Californication begins in 1769; fascist grammar is perfected by the 1930s; cybernetic governance is a post-1945 artifact of the Palo Alto complex. Historical priority suggests californication makes the self-managing subject, grammar supplies that subject's narrative habits, and only then can governance run loops on interpretive labor. Sharper still: the tariff was announced inside fascist grammar (deficit as humiliation, China as corrupt counterpart, "short-term pain" as sacrifice). Grammar did not explain the load; it generated it. On this reading the pipeline runs californication → grammar → governance.

The answer separates preconditions from throughput. That the self-managing subject and the grammatical repertoire had to exist before the loop could run is true and irrelevant to the direction of flow within a cycle: a thermostat's manufacture precedes its operation without the factory being part of the control loop. The tariff case shows this once the timing is read carefully. The grammar in the announcement discharged nothing: the load arrived from the market reaction and the reversals, which the announcing grammar had not anticipated and could not format. The grammar was re-issued after the reversal ("they are calling to make deals"), and that second issue is the explanatory deployment, timed to load, not ahead of it. The announcement was a governance act that happened to be phrased grammatically; its phrasing was inert until there was strain to point at. Grammar can accompany the generation of load but cannot substitute for it. The asymmetry survives.

## Why the residue returns

Californication does not destroy what it absorbs. It converts contradiction into content and into exhaustion, and both re-enter. Content re-enters as signal: the therapeutic post, the resilience thread, the healing arc are objects other interpreters must parse, and under [interpretive saturation](../../../glossary/terms/interpretive-saturation.md) parsing is the scarce resource. Exhaustion re-enters as reduced capacity: [Hochschild's](../bibliographies/hochschild1983.md) depleted emotional laborer meets the next cycle with less, so the same load lands as more. This is the mechanism the overview's interpretive-debt problem lacks. The sink it asks for is californication, and the sink leaks on both sides. Recursion is then not a metaphor for "everything connects" but a claim that the third stage's output is measurable at the first stage's input.

## Where the model breaks

The general claim that all three levels exist survives almost any observation. The ordering does not. It fails under three findings.

First, if crisis-declaration density in official speech systematically leads measurable load (reversal frequency, volatility) rather than lags it across cycles, grammar is generative and governance is downstream. The model predicts a positive lag: speech follows strain.

Second, if personal-register framing of a specific contradiction (therapeutic, resilience, self-care discourse about it) rises while the narrative frame for it still holds, californication is primary, not residual. The model predicts personal-register uptake climbs as frames fail, which the californication entry's frame-burn sequence implies but has not stated as a lag.

Third, if the populations that most intensively californicate a contradiction subsequently show less interpretive load about it, the residue is dissipated, not recycled, and the system is three parts with a working sink. The model predicts the opposite: heavy personal-register absorption is followed by more parsing, because the absorbed material comes back as content.

Any one of these reverses or cuts the pipeline. If none is observed, the levels are integrated as the overview claims, and this note has supplied the direction.
