## Why this is central

The note states: "In *The Spirit of Hope* (2024), Han reaches for a remedy: hope that is 'free of optimism,' that 'fully acknowledges possible catastrophe,' that 'unites people and forms communities.'" This is the note's pivot into its closing argument (§4, §6) that Han's own proposed remedy for burnout names a structure — community, obligation, relational hope — that the note claims the Church "already fills, specified to the hour, the room, and the practice."

## What it does not settle

The note quotes three short phrases from *The Spirit of Hope* without engaging the book's own argument beyond them, or Han's wider corpus on narration, boredom, and contemplative life that the book's account of hope is developed against. The note's claim that Han "has nothing for the last two rows" of its Measurement table (§5) is the note's own diagnostic reading of what it takes to be missing from Han's remedy — not a limitation Han states about his own book.
