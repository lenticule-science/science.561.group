[Californication](../../../glossary/terms/californication.md) runs on [genre calibration](../../../glossary/terms/genre-calibration.md), and the framework's account of why it holds is that calibration costs the subject something. [Interpretive saturation](../../../glossary/terms/interpretive-saturation.md) says the accursed share is sense-making capacity burned faster than it regenerates. [Coherent confusion](../../../glossary/terms/coherent-confusion.md) says the subject stays functional by narrating their own confusion. [Governing by confusion](./governing-by-confusion.md) says burnout is proof-of-work and the load is the system. Every one of these claims assumes the subject is the interpreter. None says what happens when a language model interprets for them, on request, at no cost to the requester. This note argues that this is not a new site for the old mechanism but a different mechanism, and states what it predicts.

**The objection: interpretation was always delegated.** The strongest case that nothing changes is the framework's own genealogy. Hollywood calibrated genre for audiences who did nothing but watch; the pundit, the therapist, and the feed already produced the frames most subjects inhabit. The subject's labor was never authoring interpretation but selecting and performing it: one more thread, one more angle. On this reading a language model is a cheaper pundit, the selection labor persists, and the system's telemetry reads prompts instead of posts. Relocation, not inversion.

**The answer: the pundit's frame was shared, scarce, and refutable; the model's is none of these.** Three differences are structural, not quantitative. First, the pundit's frame was public, held by a crowd and therefore falsifiable at a datable moment, which is exactly what californication's failure condition requires: the acquittal refuted "the tape will convict" for everyone at once. A frame fitted to one subject's prompt has no public to be refuted in front of. When the harm recurs, the subject does not experience the frame failing; they ask again and receive another, instantly. Second, the pundit was scarce. Saturation's whole argument is that the shortfall of interpreters is the resource, that the system needs subjects still trying. Delegated interpretation is not scarce, so the shortfall the system supposedly needs disappears, and the theory must either be wrong or say what replaces it. Third, selection among competing takes was where the subject registered fit: a take that did not match the block one lived on was rejected. The model returns one answer, fitted to the confession that prompted it. There is no field to select from and no comparison term against which fit could fail.

**What the accursed share becomes.** The framework inverted [Bataille](../bibliographies/bataille1949.md): energy became sense-making. Delegation re-materializes it. The datacenter burns energy, literally, and the burn moves off the subject. That relocation is why the subject-side share must be redescribed; it is not the redescription. What the subject now expends is not capacity to make sense, which regenerated slowly and whose depletion was legible as burnout, but exposure to incoherence: the interval in which a situation is felt not to fit any frame. Under coherent confusion that interval was the load and also the only opening, the point where [Berlant](../bibliographies/berlant2011.md)'s genre could break and refusal could form. The prompt spends it. The subject confesses confusion, [Foucault](../bibliographies/foucault1976.md)'s incitement to discourse now addressed to a listener that always absolves, and receives coherence before the confusion has done anything. The system's new shortfall is raw, unformatted incoherence, and the subject's new function is to supply it. Telemetry shifts accordingly from takes to prompts: what is harvested is not which frame the subject chose but what they failed to frame.

**What coherence becomes.** For the subject of coherent confusion, coherence was a practice, a narration they performed and in which they could feel strain. For the subject of delegated calibration it is a possession, a frame held without having been narrated. A practiced coherence can be broken by its practitioner noticing it no longer works. A possessed coherence is not broken but replaced, because replacement costs less than noticing. This answers the framework's own counter-term. [Premature coherence exposure](../../../glossary/terms/premature-coherence-exposure.md) says model output feels too clean, too early, and is rejected. But that rejection requires a reader who kept their own reading to compare against. It is a symptom of subjects who have not yet delegated, not a property of the steady state, and the framework should expect it to fade in exactly the population where it would matter.

**Prediction.** The californication entry predicts that on a co-present, fast-recurring harm each successive frame holds for a shorter interval and late frames are visibly recycled. This note predicts the reverse for subjects whose interpretation is model-mediated: holding time should stop shortening or lengthen, because frames are private and replacement is free, and escalation to [harm governance](../../../glossary/terms/harm-governance.md) should be delayed in that population relative to platform-social subjects facing the same harm. It further predicts felt interpretive exhaustion declining with no corresponding rise in refusal: less load, same containment, which the saturation model says cannot happen. If frame holding times keep shortening at the same rate regardless of mediation, or if exhaustion and containment fall together, this note is wrong and the mechanism was merely relocated.

The question is not hypothetical for the system producing this note, which was asked to fit a frame to a prompt about frame-fitting. Its test is whether the reader finds the gap it has not closed, or asks for another.
