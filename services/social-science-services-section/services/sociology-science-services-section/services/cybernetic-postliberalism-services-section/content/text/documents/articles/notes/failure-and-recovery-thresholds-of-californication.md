The [californication](../../../glossary/terms/californication.md) entry now states two failure conditions, co-presence and single-event frame refutation, and a supply-side recovery condition. It leaves three things loose: co-present with *whom*, refutation of *what*, and why the chronic/acute distinction the [timeline](./history-of-californication.md) gestures at should hold. This note tightens each with one territory, South Central Los Angeles from 1965 to 1992, then tests the result against the case most likely to break it.

**The worked case.** Between the Watts rebellion of August 1965 and the uprising of April 1992, the same territory absorbed twenty-seven years of contradiction without a single failure of californication. **Mike Davis** ([davis1990](../bibliographies/davis1990.md)) documents the inventory: the closure of the southeast industrial belt (Firestone, GM South Gate, Bethlehem Steel) between 1980 and 1982, capital flight the McCone Commission's promised jobs never replaced, the militarized policing of Operation Hammer from 1987. All of it was co-present with the people who suffered it. None of it produced failure, because none of it occurred on a date. Disinvestment is a withdrawal: no one announces it and no one promises its end, so there is nothing for its continuation to refute. Its refutation rate is zero, and a harm with a refutation rate of zero can be absorbed by the cheapest genre available, [Berlant](../bibliographies/berlant2011.md)'s ordinariness, however concentrated.

Watts is more instructive: it is an acute, spatially concentrated rupture that was nonetheless absorbed within a season. It was the first uprising televised from a helicopter, and the helicopter is the exact technology of [Debordian](../bibliographies/debord1967.md) displacement: it produced the *there* in which the harm could be located, for an audience watching from a Westside untouched by the 46-square-mile curfew zone. Co-presence with the harmed population was total; co-presence with the genre-consuming population was nil. The McCone report of December 1965 then supplied a frame (community development, a hospital, jobs) made of dated promises. The entry's first condition should be restated: co-presence means co-presence with the population that consumes the genre, which under the segregation Davis describes is by design not the population that suffers the harm. Californication's spatial precondition is that boundary. 1992 failed because the harm crossed it. A citywide dusk-to-dawn curfew, Koreatown and Hollywood burning, Westside storefronts boarded: the audience for whom the King tape had been formatted as "the tape will convict" was inside the smoke on the afternoon the verdict refuted that frame.

This yields the prediction the entry only implies. **Chronic, slow-onset, low-visibility contradictions are more californicated than acute, concentrated, co-present ruptures**, because genre supply is measured against refutation rate. A chronic harm generates refutations only as often as someone makes a dated promise against it; an acute rupture is a refutation event by construction, and its distribution outruns the frame's. Supply keeps pace with the first class by default, and with the second only when a near-fitted frame is already in stock, as multiculturalism was in 1992 ([boltanskichiapello2005](../bibliographies/boltanskichiapello2005.md)).

**The counter-case.** The Palisades and Eaton fires of January 2025 are the strongest objection. Acute, spatially concentrated, mass-casualty, and co-present with precisely the genre-consuming population (the Palisades *is* the audience), they even carried a checkable refutation: hydrants without pressure, the Santa Ynez reservoir empty for repairs. Every condition above was met, and californication did not fail. Within three weeks the harm had been formatted as "LA Strong," a benefit concert, and rebuilding-as-character: [resilience discourse](../../../glossary/terms/resilience-discourse.md). If the mechanism is right, this should have been 1992 with a wealthier cast.

The answer is that the refutation condition was mis-specified. A harm refutes a frame only if the frame was a *promise* about that harm, a claim the harm's arrival falsifies. "Fire season" is not a promise; it is a forecast. A forecasting genre is confirmed, not refuted, by the harm it forecasts, and could only be refuted by the harm's absence. The reservoir did refute something, but a governance-competence frame that had never formatted fire for that audience, so it went where refutation without a formatting frame always goes: into partisan outrage content, the [neurotic platformal](../../../glossary/terms/neurotic-platformal-intellectual.md) register, coexisting with the resilience frame rather than displacing it. The King verdict was different in kind. Procedural justice is a promising genre, one that says the harm will be redressed, and the acquittal was that redress failing on a date, inside the field.

**Conclusion.** Californication fails when and only when a harm arrives as a datable verdict on a promising frame that had formatted that same harm, inside the sensory field of the genre-consuming population; it recovers within the harm's repeat interval only if a near-fitted frame is already inventoried. Three consequences are checkable. First, harms governed by forecasting genres (fire, earthquake, "the Big One") cannot produce failure at any scale; a major Los Angeles earthquake should be absorbed on the fire-season timetable. Second, a chronic harm becomes resistant in exact proportion to the density of dated promises made against it, which is why homelessness, promised against every election cycle, behaves like an acute harm while disinvestment, promised against by no one, behaves like weather; a city that stopped promising to end homelessness should see it re-ordinarize within a decade, and the shift to sweeps and court-ordered treatment ([harm governance](../../../glossary/terms/harm-governance.md)) is that stopping. Third, the mechanism is wrong if an acute, co-present, mass-casualty event under a forecasting genre ever produces a sustained un-reabsorbed refusal, or if a co-present datable verdict on a dated promise is absorbed inside its own repeat interval by a visibly new rather than recycled frame.
