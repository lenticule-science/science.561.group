[Overview](./overview.md) states the framework as three integrated levels; [Cybernetic Postliberalism](../../../glossary/topics/cybernetic-postliberalism.md) states which of the evidence for them is portable and which is Californian décor. This note maps the corpus's full bibliography and its twelve newest terms onto those three levels, sorted by which operation a source grounds, not by its author or decade: Foucault is filed at governmentality, not at "1970s France." A handful of entries could plausibly sit one level over; that seam is named at the end, not hidden.

## Level 1: Cybernetic governance

This is the formal machinery: feedback without a controller, and the claim that "the load is the system." [Wiener 1950](../bibliographies/wiener1950.md) and [Beer 1972](../bibliographies/beer1972.md) supply the thermostat and the Viable System Model the level's own lesson borrows without paraphrase; [Luhmann 1984](../bibliographies/luhmann1984.md) sharpens Wiener's shared-signal feedback into operational closure — why an uncoded agent is excluded outright, not merely regulated badly; [Beniger 1986](../bibliographies/beniger1986.md) supplies the missing century of railroad dispatchers and statistical bureaus between Wiener's engineering and any platform. [Bataille 1949](../bibliographies/bataille1949.md)'s general economy, inverted by [Interpretive Saturation](../../../glossary/terms/interpretive-saturation.md) and [General Economy of Affect](../../../glossary/terms/general-economy-of-affect.md), turns a surplus that must be burned into a shortfall that gets burned anyway: sense-making consumed faster than a finite population can produce it. [Esposito 2002](../bibliographies/esposito2002.md)'s immunization (a controlled dose of the threat, incorporated to regulate against it) and [Caillois 1958](../bibliographies/caillois1958.md)'s ludus–paidia spectrum (how tightly an activity's own rules contain its disturbance) are homeostasis under other names; [Kelly 1956](../bibliographies/kelly1956.md) and [Peters 2019](../bibliographies/peters2019.md) supply the mathematics behind the level's sharpest claim — a system optimized over time rather than over an ensemble can be stable in aggregate while every trajectory inside it is ruined. [Viable-System Subject](../../../glossary/terms/viable-system-subject.md) is where the level stops being institutional: Beer's five nested subsystems, run on a self with no System 5 standing outside the loop to set its terms.

A feedback loop does not need to resolve anything to keep running; it needs only to keep converting disturbance into more feedback.

## Level 2: Postliberal grammar

This is the six-step sequence and the hegemonic machinery that naturalizes it into common sense. [Gramsci 1971](../bibliographies/gramsci1971.md) grounds the naturalization itself — a grammar that has stopped registering as a choice. [Deneen 2018](../bibliographies/deneen2018.md) and [Vermeule 2022](../bibliographies/vermeule2022.md) are the sequence's own postliberal output, crisis-community-enemy-destiny run by writers who would reject the label; [Hall 1979](../bibliographies/hall1979.md), read through [Authoritarian Populism](../../../glossary/terms/authoritarian-populism.md), is the same five steps rerouted through an election instead of a coup. [Slobodian 2018](../bibliographies/slobodian2018.md) gives the sequence a legal mechanism — encasing markets above a demos that might vote to redistribute — and [Cooper 2017](../bibliographies/cooper2017.md) shows that mechanism was never separable from the family-values case Deneen makes: one apparatus, two vocabularies. [Boltanski & Chiapello 2005](../bibliographies/boltanskichiapello2005.md), read through [New Spirit of Capitalism](../../../glossary/terms/new-spirit-of-capitalism.md), and [Mirowski 2013](../bibliographies/mirowski2013.md), read through [Thought Collective](../../../glossary/terms/thought-collective.md), belong here rather than at Level 3 because both describe absorption at the level of ideology and institution — critique metabolized into a new justifying spirit, doctrine outliving the collective that carries it — not absorption at the level of felt affect. [Freire 1970](../bibliographies/freire1970.md), [Fanon 1961](../bibliographies/fanon1961.md), and [Santos 2014](../bibliographies/santos2014.md) supply the grammar's underside: the oppressed reproducing the oppressor's grammar, aggression rerouted sideways at a reachable enemy instead of upward at its source, epistemicide as what naturalization does once it wins completely.

The grammar does not need to be sincere to run; it only needs to be available.

## Level 3: Californication

This is everywhere the other two levels get felt rather than argued. [Davis 1990](../bibliographies/davis1990.md), [Turner 2006](../bibliographies/turner2006.md), and [Harris 2023](../bibliographies/harris2023.md) supply Los Angeles's and Palo Alto's actual institutional histories, converted by [Palo Alto Complex](../../../glossary/terms/palo-alto-complex.md) and [Californian Ideology](../../../glossary/terms/californian-ideology.md) — the latter grounded in [Barbrook & Cameron 1996](../bibliographies/barbrook1996.md) — from décor into the mechanism's load-bearing joint and its most euphoric local belief. Its affective machinery runs on [Debord 1967](../bibliographies/debord1967.md) (spectacular diffusion), [Chun 2016](../bibliographies/chun2016.md) and [van Dijck 2013](../bibliographies/vandijck2013.md) (platformal process, synthesized against [Zuboff 2019](../bibliographies/zuboff2019.md)'s extraction in [Surveillance Intimacy](../../../glossary/terms/surveillance-intimacy.md)), [Hochschild 1983](../bibliographies/hochschild1983.md) and [Illouz 2007](../bibliographies/illouz2007.md) (feeling rules fused into a tradeable [Emotional Capitalism](../../../glossary/terms/emotional-capitalism.md)), [Rose 1999](../bibliographies/rose1999.md) and [Brown 2015](../bibliographies/brown2015.md) (responsibilization, *homo oeconomicus*), [Marcuse 1964](../bibliographies/marcuse1964.md) (read as [Repressive Desublimation](../../../glossary/terms/repressive-desublimation.md)), and [Fisher 2009](../bibliographies/fisher2009.md) with [Jameson 1991](../bibliographies/jameson1991.md) (capitalist realism and genre-compliant depthlessness as the affect this level manages). [Povinelli 2011](../bibliographies/povinelli2011.md) and [Berlant 2011](../bibliographies/berlant2011.md) supply the governing claim that endurance, not resolution, is what late liberalism delivers; [Foucault 1976](../bibliographies/foucault1976.md) supplies the confession every californicated subject keeps performing. [Resilience Discourse](../../../glossary/terms/resilience-discourse.md) names its current, 2020s instrument.

A longer theological line sits underneath, uncredited until now: [Aquinas 1274](../bibliographies/aquinas1274.md), [Calvin 1559](../bibliographies/calvin1559.md), [Luther 1520](../bibliographies/luther1520.md), and [Weber 1905](../bibliographies/weber1905.md) trace one continuous history of anxious, unverifiable self-auditing — acedia, predestination, calling, the Protestant ethic — that the californicated subject inherits as its default posture toward its own interior. [Han 2015](../bibliographies/han2015.md) and [Han 2024](../bibliographies/han2024.md) are this line's most recent installment, exhaustion followed by an individualized hope, with [Benedict 2007](../bibliographies/benedict2007.md) the one entry here arguing against the level rather than for it: a communal hope specified to the hour, the room, and the practice, against Han's unspecified one. [Tuck & Yang 2012](../bibliographies/tuck2012.md), [James 2016](../bibliographies/james2016.md), and [Césaire 1955](../bibliographies/cesaire1955.md) round out the cluster — settler moves to innocence, the captive maternal, the administrator brutalized by the violence they administer — three names for labor or narrative that relieves a subject of structural complicity without ever touching the structure.

Every mechanism on this level does the same thing to different material: it takes what would register as structural and hands it back as something to feel, manage, or narrate instead.

## The seam

This is not a tidy partition. [Foucault 1976](../bibliographies/foucault1976.md) grounds governmentality at Level 3 while the naturalization Level 2 borrows from Gramsci leans on the same late lecture courses; [Bataille 1949](../bibliographies/bataille1949.md)'s accursed share could as easily be filed under the affect it eventually produces as under the governance mechanism it explains here. The map holds anyway, because each entry above sits at the level whose specific claim it does the most direct work to ground, and because a map that could not be redrawn from another angle would be describing something other than a system built from feedback loops.
