## Why this is central

Freire's account of the oppressed becoming sub-oppressors — internalizing the oppressor's model of what it is to be human, and reproducing oppression given any position of relative power — supplies the note's mechanism for why moral policing, once it starts, tends to reproduce rather than interrupt the structure that produced the underlying harm: the institutional worker exercises power over the bootstrapper the way the oppressor exercised power over them.

## What it does not settle

Freire's book is a work of pedagogy and dialogical method, concerned with literacy, conscientization, and the banking model of education; it does not address digital platforms, AI tools, or language-preservation technology, and Freire treats sub-oppression as a structural tendency that deliberate praxis (dialogue, conscientization) can interrupt — not as an automatic or involuntary "reflex." Where the note or casual usage frames sub-oppression as reflexive, that framing risks understating Freire's own emphasis on it as a tendency requiring conscious work to break, not an inevitability given oppression alone.
