## Why this is central

Illouz's coined term "emotional capitalism" names a two-way traffic the corpus already assumes but has never sourced: economic relations are increasingly conducted in an emotional and therapeutic idiom (the "emotionally competent" manager, the well-regulated worker), while intimate and emotional life is increasingly conducted in the idiom of economic rationality (the calculating, comparison-shopping search for a partner, the self as a portfolio of "emotional capital" to be managed and optimized). This is the uncited theoretical grounding for [structural-contradiction.md](../../../glossary/terms/structural-contradiction.md)'s claim that californication "reformats" structural contradiction "as personal struggle, as therapeutic narrative, as self-improvement," and for [genre-calibration.md](../../../glossary/terms/genre-calibration.md)'s example of job loss becoming a self-help "pivot" — both describe, without naming it, the same fusion of market and therapeutic discourse Illouz's book is about. It is also the direct source the note [californication-and-affect.md](../notes/californication-and-affect.md) needs for its claim that responsibilization "operates affectively," making a subject accountable not only for their economic survival but for the correct emotional management of that survival.

## What it does not settle

*Cold Intimacies* is built mainly from case material internal to psychology's own professionalization (the rise of the therapeutic ethos inside early-to-mid twentieth-century American management and self-help culture) and, in its later chapters, from the rationalized courtship of early internet dating; it is not a study of California, Esalen, wellness platforms, or any of the specific genealogy [zen-fascism.md](../../../glossary/terms/zen-fascism.md) traces, and Illouz does not use the corpus's own vocabulary of casting, telemetric signals, or the savior-slave subject. The book also stops short of a verdict on whether "emotional capitalism" is a stable, permanent fusion or a historically specific and reversible one — it diagnoses the condition's mechanics without settling whether or how it ends.
