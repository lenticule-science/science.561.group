The [Boundary conditions](../../../glossary/topics/cybernetic-postliberalism.md) section reduced californication to three portable conditions — an exhausted legitimacy claim whose institutions persist, an interpretive apparatus whose capacity is what dissent consumes, and a local alibi coding processing as participation — and one diagnostic: does dissent extract concessions or consume interpretation? It declared the mechanism portable without running it; run on three cases, it is portable but misdescribed. What travels is narrower than affective governance: *intake governance*, in which the system solicits the subject's report on their own condition and terminates it inside the apparatus that solicited it. Californication is the variant in which the subject writes the report as a story of transformation.

**The EU.** The legitimacy claim was output legitimacy in **Fritz Scharpf**'s sense — peace and prosperity in exchange for a democratic deficit — and it was spent between 2010 and 2015 while every institution persisted. The clean test is Greece: 61 percent voted *OXI* on 5 July 2015 and the third memorandum was signed on 13 July with no term improved. Dissent extracted nothing; it consumed a decade of interpretation about betrayal and game theory. The Conference on the Future of Europe (2021–22) ran citizens' panels to 49 proposals and no treaty change; the Recovery and Resilience Facility disburses 723 billion euros against "milestones and targets," making resilience the *name of the money* and the member state the responsibilized subject, with the citizen beneath it as consultee. Nothing Californian transfers: the destiny-claim is "ever closer union," a horizon that recedes by design rather than a frontier that is crossed; images are imported; the European subject is not transformed but *asked*. The register is composure, the alibi is procedure — being consulted is participation.

**The Gulf.** The UAE appointed a Minister of State for Happiness in 2016 and put a Happiness Meter on government touchpoints; Saudi Vision 2030 carries a Quality of Life Program. The apparatus is complete and the mechanism is not running. Legitimacy is allocative and unexhausted: the 2011 response to regional unrest was roughly 130 billion dollars of Saudi social spending plus prosecutions — dissent paid and policed, never interpreted. By the corpus's own diagnostic it is a non-instance, like China. It yields the first finding: the alibi was built *before* exhaustion, for a population — in Dubai, near ninety percent non-citizen — with no political claim at all. The solicited report is customer satisfaction, not testimony; the subject is a guest. No frontier transfers, since there is no ownership horizon and exit is deportation; no Hollywood, since Dubai is a set rather than a studio; no psychedelic self, since wellness here is maintenance, not dissolution. The three conditions are therefore not co-emergent: the alibi is a stock, accumulable in advance.

**Korea.** The developmental bargain — growth for discipline — is spent while the chaebol and the examination persist. "Hell Joseon" (2015) and the N-po generation were absorbed as content; the state-cultivated culture industry supplied "healing" as a genre from about 2012, and *hallyu* itself codes participation as national success. The register is [Han 2015](../bibliographies/han2015.md)'s exhaustion; self-cultivation is exam-shaped and disciplinary, so no transformative self transfers. Korea also exposes the diagnostic's blind spot. Fertility reached 0.72 in 2023 after some 380 trillion won in incentives since 2006 — concessions at scale that failed, because the withdrawal was never addressed to the system as a demand. The concessions-or-interpretation dichotomy assumes dissent files a report. Exit files none.

**The objection**: intake is not a mechanism, it is administration as such. [Beniger 1986](../bibliographies/beniger1986.md) shows the control revolution *is* the multiplication of feedback channels — census, complaint desk, petition box — so a definition built on solicited reports covers the Qing petition system and the 1880s railroad and has no edge. And the Gulf finding cuts the other way: if the alibi exists without exhaustion, exhaustion is not a condition, and the "post" in postliberalism is décor.

**The answer** is that intake governance is defined by *where the report terminates*, not by the existence of a channel. Beniger's channels route to a control action: the railroad reroutes the train. The Happiness Meter routes to a dashboard whose consumer is the dashboard; the Conference's proposals route to a report on the proposals; the RRF's milestones route to milestone verification. The test: follow one report and record whether it ends in redress or in a document about reports. Exhaustion is a condition of the mechanism *running*, not of the apparatus *existing*. The Gulf loop is dormant, and the prediction is that when the rentier bargain fails, the same instrument will be repurposed without redesign and the diagnostic will flip from paid to interpreted.

**Definition.** Intake governance answers a structural contradiction by soliciting the subject's report on their condition and terminating that report in the soliciting apparatus, so that dissent costs interpretive capacity rather than redress. Its variants sort by who authors the report and in what register: self-authored and euphoric (California), procedural and composed (the EU), transactional and satisfied (the Gulf, dormant), curative and exhausted (Korea). Californication is the variant in which the subject volunteers the intake as autobiography, which is why it alone feels like freedom. Two falsifiers: a case where intake output is routed to redress at scale — a referendum altering a memorandum — reclassifies that case as hegemonic; and a non-reporting exit durably absorbed by any register would show the mechanism does not need the report, and the definition is wrong.
