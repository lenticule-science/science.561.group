# Sociology Science Services Section

The Sociology Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for the
study of social structure, association, and collective life: networks
and their topology, ritual and ceremony, social capital, commons
governance, and the empirics of community formation and dissolution.

Critical theory, organization, and work research are narrower fields under
this section. Sociology serves as the empirical proving ground for their
structural claims —
most immediately the coordination-cohomology predictions of the
2026-08-23 spirals note (ceremony fraction, enclosure excess loss,
ritual as load-bearing social technology), whose audit against the
network-science and ritual literatures lives here.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

[`work-science-services-section/`](services/work-science-services-section/README.md)
holds the work, governance, gig-work, and formal work research program.

## Client research: settler social reproduction (17 October 2026)

This externally commissioned research examines whether an imagined future of legitimate white reproduction can be preferred to the welfare and generational continuity of living white-classified people, while settler property and sovereignty are reproduced through migration, captive births and classification. **The central causal theory remains unproven.** The historical sources, publication drafts, primary evidence, contradictions and receiving requirements are held separately rather than compressed into the exploratory note.

**Journal-style manuscripts:** [The Future White](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-manuscript-selective-futurity-and-settler-continuity.md) (historical/critical sociology) and [Birth into Captivity](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-manuscript-captive-births-and-settler-reproduction.md) (demographic reconstruction). A third independent legal-history manuscript, [Whose Future Increase?](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-manuscript-reproductive-futures-as-property.md), examines primary court rulings, slaveholder correspondence, juristic nonuniformity and an actual freedom petition. All three are working drafts, not peer-reviewed papers.

**PDF editions actually stored in this repository:** [The Future White PDF](content/pdf/documents/articles/working-papers/2026/10/17/the-future-white-source-edition.pdf) and [Birth into Captivity PDF](content/pdf/documents/articles/working-papers/2026/10/17/birth-into-captivity-source-edition.pdf). ## Research working papers

[Reference Without Deference: Radical Political Learning, Anarchist Praxis,
and the Nihilist–Insurrectionary Horizon](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/15/2026-10-15-note-on-reference-without-deference-and-radical-political-learning.md)
(version 3.0, 15 October 2026) examines problem-relative political learning
and selective borrowing without assuming ideological succession.
Its [documentary evidence register](data/records/radical-political-learning-evidence-2026-10-15.json)
contains fourteen source-located records from ten purposively selected receiving
documents. The third round adds cross-setting DSA references and XR's declared
Holacracy source, current scholarly comparisons, normalized outcome fields,
an observation-bias counterexample, and a better-matched experimental proposal.
Textual use, reported learning, posted rules, reported adoption, implemented
practice, and deference remain distinct. This is AI-assisted conceptual and
documentary research, not a population estimate, executed experiment,
independently coded field study, or peer-reviewed publication.

[White increase and the self-consuming reproductive ideal](content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md) (2026-10-17) is a **working, unassayed historical-sociological hypothesis** about the conflict between eugenic purification and the reproduction of the population the racial ideal claims to preserve. It distinguishes nominal whiteness, *admissible* reproduction, settler property-power reproduction, and captive reproductive expropriation; states four falsifiable hypotheses; and links to the now-expanded original-source bibliography and evidence crosswalk alongside the existing Spillers reference. Its glossary additions include [white increase](content/text/glossary/terms/white-increase.md), [whiteness](content/text/glossary/terms/whiteness.md), [reproductive purity ratchet](content/text/glossary/terms/reproductive-purity-ratchet.md), and [race suicide](content/text/glossary/terms/race-suicide.md). The source work is committed to Science, not independently received as an empirical conclusion.
