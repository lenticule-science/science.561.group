# Sociology Science Services Section

The Sociology Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for the
study of social structure, association, and collective life: networks
and their topology, ritual and ceremony, social capital, commons
governance, and the empirics of community formation and dissolution.

Critical theory, organization, and work research are narrower fields under
this section. Sociology serves as the empirical proving ground for their
structural claims —
most immediately the coordination-cohomology predictions of the
2026-08-23 spirals note (ceremony fraction, enclosure excess loss,
ritual as load-bearing social technology), whose audit against the
network-science and ritual literatures lives here.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.
