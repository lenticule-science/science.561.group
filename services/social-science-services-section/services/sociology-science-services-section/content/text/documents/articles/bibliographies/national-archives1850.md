# U.S. National Archives and Records Administration (1850) — Seventh Census: Schedule 1 Free Inhabitants and Schedule 2 Slave Inhabitants (instructions and surviving schedules)

**Source:** https://www.archives.gov/research/census/1850
**Source class:** primary administrative and statistical source guide.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** National Archives questions and schedule fields read; representative original schedules not newly transcribed for this commission.

## Source-located evidence

Schedule 1 items 1–13 vs Schedule 2 items 1–9; see also federal 1850 enumerator instructions.

## What the source supports

Documents that free persons were named individually in 1850 while the corresponding slave schedule recorded owners' names, numbers, ages, sexes and racial categories, generally not enslaved people's names. This evidences that the census was an institutional classification apparatus, not transparent observation of undifferentiated people.

## What it does not establish

Some local or exceptional entries named enslaved individuals; do not claim no enslaved person was ever named anywhere. These are stock snapshots, not direct birth registers. They cannot recover all mothers, kin, births or coerced conceptions.

## Receiving and reuse

Paper II sources/identifiability and a future household linkage design; explicit measurement constraints on all claimed reproductive generations.
