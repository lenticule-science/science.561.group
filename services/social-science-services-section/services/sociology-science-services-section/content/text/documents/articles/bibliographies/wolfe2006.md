# Wolfe, Patrick (2006) — Settler colonialism and the elimination of the native

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/14623520601056240
**Publication:** Journal of Genocide Research 8(4): 387–409
**Review level (2026-10-17):** Publisher article abstract, citation and conceptual framing inspected.

## Annotation

Distinguishes the structural settler-colonial tendency to eliminate Indigenous territorial/political relations from genocide as a distinct event or outcome. Necessary to avoid treating settler expansion, captive labor increase and Indigenous kinship continuity as interchangeable population series.
