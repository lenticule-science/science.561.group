Anarcho-nihilism is the current within anarchism that refuses the constructive frame. Where classical anarchism (Bakunin, Kropotkin, the Spanish CNT-FAI) projects a future free society and organizes for it, and where reformist currents work toward incremental change inside existing institutions, anarcho-nihilism holds that both projects misread their own situation: any positive program is, in advance, available for recuperation by the order it claims to oppose, and the time spent constructing the future is time inside the present apparatus on the apparatus's terms.

The position's historical sources include Stirner's egoism, the late-nineteenth-century individualist anarchism, the post-war French ultra-left, and the contemporary insurrectionary tradition (the Invisible Committee, *Tiqqun*, the Italian autonomist anti-organizational current). The subdomain's terms — *anarcho-nihilism*, *desire*, *drive*, *emergent-obligation*, *ethics-of-presence*, *hope*, *jouissance*, *the-death-drive*, *tragic-realism* — show the school's affinity with psychoanalytic and ethical vocabulary that the constructive anarchist tradition has typically not engaged.

What the school produces, then, is not a program but an analytic and a practice. Analytically: a refusal of the cruel-optimism (Berlant) of attachments to political projects whose success would require giving up the only structure of desire that makes the present bearable. Practically: attack, refusal, the *ethics of presence* (acting in the moment of the act without subordinating it to a projected outcome), and the cultivation of forms of life — affinity groups, intimate relations, momentary insurrections — that do not depend on the order they refuse for their meaning.

The school is contested inside anarchism. Critics from the social-anarchist and platformist traditions argue that anarcho-nihilism abandons the constructive work without which insurrection cannot become liberation. Anarcho-nihilist responses cite the historical record of how exactly the constructive work was recuperated. The disagreement is internal to the tradition; both currents read each other carefully.

This subdomain holds the texts and concepts the school has produced. The neighboring [`insurrectionary-anarchism/`](../../../../insurrectionary-anarchism) subdomain holds the related but distinct insurrectionary current with which anarcho-nihilism overlaps without being identical.
