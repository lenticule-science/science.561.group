Open work for governance at the subdomain level. The sub-subdomain-specific questions live in each sub-subdomain. What is held here are the questions whose scope crosses them.

## Open structural questions

- **Can the Hohfeldian operations (assignment, succession, waiver, ratification, revocation, estoppel) be lifted to characterization theorems?** The terms are richly developed; the question is whether the structural relations between them are stable enough to admit theorem-grade statements. See [`hohfeldian/`](../../../../triage/theories/hohfeldian).
- **How are jurisdictional disputes analytically structured?** [`jurisdiction/`](../../../../triage/theories/jurisdiction) names the operations (invocation, declination, submission, excess, vires) but the structural question of how disputes between concurrent or overlapping jurisdictions get resolved — and what the discipline's analysis of those resolutions should look like — is open.
- **What is the relation between the formal analyses here and the empirical sociology of how governance actually operates inside organizations?** The subdomain currently develops the analytical apparatus; the empirical sociology of how the apparatus is enacted (and distorted) in real organizations is a separate strand of work.

## Where these questions are worked

Each question is partially worked in the relevant sub-subdomain. The cross-cutting home is here.

## Standing structural questions

- **What does this subdomain commit to substantively?** An earlier curation pass classified most sociology subdomains as staging-areas — collections of analytical resources that have not yet stabilized into a single research program with shared method and shared object. The first-order question for `military-command` is whether the existing material can stabilize into such a program, or whether what currently lives here is better understood as a regional collection routed through the school whose name it carries.
- **What is the relation between this subdomain's vocabulary (`terms/`) and the discipline's broader concept layer (`sociology/concepts/`)?** Where the same analytical role is occupied by a term here and a concept at the discipline level, the relation should be made explicit (via `realizes:` or `see-also:` edges).
- **Which writers and works should the subdomain cite, and which of those need notes in `sociology/references/` rather than ad-hoc inline mentions?** A future pass should harvest the ad-hoc figure-mentions in this subdomain and produce reference notes for the load-bearing ones.
- **What relations to neighboring subdomains are structural rather than incidental?** A future curation pass should make the subdomain's `see-also:` edges to its sibling subdomains explicit and bidirectional.
