## Quantitative or causal contribution

Appendix Table 5 pp.1956 ff. reconstructs biennial race-of-sterilization aggregates using North Carolina Board reports. 1946–48: 291 all, white238 Black53. 1964–66: 356 all, white124 Black228 Indigenous4. White fraction changes 81.79% to 34.83%, or –46.96 percentage points. These are shares of selected operations, not risk rates among populations.

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Research role:** Historical change in who the program targeted.

**Evidence available at its source:** Appendix Table 5 draws on successive Board reports: 1946–48 had 238 of 291 operations coded white (81.79%); 1964–66 had 124 of 356 coded white (34.83%). Thus the white *share of selected operations* dropped 46.96 percentage points as later operations concentrated more on Black clients; late endpoint can be checked against the original 1966 Board report.

**Interpretive/measurement limits:** Percent of sterilization operations by race does not measure a race-specific sterilization **risk**, because denominators of age, sex, class, institutional exposure and county welfare targeting are missing. A broad classed administrative program cannot be inferred from aggregate numbers alone.
