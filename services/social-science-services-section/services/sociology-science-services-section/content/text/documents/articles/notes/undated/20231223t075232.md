Yesterday I felt motivated to respond to a post from a local settler, that I thought was a clean representation of what I'd felt as a conservative impulse in other posts I'd seen from them.

Very quickly, the conversation shifted toward him accusing me of emotional manipulation and abuse, while denying any relationship to settler-colonialism.


What was especially frustrating is that this became the third local white cis settler man I'd tried to talk to about how their attitudes either endorsed or facilitated settlerism, and all three pulled the same move: claims of emotional manipulation that were used to re-set the boundaries of the conversation away from an interrogation of settlerism.

There's a lot of feelings this brings up, actually, beyond frustration. It's unfortunately a disappointing reveal into the local Progressive scene. It isn't lost on me that people who represent themselves as skilled and dedicated accomplices to social change will so quickly reveal they haven't internalized anything about what settler-colonialism really means... and clearly there's nothing happening around them that would make them internalize it.

Like sure, these conversations sucked. But it also sucks knowing that they're just expressions of a belief system that is being practiced, with a strong moral conviction, every day, by many folk around me. Nothing these folk said to me is anything different from what they say to themselves, in their head, and *that* is what's truly horrifying.

I want to thank the folk that reached out to ask how I was feeling after these conversations. And, I want to try and explain how I saw that conversation: a bit of a debrief for folk who could tell something gross was going on, but couldn't quite put words to it.

I'm going to relay the conversation and try to explain what I'm seeing happen in each exchange, to introduce to y'all a term that might make this sort of thing more legible: Deeply Unserious Koward, or DUK. (Or duk, or dukky, or whatever, depending, y'know.)
