## Contribution to the commissioned papers

1790–1800 average mid-decade national population 4.520 million and natural increase rate 26.49/1000; it does NOT provide a crude birth rate for that decade. Net international migration's proportion of national population growth by decade 1790–1859: 11.9%, 13.5%, 13.7%, 7.1%, 16.9%, 27.0%, 32.3%. Supports rejecting a broad claim that the nineteenth-century national population's growth was mostly international immigration.

## Provenance and interpretive limit

Growth decomposition is not a white-subpopulation or local settler cohort estimate; foreign-born parents can have locally born descendants. The missing 1790s crude birth rate is an assumed sensitivity parameter in the companion quantitative note.
