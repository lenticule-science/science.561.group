The study of human society — its institutions, structures, power relations, and the forces that produce and contest social order. The library's sociological content is organized into domains that correspond to real disciplines, schools, lineages, and topics within the field. Each domain's `_index.md` declares what kind of domain it is.

## Layout

- [`concepts/`](../../../../triage/concepts) — foundational sociological concepts (action, structure, agency, institution, role, status, network, power, social-fact, habitus, field, capital). Discipline-wide semantic anchors that schools specialize differently.
- [`concepts/methodology/`](../../../../triage/concepts/methodology) — method concepts (ethnography, comparative-historical analysis, network analysis, archival method, participant observation, case study, discourse analysis).
- [`terms/`](../../../../triage/terms) — sociology-wide lexical anchors carried over from the earlier convention (kept for back-compatibility; concept-grade entries are migrating to `concepts/`).
- [`texts/`](../../../../triage/texts) — sociology-wide essays not pinned to a single subdomain.
- [`references/`](../../../../triage/references) — annotated reference notes for cited works.
- [`theories/`](../../../../triage/theories) — subdomains. The major children are `critical-theory/` (the largest, with abolition, affect-theory, anarchism, benjaminian, biopolitics, black-radical-tradition, feminism, foucauldian, han, marxism, postliberalism, povinellian, queer, situationist), `cybernetic-postliberalism/`, `legalism/`, `ludics/`, `predicated-sociology/`, and `work/`.
  - [`predicated-sociology/`](../../../../triage/theories/predicated-sociology) — a theory of social structure, social action, and social change on the predication-categories mathematics. Formalizes Bourdieu's field theory (habitus-closure, capital-fiber-bundle, social-base), Foucauldian subject formation (interpellation morphism), and cybernetic postliberalism (californication, affect governance, coherent confusion) as specific structures on a bilattice-valued predication settling. The late-liberal social base, with its aperiodic settling bound, is the primary applied case.

## Charter alignment

Sociology in this library is not neutral. The project's stated commitments — Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty — are present substantively in the [black-radical-tradition](../../../../triage/theories/critical-theory/theories/black-radical-tradition), [abolition](../../../../triage/theories/critical-theory/theories/abolition), and [settler-colonialism](../../../../triage/theories/critical-theory/theories/povinellian/theories/settler-colonialism) subdomains, and gestured at in [indigenous-anarchism](../../../../triage/theories/critical-theory/theories/anarchism/theories/indigenous-anarchism).
