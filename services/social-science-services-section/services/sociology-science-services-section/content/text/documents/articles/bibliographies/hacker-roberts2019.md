# J. David Hacker; Evan Roberts (2019) — Fertility Decline in the United States, 1850–1930: New Evidence from Complete-Count Datasets

**Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9255892/
**Source class:** peer-reviewed historical demography.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Original published author-manuscript abstract and bibliographic details inspected; tables not independently reproduced.

## Source-located evidence

Abstract, county and complete-count census data 1850–1930.

## What the source supports

Reports long-term total fertility decline from approximately 7.0 in 1835 to 2.1 in 1935. Supplies a real demographic transition independent of purity narratives and a historical comparator for the client's broad claim that local descendants cannot reproduce sufficiently.

## What it does not establish

Total fertility change is not an estimate of demographic collapse nor the contribution of net migration; no automatic causal attribution to eugenics, whiteness or class exploitation.

## Receiving and reuse

Paper I place actual fertility timelines beside ideological promises; Paper II differentiate fertility indices, country stocks and immigration components.
