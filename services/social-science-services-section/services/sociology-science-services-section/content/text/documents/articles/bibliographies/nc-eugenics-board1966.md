**Venue:** NC State Board of Public Welfare, pp. 8–9, 20, 30.
**Original source:** https://archive.org/stream/biennialreporteug16nort/biennialreporteug16nort_djvu.txt.
**Reading status (2026-10-17):** Original 1966 administrative publication accessed in archive transcribed full text, totals independently summed. Historical labels are administrative classifications; original ink page not independently image-audited..

## Quantitative or causal contribution

Original Table 16 p30 reports cumulative July1929–June1966 sterilizations: 6,851 all; 4,218 white, 2,590 Black, 43 Other. White operations by type: vasectomy746, castration36, salpingectomy3,409, ovariectomy27. Biennium 1964–66 p9: 356 operations, 124 white, 228 Black, 4 Indigenous; 261 already parents and 298 outside state institutions, the latter from county public-welfare petitions. The latter indicators lack a race-by-parent-status cross-tab in narrative.
