# Eugenics Board of North Carolina (1966) — Biennial Report, July 1, 1964 to June 30, 1966

**Venue:** NC State Board of Public Welfare, pp. 8–9, 20, 30.
**Original source:** https://archive.org/stream/biennialreporteug16nort/biennialreporteug16nort_djvu.txt.
**Reading status (2026-10-17):** Original 1966 administrative publication accessed in archive transcribed full text, totals independently summed. Historical labels are administrative classifications; original ink page not independently image-audited..

## Quantitative or causal contribution

Original Table 16 p30 reports **operations**, not uniquely identified individuals; cumulative July1929–June1966 sterilizations: 6,851 all; 4,218 white, 2,590 Black, 43 Other. White operations by type: vasectomy746, castration36, salpingectomy3,409, ovariectomy27. Biennium 1964–66 p9: 356 operations, 124 white, 228 Black, 4 Indigenous; 261 already parents and 298 outside state institutions, the latter from county public-welfare petitions. The latter indicators lack a race-by-parent-status cross-tab in narrative.

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Research role:** Primary administrative operation count.

**Evidence available at its source:** The North Carolina Eugenics Board's cumulative Table 16 reports 6,851 operations from July 1929 through June 1966, of which 4,218 were categorized white, 2,590 Black and 43 Other. Procedure types for white-coded operations sum 746 vasectomies, 36 castrations, 3,409 salpingectomies and 27 ovariectomies. The 1964–66 biennium records 356 operations including 124 white, 228 Black and four Indigenous coded.

**Interpretive/measurement limits:** The table counts **operations**, not a deduplicated population of persons and certainly not births prevented. Aggregate entries do not establish consent. A state's coding of persons is not a stable biological ancestry measure; access to full personal records would require ethical and privacy-sensitive research design.

Evidence can be followed through the [23-claim crosswalk](bibliography-and-evidence-crosswalk-2026-10-17.md); the annotation is neither an assertion that every page of the source was read nor an independently replicated dataset.
