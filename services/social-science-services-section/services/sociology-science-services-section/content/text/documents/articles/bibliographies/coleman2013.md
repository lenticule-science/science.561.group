## Source-located evidence

Publisher introduction / book description concerning Indigenous and African American identities in Virginia.

## What the source supports

Shows that Virginia racial ‘purity’ regimes interfered with Indigenous peoples' political and familial recognition and forced historically contingent classifications. Essential corrective to using elite white ancestry exceptions as the sole interpretive lens: Indigenous sovereignty and Black-Indigenous kinship were the targets, not merely collateral consequences.

## What it does not establish

This book summary cannot establish exact outcomes for named Virginia communities without examining the pertinent chapters and Indigenous testimony. Its relevance is historical Indigenous positionality, not analogizing forced Native reclassification to elite white discomfort.
