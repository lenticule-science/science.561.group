# Stubblefield 2007

Anna Stubblefield, "Beyond the Pale": Tainted Whiteness, Cognitive Disability, and Eugenic Sterilization, *Hypatia* 22(2):162–181, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1527-2001.2007.tb00987.x.

## Annotation

Documents how alleged feeblemindedness linked poverty, disabilities, off-white ethnicity and judgments of women as signifiers of tainted whiteness. Evidence that eugenic selection excluded some persons nominally classified white from legitimate reproduction; no evidence by itself of inevitable demographic extinction.

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Why this source is essential:** Socially tainted whiteness.

**Recoverable evidence and locators:** The source's contribution is an **internal boundary**, not the outer white/nonwhite census line. Eugenic descriptions of disability, alleged feeblemindedness, poverty and gendered conduct can downgrade people who would otherwise be counted as white, rendering their reproduction stigmatized or subjected to forced sterilization. It is direct precedent for asking whether white ideology sacrifices some of its nominal constituency.

**Inferential boundary / competing evidence:** This is critical philosophical/legal analysis of classificatory violence, not a time series of white births or a biological study of hereditary defect. The phrase 'tainted' is an ideological target's vocabulary, not a scientifically valid diagnosis.
