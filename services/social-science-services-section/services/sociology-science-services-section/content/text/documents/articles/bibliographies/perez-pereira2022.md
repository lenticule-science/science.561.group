# Pérez-Pereira, Noelia; Wang, Jinliang; Quesada, Humberto; Caballero, Armando (2022) — Prediction of the minimum effective size of a population viable in the long term

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10531-022-02456-z
**Publication:** Biodiversity and Conservation 31: 2763–2780
**Review level (2026-10-17):** Publisher open-access article abstract and correction notice inspected.

## Annotation

Population-genetics simulations address effective population size, inbreeding, mutation, purging and survival. An imagined pair of final breeding founders cannot sustain a genetically diverse long-term human population by ordinary sexual reproduction. The study does NOT establish that whiteness is a biological population or supply an extinction forecast for racial categories. Publisher notes 2023 correction.
