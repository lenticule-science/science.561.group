# Kentucky Court of Appeals; court report (1823) — Banks' Administrator v. Marksberry

**Canonical citation:** 13 Ky. 275 (3 Litt. 275), 1823.
**Original or catalogued text:** https://www.courtlistener.com/c/ky/13/
**Reading grade (2026-10-17):** Original decision NOT directly read: exact reporter citation located in CourtListener table; reported reasoning checked in Alphonso 2023 pp.31–32 and cited by original Delaware Smith v. Milman 1839. This record is a **secondary-reconstructed case**, not independent primary holding.

## Case facts / exact analytical contribution

Reportedly concerned an enslaved woman called Pen and deeded rights to her future increase, treated by the Kentucky court as potentially transferable apart from her already living person. Alphonso's paper quotes the court's analogy to future profits of land and wool from sheep, and argues the court upheld prospective issue as alienable property. Smith v. Milman later cites this same reported decision as authority for a grant of the enslaved woman's increase.

**Source locator:** Alphonso 2023 printed pp.31–32 and notes 93–94; Smith v. Milman (1839) p.497; CourtListener Kentucky volume 13 index entry.
