## Case facts / exact analytical contribution

Analyzes Virginia's 1924 exception for certain elite claims of distant Indigenous ancestry and contrasts Native ancestry converted into a safely remote symbol with contemporary Indigenous political lives. It provides a direct prior account of selective inclusion and racial purity legislation, requiring the conceptual paper to cite and surpass rather than claim originality.
