# Australian Bureau of Statistics (2025) — National, state and territory population, December 2024

**Source:** https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/national-state-and-territory-population/dec-2024.

**Read status (2026-10-17):** Original primary provider text or original publisher/university abstract as specified below.

## Contribution to the client commission

Primary ABS original release read. Year ended Dec 2024: net overseas migration +340,800, natural increase +105,200, national population growth +445,900. Their ratio ~76.43% of national population growth is migration, under separately rounded terms. These are all-resident components, not white-specific or Indigenous population classifications.

## Limits

National headcounts and rounded components may not sum perfectly; revision statuses apply. Do not equate temporary arrivals or net overseas migration with settler ethno-racial incorporation.
