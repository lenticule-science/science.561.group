# U.S. Census Bureau (2024) — New Estimates Highlight Differences in Growth Between the U.S. Hispanic and Non-Hispanic Populations

**Source:** https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024/population-estimates-characteristics.html
**Source class:** primary official demographic release.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Complete official press release and defined population category figures inspected.

## Source-located evidence

Non-Hispanic white 2023 population 195 million; roughly 630,000 more deaths than births 2022–23; headcount −461,612.

## What the source supports

Illustrates a genuinely observed recent natural decrease in the specifically *non-Hispanic white* category, separate from nineteenth-century U.S. white marital fertility and from Canada/Australia's all-resident migration shares.

## What it does not establish

Natural decrease over one year is not biological extinction, not a global white classification, and not evidence that eugenic institutions caused it. Immigration and reclassification can affect category totals separately from births and deaths.

## Receiving and reuse

Comparative temporal test with explicit category, year and jurisdiction boundaries; not a racialized inference about all national migrants.
