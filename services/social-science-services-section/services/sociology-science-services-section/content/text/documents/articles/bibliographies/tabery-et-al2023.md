## Quantitative or causal contribution

Table1 reconstructs 830 sterilization cases in Utah (1925–1974), known non-Hispanic white665, other recorded identities15, race missing150. Minimum counted white share 80.12% of cases; 97.79% of cases with race known, not missing-at-random. Authors note 1961 substitution of an 'unfit to parent' justification for earlier hereditarian sterilization rationale. The modification is evidence of adaptive institutional authority, not a historical proof of white-population decline.
