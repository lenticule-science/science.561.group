# Michael R. Haines (1994) — The Population of the United States, 1790–1920

**Publication:** NBER Historical Working Paper 56. 
**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3386/h0056.
**Read status (2026-10-17):** Original NBER publisher summary and demographic overview inspected; not all calculations independently replicated.

## Contribution to the commissioned papers

Historical fertility and natural increase in the nineteenth-century United States, including high early period fertility, undercut an unqualified claim that all settlers required external immigration to replace themselves. Supports local-specific and period-specific cohort questions rather than a national demographic trope.

## Provenance and interpretive limit

A country-level birth/death finding is not a category-specific causal fertility estimate, and early census exclusions and race-category boundaries remain historically constructed.
