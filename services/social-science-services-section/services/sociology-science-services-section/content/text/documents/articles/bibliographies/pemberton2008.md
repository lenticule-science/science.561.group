## Quantitative or causal contribution

§2 identifies an offspring's inbreeding coefficient F with its parents' coancestry, and gives F=0.25 for first-degree parent-child matings with unrelated noninbred founders. Applying the identical formula to full siblings born to the same two unrelated founders gives F=0.25 for their offspring. This is pedigree algebra; not a per-child disease or infertility percentage, and not genetic evidence of a white species.
