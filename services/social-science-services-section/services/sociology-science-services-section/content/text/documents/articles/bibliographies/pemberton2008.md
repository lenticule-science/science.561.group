# J. M. Pemberton (2008) — Wild pedigrees: the way forward

**Venue:** Proceedings of the Royal Society B (open access via PMC).
**Original source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2386891/.
**Reading status (2026-10-17):** Open-access peer-reviewed section 2 inspected; sibling value derived conditionally using its kinship definition..

## Quantitative or causal contribution

§2 identifies an offspring's inbreeding coefficient F with its parents' coancestry, and gives F=0.25 for first-degree parent-child matings with unrelated noninbred founders. Applying the identical formula to full siblings born to the same two unrelated founders gives F=0.25 for their offspring. This is pedigree algebra; not a per-child disease or infertility percentage, and not genetic evidence of a white species.

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Research role:** Pedigree identity used only as a counterexample.

**Evidence available at its source:** The original genetic argument makes an offspring's coefficient of inbreeding depend on its parents' pedigree coancestry, with a conditional F=1/4 for descendants of full-sibling pairings from unrelated noninbred founders. This is an expected identical-by-descent coefficient, not a frequency of infant deaths or infertility.

**Interpretive/measurement limits:** Pedigree algebra assumes founders, reproduction, mating and inheritance scenarios; the result is irrelevant to whether a racial classification actually constitutes a population, and cannot prove historical ideology drove demographic decline. It is subordinate to the client's institutional-timescale problem.
