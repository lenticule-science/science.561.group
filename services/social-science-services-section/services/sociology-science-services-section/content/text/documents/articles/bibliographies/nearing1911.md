# Scott Nearing (1911) — Race Suicide vs Overpopulation

**Source:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Popular_Science_Monthly/Volume_78/January_1911/Race_Suicide_vs_Overpopulation
**Publication:** Popular Science Monthly 78 (January 1911)
**Read status:** Full original-magazine transcription read

## Annotation

Criticizes population-growth alarm and treats supposed race suicide as a quality-over-quantity decision. This is a contemporary explicit articulation of the conflict between numerical increase and supposed quality.

## Limits

No evidence here for a real racially superior human genotype or any inevitability of group extinction.
