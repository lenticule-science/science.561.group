# Scott Nearing (1911) — Race Suicide vs Overpopulation

**Source:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Popular_Science_Monthly/Volume_78/January_1911/Race_Suicide_vs_Overpopulation
**Publication:** Popular Science Monthly 78 (January 1911)
**Read status:** Full original-magazine transcription read

## Annotation

Criticizes population-growth alarm and treats supposed race suicide as a quality-over-quantity decision. This is a contemporary explicit articulation of the conflict between numerical increase and supposed quality.

## Limits

No evidence here for a real racially superior human genotype or any inevitability of group extinction.

Used in the [white-increase research note](../notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md).

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Research role:** Direct contemporary response to race-suicide panic.

**Evidence available at its source:** Nearing's 1911 public argument criticizes fear of fewer births and sets alleged population 'quality' against mere numerical quantity. It demonstrates that the quantity–selection tension was an explicit argument among historical populationists, rather than something deduced only retrospectively by a modern analyst.

**Interpretive/measurement limits:** Nearing's claims about desirable quality are historically ideological, not endorsed scientific facts. This source does not show that any government implemented a policy of grinding down all nominally white people, nor does it measure births prevented.
