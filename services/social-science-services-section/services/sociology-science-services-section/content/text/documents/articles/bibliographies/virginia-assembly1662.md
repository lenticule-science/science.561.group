# Virginia General Assembly (1662) — Negro womens children to serve according to the condition of the mother (Act XII)

**Source:** https://encyclopediavirginia.org/primary-documents/negro-womens-children-to-serve-according-to-the-condition-of-the-mother-1662/
**Source class:** primary legislation; transcription.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Primary-text transcription and its edition/folio inspected at Virginia Humanities.

## Source-located evidence

December 1662, Act XII, Hening 2:170.

## What the source supports

The act answers the question whether children fathered by Englishmen on enslaved Black women were bond or free by assigning status according to the condition of the mother. It explicitly links white male paternity to the potential birth of a child legally held enslaved, a central departure from default paternal-status rules.

## What it does not establish

The statute states Virginia law at a particular time, not the uniform law of every colony and state across all periods. Maternal legal status is not proof that every such conception was forced or that Indigenous legal cases always followed the same simple rule.
