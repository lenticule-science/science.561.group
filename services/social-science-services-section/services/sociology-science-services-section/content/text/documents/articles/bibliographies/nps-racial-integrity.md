# U.S. National Park Service (2024) — The Racial Integrity Act, 1924: An Attack on Indigenous Identity

**Source:** https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/racial-integrity-act.htm
**Source class:** governmental historical synthesis.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Full National Park Service summary and discussion of Plecker's enduring effect on Virginia Tribes inspected.

## Source-located evidence

Sections on Plecker, Indigenous identification, and damage to federal recognition records.

## What the source supports

Indigenous identity and documentation were damaged by administrative race classifications, particularly labeling Native people ‘colored’. It reveals the materially unequal stakes of Virginia's supposedly neutral racial classifications and why an elite exemption cannot stand as the entire social history.

## What it does not establish

Do not use this as a substitute for Tribes' own primary accounts, legal documentation or genealogical testimony; colonial state's erased classifications do not determine Indigenous political identity.
