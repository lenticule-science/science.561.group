# Tilly 1984 — *Big Structures, Large Processes, Huge Comparisons* (pending direct read)

Charles Tilly. *Big Structures, Large Processes, Huge Comparisons*. New York: Russell Sage Foundation, 1984.

## Status

**Pending direct read.** Foundational methodological reference for [comparative-historical analysis](../../../glossary/concepts/comparative-historical-analysis.md). Promotion to a full annotation is a follow-up.

## Why this stub exists

Tilly's typology of comparative-historical strategies (individualizing, universalizing, variation-finding, encompassing) is the methodological grammar the comparative-historical method note draws on. His later *Mechanisms in Political Processes* (with McAdam and Tarrow, 2001) and *Contentious Performances* (2008) develop the mechanism-based approach to causal explanation that the contemporary tradition has largely adopted; both should get their own reference notes when read.

Tilly's substantive work — *From Mobilization to Revolution* (1978), *Coercion, Capital, and European States* (1990), *Durable Inequality* (1998), *Contention and Democracy in Europe* (2004) — is also constitutive across the analyses of state formation, contentious politics, and inequality the library draws on. Each is a candidate reference note.
