# Virginia General Assembly (1924) — Preservation of Racial Integrity (1924)

**Publication:** Acts of Assembly of the Commonwealth of Virginia; original statute. 
**Source:** https://encyclopediavirginia.org/primary-documents/preservation-of-racial-integrity-1924/.
**Read status (2026-10-17):** Original 1924 enacted law reproduced by Virginia Humanities; supported by Library of Virginia's primary-source exhibits.

## Contribution to the commissioned papers

Defines an extremely narrow legal whiteness while expressly treating people with up to one-sixteenth American Indian ancestry and no other non-Caucasian ancestry as white. That selective exception protected a portion of established Virginia white elites claiming Pocahontas descent. The contradiction is between absolute-sounding racial-purity doctrine and law preserving already valuable elite family status.

## Provenance and interpretive limit

The original statute is enacted law; it cannot establish whether a later proposal was enacted, nor quantify population outcomes or individual implementation. Distinguish source textual definition from arbitrary biological ancestry.
