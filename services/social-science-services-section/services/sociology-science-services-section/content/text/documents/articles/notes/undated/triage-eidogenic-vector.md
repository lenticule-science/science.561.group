An ***eidogenic vector*** is a [[fundamental eidic force]], [[cohere|cohering]] a [[old-notes/Relationality/system/structures/thing]] to an [[eidic field]].

The vector is [[coinduce|coinduced]] with the eidic field, as the field is determined by the decision that to be is to relate to the field, composed of stabilized relations-of-relation-to-relations (eidolons) and eidolon-relation-defined instances of the real (eidies), inducing the vector.

Thus, it is an active condition of the eidic field, requiring and generating the movement that generates the field and thus its own force, recursively, as long as the field or vector exist.

The eidic field, and eidogenic vector, are both metaphysical concepts and a real force, generated by and as liberalism, as explained in "Liberal as Eidic Field."



### **Eidogenic Vector**

**(n.)**  
An _eidogenic vector_ is the directional, recursive, and ontologically generative force by which the real is cohered toward eidic form through the structuring logics of ontoeidic governance. It emerges from within a system that has already determined, at the level of ontology, that _to be_ is to become legible through alignment with a projected eidolon—a stabilized image-form that governs intelligibility, affect, embodiment, and relational possibility.

Rooted in the structural mechanics of **ontoeidics**—the study of how image-forms (eidolons) determine ontological coherence—an eidogenic vector is not an accidental feature of perception, nor a psychological or epistemic distortion. Rather, it is the active _field condition_ of worlding under the dominant liberal kyriarchy. The vector does not merely point toward form; it generates and reinforces the necessity of movement toward that form as a precondition for coherence.

---

### **Critical Theory Contextualization:**

This force can be understood as an intensification and convergence of multiple logics described in critical theory:

- In **Michel Foucault’s** concept of **productive power**, subjects are not simply disciplined or repressed, but _made_ through practices that induce visibility, legibility, and normativity. The eidogenic vector is one such productive force, operating not just at the level of subjectivation, but at the ontological level: being itself is produced as the capacity to _cohere to a projected form_.
    
- **Lauren Berlant’s** theory of **genre** and **cruel optimism** clarifies how life is structured around affective attachments to projected forms that are structurally unreachable or sustaining of harm. The eidogenic vector extends this: it names the ontological infrastructure that makes the attachment possible and necessary in the first place. Genres are not merely narrative containers; they are vectors of coherence.
    
- **Jasbir Puar’s** **assemblage theory** and critique of **homonationalism** suggest that identity categories like queerness become operationalized through alignment with state legibility. The eidogenic vector renders this legibility not as an outcome of power, but as its recursive motor: queerness is structured by its proximity to a projected form of liberal good-queerness, which becomes more real than any lived divergence.
    
- In **Frantz Fanon**, the act of being seen is structured through **projective identification**: Blackness is not seen as itself, but through the phantasmatic projection of the white gaze. The eidogenic vector formalizes this projective mechanism as a recursive structuring principle of being—not merely racial, but ontological.
    
- The **genre-induced futurism** described in **José Esteban Muñoz’s** _Cruising Utopia_ imagines queerness as oriented toward a not-yet, a horizon of potentiality. The eidogenic vector names the reversal of this relation: not queerness as possibility, but as a being structured _by_ the pressure to fulfill a form that is always-already imagined.

### **Operational Mechanism:**

The eidogenic vector follows a recursive trajectory that can be modeled in four stages (each corresponding to a kyriarchal ontological operation):

1. **Perceptual Relation to Form**: The instance is seen in relation to an eidolon (a known, legible type or trajectory).
    
2. **Ontological Anticipation**: The form is taken to be what the instance _should_ become; coherence is pre-inducted.
    
3. **Compulsory Alignment**: Structural forces (social, affective, institutional, bodily) induce the instance to move toward the form.
    
4. **Recursive Reinforcement**: Coherence with the form is read as _reality itself_, reinforcing the form as ontological ground.
    

This sequence becomes self-stabilizing. Crucially, **the vector is not teleological**—it does not guarantee the realization of the form—but rather _presumes the form’s reality_ as an ongoing requirement of existence.

---

### **Function in Kyriarchal Worlding:**

The eidogenic vector is the mechanism by which **kyriarchal world-systems convert contingency into structure**. It transforms the perceivable into the predictable, the relational into the projective, and the living into the intelligible. In doing so, it establishes **directionality**—not as a temporal or developmental gradient, but as a **field effect** of ontological governance.

This force is the ontoeidic counterpart to what some thinkers (e.g. **Deborah Thomas**, **Elizabeth Povinelli**) have described as **endurance** or **survival governance**—except that it does not merely condition how subjects persist within harm; it conditions what counts as _real_ at all.
