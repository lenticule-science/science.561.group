# Constitutional Convention / U.S. Constitution (1787) — Constitution of the United States, Article I, Section 2 (apportionment enumeration)

**Source:** https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript
**Source class:** primary constitutional law.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Original Article I, Section 2 transcription read from National Archives.

## Source-located evidence

Article I §2, population formula and Indians-not-taxed exception.

## What the source supports

Representatives and direct taxes were apportioned through a formula counting free persons and three-fifths of ‘all other persons’, while excluding ‘Indians not taxed’. Captive people who could not vote were thereby partially counted toward the representation of states containing enslavers.

## What it does not establish

The rule is for a particular constitutional era and apportionment; it neither measures actual franchise participation nor implies each captive birth automatically caused a discrete additional seat. After emancipation the relevant formula and disfranchisement institutions changed.
