# Nancy Fraser (2016) — Contradictions of Capital and Care

**Original:** https://newleftreview.org/issues/ii100/articles/nancy-fraser-contradictions-of-capital-and-care
**Published in:** New Left Review II/100, July–August 2016.
**Reading grade (2026-10-17):** Original NLR publisher full text read; sections 'Free-riding on the life-world', 'Housewifization', migration care chains

## Exact contribution to the commissioned thesis

Fraser conceptualizes a structural social contradiction: capitalist production depends on socially reproductive care and family provisioning, yet accumulation can progressively exhaust those unpriced conditions. Discusses 19th-century women and children's factory labor and the contemporary import of migrant caregivers with transferred familial burdens. Supplies a *mechanism by which a system may destroy the conditions of its own continuation*, close to the client's opening proposition of reproduction-through-expenditure.

## Alternative explanations and evidence limits

This is a structural theory of capitalist social reproduction across classes and genders; it neither proves that whiteness constitutes a unified choosing subject nor that white-classified people are uniquely or predominantly exhausted. No cohort-level fertility effects, policy revealed-preference comparison or racialized causal outcome are computed in this article.
