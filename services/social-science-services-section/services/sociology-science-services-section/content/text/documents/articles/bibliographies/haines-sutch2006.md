# Michael R. Haines and Richard Sutch (2006) — National population and the demographic components of change: 1790–2000

**Publication:** Historical Statistics of the United States: Millennial Edition, Table Aa9–Aa14. 
**Source:** https://hsus.cambridge.org/HSUSWeb/toc/treeTablePathIdAa9-14.html.
**Read status (2026-10-17):** Original Cambridge historical demographic table opened and annual columns inspected 1790–1859; underlying methodology documentation awaits full extraction.

## Contribution to the commissioned papers

Authoritative historical reconstruction of annual U.S. births (in thousands) from 1800 onward. It leaves annual birth/death columns blank for 1790–1799; explicitly forbids treating 1790s birth observations as present. Summed annual birth estimates: 1800–49 30,038,000 and 1800–59 41,732,000. Supplies the denominator paired with Hacker's modeled enslaved births.

## Provenance and interpretive limit

Historical estimates are not national individual birth registrations, and nation-specific population boundaries and historical classification exclusions constrain the denominator. Full underlying method documentation and uncertainty model need an independent review.
