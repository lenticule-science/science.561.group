# Musab Younis (2026) — Lineages of a conspiracy: the great replacement and demography

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/03063968261422608
**Publication:** Race & Class 67(4), first online March 5 2026
**Read status:** Full open-access Sage scholarly article inspected

## Annotation

Connects early colonial land and population arithmetic (including William Petty and Franklin) to later racialized demography and contemporary replacement panic, warning about dominant groups narrating themselves as endangered.

## Limits

Does not demonstrate the causal time-to-goal versus time-to-continuity conjecture.

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Why this source is essential:** Demography as settler colonial statecraft.

**Recoverable evidence and locators:** Younis links William Petty, Benjamin Franklin and Malthus's population calculations to colonial frontier expansion and the simultaneous promises of settler replenishment and Indigenous/subordinate disappearance. The 'great replacement' anxiety inverts older histories of conquest into claims of dominant-group vulnerability. This deep genealogy of the 'white increase' problem precedes the commission's draft.

**Inferential boundary / competing evidence:** The article offers a historical argument about population science and racist rhetoric, not an actuarial proof of white extinction. The client-specific two-clock conjecture must confront Younis rather than pretend colonial origins of demographic panic were unknown.
