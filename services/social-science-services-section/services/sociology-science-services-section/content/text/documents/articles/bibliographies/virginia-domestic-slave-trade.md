# Encyclopedia Virginia / Virginia Humanities (2020) — The Domestic Slave Trade in Virginia

**Source:** https://encyclopediavirginia.org/entries/slave-sales/
**Source class:** secondary historical synthesis of archival research.
**Read boundary (2026-10-17):** Full institutional summary and sections on Upper South economic drivers, cotton demand and slave traders inspected.

## Source-located evidence

Sections on origins and domestic trade, 1790–1860 more than one million people moved Upper to Lower South.

## What the source supports

Documents the interregional economic system for selling and transporting enslaved men, women and children from the Upper South to the Lower South. Distinguishes rising natural population in the Upper South from transfer of existing people into cotton/sugar regions; the import ban of 1808 altered markets.

## What it does not establish

A million people sold/relocated is not a million births, and the figure includes several movement modes and generations. Cannot treat it as an independent slave-birth numerator or proof that a dedicated breeding estate operated at that scale.

## Receiving and reuse

Paper II regional migration of enslaved persons separate from national net international migration; later economic capitalization investigation.
