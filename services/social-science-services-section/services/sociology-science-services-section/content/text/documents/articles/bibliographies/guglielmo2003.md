# Guglielmo, Thomas A. (2003) — White on Arrival: Italians, Race, Color, and Power in Chicago, 1890–1945

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780195155433.001.0001
**Publication:** Oxford University Press
**Review level (2026-10-17):** Publisher book abstract and metadata inspected.

## Annotation

Shows historically variable differences between race and color categories and advantages assigned to Italian immigrants. Demonstrates that whiteness as social classification can expand or change by institutional incorporation rather than births, serving as a counterexample to a universal, monotonically tightening purity ratchet.
