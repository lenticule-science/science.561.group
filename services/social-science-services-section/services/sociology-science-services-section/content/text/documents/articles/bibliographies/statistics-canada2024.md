# Statistics Canada (2024) — Canada's population estimates: Strong population growth in 2023

**Source:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/240327/dq240327c-eng.htm.
**Venue:** Statistics Canada, The Daily, March 27 2024.
**Read status (2026-10-17):** Original primary provider text or original publisher/university abstract as specified below.

## Contribution to the client commission

Primary national official statistical release read: population grew 1,271,872 in 2023; 97.6% of growth attributed to international migration including permanent and temporary residents and 2.4% natural increase; Canada 2022 fertility of 1.33 births per woman noted. The statistic is a share of national population *growth*, not a racial-group-specific flow or share of births.

## Limits

Changes in population methodology, resident population treatment, revisions, and migrant origin/identity need to be traced. Cannot call 97.6% immigration into Canada a white-settler immigration rate.
