These pages orient the reader to the figures the [black-radical-tradition](../../../../_index.md) subdomain centrally cites. The pages are short on purpose: they say what the figure contributed, name the works the subdomain treats as load-bearing, and point outward to fuller biographical notes in [`library/references/people/`](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/_index.md) where those exist.

The set is not a canon and not a survey of the tradition as a whole. It is the working set this subdomain currently leans on. Other figures (Aimé Césaire, Amílcar Cabral, Walter Rodney, Stuart Hall, Sylvia Wynter, Hortense Spillers, bell hooks, Frantz Fanon, Édouard Glissant) appear throughout the subdomain's terms and texts; some have full reference notes already, some do not, and the curation question of who else gets a figure page here is itself open work tracked in [`questions/`](../../../../triage/questions/_index.md).

## Figures with pages here

- [W. E. B. Du Bois](triage-w-e-b-du-bois.md) — color line, double consciousness, *Black Reconstruction*'s general strike of the enslaved.
- [C. L. R. James](triage-c-l-r-james.md) — *The Black Jacobins*, the Haitian Revolution as the first successful anti-colonial revolt.
- [Cedric Robinson](triage-cedric-robinson.md) — *Black Marxism*, racial capitalism, the Black radical tradition as lineage that precedes and exceeds Marxism.
- [Angela Davis](triage-angela-davis.md) — prisons, the prison-industrial complex, abolition democracy, women–race–class as a single analytical field.
- [Ruth Wilson Gilmore](triage-ruth-wilson-gilmore.md) — carceral geography, organized abandonment, abolition geography. Full reference note: [`ruth-wilson-gilmore`](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/ruth-wilson-gilmore.md).
- [Saidiya Hartman](triage-saidiya-hartman.md) — afterlife of slavery, the scenes of subjection, critical fabulation, wayward lives.
- [Fred Moten](triage-fred-moten.md) — the undercommons (with Stefano Harney), fugitivity, the break, phonic materiality. Full reference note: [`fred-moten`](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/fred-moten.md).

These figures are cited throughout the subdomain and have full notes under [`library/references/people/`](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/_index.md) but do not have a page here:

Also has a subdomain figure page in the sibling [`feminism/theories/black-feminism`](../../../../../feminism/theories/black-feminism/figures/hortense-spillers.md) subdomain, which records what black-feminism in particular takes from her.

- [Frantz Fanon](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/frantz-fanon.md) — *Black Skin, White Masks*, *The Wretched of the Earth*.
- [bell hooks](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/bell-hooks.md) — imperialist white-supremacist capitalist patriarchy, engaged pedagogy.
- [Édouard Glissant](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/edouard-glissant.md) — opacity, relation, antillanité.
- [Glen Coulthard](../../../../../../../../../../../references/people/glen-coulthard.md) — grounded normativity, critique of recognition (read into the tradition through Fanon).
