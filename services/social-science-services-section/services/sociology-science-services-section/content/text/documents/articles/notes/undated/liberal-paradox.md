- no [decision-making procedure](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/decision-making-procedure.md) can meet all the following predicates:
  - [unrestricted](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/unrestricted.md)
    - every possible ranking of each individual's preferences an all outcomes of every possible voting rule will be considered equally
  - [Pareto condition](../../../../../../services/work-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pareto-condition.md)
    - if everybody individually likes some choice better at the same time, the society in its voting rule as a whole likes it better as well
  - [liberal](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/liberal.md)
    - all individuals in a society must have at least one possibility of choosing differently, so that the social choice under a given voting rule changes as well.
    - That is, as an individual liberal, anyone can exert their freedom of chioce at least in some decision with tangible results.
