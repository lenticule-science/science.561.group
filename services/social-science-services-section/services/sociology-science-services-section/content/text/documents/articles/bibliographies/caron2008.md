## Annotation

This chapter documents simultaneous encouragement of reproduction among fit white Protestant elites and restriction of people cast as unfit. Crucial to the difference between a public ideology of white population survival and an internal, increasingly selective boundary. It does not itself establish a self-extinguishing feedback loop.
