# Theodore Roosevelt (1913) — Letter to Charles Davenport, 3 January 1913

**Source:** https://dnalc.cshl.edu/view/11219-T-Roosevelt-letter-to-C-Davenport-about-degenerates-reproducing-.html
**Publication:** Charles B. Davenport Papers; Cold Spring Harbor DNA Learning Center archive transcription
**Read status:** Original correspondence transcript read

## Annotation

Roosevelt explicitly proposes reproduction by an alleged right type and exclusion of an alleged wrong type, using livestock breeding analogy. Records the coexistence of selectivity and natalism.

## Limits

Evidence for Roosevelt's own position, not evidence of enacted specific laws, birth suppression or demographic collapse.

Used in the [white-increase research note](../notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md).

## Commission-specific source audit (2026-10-17)

**Research role:** Simultaneous expansion and exclusion in an identifiable actor.

**Evidence available at its source:** The January 3, 1913 letter to Charles Davenport couples a demand for births from individuals Roosevelt considered the 'right type' with stopping reproduction by the 'wrong type', using an openly eugenic livestock analogy. This is direct author-origin evidence of conflicting selective and pro-natalist objectives within one speaker's policy thought.

**Interpretive/measurement limits:** One letter is not a demographic survey or proof that its instructions were enacted. When attributing an outcome, find the actual institutional authority, policy, cohort affected and alternative choices; do not equate a rhetoric of future quality with realized human genetic quality.
