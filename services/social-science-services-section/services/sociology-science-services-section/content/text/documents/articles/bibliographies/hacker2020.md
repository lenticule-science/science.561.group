# Hacker, J. David (2020) — From ‘20. and odd’ to 10 million: The growth of the slave population in the United States

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/0144039X.2020.1755502
**Publication:** Slavery & Abolition 41(4): 840–855
**Review level (2026-10-17):** Article is available on PubMed Central and its publisher/university metadata was inspected.

## Annotation

Estimates enslaved population growth, births and imports by decade. Supplies the crucial captive-birth numerator and uncertainty structure for a historical share calculation; the all-birth denominator and Indigenous census exclusions must be reconstructed separately. Does not show that all captive births were directed industrial breeding.
