# Mills 1959 — *The Sociological Imagination* (pending direct read)

C. Wright Mills. *The Sociological Imagination*. New York: Oxford University Press, 1959.

## Status

**Pending direct read.** A constitutive methodological reference for the discipline's self-understanding. Promotion to a full annotation is a follow-up.

## Why this stub exists

Mills's central methodological move — locating personal trouble inside structural issue, and reading biography against history — is one of sociology's most-cited methodological commitments. The library's analytic stance across the cybernetic-postliberalism school and the affect-theory school carries the move forward implicitly; the reference note here registers the source.

Mills's *The Power Elite* (1956) is the empirical companion text — the analysis of the interlocking corporate, political, and military elites that organize American power — and should get its own reference note (`mills1956.md`) when read.

## Where used in sociology

- Implicitly across the discipline; the methodological move "personal trouble / structural issue" structures how the library reads the relation between [agency](../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agency.md) and [structure](../../../triage/teaching/explanations/structure.md).
