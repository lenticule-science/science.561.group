These pages orient the reader to the figures black feminism centrally cites. The pages are short on purpose: they say what the figure contributed, name the works the subdomain treats as load-bearing, and point outward to fuller biographical notes in [`library/references/people/`](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/references/people/_index.md) where those exist.

The set is not a canon and not a survey of the tradition as a whole. It is the working set this subdomain currently leans on — the writers whose work the front door's central claims rest on directly.

## Figures

- [Combahee River Collective](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/combahee-river-collective.md) — the 1977 statement; identity politics in the non-trivial sense; interlocking systems of oppression; Black-feminist socialist politics.
- [Audre Lorde](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/audre-lorde.md) — the master's tools; the erotic as power; the uses of anger; the transformation of silence; difference as creative function.
- [bell hooks](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/bell-hooks.md) — *Ain't I a Woman*; imperialist white-supremacist capitalist patriarchy; engaged pedagogy; love as political practice. Full reference note: [`bell-hooks`](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/references/people/bell-hooks.md).
- [Patricia Hill Collins](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/patricia-hill-collins.md) — *Black Feminist Thought*; the matrix of domination; Black-feminist epistemology; the outsider-within standpoint.
- [Hortense Spillers](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/hortense-spillers.md) — "Mama's Baby, Papa's Maybe"; flesh vs. body; the ungendering of the captive; the American grammar book. Full reference note: [`hortense-spillers`](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/hortense-spillers.md).
- [Saidiya Hartman](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/figures-saidiya-hartman.md) — the afterlife of slavery; critical fabulation; *Wayward Lives, Beautiful Experiments*. Also a figure page in the sibling [`black-radical-tradition`](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/saidiya-hartman.md) subdomain; the page here records what black-feminism in particular takes from her.

## Other figures cited throughout the subdomain

The tradition is larger than the figure pages here. Sojourner Truth, Anna Julia Cooper, Ida B. Wells, Claudia Jones, the Third World Women's Alliance, Frances Beal, Barbara Smith, Barbara Christian, June Jordan, Toni Cade Bambara, Michele Wallace, [Angela Davis](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/angela-davis.md), Kimberlé Crenshaw, [Sylvia Wynter](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/references/people/sylvia-wynter.md), Dionne Brand, Christina Sharpe, and Jennifer Nash all appear in the subdomain's vocabulary and arguments. The curation question of who else gets a figure page here is itself open work tracked in [`questions/`](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/social-sciences/sociology/archive/library/critical-theory/theories/feminism/theories/black-feminism/figures/questions/_index.md).
