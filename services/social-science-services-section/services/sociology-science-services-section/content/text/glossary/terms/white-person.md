# White person

A **white person** is a person classified or treated as white by a specified social or legal regime in a specified time and place. This is a contingent relational status, not membership in a genetically discrete species; institutions and observers may differ on who qualifies.

A person nominally recognized as white can nevertheless be condemned as unfit to reproduce a eugenic white ideal for reasons of poverty, disability, ethnicity, gender or moral judgment. Thus counting [white people](white-person.md) does not count [admissible reproducers of whiteness](white-increase.md). See [racial formation](racial-formation.md) and the [owning research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md).
