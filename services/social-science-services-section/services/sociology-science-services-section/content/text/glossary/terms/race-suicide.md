# Race suicide

**Race suicide** was a racist and eugenic claim that too few children were being born among selected white, native-born Protestant groups relative to other groups. Advocates of this ideology both encouraged births among those declared fit and restricted reproduction among those deemed unfit, especially across class and other racialized criteria.

This is a description of historical doctrine, not scientific support for a biological white race, a measured contemporary population crisis or a universal cause of low fertility. Simone M. Caron, *Who Chooses?*, chapter 3 (2008), reconstructs its historical forms. [The 2026 Science inquiry](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md) **inverts** the advocates' diagnosis by asking whether purity responses may themselves erode the reproduction they claim to protect. These propositions are not the same.

## Primary texts and the timing conjecture

Franklin's 1751 population essay makes increase among a narrow classed notion of whites distinct from migration and social reproduction; Roosevelt's 1913 Davenport letter calls for both higher fertility among favored people and exclusion of others; Nearing's 1911 essay replies to race-suicide fears by explicitly distinguishing quantity from claimed quality. The Leader's [two-clock hypothesis](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md) is a *new conjecture*, not a belief established in those works. Their original texts and Younis's 2026 critical reconstruction are annotated in this Sociology bibliography.
