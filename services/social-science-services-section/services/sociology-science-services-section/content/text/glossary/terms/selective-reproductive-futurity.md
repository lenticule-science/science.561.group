# selective reproductive futurity

An institutional situation in which the legitimate population promised for the future is not identical to living people presently included in the privileged category. Present members can be disqualified, exploited, or deprived because selection is organized around an imagined future kind of reproducer, rather than the continued flourishing of the existing people.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Do not infer a singular institution or purpose from co-occurring harms. Establish the stated ideal, observable authority over real people, decisions showing willingness to bear reproduction costs, and actual counterfactual alternatives. The client’s 'at least two' image is a hypothetical success threshold, not a genetic research target.
