***Decolonization is not a Metaphor*** is an [essay](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/essay.md) [authored](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring.md) by [Eve Tuck](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/eve-tuck.md) and [K. Wayne Yang](k-wayne-yang.md) in [2012ce](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/2012ce.md) that [analyzes](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/analyzing.md) the [appropriation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/appropriation.md) of "[decolonization](decolonization.md)" in [academic](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/academic-discourse.md) and [activist discourse](../../../../../../../engineering-science-services-section/services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/activist-discourse.md). \[cite:@TuckYang2012\] They [claim](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/claiming.md) that decolonization must refer to the [material repatriation of Indigenous being](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/material-repatriation-of-indigenous-being.md), not a class of actions they describe as "[settler moves to innocence](../../../../services/critical-theory-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/settler-move-to-innocence.md)".

> Decolonization is not an "and". It is an elsewhere.

- Using decolonization to describe educational reform, social justice, or shifts in consciousness dilutes its meaning and recenters settler futurity
  - settler moves to innocence
- Incommmensurable Ethic:
  - decolonization is incommensurable with civil rights or inclusivity project
    - rupture, not reform or reconciliation
- Triadic settler-colonial roles:
  - settler, native, slave
    - all can reproduce colonial logics with misapplied "decolonization"

"Decolonization Is Not a Metaphor" \[cite:@tuck_DecolonizationNotMetaphor_2012\] is a 2012 article by \[Eve Tuck\](../../people/eve-tuck.md) and \[K. Wayne Yang\](../../people/k-wayne-yang.md), published in **Decolonization: Indigeneity, Education & Society**. The paper argues that the term "\[decolonization\](../../../sociology/terms/decolonization.md)" has been adopted by settler institutions and progressive movements in ways that empty it of its specific meaning: the repatriation of Indigenous land and life.

\[Tuck\](../../people/eve-tuck.md) and Yang identify several "\[settler moves to innocence\](../../documents/articles/notes/undated/*sociology/terms/settler-moves-to-innocence.md)" — rhetorical strategies by which settlers adopt decolonial language without confronting the material basis of \[settler colonialism\](../../documents/articles/sociology/topics/settler-colonialism/index.md) (land theft). These moves include claiming Indigenous ancestry, equating all oppressions with colonization, adopting decolonial frameworks as metaphors for social justice, and treating Indigenous futures as compatible with continued settler occupation.

The paper insists that \[decolonization\](../../../sociology/terms/decolonization.md) is incommensurable with other social justice frameworks: it requires the return of land, not simply the reform of settler institutions. This incommensurability is not a failure of solidarity but a condition that honest solidarity must confront.
