# demographic replacement fear

A political narrative presenting changing relative population shares or migrant presence as an existential loss of dominant-group continuity. It can mobilize racial classification and exclusion without establishing that actual demographic extinction is occurring.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

This is an object of historical inquiry, not a scientifically accepted account of immigration. Measure actual births/deaths/migration and the categories used, then separately research public rhetoric and the policies it legitimizes.
