# Organization

An organization is a purposive collective: people (or offices, or other
organizations) coordinated toward a shared end, capable of being addressed
or acting as a unit at least some of the time. This is the genus. It says
nothing yet about charters, staff, perpetual succession, or legal standing
— those are what distinguish *some* organizations from others, not what
every organization needs.

## Company is a species, not a synonym

[Company](../../../../../economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/company.md)
adds four specific invariants on top of the genus: a charter $K$ encoding a
purpose the members jointly hold, ongoing staff $\mathcal{S}$ rather than
voyage-bounded crew, a governance mechanism $\mathcal{G}$ that produces
collective attribution ("the company decided"), and an institutional
standing $\ell$ that installs it as a recognized entity. An organization
that lacks one of these is still an organization; it is just not a
company. Three checkable consequences follow directly:

- **A working group, board, office, or affinity group is an organization,
  not a company**, unless it also has company.md's charter+staff+
  governance+standing structure. `561-group-board-of-directors` is an
  organization; whether it is also a company is a separate question with
  its own answer.
- **A micro-enterprise (小微, xiaowei) is explicitly not a company** by its
  own definition — "not a subsidiary, which is a legal entity under a
  parent's control" — and so is not this species either, even though it is
  an organization (an internal unit coordinated toward a shared end). This
  is why the Group's enterprises (land-contracts, heir-search, and so on)
  are never called companies in their own records; the company is the one
  legal entity that signs for them.
- **A tiyóšpaye is not an organization at all** in this sense, company or
  otherwise. [Corporate culm](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/corporate-culm.md)
  is explicit that Pheží Ocháǧe is the rhizome the 561 Group's organizational
  and corporate structure rises from, never identical to it; treating the
  band itself as an "organization" the way the Group or a company is one
  would already be the mistake the culm/rhizome distinction exists to
  prevent.

## What's still open

This entry states the relation and its immediate consequences, not a full
theory of organization. What the minimal genus-level invariants are (does
"purposive" and "can act as a unit" suffice, or does it need more), and
what the other species of organization are beside company — a federation,
a board, an office, a duty — with their own differentiae, is not settled
here and should not be treated as settled by anything this entry says
beyond the company relation itself.
