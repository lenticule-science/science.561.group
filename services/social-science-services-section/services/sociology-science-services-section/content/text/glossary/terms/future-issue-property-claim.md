# Future issue property claim

A legally asserted *present interest in the possible future children* of a woman held enslaved, which an enslaver attempted to make available for transfer, testamentary distribution or later adjudication. This is **not** identical to a child who has already been born, a current ownership right over that child, a forced conception, or a verified monetary realization.

A source for such a claim must specify the jurisdiction, event date, document or deed/will, possible future persons, actual owners/claimants, and whether a court held the claim enforceable or merely quoted a party's desire. [Alphonso 2023](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/alphonso2023.md) discusses [Banks' Administrator v. Marksberry (1823)](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/case-banks-marksberry1823.md) as transferable future issue; its original judicial report remains a required original-source check. [Nelson (1849)](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/case-nelson-nelson1849.md) shows a will's attempted assignment of future issue whose temporal reach the court limited.

A legal claim upon an unconceived child is an **anticipated asset entitlement**, not evidence that the child was conceived, survived or was produced under directed sexual coercion.
