# reproductive eligibility

The subset of people whom a specified institution, policy or ideological actor designates entitled, fit, legally able or materially supported to have recognized descendants. Eligibility is historically contingent and may be narrower or broader than the demographic category called white.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Do not reify a social or eugenic criterion as real biological fitness. Separate coercive prohibition, social stigmatization, pronatalist benefits and practical material capacity. Report the period and legal institution before counting eligibility.
