The ***University of Copenhagen*** ([Danish](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/danish-language.md): Københavns Universitet) is a [Danish university](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/danish-university.md), the largest in [Denmark](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/denmark.md).

It's a [research university](research-university.md).

# Motto

**Coelestem adspicit lucem** *It beholds the celestial light*
