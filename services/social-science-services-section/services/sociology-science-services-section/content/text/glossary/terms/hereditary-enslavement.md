# hereditary enslavement

A legal system under which a child inherits an enslaved status by operation of law. In Virginia’s December 1662 statute the child's condition was tied explicitly to the mother's condition, in contrast to a contrary English-law expectation regarding paternal status.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

This is a legal relationship, not evidence of a genetically hereditary human defect; jurisdictions and legal eras vary. Do not conflate enslaved parental status, an individual's genealogy, racial classification, and the claims asserted by an enslaver.
