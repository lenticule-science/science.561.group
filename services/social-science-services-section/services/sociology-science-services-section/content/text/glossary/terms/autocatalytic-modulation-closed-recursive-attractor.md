An ***autocatalytic modulation-closed recursive attractor*** (`β⁺`) is a [[β-structure]] that induces the stabilization of one or more additional β-structures via its own anticipatory and modulation activity.

Let `β` be a β-structure with propagation field `Π(β)` and stabilization trace `β′`.

Then `β` is autocatalytic (`β⁺`) iff:

> ∃ β″ ⊂ β′ such that:  
>  1. β″ ≠ ∅  
>  2. ∀ z ∈ β″: z satisfies all β-structure conditions  
>  3. ∃ x ∈ β: 𝒜(x → z), ∇𝒟(x) · ∇Δ(x, z) > 0  
>  4. z ∈ dom(𝒪) via recursive stabilization

This enables:
- self-sustaining propagation of recursive structure  
- formation of biogenic attractor chains  
- recursive field generation driven by modulation closure dynamics
