# captive birth

A live birth whose newborn enters an inherited legal condition of enslavement under the specific jurisdiction’s status law. The unit is the *newborn birth event*, not a price transaction, slave schedule count, forced conception, immigration event, or proof of consent.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Specify law, period, territory, mother's legal status and whether the source observes actual births or models them retrospectively. Hacker 2020 estimates births into U.S. slavery; the associated ratio to all counted U.S. births must share a coherent territorial denominator and acknowledge excluded Indigenous populations.
