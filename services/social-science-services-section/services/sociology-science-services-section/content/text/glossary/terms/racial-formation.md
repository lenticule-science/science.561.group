# Racial formation

**Racial formation** is the historical production, contestation, revision and enforcement of racial categories, meanings and their material consequences. It does not identify genetically isolated human species. Racial classification is an institutional outcome: a population comparison must declare the dated classification rule, jurisdiction, census coverage, and changes in membership.

For the white-increase inquiry, distinguish persons *classified* white from persons whose reproduction is *recognized as fit* to produce a designated white future. The latter can be a strict subset. Category changes may alter recorded white populations with no birth or death. Consult [white increase](white-increase.md), [whiteness](whiteness.md) and the [source-owned research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md).
