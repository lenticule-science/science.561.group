# institutional reproductive expendability

Observable institutional willingness to bear or impose predictable losses of a group's reproductive opportunities, family security or survival while maintaining some other institutional objective such as capital accumulation, racial status, national belonging or a purported ideal future lineage.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Not a literal preference of an anthropomorphized 'whiteness'. This construct requires an actual policy choice, available alternative, decision-maker and observed cost. Poverty and declining births by themselves do not establish a coordinated ideological preference; many harms have nonracial and competing causes.
