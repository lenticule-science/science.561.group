# settler social reproduction

The continued realization of a settler political-economic order through land control, legal jurisdiction, household continuity, inheritance, labor relations, state power, migration and demographic events. Reproducing settler rule is not identical to increasing births among people classified white or to the reproduction of Indigenous communities subject to dispossession.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Recover the actual polity and its legal boundaries. Distinguish continuing settler institutions from self-replacing population cohorts, coercively replenished labor populations and Indigenous nationhood. A national immigrant-growth share alone does not measure settler reproduction.
