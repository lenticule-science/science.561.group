Emergent strategy is a way of organizing in which small, intentional, adaptive actions by interdependent people are expected to shape large change, on the model of how complex living systems change. adrienne maree brown names the elements: fractal (the small reflects the large), adaptive, interdependent and decentralized, non-linear and iterative, resilient, and creating more possibilities, and states the working principles plainly: small is good, small is all; change is constant; there is always enough time for the right work; never a failure, always a lesson; trust the people; move at the speed of trust; less prep, more presence; what you pay attention to grows ([brown 2017](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/brown2017.md)). Its sources are Octavia Butler's fiction, biomimicry, and complexity science rather than any political program, and it is written for people organizing together rather than about them.

It differs from strategic planning in that it does not fix an end state and a path to it; it fixes the quality of relationships and of small actions and lets the shape of the whole emerge from them. Spade's account of post-2020 [mutual aid](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/mutual-aid.md) is the same stance applied to survival needs: projects that meet needs directly, build shared understanding of why the need exists, mobilize people, and solve problems by collective action rather than waiting for saviors, distinguished from charity by the absence of eligibility tests, hierarchy, and moralism ([Spade 2020](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/spade2020.md)).

For a pool of members the practical rule is that the pool is designed at the scale of one draw and one crossing, and that its rules change by iteration among members rather than by a plan handed down, which is what a ledger of small matched crossings makes possible. The house pool's draw rule ([tally credit](../../../../../economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/monetary-economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tally-credit.md)) is such a ledger, and the [rotating savings and credit association](../../../../../economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rotating-savings-and-credit-association.md) is the old form of the same small, trusted, iterated action.
