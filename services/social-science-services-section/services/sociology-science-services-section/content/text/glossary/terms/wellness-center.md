A wellness center is a grassroots care space that provides rest,
support, and other forms of survival infrastructure alongside or beyond
emergency treatment [@madrprograms2024].

Within emergent disaster response, the term matters because disasters
generate needs that are medical, emotional, social, and organizational
at the same time. A wellness center helps address that wider field of
care rather than limiting response to immediate triage.
