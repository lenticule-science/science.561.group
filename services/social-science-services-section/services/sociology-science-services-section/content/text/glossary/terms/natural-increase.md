# natural increase

Population births minus deaths during a defined period, within a consistently specified jurisdiction and counting convention. Natural increase is a population-flow component and can be positive or negative; it is not the crude birth rate or the number of children born to a particular class of settlers.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Do not equate natural increase with all growth or immigrant independence: adults who previously migrated can produce births counted as local natural increase. Historical data and census administrative categories may change; the U.S. 1790s natural increase estimate does not supply that decade's crude birth rate.
