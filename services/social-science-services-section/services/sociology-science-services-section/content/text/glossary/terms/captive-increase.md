# captive increase

An enslaver's or property's 'increase' may refer to children born to people held enslaved and, in some legal contexts, a distinct claim over the potential future descendants of an enslaved woman. It thus names the juridical transfer of reproductive possibility into the owner's anticipated capital.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Do not confuse growth of the enslaved population through natural increase, a legal title to future issue, a domestic sale of existing persons, and deliberately compelled reproduction. They require distinct sources and cannot be summed as one event count.
