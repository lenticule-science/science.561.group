# White increase

**White increase** has three nonidentical possible meanings.

1. *Classified population increase*: births, deaths, net migration and changes in whom a given jurisdiction calls white.
2. *Admissible reproducer increase*: expansion or contraction of those deemed suitable to produce an allegedly ideal white future.
3. *Institutional increase*: reproduction of settler property, hierarchy and state power that may incorporate the reproductive labor of people excluded from the white category.

The opening [research conjecture](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md) is that eugenic purification can shrink (2) despite professed allegiance to (1), while (3) can persist by different mechanisms. None of the three entails either other. The central test is whether time to **loss of supported reproductive continuity** precedes time to produce the allegedly legitimate group of *at least two* reproducers under a changing purity rule. Pedigree problems of exactly two founders are secondary, not the Leader's principal claim.
