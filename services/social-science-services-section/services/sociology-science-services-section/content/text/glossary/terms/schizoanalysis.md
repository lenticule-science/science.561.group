***Schizoanalysis*** is a form of [subjectivity analysis](subjectivity-analysis.md) that [ruptured](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rupturing.md) from [psychoanalysis](../../../../services/critical-theory-science-services-section/services/anarchism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/psychoanalysis.md), especially [Freudian psychoanalysis](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/freudian-psychoanalysis.md).
