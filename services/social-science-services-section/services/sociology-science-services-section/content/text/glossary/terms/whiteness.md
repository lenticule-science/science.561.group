# Whiteness

**Whiteness** describes historically constructed racial classification, status, legitimate family membership and distributions of privilege and authority. Its meanings, boundaries and economic effects change across places and periods. It is not a genetically distinct species or a unitary actor endowed with a literal will.

In research on settler social reproduction, separate (1) people classified white, (2) people judged fit to reproduce the favored ideal, and (3) institutions of property and rule. These populations and outputs may diverge. [White increase](white-increase.md) requires a choice of measure, and the [reproductive purity ratchet](reproductive-purity-ratchet.md) names a proposed conflict between escalating purity and maintaining a population, **not** a historical law. Sources: Stubblefield (2007), Caron (2008), Guglielmo (2003), [research note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-increase-and-reproductive-selection-contradiction.md).
