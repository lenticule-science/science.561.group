A ***naïve scientist*** is a term for a [human](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/homo-sapiens.md). [Coined](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/coining-a-term.md) by [Fritz Heider](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fritz-heider.md) in [Naïve Psychology](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/naive-psychology.md) ([1958ce](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/1958ce.md)), it [describes](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/describing.md) how humans [desire](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/desiring.md) [knowledge](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowledge.md), especially of [causal relationships](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/causal-relationship.md), especially of the [human behavior](human-behavior.md) they [experience](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experiencing.md) and thus [seek](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/seeking.md) causal analysis for that behavior, leading them to do [attributing](attributing.md).
