# Captive reproductive title under life tenancy

A legal division in slaveholding property where one person claimed temporary possession or services from an enslaved mother (*life tenant*) and another claimed the subsequent interest (*remainderman*), while courts separately adjudicated who could claim children she bore during the tenancy.

Do not confuse the child's **inherited legal condition** with the person's **owner of record**, or the mother's eventual **manumission** with the child's already acquired status. In [North Carolina *Covington v McEntire* (1842)](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/case-covington-mcentire1842.md), four children of Binah followed a remainderman's entitlement; [Delaware *Smith v Milman* (1839)](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/case-smith-milman1839.md) rejected using the maternal-status maxim to decide ownership and ordered petitioner Stephen free; [Kentucky *Johnson* (1848)](../../documents/articles/bibliographies/case-johnson-johnson1848.md) granted mother Lytha freedom while her seven children remained enslaved and were allocated to the widow's estate.

These are **different dated jurisdictions and legal constructions**, not a harmonized single rule.
