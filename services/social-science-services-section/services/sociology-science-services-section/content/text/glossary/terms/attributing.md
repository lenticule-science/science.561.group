***Attributing*** is an [act](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) of [marking](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/marking.md) one [thing](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thing.md) as being the [cause](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cause.md) or determination of another thing.

As [studied](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/studying.md) by [human science](human-science.md), *attributing* is a [structuring cognitive operation](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/structuring-cognitive-operation.md), that is, a [cognitive operation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cognitive-operation.md) that [does](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/doing.md) [structuring](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/structuring.md). See [attribution theory](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/attribution-theory.md).
