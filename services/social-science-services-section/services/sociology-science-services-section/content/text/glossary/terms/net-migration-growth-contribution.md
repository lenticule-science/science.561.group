# net migration contribution to population growth

The contribution of arrivals minus departures to change in a defined population, often expressed as a fraction of *net total growth*, not of births and not of existing residents. It may exceed 100% when natural increase is negative.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

For Canada and Australia recent national shares aggregate residents of varied ancestry, citizenship and status. They are not rates of white settlers replacing themselves through migrants. A source must specify domestic versus international migration, temporary residents, age, period and category.
