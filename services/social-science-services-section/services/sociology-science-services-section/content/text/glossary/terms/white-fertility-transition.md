# white fertility transition

The change over time in fertility measures among people classified white under specific U.S. census rules, including total fertility, marital fertility, nuptiality, child–woman ratios and own-children reconstruction. These indicators are not automatically estimates of the same underlying parameter.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Haines–Hacker (2006) report child–woman ratio decline across 1800–1860, while Hacker (2003) reconstructs a later onset for total/marital fertility using revised mortality and own-children methods. Keep disagreement explicit. Neither estimator attributes fertility decline to a purity ideology without causal evidence.
