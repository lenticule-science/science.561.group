***Capitalism and Schizophrenia*** is a [text](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md) [authored](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authoring.md) by [Gilles Deleuze](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gilles-deleuze.md) and [Félix Guattari](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/felix-guattari.md), having two volumes: [Anti-Oedipus](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anti-oedipus.md) and [A Thousand Plateaus](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/a-thousand-plateaus.md).

Together, they [reject](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rejecting.md) [Freudian psychoanalysis](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/freudian-psychoanalysis.md) and [establish](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/establishing.md) [schizoanalysis](schizoanalysis.md); [methodologies of subjectivity analysis](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/methodologies-of-subjectivity-analysis.md)
