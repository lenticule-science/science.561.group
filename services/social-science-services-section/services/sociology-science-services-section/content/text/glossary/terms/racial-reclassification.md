# racial reclassification

Change in the social, legal or administrative category assigned to people, with or without any birth, death or migration. Historical incorporation or exclusion of migrants, ancestry exceptions, interracial-family classification and changing census instructions can alter the number recorded as white.

## Boundaries and admissible measurement

Reclassification is not a residual biological evolution or a birth. Census categories can be internally inconsistent and historically harmful, especially where Indigenous legal identities are erased. Reconstruct records for named years rather than treating all historical 'white' rows as interchangeable.
