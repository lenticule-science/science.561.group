Decision points are moments in time and space where a commander must choose among options.

The key practice is to make decision points explicit:

- define the options that would be chosen,
- define the *trigger* (what observable change indicates the decision must be made),
- define what information is required to decide,
- and define who is authorized to act if the commander cannot.

Decision points are where planning becomes executable.
