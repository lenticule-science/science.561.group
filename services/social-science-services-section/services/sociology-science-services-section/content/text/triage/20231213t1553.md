## Origins

The term "vernacular" originates from Latin *vernaculus*, meaning "domestic," "native," or "indigenous." When combined with "*ethnos*," which signifies a group of people connected by culture and heritage, vernacular ethnosis encapsulates how groups define themselves from within rather than adhering to external definitions imposed upon them.
