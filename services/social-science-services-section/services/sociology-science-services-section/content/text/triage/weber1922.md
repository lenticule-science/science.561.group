# Weber 1922 — *Economy and Society* (pending direct read)

Max Weber. *Wirtschaft und Gesellschaft: Grundriss der verstehenden Soziologie*. Tübingen: J.C.B. Mohr (Paul Siebeck), 1922 (posthumous; assembled by Marianne Weber from manuscripts). Standard English: *Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology*, ed. Guenther Roth and Claus Wittich (University of California Press, 1978).

## Status

**Pending direct read.** The work is the source for several of sociology's foundational concepts the discipline cannot leave unreferenced — *social action*, *types of authority* (traditional, rational-legal, charismatic), *Stand* (status as distinct from class), the bureaucracy analysis, the *Protestant ethic* argument's structural counterpart, and the methodological commitment to *Verstehen* as the procedure of interpretive sociology. The annotation here is a stub; promotion to a full read is a follow-up.
