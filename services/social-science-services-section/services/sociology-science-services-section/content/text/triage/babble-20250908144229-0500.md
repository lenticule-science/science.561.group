#+title: Babble, [2025-09-08 Mon 14:42], On Neel's Theory of the Party
#+date: [2025-09-08 Mon 14:42]
#+author: emsenn

So I just read through [[https://illwill.com/theory-of-the-party][Theory of the Party]] from [[id:08758de3-cfe3-4c81-a8f2-2b805ed7b649][Phil A. Neel]] and it's weird how outlining how to [[id:9346c9a7-4515-4b87-900d-8e8e5d810834][commodify]] something is seen as a good contribution to [[id:38b75295-55bb-4698-9ece-ba407a62b9f2][Marxist thought]]. Like, the piece seems to explain mechanisms that would successfully commodify [[id:10e02eeb-1155-4eaf-8f57-8923619d7de8][Mao]]'s [[id:fa4169e7-41d1-4f0f-962f-3f3008e00ba9][mass line]] into what I might call platformized communism, which might be described as [[id:f91c46b6-d916-436d-9302-9edab8c20b7d][communism]] that sees its role as practicing its -ism within platforms, not, y'know, doing communism. Maybe that'd be better named platformalized communism, but the concept is the pertinent part.

Also, the just... affect... of the writing. Is a lot. Gigantic heapings of rhetorical [[id:8d954498-e0d6-4312-b0e6-64c77ec9b053][melancholy]] are a way to set a [[id:fe4ba37b-aa41-495b-8e45-8042fd11df9f][mood]], which is... well I guess that *is* what most introductions are for in academic-style texts, these days, but I don't like it. I'm busy [[id:581e5888-2a33-4e39-8e1d-d350aa46bac1][imagining history]] as a "carboniferous bonfire," as prompted by the text, I don't know if I can do that *and* qualify the logic of the arguments, in their own terms.

I will say, the establishment of [[id:34bb9410-c6e9-4139-a873-686b146ba3e9][melancholia]] as the [[id:b1cb00f8-e71f-41ef-8398-fd54b190e2af][necessary conditions]] for these productive practices is... hella in-line with [[id:574fc40f-4778-4fe1-bbfe-774373365e2f][Han's leftist melancholic hope]]. Which, fair, it's all in line with [[id:45e7e593-8a47-4aa1-9fd0-7fd9b190d1f4][Hegelian dialectics]]. But I don't understand, I guess, where the confidence is that whatever missing piece is being posed by whichever author is THE missing piece in redeeming humanity. I also just don't like the unquestioned assumption that humanity can be, or even needs to be, redeemed.

Like, is it just a given that any reasonable human practice is oriented toward [[id:2b062792-d6a6-45e1-9afb-7737049198b5][redeeming]] us against... ???... and if you do otherwise you're... well, I don't know how to talk about this stuff without leaning heavily into [[id:234e1189-61c7-4f7a-8de0-8a196a58d6f6][Christian terms]], and it all gets very complicated and, in a certain way, uninteresting. If all this is just finding the steps to [[id:0dd097d1-d1b8-4af3-91cf-79b8003ba548][Grace]], and there's no ambiguity in where or what Grace is, or even if it exists, then all things reduce toward a person's [[id:e56a685f-e87f-47e7-900b-610e9fb2f436][subjectivation]] being their [[id:5fa1e13f-fd84-41e4-9742-0a80ceb1f006][salvation]], and that's just... hideously boring.

But going back to the original post: What's the plan here? Outpace the [[id:4611cff9-6da3-43a1-bdb8-faaaae75c8ac][contemporary global kyriarchy]]'s [[id:c74c9b4e-ae92-4b7f-b577-c90dd57848b0][industrial production of memes]]? First, that seems lofty. Second, what does that on its own accomplish? Not make possible, but accomplish? Because to accomplish it, you're providing a [[id:55671733-3978-4109-bced-9bb23565980a][grammar]] that makes it trivial for existing systems to massively scale their ability manufacture [[id:2f2750bc-7485-4628-887a-515b7a3b83b4][propaganda]], making your own goals significantly more difficult to achieve, and as providing this grammar is the means of achieving those goals, you induce and pursue a [[id:ef3399e9-ac7a-4f82-a995-9b27971a73ad][paradox]].
