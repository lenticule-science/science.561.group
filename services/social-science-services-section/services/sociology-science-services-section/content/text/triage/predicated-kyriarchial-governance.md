# Predicated Kyriarchial Governance

A theory of [kyriarchial governance](../../_index.md) on the [predication-categories](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/) mathematics: kyriarchial governance structures as specific predication settlements over a kyriarchial-governance-themed predicate category. Jural relations, offices, normative systems, charters, accountability mechanisms, and decision-making processes — the kyriarchial governance vocabulary's terms — are exhibited as specific structures on this predication substrate.

Sibling to [predicated-governance](../predicated-governance/) (also a theory of governance). Predicated-kyriarchial-governance is the bilattice-substrate-native synthesis; it combines the formal rigor of predicated-governance with the critical power analysis of kyriarchial-governance.

Following the predication-categories research convention. See the [predication-categories index](../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) for frontmatter/relation details.

## What this theory commits to

Predicated-kyriarchial-governance analyzes how mathematical structures of governance intersect with systems of domination:

1. **Formalization of power** — kyriarchial relations are encoded as specific structures within the predication-categories mathematical framework
2. **Intersectional jural dynamics** — how different axes of domination interact in the settling dynamics of authority and obligation predications
3. **Legitimacy as closure structure** — how societal determinations of legitimate authority emerge from closure rules operating on intersecting identity fibers
4. **Accountability as network propagation** — how responsibility assignment and remedy distribution spread through bilattice-network structures patterned after kyriarchal distributions
5. **Resistance as cohomological obstruction** — how collective governance transformation emerges as non-trivial cohomology classes in the kyriarchial governance complex

## Key Structural Commitments

- **Predicate fiber of identity**: Each actor's position in intersecting hierarchies (gender, race, class, sexuality, ability, citizenship) forms an identity fiber over which governance predications vary
- **Closure rules of domination**: Societal rules that determine what authority counts as "legitimate", what accountability counts as "just", or what decision-making counts as "valid" for different identity positions
- **Authority networks**: How legitimacy propagates through kinship, workplace, market, and state networks structured by kyriarchal principles
- **Accountability sheaves**: How local accountability practices glue together to form global obstruction classes against kyriarchial governance structures
