# Goffman 1959 — *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life* (pending direct read)

Erving Goffman. *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life*. Garden City, NY: Anchor Books, 1959.

## Status

**Pending direct read.** Foundational reference for the dramaturgical analysis of role and the symbolic-interactionist tradition the library cites in passing. Promotion to full annotation is a follow-up.

## Why this stub exists

Goffman's analysis of role performance, role distance, and the self as the dramaturgical effect of an actor's management of impressions sits behind much of how the library writes about [role](teaching/explanations/role.md) and [action](teaching/explanations/action.md). His later works — *Asylums* (1961) on total institutions, *Stigma* (1963), *Frame Analysis* (1974), *Forms of Talk* (1981) — are each constitutive references that should get their own reference notes.
