#+title: Conjecturing affect and libidinal economies as complementary postliberal complexes
#+date: [2025-09-29 Mon 17:40]
#+author: emsenn

Considering [[id:43c20cee-7e04-4037-8d43-d16c7b227ea2][On Fediverse narrating contradictions into aesthetic cruel optimism]], if we consider [[id:496f5eec-d900-4baa-897a-7f16d9f542f8][postliberal cybernetics]] as the analytical frame, then I might say:

The [[id:2deb3b5c-96b0-40bb-b43a-feb855bc3b7d][affect economy]] and [[id:4f38cf50-0889-4e78-bd46-c212004ea100][libidinal economy]] are [[id:fca3d7bf-5be8-441c-b38a-b56ccb3d85a9][complementary postliberal complexes]] that [[id:a8470624-94ec-4dc2-b091-5d4701575b0c][process]] [[id:5442ac13-aa44-45b4-8288-52a8602c31cf][experience]] into [[id:d9f9c7cc-57e5-41f3-adb7-7346d705249f][liberal subjectivity]].

By complementary complexes, I mean two distinct but interdependent [[id:8fd6daeb-f0e5-4a57-9e91-ad30d72c53cd][postliberal complexes]] acting in response to the same [[id:7adfad30-0c57-4c5d-8017-b5f23e354fd2][pressures]]: the libidinal economy incites investment in response to [[id:d865f1e4-9fcb-481c-a071-3ec821ea8dda][desiring]], the affect economy orients feedback, and together they stabilize the [[id:2e1ea233-491b-47eb-bb99-978b38a3f253][postliberal circuit of liberal subjectivity]].

I think I'm drawing most on [[id:b22785be-253d-4c94-94ee-6c2fb8fe3818][Jean-François Lyotard]]'s [[id:4f38cf50-0889-4e78-bd46-c212004ea100][Libidinal Economy]], which explicitly treats [[id:ea981f2a-c56a-4aea-a88b-fc4e87d24600][libido]] as an [[id:103f7874-c0f8-4a56-b042-5a07e88890d2][energetic flow]] that moves through [[id:5da5b7fc-975c-4119-9b96-3726c4faf780][social]] and [[id:1cb088ba-65b2-490e-9bca-ec2a3037e4fe][economic systems]], but I'm also influenced by [[id:6c191fb5-2aa0-4d8b-beba-a7ea32c7f9f5][Jacques Lacan]], who discussed desire not as a lack to be filled but a circulating structural function of investing in the [[id:606ef67f-0bbc-4092-bcd1-2e6cb51ed083][Other]], and [[id:8f9957f7-1cd8-4426-bbe1-a81c1aae8894][A Thousand Plateaus]], which also rejected [[id:2ab83912-8889-442e-bf01-e2f0a8fa8443][Freudian lack]] to look at [[id:1887f4fb-175f-4e1e-89ee-107522820395][generative desiring]]. The three share the claim that desire is not simply personal or interior, but circulates through system, and those systems are animated and sustained by that circulation.

Looking at the [[id:e61736a1-36c3-4efb-af9c-6ee79ba81f73][Fediverse]], we can say that these investments are in a sense of preserving ethics, cultivating them in community, and doing it ([[id:7a54056a-9b86-475e-ad66-887f3af30fe7][using social media]]) "right:" the desire is routed into a system that generates it into an [[id:81ff003b-b58c-4a24-81a7-a15d8f8c3d8c][affect of ethicalness]], which is shaped by the [[id:dd4bde9c-a89b-42fc-a125-df0b23fa0beb][feeling rules of the Fediverse]].

On [[id:609e5bb9-dac4-4f0a-9544-956d0484c525][Twitter]], the same desire is captured the enjoyment of competing for visibility, and the system regulates toward that [[id:a4827df1-8271-419e-8558-c8c38fdd4cb4][affective of virality]].
