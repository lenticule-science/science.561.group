A ***liberal university town*** is a
[town](id:6eee0a82-2648-43c3-b9cd-618882983baa) with a [liberal
university](id:43f2c2e1-eb56-4f49-a6de-b9a6c212d115), which usually
strongly [influences](id:a9f58f35-38d6-4309-8958-c5c5f209738f) the [town
culture](id:a6b34d54-5257-49fb-bd08-30f8dff46ea3).
