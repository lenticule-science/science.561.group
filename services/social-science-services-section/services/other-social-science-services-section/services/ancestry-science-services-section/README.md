# Ancestry Science Services Section

The Ancestry Science Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for
ancestry and kinship-memory science: the dead as fully-settled sections of
the community sheaf, veneration as witness-set maintenance, grief as the
re-gluing of the living sheaf around a removed open, and the deliberate
persistence-records (winter counts and kin) that keep a people's history
witnessed across time.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains may continue under `services/` as the section grows.

Start at the section's seed topic:
[`content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-seed-topic-ancestry-science.md`](./content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-seed-topic-ancestry-science.md)
— it states why the section is placed here, points to the founding theory
note, and indexes the prior estate material collected on stand-up.

**On Lakota-specific material**: this section engages Lakota kinship,
governance, and winter-count material by cross-link only. That material is
never copied or paraphrased here — it stays in place under its own
authority (`lakota-services-section`, the historical `organizing-as-lakota`
source preserved under Lakota science, and the campaign's own reserved use of
"winter count"), per house protocol. Historical code is evidence of prior
formalization, not current doctrine or an operating authority.
