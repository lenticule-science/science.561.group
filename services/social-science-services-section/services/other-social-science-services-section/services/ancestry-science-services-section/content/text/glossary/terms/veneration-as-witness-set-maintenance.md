[Veneration](./veneration-as-witness-set-maintenance.md), in this
section's founding sense, is **witness-set maintenance**: the practice of
tending graves, speaking names, and keeping stories in circulation that
keeps an [ancestor's settled section](./ancestor-as-settled-section.md)
witnessed rather than letting it decay toward unread. A settled section's
charges do not disappear when nobody attends to them, but they stop being
*readable* — the structure is there, but nothing keeps drawing new
arrows to it. Veneration is the deliberate practice of drawing those
arrows back.

This reframes what veneration practices are actually doing, across very
different traditions: naming a child after a grandparent, an annual
memorial, an oral genealogy recited at a gathering, a grave tended each
season — all of these are, on this account, archival acts. They re-cite a
settled section so it stays part of what the living community actively
reads, rather than only what it once contained. The founding note
describes the practice directly as "archival science performed as love."

This is deliberately not a claim that veneration practices are *reducible*
to archiving in a narrow technical sense — many veneration practices carry
ceremonial, spiritual, and relational weight that a text-based archive
cannot hold and should not attempt to reproduce. See
[ceremonial transmission](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/ceremonial-transmission.md)
and
[knowledge sovereignty](../../../../../../../education-science-services-section/services/knowledge-transmission-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/knowledge-sovereignty.md)
for the estate's existing treatment of exactly that limit: some
transmission is relationally restricted, not withheld for lack of a
better recording technology, and this section inherits that principle
rather than restating it.

The deepest worked example of veneration-as-witness-maintenance already
running in the estate is the elder-hour speaker-recording program; see
[elder-hour multiplier](../../../../../../../education-science-services-section/services/knowledge-transmission-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/elder-hour-multiplier.md).
