# Lineage

In [1958](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/1958ce.md), [Fritz Heider](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fritz-heider.md) [posited](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/postulating.md) that [humans](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/homo-sapiens.md) act as [naïve scientists](../../../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/naive-scientist.md), [doing](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/doing.md) causal analysis on the [human behavior](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/human-behavior--002392.md) they [experience](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/experiencing.md). In Heider's analysis, attribution assigned beahvior to either [dispositional cause](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dispositional-cause.md) or [situational cause](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/situational-cause.md). This [bifurcated classification](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/29/2025-08-29-classification-terms-bifurcated-classification.md) formalized [attributing](../../../../../../../../../sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/attributing.md) as [conditioning](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/24/2025-08-24-tekodynamics-terms-conditioning-conditioning.md) an [act](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/tekodynamics-concepts-act-act.md) within a causal schema.

In [1965](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/1965ce.md), [Edward E. Jones](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/29/2025-08-29-edward-e-jones.md) and [Keith Davis](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/encoding/references/archive/people/keith-davis/keith-davis--002439.org.md) [authored](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/authoring--002211.md) [Correspondent Inference Theory](../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/correspondent-inference-theory--002275.md), which refined Heider's model by focusing on [intentionality](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intentionality.md). In their analysis, attributing occurs when [observers](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/observer.md) [infer](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inferring.md) that a behavior [correlates](../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/correlating.md) with a stable trait in the actor.
