Autonomous direct action is direct action carried out through community
initiative, shared principles, and voluntary coordination rather than
through state or nonprofit command [@madrprinciples2020].

Within emergent disaster response, the term matters because it names a
method of acting without waiting for permission while still orienting
that action toward collective survival and accountability to impacted
people [@madrmission2019; @madrprinciples2020].
