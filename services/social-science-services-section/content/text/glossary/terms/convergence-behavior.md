Convergence behavior is the movement of people, information, and resources toward disaster sites [@twiggmosel2017; @wachtendorf2010].

The term matters because convergence is not only a problem of crowding.
It is also a source of labor, knowledge, supplies, and initiative.
Grassroots disaster response depends on learning how to turn convergence into useful coordination instead of treating it only as interference.

- [Spontaneous Volunteers](spontaneous-volunteers.md) - people who often arrive through convergence behavior
