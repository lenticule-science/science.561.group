Prevention Through Deterrence is the enforcement doctrine formalized in the *Border Patrol Strategic Plan: 1994 and Beyond* (approved by INS Commissioner Doris Meissner, August 1994, after Silvestre Reyes's unilateral El Paso blockade the year before). Its logic: concentrate agents, fencing, and surveillance at urban crossing points so that crossing becomes possible only through remote, lethal terrain — raising the "risk" of entry until risk itself deters.

The doctrine's innovation was to make the desert the enforcement instrument. Jason De León's ethnography names it precisely: the strategy "weaponized the natural environment against migrants." No officer kills; the terrain does; the policy selected the terrain. Consequences are documented at scale: a roughly twenty-fold increase in Tucson-sector deaths between 1990 and 2005; at least 7,800 recorded crossing deaths nationwide 1998–2019; more than 4,500 documented on the Arizona border across 25 years. The mechanism runs independent of migration volume — July 2026 was the deadliest month in two years on the Arizona line while apprehensions sat at historic lows.

For this glossary: PTD is the purest case of an exclusion-power exercised through deniable mediation — the administrative twin of the ration-withholding in the Code of Indian Offenses (starvation as ceremony enforcement) and the ancestor logic of the wall-era waiver regime documented in [real-id-act-section-102-waiver](../../../../services/law-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/real-id-act-section-102-waiver.md) and [roosevelt-reservation](../../../../services/law-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/roosevelt-reservation.md), where 34 laws can be set aside per project because the filing category ("border security") outranks every other category of harm. It also generates its own counting crisis: the deaths are recorded by county medical examiners and volunteer groups, not the agency whose policy produces them, and the identification infrastructure (the Colibrí Center, dissolved December 2025) is left to civil society and can simply lapse.
