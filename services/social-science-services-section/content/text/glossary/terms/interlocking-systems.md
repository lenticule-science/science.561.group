*Interlocking systems* is the framing the [Combahee River Collective](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/combahee-river-collective.md) introduces in its 1977 statement: "the major systems of oppression are interlocking.
The synthesis of these oppressions creates the conditions of our lives."
The single word "interlocking" is doing the analytical work that everything downstream — [Patricia Hill Collins](../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/patricia-hill-collins.md)'s [matrix-of-domination](matrix-of-domination.md), Crenshaw's [intersectionality](../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/intersectionality.md), the [`kyriarchism`](../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/queer/theories/kyriarchism/_index.md) analysis — extends.

The framing refuses two alternatives that were available in 1977 and are still available now:

- The **additive** framing: race, class, gender, and sexuality are separate systems whose effects on a given person can be summed.
  On this reading, a Black working-class lesbian experiences the sum of race oppression + class oppression + gender oppression + sexual-orientation oppression, each independently.
  Combahee refuses this: the systems do not operate independently and their effects are not summable.
- The **hierarchical** framing: one system is primary and the others are derivative.
  Class-first Marxism, race-first Black nationalism, and gender-first radical feminism all take this form.
  Each picks out one axis as fundamental and treats the others as superstructural or secondary.
  Combahee refuses this too: no axis is primary; the systems are interlocked.

The positive content of "interlocking" is that the systems condition each other in their operation.
Capitalism in the United States operates through racial differentiation; patriarchy operates through racial and class structures that distribute its effects unevenly; heterosexual normativity operates through racial, class, and gender categories that determine which families and which sexualities count as legitimate.
The systems are not separable analytical objects whose interactions can be modeled after the fact; they are mutually constitutive.

The framing has had a long downstream life.
[`Matrix of domination`](matrix-of-domination.md) is Collins's sociological systematization.
[`Intersectionality`](../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/intersectionality.md) is the legal-theoretical translation.
[`Kyriarchism`](../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/queer/theories/kyriarchism/_index.md) is the same framing arrived at from feminist theology.
[bell hooks](../../../../../../../lib/legacy/2026-07-31-lenticule-science-corpus/animating/content/becoming/doing/working/governing/content/individual/content/agent/actuating/content/actuator/embodying/content/body/integrating/content/drone/swarming/content/swarm/content/social/_archive/sociology/content/critical-theory/theories/feminism/theories/black-feminism/figures/bell-hooks.md)'s phrase "imperialist white-supremacist capitalist patriarchy" is the same framing in essay-theoretical register: the modifiers are concatenated to refuse the move that would let any one of them be set aside as secondary.
