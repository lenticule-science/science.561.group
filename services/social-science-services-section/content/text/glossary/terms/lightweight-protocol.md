A lightweight protocol is a minimal procedural form that coordinates people and tasks without hardening response into a rigid command hierarchy [@landau2022; @occupysandyorientation2012].

Within emergent disaster response, the term matters because horizontal organizing still requires forms, guidelines, check-ins, role assignments, contact numbers, and reporting expectations.
Landau characterizes Occupy Sandy's volunteer training as a loose protocol, and the Occupy Sandy field orientation shows what that looked like in practice: tasks, roles, point people, forms, guidelines, timelines, and hotlines [@landau2022; @occupysandyorientation2012].

A lightweight protocol does not eliminate improvisation.
It gives improvisation enough structure to remain legible to others.
