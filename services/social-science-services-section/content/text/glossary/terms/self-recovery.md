Self-recovery is the process by which disaster-affected households repair, build, or rebuild through their own choices, labor, and resources, sometimes with outside support [@twigg2021; @ahmedparrack2022].

The term matters because it centers household and community agency in recovery instead of treating reconstruction as something wholly delivered from outside.
In shelter practice, support for self-recovery usually means helping people help themselves while preserving choice and local control [@pathways2022].
