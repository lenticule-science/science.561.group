**General intellect** is Marx's term, appearing in the famous "Fragment on Machines" of the [Grundrisse](marx1857) (composed 1857–1858, first published 1939–1941, English translation [1973](marx1973)), for the socialized knowledge, scientific capacity, technical skill, and accumulated cooperative intelligence of humanity considered as a *direct productive force*.
The phrase appears in a brief but consequential passage where Marx anticipates that as production becomes increasingly machine-mediated and the machinery embodies increasing scientific and technical knowledge, the operative productive force ceases to be the labor time of the individual worker and becomes the *general intellect* of the social cooperation that produced the machinery and the scientific knowledge it embodies.

The passage was relatively neglected in the Marxist tradition until the Italian operaismo and post-operaismo traditions of the 1960s–2000s seized on it as the foundational textual warrant for their analysis of contemporary cognitive capitalism.
In the post-operaismo reading, the conditions Marx anticipated for an indefinite future have arrived: the general intellect *is* directly productive in the contemporary economy, and the labor theory of value's individual-labor-time measure is correspondingly in crisis.

## What the Fragment says

The "Fragment on Machines" — Notebook VII of the Grundrisse, pages 690–712 in the standard Penguin edition — argues:

1. As capitalist production develops, the share of *living labor* in the production of any given commodity falls and the share of *dead labor* (capital, machinery, scientific knowledge embodied in the production apparatus) rises.
  The worker increasingly tends and supervises the machinery rather than directly producing the product.
2. The scientific knowledge and technical capability embodied in the machinery is not the product of any individual worker; it is the product of the *general intellect* — the cumulative socialized knowledge of humanity, developed through cooperative scientific, technical, and educational activity that vastly exceeds any individual capitalist's capacity to organize.
3. As this development proceeds, the labor theory of value — which measures value by socially necessary labor time — becomes increasingly incongruent with the actual productive process, in which the relevant productive force is the general intellect rather than the measurable labor time of identifiable workers.
4. This incongruence prepares the conditions in which capitalist measurement of value through labor time becomes obsolete, and a different organization of social production becomes both necessary and possible.

The passage is brief, suggestive, and not integrated into Marx's mature *Capital*-period theoretical apparatus, where the labor theory of value is deployed without the qualifications the Fragment gestures toward.
The interpretive question of how to relate the Fragment to *Capital* is one of the long-running debates of twentieth-century Marxism.

## The post-operaismo reading

The Italian operaismo tradition (Mario Tronti, Romano Alquati, the early autonomist Marxists of the 1960s) and the post-operaismo tradition that succeeded it (Paolo Virno [virno2004], Toni Negri, Michael Hardt [hardt-negri-2000], Carlo Vercellone [vercellone2007], Maurizio Lazzarato) made the general intellect a central category.
The reading:

- The conditions Marx anticipated have arrived.
  Contemporary production is *increasingly* dependent on the general intellect — the educational, scientific, communicative, affective, and cooperative capacities of socialized humanity — as a direct productive force.
- Capital appropriates value generated by the general intellect without organizing or paying for the activity that produces it.
  The university, the household, the social network, the language community, the broader cultural-cooperative life of society develops the capacities the firm subsequently exploits.
- The labor theory of value's measure of value by individual labor time is correspondingly in crisis: the value of contemporary informational and immaterial commodities cannot be reduced to the time any identifiable worker spent producing them.
- The political consequence is the emergence of the *multitude* — the cooperative-cognitive subject that produces the general intellect and could, in principle, reorganize production around its own self-organization rather than around capital's appropriation.

Vercellone's [cognitive-capitalism program](vercellone2007) is the most developed elaboration: he periodizes capitalism into mercantile, industrial, and cognitive regimes, with cognitive capitalism the regime in which the general intellect is the dominant productive force.
This is the framework against which his account of [real-subsumption](real-subsumption.md)'s contemporary partial reversion to a formal-subsumption-like configuration is articulated.

## Interpretive controversies

The post-operaismo appropriation of the Fragment is contested:

- **Textual fidelity** — the Fragment is a single brief passage in unpublished notebooks; reading it as the foundational text of an entire theoretical program may overweight its place in Marx's thinking.
- **Empirical reach** — the cognitive-capitalism thesis treats the knowledge-worker experience as paradigmatic when industrial, agricultural, extractive, logistical, and care work continue to occupy the majority of the global workforce.
- **Political-strategic** — the multitude as the post-operaismo subject of historical change has been criticized as too diffuse, too celebratory of capitalism's actual organization of cognitive labor, and too disconnected from organized political vehicles capable of contesting capital's power.

The general-intellect concept is, on the post-operaismo tradition's own self-presentation (Virno, Hardt-Negri, Vercellone), the operative theoretical vocabulary of cognitive-capitalism analysis, and is widely cited as such across contemporary critical literature on work, platform economies, and informational production.

## Distinguished from

- **Labor power** — labor power, in Marx's mature *Capital*-period vocabulary, is the individual worker's capacity to perform labor, sold to the capitalist through the wage relation.
  The general intellect is the *socialized* knowledge and cooperative capacity that exceeds any individual labor power; it is not sold (typically not even owned by anyone in particular) and is not measurable in labor time.
- **Human capital** — human capital theory (Becker and the Chicago school) treats knowledge and skill as individual *investments* whose returns accrue to the individual through wage premia.
  The general intellect treats knowledge and skill as *socialized* properties whose returns accrue to capital through the appropriation of cooperatively produced capacity.
  The two frameworks are theoretically opposed despite descriptively addressing similar phenomena.
- **Cognitive labor** — cognitive labor names the specific activity (programming, designing, writing, teaching) through which individuals contribute to the general intellect; the general intellect is the cumulative socialized capacity their activity produces.
  Cognitive labor is the act; the general intellect is the result.
- **[Immaterial labor](immaterial-labor.md)** — immaterial labor names a particular kind of contemporary labor whose outputs are informational, communicative, and affective; the general intellect is the broader socialized knowledge-cooperative substrate on which immaterial labor draws.
  Immaterial labor is the productive activity; the general intellect is the social-cooperative resource it both draws on and replenishes.

## Within this library

The general intellect is the foundational Marxian textual reference for the [immaterial-labor-and-post-operaismo-survey](../texts/immaterial-labor-and-post-operaismo-survey.md).
It supplies the substrate on which [immaterial-labor](immaterial-labor.md) draws, the ground on which Vercellone develops his contemporary qualification of [real-subsumption](real-subsumption.md), and the social-cooperative productive force whose political analysis the post-operaismo tradition develops as its alternative to capitalist organization of work.

General intellect is [Karl Marx](../../general/people/karl-marx.md)'s term, from the *Grundrisse* (1858), for the collective knowledge, skills, and social cooperation that become embedded in productive forces.
[Marx](../../general/people/karl-marx.md) argued that as production depends less on individual [labor](./labor.md) time and more on the general state of science and technology — on knowledge that is inherently social — the [labor](./labor.md) theory of value enters crisis.
Value becomes harder to measure by time when the productive force is collective intelligence rather than individual exertion.

[Operaismo](/sociology/terms/operaismo.md) and [autonomist](/sociology/schools/autonomism/index.md) thinkers — particularly Paolo Virno and Antonio Negri — revived and extended the concept.
Under what they term cognitive [capitalism](../../../../services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/capitalism.md), the general intellect is no longer fixed in machinery (as [Marx](../../general/people/karl-marx.md) primarily imagined) but lives in the communicative, affective, and cognitive capacities of workers themselves.
Knowledge, language, and cooperation are both the means of production and the product.
This makes the general intellect a primary site of value extraction and of struggle: capital must capture and enclose what is inherently common.

The concept connects to questions of [knowledge sovereignty](/sociology/terms/knowledge-sovereignty.md): if the general intellect is produced collectively, then its private [enclosure](../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/enclosure.md) — through intellectual [property](./property.md), platform monopolies, and proprietary data — is a form of [primitive accumulation](../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/primitive-accumulation.md) directed at the [commons](./commons.md) of shared knowledge.
