## Status and boundary

This is a **working paper, not a theorem, system specification, forecast, or
policy mandate**. It proposes a measurement programme: whether changes in a
society's accessible net-energy surplus and in the organization of its
attention are jointly predictive of its capacity to perform expensive,
error-correcting inference. It introduces no software package, ontology,
service, economic price, or institutional rule.

The paper uses *civilization* only as a descriptive scale for datasets. It
does not identify a nation, enterprise, person, or software system with a
biological organism. "Metabolism", "distal bypass", and "semiotic foam" are
analytic handles whose empirical and formal links must be earned separately.

In particular, this paper does **not** require an author-exclusion rule, a
fiction of independent assays, or any "R2-canonical" status. Provenance,
direct replication, internal tests, external replication, and criticism are
different evidence channels; none is excluded merely by authorship, and no
such label settles a claim.

## The conjecture

Let a study period and population have:

- `N`: accessible **net** energy after energy-system investment, measured with
  an explicit EROEI boundary and uncertainty interval;
- `A`: protected attention-time available for sustained work, measured as time
  and interruption structure rather than inferred from platform activity;
- `C`: coordination load, measured as concrete handoffs, reconciliation work,
  and delay, not as a metaphorical entropy value;
- `Q`: a preregistered outcome for error-correcting, long-horizon inference
  (for example, calibration improvement, replication yield, or successful
  correction of a known defect).

**Conjecture (joint-surplus hypothesis).** Holding material, educational,
health, demographic, and institutional covariates explicit, `Q` is better
explained by the joint availability of `N` and `A`, and by a manageable `C`,
than by gross energy throughput alone. A transition can therefore be
temporarily cognition-constraining even when its eventual energy source is
low-carbon, if construction, maintenance, and coordination consume the
available surplus and protected attention.

This is deliberately weaker than "energy determines thought." Net energy can
be converted into destructive work, leisure, care, surveillance, education, or
maintenance; the conversion path, distribution, and institutions are causal
variables, not residual noise.

### Distal bypass as the proposed mechanism

Here **distal bypass** means an infrastructure-supported displacement of an
immediate maintenance burden from the actor currently reasoning to a durable
external process: food preparation, sanitation, tools, records, libraries,
reliable energy service, or a well-maintained protocol. It is not a claim that
cost disappears. It changes *where*, *when*, and *to whom* the cost is charged.

The hypothesis is that durable bypass can enlarge `A` and reduce some forms of
`C`, making longer error-correction loops practical. Conversely, a system can
appear efficient while merely exporting maintenance costs to an unobserved
labor force, ecosystem, future period, or brittle supply chain. Such export
does not count as demonstrated cognitive surplus until the accounting boundary
includes it.

The closest local formal analogue is narrow: Obsmetrologia's attention module
models finite irreversible selection among priced obstructions. Its theorems
license statements about that discrete budget model only. Treating a national
energy account or a human attention ecology as the same carrier is a new
empirical modelling step.

## Semiotic metabolism: a constrained synthesis

The current semiotic-vitrimer and semiotic-foam designs motivate a second,
more tentative picture. A useful knowledge system may require both:

1. a **mutable working phase**, where uncertain material can be compared,
   recombined, and corrected; and
2. a **durable record phase**, where settled results reduce repeated search and
   preserve provenance.

Call the circulation between these phases *semiotic metabolism*. The term does
not imply that documents are cells or that an institution has a metabolism in
the biological sense. It names the measurable trade-off between present
recomputation/reconciliation and durable, revisitable external memory.

The failure modes are symmetric. Too little durable record makes each inquiry
pay repeatedly for lost context. Too much premature fixation makes revision
and local adaptation costly. The design notes' "ossification" language is
helpful as a hypothesis generator precisely because they state a frontier:
graphic cycle rank is not rigidity percolation, and assumed thresholds are not
measurements. No physical result therefore transfers automatically to a
document or organization.

## What Landauer and free-energy descent contribute

Landauer's bound is a warning against category errors. It applies to a defined
physical operation with a specified initial distribution and bath; it is not a
conversion factor from a meeting, proof, or contradiction to joules. The local
Landauer ledger is likewise a finite cohomological bookkeeping device, not an
ambient thermodynamics of society.

The proved Lyapunov results are still useful as **structural examples**:
monotone descent needs a declared potential and a declared transition regime.
For the joint-surplus hypothesis, that discipline means reporting every
potential separately—energy investment, attention interruption, reconciliation
work, error rate, and record maintenance—rather than collapsing them into a
single "civilizational free energy."

## Falsifiers and discriminating observations

The conjecture loses support if any of the following survive adequate
measurement and sensitivity analysis:

1. Across comparable cases, gross throughput predicts `Q` as well as or better
   than net energy after consistent EROEI boundaries, while `N` adds no
   explanatory or predictive value.
2. After pre-registered controls for education, health, material security,
   inequality, and institutional quality, protected attention `A` and
   coordination load `C` add no explanatory value for the specified
   error-correcting outcomes.
3. A transition period with materially lower measured `N` and `A` reliably
   improves `Q` without a compensating reduction in `C`, a measurement-boundary
   correction, or a composition change in who performs the work.
4. Interventions that create genuine distal bypass (including its maintenance
   cost) do not increase protected attention or improve the selected `Q`
   outcome relative to matched alternatives.
5. Durable external records neither reduce recomputation nor improve revision
   quality, or their maintenance cost consistently outweighs both benefits.
6. Any proposed bridge from the finite Lean carriers to physical heat,
   psychometrics, or macroeconomic EROEI fails a typed measurement model with
   explicit units, boundary conditions, and an independent replication path.

## Minimum research protocol

Use a multi-level panel or matched intervention design. Publish EROEI boundary
diagrams, energy-quality adjustments, uncertainty ranges, attention sampling
method, handoff/reconciliation definitions, outcome preregistration, missing
data policy, and all maintenance and externalized costs. Report negative as
well as positive results. Keep the formal-model tests separate from empirical
tests: Lean can verify a declared finite carrier; it cannot validate the
carrier's interpretation as an economy or a mind.
