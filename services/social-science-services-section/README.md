# Social Science Services Section

The Social Science Services Section is the direct Lenticule Science home for
research on people, institutions, collective activity, and social relations.
It sits directly under the Science Services Section; social science is not
owned by agent science or philosophy.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

## Classification contract

The direct children of this section follow the OECD *Fields of Research and
Development* (FORD) classification in the *Frascati Manual 2015*, table 2.2.
That standard defines nine second-level social-science fields:

| FORD | field | Repository section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5.1 | Psychology and cognitive sciences | Not instantiated here; psychology presently remains with medical science pending a broader science-tree review. |
| 5.2 | Economics and business | `economics-and-business-science-services-section` |
| 5.3 | Education | `education-science-services-section` |
| 5.4 | Sociology | `sociology-science-services-section` |
| 5.5 | Law | `law-science-services-section` |
| 5.6 | Political science | `political-science-services-section` |
| 5.7 | Social and economic geography | `social-and-economic-geography-science-services-section` |
| 5.8 | Media and communications | Not instantiated until it has material. |
| 5.9 | Other social sciences | `other-social-science-services-section` |

Every direct child is therefore a recognized research field and uses the
`-science-services-section` suffix. Topics such as markets, money, work,
organization, military studies, ancestry, and ludics are narrower subjects;
they do not become peer fields merely because content exists for them.

Use the Australian and New Zealand Standard Research Classification (ANZSRC)
Fields of Research hierarchy to refine a FORD field when a finer placement is
needed. ANZSRC is a three-level research classification aligned with the OECD
R&D definitions. Do not use ISIC for this tree: ISIC classifies economic
activities, not fields of knowledge. Do not use ISCED-F unless the thing being
classified is an education programme or qualification.

Cross-disciplinary material gets one owner according to its main
research question and may link to other fields. A cross-cutting topic is not a
reason to invent another top-level bucket.

It does **not** report a completed field experiment or deployed service. Its source of truth is the Business Science document above, not a copied paper.

## Humanities boundary

FORD separates humanities and the arts (class 6) from social sciences (class
5). Black philosophy therefore lives under `philosophy-services-section`, and
public and social history live under
`history-and-archaeology-science-services-section`; none is a residual social
science bucket.
