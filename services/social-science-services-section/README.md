# Social Science Services Section

The Social Science Services Section is the direct Lenticule Science home for
the study of social relations, institutions, collective activity, and the
historical organization of human life. It sits directly under the Science
Services Section; social science is not owned by agent science or philosophy.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/`.

`black-philosophy-services-section` is the home for Black philosophical
thought. Its present specialized services are the Black radical tradition and
abolition. Those traditions are substantial parts of Black philosophy, but
they do not exhaust it; work on ethics, knowledge, personhood, labor, value,
freedom, aesthetics, metaphysics, religion, and other questions can belong to
Black philosophy without first being classified as critical theory.
