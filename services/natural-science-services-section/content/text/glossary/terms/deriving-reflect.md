- [[postulate]]:: It must be [[derive|derived]] that [[oldreflex]](n), induced by iterative [[relationality]], necessarily [[derive|derives]] further relational structure by [[induce|inducing]] the reflection of prior reflexive relations.
- [[derivation]]:: As each [[oldreflex]](n) is induced from [[oldreflex]](n-1), the act of reflecting must [[derive]] the ordered relational layering of reflexive depth, constructing [[oldreflect]](p, n) as the p-th reflexive reflection at depth n.
- [[example]]::
    - reflect(0, n) is reflex(reflex(n-1))
    - reflect(1, n) is reflex(reflect(0, n-1))
    - reflect(2, n) is reflex(reflect(1, n-1))
    - reflect(p, n) is reflex(reflect(p-1, n))
- [[relational expression]]:: [[oldreflect]](p, n) is [[oldreflex]]([[oldreflect]](p-1, n))
