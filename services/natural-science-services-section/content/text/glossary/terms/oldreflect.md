- [[postulate]]:: [[oldreflex]](n) must [[derive]] the [[relation]] of [[oldreflex]](n-1) with itself, inducing the stabilization of iterative reflexivity through relational composition.
- [[derivation]]:: Reflexing [[oldreflex]](n) derives a reflected structure over reflexive depth, constructing coherent relational layering across iterations.
- [[example]]::
	- reflect(0, n) is reflex(reflex(n-1))
	- reflect(1, n) is reflex(reflect(0, n-1))
	- reflect(p, n) is reflex(reflect(p-1, n))
- [[relational expression]]:: [[oldreflect]](p, n) is [[oldreflex]]([[oldreflect]](p-1, n))
