- [[summary]]:: The **domain of relating** is the minimal relational structure induced by the tension between [[relate]] and [[triage/00-probably-redundant/20250502-relational-notes/fffstem/relating/acts/unrelate]].

It contains the acts of inclusion and exclusion, and the act that holds them in mutual tension.

This domain is not primitive — it is induced, triadic, and minimal.

## Members

- relating  
- unrelating  
- relating(relating, unrelating)

## Properties

- Triadic: exactly three elements  
- Induced: does not exist without opposing acts  
- Minimal: contains only what is required to generate structure

## Expression

domain of relating := {relating, unrelating, relating(relating, unrelating)}
