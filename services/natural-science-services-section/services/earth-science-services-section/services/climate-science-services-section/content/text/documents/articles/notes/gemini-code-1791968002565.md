## 1. Executive Thesis & The Structural Lead-Time Arbitrage Problem

Operational Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) systems (ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, NCEP HRRR) and first-generation reanalysis-initialized AI weather models (GraphCast, Pangu-Weather, GenCast) suffer from two fundamental structural bottlenecks that create systematic mispricings in intraday and day-ahead weather prediction markets (such as Kalshi):

1. **The 6-to-12-Hour Synoptic Assimilation & Compute Latency Seam:**
   Global 4D-Var and hybrid EnKF-4DVar analyses execute on fixed 6-hour synoptic windows (00Z, 06Z, 12Z, 18Z), ingesting observations up to 3 hours past the synoptic cutoff and requiring another 2 to 3 hours of supercomputing and distribution time ([WeatherNext 3, 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/papers/weathernext_3.pdf)). Furthermore, traditional 4D-Var assimilation ingests less than 5% of available raw satellite and in-situ data due to computational thinning and observation operator linearity constraints ([ECMWF GraphDOP, 2026](https://events.ecmwf.int/event/488/contributions/5669/attachments/3655/6158/ECMWF-ESA-WS_Boucher.pdf)). Even rapid-refresh regional models (HRRR) suffer a 45-to-65-minute latency from observation timestamp to public GRIB2 availability.
2. **Grid-Cell Volume Averaging vs. Point-Sensor Metrological Settlement:**
   Pure data-driven models trained on smooth reanalysis grids (such as ERA5 at $0.25^\circ \approx 25\,\text{km}$) suffer from spectral bias—smoothing out sharp frontal gradients and underestimating 99th-percentile convective precipitation by 20–35% ([AI Weather Forecasting Review, 2026](https://www.articsledge.com/post/ai-weather-forecasting)). More critically, prediction markets do not settle on grid-cell averages; they settle on *a discrete digital register inside a specific station's Acquisition Control Unit (ACU)* (e.g., an ASOS HO-1088 Platinum Resistance Thermometer at $z = 2.0\,\text{m}$ over a specific airport turf patch), governed by sub-grid surface energy partitioning, micro-frontal density currents, sensor thermal inertia, and non-invertible Fahrenheit-to-Celsius quantization cascades.

To generate strictly superior probability distributions 1 to 6+ hours before market consensus adjusts, our architecture formulates a continuous-time physical and statistical operator chain:

$$\mathcal{P}_{\text{settle}}\left(S \in B_k \;\middle|\; \mathcal{Y}_{1:t_{\text{now}}}\right) = \int \mathbb{I}\Big( \mathcal{M}_{\text{contract}}\big(\mathbf{X}_{1:L}\big) \in B_k \Big) \, d\mathbb{P}_{\text{SDA}}\left(\mathbf{X}_{1:L} \;\middle|\; \mathcal{Y}_{1:t_{\text{now}}}, \mathcal{R}_{\text{PDE}}(\mathbf{X}_{1:L}) = \mathbf{0}\right)$$

## 3. Classical & Mesoscale Physical Foundations (Governing PDEs)

### 3.1 Compressible Non-Hydrostatic Euler Equations in Exner–Potential Coordinates
Let $\mathbf{x} = (x, y, z)$ denote local Cartesian coordinates centered at the target station, with velocity vector $\mathbf{v} = (u, v, w)$, dry air density $\rho_d$, water vapor mixing ratio $q_v$, cloud liquid/ice condensate $q_c = q_l + q_i$, precipitating hydrometeor mixing ratio $q_r = q_{\text{rain}} + q_{\text{snow}} + q_{\text{graupel}}$, Exner pressure $\pi = (p / p_0)^{R_d / c_{pd}}$, and density potential temperature $\theta_\rho = \theta \frac{1 + (R_v / R_d) q_v}{1 + q_v + q_c + q_r} \approx \theta(1 + 0.608 q_v - q_c - q_r)$:

* **3D Non-Hydrostatic Momentum Conservation:**
  $$\frac{\partial \mathbf{v}}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{v} + 2\boldsymbol{\Omega} \times \mathbf{v} = -c_{pd}\theta_\rho \nabla \pi - g\hat{\mathbf{k}} + \frac{1}{\rho_d}\nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{turb}}$$

* **Compressible Exner Pressure Continuity:**
  $$\frac{\partial \pi}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla \pi + \frac{R_d \pi}{c_{vd}} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}) = \frac{R_d \pi}{c_{vd} \theta_\rho} \dot{\Theta}_{\text{diabatic}}$$

* **Thermodynamic Virtual Potential Temperature Conservation:**
  $$\frac{\partial \theta_v}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla \theta_v = -\frac{1}{\rho c_p \pi} \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}_{\text{rad}} + \frac{L_v}{c_p \pi}(C_{\text{cond}} - E_{\text{evap}}) + \frac{L_f}{c_p \pi}(F_{\text{frz}} - M_{\text{melt}}) - \nabla \cdot \overline{\mathbf{v}'\theta_v'}$$

* **Multi-Species Hydrometeor Continuity ($k \in \{v, l, i, \text{rain}, \text{snow}, \text{graupel}\}$):**
  $$\frac{\partial q_k}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla q_k - \frac{1}{\rho_d}\frac{\partial (\rho_d V_{t,k} q_k)}{\partial z} = S_{\text{micro}, k}(\mathbf{q}, T, w) - \nabla \cdot \overline{\mathbf{v}'q_k'}$$
  where $V_{t,k}$ is the mass-weighted terminal fall speed of hydrometeor species $k$.

### 3.2 Coupled Force-Restore Surface Energy Balance (SEB) & Soil-Canopy Thermodynamics
The skin temperature $T_s(t)$ and top-layer/root-zone volumetric soil moisture $\theta_{g1}(t)$ (top $5\,\text{cm}$) and $\theta_{g2}(t)$ ($1\,\text{m}$ reservoir) evolve according to the **Noilhan–Planton / Deardorff Force-Restore Differential System**:

1. **Prognostic Skin Temperature ODE:**
   $$C_{\text{eff}}(\theta_{g1}) \frac{dT_s}{dt} = (1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}}) \text{SW}_{\downarrow}(t) + \varepsilon_s \text{LW}_{\downarrow}(t) - \varepsilon_s \sigma T_s(t)^4 - H(t) - LE(t) - \frac{2\pi C_{\text{eff}}}{\tau_{\text{day}}}\big(T_s(t) - T_{\text{deep},2}(t)\big)$$
   where $\tau_{\text{day}} = 86,400\,\text{s}$ and the effective thermal heat capacity per unit area $C_{\text{eff}}$ depends on soil thermal inertia $I_{\text{soil}}(\theta_{g1}) = \sqrt{\lambda_{\text{soil}}(\theta_{g1}) \, c_{\text{soil}}(\theta_{g1})}$:
   $$C_{\text{eff}}(\theta_{g1}) = \frac{1}{2} I_{\text{soil}}(\theta_{g1}) \sqrt{\frac{\tau_{\text{day}}}{\pi}}$$

2. **Deep Substrate Temperature Evolution:**
   $$\frac{dT_{\text{deep},2}}{dt} = \frac{1}{\tau_{\text{day}}}\big(T_s(t) - T_{\text{deep},2}(t)\big)$$

3. **Penman–Monteith / Jarvis–Stewart Latent & Sensible Heat Partitioning (The Bowen Ratio $\beta = H/LE$):**
   $$H(t) = -\rho_a c_p u_*(t) \theta_*(t) = \rho_a c_p \frac{T_s(t) - T_{\text{air}}(z_1)}{r_{ah}(u_*, L_{\text{MO}})}$$
   $$LE(t) = -\rho_a L_v u_*(t) q_*(t) = \rho_a L_v \frac{h_{\text{sfc}}(\theta_{g1}) \, q_{\text{sat}}\big(T_s(t), p_s\big) - q_v(z_1)}{r_{ah}(u_*, L_{\text{MO}}) + r_{\text{canopy}}\big(\text{SW}_{\downarrow}, \text{VPD}, \theta_{g2}, T_s\big)}$$
   where surface relative humidity follows Philip's thermodynamic soil matric potential relation:
   $$h_{\text{sfc}}(\theta_{g1}) = \frac{1}{2}\left[1 - \cos\left(\pi \frac{\theta_{g1}}{\theta_{\text{fc}}}\right)\right] \quad \text{for } \theta_{g1} < \theta_{\text{fc}}, \quad \text{and } 1 \text{ otherwise.}$$

### 3.3 Exact Businger–Dyer–Högström & Beljaars–Holtslag Monin–Obukhov Profiles
To diagnose the exact $2\text{-meter}$ temperature $T(z_{2\text{m}})$ and $10\text{-meter}$ wind speed $U(z_{10\text{m}})$ from skin temperature $T_s$ and boundary-layer state, define the dimensionless stability parameter $\zeta = z / L_{\text{MO}}$, where the Obukhov length is:

$$L_{\text{MO}} = -\frac{u_*^3 \bar{\theta}_v}{\kappa g (\overline{w'\theta_v'})_s} = \frac{u_*^2 \bar{\theta}_v}{\kappa g \theta_{v*}}$$

with von Kármán constant $\kappa = 0.40$, friction velocity $u_* = \kappa U(z_2) / \left[\ln(z_2 / z_{0m}) - \Psi_m(z_2 / L_{\text{MO}}) + \Psi_m(z_{0m} / L_{\text{MO}})\right]$, and surface scaling temperature $\theta_* = -H / (\rho_a c_p u_*)$.

The exact integrated stability correction functions $\Psi_m(\zeta)$ (momentum) and $\Psi_h(\zeta)$ (heat/moisture) across both regimes are:

* **Unstable Convective Daytime Regime ($\zeta < 0$, super-adiabatic solar heating):**
  Let $x = (1 - 16\zeta)^{1/4}$. Then by the Paulson–Businger–Dyer integration:
  $$\Psi_m(\zeta) = 2\ln\left(\frac{1 + x}{2}\right) + \ln\left(\frac{1 + x^2}{2}\right) - 2\arctan(x) + \frac{\pi}{2}, \qquad \Psi_h(\zeta) = 2\ln\left(\frac{1 + x^2}{2}\right)$$

* **Stable Nocturnal Inversion Regime ($\zeta \ge 0$, radiative cooling & wind decoupling):**
  By the Beljaars–Holtslag (1991) nonlinear drainage formulation (valid even under extreme stability $\zeta \gg 1$):
  $$\Psi_m(\zeta) = -\left[ a\zeta + b\left(\zeta - \frac{c}{d}\right)\exp(-d\zeta) + \frac{bc}{d} \right]$$
  $$\Psi_h(\zeta) = -\left[ \left(1 + \frac{2}{3}a\zeta\right)^{3/2} + b\left(\zeta - \frac{c}{d}\right)\exp(-d\zeta) + \frac{bc}{d} - 1 \right]$$
  with universal constants $a = 1.0$, $b = 0.667$, $c = 5.0$, $d = 0.35$.

Substituting $\Psi_h(\zeta)$ and the **Zilitinkevich Reynolds-number thermal roughness length** $z_{0h} = z_{0m} \exp\left(-k B^{-1}\right) = z_{0m} \exp\left(-0.13 \left(\frac{u_* z_{0m}}{\nu_{\text{air}}}\right)^{0.45}\right)$ yields the exact $2\text{-meter}$ temperature diagnostic equation:

$$T(z_{2\text{m}}) = T_s - \Gamma_d z_{2\text{m}} + \frac{\theta_*}{\kappa}\left[ \ln\left(\frac{z_{2\text{m}}}{z_{0h}}\right) - \Psi_h\left(\frac{z_{2\text{m}}}{L_{\text{MO}}}\right) + \Psi_h\left(\frac{z_{0h}}{L_{\text{MO}}}\right) \right]$$

## 4. Module A: Satellite Radiative Nowcasting & Cloud-Advection-Decay PDEs

### 4.1 Geostationary Ellipsoidal Parallax Correction & Slant-Shadow Intercept
For a geostationary satellite at sub-satellite longitude $\lambda_{\text{sat}}$ ($75.2^\circ\text{W}$ for GOES-16/19 East; $137.2^\circ\text{W}$ for GOES-18 West) and orbital radius $R_{\text{geo}} = 42,164.16\,\text{km}$ viewing a target station at geodetic coordinates $(\phi_s, \lambda_s)$, a cloud top at geometric altitude $z_{\text{top}}$ above the WGS-84 ellipsoid casts its shadow on the station only when the cloud is located at the **solar-parallax offset coordinate** $\mathbf{r}_{\text{cloud\_intercept}}(t)$:

$$\mathbf{r}_{\text{cloud\_intercept}}(t) = \mathbf{r}_{\text{station}} + z_{\text{top}}(t)\tan\big(\theta_0(t)\big)\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{sun}}(t) + z_{\text{top}}(t)\tan(\theta_{\text{sat}})\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{sat}}$$

where $\theta_0(t)$ is the instantaneous solar zenith angle, $\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{sun}}(t) = (\sin\phi_{\text{sun}}, \cos\phi_{\text{sun}})$ is the unit horizontal vector pointing toward the sun's azimuth $\phi_{\text{sun}}(t)$, and $\theta_{\text{sat}}$ is the satellite viewing zenith angle.

### 4.2 Multi-Spectral Retrieval of $\tau_c$, $r_e$, and Phase from GOES-R ABI
Using ABI Band 2 ($0.64\,\mu\text{m}$ reflectance $R_{0.64}$), Band 6 ($2.24\,\mu\text{m}$ reflectance $R_{2.24}$), Band 7 ($3.90\,\mu\text{m}$ radiance $I_{3.9}$), Band 11 ($8.4\,\mu\text{m}$ cloud-phase $T_{b,8.4}$), and Band 13 ($10.3\,\mu\text{m}$ clean window $T_{b,10.3}$):

1. **Thermodynamic Cloud Phase Discrimination:**
   Using the trispectral Brightness Temperature Difference $\text{BTD}_{8.4 - 10.3} = T_{b,8.4} - T_{b,10.3}$:
   $$\text{Phase} = \begin{cases} \text{Ice}(g_0 \approx 0.75, \rho_p = \rho_i), & T_{b,10.3} < 233\,\text{K} \text{ or } \text{BTD}_{8.4 - 10.3} > +0.5\,\text{K} \\ \text{Liquid}(g_0 \approx 0.85, \rho_p = \rho_w), & T_{b,10.3} > 268\,\text{K} \text{ and } \text{BTD}_{8.4 - 10.3} < -1.0\,\text{K} \\ \text{Mixed-Phase}, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

2. **Asymptotic Sobolev–Nakajima–King Optical Depth & Droplet Radius Inversion:**
   $$\tau_c(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{4 \mu_0 \mu_v}{3\big(1 - g(r_e)\big)} \left[ \frac{R_{0.64}^{\text{obs}}(\mathbf{r}, t) - R_{\text{sfc,clear}}(\mathbf{r}, \mu_0, \mu_v)}{1 - R_{0.64}^{\text{obs}}(\mathbf{r}, t) + \tilde{\alpha}_{\text{sph}}(\tau_c)} \right] \Phi_{\text{BRDF}}(\mu_0, \mu_v, \Delta\phi)$$
   $$\text{LWP}(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{2}{3}\rho_w \tau_c(\mathbf{r}, t) r_e(\mathbf{r}, t), \qquad 1 - \omega_0(r_e, 2.24\,\mu\text{m}) \propto \frac{4\pi \kappa_{\text{imag}}(2.24\,\mu\text{m})}{\lambda} r_e$$

### 4.3 Coupled Advection–Diffusion–Entrainment–Nucleation PDE for $\tau_c(\mathbf{r}, t)$
We evolve the 2D column optical depth $\tau_c(\mathbf{r}, t)$ forward over a 0-to-180-minute horizon via:

$$\frac{\partial \tau_c}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}}(\mathbf{r}, t) \cdot \nabla_h \tau_c = \kappa_{\text{turb}} \nabla_h^2 \tau_c + \mathcal{S}_{\text{nucleation}}(z_i, z_{\text{LCL}}, w_*) - \mathcal{D}_{\text{burnoff}}(w_e, \Delta q_v, \text{SW}_{\text{abs}})$$

* **Variational Optical Flow Functional for $\mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}}(\mathbf{r}, t)$:**
  $$\mathcal{J}(\mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}}, \mathcal{S}_{\text{net}}) = \iint_{\Omega} \left[ \left| \frac{\partial \tau_c}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}} \cdot \nabla_h \tau_c - \mathcal{S}_{\text{net}} \right|^2 + \lambda_{\text{flow}} \|\nabla_h \mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}}\|_F^2 + \lambda_{\text{prior}} \|\mathbf{v}_{\text{cloud}} - \mathbf{v}_{\text{wind}}(z_{\text{top}})\|_2^2 \right] dx\,dy$$

* **Cloud-Top Entrainment & Marine-Layer Burn-Off Rate ($\mathcal{D}_{\text{burnoff}}$):**
  $$\mathcal{D}_{\text{burnoff}}(\mathbf{r}, t) = \frac{3}{2\rho_w r_e} \left[ \rho_a w_e(t)\left(q_{\text{sat}}(T_{\text{top}}) + q_l - q_v(z_i^+)\right) + \frac{\int_{z_{\text{base}}}^{z_{\text{top}}} q_{\text{SW,abs}}(z)\,dz}{L_v} \right]_+$$
  where the turbulent entrainment velocity $w_e(t)$ across the capping inversion $\Delta\theta_{v,\text{inv}}$ follows the Lilly–Nicholls–Turton parameterization:
  $$w_e(t) = \frac{A_{\text{LNT}} \left( w_*^3 + A_{\text{rad}} w_{\text{rad}}^3 + 5 u_*^3 \right)}{\frac{g}{\theta_v}\Delta\theta_{v,\text{inv}} \, z_i(t)}, \qquad w_{\text{rad}}^3 = \frac{g}{\theta_v}\frac{\Delta F_{\text{LW,top}}}{\rho_a c_p} (z_{\text{top}} - z_{\text{base}})$$

* **Mixed-Layer Growth & Cumulus Initiation ($z_i \ge z_{\text{LCL}}$):**
  $$\frac{dz_i}{dt} = \frac{(1 + 2 A_{\text{LNT}}) \, H(t)}{\rho_a c_p \gamma_\theta z_i(t)} + w_{\text{sub}}(z_i), \qquad z_{\text{LCL}}(t) \approx \frac{c_p}{g}\big(T_{2\text{m}}(t) - T_{\text{LCL}}(t)\big) \approx 125 \big(T_{2\text{m}} - T_{d,2\text{m}}\big)$$

### 4.4 Exact Two-Stream Delta-Eddington Solution (Meador–Weaver Analytical Form)
With Delta-Eddington scaled parameters $\tau^* = (1 - \omega_0 g^2)\tau_c$, $\omega^* = \frac{(1 - g^2)\omega_0}{1 - \omega_0 g^2}$, and $g^* = \frac{g}{1 + g}$, define the two-stream matrix coefficients:

$$\gamma_1 = \frac{7 - \omega^*(4 + 3g^*)}{4}, \quad \gamma_2 = -\frac{1 - \omega^*(4 - 3g^*)}{4}, \quad \gamma_3 = \frac{2 - 3g^*\mu_0}{4}, \quad \gamma_4 = 1 - \gamma_3$$
$$\alpha_1 = \gamma_1\gamma_4 + \gamma_2\gamma_3, \quad \alpha_2 = \gamma_1\gamma_3 + \gamma_2\gamma_4, \quad k = \sqrt{\gamma_1^2 - \gamma_2^2}$$

The exact analytical cloud-layer transmittance $T_{\text{cloud}}(\tau^*, \mu_0)$ and diffuse hemispherical reflectance $\bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}(\tau^*)$ are:

$$T_{\text{cloud}}(\tau^*, \mu_0) = \exp\left(-\frac{\tau^*}{\mu_0}\right) + \frac{\omega^*}{\left(1 - k^2\mu_0^2\right)\big[(k + \gamma_1)\exp(k\tau^*) + (k - \gamma_1)\exp(-k\tau^*)\big]} \Big[ (\alpha_2 + k\gamma_3)\exp(k\tau^*) + (\alpha_2 - k\gamma_3)\exp(-k\tau^*) - 2k\big(\gamma_3 - \alpha_2\mu_0\big)\exp(-\tau^*/\mu_0) \Big]$$

$$\bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}(\tau^*) = \frac{\gamma_2\big(\exp(k\tau^*) - \exp(-k\tau^*)\big)}{(k + \gamma_1)\exp(k\tau^*) + (k - \gamma_1)\exp(-k\tau^*)}$$

which directly feeds the Surface Energy Balance ODE:

$$\text{SW}_{\downarrow}(t) = S_0 e_0(n)\mu_0(t) \, T_{\text{REST2,clear}}\big(\text{PW}, \text{AOD}, p_s, \mu_0(t)\big) \left[ \frac{\big(1 - N_c(t)\big) + N_c(t)T_{\text{cloud}}\big(\tau^*(t), \mu_0(t)\big)}{1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}} N_c(t)\bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}\big(\tau^*(t)\big)} \right]$$

$$\text{LW}_{\downarrow}(t) = \big(1 - N_c \varepsilon_{\text{cloud}}(\text{LWP})\big) \left[ 1.24 \left(\frac{e_{a,2\text{m}}}{T_{2\text{m}}}\right)^{1/7} \sigma T_{2\text{m}}^4 \right] + N_c \, \varepsilon_{\text{cloud}}(\text{LWP}) \, \sigma T_{\text{cloud,base}}^4$$

where $\varepsilon_{\text{cloud}}(\text{LWP}) = 1 - \exp(-0.158 \, \text{LWP}[\text{g/m}^2])$.

## 5. Module B: Mesoscale Density Currents, Frontal Truncation & Convective Cold Pools

### 5.1 Non-Hydrostatic Density Current Kinematics & Shear-Vorticity Balance (RKW Theory)
When a coastal marine layer, lake breeze (e.g., Lake Michigan at KMDW/KORD or Lake Superior at KDLH), or convective cold pool of depth $H_d$ and virtual potential temperature deficit $\Delta\theta_v = \theta_{v,\text{amb}} - \theta_{v,\text{cold}} > 0$ advances against an ambient boundary layer with low-level vertical wind shear $\Delta U_{\text{shear}} = U(H_d) - U(0)$, **Rotunno–Klemp–Weisman (RKW) Horizontal Vorticity Balance** governs both the lifting depth at the frontal head and the propagation speed $c_{\text{front}}(t)$:

$$g'(t) = g \left( \frac{\theta_{v,\text{amb}}(t) - \theta_{v,\text{cold}}(t)}{\theta_{v,\text{amb}}(t)} + 0.608 \left(q_{v,\text{amb}} - q_{v,\text{cold}}\right) - q_{l,\text{cold}} \right)$$

$$c_{\text{front}}(t) = \text{Fr}_d\left(\frac{H_d}{z_i}\right)\sqrt{g'(t) H_d(t)} - \gamma_{\text{wind}} U_{\text{ambient},\perp}(t)$$

$$\text{Fr}_d\left(\frac{H_d}{z_i}\right) = \sqrt{\frac{(2 z_i - H_d)(z_i - H_d)}{z_i(z_i + H_d)}} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{C_d L_{\text{head}}}{H_d}\right)^{-1/2} \approx 0.78 \text{ to } 1.08, \qquad \gamma_{\text{wind}} \approx 0.62$$

### 5.2 2D Kinematic Frontogenesis & NEXRAD Dual-Pol Fine-Line Extraction
To localize the advancing 2D curve $\Gamma_{\text{front}}(s, t)$ every 2 to 5 minutes:

1. **MADIS / CWOP Anisotropic Surface Objective Analysis (2D Petterssen–Miller Frontogenesis):**
   $$\mathcal{F}_{2\text{D}}(\mathbf{r}, t) = -\frac{1}{2\|\nabla_h \theta_v\|}\left[ (\partial_x u + \partial_y v)\|\nabla_h \theta_v\|^2 + (\partial_x u - \partial_y v)\big((\partial_x \theta_v)^2 - (\partial_y \theta_v)^2\big) + 2(\partial_x v + \partial_y u)(\partial_x \theta_v)(\partial_y \theta_v) \right]$$

2. **NEXRAD Level II Dual-Pol Clear-Air Boundary Classifier ($0.5^\circ$ Base Scan):**
   $$Z_H \in [4, 24]\,\text{dBZ}, \qquad \rho_{HV} \in [0.45, 0.84] \text{ (biological/insect scatterers in updraft)}, \qquad -\frac{\partial V_r}{\partial r} \ge 3.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$$

### 5.3 Analytical First-Passage Truncation of the Diurnal Heating Curve
Let $d_{\text{station}}(t_0) = \min_{s} \|\mathbf{r}_{\text{station}} - \Gamma_{\text{front}}(s, t_0)\|_2$ be the distance from the density current to the ASOS thermometer at time $t_0$. Under stochastic frontal velocity $dx = c_{\text{front}}(t)\,dt + \sigma_c\,dW_t$, the **Time-of-Arrival (TOA) Density** is the time-transformed Inverse Gaussian distribution:

$$f_{\text{TOA}}(t \mid t_0) = \frac{d_{\text{station}}(t_0)}{\sqrt{2\pi \sigma_c^2 (t - t_0)^3}} \exp\left( -\frac{\left(d_{\text{station}}(t_0) - \int_{t_0}^t c_{\text{front}}(u)\,du\right)^2}{2\sigma_c^2 (t - t_0)} \right)$$

Let $T_{\text{dry}}(t)$ be the un-truncated terrestrial diurnal heating trajectory (monotonically rising on $[t_0, t_{\text{peak}}]$). Since frontal arrival at $\tau_{\text{arrival}} \in [t_0, t_{\text{peak}}]$ truncates the trajectory at $T_{\max} = T_{\text{dry}}(\tau_{\text{arrival}})$, while non-arrival before $t_{\text{peak}}$ yields $T_{\max} = T_{\text{dry}}(t_{\text{peak}})$, the **Exact Truncated Daily Maximum Temperature PDF** is a mixed continuous-discrete distribution:

$$p_{T_{\max}}(T) = \left[ \frac{f_{\text{TOA}}\big(T_{\text{dry}}^{-1}(T) \mid t_0\big)}{\left.\frac{dT_{\text{dry}}}{dt}\right|_{t = T_{\text{dry}}^{-1}(T)}} \right] \mathbb{I}\big(T \in [T_{\text{dry}}(t_0), T_{\text{dry}}(t_{\text{peak}}))\big) + \left(1 - \int_{t_0}^{t_{\text{peak}}} f_{\text{TOA}}(u \mid t_0)\,du\right) \delta\big(T - T_{\text{dry}}(t_{\text{peak}})\big)$$

In a convective daytime surface layer, temperature fluctuations $T'(t) = T_{5\text{m}}(t) - \bar{T}(t)$ follow a locally stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process with Monin–Obukhov turbulent standard deviation $\sigma_T$:

$$\sigma_T(t) = \frac{|\theta_*|}{\kappa} \, \phi_T\left(\frac{z_{2\text{m}}}{L_{\text{MO}}}\right) \approx C_{T1} |\theta_*| \left(1 - C_{T2} \frac{z_{2\text{m}}}{L_{\text{MO}}}\right)^{-1/3} \quad \text{for } L_{\text{MO}} < 0$$

By the **Rice / Cramér–Leadbetter Level-Crossing Formula** for a differentiable Gaussian process with mean drift $\bar{T}(t)$, variance $\sigma_T^2$, and derivative variance $\sigma_{\dot{T}}^2$, the rate of upward crossings $\nu^+(b, t)$ of threshold $b = K - 0.5$ per unit time is:

$$\nu^+(b, t) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \frac{\sigma_{\dot{T}}}{\sigma_T} \, \phi\left(\frac{b - \bar{T}(t)}{\sigma_T}\right) \left[ \sqrt{2\pi}\,\eta(t)\,\Phi\big(\eta(t)\big) + \exp\left(-\frac{\eta(t)^2}{2}\right) \right], \qquad \eta(t) = \frac{\dot{\bar{T}}(t)}{\sigma_{\dot{T}}}$$

yielding the exact analytical intra-hour peak exceedance probability between routine hourly METARs:

$$P\left( \max_{t \in [t_0, t_1]} T_{5\text{m}}(t) \ge K - 0.5 \right) = 1 - \left[ 1 - \Phi\left(\frac{\bar{T}(t_0) - (K - 0.5)}{\sigma_T}\right) \right] \exp\left( - \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \nu^+(K - 0.5, t)\,dt \right)$$

## 9. Numerical Discretization & Explicit Analytical Jacobians ($\frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{\text{PDE}}}{\partial \mathbf{X}_0}$ and $\frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_m}{\partial \mathbf{X}_0}$)

### 9.1 Space-Time Staggered Grid & State Vector Indexing
1. **Temporal Grid ($l \in \{0, 1, \dots, L\}$):**
   Macro-steps $t_l = t_{\text{start}} + l \Delta t$ with $\Delta t = 300\,\text{s}$ ($5\,\text{min}$, $L = 288$ steps over $24\,\text{hours}$), and embedded micro-steps $t_{l,m} = t_l + m \delta t$ with $\delta t = 60\,\text{s}$ ($m \in \{1,\dots,5\}$) for the ASOS ACU firmware filter cascade.
2. **1D Vertical Hybrid-Log-Grid ($k \in \{1, \dots, M_z\}$, Charney–Phillips Staggering):**
   $$z_k = z_{\text{top,PBL}} \left(\frac{\eta_k - \eta_1}{1 - \eta_1}\right)^\gamma, \quad \gamma = 2.2, \quad M_z = 16$$
   * **Thermodynamic/Scalar Nodes ($k \in \{1, \dots, M_z\}$):** Carry $\theta_{v,k}^l, q_{v,k}^l, q_{c,k}^l, u_k^l, v_k^l$, with level $k = 1$ pinned strictly at the official ASOS hygrothermometer height $z_1 = 2.0\,\text{m}$ and level $k = 2$ at anemometer height $z_2 = 10.0\,\text{m}$.
   * **Flux/Turbulence Half-Levels ($k + \frac{1}{2}$):** Carry vertical velocity $w_{k+1/2}^l$, eddy diffusivity $K_{h,k+1/2}^l, K_{m,k+1/2}^l$, and $\text{TKE}_{k+1/2}^l$, with layer thickness $\Delta z_k = z_{k+1/2} - z_{k-1/2}$ and node spacing $\delta z_{k+1/2} = z_{k+1} - z_k$.
3. **2D Horizontal Advection Patch ($p, q \in \{-P, \dots, P\}$, Arakawa C-Grid):**
   Grid spacing $\Delta x = \Delta y = 2,000\,\text{m}$ ($P = 32$, covering $128\,\text{km} \times 128\,\text{km}$), carrying surface virtual potential temperature $\Theta_{p,q}^l = \theta_{v,1}(x_p, y_q, t_l)$, cloud optical depth $\tau_{c,p,q}^l$, cloud-top height $z_{\text{top},p,q}^l$, and optical-flow velocities $u_{\text{cloud},p,q}^l, v_{\text{cloud},p,q}^l$.

### Canonical State Vector Ordering at Time Step $l$
$$\mathbf{x}_l = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{sfc}} \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{col}} \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{patch}} \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{rad}} \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{metro}} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{where:} \quad \begin{aligned} \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{sfc}} &= \big[ T_s^l, \, T_{d2}^l, \, \theta_{g1}^l, \, \theta_{g2}^l, \, u_*^l, \, \theta_*^l, \, q_*^l, \, z_i^l \big]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^8 \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{col}} &= \big[ \theta_{v,1:M_z}^l, \, q_{v,1:M_z}^l, \, q_{c,1:M_z}^l, \, q_{r,1:M_z}^l, \, u_{1:M_z}^l, \, v_{1:M_z}^l \big]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{6 M_z} \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{patch}} &= \big[ \Theta_{-P:P,-P:P}^l, \, \tau_{c,-P:P,-P:P}^l, \, u_{\text{cloud},-P:P,-P:P}^l, \, v_{\text{cloud},-P:P,-P:P}^l \big]^\top \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{rad}} &= \big[ r_e^l, \, z_{\text{top}}^l, \, z_{\text{base}}^l, \, \text{SW}_{\downarrow}^l, \, \text{LW}_{\downarrow}^l \big]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^5 \\ \mathbf{x}_l^{\text{metro}} &= \big[ T_{\text{PRT}, 1:5}^l, \, T_{5\text{m}}^l, \, M_{\text{CLI}}^l, \, P_{\text{acc}}^l \big]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^8 \end{aligned}$$

### 9.2 Discretized PDE Residual Vector $\mathcal{R}_{\text{PDE}}(\mathbf{X}_0)$
Using an IMEX Crank–Nicolson / Upwind scheme with implicitness weight $\alpha_{\text{imp}} \in [\frac{1}{2}, 1]$ and $\bar{\psi}^{l+\alpha} = \alpha_{\text{imp}}\psi^{l+1} + (1 - \alpha_{\text{imp}})\psi^l$:

* **Residual Block 1 (Force-Restore SEB $r_{T_s}^{l+1}$):**
  $$r_{T_s}^{l+1} = \frac{T_s^{l+1} - T_s^l}{\Delta t} - \frac{1}{C_{\text{eff}}(\bar{\theta}_{g1})} \Big[ (1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}})\overline{\text{SW}}_{\downarrow}^{l+\alpha} + \varepsilon_s \overline{\text{LW}}_{\downarrow}^{l+\alpha} - \varepsilon_s \sigma \big(\bar{T}_s^{l+\alpha}\big)^4 - H\big(\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}\big) - LE\big(\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}\big) \Big] + \omega_{\text{day}}\big(\bar{T}_s^{l+\alpha} - \bar{T}_{d2}^{l+\alpha}\big) = 0$$

* **Residual Block 2 (Monin–Obukhov Scaling Constraints $r_{u_*}^{l+1}, r_{\theta_*}^{l+1}$):**
  $$r_{u_*}^{l+1} = u_*^{l+1}\left[ \ln\left(\frac{z_2}{z_{0m}}\right) - \Psi_m\big(\zeta_2^{l+1}\big) + \Psi_m\big(\zeta_{0m}^{l+1}\big) \right] - \kappa U_2^{l+1} = 0$$
  $$r_{\theta_*}^{l+1} = \theta_*^{l+1}\left[ \ln\left(\frac{z_1}{z_{0h}}\right) - \Psi_h\big(\zeta_1^{l+1}\big) + \Psi_h\big(\zeta_{0h}^{l+1}\big) \right] - \kappa \left(\theta_{v,1}^{l+1} - \theta_{v,s}^{l+1}\right) = 0, \qquad \zeta(z) = \frac{\kappa g z \theta_*}{\theta_{v,1} u_*^2}$$

* **Residual Block 3 (1D Vertical Column Turbulent Diffusion + Advection $r_{\theta_{v,k}}^{l+1}$):**
  $$r_{\theta_{v,k}}^{l+1} = \frac{\theta_{v,k}^{l+1} - \theta_{v,k}^l}{\Delta t} + \mathcal{A}_{h,k}\big(\bar{\Theta}^{l+\alpha}, \bar{u}_k^{l+\alpha}, \bar{v}_k^{l+\alpha}\big) - \frac{1}{\Delta z_k}\left[ K_{h,k+1/2}\frac{\bar{\theta}_{v,k+1}^{l+\alpha} - \bar{\theta}_{v,k}^{l+\alpha}}{\delta z_{k+1/2}} - K_{h,k-1/2}\frac{\bar{\theta}_{v,k}^{l+\alpha} - \bar{\theta}_{v,k-1}^{l+\alpha}}{\delta z_{k-1/2}} \right] - S_{\text{diab},k}\big(\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}\big) = 0$$

* **Residual Block 4 (2D Horizontal Patch Differentiable Upwind Advection $r_{\tau_c,p,q}^{l+1}$):**
  With smooth upwind operators $\sigma^\pm(u) = \frac{1}{2}\big(u \pm \sqrt{u^2 + \epsilon_u^2}\big)$:
  $$\mathcal{A}_x\big(\tau_c, u_{\text{cloud}}\big)_{p,q} = \frac{\sigma^+\big(u_{\text{cloud},p,q}\big)\big(\tau_{c,p,q} - \tau_{c,p-1,q}\big) + \sigma^-\big(u_{\text{cloud},p,q}\big)\big(\tau_{c,p+1,q} - \tau_{c,p,q}\big)}{\Delta x}$$
  $$r_{\tau_c, p, q}^{l+1} = \frac{\tau_{c,p,q}^{l+1} - \tau_{c,p,q}^l}{\Delta t} + \mathcal{A}_x\big(\bar{\tau}_c^{l+\alpha}, \bar{u}_{\text{cloud}}^{l+\alpha}\big)_{p,q} + \mathcal{A}_y\big(\bar{\tau}_c^{l+\alpha}, \bar{v}_{\text{cloud}}^{l+\alpha}\big)_{p,q} - \kappa_{\text{turb}}\big(\delta_{xx}^2 \bar{\tau}_c^{l+\alpha} + \delta_{yy}^2 \bar{\tau}_c^{l+\alpha}\big)_{p,q} - \mathcal{S}_{\text{nuc}}\big(\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}\big)_{p,q} + \mathcal{D}_{\text{burn}}\big(\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}\big)_{p,q} = 0$$

* **Residual Block 5 (Two-Stream Delta-Eddington Radiative Closure $r_{\text{SW}}^{l+1}, r_{\text{LW}}^{l+1}$):**
  $$r_{\text{SW}}^{l+1} = \text{SW}_{\downarrow}^{l+1} - S_0 e_0 \mu_0^{l+1} T_{\text{REST2,clear}}^{l+1} \cdot \frac{(1 - N_c) + N_c T_{\text{cloud}}\big(\tau_{c,\text{eff}}^{*,l+1}, \mu_0^{l+1}\big)}{1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}} N_c \bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}\big(\tau_{c,\text{eff}}^{*,l+1}\big)} = 0$$
  $$r_{\text{LW}}^{l+1} = \text{LW}_{\downarrow}^{l+1} - \Big[ \big(1 - N_c \varepsilon_c(\tau_{c,\text{eff}}^{l+1}, r_e^{l+1})\big) \, 1.24\left(\frac{e_{a,1}^{l+1}}{T_1^{l+1}}\right)^{1/7} \sigma (T_1^{l+1})^4 + N_c \varepsilon_c(\tau_{c,\text{eff}}^{l+1}, r_e^{l+1}) \sigma (T_{\text{base}}^{l+1})^4 \Big] = 0$$

* **Residual Block 6 (ASOS Firmware Metrological State Transition $r_{\text{PRT},m}^{l+1}, r_{M_{\text{CLI}}}^{l+1}$):**
  $$r_{\text{PRT}, m}^{l+1} = T_{\text{PRT}, m}^{l+1} - (1 - \alpha_{\text{PRT}}) T_{\text{PRT}, m-1}^{l+1} - \alpha_{\text{PRT}}\left[ \left(\frac{9}{5}(\theta_1^{l+1}\pi_1^{l+1} - 273.15) + 32\right) + c_{\text{rad}}\frac{\text{SW}_{\downarrow}^{l+1}}{\big((u_2^{l+1})^2 + (v_2^{l+1})^2 + u_{\min}^2\big)^{p/2}} \right] = 0$$
  $$r_{T_{5\text{m}}}^{l+1} = T_{5\text{m}}^{l+1} - \frac{1}{5}\sum_{m=1}^{5} T_{\text{PRT}, m}^{l+1} = 0, \qquad r_{M_{\text{CLI}}}^{l+1} = M_{\text{CLI}}^{l+1} - \frac{1}{\beta_{\max}}\ln\Big( \exp\big(\beta_{\max} M_{\text{CLI}}^l\big) + \exp\big(\beta_{\max} \widetilde{Q}_F(T_{5\text{m}}^{l+1})\big) \Big) = 0$$

### 9.3 Explicit Analytical Entries of the PDE Jacobian $\frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{\text{PDE}}}{\partial \mathbf{X}_0}$

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{R}_{\text{PDE}}}{\partial \mathbf{X}_0} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{B}_0 & \mathbf{A}_1 & \mathbf{0} & \cdots & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{B}_1 & \mathbf{A}_2 & \cdots & \mathbf{0} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \mathbf{0} & \cdots & \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{B}_{L-1} & \mathbf{A}_L \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{A}_{l+1} = \frac{1}{\Delta t}\mathbf{I}_{\text{prog}} - \alpha_{\text{imp}} \left.\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}}{\partial \mathbf{x}}\right|_{\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}}, \quad \mathbf{B}_l = -\frac{1}{\Delta t}\mathbf{I}_{\text{prog}} - (1 - \alpha_{\text{imp}}) \left.\frac{\partial \mathbf{F}}{\partial \mathbf{x}}\right|_{\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{l+\alpha}}$$

* **Surface Energy Balance (SEB) & Penman–Monteith Partial Derivatives:**
  $$\frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial T_s^{l+1}} = \frac{1}{\Delta t} + \alpha_{\text{imp}}\left[ \frac{4\varepsilon_s \sigma T_s^3}{C_{\text{eff}}} + \frac{\rho_a L_v h_{\text{sfc}}(\theta_{g1}) s_q(T_s)}{C_{\text{eff}} R_{\text{tot}}} - \frac{\rho_a L_v\big(h_{\text{sfc}} q_{\text{sat}} - q_{v,1}\big)}{C_{\text{eff}} R_{\text{tot}}^2}\frac{\partial r_{\text{canopy}}}{\partial T_s} + \omega_{\text{day}} \right]$$
  $$\frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial q_{v,1}^{l+1}} = -\frac{\alpha_{\text{imp}} \rho_a L_v}{C_{\text{eff}} R_{\text{tot}}} \left[ 1 + \frac{h_{\text{sfc}} q_{\text{sat}}(T_s) - q_{v,1}}{R_{\text{tot}}} \gamma_{\text{vpd}} r_{\text{canopy}} \frac{p_s}{0.622} \right]$$
  $$\frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial \theta_{g1}^{l+1}} = \alpha_{\text{imp}}\left[ \frac{Q_{\text{net}} - H - LE}{C_{\text{eff}}^2}\frac{\partial C_{\text{eff}}}{\partial \theta_{g1}} + \frac{\rho_a L_v q_{\text{sat}}(T_s, p_s)}{C_{\text{eff}} R_{\text{tot}}} \frac{\pi}{2\theta_{\text{fc}}}\sin\left(\pi \frac{\theta_{g1}}{\theta_{\text{fc}}}\right) \mathbb{I}(\theta_{g1} < \theta_{\text{fc}}) \right]$$
  $$\frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial \text{SW}_{\downarrow}^{l+1}} = -\frac{\alpha_{\text{imp}}}{C_{\text{eff}}}\left[ (1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}}) + \frac{\rho_a L_v (h_{\text{sfc}} q_{\text{sat}} - q_{v,1})}{R_{\text{tot}}^2}\frac{\partial r_{\text{canopy}}}{\partial \text{SW}_{\downarrow}} \right], \qquad \frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial \text{LW}_{\downarrow}^{l+1}} = -\frac{\alpha_{\text{imp}} \varepsilon_s}{C_{\text{eff}}}$$
  $$\frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial u_*^{l+1}} = \frac{\alpha_{\text{imp}}}{C_{\text{eff}}}\left[ -\rho_a c_p \theta_* - \frac{\rho_a L_v (h_{\text{sfc}} q_{\text{sat}} - q_{v,1})}{R_{\text{tot}}^2} \frac{\partial r_{ah}}{\partial u_*} \right], \qquad \frac{\partial r_{T_s}^{l+1}}{\partial \theta_*^{l+1}} = \frac{\alpha_{\text{imp}}}{C_{\text{eff}}}\left[ -\rho_a c_p u_* - \frac{\rho_a L_v (h_{\text{sfc}} q_{\text{sat}} - q_{v,1})}{R_{\text{tot}}^2} \frac{\partial r_{ah}}{\partial \theta_*} \right]$$

* **Monin–Obukhov Stability Jacobians ($\zeta < 0$ and $\zeta \ge 0$):**
  $$\frac{d\Psi_m}{d\zeta} = \begin{cases} \frac{16}{x(1 + x)(1 + x^2)}, & \zeta < 0 \\ -\big[ a + b(1 - d\zeta + c)\exp(-d\zeta) \big], & \zeta \ge 0 \end{cases}, \qquad \frac{d\Psi_h}{d\zeta} = \begin{cases} \frac{16}{x^2(1 + x^2)}, & \zeta < 0 \\ -\Big[ a\left(1 + \frac{2}{3}a\zeta\right)^{1/2} + b(1 - d\zeta + c)\exp(-d\zeta) \Big], & \zeta \ge 0 \end{cases}$$
  $$\frac{\partial (r_{u_*}, r_{\theta_*})}{\partial (u_*, \theta_*)} = \begin{bmatrix} \Lambda_m + 2\Big(\zeta_2 \frac{d\Psi_m}{d\zeta}(\zeta_2) - \zeta_{0m} \frac{d\Psi_m}{d\zeta}(\zeta_{0m})\Big) & -\frac{u_*}{\theta_*}\Big(\zeta_2 \frac{d\Psi_m}{d\zeta}(\zeta_2) - \zeta_{0m} \frac{d\Psi_m}{d\zeta}(\zeta_{0m})\Big) \\[10pt] \frac{2\theta_*}{u_*}\Big(\zeta_1 \frac{d\Psi_h}{d\zeta}(\zeta_1) - \zeta_{0h} \frac{d\Psi_h}{d\zeta}(\zeta_{0h})\Big) & \Lambda_h - \Big(\zeta_1 \frac{d\Psi_h}{d\zeta}(\zeta_1) - \zeta_{0h} \frac{d\Psi_h}{d\zeta}(\zeta_{0h})\big) \end{bmatrix}$$

* **1D Vertical Column Tridiagonal Diffusion Jacobian:**
  $$\frac{\partial r_{\theta_{v,k}}^{l+1}}{\partial \theta_{v,k-1}^{l+1}} = -\alpha_{\text{imp}} \frac{K_{h,k-1/2}}{\Delta z_k \, \delta z_{k-1/2}}, \qquad \frac{\partial r_{\theta_{v,k}}^{l+1}}{\partial \theta_{v,k+1}^{l+1}} = -\alpha_{\text{imp}} \frac{K_{h,k+1/2}}{\Delta z_k \, \delta z_{k+1/2}}, \qquad \frac{\partial r_{\theta_{v,k}}^{l+1}}{\partial \theta_{v,k}^{l+1}} = \frac{1}{\Delta t} + \alpha_{\text{imp}} \left( \frac{K_{h,k+1/2}}{\Delta z_k \, \delta z_{k+1/2}} + \frac{K_{h,k-1/2}}{\Delta z_k \, \delta z_{k-1/2}} \right)$$

* **2D Patch Upwind Advection & Two-Stream Radiative Transfer Jacobians:**
  $$\frac{\partial r_{\tau_c,p,q}^{l+1}}{\partial \tau_{c,p,q}^{l+1}} = \frac{1}{\Delta t} + \alpha_{\text{imp}}\left[ \frac{\sigma^+(u_{p,q}) - \sigma^-(u_{p,q})}{\Delta x} + \frac{\sigma^+(v_{p,q}) - \sigma^-(v_{p,q})}{\Delta y} + 2\kappa_{\text{turb}}\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x^2} + \frac{1}{\Delta y^2}\right) + \frac{\partial \mathcal{D}_{\text{burn}}}{\partial \tau_{c,p,q}} \right]$$
  $$\frac{\partial \text{SW}_{\downarrow}}{\partial \tau_{c,\text{eff}}} = S_0 e_0 \mu_0 T_{\text{REST2,clear}} N_c (1 - \omega_0 g^2) \frac{\big(1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}} N_c \bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}\big)\frac{\partial T_{\text{cloud}}}{\partial \tau^*} + \alpha_{\text{sfc}}\big[(1 - N_c) + N_c T_{\text{cloud}}\big]\frac{\partial \bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}}{\partial \tau^*}}{\big(1 - \alpha_{\text{sfc}} N_c \bar{R}_{\text{cloud}}\big)^2}$$

### 9.4 Explicit Observation Operator Jacobians $\frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_m}{\partial \mathbf{X}_0}$

1. **ASOS 1-Minute OMT Stream ($\mathcal{H}_{\text{ASOS-1m}}$):**
   $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_{\text{ASOS-1m}, 1}}{\partial T_{5\text{m}}^l} = 1, \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_{\text{ASOS-1m}, 2}}{\partial q_{v,1}^l} = \frac{9}{5} \frac{a_M b_M}{\big(a_M - \ln(e/e_0)\big)^2} \frac{0.622}{q_{v,1}(0.622 + 0.378 q_{v,1})}, \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_{\text{ASOS-1m}, 3}}{\partial u_2^l} = 1, \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{H}_{\text{ASOS-1m}, 4}}{\partial v_2^l} = 1$$

2. **Standard Hourly/SPECI METAR Whole-$^\circ\text{C}$ Censored Probit Operator ($\mathcal{H}_{\text{METAR-C}}$):**
   $$\frac{\partial \log p(C_{\text{rep}} \mid \mathbf{x}_l)}{\partial T_{5\text{m}}^l} = -\frac{\phi(z_b) - \phi(z_a)}{\tilde{\sigma}\big[\Phi(z_b) - \Phi(z_a)\big]}, \qquad -\frac{\partial^2 \log p(C_{\text{rep}} \mid \mathbf{x}_l)}{\partial (T_{5\text{m}}^l)^2} = \frac{1}{\tilde{\sigma}^2}\left[ \frac{z_b \phi(z_b) - z_a \phi(z_a)}{\Phi(z_b) - \Phi(z_a)} + \left(\frac{\phi(z_b) - \phi(z_a)}{\Phi(z_b) - \Phi(z_a)}\right)^2 \right]$$

3. **6-Hour Synoptic Extremum (`1sTTT` / `2sTTT`) Operator ($\mathcal{H}_{6\text{h-max}}$):**

### 10.1 Conformalized Skew-Student-$t$ Distributional Calibration
Let $\{S^{(m)}\}_{m=1}^M$ be the $M = 256$ discrete integer-Fahrenheit settlement values produced by pushing our posterior SDA trajectories through the exact station settlement functional $\mathcal{M}_{\text{contract}}$:

$$S^{(m)} = \max\left( M_{\text{CLI}}(t_{\text{now}}), \; \max_{t \in (t_{\text{now}}, t_{\text{end}}]} Q_{\text{half-up}}\Big( \big(h_{\text{5m}} * (h_{\text{PRT}} * T_{\text{air}}^{(m)})\big)(t) + \varepsilon_{\text{rad}}^{(m)}(t) \Big) \right)$$

We map the trajectory ensemble moments $(\mu_S, \sigma_S, \gamma_{\text{skew}}, \kappa_{\text{kurt}})$ through a **Censored Non-Homogeneous Skew-Student-$t$ Distributional Regression (EMOS-Skew-$t$)** calibrated via out-of-sample **Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS)** minimization over historical station CLI settlements:

$$\text{CRPS}(F_{\text{cal}}, y_{\text{CLI}}) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \big[ F_{\text{cal}}(x) - \mathbb{I}(x \ge y_{\text{CLI}}) \big]^2 \, dx$$

$$F_{\text{cal}}(s) = F_{t_\nu}\left( \frac{s - (\beta_0 + \beta_1 \mu_S + \beta_{\text{front}} P_{\text{TOA}})}{\exp(\delta_0 + \delta_1 \ln \sigma_S + \delta_{\text{cloud}} \sigma_{\tau_c})} \,;\, \nu, \alpha_{\text{skew}} \right)$$

For any disjoint partition of Kalshi brackets $\{B_k = [K_{k,\text{low}}, K_{k,\text{high}}]\}_{k=1}^K$ (where integers are inclusive), the calibrated bracket probability is:

$$p_{\text{cal}}(B_k) = F_{\text{cal}}(K_{k,\text{high}} + 0.5) - F_{\text{cal}}(K_{k,\text{low}} - 0.5)$$
