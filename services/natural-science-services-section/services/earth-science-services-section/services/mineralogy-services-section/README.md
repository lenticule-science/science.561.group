# Mineralogy Services Section

This section holds knowledge and inquiry for mineralogy proper: the crystal
chemistry, microstructure, and physical behavior (hardness, toughness,
fracture, diagenesis) of minerals and mineral aggregates, as distinct from the
sibling earth-science sections' concerns with soil formation, hydrology, and
climate. It was created to receive a silicate-mineralogy inquiry program
(silicate polymerization classes, jade microstructure and fracture toughness,
opal diagenesis) whose questions belong to mineralogy specifically rather than
to any existing earth-science sibling.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`, in the house
`content/text/...` layout shared with its siblings.
