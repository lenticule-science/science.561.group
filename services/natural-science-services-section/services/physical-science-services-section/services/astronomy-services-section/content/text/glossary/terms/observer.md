# Observer

The role borne by a *site at which evidence about the universe is read off*: a worldline (or, more typically, a localized piece of one) at which [cosmological-settling](../objects/cosmological-settling.md) values are sampled. An observer is not the same as a [cosmic event](cosmic-event.md). A cosmic event is a single point in [spacetime](../../../../../physics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/spacetime.md). An observer is a *trajectory* of cosmic events together with a chosen set of predicates the observer measures along that trajectory.

The role has three pressures:

- **Locality.** An observer at event $e$ has direct access only to predications whose evidence has propagated to $e$ along causal channels — i.e., cells with at least one coordinate in the past light cone of $e$. Predications about cells outside that cone are at $\bot$ in the [knowledge order](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md) for this observer: told nothing.
- **Frame.** The observer carries a frame — a choice of clock, of spatial axes, of which predicates count as "rest frame" — that determines how the observer reindexes universal predications into local measurements. Two observers in different frames will assign different values to the *same* cell when their reindexings differ; this is relativistic frame-dependence in predication-categorical language.
- **Selection.** Out of the (in principle vast) set of cells in the observer's past light cone, the observer measures only some. Cosmology as practiced is the discipline of choosing predicates whose values, sampled over the observable cells, constrain global parameters of the [cosmological settling](../objects/cosmological-settling.md). The CMB temperature is one such predicate; the redshift-distance relation is another.

## The comoving-observer specialization

A privileged class of observers — the **[comoving observers](comoving-frame.md)** — are at rest with respect to the average matter distribution. Comoving observers share a common [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md), and their frames are related by the symmetry group of the [cosmological principle](../../../../services/cosmology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/cosmological-principle.md). When the [cosmological settling](../objects/cosmological-settling.md) is invariant under this group, comoving observers all see the same statistics — the universe looks isotropic and homogeneous to all of them. This invariance is the predicated content of "we are not at a special place."

## What an observer is not

An observer is not required to be conscious, alive, or human. A photographic plate, a CMB satellite, a telescope, or a single atom interacting with a single photon all instantiate the observer role: they are sites at which a predication-cell value passes from $\bot$ to a non-$\bot$ value through causal contact. The role is defined by the structure of evidence reception, not by any phenomenology.
