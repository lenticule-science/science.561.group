The **sphere of influence** (SOI) of a body of mass m orbiting a primary of mass M at semi-major axis a is the region, of radius r_SOI ≈ a·(m/M)^(2/5) (Laplace), inside which it is more accurate to treat the motion of a small third body as a two-body orbit about m perturbed by M than the reverse. The Moon's SOI about the Earth is about 66,000 km. In the [patched conic approximation](patched-conic-approximation.md) the SOI boundary is where one conic ends and the next begins; in reality the boundary is not sharp, and the force on a craft does not jump there.
