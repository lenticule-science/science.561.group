A light cone is the region of [spacetime](../../../terms/spacetime.md) reachable from a given event by signals traveling at or below the speed of light.
Every event $e$ has two cones: the **past light cone** of $e$ -- all events from which a light signal could have reached $e$ -- and the **future light cone** of $e$ -- all events that a light signal emitted at $e$ could reach.

Because no signal travels faster than light, the past light cone of an observer at event $e$ contains every event that could have causally influenced $e$, and the future light cone contains every event $e$ could causally influence.
Events outside both cones -- the **elsewhere** of $e$ -- are causally disconnected from $e$: no signal can pass between them in either direction.

The light cone partitions the relationship between any two events into three classes:

- **Timelike-separated.** One event lies in the other's past or future light cone.
  They can be causally connected by a signal slower than light.
- **Lightlike-separated (null).** They lie on each other's light cones.
  They can be connected only by a signal traveling at exactly the speed of light.
- **Spacelike-separated.** Neither lies in the other's light cone.
  No causal signal can pass between them.

In cosmology the light cone is operational: when an observer measures a galaxy at [redshift](../../../terms/redshift.md) $z$, the photons arriving now were emitted on the observer's past light cone, at a cosmic moment determined by $z$.
The most distant events any observer can have evidence about are precisely those on their past light cone.
The [cosmic microwave background](../../structure/terms/cosmic-microwave-background.md) is the inner surface of every observer's past light cone, set by [recombination](../../evolution/terms/recombination.md): photons from earlier times were trapped in opaque plasma and never reached the cone.

The light cone structure is the carrier of relativistic causality.
It is preserved by every coordinate change that preserves the [spacetime](../../../terms/spacetime.md) metric, and it is what gives general relativity its causal teeth: laws of physics propagate at or below light speed because they propagate within the future light cone of their source.
