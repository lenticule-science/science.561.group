# Nested cosmology as a mediated pair

A minimal cosmological reading of the Wood machinery, used here only to make the abstract definitions concrete. Take a family with exactly two worlds, $\{W_0, W_1\}$, and read them as:

- $W_0$ — *our* observable universe (treated as a single world, with $\mathrm{QC}(W_0)$ encoding the structural content described by [predicated-cosmology](../../predicated-cosmology/_index.md)'s [cosmological settling](../../predicated-cosmology/objects/cosmological-settling.md), and $\mathrm{Flow}(W_0)$ encoding cosmic-time evolution);
- $W_1$ — a hypothetical adjacent world in any of the [Tegmark senses](../../multiverse/terms/tegmark-classification.md) (a Level-II bubble, an Everettian sibling branch, a landscape neighbor — the example does not commit).

A [mediating nucleus](teaching/explanations/mediating-nucleus.md) $(M, \{Q_0, Q_1\}, \{\psi_0, \psi_1\})$ is then any structure $M$ in the ambient category with two [holes](teaching/explanations/hole.md) and two [portal morphisms](../morphisms/portal-morphism.md) — the simplest non-trivial mediator shape.

The [Wood as initial mediator](../theorems/wood-as-initial-mediator.md) theorem says: *however* such an $M$ is built, there is a unique-up-to-isotropy way for it to factor through the Wood $\mathrm{Wd}_{\{W_0, W_1\}}$ for this two-world family. In particular: any informal cosmologist's diagram with two universes and a connecting region can be read as a mediator and is automatically related to the canonical Wood by a unique structural map.

## What the example shows and does not show

- **Shows:** the categorical apparatus is non-trivially populated by a minimal cosmological reading. Two-world Woods exist (as postulated by the Wood's existence axiom) and behave like the source draft says they should.
- **Does not show:** that any actual physical structure in our cosmology *is* a Wood for our universe and a sibling. The example is a typing exercise, not a cosmological prediction.
- **Does not pick a Tegmark level:** the construction is the same for Level I, II, III, IV. The framework deliberately abstracts over what a sibling world *is* and concentrates on what a mediator *between* them must look like.

## Connection to predicated-cosmology

If $W_0$ is read with the [cosmological settling](../../predicated-cosmology/objects/cosmological-settling.md) data inhabiting $\mathrm{QC}(W_0)$ and cosmic-time evolution inhabiting $\mathrm{Flow}(W_0)$ via its [cosmic-time-flow](../../predicated-cosmology/morphisms/cosmic-time-flow.md) endomorphism, then the Wood example becomes a candidate bridge between predicated-cosmology and predicated-multiverse: a single predicated-cosmology settling becomes a single nucleus in a predicated-multiverse family. Establishing this bridge formally — what data on the settling side determines the $\mathrm{QC}$ and $\mathrm{Flow}$ structure on the nucleus side — is open. See [does-cosmological-settling-induce-world-structure](questions/does-cosmological-settling-induce-world-structure.md).
