**The Friedmann relation** sums the [constituent](../../../cosmic-constituent.md) charges into the relaxation rate:

$$H^2 = \left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\sum_i \rho_i, \qquad H^2(z) = H_0^2\big[\Omega_m(1+z)^3 + \Omega_r(1+z)^4 + \Omega_\Lambda\big].$$

In proper terms it is the **net of the modal balance** — the ◇ (dark-energy) + □ (matter) + carryover (radiation) charges combined into the per-stage rate $H$.
It makes $H$ a function of the relaxation stage, not a free number.

## The bridge to the relaxation orbit

$H = \dot a/a$ is the orbit's per-stage inflation factor $\lambda$: the Friedmann relation *is* the relaxation orbit's growth law (the thermalizing orbit reproduces $H(a)$). reflexive-friedmann-system extends it with a reflexive-energy term $\Omega_g\,g^2(z)$.
