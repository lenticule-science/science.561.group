## Two distinct φ-moves

The depth ladder ($n \to n-1$, stepping by $\varphi^{-1}$) is the **delete-last / predicating descent** (aperiodic→transfinite→scott, the limit-point Pisots). **Thermalizing** (scott→finite, φ→ρ) is a different move — the terminal stage, [`thermalizing`](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../cosmic-predicating/library/cosmic-transfinite-predication/cosmic-positing/library/cosmic-scott-posit/thermalizing/thermalizing.md), = heat death.
The CMB depth is a predicating-ladder coordinate; the Hubble tension is a scott-stage signature — distinct moves on the φ-tower.

The vacuum / fixed point $T_{\mathrm{bit}}$ carries the **thermal** constant, plastic ρ, not φ — see [`vacuum-is-padovan`](library/vacuum-is-padovan/vacuum-is-padovan.md).
