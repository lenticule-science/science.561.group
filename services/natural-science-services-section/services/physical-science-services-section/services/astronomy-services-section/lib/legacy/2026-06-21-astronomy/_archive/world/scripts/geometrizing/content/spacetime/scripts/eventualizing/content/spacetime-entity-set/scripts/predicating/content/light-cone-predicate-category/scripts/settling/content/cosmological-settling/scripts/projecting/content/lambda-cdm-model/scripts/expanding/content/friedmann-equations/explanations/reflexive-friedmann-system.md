**The Reflexive–Friedmann System** lifts the predecessor *phase-change* paper.
It is the construction in which an [H¹ obstruction in the study](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md) stops being merely epistemic and **proposes a new fact of the field**.

## g(z) as an order parameter

Take the disequilibrium $g(z)$ — the value of the static $H^1$ class (see [hubble-tension](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md)) — as a Landau-type order parameter evolving in redshift:

$$ \frac{dg}{dz} = -A(z)\, g - B\, g^3, \qquad \Phi(g; z) = \tfrac12 A(z)\, g^2 + \tfrac14 B\, g^4, $$

with $A(z) = \beta\,[\Omega_m (1+z)^3 - \Omega_\Lambda]$ changing sign at the critical redshift $z_c$ where $(1+z_c)^3 = \Omega_\Lambda/\Omega_m$ — the matter–dark-energy equality ($z_c \approx 0.9$).
Closed phase ($A>0$, $g\approx 0$): recognitions mutually entail.
Open phase ($A<0$, $g>0$): residuation fails.
The empirically observed peak of $g(z)$ near $0.7 < z < 1.3$ is, in RFS, this **phase change** — the same crossover the stage-offset reading (below) attributes to the unconverged scott spiral rather than to new physics.

## The coupling — a candidate new cosmos-cell

RFS feeds the disequilibrium back into expansion via a reflexive-energy density:

$$ \rho_g = \rho_* \, g^2(z), \qquad H^2(z) = H_0^2\big[\Omega_m (1+z)^3 + \Omega_\Lambda + \Omega_g\, g^2(z)\big]. $$

The term $\Omega_g g^2(z)$ is a **transient energy reservoir** that reconciles early and late inferences without touching $\Lambda$, vanishing at late times ($g \to 0$, recovering ΛCDM).

In this branch's terms: $\rho_g$ is a **candidate cosmos-cell** — a fact RFS proposes the cosmos contains.
If empirical correspondence confirms it, it does not merely amend [ΛCDM](../lambda-cdm/lambda-cdm.md); it enters the [cosmos](../../../../../../../classical-spacetime.md) as genuinely new world through [cosmogenerating](../../../../../../../../../cosmogenerating.md).
That is the outcome under which the Hubble tension would **confirm** the thesis that articulation generates cosmos — the disequilibrium in the study is the signal that there is more world to articulate.

## RFS as the artifact of refusing the stage-offset

RFS is the **new-physics** resolution (resolution 2 of the [hubble-tension](../hubble-tension/hubble-tension.md)).
Read against the **stage-offset** (resolution 3), its reflexive-energy $\rho_g = \rho_* g^2$ is exactly the **apparent "missing energy" you must invent if you demand that two stage-readings of an unconverged spiral already agree.** If the tension is a stage-offset (early scott vs later scott — two stages of the one golden-φ spiral of an not-yet-thermal, κ=ω cosmos), then $\rho_g$ is the residual that single-converged-value blindness manufactures — not a real new cosmos-cell.
RFS and the stage-offset are thus two faces of the same gap: invent $\rho_g$, or accept that $H_0$ is a stage-indexed reading of an not-yet-thermal spiral.
Which is correct is the empirical question.
