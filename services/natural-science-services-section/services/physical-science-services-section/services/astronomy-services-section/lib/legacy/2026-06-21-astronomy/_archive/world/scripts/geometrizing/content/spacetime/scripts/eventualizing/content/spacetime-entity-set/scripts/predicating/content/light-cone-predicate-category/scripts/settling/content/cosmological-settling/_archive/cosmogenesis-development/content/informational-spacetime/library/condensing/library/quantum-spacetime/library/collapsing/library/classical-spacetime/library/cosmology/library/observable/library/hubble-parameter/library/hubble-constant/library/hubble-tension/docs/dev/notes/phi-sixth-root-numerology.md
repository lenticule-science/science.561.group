# The φ^(1/6) coincidence

An empirical coincidence in the present data, recorded as a coincidence: it carries no derivation and may not survive sharper measurement.

## The coincidence

The archived predecessor work noted that the late/early Hubble ratio sits near a sixth root of the golden ratio:

$$ \frac{H_0^{\text{late}}}{H_0^{\text{early}}} \approx \frac{73}{67.4} \approx 1.083, \qquad \varphi^{1/6} \approx 1.0835. $$

The agreement is striking (the archive reports a match to ~0.02%).

## As a within-scott stage ratio

Both pipelines read the one cosmic-scott golden spiral (see the [stage-offset cell](../../../hubble-tension.md) and the [grounding note](../../../../../../../../../../docs/dev/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md)) at stages τ_CMB and τ_now.
The spiral grows by φ over a full turn, so the ratio is $\varphi^{\Delta}$ with Δ set by the stage interval [τ_CMB, τ_now].
The $\varphi^{1/6}$ form would mean that interval is a sixth of a golden turn — a hint about the stage interval, not a confirmed value.
(Matching 1.083 to ~1% with a free exponent is cheap, and the $H_0$ spread is wider than the gap; this is a flag.)

## The two sixes

A second flag, even more speculative: the "6" in $\varphi^{1/6}$ sits next to the **mod-6** commutating modulus from [[collatz-settlement]] (its nucleus-shadow reading: `lcm(2, 3{-}1) = 6`, the surviving image of the commutating square σ∘Δ = Δ∘σ after the strict-finite collapse).

Until that exists, $\varphi^{1/6}$ stays in this note.
