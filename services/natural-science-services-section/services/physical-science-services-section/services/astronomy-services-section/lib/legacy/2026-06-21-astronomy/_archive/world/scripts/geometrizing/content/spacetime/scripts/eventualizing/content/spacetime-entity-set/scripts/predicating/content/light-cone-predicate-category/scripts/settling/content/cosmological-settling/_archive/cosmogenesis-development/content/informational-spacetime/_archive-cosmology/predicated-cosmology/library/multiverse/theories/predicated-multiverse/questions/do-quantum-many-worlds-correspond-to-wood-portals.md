# Do quantum many-worlds correspond to Wood portals?

The Everettian (many-worlds) interpretation of quantum mechanics — the [Tegmark Level III](../../multiverse/terms/tegmark-classification.md) multiverse — treats every quantum measurement as a branching of the universal wavefunction, with each branch a separate [world](../objects/world-as-nucleus.md).
Branches are ordinarily described as having no inter-world dynamics: the worlds are causally and informationally disjoint after branching.

The Wood machinery describes inter-world structure in terms of [portals](teaching/explanations/portal.md): controlled connections that satisfy the boundary-determination axiom (each portal jump's destination state depends only on the boundary germ in the source pool).
The natural question:

**Q.** In a Wood-style picture of the Everettian multiverse, do quantum measurement events correspond to Wood [pools](../objects/wood-between-worlds.md) and the branch-projections to portal morphisms?

A "yes" reading would say: a quantum measurement is the cosmologist-side image of a Wood pool — a region of structural degeneration (the measurement basis is "thin" relative to the full Hilbert structure) from which a [portal jump](../morphisms/portal-jump.md) into each branch world carries the pre-measurement traveller-state forward.
The boundary-determination axiom would then have a quantum-mechanical reading: only the wavefunction's *projection onto the measurement basis* (the boundary data of the measurement pool) determines the post-measurement state in each branch.

A "no" reading would say: Everettian branching is not portal-style; once branches form they are by construction unconnected, and there is no Wood-style fabric in between them.
The Wood would then be irrelevant to Level III multiverses and live in higher Tegmark levels only.

## Why the question matters

The Wood is the categorical apparatus of multiverse-mediator structure.
If Level III multiverses do not realize it, the Wood is empirically inert at the level where multiverse claims have the strongest defenders (Carroll, Wallace, the decoherence-program physicists).
If Level III multiverses *do* realize it, the Wood acquires its first connection to a working physical theory.

This is a *structural* question — about whether two pieces of categorical apparatus can be made to correspond — not an empirical one about whether Everettian QM is the right interpretation.

## Status

Open.
A serious attempt would require committing to a specific categorical formalization of the Everettian multiverse (e.g. via decoherence functors on a Hilbert-bundle base) and checking whether it satisfies the Wood axioms (W-Iso), (W-PoolDeg), (W-BdyPort), (W-Univ).
No such attempt is made here.
