`measuring` / `recognition` / `pipeline` / `observation` are **general epistemics** — they exist pre-cosmos, at the root articulation level (articulating/predicating/positing/thermalizing, token/claim, the predication fiber).
Building them under `cosmos/` would just shadow the root machinery into cosmos and re-derive articulation.
The measurement *side* already has prior art in the archive (`predicated-astronomy`: observation-cell, observer-instrument, instrument-fiber); when the study needs those, it inherits them from the spine, it does not re-make them here.

What is missing is **cosmos-native content**: the facts that hold of the *cosmos's own entities* — the expanding world itself.
These are cells of cosmos (`φ = (entity, predicate, entity)` in cosmos's own fiber, per [`cosmos`](../../classical-spacetime.md)), not epistemic acts.
