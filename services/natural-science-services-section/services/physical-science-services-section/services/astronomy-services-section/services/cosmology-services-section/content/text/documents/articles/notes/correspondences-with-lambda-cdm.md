# Correspondences with Lambda-CDM

The [cosmological settling](../objects/cosmological-settling.md) is intended to recover the [Lambda-CDM model](../../../glossary/terms/lambda-cdm-model.md) on its symmetric reduction.
This narrative collects, in one place, the proposed correspondences between Lambda-CDM observables and structures in the predicated framing — partly as a roadmap for what the founding work needs to deliver, partly as a check on whether the framing is in danger of becoming empty.

## Parameter-by-parameter

| Lambda-CDM parameter | Predicated counterpart | Status |
|---|---|---|
| $\Omega_b h^2$ — baryon density | Comoving-slice average of the baryon-predicate's truth-axis projection | Sketch — requires explicit baryon predicate construction |
| $\Omega_c h^2$ — cold dark matter density | Comoving-slice average of the dark-matter predicate's gravitational-projection truth-axis content; see [dark-sector-fiber](../../../glossary/terms/dark-sector-fiber.md) | Sketch |
| $\theta_*$ — angular size of sound horizon at recombination | The [light-cone restriction](../../../glossary/terms/light-cone-restriction.md) of the photon-baryon coupled sub-settling, evaluated at the [CMB quiescent frontier](cmb-quiescent-frontier.md) | Sketch |
| $\tau$ — optical depth to reionization | A second sub-settling re-firing — closure rule "neutral hydrogen + ionizing UV → ionized" — fired at $z \sim 8$, briefly disrupting the CMB sub-settling's quiescence | Open |
| $n_s$ — scalar spectral index | Tilt of the inflationary closure-rule's perturbation output spectrum; see [is-inflation-rapid-closure](is-inflation-rapid-closure.md) | Open |
| $A_s$ — amplitude of primordial fluctuations | Magnitude of the inflationary closure-rule's perturbation output | Open |

The pattern: standard parameters that are *averages* over the comoving slice translate cleanly (their predicated counterparts are well-defined functionals on the cosmological settling).
Parameters tied to *primordial* physics (inflation, the perturbation spectrum) translate only via [is-inflation-rapid-closure](is-inflation-rapid-closure.md), which is open.

## Equation-by-equation

| Lambda-CDM equation | Predicated counterpart | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Friedmann equations on $a(t)$ | The closure-rule system restricted to the $G_t$-symmetric subspace of valuations; see [cosmological-principle-as-symmetric-settling](cosmological-principle-as-symmetric-settling.md) | Draft — reduction conjectured, not yet derived |
| Boltzmann equations for the cosmological plasma | The closure-rule firings at the photon-baryon-electron sub-settling, with rates given by the standard cross-sections | Sketch |
| Einstein field equations | The closure-rule system on the metric-component predicates | Open — the most ambitious piece of the bridge |
| Linear perturbation theory | First-order expansion of the cosmological settling around the $G_t$-symmetric solution | Open |

The pattern: dynamical equations require the closure-rule system to be written down explicitly, and that work is unfinished.
The framing claims it is possible; the construction is the central open task.

## What this table is showing

The table is a self-audit.
Most rows are "sketch" or "open."
This is appropriate for a founding-stage theory but sets the agenda clearly: until "Friedmann equations" and "Einstein field equations" are at "draft" or better, the predicated framing is more programme than theory.

The entries that are at "translation complete" are the easy ones — observables that are already cell values in disguise.
The hard work is in the dynamics: writing down the closure-rule system that recovers Einstein's equations on the symmetric subspace.
That work is the primary technical task of the next round of development.
