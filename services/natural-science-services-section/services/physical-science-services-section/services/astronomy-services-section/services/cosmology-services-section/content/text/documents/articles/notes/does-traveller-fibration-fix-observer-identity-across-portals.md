# Does the traveller fibration fix observer identity across portals?

The [predicated-cosmology](../../predicated-cosmology/_index.md) subtheory has a category-theoretic [observer](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/observer.md) concept — a comoving frame from which evidence is registered.
The predicated-multiverse subtheory has a [traveller fibration](traveller-fibration.md) describing what kinds of things move across [portals](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/portal.md), and a [boundary primacy theorem](boundary-primacy-of-traveller-identity.md) saying that a traveller's post-jump identity depends only on its boundary state at the moment of crossing.

**Q.** When a predicated-cosmology observer is taken as a traveller-state, does the boundary-primacy theorem give a well-defined notion of *the same observer in a different world* — or does it merely give a destination-world observer-state that may or may not deserve the same identity?

This is the personal-identity-across-portals question, dressed in categorical clothing.
Two readings:

1. **Identity-preserving reading.** Boundary primacy gives a deterministic post-jump observer in the destination world; the observer's *boundary-determinable identity content* (whatever that turns out to be) is preserved by construction; the destination observer is "the same observer in another world" in any sense the framework can support.

2. **Identity-non-preserving reading.** Boundary primacy gives a destination state but says nothing about whether identity-preserving content has been preserved.
  The destination observer is a *new* observer-state that happens to be deterministically related to the source one, and personal-identity questions are unanswered (or, more honestly, are simply outside the framework's vocabulary).

## Why the question matters

Multiverse cosmology has a well-known philosophical knot at exactly this point — Everettian "self-locating" probabilities, Boltzmann-brain identity, the personal-identity-across-bubbles literature.
The Wood machinery may have something formal to say about it (reading 1) or may not (reading 2).
Either answer would be progress.

## Status

Open.
The question depends on what kind of object the predicated-cosmology observer is; specifically, whether observer-identity is "boundary-determinable content" in the technical sense the boundary-primacy axiom uses.
No analysis exists.
