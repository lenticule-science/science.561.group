# Causal evidence

The role borne by *evidence whose flow is constrained by the light-cone structure of [spacetime](../../../../../../../physics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/spacetime.md)*. Causal evidence is the predicated-cosmology specialization of the [evidence](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/evidence.md) role: it is what makes a [predication cell](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/predication-cell.md) move upward in the [knowledge order](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md), under the constraint that evidence about an event $e$ can only contribute to the value of cells whose receiving end is in the future light cone of $e$.

This constraint is what distinguishes cosmological evidence from the evidence considered in the parent [predication categories](../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) program. There, evidence is unconstrained: any source can in principle assert any cell. In cosmology, no signal travels faster than light. A measurement at event $e_2$ can carry evidence about $e_1$ only if $e_1$ is in the past [light cone](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/light-cone.md) of $e_2$.

## The two pressures

- **Causality.** Evidence about a cell $(e, p, e')$ can be received at observer-event $e_O$ only if both $e$ and $e'$ are in the past light cone of $e_O$ — and even then, only via a chain of causal influences whose first link is at the past light cone's intersection with the cell's coordinates. Cells with one coordinate outside the past light cone of every observer in the universe (e.g. cells beyond the [particle horizon](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/particle-horizon.md)) carry no evidence anywhere.
- **Locality of operations.** A closure rule firing at event $e$ can only consume evidence already present in the past light cone of $e$, and can only produce evidence at $e$ itself or its future light cone. The closure-rule system of predicated cosmology is *spacetime-local*: rules respect the light cone.

## Why this matters

The two-pressure structure of causal evidence is what makes [horizon-bounds-knowledge-order](../../documents/articles/notes/horizon-bounds-knowledge-order.md) a theorem rather than an axiom. The light-cone constraint is not added by hand — it is a structural fact about which [light-cone restrictions](light-cone-restriction.md) are admissible morphisms in the [light-cone predicate category](../../documents/articles/notes/light-cone-predicate-category.md). Causality becomes a feature of the morphism class rather than a separate metaphysical postulate.
