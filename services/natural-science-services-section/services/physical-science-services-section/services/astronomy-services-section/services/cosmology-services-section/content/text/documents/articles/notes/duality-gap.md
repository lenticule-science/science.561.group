**The duality-gap** is, under this reading, the Hubble tension itself: the gap between the two dual tropical eigenvalues of the [`cosmic-scott-skeleton`](cosmic-scott-skeleton.md).

## Verified closed form

$$\text{tension} \;=\; \lambda_w^{\max} - \lambda_w^{\min} \;=\; \frac{|w_a - w_b|}{2}.$$

(From the benchmark §2E closed forms; verified.)

## Vanishes iff thermal

The gap is zero **iff $w_a = w_b$** — iff the skeleton is balanced, iff the thermalizing has *converged*.
It is nonzero **precisely when the weights are imbalanced = the spiral is unconverged = we are in cosmic-scott (κ=ω), not thermal.** A thermal (heat-death, cosmic-finite) cosmos has $w_a = w_b$ and zero tension. **So a nonzero Hubble tension is the observable signature of being not-yet-thermal** — derived here, not asserted.
This is the quantitative face of the cell's [stage-offset](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/math/hubble-tension.md): $g(z) \to 0$ only as the cosmos thermalizes.

## Bracketing

The two readings **bracket** the thermal value: $\lambda_w^{\min}$ (the CMB reading) $\le H_0^{\text{thermal}} \le \lambda_w^{\max}$ (the ladder reading), meeting only at convergence ($w_a = w_b$, heat death). **The "true" $H_0$ is not ~67 or ~73 but the (currently unreachable) value they would agree on at thermalization.**

## Which dual is which pipeline

That the CMB reads $\lambda_w^{\min}$ and the ladder $\lambda_w^{\max}$ (fixed by the observed sign $\lambda^{\max} \ge \lambda^{\min}$ and ladder ≈73 > CMB ≈67) is recorded with the [`conflating`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/logic-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/conflating.md) morphism, which also shows the two duals are *forced* to exist, not chosen.
