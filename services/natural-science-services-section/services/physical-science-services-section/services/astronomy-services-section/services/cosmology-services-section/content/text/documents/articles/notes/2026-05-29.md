# 2026-05-29 — cosmogenerating: first scaffold

Brought the long-dormant Hubble-tension work back, this time onto the live spine instead of the predecessor "Reflexive Existence / relationality" language it was written in.

## The move

The old framing put cosmology as a *domain* (`relationality/domains/cosmology`) — a downstream application that inherits relational machinery.
The user reframed it: **cosmology is a construction of articulation; the real world exists because of articulation, and that is the thesis we are trying to prove via empirical correspondence.** So cosmos belongs at the *top*, under the aperiodic-articulation-category, as a generator morphism — not at the settling end as a TAB-style domain.

That fits the regime classification exactly (GENEALOGY §1, §5): the aperiodic corner ($\kappa \geq \omega_1$, $H^1 \neq 0$) is the only one that **extends the base with new entities/facts** and **needs the outside**.
The cosmos is that base-extension; the outside is empirical correspondence.
