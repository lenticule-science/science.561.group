A research subtheory of cosmology that treats the inter-world structure of a [multiverse](../multiverse/terms/multiverse.md) categorically. Worlds are objects (nuclei) in a relational ambient category; each carries quasicrystalline structure and an internal flow defining its allowable relational transitions. *Holes* are subobjects exhibiting structural degeneration with mandatory boundary factorization. *Portals* are morphisms from holes into fully structured worlds. The central object is the **[Wood Between the Worlds](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/library/wood-between-worlds.md)**: the universal mediating nucleus that is initial among all mediators of a given family of worlds.

This is the multiversal counterpart to the sibling [predicated-cosmology](../predicated-cosmology/_index.md) subtheory. Predicated-cosmology models *one* universe as a settling; predicated-multiverse models *populations* of worlds together with the categorical fabric that connects them. The two share the underlying [predication-categories](../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/_index.md) machinery and are *intended* to compose — but whether they actually do is open work, recorded as the bridge questions [does-cosmological-settling-induce-world-structure](does-cosmological-settling-induce-world-structure.md) (multiverse side) and [does-this-cosmology-extend-to-a-multiverse](does-this-cosmology-extend-to-a-multiverse.md) (cosmology side). The composition is not assumed.

## Core claim

Any inter-world mediator factors uniquely (up to isotropy) through the Wood. This makes the Wood a canonical, structurally-determined object whose existence and uniqueness can be argued from three axioms — isotropy, hole-degeneration, and the universal mediating property — without invoking any specific multiverse mechanism. Whether the cosmologist is thinking [eternal-inflation](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/eternal-inflation.md)-style bubbles, Everettian branches, or string-landscape vacua, the structural picture of how worlds can be connected is the same.

## What this subtheory is and is not

- **It is** a structural framework: a way to talk precisely about worlds, the inter-world medium, portals, and traveller-states using categorical vocabulary. It generates well-typed objects and provable theorems about their relations.
- **It is not** a physical theory of multiverses. It does not predict observable consequences. It does not pick out which of the [Tegmark levels](../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/tegmark-classification.md) is actually realized. It does not derive the existence of any specific other world.
- **It is not** a claim that the multiverse is real. The categorical structure is consistent whether zero, one, or many worlds beyond ours exist; the apparatus describes how *if* multiple worlds exist, they would have to relate.

## Imported research

This subtheory translates a body of pre-predication-theory research into the predicated framing:

- **The Wood Between the Worlds** — a categorical formulation of a universal liminal mediator across worlds, originally written in a relational quasicrystalline hypertensor topos. Source: [`wood-between-worlds.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/library/wood-between-worlds.md). Translated into the [Wood Between the Worlds](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research/predicated-agentic-orchestration-platform/library/relational-hyperverse-repository/library/wood-between-worlds.md), [world as nucleus](world-as-nucleus.md), the [hole](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hole.md) / [portal](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/portal.md) / [mediating nucleus](mediating-nucleus.md) concepts, and the [Wood uniqueness](wood-uniqueness-up-to-isotropy.md) and [Wood initiality](wood-as-initial-mediator.md) theorems.

- **The Hypertensor Topos specification (HTT-SPEC v1)** — the ambient categorical setting in which the Wood originally lived, axiomatizing trace sites, Heyting–modal fiber algebras, the hypertensor reindexing functor, and structural closure. Source: [`quasicrystalline-hypertensor-topos-specification.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/quasicrystalline-hypertensor-topos-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/quasicrystalline-hypertensor-topos-specification.md). Cited as the upstream ambient setting; this subtheory uses the Wood-axiom layer rather than the full HTT-SPEC.

- **Voidmath (infinite + finite)** — a "void mycelium" study of the substrate from which worlds-and-portals structure emerges, deliberately retaining non-confluence, asymmetry, and the absence of equality. Sources: [`infinite-voidmath.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/infinite-voidmath.md), [`finite-voidmath.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/finite-voidmath.md). Voidmath is **not** translated as cosmology — it is foundational paradox-math, properly upstream of any cosmological reading. It is cited in [is voidmath the substrate of worlds](is-voidmath-the-substrate-of-worlds.md) as the candidate substrate for the world-tower the Wood mediates.

## Status

Draft, founding stage. The categorical apparatus is in place — objects are well-typed, dependencies discharge, and the two central theorems (uniqueness, initiality) are stated with their original arguments. The cosmological cash-out — what concrete prediction or structural insight this picture buys for working multiverse cosmology — is sketch-level only. The questions section flags the open work.
