An utterance is the ordered pair of one token and one claim.
It is the carrier articulating's act is applied to.

## Utterance is a carrier, not an act

An utterance does not articulate anything; it is the structured pair that articulating takes as input.
The two-sidedness — token on one side, claim on the other — is what gives articulating's codomain its four-cell partition.
Without the pair structure, articulating would have only two articulation-statuses to track (articulated or not); the pair gives four.

## Position in the chain

The chain is utterance, then articulating which takes an utterance and produces a charged form, then the four aperiodic-predication-cells which partition articulating's outputs by which side of the input got articulated.
Utterance sits at the bottom of this chain — it is the structure articulating presupposes, not a structure articulating constructs.

## Primitive utterance bottoms out the recursion

Articulating is the recursive pair [utterance, articulating].
The first slot is the utterance being articulated; the second slot is another articulating, recursively.
The recursion has no schema-level terminator.
Concrete instances terminate when an innermost utterance is reached whose token and claim are the non-assembling primitives — at that point the recursion bottoms out at the string-typed leaves and the whole nested structure becomes finite.

This is the proof-engine character of the potential tree: the recursive type has no finite inhabitant unless the recursion is grounded in primitives.
The two non-assembling primitives token and claim are exactly the grounding.

## Token-before-claim ordering

The schema lists token first, claim second.
The ordering matters for reading the aperiodic-predication-cell partition: under the token-before-claim working theory, articulating naturally proceeds by articulating the token side first; the cells potential (no, no) → active (yes, no) → absurd (yes, yes) are the natural progression and stable (no, yes) is off that path.
The reversed claim-before-token reading and a symmetric reading are alternatives.
