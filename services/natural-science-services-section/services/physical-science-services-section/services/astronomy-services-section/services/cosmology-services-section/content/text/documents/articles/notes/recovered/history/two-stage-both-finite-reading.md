# The two-stage reading — right structure, wrong band (superseded)

Recorded for provenance. This note proposed a **two-stage, one-band** reading of the tension —
which turned out to be the *correct structure* — but placed both stages in the wrong band
(cosmic-finite). The corrected mapping (see
[`cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow`](../../../../../../../../docs/dev/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md))
puts the one band at **cosmic-scott**, and that two-stage / both-scott reading is now the
**primary** [hubble-tension](../../../hubble-tension.md) reading.

## The reading as recorded (both-finite — wrong)

Both pipelines read at **different stages** of [[cosmic-settlement]]:

- late-time at τ_now (the [[observer-stage]]);
- early-time at τ_CMB (recombination), then carried to τ_now by [[flow]].

The original note placed both in the **cosmic-finite** band (plastic ρ), making the tension a
stage-offset along one *finite* spiral.

## Why the band was wrong

Cosmic-finite (κ<ω, plastic ρ) is **heat death** — a fully quiesced, observerless state. An
observer reading H₀ cannot be in it. We are an *unsettled* (κ=ω) observer, so the one band both
pipelines share is **cosmic-scott** (golden φ), not finite. The note's structure — one band, two
stages τ — was right; its band was wrong.

## What survives

The structural insight (one band, two stages of an unconverged spiral; the tension as a stage
offset, not a cross-band category error) is exactly the corrected primary reading, with band =
cosmic-scott. The "more conservative, explains less" framing is obsolete: the both-scott reading
*is* the primary one, and the φ^(1/6) coincidence is now a within-scott stage ratio (see the
numerology note), not a φ-vs-ρ band mechanism that this reading lacked.
