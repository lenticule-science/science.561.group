**vacuum-is-padovan**: the relational vacuum carries the plastic number ρ, not φ.

The vacuum is the **thermal fixed point** $R = U_G(R)$ = the archive's $T_{\mathrm{bit}}$ = the [`cosmic-finite`](../../../../../../../../physics-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/cosmic-finite-thermalization-algebra.md) endpoint (κ<ω, heat death).
The thermal regime carries the plastic number **ρ ≈ 1.3247** on the off-n-bonacci branch (see the [grounding note](cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md)).

## Why ρ is forced

The archive characterizes the vacuum $Q$ as **minimal and uniquely ergodic**.
Among self-similar substitution structures the minimal (smallest Pisot) growth rate is the **plastic number ρ** (Siegel 1944: ρ is the smallest Pisot number — no Pisot lies between 1 and ρ). φ ≈ 1.618 is only the smallest *limit point* of the Pisot set, not the minimum.
So "minimal + uniquely ergodic" forces the unique eigenvalue ρ.

This closes the archive's Golden-Stability conjecture piece (c) — "minimality-uniqueness forces a unique eigenvalue" — which holds with ρ as that eigenvalue.

- present-epoch **dynamics** (cosmic-scott, not-yet-thermal): golden **φ** — the [`cosmic-scott-skeleton`](cosmic-scott-skeleton.md).
- thermal **endpoint** (cosmic-finite, the vacuum): plastic **ρ**.
