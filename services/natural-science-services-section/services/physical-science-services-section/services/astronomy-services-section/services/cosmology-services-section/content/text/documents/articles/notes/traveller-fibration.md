# Traveller fibration

A **traveller fibration** is a fibration $\mathrm{Trav}$ over the ambient relational category $\mathsf{R}$ such that:

- For each nucleus $X \in \mathsf{R}$, the fibre $\mathrm{Trav}(X)$ is the category of **traveller-states over $X$** — entities whose internal structure can be supported on $X$.
- Each morphism $f : X \to Y$ in $\mathsf{R}$ induces a **transport functor** $f_* : \mathrm{Trav}(X) \to \mathrm{Trav}(Y)$ that pushes traveller-states along $f$.

Travellers are the things-that-can-cross.
Their formal home is a fibration so that a traveller's state at one nucleus maps coherently to a state at any nucleus reached by an ambient morphism, in particular by a [portal morphism](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/portal-morphism.md).

## What this object is doing

The traveller fibration is the categorical home for any account of *what crosses* a [portal](../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/portal.md).
Without it, portals would be pure structural connections in $\mathsf{R}$ with nothing of cosmological content moving across them.
The traveller fibration is the slot into which such content is plugged: in any concrete cosmological reading, the traveller objects could be matter-content, observer-states, information-quanta, or whatever the cosmology in question takes as its primary moving entity.

The fibration formulation — rather than a single set of traveller-states — is what allows different worlds to support different traveller types while still admitting transport between them, as long as ambient morphisms exist.
