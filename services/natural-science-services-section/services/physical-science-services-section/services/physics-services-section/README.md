# Physics Services Section

This section holds physics as studied by Lenticule Science, a sibling of
`chemistry-services-section`, `astronomy-services-section`, and
`optics-services-section` under `physical-science-services-section`.

Its scope is the parent section's frame: nature under the four regimes of
natural existence carried at
[four regimes of natural existence](../../../../content/text/glossary/topics/four-regimes-of-natural-existence.md)
— thermodynamic, causal, quantum, and kinetic existence, each the natural
referent of one stratum of a settling problem's closure ordinal $\kappa$ at
the ordinal boundaries $\omega$ and $\omega_1$. This section's glossary
states each of those regimes' standard physics content in its own normal
literature terms first — causal sets, global time functions, contextuality,
the PR box, the Boltzmann equation, molecular chaos, the BBGKY hierarchy,
magnetic reconnection, the Boltzmann H-theorem — and only then names where
Obsmetrologia's Lean proof tree extends or instantiates the standard result.
