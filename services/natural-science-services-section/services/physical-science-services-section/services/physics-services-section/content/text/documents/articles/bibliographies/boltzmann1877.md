# Boltzmann 1877 — Über die Beziehung zwischen dem zweiten Hauptsatze der mechanischen Wärmetheorie und der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung

*Sitzungsberichte der Kaiserlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Mathematisch-
Naturwissenschaftliche Classe* (Vienna) 76, 1877, pp. 373–435.

## Why this is central

This paper gives entropy its statistical-mechanical meaning: $S = k \log W$, where $W$
is the number of microstates (complexions) consistent with a given macroscopic
(coarse-grained) configuration. It reframes the second law of thermodynamics —
entropy does not decrease — as a statement about which macrostates are overwhelmingly
more probable, answering Loschmidt's objection to the 1872 H-theorem (that a
time-reversible dynamics cannot produce a time-asymmetric law) by relocating the
asymmetry from dynamics to counting: almost all microstates compatible with a
low-entropy macrostate evolve toward higher-entropy macrostates simply because there
are vastly more microstates realizing them.

## What it does not settle

The paper does not derive $S = k \log W$ from a fully rigorous account of the relevant
probability measure on phase space (ergodic theory, which formalizes when time averages
equal phase-space averages, is a later 20th-century development); it establishes the
combinatorial/statistical picture rather than closing every technical gap in the
justification of statistical mechanics from mechanics.
