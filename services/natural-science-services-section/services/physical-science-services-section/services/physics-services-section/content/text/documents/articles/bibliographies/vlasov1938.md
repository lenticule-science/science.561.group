# Vlasov 1938 — On Vibration Properties of Electron Gas

*Zhurnal Eksperimentalnoi i Teoreticheskoi Fiziki* 8, 1938, p. 291.

## Why this is central

This paper introduces the collisionless kinetic equation for a plasma — now called the
**Vlasov equation** — which drops the binary-collision term of the Boltzmann equation
and replaces it with the mean (self-consistent) electromagnetic field the whole charge
distribution generates. It is the founding equation of collisionless plasma kinetic
theory: phenomena such as Landau damping and, at the level this house corpus treats
computationally, magnetic reconnection are analyzed against a Vlasov (or Vlasov–Maxwell)
description of the plasma rather than a collisional Boltzmann one.

## What it does not settle

The paper proposes the equation and its physical motivation; the rigorous mathematical
theory of its solutions (existence, uniqueness, the Vlasov–Poisson and Vlasov–Maxwell
well-posedness theory) is a later 20th-century mathematical-physics development, not
part of this paper.
