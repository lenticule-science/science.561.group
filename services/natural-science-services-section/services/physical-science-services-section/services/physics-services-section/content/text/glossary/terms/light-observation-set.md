The report states: "we record how null geodesics — light rays — emitted from hidden interior events reach an observation surface", and "every interior event is uniquely identified by the incidence pattern of its light projection on the boundary" (`GR` section 3). The table in that section pairs the interior event point with its **light observation set** (arrival points on the boundary), causal order with the temporal arrival sequence of light fronts, and conformal geometry with boundary incidence patterns of light cones.

The report attributes the method to Hintz and Uhlmann; the attribution and the recovery claim are unchecked here.
