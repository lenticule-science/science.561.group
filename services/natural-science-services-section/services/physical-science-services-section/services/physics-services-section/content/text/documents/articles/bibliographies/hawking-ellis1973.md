# Hawking & Ellis 1973 — The Large Scale Structure of Space-Time

Cambridge University Press, 1973 (Cambridge Monographs on Mathematical Physics).

## Why this is central

This is the standard reference for global causal structure in general relativity,
including the **stable causality theorem**: a spacetime is stably causal — remains free
of closed causal curves under small perturbations of its metric — if and only if it
admits a **global time function**, a smooth real-valued map strictly increasing along
every future-directed causal curve. This equivalence is the continuum theorem the house
term [global time function](../../../glossary/terms/global-time-function.md) names as its
standard-literature anchor, and which the Obsmetrologia corpus's discrete
`timeSection_strict_mono` refines to a causal set.

## What it does not settle

The book's causality hierarchy (stable causality, strong causality, global hyperbolicity)
concerns continuum Lorentzian manifolds; it does not itself address discrete causal
structures such as causal sets, nor construct a discrete analogue of the global time
function — that discrete refinement is the corpus's own contribution.
