The report lists the **time-separation function** `tau` as an asymmetric function recording maximal proper time, "satisfying the **Reverse Triangle Inequality**: `tau(p,r) >= tau(p,q) + tau(q,r)`" (`GR` section 2). In section 5 it states that on a Lorentzian manifold `tau` is continuous and obeys the inequality for timelike maximizers. In the discrete setting of causal sets, volume is recovered by counting elements within a causal interval.

The report's "Causal Metric Hypothesis" attributed to Hu, Meyer and Meyer proposes that distances between causally related pairs determine spacelike separations; the attribution is unchecked.
