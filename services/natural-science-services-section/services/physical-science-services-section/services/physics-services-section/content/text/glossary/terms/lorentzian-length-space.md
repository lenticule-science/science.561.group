The report states that, to move from discrete causal relations to a formal Lorentzian length space, three components are required (`GR` section 2): (1) a **topology**, a structured space for events; (2) **causal and chronological relations**, definitions for timelike and causal connections; (3) a `time-separation-function`. It reads a Git history as a Lorentzian `causal-set` in which commits are events and parent edges are the causal order.

The report gives no axioms and no source for the term. The standard references (Kunzinger and Saemann, 2018) are outside the held notes and unchecked.
