# Informational spacetime: the aperiodic opening

**Informational spacetime** names the aperiodic field of distinguishable relational possibilities in the original four-regime cosmogenesis notes. Our current conjecture reads this field **kinematically**: it specifies which relations and histories could be articulated before any particular quantum amplitudes, spacetime metric, or thermodynamic equilibrium are selected.

Information here is a *difference articulated within relating*, not a fully formed binary digit imposed at the beginning. The generative direction is marks and utterances toward predication, contextual valuations, and stable physical readouts. See [the relationality program](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/README.md).
