# Cosmic thermalization

> **Reframe (merge):** the band-names map to fields — cosmic-scott = **classical-spacetime**, cosmic-finite = **thermal-spacetime**, thermalizing = **thermalizing**.
> "Cosmos's own tower" is the descent itself; the H=λ / stage-offset derivation below holds unchanged under that mapping.

A particular [[thermalization]] living at the floor of the descent — **thermal-spacetime** ([[cosmic-finite-thermalization-algebra]]).
It realizes the cosmic expansion history as the orbit of a [[thermalizing-endofunctor]] whose increment-sequence is the [[cosmic-increment-sequence]] (the scale-factor history a(τ)).

$$ v_\tau(\text{reached-scale})(a) \;=\; \begin{cases} t & a \in \{a_0, a_1, \dots, a_\tau\} \\ \bot & \text{otherwise,}\end{cases} \qquad a_{\tau+1} = \text{(next epoch's scale factor)}. $$

As with [[collatz-settlement]], the monotone quantity is the **knowledge order** (the set of reached epochs only grows), not any single value — so the family is a genuine thermalization.

## H₀ is the thermalization-spiral's inflation factor

The Hubble parameter is a *growth rate*: $H = \dot a / a$.
The [[thermalization-spiral]] theorem ([[residuation-forces-a-spiral]]) says every residuation-driven thermalizing carries a logarithmic spiral whose inflation factor λ is the Pisot number of the regime band. **Identify H₀ with that λ at the stage it is read.** We observe from inside an *not-yet-thermal* cosmos, so the reading is taken at the **cosmic-scott** stage (κ=ω, λ = **golden φ ≈ 1.618**; cosmic-scott = λ_2 on the n-bonacci flow — see the [grounding note](../../../../../../astronomy-services-section/services/cosmology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/cosmic-regimes-as-nbonacci-rg-flow.md)).
Both pipelines sit in this band:

- the **late-time** distance ladder, read at the present stage τ_now: **later** cosmic-scott, λ = φ.
- the **early-time** CMB, propagated forward by [[flow]] from stage τ_CMB: **earlier** cosmic-scott, λ = φ.

The two "values of H₀" are **two stage-readings of the one golden spiral**, which is the content of the Hubble stage-offset ([[hubble-tension]]).
The thermalization reaches its cosmic-finite floor (κ<ω, plastic ρ) only at **heat death**, where the spiral finally quiesces — no observer reads there.

## Position — cross-tower sibling of collatz-settlement

A `library/` child of [[thermalization]], so a particular thermalization instance — but placed in **cosmos's own tower**, because a cosmic-thermalization *comes from cosmos* (the generates-rule puts it under cosmos, not beside collatz in the root tower).
It is therefore the **cross-tower analog** of [[collatz-settlement]]: the same κ-instrument applied at opposite ends —

- **Collatz** is *finite-from-below*: an integer orbit in the **root** tower; the conjecture is "does every seed stay in κ<ω?"
- **Cosmos** is *aperiodic-from-above*: generated at the aperiodic top by `cosmogenerating`, re-descending through its **own** tower; the present expansion history is read at the κ=ω **cosmic-scott** stage (both the local ladder and the CMB), and reaches κ<ω only at heat death.

Both are thermal by the same [[thermalization-spiral]] Pisot tower (ρ < φ < τ_trib → 2); cosmos currently sits at the **φ rung** (scott), having descended from the aperiodic top through transfinite (the Big Bang being the transfinite→scott step).
That the *same* construction appears in two different towers is the fractal claim made concrete.
