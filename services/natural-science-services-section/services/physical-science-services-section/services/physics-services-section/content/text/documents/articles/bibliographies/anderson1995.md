# Anderson 1995 — Through the Glass Lightly

*Science* 267(5204), 1995, pp. 1615–1616.

## Why this is central

A short opinion piece stating, in Anderson's own words, that "the deepest and most
interesting unsolved problem in solid state theory is probably the theory of the nature of
glass and the glass transition." This is the flagship framing this section's glass work
reads against: not one problem among many but the field's own naming of its hardest open
question, from one of solid-state physics's most authoritative voices. The piece is a
position statement, not a derivation — its weight is in naming the problem's stakes, not
in solving any part of it.

## What it does not settle

The piece states the problem's difficulty; it does not itself advance a resolution,
adjudicate between RFOT and dynamical-facilitation accounts of the transition, or propose
a research program. Later reviews (Berthier & Biroli 2011) carry the field's actual
technical content.
