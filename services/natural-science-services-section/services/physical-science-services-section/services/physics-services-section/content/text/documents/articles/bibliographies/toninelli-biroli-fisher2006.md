# Toninelli, Biroli & Fisher 2006 — Jamming Percolation and Glass Transitions in Lattice Models

*Physical Review Letters* 96(3), 2006, art. 035702.

## Why this is central

Introduces the **Spiral Model**, a two-dimensional kinetically constrained model that,
unlike the Fredrickson–Andersen and East models, has a genuine sharp jamming transition:
above a critical density `p_c ≈ 0.705` of permanently blocked sites, a positive fraction of
the lattice freezes irreversibly, with the transition in the universality class of directed
site percolation. This is the paper this section's measurement instantiates directly as the
`spiral` model, and the paper whose transition this section's note reads as the case where
a facilitation closure's jamming and a KCM's dynamical glassiness genuinely coincide — in
contrast to the FA and East models, where (per this section's negative-control measurement)
no such jamming exists at any finite density.

## What it does not settle

The paper establishes the jamming transition's existence and universality class; the
precise facilitation rule for the Spiral Model (the diagonal-neighbor-pair rule, as opposed
to a naive orthogonal four-neighbor rule) needed independent confirmation against Biroli &
Toninelli's own later exposition (arXiv:0709.0583, §2) during this section's measurement
work, and the paper does not itself address whether this jamming transition is the same
kind of event as the glass transition observed in real structural glass formers, or a
distinct (if related) phenomenon — the question this section's note takes up directly (§4).
