# Elmatad, Chandler & Garrahan 2009 — Corresponding States of Structural Glass Formers

*Journal of Physical Chemistry B* 113(16), 2009, pp. 5563–5567.

## Why this is central

Shows that the relaxation times of many structural glass formers, over a wide range of
fragility, collapse onto a single **parabolic law** in inverse temperature when expressed
in reduced (corresponding-states) variables — the empirical signature dynamical
facilitation theory predicts from East-model-style hierarchical relaxation (Sollich & Evans
1999). This is the paper this section's note cites for the parabolic law itself, and the
paper the proposed next instrument (§6, a stochastic KCM run with acknowledgement-event
counting) is meant to test the nested-facilitation-chain reading of §4 against.

## What it does not settle

The corresponding-states fit is empirical: it shows many real glass formers' relaxation
data collapse onto the parabolic form predicted by East-model-style facilitation, but it
does not itself prove that dynamical facilitation (as opposed to some other mechanism
producing the same functional form) is the correct microscopic account of real glass
formers — the same open question the field's competing RFOT and facilitation schools
divide over.
