The report states: modern microlocal geometry treats "the **principal symbol** of the wave operator and its associated **singular support** as the primary objects of study", and analyzes "how singularities propagate along **null bicharacteristics** — the **Hamiltonian flow** of the characteristic variety" (`GR` section 1). The characteristic variety is the cotangent geometry `sigma(P) = 0` where non-smooth information travels (`GR` section 1, "Characteristic Variety").

In section 4 the report states that for linear waves, singularities "propagate independently along null bicharacteristics", while nonlinear interactions create new singularities at the intersection points of null hypersurfaces.

The report does not define the Hamiltonian flow or give a source; its attributions (Hintz and Uhlmann, Kurylev, Lassas and Uhlmann) were not checked.
