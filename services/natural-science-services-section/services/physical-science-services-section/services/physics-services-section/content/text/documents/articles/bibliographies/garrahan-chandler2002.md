# Garrahan & Chandler 2002 — Geometrical Explanation and Scaling of Dynamical Heterogeneities in Glass Forming Systems

*Physical Review Letters* 89(3), 2002, art. 035704.

## Why this is central

A founding paper of **dynamical facilitation theory**, the rival account to RFOT: it
argues glassy slowing-down and dynamic heterogeneity (spatially and temporally correlated
regions of fast and slow relaxation) arise purely kinetically, from the geometry of
trajectories in kinetically constrained models, with no underlying thermodynamic
singularity required. Garrahan & Chandler followed this the next year (2003, "Coarse-grained
microscopic model of glass formers," *PNAS* 100(17), 9710–9714) with a coarse-grained
facilitated lattice model realizing the same picture more explicitly; this section's note
cites both years together as the theory's founding statement. This is the theoretical
position this section's house Lean glass line (the deterministic facilitation closure of
`KCM.lean`) is a finite skeleton of — with the rates that carry the theory's actual
glassiness explicitly deleted, per that module's own header.

## What it does not settle

The paper argues for a kinetic account against a thermodynamic one; it does not itself
disprove RFOT's Kauzmann-temperature claim, and the two schools' relationship (whether they
are genuinely rival, or complementary descriptions of the same underlying physics) remains
an open question in the field it surveys.
