# Landauer 1961 — Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process

*IBM Journal of Research and Development* 5, 1961, pp. 183–191.

## Why this is central

This paper establishes that logically irreversible operations — most simply, erasing a
bit — must dissipate at least $kT \ln 2$ of heat, tying an abstract information-theoretic
quantity (bits erased) to a physical thermodynamic quantity (heat dissipated). It is the
paper the house term [Landauer's principle](../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/services/biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/landauers-principle.md)
carries the full definition, history, and calibrated-status discussion for; this carrier
exists only to give the physics section's own citekey (used by [Boltzmann H-theorem](../../../glossary/terms/boltzmann-h-theorem.md),
whose axiom profile in Obsmetrologia is exactly this principle's Lean-side accounting)
a resolvable bibliography entry within its own scope.
