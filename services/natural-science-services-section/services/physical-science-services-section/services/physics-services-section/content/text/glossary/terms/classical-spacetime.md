# Classical causal spacetime: the Scott-continuous reading

**Classical causal spacetime** is the regime in which effective events, worldlines, causal order, and geometric measurements become stable enough to read as a spacetime. We conjecture that it is generated from the relational quantum regime rather than supplied as its permanent stage.

In general relativity, a Lorentzian metric and gravitational field equations describe how causal structure and geometry interact with matter and energy. The relationality program seeks to derive the corresponding **effective classical data** from the preceding amplitude and incidence structures. We place this reading at the **Scott-continuous** stage of a [Turn](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/concepts/turn.md). That source owns the complete regime and recurrence/Pisot conjecture.

The first arrow must yield a causal relation and suitable local geometric observables. The second carries dynamics into a thermodynamic [finite settlement](thermal-spacetime.md). Scott continuity describes the proposed closure behavior, not a substitution for Einstein's equations.

Our causal spacetime is **before thermodynamics** in this construction because energy transport, entropy production, and equilibrium must be defined on a field with physical states and causal relations. The actual arrow of time is part of what the spiral aims to explain, from directed relation and irreversible admission to effective physical evolution.
