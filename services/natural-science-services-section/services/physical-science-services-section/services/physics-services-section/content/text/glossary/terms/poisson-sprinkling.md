The report states: "the bridge between discrete data sets and continuum manifolds is established through **Poisson Sprinkling**, a discretization method that maintains Lorentz-invariance in distribution" (`GR` section 6). The continuum limit varies volume `V` and density `rho` to recover volume, dimension (through the Myrheim-Meyer estimator) and causal structure.

For a sprinkled settling the report names four random variables: `kappa^(V)`, a random ordinal for the closure of the settling dynamics; `|Apex|`, the count of causal relations; `|Void|`, the count of spacelike-separated pairs; and `|L_ns|`, the firing counts of the settling process.
