# Ritort & Sollich 2003 — Glassy dynamics of kinetically constrained models

*Advances in Physics* 52(4), 2003, pp. 219–342.

## Why this is central

The standard, book-length review of **kinetically constrained models (KCMs)**: lattice
models whose equilibrium measure is trivial (typically an uncorrelated product measure)
but whose dynamics is constrained — a site may change state only when a facilitation
condition on its neighbors is met. It surveys the full family this section's note draws
on: the Fredrickson–Andersen model, the East model, and (in the KCM tradition
Toninelli, Biroli & Fisher later extend) constraint-satisfaction glass models more
broadly, together with the field's methods for studying dynamic heterogeneity,
relaxation-time divergence, and facilitation-driven slow dynamics. This is the review this
section's note cites for the KCM family as a whole, rather than any one member of it.

## What it does not settle

As a review, it surveys rather than resolves; in particular it does not settle whether
KCM-style dynamical facilitation is the correct microscopic account of real structural
glass formers (as opposed to RFOT's thermodynamic account) — the same open question
Berthier & Biroli's later review states directly.
