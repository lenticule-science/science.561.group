# Klyachko, Can, Binicioğlu & Shumovsky 2008 — Simple Test for Hidden Variables in Spin-1 Systems

*Physical Review Letters* 101, 2008, 020403.

## Why this is central

This paper introduces the **KCBS scenario**: five yes/no measurements arranged so that
each pair of cyclically adjacent measurements is jointly measurable (compatible) and
mutually exclusive when both answer "yes," while non-adjacent pairs are not jointly
measurable at all. The measurement compatibility structure is a five-cycle $C_5$. Every
non-contextual hidden-variable model is bounded by the independence number
$\alpha(C_5) = 2$ (at most two of the five outcomes can be "yes" simultaneously, in
expectation over any single run), while quantum mechanics on a spin-1 system violates
this bound. It is the simplest known state-independent contextuality test with no
entanglement required — contextuality shown on a single particle.

## What it does not settle

The paper establishes the bound and its quantum violation for the specific spin-1
construction; the general theory relating exclusivity graphs, their independence and
Lovász numbers, and the maximal quantum violation across all such graphs is the later
"graph-theoretic approach to contextuality" literature (Cabello, Severini & Winter and
successors), not this paper.
