# Boltzmann 1872 — Weitere Studien über das Wärmegleichgewicht unter Gasmolekülen

*Sitzungsberichte der Kaiserlichen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Mathematisch-
Naturwissenschaftliche Classe* (Vienna) 66, 1872, pp. 275–370.

## Why this is central

This is the paper that introduces the **Boltzmann equation**, the evolution equation for
a dilute gas's one-particle phase-space distribution function under free streaming and
binary collisions, and proves the **H-theorem**: the quantity $H = \int f \log f$ is
monotonically non-increasing under the collision dynamics, with equality only at the
Maxwell–Boltzmann equilibrium distribution. This is the founding derivation of
irreversible macroscopic behavior (entropy increase) from reversible microscopic
mechanics, and the source of the *Stosszahlansatz* (molecular chaos hypothesis) needed to
close the equation at one-particle level.

## What it does not settle

The H-theorem's apparent tension with microscopic time-reversibility (Loschmidt's
objection) is not resolved within this paper; Boltzmann's later statistical
reinterpretation of entropy (Boltzmann 1877) is the response, and the full mathematical
justification of the *Stosszahlansatz* — that it holds, propagated in time, for a genuine
many-particle system in a suitable limit — awaits Lanford's rigorous derivation
(Lanford 1975).
