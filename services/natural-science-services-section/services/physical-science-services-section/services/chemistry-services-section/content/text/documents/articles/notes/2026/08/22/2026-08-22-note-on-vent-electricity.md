# Vent Electricity

## 0. The circuit is real and measured

Deep-sea hydrothermal vents run a continuously measured electrochemical circuit that requires no light: a natural battery between the hot, reduced fluid inside a chimney and the cold, oxygenated seawater outside, conducted through the chimney's own sulfide minerals.
This is settled, quantitative, and independent of any origin-of-life proposal — though it has become load-bearing for one of the estate's own, the [dark-compass hypothesis note](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md).
The circuit is one of three vent energy channels this session mapped; the mineral substrate it runs through is covered in the [vent magnetization note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/mineralogy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-vent-magnetization-and-host-rock.md), and the vent's photon budget — a much weaker channel — in the [vent light note](../../../../../../../../../../../biological-science-services-section/services/ecology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-vent-light-and-vent-photobiology.md).
The [Vacuous Entanglement-Normalizing Topos](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/vacuous-entanglement-normalizing-topos.md) (**VENT**) term names the real hydrothermal vent this circuit runs in.

## 1. The demonstration chain

Nakamura et al. (2010, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 49(42):7692–4) showed a black-smoker chimney wall conducts electrons metal-like between an anodic interior (sulfide oxidation in vent fluid) and a cathodic exterior (oxygen reduction in seawater) — the chimney as fuel-cell electrode and membrane at once, pyrite- and chalcopyrite-rich walls as the conductor (confirmed via PubMed/Wiley records and an independent RIKEN summary corroborating the fuel-cell-electrode framing; the primary Wiley text itself was paywalled this pass, so treat as search synthesis on the exact wording).

Yamamoto et al. (2013, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 52(41):10758–61) then wired the gradient to an actual environmental fuel cell and **lit an LED on the seafloor** — electricity, not light, powering a light.
The demonstration is more quantified than the estate's earlier framing carried: the cell generated at least 21 mW (0.42 W/m² of anode), exceeding prior benthic microbial fuel cells' 0.25 W/m² (confirmed via PubMed, RIKEN, phys.org, and ResearchGate — search synthesis, primary text not fetched).

Yamamoto et al. (2017, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 56(21):5725–5728, "Spontaneous and Widespread Electricity Generation in Natural Deep-Sea Hydrothermal Fields") extended it field-wide across the Okinawa Trough: mineral-deposit surfaces hold consistently negative redox potentials against seawater, and the resulting spontaneous current flows across the whole field — not just at active orifices, but at sites "electrochemically connected to subseafloor hydrothermal fluids" at a distance — confirmed against the DOI and multiple independent secondary sources, with the specific phrase "regardless of proximity to active fluid discharge" not found verbatim but its substance well supported.

The driving figures were upgraded by a same-day primary-verification round (workflow-4 in the session records).
The ~500 mV redox gap is now confirmed at the read-in-full level twice over: Yamamoto et al.'s open-access 2022 *ISME Journal* paper states the previously measured potentials directly — hydrothermal fluid −0.04 V vs SHE against ambient seawater +0.48 V, Δ0.52 V, at the same C0014G artificial vent — and JAMSTEC's official 2013 press release (read in full) gives ~520 mV measured in situ.
The precise two-decimal figures (+478 mV / −39 mV, at ~309 °C and 1,053 m) remain search-synthesis only, consistently reproduced across independent queries but never read from the paywalled Yamamoto 2013 primary itself.
The 10–210 mV thermoelectric EMF (likely from the Yamamoto, Nakamura & Takai 2018 *ChemElectroChem* review, itself Cloudflare-blocked) stays **unverified — search synthesis**, and the 2010/2013/2017 papers' current densities could not be located anywhere this round: flagged unobtainable.

An independent line corroborates the phenomenon itself: marine self-potential (SP) surveys, used in seafloor-massive-sulfide exploration (*Nature Scientific Reports*, 2017, "Marine self-potential survey for exploring seafloor hydrothermal ore deposits"), detect natural electric-field anomalies over these deposits from the water column — search synthesis, not directly fetched, but a wholly separate confirmation method from the bioelectrochemistry probes above.

## 2. Something already eats it

The 2022/2023 *ISME Journal* study (Yamamoto et al., JAMSTEC, published online December 2022, print January 2023) at Iheya North (Okinawa Trough, 27°47.50′N, 126°53.80′E, 1,050 m) deployed a cathode at a vent for 12 days (11–27 January 2015): self-generated voltage stabilizing near 0.6 V after 8 days, maximum current density 13.7 mA/m², cumulative charge transfer 8.8×10⁶ C/m² — read in full via direct fetch of the paper's PMC record, every figure confirmed verbatim.
The current visibly **tracked the semidiurnal tide**, the moon's rhythm running through the vent's electrical output — confirmed, read in full.

On that electrified cathode a previously unknown chemolithoautotroph enriched, formally proposed as *Candidatus* Thiomicrorhabdus electrophagus: a multi-heme cytochrome *c* gene cluster for extracellular electron uptake, CO₂ fixation by the Calvin-Benson-Bassham cycle, sulfur oxidation on the side — an electroautotroph demonstrated growing on the vent's ambient current, in the dark, at depth (confirmed, read in full).
Electrotrophy's mechanistic details — direct multiheme electron transfer versus enzyme-mediated uptake at the mineral surface — remain unresolved per a 2025 mLife review (Wang et al., "Current understanding of electroautotrophy and its relevance in astrobiology-related research" — search synthesis, partial fetch), but the ecology is no longer hypothetical: the vent's electricity is already someone's food.

## 3. Spin in the minerals, noted precisely

Ang, Khan, Tsujii, Takai, Nakamura & Mori (2015, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 54(44):12909–12913) found natural chalcopyrite (Cu₁₊ₓFe₁₋ₓS₂) recovered from a deep-sea vent generates thermoelectricity with semiconducting resistivity and a high thermoelectric power factor at low *x* — and detected electron-magnon scattering with enhanced carrier effective mass, attributed to coupling between doped charge carriers and the mineral's antiferromagnetic spin lattice (confirmed against the paper's abstract, matching the claim closely: "resistivity displayed excellent semiconducting character, and large thermoelectric power and high power factor were found in the low x region," with "electron-magnon scattering and a large effective mass" from "strong coupling of doped carriers and antiferromagnetic spins").

This is real, published spin-charge coupling occurring naturally in a vent chimney mineral's electron transport.
It is **not** chiral-induced spin selectivity (CISS) and must not be cited as such; it is recorded here as the adjacent fact that spin-dependent electronic behavior is already physically active in these minerals under natural conditions.
No hard bulk-conductivity number (S/cm) for chimney chalcopyrite or pyrite was recovered in this pass, though the composite sulfide chimney wall is independently described — Nakamura 2010 and subsequent reviews — as having "metal-like" conduction through a dense, interlocking microcrystalline sulfide texture.

## 4. The benchtop analog

Barge et al. framed the alkaline vent explicitly as nature's fuel cell (2014, *Astrobiology* 14(3):254–270, "The Fuel Cell Model of Abiogenesis," with Michael Russell — the foundational alkaline-vent origin-of-life theorist — in the lineage) and then measured the analog directly (2015, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 54(28):8184–8187, with Russell and Julyan Cartwright as co-authors): self-assembling iron-sulfide and iron-hydroxide "chemical garden" membranes — lab mimics of chimney precipitates — generating on the order of 600 mV per membrane, stackable in series to just under a volt, enough to light an external LED (confirmed via independent secondary sources describing "membrane potentials... 500–600 mV" and JPL press coverage of the LED demonstration).
One reported outlier is worth keeping visible rather than smoothing away: a single Fe-hydroxide membrane during active precipitation reached a transient peak of 0.85 V, above the representative ~600 mV figure — a spike, not a new steady-state ceiling.
The underlying 2014 framing itself traces to earlier JPL fuel-cell experiments with early-Earth-plausible Fe/Ni minerals, which detected electrical current from lab-grown chimney material without a specific voltage or current figure given in the accessible source (confirmed via JPL news release, matching the claim's own caveat exactly).

Follow-on work extended the framework to Enceladus (Barge & White 2017, *Astrobiology* 17(9):820–833) and to dedicated methods papers on geoelectrodes and fuel cells for simulating vent environments (*Astrobiology*, 2018) — which joins this note to the estate's [planetary tier](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/climate-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-three-forcings-and-the-planetary-tier.md): the same circuit, exportable to any tidally kneaded ocean world.
The 2026 *Nature Communications* lead was run down the same day and read in full: the DOI resolves to a Comment by Seneca J. Velling (NASA JPL/Caltech; *Nat. Commun.* 17:3230, 2026) accompanying the primary paper it interprets, Zhong et al., "Abiotic CO₂ reduction promoted by carbonate and phyllosilicate minerals on the primitive seafloor" (*Nat. Commun.* 17:3229, 2026 — read in full).
The bench result extends vent electrochemistry beyond sulfides: common Ca/Mg carbonates and phyllosilicates with trace adsorbed Cu(II)/Zn(II) (at natural ppm levels), under cathodic potential, become catalytic — Zn-doped carbonates yielding CO and formate, Cu-doped extending to CH₄ and C₂⁺ products (ethylene, ethanol, acetate), and with NH₃ present forming a C–N bond to acetamide — with the Enceladus case argued from Cassini's H₂, organics, and NH₃ detections, and Barge's chemical-garden fuel-cell lineage cited explicitly (2015 and 2018) as the conceptual scaffold, Barge herself thanked in the acknowledgements.
Load-bearing for the estate: a full-text case-insensitive search of both papers found **zero mentions** of spin, magnetism, magnetite, chirality, enantioselectivity, or CISS — the dark-compass gap re-confirmed by direct search of the newest work in the lineage.

## 5. What this gives the estate, and the gap it does not paper over

The VENT term's physics gains a fourth gradient: alongside heat, pH, and redox chemistry, the vent maintains a standing electrical potential with measurable current — and an organism already domesticates it.
For the [dark-compass hypothesis](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md) this note supplies the electron source that replaces sunlight.

That said, the bridge itself is not a literature finding, and the record should say so plainly rather than let the two facts blur together: none of the vent-electricity literature gathered here — Nakamura, Yamamoto (2013 and 2017), Barge, the ISME electroautotroph paper, the chalcopyrite thermoelectric paper, or the 2025 electrotrophy reviews — mentions CISS, spin selectivity, or chirality at all.
The claim that this native geoelectric current *could* drive spin-selective, magnetite-mediated enantiomeric enrichment analogous to Ozturk et al.'s solar-UV mechanism is the estate's own hypothesis, not a demonstrated result; that gap is itself a finding of this research pass, and the natural next research step, rather than something to present as already shown.

A second open structural question is inherited from the [mineralogy note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/mineralogy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-vent-magnetization-and-host-rock.md): the measured currents belong to sulfide chimneys (chalcopyrite/pyrite-dominated), the strong magnetization to serpentinized wall-rock in ultramafic-hosted systems — and no single site has yet been probed for both at once, so whether magnetite is present in CISS-relevant amounts within the same conductive sulfide chimney walls that carry the measured currents is unestablished, not merely unstated.

And the tidal pulsing deserves its own future thread: a natural current source with a built-in ~12-hour rhythm, tied to the same tidal forcing the [planetary tier note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/climate-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-three-forcings-and-the-planetary-tier.md) treats as a planetary-scale energy input, is a metronome handed to any chemistry that benefits from cycling — which the estate's wet-dry and crystallization material already suggests chemistry does.
