# Hydrogen Alone

## 0. The first element, the first fuel

The estate's [VENT](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/vacuous-entanglement-normalizing-topos.md) term already gestures at proton gradients across mineral membranes.
This note supplies the element underneath the gesture.
Hydrogen's case splits in two: hydrogen the geochemical fuel (what the vents make and what the first life ate), and hydrogen the bond (what makes water anomalous and what carries the fastest signal in it).

## 1. Serpentinization: rock plus water makes fuel

Hydrate mantle olivine and it oxidizes: simplified, olivine + water → serpentine + brucite + magnetite + H₂, the redox core being 2FeO + H₂O → Fe₂O₃ + H₂ (PMC5594423 / *Nature Communications*, "Effect of water activity on olivine serpentinization"; PMC7015308, "Effect of pH on serpentinization H₂ generation"; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
The reaction is exothermic and needs no biology, no sunlight, and no magma.

Its type locality is the Lost City hydrothermal field (Mid-Atlantic Ridge, ~15 km off-axis, 750–900 m deep): effluent pH 9–11, temperatures only 40–90 °C, heat supplied by the serpentinization chemistry itself, H₂ documented up to ~15 mmol/kg (washington.edu, UW News 2005; ScienceDirect, "Serpentinization and heat generation, Lost City/Rainbow"; Wikipedia, "Lost City Hydrothermal Field").
The often-quoted "10–100× more H₂/CH₄ than black smokers" multiplier could not be independently pinned in this session's verification — location, depth, pH, and temperature range all confirmed independently, but the specific multiplier itself was not located in the literature searched, so it is graded **plausible, [UNVERIFIED]**: "directionally plausible... but that specific figure is unverified though directionally plausible," per the verifier's note.
Because its heat source is chemistry rather than magmatism, Lost City has run for at least 30,000 and possibly ~120,000 years, against the decades-to-centuries lifespan of a typical black smoker (Wikipedia, "Lost City Hydrothermal Field"; washington.edu 2005 press release; not load-bearing, search synthesis).
The alkaline vent is the long-lived kind.

Two of the estate's other notes on this same date build directly on this rock chemistry.
[The axiality conjecture note](2026-08-22-note-on-the-axiality-conjecture.md) asks whether life's handedness derives from where it emerged — vents or seeded from space — and finds the strongest known symmetry-breaking mechanism (spin-selective crystallization on magnetite, ~60% enantiomeric excess) sitting neither at the vent nor in the sky but at a third pole, the evaporative brine.
[The dark-compass hypothesis note](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md) reopens the vent branch specifically: it assembles, link by link, a light-free chiral-induced spin selectivity (CISS) mechanism running on the same serpentinization magnetite this section describes, at ultramafic-hosted systems structurally akin to Lost City, powered by the vent's own measured electric current rather than by any photon.
Both notes depend on the serpentinization chemistry stated here; neither result is settled.

## 2. LUCA ran on it

Alkaline-vent origin-of-life theory (Russell, Martin, Lane) takes that abiotic H₂ as the electron donor for the oldest carbon-fixation route, the acetyl-CoA / Wood–Ljungdahl pathway, with Fe(Ni)S mineral clusters catalyzing CO₂ reduction at the interface between alkaline vent fluid and the more acidic early ocean — the vent as a natural electrochemical cell (Sojo et al., "The Origin of Life in Alkaline Hydrothermal Vents," *Astrobiology* 2016, PMC4247476; Royal Society Publishing, *Interface Focus* 2019, "the submarine alkaline vent theory at 30"; verification verdict: **confirmed**).

The genomic side arrives independently: Weiss et al. (2016, *Nature Microbiology*) reconstructed the last universal common ancestor from 286,514 protein clusters, finding 355 families tracing to LUCA — and the portrait is specific, not hand-waving: anaerobic, thermophilic, N₂-fixing, CO₂-fixing via Wood–Ljungdahl, and **H₂-dependent** (*Nature Microbiology* 2016, nmicrobiol2016116; *Science* coverage, "Our last common ancestor inhaled hydrogen from underwater volcanoes"; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
The geochemistry and the phylogenetics converge on hydrogen as life's first fuel.

## 3. Chemiosmosis: the computation life never stopped running

Mitchell's chemiosmotic theory (1961; Nobel 1978): electron transport pumps protons across a membrane, building a protonmotive force — a ΔpH plus an electrical potential Δψ — whose reverse flow through ATP synthase makes ATP (nobelprize.org, 1978 Chemistry press release; bioc.cam.ac.uk, Mitchell biography; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
This coupling is universal across cellular life, and Lane's argument gives the universality a reason: early life adopted a proton gradient the vents were already generating for free, and never let go of the architecture (nick-lane.net, "The Cradle of Life" PDF; *Quanta Magazine* 2022 profile of Lane; verification verdict: **confirmed**).

One detail deserves its own sentence: the PMF totals roughly 200 mV across wildly different external conditions, with ΔpH and Δψ trading off to hold the sum — a defended set-point, not an emergent by-product (PMC11114223, "Spatiotemporal dynamics of the proton motive force on single bacterial cells"; bioRxiv 2021.11.19.469321, "PMF and *E. coli* robustness to extracellular pH"; not load-bearing, flagged as a surprise by the research pass itself: "the proton motive force numbers are almost suspiciously round and stable").
Life does not merely use the gradient; it regulates the gradient as the quantity that matters.

## 4. Hydrogenases and the dark biosphere

Hydrogenases catalyze H₂ ⇌ 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ in two principal classes.
[NiFe]-hydrogenases carry a dinuclear Ni–Fe center with CO and CN⁻ ligands, generally biased toward H₂ oxidation.
[FeFe]-hydrogenases carry the H-cluster: a 4Fe-4S subcluster linked through a cysteine to a **[2Fe]_H organometallic unit bridged by CO, CN⁻, and azadithiolate ligands** — this session's verification corrected the first draft's "2Fe-2S" label, which misstated the ligand chemistry.
Graded **citation corrected**: the verifier's note reads, "labeling the second FeFe subcluster '2Fe-2S' is a real inaccuracy: independent sources consistently describe it as an organometallic '[2Fe]_H' unit bridged by CO, CN⁻, and azadithiolate ligands — not an inorganic sulfide-bridged 2Fe-2S cluster like a classic ferredoxin center" (ScienceDirect, "FeFe- and NiFe-Hydrogenase Diversity, Mechanism, and Maturation"; academic.oup.com/jb, "Structure and function of NiFe hydrogenases"; PubMed 12121756, "Fe-only hydrogenases: structure, function and evolution").
The distal pair is organometallic, not a ferredoxin-type sulfide cluster.

On this enzymology runs a whole sunless economy: subsurface lithoautotrophic microbial ecosystems (SLiMEs) in granitic, basaltic, and sedimentary crust, fed by H₂ from serpentinization, radiolysis, and water-rock reaction — by some accounts the most abundant energy source in the deep subsurface (academic.oup.com/femsec, "Artificial SLiMEs and gas storage in deep subsurface"; annualreviews.org, "The Rock-Hosted Biosphere"; Pedersen SLiME review, ResearchGate; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
The deep biosphere is a hydrogen economy that never heard about the sun.

## 5. The bond: why water is strange

Each water molecule donates and accepts up to four hydrogen bonds, building the dynamic network behind every anomaly the estate's water lore leans on: the density maximum at 4 °C (an increasingly ordered open tetrahedral network beating thermal contraction as it cools), the high specific heat (4.184 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹) and enormous heat of vaporization (2,260 J/g — the figure [the estuary note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/hydrology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-estuary-and-the-salt-to-fresh-gradient.md) prices the sky's distillation with), and the surface tension that lets the small live on the interface (biologynotesonline.com, "Structure of Water and Hydrogen Bonding"; TutorChase CIE A-Level notes; Bohrium, "The Anomalous Properties of Water"; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
Breaking hydrogen bonds costs real energy; water's moderating character is that cost, paid and refunded everywhere at once.

## 6. Grotthuss: the fastest signal in the water

Protons move through water faster than any dissolved ion, and not by moving: the Grotthuss mechanism relays charge along the hydrogen-bond network — a protonation "hop" (~1–2 ps) followed by a network reorientation "turn" (Wikipedia, "Grotthuss mechanism"; ScienceDirect, "Grotthuss mechanism overview"; PMC10122652, "Search for a Grotthuss mechanism through observation of proton transfer"; verification verdict: **confirmed**).
The less-quoted half is the rate limit: the turn gates the relay, so proton mobility is a property of water's collective network dynamics, not of any proton's speed.
The medium's structure is the conductor.
pH — Sørensen, 1909, later refined from concentration to activity — is the one-number readout of this economy, logarithmic, read and written by essentially every living cell (Deranged Physiology, "The Sørensen definition of pH"; ChemTeam, "Søren Sørensen and pH"; Springer, pH chapter; verification verdict: **confirmed**).

## 7. The billion-fold refusal, which is BAMBOO's own channel

Aquaporins pass water in single file while refusing the proton — and the refusal is a mechanism, not a narrowness.
At the conserved NPA motifs, the channel's electrostatics force the water file into a bipolar orientation (dipoles pointing outward from the center in both directions), a configuration the Grotthuss relay cannot conduct through, reinforced by an electrostatic/desolvation barrier of roughly 25–30 kJ/mol (PMC1303569, "Electrostatic Tuning of Permeation and Selectivity in Aquaporin Water Channels"; ScienceDirect, "Molecular Basis of Proton Blockage in Aquaporins"; verification verdict on the mechanism itself: **confirmed**).

This session's verification corrected the magnitude upward by six orders: the cited computational literature puts proton versus water transit at roughly **10⁹-fold** discrimination — water crossing in ~10⁻¹¹ s where a proton would need ~10⁻² s — not the "~1,000-fold" of the first draft.
Graded **citation corrected**: the verifier fetched PMC1303673, "What Really Prevents Proton Transport through Aquaporin?", directly and found "the paper's own calculated ratio of proton-to-water penetration times is ~10⁹... roughly a billion-fold difference, not 'roughly 1000-fold.' The claim understates the magnitude found in its own cited source by about six orders of magnitude."
The channel defeats the fastest signal in water while passing the water itself, at near-billion-fold fidelity.

The estate connection is direct, not thematic: [BAMBOO](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bilattice-articulation-model-belief-orienting-operation.md)'s Lsi1 silicon channel is a NIP-clade aquaporin, and the NIP clade's silicon/boron permeability is a documented retuning of the ar/R selectivity filter of this same architecture (verification verdict: **confirmed** — "NIP aquaporin ar/R selectivity filter sets Si/B/As specificity; Lsi1 silicon permeability [confirmed]").
The channel that blocks the proton computation and passes the silicon is one protein family, read twice by the estate — once in the desalination story ([the estuary note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/hydrology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-estuary-and-the-salt-to-fresh-gradient.md)), once in the silica story (BAMBOO).
