Matter is the substance of [[object|objects]]: [[old-notes/domains/tekodynamics/glossary/thing|things]] with [[mass]] and [[volume]].

It includes [[solid|solids]], [[liquid|liquids]], [[gas|gases]], and [[plasma]]: states that are perceptible to our [[human senses|senses]] or detectable with [[tool|tools]]. Composed of [[atom|atoms]] and [[molecule|molecules]] bound by [[force|forces]] at various scales, physical matter forms the building blocks of [[planet|planets]], [[organism|organisms]], and everyday objects. It is characterized by occupying [[space]] and responding to fundamental forces such as [[gravity]] and [[electromagnetism]]. Physical matter is a key component of the [[material universe]] we [[zeds/Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating|relate]] with through [[life]].
