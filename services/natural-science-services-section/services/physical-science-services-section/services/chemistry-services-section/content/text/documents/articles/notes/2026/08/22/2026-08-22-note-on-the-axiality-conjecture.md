## 0. The conjecture, as stated and as sharpened

The owner's conjecture, 2026-08-22: *the axiality of life derives from whether it emerged in vents or was seeded from space.*
Offered as a real conjecture, not a figure — and it lands on a genuinely live problem.

Life is homochiral: L-amino acids, D-sugars, exclusively, and the bias propagates upward — in vertebrates, the embryo's left-right axis is broken by nodal cilia whose rotation direction is set by the handedness of their dynein motors, which is set by amino-acid chirality.
This is the accepted "nodal flow" mechanism, **confirmed with a scope caveat**: it is vertebrate-specific — key supporting work is Nonaka et al. (KIF3B knockout randomizes left-right patterning), Okada et al. (*PLoS Biology* 2005, posterior ciliary tilt converts rotation into directional flow), and Yoshiba, Hamada et al. (*Nature Communications* 2012, showing two cilia suffice) — with a competing two-cilia model inside vertebrates themselves (motile flow generators plus immotile PKD2/Pkd1l1 sensors), while snails and flies use actin-cytoskeleton chirality instead (Kuroda et al.), so the propagation route varies by lineage while the molecular root does not.
A body's axiality is the primordial symmetry breaking, amplified for four billion years.

The sharpened form: **the candidate mechanisms for the original symmetry breaking sort by origin locus, and different loci make different predictions about handedness across worlds.**
That is what makes it a conjecture with consequences rather than a framing.
This note was written after a dedicated adversarial verification pass, preserved in full as [scout-2, the homochirality verification report](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../data/records/2026-08-22-natural-computing-world-session/scout-2-homochirality-verification-report.md); every per-claim verdict below traces to it.

## 1. Orientation from above: the delivered bias

Circular polarization is directly observed in star-forming regions — **confirmed with nuance**: Bailey et al. (1998, *Science* 281(5377):672–674) measured strong **near-infrared** circular polarization in Orion OMC-1, while the extension to UV wavelengths, where photolysis is efficient, is argued by analogy rather than directly observed, a nuance kept here rather than smoothed over.
Lab photolysis with circularly polarized synchrotron UV produces real enantiomeric excesses: Meierhenrich et al. (2005, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 44(35):5630–5634), right-circularly-polarized synchrotron radiation at 182 nm on solid leucine, gives 2.6% D-leucine ee — the benchmark few-percent figure.

The Murchison and Orgueil meteorites carry real L-excesses in α-methyl amino acids biology barely uses — **corrected upward** in the verification pass: L-isovaline at 18.5 ± 2.6% and 15.2 ± 4.0% respectively (Glavin & Dworkin 2009, *PNAS* 106(14):5487–5490, doi:10.1073/pnas.0811618106), with contamination excluded on solid grounds (α-methyl amino acids are essentially absent from terrestrial biochemistry; heavy δ¹³C/δ¹⁵N signatures), corroborated by the swiftly recovered Aguas Zarcas fall (Glavin et al. 2021, *Meteoritics & Planetary Science* 56(1):519).
Earlier, smaller detections (Pizzarello & Cronin 2000: ~2–9%, up to ~15%) are superseded by these figures, not contradicted.

If the bias arrives with the feedstock, all life sharing the feedstock shares one handedness — solar-system-wide.

## 2. The tension that now cuts through the space branch

The two pristine sample-return missions came back racemic — both **confirmed**.
Bennu (OSIRIS-REx; Glavin et al. 2025, *Nature Astronomy*, doi:10.1038/s41550-024-02472-9): 14 proteinogenic amino acids and all five nucleobases detected, with every confirmed-extraterrestrial chiral amino acid racemic or nearly so, trace L-enrichment in common protein amino acids attributed to probable minor terrestrial contamination — the authors explicitly frame this against the delivered-chirality hypothesis.
Ryugu (Hayabusa2; Parker et al. 2023, *Geochim. Cosmochim. Acta*, and a 2024 enantioselective 3D-HPLC follow-up, *J. Chromatography Open* 5:100134): non-proteinogenic amino acids at 47.1–55.2% L — racemic within error.

Bennu/Ryugu versus Murchison/Orgueil is, as of 2025–2026, an open discrepancy — arguably the field's central one, per the verification pass's own assessment.
A 2024 *Frontiers* analysis adds the pivotal clue: **meteoritic isovaline excess correlates with the degree of aqueous alteration on the parent body.**
The bias may not be a primordial interstellar signal at all but something manufactured where liquid water worked the rock — a tiny photochemical seed (a 2023 *Nature Communications* paper ties the isovaline bias to asymmetric photochemistry mechanistically) amplified by wet chemistry in situ.
If so, even the space branch is secretly a water branch: the sky whispers a fraction of a percent, and amplification does the rest wherever there is water to run it.

## 3. Orientation from below: the vent branch and its missing compass

The amplifier is proven chemistry — **confirmed**.
Frank (1953, *Biochim. Biophys. Acta* 11:459–463) showed autocatalysis plus mutual antagonism amplifies infinitesimal fluctuations to homochirality, and the Soai reaction (Soai, Shibata, Morioka & Choji, 1995, *Nature* 378:767–768; refined through the 2000s) demonstrated it — amplification from ~0.00005% ee to over 99.5% ee, experimental proof of principle, though the organozinc chemistry itself is not prebiotic.
Any origin site with autocatalysis gets to homochirality from almost nothing.
The question is only what supplies the almost-nothing, and with what sign.

The vent branch's candidate compasses are weak as published — **confirmed weak**.
Chiral mineral faces select enantiomers locally (Hazen, Filley & Goodfriend 2001, *PNAS* 98(10):5487–5490, calcite growth faces differentially adsorbing L- versus D-aspartate), but Earth's chiral minerals are globally racemic — left- and right-handed quartz occur in near-equal abundance — so mineralogy gives local patches, not a planetary sign.
The parity-violating energy difference of the weak force is real in theory but tiny (~10⁻¹⁵–10⁻¹² of molecular energy) and has never been experimentally detected — only bounded, the best figure being Δν/ν < 4×10⁻¹³ (~13 Hz) on CHFClBr; the verification pass also flags a common confusion, that the Quack-group *PNAS* (1999) "demonstration" paper is theoretical and ab initio, not a measurement, despite a title that invites the opposite reading.
The Vester–Ulbricht beta-decay mechanism has weak, largely unreplicated support.
A pure vent origin, with only these, makes handedness a coin flip frozen per origin event: independent origins land opposite half the time.

Distinct from the compass question, the vent branch also has a demonstrated *electron supply*: [the vent-electricity note](2026-08-22-note-on-vent-electricity.md) carries a natural fuel-cell circuit through chimney walls, with current at the Iheya North field tracking the semidiurnal tide — a rhythm, not a handedness, but a further ingredient the [dark-compass hypothesis](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md) below folds into Link 4 of its chain.

## 4. The third pole, which is neither — and where it sits in the estate

The one mechanism with a demonstrated large excess is recent and rapidly developing.
Ozturk, Liu, Sutherland & Sasselov (2023, *Science Advances* 9, eadg8274; preprint arXiv:2303.01394) achieved **~60% enantiomeric excess** in ribo-aminooxazoline (RAO, an RNA precursor) by spin-selective crystallization on magnetite, via chiral-induced spin selectivity — the CISS effect, electron spin aligned by magnetization discriminating molecular handedness.
The companion result (Ozturk et al. 2023, "Chirality-induced avalanche magnetization of magnetite by an RNA precursor," *Nature Communications* 14, 6351) closes a feedback loop: chiral RAO magnetizes demagnetized magnetite in turn — orientation and field reinforcing each other.
Follow-ups continue into 2026 and remain a live, developing thread rather than a settled result: two 2026 preprints (arXiv:2605.19387; arXiv:2606.01656) extend the magnetite-CISS line further, unread here — flagged leads.
A 2025 *PNAS* review in the same line, "Life's homochirality: Across a prebiotic network" (2505126122), works on propagating the chirality from RNA precursors across the wider prebiotic network toward peptides — the step from RAO's handedness to protein homochirality is still open.

Two corrections attach here, and the second corrects the first.
As first written, this note recorded the mechanism as belonging to **evaporative lakes under solar UV** and stated it "needs the sun."
A same-day research pass against the primary text overturned that: the headline 60% ee crystallization was run **in the dark** — covered petri dish, 12 °C, a 325 mT permanent magnet as the only input — and the solar-UV photoelectron route in the same paper is a separate, optional way to deliver spin-polarized electrons, not a requirement.
The evaporative-lake setting belongs to that optional route only.
What remained true, even after the correction: no published paper connects CISS to hydrothermal settings.
That gap is now developed, link by link, in [the dark-compass hypothesis note](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md) (chemistry, this same date), which scopes the vent variant to ultramafic-hosted systems, using the serpentinization chemistry carried in full in [the hydrogen note](2026-08-22-note-on-hydrogen-alone.md), and names the missing capstone experiment.

In the estate's own vocabulary the corrected setting reads almost too well: crystallization at saturation in an evaporative lake, on magnetized sediment, under the sky's light and the body's field — the symmetry breaking happens in the **[BRINE](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bilattice-residuated-ion-normalizing-extremum.md)**, on magnetized MUD, powered from above, oriented by the geodynamo (carried with its own magnitudes in [the planetary note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/climate-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-three-forcings-and-the-planetary-tier.md)).
Not the VENT, not space: the residuated extremum where things crystallize out is where the orientation gets chosen — and then writes itself back into the mineral that chose it.
The conjecture asked "below or above?"; the current best mechanism answers "at the edge where above meets the body's own field."

## 5. The referee, read carefully

Chirality as an origin discriminator is an active astrobiology framework, not an estate invention — **confirmed**: Glavin, Burton, Elsila, Aponte & Dworkin, "The Search for Chiral Asymmetry as a Potential Biosignature in our Solar System," *Chemical Reviews* 120(11):4660–4689 (2020).
The decision tree for a second sample of life:

- **D-handed life on Enceladus or Europa**: universal seeding falsified on the spot; independent origin demonstrated; the coin-flip (or local-field) branches win.
- **L-handed life there**: ambiguous — shared feedstock, shared nebular bias, contamination, or a coin landing the same way once.
- **Racemic prebiotic chemistry but homochiral life**: the deepest result — the bias is not in the feedstock at all; every world breaks its own symmetry at its own site, and the sky delivers only dice.

The caveat that disciplines the test, **confirmed**: Pavlov et al. (2024, *Astrobiology*, "Radiolytic Effects on Biological and Abiotic Amino Acids in Shallow Subsurface Ices on Europa and Enceladus") show radiolysis in shallow icy-moon surface ices degrades and racemizes amino acids — a surface sample can erase the signal it was sent for.
The test wants plume or subsurface material, and the abiotic baseline characterized first, a thread continued by a 2026 *Nature Astronomy* framework paper on evaluating biosignatures against the abiotic baseline on ocean worlds.
The referee is real; the whistle must not be read through corrupted ice.

## 6. Standing of the conjecture

Held as a live inquiry program, its terrain now mapped and sourced: the amplifier is proven (§3), the delivered bias is real in some meteorites and absent in both pristine sample returns (§1–2, the field's open discrepancy), the strongest known compass sits at a third pole neither vent nor sky (§4), and the discriminating experiment exists with a known failure mode (§5).
In the estate's terms the conjecture joins the orientation doctrine at its root: obsmetrologia holds that a cell's orientation is its content, and this program asks where the world's first oriented cell got its orientation — from the light above, the noise below, or the field of the body it settled in.

Open questions: whether [the dark-compass hypothesis](2026-08-22-note-on-the-dark-compass-hypothesis.md) survives its named capstone experiment — the dark-CISS question having moved, in one day, from unbridged speculation to a link-by-link chain missing only its join; whether the aqueous-alteration correlation resolves the Murchison/Bennu tension in favor of on-body amplification; whether the RAO-to-peptide propagation problem closes, without which any answer stops one polymer short of us; and whether the two unread 2026 magnetite-CISS preprints (§4) change any of the above once read.
