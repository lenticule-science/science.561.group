## 0. Status: the estate's own synthesis

The owner's prompt — "vents aren't dark; they have different energy than UV light" — sent a ten-agent research pass into vent light, vent electricity, vent magnetization, and the chiral-induced spin selectivity (CISS) literature.
The result is stated with its status first: **every link of a light-free CISS symmetry-breaking mechanism at vents is independently demonstrated in the literature, and no published paper assembles them.**
Three independent sweeps — vent-electricity, vent-magnetization, and ciss-dark — flagged this same gap unprompted, a triangulated negative finding that raises confidence this is a genuine open niche rather than something already published and merely unread (search synthesis, critic assembly verdict).
What follows is an estate-originated hypothesis built from sourced parts — a novel synthesis to be tested, not a result to be cited.
This note draws on [workflow-2, the vent/dark-compass research record](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../data/records/2026-08-22-natural-computing-world-session/workflow-2-vent-dark-compass-research.json) in full; see [§7, Sources and records](#7-sources-and-records) below.

## 1. The correction that opened the door

The estate's [axiality-conjecture note](2026-08-22-note-on-the-axiality-conjecture.md) of this same date recorded, as a correction, that the flagship CISS result "needs the sun."
That correction is itself now corrected, against the primary text (read in full, PMC10246896): Ozturk, Bhowmick, Liu, Yeh, Sutherland & Sasselov's 2023 headline experiment — "Origin of biological homochirality by crystallization of an RNA precursor on a magnetic surface," *Science Advances*, doi:10.1126/sciadv.adg8274 — produced ~60% enantiomeric excess (ee) in ribo-aminooxazoline (RAO) crystallizing on magnetite, run **in the dark**: a covered petri dish at 12 °C, hours to days, with a 325 mT external permanent magnet as the only symmetry-breaking input.
Enantiopure (100% ee) crystals were obtainable by one recrystallization from any starting solution above ~25% ee, via ordinary conglomerate crystallization (read in full).
The solar-UV photoelectron pathway described in the same paper is a separate, *optional* route to spin-polarized electrons — a choice among known CISS-activation strategies, not a requirement of the mechanism (read in full; corroborated by search synthesis: Ozturk's own paper cites prior dark electrochemical CISS work as precedent for this optional-not-required framing).
The paper's own proposed prebiotic setting is evaporative lakes with wet-dry cycling on authigenic iron-oxide sediment; it does not discuss vents at all, and a targeted search turned up no published paper connecting CISS to hydrothermal or serpentinization settings (search synthesis, negative result — confirmed independently by a second sweep; see [§2, Link 5](#2-the-chain-link-by-link) below).
The evaporative-lake setting belongs to the optional UV route.
The mechanism itself never needed light.

## 2. The chain, link by link

**Link 1 — Vents make magnetized magnetite in situ: DEMONSTRATED, conditionally.**
Only at ultramafic-hosted (serpentinite) systems — the [vent-magnetization note](../../../../../../../../../../../earth-science-services-section/services/mineralogy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-vent-magnetization-and-host-rock.md) of this date carries the full lithology inversion.
Rainbow and Ashadze-1 (365–374 °C) show strong positive magnetic anomalies, ~28–30 A/m, carried by magnetite in sulfide-impregnated serpentinite (search synthesis, corroborated; magnetization figure independently confirmed by verification pass, but see the citation correction below).
Lost City (40–91 °C, no magmatic heat) shows only a weak positive anomaly by contrast, tracking a documented step-change in iron partitioning above ~200 °C — Szitkar, Tivey, Kelley, Karson, Früh-Green & Denny, *Earth and Planetary Science Letters* ("citation corrected" — verification directly confirmed this authorship and venue).
**Citation corrected:** the Rainbow/Ashadze-1 magnetization figures (~28–30 A/m) were traced in the estate's earlier draft to "Tivey & Dyment, *Geology* 2014" — no such paper exists; the correct source for that specific dataset is Tivey & Dyment, "The Magnetic Signature of Hydrothermal Systems in Slow Spreading Environments," *AGU Geophysical Monograph* 188 (2010) (verification pass, refuted-then-corrected).
A separate, correctly-titled review of the general ultramafic-magnetized-vs-basalt-demagnetized pattern is Szitkar, Dyment, Fouquet, Honsho & Horen, "The magnetic signature of ultramafic-hosted hydrothermal sites," *Geology* 42(8):715–718 (2014) — the estate's earlier draft had misattributed this paper's title to Tivey & Dyment as well ("citation corrected," verification pass).
Pure magnetite's Curie point is ~580 °C (verification narrowed the commonly-cited range to ~575–585 °C; no "530 °C" figure exists for pure magnetite — that lower value belongs to titanomagnetite, not Fe₃O₄, "citation corrected"), untouchable by any vent fluid measured to date.
Basalt-hosted black smokers (TAG, ASHES) are the opposite — demagnetized alteration pipes, the low centered precisely on the active upflow zone, driven by Ti/Fe²⁺ leaching of titanomagnetite whose Curie point runs as low as ~130–160 °C fresh (up to ~200–400 °C oxidized) — both well below vent fluid temperature (search synthesis, corroborated by an independent geophysical-journal source) — and are **excluded from this hypothesis's scope**.
The dark compass, if it exists, lives at hot ultramafic vents.

**Link 2 — That magnetite is spin-polarized without light: DEMONSTRATED.**
Fe₃O₄ is a half-metal with spin polarization at the Fermi level measured at −(80±5)% at room temperature by spin- and angle-resolved photoemission on epitaxial Fe₃O₄(111) films (Fonin & Dedkov; search synthesis, confirmed by verification against the exact quoted figure) — a ground-state electronic property, not a photo-induced one; the photoemission apparatus is the measurement technique, not the cause.
Its bulk band gap above the Verwey transition is ~0.2 eV (Liu & Di Valentin, *J. Phys. Chem. C* 2017, DFT) — electronically live at vent temperatures with no photon required for internal transport.
**Citation corrected:** the estate's earlier draft cited this gap as "~0.1–0.15 eV"; verification against the cited source found that figure belongs to the *low-temperature phase below* the Verwey transition, not the above-Verwey regime relevant to vent conditions — the correct above-Verwey figure is ~0.2 eV, with photoemission-based estimates as low as ~70 meV depending on method, so real disagreement remains in the literature even at the corrected value.
The one direct temperature study of the CISS-magnetization coupling (arXiv 2412.05720, preprint) finds it *strengthening*, not weakening, with heat: coercivity increases linearly from 20 °C to 80 °C, and the RAO:AAO enrichment ratio rises from ~1.2 to ~1.5 (a 23% gain) — **citation corrected**, that ratio figure is reported specifically "at 65 °C" in the primary text, not as spanning the full 20–80 °C range as the estate's earlier draft implied (verification pass, refuted-then-corrected on both points).
**Citation corrected:** the preprint's title was miscopied in the estate's earlier draft as "Temperature-Enhanced Chirality-Induced Magnetization and its Implications for RNA Homochirality"; the actual title across all versions of arXiv:2412.05720 is "Temperature-Enhanced Coercive Field by Chiral Molecules" (verification pass, refuted-then-corrected).
The paper's own setting is evaporative lakes, not vents — the 80 °C ceiling is far short of Rainbow's 365 °C, an open extrapolation flagged in [§4](#4-the-experiments-in-commissioning-order) below.

**Link 3 — CISS enantioselection works in the dark: DEMONSTRATED, three independent ways.**
Dark electrochemistry at a magnetized nickel electrode: ~11.5% ee in a camphorsulfonic-acid reduction product (mercaptoborneol), attributed to spin-polarized electron transfer rather than direct enantiospecific molecule-electrode interaction (search synthesis, scientific content confirmed by verification).
**Citation corrected:** this result is commonly shorthanded in estate notes as "Bloom & Waldeck," and was miscited in an earlier draft as *PNAS* 2017/2019; the correct source is Metzger, Mishra, Bloom, et al. (Waldeck and Naaman senior authors), "The Electron Spin as a Chiral Reagent," *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 58(4):1653–1658 (2019) — not *PNAS* (verification pass, refuted-then-corrected; separate, related *PNAS* CISS/homochirality papers by this group do exist — 2022, nos. 2204765119 and 2210505119 — but not this result).
Banerjee-Ghosh et al., *Science* 360:1331–1334 (2018), doi:10.1126/science.aar4265: enantiospecific adsorption of L- vs D-polyalanine and other oligopeptides, oligonucleotides, and amino acids onto magnetized ferromagnetic substrates, no photons — the paper, authors, journal, and pagination are confirmed, but the estate's carried-forward "~factor of three" adsorption-rate difference and "Ni/Co" substrate description could not be independently verified against primary text (paywalled, 403) and remain **[UNVERIFIED]** at that level of detail (verification pass, "plausible" verdict).
And a June 2025 result — "Highly enantioselective synthesis controlled by spin-exchange interaction," *Science Advances*, doi:10.1126/sciadv.adw5850 (PMC12175882) — reports 90% ee in [3+2] cycloadditions and 89% ee in aldol reactions via spin-exchange interaction (~8–10 kJ/mol) over a magnetized nickel film (~150 mT, insensitive to field strength up to 490 mT) during evaporative crystallization: dark, and *outperforming* the UV-linked 60% figure (search synthesis).
Supporting cast, both confirmed by verification of a general CISS review (PMC10906005, read in full, near-verbatim quote matched): Kettner-type asymmetric redox currents at magnetized electrodes coated with chiral self-assembled monolayers, and Mtangi, Tassinari, Vankayala et al. (Naaman group), *JACS* 139(7):2794 (2017), doi:10.1021/jacs.6b12971 — spin-controlled electrolytic (non-photoelectrochemical) water splitting suppressing parasitic H₂O₂ formation.
The physics does not need photons; photons were one delivery mechanism among several, and the reviewed literature states outright that transient charge polarization of a chiral molecule is sufficient to generate spin polarization, with CISS tunnel-junction and Hall-effect measurements documented operating "in the dark" with no net photocurrent (read in full, PMC10906005).
All of the dark demonstrations above used electroplated nickel or magnetized thin films as the spin-polarizing surface — none used natural or serpentinization-grown magnetite; this is the specific, narrow experimental gap this hypothesis identifies (see [§4](#4-the-experiments-in-commissioning-order)).

**Link 4 — Vents supply the electron current: DEMONSTRATED.**
The [vent-electricity note](2026-08-22-note-on-vent-electricity.md) of this date carries it in full: natural fuel-cell conduction through sulfide chimney walls, coupling internal sulfide oxidation to external O₂ reduction — Nakamura, Takashima, Kato, Takai, Yamamoto & Hashimoto, *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 49(42):7692–4 (2010) (search synthesis, confirmed by verification via independent RIKEN summary).
An LED lit on the seafloor from vent current — Yamamoto et al., *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 52(41):10758–61 (2013): at least 21 mW (0.42 W/m² of anode) from a natural hydrothermal-fluid/seawater redox gradient, exceeding prior benthic microbial fuel cells' 0.25 W/m² (verification pass, confirmed via multiple independent secondary sources).
A field-wide spontaneous-current survey across the Okinawa Trough — Yamamoto et al., *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 56(21):5725–5728 (2017), doi:10.1002/anie.201701768: in-situ redox potentials on active mineral surfaces more negative than surrounding seawater, the effect widespread — "not only around active vents but also in distant spots electrochemically connected to subseafloor hydrothermal fluids" — traced in lab work to distant electron transfer through the minerals' metallic/catalytic conduction (verification pass, citation and content confirmed; the estate's earlier "~500 mV-order driving gap" figure remains flagged needs-primary-verification, see [§5](#5-sourcing-caveats-kept-in-view)).
A 12-day in-situ cathode at Iheya North (27°47.50′N, 126°53.80′E, 1050 m depth, 11–27 Jan 2015) — Yamamoto et al., *ISME Journal* (online Sept 2022, print Jan 2023), PMC9751133: voltage stabilizing at ~0.6 V after 8 days, maximum current density 13.7 mA/m², cumulative charge 8.8×10⁶ C/m², output tracking the semidiurnal tide, with a newly described electroautotroph — *Candidatus* Thiomicrorhabdus electrophagus — carrying a multi-heme cytochrome c gene cluster for extracellular electron uptake and CBB-cycle CO₂ fixation genes, growing by eating that current (read in full, every figure independently verified verbatim against the primary text).
The living precedent for spin-selective dark electron transfer at iron-oxide interfaces also exists: *Shewanella oneidensis* MR-1 outer-membrane cytochromes OmcA (63%) and MtrF (37%), and upstream MtrA (~75%) and STC (~35%), measure as CISS spin filters — O'Rourke et al., *Journal of Chemical Physics* 159:145101 (2023) — with the four spin-polarization percentages independently corroborated by search (abstract only / search synthesis).
**[UNVERIFIED]**: the further claims that this same paper shows those cytochromes transferring electrons directly onto hematite *in the dark*, and that *Geobacter sulfurreducens* biofilm respiration rate depends on the magnetization direction of its electrode, could not be located in the O'Rourke paper by search or by its paywalled primary text — the underlying background biology (Shewanella MtrC/OmcA binding iron-oxide surfaces; magnetic-field effects on Geobacter current) is real but documented in separate literature, and the specific attribution to O'Rourke et al. 2023 is carried forward unverified rather than confirmed (verification pass, "plausible" verdict, explicitly flagging this residual as unconfirmed).

**Link 5 — All of it in one system: MISSING.**
No paper.
This is the hypothesis.
And one sub-gap inside it, flagged honestly: the measured currents (Nakamura, Yamamoto) run through *sulfide chimney* structures — pyrite, chalcopyrite; the strong magnetization sits in *serpentinized wall-rock and stockwork* — related but physically distinct structures, at sites probed by different literatures, and never cross-checked against each other by any sweep (search synthesis, flagged as an unresolved weak spot by the critic assembly).
Whether CISS-relevant magnetite sits in a current-carrying pathway at any single real site — whether Rainbow or Ashadze have themselves been probed electrically the way Nakamura and Yamamoto probed their sulfide chimneys — is itself unestablished.

## 3. The photon route, closed cleanly

The one thing vent light cannot do is the one thing the optional UV pathway used: eject photoelectrons from magnetite.
Magnetite's photoemission work function is ~5.3–5.9 eV — DFT values of 5.78 eV (bulk-truncated B-termination) and 5.32 eV (modified B-termination) agree with a measured 5.20±0.15 eV, and angle-resolved photoemission independently confirms features at −5.0 and −6.5 eV binding energy (Zalecki et al. 2006) (search synthesis, physics confirmed by verification; **citation corrected** — the estate's earlier draft attributed the specific 5.32/5.78 eV DFT figures to arXiv cond-mat/0501402, Pentcheva et al.'s Jahn-Teller paper, but a direct fetch of that text does not contain them; the values instead trace to a separate, later STM/spin-polarized-photoemission/DFT study of the Fe₃O₄(001)/(100) surface, likely Diebold/Parkinson group — the physics holds, the specific citation is uncertain).
Vent light tops out well short of that threshold.
The blackbody component, fit to a ~340±10 °C source at emissivity 0.3±0.1, rises from ~10⁶ photons cm⁻²s⁻¹sr⁻¹ at 700 nm (1.77 eV) to ~10¹⁰ at 1000 nm (1.24 eV) — near-infrared, well under 2 eV, with no UV component reported (White et al. 2002, *J. Geophys. Res. Solid Earth*, doi:10.1029/2000JB000015; search synthesis, confirmed by verification against secondary summaries of the primary text).
A real, temporally variable non-thermal excess exists at 400–600 nm (~10⁴ photons cm⁻²s⁻¹sr⁻¹), attributed to bubble luminescence, chemiluminescence, crystalloluminescence, and triboluminescence rather than blackbody radiation — White et al. describe this excess as "orders of magnitude" above thermal prediction, while a 2025 secondary source (Mullineaux & Duffy, PMC12395954, read in full) puts the same class of signal at only ~10-fold above blackbody: an internal inconsistency across sources that the [vent-light note](../../../../../../../../../../../biological-science-services-section/services/ecology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-vent-light-and-vent-photobiology.md) carries in full and this note does not attempt to resolve.
Either figure tops out near 3 eV (400 nm ≈ 3.1 eV) — even the disputed chalcopyrite/pyrite second-harmonic up-conversion claim (Li et al. 2025, *National Science Review*; disputed by Mullineaux & Duffy 2025, same journal — a live, unresolved 2025 controversy, both sides cited, neither treated as settled) would not close a 2–4 eV gap to magnetite's work function.
Said plainly: no photon-substitution version of the UV mechanism is physically possible at vents.
That is not a defeat for the hypothesis — it is what correctly routes it through Links 3 and 4, the electron current instead of the photon.
The owner's phrasing was precise: different energy, not none — and [the hydrogen note](2026-08-22-note-on-hydrogen-alone.md) of this date carries the serpentinization H₂ chemistry that supplies that different energy at the source.

## 4. The experiments, in commissioning order

1. Run an existing dark-CISS protocol — the spin-exchange crystallization design, or magnetized-electrode electrochemistry — with **natural or serpentinization-grown magnetite** in place of the electroplated nickel or magnetized thin film every published dark demonstration used, under vent-relevant conditions: pH 9–11, an H₂/CO₂ redox couple, seawater ionic strength, temperatures from 40 °C upward.
2. Co-locate the measurements at one live ultramafic site — Rainbow or Ashadze: electric current, magnetization, and mineral speciation together, to close the Link-5 sub-gap.
3. Extend the CISS temperature dependence beyond its tested 80 °C (and its 65 °C point-figure for the enrichment-ratio number, per the [§2](#2-the-chain-link-by-link) correction above) toward Rainbow's 365 °C.
4. Expose ribo-aminooxazoline, or another prebiotic precursor, to a live vent-representative redox current near magnetized natural magnetite — Barge et al., *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 54(28):8184–8187 (2015): self-assembling iron-sulfide/hydroxide "chemical garden" membranes generating ~600 mV individually, stackable in series to just under a volt, enough to light an LED (search synthesis, confirmed by verification) — a ready-made benchtop testbed.

## 5. Sourcing caveats, kept in view

A same-day primary-verification round (workflow-4 in the session records) upgraded most of the flagged figures.
White et al. 2002's vent-light numbers are now **read in full** — both ALISS papers obtained from the WHOI repository, all four targeted figures confirmed with exact quotes, with the nuance that they are per-vent fits with real vent-to-vent spread (the vent-light note carries the table).
The ~500 mV redox gap is confirmed read-in-full at Δ0.52 V via the open-access ISME 2022 paper and JAMSTEC's 2013 press release, though the exact +478/−39 mV figures remain search-synthesis (the Yamamoto 2013 primary stayed paywalled).
Still open: the 10–210 mV thermoelectric EMF (unverified, source itself blocked) and the 2010/2013/2017 papers' current densities (unobtainable this round).
Multiple independent secondary citations agree on these figures, which is reassuring, but they are not primary-text confirmed.
The verification pass corrected several citations without damaging the underlying science, collected here for visibility: the ultramafic-magnetization review is Szitkar, Dyment, Fouquet, Honsho & Horen (*Geology* 2014), not Tivey & Dyment; the Rainbow/Ashadze magnetization dataset traces instead to Tivey & Dyment (2010), *AGU Geophysical Monograph* 188; magnetite's Curie point is ~580 °C (range ~575–585 °C) with no "530 °C" figure in the standard range for pure Fe₃O₄; the magnetite band gap above the Verwey transition is ~0.2 eV, not ~0.1–0.15 eV (that lower figure belongs to the below-Verwey low-temperature phase); the arXiv preprint's title is "Temperature-Enhanced Coercive Field by Chiral Molecules," and its 23%-enrichment-gain figure is reported at 65 °C specifically, not across the full 20–80 °C span; the ~11.5% ee dark-electrochemistry result is Metzger et al., *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* 2019, not "Bloom & Waldeck, *PNAS*"; and GSB1's source vent interior is "≈300 °C," not a "300–350 °C" range (this last figure belongs to the vent-light and vent-photobiology thread rather than this note's own chain, and is recorded here only because it shares a verification pass with the rest).
The 2026 *Nature Communications* lead was read in full the same day: a Velling (JPL/Caltech) Comment accompanying Zhong et al.'s primary bench result (*Nat. Commun.* 17:3229–3230, 2026), which extends vent-relevant CO₂-reduction electrochemistry to carbonate and phyllosilicate substrates with trace Cu/Zn — Barge's fuel-cell lineage cited as the explicit scaffold — and whose full text, searched case-insensitively, contains **zero mentions** of spin, magnetism, magnetite, chirality, or CISS: Link 5's absence re-confirmed by direct search of the newest work adjacent to it, and the mechanism's substrate generality (carbonates, not just sulfides) modestly widens Link 4's reach, including for Enceladus, where the demonstrated NH₃-to-acetamide C–N chemistry now has a mineral-electrochemical route (the vent-electricity note carries the details).

## 6. Standing

For the axiality conjecture, this note upgrades the vent branch from "amplifier without a compass" to "amplifier with a candidate compass, scoped to ultramafic hosts, awaiting one well-defined experiment."
In the coherence-length ladder, a vent-CISS origin sits at the planetary-field rung — the geodynamo orients the serpentinization magnetite's chemical remanent magnetization, the magnetite's intrinsic spin polarization biases the chemistry, the vent's own current drives it, and the tide paces it.
Below, above, body, and moon, in one mechanism — which is either the estate's best synthesis so far or a beautiful thing waiting to be killed by experiment 1.
Both outcomes are worth the bench time.
