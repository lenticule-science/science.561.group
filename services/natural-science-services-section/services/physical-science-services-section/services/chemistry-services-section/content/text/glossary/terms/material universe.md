The material universe is (definition:: the collective term for all [[matter|physical matter]], [[energy]], and [[space]] that is [[zeds/Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating]] together).

It includes celestial bodies like [[star|stars]], [[planet|planets]], [[galaxy|galaxies]]; [[phenomenon|phenomena]] such as [[gravity]], [[light]], and [[time]]; and the [[fundamental particles]] that make up [[atom|atoms]]. This vast expanse relates through [[physics]] and encompasses everything from the largest galactic structures to the smallest subatomic particles. The material universe is where [[life]] takes form, [[civilization collapse|civilizations collapse]], and where you probably are as you're [[reading]] this.
