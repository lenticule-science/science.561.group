# Physical Science Services Section

This section holds physical-science work, split into four children: `chemistry-services-section`, `astronomy-services-section`, `optics-services-section`, and `physics-services-section`. Its children study the parent section's [four regimes of natural existence](../../content/text/glossary/topics/four-regimes-of-natural-existence.md) as physics.

`lib/obsmetrologia-physical-science` is the Lean package `ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience`: causal sets, condensed matter, kinetic theory, thermodynamics, dense matter, quantum contextuality and reaction networks, formalized over the pure-mathematics package Obsmetrologia, which it imports.
