# bonding

Generates [molecule](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-molecule.md) from [`atom`](../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/atom.md): atoms share or transfer electrons into bonded structures. The bonded, reactive macromolecules — proteins ([enzyme](../../../../glossary/terms/enzyme.md)) and nucleic acids ([dna](../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dna.md)) — are the chemistry from which life is built. This is the substrate floor: matter bonding into the molecules life will settle into agents.
