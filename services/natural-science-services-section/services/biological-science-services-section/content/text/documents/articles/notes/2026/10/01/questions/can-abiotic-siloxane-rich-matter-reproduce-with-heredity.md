# Can abiotic silicon/silica/silicate/siloxane-rich matter reproduce with heredity?

Operational answer criteria are deliberately narrower than "life."

R7 requires a bounded parent, a spatially separable daughter generated by material dynamics under a declared environment, time-resolved causal continuity, organizational similarity above spontaneous-background controls, and independent repetition.

R8 additionally requires two consecutive material-offspring transitions (G0 → G1 → G2), parent-specific phenotype information in descendants, nonzero variation, blinded phenotype extraction, and controls separating material inheritance from niche inheritance.

Manual cutting, aliquoting, casting, printing, protocol replication, or simulation forking do not answer this question.
