**Genetics** is the [organism](../../../../../../glossary/terms/organism.md)'s `inheriting` made science: how heritable information is stored, expressed, and passed on.

- [`encoding`](../../../undated/encoding.md) → [gene](../../../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gene.md): the unit of heredity, a segment of the [DNA](../../../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dna.md) molecule → `expressing` → phenotype (the realized trait).
- [`passing`](../../../undated/passing.md) → reproduction: the transmission of genes to offspring, with variation.

The variation genetics transmits is the raw material the `evolving` branch (adaptation, natural selection) acts on — see `explanations/genetics-and-evolution`.
