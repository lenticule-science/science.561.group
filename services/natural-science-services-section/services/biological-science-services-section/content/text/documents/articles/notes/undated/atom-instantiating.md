# instantiating

Generates a specific element from [`atom`](../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/atom.md) by fixing the proton number — e.g. the [hydrogen-atom](../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/atom/scripts/instantiating/content/hydrogen-atom/hydrogen-atom.md), the simplest. Particular elements (hydrogen, carbon, iron) are the instances the bonding chemistry draws on.
