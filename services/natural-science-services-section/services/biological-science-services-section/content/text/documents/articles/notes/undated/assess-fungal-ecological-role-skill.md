# Assess Fungal Ecological Role

Once a fungus is identified structurally (see `identify-fungal-structures`), its place in the ecosystem can be characterized: what it eats, what it provides, what it sustains.

## When to use

You have characterized a fungal species or community at the structural level and want to articulate its ecological role — nutrient flows, ecosystem function, interactions with other organisms.

## How it works

1. **Classify primary strategy.** Saprotrophic, mycorrhizal, parasitic, endophytic, or lichenized.
2. **Identify the substrate or host.** What organism or material is the fungus drawing from?
3. **Describe nutrient flows.** What does the fungus provide (carbon, signaling molecules, structural support)? What does it receive?
4. **Assess ecosystem function.** Decomposition, nutrient cycling, soil formation, plant health, pathogen pressure.
5. **Note interactions.** With bacteria, insects, plants, other fungi.
6. **Connect to broader context.** Forest health, agricultural implications, climate sensitivity.
