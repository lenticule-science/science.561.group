# The Three-Source Law of Biological Symmetry

## 0. Status and provenance

The owner directed the 561 Group's axiality program from molecular chirality up to body symmetry — bilateral and radial — and a ten-agent fleet (six researchers, three adversarial verifiers, one frame critic) ran the working hypothesis to destruction.
The hypothesis as posed: *an organism's symmetry group records the environmental anisotropies it has internalized* (Curie's principle applied to bodies).
The verdict: **it does not survive in that form**, and the three-source law below is what does.
The full sweep material — per-claim citations with access statuses, verification verdicts, and the critic's complete reasoning — is preserved in [the session records](../../../../../../../../../../../../data/records/2026-08-22-natural-computing-world-session/workflow-3-symmetry-bi-radial-research.json); this note is the synthesis, and cites (author, year) with the record as its access ledger.
Counterexamples were prized over confirmations by explicit instruction, and they delivered.

## 1. The law, as it died and as it survives

One correction comes first because it keeps the physics citation honest: Curie's 1894 paper already contains the escape clause verbatim — *"c'est la dissymétrie qui crée le phénomène"* — so spontaneous symmetry breaking is not a modern patch on Curie's principle; it is built into the original statement.

The surviving law needs three independent sources, not one:

1. **Environmental/functional anisotropy** (the original Curie channel) sets **axis orientation and coarse symmetry class** — spherical, uniaxial, biaxial — where a real directional cause is actively felt by a still-plastic system, and only at the scale it is felt.
2. **Economy/parsimony** (the Crick–Watson channel) sets **fine structure, subunit count, and packing**, independent of environment.
3. **Spontaneous/stochastic breaking** (Curie's own escape clause) governs **handedness and axis choice when no anisotropy selects an outcome**: the ensemble stays symmetric while each instance breaks.

And the "fossil of origin" language survives only relocated and de-permanentized (§4): the fossil is developmental-genetic, not a live reading of the adult's surroundings, and it is an overwritable attractor, not a stratigraphic record.

## 2. The three channels, evidenced

**Channel 1, environmental — real and quantitatively documented.**
Flower zygomorphy is the flagship: bilateral flowers evolved from radial ancestors at least ~130 independent times with ~69 reversions across ~761 sampled species, and zygomorphic flowers are presented horizontally — toward the pollinator's approach vector — in ~87% of cases against ~10% for actinomorphic relatives (Reyes et al. 2016, per the plant sweep; the underlying comparative statistics are flagged in §9 as worth pulling directly).
Diatoms supply the cleanest single-celled test with no bilaterian-ancestry confound: non-motile centric diatoms suspended isotropically build radial valves; motile pennate diatoms gliding on a raphe build bilateral valves with the raphe on the long axis.
Irregular echinoids (heart urchins, sand dollars) re-evolved bilaterality from pentaradial ancestors for directed one-way burrowing.
And *Euplectella*'s skeleton carries a helical surface-ridge pattern that suppresses vortex shedding in unidirectional flow — a textbook Curie response, on the 561 Group's own [GLASS-SPONGE](../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/genet-language-actuators-simulating-sentient-semiotic-pores-operating-nutrient-game-exchange.md) referent.

**Channel 2, economy — proven in principle by the oldest result in the set.**
Crick & Watson (1956) derived icosahedral/helical capsid symmetry from genome-size economy — a small genome can only afford one subunit specification, so the shell must be identical repeats, and maximal symmetry is what identical repeats buy — *before the genetic code was known*, later vindicated by solved structures.
Hox spatial/temporal collinearity (linear chromosome order mapping to body order) is a molecular-scale constraint producing organism-scale pattern with no environmental input.
The hexactinellid spicule is cubic/triaxonic by silica biomineralization chemistry, unrelated to the whole organism's flow-and-gravity axis.
Hexacorallia (6/12/24-fold mesenteries) versus Octocorallia (strictly 8-fold) share essentially one environment: Curie-style reasoning bounds coarse class only, never rotational count — a caution to attach to every future application.

**Channel 3, spontaneous — with no anisotropy in the room.**
Gastruloids polarize an anterior-posterior axis (Brachyury/T) with zero extra-embryonic tissue and zero localized external signal, by intrinsic Wnt/Nodal circuits amplifying stochastic fluctuation (van den Brink 2014; Turner 2017; 2024 follow-up, per the physics sweep) — the coherence length set by tissue-intrinsic reaction-diffusion parameters, not by any external scale.
*Hydra* reaggregates lock an axis from an isotropic cell spheroid.
Phyllotactic spiral handedness runs ~50:50 and non-heritable in most species despite universal molecular chirality in every cell.
Fiddler crab major-claw side is ~50:50 per individual in most species — with an unexplained >95%-right outlier genus (*Gelasimus*) — and traces neither to environment nor to the deep left-right molecular-chirality axis: a genuinely stochastic developmental channel.

## 3. The counterexample ledger

Each entry carries what it *actually* shows, which is rarely what it appears to show.

- **Box jellyfish (Cubozoa):** strictly tetraradial body, yet 24 camera-type eyes on bilaterally organized rhopalia, with quantified visually guided obstacle avoidance (≥120° avoidance turns within 2–3 swim pulses; ~0.1–0.25 collisions/min; Garm et al. 2007).
  Shows: environment-symmetry correspondence can live in a nested functional *subsystem* rather than the gross body plan — the correspondence is scale-local (§5).
- **Gastruloids:** axis with no environment at all.
  Shows: compatible with Curie the physical law, a direct refutation of the axiality program's environmental gloss.
- **Viral capsids:** high symmetry from informational economy alone.
  Shows: non-environmental symmetry generation as a matter of principle, textbook-strength.
- ***Nematostella*'s hidden bilaterality:** a "radial" anemone carries a molecular directive axis — staggered Hox expression, and a dpp/chordin double-negative network expressed on the *same* side rather than opposing sides (Finnerty et al. 2004, *Science*; Saina et al. 2009, *PNAS*; Genikhovich et al. 2015, *Cell Reports*).
  Shows: the coarse taxonomy was too coarse before the argument even started, and the axis's best explanation is inheritance or convergence (unsettled — §9), not present-day environmental reading.
- **Echinoderm pentaradiality:** bilateral, actively swimming larva; pentaradial settled adult; the mechanism a documented Hox-cluster rearrangement (anterior Hox1–3 translocated/inverted to the posterior end, breaking collinearity).
  Shows: symmetry class is ontogenetically and evolutionarily *labile*, with the locomotion correlation running backward within one life cycle — and "overwritable" is now a documented molecular mechanism, not an inference.
- **Irregular echinoids:** bilateral → pentaradial → bilateral within one clade's history.
  Shows: the mechanical logic confirmed *and* permanence destroyed in the same case.
- **Coral 6 versus 8:** same environment, clade-locked count.
  Shows: the environmental channel's scope boundary.
- **Hexactinellid spicule versus body:** cubic chemistry at one scale, helical flow-response at another, in one organism.
  Shows: the two lawlike sources coexisting in vivo — the strongest direct evidence for the multi-source structure.
- **Hydrothermal chimneys (abiotic control):** axisymmetric radial growth around a flow axis in dead mineral, no heredity.
  Shows: radial-from-anisotropy is general physics; the mere presence of radial symmetry cannot distinguish a *recorded fossil* from a *continuously regenerated live response* — that distinction needs dynamical evidence, such as reversion when the cause is removed (Malpighiaceae losing zygomorphy when the pollinator is lost is live-tracking; *Linaria*'s switch in §4 is a fossil).
- **Medusae:** directional jet-propelled swimmers that stay radial.
  Shows: "motile → bilateral" needs the propulsion mechanism as mediating variable (Holló & Novák 2012) — symmetric jet contraction does not reward bilateral differentiation the way crawling, paddling, or flying does.
- **Tunicates:** bilateral larva settles into a sessile adult that stays directional (siphon-driven feeding), never going radial.
  Shows: "sessile → radial" fails too; the real variable is whether the organism's dominant *functional* anisotropy is directional or omnidirectional.
- **Fiddler crabs and phyllotaxis handedness:** §2's channel-3 cases.
  Shows: molecular chirality does *not* universally cascade to macroscopic handedness — the vertebrate nodal-cilia cascade in [the axiality-conjecture note](../../../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-axiality-conjecture.md) is lineage-contingent, and should be stated that way wherever the axiality program cites it.

## 4. The fossil, relocated and de-permanentized

Where "axiality is a fossil of origin" survives, the fossil is **developmental-genetic**: inherited maternal determinants and ancient regulatory switches built by selection generations back — not a live reading of the adult's current surroundings.

The primary body axis is the decisive case: in *Nematostella* (and generally), the oral-aboral/animal-vegetal axis derives from maternal cytoplasmic and cortical determinants laid down in oogenesis — fixed at molecular scale before the embryo meets any external field; the polyp's oral-end-up posture is correlation, not cause (Lebedeva et al. 2021, *Nat. Commun.*, with the 2025 follow-up showing the conserved axis logic decoupled from a Bilateria-specific endomesoderm output — the axis is ancient, what rides on it is not).
*Linaria vulgaris* peloria is the fossil made literal: a single epigenetic switch — methylation silencing *Lcyc* — flips the whole flower between bilateral and radial, occasionally within one plant, with zero new environmental input (Cubas, Vincent & Coen 1999, *Nature*); the anisotropy is fossilized in a regulatory switch, and the switch can be thrown.
And permanence fails everywhere it is tested: the echinoderm Hox rearrangement, the irregular echinoids' return, medusozoans apparently lacking chordin and staggered Hox (loss versus convergence unsettled — §9).
A symmetry group is a **currently expressed developmental attractor, re-settable across generations** — which is the correction the axiality program's own settling vocabulary needs to absorb (§8).

## 5. Scale-decoupling, a general feature

Nearly every sweep independently surfaced the same pattern: body-plan symmetry and subsystem symmetry diverge within one organism.
The cubozoan's radial body carries bilateral rhopalial nerve nets; *Volvox*'s spherical colony carries an anterior-posterior functional eyespot gradient when swimming; the hexactinellid's radial body carries cubic spicules; the starfish's pentaradial body carries covert bilateral behavior (consistent arm-leading, the madreporite off-axis).
Because it recurred across unrelated taxa and unrelated sweeps, this is treated as a feature of biological symmetry, not a curiosity: **"the organism's symmetry group" is not a single well-defined label — symmetry is assigned per scale and per subsystem**, and any future axiality-program claim should say which scale it is about.

## 6. The left-right boundary, sharpened

Situs inversus totalis (a clean mirror) carries only mildly elevated congenital heart disease (~3–9% versus ~0.6% baseline), while situs ambiguus/heterotaxy (organs disagreeing with each other) carries >80%.
The axiality program's "the L-R axis is shallow" claim sharpens accordingly: the *choice* of mirror image is nearly cost-free; the machinery enforcing organ-to-organ *consistency* is under strong stabilizing selection, and its failure is catastrophic.
Shallow describes the output value, not the mechanism.
This section, with §3's fiddler-crab and phyllotaxis cases, is flagged for appending to the axiality-conjecture note once the current thickening pass on that file completes.

## 7. Refined statement of the locomotion law

Not "motile → bilateral, sessile → radial," but: **a body tracks the symmetry of its dominant functional interaction, at the scale of that interaction, while the interaction is developmentally actionable** — where jet propulsion is a symmetric interaction (medusae stay radial), a sessile siphon-feeder's current is directional (tunicates stay bilateral), a pollinator's approach is a vector (flowers go zygomorphic ~130 times), and a burrower's path re-bilateralizes even a pentaradial inheritance.

## 8. The axiality program's mappings, judged as ordered

- **Temple-radial versus wanderer-bilateral ([MIAOTANG](../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mimetic-interactive-actors-organizing-traces-of-articulated-neural-games.md) / [JIANGHU](../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jacobian-interactive-actors-negotiating-game-holonomy-unions.md)):** *not structurally sound* as literal biology — tunicates and medusae refute both directions, and every examined "radial" organism hides a directional substructure.
  The critic's recommended evolution rather than retirement, adopted here: the temple is never purely still — build it with a hidden processional axis, a threshold, a doorway; that is what every real radial organism turned out to have, and it is a richer image than the original.
- **Symmetry chains as settling filtrations:** valid for *ontogeny* (axes added roughly one at a time, near-monotonically; the underlying equivariant-bifurcation mathematics — Golubitsky & Stewart — is real and applied), **false for phylogeny**, where lineages cycle and revert; filtration language is henceforth ontogeny-scoped.
- **Oriented predication cells:** the best-grounded mapping — planar cell polarity (Frizzled–Flamingo trans-binding at one junction propagating polarity across hundreds of cells) is the cleanest literal instance of the axiality program's coherence-length logic found anywhere in the dataset, with one honesty clause: PCP propagation is frequently *seeded* by an upstream global cue (a Wnt gradient), making it a hybrid of channels 1 and 3 rather than purely spontaneous — say which channel is doing the orienting work in any given use.

## 9. Unresolved — do not cite as settled

- The dorsoventral inversion hypothesis (Geoffroy 1822; De Robertis & Sasai 1996) has a live, sourced counter-model (van den Biggelaar, Edsinger-Gonzales & Schram 2002: shared blastopore polarity plus lineage-specific gastrulation, no literal flip needed).
- Echinoderm pentaradiality's cause (adaptive for sessility versus developmental holdover) — disputed.
- Medusozoan chordin absence: secondary loss versus anthozoan-bilaterian convergence — the field cannot yet distinguish (Genikhovich & Technau 2017, *Development*).
- Flatfish asymmetry evolved once versus twice — a 2024 controversy per the transitions sweep.
- Irregular-echinoid re-bilateralization timing: Triassic (phylogenomic) versus Jurassic-and-later (fossil-based), tens of millions of years apart.

## 10. To investigate

The largest gap between evidence and claim: **everything gathered is case studies; no phylogenetically controlled comparative statistical test of symmetry class against locomotion/environment exists in the axiality program's hands** — and the Reyes et al. zygomorphy dataset almost certainly supports one; pull the paper's actual comparative-method results.
Second: the conjecture is about correlation *length*, and **no measured coherence length appears anywhere** — the evidence base is categorical where the claim is metric; the *Hydra* scale-free locking result is the nearest thread to pull.
Also open: tissue mechanics (cortical tension, luminal pressure) as a possible distinct channel doing the real work in *Hydra* and gastruloids; the Ediacaran record (glide symmetry in *Dickinsonia*) as the actual ancestral-state evidence the "fossil" language presupposes; colonial organisms (siphonophores, bryozoans) where "the organism" is itself ill-defined; designed artifacts as a heredity-free, genome-free control for the pure-Curie channel; and a targeted hunt for symmetry-class changes with *no* change in locomotion or economy — the sharpest possible test for further unaccounted channels.
