# Evolution

**Evolution** is the [organism](../../../../../../glossary/terms/organism.md)'s `evolving`: descent with modification across generations. It is a settling not of the individual but of the **lineage** — heritable variation supplied by [genetics](../../../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/genetics.md) is filtered into fit form.

- [`selecting`](../../../undated/selecting.md) → natural-selection (differential survival/reproduction of variants) → [`adapting`](../../../undated/adapting.md) → adaptation (the organism made suited to its environment over generations).
- [`speciating`](../../../undated/speciating.md) → species (reproductively/ecologically distinct lineages) → instances (echinacia, filipendula, rumex, verbascum).
