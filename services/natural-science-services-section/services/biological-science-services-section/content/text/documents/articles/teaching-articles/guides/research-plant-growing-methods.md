Research the methods available for growing a specific plant in a specific place. Use when deciding how to grow a plant you have not grown before or in a new location.

# Research Plant Growing Methods

- Identify the plant&rsquo;s species and cultivar, and record its requirements for light, water, soil, temperature, and spacing.

- Determine the plant&rsquo;s growth habit, propagation method, and timing (seed start date, transplant date, days to maturity).

- Assess the growing site: sun exposure, soil type and pH, drainage, water access, and microclimate factors.

- Compare plant requirements against site conditions and identify gaps.

- Consult cooperative extension guides, seed catalogs, and local growers for regionally specific advice.

- Select the growing method (direct sow, transplant, raised bed, container, or indoor) that best closes the gaps between plant needs and site conditions.
