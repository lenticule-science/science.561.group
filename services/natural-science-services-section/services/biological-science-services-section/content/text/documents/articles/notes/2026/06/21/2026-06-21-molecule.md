A **molecule** is two or more [atoms](../../../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/atom.md) held together by chemical bonds. In the life tree the molecules that matter are the **biomolecules**: proteins — including [enzymes](../../../../../../glossary/terms/enzyme.md), the catalysts that make metabolic reactions fast enough for life — and nucleic acids such as [DNA](../../../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dna.md), the informational polymer. Bonding atoms into reactive, foldable, information-bearing structures is the step that makes a self-sustaining reaction network — and so a [cell](../../../../../../glossary/terms/cell.md) — possible. Molecule is generated by [`bonding`](../../../undated/bonding.md) atoms; it is the substrate floor's second storey, between bare matter and the living cell.
