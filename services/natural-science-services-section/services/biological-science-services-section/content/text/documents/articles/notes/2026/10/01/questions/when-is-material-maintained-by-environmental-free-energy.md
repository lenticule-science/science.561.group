# When is a smart material maintained by environmental free-energy throughput?

R5 requires an ON → OFF → RESTART experiment with a measured source flux and a preregistered organized function Q(t).

Evidence must show that source interruption reproducibly causes loss or accelerated degradation of organized function and that restoring the source stabilizes or restores function without manual reconstruction.

A material that merely warms, moves, grows, or heals while powered is energy-responsive; it is not automatically energy-maintained. R5 also remains weaker than metabolism.
