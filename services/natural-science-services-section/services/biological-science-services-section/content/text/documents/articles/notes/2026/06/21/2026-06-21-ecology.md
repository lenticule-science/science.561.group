**Ecology** is the [organism](../../../../../../glossary/terms/organism.md)'s `relating` made into a science: the study of how organisms interact with each other and their environment. Where one organism is an agent, ecology is the **society of agents** — the multi-agent reading of life.

- [`systematizing`](../../../undated/systematizing.md) → [ecosystem](../../../../../../../../services/ecology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ecosystem.md): the community-plus-environment unit, through which `flowing` (trace energy flow / food webs), `cycling` (the nitrogen cycle and nutrient cycling), `interacting` (symbiosis, niche-construction, allelopathy, biological control), `invading` (invasive species, enemy release, monoculture), and `habituating` (grasslands, riparian habitats) run.
- [`surrounding`](../../../undated/surrounding.md) → environment: the abiotic context an organism relates to.
- [`stewarding`](../../../undated/stewarding.md) → the applied reading: land stewardship, restoration, urban ecology and forestry, food sovereignty, invasive-species management.
- [`fielding`](../../../undated/fielding.md) → field observation of specific lands — including [phahíŋ siŋté makȟóčhe](../../../../research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/_archive/atom/scripts/bonding/content/molecule/scripts/assembling/content/cell/scripts/integrating/content/organism/scripts/relating/content/ecology/scripts/fielding/content/phahin-sinte-makoce) and other place studies.
