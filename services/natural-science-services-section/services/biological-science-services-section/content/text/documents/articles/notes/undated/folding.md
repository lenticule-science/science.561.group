# folding

Generates [enzyme](../../../../glossary/terms/enzyme.md) from [`molecule`](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-molecule.md): a protein folds into a shape that catalyzes a specific reaction. Enzymes are what make metabolism — the cell's self-sustaining reaction network — run at the rates life needs.
