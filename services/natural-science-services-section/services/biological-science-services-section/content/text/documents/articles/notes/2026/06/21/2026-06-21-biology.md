# Biology (staged derivation)

## What this is (and the misnaming it corrects)

The `content/_archive/chemistry/` directory was misnamed: it held ~555 biology + ~51 earth-science files and only ~5 actual chemistry files. This is the corpus error of filing by a name rather than by what a thing is. Corrected here:

- **Earth-science** (climate, hydrology, soil, environment) → lifted to sibling [`_archive/earth-science/`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/earth-science).
- **Observation methodology** (iNaturalist, citizen-science: data-quality, observer-bias, when-not-to-id, …) → lifted to [`_archive/knowing/`](../../../../research/2026/07/31/knowing) — it is a theory of *observing*, not of life.
- The remainder is **biology**, derived here; the ~5 chemistry files sit as the **substrate floor**, since matter is what life is generated from (not a separate discipline).

## The reading: an organism is an agent

The corpus reads life relationally, not reductively. An **organism** is a self-maintaining **agent**: homeostasis is its settling (a fixed point of feedback), metabolism a reaction network settling toward steady state, and response-to-stimulus its perceive→act loop — cognition present even without neurons (Physarum maze-solving, fungal network optimization). `allovonic-biology` frames living form as "recursive response, not local construction." So the apex is the organism, and biology is the science of these living agents.

## The spine (substrate floor → apex)

- [`atom`](../../../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/atom.md) — the unit of matter (the corpus's chemistry as substrate) → `ionizing`→ ions (hydrogen-ion, alkali); `instantiating`→ elements (hydrogen-atom).
- → `bonding`→ [`molecule`](2026-06-21-molecule.md) — biomolecules → `folding`→ enzyme; `encoding`→ dna.
- → `assembling`→ [`cell`](../../../../../../glossary/terms/cell.md) — the membrane-bounded self-sustaining reaction network; the basic unit of life.
- → `integrating`→ **`organism`** (apex) — the living agent.

## What hangs off the organism

Each branch is a reading of the living agent; the relocated sub-corpora sit under it (their internal proof-tree derivation is a later pass):

- `maintaining` → **physiology** — metabolism, homeostasis (the self-maintaining settling).
- `developing` → **morphogenesis** — growth and form.
- `inheriting` → **genetics** — heredity, genes, DNA expression.
- `behaving` → **ethology / cognition** — the perceive→act loop, cognition without neurons.
- `evolving` → **adaptation / species** — variation under selection across generations (a settling over the lineage).
- `relating` → **ecology / holobiont** — life as a society of agents (multi-agent relation, symbiosis).
- `speciating` → **plant / fungus** — the kingdoms of organism (botany, mycology) and their species.
