# ionizing

Generates ions from [`atom`](../../../../../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/atom.md) by gain or loss of electrons — e.g. the [hydrogen-ion](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/services/research-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hydrogen-ion.md) (a bare proton, the unit of acidity) and the bases that define an [alkali](alkali.md). Ionic chemistry sets the pH and reactivity that biomolecules and cells must hold in homeostasis.
