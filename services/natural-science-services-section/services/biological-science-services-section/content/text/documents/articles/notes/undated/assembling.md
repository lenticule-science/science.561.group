# assembling

Generates [cell](../../../../glossary/terms/cell.md) from [`molecule`](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-molecule.md): bounded, self-maintaining assembly of biomolecules into the basic unit of life. A cell is where a molecular reaction network first closes into a self-sustaining compartment — the first settling toward life.
