# encoding

Generates [dna](../../../../../../services/genetics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dna.md) from [`molecule`](../2026/06/21/2026-06-21-molecule.md): a nucleic-acid polymer that encodes heritable information. DNA is the informational biomolecule the organism inherits and varies — the substrate of genetics and evolution.
