A **plant** is a kind of [organism](../../../../../../glossary/terms/organism.md): a photosynthetic, cell-walled, (mostly) sessile living agent. In this corpus's reading a plant is not a finished form but, in the meristem's words, *"always a system in the process of becoming"* — its body is **generated**, not assembled.

## How a plant is generated

- [`dividing`](../../../undated/dividing.md) → [meristem](../../../../../../../../services/botany-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/meristem.md): the region of undifferentiated dividing cells, **the generative origin of plant form**, persistent and indeterminate.
  - `differentiating` → the **tissues** (vascular — xylem/phloem/cambium; dermal — stomata; ground) and the cell-wall material.
  - the tissues `form` the vegetative **organs** — root, leaf, stem/rhizome.

## What a plant does

- [`photosynthesizing`](../../../undated/photosynthesizing.md) → photosynthesis (chloroplast, the pigments) — fixing light into sugar.
- `transporting` (within the vascular tissue) → transpiration (water, via xylem) and translocation (sugar, via phloem).
- `signaling` → the hormones (auxin, cytokinin, gibberellin, ethylene, abscisic acid) driving tropism, photoperiod, vernalization, bolting, senescence.
- `defending` → the defensive chemistry (anthocyanin, capsaicin, glucosinolate, allelopathy).

## How a plant reproduces

- [`reproducing`](../../../undated/reproducing.md) → the [flower](../../../../../../../../services/botany-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/flower.md): `pollinating` (pollen, ovule, double-fertilization) → `fruiting` → `seeding` (seed, germination, dispersal, dormancy).

## How a plant is classified

- [`classifying`](../../../undated/scripts-classifying.md) → [angiosperm](../../04/02/2026-04-02-angiosperm.md) (flowering plant) → monocot / eudicot → **families** → **species**. Shared ancestry predicts shared traits (chemistry, morphology, pests), so the species sit as instances under their family.
