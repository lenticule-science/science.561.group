# Artificial Abiogenesis / Smartgoo Research Program

## Program question

Can deliberately selected populations of genuinely abiotic matter be driven through experimentally measurable capacities including environmental energy capture, directed growth, persistent material memory, adaptive response, self-repair, compartmentalization, reproduction, heredity, variation, and eventually open-ended evolution, without treating silicon as a drop-in biochemical substitute for carbon or treating conventional digital computation as the organism?

The target is organized matter whose information processing is inseparable from its material dynamics.

The long-horizon possibility — carbon life deliberately originating another lineage of adaptive matter capable of inhabiting environmental regimes carbon organisms cannot — is an imaginative possibility motivating investigation, not a prediction.

## Epistemic discipline

Every program claim is maintained in one of five classes:

- **D — demonstrated internally:** observed or executed by 561 Group with preserved evidence.
- **E — externally supported:** supported by outside science but not demonstrated internally.
- **P — engineering proposal:** physically motivated design not yet demonstrated.
- **M — mathematical conjecture/model:** formal structure that may organize experiments but is not itself a materials result.
- **I — imaginative possibility:** horizon motivating search, not predication.

No claim may be silently promoted.

## What is and is not demonstrated

There is currently no accepted internal evidence of:

- a physical Smartgoo specimen;
- a J26 siloxane monocrystal;
- abiotic material reproduction;
- material heredity;
- Darwinian evolution of a silicon/silicate/siloxane lineage.

The July J26 / PDMS–PLA note is itself marked `status: design`; it is therefore mined for falsifiable hypotheses, not treated as a fabrication report.

The current program has also corrected several overstatements from that design line:

- J26 is a **challenger morphology**, not an assumed block-copolymer ground state.
- PDMS is not categorically chemically immune to strong alkaline conditions.
- residual PDMS/organosiloxane, silica/SiOx, and true zeolite frameworks are materially distinct.
- a protected defect is not metabolism merely because it preserves metastability.

Early rungs may be realized in different substrates and combined later.

The R and S ladders are orthogonal. Neither implies the other automatically.

## First material families

### Family A — dynamic siloxane memory × repair

Target R1 and R3.

Use permanent-network PDMS controls and literature-qualified dynamic siloxane networks. Measure history recoverability after matched perturbation sequences, repeated mechanical damage/healing, and creep separately.

Working P/M hypothesis: a topology-exchange timescale may organize a plasticity–retention frontier. Very slow exchange may preserve state while impairing repair; very fast exchange may repair while erasing history or allowing creep; an intermediate regime may support both. This is to be tested, not assumed.

### Family B — silica/silicate chemical-garden growth × compartment

Target R4 and R6.

The first conservative branch uses magnesium chloride / sodium silicate chemical gardens because they provide an experimentally established abiotic growth and mineral-membrane platform without beginning with more burdensome transition-metal chemistries.

Measure growth, directionality, permeability/transport, compartment lifetime, rupture/reseal behavior, pressure dynamics, and history dependence. A hollow tube is not called semipermeable without transport evidence.

### R5 — energy-coupled maintenance

Target sustained organization or functional recovery that is causally dependent on ongoing environmental free-energy throughput.

Primary causal assay:

`SOURCE ON → SOURCE OFF → SOURCE RESTART`.

A material passes only if a preregistered organized function degrades or loses stability when throughput is interrupted and stabilizes or recovers when throughput returns, with measured source flux and matched passive/source-only controls.

The first preferred implementation extends the mineral-membrane line using literature-qualified iron-hydroxide/silica chemical-garden electrochemistry. A secondary branch examines light-gated dynamic-siloxane repair with matched-temperature controls.

R5 is deliberately weaker than metabolism.

## Reproduction and heredity boundary

Research genealogy is not material reproduction.

Typed lineage relations distinguish manual replication, engineered variants, simulation forks, sample derivation, material fusion, protocol descent, and **material-offspring**.

Only a physical `material-offspring` event can directly support R7.

R7 requires a bounded parent, a spatially separable daughter generated by material dynamics under a declared environment, time-resolved causal continuity, reproduced organization beyond trivial common chemistry, spontaneous-background controls, and independent repetition.

R8 requires at least:

`G0 → G1 → G2`

through two consecutive material-offspring transitions, with parent-specific phenotype information detectable in descendants, nonzero variation, blinded phenotype extraction, and environmental randomization/cross-fostering sufficient to distinguish inheritance from persistent niche effects.

Engineering selection among manually fabricated specimens remains evolutionary engineering, not Darwinian evolution of the material lineage.

## Evolutionary-engineering architecture

Every experiment/simulation is treated as a typed specimen with:

- specimen id;
- family and assay contract;
- reality layer: physical / simulation / reference-oracle;
- genotype and environment references;
- immutable provenance;
- typed lineage edges;
- raw observations;
- derived phenotype metrics;
- epistemic capability assessments.

Missing metrics are **unknown**, never silently converted to zero.

Simulation and physical specimens do not directly compete in one selection cohort unless a separately validated calibration relation exists.

Selection is cohort-local:

- Pareto dominance for measured quality;
- explicit descriptor-space novelty for exploration;
- bounded active archives that preserve Pareto-front specimens, high-novelty dominated specimens, controls, and causally informative failures.

There is no global scalar "life score."

The canonical witness projection uses the house semiotic-EAVT shape:

`[entity, attribute, value, time]`.

The EAVT adapter records observations and provenance but does not assign object-level FOUR readings; evidence interpretation and settling remain downstream.
