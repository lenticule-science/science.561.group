---
title: "frost"
type: "term"
date-created: 2026-03-06
---

A weather condition that occurs when the air temperature at ground level
drops to 0°C (32°F) or below. Water inside plant cells freezes, expands,
and ruptures cell walls, causing damage that is often fatal to tender
plants. The average dates of last spring frost and first autumn frost
define the boundaries of a location&rsquo;s growing season.
