***Energetic herbalism*** is (definition:: a [[herbalism practice]] that is based on a [[truth]] that the [[essence]] of [[nature]] and [[human]] are the same.)

It is defined in [[Energetic Herbalism, A Guide to Sacred Plant Traditions Integrating Elements of Vitalism, Ayurveda, and Chinese Medicine]], by [[Kat Maier]].
