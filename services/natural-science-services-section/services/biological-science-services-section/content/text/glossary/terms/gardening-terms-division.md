---
name: "division"
date-created: 2026-03-07
---

The process of splitting a mature plant&rsquo;s root system into two or more
separate plants, each with a portion of roots and stems or buds.
Division works with plants that naturally form clumps or spreading root
systems. It is used to propagate perennials, to spread a plant to new
locations, or to rejuvenate an overcrowded clump whose growth has
declined. Common plants propagated by division include chives, rhubarb,
and strawberries.
