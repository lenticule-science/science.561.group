**Morphogenesis** is the process by which tissues and organisms acquire and change spatial structure through interacting developmental regulation, cell behaviors, molecular signals and material forces. It is not merely the execution of a fixed geometric blueprint, nor is it a phenomenon independent of the genome.

[Turing's 1952 reaction-diffusion model](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.1952.0012) demonstrates that suitable reaction/diffusion systems can destabilize a homogeneous equilibrium and create pattern; a specific living system requires biological measurement and validation. The [source-owned morphogenesis research](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/03/06/2026-03-06-morphogenesis-2026-03-09.md) discusses Thompson, Turing, fungal growth and relational interpretations. Folding and agentic EDA are **proposed engineered applications of constraints and search**, not proved biological equivalences.

Research context: [gene regulation and mechanics in morphogenesis (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semcdb.2025.103654).
