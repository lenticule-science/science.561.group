---
name: "tuber"
date-created: 2026-03-07
---

A thickened portion of an underground stem that stores energy for the
plant. Each &ldquo;eye&rdquo; (growth bud) on a tuber can produce a new shoot. In
gardening, tubers are planted directly in the ground at the appropriate
depth and spacing. The most common garden tuber is the potato.
