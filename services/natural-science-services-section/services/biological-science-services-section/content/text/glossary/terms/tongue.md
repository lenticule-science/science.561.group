The ***tongue*** is a muscular [organ](organism.md) of the mouth. It moves food while chewing and swallowing, carries the taste buds, and shapes the sounds of speech.

[Traditional Chinese medicine](../../../../services/medical-science-services-section/services/traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/traditional-chinese-medicine.md) reads its body colour, coating and shape as one of the four examinations of [pattern diagnosis](../../../../services/medical-science-services-section/services/traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pattern-diagnosis.md): see [tongue diagnosis basics](../../../../services/medical-science-services-section/services/traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/tongue-diagnosis-basics.md).
