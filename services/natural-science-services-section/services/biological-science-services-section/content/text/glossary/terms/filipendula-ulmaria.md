---
title: "Filipendula Ulmaria"
date-created: 2026-03-04T00:00:00
---

Common name: meadowsweet.

Family: Rosaceae.

Contains salicylic acid, a precursor to aspirin, and methyl salicylate, which gives it a slight wintergreen smell. It is generally safe and broadly anti-inflammatory; it may be particularly helpful for chronic or acute gastrointestinal pain because it is soothing on the gut.

Preparation:

- tea
- tincture
- glycerite

Parts used:

- leaves
- inflorescence

It is not native to North America.

7songsevensong suggests combining meadowsweet, catnip, chamomile, and fennel in equal parts for gastrointestinal pain.
