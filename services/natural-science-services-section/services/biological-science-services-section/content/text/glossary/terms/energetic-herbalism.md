***Energetic herbalism*** is (definition:: a [herbalism practice](herbalism practice) that is based on a [truth](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/truth.md) that the [essence](essence) of [nature](nature) and [human](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/human.md) are the same.)

It is defined in [Energetic Herbalism, A Guide to Sacred Plant Traditions Integrating Elements of Vitalism, Ayurveda, and Chinese Medicine](Energetic Herbalism, A Guide to Sacred Plant Traditions Integrating Elements of Vitalism, Ayurveda, and Chinese Medicine), by [Kat Maier](Kat Maier).
