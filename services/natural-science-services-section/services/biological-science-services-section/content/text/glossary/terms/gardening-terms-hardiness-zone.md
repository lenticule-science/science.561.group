---
name: "hardiness zone"
type: "term"
date-created: 2026-03-06
---

A geographic classification based on the average annual minimum winter
temperature. The USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map divides North America into
zones numbered 1 through 13, each subdivided into &ldquo;a&rdquo; and &ldquo;b&rdquo; halves.
A plant&rsquo;s hardiness zone rating indicates the coldest zone in which it
can survive winter.
