---
name: "seed packet"
date-created: 2026-03-07
---

The packaging in which seeds are sold, containing both the seeds and
printed growing information. A seed packet typically lists: the plant
species and cultivar name, days to maturity, light requirements, spacing
and planting depth, when to sow (relative to frost dates), and a brief
description of the plant&rsquo;s characteristics. Seed catalogs provide the
same information in a browsable format, often with more detail on
cultivar selection, disease resistance, and regional suitability.
