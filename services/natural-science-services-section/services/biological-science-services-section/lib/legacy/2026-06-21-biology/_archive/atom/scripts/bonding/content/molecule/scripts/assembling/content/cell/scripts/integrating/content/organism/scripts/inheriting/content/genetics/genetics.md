**Genetics** is the [organism](../../../organism.md)'s `inheriting` made science: how heritable information is stored, expressed, and passed on.

- [`encoding`](scripts/encoding/encoding.md) → [gene](scripts/encoding/content/gene/gene.md): the unit of heredity, a segment of the [DNA](../../../scripts/inheriting/content/genetics/../../../../../../bonding/content/molecule/scripts/encoding/content/dna/dna.md) molecule → `expressing` → phenotype (the realized trait).
- [`passing`](scripts/passing/passing.md) → reproduction: the transmission of genes to offspring, with variation.

The variation genetics transmits is the raw material the `evolving` branch (adaptation, natural selection) acts on — see `explanations/genetics-and-evolution`.
