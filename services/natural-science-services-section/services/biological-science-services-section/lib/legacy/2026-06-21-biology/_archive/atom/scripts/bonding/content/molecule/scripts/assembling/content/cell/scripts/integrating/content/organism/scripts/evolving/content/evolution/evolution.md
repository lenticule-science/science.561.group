# Evolution

**Evolution** is the [organism](../../../organism.md)'s `evolving`: descent with modification across generations. It is a settling not of the individual but of the **lineage** — heritable variation supplied by [genetics](../../../scripts/inheriting/content/genetics/genetics.md) is filtered into fit form.

- [`selecting`](scripts/selecting/selecting.md) → natural-selection (differential survival/reproduction of variants) → [`adapting`](scripts/selecting/content/natural-selection/scripts/adapting/adapting.md) → adaptation (the organism made suited to its environment over generations).
- [`speciating`](scripts/speciating/speciating.md) → species (reproductively/ecologically distinct lineages) → instances (echinacia, filipendula, rumex, verbascum).
