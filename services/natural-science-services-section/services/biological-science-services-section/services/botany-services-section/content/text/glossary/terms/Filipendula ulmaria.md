common name: meadowsweet
family: Rosaceae
contains salicylic acid (precursor to aspirin)
generally safe
broadly anti-inflammatory
- may be particularly helpful for gut pain 
  - soothing on gut
  - chronic or acute gastrointestinal pain
methyl salicylate content 
- results in slight wintergreen smell
preparation:
- tea
- tincture
- glycerite
parts used:
- leaves
- inflorescence
not native to North America
7songsevensong suggests combining meadowsweet, catnip, chamomile, fennel in equal parts for GI pain
