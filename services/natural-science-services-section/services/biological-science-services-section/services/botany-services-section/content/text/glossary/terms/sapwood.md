***Sapwood*** is a [[town]] somewhere [[west]] of [[Tyopolis]]. It is a [[spoof]] on towns of the [[Wild West]], known for being the last place to exploit saps before they'd be taken advantage of by [[Tyopolite|Tyopolites]]. There are two types of travelers that move through Sapwood: [[tourist|tourists]] and [[trader|traders]], and the two basically keep to the Old Town and Markets, respectively.

Sapwood was established along the [[Unroad]] sometime between [[40pc]] and [[50pc]], having developed into a [[one-shop town]] by [[65pc]]. As of [[82pc]], the original buildings have become known as "Old Town," with the [[Harton Hotel]] located to the northwest. Slightly southeastward are the Markets. The Unroad, which used to run through the center of Old Town, now wraps north around Sapwood to highlight the Hotel and bypass.
