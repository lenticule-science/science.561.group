# Biochemistry Services Section

The Biochemistry Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for
the chemistry of living systems: metabolism, signaling molecules,
enzymatic process, and the molecular substrate of physiological
regulation.

It sits under the biological sciences and works closely with its
physical-science cousin, the [Chemistry Services
Section](../../../physical-science-services-section/services/chemistry-services-section/README.md),
and with [Physiology](../physiology-services-section/README.md) and
[Neuroscience](../neuroscience-services-section/README.md), whose
mechanisms it grounds at the molecular level.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/` — currently
[Neurochemistry](services/neurochemistry-services-section/README.md).

Opened 2026-08-26 in service of the attention/affect research line
(hope–care–fear / attention–grace–abandonment), whose empirical
calibration runs through stress endocrinology and the neurochemistry of
threat and holding.
