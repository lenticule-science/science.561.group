# Biochemistry

Biochemistry is the study of the chemical substances and processes that
occur within living organisms: metabolism, macromolecular structure,
enzymatic catalysis, and molecular signaling. It is the level of
description at which the estate's attention-economic claims — that
attention is spent, that its renewal is income to a bounded stock, that
chronic threat is a standing tax — either acquire literal mechanisms
(metabolite accumulation, hormonal cascades, receptor dynamics) or are
revealed as structural analogies.
