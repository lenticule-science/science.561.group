# Neurochemistry Services Section

The Neurochemistry Services Section maintains knowledge and practices
for the molecular signaling of nervous systems: neurotransmitters,
neuromodulators, neuroendocrine cascades, and the metabolic chemistry of
neural computation.

It is the molecular floor under the [Neuroscience Services
Section](../../../neuroscience-services-section/README.md) and the
neural specialization of its parent, the [Biochemistry Services
Section](../../README.md).

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.

Opened 2026-08-26 in service of the attention/affect research line: the
glutamate economics of cognitive fatigue, catecholamine signaling under
uncontrollable stress, glucocorticoid dynamics, and the
social-buffering/oxytocin literature all live here as the mechanisms —
confirmed, contested, or refuted — beneath the theory's
attention-as-consumed-stock substrate.
