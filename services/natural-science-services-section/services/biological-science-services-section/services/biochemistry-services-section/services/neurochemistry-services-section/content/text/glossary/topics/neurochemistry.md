# Neurochemistry

Neurochemistry is the study of the chemical signaling of nervous
systems — neurotransmitters, neuromodulators, hormones acting on neural
tissue, and the metabolic chemistry of neural computation. For this
org it is where "attention is spent" stops being bookkeeping and
becomes measurable chemistry: what accumulates when control is
exercised, what clears it, what chronic threat does to the signaling
that top-down attention runs on, and what — molecularly — being held
does for a stressed body.
