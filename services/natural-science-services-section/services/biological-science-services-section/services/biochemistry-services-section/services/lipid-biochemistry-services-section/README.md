# Lipid Biochemistry Services Section

The Lipid Biochemistry Services Section maintains knowledge for the study of
lipids as structural components, energy stores, and signaling molecules, and
of the lipid-protein interactions that govern cellular function. The section
holds the operational definition of the lipid class, the major families
(glycerolipids, glycerophospholipids, sphingolipids, sterols), the measurement
techniques and their limitations, and the mechanisms by which lipid composition
determines membrane properties and enables protein assembly.

The section's organizing claim is that membrane lipid asymmetry — the
ATP-dependent maintenance of differing leaflet composition — is an actively
regulated state with bioenergetic cost, not a static property. This regulation
is carried out by three transporter classes (flippases, floppases, scramblases)
with different ATP dependence and directionality. Loss of asymmetry, through
ATP depletion or disease, produces externalization of normally inner-leaflet
lipids, particularly phosphatidylserine. This exposure serves two critical
functions: it is an "eat me" signal for macrophage clearance, and it is the
catalytic surface on which the prothrombinase complex assembles. That dual
role connects lipid biology to both immunology and haemostasis, and makes
phosphatidylserine externalization the mechanistic bridge between cellular
energy failure and microvascular clotting:

- **Coagulation** —
  [Hematology](../../../medical-science-services-section/services/hematology-services-section/README.md)
  holds the prothrombinase complex and the membrane-localized model of the
  coagulation cascade.
- **Immunology** — [Immunology](../../../medical-science-services-section/services/immunology-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/immunology.md)
  holds macrophage recognition and the "eat me" signal pathways.
- **Membrane Biology** — [Physiology](../../../physiology-services-section/README.md)
  and [Biochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/README.md)
  hold cellular ion transport and the ATP costs of maintaining gradients.
- **Measurement** — this section holds the lipidomics platforms and their
  reproducibility issues.

This section does not restate those carriers. It defines lipid classes and
properties, names the measurement instruments and their confounds, and holds
the syntheses connecting membrane-lipid asymmetry to the bioenergetic cost
of maintaining it.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
