## A. HOME CULTIVATION REGULATIONS

### Plant Limits and Maturity Definition

Adults 21 years or older may legally cultivate up to 8 cannabis plants at a single residence per [Minn. Stat. § 342.09, Subd. 2(a)](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09). Of these 8 plants, no more than 4 may be mature, flowering plants. Minnesota Statute does not provide an explicit statutory definition of "mature"—the term is used only to establish numerical limits tied to plant stage (mature flowering vs. immature/vegetative).

Cultivation must occur at the primary residence of an individual 21 years of age or older. Medical cannabis patients registered with the Office of Cannabis Management may be eligible for higher limits under separate provisions; a pending proposal would increase medical patient home grow limits from 8 to 16 plants, though this is not yet law as of October 2026.

### Enclosed and Locked Space Requirements

All plants must be grown "in an enclosed, locked space that is not open to public view" [Minn. Stat. § 342.09, Subd. 2(a)](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09). The statute does not prescribe specific construction materials, technical lock systems, or security features; the requirement is that the space be both physically enclosed and locked, with no visibility from public areas.

### Possession Limits at Residence and in Public

- At primary residence: 2 pounds or less of cannabis flower
- In public (transport): 2 ounces or less of cannabis flower, 8 grams or less of cannabis concentrate
- Edibles: 800 milligrams or less of tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) in combined edible products per [Minn. Stat. § 342.09, Subd. 1](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09)

### Gifting Rules

Adults 21 and older may gift cannabis to other adults 21 and older without remuneration (payment or compensation) in the following amounts per [Minn. Stat. § 342.09, Subd. 3](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09):
- 2 ounces or less of cannabis flower
- 8 grams or less of cannabis concentrate
- Edible cannabis products containing 800 milligrams or less of THC

Businesses cannot give cannabis as samples or promotional gifts. Cannabis cannot be gifted to anyone under 21.

### Sale Prohibition

No person may sell cannabis flower, cannabis products, or related items without a license issued by the Office of Cannabis Management per [Minn. Stat. § 342.09](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09). Home cultivation is limited to personal use only.

### Home Extraction and Edibles

Home extraction of cannabis concentrate using volatile solvents (butane, propane, hexane, or similar) is prohibited [Minn. Stat. § 342.09, Subd. 2(a)](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/342.09). Only licensed cannabis manufacturers, microbusinesses, and mezzobusinesses may perform solvent-based extraction. This ban applies statewide to prevent explosions and fire hazards.

Non-volatile extraction methods (e.g., cold water, ice, infusions) are not explicitly prohibited in the statute, though their status in home use is not clarified. Home preparation of edibles from flower is implicitly permitted under the framework that adults may possess flower and edibles at home.

### Penalties for Exceeding Limits

Per [Minnesota cannabis penalties](https://norml.org/laws/minnesota-penalties-2):
- More than 8 plants (or more than 4 mature) up to 16: civil penalty of $500 per plant in excess
- More than 16 and fewer than 23 plants: cultivation of cannabis in the second degree, a gross misdemeanor: not more than 1 year imprisonment and/or a $3,000 fine
- 23 or more plants: cultivation of cannabis in the first degree, a felony: not more than 5 years imprisonment and/or a $10,000 fine

### Medical Patient Home Grow

As of October 2026, registered medical cannabis patients cultivate under the same limits as adult-use (8 plants, 4 mature). A legislative proposal exists to double medical patient limits to 16 plants, but this is not yet enacted. Medical patients pay no sales tax on purchases from state-licensed dispensaries.

### Local Ordinances and City Power

Cities in Minnesota have limited authority over home cannabis cultivation. State law prohibits local governments from making cannabis use illegal in private residences, yards, or patios per [Minnesota cannabis local regulations](https://www.kaxe.org/local-news/2024-07-15/local-cannabis-regulations-mn-cultivation-dispensaries). Cities may impose moratoria on cannabis businesses (retailers, cultivators, manufacturers) with deadlines, but cannot outlaw home growing itself. Cities retain power to regulate where cannabis consumption occurs in public spaces.

### Odor Nuisance Law

Minn. Stat. § 342.09 contains no odor provision for home cultivation. Odor that interferes with neighbors' comfortable enjoyment of property falls under Minnesota's general private-nuisance law, [Minn. Stat. § 561.01](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/561.01), which allows an injured neighbor to sue for an injunction and damages. A carbon-filtered exhaust is the engineering answer.

### Landlord and Lease Rules

Landlords may prohibit smoking or vaping of cannabis in rental units. However, landlords generally cannot ban a tenant from legally possessing cannabis products in a residence. Starting March 1, 2025, smoking and vaping of cannabis are banned in multifamily housing buildings statewide, regardless of landlord policy, except for registered medical patients [Minnesota cannabis in multifamily housing](https://www.fsresidential.com/minnesota/news-events/articles/minnesota-cannabis-laws/).

### Driving and Public Possession

A peace officer's perception of cannabis odor alone is insufficient grounds for a warrantless vehicle search per [Minn. Stat. § 626.223](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2025/cite/626.223). Driving under the influence of cannabis remains illegal and is subject to DUI enforcement. Cannabis must be transported in sealed, unopen containers while in a vehicle; open consumption while driving is prohibited.

### Federal and State Schedule Status

As of October 2026, Minnesota cannabis remains illegal under federal law (Schedule I). However, medical cannabis was federally rescheduled to Schedule III in April 2026. Minnesota state law treats adult-use cannabis distinctly, and the Office of Cannabis Management regulates both adult-use and medical programs separately. The federal hemp redefinition (0.4 mg THC total per container, effective December 11, 2026) does not directly affect state cannabis programs, though it impacts lower-potency hemp edibles (LPHE).

## B. LEGAL SOURCING OPTIONS

### State-Licensed Adult-Use Dispensaries

Minnesota's adult-use cannabis retail market launched September 16, 2025. As of September 25, 2026, the Office of Cannabis Management had issued 392 cannabis business licenses covering 223 licensed adult-use retail sites; licensed sites open on their own schedules, and the [OCM Cannabis Market Monitor](https://mn.gov/ocm/) and the [Star Tribune map](https://www.startribune.com/map-see-where-you-can-legally-buy-marijuana-in-minnesota/601422464) track which are selling ([Marijuana Herald, Oct 2026](https://themarijuanaherald.com/2026/10/minnesota-400-marijuana-licenses/)). The Office of Cannabis Management projects the need for at least 381 dispensaries to serve all Minnesota residents per one capita standard (1 per 12,500 residents).

State-licensed dispensaries carry cannabis flower, pre-rolls, concentrates, and edible products. A list of licensed retailers is maintained by the OCM, though a public directory with real-time location data was not consistently accessible as of October 2026.

### Tribal Cannabis Dispensaries

Four federally recognized Minnesota tribal nations operate cannabis dispensaries under state-tribal compacts signed in 2023. These are sovereign operations not subject to all state regulations, though they sell products compliant with state standards:

1. **Red Lake Nation (NativeCare)**
   - Beltrami County location (original, on tribal land): First dispensary to open in Minnesota, August 2023
   - West St. Paul location: 2067 S Robert Street, Ten Acres Center; opened March 20, 2025; hours 9:30 AM – 7:00 PM daily; tribal member and first-time customer discounts available per [NativeCare West St. Paul](https://weststpaulreader.com/2026/03/11/first-cannabis-shop-opens-wsp)
   - Announced expansion to Thief River Falls metro region (pending 2026)

2. **White Earth Nation (Waabigwan Mashkiki)**
   - Mahnomen: 515 E Jefferson Ave, on White Earth Reservation (Northwest MN)
   - Moorhead: 2902 US-10 (Fargo-Moorhead metro)
   - Saint Cloud: 141 33rd Ave S (Central MN)
   - East Grand Forks: 926 Central Avenue NE
   - Chanhassen (coming 2026): 951 W 78th St (Twin Cities west metro)
   - Website: [Waabigwan Mashkiki](https://waabigwan.com/)

3. **Leech Lake Band of Ojibwe (Leech Lake Cannabis Co.)**
   - Walker location (Sweetest Grass): 6838 Y Frontage Road NW, at highways 371 and 200 intersection; opened June 6, 2024
   - Cass Lake location: Opened October 2025 (second location expansion)
   - Per [Leech Lake Sweetest Grass dispensary](https://lptv.org/leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe-opens-2nd-dispensary-in-cass-lake-1/)

4. **Prairie Island Indian Community (Island Peži)**
   - Location: Welch, near Red Wing, across from Treasure Island Resort & Casino, Highway 316 area
   - Opened June 29, 2024; hours 9 AM – 9 PM daily
   - Products: Flower, pre-rolls, vaporizers, edibles, concentrates
   - Per [Island Peži Prairie Island](https://kare11.com/article/news/local/cannabis-dispensary-opens-at-prairie-island-community/89-7f56a8ef-6772-4815-a67c-c043178452b5)

### Medical Cannabis Dispensaries

Registered medical cannabis patients (with qualifying conditions and state certification) may purchase from state-licensed medical cannabis dispensaries and tribal medical dispensaries. As of March 2025, recertification for qualifying medical conditions moved from annual to every 3 years. Medical cannabis is exempt from state sales tax. No separate medical-only dispensary list is maintained apart from adult-use licensees; many adult-use retailers also serve medical patients.

### Lower-Potency Hemp Edible (LPHE) Program

The LPHE program allows retail sale of hemp-derived edible products. The Office of Cannabis Management reopened LPHE licensing applications on April 1, 2026, accepting applications for retailers, manufacturers, and wholesalers on an ongoing rolling basis per [OCM LPHE Licensing](https://fox9.com/news/minnesota-cannabis-lower-potency-hemp-edible-license-applications-reopened-april-2026). 

Important: Starting November 12, 2026, a federal spending bill bans hemp-derived products containing more than 0.4 mg of total THC per container (effective December 11, 2026). This may significantly impact the LPHE market. Current LPHE products may be sold through March 31, 2026, with a transition period ending April 1, 2026.

### Seeds and Genetic Material

Purchasing cannabis seeds is legal in Minnesota for adults 21 and older. Seeds may be purchased in-state and through mail order. No per-person seed purchase limits exist under state law per [Minnesota cannabis seed regulations](https://www.mda.state.mn.us/cannabis-seed).

**Licensed Minnesota Seed Sources:**
- **Natural Harvest** (Edina): Licensed seed labeler and distributor; partners with Barney's Farm, Fast Buds, Dutch Passion, Sweet Seeds, Ace Seeds, and Humboldt Seed Co.; mail-order available per [Natural Harvest cannabis seeds](https://minnesotagrown.com/member/natural-harvest)
- **Limited Run Seeds LLC** (Spicer): Licensed craft cannabis seed breeder specializing in small-batch, high-quality seeds blending classic cultivars with innovative genetics per [Limited Run Seeds](https://minnesotagrown.com/member/limited-run-seeds-llc/)
- **Lucky Duck Genetics LLC** (Cambridge area): Produces and sells adult-use cannabis seeds with online ordering and local delivery

**Cannabis Clones:**
No retail network for cannabis clones in Minnesota was identified during this research. Licensed cultivators obtain plant materials from authorized sources as of December 1, 2025 per state regulation, but retail clone sales are not widely advertised or available. Clones may be possible to acquire through direct cultivator relationships or within gifting limits (up to 8 grams fresh weight equivalent, if applicable).

**Cultivar Sourcing:**
Licensed cultivators in Minnesota include Grown Rogue International (Fridley facility; first harvest expected Q4 2026; products reach consumers Q1 2027) and two existing cultivators whose names are not fully enumerated in available sources. Customers source cultivars indirectly through dispensary product selections rather than direct grower access.

## Compliance Constraints for the Grow System

Machine-checkable constraints for a compliant Minnesota home cannabis grow:

- `living_plants_per_residence <= 8`
- `mature_flowering_plants_per_residence <= 4`
- `grow_space_enclosed: true` (physical enclosure required)
- `grow_space_locked: true` (locking mechanism required)
- `grow_space_not_visible_from_public: true` (attestation required)
- `grow_location == primary_residence_of_adult_21_plus`
- `possession_at_residence_oz <= 32` (2 pounds = 32 oz)
- `possession_in_public_oz <= 2`
- `possession_concentrate_grams <= 8`
- `edibles_thc_milligrams <= 800`
- `volatile_solvent_extraction: false` (butane, propane, hexane prohibited)
- `sale_prohibited: true` (unlicensed sale is graded by § 152.0264 from a petty misdemeanor to a felony)
- `gifting_compensation == null` (must be no remuneration)
- `gifting_only_to_adults_21_plus: true`
- `gifting_amount_oz <= 2`
- `gifting_concentrate_grams <= 8`
- `gifting_edibles_thc_milligrams <= 800`
- `multifamily_housing_smoking_vaping: false` (banned statewide as of March 1, 2025, except medical patients)
- `landlord_prohibition_on_possession: false` (landlord cannot ban legal possession)
- `odor_nuisance_injunction_risk: true` (private nuisance under § 561.01: injunction and damages)
- `local_ordinance_prohibition_on_home_grow: false` (cities cannot ban home cultivation)
