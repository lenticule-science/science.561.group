The act of moving a plant from one growing location to another, typically
from an indoor container to an outdoor garden bed. Transplanting is
stressful for plants because roots are disturbed and environmental
conditions change abruptly. Hardening off (gradually exposing seedlings
to outdoor conditions over a week) reduces transplant shock.
