A measure of how acidic or alkaline soil is, on a scale from 0 to 14.
A pH of 7 is neutral; below 7 is acidic; above 7 is alkaline. Soil pH
controls which nutrients are available to plant roots. Most garden plants
grow best in slightly acidic to neutral soil (pH 6.0–7.0). Soil pH is
tested with an inexpensive home kit or through a cooperative extension
service.
