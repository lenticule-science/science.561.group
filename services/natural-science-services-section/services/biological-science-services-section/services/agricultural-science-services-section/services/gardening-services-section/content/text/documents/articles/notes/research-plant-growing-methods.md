- Identify the plant's species and cultivar, and record its requirements for light, water, soil, temperature, and spacing.
- Determine the plant's growth habit, propagation method, and timing (seed start date, transplant date, days to maturity).
- Assess the growing site: sun exposure, soil type and pH, drainage, water access, and microclimate factors.
- Compare plant requirements against site conditions and identify gaps.
- Consult cooperative extension guides, seed catalogs, and local growers for regionally specific advice.
- Select the growing method (direct sow, transplant, raised bed, container, or indoor) that best closes the gaps between plant needs and site conditions.
