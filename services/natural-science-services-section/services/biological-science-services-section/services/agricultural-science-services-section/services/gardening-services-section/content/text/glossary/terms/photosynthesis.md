The process by which plants convert light energy, water, and carbon
dioxide into sugars and oxygen. It occurs primarily in the leaves and is
the foundation of all plant growth. Without adequate light for
photosynthesis, a plant cannot produce enough energy to grow, flower, or
fruit.
