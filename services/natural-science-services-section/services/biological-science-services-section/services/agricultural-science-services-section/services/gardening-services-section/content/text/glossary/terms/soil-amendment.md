Any material added to soil to change its properties. Common amendments
include compost (adds organic matter and nutrients), lime (raises pH),
sulfur (lowers pH), sand (improves drainage in clay), and peat moss or
coconut coir (improves water retention in sandy soil). Amendments are
chosen based on the gap between a soil's current condition and what the
intended plant needs.
