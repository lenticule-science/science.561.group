***Vapor pressure deficit*** (VPD) is the difference, in kilopascals, between the saturation vapor pressure of water at a leaf's temperature and the actual vapor pressure of the surrounding air. It is the drying power of the air as the leaf meets it, and it drives transpiration: a low VPD slows water and nutrient flow through the plant and wets surfaces where mold starts; a high VPD closes stomata and stresses the plant. Controlled-environment growers steer VPD rather than relative humidity alone, because the same humidity means different VPDs at different temperatures. Where an infrared leaf-temperature reading exists it is used in place of air temperature, since a leaf under strong light runs warmer or cooler than the air. Indoor cannabis bands rise from about 0.6-1.1 kPa for seedlings to 1.2-1.7 kPa late in flower; see the [note on indoor cannabis cultivation](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-on-indoor-cannabis-cultivation.md).
