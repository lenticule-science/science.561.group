***Crop steering*** is irrigation (and climate) scheduled to push a plant toward vegetative growth or toward generative growth (flowers and fruit) by the size of the daily substrate dryback. A lit day has three phases: P1, a ramp of irrigation shots after lights-on up to the substrate's peak water content; P2, maintenance shots holding water content above a floor; P3, no irrigation, timed so the projected dryback reaches its target by the next lights-on. A vegetative strategy uses a small dryback (about 12 % in coco) and small shots; a generative strategy uses a large dryback (about 25 %), larger shots and no irrigation late in the day. A shot is a percentage of the substrate volume. Substrate EC climbing above its band is the sign to flush.
