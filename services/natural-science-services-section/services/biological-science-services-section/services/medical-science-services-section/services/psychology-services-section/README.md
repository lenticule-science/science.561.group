# Psychology Services Section

The Psychology Services Section maintains knowledge for the study of
mind and behavior: affect and its dimensions, motivation and its
direction, attachment, defense, and the constructs clinical work
depends on but does not itself define.

It is the constructs' home, and the clinical sibling of
[Psychiatry](../psychiatry-services-section/README.md), which holds the
diagnoses, the rating scales, and the treatment response rates. Where
the two touch — irritability as a trait against irritability as a
diagnostic criterion, anhedonia as a construct against anhedonia as a
symptom item — the construct is defined here and the clinical entity is
defined there.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
