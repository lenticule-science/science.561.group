# Fitness Services Section

This is the cross-domain fitness section of shared health services. It holds
practices whose cultivation cannot be faithfully reduced to physical, mental,
or spiritual fitness alone. Domain-specific fitness remains within its named
health domain.

## Services

- `martial-arts-services-section` — martial practices integrating more than one
  health domain.
