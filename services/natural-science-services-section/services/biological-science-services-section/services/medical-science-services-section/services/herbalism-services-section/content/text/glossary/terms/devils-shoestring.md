*devils shoestring* is recorded in this section's herbal materia medica as follows. Its properties and uses are the herbal tradition's account, drawn from the sources listed; they are not clinical evidence, and the warnings should be read before any use.

## Properties

- astringent
- antispasmodic
- gynecological support
- relaxant

## Dosage forms

- tea: Make a medicinal tea by placing 1 teaspoon of the dried root bark in 1 cup of hot water, covering and steeping for 10 to 15 minutes. Drink 1 cup up to 3 times daily to relieve menstrual cramps.

## Warnings

- energetics

## Notes

- The bark is used medicinally to treat menstrual cramps, prevent miscarriages, and to boost fertility.
