*Arctium lappa*, known as burdock, is recorded in this section's herbal materia medica as follows. Its properties and uses are the herbal tradition's account, drawn from the sources listed; they are not clinical evidence, and the warnings should be read before any use.

## Energetics

- cooling
- moistening
- nourishing

## Properties

- alterative
- anticancer
- bitter
- cholagogue
- diuretic
- hepatic
- lymphatic
- mast cell stabilizer

## Dosage forms

- standard decoction
- 1/2-1 cup 2-3 times daily
- tincture
- fresh root or seeds (1;2, 95% alcohol); dried root or seeds (1:5, 50% alcohol); 1-5 ml (0.2 tsp.) 3 times daily; roots for chronic issues, seeds for acute
- glycerite
- dried root (1;5); 2-10 ml (0.4-2 tsp.) 3 times daily
- capsule
- 1,000-3,000 mg, 1-2 times daily

## Warnings

- No known warnings

## Notes

- stimulates bile
- strengthens the liver
- leaves may be applied as a poultice to infected sores
- strong decoction of the root can be used in baths for itching
