*Viburnum opulus*, known as cramp bark, is recorded in this section's herbal materia medica as follows. Its properties and uses are the herbal tradition's account, drawn from the sources listed; they are not clinical evidence, and the warnings should be read before any use.

## Energetics

- relaxing

## Properties

- anti-abortive
- antispasmodic

## Dosage forms

- **standard decoction:**
  - 3-4 ounces 3 times daily
- **tincture:**
  - dried bark (1:5, 50% alcohol); 1-5 ml (0.2-1 tsp.) 1-4 times daily

## Warnings

- do not take with low blood pressure

## Notes

- uterine tonic, relaxes and tones uterus
- ease menstrual cramps
- prevent miscarriage
- problems related to tension, such as backache, angina
