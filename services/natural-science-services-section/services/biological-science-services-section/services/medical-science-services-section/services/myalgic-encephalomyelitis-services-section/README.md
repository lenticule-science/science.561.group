# Myalgic Encephalomyelitis Services Section

The Myalgic Encephalomyelitis Services Section maintains knowledge for
the study of myalgic encephalomyelitis / chronic fatigue syndrome and
the post-exertional malaise that defines it: the neuroendocrine,
autonomic, metabolic, and neuroinflammatory findings that characterize
the illness, the instruments that measure them, and an honest account
of which mechanisms are established and which remain hypothesis.

The section's organizing claim is that the illness is not well described
as an excess or a deficit in any single system. It is described better
as a decoupling — a stress response whose fast neural arm and slow
hormonal arm no longer fund each other, imposing metabolic demand on
cells that cannot meet it, with none of the vascular support or
inflammatory braking that normally ends the episode. That framing is
what makes the section's four upstream dependencies one subject rather
than four:

- **Neuroendocrine** — [Physiology](../../../physiology-services-section/README.md)
  holds the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis, allostasis, and
  allostatic load; [Neurochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/services/neurochemistry-services-section/README.md)
  holds cortisol, the glucocorticoids, and the cascade's upstream
  hormones.
- **Autonomic** — Physiology holds the sympathoadrenal system,
  dysautonomia, and orthostatic intolerance; Neuroscience holds the
  locus coeruleus, neurovascular coupling, and cerebral hypoperfusion.
- **Metabolic** — [Biochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/README.md)
  holds mitochondrial respiration, pyruvate dehydrogenase, and the
  anaerobic threshold.
- **Neuroinflammatory** — [Immunology](../immunology-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/immunology.md)
  holds the cytokines and the glucocorticoid brake;
  [Human Body](../human-body-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/inflammation.md)
  holds inflammation itself; [Pain](../pain-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/central-sensitization.md)
  holds central sensitization.

This section does not restate those carriers. It defines the clinical
entities, names the instruments, and holds the syntheses that cross all
four.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; the
structured literature synthesis behind the claims above lives at
[`content/text/theories/predication-literature-review-mecfs-mechanisms.md`](content/text/theories/predication-literature-review-mecfs-mechanisms.md).

## Thread-mates

Per the five-custody thread, science alone is a fragment. The named
debts for this capability:

- **Engineering** — the corpus bundling that makes this section's
  glossary and notes readable as one body by an external research
  assistant.
- **Operations** — how that bundle is kept current as carriers change.
- **561 Group** — our own running of the research line.
- **Public** — the face, if any, this material is given.
