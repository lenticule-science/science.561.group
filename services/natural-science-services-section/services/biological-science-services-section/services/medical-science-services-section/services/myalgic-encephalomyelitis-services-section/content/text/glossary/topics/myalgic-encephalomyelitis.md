# Myalgic Encephalomyelitis

Myalgic encephalomyelitis, also called chronic fatigue syndrome, is a
chronic multi-system illness within Medicine, defined by a
disproportionate and delayed worsening of symptoms following exertion.
Its study spans neuroendocrine regulation, autonomic control, cellular
energetics, and neuroinflammation, and its characteristic findings are
failures of coordination between those systems rather than failures
within any one of them.
