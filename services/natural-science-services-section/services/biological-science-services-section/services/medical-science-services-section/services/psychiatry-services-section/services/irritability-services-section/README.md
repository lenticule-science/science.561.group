# Irritability Services Section

The Irritability Services Section maintains knowledge for the clinical
study of irritability and anger: anger attacks inside depressive
episodes, irritability as a dimensional trait measured across
diagnoses, and the diagnoses built on aggressive outbursts.

The section exists because irritability sits awkwardly in the manuals.
It is a criterion for some diagnoses and excluded from the criteria of
others; it is the commonest presentation of childhood mood pathology
and among the least reliably diagnosed; and it names both a trait and
an episode without the literature always saying which it means. The
section's job is to keep those apart, and to state which measure
belongs to which claim.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
