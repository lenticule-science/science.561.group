# Psychiatry Services Section

The Psychiatry Services Section maintains knowledge for the clinical
study of mental illness: the syndromes as they are actually defined in
the diagnostic manuals, the instruments that measure them, the
treatments with their measured response rates, and an honest account of
which proposed mechanisms are established, which are hypotheses still
under test, and which are clinical impression that never acquired a
primary study.

It is the clinical sibling of
[Psychology](../psychology-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/psychology.md),
which holds the study of mind and behavior as such. A construct belongs
to psychology; a diagnosis, a rating scale, and a treatment response
rate belong here.

The section's organizing subject, and the reason it was opened, is the
**affective state transition**: the observation that people move between
affective states — a low-arousal, immobile, anhedonic state and a
high-arousal, irritable, mobilized one — abruptly rather than by
gradual drift, and the question of what a psychiatry that takes the
transition itself as its object would be able to say. That question is
what the section's three children divide:

- [Catatonia](services/catatonia-services-section/README.md) — the
  syndrome with two motor poles, retarded and excited, in which the
  transition is most visible and best documented.
- [Mood Disorder](services/mood-disorder-services-section/README.md) —
  mixed states, the switch process, cycling, and the dynamical-systems
  literature that models mood transitions formally.
- [Irritability](services/irritability-services-section/README.md) —
  anger and irritability as clinical objects: anger attacks in
  depression, the dimensional irritability program, and the aggression
  diagnoses.

Upstream, this section does not restate what its dependencies already
rule. [Neuroscience](../../../neuroscience-services-section/README.md)
holds the defensive-behavior circuitry and the arousal systems;
[Neurochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/services/neurochemistry-services-section/README.md)
holds the transmitters; [Physiology](../../../physiology-services-section/README.md)
holds allostasis, the autonomic states, and the
[dorsal vagal state](../../../physiology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dorsal-vagal-state.md);
[Pharmacology](../pharmacology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/pharmacodynamics.md)
holds drug action. This section defines the clinical entities, names the
instruments, and holds the syntheses that cross them.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.

## Thread-mates

Per the five-custody thread, science alone is a fragment. The named
debts for this capability:

- **Engineering** — the corpus bundling that makes this section
  readable as one body by an external research assistant, and the
  citation reconciliation that keeps its references honest.
- **Operations** — how the section's claims are re-checked as the
  literature moves, since several of its central findings are contested
  and one replication can invert them.
- **561 Group** — our own running of the research line.
- **Public** — the face, if any, this material is given. Nothing here
  is clinical advice, and a public face would have to say so first.
