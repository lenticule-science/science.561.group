## What this argues

People report moving between affective states abruptly: from a
low-arousal, immobile, anhedonic condition into a high-arousal,
irritable, mobilized one, fast enough that the change feels like a
switch being thrown rather than a slope being descended. Ten separate
research literatures bear on that description. Each is real, each has
instruments, and not one of them measures the transition itself.

That is the finding this note exists to state. The field has built
careful measures of how low a person is (depression severity scales),
how excited (mania scales), how catatonic (a twelve-sign examination),
how irritable (a seven-item dimensional index), and how much effort they
will spend (a behavioural choice task). It has not built a measure of
how close a person is to flipping, and the studies that could have
observed a flip mostly were not designed to catch one. The gap is
consistent across all ten literatures, which makes it a structural
feature of how affective states are studied rather than an oversight in
any one programme.

What follows names what each literature does license, marks the live
disagreements rather than smoothing them, and states the measurement
this corpus would have to build to say anything more.

## Where the transition is closest to documented

[Catatonia](../../../../../../../../services/catatonia-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/catatonia.md)
comes nearest. It is the one syndrome whose diagnostic criteria admit
both an immobile and an excited presentation, and the co-occurrence data
say plainly that these are not two kinds of patient. In a cohort of
1,456 patients across 2,130 episodes, 196 showed excitement, and 105 of
those — 53.6% — also showed stupor or immobility within the same
episode. The reverse overlap was much smaller: 11.4% of stuporous
patients also showed excitement. The authors describe stupor and
excitement as seemingly opposite states that nonetheless frequently
coexist, and read the pattern as alternation within one syndrome rather
than as two mutually exclusive subtypes. Kahlbaum's original 1874
description already had a cyclic course moving through retarded and
excited phases in the same patients.

Catatonia also has the fastest treatment response in this whole survey,
which is what makes it observable at all: in an open systematic study of
28 patients, 16 of the 21 who completed a full lorazepam trial — 76% —
had resolution of catatonic signs, and four lorazepam failures responded
promptly to electroconvulsive therapy. A syndrome that resolves in days
under a benzodiazepine is a syndrome whose state changes can be watched.

And still: a targeted search of this literature found no primary study
that measures the transition between poles — not its speed, not its
triggers, not a biomarker that precedes it. The
[section's note on the two poles](../../../../../../../../services/catatonia-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-the-two-poles-of-catatonia.md)
carries that null result as its closing finding.

## Where the field's own categories exclude the phenomenon

The mood-disorder literature has a specifier for depressive episodes
carrying excitatory features, and the crux of it is what the specifier
leaves out. The DSM-5
[mixed-features specifier](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mixed-features-specifier.md)
requires three of seven manic or hypomanic symptoms present nearly every
day for the majority of the episode — and explicitly excludes psychomotor
agitation, irritability and distractibility, on the grounds that they are
not specific to bipolarity. Those three exclusions are precisely the
features a switch-like presentation would show. Koukopoulos's competing
[mixed depression](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mixed-depression.md)
construct makes them core rather than excluded, and
[agitated depression](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agitated-depression.md)
is the older entity the argument runs through. The disagreement is not
settled and is not merely nosological: which construct a study uses
determines whether a patient with an irritable depressive episode is
counted as mixed at all.

The switch itself was studied directly once, on research wards in the
early 1970s, and what survived is descriptive: transitions occurring
over minutes to days, with accompanying sleep and REM changes. The
catecholamine mechanism proposed alongside them did not survive.
[Rapid cycling](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/rapid-cycling.md)
has a definition and a literature;
[ultradian cycling](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ultradian-cycling.md)
— switching within a day, which is the timescale the description at the
top of this note implies — is recognized by neither DSM-5 nor ICD-11 and
rests on case series. The
[treatment-emergent affective switch](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/treatment-emergent-affective-switch.md)
literature is where the disagreement is sharpest: randomized-trial
meta-analyses and registry cohorts do not agree on the rate, and the
[note on mixed features and the switch process](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-mixed-features-and-the-switch-process.md)
gives both with their sample sizes rather than picking one.

## Where the formal models are, and what they are not

One programme took transitions as its explicit object. Fitting
dynamical-systems statistics to daily mood time series, it looks for
[critical slowing down](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/critical-slowing-down.md)
— rising autocorrelation and variance as a system approaches a tipping
point — as an
[early-warning signal](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/early-warning-signal.md)
before a depressive transition. The positive results exist and so does a
developed statistical critique of them: false-positive rates,
dependence on sampling frequency, and difficulty replicating the
detection in independent series. The
[note on mood as a dynamical system](../../../../../../../../services/mood-disorder-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-mood-as-a-dynamical-system.md)
keeps both halves.

What matters for this corpus is the category error to avoid. These are
statistics fitted to observed time series. A good fit of a bistable
model to someone's mood diary is not the identification of a bistable
neural mechanism, and no study in this literature localizes one. The
formal work says the shape of the data is compatible with a system that
has two attractors. It does not say what the attractors are made of.

## Where the mechanism is, in animals

The nervous system does have a documented immobility-to-attack switch,
and it is not in the mood literature at all. Defensive behaviour is
organized along a
[threat-imminence continuum](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/threat-imminence-continuum.md),
and the
[periaqueductal gray](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/periaqueductal-gray.md)
implements opposite behavioural outputs in adjacent columns: the
ventrolateral column producing quiescence and immobility, the
dorsolateral producing active flight and defensive attack. When contact
becomes imminent, behaviour switches into
[circa-strike defense](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/circa-strike-defense.md).
There is a
[hypothalamic attack area](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hypothalamic-attack-area.md)
whose stimulation produces attack.

Every load-bearing piece of that is rodent, established by stimulation,
microinjection and lesion. The human work is correlational imaging, and
the clinical
[defense cascade](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/defense-cascade.md)
formulation that maps these circuits onto trauma presentations is stated
by its own authors as a theoretical synthesis extrapolated from animal
data. The
[note on the defense cascade](../../../../../../../../../../../neuroscience-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-the-defense-cascade-and-the-immobility-to-attack-transition.md)
records that not even the animal literature contains a study capturing a
single subject transitioning from freezing into active defense within
one session. The circuitry for a switch is well described. The switch
being thrown has not been recorded.

## What supplies the energy, and what supplies the direction

Two further literatures answer questions the others leave open, and both
answer them partially.

The energy question belongs to
[effort-based decision making](../../../../../../../../../psychology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/effort-based-decision-making.md).
Mesolimbic dopamine gates the exertion of effort and behavioural
activation, not the hedonic response to reward — a rodent-causal finding
routinely misdescribed as being about pleasure, and the reason
[anhedonia](../../../../../../../../../psychology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anhedonia.md)
and motivational anergia have to be kept apart. But the human
effort-task evidence in depression is splitting rather than converging,
and on the specific question this note asks — whether acute arousal
transiently restores activation in an already low-activation state — no
primary study was located that tests it.

The direction question belongs to
[approach motivation](../../../../../../../../../psychology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/approach-motivation.md).
Anger, unlike fear, carries an approach signature rather than a
withdrawal one, which is what makes a mobilized angry state a plausible
exit from an immobile one rather than merely a different kind of
distress. That literature's inferential base is frontal EEG asymmetry,
which has its own well-developed criticism, and
[frustrative nonreward](../../../../../../../../../psychology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/frustrative-nonreward.md)
supplies the mechanism by which a blocked goal energizes the next
response. Whether an angry state actually reduces depressive symptoms or
restores activity was searched for specifically; the direct evidence is
thin.

## What the clinic must rule out first

Before any of the above is invoked for a person, the
[note on precipitants of abrupt affective switching](2026-09-02-note-on-precipitants-of-abrupt-affective-switching.md)
holds the differential: peri-ictal states in temporal lobe epilepsy,
[akathisia](../../../../../../glossary/terms/akathisia.md)
and activation syndromes that present as agitation and get mistaken for
worsening illness,
[premenstrual dysphoric disorder](../../../../../../glossary/terms/premenstrual-dysphoric-disorder.md)
with its experimental ovarian-steroid-withdrawal evidence for abrupt
cyclical onset, thyroid dysfunction and hypoglycemia as reversible
causes, and traumatic brain injury. The
[irritability](../../../../../../../../services/irritability-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/irritability.md)
literature contributes the measurement discipline: it uses one word for
a trait and for an episode, and studies that conflate them are not
measuring the same thing.

## The shared gap, stated as a measurement problem

Across ten literatures the same absence recurs. Catatonia research
watches two poles and does not time the move between them. The switch
process was described on research wards and then studied through
cross-sectional rates. The dynamical-systems programme models
transitions but measures mood, not mechanism. The defense circuitry is
mapped in animals whose transitions were never recorded within a
session. The effort literature has no acute-arousal manipulation applied
to an anergic state. The anger literature has approach motivation but no
demonstration that approach restores activity in depression.

What every one of these is missing is the same instrument: a
within-subject design that identifies or induces a low-activation state,
samples densely enough to catch a transition, and measures something
during the transition rather than before and after it. That is a
tractable study. Its absence, not any of the individual findings, is the
most useful thing this survey found.

Two consequences for this corpus follow. First, no claim in the sections
below should be read as explaining a switch; each explains a state, or a
circuit, or a rate. Second, the founder-authored house note on
[stability dynamics in cognitive systems](../../../../../../../../../../../human-science-services-section/services/human-cognition-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/10/11/2025-10-11-note-on-stability-dynamics-in-cognitive-systems.md),
which defines affect as the time derivative of a stability reward with
precision modulating the update rate, is a house theory of exactly the
quantity these literatures fail to measure. It is a theory to test
against this evidence, not a finding drawn from it, and the note on mood
as a dynamical system is where that test would start.
