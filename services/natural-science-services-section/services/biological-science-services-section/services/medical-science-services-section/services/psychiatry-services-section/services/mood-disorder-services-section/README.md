# Mood Disorder Services Section

The Mood Disorder Services Section maintains knowledge for the study of
the mood disorders as state-transition illnesses: what a mixed state is
and which manual admits which version of it, what the switch process
was claimed to be and what has held up, how cycling is defined and
where the definitions run out, and how the dynamical-systems literature
models mood transitions formally.

The section's organizing claim is that the mood disorders are described
badly as levels and better as transitions between states — and that the
field's own instruments are built for levels. A depression score
measures how low a person is now; it does not measure how close the
system is to flipping. The literatures gathered here are the ones that
tried to measure the flip: the research-ward switch studies, the mixed
-features specifiers, and the early-warning-signal program that fits
critical-slowing-down statistics to daily mood time series.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
