# Catatonia Services Section

The Catatonia Services Section maintains knowledge for the study of
catatonia: a psychomotor syndrome with two poles — a retarded pole of
immobility, mutism, and stupor, and an excited pole of purposeless
hyperkinetic activity — that occurs across psychiatric, neurological,
and general-medical illness rather than within any single diagnosis.

The section exists because catatonia is where the affective state
transition is least deniable. The same patient can be stuporous and,
within an illness episode, hyperkinetic; the syndrome's own diagnostic
criteria admit both poles; and its treatments act quickly enough that
the transition can be watched. What the section must also carry
honestly is how little the mechanism literature establishes about the
transition itself.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
