This section reads the adaptive immune system as a distributed classification system that carries its own evidence for its decisions, rather than as a lookup table of friend/foe labels. The claim is not original to this note — it is developed at length in the founding theory note for this section, ["Immunity as Belief Board"](../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/21/2026-08-21-note-on-immunity-as-belief-board.md), written 2026-08-21 as a draft for owner review and slated to relocate into this section once it was stood up. That note is the place to read the argument itself — self/non-self as settled charge, antibodies as witnessed refutations, tolerance as residual-uncertainty classification, autoimmunity as false conflict-naming, and the field's own history (Jerne, Matzinger) read as its own obsmetrological turns, plus the falsifiers attached to the claim. This note does not restate that argument; it orients the section's glossary terms around it and links out to prior vault material the argument draws on.

## What the founding note claims, and where each claim now has a glossary term

- **Self/non-self as settled charge** — thymic negative selection reaching a fixed classification before deployment. See [self/non-self discrimination](../../../glossary/terms/self-non-self.md).
- **Antibodies as witnessed refutations** — affinity maturation in germinal centers read as an evidence-accumulation process; somatic hypermutation as variation, antigen contact as witnessing. See [antibody](../../../glossary/terms/antibody.md).
- **Tolerance as residual-uncertainty classification** — commensal and food antigens correctly filed as non-actionable despite nonzero detectable conflict. See [immunological tolerance](../../../glossary/terms/immunological-tolerance.md).
- **Autoimmunity as false conflict-naming** — the classifier promoting residual uncertainty to a named refutation against the body's own settled sections. See [autoimmunity](../../../glossary/terms/autoimmunity.md).
- **Jerne's idiotypic networks as arrows on arrows** — antibodies recognizing antibodies, a self-referential regulatory structure Nobel-recognized in 1984 and then marginalized, partly for lacking a formal mathematics. See [idiotypic network theory](../../../glossary/terms/idiotypic-network.md).
- **Matzinger's danger model as witnessed damage** — immunity keyed to damage signals (provenance) rather than to foreignness alone (category). See [danger model](../../../glossary/terms/danger-model.md).

Each glossary term above gives the standard immunological account first, in full, on its own terms, and only then adds a clearly marked section connecting it to the founding note's framing — the science does not depend on the framing being correct, and the framing is presented throughout as a hypothesis awaiting the section owner's review, not as settled fact folded into the biology.

## TCM's wei qi as a third witness tradition

The founding note proposes that "TCM's wei qi belongs in the collection as a third witness tradition, engaged on its own terms" — alongside the biomedical self/non-self and danger-model traditions. Traditional Chinese Medicine's [wei qi](../../../../../../traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/wei-qi.md) (卫气, "defensive qi") is TCM's own account of a surface-level protective system: it circulates between skin and muscle, governs pore opening and closing, and is understood to fail in ways that produce recurrent susceptibility to external pathogenic influence — a functional analog, in TCM's own vocabulary and mechanism, to what biomedicine describes as innate immunity and barrier function. That glossary term was already integrated into this vault's traditional-chinese-medicine-services-section by an earlier session; this note indexes it here as the third tradition rather than duplicating its content, in keeping with the founding note's instruction that it be "engaged on its own terms" rather than absorbed into the belief-board vocabulary by fiat.

## Obsmetrological context

The founding note's reading of Jerne's network theory as "arrows on arrows, Yoneda with lymphocytes" draws on this vault's [obsmetrological](../../../../../../../../../../math-services-section/services/obsmetrological-services-section/apps/pymetrologic/docs/THEORY.md) framework — a formal account of belief, evidence, and classification that the founding note treats as the missing mathematics Jerne's theory needed. That framework is not summarized here; readers who want to evaluate the founding note's strongest claim (that the belief-board vocabulary adds structure beyond existing network immunology, rather than merely re-describing it) will need to read both the framework and the immunology this section documents.
