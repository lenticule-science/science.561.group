Hematology is the study of blood and the physiological processes by which
bleeding is arrested and hemostasis is maintained, including the coagulation
cascade and its regulation, the cellular and molecular changes required for
platelet activation and fibrin formation, and the fibrinolytic clearance of
formed clots.
