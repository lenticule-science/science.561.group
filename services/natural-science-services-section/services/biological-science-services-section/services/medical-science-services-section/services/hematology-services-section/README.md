# Hematology Services Section

The Hematology Services Section maintains knowledge for the study of blood,
haemostasis (the process by which bleeding is arrested), and the
microcirculatory disorders that result when haemostasis fails. The section
holds the biochemistry of the coagulation cascade and the cell-based model
that supersedes it, the fibrinolytic system that clears formed clots, the
platelet and red cell membrane changes that enable coagulation, and an
honest account of which mechanisms are established, which are plausible
candidates, and which remain unverified.

The section's organizing claim is that coagulation is not a soluble cascade
happening in bulk plasma but a surface-localized process: the prothrombinase
complex (factor Xa and factor Va) assembles only on an anionic phospholipid
surface with calcium present. This surface is normally the plasma membrane of
activated platelets, exposed through phosphatidylserine externalization. That
single membrane property — phosphatidylserine availability — is the load-bearing
connection between lipid biology and coagulation biology. Understanding
coagulation therefore requires understanding the membrane-active lipids and the
ATP-dependent processes that maintain membrane asymmetry. This is what makes
lipid biochemistry downstream of haemostasis, not alongside it:

- **Lipid Biochemistry** —
  [Lipid Biochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/services/lipid-biochemistry-services-section/README.md)
  holds phosphatidylserine, membrane-lipid asymmetry, and the ATP-dependent
  flippases and scramblases that regulate externalization.
- **Coagulation** — this section holds the cascade stages, the prothrombinase
  complex's membrane requirement, and the evidence (or lack thereof) for
  microclot formation in disease.
- **Fibrinolysis** — this section holds plasminogen-to-plasmin conversion,
  the fibrinolysis regulators, and what resistance to fibrinolysis means
  mechanistically.
- **Microcirculation** — [Physiology](../../../physiology-services-section/README.md)
  holds capillary anatomy and the rheological determinants of tissue
  perfusion; [Biochemistry](../../../biochemistry-services-section/README.md)
  holds erythrocyte physiology and the determinants of red cell deformability.
- **Immunology** — [Immunology](../immunology-services-section/content/text/glossary/topics/immunology.md)
  holds the cytokine biology and complement responses relevant to vascular
  inflammation.

This section does not restate those carriers. It defines the coagulation
entities, names the measurement instruments and their limitations, and holds
the synthesis connecting the membrane-lipid basis of coagulation to the
microvascular biology that results when coagulation is dysregulated.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
