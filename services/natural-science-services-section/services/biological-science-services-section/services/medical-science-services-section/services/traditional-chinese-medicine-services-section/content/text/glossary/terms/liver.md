The Liver (肝, gān) is one of the five yin organ-function systems of [zang-fu](./zang-fu.md) theory. It stores [blood](./xue.md), releasing it to the muscles in activity and gathering it back at rest. It ensures the free, smooth flow of [qi](./qi.md) (shū xiè) through the whole body [@maciocia2015].

That smooth flow covers the emotions as well as the body. When it fails, the result is [qi stagnation](./qi-stagnation.md): distension and pain that move about, sighing, irritability, and in women premenstrual tension. It is one of the patterns [pattern diagnosis](./pattern-diagnosis.md) finds most often.

The Liver controls the sinews, opens into the eyes, shows in the nails, and houses the hún, the ethereal soul. It belongs to the wood phase of [wu xing](./wu-xing.md) and is paired with the Gall Bladder.
