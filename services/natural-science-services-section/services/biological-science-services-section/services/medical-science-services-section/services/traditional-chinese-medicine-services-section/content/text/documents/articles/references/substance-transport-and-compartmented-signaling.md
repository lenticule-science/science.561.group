This is the citation record behind "Marrow, Spleen, and the Transport of Substances" (2026-08-26). Classical citations were checked against multiple Chinese-language editions accessible via web search in the session that produced the note; chapter and section numbers are given so a reader can verify against a scholarly print translation directly. Modern citations were checked the same way. Solidity is stated plainly for each entry, and several are flagged where the note's argument depends on a caveat rather than a clean result.

## 1. Classical sources — Spleen, transformation, and blood

**Huang Di Nei Jing, Su Wen, Chapter 8, 靈蘭秘典論 (Ling Lan Mi Dian Lun).** Gives the "official metaphor" description of the twelve organ systems, including "脾胃者，倉廩之官，五味出焉" (the Spleen-Stomach is the Granary Official, from which the five flavors emerge). Solid as a foundational, frequently cited passage establishing the Spleen-Stomach's digestive-nutritive role; the "official" metaphors are widely reproduced across TCM textbooks essentially unchanged since antiquity.

**Huang Di Nei Jing, Su Wen, Chapter 21, 經脈別論 (Jing Mai Bie Lun).** Contains the specific physiological sequence underlying 運化 (transformation and transportation): "飲入於胃，游溢精氣，上輸於脾，脾氣散精，上歸於肺" (drink enters the stomach, its essential qi overflows and is transmitted upward to the Spleen; the Spleen's qi disperses that essence upward to the Lung). Solid — this passage is the standard textbook citation for the mechanism of Spleen transformation-and-transportation, quoted essentially verbatim in modern TCM physiology texts.

**Li Dongyuan (李東垣), *Pi Wei Lun* (《脾胃論》, *Treatise on the Spleen and Stomach*, Jin-Yuan dynasty, 13th century).** Source of the formula "脾胃為後天之本，氣血生化之源" (the Spleen-Stomach is the root of postnatal life, the source of Qi and Blood's engenderment and transformation) and the related doctrine "脾胃內傷，百病由生" (internal injury to the Spleen-Stomach is the source of the hundred diseases). Solid as the foundational text of the "Earth-Supplementing School" (補土派); Li Dongyuan is one of the Jin-Yuan Four Great Masters and this text is uncontroversially the origin of the "postnatal root" formula as commonly quoted, though the aphorism's precise wording has been paraphrased and popularized across many later textbooks rather than surviving as a single verbatim classical sentence — treat the exact phrasing as a well-established paraphrase of Li Dongyuan's doctrine rather than a single located quotation.

**Xue Ji (薛己), case records (*Xue Shi Yi An*, 《薛氏醫案》, Ming dynasty, 16th century).** Source of the clear articulation of 脾統血: "心主血，肝藏血，脾能統攝於血" (the Heart governs Blood, the Liver stores Blood, the Spleen is able to hold and govern Blood). Solid as the earliest commonly cited clear statement of this specific division of labor among the three organs regarding Blood; later Qing-dynasty sources (e.g., Shen Mingzong's commentary on the *Jin Gui Yao Lue*) restate and extend the same doctrine, indicating it became settled consensus rather than one physician's idiosyncratic claim.

**Nan Jing (《難經》), Difficulty 42 (四十二難).** Gives physical dimensions and description of the abdominal organs, including the Spleen's "散膏半斤" ("half a jin of scattered fat/paste"). Solid as a primary classical anatomical text; the historical interpretation of "散膏" as a description of the pancreas is a well-established position among historians of Chinese medicine, though it is an interpretive reading of a terse classical phrase rather than a claim the *Nan Jing* itself makes explicitly — flagged as interpretation, not verbatim identification.

## 2. Classical sources — Kidney, marrow, and the extraordinary organs

**Huang Di Nei Jing, Su Wen, Chapter 5, 陰陽應象大論 (Yin Yang Ying Xiang Da Lun).** The chapter's Five Phase correspondence tables are the source of both 思傷脾 ("在志為思，思傷脾，怒勝思" — pensiveness injures the Spleen, anger overcomes pensiveness) and the Water-phase generative chain "腎生骨髓" (the Kidney engenders bone and marrow, within "北方生寒，寒生水，水生鹹，鹹生腎，腎生骨髓，髓生肝，腎主耳"). Solid — this is one of the most frequently quoted chapters in the entire *Nei Jing* corpus and the primary textbook citation for both claims; the correspondences are stable across editions.

**Huang Di Nei Jing, Su Wen, Chapter 11, 五藏別論 (Wu Zang Bie Lun).** Source of the 奇恆之腑 (extraordinary/curious organs) category: "腦、髓、骨、脈、膽、女子胞，此六者，地氣之所生也，皆藏於陰而象於地，故藏而不瀉，名曰奇恆之府" (brain, marrow, bone, vessels, gallbladder, and uterus — these six are engendered by earth-qi; they store yin and resemble the earth; they store without discharging; they are named the extraordinary organs). Solid — this is the definitional locus classicus for the category and is quoted essentially unchanged across TCM textbooks and encyclopedic sources.

**Huang Di Nei Jing, Ling Shu (《靈樞》), Chapter 33, 海論 (Hai Lun).** Establishes the doctrine of the Four Seas, including "腦為髓之海，其輸上在於其蓋，下在風府" (the brain is the sea of marrow; its access points are above at the crown and below at Fengfu) and the clinical consequences of marrow-sea deficiency (dizziness, tinnitus, aching legs, blurred vision, lethargy). Solid — foundational locus for treating brain and spinal cord as continuous with 髓 (marrow) in a single category, a category modern biomedicine keeps anatomically and functionally separate from hematopoietic bone marrow.

## 3. Classical sources — Seven Affects and Triple Burner

**Chen Yan (陳言/陳無擇), *San Yin Ji Yi Bing Zheng Fang Lun* (《三因極一病證方論》, c. 1174 CE, Song dynasty).** First systematic medical documentation of the Seven Affects (七情: 喜、怒、憂、思、悲、恐、驚 — joy, anger, worry, pensiveness, grief, fear, fright) as the internal-cause (內因) category within a tripartite etiology (external, internal, neither-internal-nor-external). Solid — widely credited as the historical origin of the specific "seven affects" enumeration and its systematization as a formal diagnostic category, distinguishing it from earlier, less formalized emotion-organ associations already present in the *Nei Jing*.

**Nan Jing, Difficulty 31 (三十一難).** Defines the Triple Burner (San Jiao / 三焦) functionally rather than structurally: "三焦者，水穀之道路，氣之所終始也" (the Triple Burner is the pathway of water and grain, the place where qi begins and ends), with upper, middle, and lower divisions given distinct functions (upper: intake without outflow; middle: ripening/decomposing water and grain; lower: separating clear from turbid, outflow without intake). Solid — this is the primary classical citation for treating San Jiao as "named but without fixed form" (有名無形), a phrase associated with this doctrine in later commentary though not the *Nan Jing*'s own exact wording in Difficulty 31 itself.

## 4. Classical anatomical history — Spleen/pancreas

**Wang Qingren (王清任), *Yi Lin Gai Cuo* (《醫林改錯》, 1830).** Direct-dissection-based anatomical corrections to prior Chinese medical anatomy, including a description of the pancreas (termed 總提), its duct (珑管), and its relations to the bile duct and liver ducts, along with corrections to vascular anatomy (arterial/venous distinction) and other structures. Solid as a primary historical text and a significant episode in the history of Chinese medical anatomy; used in this note's argument to establish that classical Chinese medicine possessed and pursued direct anatomical knowledge of the pancreas as a structure distinct from the spleen, even though the functional category 脾 continued to span both organs' territory in clinical practice.

**Historical/philological scholarship on 脾-脏胰 (Spleen-pancreas) correspondence.** Multiple modern historians of Chinese medicine (synthesized via secondary Chinese-language sources reviewed for this note, not read here in primary form) argue that classical descriptions of Spleen shape and substance — Wang Bing's Tang-dynasty commentary describing it as horseshoe-shaped, other texts describing it as sickle- or tongue-shaped, and the *Nan Jing*'s "散膏半斤" — are more consistent with the pancreas than the anatomical spleen, and that the functional category 脾 in practice covers what biomedicine later split into spleen and pancreas. Moderately solid as a historical consensus position reported consistently across the secondary sources consulted, but this note has not verified each primary citation (Wang Bing's commentary, the *Lei Jing Tu Yi*, etc.) directly against a critical edition — treat the specific attributions here as a well-supported historical claim rather than independently re-verified against primary manuscripts.

## 5. Modern sources — sympathetic-hematopoietic axis

**McKim, D. B., Yin, W., Wang, Y., Cole, S. W., Godbout, J. P., & Sheridan, J. F. (2018). Social stress mobilizes hematopoietic stem cells to establish persistent splenic myelopoiesis. *Cell Reports*, 25(9), 2552–2562.** Establishes that repeated social defeat in mice mobilizes HSPCs from bone marrow into the spleen, where they engraft and continue producing monocytes, neutrophils, and erythrocytes for at least 24 days after stressor cessation, and that β-adrenergic agonism (isoprenaline) alone reproduces the splenic mobilization. Solid — the central empirical finding this note is built around; a well-controlled mouse study with pharmacological (β-adrenergic agonist) sufficiency evidence, not merely correlational stress-exposure data.

**Heidt, T., Sager, H. B., Courties, G., Dutta, P., et al. (2014). Chronic variable stress activates hematopoietic stem cells. *Nature Medicine*, 20(7), 754–758.** Establishes the β3-adrenergic-receptor → niche-cell CXCL12 suppression → HSPC release mechanism, with β3-adrenergic-receptor-knockout mice showing a blunted response. Solid — the cleanest causal (knockout-based) demonstration in this literature, per the companion biochemistry note's assessment.

**Powell, N. D., Sloan, E. K., Bailey, M. T., Arevalo, J. M. G., et al. (2013). Social stress up-regulates inflammatory gene expression in the leukocyte transcriptome via β-adrenergic induction of myelopoiesis. *PNAS*, 110(41), 16574–16579.** Closes the loop between human CTRA transcriptomic findings and mouse bone-marrow hematopoiesis findings, showing pharmacological blockade (β-adrenergic antagonists, GM-CSF antagonism) prevents the myeloid bias. Solid, mechanistically load-bearing.

**Sloan, E. K., Capitanio, J. P., Tarara, R. P., Mendoza, S. P., Mason, W. A., & Cole, S. W. (2007). Social stress enhances sympathetic innervation of primate lymph nodes: mechanisms and implications for viral pathogenesis. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 27(33), 8857–8865.** Establishes structural (axonal density) remodeling of sympathetic innervation in lymphoid tissue under chronic social subordination in macaques, on a slower timescale than hormonal signaling. Solid, primate data (stronger translational relevance than the mouse-only findings above), though the specific claim of increased anatomical fiber density is a more labor-intensive and less-replicated type of finding than a circulating-hormone measurement.

**Cole, S. W., Hawkley, L. C., Arevalo, J. M., Sung, C. Y., Rose, R. M., & Cacioppo, J. T. (2007). Social regulation of gene expression in human leukocytes. *Genome Biology*, 8(9), R189.** Founding characterization of the Conserved Transcriptional Response to Adversity (CTRA) in chronically lonely humans. Solid, foundational, widely replicated at the level of the general inflammatory-gene-up/antiviral-gene-down pattern.

**Cole, S. W., et al. (2015). Myeloid differentiation architecture of leukocyte transcriptome dynamics in perceived social isolation. *PNAS*, 112(49), 15142–15147.** Shows the human CTRA signal is substantially a population-mixture phenomenon (expansion of classical monocytes) rather than purely per-cell reprogramming, with matching macaque mechanistic data (CTRA activation, GC desensitization, impaired antiviral defense against experimental SIV). Solid and methodologically important — this is the paper establishing that "CTRA up-regulation" needs to be read as a cell-composition claim as much as a transcriptional one, which is directly relevant to how any human analogue of the mouse splenic-depot finding should be measured.

## 6. Modern sources — compartmented signaling

**Rose-John, S., et al. (2014). Trans-signaling is a dominant mechanism for the pathogenic actions of interleukin-6 in the brain. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 34(7), 2503–2512.** Establishes, using sgp130Fc as a trans-signaling-selective blocking reagent, that IL-6's pathogenic CNS effects (blood-brain-barrier disruption, neuroinflammatory angiogenesis) depend substantially on trans-signaling (IL-6 bound to soluble IL-6Rα acting on gp130-expressing cells lacking membrane IL-6Rα) rather than classic signaling (IL-6 acting on cells expressing membrane IL-6Rα directly). Solid — this is the paper this note's central "same molecule, opposite valence by compartment" example rests on, and it uses a genuinely selective pharmacological tool (sgp130Fc) rather than a correlational biomarker distinction.

**Kelly, K. M., Smith, J. A., & Mezuk, B. (2021). Depression and interleukin-6 signaling: A Mendelian Randomization study. *Brain, Behavior, and Immunity*, 95, 106–114.** Human genetic evidence bearing on IL-6 pathway involvement in depression risk, including the counterintuitive divergence between genetically predicted IL-6 pathway activity and genetically predicted CRP (a downstream classic-signaling marker) with respect to depression risk. Moderately solid — real causal genetic evidence, but the field itself (per this paper and related work) has not yet resolved which signaling arm (classic vs. trans) is doing the causal work using human genetics alone; flagged as an open question rather than a settled mechanism, consistent with the companion biochemistry note's treatment of the same literature.

## 7. Modern sources — extracellular vesicles (background, not independently re-verified in depth for this note)

**General extracellular vesicle / exosome biology (background reference, not a single paper).** The characterization of exosomes and microvesicles as lipid-bilayer-enclosed carriers of protein, RNA, and lipid cargo, addressed to specific recipient cells via surface marker composition, is now a large and mature field with its own extensive primary literature (biogenesis via the ESCRT pathway and alternative routes, uptake mechanisms, and disease-specific cargo signatures). This note cites the phenomenon at a general, textbook level of description as a third example of addressed biological transport, alongside CXCL12 gradients and IL-6 signaling-mode switching; it does not rest on any single primary paper and should be treated as well-established general cell biology rather than a specific claim requiring its own citation-level verification here.
