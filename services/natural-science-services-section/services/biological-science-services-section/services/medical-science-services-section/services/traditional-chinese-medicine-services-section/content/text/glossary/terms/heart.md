The Heart (心, xīn) is one of the five yin organ-function systems of [zang-fu](./zang-fu.md) theory, called the ruler among them. It governs [blood](./xue.md) and the vessels: it moves blood through them, and the pulse and the complexion show how well it does so [@maciocia2015].

It houses [shen](./shen.md), the mind and spirit. Clear thought, settled sleep and a fitting emotional life are read as the Heart's shen at rest; palpitations with anxiety, insomnia and disturbed dreams are read as shen unsettled. It opens into the tongue, shows in the face, belongs to the fire phase of [wu xing](./wu-xing.md), and is paired with the Small Intestine.

Practices that settle shen, among them the still forms of [qigong](./qigong.md), are traditionally said to nourish the Heart.
