The Kidney (腎, shèn) is one of the five yin organ-function systems of [zang-fu](./zang-fu.md) theory. It stores [jing](./jing.md), both the prenatal essence received at conception and the postnatal essence refined by the [Spleen](./spleen.md). Through jing it governs birth, growth, maturation, reproduction and the decline of ageing [@maciocia2015].

Kidney yin and Kidney yang are the root of the yin and yang of every other organ. Much long-standing illness is therefore read as ending in Kidney deficiency, and the conservation of jing, a traditional aim of [qigong](./qigong.md) and [neidan](./neidan.md), is conservation of the Kidney.

It produces marrow, which fills the brain and the bones. It governs water, and it "grasps" the qi the [Lung](./lung.md) sends down, which is why chronic breathlessness can be read as a Kidney pattern. It opens into the ears, shows in the head hair, houses the zhì (will), belongs to the water phase of [wu xing](./wu-xing.md), and is paired with the Bladder.
