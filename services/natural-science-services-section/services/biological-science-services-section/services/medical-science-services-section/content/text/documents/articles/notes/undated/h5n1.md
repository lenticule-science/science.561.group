## History
### 2024
#### 2024-04
- [[2024-04-30]]
  - Preliminary [[autopsy]] results have [been released](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/30/7/24-0508_article) for two [[Felis catus|cats]] in [[Texas, United States]] who were infected with [[H5N1]]. The results indicate no immune response in the [[gastrointestinal tract]], but did indicate immune response in [[lung|lungs]], [[eye|eyes]], and [[brain|brains]]. This may imply that H5N1 may be an [[airborne pathogen]].
