A five-step appraisal sequence for evaluating any FOAM post, thread, or episode, covering claim clarity, evidence chain, context fit, risk, and action threshold

Learning objectives

- Source Appraisal for Free Open-Access Medicine (FOAM)

Use this sequence when evaluating any Free Open-Access Medicine (FOAM) post, thread, or episode.

## Appraisal sequence

- Claim clarity: Is the clinical claim explicit and testable?

- Evidence chain: Are primary studies, reviews, or guidelines linked?

- Context fit: Population, setting, and resources match your context?

- Risk check: What harms follow if this claim is wrong?

- Action threshold: Is this for learning, protocol discussion, or immediate bedside action?

## Red flags

- Strong recommendations without linked evidence.

- Overgeneralization from single cases.

- Novel technique with no safety boundary language.

- Advice that conflicts with local protocol without explanation.
