## What this argues

This is the paradigm of medicine the house works in. It is a paradigm at
the level of Western biomedicine or Traditional Chinese Medicine — a
standing answer to what a body is, what illness is, what counts as
evidence, and what treatment is for — and it is derived from
Relationality, the house's own metaphysics, rather than assembled from
clinical convenience. The founder named it three ways on 2026-09-02, and
those three names are one thing seen from three sides: **holocellular**
(the whole organism at the cell's scale), **somatic biochemical** (the
body read through its chemistry), and the **biochemical manifold
coordinate system** (the geometry that chemistry is read in). This note
states the third — the coordinate system — because it is the part that
does work.

The argument in one paragraph. Chemicals are the objects of the
coordinate system and their transformations are its morphisms; a
biomechanism is a diagram of those morphisms; a body system is a *chart*,
read by its own instruments, and a person's physiology at a time is an
assignment of local readings to charts. Concentrations are coordinates on
a chart. What Western nosology looks for — a value outside its reference
range — is a fault visible **within** one chart. The pathology this
paradigm is built to see lives instead in the **transition functions**
between charts: the relations that are supposed to make two systems' local
readings agree about the ground they share. Health, on this account, is
the existence of a global section over the charts. Illness of the kind
that this paradigm sees first is a non-vanishing obstruction whose
restrictions to every single chart are individually normal.

That claim is a hypothesis with a defined measurement, not a finding. Its
mathematical spine is proved (in Lean, for the settling and the entropy
identification), partly implemented (in the house's own `metrologic`
modules), partly only documented, and — where it matters most — open: no
computation of an obstruction class over real physiological data exists
anywhere, in this house or outside it. Every section below marks which of
those four it is standing on, and every empirical claim carries its
evidence status in plain words.

## The unit of health is the relation, not the part

The paradigm's first commitment is that the unit of health is a relation,
not a part. A body is not a bag of organs each of which can be well or
sick; it is a site of charts that must agree with each other on their
overlaps, and the charts do not stop at the skin.

- **Cellular and systemic charts.** The endocrine, autonomic, metabolic,
  and immune systems each carry their own instruments and their own
  reference ranges. They are separate charts because they are separately
  measured, not because the body separates them.
- **The social chart is glued to the immune chart.** This is not an
  analogy. Cole and colleagues' leukocyte transcriptome work found chronic
  social adversity reorganizing gene expression toward pro-inflammatory
  transcripts and away from type-I interferon response [@cole2007], and
  Miller and colleagues found blunted glucocorticoid-response-element
  transcription alongside heightened NF-κB signalling in chronic
  caregivers *whose circulating cortisol was normal* [@miller2008] — a
  chart reading normal while the relation it participates in is deranged.
  Cohen and colleagues showed the same receptor-level insensitivity
  predicting inflammatory response to experimental viral challenge
  [@cohen2012]. **Well-supported, replicated across several groups;
  observational in humans.** The house's own reading of this machinery is
  in the [biochemistry cytokine-transcriptomics note](../../../../../../../../../biochemistry-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/26/2026-08-26-note-on-the-cytokine-transcriptomic-machinery-of-social-standing.md).
- **The environmental chart taxes the endocrine chart by prediction.**
  Allostasis is regulation by anticipation rather than by defence of a
  setpoint [@sterling2012; @schulkin2019]; what the environment is
  expected to demand is already a physiological quantity. **Established as
  a framework; contested as a measurement** — see the [allostatic-load
  term](../../../../../../../../../physiology-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/allostatic-load.md).
- **Communitas and immunitas are a chart boundary.** Esposito's pair names
  exactly the seam this paradigm treats as first-class: protection as
  common obligation versus protection as private exemption. The house
  carriers are [communitas](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/communitas.md) and

names is a transition function that has stopped being an isomorphism while
both charts still read in range.

## Cortisol is a transition function

Cortisol is the paradigm's worked example because it is not a coordinate
on one chart. It is the datum on three overlaps at once, and it does three
different jobs there.

**It funds the response.** Glucocorticoids drive hepatic gluconeogenesis
and mobilize substrate, so the demand the fast arm imposes can be paid
for [@kuo2015; @rui2014]. **Established**: glucocorticoid regulation of
hepatic glucose output is textbook endocrinology with mechanism worked out
at the transcriptional level.

**It permits the response to work.** Munck, Guyre and Holbrook's 1984
framework and Sapolsky, Romero and Munck's 2000 review are the classical
statements that glucocorticoids act *permissively* — enabling other
mediators' actions rather than producing the effect themselves — and the
2000 review's explicit project is to sort glucocorticoid actions into
permissive, suppressive, stimulatory, and preparative classes rather than
treating them as one [@munck1984; @sapolsky2000]. **Established as a
framework.** The specific mechanism at the vascular overlap — glucocorticoid
upregulation of α1-adrenergic responsiveness — has direct molecular
evidence that is narrower than the framework: Sakaue and Hoffman showed
glucocorticoids inducing transcription and expression of the α1B-adrenergic
receptor gene in a smooth-muscle cell line [@sakaue1991]. **Well-supported
mechanism, cell-line evidence**: the permissive relationship between
glucocorticoid tone and catecholamine vascular efficacy is not in dispute
clinically, but the human in-vivo dose-response between cortisol level and
α1-mediated vasoconstriction is not established as a quantity anyone
measures in practice, and this paradigm needs it to be.

**It terminates the response.** Two separate morphisms do this. Cortisol
feeds back on the hypothalamus and pituitary to stop further drive
[@kellerwood1984] — **established**. And the ligand-bound glucocorticoid
receptor transrepresses NF-κB, shutting down pro-inflammatory transcription
[@debosscher2003; @oakley2013] — **established as a molecular mechanism**,
with the receptor-level failure mode (glucocorticoid resistance) documented
functionally in humans under chronic stress [@miller2008; @cohen2012].

So cortisol is the transition function on the metabolic/vascular overlap,
on the metabolic/immune overlap, and on the axis's own feedback loop. That
is why its failure is not one deficit. A single molecule carrying three
transition functions is efficient and fragile at once: one failure removes
the funding, the traction, and the brake together, and none of the three
charts it joins need read abnormally for the joins to be broken.

## The temporal signature: a settling that does not close

Post-exertional malaise arrives 12–72 hours after exertion and lasts days
to weeks [@stussman2020] — **established as a qualitative pattern** across
patients, with no single trigger threshold common to them. Nothing in the
acute cascade runs on that clock: catecholamine surges resolve in minutes,
lactate clears in hours. The decoupling note's answer is that the
inflammatory phase is never terminated, so an ordinary response that does
not stop looks from outside like a delayed catastrophic one.

In this paradigm's vocabulary that is a transfer pass — Δ, the future cone
— that does not reach its fixed point: the settling never closes, so no
global section exists over the interval, and the person lives in the
obstruction rather than passing through it. **This is inference**, and it
should be read as the paradigm supplying a shape for a mechanism, not as
evidence for one. The house's own literature review names the
inflammatory arm the weakest-evidenced of the four systems it rests on.

What would test it is a time course of the *relation* between two channels,
not a level in one. The nearest existing measurement is Ruijgt and
colleagues' observational study: continuous HRV in 121 long-COVID patients
and 21 controls, with HRV remaining suppressed for 24 hours after exercise
around the first ventilatory threshold, and dropping further with more
intense and longer exercise [@ruijgt2026]. **One observational study, not
independently replicated, in a long-COVID rather than a diagnosed ME/CFS
population, and the authors explicitly do not offer it as a diagnostic
threshold.** It is the nearest thing in the literature to a measured
non-closure of a coupling over time, and it is not yet that.

## Damage and border: what this paradigm refuses to discharge

The distal bypass's second step is the one that makes this a medicine and
not a repair shop. Given an obstruction, fix a protected set P of
distinctions declared non-collapsible. The obstruction is **damage** if
some discharge avoids P entirely — and then a least-cost repair is
computable. It is a **border** if every discharge must collapse something
in P — and then the correct output is not a repair but a certificate: a
first-class record that this does not close and must not be made to.

Read as medicine:

- **Western nosology's lesions are the damage cases.** A local section
  genuinely out of range — a fracture, a deficiency, an infection, an
  untreated thyroid — is damage, and the right response is the cheapest
  repair that restores the gluing. This paradigm does not soften that. It
  consumes it.
- **A paradigm that classifies every deviation as damage is eugenic
  medicine.** That is not a rhetorical charge; it is what falls out of the
  formalism. A system with P = ∅ is structurally incapable of representing
  a difference that ought to survive and will classify every border as
  damage and discharge it. The founder's own essay on COVID eugenics names
  the political form of exactly this move — naturalizing mass harm,
  privatizing survival, and stigmatizing collective care
  ([letter](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/letters/letters-to-the-web/on-white-supremacist-covid-eugenicist-queers.md)).
  The house's ruled carriers for the alternative are
  [disability justice](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/disability-justice.md),
  which places the burden on communities to build access as a baseline
  rather than on individuals to request exceptions, and
  [access intimacy](../../../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/access-intimacy.md),
  which names access needs met without demanding they be justified.
- **What this means for treatment goals, plainly.** The goal of treatment
  is a global section, not a normal chart. Where a reading is genuinely out
  of range and repairable without collapsing a protected distinction, repair
  it. Where the person's body works differently and the "deviation" is a
  border — neurodivergence, disability, a pacing-limited energy envelope —
  the output is a certified border and an accommodation built around it,
  never a discharge. Restoring function by overriding the border (graded
  exercise prescribed against post-exertional malaise is the house's worked
  case) is an erasure, and in this paradigm an erasure is priced rather than
  performed silently.

## Evidence is charged, not binary

The paradigm's epistemology is the house's four-valued evidence carrier:
true, false, both, neither. A contested finding is held as **both** rather
than fused into a verdict; an unmeasured relation is **neither** rather
than assumed absent. The `metrologic` bilattice and its `readout` module
implement exactly this classification (`place`, `knowledgeWeight`,
`salience` over the four regimes), and every plain-words evidence label in
this note — established, well-supported, single cohort, contested,
inference — is that charge read out for a human.

The worked case is the two-day CPET. Keller and colleagues (n=22, no
control arm) and Snell and colleagues (n=51 patients, 10 controls) both
found a day-two decline; Mancini and colleagues (n=58 patients, 25
controls, 2026) found none and concluded their data "do not support using
the 2-day CPET protocol to define PEM or disability" [@keller2014;
@snell2013; @mancini2026]. **Contested, held as both.** The paradigm's rule
is that this is carried as a standing contradiction with both sides
addressable, never resolved by picking the larger or the newer study, and
never quietly dropped from a product surface.

Diagnosis, in this paradigm, is a settling over a person's *own* records.
Fix σ is that person's past-stable baseline — what their history has
saturated to; Fix Δ is what their current trajectory transfers forward; the
served surface is the global section where both hold. The unit of evidence
is therefore the n-of-1, and a population is not a pool but a settlement of
many sections joined across seams by certificate exchange. That is a
technical commitment with a governance consequence: federated, consented,
local-first research rather than a pooled database. The house's stated

## Time and energy

Two clocks run in this paradigm. Allostasis supplies the first: regulation
is anticipatory, so the body is already paying for what it predicts
[@sterling2012; @schulkin2019] — **established as a framework, contested as
a composite measurement**. Settling supplies the second: a body under
demand is running a settling, and health is that settling closing on a
glued section. Chronic illness of this kind is a settling that closes on a
wrong section, or does not close at all; recovery is re-settling to a
glued one.

Where the mathematics is proved, coordination failure and thermodynamic
entropy are the same quantity counted in bits: one irreversibly erased bit
per independent cycle (`landauer_eq_coordination_cost`), with the entropy
vanishing exactly when the structure is acyclic
(`landauer_zero_iff_reversible`) [@landauer1961]. If that identification
carries to a body — and this is **inference, with the unit weld listed open
above** — then a coordination illness has a thermodynamic price paid out of
the body's own energy budget, which is the literal content of pacing and of
the Body Budget name the house's public health channel carries. The claim
that would need proving is the weld: the bit count entering a real-valued
inequality with the thermodynamic unit attached.

whose knowledge base it is, and the answer here is the person's.

**Forbidden**, exactly as the [biometric pacing-evidence
note](../../../../../../../../services/myalgic-encephalomyelitis-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-biometric-guided-pacing-evidence.md)
already rules: no diagnosis and no post-exertional-malaise detection from
biometrics. An obstruction measure would be a description of a person's own
coupling history, flagged against their own baseline, in that person's
hands. It is not a diagnostic threshold, it does not become one by being
computed with better mathematics, and a relational measure with no
validation would be a *worse* thing to present as diagnosis than a
single-channel one, because its authority would be borrowed from a proof
about something else.

## Where this sits relative to the existing frames

- **Allostatic load** prices cumulative wear from a response that works
  being run too hard [@mcewen1998]. This paradigm describes a response
  whose parts stay in range while their relations fail. Different
  pathology, different measurement; a load composite can read normal here.
- **The NASEM infection-associated-chronic-illness umbrella** is a
  research-coordination class, adopted to harmonize outcome measures and
  share infrastructure across conditions, and explicitly not a claim that
  the grouped conditions are one pathophysiology [@nasem2024]. This
  paradigm is a claim about what illness *is*; the umbrella is a claim
  about how to organize research. They are compatible, and the umbrella is
  where the paradigm's first instance gets its cohorts.
- **Choutka and colleagues' post-acute infection syndromes** observe
  similar symptom profiles after diverse infections [@choutka2022], the
  same convergence the Dubbo prospective cohort found across
  Epstein-Barr virus, Ross River virus and *Coxiella burnetii*
  [@hickie2006]. Read here, pathogen-independent convergence is what you
  expect if the illness is a coordination failure that many triggers can
  induce; that reading is **inference**, and the convergence is equally
  compatible with several distinct mechanisms sharing a symptom phenotype.

## The first instance

Infection-associated chronic illness is the paradigm's first instance, not
its subject. The decoupling note and the ME/CFS and long-COVID literature
reviews are where the paradigm was first put to work, and the honest state
of that work is unchanged by this note: the four-system evidence is
consistent with the decoupling frame's broad shape without any single
system's evidence being strong enough to make it more than the field's best
current organizing hypothesis. The direction-of-causation problem in the
neuroendocrine data [@tak2011; @papadopoulos2012], the contested two-day
CPET, the single small PET study behind the neuroinflammatory arm
[@nakatomi2014], and the case-definition problem all stand exactly as that
section records them.

## What would falsify this

The instance-level falsifiers are in the decoupling note and are not
repeated. The paradigm-level ones:

1. **If relational measures never outperform single-chart markers, the
   paradigm is a redescription.** Concretely: if patients' cross-channel
   couplings — HR response against HRV recovery, cortisol awakening
   response against next-day activity tolerance — do not deviate from
   their own well-day baselines more than single-channel markers do, then
   the relational claim adds nothing that a panel of ordinary markers does
   not already carry, and the geometry is decoration.
2. **If the individually-normal, relationally-deranged case does not
   exist at scale.** The van Campen finding is currently the clearest
   instance and it comes from one group. If better-powered independent
   work finds that patients with deranged couplings essentially always
   have at least one chart out of range, the paradigm's distinctive
   observation collapses into ordinary multi-system disease.
3. **If the obstruction, once computed, is not stable.** The first
   computation over real data could find the class fluctuating with
   sampling rate, device, or chart choice rather than tracking anything
   about the person. That would show the construction is measuring the
   instrument set, not the body.
4. **If damage and border cannot be separated in practice.** The
   classification is derivable only given the protected set P. If clinical
   use shows that P cannot be stated in advance without the answer being
   smuggled in, then the paradigm's ethical claim reduces to the ordinary
   one of asking the patient what they want — which is good practice and
   is not a paradigm.
