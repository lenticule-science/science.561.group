A five-step controlled pathway for bringing FOAM claims into clinical practice through verification, localization, discussion, and explicit local alignment

Learning objectives

- Integrating Free Open-Access Medicine (FOAM) with Guidelines and Local Protocol

Prerequisites

- critical-appraisal
- local-protocol

Free Open-Access Medicine (FOAM) should enter practice through a controlled pathway, not direct adoption.

## Integration workflow

- Capture: summarize the FOAM claim in one sentence.

- Verify: check primary evidence and current society guidance.

- Localize: compare against your institution&rsquo;s protocol, resources, and escalation pathways.

- Discuss: raise discordant points with supervisors or specialty leads.

- Implement: apply only after explicit local alignment.

## Conflict handling

When FOAM and local protocol disagree, default to local protocol at bedside and escalate for formal review. Treat disagreement as a systems-learning event, not an individual improvisation prompt.
