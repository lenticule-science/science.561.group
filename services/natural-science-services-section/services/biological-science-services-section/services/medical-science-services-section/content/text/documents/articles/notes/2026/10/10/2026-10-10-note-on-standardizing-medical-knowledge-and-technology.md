## What the Leader stated

The Leader stated, on 2026-09-12, that the 561 Group's medical knowledge and technology, and the knowledges and technologies upstream of them, need a family of operations: platformization, standardization, architecturalization, semantic deduplication, syntactic deduplication, "and other such izations and ications".
The Leader stated that the operations are to be done "even if it breaks stuff, especially if it breaks stuff", because the content and code are early alpha and maintained by one person, so the standardization must be aggressive.

The Leader stated the goal: to increase the profitability, capacity, efficiency, capability, and/or strength of one vertical slice of product or products.
The Leader stated the means: realize the knowledge and technology the Group has by using, and in the same way massively improving, the Group's upstream technologies.
The Leader directed that the work be done at the agent's highest level of reasoning and staffed with up to two subagents of the Sonnet class.

In a correction the same evening the Leader stated that the request was for platformization of capabilities and not for removing things that have no consumers, and that the Leader cannot state the request more precisely in English.

## Theory

Platformization of capabilities is the Leader's term; see the glossary term `platformization-of-capabilities` in the engineering section. It makes each capability a shared, standardized platform capability. It does not remove a capability for lack of a consumer.

Medical knowledge and medical technology are held by this section and its children (medical-technology, public-health, pharmacology, herbalism, hematology and others). Upstream of them lie the information-science sections that hold their terms, the engineering sections that deliver them, such as the medical-glossary delivery service, and the Group's mathematics.

Semantic deduplication merges records that state the same thing in different words; syntactic deduplication merges records that state it in the same words. Both are done at the owning section, with the surviving record holding every element, constraint and example of the merged ones. This is what the Leader's "without breaking" tolerance depends on: breakage is tolerated in form, not in capability.

A vertical slice is one product traced from the knowledge it rests on to the technology that delivers it to a customer. The Leader asks that one slice be chosen and raised; the Leader has not named it.
