# Ethology Services Section

The Ethology Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for the
study of animal behavior in its natural context: play and its
preconditions, signaling, umwelt theory, and the behavioral repertoires
from which the 561 Group's play-and-freedom analyses draw their evidence.

Its holdings ground the "relaxed field" datum — that play is exercised
only from safety and is the first behavior to vanish under adversity —
on which the freedom-at-the-edge-of-the-held analysis (critical-theory
section, 2026-08-23) builds its manufacture argument.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`.
