# Neuroscience Services Section

The Neuroscience Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for
the study of nervous systems: attention and its allocation, prospection
and simulation, threat and safety learning, and the neural substrates of
affect.

It holds the org's oldest attention-as-neurocognitive-system material
(see `content/text/documents/articles/notes/`) and, as of 2026-08-26,
the neuroscience calibration of the attention/affect research line —
the audit of the hope–care–fear budget-rivalry theses against current
attention, prospection, and safety-learning literatures.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`; specialized
subdomains continue under `services/` — currently
[Neuroreflexivity](services/neuroreflexivity-services-section/). The
molecular floor beneath this section lives in
[Neurochemistry](../biochemistry-services-section/services/neurochemistry-services-section/README.md).
