# Physiology Services Section

The Physiology Services Section maintains knowledge and practices for
the study of the functions and regulatory mechanisms of living
organisms: homeostasis and allostasis, stress physiology, metabolic
regulation, and the bodily economics of demand and recovery.

As of 2026-08-26 it also holds the physiological calibration of the
attention/affect research line — the audit of chronic-threat taxation,
care-cost, and holding/social-buffering claims against the allostasis,
burnout, caregiving, and provision-experiment literatures.

Its material is organized by artifact role under `content/`. The
molecular level continues in
[Biochemistry](../biochemistry-services-section/README.md).
