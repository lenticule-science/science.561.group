# Assess Fungal Ecological Role

Given a fungal species or community:
1. Classify primary ecological strategy: saprotrophic, mycorrhizal, parasitic, endophytic, or lichenized.
2. Identify the substrate or host organism.
3. Describe the nutrient flows involved (what the fungus provides, what it receives).
4. Assess ecosystem-level function: decomposition, nutrient cycling, soil formation, plant health.
5. Note any known interactions with other organisms (bacteria, insects, plants).
6. Connect to broader ecosystem context (forest health, agricultural implications).
