# Natural Science Services Section

Natural science and its subdomains: astronomy, biology, chemistry,
cosmology, earth science, and geography, with their own subsections
beneath them.

Renamed from physical-science-services-section on 2026-08-21. The old
name was wrong on its own contents — biology has lived here all along,
which is standard for "natural science" and nonstandard for "physical
science" — and the new name carries the section's frame: nature under
[four regimes](content/text/glossary/topics/four-regimes-of-natural-existence.md) —
[kinetic](content/text/glossary/terms/kinetic-existence.md),
[quantum](content/text/glossary/terms/quantum-existence.md),
[causal](content/text/glossary/terms/causal-existence.md), and
[thermodynamic](content/text/glossary/terms/thermodynamic-existence.md).
