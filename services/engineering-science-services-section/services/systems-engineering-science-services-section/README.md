# Systems Engineering Science Services Section

The Systems Engineering Science Services Section is Lenticule Science's
source-owned home for the scientific study of closed-loop regulation: how a system
observes its own condition, decides whether and how to change it, and acts
on that decision through a persistent body of operational knowledge.

Its maintained knowledge is organized by artifact role under `content/`,
while specialized systems-engineering disciplines are developed as service
sections under `services/`.

The section studies engineering practice, not communication and regulation
in general. It takes cybernetics' account of feedback and requisite variety
— owned by `information-science-services-section`'s
`cybernetics-services-section` — as a founding premise, and asks how that
premise is organized into buildable architectures: control loops, autonomic
managers, and self-adaptive systems, for computational, physical, and
hybrid systems. It sits beside `computer-science-services-section` rather
than beneath it, because its subject — the regulation architecture itself —
is not particular to computing; `computer-science-services-section`'s own
`computer-systems-science-services-section` studies composed computer
systems (operating systems, interfaces, and the like) as artifacts, which is
a narrower and different concern. Dated source corpora are preserved under
`lib/legacy/`.

The reusable engineering programs that may realize this theory belong to
Red Cup Engineering; their running operating methods belong to Coesite Operations
and the actual customer or Group instance owns its configured resources and
independent receiving. This scientific section does not implement a scheduler,
appoint a cloud account's operator, or certify a production outcome from theory.
