# Knitting Science Services Section

Lenticule Science → Engineering Science → Fibercraft → **Knitting Science**.

**Purpose.** Study knitted clothing, loop topology, generative cable construction, fabric mechanics, and proof-carrying design. The wearer and the working knitter are primary consumers of this science. A mathematically interesting shape is not an acceptable substitute for good knitwear or executable instructions.

## Scientific posture

- **Established as algebra:** finite combinatorial statements actually declared in the source Lean libraries, such as the crossing reading, proper-alternation word rule, and Padovan matrix identity.
- **Design hypothesis:** physical mapping from the algebra to knitted loops, drape, defect motion, reinforcement and wearer experience.
- **Not tested:** all three conversation prototypes. No finished garment, physical swatch or independent knit-test was reported in the source conversation.
- **Rejected as final garments:** the four-panel branched surface and the twelve-handle Borromean sculpture. They remain relevant construction experiments, not the user's clothing objective.

## Execution boundary

This section owns the scientific questions, textile hypotheses, provenance and validation protocol. Construction tooling and machine-specific compiler implementation belong downstream in engineering, independent physical making and testing in operations, and finished pattern/media distribution in public services. A knitted chart requires stitch-by-stitch executable operations and human review; an STL mesh cannot supply that.

No mathematics-facing prose should masquerade as a publication-ready knitting pattern.
