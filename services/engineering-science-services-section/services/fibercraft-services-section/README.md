This section holds fibercraft content.

## Knitting Science

[Knitting Science Services Section](services/knitting-science-services-section/README.md) studies knitted loops, cabling, computational textile construction, measured fabric mechanics, and wearable knitwear design. Its inaugural project archives the 12 October 2026 Obsmetrologia knitting conversation and THE UNDERTOW sweater development. Its scientific notes distinguish proof from speculative garment geometry and require independent knitter review.

Domain research remains in Lenticule Science; engineering/fabrication, production and public pattern distribution belong to their downstream services sections.
