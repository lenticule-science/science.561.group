# Brenken et al. (2018) — *Fused filament fabrication of fiber-reinforced polymers: A review*

Review of fiber-reinforced FFF covering feedstock formulation (matrix selection, fiber sizing chemistry, fiber content), process modeling (Folgar–Tucker fiber alignment in non-Newtonian flow), and resulting mechanical properties. Particularly comprehensive on PA-CF and PEEK-CF, the workhorses of engineering FFF.

**Why it matters here.** The reference for [fiber-reinforcement](./predication-fiber-reinforcement.md). Establishes the rod-suspension rheology framework needed to predict bead-direction fiber orientation, and from there the in-bead vs transverse stiffness ratio.

**Useful contributions.**

- Catalog of commercial fiber-reinforced FFF feedstocks and their reported in-bead modulus and strength.
- Fiber-orientation modeling overview — how Folgar–Tucker, exit-swell, and contraction flows combine to set final orientation.
- Discussion of the unsolved Z-strength problem: fibers reinforce in-bead, do nothing for the layer interface.
