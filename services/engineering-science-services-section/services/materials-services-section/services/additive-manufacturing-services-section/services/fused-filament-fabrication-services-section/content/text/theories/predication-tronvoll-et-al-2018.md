# Tronvoll, Welo & Elverum (2018) — *The effects of voids on structural properties of fused deposition modelled parts: a probabilistic approach*

The canonical study of inter-bead void geometry in FFF and its statistical effect on tensile strength. μ-CT scans the void distribution; classical fracture mechanics relates void distribution to weakest-link strength prediction.

**Key results.**

- Void cross-sections are roughly triangular at the bead/bead/layer junction; aspect ratios ~2:1 elongated along bead direction.
- Void volume fraction scales inversely with extrusion multiplier (flow ratio) — the basis of "flow calibration" tuning.
- Tensile strength scaling can be recovered with extreme-value statistics on the void distribution.

**Why it matters here.** The empirical foundation for [inter-bead-voids](./predication-inter-bead-voids.md) and the FFF analog of the metal-AM porosity-fatigue rule. The probabilistic framing is the bridge to qualification: a part with measured void distribution has a predictable strength distribution.
