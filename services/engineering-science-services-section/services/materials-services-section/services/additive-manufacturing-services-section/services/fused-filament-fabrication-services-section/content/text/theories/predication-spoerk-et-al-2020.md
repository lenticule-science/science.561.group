# Spoerk, Holzer & Gonzalez-Gutierrez (2020) — *Material extrusion-based additive manufacturing of polypropylene: a review on how to improve dimensional inaccuracy and warpage*

Despite the polypropylene-specific title, the most thorough modern review of FFF warpage mechanisms broadly. Surveys: feedstock formulation (nucleating agents, fillers), bed-adhesion strategies, chamber-temperature effects, residual-stress measurement methods.

**Why it matters here.** Provides the empirical evidence base for the [cooling-warping-tradeoff](../../../../../content/text/theories/predication-cooling-warping-tradeoff.md) and for the chamber/bed-temperature recommendations in the FFF tuning narrative.

**Particularly cited contributions.**

- Quantitative warping force measurements on instrumented build plates (force vs cooling rate vs ambient temperature).
- Adhesion-promoter survey (PEI, glue stick, hairspray, DimaFix, Magigoo) with comparative pull-off strength data.
- The "nucleating agent for PP" trick that lets PP — historically un-printable on FFF — print with PA or PVA-coated build sheets.
