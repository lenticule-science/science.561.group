# Gibson, Rosen, Stucker & Khorasani (2021) — *Additive Manufacturing Technologies*

The standard graduate textbook on AM, organized around the seven ASTM/ISO 52900 process families: vat photopolymerization, material extrusion, material jetting, binder jetting, powder bed fusion, sheet lamination, and directed energy deposition. Third edition (2021) substantially expanded over the 2015 second edition: new chapters on hybrid AM, qualification and certification, AM economics and life-cycle assessment, software ecosystem, and post-processing.

**Why it matters here.** It is the only single source that gives consistent treatment to all process families, so it is the canonical place to ground the AM theory's process taxonomy. Chapters 5 (vat photopolymerization), 6 (material extrusion), and 9 (powder bed fusion) cover the three families that account for >95% of current installed industrial capacity.

**Strengths.** Process physics, design-for-AM, and the qualification/standardization story (Ch. 18, 19) are genuinely state-of-the-art as of 2021.

**Limitations.** Light on machine-learning-driven process control (a frontier active 2022–2026), and the metal AM cost models in Ch. 17 already lag behind 2024 LPBF productivity gains.
