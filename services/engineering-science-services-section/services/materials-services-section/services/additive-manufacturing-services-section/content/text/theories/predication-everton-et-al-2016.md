# Everton et al. (2016) — *Review of in-situ process monitoring and in-situ metrology for metal additive manufacturing*

Foundational review of in-situ monitoring sensor modalities for metal PBF and DED. Pre-dates the deep-learning wave but lays the catalog the ML literature still works from.

**Sensor modalities surveyed.**

- High-speed visible imaging of the melt pool.
- Co-axial pyrometry / thermography (single- and dual-wavelength).
- Off-axis IR cameras (whole-bed thermal field per layer).
- Acoustic emission.
- Spatter detection by side-camera.
- Layer-by-layer optical tomography.

**Defects detectable.**

- Lack-of-fusion porosity (thermal signature: cold patches, undermelt).
- Keyhole porosity (acoustic + spatter signatures).
- Cracking (acoustic + post-layer optical).
- Recoater interference (off-axis imaging).

**The unsolved problem (still open in 2026).** Mapping sensor signal → defect → mechanical-property impact in a closed loop. Sensors generate the data; turning that into a real-time corrective action (or a certifiable post-build pedigree) is the open frontier.
