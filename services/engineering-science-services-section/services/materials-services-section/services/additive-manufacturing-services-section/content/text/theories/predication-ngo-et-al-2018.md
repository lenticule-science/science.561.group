# Ngo et al. (2018) — *Additive manufacturing (3D printing): A review of materials, methods, applications and challenges*

Wide-aperture review of AM as of 2017–2018; over 9,000 citations as of 2026, making it the most-cited single AM review.

**Structure.** Surveys: (1) the seven process families, (2) feedstock materials (polymers, metals, ceramics, concrete, biomaterials), (3) application domains (aerospace, automotive, medical, construction, food), (4) outstanding challenges (cost, surface finish, anisotropy, qualification, scale).

**Most cited contributions.**

- The polymer / metal / ceramic feedstock summary table (Table 1) became the de-facto teaching reference for material families.
- The applications-by-industry breakdown is the source of the often-quoted figure that aerospace and medical jointly account for >40% of metal AM revenue (now closer to 55% per Wohlers 2024).
- The "challenges" section (§7) frames the four still-open problems: process repeatability, surface quality, anisotropy, and certification.

**Limitations.** Pre-dates the multi-laser LPBF productivity revolution (2020–) and the surge in machine-learning-based in-situ monitoring (2021–). The construction-AM section is largely speculative; that field is now empirically grounded by Buswell et al. (2018) and the Chinese precast-AM literature.

**Cited from.** AM theory _index, several open questions, sustainability discussion.
