Diagenesis is the set of low-temperature, long-duration physical and chemical changes that sediment undergoes after deposition and before metamorphism. Volcanic glass that alters to zeolite where it lies is the standard example. In this house the sedimentary material is prose, and diagenesis is what [zeolitization](zeolitization.md) is an instance of ([framing note](../../../../../../../../../../../fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 52-59).

Seating is not terminal. A living corpus accumulates burial pressure: the weight of schemas, smart contracts and reference trees stacked on early terms. An early term that cannot reorganize concentrates nonlocal stress and eventually fractures. The diagenetic ladder is the house account of how seated terms avoid this ([diagenetic ladder](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-diagenetic-ladder-of-seated-terms.md)). Stage one is the clinoptilolite phase, a porous, water-rich record that is highly queryable but of low density and that depends on a high volume of ambient validator attention, the pore water, so it carries audit debt. Stage two is the analcime phase, reached by trust-boundary burial and a collapse the note maps to hydrophobic collapse. Stage three is the albite phase, an anhydrous record that consumes no ambient validator attention and is no longer a queryable schema but a dense, compiled block. The note names three checks that would falsify it, among them that shear modulus and ionic conductivity move in opposition during compaction, and that albite blocks can or cannot be re-fluidized by an erase pulse at 175 C and above (lines 179-181).

The ladder is an instance of Ostwald's step rule: a system leaving a metastable state passes through successively more stable forms and does not jump to the most stable. It predicts the same step behaviour for any term seated under time pressure and path dependence, so two records built from the same requirements by different authoring histories can settle into different structures ([rock-cycle note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-semiotic-mineralization-and-compactification.md), abstract).

Only the sedimentary route is practiced. Igneous crystallization, metamorphic recrystallization and weathering are named in the rock-cycle note as processes the corpus has not used.
