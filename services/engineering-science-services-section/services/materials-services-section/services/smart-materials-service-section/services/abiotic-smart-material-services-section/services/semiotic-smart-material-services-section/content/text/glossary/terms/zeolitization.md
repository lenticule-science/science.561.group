Zeolitization is the process by which the organization's loose, thick prose is compacted and recrystallized into small stable records whose pores admit and catalyze future work. The Leader's statement is that institutional knowledge undergoes diagenetic zeolitization: the sedimentary prose becomes terms, duties, skills and cards, and no reader needs to ingest the bed ([framing note](../../../../../../../../../../../fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 52-59). Thickness, the prior-idea load a record demands of its reader, is informational value, and thick material is what must be zeolitized and not broadcast: broadcast prices every future turn, and a pore prices only the turns that enter it. Seating a glossary term is an act of zeolitization.

The geological process has seven commitments, and the house tests its practice against them ([diagenetic ladder](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-diagenetic-ladder-of-seated-terms.md), section 2). Replacement: the parent glass, raw prose, is consumed and not merely annotated. Active medium: the alteration needs alkaline pore water, which here is contracted inference. Microporosity: the seated crystal is queryable through channels the parent lacks. Kinetics: it is low-temperature and long-duration, with falling cost across iterations. Density: more meaning per byte. Metastable parent: only unsettled material can be seated, and a seated term is not re-seated. Metastable sequence: zeolites alter to denser phases under burial. The first six were practiced; the seventh was a gap that the ladder note supplies as the [diagenetic ladder](diagenesis.md). Real zeolite forms mostly by in-situ alteration of a metastable parent, not by cementing eroded fragments ([rock-cycle note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-semiotic-mineralization-and-compactification.md), section 1).

The practice has a method. A measurer locates where a prose bed's utterances sit, proposes term records and [process design kit](../../../../../../../../../../../fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/process-design-kit.md) projections at a cheap inference tier, and a separate judging locus seats what it accepts; the measurer never seats its own proposal ([zeolitic software development](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-zeolitic-software-development.md), section 4.9, citing a zeolitization-service `PROCESS.md` that is not in the index). That rule is thesis-grade in its source. The same draft is itself a draft for the Leader's review and binds no enterprise.

The product is [zeolite](zeolite.md): a term record, a contract or a rule list, whose cost to later readers is that of the pores they enter.
