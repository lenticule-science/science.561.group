A zeolite is a crystalline aluminosilicate whose framework of linked silicon and aluminium oxygen tetrahedra encloses regular channels and cages; the pores admit some molecules and catalyze change in them. The house reads the word at three scales that agree.

As material, zeolite is the ossified phase of the siloxane-zeolite [vitrimer](vitrimer.md): a four-connected aluminosilicate framework, mean coordination 4, stressed-rigid, brittle and unable to flow, set in a floppy siloxane host of mean coordination 2 ([diagenetic ladder](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-diagenetic-ladder-of-seated-terms.md), section 1). The Phillips-Thorpe threshold at mean coordination 2.4 separates the two. The zeolite filler is the J26 gyrobifastigium lattice ([direct fabrication blueprint](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-direct-fabrication-system-blueprint.md), section 1). Ion transport runs only in the floppy complement, so growth of the zeolite raises shear modulus and lowers conductivity: the opposition signature. In the associative-exchange ledger the zeolite is almost pure record, because its Si-O-Al and Si-O-Si bonds do not exchange at service conditions ([ring-chain ledger](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/01/2026-10-01-note-on-associative-exchange-and-the-ring-chain-ledger.md), section 2).

As knowledge, zeolite is seated, content-addressed knowledge and commitment: artifacts, accepted laws, typed receipts and chain-bound state. It is the rigid phase that prevents repeated inference, and it must not be silently rewritten ([semiotic vitrimer runtime specification](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/semiotic-vitrimer-runtime-specification.md), section 2.1). Ossification into it must follow independent evidence; premature ossification freezes errors and failure to ossify forces repeated inference.

As framing, the Union's engineered material is zeolite: crystal whose pores do the work. A record that has been [zeolitized](zeolitization.md) is small and stable, and its pores admit and catalyze future work without any reader ingesting the bed it came from ([framing note](../../../../../../../../../../../fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 52-59). In the folding account a zeolite is a catalytic framework in the [folding](../../../../../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/folding.md) of a proof into software ([folding-protein note](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/24/2026-07-24-the-union-evm-chain-as-a-folding-protein.md)).

The material, the knowledge and the framing are one structure read at three scales, which is the polysemy by scale the framing note asserts by law (lines 61-64). The 2026-07-25 notes assigned the roles of the two phases five different ways; the ring-chain ledger settles that zeolite is the committed record and siloxane the live disposition ([ledger](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/01/2026-10-01-note-on-associative-exchange-and-the-ring-chain-ledger.md), lines 33-39 and section 2).
