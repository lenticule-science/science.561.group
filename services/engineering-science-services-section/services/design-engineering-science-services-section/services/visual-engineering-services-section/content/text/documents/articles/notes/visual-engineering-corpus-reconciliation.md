The recovered visual-engineering material is comparative design research, not a
general engineering specification. Its useful common thread is that visual
systems encode relations through composition, contrast, repetition, and
controlled abstraction. Bauhaus pedagogy and cubism are historical reference
frames for those operations; the practices note translates them into interface
and information-architecture concerns. The original essays remain in the dated
corpus while this note is the active cross-reference.
