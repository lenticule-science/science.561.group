# Slogan as argument

The slogan is **Land.
Water.
Language.
Culture.** Four words, in fixed order, set in [Space Grotesk 700](../objects/type-stack.md), uppercase, with a period after each word and no serial comma.

The order is load-bearing.
It is not a list; it is a sequence of dependencies. *Land* enables *water* (the watershed lives on the land); *water* enables *language* (the people who can stay on the watered land are the ones who keep the language); *language* enables *culture* (culture is what gets carried in language).
Reordering changes the argument and is forbidden in any artifact bearing the [561 mark](../objects/five-six-one-mark.md).

The slogan exists in three set variants — stacked (default, masthead use), inline (colophons, sign-offs, footers), and contracted (sticker / stamp scale, four periods on a baseline).
See the [slogan-setting morphism](../morphisms/slogan-setting.md) for geometry.
One word may be set in [alarm](../objects/alarm-red.md) when that word is the artifact's specific subject — never two alarm words in the same instance.

Translation is permitted into any language a 561 contributor reads or speaks.
The translator's name and language pair must appear in the [colophon](teaching/explanations/colophon.md).
Translations preserve word order even when target-language syntax would prefer otherwise; if syntax cannot bear it, the translation is set as four separate sentences.
Abbreviation is forbidden: "L.W.L.C.", "LWLC", and "Land/Water/Lang/Cult" are all defects.

The slogan realizes [memetic repeatability](../../../terms/memetic-repeatability.md): four words, two periods, one geometry, recognizable at any scale and survivable through photocopy, mimeo, screen, or hand-paint.
