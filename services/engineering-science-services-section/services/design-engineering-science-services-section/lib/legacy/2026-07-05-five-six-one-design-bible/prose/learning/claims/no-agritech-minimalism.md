# § 09.2 — No agritech minimalism

## Refusal

Thin geometric sans on green gradient.
Aerial drone footage of fields.
The visual grammar of the agriculture-tech pitch deck is forbidden.

## Why

The agritech-pitch register is the bible's *other* major aesthetic attractor (the first being the [donor-appeal register](no-philanthropic-visual-language.md)).
The register reads as a request for capital from a presumed Series A investor — wrong audience, wrong voice.
Without active refusal, the page drifts toward it whenever the artifact's subject is land, water, or growing.

## Check

If the palette and type would be at home on a Series A landing page for a vertical-farming startup, return to the system palette (see [color-budget](teaching/explanations/color-budget.md), [ink](../objects/ink.md), [bone](../objects/bone.md), [alarm-red](../objects/alarm-red.md), [witness-blue](../objects/witness-blue.md)) and verify the type stack (see [type-stack](../objects/type-stack.md)).
