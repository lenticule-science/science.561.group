# Narratives

The connective prose that orients a reader to why the bible is shaped the way it is.
Narratives are not specs; they are the surrounding voice.
Each narrative carries an `introduces:` slot listing the entities it brings into the room.

## Inventory

- [cover](cover.md) — masthead, hero, the four declarative words, the eyebrow, the run of metadata.
- [manifesto](manifesto.md) — § 00, the note on this document.
  What the bible is, what it isn't, why 561 stands on its own.
- [lineage](lineage.md) — § 10, the two practices the bible cites (brutalism and Tufte) and the others in the room (constructivist agitprop, Black radical print culture).
- [colophon](colophon.md) — § 11, the closing facts: edition, drafting date, method, type, inks, license, next review, and the slogan one last time.
