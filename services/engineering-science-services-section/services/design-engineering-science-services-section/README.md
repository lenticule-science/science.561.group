# Design Engineering Science Services Section

The Design Engineering Science Services Section is Red Cup Engineering's home
for the systematic study of practices that shape perceivable, navigable, and
usable artifacts and information systems.

Its maintained knowledge is organized by artifact role under `content/`, while
specialized design-engineering disciplines are developed as service sections
under `services/`.

The section includes comparative design research as well as applied methods:
historical schools and theories supply research frames, while the nested
disciplines translate them into repeatable design-engineering practices. Dated
source corpora are preserved under `lib/legacy/`.

## Services

- `information-architecture-services-section` — classification, labeling, and
  the organization of information spaces.
- `typography-services-section` — typographic structure, history, spacing, and
  reading behavior.
- `visual-engineering-services-section` — repeatable visual practices that
  coordinate form, attention, and interpretation.
- `web-design-services-section` — design practices particular to linked web
  environments.
