# Aerospace Engineering Science Services Section

The Aerospace Engineering Science Services Section holds the group's knowledge
of aerodynamics, rocketry, and unmanned aerial vehicle design.

Its maintained knowledge lives under `content/text/glossary`: a glossary of
aerospace fundamentals (flow regimes, propulsion principles, structural
mechanics, orbital mechanics, and the terms that support them, such as
thrust, viscosity, shock waves, yield strength, and the Navier-Stokes
equations). Specialized disciplines are developed as nested service sections
under `services/`.

## Services

- `rocketry-services-section` — rocketry practice: propellant chemistry,
  staging, the rocket equation, nozzle and combustion-chamber design, and
  related glossary terms.
- `uavs-services-section` — unmanned aerial vehicle design: airframes,
  aerodynamics, propulsion, guidance and control, printed-wing fabrication,
  and dated notes on UAV design economics.
