Fabrication is the production of a durable, governed artifact — physical or
informational — through a process whose fidelity is set by the completeness
of its work order, not by the skill or tier of whichever provider executes
it. A fabricator (a lathe, a print head, an inference process) is
structurally interchangeable: it is blind to any intent its instructions did
not carry. This is the repo's Central Dogma of Fabrication, and it holds
equally for physical manufacture and for code fabrication.

Fabrication is stated generally, of a thing, and specialized in order: an
entity is a fabricated thing that predications range over; a resource is a
fabricated entity with claims bound to it; a code resource is a resource whose
claims are executable and assayed by running them. The ladder, its seating on
the philosophy and math terms, and its lineage in code from TAB's `make-thing`
through LAGOON's things and qualities to eavt's `constituteEntity`, are stated in
[the 2026-09-04 note on fabrication as the birth of a thing](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/04/2026-09-04-note-on-fabrication-as-the-birth-of-a-thing.md).

This topic gathers the vocabulary of one fabrication: the [work order](../terms/work-order.md)
that governs it, the [fabricator](../terms/fabricator.md) that performs it,
[fabrication fidelity](../terms/fabrication-fidelity.md) as what the order buys,
[admission](../terms/admission.md) as the decision to attempt, [acceptance](../terms/acceptance.md)
as the receiver's decision after evidence, and the
[realization receipt](../terms/realization-receipt.md) that binds evidence to the
admitted order. It sits at the root of the fabrication-science ladder because
every child, computer, code, JavaScript, Node, narrows the kind of thing and
inherits this vocabulary unchanged.
