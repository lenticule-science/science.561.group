title: "Fabrication"
---

Fabrication is the production of a durable, governed artifact — physical or
informational — through a process whose fidelity is set by the completeness
of its work order, not by the skill or tier of whichever provider executes
it. A fabricator (a lathe, a print head, an inference process) is
structurally interchangeable: it is blind to any intent its instructions did
not carry. This is the repo's Central Dogma of Fabrication, and it holds
equally for physical manufacture and for code fabrication.
