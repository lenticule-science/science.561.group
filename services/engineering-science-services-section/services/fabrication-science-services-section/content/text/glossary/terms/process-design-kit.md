A process design kit (PDK) is the package a fabrication process supplies so that designers can target it: design rules, models, a library of proven cells and the checks that verify a design against the rules. In this house a PDK is the compacted form of the [electronic design automation](electronic-design-automation.md) framing that carries it into a department: the small set of design rules, proven cell exemplars and checks that lets the department target the material in its own register without holding the whole framing note ([framing note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 61-68). Within a domain the register is the domain's own, and software speaks software. A PDK is therefore a product of [zeolitization](../../../../../materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zeolitization.md): thick framing compacted into rules and exemplars whose pores admit later work.

A PDK has three parts. Design rules, each forced by a proven result or a ruled practice and checked against running code before it is kept. A cell library of exemplars cited by running path, not described in the abstract. Design-rule checks that a department runs rather than re-derives by inspection ([zeolitic software development](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-zeolitic-software-development.md), section 4.8). The [finance process design kit](../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/recovered/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-finance-process-design-kit--6899c50.md) is the one surviving instance. Its rules include that a quantity's denomination is part of its identity and is never silently converted, that law is consumed by digest and drift or absence is a typed refusal, and that evidence is bilateral with no consolidated book. The software PDK of 2026-07-23 is cited by the zeolitic draft for seven rules: a unit is one pure function from exact typed input to output or typed refusal; refusal with an exact reason is a lawful output; law imports are pinned by digest and refuse on drift; wiring exists only where code demonstrates it; fabrication is subtractive; classification lives in structured records in one register; and a stored status binds the exact inputs needed to re-run its check (sections 4.1-4.7). The software PDK file is not in the index. A third PDK, for the Physarum zeosemiocenum substrate, is stated as constraint counting: zeolite as the over-constrained ossified phase, transport in the floppy matrix complement ([physarum note](../../../../../materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-physarum-zeosemiocenum-fabrication.md), description).

The scope of a PDK follows the [fabrication](../topics/fabrication.md) ladder. Different things fabricated need different kits, and a kit stated before the general theory flattens node packages, registry entries and smart contracts into one ([fabrication note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/04/2026-09-04-note-on-fabrication-as-the-birth-of-a-thing.md), lines 253-255). The conformance checks a PDK names are the design-rule checks of the flow, and the receipts they feed are [realization receipts](realization-receipt.md).
