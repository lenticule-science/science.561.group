Kinetic proofreading is the mechanism by which a molecular process discriminates between a correct and an incorrect substrate more accurately than the difference in their binding energies allows (Hopfield 1974; Ninio 1975). A single discrimination step cannot do better than an equilibrium error floor fixed by that energy difference. A proofreading process inserts an irreversible, energy-consuming intermediate step after initial binding, so that the discrimination is applied a second time and the error approaches the square of the floor. The cost is paid in free energy and time: the discarded wrong candidates are lost and the process is slower than the single step.

Two conditions make the second discrimination count, and both carry over to fabrication ([glass-sponge note](../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/01/2026-08-01-note-on-the-content-addressed-glass-sponge-semantic-compute-complex.md), section 16.4). A failed candidate must enter an effectively irreversible reject path and must not be silently readmitted. The additional pass must use genuinely independent information or a fresh draw: two passes by the same model with the same prompt and evidence are not two channels, and independence is evidential, not organizational. [Kinetic proofing](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/kinetic-proofing.md) is the house generalization: repeated, economically accountable challenge through structurally independent evidence, bought only when the expected risk reduction exceeds the cost. Fidelity is a trade against throughput, so the system records which channel caught which defect, not only how many reviewers looked.

In code fabrication the mechanism is the evidence-bound fixation gate. [Fabrication with fidelity](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/fabrication-with-fidelity-kinetic-proofreading-germline-and-error-catastrophe.md) states that the gate buys fidelity by an irreversible delay (kinetic proofreading), an exclusive write barrier (Weismann's germline and soma) and an instrumentable error threshold (Eigen's error catastrophe). It reports that the settlement's own audit of 2026-07-24 found no work order fulfilled through the gate and 383 fulfilled through a single-hire route that bypasses it, 139 of which fell outside the audit's good bucket; the audit is session-audited and self-described as unattested. The same article states that the molecular-genetics vocabulary was new synthesis in the corpus when it was written. This term seats it.

Kinetic proofreading prices the check; the [Central Dogma of fabrication](central-dogma-of-fabrication.md) prices the input to the check, and the two are distinct from author exclusion, which says who may verify ([central dogma research](../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/24/2026-07-24-research-on-the-central-dogma-of-code-fabrication.md)). The third failure of verification it bears on is kinetic obstruction: the global section exists but is not reached within the observation window ([kinetic obstruction explanation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/teaching-articles/explanations/kinetic-obstruction-as-a-third-verification-failure-mode.md)). The rules of [fabrication fidelity](fabrication-fidelity.md) and of [admission](admission.md) apply it: a fabrication line that assays a candidate only against evidence its author supplied has one channel.

The question of whether the line's assays are independent channels in fact is open ([tests before source as an independent channel](../../documents/articles/research-articles/questions/tests-before-source-as-independent-channel.md)).
