A work order is the closed statement of what a fabrication is to produce: the artifact's identity, its requirements and prohibitions, the laws and standards it must satisfy, its inputs and outputs, its acceptance cases, and the open questions that remain. It is the governed part of a governed act: the model in TAB's `make-thing`, the Markdown document the jsnode fabrication line translates, the artifact fabrication specification the language-neutral admission service admits.

A work order differs from the artifact it orders and from a prompt about it: it names the structure to be held open before the act, and it is complete only when a fabricator that is blind to any intent it does not carry can still produce the artifact. The Central Dogma of code fabrication states the consequence for repair: fidelity is set by the completeness of the work order handed to a provider, not by which provider was hired. A defect traced to a gap in the work order is repaired in the work order, never by choosing a more capable provider.
