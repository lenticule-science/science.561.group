Electronic design automation (EDA) is the software toolchain that carries a design from a high-level statement through placement, routing and design-rule checking to a layout that a fabrication process can build. The process it targets is a process node, and the rules of that node reach the designer as a [process design kit](process-design-kit.md). In this house EDA is the theory of code fabrication: agentic development is electronic design automation over the organization's own language model, each enterprise providing EDA over its own model, and the model is that enterprise's process node. The Leader admitted this framing on 2026-07-20 and refined it on 2026-07-23 ([the framing note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/07/23/2026-07-23-note-on-eda-as-diagenetic-zeolitization-of-digital-information.md), lines 15-25 and 41-45). On 2026-10-09 the Leader said of it: "AT ONE POINT WE HAD INCREDIBLE RESEARCH ON CODE FABRICATION AS ELECTRONIC DESIGN AUTOMATION AND PROTEIN FOLDING AND KINETIC PROOFING" ([recovery index](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-recovering-the-fabrication-research.md), line 59).

The stages of an EDA flow have house carriers. The design is the [work order](work-order.md). Placement is the seat: which section owns the script. Routing is the import and dependency graph under exact pins. The design-rule check is the conformance instrument whose exit status is the verdict: `read-scripts-conformance`, `import-stratification` and `exact-deps` ([the fabrication note](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/09/04/2026-09-04-note-on-fabrication-as-the-birth-of-a-thing.md), lines 245-255). The flow that realizes the design is the [fabricator](fabricator.md); its record is the [realization receipt](realization-receipt.md); the fidelity of its output is bought by independent assay, which is [kinetic proofreading](kinetic-proofreading.md); and the realization of a proof as working software is [folding](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/folding.md). Coordination between design houses is priced and receipted, and the cost of coordination on a seam equals the Landauer entropy of the seam (`landauer_eq_coordination_cost`, cited by the framing note at lines 36-39).

The material the framing designs into is semiocenic material, engineered toward zeolite, and the knowledge that governs the flow is compacted by [zeolitization](../../../../../materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zeolitization.md) into terms, duties, skills and cards. The same word has a literal sense in the smart-materials section, where an "EDA fabrication engine" for the siloxane-zeolite substrate runs a MAPE-K loop of impedance sensing, Bruggeman phi-mapping, pebble-game solving and Joule-heat write and erase ([direct fabrication blueprint](../../../../../materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/25/2026-07-25-note-on-direct-fabrication-system-blueprint.md)). That is the physical half of fabrication; this term is the code half.

The framing note grades itself: it is a framing that binds no enterprise, anchored where its mathematics is proven and thesis where it says so (lines 70-78). The fabrication note adds that a general theory of fabrication must precede the toolchain, because a premature process design kit flattens things that must be fabricated as distinct kinds, among them node packages, registry entries and smart contracts (lines 253-255). The software process design kit the framing cites is not in the index; the [recovery index](../../documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-recovering-the-fabrication-research.md) (section 3.3, item 16, and section 8) records that it must be located or rewritten.
