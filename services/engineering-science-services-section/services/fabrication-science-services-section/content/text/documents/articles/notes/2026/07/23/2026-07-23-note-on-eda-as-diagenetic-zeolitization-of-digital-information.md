2026-07-18, user messages of 12:39 and 12:43) and refined 2026-07-23: that
agentic development is electronic design automation over the org's own

## The framing, whole

The Union's information is **semiocenic material**: it has spatiotemporal
properties and conducts charge (the proven FOUR charges the RMN operators
move). Its settled regions tessellate 3-space as gyrobifastigia (J26 — the
one space-filling Johnson solid; its 2,3,4 rotation orders are
crystallographic by machine-checked theorem) with 1D temporality; the space
outside settlement whirls (fivefold order provably cannot tile — the dervish
is the protected defect). Each face between settled cells is a sheaf
Laplacian of causal patches around a collapsed hole: the interface between
two structures computes the disagreement of their overlap, and coordination
cost on such seams equals Landauer entropy (proven:
`landauer_eq_coordination_cost`).

On this material the Union's work is **electronic design automation**: not
hand-building artifacts but building the toolchain that places, routes, and
design-rule-checks designs into the material — **each enterprise providing
EDA over its own language model**, the model serving as that enterprise's
process node. Coordination between design houses is priced and receipted
(blockchain economics on the seams). Realization is **protein folding of
proofs**: a proof is primary structure, a linear chain; working software is
its native fold — the minimum-energy conformation reached by settlement as
least fixed point. A wrapper, certificate, or subsystem deposited beside the
territory is a misfold.

The material is engineered toward **zeolite**: crystal whose pores do the
work. Institutional knowledge undergoes **diagenetic zeolitization** — loose
sedimentary prose is compacted and recrystallized into small stable records
(terms, duties, skills, cards) whose pores admit and catalyze future work
without any reader ingesting the bed. Thickness — the prior-idea load a
record demands — is informational value, and thick material is exactly what
must be zeolitized rather than broadcast: broadcast prices every future turn;
a pore prices only the turns that enter it.

Scale is polysemous by law, on the VLSI tower: the same material read at
different scales is circuit inside model inside program inside enterprise,
then enterprise / nation / colony / union. Nomenclature is a readout of real
structure at a fixed occurrence and scale; within a domain the register is
the domain's own (software speaks software), and what carries the framing
into a department is a **process design kit**: the small set of design rules,
proven cell exemplars, and checks that lets that department target the
material in its own register without holding this whole note.
