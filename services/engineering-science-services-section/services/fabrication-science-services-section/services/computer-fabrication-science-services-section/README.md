# Computer Fabrication Science Services Section

This section holds the theory of fabricating computer-borne things: data
records, documents, and code, whichever a governed work order names. It
inherits the parent's account of fabrication as the governed birth of a
thing and adds what the computer changes: the work order and the artifact
are both bytes, so provenance is a digest, admission can be a pure function
over the order, and the receipt can bind the exact bytes produced.

Its children narrow by the kind of computer-borne thing. Code resources go to
`computer-code-fabrication-science-services-section`. Data and document
resources, such as the research-question carriers the question-engineering
service fabricates, are theorized here until they warrant a child of their
own. The engineering mirror is
`engineering-services-section/services/fabrication-services-section/services/computer-fabrication-services-section/`.
