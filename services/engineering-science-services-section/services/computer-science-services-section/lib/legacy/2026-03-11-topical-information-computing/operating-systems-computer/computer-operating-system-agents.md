The computer-operating-system kind is the categorical home of any POSIX-bound, Node-implemented operating system.
The concrete [run script](doing/working/hacking/doing/working/running/scripts/running.mjs) at this level spawns a child process as the supervised subsystem, inherits stdio, forwards signals, and exits with the child's status.
561 is the current instance and parameterizes this run with `process = 561`.
