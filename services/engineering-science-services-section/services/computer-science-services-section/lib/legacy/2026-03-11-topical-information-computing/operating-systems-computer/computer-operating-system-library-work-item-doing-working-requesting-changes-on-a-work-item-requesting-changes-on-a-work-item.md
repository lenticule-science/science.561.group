---
name: requesting-changes-on-a-work-item
description: Transitions a work-item from review back to in-progress with reviewer feedback. The opposite of approving.
specializing: contesting-positing-morphism
---

A **requesting-changes-on-a-work-item** call is the contesting positing that moves a work-item from review back to in-progress, sending the doer back with changes-requested.
The transition is recorded in the work-item's jsonl; specific feedback should be written into a sibling progress cell.
