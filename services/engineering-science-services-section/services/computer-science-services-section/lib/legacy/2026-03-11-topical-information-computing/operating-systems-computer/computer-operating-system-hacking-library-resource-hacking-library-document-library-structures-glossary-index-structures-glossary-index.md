Named entity-forms the 561 Group recognizes — the categorically-distinct shapes an entity can instantiate, recorded as the group's structures.
They are *not* a hierarchy but a complex configuration: a single concrete entity may instantiate multiple forms simultaneously (e.g., emsenn is at once persona, personnel, officer; codi is at once persona and agent).

The catalogue is grown by inclusion, not parsimony — each form earns its place by bearing a categorically distinct predication-shape required for some specific step-induction in an endeavor that no other form's shape could carry.

## Originating structures of the predication category

The mathematical objects from which the predication category is built.
Derived from [`relationlessness-relates`](../../prose/learning/claims/relationlessness-relates.md) and downstream claims.

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| [relationlessness](relationlessness.md) | The would-be all-$\bot$ object; proven non-existent; the failed seed whose articulation forces the originating cell. |
| [void](void.md) | The no-evidence value $\bot$ in the bilattice fiber algebra; admissible cell-internal starting position. |
| [predication-cell](predication-cell.md) | The directed asymmetric triple $(s, p, o)$ the originating cell is and every cell inherits. |
| [predicate](predicate.md) | The typed coordinate $p \in \Pi$ of a cell. |
| [predicate-category](predicate-category.md) | $\mathbf{C}$, the small category of predicates with four canonical morphism classes. |
| [bilattice](bilattice.md) | The fiber algebra $A_p$ with knowledge-order $\leq_k$ and truth-order $\leq_t$, value set $\{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}, \bot, \top\}$. |

## POSIX layer

POSIX 1003.1 filesystem objects.
Every file the 561 Group's records live in is one of these.

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| [posix-file](posix-file.md) | The POSIX-file supertype — path plus inode plus type-bit-determined content layer. |
| [regular-file](regular-file.md) | `S_IFREG` specialization: bytes addressable by offset. |
| [directory](directory.md) | `S_IFDIR` specialization: a collection of (name, inode-number) entries. |
| [file-path](file-path.md) | The pathname-string address of a regular-file. |
| [directory-path](directory-path.md) | The pathname-string address of a directory. |

The other five POSIX specializations (symbolic-link, fifo, socket, character-device, block-device) are named in [posix-file](posix-file.md) but do not yet carry their own structure records — the 561 Group's records do not currently make load-bearing use of them.

## Resource layer

Categorical objects sitting on top of the POSIX layer.
A resource is a mathematical triple; a resource-file is a regular-file that realizes one; a resource-directory is the directory analogue.

| Structure | Role |
|---|---|
| [resource](resource.md) | The triple $(v^R, \iota, T)$ — abstract resource. |
| [resource-file](resource-file.md) | The pullback of regular-file and resource along a parse functor. |
| [resource-directory](resource-directory.md) | The pullback of directory and resource. |
| [markdown-resource-file](markdown-resource-file.md) | A resource-file whose content is YAML frontmatter plus a CommonMark body. |
| [directory-index](directory-index.md) | The `_index.md` marker file that gives a directory its own record-of-itself. |

## Agent-forms

Entity-forms that *act* on the cell graph — those required to induce next steps of an endeavor.

| Form | Distinguishing predication-shape | Step-induction |
|---|---|---|
| [entity](entity.md) | Bears predications. The genus. | (Base; not an agent-form alone.) |
| [boundary](boundary.md) | Holographic identity-surface; carries the four-fiber bundle (auth, authz, attention, history) | Session-open: a session opens *as a boundary* before any further form is selected |
| [character](character.md) | A nucleus χ on the Heyting algebra of the entity's fiber: idempotent, monotone, inflationary; distinct from σ (saturation) and Δ (transfer) | Closure-rule consultation: detects out-of-character actions before witnesses land |
| [persona](persona.md) | Persistent identity: `incarnated-by` over body-naturals, `accumulates-memory`, `has-character`; the body-politic that endures across sessions | Cross-session attribution; voice; multi-session continuity |
| [personnel](personnel.md) | Polity-relational: `member-of-polity`, `enrolled-at`, `subject-to-charter` | Charter-bound action: voting in governance; office-eligibility |
| [officer](officer.md) | Incumbency: `incumbent-of(persona, office, t-from, t-to?)` — a time-bounded relation-as-object | Office-bound dispatch and audit |
| [agent](agent.md) | Operational: `has-skill`, `dispatchable-against-work-type`, `produces-witness` | Dispatch-loop matching of work to capability |
| [body-natural](body-natural.md) | Ephemeral incarnation: `session-bounded-by`, `incarnates-persona`, `incurs-cost` | Actual runtime work execution; cost-bearing |
| [agent-community](agent-community.md) | Collective: `member-set`, `governing-soul-of-scope`, `governance-process` | Scope-level emission: writing the soul, charter changes |
| [polity](polity.md) | Charter-bound institutional collective: `charter`, `personnel-roster`, `recognition-cell` | Institutional acts: amending charter, recognition of new personnel |

## Adjacent structures (not agent-forms)

Things that *agent-forms relate to* but that don't themselves induce endeavor steps:

- **Office** — the role itself (a position with authority-cells); distinct from officer (incumbency) and from any holder.
  Currently lives at [`work/governance/staff/offices/`](../../work/governance/staff/offices/).
- **Witness** — the attribution event; bears `attributed-to`, `signed-by`, `at-tick`.
  The witness records *that* an act happened; the agent-form is the bearer of the act.
- **Skill** / **runbook** — what an agent invokes.
  Bound to agents via `has-skill`.
- **Duty** — the institutional obligation an officer or personnel bears.
- **Soul** — the operational-identity-constraint of a *scope* (analog of character at scope-level rather than entity-level).

## Candidates under study

These may or may not survive as named objects in their own right vs being absorbed as relations or quotients of the above:

- **Sender** — the message-attributed entity (may be a quotient of boundary or persona at the auth-fiber surface).
- **Voice** — the speech-act-channel (may be a quotient of persona at a publication-channel surface).
- **Familiar** — bound-to-individual companion-agent (may be an agent-relation rather than its own form).
- **Companion** — attending agent without independent dispatch (likewise).

Decisions on these survive in the corresponding object-record's `status:` (one of `pending-decision`, `survived`, `absorbed-into-X`).
