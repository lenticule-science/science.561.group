name: plain technical general american english
description: Text written in plain language style, in General American English, with technical precision retained.
---

A piece of plain technical general American English is text that satisfies three constraints at once:

- **Plain language**: common words, short sentences, active voice, direct statements.
  No jargon without definition.
  No padding.
- **Technical**: precise about technical content.
  Uses standard technical vocabulary.
  Defines non-standard terms on first use.
- **General American English**: American spelling (color, organize, defense).
  Avoids regional Americanisms (no "y'all").
  Uses ISO date formats (YYYY-MM-DD) for technical content.

Examples of the style: Unix manpages, plainlanguage.gov government documents, well-written API documentation.

Not the style: legalese, marketing copy, academic prose, casual speech, corporate-speak, AI-padded boilerplate.
