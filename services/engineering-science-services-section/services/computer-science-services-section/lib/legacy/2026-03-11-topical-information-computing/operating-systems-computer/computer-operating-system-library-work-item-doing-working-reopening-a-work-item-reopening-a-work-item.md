name: reopening-a-work-item

specializing: contesting-positing-morphism
---

A **reopening-a-work-item** call is the contesting positing that moves a work-item from done or abandoned to reopened.
The reopened state is one-shot — its only outgoing transition is to open — so reopening then forgetting cannot leave the work-item in a stale state.
