A **expedition** is a work-item whose has-kind cell carries the value "expedition".
Expeditions terminate either by being upgraded to a project, by being abandoned, or by yielding a decision.

Per [the work-item-as-predication-shape derivation](../../../../../../../../../../../../doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/project/library/bootstrapping-the-workflow-library/study-notes/work-item-as-predication-shape.md), expedition specializes [work-item](../../work-item.md) via predicate-specialization with the sub-support inclusion restricting to entities whose (e, has-kind, k) cell has k = "expedition".
