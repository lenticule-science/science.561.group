specializing: contesting-positing-morphism
---

A **completing-a-work-item** call is the contesting positing that moves a work-item from in-progress to review, delegating to [transitioning-a-work-items-state](../transitioning-a-work-items-state/transitioning-a-work-items-state.md).
The transition asserts the work is done from the doer's perspective; downstream approval (or change-request) decides whether it advances to done.
