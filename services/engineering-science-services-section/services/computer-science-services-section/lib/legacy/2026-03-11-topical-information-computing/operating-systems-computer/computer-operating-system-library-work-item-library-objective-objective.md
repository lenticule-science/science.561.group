A **objective** is a work-item whose has-kind cell carries the value "objective".
Objectives are at the top of the contributes-to chain; nothing contributes-to them above.

Per [the work-item-as-predication-shape derivation](../../../../../../../../../../../../doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/project/library/bootstrapping-the-workflow-library/study-notes/work-item-as-predication-shape.md), objective specializes [work-item](../../work-item.md) via predicate-specialization with the sub-support inclusion restricting to entities whose (e, has-kind, k) cell has k = "objective".
