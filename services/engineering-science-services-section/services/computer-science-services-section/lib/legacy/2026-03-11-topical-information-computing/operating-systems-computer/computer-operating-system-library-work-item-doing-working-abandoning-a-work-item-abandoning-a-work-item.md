---
name: abandoning-a-work-item
description: Transitions a work-item to abandoned from any non-done state. Denies the work will be completed.
specializing: denying-positing-morphism
---

An **abandoning-a-work-item** call is the denying positing that moves a work-item to abandoned.
Valid from any non-done state (open, in-progress, blocked, review, deferred).
Distinct from the other transitions in that it is a denying speech-act — asserting that the work will not be done — rather than contesting (replacing one state with another along the lifecycle path).
