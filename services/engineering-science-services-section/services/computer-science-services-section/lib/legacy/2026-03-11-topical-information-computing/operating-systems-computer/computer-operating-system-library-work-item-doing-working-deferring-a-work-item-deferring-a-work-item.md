---
name: deferring-a-work-item
description: Transitions a work-item from in-progress to deferred. Acknowledges the work is paused, not blocked or abandoned.
specializing: contesting-positing-morphism
---

A **deferring-a-work-item** call is the contesting positing that moves a work-item to deferred.
Distinct from blocking (which records a specific reason) and abandoning (which denies completion); deferring acknowledges the work is paused with the intention of resuming.
