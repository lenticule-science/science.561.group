name: requesting-review-of-a-work-item
description: Synonym for completing-a-work-item. Transitions in-progress to review when the framing is 'request a review' rather than 'I am done'.
specializing: contesting-positing-morphism
---

A **requesting-review-of-a-work-item** call is the same morphism as completing-a-work-item — both transition in-progress to review.
The two skills exist as different framings of the same act; the doer may prefer 'completing' (I am done) or 'requesting review' (please review) depending on context.
