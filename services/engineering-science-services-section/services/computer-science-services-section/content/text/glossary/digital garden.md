A digital garden is a term used to describe a [[personal website]] or [[online platform]] that serves as a collection of thoughts, ideas, and knowledge in a non-linear and evolving manner. It is a metaphorical representation of a real [[garden]], where ideas and information are cultivated, nurtured, and interconnected. Unlike traditional [[blogs]] or [[websites]], digital gardens are not organized chronologically or in a hierarchical structure. Instead, they focus on creating a network of interconnected ideas through hyperlinks, allowing visitors to explore and discover information in a non-linear way. Digital gardens often encourage collaboration, feedback, and continuous improvement, as they are designed to be living and evolving spaces for knowledge sharing.
