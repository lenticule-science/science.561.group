A ***multi-user dungeon*** (MUD, also defined as muti-user dimension or domain) is a virtual environment where multiple [users](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/user.md) [interact](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interacting.md) using a [command line interface](../../../../services/computer-systems-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/command-line-interface.md). MUDs are often [role-playing games](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/role-playing-game.md), so may be [described](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/describing.md) as [similar](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/similar.md) to [interactive fiction](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/interactive-fiction-story.md), [tabletop role-playing games](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tabletop-role-playing-game.md), and [online chat rooms](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/online-chat-room.md).

MUDs are a [predecessor](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/previous.md) to [massively-multiplayer online role-playing games](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/social-theory-science-services-section/services/ludics-science-services-section/services/game-ludics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/massively-multiplayer-online-role-playing-game.md) (MMORPGs).

# History

## Prehistory

- [1975ce](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1975.md), [Colossal Cave Adventure](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/colossal-cave-adventure.md) by [Will Crowther](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/will-crowther.md)
  - introduced [text-interface virtual environment](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text-interface-virtual-environment.md)
  - [Don Woods](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/don-woods.md) brought in interactive fiction
- [Zork](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/services/being-services-section/services/doing-services-section/services/playing-services-section/services/gaming-services-section/services/role-playing-games-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/zork.md)
- [ARPANET](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/arpanet.md)

## Origins

- late [1978ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1978ce.md), [Roy Trubshaw](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/roy-trubshaw.md), student at [University of Essex](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/university-of-essex.md)
  - began writing a software literally called MUD
  - on a [DEC PDP-10](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dec-pdp-10.md) using [MACRO-10](../../../../services/computer-code-science-services-section/services/programming-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/macro-10.md)
  - rewrote in in [BCPL](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bcpl.md), precursor to C programming language
  - named MUD for *Dungeon* a [FORTRAN](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fortran.md) fork of Zork
    - thus, not a genre name, but literally just, multi-user *Dungeon*
- In 1979, MUD1 was rewritten in MUDDL, MUD Definition Language, by Trubshaw and Bartle
- in [1980](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1980ce.md), Trubshaw graduated and handed project to [Richard Bartle](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/richard-bartle.md)
  - notable, 1980 is when Essex connected to ARPANET
- 1983: 2am to 7am, British Telecom's Packet Switch Stream allowed access to the PDP-10
- By 1984, MUD1 was hosted on the PDP-20 at Dundee College of Technology
  - also in 84, [CompuNet](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/compunet.md) licensed MUD1 for it's Commodore 64 network, where it ran until 1987
- 1987, [CompuServe](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/compuserve.md) relicensed under the name British Legends, and pressured Bartle to shut down the Essex instance of MUD1, which was done in October 1987
- [1989ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1989ce.md), [Lars Pensjö C](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lars-pensjo-c.md), developed by [Lars Pensjö](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lars-pensjo.md), [University of Gothenburg](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/university-of-gothenburg.md)
  - [object-oriented programming language](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/object-oriented-programming-language.md) for building [LPMUD](lpmud.md)
  - introduced [MUD engine](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mud-engine.md) and [MUD library](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mud-library.md) [bifurcation](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/bifurcated-classification.md).
- 1990-03-27, [Hans Henrik Stærfeldt](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hans-henrik-st-rfeldt.md) announced [DikuMUD](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dikumud.md) (DIKU = Datalogisk Institut Kobenhavns Universitet ([University of Copenhagen](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/university-of-copenhagen.md)))
- 1991-02-03, first public DikuMUD instance
