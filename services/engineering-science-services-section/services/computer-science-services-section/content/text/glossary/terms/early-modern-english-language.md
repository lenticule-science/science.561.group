***Early Modern English language*** was a [language](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/language.md) used in the [British Isles](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/british-isles.md) from approximately [1500ce](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1500ce.md) to the [late 1600s](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/late-17th-century.md). It followed [Middle English](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/middle-english-language.md) and came before [Modern English](modern-english-language.md).

**Early Modern English** maintained the [West Germanic grammar](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/west-germanic-grammar.md) of *Middle English* while [including](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md) extensive [Latin](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/latin-language.md) and [French](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/french-language.md) vocabulary, due to the [Renaissance](../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/renaissance.md) cementing earlier [Norman](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/norman-culture.md) influences. Spoken **Early Modern English** was shaped heavily by the [Great Vowel Shift](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/great-vowel-shift.md), while its written form was heavily shaped by the rise of [English nationalism](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/english-nationalism.md) and [William Caxton](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/william-caxton.md)'s efforts to [standardize](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/standardizing.md) the language through printing [texts](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/text.md) like [The Canterbury Tales](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/the-canterbury-tales.md).

The most famous works of Early Modern English are the works of [William Shakespeare](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/william-shakespeare.md), the [King James Bible](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/king-james-bible.md), and the texts of [William Cecil](../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/william-cecil.md), [King James I](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/king-james-i.md), and [Francis Bacon](../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/francis-bacon.md).
