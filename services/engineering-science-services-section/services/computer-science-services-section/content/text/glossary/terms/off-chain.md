An ***off-chain*** condition is one in which signal behavior is **not logged, resolved, or rendered legible by any shared infrastructure**. There is no ledger, no register, no protocolic memory.

In off-chain contexts:

- Meaning is generated through contradiction, context collapse, or social negotiation  
- Coherence is local, ephemeral, or absent  
- Memory is personal or distributed by rumor  
- Feedback may exist, but is untraceable

Off-chain does not mean “chaotic”—it means **non-systematized**.

It is the dominant condition in **social media discourse**, **vibespace**, **face-to-face subcultural meaning**, and **interpretive anarchy**.
