A digital computer is a [[tool]] for [[managing]] [[information]] and [[connections]]. It translates our [[thought|thoughts]] and [[command|commands]] into the vast [[digital universe]]. Through this [[relationship]], we expand our [[capability|capabilities]] to [[communicating|communicate]], [[learning|learn]], create [[virtual environment|virtual environments]] like [[Synthetic Place of Ritualized Ethnosis|SPOREs]], and [[zeds/Relationality/triage/00-probably-redundant/modules/relating|relate]] more fully with both the [[material universe]] around us and the intangible [[conceptual universe|realms of ideas]].
