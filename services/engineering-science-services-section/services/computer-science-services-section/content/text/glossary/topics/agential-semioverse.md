# Agential Semioverse

Agential Semioverse is the theory register of the agent-computing kernel (formerly relational-computing) spanning agency, language, games, knowledge, and society — carried in dated research papers, not runnable code. Its engineering counterpart, where the theory is implemented as TypeScript/Node packages, is agent-computer-system-services-section. See content/text/documents/articles/notes/ for the corpus.
