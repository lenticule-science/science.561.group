# animating ⊔ organizing: the exact diagonal/off-diagonal mirror

This sharpens [the holographic-bulk note](2026-06-21-animating-as-holographic-bulk.md) and [the social-scale-governing note](2026-06-21-organizing-as-social-scale-governing.md).
Both packages were already built and green; this session deepened them into an **exact carrier-by-carrier mirror** and made the spiral between them concrete.

## The one structure, read on and off the diagonal

The self-similar faculty quartet recurs up the agency spiral.
`animating` reads it on the **diagonal** — one agent, the holographic gluing of that agent's own three faculty-faces (communicating across ⊔ knowing inside ⊔ playing outside) into the interior agent.
`organizing` reads it **off the diagonal** — relations among many agents, the gluing of a sheaf of agents into one organization over three Gen-2 faces (socializing ⊔ computing ⊔ gaming).
The bridge is the corpus's social descent: the settled individual is "settled below, open above," carries its Simondon *associated milieu* of pre-individual potential, and re-enters `relating` among other agents, accumulating into community and settling into the institution.

**The mirror is the starting point, not the destination.** The off-diagonal is genuinely *richer* than the diagonal — a mere transposition would be a regression.
The sections below record where organizing exceeds animating, grounded in the deepest corpus theory (cross-arena Mayer–Vietoris, federation-as-parallel-composition, social-choice impossibility).
The two `glued` notions are NOT the same computation: animating's is the intrinsic single-filtration H¹ of one agent (always coordination-free — one self's faces cannot contest); organizing's is the cross-arena MV over a cover of *distinct* agents, where disagreement is tolerated and the obstruction is something the diagonal never meets.

Newly built this session, off the existing green base:

- organizing's **stage-1 `social-becoming`** and **stage-4 `institution`** carriers — the social descent was missing its first and fourth named readings; the corpus `individual.md` lists exactly `becoming → relating → community → institution`.
  The `institution` is the settled order the organization *is* (mirroring how the agent *is* its `governing` carrier); `adjudication` is its proper *act*, kept distinct (governing.md: "its proper acts are adjudication and knowledge").
- organizing's **`social-factorization`** (`socialFactor`) — the Gen-2 act-factorization, mirroring animating's `factor`, with the three Austin/Searle layers read as the **receiving dual** of speaking (locution = cover received, illocution = charges held, perlocution = record converged).
- a **`dual`** field made first-class on both factorizations (`producing` / `receiving`), making the speaking↔adjudication duality machine-readable.
- **`milieu`** on the organization (social-field cells not at the knowledge-top), the Gen-2 mirror of the agent's residual potential that drives `federate`.
- a **`dual`** field on both factorizations (`producing`/`receiving`), making the speaking↔adjudication duality machine-readable (carried as a *checkable, provisional* reading, not a settled citation — and note the per-face `socialFactor` arithmetic parallels the producing case; the genuine receiving structure lives in the cross-arena MV below, not in those counts).

## The off-diagonal is richer than the mirror (the load-bearing realization)

A pure transposition would conflate Gen-2 with a re-skin of Gen-1 — the very regression the corpus warns against.
Three deep corpus results show the off-diagonal carries structure the diagonal cannot, and organizing now realizes them:

1. **Disagreement is not the obstruction (cross-arena Mayer–Vietoris).** The corpus's `cross-arena-mayer-vietoris.md` decomposes a federated cover three ways: *shared* (overlap-kernel, agreements that lift free), *contested* (disagreement-pairs on overlaps — these live in **ker δ**, are recorded as ⊤ and adjudicated *locally*, and contribute **no** federation-level H¹), and *federation-only* = **coker(diff)**: content no single constituent can express, the **only** thing the federation apparatus is invoked for.
  "Federations tolerate large amounts of inter-polity disagreement structurally."
  So organizing's `glued` is now `federationOnly == 0`, **independent of `contested`** — a society can be fully glued yet deeply contested.
  This corrects the naive reading (built first this session) that treated the merged H¹ as a single obstruction conflating disagreement with non-gluing.
  Computed faithfully in `crossArena` from the per-constituent settled sections: `federationOnly` = cells the merge settles that no constituent settles alone.
2. **Federation is parallel composition ‖ with name-passing.** `federation-is-parallel- composition.md`: federation IS the monoidal product `‖` in CGS (the *-autonomous category of concurrent games with symmetry); entity-mobility — a member of two polities — is nominal-set **name-passing** under FSym(𝔸).
  `federate(covers)` realizes this: it ‖-composes constituent societies, counts `nameMobility` (members shared across constituents), and reports the cross-arena MV.
  The diagonal has neither a parallel product nor a name-permutation symmetry — a single agent is not a ‖ of anything.
3. **The off-diagonal is the home of coordination impossibility.** `social-choice.md` / `why-multi-agent-coordination-is-hard.md`: Arrow, Gibbard–Satterthwaite, Condorcet cycles, the coordinated-attack (common-knowledge) impossibility.
  So the consensus cost adjudication reports can be **irreducible** — not "more consensus needed" but a structural impossibility no rule removes.
  The diagonal *always* glues (holographic, coordination-free); only off the diagonal can gluing fail irreducibly.

## The sharpening: adjudication is the off-diagonal cost

The one genuine asymmetry — organizing has `adjudicate`, animating does not — is now *derived*, not stipulated.
By the holographic principle the interior is a projection of its boundary; on the **diagonal** the three faces are *one agent's own* faculties, so they cannot contest — their gluing is coordination-free (H¹=0 by construction, the corpus's CRDT corollary).
Off the **diagonal**, the faces are *distinct agents'* contributions, which genuinely conflict; forming one record then requires the **receiving dual of speaking** — adjudication — to hold conflict as `contested` (Belnap), partial as `open`, and accumulate `monotone`ly.
So adjudication is exactly the coordination cost that appears when the quartet moves off the diagonal: H¹ over the social cover is the consensus cost.
This is why `factor.dual = "producing"` has no adjudication sibling on animating, while organizing's `socialFactor.dual = "receiving"` does.

## The concrete spiral

`organizing.assemble(agents)` makes the spiral literal: it takes real `@emsenn/animating` `Agent` carriers and organizes them, so an animating agent *is* a member of organizing's social base.
Each settled individual re-enters the social descent by contributing `id|individuated` — byte-for-byte what `animating.reseed` emits — plus its standing concern `id|goalPredicate`, so agents sharing a concern are bonded through that predicate cell (Simondon's transindividual field emerging from shared potential).
Gen-1 outputs become Gen-2 inputs; the diagonal feeds the off-diagonal.
Crucially `assemble` glues the agents as a genuine *multi-constituent* cross-arena cover (each agent its own arena), not a single merge — so agreement lifts free, disagreement is tolerated, and only collectively-novel concerns (federation-only content) obstruct.

## What remains

The federation-only obstruction is computed EXACTLY from the proved settling-cohomology: `federationOnly` = the merged settlement's degree-1 cohomology generators (non-Scott / limit-stage firings, via `readFirstCohomologyOfSettlementFiltration`) whose cell carries no constituent's own H¹ generator — the obstruction the parallel composition introduces.
This also vindicates "disagreement is tolerated" homologically: a finite ⊤-conflict settles at a finite stage, never a limit-stage firing, so it never appears as an H¹ generator (it lives in ker δ).
The overlap facts `shared`/`contested`/`nameMobility` remain combinatorial readings of the per-constituent sections (they are overlap-agreement and name-mobility facts, not cohomology).
`federate` composes constituent societies given as their boundaries because an `Organization` carrier does not expose its membrane — org-level ‖ from carriers alone awaits that surface.
The face↔bilattice-axis correspondence (communicating↔truth etc.) remains the corpus's open agentive triad (`axis` marked provisional).
The thick per-face L/I/P × Belnap × closure-status structure and the σ-meta symmetry for the knowing/computing (learning) face are summarized, not yet realized; likewise the multi-port cospan composition primitive the holographic-bulk note calls for.
