# Non-destructive append-summary compaction realizes the cosettling equivalence class in the materialization image

**Claim.** Let $L$ be a substrate [event log](../objects/event-log.md) producing closure-fixed-point $w = T^*(L)$ under the substrate's closure operator $T$. Let $L_{[J,K]}$ denote the prefix from ordinal $J$ through $K$ inclusive, with intermediate fixed-point $w_K = T^*(L_{[0,K]})$. Define a *non-destructive append-summary compaction* of the prefix $L_{[J,K]}$ as the operation that **appends a single new event $C$** at ordinal $K+1$ whose body holds, for each $(s, p, o, \sigma)$ triple emitted in $L_{[J,K]}$, the cell atom from the latest emitting event in that prefix (one entry per stratum), and which leaves every event in $L_{[0,K]}$ in place.

Let $s_{J-1} := T^*(L_{[0,J-1]})$ denote the substrate's pre-prefix materialization state (empty when $J = 0$; the body of the immediately preceding compact event when $J > 0$, by induction over compaction tiles). Define the *compaction representative* as the cell-set
$$v_C \;:=\; s_{J-1} \,\cup\, C.\text{body}.$$
This is the cell-set the seeded replay starts from when entering the suffix at ord $K+1$ — exactly the on-disk encoding $\{s_{J-1}, C\}$ unioned through the materialization functor.

Then the resulting log $L' = L_{[0,K]} \mathbin\Vert C \mathbin\Vert L_{[K+1,\,\text{head}]}$ satisfies:

1. **Cosettling-class membership.** $v_C$ is a representative element of the cosettling equivalence class
$$T^{*-1}(w_K) \;=\; \{v : \text{seed}(L_{[0,K]}) \subseteq v \subseteq w_K\}$$
witnessed by [iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells.md). Equivalently, $T^*(v_C) = w_K$ — closing the seeded-replay starting state recovers the prefix's fixed point.

2. **Materialization-equivalence.** The fiber-materialization that, on encountering $C$, seeds from $v_C$ and replays only events strictly after $C$ produces a state byte-identical to the from-genesis materialization of $L$.

3. **Strict refinement of the log-rewriting form.** The append-summary form is in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ *and* additionally preserves $L_{[0,K]}$ as on-disk bytes. Per [compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class](compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class.md), this is admissible: the cosettling theorem permits any representative; it does not require the log itself to be rewritten. Append-summary compaction is the realization of that freedom that additionally retains the prefix bytes.

The append-summary spec and the log-rewriting spec are therefore not competing designs; they are two points on the same cosettling-class-representative axis. The append-summary form picks a high-information representative $v_C$ via the on-disk encoding $\{s_{J-1}, C\}$ and additionally preserves prefix bytes. The log-rewriting form picks any representative $v \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$, typically lower-information, and discards the prefix bytes. Both produce the same closure-fixed-point and therefore the same settled state.

Two specializations of $v_C$ are worth naming:

- **$J = 0$ (genesis-rooted prefix).** Then $s_{J-1} = \varnothing$, so $v_C = C.\text{body}$. The unseeded statement $C.\text{body} \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$ holds directly.
- **$J > 0$ with σ/Δ-emission-completeness** (the dev-spec setting where σ-pass and Δ-pass each emit a cell event for every derived cell they produce, per [`stratified-facts.md`](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/stratified-facts.md) §3 and [`sigma-delta-passes.md`](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/sigma-delta-passes.md) §2). Then every cell of $w_K$ has at least one emission in $L_{[0,K]}$ — either pre-prefix (folded into $s_{J-1}$ by induction) or in-prefix (with its latest-per-triple representative in $C.\text{body}$). In this setting $v_C = w_K$ pointwise, the upper-bound representative.

## Why this holds

### Part 1: $v_C$ lies in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$

Per [iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells.md), $T^{*-1}(w_K) = \{v : \text{seed}(L_{[0,K]}) \subseteq v \subseteq w_K\}$ in the strict regime, with the Moore-family generalization per [iterated-cosettling-via-moore-family](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-via-moore-family.md) for non-strict regimes. We show membership of $v_C$ as a cell-information set in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$, equivalently $T^*(v_C) = w_K$.

The body $C.\text{body}$ holds the latest cell atom per $(s, p, o, \sigma)$ triple emitted in $L_{[J,K]}$. Per [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md), each successive cell at the same $(s, p, o, \sigma)$ joins onto the prior under $\vee_k$ in the [knowledge order](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md); the latest-per-triple-per-stratum cell is therefore the running-join representative for that triple — pointwise $\ge_k$ every prior emission at the same triple.

We need three facts:

- **$\text{seed}(L_{[0,K]}) \subseteq v_C$.** A seed cell of $L_{[0,K]}$ is one with no incoming closure rule in $T$ — necessarily an asserted cell, authored directly. If the seed cell was first emitted in $L_{[0,J-1]}$, it is in $s_{J-1} = T^*(L_{[0,J-1]})$ by inflation, hence in $v_C$. If first emitted in $L_{[J,K]}$, the latest-per-triple-per-stratum selection at that asserted triple yields a cell whose value is the join under $\vee_k$ of every emission at that triple in the prefix; by settling-monotonicity this cell is $\ge_k$ the original seed value at that asserted triple. Two structural notes for this case: (i) at the asserted layer, the seed *triple* is preserved (the latest summary entry at the seed's $(s,p,o,\text{asserted})$ exists in $C.\text{body}$ because the seed itself is one of the prefix emissions at that triple); (ii) per settling-monotonicity in $\le_k$, the latest cell carries an information value that is $\ge_k$ the seed value, so the cosettling-class lower-bound condition (the seed's information is present) is preserved through the running join. Hence $\text{seed}(L_{[0,K]}) \subseteq v_C$ as cell-information sets ordered by $\le_k$.

- **$v_C \subseteq w_K$.** Every cell in $s_{J-1}$ is in $w_K$ by closure-monotonicity on the prefix split $L_{[0,K]} = L_{[0,J-1]} \mathbin\Vert L_{[J,K]}$. Every cell in $C.\text{body}$ was emitted in some event in $L_{[J,K]}$; per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md), $w_K = T^*(L_{[0,K]})$ contains every cell that any event in $L_{[0,K]}$ deposited into the materialization image. Hence each summary cell is in $w_K$, and $v_C = s_{J-1} \cup C.\text{body} \subseteq w_K$.

- **$T^*(v_C) \supseteq w_K$.** Let $v_*$ denote the cell-information set of *all* emissions across $L_{[0,K]}$ (every cell ever emitted, with multiplicity at the same triple flattened to set membership). Then $T^*(v_*) \supseteq w_K$ — directly, since $v_*$ is a superset of the seed of $L_{[0,K]}$ and $T^*$ is monotone in set inclusion.

  We now show $T^*(v_C) \supseteq T^*(v_*)$. Both $v_*$ and $v_C$ are cell-sets indexed by $(s,p,o,\sigma)$ triples. At each triple $\tau$, write $v_*(\tau)$ for the cells at $\tau$ in $v_*$ and $v_C(\tau)$ for the cells at $\tau$ in $v_C$. For triples that appeared pre-prefix only, $v_*(\tau) \subseteq s_{J-1}(\tau) = v_C(\tau)$. For triples that appeared in-prefix, $v_C(\tau)$ is a singleton holding the latest-per-stratum cell, which by [settling-monotonicity](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/settling-monotonicity.md) is $\ge_k$ every cell in $v_*(\tau)$ — i.e., the join $\bigvee_k v_*(\tau)$. The substrate's closure $T$ is monotone in $\le_k$ at each triple (per [predication-valuation](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-valuation.md): closure rules join the bilattice value at each triple under $\vee_k$, and $\vee_k$ is monotone in each argument). Therefore for any cell-set $u$ obtainable from $v_*$ by closure, the corresponding cell-set $u'$ obtainable from $v_C$ by closure satisfies $u'(\tau) \ge_k u(\tau)$ at every triple. Lifting to the lattice of cell-information sets: every cell of $u$ is dominated under $\le_k$ by a cell of $u'$ at the same triple, so $u \subseteq u'$ as cell-information sets (presence of a $\ge_k$-larger cell at $\tau$ in $u'$ entails presence of $u$'s cell at $\tau$ in $u'$ under information-set membership ordered by $\le_k$). Iterating to the fixed point: $T^*(v_C) \supseteq T^*(v_*) \supseteq w_K$.

  The dev-spec's stratum-preservation discipline (see *Bridge to log-level representatives* below) is what makes this dominance hold per stratum independently — collapsing strata at the summary boundary would lose information at the asserted layer that the saturated layer's `derived_from` chain depends on, so the running-join argument applies only when run per stratum.

Combining the three: $\text{seed}(L_{[0,K]}) \subseteq v_C \subseteq w_K$ and $T^*(v_C) \supseteq w_K$, with $T^*(v_C) \subseteq T^*(w_K) = w_K$ by closure-stability of $w_K$ and the second inclusion. Therefore $T^*(v_C) = w_K$, equivalently $v_C \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$.

In the substrate setting where σ/Δ emit every derived cell as an event (the dev-spec setting per [`stratified-facts.md`](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/stratified-facts.md) §3 and [`sigma-delta-passes.md`](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/sigma-delta-passes.md) §2), the inclusion sharpens to $v_C = w_K$ pointwise: every cell of $w_K$ corresponds to at least one emission, either in $L_{[0,J-1]}$ (folded into $s_{J-1}$) or in $L_{[J,K]}$ (with its latest-per-triple representative in $C.\text{body}$). In the more general setting where some derived cells are held in fiber without an emission, equality weakens to $T^*(v_C) = w_K$ — still cosettling-class membership, just at a lower point in the interval $[\text{seed}, w_K]$.

### Part 1.5: Bridge to log-level representatives

The existing [compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class](compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class.md) is stated at the *log-trace* level: representatives in $T^{*-1}(w)$ are subsets of the log's emitted-cell content, and a compaction selects one such subset to encode as the rewritten log. The append-summary form operates at the *materialization-image* level: the representative is encoded as a single event's body unioned with the pre-prefix materialization state, separate from the prefix events themselves, and the substrate's fiber-reconcile reads the union as a seed.

The bridge is an *engineering bridge lemma*, not an asserted categorical identity: under the hypotheses
(i) every event in $L$ has a body whose cells lie at well-defined $(s,p,o,\sigma)$ triples per [stratified-facts.md](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/stratified-facts.md) §1, and (ii) the substrate's $T$ acts by closure of the union of all event bodies (no cross-event-body interaction beyond union, per the engine discipline of the dev spec),

define the materialization map $M$ on a log by $M(L) := \bigcup_{e \in L} \text{body}(e)$ — the set-union over event bodies. Then $T^*(M(L)) = T^*(L)$ by hypothesis (ii), so any log-level representative $L'$ satisfying $T^*(L') = T^*(L)$ produces a cell-set $M(L')$ satisfying $T^*(M(L')) = T^*(M(L))$. Conversely, any cell-set $v \in T^{*-1}(w)$ admits an encoding as an event-body collection whose set-union is $v$ — the append-summary form picks the singleton-body encoding $\{C\}$ with $\text{body}(C) = v \setminus s_{J-1}$, leaving $s_{J-1}$ encoded by retained prior events.

Under these hypotheses, the cosettling theorem applies indistinguishably to log-level and materialization-image representatives: any $v \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$ is admissible regardless of whether $v$ is realized by rewriting prefix events (log-side) or by appending a summary event whose body unions with the carried-forward $s_{J-1}$ (materialization-side). The append-summary spec picks the materialization-side realization; the log-rewriting spec picks the log-side realization; both produce the same $w_K$.

When hypothesis (ii) fails — e.g., when a future engine variant introduces cross-event-body interactions like settlement-only-on-checkpoint — the bridge weakens, and the two realizations may not commute through $T^*$. The dev-spec engine satisfies (ii) by construction (each `respond` invocation closes its emitted cells under σ/Δ before returning), so the bridge holds for v0.2a and is the ratification path for the spec. Future-engine reconciliation is out of scope here; that is what would make this lemma into a full categorical statement.

### Part 2: Materialization-equivalence

Let $\mathcal{M}_{\text{full}}(L')$ denote from-genesis materialization of $L'$ (walk every event from ord $0$ onward), and $\mathcal{M}_{\text{seeded}}(L')$ denote seeded materialization (encountering $C$, seed from $v_C = s_{J-1} \cup C.\text{body}$ and replay only events at ord $> K+1$).

Per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md), substrate materialization is the closure 2-monad's iteration to fixed point. Per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), the closure 2-monad $\mathrm{Cl}$ is lax-idempotent: $\mathrm{Cl}^2 \cong \mathrm{Cl}$. So applying closure to a value already in the closure's image is a no-op.

For $\mathcal{M}_{\text{full}}(L')$: the materialization walks $L_{[0,K]}$ producing intermediate state $w_K$ at the end of the prefix, then encounters $C$ — but in this path $C$ is treated as a no-op event for materialization (its body holds cells already derivable from the prefix; per Part 1, $T^*(v_C) = w_K$ and the union $w_K \cup C.\text{body} = w_K$ in the dev-spec setting where σ/Δ emit completeness, or $\subseteq w_K$ generally with closure recovering equality). The walk continues with $L_{[K+1,\,\text{head}]}$ from state $w_K$, producing final state $w'$.

For $\mathcal{M}_{\text{seeded}}(L')$: the materialization encounters $C$ first, seeds the state to $v_C$, takes one closure step to obtain $T^*(v_C) = w_K$ per Part 1, and replays $L_{[K+1,\,\text{head}]}$ from state $w_K$, producing final state $w''$. Per lax-idempotence, the closure step is folded into the suffix's first σ/Δ pass without effect on the final state — equivalently, the suffix replay is a closure iteration starting from a state in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$, which converges to the same fixed point as starting from $w_K$ directly.

Both paths apply the same suffix replay $L_{[K+1,\,\text{head}]}$ to the same effective starting state $w_K$. Per [settlement-determinism](settlement-determinism.md) (the substrate's closure is functional in $L \mapsto T^*(L)$), $w' = w''$ byte-for-byte. Per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), byte-equality implies hash-equality on every cell atom and every fiber-snapshot file.

The materialization-equivalence holds for every projection cursor in the suffix and for every per-stratum / per-peer / per-session projection of the resulting fiber.

### Part 3: Strict refinement of the log-rewriting form

The log-rewriting form per [compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class](compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class.md) selects some $v \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$ and **replaces** $L_{[J,K]}$ on disk with a shorter representative encoding $v$. The cosettling theorem guarantees materialization-equivalence regardless of which $v$ is chosen.

The append-summary form chooses $v_C = s_{J-1} \cup C.\text{body}$ (a high-information representative; the upper bound $w_K$ in the dev-spec emission-complete setting) and additionally retains $L_{[J,K]}$ as on-disk bytes. The cosettling theorem does not forbid this — it constrains the materialization image, not the bytes-on-disk encoding.

Two structural consequences follow:

- **Audit-trail preservation.** Per [witness](../objects/witness.md), every operator's pre/post-image hashes are auditable. Log-rewriting compaction discards the witness chain through $L_{[J,K]}$ (the intermediate cells are gone). Append-summary compaction preserves it: every original witness in $L_{[J,K]}$ remains addressable by ordinal and by id. An auditor asking "what was true at ord $K-1$" walks $L_{[0,K-1]}$ unchanged.
- **Recovery-determinism preservation.** Per [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md), the substrate's recovery primitive is byte-identical replay from the event log. Append-summary compaction does not modify any prior event's `prev` or `id`, so every recovery cursor that was valid pre-compaction remains valid post-compaction. Log-rewriting compaction invalidates every recovery cursor whose target ordinal lies in $[J, K]$.

The append-summary form is therefore *strictly stronger* than what the cosettling theorem requires: it satisfies the materialization-equivalence the theorem demands and additionally satisfies two audit-side invariants the theorem is silent on.

## Engineering consequence

- **The dev-spec design is theory-conformant.** The `compact` event spec described at [`dev/specs/kernel/compaction.md`](../../../../../../../../../../operations/engineering/tools/predication-computer/pypredcomp/docs/dev/specs/kernel/compaction.md) — non-destructive, append-only, with body holding latest-per-stratum summary cells — is the materialization-image realization of [compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class](compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class.md). Reconciliation: the two designs differ in *which representative of $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ they materialize* and *whether they additionally preserve the prefix bytes*; both produce equivalent settled states by the cosettling-class membership of the chosen representative.

- **Stratum-preserving discipline is structurally required.** Per Part 1, the representative $C.\text{body}$ must hold one summary entry per $(s, p, o, \sigma)$ triple, not one per $(s, p, o)$ collapsed across strata. A flat-summarized body picks a representative *outside* $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ for the per-stratum projection $\pi_\sigma : w \mapsto w|_\sigma$, since the closure-fixed-point lives in the stratum-indexed presheaf and a flat collapse loses the per-stratum component. The dev spec's stratum-preservation rule is the engineering enforcement of cosettling-class membership at each stratum independently.

- **Compaction tiles compose.** Two adjacent compactions $C_1$ over $[J_1, K_1]$ and $C_2$ over $[J_2, K_2]$ with $J_2 = K_1 + 1$ each materialize a representative $v_{C_1} \in T^{*-1}(w_{K_1})$ and $v_{C_2} \in T^{*-1}(w_{K_2})$ for their prefixes. By induction, $s_{J_2 - 1} = w_{K_1} = T^*(v_{C_1})$, so $v_{C_2} = s_{J_2-1} \cup C_2.\text{body}$ is well-defined and Part 2's materialization-equivalence applies. Seeding from $C_2$ and replaying the suffix produces the same state as seeding from $C_1$ then walking $L_{[K_1+1, K_2]}$ then $C_2$ then the suffix. Per lax-idempotence, recompaction over $[J_1, K_2]$ producing a single $C_{12}$ with $v_{C_{12}} \in T^{*-1}(w_{K_2})$ is equivalent. The tile-without-gap discipline of the dev spec is the structural condition that guarantees adjacent representatives compose into a single equivalence-class representative for the union prefix.

- **The summary-cells root hash is a witness on the equivalence-class membership.** The dev spec's `summary_cells_root_hash = sha256(canonical(sorted(summary_cells)))` is structurally a hash on the in-prefix component $C.\text{body}$ of the chosen representative $v_C$. Per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), byte-identical compaction bodies produce byte-identical root hashes. Two substrates compacting the same prefix from the same $s_{J-1}$ independently must produce the same root; the conformance gate `PCC_COMPACT_SUMMARY_ROOT_DIVERGED` is the structural verification that both selected the same body for the in-prefix component of $v_C$.

- **Snapshots and compactions remain orthogonal.** Per [snapshot](../objects/snapshot.md), a snapshot caches $T^*(L)$ as root hashes (no cell content); per this theorem, append-summary compaction caches the *cells* of $T^*$ restricted to a prefix. They compose without conflict: a substrate that records both gets fast verification (snapshot) and fast materialization (compact), and neither invalidates the other under further compaction because both operate within $T^{*-1}(w)$.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Compaction summaries that omit a stratum.** A compact event whose body lacks the asserted stratum entry for some $(s, p, o)$ that was emitted at the asserted stratum in the prefix is not in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ for the per-stratum projection. The substrate refuses; the dev-spec error `PCC_COMPACT_SUMMARY_INCOMPLETE` is the structural enforcement.

- **Compaction summaries that include cells outside $w_K$.** A compact event whose body includes a cell that was *not* derivable from the prefix violates the cosettling upper-bound condition $C.\text{body} \subseteq w_K$ (the rightmost inclusion of $T^{*-1}(w_K) = [\text{seed}, w_K]$). Such a representative produces a materialization different from $T^*(L)$ — the seeded replay starts from a state $> w_K$ in $\le_k$ and the suffix replay produces $w'' \ne w'$. The substrate refuses; the dev-spec error `PCC_COMPACT_REPLAY_DIVERGED` is the structural enforcement at the conformance gate.

- **Compaction over a non-tiled overlap.** Two compactions whose prefix ranges overlap select two representatives in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ that disagree on the overlap region. Seeded materialization from the later one would shadow the earlier one's representative, but the from-genesis walk would apply both. Per Part 2's materialization-equivalence, the two paths agree only when each compact event's $v_C$ lies in $T^{*-1}(w_{K_i})$ for *its* prefix and the prefixes tile without overlap. The dev-spec errors `PCC_COMPACT_GAP` and `PCC_COMPACT_OVERLAP` enforce the tile-without-gap-or-overlap discipline this theorem requires.

- **Compaction summaries with stale `source_event_id`.** The dev spec's `summary_cells[i].source_event_id` field cites the latest emitting event for the triple. A summary whose `source_event_id` cites a non-latest event is in $T^{*-1}(w_K)$ as a cell value (the value still lies in $[\text{seed}, w_K]$ under settling-monotonicity), but the witness chain through `derived_from` is stale. Per [witness](../objects/witness.md)'s invariant that `derived_from` cites the immediate causal predecessor, the substrate refuses; `PCC_COMPACT_SUMMARY_NON_LATEST` is the structural enforcement of witness-chain coherence under compaction.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's closure operator is in a regime admitting cosettling equivalence classes (strict per [iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-counts-derivable-cells.md), or non-strict via the Moore-family generalization per [iterated-cosettling-via-moore-family](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/iterated-cosettling-via-moore-family.md)).
- Cell atoms are content-addressed by canonical-form hash per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), so latest-per-triple selection is deterministic across implementations.
- The settling order respects $\le_k$ on the [knowledge order](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/knowledge-order.md), so the latest-per-stratum cell is the lossless running-join representative for its triple.
- The event log is preserved per [source-of-truth](teaching/explanations/source-of-truth.md): non-destructive compaction is the design point that makes audit-trail preservation a substrate invariant rather than a policy choice.

## Position

This theorem reconciles the [compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class](compaction-via-cosettling-equivalence-class.md) result — which permits any representative of the equivalence class, including log-rewriting forms — with the engineering requirement that the substrate's audit trail and recovery cursors remain valid across compaction. The append-summary realization is the materialization-functor specialization that picks a high-information representative $v_C \in T^{*-1}(w_K)$ (the upper bound $w_K$ in the dev-spec emission-complete setting) and additionally preserves the underlying event bytes; the log-rewriting realization is the event-trace specialization that picks a smaller representative and discards the prefix bytes. Both are admissible under the cosettling theorem; the substrate's choice between them is an audit-policy decision, not a correctness decision.

For the predication-computing engineer, the practical reading is: implementing compaction as `append a compact event whose body is one summary cell per (s, p, o, stratum) triple, keep the prefix events on disk, replay-from-seed at the compact event during fiber reconcile` is theory-conformant. The cosettling theorem licenses it; this theorem identifies it as the maximal-information audit-preserving point on the cosettling-representative axis.

The pattern composes with [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md) (recovery cursors remain valid because no prior event is mutated), with [cache-coherence-via-functorial-projection](cache-coherence-via-functorial-projection.md) (per-stratum projections of the post-compaction fiber are byte-identical to pre-compaction projections at the same cursor), with [snapshot](../objects/snapshot.md) (snapshots over the post-compaction log verify against the same root hashes as snapshots over the pre-compaction log, since both materialize to $w_K$ at ord $K$), and with [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md) (peers exchanging compacted prefixes converge to the same fixed point as peers exchanging full prefixes, because both encode the same equivalence-class representative in the materialization image).
