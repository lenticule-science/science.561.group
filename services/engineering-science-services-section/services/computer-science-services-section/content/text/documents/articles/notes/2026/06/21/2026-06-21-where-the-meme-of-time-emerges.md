# Where the meme of time emerges

Companion to [the calendar survey](2026-06-21-calendars-and-timekeeping-in-the-corpus.md).
That note answered *what calendar-time is* (a recurrent practice; cadence boundaries as settling stages).
This one answers the prior question: **where, in `relating → (knowing | communicating | playing) → animating → (computing | socializing | gaming) → organizing`, does the *sense of time as a meme* first emerge?**

The answer turns entirely on one distinction the corpus draws but rarely states.

## Two times: only one is a meme

The corpus carries time at two levels, and the question only resolves once they are split:

1. **Index-time** — the settling-depth $\lambda$, the **history / Mazurkiewicz trace monoid** $T$ (`knowledge-science/archive/terms/history.md`).
  This is *the order in which closure fires*: the bare prefix-order of events under true concurrency (independent steps commute; the extension diamond).
  It lives at **relating / settling itself, before any faculty.** Crucially it is **not a meme** — it is the base category the meme-presheaf is fibered *over*, the *form* of unfolding rather than a content.
  In Kant's terms it is the form of intuition, not an intuited object.
  So index-time does not *emerge* anywhere in the nine; it is the substrate's own shape, presupposed by all nine.

2. **Meme-time** — time *re-presented as carried content*: memory, the felt now, duration, the calendar.
  This is what "the sense of time as a meme" names, and it has a precise birthplace.

The whole question reduces to: **where does the index get folded back into a fiber?** Where does the system stop merely *being* in time and start *carrying* time as content.
That fold is reflexivity, and reflexivity has an exact home.

## The fold is at knowing: "the depth is the memory"

**knowing** is the comonad on the meme ([knowing = comonad on Meme](2026-06-21-knowing-as-comonad-on-meme.md)), and its **context is the held settling-depth**.
The note states it directly: `remember` = **"the depth is the memory"** — the comonad context, the join of the settling orbit so far — and it names the whole structure the **"spatiotemporal knowledge presheaf."**

That is the emergence. **Memory is the first time-meme**: the temporal stage (index) re-presented as content the agent holds.
The mechanism is the comonad's two operations:

- `know` $= \varepsilon$ (**extract**) — read the present value out of its context: *the now as content.*
- `reflect` $= \delta$ (**duplicate**) — a meme whose object is a prior meme, *knowing-that-you-know*: the **layering of nows**, recursive temporal depth — the structure of remembered-and-anticipated time.

So if "sense of time as a meme" means *represented* time — time the system can carry, recall, reflect on — **knowing is the answer.** It is the only faculty whose definition is folding the settling-index into held content.
(Its dual, communicating, presents the *monad* / free repetition — the `!
`; knowing presents the **comonad** / cofree context-carrying orbit.
The monad↔comonad pair on the one meme carrier is the precise home of the difference.)

## The three faces are three moments of the temporal meme

knowing alone gives retention.
The full sense of time is tripartite, and the corpus's own role-split — *across / inside / outside* the agent boundary; *monad / comonad / \*-autonomous* — hands the three moments cleanly.
(The labelling below is a reading built on those role-assignments, not a phrase lifted from the files.)

| Face | Position / structure | Temporal moment |
|---|---|---|
| **communicating** | across; `End(Meme)`, free monad / `!`, "where iteration enters" | **succession** — the transmissible, repeatable "and-then"; proto-tense |
| **knowing** | inside; the comonad | **retention** — the held just-past; "the depth is the memory" |
| **playing** | outside; \*-autonomous + cut, the ludics chronicle | **protention / agon** — lived interactive move-time, the *not-yet* of the response |

This is, almost exactly, Husserl's three moments of internal time-consciousness (retention / primal-impression / protention) — offered as illumination, **not** as corpus content.

## animating binds them into the lived present

The binder is where the three fuse into a *lived now*.
An agent is the perceive→act loop that is **"settled below, open above"** ([animating = agency descent](2026-06-21-animating-as-agency-descent.md)), and that phrase **is** the arrow of time as experienced:

- **settled below** = the monotone, non-retractable past — the prefix; settling never loses knowledge ($v_{\tau_n} \leq_k \Sigma_R(v_{\tau_n})$).
- **open above** = the undetermined future — the unsettled frontier.

So **the agent literally *is* a now**: the moving boundary dividing fixed-past from open-future.
Two faculties, two jobs:

- **knowing** makes time *representable* — the comonad fold: memory.
- **animating** makes time *lived* — the agent-frontier: the present, the arrow.

If one word is wanted for "the sense of time," it is **knowing, bound at animating**: knowing constructs the temporal content; animating makes it a self-locating present.

## Scale 2: the temporal meme becomes clock and calendar

When the agent re-seeds relating and the *memes become agents*, "time as a meme" lifts into "time as a *shared, reckoned* object" — the calendar.
This is the loop back to the [calendar survey](2026-06-21-calendars-and-timekeeping-in-the-corpus.md):

| Scale-2 faculty | Lifts (scale-1) | Temporal meme produced |
|---|---|---|
| **socializing** | communicating / succession | the **shared calendar** — many private nows reconciled into one coordinate ("next Tuesday means the same to us both"); synchronization |
| **computing** | knowing / retention | time **as data** — the recorded/replayable trace, the temporal-2-category, the regime/$H^1$ classification; "computation = settling." The audit log is collective remembrance made formal |
| **gaming** | playing / protention | **cadence as nested lusory magic circles** — bounded social time multiple agents consent into (the sprint, the season) |
| **organizing** | animating / lived present | the **collective now** — the operations loop, governance cadence; tradition (settled past) reaching toward the function-as-aperiodic-settling (open future): the institution's own "settled below, open above" |

The temporal meme therefore **spirals**: born as **memory at knowing**, lived as **the present at animating**, socialized as **the calendar at the computing/socializing/gaming trio under organizing**.

## Bottom line

- Bare order → **relating** (the history/trace monoid; *not* a meme — the index).
- *Represented* time-sense (memory, reflection) → **knowing**, the comonad that folds the settling-depth into held content.
  This is the literal answer to "time *as a meme*."
- *Lived* present / the arrow → **animating**, the agent as the settled-below / open-above frontier.
- *Clock and calendar* (explicit, shared reckoning) → the scale-2 lift: **computing** (trace/data) and **socializing** (shared coordinate), bounded by **gaming** and bound into the collective present by **organizing**.

One line: **the meme of time is born at knowing as memory ("the depth is the memory"), becomes a lived now when animating binds it into the agent-frontier, and becomes the calendar when the scale-2 faculties socialize that now across many agents.** Index-time at relating is its precondition — never itself a meme.
