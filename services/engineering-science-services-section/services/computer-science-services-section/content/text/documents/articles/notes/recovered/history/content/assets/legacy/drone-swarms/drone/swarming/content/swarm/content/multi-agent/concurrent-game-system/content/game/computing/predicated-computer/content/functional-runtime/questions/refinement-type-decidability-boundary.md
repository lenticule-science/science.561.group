# Where is the principled boundary between substrate-decidable and runtime-deferred refinement types?

The subtheory's [refined-type](teaching/explanations/refined-type.md) concept admits types of the form $\{x : T \mid φ(x)\}$ — a base type $T$ together with a refinement predicate $φ$ that values must satisfy. [type-soundness](../theorems/type-soundness.md) claims well-typed programs evaluate without type errors. The original question asked which substrate-level structural fact fixes the decidability boundary, what discipline applies outside that fragment, what type-soundness actually guarantees, and how check-type's decision range looks.

## Status

The **structural** part of the question is resolved by [refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension](../theorems/refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension.md): the decidability boundary is fixed by the substrate's four-regime classification, refinement subtyping is designated-domain inclusion, and weakest-precondition is fiber residuation. The theorem's `proved` status covers the structural identifications only; it does *not* assert that the substrate's current operational [check-type](../morphisms/check-type.md) signature is FOUR-valued or that the surrounding [type-soundness](../theorems/type-soundness.md) theorem has already been lifted to a regime-parametric form. Both of those are the soundness-layer uplift the comprehension theorem makes possible but does not itself perform.

What remains is therefore (a) deployment policy, and (b) finalizing the soundness-layer uplift drafted in [type-soundness-regime-parametric](../theorems/type-soundness-regime-parametric.md) — Lean realization, worked-example chain across the four regimes, and witness-record schemas for the budget-exhaustion / proof-carrying / contestation-record cases.

## What the comprehension theorem settled

- **The decidability boundary is the [four-regime classification](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), not a refinement-logic trichotomy.** Per Part 4 of [refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension](../theorems/refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension.md), a refinement's substrate-checkability is determined by the [closure ordinal](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/closure-ordinal.md) of its closure-rule system: strict and Scott-continuous regimes are checkable at admission, transfinite-bounded regime is checkable with explicit budget, aperiodic regime is not statically checkable. The boundary lives in the substrate's regime classification, not in a refinement-language hierarchy duplicated for the type system.
- **The discipline outside the strict/Scott-continuous regime is regime-named, not free-floating.** Per [regime-classification-for-substrate](../../../theorems/regime-classification-for-substrate.md) and [rule-language-realizability-determines-regime](../../../theorems/rule-language-realizability-determines-regime.md), the regime is statically inferable from the refinement language; the substrate does not need to choose refuse-vs-defer-vs-proof-carry on a per-refinement dial. With regime boundaries per the [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md) ($\kappa < \omega$, $\kappa = \omega$, $\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$, $\omega_1 \leq \kappa$), the discipline is: strict/Scott-continuous → admit-direct; transfinite-bounded → admit-with-budget; aperiodic → admit-with-proof-obligation or refuse-to-admit per the substrate's declared aperiodic policy.
- **check-type's *structural* decision range is bilattice-valued; its *current* signature is the binary projection.** Structurally, [check-type](../morphisms/check-type.md) on a refined type computes $φ(v) \in A_{p_T}$, with cardinal cases in $\mathbf{FOUR}$: $\mathbf{t}$ (valid), $\mathbf{f}$ (refuted), $\bot$ (undetermined-within-budget), $\top$ (contested). Sub-question D's "four-valued return is bilattice-shaped" intuition is structurally correct. However, the substrate's current `check-type` morphism returns the truth-designated $\{\text{valid},\text{invalid}\}$ projection of that value, and the surrounding [type-soundness](../theorems/type-soundness.md) theorem is stated against that projection. Lifting both to the FOUR-valued / regime-parametric form is a tracked uplift on the soundness side (see "Soundness-layer alignment" in [refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension](../theorems/refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension.md)), not something already in place.
- **Type-soundness will become parametric in the regime once the soundness-layer uplift lands.** Today, [type-soundness](../theorems/type-soundness.md) holds against the binary projection unconditionally for the regimes the substrate admits. Under the planned uplift it would hold unconditionally for strict and Scott-continuous regimes, modulo recorded budget for transfinite-bounded, and modulo discharged proof obligation for aperiodic — i.e. status `proved-modulo-regime-policy` with the policy as one of three regime-keyed declarations rather than a free parameter. This is the soundness-statement work that follows from the comprehension theorem; it is not a step the comprehension theorem alone has performed.
- **Refinement subtyping and weakest-precondition computation are not in scope of the boundary at all.** Per Parts 2 and 3 of the comprehension theorem, both are answered by the substrate's universal structural mechanisms: subtyping is the predicate-specialization morphism, which is set-inclusion of the designated cells $φ^{-1}(d) \subseteq ψ^{-1}(d)$ at the declared designation $d \in \{\mathbf{t}, \top\}$; weakest-precondition is the $\odot \dashv \Rightarrow$ adjunction. The original question's framing implicitly treated these as separate decidability concerns; the comprehension reading shows they are mechanisms shared with every other substrate discipline.

## What remains policy

- **Which regimes does a given substrate deployment admit at all?** A federation-portable substrate plausibly admits only strict and Scott-continuous refinements (so federation-convergence is unconditional). A research-or-trusted-deployment substrate may admit transfinite-bounded with budget, or aperiodic with proof-carrying. This is a declared deployment parameter, not a structural unknown.
- **What is the witness-chain content for an aperiodic refinement under proof-carrying discipline?** The structure of "the proof obligation is discharged here, by this artifact" needs a schema attached to [witness](../../../objects/witness.md). Candidate shape: a discharge-record cell pointing to the proof artifact (Lean module, SMT certificate, external witness), referenced from the type-record. The substrate's [signature-binds-witness-to-identity](../../../theorems/signature-binds-witness-to-identity.md) machinery already gives the binding discipline; what is missing is the schema.
- **What is the witness-chain content for a budget-exhausted transfinite-bounded refinement?** When [check-type](../morphisms/check-type.md) returns $\bot$ because budget was exhausted, the witness should record the budget, the partial settlement state, and the regime classification under which the value was admitted. The fields are clear; the schema needs writing.
- **How is contestation resolved when two refinement-discharge mechanisms disagree?** When one solver returns $\mathbf{t}$ and another returns $\mathbf{f}$, the bilattice's $\top$ cell records the contestation per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](../../../theorems/paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md). What the original question called "$\top_k$-pressure discipline" is real — the substrate carries the contestation and the [inconsistency-sheaf-record](../../../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md) machinery makes the disagreement queryable — but the engineer-facing convention for *acting* on a contested refinement (refuse the value? admit with audit flag? require third discharge?) is a deployment policy, not a structural fact.

## Why the question's status changed

The original framing assumed the decidability boundary lived in the refinement-language hierarchy (Presburger / QF-UFLIA / Peano / etc.) and that the substrate had to pick a fragment plus a discipline. The [refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension](../theorems/refinements-as-paraconsistent-comprehension.md) theorem shows the boundary lives in the substrate's *own* regime classification — the same one used for federation, settlement, and rule-language realisability — and so the type system's decidability discipline is a corollary of structural commitments the substrate has already made elsewhere, not an independent design surface.

The remaining open items are deployment policies (which regimes to admit, what to do with contestation) and witness-schema details (what fields the witness chain carries for budget-exhausted and proof-carrying refinements). They are tractable as engineering decisions and witness-record schema additions; they are not blocked on a structural unknown.
