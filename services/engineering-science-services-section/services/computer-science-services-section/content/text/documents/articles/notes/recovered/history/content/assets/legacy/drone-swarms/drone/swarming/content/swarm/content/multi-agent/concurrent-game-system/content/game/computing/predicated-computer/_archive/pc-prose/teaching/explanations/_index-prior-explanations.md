Long-form pieces whose role is *understanding*: bringing a reader from "knows the name" to "knows what the thing is and why it's that way." Distinct from sibling `learning/` kinds:

- `tutorials/` — *learning-oriented*; hand-held first-time-through.
- `guides/` — *task-oriented*; step-through to a specific outcome.
- `references/` — *information-oriented*; lookup material.

Distinct from `prose/narratives/` — a narrative threads multiple records into a structural through-line; an explanation orients to one specific thing.

Distinct from `prose/learning/claims/` (propositions with proofs) and `prose/concepts/` (role-signatures). An explanation may cite both but is not either.

An explanation is what you'd want a colleague to read before they started working with the thing being explained.
