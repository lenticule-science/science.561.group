# Strict-regime substrates have a finite, $H^1$-trivial, $H^0$-classified engineering envelope

**Claim.** Every conformant [predicated computer](../objects/predicated-computer.md) whose [regime record](../objects/regime-record.md) declares the *strict* regime — equivalently, with finite value-domain $\mathbf{L}$, finite [entity set](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/entity-set.md) $E$, finite [predicate category](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predicate-category.md) $\mathcal{C}$, and quantifier-free [closure-rule language](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-language.md) — has the following engineering envelope:

1. **Convergence.** Every settle invocation reaches quiescence in finitely many layers, with the layer count bounded by the size of the [predication fiber algebra](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/predication-fiber-algebra.md). Closure ordinal $\kappa < \omega$.
2. **Audit cohomology.** The [inconsistency sheaf](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md)'s $H^1$ is identically zero (per [strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md)); audit reduces to $H^0$-counts of $\top$-cells.
3. **Federation.** Endpoint-state shipping is admissible; trajectory shipping is over-engineered. Two strict-regime substrates federating produce a strict-regime substrate.
4. **Snapshot.** Endpoint-only snapshots reproduce historical results byte-for-byte (per [snapshot-replay-fidelity](snapshot-replay-fidelity.md)).
5. **MLD-classification.** Two strict-regime settlings are [MLD-equivalent](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/mutually-locally-derivable.md) iff their $H^0$-cell-sets agree up to settling-shift and entity-relabeling — decidable in polynomial time per [strict-regime-mld-trivial](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/strict-regime-mld-trivial.md).

The envelope is what the engineering can *assume* about a strict-regime substrate. Anything beyond the envelope (transfinite settlement, $H^1$-class auditing, trajectory federation) is over-engineering.

## Why this holds

The five envelope items are direct substrate-level instances of the strict-regime math results:

**Convergence (item 1).** Per [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md), strict regime requires finite $\mathbf{L}, E, \mathcal{C}$ and quantifier-free rule language. Per [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), this gives $\kappa < \omega$ — finite stabilization at every fiber. Per [strict-regime-realizability](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/strict-regime-realizability.md), the realizable $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-family on a strict-regime substrate is exactly the finite-settlement family — every realizable $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-subset is generated by a finite-stage settlement, with no firing ordinals required. The engine's [settle](../morphisms/settle.md) operator reaches quiescence in finitely many layers.

**Audit cohomology (item 2).** Per [strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/strict-regime-cohomology-vanishing.md), $H^n(\mathbf{T}^*, \mathcal{F}_U^{(\bullet)}) = 0$ for $n \geq 1$ in the strict regime. This applies to the inconsistency-side component $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$ as well. Per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md), substrate audit lives in this cohomology; in the strict regime it reduces to $H^0$ — a count of $\top$-cells, computable in polynomial time per [finite-settling $H^0$](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/finite-settling-h0-polynomial-time.md). The substrate's [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md) operator on a strict-regime substrate executes only the $H^0$ branch.

**Federation (item 3).** Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), federation discipline is regime-determined. With $H^1 = 0$, no cohomological invariant is lost when two endpoints are merged; the federated substrate's settled state is determined by the union of admitted content and the closure operator. No firing-ordinal pattern needs to be shipped because none generates a non-trivial $H^1$-class.

**Snapshot (item 4).** Per [snapshot-replay-fidelity](snapshot-replay-fidelity.md), the snapshot policy is regime-determined. With $H^1 = 0$ and finite $\kappa$, the endpoint state contains all the structural content of the settling; replaying from the endpoint reproduces any subsequent state byte-for-byte. Trajectory snapshots store information that is structurally redundant in the strict regime.

**MLD-classification (item 5).** Per [strict-regime-mld-trivial](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/strict-regime-mld-trivial.md), MLD-equivalence reduces to $H^0$-matching in the strict regime — a polynomial-time decision procedure on the designated-cell sets. The substrate's audit machinery for "are these two regions equivalent up to MLD?" runs the polynomial-time comparison and returns a definite answer.

## Engineering consequence

A strict-regime substrate is the *easy case* — the engineering envelope is finite, polynomial, and deterministic across every dimension. Concretely:

- **The engine is $\omega$-bounded.** Fuel policies declare finite per-cascade fuel; settle invocations consume finite fuel and produce finite witness chains.
- **Audit is polynomial.** Audit queries on the inconsistency-sheaf record run in polynomial time and return $H^0$-counts (or empty $H^1$-results); no cohomological enumeration is needed.
- **Federation is endpoint-shipped.** Two strict-regime substrates federating exchange endpoint state, not trajectory. Federation handshakes are bounded by the substrates' content sizes, not by their step counts.
- **Snapshots are endpoint-only.** Snapshot storage scales with the settled state, not with the trajectory's depth. Replay from any snapshot reproduces subsequent state byte-for-byte.
- **Equivalence is decidable cheaply.** "Are these two regions structurally equivalent?" is a polynomial-time MLD-comparison, with no transfinite-aware machinery needed.

Most relational databases, finite TBoxes, finite Kripke frames, finite knowledge graphs, and finite multi-agent epistemic models live in the strict regime. The substrate's classical-computing comfort zone — what conventional databases and rule engines have always handled — is exactly here.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Strict-regime substrates with transfinite-aware engine machinery.** Over-engineering. The substrate functions correctly but consumes implementation effort on machinery the regime cannot exercise.
- **Strict-regime substrates whose audit machinery enumerates $H^1$-classes.** The enumeration always returns empty; the implementation is dead code per the regime.
- **Strict-regime substrates shipping trajectory across federation.** The shipped trajectory contains no $H^1$-content; the federation handshake is over-sized.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's [regime record](../objects/regime-record.md) declares `regime: strict`, with `rule-language-fragment: quantifier-free` and `base-finiteness` declaring all of $\mathbf{L}, E, \mathcal{C}$ finite.
- The substrate's [closure-rule system](../objects/closure-rule-system.md) contains only quantifier-free rules per [rule-language-realizability-determines-regime](rule-language-realizability-determines-regime.md).
- The substrate's content is not federated to a non-strict-regime substrate without prefix-scoping.

## Position

This theorem is the first of four [regime-engineering-envelope theorems](regime-discipline.md) (strict / Scott-continuous / transfinite-bounded / aperiodic), each derived by promoting one row of the [four-regime dichotomy](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md) to a substrate-level engineering claim. The four together give the engineering its complete per-regime discipline: each substrate declares its regime, and the per-regime envelope follows.
