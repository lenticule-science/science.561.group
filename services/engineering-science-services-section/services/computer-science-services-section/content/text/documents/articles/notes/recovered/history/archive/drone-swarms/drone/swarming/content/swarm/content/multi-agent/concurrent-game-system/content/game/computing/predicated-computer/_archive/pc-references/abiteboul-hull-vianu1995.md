## Source

S. Abiteboul, R. Hull, V. Vianu. *Foundations of Databases*. Addison-Wesley, 1995.

## Form

Textbook. The standard reference for the foundations of relational databases, datalog, and the recursive-query tradition.

## Content summary

Develops the relational model, relational calculus, relational algebra, and their equivalence; introduces *Datalog* as the function-free Horn fragment of first-order logic, with the least-fixed-point semantics over the active domain; characterizes the expressive power of Datalog and its negation extensions (stratified, well-founded, stable); develops the dependency theory (functional, multi-valued, inclusion, embedded); covers query containment and equivalence; develops the model-theoretic and proof-theoretic perspectives on database queries; covers complexity classes (LOGSPACE, PTIME, NP) characterized by query languages. The Datalog chapters are the load-bearing reference for the substrate's recursive-query and closure-driven-settling design.
