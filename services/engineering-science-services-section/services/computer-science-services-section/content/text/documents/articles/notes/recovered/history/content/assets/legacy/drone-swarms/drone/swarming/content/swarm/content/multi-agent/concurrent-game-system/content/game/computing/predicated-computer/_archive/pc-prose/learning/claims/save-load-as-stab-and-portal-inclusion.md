# Session save is the closure-2-monad's settlement projection; session load is the world-specific portal-inclusion

**Claim.** Let $H$ be a session's *open-content fiber* in a substrate $\mathcal{S}$ — the substrate's view of the session's predications before settlement, including provisional cells, in-flight closure steps, and unresolved disagreement marked $\top$ per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md). Let $H^*$ be the session's *settled fiber* — the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotent image of $H$ per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), restricted to cells whose valuation is at the closure operator's fixed point.

Define two operators on session fibers:

$$\mathrm{Stab} \;:\; H \longrightarrow H^*, \qquad \mathrm{Stab}(v) \;=\; \mathrm{cl}(v)\,\big|_{\,\mathrm{settled}(v)}$$

— the **session-save** operator: apply the closure step (per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md)), then restrict to the settled-cell sub-support; and

$$\mathrm{Portal}_U \;:\; H^* \longrightarrow U, \qquad \mathrm{Portal}_U(v^*) \;=\; \iota_U^*\, v^* \;\oplus\; v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$$

— the **session-load-into-world-$U$** operator, parameterised by a target world-fiber $U$ (a downstream substrate session, an outpost-image, or a federation peer's fiber): reindex along the inclusion $\iota_U : \mathrm{supp}(v^*) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{supp}(U)$ per the [predication fiber functor](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md), then knowledge-join with $U$'s frame-content $v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$ via [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md).

Then:

1. **$\mathrm{Stab}$ is universal-and-lossy.** $\mathrm{Stab}$ is a left adjoint to the inclusion $H^* \hookrightarrow H$ in the fiber's $\leq_k$-poset; any $\mathrm{Stab}$-image is preserved across save-and-load with respect to *every* world-fiber $U$ for which $\iota_U$ is well-defined. Provisional content is discarded; the lossy direction of the adjunction is the projection from $H$'s in-flight cells to $H^*$'s fixed-point cells.
2. **$\mathrm{Portal}_U$ is injective on the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image.** $\mathrm{Portal}_U \circ \mathrm{Stab}$ restricted to the settled sub-support is a monomorphism into $U$'s fiber: distinct settled valuations produce distinct $U$-frame extensions. Per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image is content-addressed; per [predication-fiber-functor](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md), the reindexing along $\iota_U$ is faithful.
3. **Round-trip identity on the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image.** For any world-fiber $U$ admitting $\mathrm{Stab}(v)$'s support,
   $$\mathrm{Stab}\bigl(\mathrm{Portal}_U(\mathrm{Stab}(v))\bigr) \;=\; \mathrm{Stab}(v)$$
   modulo $U$'s frame-content (which $\mathrm{Stab}$ then re-projects out). The save/load round-trip preserves settled content byte-identically per [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md); only the world-frame differs between source and target.
4. **The $(Stab, Portal_U)$ pair is a forward-migration in the sense of [forward-migration-preserves-prior-content](forward-migration-preserves-prior-content.md).** The save/load discipline is the forward-migration discipline applied at the session-fiber surface; no new substrate primitive is introduced.

Therefore the v0.8 captain-question on session save/load (`save-load-substrate-primitive.md`, options AF/AG/AH) is *not* a kernel-API question: a substrate that already implements settlement, fiber-functor reindexing, and coherence-glue has $\mathrm{Stab}$ and $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ as derived operators on existing structure. The substrate-primitive recommendation (option AG) is the choice to *name* these operators as substrate verbs; it commits no new mathematical content.

## Why this holds

**Part 1: $\mathrm{Stab}$ is the closure-2-monad's image, restricted.** Per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md) and [closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad](closure-rule-system-realizes-closure-2-monad.md), the substrate's settlement operator is the closure 2-monad $\mathrm{cl}$ applied to the substrate's current valuation. Per [closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/closure-2-monad-lax-idempotence.md), $\mathrm{cl}$ is lax-idempotent: $\mathrm{cl}(\mathrm{cl}(v)) = \mathrm{cl}(v)$ up to unique 2-iso, and the inclusion of fixed-points $H^* \hookrightarrow H$ has $\mathrm{cl}$ as its left adjoint in the bilattice's $\leq_k$-poset.

The restriction to $\mathrm{settled}(v)$ — cells whose closure-image coincides with the cell's own valuation, i.e., cells *at* the fixed point rather than *en route* — is the substrate-content-level distinction between settled and provisional cells. Per the substrate's settlement discipline, this distinction is structural: a cell is settled iff its closure image equals itself iff no further closure-rule application changes it. The map $v \mapsto v|_{\mathrm{settled}}$ is the projection onto the settled sub-support; its composition with $\mathrm{cl}$ is the $\mathrm{Stab}$ operator.

The lossy direction is: provisional cells (in-flight admissions, contradictions still being held under the paraconsistent $\top$ per [paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline](paraconsistency-as-substrate-discipline.md), open closure-rule chains) are projected away. The discarded content is not undefined — it is structurally absent from the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image because it is not at the closure operator's fixed point.

**Part 2: $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ is the fiber-functor reindexing.** Per [predication-fiber-functor](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md), for any morphism $f : q \to p$ in the predicate category, the fiber functor produces a faithful reindexing $f^* : A_p \to A_q$. The world-fiber $U$ is, in this language, a fiber-algebra carrying the *frame content* $v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$ — the substrate cells specific to that world (the outpost-image's local data, the federation peer's local cells, the downstream session's prior content).

Loading $\mathrm{Stab}(v)$ into $U$ is: (a) reindex along the inclusion $\iota_U : \mathrm{supp}(v^*) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{supp}(U)$, which embeds the settled support into $U$'s support; (b) knowledge-join via [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md) with $U$'s frame-content. The reindexing is faithful per the fiber-functor theorem; the join is the substrate's standard $\vee_k$-glue per [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md), which is associative and commutative on the bilattice.

Distinct $\mathrm{Stab}$-images produce distinct loaded fibers in $U$ because: (a) per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), distinct settled valuations have distinct canonical hashes; (b) per the faithfulness of $\iota_U^*$, distinct settled valuations remain distinct after reindexing; (c) the $\vee_k$-glue with $v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$ is monotone in the loaded valuation, so distinct inputs give distinct (or comparable) outputs in $U$.

**Part 3: Round-trip identity.** Compute $\mathrm{Stab}(\mathrm{Portal}_U(\mathrm{Stab}(v)))$:

1. $\mathrm{Stab}(v) = v^*$ — the settled image.
2. $\mathrm{Portal}_U(v^*) = \iota_U^* v^* \vee_k v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$ — embedded settled image, glued with $U$'s frame.
3. $\mathrm{Stab}(\cdot) = \mathrm{cl}(\cdot)|_{\mathrm{settled}}$ — re-settle, then re-project.

The closure step on $\iota_U^* v^* \vee_k v_U^{\mathrm{frame}}$ closes any cells that the glue made firable; restricting to the settled sub-support drops those closure-products and $U$'s frame. The remaining content is exactly $\iota_U^* v^*$ — which, projected back to $\mathrm{supp}(v^*)$ along $\iota_U^{-1}$ (a structurally available inverse on the embedded support), is $v^*$ itself.

Per [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md), the substrate's deterministic replay guarantees the round-trip is byte-identical at the substrate-content level; per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), the canonical-form hashes match. The round-trip identity is therefore structural, not coincidental.

**Part 4: Forward-migration discipline.** Per [forward-migration-preserves-prior-content](forward-migration-preserves-prior-content.md), a forward-migration is a substrate transition that preserves prior settled content. The $(\mathrm{Stab}, \mathrm{Portal}_U)$ pair is a forward-migration: the save preserves settled content by Part 3; the load preserves the saved content under the embedding into $U$. Per [model-supersession-as-forward-migration-corollary](model-supersession-as-forward-migration-corollary.md), forward-migrations admit no new substrate primitive — they are derived from the substrate's existing closure and reindexing operators.

**Part 5: Federation alignment.** Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), federation peers' fibers converge under the four-regime dichotomy of [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md). When $U$ is a federation peer's fiber rather than a local outpost, the load operation $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ is the federation join — the settled save-image is admitted at the peer per [interfacer-counit-law-uniformity](interfacer-counit-law-uniformity.md) and joined with the peer's frame. The convergence regime applies; cross-substrate load is structurally identical to local load, differing only in the choice of $U$ as a federation-peer fiber rather than a local one.

## Engineering consequence

- **Save/load is not a new kernel API.** The v0.2 session-as-process surface already supports event-log replay (per [recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism](recovery-via-event-log-replay-determinism.md)) and the closure step (per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md)). The substrate-primitive option (AG) names $\mathrm{Stab}$ and $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ as substrate verbs, but the implementation is the existing closure operator composed with the existing fiber-functor reindexing — no new closure rules, no new envelope shape, no new conformance protocol.
- **Save is settling.** Round-8 §4.5's captain-side identification ("saving IS settling, loading IS choosing-a-world") is structural per Parts 1 and 2: $\mathrm{Stab}$ literally applies the substrate's settlement operator; $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ literally applies the fiber-functor reindexing. The identification is not a metaphor — it names the same substrate operators at a session-scoped fiber.
- **Multi-world divergent loads compose by knowledge-join.** Loading $\mathrm{Stab}(v)$ into two different worlds $U_1, U_2$ produces two distinct loaded fibers; loading the same $\mathrm{Stab}$-image into a *combined* world $U_1 \otimes_k U_2$ (the $\vee_k$-join of frames) produces the join of the two loads per the coherence-glue's associativity. The substrate's "many worlds branching from one save" pattern is the fiber-functor's parallel reindexing.
- **Outpost-image checkpoint is local $\mathrm{Stab}$.** The v0.5 ICS outpost-image checkpoint (per option AH if activated early) is $\mathrm{Stab}$ applied at the outpost-fiber boundary; recovery is $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ with $U$ the recovered outpost. The earlier-activation choice (v0.5 vs. v0.8) does not change the operators — only when the substrate names them as primitive verbs.
- **Per-app save/load is the userland-pattern option (AF) instantiated.** Each productivity-stack app composing $\mathrm{Stab}$ at its own session-fiber and $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ at its own world-fiber implements the same operators per-app rather than calling a substrate verb. The mathematical content is identical; only the API exposure differs. Per the structural derivation, no app's save/load can produce content beyond the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image — provisional content cannot survive the round-trip regardless of which option the substrate adopts.
- **Conformance vector reduces to round-trip identity.** A conformance check of save/load reduces to verifying: (a) $\mathrm{Stab}$'s output is at the closure-operator fixed point; (b) $\mathrm{Portal}_U$'s output knowledge-joins the embedded settled image with $U$'s frame; (c) Part 3's round-trip identity holds on a representative settled valuation. No protocol-level conformance test is needed; the substrate's existing settlement and reindexing conformance suffice.
- **The "Wood-Between-Worlds" reading is a corollary, not a new commitment.** The captain-side cosmology mapping ($H$ = the Wood, $\mathrm{Stab}$ = settling-into-the-Wood, $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ = entering-a-pool-into-world-$U$) is consistent with Parts 1–4 and adds no substrate content beyond the existing operators' interpretation.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Save/load preserving provisional content.** Any save discipline that retains in-flight closure steps, $\top$-marked unresolved cells, or non-fixed-point valuations is not $\mathrm{Stab}$ — it is a different operator, and its round-trip is not guaranteed by Part 3. A substrate that claims to "save the full session including in-flight" is implementing a different (more expensive, less universal) operator.
- **Load that re-derives discarded content.** $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ re-frames the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image into $U$; it does not re-run discarded closure steps from before the save. A "load" that re-derives provisional content is implementing $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ followed by a fresh closure step on the joined frame — which produces a *new* settled image not byte-identical to the saved one (Part 3 holds only on the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image side).
- **World-load that forgets $U$'s frame.** Loading without joining $U$'s frame-content is not $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ — it is the bare reindexing $\iota_U^* v^*$, which lands in a fresh fiber rather than in $U$. A substrate that "loads into a world" but discards the world's prior content is implementing world-replacement, not world-load.
- **Lossless save claim.** $\mathrm{Stab}$ is structurally lossy on the provisional sub-support; per Part 1, the lossy direction is the adjunction's left side. A substrate that claims "lossless save" of the full session fiber is making a claim the closure-2-monad's lax-idempotence does not support.
- **Save/load that depends on session history beyond the settled image.** Per Part 2's injectivity on the $\mathrm{Stab}$-image, the load is determined by the settled image and $U$'s frame. A load whose result depends on which session produced the settled image (rather than on the settled image itself) is non-functorial — it violates the fiber-functor's faithfulness.
- **Cross-world load that diverges beyond the federation regime.** Per Part 5, cross-world load is bounded by [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md)'s four regimes. A load that produces unbounded divergence between two federation peers' loaded fibers is non-conformant per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md).

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate implements settlement per [settlement-via-closure-2-monad](settlement-via-closure-2-monad.md), with the closure operator's fixed-point sub-support distinguishable from the in-flight sub-support at the substrate-content level.
- The substrate's fibers admit the fiber-functor reindexing per [predication-fiber-functor](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/predication-fiber-functor.md), so $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ is well-defined for any world-fiber $U$.
- Coherence-glue $\vee_k$ is implemented per [coherence-glue](../morphisms/coherence-glue.md), so the load can join the embedded settled image with $U$'s frame.
- Canonical-form discipline per [hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form](hash-injectivity-under-canonical-form.md), so the round-trip identity (Part 3) is byte-checkable.
- For federation cases: the federation graph carries bilattice-graded trust per [federation-trust-bilattice-graded](federation-trust-bilattice-graded.md); peers' fibers share the predicate category $\mathcal{C}$ so $\iota_U$ is well-defined across substrates.

The three options AF / AG / AH differ only in *when* the substrate names $\mathrm{Stab}$ and $\mathrm{Portal}_U$ as primitive verbs — the underlying mathematical content is identical and is derivable today from existing structure.
