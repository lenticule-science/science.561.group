# socializing: multi-agent communicating

`socializing` is the **scale-2 instance of communicating** (see the agency-spiral note): communicating lifted along a **sheaf of memes over a social base** (agents and their channels). communicating is `End(Meme)` on one agent's meme; socializing is the same faculty over *many* agents — the inter-subjective field where meaning is shared, negotiated, and conventionalized.

## Initial object: the one-agent society (= communicating)

The initial object is the **society of a single agent** — degenerate, where socializing collapses to communicating (the agent's own semiosis).
The unique morphism out of it into any larger society is **admit/join** (include the agent's memes in the social field).
So **`communicating ↪ socializing` is the initial-object inclusion**: communicating sits at the base, and everything social is generated by adding agents.
This is what "socializing = communicating lifted" means precisely.

The **generative seed** (the corpus's "potential cell" sense) is the **potential social cell**: a dyad of agent-slots with relating's handedness lifted — `Token`(left)/`Claim`(right) becomes **speaker (⊙, generative)** / **listener (⇒, interpretive)** — and *empty transmission*.
The first social act (a meme crossing the dyad) opens it, exactly as `articulate` opens relating's potential articulation cell. communicating's `messaging` (Channel/Speaker/Listener, the directed dyad) is already this cell; socializing is its many-agent closure.

## Apex / target: the glued common-ground section (consensus = H¹ = 0)

Two senses, dual — stated exactly:

- **Package apex** (`induceBins`): the coproduct of socializing's social-act morphisms — a *colimit*, the discovery carrier, as in every package.
- **Categorical target** socializing settles toward: the **glued global section of the meme-sheaf over the social base = common ground / shared culture.** This is a *limit* (the global-sections / sheaf-gluing functor), the meaning-assignment agreeing across all agents.
  Its obstruction is **H¹ of the social meme-sheaf**: `H¹ = 0` ⇒ a global section exists ⇒ **consensus**; `H¹ ≠ 0` ⇒ no gluing ⇒ **standing disagreement** (held, paraconsistent, not exploded).
  This is the **bilattice-network-sheaf goal made social.**

So **initial = one agent (communicating); target = the glued common-ground section.** **Social settling** is the descent from seed to target: repeated communication converging the field toward its global-section fixed point. communicating's own `ground` morphism (dyadic common-ground update) is the **germ** of this apex — socializing is `ground` ranged over the whole agent-sheaf rather than one partner.

## Carriers (proposed `$id`s under `socializing/…`)

- **social base** — a site of agents + channels (reuse communicating's `Channel`/`Speaker`/`Listener` carriers and animating's `agent`); the base the meme-sheaf sits over.
- **meme-sheaf** — memes assigned over the social base (each agent's local memes; the restriction maps are the channels).
- **common-ground section** — the glued global section (the apex/target), carrying its H¹ obstruction (reuse relating's settling cohomology, e.g. `readFirstCohomologyOfSettlementFiltration`).

## Morphisms = communicating's, lifted along the agent-sheaf

Each communicating morphism lifts to its social form (the generating set; derive, do not invent):

- `speak` → **broadcast / address** (fuse a meme onto one channel / many channels)
- `listen` → **receive / attend** (residuate from the social field)
- `ground` → **converge common ground** (knowledge-join across the whole sheaf — the descent toward the apex)
- `translate` → **cross-semiosphere at scale** (re-sign across sub-communities)
- `semiose` → **social interpretant chains** (rumor, tradition, the loop across agents)
- `negate`/`conflate` → social polarity/consistency (disagreement, irony at scale)
- `refer`/`presuppose` → shared reference / common-ground presupposition
- `compose`/`relay` → multi-hop social transmission (the monoid, now over the network)

The lift itself (a communicating morphism + the agent-sheaf → its social morphism) is the reusable primitive the agency-spiral note calls for; socializing is its first instance.

## The lift, realized: stalkwise vs cross-stalk (2026-06-21)

The lift is now a real function, `liftStalkwise` (`socialize/lift.mts`), and the realized design splits communicating's morphisms by a precise rule:

- **Within-agent morphisms** (no reference to another agent: `communicate`, `semiose`, `illocute`, `translate`, `negate`, `conflate`, `refer`, `modalize`, `presuppose`, `quote`, `compose`) lift **stalkwise**: `liftStalkwise(f)` applies communicating's OWN exported action `f` at every agent's stalk (or the single `member` stalk).
  This is the natural endomorphism lift of a meme-sheaf section transform — it commutes with the channel restriction maps because `f` is identical at every stalk.
  The verb **name is kept**, because the lift is the diagonal application of the same morphism.

- **Cross-stalk morphisms** (those whose communicating form references the dyad / channel / partner: `speak`, `listen`, `ground`) are **not** stalkwise — at scale 2 they move memes *between* agents (`broadcast`), take up what crossed (`attend`), or glue the whole sheaf (`converge`).
  They are **renamed and hand-written**.

The rule is exact: **rename + hand-write iff the lift is cross-stalk; keep the verb name + use the primitive iff the lift is the diagonal stalkwise application.** Richer social phenomena are then *composites*, not new primitives — rumor/tradition = `broadcast ∘ semiose↑`, cross-community translation = `broadcast ∘ translate↑`, multi-hop transmission = `broadcast ∘ compose↑`.

Two load-bearing consequences: - **ONE way.** The eleven within-agent verbs have no second implementation — they ARE communicating's actions under the lift, so they cannot drift from communicating.
Deleting `liftStalkwise` deletes the eleven verbs with it (capability is not duplicated). - **Diagonal embedding (exact, tested).** On the one-agent field, `f↑` runs `f` on that agent's memes and nothing else, so `socializing.f ↾ one agent = communicating.f` verbatim — asserted per verb in the suite (`canonical` equality against the communicating action's own output).

Surface: **15 verbs** = `admit` (scale-2-specific, initial-object inclusion) + 3 cross-stalk lifts + 11 within-agent stalkwise lifts — full morphism parity with communicating's 14.
The within-agent lifts add **no new carrier**: they are endo on the existing `social-field` schema, so no codegen change.

The primitive is deliberately generic (a faculty action + the agent-sheaf → the scale-2 field endomorphism); it is the construction `gaming` (playing↑) and `computing` (knowing↑) reuse — socializing is its first, validated instance.

## Correction: the stalkwise lift is the DISCRETE case; the realization is the multi-observer system (2026-06-21)

The stalkwise lift above is **not the social content** — it is the *degenerate diagonal*.
The corpus's `content/_archive/spatiotemporal-knowledge-presheaf` furnishes socializing's actual structure, the **multi-observer system** `V : O → Settlings_B` (`multi-observer-system.md`): a functor from an **observer category** `O` to settlings over a shared base.
Its own words name the error exactly:

> "When `O` is discrete (no non-identity morphisms), the multi-observer system is just a family of independent settlings."

Applying a communicating morphism independently at each agent's stalk **is** that discrete `O`.
The richer structure — what the agency-spiral note calls the **off-diagonal** — lives in `O`'s *non-identity morphisms*: the **translation settling-morphisms** `α : o_i → o_j` (perspective maps `V(α)`, the knowledge-presheaf `≤_k` preorder morphisms).
The deep readings that follow, all corpus theorems to be realized rather than mirrored:

- **Consensus is a LIMIT.** `V_cons = lim_{o∈O} V(o)` — the knowledge-meet over `O` (for discrete `O`, the pointwise bilattice meet; for non-discrete, the meet coherent with the translation morphisms).
  This is exactly `sheafCharges`' `common = ⋀_k`.
- **Disagreement is COHOMOLOGY.** `Dis_V = H¹(O, 𝒱)` with restriction maps the translations.
  For discrete `O`, `H¹ = 0` trivially and disagreement shows only as pairwise differences — confirming the diagonal carries *no* coherent disagreement.
- **Merge is the CRDT `∨_k`** (`distributed-settling-system.md`): replicas exchange by the knowledge-order join — monotone, idempotent, commutative — and converge.
- **CALM / regime stratification** (`crdt-convergence-via-cohomological-calm.md`): monotone merge converges **coordination-free iff `H¹ = 0`** — and `H¹` *vanishes* in the strict-finite and Scott-continuous regimes, is *non-zero* in the transfinite-bounded and aperiodic regimes.
  So coordination-freeness is regime-indexed; standing disagreement is the genuinely-transfinite phenomenon.
  `coordinationFree` is this CALM flag.

### Realized: the regime stratification (increment 3)

The four-regime dichotomy (`regime-classification-for-substrate.md`, `regime-record.md`, `crdt-convergence-via-cohomological-calm.md`) is now a SECOND, ORTHOGONAL axis of consensus (`regime.mts`, `regime.test.mjs`; 49 socializing tests):

- **The four regimes by closure ordinal κ** (`Regime`): strict (κ<ω), scott-continuous (κ=ω), transfinite-bounded (ω<κ<ω₁), aperiodic (κ≥ω₁) — κ-ordered, with `jointRegime` the **max-κ ("meet")** over observers (the strictest discipline wins; an undeclared observer defaults to `strict`).
  The regime is a **declared** substrate parameter (on the agent), not runtime-discovered.
- **CALM is regime-stratified** (`coordinationFreeByRegime`): the CRDT merge's δ⁰ vanishes — so convergence is coordination-free BY REGIME — exactly in the strict / scott-continuous regimes (κ ≤ ω); in transfinite-bounded / aperiodic it can be non-zero.
  The regime also fixes the **federation discipline** (`federationDiscipline`): endpoint-state shipping (strict/scott) vs trajectory shipping (transfinite/aperiodic), per `federation-convergence`.
- **converge writes both axes** into the section: the static opinion axis (`obstruction` = opposition-cycle H¹, `coordinationFree` = opinions glue) AND the dynamic regime axis (`regime`, `regimeCoordinationFree`, `federationDiscipline`).
- **The independence law** (the load-bearing assertion): the two axes are ORTHOGONAL.
  A strict field with opposed opinions has `obstruction > 0` (opinions don't glue) yet `regimeCoordinationFree = true`; an aperiodic field with agreeing opinions has `obstruction = 0` yet `regimeCoordinationFree = false`.
  Conflating "do the opinions glue?" with "does the merge need coordination?" is the error this law guards against.

### Realized: the settling-stage τ dynamics (increment 4)

A settling is a monotone family `(v_τ)_{τ<λ}`, not a snapshot (`settle.mts`, `settling.test.mjs`; 56 socializing tests).
The corpus dynamics (`crdt-convergence.md`): each replica's stage-τ state is `cl(⋁_k U_{i,τ})`, and once every write reaches every replica all converge to `v∞ = cl(⋁_k U)` in finitely many steps, no consensus protocol.

- **`settle`** (a scale-2-native morphism, 17-verb surface) runs the closure-driven social settling: with no inference layer `cl` is the identity, so a settling stage is one SYNCHRONOUS gossip-merge round (every observer becomes `V(o) ∨_k ⋁_neighbours V`), iterated to quiescence.
  It returns the quiesced field with `section.closureOrdinal = κ` and re-reads the consensus over v∞.
- **The settling depth κ** (the closure ordinal) is the number of rounds that changed the field = the observer graph's eccentricity: a complete graph settles in κ=1, a path of n in κ=n−1, an already-shared field in κ=0.
  Finite for a finite field (κ<ω) — the operational confirmation of the strict regime.
- **`settleTrajectory`** exposes the whole monotone family `(stages)` — the firing-ordinal record the transfinite federation discipline must SHIP (trajectory shipping); in the strict/scott regimes only the endpoint v∞ is needed.
- **Canonical normal form**: `mergeStalks` now emits memes in canonical cell order, so observers that received the same writes converge to BYTE-IDENTICAL stalks — a genuine CRDT normal form, not just equal sets.
  Tested: settling-monotonicity (knowledge only grows per stage), quiescence (a further settle is a no-op), convergence (all observers reach v∞), held-join of opposed writes (potential ∨_k active = stable, never absurd).

### Realized: the replica-cover Mayer–Vietoris gluing (increment 5)

The static consensus's explicit Čech/MV reading (`glue.mts`, `glue.test.mjs`; 60 socializing tests) — the complement to the dynamic settling.
The corpus reads a node's consensus as a sheaf gluing over its replica-cover `{ o_i → target }` (`replica-cover.md`, `crdt-convergence-via-mayer-vietoris.md`, `cohomology-as-coordination-substrate.md`): the decided value is the global section `H⁰(U)`, the obstruction to gluing is `H¹(U)` via the connecting map `δ⁰`, coordination-freeness is `δ⁰ = 0`, and the `∨_k`-merge over a connected cover IS the unique global section.

- **`glue`** (a scale-2-native morphism, 18-verb surface) presents a node's `H⁰` global section: it `∨_k`-merges the replica-cover's local sections into the `target`, then re-reads the consensus.
  The static, one-step Čech complement to `settle`'s iterated gossip.
- **`converge` gained a first-class `cover` input**: `converge(cover: target)` reads the consensus over that observer's replica-cover (the induced Čech nerve) — so `H¹` is the obstruction of THAT cover.
  Demonstrated: a 4-cycle whose whole-graph `H¹ = 1`, yet observer 0's replica-cover `{1→0, 3→0}` is a tree and glues (`H¹ = 0`). **`H¹` is tied to the cover**, exactly the corpus's reading.
- **`replicaCoverConsensus`** exposes the MV pieces: the cover members, the glued `H⁰` charge, the `δ⁰ = 0` coordination-freeness, the `H¹` obstruction, and the Čech-nerve overlap agreements `U_i ∩ U_j`.
  A held contradiction over a tree-shaped cover still glues (`δ⁰ = 0`), pooling to `stable`, never `absurd`.
