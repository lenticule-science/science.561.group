# The agency spiral: one self-similar faculty quartet

## The quartet (scale 1: the individual agent)

`relating` is the substrate (relations/memes).
Three **boundary faculties** act on the meme carrier, distinguished by *where they act relative to an agent's boundary*, and a fourth **binds** them:

- **communicating** — across the boundary; endomorphic on the meme (`meme → meme`).
- **knowing** — inside; the comonad on the meme (interior cognition/reflection).
- **playing** — outside; the meme's \*-autonomous duality + cut (interaction with the world/opponent).
- **animating** — the agency descent that binds the three into an **agent** (the interior glued-sheaf `perceive → act` loop; see the animating note).

## The spiral: the agent re-seeds relating at the next scale

The animating derivation is explicit: the individual agent is **"settled below, open above"** and **re-seeds the next-scale descent** (`individual → community → institution/federation`).
An agent, once produced, **re-enters `relating` as a node** — and a *society is a `relating` of agents*.
So the descent does not terminate at one agent; it **spirals**, the agent becoming the seed of the next turn.

## One self-similar quartet, not two generations

Because the agent re-seeds relating, the **same quartet recurs at the next scale**.
What looked like a separate "next generation" (socializing/computing/gaming/organizing) is the **scale-2 instance** of the very same four faculties:

| scale-1 faculty (on `Meme`, one agent) | scale-2 instance (over a society of agents) |
|---|---|
| **communicating** (across) | **socializing** — convention, common ground, culture, language-as-institution |
| **knowing** (inside) | **computing** — distributed/repeatable cognition ("a computer is a game made repeatable") |
| **playing** (outside) | **gaming** — concurrent games with symmetry (CGS proper; *inherently* multi-player) |
| **animating** (binds → agent) | **organizing** — binds the society's faculties → an *organization* (governance/operations) |

`gaming` and `computing` are not discarded — they **return at the higher scale**: `playing` is one agent against the outside, `gaming` is many agents playing concurrently; `knowing` is one agent's cognition, `computing` is cognition across many.
`organizing` is `animating` applied to a society (its collective `perceive → act` loop = governance) — i.e. `managing-operations` / the Operations Manifold.

## The lift: scale-2 = scale-1 faculty along a sheaf of agents

The mechanism is precise and makes the scale-2 packages **derivable, not invented**.
Scale-1 faculties operate on `Meme` (one agent's carrier).
The scale-2 instance of a faculty is **that faculty lifted along a sheaf of agents over a social base** — the society being a sheaf of agents (each agent itself an animating glued-sheaf):

- **socializing** = communicating lifted — memes *between* agents; the shared semiosphere / common-ground as a **collective sheaf** (the colimit of many agents' memes).
- **computing** = knowing lifted — the comonad over the agent sheaf (cognition distributed and made repeatable).
- **gaming** = playing lifted — the \*-autonomous cut among many players (the concurrent game).
- **organizing** = animating lifted — the agency descent of the society (the org as an agent).

Categorically the single→multi move is **from operations on one object to morphisms among many**: scale 1 lives on the *diagonal* (one agent's `End`/comonad/cut on its own meme); scale 2 is the *off-diagonal* — profunctors/relations among distinct agents over the social base.
Same operations, lifted.

## Self-similarity = the manifold property, read up the spiral

This is the **same self-similarity** built into the manifold (four faces at every node / a sheaf on the namespace site): the faculty quartet **recurs at every node of the agency spiral** (`meme → agent → society → federation → …`).
The carrier-node mounting / proof-tree composition is what threads `relating` up through each scale, so "get proof trees from relating to whatever we're assembling" *is* climbing the spiral.

## Package slotting

- **scale 1 (faculties of an agent):** `relating` (substrate) · `communicating` (across) · `knowing` (inside; the comonad) · `playing` (outside; renamed from `gaming`) · `animating` (binder).
- **scale 2 (faculties of a society):** `socializing` (**new** — communicating lifted) · `computing` (exists — knowing lifted; the gate) · `gaming` (the name **returns** at scale 2 — playing lifted) · `organizing` ≈ `managing-operations` / `ops.
  561.group` (animating lifted).

## Consequence (the work this implies)

The scale-2 packages should be **(re)derived from their scale-1 faculty + the spiral lift** (the agent-sheaf over a social base), not hand-invented — so `computing`, `gaming`, and `organizing`/`managing-operations` get a principled re-derivation as "faculty lifted along the agent sheaf," and `socializing` is authored the same way.
Each stays schema-first on `relating-experimental`, four-faced, EAVT-witnessed, exactly like the scale-1 packages.
The lift itself (faculty + agent-sheaf → scale-2 faculty) is a reusable construction worth a primitive.

## Open questions

- The exact **social-base carrier**: a sheaf of agents over what base (a communication graph? a common-ground site?).
  This sets socializing's domain/codomain the way `Meme` set communicating's.
- Whether the binder at scale *n* (`organizing`) is **literally `animating` re-applied** (pure self-similarity) or carries added governance structure (offices/duties/federation).
- How far the spiral runs — finite scales, or transfinite (federations of federations), matching the descent's own regime ladder.
- Naming: `gaming` returns at scale 2; confirm the scale-1/scale-2 names (playing/gaming, knowing/computing, communicating/socializing, animating/organizing) are the ones to commit to.
