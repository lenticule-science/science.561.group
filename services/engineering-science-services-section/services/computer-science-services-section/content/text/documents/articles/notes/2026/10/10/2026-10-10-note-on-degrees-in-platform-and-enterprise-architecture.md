# What the Leader's question asks

The Leader names the work: "technological platformization", "corporate technical systems architecture standardization", "platform engineering and systems standardization", as distinct from deduplication or bug fixing (2026-09-11 23:49-23:51). The question is which university gives a degree in it.

# What exists

1. **Enterprise architecture.** Penn State's World Campus offers a Master of Enterprise Architecture, delivered online, in enterprise architecture and business transformation, with at least 33 credits covering enterprise architecture foundations, business architecture, information technology architecture, enterprise security and risk architecture, organizational leadership, strategic management and financial management, and a Master of Professional Studies in Enterprise Architecture for professionals moving to roles with enterprise-wide scope and authority. ([Penn State World Campus](https://www.worldcampus.psu.edu/brochure/1396/view); [graduate bulletin](https://undergraduate.bulletins.psu.edu/graduate/programs/majors/enterprise-architecture-business-transformation/)).
2. **Systems architecture inside systems engineering.** The University of Maryland's master's in systems engineering teaches system architecture as the driver of systems and enterprise engineering decisions at many levels (course ENPM808, [Testudo](https://app.testudo.umd.edu/soc/202401/ENPM/ENPM808)).
3. **Architecture-based enterprise systems engineering** is listed by a third-party aggregator as a two-year master's for senior engineers and engineering managers with five years of experience ([listing](https://ladder.8b.africa/universities/program/cWvHdPc7YZuoyL2nBmkDpC)); its details are not verified.
4. A Higher School of Economics course in enterprise architecture sits inside a big-data systems master's program (2019/2020 page, possibly outdated) ([HSE](https://www.hse.ru/en/edu/courses/292691665)).

No dedicated degree titled platform engineering appeared in these results, and the job postings found for platform architects ask for a bachelor's degree and at least twelve years of experience, which suggests experience carries more weight than a dedicated degree.
