# Transfinite-bounded-regime substrates have a countable-firing-ordinal-indexed $H^1$ engineering envelope

**Claim.** Every conformant [predicated computer](../objects/predicated-computer.md) whose [regime record](../objects/regime-record.md) declares the *transfinite-bounded* regime — equivalently, with [closure-rule language](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/closure-rule-language.md) containing $\forall$-blocks (non-Scott-continuous closure), and substrate state-space cofinality countable — has the following engineering envelope:

1. **Convergence.** The settle iteration's closure ordinal $\kappa$ lies in $(\omega, \omega_1)$ — a specific countable ordinal beyond $\omega$ but bounded below the first uncountable. Convergence requires transfinite-aware fuel discipline.
2. **Audit cohomology.** The [inconsistency sheaf](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md)'s $H^1$ has *one generator per [non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/concepts/non-scott-continuous-firing-ordinal.md)* in $L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa)$, per [transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md). Audit must enumerate $H^1$-class generators by firing ordinal.
3. **Federation.** Endpoint-state shipping is *not* admissible without loss; federation must ship trajectory (the firing-ordinal record), or scope traffic to a Scott-continuous prefix.
4. **Snapshot.** Endpoint-only snapshots lose $H^1$-content; snapshots must record the firing-ordinal pattern to be replay-fidelity-correct.
5. **Realizability.** The realizable $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-subsets are exactly *initial segments of countable limit ordinals* parametrized by $\kappa$ (per [transfinite-bounded-regime-realizability](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-realizability.md)) — the substrate's expressive range over its rule-language fragment.

The envelope is what the engineering must *engineer for* in a transfinite-bounded substrate: $H^1$ is non-trivial and indexed by firing ordinals, federation and snapshots are firing-ordinal-aware, the engine's settle is transfinite-aware.

## Why this holds

The five envelope items are substrate-level instances of the transfinite-bounded math results:

**Convergence (item 1).** Per [regime-classification-for-substrate](regime-classification-for-substrate.md), transfinite-bounded regime requires $\forall$-block rules and countable state-space cofinality. Per [four-regime-dichotomy](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/four-regime-dichotomy.md), this gives $\omega < \kappa < \omega_1$. The closure operator is non-Scott-continuous: rules with $\forall$-block antecedents over infinite entity-set ranges fire at limit ordinals where the join becomes available, producing the firing-ordinal pattern. The engine's [settle](../morphisms/settle.md) operator must support transfinite stage admission per [wavefront-termination](wavefront-termination.md).

**Audit cohomology (item 2).** Per [transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md), $H^1(\mathbf{T}^*_{\leq \kappa}, \mathcal{F}_U) \cong \bigoplus_{\mu \in L_{\mathrm{ns}}(\kappa)} \mathbb{Z}$, with one generator per non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal below $\kappa$, generated by the cell newly added at $\mu$. This applies to the inconsistency-side component $\mathcal{F}_U^{(\top)}$ when the cells newly added at $\mu$ are $\top$-cells. The substrate's [query-inconsistency-cohomology](../morphisms/query-inconsistency-cohomology.md) operator must enumerate by firing-ordinal traversal of the [inconsistency-sheaf record](../objects/inconsistency-sheaf-record.md)'s `top-cells` indexed by step ordinal.

**Federation (item 3).** Per [federation-convergence](federation-convergence.md), federation between transfinite-bounded substrates requires each side's federation traffic to ship the firing-ordinal pattern (not just endpoint state) — otherwise the receiver has no way to reconstruct the $H^1$-class structure. Endpoint-state-only shipping silently drops $H^1$-classes, leading to federated substrates with the wrong cohomological signature.

**Snapshot (item 4).** Per [snapshot-replay-fidelity](snapshot-replay-fidelity.md), snapshots in the transfinite-bounded regime must record the firing-ordinal pattern (which step-ordinal each $\top$-cell entered at). Endpoint-only snapshots lose this pattern; replay from such a snapshot cannot reconstruct the firing-ordinal sequence and so cannot reproduce subsequent settle invocations' $H^1$-classes.

**Realizability (item 5).** Per [transfinite-bounded-regime-realizability](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-realizability.md), the bare $\Pi_1$+$<$ rule-language fragment realizes $L_{\mathrm{ns}}$-subsets that are exactly initial segments of countable limit ordinals up to $\kappa$ — a one-parameter family. This is the substrate's expressive range over the bare fragment; richer fragments (named-ordinal entity-equality, ordinal-arithmetic) extend the family per [rule-language-fragment-realizability-refinement](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/rule-language-fragment-realizability-refinement.md), with the engineering envelope correspondingly extending.

## Engineering consequence

A transfinite-bounded-regime substrate is the *transfinite-but-bounded* case — the engineering envelope must support transfinite settlement at countable depths, with $H^1$ non-trivial and firing-ordinal-indexed. Concretely:

- **The engine is transfinite-aware.** Fuel policies declare transfinite-stage admission with bounded-cofinality termination. Settle invocations may proceed past $\omega$, with each non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal recorded as a substrate event.
- **Audit enumerates $H^1$-classes.** Audit queries on the inconsistency-sheaf record must enumerate generators indexed by firing ordinal; the audit machinery includes a firing-ordinal-traversal primitive.
- **Federation ships trajectory.** Federation handshakes between transfinite-bounded substrates include the firing-ordinal pattern; the receiver reconstructs the $H^1$-classes from the trajectory. Federation between transfinite-bounded and Scott-continuous (or strict) substrates requires the transfinite side to scope its traffic to a Scott-continuous prefix per [regime-discipline](teaching/explanations/regime-discipline.md).
- **Snapshots record trajectory.** Snapshot storage includes the firing-ordinal pattern up to the snapshot's step ordinal. Replay from such snapshots reproduces subsequent firing-ordinal sequences byte-for-byte.
- **Equivalence requires firing-ordinal-set comparison.** Per [transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/theorems/transfinite-bounded-regime-h1-generators.md)'s MLD interpretation, two transfinite-bounded settlings are MLD-equivalent iff their firing-ordinal sets agree up to settling-shift — concretely checkable by traversing both records and comparing the firing-ordinal sequences.

Substrates handling infinite knowledge bases with infinitary closure rules whose state-space cofinality is countable — the smallest natural example being the [quantifier-induced transfinite settling](../../../../../../../humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/examples/quantifier-induced-transfinite-settling.md) — live in this regime.

## What the theorem rules out

- **Transfinite-bounded substrates with finite-only fuel discipline.** The substrate aborts at the first non-Scott-continuous firing ordinal, producing partial settlement. The substrate has under-declared its regime.
- **Transfinite-bounded substrates whose audit ignores firing ordinals.** The audit fails to enumerate $H^1$-classes; the substrate's audit-truth (per [audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology](audit-content-via-inconsistency-sheaf-cohomology.md)) is partial.
- **Transfinite-bounded substrates federating endpoint state.** $H^1$-classes are silently dropped at the federation boundary; the federated substrate has incorrect cohomological signature.
- **Transfinite-bounded substrates with endpoint-only snapshots.** Replay from the snapshot cannot reconstruct the firing-ordinal pattern; subsequent settle invocations may produce different $H^1$-classes than the original run.

## What the theorem requires

- The substrate's [regime record](../objects/regime-record.md) declares `regime: transfinite-bounded`, with `rule-language-fragment: with-forall-blocks`, `base-finiteness` declaring at least one infinite component, and `state-space-cofinality: countable`.
- The substrate's [closure-rule system](../objects/closure-rule-system.md) contains $\forall$-block rules per [rule-language-realizability-determines-regime](rule-language-realizability-determines-regime.md).
- The fuel policy supports transfinite stage admission.
- The federation discipline ships trajectory or scopes to a Scott-continuous prefix.
- The snapshot policy records firing-ordinal patterns.

## Position

Third of the four regime-engineering-envelope theorems. The transfinite-bounded envelope is where the substrate's engineering machinery first encounters non-trivial $H^1$ and firing-ordinal patterns; the aperiodic envelope extends this to uncountable depths with direct-limit assembly. The two transfinite envelopes share the same engineering character — trajectory-aware federation, trajectory-recording snapshots, $H^1$-enumerating audit — distinguished by where the closure ordinal lands relative to $\omega_1$.
