# MASH as a 4D Spatiotemporal Knowledge Manifold, with OQRA as its Nuclear Quartet

This document is the grounding for the new `mash-library` construction work.
It answers, from the adjoint predication paper and the relationality
research: what specifically MASH *is* as a manifold, how the four
ontological primitives (Object/Quality/Relation/Act) sit inside that
manifold, and which structural moves in the library work have principled
mathematical answers versus which require design judgment.

## 1. The Manifold

### 1.1 Three spatial axes

A bilattice-valued SPO system $\mathcal{S}$ (the adjoint paper, §2–3)
consists of triples $\langle s, p, o \rangle$ each carrying a value in
$\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2} = \{\bot, t, f, \top\}$.

The three coordinate axes are:

- **Subject axis** $S$ — the set of entities that can occur as subject
- **Predicate axis** $P$ — the set of predicates (relationship-names)
- **Object axis** $O$ — the set of entities that can occur as object

These are literal dimensions of the substrate. Every claim is a point
$(s, p, o) \in S \times P \times O$ with a value attached.

### 1.2 One temporal axis

The cascade (the proof engine's `assert`) iterates inference rules to a
fixpoint (Fitting 1991's closure; §5.2 of the paper). Time in MASH is
*cascade depth* — how many settlement steps have fired since a given
claim entered the wavefront.

**Proposition 8 of the paper**: *All orbits under iterated left-$\odot$
or left-$\Rightarrow$ reach fixed points in at most 2 steps.*

This is not "fast settlement" — it is bounded-depth temporal dynamics.
The manifold's temporal axis is discrete, finite, and terminates
deterministically for every input. The cascade is not an approximation
of continuous time; it is an algebraically exact iteration whose orbit
length is $\leq 2$ per morphism instance.

### 1.3 Chu-space fiber at each predicate

**Theorem 3 of the paper (Jakl 2025)**: $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}
\cong \mathrm{Chu}(\mathbf{2}, 0)$.

**Theorem 4 of the paper**: For each predicate $p$, the fiber $(E, E,
v(-, p, -))$ is an object of $\mathrm{Chu}(\mathbf{2}, 0)$, where $E$ is
the entity set on both axes and $v(-, p, -): E \times E \to \mathbf{2}
\otimes \mathbf{2}$ is the valuation.

**This is the content of "4D spatiotemporal manifold" precisely:**

> The substrate is a 3-dimensional spatial grid $(S, P, O)$ of points,
> each carrying a $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$ value — equivalently,
> a $\mathrm{Chu}(\mathbf{2}, 0)$ structure. The grid evolves over
> discrete cascade-depth time according to the five morphisms until
> fixpoint (at most 2 morphism-firings per instance).

The "Chu-space fiber at each point" wording is slightly imprecise. More
accurately: *each predicate-fiber* is a Chu-space object. An individual
$(s, p, o)$ cell carries a $\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$ value, which
under Jakl's isomorphism corresponds to a specific Chu element — but
the full Chu-space structure lives on the predicate-slice, not on the
point.

### 1.4 Priestley-dual topology

**§5.4 of the paper, Rivieccio & Jung 2012**: Bilattices are two-sorted
structures whose Priestley duals are pairs of Priestley spaces linked by
continuous order-preserving maps.

This gives the manifold a **topological dual**. Open sets in the paired
Priestley spaces correspond to "observably-coherent groupings" of
substrate content — in operational terms: natural region boundaries for
things like permission scopes, perception horizons, "what counts as
here," consensus boundaries. These boundaries are not invented
in the library work; they are forced by the dual topology of the values
the substrate carries.

## 2. OQRA as the Nuclear Quartet

### 2.1 The four primitives, per the library

From `library/terms/thing.md` and the triage entity definition, a
**thing** is the composite of Object / Quality / Relation / Act:

- **Object** — what is constituted; being; the intrinsic abstract
- **Quality** — what can be said of objects; the predicate-face
- **Relation** — connection, constraint; sequencing across states
- **Act** — generation, settlement; the dynamics-phase

From `library/theories/science/theories/relationality/terms/relational-entity.md`,
these map onto the **nuclear quartet** — the four-phase positions
generated by the saturation nucleus $\sigma$ (backward-closure,
consistency with history) and the transfer nucleus $\Delta$
(forward-closure, extension across all one-step futures):

| OQRA primitive | Nuclear position | $\sigma$-stable? | $\Delta$-stable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Object | Fixed (both closures trivial) | yes | yes |
| Quality | $\sigma$-stable sub-algebra | yes | no |
| Relation | $\Delta$-stable sub-algebra | no | yes |
| Act | Free (subject to both closures) | closure-terminal | closure-terminal |

### 2.2 Mapping OQRA into the manifold

**Object.** In MASH's substrate, an Object is a *subject-term* — a stable
coordinate on the $S$ axis whose identity does not move under the
cascade. "Xanadu" is an object. Once minted, it persists as a reference
point. The cascade settles claims *about* the object; it does not settle
the object itself. Object-terms are the fixed points of the identity
construction — the doubly-stable $H^*$ elements of the relational-entity
framework.

**Quality.** A Quality is a *monadic predicate* applied to an object:
`(subject, has-quality-X, object)` where the object-slot may be a value,
a term, or a degenerate reference. The quality-fiber $\mathrm{Fib}(p)$
for a quality-predicate is a Chu-space object whose valuation depends
primarily on the subject axis (the object-slot is often a type-tag or
degenerate). Qualities are $\sigma$-stable — consistent-with-history —
because a quality, once settled, remains a truthful fact about its
subject until explicitly retracted.

**Relation.** A Relation is a *dyadic predicate* where both subject and
object axes are meaningful and the valuation $v(-, p, -): E \times E \to
\mathbf{2} \otimes \mathbf{2}$ is nontrivial in both arguments.
"Parent-of" is a relation; it requires two entities. The relation-fiber's
Chu structure is genuinely two-sided. Relations are $\Delta$-stable —
witnessed-by-all-immediate-extensions — because a relation, once settled,
constrains all future states that include either of its arguments.

**Act.** An Act is a *cascade step* — the temporal dynamics itself. When
a claim is witnessed, an act has occurred; when a composition fires, an
act has occurred; when the cascade settles, acts have converged. Acts
are the dynamics-phase in the nuclear quartet — no closure kind because
they ARE the closing. In the substrate, acts are the application of the
five morphisms (specialization, inversion, composition, fusion,
implication) to settled claims.

### 2.3 A thing is a region of the manifold

**A thing, operationally, is a stable sub-fiber of the substrate:** a
subject-term $t$ together with the settled claims
$\{(t, p, o, v) : \text{for all } p, o \text{ where } v \neq \bot\}$
that constitute what-can-be-said of it. The thing is not a record in
a table; it is the settled region of the manifold indexed by $t$ on the
subject axis.

This is the Wiener/Barad framing of `thing`: the thing IS its pattern of
settled claims, not an object that has properties. "Xanadu" as a thing
consists of everything the substrate has settled about Xanadu, not a
row in an `entities` table with Xanadu as its id.

## 3. Principled vs Judgment

### 3.1 Structural moves with mathematical answers

These are forced by the algebra; they require no design judgment:

- **Specialization.** Formally a restriction of valuation (paper §3.2).
  `Room specializes-as Container` means the Room-fiber inherits the
  Container-fiber's structure via a bilattice homomorphism. The
  morphism's action is determined: restrict the valuation; preserve
  pointwise bilattice operations.

- **Inversion.** Pointwise negation $\sim$ (Fitting 1991, paper §3.2).
  `parent-of inverts-as child-of` means the predicate's valuation is
  negated and the subject/object slots swapped. The morphism's action
  is determined.

- **Composition (knowledge-meet).** The cascade's $\wedge_k$ (paper
  §3.2, Proposition 7). Two predicate-fibers share an $(s, o)$
  coordinate; their values meet knowledge-wise. The morphism's action
  is determined.

- **Fusion.** The Chu tensor $\odot$ (Theorems 1 & 3). Truth-extending.
  The morphism's action is determined.

- **Implication.** The Chu linear implication $\Rightarrow$ (Theorems 1 &
  3). Restricting. The morphism's action is determined.

- **Natural region boundaries.** Forced by Priestley duality (§5.4).
  Consensus scopes, perception horizons, "what counts as here" — the
  dual topology dictates the candidate partitions.

- **Fixed-point guarantees.** Proposition 8 — orbits reach fixed points
  in $\leq 2$ steps. Cascade termination is forced.

- **Decidability.** Corollary 2 — the modal calculus on $\mathbf{2}
  \otimes \mathbf{2}$ is decidable. Query answering on the substrate is
  decidable.

### 3.2 Structural moves requiring design judgment

These are underdetermined by the algebra; the library has to choose:

- **Subject-term minting.** Which entities to name as first-class
  substrate subjects. A room, a player, a character-sheet — all
  candidates. The algebra doesn't decide this; the endeavor and its
  content do.

- **Predicate vocabulary design.** What predicates to declare. The
  algebra can host any predicate; which predicates to declare reflects
  what the library wants to express. `has-name`, `has-cardinality`,
  `contains` — each is a design choice about how the library carves up
  the world.

- **Monadic vs dyadic classification.** Whether a given predicate is a
  Quality or a Relation depends on whether the object-slot matters
  structurally or is a carrier for a value/type-tag. Some cases are
  obvious (`parent-of` is a relation; `has-name` is a quality with a
  string-valued object). Many are genuinely contextual.

- **Composition-rule authorship.** Which compositions to declare — for
  example, "is-a Room ∧ contains thing → thing located-in Room" — is
  inference-rule design. The algebra supports any composition that fits
  the five morphism patterns; choosing which to declare is library
  judgment.

- **Domain boundaries.** Where to partition the predicate vocabulary
  into sub-domains (mud, ops, session, etc.). Priestley duality gives
  candidate partitions; which to use is design judgment over those
  candidates.

- **Content (claim corpus).** The specific claims that instantiate
  things — which rooms exist, which exits lead where, which offices the
  ship has. This is literary/institutional content, not mathematics.

- **User-facing naming.** How predicates and subject-terms surface to
  humans. `has-located-at` vs `is-in` vs `located-in` — the algebra
  doesn't care; humans do.

## 4. Consequences for the Library Construction

The library work is design judgment on top of a substrate whose
mathematical structure is fixed. The fixed structure is **more
determined than the current scattered implementation treats it as**:

1. **Specialization and inversion should be heavily used.** They are
   mathematically free homomorphisms — cheap to declare, they carry
   semantic content automatically. Every derived predicate should be
   expressed as a specialization or inversion where possible, not as a
   reinvented primitive.

2. **Composition compositions are the carrier of complex semantics.**
   Knowledge-meet, fusion, implication — these are where the library's
   inference rules live. Writing them once at the OQRA level gives them
   to every specialized thing for free.

3. **Natural regions are principled.** When partitioning content into
   sub-libraries or perception scopes, the Priestley-dual topology
   gives candidate partitions. Pick from those; don't invent.

4. **Thing-as-fiber-region is the operational definition.** A thing is
   identified with its stable region of the manifold. Library code
   constructs things by witnessing claims, not by instantiating
   record-types.

5. **The temporal axis is bounded.** Cascade depth $\leq 2$ per morphism
   instance means the library can treat settlement as effectively
   synchronous. No need for long-running reconciliation loops; the
   cascade finishes.

The Wiener/Barad framing is then literal, not metaphorical: things
intra-act through relational settlement on a bounded-dynamics manifold
with known topological dual, using five algebraically-forced homomorphism
classes, in at most two temporal steps per rule-firing, with decidable
modal calculus over the whole. The OQRA four-tuple is how we describe
things in terms the manifold natively supports. The library's task is
to write that description for the endeavor's content.
