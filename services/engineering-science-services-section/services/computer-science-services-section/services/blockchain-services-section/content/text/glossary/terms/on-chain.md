An ***on-chain*** interpretive condition is one in which meaning, identity, and coherence are **resolved through traceable consensus mechanisms**—like a [[blockchain]].

On-chain systems:
- Require **public legibility**
- Encode **consensus-based resolution**
- Incentivize **coherence over contradiction**
- Record **irreversible state changes**
- Operate on **permissioned or permissionless rulesets**

In on-chain conditions, what cannot be **modeled, signed, or resolved** is treated as nonexistent.

This condition supports modulation, but only when all parties are traceable and all actions are legible.
