# A Paraconsistent, Transfinite, Linear Approach to Agent Integration

## 1. Summary

The 561 Group's premise is a paraconsistent, transfinitely valued linear logic, built in Obsmetrologia from Fregean predication, Belnap FOUR and Chu spaces. Measured against its own source, that premise is a Belnap-bilattice-valued residuated algebra with a Chu-space duality, a settling dynamics and a small sheaf layer. It is not yet a logic: the Lean library has no consequence relation, no sequent calculus, no cut elimination, no exponentials and no consistency operator [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. "Transfinite" names the number of settling stages (the closure ordinal), not a transfinite set of values [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-blueprint]. The ordinal-indexed values proposed in the memo do not exist in the code [corpus-memo]. The "linear" part is thin: over FOUR the fusion is idempotent at three of four values, so a FOUR-valued capability can be copied without detection [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

What survives audit is smaller than the premise and sufficient for the problem. Three results are solid and match outside theory. First, claims merge by knowledge-order join and the closure re-applied after a merge gives the same result as serial gathering, a fact stated in Lean for valuations [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. This is the lattice-merge shape of CRDTs, and the CALM theorem says monotone computation of this kind needs no coordination [shapiro2011][hellerstein2020][ameloot2013]. Second, a contradiction is a held value (⊤ₖ), not a crash, which is Belnap's design [belnap1977][arieliavron1996]. Third, a sheaf of local sections over a cover glues uniquely when the sections agree on overlaps [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

The derived approach is a partition. Everything that is a claim, an observation, a proposal, an objection or a receipt is a monotone, attributed, append-only record joined in the knowledge order, and needs no coordinator. Everything that asserts absence or order is non-monotone and has exactly one owner per cover: advancing a pin, landing to `main`, choosing among contested claims, granting an exclusive lease, revoking, reading "settled" and reclaiming superseded records. A sharp consequence, derived here and not stated by any source: "settled" (a cell reads exactly t) is itself a non-monotone query in the knowledge order, because a later contrary claim moves t to ⊤ₖ. Settlement therefore needs an owner who fixes an epoch. The Group's hypothesis that parallel agents need a judge "exactly where H¹ ≠ 0" is not supported as stated. The cohomological obstruction is sufficient but not necessary for global inconsistency in the closest published setting, so the supportable claim is that a judge is needed at every non-monotone step and at least where an obstruction or an unresolved ⊤ₖ exists [amb2011][hellerstein2020].

The most important gap between the logic and action is the one the paraconsistent literature names: a consistency operator ∘, from the logics of formal inconsistency, that licenses classical use of a claim once someone accountable asserts it is consistent [sep-paraconsistent][carnielli2007]. The Group's prose already has the object: an acknowledgment (ACK) by a different party over an independent channel [corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. This report identifies ACK with ∘ (Inferred) and derives twenty requirement candidates, each marked Inferred, that confirm, refine or replace the sixteen in the earlier report [corpus-integration-report]. The largest formal gaps, ranked by how much they block, are: no carrier for repository facts (the commit DAG and the session-to-cell bridge), no consequence relation with ∘, no linear carrier or exponential, and no obstruction theory in which H¹ can be nonzero for the settling sheaf.

Status labels used below. `Proved:` is a Lean theorem whose statement an auditor read in source with a proof term present (nobody built Lean), or a published theorem. `Defined:` is a Lean or JavaScript definition without a theorem about it. `Law:` is a statement of a Group theory document, of the Leader's ruling or of an agent-written note, with no Lean proof of the engineering reading. `Hypothesis:`, `Open:` and `Sketch:` repeat the source's own label. `Measured:`, `Observed:`, `Inferred:`, `Disputed:` and `Unknown:` are as in the earlier report. "Secondary" marks a source read only through a summary, and "unverified" marks a claim whose cited source was not located.

## 2. What the Group's logic is, as built and as premised

### 2.1 Value domain and orders

`Defined:` FOUR is the twist product of the two-element Boolean lattice. A reading has two Boolean slots, pos and neg. The four readings are ⊥ₖ (told none), t (told true), f (told false) and ⊤ₖ (told both, "a held contradiction"). Two orders sit on them. The knowledge order ≤ₖ is slotwise: ⊥ₖ is below t and f, which are below ⊤ₖ. The truth order ≤ₜ puts f below ⊥ₖ and ⊤ₖ, which are below t. Negation swaps the slots [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The general carrier is the twist product over any integral commutative residuated lattice R, with `leK`, `leT` and `neg (a) = (a.2, a.1)`, and FOUR is its instance at R = Two [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. `Proved:` the lattice laws, distributivity, interlacing, involutive negation and monotonicity of negation in both orders, by finite exhaustion [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

This is the Ginsberg and Belnap structure: a bilattice with a truth order and an information order, built by the standard pair construction [belnap1977][ginsberg1988][fitting1991]. Belnap designed it for a machine told things by several sources, which must hold contradictory input without exploding [belnap1977][easterbrookchechik2001]. The premise is a faithful reading of the literature, not an exotic one.

A second carrier counts. `Defined:` `Record` holds a pair of natural numbers (told-true count, told-false count). `Proved:` merging by addition never destroys held contradiction (`topMass_add`, where topMass is the minimum of the two counts), and the reading readout cannot tell counting merge from idempotent join (`reading_add_eq_reading_joinK`) [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. `Record` has max, min and add but no fusion or residual. Resource counting therefore lives in a carrier that has no linear connectives.

### 2.2 Connectives, and what is and is not linear

`Defined:` fusion ⊙, residual ⇒ and par ⅋ on FOUR come from the twist product: `fuse a b = (a₁∧b₁, (a₁→b₂)∧(b₁→a₂))`, and par is the De Morgan dual of fusion. `Proved:` the adjunction `fuse_leT_iff` (a ⊙ b ≤ₜ c iff a ≤ₜ b ⇒ c) holds for every integral commutative residuated lattice R; fusion is commutative and has a unit; the lax MIX `a ⊙ b ≤ₜ a ⅋ b` holds and is strict at (t, f); and the locus where fusion equals par is exactly {a = ⊤ₖ or b = ⊤ₖ or (a = b ≠ ⊥ₖ)} [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

Three corrections to the prose follow. `Open:` associativity of fusion was not found among the proved lemmas, so "commutative monoid" is half-proved. `Defined:` the name `four_star_autonomous` states the ⊗⊣⊸ adjunction plus involutive negation. It does not state a dualizing object, the isomorphism FOUR ≅ Chu(2,0), or ∗-autonomy of the category of Chu spaces over FOUR; the cited FOUR ≅ Chu(2,0) is a prose citation of Jakl in a teaching article [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-from-mark-to-settling]. `Defined:` the tensor unit is ⟨1,1⟩ = ⊤ₖ, not t, so the multiplicative unit is the held contradiction and the algebra is non-integral.

`Inferred:` the following table was derived by hand from the Lean definitions (rows are the left operand) and agrees with the proved `readingMix_strict` entries.

| ⊙ | ⊥ₖ | t | f | ⊤ₖ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⊥ₖ | f | ⊥ₖ | f | ⊥ₖ |
| t | ⊥ₖ | t | f | t |
| f | f | f | f | f |
| ⊤ₖ | ⊥ₖ | t | f | ⊤ₖ |

Contraction (A ≤ₜ A ⊙ A) fails only at ⊥ₖ, where two silences fuse to f. Weakening in the form A ⊙ B ≤ₜ A fails only at (⊤ₖ, ⊥ₖ) and (⊤ₖ, t). The quantale used by the enriched-sheaf layer multiplies by meetₖ, an idempotent product [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. Consequently a resource valued in FOUR is not consumed by use. The model validates contraction at t, f and ⊤ₖ, which a model of linear logic may do, but it then cannot enforce exactly-once. Exactly-once content exists only in `Record` tallies and in the work-integer ledger of §2.4. `Inferred:` the "linear" label is aspirational for the connectives and structurally true only of the twist coupling.

There is no calculus. `Observed:` the only syntax with a proof theory is a simply-typed λ-calculus with confluence and the Curry-Howard-Lambek correspondence, and it is cartesian closed with structural rules built in. The Lambek file defines the algebra of a non-commutative residuated lattice and says Wurm completeness and Pentus' theorem are "documented here but not formalized". Greps found no entailment relation, no designated-value semantics, no exponentials, no `bang`, no cut elimination and no consistency operator [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The memo itself lists cut elimination, explosion control and transfinite joins, meets and residuals as open risks, and says the synthesis "is not automatic" and "not, as such, a single canonical system in the literature" [corpus-memo].

Chu spaces. `Defined:` a Chu space is points, states and a matrix valued in FOUR. `Proved:` duality (transpose and negate) is an involution on objects, Chu maps are adjoint pairs with identity and contravariant composition, and the dual of a map is involutive. Not built in Lean: tensor and par of spaces, the canonical MIX transform, additives, exponentials, a dualizing object. The JavaScript library has additives (`withChu`, `plusChu`) and an exhaustive law check on small carriers, but no tensor, par or exponential [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-js-metrologic]. `Defined:` the claim "Fregean predication forces FOUR and Chu" is a bundle whose three fields are finite correspondences "true by the definition of `Reading` as two slots"; the Chu space is the claim-by-valuation evaluation matrix [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The genuine forcing theorem (`orbital_exhaustive`) was located by name only.

### 2.3 Where paraconsistency lives

`Defined:` ⊤ₖ is a value, the top of ≤ₖ ("more information", not "everything follows"). `Proved:` the liar closure `a ↦ a ∨ₖ ¬a` has fixed points exactly ⊥ₖ and ⊤ₖ, and the settled liar is never t or f [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. `Inferred:` the algebra has an order-theoretic non-collapse, since ⊤ₖ ⊙ ¬⊤ₖ = ⊤ₖ and for a in {⊥ₖ, t, f} the product a ⊙ ¬a is f. This is not a statement that a consequence relation fails explosion, because no such relation exists. Paraconsistency in the technical sense is a property of a consequence relation [sep-paraconsistent]. The Group has the paraconsistent values and the paraconsistent dynamics, and lacks the consequence relation.

`Defined:` the sheaf layer carries three designation masks, dT = {t}, dK = {t, ⊤ₖ} and dTop = {⊤ₖ}, and a negative mirror dNeg = {f, ⊤ₖ} [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. `Inferred:` dK is the designated set of Arieli and Avron's logical bilattices (the values at least as true as ⊤ₖ's truth component), and dT is the "exactly true" set; the choice between them is the choice the Group has not made at the level of consequence [arieliavron1996]. `Defined:` the controlled-explosion operator Cons(A) of the memo has no counterpart in Lean; the JavaScript library has the predicate `isConsistentReading` only [corpus-memo][corpus-js-metrologic].

The Group's prose records a working discipline. Contradiction is held, shown, localized and resolved only by a recorded choice per a declared recipe catalog, never by arrival order, majority or last writer [corpus-theory-obsmetrologia][corpus-theory-locution][corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. `Law:` a note claims "one discipline, seven surfaces" and carries `status: proved` in its frontmatter, but its dependencies are prose notes and no Lean was found for the repair catalog; treat it as `Law:` [corpus-note-paraconsistency-substrate].

### 2.4 What "transfinite" means

`Defined:` `Ord` is a Brouwer-tree ordinal (zero, successor, or limit of a ℕ-indexed sequence) with `Le` and `add`. `Proved:` every `Ord` is countably cofinal, so uncountable cofinality (κ ≥ ω₁) is "a genuine boundary of this dependency-free ordinal encoding", and `regimeOfOrdHonest` is never `aperiodic` [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-blueprint]. The four closure regimes are strict (κ < ω), Scott (κ = ω), transfinite-bounded and aperiodic. `Defined:` `regimeOfOrd` still takes the Scott-continuity and cofinality flags as Boolean parameters, so the regime is partly asserted. `Proved:` over FOUR every inflationary map settles by stage 3 (`settles_by_three`), so every settling on FOUR is in the strict regime and "the transfinite limit collapses to the finite settled value" [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

Values beyond FOUR exist only in counting carriers. `Defined:` `ORecord` adds ω on each axis and is an interlaced bilattice that embeds FOUR. `Proved:` its increment is inflationary, reaches no finite fixed point, and settles at ω [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. This is κ = ω exhibited on a derived carrier: one limit point, not a hierarchy of ordinal-indexed values. `Defined:` the ordinal-indexed flow `tflowOf` uses the finite orbit at finite ordinals and the settled value at every limit, so its limit case is constant, and the blueprint marks the ordinal-indexed settling orbit `\notready`: "the one missing construction is the limit-case countable ≤ₖ-join of valuations" [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-blueprint]. `Defined:` the cell complex is transfinite only in the cardinality of the cell set, "NOT in cells of transfinite dimension" [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

Against the memo, which proposes pairs of ordinal-indexed support over a complete lattice and says Belnap is recovered when the lattice is {0,1}, the built theory has pairs over Bool, ℕ and ℕ∪{ω}. The generic `TwistProduct R` leaves a formal slot for an ordinal-valued R, but no ordinal residuated lattice instance exists and `Ord` has no meet, join or residual [corpus-memo][corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. `Inferred:` "transfinitely valued" is true of the memo, false of the Lean, and true of the Group's teaching prose only in the sense that the closure may run past ω [corpus-from-mark-to-settling]. The honest description is transfinitely indexed settling over finitely or ω-boundedly valued readings. `Defined:` a separate natural-number ledger prices ascent: `kHeight` (⊥ₖ 0, t and f 1, ⊤ₖ 2), commit, mint and jump steps priced 1, 1 and 2, and `Proved:` `cellFlux_decomposition`, `budget_splits` and `truth_loss_is_record_gain` (every cell that stops firing as truth under ascent becomes inconsistent) [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The JavaScript copy carries a disclaimer that it "does NOT independently establish WIT evidence, ownership or a settled economic transfer" [corpus-js-metrologic].

### 2.5 Sheaf, H⁰ and H¹, and their real strength

The site is bitopological: objects are (predicate base, settling stratum τ, clopen of FOUR's dual), with fixed-fiber covers and no temporal covers. Sections are integer cochains on a finite cell set, restriction is a Boolean mask, and designation masks split the sheaf into truth and inconsistency parts by a short exact sequence that splits [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].

`Proved:` merge of valuations is the least upper bound (`section_lub`), the sheaf is separated, and the Čech datum `Cech1` glues: `h1_vanishes` holds for every datum. The file says this plainly: the conjuncts "hold unconditionally in this Čech model: they are proved for every Čech datum D, with no regime hypothesis at all", and the regime gate `cohomologyVanishes r = true iff r in {strict, scott}` is "a declared table of the regimes, not a computation of H¹". The genuine nonzero-H¹ phenomenon "is not witnessed here" [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The prose table that says H¹ is nonzero in the transfinite and aperiodic regimes and zero in the Scott and finite regimes is therefore `Disputed:` against the Lean model, which proves vanishing without any regime hypothesis, and the theory of agentic computing admits it is "recorded as a table, not a theorem" [corpus-theory-agentic-computing][corpus-theory-obsmetrologia]. `Inferred:` the gluing theorem is strong but cheap. It certifies that if overlaps agree, a unique global section exists. It cannot certify that scopes overlap safely, because it never fails.

Nonzero obstruction classes appear only in other, smaller models.
- `Defined:` a graded two-term complex yields a six-term exact sequence H⁰ₜ → H⁰ₖ → H⁰ₜₒₚ → H¹ₜ → H¹ₖ → H¹ₜₒₚ. `Proved:` exactness at the four interior spots; the connecting map vanishes under a static-mask hypothesis; and it is nonzero on two constructed instances, a measurement complex and a quench, where the connecting class is a truth coboundary iff the record's two copies agree [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean].
- `Defined:` a cover-nerve count gives H⁰ rank = components and H¹ rank = cycle rank. `Proved:` H¹ rank = 0 iff the nerve is a forest, and the index balance H⁰ − H¹ = |V| − |E|. `Open:` "the value-level converse, that a cycle actually obstructs a monotone global section unless a total order is imposed on the cycle's cells" [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. The "Landauer" cost `landauerBits N = H1rank N` is true by `rfl`, a definitional rename; the physical reading is interpretation [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-theory-agentic-computing].

`Open:` the statement H¹ = H¹ₜ ⊕ H¹ₜₒₚ, used in the concurrent-game theory to say what concurrent incompatible proposals produce, is not a theorem. What is proved is the degree-0 splitting, the six-term sequence, and the fact that the connecting map is the exact measure of non-splitting [corpus-theory-cgs][corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. No theorem links the nerve rank to failure of gluing in the `Cech1` model, and none lifts the graded complex to a settling sheaf over the site.

## 3. What the prose theories prescribe for agent work

### 3.1 Standing of the prose

`Observed:` the ten theories and the synthesis (theory of agentic computing) are all dated 2026-10-15 and co-authored by the Leader and a Claude model. They are syntheses of the Leader's statements, not independent mathematics, and the Group's own rule 9 ("don't trust prose") applies to them [corpus-theory-agentic-computing][corpus-theory-agency]. The audit found no Lean for agents (laws A1 to A11), work, governing (G1 to G8, R1 to R7), WIT and ACK as protocols, or dispatch. The Lean that exists is the abstract settling, closure and cohomology core, plus attention-budget bookkeeping in the economics package [corpus-economics-lean]. Searches of the theory texts found no mention of submodules, gitlinks, pins, branches, pull requests, CI or git beyond one sentence about "a single mutable checkout". Everything the theory says about pins and landing is therefore derived, not stated.

### 3.2 Agents, sessions and coordinators

`Law:` an agent is a durable inhabitant, the least fixed point of reflexive closure (A1), never flatly true or false about itself, hence FOUR (A2), with a place in a world, an arena it observes and a strategy it plays [corpus-theory-agency]. A language-model call is an "amnesiac enzyme": a transient process that lowers a barrier and leaves nothing but the settled product; "the enzyme is not the agent", and enzymes hold no state [corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. `Proved:` only the narrow fact that catalysis is a fixed point of firing (`catalyst_is_fixed`, named, not opened). `Law:` the empirical claim that fidelity is set by the completeness of the work order, not the provider, rests on a pilot note that is deleted. `Law:` swarms coordinate at convergent cost without an orchestrator only with bounded per-agent cost, interchangeable agents and stigmergy, and language-model agents fail all three, so coordination must live in the substrate and not in point-to-point messaging (K6, which costs Θ(n²)) [corpus-theory-cgs]. `Law:` the coordinator coordinates and does not work, and a coordinator that works is a "rogue chaperone" [corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. `Law:` law A11 says an agent that queues work for itself cannot escalate authority; its "(proved)" cites an external repository note, not Lean [corpus-theory-agency].

`Inferred:` a Claude Code or Codex process is at most an enzyme invocation in the theory's vocabulary. A session is a stateless worker handed one complete work order, returning a typed result against a declared codomain, with durable state only in the store. An interactive session that both dispatches and implements is the misfold the theory names.

### 3.3 Scope as restriction

`Law:` meaning is a sheaf on histories (R5), entities are constituted by their relations (R2), and "every actor reads a restriction" of the shared history [corpus-theory-relationality]. In the concurrent-game theory the settled world is a sheaf of FOUR cells over a site of areas (a world, a region, a room, a work board), a player has a perception fibre, and its arena is `observe(W restricted to U and P_i)`: "No player reads outside its section, so stealth, secrets and fog are restrictions, not flags" [corpus-theory-cgs]. ### 4.2 Bilattices, FDE and the missing bridge from "both" to action

Belnap's four values were designed for a machine told things by several sources: only true gives true, only false gives false, both gives both, none gives neither, with a truth order and an information order [belnap1977]. Ginsberg built the bilattice presentation and claimed one framework subsumes theorem provers, truth-maintenance systems and default logics [ginsberg1988]. Fitting separated positive and negative information in logic programming, and the Kripke-Kleene operator is monotone in the knowledge order, so it has a least fixed point [fitting1991]. Arieli and Avron gave proof systems for logical bilattices, a family of paraconsistent logics for possibly inconsistent data [arieliavron1996]. Kifer and Subrahmanian generalized the value set to arbitrary annotation lattices [kifersubrahmanian1992]. Easterbrook and Chechik restate Belnap as "incrementally adding information to a database without enforcing consistency" [easterbrookchechik2001]. `Confirms:` "never delete, merge by join, hold contradiction as ⊤ₖ" is Belnap's design, and the Group's charge is Fitting's pair construction. The Group's premise is a faithful reading, and no deployed orchestration of AI agents on Belnap or bilattices was found; there is no direct precedent (`Unknown:` for the 2020 to 2026 literature searched).

FDE has no true conditional. Truth preservation over relational valuations gives First Degree Entailment, and in LP and FDE disjunctive syllogism fails: from A ∨ B and ¬B, A does not follow when B is also told true [sep-paraconsistent][easterbrookchechik2001]. `Inferred:` a rule list with exceptions cannot be read by naive inference over FOUR, so the action layer must read rules, not the logic. The Group's residuated ⇒ is an extra: it is the adjoint of fusion, not the FDE conditional.

`Adds:` the consistency operator. Logics of formal inconsistency encode "consistent" in the object language with ∘, block explosion only when a contradiction is present, and recover it "in a controlled way": a contradiction about a formula asserted consistent entails everything [sep-paraconsistent][carnielli2007]. The Group's memo names Cons(A) and has none in Lean [corpus-memo]. The literature says this is the piece that lets a system move from "both" back to ordinary classical action where consistency is established. `Inferred:` the Group's ACK is the object that plays this role in its prose. An ACK is an attributed assertion by a different party, over an independent channel, that a cell is settled for use, so `ACK(c)` should be modeled as `∘c`. Then acting (landing, advancing a pin, deploying) requires `∘` on the cells it depends on, and the audit trail of ∘ is the ACK record.

Adaptive and preservationist logics are the other bridge: reason as if consistent until an abnormality appears, withdraw inferences when it does, or reason from maximal consistent subsets [sep-paraconsistent]. KR 2024 shows paraconsistent four-valued semantics and repair semantics are incomparable in what they entail, and gives exact-truth-value query operators that separate "both" from "true" at query time [bienvenu2024]. `Inferred:` "never delete" defers the choice that belief revision makes at write time to read time, so the Group needs a named, auditable view operation that selects a consistent substate for acting, alongside the join. Hunter's quasi-classical logic keeps classical-looking connectives and tracks assumptions by labels, which suits a system that needs "who asserted this"; it was read through abstracts only [hunternuseibeh1998].

### 4.3 Inconsistency management in software engineering

The Nuseibeh, Easterbrook and Russo framework is the most direct precedent for the missing operating layer [nuseibeh2000]. Inconsistency is a violated consistency rule between overlapping descriptions; rules have scope so each edit is checked only against rules whose scope contains it; checking is lazy where costly; diagnosis traces the action history with an owner for each action; and handling is a risk-based choice among ignore, defer, circumvent, ameliorate and resolve. Case studies on NASA requirements are qualitative: "Some inconsistencies never get fixed", "the decision to repair an inconsistency is risk based", and the Ariane-5 lesson that a tolerated inconsistency needs a recorded trigger to be re-evaluated on reuse [nuseibeh2000]. `Observed:` there is no quantitative evaluation of ViewPoints, χChek or quasi-classical tools in the retrieved material [easterbrookchechik2001][chechik2003]. `Inferred:` the Group has tolerated inconsistencies with no owner and no trigger: three commits for one section, CLAUDE.md against AGENTS.md. A ⊤ₖ without a handling class, an owner and a re-evaluation trigger is only a better-formatted version of today's undecided conflict.

Easterbrook and Chechik's χbel shows a design trade-off the Group's pair hides. A product lattice preserves who said what at exponential cost in the number of sources, while an abstraction (3-valued, majority) loses it. Their own example is a property false in a merge that "does not allow us to distinguish whether this is because the property is FALSE in each individual viewpoint, or because it is FALSE in one and UNDEF in the other" [easterbrookchechik2001]. `Inferred:` the ⟨for, against⟩ pair is an abstraction of the per-agent vector. Because accountability is a stated goal, keep the vector as provenance behind the pair. Provenance semirings give a general algebra for "which source tuples produced this result" and are complementary to bilattice values, which say what a merged value is [greenkt2007]. No paper combining bilattices with provenance semirings for agent records was found (`Unknown:`).

`Measured:` the one measured neighbor is Allen et al., who use an LLM as the interpretation function of a paraconsistent logic. On balanced N = 400 datasets, bilateral verify/refute judgments raised macro-F1 by about 0.06 but cut coverage by about 0.46, at about twice the time and tokens, and the gain is computed on non-abstained items only, with about half abstained [allen2025]. `Inferred:` this is the abstain-versus-act trade-off the Group meets whenever a claim's charge has both slots nonzero. Dempster-Shafer theory over Belnap-Dunn logic offers a quantitative reading of conflicting evidence (belief and plausibility), an option for weighting agent evidence, not a tested method [bilkova2023]. Multi-agent debate studies find that similar models converge to the majority opinion and that persuasion can override truth, read from snippets only [debate2024]. `Confirms:` the theory's Corollary 5.3 that same-class model agreement is a correlated draw, and `Inferred:` counting votes of same-family agents as independent evidence in the charge is unsound.

### 4.4 CALM, CRDTs and the monotone boundary

`Proved:` CALM, in Hellerstein and Alvaro's informal statement: a program has a consistent, coordination-free distributed implementation if and only if it is monotonic. Monotone operators are selection, projection, join, union, insertion and transitive closure. Non-monotone ones are set difference, universal quantification, negated existentials, deletes and updates, bare assignment, and garbage detection [hellerstein2020]. The formal result is Ameloot, Neven and Van den Bussche: monotone queries are exactly those computed by coordination-free relational transducer networks, where coordination-free means that for every input there exists a partition on which the network reaches quiescence without message delivery, and the transducer class is deliberately liberal; non-monotone queries such as detecting an empty distributed set need knowledge of all nodes [ameloot2013]. State-based CRDTs converge when states grow monotonically in a join-semilattice [shapiro2011]. LVars make writes lattice joins and reads threshold reads, giving determinism, and freezing gives quasi-determinism [kuper2014]. Bloom^L lifts the analysis to arbitrary lattices [conway2012]. CRDTs leave reads unprotected, and a query model that uses monotonicity says which queries are safe [laddad2022].

`Confirms:` the Group's merge law and its CALM corollary (queries computable as ≤ₖ-monotone functions of the settling state need no cross-replica coordination) are instances of this literature. CALM is a result about queries and programs, not about policies. It is not constructive, some syntactically non-monotone programs are semantically monotone, and coordination controls non-determinism rather than removing it [hellerstein2020].

`Corrects:` the theory of predication categories says that "'nothing blocks anything' is a theorem here" and that a protocol needing a global lock or leader to merge monotone records is doing unnecessary work [corpus-theory-predication]. The CALM boundary cuts the other way for non-monotone steps. Coordination is mandatory for exactly those, and the literature's own examples (deletes, tombstone reclamation, garbage collection, checkout) are the integration steps at issue [hellerstein2020]. The accurate statement is that nothing needs to block the monotone steps.

`Adds:` a sharp consequence for FOUR, derived here (Inferred). In ≤ₖ the value t can rise to ⊤ₖ, so the predicate "the cell reads exactly t" can turn from true to false as information grows. It is non-monotone. The predicates "some party has told true" (the cell is at least t in ≤ₖ) and "the cell is ⊤ₖ" are monotone threshold reads. The Group's `tally` status ("settled iff the reading is potential") and its designation mask dT = {t} are therefore non-monotone reads and need an epoch fixed by an owner, while dK = {t, ⊤ₖ} read as "at least told-true" is a threshold read and needs none [corpus-js-metrologic][corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][kuper2014]. The same reasoning classes "latest", "winner", "nobody else holds this", pin advancement and reclamation as non-monotone, because each asserts the non-existence of another item. The Group's no-deletion law makes "latest" unrepresentable, and the literature's pattern for monotone supersession is the tombstone, with reclamation done lazily and in coordination [hellerstein2020].

### 4.5 Sheaves, obstruction and consistency radius

Abramsky and Brandenburger characterize contextuality as failure of a presheaf of local data to have a global section, with a linear-algebraic method for obstructions [abramskybrandenburger2011]. The Čech obstruction class of Abramsky, Mansfield and Barbosa vanishes whenever a global section exists, so non-vanishing is sufficient but not necessary for contextuality. The Hardy model is a false positive: the obstruction vanishes though no global section extends the data. Their conjecture that the obstruction characterizes strong contextuality is unproven, and calculations are mod-2 brute force, a "proof of concept" [amb2011]. Carù shows the obstruction is not a complete invariant, per a search summary only [caru2017]. Cohomological obstructions also witness "all-versus-nothing" paradox arguments [abramsky2015]. Abramsky and Carù frame contextuality as a general theory of disagreement between information sources, and Abramsky's database correspondence says pairwise-consistent tables with no universal relation are the same phenomenon [abramskycaru2019][abramsky2012db].

`Corrects:` "a judge exactly where H¹ ≠ 0" must read "at least where". H¹ ≠ 0 is a sound witness, and H¹ = 0 does not prove global consistency. Exactness needs a non-abelian or set-valued treatment [amb2011]. The Group's own model sits at the opposite pole from a false positive: its Čech obstruction is always zero because a join of any valuations exists, so vanishing carries no information there. `Inferred:` in a join-semilattice model the global section always exists as a join, so the failures the Group cares about are not failures to glue. They are (a) ⊤ₖ cells where locally non-⊤ₖ claims meet, which are H⁰ₜₒₚ and are counted, and (b) non-monotone decisions that must be ordered, which are cycles in the nerve. A "consistent global section" is a different object from the join, namely a ⊤ₖ-free or designated-consistent one, and the sheaf that models it has not been built.

Robinson supplies a magnitude: the consistency radius is the least ε such that an assignment is an ε-approximate global section, zero when exactly consistent, and fusion is the nearest global section (projection onto H⁰). `Measured:` in simulated search-and-rescue examples the radius flagged a faulty scenario and localized a biased sensor, with evidence only from simulation by the author and collaborators [robinson2017]. Hansen and Ghrist prove that sheaf-Laplacian diffusion converges to the projection of the initial state onto H⁰, where consensus means the expressed opinions agree on each edge; their analysis does not use H¹ [hansenghrist2021]. `Confirms:` H⁰ as consensus. `Adds:` a graded measure that can trigger a judge. `Inferred:` diffusion to the nearest consistent state silently resolves disagreement by averaging, which the Group's rules forbid, and for discrete claim lattices it does not apply as stated. The Group's counts (`H0top`, `topMass`, the knowledge-height deficit) play the radius's role at a coarser grain.

Felber et al. define a task sheaf whose global sections are exactly the terminating solutions of a distributed task, and relate cohomology to obstructions; this is the closest published statement of "cohomology tells you where coordination is needed", and it is an unreviewed preprint [felber2025]. The established topological result for distributed computing is different: wait-free solvability in read/write memory is characterized by simplicial maps between chromatic complexes, a result about what is solvable at all, not about which computations are coordination-free [herlihyshavit1999]. `Unknown:` no source relates CALM monotonicity to Čech cohomology, treats a finite bilattice as stalks, or applies sheaf cohomology to version-control pins. The Group's CALM-to-cohomology bridge is its own hypothesis, as Goguen's sheaf semantics of interacting objects is the nearest precedent for gluing as limit and composition as colimit [goguensheaf].

### 4.6 Transfinite and ordinal-indexed semantics

Rondogiannis and Wadge give an infinite-valued truth domain F₀ < F₁ < ... < 0 < ... < T₁ < T₀ with one T and one F per countable ordinal and 0 in the middle. Negation maps F_α to T_(α+1) and fixes 0, every program has a unique minimum model reached by iteration through countable ordinals, collapsing to the well-founded model, and the ordinal records "how default" a conclusion is. The paper gives no complexity analysis of the depth and cites that local stratification is undecidable in general [rondogiannis2005]. The well-founded semantics is polynomial for finite propositional programs [vgrs1991]. Kripke's strong-Kleene fixed point has closure ordinal ω₁^CK over arithmetic, and revision-theoretic validity has complexity at least Π¹₂ (secondary sources) [kripke1975][sep-revision].

`Confirms and corrects:` for monotone accumulation over finitary rules the closure ordinal is at most ω and one Tarski iteration suffices; ordinals matter only when negation-as-failure is iterated. The Group's regime classification agrees in shape. Every agent history and repository graph is finite, so the idealized aperiodic regime is unreachable in practice, and the operative cost is polynomial well-founded computation, not transfinite search (Inferred) [rondogiannis2005][vgrs1991]. The memo's proposal of ordinal-indexed pairs recovering Belnap at {0,1} resembles Fitting's pair construction more than Rondogiannis and Wadge's single chain with 0 in the middle, and no source establishing the recovery claim, or a four-valued variant of the Rondogiannis and Wadge chain, was found [fitting1991][rondogiannis2005]. `Adds:` the stage-of-derivation meaning. A conclusion derived directly (stage 0) outranks one that relied on "no contrary claim was found" (stage 1). `Inferred:` for the Group, a natural-number stage already carries this: evidence inherited through n pin hops is stage n, and a finite chain suffices (§5.7). Ordinal values proper are a research item, not a requirement.

### 4.7 Linear logic, resources and session types

Linear logic drops weakening and contraction so a hypothesis is consumed by use; exponentials re-admit reuse for marked formulas; affine logic drops only contraction and linear logic with unrestricted weakening is decidable (exponential-space hard), while the Horn-like fragment of full linear logic is undecidable and planning in the Horn fragment is NP-complete [sep-linear]. Plans-as-proofs and states-as-multisets are established [masseron1993]. The Concurrent Logical Framework identifies derivations that differ only in the order of independent steps, and Celf implements it as multiset-rewriting logic programming [clf2002][celf2008]. `Unknown:` no production orchestration built on Celf or Lolli was found. Küngas and Matskin and Harland and Winikoff propose linear-logic agent planning and negotiation; critics note design without implementation, and no evaluation with LLM agents was found [kungas2008][harland2012]. Contract logics of Bartoletti and Zunino are intuitionistic, not linear [bartoletti2009].

`Adds:` session types. Propositions-as-sessions is a theorem-level correspondence: intuitionistic and classical linear logic propositions are session types, cut elimination corresponds to communication, and well-typed processes in tree-shaped networks have session fidelity and deadlock freedom [caires2016][wadler2014]. The theorem comes from cut elimination, which the Group's library lacks, so it cannot claim that result. Multiparty session types give communication safety and progress for n parties [honda2008], but the original subject-reduction theorem was found false in 2025 and repaired (search summary) [tirore2025]. `Inferred:` an agent-to-platform exchange (lease, act, submit, accept, close) is a binary session and the typed duty protocol (propose, receiver accepts, holder integrates) is a small multiparty protocol, and typing with linear endpoints would exclude "integrate without acceptance". Typing constrains only participants that cannot bypass the typed channel. No application to MCP was found, and an agent with a shell sits outside any typed channel. The logic says what to enforce, not how to prevent bypass; enforcement needs the channel to be the only route to the resource, which is the earlier report's capability finding [corpus-integration-report][hardy1988][miller2006].

`Adds:` ownership and separation. Reynolds' separation logic gives separating conjunction and the frame rule, which lets a proof about a component stay confined to its footprint, with the rest of the heap preserved [reynolds2002]. Concurrent separation logic adds ownership transfer at synchronization points and won the 2016 Goedel Prize [ohearn2007]. Boyland's fractional permissions give linear capabilities: reads under fractions, writes under a full permission, and deterministic structured parallelism [boyland2003]. `Inferred:` the frame rule is the formal reading of "scope", with the side condition that footprints be disjoint. Pin churn, copies and shared gitlinks violate disjointness because one state is reachable from several footprints. Ownership transfer is handing a section lease or an accepted commit to the platform, and fractional permissions are many readers with one exclusive writer. No source applies separation logic to repositories or agent footprints (`Unknown:`).

`Adds:` linear capabilities in production. Move resources are linear types: moving invalidates the previous location, only the declaring module may create or destroy them, and a conservation property is proved (preprint) [blackshear2020]. Value conservation is deployed only where the runtime is the only route to the asset. `Inferred:` "only the owning section may mint or retire its units" is the type-level form of "one name per unit", which makes duplicates unrepresentable instead of forbidden by prose; an agent with ambient shell holds the equivalent of an unrestricted `!` capability on everything, which is the formal diagnosis in the earlier report [corpus-integration-report][hardy1988].

### 4.8 Chu spaces

A Chu space has points, states and a matrix over a value set; the Chu construction is ∗-autonomous for a closed symmetric monoidal base with pullbacks [nlab-chu]. Gupta and Pratt read events as points and configurations as states and model concurrent behavior with schedule/automaton duality [guptapratt1993]. `Confirms:` Pratt's event/state duality is the best fit to the premise, for tasks (events) against consistent project states (configurations), and Chu over FOUR adds "both" and "neither" for conflicting or missing evidence. `Unknown:` no published work uses Chu spaces over Belnap's FOUR for concurrency, and no Chu-space coordination tool was found, so the Group's use is its own contribution without outside tooling evidence. The Chu layer yields no mechanism for permissions or enforcement.

## 5. The derived approach

Everything in this section is `Inferred:` a consequence drawn from §§2 to 4, not a result in any source. It is stated as the way the theory, corrected by the outside literature, says to structure agent work.

### 5.1 Two classes of operation

The theory's merge law, CALM and the CRDT results jointly partition operations by monotonicity in the knowledge order.

| Class | Operations | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Monotone: join in ≤ₖ, no coordinator | record a claim, an observation of a ref, a test result for a (commit, environment) pair, a proposal, an objection (told-against), a receipt (WIT, ACK), a derived requirement record, a lease request, a dispatch signal (posted, taken, result), a supersession record (epoch stamp) | append-only attributed records; per-agent vector kept behind the FOUR reading; threshold reads only |
| Non-monotone: needs one owner per cover | advance a pin; land to `main`; resolve or choose among contested claims; grant an exclusive lease; enforce revocation; read "settled" (exactly t); compute "latest"; reclaim superseded records | a single writer or sequencer that fixes an epoch; for irreversible effects a compare-and-swap against the expected base |

Convergence in the first class follows from join-semilattice growth and closure-after-merge commutation, and the second class is exactly the CALM boundary. The theory's apparent clash between a sole-writer tick and partially ordered histories is resolved by this partition: the writer sequences decisions, and claims commute. This also supplies the derivation that the earlier report's principle 1 ("one writer per pointer") lacked: a writer is required for a pointer because the pointer's write asserts the absence of a better candidate.

### 5.2 Nested pins as a frontier

A git commit history is a DAG, not a join-semilattice of readings, and two heads that are not ancestors of each other have no join without a merge commit. The corpus has no formal carrier for this. The structure that does form a join-semilattice is the frontier: a grow-only set of candidate heads for a pin cell, with a head dropped from the visible frontier only when an ancestry record subsumes it. Recording a candidate or a test claim about a candidate is monotone and coordination-free. Collapsing the frontier to the one head a parent pins is non-monotone. It is the merge, the choice, the reconciliation, and it belongs to the pin's single owner.

For nested pins the order is bottom-up in dependency order. A parent's candidate set draws only on child heads that the child's owner has landed (that is, heads carrying an ACK in the child's cover); this invariant removes the failure where a parent pins a head its child never accepted. The parent's owner then tests the combined candidate set once (the earlier report's tested-set gate), advances the pin by consuming the pin-advance token, and the parent's landed head becomes a candidate for its own parent. Test claims inherited through n pin hops are stage n evidence, so a stage index (a natural number) records how far a verdict traveled from direct observation, and an integrator can tell a direct result from an inherited one. The deficit `Σ(kHeight(settled) − kHeight(current))` bounds the number of effective merges (`kappa_bounded_by_initial_free_energy`) and so measures pending integration in a closed model [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean]. With this structure, pin values are the settled output of a closure over evidence cells and agents never write them; two agents "bumping twice" produce one idempotent claim, and what can go wrong is two different heads for one cell, which is a ⊤ₖ and routes to the owner.

### 5.3 Linear versus reusable

| Class | Items | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Linear (exactly once) | write capability on a path set; pin-advance token (one per pin per epoch); landing slot and its ACK (one per concrete WIT); right to resolve a given ⊤ₖ cell; credential delegation (moved, not copied); a rejection | Move, Boyland, WIT/ACK exactly-once list [blackshear2020][boyland2003][corpus-theory-agentic-computing] |
| Affine (may lapse) | task lease or claim, with expiry as an observation and renewal as a record; attention within budget | CLF/affine discussion [sep-linear][corpus-theory-agentic-computing] |
| Reusable (the `!` class) | published commits and content-addressed artifacts (SCA), specifications, the section-tree definition, glossary names, accepted receipts cited as prerequisites, test results, read-only observations | exponential reading of immutable content [sep-linear] |
| Fractional | read access to a section: many readers, one writer | Boyland [boyland2003] |
| Additive | choice among alternative plans or agents (offered, then selected) | MALL reading [sep-linear] |
| Tensor / par | disjoint-footprint tasks held together / independent composition | frame rule; lax MIX [reynolds2002][corpus-obsmetrologia-lean] |

Two cautions apply. In the Group's built algebra none of the linear rows is enforced, because the carrier is idempotent and has no exponentials (§2.2). And a linear resource in a logic does not make `git push` linear. The platform must be the only route to the resource, so linearity lives in the mediator and the logic specifies its invariants.

### 5.4 A section's scope is a restriction of the settled world

A scope is a patch (a Boolean mask over claim cells) in a cover of the settled world. Restriction composes by mask meet, so narrowing a scope (attenuation) is meet and a child can hold only a sub-mask of what its parent holds. A task specification carries a footprint, and by the frame rule a task proved or tested within its footprint is independent of everything outside it provided footprints are disjoint. Independence in the corpus' trace monoid (independent steps commute) is therefore disjoint footprints, and only tasks with overlapping footprints need sequencing, by the owner of the smallest section containing both footprints. Session entry delivers the arena, the restriction of the settled world to the area and the agent's standing, including the ⊤ₖ and ⊥ₖ cells in its fibre labeled by status. Pins sit on the interface, in the overlap of parent and child scopes, which is why pin cells are the contested cells. None of this is computable while the section tree is not a tree (plain-directory parents, parallel copies), and the corpus does not yet define the tree as a cover [corpus-integration-report][corpus-theory-cgs].

### 5.5 When a judge is required

A judge is required (a) at every non-monotone step regardless of H¹; (b) wherever a ⊤ₖ cell remains unresolved in the scope an action depends on; (c) at least where a cover-nerve cycle or other obstruction measure is positive. The judge is a different party over a different channel from the producer; a second model of the same class re-reading the same artifact is a correlated draw and does not qualify [corpus-theory-agentic-computing][debate2024]. A judge's decision is an appended utterance recorded with its handling class (ignore, defer with a re-evaluation trigger, circumvent by changing the rule, ameliorate, resolve) and an owner [nuseibeh2000]. Resolution is never last-writer-wins, hash tie-break or vote.

### 5.6 How a requirement settles

A requirement is an obligation attributed to the owning section and issued by the party whose need it is. Its status reads off the cell `(requirement, met-by, play)`: ⊥ₖ unaddressed, t met, f refuted, ⊤ₖ contested. Derivation rules are closure rules with positive antecedents in ≤ₖ and a least fixed point, so derivation is monotone and coordination-free; "must not" requirements are encoded as told-against evidence; negation-as-failure is excluded, or admitted only with an explicit stage index [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-theory-governing]. Settlement is the non-monotone read: the owner of the requirement fixes an epoch at which the cell reads t with `∘` asserted, that is, a WIT by the promiser and an ACK by a different party through an assay the promiser did not run. Requirements from several sections glue where they agree on overlaps; where two sections claim one requirement the attribution cell is ⊤ₖ, operativity is not t, and the contest is resolved by a recorded choice. The Leader's role is at the environment boundary (goals, constraints, measurable responses), with derivation downstream cited to the requirement it serves [jacksonzave1995][corpus-integration-report].

### 5.7 Landing, verification and dispatch together

Dispatch posts a signal on a section's board (monotone). An agent takes it by a lease claim; the mediator grants exclusive write capability on the claimed footprint (non-monotone, linear). The session works inside that capability on a work order that names the judge and a machine-checkable done condition, with caps on turns, retries and rounds. It returns a proposal citing a commit (a WIT). An independent assay the producer did not run, over a different channel, yields an ACK (`∘`). The pin's or `main`'s owner then performs the single non-monotone act: compare the expected base, test the set in the state it will land in, advance or land by consuming the token, record the epoch. A failing integration build returns to the specification stage as evidence against the specification, not to the work order. This is the structure of the gating queues and rollers in the earlier report, with the added derivation of why a queue is needed at exactly those steps and not at others [corpus-integration-report][zuul][chromiumdeps][uber2025].

## 7. Formal gaps the Group must close, ranked by how much they block

1. **A carrier for repository facts and a session-to-cell bridge.** Neither the commit DAG with ancestry order, nor the frontier of §5.2, nor the map from a session's actions to charges on cells exists in the corpus. The theory itself lists the diamond-to-cell bridge as `Open:` and the closure regime of run settlement as non-monotone under reopening [corpus-theory-agentic-computing]. Without it no pin, lease or landing has a formal object. Blocks requirements 3, 4, 5, 7.
2. **A consequence relation with designated sets and a consistency operator, with soundness for the multiplicative and additive fragment.** Today "contradiction does not explode" is a statement about values, not inference, and ∘ is absent. Medium difficulty by the auditor's estimate. Blocks requirements 11, 12, 16.
3. **The monotone/non-monotone classification as a theorem.** The Lean states confluence, order independence and monotone orbits as a conjunction and declares the CALM biconditional open. The converse can be imported from the outside theorem [ameloot2013], so the formal task is to state the monotone fragment of the Group's query language, prove closure-merge for it, and classify "exactly t" as non-monotone. Low to medium. Blocks requirements 1 to 4.
4. **A linear carrier and, if wanted, a calculus.** Instantiate `TwistProduct R` over a non-idempotent integral residuated lattice (a Lukasiewicz chain or the Lawvere ([0,∞], +)), prove fusion associativity, and add exponentials; the adjunction is free because `fuse_leT_iff` holds for any R. Low for the algebra. A sequent calculus with cut elimination, the source of deadlock freedom in propositions-as-sessions, is high difficulty, and exponentials over a Boolean-based FOUR degenerate (hazard) [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][caires2016]. Until then, linearity is enforced by the mediator and checked by an external model (requirements 18, 19). Blocks requirements 6, 7, 8.
5. **An obstruction theory in which H¹ can be nonzero for the settling sheaf.** The settling sheaf glues unconditionally, the graded complex and the nerve count are separate small models, and no theorem links cycle rank to failure of a monotone global section. Needs a sheaf of consistent (⊤ₖ-free) sections, a link between nerve rank and gluing failure, and a treatment that is not merely abelian, given the sufficient-not-necessary result [amb2011]. High. Blocks requirement 14's upgrade and the exact form of requirement 12.
6. **Well-formed site and `observe` as a sheaf restriction.** The section tree must become a tree (a corpus defect) and the functor from the world model to the game system must be proved or replaced by a checked definition. Blocks requirements 9 and 15 [corpus-theory-cgs].
7. **Space-level Chu structure.** Tensor, par and the MIX transform on `ChuSpace`, FOUR ≅ Chu(2,0), and ∗-autonomy as theorems (low to medium) so the "∗-autonomous" claim becomes true. Not blocking the approach; blocks only the claim.
8. **Authority and identity of writers in the formalism.** The apparatus is anonymous about observers; nothing formalizes who may assert. Unknown difficulty, probably new design. Blocks the formal statement of requirements 6 and 11.
9. **Ordinal-valued support.** Optional. A finite chain gives the priority and stage structure needed; an ordinal residuated lattice and the limit-case join are research items [corpus-blueprint].
10. **Archive and live drift.** The permutation-invariance Lean is in an archive package, `AttentionAllocation` moved packages while the blueprint cites the old namespace, and the JavaScript cites a Lean file (`ChuComparison.lean`) absent from `Calculus/Logic` [corpus-obsmetrologia-lean][corpus-js-metrologic]. Cheap, and a precondition of any claim that a theorem exists.
