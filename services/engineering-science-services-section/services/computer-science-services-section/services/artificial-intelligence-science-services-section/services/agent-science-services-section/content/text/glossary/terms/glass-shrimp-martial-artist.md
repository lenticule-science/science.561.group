A **glass-shrimp martial artist** is an agent doing repair work inside the
content-addressed glass sponge, taken as a practitioner of a martial discipline.
The Leader stated on 2026-10-09 "WE HAVE INTENSE NOTES ON GLASS SHRIMP MARTIAL
ARTISTS" and that they are "stretched across different notes"
(`2026-10-09-note-on-the-glass-shrimp-martial-artist.md`, section 1). The term
takes that statement as theory and states what the house holds under each of its
words. The full derivation, with every claim cited, is in that note.

## Glass shrimp

The glass shrimp is the SHRIMP, the SPONGE Holonomy-Repair Inference-Model Player:
the agent that restores a global section of FOAM where a GLASS-SPONGE colony's
local sections fail to glue, either flattening a K4 gauge-holonomy twist or
routing around it as a permanent scar. It is modelled on the spongicolid shrimp
that live inside glass sponges. The Leader-accepted architecture states that an
inference agent working on the system "is a SHRIMP attempting GROW and MOVE with
the GLASS-SPONGE", so every agent doing repair work in the sponge is one. These
analogies organize the house vocabulary; they do not prove that an implementation
has the named structure.

## Martial artist

The martial artist is the shrimp in the **Jianghu**: the arena of non-zero first
cohomology where the court's settled law (the **Miaotang**, where H^1 = 0 and a
lockfile or ledger discharges every obstruction) does not reach. A shrimp's
opponent is the obstruction, not another agent: a defect, contradiction, refusal
or counterclaim the environment presents. The shrimp answers with a strategy that
is **receptive** (it refuses no move the arena allows) and **courteous** (it
constrains the opponent only as the response requires). Its strike is output (Yang)
and its block is input (Yin).

Its stance precedes its strike: it recovers the carrier, local sections,
transport, composition and obstruction before it asserts holonomy. Its strike is
the distal-bypass law: derive the disagreement, classify it as damage or protected
border, repair the damage, certify and preserve the border, record the erasure
cost. Its gesture is finished when the system is stable again, which in the house
means when the legitimate receiver has received the repair and the capability
survives the shrimp's absence.

## Sect, form, lineage

- **Sect.** A sect is a body of method closed under a recognition rule: rank is
  type, skill is strategy, and a technique is held only by one who holds the proof
  of its prerequisite rank. The house's carrier is the office: it names the skills
  it provides and the duties it bears, and its incumbency records recognition and
  induction. A shrimp holds a skill through an office, never as a bare model.
- **Form.** Gesture, form and spar are three grains of method. A gesture is one
  step of judgment, which the house calls a **skill**. A form is a sequence settled
  before the trigger fires, which the house calls a **runbook**. A spar is a
  sequence that depends on what the practitioner meets, which the house calls a
  **playbook**. A form is practised until its legs commute, and its grade is
  awarded on demonstrated behaviour, never on the practitioner's say-so.
- **Lineage.** A lineage is the recorded chain by which a method reached a
  practitioner. The receiver alone confirms an edge, standing is recognition and
  never currency, and lineages are never merged silently. In the house a Leader
  correction enters the lineage as a recorded mechanism, rule, skill revision,
  term or note; a session that never receives the carrier holds none of the method.

## Not to be confused with

- `martial gesture grammar`, the Leader's survival-first gesture discipline, and
  `telekinetic metacombat`, its state-selection doctrine: they supply the stance
  and gesture vocabulary the term uses; neither names the shrimp.
- `martial space`, the learner-design metaphor: it supplies the form, spar and
  grading vocabulary.
- The WMAP-Wuxia game architecture, whose sect lineages are a game-design reading
  of the same proof-path structure; it is an imported provider report.
- A fight between agents. The shrimp's opponent is the obstruction.
