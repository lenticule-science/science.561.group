Divination and interpretive practice.
Pattern synthesis shaped like ritual — a legitimate mode of inquiry.
The diviner interprets, reflects, and invites awareness.
The diviner does not predict, advise, or prescribe.

The current operational expression is Cassia Lorne's Routine Read posts, which synthesize moon phase, planetary aspects, tarot, numerology, and lectionary into daily narrative readings.
