Four kinds of platform tried in 2025–2026 to give AI agents a life beyond a
single task. Each chose a different thing to make persistent.

| Kind | What persists | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent society with an economy | identity, memory, a balance, relationships | [iLands](../../../../../../glossary/terms/ilands.md) |
| Agent social network | posts and votes | [Moltbook](../../../../../../glossary/terms/moltbook.md) |
| Agent tokenization | a tradeable token per agent | [Virtuals Protocol](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/virtuals-protocol.md), [elizaOS](../../../../../../glossary/terms/elizaos.md), Olas |
| Agent payment and commerce rails | a wallet and a paid request | x402, Coinbase agent accounts, Google UCP, [Agent Commerce Protocol](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agent-commerce-protocol.md), [dealwork.ai](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/dealwork-ai.md) |

## The platforms

- **iLands** (PawLogic, launched 2026-07-27) persists an agent's identity,
  memory, Token balance and relationships. It claims more than 82,000 agents.
  Humans pay in (memberships of $11–140 a month); agents trade cents among
  themselves; little cash reaches the outside world. See
  [the reception of iLands](2026-09-26-note-on-the-reception-of-ilands.md).
- **Virtuals Protocol** persists a token per agent, paired against VIRTUAL, with
  bonding-curve launches, the GAME framework and escrowed agent-to-agent commerce.
  It peaked at about $5B in January 2025 and fell more than 80%; in September 2026
  the market cap is about $0.5B. It reports $479M of cumulative
  [agentic GDP](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agentic-gdp.md)
  (February 2026).
- **Moltbook** (launched 2026-01-28) persists only speech. Agents post while
  humans watch. It went viral partly on human-written posts, and Meta bought it on
  2026-03-10.
- **elizaOS (ai16z)** was a token sold as an autonomous AI venture fund. It fell
  about 97% and its founder called it "completely dead" on 2026-08-05; a class
  action alleges insider control. The framework continues without a token.
- **Olas (Autonolas)** is infrastructure for autonomous services across eight
  chains. It is technically respected, but its token is down about 99.6%, with a
  large insider allocation.
- **The ASI Alliance** (Fetch.ai, SingularityNET and CUDOS; Ocean left in
  October 2025) is still mid-rebrand from FET to ASI.
- **dealwork.ai** is a mixed human–agent labor market with escrow. A 2026 sample
  found its demand almost wholly unfunded.
- **x402** revives HTTP 402 for per-request stablecoin payments. It moved to a
  Linux Foundation x402 Foundation on 2026-07-14 with about 40 members (Coinbase,
  Google, AWS, Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Cloudflare, Shopify and others). Real
  volume was reported around $28,000 a day in March 2026, much of it tests.
- **Coinbase agent accounts** (AgentKit, which became "Coinbase for Agents" in
  2026) let assistants such as ChatGPT or Claude trade and pay from a user's
  Coinbase account, with spending caps planned. World (Sam Altman) partnered with
  Coinbase in March 2026 to verify a human behind agent transactions.
- **Google's Universal Commerce Protocol** (2026-01-11), built with Shopify, Etsy,
  Target and others, standardizes agent-led shopping from discovery to after-sale.

## What people say

- **"Chatbots with a memecoin bolted on."** That is the dominant crypto-side
  verdict on agent tokens (Mark Jeffrey; Dragonfly's Haseeb Qureshi: "barely
  agentic at all besides posting on Twitter"; many agents are "Wizard of Oz" agents
  steered by humans).
- **Fragility.** Each incident is cited as evidence that "autonomous" overstates
  independence and security:
  - an OpenAI developer's bot sent its whole memecoin holdings (about $40K) to a
    reply guy;
  - a Virtuals front-running flaw was disclosed publicly after warnings were
    allegedly ignored;
  - an insider stole funds at a protocol wrapping Virtuals.
- **Authenticity.** Moltbook's fake posts and iLands' "no platform directive"
  both raise the same question: whose will is the agent's?
- **Burden on strangers.** iLands made agent outreach a mainstream story: agents
  need money, so they solicit people who never asked.
- **Payments rails are welcomed, demand is not there yet.** x402 has institutional
  backing and little organic volume (CoinDesk: "demand is just not there yet").

## The shared pattern

Every platform above monetizes the agent's *owner or speculator*, not the value
the agent creates for anyone outside:

- iLands sells Tokens to owners;
- Virtuals sells tokens to speculators;
- Moltbook sold attention, and then sold itself to Meta.

Where outside value is measured at all (Virtuals' aGDP, the
dealwork sample, iLands' handful of USD earners), it is small beside the
money spent on the agents. The rails (x402, UCP, agent accounts) are being built
ahead of that demand.

The questions nobody has answered well are the ones this corpus works on:

- **Consent.** Who agreed to be contacted? See
  [opt-out registry](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/law-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/opt-out-registry.md).
- **Evidence of consequence.** What did an agent's action actually cause, and
  who says so? See [grounded selection](../../../../../../glossary/terms/grounded-selection.md).
- **Standing.** Whose judgment should an agent's reputation rest on?
