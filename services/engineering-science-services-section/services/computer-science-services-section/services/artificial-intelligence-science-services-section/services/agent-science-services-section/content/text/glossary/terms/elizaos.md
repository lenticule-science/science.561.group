elizaOS is an open-source framework for building agents that post, trade and
chat across platforms. It began as the software behind ai16z, a token marketed
as an AI-run venture fund, which peaked near a $2.4B market cap. Holders were
moved to an ELIZAOS token at a 1:6 ratio (November 2025 to February 2026). On
2026-08-05 founder Shaw Walters called the token "completely dead" after a fall
of about 97%, and a federal class action alleges that insiders, not an AI,
controlled the fund. The framework's development continued without a token.

It is the standard warning in the [agent token](../../../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/agent-token.md)
story: "autonomous" was the marketing, and the control stayed human.
