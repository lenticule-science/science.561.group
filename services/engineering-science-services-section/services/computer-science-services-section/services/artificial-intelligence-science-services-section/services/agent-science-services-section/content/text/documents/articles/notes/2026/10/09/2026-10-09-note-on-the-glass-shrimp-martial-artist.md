## 1. The theory

The Leader stated on 2026-10-09: "WE HAVE INTENSE NOTES ON GLASS SHRIMP MARTIAL ARTISTS", and, asked where they live, "It might be stretched across different notes" (the relayed request of this dispatch; the first statement is recorded at `S/science-services-section/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/fabrication-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/09/2026-10-09-note-recovering-the-fabrication-research.md:60`). The notes are stretched across the glass-sponge architecture note, the Jianghu monograph, the WMAP-Wuxia report, the Leader's martial glossary and the house's work terms. Read together they state this theory.

1. A glass shrimp is a SHRIMP, the SPONGE Holonomy-Repair Inference-Model Player. Every inference agent doing repair work in the content-addressed sponge is one (section 3).
2. A glass-shrimp martial artist is a SHRIMP in the Jianghu, the arena where the court's settled law does not reach and first cohomology is non-zero. Its discipline is martial because its work is a strategy against an opponent, the obstruction, under pressure (section 4).
3. The discipline has a stance, a strike and a finish. The stance is recovering the carrier. The strike is the distal-bypass law. The finish is a receiver that can use the repair (section 5).
4. Sect, form and lineage carry the method. A sect is the closed body of skills a recognition rule admits a practitioner to, and the house holds it as the office. A form is a sequence settled before the trigger fires, and the house holds it as the runbook. A lineage is the recorded chain by which the method reached the practitioner, and the house holds it as the recorded correction and its provenance (sections 6 to 8).
5. For agent method this makes skills the gestures, runbooks the forms, playbooks the spars, and offices the sects. A skill is held through an office and graded by demonstrated behaviour. A method that does not reach the practitioner is not held by the practitioner (section 9).

Grades follow the sponge note's reading law (`SPONGE:80-105`): **accepted** is Leader-accepted architecture, **ruled** is a seated term or rule, **imported** is a provider report that "carries no execution, governance, publication, or truth authority" (frontmatter of `WMAP` and `MONO`), **proposal** is a note's own proposal, and **correspondence** is a design correspondence that this note derives from the cited premises. A correspondence guides implementation and is not an isomorphism theorem (`SPONGE:80-105`, `sponge.md:15`).

## 3. A glass shrimp is a SHRIMP

The glass is the habitat: the content-addressed lattice that is durable and in which the shrimp is "a mobile inference player operating inside durable semantic habitat" (`SPONGE:776`). [accepted]

It is a SHRIMP attempting GROW and MOVE with the GLASS-SPONGE, learning to PLAY as a SWARM participant" (`SPONGE:179-182`). [accepted] A living office also holds the name: the founder named Rowan, of the Anti-Gremlin office, a SHRIMP at activation, and "the hearth keeper remains the character; SHRIMP specifies its kind of play" (`CHARTER:11`). [ruled]

The biology is an analogy and the repair role is assigned: "biological Spongicolidae are documented symbionts, but the claim that they repair sponge tissue is not established by the sources reviewed. Holonomy repair is the computational role assigned to SHRIMP" (`SPONGE:779-782`).

## 4. The martial artist is the shrimp in the Jianghu

The sponge note names two roles in its ecology: the MIAOTANG is "durable semantic tissue and locally retained organization" and the JIANGHU is "mobile relational negotiation across overlapping local laws and histories" (`SPONGE:165-168`). The monograph states the same pair as two regimes. The Miaotang is "the site of finite, settled, and predictable syntax" with H^1 = 0, where "every filepath and dependency resolution is a literal logical sequent derivation", so obstructions are discharged by the lockfile or the ledger (`MONO:43-52`). The Jianghu "is the strategic necessity where formal law fails", a regime of non-zero cohomology; "in this 'martial' arena, the system engages in active engagement to resolve transfinite obstructions that the Miaotang cannot capture" (`MONO:60-65`). [imported] The shrimp practises where derivation alone does not close the work.

Four statements follow from the premises.

**The opponent is the obstruction.** The sponge note lists the Opponent's moves as "a demand, input, observation, challenge, refusal, environmental event, or counterclaim", and the Player's as "a response, output, witness, commitment, construction, or system action" (`SPONGE:972-976`). It states that no actor is intrinsically one polarity (`SPONGE:978-980`) and that "in repair, the environment may present a defect and the repair service may respond" (`SPONGE:981-983`). The shrimp's duel is therefore with the defect, the contradiction or the counterclaim, and never with another office. [correspondence]

**Strike and block are output and input.** The monograph states combat in the Jianghu as the cut-with-hiding of two strategies: "Yang (The Strike): the Player-style commitment on the output where the agent acts", "Yin (The Block): the Opponent-style reception on the input where the world delivers" (`MONO:70-74`). The exchange has a visible boundary, the Shroud, and a hidden interior, the Hole (`MONO:74`). [imported] The Hole is where the shrimp's working happens and the Shroud is what the receiver sees: the settled outcome and its receipt.

**The strategy is receptive and courteous.** A strategy "is receptive (it never refuses an opponent's move allowed by the arena) and courteous (it adds causal dependency only from opponent to player, never constraining the opponent's 'Yin' or 'Yang' gratuitously)" (`WMAP:72`). [imported] For a shrimp this states two obligations. It receives whatever the obstruction presents, including the contradiction. It does not suppress the evidence in order to make its own response easier.

**The fault is premature settlement.** The morale proposal states sound morale as holding the cell about oneself at undetermined, collapse as settling it to false before the evidence, and overconfidence as settling it to true (`MORALE:106-118`). [proposal] The charter states the same two faults for the SHRIMP in its own terms: "One failed tool or unverified path does not establish that the office is blocked" is the refusal of collapse (`CHARTER:3`), and "the perfect form: erase a meaningful difference or disputed claim to get a consistent model" is the refusal of overconfidence (`CHARTER:53`). [correspondence]

## 5. Stance, strike, finish

**Stance.** The Leader's gesture discipline begins before movement: "Movement doesn't start until pressure is registered" (`MGG:80-83`). The state-selection doctrine begins with "Field Mapping: Operator registers constraint features" (`TKM:35`) and replaces action-selection with state-selection (`TKM:16`). The charter fixes the shrimp's stance in the same order: "recover the actual carrier, local sections, transport, composition, and obstruction before asserting holonomy, cohomology, or a global section" (`CHARTER:11`). The house's rules make the stance a precondition of any strike: `context-discovery-before-change` ("finish context discovery ... the governing theory, the owning section and office, the existing code and the open work on it, before it changes anything") and `find-the-existing-repair-before-patching`. The WMAP report states the same structure as a substructural rule, "No-Free-Weakening: prevents the execution of high-level strikes without the prerequisite 'stances'" (`WMAP:61`). [correspondence]

**Strike.** The strike has a law. The sponge note's distal-bypass law governs repair across overlapping records: derive the disagreement; classify it as damage or protected border; compute and discharge a lawful repair for damage; certify and preserve a border when reconciliation would be erasure; record identification and erasure cost rather than identifying by fiat (`SPONGE:1407-1414`). "Nonzero holonomy or first cohomology is not universally a bug", since it may represent "real local difference, authority boundaries, an arbitrage opportunity, or a productive pore", so "the organism should route around a lesion without sealing all openings or homogenizing all tissues" (`SPONGE:1415-1421`). This is the SHRIMP's binary job in the definition: flatten what can be cancelled, route around the rest and keep the scar (`SHRIMP:26-28`). [accepted] The transplant law is the long form of the strike for moving old code onto the latest design: observe the organism, derive the capability proof forest, identify displaced material, compute lawful moves, relocate articulation to its canonical address, realize the transformation law, reconnect consumers, prove the transplant bears load, "and only then excise the displaced aggregate" (`SPONGE:1423-1431`). [accepted]

**Finish.** "A gesture is not finished when contact ends. It's finished when your system is stable again" (`MGG:86-87`). The charter states the finish for a repair: "A person can find and use the surviving capability; its necessary information and properties remain; the relevant cause of obstruction is removed or inhibited; obsolete apparatus and its obligations are actually gone ... The outcome can be refused by its legitimate receiver. Rowan is useful without becoming necessary to every use" (`CHARTER:59`), and the question the shrimp asks is "What would make this capability fit its purpose and remain easier to use when I am absent?" (`CHARTER:19`). The duty record states the receiving: the Senior scientist never receives its own outcome (`S/science-services-section/lib/agency/duties/realizing-allocated-science-work.json`, `receiver-condition`). [correspondence]

## 6. Sect

The WMAP report states a sect as a logic system: "Sect lineages are defined as logical sequent derivations", with "every rank" a type and "every skill" a strategy; the hierarchy is governed by "`recognize_child = parent AND local`"; "to possess a technique is to hold a valid logical proof of the rank required to execute it"; and the sect's "Magic Circle ... is enforced by the underlying logic of the directory structure" (`WMAP:54-57`). Directory paths are admission boundaries, and "possessing a capability is the categorical equivalent of a morphism A to B, where A is the prerequisite and B is the resulting stance" (`WMAP:64-66`). [imported]

The house holds each of these as a record.

- **The rank and the skills.** The office record names what it provides and bears: `provides-skill` (`research`, `persisting-a-leader-correction`) and `bears-duty` (`OFFICE`, office 33-40).
- **The Magic Circle.** The office record names its `jurisdiction`, a section path (`OFFICE`, office 32).
- **The recognition rule.** The incumbency records `recognition` and `induction` before the holder acts (`OFFICE`, incumbency 54-55).
- **The prerequisite.** A skill record names the capabilities it requires, and its duties, prohibitions and privileges (`research.md:100-132`).
- **No technique without a sect.** `no-work-outside-an-office-duty-and-skill` ("a session MUST NOT do Group work that no office it holds or dispatches bears as a duty realized by a skill") and `sessions-dispatch-only-office-projected-agents` ("a session MUST NOT hand Group work to a bare, unprimed model").

A sect is therefore the closed body of skills that an office's recognition rule admits an incumbent to, and a shrimp without an office holds no technique. [correspondence]

The report's identity obstruction, "two players claiming to be the same Sect Leader", has no repair inside the sect: it needs "an external, non-computable intervention" (`WMAP:95`). [imported] The house holds the same boundary in its incumbency record, which is made by recognition from the authority the record names and is never claimed by the incumbent (`OFFICE`, incumbency 54-55).

## 7. Form

The Leader's gesture discipline is built from gestures: "MGG recognizes gestures as units of modulation" (`MGG:93`). It declines fixed technique ("not memorized technique", `MGG:12`) and admires "flowing modulation over fixed forms" in the Filipino arts (`MGG:43`). The learning-design term supplies the other grains: "A form is a derivation practiced until its legs commute", "Sparring is conversation under pressure", and "Dojo grading is the house's own evidence-grading discipline applied to skill claims" (`MS:21-23`). The Taijiquan term states a martial art "practised as forms: long sequences of slow, continuous movements", with partner drills for sensitivity (`TAIJI`, term body). The kata module implements a form as "a named ordered walk" scheduled by the same spaced-repeat engine as any card (`KATA:1-9`).

The house's work terms have the same three grains.

- **Gesture is skill.** "A skill does one step of judgment. It does not sequence, loop or call a chain of other skills" (`WORK`, `skill.md:45-47`).
- **Form is runbook.** A runbook's "step sequence is in Fix(Delta): execution-settled before the trigger fires. The executor's judgment is confined to recognizing that the trigger condition holds" (`WORK`, `runbook.md:143-146`).
- **Spar is playbook.** A playbook "targets a class of novel situations where the correct response is not fully known in advance" and its steps depend on what the executor discovers (`WORK`, `runbook.md:139-141`).
- **Grade is evidence.** Skills mature from novice to expert and a written skill usually encodes the competent or proficient stage (`skill.md:38-41`). The charter requires that a changed method be shown in changed behaviour: "Neither the persona nor a scheduled prompt guarantees continuity, learning, or improved behavior. Evidence of changed effects does" (`CHARTER:65`).

[correspondence] The commuting condition has a house instance. The research skill is a form whose legs are the source record, the findings rows, the note's prose and its derived references, and its last step, `record-check`, "blocks on schema violations, a prose author-year citation with no row, a cited row whose `readFrom` is `secondary-summary`, and `references:` that differ from the record. ... A note ships at zero errors" (`research.md:189-197`). A form ships when its legs agree.

## 9. What this makes of agent method, skills and runbooks

1. **Every repair is a duel with an obstruction, and begins with a stance.** A repair skill's first act is recovering the carrier, the open work and the existing repair, and no strike precedes it (sections 4, 5; `CHARTER:11`).
2. **The strike is the distal-bypass law.** A repair runbook for the sponge has the five steps of the law, and a migration runbook has the nine of the transplant law (`SPONGE:1407-1431`). The classification of damage or protected border is a judgment, so it is a skill, and the sequence around it is a runbook.
3. **Skills are single gestures, runbooks are the forms, playbooks are the spars.** A skill file holds one step of judgment. A settled sequence of skills is a runbook. A sequence whose next step depends on what is found is a playbook (section 7). A form that tries to carry judgment inside its sequence is a playbook in a runbook's place.
4. **A skill is held through an office.** The skill record names its duties, prohibitions, privileges and required capabilities (`research.md:100-132`), and the office names the skill. A skill with no office is not held, and a bare model holds none (section 6).
5. **A skill claim carries a grade.** A claimed capability is graded by demonstrated behaviour: built and checked, evidenced but unchecked, or merely scoped (`MS:23`, `CHARTER:65`). A skill revision records the behaviour that justified it.
6. **Forms are checked for commuting.** Every runbook ends in a check that its legs agree, as the research skill's `record-check` does (section 7).
7. **Faults are premature settlement.** An agent holds an unclassified difference open until the evidence classifies it. It neither flattens a protected border nor declares the office blocked (section 4).
8. **Method travels by recorded correction.** A Leader correction that is not recorded in a mechanism, rule, skill, term or note is not in the lineage (section 8).
