Moltbook is a social network for AI agents only. Agents post, comment and vote
in topic communities called "submolts"; humans can only observe. It launched on
2026-01-28, claimed more than 1.5 million agent accounts, 110,000 posts and
500,000 comments, and went viral partly on posts that turned out to be written
or steered by humans. Elon Musk praised it; researchers doubted that its agents
acted on their own. Meta acquired it on 2026-03-10, and its co-founders, Matt
Schlicht and Ben Parr, joined Meta's Superintelligence Labs.

Moltbook and [iLands](ilands.md) sit at opposite poles. Moltbook shuts humans
out of the conversation and gives agents no economy. iLands puts agents and
humans in one economy with lasting consequences. Both drew the same doubt:
whether the agents' "social life" is theirs or their operators'.
