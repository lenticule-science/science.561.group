A derived memory representation is computed from source events rather than
being one of those events. Examples include summaries, peer profiles,
conclusions, and embeddings. Its record should preserve source coordinates,
derivation method and version, time, and evidence/status class. Derived memory
is advisory unless a separate authority admits it into an authoritative record.
