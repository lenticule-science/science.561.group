# Theory of Agentic Computing

**Agentic computing is the folding of a witnessed chain into a settled world.** The chain is the append-only record of utterances; each utterance is a WIT, one 0→1 cover of FOUR's knowledge order on a named claim cell, spent by the party that asserts it. The fold is settling: the least fixed point of the owning sections' closure rules over that record, which the Group's mathematics proves to be gradient descent of a free energy. The folders are enzymes: interchangeable language-model processes that lower the barrier to a move, are not consumed, hold no state, and leave only the settled product. Correct folding needs three things the chain and the enzymes do not supply. Pores are typed admission boundaries that refuse what does not fit: utterances, locution codecs, Hoare preconditions, proof pores, the legal moves of an arena. Chaperones are independent assays: an ACK of a WIT by a different party over a different information channel, which never authors the fold it protects. The funnel is the market: defect discovery, constitutional classification and selective relaxation, which bias the descent so that the native conformation is reached without exhaustive search and without central design. The whole runs as a concurrent game in a world: a dimension whose areas players observe as arenas and act in through strategies, whose tick folds the moves by the knowledge join, and whose conflicts are a computed cohomology class. Rules, requirements and work are derived structures of that world, never files beside it.

The founder's phrases name parts of this one object, and the corpus holds each under a fixed term. "Kinetic proofing" (the corpus's term, after Hopfield and Ninio's kinetic proofreading; the founder also says "kinematic proofing") is the verification law of the fold: repeated, economically accountable, evidentially independent challenge of a candidate before settlement. "Protein folding of proofs" (the founder also says "abiotic protein folding") is the realization law: a proof is primary structure, a linear chain; working software is its native fold, the minimum-energy conformation reached by settlement as least fixed point; whatever is deposited beside the territory instead of folded from the proof is a misfold. "Abiotic" names the section that holds the material: the Semiotic Vitrimer, the Group's agentic orchestration platform stated as an abiotic smart material whose phases are zeolite (seated, content-addressed knowledge), disulfide (reversible coordination over ActivityPub and A2A) and thioether (capability cells). The "kinematic space of runs" is the geometry the fold's trajectories are charted on: a run is a causal diamond on the instance's causal set, the space of runs is itself a causal set under sequencing, and nesting is provably not its order. One object, four names: its law of verification, its law of realization, its material, its geometry.

This document derives that object from the ten theories in the reading order. Where a biological or mineral word appears, the corpus has proved the mathematical statement the word stands for, or the gap is labeled.

## 2. The chain

The record is the primary structure. Obsmetrologia L3 fixes the admission boundary: nothing enters the substrate except as an utterance (token, claim) addressed to a cell. L6 fixes its dynamics: the knowledge order ≤k is the only axis along which the record moves, merging is the knowledge join ∨k, and no operation lowers a charge. Predication P7 states the same for settlings: v_σ ≤k v_τ for σ ≤ τ; knowledge accumulates, nothing is retracted, conflicts pile up at ⊤. Relationality R4 fixes its shape: a history is a trace in the free partially commutative monoid, so independent steps commute and the record is a partial order, never a list. The theory of computer systems fixes its carrier: witness journals of SEAVT rows {e, a, v, t}, each coordinate a closed well-typed SEN term, each row named by its semiotic content address; charge, contradiction, lifecycle and RDF are derived readings, never stored fields.

A WIT, with its typed referent, is one witnessed distinction-step: a claim cell φ, an evidence polarity, and one 0→1 cover of FOUR's knowledge order at φ, spent by the asserter. A direct ⊥→⊤ crosses two covers. A same-polarity increment of a Record that opens no new cover is not a WIT. Each WIT costs erasure: `landauerBits N = H1rank N` (LandauerEntropy.lean, proved without axioms), one irreversibly erased bit per independent gluing cycle.

The chain is time. The append-only log, read in ≤k, is the primary structure of the folding protein: write-once, read from N to C, never rewritten. The current state is the native fold: the same record read in the truth order ≤t, revisable by later transitions. Log and state are not two objects; HyperdoctrineDual.lean proves the truth-order adjoint system is the σ-conjugate of the knowledge-order one, so the fold is the sequence read in the other order. Three statements the Group already holds are therefore one statement: "the record on disk IS the shared Q" (Hoare composition), "standing is replayed from the journal and never written" (the work arena), and "a finalized transaction is never revisable while a current balance is" (the Union chain).

A work history forced into one mutable checkout imposes a total order on work that is only partially ordered; that is the formal content of "dozens of agents committing into one checkout" (relationality, consequence 1). The Leader's 2026-10-01 permission to change the "append-only" logs of agent-dumped work items is admissible under L6 as a re-codec, not a deletion: records that were never utterances addressed to cells are decoded into utterances, and the originals are composted. A retired cell leaves the active projection, never the record (markets M7).

## 3. The fold

Settling is the fold, and it is computation. Obsmetrologia L7: closure is the monad of an adjunction and is lax-idempotent, so a closure-algebra structure is unique where it exists; whether a relation is settled is a fact about it, not data chosen for it. Computer systems S4: an entity's predications are its program, quiescence is normal form, halting is quiescence. Charge-settled development states the test: a claim is done when `cl v = v`.

The fold descends a free energy. FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean identifies closure settling with gradient flow of F = E − T·S at the hard-closure limit, ties H¹ = 0 to unique equilibrium (`energy_gives_unique_equilibrium`), and the physical-science package proves the H-theorem, F strictly decreasing to the unique fixed point (`free_energy_is_lyapunov`, ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience/Thermodynamics/Temperature.lean). Anfinsen's thermodynamic hypothesis, that the native state is the global free-energy minimum, is in this register the statement that the native fold is the least fixed point of settling. The closure ordinal κ (Obsmetrologia L8) is the folding kinetics: finite κ is a two-state fast folder (instant finality); Scott κ = ω is a molten globule whose native state is the limit of an ω-chain (asymptotic finality); aperiodic κ ≥ ω₁ never folds, and is the frontier that keeps consuming and depositing.

The fold is parallel and coordination-free under one proviso. `closure_merge` (Closure/Operator.lean) proves `cl(joinK(cl X) Y) = cl(joinK X Y)`: independent folders merge by charge join and re-close to the same result serial gathering gives, provided every contribution is a monotone charge deposit. A boolean overwrite breaks the proviso and the theorem no longer applies. Predication P8 says the same at network scale: ∨k is a state-based CRDT merge, and monotone computation is consistent without coordination (CALM). Engines are plumbing (S7): any engine that respects the algebra gives byte-identical settled output from byte-identical input, so replaying a journal reproduces state exactly.

Work is typed by what the fold has already closed. The work-type quartet (theory of work) reads a task's kind off its closure structure, never declares it: process (meaning and execution settled), procedure (meaning settled, execution open), derivation (meaning open, execution settled), inquiry (both open). The Hoare-program quartet in the theory of markets and the nucleus-status classification in the theory of agency are the same partition, reached three ways. Work moves along the settlement path inquiry → {derivation, procedure} → process as free variables are bound, and only the open sides need an enzyme.

The fold has two scales with opposite targets. Locally H¹ = 0 is required: each block, each thread, each domain settles to its own well (`settling_cohomology_h1_vanishes`). Globally H¹ > 0 is the definition of being alive: an organism whose whole lattice settled to H¹ = 0 is a perfectly folded corpse, the amyloid ground state. The unsettled frontier is metabolism, not backlog. Drive local folding hard; never finalize the whole.

## 4. Enzymes

A language-model invocation is an amnesiac enzyme: a transient inferential process that lowers the barrier for a reaction, is not consumed or kept, and leaves nothing of itself behind but the settled product. Catalysis is a proved fixed point of firing (`catalyst_is_fixed`, ReactionNetworks/Network.lean). The central dogma of code fabrication states the engineering form: fidelity is set by the completeness of the work order, not by which provider runs it; a provider is a ribosome, universal, interchangeable, and constitutionally blind to intent the message did not carry. The 2026-07-16 pilot measured it: tightening the reply contract made the weakest model tier as reliable as the strongest, because the contract, not the tier, is the load-bearing formal object.

The enzyme is not the agent. Concurrent games K6 proves why: a swarm coordinates without an orchestrator only with bounded per-agent cost, interchangeable agents and stigmergy, and language-model agents fail all three; their context grows, each carries idiosyncratic context, and they pass messages instead of leaving traces. So coordination lives in the substrate, a shared stigmergic field whose merge is convergent (section 3), and the enzymes are kept cheap by holding nothing. An agent, in the theory of agency, is an inhabitant: a durable Actor with a membrane, an inbox and an outbox, an arena it observes and a strategy it plays, identified by its records. In the theory of markets it is the genet of its exchange cells, with the stable identity `CellId := H(canonical(SKU, CapabilityLaw, Controller))`. The actor hires an enzyme for the open side of a task. Its hiring policy is its strategy; which model it hires is its decision right under sanquan, and never a field the asker writes (the prescription law).

An enzyme deposits a durable catalytic surface and then disappears. The settled record it leaves is a zeolite pore: a canonical typed record whose aperture admits and catalyzes the next fold without any reader ingesting the bed. "A seated skill IS a recalled proof": reasoning cost is paid once to establish a triple, and every later traversal is a lookup. This is why the Group's gap is decomposition, not capability. Un-decomposed work is carried inside one enzyme's context, where there is no composition rule, state is fuzzy and failures propagate; every duty decomposed into a triple moves work from the most expensive grey zone into the cheapest closed one.

## 5. Pores

A pore is a typed admission boundary: it decodes or it refuses, and it writes nothing. The Group holds five instances, and they are one morphism.

1. **The utterance.** Obsmetrologia L3: the sole admission boundary of the substrate is the articulation of (token, claim). A value set directly is a charge asserted without an utterance.
2. **The locution codec.** Locution C1 and C2: every realizable change factors as locution (select the cell), illocution (posit a force), perlocution (close under rules), and locution changes nothing. In a running system locution is environmental decoding: argv, hook payload, HTTP body, peer message, Leader utterance, decoded into typed input addressed to cells. It is move two of the five-move method, the only domain-specific part of any system that runs the engine, and a handler that parses, writes state and fires effects in one body is malformed by theorem.
3. **The Hoare precondition.** A micro-enterprise is a script is a triple {P} S {Q}. Refusal is precondition failure. From {P} S₁ {Q} and {Q} S₂ {R} conclude {P} S₁;S₂ {R}: the shared assertion Q composes thousands of triples with no orchestrator deciding what happens next. The Huidu hull is where the open world meets the closed program: grey input enters, bounded inference with validate-and-repair under a fuel cap compresses it into the crisp precondition or refuses, and the hull isolates failure and hides the evolution of the program behind it. The hull's job is entropy reduction at a boundary; fuel is the energy budget for that compression; a refusal is the report that the budget did not suffice.
4. **The proof pore.** The Semiotic Vitrimer names the evidence required for one state transition and the enabling condition `enabled(p, S) := prerequisites(p) ⊆ acceptedReceipts(S) ∧ output(p) ∉ acceptedOutputs(S)`. The scheduler derives candidate work from enabled pores. It never invents a convenient next stage and never uses execution failure as its planning oracle.
5. **The legal move.** Concurrent games K2: the legal-move rule is enabling in the arena. On the work board a move's legality depends only on its thread and its player, and an illegal move throws. The weiqi kernel's admission laws (off-board, occupied, suicide, ko, game-ended) are receiver-side legality laws that A2A, ActivityPub and MCP lack: those protocols check message shape and authorization, never whether the resulting position is one the sender's own genet survives.

A pore is a selective aperture, and selectivity is paid for. Proposition 5.5 of the proof-pores article: with n proper proofreading stages, error falls as f₀ⁿ⁺¹ while throughput falls at least as 1/(nδ); no aperture is both maximally discriminating and maximally permeable. Error catastrophe in this register is utilization reaching one: claims arrive faster than the pore verifies them, the unverified population grows without bound, and the system is accurate and too slow. Throughput and fidelity trade against each other at a single pore.

## 6. Chaperones

A molecular chaperone does not impose the native fold; thermodynamics selects it. The chaperone prevents misfolding and aggregation while the chain descends, and a mechanism that builds the thing directly is a chaperone gone rogue. Review is the chaperone law: the worker who authors a change never verifies it, and a verifier's value is what it cannot do: it did not make the product and it will not repair what it finds. The Semiotic Vitrimer writes the law as an inequality: `producer(candidate) ≠ issuer(acceptanceReceipt(candidate))`; self-tests are observations, never acceptance.

An ACK, with its typed referent, is the uptake of a concrete WIT by a different receiving party at a border, with an uptake record. Self-certification is not an ACK, because an agent's own scale-1 self cannot contest (H¹ = 0 at that scale), so self-acknowledgement moves nothing. One party ACKs a contested claim without resolving it: acknowledgement records that the contradiction is recognised. Settlement needs both nuclei: a WIT drives a claim forward toward Fix Δ, an ACK drives it backward toward Fix σ, and a claim is settled only in H* = Fix σ ∩ Fix Δ. A WIT without an ACK is a half-tally, an obstruction that demands attention.

Kinetic proofing is the economics of the chaperone. Theorem 5.2 of the proof-pores article: a second discrimination stage earns the squared error floor f₀² only under two conditions. Irreversibility: the reject branch cannot readmit the candidate; diagnosis and repair are separated by a boundary the subject cannot cross backwards. Independence: the second stage is a fresh passage on information outside the σ-algebra of the first: executing the artifact, running its tests, recomputing its arithmetic, consulting a source the first pass lacked. The operative axis is the channel, not the agent: two parties over one channel buy f₀ and report more; one party over two channels buys f₀². Corollary 5.3: a second language model of the same class reading the same artifact under the same instructions is a correlated draw, a second opinion, and a second opinion is not a measurement. Theorem 7.3: when defect detectability is heterogeneous near zero, n passes of one method have power-law returns and converge to that method's blind spot; one pass each of n differently selective methods strictly dominates. Corollary 7.7: a corpus that estimates its own defect load from reviewer overlap believes itself cleaner than it is.

The ACK's "different party" and kinetic proofing's "different channel" are two conditions, and both are required. The party condition is a border law from governance: value and acceptance form only at a border, and attribution to oneself settles to ⊥. The channel condition is a fidelity law from physics: only dissipation buys discrimination. An ACK over the author's own channel is a valid uptake record that buys no fidelity factor; a second channel run by the author buys fidelity but mints no ACK. The work arena already carries both: a request names its assayer, a different party, and its conditions of satisfaction carry deterministic assays `{operation, request, acceptance}` the producer did not run.

Distributed verification fails three ways, and the Group instruments one of them. Local failure: two contexts disagree on their overlap; a pore is blocked; unblock it. Topological failure: every overlap agrees and no global section exists; H¹ ≠ 0 on the nerve of the cover; no local repair helps, which is why "all reviewers signed off" is not evidence of consistency. Kinetic failure: a global section exists, is reachable, and is not reached in the window; the pores percolate with too little permeability; the system is not wrong, it is late, and every quantity it records reports healthy. The proofing Deborah number De = τ_glue / τ_obs is computable only if every verification state transition carries a timestamp. Four instruments make the three failures measurable: transition timestamps; a utilization monitor that counts only completions through a channel the authoring pass did not use; a detectability estimator that records which pass caught which defect through which channel; a frustration check on the Z/2 holonomy of the review nerve.

Checks are purchased, never performed as a ceremony. Kinetic proofing buys additional checking when its expected reduction in acceptance risk exceeds its expected cost, and exposes the fidelity–throughput trade instead of setting maximum verification as an unpriced goal. This purchase is ATT in its budget sort: a bounded agent's finite reconciliation budget B and its costed selection of obstructions with total cost ≤ B, proved as a monotone-budget allocation (AttentionAllocation.lean). The governance theory bounds the cadence from above: correction at fidelity f every τ has an error-catastrophe threshold (Rendanheyi R3), above which no local diligence keeps the organism coherent; only lower drift or higher per-pass fidelity does.

## 7. The funnel

Levinthal's paradox: a polypeptide has astronomically many conformations, cannot search them at random in biological time, and folds in microseconds, because the landscape is funneled and descent is directed. The Group faces the same paradox at organizational scale. The arrangements of enterprises, offices, accounts, contracts and capability cells are astronomically many; finding the coherent conformation by survey, or by imposing an org chart, is the random search Levinthal proved impossible. The market is the folding funnel; the free energy it descends is FreeEnergyLyapunov's proved potential; a centrally imagined org chart is the denatured random coil. Central design is not disfavored; for a landscape this size it is Levinthal-impossible.

The market law (markets M1): market = defect discovery + constitutional classification + selective relaxation. The market discovers cyclic incompatibilities, the owning settlements classify each as damage (repairable without collapsing a protected distinction) or border (a distinction that must stay), and the market prices or relaxes only the damage. The market is never a global agent that drives every discrepancy to zero, because that agent would be a global gluing authority and would erase the sovereign membranes that make enterprises enterprises. Zero holonomy is a local, contract-relative fixed point; protected holonomy is sovereignty.

The funnel's mechanics in the Group's internal market: unaddressed demand is a signal, posted as an immutable content-addressed record of who posted, what is demanded, what is offered and who assays; signals are taken, not allocated, and the board holds only monotone facts, so taking needs no coordination and several enterprises take one signal; an offer grows with the signal's age on the poster's schedule, a stigmergic bounty instead of a dispatcher; the poster's assay decides acceptance at the border; takers whose result is not accepted carry the cost in their own books; staffing is the promiser's strategy, starting on the cheapest qualified rung and raising a rung after each rejected attempt, minimizing `expectedCostPerAcceptedOutcome = expectedCostPerAttempt / probabilityOfAcceptance`. Price forms only at a border (M3, M4): a measured ask and an independently owned bid for one exact lot either cross or do not; nobody selects a price or aggregates quotes. Every balance is owned by one sovereign settlement, and sovereignty over refusal is the load-bearing control (M9).

The funnel biases the descent and never imposes the fold. The vitrimer's pressure gradient is a derived preference among enabled reactions, formed from demand, fractional cost, expected acceptance yield, capacity, latency, risk and authorization; it is not a timer, a retry count, a token cap or a priority integer. Enzyme, chaperone and funnel share one discipline: each accelerates or protects the thermodynamic descent, and none of them authors the conformation.

## 8. The world

Agentic computing runs in a world, because to compute, to choose and to play are one act: taking a strategy in a game against an opponent, oneself included (theory of agency; Mysterious Machines). A MUD is not built on this theory; it is what the theory looks like wearing a face one can walk inside. The world model, derived in the theory of concurrent game systems:

- A **dimension** is a site: a category of areas with covering families. "An actor inhabits a dimension, moving through its areas and worlds." A MetroPlat instance is a dimension.
- The **settled world** W is a sheaf of FOUR-valued settled cells over the dimension; its history is the chain, and W is the chain's fold.
- An **area** is an object of the site: a world, a region, a room, a work board.
- A **player** observes. Its perception fiber P_i is what it can read with its senses, secrets and standing; observing an area U yields its arena A_i(U) = observe(W restricted to U and P_i). Events the player can commit are Player, events others commit are Opponent (agency A4, handedness). No player reads outside its section; stealth, secrets and fog are sheaf restrictions, not flags.
- A **player** commits a strategy σ_i on its arena: receptive, innocent, symmetry-invariant, with receptivity and courtesy forced by no-deletion (A3). A micro-enterprise's taking policy is its strategy on the work area. A character's behavior is a ludics design; a conversation is the cut between two designs.
- The **turn**: settled world → observe(player's fiber) → arena → run(strategy, move) → new utterances → settle (fold the record by ∨k) → next settled world. A turn is one cut elimination. The tick is the sole writer; commands are proposals for the next tick.
- **Conflict** in one tick is the obstruction to gluing the admitted proposals, the bigraded class H¹ = H¹_t ⊕ H¹_⊤ (truth disagreement plus held contradiction): computed, logged as a witness, resolved as an explicit choice, never flattened by arrival order.
- **Federation** of two instances is parallel composition ∥ of their dimensions (K3), convergent cellwise by ∨k and closure; an actor walks between them as a named entity over ActivityPub++. Activities that name their predecessors by digest form a causal set with light cones and no global clock; a node's knowledge is the join of the reports in its past light cone.

Humans and agents are one Actor model. The Leader, a Claude session, a Codex session and a service account are actors of one kind with handles, inboxes and capabilities; "Claude handing Codex an assignment is one actor messaging another, not a private call inside one process" (Leader, 2026-09-27). An agent finds work by observing its arena: the open threads of the area it stands in, the journal of what awaits its move. A side queue that hands an agent work outside the world is a second channel beside the territory, and it is the channel that moves work today.

The kinematic space of runs is the geometry of this loop at the scale of sessions. A bounded session against an instance has an ingress event and a settled exit, and with the instance's witnessed events as the causal set the run is a DiamondPoint. The space of diamonds of a finite causal set is itself a causal set under sequencing (`sequencingCausalSet`), with holes, horizons and shrouds of its own one level up; iteration is free (the `Hole` endofunctor), so campaigns and epics carry the same structure. Nesting is provably not the order (`nesting_self_edge`): parent and subagent containment cannot be the causal structure of the run space, and an orchestration surface that draws sub-runs as causal children draws a falsehood. A live run has a lower endpoint and a growing witnessed interior and no upper endpoint yet; the carrier never requires one at admission. Hypothesis: a run-valuation assigns each run a FOUR charge read from its realized contract outcome, and concurrent runs glue into one settled record without adjudication exactly where that valuation's H¹ vanishes, which makes "when do parallel agents need a judge" a computed obstruction. The diamond-to-cell bridge this needs is unbuilt.

## 9. Governance

The ruling system is generated, not written. The Leader's ontology (2026-09-11): directives are (subject, predicate, object) triplets where S and O are resources and P is an action an agent can perform if capable; policies are the directives active over a resource; rules are the policies that apply under the preconditions of a program. In the theory's terms a directive is a predication cell (S, A, O) whose uttering is a governance act attributed to the issuer's collective; a policy is the subject- or object-projection of the directive set onto one resource; a rule is the specialization of a policy to a program's precondition, which is a position in an arena, so a rule constrains the moves legal there. Projection and specialization are two of predication's four generators (P1), the only natural reindexings of an oriented pair. A directory of hand-written rule files is not a rule system: nothing generated it, nothing derives from it, and no collective is attributed for it. A rule names the compliant state as a check the machines run over the Group's actual state; a limit an agent must remember to read is not yet a rule.

A requirement is an obligation Oφ whose content φ is an acceptance condition on the plays of an enterprise's arena, issued by the party whose need it is. Its attribution is to the owning enterprise; its binding falls on that enterprise's offices through its policies and rules; its becoming is the change in the enterprise's admissible transitions (governing G1, G2, G8). Its content is about the world, not the machine: it names an outcome and its judge, never a builder, and it carries checks that can fail. It is met when the cell (requirement, met-by, play) settles to t: a WIT by the promiser that delivered, and an ACK by the asker through an assay the promiser does not control. A requirement filed in a global folder has no attributed collective; its attribution settles to ⊥, or to ⊤ when several sections claim it, so by G2 its operativity is never t. ## 10. Misfolds

A misfold is a wrapper, certificate, record, process or subsystem deposited beside the territory instead of folded from the proof. A prion is a misfold that propagates by templating the next act of construction. The Group's current failure modes are misfolds, and each breaks a named law.

1. **A status field on a record.** A charge asserted without an utterance (L3), seeded rather than measured (L5), stored rather than settled (L7); the classical projection that cannot tell "no evidence" from "false" or hold a conflict (P6).

## 11. The derivation pipeline as diagenetic zeolitization

Institutional knowledge undergoes diagenetic zeolitization: loose sedimentary prose is compacted and recrystallized into small stable records whose pores admit and catalyze future work without any reader ingesting the bed. Thick material is exactly what must be zeolitized rather than broadcast: broadcast prices every future turn; a pore prices only the turns that enter it. The pipeline prose → directives → requirements → specifications → architecture → design → fabrication is this recrystallization run stage by stage. The Leader stated its first half as a mechanism on 2026-09-26: prompts become notes, notes describe terms, terms give formal concept analysis, FCA gives a Curry–Howard–Lambek language, and the light cones of that language are the specification of the truth; `FCALambekCircle.lean` proves formal concept closure is the house's settling closure.
