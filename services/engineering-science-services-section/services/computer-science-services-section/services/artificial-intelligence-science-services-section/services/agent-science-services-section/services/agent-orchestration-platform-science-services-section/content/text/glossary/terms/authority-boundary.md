An authority boundary determines who may create or delegate work, approve an
effect, spend resources, change policy, or act on an external system. It is not
the same as tool availability: a runtime may technically expose a tool without
the agent holding organizational authority to use it. Evidence of the boundary
includes enforced credentials, approval gates, scopes, and receiver-recognized
office or role records.
