# Weiss (ed.) 2013 — Multiagent Systems (2nd ed)

Weiss, G.
(Ed.).
(2013). *Multiagent Systems* (2nd edition).
MIT Press.
(First edition 1999.)

A comprehensive multi-author textbook covering the full breadth of multi-agent systems: agent architectures, distributed problem solving, communication and ontology, multi-agent learning, computational game theory, multi-agent planning, multi-agent simulation, trust and reputation systems.

The second edition (2013) substantially updates the first, incorporating advances in multi-agent learning, computational social choice, and trust/reputation research.
Each chapter is by a recognized expert in the sub-field.

Together with Shoham & Leyton-Brown 2009, this serves as the standard graduate reference for multi-agent systems.
Where Shoham & Leyton-Brown emphasize foundations and algorithms, Weiss emphasizes practical multi-agent engineering and a broader view of agent architectures.
