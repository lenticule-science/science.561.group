id: capability-delivery-evidence

defines: capability-delivery-evidence
date-created: 2026-08-29
---

Capability-delivery evidence is evidence matched to the delivery claim. A code
artifact proves that code exists; a passing local test proves the tested local
behavior; a registry resolution proves publication; a deployed health check
proves a responding deployment at an observed time; and human acceptance proves
only the acceptance actually recorded. No single receipt proves every layer.
