# Agent Harness Science Services Section

This section studies the configuration, context, capability, permission,
observation, and lifecycle boundaries through which one artificial agent is
made operable. Multi-agent coordination and agent-platform research remain in
their own scientific loci.

The dated notes under `content/` are research holdings, not executable policy
or runtime configuration. Reusable implementations belong in the paired
engineering section at
`agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/services/agent-harness-computer-system-engineering-services-section/`.

Provider-generated reports and historical archives should retain their
provenance and advisory status when they are indexed here; they do not become
engineering requirements merely by informing this research.

See [the holdings index](HOLDINGS.md) for the classified dated notes, retained
provenance, adjacent reports, and curated legacy research roots.
