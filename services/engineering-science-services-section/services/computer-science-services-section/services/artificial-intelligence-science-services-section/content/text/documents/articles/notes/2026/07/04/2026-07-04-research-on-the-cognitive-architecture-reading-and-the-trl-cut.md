## 4. Technology Readiness Level as a derived cut (proposal, ruled in scope)

**Ruled by emsenn in session: this is specifically Technology Readiness
Level — the NASA/DARPA nine-level scale, by its external name — not an
abstract "readiness" coinage.** The external name is earned the same way
"black-hole region" was: the referent matches the standard's own definition,
because what the cut measures is exactly what a TRL assessor measures —
demonstrated maturity of a capability across environments — derived from
structures instead of interviews.

The identification: TRL is a settlement-filtration reading. A package's
level is how far its capability has demonstrably settled, measured either
abstractly or **against spec claims derived from planning** (work-ledger
goals/obligations, doctrine notes, package exports) — the "claims of what it
must have."

Candidate level mapping, every input an existing structure:

| TRL band | standard meaning | derivable predicate (structure) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | principles observed; concept formulated | named only in notes/corpus; no module (notes trees; module-topology absence) |
| 3–4 | proof of concept; validated in lab | modules exist with real bodies and intra-package consumers (module-topology cut; the register-audit "earned, not label" test) |
| 5–6 | validated in relevant environment | consumed across package boundaries; mounted on a dev-site dish (workspace-graph cut; sites' service mounts) |
| 7–8 | demonstrated operational; system qualified | mounted on an operating settlement (inhabited+live corner) with contract-witnessed traffic in `data/logs`; qualification evidence = contract reports and, where present, Lean proof of the governing law |
| 9 | proven in operation | sustained witnessed operation over time — read directly off log-tier duration and volume for the mounted capability |

Prior art already in-repo, so this is identification not addition: the quarry
canon's four maturity tags (Roadmap → Research-grade → Built+green →
Proven-in-Lean, triage AGENTS.md) are a homegrown coarse TRL; the blueprint's
`\notready`/`\leanok` is a two-level one; the agent status cut already
carries a `readiness` block; the `-izable` ruling gives the ◇ reading of
capability-before-characterization. The occupation corners supply the
environment axis (bare+live = bench; inhabited+live = operational).

Proposed artifact: `data/state/YYYY/MM/DD/${date}-cut-trl-${package}.json`,
a deriver behind THE `read`, no new verbs. Two modes: abstract (structures
only) and against-spec (structures scored against work-ledger/planning claims,
which also yields the *gap list* — the claims with no structure — as the
actionable output).

Conjecture, recorded under the four-pathologies discipline against
force-fitted quartets: the quarry's four maturity tags may be an instance of
the recurring four-corner cell (Roadmap = potential, Research-grade = active,
Built+green = stable, Proven-in-Lean = both). Not claimed; a proof target in
the `regimeCornerBij` style if ever wanted.
