***The Cathedral and the Bazaar*** is a [book](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/book.md) by [Eric S. Raymond](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/eric-s-raymond.md) in [1997](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1997ce.md) that [contrasts](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/contrasting.md) two [mode of software development](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/mode-of-software-development.md): the "cathedral" ([closed-source software](closed-source-software.md)) and the "bazaar" ([open-source software](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/open-source-software.md)). The book has become [foundational](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/foundational.md) in [open-source software discourse](../../../../../../content/text/glossary/terms/open-source-software-discourse.md), [Linux culture](../../../../../computer-systems-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/linux-culture.md), and some [hacker cultures](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hacker-culture.md).
