***The Cathedral and the Bazaar*** is a [book](id:caed3b82-143c-4344-a2c2-03824a83969b) by [Eric S. Raymond](id:0efde708-6895-4d49-890b-5793268e025c) in [1997](id:8b61a0d5-2d9e-4bae-a064-4d0a7555f28a) that [contrasts](id:19d278a2-0052-4edf-81b2-4d85a7413f61) two [mode of software development](id:600ecfbd-0499-42bd-b17b-ab907cd796b0): the "cathedral" ([closed-source software](id:30c00bf3-5afe-43d9-8760-a130f6a7b3e8)) and the "bazaar" ([open-source software](id:7a77249b-ef73-4f86-9b84-6f0189c07b43)). The book has become [foundational](id:af5c7da7-0f5b-4a80-bb5a-882b28d9aa6e) in [open-source software discourse](id:4c1ce256-f421-4601-9f46-ac8f3cf405b9), [Linux culture](id:dfaffd02-f11e-4d8d-894c-8364c1477d18), and some [hacker cultures](id:3c8bcdb2-e8f7-4b50-b4c6-23eac29d87a6).
