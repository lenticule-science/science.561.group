***Networked computing*** is any
[form](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/form.md) of
[computing](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/computing.md) that uses multiple
[computers](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/computer.md)
[connected](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/connecting.md) to a [computer
network](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/computer-network.org.md).
