## Plain definition

**POSIX holography** is the house's ruled sense of holography applied to
a POSIX system: *a local witness disclosing wider structure* (the
`holography` term of the engineering glossary, one word of TABASHEER).
The operating system's ordinary surfaces — a file's `stat` attributes,
the bytes of a regular file, directory shape — are read as charged
observations in the Obsmetrologia sense: each attribute lifted as an
utterance and folded into settled, evidence-bearing claims, so that any
local reading participates in, and partially reconstructs, the global
picture of the system.

The holographic property is precise, not decorative, per the 2026-08-22
holography studies (information-theory custody, with the optical sibling
in optics custody): reconstruction from a fragment is aperture-limited —
a partial reading yields a *blurred, not blank* image of the whole, and
more independent readings sharpen it. That is exactly the wanted
behavior for observing a live system: no single stat call knows
everything, and none is worthless.

## Provenance

The sense descends from TABASHEER's holography ruling and the
`extend-holography-study` campaign notes (2026-08-22). The name also
labeled a module family inside the deprecated
`@red-cup-engineering/computer` package ("POSIX stat-holography" in the
glass-sponge note, §7), whose capabilities are reseating into their own
sections — EAVT into `eavt-engineering-services-section`, the
predication-charge machinery toward the articulating proof tree, file
and codec primitives toward computer-data custody. As that code drains,
the name remains as this concept, not any one package.
