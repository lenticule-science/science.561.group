# Living Web Science Services Section

Groups the 561 Group's study of the Living Web: the web as a continuously revised system of living standards, protocols, and practices, and the discipline of building for it.

"Living Web" is a 561 Group coinage and initiative, not a W3C program. The W3C has no initiative by this name; the real referents this section studies are the WHATWG HTML Living Standard, the W3C Recommendation track (WCAG, ARIA in HTML, Webmention), the CSS Baseline process, IndieWeb building blocks (microformats2, Micropub, WebSub), ActivityPub federation, syndication formats, and the well-known-URI and sitemap discovery mechanisms.

The section's founding position: conformance to living standards is a continuous process property of a build system, not a one-time badge. The engineering realization of that position is VICTOR, in the Website Engineering Services Section.

## Contents

- `content/text/documents/articles/notes/` — study notes, beginning with the initiative's founding note and the dated frontier-and-engineering program.

The section observes standards bodies, browser implementations, deployed
protocols, and publishing systems; it is not itself a standards body. Its
engineering consumers receive dated evidence, conformance targets, comparative
benchmarks, and explicit uncertainty. TUFTE and VICTOR remain responsible for
product decisions and implementation.
