# Organizational Movement and Economic Witnessing in the Semioverse

## Why this note exists

The current Semioverse is the 561 Group's federated content-addressed network, not the 2025 Interactive Semioverse research artifact. Its canonical transport lineage is the composed public wire recovered and specified in September 2026: ActivityPub outside, a content-addressed CloudEvents envelope inside it, A2A carrying contracted agent work, RMN naming operations/content, and optional payment/authority material carried inside the message rather than inferred from transport.

Recent Group work exposes a missing semantic layer for what that network should carry about organizations themselves.

The immediate problem is practical. Leader statements, scientific findings, economic evidence, office actions, work, receipts, and settlement are currently too easily flattened into "work items." That destroys distinctions the house already developed and makes financial/resource observation harder than it needs to be.

This note records the research target. It does not revise the ActivityPub++ specification. The wire should change only after the semantic model is separately recovered, tested, and received.

## 1. Recovering movement as distinct carriers

### Action

The current mathematical glossary defines an Action as one thing operating on another: an operator acting on an operand and producing a transformed state. A debit posting acting on an account is an explicit house example.

An Action is therefore not synonymous with a task. A task can demand an Action; an Action is the transformation itself.

A future Semioverse record for an Action should be able to name, at minimum:

- actor/operator;
- target/operand;
- operation;
- prior content identity;
- resulting content identity;
- authority/provenance;
- resources consumed or obligated;
- witness of the transformation.

### Move

The agency/game-semantic lineage gives Move a different role. A Move occurs in an Arena: it is typed/polarized and enabled by the prior interaction history.

Working organizational reading:

> a Move is an Action situated in an interaction field with actor-side, type, enabling history, and legality/authority conditions.

This is a design correspondence, not yet a canonical definition.

That distinction is useful for the Semioverse because an actor can perform the same low-level Action under very different institutional Moves. Publish document, acknowledge result, offer service, and transfer resource acquire meaning from who is enabled to do them, in which arena, after which prior messages.

### Step

The historical relational/FARS lineage uses Step for historical advance: a witnessed transition from a prior state/history to a successor.

A Step is not the same as a scheduler Tick.

A useful target shape is:

prior state -- Move/Action --> successor state

with the Step retaining the evidence that makes the transition replayable or inspectable.

### Tick

The current platform term Tick is scheduling machinery: a heartbeat chooses an unsettled entity and applies one bounded closure rule, producing a witness.

So the research target should preserve:

heartbeat -> Tick -> possible Move/Action -> Step in history

without assuming every Tick necessarily yields a public Move or economically meaningful Step.

## 2. Leader movement is not identical to work

A Leader utterance can be an observation, question, idea, preference, hypothesis, interpretation, decision, requirement, or direction. None of these should automatically become Work simply because the platform needs a durable carrier.

A candidate Semioverse movement chain is:

Direction -> Requirement/constraint -> Work formation where needed -> enabled Move -> Action -> Step -> Receipt/acknowledgement -> settlement or renewed obstruction.

The purpose of representing this chain is not bureaucratic completeness. It is to preserve where intention changed into obligation, where obligation changed into execution, and where execution actually reached a receiver.

## 3. WIT, tally, ACK, and ATT

### WIT

The current monetary-economics glossary defines wit as one witnessed distinction-step in the FOUR knowledge order. Its contractual long form is the witness bit.

WIT is not a crypto token. It is a denomination of witnessed change.

The corpus connects witnessed distinction to several carriers—Landauer erasure accounting, index balance, proof-theoretic collapse, and commodity assay—but the theorem identifying all of those as one conserved ledger remains open. The Semioverse should therefore be able to carry WIT-denominated evidence without asserting that every physical, logical, organizational, and monetary carrier has already been proved identical.

### Tally

A tally is the reconciled release/admission receipt pair at a settlement border.

This is naturally network-shaped: a provider-side release is not the same fact as receiver-side admission. A settlement relation exists only when the relevant receipts reconcile.

### ACK

The September 2026 acknowledgement note proposes ACK as acknowledgement/uptake beside WIT. It is explicitly research/synthesis, not seated currency law.

The operational distinction is already useful even if the currency conjecture fails:

- WIT asks whether a distinction/change was witnessed.
- ACK asks whether a receiver recognized/took up what crossed the boundary.

A Semioverse carrying distributed organizational work should preserve both events instead of letting send-side evidence impersonate receiver acceptance.

### ATT

ATT is attention as an irreversible budget/resource. Existing research explicitly warns that attention is not a fourth currency.

This matters for agentic organizations because the principal scarce resource is often not compute or dollars but directed attention: a human review, an agent pulse, scientific interpretation, editorial judgment, or executive decision.

A Semioverse resource record should be able to preserve attention commitment separately from price, money, or WIT.

## 4. External accounting and house resource accounting must coexist

The Group presently needs ordinary financial statements badly enough that any house-specific accounting system must not become an excuse to avoid them.

The Semioverse should be able to carry both.

External financial records:
- native-currency cash inflow/outflow;
- revenue recognition evidence;
- vendor/platform cost;
- receivable/payable/obligation where warranted;
- founder/principal contribution or paid resource;
- custody/entity/period/source.

House resource/economic records:
- resources/capacity made available;
- ATT spent;
- Actions/Moves/Steps performed;
- WIT-bearing changes;
- ACK/receiver uptake;
- commodity/service lots;
- tally/settlement receipts;
- compute/inference/credential/service-capacity consumption.

Neither view substitutes for the other.

A USD P/L says whether the organization feeds itself in external monetary terms. The house resource ledger should help explain how that happened and where invisible subsidy or attention burn occurred.

## 5. Exchange cells and recursive organizations

Current 561 enterprise theory proposes that the basic enterprise unit is an admitted exchange, not one enterprise imagined as one giant solid.

An exchange is read as a local Hoare-shaped cell:

{P} C {Q}

with a precondition boundary, an interior operation/settlement, and a postcondition/outward liberty surface.

The J26/gyrobifastigial reading of this exchange is a design/theory correspondence. J26 combinatorics and several Obsmetrologia carriers have machine-checked components; the organizational identification is not thereby a theorem of physics.

The practical network consequence is independent of the stronger conjecture:

> exchanges need local identity, provenance, authority, resource flow, receiver evidence, and composable boundaries.

An enterprise can therefore be represented as a temporally growing colony/genet of exchange cells rather than one mutable global row.

## 6. Boards and C-suites can recur fractally

Agentic organizations make it cheap enough that governance organs can recur at local scales.

A Group, service house, service section, or sufficiently complex micro-enterprise can each have local Board/C-suite/Team-Lead functions when the exchange ecology warrants them.

Repeated role names do not imply duplicated authority. A CFO at one Cell observes that Cell's resource boundary; a higher-level CFO observes a different aggregate/federated boundary.

For the Semioverse this suggests a recursive rule:

- each organizational Cell may expose its own actor/office/governance resources;
- cross-Cell interaction occurs through explicit message/exchange boundaries;
- higher-level organization composes local Cells rather than directly owning every internal action;
- provenance should let an observer distinguish local authority from inherited/federated relations.

## 7. Content-addressed implications

The current Semioverse knowledge-meshnet direction—content and proof-path addressing, readings rather than mutable universal truth rows, causal activity structure, and coordination-free merge—fits these organizational objects unusually well.

Candidate objects to content-address independently include:

- Direction/Requirement;
- Action;
- Move;
- Step;
- resource observation;
- WIT witness;
- ACK/uptake receipt;
- tally pair;
- financial observation;
- exchange contract;
- office/authority claim;
- receiving judgment.

This prevents one mutable "work item" from becoming the sole history-bearing object.

A later read model can still render familiar task boards, P/L statements, work queues, service dashboards, or game-world observer charts. Those become projections over content-addressed events/relations rather than the ontology itself.

## 8. Research and engineering questions

Before this work becomes wire protocol, the next questions are:

1. Which Action/Move/Step definitions survive full lineage recovery across Relating/RMN, FARS, arenas/game semantics, relational histories, Work Science, Agency, Obsmetrologia, FeltOS, and current MetroPlat?
2. Which existing ActivityPub++ activity/document types can carry these records without a protocol extension?
3. Which belong inside the content-addressed SEN/A2A payload rather than the outer ActivityPub activity?
4. Which resource/financial observations should remain private/local while only their receipts or commitments federate?
5. How are WIT, ACK, ATT, money, commodity quantities, and heterogeneous resources typed so no implicit conversion occurs?
6. Can tally be represented as a relation between two independently content-addressed receipts without introducing a global settlement authority?
7. Can an enterprise's exchange-cell genet be reconstructed from distributed events under partition and later merge?
8. What does a conventional P/L projection require from these records, and which conventional accounting facts cannot be derived from house economic semantics alone?

## 9. Immediate institutional use

This note should inform three current movements without prematurely changing ActivityPub++:

- the Leader Movement Team's recovery of Action / Move / Step;
- Finance's external-accounting plus house-resource-flow instrumentation;
- the emerging international-market and opportunity-imagination Cells, which need to move ideas across research, engineering, and 561 application without flattening every handoff into undifferentiated Work.

The target is a Semioverse in which the corporation's intellectual and economic life is federable, content-addressed, provenance-bearing, and composable—while conventional accounting, authority, receiver acceptance, and protocol boundaries remain explicit.
