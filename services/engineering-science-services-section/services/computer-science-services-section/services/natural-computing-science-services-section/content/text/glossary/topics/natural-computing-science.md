# Natural Computing Science

Natural computing science is the study of computing regimes read from
natural systems — structure, process, or failure mode found in nature and
recognized as a computational architecture, a data structure, a consensus
protocol, or a diagnostic — on the thesis that **nature is computing**:
natural processes instantiate the same mathematics the estate's own
computers run, and an engineered computer is the domesticated,
deliberately-built case of a phenomenon nature already exhibits without
engineering. This is the owner's renaming and reframing, dated 2026-08-21,
of this section's earlier working name "naturomimetic computer science,"
whose mimesis framing (design that copies or imitates a natural mechanism)
the owner rejected in favor of the stronger claim: the natural process is
already computation, not merely a template for it.

## 1. What the field is

The field's material spans two kinds of natural system that the estate's
practice had, until this section was founded, been treating as one blended
thing under the name "biomimetic":

- **Biotic systems.** Living organisms and their processes: the glass
  sponge *Euplectella aspergillum* and its resident shrimp pair; bamboo's
  intercalary growth, nodal gluing, sheath discharge, phytolith deposition,
  and gregarious flowering; the silicon trace element folded into
  connective tissue; carbon's redox metabolism generally.
- **Abiotic systems.** Mineral, glass, and engineered-material processes
  with no organism attached: the zeolite-siloxane vitrimer's rigidity
  percolation and ossification dynamics; the J26 gyrobifastigium as a
  space-filling geometry for a rigid domain; jade's polycrystalline
  toughness; mud's phyllosilicate sheet structure; tabasheer's diagenetic
  maturation from opal-A toward chalcedony — a mineral process that
  happens to occur inside a bamboo culm but is not itself alive.

In both cases the claim is the same: precipitation and settling, crack
arrest, diagenesis, and tiling are not metaphors for computation, they are
computation — instances of the same settling closures, sheaf gluings, and
constraint-counting matroids that describe the estate's own software.

### The public field, and where the estate's thesis extends it

"Natural computing" already names a public, established research field —
there is a journal by that name, and an active literature on DNA computing,
molecular and membrane computing, quantum computing, evolutionary
computing, and unconventional substrates such as *Physarum polycephalum*
(slime-mold) network optimization. That field is thesis-agnostic about
exactly the question this section takes a position on: it asks whether a
natural substrate can be harnessed to compute something, without needing to
claim that the natural process was already computation in its own right,
independent of anyone building a device around it. Read this way, the
public field is the correct, well-established **weak form** of natural
computing, and this section's practice is the **strong form**: it takes the
public field's substrates and results as real prior art, and extends the
claim from "nature can be used to compute" to "nature already is
computing," whether or not a device is ever built to harness it.

## 2. Why "natural computing science" supersedes "biomimetic" as the field's name

This is the renaming's actual work, and it is worth stating plainly rather
than leaving it implicit. "Biomimetic" names design that copies living
systems, and even read generously it still frames nature as the *source* an
engineered design imitates. The estate's own practice, read honestly, has
never confined itself to that half, and even where it has stayed biotic it
has never been mimicry in the ordinary sense:

- The **biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section** program's
  strongest formal results this session are about *jade* and *tabasheer* —
  a metamorphic rock and an amorphous silica gel, neither one alive, both
  read with exactly the same rigor as the living glass sponge and the
  living bamboo culm that share the section's name.
- The **semiotic-smart-material-services-section** program is entirely
  abiotic — a synthetic vitrimer, a zeolite framework, a Johnson solid —
  and yet uses the identical settling/gluing/obstruction vocabulary
  (Čech cohomology, sheaf gluing, holonomy) that the biotic program uses
  for bamboo and glass sponges. The two programs were never doing two
  different things; they were doing one thing to two different classes of
  natural object, under two names that made the split look more real than
  it is.
- The 2026-08-21 note on **silica and carbon as yin and yang** is, read
  carefully, a note about exactly this pairing: carbon's redox, catenating,
  gaseous-completion chemistry against silicon's condensation-driven,
  siloxane-bridging, stone-completion chemistry — one dyad, its two poles
  respectively the living-metabolic and the mineral-standing halves of
  every system the estate's bestiary has touched. The note's own §5 reads
  the glass-sponge/shrimp holobiont as *elementally typed* — sessile silica
  partner, motile carbon partner — which is the biotic/abiotic pairing
  stated as one organism rather than as two separate case studies.

So "natural computing science" is not a rebrand for its own sake: the
field's actual object — nature, recognized as already running
computational structure — was never biotic-only, and was never mimesis to
begin with. A tetralogy of silica regimes running from dissolved monomer
through living deposition to metamorphic settling, with the living stages
sandwiched between two mineral ones, makes the abiotic half impossible to
bracket off without misdescribing what was actually found. `biomimetic`
remains the correct, unchanged name for the field's biotic subfield; it
should not be retired, only recognized as a part rather than the whole.

The evidence for treating the biotic and abiotic strands as one field
rather than two is direct: the jade/tabasheer readings and the
glass-sponge/bamboo readings use the same grading discipline, the same
obstruction vocabulary, and the same underlying formal machinery
(predication cells, settling closures, rigidity matroids) throughout, and
the silica tetralogy's structure is literally biotic-sandwiched-between-
abiotic. If the two strands ever turned out to need genuinely different
methods — different mathematics for the mineral cases than for the living
ones — that would be a real reason to split the field back into a biotic
one and an abiotic one, independently named; nothing in the record so far
motivates that split.

## 3. The method: the abductive spiral

The [abductive spiral](../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/abductive-spiral.md)
was named, in the biomimetic-semiotics program, as abduction under formal
constraint: speculate a structure, let the estate's own mathematics
(bilattice charge, sheaf gluing, causal cones, settling) narrow where the
guess can land, and check whether something real is already there. Natural
computing science uses the spiral in two ways, and keeping them distinct
matters for what each result actually claims:

**Recognizing an existing natural computation.** Start from a real natural
mechanism — bamboo's intercalary meristem, jade's grain-boundary crack
arrest, tabasheer's reversible index-matching transparency — and show that
it already performs a specific, nameable computation (a consensus
mechanism, a constraint solve, a filtration). This is not proposing that an
engineered system be built to resemble the mechanism; it is identifying
that the mechanism already is doing the mathematical work, prior to and
independent of any engineering. Where this recognition then informs an
engineered design, the design is a domesticated instance of a computation
nature already runs, not a copy of a natural form.

**Nature as witness (speculation confirmed by discovery).** Start from the
estate's own formal constraint — a settling closure, a cohomology
obstruction, an oriented cell complex like J26 — and speculate, under that
constraint, what natural object *ought* to exist if the constraint is doing
real descriptive work. Then check whether it already does: JADE
corresponding to an actual silicate; TABASHEER corresponding to an actual
materia medica and, one layer down, an actual diagenetic mineral sequence;
the zeolite framework turning out to be Phillips–Thorpe stressed-rigid at
exactly the coordination number the ossification account needs. This is
reverse-lightcone emergence (𝓡) run on concept-space, per the
abductive-spiral entry: imagining the entity whose constraint-valid past
turns out to already exist, where the "past" being reconstructed is
*natural history*, not corpus history.

A field that only ran the first mode would be biomimicry with better
bookkeeping. A field that only ran the second would be numerology dressed
as mathematics, since nothing would constrain the mathematics against the
world. What the estate's practice has actually done — several landings in
one session (zeolite-siloxanes, glass sponges, bamboo, jade, tabasheer, all
checking out against real literature) — is run the same constraint-
narrowing process in whichever direction the current question requires,
checking the output against whichever side (an engineering consequence, or
a natural fact) is not yet fixed.

requiring either program to change its own name, scope, or material to make
that reading possible.

## 5. Open threads

Whether the field needs subfields of its own, or whether it stays a single
reading-and-engineering practice over the existing biotic and abiotic
programs, is left open; no `contains:` list is asserted here because none
has been earned yet. Whether the two-mode statement of the abductive
spiral in §3 should itself be filed back into the semiotics glossary rather
than restated here, and how closely this section's strong thesis should
engage with the public "natural computing" field's own theoretical
literature (rather than only its substrate list) going forward, are both
decisions this document leaves open rather than settling.
