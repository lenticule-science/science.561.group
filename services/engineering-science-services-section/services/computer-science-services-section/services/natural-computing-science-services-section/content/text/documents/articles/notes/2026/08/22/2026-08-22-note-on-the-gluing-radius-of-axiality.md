## 1. The reformulation

The owner's original conjecture: *the axiality of life derives from whether it emerged in vents or was seeded from space.*
The methodological correction that produced this note's form: a conjecture about a **dependence** is not broken when one mechanism fails — the mapping just routes differently — so the right restatement is mechanism-agnostic:

**A broken symmetry's correlation length is a fossil of the coherence scale of whatever broke it.**

For homochirality, the candidate breakers form a ladder of freezing scales, each leaving a different cross-world signature:

| freezing scale | mechanism class | signature |
|---|---|---|
| light-cone / nebular | circularly polarized starlight biasing feedstock | every world fed by the cloud shares one hand |
| planet-epoch | a planetary field orienting spin-selective chemistry (the dark-compass class) | one world, one hand; possible paleofield correlation; still a frozen accident *with* a mechanism, since fields reverse |
| mineral grain | spin-polarized domains with randomized orientations | per-site coin flips with a mechanism, resolved by amplification |
| molecular | bare fluctuation plus Frank-type autocatalysis | per-event coin flips without one |

The observable is therefore not "which hand" but **the correlation length of handedness across independent samples of life**: same hand across a solar system implies nebular-scale breaking; same hand per world, uncorrelated between worlds, implies planetary-scale; multiple hands in one world's early record implies local-scale.
Every branch is the conjecture; the measurement discriminates the branches.

## 2. The house-mathematics statement

In estate terms: handedness is a **section of an orientation sheaf**, and the origin mechanism determines **the size of the cover the section glues over** — the gluing radius.
Nebular breaking yields a global section; planetary breaking a per-world section; grain-scale breaking yields local sections whose mismatches on overlaps are a genuine H¹, later repaired by amplification and competition until one global section survives.
Orientation-as-content is already load-bearing house doctrine — the predication cell's (s,p,o) orientation, and [OPAL](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/oriented-player-affine-locale.md)'s local order without global registration is precisely the grain-scale rung of this ladder rendered in stone.
So the conjecture in one line: **axiality is orientation's gluing radius, and the gluing radius is a fossil of where you came from.**

## 3. The competition branch

If the breaking was local (the two lower rungs), early Earth plausibly hosted L-chemistry and D-chemistry simultaneously in different pores — local sections with mismatched orientation — and universal L-homochirality was settled afterward by **competition**: Frank dynamics at ecological scale, one handedness excluding the other.
On that branch, today's uniform L is not a birthmark but a war memorial, and the evidence to seek is geochemical, in the oldest sediments — an unexplored search the estate flags as its own.
The H¹-repair reading makes the branch precise: amplification-and-exclusion is the repair process that forces a global section where local sections disagreed.

## 4. Amendments the symmetry fleet forced

The [three-source law note](../../../../../../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-three-source-law-of-biological-symmetry.md) tested the naive organism-scale version of this frame and killed it; the survivors bind this note too:

- **The fossil is developmental-genetic, not environmental.** Where "fossil of origin" holds at organism scale, the anisotropy is fossilized in inherited machinery — maternal determinants, ancient regulatory switches — not read live from the surroundings; *Linaria*'s single-switch peloria is the type case.
- **Gluing radii are overwritable.** A symmetry group is a currently expressed attractor, re-settable across generations (the echinoderm Hox rearrangement is the documented mechanism); filtration language is ontogeny-only.
- **Symmetry is assigned per scale and subsystem** (scale-decoupling), so "the organism's gluing radius" must always say which section, at which scale.
- **The cleanest literal instance is planar cell polarity** — a single junction-scale break propagated to tissue-scale order — with the honesty clause that PCP is usually *seeded* by an upstream gradient: a hybrid of the environmental and spontaneous channels, and the model case for saying which source does the orienting work.
- **The metric gap is the program's largest debt:** the claim is about correlation *length*, and the estate possesses no measured coherence length for any biological symmetry-breaking event — the evidence base is categorical where the claim is metric.
  Closing that gap (starting from the *Hydra* scale-free locking literature) is the theory's own named next experiment, alongside the dark-compass bench test.
