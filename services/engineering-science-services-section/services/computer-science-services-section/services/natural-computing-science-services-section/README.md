# Natural Computing Science Services Section

Natural computing science is the study of computing regimes read from
natural systems — biotic and abiotic alike — on the thesis that **nature is
computing**: natural processes (precipitation and settling, crack arrest,
diagenesis, tiling) instantiate the same mathematics the estate's own
computers run, and engineered computers are the domesticated, deliberately
built case of a phenomenon nature already exhibits without engineering. The
name and the thesis are the owner's, confirmed 2026-08-21, superseding this
section's earlier working name "naturomimetic computer science" and its
mimesis framing (design that copies or imitates natural mechanisms).

## What this section is for

The estate's own practice already spans two classes of natural system under
one method, previously carried under the narrower "biomimetic" banner:

- **Biotic**: the glass-sponge/shrimp holobiont and the bamboo/grove
  trilogy, developed under
  `information-science-services-section/services/semiotics-science-services-section/services/biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section/`.
- **Abiotic**: the zeolite-siloxane semiotic vitrimer, its ossification
  diagnostics, and the proved J26 gyrobifastigium cell, developed under
  `engineering-science-services-section/services/materials-services-section/services/smart-materials-service-section/services/abiotic-smart-material-services-section/services/semiotic-smart-material-services-section/`.
- **Both at once**: mud (phyllosilicate sheets) and jade (metamorphic
  polycrystalline silicate) sit on the same silicate-polymerization ladder
  as living bamboo's phytoliths and tabasheer — a study of the biotic/abiotic
  pairing as one dyad, not two separate subjects that happen to share a
  lattice.

Calling the field "biomimetic," or reading it as mimesis, undersells what
the estate's own practice actually shows: bamboo's intercalary growth,
jade's grain-boundary crack arrest, and a zeolite framework's rigidity
transition are not designs borrowed from nature — they are nature already
running the settling, gluing, and constraint-counting mathematics that
`pymetrologic` and the estate's other tooling also run. Engineering does not
imitate these processes so much as construct a controlled, deliberately
built instance of them. "Biomimetic" remains correct and unchanged as the
name of this program's biotic subfield; "natural computing science" is the
honest outer name for the whole practice, mineral and living alike.

### The public field, as the weak form

"Natural computing" already exists as a public research field (there is a
journal of that name), covering DNA computing, molecular and membrane
computing, quantum computing, evolutionary computing, and unconventional
substrates such as *Physarum polycephalum* (slime-mold) network optimization.
That field is thesis-agnostic: it asks whether natural substrates can be
used to compute, without committing to the claim that the natural process
itself already is computation in the same sense an engineered computer is.
The estate's program is the strong form: it takes the public field's
substrates and results as real prior art and a weak-form baseline, and
extends the claim — the natural process is not merely usable for
computation, it is already an instance of the same mathematics, independent
of whether anyone builds a device around it.

## What this section is not

This section does not modify, absorb, or supersede the
biomimetic-semiotics-science-services-section or the
semiotic-smart-material-services-section programs. Both continue exactly as
they are, under their own names, at their own paths. This section reads
their notes as source material and states the engineering programs that
follow from them; it does not re-home their content, and no existing dated
note, term, or file belonging to either program has been touched in the
course of founding this one.
