\## Imported source: archive/library/emacs/yasnippet/references/documentation-index/index–000757.org

The YASnippet documentation has been split into separate parts:

1.  [README](https://github.com/joaotavora/yasnippet/blob/master/README.mdown)

    Contains an introduction, installation instructions and other important notes.

2.  [Organizing Snippets](snippet-organization.org)

    Describes ways to organize your snippets in the hard disk.

3.  [Expanding Snippets](snippet-expansion.org)

    Describes how YASnippet chooses snippets for expansion at point.

    Maybe, you'll want some snippets to be expanded in a particular mode, or only under certain conditions, or be prompted using `ido`{.verbatim}, etc…

4.  [Writing Snippets](snippet-development.org)

    Describes the YASnippet definition syntax, which is very close (but not equivalent) to Textmate's. Includes a section about converting TextMate snippets.

5.  [The YASnippet menu](snippet-menu.org)

    Explains how to use the YASnippet menu to explore, learn and modify snippets.

6.  [Frequently asked questions](faq.org)

    Answers to frequently asked questions.

7.  [YASnippet Symbol Reference](snippet-reference.org)

    An automatically generated listing of all YASnippet commands, (customization) variables, and functions.

\## Imported source: archive/library/web/emsenn-net/references/homepage/index–002401.org

**Independent Research & Analysis of** ***Relational Dynamics***

My name is **emsenn**, and I study ***relating***: how everything that exists, does so through *relation*.

I approach this work as an independent researcher, coming to the study from lived experience as a **Lakota land steward**. (See [About emsenn](../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/biographies/emsenn/about-emsenn.md))

We're living through the breakdown of the ecology and society we've inherited, and old frameworks of sovereignty, individuality, and possession no longer explain how to hold life together.

My work develops ***relational dynamics*** as a way to find that explanation. Grounded in **Lakota epistemics**, *relational dynamics* is a framework for thinking about life, governance, and history as patterns of relation. (See [Introducing Relational Dynamics](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/08/26/2025-08-26-note-on-introducing-relational-dynamics.md))

This website is an evolving map of that work, built from connected notes, essays, and reflections: pieces that link, overlap, and change over time.

Some pages are rough. Some are formal. Together they form a field: a way of seeing how ideas relate and develop.

[My Essays](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/my-essays.md)  
A collection of essays I've written. A lot of these are hyperspecific, boring, and poorly written. You've been warned.

[My Blog](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/my-blog.md)  
Here, "blog" stands for "babble log:" a log of my written babbles. Unplanned, generally unedited, usually timely, usually unimportant, but occasionally interesting.

[Index](./theindex.org)  
The main index of my research archive.

Think of this site less as a portfolio and more as a curated park: a connected structure of thought you can walk through at your own pace, focusing on what you'd like to as you go.
