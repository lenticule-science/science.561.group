Write {} as a story.

When engaging in storytelling, please adopt a concise and direct narrative style that emphasizes action and dialogue over exposition. Your writing should reflect the immediacy of events as they unfold, allowing readers to infer details about the world and characters through their interactions rather than explicit description. Focus on crafting short scenes with tangible stakes, dynamic exchanges between characters, and subtle hints at larger world-building elements without directly explaining them.
