#+title: Making progress with GnoponEmacs
#+date: [2025-07-22 Tue 09:14]

Yesterday I made some progress with [[id:edc48bf0-336b-4b63-8505-a3730b328c9d][GnoponEmacs]], specifically setting up capture templates for [[id:8fe09255-271e-43c9-ac83-eb9ae1f6b4ed][my music log]]. Don't really love the work, and it has me wishing I had something like my ideal software for more properly spinning up the schema and functions that actually go into that, without having to shoehorn it into [[id:547430fd-7f25-4d55-978a-759f0513275f][Org-mode's subtree]] form.

That said, Org-mode's ability to serve as a metaframe above and outside my futzing, with its ability to already make a [[id:2945fa33-0393-46f0-84cc-f5c161a95de5][Website]], is definitely still a necessary tool at this point.

Today, my goal should be on getting GnoPonEmacs to publish [[id:cc253409-9a82-4c5d-a4cd-8013f4e350c6][emsenn.net]], which could be relatively easy, but I suspect I'll make it hard on myself. There's a lot of assumptions about the directory layout that don't necessarily hold up anymore, so it'll probably take a lot of failed compilations in order to get something, anything at all, publishing.
