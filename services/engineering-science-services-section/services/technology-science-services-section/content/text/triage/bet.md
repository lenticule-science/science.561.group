BET, or the *Bitcoin Exposure Token*, is a idea for a crypto token on [Hive-Engine](Hive-Engine). It will exist to accumulate [SWAP.BTC](SWAP.BTC) over time through token sales managed by a bot who offers to sell `$BET` at NAV+2% or buy it at NAV-2%. NAV will be defined as `((held SWAP.BTC)*(SWAP.BTC price in HIVE))/(BET circulating)`. Bot activity will be syndicated as posts onto [InLeo Threads](InLeo Threads).
