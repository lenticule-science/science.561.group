A specification is a document that defines conformance requirements
for an artifact, practice, or convention. It answers the question:
what must an implementation do to be considered conforming?

Specifications differ from descriptions (which say what something
is), tutorials (which show how to use something), and texts (which
argue a position). A specification is normative — it defines what
MUST, SHOULD, and MAY be done.
