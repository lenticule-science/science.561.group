A class of UAV operating at 3,000–9,000 m altitude for 12–30&#43; hours, the workhorse category for persistent surveillance and precision strike.

MALE (medium-altitude long-endurance) is a classification for UAVs designed to operate at altitudes of 3,000–9,000 m (10,000–30,000 ft) for extended periods, typically 12–30+ hours. MALE platforms are the workhorse of military UAV operations, combining enough altitude to remain above small-arms fire and short-range air defenses with enough [endurance](../../documents/articles/research/2026/07/31/lenticule-import/library/applied-sciences/technology/aerospace-engineering/archive/endurance.md) for persistent surveillance and strike missions.

MALE design is characterized by:

- High [aspect ratio](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aspect-ratio.md) wings (12–25) for cruise efficiency

- Turboprop or piston [propulsion](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/uav-propulsion-systems.md) for fuel economy at moderate altitude

- Conventional [planforms](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/wing-planform-selection-for-uavs.md) with separate wing and tail

- [Composite](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/composite.md) construction for low structural weight fraction

- Satellite datalinks for beyond-line-of-sight control

- Full sensor suites (EO/IR, SAR radar, SIGINT)

The engineering constraints of MALE UAVs differ fundamentally from small or expendable drones: [fatigue](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fatigue.md) is a primary design driver (thousands of flight hours), [safety factors](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/safety-factor.md) approach certified aircraft levels, and aerodynamic efficiency at altitude — where air density is 30–60% of sea level — demands careful [airfoil](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/airfoil.md) selection and high [L/D](../../../../services/aerospace-engineering-science-services-section/services/uavs-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/lift-to-drag-ratio.md).
