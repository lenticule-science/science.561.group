Been [thinking](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/thinking.md) more about the [neurotic platformal intellectual](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/cybernetic-postliberalism-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/neurotic-platformal-intellectual.md) (of which I am one, though I'm thinking we might all be in some regard.)

But specifically I was thinking about certain ways of shaping the corporate person that emerges from this, and like, the [feeling rules](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/feeling-rule.md) of the different places on the [Web](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/web.md) I am, and how those relate to what kind of [affect](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/services/agency-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/affect.md) can be a [commodity](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/business-science-services-section/services/accounting-services-section/services/gnucash-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commodity.md) there.

This is all related to [my public image](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-public-image.md) and my corporate person, and all of that relates to being an [influencer](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/influencer.md) and thus [authenticity](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authenticity.md).

I'm on the Fediverse, I have [my website](../../07/19/2025-07-19-index.md), I have my Substack, and I'm in [Turntide Islands](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/undated/turntide-islands-index.md), a [Discord server](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/discord-server.md). (And a few other places, like [my Ko-Fi](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-ko-fi.md), but I don't really use those at present. (Though I'm realizing I might want to participate in 2025 Ko-Fi Share-tember Challenge.))

The Fediverse likes [shitposts](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/writing-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/shitpost.md): [anger](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/anger.md), [snark](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/snark.md), and [brevity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/brevity.md) are favored (even if folk like to think they like the thoughtful pieces). This is [aestheticity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aestheticity.md).

My website is a huge part of my authority, in ways that are difficult to explain when it comes to [humans](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/homo-sapiens.md), but are a bit easier to point to with [Web algorithms](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/web-algorithm.md): the permanence, the density, the depth, are all things that [search engines](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/web-search-engine.md) love, and as [artificial intelligence](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/services/artificial-intelligence-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/artificial-intelligence.md) becomes a larger portion of the [Web user](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/web-user.md) demographic, the [semantic density](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/semantics-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/semantic-density.md) lets me develop authority for [long-tail terms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/long-tail-term.md).

My Substack, where I [syndicate](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/syndicating.md) [my Letters to the Web](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-letters-to-the-web.md), people there like [authenticity](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authenticity.md), in the standard [influencer](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/influencer.md) sense.

And the Discord lets me do [availability](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/availability.md), another component of [influencer commodity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/influencer-commodity.md) stuff.

Lemme ground that in the theory, for future reference:

- [Emily Hund](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/emily-hund.md) in [The Influencer Industry: The Quest for Authenticity on Social Media](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/the-influencer-industry-the-quest-for-authenticity-on-social-media.md), talks about [authenticity](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authenticity.md) as a [currency](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/currency.md) of [influencer labor](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/influencer-labor.md).
  - [Arlie Russell Hochschild](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/arlie-russell-hochschild.md)'s [feeling rules](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/feeling-rule.md) explains why: authenticity meets all the feeling rules of social media.
- [Grant Bollmer](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/grant-bollmer.md) and [Katherine Guinness](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/katherine-guinness.md), [The Influencer Factory: A Marxist Theory of Corporate Personhood on YouTube](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/the-influencer-factory-a-marxist-theory-of-corporate-personhood-on-youtube.md), talk about how [influencer brands](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/influencer-brand.md) function as [corporate persons](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/corporate-person.md), operating on [authority](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/services/work-science-services-section/services/governance-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/authority.md)
  - Dunno who to cite, but for [academic persons](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/academic-person.md), authority is H-index, which is a measure of how many papers published divided by citations.
- [Loes van Driel](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/loes-van-driel.md) and [Delia Dumitrica](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/delia-dumitrica.md) highlight the [aestheticity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aestheticity.md) of authenticity

  - [Theresa Senft](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/theresa-senft.md) and [Alice Marwick](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/alice-marwick.md) also talk about availability related to [microcelebrity](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/microcelebrity.md) and [parasociality](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/parasociality.md)
- [Tiziana Terranova](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/tiziana-terranova.md) also talks about the [commodification](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/commodification.md) of [producing knowledge](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/producing-knowledge.md) under [digital labor](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/digital-labor.md) conditions.
- [Richard Dyer](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/richard-dyer.md) back in [1998](../../../../../../../../../social-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/1998ce.md) with [star theory](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/star-theory.md) and [aesthetic persona](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aesthetic-persona.md)

So these things, authenticity, authority, aestheticity, and availability, are normally all available from an influencer brand.

What I'm talking about, thinking about, is in order to approach this currently-unavoidable neuroses with [jouissance](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/jouissance.md), I'm going to intentionally produce content along with these feeling rules, to [gamify](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/gamify.md) the process for me, in a way.

So, I'll do my good work on my files. This'll overflow into my Website. It'll also lead to things I feel like I need to say to others, that'll be my Letters to the Web. And it'll also lead to things that could be [aestheticized](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aestheticizing.md).

On that note I'm thinking about how I recently was thinking about [aphorisms](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aphorism.md), in the context of [intellectualism](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intellectualism.md) and [my ontology](../../../../../../../../../../../public-services-section/services/personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/my-ontology.md), and how I work to [construct](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/constructing.md) [aphoristic expressions](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/aphoristic-expression.md) of the terms I use.

And I'd already had the thought that there is probably also an [ecclesiastical expression](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/ecclesiastical-expression.md) for them, as well (if by explaining their [heresy](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/heresy.md)), and a bunch of other [form of expression](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/form-of-expression.md) - but, notably the aphoristic and ecclesiastical expressions are quasi-determinate, or quasi-formal, or something: they are derivable from other information, in a way that you'll get a similar result every time, unlike other forms of expression, like visual art, where you might get something very different trying to express "love."

But, going back, it makes me realize I could probably, when crafting another expression of a term, craft an aestheticized expression of it, and that could be material for the [Fediverse posts](../../../../../../../../services/computer-science-services-section/services/computer-systems-science-services-section/services/computer-web-science-services-section/services/activitypub-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/fediverse-post.md).

Thinking on this further, I'm realizing that it may be [good](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/good.md), in an [intellectually ethical](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/intellectually-ethical.md) way, to [center](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/centering.md) the aphoristic expressions: include them in aestheticized expressions, if feasible, and within the letters.

This would be similar to [Byung-Chul Han](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/byung-chul-han.md)'s [matric expression](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/matric-expression.md) of some ideas, but where the mantra are [hyperlinks](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/hyperlink.md) and themselves broken down: aphorisms where every meaningful word is explained with another aphorism…

…Until you get to more core vocabulary like [including](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/including.md) which have more formal expressions.

And the justification for the aphorism - not the meaning of the terms in it but the logicalness of the aphorism itself - is what's generally [cited](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/citing.md).

I think that's all the [babbling](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/babbling.md) I have on this for now. I like the four-part definition of authentic I've built here, and think it'll be useful for producing my brand.
