***Blacksmithing*** is [smithing](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/smithing.md) that is focused on [forging](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/forging.md) [iron](../../../../../../../../../natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2025/09/06/2025-09-06-iron.md) and [steel](../../../../../../../../../philosophy-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/steel.md).

The "[black](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/black.md)" in blacksmith comes from the black [oxide](../../../../../../../../../information-science-services-section/services/knowledge-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/oxide.md) scale that forms on hot iron.
