# Domestic Science Services Section

The Domestic Science Services Section studies the household-scale systems that sustain everyday life: healthful rooms, legible household accounts, and food production rooted in place and season.

Its maintained knowledge is organized by artifact role under `content/`, while specialized fields are developed as nested service sections under `services/`.

## Services

- `domestic-health-services-section` — domestic health, including indoor-air quality.
- `domestic-logistics-services-section` — household coordination and bookkeeping.
- `domestic-production-services-section` — gardening and mushroom farming, including home microgreens, sprouts, and oyster mushrooms.
