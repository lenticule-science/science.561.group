A geographic classification based on the average annual minimum winter
temperature. The USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map divides North America into
zones numbered 1 through 13, each subdivided into "a" and "b" halves.
A plant's hardiness zone rating indicates the coldest zone in which it
can survive winter.
