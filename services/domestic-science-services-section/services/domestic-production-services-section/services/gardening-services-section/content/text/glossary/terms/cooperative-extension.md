A university-affiliated program that translates agricultural and
horticultural research into practical guidance for the public.
Cooperative extension services exist in every U.S. state and many other
countries under different names. They publish regionally specific growing
guides, perform soil testing, and provide advice based on local
conditions. They are among the most reliable sources of gardening
information for a specific area.
