A layer of material spread on the soil surface around plants. Mulch
reduces water evaporation, moderates soil temperature, suppresses weed
growth, and (if organic) adds nutrients as it decomposes. Common mulch
materials include wood chips, straw, shredded leaves, and compost.
Inorganic mulches such as gravel or landscape fabric suppress weeds but
do not add nutrients.
