The climate conditions of a small, specific area that differ from the
surrounding region. In gardening, microclimates are created by factors
such as proximity to buildings, pavement, bodies of water, elevation
changes, and wind exposure. A south-facing wall, for example, creates a
warmer microclimate than an open north-facing slope at the same location.
