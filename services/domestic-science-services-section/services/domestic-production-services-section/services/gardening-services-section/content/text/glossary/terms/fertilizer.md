A material added to soil or applied to plants to supply nutrients —
primarily nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium (K). Fertilizers
are labeled with three numbers representing the percentage of each (e.g.,
10-10-10). Organic fertilizers (bone meal, blood meal, fish emulsion)
release nutrients slowly as they decompose. Synthetic fertilizers release
nutrients immediately but do not improve soil structure. Fertilizer is
applied when soil nutrients are insufficient for the plant being grown,
as determined by a soil test or by visible nutrient deficiency symptoms.
