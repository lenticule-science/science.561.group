A dormant plant embryo enclosed in a protective coat, along with a
small supply of stored food. Given the right conditions — moisture,
warmth, and sometimes light or a period of cold — the seed germinates:
the embryo breaks through the coat, sends a root downward, and pushes
a shoot upward. Seeds are the product of sexual reproduction and carry
genetic material from both parent plants.
