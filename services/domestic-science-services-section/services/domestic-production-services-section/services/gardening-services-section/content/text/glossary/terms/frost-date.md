The average date on which the last spring frost or first autumn frost
occurs in a given location. The last frost date marks when it is
generally safe to plant frost-sensitive crops outdoors. The first frost
date marks when the growing season for those crops ends. The interval
between the two is the frost-free growing season. Frost dates are
averages based on historical weather data and are published by
cooperative extension services and regional gardening organizations.
