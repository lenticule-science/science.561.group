The approach used to cultivate a plant, encompassing where it is grown
(in-ground, raised bed, container, indoors), how it is propagated (seed,
transplant, cutting, division), and any modifications to growing
conditions (soil amendment, irrigation, season extension). The choice of
growing method follows from comparing a plant's requirements to a site's
conditions.
